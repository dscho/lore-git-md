for cs in HEAD^..HEAD HEAD~10; do
```
  case "$cs"; in
  *..*)
     git format-patch --stdout "$cs"
     ;;
  *)
     git show --pretty=email "$cs"
     ;;
  esac
```

done

At least, this is what I have in mind and how I expect it to work.
