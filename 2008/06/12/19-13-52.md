**Thread**: [thread](./19-13-52.thread.md#:~:text=2008/06/12/19-13-52)

The contrib project can have test scripts like the ones in git.git.

They only need a test-lib.sh and a Makefile like the following:

[test-lib.sh]

GIT_TEST_DIR=$(pwd)/../../../t
PATH=$(pwd)/..:$PATH
. ../../../t/test-lib.sh

[end of test-lib.sh]

[Makefile]
include ../../../t/Makefile
