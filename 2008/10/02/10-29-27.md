> POSIX doth sayeth:

> ```
>   "In the regular expression processing described in IEEE Std 1003.1-2001,
>    the <newline> is regarded as an ordinary character and both a period and

> '^(FUNCNAME$)'", and more simply not '^FUNCNAME$'.
