    :pserver:anonymous@cvs.lysator.liu.se:/cvsroot/unfs3

module, and running

   cvs -d:pserver:anonymous@cvs.lysator.liu.se:/cvsroot/unfs3 co -P unfs3

   [jgarzik@core unfs3]$ git-cvsimport -v
