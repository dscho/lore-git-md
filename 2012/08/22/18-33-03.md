With two function-scope static, you can go like this:

die()
-> die_routine()
```
   -> xsomething()
      -> die_errno()
        -> die_routine()
           -> xsomethingelse()
              -> die() or die_errno()

```

Not that we probably care too deeply about, as at least we won't
infinitely recurse and die out of stack space.
