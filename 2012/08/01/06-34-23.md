I don't see how scores affect that. Suppose I have two trees and a
rename-cache file:

$ git ls-tree -r HEAD^
100644 blob d00491    path1/foo

100644 blob 0cfbf0    path1/fooo
100644 blob 00750e    path2/barr

d00491 0cfbf0 <score 1>
