Yeah! It works!!!

```diff
--- evil/Makefile
+++ good/Makefile
@@ -957,6 +957,7 @@
 	HAVE_PATHS_H = YesPlease
 	LIBC_CONTAINS_LIBINTL = YesPlease
 	HAVE_DEV_TTY = YesPlease
+	NO_THREAD_SAFE_PREAD = YesPlease
 endif
 ifeq ($(uname_S),GNU/kFreeBSD)
 	NO_STRLCPY = YesPlease

```

With this, I do have correctly working git clone.
