We could write the whole thing as a single "do these and leave as

```

	    ref_transaction_update(transaction, ref, NULL, NULL,
				   target, NULL, REF_NO_DEREF,
				   logmsg, &err) ||

	    ref_transaction_prepare(transaction, &err)) ||

	    : 0) ||
