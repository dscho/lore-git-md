This thread is about the final polish of the `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series, now at **v20** and queued for `next`. The series enables Git clients to query object metadata (currently size, with type support designed for future expansion) from remote repositories without downloading the objects themselves, and has been through extensive review and revision.

Pablo Sabater and Karthik Nayak are discussing two minor cosmetic issues in the final patch (12/13). The first is an unintentional restoration of `CAVEATS` references in the `git-cat-file` documentation—while Pablo added it for consistency, he agrees it’s unnecessary and doesn’t oppose removing it. The second is the reformatting of the `EXPAND_DATA_INIT` macro into one line per item, which Karthik finds less readable but attributes to a style-check script. Neither issue is technical, and both participants treat them as non-blocking. The only open question is whether the documentation nit warrants a reroll before the series graduates to `master`.

The series itself is **technically complete**, with all design and implementation concerns resolved. It has passed CI and is cleared for integration, pending these final cosmetic tweaks. Junio has already confirmed no unresolved issues remain, and the series is ready for graduation to `master` once these minor nits are addressed—or potentially as-is, if the maintainer deems them too trivial to delay the merge.
