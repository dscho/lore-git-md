The latest review from Karthik Nayak, however, raises substantive design questions about the client-side implementation in patch 10/13. He challenges the patch’s error-handling approach—specifically, its decision to silently skip objects missing size data rather than failing explicitly. This creates ambiguity about command success and complicates future extensibility, as it requires a "missing value" token that must avoid conflicting with valid data. His feedback suggests the need for a dedicated vtable entry for object-info queries and stricter validation of server responses, including a `BUG()` for unsupported fields.

The series remains technically sound, with comprehensive test coverage (680 lines of new tests) and cross-platform correctness, but Karthik’s review introduces open questions about its design philosophy. The discussion around silent failures versus explicit errors could influence the series’ user-facing behavior, while the transport layer concerns may require structural changes. Pablo has addressed all prior feedback, but these new points—particularly the error-handling strategy—will need resolution before the series can proceed.
