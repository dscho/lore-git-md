The feedback is purely stylistic—correcting a typo, adjusting the commit message formatting, and suggesting a clearer patch subject—without engaging with the technical rationale or implementation. This aligns with the series' current status: all substantive issues have been resolved, and only cosmetic polish remains. The series is now in its final state, with no open objections, and is expected to be merged soon.
