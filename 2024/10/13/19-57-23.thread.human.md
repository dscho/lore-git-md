This thread explores practical usage patterns and pitfalls of Git's symbolic references (symrefs). The discussion began with theoretical guidance about symref behavior and has evolved through real-world testing.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk shares their journey through several symref approaches, documenting what worked and what didn't. After initial attempts with symrefs under `refs/heads/` (which caused issues when committing) and root-level symrefs (which were worktree-specific), they settled on using `refs/H` for local branches and `refs/O` for remote branches. This approach has proven stable across worktrees while avoiding the earlier pitfalls. The email provides concrete validation of symref patterns discussed earlier, including using `git symbolic-ref` and the different behaviors between root-level and `refs/`-prefixed symrefs. The author reports these patterns have worked reliably in their daily workflow for half a year.
