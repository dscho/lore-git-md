The technical distinction remains that bracketed lines are treated as "non-trailer values" and discarded by `git interpret-trailers`, while formal trailers persist. Despite this parsing behavior difference, the thread shows senior contributors converging toward accepting the status quo bracketed syntax, with Junio's position carrying particular weight. The discussion remains open but now leans toward preserving historical practice, balancing attribution clarity with commit message hygiene while acknowledging the technical tradeoffs involved.
