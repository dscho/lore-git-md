The final version (v2) of the patch strengthens warnings about the option's risks while stopping short of full deprecation. Documentation now uses WARNING boxes (upgraded from NOTE) and clearer language about timestamp requirements, removing previous suggestions that there might be legitimate uses for this functionality.

The thread reached resolution with Phillip Wood's final approval of the v2 patch, implementing Junio's suggested approach of warning users strongly while still allowing the option.
