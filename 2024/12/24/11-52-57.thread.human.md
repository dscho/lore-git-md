This thread introduces a 2-part series adding cruft pack management to `git gc`, building on Taylor Blau's earlier work on cruft pack expiration. The first patch adds a `--expire-to` option to `git gc`, mirroring existing `git repack` functionality, while the second patch (now under discussion) addresses an interaction between `--prune=now` and `--expire-to`.

Jeff King (Peff) has reviewed the second patch and confirmed the technical correctness of how it handles the `repack -a` behavior when both cruft packs and expiration are involved. However, he suggests the fix might better belong in the first patch to avoid introducing a temporary buggy state. The discussion remains open on whether Taylor Blau, as the original author of the cruft pack expiration feature, might want to weigh in or revisit related functionality.

The series aims to streamline cruft pack management by allowing expiration during garbage collection rather than requiring separate `git repack` and `git prune` invocations. Implementation touches the gc/repack interface and includes documentation updates and test coverage. With Peff's review addressing the key technical question, the path forward appears to be either incorporating his suggestion in a reroll or waiting for potential input from Taylor.
