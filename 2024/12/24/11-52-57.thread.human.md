The first patch introduced `--expire-to` to mirror `git repack` functionality, while the second patch addresses edge cases in their combined use.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the implementation, confirming the technical correctness of checking both `cruft_packs` and `repack_expire_to` flags before disabling `-a`. He suggested restructuring the series to fold this fix into the first patch to avoid introducing a temporary buggy state. ZheNing Hu agreed with this approach, proposing the series could even be simplified to a single patch.

The discussion validated that `--expire-to` is only meaningful with `--cruft`, and the current implementation correctly handles cases where cruft packs aren't enabled. While there's consensus on the technical approach, participants noted that Taylor Blau's ongoing cruft pack work might touch related areas, though this series remains backward-compatible with those potential future developments.
