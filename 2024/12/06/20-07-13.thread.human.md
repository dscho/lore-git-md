The `git backfill` patch series has reached its conclusion with final maintainer approval from Junio Hamano, marking it ready for merging. This new command, developed by Derrick Stolee, provides efficient blob backfilling for partial clones with configurable batch sizes and robust sparse-checkout integration. The third iteration of the series addressed all technical feedback, including renaming parameters, expanding test coverage, fixing memory management, and improving sparse mode handling.

The only remaining discussion point - whether to mark the command as experimental - was deemed non-blocking and left as a policy consideration. With unanimous technical approval secured and no outstanding issues, the series is now positioned for merging into Git's main branch, building on the previously merged path-walk infrastructure. This concludes a thorough review process that has validated both the design and implementation quality of this new feature.
