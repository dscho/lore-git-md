This thread follows Taylor Blau's work on implementing incremental MIDX bitmaps, a key optimization for handling large repositories more efficiently. The current version (v4) consists of 13 patches that enable reading and writing bitmap data across multiple MIDX layers, representing the second phase of a three-part architectural effort.

The core design introduces layered bitmap files with independent tracking of commits, trees, blobs and tags, using logical OR operations to combine data across layers. Jeff King has completed a thorough review of the series, finding the implementation technically sound while recommending real-world testing on GitHub's infrastructure as the next step. Elijah Newren has provided minor editorial feedback on documentation and commit messages, mostly catching grammatical issues and suggesting clearer phrasing.

The latest email brings another small refinement from Elijah, pointing out two grammatical nits in patch 03/13 which introduces the core infrastructure for chaining MIDX bitmap layers. The patch adds `base` and `base_nr` fields to track previous layers and implements the recursive loading logic, with Elijah's feedback confirming the technical approach while polishing the wording.
