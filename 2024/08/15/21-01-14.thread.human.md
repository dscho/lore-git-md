Taylor Blau's 14-patch series implementing incremental MIDX bitmap functionality has reached a stable state with v5, now receiving final approvals. The series enables efficient bitmap operations across multi-pack index layers through a carefully designed system of structured bitmaps, representing a major step in MIDX bitmap support. 

Elijah Newren, the merge machinery expert who had previously reviewed the series, has now confirmed that all his feedback has been addressed in this version. The changes in v5 were primarily documentation improvements and minor code cleanups rather than substantive technical changes, including clearer variable naming (like renaming `bitmap_non_extended_bits` to `bitmap_num_objects_total`), consistent function naming conventions, and added test assertions. The core technical implementation around cross-layer bitmap operations, pack reuse, and the EWAH iterator optimizations remains robust and unchanged from the well-reviewed v4.

With this final review from Elijah and the series having gone through five iterations of refinement, the incremental MIDX bitmap functionality appears ready for merging. The implementation includes comprehensive test coverage validating both functional correctness and expected file layouts for split MIDX chains. This completes the second of three planned MIDX bitmap efforts in Git's ongoing work to optimize large repository performance.
