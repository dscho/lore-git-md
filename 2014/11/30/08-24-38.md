**Thread**: [thread](./08-24-25.thread.md#:~:text=2014/11/30/08-24-38)

If $GIT_COMMON_DIR is set, it should be $GIT_COMMON_DIR/hooks/, not
$GIT_DIR/hooks/. Just let rev-parse --git-path handle it.
