**Thread**: [thread](./06-54-05.thread.md#:~:text=2014/04/17/06-54-06)

Subversion can put mergeinfo on any sub-directory to track cherry-picks.
Since cherry-picks are not represented explicitly in git, git-svn should
just ignore it.
