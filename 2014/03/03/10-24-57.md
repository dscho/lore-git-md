**Thread**: [thread](./10-24-57.thread.md#:~:text=2014/03/03/10-24-57)

We used to show "(missing )" next to tests skipped because they are
specified in GIT_SKIP_TESTS.  Use "(matched by GIT_SKIP_TESTS)" instead.
