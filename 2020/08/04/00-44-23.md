5.14 has nice things like s///r:

```

   # We have to make a copy since s/// modifies its left side.
   my $foo = $ARGV[0];

   # 5.14
   # Now we have non-destructive substitution.
   my $foo = $ARGV[0] =~ s/a/b/r;
```
