Oh, it's much more horrible than that. RFC822 contains this example:

  Muhammed.(I am  the greatest) Ali @(the)Vegas.WBA

which parses to:

  Muhammed.Ali@Vegas.WBA

Perl's Mail::Address does decipher that correctly.
