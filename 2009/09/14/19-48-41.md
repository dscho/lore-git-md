offset and use of the seek in the above feels iffy.

```
#include <stdlib.h>
#include <sys/types.h>
#include <fcntl.h>
#include <unistd.h>
```


int main(int ac, char **av)
{

        int fd = open("testfile", O_CREAT|O_WRONLY, 0666);

        lseek(fd, 7, SEEK_SET);
        write(fd, "H", 1);

The only two in-tree users of ftruncate() do not care about this, though.
