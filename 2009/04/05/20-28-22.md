You can customize the encoding decision mechanism, for example:

```
    (setq mm-body-charset-encoding-alist
          '((iso-8859-1 . 8bit)
            (utf-8 . 8bit)))

```

For more info, see:

    C-h v mm-body-charset-encoding-alist RET
