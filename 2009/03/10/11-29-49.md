>     - adding necessary #include at the top of blame.c,
>
>     - surrounding blame.h with necessary
>
> ```
>         #ifndef BLAME_H
>         #define BLAME_H
>         ...
>         #endif
> ```
>
>
>       and finally
>
>     - updating Makefile to add blame.c and blame.h
