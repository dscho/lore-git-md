**Thread**: [thread](./../13/20-21-38.thread.md#:~:text=2009/06/14/23-16-12)

Scripts should use say() when they want to echo to stdout. Setting
GIT_QUIET will mute say(), allowing scripts to easily implement a quiet
option.
