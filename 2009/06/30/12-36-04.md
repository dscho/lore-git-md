>      In clearcase,I can use  :

>
>      how can I acheive it in CVS?

In CVS or in Git?

In CVS: "cvs diff -r <previous revision> <file>".
In Git: "git diff HEAD^ -- <file>".

Both assume that you want to compare with version from previous
commit.
