Hi,

git monthly links is my attempt to gather all the links people have
been tagging as "git" in delicious.com[1] (these are not chosen by
me).

The fancier blog version is here:
http://gitlog.wordpress.com/2009/06/29/git-top-links-2009-06/

This month I used an automated process, so now I'm not missing any
popular link, and you can see the actual number of bookmarks per link
(inside the parenthesis).

I also changed the format a bit, some links are repeated from previous
months. I will probably not that in next editions, but lets see.
Comments?

Enjoy ;)
