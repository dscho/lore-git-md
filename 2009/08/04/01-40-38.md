**Thread**: [thread](./01-40-37.thread.md#:~:text=2009/08/04/01-40-38)

Most users who type

  git svn init file:///tmp/repo --prefix=my-svn/

would expect the root of the svn repository to be tracked by
refs/remotes/my-svn/git-svn.
