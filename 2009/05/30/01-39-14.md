The new flags are:
```
    -a or -##: abbreviate SHA1s to 7 or ## places
    -r: reverse 'upstream' and 'head' (useful when on 'head' and wanting to
        pick from 'upstream')
    -d: only show "+" (doesn't have) patches
    -D: only show "-" (does have) patches

```
