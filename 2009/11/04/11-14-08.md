Even better, "return 0" is good under either platform (0 converts to 
void *), and LPVOID is the same thing as void*, so you can just do

```
#ifndef _WIN32
# define THREAD_RET_TYPE DWORD __stdcall
#else
# define THREAD_RET_TYPE void *
```

#endif
