It may be slightly more Perl-ish to hoist the "0-or-1" outside the group
and rely on $1 becoming undef, like this:

```
        my ($force, $local, $remote) = $refspec =~ /^(\+)?([^:]*):([^:]*)$/
		or die(...);

```

Even though it largely is a matter of taste, I think.
