> The POD rule doesn't work properly. I suspect it has to be:

>
>     "^=head[0-9] ",

>
>     =head1 WHATEVER

>
>     =head1

>
> ```
>     sub foo {
>     }
>
> ```
>

>

>     sub foo

>     }
