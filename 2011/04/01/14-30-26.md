http://www.kernel.org/pub/software/scm/git/docs/git-filter-branch.html ,

```
        'git ls-files -s | sed "s-\t\"*-&newsubdir/-" |
                GIT_INDEX_FILE=$GIT_INDEX_FILE.new \

```
