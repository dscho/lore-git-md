Yeah, that syntax is handled just fine by my bash and dash:

```
  $ cat >foo.sh <<'EOF'
  i=1
  : $((i = $i + 1))
  echo $i
  EOF

  $ bash foo.sh
  2
  $ bash --version | head -n 1
  GNU bash, version 4.1.5(1)-release (x86_64-pc-linux-gnu)

  $ dash foo.sh
  2

```

But I think your i=$(($i + 1)) is the right solution.
