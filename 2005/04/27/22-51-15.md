...and a relatively simple way to use that rule to extract just the
header lines:

      sed -n "1,/^\$/p"                     # with the separator line

or, either one of these to remove the separator line as well:

```
      sed -n "1,/^\$/s/^\(..*\)/\1/p"  
      sed -n "1,/^\$/p" | tr -s \\012

```
