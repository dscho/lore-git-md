+#AUTHOR_NAME		Author's name
+#AUTHOR_EMAIL		Author's e-mail address
+#AUTHOR_DATE		Date, perhaps from a patch e-mail
+#COMMIT_AUTHOR_NAME	Committer's name
+#COMMIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL	Committer's e-mail address
