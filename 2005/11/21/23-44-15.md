Something like this?

##########
Introduce $(ALL_PROGRAMS) for 'all:' and 'install:' to operate on.

Remove $(SIMPLE_PROGRAMS) from $(PROGRAMS) so buildrules don't have
to be overridden.

Put $(SCRIPTS) with the other target-macros so it doesn't get lonely.
