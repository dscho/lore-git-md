Instead of using //{LF}// and //{TAG}// to quote embedded tab and
linefeed characters in pathnames use URI quoting.

'\t' becomes %09
'\n' becomes %10
'%' becomes %25

Signed-off-by: Robert Fitzsimons <robfitz@273k.net>
