> Fix gcc-4 warning in accept() call

> is socklen_t, which is unsigned.  Since Linus doesn't like socklen_t
> (see commit 7fa090844f7d1624c7d1ffc621aae6aec84a1110), let's use
> unsigned int.
>
> Signed-off-by: Pavel Roskin <proski@gnu.org>
