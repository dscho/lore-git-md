For FLEX_ARRAYs, I use this:

```
	cov-build --dir cov-int \
                make -j15 DEVELOPER=1 CPPFLAGS=-DFLEX_ARRAY=65536

```

I have not had time to go over more than half a dozen CIDs, but I *think*
it helps.
