 that look strange?

	> ./git update-ref -h
	usage: git-update-ref [options] -d <refname> <oldval>
	   or: git-update-ref [options]    <refname> <newval> [<oldval>]

	    -m <reason>           reason of the update
	    -d                    deletes the reference
	    --no-deref            update <refname> not the one it points to
