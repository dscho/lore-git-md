I've had some similar problem.  I've converted two big old repositories by
first converting to SVN using:

```
	cvs2svn -s myrepo-svn /path/to/cvsmodule
	git-svnimport -i -u -C /path/to-git file://myrepo-svn

```

Worked like a charm
