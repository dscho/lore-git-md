**Thread**: [thread](./14-20-50.thread.md#:~:text=2007/09/07/14-20-50)

Pass --whitespace=<option> to git-apply.  Since git-apply and git-am
expect this, I'm always surprised when I try to give it to git-rebase
and it doesn't work.
