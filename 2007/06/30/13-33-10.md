And what do you do if the HEAD is a reflink on something not in refs/?
Like "ref: tmp"? Yes, it is unlikely, but is not forbidden.

How about "[0-9a-f]*)" instead:

```
               case "$head_sha1" in
               [0-9a-f]*)
                       git-http-fetch $v -a "$head_sha1" "$1" ||
                       rm -f "$GIT_DIR/REMOTE_HEAD"
                       ;;
               esac
```
