**Thread**: [thread](./21-57-10.thread.md#:~:text=2007/03/23/21-57-10)

The branch you are on while bisecting is always "bisect", and
checking for "refs/heads/bisect*" is wrong.  Only check if it is
exactly "refs/heads/bisect".
