```
  dcommit          Commit several diffs to merge with upstream
                     --quiet, -q 
                     --fetch-all, --all 
                     --no-auth-cache 
                     --no-rebase 
                     --no-checkout 
                     --rmdir 
                     --dry-run, -n 
                     --find-copies-harder 
                     --follow-parent, --follow! 
                     --authors-file, -A <arg>
                     --copy-similarity, -C <num>
                     --username <arg>
                     --repack-flags, --repack-args, --repack-opts <arg>
                     --repack <num>
                     --verbose, -v 
                     --edit, -e 
                     --log-window-size <num>
                     --config-dir <arg>
                     -l <num>
                     --merge, -m, -M 
                     --strategy, -s <arg>

```


Try playing with the --copy-similarity and --find-copies-harder flags.
