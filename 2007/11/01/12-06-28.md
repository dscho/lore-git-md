**Thread**: [thread](./12-06-25.thread.md#:~:text=2007/11/01/12-06-28)

Use href(-replay=>1, page=>$page-1) and href(-replay=>1, page=>$page+1)
to generate previous page and next page links.

Generate next page link only once.
