> give at least two components, while a bare command gives only one.

> ```
> 	  Linux:   puts [file split {foo}]       ==>  foo
> 	  Linux:   puts [file split {/foo}]      ==>  / foo

> 	  Windows: puts [file split {foo}]       ==>  foo
> 	  Windows: puts [file split {c:\foo}]    ==>  c:/ foo
> 	  Windows: puts [file split {.git\foo}]  ==> .git foo
> ```
