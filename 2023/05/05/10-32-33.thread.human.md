This thread documents a small but precise correction to the `git restore` man page. The original documentation incorrectly stated that `git restore --patch` couldn't handle pathspecs, a limitation that was removed in Git 2.35 when the underlying interactive machinery was rewritten in C. The patch authoritatively removes this outdated note after verification through bisection.

Johannes Schindelin, who was deeply involved in the original conversion of `add -i` to C, reviewed the change and confirmed its accuracy. The patch is now in its second version, with only minor commit message improvements since the initial submission. With this authoritative review and the straightforward nature of the documentation fix, the change appears ready for merging.
