**Thread**: [thread](./../../03/26/17-31-46.thread.md#:~:text=2023/04/02/11-36-07)

Exit codes are lost due to piping and command substitution:

- "git ... | <command>"
- "<command> $(git ... )"

Fix these issues using the intermediate step of writing output to file.
