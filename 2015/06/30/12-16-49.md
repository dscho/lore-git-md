Do not consider quote inside a recipient name as character when
they are not escaped. This interprets:

  "Jane" "Doe" <jdoe@example.com>

as:

  "Jane Doe" <jdoe@example.com>

instead of:

  "Jane\" \"Doe" <jdoe@example.com>
