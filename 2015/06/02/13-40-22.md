Salerno (Italy):  Salerno is one of the most beautiful town of Italy in Mediterranean Sea. The main town is close to the Amalfi Coast, and is mostly known for its Schola Medica Salernitana, the first medical school in the world. In the 16th century, under the Sanseverino family, among the most powerful feudal lords in Southern Italy, the city became a great centre of learning, culture and the arts, and the family hired several of the greatest intellectuals of the time. In recent history the city hosted the King of Italy, who moved from Rome in 1943 after Italy negotiated a peace with the Allies in World War II. Today Salerno is an important cultural centre in Campania and Italy and has had a long and eventful history. The city has a rich and varied culture, and the city is divided into thr
 ee distinct regions: the medieval sector with a modern state-of-the arts area, the planned 19th
century district and the more densely populated post-war area, with its several apartment blocks. There is easy transportation by aeroplane and train. The University of Salerno supports and sponsors all these conferences. Wikipedia: Salerno
