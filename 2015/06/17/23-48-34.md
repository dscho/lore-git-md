If there's no phrase, this will choose the "git@vger.kernel.org" form,
in both cases, because it'll be recognize as an address, $str_address
will be "git@vger.kernel.org" and $str_phrase will be empty before the
if ($str_address ne "" ...)
Here are some tests:
