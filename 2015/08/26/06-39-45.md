It's more than just colors. The whole format changes.

$ git branch -a
For local:
"%(if)%(HEAD)%(then)%(HEAD) %(color:green)%(refname:short)%(else)

For remote:
"  remotes/%(color:red)%(refname:short)%(color:reset)%(if)%(symref)%(then)
-> %(symref:short)%(end)"
