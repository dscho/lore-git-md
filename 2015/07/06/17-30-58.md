**Thread**: [thread](./17-30-37.thread.md#:~:text=2015/07/06/17-30-58)

As a convenience, like "git branch" and "git checkout -b", make
"git worktree add -b <newbranch> <path> <branch>" default to HEAD when
<branch> is omitted.
