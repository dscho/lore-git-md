How portable is open on a directory?  Perhaps it'd be better to
check if it's a file, first:

```
	if (-f $ENV{GIT_DIR}) {
		open(my $fh, '<', $ENV{GIT_DIR}) or
			die "failed to open $ENV{GIT_DIR}: $!\n";
		$ENV{GIT_DIR} = $1 if <$fh> =~ /^gitdir: (.+)$/;
	}
