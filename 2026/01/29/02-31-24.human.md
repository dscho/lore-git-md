This is a discussion about fixing a bug in Git's bitmap handling when exact refs are specified in `pack.preferBitmapTips`. Taylor Blau is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that addresses a BUG() condition triggered when users specify exact refs (like "refs/heads/main") rather than prefixes (like "refs/heads/").

Taylor raises two key points beyond the immediate bugfix. First, he questions whether the current behavior of matching both exact refs and their prefixes (e.g., "refs/heads/base" matching both itself and "refs/heads/base-something") is desirable, noting it contradicts recent changes to ref exclusion patterns. Second, he points out that even with the fix, `pack.preferBitmapTips` doesn't guarantee bitmap selection for all specified refs due to how commits are batched during bitmap generation.

The discussion highlights an interface design tension between backward compatibility and consistency. While the immediate bug needs fixing (and Patrick's patch does that), Taylor suggests the behavior might warrant either documentation or reconsideration to align with Git's other ref-matching patterns. This is a thoughtful review from someone deeply familiar with Git's bitmap implementation, raising valid concerns about long-term interface design while acknowledging the immediate fix is technically correct.
