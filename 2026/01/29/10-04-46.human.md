This is a follow-up in the git-gui tab alignment bugfix thread, where Johannes Sixt (the git-gui maintainer) clarifies a point about merge diff handling. The discussion had previously identified that git-gui's diff viewer misaligned tabs compared to gitk, and the proposed solution involved adjusting tab stops to match gitk's behavior.

Sixt explains that git-gui doesn't need to handle octopus merge cases (merges with more than two parents) because git-gui only displays diffs involving the index, which inherently limits it to three-way diffs (theirs, ours, and working copy). This confirms that the current patch's scope is sufficient for git-gui's use cases, as it already handles regular diffs, combined diffs, and untracked files correctly.

The response comes after Chris Idema asked how to replicate an octopus merge scenario in git-gui, showing the patch author's thoroughness in testing edge cases. Sixt's reply effectively closes that line of inquiry by explaining git-gui's architectural constraints. This keeps the focus on the core alignment fix that has already been validated across git-gui's supported diff types.
