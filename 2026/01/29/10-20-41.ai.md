This is a planning email for Git's Google Summer of Code 2026 participation, continuing the thread about project ideas and mentorship assignments. Christian Couder (Git PLC member and GSoC coordinator) responds to Kaartic Sivaraam's reminder about the February 3 deadline for finalizing project ideas.

Christian proposes eight potential GSoC projects, all building on existing Git work. The suggestions include continuing René Scharfe's `the_repository` removal effort, improving `git cat-file` based on Eric Ju's old work, enhancing promisor remote functionality (particularly around fetch ordering and disk space management), and expanding the newer `git repo` subcommands' capabilities. He also mentions potential overlap with GitLab's planned work on signature handling in fast-export/fast-import.

The email shows the project selection process in action - Christian is both suggesting technical directions and self-assessing his ability to mentor them (ruling out the `git repo` ideas due to limited familiarity). This continues the thread's focus on balancing newcomer accessibility with meaningful technical impact, using 2025's microprojects model as a template. The proposals are concrete enough to evaluate while leaving room for refinement based on student interest and mentor availability.
