Maintainer feedback on path normalization refactoring. Junio C Hamano responds to Pushkar Singh's patch that split `normalize_path_copy_len()` into helper functions. While acknowledging the `skip_slashes()` helper as a clear improvement, Junio expresses reservations about the other changes. He finds the `handle_dot_component()` helper actually reduces readability by separating the implementation from its explanatory comments and making control flow harder to follow. The `strip_last_component()` helper is seen as only marginally beneficial - Junio suggests a simple comment would suffice without introducing a new function. 

The feedback indicates this refactoring may not be worth the churn, with only one of the three proposed helpers (slash handling) being an unambiguous win. Junio's suggested alternative is a minimal documentation tweak - adding a clarifying comment about the "up_one" label's purpose rather than restructuring the code. This reflects the maintainer's preference for preserving existing control flow when readability gains are marginal. The patch appears unlikely to proceed in its current form given these concerns.
