This is a technical review response from Junio C Hamano regarding patch 2/3 in Olamide Caleb Bello's RFC series to migrate global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort.

Junio acknowledges the mechanical nature of the changes, which simply replace direct access to `core_apply_sparse_checkout` with access through `the_repository->config_values.apply_sparse_checkout`. While he confirms the technical correctness of this specific migration, he raises the same fundamental architectural question as in previous reviews: should initialization of these values require explicit calls to `repo_config_values_init()`, or should they continue relying on BSS zero-initialization as the globals did before?

The review indicates that for these particular variables (which were zero-initialized in BSS), the explicit initialization isn't strictly necessary, but the question remains unresolved for the broader effort. Junio doesn't propose blocking the current patch on this issue, but his repeated mention of it signals that the initialization strategy needs clearer resolution before the approach can scale to more complex cases or be considered for merging.

This email continues the pattern where the technical implementation of individual migrations is validated, but the series remains blocked on answering higher-level architectural questions about initialization patterns in multi-repository contexts. The work serves as a proof-of-concept for the migration pattern while the project determines how to handle the initialization guarantees needed for the broader `the_repository` removal effort.
