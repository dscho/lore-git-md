This is a review response from Junio C Hamano on the third patch in Olamide Caleb Bello's RFC series to migrate configuration variables from globals to repository-specific storage in `struct repo_config_values`. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

Junio notes that unlike previous migrations in the series, the `git_branch_track` variable being moved here was not zero-initialized. He suggests initializing it statically in the `the_repo` definition rather than through the `repo_config_values_init()` helper function, which avoids potential issues with forgotten updates or third-party callers. The proposed change would set the default value directly in the repository struct initialization.

The patch itself moves the `branch.autoSetupMerge` configuration into the new `repo_config_values` struct, updating all access points to use the repository-specific storage. This follows the same pattern as previous patches in the series for `core.attributesFile` and `core.sparseCheckout`.

Junio's feedback indicates the technical approach is sound for this specific migration, though the broader architectural questions around initialization patterns and multi-repo handling remain open from earlier discussions in the thread. The review continues the pattern of validating individual migrations while maintaining that the overall effort needs to resolve higher-level design questions before proceeding further.
