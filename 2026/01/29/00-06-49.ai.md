This is a bugfix for git-gui's diff viewer tab alignment on Windows, following up on previous discussion about matching gitk's behavior.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on the technical details of tabstop positioning in the diff viewer, responding to Johannes Sixt's observation about optimal tabstop values. The discussion clarifies that while gitk's current implementation (tabstops at 9,17,25,...) makes more logical sense than the proposed (1,9,17,...), there's no practical difference in behavior since the first column always contains a diff marker ('+', '-', or space) rather than a tab character.

The email reinforces that this is a long-standing behavior in gitk (since 2007) that git-gui should match, though Junio notes the visual difference is subtle enough that it went unnoticed for nearly two decades. This suggests the fix is more about consistency than addressing a critical usability issue.

Key technical points for future reference:
- The solution involves Tk's `apply_tab_size` configuration
- Tabstops will be offset by 1 column to account for diff markers
- The implementation preserves staging functionality (a concern with earlier approaches)
- While gitk's tabstop numbering is more theoretically correct, the practical output is identical

The thread shows the patch is nearing finalization with maintainer consensus on both the approach and its justification, though Junio's comment about the issue's low severity may indicate this isn't a high-priority fix.
