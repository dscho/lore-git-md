This is a planning email for Git's participation in Google Summer of Code 2026, continuing the thread about project idea generation. Christian Couder, a Git PLC member and the GSoC coordinator, responds to Kaartic Sivaraam's reminder about the February 3 deadline for finalizing project ideas.

Christian proposes eight potential GSoC projects, all building on existing Git work. The suggestions range from continuing René Scharfe's `the_repository` removal effort to improving promisor remote functionality and enhancing repository inspection commands like `git repo info`. He notes that while he could mentor any of these, he lacks expertise in the `git repo` subcommands and would prefer to focus elsewhere.

The email keeps the discussion moving toward the deadline, with Christian offering concrete suggestions while acknowledging potential overlaps with GitLab's internal roadmap (particularly around signature handling in fast-export/fast-import). The tone is collaborative, with an emphasis on finding projects suitable for newcomers while still providing meaningful technical contributions.
