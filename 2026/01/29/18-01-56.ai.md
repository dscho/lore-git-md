This is a review response from Junio C Hamano on the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Olamide Caleb Bello's patch to migrate `core.attributesFile` from a global variable to repository-specific storage.

Junio's feedback suggests the technical implementation is sound for this specific migration, while implicitly reinforcing his earlier concerns about the broader initialization patterns needed to make the `the_repository` removal robust across all use cases.
