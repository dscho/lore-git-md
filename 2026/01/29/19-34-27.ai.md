This is a follow-up discussion about documentation refinements for the bitmap tip selection behavior in Git's pack-bitmap functionality, part of Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing exact ref matching bugs.

1. **Chunk-based selection rationale**: Taylor proposes clarifying why multiple preferred tips in the same window may not all get bitmaps, explaining it's due to the windowing algorithm that spaces bitmaps throughout history while selecting only one commit per window. Taylor notes the original intent was for "refs/heads/foo" to match "refs/heads/foo/bar" (hierarchy) but not "refs/heads/foobar" (prefix), though the current wording doesn't clearly convey this. The discussion suggests this may need revision depending on the series' outcome.

The exchange focuses on improving documentation accuracy rather than changing behavior, with Taylor providing concrete wording for the windowing explanation while leaving the ref matching description for later resolution.
