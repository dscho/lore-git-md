Junio Hamano responds to Harald Nordgren's work-in-progress patch series that introduces a shorthand for referencing a repository's primary branch. The current iteration has shifted from using `init.defaultBranch` to resolving via upstream tracking (typically `origin/HEAD`), now calling it `@{primary}` rather than the original `@{default}` proposal.

Junio expresses concern about naming, suggesting that "primary" might be appropriate for the new upstream-tracking concept being implemented, but is confusing when applied to what `init.defaultBranch` specifies (which he argues should keep the "default" name). He notes a disconnect between Harald's stated use case and the current implementation, observing that the patch still seems tied to `init.defaultBranch` despite the shift in approach.

The response highlights ongoing tension in the thread about precisely defining the semantics of a "primary branch" reference. Junio's comments suggest the naming and conceptual model need further refinement before this feature can progress, with particular attention to distinguishing between local default branches and remote primary branches. The exchange indicates this remains an active design discussion rather than a patch ready for final review.
