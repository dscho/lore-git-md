This is an RFC patch series refining the behavior of `git show-index` when it falls back to SHA-1 outside repository contexts. The series builds on prior discussion about hash algorithm detection and warning behavior.

The first patch adds a warning when `show-index` falls back to SHA-1 due to lack of repository context, making the implicit assumption explicit. The second patch wraps all user-facing error messages in `show-index.c` with gettext for localization. 

The author has restructured the series in v2, splitting the original single patch into two distinct changes (warning behavior and i18n) as suggested by Junio C Hamano. The discussion thread shows consensus on the technical approach but ongoing debate about whether to add a `--quiet` flag to suppress the warning, with Patrick Steinhardt and the author preferring simpler solutions like requiring explicit hash specification or using configurable advice messages.

The changes are narrowly focused on polishing the UX of this edge case while maintaining the established technical direction. The series appears ready for merging pending resolution of the warning suppression question, with clean separation of concerns in the patch organization.
