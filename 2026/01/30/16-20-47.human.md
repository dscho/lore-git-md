This is Junio Hamano's review of the third patch in Olamide Bello's series to migrate configuration variables from globals to repository-specific storage as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The specific patch moves `branch.autoSetupMerge` into the new `struct repo_config_values`.

Junio initially suggested static initialization (like the original global variable) but now reconsiders, noting this approach won't scale to multiple repositories. He identifies a fundamental tension: while static initialization prevents errors for `the_repository`, future multi-repo operations will need explicit initialization via `repo_config_values_init()` - which introduces its own risks of being forgotten or called multiple times.

The review highlights two key architectural challenges that must be solved before this approach can be widely adopted: ensuring initialization happens exactly once per repository, and preventing use of uninitialized repository instances. These concerns go beyond the current patch's scope but are critical for the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

Junio's feedback maintains his consistent pattern in this thread - approving the technical implementation of individual migrations while surfacing deeper architectural questions that remain unresolved. The patch itself appears mechanically sound but the series remains blocked on these higher-level design questions.
