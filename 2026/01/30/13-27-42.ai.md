This is a refactoring patch that consolidates duplicate logic for iterating over preferred bitmap tips (configured via `pack.preferBitmapTips`) into a new `for_each_preferred_bitmap_tip()` function. The change removes the last external callers of `bitmap_preferred_tips()`, allowing it to be made static to pack-bitmap.c.

The patch unifies two nearly identical implementations - one in builtin/pack-objects.c and another in repack-midx.c - that were both subtly wrong in how they handled exact refnames. The new helper function will be used to fix this BUG condition in subsequent patches. The changes are mechanical and don't alter behavior, just structure.

Reviewers Karthik Nayak and Junio C Hamano have already approved the technical approach, with Junio specifically defending the use of small helper functions for readability. The patch is part of a larger series addressing ref iteration issues that affect both bitmap and bisect functionality, but this particular change focuses solely on the consolidation aspect.
