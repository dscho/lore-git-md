This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about modernizing `git show-index` with hash algorithm detection and i18n support. Junio C Hamano weighs in on the debate between Patrick Steinhardt and brian m. carlson regarding warning suppression in the command.

Junio expresses clear preference for Patrick's position that users who want to avoid the SHA-1 fallback warning should explicitly specify `--object-format=sha256` rather than adding a generic `--quiet` flag. He suggests that while a global `--quiet` might be useful for silencing all warnings, it's orthogonal to the current changes and doesn't need to block this series.

The exchange shows the series is technically complete (with both hash detection and i18n patches ready) and now just needs final resolution on this UX question. Junio's comment suggests the simpler approach (relying on `--object-format`) is likely to prevail, keeping the changes focused. This moves the thread closer to being ready for merging.
