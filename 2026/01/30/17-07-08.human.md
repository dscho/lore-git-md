This is the final approval for a two-patch series modernizing `git show-index`. Maintainer Junio C Hamano has queued the patches after they were split per his earlier guidance. 

The series adds two improvements to the low-level `show-index` command: first detecting hash algorithms from index size with fallback warnings, then adding gettext/i18n support for error messages. The implementation is clean and focused, touching only `builtin/show-index.c` while maintaining backward compatibility.

The only point of discussion was around warning suppression - Patrick Steinhardt and Junio favored requiring explicit `--object-format` specification, over brian m. carlson's preference for a generic `--quiet` flag. This has been resolved in favor of the more specific approach. 

With all technical questions settled and the maintainer's approval, this series is now complete and ready for merging. The changes are non-controversial and represent incremental improvements to a rarely-used plumbing command. While future SHA-256 interoperability work may eventually make some of this obsolete, these patches provide useful functionality in the meantime.
