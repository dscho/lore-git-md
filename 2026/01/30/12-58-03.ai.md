This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing issues with `refs_for_each_ref_in()` usage in bitmap and bisect functionality. The patch consolidates duplicate bitmap tip iteration logic from pack-objects and midx into a new `for_each_preferred_bitmap_tip()` function, removing the last external caller of `bitmap_preferred_tips()`.

The discussion focuses on implementation details of the consolidation. Taylor Blau questions whether `bitmap_preferred_tips()` should be inlined into its sole caller, but Patrick notes there's actually a second callsite (`bitmap_is_preferred_refname()`). They also discuss whether to forward-declare `each_ref_fn` to avoid including "refs.h", with Patrick suggesting it may not be worth the complexity.

The technical approach has already received approvals from Karthik Nayak and Junio Hamano, with Junio specifically defending the use of small static helper functions for readability. The remaining discussion points are minor implementation preferences rather than substantive objections to the refactoring itself. This patch is part of a larger effort to fix BUG() conditions when processing exact branch names in bitmap and bisect operations.
