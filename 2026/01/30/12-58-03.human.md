This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing a bug in bitmap and bisect functionality related to exact ref matching. The patch consolidates duplicate logic for iterating over preferred bitmap tips (configured via `pack.preferBitmapTips`) from two call sites into a new `for_each_preferred_bitmap_tip()` function.

The discussion reveals that while the initial review suggested inlining the helper function, Patrick points out there is still another caller (`bitmap_is_preferred_refname()`) that prevents full inlining. Taylor also notes the header inclusion of "refs.h" is necessary due to the `each_ref_fn` type, though they discuss potential workarounds.

The technical approach has already received approvals from Karthik Nayak and Junio Hamano, with Junio specifically defending the use of small helper functions for readability. The patch appears ready to proceed once the minor documentation clarifications (handled in later emails) are addressed. This is a clean refactoring that removes duplication while setting up for the bugfix in subsequent patches.
