This is a maintainer comment on the xdiff refactoring series, specifically addressing the placement of Rust/C interoperability code.

Junio C Hamano notes general approval of the series' direction but raises a concern about the location of the new `ivec` (interoperable vector) implementation files in `compat/`. He points out that while the `compat/` directory traditionally holds platform-specific POSIX compatibility shims, the new Rust/C interface layer represents a different kind of compatibility concern. 

The maintainer suggests that if more Rust-to-C interface files are expected (which seems likely given the Rustification effort), the project should consider establishing a more appropriate directory structure early on rather than overloading `compat/`. This is not a rejection of the technical approach but rather a suggestion about project organization that may affect future work in this area.

This comment comes from the maintainer after the full 10-patch series was submitted, indicating the series is under consideration for merging but may need minor adjustments to file placement. The observation doesn't impact the technical substance of the patches but could influence where related future work lands in the source tree.
