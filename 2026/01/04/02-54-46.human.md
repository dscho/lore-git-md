This is a bugfix patch addressing encoding handling in `.gitignore` files, with Junio C Hamano providing initial review feedback on the first submission.

Junio questions the patch's fundamental premise, pointing out that Git's exclude mechanism is designed to match the platform's native encoding (which may not be UTF-8) rather than assuming UTF-8 universally. He notes that some platforms have compatibility layers to convert paths to UTF-8, but this shouldn't be treated as a universal behavior. 

The maintainer also suggests terminology changes, recommending "exclude" over "ignores" in the title to match Git's internal vocabulary. This early feedback indicates the patch will need revision to properly account for platform-specific encoding behaviors rather than imposing a UTF-8 assumption.

The original patch from Matthieu Beauchamp-Boulay aimed to handle non-UTF-8 encoded exclude files (particularly UTF-16LE from Windows PowerShell) by adding BOM detection and conversion logic. Junio's response suggests this approach may need rethinking to properly align with Git's cross-platform encoding strategy.
