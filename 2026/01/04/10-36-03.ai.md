- Modifies prompt strings in `add-patch.c` to show previous decisions
- Affects all interactive patch modes (stage, stash, reset, checkout)
- Test case in `t3701-add-interactive.sh` needs updating for PAGER scenario
- The fix is trivial - just updating the test expectation to match the new prompt format
- Original motivation was to improve clarity when navigating between hunks with K/J keys
