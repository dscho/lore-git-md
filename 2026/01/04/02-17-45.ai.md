Junio C Hamano provides maintainer guidance on patch series organization in this email. He responds to Harald Nordgren's lighthearted comment about programming being a "drunken-man's walk" by emphasizing the importance of presenting a clean, logical progression in patch series submissions.

The maintainer makes two key points:
1. While development may involve iterative exploration, the final patch series should present a coherent narrative that clearly moves toward the stated goal without backtracking or inconsistency between patches.
2. This discipline serves future maintainers who will need to understand the intent behind changes when modifying or extending the code.

This email comes in the context of Nordgren's 14-iteration series adding push tracking information to `git status` output, where earlier versions had shown some organizational inconsistencies. Junio's feedback reflects Git's long-standing emphasis on maintainable history, where each patch should stand on its own and move the codebase clearly toward the desired end state.

The exchange maintains Git's characteristic technical focus while allowing for some humor (the bathrobe reference to Linus Torvalds), but the maintainer's core message remains serious: patch authors should present their work as a clean, logical progression even if the development process was more exploratory.
