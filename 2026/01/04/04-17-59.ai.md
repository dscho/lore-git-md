Junio C Hamano responds to Nico Williams' advocacy of a strictly linear rebase-based workflow (as used at Sun Microsystems) by clarifying that his own preference for clean history is not about avoiding merges entirely. He distinguishes between two types of linear histories: a coherent single-theme progression (good) versus a scattered "drunken-man's walk" of commits (bad). He further argues that using merges to combine multiple well-structured topic branches can be clearer than forcing everything into one linear sequence.

The exchange is philosophical rather than technical - no changes to Git's functionality are proposed. Junio's position aligns with Git's traditional workflow where topic branches are developed linearly but merged rather than rebased when integrating into main branches.
