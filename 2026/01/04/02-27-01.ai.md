This is a follow-up discussion in an RFC thread about adding warnings for skipped merge commits in `git format-patch`. The original patch proposed showing a warning when merge commits are silently omitted from patch output. 

Junio C Hamano responds to Dominique Martinet's agreement with the warning concept but notes the current implementation's generic message could be improved. He suggests two alternative approaches: (1) tracking skipped merges in a separate list to enable more detailed warnings if desired, or (2) simply counting merges to show "skipped %d merges" rather than a binary yes/no. 

The exchange indicates consensus on the basic idea of warning users when no patches are generated due to skipped merges, but explores ways to make the feedback more informative without being overly verbose. Junio's suggestions aim to preserve the simplicity of the initial approach while leaving room for more detailed reporting if needed. The thread shows the RFC is progressing toward a refined implementation, likely to appear in a future v2 patch.
