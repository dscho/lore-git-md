This is a follow-up in a bugfix thread addressing regressions in Git's hook subsystem. The discussion has reached consensus on merging two fixes into the upcoming 2.53 release, while deferring more complex architectural changes.

Adrian Ratiu agrees with Junio C Hamano's inclination to merge the two regression fixes (stdout/stderr separation in pre-push hooks and `ungroup` flag initialization) into Git 2.53. He presents two options: either merge these fixes or revert the entire 'ar/run-command-hook' topic that introduced the regressions. The fixes are prioritized because they address more severe user-facing issues compared to the remaining known problem (potential deadlocks in server-side hooks), which Adrian considers less likely to occur in practice.

Adrian notes he's already working on the deadlock issue separately, but these immediate fixes take precedence for the upcoming release. The thread shows the project's typical approach to regression management - quick fixes for user-impacting issues while deferring more complex architectural work for future iterations. The maintainer's approval and Adrian's agreement suggest these fixes will likely land in 2.53 without further discussion.
