Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series has reached completion with version 4 of the 14-patch set. The series introduces a unified `odb_for_each_object()` interface to replace separate functions for loose and packed object iteration, a key step in making Git's object storage layer more modular. The final patch removes the now-unused legacy functions after all callers were converted in earlier patches, demonstrating the abstraction's successful adoption across multiple subsystems including fsck, pack-objects, and reachability checks. The changes simplify the codebase by removing 97 lines while adding only 35, with most modifications concentrated in packfile.c.

The series has followed a careful incremental approach, with each patch building on the last while maintaining behavioral consistency. Reviewers Taylor Blau, Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak have consistently validated the architectural direction throughout the series. With all planned conversions complete and no remaining open questions, the work appears ready for merging into Git's main development branch. This represents a significant step forward in the ongoing effort to abstract Git's object database layer, paving the way for future pluggable storage backends.
