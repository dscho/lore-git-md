Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series continues to make steady progress, now at version 4 with 14 patches. The latest changes focus on exposing object modification times (mtimes) through the new ODB interfaces, an important capability for garbage collection. This builds on previous work that converted key subsystems like `cat-file` and commit-graph to use the new backend-agnostic APIs.

The mtime handling patch (11/14) carefully addresses the complexity of tracking modification times when objects may exist in multiple forms (loose or packed), with clear warnings about interface limitations. It maintains backward compatibility while extending the object info structure, following the series' pattern of incremental improvements. Earlier patches in the series have already been reviewed positively by Taylor Blau, Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak, with Junio Hamano noting only straightforward merge conflicts.

The series appears on track for merging, with the mtime handling being the last substantive technical discussion point before the final infrastructure patches. Patrick's approach of converting subsystems one by one while maintaining existing behavior has proven effective, as seen in the fsck, promisor, cat-file and commit-graph conversions already completed. Any remaining work on mtime specifics would likely come as follow-up patches rather than blocking the series' progression.
