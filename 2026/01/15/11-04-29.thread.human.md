Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series, which restructures Git's object storage layer to support pluggable backends, has reached its final version (v3) with all 14 patches now posted. The series systematically converts legacy object iteration functions to new unified APIs, culminating in patch 14/14 removing the now-obsolete `for_each_loose_object()` and `for_each_packed_object()` functions. The work has received thorough review from Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak throughout its progression.

Jeff King (Peff), one of Git's most experienced contributors, has weighed in with a focused technical review on patch 02/14, suggesting that flag parameters should remain as enum types rather than being changed to unsigned integers. His rationale centers on improved code readability and potential compiler benefits, noting this aligns with established patterns in the codebase. While this is a narrow implementation detail, Peff's feedback carries weight given his deep familiarity with Git codebase conventions. The series appears ready for Junio Hamano's review, with the technical work complete and all callers converted to the new APIs while maintaining functionality - a notable achievement that both simplifies the codebase and enables future extensibility.
