The patch appears to be part of the systematic conversion of Git's object storage layer to support pluggable backends. Justin's review indicates the technical approach looks correct, though he's seeking confirmation about the long-term direction where iteration handling would become backend-specific. This aligns with the series' overall goal of making object storage more modular.
