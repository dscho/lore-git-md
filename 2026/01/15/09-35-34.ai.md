This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series standardizing commit list function names to follow Git's `struct S_<verb>()` pattern. The patch renames `free_commit_list()` to `commit_list_free()`, completing the transition started in earlier patches that renamed `copy_commit_list()` and `reverse_commit_list()`. 

The change is mechanical but extensive, touching 40 files across merge, rebase, log, commit-graph, revision, and test code. A compatibility wrapper preserves the old name temporarily to avoid conflicts with in-flight patches, with removal planned after Git 2.53. The patch includes updates to Coccinelle rules and documentation to match the new naming.

This refactoring improves API consistency without functional changes, part of Patrick's ongoing work to modernize Git's internal interfaces. The series follows his typical pattern of incremental, well-documented changes with temporary compatibility layers. While wide-ranging, the changes are straightforward renames that reviewers can verify mechanically rather than needing deep semantic analysis.
