This is a cleanup patch in Taylor Blau's multi-pack-index compaction series, addressing Junio C Hamano's feedback about error handling. The patch removes two helper functions (`u64_add()` and `u64_mult()`) from git-compat-util.h that were previously used for overflow checking in MIDX writing code.

The patch demonstrates good attention to error reporting quality - the new messages clearly indicate which operation failed ("overflow during fixed-point multiply") and show the problematic values.
