His response keeps the discussion open-ended, suggesting the project is still considering how to balance simplicity with configurability.

- The push-tracking comparison is now stable in `format_branch_comparison()`
- Configuration may need to handle both branch selection and output verbosity
- Future extensions could add more comparison types (beyond upstream/push)
- The design should remain open to new axes of customization
