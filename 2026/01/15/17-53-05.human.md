Adrian Ratiu, who authored the fixes, maintains that targeted fixes are preferable and offers assistance if Junio proceeds with the revert.

The discussion highlights Git's conservative approach to stability during release cycles, with Junio weighing the tradeoffs between targeted fixes versus a more sweeping revert. The decision will impact the timing of follow-up work on related hook subsystem improvements. The community is awaiting Junio's experimental results from testing the revert before a final decision is made.
