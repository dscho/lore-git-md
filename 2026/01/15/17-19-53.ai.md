This is a follow-up in the hook subsystem bugfix thread, confirming the maintainer's decision to merge the fixes while acknowledging remaining work.

Adrian Ratiu agrees with Junio C Hamano's inclination to merge the two regression patches (stdout/stderr separation and ungroup flag initialization) into the upcoming Git 2.53 release. This maintains the separation of concerns established in the thread - quick fixes for clear regressions versus deeper redesigns requiring more consideration.
