This is a cleanup patch in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Coccinelle rules that are no longer needed. René Scharfe follows up on his 10-part series converting tree parsing functions to be repository-aware by removing obsolete semantic patch rules that have already been applied across the codebase.

The patch removes 117 lines of Coccinelle rules from `the_repository.cocci` that were used to automate conversions of functions like `get_oid`, `parse_commit`, and `init_revisions` to their repository-aware counterparts. These rules have served their purpose - the conversions are complete and no stragglers remain. The patch keeps only the newly added rules for tree.h functions (like `parse_tree` to `repo_parse_tree`) with a TODO comment noting they should be removed after the next Git release.

This is a straightforward maintenance change that tidies up the Coccinelle infrastructure now that earlier phases of the `the_repository` removal are complete. The patch has already been reviewed in the context of the larger series, with Patrick Steinhardt suggesting this cleanup and René implementing it cleanly. It represents the natural conclusion of one segment of this multi-year architectural effort.
