Performance optimization for sparse-checkout pattern handling, now at v3. This patch completes the conversion from O(n^2) to O(n log n) complexity when building sorted pattern lists in the sparse-checkout cone mode. The change was initially suggested by Jeff King (Peff) after identifying three locations in builtin/sparse-checkout.c that were using inefficient string_list_insert() when collecting patterns from a hashmap.

The v3 patch maintains the same core approach as v2: replacing string_list_insert() with string_list_append() followed by string_list_sort(), while removing redundant string_list_remove_duplicates() calls since the hashmap source already guarantees uniqueness. The changes affect pattern collection in sparse_checkout_list() and two locations in write_cone_to_file(). The diff remains clean and focused, with no behavioral changes beyond the performance improvement.
