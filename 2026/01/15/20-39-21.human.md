This is a technical response about delta islands and bitmaps interaction from Jeff King (Peff), addressing Martin Fick's question about configuring delta islands to treat each head as its own island.

Jeff clarifies that delta islands don't actually require reachability bitmaps to function, despite some confusing bitmap usage in the implementation. He explains that the islands mechanism uses its own internal bitmaps to track object groupings, separate from the on-disk reachability bitmaps Martin was concerned about.

The response provides a concrete configuration example showing how to set up per-head islands using a simple regex pattern. Jeff also offers practical advice about the tradeoffs of this approach - while it may help when fetching branches individually, it could significantly increase packfile sizes by preventing delta reuse across branches.

The email serves as both a technical clarification and a gentle warning about the performance implications of the requested configuration. Jeff's response demonstrates his characteristic combination of deep technical knowledge and practical, user-focused advice.
