Performance optimization for sparse-checkout pattern handling. This v3 patch from Amisha Chhajed completes the conversion from O(n^2) to O(n log n) complexity when building sorted pattern lists in the sparse-checkout cone mode.

The patch modifies three locations in builtin/sparse-checkout.c where patterns were previously inserted one by one into a sorted list (an O(n^2) operation). Now it appends all patterns first (O(n)) and sorts once at the end (O(n log n)). The redundant duplicate removal is also eliminated since the hashmap source already guarantees uniqueness.

This builds on Jeff King's earlier review that identified all three locations needing the same optimization. The changes are straightforward and maintain the same functionality while improving performance, particularly for repositories with many sparse-checkout patterns. The patch appears ready for inclusion with no outstanding technical concerns raised in the thread.
