This is a performance optimization patch for the sparse-checkout feature, specifically addressing pattern sorting in cone mode. The patch has gone through several iterations (now at v3) and appears ready for merging.

The change converts three locations in `builtin/sparse-checkout.c` from using `O(n^2)` insertion sort via `string_list_insert()` to a more efficient `O(n log n)` approach using `string_list_append()` followed by sorting. The optimization was identified by Jeff King (Peff) and implemented by Amisha Chhajed across all relevant code paths.
