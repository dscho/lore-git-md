This is the second patch in a refactoring series to standardize function names for commit list operations. Patrick Steinhardt renames `reverse_commit_list()` to `commit_list_reverse()` to match Git's naming convention where functions operating on a struct should be prefixed with the struct name. The change affects merge, stash, and sequencer operations, with a temporary compatibility wrapper added to ease transition until Git 2.53.

The patch follows the same pattern as the first in the series (which renamed `copy_commit_list`), touching 6 files across core commands. It's a straightforward mechanical change with no functional impact, just improving API consistency. The series as a whole shows careful attention to backward compatibility during the transition period.
