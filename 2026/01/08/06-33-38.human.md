Performance investigation into `git pack-refs` slowness on NFS with high ref counts. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Martin Fick's flame graph analysis of a Gerrit server where `pack-refs` takes 5+ minutes (vs JGit's 20s). The profiling data shows:

- 30% in SSE-optimized memcmp
- 30% in object header unpacking
- Significant time in page faults (42% total)
- Only 8.7% in the actual packed-refs creation

Patrick notes the high page fault overhead aligns with NFS mmap issues, though he's surprised by the severity. He suggests trying larger NFS rsize/wsize values (1MB) based on historical recommendations, though this remains a workaround rather than a solution. The thread continues to show tension between short-term NFS optimizations and Patrick's long-term advocacy for the reftable backend as the architectural fix. The profiling confirms the mmap/NFS interaction as the primary bottleneck but doesn't resolve the path forward - the discussion remains at an impasse between immediate mitigation and backend migration.
