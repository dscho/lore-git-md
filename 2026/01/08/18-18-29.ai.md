This is a documentation patch in a series improving the `git-patch-id` man page and related comments. The series has received positive review from Pushkar Singh, who confirms the changes improve clarity and accuracy without altering behavior.

The current patch (6/6) documents that the `--verbatim` option implicitly enables `--stable` mode in `git patch-id`, preventing use of `--unstable`. This behavior was introduced in commit 2871f4d4 (2014) but wasn't fully documented until now. The patch explains the design rationale - maintaining compatibility and reducing maintenance burden by not supporting unstable mode with verbatim output.

The author, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, has established a pattern of documentation improvements to `git-patch-id` through previous patch series. This final patch in the current series completes the work by clarifying option interactions that were previously only evident in the implementation. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with Git's documentation style guidelines.
