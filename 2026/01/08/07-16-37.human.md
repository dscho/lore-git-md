This is a minor refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to restructure Git's packfile storage for pluggable object database backends. The patch inlines a trivial wrapper function (`find_kept_pack_entry()`) into its only caller (`has_object_kept_pack()`), then makes the resulting function file-local by removing its declaration from the header. Kristoffer Haugsbakk points out a typo in the commit message ("has_oject_kept_pack" -> "has_object_kept_pack").

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, part of the broader effort to clean up packfile handling as the series moves packfile tracking to be managed by individual ODB sources. The patch has already been through one round of review (v1) and this version addresses a minor documentation nit from Toon Claes. The typo fix noted here is trivial and unlikely to require another revision.
