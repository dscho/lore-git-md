Kristoffer Haugsbakk has completed a documentation improvement series for `git-patch-id`, now in its second iteration and ready for merging. The six-part patch set standardizes terminology (particularly around "patch ID" capitalization), clarifies the interaction between `--verbatim` and `--stable` options, and improves overall documentation style. A notable technical clarification documents that `--verbatim` implies `--stable` behavior, a detail that dates back to 2014 but wasn't properly documented.

The series received positive reviews from Pushkar Singh and Patrick Steinhardt, with all feedback from the initial version addressed in v2 - including a minor markup fix Patrick noted. With no outstanding issues and clear consensus that the improvements meet Git's documentation standards, this uncontroversial series is positioned for merging in the next integration window. The work continues Haugsbakk's previous efforts to polish `git-patch-id` documentation as part of the broader project-wide documentation improvement initiative.
