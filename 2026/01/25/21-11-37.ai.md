This is the final patch in Jean-Noël Avila's documentation standardization series, converting `git-show` and `pretty-formats` man pages to the new AsciiDoc synopsis style. The patch updates asciidoc.conf.in to properly handle synopsis blocks while standardizing option formatting and placeholder syntax (`_<hash>__`).

Kristoffer Haugsbakk's review confirms the technical successes - proper backtick usage, consistent markup, and improved option descriptions - while identifying some HTML rendering quirks with `[synopsis]` blocks. The discussion reveals subtle formatting challenges around escaped quotes in format strings and cross-references between included documents. Avila explains the tradeoffs between different verbatim formatting approaches (`++` vs backticks) that work across AsciiDoc implementations.

The patch maintains the series' high quality, with Avila noting he will reroll to fix the identified issues: mixed synopsis/verbatim formatting in describe options and inconsistent rendering of placeholder italics. The changes remain strictly mechanical documentation improvements, continuing the project's systematic man page standardization effort with no behavioral impact.
