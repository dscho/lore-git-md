This thread addresses Patrick Steinhardt's work to fix geometric repacking when working with promisor remotes, part of his larger repack machinery overhaul. The core issue was that geometric repacking would either fail or corrupt promisor packs (used in partial clones) due to conflicting pack-objects flags and lack of special handling for these packs.

The series consists of five patches, with the first four establishing foundational changes: allowing --stdin-packs to work with --exclude-promisor-objects, refactoring geometric repack calculations, and improving promisor object repacking logic. The final patch, just submitted, completes the solution by implementing separate geometric progressions for promisor and non-promisor packs. This ensures promisor packs are properly repacked while maintaining their special properties, with comprehensive test coverage added in t7703.

The changes touch several key areas: pack-objects.c, repack-geometry.c, and introduce new helpers in repack-promisor.c. The implementation maintains all existing functionality while adding proper promisor pack support, marking an important step in making geometric repacking work safely in partial clone scenarios. This work builds on Patrick's prior geometric repack series and is part of the ongoing modernization of Git's repack subsystem.
