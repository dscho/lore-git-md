This is a patch series addressing an incompatibility between geometric repacking and promisor remotes, part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing work on the repack machinery. The series consists of 5 patches that:

1. Fixes a mutual exclusion between `--stdin-packs` and `--exclude-promisor-objects` in `git pack-objects`
2. Refactors geometric repacking logic to better handle promisor packs
3. Adds tests to verify the new behavior

The core issue is that geometric repacks currently fail when dealing with promisor packs (partial clone objects) because of conflicting pack-objects flags and lack of proper promisor pack handling. The series makes these features work together by:

- Modifying pack-objects to accept both flags simultaneously
- Extracting and refactoring repack geometry calculations
- Adding proper promisor pack tracking during geometric repack
- Including new test cases in t5331 and t7703

The changes touch core repacking components like pack-objects.c and introduce new helper functions in repack-geometry.c and repack-promisor.c. Given Patrick's track record with repack-related work and the thorough test coverage added, this appears to be a well-considered fix for a real limitation in the geometric repack feature.
