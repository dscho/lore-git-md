Junio Hamano weighs in on the lazy vs eager config parsing discussion in this `the_repository` removal thread. He acknowledges Phillip Wood's point about the changed failure timing for invalid `core.attributesFile` values but suggests the new behavior might actually be preferable - failing only when the setting is used rather than at startup. Junio uses `git cat-file` as an example where immediate failure would be unnecessarily strict if attributes aren't needed.

However, Junio agrees with Phillip's broader concern about the pattern of behavior changes when moving settings to `repo_settings`. He cautions against overusing this mechanism, distinguishing between the valuable goal of removing globals and the separate issue of changing parsing semantics. The maintainer suggests a more nuanced approach where commands could declare which settings they actually need, allowing irrelevant invalid configurations to be safely ignored.

This email doesn't propose concrete solutions but signals Junio's perspective that the current direction needs refinement. His comments carry significant weight as the project's long-time maintainer, indicating this architectural question merits deeper consideration before proceeding with similar migrations. The exchange highlights how technical debt removal can sometimes introduce subtle tradeoffs between correctness and usability.
