This is the fourth patch in a v3 series improving error handling and test coverage for `git replay` when given invalid commit-ish inputs. The series has been through multiple rounds of review, with feedback from Junio Hamano, Phillip Wood, and Elijah Newren incorporated.

These changes are small but meaningful - they clarify the technical direction while making the user-facing error message slightly more precise. The patch follows the series' pattern of incremental improvements to error handling and documentation.
