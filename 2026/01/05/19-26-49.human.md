This is a follow-up in the `git whatchanged` deprecation discussion thread. Wayne Rowcliffe confirms that `git log --raw` serves as an adequate replacement for his daily use of `git whatchanged` to examine changed files in commits. This comes after Kristoffer Haugsbakk's earlier explanation that `git whatchanged` is being deprecated in favor of `git log` with specific flags, as they share the same underlying machinery but with different defaults.

The exchange shows a successful resolution to the deprecation notice - the user has found a suitable alternative that meets their needs. The thread demonstrates the value of the deprecation warning mechanism, which prompted user feedback and resulted in clear migration guidance. No further action appears needed unless other users report unmet needs with the suggested alternatives.
