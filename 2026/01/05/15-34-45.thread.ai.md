Proposal to add Bazel build support to Git, similar to existing Meson integration. Author Son Luong Ngoc has implemented a working but invasive Bazel configuration in their fork, adding BUILD files throughout the repository. Current implementation supports Linux and runs most unit/integration tests. Author seeks co-maintainers before upstream consideration, offering to maintain for major releases but not all topics. Two paths proposed: 1) in-tree maintenance with co-maintainers, or 2) publishing to Bazel Central Registry as post-checkout overlay. This follows Git's build system modernization (Meson addition) but requires evaluating maintenance burden versus benefit. Key open questions: community interest level, maintenance capacity, and whether to prioritize platform support before considering upstream. Implementation exists but needs polish and broader platform coverage.
