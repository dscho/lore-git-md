This is the final patch in a v4 documentation series improving the `git reset` man page, building on Julia Evans' pedagogical approach and Junio Hamano's technical refinements. The series was already merged in v3; this iteration adds minor clarifications based on user feedback.

The patch focuses on the path-specific form of `git reset`, making several readability improvements: replacing jargon like "tree-ish" and "hunks" with clearer terms, emphasizing that this mode only affects the index (not files or HEAD), and simplifying the relationship with `git restore`. The changes maintain all technical accuracy while making the behavior more intuitive for readers.
