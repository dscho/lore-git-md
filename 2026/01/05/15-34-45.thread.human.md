This thread discusses a proposal to add Bazel build support to Git, following the project's adoption of Meson as an alternative to the traditional Makefile system. Son Luong Ngoc has implemented working Bazel support in their fork, with current functionality limited to Linux, and is seeking co-maintainers before pushing for upstream inclusion. The proposal outlines two potential paths: maintaining the support in-tree with additional maintainers, or publishing it separately via the Bazel Central Registry.

brian m. carlson has raised significant technical objections, particularly around the current implementation's lack of configurability for critical paths (like shell and interpreter locations) and platform-specific assumptions. They argue this represents a regression from Git's existing build system capabilities. While not opposed to Bazel in principle, they emphasize that any implementation would need to match Git's current flexibility before being considered for inclusion. The discussion now hinges on whether the proposer can address these concerns or whether the Bazel support might need to remain an external offering.
