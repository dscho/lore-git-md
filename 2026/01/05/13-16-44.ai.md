This is part 4/5 of Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing geometric repacking with promisor remotes, continuing his ongoing repack machinery overhaul. The patch refactors pack removal logic in preparation for handling promisor packs.

The change extracts the core pack removal functionality from `pack_geometry_remove_redundant()` into a new static helper `remove_redundant_packs()`. The key difference is that the new helper takes a direct array of packs and count rather than working through the geometry struct, making it more flexible for reuse. The original function becomes a thin wrapper that just extracts the geometry's pack array and split count.

This is a mechanical refactoring with no behavior change - the same pack removal logic is preserved, just reorganized. The commit message indicates this prepares for a future caller that will need to remove redundant packs after geometric repacking, likely related to promisor pack handling in upcoming patches. The change follows Patrick's typical style of careful, incremental refactoring with clear separation of concerns.
