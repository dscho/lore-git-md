This is a bugfix for the `git status` branch comparison logic, addressing a regression introduced in the push-tracking feature series. Harald Nordgren points out that Junio C Hamano's suggested simplification of the condition from `!sti` to `!ours && !theirs` actually breaks several existing tests. The change reveals that these conditions are not equivalent as previously assumed - the original `sti` check was doing more than just comparing the `ours` and `theirs` counts.

The patch shows the minimal change needed to restore correct behavior by reverting to the original condition. This comes after extensive discussion and testing of the push-tracking feature, where Junio had initially approved the simplification but Harald's testing uncovered the discrepancy. The fix ensures `git status` continues to accurately report branch synchronization states in all cases, maintaining compatibility with existing tests that verify this behavior.
