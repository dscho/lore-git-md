This is the fifth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing geometric repacking with promisor remotes, part of his ongoing repack machinery overhaul. The patch addresses how geometric repacking handles promisor packs (packs containing objects that may have missing dependencies, used in partial clones).

The core problem is that current geometric repacking would corrupt promisor packs by merging them with regular packs without proper marking. The patch implements a solution where promisor packs are handled via a separate geometric progression, ensuring they get repacked while maintaining their promisor status and not expanding the set of promisor objects.

Key changes include:
- Adding promisor pack tracking to the pack geometry structure
- Sorting and splitting promisor packs separately from regular packs
- New helper function `pack_geometry_repack_promisors()` to handle the repacking
- Comprehensive test cases verifying the behavior

The implementation is thorough and builds on the foundation laid by the previous patches in the series. The test coverage is extensive, verifying both the pack merging behavior and the preservation of promisor status. This completes the series' goal of making geometric repacking work correctly in partial clone scenarios.
