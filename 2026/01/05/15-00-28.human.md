This is a follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal thread about moving "core.attributesFile" configuration to repository settings. Phillip Wood reiterates his concern about a regression caused by changing the config parsing from eager (at startup) to lazy (on first use), which can lead to confusing failures when invalid paths are provided. 

He references a previous discussion about potential solutions to this general issue that arises when moving settings into `struct repo_settings`. The core problem is that lazy parsing means errors surface later in operations like rebase rather than failing immediately at startup, creating a poor user experience. 

The exchange shows this is a known challenge in the broader refactoring effort, with Phillip suggesting the team needs a systematic solution before proceeding with similar changes. The technical discussion remains focused and constructive, with experienced contributors like Phillip providing valuable feedback on the real-world implications of architectural changes.
