This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing geometric repacking with promisor remotes, part of his ongoing repack machinery overhaul. The patch addresses how geometric repacking handles promisor packs (packs containing objects that may have missing dependencies, used in partial clones).

The core problem is that current geometric repacking would either corrupt promisor packs by merging them without proper marking, or fail entirely due to flag conflicts. The patch implements a solution: maintaining separate geometric progressions for promisor and non-promisor packs. This ensures promisor packs get repacked while preserving their special properties.

Key technical changes:
- Extends `struct pack_geometry` to track promisor packs separately
- Adds `pack_geometry_repack_promisors()` to handle promisor pack repacking
- Modifies the geometric splitting logic to process promisor packs independently
- Includes comprehensive tests in t7703 verifying the behavior

The implementation maintains all existing functionality while adding proper promisor pack support. Test coverage shows the system correctly merges promisor packs while keeping them distinct from regular packs and preserving all objects. This completes the series' goal of making geometric repacking work safely in partial clone scenarios.
