Derrick Stolee's `--maximal-only` feature for `git rev-list` has reached consensus in its second iteration, with the implementation and naming (`--maximal-only` instead of the original `--maximal`) settled. The option helps select "frontier" commits for bundle URIs and bitmap optimizations by filtering commits not reachable from others in the range. Junio Hamano guided the naming and design decisions, and Johannes Sixt's interface concerns were addressed in v2.

While the technical implementation is complete with comprehensive tests and documentation of its incompatibility with `--boundary`, Johannes Sixt has raised a follow-up question about the mathematical framing. He challenges the characterization that `--maximal-only` operates on the union of histories while `--independent` works on the intersection, suggesting the key difference actually lies in how negative revisions are handled. This doesn't affect the implementation but may prompt minor documentation refinements to clarify the conceptual model for users. The thread remains resolved, but this exchange highlights the kind of subtle distinction that can surface even after consensus is reached.
