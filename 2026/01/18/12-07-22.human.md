Szeder Gabor points out that while Karthik Nayak's patch correctly identifies two unused headers (`sigchain.h` and `run-command.h`), their removal depends on changes from Adrian Ratiu's hook API refactoring that was recently reverted. The suggestion is to defer this cleanup until after the hook API changes are properly stabilized and re-merged. This is a minor housekeeping change that would be correct in isolation, but needs to wait for its dependencies to settle first. The patch itself is straightforward and uncontroversial - it's just the timing that's problematic.
