This is a follow-up to Tian Yuchen's RFC patch that optimized the p3400 rebase test setup by using `git fast-import` instead of shell loops. The original change showed a 38% speedup in test setup time (from ~29s to ~18s) while maintaining the same commit history structure.

In this email, Yuchen reports an unexpected regression in the actual rebase test performance - the rebase operation itself slowed down from ~2s to ~5s after the setup optimization. They're seeking help understanding why this might be happening and whether their `fast-import` approach is technically sound.

The thread context shows the original patch was well-received as an RFC with clear performance benefits for setup. This follow-up indicates there may be some subtle interaction between how commits are created via `fast-import` and how `rebase` processes them, though the regression could also be due to other factors. The author demonstrates good practice by transparently reporting unexpected results and asking for expert input.
