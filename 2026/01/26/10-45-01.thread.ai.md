git-gui bugfix for diff viewer tab alignment. Patch from Chris Idema via GitGitGadget addresses inconsistent tab rendering in git-gui's diff display by introducing new `expand_tabs` procedure in `git-gui/lib/diff.tcl`, converting tabs to spaces using configured `gui.tabsize` while maintaining existing functionality. Johannes Sixt (gitk/Git GUI maintainer) responds that he cannot reproduce the reported tab width issue in Tcl/Tk 8.6 and questions whether the space conversion would preserve the line/hunk staging functionality. The original patch (25+1- lines) remains technically sound but now requires clarification on: 1) environment-specific reproduction of the tab display issue, and 2) verification that the staging mechanism works correctly with space-converted diffs. Thread open pending Chris's response to these maintainer concerns. Does not affect gitk.
