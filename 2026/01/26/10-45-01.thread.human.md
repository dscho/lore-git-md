This thread discusses a bugfix for git-gui's diff viewer tab alignment, where tabs weren't properly respecting the configured width. Chris Idema submitted a patch that would convert tabs to spaces using the configured `gui.tabsize`, maintaining existing functionality while fixing the display issue. The patch was focused and technically sound, addressing only git-gui (not gitk).

Johannes Sixt (Hannes), the maintainer of both gitk and Git GUI, has responded that he can't reproduce the issue in his Tcl/Tk 8.6 environment. He's asking whether the proposed space conversion might affect git-gui's ability to stage individual lines or hunks - a key functionality that must be preserved. The thread is now waiting for Chris to clarify whether the issue is environment-specific and confirm that the staging mechanism won't be impacted by the change. This shows the careful consideration given even to small UI fixes when they touch core functionality.
