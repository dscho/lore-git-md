This thread addresses a bug in git-gui where tab characters in the diff viewer weren't rendering correctly, regardless of the configured tab width. Chris Idema submitted a focused fix that introduces a new `expand_tabs` procedure in the diff display logic, converting tabs to spaces while respecting the `gui.tabsize` setting. The change is cleanly implemented in `git-gui/lib/diff.tcl`, handling edge cases like CR line endings without disrupting other functionality.

The patch is small (25 lines added, 1 removed) and specifically targets the git-gui diff viewer, explicitly noting that similar tab alignment issues in gitk remain unaddressed. The solution appears technically sound and improves the user experience without introducing complexity. This looks like a straightforward improvement that should cleanly resolve the reported issue.
