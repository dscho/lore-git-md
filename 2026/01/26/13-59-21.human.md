This is a bugfix for git-gui's diff viewer on Windows, addressing tab alignment issues. Johannes Sixt (the Git GUI maintainer) is engaging with contributor Chris Idema about a patch that converts tabs to spaces in the diff display to ensure consistent rendering. While Chris has provided reproduction steps showing the tab misalignment occurs specifically in Git for Windows with Tcl/Tk 8.6, Johannes raises a critical concern: the proposed space conversion might break the "Stage Line/Hunk" functionality that extracts text from the diff widget to create patches. 

The thread reveals two unresolved questions: whether the tab display issue is indeed Windows-specific (partially confirmed by Chris's reproduction case), and more importantly, whether the space conversion preserves the staging mechanism (which Chris hasn't yet tested). The patch appears technically sound for its stated purpose but remains incomplete until the staging interaction is verified, as this is a maintainer requirement for approval. The discussion is specific to git-gui and doesn't affect gitk.
