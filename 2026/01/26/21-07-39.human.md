This is a follow-up discussion in the ODB abstraction thread, confirming a bugfix in `builtin/backfill.c`. The original patch from Patrick Steinhardt identified and fixed incorrect flag usage where `OBJECT_INFO_FOR_PREFETCH` flags were being passed to `odb_has_object()` (which expects `HAS_OBJECT_*` flags). While the code happened to work due to matching bit patterns, this was clearly dangerous.

Derrick Stolee has reviewed the fix and agrees it's the correct approach - removing the flags entirely since they weren't needed for the intended behavior. Junio Hamano chimes in to acknowledge both the original fix and Stolee's confirmation. The exchange shows consensus on this specific fix while the broader thread continues exploring enum type safety considerations for future ODB work.
