Refactoring Git's xdiff (diff engine) subsystem to reduce memory usage, building on prior cleanup work in 'en/xdiff-cleanup-2'. Phillip Wood's series now includes two focused optimizations: (1) removing the `line_hash` field from `xrecord_t` (used only during initial line classification), reducing its size by 25-40%, and (2) restructuring `xdlclass_t` to store only the line pointer and size needed for interning, eliminating unnecessary data copying from `xrecord_t`. This second change effectively reverts part of commit 6d507bd41a while maintaining functionality.

The changes are mechanical and localized to `xdiff/xprepare.c` and `xtypes.h`, focusing on memory efficiency during diff operations by optimizing storage in frequently allocated structures. Phillip's approach differs from Elijah Newren's competing 'en/xdiff-cleanup-3' series by being more conservative - keeping the same basic flow while optimizing storage rather than restructuring classification timing. Both series remain in discussion due to their overlap, requiring coordination before merging. The work builds on prior xdiff changes including the hash field split in commit 6a26019c81. As a contributor with deep diff/merge expertise, Phillip's proposals represent well-considered cleanups to this performance-critical subsystem.
