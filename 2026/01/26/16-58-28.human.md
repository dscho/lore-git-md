Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's completed series that cleaned up `OBJECT_INFO_*` flag definitions in the ODB abstraction effort. While acknowledging the work as "well spotted and nicely done," Junio suggests an alternative approach that could have more clearly demonstrated the benefits of converting the flags to an enum type. He notes that even after the series, functions like `odb_read_object_info_extended()` still take an `unsigned flags` parameter rather than the new enum type, missing an opportunity to catch type mismatches at compile time.

This is a maintainer-level observation about patch organization rather than a request for changes - the series has already been merged. Junio's comment reflects his long experience with how to structure changes to maximize their demonstrative value and catch potential issues. The thread remains concluded, with this email serving as a retrospective note that may inform future similar refactorings.
