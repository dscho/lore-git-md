This thread documents the completion and follow-up discussion of Patrick Steinhardt's series to clean up `OBJECT_INFO_*` flag definitions as part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The three-patch series, now merged by Junio Hamano, standardized flag usage across several key files by converting them to a proper `enum object_info_flags`. The changes fixed a subtle but dangerous mismatch in `fill_missing_blobs()` where incorrect flags were being passed, which Derrick Stolee validated could have caused problems despite working coincidentally due to bit patterns.

After merging, the conversation shifted to deeper technical considerations around enum type safety, with Junio, René Scharfe, and Patrick exploring compiler capabilities to detect incorrect enum usage. René's investigations with GCC's `-Wenum-conversion` found it catches some cases but fails on integer-to-enum conversions - a limitation Patrick confirmed in his follow-up. He presented two potential paths forward: a more rigorous but less ergonomic struct-with-bitfields approach that would provide full compiler validation, or accepting the current partial protection. Patrick has left the decision to Junio on whether to pursue the more type-safe option, offering to rework the patches if needed.

The thread showcases the project's careful balance between technical correctness and practical maintainability in API design, particularly for the foundational ODB layer. While the immediate flag cleanup is complete, these discussions will inform future refactoring decisions around type safety and compiler-enforced validation in the codebase.
