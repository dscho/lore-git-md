Patrick Steinhardt has sent a patch series addressing inconsistencies in how object info flags are used within Git's object database (ODB) subsystem. The first patch fixes `builtin/backfill.c` which was incorrectly passing prefetch-related flags to `odb_has_object()` - a function that expects different flag types. While this worked by coincidence due to matching bit values, it was technically wrong and could break in the future.

The series includes two more patches (not yet received) that will: fix similar flag misuse in `fsck` and reorganize the flag definitions themselves to eliminate gaps in the numbering. These changes are part of Patrick's broader ODB abstraction work and represent internal code hygiene improvements rather than bug fixes or feature additions. The changes are well-contained (affecting just three files) and carefully explained, with no impact on user-visible behavior. Given Patrick's deep expertise in this area and the straightforward nature of the changes, the series is likely to be accepted without significant discussion.
