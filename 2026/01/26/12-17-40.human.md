This is a refactoring patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that cleans up some technical debt around the `OBJECT_INFO_*` flag definitions in Git's object database (ODB) interface.

The series addresses three issues:
1. Fixes incorrect flag usage in `builtin/backfill.c` where `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` was being misused
2. Corrects similar flag misuse in `builtin/fsck.c`
3. Reorganizes the `OBJECT_INFO_*` flags to eliminate historical gaps in their numbering and converts them to use an enum

The motivation comes from Patrick noticing puzzling gaps in the flag values and investigating their history. While fixing this, he discovered the flag misuses in backfill and fsck commands. The changes are mostly internal cleanup - the commit messages note there's no actual bug being fixed here, just potential future issues being prevented.

This appears to be a straightforward cleanup from one of Git's most active contributors. The changes are focused and well-explained, touching only three files. The series is part of Patrick's ongoing work on the ODB abstraction effort, though this particular set of changes is more about code hygiene than major architectural work.
