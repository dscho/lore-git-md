This is a bugfix in the ODB abstraction effort, specifically addressing incorrect flag usage in `builtin/backfill.c`. Derrick Stolee is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that corrects how `fill_missing_blobs()` passes flags to `odb_has_object()`.

The issue stems from passing `OBJECT_INFO_FOR_PREFETCH` flags where `HAS_OBJECT_*` flags were expected. While the current behavior happens to work by accident (due to the specific bit patterns of the flags), Patrick's patch removes this fragile dependency by eliminating the flag passing entirely. Derrick concurs with this approach, calling it "the right fix" in his brief reply.
