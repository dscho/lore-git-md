This is Junio C Hamano's response to Patrick Steinhardt's completed patch series cleaning up `OBJECT_INFO_*` flag definitions in the ODB abstraction effort. While acknowledging the technical correctness of the changes, Junio suggests an alternative approach that could have more clearly demonstrated the benefits of converting the flags to an enum type. He notes that even after the series, `odb_read_object_info_extended()` still takes an unsigned flags parameter rather than the new enum type, and proposes that restructuring the series to first convert the type (which would enable compiler warnings) before fixing the bugs would have better illustrated the value of the enum conversion.

Junio also points out that a similar opportunity exists with `odb_has_object()` and its `HAS_OBJECT_*` flags, which were previously converted to an enum but still use an unsigned parameter. His feedback is constructive but not blocking - he explicitly thanks Patrick for the work and calls it "well spotted and nicely done." This exchange is typical of Junio's review style: identifying potential improvements while recognizing the value of what's already been accomplished. The thread appears to be concluding with this response, as the patches have already been merged.
