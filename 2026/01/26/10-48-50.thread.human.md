This thread discusses a refactoring effort in Git's xdiff (diff engine) subsystem, proposed by Phillip Wood. The two-patch series aims to reduce memory usage by removing unused data structures in the diff calculation internals - specifically eliminating the `line_hash` field from `xrecord_t` and cleaning up `xdlclass_t`. These are purely internal optimizations that won't affect diff output.

Phillip notes these changes conflict with another active xdiff cleanup series ('en/xdiff-cleanup-3') that takes a different approach to line classification. He suggests an alternative way to handle the hashes if that competing approach is kept. As an experienced contributor to Git's diff/merge machinery, Phillip's proposals are likely well-considered, but the conflict with another series may require coordination before either can be merged. The changes are small and focused, touching only xdiff preparation code.
