This thread focuses on optimizing Git's xdiff (diff engine) subsystem to reduce memory usage, building on prior cleanup work. Phillip Wood has proposed a series with two key changes: removing the `line_hash` field from `xrecord_t` (cutting its size by 25-40%) and restructuring `xdlclass_t` to eliminate unnecessary data copying. These targeted optimizations are more conservative than Elijah Newren's competing 'en/xdiff-cleanup-3' series, which takes a more aggressive approach to restructuring classification timing.

Junio C Hamano, as maintainer, has weighed in to tentatively favor Phillip's approach, removing Elijah's series from the integration branch to let these memory optimizations prove themselves first. The changes are mechanical but carefully considered, touching only `xdiff/xprepare.c` and `xtypes.h`, and build on prior work like the hash field split in commit 6a26019c81. Phillip's deep expertise in Git's diff/merge machinery lends credibility to these changes, though the discussion remains open on whether to revisit the classification timing optimizations later. The thread exemplifies Git's cautious approach to performance-critical subsystems, where even mechanical improvements undergo thorough review.
