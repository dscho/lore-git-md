This is a documentation patch series continuing Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing effort to standardize Git man pages to AsciiDoc synopsis style. The v2 series converts `git-submodule` and `git-show` documentation while fixing issues identified in previous conversions of `git-clone` and `for-each-ref-options`.

The four patches systematically reformat documentation files (git-submodule.adoc, git-clone.adoc, pretty-formats.adoc, etc.) to use consistent AsciiDoc markup without changing any command behavior. Kristoffer Haugsbakk's review of v1 identified specific formatting issues that have now been addressed, particularly around verbatim formatting of placeholders and options in the `git-show` and pretty-formats documentation.

The changes reveal subtle technical challenges in cross-toolchain compatibility - the patches carefully balance between asciidoc.py and asciidoctor rendering requirements, especially for escaped quotes in format strings. The diff shows precise adjustments to synopsis blocks, verbatim spans, and option formatting to maintain consistency across documentation while accommodating different AsciiDoc processor behaviors.

This series represents another incremental step in the multi-year documentation standardization effort, with Avila demonstrating responsiveness to reviewer feedback. The changes are mechanical but require attention to detail to ensure the formatted output renders correctly across toolchains.
