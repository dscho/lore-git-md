This thread covers a routine localization (l10n) update for Git 2.53.0, coordinated by Jiang Xin, the maintainer of the Chinese translations. The update includes contributions from translators working on Bulgarian, French, Irish, Indonesian, Swedish, Turkish, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese versions of Git's documentation and messages.

The changes are substantial in volume (over 7,000 lines modified) but follow the established workflow where individual translators submit updates to the git-po repository before they're consolidated into the main Git tree. Notable improvements include consistency fixes for Chinese glossary terms and addressing git-po-helper warnings. As with most l10n updates, this appears to be a straightforward maintenance effort to keep translations current ahead of the 2.53.0 release.
