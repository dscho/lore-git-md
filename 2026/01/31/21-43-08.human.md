This is the fourth patch in a feature series enhancing `git submodule foreach` with new traversal options. The patch introduces a `--reversive` shorthand flag that combines three behaviors: recursive traversal, reverse (post-order) processing of submodules, and including the superproject at the end.

The new flag provides a convenient way to perform full post-order traversal from deepest submodules up to the superproject, which is particularly valuable for dependency-aware operations like builds or cleanups. The implementation adds the flag to both the C helper and shell wrapper, and updates the test cases from "expect_failure" to "expect_success" now that the functionality is implemented.

This continues the series that has already added `--reverse-traversal` and `--append-superproject` flags in previous patches. The changes are straightforward - the new flag simply sets all three underlying options when enabled. The patch maintains consistency with the series' goal of supporting dependency-ordered submodule operations while keeping backward compatibility.
