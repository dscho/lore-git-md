Shreyansh Paliwal's series to remove `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` globals from `wt-status.c` has reached a turning point. While Junio acknowledges the current series' approach works, he suggests two alternatives: either fix the underlying worktree API first (by ensuring worktree objects always exist, even for primary worktrees), or proceed with the current changes while leaving the worktree-related parts for later. This shifts the discussion from implementation details to a more fundamental question about whether now is the right time to address this long-standing design issue.

The non-worktree portions of the series (covering 24 `the_repository` and 3 `the_hash_algo` removals) remain ready for merging. The decision now rests with Shreyansh on whether to expand the scope of the series to tackle the worktree API redesign, or to proceed with the narrower solution while explicitly noting the technical debt. Junio's comments suggest that while the immediate changes are acceptable, the project would benefit from addressing the root cause rather than working around it.
