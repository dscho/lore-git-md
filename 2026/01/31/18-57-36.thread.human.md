Shreyansh Paliwal's series to remove `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` globals from `wt-status.c` has now been completed with the third and final patch. The series demonstrates Git's incremental approach to eliminating global state, with each part building on the last: part 1 handled initial mechanical conversions, part 2 updated core status reporting paths, and this final part removes the last remaining uses of `the_hash_algo` by converting null OID initialization and empty tree lookups to use repository-local hash algorithm instances.

With all three parts now addressed, `wt-status.c` no longer depends on either global variable - a significant milestone in the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The changes follow a clear, mechanical pattern that could serve as a template for similar conversions elsewhere in the codebase. While some architectural questions around worktree marker conventions remain open for future discussion, this series successfully completed its focused goal of making the status subsystem fully repository-aware. The thoroughness of the conversion and multiple iterations suggest the approach is stable and ready for broader application to other files.
