This is a bugfix patch addressing user confusion around `git format-patch` silently skipping merge commits. The patch follows up on earlier discussion where Junio Hamano suggested only showing a warning when no patches are generated at all (to avoid noise for expected cases like back-merges), while Jeff King caught a potential NULL pointer dereference in the original implementation.

The updated version adds a warning when `format-patch` produces no output due to encountering merge commits, with a simple generic message rather than listing each skipped commit. The warning helps users understand why their range selection might have yielded no patches, while avoiding the annoyance of warnings in common workflows where merges are intentionally skipped.

The patch touches `builtin/log.c`, modifying the commit-walking logic to track seen merges and conditionally emit the warning. It's a focused change that addresses a specific pain point without introducing new complexity.
