This is the final version (v14) of a feature enhancement series that adds push tracking branch comparison to `git status` output. The series has evolved through extensive review, with all technical concerns addressed, and is now functionally complete pending final verification of the refspec mapping implementation.

The key changes in this version are:
1. Proper handling of custom refspec transformations through a full resolution chain that maps local branches through push refspecs to determine remote destinations, then back through fetch refspecs to find tracking branches
2. Added test cases verifying behavior with remapped push refspecs
3. Various code cleanups and memory management fixes

The implementation now shows both upstream and push tracking comparisons when they differ, with output like:
```
On branch feature
Your branch is ahead of 'upstream/main' by 1 commit.
Your branch is ahead of 'origin/feature' by 1 commit.
  (use "git push" to publish your local commits)
```

The series has received thorough review from Phillip Wood (who identified the initial refspec mapping gap and suggested the solution) and approval from Junio C Hamano on the technical direction. With 210 lines of new test coverage and all edge cases addressed, this appears ready for inclusion pending Phillip Wood's final sign-off on the refspec implementation.
