This is the final sign-off for Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` series, now ready for merging after extensive review. The v11 iteration of this 8-patch series introduces a new `git-history` command focused on non-conflict history editing operations, with `reword` as its first subcommand. The implementation builds on Git's replay machinery while drawing inspiration from Jujutsu's history editing capabilities.

Elijah Newren, the merge machinery expert, has given his final technical sign-off after reviewing all changes in v11. The series now includes standardized conflict reporting (using `1` instead of `-1`), detached HEAD support via `RESOLVE_REF_NO_RECURSE` handling, and removal of redundant fields like `merge_conflict`. The core replay logic has been moved to libgit.a with a clean interface, and the implementation includes 391 lines of tests for the `reword` subcommand.

With all outstanding feedback addressed and no remaining technical objections, this series is now in Junio Hamano's hands for final evaluation and potential merging. The controversial `split` subcommand remains deferred, keeping this initial implementation focused on safe, non-conflict operations. The work represents a significant step in Git's history editing capabilities while maintaining compatibility with existing workflows.
