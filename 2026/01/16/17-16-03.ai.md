This is a terminology discussion in the context of the `git last-modified` bugfix series.

The email exchange between Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt clarifies the historical and conceptual use of "revision" in Git. Patrick notes that in his view, a revision can resolve to any object type (not just commits) and distinguishes between the naming syntax ("HEAD~10") and the underlying commit object it may resolve to. Junio provides historical context, recalling that "revision" was used more loosely in Git's early days but typically referred to commits in practice, as seen in core components like `revision.c`.

This discussion emerged from earlier patches in the series that updated error messages to use "revision" instead of "tree" when referring to inputs. The exchange shows the careful attention paid to terminology precision in Git's development, even for seemingly small wording choices.
