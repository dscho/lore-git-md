This is a contributor housekeeping email about Google Summer of Code (GSoC) participation planning for 2026. Christian Couder, a Git Project Leadership Committee (PLC) member and long-time GSoC coordinator for Git, is initiating early discussion about the project's participation in next year's program.

The email outlines the key requirements for participation: mentors, organization administrators, project ideas, and documentation updates. Christian notes that several GitLab team members (Karthik Nayak, Justin Tobler, Siddharth, and himself) have already expressed interest in mentoring roles, and he volunteers to serve as an organization administrator. The GitLab team also offers to manage Slack channels for coordination, building on their experience from GSoC 2025.

This is an organizational message rather than a technical contribution - it seeks to gauge community interest and identify volunteers who can help shepherd Git's participation in the program. The timeline is tight, with applications for mentoring organizations opening January 19 (just 3 days after this email) and closing February 3. The email serves as a call for other potential mentors and administrators to step forward if the project wants to participate.
