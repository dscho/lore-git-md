This is a follow-up cleanup patch in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing the completion of tree-parsing conversion work. Patrick Steinhardt confirms René Scharfe's cleanup of obsolete Coccinelle rules from `the_repository.cocci` that were used during earlier phases of the global variable removal. The rules being removed had already served their purpose in converting functions like those in revision.h, and no stragglers remain that would need them. 

Patrick has verified there are no remaining instances of the old functions these rules targeted. The patch maintains the project's disciplined approach to transformation tooling - rules are added when needed for active conversions and promptly removed once their work is done. This marks the final housekeeping step for the tree-parsing portion of the multi-year effort to eliminate `the_repository` global state, following the main conversion series that introduced repository-aware tree parsing functions and updated callers across multiple subsystems.
