Performance optimization for sparse-checkout pattern handling, now addressing a duplicate-handling edge case. This is the fourth iteration of a patch from Amisha Chhajed that improves the sorting of sparse-checkout patterns from O(n^2) to O(n log n) by switching from string_list_insert() to string_list_append() followed by sorting.

Junio C Hamano follows up on the thread, pressing for clarity about why tests pass despite the earlier omission of duplicate handling. He suggests that if this reveals a test coverage gap (as identified earlier by Jeff King), Amisha should consider adding a test case to verify duplicate pattern handling, though this wouldn't block the current optimization.

The patch appears technically sound and ready for merging, having addressed all previous feedback. The only remaining consideration is whether to augment test coverage as follow-up work. This fits into the broader sparse-checkout optimization efforts led by Derrick Stolee and Elijah Newren, though it's a localized performance improvement rather than an architectural change.
