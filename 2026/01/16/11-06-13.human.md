This is a contributor housekeeping email about the Git project's potential participation in Google Summer of Code 2026. Christian Couder, a Git PLC member and long-time GSoC coordinator for the project, is gauging interest and assembling resources needed for participation.

The email outlines the key requirements for GSoC participation: mentors, organization administrators, project ideas, and documentation updates. Christian notes that several GitLab team members (including Karthik Nayak and Justin Tobler) have already expressed interest in mentoring, and he volunteers to serve as an org-admin. The GitLab team also offers to manage Slack channels for coordination, building on their experience from GSoC 2025.

The email serves as a call for broader community participation beyond the GitLab team. Christian is seeking additional volunteers for mentoring roles and input on whether to host GSoC information on git.github.io or migrate it to git-scm.org. The tone is preparatory and inclusive, aiming to gather commitments and make an early decision about participation before the January 19 application deadline.
