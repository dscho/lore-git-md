This is a final cleanup patch in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing obsolete Coccinelle rules. The patch follows René Scharfe's 10-part series that completed converting tree-parsing functions to be repository-aware.

The change removes Coccinelle transformation rules that were used to convert functions like `parse_tree()` to their repository-aware counterparts (`repo_parse_tree()`). These rules are now obsolete since all conversions in the targeted subsystems (revision, commit-reach, diff, etc.) have been completed. Patrick Steinhardt has verified there are no remaining uses of the old functions.

Junio Hamano gives his approval, confirming the cleanup is ready to be merged. This represents the final housekeeping step for the tree-parsing portion of the multi-year effort to eliminate `the_repository` global variable usage. The patch maintains the project's disciplined approach to tooling maintenance - removing transformation rules once their work is done rather than letting them accumulate.

The change is purely mechanical and doesn't affect runtime behavior. It demonstrates the project's thoroughness in completing architectural changes, ensuring both the code and its supporting infrastructure remain clean. This cleanup was anticipated in the natural progression of the series, with all technical discussion having been settled in earlier patches.
