Performance investigation of `git pack-refs` on NFS with JGit interoperability. Martin Fick reports that adding the "fully-peeled" marker (but not the "sorted" marker) to packed-refs files dramatically reduces runtime from 5+ minutes to under 4 seconds in their Gerrit environment with ~3M refs. This confirms Jeff King's hypothesis about verification overhead when markers are missing, though the specific culprit was the peeling status rather than sortedness. 

The email reveals that JGit is properly sorting refs but not writing either the "sorted" or "fully-peeled" markers. While adding the "fully-peeled" marker resolves most of the performance gap, Martin notes this still suggests a Git-side optimization opportunity when markers are absent. The thread now has a clear path forward for JGit to improve its packed-refs output format while leaving open whether Git should handle missing markers more gracefully. This builds on the earlier findings about mmap/NFS overhead but shows the marker verification was the dominant factor in this case.
