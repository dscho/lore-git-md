Maintainer feedback on gettext build-option patch.

Junio C Hamano responds to Jiang Xin's patch adding gettext support detection to `git version --build-options`. While generally approving the idea, he suggests two refinements:

1. Unlike libcurl (which reports its specific version), simply indicating gettext's enabled/disabled state is sufficient since the version isn't needed for diagnostics.

2. He prefers omitting the "disabled" line entirely, arguing that absence of an "enabled" line would be clear enough indication. This diverges from the pattern used in the Rust detection code (commit cb2badb4) that Jiang Xin followed, and Junio suggests that precedent should be reconsidered too.

The feedback is constructive but non-blocking - the core concept of exposing gettext status is sound, just needing minor output formatting tweaks. Junio has CC'd the author of the original Rust implementation to weigh in on whether that output should also be simplified.

This moves the thread into a design discussion phase about output conventions for build-option reporting, with the specific question being whether to show negative states ("disabled") or only positive affirmations ("enabled"). The patch will likely need a v2 incorporating whichever convention emerges.
