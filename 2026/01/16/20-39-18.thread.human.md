Ramsay Jones, the maintainer of Git's Cygwin port, has sent a two-patch series addressing test failures that surfaced during the v2.53.0-rc0 cycle. The first patch fixes a path type expectation in t9700/test.pl that was failing on Cygwin, while the second attempts to mitigate a flaky test in t0610-reftable-basics.sh that had also caused problems in the previous release. Ramsay notes the second issue remains somewhat puzzling despite the fix.

The changes are minimal - just two one-line modifications - but address platform-specific quirks that could block Cygwin users. Ramsay has tested the patches on Linux and initial Cygwin testing shows improvement, though full test suite runs are still in progress. He provides helpful context about the resource-intensive nature of Cygwin test runs, which make the system nearly unusable during execution, explaining his verification constraints. The patches appear straightforward and well-motivated by actual test failures.
