Here's an updated human-readable summary of the GSoC 2026 planning thread:

The Git project is preparing for Google Summer of Code 2026, with Christian Couder (Git PLC member and GSoC coordinator) leading the effort alongside GitLab team members and community volunteers. The group has identified three main technical tracks: continuing the `the_repository` global state reduction work, improving the `git repo` subcommand (with Lucas Seiki Oshiro leading path handling improvements), and extending `git cat-file` with remote-object-info capabilities. A separate Developer Pages initiative led by Ayush Chandekar provides a non-code contribution pathway.

Christian Couder is maintaining overall coordination while being careful to mentor only in areas where he has expertise - he's stepped back from directly mentoring the `git repo` work, leaving that to Lucas and Karthik Nayak. Karthik has confirmed he's available to mentor across multiple projects, providing important capacity. The group recently rejected a proposal for string/token-level blame functionality as too ambitious for this cycle.

In the latest update, Christian emphasizes keeping options open regarding the final number of projects, noting they should wait for more mentor preferences before making decisions. Some mentors may take on multiple projects, and additional proposals might still emerge. The team is working toward the February 3 deadline to finalize the project slate while maintaining flexibility in mentor assignments. Coordination continues primarily through Slack, building on last year's successful infrastructure and microprojects approach.
