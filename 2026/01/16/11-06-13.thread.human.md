Git is preparing for Google Summer of Code 2026 participation, with Christian Couder (Git PLC member and long-time GSoC coordinator) leading the effort. The initial planning has established GitLab team members (Karthik Nayak, Justin Tobler, Siddharth) as core mentors, with Christian serving as organization administrator and setting up Slack channels for coordination following last year's successful model.

The effort has now expanded beyond the GitLab team, with Ayush Chandekar joining as a volunteer co-mentor to help with Developer Pages updates. Lucas Seiki Oshiro is also involved in the Developer Pages component through Slack access, though not in a formal mentor role. This emerging Developer Pages track appears to be developing as an alternative participation path for contributors who may not have capacity for full mentoring responsibilities.

While the organizational infrastructure is taking shape, the program still needs additional volunteer mentors from the broader community to fully staff the initiative. The exact scope and structure of the Developer Pages component also remains to be defined as this alternative participation model evolves. The thread remains focused on logistics and staffing rather than specific technical projects at this stage.
