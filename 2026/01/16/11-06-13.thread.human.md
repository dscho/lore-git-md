Christian Couder, a Git PLC member and longtime GSoC coordinator, has kicked off planning for Git's potential participation in Google Summer of Code 2026. Several GitLab team members (including Karthik Nayak, Justin Tobler, and Siddharth) have already volunteered as mentors, with Christian offering to serve as organization administrator. The team also proposes using GitLab's Slack channels for coordination, building on their experience from GSoC 2025.

With mentor applications opening in just three days (January 19) and closing February 3, this email serves as a call for additional volunteers from the broader community to help meet GSoC's participation requirements. The message focuses on organizational logistics rather than technical work, seeking to gauge interest and assemble the necessary team to support student contributors.
