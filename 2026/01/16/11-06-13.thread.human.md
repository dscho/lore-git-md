Christian Couder has initiated planning for Git's participation in Google Summer of Code 2026, with a team of GitLab employees (including Karthik Nayak and Justin Tobler) already committed as mentors. Christian will serve as organization administrator, building on GitLab's experience managing Slack coordination channels from 2025. 

While the core team is forming, responses from the broader community have been limited so far. Lucas Seiki Oshiro expressed interest in contributing to Developer Pages but couldn't commit to a full mentoring role, highlighting both the positive momentum and the need for more volunteers outside GitLab. The project still needs additional mentors and administrators to meet the January 19 application deadline, with Christian continuing to drive the effort forward.
