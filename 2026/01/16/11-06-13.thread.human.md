The Git community is actively planning its participation in Google Summer of Code 2026, with Christian Couder (Git PLC member and GSoC coordinator) leading the effort. The thread has established eight potential projects, ranging from technical work like continuing the `the_repository` removal effort to improving promisor remotes and expanding `git repo` subcommands (where Lucas Seiki Oshiro has already contributed working prototypes for path handling in `git repo info`). 

Karthik Nayak from GitLab has now joined the discussion, offering to mentor across the proposed projects. While he notes varying levels of familiarity with each topic, his involvement provides valuable institutional support alongside Christian's coordination. The group is currently working through practical questions about how many projects they can realistically mentor this year, with mentor assignments still being finalized. 

Other ongoing efforts include Ayush Chandekar's Developer Pages initiative (creating a non-code contribution pathway) and adaptations of last year's successful microprojects framework for newcomer onboarding. The discussion remains focused on concrete next steps ahead of key deadlines, with the community demonstrating its structured approach to using GSoC both for technical progress and contributor growth.
