This is a follow-up cleanup patch in the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch removes obsolete Coccinelle semantic patch rules from `the_repository.cocci` that were used to convert tree-parsing functions to take explicit repository parameters. 

René Scharfe originally sent the patch noting these rules have served their purpose since the last remaining repo-less functions were converted in 2023. Patrick Steinhardt verified the rules are no longer needed, and Junio Hamano agrees the cleanup looks good. The patch also adds a reminder to remove the newly added tree.h conversion rules after the next release cycle.

This represents the final housekeeping step for the tree-parsing portion of the multi-year effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The series has already addressed all technical feedback and reached consensus on documentation wording, making this a straightforward cleanup of now-unused transformation tooling. The disciplined approach to maintaining these semantic patches - adding them when needed and promptly removing them after use - demonstrates the project's careful process for large-scale refactoring.
