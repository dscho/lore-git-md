Performance optimization discussion in xdiff refactoring series. This is patch 9/10 in Ezekiel Newren's series to refactor xdiff internals, now with performance considerations raised by René Scharfe.

The patch removes xdlclassifier dependency from xdl_cleanup_records() by introducing a new xoccurrence struct and IVec (introduced earlier in the series) to track line matches between files. René points out that since the IVec is pre-sized and never resized, CALLOC_ARRAY could be used instead of the more complex IVec machinery here. This follows earlier discussion about IVec's growth strategy potentially causing quadratic complexity.

The change itself is part of the broader xdiff subsystem refactoring to enable future optimizations and Rust compatibility. The performance discussion adds another dimension to the ongoing technical debate about the IVec implementation, which already had concerns about type safety and standards compliance. The thread shows active technical review from multiple contributors as the series evolves.
