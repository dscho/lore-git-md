This is the final integration step for Patrick Steinhardt's 14-part series on object database (ODB) abstraction, a major refactoring to enable pluggable storage backends. Junio Hamano confirms the series looks good to merge, noting only a minor procedural issue with commit hash citations in the cover letter that Patrick has acknowledged.

With Junio's approval and Patrick's confirmation of the fix, this foundational work is now ready to land in Git's main development branch, paving the way for future alternative storage backends.
