This is part 2 of a 7-patch bugfix series addressing a regression in error message reporting for batched reference updates. The patch focuses on fixing a control flow issue in `refs_verify_refnames_available()` where rejected references were being processed multiple times due to incorrect loop continuation.

The key change replaces a `continue` statement with a `goto next_ref` to properly skip to the next reference in the outer loop when a filesystem conflict is detected, rather than just continuing the inner loop. This prevents the same reference from being repeatedly rejected. The patch also adds error message handling through a new `rejection_details` field in `struct ref_update`.

The changes are mechanical but important for correct error reporting behavior, touching all three ref backends (files, packed, and reftable). The patch maintains backward compatibility while fixing the regression, and is part of a larger effort to restore detailed error messages that were lost during the introduction of batched reference updates.

This appears to be a well-constructed fix that addresses a specific technical issue without introducing new complexity. The patch follows the established pattern of the series and has been reviewed by Jeff King, suggesting it's on solid technical footing.
