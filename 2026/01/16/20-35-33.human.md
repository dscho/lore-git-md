Performance investigation update for `git pack-refs --all` on NFS with high ref counts. Martin Fick reports a key breakthrough: the "fully-peeled" marker in packed-refs files (not the "sorted" marker as initially suspected) is the critical factor affecting performance. When manually adding this marker to files created by JGit (which previously omitted it), the runtime drops from over 5 minutes to under 4 seconds.

Jeff King's earlier analysis had focused on the "sorted" marker's absence as a potential cause of slowdowns, but testing shows the "fully-peeled" marker has a much more dramatic impact. This suggests JGit should be modified to include both markers, though the "fully-peeled" one appears to be the primary performance lever in this case.

The finding raises questions about why Git's performance degrades so severely without the "fully-peeled" marker, suggesting there may be room for optimization in Git's packed-refs handling even when markers are missing. While the immediate workaround (adding markers) resolves the issue, the thread now shifts to understanding why the absence of this particular marker causes such extreme slowdowns.
