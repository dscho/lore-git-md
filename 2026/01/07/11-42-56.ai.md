Performance investigation of `git pack-refs` on NFS continues, with Patrick Steinhardt responding to Martin Fick's latest findings. The discussion has now conclusively identified mmap'd packfile access as the bottleneck when running on NFS, with Martin's tests showing pre-reading the pack file reduces runtime from 5+ minutes to under 20 seconds (matching JGit's performance). Patrick acknowledges NFS remains a hard requirement for Gerrit's mirroring needs despite its performance challenges in this case.

Patrick notes that while the files backend could theoretically be optimized to reduce lock contention by moving reads before lock acquisition, he considers this approach less valuable than focusing on the reftable backend as a long-term solution. He reiterates his suggestion to use `perf(1)` for profiling, though recognizes this may be difficult in production environments. The exchange highlights the tension between immediate workarounds for NFS performance and architectural solutions like reftables, with Patrick clearly favoring the latter approach.
