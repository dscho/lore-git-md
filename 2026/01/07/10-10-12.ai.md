This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v8 series introducing the `git-history` command. The patch modifies the replay infrastructure to expose the final rewritten commit's object ID through a new `final_oid` field in `struct replay_ref_updates`.

The change enables a planned feature in the upcoming `git-history` command where HEAD can be updated without modifying other references. The implementation carefully documents the field's limitations - it's only reliably useful when rewriting a single strand of commits, as the order of branch rewrites in more complex cases is undefined.

The patch is mechanically straightforward, adding just 18 lines across two files with clear documentation of the new field's behavior. It maintains the series' focus on building robust infrastructure for history rewriting while keeping the scope narrow to support the immediate `git-history` needs. This continues the pattern seen throughout the series of careful, incremental improvements to the replay machinery.
