This is a performance discussion about `git pack-refs` slowness on NFS-mounted repositories with high ref counts (~3M refs). Patrick Steinhardt responds to Martin Fick's investigation, which identified mmap'd packfile access (not packed-refs handling) as the bottleneck when running on NFS storage.

The discussion has shifted from general optimization to specifically addressing mmap/NFS interaction, with Martin's tests showing JGit performs 10x faster in the same environment. Patrick acknowledges NFS remains necessary for Gerrit's mirroring requirements but notes GitLab and GitHub have moved to custom replication solutions (Gitaly Cluster and Spokes respectively).

While Patrick expresses personal preference for focusing on reftables as a long-term solution rather than optimizing the files backend, he welcomes improvements to reduce lock contention during slow packing operations. The thread remains focused on diagnosis, with next steps including potential perf(1) profiling and exploring runtime NO_MMAP configuration options.
