This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB abstraction effort, specifically patch 3/7 in the v3 series. The patch extends the `is_delta` field in `struct object_info` from a simple boolean to a 4-state enum that can represent unknown, full, offset-delta, and ref-delta object types. This prepares for future changes where we may not always know an object's delta status when doing size-only queries.

Karthik Nayak reviews the change and approves the approach, noting it provides clearer information while maintaining safety (the code already bails early for bad objects). The change touches `odb.h` and `packfile.c` and is part of a larger series that has already addressed all review feedback and is ready for integration. The series as a whole shows measurable performance improvements (9% speedup in some cases) while cleaning up interfaces for the ODB abstraction work.
