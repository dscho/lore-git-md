This is part 7 of Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database backends. The series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources.

Patch 7 simplifies `packfile_store_prepare()` to only initialize the single packfile store passed to it, rather than preparing all stores in the object database. This change is possible now that packfile stores live at the source level rather than the ODB level. The patch updates callers in `builtin/grep.c` and `packfile.c` to explicitly prepare stores when needed, maintaining the same behavior through careful iteration over sources.

The changes are mechanical but important for the larger architectural goal of making packfile operations source-aware. Reviewers Justin Tobler and Toon Claes have already vetted earlier versions, and Patrick has incorporated all feedback. This continues the series' pattern of maintaining identical behavior while cleaning up the internal structure.
