This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series refactoring Git's ref backend verification. The series has systematically moved fsck checks from `builtin/fsck.c` into the refs subsystem, with this patch completing the migration by removing the now-redundant `fsck_head_link()` function.

The patch eliminates a legacy HEAD validation check that had become obsolete after previous patches centralized ref verification in `refs_fsck_ref()` and `refs_fsck_symref()`. The removed function performed three checks that are now redundant: unsafe ref detection (covered by `check_refname_format()`), missing HEAD detection (already caught earlier), and symref resolution (not used with current flags). The change inlines the remaining `refs_resolve_ref_unsafe()` call directly where needed.

This completes the series' goal of unifying ref verification across both files and reftable backends while maintaining identical error reporting. The removal of `fsck_head_link()` demonstrates confidence in the new centralized verification infrastructure, with all test coverage preserved through the refs subsystem checks. The patch follows the series' pattern of careful, incremental changes with clear rationale in the commit message.
