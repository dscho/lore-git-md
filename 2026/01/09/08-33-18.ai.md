This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 10-part series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database (ODB) backends. The patch moves the multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`, completing the architectural shift where all packfile-related data is now owned by the packfile store rather than the ODB layer.

The changes update MIDX handling across several files (`midx.c`, `odb.c`, `packfile.c`) to reference the MIDX via the packfile store instead of directly through the ODB source. Key modifications include updating the getter function `get_multi_pack_index()`, MIDX loading in `prepare_multi_pack_index_one()`, and cleanup paths in `clear_midx_file()` and `odb_close()`. The patch removes the now-obsolete `midx` field from `struct odb_source` while adding it to `struct packfile_store`.

This represents the culmination of the series' goal to consolidate packfile ownership within the packfile store abstraction. The changes maintain identical behavior while cleaning up the internal structure in preparation for pluggable backends. With 43 lines changed (19 additions, 25 deletions) across 5 files, the patch follows the established pattern of mechanical but careful refactoring seen throughout the series.
