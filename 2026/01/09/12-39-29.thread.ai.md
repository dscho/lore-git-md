Patrick Steinhardt's 17-patch series refactors Git's ref backends (files and reftable) to improve consistency checks, with this final patch completing the migration of fsck checks from `builtin/fsck.c` into the refs subsystem. The series progressed through three phases: files backend validation (1-8), shared verification infrastructure (9-14), and fsck check migration (15-17). 

The final patch removes the now-redundant `fsck_head_link()` function, eliminating legacy HEAD validation checks that have been superseded by the centralized verification in `refs_fsck_ref()` and `refs_fsck_symref()`. The removed checks (unsafe ref detection, missing HEAD detection, and symref resolution) are now covered by `check_refname_format()` and earlier validation steps. The change inlines the remaining `refs_resolve_ref_unsafe()` call where needed.

Key technical details:
- New error codes introduced earlier in series: `FSCK_MSG_BAD_REF_OID` (patch 15), `FSCK_MSG_BAD_HEAD_TARGET` (patch 16)
- Shared infrastructure fully operational: `refs_fsck_ref()`, `refs_fsck_symref()`, `backend_for_worktree()`
- Backend coverage complete: files and reftable now use identical validation paths
- Test impact: all expectations updated in t0602 (reftable), t1450 (fsck) to reflect new error message formats
