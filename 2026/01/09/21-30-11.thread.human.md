René Scharfe's patch series to remove `the_repository` dependency from tree parsing functions has reached its final polishing stage. The series introduces new repository-aware APIs (`repo_parse_tree*()`) and converts all existing callers across multiple subsystems including bloom filters, delta-islands, and path-walk. 

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the final patch adding deprecation wrappers for the old functions. While fully supporting the technical approach, he suggests two minor improvements to make the transition clearer: explicitly passing `the_repository` to new functions would highlight remaining global usage (though René already addresses this in a follow-up), and adding deprecation notices with a proposed removal timeline after Git 2.53. 

This marks the culmination of a multi-year effort to eliminate global state from tree parsing. The series appears ready for merging, having addressed all substantive feedback. The only remaining discussion points are about documentation and visibility of the deprecation process rather than the core changes. René's work follows the project's established refactoring pattern and includes a Coccinelle semantic patch to help maintain consistency going forward.
