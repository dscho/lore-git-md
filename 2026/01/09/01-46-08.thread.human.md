This thread discusses updating the Git FAQ to recommend using stash import/export (introduced in Git 2.51) as the preferred method for syncing working trees across machines, rather than direct filesystem syncing with rsync. The initial patch rewrote the FAQ to clearly position stash transfer as the primary mechanism, explaining the three-step process and its advantages, while demoting rsync to a secondary option with appropriate warnings.

Junio Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed the text and raised a terminology concern about describing the stash mechanism as "secure," suggesting "robust" might be more accurate. brian m. carlson has now responded, defending the "secure" descriptor by highlighting specific security advantages over rsync - particularly that stash transfer avoids executing hooks/config and prevents accidental transfer of secrets in ignored files (like `.env` files). However, brian has deferred to Junio's judgment on the wording, offering to send a revised version if needed.

The core technical recommendation is settled, with both Junio and brian agreeing stash import/export is the right approach. The only remaining question is whether to adjust the "secure" wording in the documentation, which brian has left to the maintainer's discretion. This appears to be the final refinement needed before the documentation update can be merged.
