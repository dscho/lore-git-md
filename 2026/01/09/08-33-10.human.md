This is part 2 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to support pluggable object database backends. The patch simplifies how packfiles are prepared by passing the ODB source directly rather than its individual components. 

The change is mechanical but meaningful - it reduces the number of parameters passed around by consolidating them into the `struct odb_source` that already exists. The diff shows the `prepare_pack_data` struct being streamlined to just hold the source and garbage collection list, while the packfile loading logic now pulls details like the MIDX and local flag directly from the source.

This continues the architectural shift where packfile stores will be managed by ODB sources rather than the ODB layer itself. The patch is small (14 lines changed) and focused, with no behavior changes - it's purely preparatory refactoring for the larger series.
