This is a feature enhancement for `git status` that shows divergence from both upstream and push tracking branches, part of a 2-patch series. The current email is a response to Phillip Wood's review of the second patch, which implements the push tracking display functionality.

Harald Nordgren addresses several technical points from Phillip's review:
1. Defends the use of enum flags for selectively controlling push/pull advice display, though open to suggestions for cleaner approaches
2. Explains why switching to `branch_get_push()` breaks tests, preferring the current reference resolution chain
3. Discusses tradeoffs around refname shortening between helper functions and callers
4. Acknowledges the advice display could be clearer with pre-computed boolean flags (which will be implemented)
5. Explains the rationale behind BRANCH_MODE_PUSH checks in advice logic
6. Notes the enum/bitmask combination is intentional for default modes
7. Justifies the control flow for handling failed push branch stats as a complexity tradeoff

The discussion reveals some remaining design questions around reference resolution and advice display that may need resolution before merging. Phillip Wood's thorough review has identified several areas where the implementation could be made more maintainable, though the core functionality appears sound. The series has undergone extensive review (17 iterations) and appears technically ready pending these final refinements.
