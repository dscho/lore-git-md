This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's v9 series introducing the `git-history` command, specifically focused on exposing the final rewritten commit's OID through the replay machinery. The patch adds a `final_oid` field to the `replay_result` struct that tracks the last commit rewritten during a replay operation, which will be used by the upcoming `git-history` command to support HEAD-only reference updates.

The implementation is straightforward - it captures the OID of `last_commit` in the result struct during replay. The accompanying documentation in replay.h carefully notes the field's limitations: it's undefined when rewriting multiple branches (due to unpredictable rewrite order) and uninitialized if no commits were rewritten. However, it remains useful for the common case of rewriting a single linear history.

This change continues the series' pattern of incrementally building up the replay infrastructure needed for the history command while maintaining clear documentation about behavior and edge cases. The patch appears mechanically sound and well-explained, with no obvious technical concerns. It builds on the refactored replay machinery from earlier in the series while keeping the specific needs of the history command in view.
