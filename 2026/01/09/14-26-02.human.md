This is the final patch in a documentation series improving the `git-patch-id` man page and related code comments. The series has already gone through review and is now in its second iteration with all feedback addressed.

The patch clarifies the interaction between `--verbatim` and `--stable` options in `git-patch-id`. It documents that `--verbatim` implies `--stable` and forbids `--unstable`, which was an implementation detail introduced in commit 2871f4d4 but not fully explained in the documentation. The change is small but meaningful, fixing both the technical accuracy and the markup formatting of option names.

This is part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's ongoing work to improve Git's documentation. The series has received positive reviews from Pushkar Singh and Patrick Steinhardt, with no outstanding issues remaining. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for merging, continuing the project's broader effort to maintain clear and accurate documentation.
