Patrick Steinhardt has completed his 17-patch series refactoring Git's ref backend verification system. The work standardizes validation checks across both the files and reftable backends by moving fsck logic from `builtin/fsck.c` into shared refs subsystem functions. The final patch removes the now-redundant `fsck_head_link()` function, whose checks are fully covered by the new centralized verification in `refs_fsck_ref()` and `refs_fsck_symref()`.

The series introduced structured error reporting with new `FSCK_MSG_*` codes and ensured both backends perform identical validation. Test coverage was updated throughout to reflect the new error message formats, including more realistic test cases. With this completion, the refs subsystem now handles all verification that was previously scattered across multiple locations, making the codebase more maintainable and consistent. The changes primarily touch `refs/files-backend.c`, `refs/reftable-backend.c`, and `builtin/fsck.c`, with corresponding test updates in t0602, t0614 and t1450.
