Phillip Wood provides a final review of the first patch, which extracts and modularizes the branch comparison formatting logic from `format_tracking_info()` into a new `format_branch_comparison()` function.

The patch maintains all existing edge case handling (gone upstream, quick mode, diverged branches) while preparing the codebase for the subsequent patch that will display push tracking information. The extensive review history (17 iterations) indicates this change has been thoroughly vetted, with Junio Hamano and others having previously signed off on the control flow and error handling.

This is clearly a well-considered refactoring that serves as groundwork for the push tracking feature. The changes are localized to `remote.c` and maintain backward compatibility, making it a low-risk but important step in the series.
