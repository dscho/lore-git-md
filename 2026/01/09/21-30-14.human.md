This is part 3 of René Scharfe's ongoing series to remove implicit `the_repository` dependencies, specifically targeting tree parsing functions. The patch converts one caller in `add-interactive.c` from the old `parse_tree_indirect()` to the new repository-aware `repo_parse_tree_indirect()`.

The change is straightforward - replacing a global function call with its repository-specific counterpart, passing the repository pointer that was already available in the context. This follows the established pattern of the series: first new repository-aware functions were introduced in patch 2, and now individual callers are being updated to use them.

The patch is a mechanical change with no behavior impact, continuing the careful refactoring approach that has characterized this long-running effort. Given René's expertise in this type of refactoring and the uncontroversial nature of the change, this patch (and the series as a whole) appears to progress smoothly toward eventual merging.
