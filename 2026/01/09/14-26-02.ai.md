Documentation patch finalizing the `git-patch-id` man page improvements. This is the sixth and last patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's v2 series, which has already received positive reviews from Pushkar Singh and Patrick Steinhardt. The series as a whole clarifies and standardizes the documentation for `git-patch-id`, with this particular patch focusing on the interaction between the `--verbatim` and `--stable` options.

The change documents that `--verbatim` implies `--stable` and forbids `--unstable`, a behavior that was introduced in commit 2871f4d4 but not fully explained in the documentation. The patch fixes this oversight with a clear, concise addition to the man page. The v2 iteration corrects minor markup issues from v1 (adding backticks around `--unstable` in the commit message) and maintains the series' consistent style of capitalizing "ID" when used standalone.

This patch series represents the final polish of `git-patch-id` documentation, addressing all feedback from reviewers. The changes are uncontroversial, well-motivated, and align with Git's documentation standards. The author has demonstrated attention to detail in both technical accuracy and stylistic consistency throughout the series.
