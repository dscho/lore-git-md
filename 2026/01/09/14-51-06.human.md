Bugfix for HTTP authentication in remote-curl when handling large requests, particularly affecting filtered clones. This is a follow-up to Aaron Plattner's patch that fixed missing auth headers in probe_rpc() calls, which caused blob requests to fail with 401 errors after initial authentication succeeded for commit history.

Patrick Steinhardt provides a review, noting the change matches existing auth handling in post_rpc() and suggests adding a test case based on t5563-simple-http-auth. He also clarifies why the HTTP_REAUTH loop doesn't cause infinite recursion in this case - the issue was specifically about missing headers rather than expired tokens. The fix is minimal (2 lines adding http_append_auth_header()) and has been tested by the original reporter.

While the immediate issue is addressed, Patrick notes there may be remaining work around HTTP_REAUTH cases, particularly for Bearer tokens that come from external agents where the multistage auth approach doesn't apply. The review moves the patch forward while suggesting some additional context that could be helpful in the commit message.
