This is part 6 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's packfile storage in preparation for pluggable object database (ODB) backends. The patch focuses on the `packfile_store_get_packs()` function, which previously prepared MIDX (multi-pack index) files for all sources in an ODB, but now only prepares the MIDX for the specific store being accessed.

The change is a logical simplification now that packfile stores are owned by individual ODB sources rather than the ODB itself. While the behavioral impact should be minimal (callers either work with one store or already iterate over sources), it tightens the coupling between a store and its MIDX preparation. The patch is small (4 lines removed) and builds on prior reviewed work in the series.

This continues the architectural shift toward making object storage more modular while maintaining identical behavior. The series has already addressed all reviewer feedback and appears ready for merging as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort.
