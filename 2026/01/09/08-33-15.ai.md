This is part 7 of Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database (ODB) backends. The series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources.

Patch 7 simplifies `packfile_store_prepare()` to only prepare the single packfile store passed by the caller, rather than preparing all stores from all sources in the ODB. This change is possible now that the store sits at the source level rather than the ODB level. The patch updates two callers:

1. `builtin/grep.c` now explicitly iterates through all sources to prepare their stores, rather than relying on the old behavior of preparing everything at once.
2. `find_pack_entry()` in `packfile.c` moves the store preparation into its source iteration, maintaining the same behavior but with clearer ownership.

The changes are mechanical but important for the architectural goal of making packfile operations source-aware. This continues the pattern seen in earlier patches of updating callers to explicitly handle multiple sources while maintaining identical behavior. The patch builds on prior work that moved MIDX (multi-pack index) tracking to operate per-source rather than globally.

Review feedback from earlier versions has been incorporated, and the series appears ready for merging as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort. This patch in particular resolves Toon Claes' initial misunderstanding about the simplification's correctness by making the single-store preparation explicit.
