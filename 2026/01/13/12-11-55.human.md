This is the first of two patches in Harald Nordgren's series to enhance `git status` with push tracking branch information. The patch refactors the existing branch comparison formatting logic in preparation for the new feature.

The change extracts the core formatting logic from `format_tracking_info()` into a new helper function `format_branch_comparison()`. This creates a clean separation between the branch status calculation and its presentation, making it easier to reuse the same formatting for both upstream and push tracking branches in the follow-up patch. The refactoring maintains all existing behavior including the quick mode (`AHEAD_BEHIND_QUICK`) and advice messages.

The patch follows Git's style conventions and has been through 25 iterations of review, with final variable naming (`cmp_fetch`) following Junio Hamano's direction. It's part of a well-reviewed series that Junio has already approved for merging into the `next` branch. The changes are mechanical and focused - exactly the kind of preparatory refactoring needed before adding new functionality.
