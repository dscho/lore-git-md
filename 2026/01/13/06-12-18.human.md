This is the final approval for the submodule path encoding series, marking the conclusion of a multi-month effort to make submodule paths filesystem-safe. Patrick Steinhardt gives his sign-off on version 9 of the 11-patch series, following Junio Hamano's earlier assessment that the changes have reached "diminishing returns" for further improvements. 

The series implements a robust solution for handling submodule paths across different filesystems through URL encoding and hashing fallbacks, with comprehensive validation to prevent configuration conflicts. All feedback from reviewers has been addressed across 10 iterations, and Josh Steadmon had previously confirmed production readiness in version 8. 

With Patrick's approval now added to Junio's, the series is fully cleared for merging into Git's main branch. This represents a significant improvement in submodule reliability, particularly for edge cases involving special characters and case-sensitive filesystems.
