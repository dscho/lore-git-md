This is a brief follow-up discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about the deprecation approach for compatibility wrappers.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to René Scharfe's earlier message about the wrapper removal strategy. The thread had previously settled on using advisory comments rather than version-tied deprecation notices for the transitional macros introduced in the tree-parsing conversion. 

Patrick agrees with René's observation that forgetting to remove the macro definitions would be a minor issue, while neglecting to clean up the associated Coccinelle rules would be more problematic. He suggests this might warrant a broader cleanup of outdated Coccinelle rules in the codebase.

The exchange is collegial and confirms the consensus from earlier in the thread - the wrappers will be marked for future removal with simple reminder comments rather than version-based requirements. This maintains the project's flexibility while still providing guidance for future maintenance.
