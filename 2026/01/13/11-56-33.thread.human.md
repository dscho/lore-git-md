This thread addresses multiple issues in Git's hook subsystem that were caught just before the 2.53 release. The core problems involved stdout/stderr separation in pre-push hooks, stdin feeding performance, potential deadlocks, and incorrect `ungroup` flag initialization. After discussion, Junio decided to revert the entire 'ar/run-command-hook' topic for release stability, creating a clean revert branch while Adrian Ratiu prepares a reworked version ('ar/run-command-hook-take-2') for future consideration.

The latest discussion involves Kristoffer Haugsbakk reviewing Adrian's v3 patch that makes the `ungroup` flag opt-out rather than opt-in. While technically sound, Haugsbakk notes the commit message could better explain the user-facing impact - this wasn't just an internal bug but would have caused confusing hangs when reading from stdin in hook scripts. This feedback highlights how even pre-release regressions benefit from documenting their potential real-world effects.

The community has aligned on a two-pronged approach: reverting immediately for 2.53 stability while Adrian prepares a comprehensive v7 series addressing all issues. The discussion shows Git's careful balance between technical correctness and user experience, even for internal fixes. Future work will need to incorporate both the technical solutions and clearer documentation of edge cases.
