This thread addresses a regression in Git's hook subsystem where the pre-push hook's output was unintentionally being redirected to stderr, breaking existing workflows. The patch introduces a configurable `stdout_to_stderr` option in the hook infrastructure, maintaining backward compatibility by defaulting to the current behavior while explicitly preserving the pre-push hook's historical ability to remain separate from stderr.

The fix is small and focused, touching just three files to make the output handling flexible. It comes with clear documentation of both the regression and the rationale for this particular solution. This seems like a well-considered solution that fixes the specific regression while leaving room for future hook implementations to choose their output handling behavior.
