This thread addresses several interrelated issues in Git's hook subsystem, primarily concerning stdout/stderr separation in pre-push hooks and performance/deadlock concerns in hook execution. The discussion has involved Chris Darroch (original author), Adrian Ratiu (hook subsystem expert), Jeff King, and Junio C Hamano.

The core problems included a regression where pre-push hooks lost their separate stream capability and incorrect initialization of the `ungroup` flag. A v3 patch series was prepared with fixes for these issues, which Junio initially approved for merging into Git 2.53. The patches included comprehensive test coverage and maintained backward compatibility while fixing the regressions.

However, in the latest development, Junio has expressed hesitation about merging the fixes and is considering reverting the entire 'ar/run-command-hook' topic that introduced these issues. While acknowledging the urgency of addressing the regressions, he's weighing this against the option of a complete revert, which would be feasible early in the 2.53 release cycle. He plans to experiment with this approach after cutting the rc0 preview.

The thread has reached consensus on deferring more complex architectural changes (like Jeff King's muxer thread proposal) to future work, focusing first on stabilizing the immediate regressions. Adrian Ratiu continues to work on server-side hook improvements separately from this decision point. The maintainer's upcoming choice will determine whether these hook subsystem changes remain in 2.53 or get deferred to a later release.
