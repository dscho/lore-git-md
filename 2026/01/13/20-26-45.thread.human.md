Maintainer Junio C Hamano has responded, noting this might relate to prior discussions about hook execution behavior, though he hasn't yet proposed a specific fix. The regression appears connected to stdin handling changes made when the hook ungroup option became configurable. While the technical details are still being investigated, this is clearly a significant performance regression for workflows that rely on pre-push hooks processing stdin data. The thread remains open as participants work to understand and address the root cause.
