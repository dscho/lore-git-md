This is a bugfix patch targeting Git's hook subsystem, specifically addressing a regression in how hook output is handled. The patch makes the `stdout_to_stderr` behavior configurable rather than hardcoded, fixing an issue where the pre-push hook's output was unintentionally being redirected to stderr.

The change introduces a new `stdout_to_stderr` option in the `run_hooks_opt` struct, defaulting to 1 (maintaining backward compatibility for most hooks) while explicitly setting it to 0 for the pre-push hook. This matches the historical behavior where pre-push hooks could use stdout and stderr separately. The patch is small (8 lines net change) and focused, touching three files: `hook.c`, `hook.h`, and `transport.c`. It comes with a clear commit message noting the regression and rationale for the fix, and includes both a bug report credit (Chris Darroch) and a suggestion credit (brian m. carlson).

The change appears well-considered - rather than forcing all hooks to adapt to the new behavior, it makes the output handling configurable while specifically fixing the pre-push hook case that broke existing behavior.
