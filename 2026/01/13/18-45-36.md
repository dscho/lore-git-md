The rationale is simple: the patch is changing the mode of this section
from a verbatim instance of example to a synopsis model of command.
I'm not settled on whether this is a good move. What is sure though is that 
when writing in synopsis grammar, it is better to switch to synopsis style, in 
order to maintain a consistent output and allow clearer and more direct 
explanations.
