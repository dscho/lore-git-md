This is a follow-up to Harald Nordgren's now-approved patch series enhancing `git status` to show divergence from both upstream and push tracking branches. The series has gone through 23 iterations and is queued for merging into `next`.

Junio noted that the advice-display logic could be cleaner by incorporating the `show_divergence_advice` flag into the existing `BRANCH_MODE_*` flags, though he explicitly stated this was just an observation rather than a blocking concern.

Harald agrees with the suggestion and confirms he has implemented the change, resulting in cleaner code with one fewer parameter. This appears to be the final tweak before the series, which has already been approved for merging after extensive review from multiple contributors including Phillip Wood, Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt.

The implementation represents a significant enhancement to `git status`'s tracking information display while maintaining backward compatibility, with thorough test coverage and careful attention to edge cases. While there are some remaining considerations around `push.default` interactions and potential future cleanup of the advice-display logic, these are noted as possible follow-ups rather than blockers.
