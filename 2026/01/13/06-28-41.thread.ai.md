gitk color configuration bugfix for ref/tag drawing. The patch fixes two issues where existing color settings were being ignored: `tagfgcolor` was unused (all labels used `headfgcolor`) and head outlines were hardcoded black instead of respecting `headoutlinecolor`. Changes are limited to the `drawtags` procedure, maintaining backward compatibility for non-head ref colors (black outline, #ddddff fill) while making configurable colors work for heads/tags. Discovered while working on VSCode extension theme export to gitk. Small, focused fix with no new complexity added.
