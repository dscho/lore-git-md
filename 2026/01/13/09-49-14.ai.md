**Hook API regression discussion**

This email from Adrian Ratiu continues the thread about the pre-push hook output regression, where commit 3e2836a742d redirected hook stdout to stderr. Adrian acknowledges Chris Darroch's report (from Git LFS) about the compatibility impact and references his own "Extending git without breaking it" presentation about avoiding such regressions. He aligns with brian m. carlson's earlier suggestion to revert to the previous stdout behavior for pre-push hooks, while leaving open the possibility of a planned future change to stderr output.

Adrian notes he will test the issue against his ongoing work on config-based hooks (the broader effort this pre-push conversion is part of) and verify the fix against Git LFS's test suite. The email reinforces that this is a real regression affecting production tools, not just test suites, and that the community is taking it seriously. The technical focus remains on the `stdout_to_stderr` flag in `hook.c`'s `pick_next_hook()` function, with Adrian positioning himself to provide concrete verification of any proposed solution.
