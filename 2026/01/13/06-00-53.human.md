This is a bugfix patch from Elijah Newren addressing an edge case in the `git replay` command's handling of detached HEAD states. The patch is part of Patrick Steinhardt's larger v10 series introducing the new `git-history` command with `reword` and `split` subcommands.

The patch simplifies the logic for detecting a detached HEAD by using `refs_read_ref_full()` with `RESOLVE_REF_NO_RECURSE` (though Newren questions whether this flag is strictly necessary). It also refactors the decoration handling in `replay_revisions()` to make the conditions for updating references clearer, particularly distinguishing between detached HEAD and branch-tracking cases.

A new test case is added to verify that replay operations correctly update a detached HEAD. The changes are clean and focused, addressing a specific technical gap in the replay infrastructure while maintaining the series' overall architecture. This appears to be a final polishing patch in the v10 series, with Newren providing his approval of the approach while raising a question about one flag's necessity.
