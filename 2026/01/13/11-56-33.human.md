This is a bugfix for the hook subsystem, addressing a regression in the pre-push hook behavior. The patch makes the `stdout_to_stderr` setting optional in the hook API, fixing a case where the pre-push hook was incorrectly having its stdout redirected to stderr.

The change adds a new `stdout_to_stderr` flag to the `run_hooks_opt` struct (defaulting to 1 for backward compatibility) and modifies the pre-push hook to explicitly set it to 0. This matches the historical behavior where pre-push hooks could use stdout normally, while maintaining the new default behavior of redirecting stdout to stderr for other hooks.

The patch is small and focused, with changes to just three files. It includes a clear explanation of the regression and rationale for the fix, plus attribution to both the reporter (Chris Darroch) and the suggestion from brian m. carlson.
