This is a bugfix patch in the ongoing `git-history` command series, addressing an edge case in the replay subsystem's handling of empty commit ranges. Elijah Newren provides a minor clarification to the commit message's explanation of when empty ranges can occur, noting that the issue applies to any branch tip, not just HEAD. The technical fix itself is straightforward: initializing the `merge_result.clean` flag to 1 (success) rather than 0 (failure) when no commits need replaying, since an empty range isn't actually a conflict case.

The patch is small (5 lines changed) and uncontroversial, with Newren approving the change after his suggested wording improvement. This represents one of the final polish items in the `git-history` series before potential integration, though larger questions about conflict handling design remain open for future subcommands. The change maintains consistency with Git's established patterns that Newren has been advocating for throughout the review.
