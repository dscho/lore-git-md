Bugfix targeting the pre-push hook performance regression. Kristoffer Haugsbakk reports that since commit 857f047e ("hook: allow overriding the ungroup option", 2025-12-26), pre-push hooks that read from stdin (even just a simple `cat` or empty loop) cause pushes to take over a minute instead of completing in seconds. The issue reproduces consistently across `master`, `next`, and `seen` branches.

The report includes a detailed bisection script that demonstrates the regression by timing out pushes with a 3-second limit when the problematic hook is present. The slowdown appears specific to hooks that process stdin - empty hooks or those using just `echo` work normally. The reporter suspects this relates to how stdin is handled after the mentioned commit.

This looks like a genuine performance bug introduced by recent hook infrastructure changes. The report is thorough, with clear reproduction steps and bisection evidence. The impact is significant for users with pre-push hooks that process stdin (a common pattern for validating pushes), making this worth prompt attention from hook subsystem maintainers.
