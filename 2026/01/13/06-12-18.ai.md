This is the final approval for the submodule path encoding series, confirming it's ready for merging after 10 iterations of review. Patrick Steinhardt gives his sign-off on v9, agreeing with Adrian Ratiu and Junio Hamano that the series has reached diminishing returns for further improvements. The implementation has been stable since v7, with v8 receiving production readiness confirmation from Josh Steadmon. The series introduces filesystem-safe handling of submodule paths through URL encoding and hashing fallbacks, with comprehensive collision resolution and configuration validation. All feedback from key reviewers (Patrick Steinhardt, Josh Steadmon, Phillip Wood) has been addressed across the versions, and the technical implementation in submodule.c, builtin/submodule--helper.c, and related files is now considered complete. This marks the conclusion of the multi-month effort to robustly handle submodule paths across all filesystems.
