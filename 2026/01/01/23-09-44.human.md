This is Junio Hamano's review of the final version (v10) of Harald Nordgren's patch series to show push branch divergence in `git status` output. The series has evolved significantly from its original approach of adding a new `status.goalBranch` config to instead using Git's existing pushRemote and tracking branch mechanisms.

Junio notes the patch organization still follows a "drunken walk" pattern where the steps don't build cleanly toward the final goal. He suggests restructuring into a more logical progression, though he approves of the technical direction to reuse `pushremote_for_branch()` rather than introducing new configuration.

The series is now in its final form after addressing all previous feedback, with robust test coverage and optimized implementation. It specifically requests reviews from Chris Torek and brian m. carlson to confirm alignment with their earlier suggestions. The maintainer's main remaining concern is about the patch organization rather than the technical approach.
