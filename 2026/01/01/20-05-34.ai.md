The implementation now shows divergence from upstream/main`-style output when the push target differs from the tracking branch, maintaining all optimizations from previous versions while fully aligning with Git's standard patterns. The test suite has been comprehensively updated for this approach, covering all scenarios with 296 lines of verification.
