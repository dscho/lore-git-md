This is a routine announcement for the 130th edition of Git Rev News, the monthly newsletter summarizing Git development activity. Christian Couder, one of the regular editors, shares that this edition was prepared with contributions from Lucas Seiki Oshiro and David Aguilar. The newsletter provides a curated overview of recent patches, discussions, and releases for those who want to stay informed without closely following the mailing list.

The announcement follows the project's standard format, including a link to the published edition and an invitation for contributions to the next issue. Work on edition #131 is already underway, with GitHub issue #817 tracking its progress. There are no technical discussions or controversies to note in this announcement.
