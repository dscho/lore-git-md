This is part 7 of Ezekiel Newren's 10-part series refactoring xdiff internals to enable future optimizations and Rust interoperability. The patch moves the `dstart` field from `xdfile_t` to `xdfenv_t` as `delta_start`, since this value logically applies to the diff environment (pair of files) rather than individual files.

The change is mechanical but touches several xdiff components - the histogram and patience diff algorithms, record cleanup logic, and type definitions. The justification is that `dstart` only makes sense in the context of comparing two files, so storing it in the environment structure is more appropriate. All callers are updated to use the new location.

This continues the series' pattern of restructuring xdiff internals while maintaining existing behavior. The change appears straightforward but will need review to confirm it doesn't subtly alter diff behavior, especially around edge cases in the diffing algorithms. As with previous patches, this prepares the xdiff subsystem for potential Rust components while improving code organization.
