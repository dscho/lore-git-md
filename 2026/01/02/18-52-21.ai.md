Refactoring patch in the xdiff subsystem as part of Ezekiel Newren's series to prepare for future optimizations. This is patch 7/10 in the series.

The patch moves the `dstart` field from `xdfile_t` to `xdfenv_t` (renaming it to `delta_start`), since this variable logically belongs to the diff environment that tracks both files being compared rather than being file-specific. The change affects several xdiff algorithms (histogram and patience diffs) and preparation functions, updating all references to use the new location.

This appears to be a mechanical change that maintains identical behavior while improving the logical organization of the diff state. The patch follows the series' pattern of restructuring xdiff internals to be more maintainable and Rust-compatible. As with previous patches in the series, careful review will be needed to verify no subtle behavioral changes were introduced, particularly in the diff algorithms' boundary calculations.
