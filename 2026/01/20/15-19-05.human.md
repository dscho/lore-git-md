This is the final part of a 3-patch series that's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically focusing on migrating repository-specific configuration variables out of global scope. The series introduces a new `struct repo_config_values` to hold configuration values that should be repository-scoped rather than global.

The current patch (v3) completes the series by moving the `branch.autoSetupMerge` configuration into the new struct, following the same pattern used in previous patches for `core.attributesFile` and `core.sparseCheckout`. The changes affect several commands including `branch`, `checkout`, `push`, and `submodule--helper`.

The implementation carefully maintains existing behavior while making the configuration repository-specific. The series has evolved through multiple iterations with input from Phillip Wood and others, and appears technically sound. The author (an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder) has addressed feedback from previous versions, improving commit messages and initialization sequencing.

While some larger architectural questions about config variable scoping remain open (as noted by Junio C Hamano), this series provides a concrete step forward in the `the_repository` removal effort by establishing patterns for future migrations. The changes are well-tested and touch about 20 files across the codebase, demonstrating the wide impact of moving configuration out of global scope.
