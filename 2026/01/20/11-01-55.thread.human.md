This thread began with Phillip Wood submitting a straightforward patch to consolidate his contributor identity in Git's mailmap file, mapping two alternate email addresses to his canonical one. The discussion took an interesting technical turn when Kristoffer Haugsbakk initially misunderstood how mailmap affects commit trailers, prompting clarification from both Junio Hamano and Jeff King.

Junio demonstrated through a test case that mailmap entries only affect author/committer metadata, not trailers like Signed-off-by - an intentional design choice since trailers may contain non-email content or omit addresses for privacy. Jeff provided deeper technical context, explaining that while mailmap doesn't change raw trailer contents, some commands like `git shortlog` do apply mailmap when displaying trailers by parsing them as idents. He also mentioned an old unmerged proof-of-concept for broader trailer handling.

In a follow-up, Kristoffer built on these explanations by proposing a hypothetical `.trailersmap` concept for normalizing trailer contents, though this remains a theoretical discussion without concrete implementation plans. The exchange has served as useful documentation of mailmap's current behavior and limitations while exploring potential future directions for identity normalization in Git. Phillip's original mailmap patch remains valid and unaffected by these broader discussions about trailer handling.
