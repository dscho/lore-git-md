This thread began with Phillip Wood submitting a straightforward mailmap patch to consolidate his contributor identity by mapping two alternate email addresses to his canonical one. Kristoffer Haugsbakk initially confirmed that Git remaps addresses in commit trailers, which would have made Phillip's patch fully effective. However, Junio C Hamano has now clarified that mailmap entries do not actually affect commit trailers, demonstrating this with a test case showing Signed-off-by trailers remain unchanged. 

Junio explains this is intentional behavior since trailers can contain non-email content or omit emails for privacy. While this means Phillip's mailmap entries won't affect trailers containing his alternate addresses, the patch remains valid for other attribution purposes. The discussion has helpfully clarified mailmap's current limitations while leaving room for potential future enhancements to trailer handling. With Junio's authoritative input, the technical question appears resolved, and Phillip's original identity consolidation patch can proceed with this clearer understanding of its scope.
