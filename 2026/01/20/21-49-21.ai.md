This is a follow-up in a documentation thread about conditional formatting in man pages. He suggests updating `Documentation/lint-gitlink.perl` to recognize this valid syntax.

The thread has already reached consensus on the technical approach, with Junio indicating the patch is ready for merging pending this final linting adjustment.
