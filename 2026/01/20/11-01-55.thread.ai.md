Phillip Wood submits a mailmap patch to consolidate his contributor identity by mapping two alternate email addresses (phillip.wood123@gmail.com and phillip.wood@talktalk.net) to his canonical address (phillip.wood@dunelm.org.uk). While all his commits use the primary address, these alternates appear in some commit trailers. The patch adds two standard mailmap entries to .mailmap, based on commit b5c409c40f and from Phillip's personal branch pw/pwood-mailmap/v1.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk initially confirmed that Git remaps addresses in commit trailers, but Junio C Hamano provides a counter-example showing trailers are not actually modified by .mailmap entries. Junio demonstrates this with a test case where a mailmap entry for his own email does not affect Signed-off-by trailer output, noting this is expected behavior since trailers may contain non-email content (like "closes:" references) or intentionally omit emails for privacy reasons. He leaves open whether Kristoffer was instead confirming that the project had previously discussed implementing such trailer remapping, though no such implementation exists currently.

The thread clarifies mailmap's current limitations regarding trailer remapping while maintaining focus on accurate contributor mapping. Phillip's original patch remains valid and useful for standard mailmap purposes, though the discussion reveals an edge case where trailer attribution won't be normalized. Junio's response effectively closes the technical question while keeping the door open for future discussion about potential mailmap enhancements.
