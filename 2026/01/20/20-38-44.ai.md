This is a documentation patch in the ongoing `git worktree` UX improvement series. The thread has largely settled on technical content but stalled on stylistic presentation of bare repository examples. Maintainer Junio Hamano notes the discussion has been inactive for two months and plans to drop the topic from the integration branch, though remains open to a refreshed submission.

The key remaining question is how to document the supported-but-not-recommended practice of creating worktrees from bare repositories. Reviewers have debated two approaches: Eric Sunshine's `-C` with sibling directories versus Michal Suchánek's `--git-dir` with explicit paths. While the technical implementations are equally valid, the debate centers on which makes a clearer teaching example.

The patch modifies `Documentation/git-worktree.txt` to clarify that while nested worktrees are technically possible (a point settled earlier in the thread), bare repository worktrees are supported but require careful path specification. The maintainer's intervention suggests this version may need a refresh to incorporate the accumulated feedback before reconsideration, particularly around example formatting and the explicit support declaration Eric Sunshine requested. The thread shows consensus on the documentation's technical content but needs resolution on pedagogical presentation.
