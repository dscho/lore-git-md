The patch modifies two files: it adds a `git-replay` attribute in `git-replay.adoc` and updates the conditional logic in `rev-list-options.adoc` to skip the "Commit Formatting" section when either `git-shortlog` or `git-replay` is defined. The multiple-attribute syntax (using commas for "any" and "+" for "all") is confirmed to work with both Python and Ruby AsciiDoc processors, though this marks its first use in Git's docs.

The change follows the same pattern previously established for `git shortlog`, keeping manual pages focused on relevant content by excluding unused sections.
