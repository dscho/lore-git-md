This is a clarification in an ongoing discussion about mailmap functionality for commit trailers. Junio C Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's confirmation that Git remaps addresses in commit trailers, providing a counter-example showing that trailers are not actually modified by .mailmap entries. He demonstrates this with a test case where a mailmap entry for his own email does not affect the Signed-off-by trailer output.

Junio notes this behavior is expected since trailers may contain non-email content (like "closes:" references) or intentionally omit emails for privacy reasons. He leaves open whether Kristoffer was instead confirming that the project had previously discussed implementing such trailer remapping, though no such implementation exists currently.

The exchange provides important technical clarification about mailmap's limitations while maintaining the thread's focus on accurate contributor mapping. Junio's response effectively closes the question about trailer remapping while keeping the door open for future discussion about potential enhancements to mailmap functionality.
