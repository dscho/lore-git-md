This is a CI optimization follow-up in the ongoing effort to skip unnecessary tests in leak-checking jobs. Phillip Wood replies to Junio C Hamano's implementation of skipping CVS and P4 tests (adding to Johannes Schindelin's earlier git-svn test skip), noting the patch looks good and asking whether these tests should also be skipped in the ASan job.

The thread has already established consensus around skipping Perl-based tests (git-svn, CVS, git-p4) in memory-leak detection contexts where they provide minimal value. Junio's patch follows the established pattern by adding `NO_CVS_TESTS` and `NO_P4_TESTS` variables that trigger early exits in their respective test library files, saving ~24 minutes of CI time. Phillip's question about extending this to ASan jobs suggests the optimization might have broader applicability, though that remains future work.

The exchange demonstrates the project's practical approach to CI efficiency - identifying low-value tests in specialized contexts and implementing clean skips through established mechanisms. No controversy here; this is incremental improvement building on prior work.
