CI optimization follow-up discussing test exclusions for leak-checking jobs.

Junio C Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's suggestion about extending the test-skipping optimization to ASan (AddressSanitizer) jobs, agreeing it's a good idea. He also raises a broader question about whether the current approach of individual `NO_*_TESTS` variables could be replaced with a more general mechanism like `NO_FOREIGN_CODE_TESTS`, noting that the rationale for skipping these tests isn't about them being foreign SCM tests per se, but rather that they exercise little Git code that would benefit from leak checking. The discussion remains open-ended on both points - whether to apply the same exclusions to ASan jobs and whether to consolidate the skip variables.

This continues the thread's theme of practical CI optimizations, now considering both broader applicability and potential generalization of the mechanism. The key files involved remain `ci/lib.sh`, `t/lib-cvs.sh`, and `t/lib-git-p4.sh`, with possible future changes to the ASan job configuration. Junio's response indicates the project's willingness to refine these time-saving measures further while being thoughtful about the underlying rationale.
