This is a contributor housekeeping patch discussion that clarifies mailmap behavior.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk corrects his earlier statement about mailmap affecting commit trailers, acknowledging he confused it with git-shortlog behavior. The thread was prompted by Phillip Wood's patch to consolidate his contributor identity in .mailmap, but took a detour into whether mailmap entries affect commit trailers (they don't, as Junio demonstrated with a test case).

The exchange serves as a useful clarification of mailmap's current limitations - while it normalizes author/committer names in most contexts, it intentionally leaves trailers (like Signed-off-by) untouched. This preserves privacy for those who omit emails in trailers and maintains the integrity of non-email trailer content. The original mailmap patch remains valid for its intended purpose of standardizing contributor attribution in the main commit metadata.

The discussion reveals an interesting edge case but doesn't block Phillip's patch, which the thread originated. It may prompt future discussion about whether trailer normalization should be an opt-in feature, but for now the behavior is clearly documented through this exchange.
