The author proposes changing the type of flag variables in symlinks.c from `int` to `unsigned int` since they are used strictly as bitmasks. The change aims to prevent potential undefined behavior with shift operations and logic errors if the most significant bit is used.

The commit message clearly explains the rationale, though it doesn't cite any specific instances where the current signed type has caused problems.
