This is a discussion about hook access control, specifically around distinguishing push sources in pre-receive hooks when multiple automated systems share the same user identity. Randall S. Becker responds to Chris Packham's inquiry about detecting push origins in hooks.

Randall acknowledges the challenge but notes there's no direct Git mechanism to identify push sources in hooks. His more practical recommendation is to use firewall rules to block CI system pushes at the network level rather than trying to distinguish them within Git.
