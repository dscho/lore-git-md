This is a follow-up in a thread about contributor housekeeping (mailmap updates). Kristoffer Haugsbakk corrects his earlier mistaken claim that Git remaps email addresses in commit trailers, clarifying that he confused the behavior with git-shortlog's mailmap handling. 

The exchange reveals that while .mailmap entries normalize author/committer names and emails in the main commit metadata, they do not affect email addresses in trailers like Signed-off-by. Junio Hamano had demonstrated this with a test case, noting this is intentional since trailers may contain non-email content or intentionally omit emails. 

Kristoffer acknowledges his error with good humor ("the curse of invented knowledge"), confirming the current limitation of mailmap functionality. The thread remains focused on accurate contributor attribution while documenting this edge case in Git's mailmap behavior. No changes to Phillip Wood's original mailmap patch are needed, as it correctly handles the cases mailmap is designed for.
