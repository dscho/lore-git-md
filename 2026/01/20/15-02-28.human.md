This is patch 3/10 in Ezekiel Newren's series refactoring the xdiff subsystem, with an eye toward future optimizations and Rust interoperability. Phillip Wood's review points out that the patch changes the line classification approach - instead of guessing line counts upfront (which required partial scanning), it now does a full scan to split lines followed by a separate classification pass. Phillip questions whether eliminating the guesswork actually improves performance, given the extra pass, and notes that error handling for classification failures appears to have been lost in the transition.

The patch removes the `xdl_guess_lines()` function entirely, as its heuristic is no longer used. The change appears to be part of a broader effort to make the diff algorithm's behavior more predictable by removing estimation steps in favor of complete processing. Phillip's review suggests the performance tradeoffs may need closer examination, and the missing error handling will need to be addressed in a follow-up revision.
