This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 14-part ODB abstraction series, which is nearing final integration. The patch performs a mechanical rename of `FOR_EACH_OBJECT_*` flags to use an `ODB_` prefix, preparing for a new `odb_for_each_object()` function that will be introduced later in the series. 

The changeset is straightforward but wide-ranging, touching 11 files across core subsystems (object-file, packfile, reachable) and commands (cat-file, pack-objects, commit-graph). The rename ensures naming consistency between the upcoming function and its associated flags. 

This foundational change has already undergone thorough review from Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak, with Junio Hamano indicating readiness to merge the series pending minor procedural fixes. The patch appears well-crafted and clearly motivated as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort to make Git's object storage layer backend-agnostic.
