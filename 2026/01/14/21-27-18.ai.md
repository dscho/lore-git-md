This is a technical discussion about hook subsystem behavior, specifically addressing the `ungroup` flag initialization in the broader context of hook execution improvements.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Adrian Ratiu's v3 patch that changes the `ungroup` flag behavior to be opt-out rather than opt-in. While acknowledging the patch as an improvement over the current state in 'seen', Peff expresses reservations about the overall approach taken in the receive-pack hook conversion. He points out that the current solution buffers hook stderr (a regression from the previous async muxer thread approach) and references earlier deadlock concerns.

Peff suggests an alternative approach where receive-pack would maintain its muxer thread and the hook API would attach stderr to that descriptor, avoiding the need for `ungroup` flag manipulation entirely. He positions this as coming from someone not deeply involved in the series but with extensive experience dealing with I/O deadlocks in Git.

The email highlights an ongoing tension between the current fix (which addresses immediate regression) and what Peff sees as a more architecturally sound solution (maintaining async behavior). This suggests the thread may continue with discussion about whether to stick with the buffered approach or revisit the async model for receive-pack hooks. The deadlock risks Peff mentions remain an important consideration regardless of which path is taken.
