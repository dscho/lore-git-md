This is a co-authored patch in a bugfix series addressing regression in error message reporting for batched reference updates. The patch (3/6) is a preparatory change that passes rejection details through to the callback function `ref_transaction_for_each_rejected_update()`, building on the infrastructure added in patch 2/6. Jeff King confirms the approach is reasonable and offers his sign-off, indicating this is a clean continuation of earlier collaborative work.

The change is part of a well-structured series that has already been reviewed and merged, with Junio Hamano validating the overall approach. This particular patch is a mechanical step that enables the subsequent patches (4/6-6/6) to display detailed error messages in `git update-ref`, `git fetch`, and `git receive-pack`. The series as a whole restores lost debugging information that was accidentally dropped during earlier refactoring work.
