This is a performance patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing repack machinery overhaul series, specifically addressing geometric repacking with promisor packs. Toon Claes provides a positive review of the solution, confirming it makes sense and works as intended.

The patch implements separate geometric progression for promisor packs, solving the incompatibility between geometric repacking and promisor remotes. The solution avoids the problematic alternatives of either never repacking promisor packs or expanding the set of promisor objects. The implementation tracks promisor packs separately in the pack geometry structure and applies the same geometric progression logic to them independently.

Key technical changes include extending struct pack_geometry to track promisor packs separately, adding pack_geometry_repack_promisors() to handle the promisor-specific repacking, and modifying the geometric split calculation to work on both regular and promisor packs. The patch includes comprehensive test coverage in t7703-repack-geometric.sh that verifies the correct merging of promisor packs while maintaining their special status.
