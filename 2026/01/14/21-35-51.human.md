This is a performance optimization for the sparse-checkout pattern sorting, with Jeff King (Peff) providing review feedback on an initial patch from amisha.

The original patch from amisha changed the pattern collection in `builtin/sparse-checkout.c` from using `string_list_insert()` (which performs an O(n^2) insertion sort) to `string_list_append()` followed by a single sort (O(n log n)). Jeff agrees this is an obvious improvement but notes two additional spots in `write_cone_to_file()` that likely have the same issue.

Jeff also provides some general patch submission advice to amisha about line wrapping and the importance of using a distinctive identity in the Signed-off-by trailer. His tone is constructive and helpful to a new contributor while still being direct about the technical points.

The change itself is small and focused - just a one-line modification to the pattern collection logic. Given Jeff's involvement in reviewing and his identification of additional similar cases, this seems likely to be picked up quickly as part of the sparse-checkout optimization work.
