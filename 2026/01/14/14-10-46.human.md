Maintainer workflow note from Junio C Hamano.

This is a brief follow-up in the thread about submodule fetching behavior. Junio responds to D. Ben Knoble's suggestion that minor style issues in the patch could be fixed during integration. While technically true, Junio expresses a preference that contributors address such issues themselves rather than relying on the maintainer to clean them up, noting the maintainer-to-contributor ratio makes this unsustainable.

The comment doesn't introduce new technical content about the submodule changes themselves, which appear technically sound based on the thread summary. Rather, it's a gentle reminder about project workflow expectations - that contributors should polish their patches fully before submission, even for minor style issues. This reflects Git's culture of valuing clean, complete contributions that don't require janitorial work from the maintainer.
