This is part 14 of Taylor Blau's 18-patch series implementing MIDX compaction with bitmap support. The series has already addressed major review feedback and established technical foundations including a new MIDX v2 format, safe arithmetic utilities, and pack permutation handling.

This patch refactors how pack IDs are enumerated in `fill_packs_from_midx()`. Rather than iterating from 0 and adjusting the index, it now directly uses the actual `pack_int_id` range. This makes the code more straightforward by removing an extra calculation step when looking up packs.

The change is small (just 3 lines modified) but improves clarity in the pack-filling logic that will be used during MIDX operations. It builds on the previous pack-filling abstraction introduced in patch 13/18, continuing the series' pattern of careful API evolution while maintaining backward compatibility.
