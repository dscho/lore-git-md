The second patch in Taylor Blau's 18-part MIDX compaction series renames `get_midx_checksum()` to `midx_get_checksum_hash()` to follow Git's naming conventions for struct-related functions. This is a straightforward refactoring that also clarifies the function's behavior - the new name makes it explicit that it returns a raw hash rather than a hex-encoded string. 

The change is mechanical, touching the function declaration in midx.h and updating all callers across midx-write.c, midx.c, pack-bitmap.c, pack-revindex.c, and test helper code. The patch was suggested by Patrick Steinhardt during review of the series and helps prepare for a subsequent change that will introduce a hex-encoded variant of the function.

This continues the series that aims to implement `git multi-pack-index compact` functionality while maintaining bitmap coverage. The current patch is purely a naming improvement with no functional changes, keeping with the incremental approach seen throughout the series.
