This is a bugfix discussion in the ongoing hook subsystem improvements, specifically addressing the `ungroup` flag behavior in hook execution. Jeff King (Peff) is responding to Adrian Ratiu's v3 patch that changes the `ungroup` flag from opt-in to opt-out to fix a regression in pre-push hooks.

Peff acknowledges the patch is an improvement over the current state in Junio's 'seen' branch, though he expresses broader concerns about the architectural approach taken in recent hook API changes. He points out that the current solution buffers hook stderr output (a regression from the previous async muxer thread approach) and could still face deadlock issues mentioned earlier in the thread.

While not blocking the immediate fix, Peff suggests a more robust long-term solution would be to maintain the async muxer thread approach from receive-pack and adapt the hook API to work with it, rather than needing to manage the `ungroup` flag. He positions this as coming from his experience with I/O deadlocks in Git, though notes he hasn't been closely following this series.

The exchange shows the patch is technically sound for its immediate purpose but hints at deeper architectural questions about hook execution that may need revisiting. Peff's comments serve as a marker that while this fixes the regression, the team may want to reconsider the broader approach in future work.
