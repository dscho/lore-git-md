Bugfix discussion regarding hook subsystem's stream handling, specifically addressing a regression in pre-push hook behavior and uncovering a deeper issue with feed_pipe interaction.

Jeff King (Peff) joins the thread with a detailed technical analysis of the v2 patch from Adrian Ratiu. While not previously involved in the discussion, Peff identifies two distinct issues that were conflated in the fix:

1. The stdout/stderr separation for pre-push hooks (addressed by `stdout_to_stderr=0`)
2. A separate stdin feeding problem that manifests when using `feed_pipe` without `ungroup=1`

Peff demonstrates through strace analysis that the current implementation causes inefficient timeouts during stdin feeding due to poll() calls when ungrouped. He suggests this reveals a more fundamental design issue - that `feed_pipe` should either require `ungroup=1` or the poll mechanism needs enhancement to properly monitor the child's input pipe.

The analysis suggests the current patch may be masking the stdin feeding issue rather than properly fixing it. Peff proposes two potential solutions:
- Require ungroup when using feed_pipe (possibly via BUG())
- Enhance the poll mechanism to properly handle child input readiness

This intervention adds significant technical depth to the discussion, moving beyond the immediate regression fix to question the hook subsystem's underlying design for handling child process I/O. While the original stdout/stderr separation issue appears properly addressed, Peff's analysis suggests the solution may have inadvertently papered over a separate but related design flaw in the hook execution machinery.
