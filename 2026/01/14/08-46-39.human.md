Bugfix addressing two separate hook subsystem issues in the pre-push hook. This is Adrian Ratiu's response to Jeff King's review of v2, proposing to split the fixes into two patches for clearer separation.

The first issue (from Chris Darroch) involves stdout/stderr separation in pre-push hooks, which was broken by recent changes that defaulted to merging streams. The v2 patch already addressed this with a new `stdout_to_stderr` flag and explicit handling for pre-push hooks.

The second issue (from Kristoffer Haugsbakk) involves stdin feeding performance when `ungroup=0`, where the poll mechanism introduces significant delays. Jeff King's analysis shows the current implementation hits poll timeouts for each ref, making the hook execution unnecessarily slow. Adrian acknowledges this as a separate problem and plans to address it in v3 by either batching stdin writes (similar to the receive hook implementation) or potentially enforcing `ungroup=1` when using feed_pipe.

The patch is part of ongoing hook subsystem work but stands independently from Adrian's larger hook configuration effort. With Kristoffer's ack and Jeff's detailed review, the stdout/stderr portion appears ready for merging, while the stdin feeding optimization will follow in a separate patch.
