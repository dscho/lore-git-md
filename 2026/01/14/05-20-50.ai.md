This is a status update from Patrick Steinhardt regarding two of his ongoing patch series in Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" report. The first series (ps/read-object-info-improvements) refactors Git's object-info API with 8 commits that clean up how object information is read from packfiles, including dropping unnecessary repository parameters and improving the handling of packed object metadata. Patrick indicates this series is ready for merging after multiple review iterations and the addition of a production-tested bug fix.

The second series (ps/packfile-store-in-odb-source) is a 12-commit refactoring that moves the packfile store data structure into the object database source as part of Patrick's larger ODB abstraction effort. This continues his work to make Git's object storage layer more modular. Patrick confirms he's fine with this series moving to 'next'.

Patrick also notes that another of his series about geometric repacking with promisor packs doesn't appear in 'seen' yet and asks whether this omission was intentional. The email demonstrates Patrick's characteristic thoroughness in tracking his contributions through the integration process while respecting Junio's maintainer workflow. Both series represent incremental but important steps in Patrick's long-term architectural improvements to Git's object storage subsystem.
