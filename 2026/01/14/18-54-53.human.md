Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about push tracking display in `git status`. This is a follow-up to Harald Nordgren's merged series that added push tracking information, where Jeff King had raised concerns about the feature being noisy for his mirror workflow.

Junio now proposes making the push tracking display optional via a new `status.compareBranches` configuration. The suggested syntax would allow users to explicitly specify which branches to compare against (e.g. `@{upstream} @{push}`), with the default being just `@{upstream}` to maintain backward compatibility. He frames this as a way to avoid regression for users who don't want the extra output while still supporting those who do.

The email suggests the configuration could be made extensible to support future comparison types beyond just push tracking. Junio is specifically asking whether Harald's implementation aligns with using `@{push}` directly or if there are differences that would affect this design.

This represents a shift in the discussion from debating the merits of push tracking display to proposing a concrete mechanism to make it configurable. Junio's position as maintainer gives this suggestion particular weight in the thread's trajectory.
