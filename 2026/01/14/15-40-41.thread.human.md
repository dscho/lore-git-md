This thread addressed a regression where batched reference updates in `git update-ref`, `git fetch`, and `git receive-pack` lost detailed error messages, making failures harder to debug. Karthik Nayak and Jeff King collaborated on a 6-patch series that restored this functionality by adding a `rejection_details` field to track errors through the stack and updating all three ref backends to pass through detailed messages. The series was reviewed and merged by Junio Hamano, with Jeff King formally acknowledging co-authorship of one patch.

After merging, Jeff King raised a refinement point about error message formatting in `git update-ref --batch-updates`, noting the need to balance machine-readable output (documented in the man page) with the new detailed error information. He suggested a short-term solution of printing raw details to stderr while preserving the structured format, acknowledging this may need future iteration. The discussion shows the project's careful attention to both functionality and interface stability, even for edge cases in error reporting. While the core fix is complete, this follow-up highlights how the team continues to polish behavior after initial merging.
