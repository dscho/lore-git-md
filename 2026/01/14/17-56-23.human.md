This is a bugfix patch addressing performance and deadlock issues in Git's hook subsystem, specifically around stdin feeding and stderr handling. The patch is part of an ongoing thread where multiple hook-related issues are being addressed in separate commits.

Adrian Ratiu responds to Jeff King's detailed analysis showing two concrete problems: a deadlock scenario when pipe buffers fill up during hook execution, and a 100ms performance penalty when feeding more than 500 lines to a hook. The examples demonstrate these issues clearly in pre-receive hook scenarios.

The proposed solution involves implementing a proper poll() loop to handle simultaneous stdin feeding and stderr reading, which would resolve both the deadlock risk and the performance impact. For client-side hooks, Ratiu plans to send a v3 series fixing the previously reported stdout/stderr separation regressions.

This patch shows good technical engagement with the reviewers' findings and proposes a reasonable path forward.
