This is a follow-up discussion in Olamide Bello's RFC patch series about moving repository-specific config values from global variables to `struct repo_config_values` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The series previously addressed `core.attributesFile` and `core.sparseCheckout`; this email responds to Junio Hamano's question about which config values should be repository-scoped versus system/user-scoped.

Bello clarifies that while some configs like identity and mailmap settings are user/system-wide (and thus should remain global), most core, branch, and push configs are repository-specific and therefore good candidates for moving into the new struct. The response focuses specifically on the scope question raised in Junio's feedback, without yet addressing his other technical concerns about initialization and access patterns.
