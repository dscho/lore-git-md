This is part 12/18 of Taylor Blau's MIDX compaction series, introducing a helper function for pack permutation handling in multi-pack-index writing. The helper centralizes all `pack_perm` array accesses, which will make it easier to implement the planned compaction logic in later patches. The change is purely internal - no user-visible behavior is modified.
