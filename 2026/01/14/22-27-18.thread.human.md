Lorenzo Pegorari's GSoC patch series addresses a long-standing issue with UTF-8 filename truncation in Git's diffstat output, where multi-byte characters were being incorrectly handled. The two-patch series introduces proper width calculation using `utf8_width()` and adds comprehensive test coverage for various edge cases involving Chinese characters and different width settings. Version 2 of the series incorporated Junio C Hamano's feedback by fixing a boundary condition and expanding test coverage.

The changes appear technically sound and ready for merging, with Junio recently catching only a minor build system inconsistency in the test filename (now corrected). This was the final polish item - the core functionality and test coverage remain robust, properly handling UTF-8 display widths during diffstat truncation. The series solves a real internationalization pain point while being carefully scoped to avoid introducing new edge cases, making it a strong candidate for inclusion in an upcoming release.
