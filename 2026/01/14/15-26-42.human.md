Performance patch in Patrick Steinhardt's geometric repacking series, specifically addressing promisor pack handling. This is the final patch in a 5-part series that enables geometric repacking to work properly with promisor remotes in partial clones.

The core issue is that geometric repacking currently fails when promisor packs are present because it tries to combine incompatible pack-objects flags. The patch implements a clean solution by maintaining separate geometric progressions for promisor and non-promisor packs, ensuring promisor objects remain properly tracked while still getting the benefits of geometric repacking.

Key technical aspects:
- Extends the pack_geometry struct to track promisor packs separately
- Adds new pack_geometry_repack_promisors() function
- Maintains existing behavior for non-promisor packs
- Includes comprehensive test coverage in t7703-repack-geometric.sh
