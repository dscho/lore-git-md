This is a bugfix series addressing two regressions in Git's hook subsystem, part of an ongoing thread about hook execution issues. The patches come from Adrian Ratiu with input from Jeff King (Peff) and others.

The first patch fixes a regression where pre-push hooks lost their ability to keep stdout and stderr separate, which broke existing hooks relying on this behavior. It adds a `stdout_to_stderr` flag to control stream merging, defaulting to merged streams for backward compatibility while explicitly disabling merging for pre-push hooks.

The second patch changes the `ungroup` flag's default from opt-in to opt-out, correcting an initialization issue that affected hook output handling. This change aligns with historical behavior and addresses performance concerns raised by Jeff King about pipe buffering.

The series is well-tested with extensive additions to `t/t1800-hook.sh` and Adrian notes that server-side I/O improvements (including poll() implementation) will come separately. The fixes are focused and clearly motivated, with good commit messages explaining both the problems and solutions. This looks ready for inclusion, representing the consensus reached in the thread about maintaining backward compatibility while fixing the regressions.
