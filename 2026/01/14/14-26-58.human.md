This is a status update in the ongoing "What's cooking" thread, where Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt about two of Patrick's patch series related to Git's object storage internals.

Junio confirms that Patrick's first series (ps/read-object-info-improvements) appears ready after multiple iterations and production testing, though he notes the lack of recent review feedback makes it harder to judge if further refinements are needed. This series cleans up the object-info API and packed object handling, with the latest version including a production-tested bug fix.

Regarding Patrick's second series (ps/packfile-store-in-odb-source), Junio explains it hasn't been merged into the 'seen' branch yet because earlier feedback indicated some confusion about parts of the implementation. This series is part of Patrick's larger effort to make Git's object storage more modular by moving packfile store structures into the object database source.

The exchange shows Junio's careful approach to merging - he prefers to see clear consensus or diminishing returns on feedback before advancing patches, even for well-tested series from trusted contributors like Patrick. The thread also highlights how the "What's cooking" reports serve as a coordination point for tracking the status of multiple concurrent development efforts.
