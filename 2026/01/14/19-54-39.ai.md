This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's MIDX compaction series (patch 8/18). It addresses a potential issue with how bitmapped pack positions are assigned during MIDX writing.

The patch removes reliance on `ctx->pack_perm` when setting `bitmap_pos` values, instead directly indexing into `ctx->info`. This change prepares for future compaction work where `pack_perm` indexing could become more complex. The current behavior works by coincidence since `pack_perm` happens to cover the full range, but the direct approach is more straightforward and avoids potential issues when compaction ranges are introduced.

The change is minimal (just one line modified) but strategically important for the series' larger goal of implementing `git multi-pack-index compact` functionality. It follows the pattern seen in earlier patches of carefully cleaning up internal APIs before introducing new features.
