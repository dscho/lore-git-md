Performance optimization for sparse-checkout list operation.

This small patch from Amisha targets the `sparse-checkout list` command, changing how patterns are collected from O(n²) to O(n log n) time. The optimization works by first appending all patterns unsorted (O(n)) and then sorting them once at the end (O(n log n)), rather than maintaining sorted order throughout with `string_list_insert()` (O(n²) total).

The commit message explains the rationale clearly: since the patterns come from a hashmap (which guarantees uniqueness), it's safe to use the simpler `string_list_append()` and defer sorting. The change is minimal - just one line modified in `builtin/sparse-checkout.c` - but could provide noticeable speedups for repositories with many sparse-checkout patterns.
