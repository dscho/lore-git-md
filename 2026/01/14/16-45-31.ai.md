This is the final patch in a bugfix series addressing a regression in error message reporting for batched reference updates. The series, co-authored by Karthik Nayak and Jeff King, restores detailed error messages that were lost when batched updates were introduced to `git update-ref --batch-updates`, `git fetch`, and `git receive-pack`.

The changes span the entire refs stack, adding a `rejection_details` field to `struct ref_update` and modifying callback signatures to propagate error messages through all three ref backends (files, packed, reftable). The series has been methodically progressing from infrastructure changes to user-facing fixes, with this final patch completing the work by fixing `git receive-pack`.

Junio Hamano has reviewed the series and indicates he will queue it for merging. The collaboration between Karthik Nayak (who drove the fix) and Jeff King (who provided the initial patch), based on a report from Elijah Newren, demonstrates the project's effective response to regressions. The changes maintain backward compatibility while restoring functionality that was unintentionally lost during the batched updates implementation.
