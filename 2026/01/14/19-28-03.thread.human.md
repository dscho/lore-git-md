This thread discusses optimizing the sparse-checkout pattern sorting from O(n^2) to O(n log n) by replacing string_list_insert() with string_list_append() followed by sorting. The original patch from amisha focused on one location in builtin/sparse-checkout.c, providing a clear performance benefit.

Jeff King (Peff) has now reviewed the patch, noting that while the optimization is sound, it should be extended to two additional similar cases in write_cone_to_file() that exhibit the same inefficiency. He also provided minor feedback on the commit message formatting, though considers it acceptable as-is. The discussion suggests this was likely an oversight in the original implementation rather than a deliberate choice, since the sorting occurs right after insertion in all cases. 

The thread appears headed toward an updated patch that will apply this optimization consistently across all three locations. Given Peff's thorough review and the straightforward nature of the changes, this seems likely to progress smoothly once the additional cases are addressed.
