This is a performance optimization patch targeting the sparse-checkout subsystem. The change improves the time complexity of building a sorted list of patterns from O(n^2) to O(n log n) by first collecting all entries unsorted and then sorting them in a single pass.

The patch modifies `builtin/sparse-checkout.c` to use `string_list_append()` instead of `string_list_insert()` when gathering patterns from a hashmap. Since hashmap entries are already unique, the uniqueness guarantee of `string_list_insert()` isn't needed, and the more efficient append-then-sort approach is safe. The author notes this rationale in the commit message.
