This is patch 17/18 in Taylor Blau's MIDX compaction series, introducing the core `git multi-pack-index compact` functionality. The feature allows combining a range of adjacent MIDX layers into a single layer while preserving the MIDX chain structure and maintaining bitmap compatibility.

The patch adds a new `compact` subcommand that takes two MIDX layer checksums as arguments, defining the range to compact. Key technical aspects include:
- The MIDX chain structure by replacing compacted layers rather than appending
- Bitmap validity for layers after the compacted range
- Pack ordering (with preferred packs first) to maintain bitmap positions
- A new `fill_packs_from_midx_range()` helper to gather packs from multiple layers

The implementation builds on previous preparatory work in the series (MIDX v2 format, safe arithmetic utilities, pack permutation handling) and includes tests verifying:
- Correct pack selection from the specified range
- Preservation of pack order (even non-lexicographic)
- Object uniqueness across layers
- Error handling for invalid ranges and v1 format attempts
