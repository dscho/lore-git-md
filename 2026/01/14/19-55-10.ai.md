This is a feature patch implementing MIDX compaction functionality, part of Taylor Blau's ongoing multi-pack-index improvements series (17/18). The patch introduces a new `git multi-pack-index compact` subcommand that combines adjacent MIDX layers into a single layer while preserving the MIDX chain structure and bitmap compatibility.

The key technical aspects are:
- New `compact` subcommand takes two MIDX layer checksums as range endpoints
- Preserves pseudo-pack order across compaction while allowing preferred packs to stay first
- Adjusts pack_int_id mappings to maintain bitmap validity
- Replaces compacted layers in the chain rather than appending
- Requires MIDX v2 format for its relaxed pack ordering requirements
- Includes comprehensive tests for both lex-ordered and non-lex-ordered pack scenarios
- Adds error handling for invalid compaction ranges and version mismatches
- Documents the feature in git-multi-pack-index.adoc

The implementation builds on previous preparatory work in the series, particularly the MIDX v2 format support and pack permutation handling.
