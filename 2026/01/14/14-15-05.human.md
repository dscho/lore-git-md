Jeff King (Peff) shares his personal workflow where he maintains a mirror repository for CI purposes, pushing to it occasionally while primarily fetching from the canonical upstream. He notes the awkwardness of a bare `git push` defaulting to the upstream remote (where he lacks write access) rather than his mirror. The discussion highlights `@{push}` as a useful tool for comparing iterations of a branch, with Jeff demonstrating how he uses `git range-diff origin @{push} HEAD` to review changes before sending patches to the list.
