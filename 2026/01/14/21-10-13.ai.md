This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about configurable branch comparison display in `git status`. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Junio Hamano's proposal for a flexible `status.compareBranches` configuration.

Peff acknowledges the value of making the push-tracking display optional but notes the trade-off between discoverability and user experience. While agreeing with Junio's configurable design, he expresses skepticism about needing comparisons beyond `@{upstream}` and `@{push}`, though he recognizes the flexibility could accommodate future needs.

The technical discussion centers on:
- The proposed `status.compareBranches` configuration syntax
- Default behavior considerations (opt-in vs opt-out)
- Edge case handling for duplicate comparisons
- Potential extension points for comparing against arbitrary branches

This exchange represents the configuration design phase of the thread, building on the already-merged implementation of push tracking display. The participants are converging on a solution that maintains backward compatibility while providing flexibility for different workflows.
