CI optimization follow-up. Junio C Hamano builds on Phillip Wood's observation that the CVS and Perforce (p4) tests also consume significant CI time (~24 minutes) without contributing meaningful leak-check coverage, similar to the earlier git-svn test optimization. His patch introduces `NO_CVS_TESTS` and `NO_P4_TESTS` environment variables to skip these tests during leak-checking jobs, mirroring the approach taken for SVN tests in the previous patch. The change modifies `ci/lib.sh` to set these flags and updates the test setup files (`t/lib-cvs.sh` and `t/lib-git-p4.sh`) to respect them. This uncontroversial change follows the same pattern as the SVN optimization and will likely be merged without further discussion, though Phillip's suggestion about broader `NO_PYTHON`/`NO_PERL` optimizations remains open for future consideration.
