CI optimization follow-up discussing additional test exclusions.

Phillip Wood builds on Johannes Schindelin's recent patch that skipped git-svn tests in leak-checking CI jobs (saving ~42 minutes per run). He notes that p4 and cvs tests add another 24 minutes of runtime while similarly offering little leak-detection value, since they also rely on Perl/Python rather than C. Phillip suggests considering NO_PYTHON and NO_PERL flags for leak tests, though acknowledges this would be a broader change than the targeted NO_SVN_TESTS approach Johannes implemented. The thread shows consensus around optimizing CI runtime by focusing leak checks on C code paths, with Phillip's comment extending the discussion to other non-C test areas that could potentially be excluded. No concrete proposal yet for the p4/cvs case - this appears to be thinking aloud rather than requesting immediate action.
