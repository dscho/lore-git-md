This is a follow-up discussion about submission mechanics for Harald Nordgren's now-merged feature adding configurable branch comparison to `git status`. The thread had previously covered the technical implementation of the `status.compareBranches` configuration option, which allows comparing against multiple branches including `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` with context-aware advice messages.

In this brief exchange, Junio Hamano clarifies that the patches were submitted via GitGitGadget (GGG) rather than directly with `git send-email`, explaining the presence of some CC addresses in the headers that wouldn't appear with direct email submission. Harald Nordgren confirms this and acknowledges the submission method.

The discussion is purely procedural at this point, touching on mailing list workflow details rather than the technical content of the feature itself. The core functionality is already merged and will appear in the next release, with only minor optimizations (switching from `string_list` to `strset` for deduplication) and documentation remaining as follow-up tasks. This email serves to close out the submission-method clarification that arose during the final stages of the patch series' review.
