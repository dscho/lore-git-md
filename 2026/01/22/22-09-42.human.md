This is a follow-up from Junio C Hamano on Amisha Chhajed's RFC patch series introducing `string_list_sort_u`. The series aims to combine the common pattern of calling both `string_list_sort` and `string_list_remove_duplicates` into a single utility function.

Junio responds to Amisha's concern about lacking unit tests for the new function by pointing out that while `string_list_sort` lacks tests, `string_list_remove_duplicates` does have coverage in `t/unit-tests/u-string-list.c`. He encourages adding tests for the new combined function, noting that unit tests are relatively lightweight to run.

The response is brief and focused on testing strategy, suggesting Junio sees this as a prerequisite for moving forward with the technical review of the series. His tone is constructive, framing test additions as an expected part of the process rather than a blocker. This aligns with Git's development culture where test coverage is highly valued, especially for foundational utilities like the string-list API.
