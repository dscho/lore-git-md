This is part 1 of a 3-part RFC series aimed at eliminating global configuration variables as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch migrates `core.attributesFile` from the global `git_attributes_file` variable to repository-specific storage in a new `struct repo_config_values` embedded in `struct repository`.

The author (Olamide Caleb Bello, an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder) responds to Phillip Wood's review feedback from the v3 iteration. Phillip had suggested: (1) providing concrete examples of the unexpected behaviors caused by global variables, (2) reconsidering the `_path` suffix on the new struct field, and (3) improving documentation. The author acknowledges these points and indicates they will be addressed in the next version.

This exchange shows the patch is making progress through review, with the remaining issues being primarily about clarity and naming rather than fundamental design. The core approach of moving configuration into repository-specific storage appears sound, though Phillip Wood's broader concerns about handling non-primary repository instances (raised in later parts of the series) remain an open architectural question for the overall effort.
