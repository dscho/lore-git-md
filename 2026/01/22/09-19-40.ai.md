This is a test patch in the hook subsystem refactoring series, specifically focusing on verifying hook output stream behavior. The patch adds comprehensive tests to ensure hooks write to stdout and stderr as expected, addressing previous regressions found during the hook API conversion.

The tests cover both client-side hooks (like pre-commit and commit-msg) and server-side hooks (like pre-push), with specific assertions about whether their output streams should be merged or kept separate. The test helpers `check_stdout_separate_from_stderr` and `check_stdout_merged_to_stderr` provide the verification logic.

Junio Hamano provided feedback about line length and test structure, suggesting breaking up the large test case into smaller, more debuggable pieces. The author acknowledges this and plans to address it in v8. The patch appears technically sound and well-motivated, being part of the larger effort to standardize hook behavior that has seen extensive review from multiple contributors including Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King.
