Junio Hamano weighs in on Taylor Blau's suggestion to prefer `enum` over `#define` constants for flags, noting two practical concerns: (1) the limited type safety provided by enums for bit operations (since `-Wenum-conversion` isn't enabled by default), and (2) the difficulty of ensuring an enum is unsigned in pre-C23 code. He demonstrates with a code snippet how debuggers can still usefully interpret enum values even when they're used as bitfields.

The exchange highlights a tension between the clarity benefits of enums (better debugger support, more semantic meaning) and the explicitness of `unsigned` for bit operations. Junio's position is pragmatic rather than dogmatic - he's open to documenting a preference for enums in the CodingGuidelines but wants to acknowledge these real-world limitations.
