Maintainer comment on flag parameter typing in ODB abstraction series.

Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about whether to use `enum` or `unsigned` for flag parameters in the ODB abstraction series. While generally supportive of using enums` over `#define` constants, he notes two practical concerns: (1) the limited type safety enums actually provide (since compilers don't reliably catch mismatched enum types), and (2) the difficulty of ensuring unsignedness in pre-C23 code. He demonstrates how enums can still be useful for debugging, showing how debuggers can interpret combined flag values meaningfully.

This comment comes as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort where flag parameter typing has been a point of discussion between Jeff King (advocating enums) and Patrick Steinhardt (preferring unsigned). Junio's remarks suggest the project may want to document a preference in CodingGuidelines, but his tone indicates this is a minor stylistic point rather than a blocking issue for the series. The observation about debugger utility provides concrete value for using enums even without perfect type safety.
