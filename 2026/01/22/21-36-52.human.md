Maintainer follow-up on branch comparison configuration.

This is Junio Hamano's response in the ongoing discussion about the recently merged `status.compareBranches` feature, which allows configuring multiple branches for `git status` comparisons. While the implementation works for remote-tracking branches (prepending `refs/remotes/` automatically), Junio argues strongly against deferring support for Git's standard DWIM (Do What I Mean) ref resolution rules, which would enable comparisons against local branches and tags.

Junio presents two clear alternatives: either implement the full DWIM rules now (his preference) or explicitly decide to permanently restrict the feature to remote-tracking branches. He rejects the middle ground of adding DWIM support later, as the current hardcoded prefixing creates a backward compatibility risk. The thread shows the maintainer's typical focus on long-term design consequences, even for already-merged code. While the feature works as merged, this discussion may lead to follow-up patches before the next release.
