This is part 3 of a patch series migrating configuration variables from global storage to repository-scoped storage in `struct repo_config_values`, as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch focuses on moving `branch.autoSetupMerge` from the global `git_branch_track` variable to the repository-specific struct.

Phillip Wood's review feedback on this patch is relatively minor compared to his more substantial comments on earlier parts of the series. He notes that the "git_" prefix in `git_branch_track` could now be dropped since the variable is no longer global, and reiterates his earlier point about repository-scoped access (likely referring to his previous concern about proper initialization for non-primary repositories).

The patch itself appears straightforward - it moves the branch tracking configuration into the new struct while maintaining existing behavior. This follows the pattern established in parts 1 and 2 of the series for `core.attributesFile` and `core.sparseCheckout`. The implementation seems technically sound, though Phillip's earlier identified issue about non-primary repository handling remains an open question for the series as a whole.

This patch represents solid incremental progress on eliminating global variables, though the series still needs to address the architectural concern Phillip raised about proper initialization across all repository instances before it can be considered complete.
