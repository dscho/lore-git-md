This is a contributor housekeeping patch that updates `.mailmap` entries for Jean-Noël Avila, the French translation maintainer and documentation contributor. The patch consolidates three additional email variants used by Avila into his primary identity (jn.avila@free.fr), ensuring proper attribution in release notes and contributor statistics.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk originally noticed that recent release notes incorrectly listed Avila as a new contributor due to email mismatches. The patch adds mappings for Avila's corporate email, personal Gmail, and GitGitGadget contributions. While the immediate issue was caused by a manual patch application error by Junio Hamano (using the wrong email header), this change follows the project's standard practice of maintaining accurate contributor attribution.

The patch is straightforward - it adds three lines to `.mailmap` with no functional impact. Junio has already accepted the change, and Avila himself has acknowledged the fix. This is a routine maintenance update similar to other `.mailmap` cleanups that periodically appear on the list.
