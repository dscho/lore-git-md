This is part 2 of a 3-part series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on migrating the `core.sparseCheckout` configuration from a global variable to repository-specific storage in `struct repo_config_values`. The patch updates 12 files to use `the_repository->config_values.sparse_checkout` instead of the global `core_apply_sparse_checkout`, affecting commands like `sparse-checkout`, `clone`, `grep`, and `worktree`.

The author acknowledges two minor issues caught by Toon Claes in review: a duplicate "environment" in the subject line and a typo in the variable name (`core_appy_sparse_checkout`). Claes also questions whether the `core_` prefix should be kept for the new struct field, given that other config sections may use this struct. The patch shows good attention to detail in updating all relevant call sites, though the broader architectural challenge (identified by Phillip Wood in earlier reviews) about proper handling of non-primary repository instances remains unresolved.

This is a well-scoped change that makes incremental improvement toward eliminating global variables, though the series still needs to address the multi-repository initialization issue before it can be considered complete. The patch demonstrates solid progress on a complex refactoring task under mentorship from Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi.
