Test patch in a hook subsystem refactoring series. This is part of a larger effort to standardize Git's hook handling with parallel execution support, now in its 7th iteration. The specific patch adds test coverage for hook output stream behavior, ensuring stdout and stderr are properly separated or merged depending on the hook type.

The tests verify that client-side hooks (like pre-commit and commit-msg) merge stdout to stderr, while the pre-push hook keeps them separate. Junio Hamano notes some stylistic issues with the test structure - overly long lines and potentially confusing test organization - which the author acknowledges will be fixed in v8 by breaking up the large test into smaller, more focused ones.
