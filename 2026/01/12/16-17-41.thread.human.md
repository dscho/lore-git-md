This thread began as a bugfix series for the relatively new `git last-modified` command, addressing input validation issues, memory leaks, and error handling. Toon Claes authored three patches (v2) that were reviewed by Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, with all feedback incorporated before merging. The technical changes are now complete and in the tree.

After the merge, an interesting discussion emerged about terminology in error messages. Patrick had initially suggested using "commit-ish" in an error message, but Toon preferred "revision" based on the glossary's definition. Kristoffer Haugsbakk later pointed to the "IDENTIFIER TERMINOLOGY" section in git(1) that includes "commit-ish" as a defined term, though this doesn't affect the already-merged changes. The conversation highlights how Git contributors often continue discussing documentation and terminology nuances even after technical consensus is reached, as these decisions can inform future work. The thread serves as a good example of the project's attention to both technical correctness and clear communication.
