This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series refactoring Git's object info handling as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The patch removes the OI_DBCACHED distinction from the object_info struct, simplifying the interface by treating all packfile objects as OI_PACKED. 

The change incidentally fixes a segfault in `git archive` that occurred when streaming deltified blobs exceeding core.bigFileThreshold. The bug was caused by an incorrect assumption about delta status checks in the packed object backend. The patch includes a new test case to prevent regression.

Junio Hamano gives his final approval ("Great. Thanks. Will requeue."), indicating the series is ready for integration. This completes a well-reviewed technical improvement that both simplifies the codebase and fixes a real-world issue, with the added benefit of new test coverage for the edge case.
