This is part 5 of an 8-patch series introducing the new `git-history` command, focusing on replay infrastructure improvements. The patch adds support for updating detached HEAD during replay operations, completing the branch update functionality.

The change modifies `replay.c` to properly handle `DECORATION_REF_HEAD` decorations when HEAD is detached, while maintaining the existing behavior for symbolic refs. The implementation:
- Adds detection of detached HEAD state in `set_up_replay_mode()`
- Refactors the decoration iteration loop for clarity
- Adds a new test case verifying detached HEAD updates
- Preserves all existing branch update behavior

The patch builds on Elijah Newren's earlier work and is part of the larger effort to make the replay infrastructure robust enough to support the new history editing commands. The changes are well-contained and come with test coverage, continuing the series' pattern of careful, incremental improvements to the replay machinery.
