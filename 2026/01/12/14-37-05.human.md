This is a naming discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ref backend validation series, where Junio Hamano weighs in on terminology for referring to non-symbolic refs. The series is technically ready but needs this naming clarification before proceeding.

Junio firmly rejects the proposed "direct refs" terminology introduced in patch 10/17, calling it "not understandable" since Git has historically used "symbolic ref" (not "indirect ref") to describe the opposite case. He clearly prefers "non-symbolic refs" despite acknowledging it may feel awkward, as it aligns with existing Git conventions.

This input from the maintainer effectively settles the naming question - the series will need a respin to adjust the terminology before merging. The exchange demonstrates Git's cultural preference for clarity and consistency over novel phrasing, even when the new term might be more concise. With Karthik Nayak having already reviewed the implementation aspects thoroughly, this naming clarification appears to be the final hurdle for this backend unification work.
