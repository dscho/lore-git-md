Refactoring of Git's ref backend validation infrastructure. This is Karthik Nayak's review response to Patrick Steinhardt's 17-patch series that centralizes ref consistency checks across both files and reftable backends.

Karthik confirms the overall quality of the series, which he had previously reviewed in detail. The patches systematically eliminate backend-specific validation paths in favor of shared checks that work consistently across storage formats. Key changes include simplifying the files backend (patches 1-8), introducing shared verification infrastructure (9-14), and migrating fsck checks (15-17). 

Notable fixes include proper worktree handling in the reftable backend (patch 14) where Karthik, as original author of the problematic code, confirmed the issue and approved the fix. The series now appears ready for merging after addressing earlier review comments about HEAD detection logic centralization. This work is part of the ongoing effort to abstract and unify behavior between Git's ref storage backends.
