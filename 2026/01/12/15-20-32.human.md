This is a policy discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about how to handle deprecated wrapper functions. 

Junio Hamano is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion about adding version-specific deprecation markers to compatibility wrappers. Junio makes it clear that while he's fine with documenting the intent to eventually remove the wrappers, he strongly opposes tying their removal to a specific Git version number.53 in this case). His concern is that this could artificially constrain the release schedule if the cleanup work isn't completed in time.

The exchange reveals an important project policy: deprecations should be removed based on technical readiness rather than calendar-based schedules. Junio suggests more neutral wording that expresses the removal intent without creating version-based deadlines. This maintains the project's flexibility while still signaling that the wrappers are temporary.

The discussion is essentially settled - Junio's position as maintainer carries, and the series can proceed without version-tied deprecation markers. This is a minor process clarification that doesn't affect the technical content of René Scharfe's patch series, which remains ready for merging.
