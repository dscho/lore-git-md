This is part 2 of an 11-patch series implementing path encoding for submodule gitdirs via a new `extensions.submodulePathConfig` setting. The series has gone through 9 iterations with extensive review and is now in its final polished form.

The patch centralizes validation of submodule gitdir paths by moving checks into `submodule_name_to_gitdir()`, which now always validates paths before returning them. This eliminates redundant validation calls and ensures all paths (including those from config) are properly checked. The change simplifies the API while maintaining safety - two validation calls remain in `builtin/submodule--helper.c` to handle parallel clone races.

The implementation touches `submodule.c` and `builtin/submodule--helper.c`, removing scattered validation checks in favor of a single authoritative check. The series has been thoroughly reviewed by multiple contributors (including Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano) and validated in production at Google. This particular patch represents a clean-up of the validation logic as part of the larger effort to make submodule paths more robust.
