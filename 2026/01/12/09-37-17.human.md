This is a performance patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing repack machinery overhaul series, specifically addressing geometric repacking with promisor remotes. The email is a response to Taylor Blau's review of the first patch in the series.

Patrick explains the technical reasoning behind handling `--stdin-packs` and `--stdin-packs=follow` separately when excluding promisor objects. The key insight is that the first case doesn't require object walking (so promisor objects can simply be excluded by not passing promisor packs), while the second case needs special handling during the revision walk. He presents two possible approaches and justifies choosing the stricter one that fails on malformed requests.

The discussion reveals some subtle edge cases around promisor pack handling that make the test setup more complex than usual - Patrick notes they can't use the standard `test_commit` helper because it would incorrectly mark all objects as promisor objects. This patch appears well-considered and builds on Patrick's deep expertise in the repack subsystem, though the complexity suggests this area could benefit from future simplification.
