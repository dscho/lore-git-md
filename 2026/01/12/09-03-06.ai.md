This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series refactoring Git's ref backend validation. The patch completes the migration of fsck checks from `builtin/fsck.c` into the shared refs subsystem by removing the now-redundant `fsck_head_link()` function.

The patch removes `fsck_head_link()` after determining its remaining HEAD validation check is either redundant or unnecessary. The function previously performed three checks, all of which are now handled elsewhere: unsafe refs are caught by `check_refname_format()`, missing HEAD is already detected as a repository corruption, and symref resolution issues don't occur with the current flag usage. The patch inlines the remaining `refs_resolve_ref_unsafe()` call directly where it was previously wrapped.

This change follows the series' pattern of centralizing validation logic in the refs subsystem while maintaining existing behavior. The removal is straightforward - 24 lines deleted versus 4 added - and builds on the infrastructure established in earlier patches. With this, the series achieves its goal of unified ref validation across both files and reftable backends.
