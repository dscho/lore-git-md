This is part 10 of Patrick Steinhardt's 17-patch series refactoring Git's ref backend validation. The patch introduces terminology ("direct refs") to distinguish non-symbolic refs from symrefs in the validation infrastructure. Karthik Nayak notes this is a slight terminology shift from the project's usual "regular refs" (which includes symrefs), but agrees the new term makes sense for the specific validation context. The exchange shows the careful attention to naming that characterizes this backend unification effort - even small semantic distinctions matter when establishing shared infrastructure. With Karthik's review confirming the technical approach, this piece appears ready to move forward as part of the larger series.
