Olamide Caleb Bello (mentored by Christian Couder) has proposed an RFC patch series to eliminate global configuration variables as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series introduces a new `struct config_values` to hold previously global config settings, demonstrating the approach with three configuration values: `core.attributesFile`, `core.sparseCheckout`, and `branch.autoSetupMerge`. The design addresses initialization timing concerns while maintaining eager loading of configuration values.

Phillip Wood, a respected contributor with deep knowledge of Git's configuration system, has reviewed the first patch (handling `core.attributesFile`) and suggested significant architectural improvements. He proposes embedding the structure directly in `struct repository` (like `repo_settings` does) rather than heap-allocating it, renaming it to the more specific `repo_config`, and considering whether `environment.[ch]` might be a better home for this code. These suggestions would simplify memory management and improve naming clarity if adopted.

The series has already shown the pattern works across different configuration types (string, boolean, and enum) while affecting numerous commands. Phillip's feedback maintains focus on the core challenge of initialization timing while opening new design directions for the RFC. His constructive review advances the discussion toward potential revisions that could shape how this aspect of global variable elimination proceeds. The thread remains open for further discussion of these architectural suggestions before the series progresses.
