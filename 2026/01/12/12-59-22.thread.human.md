Olamide Caleb Bello, an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder, is working on migrating Git's global configuration variables to repository-scoped storage as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Their RFC patch series introduces a new `struct repo_config_values` to hold these configurations within each repository instance.

The series has reached its third iteration, with three key configurations being migrated: `core.attributesFile` (part 1), `core.sparseCheckout` (part 2), and `branch.autoSetupMerge` (part 3). Each migration affects multiple commands and requires careful handling of initialization sequencing to maintain existing behavior. Toon Claes has provided thorough review feedback, catching minor issues like commit message formatting and variable naming while confirming the technical approach is sound.

The implementation follows Phillip Wood's suggestion to use a dedicated config struct rather than repurposing `repo-settings`. While the series makes concrete progress on specific migrations, larger questions about config scoping criteria and initialization of mixed-scope configurations remain open for future discussion. With all three parts now complete and no major technical objections raised, the series appears ready to be merged as a step forward in the broader effort to eliminate `the_repository` global.
