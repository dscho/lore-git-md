This thread discusses an RFC patch series by Olamide Caleb Bello (an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder) that introduces a new `struct config_values` (proposed to be renamed `repo_config`) to eliminate global configuration variables as part of Git's ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series demonstrates the approach with three configuration values: `core.attributesFile`, `core.sparseCheckout`, and `branch.autoSetupMerge`.

The initial approach created a per-repository heap-allocated struct, but Phillip Wood suggested several architectural improvements: embedding the struct directly in `struct repository` (avoiding separate allocation and matching the `repo_settings` pattern), renaming it to `repo_config` for clarity, and potentially moving it to `environment.[ch]`. The key requirement is maintaining eager loading of configuration values while eliminating globals, with proper cleanup in `repo_clear()`.

The first patch focuses on `core.attributesFile`, moving the global `git_attributes_file` variable into the new struct and affecting over 20 files. The author has acknowledged Phillip's feedback and plans to revise the approach in the next iteration. The thread remains open on questions of struct naming and placement, with the author indicating they will incorporate these suggestions before extending the pattern to more configuration values. This work represents an important step in Git's broader libification effort, showing how to handle different configuration types while maintaining existing behavior.
