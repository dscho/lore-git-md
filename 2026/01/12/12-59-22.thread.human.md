This RFC patch series by Olamide Caleb Bello (an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi) continues to make steady progress in eliminating global configuration variables as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The work introduces `struct repo_config_values` to store repository-specific configurations that were previously global.

The series has now gone through seven iterations, with all major technical concerns around memory management, initialization patterns, and behavior preservation having been addressed. Phillip Wood has reviewed and approved the technical approach, and Junio Hamano has indicated the series is nearly ready for integration, having only caught minor style issues that he plans to fix himself when queuing the patches.

The latest email brings a minor style review from Christian Couder, who suggests adding a blank line for better readability when accessing the migrated `core.sparseCheckout` configuration through the new repository-specific struct. This follows the established pattern of replacing direct global variable access with `cfg->apply_sparse_checkout` after obtaining the repository's config values.

All three targeted configuration migrations (`core.attributesFile`, `core.sparseCheckout`, and `branch.autoSetupMerge`) are now complete, with tests passing including fuzz testing. The only remaining architectural question concerns submodule initialization and config inheritance, which the author has acknowledged as the next area to address. With the maintainer now focused on minor formatting nits rather than substantive issues, this foundational work appears on track for inclusion in the near future.
