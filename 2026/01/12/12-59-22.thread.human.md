This thread documents the successful progression of an Outreachy intern's work to eliminate global configuration variables as part of Git's ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Olamide Caleb Bello, mentored by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi, has developed a series introducing `struct repo_config_values` to store repository-specific configurations. After seven iterations, the patches have reached technical maturity, with all three targeted configurations (`core.attributesFile`, `core.sparseCheckout`, and `branch.autoSetupMerge`) successfully migrated while preserving existing behaviors.

The series has garnered thorough review from key contributors - Phillip Wood shaped the memory management approach, Christian Couder provided final technical approval, and Junio Hamano has indicated he'll handle the remaining minor formatting nits when queuing. All tests pass, including fuzz testing that initially revealed a regression (since fixed). The only remaining architectural question concerns submodule initialization, which will be addressed separately. With the maintainer signaling readiness to integrate and the author inquiring about next steps for migrating additional config values, this foundational work appears poised for inclusion in the near future.
