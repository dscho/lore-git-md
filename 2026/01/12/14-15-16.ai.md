This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's v10 series introducing the `git-history` command, focused on supporting empty commit ranges in the replay infrastructure. The patch addresses a subtle issue where the replay subsystem would incorrectly report merge conflicts when processing empty commit ranges (like an implicit "HEAD" range with no commits).

The fix is straightforward - initialize the `merge_result.clean` flag to 1 (success) rather than zero (failure) when no commits are being replayed. This ensures empty ranges are handled correctly, which is important for the upcoming `git-history` command that will use implicit ranges. The change is minimal (3 lines modified) and well-contained within `replay.c`.

This patch builds on the series' ongoing work to make the replay infrastructure more flexible and library-friendly. It's a small but necessary piece of the larger effort to enable new history editing operations while maintaining robust behavior in edge cases. The change has been reviewed by Elijah Newren and fits cleanly with the series' technical direction.
