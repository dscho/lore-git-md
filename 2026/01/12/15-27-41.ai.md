This is a review response in the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, specifically addressing Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series that refactors packfile storage to support pluggable object database backends. Justin Tobler, who previously reviewed patches 1-3 in the series, confirms that the v3 changes look good. The series has already incorporated feedback from multiple reviewers (Karthik Nayak, Toon Claes, Kristoffer Haugsbakk) and addressed all open questions. 

The architectural change moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, with the final patch updating MIDX handling to reference MIDX via packfile stores rather than ODB sources. The review process has been thorough, with Junio Hamano indicating the series appears ready for inclusion in 'next'. This positive review from Justin Tobler suggests the series has reached consensus and is likely to proceed toward merging, completing a significant step in the ODB abstraction work.
