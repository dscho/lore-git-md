This is a review response in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Olamide Caleb Bello's RFC patch series that introduces `struct config_values` to eliminate global configuration variables. Phillip Wood provides feedback on the first patch in the series, which moved `core.attributesFile` from a global variable to the new struct.

Phillip suggests several improvements to the design: embedding `struct config_values` directly in `struct repository` rather than heap-allocating it (similar to how `repo_settings` works), renaming it to the more specific `repo_config`, and considering whether `environment.[ch]` might be a better home for it than `config.[ch]`. These are substantive architectural suggestions that would affect the entire series' approach.

The review maintains the technical focus on initialization timing concerns that were central to earlier discussion, while the current patch preserves eager loading, Phillip emphasizes this is the key invariant rather than preserving `git_default_core_config()`'s exact implementation. His tone is constructive, offering concrete alternatives while acknowledging the series' core goal of eliminating global state.

This email advances the thread by proposing structural changes that would simplify memory management and improve naming, setting up potential revisions to the RFC approach. As a respected contributor with deep knowledge of Git's configuration system, Phillip's feedback carries weight in shaping how this aspect of the `the_repository` elimination will proceed.
