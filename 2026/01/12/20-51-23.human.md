This is the final approval for a submodule path encoding series that's now ready to merge. The 11-patch series (currently at v9) implements filesystem-safe encoding of submodule gitdir paths to prevent conflicts, particularly on case-insensitive filesystems. The solution includes multiple fallback mechanisms (plain names, URL-encoded names, numbered variants, and finally hashed names) along with configuration validation to detect conflicts.

Junio Hamano notes the series has reached "diminishing returns" for further improvements and is ready for merging pending final confirmation from Josh Steadmon about production readiness. Adrian Ratiu (the series author) agrees, noting the core logic has been stable since v7 and thanking contributors. The implementation handles all known edge cases with comprehensive test coverage, including case-folding protection and configuration conflict detection.

This represents the culmination of several months' work to safely encode submodule paths while maintaining backward compatibility. The series has undergone extensive review from multiple contributors including Patrick Steinhardt and Phillip Wood, with all feedback now addressed. The final version includes shared URL encoding functionality and guarantees unique paths even in pathological cases through a hashing fallback.
