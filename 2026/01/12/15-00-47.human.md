This is a discussion about the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically around deprecation timelines for compatibility wrappers.

Patrick Steinhardt is responding to Junio Hamano's rejection of his suggestion to tie the removal of tree-parsing compatibility wrappers to a specific Git version (2.53). Patrick clarifies that his intent wasn't to block releases, but rather to create a clear marker that would help prevent these wrappers from being forgotten after they're no longer needed. He notes this is a common pattern - wrappers get added temporarily but then linger because there's no clear trigger for their removal.

The exchange shows differing perspectives on deprecation management: Patrick favors explicit version-based triggers as a memory aid, while Junio maintains the project's policy against version-specific commitments for individual changes. This is a minor process discussion in an otherwise technically solid series that's making significant progress on removing global state from Git's tree parsing code.
