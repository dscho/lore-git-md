This is part 3 of an RFC patch series by Olamide Caleb Bello (an Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder) that introduces `struct config_values` to eliminate global configuration variables as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series demonstrates the approach by migrating three config values from global variables to per-repository storage.

Phillip Wood provides feedback on the first patch in the series (which moved `core.attributesFile`). He suggests simplifying the design by embedding `struct config_values` directly in `struct repository` rather than heap-allocating it, similar to how `struct repo_settings` is handled. He also proposes renaming the struct to `repo_config` for clarity and questions whether `config.[ch]` is the best location for it.

The technical changes involve moving configuration values that were previously stored globally into a repository-specific structure, with proper initialization and cleanup. The series establishes a pattern that works for different configuration types (strings, booleans, enums) while maintaining the existing eager loading behavior. This is part of the broader effort to make Git's codebase more modular and reduce reliance on global state.

The feedback from Phillip indicates the series is moving in the right direction but could benefit from some design refinements before proceeding further. The approach shows promise for systematically eliminating global configuration variables across the codebase.
