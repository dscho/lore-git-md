This is part 1 of a 3-part RFC series by Olamide Caleb Bello (Outreachy intern mentored by Christian Couder) that introduces a new `struct config_values` (proposed to be renamed `repo_config`) to eliminate global configuration variables as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series demonstrates the approach with three config values, starting here with `core.attributesFile`.

The patch moves the global `git_attributes_file` variable into the new per-repository struct, affecting over 20 files that previously accessed this global. Phillip Wood's review suggests architectural improvements: embedding the struct directly in `struct repository` (avoiding separate allocation, matching the `repo_settings` pattern), renaming to `repo_config` for clarity, and considering moving the struct to `environment.[ch]`. The key invariant is preserving eager loading of config values, not maintaining the exact implementation of `git_default_core_config()`.

The author acknowledges the feedback and indicates they will revise the approach in the next iteration. This is a foundational change for the broader libification effort, showing how to handle string-type configuration values while maintaining existing behavior. The thread remains open on questions of struct naming and placement, with the author planning to incorporate these suggestions before extending the pattern to more config values.
