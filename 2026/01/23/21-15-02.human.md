This is a documentation patch in Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing series to standardize Git's documentation format. It's the third of four patches in this batch, continuing the conversion of man pages to consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style.

The patch makes several style improvements to the `git-clone` and `for-each-ref-options` documentation:
- Spells out both `--reject-shallow` and `--no-reject-shallow` forms explicitly in `git-clone`
- Converts option descriptions to imperative mood (e.g., "Do not checkout" instead of "No checkout")
- Replaces manual `*NOTE*` formatting with proper AsciiDoc `NOTE` macros
- Fixes markup issues like consistent use of `_<placeholder>_` syntax

The changes are mechanical and follow the established documentation style guidelines. This continues the high-quality, uncontroversial documentation standardization work that has been progressing smoothly through the mailing list. The patch appears well-executed and ready for inclusion.
