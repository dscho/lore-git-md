This is a technical discussion about mtime handling in the ODB abstraction series, specifically patch 11/14 which introduces mtime fields for object info requests. Taylor Blau raises several thoughtful points about the implementation details and design considerations.

The patch adds `mtimep` to `struct object_info` to let callers request an object's modification time, primarily for pruning unreachable objects. Taylor notes some confusion around the stat handling in `read_object_info_from_path()` where declarations and usage are separated by conditionals, suggesting the logic has grown organically and could be clearer.

Key technical concerns center on the mtime semantics for packed objects. Taylor questions whether exposing cruft pack awareness at this low level is appropriate, as it ties the ODB layer to GC-specific behavior. He wonders if callers might sometimes want the pack mtime regardless of cruft status, and notes the current design makes that difficult. The error message for failed mtimes loading could also be more specific by including the pack name.

Taylor also suggests zeroing the entire response section of `object_info` as a defensive measure against future field additions. While generally approving the direction, he flags these as areas needing clarity before finalizing the design. The discussion represents one of the last open questions in this otherwise mature series.
