This is a minor linguistic clarification in the ongoing ODB abstraction series discussion. Taylor Blau weighs in on a thread about commit message wording, suggesting a simpler phrasing for describing function equivalency. The original discussion was prompted by Chris Torek and Junio Hamano analyzing the grammatical distinction between noun ("an equivalent of") and adjective ("equivalent to") forms of the word "equivalent". 

Taylor proposes removing the article entirely to make the sentence read more naturally as "This function is equivalent to..." rather than debating noun vs adjective usage. This exchange is characteristic of Git's attention to precise documentation, though it doesn't affect the technical content of Patrick Steinhardt's patch series. The suggestion comes as a follow-up to the more substantive technical discussions about the ODB layer's design that have largely converged in earlier emails.
