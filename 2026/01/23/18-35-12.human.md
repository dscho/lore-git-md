This is a technical discussion about the ODB abstraction series, specifically addressing how to handle mtime information for objects in cruft packs. The exchange between Patrick Steinhardt and Taylor Blau focuses on whether mtime handling should be implemented within the object storage backend (Patrick's approach) or at a higher level via a generic stat structure (Taylor's proposal).

Taylor argues that adding a `statp` field to `struct object_info` would provide a more general solution that keeps GC-specific mtime logic separate from the backend implementation. He acknowledges this would be "kind of ugly" but maintains it would reduce ambiguity about what mtime means in different contexts (regular packs vs cruft packs). Patrick counters that this approach would break pluggability by putting backend-specific logic outside the backends themselves.

The discussion represents the last major architectural question in the otherwise largely settled ODB abstraction series. Both approaches would achieve the same functional result but differ in their abstraction boundaries. Taylor-Blau exchange shows the conversation moving toward practical solutions that could unblock the series while leaving room for more comprehensive refactoring later.
