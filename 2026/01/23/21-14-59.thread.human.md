Jean-Noël Avila continues his long-running effort to standardize Git's man pages to a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis-style format, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk providing thorough reviews. The latest work focuses on converting `git-submodule.adoc`, `git-show.adoc`, and `git-clone.adoc`, among others, with Kristoffer confirming the latter is now "spotless" after addressing all remaining style issues.

The changes are purely presentational, improving markup consistency while maintaining cross-toolchain compatibility. Key improvements include splitting ambiguous options, standardizing config option syntax, converting manual notes to AsciiDoc macros, and fixing various formatting issues. Kristoffer has been particularly active in reviewing these changes, catching issues like ambiguous terminology and inconsistent option formatting.

With `git-clone.adoc` and `git-show.adoc` now merge-ready after multiple review iterations, the documentation standardization effort is making steady progress. The work maintains a high standard of quality, focusing on markup improvements without altering technical content, demonstrating the careful attention being given to this important but often overlooked aspect of the project.
