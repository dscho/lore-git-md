Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing effort to standardize Git's man pages in AsciiDoc synopsis style continues to make steady progress, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk providing meticulous review feedback. The current focus is on `git-clone.adoc`, where they're aligning long option formatting with the project's documented conventions in gitcli(7). 

Haugsbakk confirmed there's no automated enforcement for the preferred "stuck form" (`--option=<value>`) convention, which Junio Hamano has previously endorsed. This follows earlier refinements to `git-show.adoc` and `pretty-formats.adoc` where similar formatting details were addressed. The discussion shows the project's commitment to documentation quality, with even minor presentation choices being carefully considered to maintain consistency across all man pages.

While the core work remains focused on presentation rather than content changes, some technical challenges remain - particularly around special character rendering in decoration options, where Avila is considering custom parser extensions. The collaboration demonstrates how the project is systematically improving documentation quality through this conversion effort.
