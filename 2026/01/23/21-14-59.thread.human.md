The work has received Reviewed-by tags from Kristoffer, with Junio Hamano having previously endorsed the stuck-form convention that's now being systematically applied across the documentation.

The effort remains focused purely on presentation quality without altering command behavior, with each iteration refining formatting details. Current work includes addressing rendering nuances and potentially developing custom parser extensions for special character handling. The changes maintain compatibility between asciidoc.py and asciidoctor toolchains while improving consistency across Git's documentation.
