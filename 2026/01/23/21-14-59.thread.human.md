Jean-Noël Avila continues his meticulous work standardizing Git's documentation, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk providing thorough review feedback. The current focus is on the `git-show` man page, where they're addressing subtle formatting issues in the `%(decorate:)` format specifier. Haugsbakk identified inconsistent rendering of the `->` pointer symbol and plus-sign (`++`) verbatim formatting in the AsciiDoc output.

Avila acknowledges these are limitations of the asciidoctor parser and is considering developing a custom parser extension to handle these cases more robustly. In the meantime, he suggests falling back to unformatted text when the markup can't be processed correctly, prioritizing clarity over perfect formatting. This follows the pattern seen in their earlier work on `git-clone` and other man pages, where they've systematically converted option formatting, placeholder markup, and note styles while maintaining compatibility across different documentation toolchains.

The collaboration demonstrates an exceptional attention to detail, with each iteration refining the presentation without altering the underlying command behavior. While most of the documentation conversion work is now complete, they continue to identify and address minor rendering inconsistencies in special characters and formatting edge cases.
