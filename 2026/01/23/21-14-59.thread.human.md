Jean-Noël Avila continues his systematic conversion of Git's man pages to a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis-style format, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk providing review and sign-off. The latest work focuses on `git-show.adoc` and `pretty-formats.adoc`, building on previous conversions of `git-submodule` and `git-clone` documentation. 

Key improvements in this iteration include enhanced infrastructure in `asciidoc.conf.in` to support the new formatting across different toolchains, proper use of synopsis blocks for command syntax, and standardized presentation of options and placeholders. The `pretty-formats.adoc` conversion is particularly notable for its use of definition lists to clearly structure format types. All changes maintain compatibility with both asciidoc.py and asciidoctor while strictly focusing on documentation presentation - no command behavior is altered.

The effort has reached a mature state, with Junio Hamano having previously endorsed the stuck-form option style (`--option=<value>`) that's now being systematically applied. Remaining work appears focused on polishing rendering details and potentially extending the toolchain for special character handling, but the core standardization approach is well-established and consistently applied across the targeted man pages.
