Jean-Noël Avila continues his ongoing effort to standardize Git's documentation format with a 4-patch series converting several man pages to the new AsciiDoc synopsis style. The series updates the documentation for `git-submodule`, `git-clone`, `git-show`, and `for-each-ref-options`, with the most extensive changes being in the submodule` and pretty-formats documentation. 

As with previous documentation conversion work, these changes are purely about formatting consistency and don't modify any behavior. The patches also include some fixes for style issues spotted in earlier conversions. Given this is part of an established effort that has been progressing smoothly, the changes are unlikely to generate controversy and represent routine maintenance of the project's documentation.
