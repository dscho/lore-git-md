This is a technical discussion about mtime handling in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series (patch 12/14). The exchange focuses on whether exposing mtime at the ODB layer is the right abstraction, particularly for GC-specific semantics around cruft packs.

Taylor Blau questions the current approach, suggesting it might conflate general object mtime needs with GC's specific requirements. He proposes an alternative design that would keep GC-specific logic out of the core ODB layer via a generic `statp` field in `struct object_info`, providing a `object_info_gc_mtime()` helper as proof of concept.

Patrick defends his approach, arguing that Taylor's proposal would put backend-specific logic outside the backends and wouldn't achieve the goal of making the logic pluggable. He does agree to continue passing the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag for safety, even though he believes it's not strictly necessary.

This represents one of the last open architectural questions in the otherwise largely settled ODB abstraction series. The discussion remains technical and constructive, with both sides clearly explaining their perspectives on where the mtime handling logic should live in the architecture.
