This is a technical discussion about mtime handling in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series (patch 11/14). The exchange focuses on how to expose object modification times through the new object storage layer, particularly for garbage collection purposes.

Taylor Blau raises two main concerns in his review: (1) whether zeroing out response fields in `struct object_info` should be done more systematically, and (2) whether exposing GC-specific mtime semantics at the ODB layer is the right design. Patrick agrees to improve the pack name in error messages but prefers to defer larger structural changes to follow-up work.

The discussion reveals an architectural tension - while the current approach cleanly supports GC needs by handling cruft pack mtimes automatically, it may not be the right abstraction for other potential mtime use cases. Taylor suggests this might belong in a higher layer, though acknowledges the hypothetical nature of alternative use cases. This patch represents one of the last open design questions in the otherwise largely settled ODB abstraction series.
