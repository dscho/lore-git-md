Documentation patch continuing the man page standardization effort. This is the third patch in a 4-part series converting `git-clone` and `for-each-ref-options` documentation to consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style.

The patch makes several mechanical improvements to the documentation format:
- Spells out both `--reject-shallow` and `--no-reject-shallow` forms explicitly
- Standardizes option descriptions to use imperative mood ("Do not checkout" instead of "No checkout")
- Replaces manual `*NOTE*` markup with proper AsciiDoc `NOTE` macro
- Fixes markup for placeholders (using `_<when>_` consistently)
- Tightens phrasing in several option descriptions for conciseness

Changes are limited to `Documentation/git-clone.adoc` and `Documentation/for-each-ref-options.adoc`, with no behavioral modifications. The work follows the established pattern of Jean-Noël Avila's documentation standardization effort, maintaining the high quality seen in previous conversions. The patch appears uncontroversial and well-executed, continuing the systematic improvement of Git's documentation formatting.
