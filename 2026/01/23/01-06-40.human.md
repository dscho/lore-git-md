This is part 11/14 of Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series, focusing on adding mtime tracking to object storage. The patch introduces a new `mtimep` field in `struct object_info` to let callers request an object's modification time, which is needed for operations like pruning unreachable objects.

Taylor Blau's review raises several thoughtful points about the implementation. He agrees with the goal but questions whether exposing mtime at this layer is the right approach, particularly around cruft pack handling. The review identifies some minor readability issues in the conditionals and suggests improving error messages by including the pack name when `.mtimes` loading fails. Taylor also wonders if we should zero out the entire response section of `object_info` for safety.

The most substantive concern is whether GC-specific mtime semantics (which differ between cruft and regular packs) belong in this low-level interface. While the current approach works for pruning, Taylor notes it might not match other potential use cases that want raw pack mtimes regardless of cruft status. The patch appears technically sound but may benefit from reconsidering this design choice before finalizing the series.
