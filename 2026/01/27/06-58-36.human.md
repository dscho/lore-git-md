This is a feature patch in the final stages of review for the new `git repo` subcommands, specifically addressing output format naming consistency. Patrick Steinhardt provides detailed review feedback on Lucas Seiki Oshiro's patch to rename the "keyvalue" format to "lines" across the `repo info` and `repo structure` commands.

Patrick suggests improving the commit message to better explain why "keyvalue" was problematic (it doesn't indicate newline-delimited output, is redundant with other formats, and may need to handle non-key-value output later). He also raises a minor concern about the symmetry between the new `FORMAT_LINES` and existing `FORMAT_NUL_TERMINATED` enum values, proposing `FORMAT_NEWLINE_TERMINATED` as a more balanced alternative.

The patch is part of a series that's already been through several iterations and is now in its final polishing stage before being queued for integration. Patrick's feedback is constructive and focused on making the naming more future-proof, while acknowledging the experimental nature of these new commands gives some flexibility in making such changes. The thread shows the project's attention to detail in API design, even for relatively small naming decisions.
