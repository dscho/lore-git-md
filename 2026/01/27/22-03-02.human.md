This is a status update on Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing object database (ODB) refactoring work, specifically regarding the `ps/odb-for-each-object` series that aims to revamp Git's object enumeration API. Taylor Blau is requesting a brief hold on merging the series to allow more discussion time about the mtime-related changes, while expressing general support for the overall direction.

The 16-patch series (already incorporating two previous branches) represents a major restructuring of how Git enumerates objects, replacing various `for_each_*_object()` functions with a unified `odb_for_each_object()` API. While the technical approach appears sound and the series has progressed through multiple iterations, Taylor's note suggests there may still be open questions about the mtime field additions that warrant further review before integration.

This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's broader effort to abstract and modernize Git's object storage layer, which has been one of the most significant architectural initiatives in recent development cycles. The request for a brief delay indicates the project's careful approach to such deep plumbing changes, even when the overall direction is uncontroversial.
