This is a bugfix patch for git-gui, addressing tab alignment issues in the diff viewer. It's part of an ongoing thread where the maintainer Johannes Sixt has rejected previous approaches that modified the text content directly, as they broke the "Stage Line/Hunk for Commit" functionality.

The patch introduces an `expand_tabs` procedure that converts tabs to spaces based on the configured `gui.tabsize` before rendering in Tk. While this fixes the visual alignment problem, it's important to note this is the approach that was previously rejected by the maintainer in favor of a solution that doesn't modify the text content (the `apply_tab_size` solution in the second patch of this series).

The changes are focused in `git-gui/lib/diff.tcl`, adding 24 lines for the new procedure and modifying one line to use it when inserting diff lines. The implementation handles tab expansion by tracking column positions and inserting the correct number of spaces based on the tab size configuration.

This represents one of two competing solutions being proposed in this thread - the other being a Tk-native approach that adjusts tab rendering without modifying the text content. The maintainer has indicated a strong preference for solutions that preserve the original text content to maintain compatibility with existing functionality.
