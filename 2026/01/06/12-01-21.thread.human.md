The thread focuses on improving `git add -p`'s interactive hunk selection interface by adding visual feedback about previous decisions (use/skip) when navigating between hunks. While the core approach is technically sound and well-tested, the discussion now centers on refining the user experience - particularly finding more compact wording for the status indicators without losing clarity.
