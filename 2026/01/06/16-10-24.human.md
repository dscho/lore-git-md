This is a UI improvement patch for `git add -p`'s interactive hunk selection interface, part of an ongoing GSoC project. The patch adds visual feedback showing whether each hunk was previously marked for use ('y') or skip ('n') when navigating between hunks, addressing a usability issue where users couldn't see their previous decisions.

Phillip Wood, a contributor with deep knowledge of the interactive add/apply machinery, reviews the v6 patch from Abraham Samuel Adekunle. He suggests two improvements: (1) finding a more compact notation than "(previous decision: use/skip)" to avoid overly long prompts (some already hit 80 chars), and (2) questioning why the check for `hunk_nr` is needed in the implementation. The changes appear technically sound with comprehensive test coverage, but the review focuses on polishing the user-facing strings and implementation details.

The patch modifies prompt strings in `add-patch.c` and updates tests in `t3701-add-interactive.sh` to verify the new behavior across various scenarios (navigation, regex matching, color output). This continues work originally suggested by Junio Hamano to improve hunk navigation clarity.
