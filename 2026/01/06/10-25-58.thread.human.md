Jeff King (Peff) identified and fixed a performance regression in `git cat-file` where bitmap indexes were being unnecessarily loaded even for unfiltered operations, causing a significant 331% slowdown when listing all objects in sorted order. His patch modifies `builtin/cat-file.c` to only load bitmaps when an object filter is actually enabled, while maintaining the 65% speedup benefit for filtered cases (like listing just blobs). The fix includes new performance tests that clearly demonstrate both the regression and its resolution. This is a focused optimization from a trusted contributor that restores original performance for unfiltered operations while preserving the bitmap benefits where they matter.
