This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 7-part series refactoring Git's object info handling as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The patch removes a redundant `repository` parameter from `packed_object_info()` since the same information is already available via the `packed_git` struct.

The change is straightforward and mechanical, updating all callers to omit the now-unneeded parameter. The patch touches several key subsystems that interact with packfiles (`cat-file`, `pack-objects`, `commit-graph`, and the bitmap code) but the changes are purely syntactic with no behavioral impact. 

This cleanup follows naturally from the previous optimizations in the series and helps further prepare the codebase for the ODB abstraction work. The patch has already benefited from review feedback in earlier iterations, with no outstanding concerns noted. As the final piece of the series, it represents a clean conclusion to this round of improvements to Git's object info handling.
