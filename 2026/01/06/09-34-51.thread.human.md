The changes remain narrowly focused on testing current .netrc support without modifying production behavior, despite broader discussions in the thread about potentially deprecating .netrc files entirely (a topic raised by brian m. carlson but outside this patch's scope). The patch seems poised for acceptance once the commit message clarifications are added.
