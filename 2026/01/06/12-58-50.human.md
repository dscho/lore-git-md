This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's memory management bugfix series, now in its third iteration. The patch fixes a 192-byte memory leak that occurs when accessing submodule data through `git-grep`. 

The issue stems from an incorrect cleanup sequence in `odb_free()` where sources were being freed before being closed, causing the close operation to become a no-op. The fix simply reorders these operations to ensure proper cleanup. The patch is minimal (a single-line change) but important, as it prevents leaks during submodule operations.

The thread context shows this series has gone through multiple rounds of review, with Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak providing final acks. This particular fix has been confirmed as technically sound and ready for merging. While small, it's a clean solution to a real memory management issue that could affect users working with submodules.
