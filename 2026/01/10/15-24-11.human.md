This is the 21st iteration of Harald Nordgren's feature series to enhance `git status` to show divergence from both upstream and push tracking branches. After extensive review from Junio C Hamano, Ben Knoble, Phillip Wood and others over 20 previous versions, this version makes final naming tweaks to variable names (`sti`/`push_sti` renamed to `cmp_fetch`/`cmp_push`) per Junio's suggestion for clarity.

The series consists of two well-tested patches: first refactoring the comparison formatting logic into a helper function, then implementing the push tracking display. The implementation cleanly extends the existing status output format to show both relationships when they differ, with comprehensive test coverage. All technical concerns have been addressed through the long review process, and the series appears ready for merging.

The changes in v21 are minimal - just the final variable naming polish - suggesting this long-running effort may finally be reaching completion. For users who work with different upstream and push remotes, this will provide more complete at-a-glance status information without changing the familiar output format.
