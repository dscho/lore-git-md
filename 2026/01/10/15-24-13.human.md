This is the second and final patch in Harald Nordgren's series to enhance `git status` with push tracking information, now in its 21st iteration and ready for merging. The patch adds display of divergence information relative to the push destination branch when it differs from the upstream tracking branch.

The implementation introduces a new `get_remote_push_branch()` helper to resolve the push tracking reference, then extends `format_tracking_info()` to show both upstream and push relationships when they differ. The output maintains the same familiar format, with push status appearing on a new line. For instance, when tracking origin/main but pushing to origin/feature, status will show both relationships with appropriate advice messages.

The patch includes extensive test coverage (262 lines) for various scenarios including remapped refspecs, multiple remotes, and different divergence states. After 21 rounds of review incorporating feedback from Junio Hamano, Ben Knoble, Phillip Wood and others, the implementation is polished and complete - the variable naming was finalized in this version per Junio's suggestion (`cmp_fetch`/`push_cmp_fetch`).

This concludes a well-executed effort to provide users visibility into both their upstream and push tracking relationships, particularly useful when working on feature branches that track different branches than they push to. The series is now merge-ready with all technical concerns addressed.
