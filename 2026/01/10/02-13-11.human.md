This is a minor code cleanup observation from Junio C Hamano on the final version of Harald Nordgren's patch series to show push remote tracking branch comparisons in `git status`. 

Junio points out a small inefficiency in the divergence message formatting - the `Q_()` pluralization check uses `ours + theirs` as its selector, but the control flow ensures both values are always at least 1 (so the sum is always ≥2). This means the singular form of the message can never be triggered, making the pluralization check redundant. 

This isn't a functional issue introduced by the series (the behavior existed before), just a minor optimization opportunity that could be cleaned up in a follow-up patch. The observation doesn't blocking merging the current series, which Junio has already approved as technically sound after 19 iterations of review. 

The thread shows the maintainer's attention to detail even in finalized patches, though in this case the note is more of a future cleanup suggestion than a required change.
