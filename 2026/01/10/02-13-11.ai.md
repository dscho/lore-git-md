This is a minor code observation from Junio C Hamano about a potential optimization in the push tracking display series. He notes that in the branch comparison formatting logic, the `Q_()` pluralization check uses `ours + theirs` to select between singular and plural forms, but the control flow ensures both values are always at least 1 (so the sum is always ≥2). This means the singular form can never be triggered, making the pluralization check redundant.

The observation doesn't affect functionality - the code works correctly as written - but points out an opportunity to simplify by removing the unused singular case. This is the kind of subtle detail Junio often catches during review. The series has been through 19 iterations and is otherwise ready, with this being a small polish that could be addressed in a follow-up if desired. The core push tracking feature remains unaffected.
