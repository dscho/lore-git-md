This is the second and final patch in Harald Nordgren's series to enhance `git status` output, now in its 20th iteration after extensive review. The patch adds push tracking branch comparison to `git status` when the push destination differs from the upstream branch.

The change shows both the traditional upstream comparison and an additional push branch comparison when they differ, with appropriate advice messages for each case. For example, when tracking origin/main but pushing to origin/feature, it will show divergence from main and ahead/behind status for feature. The implementation handles various edge cases including remapped refspecs and different remote configurations.

The patch includes 262 lines of new test coverage verifying all scenarios, from simple ahead/behind cases to complex remapping situations. Core reviewers Junio C Hamano, Ben Knoble, and Phillip Wood have signed off on the approach after multiple rounds of feedback. The code follows Git conventions with careful flag handling (BRANCH_MODE_PULL/PUSH) and maintains all existing behavior.

This represents the culmination of a long review process and appears ready for merging, providing users with more complete branch tracking information in their workflow.
