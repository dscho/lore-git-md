This is the final iteration of Harald Nordgren's long-running series to enhance `git status` to show divergence information for both upstream and push tracking branches. After 21 versions and extensive review from Junio Hamano, Ben Knoble, and Phillip Wood, the series is now in merge-ready form.

The first patch refactors the branch comparison formatting logic into a new helper function, while the second implements the actual push tracking display. The implementation cleanly extends the existing output format, showing both relationships when they differ (e.g. showing divergence from origin/main and origin/feature). The series maintains all existing behavior including quick mode and advice messages.

Junio has approved the final technical approach, including the naming convention for comparison variables (`cmp_fetch`/`push_cmp_fetch`). One remaining pluralization issue was noted but will be handled separately. The patches have comprehensive test coverage and follow Git's coding standards.

This represents a well-vetted improvement to `git status` that will help users better understand their branch relationships. The extensive review process has produced a clean, maintainable implementation that's ready for merging.
