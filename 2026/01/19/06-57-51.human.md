Patrick notes that while revisions typically resolve to commits, they can technically name any object type (like trees or blobs). Junio provides historical context, recalling that "revision" was originally used more broadly but became commit-associated through usage patterns, as seen in core components like `revision.c`. The discussion touches on philosophical questions about whether invalid references (like `HEAD~10` when HEAD has fewer than 10 commits) should be considered revisions.
