This is part 10 of Patrick Steinhardt's 14-part ODB abstraction series, which enables pluggable storage backends in Git. The patch focuses on removing direct uses of `for_each_{loose,packed}_object()` in favor of the new backend-aware iteration interfaces.

The key change here is in `builtin/cat-file.c`, where packed object iteration is updated to use the new `packfile_store_for_each_object()` function rather than the old `for_each_packed_object()`. Justin Tobler noted that this remains backend-specific (unlike the more generic `odb_for_each_object()` introduced earlier in the series) and Patrick confirms this is intentional - packed object handling with bitmaps requires special consideration that makes full generalization non-trivial at this stage.

The exchange shows this series is in its final refinement phase, with Justin suggesting (and Patrick implementing) a small documentation improvement to clarify why packed iteration remains backend-specific. This patch demonstrates the careful balance being struck between immediate technical needs and leaving room for future generalization - a theme throughout this foundational refactoring work.
