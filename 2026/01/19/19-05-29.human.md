This is a discussion about naming for a new `--maximal` option in `git rev-list`, part of an ongoing thread about Derrick Stolee's feature patch to filter commits to those not reachable from others in the range.

Johannes Sixt continues the debate about the option's name, arguing that `--maximal` could be confusing when used with `git log` since it might imply an opposite of the existing `--minimal` option (which is itself poorly named but established). The thread has seen multiple naming alternatives proposed, including `--only-maximal`, `--hide-reachable`, and `--topmost-only`, reflecting Git's historical preference for intuitive over mathematically precise terminology.

The discussion remains focused on user experience concerns rather than implementation quality, with Johannes maintaining a constructive tone while pressing the importance of clear, unambiguous naming.
