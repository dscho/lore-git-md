This is a review comment from Jeff King (Peff) on Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series, specifically addressing patch 02/14 which proposed changing flag parameters from enum types to unsigned integers.

Peff argues that using the enum type is preferable for flag parameters, citing two main reasons: better human readability (the enum documents which flags are valid) and potential compiler benefits (ensuring proper type sizes for large flag sets). He notes this is a common pattern in the codebase, with examples where enums are consistently used for flags.

The discussion focuses narrowly on the technical merits of enum vs unsigned for flag parameters, with Peff raising concerns that the proposed change might actually be a step backward. He suggests keeping the enum type in the packfile.h changes shown in the diff, though he doesn't object to the broader series' goals.

This is a focused technical review from one of Git's most experienced contributors, offering concrete feedback about a specific implementation choice in an otherwise large and complex series. The comment doesn't challenge the overall approach of the ODB abstraction work, but rather suggests a refinement to maintain consistency with existing patterns in the codebase.
