This is a documentation patch series (v2) clarifying the terminology and behavior of `git worktree prune` and related commands. The discussion has evolved from initial terminology standardization to broader documentation improvements about worktree management.

The series addresses inconsistencies in how Git describes worktrees with absent directories, standardizing on "missing" terminology. It also clarifies that the `--expire` option only applies to missing worktrees, not moved ones, and improves the help text by replacing "expire" with the clearer "prune" terminology.

Junio C Hamano is following up to check on progress, as the maintainers have been expecting an update. The thread shows active collaboration between the author (Sam Bostock) and reviewers (Eric Sunshine and Junio), with most technical points resolved but some final documentation phrasing still being refined. The changes are small but meaningful for users trying to understand worktree management.
