This is a performance optimization patch for the sparse-checkout pattern sorting, now marked as ready for merging by both Derrick Stolee and Junio Hamano. The patch series from Amisha Chhajed has gone through seven iterations to optimize the pattern list construction from O(n^2) to O(n log n) by switching from string_list_insert() to string_list_append() followed by sorting. The changes affect builtin/sparse-checkout.c, specifically in sparse_checkout_list() and write_cone_to_file().

The optimization maintains careful handling of duplicates that can occur in the recursive_hashmap from user input, while taking advantage of the uniqueness guarantee from parent_hashmap entries. Test coverage in t1091-sparse-checkout-builtin.sh verifies proper deduplication behavior in both cone mode (order-independent) and non-cone mode (order-sensitive) scenarios. Jeff King identified edge cases that required keeping duplicate checks even after optimization.

With Derrick Stolee's final approval and Junio Hamano's acknowledgment, this series is now merge-ready pending only minor test formatting cleanups. The discussion concluded that the algorithmic improvement is well-understood and doesn't require extensive benchmarking beyond what's already noted in the commit message. This work fits into the broader sparse-checkout/sparse-index optimization efforts led by Derrick Stolee and Elijah Newren.
