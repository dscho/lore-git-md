This is a contributor housekeeping patch that continues the discussion about mailmap behavior and identity normalization in Git. Phillip Wood follows up on Jeff King's explanation about how mailmap entries currently affect different parts of the system. 

The thread has clarified that while mailmap entries don't affect raw commit metadata or most trailer displays, they are used by `git shortlog` when parsing trailers as identities (implemented in commit 56d5dde752). Jeff mentions an unmerged proof-of-concept patch that would have expanded mailmap's reach to more trailer contexts, which he still uses locally but was never adopted upstream.

Phillip's original mailmap patch remains valid for its purpose of standardizing his contributor identity across commits, though the discussion reveals the limitations of mailmap's current scope. The exchange serves as useful documentation about mailmap's design - it normalizes author/committer names while generally leaving trailers intact unless specifically parsed by tools like shortlog. The thread leaves open the possibility of future enhancements to Git's identity normalization capabilities while clearly documenting the current behavior boundaries.
