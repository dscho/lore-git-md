This is part 9/10 of Ezekiel Newren's series refactoring the xdiff subsystem to prepare for future optimizations and Rust interoperability. The patch focuses on `xdl_cleanup_records()`, decoupling it from the classifier to enable moving it between files.

Phillip Wood questions whether the change provides meaningful performance benefits, noting it makes a second pass over data in the Myers case while offering minimal savings for patience/histogram diffs. He suggests the Rust conversion rationale might be more compelling and echoes René Scharfe's earlier comment that this doesn't fully demonstrate the IVec API's value.

The technical changes involve:
- Removing line counting from `xdl_classify_record()`
- Introducing a new `xoccurrence` struct to track line matches
- Using IVec to manage these occurrences
- Moving match counting into `xdl_cleanup_records()` itself

The patch appears mechanically sound but faces skepticism about its practical benefits. Phillip's review continues the thread's pattern of scrutinizing each change's justification, particularly when performance claims are made without measurements. This suggests the series may need stronger motivation for individual changes beyond the broader Rustification goal.
