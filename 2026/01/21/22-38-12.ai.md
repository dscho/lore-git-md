This is a contributor housekeeping patch that updates the `.mailmap` file to properly consolidate Jean-Noël Avila's contributions under his canonical identity. The patch was originally submitted by Kristoffer Haugsbakk after noticing that release notes incorrectly listed Avila as a new contributor when his contributions came through GitGitGadget.

Maintainer Junio C Hamano has now reviewed and accepted the patch, noting that he expected GitGitGadget to handle author attribution more cleanly. The change adds three new mappings to associate Avila's corporate email, personal Gmail, and GitGitGadget contributions with his primary identity (jn.avila@free.fr). This follows the standard `.mailmap` pattern seen in similar attribution fixes.

The patch is straightforward with no functional impact - it only affects how contributions are displayed in tools like `git shortlog`. The included test commands demonstrate how to verify the mappings work as intended. This is a simple but important change to maintain accurate contributor records, especially for someone as active in documentation and translation work as Jean-Noël Avila.
