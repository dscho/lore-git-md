This is a technical discussion about overflow-safe arithmetic APIs in Git, emerging from Taylor Blau's multi-pack-index compaction series. The conversation focuses on the trade-offs between different approaches to handling integer arithmetic overflow, specifically in the context of the `midx-write.c` changes from the series.

Patrick Steinhardt proposes an alternative API design that combines the safety of overflow checking with the flexibility of direct computation. His suggested `u64_mult()` helper would perform both the overflow check and the multiplication in a single atomic operation, returning a boolean to indicate success and storing the result in an out-parameter. This addresses the potential for mismatched checks and computations that exists with the current pattern of separate `unsigned_mult_overflows()` calls followed by direct arithmetic.

Taylor initially expresses hesitation about keeping specialized functions for limited use cases but acknowledges the readability and safety benefits of Patrick's approach. He suggests this pattern might be more appropriate for the frequently used `st_*` family of overflow-checking functions rather than the `u64_*` variants introduced in this series.

The discussion represents a refinement of utility function design that extends beyond the immediate MIDX compaction work. While the core functionality of the series is complete and approved, this exchange highlights ongoing attention to foundational API patterns in the codebase. The proposed change would affect how overflow-safe arithmetic is implemented across Git, not just in the MIDX subsystem.
