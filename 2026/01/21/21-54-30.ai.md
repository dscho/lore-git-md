Hook subsystem refactoring (part 6/12). This patch addresses a specific regression in the pre-push hook where some users expect separate stdout and stderr streams, contrary to the hook API's assumption of merged streams. The change introduces a configurable `stdout_to_stderr` flag in `struct run_hooks_opt`, defaulting to 1 (maintaining backward compatibility) while allowing hooks like pre-push to opt out of stream merging. The implementation is minimal - just a one-line change in `hook.c` to respect the flag and a corresponding addition to the hook API documentation in `hook.h`. This maintains the series' pattern of incremental improvements while handling a real-world compatibility issue reported by Chris Darroch, with design input from brian m. carlson. The change is well-contained and clearly motivated, fitting cleanly into the broader hook refactoring effort.
