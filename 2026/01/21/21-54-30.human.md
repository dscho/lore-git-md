This is part 6 of a 12-patch series refactoring Git's hook subsystem to support parallel execution and better stream handling. The patch addresses a specific regression where the pre-push hook needs separate stdout and stderr streams, whereas the hook API was previously forcing them to be merged.

The change makes the stdout-to-stderr behavior configurable via a new `stdout_to_stderr` flag in `struct run_hooks_opt`, defaulting to true for backward compatibility. The implementation is minimal - just one line change in `hook.c` to respect the flag, plus documentation in `hook.h`. The motivation comes from real user reports (Chris Darroch) and suggestions from brian m. carlson.

This is a focused, well-justified change that maintains the series' overall direction while fixing a concrete compatibility issue. The patch has already been reviewed and signed off by multiple contributors in previous iterations, suggesting it's a non-controversial piece of the larger hook refactoring effort. The change is small but important for ensuring the new hook API doesn't break existing pre-push hook use cases.
