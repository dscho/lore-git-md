This is patch 2/10 in Ezekiel Newren's series refactoring the xdiff subsystem to prepare for future optimizations and Rust interoperability. The patch creates an explicit `xdl_do_classic_diff()` function to separate the classic Myers diff algorithm from the preparation code.

The discussion with Phillip Wood clarifies that while Rust compatibility is a side benefit, the primary motivation is architectural - moving diff-specific logic out of the general preparation code. Phillip suggests some stylistic improvements to the control flow that Ezekiel notes will be addressed in a later patch.

The changes are part of a larger effort to reorganize xdiff internals while preserving behavior, with particular attention to type safety and future maintainability. The series appears technically sound and is nearing readiness for maintainer consideration after addressing minor review feedback.
