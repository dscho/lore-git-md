This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 10-part series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database (ODB) backends. The series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, with this patch completing the architectural shift by updating MIDX (multi-pack index) handling to reference MIDX via packfile stores rather than ODB sources.

The patch makes several technical changes: it removes the `midx` field from `struct odb_source` and adds it to `struct packfile_store`, updates MIDX-related functions in `midx.c`, `odb.c`, and `packfile.c` to work with the new structure, and modifies cleanup paths accordingly. The changes are relatively contained (43 lines changed across 5 files) given the architectural significance, maintaining the series' pattern of behavior-preserving refactoring.

Junio Hamano indicates the series looks ready for inclusion in 'next' pending any last-minute feedback, noting that the v3 iteration only contained minor fixes (a commit message typo) from v2. The series builds on two other recently merged topics and has already incorporated detailed review feedback from Justin Tobler and Toon Claes during earlier iterations. This marks the completion of a significant step toward pluggable ODB backends, though Patrick acknowledges the abstractions remain "leaky" for now.
