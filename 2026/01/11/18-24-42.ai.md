Platform compatibility patch series for macOS iconv behavior, version 1-1. This is a two-patch follow-up to René Scharfe's earlier rs/macos-iconv-workaround (now in master) that addresses UTF encoding conversion quirks in macOS 14/15 where iconv omits byte order marks (BOM).

The series adds build infrastructure (Makefile/config.mak.uname changes) to detect affected macOS versions, then implements the workaround in utf8.c with minimal changes (21 lines total). Junio indicates he will queue this version after the author corrected an email address issue from the initial submission. The patches appear narrowly scoped to handle the specific platform quirk without broader impact.
