This thread documents Michael Lyons' conversion of git-bisect.adoc to the new synopsis-style AsciiDoc format, part of Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing effort to standardize Git's documentation. The initial patch (v1) was reviewed and approved by Jean-Noël with minor feedback about preferring `[synopsis]` formatting for command examples like "git bisect run". 

Junio Hamano then raised a broader question about when to use `[synopsis]` versus verbatim blocks in documentation, seeking to establish clearer guidelines for future contributors. Jean-Noël responded that `[synopsis]` is preferred when examples follow the formal command grammar with placeholders (like `<commit>`), as it enables better rendering and explanations, though he acknowledged these conventions are still being refined through discussion. 

While the git-bisect conversion itself is ready to merge, this exchange may help shape documentation standards for the larger conversion project. Jean-Noël's position as the documentation lead gives particular weight to his formatting preferences, though the project remains open to evolving these guidelines as more conversions are completed.
