This is a bugfix patch in the `git-history` series, addressing detached HEAD handling. Elijah Newren contributes a final fixup patch that ensures the new `git-history` command properly handles detached HEAD states when rewording commits.

The patch makes three key changes:
1. Adds HEAD to the revision walk when operating on branches (previously only `--branches` was used)
2. Updates decoration handling to properly account for detached HEAD states in both `replay_revisions()` and `handle_reference_updates()`
3. Adds 47 lines of test coverage across two new scenarios: rewording commits in the middle of a detached HEAD history and rewording the detached HEAD itself

The changes touch core replay infrastructure shared with `git-replay`, though the focus here is specifically on making `git-history reword` work correctly in detached HEAD states. The implementation carefully avoids duplicate updates to the same branch by only processing HEAD decorations when actually detached.

This appears to be the last technical issue in the series, building on Patrick Steinhardt's v9 submission.
