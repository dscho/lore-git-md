This is the final approval for a 5-patch series preparing Windows symlink support, co-authored by Johannes Schindelin and Karsten Blees. Junio Hamano gives his sign-off, indicating the series looks good and will be queued for the 'next' integration branch after a brief waiting period for any last feedback.

The series addresses several technical prerequisites across mingw compatibility, config parsing, strbuf utilities, and path handling to properly support symlinks on Windows. Key changes include fixing `mingw_getcwd()` to resolve symlinks, ensuring early `core.*` config parsing during init, optimizing `strbuf_readlink()` buffer handling, and making path separator handling platform-agnostic in `trim_last_path_component()`.

With all five preparatory patches reviewed and approved - including sign-offs from the Windows experts (Schindelin and Blees) and now the maintainer (Hamano) - this work is cleared for integration. The changes maintain cross-platform compatibility while establishing the necessary foundation for Windows symlink support to be built upon in a subsequent topic. The series represents a net reduction of 20 lines across 6 files while solving several subtle edge cases.
