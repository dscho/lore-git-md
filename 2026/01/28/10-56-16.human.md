This is patch 10/10 in a series refactoring Git's xdiff subsystem, with Phillip Wood providing review feedback on the code organization changes.

His feedback suggests the current organization may be reasonable as-is.

Phillip does confirm one concrete improvement - removing an unused include from xprepare.c - but his main feedback casts doubt on whether the larger structural change provides clear benefits.

This appears to be a minor point in an otherwise well-received series, with Phillip's concerns focused on maintainability rather than functionality. The discussion remains constructive, with Phillip acknowledging the core change makes sense while questioning the broader reorganization.
