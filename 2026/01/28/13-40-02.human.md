This is a bugfix discussion for git-gui's diff viewer tab alignment on Windows. The thread has evolved from an initial patch that modified text content (rejected for breaking staging functionality) to a current approach using Tk's `apply_tab_size` configuration.

Johannes Sixt (the git-gui maintainer) responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about historical context - specifically why single-parent diffs have used `apply_tab_size 0` while two-parent diffs use `apply_tab_size 1` for the past 14 years. He agrees this distinction needs consideration, noting the current patch's tabstop values may not be optimal since diff output always starts with a +/-/space character.

The discussion reveals deeper technical nuances: tab alignment must work correctly for both regular diffs and combined diffs while preserving the ability to stage individual lines/hunks. Johannes points out that Gitk's implementation adjusted its initial approach to what's being proposed here, suggesting there may be prior art to reference.

While the architectural direction (using Tk configuration rather than text modification) is sound and meets the maintainer's original requirements, the specific tabstop calculations need refinement. The patch appears ready for regular diffs but requires verification with combined diffs and potential adjustment to maintain historical behavior differences between diff types.
