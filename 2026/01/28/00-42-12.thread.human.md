The thread explores a proposal to extend Git's ignore mechanism by supporting multiple named ignore files (like `*.gitignore`) alongside the standard `.gitignore`, aiming to better organize rules in large repositories. ZH L originally suggested this could help separate editor, build, and other rules into distinct files while maintaining compatibility.

brian m. carlson raised objections, arguing editor-specific rules belong in personal ignore files and pointing out that automatic loading wouldn't be truly backward compatible, as older Git versions wouldn't recognize the new patterns. They suggested explicit inclusion via pragma might be preferable if implemented. In response, ZH L acknowledged these concerns, clarifying they were primarily seeking feedback rather than pushing for immediate implementation. The discussion remains open, with no consensus yet on whether the feature is needed or how to address compatibility concerns if pursued. The core question is whether the benefits of organized ignore rules outweigh the potential drawbacks and complexity.
