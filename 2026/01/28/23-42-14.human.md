This is a bugfix for git-gui's diff viewer tab alignment on Windows, following up on a thread where the maintainer (Johannes Sixt) initially rejected a text conversion approach but has now accepted a configuration-based solution that matches gitk's long-standing behavior.

Junio Hamano notes that gitk has used customized tabstops for nearly 20 years (with different offsets for regular diffs vs. merges), so aligning git-gui with this precedent is reasonable. However, he pushes back slightly on the severity claims - while the misalignment is real, its long existence without complaint suggests "show stopper" may be overstating the impact.

The technical solution (using Tk's `apply_tab_size` with context-specific offsets) has been validated across all diff types and preserves staging functionality. This appears to be the final piece of consensus needed, with Johannes Sixt having reversed his initial opposition after realizing gitk's precedent. The patch seems ready to proceed, though Junio's comment serves as a gentle reminder to keep severity assessments proportional.
