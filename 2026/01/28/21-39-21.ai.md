This is part 6/12 of the hook subsystem refactoring series, focusing on allowing separate stdout and stderr streams for hooks. The patch addresses a specific case where the pre-push hook needs distinct streams, contrary to the API's default behavior of merging stdout to stderr.

The change introduces a new `stdout_to_stderr` flag in `struct run_hooks_opt` (defaulting to 1 for backward compatibility) that controls whether hook output streams should be merged. The pre-push hook will use this to maintain its existing behavior where some users expect separate streams. The implementation is minimal - just a one-line change in `hook.c` to respect the flag, plus documentation.

This patch follows the pattern established in earlier parts of the series where real-world hook behaviors are preserved while generalizing the API. It builds on the parallel execution infrastructure from patches 1-5 and demonstrates the series' careful attention to backward compatibility. The change is well-motivated by a reported use case from Chris Darroch and incorporates feedback from brian m. carlson.

Key technical details for future reference:
- New `stdout_to_stderr` bitfield in `run_hooks_opt` (default 1)
- Pre-push will be the first hook to set this to 0
- Maintains all existing behavior for other hooks
- Simple plumbing change in `pick_next_hook()`
- Documentation added to explain the exception case
