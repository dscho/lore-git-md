This is part 7/10 of Ezekiel Newren's series refactoring the xdiff subsystem, with an eye toward future optimizations and Rust interoperability. The patch moves the `dstart` field from `xdfile_t` to `xdfenv_t` as it's a property of the diff environment rather than individual files.

Phillip Wood points out a potential issue with the change - while `dstart` is indeed the same for both files, `dend` remains file-specific and is stored as an index rather than an offset. He notes this makes the new arrangement less clean than initially thought, since calculating `dend` from an offset would be error-prone. This suggests the refactoring might need reconsideration or additional justification.

The patch itself is mechanical, updating all references to `xdf1.dstart` and `xdf2.dstart` to use the new `env->delta_start` instead. The change affects several xdiff algorithms (histogram, patience) and the core preparation logic. This appears to be part of a broader effort to reorganize xdiff's data structures for better clarity and future compatibility.
