Refactoring patch in a series addressing bitmap and bisect functionality issues with exact ref matches. This first patch consolidates duplicate logic for iterating over preferred bitmap tips (configured via "pack.preferBitmapTips") into a new `for_each_preferred_bitmap_tip()` function. The change removes the last external caller of `bitmap_preferred_tips()`, allowing it to be made file-local to pack-bitmap.c. The patch touches pack-objects.c, pack-bitmap.c, pack-bitmap.h, and repack-midx.c, standardizing how both pack-objects and midx repacking handle bitmap tip iteration. This is preparatory cleanup for fixing a BUG() that occurs when exact branch names are used in the configuration. The patch follows Patrick Steinhardt's characteristic thorough approach - combining refactoring with bugfix preparation while maintaining clear separation of concerns.
