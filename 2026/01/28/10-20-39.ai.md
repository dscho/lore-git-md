This is a bugfix patch for git-gui's diff viewer, addressing tab alignment inconsistencies with gitk on Windows. The patch follows up on previous discussion where an initial text conversion approach was rejected by maintainer Johannes Sixt due to breaking staging functionality.

The current solution uses Tk's native `apply_tab_size 1` configuration to adjust tab rendering without modifying the underlying text content, preserving the ability to stage individual lines. The change makes git-gui's tab display consistent with gitk and command-line `git diff` output.

Key technical points from the thread context:
- The patch avoids the text modification approach of v2 that would break staging
- Junio Hamano raised concerns about interaction with combined diffs (two-parent diffs)
- Historical behavior from commit a43c5f51 suggests different tab handling may be needed for regular vs combined diffs
- The current patch doesn't yet address these combined diff implications

The patch appears technically sound for regular diffs but leaves open questions about combined diff behavior. Johannes Sixt's original constraints are met (no text modification), but Junio's review suggests the solution may need refinement to handle all diff contexts correctly. The change is minimal and focused, but further discussion is likely needed around the combined diff case.
