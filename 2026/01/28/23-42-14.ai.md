This is a follow-up in the git-gui tab alignment bugfix thread, where Junio C Hamano weighs in on the proposed solution. The discussion has shifted from technical implementation to assessing the severity of the issue.

Junio confirms that gitk has used custom tabstop settings (0 for plain files, 1 for regular commits, and $np for merges) since 2007, making the current git-gui patch consistent with long-standing gitk behavior. However, he pushes back on characterizing the misalignment as a "show stopper" or making diffs "unreadable", noting that the discrepancy between git-gui and gitk has existed for nearly two decades without significant complaint.

The email serves as a reality check on the urgency of the fix - while the technical approach is now validated (matching gitk's precedent), Junio suggests the impact may have been overstated. This doesn't block the patch but provides context about its priority level. The thread appears headed toward resolution with the maintainer's blessing for the technical solution but a more measured assessment of the problem's severity.
