This thread is about a documentation patch by Harald Nordgren that adds `--abort` hints to Git rebase failure and status messages for consistency. The patch is currently at its first version (v1) and targets two scenarios: when an "exec" step fails during rebase (previously only `--continue` was mentioned) and in rebase status help text during commit splitting or editing.

The uncontroversial part of the patch updates status messages in `wt-status.c` and its tests, following established patterns. The contested change is the addition of `--abort` advice in the exec-failure case in `sequencer.c`. Phillip Wood objects to this, arguing that exec failures (like test failures) are usually minor and continuing is preferable, making the `--abort` hint potentially misleading. Harald defends the change as a consistency improvement.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has now weighed in, reframing the debate. He sees the `--abort` hint as a low-cost "escape hatch" for new users in confusing situations, even if experienced users rarely need it. While he acknowledges Phillip’s workflow concerns, he treats the hint as a harmless training aid rather than a workflow recommendation. The disagreement remains unresolved, but Junio’s stance may shift the discussion toward whether the hint should be reworded for neutrality or kept as-is. No code changes have been proposed yet.
