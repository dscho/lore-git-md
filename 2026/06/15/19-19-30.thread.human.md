This thread is about a documentation patch by Harald Nordgren that adds `git rebase --abort` hints to two places where only `--continue` was previously mentioned: when an "exec" step fails during rebase, and in status help text during commit splitting/editing. The goal is to improve UI consistency by making abort advice available in all rebase failure scenarios.

The initial patch (v1) makes straightforward text additions to `sequencer.c`, `wt-status.c`, and a test file, following established patterns from prior rebase message improvements. However, Phillip Wood has raised a substantive concern about the exec-failure case, arguing that failed exec commands (often minor issues like test failures) are fundamentally different from conflicts. He believes users are more likely to want to fix and continue in exec failures, whereas conflicts often require aborting. His feedback suggests the current approach might mislead users into thinking they *should* abort when continuing is usually the better choice. The status-message changes appear uncontroversial, but the exec-failure advice may need rethinking. No other reviews or decisions have been posted yet.
