This is patch 5/7 in Harald Nordgren's v15 series introducing `git branch --delete-merged`, a feature for safely cleaning up local branches. The patch implements the core functionality after establishing the foundational `--forked` filter in earlier patches.

The new `--delete-merged` option removes local branches that meet three criteria: 1) match the `--forked` filter pattern, 2) have their tip reachable from their upstream, and 3) push to a different location than their upstream (avoiding deletion of main tracking branches). It includes several safety mechanisms - never deleting checked-out branches, skipping branches with missing upstreams, and silently skipping unmerged branches rather than erroring out.

The implementation uses the new flag-based API introduced in patch 4/7, combining `DELETE_BRANCH_SKIP_UNMERGED` and `DELETE_BRANCH_NO_HEAD_FALLBACK` flags for efficient bulk operations. The patch includes comprehensive tests covering edge cases like deleted upstreams, checked-out branches, and push/upstream divergence. Documentation explains the behavior and safety mechanisms clearly.
