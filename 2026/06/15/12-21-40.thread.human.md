Patrick Steinhardt proposed a change to improve the reliability of Windows builds in GitLab CI by replacing the flaky Chocolatey package manager with a custom PowerShell script (`ci/install-dependencies.ps1`). This script directly downloads and installs Git, Meson, and Rust, cutting setup time from about three minutes to one while adding version-controlled caching. The patch modifies `.gitlab-ci.yml` and introduces the new script, aligning with GitLab CI best practices but hardcoding dependency versions for now.

Justin Tobler reviewed the patch and endorsed the approach, noting that Chocolatey’s unreliability is a well-known issue and that direct dependency management is a sensible fix. He tested the job on GitLab CI and confirmed it works as intended. His feedback focused on two practical concerns: how often the pinned versions will need updating and whether the speed improvement comes from caching or the download mechanism itself. Neither question challenges the patch’s direction, and his comments on the script’s logic were observational rather than critical. The review suggests the change is a straightforward, well-executed improvement to the build system.
