This is the final version (v6) of Patrick Steinhardt's series introducing a `git history drop` subcommand, completing the functionality for this experimental command. The series has evolved through 6 iterations with extensive review feedback incorporated.

The key addition is the `drop` subcommand which removes a commit from history while replaying its descendants on top of its parent. The implementation required significant refactoring of Git's reset machinery along the way, including:

- Modernizing reset flags to use an enum (RESET_WORKING_TREE_*)
- Renaming `reset_head()` to `reset_working_tree()` to better reflect its purpose
- Adding dry-run capability to reset operations
- Splitting reference updates into Compute/Analyze phases
- Fixing dirty index handling assumptions

The v6 iteration specifically addresses a dry-run interaction issue identified by Junio C Hamano in the reference update path. The series now appears ready for merging with all technical concerns resolved. It includes thorough test coverage (537 lines across new test scripts) and updates the git-history documentation.

This represents a major step forward for the experimental history command while also advancing broader architectural goals like `the_repository` removal and reset machinery modernization. The changes touch multiple core components (builtin/history.c, refs.c, reset.c, read-cache.c) but maintain careful backwards compatibility. Given the extensive review process and Patrick's track record with this command, this series seems poised for smooth integration.
