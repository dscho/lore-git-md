Bugfix follow-up discussing future timestamp handling in Git.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Elijah Newren's patch fixing commit-graph generation number calculations for future-dated commits (those with timestamps beyond 2106-02-07). While acknowledging the correctness of Elijah's specific fix (changing a uint32_t to timestamp_t for parent generation number accumulation), Patrick raises a broader question about Git's handling of future dates.

He suggests expanding test coverage by creating a CI job that runs tests with future timestamps (beyond UINT32_MAX), attaching a prototype patch that modifies test infrastructure to support this. Patrick notes this would likely break many existing tests, though many failures may be benign. He clarifies this suggestion is outside the scope of Elijah's immediate fix but worth considering separately.

The exchange shows two approaches to timestamp edge cases: Elijah's focused correction of a specific bug versus Patrick's interest in more comprehensive future-proofing. Both agree the current patch is correct for its limited scope.
