This is a thread update from Kristofer Karlsson regarding his effort to consolidate reachability functions in commit-reach.c. After multiple iterations and feedback from Derrick Stolee (original author of the affected code), Kristofer has decided to abandon the patch series due to concerns about potential performance regressions in repositories without commit-graphs.

The decision comes after technical discussion revealed that the proposed consolidation would require maintaining complex traversal logic to preserve performance characteristics across different repository states. Derrick had objected to an earlier version's configurable traversal approach as overly complex, suggesting instead an automatic selection mechanism (DFS with commit-graphs, BFS otherwise). Kristofer now concludes the effort isn't worth the maintenance overhead.

This wraps up the technical side of the thread, while the parallel discussion about contribution norms (sparked by Derrick's observation that Kristofer tends to implement review feedback directly rather than discussing approach changes first) continues separately. Junio Hamano had suggested documenting these interaction patterns in project contribution guidelines, a suggestion that Weijie Yuan is now addressing in a documentation patch.

The thread demonstrates how even well-intentioned cleanups can uncover non-obvious performance constraints, and shows the project's preference for abandoning marginal improvements when they risk introducing complexity or regressions. Kristofer's graceful withdrawal models good contributor behavior by recognizing when an effort isn't yielding sufficient value to justify further work.
