This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's v6 series introducing the `git history drop` command, specifically focusing on improvements to the reset machinery. The patch adds flexibility to `reset_working_tree()` by allowing callers to specify the "from" commit OID rather than always deriving it from HEAD.

The change introduces a new `oid_from` field in `reset_working_tree_options` that callers can use to provide an explicit starting point for the reset operation. This is particularly relevant for the history drop functionality where the reset may need to operate relative to a commit other than HEAD. The implementation maintains backward compatibility by falling back to reading HEAD when `oid_from` is NULL.

This is a straightforward but useful enhancement to the reset API that enables more precise control over reset operations. The change is well-contained (just 9 lines of diff) and follows the pattern of other recent reset machinery improvements in this series. As with the rest of the v6 series, this appears ready for integration.
