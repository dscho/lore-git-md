Performance optimization for `git ls-files` with pathspecs.

Junio Hamano has marked Tamir Duberstein's performance optimization patch for `git ls-files --deleted` and `--modified` as ready for inclusion in the 'next' integration branch. The patch moves pathspec filtering before `lstat()` calls to avoid unnecessary filesystem operations, showing dramatic speedups (65.8s to 5.0s in one test case).

While the original patch only applied the optimization for single pathspecs out of performance concerns, Junio agrees with Jeff King's earlier feedback that the optimization should apply universally for consistency with Git's other filtering optimizations. He notes the special-casing of single pathspecs would be harder to justify long-term than a simpler, consistent approach, as long as the performance tradeoffs are clearly documented.

The patch is now considered complete with all technical feedback addressed, including expanded test coverage for wildcard pathspecs and error conditions. Junio's approval means this will be part of Git's upcoming releases, providing significant performance improvements for large repositories when using these `ls-files` options with pathspecs.
