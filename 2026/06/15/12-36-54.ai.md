This is a technical discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ref backend refactoring series, where Jeff King and Patrick are evaluating the performance impact of moving to absolute paths in the reference backend. Jeff's benchmarks show a 10x slowdown in pathological cases with deep directory hierarchies and many loose refs, though both agree this is mitigated by modern ref storage formats like packed-refs and reftable.

Patrick expresses some hesitation about accepting the performance regression, suggesting they might defer the final patch (which removes chdir_notify entirely) while merging the rest of the series. He also revisits the idea of using openat(3p) as a more robust solution that could avoid both the performance hit and TOCTOU issues, though acknowledges Windows compatibility challenges. Jeff agrees this could be a promising direction if implemented with a suitable abstraction layer.

The discussion remains constructive, with both contributors weighing technical tradeoffs while maintaining support for the overall series.
