This is a review of patch 4/6 in Harald Nordgren's v12 series adding `git branch --prune-merged` functionality. The series has already gone through extensive review over 12 iterations and is now in its final refinement stage.

Phillip Wood provides a substantive review focusing on two main points:

1. **Command syntax**: He suggests allowing positional arguments alongside `--prune-merged` (e.g., `git branch --prune-merged origin/master 'feature*'`) rather than requiring repeated flags, which would make the command more flexible while maintaining safety.

2. **Naming**: He questions whether "prune-merged" is the best name, noting that:
   - Other branch-deletion commands use "delete" rather than "prune"
   - The behavior doesn't exactly match what `--merged` lists
   - "delete-forked" might be more accurate

The review acknowledges the technical implementation is sound but will need adjustments for full refname matching and filtering placement in `apply_ref_filter()`. Phillip indicates the remaining patches in the series look reasonable as well.

This feedback comes late in the series' lifecycle (v12) and focuses on final polish rather than fundamental issues. The core functionality - safely deleting merged branches with comprehensive safety checks - remains unchanged and appears ready for merging after these interface refinements.
