This is patch 4/6 in Harald Nordgren's series adding `git branch --prune-merged` for safe automated local branch cleanup. The series is now in its final polishing phase after 13 iterations of refinement.

The patch introduces the core `--prune-merged` functionality that deletes local branches whose work has been integrated into their upstream branches. It builds on the `--forked` filtering capability added in earlier patches to identify candidate branches, then applies several safety checks before deletion:

1. Branches must have their tip reachable from their upstream (checked locally without fetching)
2. Three types of branches are preserved:
   - Currently checked-out branches
   - Branches whose upstream no longer exists locally
   - Branches where push destination equals upstream (to avoid deleting main branches)

The implementation uses the ref-filter infrastructure for branch selection and handles unmerged branches gracefully by warning rather than erroring. The patch includes comprehensive tests covering all the safety mechanisms and edge cases.
