The email reinforces the core tension in this thread between:
1) Peff's preference for leveraging existing URL-rewriting mechanisms
2) The need for a more systematic solution that can handle deep submodule dependencies without per-repository configuration

Benson's response indicates the discussion is moving toward finding a middle ground that preserves the flexibility of URL rewriting while addressing the scalability challenges in real-world submodule-heavy repositories. The mention of upstream-maintained mirrors suggests future proposals might explore ways to encode mirror information directly in repository metadata.
