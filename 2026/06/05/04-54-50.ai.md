This is a continuation of the discussion about repository mirroring and submodule URL resolution, with Benson Muite responding to Jeff King's (Peff) proposal to use `url.*.insteadOf` for mirroring.

Benson acknowledges Peff's URL rewriting approach but highlights a key limitation: it becomes unwieldy when dealing with deeply nested submodule structures across multiple organizations. The core issue is that recursive clones may involve dozens of repositories from different maintainers, making per-repository URL rewriting impractical. Benson suggests that upstream maintainers are better positioned to specify mirror relationships than individual users configuring rewrites.

The email reinforces the thread's central tension between:
1) Peff's preference for leveraging existing URL rewriting mechanisms
2) The need for a more scalable solution when dealing with complex submodule graphs across organizational boundaries

Benson's response indicates that while URL rewriting works for simple cases, it doesn't adequately address the archival use case (like Debian's) where many repositories may need coordinated mirror fallbacks. This keeps open the question of whether some form of repository identity or submodule-specific URL fallback syntax might still be needed alongside general URL rewriting.

The discussion remains focused on finding a solution that balances Git's existing mechanisms with the practical realities of large-scale submodule usage, particularly in archival contexts where original URLs may become unavailable.
