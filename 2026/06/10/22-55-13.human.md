This is the third and final patch in a documentation series fixing AsciiDoc markup issues in Git's config documentation. The patch specifically addresses octothorpe (#) character escaping in git-config.adoc to prevent them from being interpreted as highlight markup in the rendered output.

The change is straightforward: it escapes octothorpes with backslashes when they appear in the description of the `--comment-message` option, ensuring they'll render as literal characters rather than AsciiDoc highlighting syntax. The patch also includes a minor wording tweak ("And" -> "The") for better readability.

This completes the v2 series that has already received positive review feedback, with Junio C Hamano previously approving the approach to octothorpe escaping. The changes are purely mechanical formatting fixes with no semantic impact on the documentation content. The series appears ready for merging as part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git's documentation formatting.
