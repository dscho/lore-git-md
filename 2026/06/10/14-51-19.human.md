Maintainer decision on delta cache optimization.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on Arijit Banerjee's v3 patch series optimizing `index-pack`'s delta resolution, indicating he has no strong preference between the v2 and v3 approaches. The discussion has reached consensus that both versions perform similarly in practice, with v3's more precise memory release mechanism showing no significant runtime impact despite its theoretical advantages.

Banerjee acknowledges Derrick Stolee's earlier question about memory usage, noting that while RSS measurements don't show meaningful changes, v3's structural guarantee of proactive release (rather than cache-pressure eviction) provides cleaner memory management semantics. With all technical concerns addressed and both Jeff King and Stolee satisfied, the series appears merge-ready.

This marks the final step before integration, with the maintainers now positioned to queue the optimization that delivers consistent 13-16% speedups for delta-heavy repositories. The lack of strong preference from Junio suggests the decision may come down to which version was most recently tested, as the practical differences are negligible.
