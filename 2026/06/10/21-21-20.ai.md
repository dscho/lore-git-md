This is a documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of an 11-patch series improving the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. The series follows up on earlier discussion about trailer key format restrictions (ASCII alphanumeric + hyphen only) and aims to provide clearer documentation of the feature.

This specific patch (02/11) makes a terminology refinement, replacing "trailer lines" with "trailers metadata" in the introductory paragraph. The change aligns with the glossary definition of trailers as "key-value metadata" that will be introduced later in the series. The patch maintains the same functionality while making the documentation more precise about the structured nature of trailer information.

The change is small but part of a larger effort to standardize terminology and improve conceptual clarity in the documentation. The patch follows naturally from the previous one (which removed email header comparisons) by further distinguishing trailers from simple line-oriented text.
