This is a technical review of the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to eliminate global state in setup.c, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Justin Tobler provides a substantive review confirming the removal of the now-redundant `git_work_tree_cfg` global variable.

Justin notes that the patch cleanly replaces the global with direct access to the repository format's worktree field, which he had questioned the need for in his review of the first patch. He specifically verifies:
1. The elimination of the global variable in favor of repository format storage
2. The new memory management behavior that prevents leaks by freeing candidate->work_tree
3. The mechanical nature of the remaining changes replacing global references

The review identifies no issues with the implementation and confirms the changes maintain expected behavior while removing technical debt. This continues the pattern of careful examination seen in Justin's prior reviews of this series, particularly around initialization paths and resource management. The patch appears ready to proceed as part of the larger architectural cleanup.
