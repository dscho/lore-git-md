This is the fourth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation series updating the SubmittingPatches guidelines, focusing on commit message conventions. The patch adds documentation for the `Based-on-patch-by` trailer, which is used when incorporating someone else's patch work that wasn't formally submitted with a commit message or Signed-off-by line.

The patch explains that this trailer serves a distinct purpose from `Co-authored-by` - it's specifically for cases where someone provided patch content but didn't submit it as a complete commit. The author notes in the series notes that the wording carefully uses "can be used" rather than stronger language, recognizing that other trailers like `Helped-by` might sometimes be appropriate depending on context.

This continues the series' pattern of documenting existing practices rather than introducing new conventions. The change is straightforward and maintains the low-controversy nature of the overall effort. The patch appears well-considered, with the author showing attention to nuance in the wording choices. As with previous patches in the series, this seems likely to be accepted as it simply codifies current mailing list practices around attribution.
