This is a follow-up discussion about the ongoing refactoring to consolidate reachability functions in commit-reach.c. Derrick Stolee, the original author of both functions being consolidated, responds to Junio Hamano's review of Kristofer Karlsson's patch.

Stolee provides historical context for why the two functions (`get_reachable_subset()` and `tips_reachable_from_bases()`) were implemented differently - primarily due to the availability of commit-graph files at different times (2018 vs 2023). He explains that while the DFS approach in the newer function performs better with commit-graphs (by increasing minimum generation numbers during traversal), the older BFS approach had advantages when commit-graphs weren't available (using commit dates as fallback ordering).

The key technical insight is that the performance difference only matters in repositories without commit-graph files, which Stolee argues is now rare enough to justify the consolidation. He notes that modern Git deployments (especially servers) routinely use commit-graphs, making the newer algorithm's behavior the more relevant case. This addresses Junio's concern about the "should not matter in practice" claim by providing concrete reasoning about real-world usage patterns.

The email reinforces the technical soundness of the consolidation while acknowledging the historical reasons for the divergence. Stolee's response, coming from the original author of both implementations, carries significant weight in validating Kristofer's proposed change. The thread appears to be converging toward merging the consolidation with this additional justification.
