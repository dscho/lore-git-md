This is patch 5/11 in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`, which has already been merged. The patch focuses on improving the introductory explanation of trailer format in the man page.

The change adds clear definitions of key terms ("trailer", "trailer block", "blank line") right after the initial description, before diving into the example. This provides readers with essential vocabulary upfront, making the subsequent content easier to follow. The patch also standardizes the terminology around "blank line" to avoid inconsistency.

This is a documentation refinement that builds on earlier patches in the series (which established the "metadata" terminology) and sets up for the next patch that will define key format restrictions. The change was suggested by D. Ben Knoble and fits well with the series' goal of making the trailer parsing rules more transparent and accessible.
