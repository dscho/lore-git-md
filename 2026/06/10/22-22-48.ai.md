This is the fifth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation series standardizing trailer conventions in Git's SubmittingPatches guidelines. The patch focuses on making trailer markup consistent throughout the document by ensuring all trailer references use backticks and colons (e.g., `Signed-off-by:`).

The changes are mechanical but important for consistency, updating instances where trailers were previously written without backticks or with inconsistent punctuation. The patch affects several sections of SubmittingPatches including the Signed-off-by explanation, AI guidelines, and patch submission instructions.

This continues the series' goal of codifying existing practices around commit message trailers. The changes align with the project's preference for precise documentation and follow the pattern established in earlier patches of the series. As with previous patches, this appears non-controversial and well-scoped, focusing purely on documentation formatting rather than introducing new policies.
