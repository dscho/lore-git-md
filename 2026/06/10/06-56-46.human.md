This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 7-part series to eliminate global state in setup.c as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch focuses on removing the global `git_work_tree_cfg` variable by first making it unnecessary in `builtin/init-db.c`.

The change is a straightforward refactoring that stops `cmd_init_db()` from modifying the global `git_work_tree_cfg` variable, since the worktree location is already be determined through `repo_get_work_tree()`. The patch makes `git_work_tree_cfg` file-local to setup.c and function-local to `cmd_init_db()`, removing it from the global environment scope.

This is a mechanical change that doesn't affect behavior - it's part of the methodical approach Patrick has been taking to eliminate global state by first making callers use proper repository accessors before removing the globals entirely. The patch touches several files but the changes are focused and well-contained.
