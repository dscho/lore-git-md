This is a refactoring patch that removes redundant extern declarations in `read-cache.c` as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch is the first in a 3-part series that migrates configuration settings from global variables to repository-scoped storage.

The change is straightforward: it removes duplicate extern declarations for `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` from `st_mode_from_ce()` since these are already declared in the included `environment.h` header. This is a simple cleanup with no behavior change, maintaining consistency with the project's coding guidelines.

The patch has been reviewed by multiple mentors (Christian Couder, Ayush Chandekar, and Olamide Caleb Bello) and appears ready for inclusion. It demonstrates the incremental approach being taken in this series - tackling small, well-defined pieces of the larger refactoring effort while keeping each change focused and reviewable.
