This is the first patch in the v2 series introducing `--linearize` to `git replay`. It refactors the internal `enum replay_mode` into a single boolean `reverse` flag, eliminating the need for a two-value enum and removing all BUG() checks for invalid enum values.

The patch touches only `replay.c` and is purely preparatory: it changes no behavior. Every enum check (`mode == REPLAY_MODE_PICK` or `mode == REPLAY_MODE_REVERT`) is replaced with a corresponding boolean check (`!reverse` or `reverse`), and the `create_commit` and `pick_regular_commit` helper functions are updated to accept the new flag. The diff shows the mechanical nature of the change—no logic is altered, only the representation of the mode. This refactoring paves the way for the `--linearize` option in patch 3 by simplifying the internal state.
