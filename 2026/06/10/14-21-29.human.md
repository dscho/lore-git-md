This is a bugfix discussion about Git's trailer parsing behavior, where URLs in commit messages are being incorrectly interpreted as trailers. Kristoffer Haugsbakk confirms Jeff King's (Peff) more precise analysis that the core issue is Git's loose trailer matching rather than just the `--only-trailers` behavior.

Kristoffer agrees with Peff's proposed solution to modify `find_separator()` in trailer.c to explicitly exclude lines containing `://` from being considered as trailers. This approach would uniformly handle all URL schemes (HTTP, HTTPS, file) without requiring special cases for each one. The discussion shows consensus forming around this technical solution, with Kristoffer noting the extremely low chance of false negatives (where a legitimate trailer would be incorrectly excluded by this change).

The exchange demonstrates productive collaboration moving from problem identification to a concrete, well-reasoned fix. The proposed change appears straightforward and targeted, addressing all the problematic cases while maintaining existing trailer functionality. This would resolve the issue where URLs at the end of commit messages were being mangled into malformed trailers.
