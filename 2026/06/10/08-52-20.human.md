This is the fourth version of Patrick Steinhardt's patch series introducing a "drop" subcommand to the experimental `git history` command. The series builds on previous work to add commit-dropping capability, allowing users to remove a commit from history while replaying its descendants on top of its parent.

Key changes in v4:
- Renamed `reset_head()` to `reset_working_tree()` to better reflect its scope
- Replaced the `SKIP_REF_UPDATES` flag with a new `UPDATE_HEAD` flag (suggested by Phillip Wood)
- Continued modernization of the reset machinery with enum flags and new capabilities

The series consists of 10 patches that:
1. Refactor index handling and reset machinery
2. Remove `the_repository` usage in reset.c
3-7. Modernize the reset functionality with new flags and capabilities
8-9. Split ref update handling into two phases
10. Implement the actual `drop` subcommand with extensive test coverage

The implementation appears thorough, with 537 lines of new tests across 4 scripts. The discussion around API design has largely converged, with only minor flag semantics being finalized. This continues Patrick's pattern of careful, incremental additions to the history command, building on his previous work while modernizing underlying infrastructure. The changes touch several core files (reset.c, read-cache.c) but maintain backwards compatibility. Given the careful approach and extensive testing, this seems likely to be merged once the final API details are resolved.
