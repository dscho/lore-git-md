This is a documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of an 11-patch series improving the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. The series stems from earlier discussions about trailer key format restrictions (ASCII alphanumeric + hyphen only).

This particular patch removes references to RFC 822 email headers from the command's introduction, arguing the comparison is unnecessary and potentially confusing since most users don't work with email RFCs. The change simplifies the description by presenting trailers as metadata lines without drawing parallels to email standards. The patch keeps one reference to email line folding but removes the disclaimer about not following other RFC 822 rules, since the comparison is being deemphasized.

The change is clean and well-motivated, with the commit message providing historical context about why the RFC 822 comparison existed originally. This appears to be a straightforward documentation improvement that makes the man page more accessible without altering any functionality.
