This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation series updating SubmittingPatches guidelines, focusing on discouraging Linux-specific trailers like `Fixes:` and `Link:`. 

The patch explains why these trailers, while useful in the Linux kernel context (for tracking cherry-picks across multiple trees and linking to discussions), don't serve the same purpose in Git's development workflow. Git has fewer active trees and uses different mechanisms - inline references for fixes and `refs/notes/amlog` for linking to discussions. The change adds clear guidance to SubmittingPatches that these Linux-specific trailers aren't used in the Git project.

The patch maintains the series' high quality with a well-reasoned explanation of the project-specific differences that motivate this convention. It follows the pattern established in previous patches of documenting existing practices rather than introducing new ones. The change is small but meaningful, helping contributors avoid unnecessary confusion when adopting Git's conventions.
