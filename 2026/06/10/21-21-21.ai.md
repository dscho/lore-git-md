This is a documentation patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's 11-part series improving the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. The series has already been through review and is in its final polishing stage.

Patch 3/11 makes a small terminology change in the NAME section, replacing "structured information" with "metadata" to match the consistent terminology established in previous patches. The author explains that while both terms are accurate, "metadata" is more concise and aligns with the series' goal of standardizing language around trailer processing.

This is a straightforward consistency improvement that doesn't change any functionality or introduce new concepts - it simply applies the series' chosen terminology ("metadata") to one remaining instance where the older phrasing ("structured information") was still present. The change was suggested in earlier review feedback and has been accepted as part of the documentation polish.

The patch is part of a larger effort to clearly document trailer parsing rules (including the intentional restriction on non-alphanumeric characters in keys) while improving the man page's structure and readability. This particular change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series' goal of precise, consistent terminology.
