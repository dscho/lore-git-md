This is a documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of an 11-patch series improving the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. The series aims to clarify the trailer format specification after earlier discussion revealed confusion about key format requirements (particularly around non-alphanumeric characters like periods).

This first patch removes references to RFC 822 email headers from the trailer documentation, arguing the comparison is unnecessary and potentially confusing since most users won't be familiar with email header specifications. The change simplifies the introduction by describing trailers directly as metadata lines rather than through analogy to email headers, while keeping the technical description of line-folding behavior.

The patch maintains all functional information while making the documentation more accessible. It's a clean, focused change that sets up the subsequent patches to define the trailer format more clearly. As the first in a merged series that received positive feedback, this appears to be a well-considered improvement to the documentation's pedagogical approach.
