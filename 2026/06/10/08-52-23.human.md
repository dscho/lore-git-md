This is part 3 of Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series introducing a `git history drop` subcommand, which builds on the experimental `git history` command. The series is now at version 4 and appears to be nearing completion.

This particular patch renames `reset_head()` to `reset_working_tree()` to better reflect its actual functionality, as the function's purpose has evolved beyond just updating HEAD. The change was suggested by Phillip Wood and is part of a broader modernization of Git's reset machinery. The patch updates all callers across rebase.c, reset.c, sequencer.c, and the corresponding header file.

The change is mechanical but touches several key subsystems that handle working tree resets. The patch appears well-contained and straightforward, with no obvious technical concerns. It's part of a carefully structured series that has already incorporated significant review feedback in previous iterations.
