This thread updated Git’s `SubmittingPatches` documentation to standardize trailer conventions in commit messages. The five-patch series, authored by Kristoffer Haugsbakk and reviewed by Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, is now fully merged. It establishes clear guidelines for crediting contributions, trailer formatting, and ordering—addressing prior ambiguity while ensuring legal compliance (DCO sign-off) and practical usability.

The series codifies rules for `Helped-by`/`Co-authored-by` usage, omits Linux-specific trailers like `Fixes:` (with rationale), documents the `Based-on-patch-by` trailer’s legal requirements, enforces consistent markup, and mandates chronological ordering (e.g., `Signed-off-by` last). All feedback was incorporated, including Junio’s insistence on explicit DCO sign-off for `Based-on-patch-by` and Patrick’s agreement to omit non-identity trailers entirely.

The only post-merge note is a minor AsciiDoc formatting nit from Junio: the `[[commit-trailers]]` anchor was placed *after* the section header, unlike most other sections in the file. While purely stylistic, this observation serves as a reference for future documentation patches. The series itself is complete and uncontroversial.
