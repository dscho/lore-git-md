This thread updates Git’s `SubmittingPatches` documentation to clarify trailer conventions in commit messages. Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s six-part series establishes guidelines for crediting contributions, avoiding Linux-specific trailers, standardizing markup, and ordering trailers chronologically. The most debated point—whether to document non-identity trailers like `Closes:`—has now been resolved: Patrick Steinhardt and Kristoffer agree to omit them entirely, as they’re rare in Git’s history and mostly relevant to downstream projects. The v2 update will focus on previously agreed fixes (typos, DCO sign-off clarifications, and trailer ordering refinements), with the series now streamlined to reflect Git’s actual practices rather than edge cases.
