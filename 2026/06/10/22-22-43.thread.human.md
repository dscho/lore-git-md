Kristoffer Haugsbakk has completed a documentation series updating the SubmittingPatches guidelines with clear standards for commit message trailers. The six-part series establishes foundational principles for trailer usage, covering everything from basic conventions to specific cases like the `Based-on-patch-by` trailer. The final patch adds guidance on trailer ordering, emphasizing that `Signed-off-by` should come last since it represents signing off on the entire commit message and all preceding trailers.

The series documents existing practices rather than proposing changes, with each patch addressing a different aspect of trailer usage: when to use them, how to format them (now consistently marked up with backticks), which Linux-specific conventions to avoid, and now the significance of ordering. The documentation makes explicit what was previously implicit knowledge, particularly around the `Signed-off-by` trailer's position and its relationship to other acknowledgments.

This work builds on scattered mailing list discussions and complements Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing man page standardization effort. The series appears ready for review, having systematically covered all major aspects of trailer usage in Git commit messages. Given its documentation-only nature and alignment with established practices, it will likely receive straightforward review from regular documentation contributors.
