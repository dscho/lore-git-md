This thread is a five-part documentation patch series by Kristoffer Haugsbakk that updates Git’s `SubmittingPatches` guidelines to standardize trailer conventions in commit messages. The series is now finalized and ready for merging, with all feedback incorporated into the v2 iteration.

The goal is to clarify how Git uses trailers (like `Signed-off-by`, `Reviewed-by`, and `Based-on-patch-by`) to ensure legal compliance (DCO sign-off) and practical usability, while resolving prior ambiguity about crediting contributions and formatting. Key changes include: a new "Commit trailers" section encouraging proper attribution, explicit guidance to avoid Linux-specific trailers (like `Fixes:`), a documented `Based-on-patch-by` convention with DCO requirements, consistent trailer markup, and—most recently—rules for chronological ordering (e.g., `Signed-off-by` last). The series omits non-identity trailers entirely, as they’re irrelevant to Git’s workflow.

Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt provided substantive reviews, focusing on legal compliance (DCO sign-off for `Based-on-patch-by`), workflow-specific rationale (omitting Linux trailers), and clarity in ordering principles. Kristoffer addressed all feedback, including typo fixes, line-wrapping, and markup consistency. The final patch (5/5) codifies trailer ordering, ensuring trailers reflect the sequence of events (e.g., testing before sign-off). The series is uncontroversial and touches only `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. It complements broader documentation efforts to improve contributor onboarding.
