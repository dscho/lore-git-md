This thread is about a now-merged five-part documentation series by Kristoffer Haugsbakk that updated `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to standardize Git's trailer conventions in commit messages. The series aimed to clarify legal compliance (DCO sign-off), crediting practices, and trailer formatting and ordering, while explicitly excluding Linux-specific trailers like `Fixes:` and `Link:`.

The third patch in the series, which just arrived, documents the `Based-on-patch-by:` trailer. It explains when to use it (to credit prior patches) and the legal requirement that the original author must have signed off on the patch. This patch was uncontroversial and incorporated all prior feedback, including Junio C Hamano's insistence on clarifying the DCO sign-off requirement. The entire series is now fully merged into `master`, with all five patches applied. The updates include prescriptive guidelines for trailer principles, the exclusion of Linux-specific trailers, the `Based-on-patch-by` convention, consistent trailer markup, and chronological ordering. The series also resolved stylistic inconsistencies, such as AsciiDoc anchor placement, and improved section organization. No open questions remain, and the changes are ready for contributors to reference.
