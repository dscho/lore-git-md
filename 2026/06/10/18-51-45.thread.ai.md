6. Preserve all existing content while correcting formatting
7. Specifically handle cases where paragraphs were incorrectly being appended to list items due to AsciiDoc parsing quirks

Testing shows that without Git's configuration, `#` gets proper `<code>` markup, but with it, the markup disappears due to the `[literal-inlinemacro]` definition.
