This is a refactoring patch series that's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting global state in setup.c. The series comes from Patrick Steinhardt, who has been a major contributor to architectural changes in Git's internals.

The main goal is to eliminate remaining global state in setup.c, with the most notable change being that `is_bare_repository()` no longer needs to rely on `the_repository`. The series builds on Patrick's previous work centralizing ODB creation and consists of 7 patches that:

1. Remove the global `git_work_tree_cfg` variable by first stopping its modification in `builtin/init` and then removing it entirely
2. Split up concerns around `is_bare_repository_cfg` before eliminating its use of `the_repository`
3. Finally drop the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` flag treewide

The changes touch a wide range of files (24 modified) but appear to be mechanical refactorings rather than behavior changes. This continues Patrick's pattern of sending well-structured series that methodically tackle architectural technical debt. The patches follow the common pattern in the `the_repository` removal effort - first making callers pass the repository explicitly, then removing the global state once no longer needed.

The series is based on recent work (1ff279f340 from June 9) and appears ready for review. Given Patrick's track record with similar refactorings and the focused nature of these changes, the series seems likely to be uncontroversial and mergeable after review.
