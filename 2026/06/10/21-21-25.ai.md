This is the 7th patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's 11-part documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`, focusing on adding concrete examples of invalid trailer key formats. The patch demonstrates how the parser rejects keys containing non-alphanumeric characters (like periods in "Hash-in-v6.11") and explains the trailer block detection rules when invalid keys are present.

The example shows three attempted trailer lines, with only the properly formatted `Reviewed-by` being a valid key, while `Skapad-på` (contains non-ASCII) and `Hash-in-v6.11` (contains a period) are rejected. The accompanying text reinforces the key format rules established in previous patches and explains why the entire block fails trailer detection in this case.

This patch complements the earlier technical specifications by showing the behavior in action, helping users understand both the rules and their practical implications. The example is pedagogically useful for showing how one invalid line can prevent recognition of otherwise valid trailers in the same block.
