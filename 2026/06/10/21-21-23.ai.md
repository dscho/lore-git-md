This is patch 5/11 in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`, which has already been merged. The patch focuses on improving the introductory explanation of trailer format in the man page.

The change adds clear definitions of key terms ("trailer", "trailer block", "blank line") right after the initial description, before diving into the example. This provides readers with essential vocabulary upfront rather than making them infer it from context. The patch standardizes on the term "blank line" (defined as empty or whitespace-only) to maintain consistency throughout the documentation.

The change is purely documentation-focused, with no functional impact. It implements a suggestion from D. Ben Knoble to improve the pedagogical flow of the man page by introducing core concepts before the example rather than after. The patch maintains the series' overall goal of making the trailer format rules and terminology more explicit and accessible.
