Patrick Steinhardt has completed his 7-patch series eliminating the remaining global state in setup.c as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series systematically removed two key variables - `git_work_tree_cfg` and `is_bare_repository_cfg` - that were previously accessed globally throughout the codebase.

The work followed Patrick's established pattern: first introducing new structures to track state, then making callers pass repository state explicitly before removing the globals entirely. The final patch removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro after updating the last few callers of `is_bare_repository()` to use an explicit repository parameter instead of the global. This completes the architectural cleanup, making dependencies clearer while preserving behavior.

The series touched 53 files in total, with each patch making focused, incremental changes. The changes are mechanical in nature and follow the project's multi-year effort to eliminate implicit global state. Given Patrick's track record with similar refactorings and the uncontroversial nature of these changes, the series appears ready for merging. This represents another step forward in the broader `the_repository` removal project that René Scharfe and others have contributed to.
