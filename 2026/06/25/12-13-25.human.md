This is a refactoring patch from the ongoing `git cat-file --batch-command` security series. It makes a small but sensible code hygiene improvement by moving loop counter declarations inside their `for()` statements and switching their type from `int` to `size_t` where appropriate.

The patch touches two files: `builtin/cat-file.c` and `fetch-pack.c`. In both places, it converts loops that previously declared an `int i` outside the loop to instead declare `size_t i` inside the `for()` statement. This matches the type of the loop bound (which is already `size_t` in most cases) and keeps the variable scope tight. The changes are purely mechanical and do not alter behavior.
