This is a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt, part of a larger series (v6, 1/11). It inlines the `check_and_apply_repository_format()` helper function into its two callsites in `setup.c` to prepare for a future change where one of those callsites will need to adjust the order of reading and applying the repository format. The patch is mechanical and does not change behavior—it simply moves the function's logic directly into `enter_repo()` and `init_db()`.

The motivation is clear: the helper function is too rigid for the planned modification, and inlining it gives the author the flexibility to tweak the logic in one callsite without affecting the other. The commit message is concise but sufficient, and the diff is straightforward, showing only the removal of the helper and the insertion of its logic at the two original callsites. No tests are touched, which is expected for a pure refactoring.

This patch is part of Patrick’s ongoing work to restructure Git’s repository setup and ref backend handling.
