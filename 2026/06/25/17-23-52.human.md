This is a documentation patch, now merged into the `next` branch. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, approves the final version of Tamir Duberstein’s patch to document the long-undocumented `--no-follow` option for `git log`. The patch adds a brief entry for `--no-follow` alongside the existing `--follow` documentation and notes in `log.adoc` that `--no-follow` overrides the `log.follow` configuration setting.

The discussion in this email is a minor post-approval clarification: Junio admits he initially misread the patch’s structure in an earlier review and now confirms that the order of `--follow` and `--no-follow` in the documentation does not matter, though he personally prefers `--follow` to appear first. He also suggests that the `--no-follow` entry in `git-log.txt` could be even more concise, but the current version is acceptable and already merged. The patch is uncontroversial, well-motivated, and closes a real gap in the documentation.
