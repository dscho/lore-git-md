Tian Yuchen’s two-patch series to libify Git’s `excludes_file` global is moving toward its first revision after Junio C Hamano’s review of the second patch. The goal is to eliminate the global variable by embedding it in `struct repo_config_values` and introducing proper heap memory management, part of the ongoing effort to make Git’s internals more modular. The series is still at its initial v1, with no other reviews yet.

Junio’s feedback focuses on two technical oversights in the implementation: the new `excludes_file` field in `repo_config_values_init()` isn’t explicitly initialized to `NULL`, and the commit message incorrectly implies `excludes_file` is the only heap-allocated field in the struct—`attributes_file` is also dynamically allocated and needs cleanup. These are straightforward fixes, and the review doesn’t challenge the series’ direction or the temporary submodule guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) added to avoid BUG() assertions. The patches touch `dir.c`, `environment.c`, and related headers, introducing new symbols like `repo_excludes_file()` and `repo_config_values_clear()` while maintaining existing behavior. No behavioral changes are expected, and the series remains aligned with prior libification patterns.
