This thread is a small but focused refactoring series from Tian Yuchen, part of Git’s ongoing libification effort. The goal is to eliminate the global `excludes_file` variable by moving it into `struct repo_config_values` and adding proper heap memory management. The series is at its first iteration (v1) and consists of two patches.

The first patch introduces `repo_excludes_file()`, a getter function that encapsulates the lazy-loading logic for the global variable, while the second patch completes the migration by adding `repo_config_values_clear()` to handle cleanup. The changes are mechanical, touching `dir.c`, `environment.c`, `environment.h`, and `repository.c`, and follow established patterns for eliminating globals. A temporary guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) prevents BUG() assertions with uninitialized submodules, though the author notes this is a stopgap until broader submodule initialization issues are resolved.

No reviews have been posted yet, but the series appears uncontroversial and self-contained. It’s mentored by Christian Couder, Ayush Chandekar, and Olamide Caleb Bello, and aligns with the broader effort to make Git’s internals more modular and library-friendly. If you’re tracking libification progress, this is a clean, incremental step forward.
