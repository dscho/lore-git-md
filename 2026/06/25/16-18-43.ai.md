This is a refactoring patch series (2/2) that advances the ongoing libification effort by moving the global `excludes_file` string into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is authored by Tian Yuchen, mentored by Christian Couder, Ayush Chandekar, and Olamide Caleb Bello.

The goal is to eliminate the global `excludes_file` variable, replacing it with a field in `struct repo_config_values` and introducing proper heap memory management. The first patch abstracts the XDG fallback lazy-loading logic from `dir.c` into a dedicated getter, while the second patch performs the actual migration and adds a `repo_config_values_clear()` function to handle cleanup. A temporary guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) is included to prevent BUG() assertions when dealing with uninitialized submodules, a pattern borrowed from prior work.

The series touches `dir.c`, `environment.c`, `environment.h`, and `repository.c`, with a net increase of 36 lines. The changes are mechanical but introduce new memory management responsibilities, so the addition of `repo_config_values_clear()` is a necessary safeguard. The commit messages are clear, and the motivation (libification) is well-established in the cover letter. No behavioral changes are expected, and the series appears to be a straightforward step in the broader effort to make Git's internals more modular.
