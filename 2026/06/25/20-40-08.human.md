This is a surface-level review from Junio Hamano on the second patch in Tian Yuchen’s two-patch series to libify the `excludes_file` global. The series is part of the ongoing effort to make Git’s internals more modular by eliminating globals and adding proper memory management.

Junio’s feedback is purely mechanical: he points out two small omissions in the patch. First, he suggests explicitly initializing `cfg->excludes_file` to `NULL` in `repo_config_values_init()` for completeness, even though the function already zero-initializes the entire struct. Second, he notes that the comment in `repo_config_values_clear()` claims `excludes_file` is the only heap-allocated field in `struct repo_config_values`, but `attributes_file` is also dynamically allocated and should be freed in the same function.

The review does not engage with the patch’s design or libification goals—it simply flags two minor inconsistencies that could be addressed in a follow-up iteration. The patch itself remains uncontroversial and appears to follow established patterns for eliminating globals. If the author sends a v2, expect these two points to be fixed without changing the patch’s overall direction.
