This is a refactoring patch (part of the GSoC `git cat-file --batch-command` series) that moves the `write_fetch_command_and_capabilities()` function from `fetch-pack.c` to `connect.c`. The patch also adjusts the function signature to use a forward declaration of `struct string_list` in `connect.h`, avoiding a circular include dependency and following the pattern Junio Hamano endorsed in earlier review.
