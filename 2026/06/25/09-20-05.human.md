This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-part **v6 refactoring series** that overhauls Git’s reference backend infrastructure to resolve recursive initialization issues caused by "onbranch" configuration. This specific patch **reverts a previous refactoring** (cc42c88945) that moved the parsing of `core.logAllRefUpdates` from the backends into the generic ref layer. The goal is to prepare for the series’ core fix: **lazy-loading write-critical configuration** to avoid the chicken-and-egg problem between config parsing and "onbranch" evaluation.

The patch moves the parsing of `core.logAllRefUpdates` back into the **files** and **reftable backends**, where other backend-specific configuration is already handled. This ensures consistency in how backends parse their settings and paves the way for the lazy-loading design introduced later in the series. The changes touch nine files, including `refs.c`, `refs/files-backend.c`, `refs/reftable-backend.c`, and `setup.c`, and involve:

- **Restoring backend-specific config parsing**: The `files_ref_store_config()` and `reftable_be_config()` callbacks now handle `core.logAllRefUpdates` directly, using the newly exposed `refs_parse_log_all_ref_updates_config()` helper.
- **Removing generic-layer handling**: The `repo_settings_get_log_all_ref_updates()` function and its supporting enum are deleted from `repo-settings.c` and `repo-settings.h`, as they are no longer needed.
- **Updating initialization logic**: The `ref_store_init()` function no longer passes `log_all_ref_updates` via `struct ref_store_init_options`, and backends initialize the field to `LOG_REFS_UNSET` before parsing their config.
- **Fixing a default-value edge case**: In `setup.c`, the patch replaces a call to `repo_settings_get_log_all_ref_updates()` with a direct config check to avoid relying on the now-removed generic-layer function.

The patch is **mechanical and low-risk**, as it essentially reverts to the pre-cc42c88945 behavior. It does not change user-visible behavior but is a **necessary preparatory step** for the lazy-loading refactor in later patches. Justin Tobler’s review of this patch in v5 was positive, and the changes here address his observation about the redundant `LOG_REFS_UNSET` check (which is removed in patch 7/11 of the series). No objections are expected, as the patch aligns with the series’ broader goal of decoupling ref backend initialization from early config parsing.
