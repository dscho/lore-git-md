This is a **substantive review** of Patrick Steinhardt’s patch 9/10 in the `git history drop` series (v6). Christian Couder points out a potential code duplication between the newly introduced `compute_pending_ref_updates()` function in `builtin/history.c` and the existing `replay_result_queue_update()` function in `replay.c`.

The observation is precise: both functions allocate and populate a list of reference updates, storing the refname, old OID, and new OID. Christian does not suggest a specific resolution (e.g., whether to refactor the existing function or leave the duplication), but the comment flags a maintainability concern that could lead to future divergence or bugs if the two implementations drift apart. This is a classic refactoring hazard—splitting a monolithic function into phases is good, but doing so without reusing existing helpers risks reinventing the wheel.

The patch itself remains a well-motivated preparatory step for the `drop` subcommand, and the review does not challenge its correctness or design. However, the duplication is now a clear technical debt item that may need addressing before the series graduates to `master`. Future discussion is likely to focus on whether to extract a shared helper or document the intentional duplication.
