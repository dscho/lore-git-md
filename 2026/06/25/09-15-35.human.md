This is a reply in the ongoing discussion about the `--track=fetch` feature for `git checkout`/`git switch`. Kristofer Karlsson, a contributor from Spotify, pushes back against Harald Nordgren’s argument that Git should adapt to "real-world" chaotic workflows. He suggests the feature’s use case—automatically fetching before checking out a remote branch—can already be achieved with a simple Git alias, making it unnecessary to modify Git’s core. His position is that Git’s strength lies in providing stable, composable primitives, and that higher-level workflow customizations (like this one) belong outside the core codebase rather than as built-in features. The email is brief and pragmatic, offering a concrete alternative without engaging with the broader philosophical debate about Git’s workflow norms.
