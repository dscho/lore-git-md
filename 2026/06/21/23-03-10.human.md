This is the final patch in Taylor Blau’s **v3** series integrating `--delta-islands` with `--path-walk` in `git pack-objects`. The patch removes the long-standing incompatibility between the two options by ensuring path-walk traversal performs the same island-mark propagation as the regular traversal.

The implementation is straightforward: the `add_objects_by_path()` callback now calls `propagate_island_marks()` for commits (skipping boundary commits to match the regular traversal’s behavior) and `record_tree_depth()` for trees, using the helper introduced in an earlier patch. This ensures `resolve_tree_islands()` receives identical island inputs regardless of traversal mode, allowing the existing delta-island checks to work unchanged. The patch also updates the documentation to remove the now-obsolete incompatibility notice and adds test coverage in `t5320-delta-islands.sh` for path-walk repacks with and without bitmaps, as well as the same-island delta case.

The change is well-motivated and technically sound. The commit message clearly explains why the island side effects must be performed during path-walk (to preserve the descendant-first propagation order and tree-depth ordering) and how the implementation matches the regular traversal’s behavior. The tests confirm that path-walk now respects island boundaries and allows deltas within the same island, just like the regular traversal. This patch, combined with the rest of the series, delivers on the goal of making `--path-walk` fully compatible with both `--delta-islands` and `--use-bitmap-index`. The series as a whole is merge-ready.
