This is the maintainer's reply to the v3 cover letter of Zakariyah Ali's shell completion patch series, which aligns Git's path completion behavior with standard shell conventions by hiding dotfiles by default.

Junio C Hamano acknowledges the conceptual approval of the behavior change but raises two technical concerns about the patch structure. First, he finds the range-diff "troubling" because the second patch appears to redo work from the first patch, suggesting a less-than-ideal development path. He proposes a cleaner two-patch structure: the first patch would refactor the AWK script to improve readability (without changing behavior), and the second would introduce the dotfile-hiding logic on top of that refactored base. This approach would make the changes easier to review and maintain.

Second, Junio asks for clarification on how the patch decides which callers of the completion helper should hide dotfiles and which should not—a point he recalls being unclear in earlier reviews. The cover letter does not address this, leaving the rationale for the current design unstated. He also questions the purpose of the "Validation" section, suggesting it may be redundant if it merely lists steps the author took to verify the patch.

The email signals that while the series is conceptually sound and addresses a real usability issue, it is not yet ready for merging. The maintainer's feedback is technical and constructive, focusing on implementation clarity rather than the underlying goal. The ball is now in Zakariyah's court to restructure the series and provide the missing rationale.
