This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing worktree diff performance thread. Junio C Hamano acknowledges that while the counter-based guard in `builtin/diff.c` (`rev.diffopt.skip_stat_unmatch`) is functionally correct, its dual purpose as both a boolean flag and a counter may not be the best design choice. He notes that the mechanism—introduced in 2007 to avoid unnecessary index refreshes—has confused multiple contributors, including himself and D. Ben Knoble.

The email links to the original 2007 discussion that led to the patch (commit aecbf914), providing historical context for why the counter-based logic was chosen. This admission shifts the focus of the thread: the guard is no longer in question as "dead code," but its clarity and maintainability are now open for debate. The implication is that while the current implementation works, it might be worth revisiting for a cleaner alternative—though Junio does not propose a specific fix here. The conversation remains centered on index refresh behavior and its impact on `git diff` performance in worktrees.
