This is the second patch in Taylor Blau’s **v3** 4-patch series enabling `--delta-islands` and reachability bitmaps to work with `--path-walk` in `git pack-objects`. The series as a whole is now merge-ready, and this patch specifically removes the artificial restriction that prevented `--use-bitmap-index` from being used alongside `--path-walk`.

The patch teaches `git pack-objects` to try a bitmap-based traversal first when both `--use-bitmap-index` and `--path-walk` are specified. If bitmaps can satisfy the request, they are used; otherwise, the command falls back to the path-walk traversal. This is a one-way fallback—bitmaps are always preferred when available, and path-walk never augments bitmap results. The implementation also ensures that commits discovered during path-walk are properly indexed for bitmap selection when writing new bitmaps, so future repacks can benefit from the same optimization.

Performance data from the updated `p5311` perf test shows dramatic improvements: server-side pack generation times drop by **90–99%** when bitmaps are available, while pack sizes remain unchanged. The test now explicitly measures pack sizes with and without `--path-walk` when bitmaps are written, confirming that bitmap acceleration does not regress `--path-walk`’s compression benefits (e.g., a 558.4M pack shrinks to 164.4M with `--path-walk` regardless of whether bitmaps are used).

The patch touches `pack-objects.c` to enable bitmap traversal during path-walk, updates the documentation to clarify the new behavior, and adds a test in `t5310-pack-bitmaps.sh` to verify that path-walk repacks can both write and use bitmap indexes. A minor edge case around `--boundary` is handled by deferring its activation until after any bitmap traversal attempt, ensuring compatibility with thin packs.
