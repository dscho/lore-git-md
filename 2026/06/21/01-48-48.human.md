This is Junio Hamano's review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater's v6 series, which introduces abstraction functions in `revision.c` for `git log --graph`'s lookahead logic. The patch is part of a larger effort to implement cascading indentation for visual roots in commit graphs.

Junio identifies a technical debt issue in the implementation: the loop in `revision_has_commits_after()` directly accesses the priority queue's internal array structure (`.nr` and `.array`), which is fragile and will conflict with Kristofer Haugsbakk's ongoing `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic. He suggests using `prio_queue_for_each()` instead, once that topic lands. This is a forward-compatibility concern rather than a correctness issue—the current implementation works but will need adjustment to stay compatible with future changes to the priority queue API.

The review is substantive but not blocking. Junio proposes two integration strategies: either rebase this series on top of the prio-queue topic to resolve the conflict early, or let this series graduate first and then adjust the prio-queue topic afterward. This is a typical maintainer-level coordination question rather than a critique of the patch itself.

The rest of the patch—its core abstraction design and the two new functions (`revision_peek_next_commit()` and `revision_has_commits_after()`)—remains unchallenged. Junio's earlier conditional approval (pending a defensive `BUG()` check) still stands, and this new feedback is additive rather than contradictory. The patch is technically sound but will need a small follow-up to align with the prio-queue refactoring.
