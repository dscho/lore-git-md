This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series implementing cascading indentation for `git log --graph`. Chandra Pratap suggests two minor readability improvements to the `revision_has_commits_after()` function: replacing `visible > n-1` with `visible >= n` in both the priority-queue and linked-list loops, and clarifying the function’s header comment to say “n or more” instead of “n more.”

The feedback is purely stylistic and does not engage with the patch’s core purpose—introducing an abstraction layer that insulates `graph.c` from `revision.c` internals. The patch itself remains technically sound and is conditionally approved by Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood, pending follow-up work on defensive checks and forward-compatibility with priority-queue refactoring. This review does not affect the series’ readiness or the architectural redesign that resolved earlier blocking concerns.
