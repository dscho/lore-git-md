This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series implementing cascading indentation for `git log --graph`. Chandra Pratap suggests two minor readability improvements to `revision_has_commits_after()`: replacing `visible > n-1` with `visible >= n` in both the priority-queue and linked-list loops, and clarifying the function’s header comment to say “n or more” instead of “n”. Neither change affects functionality—the logic is already correct—but the proposed wording is more idiomatic and aligns better with the function’s documented purpose. The review is brief, focused solely on style and clarity, and does not engage with the patch’s core abstraction design or its role in insulating `graph.c` from `revision.c` internals.
