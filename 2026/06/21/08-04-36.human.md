This is a documentation patch series (v3 of 2 patches) updating contributor guidelines to clarify review etiquette. The series refines advice on two key aspects of the patch workflow: encouraging discussion of feedback before rerolling, and establishing reasonable reroll timing expectations.

The first patch remains unchanged from v2, adding guidance to `MyFirstContribution.adoc` that contributors should discuss review feedback in the email thread before sending a new version. This helps reviewers understand the author's reasoning rather than having to infer it from code changes alone. The patch frames this as encouraging "social interaction" between contributors, though Patrick Steinhardt's feedback that this largely restates existing content with more words remains unresolved.

The second patch, which updates `SubmittingPatches`, shows significant refinement based on Junio C Hamano's feedback. The most notable change is replacing the "substantial rework" exception with more concrete advice: when major changes are needed, authors should reply to the review thread signaling which parts of the current series will become obsolete, rather than rushing out a new version. The patch also drops the controversial "close to acceptance" exception that could encourage premature rerolls. Other improvements include explicit timezone considerations for global participation and encouragement to polish patches before sending them.

The series demonstrates thoughtful responsiveness to feedback while maintaining its core goals. The author has properly attributed contributions with `Helped-by` trailers, modeling the very practices the documentation seeks to encourage. The v3 changes address the most substantive concerns from the thread, particularly around timing-based exceptions that risked rushing patch quality.

While the `MyFirstContribution.adoc` changes remain in question due to redundancy concerns, the `SubmittingPatches` updates appear to be converging toward consensus. The series effectively codifies important but previously implicit norms about review workflow, making them explicit for new contributors without imposing rigid rules.
