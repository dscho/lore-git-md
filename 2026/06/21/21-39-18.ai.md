This is a technical discussion in the ongoing worktree diff performance thread, focusing on architectural tradeoffs for addressing racy-git entries. Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King’s earlier patch proposal (which introduced a `DIFF_RACY_IS_MODIFIED` flag to force index refreshes) with an alternative design suggestion.

Junio proposes adding a new field to the `istate` (index state) structure to track when `ie_match_stat()` encounters racy entries that would benefit from auto-refreshing. This would allow the "skip-stat-unmatch" logic to consider these entries without requiring a disruptive flag to propagate through the diff machinery. He also questions the necessity of the existing `refresh_index_quietly()` call in `cmd_diff()` for tree-to-tree comparisons, though he acknowledges it’s harmless.

The email advances the thread by introducing a potential refinement to Peff’s approach, avoiding the need for a new flag while maintaining the same goal: ensuring racy entries are counted as stat-dirty. This suggestion aligns with the thread’s broader theme of balancing architectural simplicity against performance tradeoffs. No new patches or performance data are provided; this is purely a design discussion. Key technical details include the proposed `istate` field and the tangential observation about `refresh_index_quietly()`. The open question remains whether this approach would avoid the first-diff performance regression seen in Peff’s patch.
