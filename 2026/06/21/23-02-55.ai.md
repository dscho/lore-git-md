This is the cover letter for the **v3** revision of Taylor Blau’s 4-patch series that integrates `--delta-islands` and reachability bitmaps with `--path-walk` in `git pack-objects`. The series is now **merge-ready**, with all prior review feedback addressed and empirical validation completed.

The key updates since v2 focus on **performance documentation** and **test robustness**:
- The **p5311 perf test** now forces a fresh repack (`-F`) to avoid reusing non-`--path-walk` deltas, ensuring accurate measurement of `--path-walk`’s compression benefits.
- The test now explicitly records pack sizes with/without `--path-walk` when bitmaps are written, confirming that bitmap acceleration does not regress `--path-walk`’s compression improvements (e.g., 558.4M → 164.4M in the fluentui repository).
- A minor test fix replaces `grep` with `test_grep` in `t5310-pack-bitmaps.sh`, as suggested by Junio.

The series remains functionally unchanged beyond these adjustments. It enables **sequential compatibility** between `--path-walk` and both bitmaps (via fallback) and delta-islands (via tree-depth recording), preserving each feature’s benefits without active synergy. The implementation touches `pack-objects.c`, test scripts, and adds the new `p5311-repack.sh` perf test, with no on-disk format changes.

**Why this matters**: This series resolves a long-standing limitation where `--path-walk` (useful for hierarchical repositories) could not leverage bitmaps or delta-islands. The empirical data now demonstrates that `--path-walk`’s compression advantages persist even when bitmaps are enabled, addressing Derrick Stolee’s earlier request for validation. The patches are well-tested, with coverage in `t5320` (delta-islands), `t5310` (bitmaps), and `p5311` (performance).
