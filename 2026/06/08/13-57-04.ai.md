This is a documentation patch fixing a formatting issue in the git-config man page. It's the first of three patches in Derrick Stolee's series about disabling config includes.

The patch corrects a missing '+' character in the AsciiDoc source that was causing incorrect rendering of environment variable documentation. Without this fix, the bulleted list about environment variables was being split into two separate lists with an intervening paragraph. The change is purely presentational and doesn't affect the actual content or behavior.

This appears to be a preparatory cleanup for the substantive changes coming in patches 2 and 3 of the series, which will introduce new ways to disable config includes. The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial, with the correct AsciiDoc syntax clearly demonstrated in the diff.
