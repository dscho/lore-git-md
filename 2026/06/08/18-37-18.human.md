This is a feature patch series introducing a `--linearize` option to `git replay` to flatten merge commits, similar to `git rebase --no-rebase-merges`. The series consists of three patches: two preparatory refactorings (converting an `enum replay_mode` to a boolean and adding a helper function) and the main implementation patch by Johannes Schindelin.

The motivation is to provide an alternative to Johannes Schindelin’s earlier patch series that aimed to replay merges directly. Instead of preserving merge topology, this series offers a way to linearize it, which may be simpler for some workflows. The new option is added only to `git replay` and not to `git history`, as the author sees little use for it there—though they invite disagreement.

The series touches `replay.c`, `builtin/replay.c`, and adds tests in `t/t3650-replay-basics.sh`. The changes look clean and well-scoped, with a clear commit message explaining the rationale. The author acknowledges potential conflicts with other ongoing work (Kristoffer’s documentation changes and Patrick’s `git history` "drop" feature) but expects them to be trivial to resolve.

If you care about `git replay` or rebase-like workflows, this is worth a look—especially if you’ve followed the earlier merge-replay discussion. The series appears ready for review and seems like a sensible addition to the tool.
