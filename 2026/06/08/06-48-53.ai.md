This is a follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt's documentation series standardizing Git contributor workflow around the `b4` tool, specifically addressing version compatibility concerns raised by Toon Claes. The exchange represents one of the final practical considerations before the series can be merged.

Patrick confirms that when older b4 versions (pre-v0.15) encounter the `send-same-thread` configuration, they will fall back to no threading behavior rather than failing. This maintains backward compatibility but could lead to inconsistent threading behavior across different users' environments depending on their b4 version. Patrick suggests this might indicate that sticking with the current threading style (without the new configuration) could be preferable to avoid version-dependent behavior.

The discussion highlights the tension between adopting new best practices (shallow threading) and ensuring consistent behavior across all contributors' environments. This practical deployment consideration comes after the technical and pedagogical aspects of the series have already been resolved through earlier discussion. The thread appears to be wrapping up, with this being one of the last open questions before maintainer decision.
