This is a review response to a performance optimization patch for ref-filter's `--contains` and `--no-contains` operations. The patch, originally from Tamir Duberstein, unifies and speeds up commit ancestry checking by reusing a memoized traversal algorithm that was previously only used for `git tag --contains`.

Karthik Nayak's review is positive overall, noting the significant performance improvements (up to 223x faster in some cases) and the clean handling of cyclic commit graphs through replacement refs. He makes a few minor observations:

1. The removal of `with_commit_tag_algo` is noted as expected since the unified approach makes it obsolete
2. The cycle detection logic (using CONTAINS_IN_PROGRESS) is analyzed and confirmed as correct
3. Suggests the changes could have been split into two commits: one for cycle handling and another for the unification
4. Notes a minor test organization nit about moving a verification check from setup to the actual test

The review confirms the technical soundness of the approach while being appropriately detailed about the implementation choices. Karthik's feedback is substantive - he clearly understands both the algorithmic changes and their practical implications, and his suggestions are constructive rather than merely superficial. The review advances the discussion by validating the core optimization while offering small refinements to the presentation.
