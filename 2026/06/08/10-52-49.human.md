This moves the technical discussion forward by proposing a concrete implementation strategy that would preserve the distinct behaviors needed for different subcommands. The exchange remains constructive, with Pablo demonstrating responsiveness to review feedback while still working to maintain their original goal of preventing unnecessary history rewrites when messages don't change.

The thread continues to explore how to balance the command's constraints (in-memory operation, branch updates) with maintaining consistency across related commands.
