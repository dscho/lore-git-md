This is a technical discussion about submodule mirroring approaches, focusing on the tradeoffs between different URL resolution strategies. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Simon Richter's concerns about using `url.*.insteadOf` for mirroring submodules in Debian's packaging workflow.

Peff acknowledges Simon's point about relative URLs potentially solving the "naive cloning" problem by assuming submodules are co-located with the superproject. However, he notes this assumption breaks down in decentralized scenarios where submodules and superprojects have no administrative relationship. The discussion highlights two competing needs: Debian's requirement for deterministic local mirroring versus Git's decentralized nature where canonical URLs are often tied to specific hosting providers.

The email clarifies that while `insteadOf` mappings could theoretically handle the mirroring case, the challenge lies in distributing these mappings securely and efficiently. Peff suggests that if an organization is already mirroring submodules, the same process could generate the necessary `insteadOf` configurations, avoiding manual recursive enumeration of submodules.

This exchange continues to explore the tension between Git's flexible URL handling and institutional needs for reproducible builds, with Peff positioning himself as seeking generalized solutions that could work beyond Debian's specific use case while acknowledging their valid requirements.
