Technical patch in Patrick Steinhardt's `git history drop` series, modifying reset behavior for dirty indexes. This is the 7th patch in v3 of the series that adds commit-dropping capability to the experimental `git history` command.

The change removes an optimization that assumed `reset_head()` would always be called with a clean index, which was valid for its existing callers (rebase and sequencer) but won't hold for the new `history drop` use case. The patch:

1. Moves the `skip_cache_tree_update` flag to only apply for hard resets (where the index will be clean afterwards)
2. Similarly restricts `prime_cache_tree()` to hard resets
3. Notes that while more granular dirty-index detection could be added, the original performance optimization was negligible anyway

This is a focused change to reset.c that enables the upcoming drop subcommand while maintaining existing behavior for current callers. The patch follows the series' pattern of careful, incremental refactoring with clear rationale - it addresses a real limitation while avoiding over-engineering. The change is small (8 lines) but meaningful for the series' goal of making `reset_head()` work correctly with dirty indexes.
