This is the third and final patch in Toon Claes' series adding a `--linearize` option to `git replay`, co-authored with Johannes Schindelin (Dscho). The option mimics `git rebase --no-rebase-merges` behavior by flattening merge commits into linear history.

The implementation modifies the commit-picking logic in `replay.c` to skip merge commits when `--linearize` is active, effectively treating them as no-ops that map to their first parent. The patch adds documentation, test coverage in t3650, and ensures the option is incompatible with `--revert` since the two modes serve opposite purposes.

The change appears well-contained, touching only the replay machinery while maintaining existing behavior for non-linearized cases. The test demonstrates the expected linearized output by verifying the commit graph structure after replay. This completes the series' goal of providing server-side rebase-like functionality through `git replay`, with Dscho's involvement suggesting alignment with his broader merge-replay work.
