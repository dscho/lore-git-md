This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing priority queue optimization series, focusing on naming conventions for internal fields. Junio C Hamano responds to Kristofer Karlsson's proposal to rename the queue's internal `.nr` field to `nr_internal` to prevent unsafe direct access.

Junio suggests an established C convention of using trailing underscores (like `nr_`) to mark private/internal fields, noting this is commonly seen in callback function parameters. He explains this is a lighter-weight approach than `nr_internal` while still signaling the field's private nature. The maintainer indicates either naming approach would be acceptable, leaving the final choice to the author.

The exchange shows the careful attention being paid to API design in this optimization work, where performance improvements (previously measured at 3-6% speedups) are being balanced with long-term maintainability concerns. This is part of the final polish before the series is likely to be merged, following three rounds of review addressing correctness and performance considerations across multiple subsystems using the priority queue.
