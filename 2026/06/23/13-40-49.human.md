This is a performance optimization patch in the ongoing `paint_down_to_common()` series, now in its second version. Derrick Stolee replies to Kristofer Karlsson’s concern about measuring the impact of the new early-exit logic in `commit-reach.c`. Stolee recommends sticking with **hyperfine** for end-to-end timing of `git merge-base` between builds, noting that unit-level microbenchmarks—while useful for isolating the paint-queue overhead—are less convincing for the project’s usual performance claims. He also mentions **trace2** markers as a fallback if external noise makes the signal too weak, but considers it overkill for this case. The exchange is purely about measurement methodology; the optimization itself is unchanged and ready for v2.
