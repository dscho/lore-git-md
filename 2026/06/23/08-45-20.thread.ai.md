Subject: [PATCH] gpg-interface: fix over-aggressive carriage-return stripping

Core problem or goal
A helper function in `gpg-interface.c` was stripping *every* `\r` character from GPG signature output, even when the `\r` was not part of a Windows-style `\r\n` line ending. This could corrupt signature data by removing `\r` characters that were legitimate parts of the payload. The goal is to restrict the stripping to only those `\r` characters that immediately precede a `\n`, preserving standalone `\r` characters.

Current status

Junio also flagged a **bug** in the patch’s logic: it fails to preserve a lone `\r` at the end of the buffer. He provided a corrected loop implementation. The patch is now blocked on addressing Junio’s feedback.
