This is a bugfix series targeting the rebase status display in `git status`. Phillip Wood’s v4 addresses two long-standing correctness issues: commit object IDs in “fixup -C” and “merge -C” lines are now properly abbreviated, and refnames (e.g., “reset main”) are no longer replaced with their pointed-to object IDs.
