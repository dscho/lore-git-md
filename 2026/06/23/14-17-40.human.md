This is a **final implementation detail resolution** in the ongoing `paint_down_to_common()` performance optimization series. Derrick Stolee and Kristofer Karlsson agree on how to encapsulate state in the newly renamed `paint_state` struct (formerly `paint_queue`).

The core design is already settled: the walk will terminate early when one side’s queue is exhausted (`(!p1_count || !p2_count) && !pending_merge_bases`), but only after entering the finite-generation region (`last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`). The remaining question was whether to store `last_gen` directly in the struct or infer it by peeking at the queue’s top commit. They now agree to **store `last_gen` in the struct**, making `paint_queue_get()` self-contained and simplifying the loop in `paint_down_to_common()`. The alternative—peeking at the queue—would require awkward sequencing (checking halt conditions *after* popping but *before* updating counters) and is rejected as less clean.

This exchange also confirms that the older fast-exit optimization (controlled by `mb_flags`) will be removed in v2, as its value is diminished by the new side-exhaustion logic. The discussion is narrowly scoped to code structure, with no new behavioral changes or performance claims. Expect v2 to land with `last_gen` stored in `paint_state`, completing the series’ implementation.
