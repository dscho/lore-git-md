Kristofer Karlsson responds to Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to move `last_gen` (and potentially `mb_flags`) into the struct rather than passing them as parameters to `paint_queue_get()`. The discussion is purely about code structure—no behavior changes are proposed. The core optimization (early termination when one side’s queue is exhausted) is already settled, and the only remaining question is whether to peek at the queue’s top commit or store `last_gen` in the struct. Kristofer seems to favor the latter, which would make `paint_queue_get()` self-contained and simplify the loop in `paint_down_to_common()`. This is a natural refinement, not a design dispute, and the series is otherwise ready for final review.
