This is the final maintainer update in a small but long-running thread to silence translation-catalog build noise during `make -s` in gitk and git-gui. The series is now complete and ready to merge.

Johannes Sixt (the gitk maintainer) reports that he has picked up the first patch, which teaches gitk’s Makefile to suppress “Generating catalog” messages when `make -s` is used. The second patch, which simplifies git-gui’s Makefile in the same spirit, is not needed because Johannes Schindelin has already applied the equivalent change to his git-gui fork. Both halves of the original goal are therefore satisfied, and the thread can be considered closed.
