This is the **first patch in a 3-patch CI/platform-compatibility series** that implements the agreed-upon fix for macOS CI hangs in `t5551` and `t5559`. Jeff King (Peff) raises Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds in `t/lib-httpd/apache.conf`, directly addressing the root cause of HTTP/2 stalls and `curl 18` mid-transfer aborts during `ls-refs` advertisements of 100,000 loose refs.

The commit message explains the problem clearly: cold-cache `upload-pack` advertisements can take ~10 seconds on a local machine, and on busy CI runners this can exceed Apache’s default 300-second CGI timeout, triggering either a connection abort (HTTP/1.1) or an indefinite hang (HTTP/2). The 600-second timeout is a conservative increase that eliminates the immediate CI failures while remaining far below Michael Montalbo’s tested 3600-second value. Peff also addresses two counter-arguments: that the test should be made faster (which he agrees with but defers to later patches) and that this masks a real-world issue (which he dismisses as an artificial edge case).

The patch touches only `t/lib-httpd/apache.conf` and is a one-line change. It is well-motivated, low-risk, and aligns with the thread’s consensus. The commit message credits Michael Montalbo and Patrick Steinhardt for their diagnostic work, and Peff’s skepticism about client-side workarounds is already reflected in the earlier discussion. This patch alone resolves the CI hang issue; the remaining two patches in the series are optimizations that reduce the risk of hitting the timeout in the first place.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the `Timeout` directive applies to both `mod_cgi` and `mod_http2`, the failure modes (`curl 18` vs indefinite hang) are tied to HTTP/1.1 vs HTTP/2, and the root cause is Apache bug 70131. The patch is ready for review and likely to be merged quickly.
