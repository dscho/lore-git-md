This is the first patch in Jeff King’s three-patch series addressing macOS CI hangs in `t5551` and `t5559`. It directly implements the thread’s consensus solution by increasing Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds in `t/lib-httpd/apache.conf`. The change is minimal—just one line—but it eliminates the root cause of the CI failures: Apache’s default 300-second timeout was being hit during slow `ls-refs` advertisements of 100,000 loose refs, triggering either a mid-transfer abort (`curl 18`) or an indefinite HTTP/2 stall.

Peff’s commit message is thorough and pragmatic. He explains why the timeout increase is necessary (cold-cache ref advertisements can take ~10 seconds on a local machine, and likely longer in CI), why it’s safe (600 seconds is conservative and far below Michael Montalbo’s tested 3600-second value), and why it’s preferable to client-side workarounds (the test’s slow performance is an artificial edge case, not a real-world issue). The patch also credits the extensive analysis by Michael Montalbo and Patrick Steinhardt that led to this fix.

This is a well-crafted, low-risk patch that addresses a persistent CI flake. The timeout increase is a straightforward solution that doesn’t mask the underlying issue (slow ref advertisements) but gives the test enough breathing room to pass reliably. The subsequent patches in the series will tackle the performance problem itself, but this patch alone resolves the immediate CI failures. It’s likely to be merged quickly, as the technical direction has already been agreed upon in the thread.
