This is a **CI/platform-compatibility bugfix** patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that implements the consensus solution for the macOS CI hangs in `t5551` and `t5559`. The series directly addresses the root cause—Apache’s 300-second timeout being hit during slow `ls-refs` advertisements—by increasing the timeout to 10 minutes (600 seconds) in the test Apache configuration. This is the pragmatic, maintainer-endorsed fix that emerged from earlier discussion, avoiding client-side workarounds or retry logic.

The series consists of three patches. The first patch (`t/lib-httpd: bump apache timeout`) is the core fix, raising the `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds in `t/lib-httpd/apache.conf`. This should eliminate the `curl 18` mid-transfer aborts and HTTP/2 stalls caused by Apache bug 70131, which were consistently triggered by cold-cache `upload-pack` advertisements of 100,000 loose refs taking nearly 10 seconds on CI runners. The choice of 600 seconds is arbitrary but well-motivated: it’s a 10x increase over the default, providing ample headroom without being excessive.

The remaining two patches are optimizations that reduce the risk of hitting the timeout in the first place. Patch 2 (`t5551: put many-tags case into its own repo`) isolates the expensive "many tags" test into a separate repository, avoiding interference with other tests. Patch 3 (`t5551: pack refs after creating many tags`) packs the refs after creating the 100,000 tags, reducing the `ls-refs` advertisement time from 1.2 seconds to 24 milliseconds on macOS. Peff is ambivalent about the third patch, noting he’s "on the fence" about its value, but it’s a clear win for test efficiency and aligns with Michael Montalbo’s earlier ref-packing optimization.

The series is well-timed and well-motivated. It follows the technical direction agreed upon by Junio, Patrick Steinhardt, and Peff himself, and it includes concrete timing data and CI results from Michael’s experiments. The patches are small, focused, and touch only the test infrastructure (`t/lib-httpd/` and `t5551`), avoiding any changes to core Git behavior. The commit messages are clear and reference the upstream Apache bug (70131) and prior discussion, making the rationale easy to follow.

This looks like a straightforward, uncontroversial fix that should resolve the CI flakes without introducing new risks. The only open question is whether the 600-second timeout is the final value, but Peff’s choice is reasonable and unlikely to be contentious. The series is ready for review and likely to be merged quickly. If you care about CI reliability or the `t5551`/`t5559` tests, this is worth a look.
