This is a follow-up in the now-resolved `git ls-remote` infinite-loop thread. Steffen Nurpmeso reports that the original hang has not recurred, but he has observed a new behavior: with the debugging settings Michael Montalbo suggested (`http.lowSpeedLimit` and `http.lowSpeedTime`), `git ls-remote` now fails cleanly with a "too slow" error instead of entering a busy loop. The process still appears to block (no CPU usage), but it no longer spins indefinitely. Steffen notes that the root cause of the original busy loop remains unknown and that Git offers no built-in way to limit ticks-per-second or enforce non-blocking I/O in this context. The thread remains closed; no patch or further investigation is proposed.
