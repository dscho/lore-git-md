This is the **core optimization patch** in the v4 series that teaches Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query is exhausted. The patch is now **corrected and uncontroversial**, fixing the regression that caused test failures in v3.

The optimization adds an early-exit condition to `paint_queue_get()`: once the walk enters the finite-generation region (where topological ordering guarantees hold), the function returns NULL when either `parent1_count` or `parent2_count` drops to zero *and* no pending merge-base candidates remain. This eliminates unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories), yielding **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. Benchmarks on git.git show step-count reductions from 72,264 to 44,599 for `merge-base --all v2.0.0 v2.55.0-rc1`, and from 110 to 7 steps for `merge-base --all v2.55.0-rc1 v2.55.0-rc1~5`.

The patch also **restores the `min_generation` guard** to the generation-monotonicity BUG assertion, ensuring correctness when the threshold is not set. The INFINITY gate (`commit_graph_generation(commit) < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`) ensures the optimization only fires in the finite-generation region, where commit-date ordering cannot violate topological guarantees. The documentation in `paint-down-to-common.adoc` is updated to describe the new "side-exhaustion" termination condition, explaining why the optimization is safe and when it activates.

Test expectations are updated to reflect the reduced step counts: the `in_merge_bases_many:self` test now expects 1 step (down from 3) in full-graph mode, and the `merge-base --all commit-walk steps` test drops from 80 to 9 steps in full-graph mode. The changes are minimal and focused: 17 lines added to `commit-reach.c` (the early-exit logic and updated doc comment), 17 lines added to the technical document (explaining the side-exhaustion condition), and 2 lines changed in `t6600-test-reach.sh` (updated step-count expectations).
