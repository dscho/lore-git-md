This thread began with Jeff King (Peff) fixing a memory leak in the reftable backend’s `reftable_writer_new` function, where the `reftable_writer` struct was allocated before validating the block size, causing it to leak if the size was invalid. The patch was straightforward—moving the allocation after the check—and was merged into `pu` with a minor textual conflict resolved during integration.

Patrick Steinhardt later chimed in to confirm the fix was idiomatic and logical, briefly considering but dismissing an alternative approach (a unified error-exit path) as unnecessary. The leak was isolated to the block-size validation, as the function’s other early return already handled cleanup. The thread is now closed, with no further action needed. The reftable backend, an ongoing effort led by Patrick and Karthik Nayak, remains unaffected beyond this self-contained fix.
