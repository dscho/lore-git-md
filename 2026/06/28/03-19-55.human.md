This is a **refactoring patch discussion** in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on the final design choice for error handling in the new `repo_excludes_file()` getter. Tian Yuchen responds to Junio C Hamano’s suggestion to replace a silent early return with a `BUG()` assertion when the repository is uninitialized.

Tian argues for consistency with existing patterns: `repo_config_values()` already checks `repo->initialized`, and some flag getters intentionally bypass this check to allow reading/writing flags even when the repository is uninitialized. He proposes removing the redundant check in the new getter and letting `repo_config_values()` enforce the rule, treating the flag getters as exceptions rather than the norm. This would make the codebase’s error-handling philosophy more uniform and self-documenting.

The discussion is narrow but consequential for the libification effort’s guardrail design. No other technical objections remain, and the patch is otherwise ready for `next`. The outcome will likely set a precedent for similar getters introduced in future refactoring work. The tone is collaborative, with Tian providing a clear rationale for his preference while remaining open to Junio’s stricter approach.
