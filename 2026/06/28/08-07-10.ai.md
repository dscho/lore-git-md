This is the **third and final patch** in Jeff King’s (Peff) 3-patch series addressing macOS CI hangs in `t5551` and `t5559`. It targets the test suite’s performance when dealing with many loose refs by packing them after creation, reducing `ls-refs` advertisement time from ~1.2 seconds to 24 ms. The patch adds a single line (`git pack-refs --all`) to the `create_tags` helper in `t/t5551-http-fetch-smart.sh`.

Peff is **openly ambivalent** about this patch’s value. While it demonstrably speeds up ref advertisement, the runtime improvement is marginal in the context of the full series: patch 2 already isolates the expensive test case, so subsequent tests never see the unpacked state, and patch 1 (the Apache timeout increase) alone resolves the CI hangs. The patch’s benefit is most pronounced if applied in isolation, but as part of the series it feels redundant. Peff suggests the series could even be reduced to just this patch, but he includes it as a "belt-and-braces" measure, acknowledging that the earlier patches’ reasoning makes this one superfluous.

The change is **low-risk and narrowly scoped**, touching only the test script and introducing no new behavior outside the test suite. It reflects a realistic workflow (packing refs after bulk creation) and aligns with Git’s maintenance recommendations, but its necessity is questionable given the other patches. The thread’s consensus has already coalesced around the timeout increase as the primary fix, so this patch may be dropped during final review if reviewers agree it adds more complexity than value.
