This is the final resolution email for the `excludes_file` migration patch, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal/libification effort. The patch is now **ready for `next`** after resolving the last design question about initialization guardrails.

Tian Yuchen and Junio C Hamano have agreed to **remove the redundant `repo->initialized` check from `repo_excludes_file()`** and rely entirely on `repo_config_values()` to enforce repository initialization. This aligns with the broader libification principle of centralizing validation logic in a single helper rather than scattering checks across individual getters. The change is a one-line removal in `environment.c` (the getter implementation), and no other modifications are needed.

The email highlights that CI failures are expected at this stage, as the stricter design surfaces previously hidden invalid calls (e.g., tests passing uninitialized repositories to the getter). Tian notes that these failures confirm the guardrail is working as intended, though they also underscore the need for follow-up cleanup in callers. The resolution here sets a precedent for future repository-aware getters (e.g., `attributes_file` migration), reinforcing that `repo_config_values()` is the canonical place for initialization validation.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion:
- **Files**: `environment.c` (getter), `repository.c` (`repo_config_values()` helper).
- **Symbols**: `repo_excludes_file()` (now assumes initialized repository), `repo_config_values()` (retains `repo->initialized` check).
- **Memory**: Heap allocation for `excludes_file` remains unchanged; cleanup via `repo_config_values_clear()`.
- **Behavior**: No user-visible changes; this is purely a refactoring for libification.
- **CI failures**: Expected and intentional, as the stricter design exposes invalid callers. These will need to be addressed in separate patches.
