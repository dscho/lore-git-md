The document is thorough and well-structured, covering the algorithm’s two use cases (finding all merge bases vs. ancestry checks), the paint-walk mechanics, generation-number regions (finite vs. INFINITY), and the four termination conditions (queue empty, stale entries, generation cutoff, single result). It also clarifies the impact of commit-graph generation numbers on traversal order and explains why paint flags are final in the finite region but not in the INFINITY region.

This patch is **ready to merge** and uncontroversial. It adds no new behavior—only documentation—but provides essential context for the subsequent patches in the series, which modify `paint_down_to_common()` to optimize early termination for one-sided histories. The document will help reviewers and future contributors understand the algorithm’s invariants and edge cases, making it easier to assess the correctness of the later performance improvements.
