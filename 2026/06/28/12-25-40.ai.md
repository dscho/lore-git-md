This is the second patch in Tian Yuchen’s v4 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. The patch adds **four edge-case test scenarios** to `t6600-test-reach.sh`, authored by Elijah Newren and co-signed by Kristofer Karlsson.

The new tests exercise the side-exhaustion optimization under conditions that the existing 10×10 grid topology does not cover:
- **`in_merge_bases_many:self`** – a commit is both the query tip and one of the inputs.
- **`get_merge_bases_many:duplicate-twos`** – duplicate entries in the input list.
- **`get_merge_bases_many:pending-stale`** – a STALE transition on an already-painted commit (ps-* diamond topology).
- **`get_merge_bases_many:infinity-both-sides`** – both tips outside the commit-graph with non-monotonic dates (pi-* topology).

The patch is **pure test coverage**: it adds 111 lines to `t6600-test-reach.sh`, including setup for two new topologies (ps-* and pi-*), and four new test cases. The ps-* topology exercises the (PARENT1|PARENT2) → (PARENT1|PARENT2|STALE) transition path; the pi-* topology exercises the INFINITY-gate fallback to commit-date ordering. All tests run in `test_all_modes`, so they are verified against all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat).

This patch is **stable and unchanged since v3**; it was originally contributed by Elijah Newren via PR #2150 and has been part of the series since v1. The test cases are **deterministic, self-contained, and ready to merge**. They provide the edge-case coverage that caught the regression in v3 and will continue to guard against future regressions in the side-exhaustion optimization.
