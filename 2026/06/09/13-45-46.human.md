This is a follow-up in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing the migration of the `trust_executable_bit` configuration from global to repository-scoped storage.

Junio C Hamano responds to Tian Yuchen's reconsideration about including `has_symlinks` migration in the same series. Tian had initially agreed to handle both configurations together per Junio's earlier suggestion, but now reports that `has_symlinks` presents unexpected complexity - its call sites have deep call stacks with minimal parameter passing, making repository plumbing cumbersome. 

Junio accepts this revised approach, agreeing it's reasonable to focus just on `trust_executable_bit` for now. This leaves open how to handle similar complex migrations in the future, but moves forward the immediate refactoring work without blocking on the more difficult case. The exchange shows the practical balancing act between architectural goals and implementation constraints in this large-scale refactoring effort.
