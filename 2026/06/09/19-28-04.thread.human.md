This thread discusses consolidating two reachability functions in Git's commit-reach.c - the older `get_reachable_subset()` and newer `tips_reachable_from_bases()` - which solve similar problems but use different algorithms (BFS vs DFS). However, he argues commit-graph usage is now widespread enough to justify consolidation around the newer implementation.
