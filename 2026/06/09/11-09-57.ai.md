Performance optimization review for `git describe` patch. Jeff King (Peff) provides substantive feedback on Tamir Duberstein's v2 patch that limits ref iteration to tags by default in `git describe`.

Peff agrees with the technical approach but raises several points:
1. The commit message's benchmark details are overly verbose - he suggests a more concise summary would better communicate the key 17x speedup
2. The performance test could be more realistic by packing refs, though acknowledges the synthetic test approach is reasonable
3. Offers a more succinct way to generate test refs using `test_seq` piped directly to `update-ref`
4. Notes the optimization might allow future cleanup in `get_name()` since non-tag refs would no longer reach it

The review also sparks a broader discussion about AI-generated commit messages, with Peff observing they tend toward excessive detail that may overwhelm human readers. While not blocking the patch, he questions whether contributors should be prompted to make such messages more concise.

The technical feedback confirms the optimization is sound but suggests minor test improvements. The patch appears ready to proceed with potential follow-up cleanups, though the commit message style discussion may continue separately.
