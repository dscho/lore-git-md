Tamir Duberstein has completed work on optimizing `git ls-files --deleted` and `--modified` to dramatically reduce filesystem operations by applying pathspec filtering before `lstat()` calls. The optimization, which shows performance improvements of up to 60x in tested cases (65.8s down to 5.0s for a large index), was initially restricted to single pathspecs but has been expanded to work universally following maintainer feedback.

Jeff King (Peff) provided technical approval and suggested removing the single-pathspec restriction, while Junio Hamano confirmed this approach aligns with similar optimizations elsewhere in Git. The only remaining changes were documentation updates to clearly explain the optimization strategy in the commit message. With all technical questions resolved and the final version submitted, this performance improvement is ready for merging. The patch includes expanded test coverage to validate the optimization works correctly with wildcard pathspecs and edge cases.
