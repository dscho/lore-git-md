This is a final discussion in the promisor remote auto-configuration thread, where Christian Couder responds to Toon Claes' suggestion about configuration precedence. The series is otherwise complete and ready to merge, having addressed all previous technical and documentation feedback.

Christian explains why he prefers keeping the current design where `acceptFromServer` and `acceptFromServerUrl` remain independent configuration options rather than making them mutually exclusive as Toon proposed. This layered approach would be impossible with Toon's suggested model.

The exchange is collegial, with Christian acknowledging Toon's review while standing by the current design.
