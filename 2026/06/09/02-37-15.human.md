Performance optimization for `ls-files` with pathspec filtering.

This patch optimizes `git ls-files --deleted` and `--modified` by applying pathspec filter before the expensive `lstat()` call when there's only a single pathspec. The change avoids unnecessary filesystem stats for entries that wouldn't match the pathspec anyway. Benchmark results show dramatic improvements - from 60+ seconds down to ~1 second for a selective pathspec on a large repository.

The optimization is carefully limited to single-pathspec cases to avoid regressions when many pathspecs are given (as identified by Jeff King). The patch includes both performance tests and functional tests to verify the behavior remains correct, particularly around the `--error-unmatch` feature.

This is v2 of the patch, addressing feedback from v1 by restricting the optimization scope and adding more comprehensive benchmarks. The performance impact is now well-characterized across different scenarios (selective pathspec, wildcard pathspec, and many pathspecs).
