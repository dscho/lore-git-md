Performance optimization follow-up for `git ls-files` pathspec filtering.

Tamir Duberstein responds to Jeff King's (Peff) question about the arbitrary cutoff in the `git ls-files --deleted`/`--modified` optimization. The current patch only applies the pathspec pre-filtering when there's exactly one pathspec, avoiding the O(n) cost of `match_pathspec()` for multiple pathspecs. Peff had noted this creates an artificial boundary, as the optimization could still benefit cases with 2-10 pathspecs, just with diminishing returns.

Tamir acknowledges the arbitrariness but takes a pragmatic approach - the current patch provides clear benefits for the single-pathspec case without complicating the implementation. He leaves the door open for future contributors to adjust the threshold if they can demonstrate further improvements, maintaining Git's culture of incremental optimization based on real-world profiling data.
