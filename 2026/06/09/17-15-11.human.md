This is a performance discussion thread about worktree diff operations, now focusing on racy-git behavior rather than a worktree-specific issue. D. Ben Knoble follows up on Jeff King's analysis that identified the slowdown as stemming from mtime-based racy-git checks rather than worktree internals.

Knoble confirms King's findings that `git update-index --refresh` resolves the performance gap, suggesting the issue was indeed from files sharing modification times with their index entries. He proposes two potential improvements: having `git diff` automatically refresh the index (though acknowledges this might be a band-aid) and having `git worktree add` perform an index refresh to ensure a clean starting state.

The discussion reveals platform-specific nuances - Knoble notes that on macOS, Meson automatically sets either USE_ST_TIMESPEC or NO_NSEC but lacks a USE_NSEC option, which King had suggested as a solution path. Knoble offers to write a patch to add this option for testing purposes.

The exchange demonstrates how initial performance observations can be redirected by deeper technical analysis, with King's expertise helping reframe the issue in terms of Git's fundamental timestamp handling rather than worktree mechanics. The thread remains open for further discussion about whether index-refreshing behavior should be modified in worktree creation or diff operations.
