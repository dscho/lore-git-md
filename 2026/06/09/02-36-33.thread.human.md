The series has evolved through four versions, addressing all substantive review feedback, and is now technically complete with all patches reviewed and approved.

The optimization delivers significant performance gains: a 100x speedup with generation numbers (104s to 467ms for 62,174 refs) and a 10x improvement without them (4.861s to 478.9ms). The implementation consists of three patches: (1) cycle detection in `commit-reach.c`, treating cycles as fatal errors and adding comprehensive test coverage; (2) enabling memoized traversal for all ref-filter commands while preserving backward compatibility; and (3) unifying error handling to ensure consistent behavior between `--contains` and `--no-contains`.
