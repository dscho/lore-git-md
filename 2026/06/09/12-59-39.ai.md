Maintainer feedback on `git switch --ensure` proposal.

Junio C Hamano responds to Lei Zhu's proposed `--ensure` (`-e`) flag for `git switch`, expressing reservations about the feature's design while offering concrete suggestions for improvement. His key points:

1. **Use case concerns**: Questions whether the proposed behavior should be supported at all, identifying four distinct scenarios users might encounter when switching branches (only one of which the patch handles optimally). Argues the current explicit failure when a branch exists provides better workflow safety by forcing users to consciously choose their next action.

2. **Naming critique**: Strongly objects to `-e` as the short option, noting it conflicts with established expectations around `--editor`. Suggests `--create-if-missing` as a clearer long option name while leaving the short form open for discussion.

3. **Command parity**: Points out the inconsistency of adding this only to `git switch` without matching `git checkout` support, noting the practical reality that `checkout` remains widely used despite the `switch/restore` split.

The feedback maintains Git's characteristic balance between welcoming contributions and upholding design standards - acknowledging the proposal's intent while clearly articulating why the current approach may not meet the project's bar for new features. Junio's suggestions focus on improving the patch's conceptual foundation (use case analysis) before addressing implementation details (naming, command coverage).
