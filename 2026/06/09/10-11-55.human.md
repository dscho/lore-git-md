This is part 4/6 of Harald Nordgren's v14 series adding `git branch --prune-merged` for safe automated local branch cleanup. The series has gone through extensive review over 14 iterations, with all major technical concerns now addressed.

The patch introduces the core `--prune-merged` option that deletes local branches whose work has been integrated into their upstream. It builds on the `--forked` filter from earlier in the series to identify candidate branches, then applies several safety checks:
- Skips branches checked out in any worktree
- Preserves branches whose upstream no longer resolves locally
- Excludes branches where push destination equals upstream (like main tracking origin/main)
- Warns rather than fails on unmerged branches, allowing bulk operations to continue

The implementation handles multiple branch patterns, shows dry-run output matching deletion messages, and maintains backward compatibility with existing branch deletion behavior. The extensive test suite verifies edge cases like missing refs, checked-out branches, and push/upstream comparisons.

This version resolves the last blocking regression around remote-tracking branch behavior and adds the warn-only mode requested in earlier reviews. The series appears ready for final consideration after its long refinement process.
