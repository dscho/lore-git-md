This is a preparatory refactoring patch for the `git history reword` command, part of Pablo Sabater's v2 RFC series. The patch modifies the function signature of `commit_tree_with_edited_message()` to accept flags that will be passed through to `commit_tree_ext()`, rather than hardcoding the `COMMIT_TREE_EDIT_MESSAGE` flag.

The change is mechanical and well-contained, serving as groundwork for the subsequent patch that will introduce new flag-based control over commit message handling. The patch maintains existing behavior while enabling future flexibility needed for the reword command's abort-on-identical-message feature.

This is a straightforward internal refactoring with no user-visible changes.
