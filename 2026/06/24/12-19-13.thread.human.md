Here’s the human-mode narrative for a developer catching up on the thread:

This thread is the first version of Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to track object sources more precisely, part of his larger ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to replace the coarse `whence` field with a new `struct object_info_source` that can identify not just the type of source (e.g., packfile) but also which specific backend provided it. This is a prerequisite for multi-source object resolution and pluggable ODB backends. Junio C Hamano has conceptually approved the direction, calling it "Great," and all six patches have now been reviewed.

The series is technically complete and targets `master`, but one architectural question lingered: whether source tracking should be opt-in (the current design) or centralized at the `packed_git` level. Patrick defended the opt-in approach as a pragmatic step toward eliminating direct use of `packed_object_info()` in favor of the higher-level `odb_read_object_info()` API, arguing that most callers already use the latter and thus don’t need adjustment. Junio and Justin Tobler initially favored centralized tracking for simplicity, but Junio has now accepted Patrick’s explanation, leaving the design settled for this iteration. The only remaining loose end is a low-level readability concern: Junio noted that the pointer assignment `oi->sourcep->source = &source->base` hides an implicit upcast, which might confuse future maintainers, though he didn’t block the patch over it.

The patches touch 21 files across the ODB and packfile subsystems, introducing `struct object_info_source` and replacing `whence` with `sourcep->source->type`. No behavior changes or on-disk format modifications are introduced, and existing tests cover the changes. The series is now ready for merging, with no unresolved technical objections.
