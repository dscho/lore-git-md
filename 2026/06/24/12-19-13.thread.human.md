Here’s the human-readable summary for a developer catching up on the thread:

This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring Git’s `struct object_info` to replace the coarse `whence` field with a more flexible `source` field, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to allow callers to opt into tracking an object’s provenance (e.g., which packfile it came from) without unconditionally populating that data, paving the way for pluggable ODB backends. Junio C Hamano has conceptually approved the direction ("Great"), and the series is now complete at v1, targeting `master` with no behavior changes.

The first five patches have been reviewed: patches 1–3/6 refactor the struct and callers, with Justin Tobler raising a medium-weight design question about whether source tracking should be centralized at the `packed_git` level rather than opt-in. Patch 4/6 mechanically converted all `whence` usage to the new `sourcep` field, and the just-reviewed patch 5/6 removes the now-unused `whence` field entirely—a straightforward cleanup. Patch 6/6 (documentation) remains unreviewed. The only open question is Tobler’s architectural concern about the opt-in design, but no alternative implementation has been proposed yet. The series is otherwise ready to merge once the remaining patch is reviewed.
