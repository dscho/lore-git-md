This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to replace its coarse `whence` field with a new `source` field, a key step in Git’s ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to allow precise tracking of an object’s origin (e.g., which packfile) without unconditionally populating provenance data, enabling multi-source object resolution and pluggable backends. Junio C Hamano has already given the series conceptual approval, calling it "Great," but substantive review is still pending.

The first patch, which adds an optional `struct odb_source_packed *source` parameter to two core ODB functions, drew a thoughtful review from Justin Tobler. He questions whether tracking the source at the `packed_git` level might be a cleaner design, though he acknowledges the current approach is intentional and mechanically sound. His feedback focuses on architectural trade-offs rather than immediate flaws, leaving the door open for further discussion about where source-tracking logic should live. The series itself is self-contained, targets `master`, and introduces no behavioral changes yet—just the groundwork for later patches to populate and use the new `source` field. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction work, this thread is worth watching for how these design questions evolve.
