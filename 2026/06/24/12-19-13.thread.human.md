Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to use an opt-in `source_infop` field has been queued by Junio C Hamano and will soon appear in the `next` branch for integration testing. The series, which replaces the coarse `whence` field and `u` union with `struct odb_source_info` to better track object provenance, was previously reviewed and conceptually approved. The second iteration resolved naming ambiguities and added documentation, addressing all remaining feedback.

The refactor is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort and is now effectively complete. It touches 22 files across the ODB and packfile subsystems but makes no on-disk format changes. The only lingering readability concern—a pointer assignment in patch 3/6—was noted by Junio but deemed non-blocking. With the series now in the integration pipeline, expect it to graduate to `master` in the coming weeks.
