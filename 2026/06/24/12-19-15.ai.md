This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to support pluggable object database (ODB) backends. It splits the backend-specific details (currently only the packed-source case) out of `struct object_info` into a new, opt-in `struct object_info_source`. Callers that need provenance information now allocate the new struct on the stack and point `sourcep` at it; those that don’t simply leave `sourcep` as `NULL`. The patch touches seven files (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `packfile.c`, and `reachable.c`), converting all existing `oi->u.packed` accesses to `oi->sourcep->u.packed` and making the backend union population conditional on `sourcep` being non-NULL.

The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior; it merely makes the source information opt-in rather than unconditionally populated. This is a necessary step toward the eventual goal of uniquely identifying objects even when they appear in multiple sources (e.g., different packfiles). The patch is well-structured, with a clear commit message explaining the motivation and the opt-in design. No substantive review feedback has appeared yet, but the patch looks clean and aligns with the series’ overall goal of preparing the ODB for pluggable backends. If you’re tracking Patrick’s ODB abstraction effort, this is a foundational piece.
