This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s three-part series refactoring Git’s connectivity checks to remove hardcoded packfile dependencies, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The patch replaces the direct use of `find_pack_entry_one()` with a generic `odb_for_each_object_ext()` call, aiming to make the code backend-agnostic. Junio C Hamano’s recent review flagged a potential regression: the removal of `odb_reprepare()`, which refreshes the ODB’s view of packfiles before iteration.

Patrick now acknowledges the issue as a bug. He explains that he initially misread `odb_reprepare()` as `odb_alternates_prepare()`, which would not reload packfiles, and assumed the new `odb_for_each_object_ext()` helper would handle all necessary preparation. However, `odb_reprepare()` also reloads packfiles and loose objects, meaning its removal could cause connectivity checks to miss promisor objects in packs added after the loop begins. While the fallback to `rev-list` ensures correctness, the change could still introduce inefficiency or edge-case failures. Patrick does not yet propose a fix, but the admission confirms the regression is real and will need addressing in a reroll. The thread is now focused on this single correctness issue, with no other aspects of the patch under active discussion.
