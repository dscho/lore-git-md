This is a **performance instrumentation patch** in the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series. It adds trace2 instrumentation to expose the number of commits walked during the paint walk, enabling deterministic measurement of the optimization's impact without relying solely on wall-clock benchmarks.

The patch introduces a step counter in `paint_down_to_common()` and logs it via `trace2_data_intmax()`, making the "commits walked" metric observable in `GIT_TRACE2_PERF` or `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` logs. This is critical for validating the upcoming optimization (patch 7/7), which aims to reduce the number of steps in asymmetric histories. The test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` verifies the instrumentation by comparing step counts across different commit-graph configurations (none, full, and partial), ensuring the metric is consistent and testable.

The change is minimal and focused: it touches only `commit-reach.c` and the test script, with no behavior changes. The test setup is thorough, covering edge cases like missing commit-graphs and partial generation-number coverage. This patch is a prerequisite for the final optimization, providing the empirical foundation to demonstrate its effectiveness. The series is now technically complete and ready to land, with all feedback incorporated.
