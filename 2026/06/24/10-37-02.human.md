This is the second iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series to make Git’s connectivity checks work generically across object database (ODB) backends, removing the hardcoded dependency on packfile internals. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is now built on top of the merged `ps/odb-source-packed` branch.

The v2 update directly addresses the two substantive issues raised in reviews of v1. First, it restores the accidentally dropped `odb_reprepare()` call, which ensures the ODB’s view of packfiles is refreshed before iteration begins—critical for correctness when promisor packs are added after the initial setup. Second, it splits the `check_connected()` function into two focused parts, with `check_connected_promisor()` now handling the promisor-specific logic. Patrick opted not to split out the `rev-list`-based fallback (`check_connected_rev_list()`), judging the additional refactoring as having diminishing returns.

The series remains well-scoped and technically sound. The changes are confined to `connected.c` and `odb/source-packed.c`, with the net code churn slightly higher than in v1 due to the added preparatory commit. The commit messages are clear, and the cover letter explicitly links to the v1 discussion, making it easy to trace the evolution of the patches. This version looks ready for another round of review, with the most likely points of scrutiny being the interaction between the new `check_connected_promisor()` and the ODB abstraction, and whether the restored `odb_reprepare()` call is sufficient for all edge cases. No new objections are expected, but reviewers may ask for additional test coverage to verify the behavior with non-packfile backends.
