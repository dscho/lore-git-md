This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part ODB refactoring series, part of his broader effort to make Git’s object storage layer pluggable. The patch restructures `struct object_info` to make backend-specific details (like packfile location and offset) opt-in rather than mandatory. Previously, the `u` union in `struct object_info` was always populated, even when callers didn’t need it. Now, those details are moved into a new `struct object_info_source`, which callers can request by setting the `sourcep` pointer in `struct object_info`.

The change is mechanical but important: it aligns the object info struct with Git’s request-response pattern (where callers opt into the data they want) and reduces unnecessary work for callers that don’t care about provenance. The patch updates seven files, including `builtin/cat-file.c`, `packfile.c`, and `odb.h`, to use the new `sourcep` field. Callers that previously accessed `oi->u.packed` now check `oi->sourcep` first, and those that don’t need source info simply pass `NULL`.

This is a preparatory step for later patches in the series, which will replace the `whence` field with a more flexible `struct odb_source` pointer. The patch is well-motivated and cleanly executed, with no behavior changes—just a refactoring to enable future work. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction effort, this is a foundational piece. No review feedback has appeared yet, so the series is still in its early stages.
