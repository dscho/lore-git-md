This is a minor cleanup patch in the now-merged `paint_down_to_common()` performance optimization series. The patch removes two wrapper functions—`nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()`—that became unused after the previous patch in the series introduced the `paint_state` struct. The core `nonstale_queue` functions remain in use by the `ahead_behind()` function, so this change is purely about dead code removal.

The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ broader goal of simplifying the codebase before introducing the final optimization. Derrick Stolee’s review confirms this, calling it a "nice cleanup" that improves readability. No behavior changes or test regressions are expected, as the functions were unused. This is a straightforward housekeeping step in a well-reviewed and already-merged series.
