This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s three-part series refactoring Git’s connectivity checks to remove hardcoded packfile dependencies, a key step in the ODB abstraction effort. The patch replaces the packfile-specific `find_pack_entry_one()` with a generic `odb_for_each_object_ext()` call, aiming to make connectivity checks backend-agnostic. However, the discussion now centers on two critical issues raised by reviewers Junio C Hamano and Christian Couder.

First, Patrick acknowledges an unintentional but serious regression: the patch removes the `odb_reprepare()` call, which refreshes the ODB’s view of packfiles before iteration. This would cause connectivity checks to miss promisor objects in packs added after iteration begins, though the fallback to `rev-list` ensures correctness at a performance cost. The removal appears to be a misreading of the original code, and Patrick agrees it must be addressed—likely by reintroducing the refresh step in a way that doesn’t reintroduce packfile-specific assumptions.

Second, Christian critiques the patch’s control flow as overly complex, with nested loops, multiple `goto` statements, and mixed error handling making the logic hard to follow. He proposes a preparatory refactoring to split `check_connected()` into two focused functions: one for promisor-object search and another for the fallback `rev-list` logic. Patrick accepts this suggestion and plans to implement it, which should improve maintainability and make edge cases easier to reason about.

The patch touches `connected.c` and `odb/source-packed.c`, introducing new symbols like `promised_object_cb()` and modifying how iteration flags are handled. While the series is foundational for ODB abstraction, these issues—particularly the `odb_reprepare()` regression—must be resolved before the patch can proceed. The discussion is substantive and technical, with no objections yet to the core goal of generalizing connectivity checks. Reviewers focused on ODB abstraction or connectivity logic are likely to engage further once Patrick posts a revised version.
