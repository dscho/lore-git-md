This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch security-hardening series for the reftable backend. It fixes a use of uninitialized memory in `reftable/block.c` that occurs when the binary-search helper `restart_needle_less()` fails to decode a record at a restart offset. The error path would call `reftable_record_release()` on a record that had never been initialized, leading to undefined behavior. The fix is a simple re-ordering: the record is now initialized immediately at the start of the function, before any error paths can be taken. The change is minimal (four lines moved), touches only one file, and comes with no new tests—this particular edge case is already covered by the fuzzer introduced earlier in the series.
