This is a refactoring patch series from Patrick Steinhardt, part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The series removes the `whence` field from `struct object_info` and replaces it with a new `struct object_info_source` field that carries both the source type and backend-specific data. The goal is to allow callers to re-query the same backend for an object and eventually uniquely identify objects even when they appear multiple times in a source (e.g., by recording packfile and offset).

The series touches 14 files, primarily in the ODB and packfile subsystems, with minor changes in built-in commands (`cat-file`, `index-pack`, `pack-objects`) and utilities (`commit-graph`, `pack-bitmap`). The commit messages are clear and the changes appear mechanical: converting users of `whence` to the new `source` field and documenting the updated structure. This looks like a well-crafted, incremental step in the ODB refactoring, with no behavior change—just restructuring to enable future work.

If you follow the ODB abstraction effort, this is worth noting as it lays groundwork for more precise object identification. The series is based on recent `master` with Patrick’s `ps/odb-source-packed` topic merged in, so it’s ready for review.
