This is a refactoring patch in the **`paint_down_to_common()` performance optimization series** (v2, 6/7). The series, authored by Tian Yuchen and now ready to land, optimizes merge-base calculations by terminating early when one side of a history is exhausted.

This specific patch removes two wrapper functions—`nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()`—that became unused after the previous patch (5/7) introduced the `paint_state` struct. The core `nonstale_queue` functions remain in use by the `ahead_behind()` function, so this is purely a cleanup of dead code. The patch touches only `commit-reach.c`, deleting 18 lines without altering behavior.

The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the codebase before introducing the final optimization (patch 7/7). The wrappers were originally introduced for deduplication logic that is no longer needed in the refactored `paint_down_to_common()` implementation. No test failures or regressions are expected, as the functions were unused. This is a straightforward housekeeping step in a well-reviewed series.
