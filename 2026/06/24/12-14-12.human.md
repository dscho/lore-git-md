This is the sixth patch in the seven-patch series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. The series is performance-focused, well-tested, and ready to land.

This patch is a clean-up step. It removes two wrapper functions—`nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()`—that became unused after the previous refactoring (patch 5/7). The core `nonstale_queue` functions remain in place because they are still needed by the `ahead_behind()` function, which was the source of a structural dispute in v1. That dispute is now resolved: the ahead/behind logic keeps its own queue, so the dedup wrappers are no longer necessary.

The change is minimal and mechanical—just 18 lines deleted from `commit-reach.c`. There is no behavior change, and the commit message clearly explains why the functions are being removed. This patch is uncontroversial and serves as a tidy follow-up to the refactoring in patch 5/7. It keeps the codebase clean without disrupting any existing functionality.
