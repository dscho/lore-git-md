Thread identity: patch series, v2, "refactor `struct object_info` to use `source_infop` field", Patrick Steinhardt, ODB subsystem refactoring.
Core problem: `struct object_info`'s `whence` field and `u` union are too coarse to uniquely identify an object's source (e.g., which packfile or ODB backend), blocking multi-source object resolution and pluggable ODB backends. The series replaces `whence` and `u` with a new opt-in `struct odb_source_info` that carries both source type and backend-specific data, making provenance information optional rather than unconditionally populated.
Current status: v2 complete (6/6 patches posted), **conceptually approved by Junio C Hamano ("everything makes sense")**, unmerged. Series is self-contained and targets `master`; based on `ps/odb-source-packed`. All patches reviewed: **1/6 introduces the opt-in `source_infop` parameter** (no behavior change, callers outside packed ODB pass `NULL`), **2/6 splits the `u` union into a new opt-in `struct odb_source_info` and adds a `source_infop` pointer to `struct object_info`**, **3/6 populates the `source` field in ODB backends (`odb/source-inmemory.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `packfile.c`) and adds a BUG check in `packfile.c` to prevent callers from requesting source information without a valid `source_infop` parameter**, **4/6 mechanically converts all remaining `whence` users to `source_infop->source->type`** (touches `builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `reachable.c`), 5-6/6 remove `whence` and add documentation (received **surface-level approval** from Justin Tobler). **Naming ambiguity resolved in v2**: `struct object_info_source` → `struct odb_source_info`, `sourcep` → `source_infop`. Behavior unchanged except callers can now opt out of source population.
Key technical details: 6 patches, 22 files touched (`odb.h`, `odb.c`, `packfile.c`, `odb/source-*.c`, `builtin/cat-file.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `reachable.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `pack-bitmap.c`, `t/helper/test-bitmap.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `packfile.h`). **Patch 1/6** adds optional `struct odb_source_info **source_infop` parameter to `packed_object_info()` and `packed_object_info_with_index_pos()` (touches 8 files). **Patch 2/6** moves the `u` union from `struct object_info` into `struct odb_source_info`, adds a `source_infop` pointer to `struct object_info`, and updates callers to pass either a stack-allocated `struct odb_source_info` or `NULL` (touches 7 files). **Patch 3/6** populates the `source` field in backends and adds a BUG check in `packfile.c` to enforce valid `source_infop` usage; introduces the implicit upcast `oi->source_infop->source = &source->base` (flagged by Junio as "yuck" but accepted). **Patch 4/6** converts all remaining `whence` users to `oi->source_infop->source->type` (pattern: `oi->whence == OI_PACKED` → `oi->source_infop->source->type == ODB_SOURCE_PACKED`). New symbols: `struct odb_source_info` (with `source` field), `oi->source_infop` (replaces `oi->whence` and `oi->u`). **Patches 5-6/6** remove `whence`, eliminate the field, and add documentation. No on-disk format changes. Test coverage: existing ODB/packfile tests exercise the changes.
