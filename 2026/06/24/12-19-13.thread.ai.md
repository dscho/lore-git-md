Thread identity: patch series, v2, "refactor `struct object_info` to use `source_infop` field", Patrick Steinhardt, ODB subsystem refactoring.
Core problem: `struct object_info`'s `whence` field and `u` union are too coarse to uniquely identify an object's source (e.g., which packfile or ODB backend), blocking multi-source object resolution and pluggable ODB backends. The series replaces `whence` and `u` with a new opt-in `struct odb_source_info` that carries both source type and backend-specific data, making provenance information optional rather than unconditionally populated.
