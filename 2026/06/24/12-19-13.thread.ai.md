Thread identity: patch series, v1, "refactor `struct object_info` to use `source` field", Patrick Steinhardt, ODB subsystem refactoring.
Core problem: `struct object_info`'s `whence` field is too coarse to uniquely identify an object's source (e.g., which packfile), blocking multi-source object resolution and pluggable ODB backends. The series replaces `whence` with a new `struct object_info_source` that carries both source type and backend-specific data, making provenance information opt-in rather than unconditionally populated.
Current status: v1 complete (6/6 patches posted), conceptually approved by Junio C Hamano ("Great"), unmerged, awaiting resolution of architectural design questions. Series is self-contained and targets `master`; based on `ps/odb-source-packed`. All patches now reviewed: 1-5/6 refactor the struct and callers (patches 1-3/6 received **substantive review** from Justin Tobler and Junio C Hamano; patches 4-5/6 received **surface-level approval** from Justin Tobler), 6/6 adds documentation. Behavior remains unchanged except callers can now opt out of source population.
Key technical details: 6 patches, 21 files touched (`odb.h`, `odb.c`, `packfile.c`, `odb/source-*.c`, `builtin/cat-file.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `reachable.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `pack-bitmap.c`, `t/helper/test-bitmap.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`).
