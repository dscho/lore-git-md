This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to use a `source` field instead of the older `whence` field. The series is part of his ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, which aims to make Git’s object storage layer pluggable.

This patch is purely mechanical: after the previous four patches migrated all callers to use the new `source` field, the `whence` field is now unused and can be removed. The diff deletes the `whence` enum from `odb.h` and removes the five places in `odb.c`, `odb/source-inmemory.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, and `packfile.c` where the field was set. No behavior changes; the patch simply cleans up the now-dead code.

The change is small (14 lines removed across five files) and uncontroversial—it’s the natural conclusion of the refactoring begun earlier in the series. If you’re following the ODB abstraction work, this patch is a routine step; if not, it’s safe to ignore. No review feedback has appeared yet, so the series is still in its first iteration.
