This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to use a `source` field instead of the now-obsolete `whence` field. The patch mechanically removes the `whence` field from `struct object_info` and all its assignments across the ODB backends (`odb.c`, `odb/source-inmemory.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `packfile.c`). The diff is small (14 lines removed) and straightforward: every line that sets `oi->whence` is deleted, and the enum definition in `odb.h` is dropped.

The patch is a clean-up step that follows the mechanical conversion of all callers to use `oi->sourcep->source->type` in patch 4/6. There are no behavior changes—this is purely a removal of dead code. The series remains part of Patrick’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort, which aims to make Git’s object storage layer pluggable. If the earlier patches in the series have been reviewed and look solid, this one should be uncontroversial and ready to merge once the rest of the series is approved.
