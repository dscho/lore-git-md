This is a follow-up in the ongoing bundle-URI protocol bugfix discussion. Toon Claes resurfaces the thread to weigh in on the balance between client-side resilience and server-side validation for malformed bundle-URI data.

Toon acknowledges the optional nature of bundle-URI and reaffirms his original position: the client should tolerate invalid data (with a warning) rather than fail, since clone can proceed without bundle-URIs. He pushes back slightly on Justin Tobler’s preference for server-side fixes, arguing that his patch doesn’t silently mask the issue—it surfaces a warning that could prompt users to report misconfigured servers. However, he concedes that the warning wasn’t tested, which weakens its visibility.

On the server-side fix proposed by Justin (filtering empty URIs during configuration-to-packet conversion), Toon expresses discomfort with silently dropping invalid entries but admits he lacks a better alternative for alerting server admins. His skepticism centers on discoverability: a single log line is easily overlooked. The email leaves the structural question open—whether to combine both fixes in one series or split them—while reinforcing the technical consensus that both approaches are needed. The tone is collaborative, suggesting the thread is nearing resolution.
