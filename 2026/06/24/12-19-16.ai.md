This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part ODB refactoring series. It adds a `source` field to the new `struct object_info_source` introduced in patch 2/6, allowing callers to identify which backend (loose, packed, in-memory) an object was retrieved from. The field is populated by each backend during object lookup, replacing the need for the `whence` enum to distinguish sources. The patch touches four files: `odb.h` (declaration), `odb/source-inmemory.c` and `odb/source-loose.c` (loose and in-memory backend updates), and `packfile.c` (packed backend logic, including a BUG check for missing source). No behavior changes yet—`whence` remains in use until patch 5/6 removes it. The change is mechanical and prepares for multi-source object resolution in future ODB work.
