This is the sixth and final patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It completes the refactoring by replacing the direct `tmp_objdir` calls in `builtin/receive-pack.c` with the new `odb_transaction` interfaces, and adds a special `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag to preserve the eager directory creation and "incoming" prefix that `receive-pack` requires.

The patch touches 14 files, but the bulk of the work is in `builtin/receive-pack.c` (46 lines changed) and `object-file.c` (22 lines added). It introduces a new `enum odb_transaction_flags` with a single `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag that tells the "files" backend to create the temporary directory immediately and use the "incoming" prefix, matching the old `tmp_objdir` behavior. Every caller of `odb_transaction_begin()` and `odb_transaction_begin_or_die()` gains a flags parameter, though most pass 0.

The series as a whole is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction effort, and this patch is the payoff: `receive-pack` now stages objects via transactions rather than temporary directories, moving the command closer to being ODB-backend-agnostic. The changes are mechanical but non-trivial, and the commit message clearly explains the motivation and the special-casing needed for `receive-pack`. There are no user-facing behavior changes, so this is purely infrastructure work. The patch looks well-scoped and uncontroversial, assuming the error handling and transaction semantics are correct.
