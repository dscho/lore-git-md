This thread is about Justin Tobler’s patch series (v3) converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of the older `tmp_objdir` mechanism. The goal is to advance Git’s ODB-backend abstraction effort, led by Patrick Steinhardt, by replacing temporary object directories with the more flexible `odb_transaction` subsystem while preserving existing behavior.

The series is now complete with the final patch (11/11), which replaces the global `tmp_objdir` variable in `git-receive-pack` with a local `odb_transaction` object. The change is non-regressive: objects are still staged in an "incoming" directory and only migrated to the main object store after pre-receive hooks and connectivity checks pass, matching the current behavior. All prior patches (1–10/11) have been reviewed and address feedback from earlier versions, including mechanical cleanups, error-propagation improvements, and plumbing for environment variables. Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB subsystem owner, has signaled strong approval, calling the series "quite happy with the shape of this" in his v2 review.

Two deferred issues remain: a header dependency concern (whether `odb/transaction.h` and `odb/source.h` should be merged) and a latent transaction-leak in `odb_transaction_commit()`. Both are acknowledged and slated for follow-up work. The series is functionally complete and ready for integration, with no outstanding objections to its goals or approach. Justin has incorporated all feedback from v1 and v2, and the patches are queued for `next` pending integration.
