This thread is about Justin Tobler’s patch series (v3) converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, a key step in Patrick Steinhardt’s broader ODB abstraction effort. The series is functionally complete, with all 11 patches reviewed and approved by Patrick, who called it "quite happy with the shape of this series now." The final patch (11/11) replaces the global `tmp_objdir` with a local `odb_transaction` in `receive-pack`, preserving quarantine semantics while advancing backend-agnostic infrastructure.

Two minor issues remain open. Junio C Hamano raised a **safety concern** in patch 6/11 about undefined state in `odb_transaction_begin()` if the backend fails, and an **error-handling gap** in patch 8/11 where `odb_transaction_files_env()` ignores preparation failures. Both are surface-level fixes (conditional assignment and error propagation) and do not block integration. Additionally, Junio’s latest reply flags a **design question** in the final patch: whether to explicitly abort transactions on unpack failures for clarity, even though `atexit()` cleanup already handles it. This is a matter of style rather than correctness, and Justin will likely address it in a follow-up.

The series is ready for `next` once these discussions conclude. All prior feedback—including mechanical cleanups, error-propagation improvements, and redundant validation removal—has been incorporated. The only deferred work is follow-up cleanup (e.g., merging `odb/transaction.h` and `odb/source.h`, fixing the transaction-leak in `odb_transaction_commit()`, and refining error handling), none of which affects the current functionality. For a developer catching up, this is a well-scoped refactoring with no behavioral regressions, and the open questions are minor and uncontroversial.
