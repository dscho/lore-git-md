This thread is about Justin Tobler’s patch series (now at v3) converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of the older `tmp_objdir` mechanism. The goal is to advance Git’s object database (ODB) abstraction effort, allowing alternative backends to be plugged in without breaking existing functionality. The series has been well-received, with Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB subsystem owner, signaling strong approval after all feedback from earlier versions was addressed.

The latest email covers patch 9/11, which adds a flags mechanism to the ODB transaction interface to preserve `receive-pack`’s current behavior—like eager directory creation and the "incoming" prefix—during the transition. This is purely plumbing work; no user-facing changes are introduced yet. The patch updates 13 files, including the core transaction logic in `object-file.c` and `odb/transaction.h`. While Patrick previously raised a design concern about the tight coupling between headers due to the new enum, this was deferred to a follow-up series. All other patches in the series have been reviewed and integrated, with the final patch (11/11) completing the conversion of `receive-pack` to use ODB transactions.

The series is functionally complete and queued for Junio’s `next` branch pending integration. Two deferred items remain: the header coupling issue and a latent transaction-leak in `odb_transaction_commit()`. Both are acknowledged and planned for future work. No objections to the approach or goals have been raised.
