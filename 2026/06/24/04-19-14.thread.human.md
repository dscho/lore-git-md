This thread is the review and refinement of Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, a key step in Patrick Steinhardt’s broader ODB abstraction effort. The series is now in its final planning stages for a v2 resubmission after addressing all feedback. Justin and Patrick have worked through the design details, with Junio Hamano also weighing in early on naming and error-handling concerns before integrating the series via an evil merge.

The core change replaces temporary object directories with ODB transactions while preserving existing behavior—objects are still written via `git-index-pack` or `git-unpack-objects`, but the staging mechanism is now backend-agnostic. The v1 patches touched 46 files, introducing or modifying interfaces like `odb_transaction_begin()`, `odb_transaction_env()`, and the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag. The most significant behavioral change, in patch 6/6, clarifies that the transaction’s object directory is no longer added as an alternate (only propagated via environment variables), a non-regressive adjustment that doesn’t affect child processes.

All blocking feedback has been resolved for v2. Key adjustments include renaming functions for consistency (e.g., `flush_loose_object_transaction()` → `odb_transaction_files_flush()`), redesigning the `env` callback to use `struct strvec`, and splitting patch 6/6 into two parts for clarity. A file-local global variable in `builtin/receive-pack.c` was introduced as a compromise to avoid churn in transaction parameter passing. Open questions—like latent error-handling gaps and the single-transaction-per-ODB limitation—are acknowledged but deferred to follow-up work, including backend-agnostic object writes and an explicit `odb_transaction_abort()` interface. The series is queued for `next` pending v2, with no objections to its goals or approach.
