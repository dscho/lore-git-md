This thread is about Justin Tobler’s eleven-patch series that replaces `tmp_objdir` usage in `git-receive-pack` with the new `odb_transaction` subsystem, advancing Git’s object-database abstraction. The series is now in its fourth version and functionally complete; all prior feedback has been addressed, including the final conversion in the last patch.

The key participants are Justin (the author) and Patrick Steinhardt (the ODB subsystem owner). Patrick has reviewed all eleven patches and confirmed that the three targeted fixes since v3—improved commit messages, defensive programming in `odb_transaction_begin()`, and proper error propagation in `odb_transaction_env()`—are now satisfactory. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, previously raised safety and design questions (such as whether explicit `odb_transaction_abort()` calls should be added in error paths), but these have been resolved as deferred follow-up work, with the understanding that the series is otherwise ready for integration.

The series touches 26 files, primarily in the ODB abstraction layer, `git-receive-pack`, and related subsystems. It introduces new interfaces like `odb_transaction_begin()` (now error-returning) and `odb_transaction_commit()` (which propagates backend errors), while preserving existing quarantine semantics via the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag. No user-facing behavior changes are introduced; the series is a pure refactoring. The patches are queued for `next` and expected to graduate to `master` in the current cycle, pending integration testing. Deferred improvements—such as explicit abort handling, lazy directory creation, and merging the `odb/transaction.h` and `odb/source.h` headers—will be addressed in future work.
