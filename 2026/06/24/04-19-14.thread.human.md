This thread is about Justin Tobler’s patch series (v2) converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, advancing Git’s ODB-backend abstraction effort. The series is functionally complete, with 10 of 11 patches reviewed and integrated, and the final patch (11/11) converting `receive-pack` to the new interfaces. All feedback from v1 has been addressed, including mechanical cleanups, error propagation, and plumbing improvements.

Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB subsystem owner, has reviewed most of the series and raised two remaining open items: (1) a request for clarification on the motivation for eliminating `flush_loose_object_transaction()` in patch 3/11, and (2) a usability gap in patch 5/11 where failures in `tmp_objdir_create()` and `tmp_objdir_migrate()` are propagated but not logged, making debugging harder. The series is otherwise uncontroversial and queued for `next` pending resolution of these items.

The latest email covers Patrick’s review of patch 6/11, which refactors `odb_transaction_begin()` to return an error code and write the transaction into an out-parameter. This is a mechanical but important improvement, enabling callers to handle backend setup failures (e.g., temporary directory creation). Patrick calls the change "very much welcome" and notes it results in "much saner behaviour with less surprises." He flags a minor style nit but otherwise accepts the patch as-is, and his only design observation is that some call sites create and immediately commit transactions, which might be worth revisiting in the future. This patch is uncontroversial and does not affect the series’ current status.
