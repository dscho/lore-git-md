This thread covers Justin Tobler's patch series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, a key step in Patrick Steinhardt's broader ODB abstraction effort. The series has reached its second version (v2), with all substantive feedback from v1 now addressed—mechanical cleanups, error propagation improvements, and the final conversion of `receive-pack` to the new transaction interfaces.

Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB subsystem owner, has signaled strong approval for v2, calling it "quite happy with the shape of this series now" and noting only minor nits remain. Two design-level questions—a header dependency concern and a latent transaction-leak in `odb_transaction_commit()`—were acknowledged and deferred to future work. Justin Tobler confirmed he will post a v3 shortly to incorporate Patrick's Reviewed-by trailers and minor wording tweaks, but no further technical changes are expected. The series is functionally complete and ready for integration into Junio's `next` branch.
