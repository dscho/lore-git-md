This is the **third patch in the v2 series** optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. The patch adds **regression tests** to validate the side-exhaustion optimization under edge-case topologies where the optimization could misbehave.

Kristofer Karlsson (co-signed by Elijah Newren in patch 2/7) introduces two new test scripts:
- **`t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`**: A new test file that exercises the case where multiple merge-base candidates exist and one is an ancestor of another. The test verifies that `git merge-base --all` correctly excludes the redundant ancestor candidate, even when the side-exhaustion optimization exits early before `STALE` propagation removes it. The test topology is a diamond-shaped graph with a redundant merge-base candidate (B and C, where B reaches C through d1), ensuring the `remove_redundant` safety net catches the ancestor.
- **`t6600-test-reach.sh`**: Extends an existing test file with a scenario where one tip is outside the commit-graph (INFINITY generation) and the other is inside (finite generation). This exercises the region transition: the walk starts in the INFINITY region (where side-exhaustion is disabled) and crosses into the finite region (where it can fire), validating that the optimization respects the finite-generation guard.

The tests are **comprehensive and well-documented**, including ASCII diagrams and commit-date annotations to clarify expected behavior. They reuse edge-case topologies from Elijah Newren’s prior work (PR #2150) and cover both commit-graph and non-commit-graph scenarios. The new test file (`t6099`) is integrated into the build via `meson.build` and `Makefile` updates.

This patch is **pure test coverage** and does not modify production code. It precedes the optimization patch (7/7) in the series, demonstrating correctness with the old logic before introducing the new behavior. The tests are **deterministic** and use `test_all_modes` to validate behavior across all commit-reach modes (commit-graph, no commit-graph, and mixed). No regressions are expected, and the patch is **ready to land** as part of the series.
