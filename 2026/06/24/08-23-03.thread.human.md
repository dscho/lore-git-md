This thread is a security-hardening patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that systematically addresses vulnerabilities in Git’s reftable backend, discovered through fuzzing with libFuzzer. The series, now complete at its initial v1, introduces both fixes for out-of-bounds reads/writes, NULL pointer dereferences, and other memory-safety issues, as well as new fuzzing infrastructure to prevent regressions. The patches are split into two parts: infrastructure (Meson build support and a reftable fuzzer) and fixes (nine targeted patches, each with unit tests).

The series is self-contained and appears ready for review, with no open technical questions or objections raised so far. The fixes are internal-only, with no changes to public APIs or on-disk formats, and they ensure graceful failure when parsing corrupted reftable files rather than crashing. The fuzzing infrastructure, integrated into Git’s CI, is designed to be reusable for other subsystems, though no immediate follow-up work is tied to this series.

While constructive, the feedback does not engage with the security fix itself or its correctness. The thread remains uncontested, with Patrick as the sole participant so far.
