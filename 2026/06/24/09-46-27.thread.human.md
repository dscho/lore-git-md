While the risk is medium (requiring attacker-controlled input), the fix is high-priority for users of the reftable backend. The series has been validated by the reporter and reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt, a key contributor to the reftable effort, suggesting it is both well-motivated and thoroughly vetted.
