This is a bugfix patch targeting the GPG/SSH signature handling in `gpg-interface.c`. It corrects a long-standing over-aggressive CR-stripping behavior that was introduced in 2008 and later carried over into SSH signing support in 2021.

The patch addresses a NEEDSWORK comment left in the code: the original `remove_cr_after()` helper stripped *all* CR characters (`\r`) from GPG/SSH signature output, but only `\r\n` sequences (Windows line endings) should be normalized. The fix renames the helper to `strip_cr_before_lf()` and modifies its logic to skip a CR only when it is immediately followed by an LF (`\n`), preserving lone CR characters. The change affects both GPG and SSH signing code paths, with updated comments reflecting the corrected behavior.

The commit message traces the history of the issue across three prior commits (c4adea82, 2f47eae2, and 29b31577) and explains why the original "simpler" approach was safe at the time but no longer appropriate. The diff is confined to `gpg-interface.c`, touching only the helper function and its two call sites. No tests or documentation are included, but the change is narrowly scoped to a well-defined edge case in signature normalization.

The fix is unlikely to be controversial, though reviewers may ask for a test case to verify that lone CRs are preserved in signature output. The patch stands alone and is not part of a larger series.
