This is the second patch in Justin Tobler’s six-part series refactoring `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions. Junio C Hamano replies to the first patch, which renames `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to reflect its broader future use (packfiles as well as loose objects). Junio’s feedback is **surface-level but forward-looking**: he notes the rename makes sense but points out other functions (`fsync_loose_object_transaction`, `flush_loose_object_transaction`) still follow the old naming convention. He suggests either renaming them for consistency (not necessarily in this series) or adding a TODO comment to address it later. The comment is **non-blocking** and does not challenge the patch’s substance, but it flags a potential follow-up task for codebase hygiene. No action is required in v2 of this series, but the observation may inform future cleanup work.
