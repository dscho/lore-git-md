This is a brief follow-up in the post-merge discussion of the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series. Kristofer Karlsson, who co-signed one of the patches and was involved in the design, replies to Derrick Stolee’s question about the missing step-count data for the `git merge-tree (across import)` benchmark in the 2.6M-commit monorepo.

Kristofer acknowledges the omission was due to "laziness" — he ran the initial benchmarks before adding the trace2 instrumentation and didn’t update all of them. He plans to include the missing data in a future update (within a day or two) or possibly trim some benchmarks to avoid redundancy, noting that `merge-tree` results may not add much beyond what `merge-base` already shows. The tone is casual and cooperative, treating the question as a minor reporting gap rather than a technical concern. No code changes are proposed; this is purely about benchmark presentation.
