This is a brief reply in the ongoing discussion about Patrick Steinhardt’s RFC to reorganize Git’s source tree by moving `libgit.a` components into a `lib/` directory. Oswald Buddenhagen weighs in with a pragmatic suggestion and a personal endorsement.

Oswald argues that the reorganization should be merged *before* the upcoming release, not after. His reasoning is that the change affects only the build system, which CI already validates thoroughly, so the risk of introducing regressions late in the cycle is low. This would at least simplify backporting fixes to the next stable release (2.55), even if it doesn’t help with older versions. He acknowledges the pain of rebasing in-flight branches but defers to Patrick’s earlier response on that point.

Beyond the logistics, Oswald states he is “totally in favor of the change,” calling the current source tree “messy and confusing.” His support is unqualified but adds little new technical detail—it’s more of a vote of confidence than a review. The message is short and aligns with Patrick’s position, offering no counterarguments to Junio’s concerns about disruption. Readers should note this as one voice in favor, but the core debate remains unresolved.
