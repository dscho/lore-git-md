This is the second patch in Tian Yuchen’s v2 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds **regression tests** to `t6600-test-reach.sh` that exercise edge cases in the upcoming side-exhaustion optimization.

The patch is authored by Elijah Newren and co-signed by Kristofer Karlsson, both of whom have reviewed the series and endorsed the approach. It introduces four targeted test scenarios:

- **`in_merge_bases_many:self`** – a commit is both the query tip and one of the merge-base candidates.
- **`get_merge_bases_many:duplicate-twos`** – duplicate entries in the candidate list.
- **`get_merge_bases_many:pending-stale`** – a STALE transition on an already-painted commit in a diamond topology (`ps-*`).
- **`get_merge_bases_many:infinity-both-sides`** – both query tips live outside the commit-graph with non-monotonic dates (`pi-*`).

The tests are **comprehensive and well-motivated**, covering the exact edge cases the optimization is designed to handle. They run in all test modes (`--full-history`, `--ancestry-path`, etc.) via the existing `test_all_modes` helper, ensuring the optimization does not regress existing behavior. The commit message clearly explains each topology and why it matters for the side-exhaustion logic.

This patch is **uncontroversial and ready to land**. It precedes the actual optimization in the series, demonstrating that the current code already passes these edge cases—so the upcoming change (patch 7/7) will not introduce regressions. The test coverage is a strong signal that the series is well-tested and that the authors have thought carefully about correctness.
