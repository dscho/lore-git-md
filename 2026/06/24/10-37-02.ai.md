This is a refactoring and feature patch series (v2) targeting Git’s connectivity check subsystem, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal remains unchanged: generalize promisor object handling to remove hardcoded packfile assumptions, enabling connectivity checks to work with non-packfile ODB backends. The series now consists of four patches (up from three in v1), addressing the two substantive issues raised in reviews of v1—an unintentional removal of `odb_reprepare()` and overly complex control flow in `check_connected()`.

The call to `odb_reprepare()` is restored, ensuring the ODB’s view of packfiles is refreshed before iteration begins. The series still touches only `connected.c` and `odb/source-packed.c`, with a net increase of 51 lines (up from 47 in v1), reflecting the added refactoring.
