This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part series refactoring Git’s connectivity checks to support ODB backend independence. It removes a long-standing restriction in `odb_for_each_object()` that prevented callers from combining object name prefixes with iteration flags.

The patch enables `odb_for_each_object()` to accept flags even when an object prefix is specified, which was previously treated as a fatal error. The change is implemented by calling the helper `should_exclude_pack()` (introduced in Patch 1) to filter packs based on the caller’s flags, and by propagating pack open errors to the caller. The logic is applied consistently across both multi-pack-index (MIDX) and individual packfile iteration paths.

This is a small but necessary step toward making connectivity checks backend-agnostic. The commit message is concise and the diff is clean, touching only `odb/source-packed.c`. The change looks well-scoped and uncontroversial, though reviewers may want to confirm that all flag combinations are handled correctly and that error propagation doesn’t introduce new edge cases. The patch is unchanged from v1, where Junio’s review was brief and did not raise substantive concerns.
