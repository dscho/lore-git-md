This resolves the "onbranch" chicken-and-egg problem by avoiding early config reads that could trigger recursive ref-store initialization. Justin’s review identifies a **structural oddity**: the `log_all_ref_updates` field is placed outside the `reftable_write_options` struct in the new `reftable_be_write_options` wrapper, despite being a write-specific setting. The question is non-blocking but suggests a minor refactor (e.g., moving `log_all_ref_updates` into `reftable_write_options`) might improve clarity.

No other issues are raised; the review implicitly approves the lazy-loading approach and the patch’s core goal.
