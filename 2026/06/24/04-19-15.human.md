This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s six-part series refactoring `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It’s a pure rename: the function `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` in `object-file.c` becomes `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to reflect that it will soon handle both loose objects and packfiles in the “files” backend. The change is mechanical—three lines touched, three lines changed—and is clearly a preparatory step for the rest of the series. No behavior changes, no new interfaces, just a name that better matches the upcoming scope. If the series as a whole is worth following, this patch is a safe, uncontroversial start.
