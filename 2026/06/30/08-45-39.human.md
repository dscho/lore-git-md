This is the latest exchange in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. The series is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s broader ODB abstraction effort and is now queued for the `next` branch pending a v2 update.

Patrick’s review here is substantive but narrowly focused on patch 6/6, the final patch that applies the transaction machinery to `receive-pack`. He raises two design points: the necessity of the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag (and its associated temp-directory naming convention) and the inconsistency of passing the transaction sometimes as a parameter and sometimes via `the_repository`. Justin agrees with both observations and confirms the v2 will (1) keep the flag for now but add a `NEEDSWORK` comment to revisit whether the naming convention is truly needed, and (2) standardize on a single transaction-passing style—either a global variable local to `receive-pack.c` or explicit parameters throughout. No new technical objections are raised; this is a clean resolution of the last open feedback before the series graduates to `next`.
