This thread began with Jeff King (Peff) submitting a two-patch series to fix memory leaks in `git format-patch` and the test harness. The fixes are small, self-contained, and have been confirmed correct by Patrick Steinhardt. The `format-patch` leak, present since 2016, was only recently detectable due to improvements in LeakSanitizer (LSan) reporting, while the test harness fix resolves TAP parse errors caused by LSan output misdirection.

The series itself is effectively complete—both patches are ready to merge, with no further changes expected. The discussion has since shifted to broader leak-detection efforts and CI improvements. Peff proposed consolidating two CI jobs (`linux-reftables-leaks` and `linux-TEST-vars`) into one, and Patrick clarified that the non-leak `linux-reftable` job is redundant and should be dropped. The overhead of LSan (~3x slowdown) makes it impractical for all CI jobs, but the consolidation is seen as a reasonable compromise. Unrelated leaks (bloom filters, `git-history`) are being addressed separately, and Peff has draft patches for OpenSSL SHA-1/SHA-256 context leaks, though these are not part of this series. No open questions remain for the original patches; the thread is now focused on procedural refinements.
