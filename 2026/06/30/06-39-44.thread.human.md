This thread began with Jeff King (Peff) submitting a two-patch series to fix memory leaks in `git format-patch` and the test harness. The `format-patch` leak, present since 2016 but only recently detectable, was traced to an unfreed `rev_info` struct in `prepare_bases()`, while the test harness issue involved LeakSanitizer (LSan) output misdirected to stdout, breaking TAP parsing with `prove`. Both fixes are small, self-contained, and ready for merging.

The discussion expanded to CI infrastructure improvements for leak detection, where Peff and Patrick Steinhardt converged on a minimalist approach: consolidating the `linux-reftables-leaks` and `linux-TEST-vars` jobs into a single `linux-TEST-vars-leaks` job, dropping the redundant non-leak `linux-reftable` job, and renaming `linux-TEST-vars` to `linux-exotic-flags` for clarity. Junio Hamano has now endorsed this plan, agreeing that the current `clang/gcc` job matrix is unnecessary and that saved CI cycles could be better used elsewhere. The original patches remain uncontested, and the CI changes are effectively approved, pending implementation. Follow-up work on OpenSSL SHA-1/SHA-256 leaks and unrelated LSan failures in other test suites remains separate from this series.
