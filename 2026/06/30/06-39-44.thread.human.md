This thread is a small but targeted bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that fixes two memory leaks: one in `git format-patch` and another in the test harness. The `format-patch` leak, which has existed since 2016, was recently exposed by LeakSanitizer (LSan) after other leaks were cleaned up. The test harness fix addresses a long-standing annoyance where LSan output was misdirected to stdout, causing TAP parse errors with `prove`.

Peff’s patches are mechanical and low-risk, touching only `builtin/log.c` and `t/test-lib.sh`. The `format-patch` fix adds a single `release_revisions()` call to free a `rev_info` struct, while the test harness fix redirects LSan output to stderr. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the `format-patch` patch and confirmed its correctness, noting that the `rev_info` struct is always initialized before the exit path. He also tested the patch and found that unrelated LSan failures (mostly in `git-history` and bloom filters) persist, but these are not tied to this fix.

Patrick additionally proposed enabling LeakSanitizer for the `linux-TEST-vars` CI job to catch similar leaks earlier, though he acknowledged the ~3x slowdown makes it impractical for all jobs. The series is complete, with no open questions or follow-up work needed, and is ready for merging. No other reviews have been posted yet.
