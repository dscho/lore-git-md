This thread began as a two-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) fixing small memory leaks in `git format-patch` and the test harness, both of which are now effectively resolved and ready for merging. The discussion has since shifted to CI infrastructure improvements for leak detection. Peff proposed consolidating the `linux-reftables-leaks` and `linux-TEST-vars` jobs into a single `linux-TEST-vars-leaks` job and dropping the redundant non-leak `linux-reftable` job, a plan Patrick Steinhardt endorsed.

The two have since agreed on a minimalist approach to CI expansion. They concurred that compiler-specific jobs (clang/gcc) rarely produce different results and that a `macos-TEST-vars` job would likely be low-value, as most `TEST-vars` are feature-selection flags unlikely to reveal macOS-specific issues. The `linux-TEST-vars` job will be renamed to `linux-exotic-flags` for clarity, and the combined leak-detection job (`linux-TEST-vars-leaks`) will proceed. The original patches remain uncontroversial and are unaffected by these procedural changes. Follow-up work on OpenSSL SHA-1/SHA-256 leaks and unrelated LSan failures in other test suites continues separately.
