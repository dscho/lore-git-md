This thread fixes two small memory leaks in Git: one in `git format-patch` (a leak in the `--base` option that has existed since 2016) and another in the test harness that misdirected LeakSanitizer output, causing parsing errors with `prove`. The patches, authored by Jeff King (Peff), are straightforward and ready for merging, touching only `builtin/log.c` and `t/test-lib.sh`. The discussion has since expanded to broader CI improvements for leak detection, where Peff, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio Hamano have agreed to consolidate two existing CI jobs into one (`linux-TEST-vars-leaks`) and drop a redundant job, while keeping the changes minimal and avoiding expansion to macOS or compiler-specific jobs.

The series itself is uncontroversial and effectively approved, with Patrick confirming the technical correctness of the `format-patch` fix and Junio endorsing the CI streamlining. Peff has also noted follow-up work on related leaks (OpenSSL SHA-1/SHA-256 contexts and bloom filters), but these are separate from the current patches. The only loose end is the pending implementation of the agreed-upon CI changes. A minor typo in the cover letter was pointed out by Karthik Nayak, but this has no bearing on the series’ content or status.
