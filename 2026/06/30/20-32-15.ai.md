This is a usability discussion about `git blame -b` output formatting and its interaction with manual workflows involving `git rebase`. Laszlo Ersek clarifies his original report: the issue is not a bug in `git rebase`’s parser but a friction in his personal workflow. When using `git blame -b` to identify a commit for editing during an interactive rebase, the abbreviated commit hash in the blame output is one nibble longer than the hashes in the rebase TODO list (due to `core.abbrev` + 1). This mismatch forces him to manually truncate the hash when pasting it into his editor’s search box to locate the corresponding rebase line.

The thread now centers on whether `git blame -b` should stop reserving an extra nibble for the unused caret marker, even if the practical impact is limited to manual workflows.
