This is a feature proposal for `git clone`, adding `--only` and `--except` options to specify sparse-checkout paths upfront. Jeff King (Peff) replies to the RFC with a substantive but cautious review.

Peff admits he has little hands-on experience with sparse-checkout, so his feedback is more exploratory than authoritative. He suggests an alternative design: allowing users to provide the sparse-checkout patterns file directly at clone time, rather than inventing new CLI options. This would reuse the existing file-based interface rather than introducing new flags.

He also raises a performance angle: whether knowing the desired paths at clone time could enable a more efficient partial clone, but notes that most servers disable path-based filters due to cost, making this unlikely in practice. The current strategy is to clone without blobs and lazy-load them during checkout, which the proposed feature would not change.

Peff recommends cc’ing Derrick Stolee and other sparse-checkout contributors for deeper technical feedback. His reply is constructive but stops short of endorsing the feature, framing it as a question of ergonomics rather than a clear win. The thread remains open for further input.
