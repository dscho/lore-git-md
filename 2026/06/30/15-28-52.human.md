This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s 11-patch series that overhauls how the sequencer handles dropped commits during rebase. The series as a whole is a **bugfix and refactoring effort** targeting the rebase machinery, specifically preventing notes from being incorrectly copied from dropped commits to the current HEAD.

This patch is a **pure refactoring** with no behavior change. It moves the `is_final_fixup()` helper function earlier in `sequencer.c` to prepare for a new caller that will be introduced in a later patch. The function determines whether the current fixup command in the todo list is the last one (i.e., no further non-fixup commands follow it). The move is mechanical: the function’s implementation is unchanged, and the diff shows only a relocation of the same 15 lines of code.

The patch is small, focused, and clearly motivated by the needs of the series. It is part of the preparatory work that simplifies the control flow in `pick_one_commit()` and `do_pick_commit()`, making the eventual behavioral change (skipping note copying for dropped commits) easier to implement and review. The commit message is concise and accurate, stating the purpose of the move without overpromising.
