This thread is about a small but useful cleanup in Git’s test suite. Gustavo S. Correa and the patch’s author have updated one of the submodule test scripts (`t/t7412-submodule-absorbgitdirs.sh`) to replace raw shell-test invocations (`test -f`, `test -d`, `! test -e`) with the more descriptive helper functions (`test_path_is_file`, `test_path_is_dir`, `test_path_is_missing`). The change is purely mechanical—no logic or behavior is altered—and is part of the broader community effort to modernize Git’s tests for better debugging and maintainability. The patch is uncontroversial and ready to be picked up.
