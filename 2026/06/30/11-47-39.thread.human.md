Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series to refactor Git’s repository discovery and setup phases continues to draw focused review. The latest feedback comes from Justin Tobler, who has given a substantive thumbs-up to patch 7/13, which moves the repository prefix out of the global `startup_info` struct and into `struct repository`. Tobler’s review confirms that the prefix is already populated in `startup_info` by the time the patch copies it into the repository object, making the change both safe and conceptually sound. He also checked how the prefix is used downstream—specifically in `git rev-parse`—and found the logic consistent.

The series remains under active review, with no merge decisions yet. Junio C Hamano has queued patch 2/13 after a minor commit-message fix, and Tobler has now provided substantive feedback on four of the first five patches (1, 2, 3, and 5) as well as this latest one. His reviews have surfaced several open questions—most notably whether patch 2/13’s silent suppression of misconfiguration warnings is intentional, and whether the shallow-file precedence rules in patch 3/13 are clearly defined—but none have been show-stoppers. Patrick has yet to respond to the earlier feedback, so those questions remain unresolved. The series is still in its initial version, and no code changes have been posted in response to reviews. If the pattern holds, expect a v2 once Patrick addresses the loose ends Tobler has flagged.
