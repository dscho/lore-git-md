This is the **final patch (11/11)** in Phillip Wood’s systemic overhaul of how the sequencer handles dropped commits during rebase. It introduces the core behavioral change: commits dropped due to empty changes, fixup sequences, or other reasons are no longer recorded as rewritten, preventing notes from being incorrectly copied to the current HEAD and ensuring post-rewrite hooks receive accurate information.

The patch adds a new `PICK_RESULT_DROPPED` enum member to `enum pick_result` and modifies `pick_one_commit()` to skip `record_in_rewritten()` for dropped commits. A key edge case is handled: if the dropped commit is the final one in a chain of fixups, the list of rewritten commits is flushed to avoid leaving stale state. The patch also adjusts the behavior of the "edit" command so that `rebase --continue` does not amend the previous pick if the commit was dropped, though a known limitation remains (the commit is still erroneously recorded as rewritten when the rebase continues, which the commit message flags as needing a separate fix).

Test coverage is expanded in two files. In `t3400-rebase.sh`, a new test verifies that notes from dropped commits are not copied to HEAD. In `t5407-post-rewrite-hook.sh`, a test ensures the post-rewrite hook receives the correct mapping of original to rewritten commits when commits are dropped during a rebase with fixups and empty changes.

The patch is well-motivated and directly addresses the original bug reported by Uwe Kleine-König while also fixing the broader systemic issues Phillip identified in his review. The commit message clearly documents the behavioral changes, edge cases, and known limitations, satisfying Junio’s earlier concerns about documentation. The test additions are thorough and cover both the immediate bug and the fixup-commit edge case. This is the culmination of a **substantive, tested-level series** that refactors the sequencer’s control flow to support the core fix while addressing additional rebase bugs (external merge strategy failures, command execution failures, and final fixup cleanup) in earlier patches. The series is ready for final review and merging.
