This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing memory-leak fix thread for the `git history reword` command. Jeff King (Peff) reports that enabling `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH=1` and running the full test suite with `SANITIZE=leak` exposes a few additional failures in the `t345x` test scripts, all of which appear to stem from the same commit-buffer leak already addressed in the merged patch. However, he also spots a distinct leak in `t4014` (diff formatting), unrelated to the current fix—this one appears to originate in `init_topo_walk()`, suggesting a separate slab leak in the topological sort machinery. The observation is diagnostic, not a call for immediate action, but it flags a potential follow-up issue for the diff subsystem. No new patches or objections are raised here.
