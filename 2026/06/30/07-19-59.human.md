His example highlights a real workflow friction: when rebasing a series of commits, finding the pre-rebase state in the reflog is cumbersome.

This isn’t a direct response to the `squash` feature’s design, but it **expands the thread’s scope** to a long-standing usability gap in Git. Harald’s suggestion—prioritizing destructive actions in the reflog and grouping related steps—aligns with the `git history` suite’s goal of making complex operations more intuitive.
