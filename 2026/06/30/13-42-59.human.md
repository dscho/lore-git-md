Toon Claes responds to Johannes Schindelin’s critique of the v5 implementation, agreeing that the `replayed_base` mechanism is necessary to prevent silent commit dropping in single-branch scenarios. He acknowledges that `--linearize` should **always flatten all replayed commits into a single linear sequence**, even when multiple branches are involved, and accepts that this behavior must be clearly documented.

The email shifts the discussion from "how to fix the regression" to "how to communicate the design intent." Toon argues that `--linearize` is the right name for this behavior (rather than something like `--replay-merges=linearize`), as it describes the outcome (a linear history) rather than the mechanism. He also notes that users can still replay branches separately if they want to preserve independence, though this comes with trade-offs like commit duplication.

This reply **solidifies the technical direction**—restoring `replayed_base` or an equivalent mechanism—but leaves the **CLI naming debate** unresolved. The tone is pragmatic, prioritizing predictable behavior over edge-case flexibility.
