This is **patch 8/11 in Phillip Wood’s systemic rebase-dropped-commit overhaul**, a **refactoring** that simplifies control flow in `sequencer.c`’s `pick_one_commit()` function. The patch is part of the preparatory work for the core behavioral change (introducing `PICK_DROPPED` in patch 11) and does not alter Git’s behavior.

The change consolidates the function’s logic by returning early if the operation is not an interactive rebase (`!is_rebase_i(opts)`), eliminating repeated calls to `is_rebase_i()` in the rest of the function. It also chains mutually exclusive conditional blocks with `else if`, making the control flow clearer. The conditions for "edit" and "fixup" commands are preserved but now appear later in the chain, as they inherently imply an interactive rebase.

This is a **pure refactoring**—no new edge cases are introduced or fixed here. The patch is small (8 insertions, 7 deletions) and focused, addressing a minor technical debt issue identified during the broader audit of the rebase machinery. The simplification will make the subsequent introduction of the `PICK_DROPPED` enum member (patch 11) cleaner. No test changes are needed, as the behavior remains identical.
