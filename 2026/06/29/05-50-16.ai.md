This is Junio’s reply to the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series (v6 4/4), which implements the `--reedit-message` flag. The patch gathers all folded-in commit messages (oldest first) into an editor template, mirroring `git rebase -i`’s squash behavior.

Junio notes a minor deviation from `git rebase -i`: the new implementation does not intersperse markers like `# This is the 1st commit message` between the folded-in messages. While this difference is unlikely to affect usability, it means the feature does not *exactly* replicate the interactive rebase experience. The observation is factual and does not criticize the design—it simply clarifies the behavior for future readers. No action is requested or implied; the series remains complete and ready for final assessment.
