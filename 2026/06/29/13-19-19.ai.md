It fixes a regression introduced in 93e5b1680e (2025-04-10) where `git merge-base` (without `--all`) can return the wrong merge base when all three of these conditions hold: the repository uses a v1 commit graph (no corrected commit dates), the topology has clock skew, and the correct merge base has a lower committer date than one of its ancestors. The fix gates the early-exit optimization on whether the queue is generation-ordered, and the series includes a new test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` that constructs a clock-skew topology to demonstrate the bug.

The commit message is unusually clear about the trigger conditions and the scope of the problem, which makes it easy to assess risk: only repositories that have not regenerated their commit graph since 2021 and that exhibit clock skew are affected.
