The issue appears to be specific to the interaction between promisor remotes and zero-object pushes, which suggests it could affect users of partial clone workflows. The reproduction script will make it easy for reviewers to verify the problem and test fixes.

No prior discussion or patch is mentioned, so this appears to be the first report of the issue. If the hang is confirmed, it’s likely to attract attention from maintainers of the promisor remote and partial clone subsystems.
