This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to track object sources. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, and this patch threads an optional `struct odb_source_packed *source` parameter through `packed_object_info()` and related functions.

Justin Tobler’s review engages directly with the patch’s design. He confirms the mechanical correctness of the changes (adding the parameter and updating callers) but raises a thoughtful question about layering: whether `struct packed_git` should record the `struct odb_source_packed` instead of passing it through `packed_object_info()`. This is a legitimate architectural concern—it suggests an alternative design where the source is tracked at the packfile level rather than the object-info level. Justin also notes that the motivation for limiting source population to the packed store (while other callers pass `NULL`) is not yet clear, though he expects this will become evident in later patches.

The review is concise but substantive, focusing on the patch’s implications for the broader ODB refactoring. It does not test the code or identify bugs, but it does surface a design question that could influence how the rest of the series lands. If you’re following the ODB abstraction work, this is a useful early signal about potential discussion points. The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-structured, but Justin’s feedback hints at deeper considerations that may emerge as the series progresses.
