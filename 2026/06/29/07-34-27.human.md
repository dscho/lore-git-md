This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch `git history drop` series, now at v7. It refactors a small piece of index-handling code to prepare for the main feature.

The patch splits `repo_read_index_unmerged()` into two functions: the original remains as a thin wrapper, while the new `index_state_unmerged_to_stage0()` takes an arbitrary `struct index_state *` and drops any unmerged entries to stage 0. This is a pure refactoring with no behavior change; the motivation is to allow later patches in the series to reuse the logic on non-repository indexes. A minor signedness fix (changing the loop counter from `int` to `unsigned int`) is included as a drive-by.

The change is mechanical and well-contained: it touches only `read-cache.c` and `read-cache-ll.h`, adds a single function, and has no test coverage of its own because it doesn’t alter observable behavior. Given the series’ overall goal—adding the `git history drop` command—this patch is clearly a preparatory step rather than a standalone improvement. It looks clean and uncontroversial.
