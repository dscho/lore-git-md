This is a brief follow-up in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

In this email, Justin replies to Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the first patch, which renamed `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to reflect its expanded future use (handling packfiles as well as loose objects). Junio noted that other functions—`fsync_loose_object_transaction()` and `flush_loose_object_transaction()`—still follow the old naming convention and suggested either renaming them for consistency or adding a TODO comment. Justin agrees and plans to add a follow-up patch in the next version (v2) to rename those functions to `odb_transaction_files_fsync()` and `odb_transaction_files_flush()`, respectively.

The change is minor and non-blocking; it’s a code hygiene improvement rather than a functional one. The series as a whole has already received substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt, who identified several blocking issues (e.g., error-handling gaps, behavioral changes in alternate handling) that will require a v2. This naming tweak is a small part of that larger cleanup.
