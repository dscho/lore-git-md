This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to prepare Git’s object database (ODB) for pluggable backends. The patch makes backend-specific provenance information (like which packfile an object came from) opt-in rather than unconditionally populated. Callers that need this information now allocate a `struct object_info_source` on the stack and point `sourcep` at it; those that don’t simply leave `sourcep` as `NULL`. The change is mechanical and does not alter behavior, but it’s a necessary step toward uniquely identifying objects when they appear in multiple sources (e.g., different packfiles).

Justin Tobler’s review is **substantive but light-touch**. He confirms the opt-in design makes sense and walks through the key changes (e.g., converting `oi->u.packed` accesses to `oi->sourcep->u.packed`). His only mild critique is that the name `sourcep` might be slightly confusing—it sounds like it should point to a `struct odb_source`, but in this patch it points to the new `struct object_info_source`. The confusion is minor and doesn’t block progress; Justin explicitly says the patch “looks good.”

The patch touches seven files (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `packfile.c`, and `reachable.c`) and is well-explained in the commit message. If you’re tracking Patrick’s ODB abstraction effort, this is a foundational piece—clean, uncontroversial, and ready for further review. The series as a whole has Junio’s conceptual approval, so this patch is likely to land once the remaining feedback is addressed.
