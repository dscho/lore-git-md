This thread is a two-patch bugfix series addressing a regression in `git merge-base` (without `--all`) introduced in April 2025. The issue surfaces under specific conditions: a v1 commit-graph (lacking corrected commit dates), clock skew in the commit topology, and a merge base with a lower committer date than one of its ancestors. The author has identified the root cause—a premature optimization in `paint_down_to_common()`—and proposed a targeted fix that gates the optimization on whether the queue is generation-ordered.

The series is straightforward: the first patch adds a regression test constructing a clock-skew topology, while the second implements the fix by introducing a `gen_ordered` flag to track queue ordering. The changes are minimal (ten lines in `commit-reach.c`) and leave on-disk formats untouched. No review feedback or objections have been raised yet, and the fix appears ready for integration. The thread is self-contained, with no dependencies or related in-flight topics, and the commit message clearly outlines the trigger conditions, making it easy to assess the scope and risk.
