This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series that addresses a regression in `git merge-base` introduced in April 2025. The bug occurs under specific conditions: a repository using a v1 commit graph (without corrected commit dates), clock skew in the commit topology, and a correct merge base with a lower committer date than one of its ancestors. The author has clearly outlined the trigger conditions, making it easy to assess the risk—only older repositories with clock skew are affected.

The fix itself is minimal (ten lines in `commit-reach.c`) and introduces a new test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` to verify the correction. The test constructs a clock-skew topology to reproduce the issue, ensuring the bug won’t resurface. So far, there’s been no review feedback or discussion, so the series appears uncontroversial and ready for merging. If you’ve been away from the list, this is a straightforward bugfix with a well-scoped impact.
