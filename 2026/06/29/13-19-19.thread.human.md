This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series that resolves a clock-skew regression in `git merge-base` (without `--all`), introduced in April 2025 by commit 93e5b1680e. The regression occurs when a repository uses a v1 commit-graph (no corrected commit dates), the commit topology has clock skew, and the correct merge base has a lower committer date than one of its ancestors.

The fix, authored by [name redacted], adds a `gen_ordered` flag to `paint_down_to_common()` in `commit-reach.c` to conditionally gate an early-exit optimization. When the queue falls back to commit-date ordering (for v1 commit graphs), the flag is cleared, preventing incorrect early exits. The change is minimal—just ten lines in `commit-reach.c`—and includes a regression test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` that constructs a clock-skew topology to verify the fix.
