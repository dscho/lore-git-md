This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to use a `source` field, part of the ODB abstraction effort. Justin Tobler engages directly with the patch’s design choices and future implications.

Justin’s review focuses on the patch’s preparatory nature—adding an optional `struct odb_source_packed *source` parameter to `packed_object_info()` and `packed_object_info_with_index_pos()`—and questions whether tracking the source at the `packed_git` level might be a cleaner alternative. He notes that the current approach (passing `NULL` for all callers outside `odb/source-packed.c`) is intentional but leaves open whether this is the best layer for bookkeeping. The review also observes that the patch itself is mechanically correct but does not yet use the new parameter, deferring that work to later commits.

The feedback is constructive and forward-looking, probing the architectural rationale without objecting to the current implementation. It signals that the series’ design is still open to discussion, particularly around where source-tracking logic should live. If you’re following the ODB abstraction effort, this review is worth noting as an early technical engagement with the series’ direction. No alternative implementation is proposed, so the discussion remains conceptual for now.
