The Git mailing list has received an announcement about Git Merge 2026, the annual conference dedicated to Git development and usage. The Call for Proposals (CFP) deadline has been extended by two weeks, moving from June 30 to July 14, 2026, at 11:59 PM (UTC-7). This adjustment is meant to give potential speakers—especially those who have been away—more time to submit their talk proposals. If you're considering presenting at the conference or have questions about the submission process, Taylor Blau is the point of contact. No technical discussions or patches are part of this thread; it’s purely about conference logistics.
