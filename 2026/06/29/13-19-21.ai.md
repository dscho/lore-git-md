This is the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series targeting the commit-reach subsystem. It implements the core fix for a regression in `git merge-base` (without `--all`) introduced by commit 93e5b1680e (April 2025). The regression occurs when all three conditions are met: the repository uses a v1 commit-graph (no corrected commit dates), the commit topology has clock skew, and the correct merge base has a lower committer date than one of its ancestors.

The patch adds a `gen_ordered` flag that tracks whether the priority queue is generation-ordered. When the fallback to commit-date ordering occurs (for v1 commit graphs), the flag is cleared, and the early-exit optimization in `paint_down_to_common()` is now gated on this flag. This ensures the optimization only fires when the queue is generation-ordered, preventing incorrect early exits when clock skew is present. The change is minimal: ten lines modified in `commit-reach.c`, plus a one-line update to flip the test from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success`.

The fix is well-scoped and directly addresses the regression without altering on-disk formats or introducing new symbols. The accompanying test (added in patch 1) constructs a clock-skew topology to verify the fix, and the commit message clearly explains the trigger conditions, making it easy to assess the risk and affected scenarios. No review feedback or objections have been raised yet, and the series appears ready for integration.
