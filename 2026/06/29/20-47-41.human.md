This is Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, replying to Justin Tobler’s review of Patrick Steinhardt’s **third patch in the six-part ODB refactoring series**. The patch adds a `source` field to `struct object_info_source`, allowing backends to record which ODB source (loose, packed, or in-memory) an object came from.

Junio echoes Justin’s earlier architectural question from patch 1/6: if the `source` parameter is often `NULL`, why make it an explicit parameter at all? He suggests the ODB source could instead be recorded when `struct packed_git` is initialized, rather than being passed opt-in to `packed_object_info_with_index_pos()`. This would centralize source tracking and avoid the need for callers to provide it. Junio also flags a naming confusion in the code: the patch assigns `&source->base` to `oi->sourcep->source`, which makes it sound like `sourcep` should point to a struct with a `.base` member, not `.source`.

The tone is curious rather than critical—Junio is trying to understand the design rationale, not rejecting the patch. His question reinforces Justin’s earlier feedback and suggests the opt-in approach may need revisiting before the series lands. The patch itself is still conceptually approved ("Great" in the cover letter), but this exchange signals that the architectural decision around source tracking is not yet settled. Readers following the ODB abstraction effort should watch for whether future iterations adopt a more centralized source-tracking mechanism.
