This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch v7 series implementing the `git history drop` subcommand. It performs a straightforward but important refactoring: renaming the `reset_head()` function to `reset_working_tree()` to better reflect its evolving purpose. The rename is motivated by an upcoming change (in a later patch) that will make updating the HEAD reference optional, decoupling it from the core working-tree reset logic.

The patch touches four files—`builtin/rebase.c`, `reset.c`, `reset.h`, and `sequencer.c`—and updates all call sites to use the new function name and its corresponding `reset_working_tree_options` struct. No behavioral changes are introduced yet; the flags and logic remain identical. This is purely a preparatory step, suggested by Phillip Wood, to make the subsequent functional changes clearer and more intuitive.

The commit message is clear and concise, explaining both the *why* (the function’s role is expanding beyond HEAD updates) and the *what* (a mechanical rename). The patch itself is well-scoped and uncontroversial, addressing a naming inconsistency before it becomes entrenched in new code. Given the thorough review history of the series and the resolution of prior feedback, this refactoring looks like a sensible, low-risk step toward modernizing the reset API.
