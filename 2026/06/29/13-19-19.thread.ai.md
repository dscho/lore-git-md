Core problem or goal
Fix a regression in `git merge-base` (without `--all`) introduced by 93e5b1680e (2025-04-10). The bug surfaces when all three conditions hold: (1) the repository uses a v1 commit-graph (no corrected commit dates), (2) the commit topology has clock skew, and (3) the correct merge base has a lower committer date than one of its ancestors. The series gates the early-exit optimization on whether the queue is generation-ordered and adds a regression test in `t6600-test-reach.sh`.
