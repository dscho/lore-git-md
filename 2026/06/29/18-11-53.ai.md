This is a brief follow-up in the ODB transaction refactoring series. Justin Tobler, the author, acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s observation about naming consistency: the patch renames `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_prepare()`, but two related functions (`fsync_loose_object_transaction()` and `flush_loose_object_transaction()`) still follow the old naming pattern. Justin agrees they should also be renamed for consistency and plans to add a new patch in v2 of the series to address this. The change is purely mechanical and does not affect functionality; it is a codebase hygiene follow-up to the broader effort of making the ODB transaction layer backend-agnostic. No controversy or technical debate here—just a small, agreed-upon cleanup.
