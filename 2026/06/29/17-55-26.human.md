This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part series refactoring `struct object_info` to replace the coarse `whence` field with a more detailed `source` field. The patch mechanically converts all remaining callers of `oi->whence` to use the new `oi->sourcep->source->type` instead, completing the transition begun in earlier patches.

Justin Tobler’s review is brief but substantive: he confirms the patch’s scope (adapting callers only) and notes that the cleanup is straightforward because earlier patches already set up the `sourcep` pointer. His "Looking good" signals approval of the mechanical conversion, though he expects further cleanup in a later patch. This aligns with the series’ incremental approach and suggests no major concerns about this step.

The patch touches four files (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, and `reachable.c`) and is a pure refactoring with no behavior change. It’s a key milestone in the ODB abstraction effort, enabling future multi-source object resolution.
