This is the latest update in Pablo Sabater’s ongoing series to implement cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`. The series is now at a critical juncture following Junio Hamano’s architectural decision to adopt Kristofer Karlsson’s lookahead buffer redesign, replacing the v6 peek-based abstraction.

Pablo confirms he is working on a v7 reroll that will replace the two peek functions (`revision_peek_next_commit()` and `revision_has_commits_after()`) with a two-slot lookahead buffer inside `struct git_graph`. The buffer will be populated by `get_revision_internal()`, ensuring commits are fully simplified before inspection, which resolves the three failing test cases from v6 and eliminates the fragility of peeking at unsimplified commits. The redesign also removes the need for defensive checks and side-effect management that were complicating the v6 approach.

The lookahead buffer approach has already been prototyped by Kristofer and shown to work, though Pablo notes he is still finalizing the implementation. The key technical changes will include a new `lookahead[2]` array in `struct git_graph`, a small adjustment to `graph_is_interesting()` to treat lookahead commits as "interesting" regardless of their `SHOWN` flag, and timestamp tweaks to ensure deterministic output. The visualization logic (patch 3/3) will be updated to consume commits from the lookahead buffer rather than peeking at `revs->commits`.

This reroll is now the immediate next step for the series, and it is expected to address the core architectural concerns raised by Junio and Jeff King. The series is on track for inclusion once the v7 patches are submitted and reviewed, though final sign-off from Junio and Phillip Wood on the visualization logic is still pending. The lookahead buffer approach appears to be a cleaner and more robust solution, but the series will need to reconcile with Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic and document the visualization rules more clearly in a future update.
