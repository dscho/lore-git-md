This is an announcement about the Git Merge 2026 conference.

The call for proposals (CFP) deadline for Git Merge 2026 has been extended from June 30 to July 14, 2026 (11:59 PM UTC-7). The extension is intended to give more time to potential speakers who have indicated interest but haven’t yet submitted their proposals, particularly those who are currently out of office. Taylor Blau, the sender, encourages questions about the submission process or the conference in general and invites people to reach out either on- or off-list. No technical changes or patches are involved—this is purely a logistical update for the event.
