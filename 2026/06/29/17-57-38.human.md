This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to replace the `whence` field with a more flexible `source` field. The series is part of the larger ODB abstraction effort, which aims to make Git’s object storage layer pluggable and support multi-source object resolution.

This patch is a straightforward cleanup: it removes the now-unused `whence` field from `struct object_info` and deletes all its assignments across the ODB backends. The change is mechanical and follows the conversion of all callers to use the new `sourcep` field in the previous patch. There are no behavior changes—this is purely dead code removal.

Justin Tobler’s review is brief but approving: he calls the patch "Nice" and confirms it "looks good." This aligns with his earlier substantive feedback on the series, where he engaged with the architectural rationale for the `source` field design. The patch should be uncontroversial and ready to merge once the rest of the series is approved.

The series as a whole has received conceptual approval from Junio C Hamano, signaling it is on track for inclusion. If you are following the ODB abstraction work, this patch is a minor but necessary step in the progression. For most readers, it is safe to skip unless you are specifically reviewing the series or interested in the low-level ODB internals.
