This is the final polish discussion for the `git history squash` feature, now in its sixth iteration. Junio C Hamano points out a small but visible inconsistency: the `--reedit-message` template omits the numbered markers (`# This is the 1st commit message`) that `git rebase -i` includes when combining commit messages. Harald Nordgren agrees to extract that logic from `rebase -i` to make the two commands behave identically in the editor.

The exchange is purely cosmetic—no functionality changes, no design questions left open. The series itself is complete, well-tested, and ready for Junio’s final assessment; this is a follow-up opportunity rather than a blocker. If you’ve been waiting for the feature to stabilize, now is the time to look at the v6 patches.
