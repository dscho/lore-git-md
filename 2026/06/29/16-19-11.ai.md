This is a **CI/build system** policy discussion in the ongoing thread about macOS CI hangs in `t5551` and `t5559`. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to align GitLab CI coverage with GitHub’s by clarifying his own position and raising a practical concern about visibility.

Junio **rejects the idea that GitHub CI is the "canonical source of truth"** for the project, calling it merely a "very handy service" that occasionally hiccups. Instead, he suggests GitLab CI could be a **superset** of GitHub’s coverage—running all the same tests *plus* additional ones—rather than a strict mirror. This would satisfy his earlier question about whether GitLab should focus on events not covered by GitHub.

The new question Junio raises is **how to make GitLab CI results more visible** to maintainers. He notes that while he knows where to find GitHub CI results for his integration branch pushes (and provides a TinyURL shortcut), he does not know the equivalent location for GitLab. This lack of visibility, he implies, is a barrier to treating GitLab CI as a first-class part of the project’s workflow. No technical changes to the patch series are proposed; the discussion remains focused on policy and infrastructure. The series’ third patch (enabling `GIT_TEST_LONG` for GitLab CI) is still on hold pending resolution of this broader question.
