This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch `git history drop` series (v7). It performs a pure refactoring step: renaming the `reset_head()` function to `reset_working_tree()` and updating the corresponding struct and call sites. The change anticipates a future commit where the function will optionally skip updating HEAD, making the old name misleading. Phillip Wood suggested the new name, and the patch touches `builtin/rebase.c`, `reset.c`, `reset.h`, and `sequencer.c`—all places where the reset machinery is invoked. No behavior changes are introduced yet; the flags and logic remain identical. This is a straightforward, well-motivated preparatory step that keeps the series bisectable and makes the eventual HEAD-update-optional change clearer.
