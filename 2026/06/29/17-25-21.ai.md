This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to support pluggable ODB backends. Justin Tobler engages directly with the patch’s core design choice: making backend-specific source information opt-in via a new `struct object_info_source` and a `sourcep` pointer in `struct object_info`. The review is constructive and focused on the patch’s implementation, noting the clarity of the opt-in mechanism and the mechanical correctness of the changes across the seven touched files (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `packfile.c`, and `reachable.c`).

Justin’s feedback is positive but includes a minor naming observation: the `sourcep` field name initially confused him, as it suggests a pointer to `struct odb_source` (which will be introduced later in the series) rather than the new `struct object_info_source`. This is a surface-level critique and does not challenge the patch’s direction. He explicitly approves the patch’s approach ("this patch looks good"), signaling that the opt-in design aligns with his expectations for the ODB abstraction effort. The review does not raise technical concerns or propose alternative implementations, but it does reinforce the patch’s role as a foundational step toward multi-source object resolution. For readers tracking Patrick’s ODB work, this patch is a key piece of the puzzle, and Justin’s review confirms its readiness for further scrutiny.
