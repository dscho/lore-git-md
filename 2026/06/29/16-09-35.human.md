This is a **CI/build system** discussion about whether GitLab CI should enable `GIT_TEST_LONG` for the same events as GitHub CI. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio C Hamano’s strategic question by advocating for alignment rather than specialization.

Patrick argues that GitHub CI is the project’s "canonical source of truth," so GitLab should mirror its coverage to catch the same issues. Splitting test coverage between platforms would likely lead to redundant merge requests, wasting resources. His position is pragmatic: consistency across CI platforms is more valuable than trying to carve out unique test scenarios for GitLab. The patch to enable `GIT_TEST_LONG` on GitLab remains on hold pending resolution of this policy question.
