This is the v7 iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series implementing the `git history drop` subcommand. The series is now complete: it exposes `replay_result_queue_update()` as a shared helper (patch 9), eliminating the code-duplication concern raised by Christian Couder, and adds a missing Signed-off-by line. All other feedback from v6 has been addressed—dry-run/UPDATE_HEAD interactions, ORIG_HEAD/HEAD edge-case protection, and reset-API modernization.

The series touches `builtin/history.c`, `reset.c`, `replay.c`, and `read-cache.c`, introducing a new `index_state_unmerged_to_stage0()` helper, a `reset_working_tree_flags` enum with explicit `UPDATE_HEAD` control, and a two-phase ref-update design that separates analysis from execution. Tests cover 537 lines of edge cases, including bare repositories, dry-run mode, and conflict detection. The `the_repository` global is fully removed from `reset.c`, and the reset machinery now handles dirty indices correctly.

This version is ready for final review; the only remaining loose end is the eventual modernization of `is_bare_repository()` to take an explicit `struct repository *`, which is low-priority and not blocking. Given the thorough test coverage, clear commit messages, and resolution of the last substantive review comment, this series looks like a solid addition to the experimental `git history` command.
