This is the final approval for the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series. Derrick Stolee confirms that the newly identified regression—where the `!FIND_ALL` early-exit optimization fails in repositories with v1 commit-graph data due to a fallback to commit-date ordering—can be fixed by **removing the fallback entirely**. His approval is based on Kristofer Karlsson’s benchmarks, which show that side-exhaustion eliminates the original performance concern (a 188ms → 17ms improvement in the Linux kernel `git merge-base --all v4.8 v4.9` case) and that the fallback is now redundant.

Stolee’s only suggestion is a **comment** when initializing the queue order to document the requirement for topological ordering, but this is a minor documentation point, not a blocker. The fix will be incorporated into a follow-up patch (or a v5 reroll) before Git 2.55 final, alongside the mechanical squash of patches 5/8 and 6/8 to resolve `-Wunused-function` warnings. With this approval, the series is now **technically complete and ready to merge** pending those administrative updates. The thread’s focus shifts to finalizing the squash and regression fix.
