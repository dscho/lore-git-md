This is the first patch in a two-patch bugfix series targeting the commit-reach subsystem. It adds a regression test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` that demonstrates a clock-skew bug in `git merge-base` (without `--all`). The test constructs a commit topology where the correct merge base (`M2`) has a lower committer date than its ancestor (`M1`) due to clock skew, causing the early-exit optimization in `paint_down_to_common()` to fire prematurely and return the wrong merge base when using a v1 commit-graph (no corrected commit dates).

The test is marked `test_expect_failure` to document the bug; the fix itself will land in the next patch. The test is thorough (41 lines) and clearly explains the trigger conditions: v1 commit-graph, clock skew, and a topology where the correct merge base has a lower date than its ancestor. The commit message and test comments make the scope and risk of the bug easy to assess—only repositories with unregenerated commit graphs and clock skew are affected.
