The patch adds a new `delete` subcommand to `git refs`, allowing users to delete references with optional old-value verification. The new subcommand is documented in `git-refs.adoc`, and a comprehensive test script (`t1464-refs-delete.sh`) verifies its behavior, including edge cases like stale old values, empty messages, and invalid arguments.
