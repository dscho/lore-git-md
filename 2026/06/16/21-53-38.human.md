This is a surface-level review of patch 7/17 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series. Justin Tobler confirms the mechanical correctness of the changes: the patch moves the `reprepare()` logic for packed object storage from `packfile.c` to `odb/source-packed.c`, wires it up as a callback, and updates all callers to use the new abstraction. He notes that the prepare-vs-reprepare naming is confusing but accepts it as an intermediate step, deferring the eventual unification to a future series. The review is purely about code relocation and callback wiring, with no engagement on deeper ODB design or the refdb infrastructure issues that are currently blocking the series.
