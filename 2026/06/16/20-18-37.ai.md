This is the sixth and final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's six-part documentation series updating `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to clarify Git's commit message trailer conventions. The patch adds a short note explaining that trailers like `Acked-by` and `Reviewed-by` should appear *before* the `Signed-off-by` trailer, with the rationale that sign-offs apply to the entire message and trailers preceding them.

In this reply, Kristoffer accepts Junio C Hamano's review suggestion to rephrase the guidance to emphasize the underlying chronological principle: trailers should be ordered to reflect the sequence of events (e.g., testing, reviewing, signing off). This avoids implying that trailer order is determined by trailer type (e.g., `Tested-by` always before `Reviewed-by`), which could be misinterpreted. The revised wording will make it clearer that the goal is to show who added which trailers, not to enforce a rigid type-based ordering.

The change is a minor but meaningful refinement to the documentation's pedagogical clarity. It resolves the last open feedback point on this patch, leaving only the broader debate about non-identity trailers (e.g., `Closes:`) in the earlier patches of the series. The series as a whole remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, addressing a recurring source of confusion for contributors.
