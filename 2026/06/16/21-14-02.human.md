This is a surface-level review of patch 4/17 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series, which is part of the multi-year effort to enable pluggable storage backends in Git. The patch itself is a mechanical rename: it replaces a generic `struct odb_source *` pointer in `struct odb_source_packed` with a specific `struct odb_source_files *` pointer to make the relationship between packed storage and its parent "files" source more explicit. The change is small (11 lines across four files) and aligns with the pattern established in earlier patches of this series.

Justin Tobler’s review is brief and focused on the patch’s immediate mechanics. He confirms the change matches the precedent set by `struct odb_source_loose` and notes the eventual goal of removing the parent pointer entirely, though he admits the intermediate step isn’t immediately obvious to him. His feedback is purely observational—no technical objections or deeper engagement with the ODB abstraction’s design. This review doesn’t address the broader refdb issues Patrick has identified as blocking the series, nor does it question the architectural direction.

The patch is uncontroversial and appears well-crafted, but it’s worth noting that the series as a whole is now blocked by newly discovered refdb lifecycle problems (memory leaks, duplicate refdb creation, and initialization ordering issues). Those issues are being addressed in a separate 11-patch fix series, which depends on Patrick’s earlier "ps/setup-centralize-odb-creation" work. Until those fixes land, this patch—like the rest of the ODB series—remains in limbo. For now, it’s a small, safe step toward a larger goal.
