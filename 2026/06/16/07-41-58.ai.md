This is a brief architectural follow-up in the `git update-ref --rename` thread. Patrick Steinhardt agrees to take over the rename functionality and implement it as a subcommand under `git refs` instead of as a flag to `update-ref`. This decision reflects the ongoing discussion about unifying Git's ref-related commands under a single, more intuitive interface (`git refs`) that can handle both reading and writing operations, rather than the current split between `update-ref` (write-only) and `git-refs` (read-only). The thread leaves the original `--rename` patch technically complete but shifts future development toward Patrick's proposed restructuring.
