This thread began with Uwe Kleine-König’s patch to stop Git from incorrectly copying notes from dropped commits during rebase. That small fix has since grown into a thorough 11-patch overhaul by Phillip Wood, which Junio has queued for final review. The series now addresses every known edge case—empty changes, fixup sequences, edit commands, skip operations, external merge failures, and even command execution failures—ensuring that notes and post-rewrite hooks no longer receive misleading information about dropped commits.

Phillip’s work is complete and self-contained; it supersedes Uwe’s original patch, which is no longer needed. The only remaining loose ends are procedural: Junio manually fixed a `b4 am` glitch that had dropped one patch from the series, and Uwe has confirmed the tooling issue is resolved by running `b4 am --no-parent`. The series is now queued with minor typofixes, and Phillip is expected to verify those before the final merge.
