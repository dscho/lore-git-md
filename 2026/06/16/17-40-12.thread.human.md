This thread began with Uwe Kleine-König’s patch to fix a bug where `git rebase` incorrectly copies notes from dropped commits to the current HEAD. The discussion quickly expanded into a broader audit of how the rebase machinery handles dropped commits across all operations, including empty changes, fixup sequences, edit commands, skip operations, external merge strategy failures, and command execution failures.

Phillip Wood took the lead with an 11-patch series that systematically addresses these edge cases. The series is now complete, queued in Junio’s tree for final review, and carries Uwe’s Tested-by. The core fix (patch 11/11) introduces `PICK_RESULT_DROPPED` to ensure notes and post-rewrite hooks no longer receive information about dropped commits. The series also restores test coverage for the "apply" backend, adds tests for external merge strategy failures and command execution failures, and refactors control flow in `sequencer.c` to prepare for the fix. A known limitation—the "edit" command still erroneously records dropped commits as rewritten—is documented and flagged for a separate fix.

The only open questions are procedural: whether Junio will land the series before or after Uwe’s original patch (which is now redundant), and whether Phillip will squash minor typofixes before the final merge. Oswald Buddenhagen’s recent review of patch 10/11 suggests widening the scope of `enum pick_result` for stylistic consistency, but this is a low-weight suggestion that doesn’t affect the series’ correctness or readiness. The series is expected to land as-is, resolving the original bug and its broader systemic implications.
