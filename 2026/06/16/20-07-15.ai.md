This is Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s reply to Junio C Hamano’s review of the fourth patch in the six-part documentation series updating `SubmittingPatches` with trailer conventions. The patch documents the `Based-on-patch-by:` trailer, which credits someone who authored parts of a patch the submitter is now sending.

Kristoffer acknowledges Junio’s concern that the original wording could be read as encouraging the use of material that lacks a DCO sign-off, which would create copyright issues. He agrees the mention of sign-off should be retained and plans to revise the text to clarify that the original author must provide sign-off for non-trivial contributions, aligning with Junio’s suggestion to encourage this process explicitly. The distinction between `Based-on-patch-by:` (for concrete code snippets or patches) and `Helped-by:` (for ideas without precise changes) is preserved, but the legal requirement for sign-off is now foregrounded.

The exchange is substantive: it addresses a real ambiguity in the documentation and ensures the guidelines reflect Git’s legal and procedural standards. The revision will be part of v2 of the series. No new technical details or examples are introduced; the focus is on tightening the wording to prevent misinterpretation.
