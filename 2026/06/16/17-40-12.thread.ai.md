Core problem or goal
When `git rebase` drops a commit (e.g., because its changes are already present in the target branch), the sequencer incorrectly copies that commit’s notes to the current HEAD commit, corrupting the repository’s note history. The discussion has expanded into a systemic audit and repair of how the rebase machinery handles dropped commits across all operations: empty-changes, fixup sequences, edit commands, skip operations, final fixup cleanup, **external merge strategy failures**, and **command execution failures** (e.g., `git commit` failing to run).

Current status

- **Patch 11/11 (final patch)**: introduces the core behavioral change by adding `PICK_RESULT_DROPPED` to `enum pick_result` and modifying `pick_one_commit()` to skip `record_in_rewritten()` for dropped commits.
