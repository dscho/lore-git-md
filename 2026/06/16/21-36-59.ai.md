This is a **surface-level review** of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction patch (v2 5/17), part of the ongoing effort to refactor Git’s object database into pluggable backends. Justin Tobler’s reply focuses on mechanical clarity and architectural consistency rather than functional behavior.

The review confirms the patch’s approach—embedding `struct odb_source` in `struct odb_source_packed` and wiring up lifecycle management—but raises a single question about directory-change handling: whether the packfile store previously ignored `chdir` notifications. Justin notes this curiosity without challenging the change, and his feedback is otherwise limited to validating the structural alignment with the loose ODB source’s design. He clarifies a potential confusion about the embedded `base` field (distinct from a parent pointer) and approves the patch’s direction.

The review does not engage with deeper implications of the ODB abstraction (e.g., callback readiness, path handling, or the refdb issues blocking the series) or test the patch’s behavior. It is narrowly scoped to the diff’s readability and adherence to the established pattern. For context, this patch is one of 17 in a series that has uncovered critical refdb lifecycle problems (memory leaks, duplicate creation paths), but those issues are not addressed here. The series remains blocked on an 11-patch fix series to resolve those problems, which depends on prior infrastructure work ("ps/setup-centralize-odb-creation").
