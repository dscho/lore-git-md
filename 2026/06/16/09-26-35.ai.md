This is the first patch in the v3 series introducing `--linearize` to `git replay`. It refactors the `enum replay_mode` into a boolean `reverse` flag, a preparatory cleanup with no behavior change.

The patch mechanically replaces the two-value enum (`REPLAY_MODE_PICK` and `REPLAY_MODE_REVERT`) with a boolean `reverse` that is true when `--revert` is given. All enum checks (`mode == REPLAY_MODE_REVERT`) become boolean checks (`reverse`), and the BUG() guard for invalid enum values is removed. The change is confined to `replay.c` and touches only the internal replay machinery; no CLI, documentation, or test files are modified.

This refactoring was previously reviewed by Justin Tobler, who raised a forward-looking concern about extensibility if additional replay modes are introduced later. Toon addressed that in the cover letter by noting that the current two-mode design is stable and that any future modes would require a separate flag. The patch itself remains unchanged from v2.
