Proposal to fold commit ranges into a single commit, implemented as `git history squash` standalone subcommand. **Series v7 by Harald Nordgren is posted, addresses all prior feedback, and is now marked "Will replace" by Junio C Hamano**, signaling intent to queue for the next release. **Finalized in v7**: explicit rejection of ranges whose oldest commit is a `fixup!`/`squash!`/`amend!` (since the marker’s target cannot be inside the range), **adoption of `git rebase -i`’s squash-message template** (ensuring identical behavior for `fixup!`/`squash!`/`amend!` messages, with `fixup!` messages fully commented out under a "will be skipped" header and `squash!`/`amend!` bodies retained with only the marker subject commented), reordered CLI usage to place dashed options before `<revision-range>`, expanded documentation to clarify the merge limitation and feature overview, and Junio’s requested clarification on why oldest-commit markers are rejected.

Core problem: efficiently collapse a linear or merge-commit-containing range into its oldest commit while preserving descendant history, avoiding the repeated conflict stops of a rebase-based approach. **Finalized behavior**: refs pointing to commits inside the squashed range are **detected and rejected by default**, with an `advice.historyUpdateRefs` message suggesting `--update-refs=head` to explicitly retarget the current branch to the squashed commit. **New in v7**: ranges whose oldest commit is a `fixup!`/`squash!`/`amend!` are now rejected, as the marker’s target cannot lie inside the range.
