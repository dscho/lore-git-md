This is the third patch in Elijah Newren's five-part series hardening merge-ort's error handling and corruption detection. The patch addresses a resource leak in the merge machinery's cleanup path.

The change fixes a case where diff pairs queues could be leaked when collect_merge_info() encounters an error before reaching resolve_and_process_renames(). The patch adds proper cleanup of renames->pairs[].queue in clear_or_reinit_internal_opts(), making it safe to call even in early error cases. In the normal path where resolve_and_process_renames() has already initialized the queue, the free() is a harmless no-op on NULL.

This is a straightforward cleanup that plugs a real (though likely rare) leak scenario. The change is small and focused, touching only the merge-ort.c file with a two-line diff that adds the missing free() and reinitialization. The patch maintains the series' trajectory toward merging, with all maintainer feedback from earlier patches already addressed.
