This thread addresses a small but noticeable build system issue: gitk and git-gui currently output translation catalog generation messages even during `make -s` (silent) builds. The two-patch series modifies their respective Makefiles to properly respect the silent flag, suppressing the "Generating catalog" and `msgfmt --statistics` output when requested while keeping verbose behavior in normal builds.

The patches have already been reviewed and found technically sound, with no open questions remaining. A follow-up email provided concrete examples of the unwanted output that motivated the changes, showing the translation statistics that currently appear during silent builds. Both Johannes Sixt (gitk maintainer) and Johannes Schindelin (git-gui maintainer) will need to ack these changes before they can be merged, but the patches are otherwise ready. The changes are minimal and focused, following established patterns from other parts of Git's build system.
