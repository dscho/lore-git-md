This thread completed a small but long-overdue cleanup: making `make -s` builds fully silent in gitk and git-gui by adopting the same quiet-build pattern already used in core Git. Harald Nordgren sent a two-patch series—one for gitk, one for git-gui—that removed the `--statistics` flag from `msgfmt` invocations and added conditional suppression of "Generating catalog" messages when `make -s` is specified.

Johannes Sixt, the gitk maintainer, reviewed and merged the gitk patch into his `j6t-testing` branch, while the git-gui patch turned out to be unnecessary because Sixt had already implemented equivalent changes in his `hn/silence-make-s` branch. Both sets of changes are now in Sixt’s testing branches and will be proposed for inclusion in upstream Git in the coming weeks, though they won’t make the v2.55 release. Junio C Hamano acknowledged the update with a simple "Thanks," confirming the thread is now procedurally closed. No objections were raised, and the goal—silent `make -s` builds across all three repositories—has been achieved.
