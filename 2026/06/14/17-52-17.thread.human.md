This thread completed a small but long-overdue cleanup: making `make -s` builds fully silent in gitk and git-gui by adopting the same quiet-build pattern already used in core Git. Harald Nordgren’s two-patch series (one for gitk, one for git-gui) removed the `--statistics` flag from `msgfmt` invocations and added conditional suppression of “Generating catalog” messages when `make -s` is specified. Johannes Sixt, the gitk maintainer, merged the gitk patch into his `j6t-testing` branch and confirmed that equivalent changes for git-gui were already present in his `hn/silence-make-s` branch. Both sets of changes will be proposed for upstream Git inclusion in the coming weeks, though they missed the v2.55 release window. Junio acknowledged the timeline without objection, and the thread is now closed. Harald’s recent follow-up simply asks for confirmation of the current status; no further discussion or changes are expected.
