This thread is about making `make -s` builds quieter for Git’s two GUI tools, gitk and git-gui. Right now, even with `-s`, you still see “Generating catalog” messages and `msgfmt --statistics` output on stderr. The original two-patch series fixes this by wrapping the `msgfmt` calls in conditionals that check `MAKEFLAGS` for `-s`, following the same pattern used elsewhere in Git’s build system.

Johannes Sixt, the gitk maintainer, has just chimed in with a simpler idea: instead of conditionally suppressing the output, just remove the `--statistics` flag and the “Generating catalog” echo entirely, matching what core Git’s Makefile already does (commit 2f12b31b746c). His suggestion would eliminate the need for any conditional logic and make the build quieter by default. The author now needs to decide whether to stick with the current approach or switch to Sixt’s proposal; either way, the patches are ready for maintainer review once that question is settled.
