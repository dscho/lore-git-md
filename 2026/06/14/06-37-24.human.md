This is the third patch in Elijah Newren's five-part series hardening merge-ort's error handling and corruption detection. The patch fixes a memory leak in the merge-ort backend that could occur when error paths were taken before rename processing completed.

The change adds cleanup of the `renames->pairs[].queue` array in `clear_or_reinit_internal_opts()`, ensuring it's freed when merges abort early. While this array is normally handled by `resolve_and_process_renames()`, early errors could bypass that cleanup. The fix is straightforward - just two lines adding the free and reinitialization - but plugs a real leak that could occur during error conditions.

This continues the series' focus on robust error handling in merge-ort, following the earlier error propagation fixes and flag cleanup.
