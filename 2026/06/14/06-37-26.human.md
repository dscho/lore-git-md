This is the final patch in Elijah Newren's merge-ort hardening series, completing the work to detect and prevent corrupt trees from being written. The patch specifically addresses a gap where verify_cache() would miss directory/file (D/F) conflicts when the conflicting entries weren't adjacent in the index (like "docs" and "docs/requirements.txt" with "docs-internal/README.md" in between).

The solution introduces a new find_df_conflict() helper that performs a binary search when the next entry doesn't immediately show a conflict, ensuring all D/F cases are caught regardless of their position in the index. The patch includes comprehensive tests that directly construct corrupt indexes to verify the detection works, including both adjacent and non-adjacent conflict cases.

This completes the series' goal of hardening merge-ort against corrupt trees by:
1. Fixing error propagation in tree traversal
2. Cleaning up unused error handling flags
3. Adding proper cleanup for diff pairs queues
4. Detecting duplicate entries
5. Now catching all D/F conflicts (including non-adjacent ones)

The changes are well-tested and maintainer-approved, with Junio Hamano having reviewed the technical approach and suggested the key find_df_conflict() refactoring. The series is now ready for merging into next/master.
