This is a refactoring patch in the `diff.<driver>.process` series, which aims to enable external tools to inject diff hunks into Git's diff and blame pipelines. The patch focuses on the subprocess management infrastructure.

The change separates the concerns of process lifecycle management from hashmap operations in the subprocess API. Previously, `subprocess_start()` and `subprocess_stop()` handled both starting/stopping processes and maintaining the hashmap. This patch introduces new functions `subprocess_start_command()` and `subprocess_stop_command()` that handle just the process management, while the original functions become thin wrappers that add hashmap operations.

The refactoring allows callers who don't use the hashmap (like the upcoming diff-process implementation) to reuse the process management code without the hashmap overhead. The changes are mechanical and maintain all existing behavior - the hashmap functionality remains available through the original functions.

This is part of the larger effort to support external diff processors while maintaining Git's existing diff features. The separation of concerns here follows the project's pattern of providing lower-level building blocks that can be composed as needed. The patch appears well-contained and focused, with no behavioral changes beyond the intended refactoring.
