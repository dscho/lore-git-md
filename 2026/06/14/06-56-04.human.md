This is the third patch in the v2 series adding a `--linearize` option to `git replay`, and it has received a substantive review from Elijah Newren. The review is thorough and constructive, focusing on clarity, completeness, and edge-case coverage.

Elijah begins by endorsing the current implementation, calling it "nicer overall" than his own earlier work in the `replay-upstream` branch.
