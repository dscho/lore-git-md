Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` feature, now in its fourth iteration, is nearly ready for Junio’s final review—but one last question has stalled progress. The series implements a new `git history squash` subcommand to collapse a commit range (including merge commits) into a single commit while preserving descendant history, avoiding the friction of interactive rebase. After addressing all prior technical and design concerns—including how to handle refs pointing into the squashed range (now rejected by default with advice to use `--update-refs=head`)—the series was considered code-complete.

However, Patrick Steinhardt has raised a procedural but consequential issue: the series doesn’t address his earlier suggestion to add an `--ancestry=` option (mirroring `git log`) to control how commit ranges are interpreted. While Patrick acknowledges that v4 resolves the ref-handling design, he expects explicit responses to reviewer questions in rerolls, even if the feedback isn’t adopted. Until Harald clarifies whether `--ancestry=` will be added or why it’s unnecessary, the series remains in limbo. No other technical concerns are outstanding, and the ball is now in Harald’s court to either implement the option or justify its omission.
