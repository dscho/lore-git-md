This new subcommand efficiently collapses a commit range—including merge commits—into its oldest commit while preserving descendant history, avoiding the repeated conflict stops of a rebase-based workflow. The series is functionally complete except for one remaining issue: how to handle case sensitivity in autosquash markers (`fixup!`, `squash!`, `amend!`).

The question now is whether to enforce strict case sensitivity (rejecting all uppercase OIDs) or adopt a more permissive approach that aligns with existing tools.
