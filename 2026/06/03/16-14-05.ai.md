This is part 6 of Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series adding a "drop" subcommand to the experimental `git history` command. The patch modifies the reset machinery to allow callers to specify the "from" commit for a reset operation, rather than always using HEAD.

The change introduces a new `oid_from` field in `struct reset_head_opts` that callers can use to provide an explicit starting commit. This gives more control to callers who may already have resolved the commit they want to reset from, or who want to reset from a commit that isn't currently HEAD. The implementation maintains backward compatibility by falling back to reading HEAD when `oid_from` isn't specified.

This is a small but meaningful change to the reset infrastructure that enables more flexible use of `reset_head()` by the history command and potentially other callers. The patch follows Patrick's typical style - focused, well-explained, and with clear documentation of the new field. It's part of the broader refactoring of reset machinery to support the history command's needs while also improving the general-purpose reset functionality.
