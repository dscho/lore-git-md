Yuan questions whether this difference could create confusion in the mailing list archives and suggests that making `git format-patch` generate comprehensive version histories like `b4` might provide better consistency.

This represents a late-stage consideration in a largely settled discussion, touching on edge cases of version tracking in the mailing list workflow.
