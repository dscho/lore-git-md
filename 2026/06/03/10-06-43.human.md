1. A v3 with a heavily modified `reset_head()`, or
2. A justification for keeping the new helper if the adaptation proves too invasive

The exchange highlights a common tension in large codebases between DRY principles and pragmatic solutions when existing functions don't quite fit new use cases. Patrick's detailed analysis demonstrates he's taking the feedback seriously while being transparent about the tradeoffs involved.
