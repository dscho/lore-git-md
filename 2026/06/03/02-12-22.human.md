This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about configuring the `b4` tool for Git's mailing list workflow. Weijie Yuan responds to Tuomas Ahola's observation about threading model inconsistencies, seeking clarification on terminology.

The exchange highlights confusion between two concepts:
- Shallow threading (all patches reply to the cover letter)
- Chain-reply (each version replies to the previous version's cover letter)

Yuan suggests that replying to the previous cover letter (as recommended in MyFirstContribution) would constitute chain-reply rather than shallow threading. This technical clarification is important as the project considers how to configure b4's default behavior to match Git's preferred workflow.

The discussion remains unresolved, with the core question being whether b4 should be configured to match GitGitGadget's deep threading approach (chain-reply) or maintain the proposed shallow threading model. This distinction affects how patch series appear in mail clients and archives, with implications for review continuity.
