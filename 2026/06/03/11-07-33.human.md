This is a documentation clarification in the ongoing discussion about configuring the b4 tool for Git's mailing list workflow, specifically regarding threading models for patch series submissions.

SZEDER Gábor provides a technical correction to Patrick Steinhardt's explanation of threading terminology in Git's mailing list context. He clarifies that shallow threading simply means all patches reply to the current cover letter, regardless of how the cover letter itself relates to previous versions. This differs from Patrick's description which suggested the cover letter should always attach to the first version's cover letter in shallow threading.

The email also corrects the definition of deep threading, noting it means each email replies to the immediately preceding one (not just cover letters replying to previous versions). This distinction is important as the thread has been discussing whether to standardize on shallow or deep threading for the project's workflow.

This exchange shows the community working to establish precise terminology around email threading behaviors, which is crucial as they document recommended practices for contributors. The clarification helps ensure the final documentation will accurately describe the threading models being configured in the b4 tool setup.
