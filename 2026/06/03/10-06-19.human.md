This is a follow-up in the `git history drop` series, where Patrick Steinhardt responds to Pablo Sabater's review feedback on the second patch. The series adds commit-dropping capability to the experimental `git history` command.

Patrick acknowledges two substantive points from Pablo's review:

1. **Error message consistency**: Agrees to make the merge commit error message more detailed, matching the explanatory style used for root commits (which currently includes context about why they can't be dropped).

2. **Test helper cleanup**: Accepts removing the redundant `--graph` flag from the `expect_graph` test helper since it's underlying `lib_test_cmp_graph` already includes it. However, he declines to consolidate the duplicated test helper into `lib-log-graph.sh` at this time, preferring to keep the series focused but welcoming a follow-up cleanup.

The exchange shows Patrick being responsive to technical feedback while maintaining scope discipline - a pattern seen in his other series. The review identified real issues (one stylistic, one about test infrastructure), and the author has committed to fixing the immediate concerns while punting the broader test helper deduplication to a future effort. This keeps the series moving forward while leaving the door open for Pablo to contribute the consolidation separately.
