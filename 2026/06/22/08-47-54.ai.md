This is a refactoring patch (1/2) in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction effort, converting the `reprepare()` callback into a more flexible `prepare()` callback with a flag to control cache invalidation. The change is mechanical and confined to the ODB layer, touching 11 files—primarily in `odb/`, plus minor updates to `builtin/grep.c`, `midx.c`, and `packfile.c`.

The patch replaces the existing `reprepare()` callback with a new `prepare()` callback that accepts an `enum odb_prepare_flags` parameter. The flag `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` determines whether caches are cleared before preparation. This generalization allows `git grep` to call `prepare()` without flushing caches, eliminating its direct dependency on the "files" backend’s `odb_source_packed_prepare()` function. The commit message clarifies the motivation: `reprepare()` was effectively two operations (flush caches, then prepare), and `git grep` only needed the latter.

Key symbols introduced or modified include the `prepare()` callback, the `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` flag, and the removal of `odb_source_packed_prepare()` from the public API. The patch maintains backward compatibility by preserving the old behavior when the flag is set, ensuring existing calls to `odb_source_reprepare()` (now a wrapper around `prepare()`) continue to work. The changes are low-risk, as they do not alter behavior but enable future generic ODB use cases.
