This is the final piece of the stacked-branch safety discussion in Harald Nordgren’s `git branch --delete-merged` series. Phillip Wood formally endorses the simplified approach: **abort deletion** (with a clear error message) when a branch is used as an upstream for another branch, rather than attempting to retarget dependent branches. This resolves the last major design ambiguity in the series.

The decision is pragmatic: aborting is safer, easier to implement, and preserves backward compatibility for future enhancements like optional retargeting. Phillip’s approval removes the last technical blocker for v17, which now includes all planned features (stacked-branch protection, per-branch opt-out, `--dry-run`, and `--forked` filtering) with a clean, well-tested implementation. The series is now ready for final review.
