This is a refactoring patch (1/2) in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction effort, converting the `reprepare()` callback into a more flexible `prepare()` callback that can optionally flush caches. The change is mechanical and confined to the ODB layer, touching 11 files—primarily in `odb/`, with minor updates to `builtin/grep.c`, `midx.c`, and `packfile.c`.

The patch replaces the existing `reprepare()` callback with a new `prepare()` function that accepts a flag (`ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES`) to control cache invalidation. This allows `git grep` to call the callback without flushing caches, which was previously impossible without reaching into the "files" backend directly. The motivation is to enable generic ODB backends to work with `git grep`, though the commit message could be clearer about the specific limitation this addresses.

The implementation is straightforward: the `reprepare()` callback is renamed to `prepare()` and updated to respect the new flag, while all callers are adjusted to use the new API. The changes are well-scoped and unlikely to introduce regressions, given their limited surface area. However, the flag-based approach might raise questions about whether it’s sufficiently future-proof or if it could lead to ambiguity in callback behavior.

This is the first iteration (`v1`) of the series, and no reviews have been posted yet. Given Patrick’s track record with ODB refactoring, the patch is likely technically sound, but reviewers may focus on the API design and whether the new flag-based approach is the right long-term solution.
