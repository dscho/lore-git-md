This is a brief follow-up in the `git replay --linearize` series thread, specifically on the first patch’s `enum replay_mode` refactoring. Junio C Hamano weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier concern, agreeing that converting the two-value enum to a boolean `reverse` flag may harm readability and future extensibility. He echoes Patrick’s point that boolean parameters obscure intent at the callsite unless the variable name is exceptionally clear, and notes that even then, the callsite loses expressiveness compared to named enum values. Junio frames this as a maintainability question: "what happens when somebody else wants a third choice?" — a recurring theme in Git’s design discussions. No new technical objections are raised; the message reinforces the conceptual hesitation already voiced by two reviewers. The thread remains open, with the enum-to-bool refactoring now facing pushback from both Patrick and Junio.
