you apply the following diff:

```diff

@@ -302,3 +302,5 @@ RewriteRule ^/half-auth-complete/ - [E=AUTHREQUIRED:yes]
 		SVNPath "${LIB_HTTPD_SVNPATH}"
 	</Location>
 </IfDefine>
+
+Timeout 1
```
