This is a CI/build system follow-up in the macOS linker warning suppression thread. Patrick Steinhardt reports that the duplicate-library warning also appears in Meson builds, though Meson itself has partially mitigated it by suppressing the warning when `--fatal-warnings` is used. He suggests the warning could be unconditionally disabled in Git’s Meson configuration, as it provides no real value, and has cc’d a Meson maintainer for input.

The email does not propose a patch or request changes to the existing v3 patch (which targets Makefile builds only). Instead, it surfaces a tangential issue: the same warning exists in Meson, and the thread’s agreed solution (linker flag suppression) might be worth extending there. This is a minor observation rather than a blocker; the original patch remains ready for merging as-is. The broader question of whether to suppress the warning in Meson builds is now open but orthogonal to the immediate thread.
