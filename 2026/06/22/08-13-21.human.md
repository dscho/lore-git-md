This is a **build system** and **platform compatibility** discussion in the ongoing `USE_NSEC` Meson parity thread. Patrick Steinhardt replies to Jeff King’s (Peff) substantive review of D. Ben Knoble’s patch, which added a `nanosec` Meson option to mirror the Autotools `USE_NSEC` knob.

Patrick initially considered whether Meson should auto-detect nanosecond timestamp support but now questions the wisdom of exposing the option at all. He agrees with Peff’s technical caution about `USE_NSEC`’s reliability on certain filesystems (e.g., CEPH, CIFS, NTFS, UDF), citing the same `Documentation/technical/racy-git.adoc` reference. His key point: if the mechanism is still subtly broken, it might make more sense to *remove* the build option entirely rather than propagate it to Meson. He argues that maintaining a rarely-used, potentially problematic knob adds no value.

The patch itself remains queued for merging, but Patrick’s reply shifts the discussion toward a broader question: whether `USE_NSEC` should exist at all. This could lead to future deprecation or removal efforts, though no immediate action is proposed. For now, the thread’s focus is on whether the option’s inclusion in Meson is justified given its known limitations.
