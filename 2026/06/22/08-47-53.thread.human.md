This thread covers a two-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt that generalizes the ODB subsystem's `reprepare()` callback into a more flexible `prepare()` API. The change enables `git grep` to work with pluggable ODB backends by decoupling cache invalidation from ODB preparation, eliminating a direct dependency on the "files" backend. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed the series and gave unconditional approval, signaling it's ready to merge without further changes.

The refactoring introduces an `enum odb_prepare_flags` with a single `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` flag, replacing the old `reprepare()` callback while preserving backward compatibility through the `odb_reprepare()` wrapper. Toon Claes initially raised two technical concerns—about undocumented downcasting and inconsistent flag usage—but both were resolved after Patrick's clarifications, with Toon retracting his objections. The series touches 12 files, primarily in the `odb/` directory, with minimal changes to `builtin/grep.c`, `midx.c`, and `packfile.c`.

This work is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB abstraction effort, building on prior refactoring to enable pluggable backends. No functional changes are intended, but the new API paves the way for future generic ODB use cases. The thread is now closed, with all review feedback addressed and maintainer approval secured.
