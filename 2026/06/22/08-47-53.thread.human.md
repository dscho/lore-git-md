Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series refactors the ODB subsystem’s `reprepare()` callback into a more flexible `prepare()` API to support pluggable backends, particularly for `git grep`. This v1 series, part of his broader ODB abstraction effort, replaces the old callback with a flag-based design (`ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES`) to decouple cache invalidation from preparation, enabling generic ODB use cases.

Toon Claes’s recent review raises substantive concerns about the patch’s technical design. He questions the omission of the `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` flag in certain calls (e.g., `find_pack_entry()`), where the old code explicitly flushed caches, and notes the shift of downcasting logic into backend implementations without clear justification. These points suggest potential behavioral inconsistencies or edge cases that could affect correctness. While Toon acknowledges the refactoring’s intent, his feedback highlights areas where the patch might diverge from the original semantics, warranting further clarification or adjustment from Patrick.
