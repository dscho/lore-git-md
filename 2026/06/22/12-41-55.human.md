This is the first patch in Toon Claes’s three-part series introducing a `--linearize` option to `git replay`. It refactors the existing `enum replay_mode` into a simple boolean `reverse` flag, eliminating redundant checks and BUG() assertions while preserving identical behavior for the current pick/revert modes.

The change is purely internal to `replay.c` and touches no other files. The boolean approach was debated in earlier rounds but accepted as sufficient for the current dual-mode needs, with future extensibility acknowledged but not required. This refactoring paves the way for the `--linearize` feature in patch 3/3 by simplifying the control flow around commit processing.

All review feedback from v1–v3 has been incorporated, and Junio Hamano has already signaled readiness to merge the series to 'next'. The patch is well-motivated, clearly explained, and carries Johannes Schindelin’s Signed-off-by, indicating broad support. If you care about `git replay`’s evolution, this is a safe, uncontroversial step toward the larger goal.
