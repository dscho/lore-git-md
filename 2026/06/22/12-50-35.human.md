This is a brief exchange in the "What's cooking" thread about the `ps/gitlab-ci-windows` patch. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, agrees with Patrick Steinhardt that the patch is safe to merge directly to `master` for inclusion in Git 2.55, rather than waiting for the next cycle.

Patrick had earlier described the issue as "extremely annoying," and Junio now echoes that sentiment by noting the patch's limited "blast radius" compared to other recent merges. There are no technical objections or open questions here—just a straightforward decision to include a low-risk, high-value fix in the upcoming release.

If you follow Git's CI or Windows development, this is a minor but welcome cleanup. The patch is unlikely to generate further discussion, but it’s a good example of how the project handles last-minute reliability fixes during feature freeze.
