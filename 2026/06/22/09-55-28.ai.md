This is a **CI/platform-compatibility** update in the ongoing macOS CI hang investigation. Patrick Steinhardt reports that the same `t5551` test suite is now failing on **Linux CI** as well, though the logs are truncated and the exact error is unclear. The macOS failure mode has shifted from a hang to a `curl 18 transfer closed with outstanding read data remaining` error, suggesting the transfer is being aborted mid-stream rather than stalling indefinitely.

The new Linux failures indicate the issue may not be macOS-specific, but could stem from a deeper problem in Git’s HTTP/2 transport layer, libcurl, or Apache’s `h2_mod`. Patrick explicitly rejects the idea of simply disabling the test again, framing it as potentially papering over a real bug. No new fixes are proposed here; the email is a status update highlighting the expanded scope of the problem and reinforcing the need for root-cause analysis before any patch (including Michael Montalbo’s proposed fix) can be considered complete. The thread remains focused on diagnosing environment factors (libcurl versions, Apache configuration) and protocol-level behavior.
