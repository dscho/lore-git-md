The key takeaway is the **philosophical consistency** Junio emphasizes: `--delete-merged` should behave like a loop over `git branch -d`, inheriting all its safety checks. This means stacked-branch protection (preserving branches used as upstreams) will apply uniformly to both commands, avoiding surprises for users. The discussion also implicitly rejects the earlier idea of clearing upstream configs for dependent branches—Junio’s reply treats the entire chain of upstreams as sacrosanct, simplifying the implementation and reducing edge cases.
