This thread is the start of a three-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction effort, aimed at making Git’s connectivity checks independent of packfile internals. This small but necessary change ensures that connectivity checks can later be generalized in the third patch. The patch also improves error handling by propagating pack-opening failures instead of silently ignoring them.

So far, the series is clean and incremental, with no objections or requests for changes.
