This is a substantive review reply to the first patch in the `git replay --linearize` v4 series. Patrick Steinhardt questions the refactoring of `enum replay_mode` into a boolean `reverse` flag, arguing that while the change simplifies the code mechanically, it may harm readability and future extensibility. He notes that boolean parameters often obscure meaning at the callsite unless the variable name is self-documenting, and expresses concern about the difficulty of adding a third replay mode later. The review does not reject the patch outright but signals hesitation about the trade-off between simplicity and clarity, and invites reconsideration of whether the enum should be retained for forward compatibility. No alternative implementation is proposed; the feedback is conceptual rather than technical. The thread remains under review, with this concern now explicitly raised by two reviewers (Patrick and Justin Tobler).
