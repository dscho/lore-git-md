This is a **substantive review comment** on the first patch of Toon Claes’s `--linearize` feature series for `git replay`. Patrick Steinhardt questions the refactoring’s long-term maintainability, specifically whether converting `enum replay_mode` to a boolean `reverse` flag improves readability or risks painting the code into a corner if a third replay mode is ever needed.

The review raises two key points: first, that boolean parameters can obscure meaning at the callsite (e.g., `create_commit(..., true)` is less clear than `create_commit(..., REPLAY_MODE_REVERT)`), and second, that the current two-mode design might not be future-proof. While the patch itself is small and mechanical—touching only `replay.c` and replacing enum checks with boolean logic—Patrick’s concern is architectural: whether this simplification could complicate later extensions, such as the `--linearize` mode being added in patch 3/3 or hypothetical future modes.

This is not a rejection, but it does revisit a debate from earlier iterations (notably with Justin Tobler) about whether a boolean is sufficient for the current dual-mode needs. The series as a whole has Junio’s approval and is otherwise uncontroversial, but Patrick’s feedback suggests the refactoring might not be an unalloyed improvement. The discussion is now open for Toon or others to weigh the trade-offs between immediate clarity and future flexibility.
