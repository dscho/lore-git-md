This is the sixth and final patch in Tian Yuchen’s six-part series optimizing `` `git merge-base` `` performance for one-sided histories. The patch adds a new technical document, `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`, that explains the `paint_down_to_common()` algorithm, its use of generation numbers, and the side-exhaustion termination condition introduced earlier in the series.

Derrick Stolee’s reply is a **surface-level review** of the documentation. He praises the idea of documenting the algorithm but raises two minor points: first, that the document risks drifting from the implementation unless the code itself links back to it; second, that it might have been instructive to add the document *before* the code changes, showing the old algorithm and then updating it alongside the patches. Neither point questions the correctness or clarity of the current document, and both are framed as suggestions rather than objections. The review does not revisit any of the unresolved structural or performance-instrumentation questions from earlier in the thread.
