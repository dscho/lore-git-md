This is a **CI/platform-compatibility** update in the macOS CI hang investigation. Patrick Steinhardt identifies the root cause of the `curl 18 transfer closed with outstanding read data remaining` errors: **Apache’s default timeout (300 seconds) is being hit** during the `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 refs. By locally setting `Timeout 1` in Apache’s configuration, he reproduces the exact `curl 18` error, confirming that the transfer is being aborted mid-stream due to Apache terminating the connection. The Apache logs explicitly report `AH01220: Timeout waiting for output from CGI script` and `AH00574: ap_content_length_filter: apr_bucket_read() failed`.

The discovery explains why the failure mode shifted from hangs to mid-transfer aborts on macOS and why Linux CI is now failing similarly. Neither TCP-level keepalives nor Git’s application-level sideband keepalives (which do not apply to the `ls-refs` endpoint) prevent the timeout. The fix proposed by Michael Montalbo (packing refs and aligning `$JOBS`) reduces runtime but does not address the underlying timeout issue; the test still hits Apache’s limit, just faster. The thread now pivots to **whether the timeout should be increased, whether the test should be optimized further, or whether the keepalive mechanism should be extended to cover `ls-refs`**. No new patch is proposed yet, but the diagnosis is now concrete.
