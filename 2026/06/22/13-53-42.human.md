This is a brief, surface-level review of the second patch in Toon Claes’s three-patch series introducing `--linearize` support to `git replay`. Patrick Steinhardt comments on the refactoring in `replay.c` that adds a `put_mapped_commit()` helper and renames `mapped_commit()` to `get_mapped_commit()`. He calls the khash map interface “awkward to use” and says the wrapper “feels sensible,” noting that it makes the code cleaner and more maintainable. The comment is positive but does not engage with the patch’s substance or suggest any changes. The review is purely about code style and readability, not functionality or design.
