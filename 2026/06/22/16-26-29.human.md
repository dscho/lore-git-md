This is a brief administrative note from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, confirming that Taylor Blau’s four-patch series integrating `--delta-islands` with `--path-walk` in `git pack-objects` is now officially queued for the `next` integration branch. The series has been merged after all technical and performance concerns were resolved.

Junio describes the implementation as "very cleanly implemented," echoing earlier praise from reviewers. While he and Derrick Stolee both expressed lingering uncertainty about the delta-islands logic in the final patch, they deferred to Taylor’s expertise and the empirical performance data provided in the v3 submission. That data—showing dramatic speedups (up to 99.3% reduction in server time) while preserving path-walk’s compression benefits—was the decisive factor in approving the series.

No further discussion or revisions are expected. The topic will appear in the next "What’s cooking" report and will graduate to `master` after its base topics (`ds/path-walk-filters` and `ps/clang-w-glibc-2.43-and-_Generic`) land. This marks the end of a long-standing limitation in `git pack-objects` and completes the integration of bitmap acceleration with path-based traversal.
