This is a brief follow-up in the macOS linker warning suppression thread, now providing direct confirmation from Paolo Bonzini—the original author of the Meson commit that inspired Git’s approach. Paolo endorses the linker flag suppression (`-Wl,-no_warn_duplicate_libraries`) as the right solution for both Git and Meson, calling the warning "mostly a nuisance" and agreeing with Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier suggestion that it should be disabled unconditionally in Meson builds. He explicitly advises against complicating Git’s `meson.build` with version checks, reinforcing the architectural soundness of the simple, unconditional approach already taken in Meson. The email does not alter the status of Harald Nordgren’s v3 patch (which remains ready for merging) but strengthens the precedent for extending the same suppression to Meson builds in a future follow-up. No new technical details or action items emerge; the thread’s focus remains on the Makefile-only patch.
