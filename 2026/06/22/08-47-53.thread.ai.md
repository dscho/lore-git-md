Core problem or goal
Generalize the `reprepare()` callback in the ODB subsystem into a more flexible `prepare()` callback that can optionally discard caches. This change enables `git grep` to work generically with pluggable ODB backends by allowing controlled cache invalidation during ODB preparation.
