This is a brief follow-up in the build system parity thread. Patrick Steinhardt chimes in to support the recently accepted patch that adds a `nanosec` Meson option to mirror the Autotools/Makefile `USE_NSEC` knob.

Patrick’s point is straightforward: the option name `-Dnanosec=true` is discoverable enough. Running `meson setup` without arguments already lists all available build options, so users who want to explore what knobs exist will see it there. No change to the patch is needed or proposed; this is just a reassurance that the naming choice is reasonable. The thread remains on track for graduation to `next`.
