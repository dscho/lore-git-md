The patch moves the parsing of `core.logAllRefUpdates` back into the individual ref backends (files and reftable), reversing a previous refactoring that had centralized it in `repo-settings.c`.

The backends initialize the setting to `LOG_REFS_UNSET` and defer parsing until the first config read, aligning with the lazy-loading approach introduced later in the series. The patch touches nine files but introduces no behavior changes—it simply redistributes where the config is parsed, ensuring all backend-specific settings are handled consistently.

This is a preparatory step for the architectural shift in patches 8–11, where deferred config parsing becomes critical to avoiding recursion. The patch is uncontroversial and unlikely to face objections, as it merely reverts to the pre-refactoring state while paving the way for the lazy-loading design.
