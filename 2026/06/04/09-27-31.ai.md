This is a technical discussion about repository mirroring and submodule URL resolution, continuing the thread's evolution toward a general mirroring solution. Simon Richter responds to Jeff King's (Peff) earlier proposal to use `url.*.insteadOf` for mirroring rather than introducing UUIDs.

Simon agrees with the URL-based approach in principle but raises two key concerns about Peff's suggestion: security implications of servers dictating `insteadOf` settings, and the instability of canonical URLs tied to specific hosting providers. He emphasizes their Debian packaging use case where CI builds need to fetch submodules exclusively from local mirrors for environmental efficiency.

The email highlights the tension between decentralized repository identity and practical needs of organizations like Debian that require deterministic mirroring. Simon's position suggests that while `insteadOf` solves some technical aspects, it may not fully address the administrative and security requirements of large-scale mirroring deployments. The discussion continues to explore how to balance Git's decentralized nature with real-world constraints of institutional users.
