This is part 7 of an 8-patch series improving test infrastructure, specifically addressing TAP output validation and CI consistency issues. The series is now in its third iteration (v3) from Patrick Steinhardt.

The current patch focuses on silencing asynchronous shell job-control notifications that appear when killing the p4d watchdog process during git-p4 tests. These notifications, while currently harmless, would break strict TAP protocol validation being introduced later in the series. The fix:

1. Adds a `wait` call to synchronously reap the watchdog process
2. Redirects stderr to suppress the kill notification
3. Deduplicates cleanup logic between `stop_p4d_and_watchdog()` and `stop_and_cleanup_p4d()`

This is a mechanical change that follows naturally from the series' goal of making test output reliably parseable. The patch builds on earlier work to standardize CI environments and enforce strict TAP validation, with all changes being well-justified and focused on technical debt in the test infrastructure.

The series as a whole has been reviewed by Jeff King (Peff) and appears ready for merging, with this patch representing one of the final pieces in ensuring consistent, reliable test output across platforms.
