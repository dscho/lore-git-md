This is a follow-up to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" report, where Jeff King identifies a test failure pattern in CI related to the TAP output fixes from Patrick Steinhardt's series. The failures occur in dockerized environments with minimal locale setups, showing warnings about locale settings being misinterpreted as invalid TAP output.

Jeff notes these warnings were likely present before but only now being caught due to the stricter TAP parsing. He raises the question of whether this indicates the tests were never working as intended on these platforms or if it's just harmless noise that's newly visible. The observation suggests the test fixes may need adjustment to handle these edge cases in constrained environments.
