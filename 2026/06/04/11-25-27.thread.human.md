Patrick Steinhardt has completed a 16-part series converting Git's packed object storage to use `struct odb_source`, a key step in the ongoing ODB abstraction effort that will enable pluggable storage backends. The series removes all remaining dependencies on the files backend while maintaining identical runtime behavior, touching 41 files with over 1,600 lines changed. 

Karthik Nayak has now reviewed patch 2/16, raising a substantive architectural question about whether `struct packfile_list` and related types should remain in the new `odb/source-packed.h` header or stay in `packfile.h` since they're also used by the transport layer. This is more than just surface-level feedback - it's a meaningful consideration about subsystem boundaries that could influence how subsequent patches organize these cross-cutting concerns. 

The series as a whole remains otherwise unreviewed, though Karthik had previously confirmed the mechanical renames in patch 1 looked good. Patrick has indicated this is an incremental step, with some cleanups deferred to avoid scope creep. The work builds on previous ODB abstraction patches and interacts with several other efforts including reftable and MIDX optimizations. With the technical implementation now covering all required callbacks and MIDX interfaces, the series appears ready for maintainer consideration pending resolution of this header organization question and a few other minor loose ends.
