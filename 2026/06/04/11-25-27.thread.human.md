Patrick Steinhardt has completed a 16-part series that fully decouples Git's packed object storage from the files backend as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The series implements all required `odb_source` callbacks and removes final dependencies, enabling future pluggable storage backends while maintaining current behavior. Changes span 41 files with over 1,600 lines modified, though the runtime behavior remains identical throughout.

Karthik Nayak provided a brief first review on the initial patch, which performs a mechanical rename of `struct packfile_store` to `struct odb_source_packed`. His surface-level review noted the rename looked good and correctly anticipated related function name updates in later commits. This exchange represents typical early-series acknowledgment rather than deep technical engagement. The series now stands complete but unreviewed, with some minor loose ends around chdir notification handoff and midx/repack integration details. It builds on Patrick's previous ODB work and forms foundations for future storage backends, interacting with both the reftable effort and geometric repacking work.
