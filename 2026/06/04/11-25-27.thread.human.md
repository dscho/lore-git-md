Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series is now mechanically complete, with Justin Tobler’s final review approving the last step in converting packed object storage to a standalone `struct odb_source`. The series has successfully decoupled the packed backend from its parent source, aligning its interface with the loose source’s precedent. Key changes include the removal of the `files` parent pointer and the adoption of a direct constructor signature for `odb_source_packed_new()`, which now mirrors the loose source’s design.

Despite this progress, the series remains blocked by deeper refdb infrastructure issues that surfaced during implementation—memory leaks, duplicate refdb creation paths, and initialization ordering problems. Patrick is preparing an 11-patch fix series to address these, but it depends on the prior merging of the "ps/setup-centralize-odb-creation" series. While the mechanical changes (including type system consistency, callback wiring, and MIDX interface refactoring) have received surface-level approval from Justin and Karthik Nayak, there’s been no substantive review of the refdb issues, callback logic, or broader architectural implications. The path forward hinges on resolving these foundational problems before the ODB abstraction can proceed.
