Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series continues to evolve, now at its second version with 17 patches aimed at decoupling packed object storage. The series was initially complete but exposed deeper refdb infrastructure problems—memory leaks, duplicate refdb creation, and initialization ordering issues—that now require an 11-patch fix series. However, the broader refdb issues remain unaddressed in review, and the series is blocked until the dependent "ps/setup-centralize-odb-creation" work is merged.

Justin’s latest feedback on patch 4/17 stays within the same narrow scope, noting the type change in the packed ODB source’s parent pointer and its consistency with the loose source. He raises a question about whether the loose source will follow suit but doesn’t engage with the refdb problems Patrick has identified. Those issues—particularly the memory leak of `struct repository::refs_private` and the chicken-and-egg initialization logic—are still the critical blocker, and no other reviewers have weighed in on them. The path handling architecture also remains a concern, though it’s now overshadowed by the refdb findings.

The series is at a standstill until the refdb fixes are ready, and even then, they’ll need to be sequenced with other infrastructure changes. For now, the ODB abstraction work is technically complete but functionally stalled, with only superficial review of the preparatory patches. The thread’s focus has shifted from the original goal to the refdb cleanup, but that effort hasn’t yet gained traction.
