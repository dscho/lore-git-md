The final patch removes the last remaining dependency on the files backend, making packed storage fully standalone. The changes span 37 files with about 1600 lines modified, following Patrick's characteristic incremental and careful approach to architectural changes.

This represents a significant step forward in Git's storage architecture, though the series hasn't yet received review feedback. The changes pave the way for future work on alternative storage backends while keeping current functionality intact.
