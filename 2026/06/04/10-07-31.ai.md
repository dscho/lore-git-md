CI standardization patch in a test infrastructure series.

This is the first of eight patches in Patrick Steinhardt's v3 series aimed at standardizing CI environments between GitLab and GitHub while fixing various test output issues. The patch mechanically reorders the Linux job definitions in GitLab CI to match GitHub's workflow order, making direct comparison between the two CI systems easier. 

The change is purely organizational - no job configurations are altered, just their sequence in the YAML file. This establishes consistency that will help maintain parity between the two CI setups going forward. The patch is straightforward and uncontroversial, serving as groundwork for the more substantive CI standardization and test fixes that follow in the series.
