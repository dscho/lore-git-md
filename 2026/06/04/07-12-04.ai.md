Performance optimization for index-pack's delta resolution.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Arijit Banerjee's v3 patch, which refined the delta base caching strategy in `index-pack`. The key change from v2 was adding precise tracking of when cached base data can be released, addressing Peff's earlier concern about memory efficiency. Peff confirms through testing that the performance difference between v2 and v3 is negligible in practice, with no measurable speed impact on either git.git or linux.git repositories.

The discussion suggests the optimization's benefits are most visible in scenarios with high cache pressure from large files, but even with artificially constrained cache sizes, the effect remains minimal. Peff notes a possible 1% slowdown with tiny caches, possibly due to thread contention from the release logic, but considers this within noise levels. He expresses satisfaction with either version, indicating the series has addressed all technical concerns. The patch appears merge-ready, having balanced performance gains with careful memory management while passing all tests.
