CI infrastructure patch adding missing Linux test environments to GitLab CI.

This patch (2/8 in the series) aligns GitLab CI with GitHub workflows by adding AlmaLinux 8 and Debian 11 test jobs, matching the platforms already tested in GitHub Actions. The changes are straightforward: new job definitions in `.gitlab-ci.yml` and a corresponding update to `ci/lib.sh` to recognize these platforms. The AlmaLinux job will run until 2029-05-31 (RHEL 8 EOL) and Debian 11 until its EOL in 2026-08-31.

This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's broader effort to standardize test environments across CI providers, ensuring consistent test coverage. The patch follows the same pattern as existing job definitions and appears mechanically sound. No technical concerns are apparent - this is a simple but important gap-filler in the CI infrastructure.
