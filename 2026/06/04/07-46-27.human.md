This is patch 3/8 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring repository initialization and object database setup, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch has already gone through multiple rounds of review and is now in its final form.

The key change here is deduplicating the logic for applying repository format settings by introducing a new `apply_repository_format()` function. This centralizes what was previously spread across three different call sites, making the code more maintainable and providing a single point where new extensions can be added. The patch also renames functions to better reflect their actual behavior - `read_and_verify_repository_format()` becomes `read_repository_format_from_commondir()` and `check_repository_format()` becomes `check_and_apply_repository_format()`.

The changes are internal plumbing improvements with no user-visible behavior changes. The series has received thorough review from Karthik Nayak and appears ready for merging, with this patch representing a clean architectural improvement that will support future ODB work.
