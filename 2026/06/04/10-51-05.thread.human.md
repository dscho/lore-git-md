Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) has been working on a series to improve Git's handling of large objects (4GB+) on Windows, where `size_t` is 64-bit but `unsigned long` remains 32-bit. The 8-patch series systematically converts object size handling to use `size_t` across multiple subsystems, addressing MSVC compatibility, delta handling, packfile operations, and the ODB API. 

The series has gone through thorough review from Patrick Steinhardt, with all technical feedback addressed in v2. Key changes include removing transitional scaffolding, simplifying delta handling code, and implementing a cleaner approach in `cat-file.c` as suggested by Steinhardt. The patches touch 72 files but maintain backward compatibility where needed, particularly for subsystems like `fast-import` that haven't been fully converted yet.

Junio Hamano has now acknowledged the series and indicated he will queue it for potential inclusion before the rc1 release, pending his final review. The mention of queueing suggests the changes are likely acceptable, though the maintainer wants to personally verify given the wide-ranging impact. With the technical discussion resolved and merge conflicts from the `ps/odb-source-loose` series addressed, this appears ready to move forward in the integration process.
