This is a test infrastructure patch that addresses Windows-specific test cleanup issues, part of Patrick Steinhardt's larger series improving TAP output validation and CI consistency.

The patch specifically targets EBUSY errors that occur during test directory cleanup on Windows, where processes (like the fsmonitor daemon in Scalar-related tests) may still hold file handles open. Rather than fixing all potential process leaks (which the author notes would be complex), this pragmatic solution simply silences the error output from `rm -rf` during cleanup while maintaining the retry logic. The change prevents these error messages from breaking the TAP output format.

The patch is narrowly focused - just two lines changing `rm -fr` calls to redirect stderr to /dev/null - and fits cleanly into the series' broader goal of making test output more reliable. It's a straightforward workaround for a known Windows-specific issue that has been causing test failures in the CI environment.
