This is a response in the ongoing discussion about mirror repositories, specifically addressing the Debian packaging use case. Simon Richter from Debian weighs in with practical concerns about archiving projects that use submodules, where current approaches either don't provide proper mirroring or require intrusive symlink solutions.

The email highlights a real-world scenario where the proposed mirror functionality would be valuable - Debian's need to maintain complete, self-contained archives of upstream projects including their submodules. Simon describes the current limitations where relative URLs don't provide proper mirroring control and absolute URLs break archive self-containment.

While acknowledging Junio's preference for a general repository solution rather than submodule-specific approach, Simon's use case suggests there may be unique submodule requirements worth considering. The proposed symlink-based workaround is acknowledged as imperfect but illustrates the kind of solution Debian currently lacks.

This response adds important context about actual deployment needs that could help shape the feature's design, whether implemented generally or specifically for submodules. The mention of Debian's mirror infrastructure being widely reused (like their current tarball mirrors) suggests the solution's impact could extend beyond just Debian use case.
