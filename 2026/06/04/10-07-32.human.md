This is a CI infrastructure patch adding missing Linux test environments to GitLab CI, part of Patrick Steinhardt's larger series standardizing test infrastructure across platforms.

The patch adds AlmaLinux 8 and Debian 11 test jobs to GitLab CI to match the existing GitHub workflows, closing a gap where these distributions were tested in one CI system but not the other. The changes are straightforward: new job definitions in `.gitlab-ci.yml` and a corresponding update to `ci/lib.sh` to recognize these distros. The AlmaLinux image will be supported until 2029, Debian 11 until 2026.

This mechanical change is part of the series' broader goal to ensure consistent test behavior across CI providers by aligning their environments. The patch appears uncontroversial and well-justified as part of the infrastructure standardization effort.
