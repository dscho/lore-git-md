This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's expanded v3 series standardizing CI environments between GitLab and GitHub. The patch makes a purely mechanical change - reordering the Linux job definitions in GitLab CI to match GitHub's job ordering. This makes it easier to visually compare the CI configurations across both services.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, touching only the `.gitlab-ci.yml` file to rearrange existing job definitions without modifying their content. This sets up the foundation for the subsequent CI standardization patches in the series. The patch has no functional impact on test execution - it's purely about maintainability and cross-platform consistency.
