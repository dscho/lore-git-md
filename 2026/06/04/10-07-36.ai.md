This is a test infrastructure patch that silences EBUSY errors during test cleanup on Windows, part of Patrick Steinhardt's v3 series improving TAP output validation and CI consistency.

The patch addresses a Windows-specific issue where test cleanup (`rm -rf`) fails with EBUSY errors when processes (like the fsmonitor daemon in Scalar tests) hold files open. These failures were breaking TAP output format. Rather than fixing all potential process leaks, the pragmatic solution redirects stderr from the cleanup commands to suppress the error messages while maintaining the retry logic. The change is minimal (just adding `2>/dev/null` to two `rm` calls) and well-contained to `t/test-lib.sh`.

This is one of several fixes in the series that collectively improve test reliability across platforms. The patch builds on the thread's established context of TAP output validation and CI standardization, with the Windows-specific cleanup being a known pain point in the test infrastructure. The approach balances correctness with practicality, acknowledging that fully fixing all process leaks would be more invasive.
