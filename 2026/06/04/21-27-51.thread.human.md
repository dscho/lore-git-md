This thread resolved a bug in Git’s trailer parsing where URLs in commit messages were incorrectly treated as trailers, causing mangled output in `--only-trailers` and `%(trailers:only)` formatting. Kristoffer Haugsbakk reported the issue with three real-world cases, and Jeff King (Peff) identified the root cause: Git’s loose trailer matching across all operations. The fix, implemented by Kristoffer, modifies `trailer.c` to exclude lines containing `://` from trailer parsing, a solution praised by Junio C Hamano as "much more robust" than alternatives.

The patch went through three versions, with the final version incorporating Peff’s readability improvements and Junio’s review feedback. It includes comprehensive test coverage for all three bug scenarios and was merged to `master` on July 12, 2024. Junio’s latest reply confirms the patch is now in the `next` integration branch, marking the thread as effectively closed. No further changes are expected.
