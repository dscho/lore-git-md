This thread fixes a bug in Git’s trailer parsing where URLs in commit messages were incorrectly treated as trailers, causing mangled output in `--only-trailers` and `%(trailers:only)` formatting. Kristoffer Haugsbakk identified three real-world cases—non-trailer URLs getting corrupted, line-wrapped URLs creating false trailers, and patch processing introducing bad trailers—and found 245 instances in the Linux kernel history.

Jeff King (Peff) diagnosed the issue as Git’s overly permissive trailer matching and proposed a targeted fix: modifying `find_separator()` in `trailer.c` to exclude lines containing `://` from trailer parsing. Kristoffer implemented this approach in a patch that updates the logic, adds comprehensive tests for all three bug scenarios, and updates documentation. The solution is minimal, well-documented, and aligns with Peff’s analysis.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch, praising the `://` heuristic as more robust than a scheme whitelist and confirming the safety of the pointer arithmetic. His feedback was clarifying rather than critical, and he concluded with approval ("OK. Makes sense to me. Thanks."). The patch is now poised for integration, with no open objections or unresolved technical concerns. The changes are confined to `trailer.c` and its tests, and the fix preserves legitimate trailer parsing while eliminating false positives.
