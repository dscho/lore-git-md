This is part 7 of an 8-patch series improving test infrastructure reliability, specifically addressing TAP output validation and CI consistency issues. The series is now in its third iteration (v3) from Patrick Steinhardt.

The current patch focuses on silencing asynchronous shell job-control messages that appear when killing the Perforce daemon (p4d) and its watchdog during git-p4 tests. These messages, while harmless currently, would interfere with strict TAP protocol validation being introduced in the series. The fix uses `wait` to synchronously reap the processes and suppress the diagnostic output.

Key technical details:
- Modifies `t/lib-git-p4.sh` to properly wait for process termination
- Deduplicates cleanup logic between two related functions
- Maintains existing functionality while preventing TAP output corruption
- The change is mechanical and well-contained
- Follows the series' theme of making test output more reliable
- Builds on previous git-p4 test modernization efforts
- Part of broader CI/test infrastructure improvements

The patch appears straightforward and uncontroversial, addressing a clear technical need in the test suite. It's one piece of a larger effort to standardize test behavior across platforms and catch output issues earlier.
