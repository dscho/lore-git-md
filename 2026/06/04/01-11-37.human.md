This is a documentation thread clarification from Junio C Hamano regarding email threading models in Git's contribution workflow.

Junio confirms SZEDER Gábor's technical explanation of Git's threading terminology, emphasizing that:
- Shallow threading means all patches in a series reply to the current cover letter
- Deep threading means each email replies to the previous one in sequence
- Git's practice prefers shallow threading within a series iteration

Junio notes that while some contributors have used alternative threading patterns (like having later iterations reply to the initial cover letter), the project's clear preference is for each iteration's cover letter to reply to the immediately previous iteration's cover letter. He expresses mild concern about potential tooling compatibility with non-standard threading approaches, though acknowledges the current lore.kernel.org archive handles them without issue.

This email represents the maintainer's authoritative confirmation of the threading model that should be documented in the b4 configuration series. It resolves any remaining ambiguity from earlier discussion while leaving open the question of how strictly to enforce the preferred threading pattern across all tooling. The exchange demonstrates the project's careful attention to workflow details that affect both human readability and tool interoperability.
