This thread is an RFC patch series from Taylor Blau proposing to combine `git repack --geometric` and `--cruft` modes, which are currently mutually exclusive. The goal is to let repositories maintain a geometric progression of reachable packs while collecting unreachable objects into cruft packs, streamlining maintenance workflows. The series introduces `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` for precise reachability filtering and a `--refs-snapshot` option to ensure consistency between `git pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer.

The series is now complete with all 10 patches submitted, but it remains in RFC stage and unmerged. Junio C Hamano’s review of patch 8/10 identified a **non-trivial correctness issue** in the two-phase traversal logic of `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable`, which risks retaining unreachable tags and objects and undermines the series’ core goal. Taylor acknowledged the flaw and proposed reversing the traversal order to prioritize reachability from refs, but no concrete implementation has been submitted yet. A minor logical detail in patch 8/10 was clarified and confirmed correct, and Junio also suggested an efficiency improvement for patch 4/10, which Taylor accepted.

Junio’s recent follow-up asks whether the series has seen further polishing since the v1 submission on July 17, or if it was already considered complete. No new patches or revisions have been posted, and the correctness issue remains unresolved. The question is procedural, probing whether Taylor intends to iterate further or if the series is on hold. The integration phase (patch 10/10) wires together the preparatory work, but the complexity of combining geometric and cruft logic may surface additional edge cases, particularly around MIDX retention, reachability traversal, and pack selection.
