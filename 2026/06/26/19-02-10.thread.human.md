Here’s the human-readable summary for a developer catching up on the thread:

This thread is an RFC patch series from Taylor Blau that lets `git repack` combine `--geometric` and `--cruft` modes, which are currently incompatible. The goal is to allow repositories to maintain a geometric progression of reachable packs while collecting unreachable objects into cruft packs in a single operation. The series introduces `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` to filter objects by reachability during pack generation and fixes a critical consistency issue between `git pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer by adding `--refs-snapshot=<path>`.

The series is now complete with all 10 patches submitted, but it’s still in the RFC stage and unmerged. Junio C Hamano’s review of patch 8/10 uncovered a **non-trivial correctness issue** in the two-phase traversal logic of `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable`: the current implementation risks retaining unreachable tags and objects, which undermines the series’ core goal of cleanly separating reachable and unreachable objects. Taylor acknowledged the flaw and proposed reversing the traversal order to prioritize reachability from refs, but no concrete fix has been submitted yet. This is the main blocker for the series moving forward.

Taylor also responded to Junio’s feedback on patch 4/10, which teaches the MIDX retention logic to skip packs included in the geometric rollup. He accepted Junio’s suggestion to rewrite a helper function for better efficiency and readability, but the patch remains a low-risk refactoring. A minor pre-existing quirk in the MIDX retention logic—where malformed pack basenames are inconsistently handled—was noted but not addressed, as it’s not a regression introduced by this series.

The rest of the series (patches 1–7 and 9) consists of preparatory refactorings and is considered low-risk. The integration patch (10/10) wires everything together, enabling `--geometric` and `--cruft` to work simultaneously. Eight new tests cover basic functionality, edge cases, and interactions with other repack options. The series is part of the ongoing repack machinery overhaul, building on prior work like geometric repacking and cruft packs. Given the timing (submitted during the -rc phase) and the complexity of the reachability fix, further iterations are likely before this lands. Taylor’s upcoming role change may also affect follow-up bandwidth.
