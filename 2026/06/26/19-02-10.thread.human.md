This thread is an RFC patch series from Taylor Blau proposing to combine Git’s `--geometric` and `--cruft` repack modes, which are currently mutually exclusive. The goal is to let repositories maintain a geometric progression of reachable packs while simultaneously collecting unreachable objects into cruft packs, streamlining maintenance workflows. The series introduces `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` to enable precise reachability filtering during pack generation and a `--refs-snapshot` option to ensure consistency between `git pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer.

The series is now complete with all 10 patches submitted, but it remains in the RFC stage and unmerged. Junio C Hamano’s review identified a **non-trivial correctness issue** in the two-phase traversal logic of `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable`, which risks retaining unreachable tags and objects, undermining the series’ core goal. Taylor acknowledged the flaw and proposed reversing the traversal order to prioritize reachability from refs, but no concrete implementation has been submitted yet. A minor clarification in this thread confirmed that the conditional `if (type != OBJ_COMMIT && type != OBJ_TAG)` in patch 8/10 was correct, resolving a surface-level concern but leaving the larger correctness issue unresolved.

The series touches several subsystems, including repack machinery, pack-objects, MIDX retention logic, and cruft pack generation. It includes 8 new tests covering basic functionality, loose unreachable objects, pack deletion behavior, and interactions with `--combine-cruft-below-size` and `--expire-to`. While patches 1/10–7/10 and 9/10 are low-risk refactorings or preparatory work, patches 8/10 and 10/10 may attract further scrutiny due to their complexity and potential impact on repack workflows. The series is part of the ongoing repack machinery overhaul and builds on prior work in geometric repack and cruft pack logic. Further iteration is expected to address the reachability flaw before the series can progress toward merging.
