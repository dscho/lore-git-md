This is a **performance optimization patch** in the commit-reach subsystem, part of a post-merge follow-up series (v3) that builds on already-merged v2 work. The patch implements early termination of `paint_down_to_common()` when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, yielding 100-1000x speedups for asymmetric histories. However, the author **accidentally introduced a regression** in this version by removing a `min_generation` check that was necessary for correct behavior in certain commit-graph modes.

The issue is mechanical: the patch deletes the line `if (min_generation && generation > last_gen)` and replaces it with `if (generation > last_gen)`, unconditionally widening the generation-monotonicity check. This breaks correctness when `min_generation` is not set, as the check now fires in cases where it should not. The author acknowledges the mistake and plans to rework the logic to preserve the original behavior while still enabling the side-exhaustion optimization.

The rest of the series (patches 1-6/8 and 8/8) remains unaffected and is considered stable. Those patches add edge-case tests, trace2 instrumentation, state refactoring, dead-code removal, and documentation. The regression is isolated to this patch and does not impact the broader optimization’s design or safety guarantees. Reviewers can still provide feedback on the non-broken parts of the patch, such as the early-exit condition logic or the widened BUG assertion.

Key technical details:
- **Subsystem**: `commit-reach.c` (merge-base calculation).
- **Behavior change**: Early termination when `(!state->p1_count || !state->p2_count) && !state->pending_merge_bases && last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`.
- **Regression**: Unconditional generation-monotonicity check breaks correctness in commit-graph modes where `min_generation` is not set.
- **Files touched**: `commit-reach.c`, `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`, `t/t6600-test-reach.sh`.
- **Test coverage**: Deterministic step-count assertions for all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat), but the regression causes failures in some modes.

The patch is **not ready for merging** in its current form, but the underlying optimization is sound. The author’s plan to rework the logic suggests a quick turnaround for a corrected version. Reviewers may focus on the early-exit condition or the accompanying documentation, which explains the side-exhaustion optimization and its safety guarantees.
