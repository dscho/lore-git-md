This is the fourth patch in Taylor Blau’s ten-patch RFC series to combine `git repack --geometric` and `--cruft`. It teaches the MIDX retention logic to skip packs that are part of the geometric rollup, ensuring that packs below the geometric split line remain eligible for deletion even if they are referenced by older MIDX layers.

The patch adds two new helpers in `repack.c`: `pack_geometry_contains_pack()` checks whether a given pack basename is present in a list of packs, and `pack_geometry_contains_rollup()` uses that to determine whether the pack is part of the geometric rollup (either in the regular or promisor pack list). The main function, `existing_packs_retain_midx_packs()`, is updated to take a `pack_geometry` parameter and now skips any pack that is part of the rollup, preventing it from being marked as retained.

The change is confined to the MIDX retention logic and does not alter current behavior, since geometric repacks do not yet use the `existing_packs` deletion path. The patch touches `builtin/repack.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack.h`, and is a prerequisite for later patches that will wire up the geometric split to the cruft pack machinery. It is a well-scoped refactoring with clear motivation and no known edge cases at this stage.
