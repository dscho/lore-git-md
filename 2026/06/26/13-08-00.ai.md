This is a **test patch** in the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series, specifically part three of eight in v3. It adds regression tests for the side-exhaustion optimization introduced in earlier patches, ensuring the code correctly handles cases where one merge-base candidate is an ancestor of another and where the walk crosses from INFINITY-generation regions into finite-generation regions.

The patch creates a new test script, `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, which verifies that `git merge-base --all` correctly excludes redundant merge-base candidates when one is an ancestor of another. The test constructs a commit graph where two merge-base candidates (B and C) exist, but B is an ancestor of C. The side-exhaustion optimization may exit early before stale propagation removes the ancestor, but the `remove_redundant` safety net in `get_merge_bases_many_0` ensures only B is returned. The test covers both `--all` and single-merge-base modes, and includes a variant without the commit-graph to confirm the optimization does not fire when generation numbers are INFINITY.

Additionally, the patch extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with a test for mixed finite/INFINITY generation numbers. This test creates a commit outside the commit-graph (INFINITY generation) and verifies that the walk correctly transitions from the INFINITY region (where side-exhaustion is disabled) into the finite region (where it can fire). The test uses the `ps-*` orphan topology to avoid polluting the grid-based rev-list tests.

The tests are **deterministic and self-verifying**, using `test_all_modes` to run against all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat). The new script is added to `t/meson.build` to ensure it is included in the test suite.
