This is a **surface-level review** of patch 5/8 in the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series. René Scharfe raises minor stylistic and type-safety questions about the new `struct paint_state` introduced in this patch, which replaces the old `nonstale_queue` with per-side counters (`p1_count`, `p2_count`, `pending_merge_bases`).

The review focuses on two points:
1. **Type choice**: Whether the counters should use `size_t` instead of `int` to match `prio_queue.nr` and avoid potential negative values. This is a low-risk suggestion; the counters are only incremented/decremented in controlled ways, but `size_t` would be more idiomatic for queue sizes.
2. **Naming consistency**: René suggests longer, more explicit names (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `pending_merge_base_count` or alternatives like `both_count`) to improve readability and align with the `PARENT1`/`PARENT2` flags. The current names are abbreviated (`p1_count`, `p2_count`), which may be less intuitive for future readers.

The feedback is **non-blocking** and purely about code clarity and style. The patch itself is mechanical and low-risk, converting `paint_down_to_common()` to use `paint_state` while preserving behavior. René does not question the correctness or design of the counters, only their presentation. The author (Kristofer Karlsson) may choose to address these suggestions in a future iteration or leave them as-is, as they do not affect functionality.
