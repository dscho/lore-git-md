He questions whether this guard is the right approach, suggesting it might mask bugs rather than expose them. Junio implies that the current guard is premature and may hinder debugging by hiding issues that should be surfacing as BUG() assertions. He suggests letting `repo_config_values()` catch offending callers now, even if it means temporary breakage, to ensure the codebase evolves correctly.

This is a **critical design discussion** for the libification effort, as it affects how Git handles repository initialization and memory management. The outcome could influence whether similar guards are used in future patches.
