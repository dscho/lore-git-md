This is a **test patch** in the ongoing `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series. It adds regression tests for the side-exhaustion optimization in two scenarios: when multiple merge-base candidates exist and one is an ancestor of another, and when one commit has a finite generation number while the other is outside the commit-graph (INFINITY generation).

The patch introduces a new test script, `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, which verifies that `git merge-base --all` correctly excludes redundant merge-base candidates when one is an ancestor of another. The test constructs a specific commit graph topology where `B` and `C` are both merge bases of `A` and `X`, but `B` is an ancestor of `C`. The test confirms that only `B` appears in the output, exercising both the side-exhaustion optimization and the `remove_redundant` safety net. It also includes a control case without the commit-graph to ensure the optimization does not fire when generation numbers are INFINITY.

Additionally, the patch extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with a test for mixed finite/INFINITY generation numbers. This test creates a commit outside the commit-graph (INFINITY generation) and verifies that the walk correctly transitions from the INFINITY region (where side-exhaustion is disabled) to the finite region (where it can fire). The test uses the `ps-*` orphan topology to avoid interfering with other rev-list tests.
