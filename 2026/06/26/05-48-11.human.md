This is the first patch in Toon Claes’s v5 series introducing `--linearize` support for `git replay`. It is a pure refactoring: the existing `mapped_commit()` function is renamed to `get_mapped_commit()` and paired with a new helper, `put_mapped_commit()`, that encapsulates the khash OID-map update logic. The change is small (20 insertions, 11 deletions) and touches only `replay.c`.

The patch is uncontroversial and has already been endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt as a sensible wrapper around khash’s awkward interface. It prepares the ground for the later patches by making the mapping logic clearer and more symmetric, but it does not alter any behavior. The series as a whole is now ready for merging, and this patch is the mechanical first step.
