This is the third patch in Taylor Blau’s ten-patch RFC series that aims to combine `git repack --geometric` and `--cruft` modes. The series is part of the ongoing repack machinery overhaul, and this patch prepares the deletion machinery for geometric repacks.

The patch teaches the `existing_packs` machinery to mark any non-kept packs above the geometric split line (and any promisor packs above their own split) as retained. This ensures that packs which would otherwise be deleted to restore a geometric progression are instead kept, allowing the later deletion pass to handle them uniformly. The change is purely internal: it does not alter which packs are written or removed, only which ones are marked for retention.

The implementation is straightforward: a new helper `existing_packs_retain_non_kept()` marks a single non-kept pack as retained, and `existing_packs_retain_from_geometry()` iterates over the packs above the split line and calls the helper for each. The patch touches `builtin/repack.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack.h`, adding about 30 lines of code.

This is a refactoring patch that paves the way for later integration between geometric and cruft repacks. It looks clean and well-scoped, but its value will become clearer once the follow-up patches land. No substantive reviews have appeared yet, but the patch itself is unlikely to attract controversy.
