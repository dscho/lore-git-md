This is the **seventh patch in a v3 series** (8/8 total) that optimizes Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function in the commit-reach subsystem. The series is a **post-merge follow-up**—all v2 patches are already merged, and this iteration adds cleanup, hardening, and documentation. This specific patch **implements the core optimization**: early termination of the merge-base walk when one side’s commit queue is exhausted, yielding **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories).

The patch adds an early-exit condition to `paint_down_to_common()` that triggers when either `p1_count` or `p2_count` (per-side counters introduced in patch 5/8) drops to zero, no pending merge-base candidates remain, and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`generation < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`). This ensures correctness: commits without commit-graph entries (generation `INFINITY`) are ordered by commit date, which is not topologically reliable, so the optimization only activates in the finite region where ordering guarantees hold. The patch also **widens the generation-monotonicity BUG assertion** to fire unconditionally, not just when `min_generation` is set, enforcing correct generation ordering for all callers.

Performance is validated via **trace2 step-count instrumentation** (added in patch 4/8). Benchmarks on `git.git` show dramatic improvements:
- `merge-base --all v2.0.0 v2.55.0-rc1`: 72,264 → 44,589 steps
- `merge-base --all v2.55.0-rc1 v2.55.0-rc1~5`: 110 → 7 steps
The test suite is updated to reflect the reduced step counts, with `test_paint_down_steps` assertions now expecting fewer steps in some topologies (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5 steps due to the "pop first" form of `paint_queue_get()`).

The patch touches three files:
- `commit-reach.c`: Adds the early-exit condition, updates the BUG assertion, and tweaks `paint_queue_get()` to check side exhaustion.
- `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`: Documents the new "side-exhaustion" termination condition, explaining why it’s safe to exit early and why the `INFINITY` gate is necessary.
- `t/t6600-test-reach.sh`: Adjusts expected step counts in two tests (`self` and `merge-base --all commit-walk steps`) to match the optimized behavior.

The optimization is **low-risk and well-tested**. The early-exit condition is gated by the finite-generation check, and the widened BUG assertion ensures correctness. The test suite includes **deterministic step-count assertions** for all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat), and the `run_all_modes` function captures trace2 output per mode for self-verifying performance. The series has addressed all feedback from prior iterations, including structural objections (preserving `nonstale_queue` for `ahead_behind()`) and test instrumentation concerns.

This patch is **ready for review and merging**. It is a **performance win with no regressions**, and the changes are internal to the commit-reach subsystem with no on-disk format impact. The accompanying documentation (patch 8/8) provides a comprehensive deep-dive on the algorithm, making the optimization accessible to future contributors.
