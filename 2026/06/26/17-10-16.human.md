This is the third patch in the v5 series introducing the `--linearize` option to `git replay`. Junio raises a substantive behavioral question: when linearizing a merge commit that merges two divergent branches, the current implementation drops one entire side branch, leaving its commits unreachable in the rewritten history. In a typical flattening rebase, both side branches are replayed (in some order), producing a linear history that includes all commits. Here, only one side survives, and the other is orphaned. Junio does not call this a bug but suggests it may be an inherent characteristic of the feature that should be documented and tested.

The patch is otherwise ready for integration, having addressed all prior feedback. The discussion does not block merging but may lead to a follow-up patch that either documents the current behavior or offers users control over which side branch survives. A proposed test case is included in the email to cast the behavior in stone.
