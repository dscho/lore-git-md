This is a refactoring patch series (2/2) that advances the ongoing libification effort by moving the global `excludes_file` variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is part of the broader push to eliminate global state and make Git's internals more modular and reusable.

The patch abstracts the XDG fallback lazy-loading logic for `excludes_file` out of `dir.c` into a dedicated getter function, then migrates the variable into `struct repo_config_values` with proper heap memory management. A helper function `repo_config_values_clear()` is introduced to handle cleanup. The implementation includes a temporary guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) to avoid triggering BUG() assertions in uninitialized submodules, a pattern borrowed from prior work.

The changes are mechanical but touch core files (`dir.c`, `environment.c`, `environment.h`, `repository.c`) and involve memory management, so correctness is critical. The author notes fixes from v1: a typo correction, proper initialization of `excludes_file` to NULL, and ensuring `attributes_file` is also freed in the cleanup function. The series is mentored by Christian Couder and two other contributors, suggesting it has guidance from experienced developers.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the new getter function, the `repo_config_values_clear()` helper, and the submodule guard. These are likely points reviewers will scrutinize. The patch does not change user-visible behavior but is foundational for future libification work.
