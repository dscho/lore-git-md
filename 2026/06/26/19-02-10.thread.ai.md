Subject: [RFC PATCH 0/8] repack: combine --geometric and --cruft
Series version: v1 (RFC)
Originating author: Taylor Blau
Thread type: patch series (RFC)

Core problem or goal
Allow `git repack` to combine `--geometric` and `--cruft` modes, which are currently mutually exclusive. The goal is to let repositories maintain a geometric progression of reachable packs while simultaneously collecting unreachable objects into cruft packs, streamlining maintenance workflows.

Current status
RFC stage; no prior versions. The series is structured in three phases:
1. **Refactoring phase (complete in v1)**: Patches 1/8–3/8 prepared cruft pack machinery by unifying non-kept pack exclusion logic, extracting helper functions, and teaching the repack machinery to mark packs above the geometric split line as *retained*. Patch 4/8 extends this by teaching MIDX retention logic to skip packs that are part of the geometric rollup, ensuring packs below the split line remain eligible for deletion. No behavior changes yet; purely internal reorganization.
2. Remaining phases: (a) Introduce `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` to `git pack-objects` (patch 7/8), and (b) wire it into `git repack` and teach the cruft writer to interpret the geometric split (patch 8/8).

Key technical details
- New CLI option: `git pack-objects --stdin-packs=follow-reachable` (planned in patch 7/8; walks from reference tips to include only reachable objects from selected packs while respecting excluded packs).
- Integration with `--refs-snapshot` to ensure consistency between `pack-objects` and MIDX bitmap writer.
- Files touched: `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `builtin/repack.c`, `repack.c`, `repack.h`, `repack-cruft.c`, `repack-geometry.c`.
- New helpers:
  - `existing_packs_retain_non_kept()` and `existing_packs_retain_from_geometry()` (patch 3/8).
  - `locate_existing_pack()` (patch 2/8).
  - `pack_geometry_contains_pack()` and `pack_geometry_contains_rollup()` (patch 4/8), used to determine whether a pack is part of the geometric rollup.
- MIDX retention logic now takes a `pack_geometry` parameter to skip rollup packs.
- New tests: `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` (patch 7/8), `t/t7704-repack-cruft.sh` (patch 8/8).
- Subsystems: repack machinery, pack-objects, MIDX retention logic, cruft pack generation, geometric repack logic.
- No on-disk format changes; behavior change confined to repack workflows.
- **Refactoring specifics**:
  - Patch 1/8 unified non-kept pack exclusion in `repack-cruft.c` by moving the `for_each_string_list_item` loop outside conditional blocks.
  - Patch 2/8 extracted `locate_existing_pack()` from `existing_packs_retain_cruft()` in `repack.c`.
  - Patch 3/8 added `existing_packs_retain_from_geometry()` to mark packs above the geometric split line as retained.
  - Patch 4/8 updated `existing_packs_retain_midx_packs()` to skip packs in the geometric rollup, using the new `pack_geometry_contains_*` helpers.
