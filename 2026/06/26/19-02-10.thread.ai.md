Core problem or goal
Allow `git repack` to combine `--geometric` and `--cruft` modes, which are currently mutually exclusive. The goal is to let repositories maintain a geometric progression of reachable packs while simultaneously collecting unreachable objects into cruft packs, streamlining maintenance workflows. The series introduces `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` to enable precise reachability filtering during pack generation, ensuring geometric repacks can coexist with cruft pack collection. A critical correctness fix ensures consistency between `git pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer by introducing `--refs-snapshot=<path>`, preventing reference updates from causing reachable objects to be incorrectly placed in cruft packs.

Current status
RFC stage; no prior versions. The series is now complete with all 10 patches submitted. The integration phase (patch 10/10) wires together the preparatory work, enabling `--geometric` and `--cruft` to work simultaneously. The patch relaxes the `ALL_INTO_ONE` implication of `--cruft` when `--geometric` is given, plumbs `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` into `git repack`, and updates the cruft writer to respect the geometric split. The refs snapshot is used to synchronize reachability between `pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer. The series is fully implemented but unmerged, pending review and potential iterations. Junio C Hamano's review of patch 8/10 identifies a **non-trivial correctness issue** in the two-phase traversal logic of `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable`, which could undermine the series' core goal of separating reachable and unreachable objects.

Key technical details
- **New CLI behavior**: `--cruft` no longer implies `-a` when combined with `--geometric`.
- **Reachability filtering**: `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` ensures only reachable objects from rolled-up packs are included in the geometric pack, leaving unreachable objects for the cruft writer. The implementation uses a two-phase traversal: first marking objects in included packs, then walking from reference tips. Junio's review highlights a flaw in this logic, where *all* tag objects found in included packs (and loose objects, if `--unpacked` is given) are marked and added to the pending list, risking retention of unreachable tags and objects reachable only from those tags.
- **Cruft pack generation**: The cruft writer (`write_cruft_pack()`) interprets the geometric split, treating packs above the split as reachable (excluded from cruft) and those below as candidates for cruft collection. Promisor packs are handled similarly, with their own split point.
- **Refs snapshot**: Used to ensure reachability consistency between `pack-objects` and the MIDX bitmap writer.
