This is the fifth patch in Taylor Blau’s ten-patch RFC series to combine `git repack --geometric` and `--cruft`. It teaches geometric repacks to use the common `existing_packs` deletion machinery instead of the ad-hoc `pack_geometry_remove_redundant()` function.

The patch is a small but important refactoring step. It removes the special-case deletion logic for geometric packs and routes everything through the same mark-then-remove path used by all-into-one repacks. When a geometric repack is run without `--combine-cruft-below-size`, it now explicitly retains all cruft packs before marking anything for deletion, ensuring they are not removed. The change is confined to `builtin/repack.c` and `repack.c`, with no new tests or user-visible behavior.

This is a clean, low-risk refactoring that aligns geometric repacks with the rest of the repack machinery. It prepares the ground for the later patches that introduce `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` and wire everything together. The commit message is clear and the diff is straightforward, making the change easy to review. No substantive concerns have been raised yet, but this patch is part of a larger, complex effort, so its correctness will depend on the follow-up work.
