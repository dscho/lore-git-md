Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating additional global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series builds on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration (v6, now in `next`) and extends the pattern to nine variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program` (patch 5/9). The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author raises two RFC questions: (1) whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`.

Current status
The series is **under review** as a follow-up to the merged `excludes_file` work. The first two patches overlap with the prior series (introducing `repo_config_values_clear()` and migrating `excludes_file`), but are re-split for clarity. Patches 3-9/9 migrate `editor_program`, `pager_program`, `askpass_program`, `attributes_file`, `push_default`, `autorebase`, `apply_default_whitespace`, `apply_default_ignorewhitespace`, and `object_creation_mode`. The series is **technically complete**, **CI-clean**, and **memory-leak-free** after addressing the regression Junio C Hamano identified in v8. The fix for the `pager_program` leak in `core_pager_config()` (used by `read_early_config()`) ensures consistent memory management across all heap-allocated strings, including `askpass_program` (patch 5/9), `apply_default_whitespace`/`apply_default_ignorewhitespace` (patch 6/9), and the enums (patches 7-9/9, though these are not heap-allocated). **No merge status has been reported; the series is not in `next` or `master`**. **Design resolution (patch 1/9)**: The destructor (`repo_config_values_clear()`) will now take a `struct repo_config_values *` (matching `_init()`), eliminating the `repo != the_repository` guardrail and enabling submodule support. This resolves the signature mismatch, silent leak risk, and initialization-order crashes Pablo Sabater flagged. The change will ripple through the series, simplifying memory management and removing the `NEEDSWORK` comment about submodule support. Expect `repo_clear()` to call the destructor via `&repo->config_values_private_` in v10.
