Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating additional global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series builds directly on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration (v6, now in `next`) and extends the pattern to nine variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program` (patch 5/9). The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author raises two RFC questions: (1) whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`.

Current status
The series is **under review** as a follow-up to the merged `excludes_file` work. The first two patches overlap with the prior series (introducing `repo_config_values_clear()` and migrating `excludes_file`), but are re-split for clarity. The remaining seven patches (including `attributes_file`, `askpass_program`, `push_default`, `autorebase`, **apply whitespace settings**, and **`object_creation_mode`**) are now complete. The series is **technically complete** and **CI-clean**, but **Junio C Hamano has identified a potential memory leak in the `pager_program` migration** (patch 4/9) that may affect other string-valued fields in `repo_config_values`. The issue arises when heap-allocated strings (e.g., `pager_program`, `editor_program`, `askpass_program`) are re-assigned without freeing the old value, risking leaks in long-running processes or during repeated configuration reloads. The RFC questions and a promised follow-up cleanup (comments/variable classification in `repo_config_values`) remain open. No merge status has been reported; the series is **not yet in `next`**.
