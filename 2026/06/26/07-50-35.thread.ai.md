Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating additional global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series builds on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration (v6, now in `next`) and extends the pattern to nine variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program`. The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author’s RFC questions—(1) whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`—remain open but do not affect correctness.

The series is **CI-clean**, **memory-leak-free**, and **ready for `next`**. **No merge status has been reported**; the series is not in `next` or `master`.
