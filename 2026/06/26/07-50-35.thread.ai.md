Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series extends the pattern established by the merged `excludes_file` migration to eleven variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program`. The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author’s RFC questions—(1) whether migrating program paths is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`—remain open but do not affect correctness.
