Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating additional global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series builds directly on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration (v6, now in `next`) and extends the pattern to nine variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program` (patch 5/9). The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author raises two RFC questions: (1) whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`.

Current status
The series is **under review** as a follow-up to the merged `excludes_file` work. The first two patches overlap with the prior series (introducing `repo_config_values_clear()` and migrating `excludes_file`), but are re-split for clarity. Patches 3-9/9 migrate `editor_program`, `pager_program`, `askpass_program`, `attributes_file`, `push_default`, `autorebase`, **`apply_default_whitespace`**, **`apply_default_ignorewhitespace`**, and **`object_creation_mode`**. The series is **technically complete** and **CI-clean**. **Junio C Hamano's memory leak concern from v7 has been fully addressed in v8**: the fix ensures proper cleanup of heap-allocated strings (e.g., `pager_program`, `editor_program`, `askpass_program`, `attributes_file`, **`apply_default_whitespace`**, **`apply_default_ignorewhitespace`**) during re-assignment, eliminating the risk of leaks in long-running processes or repeated configuration reloads. Patch 4/9 specifically reinforces this pattern by freeing the old `pager_program` value in `check_pager_config()` before re-assignment, patch 5/9 extends this to `askpass_program` in `git_default_core_config()`, and **patches 6-8/9 extend the same memory management discipline to `apply_default_whitespace`, `apply_default_ignorewhitespace`, and `autorebase` (though the latter is an enum and does not require heap allocation)**. Patch 9/9 migrates `object_creation_mode` into `struct repo_config_values`, relocating its enum definition in `environment.h` to resolve visibility and updating `finalize_object_file_flags()` to read from the repository-specific structure. The RFC questions and a promised follow-up cleanup (comments/variable classification in `repo_config_values`) remain open. No merge status has been reported; the series is **not yet in `next`**.
