Core problem or goal
Continue the libification effort by migrating additional global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. This series builds on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration (v6, now in `next`) and extends the pattern to nine variables: editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`), and the recently added `askpass_program` (patch 5/9). The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The author raises two RFC questions: (1) whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and (2) how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`.
