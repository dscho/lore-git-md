This is a **CI/platform-compatibility** discussion in the ongoing macOS CI hang thread. Jeff King (Peff) replies to Michael Montalbo’s identification of an upstream Apache `mod_http2` bug (70131) as the root cause of the CI hangs and `curl 18` mid-transfer aborts. Peff acknowledges the diagnosis but expresses skepticism about Montalbo’s proposed client-side mitigation (enabling `http.lowSpeedLimit` and `http.lowSpeedTime` to convert indefinite hangs into fast failures). He argues that this approach merely "punts on the problem" by creating spurious failures, and notes that Git has historically resisted automatic flaky-test retries, preferring to eliminate flakes at the source. Instead, Peff suggests two alternatives: **waiting for an upstream Apache fix** or **increasing Apache’s timeout value** to avoid hitting the bug-triggering threshold. His tone is cautious, emphasizing the project’s preference for root-cause fixes over workarounds, and he does not endorse any specific path forward yet. The thread remains open, with no maintainer decision on how to proceed.
