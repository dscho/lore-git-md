This is the third patch in Taylor Blau’s RFC series to combine `git repack --geometric` and `--cruft` modes. It teaches the repack machinery to mark packs above the geometric split line (both regular and promisor packs) as *retained* in the `existing_packs` struct, so that later deletion passes will keep them instead of removing them. The change is purely preparatory: no packs are actually written or deleted yet, but the infrastructure is now in place to route geometric pack deletion through the same `existing_packs` machinery used by non-geometric repacks. Files touched are `builtin/repack.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack.h`; the patch adds a new helper `existing_packs_retain_non_kept()` and the exported `existing_packs_retain_from_geometry()` function. This is a low-risk refactoring that paves the way for the more complex integration in later patches.
