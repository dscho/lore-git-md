Tian Yuchen's patch series to migrate nine more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values` continues to make progress. The v9 iteration, posted as a follow-up to the recently merged `excludes_file` work, is now technically complete and free of memory leaks after addressing the regression Junio C Hamano identified in the previous version. The series moves editor, pager, and askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, and `attributes_file`), and the new `askpass_program` into the repository-specific structure, eliminating globals and centralizing memory management.

Junio's earlier concern about a leak in `core_pager_config()`—where the old value wasn't freed during re-assignment—has been resolved in v9, and he has acknowledged the fix. The series is now CI-clean and ready for further review, though no merge status has been reported yet. Pablo Sabater chimed in with a minor procedural request for a `base-commit` trailer to ease local testing, but this doesn’t affect the code’s readiness. Two RFC questions remain open: whether migrating program paths is worthwhile (Tian argues it’s useful for developers juggling different toolchains) and how this work might eventually tie into the more complex `git_branch_track` migration. The author has also promised a follow-up cleanup for comments and variable classification in `repo_config_values`, and a few `NEEDSWORK` items—like submodule support and enum placement—are noted but not blocking.
