This thread is a follow-up to the recently merged work that moved Git’s `excludes_file` configuration into a repository-specific structure, extending the pattern to seven more global configuration variables. Tian Yuchen sent a nine-patch series that migrates editor, pager, and askpass program paths, apply whitespace settings, and three enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`) into `struct repo_config_values`. The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization.

The series is technically complete and passes CI, but two open questions remain: whether migrating program paths (which are rarely overridden per-repository) is worthwhile, and how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`. The author also promised a follow-up cleanup for comments and variable classification in `repo_config_values`. No substantive reviews have arrived yet, and the series is not yet in Junio’s `next` branch. The first two patches overlap with the already-merged `excludes_file` work but are re-split for clarity; the rest are new and introduce getters for the additional variables. A minor submodule limitation—where `repo_config_values_clear()` silently returns for submodules—is marked as `NEEDSWORK` but not a blocker.
