This thread is a follow-up to the recently merged `excludes_file` migration, extending the pattern to nine more configuration variables in Tian Yuchen’s v9 series. The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare Git’s codebase for further modularization. Tian has addressed the memory leak Junio C Hamano flagged in v8, ensuring all heap-allocated strings (including editor, pager, and askpass programs) are properly freed during re-assignment. The series is technically complete, CI-clean, and ready for review, though it’s not yet in Junio’s integration branches.

Pablo Sabater has reviewed several patches, including this one on `editor_program`. His feedback here is surface-level—minor nits about redundant function calls, inconsistent quoting, and the absence of a getter function—none of which block progress. Earlier, he raised deeper design concerns about submodule handling and initialization-order crashes in the series’ foundational patch, but Tian has pushed back, prioritizing fail-fast behavior over robustness. This philosophical divide could delay merging if not resolved in a v10.

The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort, and Tian has explicitly justified migrating program paths (like the editor) as useful for developers managing multiple repositories with different toolchains. While the patches are mechanically sound, loose ends remain: a `NEEDSWORK` comment about submodule support, temporary enum placements to avoid circular dependencies, and promised cleanup of comments in `repo_config_values`. For now, the series is under active review, with no major technical objections standing in the way of merging.
