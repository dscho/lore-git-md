This thread is about a 10-patch series (v12) from Tian Yuchen that continues Git's libification effort by moving more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The goal is to eliminate globals, centralize memory management, and prepare the codebase for further modularization. The series migrates eleven variables, including editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, and four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, and `attributes_file`).

The series is technically complete and ready for Junio's `next` branch, having addressed all prior feedback. Key changes in v12 include fixing a delivery issue (all patches are now present), applying a style fix for a declaration-after-statement warning in `apply.c`, and renumbering the series to 10 patches. The final patch adjusts a stale comment in `repository.h` to reflect the migration of `askpass_program`. No new technical feedback has been raised, and the series is CI-clean with no open concerns. The base commit remains Git 2.55-rc2 (2026-06-22), and the series is not yet in `next` or `master`.

Two open questions from the author's RFC remain: whether migrating program paths is worthwhile and how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`. Follow-up work includes cleaning up comments in `repo_config_values`, resolving a `NEEDSWORK` comment for the `push_default` enum placement, and verifying no callers of `branch_get_push_1` pass a repository other than `the_repository`. Junio and Pablo Sabater have reviewed earlier versions, and all feedback has been addressed in v12. The series builds on the merged `excludes_file` migration and is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort.
