This thread is about the tenth version of a nine-patch series by Tian Yuchen that continues Git’s libification effort by moving more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series targets nine variables: editor, pager, and askpass programs; apply whitespace settings; four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, and `attributes_file`); and is built on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration.

The series is technically complete and under active review, with all prior feedback addressed except for a few cleanups and readability tweaks slated for v11. Junio C Hamano and Pablo Sabater have been the most active reviewers. Junio flagged a critical omission in patch 5/9—failing to remove the global `askpass_program` after migrating it to `struct repo_config_values`—and suggested readability improvements in patch 4/9 by caching the result of `repo_config_values()`. Pablo Sabater identified a behavioral change in patch 4/9 (a new `BUG()` assertion enforcing `repo == the_repository`) that needs documentation, and noted missing memory-leak fixes in patch 6/9. Tian’s latest reply confirms he will document the `BUG()` assertion and cache redundant `repo_config_values()` calls in v11, addressing Sabater’s feedback. The series is CI-clean, memory-leak-free (except for the newly identified issue in patch 6/9), and ready for `next` once the remaining cleanups are applied. No merge status has been reported; the series is not yet in `next` or `master`. The main open questions are whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile and how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`, though these do not affect correctness.
