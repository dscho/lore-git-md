This thread is a v10 patch series from Tian Yuchen that continues Git's libification effort by migrating nine global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series builds on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration and extends the pattern to editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, four enums, and the attributes file. All prior feedback has been addressed, including a memory leak in `core_pager_config()` flagged by Junio and design concerns about the destructor raised by Pablo Sabater.

The series is technically complete and under active review, with no merge status reported yet. Key changes include a new destructor signature that enables submodule support, cached calls to `repo_config_values()` to avoid redundancy, and proper memory management for all heap-allocated fields. Two open questions remain from the author's RFC: whether migrating program paths is worthwhile (Tian argues it's critical for multi-repository workflows) and how this work relates to the complex `git_branch_track` variable. The series is CI-clean and ready for `next`, with follow-up work promised for comment cleanup and enum placement issues.
