This patch series from Tian Yuchen continues the long-running libification effort to eliminate global configuration variables by moving them into `struct repo_config_values`. The v11 iteration is now complete, addressing all prior feedback from Junio Hamano and Pablo Sabater, and is ready for integration into `next`.

The series migrates ten configuration variables - including editor, pager, and askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, and several enums - into the repository-scoped structure. The latest update adds the `editor_program` migration (patch 3/9), which follows the established pattern: removing the global variable, adding it to the structure, and ensuring proper memory management through the destructor introduced earlier in the series. All patches are now technically sound with no known memory leaks or redundant operations.

While the series is ready for merging, two broader questions remain open: whether migrating program paths is worthwhile (the author argues it enables per-repo configuration in long-running processes), and how this work relates to previous discussions about `git_branch_track`. The series also leaves some cleanup opportunities, including resolving a circular dependency for `push_default` enum placement and addressing comments about variable classification in the configuration structure. These are noted as follow-up work rather than blockers.
