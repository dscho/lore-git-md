This thread continues the libification effort to move more Git configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`, eliminating globals and centralizing memory management. Tian Yuchen's v9 series migrates nine variables—editor, pager, and askpass programs; apply whitespace settings; four enums (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`, `attributes_file`); and the recently added `askpass_program`—building on the recently merged `excludes_file` work. The series is technically complete, CI-clean, and memory-leak-free after addressing a regression Junio C Hamano identified in v8, where `core_pager_config()` failed to free old values during re-assignment.

The series remains under active review, with no merge status reported. Junio’s latest note clarifies how to identify the base commit for local testing but doesn’t signal readiness for `next` or `master`. Pablo Sabater’s reviews have surfaced design concerns, including a silent submodule leak risk in `repo_config_values_clear()` and redundant function calls in `repo_excludes_file()` and `git_editor()`. While Tian has agreed to align the destructor’s signature with `repo_config_values_init()`, the submodule issue and initialization-order crashes remain unresolved, revealing a philosophical divide: Pablo prefers robustness (avoiding crashes and leaks), while Tian favors fail-fast behavior to surface misuse early. The author has also flagged two RFC questions—whether migrating program paths is worthwhile and how this work relates to `git_branch_track`—but neither has drawn reviewer feedback yet. The circular dependency workaround for `push_default` (patch 7/9) and the temporary relocation of `enum rebase_setup_type`/`object_creation_mode` (patches 8–9/9) are marked as future cleanup. For now, the series is stalled on design discussions, with actionable nits and unresolved questions blocking progress.
