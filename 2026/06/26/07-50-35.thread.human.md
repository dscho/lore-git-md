This thread is a follow-up patch series from Tian Yuchen that continues the libification effort by moving more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series builds on the recently merged `excludes_file` migration and extends the pattern to nine variables, including editor/pager/askpass programs, apply whitespace settings, and several enums like `push_default` and `autorebase`.

The latest email covers patch 7/9, which migrates the `push_default` enum into the per-repository configuration structure. This change is mechanical and doesn’t alter user-visible behavior, but it does add a `NEEDSWORK` comment about the temporary placement of the enum definition to avoid circular dependencies. The series as a whole is under review and CI-clean, though it hasn’t yet received substantive feedback from other contributors. Tian has raised two RFC questions—whether migrating program paths is worthwhile and how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`—and promised a follow-up cleanup for comments and variable classification in `repo_config_values`. The series is not yet in Junio’s `next` branch, and no merge status has been reported.
