This thread is about a two-patch series from Tian Yuchen that moves the global `excludes_file` variable into `struct repo_config_values` as part of Git’s ongoing libification effort. The goal is to eliminate global state and improve memory management, with no user-visible changes. The series is now at v2, which addressed feedback on typos, initialization, and cleanup, and Junio C Hamano had approved the second patch for the `next` branch.

However, Junio has now raised a critical design question about the temporary guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) added to avoid BUG() assertions in uninitialized submodules. He argues this guard might mask bugs rather than expose them, suggesting it should either check `repo->initialized` or BUG() outright. This feedback challenges the approach taken in the series and could delay its integration until resolved. The discussion is now at a decision point: Tian will need to respond to Junio’s concerns, likely by revising the guard or justifying its current form. No other reviewers have raised objections, but this issue is central to the libification effort’s correctness and debugging.
