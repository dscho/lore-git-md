This is a **test suite bugfix** addressing a platform-compatibility issue in `t4216-log-bloom.sh`. Jeff King (Peff) clarifies why the TAP parse errors—triggered by the `SIGNED_CHAR_BY_DEFAULT` prerequisite on non-x86 architectures—only surfaced recently. The root cause is a 2024 change that assumed `char` is signed by default (true on x86), but the issue went unnoticed until a June 2026 update to the test harness (`prove`) began failing on invalid TAP output.

The fix itself remains uncontroversial: Junio and Patrick agree the prerequisite is redundant and should be removed entirely. Peff’s email doesn’t propose a new solution but explains why the problem wasn’t caught earlier—non-x86 test runs likely ignored the extraneous `test_cmp` output until the harness became stricter. This aligns with the earlier consensus that the test’s behavior (skipping on non-x86) doesn’t justify the complexity of the prerequisite.

The patch will likely touch only `t/t4216-log-bloom.sh`, removing the `test_cmp` check and the `SIGNED_CHAR_BY_DEFAULT` prerequisite. No changes to the Bloom filter logic or on-disk formats are needed. The discussion is now focused on implementation details rather than design, making this a straightforward cleanup for the upcoming 2.55.0 release.
