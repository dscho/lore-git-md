This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s seven-part v16 series adding `` `git branch --delete-merged` ``.  It introduces a new `` DELETE_BRANCH_SKIP_UNMERGED `` flag to the existing `` delete_branches() `` API so that bulk callers—such as the upcoming `` --delete-merged `` command—can silently skip unmerged branches instead of erroring out with the usual “use -D” advice.  The change is purely internal: no user-visible behavior is altered, and the patch is small (12 insertions, 5 deletions in `` builtin/branch.c ``).  The implementation is straightforward and appears ready for review.
