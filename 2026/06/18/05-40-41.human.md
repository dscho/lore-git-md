This is a **CI/build system** follow-up in the ongoing effort to improve Windows build reliability in GitLab CI. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Justin Tobler’s **substantive review** of his patch that replaces Chocolatey with a direct dependency installation script.

The exchange clarifies two key points raised in the review. First, Patrick confirms that pinned dependency versions (Git 2.46.0, Meson 1.4.0, Rust 1.78.0) are unlikely to require frequent updates, as the project is conservative about minimum version bumps. Second, he attributes the 3x speed-up (from ~3 minutes to ~1 minute) not to faster downloads but to Chocolatey’s overhead—installing Git, Meson/Ninja, and Rust now takes 40s, 4s, and 23s respectively, down from 70s, 20s, and 70s. The caching mechanism, while not a major performance driver, aims to improve reliability by reducing internet interactions.

Patrick also addresses a minor implementation detail: Git’s installation path isn’t explicitly added to `$env:Path` because Meson hardcodes the "C:\Program Files\Git" location. He acknowledges this as a potential hack but defers to Justin’s judgment on whether to change it. The tone remains collaborative, with no new objections or design challenges introduced. The patch appears ready to proceed, pending any further feedback on the path-handling quirk.
