This is a substantive review by Jeff King (Peff) of patch 7/8 in Patrick Steinhardt’s refactoring series. Peff questions the fundamental design of the patch, arguing that the recursive initialization problem stems from a deeper architectural issue: the ref backend should not be reading application-level config (like `logAllRefUpdates`) during its own initialization. Instead, he suggests either lazy-loading such settings or reading them alongside core repository format settings, which are parsed without respecting includes. His review includes a test case demonstrating that the current workaround (temporarily unsetting `ref_storage_format`) is masking a more systemic problem. While Peff acknowledges the patch’s immediate goal—preventing recursion—he implies the solution is a band-aid rather than a proper fix, and his tone suggests this could become a point of contention if not addressed. The review is well-reasoned and likely to prompt further discussion or a redesign.
