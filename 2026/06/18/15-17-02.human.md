This is a documentation patch refining the *SubmittingPatches* guide. Michael Montalbo proposes a sharper rewrite of a paragraph that advises contributors how to handle design critiques. The change is small but makes the guidance more actionable and direct. No controversy is evident; it looks like a straightforward improvement to the contributor documentation.
