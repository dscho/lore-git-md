This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Tian Yuchen replies to Junio C Hamano’s review of the first patch in a two-patch series that moves the global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The series aims to eliminate a global and tie the configuration value to the repository instance it was read from, continuing the broader initiative to encapsulate repository-specific state.

The exchange centers on a single technical point: whether the newly introduced `repo_get_ignore_case()` getter should include a `repo->gitdir` check (like the getters for `trust_executable_bit`, `protect_hfs`, and `protect_ntfs`) or rely solely on the `repo` pointer. Junio questioned the omission, noting that the commit message’s wording ("could safely accommodate") left room for doubt. Tian acknowledges the mistake, clarifies that the CI failure he encountered was unrelated, and agrees to align the getter’s logic with the existing pattern. He also proposes renaming the getter to `repo_ignore_case()` for consistency with prior work.

The patch touches 14 call sites across core Git subsystems, the Windows compatibility layer, and built-in commands, but no new tests are added. The discussion is substantive, focusing on initialization robustness and design consistency, and Tian’s response suggests the next version will address Junio’s concerns. The series looks well-motivated and technically sound, though the getter’s design was the only open question. If resolved cleanly, this should be a straightforward step in the `the_repository` removal effort.
