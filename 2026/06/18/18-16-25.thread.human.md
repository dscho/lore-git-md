This thread is about Karthik Nayak’s seven-patch series that extends `git log -L` to support range-scoped diff stat formats (`--stat`, `--numstat`, `--shortstat`), whitespace checking (`--check`), and the `-G` pickaxe. The series is now in its second version (v2), with all patches implemented, documented, and tested. Junio C Hamano reviewed the diff stat portion in the first version and suggested minor documentation tweaks, which have since been incorporated.

The key changes include fixing a bug where deletions outside the tracked range could leak into patch output, correcting an off-by-one error in hunk headers, and enabling the new features to honor the line ranges specified by `-L`. The series also explicitly excludes `--dirstat` and `-S` due to architectural constraints, with no plans for follow-up work on those. Michael Montalbo recently sent a brief "ping" to the list, likely to encourage further review or maintainer attention, but the series remains in the same state: complete, approved in principle, and awaiting additional feedback or a maintainer decision.
