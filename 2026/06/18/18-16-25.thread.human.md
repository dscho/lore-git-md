This thread is about a patch series (v3) by Michael Montalbo that extends `git log -L` to support range-scoped diff stat formats, whitespace checking, and the `-G` pickaxe. The series builds on earlier work by Karthik Nayak and has already received positive feedback from Junio Hamano on the stat-related portion.

The latest email covers the second patch in the series, which fixes a bug in the line-range filter where deletions just outside the tracked range could incorrectly appear in the output. The fix simplifies the filter's logic by removing a separate "pending removal" buffer and classifying lines immediately based on their position. This change also addresses a symmetric issue with leading deletions and reduces the complexity of state management. The accompanying test ensures the bug is resolved and won't regress.

The series is now complete at v3, with no new technical feedback since v2. While Junio has reviewed part of the series, there's no indication yet that it has been picked up for integration. The remaining patches add support for `--stat`, `--check`, and `-G` within line ranges, with clear exclusions for `--dirstat` and `-S` due to architectural constraints. The test coverage is thorough, verifying edge cases at range boundaries and ensuring accurate reporting of line numbers and whitespace errors. No open questions remain beyond the acknowledged need for future work on the excluded features.
