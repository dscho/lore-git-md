This is the second patch in Karthik Nayak’s seven-patch series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat, whitespace checking, and the `` `-G` `` pickaxe. It is a targeted refactoring of the line-range filter in `diff.c` that eliminates an unnecessary buffering layer and fixes a subtle bug where deletions just outside the tracked range could leak into the patch output.

The patch replaces the old model—where removal lines were buffered until the next addition or context line revealed their position—with a simpler one: every line is classified immediately by the post-image index it will occupy. Because xdiff emits removals before additions within a change, the post-image index of the following line is already known when a removal arrives, so the filter can decide on the spot whether the removal belongs in the current range hunk. This change removes the `pending_rm` buffer, the `discard_pending_rm()` helper, and three struct fields (`pending_rm_count`, `pending_rm_pre_begin`, and `has_changes`), and it adds a new `begin_range_hunk()` function to open the accumulator at the first in-range line.

The patch also fixes a bug where a deletion just after the tracked range could leak into the patch output, because the old code would flush the pending removal buffer whenever the hunk was active, even after the post-image cursor had advanced past the range. With the new model, the bug is impossible by construction: lines are classified as they arrive, and the hunk is flushed only when the cursor leaves the range.

The commit message is unusually thorough, including a worked example of the coordinate model and a detailed explanation of how the new logic avoids the old bug. The test coverage is correspondingly strong, adding tests for the leaked trailing deletion, the symmetric leading-deletion case, and edge cases at the first and last line of a range. The patch is well-motivated and looks like a clean step forward in the series.
