This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s seven-patch series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat formats, whitespace checking, and the `` `-G` `` pickaxe. This patch stops hand-rolling the `` `@@ -<old> +<new> @@` `` hunk headers and instead delegates their formatting to xdiff’s own emitter, which already produces every other diff’s hunk headers. The immediate effect is that `` `-L` `` headers now match `` `git diff` `` exactly: for a side with no lines (count 0), the begin is the line before the change, and a count of 1 is omitted. Two test fixtures are regenerated to reflect the corrected headers, fixing an off-by-one that has been present since the original line-log implementation. The patch also adds a small helper, `` `xdiff_emit_hunk_header()` ``, to `` `xdiff-interface.c` `` so that the line-range filter can reuse xdiff’s formatting logic. The change is confined to `` `diff.c` ``, `` `xdiff-interface.c` ``, and the test expectations; no new user-visible behavior is introduced yet.
