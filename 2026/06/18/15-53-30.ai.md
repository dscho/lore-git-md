This is a brief follow-up in the refactoring series modernizing Git’s reference backend, specifically the contested patch 7/8 that resolves recursive initialization during repository setup. Justin Tobler concedes that his proposed alternative—adding an `initialized` boolean to `struct ref_store`—would not actually solve the problem, since the recursion occurs *during* `ref_store_init()` before the ref store is fully constructed. He now acknowledges the current workaround (temporarily setting `ref_storage_format` to `REF_STORAGE_FORMAT_UNKNOWN`) as the pragmatic choice, though still a hack. The exchange leaves the patch’s design effectively settled: no superior alternative has emerged, and the series’ sole remaining open question is now closed. The rest of the series (patches 1–6 and 8) remains uncontroversial and ready for integration. Key technical detail: the recursion stems from `include_by_branch()` triggering config parsing while the ref store is still being built, a timing issue the current patch sidesteps by short-circuiting the "onbranch" logic.
