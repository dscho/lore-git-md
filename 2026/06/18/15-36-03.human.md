This is a documentation patch to `SubmittingPatches`, the guide for contributors on how to submit patches to Git. The change adds explicit guidance about how to handle design-level critiques from reviewers. It advises authors to defend their design decisions on the mailing list before investing too much effort in implementation, to provide clearer explanations in revised versions, and warns that patches that ignore fundamental design concerns will not be merged.

The patch is a direct response to Junio C Hamano’s proposed wording, which Michael Montalbo endorses without modification. There is no controversy here — it simply codifies existing practice into the contributor guide. The update is small, clear, and uncontroversial, making it a straightforward improvement to the documentation.
