This is a **test suite bugfix** addressing a platform-compatibility issue in `t4216-log-bloom.sh` that causes TAP parse failures on non-x86 architectures. Jeff King (Peff) clarifies the immediate trigger for the issue: a recent change (`389c83025d`, June 2026) tightened the TAP harness to fail on invalid output, exposing the long-standing but previously unnoticed problem where `test_cmp` diff output leaks into TAP streams on platforms where `char` is unsigned by default.

The patch context confirms the fix will remove the `SIGNED_CHAR_BY_DEFAULT` prerequisite and its associated `test_cmp` check, as previously agreed by Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. Peff’s email does not propose new code but provides critical timing context: the issue existed since mid-2024 but only became visible after the TAP harness was made stricter in June 2026. This explains why the problem went unnoticed for two years—it was masked by the test harness’s previous tolerance for extraneous output.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the failing test is `highbit1` in `t4216-log-bloom.sh`, the problematic commit is `389c83025d` (TAP parsing strictness), and the symbols involved are `SIGNED_CHAR_BY_DEFAULT`, `test_cmp`, and the `highbit1/expect` vs. `highbit1/actual` comparison. The fix will touch only the test script, leaving the Bloom filter logic and on-disk format unchanged. No objections or new questions are raised; the thread remains focused on the agreed removal of the redundant prerequisite.
