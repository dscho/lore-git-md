This is the fourth patch in Karthik Nayak’s seven-patch series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat formats, whitespace checking, and the `` `-G` `` pickaxe. This patch extracts a reusable helper, `` `line_range_filter_diff()` ``, from the open-coded line-range filter setup in `` `builtin_diff()` ``. The helper encapsulates the boilerplate of inflating `` `ctxlen` `` to the largest range span (so each range yields a single xdiff hunk), clearing `` `XDL_EMIT_NO_HUNK_HDR` `` (so hunk headers are always emitted for the filter’s position tracking), running the diff, flushing the final range hunk, and releasing the filter buffer.

The change is purely refactoring: no behavior is altered, since `` `builtin_diff()` `` already leaves `` `XDL_EMIT_NO_HUNK_HDR` `` unset. The extracted helper will be reused by the next two patches to implement `` `--stat` `` and `` `--check` `` support for `` `-L` ``. The commit message explains the invariants the helper enforces and why they belong in a single place rather than duplicated at each call site. The patch touches only `` `diff.c` `` and is a clean, well-motivated step toward the series’ goal.
