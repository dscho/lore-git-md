This is a brief follow-up in the long-running discussion about adding a `--fetch` option (or `--track=fetch`) to `` `git checkout` `` and `` `git switch` ``. D. Ben Knoble replies to Harald Nordgren’s request for support, offering a measured but non-committal take.

Knoble acknowledges the code is “in decent shape” but stops short of endorsing the feature. He still sees potential convenience, yet echoes earlier concerns: because the workflow implications vary by project, he wonders whether keeping fetch and checkout as two separate steps might be the safer default. His response is deliberately neutral—“I truly don’t know”—and does not shift the balance of the discussion. The thread’s core question—whether the convenience justifies the change—remains open.
