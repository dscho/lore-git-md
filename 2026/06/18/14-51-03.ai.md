This is a short follow-up in the ongoing refactoring series targeting Git's reference backend, specifically patch 7/8. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Justin Tobler's proposal to replace the current temporary `ref_storage_format` override with an explicit `initialized` boolean in `struct ref_store`.

Patrick acknowledges the potential for an `initialized` flag but expresses skepticism about its value, framing it as "one more bit of state" that could introduce future inconsistencies. His response is brief and does not engage with the implementation complexity Justin raised, suggesting he remains unconvinced the alternative would meaningfully improve maintainability. The exchange leaves the design question open: the current workaround is acknowledged as a hack, but no superior solution has gained traction. This patch remains the sole holdout in an otherwise uncontroversial series, with its final form still undecided.
