The change is backward-compatible and touches only the help subsystem, with no impact on other parts of Git.

Callers that can’t provide the arguments (like `builtin/help.c`) fall back to the old behavior. Junio’s earlier feedback—about building on the existing `autocorrect=prompt` code rather than reimplementing it—has been fully addressed. The patch is now a clean, incremental improvement, though the broader question of whether to change the default behavior remains open.
