The Git 2.55.0 translation cycle is wrapping up. Jiang Xin kicked off the effort earlier this week, noting 125 new strings needing translation across all languages and a small workflow tweak: translators must now generate the `po/git.pot` template locally rather than relying on a pre-generated version. The email also acknowledged AI-assisted translation as an optional first step, though human review remains mandatory. The established process—using Git filters to strip source location comments and submitting PRs to the git-l10n/git-po repository—remains unchanged, with CI validation running automatically.

Junio C Hamano followed up today with a gentle reminder that the submission deadline is tomorrow, June 27, 2026. The translations will be integrated into the Git 2.55.0 release, which is scheduled for June 29. There’s no indication of any issues or delays; the thread is purely procedural, and the tone suggests the process is on track. The l10n team leads (Jean-Noël Avila for French, Ralf Thielow for German, and others) are expected to finalize their submissions by the deadline.
