This is a follow-up from the author Harald Nordgren in the thread about optimizing `git commit --amend --no-edit` to avoid creating new commits when nothing changes. 

After maintainer Junio C Hamano's rejection of the approach due to workflow compatibility concerns, Nordgren acknowledges the feedback as a "showstopper" and suggests shelving the idea for now. He shares an interesting personal workflow detail - he had been using `git commit --amend --no-edit --date="now"` to force new commit hashes to retrigger CI, not realizing the hash would change even without substantive modifications.

The email effectively concedes the technical debate while providing a real-world example of how the current behavior (which the patch sought to change) is actively used in practice. This reinforces Junio's position about the importance of maintaining backward compatibility with existing workflows, even when they might not align with a "pure" conceptual model of Git's behavior.
