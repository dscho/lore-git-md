This is the second patch in Pablo Sabater's v5 series improving `git log --graph` rendering of commits with excluded parents. The patch introduces cascading indentation for visual root commits to make parent-child relationships clearer when history simplification filters out some parents.

The implementation adds a new `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state to connect children with indented roots, along with flags to track visual roots and their depth in the hierarchy. Visual roots (except the first in a sequence) are progressively indented to create clear separation from unrelated commits. The patch includes extensive test coverage in a new `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` file.

One known edge case remains where the lookahead mechanism can't perfectly predict when a commit will become a visual root after history simplification, documented with a NEEDSWORK comment. The author notes unfamiliarity with revision.c to fix this. The v5 iteration resolves a test environment issue from v4 by unsetting COMMIT_GRAPH.

The patch appears technically sound with thorough test coverage, building on feedback from previous iterations. The visualization improvements address a real usability issue in `git log --graph` output when dealing with filtered history. The series seems close to merge-ready pending any final review feedback on the visualization rules documentation question raised in earlier versions.
