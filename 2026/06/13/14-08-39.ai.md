Performance optimization for merge-base calculations in commit-reach.c, with a focus on monorepo scenarios. Kristofer Karlsson provides a technical clarification about the STALE bit propagation behavior in the proposed optimization, correcting his earlier statement about how STALE reaches commit D's lineage. The key insight is that imported repositories with their own root at generation 0 can trigger unnecessary traversal in the current algorithm, forcing a full graph drain even when the merge-base is already found. Karlsson reports concrete measurements showing this causes `merge-base --all HEAD HEAD~1000` to perform 2.3 million steps (mostly stale) in their monorepo. The discussion continues to refine understanding of the edge cases where the optimization would apply, particularly around imported repository histories and generation number handling. This builds on the previously established 300-1000x speedup potential for "shape 2" topologies common in large-scale development workflows.
