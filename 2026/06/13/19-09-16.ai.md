This is the second patch in Pablo Sabater's v5 series improving `git log --graph` rendering of commits with excluded parents. The patch introduces cascading indentation for visual root commits to clarify parent-child relationships when history simplification filters out some parents.

The implementation adds a new GRAPH_PRE_ROOT state to connect children with indented roots, along with flags to track visual roots and their depth in the hierarchy. Visual roots (except the first in a sequence) are progressively indented, while merge parents are exempt since they're already connected via edges. The patch includes extensive test coverage in a new t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh file.

One known edge case remains where the lookahead mechanism can't perfectly predict when a commit will become a visual root after history simplification, documented with a NEEDSWORK comment. The author notes unfamiliarity with revision.c to fix this. The v5 iteration resolves test failures from v4 by unsetting COMMIT_GRAPH in the test environment.

The patch appears technically sound with thorough test coverage, building on previous review feedback. The remaining open items are the lookahead edge case and Junio's earlier question about documenting the visualization rules. The series seems close to merge-ready pending any final review feedback on these points.
