This is a documentation patch series (2 parts) aimed at improving contributor guidelines, specifically around patch review etiquette and reroll timing.

The series comes from Weijie Yuan, a relatively new contributor, and is marked as RFC to solicit feedback on wording and structure. The patches aim to formalize two best practices that have been discussed on the list:

1. The first patch addresses a common new-contributor pitfall - sending new patch versions without first discussing the response to review feedback in the thread. It updates MyFirstContribution.adoc to explicitly recommend replying to reviews before rerolling, so the reasoning behind changes is visible to all.

2. The second patch, based on advice from Patrick Steinhardt, adds guidance to SubmittingPatches about reroll frequency. It suggests batching feedback rather than immediate updates, while acknowledging factors like series size and review depth that may affect timing. The patch needs attribution clarification for Patrick's input.

The changes look well-motivated and address real gaps in the documentation. The RFC flag is appropriate since the exact wording and placement in the docs may benefit from refinement. This is exactly the sort of contribution that helps onboard new developers while codifying the project's unwritten norms.
