This is a reply from the Git maintainer Junio C Hamano to Weijie Yuan's RFC documentation patch about patch reroll timing. Junio supports the proposed guideline of limiting rerolls to one per day, adding two key perspectives:

1. The 24-hour delay encourages contributors to be more thorough before sending, reducing trivial errors that waste reviewer time on low-level issues when higher-level design feedback is more valuable.

2. The delay also creates fairness in participation across time zones, ensuring reviewers worldwide have equal opportunity to comment before a new version appears.

Junio defers to Patrick Steinhardt (CC'd on the thread) for more substantive suggestions, but his brief reply signals general approval of the direction while adding practical rationale beyond what the documentation patch itself covered. The tone is supportive and constructive, reinforcing the value of the proposed change while expanding its benefits.
