Weijie Yuan has proposed an RFC documentation patch series to improve Git's contributor guidelines around patch review etiquette. The two-part series focuses on two key aspects: encouraging substantive discussion of feedback before rerolling patches, and establishing appropriate reroll cadence based on series complexity.

The second patch specifically addresses reroll timing, proposing concrete advice in both `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches` documents. It suggests a default of no more than one reroll per day to allow for feedback batching and reviewer response time, while acknowledging this should be flexible based on factors like series size and review progress. The changes build on Patrick Steinhardt's prior advice while making it more accessible to new contributors.

The author demonstrates the proposed best practices in the patch itself - each change is clearly motivated and explained, showing rather than just telling how review discussions should work. The RFC is currently seeking feedback on the exact phrasing and whether additional factors should be considered in the reroll timing guidance. The changes address a recognized pain point in the review process where new contributors sometimes send new versions too quickly without sufficient prior discussion.
