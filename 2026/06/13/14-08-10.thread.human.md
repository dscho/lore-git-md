Weijie Yuan has proposed an RFC patch series to improve Git's contributor documentation around patch review etiquette, focusing on two key aspects: requiring substantive discussion before rerolling patches and establishing appropriate reroll cadences. The initial proposal drew from Patrick Steinhardt's prior advice and models the very practices it advocates by carefully motivating each change.

Junio C Hamano has now weighed in with a thoughtful review of the second patch, which deals with reroll timing. While supporting the general direction, Junio adds valuable perspective about how slower iteration cycles lead to higher-quality reviews by reducing trivial errors and making the process more inclusive for contributors across timezones. His comments reinforce the philosophical rationale behind the proposed guidelines without suggesting specific wording changes, leaving that refinement to others more directly participated in the original discussions (notably Patrick Steinhardt, who was CC'd).

The thread now stands with broad agreement on the principles but awaits concrete refinements to the documentation text. Junio's endorsement lends weight to the effort while his global participation observation adds a new dimension to consider in the final wording. The author will likely incorporate this feedback into the next version while maintaining the careful, discussion-driven approach the series itself advocates.
