This RFC thread proposes updates to Git's contributor documentation (`MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches`) to formalize best practices around patch review etiquette. The series, authored by Weijie Yuan, focuses on two key aspects: requiring substantive discussion of feedback before rerolling patches, and establishing appropriate reroll cadences based on series size and review depth.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the second patch (on reroll timing) and supports the general direction, adding important rationale about how slower reroll cycles improve review quality by encouraging more careful consideration of feedback and allowing global participation across timezones. In the latest email, Yuan demonstrates the very practices being documented - thoughtfully engaging with Junio's feedback before considering a reroll, and indicating he'll incorporate the maintainer's perspective into the documentation. The exchange includes a lighthearted acknowledgment of borrowing wording from Patrick Steinhardt's prior contributions to these guidelines.

The thread remains in RFC stage as Yuan processes feedback, but the lack of technical objections suggests the series is likely to progress to a non-RFC version soon. The patches themselves model the proposed review best practices, making this both a documentation improvement and a practical demonstration of effective contributor workflow.
