This thread is about improving Git's contributor documentation to make patch review etiquette more explicit for newcomers. Weijie Yuan has sent a second version of a two-patch series that updates `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches` with clearer guidance on when and how to reroll patches after review.

The series now has strong consensus on the `SubmittingPatches` changes, which add concrete advice about batching rerolls—typically no more than one per day—to respect reviewer time and accommodate global participation. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt helped refine the wording, adding nuance about when faster or slower rerolls might be appropriate. The updates also tie reroll cadence to a broader principle: waiting encourages authors to polish patches before sending, letting reviewers focus on substantive feedback rather than minor fixes. These changes are uncontroversial and ready for merging.

The `MyFirstContribution.adoc` updates remain under discussion. Patrick Steinhardt noted that the guidance there largely duplicates what’s already in `SubmittingPatches`, and the author has acknowledged the concern but hasn’t yet revised or dropped the patch. The thread itself has modeled the collaborative practices it documents—substantive discussion before rerolling, proper attribution via `Helped-by` trailers, and responsiveness to feedback. The next step is likely a decision on whether to keep the `MyFirstContribution.adoc` changes or focus on the now-mature `SubmittingPatches` updates.
