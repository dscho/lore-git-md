This is the third and final patch in Dominik Loidolt's compiler compatibility modernization series, focusing on simplifying the GCC version comparison logic in `compat/posix.h`. The change replaces the existing bit-shift version comparison (imported from Linux headers) with a more readable major/minor number check, following the style used in BSD headers.

The patch removes the Linux attribution comment since the code no longer matches its original source, and updates the `GIT_GNUC_PREREQ` macro to use direct comparisons that are easier to understand at a glance. The behavioral equivalence of the old and new forms was verified by Patrick Steinhardt during review.

This change completes the series' goal of modernizing compiler check macros while maintaining compatibility. The patch is low-risk (compile-time only) and follows Git's careful approach to platform support code. With all technical feedback addressed across multiple iterations, this series appears ready for integration.
