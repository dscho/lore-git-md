This is a refactoring patch series (2/2) that continues the libification effort by moving the global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The goal is to tie the configuration value to the repository instance it was read from, encapsulating it without changing its eager-parsing behavior.

The series introduces `repo_get_ignore_case()` as a getter function to safely retrieve the value while preserving the existing fallback logic. The author raises three RFC questions about correctness and performance: whether the fallback logic in `repo_get_ignore_case()` is correct (particularly around `gitdir` availability during early initialization), potential performance overhead in `dir.c`, and the appropriateness of including `repository.h` in `compat/win32/path-utils.c`.

The patches touch 14 files, primarily replacing direct accesses to the global `ignore_case` with calls to the new getter. Key subsystems affected include the index (`read-cache.c`, `name-hash.c`), filesystem operations (`dir.c`, `compat/win32/path-utils.c`), and commands like `git fetch` and `git mv`. The series builds on prior work migrating `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` and references Derrick Stolee’s earlier attempt at a similar change.

This is part of the broader effort to eliminate globals and make Git’s internals more repository-aware, aligning with the ongoing `the_repository` removal initiative. The patch appears well-structured, with clear commit messages and test coverage (though no new tests are added). The RFC questions suggest the author is seeking feedback on edge cases rather than fundamental design.
