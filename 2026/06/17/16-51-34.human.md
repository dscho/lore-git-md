It updates both `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches` to recommend sending no more than one new version of a patch series per day by default, while acknowledging that timing should vary based on series size, review depth, and how close the topic is to acceptance.

The patch incorporates feedback from the v1 discussion, particularly Junio C Hamano's emphasis on global timezone considerations and Patrick Steinhardt's original contribution (properly attributed with a `Helped-by` trailer). The guidance now explicitly connects the reroll cadence to encouraging authors to polish patches before sending them, so reviewers can focus on substantial issues rather than minor mistakes.
