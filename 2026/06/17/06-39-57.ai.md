This is the fourteenth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series, completing the callback wiring by implementing the `freshen_object()` callback for the packed object source. The patch moves `packfile_store_freshen_object()` from `packfile.c` to `odb/source-packed.c`, registers it as the packed source’s `freshen_object()` callback, and removes the now-unused external declaration of `find_pack_entry()`—the last remaining caller in `packfile.c`—making it static in its new home.

The change is purely mechanical: the freshening logic (checking for the object in a pack, skipping cruft packs, updating the pack’s mtime, and marking it as freshened) is unchanged. The files source’s `freshen_object()` callback is updated to call the new ODB-prefixed function, and the callback is registered in the packed source’s constructor alongside the other seven callbacks (`close()`, `reprepare()`, `read_object_info()`, `read_object_stream()`, `for_each_object()`, `count_objects()`, and `find_abbrev_len()`). With this patch, all required callbacks except the optional `write_object()` are now wired up, and the `find_pack_entry()` helper is finally encapsulated as a file-local function in `odb/source-packed.c`.

The diff is small (24 insertions, 29 deletions) and touches only the ODB and packfile subsystems. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the patch does not address the refdb lifecycle issues that are currently blocking the series. Justin Tobler’s earlier surface-level review of the mechanical changes in patches 1–13 applies here as well: the callback registration follows the established pattern, and the encapsulation of `find_pack_entry()` is a natural next step after all external callers were removed in previous patches.
