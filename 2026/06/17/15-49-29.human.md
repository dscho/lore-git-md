This is the second patch in a two-part refactoring series that completes the libification of the `ignore_case` global variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The patch replaces all remaining direct accesses to the global `ignore_case` with calls to the new `repo_get_ignore_case(the_repository)` getter introduced in patch 1/2.

The changes touch 14 files across core Git subsystems, including the index (`read-cache.c`, `name-hash.c`), filesystem operations (`dir.c`), Windows compatibility layer (`compat/win32/path-utils.c`), and commands like `git fetch` and `git mv`. The patch also addresses one of the RFC questions from the series cover letter by introducing a dependency on `repository.h` in the Windows compatibility layer, which the author justifies as a necessary trade-off to avoid massive refactoring of function signatures.

The patch appears mechanically sound and aligns with the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. It retains existing test coverage and does not introduce new behavior, focusing instead on encapsulating the global state. The author (Tian Yuchen) acknowledges mentorship from Christian Couder and others, suggesting this is part of a structured contribution process. While the patch is large, the changes are repetitive and low-risk, making it a straightforward step toward reducing Git's reliance on globals. Worth noting for reviewers is the potential performance overhead in `dir.c` due to repeated getter calls, though this was flagged in the cover letter and may not be a blocker.
