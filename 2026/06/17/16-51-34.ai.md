This is the second patch in Weijie Yuan's v2 documentation series, now focused on advising contributors about batching patch rerolls. The patch adds concrete guidance to both `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches` about how often to send updated versions of a patch series, with a default recommendation of no more than one reroll per day to respect reviewer bandwidth and global time zones.

The changes incorporate feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, adding nuance about factors that might justify faster or slower reroll cadence—series size, review depth, required rework, and proximity to acceptance. The patch also ties the reroll timing advice to a broader principle: waiting encourages authors to polish patches before sending, letting reviewers focus on substantial issues rather than minor errors.
