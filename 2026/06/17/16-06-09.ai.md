This is a documentation patch updating `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to address a recurring issue: contributors sometimes resubmit patches without adequately responding to high-level design or viability critiques from reviewers. The patch adds explicit guidance instructing authors to defend their design choices on the mailing list, provide clearer justifications in cover letters and commit messages, and document the resolution of design concerns in the commit message body to preserve the historical record.

The change is motivated by Junio C Hamano’s observation that some patch series advance through multiple iterations without addressing fundamental objections, leaving reviewers frustrated and the project’s decision-making process opaque. The new text emphasizes that unresolved design critiques will block a topic from being merged, reinforcing the importance of engaging with feedback before refining implementation details.

The patch touches only the `SubmittingPatches` document, adding two new paragraphs in the section about responding to reviews and a bullet point in the commit-message guidelines. The tone is prescriptive but constructive, framing the requirements as best practices rather than punitive measures. This is a maintainer-driven clarification of existing expectations rather than a policy change, and it reflects Junio’s long-standing role in shaping the project’s contribution culture.
