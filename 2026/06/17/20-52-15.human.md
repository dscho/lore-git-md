This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part documentation series updating the `SubmittingPatches` guidelines for Git’s trailer conventions. It establishes a new "Commit trailers" section that encourages contributors to credit substantial help via trailers while cautioning against overuse for minor contributions.

The patch replaces the vague "If you like" phrasing with explicit guidance: trailers should follow Git’s author format (name + email) to ensure they’re picked up by `git shortlog --group=trailer:<key>` and normalized via `.mailmap`. It also introduces the list of common trailers upfront, making the section more self-contained. The change is motivated by observed mailing-list practices and aligns with Git’s release-crediting policy, which acknowledges both commit authors and trailer mentions.

This is a well-crafted documentation improvement—clear, practical, and uncontroversial. The commit message cites real-world examples and mailing-list discussions, and the patch itself is tightly scoped to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. It’s part of a fully resolved series that’s ready for merging after two rounds of review from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. If you care about Git’s contribution guidelines, this patch (and the series) is worth a quick skim.
