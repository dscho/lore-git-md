This is a documentation patch from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, updating `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to address a recurring issue: contributors sometimes resubmit patches without adequately responding to fundamental design or viability critiques from reviewers.

The patch adds explicit guidance instructing authors to (1) defend their design choices on the mailing list before investing too much effort in implementation, (2) provide clearer explanations in cover letters, responses, and commit messages for new iterations, and (3) document the resolution of design concerns in the commit message body to preserve the historical record. It also warns that topics failing to address these critiques will not be considered ready for merging.

The change is small but significant—it formalizes an expectation that has long been informally enforced. The new text is clear and actionable, and it directly targets a pain point in the review process. For contributors, this update serves as a helpful reminder; for reviewers, it provides a stronger basis to hold authors accountable. No controversy is evident, and the patch looks ready to merge.
