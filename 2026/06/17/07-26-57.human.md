This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series, now in its third iteration. The patch makes a small but important mechanical change: it replaces the generic `struct odb_source *` parent pointer in `struct odb_source_packed` with a concrete `struct odb_source_files *`, clarifying the relationship between the packed and files ODB sources. This is a temporary step—Patrick confirms the parent pointer will be removed entirely by the end of the series—but it reduces ambiguity during the transition, making subsequent patches easier to reason about.

Justin Tobler’s review was surface-level, focusing only on the patch’s alignment with the loose source’s precedent and expressing curiosity about how this intermediate change advances the goal of removing the parent pointer. Patrick’s reply clarifies that the concrete type helps avoid confusion between the generic `base` and `parent` pointers during the refactoring’s intermediate stages. There is no deeper technical discussion here—neither the refdb lifecycle issues (memory leaks, duplicate creation, initialization ordering) nor the broader ODB abstraction design are addressed in this exchange.

The patch itself is minimal (+11/-11 lines) and uncontroversial. It is part of the series’ larger effort to complete the ODB abstraction by decoupling packed object storage, but this specific change is purely preparatory. The series remains blocked by unresolved refdb problems, which Patrick is addressing in a separate 11-patch fix series. For now, this patch is a small, mechanical step forward in a much larger architectural effort.
