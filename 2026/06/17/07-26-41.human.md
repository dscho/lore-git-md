This is the eleventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series, which continues the mechanical wiring of callbacks for the packed object storage backend. The patch moves the `for_each_object()` implementation from `packfile.c` into `odb/source-packed.c` and registers it as the packed source’s callback, following the established pattern for loose and in-memory sources. Justin Tobler’s earlier review was surface-level, noting only the exposure of `packed_object_info_with_index_pos()` as a public symbol and questioning whether it was temporary.

Patrick clarifies that the symbol is intentionally public: it supports external callers (e.g., single-packfile lookups) and will remain part of the API. The callback itself is a straight relocation of existing logic, including MIDX and packfile traversal, prefix filtering, and object-info lookups, with no behavioral changes. While the patch is mechanically sound and aligns with the series’ goal of interface consistency, it has not received substantive review of edge cases (error handling, MIDX traversal corner cases, or performance implications) or the broader architectural implications of exposing this helper. The series remains blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues, so this patch is unlikely to progress until those are addressed.
