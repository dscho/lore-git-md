This email is Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s response to Junio Hamano’s final review of the `git-interpret-trailers` documentation series. It addresses editorial feedback on the man page wording and structure, confirming or adjusting the text as suggested.

Kristoffer accepts most of Junio’s suggestions: he agrees to shorten the "blank line" explanation, drop the "More concretely" transition phrase, and rephrase the trailer block definition to avoid redundancy. He also corrects minor typos ("the the" → "of the") and clarifies terminology ("input" → "commit log message"). One point of pushback: he argues that the two cases defining a trailer block (all trailers vs. at least 25% trailers) are not redundant, citing the original implementation rationale from 2016.

The changes are purely stylistic, preserving the technical accuracy of the documentation. This email represents the last round of feedback before the series was merged, closing the thread’s primary discussion. The post-merge stylistic debate about "trailer metadata" vs. "trailers metadata" remains unresolved but is noted as a low-priority suggestion.
