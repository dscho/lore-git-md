This is patch 4/17 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series, a refactoring step that makes the parent-child relationship between the "files" and "packed" sources explicit. The patch replaces the generic `struct odb_source *source` field in `struct odb_source_packed` with a concrete `struct odb_source_files *files` pointer, clarifying that the packed source’s parent is always the files source. This is a temporary measure: the parent pointer will be removed entirely by the end of the series (patch 17/17), completing the packed source’s transition to a standalone ODB source.

The change is purely mechanical, touching only type declarations and the corresponding constructor (`odb_source_packed_new()` now takes a `struct odb_source_files *` instead of a `struct odb_source *`). All downstream callers in `odb/source-files.c` and `packfile.c` are updated to pass the correct pointer, and the diff is symmetric (11 insertions, 11 deletions). No behavior changes are introduced; the patch exists solely to make the code’s intent clearer during the intermediate stages of the ODB abstraction.

This patch is part of the series’ broader goal of aligning the packed source’s interface with the loose source’s precedent, eliminating parent pointers, and enabling the packed source to function as a fully independent ODB backend. It follows the mechanical rename (patch 1/17), packfile list extraction (patch 2/17), and packed source relocation (patch 3/17), and precedes the callback wiring and MIDX interface refactoring that complete the abstraction. The series remains blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues, but this patch itself is uncontroversial and has already received surface-level approval from Justin Tobler in v2.
