This is patch 4/17 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series, now in its third iteration. The email is a reply clarifying the motivation behind a small but critical refactoring: replacing the generic `struct odb_source *` parent pointer in `struct odb_source_packed` with a concrete `struct odb_source_files *`. Patrick explains that this intermediate step reduces ambiguity during the transition by making it immediately obvious whether code is dealing with the packed source’s parent (the files source) or its embedded `base` field. The change is preparatory—it doesn’t advance the ultimate goal of removing the parent pointer entirely, but it makes subsequent patches easier to reason about by eliminating confusion between the two generic pointers.

The exchange remains surface-level, with no deeper technical discussion of the ODB abstraction’s design or the refdb lifecycle issues that are currently blocking the series. Justin’s original review was purely mechanical, and Patrick’s reply is a targeted clarification rather than a broader architectural justification. The patch itself is tiny (+11/-11 lines) and affects only the type system, but it’s a necessary stepping stone toward the fully independent packed ODB backend that the series aims to deliver.
