This thread is about a two-patch series that moves Git’s global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the ongoing effort to eliminate global state and remove reliance on `the_repository`. The series, authored by Justin Tobler (patch 1) and Tian Yuchen (patch 2), is now complete at v1 and touches 14 call sites across core subsystems, the Windows compatibility layer, and built-in commands.

The work has been generally uncontroversial so far, with the authors addressing two of three initial RFC questions in the commit messages—early-initialization robustness and Windows layering—but leaving the third (performance overhead in `dir.c`) acknowledged but unaddressed. However, Junio C Hamano has just raised a substantive design concern about the new `repo_get_ignore_case()` getter. He questions why it omits the `repo->gitdir` check used in the getters for `trust_executable_bit`, `protect_hfs`, and `protect_ntfs`, and whether the current logic (`if (repo)`) is sufficient for early initialization. The commit message claims the design "could safely accommodate" early probing, but Junio’s review suggests this may not be robust enough, and he asks what makes `ignore_case` different from the other fields. The authors will need to either justify the current approach or revise the getter to align with the existing pattern. No other reviews have been posted yet.
