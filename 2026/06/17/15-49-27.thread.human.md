This thread is about a two-patch refactoring series that moves Git’s global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`, eliminating a piece of global state and tying the configuration value to the repository instance it came from. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The authors—Justin Tobler (patch 1/2) and Tian Yuchen (patch 2/2)—sent the series in three versions, with the third now fully aligned with project conventions.

The first patch introduces the `repo_ignore_case()` getter and adds the field to `struct repo_config_values`, while the second migrates all usage sites across 14 files, including the index, filesystem operations, the Windows compatibility layer, and nine built-in commands. Junio C Hamano’s early review concerns—about the getter’s design and the use of `repo->initialized`—were fully addressed in v2, and the cover letter was updated in v3 to clarify the rationale behind the getter approach and why a previous attempt by Derrick Stolee failed. The maintainer has now given the series an unconditional "Looks good," dismissing the last open question about including `repository.h` in the Windows compatibility layer as appropriate for Git-specific code.

The series is ready for integration, with no remaining technical or design objections. The only acknowledged trade-off is a potential performance overhead in `dir.c` from repeated getter calls, which the authors accept as necessary for eliminating global state. The work builds on prior migrations of `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` and follows the established pattern for repository-specific configuration getters.
