This thread is a two-patch refactoring series that moves Git’s global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`, tying it to the repository instance it was read from. The goal is to reduce global state as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort, without changing the variable’s eager-parsing behavior. The series is at its first iteration (v1) and is authored by a contributor likely participating in a mentorship program (e.g., Google Summer of Code or Outreachy), with Junio Hamano and Christian Couder listed as mentors.

The first patch introduces the core change: adding the `ignore_case` field to `struct repo_config_values` and providing a getter function, `repo_get_ignore_case()`. It preserves the existing fallback logic for early initialization (e.g., during `git init` or clone) by skipping checks for `repo->gitdir`, addressing one of the RFC questions about correctness. The patch touches `environment.c` and `environment.h` and is mechanical, with no behavior changes or new tests. The second patch (not yet summarized) will handle the performance implications of repeated getter calls in `dir.c` and the inclusion of `repository.h` in Windows-specific code.

So far, the series has no reviews or objections, but the cover letter raises three open questions: whether the fallback logic is correct, whether the getter introduces performance overhead in `dir.c`, and whether including `repository.h` in `compat/win32/path-utils.c` is appropriate. The work builds on prior migrations of `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` into the same structure and references Derrick Stolee’s earlier attempt at a similar change. Feedback is being sought on these RFC points before the series progresses.
