This thread is about a small but important refactoring: moving Git’s global `ignore_case` setting into `struct repo_config_values` to eliminate a global variable and tie the configuration to its repository instance. The series, now merged, consisted of two patches by Justin Tobler and Tian Yuchen, and was part of the broader effort to remove reliance on `the_repository`.

The work is complete—Junio C Hamano accepted the final version (v3) with a "Looks good," resolving all prior feedback. The patches introduced a `repo_ignore_case()` getter, added the field to the repository config struct, and updated all usage sites across the index, filesystem operations, and built-in commands. A minor Windows compatibility layer dependency on `repository.h` was explicitly approved by the maintainer, and the getter’s design now consistently checks `repo->initialized` to match other repository-specific getters.

The only follow-up was a procedural question from Tian Yuchen about whether CC’ing the Windows compatibility layer’s maintainer (Johannes Schindelin) was the right approach for future changes. This doesn’t affect the series itself, which remains uncontroversial and fully integrated.
