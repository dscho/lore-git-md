This thread is a two-patch refactoring series by Justin Tobler (with contributions from Tian Yuchen) that moves the global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`, eliminating a piece of global state as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The series is now complete at v1, with both patches sent.

The goal is to tie the `ignore_case` configuration value to the repository instance it was read from, without changing its eager-parsing behavior. Patch 1 introduced the new `repo_get_ignore_case()` getter and added the field to `struct repo_config_values`, while patch 2 (just arrived) mechanically replaces all direct accesses to the global variable with calls to the getter. The changes touch 14 files across core subsystems, including the index, filesystem operations, Windows compatibility layer, and commands like `git fetch` and `git mv`.

The author has addressed two of the three RFC questions raised in the cover letter: the performance overhead in `dir.c` (justified as a necessary trade-off for eliminating global state) and the inclusion of `repository.h` in `compat/win32/path-utils.c` (accepted as the pragmatic approach to avoid refactoring function signatures). The remaining open question is whether the getter’s current design—skipping `repo->gitdir` checks—is robust enough for early initialization, though the commit message clarifies the reasoning behind this choice.

No reviews have landed yet, but the series looks ready for scrutiny. The Windows compatibility layer change and potential performance implications in `dir.c` may draw discussion, though the author has preemptively justified these trade-offs. The work builds on prior migrations of `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` and aligns with René Scharfe’s broader `the_repository` removal initiative.
