This is a substantive review comment on patch 7/8 of Patrick Steinhardt’s refactoring series for Git’s reference backend. Justin Tobler questions the design choice in the patch that temporarily sets `ref_storage_format` to `REF_STORAGE_FORMAT_UNKNOWN` as a signal to prevent recursive initialization when evaluating "onbranch" config conditions.

The patch addresses a chicken-and-egg problem where the ref store needs config to initialize, but the config needs a ref store to evaluate "onbranch" conditions. The current solution unsets the format temporarily to gate the evaluation, but Justin argues this feels awkward and suggests `include_by_branch()` shouldn’t rely on this signal at all. His comment implies the patch might be working around a deeper issue in how config conditions are evaluated, rather than fixing it cleanly.

This is part of the larger series modernizing the ref backend, which has already seen substantive reviews from Karthik Nayak, Junio C Hamano, and Jeff King. The series aims for incremental improvements while deferring more complex architectural changes (like absolute path handling) to future work. Justin’s feedback here could lead to a rethink of this specific fix, though the rest of the series appears uncontroversial and ready for integration. The discussion is now focused on whether this approach is the right long-term solution or just a temporary workaround.
