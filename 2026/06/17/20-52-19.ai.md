This is the fifth and final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s v2 documentation series updating `SubmittingPatches` to codify Git’s trailer conventions. It addresses trailer ordering, a point that generated substantive discussion in the thread.

The patch adds two short sections to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`: one under the DCO (Developer Certificate of Origin) heading, and another under the commit trailers heading. Both emphasize that trailers should be added in **chronological order**, with each person’s `Signed-off-by` appearing last—after any trailers they themselves added (e.g., `Acked-by`, `Helped-by`). This ensures the sign-off applies to the entire message up to that point, including earlier trailers. The example in the commit message illustrates the principle: a maintainer who adds a late `Acked-by` places it *before* their own `Signed-off-by`, making it clear who contributed what.

The patch reflects feedback from Junio C Hamano, who helped refine the wording to lead with chronological order as the guiding principle. The interdiff notes minor copy-edits ("Drop 'the the'") and the decision to mention the rule in both the DCO and trailers sections for redundancy. No controversy remains; the patch is uncontroversial and ready for merging.

This patch completes the series, which is now fully resolved.
