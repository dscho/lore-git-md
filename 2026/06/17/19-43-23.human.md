This is the final confirmation in the documentation patch series updating Git’s `SubmittingPatches` guidelines for trailer conventions. Kristoffer Haugsbakk agrees with Patrick Steinhardt’s suggestion to omit non-identity trailers (like `Closes:`) entirely from the documentation. The decision is pragmatic: Git itself doesn’t use these trailers, and they’re mostly relevant to downstream projects with bug trackers. Kristoffer’s earlier analysis showed only `Change-Id`, `Message-ID`, and `Fixes` appear with any frequency in Git’s history, and even those are niche.

The patch series is now ready for its v2 update, which will focus on the previously agreed changes: typo fixes, DCO sign-off clarifications for `Based-on-patch-by`, and refinements to trailer ordering principles. The omission of non-identity trailers simplifies the guidelines without losing anything essential. The series remains well-motivated, addressing real ambiguity in Git’s trailer conventions while staying aligned with the project’s workflow. For anyone following Git’s documentation efforts, this is a minor but useful cleanup.
