This is a **mechanical consistency issue** rather than a design flaw, but it’s important for maintainability and script compatibility.

On the reflog question—whether renaming a reference without a reflog should create one for the new reference—Junio asks whether the behavior should be configurable via a `--create-reflog` option. This is a **design edge case** that could affect user expectations, particularly for scripts or tools relying on reflog consistency. Junio does not prescribe a solution, but the question signals that the current behavior (leaving the new reference without a reflog) may need justification or refinement.

The review is **substantive but narrow**, focusing on two specific points without challenging the broader design of the `rename` subcommand.
