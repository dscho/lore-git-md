This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part v3 series completing the ODB abstraction for packed object storage. It is a pure mechanical rename: `struct packfile_store` becomes `struct odb_source_packed` to align the naming with the loose-source precedent and signal that the packed backend is now a proper object-database source. The patch touches only three files (`odb/source-files.h`, `packfile.c`, and `packfile.h`) and is a straight 46-line search-and-replace with no functional change. Justin Tobler’s surface-level review of the mechanical changes in v2 has already approved this step, and the diffstat (46 insertions, 46 deletions) confirms the mechanical nature of the conversion. This patch is the foundation for the subsequent interface alignment and callback wiring that will turn the packed backend into a full-fledged `struct odb_source`.
