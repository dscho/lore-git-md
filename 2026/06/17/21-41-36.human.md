Junio C Hamano points out a minor inconsistency in AsciiDoc anchor placement: the patch places the `[[commit-trailers]]` anchor *after* the section title (`=== Commit trailers`), while most other sections in `SubmittingPatches` (and likely other documentation files) place the anchor *before* the title. He cites the "patch flow" section as an example and suggests the project should standardize on one style, though he does not specify which.

The comment is purely about markup consistency and does not engage with the patch’s content or the series’ broader goal of standardizing Git’s trailer conventions. Since the series is already merged, this feedback is likely intended for future documentation patches rather than as a blocker for this one. The observation is typical of Junio’s attention to detail in project conventions.
