This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part documentation series updating the `SubmittingPatches` guidelines for trailer conventions. It addresses trailer ordering, a small but important detail that affects how contributions are credited and legally attested.

The patch adds a clear rule: trailers should appear in chronological order, with each person’s `Signed-off-by` last, as it applies to everything preceding it. This ensures the sequence of acknowledgments (e.g., `Acked-by`, `Helped-by`) reflects the actual flow of review and modification. The example in the commit message illustrates how a maintainer’s later ack appears after the contributor’s sign-off, making it unambiguous who added what.

The change is minimal—just ten lines added to two sections—but it resolves a long-standing ambiguity in the guidelines. Junio C Hamano helped shape the phrasing, emphasizing chronological order as the guiding principle. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with existing practice on the mailing list. With this, the series is now complete and ready for merging.
