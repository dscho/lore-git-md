This is the fourteenth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series, completing the callback wiring for the packed object storage backend. The patch moves the `freshen_object()` logic from `packfile.c` into `odb/source-packed.c` and registers it as the packed source’s callback, mirroring the loose source’s precedent. With this change, all required ODB callbacks except one (`freshen_object()` was the last missing piece) are now wired up, and the `find_pack_entry()` helper can finally be made static, fully encapsulating it within the packed source implementation.

The diff is small (24 insertions, 29 deletions) and mechanical: the `packfile_store_freshen_object()` function is relocated verbatim, renamed to `odb_source_packed_freshen_object()`, and registered in the packed source’s constructor. The files source’s `freshen_object()` callback is updated to use the new ODB-prefixed interface, and the now-unused `find_pack_entry()` declaration is removed from `odb/source-packed.h`. The change is straightforward and aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating all packed-source logic under the `odb/` subsystem.

This patch is part of the final large-scale ODB restructuring Patrick plans; the series as a whole is blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues (memory leaks, duplicate refdb creation) that he is addressing in a separate 11-patch fix series. Justin Tobler’s earlier surface-level reviews of the mechanical changes in this series have been positive, but no deeper technical review of the callback logic or edge cases has occurred yet. The patch itself looks clean and uncontroversial, but its value depends on the broader ODB abstraction effort progressing past the current refdb roadblock.
