This is a documentation patch in the final stages of review, part of Weijie Yuan’s ongoing effort to clarify patch review etiquette for new contributors. Junio C Hamano responds to the second patch in the v2 series, which adds guidance about how often to send updated versions of a patch series.

The patch itself is uncontroversial in its core advice—batching rerolls to respect reviewer bandwidth—but Junio questions two specific recommendations. First, he suggests that sending a new version *sooner* after substantial rework may not actually save reviewer time, since rushed updates risk introducing new issues. Instead, he proposes a simple reply to the review thread acknowledging the need for major changes and asking reviewers to hold off until the next version. Second, he sees no need to codify an exception for near-final patches, noting that marking an iteration as “final” already serves the same purpose without special timing rules.

The discussion remains constructive and narrow: these are refinements to wording, not objections to the patch’s overall goal. The rest of the guidance—defaulting to no more than one reroll per day, considering series size and review depth, and tying cadence to patch polish—stands unchallenged. Weijie will likely tweak the phrasing in a v3, but the substance of the update is already well-supported by the thread’s earlier consensus.
