This is patch 7/17 in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series, now in its second iteration. The patch moves the packed-object-store's `reprepare()` logic into `odb/source-packed.c` and registers it as the ODB source's callback. This is a purely mechanical step that maintains functional equivalence while aligning the packed source's interface with the loose source's precedent.

Justin Tobler's review remains surface-level, noting only the 1:1 relocation of the existing `packfile_store_reprepare()` and `packfile_store_prepare()` logic and observing that the prepare-vs-reprepare naming is confusing but deferring any redesign. Patrick acknowledges the confusion and confirms he has already written the follow-up series that will convert the `reprepare()` callback into a generic `prepare()` callback with a force-reprepare flag, though this work is not part of the current series.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and introduces no behavioral changes. It relocates the entire prepare/reprepare machinery—including `prepare_pack()`, `prepare_packed_git_one()`, pack-sorting logic, and `report_garbage()` infrastructure—from `packfile.c` to `odb/source-packed.c`, updates callers in `builtin/grep.c`, `midx.c`, and `odb/source-files.c` to use ODB-prefixed functions, and removes the now-redundant `packfile_store_prepare()` and `packfile_store_reprepare()` declarations from `packfile.h`. The callback is registered in the packed source's constructor, mirroring the loose source's pattern.

No deeper review of the prepare/reprepare logic, edge cases, or performance implications has occurred, and the patch remains blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues that require an 11-patch fix series. The ODB abstraction effort is now mechanically complete, with all callbacks wired up and MIDX interfaces refactored, but the refdb problems must be resolved before the series can progress.
