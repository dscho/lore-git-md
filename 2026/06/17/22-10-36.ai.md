This is a process reflection from the author of the `--track=fetch` feature series, now at v13. Harald Nordgren acknowledges that the series is unlikely to be merged after Junio C Hamano’s sustained reservations about the workflow value of automatically fetching from the network during checkout. He expresses frustration that the review process focused on technical implementation details (code quality, refactoring, tests) before resolving the fundamental question of whether the feature should exist at all. The email argues that this sequence wasted effort—both his and reviewers’—by iterating on a feature whose core premise was never accepted. Harald does not challenge the maintainer’s authority to reject the feature, but suggests that a clearer early "no" would have been more efficient for everyone involved. The tone is candid but not combative, framing the experience as a lesson in project norms rather than a dispute over the outcome. No technical changes or new arguments are introduced; this is purely a meta-discussion about how the Git project handles feature proposals.
