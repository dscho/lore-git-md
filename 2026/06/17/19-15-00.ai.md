This is the maintainer's response to the v13 series adding `--track=fetch` to `git checkout`/`git switch`. Junio C Hamano acknowledges the mechanical quality of the implementation—specifically the refactoring in patch 1/2 that exposes tracking remote lookup functions from `branch.c` and the consistent error handling between `git branch --track` and the new feature—but remains unconvinced about the workflow value of allowing checkout to fetch from the network and update remote-tracking branches without user preview. He views remote-tracking branches as solid reference points and worries the feature encourages a risky workflow. The response also includes a minor header cleanup suggestion for `branch.h` (removing an unnecessary `#include "string-list.h"` in favor of a forward declaration). The series is technically complete and well-executed, but its merge hinges on Junio’s final assessment of whether the feature’s benefits outweigh his workflow concerns.
