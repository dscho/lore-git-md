This is Junio’s biweekly "What’s cooking in git.git" report for June 2026, summarizing the state of the integration branches as Git 2.55-rc1 has just been tagged. The tree is now feature-frozen, so the focus shifts to polishing what is already in 'next' and fixing regressions.

The report lists 38 topics, 10 of which have already graduated to 'master' (mostly small fixes and cleanups). Of the remaining 28, three are marked as new and ready for 'next':

- **mh/fetch-follow-remote-head-config** – adds a global `fetch.followRemoteHEAD` configuration that sets the default for the per-remote `remote.<name>.followRemoteHEAD` setting. This is a small, well-scoped feature that should be uncontroversial.

- **ps/refs-writing-subcommands** – extends the `git refs` builtin with new subcommands (`create`, `delete`, `update`, `rename`) that allow direct manipulation of references without shelling out to `git update-ref`. The series drops the implicit `the_repository` dependency, which is a plus. It looks like a clean, incremental step toward a more unified ref-management interface.

- **po/hash-object-size-t** – fixes a long-standing limitation on Windows and other LLP64 platforms where Git could not hash objects larger than 4 GB. The patch converts object-header buffers and related functions from `unsigned long` to `size_t`, adds test cases, and demonstrates the problem clearly. This is a clear bugfix with good test coverage.

The rest of the topics are either cooking in 'next', stalled, or waiting for review. A few highlights:

- **ps/odb-source-packed** (18 patches) – a major refactoring that moves the packed-object source into the new `odb/` subsystem as a proper `struct odb_source`. This is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort; the series is large but appears to be well-structured.

- **ps/history-drop** (10 patches) – teaches the experimental `git history` command a new `drop` subcommand that removes a commit and replays its descendants onto its parent, mimicking `git rebase --no-rebase-merges`. The series touches `reset` internals and adds a dry-run mode, so it may need careful review.

- **tb/pack-path-walk-bitmap-delta-islands** (6 patches) – enables reachability bitmaps and delta-islands to work together with `--path-walk`, allowing faster packaging in partial-clone scenarios. The series is marked as needing review after recent feedback.

- **hn/branch-prune-merged** (7 patches) – adds a `--delete-merged` option to `git branch` that removes local branches already merged to their upstream. This is a user-facing feature that could be handy but may need UI polish.

Overall, the report shows a typical feature-freeze landscape: a mix of small features, bugfixes, and larger refactorings that are being polished before the final release. The three new topics look ready to graduate; the rest are either in flight or waiting for feedback.
