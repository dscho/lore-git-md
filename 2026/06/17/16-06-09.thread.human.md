This thread is about a documentation update to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` that clarifies how contributors should respond to high-level design critiques. The patch, authored by Junio C Hamano, adds explicit guidance requiring authors to engage with fundamental objections on the mailing list, justify their design choices in cover letters and commit messages, and document how design concerns were resolved. The goal is to prevent patch series from advancing through multiple iterations without addressing core viability questions, which has frustrated reviewers and obscured the project’s decision-making process.

The change is framed as a clarification of existing expectations rather than a new policy, reinforcing Junio’s long-standing emphasis on transparency and accountability in the review process. It adds two paragraphs to the section on responding to reviews and a bullet point to the commit-message guidelines, all focused on ensuring design critiques are addressed before a topic can be merged. The tone is prescriptive but constructive, positioning the requirements as best practices for effective collaboration. No other files are touched, and the update is purely documentation.
