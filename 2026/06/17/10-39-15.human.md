This is a brief procedural follow-up in the thread about the Windows LLP64 large-object support series, which was recently merged to `next`. Johannes Schindelin explains how GitGitGadget (GGG) generates the range-diff output shown in Junio C Hamano’s merge notice.

GGG uses `git range-diff` with `--creation-factor=95` to force a more aggressive matching between patch iterations, ensuring that even heavily modified patches are still compared rather than treated as unrelated. The value 95 was empirically chosen after testing against real-world patch series where later versions diverged significantly from earlier ones. The full rationale is documented in a GGG commit from 2021.

The exchange is purely about tooling mechanics and has no bearing on the technical content of the series itself, which remains merged and complete. It may interest contributors who use GGG and wonder why its range-diff output sometimes differs from a local `git range-diff` invocation.
