This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part v3 series completing the ODB (object database) abstraction. It’s a pure refactoring patch that renames `struct packfile_store` to `struct odb_source_packed` to align with the project’s goal of treating packed object storage as a proper ODB source, just like the loose object source introduced earlier.

The change is mechanical: every occurrence of `packfile_store` in function signatures, struct definitions, and variable names is replaced with `odb_source_packed`. The diffstat shows 46 lines changed across three files (`odb/source-files.h`, `packfile.c`, and `packfile.h`), with no functional changes—just symbol renaming. This sets the stage for the subsequent patches that will convert the packed source into a full-fledged `struct odb_source` and wire up its callbacks.

The patch is part of the final large-scale ODB restructuring Patrick plans; future work should be incremental. It’s already received surface-level mechanical approval from Justin Tobler in v2, and the series as a whole remains blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues that Patrick is addressing in a separate 11-patch fix series. No substantive review of the ODB design or callback implications has occurred yet.
