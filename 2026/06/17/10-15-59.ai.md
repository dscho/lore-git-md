This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series adding reference-writing subcommands to `git refs`. It introduces the `git refs delete` subcommand, which mirrors `git update-ref -d` but with a more discoverable interface. The implementation is straightforward: it parses command-line options (`--message` and `--no-deref`), validates arguments, and delegates to `refs_delete_ref()` from the refs API. The patch includes 130 lines of new tests covering basic deletion, old-value verification, symref handling, reflog updates, and error cases (empty messages, invalid OIDs, wrong argument counts).

The patch is a clean, incremental step toward consolidating reference manipulation under `git refs`. It avoids design controversy by sticking to well-established semantics (matching `git update-ref -d` exactly) and focuses on correctness and test coverage. The test suite is thorough, covering all documented functionality and edge cases, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale for a dedicated subcommand rather than a flag-based approach. This patch is uncontroversial and ready to merge; the unresolved design questions about atomicity and backwards compatibility in the series are confined to the `update` and `create` subcommands introduced later.
