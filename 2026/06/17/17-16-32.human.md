This is Junio C Hamano’s review of the first patch in the two-patch series that moves the global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The review is substantive, focusing on a single technical point: why the new `repo_get_ignore_case()` getter omits the `repo->gitdir` check that similar getters for `trust_executable_bit`, `protect_hfs`, and `protect_ntfs` include.

Junio questions the asymmetry, asking whether `ignore_case` is truly special or if the same early-initialization logic should apply to all four fields. He suggests either unifying the getter design (all with `repo->gitdir` checks or all without) or using a stricter `repo->initialized` guard instead. The review is concise but clearly identifies a design inconsistency that could affect correctness during repository startup. No other issues are raised, and the patch otherwise appears uncontroversial. The ball is now in the authors’ court to justify or adjust the getter’s behavior.
