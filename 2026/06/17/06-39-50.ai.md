This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 17-part ODB abstraction series (v3), targeting the packed object storage backend. It moves the `reprepare()` logic from `packfile.c` into `odb/source-packed.c` and wires it up as the ODB source callback, completing the mechanical alignment of the packed source’s interface with the loose source’s precedent.

The patch relocates the entire prepare/reprepare machinery—157 lines of code—into the new `odb/source-packed.c` file, including the `prepare_pack()` helper, `prepare_packed_git_one()`, the pack-sorting logic, and the `report_garbage()` infrastructure. The corresponding 160 lines are removed from `packfile.c`, and the now-redundant `packfile_store_prepare()` and `packfile_store_reprepare()` declarations are dropped from `packfile.h`. Callers in `builtin/grep.c`, `midx.c`, and `odb/source-files.c` are updated to use the new `odb_source_packed_prepare()` and `odb_source_reprepare()` functions, while `packfile_store_get_packs()` and `find_pack_entry()` switch to the ODB-prefixed variant. The `reprepare()` callback is registered in the packed source’s constructor, mirroring the loose source’s pattern.

The commit message acknowledges that the current `reprepare()` callback name is suboptimal and hints at a future redesign to introduce a generic `prepare()` callback with a force-reprepare flag, but defers that work to a separate series. No substantive review of the callback logic or edge cases has occurred yet; Justin Tobler’s earlier feedback on the mechanical changes in v2 was limited to confirming the interface alignment. The series remains blocked by unresolved refdb lifecycle issues that Patrick is addressing in an 11-patch fix series.
