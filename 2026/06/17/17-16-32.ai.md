This is a maintainer review of the first patch in the two-patch series that moves the global `ignore_case` variable into `struct repo_config_values`. Junio C Hamano questions the design choice in the newly introduced `repo_get_ignore_case()` getter, specifically why it omits the `repo->gitdir` check that is present in the getters for `trust_executable_bit`, `protect_hfs`, and `protect_ntfs`.

The review focuses on a single technical point: whether the getter’s current logic (`if (repo)`) is sufficient for early initialization scenarios, or whether it should follow the pattern used for the other three fields (`repo->gitdir ? repo_config_values(repo)->field : default`). Junio notes that the commit message claims the current design "could safely accommodate" early probing, but the wording ("could") leaves room for doubt. He asks what makes `ignore_case` different from the other fields, and suggests that either all getters should use the same logic or a more explicit check (e.g., `repo->initialized`) should be introduced.

The review is substantive but narrowly scoped, targeting a correctness concern about initialization order and repository state. It does not address the broader goals of the series (eliminating globals, `the_repository` removal) or the second patch’s changes. The tone is neutral but probing, inviting the author to justify or revise the design. Key technical details for follow-up: the `repo_get_ignore_case()` function, the `repo->gitdir` check, and the comparison to `trust_executable_bit`/`protect_hfs`/`protect_ntfs` getters.
