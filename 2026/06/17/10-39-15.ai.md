This is a procedural follow-up in the thread about the Windows LLP64 large-object support series, now merged to `next`. Johannes Schindelin explains how GitGitGadget (GGG) generates the range-diff output shown in the cover letter. GGG uses `git range-diff` with `--creation-factor=95` to force a more aggressive matching algorithm when comparing patch iterations, ensuring that even heavily modified patches are paired rather than treated as deletions and additions. The value 95 was empirically determined by testing against real-world patch series and represents a trade-off between false positives and false negatives in patch matching.

The email is purely informational and resolves Junio C Hamano’s curiosity about the range-diff mechanics. No technical discussion or code changes are involved; the series itself remains complete and merged. This detail is unlikely to affect future development but may be useful for contributors who use GitGitGadget and notice unexpected range-diff behavior.
