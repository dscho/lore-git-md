This is a follow-up in the priority queue optimization thread, addressing correctness concerns raised by the maintainer. Kristofer Karlsson acknowledges Junio C Hamano's point about unsafe direct accesses to the queue's `.nr` field in the presence of deferred removal logic.

The author reports that renaming the field to `._nr` surfaced several additional unconverted callers beyond the `get_oid_oneline()` case Junio identified, including in fetch-pack, last-modified, path-walk, and commit-reach code paths. While some callers (describe.c and show-branch.c) already account for `get_pending` in their bounds checking, they still improperly access the internal field directly.

Karlsson proposes renaming the field to `nr_internal` (or similar) in v2 to make the private nature more obvious and catch any remaining direct accesses through compilation errors. This addresses the maintainer's concern about in-flight topics potentially introducing new unsafe uses of the field. The exchange shows the optimization's performance benefits (3-6% speedups) are being carefully balanced against correctness requirements in this core data structure used across multiple subsystems.
