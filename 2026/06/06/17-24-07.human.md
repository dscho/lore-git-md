This is a follow-up in the performance optimization thread for Git's priority queue implementation. Kristofer Karlsson acknowledges Junio C Hamano's correctness concerns about direct accesses to the queue's `.nr` field in the presence of deferred removal logic.

The author confirms the issue by renaming `.nr` to `._nr` during testing, which surfaced several unconverted callers across multiple files (`object-name.c`, `fetch-pack.c`, `builtin/last-modified.c`, `path-walk.c`, and `commit-reach.c`). While some callers already account for the new `get_pending` behavior, they still improperly access the internal field directly.

Karlsson agrees with Junio's suggestion to rename the field to prevent misuse, proposing `nr_internal` as a clearer marker of private API status. This indicates the optimization work will need another revision to properly encapsulate the queue's internal state while maintaining the performance benefits. The patch remains promising but requires hardening before integration.
