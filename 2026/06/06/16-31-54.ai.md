Performance optimization follow-up review from the maintainer. Junio C Hamano identifies a potential correctness issue in the priority queue optimization patch - direct accesses to the queue's nr field may now be unsafe due to the deferred removal logic. He specifically points to `object-name.c:get_oid_oneline()` as a case where this could cause problems if a get operation doesn't result in a corresponding put (like with root commits).

The review notes that while the patch converted some direct `.nr` accesses to use the new `prio_queue_size()` helper, there may be other unconverted instances lurking. Junio suggests renaming the field as a way to catch any remaining direct accesses that might now be incorrect with the new deferred removal behavior.

This is a substantive review that identifies a subtle edge case where the optimization could break existing behavior. The concern is particularly relevant because the queue is used in commit traversal paths where correctness is critical. The maintainer is effectively asking for either broader conversion of direct field accesses or a more defensive change to prevent misuse of the internal field.
