This is a follow-up bugfix to a documentation patch series that corrected typos across Git's codebase. Johannes Schindelin identifies an unintended consequence of the typo fixes - two Subversion dump files used in Git's svn tests had their embedded checksums invalidated when the word "hapenning" was corrected to "happening". 

The issue is subtle but important: these dump files contain MD5 and SHA1 checksums of their content, which became incorrect after the spelling change. This caused test failures in CI since svnadmin strictly validates these checksums. Johannes provides a patch that recalculates all affected checksums while preserving the typo correction in human-readable comments. 

The fix demonstrates Git's attention to detail even in test infrastructure. While the original typo fixes were mechanical and well-intentioned, this shows how seemingly harmless changes can have unexpected side effects in test data. Johannes' patch is narrowly focused on the two affected dump files (t9150/svk-merge.dump and t9151/svn-mergeinfo.dump) and includes detailed explanation of both the problem and solution.

This follows Junio Hamano's earlier handling of the typo series, where he noted version control submission issues with v3 and planned to squash fixes into v2. The thread shows how multiple contributors collaborate to ensure even simple changes land correctly.
