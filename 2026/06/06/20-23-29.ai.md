This is a follow-up to the typo-fix series that addresses an unintended side effect of the mechanical corrections. Johannes Schindelin identifies a subtle but important issue caused by the `codespell`-based typo fixes - the correction of "hapenning" to "happening" in Subversion dump test files broke their embedded checksums.

The patch provides a thorough technical explanation: the dump files contain MD5/SHA1 checksums of their content, which became invalid after the spelling change. Since the word length remained the same, only the checksums needed updating. Johannes provides a detailed commit message explaining the problem (including CI failure symptoms) and the solution - recomputing all affected checksums while preserving the typo fix in surrounding comments.

This demonstrates Git's attention to detail even in mechanical changes, where seemingly harmless corrections can have subtle consequences. The patch maintains the original typo correction's intent while ensuring the test infrastructure remains functional. Johannes' contribution shows deep understanding of both the Subversion dump format and Git's test infrastructure.

The patch is ready for immediate inclusion and serves as a good example of how to handle mechanical changes that interact with checksum-sensitive test data. It complements the original typo-fix series while addressing a real-world impact that wasn't caught in initial review.
