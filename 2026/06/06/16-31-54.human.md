Performance optimization follow-up from the maintainer. Junio C Hamano responds to Kristofer Karlsson's patch that optimized Git's priority queue by fusing common get+put operations. While generally positive about the approach, Junio raises a subtle correctness concern about direct access to the queue's `.nr` field in other parts of the code.

The core issue is that the optimization introduces a logical vs physical count distinction - some elements may be "pending removal" but still inflate `.nr`. Junio identifies `object-name.c:get_oid_oneline()` as one case where this could cause problems, as it loops based on `copy.nr` while calling functions that perform get+put sequences. He suggests either auditing all `.nr` accesses or renaming the field to catch unconverted cases.

This is a substantive review that digs into edge cases and cross-file impacts, characteristic of Junio's maintainer-level perspective. The concern isn't about the optimization itself but about ensuring the change doesn't introduce subtle bugs in less-obvious queue usage patterns. The thread appears to need either a broader audit of queue usage or a mechanism to prevent direct field access before the patch can proceed.
