This is the second patch in a two-part series optimizing Git's priority queue implementation, following up on review feedback from v1. The patch hardens the safety of the queue by renaming the internal `.nr` field to `.nr_internal` to prevent direct access from outside code, forcing callers to use the new `prio_queue_size()` helper or `prio_queue_for_each()` macro instead.

The changes systematically convert all external accesses across 13 files, including builtin commands (describe, show-branch, last-modified), core subsystems (commit-reach, fetch-pack, revision walking), and negotiators. The patch introduces a new iteration macro that properly accounts for the queue's deferred removal tracking (`get_pending` offset) when walking elements.

This hardening completes the safety improvements requested in v1 review while maintaining the 3-6% performance gains on traversal-heavy operations. The patch demonstrates thorough responsiveness to feedback - every identified direct field access has been converted, edge cases are properly handled, and documentation reflects the new access patterns. The changes are mechanical but wide-ranging, touching priority queue usage across Git's codebase.

The series appears ready for integration, having addressed all substantive correctness concerns while preserving its optimization benefits. The cross-subsystem impact is now properly managed with consistent, safe access patterns. This represents the final piece of an effort to generalize the lazy_queue pattern while eliminating unsafe direct field accesses that could lead to subtle bugs.
