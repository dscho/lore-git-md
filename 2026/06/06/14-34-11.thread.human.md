Tian Yuchen's patch series to move the `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` configuration variables from global storage into repository-specific structs has reached completion after thorough review. This change is part of René Scharfe's ongoing effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The second version of the patch received detailed technical review from Christian Couder, who mentored Tian along with Ayush Chandekar and Olamide Caleb Bello, and incorporates several improvements over v1 including type-safe getters and better handling of default values.

Junio Hamano has now approved the series for inclusion in the `next` integration branch, indicating the technical work meets Git's standards. The only remaining item is a minor commit message tweak regarding whether to use "environment:" or "environment.c:" in the subject line - a point Christian noted and Junio acknowledged he may or may not adjust during merging. The thread demonstrates Git's typical workflow where substantive review happens early, leaving only trivial polish for the final version. All participants agree the patch maintains the required Windows filesystem validation behavior while successfully completing this step of the larger architectural refactoring.
