Optimization of Git's priority queue implementation with deferred removal tracking and operation fusion, now hardened against unsafe direct field access. The v3 series finalizes the lazy_queue pattern generalization from builtin/describe.c into core prio_queue functionality, incorporating all feedback from prior iterations.

Key technical changes:
- Core optimization: `get_pending` flag enables deferred removals, fusing get+put operations to avoid full remove-rebalance-insert cycles
- Safety hardening: renamed internal `.nr` to `.nr_internal` to surface direct accesses (alternative `nr_` naming convention proposed by Junio), converted all callers across 13 files to use new `prio_queue_size()` helper or `prio_queue_for_each()` macro
- Final refinement: inlined flush logic into get()/peek() functions per René Scharfe's suggestion, eliminating helper function overhead while maintaining clarity
- Maintains 3-6% speedups on traversal-heavy operations (merge-base, revision walking) with measured 1.7-2.7% improvements in common traversal patterns
- Eliminates duplicate lazy_queue from builtin/describe.c, builtin/show-branch.c, and commit.c
- Comprehensive conversion of priority queue usage in builtin commands (describe, show-branch, last-modified) and core subsystems (commit-reach, fetch-pack, revision walking, negotiators)
