Optimization of Git's priority queue implementation with deferred removal tracking and operation fusion, now finalized after four iterations. The series generalizes the lazy_queue pattern from builtin/describe.c into core prio_queue functionality, eliminating duplicate optimizations while maintaining measured 1.7-2.7% speedups on traversal-heavy operations (merge-base, revision walking).

Key technical changes:
- Core optimization: `get_pending` flag enables deferred removals, fusing get+put operations into single replaces to avoid full remove-rebalance-insert cycles
- Interface hardening: renamed internal `.nr` to `.nr_` and introduced `prio_queue_size()`, `prio_queue_for_each()` helpers to enforce proper access patterns across 13 files
- Implementation refinement: removed three independent lazy_queue implementations (describe.c, commit.c, show-branch.c) in favor of core handling
- Safety: added guard for empty-queue check with `nr_ <= get_pending` and removed now-unused `prio_queue_replace()`
- Performance: maintains speedups across all benchmarked scenarios (30-run Xeon tests show all variants within 1.3% envelope)
