This is a **reply from Taylor Blau** addressing Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of patch 8/10 in the RFC series combining `--geometric` and `--cruft` repack modes. The patch introduces `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` to ensure reachable objects from rolled-up packs are included in geometric repacks while unreachable objects go to cruft packs.

Taylor acknowledges the correctness issue Junio raised: the current implementation incorrectly includes unreachable annotated tags (and objects reachable only from those tags) in the output pack, undermining the separation of reachable and unreachable objects. He agrees the logic needs reworking and sketches a potential fix—reversing the traversal order to start from reference tips, then marking objects as starting points only if they appear in included packs and not in excluded ones. This would replace the two-phase traversal with a single pass, avoiding the over-retention of unreachable objects.

The reply is brief but confirms the issue is real and non-trivial. No revised patch is included yet, but the proposed approach suggests a significant refactor of the reachability logic. This is a critical blocker for the series, as the correctness of `--stdin-packs=follow-reachable` is central to the combined `--geometric --cruft` workflow. The discussion is now focused on how to fix the traversal, not whether the problem exists.
