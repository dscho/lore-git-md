This is the fourth patch in Karthik Nayak’s v2 seven-patch series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat, whitespace checking, and ``-G`` pickaxe. It extracts the boilerplate line-range filter setup and teardown into two reusable helpers: ``line_range_filter_init()`` for zeroing the struct and wiring the callback, and ``line_range_filter_diff()`` for inflating ``ctxlen`` to the largest range span, clearing ``XDL_EMIT_NO_HUNK_HDR``, running the diff, flushing the final hunk, and releasing the buffer. The patch moves the invariants (one xdiff hunk per range, hunk headers always emitted) into a single place so every consumer—patch, diffstat, and check—can reuse them without repeating the logic. No behavior change: ``builtin_diff()`` still leaves ``XDL_EMIT_NO_HUNK_HDR`` unset, so the helper’s clearing is a no-op until the suppressing consumers arrive in the next two patches. The diff touches only ``diff.c``, replacing 39 lines of open-coded boilerplate with 61 lines of helpers and a two-line call site.
