This is patch 2/7 in the v2 series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat, whitespace checking, and the ``-G`` pickaxe. The patch simplifies the line-range filter by classifying removal lines immediately rather than buffering them, which fixes a bug where deletions just outside the tracked range could leak into the patch output.

The change is a pure refactoring of the line-range filter internals in ``diff.c``. It removes the ``pending_rm`` buffer and related fields (``pending_rm_count``, ``pending_rm_pre_begin``), replacing them with immediate classification of each line as it arrives. This eliminates the need to track old/new counts separately from the buffered lines, as ``flush_range_hunk()`` now derives the counts from the buffer when emitting the synthetic hunk header. The patch also adds ``begin_range_hunk()`` to open the accumulator at the first in-range line and includes a detailed block comment explaining the coordinate model (pre/post-image cursors, 0-based indexing, and how removals are classified).

The test coverage is thorough, adding cases for the leaked trailing deletion bug, the symmetric leading-deletion case, and edge cases at range boundaries (changes at the first and last line of a range, and pure in-range deletions). The tests confirm that the filter now correctly scopes deletions to the tracked range and handles edge cases without leaking content. The patch is well-motivated and clearly explains the bug it fixes, making it a solid step toward the series' goal of consistent range-scoped diff behavior.
