This addresses Karthik’s concern about violating the function’s contract and the need to update transport API documentation. Second, whether the absence of a requested field (e.g., "size") should be treated as an error, a valid outcome, or undefined behavior. Pablo’s arguments for silent continuation—consistency with local behavior and avoiding new "missing value" tokens—are pragmatic but may not fully address Karthik’s concerns about future extensibility.

Pablo’s planned changes (dedicated vtable entry, documentation updates, and code hygiene fixes) will likely resolve most of Karthik’s technical concerns, but the error-handling philosophy may remain a point of debate. The patch’s readiness hinges on these architectural adjustments and whether the silent-failure behavior is deemed acceptable.
