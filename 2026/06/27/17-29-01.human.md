This is the final patch in Karthik Nayak’s seven-part series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat, whitespace checking, and pickaxe options. This patch teaches ``-G`` to honor the tracked line range, so that `` `git log -L:func:file.c -G pattern` `` only matches commits where *pattern* appears in the change to *func*, not anywhere else in the file.

The implementation reuses the line-range filter infrastructure introduced earlier in the series. ``diff_grep()`` now routes its xdiff pass through ``diff_emit_line_ranges()``, which forwards only the lines within the tracked range to the pickaxe callback. The patch exposes this helper in ``xdiff-interface.h`` and threads the filepair’s ``line_ranges`` through the pickaxe machinery. Textconv is deliberately skipped: the driver’s output lacks the original file’s line coordinates, so ``-G`` falls back to whole-file matching when textconv is active.

The documentation update clarifies the distinction between ``-G`` (scoped to the tracked range) and ``-S`` (still whole-file). The test coverage verifies that ``-G`` correctly ignores changes outside the tracked range and confirms the textconv fallback behavior. The patch is clean, well-explained, and fits neatly into the series’ overall design. With this, the series is complete and ready for final review.
