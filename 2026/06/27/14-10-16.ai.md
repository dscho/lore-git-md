This email is a **reply from the author (Tian Yuchen)** to Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of the second patch in the v2 series. The patch moves the global `excludes_file` variable into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the libification effort.

Tian acknowledges Junio’s critique of the temporary guard (`if (repo != the_repository)`) in both the getter and cleanup functions. He concedes that the guard is poorly placed and that the current logic—particularly the `if (!cfg)` check—is nonsensical, as `repo_config_values()` should never validly return NULL. Instead, Tian agrees to adopt Junio’s suggestion: replacing the guard with a check on `repo->initialized`, which aligns with existing patterns in the codebase. This change will remove the guard entirely from `repo_config_values_clear()` and likely from the getter as well, letting BUG() assertions catch callers accessing uninitialized repositories.

The email does not address the build issue (unused `repo` parameter in `repo_excludes_file()`) or the patch structure question (whether to squash the two patches) raised in earlier feedback. Those remain open for future revisions. The next version of the series will focus on implementing the `repo->initialized` check and removing the problematic guard, which should resolve Junio’s primary objection and unblock the series for integration. The discussion now centers on correctness and long-term maintainability, not just mechanical refactoring.
