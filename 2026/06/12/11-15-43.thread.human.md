Kristofer Karlsson's proposal to optimize Git's commit reachability calculations has gained significant traction. Newren confirms the optimization's validity through real-world deployment, though GitHub's current implementation only handles repositories with finite generation numbers. He offers two paths forward: either Karlsson can extend the existing GitHub PR to cover all cases, or Newren can complete the work after his vacation. This moves the discussion from theoretical validation to practical implementation choices.

The optimization shows remarkable promise, with measurements indicating 300-1000x speedups in certain scenarios. The technical details around edge cases (particularly with imported repositories and octopus merges) have been thoroughly vetted through collaboration between Karlsson and Derrick Stolee. With Newren's confirmation of independent discovery and production use, the proposal appears both sound and valuable - the remaining question is simply who will drive the final version that handles all cases.
