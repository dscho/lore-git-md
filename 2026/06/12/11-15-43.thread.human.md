Kristofer Karlsson has proposed a performance optimization for Git's merge-base calculations that could speed up reachability analysis by 300-1000x in simple cases. The initial approach suggested early termination when one branch's commits were fully traversed, but Derrick Stolee identified cases where this would miss merge bases in complex commit graphs with multiple paths between commits.

In his latest response, Karlsson has refined the algorithm to address Stolee's concerns. The key insight is to continue walking the commit graph while there are non-stale commits that could still be merge base candidates, only terminating when either branch is exhausted *and* no viable candidates remain. Karlsson walks through Stolee's counterexample, showing how the modified condition would correctly identify merge bases even in complex DAG topologies where commits are reachable through multiple paths.
