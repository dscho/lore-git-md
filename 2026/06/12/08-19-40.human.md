This is the author's reply to a **substantive review** of the first patch in the `git replay --linearize` series. Toon Claes responds to Justin Tobler’s forward-looking design question about whether the current refactoring (replacing `enum replay_mode` with a `bool reverse` flag) might need to be reverted if a third replay mode is added later.

Toon explains that the enum was primarily used to distinguish the *direction* of replay (forward for `PICK`, reverse for `REVERT`), and that `REVERT` also applies the inverse change. He does not anticipate new modes being added in the foreseeable future, but if one were needed, he suggests it would be better to reintroduce the enum (or introduce a second boolean flag) at that time. The response is pragmatic and leaves the door open for future adjustments without committing to a specific design.

The thread remains open on this point, but the exchange does not block the series—it is a design discussion rather than a correctness issue. The rest of the series (patches 2 and 3) are unaffected by this refactoring and continue to implement the `--linearize` feature as described in the cover letter. No merge status is reported; the series is still under review.
