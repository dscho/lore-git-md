This thread is about a three-patch series from Taylor Blau that fixes a bug in Git's MIDX (multi-pack index) write path when using custom base layers during incremental writes. The issue specifically affected the `--stdin-packs` path, where the MIDX write logic wasn't properly handling cases like `--base=none` or `--base=<hash>`, leading to incorrect reachability closure for bitmaps.

The series is now effectively complete. All technical issues have been resolved, and the patches have passed testing, including previously failing scenarios. The only remaining step was finalizing the commit message for the third patch, which clarifies a relaxed invariant around how `ctx->base_midx` and `ctx->to_include` interact. Patrick Steinhardt provided minor feedback on the proposed wording—suggesting a grammatical tweak and trimming an explanatory paragraph to avoid future staleness—but otherwise approved the changes. Taylor Blau has addressed these suggestions, and Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has already signaled the series is ready for integration once the final wording is incorporated.

The key participants are Taylor Blau (author), who identified and fixed the bug; Patrick Steinhardt, who reviewed the patches and requested commit-message clarifications; and Junio, who confirmed the series is otherwise ready. A minor disagreement about test coverage for `--base=none` (whether to verify the MIDX contains objects from all layers or just the newest one) remains unresolved but doesn’t affect the fix’s correctness. The series is now poised to graduate to Junio’s `next` branch.
