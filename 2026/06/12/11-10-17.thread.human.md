This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren aimed at improving Git's error messages for common command-line mistakes: missing slashes in `git branch --set-upstream-to` and slash-as-space in `git push`. The series has been resubmitted after initial silence but remains unreviewed in depth.

The key development is a substantive review from Junio C Hamano challenging the premise of the first patch. Junio argues that the current error message ("branch 'main' does not exist") is actually correct when the user types `git branch --set-upstream-to origin main` without a local `main` branch, since the command requires the local branch to exist first. He suggests the real issue may be a mismatch between user expectations and the command's intended behavior, not a problem with the error message itself. This review casts doubt on the series' motivation and may require Harald to rethink the approach or provide stronger justification for the change.

The series introduces two new config-based advice options and modifies `builtin/branch.c` and `builtin/push.c` to detect and guide users toward correct syntax. So far, only Weijie Yuan has endorsed the problem statement without engaging with the implementation, and no other participants have weighed in. The open questions now include whether the series addresses a real usability gap and whether the config-based advice approach is the right solution.
