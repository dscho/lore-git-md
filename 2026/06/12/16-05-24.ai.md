This is the first patch in a 3-part refactoring series that migrates the `trust_executable_bit` configuration from global to repository-scoped storage, as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series is now in its final iteration and ready for merging.

Patch 1/3 is a straightforward cleanup that removes redundant `extern` declarations for `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` from `st_mode_from_ce()` in read-cache.c, since these are already declared in the included environment.h header. This is a minor code hygiene improvement that doesn't change behavior.

The patch has been reviewed by multiple contributors (Christian Couder, Ayush Chandekar, and Olamide Caleb Bello) and carries their Mentored-by tags, indicating it's part of a guided contribution. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the project's coding guidelines by removing unnecessary declarations.

This is a mechanical change that prepares for the more substantive repository-scoping work in patches 2/3 and 3/3, which will introduce the new `repo_trust_executable_bit()` getter and migrate all call sites. The series as a whole demonstrates the incremental approach being taken to eliminate global state from Git's codebase.
