This is a follow-up to a documentation patch about email etiquette in code reviews. Weijie Yuan responds to Junio Hamano's positive review of their proposed addition to the MyFirstContribution guide, which advises contributors to trim quoted text in mailing list replies.

The author explains they considered integrating the advice into existing paragraphs but opted for a standalone section for clarity. They express hope that this explicit guidance will help focus review discussions and reduce reviewer burden by establishing shared expectations about quoting practices.

With the maintainer's approval already given and no substantive changes requested, this appears to be a minor but thoughtful documentation improvement that's likely to be merged. The exchange demonstrates the project practice of leaving patches open for additional feedback even after maintainer approval, though in this case no further discussion seems necessary for such a straightforward change.
