This is a substantive review of Taylor Blau's performance optimization integrating `--delta-islands` with `--path-walk` in `git pack-objects`. Derrick Stolee raises important questions about whether the patch series fully demonstrates the intended benefits of combining these features.

Stolee focuses on two key aspects: First, he notes that while the performance improvements from bitmap usage are clearly shown (99.6% time reduction for small fetches), the claimed pack size reductions from path-walk aren't evident in the test results. Second, he questions whether the test repository structure adequately exercises the path-walk benefits, suggesting additional test scenarios may be needed to validate the interaction between these features.

The review highlights that while the technical implementation appears correct (as verified by tests), the performance claims need stronger evidence - particularly around pack size reduction, which was a major motivation for path-walk. Stolee also notes the patch's surprising simplicity given the complexity of the features being integrated, suggesting there may be subtle interactions not yet surfaced.

This review moves the discussion beyond correctness to effectiveness, pushing for clearer demonstration that the combined features deliver their promised benefits. The feedback will likely require Taylor to either provide additional benchmark data or refine the performance claims in the documentation.
