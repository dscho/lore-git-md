Thread identity: Performance optimization proposal for commit reachability algorithms in `paint_down_to_common` function, affecting merge-base calculations. Authored by Kristofer Karlsson as RFC.

Core problem: Current merge-base computations may perform unnecessary traversal after one side of the comparison is fully processed. The proposal introduces an early termination condition when one branch's commits are fully traversed, since no new merge-base candidates can emerge beyond this point.

Current status: RFC stage with theoretical proposal and preliminary test results showing 300x-1000x speedups for merge-base/merge-tree in large monorepos. Not yet implemented as a patch.

Key technical details: Targets `paint_down_to_common` in commit-reach.c, which underlies merge-base operations. The optimization leverages the two-phase traversal (infinite-generation then finite-generation commits) and topological ordering properties. Correctness argument shows termination preserves all merge-base candidates while avoiding redundant work.

Open questions: Theoretical soundness of termination condition needs review from algorithms experts. Impact on edge cases (octopus merges, complex DAG topologies) requires verification. Implementation may reveal additional constraints.
