This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged five-part documentation series that standardizes Git’s trailer conventions in `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. It documents the `Based-on-patch-by:` trailer, which is used when a contributor submits a patch that incorporates work originally authored by someone else. The patch explains when this trailer is appropriate (e.g., when building on a patch sent to the mailing list) and emphasizes the legal precondition that the original author must have signed off on the patch under the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO). This clarification resolves earlier ambiguity about crediting prior work and ensures compliance with Git’s contribution requirements.

The patch is a straightforward addition to the existing list of common trailers, inserting a concise four-line entry that defines the trailer’s purpose and usage. The commit message is minimal but sufficient, given the context provided by the series’ cover letter and prior discussion. The change itself is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ broader goal of codifying established mailing-list practices into clear, prescriptive guidelines. No substantive feedback was raised on this specific patch in the v3 iteration, as the legal and practical concerns were already addressed in earlier revisions.

This patch complements the series’ other updates, such as the exclusion of Linux-specific trailers (`Fixes:` and `Link:`) and the clarification of trailer ordering. It also reinforces the distinction between `Based-on-patch-by:` (for crediting prior patches) and `Co-authored-by:` (for collaborative drafting), which was a point of discussion in the v2 review. The series as a whole is a well-motivated documentation improvement, and this patch is a small but important piece of that effort. Since the series is already merged, this patch is effectively finalized.
