This is the first patch in the GSoC RFC v13 series implementing `git cat-file --batch-command` remote object info support. It fixes a memory leak in the transport-helper subsystem where `data->name` allocated during `transport_helper_init()` was not being freed when disconnecting a transport whose helper had not fully started.

The change is minimal and surgical: moving `FREE_AND_NULL(data->name)` outside the `if(data->helper)` conditional block ensures the name buffer is always freed on disconnect, regardless of whether the helper process was successfully launched. The patch includes a clear reference to the original leak report and maintains the existing error-return behavior.

This is a straightforward bugfix with no behavioral changes, addressing a leak that could accumulate in long-running Git processes or scripts that repeatedly connect and disconnect transports. The fix is well-scoped and appears correct, though it's part of a larger security-focused series where such leaks might have more serious implications than in typical usage. No test coverage is added here, but the leak was likely caught through static analysis or valgrind runs during development of the broader feature.
