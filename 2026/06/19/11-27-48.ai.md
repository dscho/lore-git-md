This is a refactoring series (v4, 10 patches) targeting Git's reference backend infrastructure, part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing effort to modernize the ref storage layer. The series now addresses the root cause of recursive initialization issues with "onbranch" config by selectively disabling ref store usage during config parsing at initialization time, rather than working around the symptom as in v3.

The key architectural change is the introduction of a new `ignore_refdb` flag in `struct config_options` (patch 9/10), which prevents "onbranch" conditions from triggering recursive ref store initialization. This middle-ground solution preserves other include conditions (like `includeif.gitdir`) while avoiding the circular dependency that previously caused double initialization. The series also moves parsing of backend-critical config settings (`core.logAllRefUpdates` in patch 7/10, `core.sharedRepository` for reftable in patch 8/10) back into the ref stores themselves, reducing reliance on the general config subsystem during initialization.

Other notable changes include memory leak fixes (patches 1-2, 6), improved handling of the `GIT_REFERENCE_BACKEND` environment variable (patch 3), and cleanup of the `chdir_notify` subsystem (patches 4-5). The series touches core files like `setup.c`, `refs.c`, and all ref backends (files, packed, reftable), with test coverage showing behavior remains unchanged. The range-diff shows the new approach splits the previous patch 7/8 into three focused commits (7-9/10), reflecting the more targeted solution.

This version responds directly to Jeff King's critique of v3 by addressing the architectural root cause rather than applying a temporary workaround. The solution appears pragmatic and should satisfy both the need for correct initialization and the desire to avoid invasive changes to config parsing. The series remains well-motivated by the goal of simplifying ref storage code, though the expanded scope (now 10 patches) may warrant careful review of the new config-parsing logic. Related to Patrick's ODB abstraction and reftable work with Karthik Nayak.
