This is a documentation patch update in response to Junio C Hamano’s review of the second patch in Weijie Yuan’s v2 series on patch reroll etiquette. The series aims to codify norms around when and how contributors should send updated patches after review.

Weijie acknowledges Junio’s feedback and agrees to revise the contested guidance about sending new versions sooner after substantial rework. Instead of suggesting quicker rerolls, the updated text will recommend that contributors explicitly signal which parts of the series will change, preventing reviewers from wasting time on sections that will be invalidated. This aligns with Junio’s preference for deliberate, well-reviewed updates over rushed revisions.

The discussion around "close to acceptance" rerolls remains unresolved. Weijie clarifies his interpretation: if a series is nearly ready (e.g., marked "Looks good" by Junio or broadly reviewed) and only minor issues remain, a quick reroll to address them is acceptable. Junio’s counterpoint—that authors should instead mark a version as a "final candidate" to solicit last-minute feedback—highlights a procedural difference. Weijie suggests adding a reminder to double-check for unresolved issues before sending a final version, though he defers to Junio’s experience on whether this is already covered elsewhere.

The patch’s fate hinges on resolving these nuances. The `SubmittingPatches` changes are close to consensus, but the `MyFirstContribution.adoc` updates may still be dropped due to redundancy concerns. Weijie’s response models the collaborative workflow the series documents, including proper attribution and responsiveness to feedback. For readers tracking the thread, the key takeaway is that the series is refining its guidance rather than abandoning it, with Junio’s input shaping the final wording.
