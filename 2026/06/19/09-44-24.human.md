This is a small refactoring patch in Matt Hunter’s now-merged 8-patch series that adds a global `fetch.followRemoteHEAD` configuration option. Patch 5/8 plugs a minor control-flow inefficiency in `builtin/fetch.c`: the `git_fetch_config` callback was falling through to `git_default_config` for the known `fetch.output` key, even though the key was already handled. The one-line change makes it return early, matching the style used for every other known key in the same function. No behavior changes; it simply avoids unnecessary config parsing. The patch is uncontroversial and part of the final cleanup that Junio C Hamano called “excellent” before merging the entire series into `master`.
