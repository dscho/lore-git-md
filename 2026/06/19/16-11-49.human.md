This is a brief design confirmation in the ongoing `git history squash` thread. Junio C Hamano endorses Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to **detect and reject** operations where branch or tag refs point to commits inside the squashed range, rather than silently orphaning or retargeting them. The rejection will include an `advice()` message suggesting the `--update-refs=head` flag as a way to explicitly retarget those refs to the squashed commit. This aligns with the conservative, user-explicit design that has already been implemented in the v3 series and keeps the door open for future iteration. No code changes are needed; this email simply records agreement on the final behavior.
