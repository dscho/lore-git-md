This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged five-part documentation series. It adds a short section to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` that explains why trailer order matters: trailers should appear in chronological sequence, and each person who forwards a patch must add their `Signed-off-by:` last, because it covers everything that came before. The patch inserts two concise paragraphs—one under the DCO section and one under the “Commit trailers” section—so the rule is visible in both places. The example shows how a maintainer’s late ack ends up after the contributor’s sign-off, making it clear who added what. Junio C Hamano helped shape the wording, and the patch itself is a straightforward documentation clarification with no open questions.
