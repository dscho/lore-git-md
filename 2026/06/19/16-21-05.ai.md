This is the third and final patch in a now-ready-to-merge series that completes the migration of `trust_executable_bit` from a global variable into `struct repo_config_values`, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch introduces the new repository-scoped getter `repo_trust_executable_bit()`, moves the configuration field into the repository-specific struct, and updates all call sites in `apply.c` and `read-cache.c` to use the new getter (with a temporary fallback to `the_repository`). The default value (1) is initialized in `repo_config_values_init()`, and the global declaration is removed from `environment.h`. The change is purely mechanical and behavior-preserving, maintaining eager parsing for performance while eliminating another piece of global state. Reviewers (Christian Couder, Ayush Chandekar, Olamide Caleb Bello) have signed off on the implementation, and Junio C Hamano has confirmed the series is ready for merging after this final reroll.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the patch touches `environment.c/h`, `apply.c`, and `read-cache.c`; introduces the new getter `repo_trust_executable_bit()` with repository pointer validation; preserves fallback to `the_repository` due to deep integration; and removes the redundant global declaration. The migration pattern established here (repository-scoped storage with getter function) may serve as a template for future configuration migrations, though the more complex `has_symlinks` case was deferred due to implementation complexity.
