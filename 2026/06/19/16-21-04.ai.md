This is the second patch in the v4 series migrating `trust_executable_bit` to repository-scoped storage as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. It moves the `ce_mode_from_stat()` helper from `read-cache.h` (where it was a static inline) into `read-cache.c` and updates its documentation.

The change is purely mechanical and preparatory: the function's implementation is relocated to allow future access to repository-specific configuration logic, though no such logic is introduced yet. The patch removes the redundant `extern` declarations for `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` (already covered by `environment.h`) and improves the function's header documentation to clarify its purpose. Behavior remains unchanged, but the relocation is necessary groundwork for patch 3/3, which will introduce repository-scoped access to `trust_executable_bit`.

Files touched: `read-cache.c` (implementation added) and `read-cache.h` (declaration and documentation updated).
