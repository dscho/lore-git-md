Core problem or goal
Eliminate the global `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` variables by moving them into the per-repository `struct repo_config_values`, thereby removing implicit `the_repository` dependencies and aligning with the project’s ongoing libification effort.

Current status
v4 is the latest iteration. The only change from v3 is the addition of a `repo->initialized` check in the new accessor functions (`repo_protect_hfs()` and `repo_protect_ntfs()`). The patch is a mechanical refactor with no behavioral change; it has incorporated prior feedback from Derrick Stolee and Glen Choo.
