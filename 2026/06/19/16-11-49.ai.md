This is a brief follow-up in the `git history squash` design discussion, specifically about the handling of refs pointing to commits inside the squashed range. Junio C Hamano endorses Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to **detect and reject** the operation when such refs exist, accompanied by an `advice()` message suggesting the use of `--update-refs` (or `--update-refs=head`) to explicitly retarget them. This aligns with the conservative design already implemented in the v3 series and resolves the last open design question before the feature can graduate from `next` to `master`.

The email is short and decisive, closing the loop on the ref-handling ambiguity that had been under discussion. No new technical concerns are raised; the focus is purely on finalizing the user-facing behavior. The next steps for the author, Harald Nordgren, are to ensure the code matches this agreed-upon design (reject by default, advise `--update-refs`) and to address the three range-resolution concerns Patrick Steinhardt raised in a separate reply (input validation, ancestry-path filtering, and merge commit consistency).
