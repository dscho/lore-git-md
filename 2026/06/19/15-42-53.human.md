This is a **design clarification** in the ongoing `git branch --delete-merged` series, where Junio C Hamano acknowledges Phillip Wood’s point about stacked-branch safety. The email is short but pivotal: Junio concedes that preserving branches used as upstreams for unmerged stacked branches (e.g., `feature1` if `feature2` depends on it) is necessary to avoid breaking `git log @{u}..` and implicit `git rebase` workflows. This reverses his earlier stance and aligns with Phillip’s argument, removing the last major obstacle to the series’ acceptance.

The discussion now hinges on *how* to implement this protection—whether to skip deletion of such branches outright or to automatically update their dependents’ upstreams. The resolution will shape the final behavior of `--delete-merged`, particularly for users who rely on stacked branches. Harald Nordgren’s v16 implementation already includes a per-branch opt-out mechanism (`branch.<name>.deleteMerged`), but this new consensus may require additional logic to handle upstream relationships. Expect a v17 iteration to address this before the series lands.
