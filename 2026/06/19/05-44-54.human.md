This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged five-part documentation series. It adds a short but important clarification to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`: trailers must be added in chronological order, and each person who forwards a patch must place their own `Signed-off-by:` last, because it covers everything that came before it. The patch inserts two concise paragraphs—one in the DCO section and one in the trailers section—so the rule is visible in both places. Junio C Hamano helped shape the wording, and the example in the commit message makes the principle immediately clear. The change is uncontroversial, well-motivated, and already aligned with how the project actually works.
