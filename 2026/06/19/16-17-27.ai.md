This is a documentation patch updating `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to codify how contributors should address design critiques in patch resubmissions. It is the third iteration (v3) of a maintainer-driven clarification, now incorporating feedback from Kichael and Kristoffer Haugsbakk.

The patch adds 19 lines to two sections of `SubmittingPatches`. In the "Responding to Reviews" section, it instructs authors to defend high-level design choices on the mailing list before revising implementations, to explain those decisions more clearly in cover letters and commit messages, and to ensure reviewers can see why the updated version is justified. It also explicitly states that topics with unresolved fundamental critiques will not be merged. In the "Commit Message Guidelines" section, it adds a bullet point requiring commit messages to record how design concerns raised during review were resolved.

This version softens the prescriptive tone of v2 while preserving the core intent. The new phrasing ("Be particularly mindful of critiques...") is less directive than v2's "You should", and the examples of design critiques ("questioning if the feature is worth implementing") are more concrete. The patch still treats code as a tool for clarity but no longer implies that contributors who cannot implement their ideas are inherently unable to defend them. The unresolved tension from earlier rounds—whether this requirement conflicts with the project's "show me the code" norm—remains unaddressed, but the softer framing may reduce the risk of discouraging early feedback.

The changes are narrowly scoped to contributor workflow documentation and introduce no new technical terms or dependencies. The patch is unlikely to be controversial given Junio's role as maintainer and the incremental nature of the updates, though the broader cultural question about design-first contributions remains open. Future discussion may focus on whether the project should promote alternative venues (e.g., GitGitGadget's idea tracker) for speculative design discussions.
