This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v4 refactoring series (1/10) for Git’s reference backend infrastructure. It performs a targeted refactoring in `setup.c` by inlining the `check_and_apply_repository_format()` helper function into its two callsites. The motivation is preparatory: a subsequent patch will modify the order of repository format reading and application at one of these callsites, making the helper function unsuitable. The change is mechanical and does not alter behavior, but it sets the stage for the architectural improvements that follow in patches 7–9. The patch touches only `setup.c` and is uncontroversial, matching the thread’s earlier characterization of patches 1–6 as technically unchanged from v3.
