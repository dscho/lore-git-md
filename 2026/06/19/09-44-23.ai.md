This is a refactoring patch in Matt Hunter’s now-merged 8-patch series that adds the global `fetch.followRemoteHEAD` configuration option. Patch 4/8 renames the static helper `report_set_head()` to `warn_set_head()` in `builtin/fetch.c` to match the terminology used in the `followRemoteHEAD` option values (`warn-if-not-$branch`, etc.). The change is purely lexical: the function’s signature and behavior are unchanged, and only the two call sites are updated. The patch is part of the series’ final polish before integration.
