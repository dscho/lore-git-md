This is a documentation patch series (five parts, v3) updating the `SubmittingPatches` guidelines to clarify and standardize Git’s trailer conventions. The series is now fully merged, with this v3 addressing a final AsciiDoc formatting nit: moving the `[[commit-trailers]]` anchor *before* the section header to match project-wide style.

The patches codify long-standing but previously undocumented practices around crediting contributions, trailer formatting, and ordering. Key points include: encouraging trailers (like `Helped-by` or `Co-authored-by`) only for *substantial* help to avoid over-crediting; discouraging Linux-specific trailers (`Fixes:`, `Link:`) that don’t fit Git’s workflow; documenting the `Based-on-patch-by` trailer with explicit DCO sign-off requirements; enforcing consistent markup for trailer references; and mandating chronological ordering (e.g., `Signed-off-by` last, as it applies to the entire commit).

The changes are uncontroversial and reflect consensus from prior mailing list discussions. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt provided substantive feedback on legal compliance (DCO sign-offs) and ordering rationale, while the final v3 tweak aligns the anchor placement with Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing documentation standardization effort. This series is a clean, incremental improvement to contributor onboarding materials.
