Jeff King raises an important technical concern about Patrick Steinhardt's ref backend refactoring series that aims to eliminate `chdir_notify_reparent()`. He points to historical performance optimizations from using relative paths (specifically commit 044bbbcb63) and questions whether moving to absolute paths might regress those gains.

The note highlights:
- The original motivation for relative paths was measurable speed improvements in `setup_work_tree()`
- The performance impact today is uncertain - filesystems and hardware have evolved since 2008
- The depth of repository paths in the filesystem could affect the magnitude of any regression

This is a substantive review that digs into the historical context and potential second-order effects of the proposed changes. While not blocking the series outright, it surfaces a consideration that wasn't addressed in the original patches. The question doesn't challenge the technical correctness of the changes but rather probes whether they might undo past optimizations that were intentionally designed into the system.
