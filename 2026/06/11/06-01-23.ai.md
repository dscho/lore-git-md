Most substantively, Peff strongly agrees with Hunter's proposal to omit the "warn-if-not-$branch" value from the global setting, providing clear rationale about its remote-specific nature. His reasoning about the ease of adding it later versus the pain of removing it demonstrates practical experience with Git's configuration evolution patterns.
