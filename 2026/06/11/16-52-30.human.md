This is Junio Hamano's review of the fourth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's series documenting commit trailer conventions in the SubmittingPatches guidelines. The review focuses on the `Based-on-patch-by` trailer section, where Junio raises a substantive concern about copyright implications.

Junio notes that the current wording could be read as encouraging the inclusion of patches that lack proper sign-off (DCO attestation), which would be problematic for non-trivial contributions. He suggests clarifying that the trailer should only be used when the original material came with sign-off, or that contributors should first seek proper sign-off from the original author when incorporating their work.

The review maintains the series' generally positive reception while identifying an important legal consideration that wasn't fully addressed in the initial patch. This is a substantive review that engages with the real-world implications of the documented practice, typical of Junio's maintainer perspective. The feedback will likely lead to a v2 revision that tightens the wording around copyright and sign-off requirements.
