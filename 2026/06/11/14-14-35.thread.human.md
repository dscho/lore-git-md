This thread proposes adding email etiquette guidance to Git's "My First Contribution" documentation, specifically about trimming quoted text in mailing list replies. The patch from Weijie Yuan cites existing similar advice from both Git's ReviewingGuidelines and Linux kernel docs, noting that maintainer Junio Hamano has previously emphasized how over-quoting makes discussions harder to follow. The change would add a short, focused paragraph to the onboarding guide explaining when and how to trim quoted text. The proposal appears well-aligned with project norms and uncontroversial in nature, with the author openly inviting feedback on wording. As a documentation-only change that codifies existing best practices, this seems likely to be accepted with minimal discussion.
