Weijie Yuan has proposed adding email etiquette advice about trimming quoted text in mailing list replies to Git's MyFirstContribution guide, citing similar guidance from both Git's ReviewingGuidelines and Linux kernel documentation. The small but useful documentation change adds a clear five-line explanation of when and how to trim quoted text to improve discussion readability.

Maintainer Junio Hamano reviewed the patch positively, agreeing the addition makes sense and finding the proposed text well-placed between sections about handling comments and email configuration. In a subsequent email, Yuan acknowledged Junio's approval, explaining they considered integrating the text into existing paragraphs but chose a standalone presentation for clarity. With Junio indicating he will queue the patch pending any further input and no objections raised, this straightforward documentation improvement appears ready for merging. The change codifies established mailing list best practices to help new contributors participate more effectively.
