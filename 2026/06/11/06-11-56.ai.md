This is a documentation patch review from Jeff King (Peff) on the final patch in a series fixing AsciiDoc markup in git-config man pages. The patch addresses octothorpe (#) escaping to prevent incorrect rendering as AsciiDoc highlight markup.

Peff confirms the technical approach works but suggests considering backticks instead of backslashes for metacharacters, noting this would provide both escaping and semantic markup (typewriter font in output). He observes an inconsistency in how git-fast-import handles `#` versus other literals in HTML output, hinting at potential AsciiDoc quirks.

The review identifies one remaining opportunity - escaping the second octothorpe in the same paragraph for consistency, building on Junio's earlier suggestion about thorough escaping. This is a substantive review that goes beyond surface-level markup fixes to consider rendering semantics and broader documentation conventions.

The feedback doesn't block merging but offers an improvement path. The series appears otherwise complete, with all prior review feedback addressed. This continues the project-wide effort to standardize man page formatting under Jean-Noël Avila's leadership.
