This is a substantive review from Derrick Stolee pushing back on the proposed consolidation of reachability functions in commit-reach.c. The review comes after Kristofer Karlsson's v2 patch attempted to address earlier feedback by unifying `get_reachable_subset()` and `tips_reachable_from_bases()` into a single function with configurable traversal modes (DFS and priority queue).

Stolee raises several key objections:
1. The unified implementation creates unnecessary complication by essentially maintaining two algorithms in one function, defeating the original simplification goal
2. The commit message fails to adequately justify why merging the implementations is beneficial
3. The change could introduce performance regressions for repositories without commit-graphs
4. The change should be split into two logical steps if pursued (first adding the new mode, then migrating callers)
5. The author should discuss major direction changes in the thread before implementing them

The review provides important historical context - Stolee was the original author of both functions and notes they were intentionally kept separate due to different performance characteristics in different repository states (with/without commit-graphs). This feedback suggests the v2 approach may not be mergeable in its current form and that the author should either revert to the v1 approach with better performance documentation or engage in more discussion before proceeding.

The review is technically detailed, referencing specific performance tradeoffs and suggesting an alternative patch structure, while also addressing communication process concerns. This represents a significant development in the thread that will likely require either substantial revision or abandonment of the consolidation approach.
