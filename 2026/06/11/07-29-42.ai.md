This is a review of a performance optimization patch that adds cycle detection to Git's commit traversal for `--contains` queries. Jeff King (Peff) provides a substantive technical review, identifying several small improvements while generally approving the approach.

The review focuses on three main areas:

1. **Const correctness** - Peff notes the removal of `const` from a parameter and suggests propagating const-correctness through related functions (`repo_is_descendant_of` and `can_all_from_reach`), though acknowledges this may be "const pedantry."

2. **Error handling** - He questions the use of `exit(128)` in the cycle fallback path, suggesting `die()` would be more conventional. He also notes the redundant `init_contains_cache()` call and examines how errors propagate through the call stack.

3. **Test hygiene** - Points out that test cleanup commands should be &&-chained for better error detection, though recognizes this is a minor nit.

The review confirms the core approach of caching cycle detection results is sound, even if suboptimal in some edge cases, and that the test coverage is good. Peff's feedback is characteristically thorough, catching subtle implementation details while maintaining perspective on what matters for correctness versus perfection.
