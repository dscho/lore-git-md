This thread covers Miklos Vajna’s patch series fixing a long-standing limitation in `git log --follow`, which previously failed to track renames across merge commits. The issue stemmed from a global variable (`opt->diffopt.pathspec`) being overwritten during traversal of different parent paths, particularly affecting subtree merges. The solution introduces a `follow_pathspec_slab` to store per-commit pathspecs, preserving Git’s single-path constraint while enabling correct rename tracking through merges.

The series is now complete and approved.
