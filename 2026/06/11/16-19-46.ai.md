This is the third and final patch in a documentation series fixing AsciiDoc markup issues in git-config man pages. The patch specifically addresses octothorpe (#) escaping in the `--comment` option documentation.

The change replaces direct octothorpe characters with backtick-escaped versions (`#`) to prevent them from being interpreted as AsciiDoc highlight markers. This follows the approach established in v2 of the series after doc-diff testing revealed context-sensitive issues with backslash escaping. The patch also includes minor wording improvements ("And" -> "The") for better readability.

The doc-diff output shows the rendered man page now correctly displays the octothorpes rather than treating them as markup. This is a mechanical but important fix to ensure accurate documentation rendering, particularly for the `--comment` option which specifically deals with comment characters in config files.

The patch appears well-tested and uncontroversial, representing the final piece of this focused documentation cleanup series. It builds on previous discussion and testing that validated the backtick-escaping approach as the most reliable solution across different rendering contexts.
