This is patch 7/10 in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series adding a `git history drop` subcommand. The patch enhances the reset machinery to allow callers to specify the "from" commit when performing a reset operation, rather than always deriving it from HEAD.

The change adds a new `oid_from` field to `reset_working_tree_options` that callers can populate with the commit they want to reset from. This gives more control to callers like the new `history drop` command, which may need to reset from a commit other than HEAD. The implementation maintains backward compatibility by falling back to reading HEAD when `oid_from` is NULL.

The patch is a straightforward addition that follows the pattern of the existing `oid` field in the options struct. It's part of the broader reset machinery modernization that enables the history drop functionality while also improving the reset API for future use cases. The change is well-contained (just 9 lines of diff) and builds logically on the previous patches in the series.
