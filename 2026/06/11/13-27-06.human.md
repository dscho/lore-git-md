This is a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series introducing the `git history drop` command. It renames `reset_head()` to `reset_working_tree()` to better reflect the function's purpose, as it will soon be modified to optionally skip HEAD reference updates while still resetting the working tree.

The change is mechanical, updating all callers across builtin/rebase.c, reset.c, reset.h, and sequencer.c to use the new name. The corresponding struct `reset_head_opts` is similarly renamed to `reset_working_tree_options`. Phillip Wood suggested this naming change in earlier review rounds.

This patch is part of the broader effort to modernize Git's reset machinery to support the new history drop functionality while also advancing the codebase's architectural cleanliness. The series has received thorough review and appears ready for merging.
