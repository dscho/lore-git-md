This is a documentation patch addressing AsciiDoc markup in git-config man pages, specifically focusing on octothorpe (#) character escaping. Jeff King (Peff) provides a substantive review of the third patch in Tuomas Ahola's series, which has already incorporated Junio C Hamano's earlier feedback about thorough octothorpe escaping.

Peff confirms the current approach works but suggests considering backticks instead of backslashes for escaping in general, as this would provide better visual distinction in rendered output. He notes an inconsistency in how git-fast-import handles similar cases, raising a question about potential special treatment of # characters in AsciiDoc. The review also points out one remaining unescaped # in the context lines that could be addressed while making these changes.

The patch series appears technically sound and nearly ready for merging, with this review offering minor refinements rather than blocking issues. This continues the project's ongoing effort to standardize documentation formatting under Jean-Noël Avila's leadership.
