This is the seventh patch in an 8-part series completing the `the_repository` removal effort by migrating the remaining configuration globals into repository-specific storage. The patch focuses on `core.sparseCheckoutExpectFilesOutsideOfPatterns`, moving it from a global variable to `struct repo_config_values`.

The change prevents cross-repository state leakage while maintaining the existing eager parsing behavior, which is important for consistent sparse-checkout handling across commands. The patch follows the established pattern seen in previous migrations, updating references in environment.c, environment.h, and sparse-index.c.

The work has been thoroughly reviewed by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi, with the series already receiving maintainer approval from Junio Hamano.
