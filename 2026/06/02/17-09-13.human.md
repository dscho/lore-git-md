This is the fifth version of a patch series completing the migration of configuration globals into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series now includes eight patches affecting 20 files across Git's codebase.

The v5 iteration addresses the last remaining documentation issue from v4 by removing an incorrect mention of changing `core.precomposeunicode` from `int` to `bool` in the commit message. This was the final polish item identified in previous reviews, making the series effectively ready for merging.

The changes maintain all the technical characteristics established in earlier versions:
- Preserves eager parsing for performance-critical paths
- Follows the established pattern for migrating globals
- Prevents cross-repository state leakage
- Affects core functionality like compression, Unicode handling, and sparse-checkout behavior

The series has already received positive reviews from multiple contributors (Karthik Nayak, Christian Couder, Usman Akinyemi) and maintainer Junio Hamano signaled readiness to merge pending these final documentation fixes. With those now addressed in v5, this appears to be the final iteration before merging into the `the_repository` removal effort.

The changes are wide but shallow - touching many files with small, mechanical modifications. Performance-sensitive areas like `match_stat_data()` were carefully considered to avoid regressions. This represents another incremental but important step in eliminating Git's reliance on global variables.
