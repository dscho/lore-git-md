This thread documents the final stages of Patrick Steinhardt’s three-part patch series to standardize Git’s contributor workflow around the `b4` tool. The series, now at version 4, has received maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano and is ready for merging.

The goal was to reconcile inconsistencies between Git’s documentation and the `b4` tool’s defaults, particularly around email threading models. The series updates `MyFirstContribution.adoc` to recommend shallow threading, adds a new `b4` workflow section to the tutorial, and introduces project-wide configuration files (`.b4-config` and `.b4-cover-template`). The `.b4-config` file sets `send-same-thread = shallow` to align with Git’s preferred threading model, while the cover template includes a range-diff section tailored to Git’s practices. The documentation presents `b4` as an optimization layer over manual email workflows, ensuring contributors understand the underlying concepts before using the tool.

All substantive discussion has concluded. Key participants—including Junio (maintainer), Johannes Schindelin (GitGitGadget maintainer), and Derrick Stolee—have confirmed alignment with existing workflows and tooling. A minor documentation inconsistency in `SubmittingPatches` was noted by Karthik Nayak but deemed out of scope. The final change from v3 to v4 was a surface-level commit message adjustment, and Toon Claes’s review confirms the series is ready for integration. The thread represents a successful resolution of both technical and social coordination challenges.
