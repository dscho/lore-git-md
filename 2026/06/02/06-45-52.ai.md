This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about deprecating the `git-init-db` alias. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio C Hamano's pushback against deprecation, revealing that his primary motivation was actually internal code consistency rather than user-facing concerns.

The email makes two key points:
1. Patrick acknowledges he'd be fine keeping the `init-db` alias if that's the maintainer's preference, showing willingness to defer to Junio's judgment
2. He reveals the real driver for the change was annoyance at the internal naming (`builtin/init-db.c` and `cmd_init_db()`) not matching the modern `git-init` command name

This shifts the discussion's focus from user impact to codebase consistency. Patrick notes the series was structured so the first commit (internal renaming) could stand alone without the second commit (user-facing deprecation). The thread now appears headed toward accepting just the internal cleanup while preserving the historical alias, unless other contributors raise new objections.

The exchange demonstrates how maintainer feedback can reshape a proposal's direction, with the author adapting to focus on achievable improvements rather than pushing a more controversial change. It also highlights how internal code aesthetics can sometimes drive changes that initially appear user-facing.
