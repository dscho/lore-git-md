This existing mechanism has been used for repository identification since before worktrees existed, and extending it to worktrees via `$GIT_COMMON_DIR/worktrees/$worktree/description` would maintain conceptual consistency.

The review is substantive - it engages with the technical approach rather than just surface-level issues, suggesting an alternative implementation that would better align with existing Git conventions. However, it's not a blocking objection, more of a "have you considered" style suggestion that leaves room for the author to either adopt the idea or explain why the new approach is preferable.
