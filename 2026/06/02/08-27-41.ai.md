This is a maintainer response in the ongoing thread about renaming Git's init functionality. Junio C Hamano pushes back against Patrick Steinhardt's proposal to rename the internal implementation files from `init-db` to `init` to match the modern command name.

Junio expresses concern that such a rename would simply shift the annoyance from those who prefer modern naming to those accustomed to the historical names. He draws a parallel to previous renames that made certain files harder to locate, suggesting that consistency isn't always worth the disruption. This response indicates the maintainer's preference to leave the internal naming as-is if the only motivation is aesthetic consistency.

The exchange highlights Junio's role as a stabilizing force, weighing the costs of change against the benefits. His position suggests the thread may conclude without any changes to either the internal implementation or the user-facing alias, unless stronger technical motivations emerge. The discussion serves as a case study in how Git balances historical continuity with modernization efforts.
