This is a discussion about Git's deprecation workflow process, continuing from Kristoffer Haugsbakk's series removing deprecated commit list functions. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Junio C Hamano's defense of Git's current deprecation approach.

Peff acknowledges Junio's point about distributing work across the community rather than concentrating it on the maintainer, but offers two counterpoints. First, topics not yet picked up by the maintainer would naturally become the responsibility of their authors to update when rebasing. Second, there's a tradeoff between the maintainer's effort in handling fixups versus managing new topics.

The exchange remains collegial, with Peff explicitly deferring to Junio's judgment while raising the question for consideration. This continues the meta-discussion about process optimization without proposing concrete changes, as both agree the technical cleanup itself is uncontroversial. The thread shows senior contributors thoughtfully examining project workflow tradeoffs, with the immediate patch series unaffected by this higher-level discussion.
