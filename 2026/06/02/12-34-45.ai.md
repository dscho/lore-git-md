This is a follow-up in the thread about Git's init command naming, where Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's pushback against renaming internal `init-db` identifiers to match the modern `git-init` command name.

Patrick acknowledges Junio's position that renaming would just shift the burden from those who prefer modern naming to those accustomed to the historical names. While Patrick maintains his view that consistency would benefit new contributors (who might expect `cmd_init()` to match `git init`), he concedes that the tradeoff isn't clear-cut and accepts Junio's reasoning.

The exchange confirms that no technical changes will be made to either the internal implementation names or the user-facing `git-init-db` alias. The thread has effectively concluded with agreement to maintain the status quo, with only minor documentation questions remaining from earlier in the discussion. This serves as a good example of how the maintainer's judgment shapes Git's evolution, particularly when balancing modernization against historical continuity.
