Performance optimization discussion for Git's priority queue implementation, with René Scharfe proposing an alternative approach to Kristofer Karlsson's cascade-down optimization.

René suggests a different optimization path that would make `prio_queue_replace()` unnecessary by automatically handling its optimization case. - René's proposal maintains the existing `prio_queue_replace()` optimization while attempting to streamline the common case
- The 60-line patch adds state tracking via the new flag and modifies all queue operations to handle pending sifts
- Benchmark results show his approach avoids the 50%+ performance hit seen when reverting to get+put operations
- The implementation complexity and heap imbalance after gets make René hesitant about this direction

This represents a third technical approach in the thread, alongside Kristofer's cascade-down optimization and the original implementation.
