This thread clarifies the requirement for raw Unix timestamps in Git's date parsing to be prefixed with `@`, particularly for timestamps under 100,000,000. The initial patch, co-authored by Junio Hamano, documents this long-standing but previously unspecified behavior, adding both documentation and test cases. 

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the submission, noting no technical issues with the content but pointing out minor procedural improvements needed in the ordering of patch trailers and threading of emails. His review is purely about submission mechanics, explicitly stating the patch itself looks good. With the maintainer's co-authorship already signaling approval, this documentation change appears ready to proceed once the minor formatting points are addressed.
