This thread documents Git's requirement that raw Unix timestamps be prefixed with `@` to distinguish them from YYYYMMDD-style dates, particularly for values under 100,000,000. The behavior has existed since 2012 but was never formally documented until now. 

Junio Hamano co-authored and approved the patch (now at v2) for merging, confirming its technical correctness. The only discussion points were procedural - Patrick Steinhardt and Junio provided feedback about proper patch submission mechanics and the `b4` tool workflow. The author, Luna Schwalbe, has acknowledged this process feedback in a brief follow-up email that concludes the thread. 

With the documentation improvement approved and no outstanding technical questions, this change will be part of Git's next release. The thread also surfaced broader interest in better documenting contributor tooling like `b4`, which may be addressed separately.
