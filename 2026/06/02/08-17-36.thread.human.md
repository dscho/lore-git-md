This thread documents the requirement for raw Unix timestamps in Git to be prefixed with `@` to distinguish them from other date formats - a behavior that's existed since 2012 but was previously undocumented. The patch, now in its second version, has maintainer approval from Junio Hamano and will be merged unchanged from its technically sound first version.

While the core documentation change is settled, the discussion has expanded to include workflow tooling. Junio strongly endorses Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion to better document the `b4` tool in Git's contribution guidelines, sharing specific examples of how it streamlines his maintainer workflow for applying patches and comparing versions. Though this tangent doesn't affect the timestamp documentation itself, it provides valuable insight into the project's patch management practices and may lead to future improvements in contributor documentation around submission tools. The original patch remains approved and ready for merging.
