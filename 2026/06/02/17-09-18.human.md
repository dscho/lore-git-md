This is part 5 of an 8-patch series completing the `the_repository` removal effort by migrating configuration globals into repository-specific storage. The patch moves the `core.precomposedunicode` setting from a global variable into `struct repo_config_values`, maintaining its eager parsing behavior since it controls fundamental filesystem-level Unicode normalization on macOS.

The change affects several key areas: Unicode path handling in `compat/precompose_utf8.c`, configuration parsing in `environment.c`, and upload-pack operations. The implementation follows the pattern established in previous patches of the series, ensuring the setting remains tied to its originating repository while preserving existing behavior.

The patch has already received positive reviews from Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi, with maintainer Junio Hamano signaling readiness to merge the series. This appears to be a well-executed mechanical change that completes an important piece of the long-running `the_repository` removal effort. The series as a whole addresses cross-repository state leakage while maintaining performance in areas like filesystem operations and compression settings.
