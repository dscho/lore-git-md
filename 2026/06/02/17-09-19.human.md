This is part 6/8 of a series completing the `the_repository` removal effort by migrating the `core.sparseCheckoutCone` configuration into repository-specific storage. The patch moves this setting from a global variable into `struct repo_config_values`, preventing cross-repository state issues while maintaining the existing parsing behavior.

The change affects sparse-checkout operations across several commands (`mv`, `sparse-checkout`, and index handling) while preserving the eager parsing needed for early repository setup. The patch follows the established pattern seen in previous parts of this series, with mechanical updates to all access points. 

Reviewers (Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi) have already vetted the approach, and the series appears ready for merging as the final step in eliminating configuration globals. The changes are straightforward and maintain backward compatibility, touching only internal implementation details without altering user-visible behavior.
