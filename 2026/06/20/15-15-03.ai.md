This is Junio Hamano following up on his review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series for cascading indentation in `git log --graph`. The patch introduces abstraction helpers (`revision_has_more_commits()` and `revision_peek_next_commit()`) in `revision.c` to insulate `graph.c` from the walker’s internal traversal model.

Junio now proposes a concrete defensive measure: adding a `BUG()` check at the start of `revision_has_commits_after()` to prevent calls on commits that haven’t been processed by the history traversal when `-L` (line-level log filtering) is active without `rewrite_parents`. The check would guard against future misuse, particularly if someone later combines `-L` with `--graph` in a way that doesn’t set `rewrite_parents`. He also suggests documenting this constraint in the function’s header comment.

The email briefly explores a longer-term cleanup—moving the side-effecting call (`line_log_process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()`) out of `get_commit_action()` and into `simplify_commit()`—but acknowledges this is tangential to the current series. Junio explicitly states this discussion doesn’t block the patch’s progress, though the defensive check should be addressed in a follow-up. Michael Haggerty (recent line-log contributor) and SZEDER Gábor (author of the 2020 commit that introduced the side effect) are CC’d for awareness.
