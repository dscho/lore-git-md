This thread began with D. Ben Knoble’s patch to add a `nanosec` Meson build option, mirroring the existing Autotools `USE_NSEC` knob to enable sub-second timestamp tracking. The patch was reviewed and accepted by Junio C Hamano, who queued it for merging despite a minor concern about the discoverability of the option name.

Jeff King (Peff) later chimed in with a substantive review, endorsing the patch’s goal of build system parity but cautioning about the real-world reliability of `USE_NSEC`. He highlighted a long-standing issue documented in `racy-git.adoc`: enabling sub-second timestamps can lead to "meaningless changes" when in-core timestamps (nanosecond resolution) differ from on-disk timestamps, particularly on network filesystems like CEPH, CIFS, NTFS, and UDF. While this was partially fixed in Linux 2.6.11, Peff noted that some filesystems may still be affected. He suggested that a deeper investigation could justify adjusting defaults in `config.make.uname`, but that’s beyond the current patch’s scope. His feedback doesn’t block the patch but surfaces technical debt that might resurface in future discussions about build defaults or filesystem compatibility. The patch remains on track for merging.
