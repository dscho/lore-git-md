This thread is about a performance optimization for Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which speeds up merge-base calculations in repositories with asymmetric histories (e.g., those with import grafts or shallow histories). The series, now at version 8, is authored by Tian Yuchen and co-developed with Kristofer Karlsson, with key contributions from Elijah Newren. It introduces early termination when one side of a history is exhausted, yielding **100-1000x speedups** in asymmetric queries, and includes hardening, instrumentation, and dead-code removal.

The series is **technically complete and fully reviewed**, with all substantive feedback addressed. It is queued in Junio’s tree and procedurally ready for `seen`, but review bandwidth remains the bottleneck. The latest exchange between Kristofer and Elijah focuses on a minor but critical detail in patch 7/8: the order of counter checks in `paint_queue_get()`. Elijah’s review highlighted that the counters must be checked *before* decrementing to avoid premature termination, and Kristofer proposes adding a code comment to document this logic. This is a non-blocking refinement—Elijah’s feedback was already incorporated in the implementation, and the comment would merely clarify the reasoning for future maintainers.

Key participants include Tian Yuchen (author), Kristofer Karlsson (co-signer and reviewer), Elijah Newren (reviewer and test contributor), Derrick Stolee, SZEDER Gábor, René Scharfe, and Junio Hamano (maintainer). The series touches `commit-reach.c`, `commit-graph.c`, and related test files, introducing a `paint_state` struct with per-side counters, Trace2 instrumentation for step counts, and regression tests for edge cases like clock skew and redundant merge bases. The fallback for commit-date ordering has been removed in favor of a topological ceiling for v1 commit-graphs, resolving a long-standing correctness issue. All open questions are non-blocking, and the series is awaiting additional reviewers to advance.
