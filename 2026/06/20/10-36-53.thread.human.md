The series, authored by Tian Yuchen, introduces an early-termination mechanism that avoids unnecessary traversal when one side of a history query exhausts its commit queue—yielding **100-1000x speedups** in asymmetric cases (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization refactors the `paint_state` struct to track per-side counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`), centralizes termination logic in `paint_queue_get()`, and removes the commit-date ordering fallback entirely. It also adds deterministic step-count instrumentation (via Trace2) and comprehensive test coverage for edge cases like clock skew, mixed-generation graphs, and redundant merge-base candidates.
