The series, authored by Tian Yuchen and co-developed with Kristofer Karlsson, is technically complete, fully reviewed, and queued in Junio’s integration tree. The only remaining task is final documentation refinements to adopt consistent “ordered” and “unordered” region terminology.

The latest exchange between Elijah Newren and Kristofer Karlsson centers on a minor but pragmatic clarity issue in the Trace2 instrumentation patch (5/8). The discussion is purely editorial and does not affect the series’ technical readiness. The instrumentation itself—adding a `steps` counter and `trace2_data_intmax()` call—is uncontroversial and praised as a valuable addition for measuring optimization impact.
