Tian Yuchen sent a six-patch series (v1) that adds an early-exit condition when one side of the traversal exhausts its commit queue, avoiding unnecessary work. The core change (patch 3) is technically sound and endorsed by Derrick Stolee, but the series hit a snag over how to refactor the code leading up to it.

Kristofer Karlsson, who co-authored the optimization, just agreed to this approach, so patch 1 will be dropped in v2. That resolves the last major structural objection, leaving only stylistic and procedural feedback to address—like test placement, performance benchmarks, and documentation updates.

The series is now on track for merging once those smaller items are tidied up. The optimization itself is uncontroversial and shows dramatic speedups (500x+) on large repositories without regressing smaller ones. If you care about merge-base performance or the commit-reach subsystem, this is worth watching for the v2 rollout.
