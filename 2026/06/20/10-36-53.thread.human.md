This thread is about a performance optimization to Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which calculates merge bases between commit histories. The series, now at version 8 (10 patches), was authored by Tian Yuchen and is ready for integration. It introduces an early-termination mechanism that avoids unnecessary traversal when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, delivering **100-1000x speedups** in asymmetric cases—such as repositories with import grafts or shallow histories.

The optimization is technically complete and fully reviewed, with all prior feedback addressed. The commit-date ordering fallback has been entirely removed, and the queue is now strictly generation-ordered.
