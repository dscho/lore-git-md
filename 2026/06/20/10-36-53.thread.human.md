This thread is about a performance optimization for Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which calculates merge bases. The series, now at version 5, achieves **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories) by terminating early when one side of the history exhausts its commit queue. The final iteration is fully reviewed, regression-free, and ready to merge.

Key improvements in v5 include the removal of an obsolete commit-date fallback, the addition of trace2 diagnostics, new test cases for clock-skew topologies, and a technical document explaining the algorithm. Benchmarks confirm no performance regression, and the series is technically complete.
