This thread is about a performance optimization for Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which speeds up merge-base calculations in repositories with asymmetric histories. The latest version (v3) of the patch series introduced a critical regression in patch 7/8, where an overzealous BUG assertion breaks correctness when `min_generation` is not set, causing test failures in `t6600-test-reach.sh`. The regression was confirmed by Junio C Hamano, who ejected the series from the `seen` branch, and by Kristofer Karlsson, who acknowledged the oversight but noted the test suite robustly caught the bug.

The series is otherwise well-tested and instrumented, with patches 1–6/8 and 8/8 unaffected by the regression. These include edge-case tests, trace2 step-counting instrumentation, and a new technical document explaining the paint-walk mechanics. Derrick Stolee and Kristofer Karlsson have praised the implementation, particularly the test infrastructure in patch 4/8. The regression in patch 7/8 is the sole blocker, and the author plans to fix it in v4 by either reverting the problematic change or unifying halt conditions while preserving correctness. Junio’s response was constructive, treating the regression as a routine part of the review process.
