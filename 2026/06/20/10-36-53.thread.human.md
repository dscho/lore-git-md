This thread is about a performance optimization for Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which calculates merge bases. The series, now at version 6, delivers 100-1000x speedups for asymmetric queries (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories) by terminating early when one side of the history exhausts its commit queue. Authored by Tian Yuchen and co-developed with Kristofer Karlsson, the series is technically complete, fully reviewed, and procedurally unblocked—Junio has already queued it in his tree after validating the synthetic base approach.

The current sticking point is review bandwidth. Junio has explicitly asked for more reviewers, and Kristofer’s latest email acknowledges the slowdown, suggesting four possible simplifications to make the series easier to review: deferring the commit-date fallback cleanup, dropping the test diagnostic helper, squashing test commits, or squashing core logic changes. He notes that seasonal vacations may be contributing to the delay. No code changes are needed unless the series is restructured, and the patches remain ready for integration. The thread is now focused on how to attract more reviewers rather than on technical issues.
