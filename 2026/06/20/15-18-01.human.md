This is a tiny but important follow-up to the recently queued patch that moved the `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` settings into the per-repository `repo_config_values` struct. The change is purely mechanical: it replaces the `repo->gitdir` guard in the accessor functions with `repo->initialized`, aligning them with the existing pattern used for other repository-scoped config fields like `ignore_case` and `trust_executable_bit`.

The patch is already queued in Junio’s `next` branch, and the reasoning is straightforward—checking `repo->initialized` ensures the accessors don’t call `repo_config_values()` on an uninitialized repository, which is the same defensive pattern used elsewhere in the codebase. The commit message explains this clearly, and the updated comment in `environment.h` makes the behavior explicit.
