This is the third patch in Tian Yuchen’s six-patch series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. The patch implements the core performance optimization: terminating the merge-base walk early when one side’s queue is exhausted, provided the walk has entered the finite-generation region (where generation numbers guarantee topological ordering).

The change adds a 13-line early-exit condition inside the main loop of `paint_down_to_common()`. The condition checks that (1) the current commit’s generation is finite, (2) no merge-base candidates remain in the queue, and (3) either the PARENT1-only or PARENT2-only counter has dropped to zero. When all three hold, the loop breaks immediately, avoiding unnecessary traversal of the remaining side. The commit message explains that this is safe because no new merge-base can form once one side is exhausted in the finite-generation region.

The patch is minimal and tightly scoped, touching only `commit-reach.c`. It builds directly on the per-side counters (`p1_count`, `p2_count`, `pending_merge_bases`) introduced in patch 2, which refactored the queue logic to track commits by paint state. The INFINITY gate ensures correctness by deferring early termination until the walk reaches commits with valid generation numbers, where topological ordering is reliable. Benchmarks cited in the series show 100–1000x speedups for merge-base queries in large repositories with asymmetric histories (e.g., PR branches against a large mainline).
