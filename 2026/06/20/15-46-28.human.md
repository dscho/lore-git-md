This is a review of Harald Nordgren's 2-patch series that silences translation catalog output during `make -s` builds for gitk and git-gui. The reviewer, Johannes Sixt (the gitk maintainer), suggests a more aggressive approach than the original patches.

Sixt proposes completely removing the `--statistics` flag from `msgfmt` invocations rather than just suppressing its output conditionally. He points out that core Git already made this change in 2021 (commit 2f12b31b746) and argues the statistics output isn't useful. He also recommends removing the "Generating catalog" status message entirely, not just silencing it during quiet builds.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused on the build output philosophy rather than technical implementation. Sixt doesn't object to the patches as written, but clearly prefers a simpler solution that eliminates the verbose output at the source rather than adding conditional suppression logic. This would make the gitk and git-gui build behavior consistent with core Git's current approach. The ball is now in the author's court to either revise the patches or explain why the statistics output should be preserved.
