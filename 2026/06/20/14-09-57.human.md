This is a small follow-up refactoring patch to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Tian Yuchen implements Junio’s suggestion to align the accessor functions for `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` with the existing pattern used by `ignore_case` and `trust_executable_bit`: checking `repo->initialized` instead of `repo->gitdir` before calling `repo_config_values()`. The change is purely mechanical—four lines across two files—and does not alter behavior.

The patch is part of a larger series (v4) that moved these two config values into `struct repo_config_values`; that series is already queued in Junio’s `next` branch. This follow-up is the immediate, uncontroversial fix Junio proposed to ensure consistency across repository-scoped config topics. A second, deferred follow-up (comment consolidation in `environment.h`) is planned for after Git 2.55 final.

No substantive review is expected; the patch is a straightforward alignment with established patterns. It is a safe, low-risk change that advances the project’s libification goals.
