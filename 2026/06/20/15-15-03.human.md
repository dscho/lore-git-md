This is Junio Hamano following up on his review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series that adds abstraction helpers in `revision.c` for `git log --graph` lookahead. The patch itself is still conditionally approved, but Junio now proposes a concrete defensive measure: a `BUG()` check at the start of `revision_has_commits_after()` to prevent future misuse of `get_commit_action()` on unprocessed commits. He also floats the idea of eventually making `get_commit_action()` pure again by moving its side-effecting logic into `simplify_commit()`, though he acknowledges that is a separate, longer-term effort. Michael Haggerty and SZEDER Gábor are CC’d for awareness, as the side effect in question was introduced in SZEDER’s 2020 line-log work. The takeaway for the current series is that a small follow-up patch adding the `BUG()` guard will be needed before the abstraction layer can be considered fully reviewed.
