This is the final patch in Tian Yuchen’s six-part performance series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds a new technical document (`Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`) that explains the algorithm, its use cases, the role of generation numbers, and the side-exhaustion termination condition introduced in patch 3.

The document is thorough: it walks through the two primary use cases (finding all merge bases and ancestry checks), describes the paint flags (PARENT1, PARENT2, STALE) and queue ordering, and clarifies the distinction between the INFINITY and finite-generation regions. It then details the three termination conditions—empty queue, all-stale entries, and side-exhaustion—with particular emphasis on why side-exhaustion is safe only after entering the finite region. The explanation of the finite-region invariant (no future paint can be added to already-visited commits) directly justifies the early-exit optimization.

The patch itself is minimal: it adds the `.adoc` file and updates the Makefile and meson.build to include it in the build. The prose is clear and precise, making it a useful reference for future contributors working on merge-base or commit-reach code. No behavioral changes or code logic are introduced; this is purely documentation.
