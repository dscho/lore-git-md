Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This final iteration (v6) **rebases the v5 series on `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`** (already merged into `next` as of v2.55.0) to resolve a procedural merge conflict and test failure in `t6600`, ensuring the removal of the obsolete commit-date fallback (patch 8/8) cleanly supersedes the related regression fix. The series is **fully reviewed, regression-free, and ready to merge**.

- **Behavior change**: Early termination when `(!state->parent1_count || !state->parent2_count) && !state->mb_candidate_count && last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`. The commit queue ordering is strictly topological (generation-based); the commit-date fallback is removed entirely.
