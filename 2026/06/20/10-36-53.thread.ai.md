Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This final iteration (v5) **squashes mechanical fixes**, **removes the obsolete commit-date fallback entirely**, **adds trace2 diagnostics**, **introduces clock-skew topologies**, **extends test coverage**, **updates documentation**, and **adds a new technical document** explaining the algorithm. The series is now **fully reviewed, regression-free, and ready to merge**.
