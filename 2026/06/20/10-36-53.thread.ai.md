Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories. The optimization targets asymmetric histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories) and yields **100-1000x speedups** for such queries.

Current status
Series is **fully landed and complete**. All patches (1-7/7) are merged, resolving all design, technical, and procedural feedback from v1. The **core optimization (patch 7/7)** is functionally correct and endorsed by Derrick Stolee, Kristofer Karlsson, and Elijah Newren. The **structural dispute** over patch 1 from v1 is resolved: `nonstale_queue` is preserved for `ahead_behind()`, eliminating the decoupling patch. The **termination condition** is finalized as `(!state->p1_count || !state->p2_count) && !state->pending_merge_bases`, gated by `last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY` to ensure correctness. **Performance instrumentation** is fully integrated: **trace2_data_intmax()** exposes the "commits walked" metric for testable validation, and **hyperfine** benchmarks `git merge-base` end-to-end timing between builds.
