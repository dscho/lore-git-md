Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries (e.g., steps reduced from 2.1M to 3 in a 2.6M-commit monorepo). The series is **technically complete, fully reviewed, and procedurally unblocked**, with **final documentation refinements to adopt consistent "ordered/unordered" region terminology now fully incorporated**.
