The `paint_state` refactoring in patch 7/10 introduces per-side counters to make the queue’s state explicit, enabling the side-exhaustion optimization in patch 8/10. The "pop first" logic in `paint_queue_get()` reduces step counts by one in some topologies (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5). Patch 9/10 consolidates the `min_generation` termination check into `paint_queue_get()` and moves `last_gen` into `paint_state` to avoid redundant calls to `commit_graph_generation()`, centralizing all termination conditions in a single function. Patch 10/10 removes the commit-date ordering fallback entirely, relying on pure generation-number ordering with a topological ceiling for v1 commit-graphs.
- **Performance instrumentation**: Trace2 step counts via `trace2_data_intmax()` (e.g., `paint_down_to_common steps 9` vs. `80` in git.git), enabling observable measurement of optimization impact.
