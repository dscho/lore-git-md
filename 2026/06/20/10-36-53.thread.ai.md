Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization targets a known performance bottleneck where asymmetric histories force full traversal of one side even after the other side is exhausted, yielding **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries.

The **finite-generation guard** is confirmed as critical for correctness, and its activation timing is explicitly tied to the finite-generation region. **Patch ordering** is settled: the documentation patch (1/7) appears first, followed by regression tests (2-3/7), trace2 instrumentation (4/7), refactoring (5-6/7), and then the optimization (7/7). **Performance instrumentation** is fully agreed: **trace2_data_intmax()** exposes the "commits walked" metric for testable validation, and **hyperfine** benchmarks `git merge-base` end-to-end timing between builds.

The **documentation patch (1/7)** is landed: it introduces `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`, a new AsciiDoc file explaining the merge-base computation logic, including the paint walk, generation number regions, and termination conditions. The document is **comprehensive**, covering use cases, mechanics, and the `nonstale_queue`/`max_nonstale` heuristic. It is included in the build via `Makefile` and `meson.build` updates and cross-referenced in `commit-reach.c`. The **test coverage patches (2-3/7)**, authored by Elijah Newren and Kristofer Karlsson, are fully landed: patch 2/7 extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with four targeted edge-case tests (`in_merge_bases_many:self`, `get_merge_bases_many:duplicate-twos`, `get_merge_bases_many:pending-stale`, `get_merge_bases_many:infinity-both-sides`) that validate the optimization’s early-exit logic under scenarios where it could misbehave. Patch 3/7 adds a new test file, `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, which exercises the case where multiple merge-base candidates exist and one is an ancestor of another, ensuring the `remove_redundant` safety net catches redundant candidates even when the side-exhaustion optimization exits early. The tests reuse edge-case topologies from Elijah Newren’s prior work (PR #2150), including a diamond topology with redundant merge-base candidates, mixed finite/INFINITY generation numbers, criss-cross merges, and non-monotonic commit dates. The test setup includes ASCII diagrams and commit-date annotations to document expected behavior, ensuring the optimization respects the finite-generation guard even when commit dates are non-monotonic. Both test files are integrated into the build via `meson.build` and `Makefile` updates.

**Trace2 instrumentation (patch 4/7)** is fully integrated: it adds a step counter to `paint_down_to_common()` and logs the "commits walked" metric via `trace2_data_intmax()` at the end of the walk. This enables deterministic measurement of optimization impact through `GIT_TRACE2_PERF` or `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` logs. The test in `t6600-test-reach.sh` validates the instrumentation by comparing step counts across different commit-graph configurations (none, full, and half), ensuring the metric is consistent and testable. The test reuses the existing `commit-9-9`/`commit-9-1` topology and verifies that the step count matches expectations (e.g., 80 steps with a full commit-graph). This instrumentation is a prerequisite for the final optimization (patch 7/7), providing empirical validation of the early-exit logic's effectiveness.

**Refactoring patches (5-6/7)** are now fully resolved:
- Patch 5/7 introduces the `paint_state` struct to track per-side commit counters (`p1_count`, `p2_count`, `pending_merge_bases`) and replaces the old `nonstale_queue` termination logic with a counter-based approach. The refactoring is purely structural, with no functional changes, and is validated by trace2 step counts remaining identical before and after the change. The `paint_count_update()`, `paint_queue_put()`, and `paint_queue_get()` helpers manage enqueueing/dequeueing commits while keeping counters in sync. The `last_gen` value is derived from the dequeued commit in `paint_queue_get()`, making the function self-contained.
- Patch 6/7 further refines the `paint_state` struct by removing the `mb_flags` field (for the older fast-exit optimization) and simplifying the loop’s termination condition to rely entirely on `paint_queue_get()` returning `NULL`. The patch ensures the `nonstale_queue` is preserved for `ahead_behind()` to avoid disrupting other callers.
