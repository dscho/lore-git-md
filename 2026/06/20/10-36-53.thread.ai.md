Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This iteration (v3) introduces **two new patches**: (7/8) the original v3 cleanup and hardening (consolidating termination conditions, widening the generation-monotonicity assertion, and improving code clarity), and (8/8) a **new technical documentation** deep-dive on the `paint_down_to_common()` algorithm, authored by Kristofer Karlsson. The documentation explains the algorithm’s purpose, use cases, paint-walk mechanics, generation-number regions, termination conditions, and the role of the `nonstale_queue`. Patch 2/8 (new in v3) adds **four edge-case test cases** to `t6600-test-reach.sh`, covering self-referential queries, duplicate inputs, pending-stale transitions, and INFINITY-generation handling.
