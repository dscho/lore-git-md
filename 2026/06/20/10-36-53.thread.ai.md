Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This final iteration (v6) **rebases the v5 series on `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`** (already merged into `next` as of v2.55.0) to resolve a procedural merge conflict and test failure in `t6600`, ensuring the removal of the obsolete commit-date fallback (patch 10/10) cleanly supersedes the related regression fix. The series is **fully reviewed, regression-free, and ready to merge**, now expanded to 10 patches with the addition of a **refactoring cleanup patch (9/10)** that centralizes termination logic in `paint_queue_get()`.

- **Core early-termination logic** (patch 10/10): Implements the **100-1000x speedup** by terminating the walk when one side's commit queue is exhausted (`!state->parent1_count || !state->parent2_count`), no pending merge-base candidates remain (`!state->mb_candidate_count`), and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`gen_ordered` and `commit_graph_generation(commit) < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`). The technical doc is updated to explain why this is safe in the finite-generation region (topological ordering guarantees no future commits from the exhausted side can appear) but not in the INFINITY region (commit-date ordering can violate topological guarantees). Tests in `t6600-test-reach.sh` are adjusted to reflect reduced step counts (e.g., `merge-base --all commit-9-9 commit-9-1` drops from 81 steps to 9 steps in full commit-graph mode), with deterministic assertions ensuring functional equivalence. **Removes the commit-date ordering fallback** entirely, simplifying the codebase and fixing a correctness issue where the fallback could violate topological guarantees.
- **Dead-code removal** (patch 7/10): Deletes unused `nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()` functions.
