Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This iteration (v3) introduces **four new patches** (2/8, 3/8, 4/8, 5/8) and **three follow-up patches** (6/8, 7/8, 8/8) that consolidate, harden, document, and **fix a regression** in the core optimization. **Patch 7/8 (new in v3) implements the side-exhaustion optimization but introduces a critical regression** by widening the generation-monotonicity BUG assertion to fire unconditionally, breaking correctness when `min_generation` is not set. The regression **causes test failures in `t6600-test-reach.sh` (test 12, `get_merge_bases_many`)** and has been **confirmed by Junio C Hamano, Derrick Stolee, and Kristofer Karlsson**. The author and Kristofer **acknowledge the mistake, confirm the test suite robustly caught the bug**, and **plan a fix for v4** (either reverting the problematic change or unifying halt conditions while preserving correctness).

The tests verify that redundant merge-base candidates are excluded and that the walk correctly transitions between generation-number regions. Uses `test_all_modes` for multi-mode validation. **Stable and unaffected by the regression**.
- **Patch 4/8 (new in v3)**: Adds **trace2 step-counting instrumentation** to `paint_down_to_common()` to measure the number of commits visited during the paint walk. The patch increments a `steps` counter for each commit processed and emits the total via `trace2_data_intmax()` at the end of the walk. It **refactors `run_all_modes()`** to capture trace2 output per commit-graph mode (`none`, `full`, `half`, `no-gdat`) into separate files (`trace-mode-*.txt`), enabling mode-specific assertions. The test infrastructure is extended to **assert deterministic step counts** across all modes via a new `test_paint_down_steps()` helper. The test suite is updated to include step-count assertions for existing edge-case tests (self, pending-stale, infinity-both-sides, mixed-finite-infinity) and adds a new test (`merge-base --all commit-walk steps`) that verifies step counts for a large topology (81 steps in git.git). The step counts are **mode-dependent** (e.g., "self" test expects 45 steps in "none" mode but only 2 in "full" mode). **Stable, uncontroversial, and ready to merge**; received **unqualified approval** from Derrick Stolee, who praised the implementation as "clean" and "over-achieve[ing]." Kristofer Karlsson confirmed the test infrastructure changes were a deliberate simplification and noted a planned follow-up to enhance trace2 failure diagnostics with a `test_trace2_data_singular` helper.
- **Patch 5/8 (new in v3)**: Introduces `struct paint_state` to replace the old `nonstale_queue` with per-side counters (`p1_count`, `p2_count`, `pending_merge_bases`). The counters are maintained by `paint_count_update()` and drained via `paint_queue_get()`. The patch is **mechanical and low-risk**, converting `paint_down_to_common()` to use `paint_state` while preserving equivalent behavior. The loop now terminates when the counters indicate no non-stale entries remain, replacing the old `max_nonstale` pointer-based check. The "pop first" form of `paint_queue_get()` (dequeue, then check counters) causes the loop to exit one iteration earlier in some topologies, reducing step counts by one in a few test cases (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5 steps). The existing `nonstale_queue` is left untouched for `ahead_behind()` to avoid coupling. **Stable and unaffected by the regression**; received **surface-level feedback** from René Scharfe, who suggested:
  - Using `size_t` instead of `int` for the counters to match `prio_queue.nr` and avoid potential negative values (low-risk, non-blocking).
  - Longer, more explicit names for the counters (e.g., `parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `pending_merge_base_count`) to improve readability and align with the `PARENT1`/`PARENT2` flags. The current names (`p1_count`, `p2_count`) are abbreviated and may be less intuitive. This is a **non-blocking stylistic suggestion**.
- **Patch 6/8 (new in v3)**: Removes two unused wrapper functions—`nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()`—that were rendered obsolete by the introduction of `struct paint_state` in patch 5/8. The wrappers were originally introduced to deduplicate commits by checking the `ENQUEUED` flag, but the new `paint_state` counters now handle this logic directly in `paint_down_to_common()`. The removal is **mechanical and low-risk**, deleting 18 lines of dead code with no behavior change. The core `nonstale_queue` functions remain in use by `ahead_behind()`. **Stable and unaffected by the regression**.
- **Patch 7/8 (new in v3)**: **Implements the core optimization**: early termination of the merge-base walk when one side’s commit queue is exhausted. The patch adds an early-exit condition to `paint_down_to_common()` that triggers when either `p1_count` or `p2_count` drops to zero, no pending merge-base candidates remain, and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`generation < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`). This ensures correctness by only activating the optimization in the finite region, where generation numbers provide reliable topological ordering. The patch also **widens the generation-monotonicity BUG assertion to fire unconditionally**, **introducing a critical regression** by breaking correctness when `min_generation` is not set. The regression **causes test failures in `t6600-test-reach.sh` (test 12, `get_merge_bases_many`)**, where the assertion fires instead of returning the expected merge bases (`commit-5-6` and `commit-4-7`). **Junio C Hamano ejected the series from `seen`** due to this regression, and **Derrick Stolee and Kristofer Karlsson confirmed the bug**. **Kristofer Karlsson confirms the test suite caught the bug**, but the failure was missed locally before submission due to an incomplete test run. The author and Kristofer plan to rework the logic to preserve the original behavior. Performance benchmarks show dramatic improvements (e.g., `merge-base --all v2.0.0 v2.55.0-rc1`: 72,264 → 44,589 steps; `merge-base --all v2.55.0-rc1 v2.55.0-rc1~5`: 110 → 7 steps). The test suite is updated to reflect the reduced step counts, with `test_paint_down_steps` assertions now expecting fewer steps in some topologies (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5 steps due to the "pop first" form of `paint_queue_get()`). **Regression confirmed; fix planned for v4**.
- **Patch 8/8 (new in v3)**: **Consolidates termination conditions** by moving the `min_generation` check into `paint_queue_get()`, alongside existing stale-entry and side-exhaustion checks. It also **stores `last_gen` in `struct paint_state`**, ensuring `commit_graph_generation()` is called exactly once per dequeued commit. The loop in `paint_down_to_common()` now reads `state.last_gen` instead of recomputing the generation, and the `min_generation` check is performed immediately after dequeuing, before any other conditions. The "pop first" form of `paint_queue_get()` is preserved, keeping the optimization commit’s diff minimal. The patch updates the new technical documentation (`paint-down-to-common.adoc`) to include the `min_generation` cutoff as a fourth termination condition, with a dedicated subsection explaining its role in `remove_redundant()`. **Stable and ready to merge**; received **substantive but non-blocking feedback** from Derrick Stolee, who questioned whether `last_gen` belongs in `struct paint_state` but resolved his concern after seeing its use in the generation-monotonicity check. Stolee suggested moving the finite-generation guard (`state.last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`) into `paint_queue_get()` as well, but acknowledged this may not be feasible due to the loop’s control flow. Kristofer Karlsson **accepted the suggestion to clarify the generation cutoff’s role in the first patch** and proposed two follow-up options for the abstraction leak: removing the optimization entirely (after further analysis) or tweaking the logic to halt inside `paint_queue_get()` when `generation < INFINITY && !FIND_ALL && num_results >= 1`. The latter would slightly alter semantics but could unlock the optimization earlier in some cases. Both options are **forward-looking and optional**, not blocking merging. The final exchange confirms that the `min_generation` cutoff was always a termination condition but was previously undocumented and not part of the dequeue logic. Kristofer agreed to clarify this in the first patch of v4.

The series is **well-tested and instrumented**, with **deterministic step-count assertions** for all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat) and **self-verifying performance** via `run_all_modes` capturing trace2 output per mode.
