Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. This iteration (v4) **fixes the critical regression** introduced in v3 (patch 7/8) by restoring the `min_generation` guard to the generation-monotonicity BUG assertion, **renames counters for clarity**, **moves termination conditions into `paint_queue_get()`**, **improves documentation**, **adds a new technical document**, and **expands edge-case test coverage** with four new scenarios (self, duplicate-twos, pending-stale, infinity-both-sides). The series is now **collaborative, low-risk, and ready to merge**.

- **Subsystem**: commit-reach (merge-base calculation).
- **Files touched**: `commit-reach.c`, `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`, `Makefile`, `meson.build`, test scripts (`t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, `t6600-test-reach.sh`), `t/meson.build`.

- **Removed symbols**: `nonstale_queue_put_dedup()`, `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()` (unused wrappers).
- **Behavior change**: Walk terminates early when `(!state->parent1_count || !state->parent2_count) && !state->mb_candidate_count && last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`. The generation-monotonicity BUG assertion now fires **only when `min_generation` is set**, restoring the original behavior. The "pop first" form of `paint_queue_get()` reduces step counts by one in some topologies (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5 steps). The `min_generation` check and `last_gen` monotonicity assertion are now performed in `paint_queue_get()`.
- **Performance instrumentation**: Trace2 step counts (e.g., `paint_down_to_common steps 9` vs. `80` in git.git) and wall-clock benchmarks (e.g., `3.67s -> 5ms` for a 2.6M-commit monorepo). `run_all_modes` captures trace2 output per commit-graph mode for self-verifying performance.
- **On-disk format**: No changes.
- **Test coverage**: Edge cases (self/duplicate/pending-stale/infinity-both-sides), diamond topologies (`ps-*`, `pi-*`), mixed finite/INFINITY generation numbers, criss-cross merges, grafts, shallow histories, region transitions, redundant merge-base candidates, and single-merge-base selection. Deterministic step-count assertions for all commit-graph modes (full, half, none, no-gdat). The new test topologies (`ps-*`, `pi-*`) exercise:
  - **ps-* (pending-stale)**: The (PARENT1|PARENT2) → (PARENT1|PARENT2|STALE) transition path.
  - **pi-* (infinity-both-sides)**: The INFINITY-gate fallback to commit-date ordering.
  - **Diamond topology (t6099)**: Interaction between side-exhaustion optimization and `remove_redundant` safety net, ensuring correctness when one merge-base candidate is an ancestor of another.
- **Documentation**: The new `paint-down-to-common.adoc` document explains:

  - Generation-number regions (finite vs. INFINITY) and their impact on termination.
