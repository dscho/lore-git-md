Core problem or goal
Optimize the `paint_down_to_common()` function in Git’s commit-reach subsystem to terminate early when one side of a merge-base query exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries. The series is **technically complete, fully reviewed, and procedurally unblocked**, with **final documentation refinements to adopt consistent region terminology the sole remaining task** before advancement beyond its current queued state.
