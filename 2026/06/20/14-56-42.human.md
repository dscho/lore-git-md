This is Junio’s review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series, which introduces the abstraction layer in `revision.c` for `git log --graph` lookahead. The patch itself is small (48 lines) and adds two helper functions—`revision_peek_next_commit()` and `revision_has_commits_after()`—to insulate `graph.c` from the internal traversal data structures in `revision.c`.

Junio’s feedback is narrowly focused and constructive. He flags two minor style issues (extra spaces between function names and parentheses) and raises a substantive concern about the safety of calling `get_commit_action()` multiple times on the same commit. While he believes the current use is safe—because `--graph` sets `rewrite_parents`, which prevents the side-effecting code path in `line_log_process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()`—he suggests adding a defensive `BUG()` check at the start of `revision_has_commits_after()` to catch future misuse. He also recommends documenting this constraint in the header file.

The review does not question the design or necessity of the abstraction layer; it treats the patch as technically sound and focuses on tightening its implementation. The requested changes are small and should be straightforward to incorporate. Once addressed, this patch is likely to be approved, clearing the way for the final visualization patch in the series.
