This is the fifth patch in Tian Yuchen’s six-patch series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds regression tests for the side-exhaustion optimization introduced in patch 3, authored by Kristofer Karlsson.

The patch creates a new test script, `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, which verifies that `git merge-base --all` correctly handles cases where multiple merge-base candidates exist and one is an ancestor of another. The test topology is a diamond-shaped graph where `B` and `C` are both merge-base candidates, but `B` is an ancestor of `C`. The side-exhaustion optimization may exit early before `STALE` propagation removes the redundant `C`, but the `remove_redundant` safety net in `get_merge_bases_many_0` ensures only `B` is returned. The test also verifies that the optimization behaves correctly with and without the commit-graph (where generation numbers are `INFINITY` and side-exhaustion is disabled).

Additionally, the patch extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with a test case for a mixed finite/INFINITY topology, where one tip (`pm-INF`) is outside the commit-graph (generation `INFINITY`) and the other (`ps-B`) is inside (finite generation). This exercises the region transition in `paint_down_to_common()`: the walk starts in the `INFINITY` region (where side-exhaustion is disabled) and crosses into the finite region (where it can fire). The test ensures the optimization does not regress in this edge case.

The tests are well-motivated and cover both the core optimization and its interaction with commit-graph generation numbers. The new script (`t6099`) is thorough, testing `merge-base --all`, `merge-base` (single), and commit-graph/no-commit-graph scenarios. The mixed finite/INFINITY test in `t6600` is particularly valuable, as it targets a subtle edge case where the optimization’s activation depends on generation numbers. No behavioral changes are introduced; the patch purely adds test coverage for the optimization’s correctness.
