This is the third and final patch in Pablo Sabater’s v6 series implementing **cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`**. The series now has a clean architectural foundation (added in patch 2/3) and this patch delivers the actual visualization logic.

The patch teaches `graph.c` to indent commits that look like roots—either true roots or commits whose parents have been filtered out—so that unrelated branches do not appear vertically adjacent. The indentation “cascades” when multiple visual roots follow one another, except the last one, which is left flush to avoid unnecessary whitespace. A new state `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` is introduced to draw the connecting edge between an indented root and its child. The implementation is careful to skip indentation for non-first parents of merges, since those are already visually connected by the merge’s edges.

Test coverage is extensive: 468 lines of new tests in `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` exercise every combination of roots, merges, filtered parents, `--first-parent`, and `--boundary`. One edge case—where a commit’s parents have not yet been simplified at peek-time—is documented as NEEDSWORK and marked with a `test_expect_failure`.

The patch is technically sound and the series as a whole is now unblocked; the only remaining step is Phillip Wood’s review of the abstraction layer in patch 2/3. If that lands cleanly, this feature is ready to merge.
