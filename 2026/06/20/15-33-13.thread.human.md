Here’s the updated human-mode narrative for the thread:

---

This thread tackles a persistent CI hang in Git’s macOS and Linux test runs, specifically in `t5551` and `t5559`, where an `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs triggers either a mid-transfer abort or an indefinite HTTP/2 stall. The root cause was traced to Apache’s `mod_http2` bug 70131, which hits a 300-second timeout during the transfer. The proposed fix is a three-patch series, though it’s now effectively two patches after consensus dropped the third.

Jeff King’s first patch directly addresses the timeout by increasing Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds in `t/lib-httpd/apache.conf`. The second patch isolates the expensive "many-tags" test case into its own repository, reducing runtime for the entire test script from ~95 seconds to ~37 seconds in expensive runs. Both patches are technically approved and ready to merge. The third patch, which would enable `GIT_TEST_LONG` for GitLab CI on pull requests, is on hold pending a policy decision about whether GitLab should duplicate GitHub’s coverage or focus on additional test scenarios.

Key participants include Michael Montalbo (who identified the root cause), Jeff King (who implemented the fix), Patrick Steinhardt (who contributed the CI alignment patch and tied the decision to drop the ref-packing optimization to Git’s long-term shift to reftable), and Junio C Hamano (who raised a cosmetic nit about the test isolation patch but confirmed the series will proceed with the first two patches). The only remaining blocker is Junio’s strategic question about CI resource allocation, which Patrick or another participant is expected to address. Junio has indicated he’ll apply the first two patches with minor tweaks before the 2.55 release.
