The macOS CI hang in `t5551` and `t5559` has been resolved with a two-patch fix that will ship in Git 2.55. The issue, caused by Apache bug 70131 triggering HTTP/2 stalls or mid-transfer aborts during `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs, is now addressed by increasing Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds and isolating the expensive test case into its own bare repository. This reduces the test script’s runtime from ~95 seconds to ~37 seconds in EXPENSIVE runs.

Jeff King authored the patches, which Junio C Hamano has merged with a minor stylistic tweak. The series was shaped by substantive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt, who identified the root cause and confirmed the fix, and Junio, who endorsed the approach while raising broader CI policy questions. The third patch—proposed by Patrick to align GitLab CI with GitHub’s behavior—remains on hold pending a policy decision. Junio has rejected strict alignment, suggesting GitLab could instead run a superset of GitHub’s tests, but no concrete plan for improving GitLab CI visibility has emerged.

The thread leaves no open technical questions; the only unresolved discussion is about CI policy, which is separate from the patches themselves. The fix is now complete and ready for release.
