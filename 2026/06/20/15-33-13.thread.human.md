This thread began when Michael Montalbo reported CI hangs on macOS runners in Git's test suite, specifically in `t5551` and `t5559`, caused by an Apache `mod_http2` bug that triggered timeouts or protocol stalls during large ref advertisements. Jeff King (Peff) quickly diagnosed the root cause—Apache's 300-second timeout being hit—and proposed a two-patch fix: increasing the Apache `Timeout` directive to 600 seconds and isolating the problematic test case in a bare repository. Both patches were merged for Git 2.55 after consensus formed around addressing the issue at its source rather than with client-side workarounds.

The discussion then expanded to CI policy, with Patrick Steinhardt proposing to align GitLab CI with GitHub's test coverage. While that proposal remains on hold pending resource allocation discussions, Patrick followed through on a smaller, uncontroversial improvement: adding a GitLab CI status badge to the project's `README.md`. This one-line addition, now sent as a patch, directly addresses Junio C Hamano's earlier concern about visibility of GitLab CI results. With Junio's prior endorsement, this patch is expected to be merged soon.

Separately, the thread uncovered a quadratic-time performance issue in Git's reftable backend, where bulk ref deletion and re-creation scales poorly due to tombstone iteration inefficiencies. While the root cause is now well-understood—tombstones force `refs_verify_refname_available()` to scan all deleted refs during bulk operations—no fix has been proposed yet. Peff and Patrick both characterized this as a deliberate trade-off to avoid compaction overhead, though they noted potential optimizations like early iterator termination. For now, the issue remains a known limitation rather than an actionable blocker.
