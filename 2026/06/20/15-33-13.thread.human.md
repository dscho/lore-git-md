This thread is about fixing persistent CI hangs in Git’s macOS (and now suspected Linux) test suite, specifically in `t5551` and `t5559`, caused by an upstream Apache `mod_http2` bug. The issue surfaces when Git’s test suite advertises 100,000 loose refs over HTTP/2 or HTTP/1.1, triggering either a mid-transfer abort (`curl 18`) or an indefinite stall due to Apache’s 300-second timeout.

Jeff King (Peff) has sent a 3-patch series that directly addresses the root cause. The first patch increases Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds, a change endorsed by Junio C Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and Peff himself. The second patch, just summarized, isolates the expensive "many-tags" test cases into their own repository, reducing the runtime of the entire `t5551` script and avoiding hidden costs for subsequent tests. The third patch packs refs after creating many tags, further reducing `ls-refs` advertisement time. While Peff has expressed mild skepticism about the third patch’s necessity, the series as a whole is uncontroversial and expected to be merged soon.

The key participants—Michael Montalbo (initial reporter), Peff, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio—have all converged on the consensus that the timeout increase is the right fix, with the other patches serving as complementary optimizations. The only remaining open question is whether the ref-packing patch (third in the series) is worth the added complexity, but this is unlikely to block the series. Linux CI failures are still being investigated, but the current fixes are sufficient to resolve the immediate macOS CI issues.
