This thread is about fixing persistent CI hangs on macOS (and suspected Linux) runners in Git’s test suite, specifically in `t5551` and `t5559`. The root cause was traced to Apache’s `mod_http2` bug 70131, which triggers timeouts or protocol errors when the server-side 300-second limit is hit during an `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs. Jeff King proposed a 3-patch series to address this, but after discussion, the series was narrowed to two uncontroversial patches: increasing Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds (directly fixing the root cause) and isolating the expensive test case into its own repository to reduce runtime.

The series is now effectively complete. All key participants—Jeff King, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio C Hamano—agree on the solution, and the ref-packing optimization (originally patch 3/3) was dropped as redundant. The only remaining loose end is a minor stylistic nit Junio raised about the test repository’s bare/non-bare inconsistency, which is cosmetic and doesn’t affect functionality. No further changes are expected, and the series is ready for merging. The Linux CI failures remain unconfirmed but are suspected to stem from the same Apache bug; no action is planned until more data is available.
