This thread tackles a recurring CI hang on macOS (and suspected Linux) runners in Git’s `t5551` and `t5559` tests, caused by Apache’s HTTP/2 bug 70131 triggering timeouts or stalls when advertising 100,000 loose refs. Jeff King (Peff) proposed a three-patch series to fix it: the first increases Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds (directly addressing the root cause), the second isolates the expensive test case into its own repository (reducing runtime for other tests), and the third packs refs after creating many tags (shrinking `ls-refs` time from 1.2 seconds to 24 ms).

The series is now complete and ready for final review. The core fix—the timeout increase—has broad support from Junio, Patrick Steinhardt, and Peff, and is expected to be merged soon. The second patch (test isolation) is uncontroversial, but the third (ref-packing) is under mild scrutiny: Peff himself questions its necessity, noting that the other patches already resolve the CI failures and that this optimization may not justify the added complexity. The patch is low-risk but could be dropped if reviewers agree it’s redundant. No further design changes are planned; the focus is now on final approval and integration.
