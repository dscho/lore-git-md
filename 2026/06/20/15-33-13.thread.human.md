The macOS CI hang thread has uncovered a clear root cause for the persistent failures in `t5551` and `t5559`. The issue stems from Apache’s default 300-second timeout being hit during the `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 refs, causing the server to terminate the connection mid-transfer and triggering the `curl 18` error. Patrick Steinhardt confirmed this by reproducing the error locally with a reduced timeout, and Apache’s logs explicitly show the CGI script timing out.

Michael Montalbo’s earlier fix—packing refs and aligning `$JOBS` with the runner’s core count—reduced CI runtime from a 360-minute timeout to 30–44 minutes, but it didn’t address the underlying timeout issue. The test still hits Apache’s limit, just faster. The failure has now spread to Linux CI, though the exact error there remains unclear due to truncated logs. Key participants—Jeff King, Junio C Hamano, and Patrick Steinhardt—agree the fix doesn’t eliminate the deadlock risk, and the focus has shifted to whether to increase Apache’s timeout, further optimize the test, or extend keepalive mechanisms to cover `ls-refs`. No new patch has been proposed yet, but the diagnosis is now concrete. The thread remains open, with maintainer approval still pending.
