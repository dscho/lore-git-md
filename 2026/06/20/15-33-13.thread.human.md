The macOS CI hang issue in `t5551` and `t5559` is now resolved with a two-patch series from Jeff King, ready for merging. The root cause was Apache’s `mod_http2` bug 70131, which triggered hangs or protocol errors when the server-side 300-second timeout was hit during an `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs. The first patch increases Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds, directly addressing the root cause, while the second isolates the expensive test case into its own repository, reducing runtime for subsequent tests.

Michael Montalbo initially identified the Apache bug and tested the fix, while Jeff King implemented the consensus solution. Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano both endorsed the approach, with Patrick diagnosing the root cause and Junio noting a minor stylistic nit in the second patch—though this doesn’t affect correctness. The series has broad agreement, and the only remaining loose end is the unclear Linux CI failure details, but no action is planned until more data emerges. The patches are now in Junio’s hands for merging.
