Michael Montalbo’s patch aims to fix frequent CI timeouts on GitHub’s macOS runners, where the `t5551` and `t5559` tests hang during an `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs. The hang is specific to GitHub’s virtualized macOS environment and stems from an HTTP/2 flow-control stall. Michael’s fix has two parts: packing the refs to reduce advertisement time (from 1.2 seconds to 24 ms on macOS) and aligning the CI job’s parallelism with the runner’s actual core count. Testing on his branch (`mm/macos-ci-hang-fix`) shows the tests now complete in 30–44 minutes, down from the 360-minute timeout.

Jeff King (Peff) has raised a substantive concern about the fix, questioning whether it truly resolves the underlying deadlock or merely masks it. He points to past HTTP/2 flow-control issues that were sensitive to libcurl and Apache versions, citing commit 8f2146dbf1 as precedent. Peff suggests verifying the libcurl and Apache versions on the GitHub runner versus Michael’s local setup to rule out a version-specific regression. This leaves an open question about the fix’s long-term robustness, though no alternative approaches have been proposed yet. The patch remains under review, with no maintainer decision from Junio or the CI maintainers.
