Michael Montalbo’s thread reports frequent CI timeouts on GitHub’s macOS runners during the `t5551` and `t5559` tests, which simulate an `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs. The hang is an HTTP/2 flow-control stall unique to GitHub’s virtualized macOS environment. Montalbo’s proposed fix—packing the refs and aligning `$JOBS` with the runner’s core count—has cut CI runtimes from the 360-minute timeout to 30–44 minutes, but Jeff King and now Junio C Hamano caution that the fix may only mask the underlying deadlock risk. Patrick Steinhardt has since identified a libcurl version mismatch (8.6.0 on GitHub vs 8.7.1 on GitLab) and suggested updating the macOS build image as a potential mitigation. The thread remains in diagnostic mode; no maintainer has yet signaled acceptance, and the root cause—whether in Git’s curl usage, curl itself, or Apache’s h2_mod—is still under investigation.
