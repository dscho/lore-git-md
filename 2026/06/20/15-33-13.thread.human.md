The macOS CI hang in `t5551` and `t5559` that was caused by Apache’s `mod_http2` bug 70131 has been resolved with a merged 2-patch series that increased Apache’s `Timeout` directive and isolated the problematic test case. The fix will ship in Git 2.55, and a GitLab CI status badge has been added to the project’s `README.md` to improve visibility. However, a follow-up patch to enable `GIT_TEST_LONG` for GitLab CI remains on hold pending further discussion about CI resource allocation.

The thread has since shifted to a performance optimization discussion for Git’s reftable backend, where a quadratic-time scalability issue in bulk ref deletion and re-creation was identified and fixed. Kristofer Karlsson’s patch, which exposes tombstones to iterator bounds checks, dramatically improved performance in artificial benchmarks (e.g., 8,000 refs: 14.93s → 0.145s) but showed limited real-world impact when compaction was enabled. Patrick Steinhardt has now clarified a reproducible scenario—creating and then deleting a large number of refs—where the patch’s benefits should still be observable. The next step is for Kristofer to test this scenario and report whether the improvement justifies the patch’s code complexity. If measurable gains are found, the patch may still be merged as a safeguard against worst-case performance; otherwise, it may be abandoned or deferred. The discussion remains open, with no final decision yet on whether to submit the patch formally.
