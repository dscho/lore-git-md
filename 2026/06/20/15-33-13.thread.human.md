This thread is about fixing persistent CI hangs in Git’s macOS and Linux test suites (`t5551` and `t5559`) caused by an Apache `mod_http2` bug (upstream issue 70131). The root cause was identified as Apache’s 300-second timeout being hit during the `ls-refs` advertisement of 100,000 loose refs, triggering either a mid-transfer abort (`curl 18`) or an indefinite HTTP/2 stall.

Jeff King (Peff) proposed a two-patch series to resolve the issue: the first patch increases Apache’s `Timeout` directive from 300 to 600 seconds, directly addressing the root cause, while the second isolates the expensive test case into its own repository to reduce runtime. The series has received substantive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano, who both endorsed the approach and agreed to drop a third patch (a ref-packing optimization) as redundant after the timeout increase and test isolation resolved the CI failures. The only remaining loose end is the lack of clarity around Linux CI failures due to truncated logs, but no action is planned until more data is available.

The series is now ready for merging, with all key participants aligned on the solution. The patches touch Apache configuration and test isolation logic, with no on-disk format changes, and the fix is considered pragmatic given the absence of an upstream Apache fix. The thread reflects the project’s preference for addressing flakes at their source rather than relying on client-side workarounds.
