The macOS CI hang thread, which began with Michael Montalbo’s report of test failures in `t5551` and `t5559` due to an Apache `mod_http2` bug, has now reached a clean resolution. The root cause—a 300-second timeout in Apache triggering hangs or protocol errors during `ls-refs` advertisements of 100,000 loose refs—was addressed by Jeff King’s two-patch series, which increased the Apache `Timeout` directive to 600 seconds and isolated the problematic test case in a bare repository. Both patches are now merged and will ship in Git 2.55.

The only remaining loose end was Junio C Hamano’s concern about GitLab CI visibility, which Patrick Steinhardt has now resolved with a simple, uncontroversial proposal: adding a GitLab CI status badge to the project’s `README.md`. Junio has accepted this as a low-cost way to make GitLab’s CI results as discoverable as GitHub’s, calling it "nice" and endorsing the one-line change. This closes the thread’s final open question, though Patrick’s third patch—enabling `GIT_TEST_LONG` for GitLab CI—remains on hold pending further discussion about CI resource allocation. The reftable performance quirk (redundant `fstat` calls during bulk ref creation) noted by Peff and Patrick is acknowledged as a future optimization opportunity but is not actionable in the short term.
