This is a follow-up refactoring patch to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing a minor consistency issue in the recently queued v4 patch that moved `protect_hfs` and `protect_ntfs` into `struct repo_config_values`. The patch is tiny—just four lines changed across two files—and purely mechanical: it replaces the `repo->gitdir` guard in the accessor functions `repo_protect_hfs()` and `repo_protect_ntfs()` with `repo->initialized`, aligning them with the existing pattern used for `repo_ignorecase()` and `repo_trust_executable_bit()`.

The change is defensive and uncontroversial. The commit message explains the rationale clearly: checking `repo->initialized` ensures the accessors do not call `repo_config_values()` on an uninitialized repository object, which is the same guard used elsewhere in the codebase for similar repository-scoped config fields. The updated comment in `environment.h` reflects this change, making the behavior explicit for future readers.
