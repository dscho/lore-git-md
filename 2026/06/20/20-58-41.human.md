This is a brief follow-up in the macOS linker warning suppression thread. D. Ben Knoble reports test results for the final patch (v3) on an older machine with Xcode 14.2. The patch targets Xcode 15+ linkers, so the version gate correctly avoids applying the suppression flag here. He confirms no duplicate warnings appear in a Meson build, and the Makefile build works without issues. He plans to test on a newer machine (where the warning would appear) later but does not expect problems.
