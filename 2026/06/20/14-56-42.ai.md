This is Junio Hamano's review of the second patch in Pablo Sabater's v6 series implementing cascading indentation for `git log --graph`. The patch introduces an abstraction layer in `revision.c` to insulate `graph.c` from the internal traversal model.

Junio's review is substantive and identifies two technical concerns. First, he notes that the current implementation's logic for choosing between `topo_queue` and `revs->commits` is an intimate implementation detail that should ideally be hidden inside `revision.c`—a point he accepts as tolerable for now. Second, and more critically, he raises a safety issue with calling `get_commit_action()` on commits that have not yet been processed by the history traversal. While he believes the current use is safe (because `--graph` sets `rewrite_parents`, which prevents the side-effecting code path in `get_commit_action()` from being triggered), he suggests adding a defensive `BUG()` check to guard against future misuse, particularly in the presence of `-L` (line-level log filtering) without `rewrite_parents`.

The review also includes a minor style nit: removing the space between function names and their parameter lists. Junio does not object to the core design or functionality of the patch, and his feedback is focused on hardening the implementation against future regressions. The patch remains technically sound and addresses the architectural blocker that previously stalled the series, but will need a follow-up to incorporate the defensive checks before it can be considered fully reviewed.
