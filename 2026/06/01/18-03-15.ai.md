This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about migrating `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configurations as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. 

Tian Yuchen, the original author of the patch series, is now reconsidering their earlier agreement to include `has_symlinks` migration in the same series. They note that migrating `has_symlinks` appears more complex than initially thought, with some call sites having deep call stacks and minimal parameter passing, making repository plumbing potentially cumbersome. 

This represents a partial reversal from their previous position where they had committed to Junio C Hamano's suggestion to handle both configurations together for design cohesion. The author now suggests focusing solely on `trust_executable_bit` for this series, implying `has_symlinks` might need separate treatment.

The technical challenge centers around the observation that `has_symlinks` is used in contexts where plumbing a `struct repository*` parameter would require extensive call stack modifications, suggesting the migration might not be worth the architectural disruption. This raises new questions about how to balance the `the_repository` removal goals against practical implementation constraints when dealing with widely-used configuration values.
