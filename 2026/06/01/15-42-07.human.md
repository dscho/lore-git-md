This is part 4 of an 8-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically migrating the `pack_compression_level` configuration from a global variable to `struct repo_config_values`. The change ensures the compression level setting is repository-specific while maintaining its eager parsing behavior, which is important for performance in packfile operations.

The patch updates five files (`builtin/fast-import.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `environment.c`, `environment.h`, and `object-file.c`) to reference the compression level through the repository's config structure rather than the global variable. The changes are mechanical but important for eliminating cross-repository state leakage.

This iteration follows established patterns from earlier parts of the series and has already received positive reviews from Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi (listed as mentors). The changes are performance-neutral since they maintain the existing eager parsing behavior for this critical path.

The series as a whole is nearing completion, with parts 1-3 already reviewed and this part addressing one of the remaining configuration settings. The work connects to broader efforts around repository independence and libification.
