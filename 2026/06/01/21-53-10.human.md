This is the final patch in the `--max-count-oldest` feature series, now ready for merging after eight iterations of review and polish. The feature provides a new `--max-count-oldest` option for revision walks that efficiently retrieves the oldest commits in a history (the opposite of `--max-count` which gets the newest commits).

The implementation preserves Jeff King's sliding window algorithm (`retrieve_oldest_commits()`) with O(K) space and O(N) time characteristics. It has been thoroughly reviewed with all edge cases handled, including proper integration with `--boundary` and `--graph` output modes. The test suite covers all option combinations and has been made fully portable across platforms.
