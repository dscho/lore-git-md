This is part 3 of an 8-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on migrating the `zlib_compression_level` configuration from a global variable to `struct repo_config_values`. The change affects six files that handle object compression, including index-pack, diff, and loose object writing.

The patch maintains eager parsing of the compression level (as established in previous iterations) since it affects core operations where lazy parsing could cause performance issues. The modifications are mechanical - replacing direct global variable access with repository-specific configuration lookups through `repo_config_values()`.

This follows the same pattern seen in earlier patches of the series (parts 1-2 covered `trust_ctime` and `check_stat`), with similar motivation: preventing cross-repository state leakage while preserving existing performance characteristics. The changes touch compression behavior in object-file.c (loose objects), builtin/index-pack.c (pack creation), http-push.c (remote transfers), and diff.c (patch generation).

The patch has already been reviewed positively by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi (listed as mentors) and fits into the broader context of the v4 series that Junio Hamano has indicated is ready for merging pending final confirmations. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, representing incremental progress on the long-running `the_repository` removal project.
