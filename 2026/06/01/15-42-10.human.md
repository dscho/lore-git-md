This is part 7 of an 8-patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on migrating the `sparse.expectFilesOutsideOfPatterns` configuration from a global variable to repository-specific storage in `struct repo_config_values`. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by maintaining eager parsing for performance-critical sparse-checkout behavior while preventing cross-repository state leakage.

The change affects three files with straightforward mechanical updates:
- Moves the configuration from a global `int` to `repo_config_values`
- Updates the initialization and access patterns to match prior migrations
- Maintains the same behavior while making it repository-specific

The patch has already received positive reviews from Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi (listed as mentors) and fits cleanly into the v4 series that Junio Hamano has indicated is ready for merging. This continues the incremental but important work of eliminating global configuration state, with sparse-checkout being one of the final areas to address in this batch.
