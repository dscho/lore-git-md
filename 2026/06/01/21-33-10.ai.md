This marks an important milestone in Patrick's long-term effort to abstract Git's object storage layer, following similar work on other backends. The loose object handling is now fully isolated in odb/source-loose.c with no remaining dependencies on the files backend. The series appears well-vetted with its incremental approach and comprehensive test coverage showing no behavior regressions.

With Junio's indication of intent to merge, this work will likely graduate to 'master' in the next integration cycle unless last-minute issues surface. The clean separation achieved here sets the stage for future work on pluggable storage backends and transaction support.
