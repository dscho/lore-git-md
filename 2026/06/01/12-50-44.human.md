Performance optimization for index-pack's delta resolution.

Derrick Stolee provides a review of Arijit Banerjee's patch that improves index-pack performance by retaining delta bases in the cache rather than immediately freeing them. The patch had already shown promising benchmark results (15-16% speedup on linux.git with minimal memory impact) and passed the test suite.

Stolee suggests two additional validations: (1) running with SANITIZE=leak to confirm no memory leaks are introduced (though he notes the PR's CI already shows passing leak checks), and (2) re-running the benchmarks using Git's standard performance test framework (t/perf/p5302-pack-index.sh) for more comparable results. He praises the simple, focused change while offering these constructive suggestions for making the results more robust and standardized.

The review is substantive - Stolee engages with both the technical correctness and the presentation of results, suggesting concrete ways to strengthen the case while acknowledging the patch's clear merit. His feedback indicates the patch is on track but could benefit from these additional validations before final acceptance.
