This is part 6 of an 8-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on migrating the `core.sparseCheckoutCone` configuration from a global variable into `struct repo_config_values`. The change prevents cross-repository state leakage while maintaining the existing eager parsing behavior, which is important since this setting determines fundamental sparse-checkout behavior during early repository setup.

The patch updates references across 6 files (builtin/mv.c, builtin/sparse-checkout.c, dir.c, environment.c, environment.h, sparse-index.c) to use the new repository-specific storage. The changes are mechanical but widespread, following the established pattern for this refactoring effort. The commit explains the performance rationale for keeping eager parsing, as this setting affects critical paths in the sparse-checkout subsystem.

This continues the incremental approach seen in previous parts of the series, with similar scope and impact to parts 1-5 that have already received positive reviews. The changes maintain existing behavior while progressing the larger goal of eliminating configuration globals. The patch appears ready for merging as part of the broader series that Junio Hamano has indicated is nearing completion.
