This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 18-part series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The patch removes the last remaining dependency between the loose object source and the files backend by eliminating the `files` pointer from `struct odb_source_loose`.

The change makes the loose backend fully standalone - it now takes the ODB, path, and local flag directly in its constructor rather than getting them indirectly through the files source. This completes the architectural separation that the series has been building toward, with all callbacks now operating purely on the loose source's own state.

The patch follows the series' pattern of behavior-preserving changes, just updating the initialization and removing the now-unused field. With this, the loose object handling is properly isolated as a distinct odb_source implementation, achieving one of the key goals of the ODB abstraction work. The series appears ready for integration pending any final review feedback.
