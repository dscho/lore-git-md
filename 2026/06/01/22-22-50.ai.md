This is a maintainer response in the ongoing thread about deprecating `git-init-db` in favor of `git-init`. Junio C Hamano pushes back against the proposed deprecation entirely, suggesting the alias should remain indefinitely rather than adding warnings or deprecation markers.

The email makes two key points:
1. The cost of maintaining the alias is negligible, and the name "init-db" still conceptually fits Git's expanded notion of a "database" (beyond just object storage)
2. The historical naming could have been worse (e.g. "init-odb"), implying the current name isn't misleading enough to warrant removal

This represents a significant shift in the discussion - where previous emails had focused on implementation details of the deprecation (flags vs warnings), Junio is now questioning whether deprecation is needed at all. His position contrasts with the earlier consensus between Patrick Steinhardt, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, and Phillip Wood that the command should be deprecated with appropriate user-facing signals.

The maintainer's stance suggests this patch series may need substantial revision or could be rejected unless Patrick can provide compelling counterarguments. The thread now hinges on whether the historical alias provides enough value to retain versus the project's general preference for command name rationalization.
