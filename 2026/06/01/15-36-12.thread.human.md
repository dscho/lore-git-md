Patrick Steinhardt's `git history drop` subcommand series, which adds commit-dropping capability to the experimental history command, has received its first substantive review from maintainer Junio Hamano. The v2 series introduces a complete implementation that refactors ref update handling and adds the new subcommand with thorough test coverage.

Junio's review focuses on two specific areas for improvement. First, he questions whether the new `update_worktree()` helper duplicates existing checkout functionality that should be reused rather than reimplemented. Second, he notes a stylistic inconsistency where an error message incorrectly uses backticks around a command (a convention reserved for documentation). While these are relatively narrow technical points, they come from the maintainer and will likely lead to a v3 iteration addressing the feedback.

The core functionality of the drop subcommand - which allows removing a commit and replaying its descendants on top of its parent - remains uncontested. The current limitations around root and merge commits are acknowledged as future work. With Junio's review now incorporated into the discussion, the next steps will be Patrick's response and potential revision of the patches.
