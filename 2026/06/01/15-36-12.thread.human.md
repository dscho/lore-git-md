Patrick Steinhardt's `git history drop` series has reached its final form after six iterations, introducing a new subcommand that removes a commit from history while replaying its descendants. The implementation required substantial refactoring of Git's reset machinery, including modernizing flags, renaming functions for clarity, and fixing edge cases in index handling. 

Junio C Hamano identified and Patrick addressed a final issue with dry-run behavior in reference updates during v6. The series now has comprehensive test coverage (537 lines) and documentation updates. Several contributors provided feedback along the way, with Phillip Wood reviewing the reset changes and Kristoffer Haugsbakk improving documentation. 

This work not only delivers the drop functionality but also advances broader architectural goals like removing `the_repository` global variable and modernizing reset internals. With all technical concerns resolved and no outstanding objections, the series appears ready for merging into the experimental history command.
