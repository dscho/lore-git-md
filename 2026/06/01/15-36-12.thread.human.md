The `git history drop` subcommand series by Patrick Steinhardt has reached its conclusion. After eight iterations and extensive review, Junio C Hamano has accepted the entire v8 series—all eleven patches—and has already applied a trivial whitespace fix locally, clearing the way for merging. This series introduces the new `git history drop` command, which allows removing a commit from history and replaying its descendants on top of its parent. Alongside the user-facing feature, the series modernizes Git’s reset API, advances the ongoing effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable, and improves reference update architecture with better phase separation and conflict detection.

The implementation is now complete, with all prior feedback addressed, including a critical dispute over ref resolution in `find_head_tree_change()` that was resolved in v8. The command supports dry-run mode, works in both bare and non-bare repositories, preserves local changes, and includes robust test coverage for edge cases like detached HEAD states. Junio’s acceptance marks the series as technically unblocked, and no further changes or resends are expected. The thread is effectively closed, with the feature ready to land in an upcoming Git release.
