Jacob's approach would tighten the test case to eliminate ambiguity rather than making the implementation artificially rigid. This aligns with Junio's preference for maintaining the command's flexible matching behavior while making the test properly validate it.

The thread continues to focus on test design rather than implementation changes, with Jacob now working on a v2 that will restructure the test to avoid ambiguous cases.
