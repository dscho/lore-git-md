Documentation patch clarifying the `@` prefix for raw timestamps in Git's date parsing. 

This patch documents a little-known behavior where timestamps smaller than 100,000,000 (fewer than 9 digits) must be prefixed with `@` to be interpreted as raw Unix timestamps rather than other date formats. The behavior was introduced in 2012 but never documented until now. The patch adds clear documentation in `date-formats.adoc` and includes test cases in `t/t0006-date.sh` to verify the difference between prefixed and unprefixed small timestamps (e.g., `@2000` vs `2000`).

The patch is co-authored by Junio Hamano, suggesting it has maintainer approval. The changes are straightforward - documenting existing behavior rather than introducing new functionality - and the test coverage is thorough, including edge cases around the 100,000,000 threshold. This appears to be a well-executed documentation improvement that will help users understand this subtle parsing behavior.
