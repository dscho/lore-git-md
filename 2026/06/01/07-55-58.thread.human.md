This thread began as a proposal from Patrick Steinhardt to both rename Git's initialization internals (from `init-db` to `init`) and deprecate the historical `git-init-db` command alias. After pushback from maintainer Junio C Hamano, who considers the `init-db` name conceptually valid and sees no maintenance burden in keeping it, the discussion has focused solely on the internal renaming while preserving the alias.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk has now contributed a thoughtful perspective on how Git documents command aliases, suggesting that even when keeping `git-init-db`, it could be explicitly marked as a "soft deprecated" legacy alias in the documentation - following the precedent of `git-stage` (documented as an alias for `git-add`) rather than `git-whatchanged` (which was eventually removed). While this doesn't change the technical outcome - Junio's position against any form of deprecation appears firm - it highlights an inconsistency in how Git documents its command aliases that may warrant broader consideration.

The series now appears ready to proceed with the uncontroversial internal renaming (updating `builtin/init-db.c` to `builtin/init.c` and `cmd_init_db()` to `cmd_init()`) while leaving the `git-init-db` alias intact with no deprecation markers. This resolves the original naming inconsistency while respecting the project's preference for preserving historically valid interfaces.
