The thread about deprecating the `git-init-db` alias in favor of `git-init` has taken an unexpected turn. What began as a technical discussion about implementation details (documentation updates, deprecation flags, and runtime warnings) has now become a fundamental debate about whether to deprecate the alias at all.

Patrick Steinhardt originally proposed the change as part of Git's ongoing command name rationalization, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk and Phillip Wood contributing implementation suggestions. The technical work was largely settled, including code and documentation updates, with only minor questions remaining about how prominently to signal the deprecation to users.

However, maintainer Junio Hamano has now challenged the premise itself. He argues that "init-db" remains conceptually accurate (since Git's repository is indeed a database) and that the maintenance burden of keeping the alias is negligible. This contradicts the earlier consensus that the alias should be deprecated, putting the entire effort in question.

The discussion now hinges on whether historical accuracy and minimal maintenance costs outweigh the project's general preference for command name consistency. Patrick will need to either present new arguments for deprecation or revise the series substantially to address Junio's concerns. Given the maintainer's stance, this change may not proceed in its current form unless compelling new rationale emerges.
