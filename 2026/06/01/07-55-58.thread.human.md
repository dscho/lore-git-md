This thread began as an attempt by Patrick Steinhardt to align Git's internal naming (which uses `init-db`) with the modern `git-init` command name, including potentially deprecating the legacy `git-init-db` alias. However, after pushback from maintainer Junio Hamano, the scope was significantly narrowed. Junio argued that renaming would simply shift the burden from those accustomed to the modern names to those familiar with the historical ones, without clear benefit. 

The discussion has now concluded with agreement to maintain the status quo: both the internal `init-db` naming and the `git-init-db` alias will remain unchanged. The only remaining question is whether and how to document the legacy status of `git-init-db` in the man pages, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk having proposed some specific documentation markings earlier in the thread. This serves as a clear example of how the maintainer's judgment guides Git's evolution, particularly in balancing modernization against preserving historical continuity and minimizing disruption.
