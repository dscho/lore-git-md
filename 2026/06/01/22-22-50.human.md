This is a follow-up in the thread about deprecating the `git-init-db` command alias. Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, weighs in with a surprising reversal - suggesting that perhaps `git-init-db` shouldn't be deprecated at all, contrary to the emerging consensus in the thread.

Junio argues that:
1. The maintenance cost of keeping the alias is negligible
2. The name "init-db" still conceptually fits Git's expanded notion of what constitutes the "database" (beyond just objects)
3. The historical naming could have been worse (e.g. "init-odb" would have been more problematic)

This directly challenges the technical approach that had been converging in the thread, where Patrick Steinhardt's patches were preparing to deprecate the command with both a `DEPRECATED` flag and runtime warnings. Junio's position introduces new uncertainty about whether the deprecation should proceed at all, despite previous agreement from other contributors like Kristoffer Haugsbakk and Phillip Wood.

The maintainer's intervention suggests this may need to go back to the drawing board, as his stance could override the earlier technical consensus. This is a significant development in what had appeared to be a straightforward cleanup effort.
