Patrick Steinhardt, the primary developer behind the `git history` command, acknowledges that the command currently drops commit signatures during operation, unlike `git rebase` which preserves them via its `-S` flag.

He explicitly invites other contributors to implement this functionality if they're interested, noting that without volunteer effort it may take significant time to address.
