This is a feature patch series introducing `--[no-]range-diff-notes` to `git format-patch`. The series, authored by Kristoffer Haugsbakk, adds options to control whether notes (such as commit messages or other metadata) are included in the range-diff output, independent of whether notes are included in the patches themselves.

The motivation is practical: users who already use `git format-patch --notes` may want to suppress notes *only* in the range-diff portion of the output, without affecting the rest of the patch series. The author notes they’ve been using a personal hack for this purpose for months but are now submitting a clean, general implementation for upstream.

The series consists of three patches. The first two are preparatory: one simplifies parameter handling in `get_notes_arg`, and the other renames a struct member in `revision.h` for clarity. The third patch implements the new options, updates the documentation, and adds tests in `t/t3206-range-diff.sh`. The changes touch `builtin/log.c`, `log-tree.c`, `revision.c`, and `revision.h`, with no behavior changes outside the new functionality.

The implementation looks well-scoped and targeted. The commit messages are clear, the tests are thorough (105 lines added), and the CI run (linked in the cover letter) appears to have passed. There’s no indication of controversy or edge cases that might derail this, though the author does note this is "new code, not tested for months" in production, which is worth keeping in mind.

If you care about `format-patch` output or range-diff customization, this is a small but useful addition that’s likely to land without much fuss.
