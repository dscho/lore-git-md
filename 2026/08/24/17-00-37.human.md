This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `report` hook series for `git-receive-pack`. The series, which adds a server-side hook allowing administrators to filter or modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates, is currently queued in `seen` and otherwise ready for integration. The only remaining blocker is the hook’s name: Junio C Hamano objects to `report` as overly generic, and Patrick Steinhardt agrees, calling both `report` and the earlier `pre-report` "awful names." Steinhardt suggests `receive-report` as a more specific alternative. No other technical concerns are raised; the discussion is now focused solely on naming before the series can graduate to `next`. Expect a v4 addressing this feedback while preserving the agreed-upon exit semantics and implementation.
