This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s four-patch v2 series optimizing reftable stack reloads during transactions. It moves the list-file lock from `struct reftable_addition` into `struct reftable_stack` and adds a per-addition `locked` bit field to prevent premature lock release when multiple additions operate on the same stack. The change is mechanical, touching only `reftable/stack.c`, `reftable/stack.h`, and the unit-test file; it does not alter on-disk format or external APIs. A new unit test validates that a second addition correctly fails with `REFTABLE_LOCK_ERROR` instead of releasing the lock held by the first. This patch directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s safety concern from the v1 review and is a prerequisite for the final optimization in patch 4.
