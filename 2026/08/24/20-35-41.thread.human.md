This thread introduces `--[no-]range-diff-notes` options to `git format-patch`, allowing users to control whether Git notes appear in the range-diff section independently of the patches themselves. Kristoffer Haugsbakk posted the initial v1 series, which passed all tests but had not yet received review feedback.

The key development is Junio’s review of the third patch, where he raises a usability concern: the current implementation may not behave as users expect. Specifically, `--no-notes --range-diff-notes` suppresses notes in *both* the patches and the range-diff, because `--range-diff-notes` inherits the patch notes setting by default. This contradicts the intuitive expectation that `--range-diff-notes` should override notes *only* for the range-diff, leaving patch notes untouched. The discussion is now focused on whether the design needs adjustment to align with user expectations. No other reviews or objections have been recorded yet.
