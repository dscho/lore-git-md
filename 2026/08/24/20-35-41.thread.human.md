This thread is about a three-patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk that adds `--[no-]range-diff-notes` options to `git format-patch`, allowing users to control whether Git notes appear in the range-diff section independently of the patches themselves. The series is currently under review, with all tests passing in CI.

The core of the discussion revolves around a usability concern raised by Junio C Hamano about the third patch. The issue is that `--range-diff-notes` currently inherits the patch notes setting by default, which means `--no-notes --range-diff-notes` suppresses notes in both contexts rather than enabling them only for the range-diff. Kristoffer acknowledged the problem in his reply, explaining that `--no-notes` clears the notes list, leaving nothing for `--range-diff-notes` to inherit. He suggested a potential solution—maintaining a separate "shadow list" of notes for range-diffs—but also noted a conceptual hurdle: since `format-patch` doesn’t show notes by default, it’s unclear what `--range-diff-notes` should display when no explicit notes ref is provided.

The thread is now at a decision point. No concrete redesign has been proposed yet, but the discussion is open about whether to adjust the option semantics to better align with user expectations or to document the current behavior as intentional. The series touches `builtin/log.c`, `log-tree.c`, `revision.c`, `revision.h`, and the documentation, with new CLI options and struct members introduced. The preparatory patches (refactoring and renaming) are uncontroversial, but the core feature’s design is still under debate. No other reviews or objections have been recorded so far.
