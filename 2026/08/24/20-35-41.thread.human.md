This thread is about a three-patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk that adds `--[no-]range-diff-notes` options to `git format-patch`, allowing users to control whether Git notes appear in the range-diff section independently of the patches themselves. The feature aims to address workflows where iteration notes (like "v3: [description]") are useful in patches but clutter the range-diff comparison.

The series is currently stalled on a design question raised by Junio Hamano. The core issue is that the current implementation doesn’t cleanly support the intuitive use case where `--no-notes --range-diff-notes` would suppress notes in the patches while *including* them in the range-diff. Instead, `--no-notes` clears the notes list entirely, so `--range-diff-notes` has nothing to display. Junio has made it clear that this interaction is confusing and may block the feature unless a clearer semantics can be devised. Kristoffer acknowledged the problem and sketched a potential fix (a separate "shadow list" of notes for range-diffs), but no concrete redesign has been proposed yet.

The patches themselves are technically sound: they refactor existing code, rename a struct member for clarity, and add comprehensive test coverage. The series touches `builtin/log.c`, `revision.h`, and related files, and all tests pass in CI. However, the unresolved usability concern is now the primary obstacle. No other reviewers have weighed in, and the thread is waiting for Kristoffer to either revise the design or defend the current behavior. Until then, the series remains under review with no new version posted.
