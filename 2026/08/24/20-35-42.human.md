This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s three-part series adding `--[no-]range-diff-notes` options to `git format-patch`. It is a preparatory refactoring that simplifies the `get_notes_args` function by removing a redundant parameter.

The patch cleans up a minor inconsistency introduced in an earlier commit (85bd88a7, 2025-09-25). Originally, `get_notes_args` took two parameters: a `strvec` and a `rev_info` struct, even though the `strvec` was already accessible as `rev->rdiff_log_arg`. This patch removes the redundant `strvec` parameter, making the function take only the `rev_info` struct. It also tidies up a long line in the `for_each_string_list` call.

The change is confined to `builtin/log.c` and affects only internal plumbing; there is no user-visible behavior change. The patch is compile-tested and appears straightforward, with no obvious risks or edge cases. It sets the stage for the struct member rename in patch 2/3 and the new options in patch 3/3.
