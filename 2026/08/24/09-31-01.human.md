This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s four-patch v2 series that optimizes stack reloads in the reftable backend. The patch moves the list-file lock from `struct reftable_addition` into `struct reftable_stack` so the lock can be shared across multiple additions on the same stack. To prevent a second addition from releasing the lock while the first still needs it, a new `locked` bit field is added to `reftable_addition`; the lock is only released when the addition that acquired it calls `reftable_addition_close()`. A unit test in `t/unit-tests/u-reftable-stack.c` verifies that two concurrent additions behave correctly. The change is purely internal and does not alter the on-disk format or the public API. It directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s safety concern from the v1 review and paves the way for the final performance optimization in patch 4.
