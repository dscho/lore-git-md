This is the sixth patch in the v4 series adding path-related keys to `git repo info`. The patch implements `path.git-prefix`, which exposes the relative path from the repository’s working tree root to the current directory, matching the behavior of `git rev-parse --show-prefix`.

Lucas Seiki Oshiro raises a substantive design question in his reply: whether `path.git-prefix` belongs in `git repo info` at all. He argues that the key represents "current directory information" rather than "repository information," suggesting a conceptual mismatch with the command’s intended scope. This is the first pushback on the series’ design beyond mechanical or naming concerns, and it directly challenges the patch’s inclusion. The author’s rebuttal (if any) will likely focus on scriptability and consistency with other `git repo info` keys, but the objection is now on record as a potential point of contention.

The patch itself is technically straightforward, using `repo->prefix` and adding test coverage for both root and subdirectory cases. No mechanical issues remain, and the implementation aligns with the series’ established patterns.
