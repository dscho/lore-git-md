This is a reply in the ongoing bugfix thread for `contrib/credential/libsecret`, where Daniel Martí responds to Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of his patch.

Daniel acknowledges Junio’s alternative approach—retaining the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag and conditionally loading secrets only when `secret_item_get_secret()` returns NULL—but argues for his original design. He explains that `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` is not a single optimized call but rather a two-step process (SearchItems followed by GetSecrets) that silently discards errors, including locked items skipped client-side. His patch replaces this with a single SearchItems call plus one explicit GetSecret for the relevant item, keeping the round-trip count identical in the common case while ensuring all errors are surfaced. Junio’s fallback approach would add a third round trip in rare cases and leave the error-prone path untested, whereas Daniel’s version unifies the code path and ensures failures are consistently reported.

The discussion remains focused on design trade-offs: Junio prioritizes preserving the batch-fetch optimization for the common case, while Daniel emphasizes simplicity and reliability by eliminating silent failures. No new technical objections have emerged, and the patch’s correctness is not in dispute. The next step is likely a v2 incorporating this rationale or further discussion. The lack of test coverage is accepted by both parties due to the race condition’s reproducibility challenges.
