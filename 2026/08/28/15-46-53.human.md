This is a **bugfix patch** in Elijah Newren’s five-part series tightening Git’s safety checks during conflict resolution. The patch removes an overly broad restriction: it now allows `git commit <paths>` when a rebase operation results in an empty commit, since such commits pose no risk of repository corruption.

The change is minimal and uncontroversial. It deletes two lines from `builtin/commit.c` that previously blocked partial commits during a rebase of an empty commit, and updates the corresponding test in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh` to expect success instead of an error. Junio C Hamano has already reviewed and approved the patch, calling it "Looking good."

This patch resolves the last substantive technical question in the series, clearing the way for the remaining patches to extend the existing protection against unsafe commit operations (`git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`) to `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`. The series as a whole is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and ready for final review.
