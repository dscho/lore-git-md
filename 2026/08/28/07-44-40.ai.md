This is the **v3 cover letter** for a **bugfix patch series** targeting the merge machinery. The series extends Git's existing protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution to cover both `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` for `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`. The goal is to prevent users from accidentally corrupting repository state by amending the wrong commit or committing only a subset of paths while resolving conflicts.

The series now consists of **five patches** (up from three in v2), with two new preparatory patches addressing Junio C Hamano's feedback: (1) renaming `FROM_REBASE_PICK` to `FROM_REBASE_EMPTY_PICK` and `is_from_rebase()` to `is_from_rebase_empty()` to clarify their purpose, and (2) explicitly allowing partial commits when a rebase pick becomes empty (resolving the **substantive technical question** raised in earlier reviews about whether this restriction was justified). The error message for attempted amends on now-dropped empty commits has also been reworded for clarity.

Key changes since v2:
- The **controversial restriction** on partial commits during conflict resolution for empty commits has been **removed** in patch 2/5, addressing Junio's concern that the result would still be an empty commit and thus harmless. This simplifies the logic and reduces the scope of the series' behavioral changes.
- The new `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` helper (introduced in v2) is retained and now used to consolidate detection of ongoing operations, with improved path accessor functions to avoid hardcoded paths.
- Error messages have been tweaked to better distinguish between conflict stops and clean interactive rebase stops (e.g., `edit`, `break`).

Files touched include `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, `sequencer.h`, and test coverage in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`, `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, and `t4151-am-abort.sh`. The series aligns with the `the_repository` removal effort by replacing `the_repository` with `s->repo` where possible, though the partial commit callsite retains `the_repository` due to lack of a readily available repository instance.

The series is **technically sound** and addresses a real usability hazard. The preparatory patches (1-2) are uncontroversial and could likely be merged independently. The remaining patches (3-5) are narrowly focused on extending the existing protection logic to additional commands, with clear test coverage. The **substantive technical question** about partial commits for empty commits has been resolved in favor of allowing them, removing the primary source of contention. The series is now **ready for final review** and likely to proceed to `next`.
