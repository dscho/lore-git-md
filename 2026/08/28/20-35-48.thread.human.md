This thread is about a bug in `git ls-files` and `git add` where negative (exclusion) pathspecs are incorrectly affected by the common-prefix optimization meant only for positive pathspecs. The bug can cause negative pathspecs to degenerate into empty strings or trigger out-of-bounds reads. Diogo Castro proposed a minimal fix that skips prefix-stripping for negative pathspecs entirely, along with three new tests.

The patch is under review, and the key point of contention is whether the fix is correct as-is or if the common prefix should instead be recalculated to include negative pathspecs. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, raised this design question, suggesting the latter approach would make negative pathspecs relative to the full tree rather than absolute. Diogo has pushed back, arguing that the optimization is meant only for positive pathspecs and that extending it to negatives could negate its performance benefits. The discussion is now at an impasse, with no alternative implementation proposed yet. The outcome will determine whether the patch proceeds as-is or requires a more invasive change.
