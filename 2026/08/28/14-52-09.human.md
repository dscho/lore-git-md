This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 8-part series refactoring how Git handles object database (ODB) alternates during repository creation. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, aiming to simplify the ODB interface by moving alternates handling into the backend.

This specific patch renames `setup_reference()` to `collect_alternates()` and refactors it to gather alternate paths into a `strvec` instead of immediately writing them to the alternates file. The change is preparatory, deferring the actual writing of alternates until after the ODB is created. Toon Claes’s review notes that the guard clause (`if (option_required_reference.nr || option_optional_reference.nr)`) makes more sense in the context of the next patch, suggesting the refactoring is cleanly staged.

The patch is mechanical and introduces no user-visible behavior changes. It replaces `add_one_reference()` with `add_one_alternate()`, using a new `struct add_one_alternate_data` to track whether an alternate is required or optional. Reviewers are likely to focus on whether the refactoring preserves edge-case correctness in `git clone --reference` or URI rewriting, though the patch’s straightforward nature may limit substantive feedback.

This is a well-structured step in a larger effort to consolidate alternates handling, and the review so far indicates the changes are clear and intentional. The patch itself is unlikely to be controversial, but it sets the stage for more significant changes in later patches, particularly the removal of the `write_alternates()` callback.
