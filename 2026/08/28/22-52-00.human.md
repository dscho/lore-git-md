This is the second patch in Junio C Hamano’s 8-patch series refactoring the `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore` internals. It moves new-branch-name validation from the monolithic `checkout_main()` helper into `checkout_branch()`, where it logically belongs.

The change is a straightforward cleanup: the validation calls (`validate_branchname()` and `validate_new_branchname()`) are lifted from `checkout_main()` and placed at the top of `checkout_branch()`. This makes the code more modular, since `checkout_paths()`—which never creates a branch—no longer needs to carry the validation logic. The patch is small (11 insertions, 11 deletions) and passes the test suite, suggesting it is safe and uncontroversial. It could plausibly be merged independently if the rest of the series needs more time.
