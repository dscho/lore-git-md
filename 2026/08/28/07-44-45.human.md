This is the fifth and final patch in Elijah Newren’s v3 series extending Git’s conflict-resolution safety checks. It blocks partial commits (`git commit <paths>`) during conflict resolution for `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`, mirroring the existing protection for merges and cherry-picks.

The patch reuses the `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` helper introduced earlier in the series to detect ongoing operations and reject partial commits with clear error messages. It deliberately permits partial commits when a rebase pick becomes empty (handled in patch 2/5), since those are not conflict stops. Test coverage is thorough, adding new cases to `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`, `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, and `t4151-am-abort.sh`.

This is a well-motivated bugfix that closes a long-standing gap in Git’s conflict-resolution safety net. The logic is uncontroversial, the tests are comprehensive, and the error messages are clear. The series as a whole is technically sound and ready for final review; this patch is the last piece of the puzzle.
