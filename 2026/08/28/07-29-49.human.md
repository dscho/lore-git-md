This is the latest exchange in Elijah Newren’s three-patch bugfix series that addresses a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The series fixes crashes in `git replay` and other operations when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by repacking, leaving processes unable to locate objects that still exist in other packs.

The email is a reply to Jeff King (Peff)’s review of the final patch (now 3/3 after dropping the original 3/4). Elijah acknowledges Peff’s three minor points: he agrees to drop the unnecessary comment rewrite, confirms that the `stale_packs_detected` flag is no longer needed now that patch 3/4 has been dropped, and asks for suggestions on renaming `fill_midx_entry()` to better reflect its new tri-state return semantics (`MIDX_FILL_HIT`, `MIDX_FILL_MISS`, `MIDX_FILL_OWNER_UNAVAILABLE`). The core recovery logic—gating the fallback scan of MIDX-covered packs on `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ`—remains unchanged and is explicitly endorsed by Peff.

The series is now very close to merge-ready. Patches 1/3 and 2/3 are uncontroversial, and patch 3/3 has addressed all substantive feedback. The remaining discussion is about code clarity rather than correctness, and Elijah’s responses suggest he will make the requested adjustments. The only open question is whether to rename `fill_midx_entry()`; if no better name emerges, the current one will likely stay, as Peff concedes the risk of confusion is low. The series is rebased on `ps/odb-generic-corrupt-objects` and has no unresolved conflicts.
