This is Junio C Hamano's follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the `--[no-]range-diff-notes` feature for `git format-patch`. The thread remains focused on the usability issue raised in the third patch of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's three-part series.

Junio restates the core problem: the current design does not support the intuitive expectation that `--no-notes --range-diff-notes` should suppress notes in the patches while still including them in the range-diff. Instead, because `--range-diff-notes` falls back to inheriting the patch notes setting, both contexts end up suppressed. He expresses a preference for avoiding an option whose interaction with `--notes` is not straightforward, hinting that the feature may need a redesign to be acceptable.

The email does not propose a specific solution, but it reinforces the maintainer's stance that the current behavior is problematic. The ball is now back in Kristoffer's court to either revise the implementation (e.g., by maintaining a separate notes list for range-diffs) or argue for the current behavior as intentional. Until this is resolved, the series is unlikely to move forward. No other review feedback or objections are present in this message.
