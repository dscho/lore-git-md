This is a **bugfix patch** in Elijah Newren’s five-part series tightening Git’s protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. This specific patch removes an overly broad restriction: it allows `git commit <paths>` when a rebase pick becomes empty, since the resulting commit remains empty and thus poses no risk of repository corruption.

The change is minimal and uncontroversial. It deletes two lines from `builtin/commit.c` that previously blocked partial commits during a rebase of an empty commit, and updates the corresponding test in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh` to expect success instead of an error. Junio C Hamano explicitly approves the patch ("Looking good"), confirming that the restriction was unnecessary.

This patch resolves the last substantive technical question in the series, clearing the way for the remaining patches to extend the existing protection logic to `git commit --amend` and additional commands. The series as a whole is now technically sound, well-motivated, and ready for final review. No further objections or open questions remain.
