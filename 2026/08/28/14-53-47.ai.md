However, Toon’s observation highlights a potential oversight: while the old `write_alternates()` callback was used only during initial setup (e.g., `git clone`), the new creation-time mechanism still modifies the alternates file on disk, and concurrent operations (e.g., multiple clones referencing the same alternates) could theoretically race. The patch’s commit message does not address this, and the broader series does not appear to have considered locking in the new flow.

This is a substantive review comment that could prompt a rethink of the locking strategy or, at minimum, a justification for why it is no longer needed.
