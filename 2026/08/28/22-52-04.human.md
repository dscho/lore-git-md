This is the sixth patch in Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore` internals. The patch eliminates the monolithic `checkout_main()` choke point by restructuring the three command entry points (`cmd_switch()`, `cmd_restore()`, and `cmd_checkout()`) to call the extracted utility helpers directly. This is the core refactoring step that implements the libification path clarified in earlier threads: reusable logic is now invoked directly by each command, rather than routed through a shared helper.

The patch removes `checkout_main()` and its supporting `enum checkout_command`, replacing them with three independent functions that each handle their own option parsing, validation, and dispatch. `cmd_switch()` and `cmd_restore()` are rewritten from scratch; `cmd_checkout()` is simplified by removing the indirection through `checkout_main()`. The changes are mechanical and preserve CLI behavior, but the restructuring is significant: the three commands now stand on equal footing, each calling the appropriate backend (`checkout_branch()` or `checkout_paths()`) directly.

This patch touches only `builtin/checkout.c`, moving roughly 150 lines of logic and eliminating 150 lines of indirection. The test suite passes, and the refactoring aligns with the architectural direction established in the parent thread. The patch is part of an early draft series explicitly not intended for merging to `seen` due to potential conflicts with in-flight topics, but it is a concrete demonstration of the libification goal: reusable logic is extracted into standalone helpers, and built-in commands call these helpers directly. The remaining patches in the series will continue this work by moving additional helpers to top-level files.
