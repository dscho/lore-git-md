This is a review comment from Junio C Hamano on the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-patch series adding formatting options to `git format-rev`. The patch itself is a pure refactoring that groups option variables into a new `struct format_rev_data` to reduce local variables in preparation for the three new options (`--abbrev`, `--color`, `--date`) that will be added in patch 4/4.

Junio’s feedback is surface-level and mechanical: he suggests using a designated initializer (`{ .notes = STRING_LIST_INIT_NODUP }`) instead of positional initialization for the new struct. This is a style nit, not a functional concern, and the rest of the patch is explicitly approved as "good." The change is trivial and will not affect the patch’s behavior or its role in the series. No substantive discussion or objections are raised; the patch remains uncontroversial and likely to be accepted with this minor adjustment.
