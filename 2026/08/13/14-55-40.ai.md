This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The series is queued in `next` and addresses a well-documented truncation risk on platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`.

This patch widens the `bufsize` parameter of `create_delta_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The change is mechanical and confined to the function signature in `delta.h` and its implementation in `diff-delta.c`. The sole caller, `try_delta()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, already passes an `unsigned long` value, which promotes safely to `size_t`, so no caller adjustments are needed. The patch is split out to keep the subsequent widening of `diff_delta()` and `create_delta()` (which ripple to multiple callers) in a separate commit, maintaining the series’ incremental structure.

The commit message is clear and self-contained, explaining the rationale and the lack of caller impact. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in Git’s delta/pack subsystems. No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced, and the patch is purely preparatory for later type-consistent widenings in the series.

Key symbols touched: `create_delta_index()` (parameter `bufsize`). The patch is low-risk and part of a well-reviewed, type-consistent series that has already addressed all prior feedback. It is ready for integration and should be of interest to anyone tracking platform compatibility or the ongoing `unsigned long` -> `size_t` conversion effort.
