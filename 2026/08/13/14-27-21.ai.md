This is a **refactoring** discussion in the ongoing debate over Patrick Steinhardt’s RFC series to reorganize Git’s source tree. Junio C Hamano’s reply directly challenges Johannes Schindelin’s incrementalist defense of the `lib/` directory reorganization, offering a **concrete alternative**: a subsystem-by-subsystem approach that moves *specific* components (e.g., `refs/`, `odb/`) to *specific* directories, rather than a sweeping mechanical move to an arbitrary `lib/` layer.

Junio’s core argument is structural: the `lib/` directory is an **intermediate layer without clear semantics**, and its lack of focus undermines the goal of improving discoverability. He contrasts it with past successful moves (e.g., `builtin/`, which has a clear criterion: top-level command implementations) and argues that a **piecemeal, concept-driven** approach would yield better organization *and* avoid the disruption of a mass rename. His proposal—move one subsystem at a time, with a well-defined scope and name—addresses Schindelin’s "naming is hard" objection by making each step self-contained and reviewable. The implication is that the current patch’s `lib/` directory is a **premature abstraction** that will require further reorganization, forcing contributors to re-learn the structure later.

The reply also **rebuts Schindelin’s "start somewhere" framing** by flipping it: the current patch is *not* a minimal first step but a **maximalist move** that preempts future decisions. Junio’s alternative preserves the ability to iterate, allowing the community to refine the structure incrementally without committing to a flawed intermediate layer. This aligns with his broader maintainer philosophy of favoring **motivated, incremental changes** over sweeping mechanical moves, even if the latter are framed as "cleanup."

Key technical details for follow-up:
- **Junio’s alternative**: Move subsystems (e.g., `refs/`, `odb/`) to *specific* directories when they undergo major changes, rather than dumping everything into `lib/`.

- **Disruption tradeoff**: Junio implies the current patch’s mass rename is *more* disruptive than a piecemeal approach, as it forces contributors to adapt to an arbitrary structure that may later change.
