This thread is about a test-only patch by Nikolaus Schuetz that adds coverage in `t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh` for characters already documented as forbidden in refnames but not explicitly tested in embedded form (e.g., space, `~`, `^`, `:`, `*`, `[`, `".."`, and backslash). The patch also verifies that the single-character `@` refname is rejected even with `--allow-onelevel`.

The patch has converged after two iterations, with substantive feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano incorporated into v2. Both reviewers endorsed the updated version, which replaced repetitive test checks with a more maintainable loop over forbidden characters. The patch is purely additive and does not change production code or on-disk formats.

The only remaining open question, raised by Junio in the latest email, is whether the test coverage is exhaustive—specifically, whether there are characters other than dot that are forbidden at the beginning or end of refname components (e.g., `"refs/heads/foo."` or `"foo.lock"`). This is a narrow technical point about completeness, not a flaw in the patch itself. The patch is otherwise ready for integration, pending this clarification. No objections or further feedback are expected.
