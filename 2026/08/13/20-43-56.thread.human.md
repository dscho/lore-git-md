This thread is about a small but useful test-only patch by Nikolaus Schuetz that adds coverage in `t1402-check-ref-format.sh` for characters already documented as forbidden in Git refnames—space, tilde, caret, colon, asterisk, and open-bracket—but not explicitly tested in embedded form. It also verifies that the single-character `@` refname is correctly rejected even with `--allow-onelevel`.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch and broadly approved its goal, confirming that the documented rules should indeed be enforced. He suggested a minor refactoring to reduce repetition by using a loop over the forbidden characters instead of listing each case individually. He also pointed out a small inconsistency in the test fixture (using `heads/` instead of `refs/heads/`), though he didn’t consider it a blocker. The `@` edge case was singled out as a particularly valuable addition. No deeper technical concerns were raised, and the patch remains purely additive, touching only the test file. The next step is likely a v2 incorporating Patrick’s feedback.
