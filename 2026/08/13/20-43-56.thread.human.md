This thread is about a small but useful test-only patch from Nikolaus Schuetz that adds coverage in `t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh` for characters already documented as forbidden in refnames—space, tilde, caret, colon, asterisk, and open-bracket—but not explicitly tested in embedded form. It also verifies that the single-character `@` refname is rejected even with `--allow-onelevel`.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch substantively, supporting its goal and highlighting the `@` edge case as particularly valuable. He suggested a minor refactoring to loop over forbidden characters for better test readability and noted a minor inconsistency in test fixtures (`heads/` vs `refs/heads/`), though neither was treated as a blocker. Junio C Hamano has now joined the discussion, endorsing Patrick’s review and expanding on the refactoring idea to include multi-byte sequences like `".."`. The patch remains under review, with no objections or deeper technical concerns raised so far. The only open question is whether to adopt the suggested loop refactoring before merging.
