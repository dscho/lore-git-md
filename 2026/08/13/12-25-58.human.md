This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object stores. The patch itself is a straightforward cleanup: it removes the now-redundant `loaded_alternates` field from the ODB structure after the previous patch (2/4) centralized alternate initialization in `odb_new()`.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial. The patch deletes nine lines and touches only `odb.c` and `odb.h`, removing the field and all conditional checks that relied on it. Karthik Nayak’s review confirms the removal makes sense, and no one has raised objections or identified edge cases. This is a textbook cleanup patch—no behavior changes, just reduced state complexity.

The series as a whole is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, and this patch is a direct follow-up to the architectural shift in patch 2. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction effort, this is a minor but necessary step toward simplifying the codebase. For everyone else, it’s safe to ignore unless you’re debugging deep ODB internals.
