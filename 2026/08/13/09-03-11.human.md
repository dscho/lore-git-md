This is a thread follow-up in the now-closed fsmonitor macOS bugfix discussion. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Tamir Duberstein’s withdrawal of the patch with a technical observation about the difference between the proposed fix and the precedent from Watchman. The key point: Tamir’s patch used `FSEventStreamFlushAsync()`, while Watchman’s fix used the synchronous `FSEventStreamFlushSync()`. Patrick suggests this difference could explain why Watchman’s fix worked (it guarantees the cookie is processed before proceeding) while Tamir’s did not (it still races with the timeout). He expresses concern about adopting the synchronous variant without deeper understanding of potential side effects, but does not propose a path forward. The thread remains closed unless Tamir resubmits with new evidence.
