This is the fifth patch in the v5 series introducing `git repack --drop-filtered` for partial clones. It wires up the actual object removal: when `--drop-filtered` is given without `--dry-run`, the patch passes the precomputed `to_drop` oidset to `repack_promisor_objects()`, which rebuilds the promisor pack while excluding those blobs. The patch also implies `-d` (delete redundant packs) to ensure the old packs containing the dropped blobs are removed, reclaiming disk space. The repack machinery’s existing write-before-delete and fsync guarantees ensure crash safety.

The documentation update in this patch explains the core functionality: `--drop-filtered` deletes local objects matching the `--filter` specification (currently limited to `blob:limit`), requires a promisor remote, implies `-d`, and is incompatible with `--filter-to` and bitmaps. The test script verifies that a large promisor blob is absent after `--drop-filtered` runs, while a smaller one remains.

This patch is the technical heart of the feature, turning the enumeration logic from earlier patches into real disk-space reclamation. The implementation is minimal and backward-compatible: existing callers of `repack_promisor_objects()` continue to pass `NULL` for the new `to_drop` parameter, so their behavior is unchanged. The series is now functionally complete, with all feedback from v4 addressed and no known blocking issues.
