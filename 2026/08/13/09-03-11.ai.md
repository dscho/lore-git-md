This is a follow-up in the now-closed macOS fsmonitor performance regression thread. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Tamir Duberstein’s withdrawal of the patch with a technical post-mortem.

He notes that the proposed fix used `FSEventStreamFlushAsync()`, whereas Watchman (the precedent Tamir cited) uses the synchronous `FSEventStreamFlushSync()`. The synchronous call guarantees that the cookie event is processed before the call returns, eliminating the race with the timeout. The asynchronous variant, by contrast, cannot provide that guarantee, which may explain why Tamir’s empirical observations were inconsistent. Patrick expresses concern that switching to the synchronous variant could introduce new, poorly understood problems, but does not propose an alternative fix. The thread remains closed with no code changes.
