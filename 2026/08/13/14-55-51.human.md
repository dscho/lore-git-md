The change aligns the function with the rest of the series, eliminating the last remaining truncation risk in the packfile header parsing code on platforms like 64-bit Windows where `unsigned long` is 32-bit.

In `packfile.c`, the function’s signature and internal variables (`used`, `size`, `c`) are updated to use `size_t`, ensuring consistency with the widened `sizep` output parameter. Callers in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and the fuzzer are adjusted to match, with no behavior changes.
