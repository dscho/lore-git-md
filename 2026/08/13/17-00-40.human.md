This is a surface-level review comment on the fourth patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-patch series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. The series aims to improve usability by making color swatches clickable, standardizing button widths, rewriting labels in more natural language, and ensuring full translatability. This specific patch updates seven UI labels to be more descriptive, replacing technical jargon like “Marked line bg” with “Marked line background.”

Mark Levedahl points out that the label “Marked line background” is still misleading, as it controls the background color of *commit message text* found by search, not lines in the patch text or arbitrary lines of text. He suggests deferring this correction to a future effort that would expand search highlighting to cover all matches, not just those in commit messages. His feedback is purely about label accuracy and does not question the patch’s technical correctness or readiness. The discussion remains cosmetic and does not block the current series.
