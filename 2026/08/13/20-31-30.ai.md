This is Junio C Hamano’s reply to Christian Couder’s v2 series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`. The email is not a technical review but a procedural critique of the patch series’ email threading. Junio reports that the v2 series is misthreaded: while the cover letter correctly replies to the v1 cover letter, each individual patch (1/5 through 5/5) also replies to the v1 cover letter instead of to the v2 cover letter. This breaks the expected thread structure where all patches in a series should be replies to their own cover letter. Junio explains that neither `git send-email` nor GitGitGadget normally produces this error and asks whether Christian used custom settings. He provides a step-by-step example of how to format and send a correctly threaded series using `git format-patch` and `git send-email`, emphasizing that the cover letter of iteration N should reply to the cover letter of iteration 1, and every patch in iteration N should reply to the cover letter of iteration N. The email is purely about patch submission mechanics, not about the feature itself.
