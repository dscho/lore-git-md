Core problem or goal
Add test coverage in `t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh` for characters already documented as forbidden in refnames but not explicitly tested in embedded form (space, `~`, `^`, `:`, `*`, `[`, `".."`, backslash) and verify that the single-character `@` refname is rejected even when `--allow-onelevel` is passed. Junio C Hamano has raised a follow-up question about whether the test suite now exhaustively covers all forbidden characters at the start or end of refname components (e.g., `"refs/heads/foo."` or `"foo.lock"`), probing completeness rather than correctness.

Current status
Under review; second version posted and converged. The update adopts the refactoring suggested by Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano: repetitive `invalid_ref` checks are replaced with a loop over forbidden characters (including multi-byte sequences like `".."` and backslash). The patch remains purely additive and test-only. Both reviewers have endorsed the v2 iteration; no objections remain. The patch is ready for integration pending clarification on the completeness question.
