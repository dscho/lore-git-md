Core problem or goal
Add test coverage in `t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh` for characters already documented as forbidden in refnames but not explicitly tested in embedded form: space, tilde (`~`), caret (`^`), colon (`:`), asterisk (`*`), open-bracket (`[`), and multi-byte sequences like `".."`. Also verify that the single-character `@` refname is rejected even when `--allow-onelevel` is passed.
