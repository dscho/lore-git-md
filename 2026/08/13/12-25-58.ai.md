This is a surface-level review of the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch v2 series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The patch itself is a mechanical cleanup: it removes the now-unused `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database` after the preceding patch (2/4) centralized alternate initialization in `odb_new()`.

Karthik Nayak’s review is brief and confirms the change is sensible. He notes he had independently considered removing the field while reviewing the previous patch, and his feedback is limited to expressing approval ("The patch looks good") without engaging with deeper technical implications or edge cases. The review does not address the series’ broader goals, performance trade-offs, or potential risks of eager alternate loading—topics that remain open in the thread.

The patch touches only `odb.c` and `odb.h`, removing 9 lines and adding 1. It is uncontroversial and follows directly from the architectural shift in patch 2/4.
