This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-patch series extending `git format-rev` with `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` formatting options. The patch is pure documentation refactoring: it extracts the 55-line block describing `--date` alternatives from `rev-list-options.adoc` into a new, standalone file (`rev-list-option-date-alternatives.adoc`) so that the same text can be included by both `git-rev-list` and the upcoming `git-format-rev` man page. The change is mechanical and does not alter any user-visible behavior; it simply prepares the ground for the feature patch that follows. No review feedback has appeared yet, but the patch is uncontroversial and likely to be accepted as-is.
