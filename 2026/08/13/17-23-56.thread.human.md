This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-patch series (now at v2) adding `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` formatting options to `git format-rev`, a command introduced in Git 2.45. The goal is to bring `git format-rev` closer to `git log`’s formatting flexibility by reusing its existing logic for consistency. The series has addressed all feedback from its first version—fixing a memory leak, improving defensive code, and adding refactoring patches for better documentation reuse and assertion placement.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, recently noted that the v2 series (posted on 2026-08-26) has received no review feedback despite being uncontroversial and ready for further input. He clarified that he isn’t the best reviewer for this experimental command and encouraged contributors more familiar with `git format-rev` to weigh in. The series remains under review, queued as `kh/format-rev-more-options`, but hasn’t been picked up in any integration branch yet. No objections or open questions have surfaced, but broader review would help confirm the design choices, particularly around the `--date` option’s alternatives and the command’s synopsis simplification.
