This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows. This specific patch widens the delta-cache accounting variables (`delta_cache_size` and `max_delta_cache_size`) and the `delta_cacheable()` helper function to `size_t`, addressing a latent 32-bit truncation risk on platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`.

The changes are confined to `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `config.c`, `config.h`, `parse.c`, and `parse.h`. The patch introduces two new helpers, `git_config_size_t()` and `git_parse_size_t()`, to handle the now-wider `max_delta_cache_size` configuration variable, ensuring type consistency across the delta-cache accounting logic. The `delta_cacheable()` function’s parameters are also widened to `size_t`, matching the type of its inputs (e.g., `DELTA_SIZE()` returns `size_t`). This change is internally consistent and prepares the ground for removing `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shims in later patches, once `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` are similarly widened.

The patch is mechanical and low-risk, with no behavior or on-disk format changes. It is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in Git’s delta and pack-objects subsystems, aligning with the series’ goal of preventing integer truncation on 64-bit Windows. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and the preparatory nature of the work, though it assumes familiarity with the series’ context. No new tests are added, as the changes are purely internal and do not alter observable behavior.
