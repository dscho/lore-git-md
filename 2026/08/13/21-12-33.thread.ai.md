Core problem or goal
Add missing test coverage for `git symbolic-ref`'s exit codes and output behavior when querying a non-symbolic ref, both with and without the `--quiet` (`-q`) flag. The patch now also verifies that stdout is empty in both cases, addressing a review suggestion. The discussion has clarified the technical rationale behind the differing exit codes (128 vs. 1 with `--quiet`) but remains open on whether this design is intentional or a quirk worth revisiting.
