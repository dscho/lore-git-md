This is the third iteration of Johannes Schindelin’s platform-compatibility series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series now consists of 13 patches (up from 12 in v2) and addresses the two truncation risks Junio C Hamano flagged in his review of v2: `zlib_buf_cap()` and `unpack_object_header_buffer()` are now widened to `size_t`, closing the last type-consistency gaps.

The changes remain mechanical and confined to type widening; no behavior or on-disk format is altered. Files touched span the delta, pack-objects, zlib, and HTTP-push subsystems, plus test helpers. New symbols introduced in this version are `zlib_buf_cap(size_t)` and `unpack_object_header_buffer(size_t)`, completing the type chain. The series is still based on `f85a7e662054` and is queued in `next` for integration testing.

Johannes has incorporated all prior feedback: the `sizeof_delta_index()` return type, `git_parse_size_t()`/`git_config_size_t()` helpers, and the `git_deflate_bound()` fallback formula are unchanged from v2. The two new patches (13/13 and the widened `zlib_buf_cap()` in 7/13) directly resolve Junio’s concerns about truncation before comparison with `ZLIB_BUF_MAX` and the `oe_get_size_slow()` call site. The series is now fully type-consistent and ready for final review before graduation to `master`.

This is a well-scoped, uncontroversial fix targeting 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and other platforms where pointer width exceeds `unsigned long`. The patch count reflects the breadth of the delta machinery rather than complexity; each change is small and focused. Windows users and anyone working with large objects should find this series particularly relevant.
