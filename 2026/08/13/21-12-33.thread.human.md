This thread is about a small but useful test-coverage patch for `git symbolic-ref`. Nikolaus Schuetz submitted a patch adding tests for the command's exit codes and output when querying a non-symbolic ref, both with and without the `--quiet` flag. The behaviors being tested are documented but were previously untested.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch and raised two substantive points. First, he asked why the patch was written—whether it was driven by a specific issue or just opportunistic coverage. More importantly, he questioned the design rationale behind the differing exit codes (128 without `--quiet`, 1 with `--quiet`), calling the distinction "weird" and suggesting that `--quiet` should only affect output, not exit status. He also proposed an additional test to verify that stdout is empty in both cases. The discussion is now open on whether the current behavior is intentional or a quirk worth revisiting.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and touches only the test script `t/t1401-symbolic-ref.sh`. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, and the thread is still in early review. The next step will likely be a response from the author addressing Patrick's questions, either justifying the existing behavior or proposing a follow-up change to align the exit codes.
