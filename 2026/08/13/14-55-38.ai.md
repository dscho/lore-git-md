The changes remain mechanical and confined to type widening, with no behavior or on-disk format changes. The series touches 19 files, including `diff-delta.c`, `pack-objects.c`, `git-zlib.c`, `archive-zip.c`, `http-push.c`, `builtin/fast-import.c`, and test helpers. New in v3 are the widening of `zlib_buf_cap()` (previously deferred) and `unpack_object_header_buffer()`, both of which now use `size_t` to match the widened callers introduced earlier in the series. The commit messages are clear and self-contained, and the range-diff shows that the new patches (13/13) are additive rather than disruptive.

This iteration is now fully type-consistent across the delta and pack-objects subsystems on platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`, such as 64-bit Windows. The motivation—preventing integer truncation on 64-bit Windows—is well-documented and aligns with the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in Git.
