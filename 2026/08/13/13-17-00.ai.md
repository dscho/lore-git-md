This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing ODB refactoring series to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The email exchanges a minor code-style suggestion about how to initialize `o->source_paths_icase` in the new `odb_source_paths_cmp()` function introduced in patch 1/4.

Karthik Nayak proposed simplifying the conditional block that sets `o->source_paths_icase` by directly assigning the result of `repo_config_get_bool()`. Patrick Steinhardt explains why that wouldn’t work: if the configuration isn’t set, the field would remain `-1`, causing repeated config lookups. He offers an alternative one-liner but prefers the original version for readability. The discussion is purely stylistic and does not affect the patch’s correctness or the series’ broader goal of decoupling path comparisons from `the_repository`. No further changes are expected; the patch remains ready for integration.
