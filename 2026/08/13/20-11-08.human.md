This is a minor follow-up in the ongoing gitk color-preference dialog overhaul series. The discussion centers on the wording of a single label—"Marked line background"—which controls the background color of lines revealed by the "Show origin of this line" feature in the diff panel. Johannes Sixt previously clarified that the label applies to these origin lines, not search-highlighted commit messages as Mark Levedahl initially thought.

Mark now suggests tweaking the label to include the word "origin" (e.g., "Origin line background") to better reflect its function, but he acknowledges this is a cosmetic detail and proposes leaving it as-is unless someone objects. He also notes that a separate highlight color (`foundbgcolor`) for search results in commit messages exists in the config file but isn’t exposed in the dialog, and he recommends deferring any changes to that until there’s user demand. The exchange remains purely about presentation, with no impact on the patch’s technical correctness or readiness. The series as a whole is otherwise uncontroversial and addresses a clear usability gap in gitk’s UI.
