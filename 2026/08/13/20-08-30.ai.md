This is the final patch (6/6) in the v5 series introducing `git repack --drop-filtered` for partial clones. It adds two safety guards to prevent `--drop-filtered` from running during ongoing operations or dropping blobs referenced by the current index, both skipped for bare repositories.

The patch implements the checks using `wt_status_get_state()` to detect merge, rebase, am, cherry-pick, revert, or bisect in progress, and `repo_read_index()` to validate the index. Error messages are specific and now list all blocked operations explicitly, addressing Christian Couder’s feedback from v4. The documentation in `git-repack.adoc` has been updated to explain these guards as UX optimizations (avoiding surprising network fetches mid-operation or immediate re-fetching of dropped blobs).

The test script (`t/t7706-repack-drop-filtered.sh`) covers both scenarios: a merge-in-progress refusal and an index-referenced blob refusal. The implementation is clean, with the checks logically grouped and clearly commented. This patch completes the series, which is now technically sound and ready for maintainer review after addressing all feedback from earlier iterations.
