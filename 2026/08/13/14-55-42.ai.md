This is the fourth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows. The series is now fully type-consistent and queued in `next`.

This patch widens the return type of `free_unpacked()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to match the widened `SIZE(n->entry)` (previously addressed in an earlier topic). The change is mechanical and confined to a single line in `builtin/pack-objects.c`. The caller, `find_deltas()`, still uses an `unsigned long` for its `mem_usage` counter, so truncation persists there until later patches in the series address it. The commit message clearly documents the truncation risk and the rationale for the widening, aligning with the series’ broader goal of eliminating incorrect `unsigned long` usage in the delta/pack subsystems.

No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced, and the patch is uncontroversial. It is a straightforward correctness fix that resolves a silent truncation issue on platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`. The patch is part of the ongoing effort to ensure type consistency in Git’s size-related fields, particularly for large objects.
