This is the final patch in a three-part bugfix series targeting test infrastructure. It adds a concise but thorough documentation block to `t/lib-httpd.sh` that explains why CGI helpers must use atomic operations when managing shared state, describes the two atomic idioms (`mkdir` for first-request election and `rm` without `-f` for one-shot markers) that the series just introduced, and warns against the anti-pattern of a non-atomic “test-then-act” sequence. The note is placed right where the helpers are installed, so anyone adding a new script will see it immediately.

The patch is a direct response to Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback on v2: he asked for the documentation to be moved from `t/README` to `t/lib-httpd.sh` so it would be contextually visible, and this version does exactly that. The text also reflects the v3 change in patch 1, now mentioning `rm` (without `-f`) as the atomic alternative to `mv` for consuming one-shot markers. The result is a small, self-contained update that closes the series with clear guidance for future contributors.
