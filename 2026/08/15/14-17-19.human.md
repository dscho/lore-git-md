This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing **performance bug report** about Git's bash completion being slow in large repositories.

D. Ben Knoble replies to Matthew Hughes' report with a suggestion: try enabling Git's `feature.manyFiles` configuration. This setting activates optimizations like the filesystem monitor (fsmonitor), which could reduce the need for expensive directory scans during completion. The suggestion is a reasonable first step for users experiencing this slowdown, though it doesn’t address the root cause in the completion script itself. No further technical discussion or patch review is included in this email.
