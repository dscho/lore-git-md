This is Junio’s "What’s cooking" report for mid-August 2026, summarizing the state of 40+ in-flight topics across `seen`, `next`, and `master`. The report is dense but structured: new topics at the top, stalled ones next, then actively cooking topics, and finally discarded ones.

Key technical details for follow-up:
- **Integration branches**: `master` (424 commits since Git 2.55), `next` (82 commits), `seen` (286 commits).
- **Notable new topics**:
  - `kh/format-rev-more-options` extends the experimental `git format-rev` with `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date`.
  - `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation` makes packfile generation pluggable via ODB callbacks, removing hardcoded `pack-objects` references.
  - `ty/repository-fetch-if-missing` moves the global `fetch_if_missing` into `struct repository`, enabling per-repo control.
  - `ps/odb-eagerly-load-alternates` eagerly initializes alternates at ODB startup, eliminating scattered lazy-loading calls.
  - `ss/repack-drop-filtered` adds `--drop-filtered` to `git repack` to delete local promisor blobs exceeding a `blob:limit=` filter.
  - `dk/use-nsec-runtime` converts the build-time `USE_NSEC` knob to a runtime `core.useNanosec` config.
  - `mm/diff-process-hunks` introduces `diff.<driver>.process` to let external tools act as hunk providers while leaving output formatting to Git.
  - `hn/history-squash` adds a `git history squash` subcommand to fold a range of commits into one, replaying descendants on top.

- **Stalled topics**: `bl/t7412-use-test-path-helpers` (test modernization), `tb/repack-geometric-cruft` (geometric repack + cruft packs), `hn/checkout-track-fetch` (fetch-on-checkout), and `za/completion-hide-dotfiles` (dotfile completion) have been waiting for responses for 6+ weeks.
- **Controversial or complex topics**:
  - `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation` and `jt/receive-pack-pluggable-writes` are both ODB-plumbing efforts with open design questions (Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler are the primary reviewers).
  - `mm/diff-process-hunks` is stalled due to questions about the hunk-provider interface and backward compatibility.
  - `tb/pack-with-duplicates` (duplicate pack entries) and `tc/replay-linearize` (`git replay --linearize`) have unresolved edge cases.
- **Documentation efforts**: `kh/doc-trailers` (trailer format clarification) and `kh/doc-refs-migrate-limitations` (ref migration warnings) are both in `next` and ready for `master`.
- **Performance**: `js/packfile-fast-append` fixes a quadratic regression in packfile appends, and `js/pack-objects-delta-size-t` widens delta-related fields to `size_t` to avoid truncation on 64-bit Windows.
- **Security**: `gg/http-ssl-verify-status` adds OCSP stapling support via `http.sslVerifyStatus`.
- **Test flakiness**: `ps/t7900-deflake-maintenance` disables auto-detaching maintenance tasks in tests to avoid races.

The report also highlights several topics marked for reroll (e.g., `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation`, `ss/repack-drop-filtered`) and a few that Junio intends to discard (e.g., `hn/checkout-track-fetch`). The `Discarded` section lists two retracted topics (`td/fsmonitor-darwin-cookie-flush`, `tb/send-pack-no-ref-delta`).
