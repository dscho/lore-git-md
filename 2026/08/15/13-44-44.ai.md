This is a **substantive review** of the eighth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch MinGW/Windows build and runtime adjustment series. The reviewer, Johannes Sixt, identifies a **logical ordering issue**: the CMake and Meson build-system changes in this patch introduce compiler definitions (`ENSURE_MSYSTEM_IS_SET` and `MINGW_PREFIX`) that are only consumed by the C code in the **twelfth patch** of the series. He argues these changes belong in the final patch, not here, because until then the definitions are unused and could confuse readers or tools.

The review is narrowly scoped to the patch’s internal structure and does not challenge the technical correctness or motivation of the series. It is a **cleanup suggestion** rather than a correctness concern, and the feedback is framed as a matter of clarity and maintainability rather than a blocking issue. The patch remains well-motivated and uncontroversial in its goal of replacing hard-coded MSYS2 metadata with environment-variable-driven logic. If the author accepts the suggestion, the only change would be moving the CMake and Meson hunks to the final patch; no functional behavior would be affected.
