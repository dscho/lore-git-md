This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch in question is the first of a two-part v5 series that moves the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository`, making it a per-repository flag. Junio’s review of the v5 series had already confirmed the mechanical completeness of the changes, but he pointed out a design inelegance in the `pack-objects.c` code: the `--missing` option’s callback deferred setting `repo->fetch_if_missing` until after `parse_options()` returned, using an intermediate variable (`arg_missing_action`), when it could have used the `value` field in `OPT_CALLBACK_F()` to pass the repository address directly.

In this reply, Tian Yuchen acknowledges the oversight and agrees to revise the patch to use the `value` field, eliminating the need for the intermediate variable. The exchange is purely about code clarity—no new technical issues are raised, and the series remains on track for eventual queuing. The broader architectural question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` is still unresolved, but this change will make the implementation cleaner regardless. If you’ve been following the series, this is a minor but welcome refinement; if not, the patch is still worth watching as part of the larger libification and global-state removal effort.
