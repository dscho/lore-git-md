This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-patch documentation series (v2) that improves the discoverability of the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page. The series as a whole is a well-scoped, uncontroversial effort to ensure users can actually find the existing data-model tutorial, which is currently unlinked from any other documentation.

This specific patch adds cross-references from four key glossary terms—**blob**, **commit**, **object**, and **tree**—to their corresponding sections in `gitdatamodel(7)`. It also adds `gitdatamodel(7)` to the “See Also” list in `gitglossary(7)`. The changes are purely additive and touch only `Documentation/gitglossary.adoc` and `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc`. The author explains that the phrasing of each link is tailored to the relationship between the glossary entry and the tutorial: some are simple “see also” pointers, while others (like the **index** entry) direct readers to “details” in the data-model page. The patch itself is unchanged from v1; only the cover letter and commit messages were tweaked in response to surface-level feedback.

The series has already been tested by merging with the current `seen` branch and running the full test suite, with no regressions reported. The feedback so far has been limited to minor wording suggestions in commit messages, and the patches themselves appear ready for integration. This patch is a logical piece of the larger goal and fits cleanly into the series’ overall design.
