This is a brief but consequential reply from Junio C Hamano in the ongoing discussion about Unicode box-drawing characters in `git repo structure`. The maintainer clarifies that his earlier skepticism was not a blanket rejection of the feature but a specific objection to the *implementation*: the current patch series makes Unicode rendering dependent solely on locale detection, with no mechanism for users to opt out if their terminal or font renders the characters poorly. Junio’s concern is practical — he wants the feature to be configurable, not mandatory for UTF-8 users — and his tone suggests this is a solvable issue rather than a fundamental blocker. The door is now open for the author to add a configuration knob (e.g., `repo.structure.unicode`) to address the objection, which would likely move the series closer to acceptance. No new technical arguments are introduced; this is a clarification of intent.
