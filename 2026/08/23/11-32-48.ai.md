This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-patch documentation series (v2) that improves discoverability of the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page. The patch adds cross-references from four glossary terms (**blob**, **commit**, **object**, and **tree** are not directly mentioned in the diff, but the terms **object**, **index**, **ref/reference**, and **reflog** are updated) to their corresponding sections in `gitdatamodel(7)`. It also adds `gitdatamodel(7)` to the "See Also" list in `gitglossary(7)`.

The changes are purely additive and limited to documentation files (`Documentation/gitglossary.adoc` and `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc`). Each glossary entry now includes a pointer to the tutorial material in `gitdatamodel(7)`, with phrasing adjusted to reflect the relative depth of coverage between the glossary and the data model page. For example, the **index** entry now directs readers to "THE INDEX" in `gitdatamodel(7)` for details, while the **ref/reference** entry uses a "see also" link since the glossary already covers the topic in depth. The **reflog** entry links to both `gitdatamodel(7)` (for a short explanation of the format) and `git-reflog(1)` (for full details).

The patch itself is unchanged from v1, and the author’s notes clarify the rationale behind the phrasing choices, particularly for the **ref/reference** entry. The series remains uncontroversial, well-scoped, and ready for integration. No new tests are needed, as this is documentation-only and the full test suite has already been run to confirm no regressions. The patch directly advances the series’ goal of making `gitdatamodel(7)` more visible to users.
