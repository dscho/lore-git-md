This is a reply in the ongoing discussion about adding Unicode box-drawing characters to `git repo structure`. Oswald Buddenhagen pushes back against Junio C Hamano’s skepticism about cross-terminal rendering inconsistencies, arguing that Unicode box-drawing characters are already widely and reliably used in popular terminal applications like `mutt` and `midnight commander`. He suggests that if compatibility with broken terminals or fonts is a concern, the feature could be made opt-out via a configuration option rather than rejected outright. As an aside, he floats the idea of extending the same Unicode treatment to `git log --graph`, hinting at broader potential for visual improvements in Git’s output. The tone is pragmatic and challenges the maintainer’s objection without dismissing it entirely.
