This is Junio C Hamano's follow-up to his own substantive review of the first patch in Elijah Newren’s v2 series (1/3), which rewords error messages to clarify that restrictions on `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` apply specifically when resolving a commit that became empty during rebase. Junio now acknowledges that his earlier confusion about the reliability of the `whence == FROM_REBASE_PICK` logic was due to the misleading name of the `FROM_REBASE_PICK` enum value. He confirms that `sequencer_determine_whence()` only sets `FROM_REBASE_PICK` when the rebase-head and cherry-pick-head are identical, which by definition indicates an empty-pick situation. This resolves his technical objection to the patch’s core logic, but he still finds the error message phrasing ("while resolving a commit that became empty") awkward and overly verbose. To improve clarity, he proposes two mechanical changes: replacing direct `whence == FROM_REBASE_PICK` checks with calls to `is_from_rebase(whence)` (except in the function itself), and renaming `FROM_REBASE_PICK` and `is_from_rebase()` to include the word "empty" (e.g., `FROM_REBASE_EMPTY_PICK` and `is_from_rebase_empty()`). These changes are surface-level but would make the code’s intent clearer and prevent future confusion. The patch remains a bugfix targeting the merge machinery, and the series’ goal—preventing unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution—is now on firmer technical ground. However, the error message phrasing may still require revision before integration.
