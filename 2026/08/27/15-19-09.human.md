This is a minor but meaningful documentation improvement in Elijah Newren’s three-patch bugfix series, which aims to prevent unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. The patch rewords two error messages in `builtin/commit.c` to clarify that the restriction on `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` applies specifically when resolving a commit that became empty during rebase, not during rebase in general. The updated messages now read:

- *"cannot do a partial commit while resolving a commit that became empty."*
- *"You are resolving a commit that became empty -- cannot amend."*

Phillip Wood, in his review, suggests a slightly clearer phrasing (*"while committing a commit that became empty"*), but acknowledges that the current wording is already a significant improvement over the original. The patch also updates the corresponding test expectations in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh` to match the new messages.

This change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ broader goal of improving user-facing clarity. It lays the groundwork for the subsequent patches, which extend protection to additional commands (`git am`, `git revert`) and operations (`git commit <paths>`). The series as a whole is under final review and appears ready for integration, with no open questions or objections. If you’re tracking the merge machinery or conflict-resolution safety, this is a small but worthwhile refinement.
