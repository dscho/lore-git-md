This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, but Junio’s review raises two sharp questions that the original submission did not address.

The patch mechanically replaces `the_repository` with the already-passed-but-unused `struct repository *repo` parameter in five built-in commands (`blame`, `gc`, `repo`, `reset`) and in `transport.c`. The diff is tiny (ten lines across five files) and the commit message is clear about leaving harder cases for later.

Junio’s reply, however, points out that the commit message does not explain how the author verified that the touched sites are safe when `repo` is NULL, nor does it show any handling of that edge case. The review is substantive: it asks for evidence of due diligence rather than just mechanical substitution. Until those questions are answered, the patch cannot be considered ready, even though the change itself is uncontroversial in principle.
