This is a small but user-facing feature patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of the `kh/format-patch-range-diff-notes` series that Junio’s "What’s cooking" report flagged as waiting for a response. The series adds a `--[no-]range-diff-notes` option to `git format-patch`, letting users control whether notes (e.g., `Signed-off-by`) are included in the range-diff portion of the output separately from the notes shown on the patches themselves.

The patch itself is the third in a three-commit series. The first two are mechanical cleanups: renaming a struct field to clarify its purpose and simplifying the parameter list of an internal helper. The feature commit then wires up the new option, updating the command-line parser, documentation, and test suite. The implementation is straightforward, and the tests cover both the default behavior (notes appear in both patch and range-diff) and the new `--no-range-diff-notes` variant.
