This is a **translation (l10n)** issue targeting the French documentation for `git checkout`. The author, Eric Gautier, reports a grammatical error in the French translation of the `git-checkout` man page. Specifically, he identifies an incorrect use of the "ne explétif" (a stylistic negation that does not invert the meaning) in the sentence:

> L’extraction échouera s’il n’y a des changements non validés dans les fichiers où <branche> et votre commit actuel ont un contenu différent.

Gautier argues that the "n'" here is misleading and should be removed, as it could be misinterpreted as a true negation ("if there are *no* uncommitted changes") rather than the intended meaning ("if there *are* uncommitted changes"). He suggests the corrected version:

> L’extraction échouera s’il y a des changements non validés dans les fichiers où <branche> et votre commit actuel ont un contenu différent.

The email is a clear, well-motivated report of a potential source of confusion for French-speaking users, particularly beginners. It does not include a patch but provides all the information needed for a French translator to address the issue. The tone is constructive and focused on clarity, and the author explicitly states the risk of misinterpretation.
