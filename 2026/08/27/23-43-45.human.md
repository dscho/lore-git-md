This is the second patch in Wesley Schwengle’s two-part series making `git-svn` quieter and optional. It adds a `NO_GIT_SVN=YesPlease` knob to the Makefile that completely skips building, installing, and testing `git-svn` when SVN interoperability is not needed. The patch is a straightforward build-system tweak: it wraps the `git-svn.perl` entry in an `ifndef NO_GIT_SVN` guard and automatically sets `NO_SVN_TESTS` when the knob is enabled. No new symbols or on-disk changes are introduced, and the change is uncontroversial—it simply extends the existing pattern of optional components. The series as a whole remains under review, but this patch is unlikely to attract pushback.
