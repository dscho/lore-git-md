This is the **final review** of the third patch in Elijah Newren’s **bugfix series** extending Git’s protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. The series blocks both `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` (partial commits) during conflict stops in `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`, closing a long-standing gap that could corrupt repository state.

Phillip Wood’s review here is **surface-level**: he briefly endorses the patch ("Good idea and the changes look good too") without engaging with the technical implementation or raising new concerns. This confirms that the patch meets his expectations after the substantive feedback he provided earlier in the thread. The patch itself is minimal and focused, reusing the `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` helper introduced in patch 2 to extend existing partial-commit checks in `builtin/commit.c`. The new tests in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`, `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, and `t4151-am-abort.sh` verify the behavior for all relevant operations, and the error messages are specific to each scenario (e.g., "cannot do a partial commit during an am session").

The series as a whole is **ready for integration**. Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood have both endorsed the approach, and all prior feedback has been addressed. The only remaining loose end is Phillip’s earlier **substantive maintainability concern** about hardcoded paths in `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` (not directly relevant to this patch), which Elijah is expected to address in a follow-up or v3. This patch, however, is uncontroversial and completes the series’ goal: preventing users from accidentally recording partial conflict resolutions, which would corrupt the repository by failing to capture the full tree state. The change aligns with existing protections for merges and cherry-picks and introduces no new CLI options or on-disk format changes.
