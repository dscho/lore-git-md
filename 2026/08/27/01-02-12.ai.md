This is the first patch in Elijah Newren’s **v2 bugfix series** (3/3) targeting the merge machinery to prevent unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. This patch rewords two error messages in `builtin/commit.c` to clarify that the restriction on `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` applies specifically when resolving a commit that became empty during rebase, not during rebase in general.

The change replaces generic phrasing like *"cannot do a partial commit during a rebase"* with more precise language: *"cannot do a partial commit while resolving a commit that became empty."* This avoids misleading users into thinking the restriction applies to all rebase stops (e.g., `edit` or `break` directives, where amending or partial commits are valid). The updated messages now reflect the actual hazard: empty commits require the entire tree state to be recorded, so partial commits or amending the wrong commit could corrupt repository state.

The patch touches `builtin/commit.c` and updates the corresponding test expectations in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`. The diff is minimal (4 lines changed) and purely textual, with no behavioral or structural changes. The commit message explains the motivation clearly, and the test adjustments ensure the new error messages are validated.

This patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of improving user-facing clarity while laying groundwork for the subsequent patches, which extend protection to additional commands (`git am`, `git revert`) and operations (`git commit <paths>`). The series as a whole is under final review and appears ready for integration.
