This thread is about a small but important correction to the French translation of the `git-checkout` man page. Eric Gautier originally reported a grammatical error where the stylistic negation ("ne explétif") was incorrectly used, potentially confusing French-speaking users by suggesting the opposite of the intended meaning—i.e., that the condition applies when there are *no* uncommitted changes, rather than when there *are* any.

Ben Knoble, a regular contributor, has now endorsed the report and directly escalated it to the French translation maintainers (Jean-Noël Avila and Jiang Xin) by CC’ing them. This moves the issue from a passive report to an active item for the translation team, making a prompt fix more likely. The correction itself is straightforward—removing the "n’" from "s’il n’y a"—and involves no code changes, only documentation. The next step is for the maintainers to review and apply the fix. No further discussion or clarification appears needed at this stage.
