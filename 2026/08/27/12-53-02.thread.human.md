This thread is about a **French translation issue** in the `git-checkout` man page. Eric Gautier, a French speaker, has identified a grammatical error in the French documentation that could confuse users. Specifically, he points out that the current translation incorrectly uses the "ne explétif" (a stylistic negation that doesn’t invert meaning) in a way that might mislead readers into thinking the condition is the opposite of what’s intended. The problematic sentence currently reads:

> L’extraction échouera s’il n’y a des changements non validés...

Gautier argues this could be misinterpreted as "if there are *no* uncommitted changes," when the actual meaning is "if there *are* uncommitted changes." He proposes removing the "n’" to clarify the sentence.

The report is clear and actionable, providing all the context needed for the French translation maintainer (likely Jean-Noël Avila or Jiang Xin) to address it. While no patch is included, the issue is well-motivated and focused on improving clarity for French-speaking users, particularly beginners. Given the constructive tone and the low risk of misinterpretation once fixed, this is likely to be resolved quickly.
