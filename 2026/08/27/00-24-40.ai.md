This is the **v2 submission** of Elijah Newren’s bugfix patch, addressing Junio C Hamano’s feedback on v1. The patch extends Git’s existing protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution to cover both `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`, preventing users from accidentally amending the wrong commit or updating individual paths during conflict resolution for `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`.

The patch introduces a new helper function, `ongoing_operation()`, in `builtin/commit.c` to consolidate logic for detecting active operations (merge, am, apply-based rebase, and sequencer-based rebase/revert) while preserving separation of concerns between merge and sequencer logic. This addresses Phillip Wood’s earlier suggestion to improve maintainability without fully adopting his proposed refactor into `sequencer_determine_whence()`. Junio’s feedback to replace `the_repository` with `s->repo` is incorporated for the amend site, though the partial commit callsite still uses `the_repository` due to lack of a readily available repository instance.

Test coverage is expanded to include `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`, `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, and `t4151-am-abort.sh`, ensuring the new protections are exercised across all affected commands. The patch does not introduce new CLI options or on-disk format changes, and it aligns with the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort where possible.

Elijah’s commit message retains the `#leftoverbits` marker for future work, though no specific follow-up issues are identified.
