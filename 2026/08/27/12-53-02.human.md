This is a **translation (l10n) patch** — specifically, a report of a grammatical error in the French version of the `git checkout` documentation.

Eric Gautier, a French speaker, points out that the current French translation of the `git checkout` man page contains a misplaced negative particle ("n'") that makes the sentence confusing. The phrase *"L’extraction échouera s’il n’y a des changements non validés..."* is unidiomatic in French and could be misread as the opposite of what is intended. He suggests removing the "n'" to clarify that the checkout fails *if there are* uncommitted changes in conflicting files, not *if there are not*.

The report is clear and well-reasoned, with a direct suggestion for how to fix the wording. Since this is a documentation-only issue in the French translation, it should be straightforward to address and unlikely to be controversial. The patch (when sent) will likely be routed to Jiang Xin or Jean-Noël Avila, who maintain the French translations and l10n infrastructure.
