This is a surface-level review of patch 8/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable for ODB backends. The patch moves bitmap verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the series’ pattern of relocating backend-specific checks into their respective source files.

Karthik Nayak’s review is purely editorial, pointing out a single spelling correction ("focussed" -> "focused") and stating that the changes "look good" without engaging with the patch’s technical content. There are no questions about correctness, edge cases, or design trade-offs, and no substantive feedback on the relocation of `verify_bitmap_files()`, the replacement of `ERROR_BITMAP` with `ERROR_OBJECT`, or the test updates verifying flag behavior.

The patch itself is well-scoped and mechanical, following the established pattern of earlier patches in the series. It removes 5 lines from `builtin/fsck.c`, adds 3 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, and updates 26 lines in `pack-bitmap.c` to adapt the function to operate on a single `odb_source_packed` instance. The test suite is extended to confirm that bitmap checks respect `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only`, ensuring behavioral parity with the old implementation. No user-visible changes or on-disk format modifications are introduced.

This review does not advance the discussion meaningfully, but the patch appears uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of preparing Git for pluggable ODB backends. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction effort, this is a routine step in the refactoring; if not, it’s unlikely to demand attention unless you work on fsck or bitmap internals.
