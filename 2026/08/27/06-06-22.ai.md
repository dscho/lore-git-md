This is the **substantive review** of the final patch in Elijah Newren’s four-patch bugfix series, which implements the core recovery logic for the geometric-repacking race condition. Jeff King (Peff) reviews the tri-state `fill_midx_entry()` design that he previously proposed and that Elijah adopted in v2.

Peff’s feedback is narrowly technical and focused on three points. First, he notes that changing the return semantics of `fill_midx_entry()` without renaming it risks confusing future callers, though he concedes the risk is low for such a specialized function. Second, he questions the continued need for the `stale_packs_detected` flag introduced in patch 3/4, implying that the tri-state design alone should be sufficient. Third, he flags a minor but unnecessary comment rewrite as “patch fluff” that should be dropped.

The review is **not a rejection**—Peff explicitly acknowledges that the `SECOND_READ`-gated recovery logic is now implemented as expected—but it does re-open the architectural question of whether the `stale_packs_detected` machinery is still justified. The tone is matter-of-fact, not adversarial, and the feedback is framed as a preference rather than a blocker. No new test failures or correctness issues are raised; the discussion remains at the level of code clarity and maintainability.
