The thread continues Junio C Hamano’s architectural refactoring of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore` to eliminate the monolithic `checkout_main()` function and libify reusable logic. This second iteration of an eight-patch series remains an early draft for discussion, not yet intended for merging.

The latest update is the fourth patch, which extracts roughly 100 lines of initialization, validation, and pathspec handling logic from `checkout_main()` into four new static helpers: `init_checkout_opts()`, `validate_path_options()`, `prepare_common_options()`, and `parse_pathspec_from_file_options()`. These changes improve code locality and readability without altering behavior, and the patch passes the test suite. While uncontroversial, its scale makes it a key step in the refactoring effort. The extracted helpers lay the groundwork for future libification, though they are not yet exposed to other subsystems (that happens in the eighth patch). D. Ben Knoble has already expressed agreement with the architectural direction, but broader feedback from contributors like René Scharfe or Patrick Steinhardt is still pending.

The series as a whole remains self-contained and conflict-free with `master`, but Junio has explicitly deferred merging to `seen` due to potential conflicts with in-flight topics. The first seven patches could be merged independently if the eighth is deferred, and the fourth patch in particular demonstrates the mechanical nature of the refactoring. No objections or alternatives have been raised, but the series is still a discussion starter—its readiness for `seen` will depend on community feedback and coordination with other ongoing work.
