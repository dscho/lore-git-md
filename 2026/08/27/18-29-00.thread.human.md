This thread is about Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore` to eliminate the monolithic `checkout_main()` helper and extract reusable logic into standalone utility functions. The goal is to place all callers—built-in commands and external programs—on equal footing by moving shared logic into top-level files (`checkout.c`, `checkout.h`) that accept a `struct repository *` parameter, aligning with the project’s architectural direction for libification.

The series is now complete in its second iteration, with all patches passing the test suite and addressing the "cheating" pattern where `git switch` and `git restore` were bolted onto `git checkout` via a shared entry point. The first seven patches progressively extract initialization, validation, and branch setup logic into smaller, focused helpers, reducing `checkout_main()`’s complexity by roughly 450 lines before eliminating it entirely in the sixth patch. The seventh patch improves readability by wrapping long lines and replacing manual option-compatibility checks with helper macros, while the eighth patch moves `post_checkout_hook()` to `checkout.c` and updates it to work in multi-repository contexts, fully removing its dependency on `the_repository`.

Junio has framed this as an early draft for discussion, not yet intended for merging due to potential conflicts with other in-flight topics. D. Ben Knoble has agreed with the architectural direction, but broader feedback from other key contributors (e.g., René Scharfe, Patrick Steinhardt) is still pending. The series is uncontroversial and self-contained—the first seven patches could be merged independently if the eighth is deferred—but its scale and timing make it a candidate for careful review. No objections or alternatives have been raised, and the refactoring serves as a template for future libification efforts in other built-in commands. The next steps will likely depend on community feedback and coordination with related work, such as the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.
