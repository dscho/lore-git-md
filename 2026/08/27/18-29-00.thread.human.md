This thread is about refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore` to eliminate a shared helper function (`checkout_main()`) that was previously used as a "cheating" way to bolt these commands onto `git checkout`. Junio Hamano posted an 8-patch series (v2) that extracts reusable logic into standalone utility functions, making all three commands call these helpers directly. The series is an early draft for discussion, not yet ready for merging, and passes the test suite.

The core change is in the sixth patch, which removes `checkout_main()` entirely and rewrites the command entry points to call the new helpers directly. This aligns with the project's architectural direction to avoid libifying built-in commands themselves but to factor out their reusable internals. Karthik Nayak reviewed parts of the series and identified two key issues: a design question about whether to keep or eliminate a parameter (`cb_option`) introduced in the first patch, and a correctness bug in the third patch where an option-compatibility check would incorrectly trigger in `--patch` mode. His review of the sixth patch was surface-level, noting only that it clarified the series' structure, and he did not raise new concerns about it.

The series is uncontroversial in its goals, but the unresolved feedback from Karthik may require adjustments before it can proceed. The first seven patches could potentially be merged independently if the eighth is deferred, but the series is currently on hold to avoid conflicts with other in-flight work. No broader objections have been raised, and D. Ben Knoble has agreed with the architectural direction. The next steps will likely involve Junio addressing Karthik's feedback, either by accepting the proposed simplifications or explaining why the explicit parameter is preferred.
