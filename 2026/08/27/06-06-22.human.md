This is the final review round on the core fix of Elijah Newren’s four-patch series that plugs a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking. The series targets a crash in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other commands when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) still points to a packfile that has been removed. This specific email is Jeff King (Peff) reviewing the fourth and last patch, which implements the recovery logic.

Peff’s feedback is **substantive but narrow**: he accepts the tri-state return design (`MIDX_FILL_MISS`, `MIDX_FILL_HIT`, `MIDX_FILL_OWNER_UNAVAILABLE`) and the `SECOND_READ`-gated fallback that were adopted in v2, but he raises two small technical points. First, he notes that the function signature of `fill_midx_entry()` has changed semantics without a corresponding name change, which could confuse future callers; a rename would be safer. Second, he questions the need for the `stale_packs_detected` flag introduced in patch 3/4, implying that the recovery logic in this patch might already be sufficient. He also calls out a minor, unnecessary comment rewrite as “patch fluff.”

The rest of the patch—gating recovery on `SECOND_READ`, scanning MIDX-covered packs for surviving objects, and the test coverage—remains uncontested. The series is now effectively ready for merging, pending Elijah’s response to these two nits. The larger architectural debate about whether `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` is being misused (raised in Peff’s review of patch 3/4) does not resurface here, so this patch stands on its own technical merits.
