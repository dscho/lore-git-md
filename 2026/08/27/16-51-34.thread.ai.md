Core problem or goal
Allow users to skip building and installing `git-svn` (and its Perl dependencies) by defining `NO_GIT_SVN` in their build configuration. This also automatically disables the SVN interoperability tests (`NO_SVN_TESTS`), reducing build time in environments where Subversion is not needed.
