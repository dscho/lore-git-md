This is the **build system** portion of Wesley Schwengle’s two-patch series that makes `git-svn` optional. Patch 2/2 introduces a new `NO_GIT_SVN=YesPlease` Makefile knob that, when set, skips building, installing, and testing `git-svn` entirely. The knob also automatically implies `NO_SVN_TESTS`, so users who opt out of `git-svn` also skip the time-consuming SVN interoperability tests. The change is a straightforward eight-line addition to the Makefile, adding the new variable, guarding the `git-svn.perl` script inclusion, and setting `NO_SVN_TESTS` when `NO_GIT_SVN` is defined.

The patch is narrow, well-motivated, and uncontroversial. It complements the behavioral fix in patch 1/2 (already summarized) by giving users a clean way to avoid Perl dependencies and build-time overhead when SVN support is not needed. No new symbols or on-disk format changes are introduced, and the implementation follows the established pattern of other `NO_*` Makefile knobs. The series as a whole remains under review but has not attracted substantive objections; the only feedback so far was a typo fix in the error message, which was addressed in v2.
