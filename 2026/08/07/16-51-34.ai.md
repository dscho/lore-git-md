This is the **documentation patch** (3/3) in the v2 series fixing race conditions in HTTP daemon test helpers. Michael Montalbo responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s surface-level review of the new "Writing concurrency-safe helpers" section in `t/README`.

The exchange clarifies the scope and wording of the documentation. Michael acknowledges Patrick’s suggestions—adjusting the section header, restoring `rm` as a documented atomic alternative to `mv`, and explicitly mentioning PIDs instead of relying on `$$`—but pushes back slightly on making the section *exclusively* HTTP-specific. He argues the advice is broadly applicable to any concurrent test helper and prefers to keep the language generic rather than relocating the guidance to `t/lib-httpd.sh`. The changes are editorial and do not alter the technical substance of the series; the atomic idioms (`mv`, `mkdir`, `rm`) and their rationale remain unchanged.

The patch is now ready for integration with these minor wording updates. No further discussion is expected.
