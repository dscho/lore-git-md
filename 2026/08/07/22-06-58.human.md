This is a refactoring patch in Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder. It is the third patch in the ten-patch v5 series, which is now feature-complete and merge-ready.

The patch removes the `struct object_info_args` wrapper and passes its three members (`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`) directly to `fetch_object_info()` and `send_object_info_request()`. The commit message explains that the struct grouped unrelated pointers that were always accessed individually, so the abstraction added no value and could mislead readers. The change is purely mechanical—no behavior changes, no new functionality—and touches only `fetch-object-info.c`, `fetch-object-info.h`, and `transport.c`.

Junio has explicitly endorsed this patch as ready to merge, and Karthik Nayak’s earlier feedback about the commit message phrasing has been addressed. The updated commit message now clearly states that `fetch_object_info()` does use all three fields and explains why the wrapper is being removed. The patch is uncontroversial and part of the preparatory work for the object-type support that follows in later patches.
