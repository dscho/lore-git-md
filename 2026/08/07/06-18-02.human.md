This is a performance patch series (v2) reintroducing a `writev(3p)`-style wrapper to reduce system call overhead in Git’s low-level I/O operations. The series is part of a previously reverted effort (Git 2.54) that was pulled due to platform compatibility issues—now fixed—and expands the wrapper’s use to additional callsites.

The v2 update simplifies the compatibility shim by writing only the first non-empty `iovec` in the loop, ensuring non-interleaving behavior even on platforms without native `writev(2)`. This change addresses a forward-compatibility concern raised in review and removes the need for overflow checks, making the wrapper both safer and cleaner. The series touches `sideband.c` (pktline transmission) and `fast-import.c` (cat-blob responses), with build system adjustments for CMake, Meson, and NonStop.

All technical disputes from v1 are resolved: API naming (`git_write_gather()`), integer-overflow safety, and atomicity requirements have been settled by Junio C Hamano and Johannes Sixt. The series is technically complete and under review for merge appetite; no benchmarks are included in the email, but the cover letter references performance gains. The patch count is reduced from six to five, reflecting the simplified compatibility layer.
