This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series **v5** that makes on-disk object-database (ODB) structures pluggable. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is now fully approved, with only minor docstring and formatting nits remaining before merging.

This patch defers object-database creation by lifting `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()` and placing it at every call site that initializes a repository. The change is mechanical: `repo_init()`, `enter_repo()`, `setup_git_directory_gently()`, and `init_db()` now explicitly call `odb_new()` after `apply_repository_format()` succeeds. The only exception is `init_db()`, where ODB creation is deferred until `create_object_database()` is called. This encapsulation keeps ODB initialization and on-disk creation together, simplifying the path for the final patch (6/6), which moves on-disk structure creation into the `struct odb_source` backends.

The patch touches three files: `repository.c`, `setup.c`, and `setup.h`. The diff is straightforward: `apply_repository_format()` no longer creates the ODB, and each caller now does so explicitly. The `create_object_directory()` helper is renamed to `create_object_database()` to reflect its expanded role (it now also calls `odb_new()`). The `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_HONOR_ENV` flag is passed directly to `odb_new()` at each call site, eliminating the need for an intermediate `odb_new_flags` variable.

This reroll adopts Toon Claes’s alternative design, abandoning the earlier `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag in favor of explicit ODB creation. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of making ODB initialization more flexible. The patch is ready for merging, with no substantive feedback remaining.
