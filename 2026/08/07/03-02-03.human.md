This is a test patch in the v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds clock-skew edge-case coverage to `t6600-test-reach.sh`, hardening the side-exhaustion optimization against scenarios where commit-date ordering violates topological invariants.

The patch introduces two new topologies (`se-*` and `se2-*`) where the correct merge base has a *higher* committer date than its child. Without generation ordering, these cases could trigger premature side-exhaustion or return a too-deep merge base. The tests verify that generation numbers (now enforced by the series) ensure children are visited before parents, preserving correctness. Step counts are added to all edge-case tests, including a new `mixed-finite-infinity` test that exercises the transition between INFINITY and finite generation regions.

The test infrastructure (`skew_commit`, `test_paint_down_steps`) was established in earlier patches, so this is purely additive coverage. The step counts (e.g., `6 4 6 6` for `se-*`) reflect the 100-1000x speedups the optimization achieves in asymmetric queries.
