This is patch 3/10 of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The patch is a mechanical refactoring that removes the `struct object_info_args` wrapper and passes its three members (`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`) directly to `fetch_object_info()` and `send_object_info_request()`.

The change touches three files (`fetch-object-info.c`, `fetch-object-info.h`, and `transport.c`) and updates function signatures and call sites to reflect the new parameter list. The commit message explains that the struct was misleading: it grouped unrelated pointers that are accessed individually, so the abstraction added no value. This aligns with Jeff King’s (Peff) advocacy for avoiding semi-opaque structs when the fields are used independently. The patch has no behavior changes and is explicitly endorsed by Junio as ready to merge.

The refactoring is part of the series’ broader architectural shift away from `struct object_info` and toward a dedicated `struct fetch_object_info_results` (introduced in patch 4/10). This patch clears the way for that change by eliminating the intermediate wrapper. Karthik Nayak’s earlier feedback about the commit message phrasing has been addressed: the updated message now accurately states that `fetch_object_info()` *does* use all three fields and explains why the struct was removed. The patch is uncontroversial and integrates cleanly with the rest of the series.
