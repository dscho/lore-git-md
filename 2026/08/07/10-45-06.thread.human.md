The ODB pluggable packfile generation series, introduced by Patrick Steinhardt, continues to progress through the review process. The series aims to decouple Git’s transport layer from the "files" ODB backend, a key step in Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort to support alternative backends. After a merge conflict with the `--no-ref-delta` topic in Junio’s `seen` branch was identified, Taylor Blau agreed to temporarily drop that series from `seen` to unblock the ODB work. Patrick has now formally requested Junio to make this change, which will allow the ODB series to be queued in `seen` and resume review.

The series itself remains unchanged since its initial posting. It refactors fetch, push, and bundle to use a new ODB interface (`odb_generate_pack()`) while preserving all existing functionality, including thin packs, progress meters, shallow clones, and partial clones. The interface is designed for asynchronous operation and introduces new structures like `odb_generate_pack_options` and `odb_pack_generator`. No on-disk format changes are involved, and the series does not yet address repository maintenance or `git-pack-objects`, which are deferred to a future `git-objects` command. The only test adjustment was a minor update to `t5516-fetch-push.sh` to account for argument-order changes.

With the logistical conflict resolved, the next step is Junio’s integration update. Once the ODB series is in `seen`, review can proceed, focusing on potential edge-case regressions and the long-term evolution of the interface. No technical objections have been raised so far, and the series is still under active review.
