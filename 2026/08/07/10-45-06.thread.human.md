Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series continues its march toward pluggable packfile generation with a small but telling clarification. The thread, now in its third iteration (v3), decouples Git’s transport layer from the “files” ODB backend by introducing a clean interface for packfile generation. After earlier fixes for a use-after-scope bug and Junio’s objection to lingering `the_repository` usage, the series is queued in `seen` and under active review.

The latest exchange addresses Karthik Nayak’s observation that the `source` parameter in the `generate_pack` callback is unused in the current files-backend implementation. Patrick explains that the parameter is a forward-looking design choice: future ODB backends may need access to the `struct odb_source` context, and removing it now would risk breaking that extensibility. The interface therefore stays as-is, preserving consistency across the ODB layer. This leaves one minor loose end—the error message in `upload-pack.c` still leaks backend-specific terminology (“fork”)—but otherwise the series remains on track. Patches 2/6 and 3/6 have already demonstrated the interface’s applicability to both fetch and push operations, replacing manual `child_process` construction with a declarative setup while preserving all existing functionality (thin packs, shallow clones, progress meters, and stateless-RPC handling). The only substantive open question is Junio’s objection to the `extern` declaration workaround for `get_log_output_encoding()`, which sidesteps the compile-time guard against `the_repository` usage; no resolution has been reached, but Patrick has signaled openness to revisiting it. The series is still under review, with no merge to `next` or `master` yet in sight.
