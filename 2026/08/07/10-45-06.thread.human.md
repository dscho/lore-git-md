This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series making Git’s packfile generation pluggable as part of his larger ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to decouple the transport layer from the "files" backend, enabling future support for alternative object storage backends. The series is now in Junio’s `seen` branch after an administrative conflict with the `--no-ref-delta` topic was resolved, and all patches remain unchanged since the initial posting.

The technical core introduces a new ODB interface (`odb_generate_pack()`) and supporting structures, replacing manual `child_process` construction in `upload-pack.c`, `send-pack.c`, and `builtin/bundle.c` with a declarative setup. The refactoring preserves all existing functionality—thin packs, progress meters, shallow clones, partial clones, and URI protocols—while simplifying option handling in `git bundle`. Junio’s recent review flagged two lingering uses of the global `the_repository` in patch 5/5, which Patrick has committed to fixing in the next iteration by converting the entire `bundle.c` file to avoid the global, except for one encoding-related function.

The series is still under active review, with no prior versions or merges to `next` or `master`. The only open technical question is the `the_repository` cleanup, which Patrick plans to address in the next version. Junio has not objected to the overall direction, and Taylor Blau’s role was limited to coordinating the rebase logistics. The next steps are straightforward: Patrick will post a revised version incorporating the cleanup, and the series will continue its path toward integration.
