This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s second iteration of a six-patch series that makes Git’s transport layer (fetch, push, bundle) pluggable at the object-database level. The goal is to decouple the transport commands from the “files” ODB backend, a key step in Patrick’s larger ODB abstraction effort. The series is now complete: all three transport paths delegate packfile generation to the new `odb_generate_pack()` interface, and the files-backend implementation simply hands off to the existing `git pack-objects` command.

Junio C Hamano administratively resolved the merge conflict with the `--no-ref-delta` topic by dropping the latter from his `seen` branch and queuing the ODB series in its place. The series remains under active review, with no prior versions merged to `next` or `master`. Two substantive technical points are still open: Junio’s objection to an `extern` declaration in patch 5/6 that bypasses the compile-time guard against `the_repository` usage, and Elijah Newren’s report of a potential use-after-scope bug in the files-backend implementation. Justin Tobler’s recent question—whether `git bundle` will work with non-files backends while still producing packfiles—is a surface-level clarification that does not alter the current status or block progress.
