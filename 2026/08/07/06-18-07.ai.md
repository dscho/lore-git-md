This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch v2 series reintroducing a `writev(3p)`-like wrapper for performance. It applies the new `writev_in_full()` plumbing to `git fast-import`’s `cat-blob` responses, replacing three separate `write(3p)` calls (header, payload, newline) with a single batched write. The change is confined to `builtin/fast-import.c` and touches no other files.

The patch demonstrates a measurable speed-up: a synthetic benchmark with 100,000 blobs in linux.git shows a 4 % reduction in wall-clock time (1.320 s → 1.270 s), with system CPU time dropping from 0.161 s to 0.132 s. The commit message includes a concise reproduction recipe, making it easy for reviewers to verify the claim. No new tests are added, but the change is low-risk—it only batches writes that were already happening sequentially, so the on-wire protocol and on-disk format remain unchanged.

This is a clean, well-motivated micro-optimization that leverages the infrastructure introduced earlier in the series. The performance gain is modest but real, and the patch itself is small and self-contained. It reinforces the broader argument that the `writev(3p)` wrapper is worth the complexity, though the maintainer’s appetite for merging the series remains the only open question.
