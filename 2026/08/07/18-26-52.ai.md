This is the final patch in Toon Claes’s six-patch v2 series optimizing the experimental `git last-modified` command by reusing Bloom filters. It addresses a subtle but critical edge case: when the user supplies a wildcard pathspec (e.g., `*.c`), `prepare_revision_walk()` clears the `bloom_filter_settings` field because the pathspec cannot be converted into a single Bloom key. Without this patch, the per-path Bloom-filter lookups that the rest of the series relies on would fail, forcing a fallback to the slow path and negating the performance gains. The fix is minimal—eight lines in `builtin/last-modified.c` that restore the settings pointer after the revision walk—yet it ensures the optimization works for all pathspec types, including wildcards. The commit message clearly explains the problem, the solution, and the rationale, making the change easy to follow. This patch completes the series and is ready for review; no further technical blockers are known.
