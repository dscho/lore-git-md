This is a **brief follow-up in a review thread** for the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions for pluggable packfile writes. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

Justin responds to Patrick’s review of the patch, which shifts error reporting from direct string returns to a caller-provided `strbuf`. The exchange is purely about refinements: Justin accepts Patrick’s suggestions to mark the `strbuf` parameters in `report()` and `report_v2()` as `const` for const-correctness, and to reuse the `unpack_status` buffer across multiple runs to reduce memory allocations. These are small, surface-level improvements that do not alter the patch’s core design or behavior.

The thread remains in active review, with these changes likely to be incorporated in the next revision. No new technical questions or objections are raised; the discussion is focused on polishing the implementation. The patch itself is a mechanical but necessary step toward moving `unpack()` behind the ODB transaction interface, and the feedback here is constructive and uncontroversial.
