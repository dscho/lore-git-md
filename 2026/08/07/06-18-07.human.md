This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch v2 series reintroducing a `writev(3p)`-like wrapper for performance. It applies the new `writev_in_full()` plumbing to `git fast-import`’s `cat-blob` responses, replacing three separate `write(3p)` calls with a single batched write.

The change is straightforward: when answering a `cat-blob` command, `fast-import` now packs the header line, the blob payload, and the trailing newline into a three-element `iovec` array and hands it to `writev_in_full()`. The patch includes a benchmark showing a 4 % speed-up (1.32 s → 1.27 s) when processing 100 000 blobs in linux.git, demonstrating that the syscall reduction is measurable even for moderately sized repositories.

No new compatibility concerns are introduced; the patch simply consumes the infrastructure added in earlier patches. The diff is confined to `builtin/fast-import.c` and is purely mechanical. The commit message is clear and includes a reproduction recipe, making it easy for reviewers to verify the performance claim.
