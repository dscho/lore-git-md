This thread is about a use-after-free bug in Git’s `get_oid_with_context_1()` function, triggered when resolving relative paths in the `:<stage>:<path>` syntax against a sparse index. The bug, present since 2022, was fixed by Shlok Kulshreshtha with a straightforward patch that ensures a dynamically allocated path string is freed only after its final use. The fix includes a regression test in `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh` that reliably reproduces the issue under `SANITIZE=address` and confirms the fix.

The patch has received substantive review and approval from René Scharfe, who confirmed the bug’s origin and explicitly endorsed the fix.
