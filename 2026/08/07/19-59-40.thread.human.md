This thread is about a use-after-free bug in Git’s `get_oid_with_context_1()` function, which occurs when resolving relative paths in the `:<stage>:<path>` syntax against a sparse index. The bug, present since 2022, was fixed by Shlok Kulshreshtha in a standalone patch now at its third iteration. The fix ensures the string allocated by `resolve_relative_path()` is freed only after its final use in `reject_tree_in_index()`, preventing the use-after-free.

The patch has been thoroughly reviewed and tested, with explicit approvals from René Scharfe and Patrick Steinhardt. Junio C Hamano has now confirmed the fix is ready for integration, calling it "exactly what anybody would expect" and stating he will queue it. The regression test in `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh` reliably reproduces the bug under `SANITIZE=address` and covers all necessary conditions (relative path, sparse directory, and leading colon followed by a digit). The patch is uncontroversial, touches only `sha1-name.c` and sparse index handling, and introduces no new symbols or on-disk format changes. Barring unforeseen issues, it will graduate to `master` in the next cycle.
