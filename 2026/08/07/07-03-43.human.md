This is a substantive review of the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions for pluggable packfile writes. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

Patrick’s review engages with the patch’s design and ergonomics, not just its mechanics. He acknowledges the necessity of the change—shifting error reporting from direct string returns to a caller-provided `strbuf`—as preparation for moving the unpack logic behind the ODB abstraction boundary. While he notes that the `strbuf` approach is not his preferred style (he would prefer a structured error type, a long-standing wish in Git), he accepts it as a pragmatic solution given the current codebase.

The review raises three specific points: (1) whether the `unpack_status` parameter in `report()` and `report_v2()` should be marked `const` (a surface-level nit, but one that affects clarity and safety), (2) whether the `unpack_status` buffer in `cmd_receive_pack()` could be reused across multiple runs to save memory allocations (a small performance consideration), and (3) a broader observation about Git’s lack of a structured error type, which he acknowledges is outside the scope of this series.

The feedback is constructive and focused on making the patch more robust and efficient without altering its core purpose. No fundamental objections are raised, and the review does not identify any bugs or edge cases. The patch remains on track for eventual integration, pending resolution of these minor points.
