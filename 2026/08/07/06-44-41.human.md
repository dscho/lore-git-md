This is a **Bash completion patch** for the `git history` command, part of a four-patch series (this is the fourth patch, v2). The discussion here focuses on a maintainability concern raised by Patrick Steinhardt about the `__git_history_has_revision` helper function, which hardcodes options that take arguments (like `--empty` and `--update-refs`). Vincent Mailhol responds with a proposal to handle future edge cases by dispatching special subcommands *before* checking for revisions, rather than modifying the helper function itself.

The exchange is pragmatic and collaborative. Vincent’s counter-proposal—adding a pre-check for subcommands that don’t take revisions—avoids the need to update the helper function every time a new option is added. Patrick accepts this as a reasonable compromise, noting it’s a significant improvement over the current approach. The tone suggests this is unlikely to block the series, though a v3 iteration may incorporate the change. The patch itself remains uncontroversial, with no fundamental objections to its design or functionality.

Key technical details for follow-up: the `__git_history_has_revision` function, the proposed pre-check for non-revision subcommands, and the use of `__git_complete_index_file` for file-based completions. The broader series goal—adding completion for `git history` subcommands, options, and pathspecs—is unchanged. No new CLI options or config keys are introduced; this is purely shell-completion logic.
