This is a maintainer's reply to a feature request for `git fetch` that proposes decoupling the default fetch refspec from the mapping used for explicitly named branches.

Junio C Hamano responds to Douglas Puchalski's proposal by suggesting an alternative approach: rather than adding a new configuration knob or behavioral change, he points to the existing `--refmap` option as a potential solution. He demonstrates how `--refmap="refs/heads/*:refs/remotes/origin/*"` could achieve the desired outcome—fetching `topic/example` into `refs/remotes/origin/topic/example`—while preserving the restricted default fetch set. Junio hints that this mechanism could be made more user-friendly, perhaps via a configuration variable, but does not endorse or reject the broader proposal.

The reply is brief and exploratory, offering a technical workaround without committing to a design direction. It shifts the discussion from "should we do this?" to "how might we do this?" by anchoring the conversation in an existing, if underused, feature. No implementation details or next steps are proposed, and no other contributors have weighed in yet. The thread remains in the early RFC stage, with the ball now in the original author's court to either refine the proposal or demonstrate why `--refmap` is insufficient.
