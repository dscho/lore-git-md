This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch v2 series reintroducing a `writev(3p)`-like wrapper to reduce system-call overhead. It applies the new `git_writev_or_die()` plumbing to `send_sideband()` in `sideband.c`, replacing two separate `write()` calls per pktline (header + data) with a single `writev()` call. Benchmarks on a precomputed pack show a 1.37× speed-up for `git-upload-pack` in the synthetic “cached pack” scenario, and the patch also pushes back the kernel semaphore bottleneck that GitLab has observed under high load. The change is confined to a single file and is purely mechanical once the wrapper infrastructure is in place.
