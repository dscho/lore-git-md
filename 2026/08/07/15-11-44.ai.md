This is a reply in the ODB abstraction effort, specifically addressing feedback on the first patch of Justin Tobler’s six-patch series introducing `odb_transaction_write_pack()` for pluggable packfile writes in `git receive-pack`. The patch under discussion adds `odb_transaction_release()` to decouple transaction cleanup from commit, enabling backend-specific deferred cleanup such as removing ".keep" lockfiles.

Justin accepts Patrick Steinhardt’s naming suggestion, agreeing to rename `odb_transaction_release()` to `odb_transaction_finalize()` in the next version. He also acknowledges Patrick’s observation that `git receive-pack` is the only caller where the transaction is not released immediately after commit—because it needs to retain ".keep" files until references are updated—and will clarify this in the commit message. Most substantively, Justin agrees that the finalize function should be able to report errors, as failures in cleaning up ".keep" files (which can occur in the "files" backend) should not be silently ignored. This change will be incorporated in the next iteration.

The discussion remains focused on design ergonomics and robustness rather than mechanical correctness. No new technical questions are raised; the thread is converging on interface naming, caller documentation, and error reporting. The next version of the patch is expected to address these points without altering the series’ core goal of making `receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic.
