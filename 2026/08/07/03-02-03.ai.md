This is patch 6/10 in the v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. The series is queued in Junio’s tree and procedurally ready for integration, with review bandwidth as the remaining bottleneck.

The patch adds test coverage for clock-skew edge cases that could break the side-exhaustion optimization introduced earlier in the series. It introduces two new topologies (`se-*` and `se2-*`) where commit-date ordering (used in v1 commit-graphs without corrected commit dates) violates the topological invariant that children are dequeued before parents. In these topologies, the correct merge base has a *higher* committer date than its child, which could cause side-exhaustion to fire prematurely or return a too-deep merge base if the algorithm relied on dates. The tests verify that generation ordering (now enforced by the series) prevents these failures by ensuring children are visited before parents regardless of dates.

Key additions include the `se-*` topology (a simple diamond where the merge base `se-D` has a higher date than its child `se-C`) and the `se2-*` topology (a more complex case with a redundant ancestor `se2-MB2`). The patch also adds step counts to all edge-case tests, including the new `mixed-finite-infinity` test (setup in a helper block), which exercises the transition between INFINITY and finite generation-number regions. The step counts (e.g., `6 4 6 6` for `se-*`) reflect the 100-1000x speedups the optimization achieves in asymmetric queries and are validated deterministically across all commit-graph modes (none/full/half/no-gdat).
