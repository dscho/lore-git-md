This is a **feature patch for `gitk`**, adding user-configurable custom commands to context menus. The patch remains under review, but the author has now responded to the maintainer’s blocking concerns.

Tim Wiederhake clarifies the motivation: he built this feature to enable a specific workflow—editing a line in a file at a past commit during an interactive rebase—without requiring a built-in command that would likely be rejected as too niche. The broader goal is to let users adapt `gitk` to their own needs rather than forcing them to adapt to `gitk`. He acknowledges the commit message needs improvement and invites suggestions for phrasing that avoids contrived examples.
