This is a follow-up to Pablo Sabater’s GSoC v4 patch series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support `%(objecttype)` in remote-object-info queries. The email documents Pablo’s acceptance of Jeff King’s (Peff) proposed refactoring, which removes the `object_info_oids` and `object_info_results` fields from `git_transport_options` and passes them as explicit parameters to `transport_fetch_object_info()` and its vtable implementations.

Pablo confirms he will integrate Peff’s patch as-is into his series, noting only two minor typos in the commit message ("shoudl" → "should" and a duplicated "we could keep"). The refactoring is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ broader design goals: it clarifies ownership semantics (the caller retains responsibility for the OID array and results), eliminates a latent bug (dangling pointers if the transport were reused), and prepares the codebase for future enhancements like connection reuse. The change is mechanical (25 insertions, 19 deletions) and touches seven files, primarily `transport.c`, `transport-helper.c`, and `builtin/cat-file.c`. No behavior changes or protocol-level adjustments are involved.

Pablo also briefly discusses Peff’s suggestion to make the `remote-object-info` protocol stateful (e.g., allowing multiple object-info requests after a single `remote <url>` declaration). While he finds the idea compelling, he defers it to a follow-up series, citing time constraints and the current series’ focus on object type support. The discussion is speculative and does not affect the immediate patch.

The series is now effectively complete and merge-ready, pending Pablo’s integration of Peff’s patch. All prior review feedback has been addressed, including Junio’s protocol robustness concerns (under-length/over-length response validation) and Karthik Nayak’s commit message clarifications. The architectural refactoring (replacing `struct object_info` with `struct fetch_object_info_results`) is finalized, and the end-to-end implementation for object type metadata is in place. Tests in `t5701` and `t1017` verify the new functionality and ensure backward compatibility.
