This is a **substantive review** of the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions for pluggable packfile writes. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

Patrick’s review engages with the patch’s design and ergonomics, not just its mechanics. He acknowledges the necessity of the change—shifting error reporting from direct string returns to a caller-provided `strbuf`—as preparation for moving `unpack()` behind the ODB transaction interface. His feedback focuses on three areas: (1) the clarity of the new calling convention (which he finds an improvement over the previous opaque string return), (2) the const-correctness of the `strbuf` parameters in `report()` and `report_v2()` (suggesting they should be marked `const`), and (3) a potential optimization—reusing the `unpack_status` buffer across multiple runs to reduce memory allocations.

The review is constructive and forward-looking, accepting the patch’s direction while inviting small refinements. The const-correctness suggestions are surface-level but important for code hygiene; the buffer reuse idea is substantive and could improve performance in edge cases. No objections to the patch’s core goal or approach are raised. The thread remains in active review, with this feedback likely to be addressed in a future revision.
