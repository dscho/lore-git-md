This is the third patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series making `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic via ODB transactions. The patch lifts eight global variables (`fsck_msg_types`, `shallow_file`, `max_input_size`, `fsck_objects`, `unpack_limit`, `reject_thin`, `err_fd`, `quiet`) into a new `struct unpack_opts` passed explicitly through the call chain. The change is mechanical and does not alter behavior; it prepares the codebase for the later introduction of `odb_transaction_write_pack()`, which will delegate packfile processing to the ODB backend.

The author acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback on the commit message typo ("can no rely" → "can no longer rely") and agrees to fix it. Steinhardt’s review flags three ergonomic concerns: (1) the diff is hard to audit for completeness because the old global references are scattered, (2) the ternary initialization of `fsck_objects` inside the struct initializer is visually awkward, and (3) the late assignment of `err_fd` in `unpack_with_sideband()` forces mutation of the passed-in struct. The author notes that an earlier version of the patch moved the `fsck_objects` assignment outside the initializer but found the result no clearer. Both agree the trade-offs are acceptable given the larger goal, and the function will move to a different file in a later patch, where the abstraction will be easier to audit.

The patch is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt. It is plumbing-only and does not change observable behavior or on-disk format. The next patch in the series will shift error reporting from direct string returns to `strbuf` output parameters, and the final patch will introduce `odb_transaction_write_pack()` to complete the backend-agnostic refactoring. Reviewers are likely to continue focusing on the ergonomics and robustness of the new `unpack_opts` struct and its interaction with the transaction lifecycle.
