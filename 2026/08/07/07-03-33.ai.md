This is a substantive review of the second patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series making `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic via ODB transactions. The patch removes the global `alt_shallow_file` variable by passing the shallow file path explicitly through the call chain in `builtin/receive-pack.c`. Patrick Steinhardt’s review is positive and focused on the mechanical soundness of the change: he notes the reduction in global state, the logical shift of shallow-file creation to an earlier caller, and the simplification of not needing to pass shallow information separately. The review does not raise technical concerns or edge cases; it is effectively an acknowledgment that the refactoring is clean and aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating globals ahead of the ODB transaction switch. No follow-up questions or design objections are present, suggesting this patch is uncontroversial and likely to proceed as-is.
