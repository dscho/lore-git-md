This is a brief follow-up in the thread about the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable. The series is now fully merged to `next` and addresses a key part of the ODB abstraction effort.

Toon Claes clarifies why the overly long line in `enter_repo()`—which Junio noted was corrected in v4 but reverted in v5—no longer appears in the final version. The reformatting was originally tied to the now-abandoned `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag, which was removed in v4 in favor of Toon’s alternative design. Since the flag is gone, the line-length fix became unnecessary, and Patrick reverted it to avoid mixing unrelated changes. Toon agrees this is the cleaner approach, noting that other instances of the same long line remain unaddressed elsewhere in the codebase.

The exchange is purely mechanical and does not affect the patch’s substance. The series remains on track for graduation to `master` in the current cycle.
