This is the final sign-off on Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series that makes the creation of on-disk object-database (ODB) structures pluggable. The series is now fully approved: Justin Tobler and Toon Claes have both given “looks good to me” (LGTM), and Toon’s latest reply explicitly states he is “completely happy” with the v5 reroll. The two minor issues he raised earlier—a misleading docstring and a formatting inconsistency—have been addressed, and the leftover formatting change from v4 has been removed.

The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is already cooking in Junio’s `next` branch (per the 2025-06-10 “What’s cooking” report). It touches loose-object handling, ODB source management, and repository setup, introducing a new `create_on_disk` callback that lets backends control their own subdirectory layout while keeping the top-level `objects/` directory hard-coded for repository discovery. The preparatory patches fix latent correctness bugs in loose-object map loading and move environment-variable handling into the ODB layer, setting the stage for future backends that do not follow the traditional loose/pack layout. With all review feedback resolved, this series is on track to graduate to `master` in the current development cycle.
