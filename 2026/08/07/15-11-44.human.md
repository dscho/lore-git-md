This is a follow-up in the ODB abstraction effort, specifically the six-patch series from Justin Tobler that refactors `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions for pluggable packfile writes. The email is a reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the first patch, which introduces `odb_transaction_release()`.

Justin agrees with Patrick’s suggestion to rename `odb_transaction_release()` to `odb_transaction_finalize()`, as "finalize" better reflects the broader cleanup responsibilities (including lockfile removal) that the function will assume later in the series. He also accepts Patrick’s point that the function should be able to report errors, particularly for failures in cleaning up ".keep" files, which are currently ignored. The commit message will be updated to highlight that `git receive-pack` is the only caller where the transaction is not released immediately after commit, due to its need to retain ".keep" files until references are updated.

The discussion remains focused on design ergonomics and robustness rather than technical correctness. No new technical concerns are raised, and the changes are uncontroversial in principle. The next version of the patch will incorporate these adjustments, moving the series closer to a backend-agnostic `receive-pack` implementation. This is a well-motivated, incremental step in the ODB abstraction effort, and the author’s responsiveness to feedback suggests the series is on track for eventual integration.
