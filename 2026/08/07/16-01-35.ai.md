This is the author's reply to Patrick Steinhardt's **substantive review** of the final patch in the six-part series introducing `odb_transaction_write_pack()` for backend-agnostic packfile writes in `git receive-pack`. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

Justin Tobler **accepts all of Patrick Steinhardt’s technical suggestions** from the review. First, he agrees that `odb_reprepare()`—which currently refreshes the entire object database after packfile ingestion—should be narrowed to reprepare only the transaction’s source ODB, since the packfile has only been written to that backend. Second, he acknowledges that `unpack_limit` (a threshold for storing objects in loose vs. packed format) does not belong in the generic `odb_transaction_write_pack_opts` struct, as it is irrelevant to backends that do not use loose/packed storage, and will move it to be handled internally by the backend. He also accepts the minor ergonomic feedback to add spacing between fields in the struct for readability.

The reply **does not introduce new technical questions or objections**; it simply confirms the author’s intent to incorporate the feedback in the next revision. The series remains under active review, with no merge status change, and is still cooking in `seen`. The next version is expected to address the interface refinements, error reporting ergonomics, and const-correctness improvements discussed in earlier patches, in addition to these changes. The transaction lifecycle design—particularly the split between `commit` and `finalize`—remains an open design question, but this reply does not revisit it.
