This thread is about converting Git’s nanosecond timestamp precision from a build-time macro (`USE_NSEC`) to a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`). The series, now in its sixth iteration (`v6`), was authored by D. Ben Knoble and has graduated to Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch for integration testing as of July 10, 2024. The change allows users to enable nanosecond precision on supported filesystems without recompiling Git, reducing "racy Git" problems where timestamps are too coarse to distinguish file modifications.

The series is technically complete, with all prior feedback addressed, though a few loose ends remain. The most significant is an architectural constraint: the new call to `repo_config_values()` in `is_racy_stat()` only works for the global repository (`the_repository`), creating a latent bug when operating on other repositories (e.g., submodules). This limitation is now explicitly flagged as a blocker for future recursive submodule operations, such as `git diff` or `git status` traversing submodules without spawning subprocesses. For now, the series uses a safe default—treating `core.useNanosec` as false when `istate->repo != the_repository`—but this is a stopgap rather than a fix. Junio and Knoble agree this issue won’t hold up the series, though Knoble acknowledges it might reduce urgency to address the underlying problem. Other minor points include a routine conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` in `seen` and a theoretical edge case involving mixed builds (one with `NO_NSEC` and one without) on nanosecond-capable systems, which Jeff King (Peff) and Knoble consider unlikely and not worth documenting.

Key participants include Knoble, who drove the series and confirmed it’s ready for `next`; Junio, who applied it and raised the submodule limitation; Patrick Steinhardt, who endorsed the technical approach; and Peff, who surfaced the mixed-build edge case. The series touches several files, including `read-cache.c`, `environment.c`, and `Documentation/config/core.adoc`, and introduces the new `core.useNanosec` config key (defaulting to false). Performance concerns from earlier versions were resolved by caching the config value, and a Meson build-system fix ensures consistent manual page links. The topic remains in `next` pending final feedback and conflict resolution, with no further rerolls planned.
