Ben Knoble’s patch series (v5) converting Git’s build-time `USE_NSEC` macro into a runtime configuration option, `core.useNanosec`, to let users enable nanosecond precision without recompiling. The patch ensures backward compatibility on platforms lacking nanosecond support by treating the field as false when `NO_NSEC` is defined, eliminating any risk of silent corruption. Testing remains open-ended due to platform dependencies, but the patches are otherwise polished and self-contained.
