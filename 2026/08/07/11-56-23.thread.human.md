The `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series is effectively complete and ready to graduate to `master`. The thread began with D. Ben Knoble’s proposal to replace the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`), allowing users to enable nanosecond precision without recompiling Git. The series also includes a Meson build-system fix and a no-op refactor of `repo_config_values_init()`.

The core change—introducing `core.useNanosec`—has been thoroughly reviewed and is uncontroversial. Early versions faced a performance regression (~41× slowdown in `git diff` benchmarks) and a cross-platform correctness blocker (silent corruption risk on `NO_NSEC` platforms), but both were resolved: the performance issue by caching the value in `repo_config_values`, and the correctness issue by unconditionally including the `use_nanosec` field while treating it as false on unsupported platforms. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the final polish items in v4, including unconditional initialization of `use_nanosec`, simplified preprocessor guards, and improved documentation. Both maintainers rejected the idea of a runtime warning for unsupported platforms, prioritizing user experience for shared configurations.

The series is now cooking in `next` under the topic `dk/use-nsec-runtime` and is ready to graduate to `master`. The only remaining loose ends are platform-dependent test coverage (not blocking) and a textual conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` in `seen`. The Meson build-system fix and the no-op refactor are also ready to advance independently. Key participants—Knoble, Junio, and Steinhardt—are aligned on the design and implementation, leaving no open technical or design questions.
