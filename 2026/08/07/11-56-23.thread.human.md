This thread, now in its sixth iteration, replaces Git’s build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`), letting users enable nanosecond precision on supported filesystems without recompiling. The series also fixes a long-standing Meson build inconsistency and includes a minor refactor of config initialization. It was merged to Junio’s `next` branch on July 10 and is considered technically complete, with all prior feedback addressed—including a locally amended typo fix in the documentation.

The core change allows `core.useNanosec` to toggle nanosecond precision at runtime, reducing "racy Git" issues. Performance concerns from early versions were resolved by caching the config value, and the series preserves backward compatibility: platforms lacking nanosecond support (e.g., `NO_NSEC` builds) ignore the setting without warnings. Jeff King (Peff) and D. Ben Knoble discussed a subtle edge case where mixed builds (one with `NO_NSEC`, one without) on a nanosecond-capable system could cause false "racy" comparisons, but both agreed it’s unlikely to occur in practice and doesn’t warrant changes.

The series remains in `next` pending this minor update, with no other open questions.
