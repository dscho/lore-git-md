This thread is about a small follow-up to a recently graduated patch series that made Git's nanosecond precision configurable at runtime. The original series, authored by D. Ben Knoble, replaced the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with a new `core.useNanosec` configuration option, allowing users to enable nanosecond precision without recompiling Git. After six iterations addressing performance, correctness, and architectural concerns, the series was merged to Junio's `next` branch for integration testing.

The new email is a trivial one-line patch correcting a comment in `builtin/update-index.c`—changing "defined" to "enabled" to better reflect the runtime nature of `core.useNanosec`. This was the final piece of feedback from Patrick Steinhardt during the v6 review, and its inclusion completes the series. The underlying work is considered technically sound, with all major concerns resolved: the performance regression from v1 was fixed by caching the config value, and the risk of silent corruption on platforms lacking nanosecond support was mitigated by treating the field as false when `NO_NSEC` is defined.

No further discussion or testing is expected, as the change is purely cosmetic and the series has already been thoroughly vetted. The only remaining loose end is a routine textual conflict with another topic in `seen` (`ty/repo-config-cleanups`), which will need resolution if both advance. The series is otherwise ready to progress toward `master`.
