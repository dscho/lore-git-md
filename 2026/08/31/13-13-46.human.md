This is the v9 iteration of the patch series introducing a `--linearize` option to `git replay`. The series is now focused on final polish: v9 makes no functional changes but updates terminology in commit messages, documentation, and error messages from "multiple revision ranges" to "multiple branches" for clarity, addressing feedback from Justin Tobler and Elijah Newren in v8.

The series remains a three-patch refactoring plus feature addition. Patch 1/3 adds a helper function for tracking replayed commits, patch 2/3 consolidates replay base resolution logic, and patch 3/3 implements `--linearize` with the single-branch restriction introduced in v8. The restriction—disallowing `--linearize` with multiple positive revisions or `--contained`—resolves the multi-branch ambiguity that had blocked the series in `next` and aligns with `git cherry-pick`'s existing behavior. The documentation now explicitly notes that `--linearize` cannot be combined with multiple branches or `--contained` and that users should replay branches separately if they want to linearize more than one.

The series is technically complete and ready for integration. Junio has already removed the previous version from `next` and will queue v9 for review, though it has not yet been merged to `next`. The only remaining open question is whether `--linearize` should eventually support per-branch linearization (e.g., via `--ref` syntax), but this is deferred for future work. Potential trivial conflicts with Kristoffer’s documentation changes and Patrick Steinhardt’s `git history drop` series are noted but should be straightforward to resolve.
