This is a reply in the ongoing refactoring series that makes Git’s fsck checks pluggable per ODB backend. The email is a short back-and-forth between Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak about the first patch in the series, which replaces 16 lines of open-coded loose-object verification in `builtin/fsck.c` with a single call to `fsck_obj_buffer()`.

Karthik’s review was purely surface-level: he suggested dropping the word “Almost” from the commit message, noted that the `free(contents)` call had moved but was still correct, and observed that `fsck_obj_buffer()` also sets the `ERROR_OBJECT` flag (which is harmless). Patrick accepted the editorial nit and explained why he kept the redundant flag-setting: it preserves the visual flow of the code even though the function already handles it internally. No technical concerns were raised or addressed; the exchange is entirely about wording and style. The patch itself remains unchanged in substance.
