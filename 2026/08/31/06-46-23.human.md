This is patch 9/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. The patch moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`). It replaces the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and ensures the new implementation respects the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. A new test in `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` verifies these semantics.

The change is mechanical and follows the pattern established in earlier patches: 18 lines are removed from `cmd_fsck()`, 27 lines are added to `odb/source-packed.c`, and the test script is extended by 13 lines. The new `verify_midx()` helper spawns `git multi-pack-index verify` as a child process, checks the repository’s `core_multi_pack_index` setting, and threads the progress meter flag through the `odb_fsck_options` structure. No behavior changes are introduced beyond the intentional flag-based skipping of checks.

This patch is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and depends on the scaffolding introduced in earlier patches (notably patch 5/10, which added the pluggable fsck infrastructure). Karthik Nayak’s reviews of the series have been surface-level, focusing on wording and spelling rather than substantive correctness or edge cases. All feedback from v1 and v2 has been addressed, and the series is now a clean refactoring with no open technical questions. The patch looks well-crafted and aligns with the series’ goal of preparing Git for future pluggable ODB backends.
