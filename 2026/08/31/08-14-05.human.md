This is a minor follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s 8-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and aims to simplify the ODB interface by removing the ability to write alternates after repository creation.

The email is a brief reply to Toon Claes’s surface-level review of patch 6/8. Toon noted that `struct strvec` is already forward-declared later in `odb/source.h` and suggested moving the new `#include "strvec.h"` after that declaration to avoid redundancy. Patrick accepts the suggestion, indicating he will adjust the header order in the next iteration. This is a purely mechanical change—no functional or design issues are at stake, and the patch’s core purpose (supporting alternates at ODB creation time via `odb_create_on_disk_options`) remains uncontroversial.

The series itself is still under active review, with substantive discussion now focused on the locking strategy for writing the `info/alternates` file (raised by Junio C Hamano on patch 6) and the removal of the lockfile mechanism (flagged by Toon Claes in patch 8). This minor header adjustment is unlikely to affect those larger concerns, but it reflects the incremental refinement typical of a well-structured patch series.
