This is patch 6/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s object-database fsck checks to make them pluggable. The patch moves packfile verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`). It threads the progress meter and object callback through the new `struct odb_fsck_options` so the caller’s preferences are respected without exposing backend details at the `cmd_fsck()` level. The patch also simplifies error reporting by replacing the undocumented `ERROR_PACK` bit (never exercised in the test suite) with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit. The diff removes 33 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 46 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, plus a small addition to `odb.h` to expose the progress flag and callback fields. No behavior changes; the patch is a pure refactoring that continues the pattern established in earlier patches of moving backend-specific logic into the respective ODB source implementations. Karthik Nayak’s earlier reviews of this series have been surface-level, focusing on wording and spelling rather than substantive correctness or edge cases, and all feedback has been addressed in v2.
