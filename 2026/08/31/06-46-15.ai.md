This is patch 1/10 of Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. The patch replaces 16 lines of open-coded loose-object verification in `builtin/fsck.c` with a single call to `fsck_obj_buffer()`, removing redundancy without altering behavior. Karthik Nayak’s review of v1 suggested dropping the word "Almost" from the commit message ("Almost the exact same steps" → "the exact same steps"), which Patrick accepted; the change is now live in v2. The patch also moves the `free(contents)` call and sets the `ERROR_OBJECT` flag redundantly as a defensive measure, as Patrick explained in response to Karthik’s observation. No technical concerns remain; this is a clean, mechanical refactoring that aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating fsck logic.
