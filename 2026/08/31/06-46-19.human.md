This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series making Git’s object-database fsck checks pluggable. It introduces the scaffolding that later patches will use to move backend-specific verification logic out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into the respective ODB source implementations.

The patch adds a new `fsck` callback to the `struct odb_source` vtable, empty stubs for all existing source types (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, `packed`), and the `odb_fsck_options` structure with the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag. It replaces the old `check_full` boolean with this new options structure and adds the `odb_fsck()` helper, which iterates over all sources and invokes their respective `fsck` callbacks. No behavior changes yet—this is pure infrastructure.

Karthik Nayak’s review of v1 raised a minor technical question about whether `OPT_BIT` provides equivalent `--no-full` functionality to `OPT_BOOL`; Patrick confirmed that `OPT_BIT`’s bitwise negation replicates the desired semantics, and the question is now resolved. The rest of Karthik’s feedback was editorial and has already been incorporated into the series.

The patch touches eight files, adding about 100 lines of code. Key symbols introduced: `fsck_obj_buffer()` (the sole entry point for object verification), backend-specific verification hooks (`odb_source_fsck_fn`), `struct odb_fsck_options`, and the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag. The scaffolding is clean and aligns with the series’ goal of preparing for future pluggable ODB backends. No open questions remain.
