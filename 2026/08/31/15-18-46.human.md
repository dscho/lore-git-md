This is the final patch in Toon Claes’s six-part series optimizing the experimental `git last-modified` command. It ensures that per-path Bloom filter lookups continue to work for wildcard pathspecs like `*.c` after `prepare_revision_walk()` clears the `bloom_filter_settings` field.

The patch restores the settings pointer immediately after the revision walk, allowing the command to keep using Bloom filters for every path in the expanded wildcard set. To verify the fix, it adds a trace2 counter (`bloom_queries`) that records how often the per-path Bloom check runs, and a new test in `t/t8020-last-modified.sh` that confirms the counter increments exactly twice for a top-level wildcard query. The test also ensures the output remains identical with and without the commit-graph enabled, closing the last test-coverage gap identified in v2.

The change is purely a performance fix—no user-visible behavior is altered—and the instrumentation is lightweight. The patch looks clean and complete, with a clear commit message and thorough test coverage. All prior feedback on the series has been addressed, and this patch is ready for integration.
