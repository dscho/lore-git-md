This is patch 8/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. It moves bitmap verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches. The change replaces the dedicated `ERROR_BITMAP` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and adapts `verify_bitmap_files()` to operate on a single `odb_source_packed` instance rather than iterating over all sources. The patch also extends `t/t5326-multi-pack-bitmaps.sh` to verify that bitmap checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags, ensuring they are skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed and performed when `--no-full` is used. Karthik Nayak’s review of v1 flagged only a spelling nit ("focussed" → "focused"), which Patrick has accepted; no substantive feedback was raised.
