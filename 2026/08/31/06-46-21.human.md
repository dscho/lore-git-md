This is patch 7/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring `git fsck` to make object-database consistency checks pluggable. The patch moves reverse-index verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches.

The change is mechanical: 37 lines are removed from `cmd_fsck()` and 39 lines are added to `odb_source_packed_fsck()`, introducing the new `verify_reverse_indices()` helper. The patch replaces the dedicated `ERROR_PACK_REV_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting. It also aligns the reverse-index checks with the documented intent of the `--connectivity-only` and `--no-full` flags: reverse-index verification is now skipped when either flag is passed, matching the behavior of other ODB checks in the series.

A new test in `t/t5325-reverse-index.sh` verifies these semantics, ensuring that `--no-full` still performs reverse-index checks (for local sources) while `--connectivity-only` skips them entirely. The patch is a clean refactoring with no user-visible behavior changes beyond the flag alignment, and it builds directly on the scaffolding introduced in patches 5 and 6. All feedback from v1 has been addressed, and the series remains under review with no open technical concerns.
