This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s object-database fsck checks to make them pluggable. It replaces 16 lines of open-coded loose-object verification in `builtin/fsck.c` with a single call to the existing `fsck_obj_buffer()` function. The change is purely mechanical and removes redundancy without altering behavior.

The patch touches only `builtin/fsck.c` and is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, which aims to prepare Git for future pluggable backends. Karthik Nayak’s review of v1 suggested dropping the word "Almost" from the commit message ("Almost the exact same steps" → "the exact same steps"), noting that the patch makes minor, harmless changes (moving `free(contents)` and redundant `ERROR_OBJECT` flag-setting). Patrick accepted the wording nit and explained the redundant flag-setting as a defensive measure.

This patch is a straightforward refactoring with no user-visible impact. It sets the stage for later patches that will move verification logic into the respective ODB backends. The series as a whole is under review and depends on two earlier topics (`ps/odb-eagerly-load-alternates` and `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation`). No technical concerns have been raised about this patch.
