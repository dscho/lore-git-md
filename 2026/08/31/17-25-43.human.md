This is the **final patch in Derrick Stolee’s seven-part bugfix/hardening series** for the trace2 subsystem. It removes the last `die()`-triggering helper (`xcalloc()`) from trace2 and introduces a **statically allocated fallback context** to handle allocation failures gracefully.

The patch replaces `xcalloc()` in `tr2tls_create_self()` with `calloc()` and adds defensive error handling. When allocation fails, it falls back to a pre-allocated `tr2tls_thread_fallback` context (thread name: `"unknown"`), ensuring the process continues without telemetry rather than crashing. The new `tr2tls_is_fallback()` helper lets callers detect and short-circuit operations on the fallback context. All trace2 counter and timer functions now check for this state and return early with safe defaults (e.g., `0` for elapsed time).

The changes are minimal but critical: they close the last direct `die()` vector in trace2 while preserving the series’ goal of process stability over telemetry completeness. The fallback mechanism is simple and self-contained, avoiding complex error propagation. **This patch is well-crafted and addresses a real stability problem**—worth attention for anyone tracking Git’s telemetry or low-level hardening efforts. The series as a whole is now complete and ready for integration.
