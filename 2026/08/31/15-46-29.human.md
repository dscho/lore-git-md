This is the final cosmetic update to the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series, which replaces the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with the runtime `core.useNanosec` configuration option. The author, D. Ben Knoble, acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s requested change—replacing "defined" with "enabled" in a comment in `builtin/update-index.c`—and plans to send a v6 revision shortly. This is the only remaining action before the series can graduate to `next` and eventually `master`.

The series itself is technically complete and uncontroversial. It resolves a long-standing "racy Git" issue by allowing users to enable nanosecond precision on supported filesystems without recompiling Git, while preserving backward compatibility on platforms lacking nanosecond support. Performance benchmarks confirm the v1 regression (~41× slowdown) is fixed, and the architectural constraint introduced by the new `repo_config_values()` call in `is_racy_stat()` is acknowledged but not blocking. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have both reviewed the series favorably, and the only prior feedback—Junio’s rejection of a runtime warning for unsupported platforms—has been addressed in the documentation.

The patch touches `builtin/update-index.c`, `Documentation/config/core.adoc`, and several other files, but the change here is purely textual. The series is ready to advance once v6 is sent, and the trivial conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` in `seen` will require routine resolution if both topics move forward.
