This is patch 9/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s object-database fsck checks to make them pluggable. The patch moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches. It replaces the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and ensures the new implementation respects the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. A new test in `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` verifies these semantics: MIDX checks are skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed and are performed when `--no-full` is used.

The patch removes 18 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 27 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, introducing the `verify_midx()` helper. This helper spawns `git multi-pack-index verify` as a child process, checks the repository’s `core_multi_pack_index` setting, and threads the progress meter flag through the `odb_fsck_options` structure. The implementation is straightforward and mirrors the approach taken in patches 6–8 for packfile, reverse-index, and bitmap verification. No behavior changes are introduced beyond the intentional flag-based skipping of checks.
