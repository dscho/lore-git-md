This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring Git’s `fsck` checks to make them pluggable by ODB backend. It moves bitmap verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches.

The patch removes the `verify_bitmap_files()` call from `cmd_fsck()` and relocates it into `odb_source_packed_fsck()`, the backend-specific verification hook. This ensures bitmap checks now respect the `--connectivity-only` and `--no-full` flags, which they previously ignored. The dedicated `ERROR_BITMAP` bit is replaced with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting. The patch also adapts `verify_bitmap_files()` to operate on a single `odb_source_packed` instance rather than iterating over all sources, since the loop is now handled by the `odb_fsck()` helper introduced earlier in the series.

The diff touches five files: `builtin/fsck.c` (5 lines removed), `odb/source-packed.c` (3 lines added), `pack-bitmap.c` (26 lines changed), `pack-bitmap.h` (function signature updated), and `t/t5326-multi-pack-bitmaps.sh` (10 lines added). The test extension verifies that bitmap checks are skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed and are performed when `--no-full` is used, confirming the new flag-awareness.
