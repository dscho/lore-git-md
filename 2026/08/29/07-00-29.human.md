This is the second patch in Elijah Newren’s four-patch v3 series fixing a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. It plugs a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` that was discovered while writing tests for the core fix.

The leak occurs because `git mktree --batch` reuses its entry buffer across trees but never frees the `treeent` structures from previous trees. Each tree’s entries remain allocated until the next tree overwrites them, and the final tree’s entries are never freed at all. The patch adds a loop to free each entry when resetting the buffer and frees the buffer itself before returning.

The change is tiny (three lines) and self-contained, touching only `builtin/mktree.c`. It is well-motivated by the test coverage added in the series and addresses feedback from Jeff King (Peff), who noted the lack of test coverage for `git mktree --batch` since its introduction in 2009. The fix is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
