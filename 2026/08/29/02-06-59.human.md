This is the seventh patch in Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore`. The patch is a cleanup that improves readability by wrapping overly long lines and replacing manual option-compatibility checks with the `die_for_incompatible_optN()` helper macros. The changes are purely stylistic and do not affect functionality or behavior.

The patch fixes two issues from the original submission: a typo (`opts->new_new_branch_force` instead of `opts->new_branch_force`) and inconsistent option ordering in error messages produced by the new helper macros. These fixes are minor and do not change the patch’s substance. The updated diff wraps long `die()` calls (e.g., for `--unified` requiring `--patch`), replaces manual `if (opts->foo && opts->bar) die(...)` checks with `die_for_incompatible_opt2()` and `die_for_incompatible_opt3()`, and breaks up long `OPT_BOOL()` and `parse_pathspec_file()` argument lists to stay within 80 columns.

This patch depends on the earlier patches in the series, which eliminated the `checkout_main()` choke point and extracted reusable logic into standalone helpers. It is uncontroversial and aligns with Git’s coding guidelines, but its value is primarily aesthetic. The series as a whole remains an early draft for discussion, not yet intended for merging to `seen`. The patch itself is now technically correct and ready for review, though its integration depends on the broader series’ acceptance.
