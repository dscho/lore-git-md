This is a **substantive review** of the third patch in Elijah Newren’s v3 four-patch series, which removes `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` from `git mktree --batch` to fix a race condition where a resident `mktree` process fails to recover objects relocated by a concurrent repack. Jeff King (Peff) endorses the patch’s reasoning and implementation, confirming that the removal of `QUICK` is correct and aligns with the original intent of commit `817b0f602710` (which only aimed to avoid promisor-object fetches, not disable the reprepare-and-retry mechanism).

Peff’s review engages with the patch’s technical rationale, noting that while `QUICK` could theoretically be conditionally applied when `--missing` is used (to optimize for cases where missing objects are expected), this would be an unrelated optimization and is out of scope for the current fix. He also observes that the patch restores the pre-2022 behavior, reinforcing that the `QUICK` flag was an unintended side effect of the earlier commit. The test’s use of `PIPE` to simulate the race is deemed necessary and appropriate, as the deterministic test design requires feeding entries one at a time to trigger the `SECOND_READ` fallback.

The patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated, addressing a real-world issue with minimal changes. Peff’s feedback is purely confirmatory, with no requested changes or unresolved concerns. The series remains **merge-ready**, with this patch specifically praised for its clarity and correctness. Key technical details include the replacement of `QUICK` with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` (preserving promisor-object avoidance while enabling recovery) and the test’s simulation of a concurrent repack by removing a pack’s `.idx` file and creating a replacement pack. No follow-up work is needed beyond the minor error-message tweaks noted in the cover letter.
