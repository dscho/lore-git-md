This is the latest review in Elijah Newren’s four-patch series fixing a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. Jeff King (Peff) reviews the third patch, which removes the `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag from `git mktree --batch` to allow resident `mktree` processes to recover objects relocated by concurrent repacks.

Peff endorses the patch’s reasoning and implementation, confirming that the `QUICK` flag was incorrectly added in a 2022 commit and that replacing it with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` preserves promisor-object avoidance while enabling recovery. He briefly explores whether `QUICK` could be conditionally used with `mktree --missing` but concludes that any such optimization would require a separate `--yolo` option and is out of scope for this fix. The test, which simulates the race by feeding entries one at a time to a resident `mktree` process, is deemed necessary and well-constructed despite its complexity.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused on this patch’s correctness and scope. Peff’s feedback is entirely positive, with no requests for changes or follow-up work beyond the minor error-message tweak already noted in the cover letter. The series remains merge-ready and is already cooking in Junio’s `next` branch.
