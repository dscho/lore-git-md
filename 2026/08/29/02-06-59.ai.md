This is a follow-up to the seventh patch in Junio C Hamano’s 8-patch series refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore`. The patch corrects two issues in the original seventh patch: a typo (`opts->new_new_branch_force` instead of `opts->new_branch_force`) and inconsistent option ordering in error messages produced by the `die_for_incompatible_optN()` helper macros.

The changes are purely stylistic and mechanical, focusing on readability and consistency. The patch wraps long lines in `die()` calls (e.g., for `--unified` requiring `--patch`), replaces manual option-compatibility checks with `die_for_incompatible_opt2()` and `die_for_incompatible_opt3()` macros, and ensures the option names in error messages match the order of the arguments passed to the macros. It also adjusts the formatting of `OPT_BOOL()` and `parse_pathspec_file()` calls to stay within 80 columns. The corrected version touches only `builtin/checkout.c`, changing 54 lines (34 insertions, 20 deletions).

This patch depends on the earlier patches in the series, which eliminated the `checkout_main()` choke point and extracted reusable logic into standalone helpers. The fixes are uncontroversial and align with Git’s coding guidelines, but they do not affect functionality or behavior. The series as a whole remains an early draft for discussion, not yet intended for merging to `seen`. The corrected patch is now ready for review, but its integration depends on the broader series’ acceptance and coordination with other in-flight topics, particularly those touching the `the_repository` removal effort.
