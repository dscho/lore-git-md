This is a **substantive review** from Jeff King (Peff) on Junio C Hamano’s refactoring patch introducing `die_for_incompatible_opts()`. Peff restates his preference for compile-time safety, specifically advocating for `LAST_ARG_MUST_BE_NULL` (a sentinel macro that enforces a `NULL` terminator at compile time) over the `EOF` sentinel adopted in the v2 design. His concern is that the varargs helper, unlike the fixed-arity wrappers, cannot enforce argument correctness at compile time, making it more error-prone. While he acknowledges the patch is low-risk (the helper remains internal and hidden behind static inline wrappers), he frames this as a design trade-off rather than a correctness issue. The feedback is brief but directly engages with the core technical decision of the patch.
