This is the second patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series (v5, 2/3), a no-op refactoring of `repo_config_values_init()`. The patch reorders the assignments in the function to match the declaration order of `struct repo_config_values`, grouping them by config section ("core", "sparse", "branch") for clarity. The change is purely mechanical and introduces no behavioral differences.

The motivation is to make future additions easier to validate: when new fields are added to the struct, the initialization function will already be organized by section, reducing the risk of missed or misplaced assignments. Junio C Hamano’s review of v4 suggested a minor wording tweak to the commit message ("definition of 'struct repo_config_values'" instead of "struct definition"), which the author incorporated in v5. This was the last polish request before the series was marked as applied under its topic name.

The patch touches only `environment.c` and is uncontroversial. It is part of a larger effort to convert the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro into a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`), but this specific change is independent of that goal. The series as a whole is ready to graduate to `master`, and this patch is one of three self-contained improvements that are effectively complete.
