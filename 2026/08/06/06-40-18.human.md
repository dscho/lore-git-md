This is the fifth and final patch in Johannes Sixt's five-part series overhauling the gitk color-preference dialog. It addresses a localization pain point: the current implementation constructs dialog titles from fragments, forcing translators to handle partial strings. The patch replaces these fragments with full, natural-language titles provided directly by the caller.

The change is confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and updates the `choosecolor` procedure and the `coloruielems` list. Each entry in `coloruielems` now supplies a complete title (e.g., "choose background color of old lines") instead of a fragment (e.g., "diff old lines bg"). The `choosecolor` procedure is simplified to use the full title directly. This makes the strings more translator-friendly and improves the user experience by presenting coherent, natural-language dialog titles.

The patch is a logical follow-up to the earlier UI improvements in the series. It does not introduce new behavior or complexity, but rather cleans up an existing implementation detail. The changes are mechanical and well-scoped, fitting neatly into the broader goal of making the color-preference dialog more intuitive and maintainable.

This is a maintainer-driven improvement from Johannes Sixt, the current maintainer of gitk. The series as a whole addresses a clear usability issue and has been structured to make review easy: the first two patches are pure refactorings, the third and fourth implement the major UI changes, and this fifth patch polishes the details. The series looks ready for merging once any minor feedback on wording or styling is addressed.
