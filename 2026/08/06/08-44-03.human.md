This is a refactoring patch in Tian Yuchen’s three-part series on repository configuration handling. The author withdraws the third patch after Junio C Hamano’s feedback. The patch originally proposed reordering the fields in `struct repo_config_values` to match Git’s configuration sections, but Junio pointed out that the change would increase the structure’s size due to padding and questioned the maintainability benefit without an authoritative source of truth to enforce the ordering.

Tian now suggests removing the existing section comments in the structure instead, noting they don’t accurately reflect the current grouping. The discussion remains open on whether this cleanup is worthwhile, but the reordering itself is abandoned. The first two patches in the series are still under review, with patch 1/3 already approved by Junio as a mechanical improvement. This exchange highlights the trade-offs between cosmetic improvements and practical maintainability in low-level refactoring.
