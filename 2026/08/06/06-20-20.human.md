This is a documentation patch series, now in its third iteration (v3), that reorganizes the `git-refs(1)` man page to make a critical ref migration warning more visible. The series consists of two patches: the first moves the warning about ref migration limitations into a `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand synopsis, and the second adds a `linkgit` cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)` for better navigation.

The changes are purely presentational—no code or behavior is altered—and the series is now fully approved and ready for integration. Junio C Hamano explicitly approved the patches in v2, and Patrick Steinhardt, who raised minor stylistic feedback in earlier versions, has now added his `Acked-by` to the first patch in v3. The only update from v2 is the addition of Patrick’s acknowledgment, confirming the series’ readiness.

The motivation is clear: the ref migration warning was previously buried in the prose, making it easy for users to overlook the risks of concurrent writes during migration. Elevating it to a prominent admonition block ensures users see it before attempting the operation. The commit messages are well-explained, and the changes align with existing patterns in other man pages (e.g., `git-clone(1)` and `git-blame(1)`).

This is a small but meaningful improvement to the documentation, with no controversy or technical risk. The series is self-contained and part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s ongoing efforts to clean up and clarify Git’s man pages.
