This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s six-part series making `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic by extending the ODB transaction system. It introduces `odb_transaction_release()` to decouple transaction cleanup from commit, allowing backends to perform deferred operations—like removing ".keep" lockfiles—after references are updated. The change is mechanical: every call site that previously relied on `odb_transaction_commit()` to free the transaction now explicitly calls `release()` afterward. The patch touches nine files, mostly adding the new function and updating existing commit calls, but does not alter behavior yet. This is a preparatory step for the series’ main goal, and the author’s uncertainty about whether `release()` is the right place for deferred cleanup suggests this interface may see further discussion. The patch is clean, well-scoped, and clearly part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.
