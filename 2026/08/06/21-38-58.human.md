This is the fifth patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series making `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic by switching to the ODB transaction system. The patch updates the `unpack()` function to accept an explicit file descriptor for the incoming packfile instead of hard-coding stdin (fd 0). This is a mechanical but necessary step toward the later introduction of `odb_transaction_write_pack()`, which will delegate packfile processing to the ODB backend rather than directly invoking `git index-pack` or `git unpack-objects`.

The change is confined to `builtin/receive-pack.c` and touches only the function signatures and call sites of `unpack()` and its helper `parse_pack_header()`. The diff is small (9 insertions, 7 deletions) and does not alter behavior—it simply lifts the assumption that the packfile is always read from stdin. The patch is clearly motivated by the broader ODB abstraction effort and looks like a clean, uncontroversial refactoring. Reviewers are unlikely to object to the change itself, though they may comment on whether the explicit fd parameter is the most ergonomic way to pass this information through the call chain.
