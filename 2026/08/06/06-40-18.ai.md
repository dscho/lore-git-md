This is the fifth and final patch in Johannes Sixt's five-patch series overhauling the gitk color-preference dialog. The series targets usability improvements in the Tcl/Tk-based UI, and this patch addresses a translation-unfriendly pattern in the color-selection dialog titles.

The change is straightforward: previously, the dialog title was constructed by combining a static prefix ("Gitk: choose color for") with a dynamic suffix provided by the caller (e.g., "background" or "diff old lines"). This made the full title untranslatable as a coherent phrase. The patch replaces this with a full, natural-language title provided directly by the caller (e.g., "choose background color" or "choose text color of old lines"). The `choosecolor` procedure is updated to accept the full title string instead of the dynamic suffix, and all call sites in the `coloruielems` list are updated to supply the new full titles.

The patch touches only `gitk-git/gitk`, changing 12 lines of Tcl/Tk code. There are no new symbols or behavior changes beyond the improved dialog titles. The change is purely mechanical and aligns with the series' broader goal of making the UI more intuitive and translation-friendly. The new titles are clear and descriptive, matching the natural-language labels introduced in the fourth patch.

This is a clean, well-scoped change that completes the series. The patch is unlikely to be controversial, as it fixes a clear technical debt (untranslatable strings) without introducing complexity. The series as a whole is now ready for final review, with all major UI improvements implemented.
