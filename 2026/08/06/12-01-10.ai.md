This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing bug report about `git rebase -x` losing notes when combined with `git commit --amend`. The thread remains focused on the rebase and notes subsystems, specifically the edge case where an external command amends a commit during rebase.

D. Ben Knoble acknowledges Phillip Wood’s explanation of the root cause—notes are rewritten to the pre-amend commit OID rather than the post-amend one—and explores two new potential workarounds. First, he suggests exposing rebase’s internal old-to-new OID mapping to `exec` commands, allowing them to handle note copying (or other rewrites) directly. Second, he proposes a post-rebase mechanism to feed the OID mapping to `git notes copy` or similar commands, avoiding the need to parse `git range-diff` output manually. Neither idea is developed into a concrete proposal, but they introduce alternative directions for addressing the bug without modifying rebase’s internal state.
