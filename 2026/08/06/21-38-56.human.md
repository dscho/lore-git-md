This is the third patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series making `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic by replacing its hardcoded use of `git index-pack` and `git unpack-objects` with a generic ODB transaction interface. This patch lifts global state out of the `unpack()` function by introducing a `struct unpack_opts` that bundles all the previously global variables (`fsck_objects`, `shallow_file`, `max_input_size`, `unpack_limit`, `reject_thin`, `err_fd`, `quiet`, and `fsck_msg_types`) and passes them explicitly.

The change is mechanical and well-scoped: the new `struct unpack_opts` is defined, populated in the caller (`unpack_with_sideband`), and used throughout `unpack()` to replace direct references to globals. The patch does not alter behavior—it is pure refactoring in preparation for the later switch to the ODB transaction interface. The commit message is clear and the diff is straightforward, making it easy to verify that the transformation is correct. This is a solid, uncontroversial step in the ODB abstraction effort.
