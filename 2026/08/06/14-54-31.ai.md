This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable, now revised to implement Toon Claes’s alternative design. The patch abandons the `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag introduced in v3 and instead moves `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()` to make ODB creation an explicit step at every call site. This change is purely mechanical, affecting only the initialization sequence in `repository.c` and `setup.c`.

The patch lifts `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()` and places it in `repo_init()`, `enter_repo()`, `setup_git_directory_gently()`, and a new helper function `create_object_database()` (which replaces `create_object_directory()`). This ensures ODB initialization and on-disk creation are handled together, simplifying the path for the subsequent patch that moves on-disk structure creation into the `struct odb_source` backends. The only exception is `init_db()`, where ODB creation is deferred until `create_object_database()` is called, aligning with the series’ goal of decoupling repository configuration from ODB initialization.

Toon Claes’s review raises two minor points: the docstring in `setup.h` for `apply_repository_format()` is now misleading since `odb_new()` is no longer called within it, and there is a formatting inconsistency in `init_db()` where the `apply_repository_format()` call is split across lines while other call sites remain single-line. Patrick acknowledges both points and plans to address them in a final reroll. Neither issue is a blocker, and the patch remains uncontroversial and ready for merging.

This patch completes the architectural concession to Toon’s alternative design, removing the need for the `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag and ensuring ODB creation is explicit at every call site. The series remains on track for the current development cycle, with the first patch already marked "will merge to 'next'" in Junio’s "What's cooking" report. The changes here are mechanical and do not introduce new behavior, but they are necessary for the subsequent patch that implements the `create_on_disk` callback in the ODB backends.
