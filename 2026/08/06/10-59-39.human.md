The `GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY` gate ensures correctness in the commit-date-ordered region, where clock skew could otherwise cause premature termination. The documentation (`paint-down-to-common.adoc`) is updated to explain the new "side-exhaustion" condition and its interaction with generation ordering.
