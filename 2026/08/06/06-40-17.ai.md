This is the fourth patch in Johannes Sixt’s five-patch series overhauling the gitk color-preference dialog. It replaces the old, abbreviated labels with more natural, descriptive text now that the labels are static (non-clickable) following the UI changes in patch 3. The diff is confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and updates seven labels: “Foreground” becomes “Main text”, “Diff: old lines bg” becomes “Diff: old lines background”, “Diff: hunk header” becomes “Diff: hunk headers”, “Marked line bg” becomes “Marked lines background”, “Select bg” becomes “Selected text background”, and “Link” becomes “Link text”. No behavior changes or new symbols are introduced; this is purely a wording refresh to match the new visual design. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for review.
