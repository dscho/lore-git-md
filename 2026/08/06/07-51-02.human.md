This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making on-disk object database (ODB) structures pluggable, now revised in **v4** to address feedback from Toon Claes. The patch abandons the earlier `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag and instead makes ODB creation an explicit step at every call site by moving `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()`. This change is purely mechanical, affecting only the initialization sequence, and paves the way for the upcoming `create_on_disk` callback in the ODB source interface.

The patch touches `repository.c` and `setup.c`, lifting `odb_new()` into `repo_init()` and three other callers (`enter_repo`, `setup_git_directory_gently`, and `create_object_database`). The latter is renamed from `create_object_directory` to reflect its expanded role, now encapsulating both ODB initialization and on-disk structure creation. This refactoring is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of decoupling backend-specific setup from generic repository configuration. The change is small (11 insertions, 10 deletions) and does not alter behavior, but it simplifies future work by making the initialization sequence more explicit.

This patch is part of the ODB abstraction effort and is ready for merging once the revised design is finalized. The concession to Toon Claes’s alternative approach resolves the architectural discussion, and the remaining patches in the series (including the `create_on_disk` callback implementation) are uncontroversial. The first patch in the series, a correctness fix for loose-object map loading, is already slated for early graduation to `next`.
