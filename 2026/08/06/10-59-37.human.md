This is the sixth patch in the v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds test coverage for clock-skew edge cases that could break the side-exhaustion optimization, along with deterministic step counts for existing and new edge-case tests.

The patch introduces two new clock-skew topologies (`se-*` and `se2-*`) where commit dates violate the topological invariant that children should be processed before parents. In these scenarios, the optimization could fire too early (missing the correct merge base) or return a too-deep result if the algorithm relied on commit-date ordering. The tests verify that generation-number ordering (introduced in earlier patches) prevents these failures. The patch also adds step counts to the `pending-stale` and `infinity-both-sides` tests, exercises a mixed finite/INFINITY generation topology, and validates step counts for the grid-based merge-base test.

The test infrastructure is robust: it uses the `skew_commit` helper to create commits with explicit dates, covers all commit-graph modes (none/full/half/no-gdat), and leverages the `test_paint_down_steps` helper (introduced in patch 5) to assert deterministic step counts. The new topologies are well-documented in the setup code, explaining how clock skew could break date-ordered traversal and why generation ordering fixes it.

This patch is a clear win for correctness. The clock-skew scenarios are realistic (e.g., repositories with import grafts or manual commit-date manipulation), and the tests ensure the optimization remains safe even when dates are unreliable. The step counts provide a measurable baseline for future performance work. No controversies or open questions here—just thorough, well-motivated test coverage.
