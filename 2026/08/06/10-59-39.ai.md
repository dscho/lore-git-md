This is the final patch in the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series, now at v7. It implements the core performance win: early termination of the merge-base walk when one side of the history is exhausted, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories. The patch adds the termination check to `paint_queue_get()` using the per-side counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `mb_candidate_count`) introduced in earlier patches. The optimization only activates in the finite-generation region (where topological ordering holds) and waits for pending merge-base candidates to drain, ensuring correctness.

The documentation update clarifies the new "side-exhaustion" termination condition and explains why the optimization is skipped in the INFINITY region (where commit-date ordering can violate topological guarantees).
