This is the fourth patch in the ready-to-merge v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds regression tests for the side-exhaustion optimization, ensuring the algorithm correctly handles cases where one merge-base candidate is an ancestor of another.

The patch introduces a new test script, `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`, which verifies that `git merge-base --all` excludes redundant ancestor candidates even when the side-exhaustion optimization exits early. The test constructs a diamond-shaped commit graph where two merge-base candidates (B and C) exist, but B is an ancestor of C. The optimization should terminate early when one side is exhausted, but the `remove_redundant` safety net must still filter out C. The test confirms the correct output (only B) across three scenarios: with a commit-graph, without a commit-graph (where generation numbers are `INFINITY` and the optimization does not fire), and with a single merge-base query.
