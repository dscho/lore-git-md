This is a six-patch series from Justin Tobler that extends the ODB (object database) transaction system to support pluggable packfile writes in `git receive-pack`. The goal is to make the command fully backend-agnostic by replacing its direct use of `git index-pack` and `git unpack-objects` (which are hardcoded to the "files" backend) with a new generic transaction interface, `odb_transaction_write_pack()`.

The series starts by adding an `odb_transaction_release()` interface to decouple transaction cleanup from commit, which is later used to defer post-commit cleanup of ".keep" lockfiles until after references are updated. The author acknowledges uncertainty about whether this is the best place for such logic and invites feedback. Most of the series consists of preparatory refactoring in `builtin/receive-pack.c`—passing shallow files and packfile file descriptors explicitly, lifting global state out of the `unpack()` function, and improving error reporting via `strbuf`. The final patch switches `receive-pack` to use the new `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface.

The patches touch `builtin/receive-pack.c`, `odb/transaction.c`, and related headers, with minor changes in `object-file.c` and other builtins for transaction API consistency. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt, and Justin Tobler has been closely involved in that work. The motivation is clear: enabling alternative ODB backends (e.g., reftable, cloud storage) to handle incoming packfiles without being tied to the traditional files backend.

The author’s uncertainty about the transaction lifecycle design (release vs. post-commit) suggests this may be a point of discussion. The refactoring looks mechanical and well-scoped, but the final patch’s switch to the new interface is the key change—reviewers will likely focus on whether the abstraction is sufficiently generic and whether the error handling is robust. The series is under active review and not yet merged.
