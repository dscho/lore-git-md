This is the third patch in Vincent Mailhol’s four-patch v2 series adding Bash completion for the new `git history` command. It adds shell completion for the `--update-refs` option, which accepts either `branches` or `head`. The patch handles both the `--update-refs=<value>` and `--update-refs <value>` forms, matching the behavior of Git’s parse-options parser. The implementation is a straightforward addition to the existing completion logic, using `__gitcomp` to offer the two valid values. The test suite (`t/t9902-completion.sh`) is expanded to verify completion works for both forms and is suppressed after `--` (as expected). This patch is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and uncontroversial; it builds directly on the infrastructure introduced in the earlier patches of the series.
