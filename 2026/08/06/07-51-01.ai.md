This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v4 series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable. It moves the handling of ODB-related environment variables (`GIT_OBJECT_DIRECTORY` and `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES`) from `apply_repository_format()` into `odb_new()` itself, introducing a new `enum odb_new_flags` with a single flag, `ODB_NEW_HONOR_ENV`, to control this behavior.

The change is mechanical and self-contained: `odb_new()` now takes a flags bitmask instead of separate `primary_source` and `secondary_sources` strings, and the caller (`apply_repository_format()`) passes `ODB_NEW_HONOR_ENV` when environment variables should be respected. The patch also removes the now-redundant string duplication and freeing in `apply_repository_format()`, simplifying the call site. A minor cleanup in the unit test (`t/unit-tests/u-odb-inmemory.c`) updates the call to `odb_new()` to pass `0` instead of empty strings.

This refactoring is a prerequisite for the deferred ODB creation logic that appears in the revised fourth patch, and it aligns with the longer-term goal of moving environment variable handling into the ODB backends themselves. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for merging, as confirmed by Justin Tobler’s "looks good to me" in v2 and the absence of new review feedback in v4. No behavioral changes are introduced; the patch is purely preparatory.
