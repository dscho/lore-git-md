This is a surface-level review reply to the first patch in Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch v2 series converting size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series targets 64-bit Windows compatibility, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit and can truncate large object sizes.

Patrick Steinhardt notes that the patch widens the return type of `sizeof_delta_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to match the `size_t` fields in `struct delta_index`, but at least one caller still assigns the result to an `unsigned long`. This is intentional: the patch is a preparatory refactoring that keeps the internal struct consistent, while later patches in the series will update all callers to use `size_t`. The observation is factual and does not request changes; it simply confirms the staged nature of the conversion. The series remains uncontroversial and ready for integration.
