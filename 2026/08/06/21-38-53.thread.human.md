This thread is about Justin Tobler’s six-patch series to make `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic by replacing its hardcoded use of `git index-pack` and `git unpack-objects` with a new ODB transaction interface, `odb_transaction_write_pack()`. The goal is to allow alternative ODB backends (like reftable or cloud storage) to handle incoming packfiles without being tied to the traditional files backend. The series is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s broader ODB abstraction effort and remains under active review at version 1.

The first five patches laid the groundwork by removing global state, introducing explicit parameter passing, and preparing the codebase for the new transaction interface. The sixth and final patch, just posted, completes the switch by moving packfile ingestion logic into `object-file.c` and introducing `odb_transaction_write_pack()`. This patch also bundles previously global unpack options into a self-contained struct and ensures `.keep` lockfiles are managed by the transaction itself, with cleanup handled in the backend’s `release` callback. While the refactoring is mechanical, reviewers will likely focus on the new interface’s design—particularly the transaction lifecycle and error handling—to ensure it’s robust and ergonomic for future backends.

Justin has acknowledged uncertainty about whether `odb_transaction_release()` is the best place for post-commit cleanup, and the series as a whole invites feedback on the abstraction’s scalability and maintainability. No merge status changes have been reported; the series is still cooking in Junio’s `seen` branch or under review. Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB abstraction lead, hasn’t yet weighed in, but his broader effort provides the context for this work.
