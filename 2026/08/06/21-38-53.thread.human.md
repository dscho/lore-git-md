This thread is about the final piece of a nine-patch series (v5) from Justin Tobler that completes the ODB (object database) abstraction for `git receive-pack`. The goal is to make the command fully backend-agnostic by replacing its hardcoded use of `git index-pack` and `git unpack-objects` with a new generic interface, `odb_transaction_write_pack()`. This allows alternative ODB backends—like reftable or cloud storage—to handle incoming packfiles without being tied to the traditional files backend.

The series has been under active review and is now technically complete. All prior feedback has been addressed, including fixes for a lockfile lifecycle regression, enhanced test coverage for ".keep" file migration, removal of global state (like `alt_shallow_file` and unpack limit variables), and explicit passing of file descriptors and unpack options. The final patch introduces the generic interface, moving the unpack logic into the "files" ODB backend while preserving all existing behavior. The series is cooking in Junio’s `seen` branch but hasn’t yet been merged.

Key participants include Justin Tobler (author), Patrick Steinhardt (ODB abstraction lead, who approved the design and implementation), and Junio Hamano (maintainer, who identified critical issues in earlier versions). The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort and depends on prior ODB transaction infrastructure. There are no remaining substantive objections, and the series is ready for final review and merging.
