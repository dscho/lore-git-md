This is the maintainer's reply in the `writev(3p)` wrapper revival series, now nearing finalization. Junio C Hamano accepts Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed implementation tweak: the compatibility wrapper will write only the first `iovec` in the loop, restoring the non-interleaving property of `write(3p)` and avoiding interleaving even on platforms without `writev(2)`. This change directly addresses Johannes Sixt’s forward-compatibility concern about future contributors assuming atomicity, while preserving the performance gains of batching. Junio describes the tweak as "clever" and notes that it simplifies the wrapper by removing the need for overflow checks and most of the loop logic, making the code both safer and cleaner.
