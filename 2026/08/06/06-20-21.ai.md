This is the first patch in a now-approved two-patch documentation series (v3) improving the `git-refs(1)` man page. The patch moves an important warning about ref migration limitations—previously buried in a "KNOWN LIMITATIONS" section at the end of the page—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand description. The change is purely presentational: the warning text is unchanged, but its visibility is significantly improved, addressing a clear usability issue where users could easily overlook critical information about concurrent writes leading to inconsistent migrated states.

The patch touches only `Documentation/git-refs.adoc` and carries Patrick Steinhardt’s `Acked-by`, reflecting his explicit satisfaction with the v2 revision that switched the admonition block from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to align with AsciiDoc’s documented semantics. The rendered output uses a minor indentation (via a `+` continuation line), which the author confirmed is intentional and consistent with existing patterns in other man pages like `git-clone(1)` and `git-blame(1)`. The commit message provides clear rationale: the original placement made sense when `migrate` was the only subcommand, but the man page has since grown to describe nine subcommands, pushing the warning too far down to be easily noticed.

This patch is uncontroversial and technically complete. Junio C Hamano has already approved the series ("Looks good. Thanks."), and it is queued in `seen`, expected to graduate to `next` in the next "What's cooking" cycle. No further discussion or revision is anticipated. The second patch in the series adds a `linkgit` cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)` in the warning block, but this patch stands alone as a meaningful improvement even without it. The series is part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s ongoing documentation cleanup efforts, which focus on small, targeted patches to improve man page clarity and organization.
