This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Sixt (Hannes) overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog to improve usability. The series makes color swatches the primary clickable elements, standardizes button widths, rewrites labels in more natural language, and ensures all UI strings are fully translatable. The first two patches are pure refactorings with no behavior changes, while the third implements clickable swatches (with minor visual tweaks suggested by Mark Levedahl), the fourth updates labels, and the fifth ensures translatability by replacing dynamically constructed dialog titles.

The series is now complete and under review, with all patches confined to `gitk-git/gitk` (Tcl/Tk) and no impact on core Git functionality. The only feedback so far has been surface-level—Levedahl’s observations on visual presentation (button border width and padding) and a brief discussion about phrasing one label ("Diff: old lines background" vs. "Background of old lines in diffs"). No substantive concerns or edge cases have been raised, and the series appears ready for final review. While the patches have not yet received in-depth technical scrutiny, they address a clear usability gap and are structured as maintainer-driven improvements.
