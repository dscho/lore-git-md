This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Sixt (Hannes), the maintainer of gitk, overhauling the color-preference dialog to improve usability. The series is now complete with the fifth patch, which fixes a translation issue in the color-selection dialog titles. Previously, the dialog titles were constructed by combining a static prefix with a dynamic suffix, making them untranslatable as coherent phrases. The new patch replaces this with full, natural-language titles provided directly by the caller, aligning with the series' goal of making the UI more intuitive and translation-friendly.

The first two patches were pure refactorings with no behavior changes, serving as prerequisites for the UI improvements. The third patch made color swatches the primary clickable elements, and the fourth replaced abbreviated labels with more natural, descriptive text. The fifth patch completes the series by addressing the translatability of dialog titles. All changes are confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and involve no new symbols or on-disk format changes. The series is under review, with no feedback yet from other participants. The improvements are self-contained to gitk and should be uncontroversial, as they address clear usability and translation issues without introducing complexity.
