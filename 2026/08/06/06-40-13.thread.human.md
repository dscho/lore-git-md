This thread is about a six-patch series from Johannes Sixt (Hannes) that overhauls gitk’s color-preference dialog to improve usability. The series is now at v2, incorporating surface-level feedback from the first round. The key changes include making color swatches the primary clickable elements (replacing the need to click text labels), standardizing button widths, rewriting labels in more natural language, and ensuring all UI strings are fully translatable. The patches are confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and don’t affect core Git functionality.

The series is under final review, with all substantive feedback addressed. The remaining discussion is purely cosmetic, focusing on label wording (e.g., "Old line background" vs. "Diff: old lines background") and the visual ordering of color options. Mark Levedahl, the most active reviewer, has approved the visual changes in the third patch, noting the new buttons "stand out much better" with the 2-pixel border. Junio C Hamano commented on label phrasing in v1 but hasn’t weighed in on v2. The author, Hannes, considers the series ready for merging, with no open technical concerns.
