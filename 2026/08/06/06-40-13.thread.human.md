This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Sixt (Hannes), the maintainer of gitk, overhauling the color-preference dialog to improve usability. The series aims to make color swatches the primary clickable elements, standardize button widths, rewrite labels in more natural language, and ensure full translatability of all UI strings. All changes are confined to the `gitk-git/gitk` file (Tcl/Tk) and do not affect core Git functionality.

The series is now complete and under review, with no prior versions. The first two patches are pure refactorings with no behavior changes, while the third patch implements clickable color swatches by replacing `ttk::button` widgets with non-themed `button` widgets. The fourth patch updates labels to more natural language (e.g., “Diff: old lines bg” → “Diff: old lines background”), and the fifth ensures full translatability by replacing dynamically constructed dialog titles with full, natural-language titles.

Mark Levedahl provided surface-level feedback on the third patch, noting that the new colored buttons are less visually distinct than the original labels. Johannes has accepted this feedback and proposed a tweak to increase the button border width and add small vertical spacing between buttons. This adjustment is purely cosmetic and does not affect functionality or the series' readiness for merging. No substantive concerns have been raised, and no other participants have reviewed the series yet. The changes remain self-contained to gitk.
