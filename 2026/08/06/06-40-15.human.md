This is the second patch in Johannes Sixt’s five-part series to overhaul gitk’s color-preference dialog. It is a pure refactoring that replaces 60 lines of repetitive Tcl/Tk widget-creation code with two compact `foreach` loops. The patch also cleans up two minor naming inconsistencies: it drops the leftover “sep” suffix from “markbgsep” and “selbgsep” (a copy-paste artifact from “hunksep”) and renames the “but” suffix to the more idiomatic “btn” for button variables.

The change is confined to the `prefspage_colors` and `prefspage_set_colorswatches` procedures in `gitk-git/gitk` and has no user-visible effect. It simply consolidates the existing logic so that later patches in the series can re-layout the dialog without duplicating code. The commit message is clear, the diff is mechanical, and the patch is obviously correct. No controversy is expected.
