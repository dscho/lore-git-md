This is the third patch in Justin Tobler’s six-patch series extending the ODB transaction system to make `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic. The patch mechanically lifts global state out of the `unpack()` function by introducing a `struct unpack_opts` that bundles all previously global variables (`fsck_msg_types`, `shallow_file`, `max_input_size`, `fsck_objects`, `unpack_limit`, `reject_thin`, `err_fd`, and `quiet`) and passes them explicitly. The callers (`unpack()` and `unpack_with_sideband()`) are updated to populate the struct and pass it down, removing direct reliance on globals.

The change is purely preparatory: it does not alter behavior or introduce new interfaces, but it is a necessary step for the later patch that moves packfile writing behind the generic `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface. The refactoring is clean and well-scoped, touching only `builtin/receive-pack.c` and leaving the existing logic intact. Reviewers will likely focus on whether the chosen struct fields and their initialization are ergonomic and whether the explicit parameter passing scales well as the transaction interface evolves. No test failures or edge cases are introduced; the patch is a straightforward mechanical transformation.
