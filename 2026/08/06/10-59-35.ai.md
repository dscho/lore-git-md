This is the fourth patch in the v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds regression tests for the side-exhaustion optimization, focusing on two critical edge cases: (1) multiple merge-base candidates where one is an ancestor of another, and (2) mixed finite/INFINITY generation numbers when one query tip lies outside the commit-graph.

The new test script `t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh` verifies that `git merge-base --all` correctly excludes redundant ancestor candidates even when the side-exhaustion optimization exits early (before STALE propagation removes them). The test constructs a diamond-shaped topology (`A->B->C`, `A->e2->e1->C`, `X->f2->f1->C`, `X->B`) where `B` and `C` are both merge bases of `A` and `X`, but only `B` should appear in the output. The test runs with and without commit-graph to ensure the optimization behaves correctly in both scenarios.

The patch also adds a mixed finite/INFINITY test to `t6600-test-reach.sh` (though the diff here only shows the `t6099` addition), covering the case where one tip has `INFINITY` generation (outside the commit-graph) and the other has a finite generation number. This exercises the region transition logic: the walk starts in the `INFINITY` region (where side-exhaustion is disabled) and crosses into the finite region (where it can fire).

The test infrastructure is straightforward: `t6099` is added to `meson.build`, and the script follows standard Git test conventions (leak sanitizer, `test_commit`, `test_merge`). The topology is clearly documented in the script header, making it easy to understand the expected behavior. This patch is a targeted addition to the series' test coverage, addressing specific correctness concerns raised during review.
