This is the third patch in Vincent Mailhol’s four-part series adding Bash completion for the new `git history` command. It focuses on completing values for the `--update-refs` option, which accepts either `branches` or `head`.

The patch extends the existing completion logic to handle both `--update-refs=<value>` and `--update-refs <value>` forms, matching the behavior of Git’s parse-options parser. It adds a new case in `_git_history()` to complete the allowed values and updates the test suite (`t/t9902-completion.sh`) to verify the behavior across different subcommands and argument positions. The implementation is straightforward and follows the established pattern from earlier patches in the series.
