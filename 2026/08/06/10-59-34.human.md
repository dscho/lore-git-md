This is a test patch, part 3 of the 10-patch v7 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` in Git’s commit-reach subsystem. The series is queued in Junio’s tree and ready for additional review, with this patch adding edge-case test coverage for the side-exhaustion optimization.

The patch extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with four new test cases that exercise subtle but important scenarios in the `paint_down_to_common()` algorithm. The first case (`in_merge_bases_many:self`) verifies correct handling when a commit is both the query target and one of the inputs. The second (`get_merge_bases_many:duplicate-twos`) tests duplicate entries in the input list, ensuring the algorithm remains stable under redundant inputs. The third (`get_merge_bases_many:pending-stale`) constructs a diamond-shaped topology to trigger the `(PARENT1|PARENT2) -> (PARENT1|PARENT2|STALE)` transition, a critical path for correctness when merge-base candidates are descendants of other candidates. The fourth (`get_merge_bases_many:infinity-both-sides`) builds a commit history entirely outside the commit-graph with non-monotonic dates, exercising the fallback to commit-date ordering when generation numbers are `INFINITY`.

The test topologies are carefully designed to isolate specific edge cases. The `ps-*` diamond (with commits `ps-X`, `ps-Z`, `ps-W`, `ps-B`, `ps-T1`, and `ps-T2`) ensures the STALE transition occurs while commits are still pending in the queue. The `pi-*` topology (with commits `pi-X`, `pi-D`, `pi-C`, `pi-B`, and `pi-P`) forces the algorithm to rely on commit-date ordering due to `INFINITY` generation numbers, while deliberately skewing dates to test robustness. Both topologies are constructed outside the commit-graph to avoid interference from cached generation numbers.
