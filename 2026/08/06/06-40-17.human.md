This is the fourth patch in Johannes Sixt’s five-part series overhauling the gitk color-preference dialog. It replaces the old, abbreviated labels with more natural, descriptive language now that the labels are static text rather than clickable buttons. For example, “Foreground” becomes “Main text”, “Diff: old lines bg” becomes “Diff: old lines background”, and “Select bg” becomes “Selected text background”. The changes are confined to the same Tcl/Tk file (`gitk-git/gitk`) and are purely textual—no layout or behavior changes. The patch is small, clean, and directly addresses the usability goal of the series. No controversy is expected; this is a straightforward improvement that should merge smoothly once the earlier patches in the series are reviewed.
