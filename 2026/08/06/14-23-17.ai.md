This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable, now revised to implement Toon Claes’s alternative design. The patch abandons the `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag introduced in v3 and instead moves `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()` to make ODB creation an explicit step at every call site. This change is purely mechanical, affecting only the initialization sequence in `repository.c` and `setup.c`, and directly reverts the refactoring introduced in the original fourth patch.

The patch lifts `odb_new()` out of `apply_repository_format()` and places it in `repo_init()`, `enter_repo()`, `setup_git_directory_gently()`, and a new helper function `create_object_database()` (which replaces `create_object_directory()`). This ensures that ODB initialization and on-disk creation are now handled together, simplifying the path for the subsequent patch that moves on-disk structure creation into the `struct odb_source` backends. The only exception is `init_db()`, where ODB creation is deferred until `create_object_database()` is called, aligning with the series’ goal of decoupling repository configuration from ODB initialization.

Toon Claes’s review raises two minor points: the docstring in `setup.h` for `apply_repository_format()` is now misleading since `odb_new()` is no longer called within it, and a formatting inconsistency in `init_db()` where the `apply_repository_format()` call is split across lines while other call sites remain single-line. Neither is a blocker, but the docstring should eventually be updated to reflect the new behavior. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for merging, completing the architectural concession to Toon’s alternative design. The series remains on track for the current development cycle, with the first patch already marked "will merge to 'next'" in Junio’s "What's cooking" report.
