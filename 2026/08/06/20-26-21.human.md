This is the maintainer’s final technical approval for the revived `writev(3p)` wrapper series. Junio C Hamano accepts Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to restore the non-interleaving property in the compatibility wrapper by writing only the first `iovec` in each loop iteration. This change eliminates the need for explicit documentation about non-atomic emulation, simplifies the wrapper implementation (removing the overflow check and most of the loop logic), and ensures the API behaves consistently across platforms. The series is now technically complete; the only remaining step is a reroll that incorporates this one-line tweak. No further objections or open questions remain, and the series appears headed for `next` once the reroll is posted.
