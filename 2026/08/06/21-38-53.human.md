This is the cover letter for a six-patch series from Justin Tobler that extends Git’s ODB (object database) transaction system to support pluggable packfile writes in `git receive-pack`. The goal is to decouple the command from the “files” backend by introducing a new `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface, allowing alternative ODB backends to handle incoming packfiles without relying on `git index-pack` or `git unpack-objects`. The series also adds an `odb_transaction_release()` call to clean up lockfiles after reference updates, though Justin flags this design choice as tentative and invites feedback. Most of the patches are preparatory refactoring; the final patch switches `receive-pack` to the new interface. The work is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.
