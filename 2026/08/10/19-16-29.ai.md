This is a reply in the ongoing ODB abstraction series, specifically addressing Junio Hamano’s review of patch 3/7 ("builtin/receive-pack: read unpack limit config lazily"). The patch is part of Justin Tobler’s six-patch v2 series extending the ODB transaction system to make `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic by replacing hardcoded packfile ingestion with a new `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface.

Justin agrees with Junio’s critique that the `static int` caching in `get_unpack_limit()` is functionally equivalent to a global variable and unnecessary in practice, since the unpack limit is only checked once per `receive-pack` invocation. He references Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier feedback (linked in the email) and signals his intent to adopt a simpler, non-caching version that directly reads the configuration via `repo_config_get_uint()` and defaults to 100. This change will remove the `static int` and align the implementation with the broader ODB abstraction effort’s goal of avoiding global state.

The reply does not address Junio’s suggestion to store the unpack limit as a per-repository setting in `repo->settings` or `repo->config_values`, leaving that design decision open for the next iteration. The focus here is on the immediate mechanical simplification, which is uncontroversial and directly responsive to the review. The patch remains under active development, with the next version (v3) expected to incorporate this change alongside other feedback on transaction lifecycle, lockfile migration, and backend design flaws.
