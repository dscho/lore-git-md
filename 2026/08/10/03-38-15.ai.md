This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series (`[PATCH v2 1/7] odb/transaction: add transaction finalize interface`). The patch introduces `odb_transaction_finalize()` to decouple transaction cleanup from commit, enabling backend-specific deferred operations like removing ".keep" lockfiles.

Junio Hamano’s review raises two technical concerns about the patch’s implementation. First, he notes that `odb_transaction_finalize()` is called only at the end of `cmd_add()` and other functions, but there are non-local exits (e.g., `exit(128)` after `report_path_error()`) that bypass the cleanup. He suggests restructuring the code to ensure `finalize()` is always invoked, even on error paths, for conceptual clarity and robustness. Second, he observes that no callers check the return value of `finalize()`, questioning whether it should return `void` instead of `int`. The same question applies to `odb_transaction_commit()`, which also goes unchecked by callers.

The review is narrowly focused on the patch’s mechanical correctness and interface design, but the implications are significant for the series’ broader goal of making `receive-pack` backend-agnostic. The `finalize()` interface is critical for the "files" backend’s deferred cleanup of ".keep" lockfiles, and its robustness will be scrutinized as the series progresses. Junio’s feedback does not challenge the patch’s direction but highlights areas where the implementation could be made more robust or ergonomic. The author will need to address these points in a future iteration, particularly if the series is to graduate from `seen` to `next`.
