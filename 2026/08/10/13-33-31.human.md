This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part refactoring series targeting the object database (ODB) subsystem. The series as a whole removes lazy-loading of alternate object databases, simplifying the codebase by initializing all alternates upfront during ODB setup.

This specific patch removes the now-unused `alternates_db` field from the `struct object_database`. The field previously stored the value of the `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES` environment variable, but with lazy-loading eliminated in earlier patches, the value is now passed directly to `odb_prepare_alternates()` via `odb_new()`. The patch makes the necessary adjustments to pass the alternate database path as a parameter and cleans up the redundant field, reducing the struct’s footprint and removing a now-superfluous `free()` call in `odb_free()`.

The change is mechanical and follows logically from the architectural shift in patch 2, which centralized alternate initialization. It touches only `odb.c` and `odb.h`, removing 7 lines and adding 7 (a net zero change, but with fewer fields to manage). The patch is well-motivated and uncontroversial, completing the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB code. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the series as a whole appears ready for review, with the main open question being the performance implications of eager alternate loading in edge cases.
