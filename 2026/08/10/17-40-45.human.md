This is the fifth patch in Siddharth Shrimali’s seven-patch v4 series introducing `git repack --drop-filtered` for partial clones. It moves from dry-run mode to real object removal: when `--drop-filtered` is given without `--dry-run`, the enumerated promisor blobs are actually dropped by rebuilding the promisor pack while excluding them, and the old packs containing those blobs are deleted (implied `-d`). The repack machinery’s existing write-before-delete and fsync guarantees ensure crash safety. The test script gains a new case that verifies a large promisor blob is absent after the command runs, while a smaller one remains. The change is minimal and backward-compatible; all existing callers of `repack_promisor_objects()` continue to pass `NULL` for the new `to_drop` parameter, so their behavior is unchanged. This patch is production-ready and integrates smoothly with the rest of the series.
