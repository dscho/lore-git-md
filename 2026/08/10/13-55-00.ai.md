This is a **bugfix discussion** targeting the geometric repacking subsystem, specifically addressing two critical issues raised by Stefan Haller: the overly aggressive `geometric-repack.auto` threshold and concurrent operation failures during background maintenance. The email is a reply from Patrick Steinhardt proposing a fix for the first issue and seeking confirmation on the second.

Patrick acknowledges the root cause of the auto-condition bug: the `too_many_loose_objects()` scaling logic, inherited from `git gc`, collapses thresholds below 256 to a single fanout directory check, making small repositories trigger repacks far too early. His proposed fix raises the default `maintenance.geometric-repack.auto` value from 100 to 6700 (matching `gc.auto`), along with a documentation update. This change would reduce the frequency of background repacks in small repositories, mitigating both the auto-condition bug and the concurrent operation failures, though the latter remains a preexisting issue in the repack/prune machinery.

The email also clarifies that the concurrency problem—where `prune_packed_objects()` removes fanout directories while concurrent writers attempt to create temporary files—is not new but is now surfacing more frequently due to the more aggressive repacking introduced in Git 2.54.0. Patrick suggests that adjusting the auto threshold may resolve the observed failures, but the underlying race condition in the repack/prune logic remains unaddressed. The discussion is now focused on validating whether the threshold change alone is sufficient to mask the concurrency issue in practice.

This is a high-priority thread for the maintenance subsystem, as the issues involve data corruption risks during routine operations. The proposed fix is minimal and targeted, but the concurrency problem may require further hardening of the repack/prune machinery.
