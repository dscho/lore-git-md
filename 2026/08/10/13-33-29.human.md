This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part refactoring series targeting the object database (ODB) subsystem. It removes lazy-loading of alternate object databases by eagerly initializing them during ODB setup, eliminating scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` across the codebase.

The patch deletes 42 lines of code (46 deletions, 4 insertions) from 11 files, including `builtin/fsck.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `commit-graph.c`, and several ODB internals. The core change moves the `odb_prepare_alternates()` call into `odb_new()` and `odb_prepare()`, ensuring alternates are loaded upfront rather than on-demand. The function is also made `static` since it’s no longer needed outside `odb.c`.

This is a clean, well-motivated refactoring that aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB code. The commit message clearly explains why lazy-loading is no longer useful—most commands access the ODB anyway—and how the preceding patch (1/4) resolved the historical blocker (case-sensitivity handling). The patch itself is mechanical and unlikely to introduce behavioral changes, though it may affect performance in edge cases (e.g., repositories with many alternates). No objections have been raised yet, and the series appears on track for review.
