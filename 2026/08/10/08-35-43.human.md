This is a follow-up in the geometric repacking series, focusing on a concurrency bug that surfaced during review. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges Stefan Haller’s report of "invalid object" errors in lazygit’s test suite as a legitimate bug rather than a test artifact. The errors occur during basic Git operations (`git add`, `git commit`) when background maintenance runs concurrently, suggesting the auto-detach mechanism may not be robust enough for real-world use.

Patrick asks for a reproducer and clarifies that a related race condition—multiple concurrent `git maintenance` processes—was already fixed, but the fix may not have reached Git 2.54. This exchange elevates the issue from a test-suite nuisance to a potential usability problem, though no solution is proposed yet. The series itself remains technically ready for merging, but this bug could delay integration if it proves widespread or hard to fix. The request for a reproducer is the next step toward resolution.
