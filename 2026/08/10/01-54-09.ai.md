This is Junio C Hamano’s review of the final patch (7/7) in Justin Tobler’s v2 series making `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic for packfile writes via the ODB transaction system. The review is substantive: it identifies a potential design flaw in the "files" backend implementation of `odb_transaction_write_pack()`.

Junio points out that `odb_transaction_files_write_pack()` hardcodes the first entry in `repo->objects->sources` (`repo->objects->sources[0]`) when calling `odb_source_prepare()`. This assumption is problematic because the transaction (`base`) is already associated with a specific ODB source (`base->source`), which may not be the first one. The correct behavior would be to use `base->source` instead, ensuring the transaction operates on the intended ODB backend rather than defaulting to the first one. This oversight could lead to subtle bugs if multiple ODB backends are in use, as the transaction might prepare the wrong source.

The review is narrowly focused on this single line of code but carries broader implications for the transaction system’s design. The fix is straightforward (replace `repo->objects->sources` with `base->source`), but the issue underscores the importance of respecting the transaction’s source context, especially as the ODB abstraction effort matures and multiple backends become viable. No other aspects of the patch are commented on, and the review does not address the series’ overall direction or the ergonomics of the new `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface. The tone is matter-of-fact, framing the observation as a question rather than a critique.
