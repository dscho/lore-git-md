This is a gentle nudge from Paulius Zaleckas asking for review of his v6 patch series that makes submodule fetch errors configurable. The series adds `fetch.submoduleErrors` (config) and `--submodule-errors=(fail|warn)` (CLI) so users can choose whether `git fetch --recurse-submodules` fails hard or merely warns when a submodule’s gitlink points to an unreachable OID. The default remains `fail` for backwards compatibility, so existing workflows are unaffected. Patch 1 fixes a pre-existing logic flaw in submodule fetch error handling, and Patch 2 wires up the user-facing controls. The implementation touches `submodule.c`, `builtin/fetch.c`, documentation, and adds thorough test coverage in `t/t5526-fetch-submodules.sh`.

This looks like a clean, well-scoped improvement that solves a real workflow pain point—upstream topic branches that are still in flux—without introducing broader risks. The code is ready for `next`; it just needs a final review to move forward.
