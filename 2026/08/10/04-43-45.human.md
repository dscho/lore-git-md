This is a review comment on Patrick Steinhardt's geometric repacking series, specifically questioning the test-only nature of the auto-detach override.

Stefan Haller raises a practical concern about the patch's scope. While the series introduces `GIT_TEST_MAINT_AUTO_DETACH` to prevent background maintenance from interfering with tests, Stefan points out that the same race conditions could affect real-world scripts and tools like lazygit. His workaround for lazygit's test suite was to disable auto-maintenance entirely, suggesting the problem isn't confined to test environments.

The comment is substantive: it challenges the assumption that background maintenance races are only a testing issue. Stefan provides concrete evidence from lazygit's integration tests (which mimic normal Git usage) and links to the specific workaround. This isn't a surface-level nit—it questions whether the patch addresses the root cause or just papers over it for tests. The observation is particularly relevant given the series' goal of changing default maintenance behavior, which could make such races more frequent in practice.
