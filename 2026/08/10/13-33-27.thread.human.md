This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series (v3) refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The goal is to simplify the code by initializing all alternates upfront during ODB setup, reducing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and enabling future work on the ODB backend. The series has evolved significantly since v2, with a new first patch addressing a correctness issue with `core.ignoreCase` lookups by reordering repository initialization, and a revised second patch migrating from `khash` to `hashmap` while retaining (but documenting as `NEEDSWORK`) case-insensitive deduplication.

The series is currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch as of July 10, though the v3 revision may require re-queuing. The latest patch (4/5) removes the now-redundant `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`, a mechanical cleanup following the eager-loading logic introduced in patch 3/5. No user-visible changes are intended, and the patch is uncontroversial given the prior work. Key reviewers—Jeff King (Peff), Justin Tobler, and Karthik Nayak—have engaged with earlier versions, with Peff’s critique of the case-insensitivity logic shaping the current `NEEDSWORK` note in patch 2/5. Junio has not yet responded to the v3 revision or Patrick’s procedural question about merge strategy. Open questions include the future of case-insensitive deduplication and the performance implications of eager alternate loading, though neither has drawn new feedback in this iteration. The series remains part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort.
