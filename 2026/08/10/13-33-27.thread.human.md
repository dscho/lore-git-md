This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series to refactor Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem by removing lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The goal is to simplify the codebase by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, reducing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and paving the way for future work to move alternates into the "files" backend. The series is now at version 3 and consists of five patches, with the first patch addressing a correctness issue around `core.ignoreCase` lookups by reordering repository initialization.

The series has made progress but is currently blocked by a technical constraint: the first patch’s approach to fixing the `core.ignoreCase` timing issue cannot use `repo_ignore_case()` because it relies on `repo_config_values()`, which is hardcoded to `the_repository` and would break submodule support. This limitation was identified by Jeff King (Peff), who had previously argued for dropping case-insensitive deduplication entirely, a position reflected in the `NEEDSWORK` note in patch 2/5. While the series is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, its integration is on hold until the `core.ignoreCase` issue is resolved. Junio has not yet responded to the v3 revision or the procedural question Patrick raised about merge strategy.

Key participants include Patrick (the author), Peff (who raised the blocking constraint and prefers simplifying the design), and Karthik Nayak (who provided surface-level reviews of v2 and v3, declaring the series ready for integration without engaging with the open technical issues). Justin Tobler’s earlier reviews were substantive but focused on prior versions, and he has not weighed in on the current blocker. The series touches 14 files, primarily in the `odb/` subsystem, and introduces no intended user-visible changes, though it may slightly increase startup time for commands that previously avoided loading alternates. The unresolved `core.ignoreCase` issue remains the primary obstacle to merging.
