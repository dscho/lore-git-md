This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem. The goal is to simplify the code by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, reducing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and enabling future work on the ODB backend. The series is currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, but its integration is on hold due to an unresolved design discussion about the first patch.

The key point of contention is whether the added complexity for case-insensitive deduplication of alternate paths is justified. Jeff King (Peff) and Junio Hamano now agree that the patch should be simplified by dropping case-insensitivity entirely, accepting occasional duplicates as a low-risk trade-off. Peff argues that the risk of data loss from duplicates is minimal and would require deliberate misconfiguration, while Junio raises concerns about platform-specific filesystem behaviors (e.g., macOS Unicode normalization) that could complicate `stat()`-based deduplication. The current implementation remains in place, but consensus has shifted toward revisiting the design before integration. Patrick has not yet responded to this latest discussion.

The series also centralizes alternate initialization in `odb_new()` and `odb_prepare()`, removing 13 scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()`. While this change is uncontroversial, reviewers have not engaged with its performance implications, particularly for commands like `git fsck` that previously avoided loading alternates when none were present. The remaining patches are mechanical cleanups, removing unused fields from the ODB structure. Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak reviewed the series, with Tobler providing substantive feedback on the first patch and surface-level comments on the rest. The series builds on Patrick’s prior ODB refactoring work and is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.
