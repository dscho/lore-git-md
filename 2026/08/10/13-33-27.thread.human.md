This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem. The goal is to simplify the code by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, rather than deferring their loading until needed. This refactoring is part of a broader effort to abstract the ODB and enable future work, such as moving alternates into the "files" backend.

The series has evolved through three versions, with the latest (v3) addressing key design and timing concerns raised during review. The most contentious issue has been the handling of case-insensitive path comparisons for alternate ODB sources, which relies on `core.ignoreCase`. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, initially preferred dropping this mechanism entirely for simplicity, but the latest discussion confirms it will be retained as a "best effort" optimization for platforms with case-insensitive filesystems. This decision prioritizes backward compatibility and practical reliability over theoretical edge cases, such as repositories spanning filesystems with mixed case sensitivity. The mechanism is now documented as `NEEDSWORK` to signal it may be revisited in the future.

The series is currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, though the unresolved constraints in the first patch—specifically, the `repo_config_values()` limitation and Junio’s broader design concerns about `core.ignoreCase`—may still delay integration. The latest email resolves the last technical blocker for patch 2/5, which migrates the deduplication mechanism from `khash` to `hashmap` and retains case-insensitive comparisons. Junio now accepts that the timing of repository initialization in `odb_source_paths_cmp()` is safe, as `odb_new()` ensures `o->repo` is set early. The remaining patches (3–5/5) are mechanical cleanups with no behavioral impact.

Key participants include Patrick Steinhardt (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Jeff King (Peff), Justin Tobler, and Karthik Nayak. Peff’s reviews have been substantive, focusing on correctness and design trade-offs, while Justin and Karthik’s feedback has been more surface-level. The series touches 14 files, primarily in the `odb/` subsystem, and removes 16 lines of code overall. No user-visible changes are intended, but the removal of lazy-loading may affect performance in edge cases, such as repositories with thousands of alternates. The unresolved issues in patch 1/5 remain the primary open question, though they are orthogonal to the rest of the series.
