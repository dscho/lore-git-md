This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem, simplifying the codebase and enabling future refactoring. The series, now at v2, is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch but remains under review, with one key design question still unresolved.

The core change centralizes alternate ODB initialization during primary ODB setup, eliminating scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()`. The first patch decouples path comparisons from `the_repository` to fix inconsistent case-sensitivity handling, but Jeff King (Peff) has raised a substantive critique: using `core.ignoreCase` as a proxy for alternate path comparisons is flawed, as it reflects the repository’s filesystem, not the alternate’s. Peff’s concern—highlighting a potential edge case where a repository on a case-insensitive filesystem might incorrectly suppress a non-duplicate alternate—remains unaddressed, though Patrick has defended the current approach as necessary for correctness. The other patches are mechanical cleanups, with minor feedback resolved in v2.

Reviews have been mixed in depth. Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak provided mostly surface-level feedback, focusing on commit message clarity and mechanical correctness, while Peff’s critique challenges the fundamental design of the first patch. Patrick has rejected one stylistic suggestion from Karthik but has not yet responded to Peff’s broader concerns. Junio queued the series in `seen` after resolving a minor merge conflict, but Patrick has since questioned the merge strategy; no resolution has been reached. The series is otherwise ready for integration, pending Patrick’s verification of the merge result and any response to Peff’s critique.
