This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem. The goal is to simplify the code by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, reducing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and enabling future work on the ODB abstraction effort. The series is now in its second version, updated based on feedback from Justin Tobler, who reviewed all patches in v1 and raised only minor clarifications—all addressed in v2.

Junio C Hamano has queued the series in his integration branch, but a minor merge conflict arose during integration, likely due to overlap with Patrick’s earlier `ps/odb-make-creation-pluggable` series. Junio has asked Patrick to verify the synthesized merge result once it’s pushed. This is a routine step and doesn’t indicate any issues with the patches themselves. The series remains under review with no outstanding objections or requests for changes. All technical feedback from Justin Tobler was resolved in v2, including a clarifying question about `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES` handling, which Patrick addressed with an added comment.

The key changes include decoupling path comparisons from `the_repository` to fix case-sensitivity inconsistencies, eagerly initializing alternates during ODB setup, and removing now-unused fields (`loaded_alternates` and `alternates_db`). The series touches 12 files, primarily in the `odb/` directory, and results in a net reduction of 16 lines. While the performance implications of eager alternate loading (e.g., for commands like `git fsck`) remain unaddressed, the commit message frames this as a low-risk trade-off. The series is part of Patrick’s broader ODB refactoring work and depends on his prior `ps/odb-make-creation-pluggable` series.
