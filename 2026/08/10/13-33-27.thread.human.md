Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem has now reached its fifth and final iteration in v3. The series, queued in Junio’s `seen` branch since July 10, simplifies the codebase by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, eliminating scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` across 11 files. The latest revision addresses earlier concerns about timing and correctness: the new first patch reorders repository initialization to ensure `core.ignoreCase` is set before ODB creation, fixing a potential lookup issue, while the second patch retains case-insensitive deduplication but documents it as `NEEDSWORK` to signal it may be removed later.

The final patch in the series, just posted, mechanically removes the now-redundant `alternates_db` field from `struct object_database`, passing the environment variable directly to `odb_prepare_alternates()` instead. This cleanup, which reduces code complexity by 6 lines, is uncontroversial and follows from the earlier patches that already eliminated lazy-loading. Key reviewers—Jeff King (Peff), Justin Tobler, and Karthik Nayak—have engaged with the series at various stages, with Peff’s critique of case-insensitive deduplication shaping the `NEEDSWORK` note in patch 2. Junio has not yet responded to the v3 revision, but the series appears technically ready for integration, pending any new feedback. The broader goal remains enabling future work to move alternates into the "files" backend as part of Patrick’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort.
