This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem. The goal is to simplify the code by initializing all alternate ODBs upfront during primary ODB setup, reducing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and enabling future work on the ODB abstraction effort. The series is currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch as of July 10, 2024, pending Patrick’s verification of a minor merge conflict resolution.

The key changes involve decoupling path comparisons for alternate ODB sources from `the_repository` (patch 1), eagerly initializing alternates during ODB setup (patch 2), and removing now-unused fields from the ODB structure (patches 3 and 4). Justin Tobler reviewed the series in its first version, engaging substantively with patch 1’s technical rationale and raising minor clarifications for patches 2 and 4, all of which Patrick addressed in v2. Junio integrated the series with a routine conflict resolution but requested Patrick verify the result, as the merge strategy differed from Patrick’s expectations—Junio merged the dependency into the feature branch rather than using it as the base. This procedural exchange doesn’t affect the series’ content or status, though it may prompt a rebase if Junio adjusts the merge.

No objections or open technical questions remain, though the performance implications of eager alternate loading (e.g., for `git fsck`) and edge-case handling (e.g., repositories with many alternates) haven’t been deeply explored in reviews. The series is part of Patrick’s broader ODB refactoring work and depends on his earlier `ps/odb-make-creation-pluggable` series. The next step is for Patrick to confirm the merge result, after which the series will likely progress to `next` for further integration testing.
