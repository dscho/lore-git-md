This is the final review reply in the `git history` Bash completion series. D. Ben Knoble responds to Vincent Mailhol’s v2 patch that adds pathspec completion for the `split` subcommand.

Knoble’s feedback is stylistic rather than functional: he notes that the placement of the `split` case branch — after revision completion and before the final `;;` — diverges from the pattern used in other Git completion functions like `_git_notes` and `_git_reflog`, which dispatch on `subcommand,$cur` combinations earlier in the logic. He acknowledges that the current placement achieves the desired behavior (completing revisions first, then pathspecs), but suggests that aligning with the broader codebase’s style might improve consistency.

The review is surface-level: it focuses on code organization rather than correctness, and Knoble explicitly states he is unsure which approach is better. No technical concerns are raised; the patch itself is functionally sound and well-tested. The feedback is a minor suggestion for future consistency, not a blocker.
