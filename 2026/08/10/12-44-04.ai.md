This is a substantive review reply in the ongoing `core.useNanosec` series. Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on the struct choice for caching the runtime config value, confirming that `repo_config_values()` is the modern target for config-driven settings but noting its current limitation to `the_repository`. He raises the open question of whether `core.useNanosec` will ever need to vary per-repository (e.g., for submodules), which would argue against using `repo_config_values()` and in favor of `repo_settings` instead. The reply is brief but directly addresses the architectural decision the author faces for v2, leaving the door open for further discussion on submodule use cases. No performance or correctness concerns are added; the focus is purely on where to store the cached value.
