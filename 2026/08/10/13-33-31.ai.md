This is the fourth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. It is a mechanical cleanup patch that removes the now-unused `alternates_db` field from the `struct object_database`.

The patch eliminates the `alternate_db` field, which previously stored the value of the `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES` environment variable. Since the series’ second patch already centralized and eagerized alternate ODB initialization in `odb_new()`, this field is no longer needed. The patch updates `odb_prepare_alternates()` to accept the alternate database path as a parameter (passed via `odb_new()`) instead of reading it from the ODB struct. It also removes the now-redundant freeing of `o->alternate_db` in `odb_free()` and updates the call to `odb_prepare_alternates()` in `odb_prepare()` to pass `NULL` when flushing caches (since the field no longer exists).

The changes are confined to `odb.c` and `odb.h`, removing 7 lines and adding 2 for a net reduction of 5 lines. This patch is a straightforward follow-up to the architectural shift introduced in patch 2, completing the removal of lazy-loading infrastructure. No behavioral changes are intended, and the patch is unlikely to be controversial. It builds directly on the prior patches in the series, particularly the eager initialization logic introduced earlier.
