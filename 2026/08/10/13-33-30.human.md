This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, and this specific patch is a straightforward cleanup.

The patch removes the `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`, which previously tracked whether alternates had been loaded. Since the preceding patch (2/4) made alternate loading eager—happening during `odb_new()`—this field is now redundant. The change is minimal: nine lines removed across `odb.c` and `odb.h`, with a single line added to ensure alternates are always processed when added to the alternates file.

This is a mechanical follow-up to patch 2/4, with no behavioral changes or edge cases introduced. The patch is well-motivated and uncontroversial, serving as a tidy removal of dead code. If the series lands as-is, this patch will likely be merged without further discussion. For readers tracking the ODB refactoring, this is a minor but necessary step toward simplifying the codebase.
