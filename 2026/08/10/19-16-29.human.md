This is a brief reply in the ongoing review of Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series, specifically addressing Junio Hamano’s feedback on patch 3/7. The patch removes the global `unpack_limit` variable in favor of a lazy, per-repository lookup via `get_unpack_limit()`.

Justin agrees with Junio’s observation that the current implementation—using a `static int` inside `get_unpack_limit()`—is functionally equivalent to a global variable and thus not ideal for a codebase moving toward explicit per-repository state. He also references Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier point that the static variable is unnecessary in practice, since `receive-pack` only checks the unpack limit once per invocation. The implication is that the next version of the patch will likely drop the caching entirely and simply read the configuration on demand, which would address both reviewers’ concerns without requiring deeper structural changes (like adding a new field to `repo->settings`).

The reply is a small but meaningful step in the series’ evolution: it confirms the author’s intent to simplify the implementation while leaving the door open for future per-repository storage if the ODB abstraction effort demands it. The series remains under review, with the next iteration expected to focus on mechanical refinements and the broader transaction lifecycle issues raised in other patches.
