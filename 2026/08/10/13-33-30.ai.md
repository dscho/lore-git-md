This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.

The patch removes the `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`, which previously tracked whether alternates had been loaded. Since the preceding patch (2/4) made alternate loading eager—happening during `odb_new()`—this field is now redundant. The change is mechanical: the field is deleted, and the single remaining check (`if (odb->loaded_alternates)`) is replaced with unconditional execution of `odb_add_alternate_recursively()`. The patch also removes the now-unnecessary reset of `loaded_alternates` in `odb_prepare()` when `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` is set.

The diff is small (9 lines removed, 1 added) and confined to `odb.c` and `odb.h`. No behavioral changes are intended, and the patch is a straightforward cleanup following the architectural shift in patch 2. This is a low-risk, high-value removal that reduces state tracking in the ODB structure. The series as a whole is well-motivated, and this patch is a natural follow-up to the eager-loading change.
