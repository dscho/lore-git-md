This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Taylor Blau’s two-patch bugfix series for `git maintenance`’s geometric repack task. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the problem (unnecessarily frequent all-into-one repacks when non-promisor packs are small) and accepts the proposed fix—checking both ordinary and promisor pack progressions before falling back to all-into-one. He notes a potential edge case where the fix might *under*-trigger all-into-one repacks (if the combined pack set is large but neither progression alone crosses the threshold), but considers it a minor concern for now, deferring any more complex logic to future work. The review is concise, technically grounded, and signals no objections to the current approach.
