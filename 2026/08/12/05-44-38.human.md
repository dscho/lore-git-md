This is a **bugfix** patch addressing an overly aggressive geometric repacking threshold in Git's auto-maintenance. The discussion here is a brief follow-up in the review thread, where Patrick Steinhardt clarifies the rationale for his patch.

The patch raises the default threshold for loose-object repacking from 100 to 6700, matching the long-standing default in `git gc`. The motivation is to reduce the frequency of repacks in high-concurrency repositories, where the current low threshold (100) can trigger repacks too often, leading to race conditions with loose-object pruning. The fix is narrowly scoped: it changes only a configuration default in `odb/source-files.c` and updates its documentation, with no on-disk format or algorithmic changes.

Justin Tobler’s review had questioned why 6700 was chosen, noting that the commit message could better justify the number. Patrick’s reply here is straightforward: the value is borrowed directly from `git gc`, which has used it for years. This aligns the behavior of geometric repacking with the more conservative threshold already established in the codebase. The exchange confirms the patch’s technical correctness and reinforces its goal of reducing instability in busy repositories.
