This is the fifth patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-patch **gitk UI overhaul** series (v2). It eliminates the last translatability barrier by replacing dynamically constructed dialog titles (“Gitk: choose color for background”) with full, natural-language titles supplied directly by the caller (“Gitk: choose background color”). The change touches only the `coloruielems` list in `gitk-git/gitk`, updating all twelve call sites to pass the new titles. No behavior changes; the patch is purely about making the strings translatable and more readable. The series remains confined to gitk and is ready for final review.
