This is a **bugfix acknowledgment** in the `--drop-filtered` repack series. The author (Siddharth Shrimali) confirms the blocking type-mismatch bug in `list_objects_filter__filter_oidset()` identified by Junio: the function passes the address of an `unsigned long` variable to `struct object_info.sizep`, which expects `size_t *`. This violates strict aliasing rules and could cause silent data corruption or crashes on platforms where `size_t` and `unsigned long` differ (e.g., Windows 64-bit). The fix is straightforward—change the local variable from `unsigned long` to `size_t`—and will be included in the upcoming v5 iteration.

The acknowledgment is brief but clear, and the author demonstrates understanding of the issue by noting the historical use of `unsigned long` in older interfaces (`odb_read_object()`) as the source of the oversight. No new technical questions or design changes are introduced; this is purely a correctness fix. The series remains blocked on this issue until v5 is posted.
