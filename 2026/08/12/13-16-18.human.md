This is the third patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-part v2 series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. It directly addresses the long-standing usability quirk where the color swatches were purely decorative and users had to click the adjacent text label instead. By replacing the themed `ttk::button` widgets with plain `button` widgets, the patch makes the swatches themselves clickable and immediately styleable. The change is purely visual—no new strings, no translatability issues, no behavior beyond the intended click target shift—and the added `-borderwidth 2` and `-pady 1` give the buttons a slightly bolder, more inviting appearance. The patch is small (four lines changed), self-contained, and ready for merge once any remaining nits on label wording or ordering in the rest of the series are settled.
