This is a test fix targeting a flaky CI failure in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`. The patch is the second in a two-patch series by Patrick Steinhardt that addresses a race condition between foreground test processes and auto-detaching background maintenance tasks. The fix is minimal and surgical: instead of globally disabling `GIT_TEST_MAINT_AUTO_DETACH` for the entire test script (which introduced the race), it restores the default behavior (auto-detach disabled) and only unsets the variable in the three tests that explicitly require it. This isolates the auto-detach logic to where it is needed, eliminating the flake without altering the test's intent.
