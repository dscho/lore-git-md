This is a brief follow-up from Junio C Hamano in the thread about unexpected pseudo-commits appearing in a custom `git lg` alias. The thread was already resolved as not a bug, but Junio adds a note of mild surprise that `git log --all` includes *all* refs—including Git notes and potentially other non-branch, non-tag refs like stashes—rather than just branches, remote-tracking branches, and tags. He acknowledges this behavior is unlikely to change at this stage but muses whether `git log --all -p` would also traverse stash refs alongside notes.

The comment is observational rather than actionable; it doesn’t reopen the discussion or propose a change. It simply flags the behavior as potentially counterintuitive for end users, even though it’s technically correct. No further replies or maintainer follow-up are expected.
