This is the third patch in the v2 series overhauling gitk's color-preference dialog. It makes the color swatches themselves clickable by replacing the themed `ttk::button` widgets with non-themed `button` widgets, which can have their background colors set directly. The patch also swaps the grid layout so that the label appears to the left of the button (previously the button was left and the swatch right), adds a 2-pixel border and 1-pixel vertical padding to the buttons for better visual definition, and updates the `prefspage_set_colorswatches` helper to set both the normal and active background colors. The change is purely visual and mechanical, confined to the `prefspage_colors` and `prefspage_set_colorswatches` procedures in `gitk-git/gitk`. No new symbols are introduced; the patch simply reassigns widget types and layout properties. This is the foundational change that enables the usability improvements in the rest of the series.
