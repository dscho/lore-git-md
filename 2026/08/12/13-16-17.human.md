This is the second patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-patch series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. It refactors the repetitive Tcl/Tk code that sets up the color-selection buttons into a single `foreach` loop, eliminating nearly identical blocks of code. The patch also makes two minor naming cleanups: dropping the redundant “sep” suffix from “markbgsep” and “selbgsep” (a leftover from copying the “hunksep” code), and changing the “but” suffix to “btn” for consistency. These changes are purely mechanical and do not alter the dialog’s appearance or behavior; they simply prepare the codebase for the subsequent UI improvements in the series. The patch is confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and is a straightforward refactoring with no functional impact.
