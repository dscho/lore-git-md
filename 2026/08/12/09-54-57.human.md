Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) sends a revised patch that fixes a deadlock during interactive rebase on Windows, where the sequencer holds the object database (ODB) lock while spawning `git commit`, causing child processes that trigger auto-maintenance (like repacking) to fail when trying to replace files still locked by the parent.

This follows an established pattern introduced in 28d04e1ec197. The commit message now clarifies that the ODB will be reacquired in a "refreshed state" if needed after maintenance completes, addressing Junio C Hamano's earlier concern about the safety of releasing resources mid-operation.
