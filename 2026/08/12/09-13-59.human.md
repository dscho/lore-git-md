This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort and follows the architectural shift introduced in the previous patch, which eagerly initializes alternates during ODB setup.

This patch is a straightforward cleanup: it removes the now-unused `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`. The field previously tracked whether alternates had been loaded, but with eager initialization introduced in patch 2, alternates are guaranteed to be loaded after `odb_new()`, making the field redundant. The change is mechanical, removing 9 lines of code (net -8) and touching only `odb.c` and `odb.h`. Justin Tobler’s review of the v1 version noted this removal as sensible and unproblematic, and the patch remains unchanged in v2.

The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration. It follows logically from the earlier patches and does not introduce new behavior or edge cases. For readers tracking the ODB refactoring effort, this is a minor but necessary step toward simplifying the codebase.
