This is a brief, positive review from Jeff King (Peff) on the final version of Johannes Schindelin’s Coverity-driven series fixing unchecked return values. Peff confirms that the version he reviewed (v3) resolves the only issue he raised earlier (a resource leak in the bisect-run patch) and that his cursory look at the rest of the series revealed no further problems. He offers a "weak second" — effectively an informal endorsement — signaling that the series is ready for integration.

The series itself is a well-scoped bugfix effort: eleven patches across HTTP, config, reftable, last-modified, compat/pread, transport-helper, and bisect subsystems, each adding missing error checks to functions like `curl_easy_duphandle`, `deflateInit`, `repo_parse_commit`, `lseek`, `dup`, `strbuf_getline_lf`, `launch_editor`, `reftable_table_init_ref_iterator`, and `rename`. No new behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced; the patches only add fatal error paths (`die()`) or propagate errors to the caller. Junio C Hamano’s earlier review declared the series "fine with declaring victory," and Peff’s reply removes the last procedural hurdle before integration. The series is now effectively complete and uncontroversial.
