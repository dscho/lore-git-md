This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch v2 series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to eagerly load alternate object databases. It is a mechanical cleanup patch that removes the now-unused `loaded_alternates` field from the `struct object_database` after the preceding patch (2/4) centralized alternate initialization in `odb_new()`.

The patch deletes 9 lines and adds 1, touching only `odb.c` and `odb.h`. The change is straightforward: since alternates are now loaded eagerly during ODB setup, the `loaded_alternates` flag is redundant and can be removed. The diff shows the field being dropped from the struct definition and all conditional checks involving it being simplified or removed. Justin Tobler’s v1 review of this patch was surface-level, noting the removal as sensible and unproblematic.

This patch has no behavioral impact and is uncontroversial. It follows directly from the architectural shift in patch 2/4 and is a textbook example of a cleanup patch that reduces state tracking complexity. The series as a whole remains under review, with no outstanding objections to this specific change.
