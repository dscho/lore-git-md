This is the fourth patch in the v2 series overhauling gitk's color-preference dialog. It updates seven UI labels to use more natural, descriptive language, replacing technical jargon and abbreviations with clearer phrasing. The changes are straightforward: "Foreground" becomes "Main text", "Diff: old lines bg" becomes "Old line background", "Diff: hunk header" becomes "Hunk header text", "Marked line bg" becomes "Marked line background", "Select bg" becomes "Selected text background", and "Link" becomes "Link text". The "Diff:" prefix is removed from diff-related labels since the context makes it redundant.

The patch is purely presentational, confined to the `gitk-git/gitk` file, and does not alter functionality or translatability. It builds on the earlier refactoring patches that made the labels non-clickable, allowing for longer text without visual awkwardness. The new wording aligns with the series' goal of improving usability by making the dialog more intuitive. While the specific phrasing remains a minor point of discussion (e.g., "Old line background" vs. "Diff: old lines background"), the changes are uncontroversial and ready for integration. No technical concerns or edge cases are introduced.
