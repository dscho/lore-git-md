This is a **bugfix** patch in response to a substantive review of Patrick Steinhardt’s adjustment to Git’s geometric repacking threshold for loose objects. The email is a short follow-up in an ongoing thread, clarifying the rationale behind the chosen threshold value (6700) and pushing back gently on Justin Tobler’s suggestion that the commit message could better justify it.

Patrick acknowledges the heuristic’s limitations (counting loose objects in only one fan-out directory and multiplying by 256) but argues that the core issue is not estimation accuracy but the overly aggressive default threshold of 100 objects. He points out that even a perfect count would still trigger repacks too frequently at that level, and that aligning with `git gc`’s long-standing default of 6700 is the simplest and most conservative fix. The exchange confirms the patch’s narrow scope: a configuration default change in `odb/source-files.c` and its documentation, with no algorithmic or on-disk format modifications. The discussion remains focused on the trade-off between repack frequency and stability in high-concurrency repositories, with no new technical objections raised.
