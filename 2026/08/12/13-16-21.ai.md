This is the sixth and final patch in the v2 series overhauling gitk's color-preference dialog. It reorders the color options list to improve visual hierarchy by placing generic colors (selected text background, link text) immediately after the main background and text colors, before the diff-specific options. The marked-line background, which applies only to the diff panel, remains below the diff colors. The change is purely presentational: no functionality is altered, and no explicit section headers are added. The patch touches only the `gitk-git/gitk` file, swapping six lines of configuration data within the `coloruielems` list.

The reordering addresses the last remaining cosmetic feedback from the v1 review cycle, completing the series' goal of making the dialog more intuitive. With this patch, all substantive work is done; only minor label wording and ordering preferences remain open for discussion, and those are purely aesthetic. The series is now ready for final review and should be a straightforward merge once any last nits are resolved.
