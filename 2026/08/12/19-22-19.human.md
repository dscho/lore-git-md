This is a brief follow-up in the `--drop-filtered` repack series. The author, Siddharth Shrimali, acknowledges Junio’s catch of a **blocking type-mismatch bug** in patch 2/7: the new `list_objects_filter__filter_oidset()` helper passes the address of an `unsigned long` variable to `struct object_info.sizep`, which expects `size_t *`. While this works on most platforms, it violates strict aliasing rules and could cause silent data corruption or crashes on platforms where `size_t` and `unsigned long` differ (e.g., Windows 64-bit). The fix is straightforward—change the local variable to `size_t`—and will be included in v5.

The email is short and to the point, confirming the issue and the planned fix. No new technical questions or design changes are introduced; this is purely a correctness fix. The series remains on hold until v5 is posted, addressing this and any other pending feedback (e.g., Christian Couder’s documentation reorganization suggestions). The maintainer’s decision and integration branch are still unknown.
