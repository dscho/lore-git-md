This is the second patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-patch v2 series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. It refactors the repetitive Tcl/Tk code that sets up the color-selection buttons into a single `foreach` loop, reducing 59 lines of near-identical code to 55 lines of data-driven logic. The patch also makes two minor naming cleanups: dropping the redundant “sep” suffix from “markbgsep” and “selbgsep” (a leftover from when “hunksep” was copied) and changing the “but” suffix to “btn” for consistency. These changes are purely mechanical and do not alter the dialog’s appearance or behavior; they serve as a prerequisite for the subsequent patches that will make the color swatches clickable and improve the visual presentation. The patch is confined to `gitk-git/gitk` and has no impact on core Git functionality.
