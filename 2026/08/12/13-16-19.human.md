This is the fourth patch in Johannes Sixt’s six-patch series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. It replaces the old, terse labels with more natural language: “Foreground” becomes “Main text”, “Diff: old lines bg” becomes “Old line background”, “Diff: hunk header” becomes “Hunk header text”, “Marked line bg” becomes “Marked line background”, “Select bg” becomes “Selected text background”, and “Link” becomes “Link text”. The “Diff:” prefix is dropped because the remaining text is unambiguous in context. The change is purely presentational—no code logic or translatability is affected—and is confined to the `gitk-git/gitk` file. The patch is ready for final review; any remaining discussion is about label wording preferences, not technical correctness.
