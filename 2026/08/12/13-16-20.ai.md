This is the fifth patch in the v2 series overhauling gitk's color-preference dialog. It ensures full translatability by replacing dynamically constructed dialog titles (e.g., "Gitk: choose color for background") with complete, natural-language titles provided directly by the caller (e.g., "Gitk: choose background color"). The patch updates all 12 call sites in the `coloruielems` list to supply the new titles, rewriting them for clarity and consistency. For example, "diff old lines bg" becomes "choose background color of old lines". The `choosecolor` procedure is modified to accept the full title as an argument instead of a dynamic suffix. This change is purely mechanical and confined to the Tcl/Tk UI layer, with no impact on core Git functionality or on-disk format. The patch is well-motivated and directly addresses the translatability issue noted in the series' overall goal.
