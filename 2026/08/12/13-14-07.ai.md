This is a follow-up in a bug report thread about unexpected pseudo-commits appearing in a custom `git lg` alias. The thread remains under review, with no maintainer decision or deeper investigation into the source of the notes.

D. Ben Knoble replies to Marcel Svitalský’s questions about whether the appearance of Git notes in the alias output is new behavior and how to exclude them. He confirms that Git notes are not new and suggests the notes may have appeared because a tool in the user’s environment started creating them. For excluding notes, he proposes two workarounds: replacing `--all` with `--branches --tags --remotes` (which omits notes, stash, and other refs), or using `--exclude='refs/notes/*' --all` to explicitly filter out notes while retaining the broader coverage of `--all`. The reply is practical and solution-oriented, focusing on immediate usability rather than diagnosing why the notes appeared. No new technical details about the notes’ origin or the discrepancy with GUI tools are provided.
