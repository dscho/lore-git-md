This is a follow-up in a user-support thread about a cosmetic issue with a custom `git lg` alias. The thread is effectively resolved.

Marcel Svitalský confirms that D. Ben Knoble’s suggested workarounds—either replacing `--all` with `--branches --tags --remotes` or using `--exclude='refs/notes/*' --all`—successfully suppress the unwanted Git notes from the alias output. He acknowledges that the issue stemmed from his use of `--all`, which includes all refs (including notes), and that the notes themselves are not new but likely appeared due to a tool in his environment (he suspects `lazygit` or IntelliJ IDEA). The thread concludes with Marcel expressing satisfaction with the solution and no further investigation planned. No maintainer action is needed; this is purely a user-configuration matter.
