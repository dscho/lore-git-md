This thread is about a critical performance regression in Git v2.53 that causes quadratic-time behavior (`O(N²)`) when loading many packfiles, severely impacting operations like `git rev-parse --short HEAD` and clones in environments with thousands of packfiles. The issue was reported in Microsoft’s Git fork, where simple commands slowed from sub-second to several seconds, and clone times ballooned from under 2 minutes to over 30 minutes in CI scenarios.

Johannes Schindelin authored a targeted bugfix that adds a fast path to `packfile_store_add_pack()` by extending `packfile_list_append()` with an `is_new` parameter. This allows callers to skip the duplicate-check scan when the packfile is known to be new, addressing the root cause without disrupting the existing API. The patch includes a new perf test (`p5303-many-packs.sh`) to measure performance with 10,000 packs and is submitted via GitGitGadget (PR #2202).

The fix is minimal, well-documented, and focused on the packfile subsystem, making it a strong candidate for quick review and integration. No objections or alternative approaches have been raised yet, and the patch is likely to be uncontroversial given its production-critical nature.
