This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-part series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The patch centralizes alternate ODB initialization in `odb_new()` and `odb_prepare()`, eliminating 13 scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` across 10 files. The goal is to simplify the codebase and reduce maintenance overhead, though the change may have performance implications for commands like `git fsck` that previously avoided loading alternates when none were present.
