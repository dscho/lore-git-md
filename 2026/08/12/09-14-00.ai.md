This is the fourth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. It completes the architectural shift by eliminating the now-unused `alternates_db` field from `struct object_database`.

The patch mechanically removes the field and updates `odb_prepare_alternates()` to accept the alternate database path as a parameter (passed via `odb_new()`). A key clarification added in v2 explains why `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES` does not need reprocessing during cache flushing: alternates are fully initialized during `odb_new()`, and subsequent calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` only re-read the alternates *file*, not the environment variable. This resolves Justin Tobler’s v1 question and improves maintainability with a comment in the code.

The change is confined to `odb.c` and `odb.h`, removing 7 lines and adding 2 (net -5). No behavioral changes are intended, and the patch is a straightforward cleanup following the eager initialization introduced in patch 2. The series as a whole reduces scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and centralizes alternate handling, simplifying the ODB codebase for future work like moving alternates into the "files" backend. Justin Tobler’s v1 review of this patch was surface-level, focusing on the handling of `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES`, which Patrick addressed in v2. The patch appears uncontroversial and ready for integration.
