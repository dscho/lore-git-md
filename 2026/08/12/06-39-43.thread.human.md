This thread is about a bugfix for a NULL-dereference in Git's protocol v2 `promisor-remote` capability handling. The bug was introduced in February 2025 when the capability was added, and it occurs when a client sends the capability name without an `=` or value. Elijah Newren identified the issue and submitted a minimal patch that adds a NULL check in the `promisor_remote_receive()` callback in `serve.c`, along with a new test case in `t/t5701-git-serve.sh` to verify the fix.

The patch is straightforward and uncontroversial, addressing a clear bug with a targeted fix. No discussion or objections have been raised so far, and the change is limited to the affected callback and test suite. This looks like a routine bugfix that should be ready for merging once reviewed.
