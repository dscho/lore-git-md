This thread is about a bugfix for a NULL-dereference crash in Git's protocol v2 handling of the `promisor-remote` capability. The bug was introduced in February 2025 and occurs when a client sends the capability without a value, causing the server to crash instead of rejecting the malformed input.

Elijah Newren posted a minimal fix that adds a NULL check in `serve.c` and includes a test case to verify the server's error response. Christian Couder, who originally introduced the bug, reviewed the patch and confirmed its correctness. He noted that while the commit message describes the crash as happening in `strbuf_split_str()`, a later refactoring shifted the crash to `string_list_split()`—but the fix remains valid. With Christian's review, the patch is now effectively approved and ready for merging. No further changes or open questions remain.
