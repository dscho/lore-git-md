This is a brief follow-up in the now-resolved bug report about unexpected pseudo-commits appearing in a custom `git lg` alias. The thread previously established that the root cause was the `--all` flag, which includes all refs—including Git notes like `refs/notes/rewrite-analytics` and `refs/notes/chatter`—and that this behavior, while unintuitive for end users, is not a bug.

Marcel Svitalský acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s suggestion of using `--branches`, `--tags`, and `--remotes` as narrower alternatives to `--all` but notes that he prefers to keep stashes visible in the log. He confirms that the workaround he adopted—`--exclude='refs/notes/*' --all`—achieves the desired result by filtering out notes while preserving the rest of `--all`’s coverage. This closes the loop on the discussion, leaving no open questions or requests for further action. The thread remains effectively resolved.
