This is a brief reply in the ongoing `git fast-import` libification series. Christian Couder acknowledges Elijah Newren’s feedback on the documentation for `PARSE_OPT_LASTARG_DEFAULT` in patch 2/12 of the v2 series. The original wording was slightly imprecise about when the no-argument form is accepted; Newren suggested a clearer phrasing that explicitly states the option must be the last token on the command line and that it should not be combined with `PARSE_OPT_OPTARG`. Couder confirms he will incorporate the suggested rewording in the upcoming v3. This is a surface-level, editorial adjustment with no impact on code behavior or the series’ broader goals. The exchange resolves one of the three minor open points identified in Newren’s review of the series.
