This is the third iteration (v3) of Justin Tobler’s nine-patch series that makes `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic for packfile writes by introducing `odb_transaction_write_pack()`, a generic transaction interface. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

The v3 update addresses all feedback from v2: it fixes a bug that prevented ".keep" files from being removed, ensures `odb_transaction_finalize()` runs after every successful commit, stops caching the unpack limit configuration, begins explicitly tracking the ODB source used by the "files" transaction, and adds `odb_transaction_commit_and_finalize_or_die()` to handle errors at commit and finalize call sites. The series now also returns the temporary ODB source when set, avoiding reliance on the ordering of the ODB source list.

The core change remains the same: replacing `git receive-pack`'s hardcoded use of `git index-pack` and `git unpack-objects` with a new transaction interface. The first eight patches are preparatory refactoring (lifting global state, passing file descriptors explicitly, improving error reporting), while the ninth patch completes the switch by moving the packfile ingestion logic into `object-file.c` as the "files" backend implementation. The series touches `builtin/receive-pack.c`, `odb/transaction.c`, `object-file.c`, and related headers, with mechanical updates in other builtins for transaction API consistency.

The patches look well-structured and address the critical design flaws identified in v2, particularly the lockfile lifecycle issue and the misuse of `repo->objects->sources[0]`. The author has incorporated all reviewer feedback, including Junio Hamano’s suggestions on transaction lifecycle and unpack limit storage, and Patrick Steinhardt’s mechanical refinements. The series is still cooking in `seen` and appears ready for further review.
