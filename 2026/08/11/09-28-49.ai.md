This is patch 7/10 of the `paint_down_to_common()` optimization series, now at v8. The patch introduces a `paint_state` struct to replace the old `nonstale_queue` logic with per-side counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `mb_candidate_count`). This refactoring is purely mechanical—behavior remains equivalent—but it paves the way for the side-exhaustion optimization in later patches by making the queue’s state more explicit.

The new `paint_state` struct wraps a `prio_queue` and tracks how many commits are queued for each side (PARENT1-only, PARENT2-only, or both as pending merge-base candidates). The `paint_count_update()` helper adjusts these counters whenever a commit’s flags change, and `paint_queue_get()` now terminates the walk when all counters reach zero, replacing the old `max_nonstale` pointer-based check. The change is non-breaking, but the "pop first" logic in `paint_queue_get()` causes the loop to exit one iteration earlier in some topologies, reducing step counts by one in a few test cases (e.g., `pending-stale` drops from 6 to 5).

The patch also removes the now-unused `nonstale_queue_put_dedup()` and `nonstale_queue_get_dedup()` functions, though the `nonstale_queue` struct itself remains for `ahead_behind()`. Documentation in `paint-down-to-common.adoc` is updated to reflect the new counter-based termination condition, replacing the old `max_nonstale` description.

All review feedback has been addressed, including the critical ordering requirement in `paint_queue_get()` (check counters before decrementing) and the exhaustive switch in `paint_count_update()` that documents all valid flag combinations. The patch is ready for advancement and is queued in Junio’s tree alongside the rest of the series.
