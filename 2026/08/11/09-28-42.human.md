This is the ready-to-merge v8 iteration of a performance optimization series for Git’s merge-base calculation. The series teaches `paint_down_to_common()` to terminate early when one side of a history is exhausted, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories). The optimization yields **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries, reducing step counts from millions to single digits in extreme cases.

The series is technically complete and fully reviewed, with only editorial refinements remaining. Key improvements since v7 include:
- Adopting "ordered/unordered region" terminology throughout the documentation and code, replacing "finite/INFINITY" to better reflect the semantic boundary.
- Introducing a `topo_ceiling` field to handle v1 commit-graph saturation (`GENERATION_NUMBER_V1_MAX`) consistently across all early-exit gates.
- Adding a code comment in `paint_queue_get()` to explain why termination conditions must be checked before decrementing counters.
- Minor test and documentation cleanups (e.g., improved ASCII graph in `t6099`).

The series is structured logically: documentation first, followed by test infrastructure, edge-case coverage, refactoring, and the core optimization. The final patch removes the commit-date ordering fallback, which is now redundant thanks to the side-exhaustion optimization.
