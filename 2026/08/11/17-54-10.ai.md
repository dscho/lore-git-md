This is the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s nine-patch v3 series making `git receive-pack` fully ODB-agnostic. It removes the static global variables `receive_unpack_limit`, `transfer_unpack_limit`, and `unpack_limit` and replaces them with a lazy, per-repository lookup of the unpack limit via `repo_config_get_uint()`.

The change is a direct response to reviewer feedback on v2: the unpack limit is a backend-specific detail (whether to explode a packfile into loose objects or keep it as a packfile) and should not be wired through the generic ODB transaction interface. By resolving the limit lazily inside `unpack()`, the patch keeps the decision local to the backend while preserving the existing behavior. The new `get_unpack_limit()` helper reads both `transfer.unpackLimit` and `receive.unpackLimit` (with the latter taking precedence) and defaults to 100 if neither is set.

The diff is mechanical: 19 lines of global-variable plumbing are removed, and 11 lines of new helper and in-line lookup are added. No user-visible behavior changes; the patch is purely preparatory for the ODB abstraction. The series as a whole remains cooking in `seen`; this patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
