**Core problem or goal**
Fix Bash completion for `git checkout` (with or without `-C <dir>`) to prioritize tracked paths (those in the index) over untracked ones, while adding a fallback to untracked paths as a last resort. The series builds on the earlier `git diff` completion fix and addresses a usability gap where completion previously suggested untracked files inconsistently. Junio clarified in v1 that `__git_complete_index_file` must be called with the `--cached` option (or its equivalent `""`) to strictly enforce tracked-path completion, ensuring untracked files are excluded by default. The v2 series introduces a pragmatic fallback to untracked paths when no tracked paths match, though Junio notes this may have limited utility for `git checkout` use cases. The discussion also clarified that Zsh completion support for `git show <tree>:<path>` is not reusable for `git checkout <treeish> <path>` due to differing semantics and completion requirements.

**Key technical details**
