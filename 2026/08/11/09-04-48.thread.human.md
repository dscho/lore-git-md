This thread is a straightforward bugfix to Git's auto-maintenance system, addressing an overly aggressive repacking threshold that caused instability in high-concurrency repositories. The issue was that the geometric repacking strategy defaulted to repacking loose objects at just 100 objects—far lower than `git gc`'s long-standing default of 6700—leading to race conditions with loose object directory pruning.

Patrick Steinhardt proposed a one-patch fix to align the geometric repacking threshold with `git gc`'s default, and after a brief review from Justin Tobler (who initially questioned the justification but later accepted the patch as-is), the series is now ready for integration. The fix is minimal: it adjusts a single configuration default in `maintenance.geometric-repack.auto` from 100 to 6700, with no on-disk format changes or new behavior. Patrick has since provided historical context for the 6700 threshold, noting it was pragmatically chosen in 2007 to balance repacking frequency and stability, and has proven reliable over nearly two decades. There are no remaining open questions or objections.
