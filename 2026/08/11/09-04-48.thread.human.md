This thread is about a small but important fix to Git’s auto-maintenance feature. Patrick Steinhardt identified that the geometric repacking strategy was triggering repacks of loose objects far too often—every 100 loose objects, compared to `git gc`’s default of 6,700—leading to instability in repositories with high concurrency due to race conditions between repacking and loose object pruning. The patch simply raises the default threshold to 6,700 to match `git gc`, reducing unnecessary repacks without changing the underlying algorithm or on-disk format.

Justin Tobler reviewed the patch and endorsed it as "trivially correct," though he noted the commit message could better explain why 6,700 was chosen. His feedback was substantive, engaging with the heuristic’s trade-offs (a rough but cheap estimate vs. a more accurate but expensive one) and confirming the fix’s rationale. The patch itself touches only two files—`odb/source-files.c` and its documentation—and remains narrowly focused on addressing the reported issue. No objections or open questions have been raised so far, and the discussion reinforces the fix’s practical value for real-world repositories.
