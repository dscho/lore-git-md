This is a Trace2 instrumentation patch for `git fetch-pack`, reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt.

The patch adds Trace2 telemetry to track the duration and count of packfile URI downloads during protocol v2 fetches. Patrick’s review is substantive and tested: he confirms the instrumentation is useful (exposing download time and URI count without per-pack spam), notes the correct use of `the_repository` in the absence of an explicit repository parameter, and approves the test extension. His only mild critique is that the test piggy-backs on an existing test that does not otherwise use tracing, but he does not consider it a blocker.

The patch is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and unlikely to be controversial. It is ready for integration unless someone raises a late objection.
