This is a **substantive review** of Patrick Steinhardt’s bugfix patch adjusting the geometric repacking threshold for loose objects.

Justin Tobler’s review engages directly with the patch’s technical rationale and implementation choices. He confirms the mechanism behind the overly aggressive repacking (the heuristic counting loose objects in only one fan-out directory and multiplying by 256) and notes that while a more accurate—but expensive—heuristic could be considered, the simpler approach of raising the threshold is reasonable given the goal of a rough estimate. He questions the specific choice of 6700 (the same value used by `git gc`) and suggests the commit message could better explain why this number was selected, though he ultimately endorses the patch as "trivially correct."

The review surfaces a minor documentation gap—the lack of explicit justification for 6700 in the commit message—but does not identify any functional flaws. Justin’s feedback is constructive and focused on clarity rather than disputing the fix itself. The patch remains narrowly scoped: a configuration default change in `odb/source-files.c` and its documentation, with no on-disk format or algorithmic modifications. The discussion reinforces the patch’s motivation (mitigating race conditions in high-concurrency repositories) and provides additional context for future readers about the heuristic’s limitations.
