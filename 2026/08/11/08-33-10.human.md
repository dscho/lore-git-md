This is patch 8/12 in Christian Couder’s v3 series refactoring `git fast-import` for libification. It moves two global variables—`seen_data_command` and `allow_unsafe_features`—into the newly introduced `struct fast_import_state`. The change is purely mechanical: every access site already receives the `state` parameter, so the patch simply updates the dereferences and removes the now-unused `UNUSED` marker from `check_unsafe_feature()`. The `fast_import_state_init()` call is also reordered to avoid clobbering the early `--allow-unsafe-features` scan. No behavior change is intended, and the patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c`. It’s a small but necessary step toward eliminating globals and enabling future libification work.
