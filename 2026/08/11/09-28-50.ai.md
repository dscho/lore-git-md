This is the final patch in the v8 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It implements the core performance optimization: early termination when one side of the merge-base walk exhausts its commit queue, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large asymmetric histories.

The patch adds the side-exhaustion termination condition to `paint_queue_get()`, gated by two key invariants: the walk must be in the "ordered region" (where topological ordering holds), and no pending merge-base candidates may remain. The check uses per-side counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`) introduced in earlier patches to track exclusive commits from each input set. A new `topo_ceiling` field ensures the optimization respects the saturation boundary for v1 commit-graphs (`GENERATION_NUMBER_V1_MAX`) while using `GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY` for v2 graphs.

The documentation update clarifies the side-exhaustion condition and its interaction with generation regions, while the test expectations reflect the dramatic performance improvement: step counts drop from 81 to 9 in the `merge-base --all commit-9-9 commit-9-1` test (full commit-graph mode), demonstrating the 100-1000x speedup for asymmetric queries. The patch also applies the same `topo_ceiling` boundary to the existing single-result early exit for consistency.
