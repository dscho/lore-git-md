This thread is a small but practical fix to Git’s Bash completion for `git checkout -C <dir>`. Junio C Hamano sent a two-patch series that ensures path completion suggests only tracked files (those in the index) rather than falling back to untracked ones. The first patch refactors the completion logic without changing behavior, and the second implements the fix and adds test coverage.

The series is under review and uncontroversial. Junio later clarified a technical detail—using `__git_complete_index_file --cached` to strictly enforce tracked-path completion—and noted that while a future enhancement could support completing paths from other branches (like Zsh does for `git show <tree>:<path>`), the current series doesn’t tackle that. D. Ben Knoble’s feedback was procedural and didn’t engage with the technical substance. The patches are ready as-is, with no open questions or requested changes.
