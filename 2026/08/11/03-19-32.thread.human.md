This thread is a patch series from Junio C Hamano to fix Bash completion for `git checkout` when used with the `-C <dir>` option or when completing paths. The goal is to prioritize tracked paths (those in the index) over untracked ones, while adding a fallback to untracked paths only when no tracked paths match. The series, now at version 2, consists of three patches: a refactoring to prepare the code, the core fix to enforce tracked-path completion, and a pragmatic fallback to untracked paths.

The discussion has been straightforward. Elijah Newren reviewed the series and suggested a minor refinement to patch 3/3—adding the `--directory` flag to the fallback call for consistency with the earlier `git diff` completion series and to improve performance. Junio has accepted this suggestion, and the series is now technically complete with no remaining objections. The changes are well-tested, with eight new tests in `t/t9902-completion.sh` covering edge cases like missing files and the fallback behavior. The series is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration, pending Junio’s final adjustments.
