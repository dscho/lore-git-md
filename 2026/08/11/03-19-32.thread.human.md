This thread is a patch series from Junio C Hamano fixing Bash completion for `git checkout` (with or without `-C <dir>`). The goal is to prioritize tracked paths (those in the index) over untracked ones during completion, with a fallback to untracked paths only when no tracked paths match. The series is now in its third version and is technically complete.

The v3 update is minimal: the final patch (3/3) now includes `--directory` in the `__git_complete_index_file "--others"` call, aligning with the earlier `git diff` completion series and addressing a minor consistency concern raised by Elijah Newren. This change also improves performance by avoiding unnecessary stat calls on non-directory files. The series consists of three patches: the first refactors the completion logic without changing behavior, the second implements the core fix (tracked paths only), and the third adds the fallback to untracked paths. Eight new tests in `t/t9902-completion.sh` verify the behavior, including edge cases like missing files and the fallback mechanism.

The series is uncontroversial and ready for integration. Junio and Elijah Newren are the key participants, with Elijah providing a substantive review that led to the v3 refinement. D. Ben Knoble’s earlier feedback was procedural and did not engage with the technical details. The work builds on the earlier `git diff` completion fix and is rebased onto the `complete-diff` topic for better test organization. While the fallback to untracked paths may have limited utility for typical `git checkout` workflows, it ensures completion never fails silently.
