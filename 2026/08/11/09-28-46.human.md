This is the fourth patch in the v8 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds a new regression test (`t6099-merge-base-side-exhaustion.sh`) to verify that the side-exhaustion optimization correctly handles cases where multiple merge-base candidates exist and one is an ancestor of another.

The test constructs a diamond-shaped commit graph where two tips (`A` and `X`) share two merge-base candidates (`B` and `C`), with `B` being an ancestor of `C`. The side-exhaustion optimization might exit early before the `STALE` propagation removes the redundant ancestor (`C`), but the existing `remove_redundant` safety net in `get_merge_bases_many_0` ensures only `B` is returned. The test validates this behavior for both `--all` and single merge-base queries, and includes a variant without a commit-graph (where generation numbers are `INFINITY` and the optimization does not fire).

The patch is well-motivated and directly addresses a potential edge case in the optimization. The test topology is clearly documented in the commit message, and the test itself is thorough, covering both commit-graph and non-commit-graph scenarios. This is a solid addition to the series, with no obvious gaps or oversights. The series as a whole is technically complete and ready for advancement.
