This thread is about a small but important bugfix to prevent Git from crashing when the `--shallow-file` option is used without an argument. Christian Couder identified the issue—a missing validation in the option-parsing logic—and submitted a patch that adds the necessary check, along with a new test to verify the fix. The patch is minimal, defensive, and has no known downsides, as all existing in-tree callers already provide valid arguments.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and found no technical flaws, but raised two procedural points. First, he noted that the patch mixes a defensive fix with a stylistic reordering of the option-handling logic, making it slightly harder to review. While the change is harmless, he suggested splitting the two concerns for clarity. Second, he questioned the test coverage, pointing out that similar options (like `-C` or `--git-dir`) lack equivalent tests and suggesting either consolidating the new test with existing ones or expanding it to cover all such cases. Neither comment blocks the patch, but they do open a small discussion about process.

The patch is ready for merging as-is, but the thread may see a follow-up version if Christian chooses to address Junio’s suggestions. No objections or open technical questions remain.
