This is the first patch in a 12-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` to modernize its parse-options usage and prepare for libification. The patch introduces `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP`, a new macro for grouping hidden options in the parse-options API. Hidden options are shown only with `--help-all`, not with `-h`, and previously there was no way to group them meaningfully without also showing the group header in the `-h` output.

The implementation adds the macro to `parse-options.h` and updates the usage formatter in `parse-options.c` to skip hidden groups unless `--help-all` is requested. The change is purely additive and mechanical: no existing behavior is altered, and the new macro is immediately tested in both `test-parse-options.c` and `t/t0040-parse-options.sh`. The test suite is expanded to verify that hidden groups and options appear only in `--help-all` output, not in the default `-h` help.

This patch is a foundational step for the series, enabling later patches to organize `git fast-import`'s many hidden options into logical sections. The work is clean, well-tested, and aligns with Git's broader parse-options modernization efforts. No controversy or open questions are evident; the patch is ready for integration. Files touched: `parse-options.h`, `parse-options.c`, `t/helper/test-parse-options.c`, and `t/t0040-parse-options.sh`. New symbols: `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP`.
