While he acknowledges the churn and its costs—disrupted workflows, rebasing challenges, and merge conflicts—he suggests these are temporary pains that will subside once the dust settles, contrasting Junio’s view of the disruption as an ongoing burden.

The exchange underscores the **deadlock** in the debate: Patrick’s focus on long-term usability for new contributors clashes with Junio’s preference for stability and incremental, motivated changes. Neither side budges on the core issue—whether the reorganization’s benefits outweigh its costs—but Patrick’s tone is conciliatory, accepting that the patch may not proceed if the consensus remains against it.
