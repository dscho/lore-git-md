This is patch 8/12 in Christian Couder’s v3 series refactoring `git fast-import` for libification. It moves the global variables `seen_data_command` and `allow_unsafe_features` into the newly introduced `struct fast_import_state`. The change is purely mechanical: all access sites already receive the `state` parameter, so the patch only updates dereferences and removes the now-unused `UNUSED` marker from `check_unsafe_feature()`. The `fast_import_state_init()` call is reordered to avoid clobbering the early `--allow-unsafe-features` scan, ensuring the option is recorded directly into the struct. No behavior change is intended; the patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c` and is a straightforward step toward eliminating globals.
