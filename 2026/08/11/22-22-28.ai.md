This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The patch removes the now-redundant `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`, which previously tracked whether alternates had been loaded. Since the preceding patch (2/4) made alternate loading eager—happening during `odb_new()`—this field is no longer needed. The change is purely mechanical: the field is deleted, and the single remaining check for it is replaced with unconditional execution of `odb_add_alternate_recursively()`. The patch also removes the awkward reset of `loaded_alternates` in `odb_prepare()` when `ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES` is set, which Justin Tobler’s review confirms as a welcome cleanup.

The diff is minimal (9 lines removed, 1 added) and confined to `odb.c` and `odb.h`. No behavioral changes are intended, and the patch is a straightforward follow-up to the eager-loading change in patch 2. Justin Tobler’s review is surface-level but supportive, noting the removal of the field and its reset logic as sensible and unproblematic. This patch is low-risk and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB codebase by centralizing alternate initialization. The broader series remains under review, with no substantive objections raised so far.
