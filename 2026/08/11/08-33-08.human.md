This is patch 6/12 in Christian Couder’s ongoing `git fast-import` refactoring series. The patch factors out the argument-parsing logic for five command-line options (`--max-pack-size`, `--big-file-threshold`, `--signed-commits`, `--signed-tags`, and `--quiet`) into dedicated helper functions (`option_max_pack_size()`, `option_big_file_threshold()`, `option_signed_commits()`, `option_signed_tags()`, and `option_quiet()`). These helpers replace generic “unknown option” failures with specific `die()` messages when `git_parse_ulong()` fails to parse numeric arguments, improving error reporting. The refactoring is purely mechanical and preparatory, touching only `builtin/fast-import.c` and laying the groundwork for the parse-options integration in patch 11/12. No behavior change is intended beyond the improved error messages.
