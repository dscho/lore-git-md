This is a small, mechanical refactoring patch in Christian Couder’s ongoing `git fast-import` libification series. It changes the type of the `show_stats` and `quiet` global variables from `unsigned int` to `int` to match what `OPT_BOOL` expects. The patch is purely preparatory: it touches only two lines in `builtin/fast-import.c` and introduces no behavior change. The commit message is clear and concise, explaining the motivation (enabling `OPT_BOOL` parsing in a later patch) without overstating the impact.

This is the fifth patch in the twelve-patch v3 series. The earlier patches introduced infrastructure (like the `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro) and documented parse-options flags, while later patches will convert the command-line parser to use `parse_options()`. This patch is a straightforward type alignment step that paves the way for that conversion. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with Git’s coding conventions for boolean flags. No review feedback has been reported on this specific patch, but the series as a whole has been well-received by Junio and Elijah Newren, with all feedback addressed in v3.
