This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series is a platform-compatibility fix for 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.

This patch widens two related interfaces: the `*left` output parameter of `use_pack()` (in `packfile.h` and `packfile.c`) and the `avail_in`/`avail_out` fields of `struct git_zstream` (in `git-zlib.h`). The change is motivated by the fact that four call sites in `builtin/pack-objects.c` pass `&stream.avail_in` directly to `use_pack()`, so widening either type alone would require temporary bridge variables at each call. By widening both together, the patch avoids that clutter and prepares for the next commit, which will widen `do_compress()` and eliminate the last `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shim in `write_no_reuse_object()`.

The commit message clarifies that the widened locals at other `use_pack()` callers (e.g., `avail`, `remaining`, `left`) hold pack-window sizes bounded by `core.packedGitWindowSize`, so the change is about type consistency rather than enabling new >4GB capabilities. Similarly, `git_zstream.avail_in`/`avail_out` are already capped by `zlib_buf_cap()` at 1 GiB, so the wrapper effectively accepted `size_t`-shaped inputs in practice. The patch is mechanical and introduces no behavior changes.

Files touched are `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `git-zlib.h`, `pack-check.c`, `packfile.c`, and `packfile.h`. The change is low-risk and uncontroversial, fitting cleanly into the series’ overall goal of eliminating truncation risks on 64-bit Windows. The series as a whole is ready for integration, and this patch is a straightforward step in that progression.
