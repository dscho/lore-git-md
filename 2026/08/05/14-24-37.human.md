The output matches the real deletion output exactly, including branch names and commit hashes. The patch also ensures `--dry-run` is rejected unless paired with `--delete-merged`, preventing nonsensical usage. During dry runs, `spare_stacked_bases()` skips clearing upstream configurations to avoid side effects, preserving the repository state.
