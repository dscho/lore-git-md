This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series targets 64-bit Windows compatibility (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.

This patch widens the `bufsize` parameter of `create_delta_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The change is mechanical and low-risk: the sole caller (`try_delta()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`) already passes an `unsigned long`, which safely promotes to `size_t`. No caller adjustments are needed, and the patch is split out to keep the more complex `diff_delta()`/`create_delta()` widening (which affects multiple callers) in a separate commit.

The patch touches only two files (`delta.h` and `diff-delta.c`) and makes no behavior or on-disk format changes. It is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of addressing latent truncation issues on 64-bit Windows. The series as a whole is ready for integration, with all review feedback from v1 addressed in v2.
