This is a completion script patch for the `git history` command. Patrick Steinhardt reviews Vincent Mailhol’s patch, which adds bash completion for `git history` subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`), their options, and revision arguments. The implementation uses Git’s existing completion helpers (`__gitcomp_builtin` and `__git_complete_refs`) and includes thorough test coverage in `t/t9902-completion.sh`.

Patrick’s review is substantive but measured. He notes that the patch is a clear improvement over the current state (no completion at all) and works well for most subcommands. The one exception is `git history split`, where subsequent arguments after the first revision should ideally be pathspecs rather than references. Patrick suggests this could be addressed in a future iteration but does not insist on it, calling the current version "good enough" for now.

The review also flags a minor conflict with an upcoming `squash` subcommand in the `seen` branch, though this is not the author’s fault and won’t block the patch. Overall, the feedback is positive, and the patch looks ready to proceed once the minor conflict is resolved. No major objections or open questions remain.
