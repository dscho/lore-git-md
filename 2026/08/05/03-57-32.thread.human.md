This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Taylor Blau addressing a regression in `git maintenance`'s geometric repack task. The issue arose after promisor packs gained their own geometric progression support in January 2026 (dcc9c7ef47), causing the `--auto` trigger to overlook cases where promisor packs needed rollup while ordinary packs did not.

The series is now complete with v1 posted. The first patch fixed the repack decision logic to properly evaluate both ordinary and promisor pack progressions, while this second patch ensures the `--auto` trigger considers `geometry.promisor_split` alongside `geometry.split`. The changes are minimal but critical: a one-line modification in `builtin/gc.c` and targeted test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` that verifies the fix by creating three promisor packs and confirming the auto-trigger works as expected. A minor test adjustment in `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` prevents interference with existing logic.

The series appears self-contained and well-motivated, with no open questions or review feedback yet. It directly addresses a real-world issue in partial clone repositories where promisor packs weren't being handled consistently in maintenance operations.
