This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Taylor Blau addressing a regression in `git maintenance`'s geometric repack task, where promisor packs were not being handled correctly after they gained their own geometric progression support earlier this year. The issue affects partial clone repositories and could lead to suboptimal repacking behavior.

The first patch adjusted the repack decision logic to evaluate both ordinary and promisor pack progressions before falling back to an all-into-one repack. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed it and noted a minor edge case but accepted the approach. The second patch, just posted, completes the fix by updating the `--auto` trigger to consider promisor pack geometry, ensuring the maintenance task runs when promisor packs are ready for rollup. Patrick called this change "obviously correct" and approved the test adjustments.

The series is now fully reviewed with no remaining objections. It touches `builtin/gc.c` and adds test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`, with a minor test adjustment in `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh`. The fix is targeted and well-tested, demonstrating the correct behavior with promisor packs of varying sizes. This looks ready to merge once Junio picks it up.
