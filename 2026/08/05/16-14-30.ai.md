This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch v2 series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The patch targets the delta-cache accounting variables (`delta_cache_size`, `max_delta_cache_size`, and the `delta_cacheable()` helper) in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, converting them from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows where `unsigned long` is 32-bit.

The patch introduces two new helpers, `git_parse_size_t()` and `git_config_size_t()`, to correctly parse the now-widened `max_delta_cache_size` configuration value, ensuring negative values are rejected. This addresses a latent bug identified in Patrick Steinhardt’s review of v1, where the old `git_config_int()` call would silently accept negative values that could later cause misbehavior. The change is internally consistent: the accounting variables already interact with `size_t` sources (e.g., `DELTA_SIZE()`), and the `delta_cacheable()` helper’s caller in `try_delta()` still passes an `unsigned long` value, which promotes safely to `size_t`.

The patch is mechanical and low-risk, touching only the accounting logic without altering behavior or on-disk formats. It prepares the ground for later patches in the series that will widen `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` parameters, enabling the removal of `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shims. The new parsing helpers are reusable for other `size_t` conversions in the codebase. No new tests are added, as the change is covered by existing delta-cache functionality. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.
