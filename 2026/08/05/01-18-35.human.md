This is a follow-up in the review of Toon Claes’s four-patch series optimizing `git last-modified` with Bloom filters. The series aims to make the command as fast as `git log` when querying last-modified commits for pathspecs, including wildcards like `*.c`.

Jeff King (Peff) replies to Junio’s question about whether the discussion on patch 4/4 has concluded. Peff clarifies that his earlier suggestion about refactoring `bloom_filter_settings` management—while cleaner—is not a blocker and he would accept the current approach. However, he emphasizes that the `--show-trees` correctness issue raised by Taylor Blau must be addressed before the series can proceed. Peff suggests the next step is a reroll from Toon that includes a preparatory patch to fix the `--show-trees` output by filtering out ancestor tree entries not covered by the original pathspec.

The email confirms that the primary technical hurdle is now the `--show-trees` bug, not the `bloom_filter_settings` design. The series is otherwise considered technically sound, but the unresolved correctness issue means it cannot advance without further revision. The tone is matter-of-fact, and Peff’s position is clear: the fix for `--show-trees` is non-negotiable, while the settings refactoring is optional.
