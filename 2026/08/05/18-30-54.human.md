This is a bugfix patch, part of Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series addressing unchecked return values flagged by Coverity. It is the fifth of eleven patches, all of which are now technically complete and uncontroversial after incorporating review feedback.

The patch plugs a silent failure in `git last-modified`: when `repo_parse_commit()` fails (e.g., due to a corrupt or missing commit object), the command continues with uninitialized commit data, producing incorrect “last modified” results. The fix adds three simple return-value checks—one in `process_parent()` and two in `last_modified_run()`—so that unparsable commits are skipped rather than treated as root commits or silently excluded. The change is minimal (six lines added) and follows the defensive pattern already used elsewhere in Git’s revision walkers. No new behavior or on-disk formats are introduced.
