This is the second iteration of a platform-compatibility patch series that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The goal is to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and other platforms where `unsigned long` does not match the pointer width. The series is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`.

The v2 update addresses all review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on the initial version. Key fixes include widening the return type of `sizeof_delta_index()` to `size_t`, introducing new helpers (`git_parse_size_t()` and `git_config_size_t()`) to correctly parse `max_delta_cache_size` (now a `size_t`), and clarifying the provenance of the fallback formula in `git_deflate_bound()` for inputs above zlib’s `uLong` range. The series remains mechanical and confined to type widening, with no behavior or on-disk format changes.

The changes touch 18 files, primarily in the delta (`diff-delta.c`, `delta.h`), pack-objects (`builtin/pack-objects.c`), and zlib (`git-zlib.c`) subsystems, with minor adjustments in `archive-zip.c`, `http-push.c`, and test helpers. The commit messages are clear and well-motivated, and the patches are small and focused, making them easy to review. The series is authored by Johannes Schindelin, the Git for Windows maintainer, and is uncontroversial—it fixes real-world truncation issues without introducing new risks.

This is a well-crafted, low-risk fix that directly addresses a platform-specific correctness problem. The v2 iteration is ready for further review, and the changes are likely to be merged once any remaining feedback is addressed. Windows users and anyone working with large objects should pay attention, but the series is relevant to all platforms where `unsigned long` and `size_t` differ.
