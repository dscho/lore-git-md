This is a **contributor housekeeping** follow-up in the `git history` completion thread. Vincent Mailhol replies to Junio C Hamano’s suggestion about refactoring duplicated completion boilerplate, explaining why he thinks the current per-subcommand structure is more maintainable for future extensions. He illustrates his point by sketching how he would handle Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier suggestion to add special-case completion for `git history split` (switching from revision to pathspec completion after the first argument). Vincent emphasizes that the boilerplate acts as a flexible skeleton for such extensions, and he is not yet convinced that coalescing the code would preserve this flexibility. He also clarifies that his exploration of the `split` subcommand’s pathspec handling is a best-effort attempt, not a commitment to implement it. The email advances the thread by surfacing the trade-off between code duplication and extensibility, but does not propose a concrete refactoring or block the current patch.
