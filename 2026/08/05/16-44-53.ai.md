This is a brief follow-up in the Coverity unchecked-return-values series. Johannes Schindelin accepts Patrick Steinhardt’s suggestion on the eleventh patch (`bisect.c`) and extends the error check to cover the `dup2(temporary_stdout_fd, 1)` call that immediately follows the already-checked `dup(1)`. No new technical questions or design concerns are introduced; the patch is now complete and ready for final review.
