This is the tenth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series addressing unchecked return values identified by Coverity. The patch centralizes error handling in the bisect subsystem by extending `get_terms()` to accept a `file_missing_is_ok` flag, ensuring all callers properly handle failures when reading the `BISECT_TERMS` file.

Junio C Hamano’s reply reviews the design choice to use a boolean flag (`file_missing_is_ok`) rather than distinct error codes. He acknowledges the trade-off: the flag-based approach simplifies the majority of callers (which tolerate a missing `BISECT_TERMS` file) by requiring only a single parameter change, while the lone caller that treats a missing file as an error (`bisect_terms()`) passes `0` to preserve its existing behavior. Junio notes that if `get_terms()` were a public function with many callers, he would prefer distinct error codes for clarity, but for a single-file internal function, the flag is acceptable and its meaning will be clear to future maintainers.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused on the design of the error-handling interface. Junio does not question the patch’s correctness or the series’ overall goal; his feedback is a matter-of-fact assessment of the trade-offs between two idiomatic approaches.
