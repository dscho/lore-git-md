This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series converting size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series targets 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit and can silently truncate large values, and is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.

This patch widens the delta-cache accounting variables (`delta_cache_size` and `max_delta_cache_size`) and the `delta_cacheable()` helper in `pack-objects.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The change is internally consistent: the variables already interact with `size_t` sources (like `DELTA_SIZE()`), and the helper’s caller still passes `unsigned long`, which promotes safely. The patch also introduces two new helpers, `git_parse_size_t()` and `git_config_size_t()`, to correctly parse the now-widened `max_delta_cache_size` configuration value and reject negative inputs. This fixes a latent bug where negative values could be misinterpreted as large positive numbers due to type mismatches.

The patch touches five files: `builtin/pack-objects.c` (the core widening), `config.c` and `parse.c` (the new parsing helpers), and their corresponding headers. The changes are mechanical and do not alter behavior or on-disk formats. The new helpers follow the established pattern for type-specific configuration parsing, ensuring consistency with the rest of Git’s codebase.

This patch is a prerequisite for later work in the series, specifically for removing `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shims in `try_delta()` once `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` are widened. The series is uncontroversial, well-motivated by real-world truncation issues on 64-bit Windows, and ready for integration. The patch itself is clean, with clear commit messaging and no open questions.
