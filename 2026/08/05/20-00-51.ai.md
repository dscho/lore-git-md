This is a substantive review reply in the `writev(3p)` wrapper revival series. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) endorses Junio C Hamano’s architectural resolution—explicitly documenting that `writev(2)` emulation may be non-atomic and declaring competing writes to the same destination a bug—but raises a forward-compatibility concern: the name `git_writev()` (or `git_write_gather()`) advertises atomicity, which cannot be guaranteed on all platforms, and future contributors may overlook the non-atomic caveat. He therefore reiterates his strong preference for a name that does not imply POSIX semantics, such as `git_write_gather()`, to prevent misuse in contexts where atomicity is assumed. This is the sole remaining design question; the technical correctness, platform compatibility, and architectural issues have all been resolved. The series remains under review, with no immediate reroll expected while Patrick Steinhardt is at an offsite.
