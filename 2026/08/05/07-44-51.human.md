This is a refactoring patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series unifying Git’s ODB streaming API. It is the seventh patch in the v2 series and performs a purely cosmetic rename: `struct input_zstream_data` in `builtin/unpack-objects.c` becomes `struct zlib_stream`. The new name reflects the structure’s expanded role—it now holds both the zlib decompression state and the unified `odb_stream` base introduced earlier in the series. The change is mechanical, touching only six lines in a single file, and does not alter behavior. Reviewers who commented on the earlier wrapper structures (notably Justin Tobler) may weigh in on whether the new name is clearer, but the patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for merging.
