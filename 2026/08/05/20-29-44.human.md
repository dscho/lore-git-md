This is a brief but decisive maintainer reply in the ongoing discussion about reviving the `writev(3p)` wrapper for performance. Junio C Hamano fully endorses Johannes Sixt’s concern about forward-compatibility: the name `git_writev()` (or even `git_write_gather()`) risks misleading future contributors into assuming atomicity, which cannot be guaranteed on all platforms. He agrees that the API should be renamed to something that does not imply POSIX semantics, effectively resolving the last remaining design question in the series.

The thread is now technically ready for a reroll—all correctness, platform compatibility, and architectural issues have been settled, and the only outstanding task is the mechanical rename. Patrick Steinhardt, the author, is currently at an offsite, so no immediate update is expected. The series remains under review, with maintainer appetite (whether the performance gains justify the complexity) still unaddressed.
