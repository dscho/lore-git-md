This is the final patch in Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch series converting size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The patch widens `git_deflate_bound()`—the last remaining `unsigned long` holdout in the zlib wrapper—from `unsigned long` to `size_t` in both its parameter and return type.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive but brief. He notes that the entire series could have been squashed into a single patch (a surface-level observation) and asks for clarification on the fallback formula used for inputs above zlib’s 32-bit `uLong` range. The formula—`size + (size >> 5) + (size >> 7) + (size >> 11) + 7 + 18`—is zlib’s stored-block worst-case overhead, ensuring the returned bound remains conservative even when `deflateBound()` cannot be called directly. The review does not raise correctness concerns; the question is purely about understanding the mechanism.

The patch itself is mechanical and uncontroversial. It completes the series’ goal of eliminating truncation risks in the delta and pack machinery on platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`. All callers were already widened in earlier patches, so no narrowing conversions remain. This is a platform-compatibility fix with no behavior change for inputs that fit in `uLong`.
