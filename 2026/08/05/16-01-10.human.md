This is a brief follow-up in the review thread for Toon Claes’s four-patch series that speeds up the experimental `git last-modified` command by reusing Git’s Bloom filters. Junio C Hamano checks in on the stalled discussion about patch 4/4—the part that keeps the optimization working for wildcard pathspecs like `*.c`. Taylor Blau replies that the real blocker isn’t that maintainability nit but the earlier correctness issue with `--show-trees`: when the command lists ancestor trees that aren’t actually covered by the user’s pathspec, the output can be misleading. The fix (filtering results with `match_pathspec`) was agreed in principle weeks ago, but Taylor’s reminder signals it still needs to be coded and confirmed in the next reroll. Until that lands, the series won’t move forward.
