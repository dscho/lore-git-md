This is a brief, surface-level review comment on the sixth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series targets 64-bit Windows correctness, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit and can truncate large object sizes.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges a small but helpful detail in the patch: the initialization of `deltalen` to 0, which works around a GCC warning about potentially uninitialized use. The commit message explains this is a temporary measure that will be removed once `deltalen` is widened to `size_t` in a later series. Patrick’s comment is purely appreciative and does not raise any concerns or suggest changes. The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`. No substantive discussion or issues are introduced here.
