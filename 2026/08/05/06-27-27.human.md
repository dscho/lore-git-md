This is a bug report follow-up about `git-replay` and `git-history` silently dropping Git notes during history rewriting.

Patrick Steinhardt replies to D. Ben Knoble’s observation, confirming that the behavior is likely an unimplemented feature rather than a deliberate design choice. He notes that while `git-replay` is considered plumbing (and thus might not need to handle notes), `git-history` is user-facing and should preserve notes automatically. Patrick has logged the issue in GitLab’s tracker for potential inclusion in the next release cycle but invites others to contribute a fix sooner.

The tone is collaborative and pragmatic: no controversy, just a clear acknowledgment that this is a gap worth addressing. The thread remains open for someone to pick up the work, but there’s no urgency or pressure implied. If you care about notes preservation in history-rewriting commands, this is a straightforward, well-scoped issue to tackle.
