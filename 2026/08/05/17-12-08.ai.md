This is a **platform compatibility** review comment on the ninth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series for MinGW/Windows. Junio C Hamano’s reply is **surface-level** and brief, but it introduces a forward-looking suggestion: he proposes that the deprecation of the dashed-form `git-<command>` executables (e.g., `git-commit`) be completed by removing them entirely at the Git 3.0 boundary. The comment is not a request for Johannes to act—Junio explicitly notes that someone else could champion the effort—but it signals that the project may be open to a broader cleanup in the next major release. No objections or technical concerns are raised about the current patch, which remains a targeted, Windows-only change.
