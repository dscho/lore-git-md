This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about reintroducing a `writev(3p)`-like wrapper for performance. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) responds to Junio C Hamano’s endorsement of the proposed `git_write_gather()` API, agreeing that it is acceptable to document that emulation may be non-atomic and that competing writes would be a bug. However, he reiterates his concern about future misuse: the function name itself should not imply atomicity, as developers might overlook the documentation and assume POSIX `writev(2)` semantics. His position remains firm that the API should be renamed to something like `git_write_gather()` to avoid misleading expectations, reinforcing the architectural resolution reached earlier in the thread. The discussion is now focused on naming rather than technical correctness, with no new objections to the underlying design.
