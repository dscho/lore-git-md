This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch v2 series unifying the ODB streaming API. It extends the write-stream infrastructure to support arbitrary object types (not just blobs) by adding an `enum object_type type` field to `struct odb_write_stream` and updating all callers to pass the correct type. The patch fixes the critical bug identified in v1: the in-memory ODB backend now correctly propagates `stream->type` to `hash_object_file()`, preventing silent corruption or hash collisions for non-blob objects. It also updates a stale comment in `odb/transaction.h` that previously assumed blob-only streaming.

The changes are mechanical and touch eight files: `builtin/unpack-objects.c`, `object-file.c`, `odb/source-inmemory.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `odb/streaming.c`, `odb/streaming.h`, `odb/transaction.h`, and the unit test `t/unit-tests/u-odb-inmemory.c`. Key renames include `hash_blob_stream()` → `hash_stream()` and `stream_blob_to_pack()` → `stream_to_pack()`, reflecting the new generic infrastructure. The patch is a prerequisite for the later unification of `odb_write_stream` and `odb_read_stream` into a single `odb_stream` structure.

The fix for the in-memory ODB backend (propagating `stream->type` to `hash_object_file()`) resolves the blocker identified by Junio in v1, clearing the way for the series to advance. The patch is well-motivated and aligns with the broader ODB abstraction effort, though it does not yet exercise the new functionality (streaming non-blob objects) in practice. Reviewers may focus on edge cases around object type propagation or the completeness of the mechanical updates.
