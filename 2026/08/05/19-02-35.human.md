This is a documentation patch (part 1 of 2 in v2) improving the `git-refs(1)` man page. The patch moves a critical warning about ref migration limitations—currently buried in a "KNOWN LIMITATIONS" section at the end of the page—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand description. The warning text itself is unchanged; only its location and formatting are updated to make it harder for users to overlook.

The motivation is clear: the current placement requires scrolling three screens in `man(1)` from the `migrate` subcommand to reach the warning, which describes a serious risk (concurrent writes can lead to inconsistent migrated state). The patch addresses this by elevating the warning to a visually distinct admonition block immediately after the subcommand synopsis, following established patterns in other Git man pages (e.g., `git-clone(1)` and `git-blame(1)`).

This is the first patch in a two-part series. The v2 update switches the admonition block from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to align with AsciiDoc’s documented semantics, addressing the only feedback from v1.
