No new behavior or symbols are introduced, and the patch is uncontroversial. The fix aligns with the series' broader goal of hardening code paths against silent failures without altering functionality or on-disk formats. The reftable subsystem is the only area touched, and the existing unit tests implicitly cover the new error paths. All substantive review feedback for the series has already been addressed in other patches.
