This is the v2 iteration of Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series is a platform-compatibility fix targeting 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.

The v2 revision addresses all review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on v1. Key fixes include: widening the return type of `sizeof_delta_index()` to `size_t` (Patch 1), introducing `git_parse_size_t()` and `git_config_size_t()` helpers to correctly parse the now-widened `max_delta_cache_size` (Patch 3), and widening the return type of `free_unpacked()` to `size_t` (Patch 4). The commit messages for Patch 12 now include two references to zlib’s stored-block logic for clarity. The series remains mechanical, with no behavior or on-disk format changes.

Files touched span the delta (`diff-delta.c`, `delta.h`), pack-objects (`pack-objects.c`, `pack-objects.h`), zlib (`git-zlib.c`, `git-zlib.h`), and related subsystems (`http-push.c`, `archive-zip.c`, test helpers). New symbols include the `git_config_size_t()` helper and widened fields like `git_zstream.avail_in`/`avail_out`. The series is uncontroversial and preparatory for further `unsigned long` -> `size_t` conversions.

This iteration is ready for review and likely to be merged once any remaining feedback is addressed. The changes are low-risk, well-scoped, and directly address real-world truncation issues on 64-bit Windows. The patch count (12) reflects the breadth of the delta/pack machinery rather than complexity, and the split remains unchanged from v1 despite earlier discussion about squashing.
