This is the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series for `git maintenance`'s geometric repack task, specifically addressing how the `--auto` trigger handles promisor packs. The patch fixes a regression where the auto condition ignored promisor pack geometry, causing the task to skip when promisor packs were ready for rollup but ordinary packs and loose objects didn't meet the threshold.

The change is simple but important: it modifies the auto condition in `builtin/gc.c` to check `geometry.promisor_split` alongside `geometry.split`, ensuring the task runs when either progression indicates work is needed. The test coverage demonstrates this by creating three promisor packs (two small, one large) and confirming the task triggers correctly, rolling up the smaller packs while leaving the large one untouched.

The patch also includes a minor adjustment to `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` to disable auto-maintenance in a filtered clone test, preventing the new condition from interfering with the test's original purpose. This is a clean, well-motivated fix that directly addresses the regression introduced by dcc9c7ef47 (2026-01-05). The series looks ready for review and appears to solve a real-world issue in partial clone repositories.
