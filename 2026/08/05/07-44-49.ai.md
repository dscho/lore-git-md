This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch v2 series refactoring the object database (ODB) streaming API. It merges `struct odb_read_stream` and `struct odb_write_stream` into a single `struct odb_stream` that can handle both directions, eliminating redundancy and preparing the codebase for pluggable ODB backends.

The patch is mechanical except for two special cases: `builtin/unpack-objects.c` and `odb/streaming.c`. In both files, the old `struct odb_write_stream` was embedded directly in a wrapper (`struct input_zstream_data` and `struct read_object_fd_data`, respectively). Now, the wrapper instead embeds the new `struct odb_stream` base and adds its own fields, requiring allocation and container-of access. The rest of the changes are straightforward type updates: replacing `struct odb_write_stream *` with `struct odb_stream *`, renaming `odb_write_stream_read()` to `odb_stream_read()`, and updating function signatures across 14 files.

No behavior changes are intended, and the patch does not introduce new symbols or touch on-disk formats. The wrapper structures (`struct input_zstream_data` and `struct read_object_fd_data`) are left with slightly misleading names; these will be addressed in subsequent patches (Patch 7/8 and Patch 6/8, respectively). Reviewers familiar with the ODB (e.g., Justin Tobler, Taylor Blau) may focus on the wrapper allocation logic and whether the container-of pattern is used correctly, but the patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
