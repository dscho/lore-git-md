This is the third patch in Tian Yuchen’s three-patch refactoring series that reorganizes the `repo_config_values` structure in `environment.c` and `environment.h`. The patch reorders the structure’s fields and their initialization to match the order of Git’s configuration sections (core, apply, branch, pack, push, sparse). The goal is purely cosmetic: aligning declaration and initialization order to make the code easier to review and maintain. No new fields are added, no function signatures change, and no behavior is altered. The patch is a clean, mechanical reordering that follows the pattern established in the earlier patches of the series.
