This is the final patch in Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The patch widens `git_deflate_bound()`—the last remaining `unsigned long` holdout in the zlib wrapper—from `unsigned long` to `size_t` in both its parameter and return type.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive but brief. He questions whether the twelve-patch split was necessary, suggesting the changes are trivial enough to squash into a single patch. He also asks for clarification on the fallback formula used for inputs above zlib’s `uLong` range (>4 GiB on 64-bit Windows), noting unfamiliarity with zlib’s stored-block mechanism and asking whether the formula is an upper-bound estimate. His feedback is technical and focused on understanding the implementation, not on disputing the change itself.

The patch itself is mechanical and platform-specific. It introduces a compile-time guard (`#if SIZE_MAX > ULONG_MAX`) that detects platforms where `uLong` is narrower than `size_t`. On those platforms, inputs above `uLong`’s range fall back to zlib’s stored-block formula plus worst-case overhead, while smaller inputs continue to use the existing `deflateBound()` path. All callers were already widened in earlier patches, so no narrowing conversions remain. The change is uncontroversial and completes the series’ goal of eliminating truncation risks in the delta and pack machinery.
