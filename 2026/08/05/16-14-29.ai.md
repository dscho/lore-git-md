This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The series is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`.

This patch widens the `bufsize` parameter of `create_delta_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The change is mechanical and affects only the function signature in `delta.h` and its implementation in `diff-delta.c`. The sole caller (`try_delta()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`) passes an `unsigned long`, which promotes safely to `size_t`, so no caller-side changes are needed. The patch is split out to keep the subsequent widening of `diff_delta()` and `create_delta()` (which ripple to multiple callers) in a separate commit, maintaining the series’ incremental structure.

The patch is uncontroversial and directly addresses the truncation risk on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced. The change is low-risk and aligns with the series’ goal of type correctness. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and scope, and the patch itself is minimal and focused.
