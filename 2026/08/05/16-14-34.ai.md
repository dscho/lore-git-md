This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 twelve-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The patch targets platform compatibility, specifically addressing truncation risks on 64-bit Windows where `unsigned long` is 32-bit.

The patch widens two tightly coupled types: the `*left` output parameter of `use_pack()` (in `packfile.h` and `packfile.c`) and the `avail_in`/`avail_out` fields in `struct git_zstream` (in `git-zlib.h`). These changes are bundled together because four call sites in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `pack-check.c` pass `&stream.avail_in` directly to `use_pack()`, making separate widening impractical without introducing bridge variables. The patch also updates local variables in `builtin/pack-objects.c` (`avail`, `remaining`) and `pack-check.c` (`remaining`) to `size_t` for consistency, though these are bounded by `core.packedGitWindowSize` and do not introduce new >4 GiB capabilities.

The change is mechanical and preparatory: it enables the next patch (08/12) to widen `do_compress()`, removing the last `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shim in `write_no_reuse_object()`. The `git_zstream` fields already accept `size_t`-shaped inputs in practice due to `zlib_buf_cap()`’s 1 GiB cap, so this patch aligns the type system with existing behavior. Files touched include `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `git-zlib.h`, `pack-check.c`, `packfile.c`, and `packfile.h`.

The patch is uncontroversial and part of the broader `unsigned long` -> `size_t` conversion effort. It carries no behavior or on-disk format changes, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale for bundling the two widenings. The series as a whole is ready for integration, with all prior review feedback addressed.
