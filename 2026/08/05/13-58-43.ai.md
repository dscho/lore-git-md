This is the final reply in the thread wrapping up Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch series to widen size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The exchange clarifies the last open question about the fallback formula in `git_deflate_bound()` and lightly revisits the series’ structure.

Johannes confirms that the fallback formula in Patch 12 is indeed an upper-bound estimate for the worst-case deflated size when the input exceeds zlib’s `uLong` range (>4 GiB on 64-bit Windows). The formula mirrors zlib’s own stored-block logic, simplified for large inputs. He also notes that zlib 1.3.2 introduced a `deflateBound_z()` function that accepts `size_t`, but its recency (7 months old) makes it unavailable on stable distributions, so the manual fallback remains necessary for now.

On the series structure, Johannes reiterates his preference for small, trivial patches, while Patrick restates his view that the changes could have been squashed into a single patch. Neither presses the point further; the discussion remains collegial and focused on technical clarity rather than process. All three review issues Patrick raised earlier (the `sizeof_delta_index()` type mismatch, the latent `max_delta_cache_size` parsing bug, and the `free_unpacked()` return type mismatch) were acknowledged and will be fixed in the next iteration, which will also include a new `git_config_size_t()` helper to reject negative configuration values.
