This is a maintainer follow-up in the `writev(3p)` wrapper revival series. Junio C Hamano reviews the two current call sites of `writev()` (or its proposed replacement) in `fast-import.c` and `sideband.c` and confirms Johannes Sixt’s architectural resolution: neither use case requires atomicity, and both are inherently single-writer scenarios. He therefore endorses the proposed `git_write_gather()` API with explicit documentation that its emulation may be non-atomic, and declares that competing writes to the same destination would be a bug regardless of atomicity. This removes the last technical obstacle to the series’ design, leaving only maintainer appetite (whether the performance gains justify the complexity) and self-documentation (the absence of a range-diff to the reverted version) as open questions. The series remains in technical review phase, with no immediate reroll expected while Patrick Steinhardt is at an offsite.
