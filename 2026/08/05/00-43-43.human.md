This is a brief follow-up in the review of Toon Claes’s four-patch series optimizing `git last-modified` with Bloom filters. The series aims to make the experimental command as fast as `git log` when querying file modifications, especially with wildcard pathspecs like `*.c`.

Taylor Blau responds to Junio C Hamano’s question about whether the discussion on patch 4/4 has concluded. Taylor notes that while he hasn’t followed the specific sub-thread between Jeff King (Peff) and Toon, he believes his earlier review comment—regarding the maintainability of the current approach to restoring `bloom_filter_settings` after `prepare_revision_walk()` clears it—still needs resolution before the series can proceed. His comment suggested managing the settings pointer within the `last_modified` struct instead of relying on the revision machinery, arguing this would be cleaner and less fragile.

The series is otherwise technically sound, with prior patches addressing correctness bugs (e.g., `--show-trees` output filtering) and performance improvements. However, this last open point remains a blocker for merging. The thread appears to be waiting for either a consensus on the current approach or a revised implementation. If you’re following Bloom filter optimizations or the `last-modified` command, this is worth monitoring for the final design decision.
