This is the sixth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch v2 series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series is a platform-compatibility fix for 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit) and part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage.

This patch widens the `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` functions in `delta.h` to use `size_t` for all size parameters and output variables. The change is mechanical and affects seven files: `builtin/fast-import.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `delta.h`, `diff-delta.c`, `diff.c`, `t/helper/test-delta.c`, and `t/helper/test-pack-deltas.c`. Callers are updated to match the new signatures, with temporary `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shims where downstream consumers (e.g., zlib’s `avail_in` or test helpers) still expect `unsigned long`. The patch includes a workaround for a GCC warning about uninitialized `deltalen` by initializing it to 0, with a note that this will be cleaned up in a later series.

The patch is uncontroversial and directly addresses the truncation risk for large blobs (>4 GiB) on 64-bit Windows. It builds on earlier patches in the series that widened `create_delta_index()` and related internal fields. No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced. The split between this patch and the rest of the series remains pragmatic, focusing on the delta-encoding API boundary.
