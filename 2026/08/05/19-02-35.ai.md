This is the first patch in a now-approved documentation series (v2) improving the `git-refs(1)` man page. The patch moves a critical warning about ref migration limitations—previously buried in a "KNOWN LIMITATIONS" section at the end of the page—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand description. The change addresses a usability issue: users had to scroll three screens in `man(1)` to find the warning, which describes a risk of inconsistent state due to concurrent writes during migration. The warning text itself is unchanged; only its location and presentation (now a `WARNING` block instead of `CAUTION`) are altered.

The patch touches only `Documentation/git-refs.adoc` and is part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s ongoing documentation cleanup efforts. The v2 update responds to the sole feedback from v1: switching the admonition block from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to align with AsciiDoc’s documented semantics. This change is purely stylistic and does not affect the substance of the patch. The interdiff confirms the admonition block is the only modification from v1.

Junio C Hamano approved the series without reservations, and Patrick Steinhardt’s `Acked-by` has been added to the second patch (which adds a cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)`). The series is queued in `seen` and expected to graduate to `next` in the next "What's cooking" cycle. No open questions remain, and the patch is uncontroversial. The commit message clearly explains the rationale—elevating a buried warning about a potentially destructive operation—and the implementation follows existing patterns in other man pages (e.g., `git-clone(1)` and `git-blame(1)`).

Key technical details: the `WARNING` block uses a `+` continuation line, producing a minor indentation in the rendered output, which the author confirmed is intentional and consistent with other man pages. The patch introduces no new symbols, on-disk format changes, or test coverage requirements. The series is self-contained and complete as of v2.
