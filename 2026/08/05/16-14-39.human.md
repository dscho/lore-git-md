This is the final patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. It widens the `git_deflate_bound()` function—both its parameter and return type—to `size_t`, completing the series’ goal of eliminating truncation risks on 64-bit Windows and other platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`.

The patch is purely mechanical: all callers were already widened in earlier patches, so this change introduces no new narrowing or behavior changes. For inputs above zlib’s `uLong` range (notably >4 GiB on 64-bit Windows), it falls back to zlib’s stored-block formula plus worst-case overhead, matching zlib’s own fallback logic. The commit message references zlib’s source and an upstream issue for context, making the rationale clear.

This is a low-risk, well-scoped fix that directly addresses real-world truncation issues. The series as a whole is uncontroversial, ready for integration, and part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in Git. No further changes are expected.
