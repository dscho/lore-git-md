This is patch 8/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s series making ODB fsck checks pluggable. It moves bitmap verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`). The change is mechanical: the `verify_bitmap_files()` function is relocated and adapted to operate on a single `odb_source_packed` instance instead of iterating over all sources, since the loop is now handled by the new `odb_fsck()` helper. The patch also replaces the dedicated `ERROR_BITMAP` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, matching the pattern established in patches 6 and 7. Tests in `t/t5326-multi-pack-bitmaps.sh` are extended to verify that bitmap checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags, ensuring the new backend-specific logic behaves identically to the old monolithic implementation.

Files touched: `builtin/fsck.c` (5 lines removed), `odb/source-packed.c` (3 lines added), `pack-bitmap.c` (26 lines changed), `pack-bitmap.h` (function signature updated), and `t/t5326-multi-pack-bitmaps.sh` (8 lines added). Key symbols: `odb_source_packed_fsck()`, `verify_bitmap_files()`, `ERROR_OBJECT`. No on-disk format or CLI changes; this is pure refactoring. The patch is well-scoped and follows the series’ established pattern of moving backend-specific checks into their respective source files while preserving existing behavior.
