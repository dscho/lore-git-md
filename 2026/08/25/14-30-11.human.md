This is patch 9/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s series making Git’s object database (ODB) fsck checks pluggable. It moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches.

The change is mechanical and consistent with the series’ goal: backend-specific checks belong in the backend, not in the top-level fsck command. The patch removes 18 lines from `cmd_fsck()`, adds 27 lines to `odb_source_packed_fsck()`, and extends `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` with a new test that verifies MIDX checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. As in patches 6–8, the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit is dropped in favor of the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting.

No surprises here—this is exactly the kind of refactoring the series promised. The patch is clean, well-tested, and aligns with the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. If the earlier patches in the series looked solid, this one is more of the same and should be uncontroversial.
