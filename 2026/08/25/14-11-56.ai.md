This is patch 7/8 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB alternates refactoring series, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch wires up the new alternates-at-creation mechanism introduced in earlier patches, eliminating the need for a separate `write_alternates()` callback.

The change is straightforward: `create_object_database()` now accepts a `strvec` of alternates paths, and `cmd_clone()` passes the collected alternates directly to it instead of manually writing them afterward. This consolidates alternates setup into a single step during ODB creation, aligning with the series’ goal of making alternates an ODB backend implementation detail. The diff is small (5 insertions, 8 deletions) and mechanical, touching only `builtin/clone.c`, `setup.c`, and `setup.h`.

No new behavior is introduced; this is purely an internal refactoring. The patch removes the last direct use of `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`, a function that will be removed in the next patch. Reviewers will likely focus on whether the alternates deduplication and error handling in `odb_source_files_create_on_disk()` (introduced in patch 6) are sufficient for this new usage, though the patch itself is unlikely to be controversial.

The series remains on track for its goal of simplifying the ODB interface. This patch is a key step toward removing the `write_alternates()` callback entirely, which will happen in patch 8. The broader ODB abstraction effort continues to progress incrementally.
