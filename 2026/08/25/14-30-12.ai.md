This is the final patch (10/10) in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB fsck pluggability series. It moves loose-object verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the loose ODB backend (`odb/source-loose.c`), completing the refactoring begun in earlier patches. The patch removes 90 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 89 lines to `odb/source-loose.c`, introducing a new `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` flag to preserve the original behavior of printing status messages when `--verbose` is passed. A latent bug in the progress-meter logic is fixed as a side effect: the old code captured the progress meter in the callback data before it was initialized, causing the meter to jump from 0 to 256 at completion; the new code initializes the meter first, restoring incremental updates. The patch is purely mechanical and does not change user-visible behavior.
