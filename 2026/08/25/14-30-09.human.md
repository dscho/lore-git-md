This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s object integrity verification (`fsck`) to make it pluggable per ODB backend. This patch moves reverse index verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), completing the backend-specific migration of packfile checks begun in patch 6.

The change removes 37 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 39 lines to `odb_source_packed_fsck()`, including a new `verify_reverse_indices()` helper. It also drops the undocumented `ERROR_PACK_REV_INDEX` bit in favor of the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting. Two subtle behavior changes are introduced: reverse index checks are now skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed (aligning with the flag’s documented intent) and when `--no-full` is used with non-local sources. A new test in `t/t5325-reverse-index.sh` verifies these semantics.

The patch is clean and well-motivated, continuing the series’ theme of de-globalizing fsck logic and encapsulating backend-specific checks. The test addition is minimal but sufficient to lock in the new behavior. No controversy is evident; this looks like a straightforward step in the ODB abstraction effort.
