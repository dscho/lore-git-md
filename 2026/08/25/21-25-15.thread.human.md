This thread is about a proposed change to `git branch -d` that would prevent accidental deletion of a local branch if it serves as the upstream for another local branch, requiring `-D` (force) to override. The series, submitted by Harald Nordgren, consists of two patches: a preparatory refactoring of helper functions in `builtin/branch.c` and the core logic implementing the protection.

The key point of contention is backward compatibility. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has raised a substantive objection, arguing that altering the long-established semantics of `-d` could break existing workflows. The author acknowledges this concern but suggests there may have been a misunderstanding, referencing prior discussions where Junio and Phillip Wood expressed interest in extending branch protections. However, no concrete solution or revision has been proposed to address Junio’s objection, leaving the thread unresolved. The core question remains whether the protective intent justifies changing `-d`’s behavior, even if it aligns with broader goals. The series is still under review, with no merge status reported.
