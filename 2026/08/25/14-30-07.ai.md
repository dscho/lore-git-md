This is patch 5/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s series making ODB fsck checks pluggable. It introduces the infrastructure that will allow each ODB backend (loose, packed, multi-pack, etc.) to implement its own consistency checks.

The patch adds a new `fsck` callback to the `struct odb_source` vtable and wires up empty stubs for all existing source types (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, `packed`). It also defines the `odb_fsck_options` structure and the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag that will control whether full or lightweight checks are performed. In `builtin/fsck.c`, the patch replaces the old `check_full` boolean with the new options structure and calls the new `odb_fsck()` helper, which iterates over all sources and invokes their respective `fsck` callbacks.

No behavior changes yet—this is pure scaffolding. The real work of moving the existing fsck logic into the backend-specific callbacks will follow in later patches. The patch touches eight files, adding roughly 100 lines of new declarations and boilerplate.
