This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s v2 series optimizing reftable stack reloads during transactions. The patch moves the list-file lock from `struct reftable_addition` into `struct reftable_stack` and adds a per-addition `locked` bit field to enforce correct lock ownership when multiple `reftable_addition` instances operate on the same stack. The change is mechanical and touches only `reftable/stack.c`, `reftable/stack.h`, and the unit-test file; it does not alter on-disk format or external APIs.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive but narrowly scoped. He expresses mild unease about the fragility of tracking lock state via a bit field, suggesting a preference for a more direct proxy mechanism, but concedes that the limited scope of the change may make the current approach acceptable. His feedback does not identify a concrete flaw or propose an alternative implementation; it is a cautionary note rather than an objection. He concludes by endorsing the series as a whole.

The patch directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s safety concern from the v1 review (premature lock release when multiple additions operate on the same stack) and is a prerequisite for the final optimization in patch 4. A new unit test validates that a second addition correctly fails with `REFTABLE_LOCK_ERROR` instead of releasing the lock held by the first. The thread’s earlier summary confirms that all substantive feedback from v1 has been incorporated and that the series is technically complete, with the only remaining open question being Junio’s acceptance of the safety resolution. Patrick’s review does not reopen that question.
