This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. It moves packfile verification out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into the packed ODB source (`odb/source-packed.c`), a key step in the series’ goal of decentralizing fsck logic.

The patch threads progress meters and object callbacks through the new `struct odb_fsck_options`, letting the caller’s preferences (e.g., progress display) flow through without exposing backend-specific details at the `cmd_fsck()` level. It also drops the undocumented `ERROR_PACK` bit in favor of the generic `ERROR_OBJECT`, simplifying error reporting for pluggable backends. The change is mechanical but non-trivial: 33 lines are removed from `builtin/fsck.c`, while `odb/source-packed.c` gains 46 lines of new verification logic.

No user-visible behavior changes are introduced, but the patch does alter internal error semantics by removing `ERROR_PACK`. The commit message acknowledges this, noting the bit was neither tested nor documented, and its backend-specific nature made it incompatible with the new pluggable design. The patch looks clean and well-motivated, aligning with the series’ broader refactoring goals. Reviewers may want to verify the progress meter and callback plumbing work as intended across all backends.
