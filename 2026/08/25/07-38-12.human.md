This is the latest update in Elijah Newren’s two-patch bugfix series addressing a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The series targets a crash in `git replay` (and missing-object errors in other commands) caused when a stale multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by geometric repacking. The core fix, now in its second iteration, adopts Jeff King’s tri-state design for `fill_midx_entry()` and gates the fallback recovery logic on `SECOND_READ` to optimize performance for `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` callers.

This email is a direct reply to Derrick Stolee’s substantive review of patch 2/2. Stolee highlighted that the race condition is already partially mitigated by `git multi-pack-index expire`, which deletes packfiles only after ensuring no processes are using them—critical for Windows stability. Elijah acknowledges Stolee’s point and proposes teaching geometric repacking to use the `expire` mechanism as a complementary measure. This would reduce the frequency of the race condition while preserving the fallback logic in patch 2/2 as a safeguard. The response is brief but signals alignment with Stolee’s architectural suggestion, framing it as a natural extension of the current fix rather than a replacement.

The thread is now converging on follow-up work rather than the core fix itself. The tri-state design and `SECOND_READ`-gated fallback have addressed the main technical objections, and no further changes to the current patches are expected. The next steps will likely involve a separate patch series to integrate geometric repacking with the `expire` mechanism, reducing reliance on the fallback logic. For now, the series remains a high-priority candidate for merging, with no unresolved technical concerns.
