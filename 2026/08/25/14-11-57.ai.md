This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 8-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation. It removes the now-unused `odb_source_write_alternates()` function and its supporting infrastructure across all ODB backends (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, `packed`).

The patch deletes 118 lines of code, including the `write_alternate` callback from the `odb_source` struct and all backend-specific implementations. The `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` helper, which previously wrapped `odb_source_write_alternate()`, is also removed. This cleanup is the culmination of the series’ goal: alternates are now written exclusively during ODB creation via the new `odb_create_on_disk_options` mechanism introduced in patch 6, eliminating the need for post-creation alternates manipulation.

No user-visible behavior changes are expected, as the series has already migrated all alternates handling to the new creation-time API. The patch is mechanical and uncontroversial, completing the refactoring by removing dead code. Reviewers will likely focus on verifying that no remaining callers exist and that the removal doesn’t introduce regressions in edge cases (e.g., error handling during alternates setup). The changes align with the broader ODB abstraction effort, giving backends full control over their configuration.
