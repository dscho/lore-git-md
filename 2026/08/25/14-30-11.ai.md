This is patch 9/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB fsck pluggability series. It moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`). The change follows the pattern established in earlier patches: the verification logic is relocated to the backend-specific `odb_source_packed_fsck()` function, the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit is dropped in favor of the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, and the new implementation respects the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. A new test in `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` verifies that MIDX checks are skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed and are performed when `--no-full` is used.

The patch removes 18 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 27 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, introducing the `verify_midx()` helper that spawns `git multi-pack-index verify` as a child process. The helper checks the repository’s `core_multi_pack_index` setting and threads the progress meter flag through the `odb_fsck_options` structure. No user-visible behavior changes are introduced beyond the flag-respecting semantics already described in the series cover letter.
