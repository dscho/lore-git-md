This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s four-patch v2 series optimizing reftable stack reloads during reference transactions. The series targets a performance bottleneck in the reftable backend where excessive `fstat()` calls during bulk ref creation were causing linear slowdowns with ref count.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply here is a brief, substantive review of the patch that moves the list-file lock from `struct reftable_addition` into `struct reftable_stack` and adds a per-addition `locked` bit field. He expresses mild unease about the fragility of tracking lock state via a bit flag, suggesting a preference for a more direct mechanism, but ultimately accepts the approach as workable given the limited scope of its use. His feedback is narrowly focused on this implementation detail and does not question the patch’s correctness or the series’ overall goal. The rest of his message is a straightforward acknowledgment that the series looks good to him.

This patch directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s safety concern from the v1 review—premature lock release when multiple `reftable_addition` instances operate on the same stack—and is validated by a new unit test. With Patrick’s qualified approval, the series now has positive feedback from two key reviewers (Patrick and Jeff King), and the only remaining open question is whether Junio will accept the resolution of his concern, which this patch implements. The series is technically complete, well-tested, and shows clear performance gains.
