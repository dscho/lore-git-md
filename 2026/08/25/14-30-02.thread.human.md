The refactoring touches `builtin/fsck.c` and the ODB backend files, introducing a new `fsck` callback in the ODB source vtable and moving each backend’s verification logic into its own file. Along the way, it fixes a long-standing discrepancy in the `--full` flag’s behavior (ensuring it verifies loose objects as documented) and replaces several undocumented error bits (`ERROR_PACK`, `ERROR_BITMAP`, etc.) with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT`. Tests verify that the new backend-specific checks respect `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only`. The series depends on two earlier ODB topics but has no other open dependencies.
