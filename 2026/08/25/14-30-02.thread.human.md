This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s object integrity checks (fsck) to make them pluggable per ODB backend, preparing the ground for future storage backends like reftable. The series is now complete with the tenth patch, which moves loose-object verification into the loose ODB backend, tidying up a latent progress-meter bug along the way. No user-visible behavior changes; the goal is purely internal restructuring.

The series has been under review since its v1 posting on 2026-08-24. Earlier patches already relocated packfile, reverse-index, bitmap, and multi-pack-index checks into their respective backends, replacing dedicated error bits with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and ensuring all checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. The final patch mirrors that pattern for loose objects, completing the migration. No substantive review feedback has surfaced yet; the thread is still in its early review phase.
