This is patch 6/10 in Patrick Steinhardt’s series making ODB fsck checks pluggable. It moves packfile verification out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into `odb/source-packed.c`, threading the progress meter and object callback through the new `struct odb_fsck_options` so that the caller’s preferences are respected without leaking backend details. The patch drops the undocumented `ERROR_PACK` bit (which was never exercised in the test suite) in favor of the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting for pluggable backends. The change is mechanical: 33 lines removed from `cmd_fsck()`, 46 lines added to `odb/source-packed.c`, and a small addition to `odb.h` to expose the progress flag and callback fields. No behavior change is intended beyond the error-bit simplification.
