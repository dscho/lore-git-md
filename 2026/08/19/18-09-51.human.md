This is a review of the first patch in Elijah Newren’s two-patch bugfix series that prevents `git replay` from crashing when merge inputs are unreadable. Junio C Hamano’s feedback is surface-level: he confirms the logic error (treating any non-zero `result->clean` as success) is obvious and well-fixed, but points out two minor test-script issues. First, the explicit check for “Segmentation” in the test output is redundant because `test_must_fail` already guarantees the command did not segfault. Second, the spelling of “Segmentation fault” is implementation-dependent, so grepping for it is unreliable. The review does not question the patch’s substance or suggest deeper changes; it is a clean bill of health with two small test-cleanup nits.
