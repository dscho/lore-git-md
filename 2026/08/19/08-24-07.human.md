This is a surface-level review of the third patch in D. Ben Knoble’s `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series, which is cooking in `next`. Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback focuses on wording, documentation clarity, and minor code structure improvements—no substantive technical concerns remain.

The feedback is purely mechanical and doesn’t challenge the patch’s core design or correctness. The series remains on track for integration once these minor adjustments are addressed.
