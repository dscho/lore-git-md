This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Vincent Mailhol’s four-part v3 series adding Bash completion for `git history`. Patrick Steinhardt explicitly endorses the v3 revision, calling it "a lot more maintainable" than earlier versions. His comment acknowledges a deliberate trade-off: the new `__git_history_has_revision()` helper may produce false positives in revision detection (e.g., misinterpreting an option value as a revision), but this is an acceptable compromise to avoid hard-coded option names and improve long-term maintainability. The review is brief but substantive—it engages with the core design decision and signals readiness for integration. No further changes are requested or implied. The series remains on track for inclusion once the trivial rebase for the `squash` subcommand is applied.
