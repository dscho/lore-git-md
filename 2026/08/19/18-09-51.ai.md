This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano to the first patch in Elijah Newren’s two-patch bugfix series. The series targets a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism that can cause `git replay` to crash when objects become temporarily unreadable due to a stale multi-pack-index (MIDX) referencing removed packfiles.

Junio’s review is **substantive but narrow**. He confirms the root issue—`pick_regular_commit()` incorrectly treated any non-zero `result->clean` as success, when in fact negative values signal unreadable objects—calling it “an obvious mistake” and endorsing the fix. On the test side, he points out that `test_must_fail` already guarantees the command fails gracefully, making the explicit `test_grep ! "[Ss]egmentation"` redundant and unreliable (since `strsignal()` output is implementation-defined). The review does not question the overall design or the need for the patch, nor does it touch the second patch in the series.

The feedback is purely mechanical and uncontroversial; expect a v2 that drops the redundant grep check while keeping the rest of the patch intact.
