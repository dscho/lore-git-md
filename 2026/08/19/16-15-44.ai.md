**Summarizer brief mode**

Junio C Hamano replies to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the final patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series (v3 3/3), addressing whether to warn users when `core.useNanosec=true` is set on a `NO_NSEC` platform. The series replaces the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with the runtime `core.useNanosec` config option and is cooking in `next` under the topic `dk/use-nsec-runtime`.

Junio **rejects the idea of a runtime warning**, arguing that users who share a `.gitconfig` across machines with different Git builds (some with `NO_NSEC`, some without) would find a warning "obnoxious." This aligns with D. Ben Knoble’s earlier position that better documentation (rather than runtime warnings) is sufficient to address platform/filesystem limitations. The decision leaves the responsibility on users to understand the risks of enabling `core.useNanosec` on unsupported platforms, relying on the documentation’s warning (which Patrick Steinhardt requested and the author agreed to add).

The exchange does not revisit the other open polish items from Patrick’s review (unconditional initialization of `use_nanosec`, simplifying preprocessor guards, or the missed `USE_NSEC` reference in `builtin/update-index.c`), nor does it touch on the broader architectural question of cache location (`repo_config_values` vs. `repo_settings` vs. global variable). The focus is narrowly on **user experience** and **cross-platform compatibility**, with Junio prioritizing silence over proactive warnings for shared configurations.

Key technical context for follow-up:
- **Config key**: `core.useNanosec` (defaults to false).
- **Platform behavior**: On `NO_NSEC` platforms, the `use_nanosec` field and related code paths are `#ifdef`-ed out, preventing silent corruption but leaving the config setting ignored.
- **Documentation**: The patch will include a warning about platform/filesystem limitations (e.g., "may not be safe on all platforms"), but no runtime warning will be emitted.
- **Shared config scenario**: Users with a single `.gitconfig` across machines with different builds (e.g., one with `NO_NSEC`, one without) will not receive warnings when `core.useNanosec=true` is set on the unsupported platform.

The thread’s remaining open questions (initialization, preprocessor guards, missed reference) are unaffected by this decision. The series is otherwise technically complete pending the author’s response to Patrick’s review.
