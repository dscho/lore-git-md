This is the **author's response** to Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of the final patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series (v3 3/3). The series converts the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro into a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`) and is cooking in `next`.

D. Ben Knoble **acknowledges the correctness issue** Junio raised: the `use_nanosec` field in `struct repo_config_values` is unconditionally defined but only initialized when `NO_NSEC` is *not* defined, risking uninitialized reads in `NO_NSEC` builds. Knoble now plans to **unconditionally initialize the field to `0`** in `repo_config_values_init()`, regardless of `NO_NSEC`, and to **remove the `#ifndef NO_NSEC` guards** around the field’s usage in `is_racy_stat()`. This aligns with Junio’s suggestion to simplify the logic into a single ternary expression, ensuring the field always holds a valid value (either `0` or the configured setting).

Knoble also **clarifies the intent** behind the original conditional compilation: to avoid carrying dead code in `NO_NSEC` builds. However, he now agrees that unconditionally compiling the `use_nanosec` checks (while ignoring the config variable in `NO_NSEC` builds) is cleaner and safer, as it avoids hidden dependencies on preprocessor state. The compiler should optimize away the redundant checks in `NO_NSEC` builds, preserving performance.

The response **does not address the other polish items** Patrick Steinhardt raised (documentation warnings, the missed `USE_NSEC` reference in `builtin/update-index.c`), but those are minor and unrelated to the correctness fix. The series remains technically complete pending this update, and the change is straightforward. The patch is now one step closer to readiness for `master`.
