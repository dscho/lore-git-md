The review is **not surface-level**: it engages with the code’s behavior (e.g., warning users about unsafe configurations), identifies a potential correctness issue (uninitialized field), and suggests structural improvements (simplifying preprocessor guards). No performance concerns are raised, and the architectural question about cache location (`repo_config_values` vs. `repo_settings` vs. global variable) is not revisited here.
