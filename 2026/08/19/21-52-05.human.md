This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing ODB pluggable packfile series. Justin Tobler asks a clarifying question about patch 6/6: whether the goal is to let `git bundle` work with non-files ODB backends while still producing standard packfiles as the bundle format. The answer is yes — the series decouples transport commands (fetch, push, bundle) from the files backend, but the on-disk format remains unchanged, so bundles are still ordinary packfiles. The question is surface-level and does not challenge the patch; it simply confirms the intended design.
