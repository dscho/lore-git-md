This is a short follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the new `report` hook for `git receive-pack`. The patch, introduced by Karthik Nayak, adds a server-side hook that lets administrators filter or modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates are committed but before the client receives confirmation. The hook is motivated by GitLab’s need to implement multi-version concurrency control (MVCC), where the status report must reflect the repository state after all updates are finalized.

The thread has focused on two unresolved questions: whether the hook’s exit status should override its stdout, and how to document the `pkt-line` format. In this email, Karthik and Patrick Steinhardt reach a tentative agreement that a non-zero exit status should remain a “nuclear option” for exceptional cases, even if it means discarding the hook’s output. This resolves one ambiguity but leaves the design choice open for further review. Karthik also acknowledges Patrick’s feedback on balancing detail in commit messages, though no changes to the patch itself are announced here.

The patch is still under active review, with the exit status behavior now framed as a deliberate design decision rather than a bug. The discussion has clarified the hook’s necessity—none of Git’s existing hooks cover the timing window between ref updates and client confirmation—but the implementation may yet evolve before merging. For now, the patch remains unchanged, and the thread is converging on a shared understanding of the trade-offs involved.
