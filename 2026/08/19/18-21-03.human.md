This is the second patch in Elijah Newren’s two-part bugfix series addressing a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The patch modifies the packed-object backend (`odb/source-packed.c`) to recover gracefully when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed by a concurrent repack. The fix ensures that object lookups can still succeed by scanning all packs covered by the MIDX when the normal lookup fails, preventing crashes or missing-object errors in commands like `git replay`, `git merge-tree`, and `git cat-file`.
