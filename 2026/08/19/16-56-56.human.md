This is a substantive review comment on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series v2 introducing a pluggable ODB interface for packfile generation. Elijah Newren identifies a potential use-after-scope bug in the files-backend implementation (`odb_source_files_generate_pack()`). The issue stems from the interaction between `clean_on_exit = 1` (which registers the local `child_process` struct for cleanup) and the fact that the struct is function-local, while the cleanup handler retains a pointer to it. Although the normal teardown path works correctly, an abnormal exit could trigger cleanup of a dangling pointer, risking undefined behavior.

The review is narrowly focused on this one technical detail in the patch’s error-handling logic, but it is a serious correctness concern. Elijah’s analysis is precise: he traces the code path through `start_command()`, `mark_child_for_cleanup()`, and `cleanup_children()`, and explains why the bug might not manifest in the happy path but would surface on abnormal exit. No other aspects of the patch are commented on, and the rest of the series is not discussed. Contributors working on the ODB abstraction or transport-layer refactoring should take note, as this issue could affect reliability under edge conditions.
