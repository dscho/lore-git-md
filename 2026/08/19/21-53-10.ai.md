This is the eighth patch in Justin Tobler’s nine-patch v4 series making `git receive-pack` fully ODB-agnostic. The patch addresses a fragility in the ODB transaction system by returning the newly installed temporary ODB source directly from `odb_set_temporary_primary_source()` instead of relying on the caller to assume it is the first entry in the ODB source list.

The change is mechanical and targeted: `odb_set_temporary_primary_source()` now returns the newly installed `struct odb_source *` and reports the previous primary source via an optional `prev_source` out parameter. The propagation continues through `tmp_objdir_replace_primary_odb()`, which now also returns the installed source, and the "files" ODB transaction struct (`struct odb_transaction_files`) gains a `quarantine` field to store it. This eliminates the need for fragile source list ordering assumptions and enables the transaction commit logic to access the quarantine source directly during finalization.

The patch touches `object-file.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `tmp-objdir.c`, and `tmp-objdir.h`, with no behavior change or user-visible impact. It is a refactoring that improves the robustness of the ODB transaction system, aligning with the broader ODB abstraction effort. The change was explicitly approved by Patrick Steinhardt in v3 and remains uncontroversial in v4. The patch is well-motivated, clearly explained, and ready for merging as part of the series.
