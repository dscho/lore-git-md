This is a brief, positive review of the first patch in Vincent Mailhol’s four-part v3 series adding Bash completion for `git history`. Patrick Steinhardt, a seasoned contributor, endorses the latest iteration, calling it "a lot more maintainable" than earlier versions. The key improvement he highlights is the elimination of hard-coded option names in revision detection logic, which simplifies the code and reduces future maintenance burden. While he acknowledges the solution may have false positives, he considers it a pragmatic trade-off—good enough for now. This review signals the patch is on track for integration, with no further changes needed. The series as a whole is now feature-complete and ready for a trivial rebase to include the upcoming `squash` subcommand.
