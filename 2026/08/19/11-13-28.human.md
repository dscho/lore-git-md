This is the second patch in Vincent Mailhol’s four-part series adding Bash completion for `git history`. The patch implements value completion for the `--empty` option, which is supported by the `drop` and `fixup` subcommands and accepts `drop`, `keep`, or `abort`.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a brief, surface-level review. He notes that while the patch restricts completion to the two subcommands that actually support `--empty`, it would also have been acceptable to allow completion for all subcommands (even those that reject the option at runtime). His comment is purely stylistic and does not request a change; he explicitly states that the current version “works for me.” No substantive issues remain, and the patch is ready for integration once the trivial rebase to include the upcoming `squash` subcommand is applied.
