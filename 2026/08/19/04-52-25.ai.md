This is a **bugfix** follow-up in the geometric repacking threshold thread, closing the loop on the last open question.

Patrick responds to Justin Tobler’s acceptance of the patch by digging into Git’s history to explain why the 6700 threshold was originally chosen for `git gc --auto` in 2007. The value was arbitrary but pragmatic: it was set high enough to avoid triggering on a fully unpacked Git v0.99 repository, low enough to trigger on v1.0, and calibrated to avoid redundant repacks during incremental updates. The explanation reinforces that the choice, while not rigorously derived, has been stable for nearly two decades and is now being extended to the geometric repacking heuristic for consistency. No code changes are proposed or implied; this is purely historical context for the benefit of future readers. The patch itself remains unchanged and ready for integration.
