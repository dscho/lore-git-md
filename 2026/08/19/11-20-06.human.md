This is a test patch that adds coverage for documented but untested refname restrictions. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed it and gave it a thumbs-up ("Okay."), though he noted the repetition in the test cases and suggested a loop might be cleaner. He also pointed out a minor oddity: the tests use `heads/foo` instead of the more conventional `refs/heads/foo`, but deemed it harmless.

The patch itself is straightforward and well-motivated. It plugs a small but clear gap in test coverage by explicitly verifying that refnames containing space, `~`, `^`, `:`, `*`, or `[` are rejected, and that the single-character `@` refname is forbidden even with `--allow-onelevel`. The changes are confined to `t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh` and don’t touch production code. Patrick’s feedback is surface-level (style and repetition), so the patch looks ready to land unless someone objects to the loop suggestion.
