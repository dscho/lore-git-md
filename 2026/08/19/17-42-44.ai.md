If that happens, the error message (added in patch 7/7) would not be able to distinguish which copy of the object was corrupt. The reply is brief but identifies a latent correctness issue in the error-reporting mechanism, one that could surface if the multi-pack index (MIDX) ever encounters a malformed pack with duplicate objects.
