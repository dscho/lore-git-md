This is the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s nine-patch v4 series making `git receive-pack` fully ODB-agnostic. The patch removes the global `receive_unpack_limit`, `transfer_unpack_limit`, and `unpack_limit` variables and replaces them with a new helper, `get_unpack_limit()`, that reads the configuration lazily inside the `unpack()` function.

The change is a straightforward refactoring that prepares for the later introduction of the `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface. By moving the unpack limit resolution into the `unpack()` function, the patch ensures that the decision to explode a packfile into loose objects or keep it as a packfile is deferred to the ODB backend, where it belongs. The new `get_unpack_limit()` helper reads `transfer.unpackLimit` and `receive.unpackLimit` (with the latter taking precedence) and defaults to 100 if neither is set.

The patch is clean and well-motivated, with no user-visible behavior change. It touches only `builtin/receive-pack.c` and removes 19 lines while adding 11, making the code simpler and more maintainable. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and the context for the change, and the patch itself is uncontroversial. This is a solid step toward the series’ overall goal of making `git receive-pack` backend-agnostic.
