This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing architectural discussion about how to store batch results in `fetch-object-info.c`. Junio C Hamano acknowledges Jeff King’s point that a separate bit is less awkward for signaling unknown objects, and agrees that sentinel values like `OBJ_NONE` work for `type` but do not extend cleanly to other fields such as `size`. He notes in passing that some recently posted patches abuse `((size_t)-1)` as a sentinel, but does not endorse or reject the practice here. The exchange remains focused on refining the hybrid design (single array of lightweight structs, caller-controlled field population) that will replace the current `object_info`-based parsing logic in the next revision of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series. No new technical direction is introduced; this is a minor clarification confirming the design constraints.
