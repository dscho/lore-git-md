This is a brief follow-up in the `git add --resolved` series, specifically addressing a technical correction to patch 3/4. Junio C Hamano acknowledges a logic error in the option incompatibility check Michael Montalbo flagged in his review. The fix is straightforward: replacing `0 <= addremove_explicit` with `0 < addremove_explicit` in `die_for_incompatible_opt3()` ensures the check only triggers for explicit `-A` or `--all` usage, not when `--resolved` is combined with `--no-all` or `--ignore-removal`. Junio also reflects on the broader confusion in `git add`'s option parsing, where behavior flags (like `addremove`) are conflated with command-line options, making the logic harder to follow. He suggests a future refactoring to separate these concerns—first detecting incompatible options, then deriving behavior from the primary mode—but this is a tangential observation, not a blocker for the current series. The correction is minor and uncontroversial, and the series remains on track for integration once this and the previously noted test infrastructure fix are applied.
