Junio C Hamano replies to Michael Montalbo’s correctness concern about the binary-file check in `has_conflict_markers()` (patch 3/4 of the `git add --resolved` series). This is a broader design consideration that would affect the entire series and potentially other conflict-marker consumers (`diff.c`, `rerere.c`). No decision is made; the question is left open for further discussion.
