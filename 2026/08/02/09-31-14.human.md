This is the first patch in the v5 series adding the `--reset-when-found` option to `git bisect`. It is a preparatory refactoring that adds a `quiet` parameter to the `bisect_reset()` function, allowing callers to suppress the progress and branch-status output of the checkout that restores the original HEAD. The change is purely internal: no behavior changes yet, and no callers use the new parameter. The patch touches only `builtin/bisect.c` and is uncontroversial.

The motivation is to clean up the eventual auto-reset logic: when `git bisect` automatically resets after finding the first bad commit, the checkout should be silent to avoid cluttering the output in automated workflows. The patch is small, well-explained, and mechanically sound. It is clearly part of a larger feature effort, but stands on its own as a sensible cleanup. No tests are added here; the feature’s test coverage will come in the next patch.
