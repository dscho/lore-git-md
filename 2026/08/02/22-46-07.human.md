This is a maintainer response in the thread about the promisor pack performance patch. Junio C Hamano acknowledges the procedural concern raised by brian m. carlson regarding AI-assisted contributions but does not immediately reject the patch. Instead, he points to the Linux kernel's policy on coding assistants as a reference, noting that while the kernel allows AI-generated code under strict human review and DCO certification, enforcement is challenging. Junio expresses skepticism about how Git could reliably verify that contributors have genuinely reviewed and understood AI-generated code, leaving the policy question unresolved.

The email stops short of offering a decision or even a clear path forward. It neither endorses nor dismisses the patch, nor does it propose a mechanism for handling AI-assisted contributions in the future. The thread remains stalled on the procedural question, with no technical discussion of the patch's merits or performance benefits. For a reader following Git development, this signals that the patch is unlikely to move forward until the project clarifies its stance on AI assistance — a decision that would likely require input from the Project Leadership Committee.
