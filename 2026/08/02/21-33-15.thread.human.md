This thread is about a patch from Arijit Banerjee that speeds up `git index-pack` for promisor packs by 15–26% during `--filter=blob:none` clones. The patch avoids a per-object mutex during link recording, replacing it with thread-local oidmaps, and includes thorough test coverage for edge cases like malformed commit headers. However, the patch was submitted with an AI-assistance disclosure, which triggered a policy objection from brian m. carlson.

The discussion has since shifted entirely to the procedural question of whether the project’s policy on AI-generated code should make an exception for this patch, given its performance benefits. Brian’s latest reply doubles down on the policy stance: the patch must be discarded and rewritten manually to comply with `SubmittingPatches`, with no engagement on whether the speedup justifies an exception. Arijit had already repositioned the patch as an RFC to solicit maintainer guidance on this tradeoff, but the thread remains stalled—no input from Junio or the PLC has arrived to resolve the tension between policy and performance. Until that happens, the patch is effectively blocked.
