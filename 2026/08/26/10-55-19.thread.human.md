Git is considering whether to participate in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort, with Christian Couder initiating the discussion to gather volunteers and community feedback before the September 11 deadline. Christian, who served as both mentor and org admin last year, is willing to reprise those roles unless the community opts out. The project previously funded its own intern after GitHub and GitLab declined sponsorship, and the key questions remain: whether to participate, who can mentor or admin, what projects are suitable, and whether new sponsorship options exist.

Usman Akinyemi, a former Git Outreachy intern, has volunteered to co-mentor this year, adding momentum to the proposal. He emphasized the program’s value by noting that last year’s intern later co-mentored a GSoC participant and will present their work at the upcoming Git conference. His message reinforces the positive impact of Outreachy on the community and provides social proof of its effectiveness. No objections or concerns have been raised so far, and the discussion remains focused on logistics and volunteer recruitment. The thread is still in the proposal stage, with no final decision made.
