Git is moving forward with its application to participate in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort, with Christian Couder set to submit the community application imminently—just days before the September 11, 2026 deadline. The thread, which began as a call for volunteers and feedback, has gained solid momentum: three mentors (Usman Akinyemi, Kaartic Sivaraam, and Pablo Sabater) have confirmed their involvement, alongside Christian and Kaartic as org admins. No objections to participation have been raised, and the focus has shifted to finalizing project ideas and securing sponsorship.

Two concrete project proposals have emerged for potential interns. The first, continuing the removal of global state, may now focus on **libifying parts of the codebase**—a task that aligns with broader architectural goals. The second, **improving command argument and option parsing**, aims to replace scattered, hand-rolled parsing code with standardized APIs like `parse-options` or Christian’s proposed `early-scan` API. Both projects are designed to be newcomer-friendly while addressing long-term maintainability. Christian has also indicated plans to seek corporate sponsorship, specifically reaching out to GitLab, though no new funding commitments have been announced yet. The thread remains open for refining these ideas and ensuring all mentors are signed up before submission.
