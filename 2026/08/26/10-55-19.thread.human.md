This thread is about Git’s potential participation in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort. Christian Couder initiated the discussion to gauge interest and gather volunteers for mentoring and organizational roles before the September 11, 2026, deadline. Momentum has grown steadily: Christian, Usman Akinyemi, Kaartic Sivaraam, and now Pablo Sabater have all volunteered to mentor or serve as org admins. Usman, a former Git Outreachy intern, highlighted the program’s positive impact, noting that last year’s intern went on to co-mentor a GSoC participant and will present at the upcoming Git conference.

The thread remains in the proposal stage, with no formal decision yet, but the expanding volunteer pool has addressed the main hurdle for participation. No new project ideas or sponsorship offers have emerged, and the question of funding—whether Git will again self-fund after GitHub and GitLab declined sponsorship—remains unresolved. With the deadline looming, the focus is now on finalizing project proposals and submitting the application. No objections or alternative proposals have been raised, suggesting broad support for participation.
