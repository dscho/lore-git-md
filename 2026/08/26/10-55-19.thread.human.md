This thread is about Git’s potential participation in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort, an initiative to bring new contributors into open-source projects. Christian Couder kicked off the discussion, seeking volunteers for mentoring and organizational roles, as well as project ideas, before the September 11 deadline. Last year, Git funded its own intern after sponsorship fell through, and Christian is open to repeating his roles unless the community opts out.

Momentum has grown with two confirmed volunteers: Usman Akinyemi, a former Git Outreachy intern who credits the program with his continued involvement, and Kaartic Sivaraam, an experienced mentor for both Outreachy and GSoC. Kaartic has offered to co-mentor or serve as an org admin, and a third potential mentor, Pablo, has also been looped into the discussion. The thread has yet to settle on specific project ideas or secure additional sponsorship, but the expanding pool of volunteers makes participation more feasible. No objections have been raised, and the focus is now on finalizing roles and projects before the deadline.
