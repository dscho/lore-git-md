This is the v4 iteration of Karthik Nayak’s patch series introducing the `receive-report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. The series is now **ready for integration** into `next`; the only change since v3 is the hook’s name, which has been renamed from `report` to `receive-report` to address Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt’s objection to its prior genericness.

The series remains split into three patches: (1) a preparatory documentation patch adding a `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section to `git-receive-pack.adoc`, (2) a mechanical refactoring that extracts the status-report generation logic into a reusable `generate_response()` helper in `builtin/receive-pack.c`, and (3) the core implementation of the new hook. The hook runs after all ref updates are committed but before the status report is sent to the client; it receives the pkt-line encoded report on stdin and replaces it with its stdout, while forwarding stderr to the client via the sideband channel. A non-zero exit status rewrites all ref status lines to `"receive-report hook failed"` and discards the hook’s stdout, matching the behavior of the `pre-receive` hook. The implementation is self-contained and does not use the config-based hook infrastructure.

Files touched are `builtin/receive-pack.c`, `Documentation/githooks.adoc`, `Documentation/git-receive-pack.adoc`, and the new test script `t/t5412-receive-report-hook.sh` (renamed from `t5412-report-hook.sh`). The test suite exercises passthrough, failure modes, stdin/stdout handling, sideband relay, and the interaction between exit status and stderr output. Documentation links to `gitprotocol-pack[5]` for pkt-line format details and justifies the single-script design in the commit message. The series has been queued in `seen` and Junio has manually resolved a conflict with Justin Tobler’s concurrent "pluggable writes" series; Karthik has confirmed the resolution is correct. All prior feedback has been addressed, and the series is now **technically complete**.
