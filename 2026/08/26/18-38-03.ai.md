This is the maintainer's review of the final patch in Delilah Ashley Wu’s three-patch series fixing Git’s `--global` config listing inconsistency. The patch itself is technically sound and aligns with the series’ goal, but Junio C Hamano identifies several structural and stylistic improvements to make the implementation cleaner and more maintainable.

The core suggestion is to consolidate config-scope filtering logic into `config_options` rather than scattering it across multiple functions. Currently, `git_config_system()` checks the `GIT_CONFIG_NOSYSTEM` environment variable separately from the `ignore_system` flag in `config_options`, creating an inconsistency. Junio proposes either teaching `git_config_system()` to respect `config_options.ignore_system` or eliminating the function entirely in favor of the unified `config_options` approach. This would simplify the codebase and avoid awkward conditionals like `!opts->ignore_system && git_config_system()`.

Junio also flags a misleading conditional (`if (!opts->ignore_global)`) that is always true, since no caller currently sets `ignore_global`. While harmless, this could confuse future maintainers. The review further critiques the patch’s line-wrapping (exceeding 100 columns) and suggests restructuring the `config_with_options()` cascade to handle the global scope more explicitly. The proposed fix-up diff replaces the convoluted ternary logic with a dedicated `else if` clause for `CONFIG_SCOPE_GLOBAL`, improving readability.

The review is substantive but constructive, focusing on long-term maintainability rather than functional flaws. The changes requested are mechanical and do not alter the patch’s behavior—only its clarity and consistency with Git’s existing patterns. No new edge cases or regressions are introduced by the suggestions. The series remains on track for integration once these refinements are addressed, with Johannes Schindelin’s Reviewed-by tags still applicable to the technical content. Key files affected: `config.c` (primary) and `config.h` (struct definitions).
