This thread began as a refactoring effort by Junio C Hamano to replace Git’s fixed-arity `die_for_incompatible_optN()` helpers (for 2–4 options) with a single varargs function, `die_for_incompatible_opts()`. The revised v2 series addressed early feedback—fixing a missing `va_end()` call, adopting an `EOF` sentinel, and dropping a parameter-reordering patch—but retained hard-coded error-message formatting to avoid i18n breakage. Key reviewers, including Jeff King (Peff) and René Scharfe, engaged with the design’s trade-offs: Peff advocated for compile-time safety via a `NULL` sentinel, while René proposed alternatives like a macro-enforced `EOF`+`NULL` pattern or type-safe compound literals. Elijah Newren’s review caught the `va_end()` omission, which was fixed in v2.

Junio has now withdrawn the series entirely, citing dissatisfaction with the revised design and the earlier parameter-reordering step. The withdrawal follows Peff’s preference for compile-time safety over the `EOF` sentinel’s flexibility, though no consensus was reached on the technical merits. The thread is now closed, with the topic abandoned pending a potential future redesign. The existing fixed-arity helpers remain in place, and the compile-time safety debate remains unresolved.
