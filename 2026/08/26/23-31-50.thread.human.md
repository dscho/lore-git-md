This thread is a refactoring effort by Junio C Hamano to replace Git’s fixed-arity `die_for_incompatible_optN()` helpers (for 2–4 options) with a single varargs helper, `die_for_incompatible_opts()`. The change is purely internal—no user-visible behavior is altered—and touches `parse-options.c`, `parse-options.h`, and 14 built-in commands.

The initial version drew feedback from Elijah Newren and Jeff King, who identified a missing `va_end()` call and raised concerns about compile-time safety and error-message formatting. Junio has now posted a revised v2 that addresses all issues: the `va_end()` omission is fixed, the helper uses `EOF` (-1) as a sentinel to preserve existing signatures, and static inline wrappers retain compile-time protection for the common 2–4 option cases. The hard-coded error-message formatting (capped at four options) remains to avoid i18n breakage, but the helper is now future-proof for more options if needed.

The patch is under review and appears to be in good shape. All substantive feedback has been incorporated, and the implementation is mechanical with low risk of side effects. No test failures or edge cases have been reported, and the series is expected to graduate soon.
