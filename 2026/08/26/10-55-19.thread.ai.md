Core problem or goal
Git is considering whether to participate in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort. The immediate goal is to gather volunteers (mentors, org admins), project ideas, and community feedback on participation before the September 11, 2026 deadline for mentoring organizations to sign up.

No decision yet; the thread remains in the proposal stage. Last year Git mentored one intern, funded directly by the project after GitHub and GitLab declined sponsorship. Christian Couder is willing to reprise his roles as mentor and org admin unless the community opts out. The thread has gained momentum with the addition of a second volunteer mentor.
