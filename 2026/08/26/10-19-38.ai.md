This is the second patch in Karthik Nayak’s three-patch v4 series introducing the `receive-report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. It is a **refactoring patch** that extracts the status-report generation logic from `report()` and `report_v2()` into a new shared helper, `generate_response()`.

The patch is mechanical: it moves the pkt-line formatting code that both functions share into `generate_response()`, which takes a pre-allocated `strbuf`, the command list, the unpack status, and a boolean flag to control whether v2-specific reports are added. The two callers (`report()` and `report_v2()`) are updated to pass their `strbuf` and the appropriate boolean. No behavior changes are introduced; the patch exists solely to prepare the codebase for the next commit, which will reuse `generate_response()` to regenerate the status report during hook failure.

The diff touches only `builtin/receive-pack.c` and is 84 lines long, with 44 insertions and 40 deletions. The new helper is documented with a brief comment explaining its purpose and the meaning of the `add_reports` parameter. The patch is part of a series that is now technically complete, queued in `seen`, and ready for integration into `next`.
