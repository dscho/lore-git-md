This is a brief follow-up in the review thread for the fourth patch of Justin Tobler’s six-patch series converting `git receive-pack` to use ODB transactions for pluggable packfile writes. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt.

Justin clarifies a minor point from Patrick’s review: the `unpack_status` buffer is not reused in a loop, so there is no memory-allocation inefficiency to address. The earlier suggestion to reuse the buffer was based on a misunderstanding of the control flow in `cmd_receive_pack`. This exchange is purely surface-level and does not affect the patch’s core design or behavior.

The discussion remains constructive and uncontroversial. The next revision of the series will incorporate the already-agreed const-correctness refinements but will leave the buffer initialization unchanged. No new technical questions or objections are raised.
