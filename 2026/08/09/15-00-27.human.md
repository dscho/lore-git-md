The patch is part of a larger libification push but has hit a snag: it conflicts with a recent topic in `seen` (`cf1687a41c`) that splits `setup_git_env_internal()`, the very function this patch modifies. The author, Tian Yuchen, acknowledges the conflict and proposes to wait for the other topic to settle before rerolling.

The implication is that the patch may need to harmonize these two code paths in its next iteration.

For now, the patch is effectively blocked until the conflicting topic lands. That gives the author time to address the architectural loose ends—most notably, whether `fetch_if_missing` truly belongs in `struct repository` or should live elsewhere (Junio’s review in v2 outlined three possible designs, and the current patch adopts only the narrowest fix). The thread is a good example of how even a well-scoped refactoring can get tangled in integration timing and subsystem boundaries.
