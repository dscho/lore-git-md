This is the fifth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series `kh/doc-trailers` (v5). The patch adds a concise preamble to the DESCRIPTION section of `git-interpret-trailers.txt` that explicitly defines the simplest form of a trailer (key-value pair with colon separator), introduces the term “trailer block” for one or more trailers, and standardizes the term “blank line” (empty or whitespace-only) so it can be reused consistently throughout the man page. The change is purely additive: six lines of explanatory text replace a single introductory sentence, with no structural reordering or removal of existing content. The commit message notes that the next patch in the series will define the key format itself.
