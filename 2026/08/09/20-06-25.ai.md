This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s 11-patch documentation series (v5) that was recently merged to `master` as `kh/doc-trailers`. The patch removes the RFC 822 email-header analogy from the opening of `git-interpret-trailers.txt`, replacing it with a direct description of trailers as key–value metadata. The commit message explains that the email-header framing is unnecessary for most users and risks inviting misplaced assumptions about the format’s rules. This change is preparatory: the next patch will introduce a more precise definition of the trailer format. The diff itself is small (three lines added, six removed) and purely textual, with no behavioral or structural changes to the documentation. The patch is well-motivated and uncontroversial, aligning with the series’ overall goal of making the man page clearer and more self-contained.
