This is the latest exchange in Johannes Sixt’s five-patch series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. The discussion remains purely about phrasing and layout of UI labels—no code changes are proposed or needed.

Sixt and Mark Levedahl are refining the wording for the “Diff: old lines background” label. Levedahl suggests grouping related items under section headings like “General Canvas Area” and “Commit Differences” to provide clearer context, while Sixt prefers omitting “Diff” entirely since the dialog’s primary panel is always the diff view. Both agree that “old line background” (singular) is a natural phrasing, and Sixt plans to rearrange the list so that selection, marked lines, and link text appear before the diff-specific colors.

The series itself is technically complete and ready for final review. All patches are confined to `gitk-git/gitk` (Tcl/Tk) and do not affect core Git functionality. The only remaining question is whether to adopt Sixt’s streamlined phrasing or Levedahl’s hierarchical organization for the dialog’s labels. No substantive concerns or edge cases have been raised, and the patches have otherwise received only surface-level review. The thread does not alter the series’ readiness but may influence the final wording of a few UI strings.
