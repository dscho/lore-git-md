This is the eighth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s already-merged 11-patch documentation series (`kh/doc-trailers`) that overhauled the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. The series was motivated by a bug report about silent failures when parsing trailer keys containing periods, and this patch is a purely structural improvement.

The patch reorders three paragraphs that explain how new trailers are added so they appear consecutively, moving two intervening paragraphs about `--trailer` key aliases to a more logical place after the core explanation. The commit message traces the document’s history to show that the current layout was an accidental drift rather than a deliberate choice, and the change restores a more coherent flow without altering any technical content. No new behavior or terminology is introduced; this is purely about readability. The patch is uncontroversial and part of a series that has already reached `master`.
