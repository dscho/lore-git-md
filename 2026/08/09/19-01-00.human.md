This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series making `git receive-pack` fully backend-agnostic for packfile writes. It introduces `odb_transaction_finalize()`, splitting the old `odb_transaction_commit()` into two steps: commit (which makes staged objects visible) and finalize (which performs backend-specific deferred cleanup and frees the transaction). The immediate motivation is the “files” backend’s need to hold on to “.keep” lockfiles after commit until references are updated, but the new interface is generic and will be used by future backends. The patch touches nine files—mostly mechanical updates to call `odb_transaction_finalize()` after every `odb_transaction_commit()`—and lays the groundwork for the rest of the series. It is a refactoring patch with no observable behavior change.
