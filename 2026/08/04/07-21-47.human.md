This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing ODB abstraction series, specifically addressing Junio C Hamano’s review of the third patch. The patch defers object database (ODB) creation until after the ref database is set up, a change that enables future pluggable ODB backends to handle their own on-disk structures.

Patrick confirms Junio’s interpretation: the deferred ODB creation is indeed a preparatory step for introducing an `objectStorage` repository extension, analogous to the existing `refStorage` extension. He explains that the refactoring in `setup.c` is intentionally disentangling repository setup and configuration logic to avoid the pain points encountered when the `refStorage` extension was introduced. The goal is to make the infrastructure clearer and more maintainable before adding the new extension.

The reply is short and to the point, reinforcing the patch’s role in the larger ODB abstraction effort. There’s no new technical detail here, but the confirmation solidifies the patch’s purpose: it’s not just a mechanical refactoring, but a deliberate step toward enabling custom ODB backends. For readers tracking the series, this makes it clear that the patch is foundational work rather than an isolated cleanup.
