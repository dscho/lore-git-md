This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) streaming API. It’s a small, mechanical rename: `struct read_object_fd_data` becomes `struct fd_stream` to reflect that the structure now holds both the stream state and the file descriptor data, not just the latter.

The change is confined to `odb/streaming.c` and updates the structure name, its constructor (`CALLOC_ARRAY`), and the two callback functions (`read_object_fd` → `fd_stream_read`, `close_object_fd` → `fd_stream_close`). The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of unifying the read/write streaming interfaces. No behavior changes are introduced, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale. This is a typical cleanup step in a larger refactor and should merge smoothly once the rest of the series is reviewed.
