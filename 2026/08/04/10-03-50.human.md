This is part of Christian Couder’s 12-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` to reduce global variables and prepare for libification. This specific patch (7/12) introduces `struct fast_import_state` and moves the first three globals—`global_argc`, `global_argv`, and `global_prefix`—into it. The patch is a mechanical refactoring: every function that previously accessed those globals now takes a `struct fast_import_state *` parameter, and the globals are removed from the file. Some functions receive the new parameter only for future use and are marked with `UNUSED` to silence compiler warnings.

The patch is clean and well-structured, with no intended behavior change. It directly addresses Junio’s feedback on the v1 series by eliminating placeholder local variables and fully integrating the parse-options API. The new struct is initialized early in `cmd_fast_import()` and passed through the call stack, setting the stage for further globals to be moved in subsequent patches. The diff is large but straightforward, making it easy to verify that the transformation is purely mechanical. This is a solid step toward the series’ goal of modernizing `git fast-import` and enabling libification.
