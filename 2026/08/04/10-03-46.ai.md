This is a documentation patch, the third in Christian Couder’s 12-patch v2 series refactoring `git fast-import` and modernizing the parse-options API. It adds missing documentation for the `OPT_*_F()` macro variants (which accept a trailing `flags` argument) and the new `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()` and `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()` convenience macros to `Documentation/technical/api-parse-options.adoc`.

The patch explains that the `_F` suffix indicates a macro variant that takes a `flags` argument, which is the bitwise-or of per-option flags like `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN` or `PARSE_OPT_OPTARG`. It then documents `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()`—introduced in patch 1/12—as a way to group hidden options under a header that appears only when `--help-all` is used, and `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()` as a boolean option hidden from `-h` but visible with `--help-all`. Both macros are placed adjacent to their non-hidden counterparts (`OPT_GROUP()` and `OPT_BOOL()`) for clarity.

The change is purely additive and does not alter behavior or code. It completes the documentation coverage for the parse-options API’s hidden-option support, which is used later in the series to standardize `git fast-import`’s usage string. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of improving API clarity and consistency. No review feedback has been reported yet.
