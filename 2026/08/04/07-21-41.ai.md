This is a reply in the ODB abstraction series, specifically addressing Justin Tobler’s design question about the third patch. The patch introduces a flag (`APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION`) to defer object database (ODB) creation, allowing backend-specific on-disk setup. Justin asked why the flag-based approach was chosen over requiring an explicit `odb_new()` call after `apply_repository_format()`, noting there are few call sites.

Patrick’s response is pragmatic. He explains that the current logic for determining the object directory and alternates is complex and intertwined with `apply_repository_format()`, making a clean split difficult. However, he acknowledges that the design might simplify in the future when environment variable handling is moved into the "files" backend, as discussed in a related thread. This suggests the flag is a transitional step rather than a permanent solution. The reply does not resolve the immediate question but signals openness to revisiting the approach later. No new technical details or objections are introduced; the focus remains on initialization sequence design.
