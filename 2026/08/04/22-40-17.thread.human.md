This thread is about a bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper, submitted by Daniel Martí. The patch addresses a crash and silent data loss that occurs when the GNOME keyring daemon silently omits locked or concurrently deleted items from its response. The current implementation uses a flag that discards failures, leading to NULL secret values and subsequent assertion failures in `secret_value_get_text()` or `secret_value_unref()`. Martí’s fix replaces this flag with an explicit error-handling call, preventing the issue without adding extra round-trips to the keyring daemon.

The patch is small (13 lines added, 1 removed) and self-contained, touching only `contrib/credential/libsecret/git-credential-libsecret.c`. Martí reports encountering the bug several times per week, confirming it’s a real and reproducible issue. The commit message clearly explains the problem and solution, and the patch is ready for review. So far, there’s been no feedback from other contributors, and the patch remains unreviewed two weeks after submission.
