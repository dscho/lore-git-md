This thread is about a bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper, which currently crashes and silently loses data when credential items are locked or deleted concurrently. Daniel Martí posted a patch two weeks ago that replaces the problematic `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag with explicit error handling to prevent these issues. Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and proposed an alternative approach that retains the flag while conditionally loading secrets only when needed, aiming to preserve a batch-fetch optimization.

The discussion has narrowed to a design trade-off: Junio’s version keeps the optimization for the common case but adds complexity for rare error scenarios, while Daniel’s version simplifies the code path and ensures all errors are surfaced, though it may slightly alter the optimization. Both agree the patch is correct and that the lack of test coverage is acceptable given the reproducibility challenges. Daniel has just responded to Junio’s feedback, defending his original design and explaining why it avoids the pitfalls of the alternative. A v2 patch is likely next, either incorporating this rationale or sparking further discussion. The series remains under review with no merge decisions yet.
