This is the review feedback on the v2 patch series introducing `git repack --drop-filtered` for partial clones. The reviewer, Siddharth Asthana, is a substantive contributor who previously raised critical safety concerns now addressed in the series.

The review is brief but substantive. Asthana endorses the current implementation, particularly the safety guards (merge/rebase/cherry-pick checks and index validation) and the decision to defer the drop log. He suggests a minor wording tweak in the commit message or documentation: soften the claim that dropped objects are "guaranteed" recoverable, since this depends on the promisor remote still having them (a point Junio also raised). The current phrasing is acceptable for now, but a more precise formulation would help users understand the dependency on the remote.

Asthana also reaffirms the value of the index guard, which prevents dropping blobs that would immediately be re-fetched by the next worktree command. He views the mid-operation guard (preventing drops during merges, etc.) as more of a UX improvement than a correctness requirement, and suggests the documentation should clarify this distinction.

No new technical issues are raised, and the feedback does not block the series. The review signals that the core design and safety mechanisms are now well-justified and ready for broader scrutiny. The series remains a well-crafted, production-ready effort targeting a real pain point for partial clone users.
