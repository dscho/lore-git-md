This is the first patch in Christian Couder’s 12-patch v2 series refactoring `git fast-import` for libification. It introduces the `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro to the parse-options API, allowing hidden options to be grouped under a heading that appears only when `--help-all` is used.

The patch adds the macro to `parse-options.h`, updates the usage formatter in `parse-options.c` to skip hidden groups unless `--help-all` is requested, and extends the test helper (`test-parse-options.c`) and test suite (`t/t0040-parse-options.sh`) to verify that hidden groups and their options are correctly suppressed in normal help output but shown in `--help-all`.

The change is purely additive and does not alter existing behavior; it simply enables future patches in the series to organize hidden options without cluttering the default help text. The implementation is clean and the test coverage is thorough, covering both the presence and absence of the hidden group in the expected outputs.
