This is a follow-up in the ODB abstraction series, specifically about the third patch which defers object database (ODB) creation. Patrick responds to Justin Tobler’s question about why the series uses a flag (`APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION`) to defer ODB creation rather than requiring an explicit `odb_new()` call after `apply_repository_format()`.

Patrick explains that the current logic for determining the object directory and alternates is complex and tightly coupled with `apply_repository_format()`, making a clean split difficult. However, he acknowledges that the design might simplify in the future when environment variable handling is moved into the "files" backend, as discussed in a related thread. This suggests the flag is a pragmatic, transitional step rather than a permanent solution. The reply does not introduce new technical details or objections but clarifies the reasoning behind the current approach and signals openness to revisiting it later.

The patch remains part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, enabling pluggable on-disk ODB structures. The third patch’s role as infrastructure for future non-filesystem backends (via the planned `objectStorage` extension) was previously confirmed, and this reply reinforces that the flag-based design is a necessary intermediate step. No merge status is reported; the series is still under review.
