This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch introduces a `create_on_disk` callback in the ODB source interface and moves the creation of backend-specific directories (`objects/pack`, `objects/info`) from the generic `create_object_database()` function into the "files" backend’s callback.

In this reply, Patrick acknowledges Junio’s suggestion to rename the callback to something more generic (e.g., `initialize`) to better accommodate non-filesystem backends (such as cloud-based object stores requiring provisioning). However, he defers to consistency with the existing `ref_store_create_on_disk()` naming in the ref subsystem, opting to retain the `create_on_disk` name for now. The decision is pragmatic rather than architectural: the callback’s purpose is clear within the current scope, but the naming may need revisiting if non-filesystem backends materialize. No code changes are proposed; the discussion remains about interface design and future extensibility.

The series as a whole is still under review, with this patch now having received its first substantive feedback. The naming question is not a blocker, but it highlights a potential tension between consistency and forward-looking design. Future ODB backends may force a reevaluation of the callback’s name and role.
