This is a **substantive review** of the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series unifying Git’s ODB streaming API. The series refactors the redundant `odb_read_stream` and `odb_write_stream` structures into a single `odb_stream` type, and this patch merges the two into one.

Justin Tobler’s review focuses on the mechanical and structural changes in the patch, particularly the introduction of wrapper structures around the new `odb_stream` base in `builtin/unpack-objects.c` and `odb/streaming.c`. He raises two naming questions: whether `struct input_zstream_data` and `struct read_object_fd_data` (now both wrapper streams) should be renamed to reflect their new role, and whether `odb_write_stream_from_fd()` should be renamed to match the unified API. These are minor but valid points about clarity and consistency. The rest of the patch—renames, call site updates, and the removal of the old `odb_write_stream` API—receives a straightforward "Looks good."

The review is thorough but not deeply technical; Justin does not question the correctness of the wrapper allocations, the lifecycle of the new structures, or edge cases like error handling or file descriptor ownership. His feedback is constructive and aligns with the patch’s goal of eliminating redundancy. The patch itself is clean and well-motivated, touching 14 files without introducing behavior changes. Reviewers may still scrutinize the wrapper allocations and the completeness of the rename, but Justin’s comments suggest no major issues. This is a solid step in the ODB abstraction effort.
