This is a documentation patch follow-up, part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s already-approved two-patch series improving the `git-refs(1)` man page. The series itself is done — Junio has queued it in `seen` and it will graduate to `next` shortly — but this email is a low-key, surface-level exchange about AsciiDoc formatting.

Kristoffer replies to Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier observation that the new “CAUTION” admonition block in the `migrate` subcommand section renders with an extra level of indentation. He clarifies that the open-block syntax (the `+` continuation line followed by the block) is indeed used elsewhere in Git’s documentation, though less frequently than the single-line `NOTE:` style. He points to `git-clone(1)` and `git-blame(1)` as examples where both styles coexist, and includes a quick comparison of how the two render in man and HTML output. The tone is purely informational; he is not arguing for or against the formatting choice, just documenting the precedent.

The email also notes that he originally chose `CAUTION` over `WARNING` after consulting the AsciiDoc reference, though he does not recall the exact reasoning. This is a minor detail and not something he is defending or revisiting.

Nothing here changes the status of the series. The discussion is a casual footnote to an already-resolved thread, useful only for future documentation contributors who might wonder about admonition styling.
