This is a surface-level review comment on patch 3/8 of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support `%(objecttype)` in remote-object-info queries. Karthik Nayak questions the commit message’s claim that `struct object_info_args` “suggests that fetch_object_info() uses all three of them” — he points out that the function *does* use all three fields (`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`) in its current implementation. The comment is brief and does not engage with the broader architectural refactoring (replacing `struct object_info` with `struct fetch_object_info_results`) or the series’ overall goal; it focuses solely on the phrasing of the commit message. No technical concerns are raised, and the feedback is advisory rather than blocking.
