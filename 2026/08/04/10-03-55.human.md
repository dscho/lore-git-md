This is the final patch in Christian Couder’s 12-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` to modernize its parse-options usage and begin libification. The patch itself is a small, mechanical cleanup: it removes the now-redundant `from_stream` argument from `parse_one_feature()` and `check_unsafe_feature()` after an earlier patch (11/12) eliminated the only call site that passed `from_stream=0`.

The change is straightforward and safe. Every remaining call to `parse_one_feature()` comes from the input stream, so the `from_stream` parameter was always `1` and no longer needed. The patch updates the function signatures, removes the parameter from the calls, and adjusts the `option_import_marks()` invocations to hard-code `1` for the `from_stream` argument. The `check_unsafe_feature()` helper is similarly simplified, removing the conditional that checked `from_stream`.

This patch does not alter behavior, introduce new symbols, or touch any files outside `builtin/fast-import.c`. It is purely a cleanup step that tidies up the codebase after the larger refactoring in patch 11/12. The commit message is clear and the diff is minimal, making the change easy to review. No controversy or open questions remain; this is a routine follow-up to earlier work in the series.
