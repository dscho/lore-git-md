This is the third patch in Michael Montalbo’s v2 series fixing race conditions in Git’s HTTP daemon test helpers. The patch adds documentation to `t/README` explaining how to write concurrency-safe test scripts, codifying the atomic idioms (`mkdir` and `mv`) introduced in the earlier patches.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is surface-level, focusing on clarity and wording rather than the technical substance. He suggests two minor improvements: renaming the section to "Writing concurrency-safe lib-httpd helpers" to better reflect its scope (since the guidance is specific to the HTTP daemon scripts), and clarifying that `$$` expands to the process ID rather than assuming readers will recognize the shell variable. He also asks whether `rm` (without `-f`) could serve as an atomic alternative to `mv`, though this is more of a curiosity than a request for change.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. Junio C Hamano previously called it a "nice finishing touch," and the series as a whole is ready for integration once the open question about error handling in patch 1 is resolved. This documentation patch is unlikely to hold up the series, as the feedback here is purely stylistic.
