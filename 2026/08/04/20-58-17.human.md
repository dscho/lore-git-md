This is Junio’s reply to the fourth patch in Pablo Sabater’s nine-patch GSoC series (v4), which refactors `fetch_object_info()` to use a dedicated `struct fetch_object_info_results` instead of `struct object_info`. The patch is part of an effort to extend `git cat-file --batch-command` with `%(objecttype)` support for remote-object-info queries.

Junio’s feedback is surface-level and advisory. He notes that the patch removes about 30 lines of code, primarily by eliminating the intermediate `remote_atom_map[]` array and its associated `struct protocol_placeholder_entry`. His only question is why the "type" entry was already present in the map, given that the actual type support is introduced later in the series (patches 6/9 and 7/9) and this iteration no longer introduces the `ask_type` flag prematurely (as it did in v3). This is a minor curiosity rather than a technical concern, and it does not block the patch’s readiness for review.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. It implements the hybrid design proposed by Junio and Peff in earlier reviews, replacing `struct object_info` with a dedicated results struct that holds only the fields the caller requests (currently size, with type coming later). The refactoring is mechanical and prepares the codebase for the `%(objecttype)` support added in later patches. No behavior changes or test coverage are introduced in this patch, and all prior review feedback has been addressed. The series is feature-complete and merge-ready.
