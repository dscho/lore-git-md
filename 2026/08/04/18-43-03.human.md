This is the final patch in Pablo Sabater’s nine-part GSoC series extending `` `git cat-file --batch-command` `` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The patch removes the temporary workaround that excluded `%(objecttype)` from the default format for remote queries, unifying it with the format used by local queries (`%(objectname) %(objecttype) %(objectsize)`). Documentation is updated to reflect the new support, and tests are adjusted to expect the unified format across all transport protocols (git://, file://, http://).

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial: it simply removes the conditional format override in `builtin/cat-file.c` and updates the man pages and test expectations. The patch is the logical conclusion of the series, ensuring consistent output regardless of whether the query is local or remote. No new technical questions are raised; this is a straightforward cleanup that completes the feature. The series as a whole is now ready for integration.
