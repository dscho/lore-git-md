This is a bug report about `git-replay` and `git-history` losing notes during history rewriting.

The email reports that both `git replay --onto=...` and `git history fixup` (and likely other modes) silently drop Git notes when rewriting commits. The author has observed this behavior and works around it by manually copying notes (`git notes copy @{1}`) for the tip commit, but notes on intermediate commits are harder to recover. The question is whether notes are intentionally out of scope for these commands or if this is an unimplemented feature.

No prior discussion or patch is referenced, so this appears to be a new observation. The report is clear but lacks reproduction steps or test cases; it relies on the reader’s familiarity with how notes should behave during history rewriting. The tone suggests this is an unexpected gap rather than a deliberate design choice.
