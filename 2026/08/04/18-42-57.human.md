This is a refactoring patch (part 3 of 9) in Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The series is now at v4, feature-complete, and under final review.

The patch removes `struct object_info_args` and passes its three members (`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`) directly to `fetch_object_info()` and `send_object_info_request()`. The commit message clarifies that while the struct was introduced to reduce parameter count, it created a misleading abstraction: the fields are accessed individually and the struct is constructed immediately before the function call. This change is purely mechanical and has no behavioral impact, but it prepares the codebase for the architectural refactoring in patch 4/9 (introducing `struct fetch_object_info_results`).

The patch touches `fetch-object-info.c`, `fetch-object-info.h`, and `transport.c`, updating function signatures and call sites. Reviewers have already confirmed the change is uncontroversial, and the commit message now accurately reflects that `fetch_object_info()` *does* use all three fields of the original struct. This addresses Karthik Nayak’s surface-level feedback from v3 without requiring further technical changes.

For a reader tracking the series, this patch is a necessary but unexciting step toward the hybrid design (single array of lightweight structs) that replaces `struct object_info` in the next patch. It’s worth noting only if you’re following the architectural evolution of the remote-object-info subsystem.
