This is the fourth patch in Pablo Sabater’s nine-patch GSoC series (v4), which refactors the `fetch-object-info` subsystem to use a dedicated `struct fetch_object_info_results` instead of the generic `struct object_info`. The patch is a mechanical cleanup that removes the intermediate `struct protocol_placeholder_entry` and its associated `remote_atom_map[]`, replacing them with explicit flags (`wants_size`, `wants_type`) and dedicated arrays for each attribute. This aligns with the series’ broader goal of avoiding string-based lookups and sentinel values, and it prepares the codebase for the `%(objecttype)` feature introduced in later patches.

Junio’s feedback was surface-level and curiosity-driven, asking why the `remote_atom_map[]` already included the "type" entry despite `%(objecttype)` support not being introduced until patches 6/9 and 7/9. Pablo clarified that the list was designed to be idempotent: new placeholders could be added without changing behavior until the server advertised support. The "type" entry was included to test the client’s handling of known-but-unsupported placeholders (e.g., new client vs. old server), leveraging the fact that `struct object_info` already had a type field. With the new design, each attribute requires a dedicated array, so carrying an unused `types` array would be wasteful. Pablo opted to drop the "type" entry here and implement it fully in the later patches where it belongs.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for integration. It removes about 30 lines of code, primarily by eliminating the string-based lookup logic, and does not introduce any new functionality or partial features. The series as a whole is feature-complete, with all prior review feedback addressed and the architectural design finalized. The remaining patches (5/9 through 9/9) are similarly uncontroversial and focus on completing the `%(objecttype)` implementation, including server-side capability advertisement, client-side parsing, and test updates. No open questions or loose ends remain for this patch.
