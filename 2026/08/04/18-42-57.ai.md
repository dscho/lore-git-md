This is a refactoring patch (part 3/9 of the GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support `%(objecttype)`). It removes the `struct object_info_args` wrapper and passes its three members—`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`—directly to `fetch_object_info()` and `send_object_info_request()`. The commit message explains that the struct was misleading: although it reduced parameter count, its members are unrelated and are accessed individually, so the abstraction added no clarity.

The change is mechanical and touches only function signatures and call sites in `fetch-object-info.c`, `fetch-object-info.h`, and `transport.c`. No behavior changes are intended, and the patch is explicitly marked as ready to merge after addressing Karthik Nayak’s feedback about the original phrasing in the commit message. The updated message now clarifies that `fetch_object_info()` *does* use all three fields in its current implementation, and the rationale for removing the struct is its lack of semantic cohesion rather than any functional deficiency. This patch paves the way for the architectural refactoring in patch 4/9, which introduces `struct fetch_object_info_results`.
