Core problem or goal
Fix a regression in `git worktree add` where the `-b` or `-B` options were ignored when a branch name could be DWIMmed to a remote-tracking branch. The intended behavior is that explicit `-b` or `-B` should take precedence over DWIM, preventing unintended branch creation.
