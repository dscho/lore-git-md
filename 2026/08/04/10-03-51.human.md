This is the eighth patch in Christian Couder’s 12-patch v2 series refactoring `git fast-import` to modernize its parse-options usage and reduce global variables. The patch moves two more globals—`seen_data_command` and `allow_unsafe_features`—into the newly introduced `struct fast_import_state`. The change is purely mechanical: all access sites already receive the `state` parameter, so no new parameter threading is needed. The initialization order is adjusted to avoid a `memset()` clobber during the early `--allow-unsafe_features` command-line scan, and the `UNUSED` marker is removed from `check_unsafe_feature()` since its `state` parameter is now used. No behavior change is intended. The patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c` and is a straightforward continuation of the libification effort.
