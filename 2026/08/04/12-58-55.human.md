This is a bugfix for the `git maintenance` command. Patrick Steinhardt reviews David Lin’s patch that restores the documented `"none"` strategy after a regression introduced in October 2025 (commit d465be2327). The patch itself is minimal—two lines in `builtin/gc.c` to accept `"none"` case-insensitively, plus three lines of test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`—and Patrick confirms it looks correct.

The regression broke both the documented fallback behavior (silently defaulting to `"none"` for unknown strategies) and explicit use of `--strategy=none`. The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial; Patrick notes that while disabling maintenance entirely achieves the same end, users expect the documented `"none"` option to work. The only remaining loose end is the author-signoff mismatch (David’s patch uses `davidzylin@gmail.com` while the sign-off is `davidlin@stripe.com`), which Junio already flagged and suggested fixing with an in-body `From:` line. With that addressed, this patch should be ready to merge.
