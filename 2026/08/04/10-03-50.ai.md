This is patch 7/12 in Christian Couder’s v2 series refactoring `git fast-import` to reduce global variables and prepare for libification. The patch introduces `struct fast_import_state` to encapsulate the first three globals (`global_argc`, `global_argv`, and `global_prefix`) and passes the struct as the first argument to nearly every function in `builtin/fast-import.c`. The change is purely mechanical: no behavior is altered, and many functions receive the new parameter only to forward it or for future use, so they are marked `UNUSED` to silence compiler warnings. The patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c` and is a straightforward step toward the series’ goal of eliminating globals and modernizing the parse-options API.
