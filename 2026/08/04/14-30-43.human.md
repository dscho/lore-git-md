This is a documentation patch, part of a two-patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk improving the `git-refs(1)` man page. The series is already approved and queued for integration, but this email captures a minor follow-up comment from Patrick Steinhardt on the first patch.

The patch moves a critical warning about ref migration limitations—currently buried in a "KNOWN LIMITATIONS" section at the end of the page—into a prominent "CAUTION" admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand description. The change is purely presentational and uncontroversial, addressing a real usability issue: the warning, which notes that concurrent writes can lead to an inconsistent state, was effectively hidden from users reading about the `migrate` command.

Patrick’s reply is a surface-level review. He notes that the AsciiDoc formatting for the admonition block looks slightly indented in the rendered output, but calls it a "tiny nit" and implies it may not be worth addressing. His comment does not question the substance of the change—only the visual presentation—and the thread context confirms the series is already approved by Junio C Hamano and queued in `seen`. No further action is needed or expected on this patch.
