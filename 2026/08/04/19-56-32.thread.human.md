This thread is about adding comprehensive bash completion for the `git history` command, covering its subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`, and soon `squash`) and their options. Vincent Mailhol authored the four-patch series, now in its third iteration (v3), which is feature-complete and under review.

The series has addressed all prior feedback, including improvements to revision detection logic, subcommand-aware value completion for `--empty` and `--update-refs`, and idiomatic pathspec handling for the `split` subcommand. Key reviewers—Patrick Steinhardt and D. Ben Knoble—have endorsed the changes, and Junio C Hamano noted a forward-looking suggestion to refactor duplicated completion boilerplate, though this doesn’t block the current series. The only remaining loose end is a trivial rebase to include the `squash` subcommand once it graduates from `seen`.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s recent email flags a minor formatting issue in the cover letter (a misplaced `#3` that might be treated as a comment), but this is purely editorial and doesn’t affect the patchset’s readiness. The series is otherwise complete, with tests verifying subcommand/option ordering, revision completion, and pathspec behavior. No substantive objections remain, and the implementation is now idiomatic and maintainable.
