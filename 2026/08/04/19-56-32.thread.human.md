This thread is about adding bash completion for the `git history` command, covering its subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`), their options, and revision arguments. Vincent Mailhol authored the patch, which is currently under review.

The patch is seen as a clear improvement, with Patrick Steinhardt providing substantive feedback on a refinement opportunity for the `split` subcommand’s pathspec handling—specifically, switching from revision to file path completion after the first argument. While this refinement is noted as non-blocking, D. Ben Knoble later suggested modeling the pathspec completion after existing patterns in `_git_reflog` or `_git_config`. Both reviewers agree the patch is sufficient for now, with the pathspec edge case left as a potential follow-up. The patch will also need a trivial rebase once the `squash` subcommand (currently in `seen`) lands.

No major objections or changes have been requested, and the patch is expected to proceed once the minor rebase is addressed. The discussion remains focused on the completion script’s implementation, with no broader design concerns raised.
