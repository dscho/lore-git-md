Vincent Mailhol has refined the series across three iterations, addressing all prior feedback on revision detection, pathspec handling, and value completion. The v3 series is now feature-complete, with tests verifying subcommand/option ordering, revision completion, and pathspec behavior for the `split` subcommand.

The series is under review and ready for integration, pending a trivial rebase to include the `squash` subcommand once it graduates from `seen`. Key reviewers—Patrick Steinhardt, D. Ben Knoble, and Junio C Hamano—have weighed in, with their feedback fully incorporated. The only remaining loose end is a minor formatting glitch in the v3 cover letter, where a line starting with `#3` was misinterpreted as a comment by the `b4` tool.
