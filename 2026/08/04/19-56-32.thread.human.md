This thread is about adding bash completion for the `git history` command, covering its subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`), their options, and revision arguments. Vincent Mailhol authored the patch, which is under review and generally well-received.

The patch is a clear improvement, with Patrick Steinhardt and D. Ben Knoble both endorsing it. Junio C Hamano noted some boilerplate duplication in the completion script but did not request changes. The main open question is whether to refine the `split` subcommand’s completion to handle pathspecs after the first argument—this is considered a non-blocking improvement for a future iteration. Vincent sketched a possible approach but hasn’t committed to implementing it. Patrick’s latest email is a procedural nudge, asking Vincent to confirm within a few days whether he plans to submit a v2 with this refinement, to help reviewers and the maintainer plan.

The patch will need a trivial rebase once the `squash` subcommand (currently in `seen`) lands. No major objections remain, and the discussion is now focused on forward-looking refinements rather than blocking issues.
