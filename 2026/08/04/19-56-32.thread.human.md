This thread is about adding comprehensive bash completion support for the `git history` command, covering its subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`, and soon `squash`) along with their options and arguments. Vincent Mailhol authored the four-patch series, now in its third iteration (v3), which is feature-complete for the current subcommands and addresses all prior feedback.

The series has received strong support from Patrick Steinhardt, who provided substantive reviews on earlier versions and now explicitly endorses v3 as "a good base to build on." His feedback on maintainability and design trade-offs has been fully incorporated, and the series is now ready for integration. Other reviewers, including D. Ben Knoble and Kristoffer Haugsbakk, contributed surface-level observations that were addressed without requiring a reroll. Junio C Hamano previously suggested a future refactoring of duplicated completion boilerplate, but this is not a blocker.

The only remaining loose end is a trivial rebase to include the `squash` subcommand once it graduates from Junio’s `seen` branch. The implementation touches `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash` and adds tests in `t/t9902-completion.sh`, covering subcommand/option ordering, revision completion, and pathspec handling for the `split` subcommand. No substantive objections remain, and the series is unblocked for merging.
