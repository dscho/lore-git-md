Core problem or goal
Add bash completion support for the `git history` command, covering its four subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`), their options, and positional revision arguments. The completion should handle both revision names and, for the `split` subcommand, pathspecs after the first argument.

The patch will require a trivial rebase once the `squash` subcommand (currently in `seen`) lands.
