By repeatedly invalidating nearly-complete reviews, Harald’s approach has made the process feel slower, not faster.

The email is a procedural correction, not a technical one. Junio doesn’t engage with the substance of the `git history squash` series or Phillip Wood’s patches; instead, he diagnoses why the thread has stalled. His advice is implicit: slow down, let reviews land, and avoid rerolling until feedback is fully addressed. The tone is matter-of-fact, but the message is clear—Harald’s impatience has backfired, and the path forward requires patience and coordination. This sets up a test for Harald: whether he can adapt to the project’s rhythm or risk further disengagement.
