This is a **substantive review** of the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring the Git object database (ODB) streaming API. The series aims to unify `odb_write_stream` and `odb_read_stream` into a single `odb_stream` structure as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.

Justin Tobler’s review engages directly with the patch’s core changes, noting the addition of an `enum object_type type` field to `struct odb_write_stream` and the mechanical updates to callers (e.g., renaming `hash_blob_stream` to `hash_stream`). He highlights a now-outdated comment in `odb/transaction.h` that still assumes blob-only streaming, suggesting it should be updated to reflect the new generic infrastructure. While the patch itself does not expose new functionality—streaming is still limited to blobs in practice—Justin observes that the underlying design is now more flexible, which aligns with the series’ goal of unifying the read and write APIs.

The review is positive and technically grounded, focusing on the patch’s role in preparing for the unified `odb_stream` structure. Justin’s feedback is narrowly scoped to this patch (no discussion of later patches in the series) but confirms the change is a logical step toward the broader refactoring. The only loose end is the stale comment in `odb/transaction.h`, which could be addressed in a follow-up or a minor revision to this patch. No performance or correctness concerns are raised, and the review suggests the patch is on track for inclusion.
