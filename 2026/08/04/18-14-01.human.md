This is the first patch in Domen Kožar’s v2 series introducing `post-worktree-add`, `post-worktree-remove`, and `post-worktree-move` hooks. It implements the `post-worktree-add` hook, which fires after a new worktree is fully set up, regardless of whether `--no-checkout` or `--orphan` was used.

The hook runs inside the new worktree with `GIT_DIR` and `GIT_WORK_TREE` cleared, takes no arguments, and cannot affect the outcome of `git worktree add`—its exit status merely becomes the command’s exit status. It executes after `post-checkout`, even if `post-checkout` fails, because the worktree is already populated and remains present. The patch updates `builtin/worktree.c`, adds comprehensive test coverage in `t/t2400-worktree-add.sh`, and documents the hook in `Documentation/githooks.adoc` and `Documentation/config/hook.adoc`.

The implementation is clean and well-tested, addressing the v1 feedback by removing arguments from `post-worktree-add` and ensuring it runs unconditionally after `post-checkout`. The only remaining loose end is the undocumented relative order of `post-worktree-add` and `post-checkout`, which Phillip Wood requested be clarified. This patch is a solid foundation for the series and should interest anyone building tooling around Git worktrees.
