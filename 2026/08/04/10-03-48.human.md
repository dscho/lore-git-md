This is a small refactoring patch in Christian Couder’s ongoing `git fast-import` libification series (v2, 12 patches). It changes the types of two boolean flags—`show_stats` and `quiet`—from `unsigned int` to `int` in preparation for parsing them with `OPT_BOOL` in a later patch.

The change is purely mechanical: the flags are still used as booleans, but `OPT_BOOL` expects an `int` rather than an `unsigned int`. The patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c` and has no behavior impact. It is a straightforward, uncontroversial step toward modernizing the command-line parsing infrastructure.
