This is a refactoring and libification series for `git fast-import`, now expanded to 12 patches in its second iteration. Christian Couder has addressed Junio’s feedback from v1 by fully integrating the parse-options API, eliminating the placeholder variables that drew criticism, and completing the libification step that was left unfinished in the first version.

The series now standardizes the command’s usage string to match its SYNOPSIS documentation, removes `git fast-import` from the `t0450/adoc-help-mismatches` test, and introduces a new `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro to organize hidden options in the parse-options API. The core work is the libification of `builtin/fast-import.c`: a new `struct fast_import_state` encapsulates global variables, and the parse-options API now fully parses command-line arguments, replacing the previous hand-written usage string. Two undocumented options (`--alias` and `--get-mark`) and three stream-only options (`--cat-blob`, `--ls`, and `--notes`) are no longer accepted on the command line, as their functionality was never intended for CLI use.

The series touches `parse-options.h`, `parse-options.c`, `builtin/fast-import.c`, and related documentation, with no on-disk format or CLI behavior changes beyond the removal of the five undocumented options. CI tests pass except for a likely unrelated Windows+Meson failure. The work is incremental and well-structured, with clear commit messages and no regressions in the test suite. This is a solid refactoring effort that modernizes the codebase and paves the way for future libification work.
