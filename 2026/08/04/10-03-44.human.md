This is the first patch in Christian Couder’s 12-patch v2 series refactoring `git fast-import` to modernize its parse-options usage and begin libification. The patch introduces `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP`, a new macro that lets developers group hidden options (those shown only with `--help-all`) under a labeled heading that also appears only when hidden options are displayed. The implementation is minimal: it adds the macro to `parse-options.h`, adjusts the help-text formatter in `parse-options.c` to skip hidden groups unless `--help-all` is used, and extends the test helper and its test suite to verify the new behavior. The change is purely additive and does not alter existing functionality; it simply provides better organization for hidden options in help output.
