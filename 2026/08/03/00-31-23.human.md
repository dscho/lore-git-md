Brian argues that these risks are not hypothetical—citing active litigation around LLM output and Git's global distribution footprint—and that they outweigh any potential benefits, even for performance-critical patches. The tone is firm: the project is unlikely to relax its policy on nontrivial AI-assisted contributions, and further advocacy for exceptions is unlikely to be productive.
