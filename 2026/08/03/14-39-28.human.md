This is the first patch in Pablo Sabater’s eight-patch GSoC v3 series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The change is purely mechanical and preparatory: it removes a portability wart (the `xargs` pipe) and makes the test slightly more readable. The commit message explains the rationale clearly, and the patch itself is small and focused. No behavior changes are introduced, and the patch has no dependencies on later parts of the series. This is a surface-level improvement that aligns with Git’s test style; it should be uncontroversial and is likely to be merged as-is.
