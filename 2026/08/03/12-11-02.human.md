This is a bugfix patch addressing Git's trailer parsing misinterpreting URLs as trailers. The discussion here is a follow-up to Junio C Hamano's maintainer review of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's implementation, which adopts Jeff King's proposed solution to exclude lines containing `://` from trailer parsing.

Kristoffer responds to Junio's two review points with clarity. First, he acknowledges that while the `://` heuristic is robust, it does have a theoretical false positive: a trailer key consisting solely of digits (e.g., `1://`) would be incorrectly rejected, though this is unlikely in practice. He notes that trailer keys (alphanumerics and hyphens) are a strict subset of URL schemes, so the heuristic remains sound for real-world cases. Second, he confirms the pointer arithmetic safety in the patch, explaining why `c[1]` and `c[2]` are safe to access due to C's NUL-terminated strings and boolean short-circuiting.

The patch itself is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and now has maintainer approval. Junio's questions were clarifying rather than challenging, and Kristoffer's responses address them without requiring changes to the core logic. The fix is minimal, targeted, and resolves a real-world issue (245 documented cases in the Linux kernel) with no apparent downsides. This looks like a straightforward bugfix ready for integration.
