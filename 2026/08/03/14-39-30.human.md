This is the third patch in an eight-patch GSoC series that extends `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder for remote-object-info queries. This patch is a refactoring step: it removes the `struct object_info_args` wrapper and passes its three fields (`server_options`, `oids`, and `object_info_options`) directly to `fetch_object_info()` and `send_object_info_request()`.

The change is mechanical and has no behavior impact. The commit message explains that the struct was misleading because it suggested `fetch_object_info()` used all three fields, when in fact it only needed them to forward to `send_object_info_request()`. The patch aligns the code with the hybrid design introduced in v3, which replaces `struct object_info` with a dedicated `struct fetch_object_info_results` in later patches. Reviewers familiar with the series will recognize this as a preparatory cleanup; others may want to glance at the cover letter or earlier patches to see the bigger picture. The patch is well-explained and appears uncontroversial.
