This introduces a new edge case—multiple `amend!` commits—that Phillip’s proposal did not explicitly address.

Second, Junio suggests that the dynamic behavior could be confusing for users. While Phillip’s proposal aims to reduce friction for common cases, Junio implies that the trade-off may not be worth the potential inconsistency. This aligns with his earlier hesitation about introducing subtle, context-dependent defaults in Git commands. The email does not resolve the debate but instead frames it as a tension between usability and predictability.
