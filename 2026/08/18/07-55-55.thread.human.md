The `receive-report` hook series by Karthik Nayak is now in its fourth iteration and queued in Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch, awaiting graduation to `next`. The series adds a new server-side hook that lets administrators filter or modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates, motivated by GitLab’s need for MVCC support. All prior feedback—including objections to the hook’s name and exit status semantics—has been addressed, and the series is split into three patches: a documentation update, a refactoring of the status-report logic, and the core hook implementation.

Patrick Steinhardt recently reviewed the refactoring patch, suggesting minor improvements to the commit message and function names for clarity. He proposes renaming the new helper from `generate_response()` to `generate_report()` and adjusting a boolean parameter to better reflect its purpose, but notes the patch is otherwise sound. These tweaks are unlikely to delay the series, which Junio has already marked as ready for `next`. The only remaining step is integration, as all technical and design questions have been resolved.
