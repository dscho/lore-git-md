This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The issue causes objects to be incorrectly reported as missing when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed, leading to crashes in `git replay` and other server-side operations. The series is now at its second version and is ready for merging.

The core fix, implemented in the final patch (4/4), adopts Jeff King's tri-state design for `fill_midx_entry()` to distinguish between objects absent from the MIDX, those present but with an unavailable owning pack, and successful lookups. The recovery logic scans other MIDX-covered packs for a surviving copy of the object, but only during the second read (`OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ`), optimizing performance for `QUICK` callers while ensuring correctness. The patch also ensures that the `stale_packs_detected` flag, introduced in patch 3/4, is re-armed on every lookup of a vanished pack, preventing `QUICK` callers from skipping the second read.

The series has converged with no remaining technical objections. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the first patch substantively and acknowledged the final patch, noting no action is required from Elijah despite an upcoming merge conflict. Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King, and Derrick Stolee have all provided substantive reviews, with their feedback incorporated into the current version. Derrick Stolee suggested a complementary follow-up to reduce the race's frequency by adopting a grace period for redundant packfiles, but this is not part of the current series. The patches touch several subsystems, including geometric repacking, MIDX, and the packed-object backend, and include comprehensive test coverage. The series is a high-priority candidate for merging.
