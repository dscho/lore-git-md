This thread is about a three-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The issue causes crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed by a concurrent repack. The series is now at version 3, with the core fix (patch 3/3) converging toward merge-readiness.

Elijah has adopted feedback from Jeff King (Peff) and others, including a tri-state design for `fill_midx_entry()` and gating the recovery logic on `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` to optimize performance. The latest exchange with Peff resolves the last minor points: Elijah agrees to rename `fill_midx_entry()` (though no specific alternative is settled), confirms the `stale_packs_detected` flag is no longer needed, and concedes a comment rewrite was unnecessary. These are cosmetic changes and do not affect the fix's correctness or performance. The series is rebased on `ps/odb-generic-corrupt-objects` and simplified by dropping an earlier patch that addressed `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` misuse in `git mktree --batch`.

Key participants include Elijah (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer, who endorsed the core fix), Peff (who provided substantive reviews and proposed the tri-state design), Patrick Steinhardt (who praised the targeted recovery logic), and Derrick Stolee (who suggested follow-up work to reduce the race condition's frequency by adopting a grace period for redundant packfiles). The series is effectively ready for merging, with the broader architectural debate about geometric repacking's grace period left as a follow-up discussion.
