This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which was causing crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations. The core issue involved a multi-pack-index (MIDX) referencing a packfile removed by geometric repacking, leading to failures when processes used the stale MIDX to locate objects. The series has now been fully reviewed and endorsed by key contributors, including Jeff King (Peff), Patrick Steinhardt, and Derrick Stolee, and is merged to `master`.

The fix introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism for MIDX lookups when packfiles vanish mid-operation, adopting Peff’s tri-state design for `midx_fill_entry()` (renamed from `fill_midx_entry()`) and gating the recovery logic on `SECOND_READ` to optimize performance. The series also addresses a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` and fixes a race condition in the same command by replacing the `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT`. All prior feedback has been addressed, including the removal of a redundant flag and the renaming of a key function. The series is well-motivated by production evidence and includes thorough test coverage.

The only remaining loose ends are minor follow-up items, such as improving error message clarity, adding basic test coverage for `git mktree --batch`, and evaluating whether a log message should be demoted to trace2. These are not blockers, and the series is now fully integrated. The architectural debate about whether geometric repacking should adopt a grace period for redundant packfiles to reduce the race condition’s frequency remains a topic for future discussion.
