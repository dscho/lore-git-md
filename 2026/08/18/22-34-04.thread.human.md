This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which was causing crashes in `git replay` and other server-side operations due to objects being incorrectly reported as missing. The series is now at version 3 and has been deemed merge-ready, with Junio Hamano integrating it into the `next` branch for integration testing.

The core issue involves a race where a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by geometric repacking, leading to processes using the stale MIDX failing to locate objects. The fix introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism for MIDX lookups when packfiles vanish mid-operation, adopting Jeff King's (Peff) tri-state design for `midx_fill_entry()` and gating the recovery logic on `SECOND_READ` to optimize performance. The series also addresses a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` and fixes problems with `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` readers.

Key participants include Elijah Newren (author), Junio Hamano (maintainer), Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King, and Derrick Stolee. All prior feedback has been addressed, including the removal of the `stale_packs_detected` flag, renaming `fill_midx_entry()` to `midx_fill_entry()`, and replacing `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` in `git mktree --batch`. The latest email from Peff endorses the third patch in the series, confirming its correctness and alignment with the original intent of the code. No unresolved technical disagreements remain, though minor cosmetic issues like error message clarity are noted as potential follow-ups. The series is well-motivated by production evidence and includes thorough test coverage.
