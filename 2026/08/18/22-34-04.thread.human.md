This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series from Elijah Newren that fixes a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which was causing `git replay` to crash with a SIGSEGV when objects became unreadable due to a stale multi-pack-index (MIDX). The series is effectively ready for integration, with no remaining technical objections.

The first patch ensures `git replay` fails gracefully when merge inputs are unreadable, while the second patch modifies the packed-object backend to recover from MIDX-packfile mismatches by scanning all packs covered by the MIDX when the normal lookup fails. Junio C Hamano reviewed the first patch substantively, endorsing the core fix and requesting a minor test adjustment (removing a redundant `test_grep` check), which Elijah has addressed in this response. Junio also noted a merge conflict with an in-flight topic for the second patch, which he will resolve during integration. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the second patch, praising the recovery logic and raising a nuanced question about its scope, which Elijah clarified—confirming the fix targets `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` callers (e.g., `git replay`, `upload-pack`) that would otherwise fail permanently.

Elijah has now responded to Junio’s feedback, agreeing to remove the redundant test check and asking whether to resubmit the patch or leave the adjustment to Junio. The series is a high-priority candidate for merging due to its impact on server-side stability, and Elijah plans to submit follow-up patches addressing additional race-condition scenarios. The current patches are well-tested and introduce no on-disk format changes.
