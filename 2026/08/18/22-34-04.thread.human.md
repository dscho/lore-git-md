This bugfix series by Elijah Newren, now at v3, addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism that could cause crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations. The issue occurs when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by repacking, leaving processes using the stale MIDX unable to locate objects. The series introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism in the packed-object backend that transparently scans remaining packs when the preferred pack is missing, gated by `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` to avoid performance overhead for quick lookups.
