This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which was causing crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations. The series is now merge-ready and has been integrated into Junio Hamano's `next` branch for integration testing.

The core issue involves a race where a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by geometric repacking, leading to failures when processes use the stale MIDX. The fix introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism in the MIDX lookup logic, gated by `SECOND_READ` to optimize performance. All prior feedback—including from Jeff King (Peff) and Derrick Stolee—has been addressed, with Peff confirming the recovery logic is correct and optimal. The series also fixes a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` and resolves a race condition in the same command by replacing the `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT`.

Junio's latest email is a procedural step, seeking Peff's final confirmation before marking the series for `next`. No new technical concerns are raised, and the series remains unchanged. The only remaining loose ends are minor follow-ups, such as improving error message clarity and adding basic test coverage for `git mktree --batch`, but these are not blockers. The series is expected to graduate to `master` in the near future.
