This thread is about a bugfix series that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which could cause crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations. The issue occurs when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed by a concurrent repack, leading to failures in processes using the stale MIDX.

The series, now at its third iteration (v3) by Elijah Newren, consists of four patches. The core fix (patch 4/4) introduces a recovery mechanism in `find_pack_entry()` that transparently handles MIDX-packfile mismatches by scanning remaining packs when the preferred pack is missing. This fix is gated by the `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag to optimize performance for `QUICK` callers. Earlier patches in the series address a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` and a race condition in the same command caused by the misuse of the `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag. The series has been thoroughly reviewed and endorsed by key contributors, including Jeff King (Peff), Patrick Steinhardt, and now Derrick Stolee, who provided a final, unconditionally positive review of the core fix.

The series is effectively merged to `master`, with all prior feedback addressed. The only remaining loose end is the clarity of an error message, which is non-blocking. The fix is minimal, well-tested, and introduces no on-disk format changes. It touches several subsystems, including geometric repacking, MIDX, the packed-object backend, and `git replay`. Follow-up work may explore architectural changes to reduce the race condition's frequency, such as adopting a grace period for redundant packfiles during geometric repacking.
