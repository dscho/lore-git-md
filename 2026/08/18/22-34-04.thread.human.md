The fix, which landed in `master` on July 10, 2025, introduced a lightweight recovery mechanism in the packed-object backend (`odb/source-packed.c`) to scan MIDX-covered packs when the preferred pack is missing. The recovery is gated by the `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag to optimize performance, and the core function (`midx_fill_entry()`) now returns a tri-state value to distinguish between hits, misses, and unavailable packs.
