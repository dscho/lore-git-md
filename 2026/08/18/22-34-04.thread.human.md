This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series by Elijah Newren that addresses a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism, which causes crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations. The issue arises when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by geometric repacking, leading to processes using the stale MIDX failing to locate objects. The series has been under active debate, particularly around the architectural approach to fixing `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` lookups.

Elijah has now responded to Jeff King's (Peff) substantive critique of patch 3/4, conceding that the patch's approach was flawed. The `stale_packs_detected` flag introduced in patch 3/4 was intended to force `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` lookups to recover from vanished packfiles, but Peff argued this violated the flag's intended semantics. Elijah agrees and will drop patch 3/4 entirely, replacing it with a targeted fix for `git mktree --batch` that removes the `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag while retaining `SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT`. This change simplifies the series, eliminates the need for the `stale_packs_detected` flag, and aligns with Peff's suggestion that `mktree --batch` was misusing the `QUICK` flag. The remaining patches (1/4, 2/4, and 4/4) are unaffected, though patch 4/4's use of the `stale_packs_detected` flag may still face scrutiny.

The core fix in patch 4/4, which implements MIDX recovery logic with a tri-state return design and `SECOND_READ`-gated fallback, remains the primary solution for the race condition. The series is now one patch shorter, and the architectural debate appears to be converging. However, Peff's pending feedback on patch 4/4 could still prompt further adjustments. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have reviewed parts of the series, but no new feedback has emerged since the v2 updates. The series is rebased on `ps/odb-generic-corrupt-objects` and remains under review.
