This critique applies to the loose backend’s updated logic in this patch, where `ret = 1` now indicates a missing object.

The review is narrowly focused on the return-value semantics and does not question the patch’s functional correctness or its alignment with the series’ goal of consistent error handling. Junio’s feedback is consistent with his earlier comments on patch 1/7, where he first flagged the tri-state convention as potentially confusing. No resolution or counter-argument from Patrick has been posted yet, leaving the design question open. The patch itself remains uncontroversial in its mechanical changes (e.g., removing the `corrupt:` label, consolidating error paths), but the broader return-value convention could require revisiting if Junio’s suggestion gains traction.
