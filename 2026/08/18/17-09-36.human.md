This is a design discussion in the ongoing `[PATCH v4]` series that adds six new path-related keys to `` `git repo info` ``. Junio C Hamano weighs in on the unresolved question of whether `` `path.git-prefix` `` belongs in the command, siding firmly with the author’s position.

Junio dismisses Lucas Seiki Oshiro’s objection that `` `path.git-prefix` `` is “current directory information” rather than “repository information.” He analogizes it to `` `git rev-parse --show-cdup` ``, which also describes the relationship between the current directory and the repository root, and argues that the relative-path variant of any repository location (e.g., `` `path.commondir.relative` ``) is equally valid. His conclusion: `` `path.git-prefix` `` is simply the inverse of `` `path.toplevel.relative` `` (or `` `path.cdup` ``) and therefore fits naturally in `` `git repo info` ``. As a corollary, he suggests that if `` `path.git-prefix` `` is included, the series should also add a `` `path.cdup` `` key for symmetry.

The email does not introduce new code or tests, but it shifts the design debate: the objection is now unlikely to prevail, and the series will probably proceed with both `` `path.git-prefix` `` and a new `` `path.cdup` `` key unless other reviewers raise substantive concerns.
