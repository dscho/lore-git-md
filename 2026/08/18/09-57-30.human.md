This is a cosmetic documentation patch, the first in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part v2 series extending `git format-rev` with `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` formatting options. The patch aligns the option descriptions in `builtin/name-rev.c` with Git’s style guideline by converting mixed-case phrasing (“Use NUL…”) to lowercase (“use NUL…”). No functional changes are made; the diff touches only the help strings passed to `OPT_CALLBACK_F` and `OPT_BOOL`.

The change is uncontroversial and follows Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the v1 series. It has not yet received review comments, but given its purely stylistic nature and prior agreement on the pattern, it is likely to be accepted as-is. This patch sets the stage for the subsequent refactoring and feature work in the series.
