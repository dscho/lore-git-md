This is a surface-level review comment from Junio C Hamano on patch 5/7 of Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring how the object database (ODB) handles corrupt objects. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort to make error reporting consistent and backend-agnostic when `OBJECT_INFO_DIE_IF_CORRUPT` is used.
