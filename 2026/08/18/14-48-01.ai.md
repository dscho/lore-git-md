Junio argues that `git pull` is fundamentally about *history integration* for users who make their own changes, and adding a destructive option risks accidental data loss for those users. He acknowledges that a subset of users (those who never make local changes) might benefit from a convenient "hard reset to upstream" operation, but notes that the same outcome is already safely achieved by running `git fetch` followed by `git reset --hard @{u}`. His preference is to keep destructive operations explicit and separate rather than bundling them into a command whose primary purpose is integration.

The tone is measured but firm: "I am mildly against adding anything of this sort to `git pull`."
