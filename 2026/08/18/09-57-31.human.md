This is a defensive coding patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part series adding formatting options to `git format-rev`. It moves `BUG_ON_OPT_NEG(unset)` and `BUG_ON_OPT_ARG(arg)` assertions to the very top of the option callback in `builtin/name-rev.c`. The change is purely stylistic—functionally the callback behaves identically—but it follows the principle that preconditions should be checked before any variable assignment. The patch is uncontroversial, addresses feedback from the v1 review, and is already queued in the author’s local v2 branch. No objections are expected.
