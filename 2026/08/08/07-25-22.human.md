Elijah Newren’s review focuses on a single entry in the new documentation: the description of `PARSE_OPT_LASTARG_DEFAULT`. He quotes the existing comment from `parse-options.h` and suggests a more explicit rewording that clarifies this positional dependency and the incompatibility with `PARSE_OPT_OPTARG`.

The review is narrow but substantive: it targets a potential source of confusion in the documentation, where an imprecise description could mislead future contributors about when and how the flag can be used.
