This is the final, surface-level follow-up in Johannes Sixt’s five-patch series overhauling gitk’s color-preference dialog. The series is now complete and ready for final review; this email exchanges only cosmetic suggestions about the phrasing of one UI label.

The discussion centers on whether “Diff: old lines background” should be reworded to sound more like natural language. Sixt proposes “Background of old lines in diffs” as an alternative, while Mark Levedahl suggests a hierarchical grouping that would move the “Diff” category into a nested list under “Commit Differences,” with “old line background” as a sub-item. Neither proposal changes functionality or translatability—both are purely about readability.

The series itself remains well-structured and uncontroversial. All five patches are confined to `gitk-git/gitk` (Tcl/Tk), address a real usability issue (non-clickable swatches, inconsistent button widths, technical labels, and untranslatable dialog titles), and have received only light, surface-level review so far. The open question here is minor and does not affect the series’ readiness. If you care about gitk’s UI polish, this is worth a quick glance; otherwise, it can safely be ignored.
