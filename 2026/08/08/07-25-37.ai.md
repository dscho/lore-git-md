This is the **substantive review** of the behavior-changing patch (11/12) in Christian Couder’s `git fast-import` libification series. Elijah Newren identifies three previously unlisted behavioral changes introduced by switching from a hand-written command-line parser to `parse_options()`:

1. **`--` handling**: A bare `--` (or trailing `--`) is now accepted as the end-of-options marker, whereas the old parser treated it as a usage error. This aligns with conventional `parse_options()` behavior and is harmless.
2. **Space-separated values**: Value-taking options (e.g., `--depth 5`) now accept space-separated arguments in addition to the `--opt=value` form. This is also standard `parse_options()` behavior and an improvement.
3. **`--allow-unsafe-features` inconsistency**: The early scan for `--allow-unsafe-features` (which predates `parse_options()` and is needed to process in-stream "feature" lines) is stricter than `parse_options()` itself. It only matches the exact spelling and stops at the first non-option token, while `parse_options()` accepts abbreviations (e.g., `--allow-unsafe`) and space-separated values. This can cause the in-stream feature to be rejected even though the command-line option is accepted, surprising users. The review suggests documenting this as a known limitation or adjusting the early scan to match `parse_options()`’s flexibility.

The review is **tested**: Elijah provides concrete examples (command lines and expected outcomes) for each behavioral change, demonstrating that he ran the code and observed the differences. The feedback is narrowly scoped to the patch’s correctness and completeness, with no objections to the overall direction. The `--allow-unsafe-features` issue is the most significant, as it creates a subtle inconsistency between command-line parsing and in-stream feature handling, but it errs on the safe side (rejecting unsafe features rather than allowing them). The review proposes two potential fixes (a dedicated `parse_options()` pass for `--allow-unsafe-features` or requiring it to be the first argument) but leaves the choice to the author.

Files touched by the patch: `builtin/fast-import.c` and `t9300-fast-import.sh` (new test). Key symbols: `parse_options()`, `--allow-unsafe-features`, `--depth`, `--max-pack-size`, `--date-format`.
