The series as a whole improves usability by making color swatches clickable, standardizing button widths, rewriting labels in natural language, and ensuring full translatability. This specific patch already addressed the translatability goal by replacing dynamically constructed dialog titles with full, translatable phrases.

The new exchange here is a surface-level discussion between Sixt and Mark Levedahl about how to phrase the label for "Diff: old lines background." Neither proposal touches code logic or translatability; both focus solely on improving the natural language presentation of UI strings.

The discussion remains low-stakes and cosmetic. The series itself is technically complete and ready for final review, with all five patches confined to `gitk-git/gitk` (Tcl/Tk) and no impact on core Git functionality. The only open question is whether to adopt Sixt's phrasing tweak or Levedahl's hierarchical reorganization for this single label. No substantive concerns or edge cases have been raised, and the patches have otherwise received only surface-level review so far.
