This is the maintainer’s review of the final patch in Toon Claes’s six-patch v2 series that optimizes the experimental `git last-modified` command by reusing Bloom filters. The patch itself is small—eight lines in `builtin/last-modified.c`—and fixes a critical edge case: when the user supplies a wildcard pathspec (e.g., `*.c`), `prepare_revision_walk()` clears the `bloom_filter_settings` field, breaking the per-path Bloom-filter lookups that the rest of the series relies on. Junio’s feedback is brief but substantive: he asks whether the series should include a test that verifies the restoration of `bloom_filter_settings` actually works for wildcard pathspecs. The question is practical—without trace instrumentation, the test might not show a visible behavior difference—but he acknowledges the failure scenario is specific enough to justify coverage. This is a surface-level review, focusing on test completeness rather than the patch’s technical correctness, which was already addressed in earlier feedback. The patch is otherwise ready for integration, assuming the test is added or the author explains why it’s unnecessary.
