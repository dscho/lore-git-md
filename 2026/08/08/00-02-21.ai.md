This is patch 6/10 of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC v6 series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The patch is a mechanical refactoring by Jeff King (Peff) that removes the `object_info_oids` and `object_info_results` fields from `git_transport_options` and passes them as explicit parameters to `transport_fetch_object_info()` and its vtable implementations.

The change clarifies ownership semantics: the caller retains responsibility for the OID array and results, and the transport no longer needs to manage their lifetimes. This eliminates a latent bug where dangling pointers could persist if the transport were reused, and prepares the codebase for future enhancements such as reusing transport connections for multiple object-info requests. The patch touches seven files (`transport.c`, `transport-helper.c`, `builtin/cat-file.c`, `fetch-object-info.c`, `fetch-object-info.h`, `transport-internal.h`, `transport.h`), marking the OID array as `const` to emphasize its input-only role. No behavior changes are introduced, and the patch is explicitly endorsed by Junio as ready to merge. The commit message explains the rationale clearly, noting that the current design is harder to understand and could lead to subtle bugs.
