He also confirms he will address Knoble’s minor grammatical nit in the commit message of patch 11/11 but does not plan to revise the series further.

The email underscores the thread’s closure: the series remains merged to `master`, and no substantive technical or structural changes are expected. The discussion reflects the project’s norm of deferring to authors on stylistic matters unless there is a clear functional or readability issue.
