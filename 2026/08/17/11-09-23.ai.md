This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch v3 series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The patch eagerly initializes alternates during ODB creation and cache flushing, eliminating scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()` across 11 files (46 lines removed, 4 added). The change is mechanical and well-justified: the commit message explains that lazy-loading was historically motivated by a timing issue with `core.ignoreCase` (now fixed in the preceding patch) and that most commands already load alternates eagerly during object access, making the optimization ineffective. The patch touches core ODB plumbing (`odb.c`, `odb.h`) and call sites in `builtin/fsck.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `commit-graph.c`, and other subsystems, but introduces no user-visible behavior changes. It is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort and builds on the `ps/odb-make-creation-pluggable` series. The series is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, and this patch is unchanged from v2, suggesting it is stable and uncontroversial. The only notable detail is the removal of the `odb_prepare_alternates()` function’s public declaration in `odb.h`, now made static, which is a clean encapsulation improvement.
