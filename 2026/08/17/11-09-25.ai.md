This is the fifth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s **v3** series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The patch drops the now-redundant `alternates_db` field from `struct object_database` and adjusts the code to pass the environment variable value directly to `odb_prepare_alternates()` during ODB initialization.

The change is mechanical and follows from earlier patches in the series: patch 3/5 already eliminated lazy-loading by eagerly initializing alternates during ODB setup, making the `alternates_db` field unnecessary. The patch updates `odb_new()` to pass the environment variable (`secondary_sources`) directly to `odb_prepare_alternates()` and frees it immediately after use, rather than storing it in the struct. The `odb_prepare()` function is also updated to pass `NULL` when flushing caches, since the environment variable no longer needs reprocessing. The net effect is a small reduction in code complexity (15 lines removed, 9 added) and a cleaner separation of concerns.

No user-visible behavior changes are intended, and the patch is unlikely to be controversial. It builds directly on the earlier patches in the series, which have already addressed the key design and correctness concerns (e.g., the `core.ignoreCase` timing issue fixed in patch 1/5). The series as a whole is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort and is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, though the v3 revision may require re-queuing. This patch is the last step in the cleanup, and its inclusion is a formality given the groundwork laid by the earlier patches.
