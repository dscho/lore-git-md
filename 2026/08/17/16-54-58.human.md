This is a brief follow-up in the `git format-rev` formatting options series. Junio C Hamano and Kristoffer Haugsbakk agree on a small but important defensive programming improvement: moving the `BUG_ON_OPT_NEG(unset)` assertion to the start of the callback functions in `builtin/name-rev.c`. The change affects both the new `date_cb()` callback (introduced in patch 4/4) and the existing `format_nul_cb()` callback, ensuring neither can dereference `option->value` before validating the `unset` flag. The fix is mechanical—reordering two lines per callback—and will appear in the next revision of the series. No other changes are planned, and the core functionality remains uncontroversial. This is a surface-level review adjustment, not a substantive design discussion.
