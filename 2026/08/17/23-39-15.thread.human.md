This thread is about a small but useful observability improvement: adding a Trace2 event to `git pack-objects` that records the total bytes written during packfile generation, including headers and checksums. The patch, now in its second version, addresses a gap where Trace2 already tracks object counts but not byte counts—a metric needed to measure how compression settings affect push performance.

The author, friel@openai.com, posted v2 after incorporating Junio C Hamano’s feedback, simplifying the byte-accumulation logic. The patch is narrowly scoped to `builtin/pack-objects.c` and the test suite, with no on-disk format changes. Two substantive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff) raised a minor but important implementation detail: whether to use `the_repository->hash_algo->rawsz` or `f->algop->rawsz` for the trailing checksum size. The reviewers have now converged on the latter as the more consistent and future-proof choice, and the author is expected to adopt Peff’s suggested refactoring in v3.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and likely to land once the hash size consistency point is resolved. A side discussion about the broader performance implications of Git’s current hash recomputation (three times per packfile) emerged but was explicitly deferred to a separate effort. The reviewers’ tone was collaborative, with no disagreement on the patch’s core goal. The series remains under review, with no merge status reported.
