This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort and is now in its third revision (`v3`).

This patch removes the `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`. The field was used to track whether alternates had been loaded, but since the previous patch (`3/5`) now eagerly initializes alternates during ODB creation, the field is redundant. The change is mechanical: the patch deletes the field and removes all conditional checks that relied on it, simplifying the code. The diff shows the field being dropped from `odb.h` and all references to it being removed from `odb.c`, including the now-unnecessary reset during cache flushing (`ODB_PREPARE_FLUSH_CACHES`).

The patch is small (1 insertion, 10 deletions) and uncontroversial. It follows logically from the earlier patches in the series and aligns with the goal of centralizing alternate initialization. No user-visible behavior changes are intended, and the patch has no dependencies beyond the earlier changes in the series. The commit message is clear and concise, explaining the rationale without overstating the impact.

This patch looks ready for integration. It’s a clean follow-up to the eager-loading change and doesn’t introduce any new complexity or edge cases. If the series as a whole is accepted, this patch should merge without issue.
