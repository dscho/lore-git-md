This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch v3 series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The patch removes the `loaded_alternates` field from `struct object_database`, which tracked whether alternates had been loaded. Since the previous patch (3/5) now eagerly loads alternates during ODB initialization, this field is redundant and can be safely dropped.

The change is mechanical: the field is removed from `odb.h`, and all checks or assignments to `odb->loaded_alternates` in `odb.c` are deleted. The patch also simplifies `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` by removing the conditional call to `odb_add_alternate_recursively()`, as alternates are now always loaded. The diff shows a net reduction of 9 lines (1 insertion, 10 deletions) across two files.

This patch is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort and depends on the earlier patches in the series, particularly the eager-loading logic introduced in patch 3/5. There are no user-visible behavior changes, and the patch is uncontroversial given the prior work in the series. The removal of the field is a natural cleanup step after the lazy-loading mechanism was eliminated. No reviewer feedback has yet targeted this specific patch, but it aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB codebase.
