However, he cautions that this test may not isolate the list-management overhead, as lookup across packs is inherently linear. The key takeaway is that while the MRU logic *should* optimize prepend operations, the current implementation still risks quadratic behavior in edge cases. Peff suggests that the MRU updates should use a constant-time list movement (e.g., a doubly-linked list) rather than relying on explicit duplicate checks, which would address the inefficiency more robustly than a hashmap-based solution.
