This is the fifth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases. The patch is a clean-up: it removes the now-unused `alternates_db` field from `struct object_database` and adjusts the code to pass the alternate object directories directly to `odb_prepare_alternates()` during ODB initialization.

The change is mechanical and well-justified. Since the previous patches in the series already eliminated lazy-loading (patches 3 and 4), the `alternates_db` field no longer serves any purpose. The patch updates `odb_new()` to free the `secondary_sources` string immediately after use, rather than storing it in the ODB struct, and simplifies `odb_prepare()` by removing the redundant reprocessing of environment variables. The diff is small (17 lines changed, 9 insertions, 15 deletions) and touches only `odb.c` and `odb.h`.

This patch completes the series’ goal of centralizing alternate ODB initialization and removing scattered calls to `odb_prepare_alternates()`. There are no user-visible behavior changes, and the patch looks ready for integration. The series as a whole is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, and this final patch should not introduce any new concerns.
