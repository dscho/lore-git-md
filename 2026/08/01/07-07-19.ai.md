This is a call for contributions to **Git Rev News edition 137**, the project's community newsletter.

Christian Couder has posted a draft of the next edition on GitHub and invites the community to help finalize it. The draft is available at <https://github.com/git/git.github.io/blob/master/rev_news/drafts/edition-137.md>; contributions can be made either by sending a pull request or by commenting on the GitHub issue (<https://github.com/git/git.github.io/issues/853>) or replying to this email. The edition covers recent mailing list activity, new features, and community updates.

The team plans to publish the edition on **Monday, August 3, 2026**, so feedback is needed soon. All types of contributions—proofreading, article suggestions, link submissions, or volunteering for interviews—are welcome. Christian has CC’d everyone mentioned in the draft but apologizes for any omissions.
