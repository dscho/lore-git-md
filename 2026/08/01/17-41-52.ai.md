This is a small but essential patch in the **diff provider interface** RFC series. It adds the `process` field to `struct userdiff_driver` and teaches the config parser to populate it from `diff.<driver>.process`. This field will name the long-running hunk provider process for a given diff driver, but nothing actually uses it yet — the consult logic, protocol, and documentation arrive in the next patch (10/10).

The change is mechanical: two lines in `userdiff.h` to declare the field and its owned string, and seven lines in `userdiff.c` to parse the config key and manage ownership. This is the plumbing that enables the external process provider introduced later in the series. The patch is uncontroversial and ready to merge once the rest of the series is accepted.
