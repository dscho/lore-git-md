This is a minor test cleanup patch in Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder. The patch replaces `wc -c <two.t | xargs` with Git’s `test_file_size()` helper in `t/t5701-git-serve.sh`, a change Junio previously endorsed in principle but questioned for its weak commit message.

The discussion clarifies the motivation: `wc -c` pads its output with leading blanks on some platforms (e.g., macOS), and `xargs` was used to strip them. `test_file_size()` avoids this issue entirely by returning the size without padding. Pablo now plans to strengthen the commit message and restructure the test to avoid redundant `git rev-parse` invocations, addressing Junio’s feedback about error handling and efficiency. The change itself is uncontroversial and aligns with established test suite practices, but the revised version will make the benefits clearer.
