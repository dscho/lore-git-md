This is a **substantive technical review** of the third patch in brian m. carlson’s RFC series to enforce lowercase-only hex object IDs in Git 3.0. Jeff King (Peff) weighs in on Junio’s micro-optimization suggestion for `hex_to_bytes()`, where Junio proposed hoisting the `hexval` table selection outside the loop to avoid repeated conditional checks. Peff confirms that the compiler (gcc-15) does not perform this optimization automatically, even with inlining, but questions whether the performance gain would be noticeable in practice, given modern branch prediction. His comment is narrowly focused on the trade-off between code clarity and micro-optimization, not on the broader design or motivation of the series. The review suggests that while Junio’s suggestion is technically sound, its practical impact may be minimal, leaving the decision to brian whether to adopt it. No objections to the patch’s intent or correctness are raised.
