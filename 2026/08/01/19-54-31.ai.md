This is Junio C Hamano's review of the second patch in the `--reset-when-found` series, offering a targeted refactoring suggestion to improve code clarity and maintainability.

The suggestion is framed as a question ("I am wondering if...") rather than a demand, and Junio sketches how the change would work by listing the existing callers of `bisect_reset()` and asking whether they could call `bisect_clean_state()` separately instead.

The review does not challenge the feature's design or readiness for `next`; it is purely about internal code organization. Junio's tone is collaborative, and the feedback is specific enough to be actionable without being prescriptive.
