This is a surface-level review from Jeff King (Peff) on the second patch in Ted Nyman’s v6 series fixing packfile URI race conditions. The patch removes a redundant `close(tmpfile_fd)` call in `finish_http_pack_request()` to avoid a latent double-close bug, as `start_command()` already takes ownership of the descriptor and closes it even if the child process fails to start.

Peff acknowledges the patch’s purpose and the value of splitting it out for clarity, noting that the change could have been even more minimal (a one-line removal of the `close()` call) but accepts the current version as a reasonable simplification. He suggests the commit message could have mentioned that the `cleanup` label is now unused, but considers further polishing unnecessary. The review is purely stylistic and does not raise any technical concerns—this patch remains uncontroversial and ready for merge.
