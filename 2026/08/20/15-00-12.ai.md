This is a gentle ping from the author of a bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper. The patch itself was posted two weeks ago and remains unreviewed.

The fix targets a real and reproducible issue: when the GNOME keyring daemon silently omits locked or concurrently deleted items from its reply, the current code passes a NULL secret to `secret_value_get_text()` and `secret_value_unref()`, triggering assertion failures and losing the password even if it was still retrievable. The patch replaces the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag with an explicit `secret_item_load_secret_sync()` call, ensuring proper error handling without increasing round-trips to the keyring daemon. The change is small (13 lines added, 1 removed), self-contained, and confined to `contrib/credential/libsecret/git-credential-libsecret.c`.

The author reports encountering the error several times per week, indicating the bug is not theoretical. No tests are included, but the problem is easy to reproduce with concurrent credential operations. The commit message is clear and detailed, explaining both the problem and the solution. The patch is ready for review and appears to address the issue directly.
