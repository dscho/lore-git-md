This is the third iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making packfile generation pluggable in Git’s object database (ODB) layer. The series is part of the larger ODB abstraction effort, which aims to support alternative backends beyond the traditional loose-object and packfile storage.

The goal is to decouple the transport layer (fetch, push, bundle) from the "files" backend by introducing a generic ODB interface for packfile generation. This iteration addresses feedback from the previous version: it fixes a use-after-scope bug in the files-backend implementation (noticed by Elijah Newren) where a stack-allocated `child_process` could be dereferenced during abnormal exits, and it retains the progress made in v2 to eliminate most `the_repository` dependencies in `bundle.c`. The series now appears technically sound, with detailed commit messages and targeted refactoring.

Key changes include a new ODB interface (`odb_generate_pack()`) and its files-backend implementation, refactoring of `upload-pack`, `send-pack`, and `bundle` to use the new interface, and minor adjustments to option handling in `builtin/bundle.c`. The series touches core transport mechanisms and the bundle subsystem, so it’s likely to interest contributors working on fetch/push plumbing or alternate ODB backends. The long-term plan for `git-pack-objects` and repository maintenance remains deferred to a future `git-objects` command, but this series completes its stated goal: all pack streams leaving the repository now delegate generation to the ODB layer.

The series is queued in `seen` and appears ready for broader review. The remaining loose ends—such as the `extern` declaration in patch 5/6 (noted by Junio) and edge-case testing—are minor and unlikely to block progress. If you’re following the ODB abstraction effort or transport-layer refactoring, this is worth reviewing.
