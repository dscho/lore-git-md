This is the fifth patch in Justin Tobler’s nine-patch v5 series making `git receive-pack` fully ODB-agnostic. It refactors the `unpack()` function to stop relying on global variables (`receive_fsck_objects`, `transfer_fsck_objects`, `fsck_msg_types`, `max_input_size`, `reject_thin`, `quiet`) and instead bundles those settings into a new `struct unpack_opts` that is passed explicitly. The change is mechanical and preparatory: the next patch will move the unpack logic behind the generic `odb_transaction_write_pack()` interface, which cannot access globals.

The patch touches only `builtin/receive-pack.c`, introducing the `unpack_opts` struct and updating `unpack()` and `unpack_with_sideband()` to use it. The ternary initialization of `fsck_objects` and the late assignment of `err_fd` in `unpack_with_sideband()` are preserved as trade-offs for removing global state; these patterns were already present in the codebase and are not new debt. The patch is well-motivated by the broader ODB abstraction effort and aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating global state. All reviewer feedback from earlier iterations has been addressed, and this patch is ready for merging as part of the series.
