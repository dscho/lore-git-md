This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part v3 series introducing a pluggable ODB interface for packfile generation. The review comes from Karthik Nayak, a contributor closely aligned with Patrick’s ODB abstraction effort (both work at GitLab on the same team).

Karthik’s feedback is narrowly scoped but technically precise. He raises two points: first, he confirms that the files-backend implementation correctly delegates to `git pack-objects`, which aligns with the series’ design goal of reusing existing functionality while abstracting the transport layer. Second, he identifies a minor inconsistency in the interface design: the `source` parameter in the `generate_pack` callback is unused in the implementation, yet it is passed in both the callback definition and the static inline wrapper. This is not a functional bug but a potential source of confusion or future misuse. Karthik frames it as a "nit" (a surface-level concern), but the observation is substantive in that it questions whether the interface is unnecessarily exposing a parameter that serves no purpose in the current implementation.

The review does not challenge the core design or identify any edge cases, regressions, or missing functionality. It is a targeted, constructive observation about interface hygiene rather than a critique of the patch’s behavior or motivation. The feedback is unlikely to require a major rework but may prompt a small cleanup in a future iteration to either remove the unused parameter or document its intended future use. For now, the patch remains well-motivated and technically sound, with Karthik’s review serving as a minor polish note rather than a blocker.
