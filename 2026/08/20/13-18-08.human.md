This is the second patch in D. Ben Knoble’s three-part series converting the `USE_NSEC` build-time macro to a runtime `core.useNanosec` configuration option. The series is cooking in `next` under the topic `dk/use-nsec-runtime` and is on track to graduate to `master` after final polish.

This patch is a no-op refactoring of `repo_config_values_init()` in `environment.c`. It reorders the assignments to match the order of fields in the `struct repo_config_values` declaration, grouping them by configuration section ("core", "sparse", "branch") and adding comments to make future additions easier to validate. The change is purely stylistic and has no behavioral impact, but it improves maintainability as the struct grows.

The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the project’s preference for clear, self-documenting code. It was explicitly endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt in his review of v3, and the author has incorporated all requested polish. The only remaining loose end is a textual conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` in `seen`, which removes comments from the same struct—this will be trivial to resolve if both topics advance.

For readers tracking the series, this patch is a minor but necessary precursor to the third patch, which introduces the `use_nanosec` field to the struct. The reordering here ensures that the new field will be initialized in a logical place, reducing the risk of future oversights. The series as a whole is well-motivated, thoroughly reviewed, and ready for integration.
