This is the third patch in the `git history squash` series (v14, 8 patches total). It refactors the sequencer’s squash-message formatting helpers into reusable functions and moves the `todo_item_flags` enum to the header so other commands can present squash templates consistently.

The patch extracts three static helpers from `sequencer.c` into public functions in `sequencer.h`: `add_squash_combination_header` (the “This is a combination of N commits” banner), `add_squash_message_header` (the “This is the 1st/Nth commit message” or “will be skipped” header), and `squash_subject_comment_len` (determines whether a subject line should be commented out). It also relocates the `todo_item_flags` enum so callers can distinguish `amend!` from plain `fixup!` after `todo_list_rearrange_squash`. The changes are behavior-preserving; the only caller is still `git rebase -i`, but the next patch will reuse these helpers in `git history squash` to give it the same autosquash-style template.
