This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing ODB abstraction series, specifically the second patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch v3 iteration. The email captures a narrow but substantive refinement to the patch’s error messaging, addressing feedback from Karthik Nayak.

Karthik’s critique highlighted that the original message exposed backend-specific terminology ("fork"), undermining the abstraction’s goal of hiding implementation details. The new wording is neutral and consistent with the ODB interface’s design, removing the leak of internal mechanics.

The email also includes Karthik’s brief acknowledgment ("Okay that makes sense. Thanks!") and Patrick’s confirmation of the change. No other technical adjustments are proposed, and the patch remains mechanically sound.
