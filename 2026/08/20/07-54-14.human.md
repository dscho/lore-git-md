This is the second patch in Elijah Newren’s two-part bugfix series addressing a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The series fixes crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other commands when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed by a concurrent repack.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review here is **substantive**. He rephrases the race condition clearly and raises a thoughtful question about whether the fix is needed for all callers or just those using `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK`. His concern is that without `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK`, Git would normally reload packfiles and recover automatically, making the fix unnecessary for some commands. However, he acknowledges he might be missing something and ultimately endorses the patch as written. He also considers an alternative design (eagerly loading MIDX-referenced packfiles at startup) but agrees the current approach is better because it avoids unnecessary overhead and only triggers in edge cases.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. It adds lightweight recovery logic to the packed-object backend, ensuring object lookups succeed even when the MIDX references a removed packfile. The fix is confined to `find_pack_entry()` and benefits all callers without requiring changes elsewhere. With Junio’s earlier acknowledgment and Patrick’s endorsement, this patch is effectively ready for integration, pending Junio’s resolution of a mechanical merge conflict with an in-flight topic. No further changes are expected.
