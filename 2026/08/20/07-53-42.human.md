This is the third patch in Karthik Nayak’s three-part series optimizing reftable stack reloads during transactions. Jeff King (Peff) replies with benchmark results that show a much larger performance improvement than the 1–2% originally reported: his measurements indicate a consistent 25% speedup for `git update-ref --stdin` across ref counts from 10,000 to 50,000. The discrepancy in numbers is noted but not investigated further; Peff endorses the optimization as worthwhile regardless, citing the general cost of unnecessary syscalls.

The patch itself remains unchanged and is now backed by stronger performance data. The series as a whole is still under review, with Junio C Hamano’s safety concern about patch 2 (lock relocation) and Justin Tobler’s suggestion for patch 3 (avoiding the `skip_if_locked` parameter) still unresolved. Peff’s feedback does not address those open questions but reinforces the value of the optimization.
