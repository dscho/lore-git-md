This thread is about improving the visibility of Git’s `gitdatamodel(7)` man page, a tutorial explaining Git’s data model. The series, authored by Kristoffer Haugsbakk, has now been fully merged into Junio’s `next` branch as of December 3, 2025. The goal was to ensure the page isn’t overlooked by adding cross-references from key documentation files like `git(1)` and `gitglossary(7)`, while also linking back to the glossary from the tutorial itself.

The final patch in the series (v3) was purely cosmetic, implementing minor stylistic tweaks suggested by Julia Evans—removing an inline cross-reference to `gitglossary(7)` from the tutorial’s introduction and simplifying the wording in the "SEE ALSO" section. These changes were uncontroversial and addressed the last of the feedback. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the series but focused on surface-level details like commit message wording, while Kristoffer confirmed no regressions after testing against the `seen` branch.

With all four patches now integrated, the effort is complete. The `gitdatamodel(7)` page is now properly advertised in `git help --guides`, linked from relevant sections of `git(1)`, and cross-referenced in the glossary, making it far more discoverable for users. No further changes are expected.
