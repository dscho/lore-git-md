This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s 4-patch documentation series improving discoverability of the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page. It adds cross-references from four glossary terms—**blob**, **commit**, **object**, and **tree**—to the corresponding sections in `gitdatamodel(7)`, and also adds `gitdatamodel(7)` to the "See Also" list in `gitglossary(7)`.

The changes are confined to `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc` and `Documentation/gitglossary.adoc`. Each glossary entry is tweaked to include a short pointer ("See ... in linkgit:gitdatamodel[7]") that directs readers to the more detailed tutorial material. The phrasing varies slightly depending on how much the glossary already covers: for example, the **ref** entry keeps its existing detail but adds a "see also" link, while the **object** entry simply points to the data-model page for further explanation. The patch also adds `gitdatamodel(7)` to the "See Also" section of `gitglossary(7)`, placing it alongside other tutorial pages.

No code, tests, or on-disk formats are touched. The patch is purely additive and does not alter existing behavior. The rationale is straightforward: the glossary already defines these terms, and the data-model page provides a deeper, tutorial-style explanation that users would benefit from discovering. The commit message explains the reasoning behind the phrasing choices and confirms that the links are relevant and non-redundant.

This patch is uncontroversial and well-scoped. It complements the earlier patches in the series (which added `gitdatamodel(7)` to `git(1)` and `command-list.txt`) and continues the theme of making the data-model tutorial more visible. No objections or requests for changes have been raised so far. The series as a whole remains a low-risk, high-value documentation improvement.
