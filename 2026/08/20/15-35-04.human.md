The exchange confirms that the broader performance curiosity Jeff raised is now on the radar of the maintainer, but the Trace2 instrumentation patch itself remains unaffected. The author is still expected to post v3 incorporating the consensus on hash algorithm consistency (`f->algop->rawsz`) and Jeff’s refactoring, while the hash recomputation issue will likely be deferred to a future effort.
