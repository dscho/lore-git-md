This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The patch migrates the deduplication logic for alternate ODB sources from `khash` to `hashmap`, eliminating an implicit dependency on `the_repository` and replacing the flaky `repo_ignore_case()` check with a runtime-resolved `core.ignoreCase` value. The commit message and code both acknowledge the mechanism’s dubious value, marking it as `NEEDSWORK` for potential future removal.

Junio’s reply engages directly with the core design question: whether `core.ignoreCase` should even be part of the repository configuration when dealing with alternates. Junio also spots a potential correctness issue in the implementation: the patch assumes `o->repo` is fully initialized when `odb_source_paths_cmp()` is called, but this may not hold during early repository setup. His suggestion to default to case-sensitive comparison when the repository isn’t ready is a pragmatic fix, though it could reintroduce the very flakiness the patch aims to eliminate.

The exchange leaves the patch’s future direction open. The `NEEDSWORK` note already signals the project’s ambivalence about case-insensitive deduplication, and Junio’s questions reinforce that sentiment. The most likely outcome is that the patch will proceed as-is for now—retaining the mechanism but documenting its fragility—while the broader question of whether to drop it entirely lingers for a future cleanup. For readers tracking the ODB refactoring effort, this is a minor but revealing skirmish: the tension between correctness, complexity, and real-world edge cases is far from settled.
