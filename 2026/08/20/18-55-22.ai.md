This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s 4-patch documentation series that makes the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page discoverable. It adds three cross-references to `git(1)`: under “Description” (alongside the other tutorials), under “Terminology” (as a pedagogical complement to `gitglossary(7)`), and under “See Also” (replacing the now-redundant mention in “Further Documentation”). The patch touches only `Documentation/git.adoc`, changing eight lines to insert the new links. The commit message quotes the original rationale for `gitdatamodel(7)`—that a glossary is not a good way to learn concepts—and explains why each placement is appropriate. No code, tests, or on-disk formats are affected; the full test suite was run on the merged series to confirm no regressions. This patch is uncontroversial and directly advances the series’ goal of advertising an existing but unlinked tutorial.
