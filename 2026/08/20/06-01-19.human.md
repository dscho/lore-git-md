This is a brief follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB packfile-generation series, specifically addressing a safety issue in patch 1/6. Elijah Newren’s review identified a potential use-after-scope bug in the files-backend implementation (`odb_source_files_generate_pack()`): the local `child_process cp` is registered for cleanup via `clean_on_exit = 1`, but since `cp` is stack-allocated, an abnormal exit would cause `cleanup_children()` to dereference reclaimed stack space. Patrick acknowledges the bug and confirms the normal teardown path happens to avoid the issue (because `finish_command()` matches by PID, not pointer), but agrees the abnormal-exit case is unsafe. He plans to fix it in the next iteration. The exchange is narrowly focused on this one technical point and does not revisit the rest of the series.
