Previously, such entries were silently ignored; now, Git emits a warning using the standard `config_error_nonbool()` helper when a valueless key is encountered. The patch also adds a test case in `t7400-submodule-basic.sh` to verify the warning behavior.

The change is a direct response to maintainer feedback on v1, which suggested using `config_error_nonbool()` instead of a custom warning message. The author has incorporated this feedback and resubmitted the patch inline to avoid whitespace corruption issues that previously blocked review.
