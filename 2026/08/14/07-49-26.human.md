This is a short follow-up in the ongoing packfile performance regression thread. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges Jeff King’s observation that Git already maintains a hashmap for tracking packfiles in `packfile_store_add_pack()` (introduced in 2019) and agrees it makes his earlier hashmap counterproposal redundant. He now suggests moving that existing hashmap from the packfile store into the packfile list itself to make it more broadly usable, though he does not elaborate on how this would address the remaining O(N) scan or whether it would supersede Johannes Schindelin’s fast-path optimization. The comment is brief and does not reopen the design debate, but it signals that Patrick is still thinking about a longer-term refactoring. For now, the fast-path patch remains the focus of the thread.
