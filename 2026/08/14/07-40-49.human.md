This is a brief follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series making Git’s packfile generation pluggable as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The series is now in `seen` after Junio C Hamano resolved a merge conflict by temporarily removing the `--no-ref-delta` topic.

Junio’s earlier review of patch 5/5 flagged two lingering uses of the global `the_repository` variable in `bundle.c`—one direct, one via `the_hash_algo`—that violate the series’ goal of eliminating implicit repository references. Patrick accepts the feedback and plans to address it in the next iteration. He notes that fully removing `the_repository` from the file is complicated by `get_log_output_encoding()`, which still depends on it, but intends to minimize the dependency and add an explicit `extern` declaration. The fix is straightforward and should not affect the patch’s functionality or design. No other issues are raised, and the series remains on track.
