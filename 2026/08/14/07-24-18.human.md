This is the first patch in a two-part refactoring series targeting the `the_repository` removal effort. It prepares `builtin/pack-objects.c` for the upcoming move of the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository` by ensuring every call site in the file has access to a `struct repository *`.

The patch drops the `UNUSED` marker from `cmd_pack_objects()`'s `repo` parameter and adjusts the call graph to pass `repo` through to `read_stdin_packs()`. Since `option_parse_missing_action()` cannot easily gain a `repo` parameter (its signature is fixed as an `OPT_CALLBACK`), the patch defers the side effect of setting `fetch_if_missing` until after `parse_options()` returns in `cmd_pack_objects()`, where `repo` is available. The change is mechanical and preserves existing behavior, but it is necessary scaffolding for the second patch, which will replace the global with a per-repository flag.

The patch is well-scoped and addresses the feedback from Junio’s v4 review, which identified the need to pass `repo` through the call graph before updating the assignments. No new test coverage is introduced, but the change is straightforward and unlikely to introduce regressions. The series as a whole remains under review for the broader architectural question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository`, but this patch is a necessary and uncontroversial step toward that goal.
