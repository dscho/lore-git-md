This is a refactoring patch series (2 patches) targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The goal is to move the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository`, making it a per-repository flag that controls whether missing objects trigger lazy fetches from promisor remotes. This is the fifth iteration of the patch, addressing the five remaining `the_repository` references identified in Junio’s review of v4.

The series updates 15 files to replace the global `fetch_if_missing` with `repo->fetch_if_missing`. The first patch ("pack-objects: give fetch_if_missing call sites access to 'repo'") is new in v5 and focuses on plumbing the `repo` parameter through the call graph in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to ensure the flag is accessed via the caller-supplied repository. The second patch ("repository: move fetch_if_missing into struct repository") is the core change, carrying forward the work from v4 while addressing the remaining `the_repository` references in `builtin/fetch-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `builtin/rev-list.c`, `common-init.c`, and `git.c`.

The patch preserves existing behavior: the default value remains `1` (enabled), and initialization occurs in `initialize_repository()`. Special handling in `builtin/fsck.c` and `builtin/index-pack.c` defers operations on the repository until after parameter parsing to avoid null pointer dereferences. The series also updates the partial clone documentation to reflect the new per-repository nature of the flag. The architectural debate about whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` remains unresolved, but the v5 patch resolves the immediate technical blockers by ensuring all call sites use the caller-supplied `repo` parameter.
