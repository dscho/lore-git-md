This thread is about a small but tidy refactoring patch from Colin Hinton that removes an unused `name` parameter from Git’s `chdir_notify` API. The parameter became obsolete after earlier commits stopped passing descriptive names, and the field was never read after `chdir_notify_reparent()` was removed in mid-2026. The patch itself was complete in its first version, but v2 added a detailed commit message tracing the parameter’s deprecation history across three prior commits, addressing the only remaining feedback from Jeff King and Junio Hamano.

The change touches ten files across the object database, refs, setup, and temporary object directory subsystems, mechanically removing the `name` argument from registration, unregistration, and callback definitions. There are no behavioral changes, no new symbols, and no compatibility concerns—just a straightforward cleanup. Both Peff and Junio have reviewed the patch and confirmed its correctness, and the updated commit message now fully documents the historical context. The patch is ready for merging and has no open questions or loose ends.
