This is a refactoring patch under review, with a substantive review from Jeff King (Peff). Subsequent patches (like `1f43ff2c7e` and `0de2467e6c`) then stopped passing descriptive names, setting the field to `NULL` instead. The commit message currently states the parameter is unused but doesn’t connect it to the earlier removal of the trace output. Peff’s concern is that without this context, a future reader might wonder if the unused parameter was a bug rather than an intentional simplification.
