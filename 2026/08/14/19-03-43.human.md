The discussion underscores that there is no perfect solution, but both reviewers lean toward the simplest approach: case-sensitive comparisons, even if it means tolerating rare duplicates.

This exchange shifts the consensus toward a simpler design, which could lead Patrick to revise the patch. The unresolved question is whether the performance impact of occasional duplicates (e.g., extra lookups for objects not found in the first alternate) is acceptable.
