This is a surface-level review of Tilak Raaz's GSoC microproject patch. Weijie helpfully links to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` for guidance and demonstrates the `b4 am` failure.

The feedback is purely about patch formatting and tooling compatibility, not about the substance of the change itself. Tilak's patch—switching `submodule.active` parsing to warn on valueless entries—remains unaddressed on its technical merits. The review is a gentle nudge toward mailing-list conventions rather than a critique of the code.
