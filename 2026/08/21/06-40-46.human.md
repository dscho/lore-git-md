The patch itself is a minimal, targeted fix: it raises the default threshold for loose-object repacking in Git’s auto-maintenance from 100 to 6700, aligning it with `git gc`'s long-standing default. This reduces the frequency of repacks in high-concurrency repositories, mitigating race conditions where loose object directories are pruned while other processes are still writing to them. The change touches only two files (`Documentation/config/maintenance.adoc` and `odb/source-files.c`) and involves no on-disk format modifications.

Patrick Steinhardt’s original commit message provides clear historical context for the 6700 threshold, tracing it back to a 2007 calibration that balanced pragmatism and stability.
