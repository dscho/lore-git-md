Core problem or goal: Eliminate the inefficiency where `git push` from shallow clones resends the entire toplevel tree (gigabytes in large repos) even for tiny changes, due to walking back to shallow grafts and assuming the server lacks them. The goal is to reduce unnecessary data transfer while maintaining backward compatibility.
