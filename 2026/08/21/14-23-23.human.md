This is the third and final patch in Alexey Samsonov’s series to replace the deprecated `utime()` function with `utimensat()` for POSIX.1-2024 compatibility. This patch cleans up the legacy headers and shims that are no longer needed now that all callers have been converted.

The patch removes the `#include <utime.h>` directive from `compat/posix.h` and test fixtures, deletes the `mingw_utime()` function from `compat/mingw.c`, and removes the legacy header shims in `compat/vcbuild/include/` that were used for Visual C++ builds. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial: they simply remove code that is no longer referenced after the earlier patches in the series.

This is a straightforward cleanup that follows naturally from the rest of the series. The patch is well-scoped and unlikely to introduce regressions, as it only removes unused code. Reviewers may focus on verifying that no callers of `utime()` or the removed shims remain, but the earlier patches in the series appear to have handled that thoroughly. The series as a whole is a well-motivated, cross-platform effort to future-proof Git’s timestamp handling.
