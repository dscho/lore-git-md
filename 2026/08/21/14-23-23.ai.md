This is the third and final patch in Alexey Samsonov’s three-part series to replace the deprecated `utime()` function with `utimensat()` for POSIX.1-2024 compatibility. This patch cleans up the legacy headers and shims that are no longer needed after the earlier patches converted all callers to the new `git_utimensat()` wrapper.

The patch removes the `#include <utime.h>` directive from `compat/posix.h` and deletes the now-unused `mingw_utime()` function from `compat/mingw.c`. It also removes the legacy header shims in `compat/vcbuild/include/` (both `sys/utime.h` and `utime.h`), which were previously used to provide a Windows-compatible `utime()` implementation. Finally, it removes redundant `#include <utime.h>` directives from `t/helper/test-chmtime.c` and `t/t4051/includes.c`, as these files no longer reference any `utime.h` symbols.

This is a straightforward cleanup patch that follows logically from the earlier work in the series. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial, as they only remove code that is no longer referenced. The patch is well-scoped and does not introduce any new behavior or risk. Reviewers are unlikely to raise objections, though they may verify that no callers of `utime()` remain in the codebase. The series as a whole is a well-motivated platform-compatibility effort with clear benefits for future-proofing Git.
