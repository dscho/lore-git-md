This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem. The email is a reply from Junio C Hamano to Patrick’s defense of retaining case-insensitive deduplication for alternate paths, despite its theoretical flaws.

Junio and Patrick agree that removing the deduplication mechanism entirely would not solve the underlying problem of case-insensitive filesystems, and both are reluctant to break existing setups that rely on the current behavior. Junio proposes a pragmatic direction: treat case-insensitive matching as a "best effort" optimization rather than a strict correctness requirement. This aligns with Patrick’s earlier argument that the mechanism, while imperfect, has worked reliably for years without reported issues. The discussion clarifies that the worst-case outcome of a failed deduplication is a minor performance penalty (an extra lookup in a duplicate alternate), not a correctness issue.

The email also addresses Junio’s earlier concern about whether `o->repo->initialized` is guaranteed to be true in `odb_source_paths_cmp()`. Junio now concedes that, since case-insensitivity is already a best-effort feature, the code can safely proceed as-is even if the repository isn’t fully initialized. This removes a potential blocker for the series, though the broader design constraints around `core.ignoreCase` (e.g., its reliance on `the_repository` and the lack of a submodule-compatible alternative) remain unresolved. The thread leaves the patch’s `NEEDSWORK` note intact, signaling that the mechanism may still be revisited in the future, but no immediate changes are required. The series is still queued in Junio’s `seen` branch.
