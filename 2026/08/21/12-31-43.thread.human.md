This thread introduces parallel packfile URI downloads during `git fetch` and `git clone` to reduce transfer time, an opt-in feature controlled by the `fetch.packfileURIThreads` configuration key. The initial v1 series by Patrick Steinhardt refactors packfile URI handling and implements parallel downloads using POSIX threads, with benchmarks showing significant speedups (5.35x–7.58x).
