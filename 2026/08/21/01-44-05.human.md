This is the v2 submission of the first patch in Elijah Newren’s two-patch bugfix series for a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The series addresses a crash in `git replay` when objects become temporarily unreadable due to a stale multi-pack-index (MIDX) referencing removed packfiles.

The patch itself is a targeted fix for `pick_regular_commit()` in `replay.c`, which previously treated any non-zero `result->clean` value as success—including negative values, which signal unreadable objects. This oversight caused `git replay` to dereference a NULL `result->tree`, resulting in a SIGSEGV. The fix adds an explicit check for `result->clean < 0` and returns an error message when merge inputs are unreadable. The change is minimal and well-motivated, with no behavioral side effects beyond preventing the crash.

Elijah has incorporated Junio C Hamano’s feedback from the v1 review, removing a redundant `test_grep ! "[Ss]egmentation"` check in the test. The test now relies solely on `test_must_fail` to verify graceful failure, which is the correct approach. The rest of the patch remains unchanged. Elijah also clarifies that he won’t resubmit the series just to remove the line, deferring to Junio’s preference for handling the change during an "evil merge" to resolve an unrelated conflict with an in-flight topic.

This patch is effectively ready for integration. Junio’s review was substantive and endorsed the fix, and the only requested change has been addressed. The series as a whole is a high-priority candidate for merging due to its impact on server-side stability, particularly for operations like `git replay` that rely on `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK`. No technical objections remain, and the patch is part of a broader effort to harden Git against race conditions in the repacking and MIDX subsystems.
