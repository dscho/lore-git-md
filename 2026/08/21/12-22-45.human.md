Neither comment engages with the substance of the patch—only the wording of the commit message. The patch itself (adding three cross-references to `git(1)`) remains uncontroversial and directly supports the series’ goal.

No technical concerns or objections have been raised about this patch or the series as a whole. The feedback here is purely editorial and does not require a re-roll unless the author chooses to address it. The series is still under review, with no indication of merge status from the maintainer.
