This thread is about a patch series that replaces the deprecated `utime()` function with `utimensat()` for POSIX.1-2024 compatibility, authored by Alexey Samsonov. The series introduces a `git_utimensat()` wrapper, updates 23 files across the codebase, and includes MinGW-specific support for nanosecond-precision timestamps. Junio C Hamano acknowledged the series positively, noting that `utimensat()` has been supported on macOS since 2017, but no explicit approval or merge intent has been stated yet.

However, the thread has hit a procedural roadblock. brian m. carlson raised a legal and policy concern: the series was generated by an AI tool (Gemini Flash) and then manually verified by the author. The Git project's `SubmittingPatches` document requires contributors to certify they know the origin of their contributions and have the right to submit them under Git's license. The policy currently does not allow significant content generated by AI tools due to unclear legal implications. Until the project's leadership (likely Junio or the PLC) clarifies or enforces this policy, the series is effectively blocked from further consideration.
