Thread identity: Patch series to replace deprecated `utime()` with `utimensat()` for POSIX.1-2024 compatibility. Series version: initial (v1). Originating author: Alexey Samsonov (via GitGitGadget). Thread type: patch series.

Core problem or goal: Remove all uses of the deprecated `utime()` function, which was removed from POSIX.1-2024 and may disappear from future libc implementations (e.g., LLVM-libc). Replace it with `utimensat()` to ensure long-term compatibility while enabling nanosecond-precision timestamps. The series introduces a `git_utimensat()` wrapper in `compat/posix.h`, replaces all `utime()` calls across the codebase, removes legacy headers/shims, and includes MinGW-specific support with full interface compatibility (including `UTIME_NOW`/`UTIME_OMIT` flags and `AT_FDCWD` handling).
