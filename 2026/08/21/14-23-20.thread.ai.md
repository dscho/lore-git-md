Core problem or goal: Remove all uses of the deprecated `utime()` function (removed in POSIX.1-2024) and replace it with `utimensat()` to ensure long-term compatibility and enable nanosecond-precision timestamps. The series introduces a `git_utimensat()` wrapper, replaces all `utime()` calls, removes legacy headers/shims, and includes MinGW-specific support with full interface compatibility (e.g., `UTIME_NOW`, `UTIME_OMIT`, `AT_FDCWD`).
