This is a maintainer reply in the `report` hook thread, weighing in on the exit-status semantics debate. Junio C Hamano endorses Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to align the `report` hook’s failure mode with the `pre-receive` hook: instead of having `receive-pack` die on a non-zero hook exit (discarding stdout and leaving the client with a generic "remote end hung up" error), the hook should rewrite all ref status lines to `"report hook failed"` and allow `receive-pack` to exit cleanly. This change would give clients a more actionable error message and reduce surprise for administrators. The reply explicitly calls this "the most valuable input" in Patrick’s review, signaling that the current "nuclear option" behavior is unlikely to survive in its present form. No other technical concerns are raised; the discussion remains focused on refining the hook’s contract rather than its core functionality. The thread is now effectively blocked on Karthik Nayak addressing this behavioral change.
