This is a brief but substantive exchange in the ongoing discussion about the new `report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, endorses Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to align the hook’s failure behavior with the existing `pre-receive` hook. Specifically, instead of having `receive-pack` die on a non-zero hook exit (which discards the hook’s stdout and leaves the client with a generic "remote end hung up" error), the hook should rewrite all ref status lines to `"report hook failed"` and allow `receive-pack` to exit cleanly. This change would give clients a more actionable error message and reduce surprise for administrators.

The exchange is notable for its brevity and clarity: Junio explicitly calls out the proposal as "the most valuable input" in Patrick’s review, signaling strong maintainer support for the behavioral change. No new concerns are raised, and the discussion remains focused on refining the hook’s contract rather than its core functionality. The next step is likely for Karthik Nayak to implement the change, which would resolve the last major open question in the thread. The patch is otherwise well-documented, tested, and aligned with GitLab’s MVCC use case, making it a strong candidate for eventual inclusion.
