This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series to remove lazy-loading of alternate object databases in Git’s ODB subsystem. The email is a reply from Junio C Hamano to Patrick’s v3 patch 2/5, which migrates the deduplication mechanism for alternate ODB sources from `khash` to `hashmap` and retains case-insensitive path comparisons using `core.ignoreCase`.

Junio’s reply clarifies the design consensus: the case-insensitive deduplication mechanism, while imperfect, is retained as a "best effort" optimization for platforms with case-insensitive filesystems. He acknowledges that removing the mechanism entirely would not solve the underlying problem (inconsistent path comparisons across filesystems) and instead proposes a pragmatic approach: treat `core.ignoreCase` as a hint for case-insensitive matching, accepting that edge cases (e.g., repositories spanning filesystems with mixed case sensitivity) may incur minor performance penalties rather than correctness issues. This aligns with Patrick’s earlier argument that the mechanism has worked reliably for two decades and avoids regressing edge cases.

The discussion also resolves Junio’s earlier concern about the timing of repository initialization in `odb_source_paths_cmp()`. Junio now accepts that the code can safely assume `o->repo` is initialized (as `odb_new()` sets it early), and even if it isn’t, the worst-case outcome is a redundant search in a duplicate alternate path—a minor performance hit, not a correctness failure. This removes the last technical blocker for the patch, though the `NEEDSWORK` note remains to signal that the mechanism may be revisited in the future.

The email does not address the unresolved constraints in patch 1/5 (the `repo_config_values()` limitation or Junio’s broader design concerns about `core.ignoreCase`), but those are orthogonal to this patch’s scope. The series remains queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, with this patch now effectively unblocked.
