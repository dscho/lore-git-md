This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove lazy-loading of alternates. The email is a reply from Patrick to Junio C Hamano’s review of the patch, which questioned the use of `core.ignoreCase` for deduplicating alternate object database sources.

Patrick pushes back on Junio’s suggestion that `core.ignoreCase` should not be part of the repository configuration. He argues that the current mechanism, while imperfect, has worked for 20 years without reported issues, and that introducing new limitations (such as declaring repositories cannot span filesystems with different case sensitivities) would retroactively break existing use cases. He emphasizes that the patch preserves existing behavior and that the complexity is justified to avoid regressing performance in edge cases with many alternates. Patrick also notes that the repository is guaranteed to be fully initialized when `odb_source_paths_cmp()` is called, addressing Junio’s correctness concern about `repo_config_get_bool()`.

The discussion remains unresolved. Junio’s review raised fundamental questions about whether case-insensitive deduplication is worth the complexity, while Patrick defends it as a practical necessity. The patch retains the mechanism but documents it as `NEEDSWORK`, signaling it may be removed in the future. The thread highlights a tension between simplicity and backward compatibility, with no clear consensus yet on the path forward. The series is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch but may require further revision to address these design concerns.
