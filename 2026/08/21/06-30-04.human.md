This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series making packfile generation pluggable in Git’s ODB layer. It refactors the progress-meter option handling in `git bundle` to simplify the interface and prepare for the ODB migration in the next patch.

The current code passes four related options (`--quiet`, `--progress`, `--all-progress`, `--all-progress-implied`) directly to `git pack-objects`, creating a confusing interface where two of the options are redundant (`--progress` and `--all-progress` do the same thing) and one (`--all-progress-implied`) is always passed unconditionally. The patch replaces these with a single boolean `progress` flag, defaulting to `isatty(STDERR_FILENO)`, and pushes either `--progress` or `--quiet` into the `pack_opts` strvec accordingly. The `--all-progress-implied` option is preserved for backward compatibility but becomes a no-op.

The change is mechanical and well-motivated: it reduces cognitive overhead without altering behavior, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale. The patch is part of a larger effort to migrate `git bundle` to the new ODB interface, which will eliminate the direct `git pack-objects` invocation in favor of a declarative setup. Karthik Nayak’s review of an earlier iteration praised the refactored code as "much nicer to read," and the v4 update is functionally identical to v3, suggesting stability.

This patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration. It touches only `builtin/bundle.c` and makes no functional changes, so it carries minimal risk. The next patch in the series (5/6) will eliminate most `the_repository` references in `bundle.c`, and the final patch (6/6) will complete the ODB migration. The series as a whole is queued in `seen` and nearing readiness for `next`.
