Core problem or goal
A Windows-specific bug where misconfigured `diff.textconv` filters (particularly AI-based or external tools) fail to handle CRLF line endings, leading to silent corruption of diff output. The issue manifests when filters do not strip `\r` characters, causing downstream tools (e.g., Windows Terminal) to misbehave or crash. A proposed workaround (`git config --global diff.lfclean.textconv "sed -e s/\\r//"`) strips `\r` characters, but its safety and sufficiency are unconfirmed.
