This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s 4-patch documentation series improving discoverability of the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page. The reviewer, Patrick Steinhardt, points out two minor copy-editing nits in the commit message: the man page should be referenced as `gitdatamodel(7)` (not `(1)`), and the phrase “since it as useful” is missing the verb “is.” Neither comment engages with the substance of the patch—only with the wording of the commit message. The patch itself (adding cross-references to `git(1)`) remains uncontroversial and directly advances the series’ goal of advertising the existing tutorial. No re-roll is required unless the author chooses to address these editorial suggestions.
