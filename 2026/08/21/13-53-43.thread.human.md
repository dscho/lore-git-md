This thread is about a Google Summer of Code (GSoC) patch series by K Jayatheerth that proposes adding Unicode box-drawing characters (`│`, `├`, `─`, etc.) to the output of `git repo structure` when the user’s locale is UTF-8, while preserving ASCII fallback for non-UTF-8 environments. The series also fixes a latent bug in `is_utf8_locale()` to ensure robust locale detection.

The series is currently under review and consists of two patches: one fixing the locale detection bug in `gettext.c`, and another implementing the Unicode rendering in `builtin/repo.c`. The changes are narrowly scoped, maintain backward compatibility, and include test coverage for both normal and edge cases. No objections or requests for changes had been raised until now.

However, Junio C Hamano, the project maintainer, has expressed skepticism about the feature. His feedback highlights concerns about inconsistent rendering of Unicode box-drawing characters across terminals due to varying width assumptions, suggesting the change may not be universally reliable or visually coherent. This response carries significant weight and signals that the series may face challenges in being accepted unless the author can address these rendering concerns or demonstrate robust cross-terminal compatibility. The thread remains open, but the maintainer’s feedback shifts the burden of proof to the author.
