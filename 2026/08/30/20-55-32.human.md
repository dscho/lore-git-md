This is a thread update from Junio C Hamano withdrawing the `[PATCH v2]` refactoring series that introduced `die_for_incompatible_opts()`. The series aimed to replace the fixed-arity `die_for_incompatible_optN()` family with a single varargs helper, but Junio now says he dislikes both the revised design (v2) and the preliminary parameter-reordering step from the first iteration. No alternative design or resubmission is proposed; the topic is abandoned for now.

The withdrawal follows Jeff King’s substantive review expressing a preference for compile-time safety (via a `LAST_ARG_MUST_BE_NULL` sentinel) over the `EOF` sentinel adopted in v2. While the discussion remained framed as a design trade-off rather than a correctness issue, Junio’s decision to drop the series suggests the compile-time safety concern carried enough weight to halt the effort. René Scharfe’s proposed alternatives (combining `EOF` and `NULL` sentinels or using a type-safe compound literal) are not addressed in this email, leaving them as unexplored options for any future resubmission.
