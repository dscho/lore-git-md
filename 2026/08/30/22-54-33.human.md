Junio C Hamano replies to Harald Nordgren’s v2 patch with a final edge-case question: if a user has CI enabled on their own repository, pushes a commit, and then opens a pull request, GitHub Actions might trigger two workflow runs for the same commit. Under the new configuration, these runs would no longer share a concurrency group, potentially leading to redundant builds of the same commit.

The question is narrow and practical: does the new grouping logic risk wasting CI resources in this specific scenario? Junio notes that in practice, most contributors push to repositories where CI is disabled, so the scenario may not arise often. He raises the point not as a blocker but as a last check before merging. The patch itself is otherwise ready, addressing all prior feedback and clearly documenting its behavior.
