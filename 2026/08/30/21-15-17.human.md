This is the maintainer’s final reply to the third patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series, which converts the build-time `USE_NSEC` macro into the runtime `core.useNanosec` configuration option. The patch itself is already applied under its topic branch and is effectively ready to graduate to `master`.

Junio’s message is brief and focused: he acknowledges the patch is correct for now but flags a long-term architectural constraint. The use of `repo_config_values()` in the new code path means that any future effort to support submodule operations in-process (e.g., `git diff --recurse-submodules`) will require `repo_config_values()` to work on repositories other than `the_repository`. Until that happens, the current implementation will hit a `BUG()` if a submodule’s index is loaded and `is_racy_stat()` is called. He notes that Patrick Steinhardt already raised this concern earlier in the thread and links to the relevant message.

The observation is not a blocker for this patch—Junio explicitly says “this is probably fine” and “this looks good to me”—but it serves as a heads-up for future work. Readers who follow Git’s libification or submodule efforts will recognize this as a small but real constraint on the order in which those projects can proceed. For everyone else, the takeaway is that the patch is uncontroversial and ready to merge, with no immediate action required.
