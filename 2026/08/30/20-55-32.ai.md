This is a thread update from Junio C Hamano withdrawing the `[PATCH v2]` refactoring series that introduced `die_for_incompatible_opts()`. The series aimed to replace the fixed-arity `die_for_incompatible_optN()` family with a single varargs helper, but Junio now finds the revised design unsatisfactory and the preliminary parameter-reordering step from the first iteration even less appealing. The withdrawal follows Jeff King’s substantive review expressing a preference for compile-time safety (via `LAST_ARG_MUST_BE_NULL`) over the `EOF` sentinel adopted in v2. No further iterations or alternatives are proposed at this time; the topic is abandoned pending a potential future redesign. The withdrawal is brief and does not revisit the technical trade-offs, leaving the compile-time safety vs. flexibility debate unresolved.
