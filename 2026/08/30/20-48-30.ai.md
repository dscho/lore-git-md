This is the third patch in Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the `git checkout` internals to eliminate the monolithic `checkout_main()` helper. The patch moves the validation of `--ours`, `--theirs`, `--force`, and `--merge` option compatibility from `checkout_main()` into `checkout_paths()`, where it logically belongs.

The change is a straightforward refactoring: the validation logic is unchanged, but its location is improved. In the original code, the check was performed in `checkout_main()` only when pathspecs were present, before dispatching to `checkout_paths()`. Since checking out a branch does not use index stages, the validation is irrelevant there and should be scoped to the pathspec code path. The patch moves the check into `checkout_paths()` and inverts the conditional in `checkout_main()` to skip it when no pathspecs are given.

The diff is minimal (10 lines, 5 insertions, 5 deletions) and mechanical. No behavior changes are introduced, and the test suite continues to pass. This patch is part of the series’ broader goal of extracting reusable logic into focused helpers, making the codebase easier to maintain and extending the libification effort beyond built-in commands. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the architectural direction established in the parent thread.
