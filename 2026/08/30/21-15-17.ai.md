This is the maintainer's reply to the final patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series (v5, 3/3). Junio C Hamano acknowledges the patch is technically sound but raises a long-term architectural concern: the new dependency on `repo_config_values()` in `is_racy_stat()` forces an ordering constraint on future work. Specifically, any effort to support submodule operations in-process (e.g., `git diff --recurse-submodules`) will now require `repo_config_values()` to work on arbitrary repositories, not just `the_repository`. This is not a regression—`repo_config_values` was already limited to `the_repository`—but the new call site in `is_racy_stat()` makes the limitation more visible. Junio references Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier email (linked) where this constraint was first noted. The patch itself remains ready for `master`; the concern is forward-looking and does not block graduation.
