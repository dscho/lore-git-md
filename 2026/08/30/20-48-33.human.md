This is the sixth patch in Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore`. The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort and directly implements the libification path clarified in the parent thread: reusable logic is extracted from built-in commands into standalone helpers, and command entry points call these helpers directly.

This patch is the core architectural change. It eliminates the monolithic `checkout_main()` choke point by rewriting `cmd_switch()`, `cmd_restore()`, and `cmd_checkout()` to call the extracted utility helpers directly. The `enum checkout_command` is also removed, as it is no longer needed. The patch is mechanical and preserves all existing behavior: `cmd_switch()` now initializes its options, validates them, and calls `checkout_branch()`; `cmd_restore()` handles the `--staged` default (`from_treeish = "HEAD"`) and resolves the source tree into `new_branch_info` before calling `checkout_paths()`; and `cmd_checkout()` dispatches to either `checkout_branch()` or `checkout_paths()` based on the arguments. The patch reduces `builtin/checkout.c` by 148 lines and is uncontroversial, passing the test suite.

The refactoring places all three commands on equal footing, eliminating the "cheating" pattern where `git switch` and `git restore` were bolted onto `git checkout` via a shared helper. This aligns with the architectural direction established in the parent thread and serves as a template for similar refactorings in other built-in commands. The patch is self-contained and could be merged independently if the final two patches (which introduce new top-level files) are deferred. No objections or alternatives have been raised, but broader feedback from contributors like René Scharfe or Patrick Steinhardt is still pending. The series remains an early draft for illustration, not intended for merging to `seen` due to potential conflicts with in-flight topics.
