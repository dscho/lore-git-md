This thread is about a use-after-free regression in `git stash show` with `--src-prefix` or `--dst-prefix`, introduced in Git 2.52.0. The bug causes garbage diff headers and affects scriptability. Nicolas Le Cam reported the issue, and Jeff King proposed a fix that delays freeing `argv` elements until `release_revisions()` is called, using a new `argv_to_free` field in `struct rev_info`. A follow-up patch refactors the `mark_argv_for_free()` helper to improve code clarity.

The fix is under review and targets the regression’s source commit. Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt agree on the centralized approach’s correctness and suitability for the `maint` branch, though Patrick raised a maintainability concern about the helper’s `const char *` parameter obscuring ownership transfer. Jeff responded by clarifying the design’s intent and referencing a follow-up patch that simplifies the flag logic. The discussion remains collaborative, with no controversy over the fix itself. The thread is now awaiting Patrick’s reaction to the follow-up patch. No new risks or open questions have emerged.
