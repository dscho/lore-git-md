This is a substantive review of Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy’s v9 patch converting `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab for per-commit state. Junio C Hamano reports that the patch introduces a critical regression: removing the `init_commit_name_slab(&name_slab)` call without replacement causes a division-by-zero crash when `commit_to_name()` attempts to access the uninitialized slab. The crash is reproducible with the simplest invocation (`git show-branch master next`) and stems from the slab’s `.slab_size` member remaining zero. Junio explicitly declines to hunt for further bugs, stating that the patch is not yet ready for integration. The technical issue is clear and blocking; no cultural or stylistic concerns are raised in this email.
