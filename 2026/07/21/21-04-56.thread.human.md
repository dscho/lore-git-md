This thread is about a targeted bugfix for a macOS-specific performance regression in Git’s fsmonitor daemon, introduced in April 2026. The regression caused the daemon to time out waiting for filesystem events on busy macOS systems, leading to multi-second delays during `git status`. Tamir Duberstein authored the patch, which flushes pending FSEvents asynchronously before the cookie wait, addressing the issue without affecting other backends.

The patch has been reviewed by Koji Nakamaru, who confirmed its scope and effectiveness but requested additional benchmark data in the commit message to quantify the timeout reduction. Junio C Hamano forwarded Koji’s review as a gentle nudge for Tamir to respond, but the patch itself remains unchanged and technically uncontested. The fix is narrowly scoped to the macOS fsmonitor backend, touching only `fsmonitor--daemon.c` and `compat/fsmonitor/fsmonitor-fs-listen-macos.c`, with no new symbols or on-disk format changes.

The ball is now in Tamir’s court to either provide the requested benchmark data or clarify why it’s unnecessary. Until then, the patch remains under review, though it is considered low-risk and well-scoped by Koji. No other objections or open questions have been raised.
