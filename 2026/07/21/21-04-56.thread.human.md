This thread began as an attempt to fix a macOS-specific performance regression in Git’s fsmonitor daemon, where timeouts on loaded systems led to excessive lstat() calls and multi-second delays during `git status`. Tamir Duberstein proposed a patch to flush pending FSEvents before the cookie wait, but withdrew it after further testing failed to confirm the root cause or the fix’s effectiveness.

The discussion left the underlying issue unresolved. Patrick Steinhardt’s follow-up clarified why the proposed fix was unlikely to work: Tamir had used `FSEventStreamFlushAsync()`, while the precedent from Watchman (which inspired the patch) relies on the synchronous `FSEventStreamFlushSync()`. The synchronous call guarantees the cookie event is processed before returning, avoiding the race with the timeout, whereas the asynchronous variant cannot. Patrick noted that adopting the synchronous call might introduce new risks, but did not suggest an alternative. No further action is planned unless new evidence emerges. The thread is now closed.
