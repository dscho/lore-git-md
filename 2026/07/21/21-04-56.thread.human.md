This thread is about a macOS-specific bugfix for Git’s fsmonitor daemon. The patch, authored by Tamir Duberstein, addresses a performance regression introduced in April 2026 that caused timeouts and excessive `lstat()` calls during `git status` on loaded macOS systems. The fix flushes pending FSEvents asynchronously before the daemon’s cookie wait, avoiding the timeout without affecting other backends.

The patch is under review and has received a substantive tested review from Koji Nakamaru, who confirmed its narrow scope and low risk but suggested improving the commit message with more benchmark data. Junio Hamano forwarded Koji’s review to the list without additional commentary, so the patch remains under review with no new version posted. The only remaining open item is addressing Koji’s request for a stronger commit message. No objections or merge signals have been raised.
