This thread is about a macOS-specific fix for Git’s fsmonitor daemon, which was timing out on loaded systems due to non-deterministic FSEvents queue drainage. The patch, authored by Tamir Duberstein, adds an asynchronous flush of pending FSEvents notifications before the cookie wait to address the issue. It’s narrowly scoped to the macOS backend and has no impact on other platforms.

The patch itself is technically uncontested: Koji Nakamaru’s tested review confirmed it’s well-scoped and low-risk, and Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier review raised only a question about FSEvents behavior, which Tamir addressed with a detailed explanation. However, the thread is now stalled on a process issue—Patrick has pushed back on Tamir’s use of an AI-generated rationale in the discussion, insisting the author personally demonstrate understanding of the FSEvents queue behavior and the change’s consequences.
