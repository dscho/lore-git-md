This is a follow-up in the ongoing `paint_down_to_common()` optimization thread, now in its sixth iteration. The series—already technically complete, fully reviewed by Derrick Stolee and SZEDER Gábor, and queued in Junio’s tree—remains stalled for lack of reviewer bandwidth. Kristofer Karlsson, who co-authored most of the patches, responds to Junio’s call for additional reviewers by suggesting ways to shrink the series if size is the bottleneck.

He proposes three optional simplifications: dropping the final commit that removes the commit-date fallback (a cleanup that could be deferred), omitting the test helper that improves assertion diagnostics (non-critical), or squashing some test or logic commits to reduce patch count. He notes that the current lull may simply reflect seasonal vacation schedules rather than a fundamental objection. The thread is now in a holding pattern, awaiting either more reviewers or a decision on whether to streamline the series.
