This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about René Scharfe’s patch series converting tempfile and lockfile APIs to repository-aware variants. The email is from Patrick Steinhardt, responding to Junio Hamano’s neutral stance on whether to add a compile-time guard (`USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE`) to the old global lockfile functions.

Patrick clarifies his position: while he still believes the guard would be a worthwhile safety measure to prevent accidental reintroduction of `the_repository` dependencies, he is comfortable merging the current series as-is. He describes the guard as "an easy thing" but defers it to a later patch, emphasizing that the series already improves the codebase. This effectively closes the open design question from earlier review without requiring changes to the series.

The series remains queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, and no further revisions are expected. The compile-time guard is now acknowledged as a medium-term goal but decoupled from this effort. For readers tracking the `the_repository` removal, this email confirms the series is ready to proceed while leaving room for future cleanup.
