This is a review of the first patch in Ted Nyman’s v2 bugfix series for packfile URI download race conditions. Junio C Hamano responds to the reroll with a mix of conceptual unease and mechanical nitpicks.

Junio grasps the core idea—multiple processes open the same partial-pack file read-write, use `pwrite` to deposit data at fixed offsets, and thereby avoid interleaving corruption—but calls it “dirty” without further elaboration. He also notes that the MinGW unlink-while-open workaround “somehow works,” implying he is still puzzling through the Windows-specific behavior rather than rejecting it outright.

On the mechanical side, Junio applauds the long-standing `--index-pack-args` → `--index-pack-arg` typo fix but asks that it be split into a separate preliminary patch. He also points out that the HTTP 416 status code is hard-coded as a magic number, though he concedes that introducing symbolic constants is outside this topic’s scope.

The review is substantive but not yet a verdict; Junio is still digesting the design and has not weighed in on whether the trade-offs (preserving resumability at the cost of cross-process coordination) are acceptable. The only concrete request so far is the mechanical split of the typo fix.
