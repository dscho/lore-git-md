This is a maintainer follow-up to a previously approved platform-compatibility test fix for the Trace2 ancestry tracking feature. The patch, authored by Jamie Magee, updates `t/t0213-trace2-ancestry.sh` to skip tests in user-mode emulation environments (e.g., QEMU) where `/proc/<pid>/stat` reports the emulator's name instead of the guest process. Junio C Hamano initially approved the patch and noted its "tricky" but acceptable grep pattern, planning to queue it for merging.

In this email, Junio clarifies that "Will queue" means the patch will appear in the `seen` branch, not that it has been reviewed or deemed ready for `next`. He explicitly requests a review from someone familiar with the Trace2 subsystem, indicating the patch is not yet fully vetted despite his earlier approval. The patch remains narrowly scoped to the test script, addressing a real portability issue without altering core functionality, but now lacks a substantive review from a Trace2 domain expert. This adds a small procedural hurdle before the fix can progress further.
