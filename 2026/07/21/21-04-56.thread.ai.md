Subject: [PATCH] fsmonitor: macOS: flush pending FSEvents before cookie wait
Series version: standalone patch (no series)
Originating author: Tamir Duberstein
Thread type: bugfix

Core problem or goal
Fix a macOS-specific performance regression in Git’s fsmonitor daemon introduced by 56cef9cb1a (April 2026). The regression caused the daemon to time out waiting for filesystem events on loaded macOS systems, triggering excessive lstat() calls and multi-second delays during `git status`. The root cause is the non-deterministic drainage of the FSEvents queue within the 1 ms latency interval, a behavior explicitly documented by Apple and previously warned about in the project’s own history (b05880d357, 2022).
