Open questions and loose ends
Junio C Hamano noted two minor stylistic inconsistencies in the v1 series:
1. In `git format-patch`, “base commit” and “prerequisite patch(es)” appear without backticks, while later instances (`prerequisite patch`, `patch id`) are backtick-quoted.
