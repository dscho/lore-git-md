This thread is part of Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing effort to convert Git’s man pages to a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style. The series targets four commands—`git imap-send`, `git format-patch`, `git send-email`, and `git request-pull`—with purely presentational changes that don’t alter behavior or CLI semantics. The first two patches (covering `imap-send` and `format-patch`) have been posted, and the third patch, converting `git send-email`, has now arrived. It mechanically updates the SYNOPSIS block and OPTIONS section to use backtick-quoted terms and placeholders, matching the style of prior conversions. The series remains under initial review, with no substantive feedback yet, and the final patch for `git request-pull` is still pending.
