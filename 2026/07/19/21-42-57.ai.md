This is a **substantive review comment** from Junio C Hamano on the core implementation of Harald Nordgren’s `git branch --delete-merged` feature (patch 5/7 in the v19 series). Junio clarifies a key design choice in the stacked-branch protection logic that initially confused him: the `deletable` and `spared` strsets are indexed by **branch names** (e.g., `main`) rather than full refnames (e.g., `refs/heads/main`). This ensures the logic only interacts with local branches, avoiding unnecessary or incorrect handling of remote-tracking branches (e.g., `refs/remotes/origin/main`).

The review confirms the design is **correct and sound**, addressing Junio’s initial concern that the use of `up_short` (the branch name after stripping `refs/heads/`) might be buggy. He now acknowledges the consistency of the approach: since `branch_get()` and `branch_get_upstream()` operate on branch names, indexing the strsets by branch names is appropriate and avoids mixing local and remote refs. The explanation also serves as documentation for future readers, reducing the risk of similar confusion.
