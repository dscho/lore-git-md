This is a documentation patch, the second in a four-patch series converting the man pages for `git format-patch`, `git imap-send`, `git send-email`, and `git request-pull` to the project’s new AsciiDoc synopsis style. The series is part of the ongoing effort led by Jean-Noël Avila to standardize all Git man pages on a consistent markup format.

The patch rewrites the SYNOPSIS and OPTIONS sections of `Documentation/git-format-patch.adoc` to use `[synopsis]` instead of `[verse]` and replaces single-quoted command names (`'git format-patch'`) with backtick-quoted ones (``git format-patch``). Every option term is now backtick-quoted, placeholders are consistently marked as `_<placeholder>_`, and prose references to commands and tools (e.g., `patch(1)`) are converted to backtick form. Two included files, `diff-options.adoc` and `format-patch-caveats.adoc`, receive minor updates to keep cross-references in sync.

No behavior, CLI interface, or rendered output changes are introduced; the patch is purely stylistic. The diffstat shows 264 lines changed in `git-format-patch.adoc` and 8 in `diff-options.adoc`, reflecting a mechanical conversion rather than new content. Reviewers are likely to focus on consistency with earlier conversions and on catching any accidental markup errors. The patch is well-structured and follows the established pattern for this effort, so it appears ready for routine review.
