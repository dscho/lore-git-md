If the error message simply says the branch is "being rebased," users may be confused when `git status` in the other worktree shows a different branch name. Phillip proposes two solutions: either improve `git status` to show all branches being rewritten (the better but more involved option), or adjust the error message to clarify that multiple branches are being updated. His example message—"cannot delete branch 'feature' because it is being updated by a rebase running in '../feature' which is updating multiple branches"—strikes a good balance between clarity and brevity.
