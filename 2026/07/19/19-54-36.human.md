This is a documentation patch, the third in a four-patch series converting the man pages for `git send-email` and its related configuration to the project’s new AsciiDoc synopsis style. The patch mechanically rewrites the SYNOPSIS block (replacing `[verse]` with `[synopsis]` and removing single quotes from the command name) and the OPTIONS section (backtick-quoting every option term, placeholders, and cross-references). It also updates `Documentation/config/sendemail.adoc` to backtick-quote all configuration keys and fix a bare `sendemail.aliasesFile` reference in the prose.

The changes are purely presentational: no behavior, option semantics, or CLI interface is altered. The patch is part of the ongoing “Documentation synopsis-style conversion” effort led by Jean-Noël Avila, and follows the established pattern of earlier batches. Reviewers will likely focus on consistency with prior conversions and on accidental markup errors rather than on technical correctness.
