This is the maintainer’s feedback on the core implementation of `git branch --delete-merged` (patch 5/7 in Harald Nordgren’s v19 series). Junio C Hamano clarifies a design choice that initially confused him: the `spare_data` structure uses **branch names** (e.g., `main`) rather than full refnames (e.g., `refs/heads/main`) as keys in its `strset` fields (`deletable` and `spared`). This is intentional and correct, as the stacked-branch protection logic only applies to local branches, and `branch_get()` expects branch names, not full refnames. Junio confirms the approach is sound and documents the rationale to help future readers avoid the same confusion.

The feedback is **purely explanatory**—no bugs or design flaws are identified. Junio’s note serves as a retrospective endorsement of the data structure design, which was already implemented correctly in the patch. This removes one potential point of uncertainty for reviewers and signals that the core logic is now well-understood by the maintainer.
