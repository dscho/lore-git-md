This is a follow-up in the ongoing **sequencer bugfix discussion** about the leftover `REBASE_HEAD` file after a successful rebase. Long 76 clarifies a point raised by Phillip Wood regarding the need for `--force` when pushing after a rebase.

Long acknowledges that the requirement to force-push is unrelated to the `REBASE_HEAD` bug itself. Instead, it stems from the fact that rebasing rewrites history, which naturally prevents a fast-forward push. However, he notes that in his case, the rebase involved conflict resolution, meaning the commit had to be modified, which is a typical scenario where force-pushing becomes necessary. This clarification reinforces that the `REBASE_HEAD` issue is a separate, though still problematic, cleanup oversight in the sequencer.

The email does not introduce new technical details or propose a fix, but it helps disentangle two distinct concerns: the expected behavior of force-pushing after a rebase (which is correct) and the unexpected persistence of `REBASE_HEAD` (which is a bug). The discussion remains focused on the latter, with the root cause and expected fix already well-defined by Phillip Wood in the parent email. No patch has been submitted yet, but the thread continues to build consensus around the need for a fix.
