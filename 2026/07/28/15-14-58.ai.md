This is a **bugfix-adjacent refactoring** in the diff plumbing subsystem, specifically a follow-up to the already-approved `jk/diff-relative-cached-unmerged` series. Jeff King (Peff) proposes adding a `BUG()` assertion to `oneway_diff()` in `diff-lib.c` to document and enforce an invariant that was previously implicit: at least one of the `idx` or `tree` parameters must be non-NULL. The change is motivated by a Coverity static-analysis warning triggered by the earlier refactoring patch, which moved the `--relative` prefix check earlier in the function. The warning is a false positive, but Peff and Junio agree that adding the assertion could help both human readers and static analyzers.

The patch itself is tiny (10 lines added) and purely defensive. It does not change behavior, fix a bug, or alter the logic of the function—it merely codifies an existing invariant that would be impossible to violate under normal operation. The commit message explains the reasoning clearly: a NULL `idx` implies the entry was deleted from the index but present in the tree, which in turn means `tree` cannot be NULL (and vice versa). The assertion message is neutral and factual, avoiding the humorous tone Junio suggested in the prior exchange.

This is the final loose end in the three-patch series (original bugfix + two refactorings). The entire series is already approved and queued for `next`, and this patch is optional—Peff notes he would be "happy to just let it be" if the assertion is deemed unnecessary. The discussion has been lighthearted and low-stakes, with no substantive objections or technical concerns. If merged, this would be the fourth patch in the series, but it is not blocking integration.
