This is a maintainer response to the second patch in Harald Nordgren’s two-patch series refining `git checkout -m` autostash behavior. Junio C Hamano accepts the patch as-is and signals intent to queue it for integration. The patch implements two user-facing improvements: a fast-path that skips the autostash retry loop when no tracked local changes exist, and a visual separation (blank line) between autostash conflict advice and the branch-switch confirmation message. Both changes are motivated by the cover letter’s stated goal of reducing redundant failures and improving output clarity. The patch touches `builtin/checkout.c` and adds test coverage in `t/t7201-co.sh`. No further changes are requested or implied.
