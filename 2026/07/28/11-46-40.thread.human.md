This thread is about a build system bug in Git’s Rustification effort, where the Makefile fails to respect the `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` environment variable. William Pursell initially reported the issue, which causes linker failures when users set `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` to a custom location—the Makefile hardcodes the path `target/release/libgitcore.a`, but the `cargo build` step doesn’t honor the variable, leading to a mismatch.

Todd Zullinger has now proposed a fix: passing `--target $*` to the `cargo build` invocation in the Makefile to ensure it uses the correct target directory. His review also flags a potential broader issue—whether similar hardcoded path assumptions exist in other `cargo build` calls, such as those for `libgit-sys` and `libgit-rs`. While the fix itself is straightforward and aligns with existing Makefile patterns, Todd’s review is surface-level; it doesn’t confirm whether those other calls are actually affected or test the proposed solution.

The patch is uncontroversial in scope but touches a historically sensitive area (Rustification and platform compatibility). No substantive reviews have landed yet, so the next steps are likely either a tested patch or further investigation into whether the issue is systemic. The broader Rustification effort, driven by Ezekiel Newren, remains a recurring topic, and this fix may interact with other build system changes or platform-specific work (e.g., Randall S. Becker’s NonStop port).
