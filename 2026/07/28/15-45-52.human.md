This is the second patch in the v8 series introducing `--linearize` to `git replay`. It is a **pure refactoring** that moves the logic for resolving the replay base out of `pick_regular_commit()` and into `replay_revisions()`, the function’s caller. The goal is to consolidate base-selection logic in one place, improving maintainability and reducing confusion about where the replay base is determined.

The change reduces `pick_regular_commit()`’s parameters from five to four by removing the `onto` and `replayed_commits` arguments and instead passing the already-resolved `replayed_base` directly. The new helper functions `put_mapped_commit()` and `get_mapped_commit()` (introduced in patch 1/3) are used to maintain consistency in how replayed commits are tracked. The commit message explains that this refactoring is preparatory for `--linearize` and does not alter behavior.

The diff is clean and focused, touching only `replay.c`. The logic for deciding the replay base is now fully contained in `replay_revisions()`, with clear comments explaining the fallback behavior for `--revert` mode. This patch has been stable since v5 and has received **substantive review** from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler, who raised concerns about earlier refactoring approaches (later reverted in favor of this design). The current implementation is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
