This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB abstraction series, and Justin Tobler’s reply raises a design question about the initialization sequence.

Justin’s review is substantive. He understands the patch’s goal—deferring ODB creation to allow backend-specific on-disk setup—but questions whether the new `APPLY_REPOSITORY_FORMAT_SKIP_ODB_CREATION` flag is the simplest approach. He suggests that requiring an explicit `odb_new()` call after `apply_repository_format()` might be cleaner, noting there are few call sites. This is not a rejection of the patch’s direction but a request for clarification on why the flag-based approach was chosen over a more direct refactoring. The question is narrowly scoped to the initialization flow and does not touch the rest of the series. No objections to correctness or implementation are raised.
