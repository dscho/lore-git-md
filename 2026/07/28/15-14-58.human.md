This is a small follow-up patch to the already-approved three-patch series (bugfix + two refactorings) addressing the `git diff --relative --cached` segfault. Jeff King (Peff) proposes adding a `BUG()` assertion to `oneway_diff()` in `diff-lib.c` to document the invariant that at least one of the `idx` or `tree` parameters must be non-NULL. This is a response to a Coverity static-analysis warning triggered by the second refactoring patch, which moved the `--relative` prefix check earlier in the function.

The patch itself is a defensive clarification: it adds a comment explaining why both `idx` and `tree` cannot be NULL simultaneously, followed by a `BUG()` assertion to enforce the invariant. The assertion message is neutral ("oneway_diff with neither idx nor tree") rather than the tongue-in-cheek suggestion Junio made in the prior email. Peff notes this is optional and could be dropped if the extra noise isn't worth it, but the patch is ready to apply if Junio wants it.

This is a purely internal change with no user-visible impact. The series remains queued for `next`, and this patch is the only open question—whether to include the assertion or not. The discussion is lighthearted and unlikely to generate further debate; the patch is uncontroversial and directly tied to the already-approved refactoring.
