This is a refactoring patch series (v2) targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Tian Yuchen narrows the scope to two global configuration variables—`pack_size_limit_cfg` and `assume_unchanged`—after dropping the controversial `minimum_abbrev` and `default_abbrev` migration from v1. The series moves these variables into the `repo_config_values` struct, making them repository-scoped rather than process-global, and updates all direct references to use the repository-specific instances.

The changes are mechanical but touch several subsystems: `pack-objects.c` (pack size limits), `update-index.c` and `read-cache.c` (`assume_unchanged` behavior), and `object-file.c` (ODB transaction logic). No getter functions are introduced for these variables due to their limited call sites, and backward compatibility is maintained by accessing values via `the_repository` where needed. The series remains infrastructure-only, with no intended behavior change, and is positioned as a stepping stone for future libification.

Files modified include `environment.c`/`environment.h` (central variable storage) and the caller sites listed above. The v2 iteration addresses Junio’s earlier objection by avoiding silent fallbacks to `the_repository` when `repo != the_repository`, a precedent that may influence future repository-scoped getter designs. No review feedback has yet been posted on these two patches, leaving their technical soundness unchallenged so far.

Key symbols introduced or modified: `repo_config_values.pack_size_limit_cfg`, `repo_config_values.assume_unchanged`. The series builds directly on prior `repo_config_values` work and is part of the broader effort to eliminate implicit reliance on the global `the_repository`. Test coverage is not explicitly discussed, but the mechanical nature of the changes suggests existing tests should suffice, though edge cases in ODB transactions and `core.ignorestat` behavior may warrant attention.

For follow-up discussion, expect focus on whether the remaining variables are the right candidates for migration, or if other globals might be more urgent for libification. The resolution of the v1 design debate (no silent fallbacks) sets a clear constraint for future patches in this area.
