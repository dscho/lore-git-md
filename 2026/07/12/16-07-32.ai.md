This is a **documentation patch** in Junio C Hamano’s six-part series clarifying contributor guidance. The email is a reply to Weijie Yuan’s surface-level review of the first patch, addressing three points: the phrasing of the proposed three-part commit-message template, the choice of AsciiDoc section markers, and the column-wrapping guideline.

Junio revises the template’s third section from **"Command (The Instruction)"** to **"Implementation (The Changes)"**, distinguishing it from the second section (**"Design (The Approach)"**). This change responds to Weijie’s confusion about the logical separation between "Solution" and "Command" by reframing the third section as a description of *how* the solution is implemented rather than *what* the solution is. The imperative-mood guidance remains, but the section’s purpose is now clearer: it focuses on the concrete changes, not the abstract design. Junio also accepts Weijie’s suggestion to use `====` for AsciiDoc section markers to match existing style.

On column wrapping, Junio clarifies that the "around 70 columns" guideline is pragmatic, ensuring readability in quoted email threads where indentation consumes space. He dismisses the discrepancy between `SubmittingPatches` (68–72 columns) and `MyFirstContribution.adoc` (72 columns) as unimportant, noting that editor defaults are irrelevant to the rule’s intent. No changes to the example commit message in `MyFirstContribution.adoc` are proposed.

The discussion remains narrowly focused on editorial details. No objections to the series’ core goals—codifying commit-message structure, clarifying trailers, or disambiguating the `seen` branch—have surfaced. The revised template and formatting tweaks will likely appear in a reroll.
