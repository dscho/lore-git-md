This is a brief follow-up from the patch author, Harald Nordgren, asking about the current status of his two-patch series to silence translation-catalog build output during `make -s` for gitk and git-gui.

The series is already procedurally complete: both patches have been merged into Johannes Sixt’s testing branches (`j6t-testing` for gitk and `hn/silence-make-s` for git-gui) and are slated for upstream Git proposal in the coming weeks, though not in time for the v2.55 release. Junio C Hamano and Sixt have both confirmed this timeline with no objections. Harald’s question is essentially a check-in on when the changes might land in upstream Git’s `master` branch; the answer, as previously stated, is that they will be proposed soon but are not yet scheduled for a specific release.
