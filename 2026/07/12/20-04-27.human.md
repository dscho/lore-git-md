This is a platform-compatibility review of Ramsay Jones’s Cygwin IPv6 URL regression fix. Torsten Bögershausen replies to Ramsay’s patch with a substantive, tested review that pushes back on one of the three changes.

The discussion centers on the `connect.c` hunk that removes a conditional originally added in 2018 to support UNC paths on Windows (`file://server/share/repo`). Torsten argues that the conditional is still needed for Git for Windows (GfW) and should not be removed for Cygwin either, since Cygwin itself supports Windows-style paths. He sees no reason to drop the logic and points to the original commit (ebb8d2c90fb) as justification.

Ramsay had suggested replacing the `#ifdef DUMMY` with `#ifdef GIT_WINDOWS_NATIVE` to keep the behavior for GfW while removing it for Cygwin, but Torsten’s objection implies the conditional should stay for both platforms. The rest of the patch—Cygwin-specific path-validation helpers and restored test coverage—is not contested.

This exchange leaves the patch in a state where the `connect.c` change is likely to be revised or dropped, while the other two hunks remain viable. The thread is now waiting for Ramsay to respond or send a v2.
