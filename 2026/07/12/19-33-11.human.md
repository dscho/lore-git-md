This is a **design discussion** in the ongoing `git log --graph` visual root indentation series (v9). Mirko Faina agrees with Pablo Sabater’s counter-proposal to cap indentation at **four levels** instead of two before resetting to the left margin. The four-level wrap avoids the "pyramid effect" that could create ambiguity in edge cases (e.g., when the number of visual roots is a multiple of 3 or 4), making the reset more visually abrupt and reinforcing the unrelatedness of commits. Mirko also endorses retaining the `--no-graph-indent` opt-out flag, particularly for non-oneline formats where indentation may feel intrusive.

The discussion is now settled on the **four-level wrapping design** as the preferred approach, addressing prior objections while keeping the lookahead buffer infrastructure intact. The only remaining open question is whether the opt-out flag should be included in the final implementation, but this is a minor detail compared to the core design decision.
