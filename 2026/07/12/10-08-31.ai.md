This is a **refactoring patch** (part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort) and a **follow-up to the recently merged `excludes_file` migration**. Specifically, patch 2/9 in Tian Yuchen’s v9 series moves the global `excludes_file` variable into `struct repo_config_values` and introduces a getter (`repo_excludes_file()`) that implements lazy-loading fallback to the XDG default path.

The patch is **technically sound** and **memory-leak-free** after the author addressed Junio C Hamano’s regression concern in v8. Pablo Sabater’s review here is **substantive but narrow**: he identifies a **performance nit** in the getter—it calls `repo_config_values(repo)` three times, which is redundant since the accessor returns a pointer. The suggested fix (caching the result in a local variable) is **actionable and uncontroversial**, and Tian has already acknowledged it. The rest of the patch is explicitly approved ("The rest looks fine").

No new design questions or blocking issues are raised. The patch remains **under review** and is not yet in `next` or `master`. Expect a v10 reroll incorporating the performance improvement, but the change is minor and does not affect the series’ overall trajectory. This is a **clean, incremental step** in the libification effort, with no user-visible behavior changes.
