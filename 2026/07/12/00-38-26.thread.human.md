This thread is about a bugfix patch by Farid Zakaria that makes `git rebase --autosquash` respect the `--empty` option when a `fixup!` or `squash!` operation cancels out its target commit, producing an empty commit. The goal is to allow workflows like reverting a commit via `fixup!` and having `--empty=drop` remove both the original and its revert.

The patch is currently stalled. The latest version (v3) received substantive review from Phillip Wood, who identified several edge cases—such as handling commits that were already empty, conflict resolution paths, and multiple fixups—that require a v4. The patch is also blocked on a dependency (`pw/rebase-drop-notes-with-commit`), which Phillip expects to graduate to `next` soon. Junio recently pinged Farid to check on progress, noting no updates have been posted since Phillip’s review. The core idea has no technical objections, but the patch isn’t ready for integration until the edge cases are resolved and it’s rebased on the dependency.

Key participants are Farid (author), Phillip (reviewer), and Junio (maintainer). Phillip’s feedback remains constructive, focusing on correctness and consistency with Git’s existing behavior. The next step is for Farid to post v4 once the dependency lands, addressing Phillip’s concerns. Until then, the patch remains under review.
