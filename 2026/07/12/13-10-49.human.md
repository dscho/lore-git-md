This is a **design discussion reply** in the ongoing `git log --graph` cascading indentation thread. Mirko Faina responds to Chandra Pratap’s objections to the bounded zig-zag counter-proposal with a concrete example and clarification. He refines his earlier suggestion: instead of a true zig-zag (which could create ambiguity), he proposes **capping indentation at two levels and then resetting to the left margin** for subsequent visual roots. This "bounded indentation" would avoid excessive horizontal drift while still distinguishing excluded parents, addressing the original concern about one-patch series stacking into a deep staircase. Mirko argues that the visual break between two levels and zero is unambiguous and does not resemble merge topology. The reply advances the usability debate by offering a middle-ground design that could satisfy both the need for clarity and the desire to avoid over-indentation. The ball is now back in Pablo Sabater’s court to either adopt this refinement or defend the original cascading approach.
