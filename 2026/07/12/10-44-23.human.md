This is a brief but important update in the ongoing libification effort. Tian Yuchen concedes to Pablo Sabater’s argument about the destructor signature in patch 1/9 of the v9 series. Instead of taking a `struct repository *`, `repo_config_values_clear()` will now take a `struct repo_config_values *` and access the private field directly, eliminating the submodule limitation and avoiding silent leaks. This resolves the philosophical divide: Pablo’s robustness-oriented approach wins out over Tian’s fail-fast preference.

The change is straightforward but consequential. It removes the `NEEDSWORK` comment about submodule support, simplifies error handling (no initialization checks needed), and aligns the signatures of `_init()` and `_clear()`. Expect a v10 reroll with this adjustment, plus the previously acknowledged performance nits in patches 2/9 and 3/9. The series remains technically complete and CI-clean; this design resolution should clear the last blocker for merging.
