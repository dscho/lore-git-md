This is a refactoring series (v10, 9 patches) that continues the `the_repository` removal effort by migrating more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is now technically complete, with all known memory leaks fixed and the design issues Pablo Sabater raised fully resolved.

The core work is split into two parts: infrastructure and strings (patches 1-6) and enums (patches 7-9). Patch 1 introduces `repo_config_values_clear()` as a destructor for heap-allocated strings, now taking a `struct repo_config_values *` to avoid initialization-order crashes and enable submodule support. Patches 2-6 migrate `excludes_file`, `editor_program`, `pager_program`, `askpass_program`, `apply_default_whitespace`, and `apply_default_ignorewhitespace` into the repository-specific structure, with proper memory management and getters where appropriate. Patches 7-9 handle the enum migrations (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`), relocating their definitions to avoid circular dependencies.

The series addresses all feedback from v9: redundant calls to `repo_config_values()` are eliminated, the destructor now works for any repository (including submodules), and minor typos are fixed. The RFC questions about migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) and the relationship to `git_branch_track` remain open but are not blocking. The author has promised follow-up patches to clean up comments and variable classification in `repo_config_values`.

This looks like a well-crafted, incremental step in the libification effort. The memory management is now robust, the design is settled, and the series is CI-clean. If you care about `the_repository` removal or repository abstraction, this is worth reviewing.
