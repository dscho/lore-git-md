This is a valid point, though the risk is low given the series’ focus on `the_repository` for now. The `NEEDSWORK` comment about the circular dependency (keeping `enum push_default_type` in `environment.h` instead of `remote.h`) remains unchanged and is still deferred for future cleanup.

The patch is otherwise complete, CI-clean, and ready for integration once the readability tweak is applied. No new memory leaks or behavioral changes are introduced. Reviewers should focus on the enum placement trade-off and the direct field access pattern, which is consistent with the rest of the series. The patch is part of a larger, well-reviewed effort to eliminate globals and centralize configuration management, and this step is uncontroversial in isolation.
