This is a documentation patch in Junio C Hamano’s six-part series to clarify contributor guidance. Michael Montalbo’s reply to the first patch offers surface-level feedback on wording, structure, and conciseness. He suggests rephrasing the "Solution (The Approach)" section to be more direct, merging overlapping guidance about alternative solutions, and replacing the term "Command (The Instruction)" with something like "Implementation" to better distinguish the mechanics of the change from its design. He also proposes reorganizing the "Formatting and Style Guidelines" section to co-locate formatting advice with the relevant commit-message components, reducing redundancy. Finally, he recommends adding examples of well-formed versus poorly formed commit-message components to make the guidance more concrete for newcomers. The feedback is editorial and does not engage with the series’ core goals (e.g., trailer semantics or integration branch disambiguation), but it could help make the guidance more accessible. Junio has already acknowledged similar suggestions from other reviewers and is preparing a reroll.
