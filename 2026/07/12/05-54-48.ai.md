The email is brief and does not include a concrete implementation or patch. It focuses on the user experience and workflow improvement rather than technical details. The idea targets developers who frequently use interactive rebase to clean up their commit history before submission. No prior discussion or related efforts (like existing commit message conventions or hooks) are mentioned in the email.

This is a lightweight, user-facing feature request rather than a deep technical change. The proposal's scope is narrow: it would affect only interactive rebase (`git rebase -i`) and possibly client-side hooks, without altering core Git behavior or on-disk formats.
