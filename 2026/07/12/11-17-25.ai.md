This is the first patch in v10 of Tian Yuchen’s nine-patch series migrating more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. It introduces the destructor `repo_config_values_clear()` and wires it into `repo_clear()`. The patch is **infrastructure-only**: it adds the function, documents its purpose, and frees only the `attributes_file` field (the rest will be handled in later patches). The signature takes a `struct repo_config_values*` (resolving Pablo Sabater’s review concern about submodule support and initialization-order crashes), and the function is called from `repo_clear()` to ensure proper teardown. No user-visible behavior changes; this is a pure refactoring step in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch is **ready for `next`** and sets the stage for the rest of the series. Reviewers should note the updated destructor signature and the fact that the function is now safe for any repository that has undergone `memset()`.
