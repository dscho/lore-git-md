Mirko Faina clarifies and sharpens his earlier counter-proposal for bounded indentation, addressing Chandra Pratap’s concerns about visual ambiguity. The key idea is to cap indentation at two levels before abruptly resetting to the left margin—creating a "wrapping" effect rather than a gradual zig-zag. This ensures the reset is unmistakable (no intermediate levels to confuse with branching) while preventing excessive indentation in simple cases like one-patch series.

The refinement directly resolves Chandra’s two objections: it decouples the feature from formatting flags (making it a general graph-drawing rule) and eliminates visual ambiguity by avoiding gradual transitions. Mirko’s ASCII illustration in the parent email makes the behavior concrete and testable. The change requires only a simple counter in `struct git_graph`, minimizing implementation risk.
