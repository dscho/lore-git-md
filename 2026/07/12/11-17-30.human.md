This is a refactoring patch (part 6 of 9 in v10) that migrates the global variables `apply_default_whitespace` and `apply_default_ignorewhitespace` into `struct repo_config_values`. The change is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, eliminating globals to enable per-repository configuration and prepare the codebase for libification.

The patch updates `git_apply_config()` to accept a `struct repository *` argument instead of relying on the `the_repository` global, and moves the two whitespace-related variables into the repository-specific config struct. Memory management is handled by `repo_config_values_clear()`, which now frees these fields during cleanup. The changes are mechanical and well-contained, touching only `apply.c`, `environment.c`, and `environment.h`.
