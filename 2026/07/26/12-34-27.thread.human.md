This thread is about a refactoring patch by Hardik Kumar that changes the return type of UTF-8 string-width calculation functions (`utf8_strwidth()` and `utf8_strnwidth()`) from `int` to `size_t` in `utf8.c` and `utf8.h`. The patch is a type-system cleanup addressing a long-standing `//TODO` comment, and its header-level change affects all callers of these functions.

The initial version (v1) sparked discussion about two key concerns: whether the patch should rely on implicit conversion at call sites or explicitly update all callers to use `size_t`, and the safety of removing an overflow check in the now-removed `cast_size_t_to_int()` helper. Hardik has now posted a v2 patch that addresses the first concern by updating all 11 callers to use `size_t` for local variables holding the return value, avoiding implicit conversions. The patch also removes unnecessary parentheses in a ternary and improves the commit message, as requested in the v1 review. However, the question of whether any callers relied on the overflow check remains unanswered, leaving a potential safety issue unresolved.

René Scharfe and Pablo Sabater provided substantive reviews of the v1 patch, raising high-weight concerns about compatibility and safety. Their feedback has largely been addressed in v2, though the overflow check question lingers. Hardik has signaled openness to further guidance on this point. The patch is now more comprehensive and better structured, but the remaining open question may require additional discussion before it can be merged.
