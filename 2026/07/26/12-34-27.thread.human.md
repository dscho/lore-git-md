This thread began as a refactoring effort by Hardik Kumar to change the return type of Git’s UTF-8 string-width functions (`utf8_strwidth()` and `utf8_strnwidth()`) from `int` to `size_t`, aiming to better reflect their inherently non-negative output. After three iterations (v1–v3), the series was withdrawn following sustained opposition from Junio C Hamano, Pablo Sabater, and Phillip Wood, who argued the practical benefits were minimal compared to the risks—regressions, pervasive explicit casts, and unnecessary complexity. The withdrawal resolved the debate in favor of the status quo, with the only remaining action being improved documentation to clarify the existing design’s rationale.

The final decision on documentation placement has now been made: Junio suggested replacing the TODO comment in `utf8.c` directly with the new rationale, rather than creating a separate file in `Documentation/technical/`. This keeps the explanation colocated with the code it describes, avoiding fragmentation. The thread is effectively concluded, with no further technical discussion expected unless Phillip Wood or others weigh in on the placement. The follow-up patch will document why the functions return `int` (to align with `printf()`-style APIs) and why the `cast_size_t_to_int()` helper remains necessary (to prevent overflows). All prior regressions and open questions are resolved by the withdrawal.
