This is the final review comment in Junio C Hamano’s assessment of Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable. The email focuses on the fifth patch, which introduces the `create_on_disk` callback in the ODB source interface and moves backend-specific directory creation into the "files" backend.

Junio’s feedback is substantive but forward-looking. He acknowledges the patch’s immediate correctness and the note about the mandatory `objects/` directory, then raises a design question: whether the `create_on_disk` callback is the right place for backends that do not use a traditional filesystem (e.g., cloud-based object stores requiring provisioning). He suggests renaming the callback to something more generic, like `initialize`, to better reflect its broader purpose beyond on-disk structures. The comment is not a blocker for this series but hints at future-proofing the interface for non-filesystem backends. No implementation changes are requested for the current patch; the feedback is purely about naming and long-term extensibility. The series remains under review, with the fifth patch now receiving its first technical engagement.
