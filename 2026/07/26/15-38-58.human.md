This is a bugfix series for the interactive rebase (`rebase -i`) subsystem, addressing two edge cases with skipped "fixup" and "squash" commands. Phillip Wood has posted a v2 of the two-patch series, incorporating feedback from Junio C Hamano on the first patch.

The series fixes two specific issues: an incorrect commit count in the template message when a preceding fixup or squash command is skipped, and the failure to open the editor after a `fixup -c` command if a preceding fixup or squash was skipped. These are niche but real bugs that affect users who skip fixup or squash commands during an interactive rebase.

The changes are minimal and surgical, touching only `sequencer.c` (the core rebase logic) and adding thorough test coverage in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` and `t/t3437-rebase-fixup-options.sh`. The v2 update addresses Junio’s feedback on the first patch by ensuring the reading logic correctly handles trailing newlines in legacy command-list files, a robustness improvement that guards against off-by-one errors.
