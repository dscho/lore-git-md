This is a follow-up cleanup series from Jeff King (Peff) that builds on his recently accepted bugfix for a segfault in `git diff --relative --cached`. The original fix—a minimal NULL check—was reviewed and endorsed by Junio, but Peff has since dug deeper into the history and identified two small cleanups that could make the code more robust and self-documenting.

The series consists of two patches touching `diff-lib.c`. The first removes a stale comment about advancing `o->pos` that no longer reflects the current code flow. The second refactors `oneway_diff()` to skip paths outside the `--relative` prefix *before* attempting to process them, rather than relying on a later NULL check. This second patch would make the original bugfix redundant (since the problematic paths are now filtered out earlier), but Peff suggests keeping both for safety and clarity.

The changes are purely internal to the diff plumbing and introduce no new behavior or user-facing features. They’re motivated by code hygiene and a desire to avoid future regressions in this historically fragile area. Junio’s earlier review of the bugfix didn’t raise any objections to the cleanup direction, and the patches themselves are small and well-explained. Given Peff’s track record and the low-risk nature of the changes, this series looks like a straightforward improvement that’s likely to be accepted once Junio has a chance to review it.
