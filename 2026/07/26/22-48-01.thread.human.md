This thread introduces a small but useful improvement to Git’s test suite: a new shell helper `commit_body()` that replaces about 61 duplicated instances of the pattern `git cat-file commit <rev> | sed -e '1,/^$/d'`. The duplication was not just stylistic—it masked exit codes, which a recent bugfix in `t7614` (commit `b6b276974e`) highlighted. Shlok Kulshreshtha authored the series, following Junio Hamano’s suggestion to consolidate the idiom.

The series is now at version 2, addressing two call sites missed in the initial version: the `message_body()` helper in `t7509` and an inline pipe in `t3404`. The conversion is mostly mechanical, but a few scripts needed special handling to avoid reintroducing the exit-code hole or to preserve existing behavior. For example, `t7600` now greps the stripped commit body instead of the raw commit object, and `t3900` writes the output to a file before piping it to `iconv`. All 13 affected test scripts pass, including cases where `git cat-file` deliberately fails.

Junio Hamano, who originally identified the exit-code issue, reviewed the first version and pointed out the missed sites. There are no open questions or objections, and the series is under review with no indication of merge status yet. The change is purely internal to the test suite and has no user-visible impact.
