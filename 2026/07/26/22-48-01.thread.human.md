This thread introduces a small but useful test-suite cleanup: a new `commit_body()` helper function that replaces about 60 scattered instances of a shell pipeline that masked exit codes. The series, now at v2, was authored by Shlok Kulshreshtha and is based on a suggestion Junio Hamano made while fixing a related bug in `t7614`. After review, Junio marked it for the `next` branch, where it is currently undergoing integration testing. The conversion touches 13 test scripts, all of which now pass; the only special cases were mechanical adjustments to avoid reintroducing exit-code holes. No open questions remain, and the author considers the series complete.
