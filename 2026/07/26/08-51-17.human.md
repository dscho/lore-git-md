This is a brief but substantive review from Jeff King (Peff) endorsing Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to move alternates handling into the "files" backend as part of the ODB abstraction rework. Peff agrees that treating alternates as an implementation detail of the files backend is a reasonable architectural direction, noting that the original abstraction was not intended to support arbitrary combinations of sources but rather to hide low-level details. He dismisses the theoretical loss of flexibility (e.g., mixing alternates with non-files backends like an SQL database) as unlikely to be a practical concern, suggesting that even in such cases, alternates could be handled as separate files-backend sources.

The review is short but high-weight: Peff is a deeply knowledgeable contributor whose endorsement carries significant weight, especially in a thread where he previously identified the root cause of the performance regression. His agreement with Patrick’s proposal reinforces its credibility and suggests the architectural shift is likely to proceed, though the timeline (Patrick’s patches are delayed until late July) means a near-term fix for the regression may still be needed. The email advances the thread by removing one potential roadblock to Patrick’s redesign.
