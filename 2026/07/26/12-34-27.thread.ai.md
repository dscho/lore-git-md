The withdrawal resolves the debate in favor of the status quo, with **improved documentation** to clarify the existing design’s rationale. The current patch (v4) documents why the functions return `int` (alignment with `printf()`-style APIs) and the necessity of `cast_size_t_to_int()` for overflow prevention.
