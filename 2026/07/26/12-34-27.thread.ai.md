Core problem or goal: eliminate a type mismatch where functions computing inherently non-negative display widths (UTF-8 string width) return `int` instead of `size_t`. The effort was intended as a purely type-system cleanup but **reviewers unanimously concluded the practical benefits were minimal** for functions measuring display widths of strings that fit on a single line, while risks (regressions, complexity, and pervasive explicit casts) outweighed benefits. The withdrawal resolves the debate in favor of the status quo, with **improved documentation** to clarify the existing design’s rationale.
