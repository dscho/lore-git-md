This thread is a small but practical CI improvement from Jeff King (Peff). He’s bumping the Ubuntu image used in Git’s static-analysis CI jobs from `ubuntu-22.04` to `ubuntu-latest` so the jobs pick up a newer Coccinelle version, eliminating a known performance issue that had previously been worked around in the code. The series also includes a preparatory cleanup in `bloom.c`—replacing an assertion macro with an explicit `if/else if/else` cascade ending in `BUG()`—to silence a Coccinelle false positive triggered by GCC 15 under `-O2`.

Peff posted the two-patch v1 series a few days ago; it’s currently under review. The first patch handles the `bloom.c` cleanup, and the second updates the CI configurations for both GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. Elijah Newren, who originally wrote the bloom filter code, was cc’d on the first patch but hasn’t responded yet. The series is self-contained, touches only the static-analysis job, and doesn’t change Git’s behavior or on-disk format—it just removes an old workaround and modernizes the CI toolchain. No objections or open questions have been raised so far.
