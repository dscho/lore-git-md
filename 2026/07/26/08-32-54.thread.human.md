This thread is about Jeff King’s patch series to update Git’s static-analysis CI job to use a newer Ubuntu image (`ubuntu-latest`, resolving to 24.04), aiming to pick up a faster Coccinelle version (1.3.0+) and remove a workaround for a performance issue. The series also includes a preparatory cleanup in `bloom.c` to silence a Coccinelle false positive triggered by GCC 15.

The patches have received positive feedback from Junio C Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and Elijah Newren, with Junio signaling approval in principle and Patrick calling the change "great." However, SZEDER Gábor’s recent review introduces a significant wrinkle: his benchmarks show that Coccinelle 1.3.1 is *4.5× slower* than 1.1.1 on the same Git revision, directly contradicting the patch’s assumption that newer versions are faster. Gábor also points out an unrelated CI inefficiency—static-analysis and documentation jobs install unnecessary build dependencies due to a 2021 pattern-matching change—which could be addressed in a follow-up.

The series is still under review, with no merge status reported. The key open question is whether the performance regression Gábor identified will require revisiting the image bump or if the trade-off (removing the workaround vs. slower Coccinelle runs) is still acceptable. The broader Coccinelle audit suggested by Patrick and Junio’s proposal to add exit criteria to `.cocci` rules remain speculative follow-ups. For now, the focus is on resolving the performance discrepancy before the series can move forward.
