This thread is about a small but practical improvement to Git’s CI infrastructure. Jeff King (Peff) sent a two-patch series to bump the Ubuntu image used in the static-analysis CI jobs from `ubuntu-22.04` to `ubuntu-latest` (which resolves to `ubuntu-24.04`), picking up a newer Coccinelle version that eliminates a known performance issue. The first patch cleans up a `bloom.c` assertion macro to silence a Coccinelle false positive, while the second updates the CI configurations for both GitHub Actions and GitLab CI.

The series has received positive feedback from Junio C Hamano, who called it "interesting" and thanked Peff, and Patrick Steinhardt, who described the change as a "great" and nearly free improvement. Elijah Newren, the original author of the bloom filter code, has now chimed in with a brief endorsement of the CI image bump, calling it "Makes sense to me; looks good." With no substantive objections raised, the series is likely to be queued in Junio’s `seen` or `next` branch soon.

The thread has also sparked a side discussion about a potential follow-up effort to audit and prune obsolete Coccinelle semantic patches, which could further speed up CI runs. Junio suggested adding clear exit criteria to each `.cocci` rule at introduction time, while Patrick proposed a broader audit of the `.cocci` files to identify completed migrations. However, this discussion remains speculative for now, with no concrete plan or measurement of potential gains yet proposed. The immediate focus remains on the CI image bump, which is ready to move forward.
