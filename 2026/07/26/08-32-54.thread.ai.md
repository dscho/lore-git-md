Subject: [PATCH 0/2] ci: bump Ubuntu image for static-analysis job
Series version: v1 (initial)
Originating author: Jeff King (peff@peff.net)
Thread type: patch series (CI/build system)

Core problem or goal
Bump the Ubuntu image version used in Git’s static-analysis CI jobs from `ubuntu-22.04` to `ubuntu-latest` (resolving to `ubuntu-24.04` at time of posting) to pick up a newer Coccinelle version (1.3.0+), eliminating a known performance issue previously worked around in code. The series also includes a preparatory cleanup in `bloom.c` to silence a Coccinelle false positive (`CHECK_ASSERTION_SIDE_EFFECTS`) triggered by an assertion macro when compiled with GCC 15 under `-O2`.

Current status
Under review; v1 posted. Junio C Hamano has signaled approval in principle ("interesting" and "thanks"), and Patrick Steinhardt has endorsed the change as a "great" improvement. No merge status reported yet; next step is likely queuing in `seen` or `next`.

Key technical details
- Files touched: `bloom.c` (preparatory cleanup: assertion macro replaced with explicit `if/else if/else` cascade ending in `BUG()`), `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml` (CI config updates: `ubuntu-22.04` → `ubuntu-latest`).
- Subsystems: CI/build system (static-analysis job only), bloom filter code.
- New/renamed symbols: none.
- Old-vs-new behavior: no functional change to Git; CI toolchain version updated and Coccinelle false positive silenced.
- On-disk format: unchanged.
- Test coverage: none added; CI job itself is the verification.
