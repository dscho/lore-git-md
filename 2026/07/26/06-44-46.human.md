This is a documentation and error-message patch, the first of three in Ted Nyman’s v5 series fixing race conditions in Git’s HTTP transport. The patch corrects the spelling of `--index-pack-arg` (singular) in both the `git-http-fetch` man page and the error messages in `http-fetch.c`, and clarifies that the first instance specifies the command while subsequent instances specify its arguments. The change is purely textual and uncontroversial; it was split out from the larger series in response to Junio’s request and has already been reviewed and approved by Taylor Blau and Jeff King. The patch is ready for merging and is effectively a baseline for the rest of the series.
