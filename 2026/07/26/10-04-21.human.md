This is the second patch in Ted Nyman’s **v5 series fixing packfile URI download race conditions**, now merged to Junio’s `next` branch. The email is a short reply in an ongoing discussion with Jeff King (Peff) about three minor, advisory improvements to the already-approved patch.

Ted confirms that the HTTP 416 handling is still needed: a concurrent downloader can finish the staging file before another process issues its Range request, triggering a 416 response that must be handled so `index-pack` can validate the local pack. He also acknowledges Peff’s observation that the explicit `close(tmpfile_fd)` was redundant because `run_command()` already closes the descriptor, and agrees to extract that cleanup into a preparatory patch for clarity. On the Windows/MINGW workaround, Ted explains the subtle sharing behavior and agrees to split the Windows-specific bits into a separate commit if Peff prefers, though he kept them together to avoid an intermediate state without the required sharing flags.

The patch itself remains unchanged and technically complete; these are purely cosmetic adjustments to the commit structure. The series is still on track for `master`.
