This is a **build system** and **platform compatibility** update from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer. He responds to the ongoing discussion about `USE_NSEC` and the proposed runtime-configurable approach (`core.useNsec`) by stepping back from the original patch.

Junio acknowledges that the conversation has evolved beyond the original scope of adding a `nanosec` Meson build option to mirror Autotools. The focus has shifted to a broader re-evaluation of `USE_NSEC` itself—whether it should be runtime-configurable, auto-detected, or deprecated entirely. Because this new direction would affect all build systems (not just Meson) and require deeper changes to Git’s behavior, Junio is **dropping the original patch from his integration tree** for now. He invites the participants to resubmit a revised patch series with the new focus, likely centered on implementing `core.useNsec` and deprecating the build-time knob.

The decision is pragmatic: the original patch, while technically sound, is now overshadowed by a more ambitious design discussion. Junio’s move signals that the project is open to the runtime-configurable approach but wants a clear, well-motivated proposal before proceeding. The ball is now in the court of Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King, and D. Ben Knoble to draft a patch series that implements the consensus—always compiling nanosecond support, exposing it via `core.useNsec`, and deprecating the build-time knob. Until then, the status quo remains: `USE_NSEC` is a build-time option, disabled by default, and available only in Autotools and Makefile.
