This is the fifth patch in Junio C Hamano’s six-patch documentation series clarifying contributor guidance. It updates `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to explicitly state that the phrase “Will queue” — which contributors often receive in reply to their patches — is simply a promise to add the topic to the `seen` branch and carries no implication of acceptance or approval. The change removes ambiguity by making it clear that neither “Will queue” nor presence in `seen` signals maintainer intent to merge; the actual signal of intent is the “Will merge to `next`” annotation in the “What’s cooking” report, as already documented in the previous patch (4/6). The patch is a single-line addition that directly addresses a recurring source of confusion among contributors.
