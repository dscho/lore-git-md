This is a **feature patch series** for `git log --graph` that adds cascading indentation to clarify visual roots—commits with excluded parents. The series is technically complete (v9) and has Junio Hamano’s architectural approval, but this reply from Mirko Faina escalates a **late-stage usability debate** about whether the indentation should be opt-in or opt-out.

Mirko argues the current always-on indentation clutters output for simple cases like sequential one-patch series, where the ambiguity it fixes doesn’t arise. He proposes `--oneline` or `--format=reference` should imply an opt-in flag (e.g., `--graph-indent`) rather than forcing users to disable indentation with `--no-graph-indent`. His alternative—a **zig-zag pattern with a depth limit**—would indent only the first two levels before resetting, avoiding ambiguity while reducing visual noise.

Pablo Sabater had previously defended the opt-out default as the least surprising choice for fixing a real ambiguity, but Mirko’s objection has shifted the discussion toward **backward compatibility and user experience**. The series is now at an inflection point: the maintainers must decide whether to merge as-is (accepting clutter for simple cases), switch to opt-in, or adopt a heuristic like zig-zag. No further technical work is needed, but the usability trade-off may delay progression to `next`. The thread is waiting for more opinions or a maintainer ruling.
