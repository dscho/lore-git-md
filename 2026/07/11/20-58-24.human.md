This is a procedural clarification from Junio C Hamano about the base for the v6 patch series. The series, which optimizes `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories, was rebased on `next` to resolve a merge conflict with `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`. Junio rejects this approach, explaining that `next` is an unstable integration branch containing many unrelated topics, and merging such a series into `master` would pull in all those topics prematurely.

Junio instructs the authors to instead identify the specific dependent topics (in this case, `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`), merge them into a stable base like `v2.55.0` or `master`, and rebase the series on that. This ensures the series can be cleanly applied and merged without dragging in unrelated changes. The technical content of the series remains unchanged; this is purely about the correct way to prepare the final submission for merging. The series is otherwise ready to merge, with all reviews completed and no open technical issues.
