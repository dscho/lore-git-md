This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. It introduces `repo_config_values_clear()`, a destructor for heap-allocated configuration variables stored in `struct repo_config_values`. The patch is the first in a nine-part series migrating nine additional global configuration variables into the repository-specific structure, following the recently merged `excludes_file` migration.

The discussion here centers on a design disagreement about the function's signature. Pablo Sabater argues that `repo_config_values_clear()` should take a `struct repo_config_values *` to match the `_init()` function and avoid silently bypassing submodules, while Tian Yuchen prefers keeping the `struct repository *` parameter for consistency with `repo_settings_clear()` and to maintain the existing guardrail that prevents crashes on submodule repositories. The exchange is substantive, focusing on initialization-order safety, submodule support, and whether the destructor should fail loudly or silently for invalid repositories. Tian acknowledges the signature inconsistency and plans to address it in the next reroll, but defends the current approach as a deliberate choice to tighten assertions over time rather than silently tolerate invalid usage.

The patch itself is technically sound and memory-leak-free after the v9 fix, but the design questions raised here could affect long-term maintainability. The series as a whole is under active review and not yet in `next`. If you care about libification or repository abstraction, this thread is worth following for the broader pattern it establishes.
