This thread is a documentation-only patch series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying contributor guidance in `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. The series addresses recurring questions about patch submission, review, and integration, formalizing existing practices without introducing new workflows. Key updates include a three-part commit-message template (**Observation**, **Design**, **Implementation**), explicit rules for `Reviewed-by:`/`Acked-by:` trailers, and disambiguation of the `seen` branch’s role (emphasizing it’s an early integration step, not a signal of acceptance).

Junio has responded to surface-level feedback from Weijie Yuan on the first patch, refining the commit-message template to replace "Command" with "Implementation" for clarity and aligning AsciiDoc section markers with existing style. The column-wrapping guideline (around 70 columns) was reaffirmed as pragmatic, with minor discrepancies between documents deemed unimportant. No substantive objections to the series’ goals have emerged, and the changes remain under review. The next version will likely incorporate these editorial tweaks, but the core clarifications—such as the meaning of "Will queue" vs. "Will merge to `next`" and the style for "What's cooking" reports—remain uncontested.
