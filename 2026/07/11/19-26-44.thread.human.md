This thread is a six-patch documentation series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying contributor guidance in Git’s documentation. The series updates `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches` to codify existing practices around patch submission, review, and integration, with a focus on reducing ambiguity for new contributors. Key changes include formalizing commit message structure (Observation, Solution, Command), clarifying the roles of the `seen` branch and "What's cooking" annotations, and providing guidance on trailer carry-over and unresponsive patches.

The series is currently under review, with no prior versions or merge status reported. The fifth patch, just posted, addresses a common point of confusion by explicitly stating that "Will queue" in maintainer replies is merely a promise to add the topic to the `seen` branch—not a signal of acceptance or approval. This builds on the fourth patch, which clarified that the actual signal of maintainer intent is the "Will merge to `next`" annotation in "What's cooking" reports. So far, the series has attracted no objections or reviews, and it remains uncontroversial, as it documents existing practices rather than proposing changes. The remaining patch (6/6) is still to come.
