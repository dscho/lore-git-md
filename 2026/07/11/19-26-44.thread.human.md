This thread is a documentation-only patch series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying contributor guidance in `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches`. The series codifies existing practices around patch submission, review, and integration, including commit message structure, trailer semantics, and the role of Git’s integration branches. Key updates include formalizing a three-part commit message template (**Observation**, **Design (The Approach)**, **Implementation (The Changes)**), disambiguating the `seen` branch’s role (it’s for early integration testing, not acceptance), and adding style guidance for "What’s cooking" report entries.

The series is under review, with minor editorial revisions pending in a reroll. Feedback from Weijie Yuan, Matt Hunter, Michael Montalbo, Patrick Steinhardt, and D. Ben Knoble has led to tweaks like consolidating redundant formatting guidance, rephrasing the **Design** section to clarify its purpose, and acknowledging reviewer disinterest as a possible reason for silence. However, two unresolved issues remain: the structural ambiguity in the commit-message template’s numbering (where **Observation**, **Design**, and **Implementation** are misleadingly presented as parallel to **Title** and **Body**) and the redundant parenthetical phrasing (e.g., "Observation (The Status Quo)"), which Knoble and Yuan argue is verbose and confusing. Junio has not yet responded to these points, so the template’s final form is still in flux. No substantive objections to the series’ goals have been raised, and the changes are uncontroversial. The next reroll will likely incorporate the pending editorial fixes.
