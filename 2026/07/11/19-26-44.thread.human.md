This thread is a documentation-only patch series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying contributor guidance in `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. The series codifies existing practices around patch submission, review, and integration, addressing recurring ambiguities such as commit message structure, the role of the `seen` branch, and the meaning of maintainer communications like "Will queue" and "Will merge to `next`".

The series is currently under review, with minor editorial revisions pending in a reroll. Junio has incorporated feedback from Weijie Yuan, Matt Hunter, and Michael Montalbo on phrasing, AsciiDoc formatting, and structural organization. Notably, the commit-message template has been refined to use **Observation**, **Design (The Approach)**, and **Implementation (The Changes)**, with the latter replacing the earlier "Command (The Instruction)" label. The series also introduces explicit style guidance for "What's cooking" report entries, specifying past or present perfect tense in passive voice.

The latest feedback from Weijie Yuan highlights a structural ambiguity in Junio’s draft diff for consolidating the formatting guidelines: the current numbering misleadingly presents **Observation**, **Design**, and **Implementation** as parallel to **Title** and **Body**, when they are actually subparts of the body. This observation, while editorial, addresses a readability issue in the pending reroll. No substantive objections to the series’ goals have been raised, and the series remains focused on documentation improvements with no dependencies on other in-flight work.
