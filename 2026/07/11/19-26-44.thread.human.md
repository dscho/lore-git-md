This thread is a six-part patch series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying and streamlining contributor guidance in Git’s documentation. The series updates `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to codify existing practices around patch submission, review, and integration, addressing recurring ambiguities such as commit message structure, the role of the `seen` branch, the meaning of maintainer annotations like "Will queue" and "Will merge to `next`," and the style of "What's cooking" report entries.

The series is under review, with no substantive objections raised. Junio has already incorporated feedback from Weijie Yuan, Matt Hunter, and Michael Montalbo, including revisions to the commit-message template (e.g., renaming "Command (The Instruction)" to "Implementation (The Execution)"), consolidating redundant formatting guidance, and reflowing awkward line breaks. The latest exchange in the thread is a minor editorial correction: Junio agreed to switch a "TIP" section marker to "NOTE" for consistency with the rest of `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`, a purely mechanical change with no impact on the series’ substance.

Key clarifications introduced by the series include the role of the `seen` branch (an early integration step, not a signal of acceptance), the distinction between "Will queue" (a promise to add to `seen`) and "Will merge to `next`" (the actual signal of maintainer intent to merge), and explicit style guidance for "What's cooking" report entries (past or present perfect tense in passive voice). The series is self-contained, targets only documentation files, and is on track for eventual integration once the pending reroll addresses the remaining editorial tweaks.
