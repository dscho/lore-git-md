This thread is a documentation-only patch series from Junio C Hamano aimed at clarifying contributor guidance in `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches`. The series codifies existing practices around patch submission, review, and integration, including commit message structure (now formalized as **Observation**, **Design**, and **Implementation**), the role of the `seen` branch, the meaning of maintainer annotations like "Will queue" and "Will merge to `next`", and trailer semantics. It also adds style guidance for "What's cooking" report entries.

The series is under review with minor editorial revisions pending in a reroll. Junio has incorporated feedback from Weijie Yuan, Matt Hunter, and Michael Montalbo on phrasing, formatting, and structural organization, but a key ambiguity remains: the commit-message template’s numbering misleadingly presents **Observation**, **Design**, and **Implementation** as parallel to **Title** and **Body**, when they are actually subparts of the body. D. Ben Knoble recently weighed in, agreeing with Weijie Yuan’s observation and suggesting clearer headings ("Observe the status quo", "Explain your approach", "Command the code to change") and removing redundant parenthetical phrasing. Junio has not yet responded to this feedback, leaving the diff unfinalized. Other open items include whether to include concrete commit-message examples and a lingering presentational nit about line wrapping in the integration branches description.

All reviews so far have been surface-level, focusing on wording, formatting, and structural clarity rather than the series’ core goals. No substantive objections have been raised, and the series remains uncontroversial. The next steps are for Junio to address the structural feedback and post a reroll.
