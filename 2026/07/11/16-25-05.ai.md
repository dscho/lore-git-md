This is a **substantive review reply** in the ongoing `git log --graph` cascading indentation thread. Mirko Faina responds to Pablo Sabater’s proposal for a zig-zag indentation pattern by refining it into a **bounded zig-zag** that caps indentation at two levels before resetting to the left margin. The idea is to prevent the visual clutter that Pablo acknowledged while still addressing the original ambiguity for excluded parents.

Mirko’s counter-proposal keeps the feature opt-in (triggered by `--oneline` or `--format=reference`) but avoids the ambiguity that Pablo’s zig-zag sketch introduced. He suggests tracking indentation depth with a simple counter, making the change purely mechanical and low-risk. The bounded zig-zag would satisfy both the need for clarity in ambiguous cases and the desire to avoid excessive indentation in simple one-patch series. This reply shifts the design discussion from “whether to indent” to “how to indent minimally,” offering a concrete middle ground that could break the current stalemate.
