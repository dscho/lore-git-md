This is a test modernization patch, part of a two-patch series that updates the Git-Perforce (`git p4`) label-import test script. The patch breaks long `&&` chains into multiple lines for better readability, a purely mechanical change with no behavioral impact. The series is uncontroversial and follows the ongoing community effort to modernize Git’s test suite.

The patch touches only `t/t9811-git-p4-label-import.sh`, splitting chains even when they are under 80 characters for consistency, as agreed in the v1 review. The commit message is clear and acknowledges the co-author, Vinicius Lira de Freitas. No new symbols or test logic are introduced—this is purely a style improvement.

Feedback from the v1 review (from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano) has been fully incorporated. The changes align with Git’s test modernization guidelines, which favor breaking long chains to improve error reporting and maintainability. The patch is well-crafted and ready for integration.
