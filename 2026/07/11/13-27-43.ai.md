This is the **eighth patch in the v6 series** optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for asymmetric merge-base queries. It implements the **core early-termination logic** that delivers the series' advertised **100-1000x speedups** by stopping the walk when one side's commit queue is exhausted and no pending merge-base candidates remain.

The patch adds a new termination condition to `paint_queue_get()`: if either `parent1_count` or `parent2_count` drops to zero, there are no pending merge-base candidates (`!mb_candidate_count`), and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`gen_ordered` and `commit_graph_generation(commit) < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`), the function returns NULL, halting the traversal. This prevents unnecessary work when one side of the history is much larger than the other (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories).

The technical documentation (`paint-down-to-common.adoc`) is updated to describe the new "side-exhaustion" condition, explaining why it is safe to terminate early in the finite-generation region (where topological ordering guarantees no future commits from the exhausted side can appear) but not in the INFINITY region (where commit-date ordering can violate topological guarantees). The doc also clarifies how clock skew could cause premature termination if the optimization were applied in the INFINITY region.

Tests in `t6600-test-reach.sh` are adjusted to reflect the reduced step counts: for example, the `merge-base --all commit-9-9 commit-9-1` test drops from 81 steps to 9 steps in full commit-graph mode, demonstrating the optimization's impact. The deterministic step-count assertions ensure the change is functionally equivalent despite the performance improvement.

This patch is **ready to merge** as part of the v6 series. It has no unresolved technical concerns, and the rebase on `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix` (now in `next`) ensures clean integration. The only remaining non-blocking items are feedback on the test diagnostic helper and the commit-date fallback removal, neither of which affect this patch's correctness or performance.
