This is the fifth patch in the **ready-to-merge v6 series** optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` in Git’s commit-reach subsystem. The patch adds **Trace2 instrumentation** to measure the number of commits visited during the paint walk, enabling deterministic performance analysis without relying on wall-clock benchmarks.

The change is minimal and focused: it introduces a `steps` counter in `paint_down_to_common()` and emits a `trace2_data_intmax` event at the end of the walk, reporting the total steps taken. The test suite (`t6600-test-reach.sh`) is updated to verify the step counts across all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat) using the new `test_paint_down_steps` helper (introduced in patch 2/10). For example, the test now asserts that `merge-base --all commit-9-9 commit-9-1` takes exactly 81 steps in most modes, while the full commit-graph mode takes 80 steps due to generation-number optimizations.

This instrumentation is **critical for validating the series’ performance claims** (100-1000x speedups in asymmetric queries) and will serve as a baseline for future optimizations. The patch is **low-risk**—it only adds observability—and the test updates are **deterministic**, ensuring the optimization does not regress behavior.
