This is a **substantive review follow-up** in the ongoing ODB abstraction series. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Taylor Blau’s observation about the incomplete refactoring in patch 3/8, where `traverse_bitmap_commit_list()` does not propagate non-zero return values from its callback. Peff notes that the non-bitmap counterpart, `traverse_commit_list()`, also does not support early termination—its callbacks return `void`, not `int`. He suggests that the two functions should remain consistent: either both should allow early termination via non-zero returns, or both should keep their current `void`-returning behavior.

The comment is a technical clarification rather than a blocker. It frames the design choice as a matter of internal consistency across the bitmap and non-bitmap code paths, not as a correctness issue. Peff does not advocate for either direction, but his observation implies that the decision should be deliberate and applied uniformly. This feedback does not require immediate action from Patrick Steinhardt, but it does highlight a design question that may need resolution before the series lands: whether early termination is a desirable feature for both bitmap and non-bitmap object iteration, or whether the current asymmetry should be preserved. The tone is neutral and matter-of-fact, reflecting Peff’s role as a historical context provider rather than an advocate for a specific outcome.
