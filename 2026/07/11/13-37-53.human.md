This is the final patch in Pablo Sabater’s **v9 series** implementing **cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`**. The patch delivers the user-visible behavior: commits that appear as roots (either true roots or commits whose parents are filtered out) are now indented by two characters per level, creating a clear visual cascade that prevents unrelated commits from being misread as children.

The implementation is built on the infrastructure introduced in patches 2/4 (unified lookahead population) and 3/4 (two-slot lookahead buffer). It adds a new `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state to connect indented roots to their children, tracks indentation depth via `visual_root_depth` and `visual_root_cascade` flags, and excludes merge parents from indentation to avoid redundant edges. The lookahead buffer ensures the logic works deterministically regardless of command-line options like `--author`, `--grep`, or `--max-count-oldest`.

The patch includes **514 lines of new tests** in `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh`, covering edge cases such as filtered parents, boundary commits, octopus merges, `--first-parent`, `--left-right`, `--max-count`, and `--max-count-oldest`. All failing test cases from earlier versions now pass, and the series is **technically complete**—no further revisions are expected unless final reviews turn up new issues.
