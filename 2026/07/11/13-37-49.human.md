This is the v9 revision of Pablo Sabater’s feature series implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`. The series addresses a long-standing readability issue where commits with excluded parents (visual roots) can appear vertically adjacent to unrelated commits, creating false visual relationships.

The core change remains the same: visual roots after the first are indented by two characters per level, with a new `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state connecting indented roots to their children. The architectural foundation—a two-slot lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`—was finalized in v8 and is unchanged here. This version focuses solely on fixing a design oversight identified in v8: the lookahead buffer’s logic for detecting visual roots failed when commits were excluded by filters like `--author` or `--grep`, which leave parent pointers intact rather than setting them to NULL.

The fix is straightforward and targeted. The `graph_is_visual_root_candidate()` helper now iterates over each parent and calls `graph_is_interesting()` (which internally uses `get_commit_action()`) to determine if the parent is excluded by the filter. This generalizes the logic to handle any filter that operates via `simplify_commit()`, not just those that nullify parent pointers. The change is minimal—just 10 lines of new code—and preserves all existing behavior for boundary commits, merge parents, and octopus merges. Two new tests in `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` verify the fix works with `--author` and `--grep`.

The series is now complete and addresses all known edge cases. Patches 1-3 are unchanged from v8 (patch 1 is already merged), and patch 4 is a surgical update to the visualization logic. The redundant-indentation edge case (marked NEEDSWORK in v8) remains, but it is documented and deemed acceptable. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood’s final reviews are still pending, but the architectural and correctness issues appear resolved. This version looks ready for `next` unless new edge cases emerge during review.
