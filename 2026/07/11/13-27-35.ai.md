This is the **final, ready-to-merge version (v6)** of a **performance optimization series** for Git’s commit-reach subsystem, targeting `paint_down_to_common()`. The series is **fully reviewed, technically complete, and rebased on `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`** to resolve the last procedural blocker. It delivers **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric merge-base queries (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories) by terminating early when one side of the walk is exhausted.

### Key changes since v5
The **only changes** in v6 are a **rebase on `next`** (which now includes `kk/commit-reach-find-all-fix`) and **minor documentation/comment tweaks**. The rebase ensures the removal of the commit-date fallback (patch 10/10) cleanly supersedes the related regression fix, resolving a merge conflict in patch 7/10 and fixing the `t6600` test failure. **No technical changes** were made to the v5 patches.

### What the series does
1. **Optimization**: Adds early termination to `paint_down_to_common()` when one side’s exclusive commit queue drains, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large one-sided histories. The walk now stops when `(!state->parent1_count || !state->parent2_count) && !state->mb_candidate_count && last_gen < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`.
2. **Instrumentation**: Introduces Trace2 step-count reporting (`trace2_data_intmax`) to measure performance improvements deterministically. Benchmarks show dramatic reductions (e.g., 2.6M-commit monorepo: **3.67s → 5ms** for `merge-base --all` across an import graft).
3. **Cleanup**: Removes the obsolete commit-date ordering fallback (introduced in 2018), ensuring the queue is always generation-ordered. This simplifies termination conditions and fixes edge cases with clock-skewed commits.
4. **Documentation**: Adds `Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`, explaining the algorithm, generation-number regions, termination conditions, and side-exhaustion logic.
5. **Tests**: Extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with edge-case topologies (clock skew, redundant ancestors, mixed finite/INFINITY generations) and deterministic step-count assertions for all commit-graph modes.
