This is the third patch in the **ready-to-merge v6 series** optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for one-sided histories. It adds **edge-case test coverage** to `t6600-test-reach.sh` for the side-exhaustion logic introduced earlier in the series.

The patch exercises four specific scenarios:
- **Self-check**: A commit is both the query target and one of the inputs (e.g., `in_merge_bases_many(A, X)` where `A` is also in `X`).
- **Duplicate inputs**: The `X` list contains repeated commits, testing deduplication and queue behavior.
- **Pending-stale transition**: A diamond topology (`ps-*`) where a commit transitions from `(PARENT1|PARENT2)` to `(PARENT1|PARENT2|STALE)` during the walk, ensuring the STALE propagation doesn’t miss pending candidates.
- **Infinity-both-sides**: A topology entirely outside the commit-graph with non-monotonic dates (`pi-*`), forcing the walk to rely on commit-date ordering (now deterministic after the fallback removal in patch 8/8).

The test setup is meticulous, with detailed ASCII diagrams and commit-date manipulation to create controlled edge cases. These tests are **critical for regression prevention**, as they cover behaviors that the existing 10x10 grid topology in `t6600` does not exercise. The patch is **low-risk** (test-only) and **high-value**, addressing reviewer feedback about missing coverage for the new termination conditions.

No substantive changes were made to the core logic; this is purely test infrastructure. The series remains **fully approved and ready to merge** after the v6 rebase resolved the last procedural blocker.
