This is a documentation patch, part of Junio C Hamano’s six-patch series clarifying contributor guidance. It adds a new section to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` that explicitly tells newcomers what to do when their patch receives no response: wait a week or two, then send a polite “ping” to the thread, optionally CC’ing additional reviewers identified by the `git-contacts` script. The text makes it clear that silence does **not** mean approval, addressing a common point of confusion for first-time contributors. The change is small, self-contained, and uncontroversial; it simply codifies the long-standing practice of pinging overlooked patches.
