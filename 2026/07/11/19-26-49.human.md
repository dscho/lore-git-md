This is the fifth patch in Junio C Hamano’s six-part documentation series clarifying contributor guidance. It updates `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to explicitly state that a maintainer’s "Will queue" reply is simply a promise to add the patch to the `seen` branch—nothing more. The change removes any ambiguity: being queued in `seen` (or receiving a "Will queue" response) does not signal acceptance, approval, or even a commitment to merge. This patch directly addresses a recurring source of confusion for contributors, who sometimes misinterpret "Will queue" as an early sign of success. The update is a small but important clarification, reinforcing the series’ overall goal of making the contribution process more transparent. No controversy is expected, as the patch merely documents existing practice.
