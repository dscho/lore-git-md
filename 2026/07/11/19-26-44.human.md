This is a documentation update from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, aimed at improving contributor guides. The six-patch series addresses gaps and ambiguities in `SubmittingPatches` and `MyFirstContribution` documentation, focusing on practical advice for new contributors navigating the mailing list workflow.

The first patch streamlines the commit log message guidelines in `SubmittingPatches`, cutting wordiness to make the expectations clearer upfront. The second adds guidance for contributors whose patches receive no initial response, a common point of confusion. The third clarifies when to add `Reviewed-by:` and `Acked-by:` trailers, while the fourth and fifth patches emphasize that landing in `seen` is just the start of the integration process—not a sign of acceptance.

The final patch improves the experimental "What's cooking" draft description feature by defining the expected tone rather than just telling contributors to mimic existing entries. The changes are modest in scope (135 insertions, 89 deletions across two files) but directly target recurring pain points for newcomers. Given Junio’s role and the practical nature of the updates, this series is likely to be uncontroversial and well-received.
