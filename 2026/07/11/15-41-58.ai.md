This is a **feature patch series** (v9) for `git log --graph` that implements cascading indentation for visual roots. The email is Pablo Sabater’s reply to Mirko Faina’s **substantive usability objection**—the indentation, while solving a real ambiguity, clutters output for simple cases like sequential one-patch series.

Pablo acknowledges the clutter concern but argues the feature should remain **opt-out** (`--no-graph-indent`) rather than opt-in, since it fixes a genuine ambiguity. He floats two alternatives: (1) a **zig-zag pattern** that alternates indentation direction, or (2) a **depth limit** on cascading. Both have edge cases—zig-zag risks reintroducing ambiguity, and a limit could leave some roots unindented. He explicitly asks for more opinions, signaling the design is now in flux.

The thread is at a **late-stage inflection point**: the series is technically complete (lookahead buffer, edge-case fixes, test coverage) and has Junio Hamano’s architectural approval, but Mirko’s objection has shifted the discussion from correctness to **user experience and backward compatibility**. Pablo’s reply keeps the door open for a redesign—either a new flag, a heuristic, or a zig-zag pattern—while defending the current opt-out default. The next step is likely a maintainer ruling on whether the usability trade-off is acceptable as-is or requires a follow-up patch.
