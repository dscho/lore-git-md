This is the sixth patch in the **v6 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()`** for one-sided histories, a performance-critical part of Git’s merge-base computation. The series is **fully reviewed and ready to merge**, with this patch adding **deterministic test coverage for clock-skew edge cases** that could break the new side-exhaustion optimization.

The patch extends `t6600-test-reach.sh` with two clock-skew topologies (`se-*` and `se2-*`) that expose flaws in commit-date ordering (used in v1 commit-graphs without corrected commit dates). In these topologies, children can have *lower* dates than their parents, violating the topological invariant that children should be dequeued first. The new tests verify that generation-based ordering (now mandatory after the series removes the commit-date fallback) correctly handles these cases, ensuring side-exhaustion fires only when one side’s queue is truly empty. The patch also adds **step-count assertions** to existing edge-case tests, including a new topology mixing finite and `INFINITY` generation numbers, to validate the optimization’s performance across all commit-graph modes.

The changes are **low-risk** (test-only) and **high-value**: they catch regressions in clock-skewed repositories, which are common in import grafts or shallow histories. The step counts provide a baseline for future optimizations and confirm the **100-1000x speedups** claimed by the series. No code changes are made to `commit-reach.c`; this is purely about hardening the test suite.
