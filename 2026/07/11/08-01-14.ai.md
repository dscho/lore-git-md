This is a **substantive review follow-up** in the ODB abstraction series thread. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Taylor Blau’s observation about the incomplete refactoring in patch 3/8, providing historical context and a broader design consideration. He notes that the non-bitmap counterpart to `traverse_bitmap_commit_list()`—`traverse_commit_list()`—also does not propagate callback return values, and in fact its callbacks return `void`, not `int`. Peff suggests that the two traversal functions should remain consistent with each other: either both should allow early termination via non-zero returns, or both should ignore callback return values entirely. This feedback elevates the scope of the discussion from a targeted fix to a design question about Git’s object traversal APIs, with implications for how callers interact with both bitmapped and non-bitmapped object enumeration. The tone is neutral and technical, framing the issue as a matter of internal consistency rather than a correctness bug. No objections to the patch’s core purpose are raised, but the response introduces a new dimension to the review that may require coordination with other parts of the codebase.
