This is the fourth patch in Junio C Hamano’s six-patch documentation series clarifying contributor guidance. It updates `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` to explicitly state that a patch landing in the `seen` branch is not an acceptance signal—it is merely a step for integration testing and wider exposure. The change replaces a terse description of the four integration branches with a more detailed explanation: `seen` is where patches go early, sometimes even before review, and advancing to `next` requires positive reviews and community consensus. The patch also clarifies that the maintainer’s "Will merge to `next`" annotation in the "What's cooking" report is the actual signal of intent to merge, not the patch’s presence in `seen`. This addresses a common point of confusion for newcomers and aligns with the series’ goal of codifying existing practices rather than introducing new ones. The update is uncontroversial and directly targets a recurring pain point in contributor onboarding.
