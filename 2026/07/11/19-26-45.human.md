This is a documentation patch, the first in Junio C Hamano’s six-part series to clarify contributor guidance. It overhauls the section of `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` that explains how to write a commit log message, replacing a long-winded rationale with a concise, three-part template: **Observation** (what’s wrong), **Solution** (why this approach), and **Command** (imperative instruction). The patch also tightens formatting rules—subject-line length, area prefixes, capitalization, and line wrapping—so they are easier to follow. The changes are purely editorial and codify existing practice rather than inventing new rules. If the rest of the series is equally crisp, this looks like a straightforward improvement that will save newcomers (and reviewers) time.
