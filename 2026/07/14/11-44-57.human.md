This is the first patch in the seventeenth and final iteration of Pablo Sabater's GSoC series adding `remote-object-info` support to `git cat-file --batch-command`. It fixes a memory leak in the transport helper subsystem where `data->name` (allocated during transport initialization) was not being freed when disconnecting a transport whose helper had not fully started.

The fix is straightforward: moving `FREE_AND_NULL(data->name)` outside the conditional block ensures the memory is always released during disconnect, matching Git's established resource cleanup patterns. This leak would most likely surface in edge cases where transport initialization fails or is interrupted, making it a subtle but real concern for long-running Git processes. The patch is a targeted, well-motivated bugfix that addresses feedback from earlier versions of the series.
