This is the maintainer’s follow-up to the second patch in Paulius Zaleckas’s v4 series introducing configurable submodule fetch error handling. Junio C Hamano identifies a test-portability issue: one of the new tests uses `grep` directly instead of Git’s `test_grep` helper, which could fail on platforms where `grep` behaves differently. He provides a one-line fixup commit that replaces `grep` with `test_grep` in the test verifying that `--submodule-errors=fail` overrides a `warn` config setting when using `fetch --all`. The fix is mechanical and uncontroversial, addressing a surface-level review nit raised by Ramsay Jones. No further discussion or objections are present in this email.
