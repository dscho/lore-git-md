This thread introduces a 5-patch series by René Scharfe to make Git's tempfile and lockfile APIs repository-aware, eliminating their implicit reliance on the global `the_repository` variable. The changes are mechanical and confined to plumbing, touching 38 files across subsystems like ref backends, commit-graph, and ODB. The series adds new functions like `repo_create_tempfile` and `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, then migrates existing callers to use them, ensuring correct `core.sharedRepository` resolution per repository.

Junio Hamano queued the series in his `seen` branch on July 10, noting no glaring issues but deferring deeper review. Patrick Steinhardt later chimed in to welcome the change, confirming it addresses a practical obstacle in his ODB abstraction work. No substantive objections or requests for changes have been raised, and the series remains under review with no user-visible impact. The work is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort, with future patches expected to convert additional callers.
