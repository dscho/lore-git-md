This thread is about René Scharfe’s 5-patch series (v1) introducing repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs as part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository`. The series is mechanical and plumbing-only, touching 38 files across subsystems like ref backends, commit-graph, and ODB to eliminate implicit reliance on `the_repository`.

The patches add new functions (e.g., `repo_create_tempfile`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`) and migrate callers to use them, ensuring correct `core.sharedRepository` resolution per repository. Junio Hamano queued the series in his `seen` branch on July 10 without requesting changes, while Patrick Steinhardt—who had earlier raised a design question about whether passing the full `struct repository *` was necessary—welcomed the change and provided a minor editorial suggestion in the latest email. That suggestion, to tease the upcoming removal of compatibility wrappers in an earlier commit message, is the only open item, and it’s purely stylistic. No substantive objections or additional reviews have emerged, and the series remains on track for integration.
