This thread is about René Scharfe’s 5-patch series that introduces repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs, a step in the long-running effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The series is mechanical and affects plumbing across 38 files—including apply, builtins, bundle, commit-graph, config, and ref backends—without changing user-visible behavior. The new functions (`repo_create_tempfile`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, etc.) let callers explicitly pass a `struct repository *`, ensuring `core.sharedRepository` is read from the correct repository.

The discussion has been technical but low-drama. Patrick Steinhardt, whose ODB abstraction work benefits from this change, initially questioned whether passing the full repository object was overkill when only the shared-permissions setting was needed, but ultimately accepted the design as sufficient for now. He also proposed a compile-time guard to prevent new callers from reintroducing reliance on `the_repository`, but after Junio Hamano took a neutral stance, Patrick withdrew the request, allowing the series to proceed as-is. René clarified the incremental approach—converting only callers that already hold a non-default repository reference—and Junio queued the series in his `seen` branch on July 10. The only remaining loose end is the medium-term implementation of the compile-time guard, which is now acknowledged as a future task but not a blocker.

The series is ready to graduate from `seen` without further changes. Key adopters include `commit-graph.c`, `midx-write.c`, and the ref backends, and the pattern is expected to expand in later patches. No substantive objections remain, and the mechanical nature of the changes makes this a straightforward step in the `the_repository` removal effort.
