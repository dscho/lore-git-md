This thread is about René Scharfe’s 5-patch series (v1) that introduces repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs, part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The series is mechanical, touching 38 files across subsystems like ref backends, commit-graph, and ODB, with no user-visible changes. Junio Hamano queued it in his `seen` branch on July 10, noting no glaring issues, while Patrick Steinhardt—who had earlier welcomed the change as a practical improvement for his ODB abstraction work—provided substantive feedback on the design and later reviewed the final patch.

The latest development is Patrick’s detailed review of patch 5/5, where he raises two maintainability concerns. First, he asks for a clearer rationale in the commit message about why only certain callers were converted, to avoid confusion about future work. Second, he proposes adding a compile-time guard to the old global functions to prevent accidental reintroduction of `the_repository` dependencies. Neither point blocks the series, but they may lead to a v2 if René incorporates the suggestions. The thread remains focused on plumbing-level refactoring, with no objections to the technical approach so far.
