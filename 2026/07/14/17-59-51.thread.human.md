This thread is about René Scharfe’s 5-patch series (v1) that introduces repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs, part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The series is mechanical and targets plumbing code across 38 files, with no user-visible changes. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, queued it in his `seen` branch on July 10 without requesting changes, and Patrick Steinhardt, who leads the ODB abstraction work, welcomed the change as a practical improvement.

Patrick’s recent review of the first patch raises a design question: whether passing a full `struct repository *` is necessary when only the `core.sharedRepository` setting is used. He suggests an alternative—letting callers pass the configuration value directly—but acknowledges this could lead to duplication. He also notes a minor nit about the wrapper implementation but doesn’t treat it as a blocker. The feedback is forward-looking and doesn’t require changes to the current series, though it hints at potential future refactoring if new callers emerge. No other participants have weighed in on this point, and the series remains under review in `seen`.
