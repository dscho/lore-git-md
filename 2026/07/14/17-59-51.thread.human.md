This thread introduces a 5-patch series by René Scharfe that refactors Git's tempfile and lockfile APIs to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, eliminating their implicit reliance on the global `the_repository`. The changes are part of the ongoing effort to remove `the_repository` and are purely mechanical, touching 38 files across Git's plumbing subsystems (apply, ref backends, commit-graph, midx, ODB, etc.). No user-visible behavior changes are expected.

The series adds new repository-aware variants of existing functions (`repo_create_tempfile`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, etc.) and migrates callers to use them. Junio has reviewed the patches and will queue them in his integration branches (`seen`), noting no obvious issues but leaving room for deeper review. The patches are incremental, focusing on callers that already work with non-default repositories, and no substantive objections have been raised. The series is now on track for integration, though not yet merged. Future patches are expected to extend the pattern to additional lockfile users.
