This thread is about René Scharfe’s 5-patch series to add repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs, part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The series is mechanical, touching 38 files across Git’s plumbing layers—apply, builtins, bundle, commit-graph, config, ODB, ref backends, and more—without changing user-visible behavior. The new functions (`repo_create_tempfile`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, etc.) let callers explicitly pass a `struct repository *`, ensuring correct `core.sharedRepository` resolution in multi-repository contexts.

The series is now queued in Junio’s `seen` branch (as of July 10), with no objections from the maintainer. Patrick Steinhardt, who leads the ODB abstraction work, welcomed the change in principle but raised two substantive points in his review of the final patch: he asked for clearer documentation of why certain callers were converted (those already holding a `struct repository *` reference) and proposed adding a compile-time guard to the old global functions to prevent accidental regressions. René responded by clarifying the selection criteria—only callers already working with non-default repositories were converted—and declined to add the guard, calling it a task for a separate series. He even offered to drop the patch if the guard requirement became a sticking point, though no v2 has been posted yet.

The discussion leaves two loose ends: whether Patrick will push for the compile-time guard in a follow-up, and whether future patches will convert additional callers. For now, the series remains incremental, targeting only the "low-hanging fruit" of callers already holding repository references. No other substantive reviews have surfaced, and the changes are confined to plumbing with no impact on end users.
