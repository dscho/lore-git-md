This thread is about René Scharfe’s five-patch series that introduces repository-aware variants of Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs (`repo_create_tempfile`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, etc.). The goal is to eliminate implicit reliance on the global `the_repository` variable, a long-running effort René has been leading. The series touches 21 files across core subsystems like apply, ref backends, commit-graph, midx, ODB, and config, but the changes are purely mechanical and confined to plumbing—no user-visible behavior changes are expected.

The first four patches lay the groundwork: adding new repo-aware functions, converting early adopters (like `refs/packed-backend.c`), and removing now-unused compatibility wrappers. The fifth and final patch (just posted) converts the bulk of remaining lockfile users—17 files in total—by replacing direct calls to the old global `hold_lock_file_for_update*` family with the new repository-aware variants. Each conversion ensures `core.sharedRepository` is read from the correct repository rather than the global one. The series is incremental, well-structured, and aligns with the project’s broader goal of removing `the_repository`.

No substantive reviews or objections have been raised yet, and the patches are likely uncontroversial given René’s role as the primary driver of this effort. The series is currently under review (v1 posted), with no merge status reported. While future patches may convert additional callers, this series focuses on those that already accept a `struct repository *` parameter.
