This is the final patch (5/5) in René Scharfe's series converting lockfile and tempfile callers to use new repository-aware variants (`repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update_mode`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update_timeout`). The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

The patch mechanically replaces direct calls to the old global `hold_lock_file_for_update*` family with the new repository-aware variants across 17 files: apply, builtins (difftool, gc, history, sparse-checkout), bundle, commit-graph, config, loose object storage, midx, ODB, ref backends (files and packed), repack-midx, repository, and rerere. Each conversion passes the appropriate `struct repository *` parameter to ensure `core.sharedRepository` is read from the correct repository rather than the global `the_repository`. The diff is purely mechanical: 46 insertions, 36 deletions, no new logic, and no behavior changes.

This is the payoff patch of the series: it converts the bulk of the remaining lockfile users that were not covered in earlier patches. The changes are confined to plumbing and are consistent with the pattern established in patches 1–4. The commit message is concise but sufficient, and the patch boundaries are logical. No user-visible impact is expected.

The series as a whole is well-structured, incremental, and aligned with the project's long-term goal of eliminating implicit reliance on `the_repository`. René is the primary driver of this effort, so the patches are likely to be uncontroversial. The only open question is whether additional callers will need conversion in future patches, but this series does not attempt to convert every lockfile user—only those that already accept a `struct repository *` parameter.
