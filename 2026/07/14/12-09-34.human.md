This is the third patch in Pablo Sabater’s **v12 series** implementing visual root indentation for `git log --graph`. It introduces a **two-commit lookahead buffer** in `struct git_graph` to safely peek ahead during revision traversal, a prerequisite for the indentation logic in later patches.

The patch adds 84 lines across `graph.c`, `graph.h`, and `revision.c`, including three new helper functions (`graph_pop_lookahead`, `graph_get_lookahead_room`, `graph_push_lookahead`) and BUG assertions to enforce buffer invariants. The buffer is managed as a FIFO queue, pre-fetching up to two commits ahead when a graph is active. The key change in `revision.c` wires the buffer into `get_revision()`, ensuring commits are pre-fetched via `next_commit_to_show()` (introduced in patch 2/7) and marked as SHOWN before inspection. `graph_is_interesting()` is updated to treat buffered commits as "interesting," preserving correct parent-child relationships.

This is **infrastructure-only**—no user-visible behavior changes—but it resolves the architectural challenge of safely accessing future commits without peeking at `rev_info` internals. The design was approved by Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson in earlier iterations, and the implementation faithfully follows the agreed approach. The patch is **low-risk**, with clear boundaries: it only interacts with `next_commit_to_show()` and excludes the `--reverse` branch to avoid side effects. The BUG assertions and strict buffer management suggest careful attention to edge cases.
