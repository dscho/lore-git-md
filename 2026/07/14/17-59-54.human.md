This is the third patch in René Scharfe’s five-part series extending the `the_repository` removal effort to the lockfile API. It adds repository-aware variants of the core lockfile functions—`repo_hold_lock_file_for_update`, `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update_timeout`, and `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update_mode`—and wires them into the existing internals so that the repository parameter flows down to `adjust_shared_perm` and the tempfile layer.

The patch is mechanical scaffolding: the new functions are thin wrappers that delegate to the existing `lock_file_timeout` helper, now modified to accept a `struct repository *` parameter. No callers are converted yet; that happens in later patches. The diffstat shows +31 lines in `lockfile.h` (declarations) and +22 lines in `lockfile.c` (definitions and plumbing), with only eight lines removed—all of them the old `lock_file` and `lock_file_timeout` signatures.

Because this is part of the long-running `the_repository` removal project, the motivation is well understood: eliminating implicit reliance on the global `the_repository` by pushing repository context outward through the stack. The changes are confined to plumbing and should not affect user-visible behavior. René is the primary driver of this effort, so the patch is consistent with the project’s architectural direction.

No reviews or objections have appeared yet, but the patch looks clean and uncontroversial—exactly the kind of incremental refactoring the project expects from this series.
