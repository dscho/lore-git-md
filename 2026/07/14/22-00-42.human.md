This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` in Git. Jeff King (Peff) chimes in on Junio C Hamano’s review of Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy’s v2 patch, which converts `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab with `uint64_t` storage.

Peff endorses Junio’s critique that the current fixed-width slab design does not fully exploit the slab’s flexibility. He points to an earlier discussion (February 2025) where he proposed adding arbitrary-sized bitset support to commit-slabs, demonstrating how it could handle hundreds of branches without imposing a hard limit. The linked thread includes patches that introduce a bitset abstraction and show its use in a similar context.

This reply does not introduce new technical objections but reinforces Junio’s feedback: the patch’s current design is a missed opportunity to future-proof the code. The ball is now back in the author’s court to either adopt a variable-length slab or justify why the fixed-width approach is sufficient. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, so the patch’s direction remains contested.
