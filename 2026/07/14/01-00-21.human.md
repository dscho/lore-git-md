This is a review comment from Junio on the first patch of Ted Nyman’s two-patch bugfix series for packfile URI download race conditions. Junio acknowledges the correctness of the current approach—using unique tempfiles to prevent corruption—but questions whether it is unnecessarily wasteful. He suggests an alternative design: have the second process detect that another download is already in progress (via `O_CREAT | O_EXCL` on a predictable tempfile path) and wait for it to finish rather than starting a redundant download. The comment is substantive, raising a coordination question that could improve efficiency without sacrificing safety. No objections to the patch as-is, but the door is open for a follow-up iteration if the coordination idea gains traction.
