This is a meta-level follow-up in the ongoing bugfix series for packfile URI download races. Taylor Blau, a co-worker of the original author Ted Nyman, explains the root cause of the message threading issue Junio C Hamano flagged: his personal Mutt-based patch-sending workflow was rewriting `Message-ID` headers when resending patches, breaking the chain for tools like `b4 am`. He details the now-resolved fix (switching from a patched Mutt to `mutt -H` for sending) and shares the historical context of his workflow, including a small patch he carried for years to prevent Mutt from dropping `Message-ID` headers. The email is purely procedural and does not engage with the technical content of the patches themselves. No substantive review or feedback on the race-condition fixes is provided. The threading issue is now resolved, removing the procedural blocker for integration.
