This thread is about a refactoring of `git show-branch` to stop using the shared `object.flags` field for per-commit state, replacing it with a dedicated commit-slab that lifts the previous 27-branch limit to 64 branches. The patch, now in its sixth iteration by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, has addressed all feedback from Junio C Hamano and is technically ready for maintainer sign-off.

The change moves all per-branch reachability bits to a configurable `uint64_t`-based slab while keeping the `UNINTERESTING` bit in `object.flags` for compatibility with revision walking. The latest version restores two critical behaviors in `join_revs`—propagating `UNINTERESTING` to parents and avoiding redundant traversal—that were accidentally dropped in earlier iterations. It also updates documentation and error messages to reflect the new 64-branch limit and adds tests verifying correct operation with 30+ branches, including `--independent` and `--merge-base` modes.

Junio’s feedback on earlier versions surfaced behavioral regressions (notably in `show_independent` and `join_revs`) and minor cleanups, all of which have been addressed. Jeff King endorsed the slab-based approach in principle, and Patrick Steinhardt reminded the author to acknowledge review comments—a norm the author has since followed. With no open questions or objections, the patch is now uncontested and awaiting Junio’s final approval.
