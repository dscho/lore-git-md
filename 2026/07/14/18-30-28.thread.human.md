This thread is about a refactoring patch to `git show-branch` that replaces its use of the shared `object.flags` field with a dedicated commit-slab (`commit_rev_flags`). The patch, now at version 8, removes the previous 27-branch limit entirely by dynamically growing arrays and computing the slab stride at runtime. Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy is the author, taking over from an earlier RFC by Meet Soni.

The patch has been through several iterations, with Junio C Hamano providing detailed feedback on each version. The latest version (v8) addresses Junio’s concerns from v7 by fixing internal inconsistencies: the documentation no longer mentions a 64-branch limit, and the commit message accurately reflects the change. The patch also removes the local `UNINTERESTING` definition and includes `revision.h`, fulfilling a TODO comment from earlier versions. Helper functions encapsulate slab access, preserving the original semantics of `show_independent` and `join_revs`, and new tests verify correct operation with 30+ branches.

Patrick Steinhardt previously raised concerns about the author’s communication style, interpreting some replies as likely AI-generated. Gatla acknowledged this and committed to writing his own responses, resolving the issue without affecting the patch’s technical readiness. The patch is now technically sound, with no remaining open questions, and is ready for further review or integration. It is part of the broader effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` across Git’s codebase.
