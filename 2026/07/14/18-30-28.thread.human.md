This thread is about a refactoring patch that replaces Git’s shared `object.flags` field with a dedicated commit-slab in `git show-branch`. The patch, now in its fifth iteration (v5) by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, lifts the long-standing 27-branch limit to 64 branches by using a configurable 64-bit stride for per-commit state storage. The change is part of a broader effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` across Git’s codebase.

The patch has addressed all prior feedback. Junio C Hamano initially identified a behavioral regression in the independence check (`show_independent()`), which was fixed in v4 by introducing the `has_only_rev_flag_bit()` helper. In v5, Gatla incorporated Junio’s final mechanical cleanups—retaining a TODO comment about future ref-name storage and removing a redundant `#define UNINTERESTING`. Jeff King (Peff) had earlier endorsed the slab-based approach but deferred to Junio’s review for the final sign-off. Patrick Steinhardt’s meta-level reminder about replying to review comments was acknowledged, and the patch is now uncontested and technically ready for merging.

The refactoring touches only `builtin/show-branch.c`, introducing new slab infrastructure (`commit_rev_flags`, `flags_stride`) and helper functions while preserving the `UNINTERESTING` bit in `object.flags` for compatibility with revision walking. Existing tests continue to pass, and the patch reuses prior art from the 2025 variable-length bitset slab series. No open questions remain; the patch awaits Junio’s final approval.
