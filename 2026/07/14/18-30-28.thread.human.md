The patch, now at version 7, is the work of Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy and supersedes earlier iterations by Meet Soni and Gatla himself.

The refactoring is complete and addresses all feedback from Junio C Hamano, the maintainer. Junio’s reviews identified subtle behavioral regressions in earlier versions—such as weakened independence checks and missed parent traversals—which have all been fixed.
