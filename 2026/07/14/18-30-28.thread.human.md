This thread is about a refactoring patch that converts `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab for per-commit state instead of the shared `object.flags` field, eliminating the risk of flag-bit collisions and lifting the previous 27-branch limit. The patch, now in its fifth iteration by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, had previously addressed feedback from Junio C Hamano and appeared technically ready for maintainer sign-off.

However, Junio’s latest review of v5 has uncovered two behavioral regressions in the `join_revs()` function: the slab-based version fails to propagate the `UNINTERESTING` bit to parents and omits logic that avoided redundant traversal. He also pointed out that the documentation and error messages still reflect the old 26-branch limit and that the `%zu` format specifier isn’t portable. These issues mean the patch isn’t yet ready for merging and will require a v6 to address them. The discussion remains focused on correctness and edge cases, with no other participants having weighed in on these new findings. The patch is part of the broader effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` across Git’s codebase.
