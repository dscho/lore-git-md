This thread is about a refactoring patch from Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy that converts `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab instead of the shared `object.flags` field for per-commit state. The change is a clean, mechanical conversion that replaces direct access to `commit->object.flags` with a dedicated `commit_rev_flags` slab and helper functions, eliminating the risk of flag conflicts with other subsystems. The patch touches only `builtin/show-branch.c`, adding 39 lines and removing 23, and is part of the broader effort to remove misuse of `object.flags` across Git.

The patch is well-scoped and clearly motivated, with no intended behavior change. It aligns with Git’s established pattern for per-commit data and appears uncontroversial. So far, there’s been no review feedback, but the patch looks like a solid contribution from a new contributor. If it follows the usual path, it will likely be picked up by Junio once it receives a Reviewed-by or Acked-by from a regular contributor.
