This thread is about a refactoring patch to `git show-branch` that replaces its use of the shared `object.flags` field with a dedicated commit-slab, lifting the previous 27-branch limit to 64. The patch, now in its sixth iteration by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, has been under review by Junio C Hamano, who previously identified and helped fix behavioral regressions in earlier versions.

The latest feedback from Junio raises two new concerns. First, there’s a potential performance regression in the `join_revs()` function, where the slab-based logic might cause redundant queueing and parsing of commits in merge-heavy histories. Second, and more fundamentally, Junio questions why the patch retains a hardcoded 64-branch limit when the underlying infrastructure already supports dynamic stride. He proposes dynamically growing the arrays that currently enforce this limit, which would eliminate the last artificial constraint and align the implementation with Git’s variable-length bitset slab pattern.

The patch is otherwise technically ready for maintainer sign-off, with all prior feedback addressed and no open objections. Gatla will likely need to post a v7 to resolve these new concerns, after which the patch should be in good shape for merging. No other reviewers have weighed in on these latest points.
