This thread is about a refactoring patch to `git show-branch` that replaces its use of the shared `object.flags` field with a commit-slab for per-commit state. The patch, now in its second version by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, lifts the previous 27-branch limit to 62 by widening the slab storage to `uint64_t`. However, Junio C Hamano’s review identified conceptual gaps: the slab’s fixed-width design doesn’t fully exploit its flexibility, and the `UNINTERESTING` bit (used in revision walking) should remain in `object.flags`. Jeff King (Peff) has now weighed in, endorsing Junio’s critique and pointing to a 2025 series that introduced a variable-length bitset slab as prior art. This suggests the patch will need a third version to address these design concerns, likely adopting the bitset approach to avoid imposing a hard branch limit. No other reviewers have engaged, and the patch remains under review with its design direction still contested.
