This thread is about a long-running refactoring effort to eliminate `git show-branch`'s reliance on the shared `object.flags` field, replacing it with a dedicated commit-slab that removes the previous 27-branch limit and aligns with Git’s canonical patterns. The patch, now at version 10, is authored by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy and has been under review since an earlier RFC by Meet Soni.

The core change is technically complete: the v10 patch addresses all prior regressions, including a critical division-by-zero crash caused by an uninitialized `name_slab`, and passes all tests. Junio C Hamano’s review paused briefly over a communication concern—whether Gatla was engaging with feedback in his own words—but that was resolved in a follow-up where Gatla explained the regression’s root cause and committed to mailing list norms. The patch is now waiting on Junio’s next move, though Gatla has since self-reviewed v10 and identified four minor mechanical issues: inconsistent indentation, a mid-block variable declaration, the scoping block style in `join_revs()`, and the test-coverage gap Junio noted. He’s seeking guidance on whether to add a minimal smoke test before sending v11.

The key participants are Junio, who has driven the technical review with detailed feedback across versions, and Gatla, who has addressed each concern methodically. Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff) have also weighed in, with Patrick emphasizing the importance of human communication in the process. The patch touches `builtin/show-branch.c`, removes the branch limit from documentation, and adds new tests for 30+ branches. No major technical questions remain open—just these final cleanups before integration.
