This thread is about a refactoring patch that replaces Git’s shared `object.flags` field with a dedicated commit-slab in `builtin/show-branch.c`, lifting the previous 27-branch limit to 64. The patch, now in its fourth iteration by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy, has addressed all prior technical feedback—most notably a behavioral regression in `show_independent()` that Junio C Hamano identified in v3. The fix restores the exact-bit-set check via a new helper, `has_only_rev_flag_bit()`, and the patch is otherwise uncontested.

The only remaining task before maintainer sign-off is a small cleanup: Junio’s latest review asks the author to remove a redundant local `#define UNINTERESTING` and use the common definition from `<revision.h>`, while preserving a TODO comment about future work. This is a mechanical change with no impact on the patch’s logic. Jeff King (Peff) had previously endorsed the slab-based approach but hasn’t reviewed v4 yet. The patch reuses infrastructure from the 2025 variable-length bitset slab series and is part of the broader effort to eliminate `object.flags` misuse across Git. Once the cleanup is addressed, the patch should be ready for merging.
