This thread is about a refactoring patch to `git show-branch` that replaces its use of the shared `object.flags` field with a dedicated commit-slab, lifting the previous 27-branch limit to 64 and removing the risk of flag collisions. The patch, now at version 4, fixes a behavioral regression in `show_independent()` that Junio C Hamano identified in the previous version by restoring the exact-bit-set check via a new helper function.

The patch itself is uncontested and ready for maintainer sign-off once reviewers confirm the fix. Junio and Jeff King (Peff) have both engaged with earlier versions, with Junio’s feedback now addressed. The most recent email, from Patrick Steinhardt, is a gentle reminder about mailing-list etiquette—asking authors to reply to review comments directly—rather than a technical review. The patch remains under review, with no merge status reported.
