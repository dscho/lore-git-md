This is the final patch in Pablo Sabater’s **v11 series** implementing visual root indentation for `git log --graph`. The patch adds an opt-out mechanism for the new indentation feature via a command-line flag (`--[no-]graph-indent`) and a configuration variable (`log.graphIndent`), defaulting to enabled. Pablo’s reply here **acknowledges and accepts** Chandra Pratap’s **surface-level review**, which caught two typos: "deafault" instead of "default" in the documentation, and "graph.indent" instead of "log.graphIndent" in both the documentation and a test comment. These are trivial fixes and will be incorporated without affecting functionality.

The series as a whole is **technically complete**, with all design decisions resolved and no further changes planned by the author. The opt-out mechanism was the last remaining usability question, and the implementation has been stable since v10. The only remaining step is Junio Hamano’s final review before the series can proceed to `next`. If you follow Git’s graph rendering, this is the last piece of a long-running effort to make skewed merges and visual roots easier to distinguish; the feature looks ready for production use.
