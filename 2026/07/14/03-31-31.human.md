This is a **follow-up architectural discussion** about the recently merged commit-graph regression fix. Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s exploration of whether commit graphs belong at the object database (ODB) level or as a backend-specific property. Taylor agrees with Patrick’s skepticism about treating commit graphs as a universal ODB feature, particularly for distributed or alternative backends, but notes that commit graphs differ from other optimizations like multi-pack-index (MIDX) or reachability bitmaps because generation numbers are a fundamental property of commits, not just an optimization over storage format. He suggests that while the *representation* of commit graphs (e.g., row-oriented layout) may not suit all backends, the *concept* of generation numbers remains useful regardless of storage. The discussion remains exploratory and non-blocking; no immediate code changes are proposed, and the merged bugfix stands. This exchange provides useful context for future ODB abstraction work, particularly around how to handle commit-graph internals in a pluggable backend world.
