This is a lighthearted follow-up in the ongoing bugfix series for packfile URI download races. Taylor Blau, a co-worker of the original author Ted Nyman, explains how a quirk in his personal patch-sending workflow caused the message threading issue Junio C Hamano flagged earlier. The problem stemmed from a years-old local patch to Mutt that prevented it from preserving `Message-ID` headers when resending messages. Taylor shares the patch (now removed) and confirms he has switched to using `mutt -H` to avoid the issue in the future. The technical patches themselves remain unchanged and are still awaiting substantive review; this email is purely about the procedural hiccup and its resolution. The tone is conversational and self-deprecating, with no new technical content or review feedback.
