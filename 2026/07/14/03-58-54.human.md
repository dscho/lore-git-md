This is a **design discussion follow-up** in the ongoing ODB abstraction series. Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to leave the inconsistent object traversal APIs (`traverse_bitmap_commit_list()` and `traverse_commit_list()`) unchanged for now. Taylor agrees with the pragmatic decision to defer a large-scale refactoring but suggests an alternative: instead of adapting `traverse_commit_list()` to propagate non-zero returns (which would require widespread changes), he proposes splitting the callback type used by `for_each_bitmapped_object()` from `show_reachable_fn`. This would allow `for_each_bitmapped_object()` to remain abortable while making `show_reachable_fn` return `void`, aligning `traverse_bitmap_commit_list()` with `traverse_commit_list()` without touching the non-bitmap traversal code. He mentions having a small two-patch follow-up ready to implement this change on top of v3.

The discussion remains collaborative and focused on technical trade-offs. Taylor’s proposal addresses the "foot-gun" identified earlier while minimizing disruption to existing code. This follow-up is likely to be uncontroversial, as it preserves the series’ core goals and avoids the complexity of modifying `traverse_commit_list()`. The tone suggests this is a refinement rather than a blocker, and the series itself remains on track for integration.
