This is a **substantive review** from Jeff King (Peff) on the first patch in Ted Nyman’s series fixing packfile URI download race conditions. Peff clarifies a subtle ownership issue in the patch’s cleanup logic: the `packfile` member (a `FILE *`) is owned by the `tempfile` struct, so explicitly nulling it after `delete_tempfile()` is redundant but harmless. He suggests either dropping the `NULL` assignment entirely (since the struct is about to be freed) or restructuring the conditional to make the ownership clearer, possibly with a comment. The review does not challenge the patch’s core design—unique tempfiles for packfile URIs—but it does highlight a minor correctness nuance that could prevent future confusion. This feedback is narrowly scoped to the patch’s mechanical details and does not reopen the broader design discussion about resumption or coordination.
