This is a minor follow-up to the GSoC v16 series that adds `remote-object-info` support to Git. The author, Pablo Sabater, reports a small but unnecessary `#include "compat/posix.h"` line that was mistakenly added in patch 10/13 of the series. The include was not causing build failures in CI, but it is incorrect and will be removed in the next reroll.

The patch in question implements the core client-side logic for querying object metadata from remote repositories, and this follow-up is purely about cleaning up a stray header inclusion. The change is trivial and does not affect functionality, but it is worth noting for completeness as the series is otherwise ready for merging to `next`.
