This is the **v12 revision** of Pablo Sabater’s feature series adding **visual root indentation to `git log --graph`**, a user-visible enhancement that improves clarity in graph output by indenting commits with excluded parents. The series is now **seven patches** and addresses all prior feedback, including mechanical fixes from the v11 review.

The key change in v12 is a **correction to a conditional check** in `revision.c` (patch 7/7): the test `revs->graph_indent_set > 0` is now `revs->graph_indent_set`, since the field was converted to a single bit in v11. This is a **purely mechanical fix** with no behavior change. The series also fixes two typos in documentation (`deafault` → `default`, `graph.indent` → `log.graphIndent`) and a test comment.

All design decisions are settled: the **lookahead buffer** (patch 3/7) safely determines visual root status, the **four-level wrap** (patch 5/7) avoids symmetry-induced ambiguity, and the **opt-out mechanism** (patch 7/7) allows users to disable indentation via `--[no-]graph-indent` or `log.graphIndent`. The default remains **indented**, and the implementation is backward-compatible. The **GitHub CI run** shows no failures, and the **new test file** (`t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh`) covers edge cases for wrapping, opt-out behavior, and cascading roots.

The series is **technically complete** and ready for final review. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have not yet signed off, but all substantive feedback has been addressed. The only remaining work is reconciliation with Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic and cleanup of `get_commit_action()` side effects, both of which are deferred to follow-up patches. This is a **well-crafted, thoroughly tested feature** that addresses a long-standing usability issue in `git log --graph`. Worth a look for anyone who reviews graph-related changes.
