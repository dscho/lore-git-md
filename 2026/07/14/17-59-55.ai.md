This is the fourth patch in René Scharfe’s five-patch series converting the tempfile and lockfile APIs to be repository-aware as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch removes the now-unused compatibility wrappers `create_tempfile_mode()` and `create_tempfile()` from `tempfile.c` and `tempfile.h` after all callers have been migrated to the new `repo_create_tempfile_mode()` and `repo_create_tempfile()` variants in earlier patches. The diff is purely mechanical: 14 lines removed, no new code added, no behavior changes. The patch is a clean-up step that tidies up the API surface once the transition is complete. No review feedback or objections have been recorded yet; the series remains under review.
