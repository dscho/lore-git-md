This is a follow-up in the ongoing series to make submodule fetch errors configurable. Paulius Zaleckas posts a brief reply to Junio C Hamano’s review of the second patch, confirming the identified issue and announcing a fix in v4.

The problem was that when a user explicitly sets `--submodule-errors=fail` on the command line, child fetches (such as those spawned by `git fetch --all`) would silently revert to the configured default (`warn` or `fail`) instead of respecting the CLI override. This inconsistency could surprise users who expect the command-line setting to take precedence everywhere. Paulius acknowledges the issue, explains why it happens (child processes re-read the repository configuration), and states that v4 fixes it by forwarding the resolved value whenever it was set explicitly, in either direction. He also adds a test to verify the fix.

The tone is matter-of-fact and collaborative; the fix is small and targeted, addressing exactly the edge case Junio raised. No new technical concerns are introduced, and the series remains under review. Readers following the thread can expect v4 to resolve the last known issue before potential merging.
