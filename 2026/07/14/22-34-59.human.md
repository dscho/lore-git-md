This is a gentle nudge in the zsh completion bugfix thread. The patch from Lutz Lengemann (sent June 17) fixes command and argument completion for `git -C <path> <command>`, but discussion stalled after D. Ben Knoble’s substantive review on June 18.

Knoble’s follow-up email simply asks whether the author has made progress, noting that the patch hasn’t yet been picked up. No new technical points are raised; the thread remains in the same state it was two weeks ago: the patch works for the `-C` case but leaves other global options (`--git-dir`, `--work-tree`, `-c`, etc.) broken, and Knoble’s review identified two specific limitations (unused local variables and chained `-C` directory completion) plus a separate but related bug (`git -<tab>` producing malformed output).

If you care about zsh completion, this is a small, well-scoped fix that improves the status quo. The open questions are whether the author will expand it to cover all global options or leave that for a follow-up, and whether anyone will address the `git -<tab>` bug. For now, the patch is still under review and not yet in Junio’s integration branches.
