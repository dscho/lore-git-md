This is the fourth patch in René Scharfe’s five-patch series converting the tempfile and lockfile APIs to be repository-aware as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. It is a clean-up patch that removes the now-unused compatibility wrappers `create_tempfile_mode()` and `create_tempfile()` from `tempfile.c` and `tempfile.h`.

The patch is a straightforward deletion: the wrappers were thin shims that called the new repository-aware variants (`repo_create_tempfile_mode()`, `repo_create_tempfile()`) with `the_repository` as the argument. Since all callers have already been converted to use the repo-aware functions directly, these wrappers are no longer needed. The diff is minimal (14 lines removed) and mechanical, with no behavior change.

This is a typical final step in a refactoring series: the scaffolding introduced earlier is now removed once it is no longer used. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the project’s long-standing goal of eliminating implicit reliance on `the_repository`. No review feedback or objections have been raised so far, and the series as a whole looks well-structured and incremental.
