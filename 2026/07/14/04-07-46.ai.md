This is a reply in the ongoing packfile URI race-condition bugfix series, specifically a follow-up from Taylor Blau to Ted Nyman’s response to Junio C Hamano’s design concern about Patch 1.

Taylor Blau endorses Ted Nyman’s argument that the current approach—using unique temporary files for each packfile URI download—is the correct trade-off for this series. He agrees that reliably distinguishing between an in-progress download and a leftover `.pack.temp` file from a crashed process is not feasible with the existing temporary-file cleanup mechanisms. The reply reinforces the position that the simplicity and robustness of unique tempfiles outweigh the inefficiency of redundant downloads, at least for the immediate bugfix. No new technical objections or alternatives are introduced; the focus remains on deferring coordination logic to a follow-up if needed. The thread is now effectively waiting for Junio’s final verdict on whether the current design is acceptable for merging.
