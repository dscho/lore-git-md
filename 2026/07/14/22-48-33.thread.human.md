This thread is a second iteration (v2) of an 11-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that adds missing error checks to 11 Git code paths flagged by Coverity static analysis. The series hardens Git against silent failures in functions like `curl_easy_duphandle`, `deflateInit`, `repo_parse_commit`, `lseek`, `dup`, `strbuf_getline_lf`, `launch_editor`, `reftable_table_init_ref_iterator`, and `rename` without changing behavior or on-disk formats.

All substantive review feedback has been addressed. Junio C Hamano raised four correctness concerns in v1 (patches 5 and 9, plus additional fixups), and Patrick Steinhardt posed two design questions (patches 10 and 11); all are now resolved by six fixup patches in v2. The series is technically complete and uncontroversial, with patches 6 (`compat/pread.c`) and 7 (`transport-helper.c`) explicitly confirmed as ready for integration. The latest email (patch 8) adds a non-fatal `warning_errno()` for failed `rename()` calls during fast-export marks file finalization, allowing manual recovery without disrupting the push operation. No open questions remain, and the series awaits a maintainer decision.
