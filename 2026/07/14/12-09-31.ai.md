This is the **v12 revision** of Pablo Sabater’s feature series introducing **visual root indentation for `git log --graph`**, a user-visible enhancement that indents commits with excluded parents to clarify ancestry relationships in graph output. The series remains **seven patches** and is now **mechanically finalized** after addressing all feedback from v11.

The **only changes** in v12 are **typo fixes** and a **boolean logic correction** in patch 7/7:
- Fixed two typos in `Documentation/rev-list-options.adoc`: "deafault" → "default" and "graph.indent" → "log.graphIndent".
- Corrected a boolean check in `revision.c` (`revs->graph_indent_set > 0` → `revs->graph_indent_set`), aligning with the bit-packed field introduced in v11.
- Updated a test comment in `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` to clarify the default state ("log.graphIndent unset" instead of "graph.indent true").

**No functional changes** were made; the series is **technically identical** to v11. The **range-diff** confirms this, showing only the mechanical adjustments above. All architectural and usability decisions remain settled:
- The **lookahead buffer** (patch 3/7) safely determines visual root status during revision traversal.
- The **four-level wrap** (patch 5/7) prevents symmetry-induced ambiguity in cascading roots.
- The **opt-out mechanism** (patch 7/7) allows disabling indentation via `--[no-]graph-indent` or `log.graphIndent`, with indentation enabled by default.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion:
- The **lookahead buffer** (`lookahead[2]` in `struct git_graph`) is populated by `next_commit_to_show()` (patch 2/7), ensuring commits are fully simplified before inspection.
- The **visual root detection** logic (patch 4/7) uses the buffer to set the `is_visual_root` flag in `struct git_graph`, with indentation applied in `graph_output_commit_line()`.
- The **four-level wrap** (patch 5/7) resets indentation after four levels, avoiding alignment ambiguity in edge cases (e.g., sequences of orphaned roots).
- The **opt-out mechanism** (patch 7/7) introduces a bit-packed `no_graph_indent` field in `struct rev_info`, controlled by `--[no-]graph-indent` and `log.graphIndent`.

The **redundant-indentation edge case** (marked NEEDSWORK in v8) remains documented but unaddressed. Follow-up work includes reconciliation with Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic and cleanup of `get_commit_action()` side effects. These are **not blockers** for merging.
