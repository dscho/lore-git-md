This is the second patch in René Scharfe’s five-patch series converting the tempfile and lockfile APIs to be repository-aware as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. It touches only `refs/packed-backend.c` and replaces a single call to `create_tempfile()` with `repo_create_tempfile()`, passing the ref store’s base repository (`refs->base.repo`) instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. The change is mechanical and confined to plumbing; it ensures that the `core.sharedRepository` setting is read from the correct repository rather than the global one. No user-visible behavior changes are expected, and the patch is a straightforward step in the broader refactoring.
