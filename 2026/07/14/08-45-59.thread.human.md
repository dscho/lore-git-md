This thread is a small but clean refactoring patch by René Scharfe that removes a redundant `strbuf_reset()` call in `strbuf_getwholeline()` when `HAVE_GETDELIM` is defined. The change is a micro-optimization with no behavioral impact, as the function already handles buffer initialization and reset on both success and error paths. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed and accepted the patch, queuing it in the `next` branch for integration testing—meaning it’s on track to graduate to `master` in the current cycle.

Jeff King (Peff) chimed in with a brief but confirming review, noting that the redundant call was originally added to address a missing reset on error, but that issue was fixed back in 2016 (commit `b70904306f`). His comment reinforces the patch’s correctness without raising new concerns. No objections or open questions remain, and the patch is uncontroversial. This is a standalone cleanup, unrelated to larger efforts like ODB abstraction or `the_repository` removal.
