This is the **final patch in Pablo Sabater’s v11 series** implementing **visual root indentation for `git log --graph`**, and it’s receiving a **surface-level review** from Chandra Pratap. The patch adds an **opt-out mechanism**—both a command-line flag (`--[no-]graph-indent`) and a configuration variable (`log.graphIndent`)—so users who dislike the new indentation can disable it. The default remains indented (`log.graphIndent=true`), preserving the series’ core behavior unless explicitly overridden.

Chandra’s feedback is purely mechanical: two typos in the documentation (`deafault` → `default` and `graph.indent` → `log.graphIndent`). These are **trivial fixes** with no impact on functionality, and Pablo has already acknowledged similar feedback in earlier revisions. The rest of the patch—bit-packed booleans in `struct rev_info`, updated configuration plumbing, and three new tests—remains unchanged and **ready for final review**.

The series as a whole is **technically complete**, with all design decisions resolved (lookahead buffer, four-level wrap, opt-out mechanism) and no known issues. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have not yet signed off on this final patch, but the feedback here is **non-blocking** and will likely be addressed in a follow-up.
