This is a refactoring patch that converts `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab for per-commit state instead of the shared `object.flags` field. The v2 iteration addresses feedback from the maintainer by switching from `unsigned int` to `uint64_t` for the slab element type, lifting the branch limit from 27 to 62. The patch also fixes printf format specifiers and ensures correct 64-bit bit-shifting with `UINT64_C(1)`.

The change is mechanical and confined to `builtin/show-branch.c`, replacing direct `commit->object.flags` access with slab helpers (`get_rev_flags`, `or_rev_flags`). The commit message clearly explains the motivation (avoiding `object.flags` conflicts) and ties the work to a prior RFC from February 2025 that Junio and Jeff King reviewed. No behavior change is intended, and the patch appears to be a clean, well-scoped contribution from a new contributor.

This is part of the ongoing effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` across Git. The v2 update directly responds to maintainer feedback, making the patch more robust and future-proof. It looks like a solid, uncontroversial refactoring that aligns with Git’s canonical patterns.
