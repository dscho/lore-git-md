This is a follow-up in the packfile URI race-condition bugfix series. Ted Nyman responds to Junio C Hamano’s design concern about the efficiency of using unique temporary files for concurrent packfile URI downloads. The exchange clarifies the trade-offs between the current approach (correct and simple, but potentially wasteful) and a more coordinated mechanism (efficient, but complex and riskier).

Ted acknowledges that redundant downloads are wasteful but argues that the unique tempfile approach preserves the existing "download, index, then install" behavior for each process, avoiding the need for cross-process coordination, timeouts, or crash recovery logic. He suggests that optimizing away duplicate transfers could be addressed in a follow-up patch unless Junio considers it essential for the correctness fix. The discussion remains open on whether the simplicity of the current patch is preferable to the efficiency gains of a more sophisticated coordination mechanism.
