Johannes Schindelin explains that ownership of these allocations transfers to the caller at the `xmalloc`/`memcpy` line, where the `ud` struct is copied into a newly allocated `*untracked_` struct. Johannes plans to address this separately, keeping the current series focused on Coverity’s leak reports.
