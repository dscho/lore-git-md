This is the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s **v7 series** implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `` `git log --graph` ``. It introduces the **lookahead buffer** that Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson designed to replace the fragile peek-based approach from v6.

The patch adds a two-slot lookahead buffer to ``struct git_graph`` and wires it into the revision walk. ``get_revision_internal()`` now pre-fetches up to two commits whenever the graph is active and there is room in the buffer, ensuring that commits are fully simplified before the graph layer inspects them. The buffer is managed by three new functions—``graph_pop_lookahead()``, ``graph_get_lookahead_room()``, and ``graph_push_lookahead()``—and ``graph_is_interesting()`` is updated to treat lookahead commits as interesting parents even if they have not yet been shown.

The implementation is small (80 lines) and self-contained: it touches only ``graph.c``, ``graph.h``, and ``revision.c``. The buffer is deterministic—timestamps are tweaked to guarantee stable output—and compatible with Jeff King’s planned removal of ``revs->commits`` and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s ``kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion`` topic.

This patch is the architectural foundation for the visualization logic in patch 3/3. It resolves the core fragility that blocked earlier versions and is ready for final review. The only remaining work is the visualization implementation itself, which is now decoupled from the revision-walker internals.
