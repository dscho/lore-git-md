This is a **bugfix refactoring** in the submodule subsystem, specifically addressing a memory leak in `get_superproject_working_tree()` (submodule.c). The email is Johannes Schindelin’s reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of patch 8/13 in the Coverity-driven cleanup series, confirming that the v2 revision consolidates all cleanup into a single exit path.

The patch plugs a leak of the `cwd` buffer (allocated via `xgetcwd()`) on two early-return paths. In v1, the fix added a new `out:` label to free `cwd` before returning, but Patrick Steinhardt noted a fragility risk: the function already had an earlier `free(cwd)` call, and a future `goto out` after that first `free` would trigger a double-free. Johannes Schindelin acknowledges the concern and confirms that v2 resolves it by redirecting *all* late returns through the `out:` label, which now also releases `sb` and `one_up`. This consolidation additionally closes a latent leak on the `strbuf_realpath(&one_up, "../", 0)` failure path, where `one_up`’s backing buffer was never freed.

The change is purely internal and does not affect user-visible behavior. It is part of a larger 13-patch series targeting Coverity-flagged resource leaks and error-path bugs; the series as a whole has strong maintainer approval (Junio C Hamano stated he will queue it for merging). The v2 revision of this patch is now complete, addressing both the original leak and the maintainability concern raised in review. No new symbols or on-disk format changes are introduced. The fix is mechanical and covered by existing tests.
