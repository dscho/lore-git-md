This is a **bugfix refactoring** in Johannes Schindelin’s ongoing Coverity-driven cleanup series. The email is a reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of patch 8/13, which plugs a memory leak in `get_superproject_working_tree()` by ensuring the `cwd` buffer is freed on early-return paths.

Johannes acknowledges Patrick’s maintainability concern about the fragility of having two separate `free(cwd)` calls in the function. In the upcoming v2, he consolidates all cleanup into a single exit path via the `out:` label, which now also releases the `sb` and `one_up` strbufs. This change not only eliminates the double-free risk but also fixes a latent leak on the `strbuf_realpath(&one_up, "../", 0)` failure path, where `one_up`’s backing buffer could remain allocated. The revised patch is a clear improvement in robustness and correctness, aligning with the series’ goal of thorough error-path cleanup. The series as a whole has strong maintainer approval and is expected to be queued soon.
