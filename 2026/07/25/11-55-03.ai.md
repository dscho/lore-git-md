This is the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s five-patch GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. It prepares the client to parse the "type" field from the server response, even though the server does not yet advertise the capability (that happens in patch 1). The change is confined to `fetch-object-info.c`: it adds a `type_index` variable to track the position of the "type" field in the response, allocates storage for the parsed type in the `object_info_data` array, and updates the BUG message to reflect that both "size" and "type" are now supported. The patch is mechanical and follows the established pattern from the predecessor series.
