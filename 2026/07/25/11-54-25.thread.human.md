This thread is a refactoring effort by Tian Yuchen to migrate two global configuration variables—`pack_size_limit_cfg` and `assume_unchanged`—into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal project. The series is now in its second iteration (v2), having dropped the controversial migration of `minimum_abbrev` and `default_abbrev` after Junio Hamano objected to the design of their repository-aware getters, which unconditionally fell back to `the_repository` when accessed outside the main repository. Tian accepted this feedback, and the current patches avoid such fallbacks entirely, setting a precedent for future work in this area.

The changes are mechanical but touch several subsystems, including pack-objects, index updates, ODB transactions, and sequencer logic. The patches update all direct references to the global variables to use their repository-scoped counterparts, with backward compatibility maintained by accessing values via `the_repository` where necessary. No getter functions are introduced for these variables due to their limited call sites, and the series remains purely infrastructural, with no intended behavior changes. While the patches have not yet received review feedback, they address Junio’s earlier concerns and are positioned as a stepping stone for future libification efforts.

The thread is uncontested so far, but the broader question of how to extend APIs (like those in `merge-ort.c`) to handle repository-scoped configuration without relying on `the_repository` remains open. Future discussion may also explore whether these two variables are the most urgent candidates for migration or if other globals should take priority. The mechanical nature of the changes suggests existing tests should suffice, though edge cases in ODB transactions and `core.ignorestat` behavior might warrant closer attention.
