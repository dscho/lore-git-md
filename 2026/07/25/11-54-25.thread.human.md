This thread is about a small but important step in Git’s long-running effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. Tian Yuchen’s initial v1 series proposed moving four configuration variables (`minimum_abbrev`, `default_abbrev`, `pack_size_limit_cfg`, and `assume_unchanged`) into the `repo_config_values` struct, making them repository-scoped. The goal was to allow code paths that explicitly avoid `the_repository`—like `merge-ort`—to access these settings safely.

Junio objected to the first patch’s design, arguing that the fallback to `the_repository` when `repo != the_repository` was a workaround that obscured a deeper API limitation. Tian has now conceded this point and will drop the migration of `minimum_abbrev` and `default_abbrev` from the series entirely. The next version will focus on the uncontroversial parts: migrating `pack_size_limit_cfg` and `assume_unchanged`, which have limited call sites and no fallback complications. Tian may add other variables to keep the series at a meaningful size, but the architectural question Junio raised remains unresolved for now. The series is still under review, with no decisions or merges yet.
