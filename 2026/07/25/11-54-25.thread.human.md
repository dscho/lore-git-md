This thread is about a three-patch series from Tian Yuchen that migrates four global Git configuration variables (`minimum_abbrev`, `default_abbrev`, `pack_size_limit_cfg`, and `assume_unchanged`) into the `repo_config_values` struct. The goal is to eliminate process-global state as part of the ongoing effort to remove implicit reliance on the global `the_repository` variable. The series is purely infrastructural and doesn’t change behavior.

The patches themselves are straightforward: each moves one or two variables into `repo_config_values`, updates callers, and introduces repository-aware getters where needed. Patches 2 and 3 (migrating `pack_size_limit_cfg` and `assume_unchanged`) appear uncontroversial, but Junio C Hamano has raised a significant architectural concern about patch 1. He questions the design of the new getters for `minimum_abbrev` and `default_abbrev`, which unconditionally fall back to `the_repository` when called with a non-`the_repository` argument. Junio argues this approach merely papers over the real problem—subsystems like `merge-ort` that explicitly avoid `the_repository` but rely on APIs that only support it—and suggests the getters should instead `BUG()` to force a proper fix. His feedback implies the series may need a v2 or at least a deeper discussion about how to handle repository-scoped configuration in subsystems that reject `the_repository`.

No other reviews have been posted yet, so the thread’s next steps hinge on Tian’s response to Junio’s objection. The series is still under review, and no merge status has been indicated.
