This is part two of Tian Yuchen’s three-patch series that migrates four global configuration variables into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

Patch 2/3 moves the global `pack_size_limit_cfg` variable into `repo_config_values`.  The change is minimal: the variable is removed from `environment.h`, added to the `repo_config_values` struct, and initialized in `repo_config_values_init()`.  Callers in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `object-file.c` are updated to read the value from the repository’s config struct instead of the global.  No getter is introduced because the variable has no hard-coded fallback and only two call sites.  The patch is mechanical and does not alter behavior.
