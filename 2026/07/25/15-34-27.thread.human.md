This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s two-patch series refining how `git checkout -m` handles autostash conflicts. The goal is to make the sequencer’s autostash logic report conflicts explicitly rather than silently failing, skip unnecessary autostash retries when no tracked changes exist, and improve the visual separation of autostash conflict advice from the branch-switch confirmation message.

The series is under active review. Junio C Hamano has accepted patch 2/2—the uncontroversial advice-formatting improvement—as-is and signaled intent to queue it, but this is now complicated by Phillip Wood’s new objection to the same patch’s fast-path optimization. Patch 1/2, which introduces the `conflicted` output parameter to report stash-apply failures, remains contested on API design grounds. Phillip now argues that the fast-path check (skipping autostash when no tracked changes exist) may not be worth its overhead, suggesting a deeper refactoring of `unpack_trees()` might be needed instead. The improved advice layout is still widely endorsed, but the rest of the series is unlikely to proceed without revision.

Key participants are Junio, who wants to merge the series once the objections are resolved, and Phillip, who has raised substantive concerns about both the API design and the performance trade-offs of the fast-path optimization. The next step is likely a v2 from Harald addressing these points, possibly by dropping or reworking the fast-path logic. The thread is now at a decision point: either simplify the series, revise the optimization, or pursue a larger refactoring.
