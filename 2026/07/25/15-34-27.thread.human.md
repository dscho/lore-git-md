This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that refines how `git checkout -m` handles autostash conflicts. The goal is to make the sequencer’s autostash logic explicitly report conflicts (rather than silently failing) and to skip unnecessary autostash retries when no tracked local changes exist, while also improving the visual separation of conflict advice from the branch-switch confirmation message.

The series is under review, with patch 2/2 tentatively accepted by Junio C Hamano—though its integration now depends on resolving a design objection to patch 1/2. Phillip Wood, the primary reviewer, has raised two key concerns: the API design in patch 1/2 (using an output parameter to report conflicts) and the performance trade-off of the fast-path optimization in patch 2/2. While Phillip finds the advice-layout improvement in patch 2/2 "very welcome" and uncontroversial, he questions whether the fast-path’s benefits justify its overhead, suggesting a deeper refactoring of `unpack_trees()` might be needed for a cleaner solution. He also proposes a test improvement to validate output more robustly.

The series touches `sequencer.c`, `builtin/checkout.c`, and related files, introducing new parameters (`conflicted`, `allow_autostash`) and adjusting how autostash conflicts are reported and displayed. A new test in `t/t7201-co.sh` covers the fast-path and advice formatting. The open question remains whether patch 1/2 will be revised to address Phillip’s API objection or if the series will proceed with a modified approach. For now, patch 2/2’s fate is tied to resolving this feedback.
