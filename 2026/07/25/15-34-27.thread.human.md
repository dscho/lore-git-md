Harald Nordgren’s v4 patch series (two patches) cleans up the visual clutter in conflict advice messages and aligns exit codes with Git’s conventions—using 1 for conflicts and other non-zero values for errors. The series has been reviewed and accepted by Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood, with all technical and design issues resolved.

Tests in `t/t3903-stash.sh` and `t/t7201-co.sh` now validate the output formatting and exit codes, including edge cases like `stash branch`. Junio’s latest email is a lighthearted note about commit-message verbosity, offering an AI-generated rewrite as a stylistic suggestion but making it clear the original is fine. The series is uncontroversial and ready for merging to `next`.
