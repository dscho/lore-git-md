This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that refines how `git checkout -m` handles autostash conflicts. The goal is to make the sequencer’s autostash logic explicitly report conflicts (rather than failing silently) and to skip the autostash retry loop when no tracked local changes exist, while also improving the clarity of conflict advice messages.

Junio C Hamano has already accepted the second patch as-is and plans to queue it for integration, but the first patch (which touches the sequencer plumbing) is still under review. The series was posted a week ago with no other feedback, so Junio has now reached out to Phillip Wood—cited in the cover letter as the inspiration for this work—to solicit his input. No technical objections or requests for changes have been raised yet, so the main open question is whether Phillip (or others) will weigh in on the first patch’s design or implementation. The series is otherwise ready to proceed once review is complete.
