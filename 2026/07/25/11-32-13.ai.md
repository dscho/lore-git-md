This is part 4 of Harald Nordgren’s **`git branch --delete-merged`** series, now in its 23rd iteration and ready for merging. The patch prepares the existing `delete_branches()` function for the upcoming `--delete-merged` subcommand by adding a new flag, `DELETE_BRANCH_NO_HEAD_FALLBACK`, that prevents falling back to `HEAD` when checking whether a branch is merged into its upstream.

The change is minimal and targeted: it modifies `branch_merged()` to suppress the "gentle reminder" message when the caller explicitly opts out of the `HEAD` fallback, and it updates `delete_branches()` to skip the `HEAD` lookup when the new flag is set. The flag is plumbed through the existing `flags` parameter, preserving the API’s consistency with the earlier patches in the series (which refactored boolean arguments into flags). No behavioral changes are introduced for existing callers; the new flag is only exercised by the `--delete-merged` subcommand in patch 5/7.

The patch touches only `builtin/branch.c` and is purely mechanical, addressing a specific need of the `--delete-merged` workflow: ensuring the command only deletes branches that are merged into their upstream, without considering `HEAD` as a fallback. This aligns with the series’ goal of providing safe, automated branch cleanup with precise control over the upstream selector. The implementation is low-risk, well-tested (as part of the broader series), and directly supports the core functionality introduced in later patches.
