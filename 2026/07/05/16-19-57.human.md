This is a **withdrawal notice** from Junio C Hamano for the `--sstat` alias patch. After Phillip Wood’s substantive review raised concerns about UI clutter and the obscurity of the proposed `--sstat` option, Junio has decided to drop the patch.

The thread opened with Junio proposing `--sstat` as a hidden synonym for `git diff --compact-summary`, motivated by personal workflow convenience. Phillip’s review argued that the alias was cryptic, unnecessary, and could be confused with `--shortstat`, without offering meaningful typing savings over tab-completing `--compact-summary`. Junio’s reply acknowledges the feedback and closes the discussion by withdrawing the patch. No further iterations or alternative proposals are expected.
