This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch v2 series fixing Coverity-identified resource leaks in Git. It plugs a file-descriptor leak in `download_https_uri_to_file()` (in `bundle-uri.c`) that occurs when the `git-remote-https` child process fails: the `child_out` file handle was not closed on the error path. The fix is a one-line change that moves the `fclose(child_out)` call before the `finish_command()` check so that the handle is always released, regardless of whether the child process succeeded. The patch is mechanical, well-scoped, and carries no risk of regression. It is part of a series that Junio has already indicated he will queue for merging.
