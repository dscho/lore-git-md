This is the sixth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series of Coverity-driven leak fixes. It plugs two memory leaks in `read_one_dir()` (in `dir.c`), a function that parses the untracked-cache extension from the index. When the function returns early due to malformed input (either a truncated varint or a missing NUL terminator), the `ud.untracked` and `ud.dirs` arrays allocated earlier are never freed. The fix adds explicit `free()` calls on those two error paths. The patch is mechanical, well-scoped, and carries no risk of regression; it simply ensures that resources allocated before the parse error are released. No new symbols or user-visible behavior changes are introduced. The leak was flagged by Coverity and the fix is straightforward, making this a clear candidate for early-cycle merging.
