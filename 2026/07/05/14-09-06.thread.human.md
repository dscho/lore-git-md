Trevor Gross’s patch series proposing `git rebase -i -x` to append "(cherry picked from commit ...)" trailers during interactive rebases remains under active review, but the thread has shifted decisively toward an alternative design. Peff’s latest review reinforces this direction, agreeing with Phillip that `cherry-pick -i` is technically feasible and would avoid the conceptual inconsistencies of the rebase-centric approach. He notes that `cherry-pick` already uses the sequencer backend and generates a todo file, making an interactive mode a natural extension. While Peff adopts a permissive stance on edge cases (e.g., whether `edit -x` or `fixup` should support trailers), he emphasizes that users can document such modifications in commit messages, leaving the door open for future validation if needed. The discussion has also expanded to include a `--exec` option for `cherry-pick` (analogous to `rebase --exec`), though Peff observes that most post-pick operations can already be handled by existing workflows.
