This is a **substantive review** of Junio C Hamano’s patch introducing `--sstat` as a hidden synonym for `git diff --compact-summary`. Phillip Wood raises a principled objection to the change, arguing that the new alias is cryptic, unnecessary, and could clutter the UI.

He suggests the alias could be confused with `--shortstat` and does not meaningfully reduce typing effort. The critique is framed as a matter of UI clarity and maintainability, not as a technical flaw.

No alternative proposals or technical concerns are raised; the feedback is purely about whether the feature is justified. The patch remains self-contained (no dependencies or follow-ups), and the discussion is now open for others to weigh in on the trade-off between convenience and UI cleanliness.
