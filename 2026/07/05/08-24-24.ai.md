This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series of Coverity-driven leak fixes. It plugs a memory leak in `get_superproject_working_tree()` (in `submodule.c`) where the current working directory (`cwd`) allocated via `xgetcwd()` was not freed on two early-return paths: when the caller is not inside a work tree, and when `strbuf_realpath(&one_up, "../", 0)` fails. The fix redirects both early returns through a new `out:` label that frees `cwd`, `sb.buf`, and `one_up.buf` before returning. The patch is mechanical, well-scoped, and carries no user-visible behavior changes. It is part of a larger, maintainer-approved series that Junio C Hamano has stated he will queue for merging.
