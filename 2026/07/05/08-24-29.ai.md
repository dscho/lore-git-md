This is the final patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch v2 series fixing Coverity-identified resource leaks and error-path bugs in Git. It targets Windows-specific code in `compat/mingw.c` and `compat/win32/exit-process.h`, addressing a handle-leak and a latent use-after-free in the process termination logic.

The patch enforces a clear ownership rule: `exit_process()` and `terminate_process_tree()` now unconditionally own the process handle passed to them and close it on every return path. This plugs the leak in the early-return branch where `GetExitCodeProcess()` fails or reports the process is still active, and it removes the redundant (and dangerous) `CloseHandle(h)` call in `mingw_kill()` that Coverity flagged as a use-after-free (CID 1437238). The change is minimal—one line added in the header, three lines removed from `mingw.c`—and mechanically sound.

The fix is Windows-only and has no effect on other platforms. It carries no user-visible behavior change and is covered by existing tests. With the rest of the series already carrying Reviewed-by tags from Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King, Eric Sunshine, and Junio C Hamano, and Junio’s explicit statement that he will queue the series, this patch is effectively pre-approved and ready for merging.
