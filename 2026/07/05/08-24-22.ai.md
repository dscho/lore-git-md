This is the fifth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series of Coverity-driven leak fixes. It targets the line-log subsystem (`line-log.c`), specifically the `line_log_process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()` function.

The patch removes a redundant intermediate copy of `struct line_log_data` that was leaking whenever `bloom_filter_check()` determined a commit did not touch any tracked paths. The caller was making a copy (`line_log_data_copy(range)`) and passing it to `add_line_range()`, but `add_line_range()` always makes its own internal copy, so the caller’s copy was never freed. The fix simply passes the original `range` directly to `add_line_range()`, letting the callee handle ownership. The change is a single-line deletion in `line-log.c` (line 1144), replacing the redundant copy with the original pointer.

The commit message is clear and concise, explaining both the leak and the fix. No new symbols are introduced, and the on-disk format is unaffected. The patch carries no Reviewed-by tag yet, but the series as a whole has strong maintainer approval (Junio will queue it), and the fix is mechanical and low-risk. This is a textbook example of a Coverity-flagged leak: small, well-scoped, and unlikely to introduce regressions.
