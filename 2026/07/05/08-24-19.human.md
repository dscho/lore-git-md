This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch v2 series fixing Coverity-identified resource leaks and error-path bugs in Git. The series as a whole has strong maintainer approval and is slated for merging.

This patch plugs a latent bug in `loose.c`’s `write_one_object()` function. When `open()` fails, the code jumps to the `errout` label and unconditionally calls `close(fd)`, but `fd` is still -1. While `close(-1)` is harmless on most platforms, it is technically undefined behavior per POSIX and can confuse sanitizers. The fix adds a simple `fd >= 0` guard before the `close()` call. The change is minimal (two lines added, one removed) and purely defensive. It carries a Reviewed-by from Patrick Steinhardt and is uncontroversial.
