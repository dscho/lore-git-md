This is a review of Junio C Hamano’s patch introducing `--sstat` as a hidden synonym for `git diff --compact-summary`. The reviewer, Phillip Wood, pushes back on the change, arguing that the new alias is cryptic and unnecessary.

Phillip acknowledges the usefulness of `--compact-summary` (as demonstrated in Junio’s example) but questions the value of adding a shorter alias. He points out that `--compact-summary` is already easy to tab-complete after typing `--comp`, so the new option doesn’t save much typing. More importantly, he finds `--sstat` obscure and potentially confusing—it could plausibly be mistaken for an alias of `--shortstat` rather than `--compact-summary`. His concern is that the alias clutters the UI without clear benefit.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused: it doesn’t engage with the implementation (which is trivial) or test coverage (which is adequate), but instead critiques the design choice of adding a short, hidden option. This is a matter of taste and project conventions rather than technical correctness, so the discussion may hinge on whether others share Phillip’s view that the alias is more confusing than helpful. The patch is small and self-contained, but the feedback suggests it may not gain traction unless Junio can articulate a stronger rationale for the alias.
