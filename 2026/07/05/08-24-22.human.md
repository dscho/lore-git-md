This is the fifth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series of Coverity-driven leak fixes. It plugs a memory leak in the line-log subsystem (`line-log.c`) where `line_log_process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()` made a redundant copy of `range` before passing it to `add_line_range()`, which always makes its own copy internally. The intermediate copy was never freed, leaking memory every time the Bloom-filter short-circuit path was taken.

The fix is a one-line change: pass `range` directly to `add_line_range()` instead of the redundant copy. The commit message explains the ownership semantics clearly and confirms that `add_line_range()` already handles its own copy. No behavior change, just a leak plugged. The patch is small, well-explained, and carries no risk of regression. It’s part of a series that Junio has already marked as “looking good” and ready to queue.
