This is patch 8/12 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series **"Make ODB optimizations pluggable"**, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch unifies Git’s two ODB optimization strategies—incremental repacking (used by `git gc`) and geometric repacking (used by `git maintenance`)—under a single entry point, `odb_optimize()`, by introducing a new `enum odb_optimize_strategy` (`ODB_OPTIMIZE_INCREMENTAL` or `ODB_OPTIMIZE_GEOMETRIC`) and moving the geometric repack logic into the `odb_optimize()` function.

The patch is a mechanical refactoring with no intended behavioral changes. It replaces the standalone `maintenance_task_geometric_repack()` function with a call to `odb_optimize()`, passing `ODB_OPTIMIZE_GEOMETRIC` as the strategy. The geometric repack logic is now implemented as a case in the `odb_optimize()` switch statement, alongside the existing incremental repack logic. The patch also updates the prune logic to skip pruning when using the geometric strategy (since geometric repacking uses cruft packs), and adjusts the `maintenance_task_odb()` helper to pass `ODB_OPTIMIZE_INCREMENTAL` as the default strategy.

Tests in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` are updated to expect `-q` instead of `--quiet` in `git repack` invocations, reflecting the change in how the `odb_optimize()` function constructs its command-line arguments. The patch is a prerequisite for the final relocation of ODB optimization logic into the ODB source backend (patch 12), and is uncontroversial—it preserves all existing logic (geometric split factors, cruft pack handling, multi-pack-index writes, auto-condition checks) while consolidating the two strategies under a single interface. No objections or design concerns have been raised in prior discussion.
