This is a **substantive architectural discussion** in the follow-up to the recently merged commit-graph regression fix. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau’s endorsement of Kristofer Karlsson’s proposal to move the `topo_levels` slab from `struct commit_graph` to `struct object_database`. Patrick confirms that he has explored this idea before but found it complicated due to the commit-slab API’s current global scope and its reuse across multiple subsystems. He raises a deeper question: whether commit graphs are inherently a property of the object database or of a specific backend, noting that distributed or alternative backends might not benefit from the current monolithic, filesystem-based design. His reply outlines two long-term architectural paths—making commit graphs pluggable as a standalone mechanism or treating them as a backend property—but does not commit to either. The discussion remains exploratory and non-blocking; no immediate code changes are proposed, and the merged bugfix stands. Key technical context for future follow-ups includes the ODB abstraction effort, the commit-slab API’s global treatment, and the potential need to detangle commit-graph internals to support pluggable backends.
