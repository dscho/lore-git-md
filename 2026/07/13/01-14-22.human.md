This is a follow-up to Taylor Blau’s four-patch series introducing the `no-ref-delta` capability for Git’s pack protocol. The series itself is already on the list (starting at the link Taylor provides), but the cover letter’s Message-ID was mangled, so the patches appear as a separate thread instead of replies to the cover.

The substance of the series is unchanged: it adds a `--no-ref-delta` option to `git pack-objects` and a matching protocol capability, allowing receivers to opt out of `REF_DELTA` entries in incoming packs. The implementation is clean, the commit messages explain the delta-reuse edge cases, and the test coverage looks thorough. Nothing here alters the earlier assessment—this is a well-scoped feature that should be uncontroversial. The only action for readers is to follow the link to the actual patches if they want to review or comment.
