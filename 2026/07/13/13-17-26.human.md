This is the ninth patch in Phillip Wood’s 10-patch series fixing the sequencer’s incorrect copying of notes from dropped commits during rebase. The patch introduces `enum pick_result` to replace the integer return value of `do_pick_commit()`, making the code’s intent clearer and paving the way for the final patch’s behavioral change.

The enum defines four states: `PICK_RESULT_ERROR` (-1), `PICK_RESULT_OK` (0), `PICK_RESULT_CONFLICTS` (1), and—reserved for the next patch—`PICK_RESULT_DROPPED`. The patch converts `do_pick_commit()` to return the enum and updates its callers (`pick_one_commit()` and `single_pick()`) to handle the new type. The conversion is mechanical and preserves existing behavior; the only functional change is replacing the trailing `return res` in `do_pick_commit()` with a `BUG()` to catch any unhandled enum values.

This is a **pure refactoring** with no user-visible impact. The patch is small (45 insertions, 16 deletions), touches only `sequencer.c`, and is well-motivated by the need to distinguish dropped commits in the next patch. Oswald Buddenhagen’s suggestion to widen the enum’s scope further was rejected as unnecessary churn, and Phillip’s follow-up replies confirm the incremental approach is intentional. The patch looks clean and ready for review.
