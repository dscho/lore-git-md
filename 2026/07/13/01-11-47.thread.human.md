This thread introduces a new `no-ref-delta` capability for Git’s pack protocol, allowing receivers to instruct senders to omit `REF_DELTA` entries from generated packs. The feature, authored by Taylor Blau, is currently under review in its first version (v1). It adds a `--no-ref-delta` option to `git pack-objects`, integrates the capability into the protocol via `send-pack` and `receive-pack`, and includes a test helper to inspect delta representations. The implementation avoids on-disk format changes and is opt-in through protocol negotiation.

So far, the series has not received substantive review feedback, though the author has flagged a minor housekeeping issue: a malformed Message-ID in the cover letter caused the patches to appear as a separate thread, making them harder to follow. Taylor provided a direct link to the series on lore.kernel.org to help reviewers navigate the patches. The issue does not affect the technical content of the series, which remains unchanged. The key open question is whether reviewers will raise concerns about edge cases in delta reuse, particularly around bitmap pack reuse safety.
