This is a refactoring patch in Pablo Sabater’s **`git log --graph` visual root indentation series (v10)**. It is the sixth of seven patches and **prepares for future graph-related configuration options** by extracting the existing `log.graphcolors` configuration reading logic from `graph_init()` into a new helper function, `graph_read_config()`.

The change is **purely internal**: no user-visible behavior is altered. The patch moves the `repo_config_get_string()` call for `log.graphcolors` out of `graph_init()` and into `graph_read_config()`, which is then called by `graph_init()`. This simplifies `graph_init()` and establishes a dedicated function for reading graph-related configuration, making it easier to add new options (such as the `log.graphIndent` option introduced in patch 7/7) without cluttering the initialization logic.

The diff is small (9 lines added, 4 removed) and touches only `graph.c`. The new `graph_read_config()` function takes a `struct rev_info *` parameter, mirroring the signature of `graph_init()`, and encapsulates all configuration-related setup. The patch is **mechanically straightforward** and does not introduce any new edge cases or dependencies.

This patch is **ready for review** and has no known issues. It is a **clean preparatory step** for the opt-out mechanism implemented in the next patch (7/7). The series as a whole is **technically complete** and awaiting final review from Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood.
