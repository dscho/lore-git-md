This is the second iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series making Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, aiming to let different backends (like reftable or custom storage) implement their own housekeeping logic without hardcoding it in `builtin/gc.c`.

The v2 update is minor: the only substantive change is in the test suite (`t/t7900-maintenance.sh`), where Patrick replaced brittle checks for exact command invocations with a more robust helper (`test_maintenance_tasks`) that verifies which tasks ran rather than how they were executed. This addresses Junio’s feedback from v1 about test fragility while keeping the same coverage. The rest of the series is unchanged—patches 2 through 12 are identical to v1, preserving the refactoring of the "pre-auto-gc" hook, the reordering of maintenance tasks, the extraction of ODB optimizations into a modular system, and the final relocation of optimization logic into the "files" ODB source backend.

The series remains well-structured and focused. It shrinks `builtin/gc.c` by over 500 lines while adding a comparable amount of code to `odb/source-files.c`, introducing new headers (`odb.h`, `odb/source.h`, `odb/source-files.h`) and a pluggable interface for backend-specific optimizations. The commit messages are clear, and the test updates in v2 show responsiveness to review feedback. No objections to the design have been raised, and Junio’s earlier reviews of patches 1–3 (now renumbered in v2) were positive, with only minor test-related points to address. This looks like a solid step toward modular ODB backends and is likely to proceed smoothly.
