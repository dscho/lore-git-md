This is the **v11 revision** of Pablo Sabater’s feature series introducing **visual root indentation for `git log --graph`**. The series is now **seven patches long** and addresses all prior feedback, including a mechanical improvement to pack boolean fields into bitfields in `struct rev_info`.

The core idea remains unchanged: when `git log --graph` renders commits with excluded parents (visual roots), it now indents them to prevent false adjacency with unrelated commits. The indentation cascades for consecutive visual roots, wraps after four levels to avoid ambiguity, and can be disabled via `--no-graph-indent` or `log.graphIndent=false`. The lookahead buffer introduced in earlier versions ensures the graph renderer can safely determine visual root status before rendering.

The only change in v11 is in **patch 7/7**, where the two boolean fields in `struct rev_info` (`no_graph_indent` and `graph_indent_set`) are now packed into single bits, addressing Mirko Faina’s feedback from v10. The rest of the series is identical to v10, which was already **technically complete** and had resolved all design questions (four-level wrap, opt-out mechanism, configuration handling). The **GitHub CI run** shows no failures, and the **test coverage** remains thorough, including edge cases for wrapping, opt-out behavior, and filtering options.

This version is **ready for final review** and likely **ready for `next`** once Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood sign off. The only deferred work is the **redundant-indentation edge case** (marked NEEDSWORK in v8) and reconciliation with Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic. The series is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and addresses a real usability issue in graph rendering. If you care about `git log --graph` output, this is worth reviewing.
