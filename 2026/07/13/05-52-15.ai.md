This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series **"Make ODB optimizations pluggable"**, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch relocates the `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` functions from `builtin/gc.c` into the "files" ODB source backend (`odb/source-files.c`) and exposes them via new vtable callbacks in the ODB source interface. This completes the series’ goal of making object housekeeping (repacking, geometric repacking, auto-gc) pluggable, allowing future ODB backends to implement their own optimization strategies.

The patch is a mechanical relocation of 466 lines from `builtin/gc.c` to `odb/source-files.c`, with minimal changes to adapt the code to its new context. Key adjustments include:
- Replacing direct accesses to `the_repository` with the repository pointer stored in the ODB source (`source->odb->repo`).
- Updating configuration reads to use the repository pointer (`repo_config_get_*()` instead of `repo_config_get_*()` with `the_repository`).
- Moving the `add_repack_all_option()` helper into `odb/source-files.c` and updating its signature to accept a `struct repository *` parameter.
- Adding the `optimize` and `optimize_required` callbacks to the `struct odb_source` vtable and implementing them in the "files" backend.

The patch introduces no behavioral changes: all existing logic for incremental/geometric repacking, auto-condition checks, cruft pack handling, multi-pack-index writes, promisor object exclusions, and memory estimation is preserved. The `enum odb_optimize_strategy` and `enum odb_optimize_flags` types, along with `struct odb_optimize_options`, are moved from `builtin/gc.c` to `odb.h` to make them available to other backends. The patch also adds thin wrappers in `odb.c` (`odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()`) to delegate to the ODB source’s vtable.

Files touched:
- `builtin/gc.c`: Removes the relocated functions and their helpers, reducing the file by 466 lines.
- `odb.c`: Adds thin wrappers for `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()`.
- `odb.h`: Moves the `odb_optimize_*` types and declares the new ODB-level functions.
- `odb/source-files.c`: Adds the relocated functions and their helpers (470 lines), along with the vtable callbacks.
- `odb/source-files.h`: Declares the new backend-specific functions.
- `odb/source.h`: Adds the `optimize` and `optimize_required` vtable callbacks.

New symbols:
- `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` (ODB-level wrappers in `odb.c`).
- `odb_source_files_optimize()` and `odb_source_files_optimize_required()` (backend-specific implementations in `odb/source-files.c`).
- `optimize` and `optimize_required` vtable callbacks in `struct odb_source`.

Removed symbols:
- The original `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` functions from `builtin/gc.c`, along with their helpers (`too_many_loose_objects()`, `find_base_packs()`, `too_many_packs()`, `estimate_repack_memory()`, `add_repack_all_option()`, `add_repack_incremental_option()`).

The patch is the culmination of the series’ refactorings (patches 4–11) and is uncontroversial, as it merely relocates existing logic to enable pluggability. Reviewers may focus on verifying that the relocated code compiles cleanly and that the vtable delegation works as intended, but no behavioral regressions are expected. The series is now complete and ready for integration, pending review of this final patch.
