This is the fifth patch in Pablo Sabater’s **v10 series** implementing **visual root indentation for `git log --graph`**. The patch introduces **four-level wrapping** for cascading visual roots, addressing a usability issue where long sequences of single-parent commits (e.g., from `--author` filters) could create excessive horizontal indentation.

The change modifies `graph.c` to reset indentation after four levels, preventing "pyramid" effects while maintaining clarity. The logic handles two cases: unambiguous wrapping (where commits clearly reset to the initial column) and ambiguous cases (where a non-visual-root commit might align vertically with a wrapped commit, requiring forced indentation). The magic number "4" was chosen as the smallest value that balances visual distinction and compactness.

The patch includes a new test in `t/t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` that verifies the wrapping behavior across ten commits, including the forced-indentation edge case. This is a **usability refinement** to the core visualization logic introduced in patch 4/7, and it resolves the last major design question from earlier revisions.

The implementation is clean and well-tested, with no known issues. This patch, along with the rest of the series, is **ready for final review** and likely **ready for `next`** once Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood sign off. The series as a whole is technically complete and addresses all prior feedback.
