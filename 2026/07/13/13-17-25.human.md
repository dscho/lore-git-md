This is the eighth patch in Phillip Wood’s 10-patch series fixing how `git rebase` handles dropped commits. The patch simplifies the control flow in `pick_one_commit()` by returning early when the operation is not part of an interactive rebase (`!is_rebase_i(opts)`). This eliminates redundant calls to `is_rebase_i()` and makes the function’s logic clearer by chaining mutually exclusive conditions with `else if`.

The change is a **pure refactoring** with no behavior change. It prepares the codebase for the next patch, which introduces `enum pick_result` to replace the integer return value from `do_pick_commit()`. The commit message explains that the redundant `res` checks in the `else if` branches are left intentionally to preserve clarity during the phased introduction of the enum, and that these will be addressed in the next patch.

The patch touches only `sequencer.c` and is small (11 insertions, 8 deletions). It is part of a well-structured series that has already addressed Junio’s earlier concerns about edge cases and test coverage. The series is queued in Junio’s tree and looks ready to land after Phillip squashes the queued typofixes. This patch itself is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the sequencer’s control flow.
