This thread is a bugfix series from Ted Nyman addressing race conditions in Git's HTTP transport when multiple processes concurrently fetch the same packfile URI. The series, now in its initial version, targets two specific issues: corruption of temporary pack files and failed resume requests at EOF. Patch 1 modifies the HTTP layer to use unique temporary files for each direct packfile URI download, while Patch 2 handles a race condition with `.keep` files by making `fetch-pack` more tolerant of concurrent processes.

The discussion has centered on a design trade-off raised by Junio C Hamano: whether the simplicity of unique tempfiles (which prevent corruption but may lead to redundant downloads) is preferable to a more complex coordination mechanism that could avoid those redundant requests. Ted has responded by defending the current approach, arguing that the unique tempfile design preserves the existing workflow and avoids the complexity of cross-process coordination, which he suggests should be deferred to a follow-up. The thread is now awaiting Junio’s reaction to this rationale, with no code changes proposed yet. The series remains under review, with no merge status indicated.
