This thread is about a bugfix series from Ted Nyman that addresses race conditions in Git's HTTP transport when multiple processes fetch the same packfile URI or dumb HTTP pack. The series, now at version 3, was previously considered ready for review after addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano, Taylor Blau, and Jeff King (Peff). It uses deterministic staging paths and per-descriptor offsets to prevent corruption while preserving resumable downloads, and includes new regression tests.

Junio recently merged the series into the `seen` integration branch, but this triggered a test hang in `t5550-http-fetch-dumb.sh`. The hang only occurs when the series is present, and CI runs without it complete successfully. Junio has asked Ted to investigate the cause, though no specific test case or logs were provided. The series remains in `seen` but is now blocked from progressing to `next` until the hang is resolved. The investigation will likely focus on whether the issue is a deadlock, infinite loop, or resource leak introduced by the new code.
