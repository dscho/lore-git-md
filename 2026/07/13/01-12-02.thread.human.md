This thread is about a four-patch series from Taylor Blau refining how `git pack-objects` handles delta reuse when the `--no-ref-delta` option is used. The series addresses an earlier simplification that disabled all delta and bitmap reuse in this mode, which was too restrictive.

The third patch in the series (the one just summarized) teaches `pack-objects` to keep delta reuse enabled when `--delta-base-offset` is also specified, even with `--no-ref-delta`. The key idea is that reused deltas whose bases appear earlier in the output can be encoded as `OFS_DELTA` instead of `REF_DELTA`, so the restriction no longer applies. Preferred bases and external thin-pack bases still require `REF_DELTA` encoding and remain disabled. The patch also updates bitmap reuse to inspect each candidate object individually, skipping `REF_DELTA` entries when `--no-ref-delta` is in effect.

The changes are narrowly scoped to `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `pack-bitmap.c`, with tests in `t/t5300-pack-object.sh` and `t/t5332-multi-pack-reuse.sh` verifying the behavior. The patch is well-motivated and includes thorough test coverage, making it a clean incremental improvement to the pack-reuse machinery. There’s no indication yet of reviews or maintainer decisions, so the series is still under discussion.
