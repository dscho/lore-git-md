This is the fifth patch in Phillip Wood’s 10-patch v2 series addressing how `git rebase` handles dropped commits, specifically focusing on sequencer cleanup. Since `error_with_patch()` either returns `res` on success or `-1` on failure, the OR operation has no effect and can be safely removed.
