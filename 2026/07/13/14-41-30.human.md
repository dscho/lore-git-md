This is a mechanical refactoring patch in the ODB abstraction series. It drops the `_1` suffix from the internal bitmap-opening functions `open_pack_bitmap_1()` and `open_midx_bitmap_1()`, renaming them to `open_pack_bitmap()` and `open_midx_bitmap()` respectively. The change follows the previous patch (5/9), which consolidated the original `open_pack_bitmap()` and `open_midx_bitmap()` into a new `open_bitmap_for_source()` function, leaving the `_1`-suffixed variants as the only remaining callers.

The patch is uncontroversial and purely cosmetic. It touches only `pack-bitmap.c`, updating seven call sites to match the new function names. No behavior changes are introduced; the patch simply cleans up naming after the earlier refactoring. This is the sixth of nine patches in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series, which overall aims to enable ODB-mediated object filtering and remove backend-specific logic from `git cat-file`. The series is under active review and has incorporated all substantive feedback from earlier iterations.
