This is the third patch in Tian Yuchen’s v11 series migrating global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. This patch moves the `editor_program` global variable into the repository-scoped configuration structure.

The patch is a straightforward refactoring: it removes the global `editor_program` declaration from `environment.h`, adds the field to `struct repo_config_values`, initializes it to `NULL` in `repo_config_values_init()`, and frees it in the destructor `repo_config_values_clear()`. The only caller (`git_editor()` in `editor.c`) is updated to access the field via `repo_config_values(the_repository)->editor_program` instead of the global variable. Memory management is handled correctly: `FREE_AND_NULL()` is used before reassigning the field in `git_default_core_config()`, and the destructor ensures cleanup.

The commit message explains the rationale for migrating program paths (per-repo toolchain preferences and elimination of mutable global state) and notes that no getter function is introduced, as direct field access is sufficient. The patch is technically sound, with no user-visible behavior changes, and aligns with the series’ goal of centralizing configuration management. It builds on the infrastructure introduced in patches 1–2 (destructor and `excludes_file` migration) and prepares for subsequent patches migrating pager and askpass programs. No open questions or loose ends are introduced here.
