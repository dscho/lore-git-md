This is the fourth iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s refactoring series to move all `libgit.a` sources into a new `lib/` directory. The patch remains a pure mechanical reorganization—no code changes, just file moves—updating build systems (Makefile, meson, CMake), CI workflows, and `.gitmodules` to reflect the new paths.

Version 4 incorporates Ramsay Jones’s Windows compatibility fix and otherwise carries forward the same trade-offs: the reorganization reduces root-directory clutter (now framed as improving discoverability of *non-library* files) but disrupts `git log --follow`, complicates rebasing for in-flight topics, and risks merge conflicts for downstream forks. The series is still RFC, signaling openness to rejection or alternative approaches.

The discussion has settled on two key points: (1) whether the structural benefits (enforcing coding conventions like avoiding `the_repository` or `die()` in library code) justify the ongoing friction, and (2) whether tooling improvements (e.g., Kaartic Sivaraam’s proposed `git show` hint for renamed files) could mitigate the pain. Junio and Phillip Wood remain skeptical about the cost-benefit balance, while Patrick emphasizes the long-term organizational clarity. No new objections or endorsements have emerged in this iteration—it’s largely a rebase with a minor fix.
