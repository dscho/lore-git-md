This is the third patch in a four-patch series from Taylor Blau that refines how `git pack-objects` handles delta reuse when the `--no-ref-delta` option is given. The series as a whole relaxes an earlier, overly conservative restriction that disabled all delta and bitmap reuse in this mode.

The key insight here is that `--no-ref-delta` does not require disabling reuse entirely. When `--delta-base-offset` is also given, deltas whose bases appear earlier in the output pack can be rewritten from `REF_DELTA` to `OFS_DELTA` and reused safely. The patch updates both the general delta-reuse logic in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and the bitmap-driven reuse paths in `pack-bitmap.c` to inspect each candidate object and skip any `REF_DELTA` entries when `--no-ref-delta` is in effect. It also disables the fast “whole-word” reuse optimization for the preferred or single pack, since that optimization assumes all deltas in the range can be reused verbatim.

The tests in `t5300` and `t5332` verify that reused deltas are correctly rewritten as `OFS_DELTA` and that the bitmap-reuse machinery still works when `--no-ref-delta` is given. The patch is narrowly focused on the reuse machinery and does not change the on-disk format or user-visible behavior beyond allowing reuse where it was previously forbidden.
