This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-part series **"Make ODB optimizations pluggable"**, completing the relocation of Git’s object housekeeping logic from `builtin/gc.c` into the "files" ODB backend. The patch moves the `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` functions into `odb/source-files.c` and exposes them via new vtable callbacks in the ODB source interface, allowing future backends (e.g., reftable) to implement their own optimization strategies.

The patch is a mechanical refactoring with no intended behavioral changes. It removes 489 lines from `builtin/gc.c` and adds 470 lines to `odb/source-files.c`, preserving all existing logic for repacking, pruning, geometric repacking, auto-condition checks, and configuration handling. The only functional difference is that the optimization logic is now invoked through the ODB abstraction layer rather than directly from `cmd_gc()`. The patch also moves the `enum odb_optimize_strategy` and `enum odb_optimize_flags` definitions into `odb.h`, making them available to other backends.

This is a well-structured, uncontroversial patch that delivers on the series’ goal: enabling pluggable ODB optimizations. The test suite (updated in earlier patches) verifies that all maintenance tasks continue to execute as expected, and the series as a whole has addressed Junio’s feedback from v1. The patch is ready for review, with no known objections to the design or implementation. It is a prerequisite for future ODB backends to implement custom housekeeping logic.
