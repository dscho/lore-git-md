This is the **v11 revision** of Pablo Sabater’s feature series implementing **visual root indentation for `git log --graph`**, a user-visible enhancement that indents commits with excluded parents to clarify ancestry relationships in graph output. The series is now **seven patches long** and addresses all previously raised design and implementation concerns: the **four-level wrapping** indentation style, an **opt-out mechanism** (`--[no-]graph-indent` flag and `log.graphIndent` configuration), and a **lookahead buffer** to safely determine visual root status during revision traversal.

The **only change** since v10 is in **patch 7/7**, where the two boolean fields in `struct rev_info` (`no_graph_indent` and `graph_indent_set`) are now packed into single bits, addressing Mirko Faina’s mechanical feedback from the v10 review. This is a **purely internal** change with no user-visible impact. The rest of the series is **unchanged** from v10, as confirmed by the range-diff.

The series remains **technically complete** and **ready for final review**. All architectural and usability decisions are settled: the lookahead buffer approach was approved by Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson, the four-level wrap resolves symmetry-induced ambiguity, and the opt-out mechanism addresses concerns about forced visual changes. The **GitHub CI run** shows no failures, and the **new test file** (`t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh`) covers edge cases for the four-level wrap, opt-out behavior, and cascading roots. Existing tests (`t4215`, `t6016`) have been updated to reflect the new indentation rules.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion:
- The **lookahead buffer** (`lookahead[2]` in `struct git_graph`) is populated by `next_commit_to_show()` in `revision.c`, ensuring commits are fully simplified before inspection.
- The **visual root detection** logic in `graph.c` uses the lookahead buffer to determine whether a commit is a visual root before rendering, setting the `is_visual_root` flag in `struct git_graph`.
- The **four-level wrap** (patch 5/7) resets indentation after four levels, eliminating alignment ambiguity in edge cases (e.g., when the number of visual roots is a multiple of 3 or 4).
- The **opt-out mechanism** (patch 7/7) introduces a new `no_graph_indent` bit field in `struct rev_info`, controlled by `--[no-]graph-indent` and `log.graphIndent`. The default remains **indented** (`log.graphIndent=true`).
- The **redundant-indentation edge case** (marked NEEDSWORK in v8) is documented but not addressed in this revision.

The series is **awaiting final review** from Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood. Once signed off, it is likely ready for `next`. No unresolved technical or usability questions remain.
