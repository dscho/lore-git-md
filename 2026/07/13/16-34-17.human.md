This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-part series making Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. Junio C Hamano’s review confirms the patch is almost entirely a clean code relocation: 466 lines moved from `builtin/gc.c` into the "files" ODB backend (`odb/source-files.c`) with only minor adjustments to adapt to the new context (e.g., replacing `odb->repo` with a local `repo` variable). The patch wires the relocated functions into the ODB source vtable, exposing them to future backends via thin wrappers in `odb.c`.

The review is surface-level but positive. Junio used `git show --color-moved` to verify the mechanical nature of the move and noted the adjustments were minimal and correct. There are no objections or requests for changes; the patch appears ready to merge once the earlier cast ambiguity in patch 11 is resolved. This completes the series’ goal of isolating ODB housekeeping logic for pluggability, a key step in the broader ODB abstraction effort.
