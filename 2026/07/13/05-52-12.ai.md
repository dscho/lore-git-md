This is patch 9/12 in Patrick Steinhardt’s **"Make ODB optimizations pluggable"** series, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch introduces `odb_optimize_required()`, a new helper that centralizes the auto-condition checks for both incremental and geometric repacking strategies. Previously, this logic was duplicated in `need_to_gc()` (for incremental repacks) and `geometric_repack_auto_condition()` (for geometric repacks); now both callers delegate to the new function, which dispatches based on the requested `enum odb_optimize_strategy`.

The patch is a mechanical refactoring with no intended behavioral changes. It moves the auto-condition logic—loose-object thresholds, pack counts, geometric split factors, and configuration reads—into a single function, parameterized by a `struct odb_optimize_options` that specifies the strategy (`ODB_OPTIMIZE_INCREMENTAL` or `ODB_OPTIMIZE_GEOMETRIC`). The `gc_condition()` and `geometric_repack_auto_condition()` helpers are updated to construct the appropriate options struct and call `odb_optimize_required()`, while `need_to_gc()` is inlined into the new function. The patch preserves all existing logic, including the handling of negative auto-threshold values (which force a repack) and the geometric split-factor calculation.

The change touches only `builtin/gc.c`, replacing 68 lines of duplicated logic with 92 lines of consolidated code. The new function is backend-agnostic, taking a `struct object_database *` parameter to support future ODB backends, though it still reads configuration from the repository. The patch is a prerequisite for the final relocation of the optimization logic into `odb/source-files.c` in patch 12, and it maintains the series’ invariant of no behavioral changes. Reviewers may wish to verify that the strategy dispatch and options struct initialization do not introduce subtle edge cases in the auto-condition logic, though the patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of modularizing ODB housekeeping.
