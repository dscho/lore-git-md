This is a brief follow-up in the `git_hash_*()` API refactoring series, specifically addressing the historical context for direct `init_fn()` calls that patch 1/7 eliminates.

Jeff King (Peff) acknowledges Brian M. Carlson’s explanation about why Rust bindings treated `const struct git_hash_algo *` as an opaque `const void *`, forcing direct calls to `algop->init_fn()` instead of using the `git_hash_init()` wrapper. Peff notes that while the `init_fn()` calls had a technical justification, the direct use of `algo->update_fn()` and similar hooks in other parts of the codebase was less clear and harder to maintain. The comment is purely contextual and does not introduce new technical points or objections; it reinforces the motivation for the series’ goal of standardizing on the public `git_hash_*()` wrappers.

Key technical detail: The Rust binding constraint (opaque `const void *` treatment) is now resolved or no longer relevant, allowing the series to proceed with its cleanup. No changes to the patch series are implied or requested.
