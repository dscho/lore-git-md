This is a **clarification reply** in the ongoing ODB transaction refactoring series. Justin Tobler responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s question about why `flush_loose_object_transaction()` is being eliminated in patch 3/11. The rationale is organizational: the helper does more than just "flushing" (it also migrates objects to the primary ODB and reconfigures repository sources), so embedding its logic directly into `odb_transaction_files_commit()` better reflects the commit-phase semantics. Justin acknowledges the commit message’s vagueness and commits to updating it for clarity. No code changes are proposed; this is purely a documentation fix to address the open question from the v2 review. The series remains on track for `next` once this minor update is posted.
