This is the final review and merge decision for the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series targeting the commit-reach subsystem. Junio C Hamano reviews the patch that guards the `!FIND_ALL` early exit in `paint_down_to_common()` with a generation-ordering check, addressing a clock-skew regression in `git merge-base` (without `--all`) introduced by commit 93e5b1680e (April 2025).

The patch adds a `gen_ordered` flag to track whether the priority queue is generation-ordered. When the fallback to commit-date ordering occurs (for v1 commit graphs without corrected commit dates), the flag is cleared, preventing the early-exit optimization from firing incorrectly. The change is minimal: ten lines modified in `commit-reach.c` to conditionally gate the optimization, plus a one-line update to flip the accompanying test from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success`. Junio describes the solution as "simple and straight-forward" and marks the patch for the `next` branch, indicating it will be merged for the 2.55 release.

The fix is well-scoped, directly addressing the regression without altering on-disk formats or introducing new symbols.
