This is the third and final patch in the v8 series adjusting Git’s build system to support Rust components on macOS, specifically targeting the `git-credential-osxkeychain` helper. The patch ensures the macOS credential helper is included in standard CI test runs by wiring it into `contrib/Makefile` on macOS (Darwin).

The change is straightforward: it adds `credential/osxkeychain` to the `OS_CONTRIB` list when building on macOS, then extends the `all`, `test`, and `clean` targets in `contrib/Makefile` to invoke the corresponding targets in `osxkeychain/Makefile`. Additionally, it introduces a `test` target in `contrib/credential/osxkeychain/Makefile` that depends on building the helper, ensuring the build and link steps are exercised during CI.

This is a **well-motivated, low-risk build system fix** that closes a long-standing gap in CI coverage for the macOS credential helper. The patch is part of a larger, already-queued series (v6 is in `next`) and resolves the last remaining technical nit from earlier iterations. No new tests are needed, as the change is implicitly covered by existing CI workflows. The series as a whole is effectively ready for integration, with this patch completing the CI verification piece.
