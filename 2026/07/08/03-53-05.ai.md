This is the final patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API. It adds defensive runtime checks to all wrapper functions (`git_hash_clone()`, `git_hash_update()`, `git_hash_final()`, and `git_hash_final_oid()`) to ensure they are only called on an active (initialized but not yet finalized or discarded) hash context. The checks trigger a `BUG()` if the `active` flag is unset, catching misuse early and consistently across all hash backends. The patch leaves `git_hash_discard()` unchanged, preserving its idempotent behavior for safer cleanup. The commit message justifies why `git_hash_final()` is not made idempotent: double-calls are likely a bug, and the current behavior (nonsense output or undefined behavior) is better replaced with a loud failure. This patch touches only `hash.c` (10 lines added) and complements the compile-time enforcement provided by the Coccinelle semantic patch introduced earlier in the series. No user-visible behavior changes are expected; the goal is to harden the API against misuse. The series is now complete and ready for integration. Key symbols: `git_hash_ctx.active`, `BUG()`.
