This is a minor review exchange on the second patch of Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions. The patch itself is a **purely mechanical rename and refactor** of the `fsync_loose_object_transaction()` helper to `odb_transaction_files_fsync()`, aligning its name with the rest of the ODB transaction API. The change also reorders a conditional to make the logic slightly clearer, but **behavior is unchanged**—the function still issues a writeout-only fsync during the transaction and a full hardware flush at commit time.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is **surface-level**, noting only that the reordered conditional introduces a tiny bit of duplication but is easier to reason about. He also comments on a quirk in the diff rendering (the hunk header appearing mid-function) but does not raise any technical concerns. The exchange is brief and confirms that the patch is uncontroversial and ready to proceed.

This patch is part of the larger ODB abstraction effort, but it’s a small, self-contained step—no new functionality, no behavioral changes, and no open questions. It directly addresses feedback from v1 about naming consistency and is queued for `next` alongside the rest of the series. For a reader tracking the ODB refactoring, this is a routine cleanup patch; for everyone else, it’s safe to ignore unless you’re specifically interested in the transaction API’s internals.
