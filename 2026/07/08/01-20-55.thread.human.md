This thread updates Git’s contributor documentation to clarify how to handle abandoned or retracted patch series. Junio C Hamano has added a new section to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` that encourages contributors to explicitly communicate when they are no longer pursuing a topic—whether due to technical issues, time constraints, or other reasons. The goal is to reduce ambiguity in the review pipeline, allowing the maintainer and community to either reassign the work or safely discard it without lingering uncertainty. The change is framed as a practical improvement, reflecting the project’s preference for clear communication, and is expected to be uncontroversial. No further discussion or revisions are anticipated.
