This is a brief follow-up in Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series, specifically addressing a usability gap in patch 5/11. The patch converts the "files" transaction backend’s `prepare`, `begin`, and `commit` functions from `void` to `int` so that previously ignored failures are propagated to callers. Patrick Steinhardt’s review pointed out that while the patch correctly propagates errors, it does so silently—`tmp_objdir_create()` and `tmp_objdir_migrate()` failures return `-1` without logging an error message, making debugging harder.

Justin acknowledges the feedback and confirms he will add error logging in the next version. The change is purely additive (no behavioral or structural impact) and aligns with the series’ broader goal of surfacing ignored errors. This is a minor but worthwhile improvement; the patch itself remains a mechanical, uncontroversial step toward ODB-backend agnosticism. No further discussion or objections are expected.
