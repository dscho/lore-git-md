This is the first patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s v3 series optimizing Git’s priority queue. It extracts the bubble-up logic from `prio_queue_put()` into a standalone `sift_up()` helper. The change is purely refactoring—no behavior change—preparing the codebase for the cascade-down optimization that follows in patch 2/2.

The patch is small (12 insertions, 9 deletions) and mechanical: the loop that promotes a newly inserted element up the heap is lifted verbatim into its own function. René Scharfe suggested this split to keep the subsequent optimization patch focused and readable. The commit message is clear and the diff is clean, making the refactor easy to verify. No test failures or regressions are expected, and the patch is already covered by existing prio-queue tests.
