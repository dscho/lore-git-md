This is a **performance bugfix** targeting the `unpack-trees` subsystem. The patch eliminates a quadratic-time index scan in `next_cache_entry()` that occurs when running `git diff` with a pathspec matching a subtree whose entries appear early in the index. The motivation is compelling: on a Chromium-scale repository (~500k index entries), `git diff HEAD -- .agents/OWNERS` improved from **8 minutes to 0.07 seconds** (a 99.7% speedup).
