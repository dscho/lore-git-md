This is a documentation patch updating `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to explicitly address how contributors should handle abandoning or retracting a patch series. The patch adds a new section acknowledging that not all patch series succeed and encouraging contributors to communicate clearly when they are no longer pursuing a topic, whether due to technical concerns, lack of time, or other reasons.

The change is motivated by the maintainer's desire to reduce ambiguity: leaving a topic in limbo creates unnecessary work for the maintainer and the community, while an explicit retraction allows others to take over or the maintainer to safely discard it. The new text also leaves the door open for resurrection later, emphasizing that abandonment is not permanent.

The patch is concise, well-written, and directly addresses a real friction point in the contribution process. It reflects Junio C Hamano's long-standing preference for clear communication and aligns with the project's culture of reducing uncertainty in the review pipeline. No controversy is expected; this is a straightforward improvement to contributor guidance.
