This is a **performance bugfix** for `unpack-trees.c` that addresses a quadratic-time index scan in `next_cache_entry()`. The patch is part of an ongoing review thread where Junio raised a critical correctness concern about whether the new optimization interacts safely with existing logic designed to handle ordering mismatches between the index and tree objects.

Henrique responds to Junio’s concern by clarifying that the existing `.cache_bottom` field (which bounds backward scans) remains unchanged and continues to function as intended. He points to commit `e53e6b4433` (2010) where `find_cache_pos()` was already updated to advance `.cache_bottom` past unpacked entries, suggesting the new forward-scan optimization (`o->internal.cache_bottom`) should not interfere. This explanation appears to address the core correctness question, though Junio has not yet replied to confirm.

If the correctness argument holds, this patch looks ready to land. It’s a targeted fix for a real performance regression, with clear motivation and test coverage. The remaining loose end is the `EXPENSIVE` scaling in the test, but that’s a minor polish step rather than a blocker.
