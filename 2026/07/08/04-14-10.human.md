This is the ninth patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It introduces `enum odb_transaction_flags` and the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag to let the "files" backend mimic the two quirks that `git-receive-pack` currently relies on: eager directory creation and the "incoming" prefix. The patch is mechanical—every call site of `odb_transaction_begin()` and its convenience wrapper gains a flags parameter, and the "files" backend inspects the flag to adjust its behavior. No user-visible changes yet; the actual conversion of `receive-pack` happens in the next patch. The commit message clearly documents the NEEDSWORK comment about the flag being backend-specific and the follow-up work that remains.
