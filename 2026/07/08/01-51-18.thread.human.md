This thread is about a bugfix for `git merge --abort` that aims to prevent data loss of the autostash when the abort fails (e.g., due to a locked index). The patch, submitted by Kris Point, defers deletion of the `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` ref until after `git reset --merge` succeeds, but Phillip Wood has raised a substantive technical objection.

Phillip argues the patch’s logic is flawed: it would unconditionally save the autostash even if it applies cleanly (which shouldn’t happen), and if the reset fails, the autostash would still be saved rather than preserved in the merge state. He proposes a more involved fix—decoupling the reset logic from `cmd_reset()` and introducing a new flag (`RESET_WORKING_TREE_MERGE`) to `reset_working_tree()` (or `reset_head()` in the target branch). This would ensure the autostash is only saved if conflicts occur during application and that merge state persists if the reset fails. The thread is now at a decision point, with no other participants having weighed in yet. The patch remains under review, and the author will likely need to revise it to address Phillip’s concerns.
