This is a review comment on the third patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions. The patch inlines the `flush_loose_object_transaction()` helper into `odb_transaction_files_commit()` as a mechanical cleanup. Patrick Steinhardt, the ODB subsystem owner, asks why this refactoring is necessary—what future change it enables that would be harder without it. His question is forward-looking: he isn’t objecting to the patch itself (which he calls “simple enough”), but wants to understand the architectural rationale behind the indirection removal. The answer is likely in a later patch in the series, but this review comment flags the need for clearer motivation in the commit message or cover letter. The exchange is substantive but not blocking; it’s a request for context rather than a technical objection.
