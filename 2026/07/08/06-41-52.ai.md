This is the ninth patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 ODB transaction series, introducing transaction flags. The email is a **substantive review** from Patrick Steinhardt (ODB subsystem owner) focusing on header organization and the long-term design of the flags interface.

Patrick’s feedback centers on the header dependency introduced by the new `enum odb_transaction_flags`. The patch moves the enum into `odb/transaction.h` but still requires `odb/source.h` to be included elsewhere because the flags are referenced in both headers. Patrick questions whether splitting `odb/transaction.h` from `odb/source.h` is worthwhile, given the tight coupling. His comment is not a blocker — he explicitly acknowledges the patch’s correctness — but it flags a potential layering issue that could complicate future ODB abstraction work.

The review is narrowly scoped: it does not revisit the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag’s purpose (preserving `receive-pack`’s eager directory creation and "incoming" prefix) or its temporary nature, both of which were previously accepted. Instead, it zooms in on the mechanical consequence of the patch — the header include chain — and asks whether the separation of `odb/transaction.h` from `odb/source.h` is pulling in the opposite direction of the ODB abstraction effort’s goal of reducing inter-module dependencies.

No code changes are requested yet; this is a design-level observation that may inform future refactoring. The series remains queued for `next` pending Justin’s response.
