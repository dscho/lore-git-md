This is a documentation patch to `SubmittingPatches`. Junio C Hamano adds a new section explicitly encouraging contributors to announce when they are abandoning a patch series rather than leaving it in limbo. The text covers two common scenarios: realizing the change is not a good idea after discussion, or having to step away due to time or priorities. In both cases, the maintainer is relieved of tracking the topic, and others can optionally take it over later. The change is small (15 lines) and uncontroversial, simply formalizing a practice that already happens informally.
