This is the ninth patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. The patch introduces `enum odb_transaction_flags` and the `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag to preserve `receive-pack`’s existing behavior (eager directory creation and "incoming" prefix) during the transition.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is brief and focused on a header-file layering question. He notes that the patch removes `#include "odb/source.h"` from `odb/transaction.h`, but the new `enum odb_transaction_flags` is needed in both headers, forcing `odb/source.h` to include `odb/transaction.h` instead. This circularity makes him question whether splitting the headers is worthwhile. His tone is curious rather than critical; he is not objecting to the patch itself, only probing the design choice. The patch remains queued for `next` and the series as a whole is still on track.
