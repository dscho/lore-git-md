This is a short follow-up in the ODB transaction series, specifically addressing Patrick Steinhardt’s design-level feedback on patch 9/11. Justin Tobler agrees with Patrick’s observation that splitting `odb/transaction.h` from `odb/source.h` may not be worthwhile, given the tight coupling introduced by `enum odb_transaction_flags`. The transaction implementation is inherently tied to its ODB source, and the current header separation could complicate future ODB abstraction work. Justin plans to leave the code as-is for now but will explore merging the two headers in a future series. No code changes are made in this reply; the discussion remains at the design level, and the series continues to be queued for `next`.
