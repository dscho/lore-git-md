This is the fourth patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API. The patch makes `git_hash_discard()` idempotent by adding an `active` flag to the `git_hash_ctx` struct. This change allows callers to safely call `git_hash_discard()` multiple times without risking resource leaks or inconsistent state, simplifying error-handling code paths.

The patch is small (7 lines added) and focused: it introduces the `active` flag, sets it to `true` in `git_hash_init()`, clears it in `git_hash_final()` and `git_hash_final_oid()`, and checks it in `git_hash_discard()` to avoid redundant calls. The change is backward-compatible for well-behaved callers and does not alter user-visible behavior. Future patches in the series will leverage this idempotency to simplify existing callers.

The motivation is clear: eliminating the need for callers to track whether a hash context has already been finalized or discarded reduces boilerplate and the risk of bugs. The design choice to leave `git_hash_final()` unprotected (triggering a `BUG()` on misuse) is now explicitly justified in the series’ final patch, aligning with industry-standard practice for hash libraries.
