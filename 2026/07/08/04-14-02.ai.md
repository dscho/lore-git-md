This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It performs a straightforward rename: `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` becomes `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to reflect that the function will soon be used for both loose objects and packfiles. The change is purely mechanical—three call sites in `object-file.c` are updated—and carries no behavioral or interface changes. The patch is part of the ODB abstraction effort and addresses a naming consistency request from the v1 review. No objections remain; the patch is queued for `next`.
