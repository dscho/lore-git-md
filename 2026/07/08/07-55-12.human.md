This is a brief follow-up in the "Make ODB optimizations pluggable" series, specifically addressing Junio C Hamano’s review of the first patch. The patch extends the "pre-auto-gc" hook to run for all automated maintenance tasks, not just `git gc`, and Junio’s feedback focused on two minor points: the hook’s use of a relative path for its log file (deemed safe but not ideal) and the test’s reliance on `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to verify subprocess spawning (acknowledged as fragile).

Patrick agrees the test is brittle and notes that trace2 regions—already present in the codebase since 2020—could provide a more robust way to verify task execution without depending on subprocess details. He has local changes ready but is waiting for additional feedback before sending a v2. The exchange is purely about test quality; the core design (extending the hook to all maintenance tasks) remains unchallenged and implicitly supported. No other patches in the series are discussed here.
