This is the ninth patch in Justin Tobler’s v2 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. The email is a brief follow-up to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the patch that introduces `enum odb_transaction_flags`.

Justin agrees with Patrick’s observation that splitting `odb/transaction.h` from `odb/source.h` may not be worthwhile, given the tight coupling between transactions and their ODB sources. The `ODB_TRANSACTION_RECEIVE` flag (used to preserve `receive-pack`’s existing behavior) is referenced in both headers, creating a circular dependency that complicates the ODB abstraction effort. Justin plans to leave the headers separate for now but will likely merge them in a future series. No code changes are proposed in this reply; the discussion remains at the design level.

The series as a whole is functionally complete and has addressed all prior feedback. This exchange does not block integration — the patch is correct and the header organization is a forward-looking concern rather than a regression. The series remains queued for `next`.
