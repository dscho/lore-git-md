This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s v3 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It is a pure refactoring: the function `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` is renamed to `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to better reflect its role in the transaction lifecycle, now that the same temporary directory will also stage packfiles. The change is mechanical—only the name and three call sites in `object-file.c` are updated—and introduces no behavioral differences. The patch is uncontroversial and already carries Patrick Steinhardt’s Reviewed-by from v2. It sets the stage for the rest of the series by aligning naming with the broader ODB abstraction effort.
