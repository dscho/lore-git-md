This thread is about a bugfix series that makes two CGI test helpers in Git's HTTP daemon test suite concurrency-safe. The series, now at version 5, fixes race conditions in `apply-one-time-script.sh` and `http-429.sh` where non-atomic shell operations (`test -f` followed by `rm` or `touch`) could cause HTTP 500 errors when Apache served concurrent requests to the same endpoint. The fixes replace these with atomic operations (`rm` without `-f` and `mkdir`), and add a new deterministic test to verify the fix.

Michael Montalbo is the author, and the series has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. Junio initially provided substantive feedback on the commit messages and rationale, while Patrick focused on editorial improvements to the documentation. All feedback has been addressed, with the final tweak—a rewording of documentation advice to clarify the relationship between atomic operations and the logic they guard—now incorporated. Patrick has explicitly endorsed the series as ready for integration, and no further changes are expected. The series is minimal, well-tested, and confined to the test infrastructure, with recent CI failures underscoring the urgency of the fix. It awaits Junio’s merge into the `next` branch.
