This thread is about a small but important bugfix series that makes Git’s HTTP daemon test helpers robust against race conditions. Michael Montalbo identified a real flake in the test suite: two CGI scripts in `t/lib-httpd/` (`apply-one-time-script.sh` and `http-429.sh`) use non-atomic shell operations to manage shared state, which can cause HTTP 500 errors when Apache serves concurrent requests to the same endpoint. The series replaces these checks with atomic operations (`mv` and `mkdir`) and adds a new test to verify the fix.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the first version and provided substantive feedback on the commit messages and rationale for the atomic operations. Michael addressed all of it in v2, clarifying the distinction between shrinking the race window and preventing concurrent execution, and explicitly documenting why the atomic `mkdir` check is required even in the `"permanent"` case. The documentation patch (patch 3) was left unchanged, as Junio called it a “nice finishing touch.” No other reviews or objections have been raised, and the series now looks complete and ready for integration.

Michael recently pinged the thread after a month of silence, noting that the fixes are minimal, self-contained, and target a real, observed issue in macOS CI runners. He also linked to recent CI failures that appear to be caused by the same race, reinforcing the urgency. The series is now waiting for Junio to pick it up for integration.
