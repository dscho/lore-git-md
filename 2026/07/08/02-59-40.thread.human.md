This thread fixes race conditions in Git’s HTTP daemon test helpers that could cause HTTP 500 errors during concurrent requests. The series, now at v4, replaces non-atomic shell operations in two CGI scripts (`apply-one-time-script.sh` and `http-429.sh`) with atomic alternatives (`rm` without `-f` and `mkdir`) to ensure robust state management under concurrency. Michael Montalbo authored the patches, addressing all feedback from earlier versions, including Junio C Hamano’s and Patrick Steinhardt’s requests for clearer commit messages and documentation placement.

The fixes are purely internal to the test infrastructure, with no changes to user-facing behavior. A new test (`t5567-one-time-script.sh`) verifies the atomicity of `apply-one-time-script.sh` using FIFOs to force races deterministically. The documentation update in `t/lib-httpd.sh` now explicitly recommends atomic idioms and process-ID suffixes for scratch files, incorporating Patrick’s editorial suggestions. Junio marked the series as ready for integration in v2, and Patrick endorsed it as "good enough" in v4 after a minor wording tweak. The series is complete and awaits queueing into `next`. Recent CI failures linked by Michael underscore the urgency of the fix.
