This thread is a three-patch series from Michael Montalbo that fixes race conditions in Git's HTTP daemon test helpers, which have caused flaky test failures in CI (notably `t5616.47` on macOS). The series replaces non-atomic shell operations in two CGI scripts (`apply-one-time-script.sh` and `http-429.sh`) with atomic alternatives—`rm` (without `-f`) for one-shot markers and `mkdir` for first-request election—to prevent concurrent HTTP requests from triggering HTTP 500 errors. The final patch, just posted, adds documentation to `t/lib-httpd.sh` to guide future developers in writing race-free helpers, incorporating all prior feedback, including a recent wording tweak from Patrick Steinhardt.

The series has been through five iterations, with all substantive and editorial feedback addressed. Junio C Hamano reviewed early versions, calling the fixes "well thought out" and "nicely done," while Patrick Steinhardt endorsed the series as "good enough" in v3 and "a lot better now" in v4. Michael has confirmed no further changes are needed, and the series is ready for integration into Junio's `next` branch. The fix is tied to real-world CI flakes, reinforcing its urgency.
