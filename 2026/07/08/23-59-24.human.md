This is a small cleanup patch in Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series. It removes a redundant call to `tmp_objdir_env()` in `update_shallow_ref()` inside `git-receive-pack`. The call is unnecessary because `tmp_objdir_migrate()` has already moved the temporary objects into the main object store by the time `update_shallow_ref()` runs, so there is no temporary directory left to expose to child processes. The change is purely mechanical—no behavior changes—and was explicitly endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed the redundancy by tracing the call chain. This patch is the tenth of eleven in the series, which is functionally complete and queued for `next`.
