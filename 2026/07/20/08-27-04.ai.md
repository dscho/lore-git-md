This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series, implementing the `--reedit-message` feature. The patch completes the series by allowing users to interactively combine commit messages from the entire squashed range using a template that **exactly matches `git rebase -i --autosquash`**.

The implementation reuses sequencer helpers (`add_squash_combination_header`, `add_squash_message_header`, and `squash_subject_comment_len`) to construct the template, ensuring consistency with `git rebase -i`. The template groups `fixup!`, `squash!`, and `amend!` commits under their targets, comments out `fixup!` messages in full, retains `squash!` bodies (with only the marker subject commented), and lets `amend!` replace its target’s message unless a `squash!` already folded into that target. The new `build_squash_message` helper walks the range in reverse topo-order, gathers each commit’s message, and constructs the template.

The documentation updates clarify how `--reedit-message` behaves, explicitly stating that the template mirrors `git rebase -i --autosquash` and detailing how `fixup!`, `squash!`, and `amend!` messages are handled. The test suite adds comprehensive coverage for the feature, including edge cases like `amend!` message replacement, grouping of multiple `fixup!` commits under their targets, and aborting on an empty message.
