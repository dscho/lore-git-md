This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series, implementing the `--reedit-message` feature. The patch completes the series by letting users interactively combine commit messages from the entire squashed range using a template that exactly matches `git rebase -i --autosquash`.

The implementation reuses sequencer helpers (`add_squash_combination_header`, `add_squash_message_header`, and `squash_subject_comment_len`) to ensure the template is consistent with rebase’s autosquash behavior. The new `build_squash_message` helper walks the range in reverse topo-order, gathers each commit’s message, and constructs the template. Fixup, squash, and amend commits are grouped under their targets, with fixup messages fully commented out, squash messages retaining their bodies, and amend messages replacing their target’s message unless a squash already folded into that target.

The documentation updates clarify how `--reedit-message` works, including the autosquash-style grouping and the behavior of `amend!` messages. The test suite adds thorough coverage for the feature, verifying the template construction, grouping logic, and edge cases like empty messages and message replacement.
