Originating author: tnyman@openai.com
Thread type: patch series

Core problem or goal
Optimize `git stash push -- <pathspec>` in sparse-checkout repositories by avoiding unnecessary sparse-index expansion when the pathspec is wholly inside the sparse-checkout cone. The current implementation expands the index to full form before checking pathspec matches, causing performance to scale with total tracked paths rather than sparse-directory entries. The series also fixes a latent heap-buffer-overflow bug in `pathspec_needs_expanded_index()` triggered by wildcard pathspecs from subdirectories.
