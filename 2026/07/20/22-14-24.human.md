This is Junio C Hamano’s **substantive reply** in the ongoing debate over Patrick Steinhardt’s RFC patch series to reorganize Git’s source tree by moving `libgit.a` components into a new `lib/` directory. This, he argues, is not a one-time cost but a **persistent burden** that will continue until all in-flight topics (including the reorganization itself) graduate.

The proposed reorganization, in his view, amounts to **churn for churn’s sake**, lacking the clear organizational improvement of past subsystem moves (like the creation of `builtin/`, `refs/`, or `odb/`).

His position suggests the patch is unlikely to be merged in its current form unless the pro-reorganization camp can address the **ongoing friction** it introduces—something the latest replies from Dscho and brian have not done.
