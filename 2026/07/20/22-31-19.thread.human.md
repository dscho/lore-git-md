This thread introduces a two-patch optimization for `git stash push` in sparse-checkout repositories, authored by tnyman@openai.com. The goal is to avoid unnecessary sparse-index expansion when stashing paths inside the sparse-checkout cone, dramatically improving performance (from 18.9 seconds to 0.06 seconds in cone mode) without regressing full-index behavior. The series also fixes a latent heap-buffer-overflow bug in `pathspec_needs_expanded_index()` triggered by wildcard pathspecs from subdirectories.

Both patches are now reviewed. Taylor Blau provided a **substantive review** for patch 1/2, confirming the bug fix with AddressSanitizer and endorsing the test coverage. His review of patch 2/2 was **surface-level**, focusing on structure and alignment with the series' goals rather than technical deep dives. No open questions remain, and the series passes all test suites, including sparse-index, stash, pathspec, SHA-256, and ASan regression tests. The optimization builds on existing sparse-index logic from `git rm` and is self-contained, touching `builtin/stash.c`, performance tests, and compatibility tests without introducing new symbols or on-disk format changes.
