This thread is about a two-patch series from Ted Nyman that optimizes `git stash push` in sparse-checkout repositories by avoiding unnecessary index expansion when the pathspec is inside the sparse-checkout cone. The series also fixes a latent heap-buffer-overflow bug in the pathspec helper triggered by wildcard pathspecs from subdirectories.

The patches have been fully reviewed: Taylor Blau provided a substantive review of the first patch, confirming the heap-buffer-overflow fix with an ASan reproduction and endorsing the pathspec helper refactor, while his review of the second patch was more surface-level, approving its structure but not engaging deeply with the sparse-index logic. All tests pass, including sparse-index, stash, pathspec, SHA-256, ASan regression, and performance benchmarks. Junio C Hamano has now marked the series for inclusion in the `next` branch, signaling it is ready for integration testing and will graduate to `master` unless issues arise.

The optimization reduces `git stash push` time for cone-internal pathspecs from 18.9 seconds to 0.06 seconds in cone mode, with no performance regression in full-index mode. The series touches `builtin/stash.c`, adds a performance test in `t/perf/p2000-sparse-operations.sh`, and extends coverage in `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh`. No new symbols or on-disk format changes were introduced. The thread is now resolved, with no open questions or objections.
