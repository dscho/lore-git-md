This is a **substantive, tested review** of the first patch in the `git stash push` sparse-index optimization series. The reviewer (Taylor Blau) confirms the heap-buffer-overflow bug in `pathspec_needs_expanded_index()`—dating back to its 2021 introduction—by reproducing it with AddressSanitizer using a minimal script that invokes `git reset` from a subdirectory with a wildcard pathspec. The crash occurs in `strspn()` when the helper incorrectly uses `item.original` (the unprefixed argument) instead of `item.match` (the prefixed path), causing an out-of-bounds read.

Taylor’s review walks through the patch’s mechanical replacement of `item.original` with `item.match` in the helper, endorsing the change as correct and consistent with the function’s intent. The added test in `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh`—which verifies that `git rm` and `git reset` now keep the index sparse when given prefixed wildcard pathspecs—mirrors the reproduction script and is likewise approved. No concerns or open questions are raised; the patch appears ready to proceed.
