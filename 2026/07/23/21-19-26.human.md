This is the fourth and final patch in Jean-Noël Avila’s v2 series converting the man pages for `git imap-send`, `git format-patch`, `git send-email`, and `git request-pull` to the project’s new AsciiDoc synopsis style. The series is part of the ongoing effort to standardize all Git documentation on a consistent markup format.

This patch updates `Documentation/git-request-pull.adoc` to use the new style: replacing `[verse]` with `[synopsis]` in the SYNOPSIS block, removing single-quote formatting from the command name, backtick-quoting the `-p` flag, and converting placeholders like `<start>` to `_<start>_`. The changes are purely presentational and do not alter the command’s behavior or options. The patch also updates prose references to placeholders and command examples to match the new formatting, ensuring consistency throughout the document.

The series as a whole has already addressed the two stylistic issues Junio C Hamano raised in v1—backtick usage in `git format-patch` and uniform four-dash fences in AsciiDoc blocks—so this patch is ready for final review. Given the mechanical nature of the conversion and the author’s track record on this effort, the patch looks uncontroversial and is likely to be merged once reviews are complete. No further changes are expected.
