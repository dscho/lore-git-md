This is a follow-up in the `show-branch` commit-slab refactoring thread, authored by Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy in direct response to Junio C Hamano’s latest email. The email is not a patch or review, but a human engagement demonstrating the author’s understanding of the regression and their commitment to mailing list communication norms.

Gatla acknowledges the division-by-zero crash in v9 was caused by an accidental removal of `init_commit_name_slab(&name_slab)` during a v7 restructuring that moved the `rev_flags_slab` initialization after ref collection. He explains the root cause: the `name_slab` initialization was in the same block and was dropped, leaving `slab_size` at 0, which triggered the crash when `commit_to_name()` computed `c->index / s->slab_size`. He confirms the API has not changed, the structure is still used throughout (`name_commits()`, `name_commit()`, `name_parent()`), and the initialization is not performed elsewhere. He takes responsibility for both the AI’s oversight (it was focused on `rev_flags_slab` changes and did not flag the deletion) and his own (he did not run the binary before sending).

The email also responds to Junio’s communication expectations: Gatla commits to not sending another patch version until every question is answered through discussion first, and invites Junio to ask any questions needed to confirm the patch is human-understood. This resolves the immediate communication concern that had paused technical review, though Junio has not yet indicated whether he will resume reviewing the v10 patch itself. The thread remains technically ready for integration, pending Junio’s next move.
