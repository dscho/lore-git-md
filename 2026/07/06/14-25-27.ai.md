This is patch 6/9 in Tian Yuchen’s v7 series migrating more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. It moves the `apply_default_whitespace` and `apply_default_ignorewhitespace` globals into the repository-specific struct and updates `git_apply_config()` to accept a `struct repository *` instead of relying on `the_repository`.

The patch touches `apply.c`, `environment.c`, and `environment.h`. In `apply.c`, the `git_apply_config()` function is modified to take a repository argument, and all direct references to the globals are replaced with `repo_config_values(repo)-><field>`. In `environment.c`, the globals are removed from the global namespace and added to `struct repo_config_values`, with corresponding cleanup in `repo_config_values_clear()`. The changes are mechanical and follow the established pattern from earlier patches in the series.

This is a straightforward refactoring with no user-visible behavior changes. The patch is well-motivated as part of the ongoing libification effort, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale. The only notable detail is the explicit passing of the repository argument to `git_apply_config()`, which aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating implicit globals. The patch is CI-clean and ready for review, though it depends on earlier patches in the series (particularly the introduction of `repo_config_values_clear()` and the migration infrastructure).

Key symbols for follow-up: `repo_config_values()->apply_default_whitespace`, `repo_config_values()->apply_default_ignorewhitespace`, and `git_apply_config(struct repository *)`. The patch is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort and continues the pattern established in the merged `excludes_file` series. No open questions or loose ends specific to this patch.
