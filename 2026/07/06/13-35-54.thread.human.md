This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series (now at v3) that resolves a quadratic performance regression in Git’s reftable backend. The issue occurs when re-creating refs that were previously deleted (tombstoned), causing operations like `refs_verify_refnames_available()` to scan all tombstone records before bounds checks can terminate. The fix moves the `suppress_deletions` flag from the merged iterator to `struct reftable_stack_options`, allowing explicit tombstone handling at call sites where bounds are known, improving performance from ~14s to ~0.2s in the perf test’s worst-case scenarios.

The series is finalized and ready for Junio’s merge, with all substantive feedback incorporated. Patrick Steinhardt acked the series after minor cosmetic feedback (a commit message typo and missing comment) was noted but deemed insufficient for a reroll. Kristofer Karlsson, the author, confirmed the API shape preserves libgit2’s current behavior while future-proofing the interface. Brian M. Carlson endorsed the fix based on real-world benchmarks, though no code-level review was provided.

The only open discussion is forward-looking: whether `suppress_deletions` should eventually be removed entirely. Kristofer explored this possibility, noting libgit2’s call sites already perform explicit deletion checks, but the question remains speculative and does not block this series. The patch is backward-compatible for libgit2, though future updates will be needed to use the new `reftable_stack_options` field. No further action is required before merge.
