This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s five-part v7 series introducing the `git history squash` subcommand. It is a pure refactoring that extracts a small helper, `first_parent_tree_oid()`, to centralize the logic for resolving a commit’s first-parent tree (or the empty tree for a root commit). The helper is then used in three places in `builtin/history.c` that previously repeated the same parse-and-copy dance.

The patch is mechanical and well-contained: 26 insertions, 32 deletions, no behavior change. It is clearly motivated by the need to reuse this logic in the later squash implementation, and the helper’s interface is clean. No controversy or open questions remain on this patch; it is ready to merge.
