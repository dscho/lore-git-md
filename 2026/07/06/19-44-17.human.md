Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, raises a critical question about the patch's premise: whether `remove_file_from_index()` actually sets `errno` when it fails. Without this, `die_errno()` would pick up an irrelevant or garbage value, misleading users rather than helping them. Junio asks the author to provide a call graph showing where `errno` is set in the error path.

The patch itself is small and well-scoped, but this review comment casts doubt on its correctness.
