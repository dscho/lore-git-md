This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch v3 series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient in CI. It removes the macOS RAM-disk workaround that was introduced in 56090a35ab (2024-01-18) to mitigate slow network-attached storage on GitLab’s macOS runners.

The patch trades a 7–12 minute runtime increase (from 18–21 minutes to 25–33 minutes) for stability: the 4 GB RAM disk is too small for the 8 GB of test data that some `EXPENSIVE` tests generate, and expanding it to 12 GB still proved flaky. The change simply reverts `TEST_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` to `/tmp/test-output` and drops the `diskutil`/`hdiutil` commands from the `before_script` section of `.gitlab-ci.yml`.

No other files are touched, and the patch is uncontroversial. The commit message explains the trade-off clearly and notes that macOS jobs are not the slowest in the pipeline, so the runtime penalty does not extend the overall CI cycle. This patch is ready for integration and has no unresolved feedback.
