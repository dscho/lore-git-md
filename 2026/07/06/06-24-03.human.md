This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch v3 series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient in CI. It removes the macOS RAM-disk workaround that was added in early 2024 to speed up test runs on GitLab’s slow network-attached storage. The patch trades a 7–12 minute runtime increase for stability: without the RAM disk, the macOS jobs now write test output directly to `/tmp/test-output` instead of a 4 GB in-memory filesystem. This avoids ENOSPC errors when `GIT_TEST_LONG` is enabled (next patch) and runs tests that generate up to 8 GB of data. The change is uncontroversial and already justified in the commit message: slower but stable is preferable to faster but flaky. No objections have been raised; the patch is ready to merge.
