This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch v3 series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient for CI. The change is minimal (one line) and uncontroversial, addressing a correctness issue without altering the test’s intent or behavior on supported platforms.
