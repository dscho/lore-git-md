This is a refactoring series that continues the libification effort by moving more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is a follow-up to Tian Yuchen’s recently merged work on `excludes_file`, now expanding the pattern to nine additional variables: editor, pager, askpass, apply whitespace settings, and three enumerations (`push_default`, `autorebase`, `object_creation_mode`).

The first two patches overlap with the just-merged v6 series: patch 1 introduces `repo_config_values_clear()` as a lifecycle helper for heap-allocated strings, and patch 2 migrates `excludes_file`. The remaining seven patches apply the same pattern to the other variables. The author notes that the v6 structure was discarded on Christian Couder’s suggestion, so this is effectively a new series rather than a true v7.

Two RFC questions are flagged: whether migrating the external program variables (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and whether the enum migrations might conflict with a stalled `git_branch_track` effort. The commit messages do not yet address these points, so the answers will likely emerge in review. The series is CI-clean, touches a dozen files, and is clearly motivated by the ongoing libification goal. It looks well-structured and ready for review, though the RFC questions may prompt some debate.
