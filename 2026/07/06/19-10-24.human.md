This is a small but sensible bugfix for the `git rm` command. The patch replaces a call to `die()` with `die_errno()` in `builtin/rm.c` when `remove_file_from_index()` fails, ensuring that the system error message (from `errno`) is included in the output. This makes failures easier to diagnose by showing *why* the operation failed, not just that it did.

The change is minimal—just one line—and the rationale is clear: preserving error context is always helpful for debugging. The commit message is concise and explains the motivation well. This is a classic "low-hanging fruit" patch: a small improvement that doesn’t introduce risk or controversy.
