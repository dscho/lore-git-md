This is a surface-level review of patch 9/11 in Phillip Wood’s 11-patch series fixing how Git handles dropped commits during rebase. The series is already queued in Junio’s tree and addresses a long-standing bug where notes from dropped commits were incorrectly copied to the current HEAD, corrupting note history.

Oswald Buddenhagen suggests squashing this refactoring patch into its parent commit. It introduces no behavior change—its only purpose is to prepare for the next two patches, which add an enum for pick results and the `PICK_RESULT_DROPPED` state. Oswald’s feedback is purely stylistic and does not question the patch’s correctness or necessity.

The series as a whole is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and ready for final review.
