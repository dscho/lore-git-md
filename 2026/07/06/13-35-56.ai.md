This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s two-patch series fixing a quadratic performance regression in the reftable backend. The patch removes the `suppress_deletions` flag from the merged iterator and pushes tombstone handling out to the reftable backend’s call sites, where prefix and refname bounds are known. This change restores O(1) behavior for `refs_verify_refnames_available()` and `reftable_backend_read_ref()` when many tombstones are present, cutting runtime from ~14 s to ~0.2 s in the 8 000-ref perf test.

The diff touches four files: `refs/reftable-backend.c` (core logic), `reftable/merged.{c,h}` (removing the flag), and `reftable/stack.c` (removing the now-unused assignment). Every call site that previously relied on the merged iterator’s suppression now explicitly checks for and skips deletion records after its own bounds checks, allowing early termination. The log-iteration paths are updated in parallel, since `suppress_deletions` applied to both ref and log iterators.

No new symbols are introduced; the on-disk format is unchanged. The patch is narrowly scoped to the reftable backend and does not attempt broader refactoring. Test coverage is already provided by the first patch in the series (`p1401-ref-store-tombstones.sh` and `t0610-reftable-basics.sh`), which exercises both the quadratic scenario and edge cases around deleted-then-recreated refs. The commit message links to prior discussion with Jeff King and a lore thread, providing context for the regression’s discovery.
