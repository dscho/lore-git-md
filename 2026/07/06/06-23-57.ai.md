This is a test patch, part 2/9 of Patrick Steinhardt’s series fixing issues with `GIT_TEST_LONG` and expensive tests. The patch skips a test in `t0021-conversion.sh` that writes and roundtrips a 2 GB file through clean/smudge filters. The test is broken on 32-bit platforms because they cannot handle files larger than `SSIZE_MAX` reliably—it either fails with `ENOMEM` or hangs indefinitely. The fix adds `SIZE_T_IS_64BIT` to the test prerequisites, effectively skipping it on 32-bit systems. The commit message explains that while CI includes a `linux32` job, the issue went unnoticed until expensive tests were enabled in CI in May 2026, and that reliably detecting the failure is difficult. The change is minimal: one line in the test script.
