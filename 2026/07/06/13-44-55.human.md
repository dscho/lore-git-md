This is a **substantive technical reply** in the ongoing thread about Pablo Sabater’s v7 patch series implementing cascading indentation for `` `git log --graph` ``. Kristofer Karlsson, who originally proposed and prototyped the lookahead buffer redesign, responds to Chandra Pratap’s surface-level review of the buffer implementation (patch 2/3).

Kristofer clarifies the design intent behind the hardcoded two-slot lookahead buffer, confirming it was his suggestion and explaining why a more general ring buffer was deemed unnecessary. He addresses Chandra’s question about NULLing out retrieved buffer entries: while not technically required, it could prevent subtle bugs by crashing on accidental reuse rather than silently operating on stale data. He also suggests that if assertions are added, they should verify non-NULL values on read rather than NULL values on empty slots, though he considers even that optional for such a small internal buffer.

On the use of ``ARRAY_SIZE()`` instead of the hardcoded value 2, Kristofer agrees it is an improvement and defers to Pablo Sabater for the final decision. His tone is pragmatic, balancing engineering rigor with the practical constraints of a small, internal data structure. The exchange does not introduce new technical concerns but reinforces the robustness of the current design while leaving room for minor cosmetic refinements.
