It has no behavioral impact and is a straightforward follow-up to the earlier consolidation. While small, it is part of the series’ broader goal of simplifying and generalizing bitmap-handling logic, which ultimately enables `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` to work with pluggable ODB backends.
