The patch addresses a latent bug: `for_each_bitmapped_object()` currently ignores the return value of its callback, which works for its only existing caller (`git cat-file`) but would break once the logic is lifted into the generic ODB infrastructure where callbacks are expected to signal early termination. Two existing callbacks in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `builtin/rev-list.c` that previously returned `1` are changed to return `0` to preserve their current behavior, since their non-zero returns were never acted upon.
