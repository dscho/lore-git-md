- **Performance:**
  - `hf/unpack-trees-quadratic-scan` (1 commit): Fixes quadratic behavior in `next_cache_entry()` when diffing with pathspecs matching early index entries.

  - `kk/reftable-tombstone-quadratic-fix` (2 commits): Fixes quadratic behavior in reftable backend with many tombstones by skipping them at higher-level call sites.
  - `ps/reftable-hardening` (12 commits): Hardens reftable parsing against corrupted tables (OOB reads/writes, NULL derefs). Adds fuzzer and Meson build support.
- **New features:**

  - `ps/history-drop` (11 commits): Teaches `git history drop` to remove a commit and replay descendants onto its parent.
- **Bugfixes:**
  - `js/coverity-fixes-null-safety` (11 commits): Hardens code against NULL derefs (e.g., `shallow`, `bisect`, `pack-bitmap`).
