This is the second patch in Christian Couder’s three-patch series extending `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH` with a `fromAccepted` mode. It introduces an `enum allow_lazy_fetch` and a dedicated parser (`parse_allow_lazy_fetch_env()`) to replace the current boolean parsing of the environment variable. The enum currently defines two values—`LAZY_FETCH_NONE` and `LAZY_FETCH_ALL`—but the commit message makes it clear that a third value (`LAZY_FETCH_FROM_ACCEPTED`) will be added in patch 3/3. The change is purely preparatory: no behavior changes yet, only the plumbing needed for the upcoming feature. The patch touches `promisor-remote.c`, `promisor-remote.h`, and `setup.c`, and the diff is small and mechanical. No review feedback has appeared yet.
