This thread introduces a patch series by Christian Couder that replaces the earlier `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH=fromAccepted` proposal with a server-side configuration variable, `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, to mark repositories as trusted for lazy fetching. The goal is to address security concerns by shifting trust decisions to server operators, modeled after Git’s `safe.directory` mechanism. The series consists of six patches (v1) covering refactoring, reusable allowlist machinery, configuration reading, and integration, with no substantive technical review feedback yet beyond the unresolved security objection from brian m. carlson.

The series remains under review but has hit a **blocking issue**: Junio C Hamano reported that the patches failed to apply due to incorrect threading. The cover letter for this series was mistakenly threaded as a reply to the unrelated `fromAccepted` proposal, confusing the `b4` tool and preventing clean application. Junio emphasized that new series must either avoid threading unrelated patches together or use proper version numbering (e.g., `v2`) to signal iteration. Until this formatting issue is resolved, the series cannot proceed in the integration pipeline. No merge status is implied, and the series remains stalled pending a corrected resubmission. The core security objection and technical details of the proposal remain unchanged.
