This is the second patch in Justin Tobler’s 11-patch series converting `git-receive-pack` from `tmp_objdir` to ODB transactions. It is a pure refactoring that renames `fsync_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_fsync()` to align with the naming convention of other "files" ODB transaction helpers. The change is mechanical: the function’s logic is unchanged, but the conditional is slightly restructured for clarity, and the call site in `close_loose_object()` is updated to use the new name.

The patch touches only `object-file.c` (16 lines changed) and carries no behavioral change. It is uncontroversial and part of the broader ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt. All prior feedback on the series has been addressed in v4, and this patch carries Patrick’s Reviewed-by from earlier iterations. The series is now queued for `next` pending integration.
