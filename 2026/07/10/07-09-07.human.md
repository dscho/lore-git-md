This is a reply in the ongoing ODB abstraction series from Patrick Steinhardt, addressing a design question about object filters in `odb_for_each_object()`. The exchange clarifies the rationale behind the current interface: backends are allowed to ignore filters they cannot efficiently handle, leaving callers responsible for re-filtering results. Patrick argues that enforcing a common subset of supported filters would artificially constrain future backends, whose storage models may differ radically from today’s packed or loose backends. He notes that the only current caller (`git cat-file --batch-all-objects`) already handles manual filtering, so the awkwardness is contained. The response is matter-of-fact and closes the open design question; expect a v2 with an expanded commit message but no interface change.
