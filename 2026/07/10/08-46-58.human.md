This is a contributor housekeeping patch for the `contrib/b4` tooling. The patch adds a `change-id` trailer to Git’s `.b4-cover-template` to fix a workflow breakage with b4 0.15.2 and later. Without this trailer, b4 fails when users try to reroll or compare patch versions, producing a critical error because the sent tag lacks the expected `change-id` field.

The change is minimal—just one line added to the template—and matches the existing `base-commit` trailer format. The motivation is clear and practical, addressing a real pain point for users who rely on b4 to manage patch series on the mailing list.

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the patch and explicitly states it "looks good," calling the omission an oversight on his part. This is a straightforward, uncontroversial fix that should be safe to merge. No further discussion or revisions appear necessary.
