This thread is about a two-patch series by Paulius Zaleckas that makes submodule fetch errors configurable. The goal is to let users choose whether `git fetch --recurse-submodules` should fail hard or just warn when a submodule’s gitlink points to an unreachable OID, with the default (`fail`) keeping backwards compatibility.

The series is now at version 3, posted after substantive feedback on earlier versions. The first patch restructures error handling in `submodule.c` to defer failure decisions until after all fetch attempts, fixing a logic flaw where transient failures could abort the entire fetch prematurely. This is a correctness fix that doesn’t change user-facing behavior but sets up the configurable policy in the second patch. The second patch (not yet summarized here) will introduce the new `fetch.submoduleErrors` config key and `--submodule-errors` CLI option.

So far, the feedback on v2 was technical and engaged with edge cases, leading to the v3 changes. No maintainer approvals have landed yet, but the series is under active review. The work touches the submodule subsystem, fetch plumbing, and test coverage, with no open questions remaining from the prior round.
