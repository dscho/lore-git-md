This is the second patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s priority-queue performance series, now withdrawn by the author. The patch aimed to optimize Git’s `prio_queue_get()` operation by replacing the standard sift-down algorithm with a cascade-down approach, reducing comparison counts by 14–17% in benchmarks. However, follow-up testing by René Scharfe on Apple silicon revealed a puzzling 1% wall-clock slowdown despite the reduction in comparisons, suggesting hardware-specific microarchitectural penalties. Kristofer, lacking Apple hardware to investigate further, concludes the optimization is not worth the complexity and withdraws the patch, calling the change "pure noise" given current comparator costs. The series is now effectively abandoned unless future use cases make the comparator function significantly more expensive.
