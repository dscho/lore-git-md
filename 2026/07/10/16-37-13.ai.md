This is the second patch in Justin Tobler’s 11-patch v4 series converting `git-receive-pack` from `tmp_objdir` to ODB transactions. It is a mechanical refactoring that renames `fsync_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_fsync()` to align with the naming convention of other "files" ODB transaction helpers. The patch also restructures the conditional logic for clarity, moving the null-check for `transaction` and `transaction->objdir` to the top of the function. No behavior changes are introduced; the fsync semantics (writeout-only vs full hardware flush) remain identical.

The change is uncontroversial and carries Patrick Steinhardt’s Reviewed-by from v3. It touches only `object-file.c` (16 lines changed) and is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt. The new name reflects the function’s expanded role in future patches, where it will handle both loose objects and packfiles within ODB transactions. The restructuring improves readability by making the null-check explicit and separating it from the fsync logic.
