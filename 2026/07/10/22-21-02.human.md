This is the maintainer’s review of the second patch in Paulius Zaleckas’ v3 series that adds `fetch.submoduleErrors` and `--submodule-errors=(fail|warn)` to let users opt for non-fatal submodule fetch errors.

Junio’s feedback is narrowly focused on the propagation logic in `add_options_to_argv()`.  He points out that the helper currently only forwards `--submodule-errors=warn` to child fetches, but never forwards `--submodule-errors=fail`.  That means if a user explicitly sets `--submodule-errors=fail` on the command line, the setting is not passed down to recursive or `--all`/`--multiple` fetches, so those child processes revert to the config default (which might be `warn`).  Junio asks whether this asymmetry is intentional or whether the command-line override should always be forwarded, regardless of its value.  The question is purely about consistency and edge-case behavior; the rest of the patch—config parsing, documentation, tests, and error routing—is not under scrutiny.
