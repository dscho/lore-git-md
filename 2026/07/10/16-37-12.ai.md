This is the first patch in Justin Tobler’s v4 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions. It is a pure refactoring patch that renames the internal function `prepare_loose_object_transaction()` to `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` to better reflect its expanded future role: the same temporary directory will soon be used for both loose objects and packfiles. The change is mechanical (three call sites in `object-file.c`) and carries no behavior change. The commit message clearly explains the motivation for the rename, and the patch itself is uncontroversial. All prior feedback on this patch has been addressed, and it carries Patrick Steinhardt’s Reviewed-by from v3. This patch is a prerequisite for later patches in the series that introduce packfile support in the same transaction.
