This is the third patch in Justin Tobler’s v4 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`. It refactors the "files" ODB backend by embedding the transaction-flush logic directly into `odb_transaction_files_commit()`, eliminating the standalone `flush_loose_object_transaction()` helper. The change is purely mechanical: the same hardware-flush and object-directory-migration logic is preserved, but the function is now inlined where it belongs. This prepares the codebase for future patches that will extend transactions to handle packfiles (not just loose objects), making the old "loose object" naming misleading. The patch touches only `object-file.c` (64 lines changed) and carries Patrick Steinhardt’s Reviewed-by from v3. No behavior changes; the series remains a pure refactoring.
