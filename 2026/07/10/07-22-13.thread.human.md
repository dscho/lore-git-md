The change is minimal—just one line in the template—and was quickly acknowledged as an oversight by Patrick Steinhardt, who gave it a Reviewed-by.

There are no open questions or objections, and the patch is uncontroversial. Since it touches only a template file and introduces no new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk changes, no test coverage was needed. The thread is effectively resolved, and the fix should appear in a future Git release.
