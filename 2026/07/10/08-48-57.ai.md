This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-part series introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. Specifically, this is patch 5/8 in v2, a clean-up step that drops the `_1` suffix from two internal bitmap-opening functions (`open_pack_bitmap_1()` and `open_midx_bitmap_1()`) after the preceding patch consolidated their logic into `open_bitmap_for_source()`.

The patch is purely mechanical: it renames the two functions to `open_pack_bitmap()` and `open_midx_bitmap()`, updates all call sites (seven lines changed in `pack-bitmap.c`), and leaves behavior unchanged. The `_1` suffix was a historical artifact that no longer serves a purpose after the refactoring in patch 4/8. No review feedback has surfaced on this patch yet, but it is a straightforward follow-up to the earlier consolidation and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying and generalizing bitmap-handling logic.

This patch is safe to review in isolation, but its context is the broader ODB abstraction effort. It prepares the codebase for patch 6/8, which introduces per-source bitmap operations, and ultimately for patch 8/8, which refactors `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` to use ODB-mediated filtering.
