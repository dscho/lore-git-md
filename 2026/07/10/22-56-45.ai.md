This is a **post-merge discussion** about patch presentation style in the recently merged commit-graph regression fix. Taylor Blau responds to Junio C Hamano’s caution about test restructuring by defending the practice of splitting a failing test and its fix into separate commits, especially when either the test setup or the bugfix is non-trivial.

Taylor argues that separating the test and fix improves clarity in two scenarios: (1) when the test is complex but the complexity is incidental to the bug, and (2) when the bugfix itself is complex, because the two-commit structure cleanly demonstrates that the bug existed before and is resolved afterward. He notes that this has been his preferred style for past series and asks whether Junio feels strongly enough to formalize the guidance in `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` or `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`. The exchange remains a stylistic discussion; no code changes are proposed or required.
