This is the final patch (4/4) in Pablo Sabater’s **v8 series** implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `` `git log --graph` ``. The series is now complete and ready for final review.

The patch adds the visualization logic that indents visual roots (commits with excluded parents) to prevent them from appearing vertically adjacent to unrelated commits. It introduces a new `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state to connect indented roots to their children, tracks indentation depth via `visual_root_depth` and `visual_root_cascade` flags, and explicitly excludes merge parents from indentation. The lookahead buffer (introduced in patch 3/4) ensures the graph renderer can peek at future commits to determine whether cascading indentation is needed.

The test coverage is comprehensive: the new `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` script (473 lines) exercises edge cases including `--max-count`, `--max-count-oldest`, `--first-parent`, filtered parents, boundary commits, and octopus merges. All failing test cases from earlier versions now pass. The patch is compatible with in-flight topics (Jeff King’s `revs->commits` removal and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion`) and preserves existing behavior for `--reverse` and `--left-right`.

The implementation is technically sound and addresses the last known edge case (inconsistent lookahead population) resolved in patch 2/4. The redundant-indentation edge case (when filtered parents are present) is documented as NEEDSWORK but deemed acceptable for now.
