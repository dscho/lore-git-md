This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s five-part v8 series introducing `git history squash`. It is a pure refactoring that extracts a small helper, `first_parent_tree_oid()`, to centralize the logic for resolving a commit’s first-parent tree (or the empty tree for a root commit). The helper is then used in three places in `builtin/history.c` that previously repeated the same parse-and-copy dance. There are no behavior changes—this is strictly about code hygiene and maintainability, setting the stage for the functional patches that follow. The patch is small, focused, and ready to merge.
