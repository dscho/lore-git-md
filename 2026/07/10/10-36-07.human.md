This is the second patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s two-patch v3 series fixing a quadratic performance regression in the reftable backend. It removes the `suppress_deletions` flag from the merged iterator and moves tombstone suppression to the reftable backend’s call sites, where prefix and refname bounds are available. This allows iteration to terminate early when tombstones exceed the relevant bound, restoring linear performance.

The patch touches `refs/reftable-backend.c` (core logic), `reftable/reftable-stack.h` (new `suppress_deletions` field in `struct reftable_stack_options`), and `reftable/stack.c` (removal of Git’s internal use of the flag). It adds explicit deletion checks to all ref and log iteration paths, ensuring tombstones are skipped only after bounds checks. The on-disk format is unchanged, and the fix is narrowly targeted at the performance regression.

Downstream users like libgit2 can still control tombstone suppression via the new `reftable_stack_options` field, preserving their current behavior while future-proofing the API. The perf test in patch 1/2 shows the fix reduces runtime from ~14s to ~0.2s in tombstone-heavy scenarios. The series is well-motivated, addresses a real-world issue observed by brian m. carlson, and appears ready for integration.
