This thread is about a targeted bugfix for a double-free regression in Git's object-file subsystem, introduced in February 2026. The patch, authored by Patrick Steinhardt, removes an erroneous `odb_read_stream_close()` call in the error path of `stream_object_signature()`, preventing a crash when reading truncated loose blobs. The fix includes a new test in `t/t1450-fsck.sh` to verify that `git rev-list --verify-objects` detects hash mismatches in such cases.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the patch and confirmed it looks correct, though he raised a mild disagreement about the security implications. While Patrick's commit message dismissed the issue as outside Git's threat model, Peff noted that the bug could theoretically violate Git's stated goal of safely cloning from a local untrusted `.git` repository. However, he acknowledged that the `git(1)` SECURITY man page already frames this as a goal rather than a guarantee, and the documented mitigation (serving from an untrusted user) remains valid. No changes to the patch were requested, and the discussion ended there.

The patch is under review and merges cleanly to `master`. There are no open questions or loose ends, and the fix is considered complete. The only remaining point of discussion was the security framing, which was resolved without requiring further action.
