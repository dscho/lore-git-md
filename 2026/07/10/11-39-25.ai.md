This is the first patch in the `js/coverity-fixes-v2` series, a bugfix targeting a NULL-dereference vulnerability in `diffcore-break.c`. The patch adds a guard to the inner loop of `diffcore_merge_broken()` to skip queue entries that were previously NULLed by the outer loop, mirroring an existing pattern. The issue is rare but real: it requires at least two broken pairs whose peers survive rename/copy detection and appear later in the queue. The fix is minimal (two lines), well-scoped, and uncontroversial. Junio previously reviewed the v1 version and confirmed it as "obviously correct." This patch is part of a larger, now-ready-for-merge series addressing Coverity-identified NULL-dereference issues across eleven files. No new behavior is introduced; the change is purely defensive. The commit message clearly names the function, the specific condition being guarded against, and the practical scenario that could trigger the bug.
