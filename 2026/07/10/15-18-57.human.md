This is a review comment on the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s seven-patch v18 series implementing `git branch --delete-merged`. The series is now implementation-complete and ready for merging, with all design questions resolved.

Phillip Wood’s feedback here is narrow and technical: he points out that the patch introduces a local boolean variable (`skip_unmerged`) derived from the `flags` parameter, then later modifies the code to check that boolean instead of the flag directly. This creates two sources of truth for the same condition, which could lead to maintenance problems if the flag is later modified but the boolean is not updated. He recommends keeping `flags` as the single source of truth by replacing all local boolean uses with direct flag checks (e.g., `flags & DELETE_BRANCH_SKIP_UNMERGED`).

The issue is identical to one he raised in patch 2/7 and anticipates in patch 4/7. It is a mechanical fix that does not affect the patch’s functionality but improves long-term maintainability. The series is otherwise ready for merging once this pattern is corrected across all three patches.
