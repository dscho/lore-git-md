This is the v8 submission of Pablo Sabater’s feature series adding cascading indentation to visual roots in `` `git log --graph` ``. The series now consists of four patches, up from three in v7, after splitting out a helper function in `revision.c` to address a test breakage with `--max-count-oldest`.

The core goal remains unchanged: visual roots (commits that appear root-like because their parents are filtered out) are now indented by two characters per level, except the first in a cascade, and connected to their children with an edge on a new row. The lookahead buffer introduced in v7 is retained, ensuring commits are fully simplified before the graph layer inspects them. This version adds `next_commit_to_show()` in `revision.c` to ensure the lookahead buffer is populated from the same source regardless of `--max-count` or `--max-count-oldest`, fixing a test failure in `t4202`.

Patch 1/4 is the same test infrastructure cleanup from earlier versions, moving `check_graph` into `t/lib-log-graph.sh`. Patch 2/4 is new: it introduces `next_commit_to_show()` to centralize the logic for retrieving the next commit to show, replacing the conditional that was causing the `--max-count-oldest` breakage. Patch 3/4 is the lookahead buffer implementation, now with `ARRAY_SIZE()` and an assertion in `graph_pop_lookahead()` as suggested by Chandra Pratap. Patch 4/4 is the visualization logic, unchanged from v7 except for expanded test coverage (now including `--max-count` and `--max-count-oldest` scenarios).

The series is technically sound and addresses the last known functional issue. The lookahead buffer is small, deterministic, and compatible with other ongoing work (Jeff King’s `revs->commits` removal, Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion`). The redundant-indentation edge case is still documented as NEEDSWORK but is not a blocker. Junio Hamano’s concern about test brittleness remains, but the lookahead buffer’s deterministic behavior should mitigate it.

This version is ready for final review. The only remaining loose ends are documentation of the visualization rules and the redundant-indentation edge case, neither of which blocks merging. Junio and Phillip Wood’s review of the visualization logic (patch 4/4) is the next step.
