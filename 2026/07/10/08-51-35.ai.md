This is the first patch in Christian Couder’s three-patch series introducing a `fromAccepted` option to `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH`. It refactors the promisor-remote subsystem to extract the lazy-fetching logic into a new `lazy_fetch_objects()` helper function.

The patch is a pure refactoring with no intended behavior change. It consolidates the `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH` environment-variable check into a single up-front call instead of repeating it for each promisor remote, and it separates the logic that decides which remotes to try from the logic that validates whether unfetched objects are promisor objects. The `try_promisor_remotes()` function is also converted from returning an `int` to returning a `bool` for clarity, and its return value is now documented.

The changes are confined to `promisor-remote.c` and lay the groundwork for the subsequent patches that introduce the `fromAccepted` option. The refactoring appears clean and well-motivated, with clear separation of concerns and no observable behavior change beyond the mechanical consolidation of the environment-variable check.
