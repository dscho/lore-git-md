This is a brief maintainer update on the `the_repository` removal series. Junio C Hamano confirms that the integration conflicts blocking Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series were caused by a mismerge in `setup.c`—specifically, a hunk that calls `apply_and_export_relative_gitdir()` and `repo_get_git_dir()` was either omitted or misplaced during conflict resolution. With that resolved, the series is now back in the integration branches (`seen` and `jch`) and should proceed toward `master` in the next cycle. No new technical issues are introduced; this is purely a mechanical merge fix. The latent packed-refs timeout bug (zero-initialized instead of 1000 ms) remains slated for v2, but the current merge resolution does not affect that.
