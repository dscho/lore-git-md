This is the fourth patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series (v8 4/5). It refactors the sequencer’s squash-message helpers into reusable functions and moves the `todo_item_flags` enum to the header so other commands can produce the same template format as `git rebase -i --autosquash`.

The patch extracts three static helpers from `sequencer.c`—`add_squash_combination_header`, `add_squash_message_header`, and `squash_subject_comment_len`—and exposes them in `sequencer.h`. It also relocates the `todo_item_flags` enum (including the `TODO_REPLACE_FIXUP_MSG` flag) to the header, allowing callers to distinguish `amend!` commits from plain `fixup!` ones after `todo_list_rearrange_squash` rearranges the todo list. The next patch in the series will reuse these helpers to give `git history squash --reedit-message` an editor template that exactly matches `git rebase -i --autosquash`.
