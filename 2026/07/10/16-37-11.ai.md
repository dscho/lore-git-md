This is the v4 submission of Justin Tobler’s 11-patch series converting `git-receive-pack` from `tmp_objdir` to ODB transactions, completing the infrastructure refactoring for ODB-backend agnosticism. The series is now functionally complete and addresses all feedback from v3: the safety concern in `odb_transaction_begin()` (conditional assignment to guard `odb->transaction`), the error-handling gap in `odb_transaction_env()` (propagating `odb_transaction_files_prepare()` failures), and the latent transaction-leak in `odb_transaction_commit()` (fixed by clearing the transaction even on failure). The two deferred design questions—header dependency (`odb/transaction.h` vs `odb/source.h`) and explicit `odb_transaction_abort()` calls in error paths—remain deferred to follow-up work, as previously agreed.

The changes since v3 are minimal and targeted: commit message line breaks adjusted for readability, defensive programming in `odb_transaction_begin()`, and proper error propagation in `odb_transaction_env()`. No behavioral changes were introduced; the series remains a pure refactoring with identical quarantine semantics. All 11 patches carry Reviewed-by trailers from Patrick Steinhardt (ODB subsystem owner), and the series is queued for `next` pending integration. The heaviest changes are in `object-file.c` (161 lines) and `builtin/receive-pack.c` (69 lines), with the rest being mechanical plumbing (renames, error propagation, flag plumbing). Test coverage relies on the existing `receive-pack` test suite, which exercises the quarantine flow and child processes (`git-index-pack`, `git-unpack-objects`).
