This is a surface-level review comment on Farid Zakaria’s v2 patch for the `git rebase --autosquash` empty-commit handling fix. The reviewer, Michael Montalbo, notes a minor test-script hygiene issue: the patch uses bare `grep` and `! grep` in its test assertions, but the project is moving toward requiring `test_grep` and `test_grep !` instead. These wrappers provide better error messages and will soon be enforced by the test-linting infrastructure. The comment is purely mechanical—no logic or behavior is questioned—and the change is trivial to apply. The patch itself remains a well-motivated, localized bugfix that respects the `--empty` option when a `fixup!` or `squash!` commit cancels out its target.
