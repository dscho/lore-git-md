This is the tenth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s eleven-patch bugfix series addressing NULL-dereference issues identified by Coverity. The patch targets the bisect subsystem, specifically the `bisect_start()` function in `builtin/bisect.c`.

Junio C Hamano’s review raises a question about the real-world plausibility of the edge case the patch addresses: `refs_resolve_ref_unsafe()` returning NULL while `repo_get_oid("HEAD")` succeeds. The scenario is described as a detached bare OID that the ref backend (notably reftable) cannot resolve symbolically, but Junio expresses skepticism and requests a test to demonstrate the bug and prevent future regressions. The fix itself—setting `head` to the string literal `"HEAD"` as a fallback—is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ defensive programming goals, but the lack of a concrete reproduction case leaves the necessity of the guard slightly ambiguous.

The patch is part of a larger, uncontroversial series that has otherwise received positive feedback for its thoroughness and correctness. Junio’s request for a test does not challenge the patch’s technical approach but reflects a preference for regression coverage when edge cases are subtle or hard to trigger. The author will need to either provide a test or clarify why the scenario is impossible to demonstrate, though the fix itself is unlikely to be dropped—it is a minimal, low-risk guard that does not alter normal behavior. No other objections or design disagreements have been raised.
