This is a contributor housekeeping patch for the `contrib/b4` tooling, now with an explicit approval from Patrick Steinhardt.

The patch adds the missing `change-id: {{ change_id }}` trailer to Git’s `.b4-cover-template` so that b4 0.15.2+ can parse the sent tag it creates. Without this line, b4 commands that try to read the sent tag (for rerolling or comparing versions) fail with a critical error. The change is a single-line addition that mirrors the existing `base-commit` trailer, requires no new symbols or on-disk format changes, and is already acknowledged by Patrick as an oversight in the original template. No further discussion or objections have appeared.
