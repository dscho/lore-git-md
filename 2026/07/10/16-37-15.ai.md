This is the fourth patch in Justin Tobler’s v4 series converting `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions. It removes redundant validation from the "files" backend’s `odb_transaction_files_begin()` function: the generic ODB transaction layer already ensures only one transaction can exist at a time, so the backend’s own check is unnecessary. The change is a clean-up that touches only `object-file.c` (removing four lines) and `object-file.h` (updating the function’s docstring to remove the "already pending" caveat). No behavior changes; the patch is purely mechanical and uncontroversial, carrying Patrick Steinhardt’s Reviewed-by from earlier iterations. The series remains on track for `next` integration.
