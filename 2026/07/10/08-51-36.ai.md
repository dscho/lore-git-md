This is the second patch in Christian Couder's three-patch series introducing a `fromAccepted` option to `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH`. The patch touches three files: `promisor-remote.c` (where the new `parse_allow_lazy_fetch_env()` function is defined), `promisor-remote.h` (where the enum and function are declared), and `setup.c` (where the enum is used to replace the previous boolean check). The refactoring is mechanical and does not change behavior; it simply replaces `git_env_bool()` calls with the new enum-based parser. The error message for invalid values is updated to reflect the new enum-based parsing, but the logic remains the same.
