Omri notes that in workflows involving frequent rebasing (common in many teams), the author date can appear inconsistent or misleading, as it reflects when the commit was originally created rather than when it was applied to the current branch. His teams often override this default to show the committer date instead, which they find more intuitive for tracking when changes actually landed in the main branch. He asks whether the current default is a legacy decision or if there are workflows where the author date is genuinely more useful.
