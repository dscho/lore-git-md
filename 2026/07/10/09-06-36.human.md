This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series, now at v8. It implements the `--reedit-message` feature so that users can combine the messages of all commits in the squashed range, not just the oldest one.

The patch gathers every commit’s message, builds an editor template identical to what `git rebase -i --autosquash` shows, and feeds the range through `todo_list_rearrange_squash()` so that `fixup!`, `squash!`, and `amend!` commits are grouped under their targets rather than shown in chronological order. The result is a template that exactly matches interactive rebase’s autosquash behavior: `fixup!` messages are fully commented out, `squash!` bodies are preserved with only the marker subject commented, and `amend!` replaces its target’s message unless a `squash!` already folded into that target.

The documentation is updated to explain the new template format, and the test script (`t/t3455-history-squash.sh`) grows by 210 lines to verify the grouping logic, message preservation, and abort-on-empty behavior. This patch completes the series; all prior feedback has been addressed, and Junio’s “Will replace” sign-off on v7 signals the series is ready for the next release.
