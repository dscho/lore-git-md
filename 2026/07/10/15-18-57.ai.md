This is a review comment on the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s seven-patch v18 series implementing `git branch --delete-merged`. The series is now implementation-complete and ready for merging, with all design questions resolved.

Phillip Wood’s feedback here is narrow and technical: he reiterates a maintenance concern first raised in patch 2/7 about the flags-based API refactoring. The issue is that the patch splits the `flags` variable into local booleans (`force`, `quiet`, `skip_unmerged`), creating two sources of truth. This risks future regressions if the flags are modified later but the local booleans are not updated. Phillip recommends replacing all local boolean uses with direct flag checks (e.g., `flags & DELETE_BRANCH_SKIP_UNMERGED`) to keep `flags` as the single source of truth. This is a straightforward fix that does not affect the patch’s functionality but improves long-term maintainability.

The comment applies equally to patches 2/7, 3/7 (this patch), and 4/7, all of which introduce or use the flags-based API. The fix is mechanical and does not require rethinking the series’ design. Once addressed, the series remains ready for merging. No other issues are raised in this email.
