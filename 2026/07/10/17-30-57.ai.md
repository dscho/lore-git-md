This is the documentation patch (3/3) in the v2 series fixing race conditions in HTTP daemon test helpers. It adds a new section to `t/README` titled "Writing concurrency-safe helpers" that generalizes the atomic idioms introduced in patches 1 and 2. The guidance explains why non-atomic shell operations (`test -f` followed by `rm` or `touch`) are unsafe in concurrent contexts, presents the two safe alternatives (`mkdir` for electing a winner, `mv` for consuming a one-shot marker), and cites Git’s lockfile machinery (`O_CREAT|O_EXCL`) and `make_symlink()` in `t/test-lib.sh` as precedents. The patch also adds a brief pointer in `t/lib-httpd.sh` linking to the new documentation.

The content is clear, technically precise, and directly addresses the anti-pattern that caused the observed flakes in `t5616.47`. By documenting the idioms in `t/README` rather than scattering comments in individual scripts, it makes the guidance discoverable for future test authors. The patch is minimal, self-contained, and unlikely to be controversial, as it merely codifies the fixes already applied in the series. Junio C Hamano called this patch a "nice finishing touch" in his v1 review and did not request any changes, so it remains unchanged in v2. The series as a whole now looks complete and ready for integration.
