This is the resolution of the design question in the final patch of Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series. The patch converts `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, completing the v3 series that advances the ODB abstraction effort.

Junio C Hamano’s substantive review question — whether to add an explicit `odb_transaction_abort()` call in error paths where `execute_commands()` returns early — is now resolved. Justin agrees that an explicit abort would improve clarity and consistency, especially for non-file-based backends that might not rely on `atexit()` cleanup. However, he defers the change to a follow-up series, noting that the current behavior (relying on `atexit()` cleanup) is functionally correct and matches the old `tmp_objdir` behavior. The deferral is pragmatic: the series is otherwise complete, has strong approval from Patrick Steinhardt (the ODB subsystem owner), and the explicit abort is not a blocker for integration.
