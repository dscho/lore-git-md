This is a documentation patch, the first in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-part v4 series to standardize the `replay.refAction` configuration variable across Git’s man pages. The patch adds the missing `include::config/replay.adoc[]` directive to both `git-replay(1)` and `git-config(1)`, ensuring the `replay.refAction` documentation appears in both places. The change is purely mechanical: two lines added, no text altered. All prior feedback on placement and conditional linking has been resolved in earlier iterations, and this patch carries forward the agreed structure. The series is now uncontroversial and ready for integration.
