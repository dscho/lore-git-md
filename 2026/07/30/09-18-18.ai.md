This is the fifth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`. It adds a concise preamble to the DESCRIPTION section that defines the minimal trailer format (key–value pairs separated by a colon) and introduces the terms “trailer” and “trailer block” right after the introductory example. The patch also standardizes the term “blank line” (empty or whitespace-only) so it can be reused consistently throughout the rest of the man page. The change is purely explanatory and has no effect on code behavior.
