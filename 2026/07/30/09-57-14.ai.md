This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-part documentation series (v4) standardizing the `replay.refAction` configuration variable across `git-config(1)` and `git-replay(1)`. The patch mechanically converts the existing bullet-list markup for `replay.refAction` into a nested description list (`;;` delimiter) to align with Git’s documentation conventions, removes unnecessary indentation, and replaces the inline-verbatim `git replay` with a conditional cross-reference to `git-replay(1)`—avoiding a self-link when the text appears in the `git-replay(1)` man page itself. The only substantive change from v3 is a phrasing tweak in the cross-reference text, replacing the redundant “for … for” with “See `--ref-action` in linkgit:git-replay[1] for details” as suggested by Toon Claes. The patch touches `Documentation/config/replay.adoc` and adds a conditional include guard in `Documentation/git-replay.adoc`.

The patch is purely cosmetic and follows the structural precedents established in earlier commits (e7b3a768, 336ac90c) for conditional linking and description-list formatting. All prior feedback—from Junio Hamano on list structure, Patrick Steinhardt on AsciiDoc syntax, and Toon Claes on phrasing—has been addressed. The series is queued under topic `kh/doc-replay-config` and is marked “Will merge to 'next'” in Junio’s 2025-07-10 “What’s cooking” report, indicating it is ready for integration testing.
