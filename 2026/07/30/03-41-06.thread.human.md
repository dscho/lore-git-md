This thread is about a small but safety-focused change to `git stash`: preventing time-based selectors like `stash@{2.days.ago}` from being used with `drop` and `pop`, where they can silently remove more stash entries than the user expects. Junio Hamano posted the two-patch series, which is now under review.

The first patch adds a `stash_idx` field to track the positional index of a stash entry, and the second uses that field to reject time-based selectors in `drop` and `pop` while keeping them working in other subcommands like `show` and `apply`. The implementation is complete and touches `builtin/stash.c`, the documentation, and the test suite.

Ben Knoble raised the first review comment, asking why the code imposes a hard-coded limit (100 million) on the numeric index when converting `@{n}` to an integer. His question is technical and narrowly scoped—he’s not challenging the overall design, just probing whether the limit is necessary or might constrain valid use cases. The series is otherwise uncontroversial and likely to proceed once this detail is clarified. No other feedback has appeared yet.
