This thread began with Junio C Hamano proposing a two-patch series to make `git stash drop` and `git stash pop` reject time-based stash selectors like `stash@{2.days.ago}`. The goal was to prevent users from accidentally removing more stash entries than intended—a potential footgun in the existing interface. The patches added a `stash_idx` field to track positional indices and used it to block time-based selectors in those subcommands while allowing them elsewhere (e.g., `show`, `apply`).

After review, Junio retracted the series entirely. His retraction email explained that further testing revealed the reflog expiration machinery already limits removal to a single stash entry, even with time-based selectors. The original safety concern was thus unfounded, and the proposed change was unnecessary. No code from the series was merged, and the thread is now closed. Ben Knoble had raised a technical question about a hard-coded numeric limit in the implementation, but this was resolved before the retraction.
