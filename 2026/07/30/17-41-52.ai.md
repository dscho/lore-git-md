This is the sixth patch in the RFC v2 series introducing `--drop-filtered` to `git repack` for partial clones. It implements the two critical safety guards that were blocking concerns from the v1 review: preventing drops during ongoing operations (merge, rebase, cherry-pick, etc.) and refusing to drop blobs referenced by the current index.

The patch adds 47 lines to `builtin/repack.c`, using `wt_status_get_state()` to detect in-progress operations and `repo_read_index()` to scan the index for any blob in the `drop_oids` set. Both checks are skipped for bare repositories. The error messages are specific: "cannot be used while another operation is in progress" and "cannot drop '<path>' (<oid>): it is referenced by the current index". The test script (`t/t7706-repack-drop-filtered.sh`) adds two new tests that verify the guards work: one creates a conflicting merge and confirms the command fails, the other stages a large blob, marks it as promisor, and verifies the drop is refused.

This is a well-motivated, narrowly scoped patch that directly addresses the safety concerns raised in v1. The implementation is clean, the tests are thorough, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale. No new loose ends are introduced; the guards are complete and ready for review.
