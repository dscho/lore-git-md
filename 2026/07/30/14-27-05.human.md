Toon Claes steps in to clarify a key design point that Justin Tobler raised in his earlier review: why the `should_use_loose_object_map()` guard was moved from the repository-level function (`repo_read_loose_object_map()`) into the per-source function (`loose_object_map_load()`).

Toon explains that the change is necessary to support the new lazy-loading design introduced in this patch. This addresses Justin’s concern about whether each source might answer the guard differently—it won’t, because the guard is now consistently applied at both the repository and source levels.
