This is a maintainer response to an RFC series proposing a breaking change for Git 3.0: restricting hex object IDs to lowercase only. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, is pushing back on the motivation and asking for a stronger case before the project considers the change.

Junio acknowledges the technical completeness of the series but questions whether the benefit justifies the risk. He explicitly rejects the argument that the change is acceptable merely because it might not break many users ("just because we might be able to without harming too many users"). Instead, he wants to see a clear, long-term benefit that outweighs the disruption of a breaking change.

The thread remains in the RFC stage, with no decision made. The ball is now back in brian m. carlson’s court to either strengthen the justification or abandon the proposal. This is likely to be a sticking point—breaking changes in Git 3.0 are rare and require broad consensus, and Junio’s skepticism suggests the bar is high. If no compelling benefit emerges, the series may stall here.
