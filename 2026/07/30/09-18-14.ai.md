This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`. It removes the RFC 822 email header analogy from the command's introduction, replacing it with a direct description of trailers as key–value metadata. The change is part of a broader effort to simplify and clarify the documentation by eliminating unnecessary technical references that might confuse users unfamiliar with email RFCs.

The patch deletes the initial comparison to RFC 822 and a later disclaimer about differences between trailers and email headers, while preserving a single, more neutral reference to email-style line folding. The commit message explains that the RFC 822 framing was historically tied to the `--fixes` trailer feature but is no longer needed for modern users. The v4 update adjusts terminology ("trailer metadata" instead of "trailers metadata") to align with later patches in the series.

This is a refactoring of the documentation's conceptual framing rather than a behavior change. The patch is uncontroversial and part of a larger, already-merged effort to improve the man page's clarity and precision. No further changes are expected to this specific commit.
