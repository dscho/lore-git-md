This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git interpret-trailers`. The patch removes the RFC 822 email-header comparison from the command’s introduction, replacing it with a simpler description of trailers as key–value metadata. The motivation is straightforward: most users don’t know or care about email RFCs, so the analogy is more confusing than helpful. The change keeps one passing reference to email line-folding (which is genuinely similar) but drops the disclaimer that trailers don’t share other RFC 822 features, since the comparison is no longer front-and-center. The commit message explains the history—this RFC 822 framing dates back to the 2013 `--fixes` trailer thread—and the v4 iteration adjusts terminology to match later patches in the series. The patch is a clean, uncontroversial cleanup that sets the stage for the more detailed format explanation that follows in patch 2.
