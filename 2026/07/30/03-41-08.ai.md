This is the second patch in Junio C Hamano’s two-patch series that tightens the safety of `git stash drop` and `git stash pop` by rejecting time-based stash selectors (e.g., `stash@{2.days.ago}`). The patch implements the core behavior change: it modifies `get_stash_info_assert()` to check the `stash_idx` field (introduced in patch 1/2) and fail early if the selector is time-based rather than positional. The documentation is updated to clarify that only positional selectors (e.g., `stash@{4}`) are accepted for `drop` and `pop`, and new tests verify the rejection of time-based selectors. The change is a straightforward safety improvement that addresses a known footgun in the interface.
