This is the seventh patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`. It adds a concrete failure-case example that illustrates the strict key format (ASCII alphanumeric + hyphen only) introduced in the previous patch. The example shows a three-line “trailer block” where only the last line (`Reviewed-by`) is a valid key; because the first two lines (`Skapad-på` and `Hash-in-v6.11`) contain non-ASCII or non-hyphen characters, the entire block is rejected. The accompanying prose spells out why the block fails to satisfy either of the two trailer-block definition cases, reinforcing the key-format rule and the requirement that at least one Git-generated or user-configured trailer must be present for case (ii). The patch is purely illustrative, with no code or behavioral changes.
