This is a retraction of Junio C Hamano’s two-patch series that proposed rejecting time-based stash selectors (e.g., `stash@{2.days.ago}`) in `git stash drop` and `git stash pop`. The series was under review but is now withdrawn.

The original motivation was to prevent a potential footgun: time-based selectors could silently remove more stash entries than the user expected. However, Junio discovered during further testing that the reflog expiration machinery already ensures only a single stash entry is removed, even with time-based selectors. The "collateral damage" concern was unfounded, making the safety improvement unnecessary. No alternative approach is proposed; the series is simply dropped.

The retraction affects `builtin/stash.c`, `Documentation/git-stash.adoc`, and `t/t3903-stash.sh`, though no code from the series was merged. The thread is now closed with no further action expected.
