This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git interpret-trailers`. It adds a short “OTHER RULES” section that explicitly documents how comment lines (governed by `core.commentString`) are handled. The commit message explains that this fills a gap in the man page’s coverage of the trailer format, even though the behavior itself is long-standing and unlikely to surprise users. The v4 iteration softens the motivation—acknowledging that the point is more about completeness than urgent need—and ties the change to the earlier patch that clarified the command works on tag messages as well. The patch is purely editorial and does not alter code behavior.
