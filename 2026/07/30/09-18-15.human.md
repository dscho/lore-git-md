This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`. It replaces the vague term “trailer lines” with the more precise “trailer metadata” in the command’s introductory paragraph. The change aligns the opening description with the glossary definition (“key-value metadata”) and removes the last implicit comparison to email headers, reinforcing that trailers are structured data rather than free-form text. The commit message explains the reasoning behind the singular “trailer metadata” over the plural “trailers metadata,” noting that it reads more naturally while still conveying plurality. This patch is a small but deliberate step in the series’ broader effort to standardize terminology and improve conceptual clarity.
