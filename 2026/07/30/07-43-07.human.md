This is the first review reply in Junio C Hamano’s two-patch series that makes `git stash drop` and `git stash pop` reject time-based selectors like `stash@{2.days.ago}`. The reviewer, Ben Knoble, raises a surface-level question about the patch’s implementation.

Ben notes that the new `stash_idx` field in `struct stash_info` is not yet used beyond rejecting invalid entries (which is the series’ goal), so the patch is purely preparatory plumbing. His only technical comment is about the hard-coded limit of 100,000,000 (`1e8`) in the positional index parser: he wonders why such a large but arbitrary cap exists, given that even 32-bit integers can comfortably hold values far beyond it. The question is well-placed but minor; it does not challenge the patch’s correctness or motivation, and the series as a whole remains uncontroversial and ready for integration.
