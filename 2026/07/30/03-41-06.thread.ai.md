Subject: [PATCH 0/2] stash: reject time-based stash selectors in "drop" and "pop"
Series version: v1 (retracted)
Originating author: Junio C Hamano
Thread type: patch series (withdrawn)

Core problem or goal
Tighten the safety of `git stash drop` and `git stash pop` by rejecting time-based stash selectors (e.g., `stash@{2.days.ago}`), which were perceived as a footgun due to potential silent removal of multiple stash entries. The goal was abandoned after discovering that the reflog expiration machinery already limits removal to a single entry, making the safety improvement unnecessary.

Current status
Retracted. The two-patch series (adding a `stash_idx` field to `struct stash_info` and rejecting time-based selectors in `drop`/`pop`) was under review but withdrawn after Junio determined the original concern was unfounded. No code from the series was merged, and no further action is expected.

Key technical details
- Files touched (proposed): `builtin/stash.c`, `Documentation/git-stash.adoc`, `t/t3903-stash.sh`.
- New symbol (proposed): `stash_idx` field in `struct stash_info` (0 for latest stash, numeric index for positional selectors, -1 for invalid/non-positional references).
- Behavior change (proposed): Reject time-based selectors in `drop`/`pop` while allowing them in other subcommands (`show`, `apply`). Positional selectors (e.g., `stash@{4}`) would remain accepted.
- On-disk format: Unchanged (proposed changes were behavioral only).
- Test coverage (proposed): New tests in `t/t3903-stash.sh` to verify rejection of time-based selectors in `drop`/`pop` and continued acceptance of positional selectors.
- Implementation detail (proposed): Hard-coded limit (`100000000`, or 1e8) in `strtoul` conversion to distinguish positional selectors from time-based ones, mirroring Git’s `approxidate` parser behavior. This was clarified as intentional and not arbitrary.

Open questions and loose ends
