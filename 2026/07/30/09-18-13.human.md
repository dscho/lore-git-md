This is a documentation patch series, now fully merged to `master`. The 11-patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk completes the effort to overhaul the `git-interpret-trailers` man page, addressing the earlier bug report about silent failures when parsing trailer keys containing periods. The series explicitly documents the strict key format (ASCII alphanumeric plus hyphens only), standardizes terminology ("trailer metadata", "trailer block"), removes outdated RFC 822 references, and adds clear examples of valid and invalid usage. It also explains how comment lines (via `core.commentString`) are handled during parsing.

The final version (v4) incorporates feedback from Junio Hamano and others, with minor stylistic refinements like shortening redundant phrasing ("a blank line (specifically an empty line)" → "a blank line") and clarifying technical distinctions. One small post-merge debate emerged about singular vs. plural phrasing ("trailer metadata" vs. "trailers metadata"), but the author conceded to the more natural-sounding singular form. The series is now technically settled and serves as a model for how the Git project documents intentional but potentially surprising behavior.
