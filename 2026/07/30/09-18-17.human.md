This is a documentation patch, part of Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s already-merged 11-patch series that overhauled the `git-interpret-trailers` man page. This specific patch (4/11) adjusts the DESCRIPTION section to clarify that the command works on any text, not just commit messages. The change is small—replacing “commit message” with “commit message, or any other kind of text”—but it corrects a long-standing inaccuracy, since the command has always been general-purpose and `git tag` gained `--trailer` support in 2024. The commit message explains the rationale clearly and cites the relevant Git history. No controversy or follow-up is expected; this is a straightforward, well-justified improvement.
