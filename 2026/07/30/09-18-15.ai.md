This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's now-merged 11-patch documentation series for `git-interpret-trailers`. The patch replaces the term "trailer lines" with "trailer metadata" in the command's introductory paragraph, aligning the language with the series' broader goal of standardizing terminology around Git's trailer functionality.

The change is motivated by the removal of the email-header analogy in the previous patch (1/11) and the upcoming glossary definition of trailers as "key-value metadata". Kristoffer argues that "trailer metadata" reads more naturally than "trailers metadata", despite initial preference for the latter based on Git's conventional plural usage ("trailers"). The commit message includes a detailed rationale for this stylistic choice, reflecting the series' attention to phrasing precision.

This patch is part of the series' foundational work to establish consistent terminology before introducing more technical definitions in later commits. The interdiff notes show the change was refined in v4 based on feedback from Matt Hunter, who helped settle the singular/plural debate. While minor, the adjustment contributes to the series' overall clarity improvements by framing trailers as structured data rather than generic lines of text. No functional changes are introduced.
