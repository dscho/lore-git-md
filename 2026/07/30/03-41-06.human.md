This is a small but user-visible change to `git stash`. Junio proposes to forbid time-based selectors like `stash@{2.days.ago}` in `git stash drop` and `git stash pop` because they behave unintuitively: instead of operating on a single stash entry, they remove *all* entries newer than the specified time.

The two-patch series first records the positional index in the internal `struct stash_info` (patch 1) and then rejects any time-based selector in `drop` and `pop` (patch 2). The commit messages and tests are clear, and the motivation is straightforward: preventing a foot-gun that could silently discard multiple stashes when the user expects to affect only one. The change is unlikely to be controversial, but users who rely on the current behavior will need to adjust their scripts.
