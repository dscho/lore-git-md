This is the second patch in Junio C Hamano’s two-patch series that tightens the safety of `git stash drop` and `git stash pop`. It implements the actual restriction: time-based stash selectors (e.g., `stash@{2.days.ago}`) are now rejected with a clear error message, while positional selectors (e.g., `stash@{4}`) remain accepted. The patch updates the documentation to explain the new rule and adds tests that verify the rejection behavior. The change is a straightforward, well-motivated safety improvement that closes a long-standing footgun in the interface.
