This is Junio's final approval of the two-patch bugfix series for `git mv`. The series improves error messages and adds an early check for missing or problematic destination directories, including rejecting any tracked symlink in the destination path to prevent index corruption. In v5, the author implemented Junio's requested refactoring by extracting the shared "will this move rename on disk?" logic into a `needs_worktree_rename()` helper, ensuring consistency between the early check and the actual rename operation. The helper also optimizes memory allocation by deferring the `dirname` copy until needed. Junio confirms the changes match his expectations and explicitly states "Looking good," signaling the series is ready for integration into `next`.

The series touches `builtin/mv.c` and `t/t7001-mv.sh`, adding 146 new test lines covering symlinks, missing directories, non-directory components, `--dry-run` detection, and edge cases like normalized destinations and bare filenames. Two new translatable strings were introduced for improved error messages. The TOCTOU race (where the destination could disappear between check and `rename()`) remains acknowledged but unresolved, as no better solution has been proposed. The only remaining loose ends are the TOCTOU race and potential bikeshedding on the helper function name, neither of which affect functionality. Junio's approval marks the end of the review cycle; the series is now technically complete and awaiting integration.
