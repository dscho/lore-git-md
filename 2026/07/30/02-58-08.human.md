This is a bugfix patch for the `git maintenance` command. Junio C Hamano replies to David Lin’s patch with a minor administrative note about the mismatch between the author’s email address in the From header (`@gmail.com`) and the sign-off (`@stripe.com`). He suggests using an in-body `From:` line to align the author identity with the sign-off address, a common practice for contributors who use different addresses for sending versus signing off.

The patch itself remains unchanged and uncontroversial. It fixes a regression introduced in October 2025 (commit d465be2327) where `git maintenance` began rejecting the documented `"none"` strategy, breaking both the fallback behavior and explicit use of `--strategy=none`. The fix is minimal: two lines in `builtin/gc.c` to accept `"none"` case-insensitively in the strategy parser, plus three lines of test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` to verify the strategy is accepted with and without a schedule. The patch restores the documented behavior and includes adequate test coverage. No substantive review feedback has been posted yet.
