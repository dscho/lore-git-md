This is a retraction from Junio C Hamano of his own two-patch series that proposed rejecting time-based stash selectors (e.g., `stash@{2.days.ago}`) in `git stash drop` and `git stash pop`. The series was initially motivated by a concern that these selectors could silently remove more stash entries than the user expected, acting as a footgun.

Junio discovered during further review that the concern was unfounded: while `git stash` does use the reflog expiration machinery, it is already designed to remove only a single stash entry, not all entries matching the time-based selector. The "collateral damage" scenario he described in the original cover letter does not actually occur. As a result, he has withdrawn the patches entirely.

The thread had previously seen a substantive review from Ben Knoble, who raised a technical question about the hard-coded numeric limit in the proposed implementation. That question is now moot, as the series is no longer being pursued. No other feedback or objections had been raised, and the patches were under active review at the time of retraction.
