This is the third patch in Christian Couder’s seven-patch series refactoring `git fast-import`’s usage string and beginning its libification. It adds missing documentation for the `OPT_*_F()` macro variants (which accept a trailing `flags` argument) and the recently introduced `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()` and `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()` convenience macros to `Documentation/technical/api-parse-options.adoc`.

The patch inserts a short paragraph explaining the `_F` convention—how it relates to the per-option flags documented in patch 2/7—and then documents `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()` immediately after `OPT_GROUP()` and `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()` immediately after `OPT_BOOL()`. The change is purely additive: 18 lines of AsciiDoc, no code or behavior changes. It is a straightforward documentation cleanup that supports the broader parse-options modernization effort.
