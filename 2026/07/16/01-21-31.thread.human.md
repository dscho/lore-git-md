This thread is about a Google Summer of Code contributor’s patch series (`[PATCH v4]`) that adds six new path-related keys to `git repo info`, exposing filesystem locations of repository components like the hooks directory, index file, and superproject root in a scriptable format. The series is now in its fourth version, mechanically clean, and ready for substantive technical review after addressing all prior feedback—including dropping the `path.objects` key due to concerns about pluggable ODB backends and refining naming and edge-case handling.

The main point of contention has shifted from mechanical issues to design scope. Lucas Seiki Oshiro raised a substantive objection to the `path.git-prefix` key, arguing it represents "current directory information" rather than "repository information" and thus doesn’t belong in `git repo info`. The author, K Jayatheerth, responded by acknowledging the conceptual mismatch but defending the key’s inclusion for scriptability and consistency with `git rev-parse --show-prefix`, while offering to drop it if reviewers deem it out of scope. This exchange frames the key’s inclusion as a design decision rather than a technical one, and it may now block acceptance unless consensus emerges in its favor. Other open questions—such as key naming consistency and unconditional path-return behavior—remain unresolved but are less likely to derail the series.

Junio C Hamano has confirmed the series is mechanically sound and free of glaring design issues, explicitly inviting broader review. The implementation touches `builtin/repo.c`, documentation, and tests, introducing 18 new keys with suffix-based formatting (e.g., `path.toplevel.absolute` and `.relative`). The series aligns with `git rev-parse --git-path` precedent for unconditional path returns, a behavior Junio endorsed for `path.grafts` and `path.index`. While the author has addressed all prior feedback, the `path.git-prefix` objection is the first pushback on the series’ core design, and its resolution will likely determine whether the series proceeds as-is or with the key removed.
