The `git repo info` path-keys series, now at version 5, has been unblocked after Junio C Hamano rebased it onto a newer base that includes the missing `repo->prefix` dependency. This resolves the build-breakage issue reported by SZEDER Gábor, allowing the series to proceed to substantive review and testing. The series adds seven new path-related keys (`path.toplevel`, `path.superproject-root`, `path.hooks`, `path.index`, `path.grafts`, `path.git-prefix`, and `path.cdup`) to `git repo info`, exposing filesystem locations of repository components in a scriptable format. All prior mechanical and design feedback has been addressed, including the addition of `path.cdup` for symmetry with `path.git-prefix`, but Junio’s architectural concern about duplicated logic with `git rev-parse` remains open. The series is feature-complete and mechanically clean, though the proposed refactoring to consolidate shared logic could require a v6 or follow-up effort. K Jayatheerth (the GSoC contributor driving the series) has not yet responded to the architectural feedback or the rebase. The series is under active review, with no merge status reported.
