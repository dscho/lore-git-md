This thread is about a Google Summer of Code contributor’s patch series, `[PATCH v3]`, that adds seven new path-related keys to the `git repo info` command. The goal is to expose filesystem locations of key repository components—like the hooks directory, index file, grafts file, and superproject working tree—in a scriptable, user-facing format. The series is now in its third version, with all seven patches posted and mechanically clean.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the series and confirmed it’s free of glaring design or mechanical issues, explicitly inviting broader technical review. The design for `path.hooks`, `path.index`, and `path.grafts` is settled: these keys return paths unconditionally, even in bare repositories or when the files don’t exist, aligning with `git rev-parse --git-path` precedent. The series touches `builtin/repo.c`, documentation, and tests, and introduces 21 new keys with suffix-based formatting (e.g., `path.toplevel.absolute` and `path.toplevel.relative`).

The main open question is whether the community will suggest adjustments to key naming or suffix semantics for consistency with existing Git terminology. Lucas Seiki Oshiro’s recent "looks good to me" is a surface-level approval and doesn’t address these design points. The series remains under active review, with no merge status reported. The author, K Jayatheerth, has signaled intent to shift focus to another effort while awaiting further feedback.
