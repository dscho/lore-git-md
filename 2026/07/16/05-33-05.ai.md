This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem and worktree code. The changes are purely internal to the file: every call to `repo_get_common_dir()`, `repo_common_path_replace()`, and repository-aware helpers now uses the passed-in repository pointer. The patch touches only `worktree.c` (47 lines changed) and is a straightforward continuation of the series’ refactoring goal. No behavior changes are introduced, and the series remains on track for graduation to `master`.
