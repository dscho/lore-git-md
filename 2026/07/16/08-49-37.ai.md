This is the v6 iteration of a refactoring series that migrates `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags into `struct repo_config_values`, completing the work begun in the now-merged v4 and revised in v5. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and targets core index handling and configuration plumbing.

The key change since v5 is the elimination of the contested global fallback variable (`default_has_symlinks`) in the mingw compatibility layer, as requested by Junio C Hamano. Instead, the series now uses a compile-time macro (`platform_has_symlinks()`) and a platform-specific function (`mingw_platform_has_symlinks()`), modeled after `is_dir_sep()`. This design removes mutable global state while preserving the ability to detect native symlink support via environment variables. The author has adopted Junio’s proposal in full, addressing the last substantive review concern from v5.

The series remains a well-scoped, mechanical migration with no behavioral changes. It moves both flags into repository-scoped storage, establishes new getter functions (`repo_trust_executable_bit()` and `repo_has_symlinks()`), and updates all call sites to use repository-scoped access. The mingw adjustments are now cleaner and more consistent with Git’s existing platform-compatibility patterns. The author has also incorporated prior feedback, including dropping redundant comments and relocating `ce_mode_from_stat()` to reduce header exposure.

Files touched include `read-cache.c`, `environment.c/h`, `apply.c`, `compat/mingw.c`, and related headers. The series removes redundant extern declarations, migrates both flags into `repo_config_values` with proper initialization, and ensures repository pointer validation in the new getters. The mingw compatibility layer now uses the new macro/function pattern, which may serve as a template for future platform-specific overrides.

The series builds on prior work (including an unsuccessful attempt to use `struct repo_settings`) and demonstrates incremental progress in eliminating global state. The inclusion of `has_symlinks`—previously deferred—reflects growing confidence in the approach, though the author notes that `repo_config_values()` still does not support non-`the_repository` instances due to deep integration of these flags. This limitation is acknowledged as a future challenge, but the series lays groundwork for eventual elimination of the global.

This iteration appears ready for final review, with the author having addressed all prior feedback, including Junio’s design-level concern about the mingw compatibility layer. The migration pattern established here could inform similar configuration refactoring efforts, and the mingw adjustments may attract attention from platform maintainers (e.g., Johannes Schindelin or Randall S. Becker). The series is uncontroversial in its current form and likely to proceed to `next` once review concludes.
