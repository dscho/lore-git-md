This patch is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series that reintroduces `writev(3p)` support to Git’s low-level I/O layer. It adds the generic wrapper functions `writev_in_full()` and `writev_or_die()` in `wrapper.c`/`wrapper.h` and `write-or-die.c`/`write-or-die.h`, mirroring the existing `write_in_full()` and `write_or_die()` helpers. The implementation handles partial writes, `EINTR`/`EAGAIN` retries, and short-write detection, and it modifies the passed `iovec` array to reflect any remaining data. No callers are introduced yet; this is pure plumbing for later patches in the series.
