This framing reframes the patch’s motivation: rather than improving rebase’s user interface, it might be catering to a niche workflow that could be better served by a dedicated command or script. The reply leaves the thread’s central question unresolved—whether the hint should be added—but introduces a new angle: whether the underlying workflow is even a valid use case for `git rebase`.
