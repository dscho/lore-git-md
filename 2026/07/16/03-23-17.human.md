This is the maintainer’s feedback on the final patch in K Jayatheerth’s seven-part GSoC series adding path-related keys to `` `git repo info` ``. Junio points out two mechanical issues: the patch incorrectly adds `` `#include "compat/posix.h"` `` as the very first line instead of relying on `` `<builtin.h>` `` (which already includes `` `<git-compat-util.h>` ``), and the commit title’s “validation” wording is misleading—there is no actual validation logic in the patch. He suggests a simpler title such as “repo: add path.git-prefix key.” The feedback is purely surface-level and does not engage with the patch’s substance; the series as a whole remains under review.
