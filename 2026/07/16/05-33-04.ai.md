This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem. It is a focused refactoring of `worktree.c` that replaces seven direct uses of `the_repository` with the repository pointer (`wt->repo`) that is already available in every `struct worktree`. The changes are purely mechanical: each call to `repo_common_path_replace(the_repository, ...)` or `repo_common_path(the_repository, ...)` is updated to use `wt->repo` instead. No new behavior is introduced, and the patch is uncontroversial. It fits cleanly into the larger `the_repository` removal effort and is expected to graduate to `master` in the next cycle.
