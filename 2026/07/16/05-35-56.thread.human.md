This thread introduces a `--auto-reset=<where>` option to `git bisect`, letting users automatically return the working tree to either the pre-bisect commit (`original`, the default) or the first bad commit (`found`) after the bisect completes. Harald Nordgren posted a v2 series incorporating Junio Hamano’s design feedback, replacing the original boolean flag with a parameterized option.

The series is now under review, with the first patch—a preparatory refactoring of `bisect_reset()` to support quiet checkouts—considered uncontroversial and ready for integration. The second patch, just posted, implements this quiet-checkout plumbing without changing behavior yet; the actual `--auto-reset` logic follows in the third patch. Junio has noted a practical limitation: users who want to conditionally reset based on the bisect outcome (e.g., choosing between `original` and `found` after seeing the result) will need to skip `--auto-reset` and reset manually, since the option requires the target to be specified upfront. No other open questions remain, and no reviewers beyond Junio and Harald have weighed in so far.
