This is a performance optimization update for the `git last-modified` command, part of an ongoing thread about its speed relative to a hand-rolled `git log` pipeline. Toon Claes, the command’s author, acknowledges the 2.5× slowdown Gusted reported on the ziglang/zig repository and explains why GitLab hasn’t prioritized the single-file case: their real-world workload batches 25 files at once, where `git last-modified` already outperforms the naive loop. He notes that Jeff King (Peff) has just posted a proof-of-concept patch that cuts runtime from 64 ms to 16 ms by reusing Bloom-filter logic from `revision.c`, and says he will coordinate with Peff to combine their efforts into a single patch series. No new technical details or edge cases are introduced here; the email is primarily a status update and a promise of follow-up patches.
