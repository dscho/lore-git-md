This is Junio Hamano’s reply to the v5 RFC series introducing `diff.<driver>.process`, a mechanism for external tools to inject diff hunks into Git’s pipelines. The series is now technically complete, with all requested features—blame integration, stat/numstat/shortstat support, and `git log -L` range tracking—wired to the external process. Junio’s sole concern is a merge conflict with the in-flight `mm/line-log-limited-ops` topic, which modifies the same `line-log.c` code that patch 9 touches to integrate `git log -L` with the external diff process. The conflict is mechanical (both series modify `collect_diff()`), and the resolution should be straightforward once the two topics are queued together. No objections to the design or implementation are raised; the series is otherwise ready for merging once the conflict is resolved.
