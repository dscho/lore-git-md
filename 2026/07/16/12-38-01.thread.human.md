This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s three-patch series removing the global `the_repository` dependency from `refspec.c`, part of the ongoing effort to eliminate implicit global state in Git. The series refactors the refspec parsing API to explicitly accept a hash algorithm parameter, touching 13 files across built-in commands, transport code, and core refspec logic. No behavior change is intended—all callers currently pass `the_hash_algo` or `repo->hash_algo`.

The series is under initial review after its v1 submission. Junio C Hamano provided the first substantive feedback on the final patch, noting that while the series removes direct `the_repository` usage, it introduces a new dependency on `the_hash_algo`, which is itself tied to the global repository. This leaves callers with lingering cleanup work and doesn’t fully advance the goal of eliminating `the_repository`. Junio acknowledged the series as a reasonable step forward for the isolated refspec subsystem but implied that future iterations may need to address this structural limitation. No other reviews have been posted yet, and the series remains under review with no evidence of merge to `seen`, `next`, or `master`. The open question is whether Patrick will revise the approach or proceed with the current design.
