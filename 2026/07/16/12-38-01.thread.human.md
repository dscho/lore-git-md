This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s three-patch series (v1) that removes the global `the_repository` dependency from `refspec.c`, part of the ongoing effort to eliminate `the_repository` from Git’s codebase. The series refactors the refspec parsing API to explicitly accept a hash algorithm parameter, touching 13 files across built-in commands (`fetch`, `push`, `pull`, `fast-export`, `send-pack`, `submodule--helper`), transport code, and core `refspec.c`/`refspec.h`.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the series and raised a key concern: while the patches remove direct dependencies on `the_repository`, they introduce a new dependency on `the_hash_algo` (which is derived from `the_repository`), leaving callers with residual cleanup work. Patrick acknowledged this in his reply, clarifying that the approach is intentional and aligns with the project’s incremental strategy for `the_repository` removal. He noted that follow-up work is planned to address higher-level dependencies, particularly in the transport layer, with the long-term goal of making `git clone` work without `the_repository`. The series remains under review, with no revisions posted yet. No other reviewers have weighed in, so the discussion is currently between Patrick and Junio.
