This is a brief update in the `diff.<driver>.process` RFC series. The series, now at v5 (9 patches), proposes a long-running subprocess protocol to let external tools inject hunks into Git's diff and blame pipelines while preserving downstream features like word-diff, color-moved, and function context. All substantive review feedback has been addressed, and the series is technically complete, with every "which lines changed" operation now wired to consult the external tool.

The only remaining blocker is a **merge conflict** with the `mm/line-log-limited-ops` topic, which is currently in `next`. The conflict is in `line-log.c` (specifically `collect_diff()`), where the series integrates `git log -L` range tracking with the external diff process. Junio reported the conflict earlier today; Michael now acknowledges it and plans to rebase the series on top of `mm/line-log-limited-ops` to resolve the structural overlap. No other technical objections remain, and the rebase should be straightforward once the two topics are aligned. The series remains a well-motivated, thoroughly tested effort to enable structural/AST-based diffing in Git.
