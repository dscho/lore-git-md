This is the second patch in the v6 series refactoring `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch moves the `ce_mode_from_stat()` helper function from `read-cache.h` to `read-cache.c` to prepare for repository-scoped configuration access.

The change is mechanical and preparatory: `ce_mode_from_stat()` is currently a static inline function in the header, but future patches will need it to access repository-specific configuration. Relocating it to the `.c` file allows that access without exposing internal dependencies in the header. The patch also removes redundant `extern` declarations for `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` (already declared in `environment.h`) and adds a descriptive comment explaining the function’s purpose. There are no behavioral changes, and the commit message explicitly states this is a prerequisite for later work.

This patch is uncontroversial and aligns with prior feedback on the series. It was present in earlier iterations (v4 and v5) and received no objections, suggesting it is a straightforward cleanup. The relocation of `ce_mode_from_stat()` is a necessary step for the subsequent migration of the configuration flags into `struct repo_config_values`, which is the core goal of the series. The added comment improves clarity without altering functionality.

Files touched are limited to `read-cache.c` and `read-cache.h`, and the diff is minimal. The patch is well-scoped and unlikely to attract further discussion, as it is purely preparatory and does not introduce new logic or dependencies. It should proceed smoothly to integration once the series as a whole is approved.
