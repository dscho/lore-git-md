This is a brief follow-up in K Jayatheerth’s GSoC series adding seven new path-related keys to the `git repo info` command. The email responds to Junio C Hamano’s surface-level review of the final patch (7/7), which exposed `path.git-prefix` by mirroring `startup_info->prefix`. The author acknowledges two mechanical issues: (1) an incorrect include order (`#include "compat/posix.h"` was mistakenly added as the first line, violating Git’s include rules) and (2) a misleading commit title ("add validation" when no validation logic exists). Both issues are minor and will be fixed in the next iteration.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and completes the series by exposing the relative path from the top-level working tree to the current directory (e.g., `subdir/` when run in `toplevel/subdir/`). The series touches `builtin/repo.c`, `Documentation/git-repo.adoc`, and `t/t1900-repo-info.sh`, introducing eighteen new keys with suffix-based formatting (e.g., `path.toplevel.absolute`, `path.git-prefix`). No substantive review feedback has been posted yet; the series still awaits technical review of its design and implementation. Key details to track in follow-up discussion include the naming and semantics of the new keys (e.g., `objects` vs. `gitdir`, `hooks` vs. `hookdir`) and whether the exposed paths respect environment/config overrides as intended.
