Junio C Hamano provided initial feedback on the final patch, noting discomfort with unused local variables in the `struct option` array as placeholders for future parsing logic—though he didn’t reject the approach outright. The series is otherwise under review, with no substantive technical feedback yet beyond Junio’s comments. A tangential discussion about `.mailmap` update etiquette arose when Junio clarified that such updates should be sent as standalone patches to `master`, regardless of when commits with the new address land. This procedural point is now resolved, but the core refactor awaits Christian’s response to Junio’s feedback. No other reviewers have engaged, and the series remains in its first version.
