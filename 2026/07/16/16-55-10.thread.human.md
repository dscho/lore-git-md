This thread is a patch series from Christian Couder refactoring `git fast-import` to standardize its usage string, reduce global variables, and modernize its parse-options API. The v1 series, posted recently, introduces a new `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro, documents parse-options flags, and begins libifying the command by encapsulating globals into a `struct fast_import_state`.

Junio C Hamano provided initial feedback, particularly on the final patch, where he noted the awkwardness of introducing unused local variables in the `struct option` array as placeholders for future parsing logic. While he didn’t reject the approach outright, he expressed discomfort with the intermediate state. Christian has since responded to a procedural side issue—Junio’s observation of a `.mailmap` mismatch between his From header and Signed-off-by trailer—by proposing a `.mailmap` update to standardize on his Gmail address. This aligns with Junio’s preference for standalone `.mailmap` patches and resolves the discrepancy without affecting the series’ technical content.

Beyond Junio’s feedback, the series hasn’t yet received substantive technical review. The libification effort remains incomplete, with many globals still unaddressed, and the open question about the clarity of the unused variables in patch 7/7 is still unresolved. The series is under review, with no changes merged or queued in Junio’s integration branches.
