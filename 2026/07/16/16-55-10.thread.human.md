The `git fast-import` libification series (v3, 12 patches) by Christian Couder continues to progress toward readiness for integration. The series refactors the command-line parser to standardize usage strings, reduce global variables, and modernize the parse-options API, with the ultimate goal of enabling libification.

Elijah Newren has now confirmed that all his feedback on the three patches he reviewed (2/12, 7/12, and 11/12) has been addressed. The documentation for `PARSE_OPT_LASTARG_DEFAULT` is now accurate, function signatures in patch 7/12 have been reformatted to Git’s line-length conventions, and the behavioral changes introduced by patch 11/12—support for space-separated values, bare `--`, and the `--allow-unsafe-features` inconsistency—are now explicitly documented in the commit message, man page, and code. The `--allow-unsafe-features` inconsistency remains a known limitation, flagged with a `NEEDSWORK` comment, but the author has deferred a general fix. With Elijah’s sign-off, the series has cleared its most substantive review hurdle and is now awaiting Junio’s integration decision or further community feedback.

The series is otherwise complete, with all 12 patches posted, CI tests passing (except for a likely unrelated Windows+Meson failure), and no outstanding technical objections. The libification effort remains incomplete, but the current work lays the necessary groundwork for future patches. Key changes include the introduction of the `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro, improved error messages for numeric argument parsing, and the rejection of undocumented options on the command line. The man page and SYNOPSIS documentation are now consistent, and the parse-options integration is functionally complete.
