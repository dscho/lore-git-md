Christian Couder has posted a follow-up to his `git fast-import` refactoring series, responding to Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the final patch. The series aims to standardize the command’s usage string, reduce global variables, and modernize its parse-options API as part of broader libification efforts. Junio had flagged the awkwardness of introducing unused local variables in the `struct option` array as placeholders for future parsing logic, though he didn’t reject the approach outright.

Christian now confirms he’s working on a v2 that will fully integrate the parse-options API to parse command-line arguments, eliminating those placeholders. This implies the globals Junio referenced (like `max_packsize`) will eventually be replaced by struct members, aligning with the libification goal. The series remains under review, with no substantive technical objections beyond Junio’s aesthetic concern, and no other reviewers have engaged yet. The next version is expected to address the feedback and complete the libification step.
