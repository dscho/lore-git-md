This thread is a 12-patch series from Christian Couder refactoring `git fast-import` to standardize its usage string, reduce global variables, and modernize its command-line parsing with the `parse-options` API. The goal is to pave the way for libification by eliminating hand-written argument parsing and improving consistency with Git’s other commands. The series is now at v3, addressing all feedback from Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren.

The key changes include grouping options into logical sections (e.g., "Common", "Marks"), rejecting five undocumented options (`--alias`, `--get-mark`, etc.) on the command line (they’re only valid in the input stream), and enabling abbreviated options (`--dep=5` for `--depth=5`) and space-separated values (`--depth 5`). The series also introduces a new `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro for organizing hidden options and encapsulates globals in a `struct fast_import_state`. One subtle behavioral change—a stricter early scan for `--allow-unsafe-features` compared to `parse_options()`—was documented as a known limitation in the man page, with a `NEEDSWORK` comment left for future resolution. All CI tests pass except for a likely unrelated Windows+Meson failure.

The series is now ready for broader review. The only remaining open question is whether to adjust the early `--allow-unsafe-features` scan to match `parse_options()`’s flexibility, but this has been documented and deferred. No merge status has been reported yet.
