This thread is a 12-patch series from Christian Couder refactoring `git fast-import` to standardize its usage string, reduce global variables, and modernize its command-line parsing. The series is now at v2, addressing Junio Hamano’s feedback on the initial version by fully integrating the parse-options API and eliminating placeholder variables. The core goal is to prepare `fast-import` for libification by making its command-line handling more robust and consistent with Git’s broader conventions.

The series introduces several technical improvements, including a new `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP` macro for organizing hidden options in help text, a `struct fast_import_state` to encapsulate global variables, and documentation updates for parse-options flags. One notable behavior change is the rejection of undocumented options (like `--alias` and `--get-mark`) on the command line, as these are meant only for the input stream. Most patches are mechanical or documentation-related, but this change could affect scripts or workflows relying on those options.

So far, the series has received light review—Junio’s feedback on v1 was addressed, but broader technical engagement is still pending. The CI results are clean except for a likely unrelated Windows+Meson failure. The next steps are to gather more substantive reviews, particularly on the behavior change in patch 11/12, and to document the new restrictions if the series is accepted. The libification effort remains incomplete, with more globals slated for future work.
