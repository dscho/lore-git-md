This is a refactoring patch (part 4/7 of Christian Couder's `git fast-import` libification series). It localizes the loop counter `i` into each `for` loop in `cmd_fast_import()` to use the most appropriate type (`int`, `unsigned int`, or `size_t`) for its bounds, rather than declaring a single `unsigned int i` at the function scope. The change is mechanical and confined to `builtin/fast-import.c`; it removes one line (the global `i` declaration) and adjusts four loops to declare their own counters. No behavior or interface changes are introduced. The patch is a small, uncontroversial cleanup that aligns with the series' broader goal of modernizing the codebase.
