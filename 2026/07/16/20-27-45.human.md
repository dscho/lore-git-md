This is Junio C Hamano’s final review feedback on the fourth and last patch in Tian Yuchen’s v6 series, which migrates the `has_symlinks` configuration flag into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch itself is uncontroversial and appears ready for inclusion. The series as a whole (v6) is now blocked only by Junio’s **design-level objection** to patch 2/4 (relocation of `ce_mode_from_stat()`), which is unrelated to this patch. Once that is resolved, the series looks likely to proceed to `next`.
