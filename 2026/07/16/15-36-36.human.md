This is a brief follow-up in K Jayatheerth’s GSoC patch series adding seven new path-related keys to the `git repo info` command. The email responds to Junio C Hamano’s surface-level review of the final patch (7/7), which exposed `path.git-prefix` by mirroring `git rev-parse --show-prefix`.

Jayatheerth acknowledges two mechanical issues: an editor-induced misplaced `#include "compat/posix.h"` at the top of `builtin/repo.c` (violating Git’s include-order rules) and a misleading commit title that claimed “validation” where none exists. Both are minor and will be fixed in the next iteration. The patch itself remains uncontroversial—it simply exposes `startup_info->prefix` as a structured key, completing the series’ goal of making repository component paths scriptable without requiring direct access to Git’s internals.

No substantive feedback has been posted yet, so the series’ design and implementation are still untested by technical review. The next version should address the stylistic nits and is likely to proceed smoothly unless deeper concerns emerge.
