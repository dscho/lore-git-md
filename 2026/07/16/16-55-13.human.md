This is a documentation patch, part of Christian Couder’s seven-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` and modernizing the parse-options API. It updates `Documentation/technical/api-parse-options.adoc` to document three previously undocumented convenience macros: the `OPT_*_F()` variants that accept a trailing `flags` argument, `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()`, and `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()`.

The patch adds a short explanatory paragraph clarifying that the `_F` suffix macros (e.g., `OPT_BOOL_F`, `OPT_STRING_F`) take a `flags` argument that is the bitwise-or of per-option flags like `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN` or `PARSE_OPT_OPTARG`. It then documents `OPT_HIDDEN_GROUP()`—introduced in an earlier patch in the series—as a way to group hidden options under a header that only appears in `--help-all`, not in `-h`. Finally, it documents `OPT_HIDDEN_BOOL()`, which behaves like `OPT_BOOL()` but hides the option from `-h` while still showing it in `--help-all`.

The change is purely additive and does not alter any code behavior. It fills a gap in the API documentation, making it easier for contributors to discover and correctly use these macros. The patch is well-motivated by the series’ broader goal of standardizing and libifying the parse-options infrastructure, and it looks like a straightforward, uncontroversial improvement.
