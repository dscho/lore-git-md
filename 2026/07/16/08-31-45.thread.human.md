This thread introduces a new `git stash rename` subcommand, authored by Emin Özata, which allows users to edit stash messages after creation—a feature long requested by the community. The implementation avoids the pitfalls of earlier proposals (which attempted to rewrite reflog entries in place) by instead performing a drop-and-re-store sequence, preserving the original order and contents of stash entries while refreshing their timestamps. The command is designed with safety in mind: it only accepts index-based selectors (e.g., `stash@{1}`) to avoid race conditions, rejects operations on non-stash entries, and handles mid-operation failures gracefully.

The patch is well-documented and thoroughly tested, touching `builtin/stash.c`, updating the `git-stash` man page, adding bash completion support, and including comprehensive tests in `t/t3903-stash.sh`. The commit message links to prior discussions (dating back to 2010 and 2013) and transparently acknowledges trade-offs, such as the non-atomicity of the operation and the performance cost of reflog rewrites. The author suggests follow-up work if the feature gains traction, indicating openness to iteration.
