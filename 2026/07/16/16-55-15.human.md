This is the fifth patch in Christian Couder’s seven-patch series refactoring `git fast-import`. It introduces a `struct fast_import_state` to begin encapsulating the command’s global variables, starting with `global_argc`, `global_argv`, and `global_prefix`. The patch is a mechanical refactoring: it adds the new struct, initializes it in `cmd_fast_import()`, and updates function signatures to accept a `struct fast_import_state *` parameter. Many functions receive the parameter only to pass it along or for future use, so they are marked `UNUSED` to silence compiler warnings.

The change is part of the ongoing libification effort and follows the pattern established by earlier work on `builtin/apply.c`. No behavior or CLI changes are intended, and CI tests have passed. The patch is clean and incremental, but the libification is explicitly incomplete—many globals remain for future patches. This is a solid, uncontroversial refactoring step that sets the stage for further modernization.
