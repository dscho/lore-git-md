This thread is about a small but important refactoring step in Git’s ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. Patrick Steinhardt’s patch updates two utility functions in `copy.c`—`copy_file()` and `copy_file_with_time()`—to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global variable. The change is mechanical, touching call sites across several files, including a correctness fix in `sequencer.c` where a local repository instance was previously mixed with `the_repository`.

Phillip Wood provided a substantive review, catching the latent bug in `sequencer.c` and later giving his **Reviewed-by** to the v2 patch, which resolved the issue. Junio C Hamano has now queued the patch in the `next` branch, signaling it’s ready for integration testing. There are no open questions or objections, and the patch is expected to graduate to `master` in due course. This is a routine, uncontroversial step in the broader `the_repository` removal effort, with no functional impact or test coverage concerns.
