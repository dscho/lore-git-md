This thread is about a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt as part of the long-running effort to remove Git’s global `the_repository` variable. The patch updates two utility functions in `copy.c`—`copy_file()` and `copy_file_with_time()`—to take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global. The change is mechanical, touching ten files across commands (`clone`, `difftool`, `worktree`) and subsystems (refs, rerere, sequencer, setup), but introduces no behavioral change. It’s a routine step in the larger cleanup series and should be uncontroversial, though reviewers may double-check for missed call sites or incorrect repository object usage. So far, no reviews have been posted.
