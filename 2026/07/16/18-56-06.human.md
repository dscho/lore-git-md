This is a surface-level review of Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series reintroducing a `writev(3p)` wrapper for performance. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) raises two procedural concerns rather than engaging with the technical substance of the patch.

First, he suggests renaming the wrapper from `writev` to something like `write_gather` to avoid implying POSIX compliance, since the emulated implementations on some platforms (like NonStop) don’t provide the same guarantees. Second, he asks for clearer documentation stating that the wrapper’s sole purpose is to reduce system call overhead, not to provide full `writev` semantics. He also notes that a range-diff against the earlier, reverted version would have been helpful for reviewers.

The feedback is polite and constructive but remains at the naming and documentation level. There’s no discussion of the integer overflow issue Simon Richter flagged, nor any evaluation of the performance claims or platform fixes. The tone suggests mild skepticism about the need for the wrapper, but the review doesn’t dig into whether the approach is technically sound or worth pursuing. If the author addresses these points, the series could proceed, but this email alone doesn’t move the conversation toward a merge decision.
