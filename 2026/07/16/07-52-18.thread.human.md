This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s attempt to revive a `writev(3p)` wrapper for Git, originally merged in Git 2.54 but reverted due to platform compatibility issues. The goal is to reduce system call overhead in low-level I/O operations, particularly in pktline transmission and cat-blob responses. The v1 series includes fixes for NonStop and CMake but has drawn substantive feedback.

Johannes Sixt (Hannes) has raised two key concerns: the wrapper’s name, `git_writev()`, misleadingly implies POSIX semantics that the emulation layer can’t guarantee, and the lack of a range-diff makes it harder to compare this version with the reverted one. Simon Richter previously flagged an integer overflow risk in the bounds-checking logic. Patrick acknowledges the prior revert and is open to dropping the effort if the approach isn’t welcomed, but the performance motivation remains.

The series touches build system files, wrapper code, and specific call sites in `sideband.c` and `fast-import.c`, introducing `git_writev()` and `git_writev_or_die()` wrappers. No on-disk format changes are involved, but the integer overflow issue and naming debate are unresolved. Benchmark results and maintainer appetite are still unclear.
