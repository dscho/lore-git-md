This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series to reintroduce a `writev(3p)`-like wrapper (`git_writev()`) that was originally merged in Git 2.54 but reverted due to platform compatibility issues. The goal is to reduce system call overhead in low-level I/O operations, particularly for pktline transmission and cat-blob responses. The series includes fixes for NonStop and CMake compatibility, but the API design and naming remain contentious.

The series is still under early technical review, with no maintainer decision on whether it will be merged. Key participants include Patrick (the author), Simon Richter (who raised an integer-overflow concern), Johannes Sixt (who objects to the `git_writev()` name for implying POSIX semantics it can’t provide), and Junio C Hamano (the maintainer). The integer-overflow issue has now been resolved: Junio retracted his earlier proposed fix after confirming the existing bounds-checking logic is safe, removing a potential blocker. However, the naming dispute and broader maintainer appetite for the series remain open. The series touches multiple files, including `wrapper.c`, `sideband.c`, and `fast-import.c`, but does not change on-disk formats. No benchmarks have been provided yet, and the lack of a range-diff to the reverted version complicates review.
