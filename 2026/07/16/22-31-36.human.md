This is a **contributor housekeeping** discussion about `.mailmap` update etiquette. Junio C Hamano responds to D. Ben Knoble’s question about how to handle a future email address change in Git’s `.mailmap` file.

Junio clarifies that the simplest approach is option (b): sending a standalone `.mailmap` update as a one-patch email, which he would apply directly to `master`. He explains that adding a new entry to `.mailmap` does not invalidate the old address but merely links the new one to the same contributor. This avoids any procedural complexity and ensures the mapping is in place without tying it to a specific patch series or waiting for commits to stabilize in integration branches.

The discussion is purely procedural and does not involve code or documentation changes. It resolves the open question from the earlier thread, where Knoble sought project guidance on the timing of `.mailmap` updates. No further technical review or follow-up is needed for this topic.
