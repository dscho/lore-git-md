This is the fifth patch in Christian Couder’s seven-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` to reduce global variables and prepare for libification. The patch introduces a `struct fast_import_state` to begin encapsulating globals, starting with `global_argc`, `global_argv`, and `global_prefix`. These are moved into the new struct, which is passed as the first argument to most functions in `builtin/fast-import.c`. Many functions receive the `state` parameter only to pass it along or for future use, so they are marked with `UNUSED` to satisfy `-Werror=unused-parameter`.

The refactoring is mechanical and does not change behavior. It lays the groundwork for future patches to move more globals into the struct, continuing the libification effort. The patch touches only `builtin/fast-import.c` and is a clear, incremental step in the series. No review feedback has been recorded yet, but the approach mirrors prior work (e.g., `struct apply_state` in `builtin/apply.c`), suggesting it is a well-established pattern for this type of modernization.
