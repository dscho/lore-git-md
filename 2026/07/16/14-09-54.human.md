This is the second patch in Paulius Zaleckas’s v6 series introducing configurable submodule fetch errors. It adds the user-facing controls—`fetch.submoduleErrors` config and `--submodule-errors=(fail|warn)` CLI option—while preserving the default `fail` behavior for backwards compatibility. The patch wires the setting through the entire fetch stack, including forwarding it to child processes for `--all`/`--multiple` and nested recursion, ensuring the user’s choice is respected everywhere. Documentation and comprehensive test coverage are included, and all prior review feedback (including Junio’s latest mechanical requests) has been addressed. The implementation is clean, the rationale is clear, and the series as a whole looks ready for `next`.
