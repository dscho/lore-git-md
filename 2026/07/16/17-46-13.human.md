He praises Emin Özata’s summary of those discussions but insists the commit message must go further: it should describe a *specific task* users are currently blocked from completing because they can’t edit stash messages. Junio’s own skepticism is clear: he can’t think of a scenario where editing a stash message is truly critical, and his preferred alternative—unstashing to a branch—offers more flexibility than a corrected stash entry. This suggests the patch faces an uphill battle unless the author provides a compelling, practical example that resonates with reviewers. The email doesn’t address the naming (`rename` vs. `reword`) or performance concerns Patrick raised, but the motivation question is now the primary hurdle for the patch to clear.
