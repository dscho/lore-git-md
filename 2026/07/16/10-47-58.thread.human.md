This thread is about a small but important regression fix in Git's revision-walking logic. The bug, introduced in May 2026, caused `git rev-list --no-walk <commit> -- <path>` to ignore path filtering and output all commits regardless of whether they touched the specified files. Kristofer Karlsson submitted a minimal two-line patch that restores the correct behavior, along with three new tests to verify the fix.

The patch has received a positive response from Junio Hamano, the project maintainer, who confirmed the fix works as intended and will queue it for integration. This means the patch is now on track to land in the `next` branch soon, and will likely graduate to `master` in the current development cycle. The regression was well-understood, and the fix is self-contained with no open questions or requested changes. Peter Colberg originally reported the issue, and his reproduction case was used to verify the solution.
