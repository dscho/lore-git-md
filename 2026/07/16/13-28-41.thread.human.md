This thread is about an RFC patch series from first-time contributor Siddharth Shrimali that adds a `--drop-filtered` option to `git repack`. The feature targets partial clones, letting users reclaim disk space by safely removing locally cached promisor blobs above a specified size threshold (e.g., `--filter=blob:limit=10M`). The v2 series is now complete at 7 patches, addressing all major feedback from v1—most critically, safety guards that prevent drops during merges, rebases, or cherry-picks and refuse to drop blobs referenced by the current index.

The implementation is finalized: the core logic enumerates promisor blobs via `odb_for_each_object()`, rebuilds promisor packs while excluding the dropped blobs, and deletes the old packs (implying `-d`). The CLI design (`--drop-filtered` plus `--dry-run`) is settled, and the documentation patch just posted explains the behavior, constraints, and safety mechanisms in detail. While the drop log and remote verification are deferred to follow-ups, the series is functionally complete and awaiting maintainer review. No objections remain from the reviewers who engaged with v1, though the integration branch is still unknown.
