This thread is an RFC patch series from first-time contributor Siddharth Shrimali proposing `git repack --drop-filtered`, a new option to safely reclaim disk space in partial clones by removing locally cached promisor blobs exceeding a user-specified size threshold. The series is now complete at seven patches (RFC v1), with the final patch implementing a drop log to record removed objects for better error messages during future lazy-fetch failures.

The core functionality is implemented, but a blocking logical flaw in the validation of `--drop-filtered` against `--write-bitmap-index` was identified by Junio Hamano in patch 1/7. The check occurs too late in the control flow to distinguish between user-requested bitmaps (which should be rejected) and config-driven bitmaps (which should be silently disabled), rendering the safety mechanism ineffective. Siddharth has acknowledged this issue and plans to restructure the validation in v2. Other safety guards—such as preventing drops during merges or rebases—remain deferred to follow-ups, and the series has not yet been assigned to an integration branch.

Christian Couder’s recent review was surface-level, focusing only on the command-line interface design. He suggested an alternative syntax but ultimately endorsed the current separation of `--drop-filtered` and `--dry-run`, requesting only a clearer commit message rationale. This feedback does not address the blocking validation flaw or the broader architectural questions, so the series remains in flux pending v2. The next steps will likely focus on fixing the bitmap validation issue before further review or integration.
