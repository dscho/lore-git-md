This thread is an RFC patch series from first-time contributor Siddharth Shrimali proposing `git repack --drop-filtered`, a new option to safely reclaim disk space in partial clones by removing locally cached promisor blobs exceeding a size threshold. The series is now preparing for its second iteration (v2) after completing the initial RFC v1 (7 patches).

The core idea is to extend `git repack` with a `--drop-filtered` flag that targets only promisor objects—those marked as recoverable from a promisor remote—while avoiding locally created objects and introducing safety checks to prevent drops during merges, rebases, or cherry-picks, or of blobs referenced by the current index. The v1 series revealed a critical flaw in the validation logic for `--drop-filtered` and `--write-bitmap-index`, which Junio identified: the check occurs too late to distinguish between user-requested bitmaps (which should be rejected) and configuration-driven ones (which should be silently disabled). This, along with safety guards and index validation, is now confirmed as non-negotiable for v2. The author has also clarified that `--drop-filtered` will imply `-d`, removing old promisor packs containing dropped blobs to ensure actual disk space reclamation, a behavior that will be documented in the v2 man page.

The series touches several subsystems, including the repack machinery, promisor object handling, and the list-objects filter API, and introduces new files like `repack-filtered.c` and a test script. While the CLI design (`--drop-filtered` and `--dry-run`) is settled, the drop log mechanism and remote-object attribution remain deferred to a follow-up. Reviewers Siddharth Asthana and Christian Couder have endorsed the technical direction and CLI design, though Asthana emphasized the safety guards as blocking for v2. Junio’s feedback remains the primary technical hurdle, and no maintainer decision on integration has been made yet. The series is still in active design iteration with no branch assignment.
