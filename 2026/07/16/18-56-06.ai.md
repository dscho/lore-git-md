This is a substantive review of Patrick Steinhardt’s `writev(3p)` wrapper revival series. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) raises two key points that shift the technical framing of the patch.

First, he strongly objects to the name `git_writev()` because it implies POSIX semantics (atomicity, scatter-gather guarantees) that the emulation layer cannot provide. He suggests `git_write_gather()` instead, explicitly calling out that the wrapper’s sole purpose is to collapse multiple `write()` calls into one system call for performance, not to offer the full `writev(3p)` contract.

Second, he notes the absence of a range-diff to the earlier (reverted) version, making it harder to assess what changed beyond the NonStop and CMake fixes already mentioned in the cover letter.

The review is concise but substantive: it questions the API design and the patch’s self-documentation, not just its implementation. No alternative implementation is proposed, but the naming concern is likely to require a respin if the series proceeds.
