This is the sixth patch in Christian Couder’s seven-patch series refactoring `git fast-import` to reduce global state and prepare for libification. It mechanically moves two more global variables—`seen_data_command` and `allow_unsafe_features`—into the `struct fast_import_state` introduced in patch 5/7. The patch also adjusts the initialization order so that the early `--allow-unsafe-features` command-line scan writes directly into the struct, avoiding a memset clobber. No behavior change is intended; the diff is confined to `builtin/fast-import.c` and is purely about encapsulation. The patch is ready for review but has not yet received substantive feedback.
