This is a **substantive technical reply** in the ongoing `writev(3p)` wrapper revival thread. Junio C Hamano retracts his earlier proposal for a three-case integer-overflow fix after re-examining the original bounds-checking logic in `git_writev()`. The original condition therefore correctly guards against overflow without needing additional checks.
