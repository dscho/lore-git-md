This is a review reply to the first patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing Coverity-flagged resource leaks. The patch plugs a file-descriptor leak in `load_one_loose_object_map` by ensuring the `FILE *fp` is always closed before returning on error. Patrick Steinhardt’s review is brief but substantive: he confirms the fix is safe because the `fclose(fp)` call cannot be reached before `fp` is assigned (no early `goto err`) and the `fopen` failure path does not use the `err` label. His sign-off is implicit in the “Makes sense” phrasing, and the review removes any doubt about double-close risks. This patch is now effectively ready to merge.
