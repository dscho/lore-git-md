This is the sixth patch in the **v5 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()`** for one-sided histories, now **fully reviewed and ready to merge**. The patch adds **clock-skew topologies** and **step-count instrumentation** to `t/t6600-test-reach.sh`, targeting edge cases where commit-date ordering (v1 commit-graph without corrected commit dates) violates topological invariants and triggers premature side-exhaustion.

The new test cases (`se-*` and `se2-*`) expose two failure modes under clock skew:
1. **Premature side-exhaustion**: The walk terminates early when one side drains while a low-date ancestor on the other side remains queued (e.g., `se-D` is dequeued before `se-C`, missing the merge base).
2. **Wrong merge base**: The walk returns a too-deep merge base (e.g., `se2-MB2` instead of `se2-MB1`) because the correct candidate never receives paint from both sides.

The patch also adds **step-count assertions** (`test_paint_down_steps`) for existing edge cases (e.g., `infinity-both-sides`, `pending-stale`) and introduces a **mixed finite/INFINITY generation topology** to test region transitions. These additions ensure the optimization remains correct across all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat) and edge cases.

**Key technical details**:
- **Files touched**: Only `t/t6600-test-reach.sh` (139 insertions, 2 deletions).
- **New test topologies**: Three hand-crafted clock-skew scenarios (`se-`, `se2-`, and `skew-*`) with explicit committer dates to force date-ordering violations.
- **Step-count instrumentation**: Validates the optimization’s efficiency (e.g., `test_paint_down_steps 6 4 6 6` for `se-A se-B` with v1 commit-graph).
- **Mixed-generation test**: Exercises the transition from INFINITY-generation commits (outside the commit-graph) to finite-generation commits (inside the graph).
