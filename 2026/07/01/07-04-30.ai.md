This is the twelfth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing Coverity-flagged resource leaks and error-path bugs in Git. It plugs a memory leak in the fsmonitor daemon’s startup sequence: when `fsmonitor_run_daemon()` fails early (e.g., during alias resolution, listener construction, or IPC server initialization), the `state.current_token_data` allocation—created before any of those steps—was never freed. The fix adds two lines at the top of the `done:` cleanup block to call `fsmonitor_free_token_data()` and clear the pointer, ensuring the leak is plugged on all error paths while remaining a no-op on the success path. The patch touches only `builtin/fsmonitor--daemon.c` and is mechanical, NULL-safe, and covered by the existing assertion that the client reference count is zero at this point. Eric Sunshine has already reviewed the first eleven patches in the series; this one follows the same pattern and is equally uncontroversial.
