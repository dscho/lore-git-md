This is a **bugfix follow-up** in the ongoing memory-leak cleanup thread. Jeff King (Peff) replies to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of his `format-patch` leak fix, expanding the discussion to broader leak-detection infrastructure and related issues.

The idea is to create a single "leaks plus test-vars" job, simplifying CI while maintaining coverage.

Peff notes this also affects SHA-256 and plans to add a `git_hash_discard()` primitive to the hash API, though the work is still in progress.
