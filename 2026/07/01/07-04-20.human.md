This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity. It targets a small but sharp edge case in `loose.c`: the `write_one_object` function unconditionally calls `close(fd)` on its error path, even when `fd` is still `-1` because `open()` failed. While most platforms treat `close(-1)` as a no-op, POSIX marks it as undefined behavior, and sanitizers can flag it as a bug. The fix is a one-line guard (`if (fd >= 0)`) that makes the cleanup safe. The change is mechanical, well-explained, and unlikely to cause regressions.
