This is a maintainer review of the first patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch Coverity-driven cleanup series. Junio C Hamano identifies a critical correctness issue: the patch’s reliance on `errno` to determine success or failure is unsafe because `errno` may have been clobbered by intervening function calls (e.g., `strbuf_release`, `insert_loose_map`). He suggests replacing the `errno` check with an explicit `ferror(fp)` call to reliably detect file-reading errors. The review is substantive, focusing on a latent bug that the original patch would have introduced rather than merely fixing the Coverity-reported leak. Junio’s feedback is authoritative and likely to block this patch until addressed. The fix is small but essential for correctness.
