This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity. The patch plugs two memory leaks in `read_one_dir()` (dir.c) that occur when parsing untracked-cache data fails before ownership is transferred to the caller. Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises a new concern: the function has two additional early returns *after* the point where ownership is transferred (`xmalloc` at line 3857), and those paths appear to leak the newly allocated `*untracked_` structure. The patch does not address these later leaks, leaving the question open whether callers are expected to handle cleanup in those cases. The review shifts focus from the immediate Coverity-reported leaks to a broader correctness issue in the function’s error handling.
