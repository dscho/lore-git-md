This is the first patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch Coverity-driven cleanup series. It fixes a resource leak in `load_one_loose_object_map` (in `loose.c`), where the file pointer `fp` was not closed on the error path. The patch also consolidates the near-duplicate cleanup code at the end of the function by introducing a `ret` variable to track success or failure, ensuring `fp` is always closed via `fclose(fp)` before returning. The change is mechanical and confined to the error-handling flow; it does not alter the function’s public behavior or introduce new symbols. The leak was flagged by Coverity and the fix is straightforward, making this a low-risk, uncontroversial cleanup suitable for early integration.
