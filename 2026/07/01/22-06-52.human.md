This is the maintainer's final sign-off on the four-patch series that standardizes help-flag exit codes across Git's parse-options infrastructure. Junio C Hamano acknowledges the long-standing inconsistency—dating back to 2007—and confirms the series is now approved for merging as a regular bugfix ahead of Git 3.0.

The key points: the change aligns `git rev-parse --parseopt` and all parse-options users with Unix conventions (exit 0 on `--help`/`-h`), while preserving exit 129 for invalid options. Junio notes the risk of breaking scripts is low because `--help` is rarely scripted, and the change only turns a failure exit into a success. The series touches 37 test files to update hard-coded expectations, but the core logic change is minimal and well-contained in `parse-options.c` and `usage.c`.

No further discussion is expected; this is the green light for integration.
