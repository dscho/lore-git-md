This is the eighth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch Coverity-driven cleanup series (v1). It plugs a memory leak in `get_superproject_working_tree()` (submodule.c) by ensuring the `cwd` buffer allocated via `xgetcwd()` is freed on the two early-return paths that previously bypassed cleanup. The fix is mechanical: the early returns are redirected through a new `out` label that calls `free(cwd)` before returning 0. No user-visible behavior changes; the patch is purely about internal correctness. The leak was flagged by Coverity and the fix is uncontroversial.
