This is Junio C Hamano weighing in on the final design question for the `git history squash` series. He asks Phillip Wood to specify concrete problems with `git rebase -i`’s current squash-message template that the new `git history squash` template is meant to solve. Without clear, documented pain points, Junio suggests that diverging from the established `git rebase -i` format would be premature and risks introducing inconsistency for no proven benefit. The question is not whether experimentation is allowed, but whether the experiment is justified by real, observable usability issues. The series remains ready for final review, but this reply puts the template decision on hold until Phillip provides specific examples of what is broken in the current `git rebase -i` workflow.
