This is the sixth patch in the **v5 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()`** for one-sided histories. The patch adds **clock-skew topologies** and **step-count instrumentation** to the test suite, targeting edge cases where commit-date ordering (used in v1 commit graphs) violates topological invariants and triggers premature side-exhaustion.

The patch introduces three new test topologies in `t/t6600-test-reach.sh`:
- **`se-*`**: A side-exhaustion scenario where the P2 side drains while a low-date P1-only commit remains queued, causing the optimization to fire too early and miss the correct merge base.
- **`se2-*`**: A deeper variant where side-exhaustion returns a wrong (too-deep) merge base because the correct candidate never receives both paint sides.
- **Mixed finite/INFINITY generation**: Tests the transition from INFINITY-generation commits (outside the commit-graph) to graph-backed commits, ensuring side-exhaustion behaves correctly at region boundaries.

Each test now includes **deterministic step-count assertions** via `test_paint_down_steps()`, verifying the optimization’s impact across all commit-graph modes (none, full, half, no-gdat). The step counts reveal how date ordering (v1 graphs) can reduce or inflate traversal steps compared to generation ordering.
