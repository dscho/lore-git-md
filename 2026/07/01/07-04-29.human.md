This is the eleventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity. It plugs a leak in the reftable backend: when `reftable_table_refs_for_unindexed()` fails after allocating a `filtering_ref_iterator` but before transferring ownership to the output iterator, both the filter struct and its internal `oid` buffer are left unfreed. The fix adds four lines to the `out` label that release the buffer and free the filter alongside the existing table-iterator cleanup. The change is mechanical, matches the pattern used elsewhere in the series, and is covered by Eric Sunshine’s Reviewed-by on the earlier patches. No user-visible behavior changes; purely internal correctness.
