This is the seventh patch in a **ready-to-merge performance optimization series** (v5, 10 patches total) targeting Git’s `paint_down_to_common()` function, which underpins merge-base calculations. The series delivers **100-1000x speedups** for asymmetric queries (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories) by terminating early when one side of the history is exhausted.

This specific patch **introduces a `paint_state` struct** to track per-side commit counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `mb_candidate_count`) in `paint_down_to_common()`. The counters replace the old `max_nonstale` pointer-based termination logic, enabling cleaner early-exit conditions and paving the way for the side-exhaustion optimization in later patches. The change is **mechanical and behavior-preserving**, though it reduces step counts by one in some topologies due to a "pop first" queue-drain strategy.

Key changes:
- **New struct**: `paint_state` wraps a `prio_queue` and adds counters for PARENT1-only, PARENT2-only, and pending merge-base commits.
- **Counter updates**: `paint_count_update()` adjusts buckets based on paint flags, with an exhaustive switch documenting all valid flag combinations.
- **Queue helpers**: `paint_queue_put()` and `paint_queue_get()` manage enqueue/dequeue and counter updates, replacing the old `nonstale_queue_*` wrappers (which are now unused and removed).
- **Termination logic**: The loop exits when all counters reach zero, equivalent to the old `max_nonstale` check but more explicit.

The patch also updates **documentation** (`paint-down-to-common.adoc`) to reflect the new counter-based termination and tweaks **test expectations** in `t6600-test-reach.sh` to account for the reduced step counts. The changes are **low-risk**, with no on-disk format or user-visible behavior changes.
