This is a three-patch bugfix series from Jeff King targeting memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph bloom-filter code. The leaks only surface when the test suite is run with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`, so they are not user-facing regressions but rather latent issues that would affect CI jobs if leak-checking were enabled. Peff ties these fixes to two other recent patches—one of his own and one from Kaartic Sivaraam—claiming that together they resolve “most of the leaks” that would appear in a leak-checked `linux-TEST-vars` job. The series is small (8 insertions, 2 deletions across three files) and looks like a straightforward cleanup. If you care about test hygiene or plan to enable leak-checking in your CI, these patches are worth a quick look.
