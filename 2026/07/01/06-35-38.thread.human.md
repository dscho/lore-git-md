This thread is a straightforward bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that plugs three memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom-filter code. The leaks—found in `bloom.c`, `revision.c`, and `line-log.c`—only surface when running the test suite with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`, but fixing them is part of a broader push to clean up leaks that would appear if the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs enabled leak-checking. Peff’s patches are conservative, avoiding clever reuse of data structures to sidestep subtle bugs, and the design choices (like explicit freeing in `revision.c` instead of reusing keyvecs) have been endorsed by both Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt.

The series is now effectively complete. All three patches have been reviewed and praised by Junio, Derrick Stolee, and Patrick Steinhardt, with no technical objections or requests for changes. Junio’s latest reply calls the patches "a delight to read," signaling final approval. The fixes are self-contained, touch no on-disk formats, and introduce no behavior changes—just leak fixes that require leak sanitizers to detect. With the OpenSSL SHA-1–related leaks deferred to a future series, this effort will eliminate most of the remaining leaks in the CI jobs once merged. No further discussion or revisions are expected.
