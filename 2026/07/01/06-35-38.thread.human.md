This thread is a focused bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that plugs three memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom-filter code, surfacing only when the test suite runs with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`. The series is now fully reviewed: all three patches have been endorsed by Derrick Stolee, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio C Hamano. The key design decision—explicitly freeing old Bloom-filter keyvecs in `revision.c` rather than reusing them—has drawn particular praise for its conservative, safety-first approach, with Junio’s latest reply reinforcing that choice as the right one. No open questions remain; the series is ready for merging and, together with two other recent leak fixes, will eliminate most leaks in the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs once leak-checking is enabled. The only deferred work is OpenSSL SHA-1–related leaks, which are out of scope for this series.
