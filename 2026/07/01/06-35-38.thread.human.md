This thread is a three-patch bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that plugs memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom filter code. The leaks only appear when the test suite runs with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`, and this series—together with two other recent fixes—will eliminate most leaks that would surface if the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs enabled leak-checking. The remaining OpenSSL SHA-1 leaks are deferred to a later effort.

The first patch, just posted, makes the Bloom-filter slab initialization idempotent by adding a static flag that prevents re-initialization without an intervening cleanup call. The fix is minimal (five lines in `bloom.c`) and purely internal: it changes no behavior or on-disk format, only ensuring that repeated calls to `init_bloom_filters()` do not leak memory. Peff’s commit message walks through three scenarios where the leak could occur, including a concrete reproduction case, and explains why the conservative approach was chosen over deeper refactoring.
