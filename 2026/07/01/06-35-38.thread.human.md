This thread is a short bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that plugs three memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom filter code, surfacing only when the test suite runs with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`. The leaks are in `bloom.c`, `revision.c`, and `line-log.c`; together with two other recent fixes, this series will eliminate most leaks that would appear in the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs if leak-checking were enabled.

Peff’s initial patches drew a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt, who engaged with the technical rationale behind the `revision.c` fix. Peff had rejected an alternative approach—reusing existing Bloom-filter keyvecs—because it could introduce subtle bugs if the pathspec changed between traversals. Patrick endorsed the chosen fix (explicitly freeing old keyvecs before generating new ones), agreeing that the safety trade-off outweighed any negligible efficiency impact. No other participants have weighed in, and the series remains at its initial v1 with no open questions or requested changes. The patches are pure leak fixes with no behavior or on-disk format changes.
