This thread is a three-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) fixing memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom filter code. The leaks appear only when running the test suite with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1` and leak sanitizers enabled. The series targets three specific leaks: one in Bloom-filter slab initialization (`bloom.c`), one in revision-walk keyvec handling (`revision.c`), and one in line-log range-copy logic (`line-log.c`).

Peff’s patches are minimal and self-contained, addressing each leak without changing behavior or on-disk format. The most recent patch fixes a redundant range copy in `line-log.c` that was introduced in 2020 but only recently identified. Together with two other recent leak fixes (one from Peff and one from Kaartic Sivaraam), this series will eliminate most leaks in the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs when leak-checking is enabled. OpenSSL SHA-1–related leaks remain unaddressed and are deferred to a future effort.

No reviews or objections have been raised yet, and the series appears ready for merging. The fixes are well-motivated and directly address the stated issues without introducing new complexity.
