This thread is a straightforward bugfix series from Jeff King (Peff) that plugs three memory leaks in Git’s commit-graph Bloom filter code. The leaks—found in `bloom.c`, `revision.c`, and `line-log.c`—only appear when running the test suite with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1` and leak sanitizers enabled. Peff’s fixes are narrow and surgical: a static flag to prevent redundant slab initialization in `bloom.c`, explicit freeing of old keyvecs in `revision.c` (chosen over a rejected reuse approach for safety), and eliminating a redundant range copy in `line-log.c`.

The series has received light but positive review from Patrick Steinhardt and Derrick Stolee. Patrick endorsed the `revision.c` fix as safer and consistent with Git’s existing behavior, while Derrick called the `bloom.c` and `revision.c` patches "the right fix" and "obviously-correct," respectively. His latest reply adds no new technical feedback, simply thanking Peff for the fixes. No objections or open questions remain, and the series is ready for merging. Together with two other recent leak fixes, this work will eliminate most leaks in the `linux-TEST-vars` CI jobs when leak-checking is enabled; only OpenSSL SHA-1–related leaks are deferred.
