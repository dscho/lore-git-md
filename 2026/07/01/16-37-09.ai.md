This is the eighth patch in the **v5 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()`** for one-sided histories. It implements the **core early-termination logic** that delivers the series' **100-1000x speedups** by exiting the merge-base walk when one side's commit queue is exhausted and no pending merge-base candidates remain.

The patch adds a **new termination condition** to `paint_queue_get()`: when either `parent1_count` or `parent2_count` drops to zero, `mb_candidate_count` is zero, and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`gen_ordered` and `generation < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`), the function returns `NULL` to stop the walk. This ensures correctness by waiting for all merge-base candidates to be dequeued before exiting, and the `INFINITY` gate prevents premature termination in the commit-date-ordered region where topological guarantees do not hold.

**Key changes**:
- **`commit-reach.c`**: The `paint_queue_get()` function now checks for side exhaustion after confirming no merge-base candidates remain. The logic is gated by `gen_ordered` and finite-generation checks.
- **Documentation**: Adds a new section to `paint-down-to-common.adoc` explaining the side-exhaustion condition, its reliance on topological ordering, and why it is skipped in the `INFINITY` region.
- **Tests**: Updates `t6600-test-reach.sh` to reflect reduced step counts (e.g., `45 2 25 3` → `45 1 25 1`) due to earlier loop exits, documented as equivalent behavior.

**Performance impact**: The patch reduces step counts by **30-60%** in common cases (e.g., `merge-base --all v2.0.0 v2.55.0-rc1` drops from 72,264 to 44,589 steps) and by **90%+** in asymmetric queries (e.g., `merge-base --all v2.55.0-rc1 v2.55.0-rc1~5` drops from 110 to 7 steps). The optimization is **deterministic** and verified by step-count assertions in the test suite.
