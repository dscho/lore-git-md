This is the eighth patch in the now-ready-to-merge **v5 series optimizing `paint_down_to_common()`** for one-sided histories. The patch implements the core performance optimization: **early termination when one side of the merge-base walk is exhausted**, eliminating unnecessary traversal of large asymmetric histories (e.g., repositories with import grafts or shallow histories).

The change adds a new termination condition to `paint_queue_get()`: when either `parent1_count` or `parent2_count` drops to zero, no pending merge-base candidates exist (`!mb_candidate_count`), and the walk has entered the finite-generation region (`gen_ordered && generation < GENERATION_NUMBER_INFINITY`), the function returns `NULL` to stop the walk. This ensures correctness by waiting for all merge-base candidates to be dequeued before exiting, while the `INFINITY` gate prevents premature termination in the commit-date-ordered region where topological guarantees do not hold.

**Performance impact is dramatic**: trace2 step counts in git.git drop from **72,264 to 44,589** for `merge-base --all v2.0.0 v2.55.0-rc1` and from **110 to 7** for `merge-base --all v2.55.0-rc1 v2.55.0-rc1~5`. The updated `test_paint_down_steps` assertions in `t6600-test-reach.sh` reflect the reduced step counts (e.g., from 45 to 1 for `in_merge_bases_many:self`), which are documented as equivalent behavior due to an earlier loop exit.

The patch also updates the new technical document (`Documentation/technical/paint-down-to-common.adoc`) to explain the **side-exhaustion condition**, clarifying that the optimization only activates in the finite-generation region where children are always visited before parents, making paint flags final at visit time. This complements the earlier patches in the series that introduced per-side counters (`parent1_count`, `parent2_count`, `mb_candidate_count`) and trace2 instrumentation.
