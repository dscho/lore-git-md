This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity. It plugs a file-descriptor leak in `download_https_uri_to_file()` (defined in `bundle-uri.c`): when the `git-remote-https` child process fails, the patch ensures the read end of the pipe (`child_out`) is closed before returning. The fix is a one-line control-flow tweak that moves the `fclose(child_out)` call before the early return, so the descriptor is always released. No user-visible behavior changes; purely internal correctness. The patch is small, mechanical, and uncontroversial—exactly the kind of cleanup that belongs early in a release cycle.
