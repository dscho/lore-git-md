This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch Coverity-driven cleanup series. It plugs two memory leaks in `read_one_dir()` (dir.c) that occur when parsing the untracked-cache data fails: `ud.untracked` (allocated at line 3846) and `ud.dirs` (allocated at line 3851) are now freed before the function returns -1 on either of the two error paths that lie between those allocations and the point where ownership is transferred to the final `xmalloc`’d struct at line 3857. The fix is mechanical, adds no new symbols, and is covered by existing tests.
