This underscores the practical cost of the change, particularly for maintainers and contributors who frequently work with the codebase.

Overall, Junio’s email reinforces the skepticism about the reorganization’s benefits while validating the concerns about its downsides. His tone suggests that the proposal’s merits may not outweigh the logistical headaches it introduces, especially during the prerelease freeze.
