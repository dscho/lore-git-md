This is a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt, part of his ongoing `git history` series (v8, patch 1/11). It extracts the logic that drops unmerged index entries to stage 0 from `repo_read_index_unmerged()` into a new reusable function `index_state_unmerged_to_stage0()` that operates on an arbitrary `struct index_state *`. The patch also fixes a signedness mismatch in the loop counter (changing `int i` to `unsigned int i`).

The motivation is clear: a subsequent commit will reuse this logic on indexes other than the repository’s default one. The change is mechanical and does not alter behavior; it simply prepares the codebase for the next step in the series. The commit message is concise and the diff is small, focused, and well-explained. No controversy or open questions are evident from this patch alone.
