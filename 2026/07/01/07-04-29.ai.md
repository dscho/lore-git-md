This is the eleventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch Coverity-driven cleanup series (v1). It plugs a resource leak in the reftable backend’s `reftable_table_refs_for_unindexed()` function: when `reftable_buf_add()` fails, the `filter` allocation and any memory it may have grown for its `oid` buffer are left unreleased. The fix adds four lines to the existing `out` label to call `reftable_buf_release(&filter->oid)` and `reftable_free(filter)` alongside the table-iterator cleanup. The leak was flagged by Coverity as CID 1671512. The patch is mechanical, confined to `reftable/table.c`, and does not change any user-visible behavior. Eric Sunshine has already reviewed patches 1–10; this one is the next in line and looks equally straightforward.
