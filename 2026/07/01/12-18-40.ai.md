This is a refactoring patch (part of the `the_repository` removal effort's periphery) in the ongoing `remote-object-info` feature series. It moves the `write_fetch_command_and_capabilities()` function from `fetch-pack.c` to `connect.c` to make it reusable for future protocol commands beyond `fetch`.

The patch is mechanical: it relocates the function verbatim, adds a forward declaration for `struct string_list` in `connect.h`, and removes the original from `fetch-pack.c`. The function itself remains unchanged, including its use of `the_repository` (which will be addressed in later patches). This is a preparatory step for generalizing the function in patch 7/13, where it will be renamed and made available to other commands like `remote-object-info`.

The commit message is clear and links to prior discussion about forward declarations. The patch includes `Helped-by` trailers crediting Jonathan Tan and Christian Couder, and `Signed-off-by` from Calvin Wan, Eric Ju, and Pablo Sabater, indicating broad review engagement. No functional changes are introduced, and the patch is well-scoped for its purpose.
