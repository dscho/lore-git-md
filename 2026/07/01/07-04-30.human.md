This is the twelfth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity in Git’s codebase. It plugs a memory leak in the fsmonitor daemon’s startup sequence: when the daemon fails early (during alias resolution, listener setup, or IPC server initialization), the `current_token_data` structure allocated before those steps is never freed. The fix adds two lines at the start of the `done:` cleanup block to free the token data and clear the pointer, ensuring no leak occurs on error paths while remaining a no-op on the success path. The patch is small, mechanical, and uncontroversial, fitting the series’ goal of internal correctness without changing user-visible behavior. Eric Sunshine has already reviewed the first eleven patches positively; this one looks equally straightforward.
