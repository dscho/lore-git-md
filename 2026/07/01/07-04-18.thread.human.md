Johannes Schindelin has sent a 13-patch series to fix resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity Scan after its recent outage. The series targets internal correctness issues—memory, file-descriptor, and process-handle leaks—without changing user-visible behavior. Most patches have received positive feedback: Eric Sunshine reviewed all 13 and gave his Reviewed-by, while Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff) confirmed the correctness of several key fixes, including patches 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7. Peff even independently reproduced the leak fixed in patch 6 using `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1`.

However, two patches are currently contested. Patch 5, which claims to plug a leak in `run_diff_files()`, was found by Patrick to be ineffective—the `free(dpath)` call operates on a `NULL` pointer, leaving the leak unaddressed. Patch 8, while correct, drew a maintainability warning from Patrick: the function has two separate `free(cwd)` calls, creating a fragility risk if future changes introduce a `goto` after the first free. Junio C Hamano’s recent review of patch 1 adds another blocker: the patch’s reliance on `errno` to detect file-reading errors is unsafe, as `errno` may be clobbered by intervening calls. Junio recommends using `ferror(fp)` instead for reliable error detection.

The series is still at v1, with no maintainer branch assignment yet. The immediate next steps are clear: Johannes will need to revise patches 1 and 5 to address Junio’s and Patrick’s feedback, respectively. The other patches are either approved or awaiting minor follow-up (like patch 8’s maintainability note). If these issues are resolved quickly, the series could progress to Junio’s `next` branch soon, given the straightforward nature of most fixes and the lack of broader design objections.
