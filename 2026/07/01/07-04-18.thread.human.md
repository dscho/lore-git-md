This thread is a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) addressing resource leaks and error-path bugs identified by Coverity Scan in Git's codebase. The series is now complete with the final patch targeting Windows-specific process handle leaks in `compat/mingw.c`.

The goal is purely internal correctness—plugging memory, file-descriptor, and process-handle leaks without changing user-visible behavior. The series has strong maintainer support: Junio C Hamano has stated he will queue it for merging, and most patches carry Reviewed-by tags from Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King (Peff), Eric Sunshine, and Junio himself. Key changes include consolidating error-path cleanup in several functions, adding explicit resource release calls, and fixing unsafe error detection (notably replacing `errno`-based checks with `ferror()` in patch 1/12).

The final patch (12/12) enforces clear ownership of process handles in Windows termination logic, closing a leak and removing a latent use-after-free. With all patches now reviewed and approved, the series is effectively ready for merging. One orthogonal bug in `read_one_dir()` (uninitialized slots in failure paths) will be addressed separately. The fixes are mechanical, well-scoped, and covered by existing tests.
