This thread is a 13-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) addressing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity Scan after its recent resumption. The series targets internal correctness—memory, file descriptor, and process handle leaks—without changing user-visible behavior. All patches have received Reviewed-by from Eric Sunshine, and most are uncontroversial, but two remain contentious.

Patch 6/13, which removes a redundant memory copy in `line_log_process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()`, just gained strong validation from Jeff King (Peff). He independently identified the same leak while testing with `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH_CHANGED_PATHS=1` and confirmed the fix is correct: the intermediate copy was never freed because `add_line_range()` always makes its own internal copy. Peff’s confirmation, especially his reproduction of the issue, reinforces the patch’s soundness.

However, two patches still face unresolved concerns. Patch 5/13’s fix is ineffective—Patrick Steinhardt pointed out that the `dpath` variable is never assigned a non-NULL value before the `free(dpath)` call, leaving the leak unplugged. Patch 7/13 plugs leaks on two early-return paths but leaves two others later in the function that may still leak `ud.untracked` and `ud.dirs`; Steinhardt questioned whether callers are responsible for freeing these. Patch 8/13 is correct but carries a maintainability caveat: Steinhardt flagged a fragility risk due to two separate `free(cwd)` calls in the function, which could lead to double-frees if future changes add a `goto` after the first free.

The series is still at v1, with no maintainer feedback or branch assignment yet. The remaining open questions on patches 5/13 and 7/13 are blocking progress, while the rest of the series appears ready for integration once those are resolved.
