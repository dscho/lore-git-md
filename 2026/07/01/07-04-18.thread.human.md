This thread is a 13-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) addressing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity Scan after its recent outage. The series targets internal correctness—memory, file-descriptor, and process-handle leaks—without changing user-visible behavior. All patches have been reviewed by Eric Sunshine, who gave his Reviewed-by to the entire series.

The first patch, which plugs a file-descriptor leak in `load_one_loose_object_map`, has now received a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt. He confirmed the fix is correct and safe, ruling out any risk of closing an uninitialized file descriptor. This patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration. However, two other patches remain contentious: **Patch 5/13** (a memory-leak fix in `run_diff_files`) was found to be logically flawed—it initializes `dpath` to `NULL` but never assigns it a non-NULL value before freeing, leaving the leak unplugged. **Patch 7/13** (another memory-leak fix in `read_one_dir`) was noted to address only two of four early-return paths, raising concerns about completeness. Additionally, **Patch 8/13** (a memory-leak fix in `get_superproject_working_tree`) was flagged for maintainability risks due to redundant cleanup paths.

The series is still at v1, with no maintainer feedback or branch assignment yet. The unresolved issues in Patches 5 and 7 will need clarification or revision from Dscho before the series can progress. The rest of the patches appear ready for merging once the blocker issues are addressed.
