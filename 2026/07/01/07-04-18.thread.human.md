This thread is a 13-patch cleanup series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) addressing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity Scan after its recent outage. The goal is purely internal correctness—plugging memory, file-descriptor, and process-handle leaks—with no user-visible changes. The series is at its initial v1, with no maintainer feedback yet, though Eric Sunshine has already reviewed the first two patches (both in `loose.c`), giving them his `Reviewed-by`.

The third patch, just posted, fixes a file-descriptor leak in `bundle-uri.c`'s `download_https_uri_to_file()`: when the `git-remote-https` child process fails, the `child_out` stream was left open. The fix is a simple reordering to ensure `fclose(child_out)` runs before returning on error. Like the rest of the series, it’s mechanical and covered by existing tests. So far, the patches have been uncontroversial, and the series is expected to proceed smoothly.
