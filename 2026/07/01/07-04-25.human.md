This is the seventh patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series fixing resource leaks and error-path bugs flagged by Coverity. It plugs two memory leaks in `read_one_dir()` (dir.c), the function that parses the on-disk untracked-cache data. When a parse error occurs after `ud.untracked` and `ud.dirs` have been allocated but before they are transferred into the final `xmalloc`’d structure, the patch now frees both arrays before returning -1. The fix is mechanical, uncontroversial, and already carries Eric Sunshine’s Reviewed-by on the first six patches of the series. No new symbols or user-visible behavior are introduced.
