This is a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt, part of his larger `git history` series (v8, patch 1/11). The patch splits out a small piece of functionality from `repo_read_index_unmerged()` into a new helper function `index_state_unmerged_to_stage0()` that can operate on any index state, not just the repository's default one. The motivation is to prepare for a later commit in the series that will need to perform the same operation on arbitrary indexes.

The change itself is straightforward: the loop that drops unmerged entries to stage 0 is moved into its own function, and the signedness mismatch in the loop counter is fixed (changing `int i` to `unsigned int i`). The patch is well-contained, touching only two files (`read-cache-ll.h` and `read-cache.c`), and the commit message clearly explains the purpose and the mechanical nature of the change.

This looks like a clean, incremental step in a larger effort. The patch is unlikely to be controversial, but it’s also not something that would stand out on its own—it’s clearly part of a bigger picture.
