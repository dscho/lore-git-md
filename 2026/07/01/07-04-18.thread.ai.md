Core problem or goal
Coverity Scan recently resumed after an outage; this 13-patch series addresses the first batch of real (non-false-positive) resource leaks and error-path bugs it flagged in core Git. The goal is to plug memory, file-descriptor, and process-handle leaks, and to ensure proper cleanup when functions return early due to errors. No user-visible behavior changes; purely internal correctness.

Current status
Series is at v1, just posted. No prior versions. No maintainer feedback yet; no branch assignment. Patches 1/13 (loose.c: load_one_loose_object_map) and 2/13 (loose.c: write_one_object) have been reviewed by Eric Sunshine: Reviewed-by given for both, no objections raised. Patch 3/13 (bundle-uri.c: download_https_uri_to_file) has also been reviewed by Eric Sunshine: Reviewed-by provided, confirming the fix as straightforward and correct.

Patch 1/13 consolidates error-path cleanup in `load_one_loose_object_map` by introducing a `ret` variable to ensure `fclose(fp)` is always called before return. Patch 2/13 adds a `fd >= 0` guard in `write_one_object` to prevent undefined behavior from `close(-1)` on open failure. Patch 3/13 reorders `fclose(child_out)` in `download_https_uri_to_file()` to execute unconditionally before returning on error.
