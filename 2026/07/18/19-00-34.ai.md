This is a **build fix** for the `remote-object-info` series, specifically targeting patch 5/13 of v19. The issue is a signed/unsigned comparison warning in `write_fetch_command_and_capabilities()`: `hash_algo_by_ptr()` returns an `unsigned int`, but the local `hash_algo` variable is declared as `int`. While this warning was suppressed in `fetch-pack.c` (which defines `DISABLE_SIGN_COMPARE_WARNINGS`), it breaks the build when the function is moved to `connect.c` (which does not suppress such warnings).

SZEDER Gábor identified the problem and proposed the fix: change `hash_algo` to `unsigned int` in the function. Pablo Sabater acknowledges the issue and agrees to incorporate the fix as a preparatory step in `fetch-pack.c` before the function is relocated to `connect.c`. The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior, but it is critical for the series to compile cleanly. Given the series is already queued for `next`, this fix should be included before graduation to `master`. The discussion confirms the fix is straightforward and uncontroversial.
