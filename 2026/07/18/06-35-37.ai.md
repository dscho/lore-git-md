This leaves the door open for future work but signals no intent to address it here. The response is matter-of-fact and avoids escalating the discussion into a design debate.

The tone remains collaborative, and no v2 is explicitly promised. The series remains queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, but René’s willingness to drop patch 5/5 if needed suggests flexibility.
