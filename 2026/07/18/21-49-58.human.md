The patch ensures that bare OID requests (those without any requested attributes) trigger an unconditional existence check on the server, closing a gap where such requests could silently succeed without validating the object.

The change introduces a new `get_object_info()` wrapper around `odb_read_object_info_extended()` that sets `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` and `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flags to prevent partial-clone servers from fetching missing objects or rescanning packs unnecessarily. The wrapper returns `OBJ_BAD` for unknown objects, and the condition for recognizing objects is tightened from `< 0` to `<= OBJ_NONE` to catch both `OBJ_BAD` and `OBJ_NONE` as unrecognized cases. This ensures that unrecognized OIDs now return `<OID> SP` regardless of the number of attributes requested, while recognized OIDs return just `<OID>` for bare requests.

It also adds three new tests in `t/t5701-git-serve.sh` to verify the behavior for bare OID requests, unrecognized OIDs, and unrecognized OIDs with the `size` attribute requested. The tests cover all edge cases introduced by the change and confirm the server's response matches the updated protocol specification.
