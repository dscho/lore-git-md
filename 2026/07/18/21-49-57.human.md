This is a refactoring patch in the long-running `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series, now at v20 and queued for `next`. It moves variable initialization out of the `FETCH_CHECK_LOCAL` state in `do_fetch_pack_v2()` to a point before the state machine begins, ensuring the variables are set regardless of the initial state. The change is mechanical and preparatory: a later patch in the series will introduce an alternative initial state, and this patch ensures the necessary variables (`allow_unadvertised_object_request`, `use_sideband`, and `args->deepen`) are initialized in both cases. No behavior changes are introduced.

The patch is authored by Calvin Wan, carried forward by Eric Ju and Pablo Sabater. It touches only `fetch-pack.c` and is uncontroversial, with no review feedback or objections in the thread. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and confirms the lack of behavior change. This is a small but necessary step in the series' transport-layer refactoring, enabling future work without disrupting existing functionality.
