This is a surface-level review of the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series, focusing on documentation clarity. Matt Hunter raises two minor suggestions about the updated `Documentation/git-history.adoc` file, both aimed at improving readability rather than addressing technical correctness.

First, he questions a sentence that explains how the *effects* of all commits in the range are preserved even when the message changes. While accurate, he finds it distracting in context and suggests removing it to keep the paragraph focused on the `amend!` message behavior. Second, he proposes splitting the description of `--reedit-message` into its own paragraph to avoid overloading the reader with details about `fixup!`, `squash!`, and `amend!` handling in a single block.

Neither suggestion affects the implementation or functionality of the `--reedit-message` feature, which remains complete and ready for integration. The feedback is purely editorial and unlikely to block merging.
