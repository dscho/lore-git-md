This is a **substantive review** of patch 3/4 in Toon Claes’s performance series for `git last-modified`. Jeff King (Peff) engages with Taylor Blau’s earlier correctness concern about `--show-trees` and proposes a design alternative.

Peff confirms Taylor’s observation that `--show-trees` currently includes ancestor directories even when the pathspec only matches files inside them, calling this behavior surprising. He notes that the documentation incorrectly claims `--show-trees` has no effect without `-r`, which is demonstrably false. Instead of guarding the Bloom-filter optimization with `!lm->show_trees` (which would make the optimization nearly useless, since `--show-trees` is the common case), Peff suggests a more fundamental change: filter out ancestor directories from the output when they are not explicitly covered by the pathspec. This would restore the optimization’s effectiveness while producing less surprising output. He frames this as an acceptable trade-off for an experimental command, where such corner cases are expected to be ironed out.
