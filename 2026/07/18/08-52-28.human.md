This is a surface-level review of the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series. Matt Hunter points out two minor documentation issues in the `git-history.adoc` man page, both related to clarity and flow rather than technical correctness.

The first concerns a sentence that explains how all commit changes are folded in even when the message is altered by an `amend!` marker. Hunter suggests removing it entirely, arguing it disrupts the paragraph’s focus and doesn’t add meaningful understanding. The second is a paragraph break suggestion: the description of `--reedit-message`’s template behavior would read better as a standalone paragraph rather than continuing the previous one. Neither issue affects functionality, and both are framed as personal readability preferences rather than blocking concerns. The series itself remains ready for integration, with all technical feedback resolved in v9.
