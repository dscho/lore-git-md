This is the final reply in René Scharfe’s five-patch series converting Git’s lockfile and tempfile APIs to be repository-aware as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The email responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review of patch 5/5, which requested two clarifications: an explanation for why only a subset of callers were converted, and a compile-time guard to prevent accidental reintroduction of `the_repository` dependencies.

René’s response is brief and pragmatic. He explains that the converted callers were simply those that already had access to a `struct repository *` other than `the_repository`—a straightforward criterion that aligns with the series’ incremental approach. On the safety guard, he acknowledges the value of Patrick’s suggestion but suggests it belongs in a separate follow-up series, noting that patch 5/5 was included in this series only because it was "easy to write." He leaves the door open to dropping the patch entirely if the partial conversion is deemed too messy.

The tone is cooperative but non-committal. No v2 is explicitly promised, but the exchange leaves the series in a holding pattern: Junio’s `seen` branch already includes it, and the feedback is more about future-proofing than blocking. The unresolved question is whether the compile-time guard will materialize as a follow-up or be deferred indefinitely. For now, the series remains a clean, mechanical step toward eliminating `the_repository` globals, with no user-visible impact.
