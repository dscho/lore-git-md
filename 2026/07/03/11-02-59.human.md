This is a **substantive review** of the `includeIf.worktree` patch in the v6 series. Patrick Steinhardt identifies a behavioral inconsistency between the new `worktree` condition and the existing `gitdir` condition when dealing with symlinked paths.

The review demonstrates that `gitdir` patterns match *both* the symlinked path and the real (resolved) path, whereas the current `worktree` implementation only matches the real path. This creates an unexpected asymmetry for users who rely on symlinks in their worktree setups. Patrick provides a concrete test case showing how `gitdir` allows both forms while `worktree` rejects the symlinked path, which could break existing workflows or force users to update their configurations unnecessarily.

The feedback is narrowly scoped but technically significant: it doesn’t question the overall design of the feature, but rather a specific edge case in path resolution that could affect real-world usability. The proposed test case is clear and actionable, making it easy for the author to verify the fix. This is a classic example of a substantive review—it engages with the *behavior* of the code, not just its style or structure, and highlights a potential source of user confusion. The patch series otherwise appears ready for merging, but this issue should be addressed before final integration.
