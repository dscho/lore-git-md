This thread is about a patch series (v6) by Toon Claes that adds `includeIf.worktree:<pattern>` and `includeIf.worktree/i:<pattern>` conditions to Git’s config system, allowing conditional config inclusion based on the working tree’s realpath. The feature is designed to improve usability in multi-worktree setups where `gitdir`-based patterns are cumbersome due to their reliance on Git’s internal `.git/worktrees/` directory structure.

The series has been well-received, with all prior feedback from Junio C Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and Phillip Wood addressed. The preparatory refactoring (generalizing `include_by_gitdir()` to `include_by_path()`) and the core implementation are uncontroversial, and Junio has endorsed the current documentation and test patterns, suggesting only a minor follow-up for documentation consolidation. However, Patrick Steinhardt recently identified a **behavioral inconsistency** between the new `worktree` condition and the existing `gitdir` condition: while `gitdir` matches both symlinked and real paths, `worktree` resolves symlinks to the real path only. This discrepancy could confuse users who expect the two conditions to behave similarly when dealing with symlinks. Patrick’s feedback is precise and actionable, providing a test case to demonstrate the issue and suggesting alignment with `gitdir`’s behavior.

The series is effectively ready for merging, pending resolution of this edge case. The author will need to decide whether to adjust `worktree` to match symlinks (like `gitdir`) or document the divergence as intentional. All reviews to date have been substantive, engaging with pattern-matching edge cases, Windows compatibility, and test coverage. No other open questions remain.
