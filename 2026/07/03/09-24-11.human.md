This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch v2 series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient in CI. It removes the macOS RAM-disk workaround that was added in early 2024 to speed up slow network-attached storage. The patch is a straightforward CI configuration change: it drops the `before_script` that created a 4 GB RAM disk and points `TEST_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` back to `/tmp/test-output`.

The motivation is simple: the next patch in the series enables `GIT_TEST_LONG` on macOS, and several expensive tests write up to 8 GB of data. A 4 GB RAM disk is too small, and even a 12 GB disk (possible on the new 16 GB runners) proved flaky in practice. Patrick measured the slowdown: clang jobs go from 18 to 25 minutes, meson from 21 to 33 minutes. That’s acceptable because the macOS jobs are still not the slowest in the pipeline, and the trade-off is a stable, predictable environment.

The change is uncontroversial and well-justified. No one has objected, and the patch is ready to merge. If you care about CI reliability or macOS test performance, this is a small but important piece of the larger series.
