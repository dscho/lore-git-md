This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient for CI. It targets `t7900-maintenance.sh`, an expensive test that creates a 2 GB repository but never cleans it up, wasting disk space and potentially interfering with later tests.

The patch isolates the 2 GB repository into a dedicated sub-repository (`expensive-repo`) that is deleted immediately after the test via `test_when_finished`. It also disables auto-maintenance in the sub-repository to prevent races with the test’s manual maintenance commands. The change reduces peak disk usage and ensures the expensive test data does not linger or affect subsequent tests.

The refactoring is mechanical and well-motivated: the commit message clearly explains the problem (clogged disk, potential interference) and the solution (isolation and cleanup). No user-facing behavior changes are introduced, and the patch is uncontroversial. It complements earlier patches in the series that reduce disk or CPU usage in other expensive tests. The series as a whole looks ready for integration, with no remaining substantive objections.
