This is a **build system** and **platform compatibility** follow-up in the ongoing `USE_NSEC` discussion. D. Ben Knoble responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier skepticism about the `nanosec` Meson option, reaffirming his support for the original patch while acknowledging the broader debate about whether the build knob should exist at all.

Ben still favors merging the patch as-is, arguing that it maintains parity with Autotools and Makefile builds, allowing distributions like Gentoo to offer the option to users who know their filesystem is safe. He notes that if the discussion leads to removing `USE_NSEC` entirely, the Meson option would naturally go away too—but until then, consistency across build systems is valuable. He also signals openness to exploring a runtime flag (e.g., `core.useNsec`) if the consensus shifts in that direction, though he’s unsure how to test such a change.

The email doesn’t resolve the deeper questions about `USE_NSEC`’s viability or default behavior, but it keeps the original patch alive while the larger design discussion continues. For now, the patch remains queued for merging, but the thread’s focus has clearly expanded beyond build system parity to fundamental questions about timestamp handling.
