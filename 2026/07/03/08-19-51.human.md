This is a test modernization patch, part of the ongoing effort to replace low-level shell test constructs with Git’s modern test helpers. Patrick Steinhardt reviews Marcelo Machado Lage’s patch that updates `t/t9811-git-p4-label-import.sh` to use `test_path_is_file` and `test_path_is_missing` instead of `test -f` and `! test -f`.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused. Patrick notes that the new helpers provide stronger guarantees—`test_path_is_missing` checks for the absence of the path entirely, not just that it isn’t a file—though he considers this a strict improvement. He also suggests splitting a long chained command into separate lines for better readability and recommends briefly explaining the motivation in the commit message (why the modern helpers are preferable). The feedback is constructive and minor; the patch itself is already well-scoped and uncontroversial. No fundamental objections or deeper technical concerns are raised.
