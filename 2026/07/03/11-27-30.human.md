This is a **substantive review** in Jeff King’s memory-leak bugfix series, specifically on patch 4/9. Patrick Steinhardt engages with the design choice of `git_hash_discard()` versus `git_hash_release()` and proposes an alternative: making `git_hash_discard()` idempotent so it can be called unconditionally, eliminating the need to track whether the hash context was already finalized. His question is technical and forward-looking—if the answer is "yes," it could simplify several patches in the series (notably patch 7, which currently uses a `hash_ctx_valid` flag to avoid double-discards). The tone is collaborative, not prescriptive, and the suggestion is clearly motivated by a desire to reduce complexity rather than by a naming preference. No objections to the current patch; this is an exploration of a potential future improvement.
