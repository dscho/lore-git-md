This is the test coverage patch for Souma’s three-part series that teaches `git history` to sign rewritten commits. It adds GPG-gated regression tests in `t3451` (reword), `t3452` (split), and `t3453` (fixup) to verify that signing is triggered by both the `commit.gpgsign` config variable and the `-S/--gpg-sign` command-line flags, that command-line flags override config, and that replayed descendant commits retain their original signing status. The tests are thorough and follow the established pattern of the existing history test scripts.
