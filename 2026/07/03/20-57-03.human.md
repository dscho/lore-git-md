This is the third and final patch in the v6 series introducing the `--linearize` option to `git replay`. Junio C Hamano’s reply is a surface-level review focusing on two documentation points in the man page.

First, he questions whether Johannes Schindelin should remain as a co-author and retain the Signed-off-by line, given the extensive changes in this iteration. The implication is that Toon Claes may now be the sole responsible author for any latent issues, though Junio does not insist on a change—this is a gentle nudge rather than a demand.

He suggests either adding a brief rationale (e.g., “because linearizing merges would break the revert order”) or moving the incompatibility to a BUGS/LIMITATIONS section rather than stating it as an absolute rule. The tone is constructive, leaving the decision to the author.

No technical objections are raised; the patch remains technically complete and ready for review. The interface design debate is not revisited.
