This is the documentation patch in Souma's three-part series teaching `git history` to sign rewritten commits. It updates `Documentation/git-history.adoc` to document the new `--gpg-sign[=<key-id>]`, `-S[<key-id>]`, and `--no-gpg-sign` options for the `fixup`, `reword`, and `split` subcommands.

The patch adds a short section under OPTIONS that mirrors the wording used in the `git-commit` man page: the key-id argument is optional, sticks to the option without a space, and `--no-gpg-sign` overrides both configuration and earlier `--gpg-sign` on the command line. The synopsis lines for all three subcommands are updated to include the new flags. No other documentation files are touched, and the change is purely additive. The commit message is concise and to the point.
