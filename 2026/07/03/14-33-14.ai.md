This is a follow-up in the feature-request thread about programmatic commit editing during interactive rebase. The email is a brief, surface-level reply from Matt Hunter that adds a small but useful clarification to brian m. carlson’s earlier demonstration of `GIT_SEQUENCE_EDITOR`.

Matt thanks brian for the tip, then points out that `--autosquash` no longer requires `-i` (interactive mode) as of commit f8f87e082798. This means the alias brian shared (`srebase`) can be simplified: `GIT_SEQUENCE_EDITOR=true git rebase --autosquash` is now sufficient, and the `-m -i` flags are redundant. The observation is factual and directly relevant to anyone scripting rebase automation, but it does not engage with the original feature request, the sequencer internals, or any broader design questions. It is a minor correction rather than a substantive review or new proposal.
