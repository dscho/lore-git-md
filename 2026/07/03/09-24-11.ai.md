This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch v2 series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient in CI. It removes the macOS RAM-disk workaround that was introduced to mitigate slow network-attached storage on GitLab’s macOS runners. The change is motivated by the upcoming enablement of `GIT_TEST_LONG` (patch 9), which will run expensive tests that write up to 8 GB of data—far exceeding the 4 GB RAM-disk capacity. Benchmarks show the workaround’s removal increases job runtime by 7–12 minutes (from 18–21 minutes to 25–33 minutes), but the trade-off is justified by eliminating flakiness and avoiding out-of-space errors. The reftable backend job remains unaffected, likely due to lower I/O demands. The patch touches only `.gitlab-ci.yml`, removing the RAM-disk creation and reverting `TEST_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` to `/tmp/test-output`. No new configuration keys or user-facing behavior changes are introduced. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of stable, predictable CI execution.
