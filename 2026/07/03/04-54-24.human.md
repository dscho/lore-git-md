This is the fifth patch in Michael Montalbo’s six-patch series (v3) converting bare `grep` assertions in Git’s test suite to `test_grep`. The patch itself is a mechanical conversion of roughly 2800 `grep` calls across 340 test files, replacing them with `test_grep` to improve debuggability—`test_grep` prints the file contents on failure, whereas bare `grep` fails silently.

The conversion is driven by the `greplint.pl` linter introduced in the next patch (6/6), but the author took care to handle edge cases manually beforehand. Two notable exceptions are preserved with `# lint-ok` annotations: one in `t1400-update-ref.sh` where `.git/packed-refs` is absent under the reftable backend (guarded by a `REFFILES` prerequisite), and another in `t7450-bad-git-dotfiles.sh` where `git~2` (an NTFS 8.3 short name) may not exist depending on platform settings. The linter skips `grep` calls used as data filters (e.g., `grep -vf` or with redirects) or in pipelines, as these require judgment calls about output behavior.

The patch also reorders pre-existing `! test_grep` calls to `test_grep !` to preserve diagnostic output on failure. Three pre-existing bugs—where `grep` was missing file arguments—were fixed in earlier patches (4/6) to avoid `test_grep`’s `BUG` on missing files. The conversion is exhaustive but intentionally conservative, leaving room for a follow-up series to address the remaining edge cases once conventions are established. The series is now ready for merging, with all feedback incorporated in v3.
