It prints one line per affected branch (e.g., *"Would delete branch merged (was abc123)."*) and rejects nonsensical combinations like `--dry-run --quiet`. The implementation is minimal and safe: it skips ref/config updates when the `DELETE_BRANCH_DRY_RUN` flag is set, ensuring no side effects.
