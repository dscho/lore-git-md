This is the third patch in Taylor Blau’s five-part bugfix series hardening Git’s packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. The patch adjusts MIDX verification to accept any duplicate copy of an object, not just the one returned by `find_pack_entry_one()`.

The core change is in `midx.c`: instead of comparing the MIDX-recorded offset directly against the result of `find_pack_entry_one()` (which may return a different member of a duplicate run), the new helper `pack_index_has_oid_at_offset()` scans the contiguous block of duplicate OIDs in the source pack index to confirm that the exact OID/offset pair exists somewhere in the run. This matches reader behavior—`midx_to_pack_pos()` reconstructs the RIDX position from the recorded (pack, offset) pair, and if the offset is not the one selected by the MIDX writer, the lookup falls back to normal packing rather than failing.

The test in `t5308-pack-detect-duplicates.sh` writes a MIDX over an existing duplicate-pack fixture, confirms that the MIDX-selected offset differs from the one returned by `find_pack_entry_one()`, and then verifies the MIDX and runs `git fsck` as an application-level check. The `test-find-pack` helper is extended with a `--show-offset` flag to expose the offset chosen by `find_pack_entry_one()` for testing.

The patch is narrowly scoped, well-motivated, and directly addresses the invariant described in the commit message: same-pack duplicates have no canonical representation, so every matching physical copy is valid. No new symbols are introduced; the change is confined to MIDX verification logic and its test coverage.
