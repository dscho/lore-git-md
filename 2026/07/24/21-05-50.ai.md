This is the first patch in Taylor Blau’s five-part bugfix series hardening Git’s packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. It adds a new test in `t/t5308-pack-detect-duplicates.sh` to verify that per-pack reverse indexes correctly retain and map duplicate entries back to their physical pack order.

The test constructs a pack containing four objects in the sequence A, B, A, C, then confirms that the reverse index preserves both copies of A. It checks that the on-disk size of B (reported by `git cat-file --batch-check="%(objectsize:disk)"`) stops at the second A rather than extending through to C, exercising both the on-disk and in-memory reverse-index implementations. The patch is narrowly scoped: it introduces no new code, only test coverage for an edge case that the rest of the series will address. The rationale is clear and the test design is thorough, covering both reverse-index backends and verifying the expected behavior with precise assertions.
