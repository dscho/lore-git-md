This thread began with a patch from tnyman@openai.com to fix a Coccinelle 1.1.1 performance regression in `builtin/branch.c`. The issue, caused by reusing a loop index variable declared at function scope across two separate loops, triggered a six-hour timeout in the static-analysis CI job. The patch resolved this by declaring each loop index in its respective `for` loop, reducing the Coccinelle check from a timeout to 0.06 seconds while preserving the function's runtime behavior.

The discussion initially centered on whether to work around the toolchain limitation in the codebase or upgrade the CI toolchain to Coccinelle 1.3.0+, which includes a fix for the exponential slowdown. Jeff King (Peff) advocated for upgrading the toolchain, while the patch author and Harald Nordgren (whose `hn/branch-delete-merged` topic this patch applies to) supported the workaround as a pragmatic solution. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has now accepted the patch as a stylistic improvement and a necessary workaround for the Coccinelle 1.1.1 limitation. He incorporated it into Harald's topic, which is now confirmed stable. The patch touches only three lines in `builtin/branch.c`, passes all 190 tests in `t3200-branch.sh`, and introduces no new symbols or behavior changes.

While the broader question of whether to upgrade the CI toolchain remains open, this specific patch is now resolved and merged. The decision to accept the workaround reflects a balance between addressing the immediate CI issue and avoiding potential disruptions from a toolchain upgrade.
