This thread began with a patch from tnyman@openai.com addressing a Coccinelle 1.1.1 performance regression in `builtin/branch.c` that caused the static-analysis job to time out after six hours. The fix—a mechanical change to declare loop indices in their respective `for` loops rather than sharing one declared at function scope—was accepted as a pragmatic workaround, reducing the Coccinelle check from a timeout to 0.06 seconds and completing the full `coccicheck` run in 47 seconds. Junio C Hamano merged the patch into Harald Nordgren’s `hn/branch-delete-merged` topic, confirming it as both a stylistic improvement and a resolution to the CI issue, with all 190 tests in `t3200-branch.sh` passing.

The discussion also touched on whether to address the root cause by upgrading the CI toolchain to Coccinelle 1.3.0+ or continue working around toolchain limitations in the codebase. Junio and Jeff King (Peff) favored the toolchain upgrade, while the patch itself represented a symptom-level fix. A secondary issue about type consistency in the loop indices was resolved in favor of an immediate, low-risk correction (changing the second loop index from `size_t` to `int` to match `candidates.nr`), with broader standardization of ref-counting types deferred. Taylor Blau’s recent follow-up simply reaffirmed the patch as a "strict improvement" to the code, closing the thread on a confirmatory note. The patch remains merged to `master`, and the immediate follow-up for the type correction is the next expected step.
