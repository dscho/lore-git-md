This is Junio C Hamano’s reply to the cover letter of Taylor Blau’s five-patch series hardening the packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. Junio corrects the framing: packs with duplicate entries are not merely “unusual” but **invalid**, and Git’s current tolerance of them is analogous to its tolerance of corrupted loose objects—it does not actively reject them until a command like `git fsck`, `git repack`, or another operation tries to use the object. He explicitly welcomes the series’ goal of **early detection** before such invalid packs can propagate or cause downstream issues. No technical objections or requests for changes are raised; the tone is supportive and the message serves as an implicit Ack for the overall direction.
