This is a CI/build system patch that has reached a resolution. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed and accepted the patch as a pragmatic workaround for a Coccinelle 1.1.1 performance regression. The patch modifies `builtin/branch.c` to declare loop indices inside their respective `for` loops instead of sharing one declared at function scope, avoiding a pathological slowdown in the `separate_loop_index` rule from `tools/coccinelle/strvec.cocci`. The change is mechanical and preserves the function's runtime behavior while reducing the Coccinelle check from a six-hour timeout to 0.06 seconds.

Junio acknowledges that the patch is a workaround for a toolchain limitation but endorses it as a stylistic improvement in this case. He notes that the performance issue stems from the interaction between Coccinelle and the `strvec.cocci` rule, not from Coccinelle alone, and muses about whether the rule could be made more efficient. The patch has already been incorporated into Harald Nordgren's `hn/branch-delete-merged` topic, which is now confirmed stable with this fix.

The discussion remains open on whether the project should upgrade the CI toolchain to Coccinelle 1.3.0+ (which includes a fix for the exponential slowdown) or continue working around such issues in the codebase. However, for this specific patch, the path forward is clear: the workaround is accepted, and no further changes are required at this time. The patch touches only three lines in `builtin/branch.c`, passes all 190 tests in `t3200-branch.sh`, and does not introduce new symbols or behavior changes.
