This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series (v3) to make Git’s on-disk object database (ODB) structures pluggable, part of the larger ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to decouple backend-specific directory creation (like `objects/pack/` and `objects/info/`) from the core repository setup code, enabling future ODB backends to implement custom layouts without modifying Git’s internals. The first patch in the series is a correctness fix for loose-object map loading in repositories with compatibility hash algorithms (e.g., SHA-256 with SHA-1 compatibility) and alternates, and Junio has marked it as "will merge to 'next'" in his latest "What's cooking" report. The rest of the series is uncontroversial and nearly ready for merging, though one architectural question remains open.

The series has seen thorough review from Junio, Justin Tobler, and Toon Claes. Junio’s feedback focused on latent correctness issues (e.g., an unconditional downcast that would BUG if a non-"files" backend were used) and design questions about the `create_on_disk` callback’s naming and extensibility. Justin Tobler’s review raised technical concerns about downcast safety and initialization sequence design, but he later approved the series after Patrick addressed them. Toon Claes provided a critical test case for the first patch, elevating its importance, and proposed an alternative design for the fourth patch that would revert its refactoring of the ODB initialization sequence. This proposal remains unresolved and could require a v4 if Patrick adopts it.

The sixth and final patch, which Toon just reviewed, implements the `create_on_disk` callback in the "files" backend, completing the series. Toon’s feedback here is minor: he notes a redundant use of `DB_ENVIRONMENT` in `setup.c` and a non-translatable `die()` message, neither of which are blockers. The patch itself is straightforward and aligns with the series’ architectural direction. The only remaining open question is Toon’s alternative design for the fourth patch, which challenges the current approach of deferring ODB creation to `create_object_database()`. If Patrick adopts this simpler API, the series may need a v4, but otherwise, the remaining patches (including this one) are ready for merging.
