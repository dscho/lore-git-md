This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series to make Git’s on-disk object database (ODB) structures pluggable, part of the larger ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to decouple backend-specific directory creation (like `objects/pack` or `objects/info`) from the core repository setup code, allowing future ODB backends to define their own layouts without modifying Git’s internals. The series also fixes two latent bugs in loose-object map loading and removes hardcoded assumptions about initialization order.

The first version of the series was posted recently and is under review. Junio Hamano has already chimed in on the first patch, agreeing it correctly addresses a bug by ensuring loose-object maps are loaded from the right ODB source path. His feedback was brief and positive, focusing on interface consistency rather than deeper technical concerns, so this patch is likely to move forward unchanged. The rest of the series hasn’t seen substantive review yet, but the infrastructure changes are incremental and don’t alter the on-disk format or user-facing behavior. The work is foundational for future ODB backends and doesn’t depend on external changes.
