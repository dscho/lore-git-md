This is the first patch in the twenty-first iteration of the GSoC-driven `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series. It fixes a **memory leak** in `transport-helper.c` where `data->name` (allocated during `transport_helper_init()`) was not being freed when disconnecting a transport whose helper had not yet fully started. The fix is minimal: moving `FREE_AND_NULL(data->name)` outside the conditional block ensures the field is always freed on disconnect. The change is mechanical, well-contained, and directly addresses a leak that was identified and documented in an earlier review. No controversy or follow-up is expected; this is a straightforward correctness fix that supports the robustness goals of the larger series.
