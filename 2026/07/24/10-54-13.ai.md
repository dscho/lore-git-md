This is a refactoring patch in the GSoC `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series (v21, 2/13). It tightens variable scope and type consistency in `builtin/cat-file.c` and `fetch-pack.c` by moving loop counters into the `for` statement and switching from `int` to `size_t` for object-counting variables.

The patch declares loop counters (`i`) inside the `for` statement when they are only used within the loop, which limits their scope and improves readability. It also updates the type of these counters from `int` to `size_t` to match the type of the variables they iterate over (e.g., `nr`, `alloc`), avoiding signed/unsigned mismatches. The `nr` parameter of `dispatch_calls()` is updated to `size_t` to align with its callers, which already pass values of that type.

The changes are mechanical and do not alter behavior. They touch two functions in `cat-file.c` (`dispatch_calls` and `free_cmds`) and one loop in `fetch-pack.c` (`write_fetch_command_and_capabilities`). The patch is part of a larger series that is technically complete and queued for `next`, with no unresolved concerns. This specific patch addresses minor hygiene improvements and is unlikely to be controversial.
