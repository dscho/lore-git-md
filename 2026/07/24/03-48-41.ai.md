This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series making on-disk ODB structures pluggable, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. It refactors the loading of loose object maps to remove two hard-coded assumptions: that the primary ODB is always the "files" backend, and that the ODB must be initialized before the repository’s compatibility hash algorithm is set.

The patch moves the loose-object-map loading logic into the "loose" backend itself, calling `loose_object_map_load()` during backend initialization rather than during repository setup. This change ensures that the map is loaded from the correct ODB source path (fixing a latent bug where the map was always loaded from the common `objects/` directory) and defers ODB creation until after the backend is known. The diff touches `loose.c`, `loose.h`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `repository.c`, and `setup.c`, exposing a new public function (`loose_object_map_load()`) and reordering initialization steps in `apply_repository_format()`.

This is a clean, mechanical refactoring with no user-visible behavior change. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and the specific downsides being addressed, and the patch itself is narrowly focused on the stated goal. It’s a prerequisite for future ODB backends that don’t follow the traditional loose/pack layout, and the changes align with the series’ overall goal of decoupling backend-specific directory creation from core setup logic.
