This is the first patch in Taylor Blau’s five-part bugfix series hardening Git’s packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. It adds a new test in `t/t5308-pack-detect-duplicates.sh` to verify that reverse indexes—both on-disk and in-memory—correctly preserve duplicate entries rather than collapsing them.

The test constructs a pack with four objects in the order A, B, A, C, then confirms that the disk size reported for B stops at the second A (not extending through to C). It exercises both reverse-index implementations by toggling `GIT_TEST_REV_INDEX_DIE_IN_MEMORY` and `GIT_TEST_REV_INDEX_DIE_ON_DISK`. The patch is narrowly scoped, well-motivated by the cover letter’s explanation of current mishandling, and includes clear assertions about expected behavior. No code changes are made here—only test coverage—so the patch itself is uncontroversial and looks ready for review.
