This is a brief but significant reply from Junio C Hamano to Taylor Blau’s five-patch series hardening Git’s packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. Junio corrects the framing of the problem: duplicate entries in packfiles are not merely “unusual” but **invalid**, and Git’s current tolerance of them is an accident of implementation rather than a design choice. He likens the situation to corrupted loose objects, which Git also accepts until an operation like `git fsck` or `git repack` triggers detection. Junio explicitly welcomes the series’ goal of catching these problematic packs earlier, before they can propagate or cause subtle breakage.

The message is short and carries no technical objections or requests for changes. Instead, it provides authoritative context that elevates the series from a niche edge-case fix to a general correctness improvement. By endorsing the motivation, Junio signals that the patches are on the right track and likely to be accepted once review converges. No merge status is implied; the series remains under review.
