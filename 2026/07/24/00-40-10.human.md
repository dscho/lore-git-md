This is a test patch, part of Fabian Pottbäcker’s three-patch series adding scp-style URL support to `git config --url`.

The patch plugs a small gap in the test suite: it mirrors the existing `--get-urlmatch` tests for the new `--url` option. Fourteen lines are added to `t/t1300-config.sh`, duplicating each `--get-urlmatch` invocation with a corresponding `--url` call. The tests cover boolean lookups, wildcard matching, and multi-value returns, ensuring that `--url` behaves identically to `--get-urlmatch` for the same URLs.

The change is purely mechanical and uncontroversial; it simply extends coverage to the new option without altering any logic. No new edge cases or failure modes are introduced. The patch is the second of three in the series, following the unit-test normalization in patch 1 and preceding the actual implementation switch in patch 3.
