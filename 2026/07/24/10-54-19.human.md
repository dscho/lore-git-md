This is a refactoring patch in the long-running `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series, now at its 21st iteration and cooking in `next`. The patch moves variable initialization out of the `FETCH_CHECK_LOCAL` state and into the function prologue of `do_fetch_pack_v2()` so that the same setup will run regardless of which state the state machine starts in. A follow-up patch will introduce a second entry point, making this change necessary. The diff is mechanical—six lines moved up, six deleted—and the commit message explains the motivation clearly. No behavior change is claimed or expected. The patch is authored by Calvin Wan, with help from Jonathan Tan and Christian Couder, and carries sign-offs from all three plus the series maintainer Pablo Sabater. It looks like a clean, uncontroversial preparatory step.
