This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series, now at version 21 and queued for `next`. The patch tightens variable scope by declaring loop counters inside `for()` statements where they are only used within the loop, and updates the types of counters that iterate over object counts from `int` to `size_t` for consistency.

The changes are mechanical and confined to two files: `builtin/cat-file.c` and `fetch-pack.c`. In `cat-file.c`, the patch moves the loop counter `i` into the `for()` statement in three places (`dispatch_calls()`, `free_cmds()`, and `batch_objects_command()`), and updates the `nr` parameter of `dispatch_calls()` to `size_t` since all callers already pass a value of that type. In `fetch-pack.c`, it moves the loop counter `i` into the `for()` statement in `write_fetch_command_and_capabilities()`. The patch is well-motivated: limiting variable scope reduces the risk of accidental misuse, and using `size_t` for object counts avoids signed/unsigned mismatches.
