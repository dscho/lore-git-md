This is the fourth patch in Taylor Blau’s five-patch bugfix series hardening Git’s packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries. The patch makes the `test-tool bitmap` helper explicitly reject packs that contain duplicate objects, preventing a BUG() crash in the bitmap writer’s internal hash table.

The change is minimal: a three-line guard in `t/helper/test-bitmap.c` checks for duplicate OIDs before calling `packlist_alloc()`, and the existing `t5308-pack-detect-duplicates.sh` test is extended with a single new case that verifies the fatal diagnostic. The patch preserves the internal uniqueness invariant for other callers while failing gracefully on unsupported input. No new symbols or on-disk format changes are introduced.
