This is a bugfix patch in the GSoC `git cat-file --batch-command` remote-object-info series (v21, 1/13). It plugs a memory leak in `transport-helper.c` that occurs when a transport is disconnected before its helper process is fully started. The leak was introduced in an earlier version of the series and is now fixed by moving `FREE_AND_NULL(data->name)` outside the conditional block so it runs unconditionally. The change is minimal (one line moved), well-motivated, and directly addresses a leak that would otherwise persist in the final series. No controversy or follow-up is expected.
