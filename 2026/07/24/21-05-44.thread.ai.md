Thread identity: patch series titled "harden packfile subsystem against duplicate object entries", v1, authored by Taylor Blau. Thread type: bugfix series.

Core problem or goal: make Git's packfile subsystem consistently handle packs containing duplicate object entries without breaking existing functionality. Currently, components like reverse indexes, delta resolution, MIDX verification, and bitmap generation either mishandle or reject such packs, despite Git's general acceptance of duplicates outside strict indexing. The goal is to ensure these packs are readable, verifiable, and safe for bitmap-assisted operations while preserving `index-pack`'s duplicate policy.
