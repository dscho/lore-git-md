This is the second patch in Ted Nyman’s six-part v6 series fixing race conditions in Git’s HTTP transport during concurrent packfile downloads. It addresses a latent bug in `http.c`: `finish_http_pack_request()` closes the staging-file descriptor twice—once when `start_command()` takes ownership of it (even if the child fails to start), and again explicitly in the cleanup block. The fix removes the redundant `close()` call, leaving descriptor management to `start_command()`.

The change is minimal (one line added, six removed) and confined to `http.c`. It is a pure bugfix with no behavior change, but it eliminates a potential double-close that could cause subtle issues in error paths. The commit message is clear and the diff is straightforward, making this a well-crafted, uncontroversial patch that should proceed smoothly. No test changes are needed because the bug is latent and does not affect normal operation.
