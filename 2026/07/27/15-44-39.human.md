This is a maintainer's commentary on the revived `writev(3p)` wrapper series, responding to Johannes Sixt’s naming and API-design concerns. Junio C Hamano endorses the direction of renaming the wrapper to something like `git_write_gather()` to avoid implying POSIX semantics the emulation cannot guarantee. He frames this as a philosophical improvement that would make the codebase more portable and self-documenting in the long run, explicitly stating that the wrapper should only promise the subset of `writev` behavior the application actually needs (collapsing multiple `write()` calls into one system call for performance) and nothing more.

The email is not a formal decision or a call for a reroll, but it signals clear maintainer support for the naming change. Junio notes that Patrick Steinhardt and the GitLab team are likely at an offsite, so no immediate action is expected. The thread remains in technical review, with the naming dispute now effectively resolved in favor of `git_write_gather()` or similar, but the series still needs a respin to reflect that consensus.
