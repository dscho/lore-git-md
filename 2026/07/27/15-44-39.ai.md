This is a maintainer response in the `writev(3p)` wrapper revival thread, addressing the API naming dispute. Junio C Hamano endorses Johannes Sixt’s suggestion to rename the wrapper from `git_writev()` to `git_write_gather()` (or similar) to avoid implying POSIX semantics the emulation cannot guarantee. He frames this as a long-term architectural improvement: a two-level abstraction where the application uses a Git-specific `write_gather()` API that platforms can implement either via `writev(2)` or as a series of `write(2)` calls, explicitly documenting which guarantees are *not* required. This approach would reduce the risk of applications inadvertently relying on POSIX-specific behavior and make it easier for niche platforms to provide compliant implementations.

The email is brief but decisive on the naming question. Junio does not address the broader maintainer appetite for the series (performance gains, benchmark results, or whether the effort is welcome at all), nor does he comment on the self-documentation issue (the missing range-diff to the reverted version). His focus is narrowly on API design and platform compatibility. He also notes that Patrick Steinhardt and the GitLab team are at an offsite, so no immediate reroll is expected.
