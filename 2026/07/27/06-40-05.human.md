This is a follow-up in the ongoing refactoring thread to change UTF-8 string-width functions (`utf8_strwidth()` and `utf8_strnwidth()`) from returning `int` to `size_t`. Hardik Kumar responds to Pablo Sabater’s substantive review of v2, which identified multiple type-safety regressions across several callers.

Hardik pushes back on some of Pablo’s suggestions. For `builtin/blame.c`, he proposes casting the `utf8_strwidth()` result back to `int` rather than splitting the `num` variable, arguing this avoids unnecessary complexity. In `builtin/repo.c`, he resists promoting the `stats_table` struct fields to `size_t`, citing potential ripple effects and the unlikelihood of needing very large column widths. For `diff.c`, he suggests reverting `len` to `int` to resolve unsigned underflow risks, rather than rewriting the logic as Pablo proposed. He also acknowledges the `gettext.h` conditional compilation issue and agrees to cast the result back to `int` at the call site.

The responses are pragmatic but stop short of fully addressing the regressions. Hardik’s approach leans toward minimal changes (casting) rather than deeper refactoring (type promotion or logic rewrites), which may not satisfy Pablo’s concerns about type consistency and safety. The thread remains blocked on these unresolved issues, and Junio’s earlier skepticism about the patch’s overall value still looms. A v3 will likely be needed, but the viability of the effort is now in question.
