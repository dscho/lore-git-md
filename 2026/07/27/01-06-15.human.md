This is a substantive review of Hardik Kumar’s v2 refactoring patch that changes the return type of UTF-8 string-width functions (`utf8_strwidth()` and `utf8_strnwidth()`) from `int` to `size_t`. Pablo Sabater identifies multiple new issues introduced by the patch, all related to mixed-sign comparisons and unsigned integer underflow. The review is thorough, pointing out specific lines in `builtin/blame.c`, `builtin/branch.c`, `builtin/repo.c`, `column.c`, `diff.c`, `gettext.h`, and `pretty.c` where the type change breaks existing logic or introduces potential bugs.

The most critical problems are in `diff.c` (where `len -= 3` can underflow if `len` is now `size_t`) and `pretty.c` (where `len > padding` becomes a comparison between `size_t` and `int`). Pablo also flags that the `gettext_width()` signature change inside an `#ifndef` block must be mirrored in the `#else` branch to avoid compilation errors. His feedback is actionable and includes concrete suggestions for fixing each issue, though he cautions Hardik to verify the proposed solutions rather than applying them blindly.

The patch is not ready for `next` and will require a v3. The review’s depth and specificity make it clear that this is a high-weight blocker; the issues are not cosmetic but affect core formatting logic in `git blame`, `git branch`, and `git diff --stat`.
