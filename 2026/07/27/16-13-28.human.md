This is the author conceding to reviewer opposition on a refactoring patch. Hardik Kumar’s v3 attempt to change `utf8_strwidth()` and `utf8_strnwidth()` to return `size_t` instead of `int` has run aground on sustained, substantive pushback from Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood, and Pablo Sabater. The reviewers’ core objection—that the functions’ return values are almost always immediately cast back to `int` for use with `printf()` and other APIs—proved decisive. Hardik now acknowledges that the patch “might just not be worth after all” and will send a follow-up to remove the TODO comment in `utf8.c`, replacing it with documentation explaining why the functions return `int` and why `cast_size_t_to_int()` is necessary to prevent overflows. This outcome leaves the codebase in its original state, but with clearer rationale for the design. The episode underscores the project’s high bar for type-system cleanups when they introduce pervasive explicit casts and fail to deliver tangible benefits.
