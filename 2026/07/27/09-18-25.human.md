This is a brief follow-up in the `git stash rename` feature thread. The author, Emin Özata, acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s acceptance of the feature’s justification—now backed by concrete user testimonials—and confirms plans to send a revised version (`v2`) that addresses Patrick Steinhardt’s technical concerns.

The key updates: the subcommand will be renamed from `rename` to `reword` to avoid conflict with `git refs rename`, and the implementation will switch to a single-transaction reflog rewrite to eliminate the non-atomic, quadratic-performance risks of the original drop-and-re-store approach. No code is included here, but the email signals that the next iteration will tackle the open design questions head-on. The patch remains under review, with the justification hurdle cleared but no merge status yet.
