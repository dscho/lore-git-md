This thread is a focused bugfix for the pack-bitmap subsystem, submitted by David Lin. The patch corrects an off-by-one error in `find_objects()` where objects at bitmap position zero were incorrectly excluded from being recognized as already represented in the base bitmap, leading to redundant work. The fix itself is minimal—changing a single conditional from `pos > 0` to `pos >= 0`—and includes test coverage for pseudo-merge bitmap behavior.

Taylor Blau has reviewed the patch substantively, confirming its correctness and providing a detailed technical walkthrough of the logic. He ruled out broader fallout by verifying no other callers of `bitmap_position()` are affected. While the core fix remains unchanged, Taylor suggested two improvements: wrapping the commit message at 72 characters (a minor nit) and adding a test case for non-pseudo-merge scenarios where a position-zero object appears after stored bitmaps. The latter is the only open action, and his provided example (`git rev-list --objects --use-bitmap-index HEAD HEAD`) makes it straightforward to implement. The patch is otherwise ready for integration, with no controversy or unresolved objections.
