This thread is about a small but important bugfix in Git’s pack-bitmap subsystem. David Lin identified and fixed an off-by-one edge case in `find_objects()` where objects at position zero in the base bitmap were incorrectly excluded, leading to redundant work. The fix is minimal—just changing `pos > 0` to `pos >= 0`—but it eliminates unnecessary bitmap reloads or additions to the not-mapped list.

The patch has gone through two iterations. The first version (`v1`) received a substantive review from Taylor Blau, who confirmed the fix’s correctness and suggested two improvements: a clearer commit message and an expanded test to cover non-pseudo-merge scenarios. Both were addressed in `v2`, which now includes thorough test coverage for both pseudo-merge and non-pseudo-merge cases. Junio C Hamano has now marked the patch as ready for integration with a simple "Will queue," meaning it will soon land in the `seen` branch and, barring any issues, progress to `next` and eventually `master`. No further discussion or changes are expected unless integration testing turns up something unexpected.
