This is a well-crafted bugfix series targeting `git rebase --update-refs` behavior with symbolic references. The v3 submission addresses Phillip Wood's latest review feedback about symref resolution edge cases and checked-out branch detection.

The series fixes a real problem: when local branches are symbolic references (like `main` pointing to `master` during branch rename migrations), `git rebase --update-refs` can fail after successfully rebasing the primary branch, leaving refs partially updated. The solution now properly handles both branch-to-branch symrefs (skipping them to avoid duplicate updates) and non-branch symrefs (checking their targets against checked-out reservations).

Key improvements in v3 include:
- Restored unconditional current-branch skipping
- Proper distinction between symrefs pointing to branches vs non-branch targets
- Deduplication of aliases sharing non-branch targets
- Protection against cross-worktree updates
- Clean separation of the branch-alias fix and non-branch safeguards into distinct patches

The implementation looks solid, with careful memory management (using `refs_resolve_refdup()`) and thorough test coverage in both t3400 and t3404. The tests verify symref chain behavior and checked-out branch handling across both files and reftable backends.
