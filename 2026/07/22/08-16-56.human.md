This is the second patch in Son Luong Ngoc’s two-patch v3 series fixing `git rebase --update-refs` behavior with symbolic references. The patch targets non-branch symrefs (e.g., a symref pointing to a tag or a ref outside `refs/heads/`), ensuring they are handled safely without causing rebase failures or duplicate updates.

The implementation extends the reservation logic introduced in patch 1 to cover both the literal symref name and its resolved target. It tracks resolved symref targets in `todo_add_branch_context` to deduplicate updates when multiple aliases point to the same target, and checks both the symref and its target against checked-out branches to prevent cross-worktree conflicts. The changes are confined to `sequencer.c` and are accompanied by new tests in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh` covering non-branch symrefs, symref chains, and cross-worktree scenarios.

This patch directly addresses Phillip Wood’s review feedback from v2, particularly his concern about symrefs pointing outside `refs/heads/`.
