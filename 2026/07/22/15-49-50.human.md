This is a brief follow-up from Junio in the ongoing discussion about the `git receive-pack` connectivity-check performance regression introduced by the ODB abstraction rework. Junio acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed transaction-aware ODB write redesign but notes that it feels orthogonal to the immediate performance problem. He points out that Peff’s reproduction case (using `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES` to set up a packed repository) demonstrated the regression *without* any dynamic modification of ODB sources, meaning the issue isn’t tied to transaction directories or quarantine behavior. The core problem remains the lookup order change—checking packs and loose objects *per backend* rather than *across backends*—which hurts performance even in static setups. Junio’s comment reinforces that a targeted fix (like his earlier "fast/slow" lookup proposal) may still be needed alongside Patrick’s broader architectural work. The tone is collegial, and Junio wishes Patrick a good vacation, leaving the thread open for further discussion when Patrick returns in two weeks.
