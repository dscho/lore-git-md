This is the ninth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring the loose object backend as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch moves the remaining loose-object writing logic from `object-file.c` into `odb/source-loose.c`, making the loose backend fully self-contained. Toon Claes’s review is substantive but positive, focusing on the mechanical correctness of the move and the elimination of a lingering `the_repository` reference.

The patch exposes two previously static transaction helpers (`odb_transaction_files_prepare` and `odb_transaction_files_fsync`) and relocates the entire loose-object writing machinery—including `start_loose_object_common`, `write_loose_object_common`, `end_loose_object_common`, `create_tmpfile`, and `close_loose_object`—into the loose backend. The only loose-object function left in `object-file.c` is `odb_source_loose_write_stream`, which carries a TODO comment indicating it should eventually move as well. The diff shows a net deletion of ~379 lines from `object-file.c` and a corresponding addition of ~357 lines in `odb/source-loose.c`, with no behavior changes.

Toon’s review confirms the patch aligns with the series’ goals. He notes that exposing the transaction helpers could have been a separate commit but accepts it as part of the larger refactoring. He also highlights the removal of the `the_repository` reference as a welcome improvement, and his comments on the zebra diff confirm the code relocation is mechanically sound. The review does not raise new technical questions or objections, suggesting the patch is ready for further review or integration. This completes the series’ goal of consolidating loose-object logic under the ODB abstraction.
