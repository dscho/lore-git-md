Core problem or goal
The revision walker’s `--exclude-first-parent-only` option was not being honored when the first parent of a merge commit had already been marked `SEEN`. This caused the walker to incorrectly propagate the `UNINTERESTING` flag to subsequent parents, breaking the intended “stop after the first parent” semantics.
