Junio C Hamano weighs in on the usability debate that SZEDER Gábor raised: whether the completion list should show both refs and filesystem paths when they share a prefix, or only refs. He does not propose a resolution, but his comment underscores the tension between consistency with other commands and practical usability for `send-email` specifically.
