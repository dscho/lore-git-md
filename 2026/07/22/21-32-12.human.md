This is a follow-up in the `git mv` bugfix thread. The author, Lucas Zamboni Orioli, acknowledges Ben Knoble’s feedback about the TOCTOU (time-of-check to time-of-use) race and agrees to improve the error message by including both source and destination paths. The revised approach splits the fix into two commits: the first makes the error message more informative (and race-free), while the second adds the early check for the missing destination directory. The new error message will follow Git’s existing pattern (`'%s' to '%s'`) rather than the `cp`-style arrow notation. The author plans to send a v2 series with this structure and leaves localization updates to the l10n team. The patch remains narrowly scoped to `builtin/mv.c` and its tests.
