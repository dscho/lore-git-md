This thread is a small but important bugfix to Git’s revision-walking machinery. Junio C Hamano identified and fixed a logic error in `process_parents()` where the `--exclude-first-parent-only` option was being ignored when the first parent of a merge commit had already been marked `SEEN`. This prevents the `UNINTERESTING` flag from incorrectly propagating to subsequent parents.
