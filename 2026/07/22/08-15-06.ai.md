This is the second patch in Son Luong Ngoc’s v3 two-patch bugfix series for `git rebase --update-refs` handling of symbolic references (symrefs). It specifically guards against duplicate or cross-worktree updates when symrefs point outside the `refs/heads/` namespace—such as to tags or other non-branch refs.

The patch extends the reservation logic introduced in the first patch to cover both the literal symref name and its resolved target. When loading another worktree’s `--update-refs` state, it now records both forms, ensuring that aliases pointing to the same target are treated as a single reservation. During decoration processing, it checks both the symref and its target against the list of checked-out branches, preventing updates if either form is already checked out or reserved. A new `string_list` field in `todo_add_branch_context` tracks resolved symref targets to deduplicate updates.

The implementation touches `branch.c` (to record both symref forms during checked-out branch preparation), `sequencer.c` (to check and deduplicate symref targets during decoration processing), and adds three new tests in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`. The tests verify that non-branch symrefs are correctly guarded when their targets are checked out, that multiple aliases to the same target are deduplicated, and that reservations from other worktrees are honored. The changes are minimal and focused, avoiding unnecessary restructuring of unrelated code.

This patch directly addresses the edge cases Phillip Wood identified in his review of v2, completing the series’ goal of making `--update-refs` robust against symref-related failures. The series is now technically complete, with clear commit messages and comprehensive test coverage demonstrating the fixes for all identified scenarios. It appears ready for maintainer consideration.
