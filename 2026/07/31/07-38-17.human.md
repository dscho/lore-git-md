This is Junio C Hamano’s review of the third patch in brian m. carlson’s six-patch RFC series that prepares Git 3.0 to enforce lowercase-only hex object IDs. The patch extends the `hex_to_bytes()` helper to accept an `enum hexkind` parameter, updating all callers to pass `HEX_KIND_MIXED` without changing runtime behavior.

Junio’s feedback is a **surface-level performance suggestion**: he notes that if `len` is large, repeatedly passing the same `kind` parameter inside the loop could be less efficient than selecting the appropriate hex-value table once outside the loop and reusing it. He sketches a refactored API where `hexval()` would take a precomputed `void *hextable` instead of an `enum hexkind`, avoiding repeated table lookups. The suggestion is purely mechanical and does not engage with the patch’s substance or the series’ overall goal; it is a micro-optimization observation rather than a critique of the design or motivation. The tone is constructive and matter-of-fact, leaving the decision to the author.
