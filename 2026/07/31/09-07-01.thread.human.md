This thread is about a small but useful documentation improvement to the `git-refs(1)` man page. Kristoffer Haugsbakk sent a two-patch series (now at v2) to reorganize an important warning about ref migration limitations into a more visible `WARNING` admonition block and add a proper cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)`. The series has been fully approved by Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, with no outstanding concerns.

The first patch moves the warning from a buried section to a prominent spot under the `migrate` subcommand, changing its admonition type from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to better match AsciiDoc’s documented semantics. The second patch, which this email covers, simply replaces a plain-text reference to `git-maintenance(1)` with the `linkgit` macro, making the link clickable in HTML output and properly cross-referenced in the man page. Both patches are uncontroversial and follow established patterns in Git’s documentation.

The series is now queued in Junio’s `seen` branch and expected to move to `next` in the next integration cycle. No further changes are needed, and the patches are ready for graduation to `master`. This is part of Kristoffer’s ongoing work to improve man page clarity and navigation across Git’s documentation.
