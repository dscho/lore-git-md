This thread is about a small but important documentation improvement to the `git-refs(1)` man page. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed a two-patch series to reorganize a warning about ref migration limitations—currently buried in the prose—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand synopsis. The second patch adds a cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)` for better navigation.

The series was approved by Junio C Hamano in its first version and is now queued in `seen`, awaiting graduation to `next`. The only change in v2 was a stylistic update: switching the admonition block from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to align with AsciiDoc’s documented semantics, a suggestion from Patrick Steinhardt. This change doesn’t affect the substance of the series, and Patrick’s `Acked-by` has been added to the second patch. The series is uncontroversial, touches only the documentation, and is ready for integration.

Kristoffer’s ongoing documentation cleanup efforts often focus on small, targeted improvements like this one, and this series is a good example of how even minor changes can make Git’s documentation more user-friendly. The patches are self-contained and follow existing patterns in other man pages. No further action is needed beyond the maintainer’s usual integration process.
