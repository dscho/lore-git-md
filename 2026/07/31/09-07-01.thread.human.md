This thread is about a small but useful documentation improvement to the `git-refs(1)` man page. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed reorganizing an important warning about ref migration limitations—previously buried deep in the page—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand, and adding a cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)`. The series went through three versions, with the only feedback being a suggestion to use `WARNING` instead of `CAUTION` for the admonition block, which the author addressed in v2.

The series is now fully approved and queued for integration. Junio C Hamano explicitly approved both patches, and Patrick Steinhardt added his `Acked-by` after the v2 update. Karthik Nayak’s recent reply simply confirms the patches "look good to me," but adds no new discussion or changes. The patches touch only the `Documentation/git-refs.txt` file and are uncontroversial. No further revisions are expected, and the series should graduate to `next` in the next "What's cooking" cycle.
