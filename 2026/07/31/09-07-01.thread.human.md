This thread is about a small but useful documentation improvement to the `git-refs(1)` man page. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed a two-patch series to reorganize an important warning about ref migration limitations—previously buried deep in the prose—into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand, and to add a cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)` for better navigation. The series went through three versions, with the only feedback in v1 being a suggestion to switch the admonition from `CAUTION` to `WARNING` to match AsciiDoc’s documented semantics, which the author addressed in v2.

The series has now been fully approved and is on track for integration. Junio C Hamano reviewed and explicitly approved both patches, and the topic has been marked for promotion to the `next` branch, meaning it will likely graduate to `master` in the next cycle. Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak also provided their `Acked-by` in v3, confirming their satisfaction with the changes. No further discussion or revision is expected, and the series is effectively complete. The changes are purely documentation-related, touching only `Documentation/git-refs.txt`, and carry no risk of regression or on-disk format changes.
