This is a documentation patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, consisting of two small changes to the `git-refs(1)` man page.

The first patch moves a warning about ref migration limitations out of a footnote and into an admonition block directly under the `git refs migrate` command synopsis. The goal is to make the warning more visible to users who might otherwise overlook it in the footnotes. The second patch adds a missing `linkgit` macro to the same warning text, linking to the `git-maintenance(1)` man page for further context.

The changes are purely presentational—no behavior is altered, and the content of the warning remains the same. The commit messages are clear and the rationale is straightforward: improving the discoverability of important usage caveats. The patches touch only the AsciiDoc source for the man page, so there’s no risk of introducing regressions or behavioral changes.

This series is part of Kristoffer’s ongoing documentation cleanup work, though the two follow-up patches mentioned (placeholder formatting and verbatim markup) are not included here. The changes here are uncontroversial and align with the project’s emphasis on clear, user-facing documentation. If you care about man page readability or work with the refs subsystem, this is worth a quick look.
