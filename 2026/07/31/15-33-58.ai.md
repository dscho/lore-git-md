This is a maintainer feedback email on the RFC v2 series introducing `git repack --drop-filtered` for partial clones. Junio C Hamano raises three substantive technical questions that could shape the design or reveal hidden assumptions.

First, he probes the reliability of `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_PROMISOR_ONLY`: the phrase "by construction" in the commit message implies the traversal is infallible, but Junio wants confirmation that the promisor-object enumeration is robust in practice. He explicitly asks for input from other partial-clone users to validate this mechanism.

Second, he confirms the repack machinery’s behavior: the rebuilt promisor pack must be constructed from locally available data only, without triggering lazy fetches, and should reuse the pre-enumerated list of promisor objects (minus the dropped blobs) rather than performing a fresh traversal.

Third, he questions the necessity of the safety guards (merge/rebase/cherry-pick checks and index validation). He describes two scenarios—an in-progress operation that pauses, and a subsequent command that would re-fetch dropped blobs—and asks why the safeguards are needed if lazy-fetching will transparently restore the objects. His tone suggests skepticism that the guards add value beyond what the promisor infrastructure already provides.

The email does not address the series’ other aspects (CLI design, documentation, or deferred work like the drop log) and does not indicate whether the topic is queued for any integration branch. The questions are narrowly focused on correctness and design rationale, not on code style or implementation details.
