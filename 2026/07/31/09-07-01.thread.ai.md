Thread identity: Patch series improving the `git-refs(1)` man page by reorganizing a ref migration warning into a `WARNING` admonition block and adding a cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)`. Series version: v3. Originating author: Kristoffer Haugsbakk. Thread type: documentation patch series.

Core problem or goal: The `git-refs(1)` man page previously buried an important warning about ref migration limitations (concurrent writes can lead to inconsistent state) deep in the prose, making it easy for users to overlook. The goal is to elevate this warning into a prominent `WARNING` admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand synopsis and add a missing cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)` for better navigation.
