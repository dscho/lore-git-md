This is a documentation patch series that has just received Junio’s explicit approval. The series consists of two small patches to the `git-refs(1)` man page, both authored by Kristoffer Haugsbakk. The goal is to make a critical warning about ref migration limitations more visible by moving it from a buried section into a prominent admonition block directly under the `migrate` subcommand synopsis, and to add a proper cross-reference to `git-maintenance(1)`.

The changes are purely presentational—no code or behavior is altered—and touch only the AsciiDoc source for `git-refs(1)`. Junio’s reply is brief but clear: he endorses the reorganization as a logical improvement. There are no open questions, no controversy, and no technical risk. This is a straightforward usability fix that should land in the next integration cycle.
