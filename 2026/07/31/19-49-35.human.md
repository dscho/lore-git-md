This is a small test portability patch in Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder. It replaces a shell pipeline (`wc -c <two.t | xargs`) with Git’s `test_file_size` helper in `t5701-git-serve.sh`, addressing a review comment from Junio that the original was unnecessarily platform-dependent.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial: `test_file_size` is the project’s standard way to measure file sizes in tests, and the patch simply updates two lines in the expected output. This patch is part of the v2 series that has already incorporated all review feedback; the series as a whole is now complete and ready for maintainer consideration.
