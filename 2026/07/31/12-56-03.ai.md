This is the second patch in Junio C Hamano’s `git add --resolved` v3 series, a refactoring that consolidates duplicate conflict-marker scanning logic into a single helper. The patch replaces two near-identical functions—`diff.c:is_conflict_marker()` and `rerere.c:is_cmarker()`—with a new `merge-ll.c:is_conflict_marker_line()` that enforces a stricter rule: conflict markers (`<<<<<<<`, `>>>>>>>`) must be followed by a space, not just any whitespace. The helper also returns the marker character (`<`, `=`, `>`, or `|`) to distinguish marker types, which `rerere.c` now uses to simplify its conflict-handling logic.

The change touches four files: `diff.c` (removes its local implementation), `merge-ll.c` (adds the new helper), `merge-ll.h` (exports the function), and `rerere.c` (replaces its `is_cmarker()` calls with the new helper). The stricter rule aligns with Git’s actual conflict output, which always includes a space after the marker labels (e.g., `<<<<<<< ours`). The patch is purely internal and introduces no user-visible behavior changes, but it lays the groundwork for the `--resolved` option in patch 3/4 by providing a shared, consistent way to detect conflict markers.
