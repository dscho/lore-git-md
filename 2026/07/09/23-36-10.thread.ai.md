Core problem or goal
Add a `post-worktree-remove` hook that runs after `git worktree remove` deletes a worktree, giving external tools a reliable signal that a worktree has been removed. The hook receives the absolute path of the removed worktree and its administrative identifier, runs even if the worktree directory is already gone, and cannot affect the command’s outcome.
