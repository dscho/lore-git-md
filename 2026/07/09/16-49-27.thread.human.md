This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` or `int` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is purely mechanical—no behavior or on-disk format changes—and is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`.

The series is under review in its first version, with no prior iterations. It touches 20 files across the delta, pack-objects, zlib, packfile, and diff subsystems, as well as test helpers and a few other areas. The changes are confined to type widening, with temporary shims introduced where downstream callers still expect `unsigned long`; these shims are noted as temporary and will be removed in later work.

So far, the series has been uncontroversial. Johannes posted v1 to address real-world truncation issues on 64-bit Windows, and the patches are preparatory for the larger `unsigned long` to `size_t` conversion effort. No open questions or objections have been raised yet, and the latest email (the eleventh patch) continues the mechanical widening with a one-line change in a test helper. The series remains focused on correctness and platform compatibility.
