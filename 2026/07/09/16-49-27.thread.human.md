This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is now fully ready for integration after two rounds of review.

Johannes posted v2 addressing all feedback from Patrick Steinhardt, who reviewed both versions. Patrick’s v1 review identified technical issues and requested clarifications, all of which were resolved in v2—including widening the `sizeof_delta_index()` return type, ensuring the new `git_parse_size_t()` and `git_config_size_t()` helpers handle the wider `max_delta_cache_size`, and documenting the fallback formula for `git_deflate_bound()` when inputs exceed zlib’s `uLong` range. Junio C Hamano has now confirmed the series is ready to be queued in the `next` branch, marking the end of the review process.

The patches touch 24 files across subsystems like `diff-delta.c`, `pack-objects.c`, and `git-zlib.c`, but introduce no behavior or on-disk format changes. The work is part of the broader effort to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for correctness on platforms where pointer and integer widths differ. No further revisions are expected, and the series should appear in the next "What's cooking" report.
