This thread is about a twelve-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is part of a broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`.

The patches are under review, with no prior versions, and remain mechanical—confined to type widening without behavior or on-disk format changes. Patrick Steinhardt identified three technical issues in the initial submission: a type mismatch in `sizeof_delta_index()`, a latent configuration-parsing bug in `max_delta_cache_size`, and a truncation risk in `free_unpacked()`. Johannes has acknowledged all three and confirmed they will be fixed in the next iteration, including a new `git_config_size_t()` helper to address the configuration bug. The series also sparked a minor discussion about patch granularity, with Patrick suggesting the changes could be squashed into a single patch, while Johannes prefers smaller, trivial patches for reviewability.

The final exchange clarified the fallback formula in `git_deflate_bound()` for inputs exceeding zlib’s `uLong` range (>4 GiB on 64-bit Windows). Johannes confirmed the formula is an upper-bound estimate, mirroring zlib’s stored-block logic, though a newer `deflateBound_z()` function in zlib 1.3.2 could eventually replace it. All review feedback has been addressed, and the next iteration is expected to resolve the remaining issues. The series is uncontroversial and preparatory for the broader `unsigned long` to `size_t` conversion effort.
