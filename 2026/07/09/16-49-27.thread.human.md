This thread is about a twelve-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is uncontroversial, mechanically correct, and currently queued in Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch for integration testing. All prior review feedback has been addressed, and the patches remain split into twelve parts as originally posted.

The key technical work is complete: the series touches 24 files, widens critical fields and parameters (including `git_deflate_bound()` in the final patch), and ensures type consistency across the codebase. There are no behavior or on-disk format changes—this is purely a correctness fix. The only remaining open questions come from Junio’s latest review, which flags two potential truncation risks in patch 07/12 where widened `size_t` values interact with existing `unsigned long` interfaces. These are narrow, type-consistency concerns rather than immediate bugs, but they may require follow-up patches to fully align the type system before the series graduates to `master`.

Johannes and Patrick Steinhardt have both reviewed and approved the series, with Patrick’s feedback on earlier versions fully addressed. Junio previously confirmed the series was ready for merging, and his latest questions are the only technical loose ends. The series is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`, and it remains on track for inclusion in the next release.
