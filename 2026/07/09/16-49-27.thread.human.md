This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t` for size-related values.

The patches have been queued in Junio’s `next` branch for integration testing and are uncontroversial, with all prior review feedback addressed in the v2 iteration. The series touches 24 files, including `diff-delta.c`, `pack-objects.c`, and `git-zlib.c`, and introduces no behavior or on-disk format changes—it’s purely a type-widening correctness fix. The final patch completes the type chain by widening `git_deflate_bound()` and updating all callers, including a fallback formula for inputs exceeding zlib’s `uLong` range.

The only remaining technical loose end comes from Junio’s latest review, which flags two narrowly scoped type-consistency concerns in patch 07/12. These involve potential truncation risks where widened `size_t` values interact with existing `unsigned long` interfaces (`zlib_buf_cap()` and `unpack_object_header_buffer()`). While these are unlikely to cause immediate bugs, Junio’s phrasing suggests they’ll likely be addressed with follow-up patches before the series graduates to `master`. The series itself is otherwise ready for integration, with no further revisions expected to the existing patches.
