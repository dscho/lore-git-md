This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is purely mechanical, focusing on type widening without changing behavior or on-disk formats, and is part of a broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`.

The series is currently under review, with v1 posted and no prior versions. The patches touch 14 files, primarily in the delta (`diff-delta.c`, `delta.h`), pack-objects (`pack-objects.c`, `pack-objects.h`), and zlib (`git-zlib.c`) subsystems, as well as `http-push.c`, `archive-zip.c`, and test helpers. Key changes include widening fields in `struct delta_index`, parameters in `create_delta_index()` and `create_delta()`, and counters like `delta_cache_size` and `mem_usage`. The latest email covers the sixth patch, which updates `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` to use `size_t` for size parameters, introducing temporary shims for downstream compatibility where needed.

Johannes is the sole participant so far, and the series is uncontroversial, motivated by real-world truncation issues on 64-bit Windows. No open questions or objections have been raised yet, and the changes are straightforward, making this a low-risk, platform-specific fix. The series is expected to proceed smoothly through review.
