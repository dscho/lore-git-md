This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows (where `unsigned long` is 32-bit). The series is now complete with the final patch widening `git_deflate_bound()`, the last remaining `unsigned long` holdout in the zlib wrapper.

The patches are mechanical and confined to type widening, with no behavior or on-disk format changes. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the series and identified three issues in earlier patches—type mismatches in `sizeof_delta_index()` and `free_unpacked()`, and a latent configuration-parsing bug in `max_delta_cache_size`—all of which are expected to be fixed in the next iteration. His review of the final patch was brief but substantive, questioning whether the 12-patch split was necessary and asking for clarification on the fallback formula for inputs above zlib’s `uLong` range. Johannes’s response about the temporary `deltalen` initialization in Patch 6 was acknowledged by Patrick as reasonable.

The series remains under review, with no objections to the core goal. The changes are uncontroversial and part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`. The next steps will likely involve addressing Patrick’s feedback and posting a v2.
