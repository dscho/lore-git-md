This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta-compression and pack-objects code from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit. The series is now in its second version (v2), addressing all feedback from Patrick Steinhardt’s review of v1, and is ready for integration.

The final patch in the series, just posted, widens `git_deflate_bound()`—both its parameter and return type—to `size_t`, completing the type-consistency chain across all callers. This patch handles edge cases where inputs exceed zlib’s `uLong` range (>4 GiB on 64-bit Windows) by falling back to zlib’s stored-block formula, ensuring correctness without changing behavior for smaller inputs. The series remains mechanical, with no behavior or on-disk format changes, and is uncontroversial. All prior patches have already widened the relevant callers, so this last patch is a straightforward and low-risk change.

The series is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t` and is motivated by real-world platform-compatibility issues. With all review feedback addressed and no open questions, the series is now ready for Junio to pick up. Key participants include Johannes Schindelin (author) and Patrick Steinhardt (reviewer), whose feedback was fully incorporated in v2.
