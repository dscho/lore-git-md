This thread is about a 12-patch series from Johannes Schindelin that widens size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent integer truncation on 64-bit Windows and other platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than a pointer. The series is part of a broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t` and is now in Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch for integration testing after addressing all prior review feedback.

The series itself is uncontroversial and mechanically sound, with all review feedback from earlier versions incorporated. However, Junio has just raised two technical questions about patch 7/12, which widens `avail_in` and `avail_out` in `struct git_zstream` to `size_t`. He points out that `zlib_buf_cap()` remains `unsigned long`, and `zlib_pre_call()` passes the now-wider fields to it, potentially risking truncation before comparison with `ZLIB_BUF_MAX`. He also notes that `unpack_object_header_buffer()` still takes an `unsigned long` length parameter, and `oe_get_size_slow()` passes a `size_t` value to it, which could trigger truncation warnings from static checkers. These questions are narrowly scoped to type consistency and do not challenge the series’ overall goal, but the answers may determine whether follow-up patches are needed to fully align the type system.

Johannes and Patrick Steinhardt have been the key participants, with Patrick providing substantive review on both v1 and v2, and Junio confirming the series’ readiness for `next` after all feedback was addressed. The series touches 24 files and completes the type chain by widening `git_deflate_bound()` in the final patch, ensuring no behavioral changes or on-disk format modifications. No further revisions are expected unless Junio’s latest questions uncover a need for adjustments.
