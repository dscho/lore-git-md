The series is now complete, fully type-consistent, and addresses all prior review feedback, including the final truncation risks in the delta-window memory accounting chain, the delta-encoding API, and zlib stream interfaces.

The patches touch 27 files, introducing no behavior or on-disk format changes—this is purely a correctness fix. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, has confirmed the series is ready for integration, explicitly approving the final adjustments in v3 and queuing it in `next`. No further objections are expected, and the series is uncontroversial, aligning with the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in Git. Some in-flight topics may need minor adjustments to remove workarounds once this lands, but these will be straightforward.

The work is particularly relevant for Windows users and large-object scenarios, completing the type-widening effort for the delta/pack subsystems. With all review feedback addressed and Junio’s approval secured, the series is effectively final and awaiting graduation to `master`.
