This is the sixth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The patch targets the core delta-encoding API: it widens the `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()` functions (and their callers) from `unsigned long` to `size_t` so that blobs larger than 4 GiB can be handled correctly on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32-bit.

The change is mechanical and confined to type widening. The patch updates both declarations (in `delta.h`) and definitions (in `diff-delta.c`), then adjusts every caller in the tree: `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `builtin/fast-import.c`, `diff.c`, and the two test helpers `test-delta.c` and `test-pack-deltas.c`. Callers that still feed downstream `unsigned long` consumers (zlib’s `avail_in`, `deflate_it()`, or the test helper’s own `do_compress()`) bridge the gap with a temporary `size_t` variable and a `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shim; these shims are explicitly marked as temporary and will be removed in later topics that widen the downstream consumers.

The commit message is clear and detailed, explaining the motivation, the scope, and the minimal nature of the caller updates. The patch itself is small (22 lines added, 15 removed) and does not change behavior or on-disk formats. It is a straightforward, low-risk correctness fix that is part of the broader `unsigned long` → `size_t` conversion effort.
