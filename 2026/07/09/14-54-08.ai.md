This is a brief follow-up in the reftable backend performance regression fix thread. Patrick Steinhardt and Kristofer Karlsson agree on the final API shape: the `suppress_deletions` flag will move from the merged iterator to `struct reftable_stack_options`, allowing libgit2 (and other downstream users) to control tombstone suppression at stack creation time. Kristofer acknowledges that libgit2 will need updating regardless due to its use of pre-options-stack code, so the compatibility break is already inevitable. The change is mechanical and preserves libgit2’s current behavior while future-proofing the API. No further discussion or open questions remain on this point.
