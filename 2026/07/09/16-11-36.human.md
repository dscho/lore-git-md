This is the v9 iteration of Tian Yuchen’s nine-patch series that migrates more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. It is a direct continuation of the recently merged `excludes_file` work and is part of the ongoing libification effort.

The series is split into two logical halves: patches 1–6 handle infrastructure and heap-allocated string variables (editor, pager, askpass, excludes_file, apply whitespace settings), while patches 7–9 migrate three enums (push_default, autorebase, object_creation_mode). The only change since v8 is the fix for the memory leak in `pager.c` that Junio identified; the rest of the series is unchanged. All patches are CI-clean and the author considers the series technically complete.

Two RFC questions remain open: whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and how this work relates to prior discussions about `git_branch_track`. Neither is a blocker, but reviewers may still weigh in on the design. The author also promises follow-up cleanup for comments and variable classification in `repo_config_values`, as well as resolution of the `NEEDSWORK` items (submodule support, enum placements).

This looks ready for `next`; the memory leak is fixed and the series is well-motivated as a step toward eliminating globals.
