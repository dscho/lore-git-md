This is a substantive review comment on the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch ODB abstraction series. Justin Tobler questions the design choice to allow backends to ignore the new `filter` field in `odb_for_each_object_options`, forcing callers to re-apply the filter for correctness. He finds this "awkward" and suggests defining a subset of object filters that all backends must support, with unsupported filters delegated to the caller. The comment is narrowly focused on the interface contract and does not challenge the patch’s technical implementation or its role in the series. It raises a valid design question about API ergonomics and backend expectations, but does not identify a bug or blocker. Patrick’s response will likely clarify whether the current design is intentional (e.g., to avoid over-constraining backends) or if the interface will be refined.
