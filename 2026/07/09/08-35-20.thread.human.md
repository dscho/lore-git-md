This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in Git’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to let `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` work with pluggable object databases by removing its dependency on internal details of the packed backend, while also fixing inefficiencies in object lookups during enumeration.

The series is under active review, with v1 posted and no prior versions. Junio C Hamano praised the final patch (7/7) for its clean diffstat and requested only a minor commit message tweak to better motivate the change. Justin Tobler has reviewed patches 1/7, 3/7, and now 4/7, raising technical questions about edge cases and style but no objections to the core design. His latest review of patch 4/7—refactoring how bitmaps are opened—confirmed the new loop order is clearer and the altered bitmap precedence is acceptable, though he suggested minor style improvements for readability. The series is likely to be accepted once Patrick addresses the remaining questions and tweaks, with no major technical hurdles remaining. The only open questions are Justin’s style suggestions for patch 4/7 and his earlier queries about patch 1/7’s condition changes and object lookup behavior.
