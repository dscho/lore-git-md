This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series (v3) to introduce object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in Git’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to enable `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` to work with pluggable object databases while improving performance by eliminating redundant index traversals and removing backend-specific logic. The series has evolved through three versions, incorporating feedback from Junio C Hamano, Justin Tobler, Taylor Blau, and Jeff King, including a cleanup patch from Peff to remove dead code.

All review feedback has been addressed, and the series is now ready for integration. Taylor Blau’s latest reply—“Thanks, this version looks good to me”—signals no remaining technical objections, though two follow-up items remain: a minor stylistic nit about patch organization and a planned two-patch series from Taylor to resolve a callback-type inconsistency between `traverse_bitmap_commit_list()` and `traverse_commit_list()`. The series touches the ODB, packed backend, pack-bitmap, and several built-in commands (`cat-file`, `pack-objects`, `rev-list`), introducing new functions like `prepare_bitmap_git_for_source()` and renaming others to improve clarity. No on-disk format changes are involved.

The next step is Junio’s final review, after which the series is likely to be queued in `next`. The only loose end is the deferred follow-up work on callback types, which will not block integration.
