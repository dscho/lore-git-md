This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series (v2) introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in Git’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to enable `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` to work with pluggable object databases by removing its tight coupling to the "packed" backend’s internals, while also fixing inefficiencies in object enumeration.

The series is under active review, with v2 incorporating all prior feedback. Junio C Hamano praised the final patch (8/8) for its clean diffstat and design, requesting only a minor commit message improvement that Patrick has since addressed. Justin Tobler and Taylor Blau provided substantive reviews on earlier versions, raising questions about edge cases, performance, and design consistency—all of which have been resolved. Taylor’s latest review of patch 7/8 is surface-level, merely noting the patch "looks plausibly correct" without deeper engagement, reflecting limited familiarity with the pluggable ODB subsystem rather than opposition.

The only remaining open items are Taylor’s suggestions for performance optimizations in patch 1/8 (narrowing MIDX iteration ranges) and a minor refactoring in patch 3/8 (extending error propagation to `traverse_bitmap_commit_list`). Neither is a blocker, and the series is otherwise ready for integration. The changes touch the ODB, packed backend, and `git cat-file`, introducing new filtering infrastructure while removing backend-specific logic. No on-disk format changes or end-user behavioral changes are expected. The series builds on Patrick’s earlier `ps/odb-drop-whence` topic and remains part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.
