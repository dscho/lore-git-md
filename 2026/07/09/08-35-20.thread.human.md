The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort to support alternative storage backends and addresses inefficiencies in object enumeration.

This removes the performance concern, leaving no unresolved objections. The series has incorporated all prior feedback, including Jeff King’s cleanup patch (removing an unused return value), Patrick’s `const` filter parameter patch, and a mechanical rename of bitmap-opening functions.
