This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series (v2) introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in Git’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to enable `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` to work with pluggable object databases by removing its dependency on internal details of the packed backend, while also fixing inefficiencies in object enumeration.

The series is under active review, with v2 incorporating all feedback from v1. Junio C Hamano reviewed the final patch (now 8/8) and praised its implementation, requesting only a minor commit message improvement, which Patrick has addressed. Justin Tobler reviewed several patches in v1, raising technical questions that have since been resolved. The new patch in v2 (2/8) marks object filter parameters as `const`, and the series now includes a cleanup patch (5/8) to drop unnecessary suffixes from bitmap-opening functions. The core technical changes involve refactoring `pack-bitmap.c` to support early termination of bitmapped object iteration (patch 3/8, the focus of the latest email) and introducing ODB-mediated filtering to replace backend-specific logic in `cat-file`.

No open questions remain, and the series is likely to be accepted once any final minor adjustments are made. The work is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort and depends on his `ps/odb-drop-whence` topic. Key participants—Junio and Justin—have been supportive, with all review feedback addressed. The changes are internal and should not affect end-user behavior.
