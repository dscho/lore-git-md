This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in the ODB abstraction effort to make Git’s object storage layer pluggable. The goal is to let `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` work with alternative backends by removing its dependency on packed-backend internals, while also fixing an inefficiency in object lookups during enumeration.

The series is at v1, posted for review, and builds on Patrick’s earlier `ps/odb-drop-whence` topic. The fourth patch, just summarized, refactors bitmap opening to iterate over object sources first, then attempt to load a bitmap for each. This is a preparatory change for later patches, with no user-visible impact but a subtle shift in bitmap precedence: local bitmaps now take priority over multi-pack index bitmaps in alternates, which Patrick argues is an improvement. The patch is clean and well-justified, though reviewers focused on ODB backends or bitmap performance may want to verify the new logic.

The series touches the ODB, packed backend, and pack-bitmap subsystems, with changes spanning `builtin/cat-file.c`, `pack-bitmap.c`, and related files.
