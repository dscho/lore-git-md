This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series (v2) introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()` as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to let `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` work with pluggable object databases by removing its tight coupling to the "packed" backend’s internals, while also fixing an inefficiency in object lookups during enumeration. The series has been well received, with Junio C Hamano calling the final patch "very nice" and praising its clean diffstat. All review feedback from v1 has been addressed in v2, which also adds one new patch to drop unnecessary suffixes and improve commit messages.

The series is now in its second revision, with only minor loose ends remaining. Taylor Blau recently reviewed patch 3/8 and pointed out that while it correctly propagates non-zero return values from `show_objects_for_type()` in one caller, it misses another—`traverse_bitmap_commit_list()`—leaving a potential inconsistency in the bitmap iteration logic. This is a targeted improvement rather than a blocker, and Patrick has not yet responded. Earlier, Taylor also identified a performance inefficiency in patch 1/8’s object lookup logic, suggesting an optimization to narrow the iteration range in the multi-pack index, though this too is not a correctness issue. Junio and Justin Tobler have reviewed other patches in the series, with all substantive feedback resolved. The series is under active review and appears headed for acceptance once these final details are addressed.
