This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The patch changes the `create_delta_index()` function’s `bufsize` parameter from `unsigned long` to `size_t` in both its declaration (in `delta.h`) and definition (in `diff-delta.c`). The commit message notes that the sole caller (`try_delta()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`) already passes an `unsigned long`, which safely promotes to `size_t`, so no caller adjustments are required here. This change is purely mechanical and preparatory, isolating the `create_delta_index()` widening from the more far-reaching `diff_delta()`/`create_delta()` changes that follow in later patches. The patch touches two files and makes no functional or behavioral changes.
