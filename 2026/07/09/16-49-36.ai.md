This is the ninth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows correctness. It fixes a silent integer narrowing in `diff.c`: the `bound` local in `deflate_it()` was declared `int`, but `git_deflate_bound()` returns `unsigned long`, so any value above 2 GiB would wrap negative and then be sign-extended back to `size_t` in the subsequent `xmalloc()`. The patch widens `bound` to `size_t`, eliminating the truncation and preparing for the upcoming `git_deflate_bound()` return-type change to `size_t`. The change is confined to a single line in `diff.c` and introduces no new behavior or on-disk format changes.
