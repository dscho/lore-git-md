Patrick Steinhardt's review focuses on the new `repo_get_work_tree_original()` accessor and the complex `logical_path_from_cwd()` helper, which reconstructs the symlink-preserving worktree path after Git's discovery process changes the working directory.

The review does not object to the feature's core goal but questions whether the implementation complexity is justified.
