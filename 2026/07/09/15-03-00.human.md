This is the first patch in a two-patch bugfix series that restores correct topological-level propagation in split commit-graph chains. The series targets a performance regression introduced in September 2025 by commit `199d452758`, which caused incremental commit-graph writes to re-walk the entire commit ancestry instead of reading existing topological levels from lower graph layers.

This patch adds trace2 instrumentation to `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to count and log the number of DFS steps taken during generation-number computation. The instrumentation exposes the regression's impact—expected to show a single step when topological levels are available, but currently showing thousands when the bug forces a full ancestry walk. The test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` is updated to use `test_expect_failure` to demonstrate the bug, verifying that the trace2 output reports more than one DFS step. The test will flip to `test_expect_success` in the second patch once the fix is applied.

The change is narrowly scoped to diagnostic instrumentation and test scaffolding, with no behavior changes. The instrumentation is lightweight (a single counter and trace2 call) and will remain useful for future performance monitoring. The test setup creates a four-layer split commit-graph chain to replicate the real-world scenario where the regression manifests. This patch is uncontested and part of a series already marked for merge by Junio C Hamano after the v2 reroll addressed a minor commit-message correction.
