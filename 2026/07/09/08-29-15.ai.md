This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem and worktree code. It is a pure refactoring: every call to `the_repository` in `worktree.c` is replaced with the repository pointer (`wt->repo`) that is already available in the same function. The diff touches seven call sites in five functions (`validate_worktree`, `update_worktree_location`, `repair_gitfile`, `repair_worktree_after_gitdir_move`, and `init_worktree_config`). No new symbols are introduced; the change is strictly mechanical and does not alter behavior. The patch is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and depends on earlier patches in the same series that have already converted the refs backends.
