This is the v2 reroll of Kristofer Karlsson’s two-patch bugfix series that restores correct `topo_levels` slab propagation in split commit-graph chains. The regression was introduced by commit `199d452758` (September 2025) in the `ps/commit-graph-via-source` series and caused incremental commit-graph writes to re-walk the entire commit ancestry instead of reading existing topological levels from lower graph layers, degrading performance from ~233 ms to ~4133 ms on large repositories.

Patch 1/2 adds trace2 instrumentation to `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` so the cost of the DFS can be observed; the test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` is now marked `test_expect_failure` to demonstrate the bug. Patch 2/2 fixes the loop variable in the propagation logic (`chain->topo_levels` instead of `g->topo_levels`), ensuring the slab is visible to every layer in the chain, and flips the test to `test_expect_success`. The only changes since v1 are corrected commit-message metadata (date and title of the regressing commit) and a cleaner test assertion. Junio C Hamano has already marked the topic as “Expecting a reroll” and is ready to merge once this v2 lands. Reviews from Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt remain positive; no new objections or concerns have surfaced. The series is uncontested and the fix is narrowly scoped to the identified issue.
