This thread is about a patch series introducing `includeIf worktree:<pattern>` and `worktree/i:<pattern>` to Git’s configuration system, allowing conditional config inclusion based on the working tree’s path. The feature, now in its eighth version, addresses a gap in multi-worktree setups where the `.git` directory’s location isn’t intuitive. Authored by Chen Linxuan, the series has evolved through feedback, most recently dropping symlink-preserving logic to resolve architectural concerns and integration conflicts with Patrick Steinhardt’s in-flight `ps/setup-*` series.

The current version (v8) consists of two patches: a preparatory refactoring of `include_by_gitdir()` into `include_by_path()`, and the core implementation, which stores the realpath-resolved worktree path. The series is now unblocked, passing all tests standalone and in `seen`, and is eligible for `next` pending final review. Key technical details include new config keywords (`worktree:` and `worktree/i:`), glob pattern support, and thorough test coverage for edge cases like bare repositories and `~` expansion. The symlink limitation—where symlink spellings may not match due to realpath resolution—is documented as a pragmatic compromise.

Patrick Steinhardt’s latest review signals approval for the series, calling it "good enough" for now and removing the last technical blocker. He suggests minor documentation and test improvements to clarify the symlink limitation’s future-proofing intent but does not require them for merging. With no further objections and the author’s responsiveness to feedback, the series appears ready for integration into `next`. The only remaining loose end is Junio’s confirmation of its graduation status, which hasn’t yet appeared in a "What’s cooking" report.
