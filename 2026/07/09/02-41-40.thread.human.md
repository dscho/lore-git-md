This thread is about a patch series (v7) by Chen Linxuan that adds `includeIf worktree:<pattern>` support to Git’s config system, allowing conditional inclusion of config files based on the working tree’s path. The feature is designed for multi-worktree setups and reuses existing path-matching logic, but it’s now facing two hurdles: design concerns from Patrick Steinhardt and a test failure in Junio’s integration branch.

Patrick’s review of v7 raised architectural questions about the series’ complexity, particularly around the `repo_get_worktree_original()` accessor function and the symlink-preserving path logic. He also noted a conflict with his in-flight `ps/setup-split-discovery-and-setup` series and asked the author to either refactor the logic, document its limitations, or coordinate with his work. Meanwhile, Junio’s latest update reports that the series passes tests on its own but fails `t1305` when merged into `seen`, meaning it’s blocked from advancing to `next` until the conflict is resolved. The author will need to investigate the failure and address both the integration issue and Patrick’s feedback before the series can progress.

The series itself is technically sound—it refactors `include_by_gitdir()` into a more general `include_by_path()` helper, adds the new keywords, and includes thorough test coverage—but it’s now paused while these issues are sorted out. No prior versions are merged, and the thread is still under active review.
