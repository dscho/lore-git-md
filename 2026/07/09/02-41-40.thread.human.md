This thread is about a patch series introducing `includeIf worktree:<pattern>` to Git’s config system, allowing conditional includes based on the working tree’s path. The feature aims to improve multi-worktree setups by reusing existing path-matching logic while preserving symlinks, similar to the existing `gitdir:` behavior. Chen Linxuan is the author, and the series is now at version 7.

The current version addresses earlier feedback but has hit a design snag: Patrick Steinhardt raised concerns about the complexity of the symlink-preserving logic and its interaction with his in-flight `ps/setup-split-discovery-and-setup` series. He proposed either removing a `chdir()` call during repository discovery (a larger change) or documenting the limitation for now. The author hasn’t yet responded to these concerns, leaving the series in limbo until a resolution is found.

The series consists of three patches: a preparatory refactoring, the core implementation (with new `worktree:` and `worktree/i:` keywords), and tests/documentation. No prior versions are merged, and the feature touches `config.c`, `repository.c`, and `setup.c`. Patrick’s latest email was a procedural note asking the author to keep future patch versions in the same thread, but the substantive discussion remains focused on the architectural tradeoffs he highlighted earlier.
