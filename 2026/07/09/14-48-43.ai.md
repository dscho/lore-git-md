This is the reftable backend performance regression fix series, now in its third iteration. Kristofer Karlsson acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s API-compatibility refinement from the v2 review: the `suppress_deletions` flag will be moved from the merged iterator to `struct reftable_stack_options`, allowing downstream users like libgit2 to control tombstone suppression at stack creation time. This change is mechanical and preserves libgit2’s current behavior while future-proofing the API. The author confirms that libgit2 will need updating regardless due to its use of pre-options-stack code, so the compatibility break is already inevitable. No other open questions remain; the series is on track for v3.
