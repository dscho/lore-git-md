This is the second patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s two-patch bugfix series that restores correct performance for incremental commit-graph writes on repositories with split commit-graph chains. The regression was introduced in September 2025 by commit `199d452758` and caused a 17× slowdown (233 ms → 4133 ms) because the `topo_levels` slab was only attached to the topmost graph layer, forcing `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to re-walk the entire commit ancestry via DFS.

The fix itself is a one-line change in `commit-graph.c`: the loop that propagates the `topo_levels` slab now uses `chain->topo_levels` instead of `g->topo_levels`, ensuring every layer in the split chain can see the cached topological levels. The accompanying test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` flips from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success` to confirm that only a single DFS step is needed after the fix. Reviews from Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt were positive, and Junio has already queued the series for `master`. The patch is uncontroversial, narrowly scoped, and directly addresses the identified performance regression.
