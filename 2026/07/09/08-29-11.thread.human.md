This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series to remove more uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable in the refs subsystem and worktree code. The goal is to make repository handling explicit by replacing implicit global references with explicit `struct repository *` parameters. The series also fixes a latent bug in the packed-refs backend where the `core.packedRefsTimeout` configuration was incorrectly shared across repositories.

The series is now merged to `master` as of May 27, 2026. The latest version (v3) resolved a behavioral regression in the packed-refs timeout initialization and eliminated redundancy in the patch organization. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed the series thoroughly, catching the timeout bug in an earlier version and resolving minor integration conflicts. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial, touching 25 files across the refs backends, worktree internals, and built-in commands. No new test failures were introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.

Key participants include Patrick Steinhardt (the author), Junio Hamano (who provided substantive review and merged the series), and Toon Claes and Christian Couder (who offered surface-level endorsements). The series is part of the broader effort to remove `the_repository`, led by René Scharfe, and depended on an earlier merged series (`ps/refs-writing-subcommands`). The latest email from Toon Claes simply acknowledges the v3 adjustment, adding no new feedback. There are no open questions or loose ends.
