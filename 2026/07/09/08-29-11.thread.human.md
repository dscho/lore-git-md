This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series to remove more uses of the global `the_repository` variable in Git’s refs and worktree code. The series is part of the long-running effort to make repository handling explicit throughout Git, and it touches 25 files, including core ref-handling functions and public worktree APIs. A latent bug in the packed-refs backend—where the `core.packedRefsTimeout` setting was incorrectly shared across repositories—was fixed along the way.

The series is currently blocked by two issues. First, Junio C Hamano caught a behavioral regression in the first patch: the new per-repository timeout field defaults to 0 ms instead of the old 1000 ms default, which Patrick will fix in the next version. More critically, the series causes widespread test failures when merged into Junio’s integration branches (`jch` or `seen`), suggesting integration conflicts with other in-flight topics. Patrick has since diagnosed a likely mismerge in `setup.c` and is rebasing the series to confirm and resolve the issue. Until these conflicts are sorted out, the series cannot move forward.

No other contributors have weighed in yet, and the open questions remain focused on the integration conflicts and the timeout initialization fix. The next step is for Patrick to post a v2 that addresses both issues.
