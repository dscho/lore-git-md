This thread is a focused refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt that continues the long-running effort to eliminate Git’s global `the_repository` variable. The series, now at v1, systematically replaces implicit uses of the global with explicit `struct repository *` parameters across the refs subsystem and related worktree code. The second patch—a one-line removal of a macro—is purely mechanical and typical of the larger effort, though the series also fixes a latent bug in the packed-refs backend where `core.packedRefsTimeout` settings were incorrectly shared across repositories.

So far, the series has drawn no review feedback, but it’s early days. Patrick frames it as incremental progress with no intended behavioral changes beyond the timeout fix, and the patches are based on the start of the Git 2.56 cycle with his earlier `ps/refs-writing-subcommands` topic already merged. Minor conflicts with the `seen` branch are documented but easily resolved. If you’re tracking the `the_repository` removal project, this is a straightforward step forward; otherwise, it’s unlikely to demand attention unless you work closely with the refs or worktree internals.
