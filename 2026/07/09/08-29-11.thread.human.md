This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series to remove more uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable from the refs subsystem and worktree code. The goal is to make repository handling explicit throughout Git by replacing implicit global references with explicit `struct repository *` parameters. The series also fixes a latent bug in the packed-refs backend where the `core.packedRefsTimeout` configuration was previously stored in function-static variables, causing only the first repository’s setting to be used.

The series is now in its third version, posted on May 23, 2026, and is considered integration-ready. It has been queued in Junio’s `seen` and `jch` branches and is expected to graduate to `master` in the next cycle. The earlier behavioral regression in the packed-refs timeout initialization (where a zero-initialized `timeout_value` replaced the old 1000 ms default) has been fixed, and the redundancy in patch organization from v2 has been resolved. Junio’s review caught the timeout regression, and he also resolved minor integration conflicts caused by a mismerge in `setup.c`. Toon Claes provided a surface-level review, noting the series was straightforward and ready to proceed.

The final patch in the series, just posted, mechanically converts the last remaining callsites in built-in commands and `refs.c` to use explicit repository parameters. It updates the signatures of `copy_branchname()` and `check_branch_ref()`, adjusts their callers, and removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `refs.c`. The changes are uncontroversial and complete the series’ stated goal. No open questions remain, and the series is on track to merge.
