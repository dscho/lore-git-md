This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series (v3) that removes more uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable from the refs subsystem and worktree code. The series is part of the long-running effort to make repository handling explicit throughout Git, and it also fixes a latent bug in the packed-refs backend where the `core.packedRefsTimeout` configuration was incorrectly shared across repositories. After addressing a behavioral regression in the timeout initialization and resolving minor integration conflicts, the series is now mechanically complete and has been queued in Junio’s `seen` and `jch` branches. Junio’s latest email gives the final go-ahead, confirming the series will graduate to `master` in the next cycle unless new issues arise. No further discussion or follow-up is expected.
