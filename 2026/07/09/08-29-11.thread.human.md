This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series to remove more uses of the global `the_repository` variable from Git’s refs subsystem and worktree code. The goal is to make repository handling explicit by passing `struct repository *` parameters instead of relying on the global variable. The series also fixes a latent bug in the packed-refs backend where the `core.packedRefsTimeout` configuration was incorrectly shared across repositories due to static variables.

The series is under review, with v1 posted on May 15, 2026. Junio C Hamano recently identified a subtle but important issue in the first patch: the new per-repository `timeout_value` field is zero-initialized by `xcalloc()`, but the old static variable defaulted to 1000 ms. This would cause the timeout to regress to 0 ms when the config key is unset, which is likely unintended. Junio’s review is substantive, as it catches a behavioral change that could have slipped through as a regression. The fix is straightforward—explicitly initializing `timeout_value` to 1000 in `packed_ref_store_init()`—but the oversight highlights the importance of careful review even in mechanical refactoring series.

The rest of the series is uncontroversial and focuses on systematic refactoring, including updates to the public worktree API, built-in commands, and core ref-handling functions. No other open questions or objections have been raised yet, though Patrick noted minor conflicts with the `seen` branch that are easily resolvable. The series is part of the ongoing effort to eliminate `the_repository`, driven primarily by René Scharfe.
