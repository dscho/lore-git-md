This is the second patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s two-patch bugfix series that restores correct topological-level propagation in split commit-graph chains. The patch fixes the core regression: the `topo_levels` slab was being attached only to the topmost graph layer (`g->topo_levels`), so commits parsed from lower layers appeared to have no generation numbers, forcing `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to re-walk the entire commit ancestry via DFS. The one-line change replaces `g->topo_levels` with `chain->topo_levels` in the propagation loop, ensuring the slab is visible to every layer in the chain. The accompanying test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` flips from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success` to verify that the DFS now stops after a single step, confirming the fix. This patch is uncontested, has received positive reviews from Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt, and is already merged to `master` as part of the v2 series.
