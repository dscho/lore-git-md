This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem and worktree code. It is a pure refactoring: every call to `the_repository` in `worktree.c` is replaced with the repository pointer that is already available in the `struct worktree` (`wt->repo`). The diff is small (14 lines changed) and mechanical, touching only the seven spots where `the_repository` was used to construct paths inside the worktrees directory. No new behavior is introduced, and the patch is presented as a straightforward step toward the larger goal of eliminating the global repository variable.
