Johannes Schindelin’s twelve-patch bugfix series (`js/coverity-fixes-v2`) has been approved for integration. The series addresses NULL-dereference vulnerabilities in eleven files across diff, remote, reftable, mailsplit, bisect, replay, revision, pack-bitmap, and shallow subsystems, plus a latent bug in `write_one_shallow()` related to `oid_to_hex()`’s rotating static buffer.
