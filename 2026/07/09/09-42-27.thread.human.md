This thread is a bugfix series by Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) addressing eleven NULL-dereference vulnerabilities identified by Coverity, spanning multiple subsystems. The series has been well-received as uncontroversial and motivated, with seven of the eleven patches already approved by Junio C Hamano. The fixes are purely defensive, adding guards or early checks to prevent crashes in edge cases without altering normal behavior.

The latest update concerns the tenth patch, targeting `bisect_start()` in `builtin/bisect.c`. Junio’s review questions the real-world plausibility of the edge case—where `refs_resolve_ref_unsafe()` returns NULL but `repo_get_oid("HEAD")` succeeds—and requests a test to demonstrate the bug and prevent regressions. While the fix itself (falling back to `"HEAD"`) is uncontroversial, the lack of a concrete reproduction case leaves its necessity slightly ambiguous. This follows a pattern in the series where Junio has requested tests for subtle edge cases, such as in the eighth patch (revision.c), to ensure long-term robustness.

The series remains in review, with four patches requiring updates: the fourth (`remote.c`) due to a design disagreement over error handling (silent `-1` vs. `BUG()`), the sixth (`bisect_successful()`) for a symbolic constant fix, the eighth (revision.c) for a regression test, and now the tenth (`bisect_start()`) for either a test or clarification. The final patch (shallow.c) also awaits a v3 update to address a latent `oid_to_hex()` bug. Despite these loose ends, the series is on track for integration, with no objections to its core defensive approach. Key participants include Dscho (author) and Junio (maintainer), whose reviews have been substantive but narrowly focused on correctness and maintainability.
