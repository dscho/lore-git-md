This thread is a twelve-patch bugfix series (`js/coverity-fixes-v2`) by Johannes Schindelin (Dscho), addressing NULL-dereference vulnerabilities identified by Coverity across eleven files in Git’s codebase. The series targets edge cases in diff, remote, reftable, mailsplit, bisect, replay, revision, pack-bitmap, and shallow subsystems, plus a latent bug in `write_one_shallow()` related to `oid_to_hex()`’s rotating static buffer. Each patch adds defensive checks or guards to prevent crashes or undefined behavior.

The series is now in its second iteration, incorporating all feedback from Junio C Hamano’s reviews of v1. The series is uncontroversial, with no open questions or loose ends, and is ready for final review and integration. Taylor Blau was CC’d for incremental MIDX expertise but has not yet reviewed.
