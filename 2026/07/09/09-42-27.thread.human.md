This eleven-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) continues to address NULL-dereference vulnerabilities identified by Coverity, now with all but one patch reviewed by Junio C Hamano. The series targets edge cases across diff, remote, reftable, mailsplit, bisect, replay, revision, pack-bitmap, shallow, and diffcore-break subsystems, adding guards or early checks to prevent crashes. The first five patches—covering diffcore-break, diff, reftable stack, remote, and mailsplit—have been approved by Junio, who called them "obviously correct" or "well done." The seventh patch, addressing a missing MIDX bitmap in incremental chains, has now also received Junio’s endorsement as "nicely reasoned," leaving only procedural notes about reviewer CCs.

Three patches still require updates: the bisect patch needs a v3 to use the `BISECT_FAILED` constant instead of a raw `-1`; the revision patch awaits a regression test; and the shallow patch must incorporate Junio’s fix for a latent `oid_to_hex()` bug. The only substantive disagreement remains in the remote patch, where Dscho prefers returning `-1` for a NULL `remote` while Junio argues for a `BUG()` to expose programming errors. With the MIDX patch now approved, the series is otherwise uncontroversial and ready for integration once the remaining updates are posted.
