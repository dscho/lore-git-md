This is a refactoring series (v9, 9 patches) that continues the libification effort by migrating more global configuration variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is now **technically complete and memory-leak-free**, addressing the regression Junio C Hamano identified in v8. The fix for the `pager_program` leak in `core_pager_config()` (used by `read_early_config()`) is the sole change since v8, ensuring consistent memory management across all migrated fields.

The series remains split into two parts: infrastructure and strings (patches 1-6) and enums (patches 7-9). The infrastructure work introduces `repo_config_values_clear()` to manage heap-allocated strings, while the enum migrations handle `push_default`, `autorebase`, and `object_creation_mode`. The RFC questions from earlier versions—whether migrating program paths (editor, pager, askpass) is worthwhile, and how this work relates to `git_branch_track`—remain open but do not block progress.

Key technical details:
- **Memory management**: All heap-allocated fields (`pager_program`, `editor_program`, `askpass_program`, `excludes_file`, `attributes_file`, `apply_default_whitespace`, `apply_default_ignorewhitespace`) are now properly freed during re-assignment, resolving the leak Junio flagged.
- **Files touched**: Primarily `environment.c`/`environment.h`, with smaller updates to `apply.c`, `branch.c`, `builtin/push.c`, `pager.c`, and others.
- **New symbols**: `repo_config_values_clear()` (destructor), getters like `repo_askpass_program()`, and direct field access for migrated variables.
- **Unresolved `NEEDSWORK` items**: Submodule support for `excludes_file`/`attributes_file`, circular dependency for `push_default` enum placement, and cleanup of comments/variable classification in `repo_config_values`. These are not blockers.

The series is **CI-clean** and ready for `next`. The remaining open questions are design-oriented (RFCs) rather than correctness issues. The author has promised follow-up patches to address the `NEEDSWORK` items and improve documentation. This iteration looks like a solid step forward in the `the_repository` removal effort.
