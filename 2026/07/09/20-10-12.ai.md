This is a maintainer review of the final patch in Johannes Schindelin’s eleven-patch `js/coverity-fixes-v2` series, which fixes a NULL-dereference in `shallow.c`. Junio C Hamano identifies a latent bug in the same code path: the existing use of `oid_to_hex()`’s rotating static buffer means that the `hex` variable computed at the start of `write_one_shallow()` may be clobbered by subsequent calls before it is used in the verbose logging message. The fix proposed here is to replace the static buffer with a stack-allocated buffer (`hex[GIT_MAX_HEXSZ + 1]`) and use `oid_to_hex_r()` to populate it, ensuring the string remains valid throughout the function. Junio’s suggestion is a small but technically important improvement that eliminates a subtle correctness risk without changing the patch’s defensive goal. The review is substantive, addressing a real (though latent) issue, and the proposed change is narrowly scoped and well-justified. The series as a whole remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, but this final patch will likely need a v3 update to incorporate the fix.
