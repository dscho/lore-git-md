This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem. It is a one-line cleanup: the packed-backend no longer needs the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro because the single remaining use of `the_repository` has already been converted to use the backend’s own repository pointer. The change is purely mechanical and has no effect on behavior. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
