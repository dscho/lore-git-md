This is a follow-up in Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series, specifically addressing Junio C Hamano’s safety concern about the error-handling flow in `odb_transaction_begin()` (patch 6/11). The series refactors `git-receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of `tmp_objdir`, advancing the ODB abstraction effort.

Justin confirms that `odb_source_begin_transaction()` can fail in later patches (though not in this one) and that the backend guarantees `*out` remains untouched on failure. He agrees with Junio’s suggestion to conditionally assign `odb->transaction` only when the backend call succeeds, adopting the defensive pattern Junio proposed (`if (!(ret = ...)) odb->transaction = *out;`). This is a low-level C safety fix: it ensures the ODB’s internal state is updated only when the transaction is successfully initialized, preventing potential dangling references or double-frees if the caller proceeds under the assumption that `*out` is valid.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial. Justin plans to include it in the next version of the series, which is otherwise functionally complete and unblocked for `next`. No other aspects of the patch are under discussion here. The series remains part of Patrick Steinhardt’s larger ODB abstraction effort, with deferred follow-up work scoped for error-handling improvements, explicit abort interfaces, and transaction lifecycle management.
