This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. The patch widens the `bufsize` parameter of `create_delta_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`.

The change is mechanical and safe: the sole caller (`try_delta()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`) already passes an `unsigned long`, which promotes cleanly to `size_t`. The patch splits this widening from the later, more invasive changes to `diff_delta()` and `create_delta()` to keep each commit small and reviewable. No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced; this is purely a type-correctness fix for platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`.

The patch touches only two files (`delta.h` and `diff-delta.c`) and is uncontroversial. It is part of the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`, driven by real-world truncation issues on 64-bit Windows. The series as a whole is well-scoped and low-risk, making this patch a straightforward step in the larger conversion.
