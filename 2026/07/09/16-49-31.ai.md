This is the fourth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size-related fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility.

The patch widens the return type of `free_unpacked()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The function sums two byte counts: `sizeof_delta_index()` (already `size_t`) and `SIZE(n->entry)` (widened to `size_t` in an earlier topic). On 64-bit Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32-bit, this sum was silently truncated. The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior or on-disk formats.

The sole caller, `find_deltas()`, still uses an `unsigned long` for its `mem_usage` counter, so truncation persists there for now. The patch is a small, focused step in the broader effort to eliminate incorrect `unsigned long` usage in favor of `size_t`. No new tests are added, as the change is a correctness fix for platforms where `unsigned long` is narrower than `size_t`. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of preventing integer truncation in large-object scenarios.
