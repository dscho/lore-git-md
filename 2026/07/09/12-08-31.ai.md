This is the second patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s v2 two-patch bugfix series targeting a quadratic performance regression in the reftable backend when tombstones (deleted refs) are present. The patch removes the `suppress_deletions` flag from Git’s internal use of the merged iterator, moving tombstone handling to the reftable backend’s call sites where prefix and refname bounds are available. This enables early termination of iteration when tombstones exceed the relevant bound, restoring linear performance. The `suppress_deletions` flag is retained in the reftable library API for downstream users like libgit2, preserving compatibility.

The patch touches `refs/reftable-backend.c` (core logic) and `reftable/stack.c` (single-line removal). In `reftable-backend.c`, it adds explicit deletion checks to all ref and log iteration paths, ensuring tombstones are skipped only after bounds checks. The changes are mechanical and narrowly scoped: no new symbols are introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. Performance improvements are dramatic—both test scenarios in `p1401-ref-store-tombstones.sh` now run in ~0.2s (down from ~14s).

The patch is well-motivated and addresses prior feedback about compatibility and test isolation. It is part of a series that has already seen substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt and real-world validation from brian m. carlson, who observed the regression in production-scale repositories. The fix is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration. No open questions remain; the series is under active review and likely to land soon.
