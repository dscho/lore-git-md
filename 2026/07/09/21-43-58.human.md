This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing object filters to `odb_for_each_object()`, a key step in the ODB abstraction effort. Justin Tobler’s reply raises a design question about the new `filter` field: he finds it awkward that callers must re-apply the filter to returned objects for correctness, even when the backend claims to support filtering. His suggestion is to define a subset of object filters that all backends are expected to handle, with unsupported filters delegated to the caller, rather than the current all-or-nothing approach.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused on the interface contract. Justin understands the motivation—letting backends optimize filtering—but questions whether the current design achieves the right balance between efficiency and correctness. His alternative would make the interface more predictable by guaranteeing that certain filters are always handled by the backend, while others remain the caller’s responsibility. This is not a rejection of the patch, but a request for clarification or possible refinement of the design. The thread remains open; Patrick’s response will determine whether the interface stays as-is or is adjusted.
