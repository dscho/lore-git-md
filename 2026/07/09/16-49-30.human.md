This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch series widening size fields in Git’s delta and pack machinery to `size_t` for 64-bit Windows compatibility. It targets the delta-cache accounting variables and helper in `builtin/pack-objects.c`.

The patch widens three related globals—`delta_cache_size`, `max_delta_cache_size`, and the `delta_cacheable()` function’s parameters—from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. On 64-bit Windows, `unsigned long` is 32 bits, so a large pack with many cached deltas could silently wrap the cumulative byte count. The change is internally consistent: the arithmetic already involves `size_t` values (from `DELTA_SIZE()`), and the sole caller (`try_delta()`) still passes `unsigned long`, which safely promotes to `size_t`.

This is a mechanical, low-risk type widening with no behavior or on-disk format changes. It’s a prerequisite for removing `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` shims in `try_delta()` once later patches widen `create_delta()` and `diff_delta()`. The patch is well-motivated by real-world Windows truncation issues and fits cleanly into the broader `unsigned long` → `size_t` conversion effort. No controversy is expected.
