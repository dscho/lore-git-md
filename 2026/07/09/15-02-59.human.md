This is the rerolled v2 of a two-patch bugfix series targeting the commit-graph subsystem. The series fixes a performance regression introduced in September 2025 by commit `199d452758` (part of the `ps/commit-graph-via-source` series), where the `topo_levels` slab was incorrectly propagated only to the topmost layer of a split commit-graph chain. This forced `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to re-walk the entire ancestry of commits parsed from lower layers, degrading performance from 233ms to 4133ms for an incremental commit-graph write on a large repository.

Patch 1/2 adds trace2 instrumentation to `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to expose the cost of the generation-number DFS, making it easier to detect similar regressions. The test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` is updated to use `test_expect_failure` to demonstrate the bug, expecting 7 DFS steps instead of the correct 1. Patch 2/2 fixes the regression by correcting the loop variable in the propagation logic from `g->topo_levels` to `chain->topo_levels`, ensuring the slab is propagated to all layers of the split commit-graph chain. The test flips to `test_expect_success` to verify the fix.

The series is narrowly scoped, well-motivated, and uncontested. Both patches were reviewed by Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed the fix as correct and the test change as sensible. Junio C Hamano marked the topic as "Expecting a reroll" after v1 and is ready to merge v2. The only changes since v1 are corrected commit message details (date and title) and a simplified test assertion. The fix is minimal and targeted, addressing the root cause without architectural changes, though Taylor Blau suggested a follow-up refactor to prevent similar bugs. The series is ready for merge.
