This is a substantive review comment on the second patch in Pablo Sabater’s five-patch GSoC series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. The reviewer, Chandra Pratap, identifies a potential out-of-bounds array access in `fetch-object-info.c` if the server sends a malformed or truncated response. Specifically, the code dereferences `object_info_values.items[type_index + 1]` without first checking whether `type_index + 1` is within the bounds of the `object_info_values` array. This could lead to a segmentation fault rather than a graceful protocol error.

The concern is narrowly scoped to the parsing logic introduced in this patch and does not challenge the overall design or scope of the series. The reviewer suggests adding a bounds check before the dereference and recommends a test using `test-tool pkt-line` to verify that the client fails gracefully when given malformed input. No other issues are raised in this email, and the feedback is framed as a correctness and robustness improvement rather than a fundamental objection. The series remains under active review, with this comment likely to be addressed in a future revision.
