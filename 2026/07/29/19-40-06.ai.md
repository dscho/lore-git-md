This is a bugfix patch for the `git maintenance` command.

The patch addresses a regression introduced in commit d465be2327 (October 2025), which made `git maintenance` error out on unknown strategies instead of silently defaulting to the `none` strategy. However, the parser function `parse_maintenance_strategy()` did not actually recognize `"none"` as a valid strategy, even though it is documented and can be used to explicitly disable maintenance tasks. The patch adds a case-insensitive check for `"none"` in the parser and updates the test suite to verify that `none` is accepted both with and without a schedule.

The change is minimal: two lines added to `builtin/gc.c` and three lines to `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`. The fix is well-motivated by the regression reference and the test coverage ensures the documented behavior is restored. The patch looks uncontroversial and ready for review.
