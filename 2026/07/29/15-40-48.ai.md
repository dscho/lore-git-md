This is a **maintainer reply** in the ongoing review of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC series adding `%(objecttype)` support to `git cat-file --batch-command` for remote-object-info queries. Junio C Hamano responds to Chandra Pratap’s review of the first patch (`protocol-caps: add type support to object-info`).

Junio **agrees with Chandra’s technical points** but **pushes back on the grep-ability argument**. He notes that the original error message line (92 columns) exceeds Git’s usual 70-column limit, justifying the wrap, and dismisses the grep concern by pointing out that partial matches (`"object-info: protocol error"`) remain effective. On the test portability issue, Junio endorses Chandra’s suggestion to replace `wc -c <file | xargs` with Git’s `test_file_size` helper, calling it a "well spotted" improvement.

The exchange is **surface-level** but clarifies maintainer preferences: adherence to line-length limits trumps grep-ability dogma, while test portability is non-negotiable. No objections to the patch’s core functionality are raised, and the feedback is likely to be incorporated in the next iteration. The series remains under review.
