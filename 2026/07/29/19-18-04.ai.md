This is a substantive follow-up in the `pack.allowDuplicateObjects` RFC thread, where Taylor Blau (ttaylorr@openai.com) pushes back on Junio C Hamano’s philosophical objection to allowing duplicate objects in packfiles, even as an opt-in feature. The email advances the discussion by arguing that Git’s internals are already hardened against duplicates (via the `tb/pack-with-duplicates` series) and that the proposed design can be refined to address Junio’s concerns without abandoning the performance use case.

Taylor’s response focuses on three key points. First, he challenges the historical precedent Junio cites (a discussion with Shawn Pearce) by noting that Git’s delta resolution and reverse-index handling have since been hardened to safely process packs with duplicates, as demonstrated in his earlier `tb/pack-with-duplicates` series. This undermines the "slippery slope" argument by showing that the technical risks are already mitigated. Second, he reframes the feature as a pragmatic trade-off: while duplicate objects may be "buggy," they can contain the *only* copy of an object a user needs (echoing Jeff King’s 2014 argument), and the current `--strict` mode forces users to choose between accepting duplicates or losing other safety checks. Third, he proposes a narrower design where `--allow-duplicate-objects` would *only* relax the duplicate-OID check in `--strict` mode, preserving all other validation (connectivity, object integrity, etc.), and suggests this could eventually be adopted by Git’s own `upload-pack` for performance gains.

The email also clarifies that duplicate objects are not a new problem—non-strict `index-pack` and shallow/filtered clones already allow them—and argues that an explicit opt-in is cleaner than the current implicit acceptance. Taylor’s tone is collaborative but firm, treating Junio’s objection as a design challenge rather than a veto. The ball is now back in Junio’s court to either accept the refined approach or articulate why the hardened internals and narrower scope still violate Git’s principles. - **Related work**: The `tb/pack-with-duplicates` series is positioned as a prerequisite, demonstrating Git’s ability to handle duplicates safely.
