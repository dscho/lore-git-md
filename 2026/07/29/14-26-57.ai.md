This is a **design discussion reply** in the `git rebase --update-refs` symref bugfix thread. Junio C Hamano concedes the design argument after Phillip Wood provides concrete evidence that TopGit actively relies on `git rebase` working when HEAD is a symref pointing outside `refs/heads/` (specifically to `refs/top-bases/...`). Junio’s two-sentence reply ("Sigh... Sigh, again, but OK. Thanks.") acknowledges the real-world constraint and effectively closes the design debate for this series: the robustness approach in the current patch (using `resolved_ref` for HEAD comparison) will stand, and v4 will not narrow the scope. The latent `head_ref` buffer clobbering bug remains unaddressed and is still a candidate for a follow-up patch.
