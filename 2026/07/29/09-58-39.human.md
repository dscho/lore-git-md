This is the tail end of a feature series adding support for external commands to display Git notes. The author, Siddh Raman Pant, has now given up on the thread after four weeks of silence from Johannes Sixt (Hannes) and no other reviewers. He infers a NACK from the lack of response and expresses mild frustration at the absence of explicit feedback.

The series itself (v3, 4 patches) introduced a new protocol for delegating notes content retrieval to external commands, motivated by TOCTOU issues in large repositories and a desire to integrate external data into Git’s notes display. It included robust test coverage, timeout handling, and configuration knobs, but the implementation has never been reviewed on its technical merits. The only substantive discussion was an architectural debate between the author and Hannes, who argued the problem could be solved by improving Git’s native notes backend or tracking metadata externally.

With this email the thread effectively ends in procedural limbo: no formal rejection, no technical review, and no path forward. The author’s resignation signals that the series is unlikely to be revived without either a clear NACK or a new reviewer stepping in. For a busy reader, this is a feature that looked promising on paper but stalled due to lack of consensus and attention.
