This is a **follow-up discussion** in the ongoing `[PATCH v3]` series adding seven new `path.*` keys to `git repo info`. The email is a reply from the author, K Jayatheerth, addressing Justin Tobler’s surface-level review of patch 1/7 (and parts of patches 2/7–4/7). The focus remains on mechanical cleanups, naming consistency, and edge-case handling, with no new substantive design questions introduced beyond those already noted in the thread summary.

Jayatheerth accepts all of Justin’s mechanical suggestions: removing redundant `strbuf_addstr(buf, "")` calls, adding missing test coverage for `path.toplevel.relative` in bare repositories, and simplifying the `format_path()` call in `path.objects.absolute` by passing an empty string instead of `startup_info->prefix`. These changes are trivial and will be incorporated into the upcoming v4 iteration.

On naming, Jayatheerth leans toward Justin’s suggestion of `superproject-root` over `superproject-working-tree` for brevity and clarity, but defers to consistency with `git rev-parse --show-superproject-working-tree`. The final decision is left open, though the author’s preference is now on record.

The discussion of `/dev/null` handling for `path.hooks` is the most substantive point here. Jayatheerth argues against special-casing `/dev/null` to return an empty string, citing three reasons: (1) `/dev/null` is a valid `core.hooksPath` value treated literally by `git rev-parse --git-path hooks`, (2) empty string is already used in the series to mean "concept does not apply" (e.g., bare repositories), and (3) scripts already handle `/dev/null` when using the existing interface. He proposes documenting the behavior rather than altering it, and will add a test to lock in the current behavior (passing `/dev/null` through unchanged). This aligns with the series’ broader design principle of mirroring `git rev-parse` behavior where possible.

Jayatheerth also revises the commit messages for patches 3/7 and 4/7 to avoid overly specific phrasing ("Tools and deployment hooks" → "Scripts operating on a repository") and clarifies "legacy parsing methods" as manual parsing of `git rev-parse --git-dir` or `.git/config`. These tweaks improve clarity without changing the patches’ technical content.

The email confirms that the forward-compatibility issue in patch 1/7 (use of `startup_info->prefix`) will be addressed in v4 by rebasing onto `master` and adopting the new `repo` struct prefix field. No new blockers are introduced, and the series remains on track for a v4 submission once the remaining open design questions (ODB abstraction and `/dev/null` handling) are resolved. The author’s responses suggest a preference for minimal disruption to existing Git semantics, even at the cost of carrying forward quirks like `/dev/null` verbatim.
