This thread is about a small but important bugfix to the `git maintenance` command. The issue is that since October 2025, Git has been rejecting the documented `"none"` maintenance strategy, causing the command to fail instead of defaulting to no maintenance as intended. David Lin (posting as davidzylin@gmail.com) sent a minimal patch that adds case-insensitive support for `"none"` in the strategy parser, along with tests to verify it works.

The patch is under review and has received positive feedback from both Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. Both agree the fix is correct and addresses the regression cleanly. Junio noted a minor housekeeping issue with the author's email address not matching the sign-off, but this is the only remaining hurdle. Patrick's review was substantive, confirming the fix restores the expected behavior and that the tests are appropriate. The patch touches just two files (`builtin/gc.c` and `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`) and is ready to merge once the author identity is aligned.
