This thread is about a small but important bugfix to the `git maintenance` command. David Lin identified a regression from October 2025 where `git maintenance` would error out on unknown strategies, breaking the documented behavior that it should default to the `none` strategy. The catch: the parser didn't actually recognize `"none"` as valid, even though it's in the documentation. The patch adds case-insensitive support for `"none"` and updates the tests to verify it works with or without a schedule.

The fix is tiny—just two lines in `builtin/gc.c` and three in the test file—and looks uncontroversial. No reviews have come in yet, but given the straightforward nature of the change and the clear regression it fixes, it's likely to be merged quickly once someone takes a look. If you use `git maintenance`, this is one to watch for in the next release.
