This is a **substantive review** of Pablo Sabater’s GSoC patch series extending `git cat-file --batch-command` to support the `%(objecttype)` placeholder in remote-object-info queries. Chandra Pratap engages with the series’ structure and suggests a reordering of patches to improve narrative clarity for reviewers.

The review focuses on two main points. First, Chandra endorses the narrow scope of the series—supporting only object type, not disk size, delta base, or object mode—arguing that the excluded metadata is inherently unreliable or context-dependent. Second, Chandra proposes a reordering of the patch sequence: moving the dynamic request logic (currently patch 3) to the front of the series, so that the client is already generalized before the type-specific patches land. This reframing would make the feature patches (adding type support) more focused and the series easier to follow.

The review is constructive and technical, but remains advisory; no code-level issues are raised, and the series itself is described as small and well-scoped, building cleanly on the recently merged remote-object-info infrastructure. No merge status is implied; the series remains under review.
