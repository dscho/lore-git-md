This is the author's follow-up to Taylor Blau’s substantive review of the second patch in the commit-graph regression fix series. Kristofer Karlsson acknowledges Taylor’s architectural suggestion (moving the `topo_levels` field from `commit_graph` to a broader scope) but argues for deferring it in favor of the immediate one-line fix.

He explores three possible approaches to the deeper refactoring: (a) threading `topo_levels` through the call chain, which would touch many functions and introduce noise; (b) attaching it to the `object_database` struct, which avoids signature changes but raises semantic questions about whether the ODB should own this data; and (c) introducing a dedicated chain-wide struct to separate per-layer and chain-wide state, which he considers the cleanest but most invasive. He has a working prototype of option (b) but reiterates that the minimal bugfix is the right priority for now.

The email does not introduce new objections or block the series; it simply documents the trade-offs of the architectural follow-up. The series remains uncontested and ready for merge once the test restructuring (agreed in earlier discussion) is implemented. For a busy reader, this confirms the fix is solid and the deeper refactoring is a non-blocking follow-up.
