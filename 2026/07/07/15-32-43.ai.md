This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series making object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. It completes the relocation of the `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` functions from `builtin/gc.c` into the new "files" ODB source backend (`odb/source-files.c`), exposing them through vtable pointers in the ODB source interface (`odb/source.h`). The patch removes 489 lines from `builtin/gc.c` and adds 470 lines to `odb/source-files.c`, with small additions to `odb.c` and `odb.h` to wire up the new API.

Key technical details: the patch introduces two new vtable callbacks, `optimize` and `optimize_required`, allowing any ODB backend to implement its own housekeeping logic. The "files" backend’s implementation is a direct transplant of the existing incremental and geometric repack logic, now parameterized by the `struct odb_optimize_options` introduced earlier in the series. The patch also moves the `enum odb_optimize_strategy` and `enum odb_optimize_flags` definitions into `odb.h`, making them available to all backends. No behavior changes are introduced; the patch is purely a refactoring to enable future backends (e.g., reftable or custom storage) to define their own optimization strategies.

The patch is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and builds directly on the preparatory work in patches 1–10. It is the culmination of the series, providing the pluggable interface that was the stated goal. The changes are mechanical and well-contained, with no new user-visible features or on-disk format changes. The test coverage added in earlier patches remains sufficient to validate the new structure.
