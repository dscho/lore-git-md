This is the third patch in the v6 series that fixes and documents the `--linearize` option for `git replay`. The series as a whole is now a bugfix and documentation update for a feature that was merged in v5 but had critical regressions.

Toon Claes responds to Junio C Hamano’s review of the final patch. On authorship, Toon agrees to take sole responsibility for the v6 changes—removing Johannes Schindelin as co-author and adding a "Based-on-patches-by" trailer—since the bulk of the regression fixes, expanded test coverage, and design clarifications were his work. This addresses Junio’s process concern about accountability for latent bugs.

On the technical side, Toon removes the blanket incompatibility between `--linearize` and `--revert`. Junio had pointed out that reverting a closed set of commits containing merges *could* work if the inverse commits are applied linearly, and Toon confirms through testing that there is no inherent reason to forbid the combination. The restriction will be lifted in v7, simplifying the interface without introducing new edge cases.

The patch itself remains unchanged in its core functionality: `--linearize` flattens all revision ranges into a single linear history, drops merge commits, and updates refs to their new positions. The documentation already explains this behavior clearly, and the expanded test coverage in v6 verifies edge cases like divergent merges and root commits. With the authorship and incompatibility issues resolved, the series is now ready for final review and re-merge. No further design changes are proposed; this is purely a bugfix and documentation update.
