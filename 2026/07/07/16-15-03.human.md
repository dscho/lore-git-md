This is the second patch in Jeff King’s seven-part refactoring of the `git_hash_*()` API, which aims to standardize and harden its usage. Junio C Hamano’s reply is a surface-level review: he calls the patch "very straight-forward" but does not engage with the technical rationale or edge cases. The patch itself mechanically converts direct calls to the hash-algo vtable’s `update_fn()`, `final_fn()`, `final_oid_fn()`, `clone_fn()`, and `discard_fn()` into the corresponding `git_hash_*()` wrapper functions, touching three files (`builtin/submodule--helper.c`, `t/helper/test-synthesize.c`, and the new Coccinelle semantic patch in `tools/coccinelle/hash.cocci`). The change eliminates redundant `algo` pointer passing and paves the way for the later idempotent `git_hash_discard()` behavior. No functional changes are introduced, and the Coccinelle rule will prevent future regressions by enforcing the wrapper-only interface. The series remains on track for integration, with only cosmetic feedback outstanding from Junio’s earlier review of patch 1/7.
