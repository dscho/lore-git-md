This is the final patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series hardening the `git_hash_*()` API. Junio C Hamano replies to patch 7/7 with a narrow but pointed question: while he agrees that calling `git_hash_clone()` or `git_hash_update()` on an inactive context should trigger a `BUG()`, he is uneasy about the asymmetry that allows `git_hash_discard()` after `git_hash_final()` but forbids calling `git_hash_final()` twice. His concern is not a technical objection—he acknowledges the caffeine factor—but a design-level preference for consistency. The patch itself remains unchanged; this is a request for clarification rather than a demand for revision. The series is otherwise complete, with all substantive feedback addressed and only minor cosmetic nits remaining.
