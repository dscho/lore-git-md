This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Kristofer Karlsson that addresses a performance regression in Git’s commit-graph subsystem, specifically in split commit-graph chains. The regression was introduced by a September 2025 commit in the `ps/commit-graph-via-source` series (OID `199d452758`), which caused the `topo_levels` slab to be propagated only to the topmost layer of the chain, forcing unnecessary re-walks of commit ancestry during incremental writes. The fix restores the original performance—reducing time from 4133ms to 233ms in the reported case—by correcting a loop variable in the propagation logic.

The series is now uncontested and ready for merge. Taylor Blau reviewed both patches, approving the one-line fix in patch 2/2 as "obviously good" and suggesting an architectural refactor (moving `topo_levels` to `write_commit_graph_ctx`) as a follow-up. Kristofer explored the refactor but deferred it, concluding the current fix is the best near-term solution. The test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` will be updated to flip from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success` to verify the fix.
