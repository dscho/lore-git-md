This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Kristofer Karlsson addressing a performance regression in Git’s commit-graph subsystem, where incremental writes slowed dramatically due to a mispropagation of topological level data in split commit-graph chains. The fix itself—a one-line correction to ensure the `topo_levels` slab is propagated to all layers—has been reviewed and approved by Taylor Blau as "obviously good," with no technical objections from other participants. The series is now blocked only on a clarification from the author: Junio C Hamano has twice asked whether the commit message’s reference to `199d452758` as the regression’s source is accurate, as its subject and date don’t match the described issue. Once this detail is resolved and the test is restructured to use `test_expect_failure`, the series is expected to merge.

The discussion also touched on a potential architectural improvement—moving the `topo_levels` field from `commit_graph` to `write_commit_graph_ctx` to reduce future mispropagation risk—but Kristofer deferred this as a non-blocking follow-up after exploring three refactoring paths and concluding the current fix is sufficient for now. The thread remains uncontested, with all participants treating the regression as a clear bug and the fix as correct. Users with `fetch.writeCommitGraph` enabled or running `git maintenance` after fetch will see restored performance once the fix lands.
