This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series from Kristofer Karlsson that addresses a performance regression in Git's commit-graph subsystem. The issue, introduced in September 2025 by commit `199d452758`, caused the `topo_levels` slab to be incorrectly propagated only to the topmost layer of split commit-graph chains, leading to a dramatic slowdown in incremental commit-graph writes—from 233ms to 4133ms in large repositories.

The series has now been merged to `master` after a v2 reroll that corrected a minor metadata error in the commit message (fixing the date from April to September 2025). The fix itself is a one-line change in `commit-graph.c`, ensuring the `topo_levels` slab is propagated to all layers of the split commit-graph chain. The accompanying test in `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh` was restructured to first expect failure (patch 1/2) and then success (patch 2/2), demonstrating the fix. Taylor Blau suggested a post-merge refinement to further simplify the test structure, but this is non-blocking.

Key reviewers—Junio C Hamano, Taylor Blau, and Patrick Steinhardt—all endorsed the fix. Junio merged the series after the v2 reroll, while Taylor and Patrick confirmed the correctness of the change and the test updates. Kristofer deferred a deeper architectural refactor (moving the `topo_levels` slab to `struct object_database`) as a follow-up, keeping the immediate fix minimal and focused. The thread is now closed, with no open questions or objections.
