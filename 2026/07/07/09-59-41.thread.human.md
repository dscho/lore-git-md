This thread is a focused bugfix series addressing a performance regression in Git’s commit-graph subsystem, introduced by an earlier change (`ps/commit-graph-via-source`). The regression caused incremental commit-graph writes to slow dramatically—from 233ms to over 4 seconds—on repositories with split commit-graph chains, due to a mispropagation of the `topo_levels` slab that forced unnecessary ancestry traversals.

The series, authored by Kristofer Karlsson, consists of two patches: one adding diagnostic trace2 instrumentation, and another fixing the core bug by correcting a loop variable in the propagation logic. Taylor Blau reviewed both patches, approving the fix as "obviously good" while suggesting minor refinements to the instrumentation and test structure. The only substantive discussion revolved around an architectural proposal (moving `topo_levels` to a broader context struct), which Kristofer deferred as a non-blocking follow-up.

In the latest exchange, Kristofer addressed Taylor’s feedback on the instrumentation and test design. He defended the use of `intmax_t` for the trace2 counter as a pragmatic choice but acknowledged Taylor’s preference for `uintmax_t`. More significantly, he agreed to restructure the test to use `test_expect_failure`, which would simplify the series by making the fix a single-line change. The series remains uncontested, with no objections to its motivation or correctness, and is effectively ready for merge once the test restructuring is finalized. The fix restores the original performance without altering the on-disk format or requiring user intervention.
