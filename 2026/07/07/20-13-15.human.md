This is a minor follow-up in Jeff King’s 7-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API. The series aims to standardize and harden the hash API, particularly making `git_hash_discard()` idempotent to prevent misuse. This specific email responds to Junio C Hamano’s surface-level review of the first patch, which mechanically converts direct calls to `algop->init_fn()` into the wrapper function `git_hash_init()`.

Peff acknowledges Junio’s observation that Coccinelle unnecessarily added braces around single statements in two places (`object-file.c` and `rerere.c`). He explains that while he had initially removed the braces, they were reintroduced during final testing when he reapplied the semantic patch. He agrees to remove them in the upcoming v2. This is purely a cosmetic fix and does not affect the patch’s functionality or the series’ overall goal.

The series itself is well-motivated and technically sound, with all substantive feedback already addressed. Junio’s comment here is the only remaining loose end, and it is minor. The v2 will incorporate these cosmetic tweaks, after which the series is expected to be queued for integration. If you’re following the `git_hash_*()` API cleanup, this is a routine update; if not, the series is unlikely to demand your attention.
