Thread identity: patch series titled "Make ODB optimizations pluggable", v2, 12 patches, authored by Patrick Steinhardt. Thread type: patch series introducing modular ODB optimization logic as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort.

Core problem or goal: restructure Git's object database (ODB) to allow different backends to implement their own optimization strategies (repacking, geometric repacking, auto-gc) without hardcoding them in `builtin/gc.c`. The series prepares the codebase by extracting existing optimization logic into a modular system, enabling future backends (e.g., reftable, custom storage) to define their own housekeeping tasks. The first eight patches (v1 patches 1-7 plus the new v2 patch 1) address a long-standing inconsistency in the "pre-auto-gc" hook, reorder maintenance tasks to group ODB-related optimizations, refactor `cmd_gc()` to isolate repack and prune logic into `maintenance_task_odb()`, eliminate global state in repack argument assembly, relocate backend-specific configuration reads to call sites, introduce `struct odb_optimize_options` to encapsulate all ODB optimization settings, unify the incremental and geometric repacking strategies under the new `odb_optimize()` entry point, and improve test robustness by replacing brittle `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT`-based checks with a new `test_maintenance_tasks` helper that verifies task execution via trace2 output. Patch 2 (v2) expands the "pre-auto-gc" hook to intercept all auto-maintenance tasks, not just `gc`, ensuring it can inhibit tasks like `reflog-expire`, `geometric-repack`, and `rerere-gc`. Patch 3 (v2) reorders maintenance tasks in `builtin/gc.c` to group all ODB-related optimizations (repack, prune) together. Patch 4 (v2) extracts the repack and prune logic from `cmd_gc()` into a new helper function, `maintenance_task_odb()`. Patch 5 (v2) eliminates global state in repack argument assembly by moving the `strvec` of repack arguments into `maintenance_task_odb()`. Patch 6 (v2) inlines eight backend-specific configuration values into their call sites in `builtin/gc.c`. Patch 7 (v2) introduces `struct odb_optimize_options` to encapsulate all ODB optimization settings. Patch 8 (v2) unifies the incremental and geometric repacking strategies under the new `odb_optimize()` entry point. Patch 9 (v2) introduces `odb_optimize_required()`, a new helper that centralizes the auto-condition checks for both incremental and geometric repacking strategies. Patch 10 (v2) mechanically refactors ODB optimization helpers to operate on a `struct odb_source_files *` parameter. Patch 11 (v2) fixes signedness issues in `too_many_loose_objects()`, `find_base_packs()`, and `odb_optimize()` to prevent compiler warnings. Patch 12 (v2) completes the series by relocating the `odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()` functions into the "files" ODB source backend, exposing them through vtable pointers in the ODB source interface.
