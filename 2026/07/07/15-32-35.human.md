This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series making Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. It extracts the repack and prune logic from `cmd_gc()` into a new helper function, `maintenance_task_odb()`, as a pure refactoring with no intended behavior change. The patch moves the “too many loose objects” warning into the helper, which may shift its timing slightly but is not expected to have practical consequences.

The change is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, preparing the codebase so that future backends (reftable, custom storage, etc.) can implement their own optimization strategies without hardcoding them in `builtin/gc.c`. This patch is purely mechanical: it cuts 30 lines from `cmd_gc()` and pastes them into the new helper, leaving the rest of the series to build on this separation. The commit message is clear and the diff is straightforward, suggesting a well-crafted preparatory step.
