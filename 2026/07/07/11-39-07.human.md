This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing config file locking timeout discussion. Johannes Schindelin reaffirms his support for the current implementation of `core.configLockTimeout` (without an "MS" suffix), aligning with Git's established naming conventions despite his personal preference for explicit units.

He expresses mild frustration that the patch appears stalled, noting there’s no obvious reason it shouldn’t progress to `next` and eventually `master`. This comment doesn’t engage with the deeper technical debate between Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt about whether the timeout should be process-wide or per-repository, but it does signal that at least one maintainer considers the naming question settled and the patch ready for advancement. The tone is collegial but slightly impatient, nudging the discussion toward resolution.
