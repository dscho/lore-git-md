This is a brief but significant reply from Junio C Hamano in the ongoing `includeIf.worktree` patch series. The series, now targeting its seventh iteration (v7), aims to add conditional config inclusion based on the working tree’s realpath, addressing a usability gap in multi-worktree setups.

Junio’s email confirms that he is satisfied with the resolution of the symlink-handling inconsistency, which has been the primary blocker for v7. The solution—storing an additional non-realpath worktree path in `struct repository` to align `worktree` and `gitdir` behavior—was proposed by Chen Linxuan and endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt. Junio’s reply signals that he expects v7 to proceed with this approach and that the series is likely to be merged once the implementation is finalized. His tone is optimistic, suggesting no remaining objections to the core design or the symlink-handling fix.

The patch touches `config.c`, `setup.c`, and possibly `repository.h`, with test coverage expanded to handle symlinked paths, nested symlinks, and edge cases like Windows junctions. While the plumbing changes add some complexity, the consensus among reviewers is that user-facing consistency justifies the trade-off. Junio’s approval here removes the last major hurdle for the series, though Phillip Wood’s review of the final implementation remains pending.
