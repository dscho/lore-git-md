Thread identity: bugfix series for commit-graph regression in split commit-graph chains; 2 patches; originating author Kristofer Karlsson; thread type: patch series.

Core problem or goal: fix a performance regression in the commit-graph subsystem introduced by an April 2025 commit in the `ps/commit-graph-via-source` series. The bug causes the `topo_levels` slab to be incorrectly propagated only to the topmost layer of a split commit-graph chain, forcing `compute_reachable_generation_numbers()` to re-walk the entire ancestry of commits parsed from lower layers. This degrades performance from 233ms to 4133ms for an incremental commit-graph write on a large repository with a 4-layer split graph. The exact OID of the regression-introducing commit remains under clarification; the commit message cites `199d452758`, but Junio C Hamano has noted that this commit's subject and date do not match the described regression.

Current status: under review; series at v1. Junio C Hamano requested clarification on the commit OID cited in the commit message (whether the regression was introduced by `199d452758` or another April 2025 commit). Patch 1/2 adds trace2 instrumentation for diagnostics; Patch 2/2 fixes the loop variable in the propagation logic to use `chain` instead of `g`, ensuring the `topo_levels` slab is propagated to all layers of the split commit-graph chain. Taylor Blau reviewed both patches: approved Patch 2/2 as "obviously good" and suggested an exploratory architectural change (moving `topo_levels` from `commit_graph` to `write_commit_graph_ctx`) to prevent similar bugs, which Kristofer Karlsson deferred as a non-blocking follow-up. Kristofer later analyzed three refactoring paths for Taylor’s suggestion—threading the slab through the call chain, attaching it to `struct object_database`, or introducing a dedicated chain-wide struct—but concluded the current one-line fix remains the best near-term solution. The series remains uncontested and ready for merge once the commit-message clarification and test restructuring (using `test_expect_failure`) are implemented. Open questions about trace2 instrumentation type (`intmax_t` vs `uintmax_t`) and test structure are now resolved in principle.
