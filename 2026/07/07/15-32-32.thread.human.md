This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series “Make ODB optimizations pluggable,” which restructures Git’s object database to let different backends define their own housekeeping strategies (repacking, geometric repacking, auto-gc) without hard-coding them in `builtin/gc.c`. The series is part of the larger ODB abstraction effort and has reached its first posted version.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the first three patches. He endorsed the design goals—extending the “pre-auto-gc” hook to all automated maintenance tasks (patch 1) and reordering maintenance tasks to group ODB-related work (patch 2)—while raising minor, non-blocking points on patch 1. His review of patch 3 was surface-level, noting only the shift from `die()` to `error()` as appropriate. In a follow-up email, Patrick acknowledged the fragility of the test’s reliance on `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` and proposed a more robust alternative using trace2 regions; he plans to include this change in the next iteration. No objections to merging have surfaced, and the broader goal of pluggable ODB optimizations is implicitly supported.

The series is still under initial review, with no prior versions or merge status recorded. The next version may include test improvements, but the core functionality remains unchanged.
