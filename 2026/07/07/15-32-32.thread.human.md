This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series to make Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to refactor Git’s housekeeping logic—repacking, geometric repacking, and auto-gc—so that different ODB backends (like reftable or custom storage) can define their own optimization strategies without hardcoding them in `builtin/gc.c`.

The first patch in the series addresses a long-standing inconsistency: the "pre-auto-gc" hook, meant to block automated housekeeping, currently only runs for `git gc` and the "gc" maintenance task. This patch extends the hook to run once before *any* auto-maintenance task (reflog expiry, geometric repack, etc.), ensuring it fires exactly once per `git maintenance run --auto`. The change is mechanical and backward-compatible, with expanded test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` to validate the new behavior. No reviews or objections have been posted yet, and the series remains under initial review. The patch sets the stage for the rest of the series by ensuring the hook guards all backend housekeeping, not just the files backend’s gc.
