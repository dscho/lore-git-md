This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-part series making Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. It fixes a long-standing quirk in the "pre-auto-gc" hook: today the hook only runs for `git gc` and the "gc" maintenance task, even though its stated purpose is to block *any* automated housekeeping. With this patch the hook now fires exactly once, before the first auto-maintenance task that would actually run, regardless of whether that task is reflog expiry, geometric repack, or rerere gc. The change is purely behavioral—no new user-facing options or on-disk format changes—but it restores the hook’s original intent and is a prerequisite for the rest of the series, which will move all optimization logic out of `builtin/gc.c` and into pluggable ODB backends. The patch is well-motivated, includes thorough test coverage (121 new lines in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`), and looks ready for review.
