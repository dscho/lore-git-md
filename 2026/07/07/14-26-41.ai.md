This is the seventh and final patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API to make it more robust and misuse-resistant. The patch adds defensive runtime checks to all wrapper functions (`git_hash_clone`, `git_hash_update`, `git_hash_final`, and `git_hash_final_oid`) to ensure they are only called on an active hash context. If the `active` flag (introduced in patch 4) is not set, the functions now trigger a `BUG()` to catch misuse early.
