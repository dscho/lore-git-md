This thread is a seven-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the `git_hash_*()` API to make it more robust and consistent. The series focuses on making `git_hash_discard()` idempotent—safe to call multiple times—while standardizing how the hash API is used across the codebase. The first four patches laid the groundwork: converting callers to use wrapper functions, improving documentation, and introducing an `active` flag in `git_hash_ctx` to enable idempotency.

The latest patch (the fifth in the series) applies this new behavior to `csum-file.c`, simplifying cleanup logic by removing redundant checks and a helper function. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial, relying on the idempotency introduced earlier. The series as a whole is under review, with no major objections raised so far. Peff’s motivation stems from a prior observation about the unsafe behavior of `git_hash_discard()`, and the series is designed to be a one-time cleanup with no ongoing maintenance burden. The remaining patches will further enforce the new invariants and update additional callers. No user-visible changes are expected, and the work is self-contained with no external dependencies.
