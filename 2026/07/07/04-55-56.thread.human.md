This thread is a cleanup and refactoring series from Jeff King (Peff) that makes the `git_hash_*()` API more robust by ensuring `git_hash_discard()` is idempotent—safe to call multiple times—and standardizing its usage across the codebase. The series consists of seven patches and has been under review with all substantive feedback addressed.

The core goal is to eliminate inconsistent and unsafe usage of the hash API, particularly the risk of leaving the hash context in an inconsistent state if `git_hash_discard()` is called twice. The series also improves documentation, enforces consistent usage via Coccinelle semantic patches, and adds defensive runtime checks to catch misuse early. No user-visible behavior changes or on-disk format changes are expected.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed several patches, providing mostly surface-level feedback—such as noting unnecessary braces in patch 1/7 and confirming the straightforward nature of patch 2/7. His review of patch 4/7 included clarifying questions about the design intent behind making `git_hash_discard()` idempotent while leaving `git_hash_final()` unprotected, but these were framed as confirmations rather than objections. The latest patch (6/7), which removes a now-redundant flag in the HTTP transport layer, received positive feedback from Junio, who called the change "nice" for its safety and simplicity. The series is now effectively complete, with no further changes expected unless Junio raises additional cosmetic issues. The patches are ready for integration, pending any final maintainer approval.
