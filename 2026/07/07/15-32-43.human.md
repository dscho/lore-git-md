This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-part series making object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. It completes the refactoring by moving the core optimization logic—`odb_optimize()` and `odb_optimize_required()`—from `builtin/gc.c` into the new "files" ODB source (`odb/source-files.c`) and exposing them through the ODB source vtable. The patch is a mechanical relocation: it cuts 489 lines from `builtin/gc.c`, pastes them verbatim into `odb/source-files.c`, and wires up the vtable callbacks so that any ODB backend can now implement its own housekeeping strategy.

The result is a clean separation: `builtin/gc.c` no longer contains backend-specific repack or prune logic, and the "files" source now owns all of its optimization code. No user-visible behavior changes; the patch is purely preparatory for future backends (reftable, custom storage, etc.) that will be able to plug in their own `optimize` and `optimize_required` callbacks. The series as a whole is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, and this patch is the payoff—it makes the optimization machinery truly modular.
