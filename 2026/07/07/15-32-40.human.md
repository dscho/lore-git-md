This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series making Git’s object database (ODB) optimizations pluggable. It introduces `odb_optimize_required()`, a new helper that consolidates the logic for deciding whether an auto-repack or auto-gc is needed. The patch moves the existing incremental and geometric-repack decision logic out of scattered functions (`need_to_gc()` and `geometric_repack_auto_condition()`) into a single, backend-agnostic entry point. No user-visible behavior changes; the patch is purely refactoring to prepare for future ODB backends that can plug in their own optimization criteria. The change is well-motivated, touches only `builtin/gc.c`, and keeps the existing configuration semantics intact. If the series lands, this patch will be a key building block for modular housekeeping.
