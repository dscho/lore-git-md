This is a **follow-up in the `git history squash` series thread**, where Harald Nordgren responds to Phillip Wood’s substantive review of v7. The email addresses three key design questions left unresolved in the previous iteration, with implications for usability, correctness, and implementation complexity.

Harald **acknowledges but disagrees with Phillip’s suggestion** to make `--reedit-message` the default behavior. He notes the discussion "died down" and does not elaborate further in this email, but the omission suggests the series will retain the current opt-in design unless Junio or others weigh in. This leaves the **commit hygiene debate open**: Phillip’s argument (that users should review/refine squashed messages by default) remains unaddressed, and the series may ship with a behavior that diverges from `git rebase -i`’s precedent.

On **template formatting**, Harald **confirms the current implementation does not reorder commits** to place `fixup!`/`squash!`/`amend!` markers after their targets, which could make the template confusing if multiple commits target the same base. He expresses willingness to explore this ("I can take a look"), but the lack of a concrete plan suggests this may not be resolved in v8. The **deviation from `git rebase -i`’s autosquash behavior** (which reorders commits) is now explicitly documented, and Phillip’s concern about template clarity remains valid.

The **input validation gap** is the most actionable item. Harald **agrees to adopt `git replay`’s approach** to sanity-check `rev-list` options like `--reverse`, which could prevent incorrect range resolution. He also **recognizes the need to expand `reject_fixupish_oldest()`** to check all commits in the range (not just the oldest) and verify whether marker targets lie within the range. This would address Phillip’s edge case (e.g., a `fixup!` commit whose target is outside the squashed range) but adds significant complexity to the validation logic. The admission that "it just makes the logic a lot bigger" hints this may be deferred or implemented minimally.

**Key technical details for follow-up discussion**:
- **Files touched**: `builtin/history.c` (validation logic in `resolve_squash_range()` and `reject_fixupish_oldest()`), `sequencer.c` (template helpers), and tests (`t/t3455-history-squash.sh`).
- **New helpers**: Potential expansion of `reject_fixupish_oldest()` to check all commits and their targets.
- **Options**: `--reedit-message` (default behavior), `--reverse`/`--topo-order` (sanity-checking).
- **Edge cases**: Marker targets outside the range, `rev-list` option misuse.

**Status**: The series is **not yet ready for integration** despite Junio’s "Will replace" sign-off. The unresolved questions (default behavior, template reordering, expanded input validation) are **usability and correctness issues**, not just polish. A v8 is likely needed to address Phillip’s feedback, particularly the `rev-list` option sanitization and marker target validation. The **reordering of autosquash commits** and **default editor behavior** may remain contentious unless Junio or other maintainers intervene.
