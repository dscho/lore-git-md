This is a follow-up in the `git history squash` series, now at v7. Harald Nordgren responds to Phillip Wood’s substantive review with clarifications and partial concessions on three open design questions.

Harald acknowledges that the discussion about making `--reedit-message` the default behavior was not overlooked but intentionally set aside; he disagrees with the idea and will add a rationale to the next cover letter. On the template format, he agrees to investigate reordering commits so that `fixup!`/`squash!`/`amend!` markers appear immediately after their targets, matching `git rebase -i`’s behavior. For input validation, he concedes that the current `reject_fixupish_oldest()` helper is insufficient—it should check every commit in the range, not just the oldest, and verify that each marker’s target is included. He also accepts Phillip’s suggestion to adopt `git replay`’s approach for sanitizing `rev-list` options like `--reverse`.

The tone is constructive and the changes proposed are incremental rather than structural, so the series remains on track for integration once these tweaks land in v8.
