This is a surface-level review comment on the third patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API. The series aims to standardize and harden the hash API, and this patch specifically improves the documentation in `hash.h` to guide developers toward safer usage patterns.

Patrick Steinhardt points out a minor typo in the updated documentation: the phrase *"release the context with with git_hash_final()"* should read *"release the context with git_hash_final()"*. This is purely a grammatical fix and does not affect the technical content or the series’ goals. The patch itself remains focused on clarifying the API’s usage, warning against direct use of low-level hooks, and setting the stage for the Coccinelle semantic patch that will enforce wrapper usage in later patches.

Since this is a trivial correction, it’s unlikely to generate further discussion. The series as a whole is well-structured and addresses a real inconsistency in the codebase, making it worth following for anyone interested in Git’s low-level plumbing.
