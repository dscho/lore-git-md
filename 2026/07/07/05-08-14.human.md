This is the sixth patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series refactoring the `git_hash_*()` API. It updates the HTTP object-fetching code in `http.c` to rely on the newly idempotent `git_hash_discard()` function, removing the `hash_ctx_valid` flag that was introduced just five days ago (in commit `a2d8ea5a76`) to work around the old non-idempotent behavior.

The change is mechanical: the patch deletes the `hash_ctx_valid` field from the `http_object_request` struct and all the conditional logic that checked it, replacing it with a single unconditional call to `git_hash_discard()`. This simplifies the cleanup path in `release_http_object_request()` and removes a redundant field from `http.h`. The patch is small (5 lines removed, 1 added) and clearly motivated by the earlier patches in the series. No user-visible behavior changes are expected.
