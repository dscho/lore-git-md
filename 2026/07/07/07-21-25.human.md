This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s **v2 refactoring series** to separate repository discovery from setup in Git’s core code. The patch embeds `struct repository_format` directly into `struct repo_discovery`, eliminating the need to pass both structures separately to discovery functions.

The change is mechanical but foundational: every function that previously took both `struct repo_discovery` and `struct repository_format` now only needs the former. The patch updates `repo_discovery_release()` to clear the embedded format, initializes `REPO_DISCOVERY_INIT` with `REPOSITORY_FORMAT_INIT`, and propagates the embedded format through the discovery call chain (`repo_discover_explicit_gitdir()`, `repo_discover_implicit_gitdir()`, `repo_discover_bare_gitdir()`). The diff shows a straightforward transformation—replacing `repo_fmt->field` with `discovery->format.field`—but the real impact is architectural: it tightens the coupling between discovery results and their format, reducing scattered state.

This aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating repository setup into `repo_init()`. By embedding the format, the patch makes it harder to accidentally apply discovery results to the wrong repository, a risk that existed when the two structures were passed independently. The change is internal-only; no user-visible behavior is altered, and the patch touches only `setup.c`.

Justin Tobler’s earlier reviews of the series have focused on API clarity and edge cases, but this patch is unlikely to raise new concerns—it’s a clean step toward the stated architecture. The next patches will build on this by further propagating the embedded format through the setup process. If the series lands, this patch will be a key enabler for future refactoring, particularly around `the_repository` removal and ODB abstraction.
