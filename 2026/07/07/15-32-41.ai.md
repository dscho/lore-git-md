This is patch 9/11 in Patrick Steinhardt’s "Make ODB optimizations pluggable" series, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. It refactors the ODB optimization helpers in `builtin/gc.c` to operate directly on a `struct odb_source_files` instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. The patch mechanically updates the signatures of `too_many_loose_objects()`, `find_base_packs()`, `too_many_packs()`, and `estimate_repack_memory()` to accept a `files` parameter, and adjusts their call sites in `odb_optimize_required()` and `odb_optimize()` to pass the downcast source. All existing logic—including auto-condition checks, geometric split factors, cruft pack handling, and promisor object exclusions—is preserved. The patch is a preparatory step for moving the entire "files" source optimization logic into `odb/source-files.c` in the next patch. No new behavior or user-visible changes are introduced.
