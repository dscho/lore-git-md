This is patch 7/11 in Patrick Steinhardt's "Make ODB optimizations pluggable" series, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch merges the geometric repacking strategy—currently a separate code path in `maintenance_task_geometric_repack()`—into the new `odb_optimize()` function, unifying both the legacy incremental repack and the newer geometric repack under a single entry point.

The change introduces an `enum odb_optimize_strategy` with two variants (`ODB_OPTIMIZE_INCREMENTAL` and `ODB_OPTIMIZE_GEOMETRIC`) and adds a `strategy` field to `struct odb_optimize_options`. The `odb_optimize()` function now uses a `switch` on this field to dispatch to the appropriate repack logic. The geometric repack code is lifted verbatim from `maintenance_task_geometric_repack()` into the new `ODB_OPTIMIZE_GEOMETRIC` case, while the existing incremental logic is refactored into the `ODB_OPTIMIZE_INCREMENTAL` branch. The `maintenance_task_geometric_repack()` helper is reduced to a thin wrapper that constructs an `odb_optimize_options` instance and delegates to `odb_optimize()`.

Test updates are mechanical: every `--quiet` flag in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` is replaced with `-q` to match the new unified command-line style. The patch preserves all existing behavior—geometric split factors, cruft pack handling, multi-pack-index writes, and auto-condition checks—while preparing the ground for future backends to plug in their own optimization strategies. No new user-visible features or configuration knobs are introduced; the change is purely internal refactoring.
