This is a performance patch targeting the `wt-status` subsystem, part of a first-time contribution from Sahitya Chandra to address a quadratic-time inefficiency in collecting untracked and ignored paths.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive and constructive. He asks whether the inefficiency is a real-world bottleneck or a theoretical one, suggesting that adding a benchmark to the commit message would strengthen the case. He also points out that the patch’s manual sort-and-deduplicate sequence can be replaced with the existing `string_list_sort_u()` helper, which does the same thing in one call. The review is brief but identifies a concrete improvement and a potential follow-up (benchmarking), while endorsing the overall approach.

The patch itself remains small and focused: it replaces `string_list_insert()` with `string_list_append()` followed by a single sort and deduplicate per list, reducing the complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n). The change is confined to `wt-status.c` and preserves the existing behavior of producing sorted, duplicate-free output. Sahitya’s commit message already notes the trade-off between safety (explicit deduplication) and a more aggressive optimization that would rely on `fill_directory()`’s pre-sorted output, so the discussion is well-framed for any follow-up reviewers.

Key technical details for future reference: the patch modifies `wt_status_collect_untracked()`, affects the `s->untracked` and `s->ignored` string lists, and uses the `string_list_sort_u()` helper.
