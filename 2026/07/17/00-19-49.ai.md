This is a user follow-up in the `git last-modified` performance thread. Gusted, the Forgejo maintainer, confirms that Forgejo’s use case matches GitLab’s: retrieving the last-modified commit for every entry in a directory, not just a single file. He acknowledges that Forgejo’s current implementation—falling back to repeated `git log` calls for paths not found in the first batch—scales poorly on large repositories like GitLab’s, where it can take minutes instead of milliseconds. The email does not introduce new technical details or patches; it simply aligns Forgejo’s real-world needs with the optimization effort already underway by Toon Claes and Jeff King. No review or testing is reported.
