This is the third patch in Toon Claes’s four-patch series optimizing the experimental `git last-modified` command. It wires the Bloom-filter helper (`revs_maybe_changed_in_bloom`) exposed in patch 2 into the command’s main revision walk. The change is tiny—three lines in `builtin/last-modified.c`—but it eliminates the need to diff trees for commits whose Bloom filters definitively say “no changes” to the entire pathspec. Benchmarks in the cover letter show this cuts runtime from 52 ms to 14 ms on the ziglang/zig repository, matching the speed of `git log`. No new symbols or on-disk formats are introduced; the patch simply reuses the existing Bloom-filter machinery more efficiently.
