This thread is about adding a built-in Swift language driver to Git’s userdiff system, allowing `git diff` and `git log -p` to produce meaningful hunk headers and word-level diffs for Swift files without manual configuration. The patch, submitted by Shlok Kulshreshtha, is self-contained, touching only `userdiff.c`, `Documentation/gitattributes.adoc`, and adding 13 test cases in `t/t4018/`.

Junio C Hamano has queued the patch in the `seen` branch for review, but it won’t progress to `next` or be merged until active Swift users on the mailing list validate the regex patterns against real-world codebases. The patch itself is complete and ready for review, with no technical objections raised so far. The next step is for Swift developers to test the driver and confirm its accuracy. No changes from the author are needed unless reviewers request them.
