The Swift userdiff driver patch, originally submitted by Shlok Kulshreshtha, is now effectively ready for the next stage of integration. The patch adds built-in support for Swift in Git’s diff machinery, allowing `git diff` and `git log -p` to produce meaningful hunk headers and word-level diffs for `.swift` files without manual configuration.

The v2 revision of the patch has addressed all substantive feedback from Johannes Sixt’s review of the initial version, including fixes for test ambiguities and regex optimizations. The author has also provided thorough empirical validation, demonstrating 99.9% accuracy in hunk header identification across 20,454 hunks from seven real-world Swift projects. Junio C Hamano has queued the patch in `seen` and indicated it will be promoted to `next` unless further comments arise in the coming days. The only remaining step is a brief waiting period to allow Swift users on the mailing list to validate the regex patterns in practice, though no technical concerns have been raised at this stage.

If no objections surface, this feature is poised to graduate to `next` shortly, bringing native Swift support to Git’s diff system. The patch touches `userdiff.c`, `Documentation/gitattributes.adoc`, and the test suite, with comprehensive coverage of Swift’s 26 declaration forms and language-specific literals and operators.
