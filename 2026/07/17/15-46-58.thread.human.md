This thread is about a four-patch series from Toon Claes that speeds up the experimental `git last-modified` command by reusing Git’s Bloom filters. The goal is to make it as fast as `git log` when finding the last commit that modified files matching a pathspec (including wildcards like `*.c`). On the ziglang/zig repository, the series cuts runtime from 52 ms to 14 ms, a 3.7× improvement.

The series is under review in its first version. The first three patches refactor and wire up the Bloom-filter logic, while the fourth (just summarized) ensures wildcard pathspecs keep working by restoring Bloom filter settings after `prepare_revision_walk()` clears them. Toon notes a minor process oversight—missing trailers for Jeff King’s contributions—and plans to add them in a reroll. Jeff King is acknowledged as having helped shape the series, though the exact details aren’t yet clear from the thread.

The changes are confined to `revision.c`, `revision.h`, and `builtin/last-modified.c`, with no on-disk format changes. The series is self-contained, with benchmarks included in the cover letter but no new test files. The only loose end is the missing trailers, which Toon will address. So far, no substantive objections or open questions have been raised.
