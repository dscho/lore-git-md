This is a surface-level review from Junio C Hamano on the third patch in Tian Yuchen’s v7 series, which migrates `trust_executable_bit` into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Second, he questions whether the function should defensively check for a NULL `repo` pointer (as some other repository helpers do) or instead allow a segfault to expose bugs. He leans toward the latter, arguing that defensive checks elsewhere in the codebase may be papering over real issues.

Neither comment challenges the patch’s correctness or design; both are cosmetic or philosophical. The series has already addressed all prior substantive feedback, and this review does not reopen any settled questions. The author can incorporate these suggestions in a trivial reroll or leave them for Junio to tweak during merge, as is common for such minor stylistic points. The patch remains on track for inclusion.
