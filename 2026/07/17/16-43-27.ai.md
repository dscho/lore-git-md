This is a **design clarification** in the ongoing `git bisect --auto-reset` feature discussion. Junio C Hamano briefly revisits the parameterized `--auto-reset=<where>` design he previously proposed and that Harald Nordgren has accepted. He notes that the flexibility of choosing between `original` (reset to the pre-bisect commit) and `found` (leave the working tree on the first bad commit) means users cannot always rely on `--auto-reset` alone—if their desired reset target depends on the bisect outcome, they may need to forgo the option and manually reset afterward. This observation is not a rejection or a request for change; it simply acknowledges a practical limitation of the feature’s design. The thread remains on track for a v2 patch series implementing the parameterized option.
