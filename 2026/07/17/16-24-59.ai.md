He notes that `tr2_tbuf.c` bypasses `gettext.h`, so its `snprintf()` calls avoid this redirection, explaining why Derrick’s patch works. However, Taylor questions whether the fix should address the broader `xsnprintf()`/`libintl` issue instead of treating trace2 reentrancy in isolation.

The proposed alternative (untested) adds a preprocessor guard to `wrapper.c` to prevent `xsnprintf()` from using `libintl_vsnprintf()`, avoiding the allocation entirely. Taylor acknowledges uncertainty about why the allocation fails but suggests this approach might be cleaner. The review highlights a platform-specific edge case (Git for Windows) and raises a design question: should the fix target the symptom (trace2 recursion) or the underlying cause (libintl’s behavior)?
