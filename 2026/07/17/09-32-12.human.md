This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch refactoring series that continues the ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The patch lifts the object-existence check out of the loose-object backend and into the generic ODB layer, eliminating a layering violation and removing the need for every backend to re-implement the same check. The change is small (9 insertions, 6 deletions) and mechanical: the check now happens in `odb_write_object_ext()` before any backend is invoked, so no callers need adjustment. The loose backend’s `odb_source_loose_write_object()` becomes simpler and no longer needs to know about other sources. The patch is clearly motivated and fits cleanly into the series’ goal of making the loose backend more self-contained.
