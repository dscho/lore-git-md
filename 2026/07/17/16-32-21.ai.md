This is a refactoring patch targeting the `show-branch` command. Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy submits v8 of the patch, which converts `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab (`commit_rev_flags`) for per-commit state instead of the shared `object.flags` field. The patch removes the previous 27-branch limit entirely by dynamically growing arrays and computing the slab stride at runtime as `(ref_name_cnt + 63) / 64`. The `UNINTERESTING` bit remains in `object.flags` for revision-walking compatibility.

The patch addresses Junio C Hamano’s feedback from v7 by removing the "cannot show more than 64 branches" sentence from the documentation and clarifying the commit message to accurately reflect the change. The TODO comment about migrating `UNINTERESTING` usage remains, as the local `#define UNINTERESTING` was removed and `revision.h` is now included, fulfilling the comment’s request. The patch introduces helper functions (`get_rev_flags_ptr`, `or_rev_flag_bit`, `test_rev_flag_bit`, `has_only_rev_flag_bit`, `has_subset_rev_flags`) to encapsulate slab access and preserve the original semantics of `show_independent` and `join_revs`. Three new tests in `t/t3202-show-branch.sh` verify correct operation with 30+ branches, including `--independent` and `--merge-base` modes.

The patch is part of the ongoing effort to eliminate misuse of `object.flags` and directly reuses infrastructure from the 2025 variable-length bitset slab series. It touches `builtin/show-branch.c`, `Documentation/git-show-branch.adoc`, and `t/t3202-show-branch.sh`. The refactoring is thorough, with clear commit messages and test coverage, and appears technically sound after addressing Junio’s feedback about internal inconsistencies. The patch is ready for further review, with no remaining open questions or loose ends from prior discussion.
