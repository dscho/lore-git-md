This is a brief but substantive follow-up in the `wt-status` performance thread. Peff still endorses the patch, calling it “worth doing” because it makes the code’s behavior more obvious and easier to analyze. He even speculates that the new approach could be O(n) if the sort implementation optimizes for pre-sorted inputs. The tone is matter-of-fact and supportive, reinforcing Patrick’s earlier feedback without adding new technical objections. No action is demanded from the author; this is more of a nod to the patch’s clarity than a call for revision.
