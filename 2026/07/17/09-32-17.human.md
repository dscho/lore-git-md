This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring the loose object backend as part of the ODB abstraction effort. It completes the relocation of loose-object writing logic from `object-file.c` into `odb/source-loose.c`, making the loose backend fully self-contained.

The patch moves roughly 360 lines of code—including the core functions `write_loose_object()`, `start_loose_object_common()`, `write_loose_object_common()`, and `end_loose_object_common()`—from `object-file.c` to `odb/source-loose.c`. To support this, it exposes two transaction-related helpers (`odb_transaction_files_prepare()` and `odb_transaction_files_fsync()`) that were previously static in `object-file.c`. The only other change is replacing a reference to `the_repository` with the source’s repository in `start_loose_object_common()`, a small step toward the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

This is a pure refactoring with no behavior changes. The commit message acknowledges that the transaction interface remains messy and will require further work, but the patch itself is straightforward and well-motivated. The series as a whole looks like a careful, incremental step in Patrick’s long-running ODB abstraction project, and this final patch ties off the loose ends cleanly. Worth following if you care about the ODB layer, but nothing controversial here.
