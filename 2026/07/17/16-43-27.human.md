This is the maintainer’s follow-up in the `git bisect --auto-reset` thread. Junio C Hamano acknowledges Harald Nordgren’s acceptance of the parameterized `--auto-reset=<where>` design but adds a small caveat: the feature may not always be usable if the desired reset target depends on the bisect outcome. He clarifies that in such cases, users would simply omit the `--auto-reset` option and manually reset afterward. The comment is brief and does not introduce new technical requirements or objections; it merely refines the intended use case. The thread remains on track for a v2 patch series implementing the parameterized option.
