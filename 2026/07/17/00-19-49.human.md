This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the performance of `git last-modified`. Gusted, the original reporter, confirms that Forgejo (a GitLab/Gitea fork) shares GitLab’s use case: determining the last-modified commit for every entry in a directory, not just a single file. He acknowledges that Forgejo’s current implementation—calling `git log` repeatedly for each remaining path—scales poorly, sometimes taking minutes on large repositories like GitLab’s. The email does not introduce new technical details or propose changes; it simply aligns Forgejo’s needs with GitLab’s batching approach and expresses appreciation for the ongoing optimization work by Toon Claes and Jeff King. The tone is constructive, and the message reinforces the practical value of the performance improvements already in progress.
