This is the fifth patch in K Jayatheerth’s seven-part GSoC series adding path-related keys to `` `git repo info` ``. It introduces ``path.index.absolute`` and ``path.index.relative``, exposing the location of the index file in both absolute and relative-to-cwd formats. The implementation respects the ``GIT_INDEX_FILE`` environment override, mirrors the behavior of ``git rev-parse --git-path index``, and includes tests for both the default case and the environment-override scenario. The patch is mechanically clean, well-documented, and ready for technical review; no substantive feedback has been posted yet.
