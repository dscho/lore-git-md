This is the third patch in K Jayatheerth’s seven-part GSoC series adding path-related keys to `` `git repo info` ``. It introduces ``path.objects.absolute`` and ``path.objects.relative``, exposing the object database location in both absolute and relative forms while respecting the ``GIT_OBJECT_DIRECTORY`` environment override. The implementation is straightforward: two new getter functions call ``repo_get_object_directory()`` and pass the result through ``format_path()`` with the appropriate formatting flag. Documentation and tests are included, covering both the standard case and the environment-override scenario. The patch is mechanically clean and follows the pattern established in the first two patches. No substantive review feedback has appeared yet, so the design—particularly the key naming and suffix semantics—remains open for discussion.
