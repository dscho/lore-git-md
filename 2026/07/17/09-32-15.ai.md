This is patch 7/9 in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction refactoring series. It prepares `force_object_loose()` to be moved out of `object-file.c` by switching it from the internal `write_loose_object()` helper to the generic `odb_source_write_object()` interface. The patch is small (19 lines, 7 insertions, 12 deletions) and mechanical: it replaces direct loose-object writing with a call to the ODB layer, passing the precomputed compatibility object ID (if needed) and mtime. The change is possible now that the object-existence check has been lifted out of the loose backend (done in patch 4/9) and mtime support has been plumbed through all backends (patch 5/9). No behavior change is expected; this is pure refactoring to enable the next patch in the series. The commit message clearly states the motivation and the preparatory work that made this step possible.
