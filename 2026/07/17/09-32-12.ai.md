This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch ODB abstraction refactoring series. It lifts the object-existence check out of the loose backend (`odb/source-loose.c`) and into the generic ODB layer (`odb.c`). The change removes a layering violation (the loose backend no longer needs to know about objects in other backends), eliminates redundant checks across backends, and makes it possible to write an object into a specific source even if the same object exists in another source. The patch is small (9 insertions, 6 deletions) and mechanical: the check is simply moved from `odb_source_loose_write_object()` to `odb_write_object_ext()`, with no caller adjustments required. No behavior changes are introduced.
