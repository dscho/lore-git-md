This is the seventh iteration of a refactoring series that migrates the `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags into `struct repo_config_values`, completing the `the_repository` removal effort for these index-related settings. The series is now uncontroversial and appears ready for merging.

The v7 iteration addresses Junio C Hamano’s design-level concern from v6 by keeping `ce_mode_from_stat()` as a `static inline` function in `read-cache.h` and teaching it to accept a `struct repository *` parameter. This preserves the potential performance benefits of inlining while enabling repository-scoped configuration access. The commit message for patch 4/4 has also been reworded to emphasize the platform-specific override mechanism, as requested.

The series remains a well-scoped, mechanical migration with no behavioral changes. It moves both flags into repository-scoped storage, establishes new getter functions (`repo_trust_executable_bit()` and `repo_has_symlinks()`), and updates all call sites to use repository-scoped access. The mingw compatibility layer now uses a compile-time macro (`platform_has_symlinks()`) and a platform-specific function (`mingw_platform_has_symlinks()`), eliminating mutable global state while preserving native symlink detection. This design aligns with Git’s existing platform-compatibility conventions and resolves the last substantive review concern from v5.

Files touched include `read-cache.c`, `environment.c/h`, `apply.c`, `compat/mingw.c`, and related headers. The series removes redundant `extern` declarations, migrates both flags into `repo_config_values` with proper initialization, and ensures repository pointer validation in the new getters. The mingw adjustments are now cleaner and may serve as a template for future platform-specific overrides.

The series builds on prior work (including an unsuccessful attempt to use `struct repo_settings`) and demonstrates incremental progress in eliminating global state. The inclusion of `has_symlinks`—previously deferred—reflects growing confidence in the approach, though the author notes that `repo_config_values()` still does not support non-`the_repository` instances due to deep integration of these flags. This limitation is acknowledged as a future challenge, but the series lays groundwork for eventual elimination of the global.

This iteration has addressed all prior feedback, including Junio’s design-level concerns, and is likely to proceed to `next` once final review concludes. The migration pattern established here could inform similar configuration refactoring efforts, and the mingw adjustments may attract attention from platform maintainers like Johannes Schindelin. The series is uncontroversial in its current form.
