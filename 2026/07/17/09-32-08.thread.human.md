This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring Git’s loose object backend to fit into the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to move loose object logic out of `object-file.c` and into a self-contained `odb/source-loose.c`, making the codebase more modular and paving the way for alternative object storage backends. The series is still in its first iteration, with no reviews posted yet.

The fourth patch, just posted, addresses a layering violation by lifting the object-existence check out of the loose backend and into the generic ODB layer. This small, mechanical change removes redundant checks across backends and allows writing an object to a specific source even if it already exists elsewhere. No behavior changes are introduced, and the patch requires no adjustments from callers. The series as a whole touches a dozen files, primarily moving code between `object-file.c` and `odb/source-loose.c`, and introduces new symbols like `odb_source_write_object()` and `odb_write_object_ext()` to standardize the write interface across backends.

The refactoring depends on two recent topics (`jt/receive-pack-use-odb-transaction` and `jk/git-hash-cleanups`) and requires an evil merge to resolve a conflict in the packed backend’s transaction handling. So far, only Patrick has spoken, and no open questions or objections have surfaced—though reviewers may yet weigh in on the transaction interface changes or the necessity of the new `compat_oid` parameter in the generic write function.
