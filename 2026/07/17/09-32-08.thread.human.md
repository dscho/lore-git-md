This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring Git’s loose object backend to make it self-contained as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to move all loose-object writing logic out of `object-file.c` and into `odb/source-loose.c`, using the generic `odb_source_write_object()` interface. The series has been posted in its first version, with a build-breaking issue (resolved by Junio) now fixed, allowing substantive review to proceed.

Key participants include Patrick as the author, SZEDER Gábor who caught the build issue, and Junio who applied the fix. Toon Claes has now weighed in with a focused review of the fourth patch, confirming that the lifted object-existence check behaves correctly for both loose and packed backends. His feedback is positive and doesn’t raise new concerns, suggesting this part of the refactoring is on solid ground. The series remains under review, with no major objections or open questions beyond what was already noted—primarily around transaction interfaces, the new `compat_oid` parameter, and potential performance impacts of the mtime plumbing. The next steps will likely involve broader review feedback before a v2 is posted.
