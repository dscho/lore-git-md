This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring Git’s loose object backend to make it fully self-contained as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to move all loose-object writing logic from `object-file.c` into `odb/source-loose.c`, consolidating the loose backend’s internals and eliminating direct calls to `write_loose_object()` in favor of the generic `odb_source_write_object()` interface. The series also centralizes object hash computation, compatibility ID handling, object-existence checks, and mtime support in the generic ODB layer, reducing duplication across backends.

The initial version of the series was blocked by a build-breaking issue: a missing evil merge resolution in the packed backend’s transaction function, which caused a type mismatch error. Junio C Hamano confirmed the oversight and has now fixed it by rebuilding the topic with the correct merge resolution, allowing the series to compile. This was purely a mechanical correction, not a technical disagreement, and reviewers can now proceed with substantive review. The series itself is complete, with all nine patches posted, and achieves its goal of making the loose backend self-contained—though one function, `odb_source_loose_write_stream`, remains in `object-file.c` with a TODO for future relocation.

Key participants include Patrick Steinhardt (the author), SZEDER Gábor (who identified the build-breaking issue), and Junio C Hamano (who confirmed and fixed the problem). The series depends on `jt/receive-pack-use-odb-transaction` and `jk/git-hash-cleanups` and is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick. No behavior or on-disk format changes are introduced; this is a pure refactoring. Open questions may focus on the transaction interface changes, the impact on other ODB backends, or the performance implications of the new mtime parameter plumbing.
