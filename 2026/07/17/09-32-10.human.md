This is a test infrastructure patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch ODB abstraction series. Patch 2/9 prepares the in-memory unit-test backend for the upcoming refactoring of the loose-object write path by introducing a small wrapper function (`cl_assert_write_object`) that hides the direct call to `odb_source_write_object`. The change is purely mechanical: 23 lines of new wrapper code replace 25 lines of duplicated calls, making future adjustments easier without altering any test behavior. The patch is clean, well-contained, and clearly motivated by the needs of the larger series.
