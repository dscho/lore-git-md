This is a surface-level review from Junio C Hamano on the third patch of Tian Yuchen’s v7 series, which migrates `trust_executable_bit` into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. He expresses a preference for the latter, arguing that defensive checks may mask issues and that the project should consistently choose one approach rather than mixing patterns.

The feedback is purely stylistic and does not challenge the patch’s correctness or design. It is likely to be addressed in a future iteration or as a follow-up, but it does not block the series’ progress. The broader series (v7) has already resolved all prior substantive feedback, and this review does not introduce new technical concerns.
