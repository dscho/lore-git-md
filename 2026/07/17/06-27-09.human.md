This is a performance patch targeting the `wt-status` subsystem, part of a first-time contribution from Sahitya Chandra. The patch addresses a quadratic-time inefficiency in how untracked and ignored paths are collected by replacing repeated `string_list_insert()` calls with a single append pass followed by sorting and deduplication.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive and constructive. He asks whether the inefficiency is theoretical or observed in practice, suggesting the addition of a benchmark to the commit message. He also points out that `string_list_sort_u()` could replace the explicit sort-and-deduplicate sequence, simplifying the code slightly. The review is positive overall, calling the change "sensible," but the benchmark question remains open.

The patch itself is small and focused, touching only `wt-status.c` and preserving the existing behavior of producing sorted, duplicate-free lists. The commit message already acknowledges an alternative approach—relying on `fill_directory()`’s pre-sorted output—but opts for the safer, explicit deduplication. Patrick’s feedback does not challenge this trade-off, so the core design appears uncontroversial.
