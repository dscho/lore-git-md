This is the final patch in Toon Claes’s four-part performance series for the experimental `git last-modified` command. It targets wildcard pathspecs (e.g., `*.c`) that the Bloom-filter machinery cannot directly convert into a single key. The patch restores the `bloom_filter_settings` field after `prepare_revision_walk()` clears it, allowing the per-path Bloom-filter lookups (introduced in patch 3) to continue working. The change is confined to `builtin/last-modified.c` and adds eight lines of straightforward plumbing. No new symbols are exposed, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. Benchmarks in the cover letter already show the series as a whole cuts runtime from 52 ms to 14 ms on the ziglang/zig repository; this patch ensures those gains extend to wildcard pathspecs.
