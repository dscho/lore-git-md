This is the third patch in the nine-patch `diff.<driver>.process` RFC series (v5). It adds the plumbing for the new `diff.<driver>.process` configuration option, allowing users to specify a long-running subprocess that will generate diff hunks for files matching the driver's pattern. The patch is minimal and mechanical: it extends the `userdiff_driver` struct with `process` and `process_owned` fields (mirroring the existing `command`/`command_owned` pattern) and teaches `userdiff_config()` to populate them from Git's config system. No behavior changes yet—this is purely infrastructure for later patches that will actually invoke the subprocess and integrate its output into Git's diff pipeline.

The patch is well-structured and follows Git's established patterns for config-driven driver fields. It does not introduce any new dependencies or edge cases, and the commit message is clear about its limited scope. This is a foundational piece of the series, enabling the more complex xdiff integration and protocol handling that follow in patches 4–9. The series as a whole is now technically complete, with all substantive review feedback addressed in earlier rounds, so this patch is unlikely to attract controversy on its own.
