This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series removing the last uses of `the_repository` from the refs subsystem. It targets the remaining callsites in built-in commands (`branch.c`, `builtin/branch.c`, `builtin/check-ref-format.c`, `builtin/checkout.c`, `builtin/merge.c`, `builtin/worktree.c`) and the core refs library (`refs.c`, `refs.h`).

The patch is purely mechanical: it updates `copy_branchname()` and `check_branch_ref()` to take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, adjusts all callers to pass `the_repository` (or the appropriate repository pointer), and removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `refs.c`. A small internal helper, `has_worktrees()`, is also updated to take a repository parameter, fixing a TODO comment that noted the previous inconsistency. The changes are confined to function signatures and call sites; no logic is altered.

This is the capstone of the series, completing the conversion of the refs and worktree subsystems to explicit repository handling. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. With this applied, the series is ready to graduate to `master`.
