This is the **final patch (9/9)** in Phillip Wood’s 9-patch series fixing how the sequencer handles dropped commits during rebase. It introduces the core behavioral change: **commits dropped during rebase (e.g., because their changes are already upstream) are no longer recorded as "rewritten"**, preventing incorrect note copying and misleading post-rewrite hooks.

The patch adds `PICK_RESULT_DROPPED` to the `enum pick_result` (introduced in patch 8/9) and modifies `pick_one_commit()` to skip calling `record_in_rewritten()` for dropped commits. It also ensures that if the dropped commit is the final one in a fixup chain, the list of rewritten commits is flushed. The initialization of `drop_commit` is moved to avoid uninitialized use when rewording fast-forwarded commits.

A **known limitation** is documented: the "edit" command still erroneously records dropped commits as rewritten when `rebase --continue` is run. This is flagged for a separate fix.

Test coverage is expanded in `t3400-rebase.sh` (verifying notes are not copied from dropped commits) and `t5407-post-rewrite-hook.sh` (ensuring post-rewrite hooks receive accurate data for fixups and commits that become empty). The diff is minimal (24 lines added, 5 removed in `sequencer.c`) and focused.

This patch completes the series, which is **approved by Junio C Hamano** and ready for `next` after typofixes are squashed. The fix is **well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and addresses a real bug** without introducing broader churn. The known limitation is clearly documented, and the series as a whole is a **substantive, tested-level contribution** to the rebase machinery.
