Both look like solid, well-motivated changes that are close to graduation.

Overall, this is a typical "What’s cooking" report: a mix of small fixes, ongoing refactoring, and a few user-visible features. The ODB pluggability work and the rebase notes fix are the most significant changes currently in flight, while the stalled topics and new features provide clear pointers to where future discussion is likely to focus.
