This thread is about a patch (v2) by Tian Yuchen that moves the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository` as part of Git’s ongoing libification and `the_repository` removal efforts. The goal is to make the flag repository-specific while preserving existing behavior (default remains enabled). The patch touches 15 files, including `odb.c`, `fetch-pack.c`, and `revision.c`, and updates partial clone documentation to reflect the change.

Feedback on v1 identified a critical correctness issue in `setup.c`, which the author fixed in v2. However, Junio C Hamano recently spotted a new edge case: in repository-less invocations (e.g., `git index-pack` outside a repository), the patch could inadvertently enable lazy fetches where they were previously suppressed, altering behavior. The author is now seeking input to resolve this before posting v3. Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier concerns about multi-repository behavior in `index-pack.c` and `revision.c` were addressed by retaining `the_repository` in those subsystems, but the lack of explicit testing in repository-less or multi-repository contexts remains a risk.

The patch is otherwise ready for pickup, with all prior feedback addressed. The next step is a v3 that ensures lazy fetches remain suppressed in repository-less invocations, aligning with existing behavior. The broader context is the project-wide push to eliminate global state, making this a logical and incremental step forward.
