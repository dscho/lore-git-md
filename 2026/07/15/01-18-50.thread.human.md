This patch series, now at v3, continues the effort to eliminate global state in Git by moving the `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository`. Originally introduced by Tian Yuchen, the patch makes this flag per-repository, controlling whether missing objects trigger lazy fetches from promisor remotes. The default behavior remains unchanged (enabled), but the change is part of the broader libification and `the_repository` removal initiatives.

The v3 patch addresses critical feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, resolving a null pointer dereference risk in `builtin/index-pack.c` and clarifying the handling of repository-less invocations. The author has adopted Junio’s suggestion to require `index-pack` to always run inside a repository, simplifying the logic and eliminating the need for conditional checks. While this restricts `index-pack`’s flexibility, it resolves the immediate crash risk and aligns with the patch’s goals. The broader architectural question—whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` at all—remains unresolved, but the v3 patch is a pragmatic step forward.

Key participants include Junio, who surfaced the design concerns and null pointer risks, and Patrick Steinhardt, who identified the inconsistency in `index-pack.c`. The author has addressed all feedback, though the patch still lacks explicit test coverage for per-repository behavior or repository-less invocations. The series is now technically sound but may require further discussion on the long-term design direction. For now, it appears ready for pickup, pending any additional review comments.
