This thread is about a patch series (v3) by Tian Yuchen that moves the global `fetch_if_missing` flag into `struct repository` as part of Git’s ongoing libification and `the_repository` removal efforts. The goal is to make the flag per-repository, preserving existing behavior while eliminating global state. The patch touches 15 files, including `odb.c`, `repository.c`, `fetch-pack.c`, `fsck.c`, `index-pack.c`, and `revision.c`, and introduces `struct repository->fetch_if_missing` to replace the global variable.

The patch is under active review, with v3 addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, particularly a null pointer dereference risk in `index-pack.c` and behavior changes in repository-less contexts. However, two key issues remain unresolved: a conflict with `cf1687a41c` (a recent topic in `seen` that splits `setup_git_env_internal()`) and an inconsistency in `setup.c` where `the_repository` is still used despite the function receiving a `repo` parameter. Junio has requested a rebase and clarification on the latter, while the author has proposed waiting for `cf1687a41c` to settle before rerolling—a passive approach that doesn’t address the architectural concern. The broader question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` at all is also still open, though the immediate focus is on correctness and integration.

The patch is not yet ready for pickup. The next iteration will need to resolve the conflict, fix the `setup.c` inconsistency (likely by using the passed-in `repo`), and ideally add test coverage for per-repository behavior and repository-less invocations. Patrick Steinhardt has no further concerns, but Junio’s feedback suggests the patch still needs work before it can be queued.
