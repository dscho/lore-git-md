This thread is about a two-patch series (v5) by Tian Yuchen that moves the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository` as part of Git’s ongoing libification and `the_repository` removal efforts. The goal is to make the flag controlling lazy fetches from promisor remotes repository-specific, eliminating direct uses of the global `the_repository` variable.

The series is now technically complete, with v5 addressing all remaining feedback from Junio’s v4 review. The changes resolve the last five direct `the_repository` references in `builtin/fetch-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `builtin/rev-list.c`, `common-init.c`, and `git.c`, ensuring the `repo` parameter is passed through the call graph. The preparatory patch (1/2) propagates the `repo` parameter in `pack-objects.c`, fixing the last mechanical blocker. Behavior is preserved: the default value remains `1` (enabled), and existing logic for disabling lazy fetches now operates on the repository-specific flag. Special handling for repository-less invocations (e.g., in `index-pack.c`) and deferred initialization in `fsck.c` and `index-pack.c` avoids null pointer dereferences.

While the series is ready for maintainer review, the broader architectural question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` remains unresolved. Junio’s earlier review outlined three possible approaches, and the author has adopted the pragmatic solution of restricting `index-pack` to repository-bound operation. However, alternatives—such as a fallback to `the_repository` or a new global "basic settings" structure—are still on the table. The series is coordinated with other topics in `seen` and `next` to avoid conflicts, but no new test coverage has been added, leaving multi-repository behavior unverified. Key participants include Tian Yuchen (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), and Patrick Steinhardt, all of whom have reviewed the changes favorably for the immediate issues.
