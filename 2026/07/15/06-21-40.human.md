This is the fourth and final patch in Tian Yuchen’s v5 series refactoring `has_symlinks` and `trust_executable_bit` configuration as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch moves the global `has_symlinks` flag into repository-scoped storage (`struct repo_config_values`) and introduces a new getter function, `repo_has_symlinks()`, to access it. All call sites are updated to use the new getter, with a fallback to `the_repository` where necessary.

Christian Couder’s review is surface-level, noting only a minor redundancy in the commit message ("fallback" appears twice) and otherwise stating the series looks good. The patch itself is a mechanical migration with no behavioral changes, but it introduces a pragmatic compromise for mingw compatibility: a global fallback variable (`default_has_symlinks`) to handle early platform-specific overrides before repository initialization. This approach reflects the tension between the ideal of full repository-scoped configuration and the practical need to support platform-specific adjustments early in the startup sequence.

The series as a whole is well-structured, with the first two patches serving as preparatory cleanups and the latter two implementing the core migrations. The v4 iteration (which migrated only `trust_executable_bit`) was merged to `master` without objections, and this v5 iteration reintroduces the `has_symlinks` migration with the same pattern. The mingw compatibility question may attract further discussion, but the patch itself is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration.
