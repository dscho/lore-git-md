This is a refactoring patch in the `cat-file` feature series, now at its eighteenth iteration. The patch moves loop counter declarations inside their `for()` statements and changes their type from `int` to `size_t` where appropriate, improving code hygiene and eliminating signed/unsigned comparison warnings.

The changes are mechanical and localized: in `builtin/cat-file.c`, the loop counters in `dispatch_calls()`, `free_cmds()`, and `batch_objects_command()` are moved inside their loops and retyped to `size_t` to match the bounds they iterate over. In `fetch-pack.c`, the server-options loop counter is similarly moved and retyped. The patch touches no logic beyond these declaration changes, and the commit message clearly states the motivation (readability and type safety).

This is the second patch in the ten-patch v18 series. The patch was originally authored by Eric Ju and carries a Helped-by trailer for Christian Couder; Pablo Sabater is the series integrator. The changes are uncontroversial and align with Git’s coding guidelines, which prefer tight variable scoping and type consistency. No substantive review comments are expected or needed—this is purely a cleanup that prepares the codebase for the feature work that follows.
