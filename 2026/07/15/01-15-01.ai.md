This is a review comment on the fifth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 11-patch series fixing unchecked return values from Coverity-reported issues. Junio C Hamano identifies a subtle correctness issue in the error-handling logic for `last_modified_run()` in `builtin/last-modified.c`. The patch currently skips unparsable commits in the `--not` queue with a `continue`, but this bypasses the cleanup of the `active_paths` slab associated with the commit being processed (`c`). Junio suggests replacing the `continue` with a `goto cleanup` to ensure proper resource cleanup even when a commit in the `--not` queue fails to parse. The concern is that silently skipping cleanup could lead to memory leaks or inconsistent state in the slab allocator, though the immediate impact is likely minor. This is a substantive review: it engages with the code’s control flow and resource management, not just style or commit message wording. No test results or alternative implementations are proposed; the feedback is narrowly focused on the error-handling path.
