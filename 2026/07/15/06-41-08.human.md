This is a refactoring patch in the `remote-curl` transport code, part of René Scharfe’s ongoing effort to simplify redundant patterns in Git’s internals. The patch removes an unnecessary `nr_spec` parameter from the HTTP push handlers (`push_dav` and `push_git`), relying instead on the NULL-termination of `strvec` arrays to determine the end of the push specification list. The change is confined to `remote-curl.c` and makes no observable behavior difference—it’s purely about code hygiene.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review suggests a minor interface improvement: passing a `const struct strvec *` directly instead of a raw `const char **` to make the guarantees clearer. He also notes the absence of a hypothetical `strvec_pushvec()` helper that could optimize repeated vector appends, though this isn’t a blocker. His feedback is otherwise positive, calling the patch "good" with only surface-level nits.

The discussion is lightweight and focused on implementation details rather than design.
