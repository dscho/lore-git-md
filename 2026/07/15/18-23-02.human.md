This is Junio C Hamano’s review of the final patch in Tian Yuchen’s four-part series that migrates `has_symlinks` into repository-scoped configuration as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series as a whole is a well-executed, mechanical refactoring with no behavioral changes, and the previous version (v4) was already merged to `master`.

Junio’s feedback here is substantive but narrowly targeted: he questions the use of a new global variable (`default_has_symlinks`) as a fallback for early platform-specific overrides on MinGW. Instead, he suggests adopting the pattern already used for `is_dir_sep()` and similar platform-specific helpers—defining a macro in a generic header that platforms can override, and implementing the logic in a platform-specific function. This approach avoids exposing a mutable global variable that could be misused elsewhere in the codebase.

The review does not challenge the core migration or the repository-scoped getter pattern; it focuses solely on the MinGW compatibility layer. Junio’s suggestion is a cleaner architectural choice that aligns with Git’s existing conventions for platform-specific behavior. The author had already flagged the global fallback as a potential compromise, so this feedback is likely to be incorporated in a future iteration. No other technical concerns are raised, and the series remains under review pending this adjustment.
