This is a refactoring patch in Phillip Wood’s 9-patch series fixing how the sequencer handles dropped commits during rebase. It is patch 4/9 in the v3 iteration, which Junio has already approved for `next` after typofixes are squashed.

The function `error_with_patch()` either returns its input `res` on success or `-1` on failure, so OR-ing its return value with `res` is a no-op. The change is purely mechanical: three lines become two, with no behavior change. The commit message explains the rationale clearly, and the diff is minimal and focused.

The patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated. It touches only `sequencer.c` and has no test coverage of its own, relying on the broader series’ expanded test suite to verify the sequencer’s behavior. No further changes are expected.
