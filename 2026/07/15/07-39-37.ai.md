This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem and worktree code. It targets `worktree.c`, converting seven call sites that still use the global `the_repository` to instead use the `wt->repo` pointer already available in the `struct worktree`. The change is purely mechanical: every instance of `the_repository` is replaced with `wt->repo`, and the patch touches only `worktree.c` (14 lines, 7 insertions, 7 deletions). No new behavior is introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.

The patch is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and follows the established pattern of making repository handling explicit. It is uncontroversial and ready for integration; the series as a whole is already in `seen` and `jch` and expected to graduate to `master` in the next cycle. The only substantive feedback from Junio (a behavioral regression in the packed-refs timeout initialization) was addressed in v2, and the integration conflicts with `seen` have been resolved. This patch is a straightforward, low-risk refactoring that fits cleanly into the larger effort.
