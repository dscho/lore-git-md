This is the final patch in René Scharfe’s 5-patch series converting lockfile and tempfile APIs to repository-aware variants as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch itself is a mechanical conversion of 17 files to use the new `repo_hold_lock_file_for_update*` functions, ensuring `core.sharedRepository` is read from the correct repository rather than the global `the_repository`.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises two points. First, he asks for clarification in the commit message about why only a subset of callsites were converted—specifically, why some lockfile users remain on the old global functions. This is a fair request; the current message does not explain the selection criteria, leaving readers to guess whether the conversion is incomplete by design or by omission. Second, he proposes an additional commit to guard the old global-dependent functions with `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE`, preventing accidental use in subsystems that have already eliminated reliance on `the_repository`. This is a defensive measure to catch future regressions, though it would require a small follow-up patch rather than a change to this series.

The series remains well-structured and uncontroversial, but Patrick’s feedback highlights a minor gap in documentation and a potential safety improvement. Neither point blocks the patch, but addressing them would make the migration more robust and transparent. The patches are still queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, and no other substantive reviews have surfaced.
