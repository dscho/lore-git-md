The diff is confined to `sequencer.c` and introduces the `enum pick_result` with three members: `PICK_RESULT_ERROR` (-1), `PICK_RESULT_OK` (0), and `PICK_RESULT_CONFLICTS` (1). The enum replaces the integer return values in `do_pick_commit()` and updates its callers (`pick_one_commit()` and `single_pick()`) to handle the new type. The patch does not alter behavior; it merely makes the existing semantics more explicit.
