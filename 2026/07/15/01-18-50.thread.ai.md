Subject: [PATCH v6 0/2] repository: move `fetch_if_missing` into `struct repository`
Series version: v6 (2-patch series, part of libification and `the_repository` removal efforts)
Originating author: Tian Yuchen <cat@malon.dev>
Thread type: patch submission

Core problem or goal
Move the global `fetch_if_missing` variable into `struct repository` to make it a per-repository flag controlling whether missing objects should trigger lazy fetches from promisor remotes. This continues the project-wide effort to eliminate global state in favor of repository-specific configuration, preserving existing behavior (default value remains `1`, enabled).

Current status
**Technically complete for the immediate goal**; v6 fully addresses all prior feedback, including Junio’s v5 review suggestion to use the `value` field in `OPT_CALLBACK_F()` to pass the repository address directly to `option_parse_missing_action()`. This eliminates the intermediate `arg_missing_action` variable and the deferred side effect in `cmd_pack_objects()`, resolving the design inelegance noted in v5. The series is now **mechanically clean** and **ready for maintainer decision**. Junio’s latest reply to v5 confirmed mechanical completeness ("Much better") but did not signal whether the series will be queued; the broader architectural question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` remains unresolved. The preparatory patch from v5 (propagating the `repo` parameter in `pack-objects.c`) is now unnecessary due to the callback adjustment.

Key technical details
- **Files touched**: 15 files, including `builtin/fetch-pack.c`, `builtin/fsck.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `builtin/rev-list.c`, `common-init.c`, `git.c`, `odb.c`, `repository.c`, `setup.c`, `revision.c`, and partial clone documentation. All direct uses of `the_repository` for `fetch_if_missing` are resolved; the remaining reference in `common-init.c` is explicitly excluded from the goal.
- **New symbols**: `struct repository->fetch_if_missing` (replaces global `fetch_if_missing`).
- **Behavior**: Default value remains `1` (enabled); initialization occurs in `initialize_repository()`. Special handling in `fsck.c` and `index-pack.c` defers operations until after parameter parsing to avoid null pointer dereferences. `revision.c` uses `revs->repo` instead of `the_repository`, ensuring consistency with the function's parameters. The ternary fallback in `index-pack.c` (`(repo ? repo : the_repository)->fetch_if_missing`) remains unchanged to handle repository-less invocations.
- **Edge cases**:
  - The `setup.c` fix (`repo->fetch_if_missing = 0` when `NO_LAZY_FETCH_ENVIRONMENT` is set) now uses the passed-in `repo` parameter, resolving the inconsistency noted in v3.
  - The v6 series resolves the design inelegance in `option_parse_missing_action()` by using the `value` field in `OPT_CALLBACK_F()` to pass the repository address directly, eliminating the intermediate `arg_missing_action` variable and the deferred side effect in `cmd_pack_objects()`.
  - Junio’s v2 review outlined three possible approaches to resolve repository-less invocation issues:
    1. Require `index-pack` to always run inside a repository (adopted in v3 for the immediate fix).
    2. Use `the_repository` as a fallback when `repo` is NULL (partially adopted in v3 and v4 via the ternary operator).
    3. Introduce a new global "basic settings" structure to hold flags like `fetch_if_missing` that apply even outside a repository, with per-repository settings overriding them.
  - The broader design question of whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` remains open. The author has deferred a more comprehensive solution (e.g., Junio’s third approach) until subsystems are fully prepared to handle `repo` instead of `the_repository`.
- **On-disk format**: No changes.
- **Test coverage**: No new tests; existing behavior is preserved. The series addresses mechanical correctness, but explicit testing of per-repository behavior (especially in `index-pack` and `revision`) and repository-less invocations remains unconfirmed. The author has coordinated with other topics to avoid conflicts, but verification of multi-repository behavior is still pending.

Open questions and loose ends
- **Repository-less invocations**: The broader architectural question of whether `fetch_if_missing` should be tied to `struct repository` remains unresolved. Junio’s v2 review outlined three approaches, and the author has adopted the first (restricting `index-pack` to repository-bound operation) as a pragmatic solution. The other two approaches—a fallback to `the_repository` or a new global "basic settings" structure—remain on the table for future discussion.
- **Testing**: The author has not provided explicit confirmation that the series was tested with repositories other than `the_repository` or in repository-less contexts. While the fixes address the raised concerns, the absence of new test coverage or multi-repository verification leaves a risk of undetected regressions. The next iteration may need to include tests for the new repository-bound behavior of `index-pack`, per-repository `fetch_if_missing` consistency, and the resolved `the_repository` references.
- **Design preference**: Patrick Steinhardt previously suggested moving the flag into the object database rather than `struct repository`, but acknowledged the current approach aligns with libification goals. Junio’s review now challenges whether the flag belongs in `struct repository` at all, making this a more fundamental point of discussion. The v6 series resolves the immediate technical blockers, but the long-term architectural direction remains open.
- **Architectural clarification**: Junio may still request further clarification on whether `fetch_if_missing` belongs in `struct repository` before queuing the series. The author has deferred a more comprehensive solution (e.g., Junio’s third approach) until subsystems are fully prepared to handle `repo` instead of `the_repository`.
