This is a **bugfix follow-up** to the GSoC v18 `remote-object-info` series, which is now cooking in `next`. The patch in question (11/13) adds the `remote-object-info` subcommand to `git cat-file --batch-command`, but Junio spotted a **silent failure mode** in `parse_cmd_remote_object_info()`: if `get_remote_info()` fails, the function returns without notifying the caller or user, leaving the output buffer in an undefined state. This could mislead callers (like `batch_command()`) into treating stale or partial data as valid.

The author, Pablo Sabater, acknowledges the issue and confirms the fix: replacing the silent `goto cleanup` with a `die()` call. This aligns the behavior with other `parse_cmd_*` functions in `cat-file.c` and ensures failures are loud and unambiguous. The change is trivial but critical for robustness—this was the last unresolved technical concern before the series could graduate to `master`. The thread context shows all other feedback (including security hardening and edge-case handling) has been addressed, and the series is otherwise ready for integration. Expect a v19 with this fix shortly.
