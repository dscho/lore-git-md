This is the fourth patch in the nine-patch `diff.<driver>.process` RFC series (v5). It refactors the subprocess API to separate process lifecycle management from hashmap operations, a change requested in earlier review rounds to accommodate callers that manage their own process indexing.

The patch extracts two new functions, `subprocess_start_command()` and `subprocess_stop_command()`, which handle the actual child process setup, handshake, and teardown without touching the hashmap. The existing `subprocess_start()` and `subprocess_stop()` become thin wrappers that add hashmap operations on top. This decoupling allows the diff-process driver (introduced later in the series) to reuse the subprocess machinery while managing its own process lifetime through the `userdiff_driver` struct, avoiding redundant hashmap lookups.

The implementation is straightforward: the new functions are carved out of the existing ones, and the original functions now delegate to them before performing hashmap operations. The patch adds no new behavior but enables the subsequent diff-process integration (patch 6) to avoid the hashmap entirely. The change is mechanical and well-contained, touching only `sub-process.c` and `sub-process.h`. No test failures are expected, and the commit message clearly explains the motivation and design. This is a clean refactoring that addresses a specific reviewer concern without introducing new complexity.
