This is a refactoring patch in the final, ready-to-merge `remote-object-info` series (v18). It moves three variable initializations (`allow_unadvertised_object_request`, `use_sideband`, and `args->deepen`) from inside the `FETCH_CHECK_LOCAL` state to the function prologue of `do_fetch_pack_v2()` in `fetch-pack.c`. The change is purely mechanical and has no behavior impact—it simply ensures the variables are set regardless of the initial state, which will be necessary for a later patch that introduces an alternative initial state for the `remote-object-info` command.

The patch is authored by Calvin Wan but carried forward by Pablo Sabater, who has taken over as the primary author of the series. It includes Helped-by trailers crediting Jonathan Tan and Christian Couder for design input. The diff is minimal (12 lines changed, 6 insertions, 6 deletions) and the commit message clearly explains the motivation: preparing for future extensibility without altering current behavior. This is a well-justified, low-risk refactoring that aligns with the series' overall goal of reusing the fetch-pack state machine for new protocol commands.
