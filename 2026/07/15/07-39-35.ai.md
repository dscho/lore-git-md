This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series removing `the_repository` from the refs subsystem. It is a one-line cleanup in the packed refs backend: the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro is removed from `refs/packed-backend.c` now that the backend’s single use of `the_repository` has already been converted to use the backend’s own `struct repository *` (done in patch 1/7). The change is purely mechanical and has no behavioral impact; it simply drops an unused macro definition. The patch is uncontroversial and ready to merge as part of the larger `the_repository` removal effort.
