This is a maintainer review comment on the second patch of Paulius Zaleckas’s `fetch.submoduleErrors` series (v5). Junio critiques the coding style choices made to silence `-Wsign-compare` warnings, specifically the use of `size_t` for array indices where a plain `unsigned` (or even `int`) would be more natural and readable. He illustrates the point with two examples from the patch: the `submodule_errors_to_string` helper (where a cast to `size_t` is forced) and the `parse_submodule_errors` loop (where `size_t i` is unnecessary). Junio’s preference is to declare the parameter and loop variable as `unsigned` instead, avoiding the cast and making the intent clearer. He also notes that the array name `submodule_errors_names` should be singular (`submodule_error_name`) per Git’s coding guidelines. The review is purely mechanical—no functional concerns are raised—and the series remains technically complete and ready for `next`.
