This is Junio C Hamano’s review of Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy’s v5 patch converting `git show-branch` to use a commit-slab for per-commit state. The review is substantive and identifies two behavioral regressions in the `join_revs()` function, where the slab-based version fails to propagate the `UNINTERESTING` bit to parents and omits the original logic that avoided redundant traversal. Junio also notes that the documentation and error messages still reflect the old 26-branch limit and that the `%zu` format specifier is not portable. The review is constructive and technically precise, focusing on correctness and edge cases rather than style. The patch is not yet ready for merging; the author will need to address these issues in a v6. Key symbols to track in follow-up discussion: `join_revs()`, `UNINTERESTING`, `has_all_rev_flags()`, and `MAX_REVS`.
