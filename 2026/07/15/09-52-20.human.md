This is the fourth patch in René Scharfe’s five-patch series converting Git’s tempfile and lockfile APIs to be repository-aware, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch itself is a straightforward cleanup: it removes the now-unused compatibility wrappers `create_tempfile_mode()` and `create_tempfile()` from `tempfile.c` and `tempfile.h` after all callers have been migrated to the new `repo_create_tempfile*` variants in earlier patches. The change is purely mechanical—14 lines removed, no new code, no behavior changes—and is uncontroversial.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a brief, surface-level review. He notes the removal with approval ("Nice") and suggests a minor improvement to the commit message of the *preceding* patch (3/5): hinting that the compatibility wrappers would be removed in a follow-up patch. This is a nit about commit message clarity rather than a technical concern, and it does not request changes to the code or the current patch. The feedback is friendly and constructive but does not engage with the substance of the patch itself.

The series remains queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, with no substantive objections or additional reviews.
