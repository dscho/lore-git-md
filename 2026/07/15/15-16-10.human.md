This is a refactoring patch in Harald Nordgren’s `git history squash` series, now in its ninth iteration. The patch adds an optional `message_template` parameter to the internal `commit_tree_ext()` helper in `builtin/history.c`. When the caller supplies a template, the function uses it instead of the commit message from the passed-in commit object; when NULL, the original behavior is preserved. This plumbing change enables the `--reedit-message` feature introduced later in the series, allowing users to interactively combine commit messages from the entire squashed range.

The patch is small (10 insertions, 6 deletions) and touches only `builtin/history.c`. It passes NULL from the existing `fixup` and `split` callers, ensuring no user-visible behavior change. This is the second of five patches in the series, which is now functionally complete and ready for integration. Junio’s "Will replace" sign-off on v7 signals intent to merge, and v9 addresses all prior feedback, including the last open technical concern (`amend!` message handling). The series is part of a larger architectural vision to modernize Git’s history-editing infrastructure, with `git history squash` positioned as a foundational piece.
