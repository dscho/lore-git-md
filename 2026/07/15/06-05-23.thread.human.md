This thread is about a small but long-standing bug in Git’s diff plumbing: `git diff --relative --cached` crashes with a segfault when it encounters unmerged paths outside the current directory. The issue dates back to 2011 and was partially addressed in 2021, but the cached case remained broken. Jeff King (Peff) posted a minimal fix—a single NULL check in `diff-lib.c`—along with a regression test to prevent future regressions.

The patch is straightforward and uncontroversial. Peff’s analysis and test coverage leave little room for debate, and Junio C Hamano’s response, while lighthearted, doesn’t raise any concerns. The fix is still under review, but no substantive objections or open questions remain. Unless someone spots an edge case in the coming days, this will likely be queued for the next maintenance release.
