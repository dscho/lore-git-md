This thread began with a bugfix for a long-standing segfault in `git diff --relative --cached` when operating on unmerged paths outside the current directory. Jeff King (Peff) identified and fixed the NULL pointer dereference in `do_oneway_diff()`, then followed up with a two-patch refactoring series to clean up the same code path. The entire three-patch set—bugfix plus refactorings—has been reviewed and approved by Junio C Hamano and is now queued for integration into the `next` branch.

The refactorings are small and targeted: one removes a stale comment, and the other moves the `--relative` prefix check earlier in `oneway_diff()` to eliminate redundant work, mirroring an optimization made in 2021. In a recent email, Peff noted a Coverity static-analysis warning about a potential NULL dereference in the new code, but explained it is a false positive—`tree` can only be dereferenced when `idx` is NULL, and in that case `tree` cannot be NULL. He suggested adding a `BUG()` assertion to clarify the invariant for static analyzers, though the patch remains correct and ready for integration as-is. No further discussion or changes are expected.
