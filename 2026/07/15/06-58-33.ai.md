This is the tenth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 11-patch series addressing unchecked return values identified by Coverity, focusing on the bisect subsystem. The patch adds error checks at all six call sites of `get_terms()` in `builtin/bisect.c` to ensure a failed `get_terms()` (e.g., due to a missing or truncated `BISECT_TERMS` file) produces a clear "no terms defined" error rather than confusing downstream behavior.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply raises a design question: instead of checking the term pointers (`term->bad` and `term->good`) at each call site, should `get_terms()` itself return an error if either term is `NULL`? This could centralize error handling and deduplicate error messages, simplifying the callers. His suggestion is substantive, engaging with the patch’s approach rather than its correctness, and proposes an alternative that could reduce redundancy. The question is whether any callers legitimately accept `NULL` terms, which would determine whether such a change is feasible. No decision has been made yet, and the discussion remains open.
