This is a refactoring patch in Phillip Wood’s 9-patch v3 series fixing how the sequencer handles dropped commits during rebase. It removes a redundant null check on `item->commit` in `pick_one_commit()` in `sequencer.c`.

The patch is the sixth in the series and follows the established pattern of incremental cleanup to prepare for the core behavioral change in patch 9/9. The null check is unnecessary because `item->commit` is unconditionally dereferenced in `do_pick_commit()` (called at the start of `pick_one_commit()`) and must be non-null for the function to know which commit to pick. The diff is minimal: one line changed (removing the `&& item->commit` condition).

This is a **low-risk, mechanical cleanup** with no behavior change. It is part of the broader effort to simplify control flow before introducing `enum pick_result` in later patches. The series as a whole is approved by Junio C Hamano and marked ready for `next` after typofixes are squashed. No substantive discussion or objections have been raised about this specific patch.
