This thread is about a bugfix patch from Derrick Stolee that prevents a Git crash in the trace2 telemetry subsystem. The crash occurs when timestamp formatting fails in `tr2_tbuf.c`, triggering `die()` during `atexit` events and causing infinite recursion in the die handler. Derrick’s patch replaces `xsnprintf()` with `snprintf()` and adds defensive initialization to avoid the crash, falling back to a hardcoded "blank" timestamp on failure.

Taylor Blau’s review raises a deeper question about the root cause. He suggests the crash may stem from Git for Windows’s redirection of `vsnprintf()` to `libintl_vsnprintf()`, which allocates memory for format strings with more than six placeholders—like the seven-place timestamp format in trace2. His alternative fix would address the `xsnprintf()`/`libintl` boundary in `wrapper.c` rather than treating the trace2 recursion in isolation. While Taylor’s approach might be architecturally cleaner, it’s untested and leaves the root cause of the libintl allocation failure unresolved.

The thread is now at a crossroads: Derrick’s patch is a targeted, low-risk fix for the immediate crash, while Taylor’s proposal could resolve a broader compatibility issue but introduces uncertainty. No other participants have weighed in yet, so the next steps depend on whether the community prioritizes the immediate fix or a deeper investigation into the libintl behavior. The patch remains under review, with no merge decisions made.
