The series introduces `banned-die.h`, a compile-time enforcement mechanism to block these functions, and updates trace2 to handle failures gracefully (e.g., hardcoded timestamps, `NULL` returns, or a static fallback context).

Derrick acknowledges the limitations but defends the series as incremental progress, noting that a rewrite would be a significant effort. His latest email suggests he might remove `banned-die.h` in a future version to simplify the series while keeping the core hardening work, but no decision has been made.
