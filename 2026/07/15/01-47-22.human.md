This is a refactoring patch targeting the `show-branch` built-in. Gatla Vishweshwar Reddy’s v3 patch completes the conversion of `git show-branch` from the shared `object.flags` field to a dedicated commit-slab (`commit_rev_flags`) for per-commit branch-tracking state. The patch keeps the `UNINTERESTING` bit in `object.flags` (as requested by Junio and Peff) and widens the slab element to `uint64_t`, lifting the previous 27-branch limit to 64 branches. All bit operations now use `UINT64_C(1)` for correct 64-bit shifts, and the slab is initialized with a stride that can later be extended for even more branches. The commit message and code are clean, the tests still pass, and the patch directly addresses the conceptual gaps identified in v2. It looks ready for serious review and is likely to land once any remaining nits are ironed out.
