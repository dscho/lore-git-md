This thread is a two-patch series modernizing the test style in `t/t9811-git-p4-label-import.sh`, posted by Marcelo Machado Lage (with Vinicius Lira de Freitas as co-author). The goal is to replace legacy shell test constructs (`test -f` and `! test -f`) with Git’s modern test helpers (`test_path_is_file` and `test_path_is_missing`) and split long `&&` chains for better readability. The series is part of the ongoing community-wide effort to improve test maintainability and error reporting.

Version 2 of the series incorporated all feedback from the initial review, expanding the scope to split *all* multi-command `&&` chains in the file for consistency. The first patch (splitting `&&` chains) was posted earlier, and the second patch—just submitted—completes the series by replacing the remaining legacy test constructs. Both patches are purely mechanical, confined to the same test file, and make no behavioral changes. The modern helpers provide stricter guarantees (e.g., `test_path_is_missing` checks for file nonexistence rather than just absence of a regular file) and better error messages, but the test logic remains unchanged.

The series has received surface-level but supportive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano, who endorsed the expanded scope and the use of modern test helpers. No substantive objections or design concerns remain, and the series appears uncontroversial and ready for integration. It is currently under review, with no evidence of merge to `seen`, `next`, or `master`. The work touches only the Git-Perforce integration test suite and has no dependencies or conflicts with other in-flight topics.
