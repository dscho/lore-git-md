This is a follow-up to Junio C Hamano’s "What’s cooking" report, focusing on three of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB and refs topics. Patrick responds to Junio’s questions about readiness and outlines minor updates.

For **ps/odb-drop-whence**, Patrick will send a small reroll to rename a variable (`sourcep` → `source_infop`) based on Justin Tobler’s review feedback. The series removes the `whence` field from `struct object_info` and refactors backend-specific object information into a new `struct object_info_source`. It depends on the already-merged `ps/odb-source-packed` and is otherwise ready for `next`.

The **ps/odb-generalize-prepare** series is marked as ready. It introduces `odb_prepare()` and generalizes the `reprepare()` callback into a `prepare()` callback with a flush-cache flag. This is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and also depends on `ps/odb-source-packed`.

Finally, **ps/refs-writing-subcommands** adds four new subcommands to `git refs`: `create`, `delete`, `update`, and `rename`. The series is ready for `next` and drops the `the_repository` global, aligning with the broader `the_repository` removal effort. No further changes are planned.
