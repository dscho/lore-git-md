This is the eighth patch in Toon Claes’s nine-patch series that makes `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient enough to run in CI. It removes the RAM-disk workaround on macOS GitLab runners because the upcoming `GIT_TEST_LONG` jobs will write up to 8 GB of data, which exceeds the 4 GB RAM disk and causes out-of-space errors. The patch simply stops mounting the RAM disk and lets tests write to the regular `/tmp` filesystem instead.

The commit message explains the trade-off: macOS jobs slow down (from 18–21 minutes to 25–33 minutes), but the pipeline becomes more stable. Since the macOS jobs are not the slowest in the pipeline, the extra runtime does not extend the overall CI duration. The change is a single deletion in `.gitlab-ci.yml` that removes the `diskutil` incantation and points `TEST_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` back to `/tmp`. It is a straightforward, uncontroversial fix that clears the way for the next patch, which enables `GIT_TEST_LONG` on GitLab.
