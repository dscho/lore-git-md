This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part series refactoring `struct object_info` to use the new `source_infop` field. The series is part of the ODB abstraction effort and is now conceptually approved by Junio C Hamano.

This patch removes the now-unused `whence` field from `struct object_info` after all callers have been migrated to use the new `source_infop->source->type` pattern. The change is mechanical: the field is deleted from the struct definition in `odb.h`, and all assignments to it are removed from `odb.c`, `odb/source-inmemory.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, and `packfile.c`. No behavior changes are introduced—this is purely cleanup.

The patch touches five files and removes 14 lines. It’s a straightforward follow-through on the earlier refactoring and received surface-level approval from Justin Tobler in the thread. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction work, this patch is a minor but necessary step toward eliminating direct `packed_object_info()` usage. There are no open questions or concerns about this specific change.
