This is a brief follow-up in the **bugfix series** plugging memory leaks in Git’s non-default hash implementations. Jeff King (Peff) replies to Junio C Hamano’s **maintainer approval** of the first patch, which removes the redundant `discard_hashfile()` function in favor of `free_hashfile()`.

Peff acknowledges Junio’s lighthearted nod to Eric Sunshine’s naming preferences but notes that renaming `free_hashfile()` to `hashfile_free()` (or swapping "free" for "discard") would require a broader interface change. He suggests such tweaks could be made later, implying the current patch’s naming is acceptable for now. The exchange confirms the patch’s direction is settled, with only minor stylistic refinements left open. The rest of the series (patches 2–9) remains unreviewed, but this interaction signals smooth sailing for the cleanup’s foundational change.
