This thread is about a patch series aimed at making Git’s long-running tests (`GIT_TEST_LONG`) more reliable and efficient in CI environments. The series, now in its second version submitted by Patrick Steinhardt, addresses two key problems: broken or hanging tests in GitHub and GitLab CI, and the lack of coverage for these expensive tests in GitLab CI. The v2 revision incorporates all feedback from the initial submission by Toon Claes, including a correction to retain the `EXPENSIVE` label for a memory-intensive test despite runtime improvements.

The series is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration, with all nine patches addressing either CI configuration, test correctness, or efficiency improvements. Key changes include skipping tests on platforms where they are known to fail due to `size_t` limitations, reducing disk and CPU usage in resource-heavy tests, and enabling `GIT_TEST_LONG` in GitLab CI for integration branches (excluding Windows due to hardware constraints). One minor nit remains: a trivial update to the subject line of patch 2 to clarify that the test is skipped *with* `SIZE_T_IS_64BIT`, not without it. Junio C Hamano and Jeff King (Peff) have reviewed the series, with Peff endorsing the technical changes and Junio flagging the wording issue. No substantive objections remain, and the series is poised for merging once the subject line is corrected.
