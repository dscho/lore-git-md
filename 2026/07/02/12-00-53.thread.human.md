This thread is a nine-patch series aimed at making Git’s `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient enough to run in CI. Originally identified by Toon Claes, the issues included broken or hanging tests in GitHub and GitLab CI, as well as GitLab’s lack of coverage for these expensive tests. Patrick Steinhardt took over the series in its second iteration, addressing all feedback from the first version, including restoring the `EXPENSIVE` prerequisite in one test after SZEDER Gábor pointed out its high memory usage justified the label.

The series is now complete and uncontroversial. It fixes test correctness issues (e.g., skipping tests on platforms where they’re known to break), improves efficiency (e.g., reducing disk usage and runtime bottlenecks), and aligns GitLab CI with GitHub Actions by enabling `GIT_TEST_LONG` for pushes to integration branches. The final patch, just summarized, excludes Windows runners due to hardware limitations but otherwise closes the coverage gap between the two CI systems. Jeff King (Peff) and Gábor reviewed key patches, and their feedback has been incorporated. The series appears ready for merging, with no open technical objections or unresolved questions.
