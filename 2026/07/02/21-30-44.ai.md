This is a **test efficiency patch** and part of Toon Claes’s nine-patch series aimed at making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient for CI. This specific patch (6/9) refactors `t7900-maintenance.sh` to clean up the 2 GB repository immediately after the expensive test completes, reducing peak disk usage and preventing the data from being repacked by later tests.

The patch moves the expensive test into a dedicated sub-repository (`expensive-repo`) and deletes it via `test_when_finished`, while also disabling auto-maintenance inside the sub-repository to avoid races. The change is purely mechanical—no test logic is altered—but it isolates the large repository and ensures it is ephemeral. Jeff King (Peff) tested the patch and found that it does not reduce runtime (still ~1m40s with `--long`), likely because subsequent repacks are incremental or geometric and do not rewrite the large pack. However, he endorses the patch as an "obvious improvement" for reducing peak disk usage and avoiding future surprises as more tests are added.

The patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated, addressing a clear inefficiency in the test suite. It complements the broader series goal of making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests CI-friendly by minimizing resource consumption. No open questions or objections have been raised; Peff’s feedback is observational rather than critical. The patch is authored by Patrick Steinhardt and touches only the test script (`t7900-maintenance.sh`), with no impact on user-facing behavior or other subsystems.
