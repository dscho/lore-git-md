This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring `struct object_info` to use a `source_infop` field, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch threads an optional `struct odb_source_packed *source` parameter through `packed_object_info()` and `packed_object_info_with_index_pos()`, preparing the ground for later commits to record object provenance. No behavior changes are introduced yet—all callers outside the packed ODB backend pass `NULL` for the new parameter, and the packed backend passes its already-available source.

The patch touches eight files (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, `pack-bitmap.c`, `packfile.c`, `packfile.h`, and `t/helper/test-bitmap.c`), updating function signatures and call sites to accommodate the new parameter. The changes are mechanical and confined to the ODB and packfile subsystems, with no impact on on-disk formats or user-visible behavior. The `odb/source-packed.c` caller is the only one that passes a non-NULL source, reflecting the design choice to make source tracking opt-in for now.

This is a foundational refactoring step, enabling future patches to populate the `source_infop` field with backend-specific provenance data. The patch is conceptually approved as part of the series, and the changes align with the long-term goal of eliminating direct `packed_object_info()` usage in favor of `odb_read_object_info()`. If you’re tracking the ODB abstraction effort, this patch is a critical intermediate step—it introduces the plumbing without changing behavior, setting up the next patches to actually use the new field.
