He suggests this could be done in a follow-up patch to avoid introducing subtle behavioral changes in the current series. The reply is **low-stakes and procedural**, focusing on naming consistency rather than technical concerns. No objections or new issues are raised; the thread remains on track for patch 1’s inclusion, with potential naming tweaks deferred to a later iteration. The broader series’ goal—plugging hash-context leaks—is unaffected.
