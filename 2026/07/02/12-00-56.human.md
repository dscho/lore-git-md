This is the third patch in Toon Claes’s nine-patch series making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests reliable and efficient for CI. It removes a performance bottleneck in `t4141-apply-too-large.sh`: the test previously used `dd` to copy 1 GB of zeros one byte at a time, taking up to 40 minutes in CI. The fix replaces the slow `dd` loop with a single call to `test-tool genzeros` that already knows how to generate the data quickly. On the author’s machine the test now finishes in under a second, so the `EXPENSIVE` prerequisite is dropped. The change is a straightforward, well-motivated efficiency win that directly addresses the series’ goal of making long-running tests CI-friendly.
