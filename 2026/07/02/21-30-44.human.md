This is a **test infrastructure patch** in Toon Claes’s nine-patch series aimed at making `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests more reliable and efficient in CI. This specific patch (6/9) refactors `t7900-maintenance.sh` to clean up the 2 GB repository immediately after the expensive test finishes, reducing peak disk usage and preventing the data from being repacked by later tests.

The patch moves the test into a dedicated sub-repository (`expensive-repo`) that is deleted via `test_when_finished`, and disables auto-maintenance inside it to avoid races. Jeff King (Peff) tested the change and found that while it doesn’t reduce runtime (still ~1m40s with `--long`), it’s still a clear improvement for disk usage and future-proofing. His review is brief but positive, calling it an "obvious improvement" without raising any concerns.

This is a straightforward, uncontroversial patch that addresses a real issue—clogging disk space and risking unintended repacks—without changing test behavior. It’s part of the broader effort to make `GIT_TEST_LONG` tests CI-friendly, and Peff’s feedback suggests it’s ready to proceed.
