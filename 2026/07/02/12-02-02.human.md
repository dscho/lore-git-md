This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-part series refactoring Git’s `struct object_info` to replace the `whence` field with a new `source_infop` pointer and `struct odb_source_info`. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is conceptually approved by Junio C Hamano.

This patch converts the remaining callers of the now-redundant `whence` field to use the new `source_infop->source->type` instead. The changes are mechanical and straightforward: every instance of `oi->whence == OI_PACKED` becomes `oi->source_infop->source->type == ODB_SOURCE_PACKED`. The four files touched (`builtin/cat-file.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, and `reachable.c`) are all high-level consumers of object-info queries, and the conversions are consistent with the pattern established in earlier patches.

The patch is small (four files, five insertions, four deletions) and uncontroversial. All callers already set up the `source_infop` request pointer, so the new field is guaranteed to be populated. No behavior changes are introduced—this is purely a refactoring step that paves the way for removing the `whence` field in the next patch.

If you’re following the ODB abstraction work, this patch is a routine but necessary step in the series. It received surface-level approval in v1 and is unlikely to generate further discussion. The next patch (5/6) will remove the `whence` field entirely.
