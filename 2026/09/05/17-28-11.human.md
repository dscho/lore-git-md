This is a documentation patch series, now in its third iteration (v3), that improves the discoverability of the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page. The series is part of an effort to ensure users can easily find Git’s tutorial on its data model, which was previously unlinked from other documentation. The v3 update addresses minor stylistic feedback from Julia Evans on the final patch (4/4), removing an inline cross-reference to `gitglossary(7)` from the introductory paragraph of `gitdatamodel.adoc` while retaining it in the "SEE ALSO" section. The series has already been merged to `next` in its v2 form, and this v3 submission finalizes the agreed-upon tweaks.

The changes are purely additive and touch only documentation files: `Documentation/git.adoc`, `Documentation/gitdatamodel.adoc`, `Documentation/gitglossary.adoc`, `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc`, and `command-list.txt`. No code, tests, or on-disk formats are affected. The patches add `gitdatamodel(7)` to the list of concept guides in `git(1)`, include direct links to it in the tutorial and terminology sections, and ensure reciprocal links between the data model page and the glossary. The interdiff shows the sole change in v3 is the removal of the redundant inline link in `gitdatamodel.adoc`, simplifying the text as requested.
