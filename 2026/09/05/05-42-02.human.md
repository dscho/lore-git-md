This is the author's reply to Junio C Hamano's surface-level review comment on the third patch in the v2 series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion. The series is part of an effort to make `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert` behave consistently by running auto maintenance only once at the end of the sequence, rather than during intermediate steps.

Thomas Bachem acknowledges Junio's suggestion to store the cached `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` string as a `const char *` instead of a `struct strbuf` for clarity and immutability. He agrees the change is straightforward and will implement it in the next version by constructing the string with a local `strbuf`, detaching it, and storing it as a `char *` that `replay_ctx_release()` will free. This is a mechanical adjustment that does not affect the patch's functionality or scope.

The series itself remains technically complete and addresses all prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Patrick Steinhardt. No open technical objections remain; the only watch item is a hypothetical edge case about very long sequences where mid-sequence repacking might improve performance, which Phillip Wood wants to monitor in real-world usage. The next revision is expected to be a final polish before merging.
