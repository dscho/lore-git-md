This thread began as a straightforward update to Git’s CI workflows, replacing Debian 11 with Debian 12 after Debian 11 reached end-of-LTS. Jeff King (Peff) posted the patch, which Junio C Hamano has already queued in `seen` with the intent to merge. The change itself is mechanical—updating Docker image references in `.github/workflows/main.yml` and `.gitlab-ci.yml`—and should graduate to `next` shortly.

Peff’s follow-up email expands the scope slightly, noting two other CI jobs that still rely on outdated LTS releases: the `linux32` job (Ubuntu 20.04, last LTS with i386 support) and the `linux-TEST-vars` job (also Ubuntu 20.04). He suggests leaving both as-is for now, reasoning that the `linux32` job still provides some 32-bit coverage (even if only for x86) and that the `TEST-vars` job might serve a dual purpose. Neither issue is treated as urgent, and neither blocks the Debian 12 update. The thread remains focused on the original patch, with these observations flagged as potential future maintenance tasks rather than immediate action items.
