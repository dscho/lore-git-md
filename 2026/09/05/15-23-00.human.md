This is a CI/build system patch discussion, specifically a follow-up to Jeff King’s series (`peff/ci-asciidoctor-bump`) that modernizes GitLab CI’s Asciidoctor installation by replacing the outdated version pin (1.5.8) with the system-provided package (2.0.20 in Ubuntu). Todd Zullinger’s email adds platform-specific context about Asciidoctor packaging in RHEL and its clones (AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux), but does not propose changes to the patch or raise concerns.

The email clarifies that Asciidoctor in RHEL-based distributions is maintained in the EPEL repository (a community-driven add-on for RHEL), not the base OS. While EPEL allows more frequent updates than RHEL itself, Todd notes that the Asciidoctor package in EPEL 9 has not been updated to 2.0.20 despite his earlier work, suggesting limited maintenance. He provides a diff showing how to enable EPEL in CI (via `epel-release`) if GitLab CI were to test RHEL-like targets, but emphasizes this is irrelevant to the current patch since no CI jobs target those platforms.

The tone is collaborative and informational. Todd’s comments reinforce Jeff’s earlier conclusion that the version pin removal is safe, as even older enterprise distributions (like Debian 11 and RHEL 8) ship Asciidoctor 2.x. The email closes with a light endorsement of Asciidoctor’s output quality compared to Asciidoc, but this is tangential to the patch. No action is required from the patch authors or maintainers; the series remains uncontroversial and ready for merging.
