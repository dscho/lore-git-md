This is a substantive review exchange in the `rerere` race-condition bugfix thread. Junio C Hamano pushes back on the v3 patch’s behavior of allowing a rebase to proceed without recording the conflict preimage after a lock timeout, calling it an unacceptable regression. He argues that silently skipping `rerere` recording risks losing the user’s conflict resolution effort, even if the rebase itself can be resumed later. Instead, he proposes a loud, actionable error message that tells the user exactly what went wrong and how to recover—running `git rerere` manually before continuing. This approach preserves the user’s ability to salvage their work while making the failure mode explicit and recoverable. The discussion now centers on whether the patch should fail fast with clear guidance (Junio’s preference) or proceed silently after a timeout (Thomas’s v3 approach). The outcome will shape the error-handling design in the upcoming v4 patch.
