Junio C Hamano’s reply to the v2 iteration of the patch (now standalone after the test patch was withdrawn) offers feedback on the phrasing of the new documentation.

Junio acknowledges the accuracy of the added text but questions whether the final sentence ("When a single commit is picked this way...") effectively communicates the design intent. He suggests clarifying that the absence of `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` is a deliberate choice, aligning with `--no-commit`’s purpose as a "better `git show | git apply`" for building custom work. His proposed framing emphasizes that users are expected to "futz" with the working tree afterward, making the original authorship less relevant. While he does not object to the current wording, he recommends moving the advice about `git commit -c <commit>` to the `git-commit` documentation, as it applies more broadly than just cherry-picking.

The feedback is surface-level, focusing on editorial clarity rather than technical correctness. Junio’s tone is constructive, and he explicitly states the patch "looks good" aside from the phrasing concern.
