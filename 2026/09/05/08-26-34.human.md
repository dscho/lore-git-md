This is an administrative update about Git's participation in the **Outreachy December 2026 cohort**. The thread is now moving from discussion to action: Christian Couder confirms he will submit Git's application as a mentoring organization by the **September 11, 2026 deadline**, citing strong community support and no objections to participation.

Four volunteers have stepped forward to mentor or co-mentor: Christian Couder, Usman Akinyemi (a former Git Outreachy intern), Kaartic Sivaraam, and Pablo Sabater. Christian and Kaartic are also prepared to serve as organization admins. The project still lacks concrete project ideas for interns, however. Christian proposes two potential projects: (1) continuing the removal of global state (or beginning to libify parts of the codebase), and (2) improving how command arguments and options are parsed by replacing ad-hoc code with standardized APIs like `parse-options`. He invites feedback on these ideas and welcomes additional proposals.

Funding remains a concern. Last year's intern was sponsored directly by the Git project after GitHub and GitLab declined to fund internships. Christian plans to reach out to GitLab again but encourages others to seek support from other companies. The tone is pragmatic and collaborative, focusing on logistics and next steps rather than technical details. If you care about Git's outreach efforts or have project ideas suitable for an Outreachy intern, this thread is worth following.
