Jeff King (Peff) expands on his earlier patch to update CI from Debian 11 to Debian 12, noting that the `linux32` build job still uses Ubuntu 20.04, which has been out of LTS for a year. He also notes that the `linux-TEST-vars` job uses Ubuntu 20.04, which could theoretically be updated to `ubuntu-latest`, but speculates it might serve a dual purpose: testing exotic configurations *and* testing on an older platform. Again, he defers action until problems arise, treating these as out-of-scope for his current Debian 12 patch.

The tone is pragmatic and low-key: Peff is flagging potential future work rather than pushing for immediate changes. The discussion is unlikely to derail the Debian 12 patch, but it surfaces two latent CI maintenance tasks (32-bit coverage and Ubuntu LTS alignment) that may resurface if either job starts failing or if someone volunteers to address them.
