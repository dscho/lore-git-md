The fix ensures the traversal covers the entire local branch history when no remote-tracking reflog exists, but Junio’s reply probes the trade-off between safety (full reflog scan) and performance. He suggests alternatives like `gc.reflogExpire` (default: 90 days) or "now" might be more reasonable than `0`, which forces a full scan back to 1970.
