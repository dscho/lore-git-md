This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 8-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch defers object database (ODB) initialization in `git clone` until after the clone source URI is resolved, enabling alternates to be written with full context — including any URI rewrites from template `.git/config` files — and preparing for future ODB backends that may require object format information at creation time.

The change introduces a new `INIT_DB_SKIP_ODB` flag to `init_db()`, skips ODB creation when set, and exposes `create_object_database()` so it can be called later in `cmd_clone()`. The patch is mechanically straightforward and well-motivated, with a clear commit message tying it to the broader goal of moving alternates handling into ODB creation. Justin Tobler’s review is surface-level, noting the growing awkwardness of `init_db()`’s "skip" flags but not engaging with the technical rationale or edge cases. His only question — why reference setup is also deferred — is left unanswered in this reply, though the thread context suggests it is a natural consequence of the ODB deferral rather than an independent design choice.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration. Its significance lies in enabling the subsequent patches, particularly the core design change in patch 6 (writing alternates during ODB creation using Git’s lockfile API). No substantive concerns have been raised about this patch, and the broader series has addressed its primary blocker (TOCTOU race concerns) in v2. Reviewers focused on the ODB abstraction effort — such as Justin Tobler or Karthik Nayak — may still weigh in on the later patches, but this one looks like a clean preparatory step.
