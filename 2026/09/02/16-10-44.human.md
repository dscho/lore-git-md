This is the third patch in Christian Couder’s six-part series introducing a new `parse-options` sub-API for early argument scanning. It fixes a specific bug in `git rev-parse` where `--default -- <not-a-rev>` misinterprets the `--` option value as the revision/path separator, producing a misleading "bad revision" error instead of the usual "ambiguous argument" message.

The patch replaces the hand-rolled `--` detection loop in `cmd_rev_parse()` with a call to the new `early_scan_options()` function, passing it a list of options (`--default`, `--prefix`, `--resolve-git-dir`) that take their values as separate arguments. This ensures that when one of these options is given `--` as its value, the scanner correctly skips over it rather than treating it as the separator. The fix is minimal, idiomatic, and comes with a new test in `t/t1500-rev-parse.sh` that verifies the error message is now the expected "ambiguous argument" diagnostic.
