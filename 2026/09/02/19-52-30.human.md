This is the final piece of a two-patch series that refines how `git checkout -m` presents autostash conflicts to the user. The patch adds a single blank line between the conflict-resolution advice and the "Switched to branch ..." confirmation message, making the two visually distinct without changing any underlying logic. Junio C Hamano has reviewed this version and considers it ready.

The change is small and targeted: it modifies only `builtin/checkout.c` to check the return value from `apply_autostash_ref()` (now an `enum stash_apply_result`) and print a newline to `stderr` only when conflicts occur. The accompanying test in `t/t7201-co.sh` validates the new output format, ensuring the blank line appears exactly where intended. This is purely an ergonomic improvement, addressing a minor but real source of visual clutter for users who encounter conflicts during `git checkout -m`.

The patch is uncontroversial and complements the first patch in the series, which aligned exit codes with Git’s established conventions. All prior review feedback has been addressed, and Junio’s "looks good to me" signals the series is ready for merging. No further iterations are expected.
