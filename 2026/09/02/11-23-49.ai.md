This is Karthik Nayak’s reply to Junio C Hamano’s comment on the refactoring patch in the `receive-report` hook series. The patch introduces a `generate_report()` helper with a boolean parameter (`detailed_report`) to control whether the v2 protocol’s detailed per-command status is included.

Karthik acknowledges Junio’s concern that the boolean name (`detailed_report`) might mislead future developers into treating it as a general "detail level" knob rather than a protocol-version switch. He agrees that an `enum` (e.g., `receive_pack_report_v0` vs `receive_pack_report_v2`) would better reflect the protocol-level distinction, though he shares Junio’s view that it might be overkill. He commits to incorporating the change if he rerolls the series, but no reroll is imminent—the series is already queued in `seen` and ready for `next`.

The exchange is a minor design note rather than a blocking issue. Junio’s suggestion is substantive but non-urgent, and Karthik’s response leaves the door open for a future update without derailing the current integration. The broader series remains on track, with all prior feedback addressed and no outstanding objections.
