This is a brief follow-up in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series, which graduated to `next` in July and is now awaiting final merge to `master`. Junio responds to the recent discussion between Jeff King and D. Ben Knoble about the `#ifndef NO_NSEC` guard in `environment.c`.

Junio acknowledges the series is in "great shape" but suggests one last cosmetic improvement: replacing the remaining `#ifndef NO_NSEC` checks in the codebase with calls to `ST_XTIME_NSEC(st)`, which already encapsulates the build-time difference. This would further reduce preprocessor clutter without changing behavior. He also reiterates a deeper architectural concern he raised earlier: the new call to `repo_config_values()` in `is_racy_stat()` could trigger a `BUG()` when operating on a repository other than `the_repository`. While he does not block the series, he suggests adopting a safe default (e.g., treating `core.useNanosec` as false) when `istate->repo` differs from `the_repository` to avoid future issues.

The tone is constructive and forward-looking. Junio’s suggestion about `ST_XTIME_NSEC` is a minor cleanup, while the `repo_config_values()` concern is a known limitation that the author has already acknowledged. No new technical problems are introduced, and the series remains on track for `master`. The next step is likely a small reroll from the author incorporating Junio’s cosmetic suggestion, unless the maintainer decides to make the change himself during integration.
