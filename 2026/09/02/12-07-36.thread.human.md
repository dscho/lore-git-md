This thread is about a simple but important bugfix for the `git history` command. The patch, posted by zkd18cjb@mail.ustc.edu.cn, prevents a crash when `git history split` encounters a missing tree object (e.g., due to repository corruption or incomplete restore). The fix adds a NULL check in `write_ondisk_index()` to error out gracefully instead of dereferencing a NULL pointer.

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the patch and called it "obviously good," though he noted minor nits: the commit message could drop redundant UBSan details, and the author's email address doesn't match the `Signed-off-by` trailer. Neither affects the fix's correctness, and Patrick dismissed the idea of adding a test as unnecessary. The patch is minimal, uncontroversial, and follows Git's established patterns for handling unloadable objects. It’s now ready to be merged once the trivial nits are addressed. No further discussion or follow-up is expected unless another reviewer raises a concern.
