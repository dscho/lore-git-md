This thread is the second iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series refactoring Git’s codebase to remove implicit dependencies on the global `the_repository` variable. The first patch, which just arrived, mechanically updates `cache_tree_fully_valid()` to accept a `struct index_state *` instead of hard-coding `the_repository->objects`. This change is small and uncontroversial: it touches seven files (six callers plus the core implementation) and introduces no behavior change, but it ensures that the object database used for validation always matches the repository that owns the index. The rest of the series is expected to follow the same pattern—each patch will make a single, focused change to eliminate another implicit use of `the_repository`. So far, there are no reviews or objections; the thread is just getting started.
