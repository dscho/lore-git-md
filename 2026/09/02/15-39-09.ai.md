This is a **refactoring patch** (v4 1/5) in a **bugfix series** targeting the merge machinery. The patch renames `FROM_REBASE_PICK` to `FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` and `is_from_rebase()` to `is_from_rebase_now_empty()` to clarify that these symbols refer to commits that *became* empty during a rebase, rather than being empty from the start. The change is purely mechanical, with no functional or behavioral impact, and updates all call sites in `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h`.

The patch directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s stylistic feedback from earlier iterations, where he noted that the original names did not clearly convey that the commit *became* empty. The new names make this distinction explicit, aligning with the series’ broader goal of preventing unsafe commit operations (`git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`) during conflict resolution. Phillip Wood’s review confirms the improvement, calling the new names "much clearer."

This is the first of five patches in the series, which is **technically complete and ready for integration**. The series extends Git’s existing protections to cover `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`, and has received explicit approval from Junio for all patches. No substantive objections or requests for changes remain. The patch is uncontroversial and serves as a preparatory step for the functional changes in later patches. Key files touched: `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h`.
