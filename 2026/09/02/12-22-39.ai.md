This is a **bugfix** targeting the pathspec-matching subsystem. The patch addresses a buffer overflow in `match_pathspec_with_flags()` that occurs when a negative pathspec (marked with `PATHSPEC_EXCLUDE`) is shorter than the common prefix derived from positive pathspecs. The bug causes `match_pathspec_item()` to read out-of-bounds memory, potentially leading to crashes or undefined behavior.

The fix is minimal: it passes a zero prefix (`0`) instead of the derived `prefix` when matching negative pathspecs, ensuring the function does not attempt to advance beyond the allocation. The commit includes a regression test that reproduces the issue by combining a long positive pathspec (creating a deep prefix) with a short, unrelated negative pathspec. The test verifies that `git ls-files` handles the case correctly without crashing.
