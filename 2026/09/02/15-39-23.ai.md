This is a **surface-level review** of the third patch in Elijah Newren’s v4 bugfix series (5 patches total). The patch rewords an error message in `builtin/commit.c` to clarify why `git commit --amend` is unsafe after a rebase drops an empty commit, replacing the generic *"You are in the middle of a rebase -- cannot amend."* with *"The now-empty commit has been dropped -- cannot amend."*

Phillip Wood’s reply is brief and confirms the new message is clearer, offering no substantive feedback or objections. The change itself is uncontroversial: it improves usability by explaining *why* amending is blocked (the commit the user intended to amend was dropped) rather than implying a blanket prohibition during rebase. Junio C Hamano had already approved this wording in an earlier iteration ("Much less awkward than the previous round"), and the patch carries no functional changes beyond the message update.

The patch is ready for integration and aligns with the series’ goal of improving error clarity during conflict resolution. No further discussion or revisions are expected for this specific change. The broader series (extending protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution) remains technically complete and approved, with only a **design question** about UI consistency still open for patch 2/5.
