This is a cleanup patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-part ODB refactoring series. It removes the now-unused `tmp_objdir_add_as_alternate()` function from `tmp-objdir.c` and its declaration from `tmp-objdir.h`. The function became obsolete after commit bdee7b3013 (July 2026) switched `builtin/receive-pack` to use ODB transactions instead of ad-hoc alternate registration.

The patch is straightforward: eleven lines deleted, no behavior changes. It is part of the broader effort to eliminate dual-use of the ODB sources list (alternates vs. temporary in-memory sources) and prepare for future alternate-handling improvements. This specific change is uncontroversial and ready for integration; it simply tidies up dead code after its last caller was removed.
