This thread is a two-patch refactoring series by Yuvraj Singh Chauhan, part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The first patch moved the `grafts_keep_true_parents` configuration variable into the repository-specific `repo_config_values` struct, introducing a repository-aware getter function while preserving existing behavior. The second patch, just posted, adds comprehensive test coverage for the `--keep-true-parents` option in `git pack-objects`, verifying its behavior with grafts, the flag’s ability to reveal grafted-over parents, and its handling of shallow commits.

The series is now complete and under initial review, with no feedback yet from other contributors. The tests are well-structured, using separate repositories to isolate scenarios and addressing edge cases like shallow commits that could otherwise lead to undefined behavior. Together, the patches provide both the mechanical refactoring and the safety net needed for the `the_repository` removal effort, though the broader impact of the series remains to be seen pending review.
