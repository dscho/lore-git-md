This is a surface-level review of the first patch in Elijah Newren’s five-part bugfix series (v4 1/5). The patch renames `FROM_REBASE_PICK` to `FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` and `is_from_rebase()` to `is_from_rebase_now_empty()` to clarify that these symbols refer to commits that *became* empty during a rebase, rather than being empty from the start. The change is purely mechanical, updating all call sites in `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h` without altering behavior.

Phillip Wood’s review is brief and positive, calling the new names “much clearer.” This aligns with Junio C Hamano’s earlier feedback, which prompted the rename to better convey the intent. The patch is uncontroversial and serves as a preparatory step for the rest of the series, which extends Git’s existing protections against unsafe commit operations (`git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`) during conflict resolution to cover `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`.

The series as a whole is technically complete, with all substantive feedback addressed in earlier iterations. This patch is ready for integration and has no outstanding objections. If you’re tracking the series, this is a minor but necessary cleanup that won’t affect functionality.
