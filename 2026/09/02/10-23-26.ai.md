This is a reply in the ongoing ODB refactoring series targeting the `the_repository` removal effort. The email is a short follow-up from Patrick Steinhardt to Junio C Hamano’s review of patch 1/12 ("cache-tree: remove dependency on `the_repository`").

Patrick accepts Junio’s suggestion to encapsulate the repository dependency within `cache-tree.c` but proposes a slightly different implementation: instead of introducing a new wrapper `has_valid_cache_tree(struct index_state *)`, he will rename the existing `cache_tree_fully_valid()` to `cache_tree_fully_valid_recursive()` (making it file-scoped static) and adapt the current `cache_tree_fully_valid()` to take a `struct index_state *` directly. This keeps the external interface unchanged while still removing the global dependency. The change is mechanical and does not alter the patch’s core goal of eliminating `the_repository` usage in the cache-tree subsystem.

No new technical concerns are raised; the discussion remains focused on code organization. The series as a whole continues to progress toward removing ad-hoc ODB source linking, with this patch addressing a prerequisite cleanup. The next step is likely a v2 of patch 1/12 incorporating this feedback, after which the rest of the series can proceed.
