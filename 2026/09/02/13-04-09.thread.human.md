This thread is about a bugfix series that corrects a long-standing edge case in Git’s pathspec handling. The issue occurs when an exclude pathspec (like `:!unrelated/path`) appears first in the list, causing `common_prefix_len()` to incorrectly return zero as the common prefix length even when subsequent positive pathspecs (e.g., `foo/bar` and `foo/baz`) share a directory (`foo/`). The series also fixes a memory-safety issue where negative pathspecs shorter than the common prefix derived from positive pathspecs could trigger a heap-buffer-overflow.
