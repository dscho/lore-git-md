This thread is about a bugfix series that fixes a long-standing edge case in Git’s pathspec handling, where an exclude pathspec appearing first in the list could cause `common_prefix_len()` to incorrectly return zero as the common prefix length. The series also addresses a memory-safety issue in `match_pathspec_with_flags()` that could trigger a heap-buffer-overflow when negative pathspecs were shorter than the common prefix derived from positive pathspecs.

The series, now at version 3, has been reviewed and is queued for merging. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has confirmed the integration plan: a preparatory patch (making `match` and `original` in `struct pathspec_item` `const char *`) will be queued independently, and a synthetic merge base will be created for the two-patch series (memory-safety fix and core `common_prefix_len()` refactor). No further technical changes or author action are needed; the only remaining step is Junio’s integration work.

Yannik Tausch, the author, has followed up to ask whether any further action is required on their part. The answer is no—Junio has already stated that the author does not need to resend the series as three patches, and the integration is in progress. The series is effectively ready to merge, with no open technical or design questions. The only loose end is an unexplained phone number in an earlier reply, which remains irrelevant to the patch.
