This thread is about a race condition in Git’s `rerere` subsystem, where background maintenance (triggered by the geometric maintenance strategy in Git 2.54) clashes with an in-progress rebase, causing lock contention and rebase failures. The original patch proposed a timeout-based fix, but after substantive review from Phillip Wood, the author, Thomas Bachem, has shifted to a two-part solution: disabling background maintenance during rebase via config overrides (`-c gc.auto=false -c maintenance.auto=false`) and fixing error handling in `rerere_setup()` to propagate errors instead of crashing.

The revised approach addresses the root cause by preventing the background task from running during interactive operations, rather than mitigating lock contention after the fact. Thomas’s responses show a clear grasp of the tradeoffs, and the new plan aligns with Phillip’s critique, making it more likely to gain maintainer approval. The upcoming v2 will touch `sequencer.c` and `rerere.c`, with tests adapted to verify the new behavior. While Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt haven’t weighed in yet, the direction is now more architecturally sound. This is a key thread for anyone tracking the sequencer, maintenance subsystem, or `rerere` internals.
