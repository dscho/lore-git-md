This thread is about a new `parse-options` sub-API designed to fix argument-parsing bugs where early hand-rolled scans fail to handle option values that appear as separate arguments. Christian Couder’s six-patch series introduces `early_scan_options()` and converts three commands—`git bisect`, `git rev-parse`, and `git fast-import`—to use it, addressing concrete misparsing issues. The `git fast-import` fix is widely acknowledged as a real problem, but Junio C Hamano has raised substantive concerns about the series’ design and scope, particularly questioning the practical value of the `git bisect` and `git rev-parse` fixes.

The series is currently under review, with Junio’s latest feedback pushing back specifically on the `git bisect` fix. While he doesn’t dispute the correctness of the implementation, he doubts whether users are likely to encounter the edge case (`git bisect start --term-good -- hello`) in real workflows, suggesting the patch may not justify the new API’s introduction. This narrows the discussion to whether the series should focus solely on the `git fast-import` bug or include the other fixes. Junio has also previously critiqued the API’s long-term viability, arguing that even a simplified early scanner could use the existing `struct option[]` array to reduce technical debt. The series is not yet ready for integration and will likely require at least one more iteration to address these concerns.

Key technical details include new functions (`early_scan_options()`, `find_early_scan_option()`), types (`struct early_scan_option`), and macros (`EARLY_SCAN_WANT`, `EARLY_SCAN_SKIP_VALUE`), along with thorough test coverage for the bugs and API. The implementation is clean and well-tested, but Junio’s feedback highlights inconsistencies in how option values are handled and hints at potential alignment issues with future `parse_options()` refactoring. Christian’s position remains that the series is a well-scoped, minimal solution to a real problem, but Junio’s skepticism may force a narrower focus or further design revisions.
