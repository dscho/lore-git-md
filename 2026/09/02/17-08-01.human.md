This is a minor documentation follow-up to the already merged `gitdatamodel(7)` cross-referencing series. Julia Evans and Kristoffer Haugsbakk are discussing two small stylistic tweaks to Patch 4/4, which added reciprocal links between `gitdatamodel(7)` and `gitglossary(7)`.

The first suggestion is to remove the inline cross-reference ("See linkgit:gitglossary[7] for a comprehensive terminology reference") from the introductory paragraph of `gitdatamodel(7)` and rely solely on the existing "SEE ALSO" section. Julia initially missed the link in "SEE ALSO" but now agrees dropping the inline reference is fine. The second suggestion is to simplify the phrasing "comprehensive terminology reference" to something less aspirational, such as "terminology reference" or "general terminology reference". Kristoffer is open to alternatives but notes the adjective was intended to contrast the roles of the two pages—`gitglossary(7)` covers all of Git's terminology, while `gitdatamodel(7)` focuses on core concepts.

The discussion is purely about wording and presentation, with no disagreement on the substance of the patch or the series' goal of improving `gitdatamodel(7)` discoverability. Since the series is already in `next`, any follow-up would likely be a small incremental patch rather than a resend. The tone is collaborative and low-stakes, reflecting routine documentation polish.
