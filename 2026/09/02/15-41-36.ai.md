This is the **v4 iteration of a bugfix series** targeting the merge machinery, now fully approved and ready for integration. Phillip Wood’s reply to the cover letter confirms the series is in its final form: he expresses mixed feelings about patch 2/5 (which removes the restriction on `git commit <paths>` during conflict resolution for empty commits) but is otherwise happy with the changes. This is the only remaining point of discussion, and it is not a blocker—Phillip’s feedback is a surface-level observation rather than a substantive objection.

The series extends Git’s existing protection against unsafe commit operations (`git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`) during conflict resolution to cover `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`. The only change since v3 is a purely mechanical rename in patch 1/5: `FROM_REBASE_EMPTY` becomes `FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY`, `is_from_rebase_empty()` becomes `is_from_rebase_now_empty()`, and `ONGOING_REBASE_EMPTY` becomes `ONGOING_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY`. This addresses Junio C Hamano’s stylistic feedback about whether the original names adequately conveyed that the commit *became* empty (rather than being empty from the start). The rename is applied consistently across `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h`, with no functional changes.

Key technical details remain unchanged from v3:
- **Files touched**: `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, `sequencer.h`, `wt-status.h`, and test coverage in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`, `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, `t4151-am-abort.sh`, and `t3403-rebase-skip.sh`.
- **New behavior**: blocks `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` during conflict resolution for `git am`, `git revert`, and all `git rebase` variants, except for interactive rebase directives (`squash`, `reword`, `edit`, `break`). The restriction on `git commit <paths>` for empty commits has been removed (patch 2/5), as the result is still an empty commit and thus harmless.
- **New symbols**: `enum ongoing_operation` and `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` consolidate logic for detecting active operations, distinguishing between conflict stops (marked by `stopped-sha` in `rebase-merge/`) and clean interactive rebase stops (marked by `amend`).
- **Error messages**: "The now-empty commit has been dropped -- cannot amend." (for `--amend` after an empty commit is dropped), "cannot amend while resolving conflicts" (for `--amend` during conflict resolution), and "cannot do a partial commit while resolving conflicts" (for `<paths>` during conflict resolution).

The series has received explicit approval from Junio C Hamano for all substantive changes, including the error message rewording ("Much less awkward than the previous round") and the structural improvements in patch 5/5. Phillip Wood’s review of v4 confirms the new names are "much clearer" and that patches 3/5 and 4/5 are ready for integration. The only open question—why `git commit <paths>` is allowed during rebase for empty commits but blocked during cherry-pick—was raised by Phillip in his review of patch 2/5 and remains unanswered, but it does not affect the series’ readiness for `next`. The series aligns with the project’s ongoing efforts to improve user safety during conflict resolution and is likely to proceed to integration in the near term.
