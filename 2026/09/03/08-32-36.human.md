This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about fixing a `rerere` lock race that can crash or deadlock rebases. Patrick Steinhardt chimes in to endorse Thomas Bachem’s planned v2 approach: a bounded wait (up to `rerere.lockTimeout`, defaulting to 1 second) for non-maintenance callers like `git commit`, while background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) skips the lock entirely with a timeout of 0. He argues that the wait is a sensible preemptive fix for broader race conditions, not just the rebase-specific issue introduced by Git 2.54’s geometric maintenance strategy. The race can also occur with concurrent writers, so the wait is a general robustness improvement.

Patrick also reaffirms his plan to send a separate patch series adjusting `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` to consider garbage-collection eligibility, which should reduce the frequency of `rerere gc` runs and make the rebase-specific config overrides less critical. He remains agnostic about whether to disable auto-maintenance entirely during the sequencer, suggesting that discussion belongs in a separate thread. The tone is collaborative, with both contributors aligning on the technical direction for v2. No new objections are raised, and the focus is on implementation details rather than revisiting settled design choices.
