This is the first patch in a three-patch bugfix series targeting Git's pathspec handling. It is a preparatory refactoring originally authored by Junio C Hamano and now integrated into the series by Yannik Tausch. The patch declares the `match` and `original` fields in `struct pathspec_item` as `const char *` to reflect their immutability after initialization, and updates `clear_pathspec()` to cast away constness when freeing these strings.

The change is purely mechanical and touches only `pathspec.c` and `pathspec.h`. It resolves a compiler warning in the unit test added by the later patches in the series, where the test initializes these fields with string literals. No behavior change or new functionality is introduced; this is a hygiene improvement to make the code's intent clearer. The patch carries Junio's Signed-off-by followed by Yannik's, indicating the integration into the series.

This refactoring is uncontroversial and has already been reviewed and agreed upon in the thread. It is a prerequisite for the subsequent memory-safety and correctness fixes, which rely on the immutability guarantee to avoid introducing new warnings. The series as a whole is now ready for merging, with this patch serving as the foundation for the two that follow.
