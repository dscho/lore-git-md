This is a bugfix patch targeting the `rerere` subsystem, part of an ongoing two-patch series to fix a race condition between rebase and background maintenance. The author, Thomas Bachem, is finalizing the approach after feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Phillip Wood.

The patch retains a bounded wait (up to 1 second) for `rerere` lock acquisition during normal operations like `git commit`, while background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) skips the lock entirely. This avoids the crash introduced in Git 2.54 by the geometric maintenance strategy’s aggressive triggering of `rerere gc` after every commit. The fix is now scoped to the `rerere` locking logic alone; the sequencer-side config overrides (disabling maintenance during rebase) will be sent as a separate patch, as the wait makes them redundant for the immediate crash.

The implementation is settled: unified skip/timeout logic, `LOCK_REPORT_ON_ERROR` for better error messages, and a default 1-second wait that covers most pruning operations. The patch is based on `maint` and conflicts trivially with a recent lockfile helper refactoring; the resolution is straightforward. Tests pass, and the author plans to reroll after Phillip Wood’s review. The geometric maintenance strategy’s defaults will be addressed separately by Patrick Steinhardt.
