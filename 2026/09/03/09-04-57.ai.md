This is a refactoring patch in the `rerere` subsystem, part of a two-patch series by Patrick Steinhardt. The patch extracts the logic that determines whether a rerere entry is stale into two new helper functions: `rerere_gc_cutoffs()` (which computes the expiration thresholds for resolved and unresolved entries) and `rerere_id_is_stale()` (which applies those thresholds to a specific entry). The immediate motivation is code reuse: a subsequent patch will introduce a second caller for this logic. The changes are mechanical and confined to `rerere.c`, moving existing logic into new functions without altering behavior. The patch is well-structured, with clear commit message and function-level comments, and appears to be a straightforward preparation step for the next patch in the series.
