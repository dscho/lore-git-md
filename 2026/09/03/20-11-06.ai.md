This is a maintainer clarification on Git's policy regarding AI-assisted contributions, posted in response to Aleksei Sviridkin's v1 patch for the `--force-if-includes` uninitialized variable bug. Junio C Hamano explains that while the `Assisted-by:` trailer itself is not prohibited, the specific attribution "LLM" is uninformative and therefore unwelcome. He directs Aleksei to Git's `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` section on AI, which explicitly rejects content that appears AI-generated, sounds overly formal, or cannot be explained by the sender. The policy emphasizes responsible use of AI as a step-by-step guide or debugging aid rather than a source of copy-pasted solutions. This reply does not revisit the technical substance of the patch (the `timestamp_t date = 0` fix remains unchanged in v2) but clarifies project norms for future contributors who may consider AI assistance. The tone is factual and advisory, not punitive.
