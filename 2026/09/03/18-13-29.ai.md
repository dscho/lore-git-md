This is a preparatory refactoring patch from Junio C Hamano, addressing the compiler warning in Yannik Tausch’s v2 bugfix series for pathspec handling. The patch declares the `match` and `original` fields in `struct pathspec_item` as `const char *` to reflect that these strings are never modified after creation, and updates `clear_pathspec()` to cast away constness when freeing them. This change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior, but it enables the unit test in `t/unit-tests/u-dir.c` to initialize `.match` with a string literal without triggering a discarded-qualifiers warning. The patch touches `pathspec.c` and `pathspec.h` and is a prerequisite for merging the second patch in Yannik’s series, which remains otherwise ready for integration. The refactoring aligns with the project’s preference for const-correctness and is uncontroversial.
