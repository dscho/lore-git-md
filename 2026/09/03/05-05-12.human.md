This is the third iteration of a seven-patch series extending `` `git log -L` `` to support range-scoped diff stat formats (`` `--stat` ``, `` `--numstat` ``, `` `--shortstat` ``), whitespace checking (`` `--check` ``), and the `` `-G` `` pickaxe. The key improvement in v3 is a significant rewrite of the prose throughout the series to remove verbosity and extraneous detail, replacing block comments with more descriptive field and variable names. The test implementations have also been pared down and simplified. There are no logical behavior changes from v2—this is purely a clarity and maintainability update.

The series builds on the recent `` `mm/line-log-cleanup` `` topic, which integrated `` `-L` `` with the standard log output pipeline and added support for non-patch formats like `` `--raw` ``, `` `--name-only` ``, and `` `--summary` ``. With this series, `` `-L` `` now supports nearly all common diff options, making it a more versatile tool for focused code history inspection. The only major exclusions are `` `--dirstat` `` (which measures whole-file churn and cannot meaningfully scope to line ranges) and `` `-S` `` (which operates on whole blobs and is left unchanged for now).

The first four patches are preparatory cleanup and refactoring, while the last three implement the new features. The series touches `` `diff.c` ``, `` `diffcore-pickaxe.c` ``, `` `revision.c` ``, and `` `xdiff-interface.c` ``, along with updates to the line-range documentation and extensive test coverage in `` `t/t4211-line-log.sh` ``.
