To maintain compatibility with the existing `free()` calls in `clear_pathspec()`, the patch casts the pointers to `void *` when freeing them.

The change is mechanical and safe, touching only `pathspec.h` and `pathspec.c`. It does not alter behavior but makes the code’s intent clearer and prevents accidental modification of these strings. This refactoring directly resolves the compiler warning in the unit test from Yannik’s series, where a string literal was assigned to a non-`const` field. The patch is well-motivated and uncontroversial, and it keeps the codebase consistent with the principle that immutable strings should be declared `const`.
