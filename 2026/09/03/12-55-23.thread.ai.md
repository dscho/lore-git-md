Core problem or goal
Document the long-standing but undocumented edge case in `git cherry-pick --no-commit` where `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` is *not* created when the operation fails due to conflicts, contrasting with `git revert --no-commit` (which *does* write `REVERT_HEAD`). The goal is to clarify this asymmetry for users, explain the implications for `git commit` (e.g., requiring `git commit -c <commit>` to preserve authorship for single-commit picks), and defend the existing design against potential misinterpretation. The behavior was introduced in 2011 (commit d7e5c0cbfb) and is confirmed as intentional by Phillip Wood, but it remained undocumented until now.
