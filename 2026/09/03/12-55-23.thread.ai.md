Core problem or goal
Document and test the long-standing but undocumented edge case in `git cherry-pick --no-commit` where `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` is *not* created when the operation fails due to conflicts, contrasting with `git revert --no-commit` (which *does* write `REVERT_HEAD`). The goal is to clarify this asymmetry for users, ensure the behavior is explicitly covered by tests, and defend the existing design against potential scrutiny. The behavior was introduced in 2011 (commit d7e5c0cbfb) and is confirmed as intentional, but it remains undocumented, leaving it vulnerable to misinterpretation.

Current status
Under review; v1 posted with anticipation of a v2 for the test patch (part 1). Junio’s initial reply misinterpreted the patch as proposing a behavioral change, but Phillip Wood’s reviews have clarified that the current behavior is intentional and correct. Patrick Steinhardt’s review of part 1 questions the necessity of the test, arguing it adds maintenance overhead without clear justification. Phillip Wood’s latest review proposes a tighter integration of the test with an existing conflict-advice test to improve robustness and avoid redundancy. The documentation patch (part 2) remains unchallenged but is now implicitly tied to the broader discussion about whether the test is justified and whether its design meets maintainability standards. No prior versions of the series exist.
