Core problem or goal
Document and test the long-standing but undocumented edge case in `git cherry-pick --no-commit` where `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` is *not* created when the operation fails due to conflicts, contrasting with `git revert --no-commit` (which *does* write `REVERT_HEAD`). The goal is to clarify this asymmetry for users, ensure the behavior is explicitly covered by tests, and defend the existing design against potential scrutiny. The behavior was introduced in 2011 (commit d7e5c0cbfb) but never documented, leaving it vulnerable to misinterpretation.
