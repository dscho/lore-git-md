This is a review reply in an ongoing bugfix thread targeting the `rerere` subsystem. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Thomas Bachem’s patch that introduces a `rerere.lockTimeout` configuration to prevent background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) from killing an in-progress rebase when both try to acquire the `MERGE_RR.lock`.

Patrick’s review is substantive. He agrees the race condition is real and the timeout mechanism sensible, but suggests two improvements. First, he proposes tweaking the default `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` threshold to reduce how aggressively `git rerere gc` is triggered—specifically, to check whether entries would actually be garbage-collected rather than just counting them. He plans to send a separate patch series for this. Second, he critiques the lockfile error handling: the patch should use `LOCK_REPORT_ON_ERROR` instead of a custom warning, and the timeout and skip-locked branches can be unified by setting timeout=0 when `RERERE_SKIP_LOCKED` is passed.

The review does not object to the overall direction but tightens the implementation details. No merge status is reported; the thread remains under review.
