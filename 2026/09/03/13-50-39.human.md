This is a bugfix patch discussion targeting the `rerere` subsystem. The thread focuses on resolving a race condition between background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) and an in-progress rebase, which can cause the rebase to fail when the `MERGE_RR.lock` is held. The fix is now split into two parts: a locking patch (v2 posted) and a deferred sequencer config-override patch to disable maintenance during rebase.

Phillip Wood’s latest reply clarifies his position on the proposed solution. He agrees with Patrick Steinhardt and Thomas Bachem that the locking patch’s 1-second timeout is a reasonable compromise, though he initially opposed it as a standalone fix. His primary concern was that the timeout alone would not prevent rebase failures if the lock was held longer than the timeout period. However, he now accepts it as part of a broader solution, especially since the sequencer patch (disabling maintenance during rebase) and Patrick’s planned changes to the geometric maintenance strategy’s heuristic will reduce the likelihood of such races.

Phillip also reiterates his view that background maintenance—particularly garbage collection—is not useful during a rebase, as all objects and `rerere` entries created during the rebase are too fresh to be collected. He argues that disabling auto-maintenance during rebase is a pragmatic approach, though he acknowledges that repacking *might* be beneficial for very large rebases. His stance is that the project should disable maintenance during rebase by default and revisit the decision if users complain.

The discussion is now largely settled on the technical approach. The locking patch (v2) is ready for review, while the sequencer patch remains deferred. Phillip’s feedback has been incorporated, and the remaining open question is whether Junio will weigh in on the revised approach. The patch looks well-motivated and addresses a real issue introduced in Git 2.54, making it worth attention from reviewers.
