This thread is about a bugfix for a performance regression in Git’s `pack-objects` subsystem, specifically affecting partial clones. Aleksei Sviridkin identified and fixed a logic error in `prefetch_to_pack()` that caused inefficient fetching of missing objects when loose objects were present locally. The bug, introduced in commit `e00549aa9b`, forced individual fetches for each missing object instead of batching them, leading to measurable slowdowns in synthetic benchmarks (8 fetches reduced to 1 after the fix).

The patch is minimal and targeted, modifying only `builtin/pack-objects.c` and adding a test (`t5300-pack-object.sh`) to verify the fix under both SHA-1 and SHA-256. The commit message is unusually detailed, explaining the root cause, benchmark results, and rejected alternatives. While the issue may be more pronounced in edge cases, it addresses a real pain point for partial clones. So far, there’s no controversy or review feedback—this appears to be a straightforward, well-motivated fix.
