This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s two-patch series to improve the `git maintenance` heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. The series replaces the hard-coded 60-day cutoff with a dynamic heuristic that estimates stale `rerere` entries and triggers the task only when the estimate meets or exceeds a configurable threshold (default: 512). The goal is to reduce unnecessary invocations, lower overhead, and fix a long-standing race condition reported in GitGitGadget PR 2214.

The series is under active review, with v1 posted and no prior versions. Key changes include new functions (`rerere_entry_is_stale()`, `rerere_gc_estimate()`), a behavioral shift to a sampling-based heuristic (using a "17" prefix and scaling by 256), and updated test coverage in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`. The configuration `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` now defines the minimum estimated stale entries required to trigger the task, with negative values forcing the task and zero disabling it.

Derrick Stolee and Thomas Bachem have provided substantive feedback. Thomas tested the series on real-world and synthetic cases, confirming the heuristic works as intended and adding his `Tested-by`. Stolee reviewed both patches, suggesting minor stylistic improvements (e.g., clarifying ternary logic in `rerere_gc_condition()` and considering a rename for `rerere_gc_estimate()`) but no blocking issues. He concluded with "Both patches LGTM," signaling readiness for integration. The only open question—a non-blocking refactoring suggestion from Stolee on patch 1—has been resolved in favor of the current implementation. The series is now awaiting maintainer action.
