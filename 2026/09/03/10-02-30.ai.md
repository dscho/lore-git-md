This is the v2 cover letter for a two-patch bugfix series targeting Git's pathspec handling in directory traversal. The series fixes a long-standing edge case where an exclude pathspec (e.g., `:!unrelated/path`) appearing first in the list causes `common_prefix_len()` to incorrectly return zero as the common prefix length, even when subsequent positive pathspecs (e.g., `foo/bar` and `foo/baz`) share a directory (`foo/`).

The v2 iteration adopts Junio C Hamano's feedback to replace the v1 `pathspec_prefix` struct with an output-parameter approach (`common_prefix_len(pathspec, &matched_prefix)`), minimizing caller churn and aligning with Git's existing C conventions. The series is now split into two patches: the first ensures exclude pathspecs are matched against the full pathname (not the common prefix), while the second refactors `common_prefix_len()` to correctly derive the common prefix from the first *non-exclude* pathspec item. Both patches touch `dir.c` and add test coverage—one in `t/t6132-pathspec-exclude.sh` (functional test) and the other in `t/unit-tests/u-dir.c` (unit test).

The implementation details of the output-parameter approach (e.g., `char **` vs. `const char **`) remain unresolved, but the core design is settled. The series is narrowly scoped to pathspec handling and directory traversal, with no broader subsystem changes. Test coverage for the edge case is thorough, and the commit messages clearly explain the bug and fix. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, but the discussion with Junio suggests the series is converging toward readiness. The patches are well-motivated and address a real (if niche) bug with minimal risk of regression.
