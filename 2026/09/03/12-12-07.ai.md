This is the third patch iteration in a bugfix series targeting the `rerere` subsystem. Thomas Bachem confirms the final design for the locking changes: `rerere_setup()` will wait up to the default 1-second `rerere.lockTimeout` for non-maintenance callers (e.g., `git commit`), while background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) skips the lock entirely (timeout=0). The unified skip/timeout logic and `LOCK_REPORT_ON_ERROR` improvements suggested by Patrick Steinhardt are now settled and incorporated.

The series is now split: the `rerere.c` locking patch will proceed as v2, while the sequencer config-override patch (disabling maintenance during rebase) will be sent separately. Thomas argues the sequencer patch no longer provides meaningful benefit beyond the locking fix, since the 1-second wait allows rebases to proceed after maintenance completes. The only remaining question is whether rebases using the merge backend should run maintenance at all—a topic deferred to a future discussion.

Technical details for follow-up: the patch is based on `maint` and conflicts with a recent lockfile helper refactoring (`d43f701d32`). The resolution is straightforward (adopt the repo-scoped helper). Thomas plans to reroll after Phillip Wood’s review. The geometric maintenance strategy’s aggressiveness (triggering `rerere gc` after every commit) remains the root cause, but Patrick Steinhardt’s planned follow-up series will address that separately.
