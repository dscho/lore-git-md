This is the fifth iteration of a two-patch series refining how `git checkout -m` handles autostash conflicts. The series is now uncontroversial and queued for merging to `next`. Harald Nordgren addresses the last round of feedback—improved commit messages and a helper function for stash application—without reopening any settled design questions.

The goal is modest but user-facing: when `git checkout -m` applies an autostash and hits conflicts, the conflict advice now appears on its own line, separated from the subsequent “Switched to branch …” message. This eliminates visual clutter while preserving the existing conflict-resolution workflow. Under the hood, the series also reserves exit code 1 exclusively for conflicts (aligning with `git merge-tree`), uses other non-zero codes for errors, and documents the convention in `git-stash.adoc`.

Files touched include `sequencer.c` (new helper `do_stash_apply` and return-value semantics), `builtin/checkout.c` (advice formatting), `builtin/stash.c` (exit-code mapping), `stash.h` (new `enum stash_apply_result`), and tests in `t/t3903-stash.sh` and `t/t7201-co.sh`. The enum cleanly encodes the three outcomes—clean apply, conflict, or error—so callers can distinguish them without parsing exit codes.

Junio and Phillip Wood have both signed off on the design and implementation; the only changes in v5 are cosmetic (commit-message wording and a small helper function). The series looks ready for integration.
