This is a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt on Thomas Bachem’s patch addressing the `rerere` lock race during rebase. The review engages deeply with the patch’s technical approach and suggests improvements to both the locking logic and the broader maintenance strategy.

Patrick’s key points are twofold. First, he identifies that the root issue is not just the race condition but the overly aggressive triggering of `git rerere gc` by the geometric maintenance strategy. He proposes adjusting `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` to be less aggressive, considering whether entries would actually be garbage-collected rather than just counting them. He plans to send a separate patch series to address this.

Second, he critiques the locking implementation in the patch. He agrees that a locking timeout is sensible but suggests using `LOCK_REPORT_ON_ERROR` instead of silently skipping when the lock is held, so the lockfile machinery can provide more accurate error reporting. He also proposes simplifying the code by combining the `RERERE_SKIP_LOCKED` and timeout branches, using a timeout of 0 for the skip case.

The review is constructive and forward-looking, acknowledging the patch’s intent while pushing for a cleaner and more robust solution. It also signals that the geometric maintenance strategy’s defaults may need broader reconsideration beyond this specific race condition. No merge status is implied; this remains under active discussion.
