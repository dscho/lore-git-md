This thread is about a one-line bugfix to `git push --force-if-includes` that prevents an uninitialized variable from causing premature termination of a reflog walk, which could allow unsafe force-pushes in fresh clones. Aleksei Sviridkin identified the issue—a build-dependent bug introduced in 2020—and provided a minimal fix (`timestamp_t date = 0` in `remote.c`) along with a new test. Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch, confirmed the fix was correct, and requested only non-technical tweaks (removing the `Assisted-by: LLM` trailer and adjusting test cleanup placement), which were incorporated in v2.

The thread also includes a clarification from Junio on Git’s policy regarding AI-assisted contributions. While the project doesn’t outright ban AI tools, it rejects uninformative attributions like "LLM" and expects contributors to fully understand and explain any AI-generated content. This guidance is aimed at future contributors rather than a critique of Aleksei’s patch, which remains unchanged in substance. The bug itself is now fully addressed, with no open technical questions, and the patch is ready for integration.
