This is the first patch in the v6 series introducing the `receive-report` hook. It is a **documentation patch** that adds a missing section about the existing `proc-receive` hook to `git-receive-pack.adoc`.

The change is straightforward: it adds an eight-line `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section explaining that the hook is invoked when `receive.procReceiveRefs` is configured and matching commands are sent, and that the hook (rather than `execute_commands()`) is responsible for updating references and reporting results. The patch was helped by Patrick Steinhardt and addresses a gap in the man page where the `proc-receive` hook was previously undocumented. This patch has already been applied in earlier iterations; no new feedback or changes are introduced here. The context for the series (GitLab’s MVCC use case, the consistency trade-off, and the hook’s design) is documented in the cover letter and later patches, not in this one.
