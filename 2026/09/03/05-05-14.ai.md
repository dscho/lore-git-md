This is the second patch in Michael Montalbo’s v3 series extending `git log -L` to support range-scoped diff stat, whitespace checking, and `-G` pickaxe. It is a **bugfix and refactoring** targeting the line-range filter in `diff.c`.

The patch simplifies the line-range filter by removing the separate “pending removal” buffer and classifying preimage removal lines immediately based on the postimage line number. This eliminates a bug where deletions just outside the tracked range could leak into the output if they immediately followed an in-range postimage line. The fix is achieved by construction: preimage lines are now stored directly in the accumulating hunk’s line buffer, and the filter flushes them based solely on whether the postimage lines fall within the target range. The patch also defers calculation of the old and new line counts for the hunk header until flush time, further reducing state management complexity.

The test added to `t/t4211-line-log.sh` verifies that the bug is fixed: a deletion just outside the tracked range (a trailing comment) is no longer included in the patch output when the tracked function is modified. The test is minimal and focused, covering only the specific regression scenario.
