This is a **bugfix patch discussion** targeting the `rerere` and sequencer subsystems, specifically addressing a race condition between background maintenance (`git rerere gc`) and an in-progress rebase. The thread has converged on a two-patch solution: (1) fixing error handling in `rerere_setup()` to propagate errors instead of dying, and (2) disabling auto-maintenance during rebase via config overrides (`-c gc.auto=false -c maintenance.auto=false`).

In this latest exchange, Phillip Wood and Thomas Bachem finalize the approach. Phillip confirms that the merge backend should run maintenance at the end of a rebase (matching the apply backend’s behavior) and agrees that `rerere_setup()` should return errors rather than dying, allowing the rebase to stop gracefully at conflicts. Thomas notes that this aligns with existing behavior—errors from `repo_rerere()` are already ignored, and the merge result remains in the index/worktree for `--continue` to handle.

The discussion leaves one open question: whether to add a single maintenance run at the end of the rebase sequence. Thomas leans toward keeping the change minimal, while Phillip’s preference for consistency between backends suggests it may be added. The patch quality appears high: the revised approach addresses the root cause (background maintenance interfering with interactive operations) rather than papering over lock contention, and the error-handling improvements are well-motivated. This is worth tracking for anyone interested in the sequencer, maintenance subsystem, or `rerere` internals, as it resolves a user-visible bug introduced in Git 2.54.
