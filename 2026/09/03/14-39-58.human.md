This is the second patch in a now-ready-to-merge series refining how `git checkout -m` presents autostash conflicts. The patch adds a single blank line between the conflict-resolution advice and the branch-switch confirmation message, making the two visually distinct without changing any underlying logic. It touches only `builtin/checkout.c` and the corresponding test in `t/t7201-co.sh`, relying on the new `enum stash_apply_result` introduced in patch 1/2 to detect when conflicts occur. The test now uses `test_cmp` to validate the exact screen output, including the blank line. Junio and Phillip Wood have both signed off on the design and implementation; no further changes are expected.
