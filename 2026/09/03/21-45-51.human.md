This is a bugfix for the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The author, Aleksei Sviridkin, has posted a v2 patch addressing Junio Hamano's review requests from the initial submission. The patch itself is minimal—just one line in `remote.c` initializing an uninitialized `timestamp_t` variable to zero—and remains technically unchanged from v1. The fix ensures the reflog walk covers the entire local branch history when the remote-tracking ref lacks reflog entries, preventing false positives in the "remote ref updated since checkout" check.

The discussion here is a brief policy clarification about AI-assisted contributions.
