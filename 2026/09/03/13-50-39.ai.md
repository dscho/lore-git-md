This is the next round of discussion in the `rerere` lock-race bugfix thread. Phillip Wood replies to Patrick Steinhardt and Thomas Bachem, clarifying his stance on the two-part solution that is now coalescing.

Phillip accepts the bounded wait (up to `rerere.lockTimeout`, default 1 second) for non-maintenance callers like `git commit` as a pragmatic part of the fix. He reiterates that his earlier objection was to *only* using a timeout—without also disabling background maintenance during rebase—because a timeout alone can still fail the rebase if the lock is held longer than the wait period. With the sequencer config-override patch deferred but still planned, and Patrick’s separate series to adjust the geometric maintenance heuristic (`maintenance.rerere-gc.auto`) in the pipeline, Phillip now sees the timeout as a reasonable stopgap that avoids crashes while the deeper fixes are implemented.

He also reaffirms his position that background maintenance should be disabled entirely during rebase, citing recent bugs (including one fixed by Johannes Schindelin) and arguing that garbage collection is useless mid-rebase—all objects and `rr-cache` entries created during the rebase are too fresh to be collected. He proposes disabling auto-maintenance during rebase as a trial, with the option to reintroduce incremental repacking later if users complain. No new technical details or open questions are introduced; the email is a concise endorsement of the current direction.
