This is a **substantive review** from Derrick Stolee on the second patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s two-patch series improving the `git maintenance` heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. The series replaces the hard-coded 60-day cutoff with a dynamic heuristic that estimates stale `rerere` entries and triggers the task only when the estimate meets or exceeds a configurable threshold (default: 512).

Stolee’s review focuses on the implementation details in `builtin/gc.c` and `rerere.c`. He raises minor stylistic preferences but no correctness concerns. Specifically, he suggests that the ternary logic in `rerere_gc_condition()` could be rewritten for clarity to more directly map to the documented behavior (negative values force the task, zero disables it, positive values trigger it based on the estimate). He also muses about renaming `rerere_gc_estimate()` to `rerere_stale_above_limit()` to better reflect its boolean-like usage, though he acknowledges the current name leaves flexibility for future callers that might care about the exact count.

The review notes the value of the short-circuit optimization in `rerere_gc_estimate()` and praises the test coverage. Stolee concludes with "Both patches LGTM," indicating he finds the series ready for integration. This feedback is consistent with his earlier review of the first patch, where he engaged with the refactoring but ultimately accepted the design as clean and necessary for the series’ goals. No blocking issues or edge cases are raised.
