This is a brief follow-up in the exploratory discussion about decoupling Git’s `parsed_object_pool` from the repository instance. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s point that the hash algorithm is intentionally tied to the repository, but questions whether propagating the *entire* repository structure to subsystems is necessary. He suggests that passing only the relevant pieces—such as the hash algorithm and compatibility hash—might be a cleaner approach than exposing the full repository. However, he notes that he ultimately abandoned this idea, likely due to the complexity or churn involved. The exchange remains theoretical and does not propose actionable changes or affect the original bugfix series, which is still under review. The discussion highlights the tension between subsystem isolation and Git’s existing architectural commitments.
