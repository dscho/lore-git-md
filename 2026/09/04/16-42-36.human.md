This is a **contributor housekeeping discussion** about Git’s style enforcement tooling, spinning off from the now-approved `git checkout -m` autostash conflict series. The patches themselves are queued for `next` and no longer under debate; this thread explores whether Git should tighten its `.clang-format` CI job to enforce consistent brace usage and other style rules.

Harald Nordgren and Phillip Wood exchange views on the trade-offs. Harald argues that a fully automated, consistent style would eliminate an entire class of review nits, while Phillip notes that `clang-format`’s output does not perfectly match Git’s style guide and varies across versions, making the current advisory CI job of limited value. Harald suggests version-pinning the tool and eventually applying a full reformatting pass, but acknowledges the disruption it would cause for in-flight topics. The discussion remains exploratory—no policy change or code change is proposed yet.
