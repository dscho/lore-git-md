This is a **substantive review** of the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series refactoring the Git object database (ODB) subsystem. The series removes the ad-hoc mechanism for registering submodule ODBs as in-memory sources, a cleanup that follows earlier patches eliminating the last callers of this infrastructure.

Karthik Nayak’s review confirms the patch’s correctness and completeness. He traces the key changes: the removal of `odb_add_submodule_source_by_path()` (which previously added submodule paths to a string list in `struct object_database`), the deletion of `register_all_submodule_sources()` (which converted those paths into in-memory alternates), and the cleanup of the retry mechanism in `do_oid_object_info_extended()` that triggered when object reads failed. The review also notes the removal of the now-obsolete `GIT_TEST_FATAL_REGISTER_SUBMODULE_ODB` test mode, which was introduced as a transitional safeguard and is no longer needed.

The patch itself is mechanical and uncontroversial. It deletes 78 lines across 10 files, including the `submodule_source_paths` field from `struct object_database` and its associated functions, as well as test script exports of the now-defunct test mode. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the test suite continues to pass. This aligns with the series’ broader goal of simplifying the ODB codebase by eliminating dual-use of the ODB sources list for both alternates and temporary sources.

Karthik’s feedback is thorough but brief, focusing on the patch’s logical flow rather than nitpicking. He explicitly acknowledges the removal of the retry mechanism and the cleanup of the string list, signaling that the patch achieves its stated purpose. There are no open questions or objections raised, and the review does not request any changes.

This patch is a prerequisite for the final removal of the ad-hoc source linking API in patch 13/13. The series as a whole is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort and the broader `the_repository` removal initiative. Junio C Hamano’s earlier substantive approvals of other patches in the series ("Yay. Very nice.") suggest this cleanup is likely to be merged once the full series is reviewed.
