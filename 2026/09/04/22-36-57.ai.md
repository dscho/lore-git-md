This is a **substantive review** of the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series refactoring the Git object database (ODB) subsystem. The patch removes the now-unused infrastructure for registering submodule ODBs as in-memory sources, following the removal of its last callers in earlier patches.

Karthik Nayak’s review confirms the correctness of the cleanup. He traces the patch’s logic, noting that the removed functions (`odb_add_submodule_source_by_path()` and `register_all_submodule_sources()`) were previously used to register submodule ODBs as alternates of `the_repository` and retry object reads when they failed. The review explicitly acknowledges the removal of the retry mechanism in `do_oid_object_info_extended()` and the deletion of the `submodule_source_paths` string list from `struct object_database`. Karthik also observes the removal of the `GIT_TEST_FATAL_REGISTER_SUBMODULE_ODB` test mode, which was introduced as a transitional safeguard and is now obsolete.

The review is substantive because it engages with the patch’s technical rationale and verifies the removal of the infrastructure matches the series’ stated goal: eliminating the ad-hoc source registration mechanism. Karthik’s comments align with the broader ODB refactoring effort, which aims to simplify the codebase and prepare for future alternate-handling improvements. No objections or concerns are raised; the review implicitly endorses the patch as a safe and necessary cleanup step.

This patch touches `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `t/README`, and seven test scripts, removing 78 lines of code. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial, with no behavioral or on-disk format implications. The patch is a prerequisite for the final removal of the ad-hoc source linking API in patch 13/13. Karthik’s approval, combined with Junio C Hamano’s earlier substantive reviews of other patches in the series, suggests the refactoring is on track for eventual integration.
