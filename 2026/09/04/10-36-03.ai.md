This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series unifying Git’s reference storage terminology. It mechanically renames the `--ref-format=` option to `--ref-storage=` in `git clone`, retaining the old name as a hidden backward-compatibility alias. The change touches the command-line parser in `builtin/clone.c`, the man page, and six test scripts that exercise the option. No new functionality is introduced; the patch is purely a search-and-replace with a one-line addition to keep the old option working. Given the series’ goal of consistency ahead of the object storage extension, this step is uncontroversial and well-motivated. Reviewers are likely to focus on whether the backward-compatibility alias is sufficient and whether any test edge cases were missed.
