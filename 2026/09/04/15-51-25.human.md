This is the second patch in Thomas Bachem’s v2 series (3/3) that makes auto maintenance run exactly once at the end of any sequencer-driven operation—`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert`—instead of sporadically during intermediate steps. The patch itself adds the actual call to `run_auto_maintenance()` in the three places where the sequencer finishes: after a successful multi-step sequence, after a single-pick continuation, and after a lone cherry-pick or revert. It also extends the test coverage in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` and `t/t3510-cherry-pick-sequence.sh` to verify that `git maintenance run --auto` fires exactly once at the end and not during the sequence.

The implementation is straightforward: a single helper call is added at each exit point, and the tests use `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to confirm the timing. The commit message explains the motivation clearly—consistency with the apply backend and avoiding mid-sequence maintenance—and the code matches the description. Phillip Wood’s feedback from v1 has been fully addressed, and the series as a whole looks ready. If you care about sequencer behavior or auto maintenance, this patch delivers the promised behavior change.
