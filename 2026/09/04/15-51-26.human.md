This is the third and final patch in a v2 series that completes the effort to defer auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The patch ensures that commands spawned *during* the sequence—such as `git commit`, `git merge`, or any `exec` command—do not trigger background maintenance, preventing lock races and packfile conflicts that could disrupt the sequence.

The implementation is clean: it introduces a new helper, `disable_auto_maintenance()`, that builds a `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` string disabling `maintenance.auto` and `gc.auto`, and caches this string in the `replay_ctx` struct to avoid repeated construction. The helper is called before spawning any command, and the config string is appended *after* the user’s own `-c` settings, ensuring the sequencer’s settings take precedence. Test coverage verifies that maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion and not during intermediate steps, even in conflict-resolution scenarios.
