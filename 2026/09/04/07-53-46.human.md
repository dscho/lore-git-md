This is the second patch in a two-patch series adjusting when auto maintenance runs during a `git rebase`. The series aims to make the merge backend defer auto maintenance until the end of the rebase, matching the apply backend’s behavior and avoiding lock races with intermediate steps.

This patch disables auto maintenance for the individual commands (`git commit`, `git merge`, and exec commands) spawned during a rebase by passing `maintenance.auto=false` and `gc.auto=0` through the `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` environment variable. This ensures that maintenance does not interfere with the rebase process, such as by holding locks or deleting packs that the sequencer still needs. The change is scoped to the commands the rebase itself spawns; user-initiated commands (e.g., `git commit --amend` during an edit) are unaffected. The patch also adds a test in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` to verify that auto maintenance does not run during intermediate steps but does run at rebase completion.

The implementation is clean and focused, using `sq_quote_buf` to safely append the configuration settings to `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS`. The test coverage is thorough, checking both the absence of maintenance during intermediate steps and its presence at the end. The commit message clearly explains the motivation, referencing the prior discussion and the specific issues (e.g., rerere lock races, repack conflicts) that this change avoids. This looks like a well-crafted, uncontroversial improvement to rebase’s interaction with auto maintenance.
