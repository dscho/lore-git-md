This is a brief follow-up in the thread improving `git maintenance`'s heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. Thomas Bachem clarifies that Patrick Steinhardt's patch series (v2) only reduces the *frequency* of `rerere gc` runs by introducing a dynamic threshold, but does not alter the locking behavior. The series does not address concurrent `rerere gc` invocations—those remain subject to the existing `MERGE_RR.lock` with `LOCK_DIE_ON_ERROR`, meaning the second invocation will still fail if another is already running.

Thomas points to a separate, ongoing effort (pull.2214.v3) that tackles the locking issue directly: it modifies `setup_rerere()` to wait for `rerere.lockTimeout` before proceeding without rerere, and makes the `rerere gc` task exit immediately if the lock is held. This is a complementary fix, not part of the current series. The two efforts together would fully resolve the race condition between `git maintenance` and `git rebase` (and other concurrent operations), but they remain distinct patches. No action is requested on the v2 series itself; the clarification is purely informational.
