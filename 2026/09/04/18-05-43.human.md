This is a user report listing four potential regressions or odd behaviors in Git 2.55.0 on macOS.

Ramkumar Ramachandra, a long-time contributor, describes his experience after upgrading via Homebrew. The most notable item is that ``git replay`` now updates the ref, which he considers a welcome improvement for his workflow. The other three observations are all negative: ``git pull`` sometimes fails with a dangling remote ref error, ``git diff`` occasionally exits successfully without showing a diff on a dirty worktree, and ``git checkout -`` sporadically complains about an invalid ref after a pull. He asks whether these are known issues or if there is an explanation he is missing before he invests time in debugging.
