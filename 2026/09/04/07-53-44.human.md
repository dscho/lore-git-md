This is a two-patch series targeting the sequencer subsystem, specifically how `git rebase` interacts with auto maintenance.

The series addresses a long-standing inconsistency between the merge and apply backends of `git rebase`. Currently, the merge backend can trigger auto maintenance during the rebase (e.g., when committing a resolved pick), while the apply backend runs it only once at the end. The patches align the merge backend with the apply backend by moving auto maintenance to the end of the rebase and suppressing it during the rebase itself.

Patch 1 adds a call to `run_auto_maintenance()` at the point where the sequencer finishes a rebase, mirroring the apply backend’s behavior. Patch 2 passes `maintenance.auto=false` and `gc.auto=0` to the `git commit`, `git merge`, and `exec` commands spawned during a rebase, ensuring no auto maintenance runs mid-rebase. The changes are confined to `sequencer.c` and a new test in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh`.

The motivation is clear: avoid running maintenance operations against an in-progress rebase, which can interfere with the rebase itself or with other processes (e.g., the rerere lock race mentioned in the cover letter). The series is independent of the rerere lock fix but complements it by removing another source of contention.

The patches look well-scoped and uncontroversial. They address a real inconsistency and come with a new test to verify the behavior.
