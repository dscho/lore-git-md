This is a review comment on Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series to unify Git’s terminology for "ref storage." Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, questions the practical value of renaming `--ref-format=` to `--ref-storage=` and related terms. He argues that neither "ref-format" nor "ref-storage" is sufficiently precise: "ref-format" is ambiguous about whether it refers to storage, naming, or another aspect of refs, while "ref-storage" is similarly vague about whether it refers to the format, medium, or another property of storage. Junio’s tone suggests skepticism that the change improves clarity enough to justify the churn.

The comment does not engage with the series’ other goals—centralizing URI parsing, fixing the silent-corruption bug on reinitialization, or preparing for an object storage extension. It focuses narrowly on the terminology change, implying that the effort may not be worth the disruption unless the new terms are more specific. This could signal a need for Patrick to either refine the naming further or provide stronger justification for the current choice. The review is substantive but limited in scope, leaving the rest of the series (including the bug fix and URI support) unaddressed.
