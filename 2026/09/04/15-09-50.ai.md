This is a **tooling discussion** that spins off from the now-approved `git checkout -m` autostash conflict series. The thread is no longer about the patch itself—both patches in the series are queued for `next`—but about Git’s `.clang-format` enforcement in CI.

Phillip Wood weighs in on why Git’s GitHub CI job (`.github/workflows/check-style.yml`) runs `git clang-format` but never fails the build. The job was deliberately configured to be advisory only because `clang-format` does not perfectly match Git’s style guide and its output can vary across versions. Phillip suggests that the project faces a trade-off: either accept `clang-format`’s deviations and stop debating style, or keep the current advisory job and continue to handle style manually. The discussion is purely about contributor ergonomics and has no bearing on the integration status of the autostash series.
