This is the **withdrawal of the test patch** from Aleksei Sviridkin’s series documenting the `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` behavior in `git cherry-pick --no-commit`. The author has dropped the test (formerly part 1/2) after Patrick Steinhardt’s review questioned its necessity, leaving only the documentation patch (part 2/2) as a standalone update.

The email explains the decision: Aleksei revisited the test’s justification and concluded that the existing test coverage (`t3507`) already suffices. The clean and conflicted paths for `--no-commit` share the same `!opts->no_commit` guard in `do_pick_commit()`, so the absence of `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` in conflict cases is already implicitly tested by the clean-case test. No regression risk was identified, and the author now agrees the test was redundant.

The thread now focuses solely on the documentation patch, which remains unchallenged. The withdrawal resolves Patrick’s concern about "random tests" without clear value, and the series is effectively reduced to a single patch that fills a long-standing documentation gap. No further iterations are expected unless reviewers raise issues with the documentation itself.
