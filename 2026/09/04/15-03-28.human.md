This is a review reply to the first patch in Thomas Bachem’s two-patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of a `git rebase` (merge backend). The patch itself is a small, focused change that adds a call to `run_auto_maintenance()` in the sequencer’s cleanup path, after the autostash is applied.

Phillip Wood’s review is substantive. He agrees the patch is reasonable and in scope, but raises two forward-looking questions: whether the two rebase backends (apply and merge) should share more cleanup code (outside this series), and whether the same deferral logic should eventually be extended to `git cherry-pick` and `git revert`, since they can also create many loose objects and suffer from the same mid-operation maintenance interference. His feedback is constructive and does not block the current patch, but it hints at potential future work. The review also implicitly endorses the patch’s current approach, calling it “reasonable.”
