This is Junio’s biweekly "What’s cooking in git.git" report for September 2026 (#02), summarizing the state of 60+ in-flight topics across the `next`, `seen`, and stalled integration branches.

The report covers 66 commits cooking in `next` and 223 in `seen`, with 604 non-merge commits already in `master` since Git 2.55. Below are the key themes and notable topics, grouped by status and significance for a future AI summarizer session.

### New Topics (likely to graduate soon)
- **jc/history-missing-tree-errorfix**: A one-commit bugfix preventing a segfault in `git history` when a parent tree is missing in a corrupt repository. The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial; it’s marked for `next` pending a minor review comment.
- **jc/pathspec-match-const**: A refactoring patch making two `pathspec_item` fields `const char *` since they are never modified. Depended on by **yt/pathspec-negative-prefix**, which fixes an out-of-bounds access with negative pathspecs.
- **ps/tune-rerere-gc**: A two-patch series adjusting the heuristic for `rerere gc` to avoid interfering with `git rebase` and other operations. The change is motivated by real-world interference reports and is marked for `next`.
- **as/push-force-if-includes-no-reflog**: A one-line fix for an uninitialized timestamp when `--force-if-includes` encounters a remote-tracking ref with no reflog. The fix is trivial but awaits author response.
- **tb/rerere-lock-grace**: A three-patch series deferring auto-maintenance until the end of a sequencer operation (rebase, cherry-pick, revert) to avoid lock contention. The series introduces a new `git_config_append_parameter()` helper and is marked "Needs review."

### Cooking Topics (active, likely to graduate)
- **ps/odb-stop-registering-in-memory-sources**: A 13-patch series removing the ad-hoc mechanism for registering in-memory alternate object sources, simplifying ODB management. This is part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is depended on by several other topics.
- **ps/odb-alternates-at-creation**: An 8-patch series deferring alternates setup to ODB creation time during clone, dropping an unused API. Also part of the ODB abstraction work.
- **ps/odb-pluggable-fsck**: A 10-patch series moving fsck checks into backend-specific layers, making them pluggable. This is a prerequisite for supporting alternative object storage formats.
- **en/no-amend-during-conflicts**: A 5-patch series teaching `git commit --amend` and partial commits to refuse operation during conflict resolution, aligning behavior with `cherry-pick` and `merge`. The series is marked for `next` pending minor review comments.
- **kn/receive-report-hook**: A 4-patch series introducing a new `receive-report` hook to filter/modify the packet-line status report sent back to clients. The hook is motivated by server-side filtering needs and is marked "Needs review."
- **tc/last-modified-bloom**: A 6-patch series optimizing `git last-modified` with Bloom filters, reusing revision walk logic from `git log`. The series is marked "Needs review."
- **mm/line-log-limited-ops**: A 7-patch series teaching `git log -L<range>:<path>` to limit `--stat`, `--check`, and `-G` to the specified range. This is part of an ongoing effort to make line-log operations more efficient and is depended on by **mm/diff-process-hunks**.
- **mm/diff-process-hunks**: An 11-patch series introducing `diff.<driver>.process` to allow external tools to act as hunk providers. This is a major new feature enabling external tools to control which lines Git considers changed while leaving output formatting to Git. The series is marked "Expecting a reroll" due to design feedback.

2. **Line-Log and Diff Improvements**: **mm/line-log-limited-ops** and **mm/diff-process-hunks** are major new features enabling more efficient and flexible diff operations.
