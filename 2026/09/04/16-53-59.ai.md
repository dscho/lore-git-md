This is a reply in the thread improving the `git maintenance` heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. Thomas Bachem clarifies that Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series (v2) **only reduces the frequency** of `rerere gc` runs via a dynamic threshold heuristic but **does not alter the locking mechanism**. The series does not address concurrent `rerere gc` collisions—those remain governed by `MERGE_RR.lock` and `LOCK_DIE_ON_ERROR`, meaning the second invocation will still fail if the lock is held.

The email explicitly points to a **separate, ongoing effort** (pull.2214.v3) that tackles the lock contention problem directly. That patch modifies `setup_rerere()` to wait for `rerere.lockTimeout` before proceeding without rerere, and makes `rerere gc` abort immediately if the lock is held. This distinction—frequency reduction vs. concurrency control—is now unambiguous for future discussion. No code changes to the v2 series are implied or requested.
