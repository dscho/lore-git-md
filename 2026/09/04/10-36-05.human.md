This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch series to unify Git’s terminology for "ref storage" across CLI options, environment variables, and documentation. The series is a refactoring effort aimed at consistency ahead of an upcoming "object storage" extension.

This patch specifically updates `git submodule` and its helper to rename the `--ref-format=` option to `--ref-storage=`, while retaining the old name as a backward-compatible alias. The changes are mechanical but slightly more involved than in previous patches because they also touch the submodule helper (`builtin/submodule--helper.c`) and the shell script wrapper (`git-submodule.sh`). The patch updates the `submodule add` and `submodule update` commands, their documentation, and the internal logic that passes the option to `git clone`. Test scripts are adjusted to use the new option name, ensuring coverage remains intact.

The patch is well-structured and clearly motivated by the series’ goal of eliminating inconsistent terminology. The backward-compatibility alias (`--ref-format=`) is preserved, which should ease adoption for scripts and users. No functional changes are introduced—this is purely a rename with no impact on on-disk formats or behavior. Given Patrick’s role as the primary driver of the reftable backend and ODB abstraction efforts, this cleanup is likely to be uncontroversial, though reviewers may check for edge cases in how the option is parsed or passed through the submodule machinery. The patch is part of a larger, well-justified effort, and this step looks like a straightforward and necessary piece of that work.
