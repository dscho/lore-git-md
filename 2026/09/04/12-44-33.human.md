This is a well-crafted bugfix for the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The patch addresses an uninitialized variable in `remote.c` that causes the reflog walk to terminate prematurely when the remote-tracking ref has no reflog (e.g., in fresh clones). This leads to false positives in the "remote ref updated since checkout" check, potentially allowing unsafe force-pushes.

The issue was introduced in 2020 and affects only the files backend, as remote-tracking refs created by `git clone` lack reflog entries until they first move. The fix is minimal—initializing `timestamp_t date` to zero in `is_reachable_in_reflog()`—and ensures the reflog walk covers the entire local branch history when the remote-tracking ref has no reflog, matching the documented behavior. The patch includes a new test in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` that reproduces the bug by expiring all reflogs in a fresh clone and attempting a `--force-if-includes` push.
