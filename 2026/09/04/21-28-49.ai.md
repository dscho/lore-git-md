This is the first patch in Karthik Nayak’s four-patch series introducing the `receive-report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. It is a **documentation-only patch** that adds an eight-line `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section to `git-receive-pack.adoc`, explaining the existing `proc-receive` hook’s purpose and linking to `githooks(5)` for protocol details.

The patch plugs a gap in the man page, which previously documented all other hooks invoked during a push but omitted the `proc-receive` hook. The new section clarifies that the hook is triggered when the server’s `receive.procReceiveRefs` config variable matches the commands sent to `receive-pack`, and that the hook itself is responsible for updating references and reporting results. This change is uncontroversial and addresses prior feedback from Patrick Steinhardt, who is credited with a `Helped-by` trailer.

The patch touches only `Documentation/git-receive-pack.adoc` and is purely additive. It is part of the v7 iteration, which is rebased onto `master` and ready for integration into `next`. No substantive objections remain; the patch is a straightforward documentation improvement.
