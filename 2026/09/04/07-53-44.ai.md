This is a two-patch series targeting the sequencer subsystem, specifically adjusting when auto maintenance runs during a rebase.

The series responds to a discussion that surfaced while investigating a rerere lock race. Currently, the merge backend of `git rebase` triggers auto maintenance during intermediate commits, which can interfere with the rebase itself. Phillip Wood had previously suggested that auto maintenance should be deferred until the end of the rebase, similar to how the apply backend behaves. Patrick Steinhardt preferred that this change be addressed separately, so this series isolates the auto maintenance adjustment.

Patch 1 modifies the sequencer to run auto maintenance once at the end of a rebase, matching the apply backend’s behavior. Patch 2 then disables auto maintenance for the individual commands (`git commit`, `git merge`, and exec commands) spawned during a rebase by passing `maintenance.auto=false` and `gc.auto=0`. The changes do not affect cherry-pick or revert operations.

The implementation touches `sequencer.c` and adds tests in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh`. The series is based on `master` and is independent of the rerere lock race fix mentioned in the cover letter. The patches are small and focused, with clear test coverage for the new behavior. The motivation is well-documented, referencing prior discussion and linking to relevant threads. This looks like a straightforward improvement to rebase’s interaction with auto maintenance.
