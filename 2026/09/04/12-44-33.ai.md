This is a bugfix for the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. Aleksei Sviridkin sends v2 of a standalone patch that fixes an uninitialized variable bug in the files backend, which causes the reflog walk to terminate prematurely when the remote-tracking ref has no reflog (e.g., in fresh clones). The issue was introduced in 99a1f9ae10 (2020) and can lead to false positives in the "remote ref updated since checkout" check, potentially allowing unsafe force-pushes.

The fix itself is minimal: a one-line change in `remote.c` (`timestamp_t date = 0`) ensures the reflog walk covers the entire local branch history when the remote-tracking ref lacks a reflog. The patch also adds a new test in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` that reproduces the bug by expiring all reflogs in a fresh clone and attempting a `--force-if-includes` push. The test fails without the fix on the author's macOS/arm64 system, though the uninitialized stack value means behavior may vary across platforms.
