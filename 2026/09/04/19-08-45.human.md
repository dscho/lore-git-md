This is Junio’s reply to the v3 locking patch in Thomas Bachem’s series fixing the `rerere` race between rebase and background maintenance. Junio flags a potential user-experience regression: if `rerere_setup()` times out and skips recording the conflict preimage, the user loses the chance to reuse that resolution later in the rebase. He accepts that background `rerere gc` can skip entirely, but warns that silently dropping conflict recordings mid-rebase would be a serious usability hit. The comment is brief and does not reject the patch outright, but it raises a substantive concern that Thomas will need to address before the patch can land.
