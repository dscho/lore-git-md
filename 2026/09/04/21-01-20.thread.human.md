This thread fixes a bug in Git’s `--force-if-includes` safety check for `git push`. The mechanism was incorrectly checking the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the *remote* destination (e.g., `main` when pushing to `origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `src` when pushing `src:main`). This could cause false rejections or unintended data loss, especially when pushing from a differently named local branch or a detached HEAD.

Tyler Cipriani’s two-patch series (v1) corrects the logic to consult the reflog of the pushed ref, adds special handling for `HEAD`-based pushes and detached HEAD states (rejecting them by default), and introduces a new `advice.forceIfIncludesDetachedHead` config knob to make the behavior configurable. The advice messages are now actionable and consistent across all transport layers. D. Ben Knoble provided a substantive review, confirming the root cause and verifying the fix’s behavior, including the reflog limitation for detached HEAD as a necessary trade-off. The only remaining feedback is a minor "nit" on the first patch, which Tyler plans to address in v2.

The thread is now in a stable state: the detached HEAD rejection policy is settled, the advice improvements are uncontroversial, and no open questions remain. A v2 resubmission is expected soon, incorporating minor polish before the series is ready for merging.
