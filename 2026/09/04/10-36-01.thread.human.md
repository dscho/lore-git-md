This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eleven-patch series (v1) that renames Git’s “ref format” terminology to “ref storage” across CLI options, environment variables, config keys, and documentation. The goal is to unify the language ahead of a planned object-storage extension and to enable URI-style arguments like `--ref-storage=files://path/to/alt-refs`. The series also fixes a bug where reinitializing a repository with a different ref-storage format silently corrupts it.

So far the patches are under review. Karthik Nayak has given the series a light but positive once-over, calling it “relatively quite an easy read” and flagging only minor surface issues—an inconsistent help-text placeholder and a missing environment-variable macro, both of which have since been addressed. Junio’s latest message, however, questions whether “ref storage” is actually clearer than the old “ref format”; both terms are ambiguous about what exactly they control. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, so the main open question is whether the chosen terminology will fly or whether the series will need a rebranding before it can move forward.
