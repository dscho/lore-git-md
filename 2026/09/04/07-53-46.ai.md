This is the second patch in Thomas Bachem’s two-patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of a `git rebase` (merge backend). It prevents the individual commands spawned during a rebase—`git commit`, `git merge`, and any exec command—from running auto maintenance by passing `maintenance.auto=false` and `gc.auto=0` through the `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` environment variable. The change is confined to the sequencer and does not affect cherry-pick or revert. A new test in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` verifies that no auto maintenance runs mid-rebase and that exactly one maintenance run happens at the end. The implementation is clean, the rationale is well-explained, and the patch is ready for review.
