This thread is a patch series from Mark C. Chu-Carroll proposing to modernize Git’s test scripts by replacing older shell test idioms (`test -f`, `! test -f`, `test -d`) with more semantic helper functions (`test_path_is_file`, `test_path_is_missing`, `test_path_is_dir`). The goal was purely syntactic, aiming to align with the project’s test guidelines without changing behavior.

The series, consisting of two patches covering 61 test scripts, was initially uncontroversial but has now hit a major roadblock. Junio C Hamano reviewed the first patch and rejected it outright, identifying a fundamental misunderstanding in the approach. The patch mechanically replaced `test -f` with `test_path_is_file` even in conditional contexts where failure is *not* an error (e.g., checking for platform-specific files like `/proc/$shell_pid/winpid`). Junio clarified that the helper functions are designed for assertions, not control flow, and their misuse would turn silent checks into misleading error messages. He declined to review the rest of the series until the approach is rethought, noting that the patch contains more incorrect conversions than correct ones. The absence of a Signed-off-by trailer was also flagged as a procedural issue, though it’s secondary to the technical critique.

The series remains under review, but no further progress can be made until the author addresses Junio’s feedback. No other reviews or maintainer decisions have been recorded. The thread is part of the ongoing community-wide effort to modernize Git’s test suite, but this particular attempt will need significant revision before it can move forward.
