This thread is about a two-patch series (v2) by Thomas Bachem that adjusts when auto maintenance runs during sequencer-driven operations—`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert`—so it defers until the sequence completes, matching the apply backend’s behavior. The goal is to prevent maintenance from interfering with intermediate steps and ensure consistent behavior across all three commands.

The series is technically complete and under review. All prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Patrick Steinhardt has been addressed, and Junio C Hamano recently reviewed the third patch, suggesting a minor mechanical change: storing the cached `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` string as a `const char *` instead of a `struct strbuf` for clarity. Thomas has acknowledged this and will include it in the next revision. The only open item is a hypothetical edge case about very long sequences where mid-sequence repacking might improve performance, but Phillip Wood considers this a watch item rather than a blocker.

Key changes include a new helper function, `git_config_append_parameter()`, and adjustments to the sequencer to cache the config string in `replay_ctx`. Tests verify that auto maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion. The series is ready for integration once the minor feedback is incorporated.
