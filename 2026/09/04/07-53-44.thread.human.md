This thread introduces a small but targeted improvement to `git rebase`: deferring auto maintenance until the rebase completes, rather than running it after every intermediate commit or merge. The change aligns the merge backend’s behavior with the apply backend’s and prevents maintenance from interfering with the rebase process.

Thomas Bachem posted the initial version (v1) of the two-patch series. The first patch disables auto maintenance for individual commands during rebase, while the second (just summarized) adds a test to confirm that maintenance runs exactly once at the end. The implementation is clean, passing maintenance flags through environment variables and ignoring maintenance errors to avoid masking user-facing issues.

Phillip Wood and Patrick Steinhardt had previously suggested this change in a separate discussion, and the series addresses their feedback directly. No objections or open questions have been raised yet, and the patches appear ready for review. The only related work mentioned is an unrelated rerere lock race investigation, which this series deliberately avoids.
