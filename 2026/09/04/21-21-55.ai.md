This is a review comment from Junio C Hamano on the third patch in Thomas Bachem’s v2 series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion. The patch adds a `config_parameters` field to the `replay_ctx` struct to cache the `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` string that disables auto maintenance for spawned commands.

Junio questions whether the field needs to be a `struct strbuf` rather than a simple `const char *`. His suggested alternative uses a local `strbuf` to construct the string once, then detaches it into a `const char *` that is stored in the context. This would make it clearer that the value never changes after initialization and avoids the overhead of keeping a `strbuf` around. The change is purely mechanical and does not affect the patch’s behavior or scope. No other concerns are raised; the feedback is surface-level and targets code clarity rather than functionality.
