This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Thomas Bachem’s two-patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of a `git rebase` (merge backend). Phillip Wood’s feedback is thorough and technical, focusing on both the implementation and the broader design.

The patch itself disables auto maintenance for commands spawned during a rebase (`git commit`, `git merge`, and exec commands) by passing `maintenance.auto=false` and `gc.auto=0` via `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS`. Phillip’s review raises three main points: (1) the commit message’s prose is convoluted and could be clearer, (2) the reasoning for deferring maintenance might apply equally to `git cherry-pick` and `git revert`, and (3) the implementation could be cleaner by reusing existing config-parameter helpers and caching the constructed environment string.

Phillip also suggests a small refactoring: factoring out a helper to append config parameters to an `strbuf` (rather than hardcoding the quoting logic in `sequencer.c`) and caching the result in `replay_ctx` to avoid reconstructing it for every spawned command. The review is constructive and identifies real opportunities to improve the patch without questioning its core goal. If Thomas addresses these points, the series looks likely to land.
