This is the **v2 update** of Thomas Bachem’s patch series that defers Git’s auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert`). The series now consists of three patches: a new helper in `config.c` to build `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` strings, followed by the two original patches that implement the deferral logic.

The key change in this version is structural: Phillip Wood’s suggestion to factor out the config-string construction into a reusable helper (`git_config_append_parameter()`) is now implemented, and the constructed string is cached in `replay_ctx` to avoid repeated work. The commit messages have also been rewritten for clarity, addressing Phillip’s readability concerns from the v1 review. The series now covers all three sequencer commands (rebase, cherry-pick, revert) rather than just rebase, ensuring consistent behavior across the board.

The patch set is technically complete and addresses all prior feedback. The only open question—a hypothetical edge case about very long sequences where mid-sequence repacking might improve performance—is explicitly noted as a watch item rather than a blocker. Test coverage (using `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to verify maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion) remains thorough. This looks like a well-crafted, uncontroversial fix that aligns the sequencer backend with the apply backend’s existing behavior.
