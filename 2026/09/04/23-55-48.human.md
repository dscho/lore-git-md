This is Junio’s biweekly "What’s cooking in git.git" report for September 2026, summarizing the state of 60+ in-flight topics. It’s the best single snapshot of where the project stands between releases—what’s about to graduate, what’s stalled, and what’s still cooking.

The 22nd batch of topics just graduated to `master`, bringing the total since Git 2.55 to 604 non-merge commits. Right now 66 topics are in `next` (ready for the next release unless something breaks) and 223 are in `seen` (still under review or half-baked).

New topics worth a quick look
• jc/history-missing-tree-errorfix – a one-line NULL-deref fix in `git history` that stops a segfault when a parent tree is missing in a corrupt repo. Small, obvious, and already marked “Will merge to next.”
• ps/tune-rerere-gc – tweaks the heuristic for when `git maintenance` triggers `rerere gc` so it interferes less with interactive rebase. Two patches, both look like sensible tuning.
• as/push-force-if-includes-no-reflog – plugs an uninitialized timestamp bug in `--force-if-includes` when the remote-tracking branch has no reflog. Single patch, waiting for author response.
• tb/rerere-lock-grace – defers auto-maintenance until the end of a sequencer operation (rebase, cherry-pick, revert) so nested commands don’t contend for locks. Three patches, needs review but the idea is uncontroversial.

Stalled topics (likely to be dropped unless someone revives them soon)
• ps/libgit-in-subdir – moving libgit.a sources into a `lib/` directory. It’s been stalled since July because it causes too many merge conflicts with other topics. Junio ejected it; if it comes back it will need a rebase.
• hs/rebase-continue-edit – adds `--[no-]edit` to `rebase --continue`. Waiting for a response since July; Junio asked for a re-roll and nothing arrived.
• sn/rebase-update-refs-symrefs – teaches `--update-refs` to skip branch symref aliases. Two patches, no response since July.
• tb/pack-with-duplicates – hardens pack handling for duplicate objects. Five patches, but Junio pointed out a subtle issue and the author hasn’t replied.
• cl/regexec-macos-leak – a macOS-specific regex leak workaround. Two patches, needs a reroll.

Cooking topics that look ready to graduate
• jk/rev-info-argv-to-free – fixes a use-after-free in the revision machinery by keeping “freed” argv elements alive until the rev_info struct is released. Two patches, already in `next`, marked “Will merge to master.”
• kn/reftable-optimize-reloading – avoids unnecessary stat/reload of the reftable stack when the lock is already held. Four patches, in `next`, “Will merge to master.”
• rs/worktree-add-basename-fixes – fixes out-of-bounds reads and trailing-slash handling in `git worktree add`. Four patches, in `next`.
• en/midx-missing-pack-fallback – makes object lookup recover gracefully when a multi-pack-index points to a removed pack. Four patches, in `next`.
• yn/worktree-repair-relative – fixes `git worktree repair` when the .git file contains a relative path. One patch, in `next`.

Notable longer efforts still cooking
• ps/odb-stop-registering-in-memory-sources – removes the ad-hoc in-memory alternate registration mechanism, part of the ODB abstraction work. 13 patches, needs review.
• ps/odb-pluggable-fsck – moves fsck checks into the backend-specific object source layers. 10 patches, needs review.
• ps/odb-alternates-at-creation – defers alternate setup to object database creation time during clone. 8 patches, needs review.
• mm/diff-process-hunks – introduces `diff.<driver>.process` to let external tools act as hunk providers. 11 patches, needs a reroll.
• hn/history-squash – adds a `git history squash` subcommand to fold a range of commits into one. 8 patches, waiting for response.

If you only have time for one thing, skim the “New Topics” and “Cooking” sections—those are the patches most likely to land in the next release. The stalled topics are candidates for cleanup unless their authors step up.
