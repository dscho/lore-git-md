This is the final reply in Patrick Steinhardt’s two-patch series improving the `git maintenance` heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. The series is now ready for integration: both patches have received substantive reviews from Derrick Stolee and tested confirmation from Thomas Bachem, and Patrick has addressed the last non-blocking stylistic feedback.

The core change replaces the hard-coded 60-day cutoff with a dynamic heuristic that samples the `rr-cache` directory, scales each sampled entry by 256, and runs the task only when the estimate meets or exceeds the configurable `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` threshold (default: 512). Negative values force the task to run unconditionally; zero disables it. The update eliminates unnecessary invocations, reduces overhead, and fixes a long-standing race condition reported in GitGitGadget PR 2214.

In this email Patrick accepts Stolee’s suggestion to clarify the ternary logic in `rerere_gc_condition()` and renames `rerere_gc_estimate()` to the more intuitive `rerere_gc_needed()`. The changes are cosmetic and do not alter the series’ behavior or logic. All tests continue to pass, and the patches remain “LGTM” by Stolee and “Tested-by” Bachem. The series is now queued for Junio’s next integration cycle.
