This is the sixth patch in Junio C Hamano’s eight-patch series refactoring the internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore`. The patch eliminates the monolithic `checkout_main()` function, which previously served as a shared entry point for all three commands, and restructures each command to call the extracted utility helpers directly. This removes the "cheating" pattern where `git switch` and `git restore` were bolted onto `git checkout` via a shared helper, placing all three commands on equal footing.

The patch is mechanical and preserves all behavior: `cmd_switch()`, `cmd_restore()`, and `cmd_checkout()` now initialize options, validate them, and dispatch to `checkout_branch()` or `checkout_paths()` without relying on `checkout_main()`. The `enum checkout_command` is also removed, as it was only used by the now-eliminated `checkout_main()`. The restructuring touches only `builtin/checkout.c`, moving roughly 150 lines of logic from `checkout_main()` into the three command-specific functions. The patch passes the test suite and is uncontroversial, with Karthik Nayak’s reply being purely retrospective—he notes that seeing this patch clarifies how the first patch’s `cb_option` parameter fits into the series.

This is a clean refactoring that aligns with the architectural direction established in the parent thread: built-in commands are not libified, but their reusable internals are extracted into standalone helpers. The patch could be merged independently if the final two patches (which introduce new top-level files) are deferred, but the series is explicitly not intended for `seen` due to potential conflicts with in-flight topics. The unresolved design question from the first patch (whether to eliminate `cb_option` entirely) does not affect this patch, but it may require adjustments to the extracted helpers in later patches. No further feedback is needed on this patch itself.
