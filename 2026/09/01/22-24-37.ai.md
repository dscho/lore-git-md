This is the first patch in a **bugfix series (v4 1/5)** that refactors naming to clarify intent. The patch renames `FROM_REBASE_PICK` to `FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` and `is_from_rebase()` to `is_from_rebase_now_empty()` to better convey that these symbols refer to commits that *became* empty during a rebase, rather than being empty from the start. The change is purely mechanical, with no functional or behavioral impact.

The patch updates all call sites in `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h` to use the new names. This addresses Junio C Hamano’s stylistic feedback from earlier iterations, where he noted that the original names (`FROM_REBASE_PICK` and `is_from_rebase()`) did not clearly indicate that the commit *became* empty. The new names (`FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` and `is_from_rebase_now_empty()`) make this distinction explicit.

The series as a whole extends Git’s existing protection against unsafe commit operations (`git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>`) during conflict resolution to cover `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`. This patch is preparatory, ensuring the naming aligns with the series’ goal of preventing users from accidentally amending the wrong commit or updating individual paths during conflict resolution. The rename is uncontroversial and has been explicitly approved by Junio.

Key files touched: `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h`. No new symbols or behaviors are introduced in this patch—only renames. The change is part of a larger effort to improve user safety during conflict resolution, and the series is technically complete and ready for integration.
