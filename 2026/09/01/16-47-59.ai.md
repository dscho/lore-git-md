This is the final clarification exchange in a now-merged bugfix series targeting a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The series, authored by Elijah Newren, fixes crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by a concurrent repack. Patch 4/4, the core fix, introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism in `odb/source-packed.c` that scans MIDX-covered packs when the preferred pack is missing, gated by the `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag to optimize performance.

Derrick Stolee's review of this patch (sent earlier) was unconditionally positive ("Good patch. LGTM."), confirming the tri-state return value from `midx_fill_entry()` and the `SECOND_READ`-gated recovery logic. In this follow-up, Elijah clarifies two minor points in Stolee's review: (1) the comment's description of the race condition is slightly off—new packfiles *are* created during repacking, but they don’t contain the object in question (which survives in a pre-existing MIDX-covered pack), and (2) the recovery loop’s complexity is O(num_packs_in_the_midx), not O(num_packs), which is significant for busy servers where the total pack count may far exceed the MIDX’s coverage. These clarifications are technical nits and do not affect the patch’s correctness or merge status. The series is already in `master` as of 2025-07-10, with no unresolved concerns. Key files: `odb/source-packed.c`, `midx.c`/`midx.h`.
