This is the final patch in a well-crafted three-part bugfix series targeting race conditions in Git's HTTP daemon test helpers. The series addresses a real, observed flake in `t5616.47` on macOS CI runners, where concurrent HTTP requests to the same endpoint could race on shared state, causing HTTP 500 errors.

This patch documents the atomic patterns introduced in the previous two patches for future test authors. It adds a concise but clear section to `t/lib-httpd.sh` explaining why concurrency matters in these CGI scripts and providing concrete examples of safe and unsafe patterns. The examples directly reference the fixes in the series (`mkdir` for `http-429.sh`, `rm` without `-f` for `apply-one-time-script.sh`) and include a reminder to use process IDs (`$$`) in scratch file names to avoid collisions. The wording is precise and actionable, reflecting feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on earlier versions.

The documentation is minimal and targeted, confined to the HTTP daemon helper context where the race conditions actually occur. It avoids over-generalizing while still providing enough guidance to prevent similar issues in new scripts. The patch is unlikely to be controversial and completes the series on a strong note. All substantive and editorial feedback from previous iterations has been addressed, and the series is ready for integration into `next`.
