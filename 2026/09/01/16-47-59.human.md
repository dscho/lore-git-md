This is the final exchange in a now-merged bugfix series addressing a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The series, authored by Elijah Newren, fixes crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations caused by a multi-pack-index (MIDX) referencing a packfile removed by a concurrent repack. The core fix (patch 4/4) teaches `find_pack_entry()` to recover when the MIDX's preferred pack is missing but the object survives in another MIDX-covered pack, gating the recovery logic on `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` to optimize performance.

In this email, Elijah clarifies two minor points in response to Derrick Stolee's **tested, substantive review** of patch 4/4 (v3). Stolee's review praised the patch as correct and well-designed, walking through the tri-state return values (`MIDX_FILL_MISS`, `MIDX_FILL_HIT`, `MIDX_FILL_OWNER_UNAVAILABLE`) and the `SECOND_READ`-gated recovery logic. Elijah's clarifications address Stolee's comment about the recovery scenario: while Stolee assumed the object was missing from the new packfile, Elijah notes that the new packfile typically *does* exist but simply doesn't contain the object in question, as the object is already present in a pre-existing MIDX-covered pack. Elijah also corrects Stolee's characterization of the recovery's complexity, noting that the fallback scans only the packs in the MIDX (not all packs), which is more efficient on busy servers where the total number of packs often exceeds the number of packs in the MIDX.

The series is now merged to `master` (as of 2025-07-10), with all prior feedback addressed. The only unresolved loose end is the clarity of error messages when recovery succeeds, which is non-blocking. This email serves as a minor technical clarification rather than a substantive change, confirming the patch's correctness and the efficiency of its recovery mechanism. The fix is minimal, well-motivated by production evidence, and introduces no on-disk format changes. Key files touched include `odb/source-packed.c`, `midx.c`/`midx.h`, and `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh`.
