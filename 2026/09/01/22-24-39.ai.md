This is the third patch in Elijah Newren’s **v4 bugfix series** (5 patches total) that extends Git’s protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. This specific patch **rewords the error message** shown when a user attempts `git commit --amend` after a rebase drops an empty commit, replacing the generic *"You are in the middle of a rebase -- cannot amend."* with the more precise *"The now-empty commit has been dropped -- cannot amend."*

The change is minimal: a single-line update in `builtin/commit.c` and a corresponding test adjustment in `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh`. The new phrasing clarifies *why* amending is unsafe in this context—because the commit the user intended to amend was dropped due to becoming empty—rather than implying a blanket prohibition during rebase. Junio C Hamano explicitly approved this wording in an earlier iteration ("Much less awkward than the previous round"), and the patch carries no functional changes beyond the message itself.

This patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration. It directly addresses feedback from Junio while improving usability without altering behavior. The series as a whole (targeting `git commit --amend` and `git commit <paths>` during conflict resolution) is technically complete and has received approval for all substantive changes.
