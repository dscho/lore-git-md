This is the first patch in the v4 series optimizing `git last-modified` performance by reusing Bloom filters. It refactors a small but important precondition check in the revision-walking code.

The patch moves the `revs->bloom_keyvecs_nr` check — currently duplicated at two callsites — into `check_maybe_different_in_bloom_filter()` itself. This simplifies the calling code and makes the precondition part of the function's contract. The change is purely internal to `revision.c` and has no user-visible behavior change; the Bloom filter statistics reporting remains unchanged because the statistics are only emitted when key vectors are actually set up.

The diff is minimal (7 lines total) and the commit message clearly explains the motivation and the unobservable nature of the change. This is a clean, low-risk refactoring that prepares the ground for the more substantial Bloom filter optimizations that follow in the series. No controversy or open questions here — it’s a straightforward improvement.
