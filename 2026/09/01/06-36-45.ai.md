This is a **refactoring follow-up** to Jeff King’s earlier bugfix for the `git stash show` use-after-free regression. It cleans up the `mark_argv_for_free()` helper by moving the `free_removed_argv_elements` flag check into the helper itself, eliminating redundant checks in all callers. The change is purely internal to `revision.c` and has no user-visible impact.

The patch simplifies the code by consolidating the flag logic into a single place, making the call sites cleaner and reducing the chance of future inconsistencies. The one exception—`setup_revisions_from_strvec()`—always sets the flag explicitly, so the new check is safe even though the caller wasn’t previously checking it. This is a minor but logical improvement that builds on the earlier fix without altering its behavior.

No new symbols or files are introduced; the patch only adjusts the helper’s signature and updates its callers. The change is uncontroversial and suitable for `maint` alongside the regression fix. If the earlier patch is accepted, this cleanup should follow naturally.
