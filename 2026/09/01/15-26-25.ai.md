This is the final substantive review in a now-merged bugfix series. Derrick Stolee provides a **tested, substantive review** of patch 4/4 (v3), the core fix for a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The patch teaches `find_pack_entry()` to recover when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile that has been removed by a concurrent repack, preventing crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations.

Stolee's review walks through the patch line-by-line, confirming the correctness of the tri-state return value (`MIDX_FILL_MISS`, `MIDX_FILL_HIT`, `MIDX_FILL_OWNER_UNAVAILABLE`) introduced in `midx_fill_entry()` (renamed from `fill_midx_entry()`). He praises the function rename as a way to ensure all callers handle the new semantics, and endorses the gating of recovery logic on `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` to avoid unnecessary rescans for `QUICK` callers. The test case, which simulates the race by removing a packfile while a MIDX still references it, is also validated as appropriate and necessary.

The review is **unconditionally positive** ("Good patch. LGTM.") and adds Stolee's explicit endorsement to the series, which has already received final confirmation from Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt. No new concerns are raised; the only remaining loose end (error message clarity) is noted as non-blocking. The series is now effectively merged to `master`.
