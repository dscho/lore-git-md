This is a minor follow-up in a now-merged bugfix series that fixed a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The series, already in `master`, addressed crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) referenced a packfile removed by a concurrent repack. The core fix (patch 4/4) introduced a lightweight recovery mechanism in `odb/source-packed.c` that scans MIDX-covered packs when the preferred pack is missing, gated by the `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag.

In this exchange, Derrick Stolee provides a late but unconditionally positive review of the final patch ("Good patch. LGTM."), confirming the tri-state return value from `midx_fill_entry()` and the `SECOND_READ`-gated recovery logic. Elijah Newren then clarifies two minor technical points in Stolee's review: (1) the comment's description of the race condition is slightly inaccurate—new packfiles *are* created during repacking, but they don’t contain the object in question (which survives in a pre-existing MIDX-covered pack), and (2) the recovery loop’s complexity is O(num_packs_in_the_midx), not O(num_packs), which matters for busy servers where the total pack count may far exceed the MIDX’s coverage. Stolee acknowledges both clarifications as correct and non-blocking. No changes to the patch are needed; this is purely a technical nit exchange. The series remains merged to `master` with no unresolved concerns. Key files: `odb/source-packed.c`, `midx.c`/`midx.h`.
