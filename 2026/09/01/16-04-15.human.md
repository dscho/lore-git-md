This is a procedural confirmation in the thread about Elijah Newren’s four-patch bugfix series that fixes a race condition in Git’s geometric repacking mechanism. The series has already been fully reviewed and endorsed by Jeff King (Peff), Patrick Steinhardt, and now Derrick Stolee, who explicitly states, “I support the series, too!” in this email. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, responds with a brief acknowledgment (“Thanks, all.”), signaling that the series is now cleared for integration.

The core issue—a race where a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a packfile removed by geometric repacking, causing crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors in other operations—is resolved by patch 4/4, which introduces a lightweight recovery mechanism in the packed-object backend. The fix is gated by a tri-state return from `midx_fill_entry()` and triggers only during `SECOND_READ` to optimize performance. Earlier patches in the series address a memory leak in `git mktree --batch` and remove the problematic `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` flag from that command, replacing it with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` to preserve promisor-object avoidance while allowing recovery.

The series is well-motivated by production evidence, includes thorough test coverage, and has no unresolved technical disagreements. The only loose ends are minor follow-up items (error message clarity, basic test coverage for `git mktree --batch`, and log message demotion), none of which are blockers. With this final endorsement, the series is now ready to graduate from `next` to `master`.
