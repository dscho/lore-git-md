This is the first patch in the v5 series introducing the `receive-report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. It is a **documentation-only patch** that adds a `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section to `git-receive-pack.adoc`, explaining the purpose of the existing `proc-receive` hook and linking to `githooks(5)` for protocol details. This patch was previously reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt, who suggested minor wording and formatting improvements—using `linkgit:git-receive-pack[1]` instead of a plain command name and copying the first paragraph from `githooks(5)` to avoid redundancy. Karthik Nayak has incorporated all of Patrick’s suggestions, and the patch is now finalized.

The addition plugs a gap in the documentation by explicitly listing the `proc-receive` hook alongside other hooks invoked during the push process. While this patch does not introduce the new `receive-report` hook (that comes in patch 3/3), it establishes the context for hooks in the `git-receive-pack` lifecycle, which will be extended by the later patch. The change is purely additive and does not alter any behavior. The commit message is clear and concise, and the patch itself is small and focused, touching only the documentation file. No further feedback is expected, and this patch is ready for integration.
