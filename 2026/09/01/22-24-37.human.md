This is the first patch in Elijah Newren’s **v4 bugfix series** (5 patches total) that extends Git’s protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution. This patch is a **pure refactoring** with no behavior change: it renames `FROM_REBASE_PICK` to `FROM_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` and `is_from_rebase()` to `is_from_rebase_now_empty()` to clarify that these symbols refer to commits that *became* empty during a rebase, not commits that were empty from the start. The rename is applied consistently across `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.h`, and all direct enum checks are replaced with calls to the renamed helper function.

The change addresses Junio C Hamano’s stylistic feedback from v3, where he noted that the original names (`FROM_REBASE_PICK` and `is_from_rebase()`) did not adequately convey that the commit *became* empty. While the new names are more precise, the logic remains functionally identical. This patch is uncontroversial and serves as a preparatory step for the behavioral changes in later patches (extending protection to `git am`, `git revert`, and all `git rebase` variants). The series as a whole is **technically complete** and ready for integration, with all substantive feedback resolved in earlier iterations.
