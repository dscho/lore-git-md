This is a brief follow-up in the now-merged bugfix series that fixed a race condition in Git's geometric repacking mechanism. The series, which landed in `master` on 2025-07-10, addressed crashes in `git replay` and missing-object errors by introducing a lightweight recovery mechanism in the packed-object backend when a multi-pack-index (MIDX) references a removed packfile.

In this email, Derrick Stolee and Elijah Newren clarify two minor technical points about the core fix (patch 4/4). Stolee initially misread the race condition's dynamics, thinking no new packfiles were created during repacking, but Elijah corrects this: new packfiles *are* created, though they don’t contain the object in question (which survives in a pre-existing MIDX-covered pack). Stolee also overestimated the recovery loop’s complexity, assuming it was O(num_packs), but Elijah notes it’s actually O(num_packs_in_the_midx), a critical distinction for busy servers where the total pack count may far exceed the MIDX’s coverage. Both clarifications are technical nits—neither affects the patch’s correctness or merge status—and Stolee acknowledges them without further discussion. The series remains unconditionally endorsed by all reviewers.
