This is Junio C Hamano's reply to the refactoring patch in Karthik Nayak's `receive-report` hook series. The patch extracts the status-report generation logic into a shared helper, `generate_report()`, and Junio is commenting on the choice of a boolean parameter (`detailed_report`) to control v2-specific behavior.

Junio acknowledges the parameter name is technically accurate—it controls whether detailed per-command status is included—but expresses concern that the name might mislead future developers into thinking it is a general "detail level" knob rather than a protocol-version switch. He suggests an `enum` (e.g., `receive_pack_report_v0` vs `receive_pack_report_v2`) would be more explicit and extensible, though he concedes it might be overkill.

The comment is substantive but not blocking: Junio does not demand a change, only flags a potential maintainability risk. The series as a whole is already queued in `seen` and ready for `next`, so this is a late-stage design note rather than a review objection. Karthik will need to decide whether to address it in a reroll or leave it as-is.
