This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem to remove ad-hoc source linking. The patch deletes the now-unused `odb_add_to_alternates_memory()` function from `odb.c` and its declaration in `odb.h`, completing the removal of the mechanism that allowed in-memory registration of ODB sources (primarily used for submodules).

The series as a whole simplifies the ODB by ensuring the sources list contains only the primary source and its alternates, eliminating a long-standing design ambiguity. This patch is purely mechanical: it removes 14 lines of code that no longer have any callers after the earlier patches in the series. The commit message notes that this cleanup paves the way for future work moving alternate handling into the "files" backend, though that effort is not part of this series.

The patch is uncontroversial and appears well-motivated. The series has been self-reviewed by the author, and no external feedback has been raised yet. If the earlier patches in the series hold up under review, this one should merge cleanly as the logical conclusion of the refactoring. The change is internal to the ODB subsystem and introduces no user-visible behavior changes.
