This is a refactoring patch in Junio’s eight-part series to libify the reusable internals of `git checkout`, `git switch`, and `git restore`. The series aims to eliminate the monolithic `checkout_main()` helper and extract shared logic into standalone functions that accept a `struct repository *` parameter, aligning with the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

The third patch moves the validation of `--ours`, `--theirs`, `--force`, and `--merge` option compatibility from `checkout_main()` into `checkout_paths()`, where it logically belongs. Since `--patch` does not interact with index stages, the check should only apply when pathspecs are present *and* `--patch` is not active.
