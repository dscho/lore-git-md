This is a follow-up in the GitLab CI/build system thread about the recent fix for the "documentation" job. Jeff King (Peff) expands on his earlier observation that the CI configuration still pins Asciidoctor to version 1.5.8, an eight-year-old release. He digs up the historical context: the pin was introduced in 2019 due to compatibility issues with Asciidoctor 2.0.0, but those issues were later resolved in a 2019 patch. The original discussion even suggested removing the pin once the fixes landed, but the change was never made.

Peff’s tone remains matter-of-fact and non-urgent. He is not demanding action, just pointing out that the CI configuration is now unnecessarily conservative. The implication is that someone could propose a patch to update the pinned version to 2.0.x or later, but no such patch has been posted yet. The original bugfix (adding Ruby as a dependency) remains uncontroversial and ready for merging; this follow-up is purely about future maintenance.
