Thread identity: standalone patch and follow-up series addressing GitLab CI documentation job failures and Asciidoctor version pinning modernization. The thread began with Patrick Steinhardt’s minimal fix (`ps/ci-depends-on-ruby`) to restore Ruby availability for `gem install asciidoctor` after a transitive dependency removal broke the documentation build. It evolved into a forward-looking discussion about CI policy, culminating in Jeff King’s two-patch series (`peff/ci-asciidoctor-bump`) to update the Asciidoctor version tested in CI from 1.5.8 (2018) to a system-provided version (2.0.20 in Ubuntu), eliminating manual version pinning entirely.

Core problem or goal: restore and modernize GitLab CI’s documentation build. The immediate goal was to fix a broken CI job by ensuring Ruby is available for Asciidoctor installation. The broader goal is to eliminate an outdated version pin and simplify CI configuration by using the system-provided Asciidoctor package, reflecting what users are likely to have installed locally and reducing maintenance overhead.
