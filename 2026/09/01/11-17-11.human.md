This is the final documentation patch in Michael Montalbo’s three-part series fixing race conditions in Git’s HTTP daemon test helpers. The patch adds a short comment block in `t/lib-httpd.sh` that explains why state management in these scripts must be atomic and gives concrete examples of safe and unsafe patterns. Patrick Steinhardt’s review is purely surface-level: he suggests a minor wording tweak (“guarded by the marker” instead of “claims the marker”) but calls the series “a lot better now” and does not ask for any technical changes. The series as a whole is ready for integration into `next`; this patch simply documents the atomic idioms already introduced in patches 1 and 2.
