This is the final patch in Elijah Newren’s five-part bugfix series, which extends Git’s protection against unsafe commit operations during conflict resolution to cover partial commits (`git commit <paths>`). The series targets the merge machinery, preventing users from accidentally corrupting repository state by recording only a subset of conflict resolutions during `git am`, `git revert`, and all forms of `git rebase`.

This patch (5/5) reuses the `sequencer_ongoing_operation()` helper introduced earlier in the series to detect active conflict-resolution operations and refuse partial commits. The logic mirrors the `--amend` protection added in patch 4/5, with careful exceptions for non-conflict cases (e.g., interactive rebase directives like `squash` or `edit`). The switch statement in `builtin/commit.c` now covers all relevant operations (`ONGOING_MERGE`, `ONGOING_CHERRY_PICK`, `ONGOING_REVERT`, `ONGOING_AM`, and `ONGOING_REBASE_CONFLICT`), while explicitly permitting `ONGOING_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY` (picks that became empty) and `ONGOING_NONE`.

Test coverage is thorough, adding new cases to `t3404-rebase-interactive.sh` (both interactive and apply backends), `t3507-cherry-pick-conflict.sh`, and `t4151-am-abort.sh`. The error messages are clear and specific: "cannot do a partial commit during a revert", "cannot do a partial commit during an am session", and "cannot do a partial commit while resolving conflicts during a rebase".

The series is technically complete, with all substantive feedback addressed in earlier iterations. Junio C Hamano explicitly approved the structural improvements in this patch, noting the deliberate omission of a `default:` case in the switch statement as a positive detail. The only changes since v3 are mechanical renames (e.g., `ONGOING_REBASE_EMPTY` → `ONGOING_REBASE_NOW_EMPTY`) to clarify intent, with no functional impact. The series is ready for integration and aligns with the project’s ongoing efforts to improve user safety during conflict resolution. Key files touched: `builtin/commit.c`, `sequencer.h`, and test scripts.
