Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series to remove ad-hoc object database (ODB) source linking is now complete. The series refactors the ODB subsystem to eliminate the mechanism for registering in-memory sources—primarily used for submodules and temporary object directories—simplifying the codebase and preparing for future work on alternate handling. The final patch, posted as v2 on September 5, removes the now-unused `odb_add_to_alternates_memory()` function, marking the culmination of the cleanup.

Key changes include removing implicit dependencies on `the_repository` in the `cache-tree` and `submodule-config` subsystems, fixing a memory leak in the packed ODB source backend, and enforcing a long-documented constraint in `git multi-pack-index --object-dir=` that now rejects non-alternate directories. The series also updates test helpers to manage ODB sources explicitly, ensuring they remain functional after the API removal. Junio C Hamano provided substantive approval for earlier patches in v1, and no new objections have emerged. The series is self-contained, with no external dependencies, and is poised for integration once Junio signals his intent to merge it.
