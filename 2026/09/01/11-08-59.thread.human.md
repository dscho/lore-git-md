Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series to remove ad-hoc ODB source linking—a cleanup that also advances the `the_repository` removal effort—is under review, with Junio C Hamano now raising two correctness concerns in the first patch. The series aims to simplify the object database by eliminating the dual-use of ODB sources for both alternates and temporary submodule sources, a refactoring that touches the ODB, submodule handling, cache-tree, and related subsystems.

Junio’s latest feedback on patch 1/12 goes beyond his earlier style-focused suggestion to encapsulate the repository dependency in `cache-tree.c`. He now identifies two potential bugs: one in `write_in_core_index_as_tree()`, where the patch passes an explicit `repo` parameter to `cache_tree_fully_valid()` instead of using the repository embedded in the index state, and another in `unpack_trees.c`, where `the_repository` is still used despite the index state having its own repository field. Both issues could lead to subtle inconsistencies, particularly in submodule or alternate repository scenarios. Junio reiterates his earlier proposal to introduce a thin wrapper like `has_valid_cache_tree(struct index_state *)` to make the interface more robust.

The series remains uncontested on its broader goals, with Junio previously signaling unqualified approval for patch 6/12 (“Yay. Very nice.”). However, the correctness issues in patch 1/12 will need to be addressed before the series can move forward. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, and the series is still at its initial v1, posted on August 31, 2026. The next steps hinge on whether Patrick incorporates Junio’s feedback or engages in further discussion about the proposed fixes.
