This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove ad-hoc source linking, primarily targeting submodule handling. The goal is to simplify the codebase and prepare for future ODB abstraction work, while continuing the broader effort to eliminate global state dependencies like `the_repository`.

The series, posted on August 31, 2026, is under review with no external feedback yet. It combines incidental cleanups (removing `the_repository`/`the_hash_algo` dependencies in subsystems like `cache-tree` and `submodule-config`) with core refactoring (removing submodule ODB source registration in `submodule-config`, `builtin/grep`, and ODB infrastructure). The latest update (patch 7/12) removes a now-dead function, `tmp_objdir_add_as_alternate()`, following its disuse after `builtin/receive-pack` switched to ODB transactions in July. This patch is purely mechanical and uncontroversial, reinforcing the series’ focus on cleanup.

No objections or open questions have been raised so far, and the test suite passes, confirming the changes are behaviorally neutral. The series is self-contained but paves the way for future performance improvements in alternate handling. Patrick has self-reviewed several patches, but broader community review is still pending.
