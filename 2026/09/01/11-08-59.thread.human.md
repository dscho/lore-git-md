This is the second iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series (now 13 patches) refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove the mechanism for registering in-memory sources, primarily used for submodules. The goal is to simplify the codebase by eliminating dual-use of the ODB sources list—currently shared between alternates and temporary sources—preparing for future work to migrate alternate handling into the "files" backend. The series also continues the broader effort to remove implicit dependencies on the global `the_repository` variable.

Version 2 addresses Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the first patch, which now adapts `cache_tree_fully_valid()` to take a `struct index_state *` instead of a repository and cache tree, encapsulating the repository dependency within `cache-tree.c`. This change also fixes two correctness issues Junio identified: ensuring `write_in_core_index_as_tree()` uses `index_state->repo` and removing a lingering `the_repository` reference in `unpack_trees.c`. The rest of the series remains unchanged, including the removal of the ad-hoc source linking API (patch 13/13), updates to test helpers, and incidental cleanups in submodule handling and grep plumbing. Junio’s earlier substantive approval of patch 6/13 ("Yay. Very nice.") suggests the core refactoring is uncontroversial, but the correctness fixes in patch 1/13 will likely determine whether the series is ready for Junio’s `next` branch. No new objections or design concerns have emerged, and the series is now awaiting further review or merge consideration.
