This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series to remove Git’s ad-hoc mechanism for registering in-memory object database (ODB) sources, primarily used for submodules and temporary object directories. The goal is to simplify the ODB subsystem and eliminate implicit dependencies on global state, paving the way for future ODB abstraction work. The series is now in its second version, posted on September 5, 2026, with all patches complete and uncontroversial.

Junio C Hamano gave unqualified approval to two key patches in the first version, and Karthik Nayak reviewed several patches in the second version, confirming the correctness of the repository dependency encapsulation and the removal of unused infrastructure. The series touches multiple subsystems—ODB, submodule handling, cache-tree, and test infrastructure—without changing on-disk formats or user-visible behavior, except for a breaking change in `git multi-pack-index --object-dir=`, which now rejects non-alternate directories as documented. A memory leak in the packed ODB source backend is also fixed.

Justin Tobler recently reviewed patch 5/13, which removes the last non-test call to `odb_add_submodule_source_by_path()` in `submodule-config.c`. His review confirms the patch safely eliminates a 2019 workaround that became obsolete after a 2021 fix, and his approval adds confidence to the series’ correctness. No new objections or open questions remain, though Junio has not yet indicated whether he intends to merge the series to `next`. The refactoring is now fully self-contained, with all callers of the ad-hoc source registration API updated or removed.
