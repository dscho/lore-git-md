This thread is a 12-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that refactors Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem to remove ad-hoc source linking, a mechanism previously used to register in-memory ODB sources (primarily for submodules). The goal is to simplify the codebase by ensuring the ODB sources list contains only the primary source and its alternates, which also prepares the ground for future work to migrate alternate handling into the "files" backend. The series was posted on August 31, 2026, and is under review.

The refactoring touches multiple subsystems, including ODB internals, submodule handling, grep plumbing, cache-tree, and test infrastructure. Key changes include removing the `odb_add_submodule_source_by_path()` and `register_all_submodule_sources()` functions, eliminating the `submodule_source_paths` field from `struct object_database`, and fixing a memory leak in the packed ODB source. The series also enforces an existing constraint in `git multi-pack-index --object-dir=`, which now rejects non-alternate directories, aligning with documented behavior. No on-disk format or behavioral changes are expected beyond the memory leak fix and the breaking change in `multi-pack-index`.

The final patch in the series, just posted, removes the now-unused `odb_add_to_alternates_memory()` function, completing the cleanup. The series has been self-reviewed by Patrick, and no external reviews or objections have been raised yet. The work is part of his broader ODB abstraction effort and continues the ongoing `the_repository` removal project. There are no open questions or loose ends at this stage, and the series is uncontroversial given the incremental and well-scoped nature of the changes.
