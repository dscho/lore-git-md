This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing trace2 hardening discussion. Derrick Stolee acknowledges Jeff King’s point that the `banned-die.h` mechanism, while useful as a short-term enforcement tool, does not fully solve the problem of transitive `die()` calls via dependencies like `strbuf` or `json-writer.c`. He suggests that a future version of the series could drop the `banned-die.h` patch entirely and focus on incremental reductions of `die()` usage in trace2, though he concedes this would still leave the subsystem vulnerable to crashes under memory pressure or system call failures. The exchange underscores the tension between pragmatic progress and the architectural ideal of a ground-up rewrite, but no immediate changes to the series are proposed—this is more of a conceptual checkpoint than a course correction. The thread remains open on whether to land the current series as-is or hold for a more comprehensive solution.
