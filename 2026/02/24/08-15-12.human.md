This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy, marking a significant shift in how Git handles repository maintenance. The patch updates `builtin/gc.c` to set the geometric strategy as the new default while maintaining backward compatibility through the `maintenance.strategy=gc` config option.

The discussion clarifies there are now three maintenance strategies: the traditional "gc" (incremental + all-into-one), "incremental" (fixed-size packs without consolidation), and the new default "geometric" (which delays expensive repacks longer than "gc"). Justin Tobler's review confirms the technical approach is sound, particularly noting geometric's advantages for large repositories.

The patch comes after thorough preparation in the first seven patches of the series, which updated infrastructure and tests. The change represents a well-executed modernization of Git's maintenance approach, with consensus from key reviewers including Derrick Stolee (who designed geometric repacking) and Justin Tobler (who verified test coverage). This is now ready for merging as a clear improvement over the 20-year-old default strategy.
