This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy. It modifies a test in t5400 that verifies git-receive-pack's automatic maintenance behavior.

The test currently assumes maintenance will use the "gc" strategy (which prunes temporary objects), but the series will soon change the default to "geometric" repacking. This patch explicitly configures the test to use "gc" strategy to maintain the expected behavior, ensuring the test continues to pass after the default changes.

The change is straightforward - just adding one config line to the test setup. This follows the pattern seen in earlier patches of methodically preparing the test suite for the upcoming behavior change. The patch maintains the test's verification of receive-pack's maintenance triggering while making it robust against the default strategy change.

This continues the series' careful approach of addressing test interactions before making the actual behavior change in the final patch. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the overall direction already approved by Derrick Stolee.
