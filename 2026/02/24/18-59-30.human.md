This is the third iteration of Taylor Blau's patch series implementing MIDX (multi-pack-index) compaction functionality. The series introduces a new `git multi-pack-index compact` subcommand that combines adjacent MIDX layers while maintaining bitmap compatibility, addressing technical feedback from previous rounds.

The key changes in this version are documentation fixes around the new "midx.version" setting, a bugfix for object selection during compaction, and removal of a now-unneeded utility function (`u32_add()`). The series builds on earlier MIDX infrastructure to enable combining multiple MIDX layers into one while preserving the chain structure and bitmap support.

Taylor notes this should be close to final, with the next planned series introducing compaction-based repacking strategies on top of these changes. The implementation has been reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King, with Junio Hamano evaluating merge readiness. The changes are well-isolated to compaction-specific logic, minimizing risk to existing functionality.

This represents infrastructure work enabling future repacking optimizations rather than a direct user-facing feature. The technical discussion around arithmetic safety and version handling has been thorough, with platform concerns addressed through Git's standard overflow-checking patterns.
