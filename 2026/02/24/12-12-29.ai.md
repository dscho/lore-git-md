This is the final approval for Patrick Steinhardt's series changing Git's default maintenance strategy to geometric repacking. Derrick Stolee, who originally designed the geometric repacking approach, gives his sign-off on the documentation updates in patch 8/8 that make geometric the default for manual maintenance while keeping incremental as the scheduled default.

The change reflects consensus that geometric repacking provides better scaling for modern repositories while maintaining backward compatibility through the preserved "gc" strategy option. The series followed a careful progression from test infrastructure hardening to this final behavior change, with positive reviews throughout from key contributors including Justin Tobler on the infrastructure pieces.

With Stolee's approval, this completes the multi-year effort to improve Git's large-repository performance through geometric repacking, now positioned as the recommended approach. The patch maintains clear documentation of all three options (none, gc, geometric) while updating the defaults to reflect current best practices.
