This is the final approval for Patrick Steinhardt's ref iteration API unification series, marking the completion of a major refactoring effort in Git's refs subsystem. Karthik Nayak gives his sign-off on version 2 of the 17-patch series that consolidates 14 specialized ref iteration functions into a single `refs_for_each_ref_ext()` interface with a configurable options struct.

The series has undergone thorough review, with Karthik noting specific improvements in v2 iteration including better handling of prefix stripping in `refs_for_each_glob_ref_in()` and reduced churn from moving the `refs_for_each_include_root_ref()` removal earlier in the series. Performance optimizations remain intact, notably a 100x speedup (115ms to 1.1ms) for glob iteration demonstrated in patch 7/17.

With this approval, the technical work is complete - all callers have been converted to the new unified API across multiple subsystems, documentation has been updated, and only `refs_for_each_replace_ref()` remains as a special case for future consideration. The series now awaits Junio's merge into the integration branches, representing a significant simplification of Git's internal ref iteration API surface while maintaining all existing behavior.
