This is a bugfix follow-up to the recently merged UTF-8 alias subsection support. Patrick Steinhardt reports a regression where aliases with leading dots (like `alias..foobar`) no longer work after the changes. This was an undocumented but apparently used feature, notably in Johannes Schindelin's "shears" scripts for Git for Windows.

The issue stems from the new subsection-based alias handling unintentionally breaking the ability to define aliases with leading dots in the traditional `[alias]` section format. While this wasn't an officially supported feature, it was being used in practice. Patrick provides a test case demonstrating the regression and notes the impact on existing workflows.

The patch in question has already been merged into `next` with all its core functionality, so this will likely require a follow-up fix to restore the dot-prefix alias behavior while preserving the new UTF-8 subsection support. Given that the original author (Jonatan Holmgren) and reviewers (including Junio Hamano) are aware of the thread, we can expect either a revert of the behavioral change or an adjustment to maintain backward compatibility.
