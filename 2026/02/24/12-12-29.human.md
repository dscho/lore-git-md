This is the final approval for Patrick Steinhardt's series changing Git's default maintenance strategy to geometric repacking. Derrick Stolee, who originally designed the geometric repacking approach, gives his sign-off on the documentation updates in patch 8/8. The patch makes geometric repacking the default for manual maintenance operations while keeping incremental as the scheduled default, completing a carefully staged transition that began with test infrastructure updates in earlier patches.

The change reflects Git's evolution toward better handling of large repositories, where geometric repacking provides significant performance advantages over traditional garbage collection. The series has received thorough review from multiple contributors, with Stolee's approval here representing the last needed validation before Junio can consider merging it. The documentation updates clarify the geometric strategy's role as a full replacement for `gc` while maintaining backward compatibility.
