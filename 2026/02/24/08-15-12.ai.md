This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy, completing a well-reviewed effort to modernize Git's repository maintenance approach for large repositories. The patch modifies `builtin/gc.c` to set `geometric_strategy` as the new default while maintaining backward compatibility through the `maintenance.strategy=gc` config option.

The discussion clarifies there are now three maintenance strategies: the legacy "gc" strategy (incremental packs with periodic all-into-one repacks), the "incremental" strategy (fixed-size packs without all-into-one), and the new "geometric" strategy (which delays expensive repacks longer than "gc"). Justin Tobler confirms the technical correctness of the approach, noting this is particularly benefit large repositories where all-into-one repacks can be prohibitively expensive.

The series demonstrates careful preparation through infrastructure updates before the behavioral change, with thorough test coverage including explicit maintenance.auto=false settings in multiple test files and documentation updates to clarify the new default. Derrick Stolee (designer of geometric repacking) and Justin Tobler (test infrastructure) have both reviewed and approved the approach, showing consensus that this is ready for merging. The change moves Git to a more flexible and scalable maintenance model while maintaining backward compatibility.
