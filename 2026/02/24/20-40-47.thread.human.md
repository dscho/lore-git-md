This thread began as an RFC for an experimental `git repo info` command proposed by GSOC contributor SoutrikDas, exploring default behavior when no fields are specified. The initial plumbing-style design (suggested by Phillip Wood) showed nothing by default, while version 2 proposed showing all fields when using `--all` or no arguments. Maintainer Junio Hamano strongly opposed this, citing Git's plumbing command principles that zero arguments must mean zero output for scripting. This position was reinforced by K Jayatheerth pointing to Pro Git's "Plumbing and Porcelain" chapter, with Lucas Seiki Oshiro further endorsing the maintainer's stance.

The technical consensus is now settled: the command will follow plumbing conventions, showing nothing by default. What followed was a series of encouraging messages to SoutrikDas, emphasizing that proposal rejections are a normal part of Git's review process. Jayatheerth's latest email reassures the contributor that there's no need to apologize, noting how Git's informal communication style differs from more formal Indian conventions. The thread has become a case study in both Git's plumbing design philosophy and the community's approach to mentoring new contributors through rejected proposals - firm on technical decisions while supportive of continued participation. All technical questions about the command's behavior are resolved, with the remaining discussion focused on community norms and contributor experience.
