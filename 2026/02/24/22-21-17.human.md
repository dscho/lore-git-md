This is a feature proposal for configurable branch comparisons in `git status`, now in its 28th iteration. The thread has been ongoing for months, with Junio Hamano (the maintainer) and Jeff King (Peff) discussing implementation trade-offs.

Jeff proposes a simplified initial version that would only support comparing against `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` references, deferring more complex ref resolution cases. This would avoid edge cases around dangling refs and tag handling that came up in earlier discussion. Junio signals approval of this phased approach with his "Sounds like a good way forward" and winking smiley.

The patch author Harald Nordgren and supporter D. Ben Knoble have provided use cases (like comparing work vs home environments), and Nordgren recently added personal testimony about daily use of this feature in his workflow. All technical implementation questions appear resolved at this point - the remaining open question is whether the demonstrated utility justifies inclusion in Git proper.

Junio's reply suggests the feature may soon move forward in this more limited form, though the maintainer's final decision is still pending. Given the thorough discussion and Jeff's proposed simplification, this longstanding proposal appears to be reaching consensus.
