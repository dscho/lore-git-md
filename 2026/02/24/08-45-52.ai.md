This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy, marking a significant shift in Git's repository maintenance approach. The patch changes the default strategy from "gc" to "geometric" for manual maintenance operations while keeping "incremental" as the default for scheduled maintenance. The geometric strategy offers better performance for modern repositories by maintaining a geometric progression of packfiles, reducing the need for expensive all-into-one repacks that were problematic in large repositories.

The patch updates both the documentation (to reflect the new default and clarify strategy recommendations) and the implementation in builtin/gc.c. The change is well-motivated by the evolution of repository sizes and maintenance needs since Git's early days, though the commit message notes that geometric repacking isn't a perfect solution for all cases - extremely large repositories may still need the "incremental" strategy.

This concludes the carefully structured 8-patch series that first hardened test infrastructure (patches 1-7) before making this behavior change. The series has received positive feedback, particularly from Derrick Stolee who designed the geometric repacking approach. With this patch, Git's default maintenance behavior will better serve modern repository scales while maintaining backward compatibility through configurable strategies.
