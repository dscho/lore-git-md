This is a follow-up discussion about a const-correctness patch series that addresses new compiler warnings from glibc 2.43 in C23 mode. Jeff King (Peff) is reviewing Collin Funk's patch that adds const qualifiers to pointers that aren't modified, particularly those receiving results from string search functions.

Peff confirms the technical correctness of all changes and notes they match his own earlier review pass. While the change is technically correct, Peff suggests keeping the patch focused on const-correctness alone, either by preserving the initialization or splitting that cleanup into a separate patch.

The exchange demonstrates Git's attention to both technical correctness and patch organization - even small, mechanical changes receive careful review for both functional impact and maintainability.
