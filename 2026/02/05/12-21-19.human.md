Bugfix discussion regarding symlink handling in whitespace checks. Patrick Steinhardt raises two thoughtful points about Junio's proposed fix for incorrect WS_INCOMPLETE_LINE handling on symbolic links.

He suggests more precise conditions: only disable when one side is missing (and the other a symlink) or when both sides are symlinks.

Second, he questions whether symlinks with trailing newlines should be supported (acknowledging this is a rare edge case). While he doesn't strongly object, the observation shows careful consideration of corner cases.

This is a constructive review from a senior contributor, identifying potential edge cases in what is otherwise a straightforward fix.
