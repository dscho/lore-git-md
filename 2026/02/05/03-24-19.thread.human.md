This thread concerns a patch series by Collin Funk addressing const-correctness warnings that appear when compiling Git with newer versions of glibc (2.43) in C23 mode. The changes add const qualifiers to pointers receiving results from string search functions where the underlying data isn't modified, maintaining correctness without altering behavior. A separate cleanup patch removes an unnecessary NULL initialization in gpg-interface.c.

The series has gone through two iterations. In v1, Jeff King (Peff) reviewed and approved the technical approach. Junio Hamano then suggested splitting the changes into two patches - one for the const-correctness fixes (touching 28 files) and another for the isolated gpg-interface.c cleanup. Collin incorporated this feedback in v2, and Junio has since reviewed the gpg-interface.c patch, suggesting a minor wording improvement in the commit message.

The latest review comes from Ben Knoble, who offers a purely stylistic suggestion about patch email formatting - placing review notes after rather than before the triple-dash separator. This represents final polish on the presentation rather than any substantive change. With the technical approach approved and the organizational feedback addressed, the series appears ready for merging once these last minor presentation tweaks are incorporated.
