This thread addresses a series of mechanical changes to improve const-correctness in Git's codebase, prompted by new warnings from glibc 2.43 in C23 mode. Collin Funk authored patches adding const qualifiers to pointers receiving results from string search functions (like strrchr() and memchr()) where the pointed-to data isn't modified, without changing any behavior.

The series received thorough technical review from Jeff King (Peff), who confirmed the correctness of the approach, and final approval from maintainer Junio Hamano. The only remaining discussion point was whether to split out a NULL initialization removal in gpg-interface.c from the const changes. While Collin had included it in the same patch since he was modifying the line, he acknowledged Git's preference for more granular changes and committed to sending a v2 that separates this change.

With Junio's approval of the overall approach and Collin's agreement to the organizational suggestion, the technical discussion appears concluded. The series is now ready for integration once Collin submits the final version with the split patch. This work continues Git's ongoing effort to maintain compatibility with modern C standards and compiler versions.
