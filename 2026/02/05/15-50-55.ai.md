This is a follow-up discussion in the whitespace/symlink bugfix thread, where Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review of the proposed fix. The exchange focuses on refining the conditions under which whitespace checks should be skipped for symbolic links.

Junio acknowledges Patrick's suggestion to only disable WS_INCOMPLETE_LINE checks when either (1) one side is missing and the other is a symlink, or (2) both sides are symlinks. However, Junio notes this may need further adjustment to properly handle text-to-symlink transitions where we wouldn't want to complain about missing newlines in the post-image.

The discussion also touches on the theoretical edge case of newline-terminated symlink targets, with both agreeing this is sufficiently rare to not be a priority. Junio questions whether Git even handles symrefs (like .git/HEAD) in the diff/apply context, suggesting they may be relevant to the discussion.

The key outstanding issue is determining the precise conditions for skipping whitespace checks during mode changes involving symlinks. While the initial approach seems sound, the maintainer recognizes the need for additional refinement to cover all transition cases properly. The exchange shows the maintainer and reviewer collaboratively working through the edge cases of what initially appeared to be a straightforward fix.
