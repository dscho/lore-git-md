Const-correctness patch series addressing compiler warnings in C23 mode. This is the final patch in Collin Funk's series that adds const qualifiers to pointers receiving results from search functions like strrchr() and memchr() where the pointed-to data isn't modified, fixing warnings from GCC 15 with -std=gnu23.

The changes are mechanical and behavior-preserving, touching 28 files but with minimal actual code impact - mostly just adding 'const' to pointer declarations. Junio Hamano has reviewed and approved the patch, agreeing with Jeff King's earlier assessment that the changes are correct, though noting that the removal of a NULL initialization in gpg-interface.c (from an earlier patch in the series) would have been better as a separate change.

This work is part of Git's ongoing effort to maintain compatibility with modern C standards and compiler versions. The patch follows established patterns for const-correctness that Jeff King has worked on previously. With Junio's approval, this series appears ready for merging into the main branch.
