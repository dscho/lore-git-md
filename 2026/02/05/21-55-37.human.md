This is a CI infrastructure patch addressing a test breakage caused by a buggy `dirname` implementation in Ubuntu rolling release's uutils coreutils. The thread started with Junio Hamano proposing to switch several CI jobs from `ubuntu:rolling` to `ubuntu:latest` as an immediate fix, affecting tests for SHA-256, reftable, leaks detection, and other key areas.

Collin Funk's reply offers an alternative approach - keeping `ubuntu:rolling` but installing GNU coreutils alongside uutils - while explicitly stating no preference between the options. The suggestion demonstrates technical awareness but doesn't push for adoption, leaving the decision to maintainers. The exchange highlights the tradeoff between stability (using known-good `ubuntu:latest`) versus helping test bleeding-edge components (sticking with `ubuntu:rolling`).

The patch itself is straightforward infrastructure maintenance with minimal risk, but the discussion touches on the broader question of how aggressively Git's CI should adopt rolling distro releases. Junio's original patch seems likely to proceed given its simplicity and the importance of CI stability, though the thread may continue exploring long-term strategy.
