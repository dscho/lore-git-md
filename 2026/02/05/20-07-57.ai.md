Maintainer response to l10n AI workflow patches. Junio C Hamano acknowledges Jiang Xin's two-part series introducing AI-assisted translation workflows with a measured response. He expresses mild regret about the need for two different PO file filters (one stripping both filenames and locations, another preserving filenames) rather than a single unified approach, but recognizes this as a practical starting point. The maintainer particularly praises the inclusion of ready-to-use configuration snippets in po/README.md that translators can copy-paste, noting this will lower adoption friction.

This email signals Junio's tacit approval of the technical implementation while avoiding direct engagement with the ongoing philosophical debates about AI assistance that other thread participants (notably brian m. carlson and Michal Suchanek) have raised. His focus remains narrowly on the mechanics of the filter configuration rather than broader questions about translation quality or legal implications. The response suggests these patches are likely to be merged in their current form, leaving the more contentious discussions about AI's role in l10n work for future threads.
