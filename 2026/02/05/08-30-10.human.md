This is a discussion thread about using AI tools in Git's localization (l10n) workflow, specifically for translating and reviewing `.po` files. Michal Suchánek responds to Jiang Xin's proposal for optional AI-assisted translation aids, expressing skepticism about the approach.

Michal argues that AI tools cannot effectively assess translation quality and that relying on them would effectively put AI "in charge" of reviews. He interprets Jiang Xin's mention of using AI to detect inappropriate content (like ads or political text) as evidence that human reviewers are not adequately scrutinizing translations. His position aligns with earlier objections from Peter Krefting, who strongly opposes AI use for Swedish translations.

The exchange highlights a cultural divide in the l10n community between those who see AI as a pragmatic aid (Jiang Xin demonstrates successful use in Chinese translations) and those who view it as a threat to quality and human oversight. The thread remains unresolved, with fundamental disagreements about whether AI should play any role in the translation process.
