The series consists of two well-polished patches:
1. A refactoring preparation patch (159 lines) that reorganizes format_branch_comparison() in remote.c
2. The feature implementation (337 test lines) adding the new `status.compareBranches` config option that accepts a space-separated list of @{upstream}/@{push} comparisons
