This is a follow-up review from Junio Hamano on Justin Tobler's repository statistics series, specifically addressing patch 2/5 which tracks largest objects by type. The email focuses on two technical concerns in the implementation.

This is particularly relevant for repositories with minimal objects where sentinel values matter.
