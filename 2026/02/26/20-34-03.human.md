This is a small infrastructure patch in Taylor Blau's RFC series about incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The patch introduces `strvec_init_alloc()`, a variant of `strvec_init()` that allows pre-allocating space for a known number of elements in a string vector.

Junio notes in his reply that while it would be cleaner to make `strvec_init()` a thin wrapper around this new function, that isn't straightforward due to an existing optimization where empty strvecs avoid allocation entirely. He gives the change his tacit approval despite this minor aesthetic concern.

The patch is a straightforward addition to Git's string handling utilities, providing a more efficient way to initialize strvecs when the eventual size is known in advance. It's a small but useful building block for the larger repacking work in the series. The change is well-contained (just 7 lines of implementation) and clearly motivated by the kind of performance-sensitive string handling needed in the MIDX repacking code.
