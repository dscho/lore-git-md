The patch adds documentation for the `am.messageId` config option, which controls whether `git am` adds a Message-ID trailer based on the email header when applying patches. The text explains this relates to the `--message-id` and `--no-message-id` command-line options and references the `git-interpret-trailers` documentation for context about trailer handling.

The change follows the pattern seen in earlier patches of the series - standardizing command references with `linkgit` markup and connecting related documentation.
