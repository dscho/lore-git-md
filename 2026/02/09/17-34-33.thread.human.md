This thread documents a series of improvements to the `git am` documentation by Kristoffer Haugsbakk, a frequent documentation contributor. The four-patch series started with mechanical changes to standardize command references and progressed to substantive clarifications about trailer behavior and configuration options.

The first three patches standardized `linkgit` markup for command references, clarified that `--message-id` and `--signoff` operate by adding trailers (linking to `git-interpret-trailers`), and documented the `am.messageId` config option. The just-added fourth patch completes the series by improving hook documentation - it adds the positive `--verify` option to the synopsis (previously only `--no-verify` was shown) and clarifies that `post-applypatch` hooks cannot be skipped.

The author noted a conflict with another of their documentation topics in Junio's `seen` branch, but no technical concerns have been raised about the changes themselves. The series follows established documentation patterns and appears ready for Junio to consider picking up into `next`.
