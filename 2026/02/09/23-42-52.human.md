Bugfix for `git send-email` option handling. Jim Cromie reports that the `--suppress-to` and `--suppress-to=all` options are being incorrectly passed to `format-patch` instead of being handled by `send-email`, causing fatal errors. The issue also affects `--no-to` and `--no-cc` options, which fail to suppress CC addresses as expected.

The problem appears to be a misrouting of email-sending options to the patch-formatting step. This is a clear bug that would affect anyone trying to use these suppression flags with `send-email`. The report includes concrete error messages showing the failure modes, making it easy to reproduce. No patch is attached - this appears to be an initial bug report rather than a proposed fix.
