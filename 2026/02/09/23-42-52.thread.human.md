Here's a human-readable summary of the thread so far:

The thread reports a bug in `git send-email` where suppression options like `--suppress-to` and `--no-to` aren't working as expected. Jim Cromie found that these options, which should prevent automatic recipient additions when sending patches, are being incorrectly passed through to `format-patch` instead of being handled by `send-email` itself. This causes fatal errors when trying to precisely control the recipient list.

The problem affects both the bare `--suppress-to` option and its `=all` variant, as well as the `--no-to` and `--no-cc` combinations. The bug appears to be in how the options are processed between the two commands rather than a fundamental design issue. While no fix has been proposed yet, the report clearly demonstrates the broken behavior through example command invocations and their error outputs. The issue primarily affects users who need fine-grained control over email recipients when sending patches.
