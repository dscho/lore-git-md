Tso highlights the risks of Unicode handling by referencing a past CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures) where ext4's case folding with Unicode normalization created a security vulnerability in Git. He strongly warns against diving into Unicode complexity, noting that while UTF-8 aliases might be desirable for some users, the implementation risks are substantial. The comment serves as a reality check on the technical challenges raised earlier in the thread, particularly around normalization and case folding.
