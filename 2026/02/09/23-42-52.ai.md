Bugfix targeting `git send-email`'s handling of suppression options. Jim Cromie reports that `--suppress-to` and related options are being incorrectly passed through to `format-patch` instead of being handled by `send-email` itself, causing fatal errors. The issue manifests when trying to suppress automatic recipient additions (To/Cc lines) during email sending.

The problem occurs with both bare `--suppress-to` and the `=all` variant, suggesting a general parsing failure in the option handoff between `send-email` and `format-patch`. The commit message notes that even `--no-to --no-cc` fails to prevent CC additions from the file headers as expected.

This appears to be a straightforward bug in option processing rather than a design issue. The patch doesn't propose a fix but clearly demonstrates the broken behavior through command-line invocations and error outputs. The impact is limited to users trying to precisely control recipient lists when sending patches via email.
