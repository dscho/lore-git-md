This is a continuation of the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the `read_mmblob()` function in `xdiff-interface.c`. The change propagates through callers in several merge-related files (`apply.c`, `checkout.c`, etc.), updating them to pass `the_repository->objects` explicitly.

Junio Hamano notes in his reply that passing the full `struct repository *` would have been another valid approach, but approves of the current patch's choice to pass just the minimal required `object_database` structure. The changes are mechanical and don't affect functionality, just making the object database dependencies more explicit.
