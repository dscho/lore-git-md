The patch adds `STATIC_CURL_LIBS` to relevant Makefile targets to properly handle static linking of curl dependencies. It defines this variable as empty by default in `Shared.mak` to maintain backward compatibility with existing builds. 

A more subtle change renames the "error" symbol in `git-compat-util.h` and `usage.c` to avoid naming conflicts during static linking. This appears to be a real issue the author encountered in their container builds, though the commit message doesn't specify what exactly was conflicting with Git's error symbol.

The patch is narrowly focused and appears to solve a concrete problem the author faced in their environment. While the changes are small, they touch build infrastructure which can be sensitive to platform differences.
