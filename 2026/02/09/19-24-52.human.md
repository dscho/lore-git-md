This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the `xdiff-interface.c` file. René Scharfe's patch refactors the `read_mmblob()` function to take an explicit `struct object_database *odb` parameter instead of implicitly using `the_repository->objects`. This change propagates to all callers in `apply.c`, `checkout.c`, `merge-file.c`, `merge-ort.c`, and `notes-merge.c`.

The patch also includes two notable improvements: replacing `oideq(oid, null_oid(the_hash_algo))` with the more direct `is_null_oid(oid)`, and removing an unnecessary `#include "repository.h"` that was identified during review. The changes are mechanical but carefully considered, with Elijah Newren noting that while this patch correctly passes `the_repository->objects` in merge-ort.c, future work should use `opt->repo` where available to fully eliminate `the_repository` dependencies.

The patch has gone through review with Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano, who approved the minimal parameter design approach. This is a well-executed step in the larger effort to make Git's codebase more modular by removing global state dependencies.
