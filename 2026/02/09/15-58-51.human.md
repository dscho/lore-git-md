Security-focused discussion on `git am` patch parsing ambiguities. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's analysis of the long-standing issue where `git am` can misinterpret embedded diffs in commit messages as actual patches to apply. While acknowledging that unambiguous parsing may be impossible, Patrick proposes a middle ground: have `git am` detect ambiguous patches and require explicit confirmation (via a new `--accept-ambiguous-patch` flag) before proceeding, shifting the security burden to the user while maintaining awareness.
