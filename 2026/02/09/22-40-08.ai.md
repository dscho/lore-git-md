This is a technical discussion about Unicode normalization in the context of the UTF-8 alias names implementation. brian m. carlson raises concerns about the lack of Unicode normalization handling in Git's proposed UTF-8 alias support, arguing it's not just a macOS issue.

The email explains that without normalization, composed and decomposed forms of the same character (like French "où" written as "où" with combining accent) won't match byte-for-byte, potentially causing alias lookup failures. It also notes the complexity of different normalization forms (canonical vs compatibility) and mentions macOS's non-standard normalization approach.

This is a substantive technical concern about edge cases in the alias implementation, though it comes after the design appears largely settled. The argument suggests the current patch may need additional work to properly handle Unicode equivalence, particularly for users mixing input methods or platforms. The email doesn't propose a specific fix but implies the current bytewise comparison approach could lead to subtle usability issues with non-ASCII aliases.
