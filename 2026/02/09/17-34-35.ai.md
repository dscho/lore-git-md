Documentation patch clarifying `git am` behavior and cross-references. This is the second patch in a 4-part series improving `git am` documentation, following an initial mechanical standardization of command references.

The patch makes two substantive improvements to `git-am.adoc`:
1. Explicitly states that `--message-id` adds a trailer (not just any header), linking to `git-interpret-trailers` for context. Also standardizes the formatting of `Message-ID` as inline-verbatim.
2. Similarly updates the `--signoff` documentation to reference `git-interpret-trailers`, making it clear that signoff is also a trailer operation.

The changes are straightforward and align with the project's documentation standards.
