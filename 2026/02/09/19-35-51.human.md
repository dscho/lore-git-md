Junio C Hamano responds to Toon Claes's proposal for visualizing truncated history in the `--untangle` graph output. He recalls that `gitk` used a similar approach with arrow markers to show where graph lines were connected when parts of the history were omitted. Junio suggests extending Toon's proposed '.' marker idea by adding labels (like (a)..(z)) to help users match the truncated connections, though he notes this might be more feasible in an interactive tool like `gitk` than in the terminal-based `git log --graph`.

This continues the thread's focus on how to clearly represent complex merge histories when using the proposed `--untangle` option, particularly when parts of the ancestry are intentionally omitted. Junio's input carries weight as the maintainer, indicating he sees value in the general approach but is thinking about how to make the visualization more informative. The discussion remains in the RFC stage, with no immediate resolution on the visualization markers.
