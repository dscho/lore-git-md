This thread covers Patrick Steinhardt's patch series addressing test failures that occur on Windows/MSVC when Git is built with ICONV support but the `iconv(1)` executable is unavailable - a common scenario since MinGW no longer ships the executable. The series modifies several test scripts (t6006, t4205, t4041, t4059, t4060, and t5550) to handle this distinction between libiconv (linked into Git) and the iconv executable (used in tests).

The fifth and final patch in the v4 series modifies t6006-rev-list-format.sh, making two tests conditional on the ICONV prerequisite with UTF-8 encoding as a fallback. This follows the consistent pattern established across all patches in the series. The approach has been reviewed and approved by Christian Couder and Junio Hamano, with Eric Sunshine providing final polish for style consistency (using test_grep) in t5550.

The solution maintains test coverage while improving portability, particularly for Windows/MSVC environments. While a future consideration remains about potentially splitting ICONV prerequisite checks (noted as NEEDSWORK), the immediate issue is fully addressed. The series is now ready for merging after three rounds of refinement, demonstrating careful attention to both test correctness and cross-platform compatibility.
