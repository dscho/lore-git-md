Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing ICONV-related test failures has reached its final form after three iterations, with all major technical concerns resolved. The patches handle the distinction between libiconv (used by Git itself) and the `iconv(1)` executable (used in test scripts) across multiple test files, particularly important for Windows/MSVC environments where the executable may be unavailable. Christian Couder and Junio Hamano have already approved the series, with Junio noting the clever handling of UTF-8 fallback behavior when ICONV is unavailable.

These are minor style adjustments that don't affect the technical correctness of the solution. The series demonstrates Git's attention to test portability and completes the work started in GitHub issue #6083 regarding iconv(1) in Git for Windows. With all feedback incorporated and approvals in place, the patches are ready for merging into the main branch.
