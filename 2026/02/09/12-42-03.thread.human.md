Here's a concise human-readable summary of the thread:

Patrick Steinhardt has completed a series addressing test failures that occur on Windows/MSVC when Git is built with ICONV support but lacks the `iconv(1)` executable. The key insight was distinguishing between libiconv (used by Git internally) and the `iconv(1)` command-line tool (used in tests). The five-patch series updates six test scripts to properly handle environments where the executable isn't available, making them depend on an ICONV prerequisite and falling back to UTF-8 encoding when needed.

After three rounds of iteration, the series received approval from both Christian Couder and Junio Hamano. The final v4 version incorporates Eric Sunshine's polish - fixing a typo and standardizing test_grep style in the t5550 HTTP tests. The changes maintain test coverage while improving portability, particularly important for Windows/MSVC CI environments where MinGW no longer ships `iconv(1)`. While a future refinement to split ICONV prerequisite checks remains possible (noted as NEEDSWORK), the immediate issue is fully resolved and the series is ready for merging.
