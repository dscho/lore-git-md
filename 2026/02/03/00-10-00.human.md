This is part 2 of a 4-patch series in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, preparing for pluggable backends to handle transactions differently. The patch focuses on renaming transaction-related functions from `object_file_transaction_*` to `odb_transaction_files_*` to better reflect their role in the files backend specifically.

The changes are mechanical but important for clarity as the architecture evolves - these functions will soon be just one possible implementation of the transaction interface rather than the only one. The patch updates callers in `object-file.c` and `odb.c` while keeping the same behavior. This is part of a larger consensus-driven effort that has seen detailed review from both Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, with agreement that the direction is sound.

The series appears to be in its final polishing stages, with this patch representing straightforward cleanup in service of the broader goal. The rename helps distinguish files-backend specifics from the generic ODB transaction interface that will accommodate future backends.
