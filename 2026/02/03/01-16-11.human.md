This is the final approval for a patch series in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, specifically addressing transaction handling for pluggable backends. Junio C Hamano gives the green light to Justin Tobler's 4-part series after reviewing the changes from v1, which include renaming transaction types to clarify their purpose (`odb_transaction_files`) and deferring work on the `tmp_objdir` subsystem to a future series.

Junio notes the changes appear minimal in this iteration - mostly renaming and leaving the tmp_objdir integration for later - but agrees this focused approach makes sense. His "Looking good. Will replace." indicates the series meets Git's quality bar and will be merged into the integration branches. This marks successful completion of an architectural shift that moves common transaction management logic from the files backend to the generic ODB layer while preserving backend-specific behavior, a key step in enabling alternative object storage implementations. The work builds cleanly on prior ODB abstraction patches from Justin and Patrick Steinhardt, with all technical concerns addressed through the review process.
