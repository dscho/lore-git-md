Justin Tobler's 5-patch series for repository statistics in `git repo` (v1) continues evolving through community feedback. The patches modify `builtin/repo.c` and tests (t1901) to track repository metrics, with technical foundation including `largest_objects` struct, SHA-1/SHA-256 compatible OID handling, and comprehensive t1901 tests.

Key developments:
- Octopus merge tracking (commits with most parents) faces strong opposition from Junio Hamano due to bisection complications and historical irrelevance, with git-sizer precedent (Kristoffer Haugsbakk) suggesting removal
- Emerging consensus favors "most children" as more operationally useful metric
- Junio Hamano clarifies his position on maximum-value reporting versus histograms, acknowledging that experienced users can use extreme values as effective signals for anomalies without full distribution data
- Patrick Steinhardt's defense of maximum-value reporting finds acceptance, suggesting these metrics may remain in the series while histogram functionality could be deferred
- Technical discussion on streaming histogram implementation with dynamic bucket sizing continues as potential follow-up work
- Current focus areas:
  * Finalizing octopus merge metric exclusion (highly likely)
  * Including maximum-value reporting for key metrics
  * Considering "most children" addition
  * Evaluating histogram implementation as separate future work
