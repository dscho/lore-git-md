This is a bugfix and behavior modification for `git stash` that addresses how it handles ignored files during index updates. The patch introduces new `--overwrite-ignore` and `--no-overwrite-ignore` options to control whether ignored files should be overwritten, with backward-compatible defaults.

Elijah Newren raises several substantive concerns about the patch's design and justification. He questions whether the new flags are solving a real user need rather than just addressing internal consistency, and points out confusing interactions between `--all` and `--no-overwrite-ignore` that silently modify behavior rather than erroring out. He also notes the patch appears to be based on local changes not yet in upstream Git, making review difficult.

The technical implementation in `builtin/stash.c` modifies how `INCLUDE_ALL_FILES` interacts with the new options, downgrading to `include-untracked` mode when `--no-overwrite-ignore` is specified. Tests in `t3905` verify the behavior, though Newren argues they don't fully capture the user-facing problem being solved.

While previous reviewers (D. Ben Knoble, Kristoffer Haugsbakk) had their technical and documentation feedback addressed, Newren's latest critique goes deeper, challenging the fundamental approach. The patch now faces questions about its user benefit justification and whether silent behavior modification is the right design choice versus explicit errors for incompatible options.
