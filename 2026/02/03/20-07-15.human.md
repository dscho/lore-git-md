Patch withdrawal: `git stash` behavior modification

Pushkar Singh is withdrawing his patch series that aimed to modify how `git stash` handles ignored files. The series had introduced new `--overwrite-ignore` and `--no-overwrite-ignore` options to control whether stash operations would overwrite ignored files, attempting to address a long-standing FIXME in the code.

The withdrawal comes after substantial critique from Elijah Newren, who questioned the fundamental design and user-facing justification for the changes. Newren raised several concerns about silent behavior changes, contradictory option combinations, and whether the patch solved real user problems rather than just implementation details.

Pushkar acknowledges misunderstanding some of the earlier feedback and agrees the approach needs rethinking from first principles. He plans to study the existing stash behavior more thoroughly before potentially resubmitting with a clearer user-driven rationale. This marks a pause in what had been an active discussion thread with multiple rounds of technical feedback from other contributors.
