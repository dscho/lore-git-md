This is part 2 of a 4-patch series in the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, focusing on making object database transactions more generic to support pluggable backends. The patch renames transaction-related functions from `object_file_transaction_{begin,commit}` to `odb_transaction_files_{begin,commit}` to better reflect their role as files-backend-specific implementations of the broader ODB transaction interface.

The changes are mechanical renames affecting three files (`object-file.c`, `object-file.h`, and `odb.c`), updating both function definitions and their declarations. The new names make clear these are files-backend implementations, while the generic ODB transaction interface remains unchanged (still using `odb_transaction_{begin,commit}`). This prepares for future patches where different ODB sources could provide their own transaction handling while maintaining a common interface.

The patch builds on prior discussion where Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt agreed on moving from low-level temporary directory management to higher-level transactional semantics. It follows the architectural direction established in earlier iterations of the series, with no functional changes - just renaming to clarify the division between generic ODB transactions and files-backend specifics. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial given the prior consensus on approach.
