This is a bugfix follow-up in the LOP (Large Object Promisors) series, specifically addressing a NULL pointer dereference issue in the `list_objects_filter_release()` function that Jeff King identified in the v3 iteration. Christian Couder acknowledges the issue and confirms the fix has been incorporated into v4 by moving the `allow_auto_filter` assignment after the NULL check. The change is minimal but important for robustness, ensuring the function won't segfault if passed a NULL pointer. This email represents the final resolution of the issue Jeff raised, with the fix now included in the v4 series that Patrick Steinhardt has approved as merge-ready. The thread shows the project's attention to edge cases even in final review stages of a well-vetted series.
