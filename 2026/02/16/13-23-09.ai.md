This is part 3 of a 9-patch series implementing secure storage of promisor remote fields and auto-filtering for partial clones. The series is now in its fourth iteration and has received extensive review, with Patrick Steinhardt, Jean-Noël Avila, and Christian Couder providing feedback across the patches.

This particular patch is a refactoring change that makes the `filter_options` variable in `builtin/clone.c` local to the `cmd_clone()` function rather than being a static file-level variable. The change clarifies ownership of the filter options by making it explicit that they are managed entirely within the clone command's scope. The patch passes the filter options as a parameter to `checkout()` and ensures proper cleanup by adding a `list_objects_filter_release()` call before returning from `cmd_clone()`.

The change is straightforward and mechanical, with no behavior modifications. It serves as preparation for subsequent patches in the series that will make more substantial use of the filter options. The diff shows careful attention to updating all relevant function calls and parameter passing while maintaining the existing functionality. This kind of ownership clarification is typical in the ongoing effort to make Git's codebase more maintainable and easier to reason about.
