René responds to Elijah's suggestion about using `opt->repo` instead of `the_repository` in the merge machinery. The email then shifts to discussing strategies for the broader `the_repository` removal effort. Finally, René asks if anyone knows of good tools for analyzing C function call graphs to identify indirect `the_repository` dependencies, mentioning cscope could potentially be scripted for this but might not be efficient. This technical question about tooling reflects the challenge of systematically eliminating global state in a large codebase.
