This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about a patch that refactored `read_mmblob()` to take an explicit object database parameter.

René Scharfe responds to earlier feedback from Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren about his patch that removed `the_repository` usage from `xdiff-interface.c`. The key point of discussion was whether the merge-ort caller should use `opt->repo->objects` rather than `the_repository->objects` to avoid reintroducing global dependencies. 

Junio had strongly advocated for using the existing `opt->repo` parameter when available, rather than deferring the cleanup. René acknowledges this was the right approach, noting that using `opt->repo` wouldn't have been immediately obvious to him as the patch author. His response shows the collaborative nature of these refactorings - even experienced contributors benefit from others spotting better ways to structure the changes.

The exchange highlights how removing `the_repository` often requires considering both the immediate change and the broader architectural context of call chains. While the technical change here is small (just a few lines), it's part of Git's careful, incremental approach to large-scale refactoring where each patch moves the codebase toward the long-term goal while maintaining correctness.
