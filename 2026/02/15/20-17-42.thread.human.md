The three-part solution first reduced function side effects by converting `process_subtree_split_trailer()` to use subshell output (patch 1), then functionalized the traversal logic (patch 2), and now completes with this third patch that modifies `check_parents()` to process commit ranges in bulk rather than recursively. This final change specifically targets the problematic deep history cases by discovering and handling entire commit ranges at once until reaching the next rejoin point.

All changes remain contained to `contrib/subtree/git-subtree.sh`, with the series totaling approximately 150 lines added and 13 removed across the three patches. While the cover letter mentioned testing on a large repository, the thread hasn't yet included specific performance measurements or confirmation that the changes resolve the issue on affected Debian/Ubuntu systems.
