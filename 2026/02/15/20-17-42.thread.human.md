Colin Stagner has sent a patch series addressing recursion limits in `git subtree split` that occur on Debian/Ubuntu systems with default POSIX shells, where deep history graphs can hit a 1000-call recursion limit. The series has progressed to version 2, rebased onto current master but with no substantive changes to the patches themselves.

The three-patch series restructures the split operation's rejoin-deepening algorithm through coordinated changes: first reducing function side effects in trailer processing, then functionalizing the traversal logic, and most significantly replacing recursive processing in `check_parents()` with bulk range processing. The final patch adds 54 lines of carefully commented code to handle commit ranges in bulk until the next rejoin point.

While the technical approach appears sound, the series still lacks performance measurements or confirmation that it resolves the issue on affected Debian/Ubuntu systems, despite the cover letter mentioning testing on a large repository. The changes are confined to `contrib/subtree/git-subtree.sh` and don't interact with other in-flight topics. The series is technically complete but awaits its first round of review feedback from the community.
