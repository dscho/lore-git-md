This is a follow-up to a refactoring series that cleaned up the ref-filter component stripping functions. Jeff King (Peff) notes one remaining cleanup opportunity but deliberately chooses not to pursue it in this series.

He humorously notes this demonstrates his restraint in not chasing every possible cleanup.

The series appears complete and well-received, with this email serving as a thoughtful postscript about knowing when to stop refactoring. It shows Git's careful balance between thorough code improvement and scope control.
