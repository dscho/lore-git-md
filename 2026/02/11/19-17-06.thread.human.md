Junio C Hamano has proposed documenting the meaning and handling of `NEEDSWORK` comments in Git's codebase, adding a section to `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`. These comments, which are common throughout the code, represent unvetted ideas for potential future work rather than mandatory todos. The patch clarifies that evaluating whether the suggested work makes sense is often more important than implementing it, and that removing such comments with justification can be a valid resolution.

Patrick Steinhardt has responded in agreement with Junio's proposal, noting that some recent patch series have incorrectly treated `NEEDSWORK` comments as direct instructions. While Patrick mused that "NEEDSTHOUGHTS" might be a more accurate term, he does not propose changing the established convention. The discussion highlights alignment between the maintainer and core contributors on this documentation improvement, which will help set clear expectations for contributors encountering these comments in the future. The patch appears uncontroversial and likely to be merged as-is.
