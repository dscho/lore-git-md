This thread documents the previously unwritten conventions around `NEEDSWORK` comments in Git's codebase. Junio C Hamano proposed formalizing that these comments represent unvetted ideas that may or may not be worth implementing, emphasizing that evaluating whether the suggested work makes sense is often more important than doing the work itself. Patrick Steinhardt provided substantive review feedback, initially suggesting `NEEDSTHOUGHTS` might better convey the intent, but ultimately agreed with Junio's position that thinking is inherent in the work.

After Junio incorporated Patrick's feedback into a v2 patch, D. Ben Knoble caught a minor formatting issue - an unclosed quotation mark in the commit message. This small but precise observation is characteristic of Knoble's attention to detail in documentation and build system matters. The thread demonstrates Git's collaborative review process in action, where even maintainer-authored patches receive public scrutiny before merging. With this documentation now finalized, contributors have a clear reference point for understanding and working with `NEEDSWORK` comments throughout the codebase.
