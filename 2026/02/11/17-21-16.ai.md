Performance optimization for `git pack-objects` in partial clone scenarios, specifically addressing unnecessary object fetches during geometric repacking of promisor packs.

Junio Hamano has reviewed and approved Patrick Steinhardt's patch that prevents unnecessary object fetches when merging packs with `--stdin-packs` during geometric repacking. The patch addresses a performance issue that emerged after commit dcc9c7ef47 enabled geometric repacking of promisor packs, where the rev walk for delta selection heuristics was triggering backfill fetches for missing promisor objects.

The solution is straightforward - temporarily disabling fetching during this specific rev walk since the fetched objects aren't actually needed for the pack merging operation. Junio notes the implementation's simplicity (relying on a global variable) and the clear explanation in the commit message. He points out a minor typo ("passe" -> "pass") but otherwise considers the patch ready for inclusion.

This change is particularly beneficial for large repositories using partial clones, where unnecessary fetches during repacking operations can significantly impact performance. The patch builds on earlier work around geometric repacking infrastructure and promisor pack handling. With this approval from the maintainer, the optimization will be queued for inclusion in the next release.
