Patrick Steinhardt's optimization to prevent unnecessary object fetches during geometric repacking of promisor packs has reached a key milestone. The thread began with Patrick identifying a performance issue where `git pack-objects` was triggering backfill fetches during pack merging operations, even though these objects weren't needed for the actual repacking. This particularly affected large repositories using partial clones.

After initial review from Jeff King (who suggested test improvements) and supportive feedback from Taylor Blau about the optimization's value, the patch has now received maintainer approval. Junio Hamano reviewed the final version, praising its straightforward implementation (using a global variable to temporarily disable fetching) and clear commit message. He noted only a minor typo to fix before merging. The change builds on earlier work enabling geometric repacking of promisor packs and will help avoid performance hits in large-scale partial clone scenarios. With Junio's approval, this optimization is now queued for inclusion in an upcoming release.
