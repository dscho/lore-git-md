Bugfix addressing a dangling pointer in diffcore-break for blobless clones. This is Han Young's first contribution to the Git project, catching a case where `diffcore_break()` could leave a dangling pointer after freeing a filepair during diff operations in repositories cloned with `--filter=blob:none`.

The patch adds a single line to NULL the queue entry after freeing, preventing potential segfaults when diff operations trigger blob prefetching. Junio Hamano points out the patch is missing a Signed-off-by line and tests, and notes it resembles a similar fix from December 2025 (56d388e6).

While the fix appears straightforward, the maintainer's quick response suggests this is a legitimate issue worth addressing. The patch is minimal but would benefit from adding the missing sign-off and potentially a regression test to verify the fix for this edge case in partial clone scenarios.
