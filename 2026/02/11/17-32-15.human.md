This is a documentation patch in an already-accepted series improving `git am` man pages. Kristoffer Haugsbakk is following up on Jean-Noël Avila's earlier feedback about self-references in the documentation - specifically whether it's acceptable to have `git-am(1)` link to itself within its own man page.

The patch in question (1/4 in the series) standardizes command references by converting inline verbatim/emphasis markup to proper `linkgit` links. The author is now double-checking whether this creates problematic circular references when the command links to its own documentation. This is a minor stylistic question that surfaced after the series was already queued by Junio.

The exchange shows careful attention to documentation consistency - even for small details - which is characteristic of the project's high standards for man pages. Since the series is already accepted, this appears to be a post-acceptance clarification rather than a blocking issue.
