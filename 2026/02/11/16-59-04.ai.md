This is a brief follow-up email in the ongoing LOP (Large Object Promisors) patch series discussion. Maintainer Junio C Hamano weighs in on a naming question regarding how to refer to the set of filters that have been accepted from those advertised by promisor remotes. The exchange is lighthearted (note the smiley) and serves to confirm agreement on a minor implementation detail in the larger series. The email doesn't introduce new technical concerns or require any substantive changes to the patch series.
