Thread identity: Bugfix for diffcore-break in partially cloned repositories, addressing a dangling pointer issue during diff operations with prefetched blobs. Patch author is Han Young from Bytedance (first-time contributor).

Core problem: The `diffcore_break()` function in diffcore-break.c frees a queue structure without NULLing the pointer, which can lead to segmentation faults when diff operations trigger blob prefetching in blobless clones (`--filter=blob:none`). The issue manifests when working with large files (particularly YAML) during operations like `git reset` that involve diff calculations.
