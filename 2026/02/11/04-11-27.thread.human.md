This thread addresses a bug in Git's diffcore-break machinery that causes segmentation faults in blobless clones during operations like `git reset`. The issue stems from a dangling pointer when freeing a queue structure in `diffcore_break()`, particularly affecting large YAML files. First-time contributor Han Young from Bytedance has submitted the patch, now at version 3 after incorporating feedback from maintainer Junio C Hamano.

The technical one-line fix (NULLing the pointer after free) is straightforward, but the discussion has revealed deeper questions. Junio raised concerns about test reliability - the failure only reproduces 95% of the time - though he suggests this may be acceptable given that ASAN would catch such issues. More fundamentally, he questions whether break detection should be used at all for internal diff operations, suggesting this might represent wasted effort. This architectural consideration remains open.

While the immediate fix appears ready pending potential test refinements, the conversation has highlighted broader implications for how Git's diff machinery handles partial clones. The issue resembles a similar December 2025 fix, suggesting this may be part of a pattern worth wider investigation. Han has addressed Junio's specific feedback about test reliability and comment clarity in v3, but the maintainer's deeper question about break detection's role in internal operations may warrant follow-up discussion.
