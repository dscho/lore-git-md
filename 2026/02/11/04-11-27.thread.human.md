This thread addresses a bug in Git's diffcore-break functionality that causes segmentation faults in blobless partial clones during operations like `git reset`. The issue stems from a dangling pointer in `diffcore_break()` when handling prefetched blobs, particularly with large files. Han Young (a first-time contributor from Bytedance) has submitted version 3 of the fix, which simply NULLs the pointer after freeing the queue structure.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and raised two key points: first, about the reliability of the test case (which only reproduces the issue 95% of the time), and second, a deeper architectural question about whether break detection should be happening at all for internal diff operations. Han has incorporated Junio's feedback on test simplicity and comment clarity but acknowledges uncertainty about both testing the memory issue reliably and the broader architectural implications.

The discussion suggests this may indicate wider patterns in the diff machinery's handling of partial clones, following a similar fix from December 2025. While the immediate patch appears technically sound and nearly ready, Junio's question about the necessity of break detection for internal operations may warrant follow-up investigation. The thread currently awaits either test refinements or further discussion about the architectural concern before proceeding.
