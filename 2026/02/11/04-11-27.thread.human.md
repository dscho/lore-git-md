This thread addresses a bug in Git's diffcore-break machinery that causes segmentation faults in partially cloned repositories (specifically blobless clones using `--filter=blob:none`). The issue occurs when `diffcore_break()` frees a file pair during diff operations but leaves a dangling pointer, which can crash operations like `git reset` that involve prefetched blobs, particularly with large files.

First-time contributor Han Young from Bytedance submitted the fix, now at version 2 after addressing Junio C Hamano's initial feedback about missing sign-off and test coverage. The one-line solution properly NULLs the queue pointer after freeing. The test case demonstrates the crash scenario in blobless clones.

Junio's latest review raises several points about the test's reliability and simplicity, suggesting the removal of an unnecessary `rm` command and clearer phrasing in test comments. More significantly, he questions whether break detection should occur at all for internal diff operations in these cases, hinting at a potential architectural issue in how the diff machinery interacts with partial clones.

While the immediate fix appears sound and follows the pattern of a similar December 2025 fix (commit 56d388e6), Junio's comments suggest this may be part of a larger pattern needing review. The patch seems ready for merging once the test case concerns are addressed, but there may be deeper questions about the diff machinery's behavior with partial clones that warrant future investigation.
