This thread addresses a bug in Git's diffcore-break subsystem where a dangling pointer could cause segmentation faults during diff operations in partially cloned repositories. Han Young (a first-time contributor from Bytedance) identified the issue and proposed a one-line fix to properly NULL a pointer after freeing a queue structure in diffcore-break.c. The problem specifically manifests in blobless clones (`--filter=blob:none`) when performing operations like `git reset` that trigger diff calculations on large files, particularly YAML files.

Maintainer Junio C Hamano has reviewed the initial patch and noted two important missing elements: a Signed-off-by line (required for contribution compliance) and accompanying tests to verify the fix. Junio also pointed to a similar fix from December 2025 (commit 56d388e6) that addressed a related segfault, suggesting this might be part of a pattern of issues around freed diff entries. While the technical fix itself appears correct and narrowly scoped, Han will need to submit an updated version addressing these points before the patch can be considered for merging. The discussion highlights that this area of the diff machinery in partial clone scenarios may warrant broader scrutiny for similar issues.
