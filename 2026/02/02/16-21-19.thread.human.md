`git log --graph` misplaces graph characters when combined with `-p` and custom pretty formats that omit trailing newlines, breaking the visual graph structure. The bug occurs when using `--graph --pretty='format:%h' -p`, causing connection characters (`|`, `\`, `/`) to appear at line ends rather than starts. Two independent reports (original and Pushkar Singh's confirmation) demonstrate the issue persists from at least Git 2.43.0 through 2.51.0 across different environments (WSL/Linux and the original reporter's setup). The problem stems from `log-tree.c`'s graph/patch output interleaving logic, but no patches or deeper technical analysis have been proposed yet. Workarounds exist (using `tformat:` or adding `%n` to the format string) but introduce other formatting side effects. The thread currently consists of bug confirmation without active development toward a fix.
