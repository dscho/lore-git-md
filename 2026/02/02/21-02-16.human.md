This is a response to maintainer feedback on a feature series adding new traversal options to `git submodule foreach`. The author, William Hatfield, is seeking clarification on Junio C Hamano's naming concerns for the new flags.

The series introduces three flags for dependency-ordered submodule operations:
- `--reverse-traversal` (post-order processing)
- `--append-superproject` (includes superproject after submodules)
- `--reversive` (combined shorthand)

Junio raised several structural and naming issues in his review:
1. Tests should be integrated with implementation patches rather than separate
2. Flag names should be more descriptive of behavior rather than implementation
3. `--append-superproject` is unclear about what's being appended to what

Hatfield is now proposing alternative names:
- For reverse traversal: `--postorder-traversal` or `--bottom-up-traversal`
- For superproject inclusion: `--include-superproject` or `--execute-in-superproject-last`

The exchange shows the series is technically sound but needs refinement in presentation and naming before it can proceed. This is typical mid-review iteration where the maintainer provides high-level direction and the author works to align with project conventions. The substantive functionality appears uncontroversial - the discussion is about how best to expose it to users.
