Bugfix discussion regarding pathspec exclusion behavior with `.gitignore` rules.

Tian Yuchen responds to Remy D. Farley's initial patch that aimed to prevent `.gitignore` checks from applying to explicitly excluded pathspecs (those prefixed with `:!`). While agreeing the reported behavior is problematic, Yuchen argues the proposed fix in `dir.c` is too broad - it would skip pathspec matching entirely for any excluded pathspec, effectively disabling the exclusion mechanism rather than just preventing the unwanted ignore-rule interaction.

The key technical concern is that the current patch checks the global `PATHSPEC_EXCLUDE` flag on the pathspec struct rather than examining individual path matches, which would make all exclusions ineffective. Yuchen suggests the solution needs to be more targeted, preserving exclusion functionality while only fixing the ignore-rule interaction. They plan to work on a test script to better demonstrate the issue and validate any fix.
