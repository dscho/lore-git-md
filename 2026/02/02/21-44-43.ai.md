This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about adding a `@{default}` shorthand for referencing a repository's primary branch. Junio Hamano responds to Harald Nordgren's explanation of their workflow for using the primary branch as a read-only baseline.

Junio acknowledges Harald's use case of maintaining a local branch as a mirror of upstream for release management and bug investigation, but now questions whether this justifies a new syntax. He points out that a detached HEAD would serve equally well for read-only inspection purposes, diminishing the urgency of the feature. While he still considers it potentially useful for some users, he no longer views it as critical.

The exchange shows the maintainer reassessing the necessity of the proposed shorthand after hearing concrete workflow examples. Junio's stance has shifted from seeing this as a "must have" to a "nice to have" feature, which may influence whether and how the patch proceeds. This suggests the discussion may need to stronger justification for the branch-attached requirement that makes the shorthand necessary beyond what detached HEAD can provide.
