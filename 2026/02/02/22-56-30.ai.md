This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about syntax for referencing a repository's default branch. Harald Nordgren is responding to Junio Hamano's observation that detached HEAD mode can serve the "read-only" use case for examining the default branch without needing a local tracking branch.

This moves the discussion forward by narrowing the remaining justification for new syntax to cases where modifier chaining (`feature@{upstream}@{local}`) or other advanced reference scenarios are needed.
