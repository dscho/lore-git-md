This is a review response from the maintainer (Junio C Hamano) on a feature series adding new traversal options to `git submodule foreach`. The series introduces flags for reverse-order processing (`--reverse-traversal`), including the superproject (`--append-superproject`), and a combined shorthand (`--reversive`).

Junio raises three main points about the series structure and naming. First, he strongly prefers tests to be included in the same commit as the feature they verify, rather than in separate commits, as this makes the relationship between test and implementation clearer during review. Second, he questions whether "reverse-traversal" is the most intuitive name, suggesting "bottom-up" might better convey the actual behavior since there's no single natural traversal order. Third, he finds "--append-superproject" unclear and asks whether it might better describe as leaving superproject processing until last.

The feedback is constructive but signals that the series will need revision before merging - particularly around commit organization and naming. Junio hasn't identified any technical flaws in the implementation itself, focusing instead on making the changes more reviewable and the interface more intuitive. The author will need to address these points in a v2 submission.
