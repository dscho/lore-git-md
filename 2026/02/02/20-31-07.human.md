Bugfix for `git stash`'s handling of ignored files, addressing a long-standing issue where it would overwrite ignored files during index updates. This is part of Pushkar Singh's ongoing effort to add `--overwrite-ignore` and `--no-overwrite-ignore` options to control this behavior.

Elijah Newren raises several concerns about the current patch (v2). He questions the technical basis for the changes, noting that `overwrite_ignore` isn't referenced elsewhere in `builtin/stash.c`. He also points out that silently downgrading `INCLUDE_ALL_FILES` to just include-untracked when `--no-overwrite-ignore` is given may be the wrong approach - these options should likely be reported as incompatible rather than having one silently override the other.

Newren also notes the commit message references removing a FIXME comment that isn't actually removed in this patch, suggesting the patch may be based on an earlier version. He references previous discussions about related stash issues that may still need addressing, implying this fix might be incomplete without considering those other cases.

The technical discussion focuses on the interaction between `-a/--all` and the new overwrite-ignore options, with Newren suggesting the flags may be fundamentally incompatible rather than one simply modifying the other's behavior. This suggests the patch may need significant reworking to properly handle these edge cases before it can be merged.
