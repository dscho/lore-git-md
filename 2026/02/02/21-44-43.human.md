This is a discussion about the proposed `@{default}` shorthand for referencing a repository's primary branch, now in its fourth iteration. Junio Hamano responds to Harald Nordgren's use case for maintaining a local branch that tracks the upstream's primary branch (main/master/trunk) for release management and bug investigation.

Junio acknowledges the convenience of having a local branch mirror the upstream's primary branch, but questions whether this is truly essential. He points out that a detached HEAD could serve the same purpose for read-only operations like checking the upstream state or investigating bugs. While he sees the appeal of the feature, he no longer considers it critical enough to warrant immediate inclusion.

The exchange highlights the ongoing tension between practical workflow improvements and maintaining Git's conceptual purity. Junio's response suggests the proposal may need stronger justification to move forward, as he now views this more as a "nice to have" rather than a must-have feature. The thread shows the maintainer pushing back on the urgency while leaving the door open for continued discussion.
