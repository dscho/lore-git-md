This is a patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the encoding-related global variables `git_commit_encoding` and `git_log_output_encoding`. The series consists of three patches that systematically migrate these settings into `struct repo_settings` while maintaining backward compatibility.

The first patch moves the encoding configurations into repository-specific storage with lazy loading, carefully handling command-line overrides in `revision.c`. The second updates commit creation APIs to accept a repository context, and the third propagates this context through pretty-printing functions. The changes affect 35 files across Git's codebase, primarily touching commit handling, mail operations, and revision walking logic.

The author positions this as part of their GSoC 2026 proposal on global state libification, noting successful test suite verification with sanitizers. While the series appears mechanically sound, the author acknowledges it may not yet be complete - they specifically mention possibly missing some existing contextual pointers in the second patch and frame this submission as a "self-archival exercise" rather than a final version.

This work aligns with the broader architectural direction of removing `the_repository` global, following patterns established by René Scharfe and others. The changes maintain backward compatibility by falling back to `the_repository` for leaf callers in `builtin/` and top-level components. The patch touches several sensitive areas (commit creation, pretty-printing, revision walking) but appears focused specifically on the encoding subsystem changes.
