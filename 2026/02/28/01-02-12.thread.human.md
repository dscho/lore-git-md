Junio Hamano's February 2026 "What's cooking" report highlighted several active topics, including Adrian Ratiu's ongoing work to modernize Git's hook system. The `ar/config-hooks` series, which introduces configurable hooks and multi-hook support, had been merged to 'next' but hit a snag when Patrick Steinhardt identified a performance regression in `receive-pack` update hooks - a critical path for server operations. 

Adrian has now asked Junio to hold the series from progressing to 'master' while he prepares a v3 revision. He clarifies that the performance issue Patrick raised is separate from the changes already addressed in v3, which include foundational improvements like `git hook list` and centralized hook configuration. This late-stage performance concern underscores the project's careful approach to balancing architectural improvements with core performance, especially for server-side components. The hook conversion represents a major shift from direct `run_command()` calls to a more flexible API, with implications for Adrian's parallel hooks work still in progress. Patrick's involvement as a performance expert adds weight to the need for resolution before merging.
