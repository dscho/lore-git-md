This is a patch series (v2) from Tian Yuchen that continues the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting encoding-related global variables. The series consists of 3 patches that migrate commit and log output encoding settings from global variables into `struct repo_settings`.

The first patch moves the encoding configurations (`git_commit_encoding` and `git_log_output_encoding`) into repository-specific storage while maintaining backward compatibility through transitional APIs. The second and third patches update commit creation and pretty-printing functions to accept and propagate a `struct repository*` parameter, leveraging existing context pointers where available.

The author notes this work is part of their GSoC 2026 proposal on global state libification. While the series passes the test suite and memory checks, Yuchen acknowledges there may be remaining edge cases to address, particularly around properly propagating repository contexts in all call paths. The changes touch 35 files across Git's codebase, primarily affecting commit handling, mail operations, and revision walking logic.

This appears to be a well-scoped, incremental step in the broader architectural effort to eliminate implicit global state. The patch series follows established patterns for such migrations while maintaining compatibility through transitional fallbacks to `the_repository`. The changes are focused specifically on the encoding subsystem rather than attempting broader refactoring.
