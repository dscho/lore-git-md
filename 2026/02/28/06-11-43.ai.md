This is a bugfix targeting `git describe`'s commit traversal order, continuing the discussion about how to properly implement topological sorting for tag descriptions. Ben Boeckel responds to Jeff King's performance analysis of his initial solution, which used `repo_is_descendant_of()` comparisons but suffered from O(n²) performance.

The email focuses on implementation strategies for efficient topological sorting. Ben considers two approaches: (1) leveraging generation numbers (either from commit-graphs or computed during traversal) to optimize ancestor checks, and (2) potentially rewriting `git describe` to use Git's existing revision machinery which already handles topological ordering. He notes the revision walker's `init_topo_walk()` and `sort_in_topological_order()` as possible models.

Key technical points include the performance implications of commit-graph availability, the distinction between streaming vs batch topological sorting, and the architectural question of whether to adapt `git describe`'s custom traversal or migrate it to the standard revision walker. The discussion has moved from proving the bug exists to evaluating specific implementation tradeoffs, with correctness now established and performance optimization as the remaining challenge.
