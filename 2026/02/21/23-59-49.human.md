This is part 2 of a 6-patch series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch comes from Elijah Newren, the primary expert on Git's merge machinery.

The change modifies `write_tree()` in merge-ort.c to take a `struct repository*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. This allows the function to use the repository context from the merge options (`opt->repo`) rather than the global variable. As a side benefit, the patch simplifies the function signature by having `write_tree()` look up the hash algorithm size internally rather than requiring it as a parameter.

The changes are mechanical but well-justified, with Elijah's deep knowledge of merge-ort ensuring the modifications maintain correct behavior. This is a straightforward step in the broader effort to eliminate global repository state, following established patterns seen in other parts of the codebase. The patch has already received positive feedback in previous iterations of the series.
