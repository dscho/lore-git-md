This is the final coordination email for Harald Nordgren's long-running effort to add configurable branch comparison to `git status`. After 28 iterations, the feature is now marked as "Stale" in Junio's integration branches due to lack of recent activity, prompting Harald to check in.

The patch series implements `status.compareBranches` configuration to show comparisons against multiple branches in `git status` output. The implementation follows Junio's earlier direction to first support only `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` syntax, deferring full DWIM resolution to a future release. Jeff King contributed foundational changes to `repo_dwim_ref()` and `interpret_branch_name()` to handle missing upstreams and preserve refnames during @-mark resolution.

Junio's response indicates the topic had fallen off his radar due to inactivity, and he solicits feedback from the broader list about whether this functionality would see real-world use. The technical implementation appears sound, with 159 lines of core changes and extensive test coverage (337 lines in t6040-tracking-info.sh), but adoption interest remains an open question.
