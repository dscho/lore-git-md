This is the final coordination email for Harald Nordgren's long-running effort to add configurable branch comparison to `git status`. After 28 iterations and extensive review from Jeff King and Junio Hamano, the feature is now ready for merging in its initial limited form.

The patch implements `status.compareBranches` configuration to allow users to compare against multiple branches in `git status` output. The implementation follows Junio's guidance to first support only the basic `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` syntax, with plans to expand to full DWIM resolution in a future phase. Jeff King contributed key technical foundations including `repo_dwim_ref()` modifications to handle missing upstreams cleanly.

All technical concerns have been addressed, with 337 lines of test coverage and edge case handling for dangling refs and tag resolution. The thread shows clear consensus among the participants, and Harald is now seeking final confirmation to proceed with merging the initial implementation. This represents the culmination of a carefully reviewed feature that will enhance `git status` functionality while maintaining backward compatibility.
