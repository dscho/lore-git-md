This is the third version of a patch series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal project. The key change in v3 is fixing the remaining issue Patrick Steinhardt identified in v2 - the prefetch logic now properly uses `opt->repo` with `repo_has_promisor_remote()` rather than falling back to `the_repository`. This completes the repository context propagation that was missing in earlier versions.

The series follows a clear progression:
1. Patch 1 removes obsolete `the_repository` checks from prefetch logic in merge-ort and related diff machinery
2. Patches 2-4 mechanically convert remaining `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` usage to `opt->repo` equivalents
3. Patches 5-6 add compile-time enforcement to prevent regression via `DO_NOT_USE_THE_REPOSITORY` macros

Elijah Newren's expertise with the merge machinery shows in the careful handling of repository context throughout merge-ort.c. The changes are well-contained and maintain existing behavior while removing global state. With the prefetch fix addressing Patrick's concern, this series appears ready for merging as part of the broader effort to eliminate `the_repository`. The compile-time enforcement in the final patches will help prevent backsliding in these subsystems.
