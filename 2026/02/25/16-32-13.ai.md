This is part 4/8 of a refactoring series focused on naming consistency in the `repo-info` subsystem. The patch renames `struct field` to `struct repo_info_field` throughout `builtin/repo.c` to make the type's purpose more explicit. This is a mechanical change that follows the naming conventions established earlier in the series, with no behavior modifications.

The change propagates through all related functions and data structures - the field array, comparison function, lookup helper, and iteration patterns all now use the more specific type name. The patch maintains the existing bsearch-based lookup behavior while making the code's domain more self-documenting. As with other patches in this series, the change is small, focused, and clearly motivated by the subsystem's evolution.
