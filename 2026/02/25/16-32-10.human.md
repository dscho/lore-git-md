This is a documentation patch that establishes a new naming convention for arrays in Git's codebase, part of a larger series focused on naming consistency in the `repo-info` subsystem.

The patch adds a rule to `CodingGuidelines` recommending that arrays be named in singular form when individual elements are accessed (e.g. `dog[0]`), while allowing plural names when the array is treated as a collection (e.g. `dogs` passed to `walk_all_dogs()`). This distinction comes from Eric Sunshine's feedback on the v1 series, which the author has incorporated here.

The change is purely stylistic and doesn't affect functionality. It's a small but thoughtful addition to Git's style guide that should make array usage more consistent and readable. The patch follows the series' pattern of mechanical improvements to code organization and documentation, with clear motivation and attribution to the commit message.
