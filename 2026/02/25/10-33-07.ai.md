This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy. The patch has now received approval from both Derrick Stolee (who originally designed the geometric repacking approach) and Toon Claes, with no outstanding review comments. 

The series has followed a careful progression, first hardening test infrastructure in earlier patches before making the actual strategy change in this final patch. The change switches the default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric" for manual operations while keeping "incremental" as the default for scheduled maintenance. 

Key technical details include the implementation in builtin/gc.c, documentation updates explaining the three preserved strategies ("gc", "incremental", and "geometric"), and test isolation measures to preserve legacy behavior testing. The geometric strategy maintains packfile progression to reduce expensive full repacks while still keeping repository size manageable.

With all review feedback addressed and approvals from key domain experts, this series appears ready for merging.
