This is a discussion about refining the behavior of `git log`'s in relation to decorations and format placeholders. Marc Branchaud joins the thread between Alejandro Colomar and Junio Hamano, offering additional perspective on the proposed changes to decoration handling.

Marc argues that since `%d` is documented as showing "ref names, like the --decorate option", it would be reasonable for `--decorate=no` to suppress `%d` output. He points out some current inconsistencies - notably that `--decorate=auto` doesn't properly suppress decorations when piping output, and that `--decorate=no` currently behaves identically to `--decorate=short` rather than suppressing decorations entirely.

The email suggests two possible approaches: either make `--decorate=no` affect `%d` (which Marc sees as fixing a bug in the current behavior), or alternatively make `--decorate=no` suppress all decoration-related placeholders including the proposed new `%(decorate:optional=yes)` syntax. Marc leans toward the first approach as more natural given the existing documentation.

This continues the thread's careful balancing act between maintaining backward compatibility and making the decoration system more internally consistent. The discussion remains technical and constructive, with Marc providing concrete examples of current behavior that support his position.
