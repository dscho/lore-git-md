This is patch 13/14 in Taylor Blau's RFC series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The patch adds a new `--write-midx=incremental` option to `git repack` that builds on the infrastructure from previous patches to enable geometric repacking of MIDX layers.

The key additions are:
- New config options `repack.midxSplitFactor` (default 2) and `repack.midxNewLayerThreshold` (default 8) to control compaction behavior
- Integration with the geometric repacking machinery to only consider packs in sufficiently large tip layers for repacking
- Extensive test coverage in t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh verifying the new mode works across various scenarios including threshold behavior, layer preservation, and chain integrity

The patch follows the series' pattern of careful infrastructure work with thorough testing. It represents a significant step toward the overall goal of more efficient large repository maintenance through MIDX-aware geometric repacking. The implementation appears well-considered, with attention paid to edge cases and backward compatibility.

As an RFC, it will likely attract review from pack/MIDX experts like Patrick Steinhardt and Derrick Stolee, particularly around the compaction heuristics and layer management. The interaction between the new incremental logic and existing flags may need clarification during review.
