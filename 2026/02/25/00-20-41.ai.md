This is an RFC patch series introducing an incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking strategy, building on Taylor Blau's previous work on MIDX compaction. The series represents the third major component of a larger effort to improve repacking in large repositories.

The key innovation is a new repacking approach that avoids periodic all-into-one repacks by:
1. Performing geometric repacks on non-MIDX'd packs
2. Adding new MIDX layers for fresh packs
3. Compacting adjacent MIDX layers to maintain geometric progression
4. Pruning redundant layers

The series includes preparatory refactors, new MIDX write/compact options needed for the strategy, and the core implementation. It touches multiple subsystems including the MIDX machinery, repack geometry calculations, and the repack command itself.

This appears to be a substantial architectural change that could significantly impact performance in large repositories. The RFC status suggests the author expects non-trivial feedback and iteration. The implementation spans 14 patches with extensive test coverage (adding 3 test files). The work builds on Taylor's deep expertise in Git's pack subsystem and follows through on ideas presented at Git Merge 2025.

Notable technical details:
- Introduces `--checksum-only` and `--base` MIDX options
- Adds new configs `repack.midxNewLayerThreshold` and `repack.midxSplitFactor`
- Maintains backward compatibility with existing `--write-midx` behavior
- Lays groundwork for future bitmap optimization work
