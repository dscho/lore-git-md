This is patch 10/14 in Taylor Blau's RFC series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The series builds on previous MIDX compaction work to improve repacking in large repositories.

This patch teaches the pack geometry machinery to optionally restrict repacking candidates to packs in the tip MIDX layer when a threshold is configured. If the tip layer has fewer packs than the threshold, those packs are excluded entirely; otherwise only tip-layer packs participate in the geometric repack. The patch also tracks whether any tip-layer packs were included in the rollup (via `midx_tip_rewritten`), which will be used later to decide how to update the MIDX chain.

The changes are focused on `repack-geometry.c`, adding logic to filter packs based on their MIDX layer membership and track whether the tip layer is being rewritten. The patch maintains the existing geometric repacking behavior while adding the new MIDX-aware mode. This is infrastructure work that enables the incremental repacking strategy proposed in the full series.

As an RFC patch, this is still in the proposal stage and will likely receive review feedback on the approach and implementation details. The changes are fairly mechanical and focused, building on the MIDX infrastructure added in earlier patches of the series.
