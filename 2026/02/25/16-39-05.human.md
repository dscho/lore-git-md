This is a follow-up discussion in the worktree API refactoring series, where Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano are examining deeper architectural questions about worktree-repository relationships that emerged from the refactoring.

Phillip notes that the current implementation of `is_current_worktree()` uses `the_repository` rather than the worktree's own repository (`wt->repo`), which appears inconsistent with the series' goal of eliminating `the_repository` dependencies. He traces this back to mechanical replacements made during earlier refactoring efforts, where `get_git_dir()` and `git_common_path()` were replaced with their repository-aware counterparts but defaulted to `the_repository`.

The key question raised is whether the concept of a "current worktree" should be process-wide (using `the_repository`) or repository-specific (using `wt->repo`). Phillip argues the latter would be more intuitive in a multi-repository context. He offers to either reroll the series with a preparatory patch to fix this or address it separately after the current patches land.

This discussion reveals an important invariant that wasn't fully considered in earlier refactoring: the relationship between worktrees and their owning repositories. The thread shows how incremental refactoring can surface deeper design questions, even as the immediate technical changes (removing NULL worktree parameters) have been successfully completed and approved.
