Taylor Blau's RFC series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking continues to evolve with infrastructure improvements. The series (14 patches) introduces geometric repacking with MIDX layer awareness, including a new append-only mode, building on Taylor's prior MIDX compaction work. Junio Hamano has contributed several infrastructure patches to support this, first refactoring MIDX string handling to use `string_list`, then optimizing the `strvec` initialization API.

In the latest exchange, Taylor responds to Junio's suggestion about `strvec` initialization, moving the zero-allocation optimization into `strvec_init_alloc()` and making `strvec_init()` a simple wrapper. This consolidates the logic while preserving the empty-strvec optimization. Taylor also caught and fixed a small typo in Junio's suggested diff. The discussion confirms that while `string_list` may not be maximally performant for lookups, it's adequate for MIDX file tracking where the number of entries is typically small.

The series remains in RFC status, with open questions about default modes, flag interactions, config defaults, and test coverage. Key participants so far are Taylor (the series author) and Junio (infrastructure improvements), with likely future review from Patrick Steinhardt (repack machinery), Derrick Stolee (MIDX), and Jeff King (performance). The work connects to broader efforts around repack optimization and code quality improvements.
