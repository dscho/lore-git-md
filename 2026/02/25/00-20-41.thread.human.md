This RFC thread from Taylor Blau proposes a new incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking strategy, building on his previous work with multi-pack indexes. The 14-part series introduces geometric repacking of non-MIDX'd packs while maintaining MIDX layer structure, with an optional append-only mode. The infrastructure-heavy first half (patches 1-11) prepares the ground for the core repacking logic in patches 12-14.

The latest email contained a duplicate of patch 6/14 - a minor mechanical change that moves ODB source tracking into the `existing_packs` struct as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. Taylor acknowledged this was an accidental duplicate that will be removed in the non-RFC version. The patch itself is sound but adds no new technical content beyond the original submission.

As an RFC, the series hasn't yet received review feedback, though it will likely attract attention from pack/MIDX experts like Patrick Steinhardt and Derrick Stolee once discussion begins. The proposal touches several active areas of development including geometric repacking, MIDX layer management, and the ODB abstraction work.
