Here's the updated human-readable thread summary:

Taylor Blau has proposed an RFC patch series (14 patches) introducing a new incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking strategy for large repositories. This builds on his prior work on MIDX compaction and aims to improve repacking by avoiding periodic full repacks, instead maintaining a geometric progression of pack layers. The series adds new MIDX write options and configuration knobs while maintaining backward compatibility.

The sixth patch in the series, just submitted, refactors how the repack command tracks object database sources. It moves this tracking into a new field in the `existing_packs` struct, preparing for future needs in the incremental MIDX machinery and potential support for repacking from alternate object sources. While functionally equivalent to the current behavior, this change lays groundwork for later patches in the series.

As an RFC, the overall approach is still open for discussion, and we can expect review feedback from other pack/MIDX experts like Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King given Taylor's track record in this subsystem. The series connects to broader efforts to overhaul Git's repacking machinery.
