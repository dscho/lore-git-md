This is feedback on a GSoC application proposal about improving disk space management in partial clones, particularly for objects outside sparse-checkout areas. Derrick Stolee responds to Amisha Chhajed's initial proposal with practical considerations.

Stolee acknowledges the value of the goal but suggests focusing on cleaning up old blobs from previous checkouts rather than just spatially-unneeded objects (those outside the current sparse-checkout). He cautions that some tools like VS Code's background blame operations may trigger redownloads of purged objects, potentially worsening resource usage rather than improving it.

The response positions this as an area needing careful design to avoid unintended consequences, while still recognizing it as a worthwhile optimization space. Stolee's comments suggest the proposal would benefit from deeper analysis of real-world workflows that might interact with such cleanup mechanisms.
