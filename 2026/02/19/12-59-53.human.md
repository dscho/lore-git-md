This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the mailmap subsystem. The patch comes from a GSoC 2026 applicant and is the first in a two-part series to reduce global state in mailmap handling.

The patch converts `read_mailmap()` and `read_mailmap_blob()` to take explicit `struct repository` parameters instead of implicitly using `the_repository`. It updates all callers across various commands (`blame`, `log`, `shortlog`, etc.) to pass `the_repository` to maintain current behavior. The changes are mechanical but thorough, touching 10 files with straightforward parameter additions.

The implementation appears correct with all call sites updated, though real-world testing would be needed to verify no behavioral regressions. This is a well-scoped contribution that aligns with the broader global state reduction initiative, focusing on a non-controversial area of the changes. The second patch in the series will tackle the remaining global configuration variables in mailmap.
