This is a refactoring patch from Adrian Ratiu, part of his ongoing series to improve Git's hook infrastructure. The patch moves hook callback data management from stack-allocated variables to dynamically allocated memory with custom alloc/free functions, making the hook execution machinery more flexible and self-contained.

Junio Hamano, the maintainer, reviews the patch positively, noting the clean separation of concerns where the hook management now handles its own allocation and cleanup. He suggests a minor stylistic improvement for the BUG() check (using boolean operators to avoid repetition) but acknowledges it might be too "cute" for some tastes. The implementation itself is solid, with the refs.c and transport.c changes demonstrating consistent application of the new pattern.

This patch is the first in an 8-part series that has already received maintainer approval for its overall direction, with the prerequisite hooks-conversion work likely to move to the 'next' integration branch soon. The changes here are foundational plumbing for the rest of the series, which will introduce config-based hooks and other user-facing features.
