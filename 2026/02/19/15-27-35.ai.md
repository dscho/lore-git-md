The patch in question (3/5) replaces `the_hash_algo` with `opt->repo->hash_algo` in merge-ort.c as part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate implicit repository dependencies.

Patrick Steinhardt suggests adding a one-line explanation to the commit message, even though the subject line already describes the change. This is a minor documentation suggestion rather than a technical concern - the patch itself appears correct and uncontroversial, being a straightforward mechanical conversion that follows the established pattern for these types of changes. The comment reflects Git's culture of preferring commit messages that explain both what changed and why, even for simple mechanical transformations. Since this is part of a well-understood project-wide effort (the `the_repository` removal), a brief mention of that context would suffice to satisfy the suggestion.
