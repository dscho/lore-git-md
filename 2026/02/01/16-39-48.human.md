This is a documentation patch from Jean-Noël Avila, part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git's man pages to consistent AsciiDoc synopsis-style formatting. The email is a response to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's review of the `git-show` man page conversion, specifically addressing rendering issues with decoration options in the `--pretty=format:` section.

The discussion focuses on technical challenges with AsciiDoc formatting, particularly around how certain symbols (like `->` in decoration options) are rendered differently between asciidoc.py and asciidoctor processors. Avila acknowledges the rendering inconsistencies and proposes a longer-term solution involving a custom parser extension, while suggesting a temporary fallback to unformatted text where the current toolchain produces confusing output.

This exchange demonstrates the meticulous attention to detail in the documentation standardization work, where even minor rendering differences between toolchains are being carefully considered. The patch itself is part of a larger, well-established effort that has seen multiple iterations and thorough review from Haugsbakk and others. The technical challenges being discussed here are specific to the documentation tooling rather than the Git functionality being documented.
