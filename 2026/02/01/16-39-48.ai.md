This is part of Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing documentation standardization effort, specifically addressing formatting issues in the `git-show` man page conversion to synopsis-style AsciiDoc. The discussion focuses on rendering problems with decoration options in the `%(decorate:)` format specifier, particularly the inconsistent display of the `->` pointer symbol and plus-sign (`++`) verbatim formatting.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk identified that while most decoration options render acceptably, the pointer option's hyphen and greater-than symbol display differently (with the hyphen in verbatim typeface but the `>` not). He also notes confusing rendering of plus signs in example formatting blocks. Avila acknowledges these are asciidoctor parsing issues and mentions working on a custom parser extension that could potentially be integrated into Git's build system to handle these cases more robustly.

The exchange demonstrates the meticulous attention to detail in the documentation conversion project, where even minor rendering inconsistencies in special characters trigger investigation and potential tooling improvements. For now, Avila suggests falling back to unformatted text when asciidoctor cannot process the markup correctly, prioritizing clarity over perfect formatting. This patch iteration remains strictly focused on presentation, maintaining cross-toolchain compatibility between asciidoc.py and asciidoctor while improving documentation's consistency.
