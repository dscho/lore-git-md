This is a documentation patch in Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing effort to standardize Git's man pages to synopsis-style AsciiDoc formatting. Kristoffer Haugsbakk provides feedback on the `git-clone` documentation conversion, focusing on consistent formatting of command-line options.

Haugsbakk notes the patch correctly converts option placeholders from manual `_<name>_` markup to backtick-wrapped forms like `` `-o <name>` ``, which the synopsis style now handles automatically. He points out one remaining inconsistency: the documentation mixes "stuck" (`` `--option=<value>` ``) and "unstuck" (`` `--option <value>` ``) forms for long options, where gitcli(7) recommends the former. While not blocking this patch, he suggests addressing this in a future iteration.

The review confirms the changes maintain cross-toolchain compatibility between asciidoc.py and asciidoctor while improving clarity. Haugsbakk also verifies that the stuck form (with equals sign) works correctly with Git's option parser, noting this could inform future formatting decisions. This continues the meticulous, iterative refinement characteristic of the documentation standardization project.
