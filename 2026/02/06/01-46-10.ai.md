This is the second patch in a two-part series addressing const-correctness warnings from modern C compilers. The patch is a straightforward cleanup of `gpg-interface.c` that removes an unnecessary NULL initialization of a variable that gets unconditionally assigned later.

The change is minimal - just removing the `= NULL` from a `const char *key` declaration since the variable is always set before use. This was originally part of the first patch in the series but was split out at Junio Hamano's request to keep the const-correctness changes separate from this simple initialization cleanup.

The patch has already been reviewed and approved in previous iterations, with Jeff King (Peff) signing off on the technical approach and Junio requesting only the organizational split. With this v2 submission meeting that requirement, the series appears ready for merging as part of Git's ongoing effort to maintain compatibility with modern C standards and compiler versions.
