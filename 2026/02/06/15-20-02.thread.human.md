This RFC patch from a GSoC 2026 student proposes refactoring `get_layout_bare()` in `builtin/repo.c` to remove its dependency on the global `the_repository` state, making it properly repository-aware by checking local config and worktree status. The change follows established patterns in the ongoing repository independence effort.

Junio Hamano responded that while the technical approach is correct, he questions the practical value of this particular change. He notes that built-in commands inherently operate on `the_repository` due to their single-repository design, making such refactoring less impactful here than in shared code that might be reused across contexts. While leaving room for others to weigh in, Junio's response suggests this may be more valuable as a learning exercise than as a change likely to be merged in its current form. The discussion highlights Git's pragmatic approach to the `the_repository` removal effort - prioritizing changes that enable new capabilities over mechanical conversions without clear benefit.
