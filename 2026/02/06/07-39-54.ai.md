This is a CI infrastructure discussion about handling Ubuntu's switch to uutils coreutils in their rolling release. The thread began with Junio C Hamano proposing to switch Git's CI jobs from `ubuntu:rolling` to `ubuntu:latest` as a temporary workaround for a buggy dirname implementation in uutils. Patrick Steinhardt now suggests a more nuanced approach: while accepting the immediate fix, he advocates for eventually serving as a test case for uutils by reporting bugs upstream, since Ubuntu may fully adopt it in the future.

Patrick proposes a middle path - keeping most of the rolling release testing while selectively disabling only the broken components (similar to an existing sudo.ws workaround). He plans to revisit the CI configuration next week when addressing other infrastructure issues, potentially replacing Junio's blanket downgrade with targeted fixes. This positions Git to both maintain testing stability now and contribute to uutils' maturation, though the immediate patch remains focused on the bleeding-edge breakage. The discussion reflects Git's balancing act between CI reliability and helping shape emerging platform changes.
