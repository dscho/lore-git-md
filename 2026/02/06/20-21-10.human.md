This is the first patch in a series introducing inter-file navigation to Git's interactive hunk selection interface. The patch adds a new `--rework-with-file` flag to `git add -p` and related commands, disabling the automatic file advancement that normally occurs after all hunks in a file are processed.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and suggested several improvements: rewording the commit message for clarity, keeping line lengths under 70 columns for email quoting, and changing the internal flag from `no_auto_advance` to the more readable `auto_advance` (defaulting to true). The author acknowledges these points and will address them in the next iteration.

The change is straightforward plumbing - adding the flag and its supporting infrastructure without yet implementing the navigation behavior (which comes in patch 2). This is a clean separation that makes the series easier to review. The patch appears well-scoped and maintains backward compatibility by keeping auto-advance as the default behavior.
