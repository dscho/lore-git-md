This is a two-patch series integrating the `merge-ours` strategy with Git's sparse-index functionality, part of the ongoing sparse-checkout/sparse-index effort.

The cover letter explains that `merge-ours` (used by `git merge -s ours`) wasn't properly reading sparse-checkout settings, causing unnecessary index expansion. The first patch removes reliance on the `the_repository` global as preparatory cleanup, while the second adds proper sparse-index support by initializing the sparse-checkout globals via `repo_config()`.

The changes are relatively straightforward - 15 lines modified in the builtin and 14 lines of new tests. The series appears well-structured with a clear separation between refactoring and functional changes. The use of AI assistance is noted but doesn't raise any obvious quality concerns. This looks like a focused, incremental improvement to sparse-index compatibility.
