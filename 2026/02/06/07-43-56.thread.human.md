The community is pursuing a multi-pronged solution combining documentation warnings, commit-msg hooks, and new parsing modes.

It adds explicit warnings about patch roundtrip hazards in the official docs, particularly targeting distribution maintainers who frequently process patches via email. While this doesn't fix the technical issue, it raises awareness of the risks.

Several technical solutions are progressing in parallel: Phillip Wood has a v3 commit-msg hook under final review (with Junio asking about scissors line handling), Jeff King proposed a `--strict` mode for `git am` that Junio endorses, and Patrick Steinhardt is exploring an `--accept-ambiguous-patch` flag. There's also discussion of boundary markers and reversible quoting mechanisms.

The documentation component is now settled, while the technical solutions continue to evolve through careful review - particularly Wood's hook implementation that aims to catch dangerous patterns while minimizing false positives. The layered approach reflects both the complexity of the issue and the community's commitment to maintaining backward compatibility while improving security.
