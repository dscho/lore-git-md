This is a final refinement to the whitespace handling bugfix for symbolic links in Git's diff/apply machinery. The patch series, originally from maintainer Junio C Hamano with review from Patrick Steinhardt, addresses incorrect triggering of incomplete-line checks (WS_INCOMPLITE_LINE) on symlinks where such endings are normal.

The discussion has converged on a clear rule: disable the whitespace check when the post-image side (accounting for `--reverse`) is a symlink. This properly handles text-to-symlink transitions while maintaining validation for regular files. The implementation now lives in both `apply.c` and `diff.c`, with the edge case of newline-terminated symlink targets explicitly deferred as not worth the complexity.

Patrick Steinhardt's reply confirms the approach is sensible, though he raises a theoretical question about whether readers could distinguish symlinks with/without trailing newlines. The maintainer had already dismissed this as impractical to support, and the thread appears settled with no further changes needed. The patch is now ready for integration, representing consensus between the maintainer and reviewer.
