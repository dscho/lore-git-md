This is a documentation patch series continuing Jean-Noël Avila's effort to standardize Git man pages into consistent AsciiDoc synopsis-style formatting. The v4 iteration converts `git-submodule.adoc` and `git-show.adoc` to the new format while also fixing some lingering style issues in previously converted documents (`git-clone.adoc` and `for-each-ref-options.adoc`).

The changes focus on markup improvements rather than content changes, with notable refinements including: standardizing references to `HEAD` and `origin` with backticks, fixing decoration rendering, converting inline lists to proper definition lists, and ensuring cross-toolchain compatibility (asciidoc.py and asciidoctor). Kristoffer Haugsbakk has provided thorough feedback across all versions and now gives his Reviewed-by for v4, indicating the quality meets his standards after several rounds of review.

This series demonstrates the careful, iterative review process characteristic of the documentation standardization effort - multiple rounds of detailed feedback leading to consensus quality. With Kristoffer's Reviewed-by applied and all identified issues addressed, these conversions appear ready for maintainer consideration. The work maintains the project's documentation quality while improving consistency and tooling support.
