Patrick Steinhardt provides a minor style nit on Junio C Hamano's patch series that improves `git switch` advice messages. The series introduces an `enum checkout_command` to track whether operations come from `checkout`, `switch`, or `restore` commands, replacing string-passing with type-safe enums.

Patrick points out that the BUG() message in the switch statement should start with a lowercase letter to match Git's convention ("no such checkout variant" rather than "No such checkout variant"). He notes this is a trivial point not worth a separate revision, and otherwise approves of the change's reduction of global constants.

This is a small but thoughtful review comment from Patrick, who as a frequent contributor to large refactoring efforts himself recognizes the value in type-safe enums over string constants. The feedback demonstrates Git's attention to consistent style even in error messages, while acknowledging when a point isn't significant enough to block progress.
