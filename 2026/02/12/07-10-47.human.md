He suggests "NEEDSTHOUGHTS" might be a more accurate label than "NEEDSWORK" since the primary need is often evaluation rather than implementation, though he's not proposing a mass rename.

The exchange highlights a common tension in open source projects between documenting best practices and ensuring contributors actually encounter them. Patrick acknowledges the documentation is valuable as a reference point for future discussions, even if some contributors miss it initially. As a response from an active core contributor to the maintainer's proposal, this email signals broad alignment on the substance while noting real-world adoption challenges.
