This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, specifically focusing on improving type safety and flag handling in the object storage layer. The patch is the third in a five-part series (v2) that addresses inconsistencies in flag definitions.

The change removes gaps in the bit values used by `OBJECT_INFO_*` flags in `odb.h`, where some bits were skipped due to previously removed flags. The patch documents that these gaps came from flags that were removed in past commits (`OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_CACHED` and `OBJECT_INFO_ALLOW_UNKNOWN_TYPE`). By renumbering the remaining flags to use consecutive bits, the code becomes more maintainable and less likely to cause confusion about whether there are hidden flag definitions elsewhere.

This is a straightforward cleanup that doesn't change behavior - it just makes the flag definitions more logical and self-documenting. The patch follows the series' pattern of meticulous attention to detail in the ODB API's flag handling. Given Patrick's role as the primary driver of the ODB abstraction work and the non-controversial nature of this change, it's likely to be merged without significant discussion.
