This is a performance optimization patch for `git pack-objects` that has now received maintainer approval. The patch addresses an issue where geometric repacking of promisor packs in partial clones could trigger unnecessary object fetches during delta selection heuristics. 

The problem emerged after commit dcc9c7ef47 enabled geometric repacking of promisor packs. During this operation, the rev walk used for delta selection would attempt to fetch missing promisor objects, despite this being unnecessary for the packing operation. Patrick Steinhardt's solution temporarily disables fetching during this specific rev walk using a global variable, as there's only ever one packing operation running at a time.

The patch has gone through review with Jeff King (Peff) providing feedback on coverage and Taylor Blau acknowledging its value for large repositories. Junio Hamano has now approved the final version after a minor typo fix ("passe" -> "pass") was addressed. This optimization will be beneficial for large repositories using partial clones where unnecessary fetches impact performance. The patch is now queued for inclusion in the next release.
