This is a follow-up to Junio's "What's cooking" report regarding Phillip Wood's sample commit-msg hook improvements. The original series (pw/commit-msg-sample-hook) aimed to detect problematic commit messages that might confuse `git mailinfo`, such as those containing separator lines or diffs.

Phillip now plans to revise the series to handle an edge case where subject lines begin with "diff -" (which are actually safe when prefixed with [PATCH] by `git format-patch`). He also mentions likely dropping the final patch in the series (which added a .gitattributes entry for sample hooks), though he doesn't specify the reason for this change.

The update appears to be a minor refinement of the original work rather than a major change in direction. Phillip's note suggests he's responding to real-world testing experience while preparing another unrelated patch series. The changes seem well-motivated and focused on improving the hook's accuracy without over-policing valid commit messages.
