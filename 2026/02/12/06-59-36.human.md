This is a follow-up patch series in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, focusing on cleaning up flag definitions for object information queries. Patrick Steinhardt addresses some historical inconsistencies in how `OBJECT_INFO_*` flags are defined and passed to `odb_has_object()`.

The series starts by fixing two cases where incorrect flags were being passed to `odb_has_object()` in `builtin/backfill.c` and `builtin/fsck.c`. These weren't causing bugs in practice due to coincidental flag values, but the corrections make the intended behavior explicit. The later patches then reorganize the flag definitions to eliminate historical gaps in numbering and convert them to proper enums for better type safety.

The changes are relatively mechanical but demonstrate the project's attention to detail in API design. The thread context shows this builds on previous discussion about enum type safety in the ODB interface, with multiple contributors having validated the changes across different subsystems. While the immediate impact is small, these cleanups contribute to the larger effort of making the object storage layer more maintainable and type-safe.
