This is a status update on Phillip Wood's commit-msg hook improvements, part of the ongoing hook subsystem improvements effort.

Phillip Wood is preparing a reroll of his series that enhances the sample commit-msg hook to detect problematic commit messages. The current version in `next` identifies messages containing separator lines (which `mailinfo` would treat as message terminators) and those containing diffs. In response to real-world testing, Phillip plans two changes: first, to handle subject lines starting with "diff -" (which are safe when prefixed by `git format-patch`'s "[PATCH]" marker), and second, to likely drop the `.gitattributes` patch from the series.

Junio Hamano confirms the safety of Phillip's approach to handling "diff -" prefixed subjects, noting that even when format-patch's prefix is optional, it will still appear on the Subject: line. This exchange shows the series progressing through the typical Git development cycle - initial implementation, real-world testing, and refinement based on that testing. The changes appear focused and well-considered, addressing edge cases while maintaining the hook's core functionality of preventing problematic commit messages.
