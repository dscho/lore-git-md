This thread documents the evolution of Patrick Steinhardt's patch series improving the user interface of the new `git history` command, now at its third iteration. The work focuses on making the command more intuitive by moving validation checks earlier, replacing the technical `--ref-action` flag with clearer alternatives (`--dry-run` and `--update-refs`), and thoroughly documenting all behaviors.

The series has undergone careful review from key contributors. Junio Hamano approved the test approach and verified the dry-run behavior, while D. Ben Knoble validated the transaction safety and merge detection implementation. Kristoffer Haugsbakk provided detailed documentation refinements, including a recent minor wording clarification about HEAD updates in the context of `--dry-run`. This last point was the final open question before the series could be considered complete.

With all five patches now polished through three iterations and all reviewer feedback addressed, the series appears ready for maintainer consideration. The changes maintain clean architectural separation while significantly improving usability, particularly through the new `handle_ref_update()` helper and the clearer flag naming. The comprehensive documentation now covers all behaviors, including default values and dry-run semantics.
