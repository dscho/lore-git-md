Bugfix for diffcore-break machinery addressing a dangling pointer issue in partially cloned repositories. This is version 2 of the patch from first-time contributor Han Young, now including both the required Signed-off-by line and a test case as requested in earlier review.

The issue occurs in blobless clones (`--filter=blob:none`) when performing diff operations that involve prefetched blobs, particularly with large files. The `diffcore_break()` function was freeing a queue structure without NULLing the pointer, which could lead to segmentation faults during operations like `git reset`. The one-line fix properly NULLs the pointer after freeing.

The new test case demonstrates the fault by creating a scenario with large YAML files in a blobless clone, where checkout and reset operations would previously crash. This follows a similar fix from December 2025 (commit 56d388e6) for another diff machinery segfault, suggesting there may be a pattern of similar issues to audit in this code.

The patch appears well-constructed - it's minimal, clearly explained, and now includes both the legal attestation and test coverage that were missing in v1. While focused on a specific crash, it touches on the intersection of two complex subsystems (diff machinery and partial clone functionality), so reviewers may want to consider whether similar patterns exist elsewhere in the codebase.
