This is a follow-up to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" report, specifically addressing Phillip Wood's `pw/commit-msg-sample-hook` series about improving the sample commit-msg hook. The series currently in `next` detects problematic commit messages (those containing separator lines or diffs) and adds a `.gitattributes` entry for sample hooks.

Phillip Wood announces plans for a reroll that will handle subject lines starting with "diff -" (which are safe when prefixed by `git format-patch`'s "[PATCH]") and likely drop the `.gitattributes` patch. This comes from real-world testing while preparing another change (referenced by its object ID). The update suggests the hook's diff detection was being overly aggressive in some cases, and the `.gitattributes` addition may not be necessary after all. The thread shows typical Git development workflow - a series gets merged to `next`, then refined based on further experience before graduating to `master`.
