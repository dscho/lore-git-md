This is a feature patch series that changes Git's default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric". The change comes from Patrick Steinhardt, who has been heavily involved in Git's maintenance and repacking infrastructure.

The main goal is to complete the transition to a more flexible maintenance framework where tasks can be toggled individually. The geometric repacking strategy, which has been available for some time, is more efficient especially for large repositories. While the actual code change is small (a one-line switch in builtin/gc.c), most of the series deals with updating tests that were written with assumptions about the old gc strategy.

The author acknowledges some test fragility - the new strategy may optimize data structures in different scenarios than the old one, causing test failures. He's open to feedback on whether this should first go behind the feature.experimental flag, but chose the direct approach here to stimulate discussion. Users can still revert to the old behavior with maintenance.strategy=gc.

This appears to be a well-considered change from a trusted contributor, though the test updates suggest there may be some subtle behavioral differences that could affect existing setups. The series is thorough in its test coverage, modifying 22 files to ensure the new default works reliably. Given Patrick's track record with maintenance infrastructure, this seems like a positive evolution of Git's maintenance capabilities.
