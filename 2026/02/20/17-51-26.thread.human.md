This thread involves a small but meaningful refactoring patch from Shreyansh Paliwal as part of the ongoing effort to eliminate global state in Git's codebase. The change removes the last remaining uses of the `the_hash_algo` global variable in tree-diff.c, replacing them with repository-specific access via `opt->repo->hash_algo`. 

The patch is clean and focused - it simply updates two calls to use the repository's hash algorithm instead of the global one, then removes now-unneeded definitions and includes. This follows the established pattern seen in many similar cleanups from contributors like René Scharfe who have been driving the `the_repository` removal effort. 

Since the change is purely mechanical with no functional impact, it should be uncontroversial. The patch appears ready for merging as it stands, representing another incremental step in the long-running project to make Git's codebase more modular and less reliant on global state.
