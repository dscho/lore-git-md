Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking the default maintenance strategy has reached its conclusion with the final patch receiving approvals from key reviewers. The series, which began with test infrastructure preparation, now switches the default strategy from "gc" to "geometric" for manual operations while keeping "incremental" as the scheduled maintenance default. This change optimizes packfile maintenance by reducing expensive full repacks while still controlling repository size.

The implementation in builtin/gc.c preserves three strategies: the legacy "gc", scheduled "incremental", and new "geometric" approaches. Documentation has been updated to clearly explain the options and their interactions with tools like Scalar. The series took a careful infrastructure-first approach, with patches 1-7 hardening tests and ensuring compatibility before the final strategy change in patch 8. Test isolation measures were implemented to preserve legacy behavior verification.

Derrick Stolee, who originally designed the geometric repacking approach, has approved the documentation updates. Justin Tobler reviewed the test infrastructure changes, and Toon Claes verified the test isolation approach. With all feedback addressed and no outstanding concerns from reviewers, this well-vetted series appears ready for merging. The change represents a significant optimization for Git's maintenance behavior, particularly benefiting large repositories.
