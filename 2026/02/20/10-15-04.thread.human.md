Patrick Steinhardt's 8-patch series to make geometric repacking the default `git maintenance` strategy is nearing completion, with all prior review feedback addressed and approvals from key reviewers (Derrick Stolee, Justin Tobler, and Toon Claes). The series prepares test infrastructure before switching the default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric" for manual operations, while keeping "incremental" as the default for scheduled maintenance.

The discussion took a small turn when Stefan Haller raised a concern about background maintenance races affecting real-world tools like lazygit, not just Git's test suite. Patrick responded by clarifying that the auto-detach mechanism is designed to make concurrent maintenance transparent to users, though he acknowledged the race condition exists in ordinary usage. He argued that any visibility of this race would be a bug worth investigating, while defending the test-specific controls as necessary for reliable test execution. No code changes were proposed, leaving the series technically ready for merging, but Stefan's observation about lazygit's tests remains unaddressed, leaving a small open question about the robustness of Git's background maintenance in practice.
