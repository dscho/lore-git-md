Patrick Steinhardt's series to make geometric repacking Git's default maintenance strategy has reached its final review stage. The 8-patch series, which began with test infrastructure improvements before introducing the behavior change, has now received approval from all key reviewers. Derrick Stolee, who originated the geometric repacking design, signed off on the documentation updates, while Justin Tobler confirmed his feedback on test infrastructure was addressed in v2 of the series.

The change transitions Git's manual maintenance operations from the legacy "gc" strategy to the more efficient "geometric" approach, while keeping "incremental" as the default for scheduled maintenance. The series includes thorough documentation explaining all three available strategies (gc, incremental, geometric) and their use cases, with particular attention to how geometric repacking interacts with Scalar. Additional test annotations were added to help future maintainers understand the changes.

With all review feedback incorporated and no outstanding concerns, this long-running effort to improve Git's large-repository performance appears ready for merging. The careful progression from infrastructure hardening to behavior change, combined with positive feedback from multiple domain experts, demonstrates the change's safety and readiness for integration.
