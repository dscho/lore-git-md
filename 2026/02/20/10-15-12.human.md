This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to change git maintenance's default strategy from "gc" to "geometric". The change is a simple one-line modification in builtin/gc.c, but comes after extensive preparation across the test suite in the previous 7 patches.

The patch explains the historical context - git-gc's 2006-era strategy of incremental repacks with occasional all-into-one repacks doesn't scale well for today's larger repositories. The newer "geometric" strategy (introduced in Git 2.52) maintains a progression of packfiles that reduces expensive full repacks while still handling expiration tasks that the "incremental" strategy misses.

Patrick notes this isn't a perfect solution - very large repositories may still need Scalar's "incremental" strategy - but represents a significant improvement for most cases. The change maintains backward compatibility via the maintenance.strategy=gc config option.

This concludes a characteristically thorough series from Patrick, where most of the complexity went into ensuring test stability before making the actual behavior change. The patch is well-motivated and comes with comprehensive test coverage, making it likely to be accepted.
