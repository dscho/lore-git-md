This is a cover letter for Adrian Ratiu's v2 series implementing config-based hooks, part of his ongoing effort to make Git hooks configurable via Git configuration rather than just filesystem paths. The series introduces `hook.<name>.command`, `hook.<name>.event`, and `hook.<name>.enabled` configuration options while maintaining backward compatibility with filesystem hooks.

Junio Hamano expresses some concern about discoverability when hooks move from the filesystem to config files, but notes that the new `git hook list` subcommand (included in this series) addresses this by providing visibility into all configured hooks. He also indicates that the prerequisite "hook conversion" series appears ready to be merged to 'next', which will help testing and integration of this work.

Adrian confirms he's preparing a v2 of his parallel execution series (a separate but related effort) and that both will benefit from the base hook conversion work landing first. The thread shows steady progress on this feature, with maintainer engagement and approval of the overall direction while noting practical considerations around discoverability. The series is in its final polishing phase with constructive reviews from both Junio and Patrick Steinhardt focusing on implementation details.
