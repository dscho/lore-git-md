**New critical issues raised post-approval:**

Root cause identified as a legacy scaling bug in `too_many_loose_objects()`.
2. **Concurrency failures**: The same report demonstrates real-world impact with:
   - "Invalid object" errors during concurrent operations
   - Temporary file failures in `objects/XX/` directories
   - Commit failures at ~1/1,100 commits in stress testing
   - Reproducible in lazygit CI (real-world tooling)
   - Root cause: `prune_packed_objects()` removes fanout directories while concurrent writers attempt to create temporary files, defeating Git’s single-retry mitigation
   - Potential midx staleness in long-lived processes (unconfirmed connection)
