This is a maintainer note about contributor workflow rather than a technical discussion. Junio C Hamano points out that the email address used in the author field and Signed-off-by trailer of the original branch prefixing patch is bouncing. He makes two requests:

1. To the patch author (Yoann Valeri), he asks to ensure sign-offs use a reachable email address, noting that while addresses becoming unreachable over time is unavoidable, it shouldn't happen before patch acceptance.

2. To GitGitGadget maintainers, he suggests implementing a verification step - perhaps a ping email to first-time contributors - to catch unreachable addresses before patches are submitted to the list.

The message doesn't engage with the technical merits of the branch prefixing feature itself, which was the subject of earlier discussion. Instead, it focuses on maintaining contributor hygiene and preventing similar issues in the future. This is typical of Junio's role in shepherding the contribution process - catching administrative problems early while letting technical discussions play out separately.
