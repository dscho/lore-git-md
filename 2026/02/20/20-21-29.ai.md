This is a straightforward refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Junio C Hamano (the Git maintainer) is responding to Shreyansh Paliwal's patch that eliminates the last remaining uses of the `the_hash_algo` global variable in tree-diff.c. The change replaces two calls to `null_oid(the_hash_algo)` with `null_oid(opt->repo->hash_algo)`, making the hash algorithm access repository-specific rather than global.

Since this removes all global state usage from the file, the patch also cleans up by removing the now-unneeded `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define and `environment.h` include. The change follows established patterns seen in similar refactorings and references prior commits that introduced `opt->repo` to tree-diff.c. Junio's response indicates the patch looks good and will be queued for inclusion - a clear sign of approval from the maintainer for this mechanical but meaningful step in eliminating global state.
