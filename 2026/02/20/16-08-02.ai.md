Maintainer housekeeping note regarding contributor email addresses.

Junio C Hamano points out that the email address used in the author identity and sign-off line for the proposed branch prefix configuration patch is bouncing (returning "550 mailbox unavailable"). He makes two requests:

1. To the patch author (Yoann Valeri): Ensure the sign-off uses a reachable email address. While it's understood that contributors may become unreachable over time, having an immediately bouncing address at submission time is problematic.

This is a procedural note about contribution logistics rather than technical feedback on the branch prefix feature itself. The core technical discussion about whether to implement this as a command-line flag first (as Junio previously suggested) remains ongoing in the thread.
