Part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, this patch continues Elijah Newren's series to eliminate global variable usage in merge-ort.c. Patch 4/6 specifically replaces all instances of `the_hash_algo` (which implicitly depends on `the_repository`) with direct access to the hash algorithm via `opt->repo->hash_algo`.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching 13 locations where null object IDs are created or copied during merge operations. The patch maintains identical behavior while properly parameterizing the hash algorithm access. This follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, where repository-specific parameters replace global state.

As with previous patches in the series, this change has been reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt and incorporates feedback from the v1 iteration. The merge-ort subsystem remains fully functional, just with cleaner internal architecture. Given Elijah's deep expertise in the merge machinery and the straightforward nature of these substitutions, the patch appears ready for inclusion.
