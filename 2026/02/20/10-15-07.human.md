This is part 3 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to make "geometric" the default maintenance strategy instead of "gc". The patch focuses on test stability by preventing reflog expiration in several rebase-related test files.

The issue arises because Git's test suite hardcodes dates to 2005, making all reflog entries appear immediately stale. While this wasn't previously problematic, the new geometric strategy will actively expire reflogs during maintenance, which would break tests that rely on reflog data. The patch adds `gc.reflogExpire` config settings to four rebase test files to preserve their reflogs.

This is a preparatory change that demonstrates Patrick's thorough approach - identifying and fixing potential test failures before they occur when the maintenance strategy changes. The patch is straightforward and well-explained, showing how the geometric strategy's different task evaluation could surface previously hidden test dependencies.
