The core prefixing logic is cleanly encapsulated and handles recursive cases (like prefixing "main-test-sub" when on branch "main-test"). Test coverage demonstrates all expected behaviors including config/CLI interaction and duplicate name detection.

While the technical implementation appears solid, two open questions remain: whether Junio will find the narrowed scope compelling enough to include, and whether the author's ongoing email delivery issues might complicate final review.
