This is the first patch in a series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch focuses on cleaning up prefetching logic that became obsolete after promisor-remote changes in 2021.

The change removes redundant checks that prevented blob prefetching unless operating on `the_repository`, a restriction that was lifted by commit ef830cc434 ("promisor-remote: teach lazy-fetch in any repo"). The patch updates four files (diff.c, diffcore-break.c, diffcore-rename.c, and merge-ort.c) to eliminate these now-unnecessary conditions.

Elijah Newren provides thorough historical context in the commit message, tracing how these checks were originally added for correctness but became vestigial after the promisor-remote improvements. The changes are straightforward and mechanical - just removing the `the_repository` comparisons while keeping the core prefetching behavior intact.

As the first patch in v2 of this series, it addresses feedback from the initial review by properly integrating this prefetch cleanup that was identified as a logical follow-up. The patch appears well-justified and low-risk, maintaining existing functionality while advancing the broader goal of removing `the_repository` dependencies.
