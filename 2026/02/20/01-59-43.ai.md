This is the first patch in a v2 series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch focuses on cleaning up obsolete checks for `the_repository` in blob prefetching logic.

The change removes redundant repository checks that were originally added when lazy fetching was only supported for `the_repository`. Since commit ef830cc434 (2021) enabled lazy fetching in any repository context, these checks are no longer needed. The patch updates four files - diff.c, diffcore-break.c, diffcore-rename.c and merge-ort.c - to simply check for promisor remotes without the repository comparison.

The commit message provides thorough historical context, tracing how these checks propagated through the codebase and why they became unnecessary. It also notes this was identified as a follow-up during v1 review, showing the series is responsive to feedback. The changes are mechanical and behavior-preserving, just removing now-redundant conditions.

This is a straightforward cleanup that advances the broader goal of removing `the_repository` dependencies while maintaining existing functionality. The patch appears well-researched and low-risk, coming from Elijah Newren who has deep expertise in the merge machinery being modified.
