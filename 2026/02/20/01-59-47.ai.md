This is the final patch in Elijah Newren's series to remove `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate this global variable. The patch introduces a compile-time enforcement mechanism to prevent future regressions by defining `the_repository` as `DO_NOT_USE_THE_REPOSITORY` in merge-ort.c, making it a compilation error if anyone tries to use it again.

The patch acknowledges one unavoidable exception - `DEFAULT_ABBREV` still requires `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE`, but all other uses have been systematically removed in previous patches. The enforcement comes with a clear comment directing developers to use `opt->repo` instead, maintaining the parameterized approach established in the series.

This is a defensive programming measure that follows the pattern seen in other subsystems that have completed their `the_repository` removal. The patch builds on Patrick Steinhardt's positive review of the compile-time enforcement approach from the v1 discussion. With all substantive changes already addressed in earlier patches, this final enforcement step appears uncontroversial and ready for merging as part of the broader effort.
