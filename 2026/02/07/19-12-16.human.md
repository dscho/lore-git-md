This is a performance discussion thread related to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Ayush Jha's RFC patch that modifies `read_attr()` in `attr.c` to use repository-local state instead of the global `the_repository`.

Tian Yuchen raises a concern about potential performance regression from replacing `is_bare_repository()` with `repo_config_get_bool()` calls. They describe a series of benchmark tests they conducted with large numbers of files and attribute checks, finding no measurable performance difference even in stress-tested scenarios designed to defeat attribute caching. The tests include creating repositories with thousands of files and attributes, then measuring `git ls-files` performance before and after the change.

The email serves as a request for validation of these benchmark results, asking whether others have conducted similar tests and whether the expected outcome should indeed be no performance impact. The tone is constructive and curious, coming from a new contributor who has taken the initiative to test the patch's performance implications thoroughly. The question appears well-motivated and technically grounded, though it remains open whether the testing methodology fully captures all real-world usage patterns.
