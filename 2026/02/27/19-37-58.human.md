Performance optimization discussion for `git-upload-pack` lock contention. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that reduces write syscalls by buffering packfile data, suggesting alternative approaches that might be simpler and more effective.

Peff proposes two main ideas: first, increasing the buffer size in `csum-file.c` from 8KB to 32KB to reduce write calls between pack-objects and upload-pack (demonstrating this cuts writes from ~420k to ~160k when cloning Linux). Second, he suggests using `writev()` in `sideband.c` to combine the pkt-line header and data writes, which could further reduce syscalls to ~82k. He also references prior discussion about packet-forming optimizations.

The email provides concrete performance measurements and alternative implementation paths while maintaining the same goal of reducing lock contention. Peff's suggestions appear technically sound and build on Patrick's work rather than contradicting it, offering complementary optimizations that might address the keepalive timeout concerns more cleanly. The tone is collaborative, with Peff sharing his testing methodology and acknowledging the tradeoffs of each approach.
