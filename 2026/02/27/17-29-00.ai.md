Performance optimization in `git-upload-pack` addressing lock contention. This is the second patch in a two-part series from Patrick Steinhardt, with Junio C Hamano now weighing in on the technical approach.

The patch reduces write syscall frequency during packfile transfers by buffering more data before writing (waiting until the buffer is at least 2/3 full). This builds on the first patch's removal of debug output that was inadvertently sent to clients. The change is minimal (7 lines) but impactful - reducing write syscalls from ~400,000 to ~130,000 when cloning the Linux repository, addressing kernel-level lock contention observed in high-concurrency production scenarios.

Junio approves the change with characteristic enthusiasm for well-reasoned, small-but-impactful patches ("I love this size ratio between explanation and code change"). His review confirms the technical approach while brian m. carlson's earlier concern about potential keepalive interference remains the only outstanding question. The patch appears ready for merging pending resolution of that edge case, with Junio's approval signaling likely acceptance into the next integration window.
