This thread addresses a bug where Git's option parser silently accepts duplicate long-form options, discovered in `cat-file` and `pack-objects`. Junio Hamano originally reported the issue, and René Scharfe provided a two-part fix: first removing the concrete duplicate in `pack-objects`, then adding systematic validation to detect such issues across all commands. The validation runs during help display to minimize performance impact.

The solution went through rigorous performance analysis. Jeff King suggested optimizations, but René's benchmarks on an M1 system confirmed that his original `strset` implementation (adding ~5ms overhead) outperforms a sorted-array approach. The project's engineering culture shines here - multiple implementations were tested across systems before concluding the current solution offers the best balance. All participants agree the validation's benefits outweigh its negligible overhead when limited to help contexts.
