The thread began with Junio Hamano reporting that Git's option parser silently accepts duplicate long-form option names, which could lead to subtle bugs. He demonstrated the issue with `--batch-check` in `cat-file` and noted a redundant `--stdin-packs` in `pack-objects`. René Scharfe quickly fixed the concrete `pack-objects` case by removing the shadowed `OPT_BOOL` version of `--stdin-packs` while keeping the newer `OPT_CALLBACK_F` version.

Now René has followed up with a systematic solution - a patch that adds comprehensive duplicate detection for long option names and numerical options in `parse-options.c`. The implementation uses a `strset` to track long names and maintains the existing short option validation. While there's a performance cost to these checks, René leaves optimization (like making them DEVELOPER-mode only) as future work. This provides a general fix for the issue Junio identified, going beyond just addressing the specific cases that surfaced the problem. The discussion remains focused between these two core contributors, with Junio having identified the issue and René providing both the specific fix and now the general solution.
