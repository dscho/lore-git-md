The issue specifically involves zero-width ANSI color codes (like `\033[31m` for red text) rather than UTF-8 characters.

Junio also notes an accidental inclusion of an empty file `t/b` in the patch's diffstat, suggesting this was unintended. The thread shows the maintainer providing careful review of both technical substance and presentation details, with the core fix being technically sound but needing minor documentation adjustments.
