This is part of a feature series implementing configurable cover letter formatting for `git format-patch`, now in its third iteration. The series has evolved through maintainer feedback and incorporates Jeff King's technical design for patch numbering.

This specific patch adds two new placeholders to `pretty.c`: `%(count)` and `%(total)`, which will be used to display patch numbers in cover letters. The placeholders access `rev_info` fields that were previously only used for email subjects. The implementation includes proper error handling when these placeholders are used in contexts without revision information.

The patch is well-contained, adding just 15 lines to handle the new placeholders while maintaining the existing error checking pattern. It's part of a larger effort that has already addressed architectural concerns through previous rounds of review, with Junio C Hamano and Jeff King actively shaping the implementation. The series appears production-ready, with comprehensive test coverage added in later patches.
