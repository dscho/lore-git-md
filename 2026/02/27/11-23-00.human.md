This is a preparatory patch in a two-part series addressing performance issues in `git-upload-pack`. The patch removes a stray debug message ("flushed.") that was inadvertently being sent to clients during packfile transfers. The message was introduced in 2006 and appears to be a leftover debugging artifact that should not have been visible to end clients.

The change is straightforward - just removing the `fprintf` statement - but serves as groundwork for the more substantial performance optimization in the next patch. The commit demonstrates good hygiene by including a reproduction showing the unwanted message appearing in client output, though the actual impact is minor since it's just an aesthetic issue rather than a functional one.

This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing work to optimize server-side Git performance, particularly in high-concurrency scenarios. The next patch in the series will address the more significant issue of lock contention during packfile writes.
