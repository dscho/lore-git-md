Bugfix for the fsmonitor-watchman hook addressing error handling and protocol compliance issues. This is a follow-up ping on an earlier patch that remains unreviewed.

The patch fixes two specific issues in the watchman integration for fsmonitor: (1) an incorrect variable reference (`$o->{error}` instead of `$output->{error}`) that caused error conditions to be silently ignored, and (2) a protocol violation where the hook was incorrectly outputting two clock tokens during fallback to full scan operations when only one is permitted by Git's fsmonitor v2 protocol.

The changes are focused and well-contained, touching only the watchman-specific components in the sample hook and test infrastructure. The author has maintained backward compatibility while fixing the protocol compliance issue by removing a redundant recursive call to launch_watchman() - the "/" flag already properly signals when a full scan is needed.
