This thread has finalized a two-patch series introducing type-safe cleanup for oidmap structures through a new `oidmap_clear_with_free()` API. The series, originally broader in scope, was narrowed down to focus on converting rev-list's missing_objects and odb's replace_map after maintainer feedback.

The key technical change is the introduction of a callback-based cleanup mechanism that makes memory ownership semantics clearer, particularly when oidmap entries are embedded in larger structures. The rev-list conversion demonstrates this well by replacing manual path cleanup with a type-safe callback, while the odb conversion shows replace_map handling. Comprehensive test coverage was added in this final iteration to validate the API behavior.

Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have both approved the current version, with Patrick driving the scope reduction to focus on the most valuable conversions. The author, Seyi Kuforiji, incorporated all requested changes including renaming the callback to `missing_objects_map_entry_free` and making the path field non-const for proper cleanup. While one minor question about `map->map.cmpfn` remains open, it doesn't block the series, which appears ready for merging in its current two-patch form.
