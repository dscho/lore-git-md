This is a bugfix for `git format-patch` that ensures the `--from` option is properly applied to cover letters, not just individual patches. The patch addresses an inconsistency where the specified sender identity was being ignored for cover letters while working correctly for the patches themselves.

The fix modifies `make_cover_letter()` in `builtin/log.c` to check the configured `from` value before falling back to the committer info, and renames a variable from `committer` to `from` for clarity. A new test case verifies the corrected behavior while maintaining existing functionality. The documentation is also updated to explicitly state that `--from` applies to both patches and cover letters.

This is the final version of the patch after review feedback, with all maintainer concerns addressed. The change is small (6 lines modified) and focused, with Junio Hamano and other reviewers confirming its technical soundness. While the immediate issue is resolved, the thread discussion has surfaced some related documentation inconsistencies and potential improvements to cover letter formatting that may be addressed in future work.
