This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing ref backend selection series, which is now in its final polishing stages with maintainer approval. The patch simplifies `create_reference_database()` by removing redundant ref storage format setting, as the format is already properly set by callers (`repository_format_configure()` for init and direct setting for clone). 

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, with Patrick Steinhardt (the series author) approving the simplification. This is one of several small cleanups being made to the series before it graduates to the 'next' branch. The patch demonstrates good attention to detail in removing unnecessary operations that could cause issues if the ref storage format handling becomes more complex in the future.
