Koji Nakamaru attempts to reproduce the issue doesn't surface on newer macOS versions (15.7.4 and 14.8.4) with current Homebrew formulas, suggesting this may be a platform-specific edge case affecting only certain older configurations. The exchange highlights a potential need for additional robustness in the Makefile rules to handle missing dependency directories across all macOS versions, though the core solution remains sound.
