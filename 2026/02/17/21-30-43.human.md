This is a documentation and test fix addressing behavior in `git format-patch`. The patch clarifies that the `--from` option applies to both individual commit emails and the cover letter in a patch series, removing ambiguity in the documentation. It also improves the test case to specifically verify the cover letter's From header rather than checking both the cover letter and a patch in the same test.

The change comes in response to Patrick Steinhardt's review of an earlier version, where he noted the documentation could be clearer and suggested a more focused test. The author (Mirko Faina) agrees with both points and will update the patch accordingly. This appears to be a straightforward improvement with clear reviewer consensus.
