This appears to be a platform-specific edge case, as Koji Nakamaru couldn't reproduce it on newer macOS versions (14.8.4 and 15.7.4). Knoble provides a manual workaround (creating the directory) and details about his environment, while Nakamaru seeks more precise reproduction steps to understand the failure scenario.

The core solution remains sound, but this discussion highlights a subtle interaction with older macOS/Homebrew configurations where the Makefile changes may need explicit directory creation handling.
