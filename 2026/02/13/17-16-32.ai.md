Bugfix addressing a dangling pointer in diffcore-break during partial clone operations, now at version 3 with test reliability questions remaining. This patch fixes a segmentation fault that occurs in blobless clones (`--filter=blob:none`) when performing diff operations like `git reset` on prefetched blobs, particularly with large files. The core issue is in `diffcore_break()` where a queue structure is freed without NULLing the pointer.

The one-line fix (setting queue entry to NULL after freeing) remains technically sound, but discussion has expanded to consider broader implications. Junio C Hamano questions why break detection is being performed at all for internal diff operations where it appears unnecessary, suggesting this may indicate wasted effort in rename detection during internal operations. The author, Han Young, acknowledges limited familiarity with reset/diff internals and expresses uncertainty about reliably testing the memory issue.

Test reliability remains an open question - the failure reproduces only about 95% of the time on the author's system due to memory allocation patterns. Junio suggests this may be acceptable since ASAN would catch such issues, though the test could potentially be improved. The architectural question about break detection applicability to internal diffs remains unresolved and may warrant follow-up investigation.

This follows a similar December 2025 fix (commit 56d388e6) for related diff machinery segfaults, suggesting a potential need for wider review of diff machinery memory handling in partial clone contexts. The patch appears nearly ready pending test refinements, but Junio's deeper architectural question may require separate discussion.
