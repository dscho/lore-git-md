This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series improving the `git history` command's interface. It's a straightforward documentation update that clarifies the default behavior of the `--update-refs` option, which was introduced in earlier patches to replace the more technical `--ref-action` flag.

The patch adds a single line specifying that `--update-refs` defaults to updating all local branches (`branches`) rather than just HEAD when no value is explicitly provided. This completes the series' goal of making the command's behavior more transparent to users.

The change is minimal but important for usability - it ensures users understand what will happen when they run the command without specifying the `--update-refs` option. The series as a whole has been well-received, with Junio Hamano and D. Ben Knoble providing thorough reviews that have been addressed in this iteration. With all feedback incorporated and no outstanding concerns, this documentation patch wraps up the series cleanly.
