This thread documents the successful completion of Shreyansh Paliwal and Phillip Wood's worktree API refactoring series, which removes the redundant `struct repository` parameter from `worktree_git_path()`. Junio Hamano has approved the final patch as a "well-reasoned cleanup" that eliminates potential repository/worktree mismatches while maintaining cross-component functionality. The changes demonstrate concrete progress in the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

However, Junio has raised an important architectural question about the relationship between worktrees and repositories. He initially considered whether we'd need a `repo_is_current_worktree()` variant, but realized this might not make sense given that worktrees now explicitly track their owning repository (`wt->repo`). He identified a potential bug in the current `is_current_worktree()` implementation, which uses `the_worktree` instead of `wt->repo`, highlighting subtle invariants about worktree-repository relationships that need careful consideration.

While the technical changes are complete and approved for merging, this discussion surfaces deeper design questions that remain open: the semantics of worktrees in submodule contexts, proper definition of "current worktree," and documentation of worktree/repository invariants. These will need to be addressed in future work as the project continues to refine its model of worktree-repository relationships without relying on global state.
