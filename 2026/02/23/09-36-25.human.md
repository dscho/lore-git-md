This is a technical discussion in the ongoing thread about `git fsck` packfile verification issues. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's concerns about the root cause of the infinite loop bug in packfile processing.

The exchange clarifies that while the immediate issue was fixed by adding a `skip_mru_updates` flag, the deeper architectural problem remains: any iteration over the packfile list that calls `find_pack_entry()` risks modifying the MRU list during traversal. Patrick acknowledges this but suggests the solution space includes either reinstating a separate iteration list or extending `repo_for_each_pack()` to handle MRU updates safely.

Notably, Patrick reveals his longer-term goal to eliminate `repo_for_each_pack()` entirely, which would naturally resolve the iteration safety issue by encapsulating pack traversal within the backend. This connects to his broader ODB abstraction work. The discussion remains technical but productive, with both contributors recognizing the need to balance immediate fixes against architectural improvements.
