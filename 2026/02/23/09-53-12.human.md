This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about `git fsck` packfile verification issues. Patrick Steinhardt confirms the root cause analysis from earlier in the thread, noting that the infinite loop occurs specifically with large objects (exceeding core.bigFileThreshold) in multiple packfiles. He points out a deeper verification correctness issue - when duplicate objects exist in multiple packs, `git fsck` may verify the wrong instance due to MRU list updates during iteration.

Patrick mentions he has prepared patches to fix these verification correctness issues (referenced in his footnote) and reiterates his long-term plan to eliminate `repo_for_each_pack()` entirely as part of the ODB abstraction work. The discussion continues to balance immediate fixes (like brian m. carlson's MRU skip flag) with architectural improvements to the verification process.

The thread shows good technical alignment between the core contributors, with Patrick building on Jeff King's earlier analysis while preparing more comprehensive fixes. The verification correctness issues appear to be a known but previously unaddressed limitation in Git's packfile handling.
