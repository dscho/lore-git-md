This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing an infinite loop in `git fsck` when verifying large objects across multiple packfiles. The core fix replaces `odb_read_stream_open()` with direct packfile access via `packfile_read_object_stream()`, which Jeff King describes as "delightfully simple" in his review.

The discussion now focuses on improving the test case. Jeff King suggests making it more concrete by explicitly testing corruption detection when the same object exists in multiple packs (one corrupt, one valid). Patrick acknowledges this would be better but notes the difficulty of reliably creating corrupted objects with hash mismatches, promising to look into it for v2.

The series appears technically sound with all reviewer feedback addressed, though the test coverage could be strengthened. This is characteristic of Patrick's careful approach - structural refactoring first, then the core fix, with thorough review feedback incorporated. The fix resolves a real issue (originally reported by brian m. carlson) where `git fsck` could enter an infinite loop when checking large objects.
