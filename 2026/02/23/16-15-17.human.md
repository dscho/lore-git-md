This is a test infrastructure patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to change Git's default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric". The patch focuses on rebase tests (t34xx range) that would be affected by the new maintenance behavior.

The issue arises because Git's test suite hardcodes commit and reflog dates to 2005, making reflog entries appear stale under the geometric strategy's more aggressive expiration. The patch disables reflog expiration in these tests by setting `gc.reflogExpire=never`. Justin Tobler confirms the approach makes sense but suggests adding a comment to clarify why the configuration is needed, since the hardcoded date behavior isn't immediately obvious.

This is part of the careful preparation for the maintenance strategy change, showing attention to preserving existing test behavior. The patch has already been reviewed positively by Justin Tobler, who is familiar with the test infrastructure implications. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, focused solely on maintaining test reliability under the new defaults.
