This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing an infinite loop in `git fsck` when verifying large objects across multiple packfiles. The patch refactors `stream_object_signature()` to accept a preconstructed `odb_read_stream` rather than creating its own, shifting stream lifecycle management to callers. This is a preparatory change for the core fix in patch 4/4.

Jeff King reviewed the change and confirmed the technical approach is sound, suggesting minor improvements: separating error conditions for better error messages and removing a redundant stream check. Patrick agrees with both suggestions and will incorporate them in v2. The discussion also touches on whether the repository parameter could be eliminated by having the stream track its source, but Patrick notes this would be a larger change best handled separately.

The patch follows Patrick's careful style - structural refactoring first, with clear commit messages explaining each step. Jeff's review confirms this is moving in the right direction with only minor polish needed. The change is low-risk as it doesn't alter behavior, just reorganizes control flow in preparation for the actual bugfix.
