This is a GSoC draft proposal from Tian Yuchen to work on reducing Git's global state by continuing René Scharfe's long-running `the_repository` removal effort. The proposal comes after initial format feedback from Usman Akinyemi prompted a move from Google Docs to plain-text email.

The student demonstrates solid preparation with multiple small contributions already merged or in review, including test modernization and symlink handling patches. Their most involved work so far is a setup.c refactoring that required deep code analysis. The proposal itself shows good technical understanding of the problem space, identifying thread safety and testing limitations caused by global variables like `git_commit_encoding`.

The suggested approach is methodical: introducing a `struct git_env` within `struct repository`, plumbing context parameters through call chains, and using tools like AddressSanitizer for verification. The timeline proposes frequent small patch series submissions (3-5 patches at a time) with clear milestones, though the scope may be ambitious given the pervasive nature of global state in Git.

While the technical direction aligns with ongoing community efforts, substantive review will likely wait until the proposal is properly submitted as a patch series rather than a standalone email. The student's demonstrated persistence in previous reviews suggests they can handle the iterative feedback this complex refactoring will require.
