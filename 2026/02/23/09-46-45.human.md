This is a technical discussion about `git fsck` packfile verification, continuing the thread about MRU list iteration safety and verification correctness. Jeff King (Peff) and Patrick Steinhardt are aligning on the deeper architectural issues exposed by the recent infinite loop bug.

The conversation clarifies that there are two distinct but related problems: the immediate MRU iteration safety issue (fixed in v1 with a `skip_mru_updates` flag) and a more fundamental verification correctness problem where `fsck` may check the wrong pack's copy of duplicate objects. Peff expresses preference for the double-list approach, considering the `skip_updating_mru` flag a temporary hack.

The discussion remains focused and technical, with both contributors clearly understanding each other's positions.
