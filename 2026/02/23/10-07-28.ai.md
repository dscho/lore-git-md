Bugfix for URL handling in `git fetch`. This patch corrects an evaluation order issue in the trailing-slash trimming logic that could lead to undefined behavior when accessing `url[i]` with a negative index. The fix swaps the order of conditions in the for-loop to check the array bounds before accessing the element.

The change is small and focused - just reordering two conditions in a loop that trims trailing slashes from URLs displayed during fetch operations. The commit message is minimal but correctly identifies the undefined behavior risk. This appears to be a straightforward correctness fix with no obvious downsides or controversy. The patch touches only `builtin/fetch.c` and doesn't introduce any new concepts or dependencies.
