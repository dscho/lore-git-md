Justin Tobler acknowledged the point but didn't feel strongly either way. The discussion then expanded to consider broader use cases, with Junio noting the current limitation that only previously-signed commits are handled. He proposed separating the concerns - using `strip-if-invalid` for bad signatures while adding a distinct `--sign-commits` option for unsigned commits during history rewriting.

While the core functionality remains stable post-merge, this exchange highlights potential future directions for the feature. The naming question appears likely to evolve toward `sign-if-invalid`, though no immediate follow-up patch has been promised. The thread also surfaces considerations for handling mixed signed/unsigned histories - an area that may see future development.
