This thread covers a three-patch series that adds a `re-sign-if-invalid` mode to `git fast-import`, allowing invalid commit signatures to be re-signed during import rather than just being stripped or preserved. The series was authored by Christian Couder with implementation by Justin Tobler, and has already been merged after addressing all feedback from the initial version.

The implementation builds on preparatory refactoring that moved signing logic to gpg-interface.c, then adds the new mode with comprehensive test coverage for OpenPGP, X.509, and SSH signatures. The series was reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt and brian m. carlson, with Christian providing naming suggestions and minor wording feedback. Junio Hamano approved the direction without objections.

In a follow-up discussion after merging, Christian suggested two small improvements: using "RE_SIGN" for better enum naming consistency and extracting duplicated warning logic. Justin agreed to both changes, noting some complexity in generalizing the warning strings. These are minor polish items that don't affect the core functionality, which already has thorough test coverage. The thread also notes future work may address tag signatures and interoperability modes, but these are left for separate follow-up efforts.
