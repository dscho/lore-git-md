This is a bugfix patch in Vaidas Pilkauskas's strbuf_attach fixes series (v5 1/4). It corrects an allocation size mismatch in `strbuf_reencode()` where the function was passing `len` instead of `len+1` to `strbuf_attach()`, causing unnecessary reallocations.

The patch fixes a subtle memory management issue where the strbuf would always reallocate immediately after attaching a buffer, even when no extension was needed. This happened because the allocation size was one byte too small to hold the NUL terminator. The change ensures proper buffer reuse and avoids the redundant reallocation.

This follows up on earlier discussion in the thread where Junio Hamano noted the performance importance of strbuf_attach's slack space optimization. The fix is straightforward and well-contained, touching only one line in strbuf.c. It's part of a larger audit of strbuf_attach usage that Vaidas has been working through across multiple subsystems.
