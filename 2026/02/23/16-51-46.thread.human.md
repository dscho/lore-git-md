This thread addresses a bug where Git running as PID 1 in containers would leave zombie processes after abnormal exits from network operations (like git-remote-https or ssh). The proposed fix adds atexit handlers in transport-helper.c and connect.c to ensure child processes are reaped regardless of exit path, while preserving normal cleanup behavior.

Andrew Au submitted the initial patch, which received a note from Kristoffer Haugsbakk about an email address discrepancy - the From header used a GitHub noreply address while the Signed-off-by trailer used a personal email. Andrew has now responded, attributing this to GitHub's privacy settings, and indicated he'll update the commit author information to use his real email address (cshung@gmail.com) in a v2 submission.

The technical solution remains unchanged and appears well-targeted at the container-specific issue. The thread is now waiting for an updated patch submission that resolves the email consistency matter. No technical objections or alternative approaches have been raised yet, though the question of whether to include a test case remains open.
