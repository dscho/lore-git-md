This thread addressed a subtle bug in Git's `receive.denyCurrentBranch=updateInstead` behavior when pushing to bare repositories that have associated worktrees. The issue was originally reported by Runxi Yu, who identified that Git would incorrectly reject pushes when: (1) the bare repository's HEAD pointed to an unborn branch (not matching the worktree branch), and (2) the worktree itself was clean.

Pablo Sabater took the lead on developing the fix, which involved refactoring the staged changes detection logic in `builtin/receive-pack.c` to properly examine the worktree's HEAD rather than the bare repository's HEAD. The solution passed the worktree object (not just its path) through the call chain and replaced `head_has_history()` with a direct `is_null_oid()` check. The series progressed through several iterations, including test case development and test infrastructure improvements to ensure reliable execution.

Maintainer Junio C Hamano has now reviewed and approved the changes, noting that the fix is "surprisingly small" given the complexity of the edge case. The series will be included in the `next` integration branch, resolving this corner case while maintaining backward compatibility for non-worktree scenarios. This concludes a well-structured thread that moved cleanly from problem identification through to solution, with contributions from both the original reporter and the developer who implemented the fix.
