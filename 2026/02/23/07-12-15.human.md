This is a follow-up discussion about a bugfix for `git fsck`'s packfile verification that could cause an infinite loop. The original fix from brian m. carlson added a `skip_mru_updates` flag to prevent MRU list modifications during verification, which Junio Hamano has already approved for merging.

Jeff King (Peff) raises deeper architectural concerns about the MRU list design. While acknowledging the current fix is correct for the immediate issue, he questions whether maintaining a single MRU list for pack iteration is fundamentally sound. He points out that the workaround requires callers to explicitly opt out of MRU updates, which is both error-prone (missing a case could reintroduce the bug) and potentially inefficient (preventing natural MRU optimization during iteration).

The discussion has shifted from the specific fix to broader design questions about pack iteration stability. Peff suggests the project may need to either reintroduce a separate stable list for iteration or provide a guaranteed-stable iteration method. This would avoid requiring every iteration caller to remember the special flag.

The thread shows consensus on the immediate fix but opens a larger conversation about the MRU list's role in pack access patterns. The issue appears limited to explicit `git fsck` invocations with no server-side impact, reducing urgency for backporting while the design questions are considered.
