This is a reply in the ongoing thread about changing Git's default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric". Derrick Stolee acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt's rationale for the change but suggests two refinements to the documentation.

Stolee agrees the geometric strategy is a better default than gc strategy for most users, particularly for foreground maintenance operations. However, he notes the incremental strategy (used by Scalar) may still be necessary for extremely large repositories that need strict limits on data processed during maintenance.

The reply suggests two documentation improvements: (1) clarifying that the incremental strategy is set via `git maintenance start` rather than being Scalar-specific, and (2) proposing an update to maintenance.adoc to document the new default strategy. These are minor but important clarifications to ensure users understand how to configure different maintenance approaches.

The exchange shows consensus on the technical merits of the change while ensuring proper documentation accompanies this behavioral shift in Git's maintenance subsystem. Stolee's feedback indicates the series is nearing readiness for merging, pending these final documentation tweaks.
