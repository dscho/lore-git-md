Patrick acknowledges there's currently no use case where the trim length would differ from the prefix length, but suggests leaving this as a potential future optimization rather than changing it now. The discussion shows the careful attention to API design details in this refactoring effort, where even small interface decisions are being scrutinized for future flexibility.
