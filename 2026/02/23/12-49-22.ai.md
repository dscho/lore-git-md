This is a bugfix patch addressing incorrect usage of `strbuf_attach()` in the rerere and apply subsystems. The patch is part of a series (v4 2/5) that aims to ensure all callers properly handle memory allocation when attaching buffers to strbufs.

The discussion centers around whether to use `strbuf_attach()` directly or switch to `strbuf_add()` followed by `free()`. Junio Hamano points out that while the proposed change would work, it could lead to unnecessary reallocations in some cases. The original `strbuf_attach()` implementation may be more efficient since it can sometimes avoid extra allocations by utilizing slack space from the system allocator.

The author, Vaidas Pilkauskas, agrees with Junio's assessment and decides to keep the original `strbuf_attach()` calls in rerere and apply. They also mention removing a separate patch that would have added a BUG() check to enforce the strbuf_attach contract, suggesting this validation may not be necessary after all.

This patch shows good attention to performance implications of memory management choices. The discussion demonstrates careful consideration of tradeoffs between correctness patterns and allocation efficiency. The resolution to maintain the existing approach while dropping the BUG() check seems pragmatic given the analysis of real-world allocator behavior.
