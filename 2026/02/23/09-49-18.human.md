This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to change Git's default maintenance strategy from "gc" to "geometric". The patch itself is straightforward - a one-line change in `builtin/gc.c` to use the geometric strategy by default. 

The discussion with Derrick Stolee (who originally designed geometric repacking) confirms this is the right direction, with two documentation updates needed: clarifying that the incremental strategy is available via `git maintenance start` (not just Scalar), and updating `maintenance.adoc` to document the new default. 

The thread shows strong consensus on this change, which offers better scaling for large repositories while maintaining backward compatibility. The thorough preparation (7 preceding test infrastructure patches) and careful review by Stolee demonstrate this is ready to merge once the documentation updates are addressed. This represents a significant but well-considered shift in Git's maintenance behavior.
