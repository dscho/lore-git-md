This thread began with brian m. carlson identifying and fixing an infinite loop in `git fsck`'s packfile verification, where MRU list updates during iteration could cause the process to hang. The immediate fix (adding a `skip_mru_updates` flag) was approved by Junio for inclusion in the mainline. Jeff King then raised deeper concerns about the architectural fragility of the MRU list design, sparking discussion about potential long-term solutions.

Patrick Steinhardt has now weighed in, confirming the immediate fix works but uncovering two more subtle issues. First, he notes the test case's placement in t1050-large.sh isn't ideal since the bug requires both multiple packs and large objects to trigger. More significantly, he points out that the current verification approach may incorrectly check objects from the wrong pack when duplicates exist, as large objects bypass the direct offset-based verification path. Patrick hints at upcoming patches that will address these issues more fundamentally as part of his broader ODB abstraction work.

The thread now contains three layers: the immediate bugfix (ready for merging), test placement questions, and deeper architectural concerns about pack verification that connect to Patrick's ongoing object storage refactoring. While the first issue is resolved, the latter points suggest this area will see more changes as the ODB work progresses.
