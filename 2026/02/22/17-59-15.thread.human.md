Tian Yuchen continues refining their GSoC proposal to reduce Git's global state by removing `the_repository`, now in its third iteration after incorporating technical feedback from Karthik Nayak. The proposal has evolved to better align with Git's existing structures, particularly by leveraging `struct repo_settings` rather than introducing new containers as originally envisioned. 

In this latest update, Tian announces plans to test their refactoring approach on a simple subsystem before the GSoC coding period begins, demonstrating practical engagement with the review process. While Karthik remains the primary technical reviewer, the thread still awaits input from René Scharfe, who drives the broader `the_repository` removal effort. Tian's responsiveness to feedback and proactive validation approach shows promising development through the proposal iteration process, though key architectural decisions about global variable categorization remain pending. The proposal continues to demonstrate how Git's email-based mentorship model can effectively onboard new contributors to complex, long-term refactoring efforts.
