This is a discussion about a GSoC proposal to enhance the experimental `git repo info` command, which aims to modernize parts of `git rev-parse`. Lucas Oshiro, who worked on the initial implementation during GSoC 2025, is providing feedback to Jayatheerth Kulkarni's proposal for continuing the work.

Kulkarni's proposal outlines several ambitious goals: removing `the_repository` global variable usage (part of Git's ongoing libification effort), adding category-based queries for repository information, expanding path resolution features, and potentially integrating repository analysis from git-sizer. Oshiro cautions that the scope may be too broad given the typical review cycle pace, noting that his own initial GSoC work required 11 patch iterations before acceptance.

The exchange highlights the careful balance new contributors must strike between ambition and practicality when proposing substantial changes to Git. Oshiro's response suggests focusing the scope to ensure the work can be completed within the GSoC timeline while still making meaningful progress on the command's development. The thread demonstrates the project's mentorship culture, with prior GSoC participants guiding new applicants toward successful contributions.
