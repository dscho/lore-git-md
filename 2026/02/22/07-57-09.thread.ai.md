Thread summary:

GSoC proposal from Jayatheerth Kulkarni to enhance the experimental `git repo info` command as a modern replacement for parts of `git rev-parse`, building on Lucas Oshiro's GSoC 2025 work. The proposal focuses on: removing `the_repository` global variable in builtin/repo.c (part of Git's libification), implementing category-based queries ("paths", "core"), expanding path resolution (git-dir, common-dir, toplevel), and optional git-sizer integration. Lucas Oshiro (original implementer) responds with technical support but cautions about scope given mailing list review realities, noting his own experience requiring 11 iterations for acceptance. He suggests prioritizing given the 12-week GSoC timeframe while affirming the technical direction (global variable removal, category queries, path resolution) as valid. Key files impacted: builtin/repo.c (global state refactoring), path.c, setup.c (path features), with updates to t/ tests. The discussion highlights Git's iterative review process as a practical constraint for time-bound contributions, with Oshiro's implementer perspective adding weight to feasibility assessment. Open questions remain about phasing the work (core functionality vs git-sizer integration) and managing review cycles within the GSoC window.
