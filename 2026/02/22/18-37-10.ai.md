Bugfix targeting the fsck packfile verification process. This patch addresses an infinite loop that could occur when `git fsck` processes multiple packfiles, caused by the MRU (most recently used) list being updated during iteration and causing the pack traversal to revisit packs it had already seen. The fix introduces a `skip_mru_updates` flag that prevents the MRU list from being modified during fsck's pack verification pass. The patch includes a test case in t1050-large.sh that would trigger the infinite loop without this fix. The commit message explains the issue clearly and notes the test placement (though unusual) was chosen because it naturally reproduces the bug during development. The change is relatively small (18 lines net) and focused on the specific issue.
