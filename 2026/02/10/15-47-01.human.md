Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's consideration of whether Keller's idea could be implemented in a backwards-compatible way. The exchange shows the tension between security hardening and backwards compatibility that characterizes this long-running thread. While technical solutions are being explored (Steinhardt's `--accept-ambiguous-patch` flag, Keller's format changes), the maintainer is thinking about how to phase in protections without disrupting legitimate use cases.
