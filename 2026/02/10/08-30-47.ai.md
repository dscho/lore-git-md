This is a follow-up review comment on the UTF-8 support for Git config subsections, specifically addressing the alias implementation. Torsten Bögershausen suggests splitting the work into two logical parts: one for the subsection syntax itself (a general feature) and another for the UTF-8-specific handling (with platform considerations). 

The reviewer raises important platform-specific concerns about locale handling and Windows (mingw) compatibility that weren't fully addressed in the current implementation. While the core functionality has been validated by Jeff King and Junio Hamano, this email highlights that the UTF-8 aspects may need separate attention, particularly around:

1. Locale requirements - whether explicit locale setting is needed for consistent behavior
2. Windows command-line handling - why mingw platform is excluded and whether that exclusion is justified
3. Potential need for platform-specific testing or documentation about limitations

The patch remains fundamentally sound but this feedback indicates the UTF-8 support may need more platform-specific vetting before merging. The author will likely respond with either a justification for the current approach or a revised patch series that separates the concerns as suggested.
