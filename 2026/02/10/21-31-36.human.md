This is a refactoring patch as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. René Scharfe's patch removes the dependency on the global `the_repository` from `xdiff-interface.c` by refactoring `read_mmblob()` to take an explicit `struct object_database *odb` parameter instead. The change affects several callers across the codebase.

The patch has gone through two rounds of review, with Patrick Steinhardt initially suggesting a potential split but ultimately approving the combined approach. Junio Hamano has now marked it for inclusion in the 'next' integration branch after confirming all feedback was addressed, including removal of an unnecessary include file.

While the patch itself is ready, the discussion surfaced an additional improvement opportunity in `merge-ort.c` where callers should use `opt->repo->objects` rather than `the_repository->objects` to avoid reintroducing dependencies. This will be handled as a follow-up change. The patch exemplifies Git's incremental refactoring approach, where even small interface changes receive thorough cross-file review.
