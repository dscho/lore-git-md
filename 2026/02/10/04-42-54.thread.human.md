Derrick Stolee continues to refine his performance improvement work for `git config list --type=<X>` filtering, now at version 2 of his 13-patch series. The core goal is to make type filtering more efficient by skipping full parsing of non-matching values, particularly benefiting git-credential-manager's handling of path-type config values.

The series has evolved from its original 5-patch form through careful refactoring, with all major design questions now resolved. The current patch (5/13) introduces gentle parsing for boolean config values, following the established pattern from earlier patches. It moves boolean formatting into a new `format_config_bool()` helper that operates in both strict (error on invalid values) and gentle (skip invalid values) modes. This maintains consistency with the series' incremental approach while delivering the performance benefits of skipping irrelevant values during parsing.

Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have been closely involved throughout the review process. Patrick has approved the final implementation direction and suggested some final documentation tweaks, while Junio has engaged on both fundamental concerns and implementation details. The series appears ready for merging once these last documentation updates are complete, having demonstrated clear performance improvements while maintaining compatibility with existing behavior.
