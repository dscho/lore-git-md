This is Junio Hamano's biweekly "What's cooking in git.git" status report, providing updates on the state of various Git development branches and patches. The report covers topics that have graduated to 'master', new topics under consideration, and ongoing work in the 'seen' and 'next' branches.

- A new string_list_sort_u() function and related unit tests

- Improvements to "git replay" to drop commits that become empty

Ongoing work includes:
