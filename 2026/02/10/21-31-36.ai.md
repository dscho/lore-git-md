This is a refactoring patch as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. René Scharfe's patch removes the dependency on the global `the_repository` in `xdiff-interface.c` by refactoring `read_mmblob()` to take an explicit `struct object_database *odb` parameter instead. The change affects several callers across the codebase (`apply.c`, `checkout.c`, `merge-file.c`, `merge-ort.c`, and `notes-merge.c`).

The patch has gone through two rounds of review, with Patrick Steinhardt initially suggesting a potential split but ultimately approving the combined approach after René removed an unnecessary include. Junio Hamano endorses the minimal parameter design since `read_mmblob()` only needs object database access. Elijah Newren noted an additional improvement opportunity in `merge-ort.c` where the caller should use `opt->repo->objects` rather than `the_repository->objects`, though this is left as a follow-up.

The final version incorporates all feedback: it remains a single patch, eliminates the unnecessary include, maintains the minimal `odb` parameter design, and uses `is_null_oid()` consistently. With Patrick's final approval and all previous feedback addressed, Junio has marked the patch for inclusion in the 'next' integration branch. This exemplifies Git's incremental refactoring approach, where even small interface changes undergo thorough cross-file analysis.
