This is part 3/8 in a series enhancing Git's promisor remote functionality, focusing on a refactoring change in the clone command. The patch converts a static `filter_options` variable in `builtin/clone.c` from file-scoped to function-local within `cmd_clone()`, passing it explicitly to `checkout()`. This is a straightforward cleanup that makes the ownership and lifetime of the filter options clearer as the series adds more filter-related functionality.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial, following a pattern seen elsewhere in the codebase. It touches only the clone command's implementation, adjusting how filter options are passed internally without changing any user-visible behavior. The patch includes the necessary cleanup call to `list_objects_filter_release()` to maintain proper resource management.

This refactoring prepares for subsequent patches in the series that will build on the filter infrastructure, particularly the `--filter=auto` functionality. The change has already been approved by Patrick Steinhardt in previous iterations of the series.
