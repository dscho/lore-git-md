This is part 7/8 of a series enhancing Git's promisor remote functionality, focusing on storing and combining advertised filters in memory. The patch adds infrastructure to support the upcoming `--filter=auto` feature.

The key changes are:
1. Adding an `advertised_filter` field to `struct promisor_remote` to persist server-advertised filters beyond initial parsing
2. Modifying `filter_promisor_remote()` to capture and store these filters for accepted remotes
3. Introducing `promisor_remote_construct_filter()` to combine filters from all accepted remotes into a single filter spec

The implementation carefully handles memory management and error cases, with warnings for invalid filters. This builds on Patrick Steinhardt's earlier review of the series and prepares for the final patch that will wire up the `--filter=auto` functionality in clone/fetch operations.

The changes are focused on the promisor-remote subsystem, touching `promisor-remote.[ch]` to add the new capabilities while maintaining backward compatibility. The patch represents a significant step toward dynamic filter combination while keeping the security-conscious design established earlier in the series.
