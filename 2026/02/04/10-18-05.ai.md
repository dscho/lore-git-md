This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing the third patch in a series refactoring `wt-status.c`. The thread has progressed through three versions with reviews from Karthik Nayak, Junio C Hamano, and Phillip Wood.

The current discussion clarifies that while this patch series removes direct usage of `the_hash_algo` and `the_repository` from `wt-status.c`, it doesn't yet allow removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard. Shreyansh Paliwal explains that other globals (`core_apply_sparse_checkout`, `comment_line_str`, and `DEFAULT_ABBREV`) remain in use, with the first already being addressed in a separate series. Karthik Nayak agrees with the incremental approach and suggests documenting this limitation in the cover letter.

The exchange reveals two remaining technical challenges: how to handle `comment_line_str` and `DEFAULT_ABBREV` in future refactoring (which may require API changes), and whether to keep the `r` parameter in `wt_status_prepare()` now that repository access flows through `s->repo`. Karthik offers to assist with an RFC series for these remaining issues, maintaining the project's pattern of tackling global variable removal in manageable chunks. The thread demonstrates effective collaboration between contributor and reviewer to scope work appropriately within a large-scale refactoring effort.
