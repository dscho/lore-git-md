This is a response in the ongoing discussion about AI-assisted localization workflows for Git's translation process. Peter Krefting, the Swedish translation maintainer, strongly opposes the proposed AI tooling for l10n work.

The email is a direct rejection of Jiang Xin's RFC proposing AI assistance for .po file translations. Krefting requests that the Swedish localization be excluded from any AI integration, calling current machine translation tools "stochastic parrots" that produce low-quality output requiring more cleanup effort than manual translation. The tone is firm but professional, reflecting a clear position against the technology rather than the proposal's implementation details.

This marks the first substantive response to the RFC, establishing a clear point of contention around whether AI should be involved in Git's translation process at all. The objection comes from an experienced l10n maintainer (Krefting maintains the Swedish translations) and suggests the proposal may face resistance from parts of the translation team who prefer traditional human translation workflows. The response doesn't engage with the technical specifics of Jiang Xin's implementation but instead rejects the fundamental premise of using AI for this purpose.
