Adrian Ratiu's config-based hooks series (now at v4) has reached a stable point in its development. The series implements configuration-based hook management through new `hook.<friendly-name>.*` options while maintaining compatibility with traditional filesystem hooks. Key features include out-of-repo execution support, scope visibility for hook definitions, and the ability to mark hooks as disabled in configuration.

The technical work has received thorough review from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, with all major feedback addressed. Junio has now directed that the series should target the 'master' branch directly, since its prerequisite changes have already been in 'next' for several days. Adrian has acknowledged this guidance and will restructure the patches accordingly. This administrative adjustment doesn't change the series' technical content, which remains ready for integration after final documentation updates. The work represents a significant expansion of Git's hook system while carefully maintaining backward compatibility.
