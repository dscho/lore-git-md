Adrian Ratiu's config-based hooks series is now in its final stages, having successfully implemented configuration-based hook management while maintaining compatibility with traditional filesystem hooks. The series introduces new configuration options (`hook.<friendly-name>.command`, `hook.<friendly-name>.event`, `hook.<friendly-name>.enabled`) and adds a `git hook list` command to inspect configured hooks.

Key contributors Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have provided thorough reviews, with their suggestions (like adding a `-z` option for NUL-terminated output and showing hook scope information) incorporated into the series. The implementation now includes all core functionality: out-of-repo execution support, type-safe cache infrastructure, and the ability to view disabled hooks in the listing.

With the prerequisite work now in the 'next' branch, Junio has directed that the series should target 'master' directly. Adrian has confirmed he will restructure the patches accordingly. This is purely an administrative step in Git's standard contribution workflow - the technical aspects of the series are complete and approved. The thread shows consensus that the implementation is ready for integration, marking the successful conclusion of this multi-iteration effort to bring configuration-based hooks to Git.
