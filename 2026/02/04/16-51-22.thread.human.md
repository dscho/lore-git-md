Adrian Ratiu's config-based hooks series continues to refine its implementation details as it approaches finalization. The latest discussion focuses on a small but meaningful improvement to the validation logic for hook callback data allocation. Junio Hamano suggested simplifying the check that ensures allocation and free callbacks are either both provided or neither is provided, first proposing a boolean negation approach and then refining it further to a straightforward inequality check (`!A != !B`). Adrian promptly acknowledged the suggestion and will incorporate it into v3 of the series.

This exchange exemplifies the polishing phase the series is in - the core architecture and functionality for configurable hooks (including the `git hook list` subcommand and support for hooks defined via `hook.<name>.*` config options) has already received maintainer approval and substantial review from Patrick Steinhardt. The current discussion touches on just one of many small refinements being made before the series is ready for merging, following the prerequisite hooks-conversion series that is queued for the 'next' branch. With all major design questions resolved and only implementation details being fine-tuned, the series appears to be on track for inclusion once these final adjustments are complete.
