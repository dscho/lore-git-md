Adrian Ratiu's parallel hook execution series is nearing completion, with all core technical components receiving maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano. The series introduces comprehensive controls for parallel hook execution, including global and per-hook job settings, CLI overrides, and now a per-event master switch via `hook.<event>.enabled`.

This discussion reflects the series' careful attention to both technical correctness and user experience, even as it approaches the final polishing stage.

With all foundational patches acked and only this final user-interface consideration remaining, the series appears ready for integration once this last design question is resolved.
