This thread discusses Adrian Ratiu's patch series introducing parallel hook execution (currently at v4 with 9 patches). The series builds on his prior work making hooks configurable, adding multiple layers of control over parallel execution: global `hook.jobs` config, per-hook `hook.<name>.parallel` markers, CLI `-j/--jobs` overrides, and output stream handling.

Junio Hamano has already acked the foundational patches (1/9 fixing a memory leak and 3/9 introducing the global job count config). His recent feedback has focused on refining implementation details - in patch 4/9 he suggested improvements to type casting and job validation, and in patch 6/9 he clarified that all `-jN` values (not just `-j1`) should override configuration. He's also requested expanded documentation explaining why certain hooks like `pre-commit` can't safely run in parallel.

The technical approach remains solidly approved, with Junio's feedback now addressing edge cases in the interaction between configuration layers and improving long-term maintainability through clearer documentation. The series appears to be in its final polishing phase, with the maintainer's attention shifting from core functionality to ensuring consistent behavior and good explanations for future contributors.
