Jiang Xin, the Chinese translation maintainer, has proposed introducing AI-assisted workflows to streamline Git's localization (l10n) process. In an RFC to the l10n team, he's added two experimental commits to the git-po repository's next branch that enable AI tools to help with updating and quality-checking .po files. The proposal includes specific git commands to test the new workflows and suggests standard prompts for interacting with the AI tools—one for translation updates and another for reviews.

The changes focus on the translation infrastructure rather than the core Git codebase, and are aimed at Git's distributed team of translators (many of whom are CC'd on the email). While the email doesn't detail the specific AI implementation, this could represent a notable shift in how translations are managed if adopted. Jiang Xin is soliciting feedback from the l10n team before considering wider rollout.
