Jiang Xin's RFC series on AI-assisted Git localization workflows has reached its final form (v3), with all five patches now submitted. The series introduces optional AI tooling to improve translation efficiency while maintaining human oversight, with comprehensive documentation split between technical AI workflows (po/AGENTS.md) and traditional human processes (po/README.md). The implementation includes standardized .gitattributes filters, git-po-helper integration, and detailed workflows for both generating translation templates (Task 1) and updating language-specific PO files (Task 2).

The final patch adds a robust review system, featuring resumable batch processing, a quality scoring mechanism, and JSON specifications for review results. Benchmarks show significant efficiency gains - the Qwen model achieved a 96/100 score when processing 63 entries. While Junio Hamano appears satisfied with the technical implementation, brian m. carlson's legal concerns about AI-generated content and DCO compliance remain unresolved.
