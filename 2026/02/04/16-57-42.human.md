This is a refactoring patch series that's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting repository-specific configuration values. The series introduces a new `struct repo_config_values` to store configuration values that were previously global variables.

Phillip Wood provides a positive review of version 6 of the series, noting it looks "pretty good" with just a couple of remaining suggestions. The series has evolved through multiple iterations with feedback from both Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano.

The key changes in v6 include:
- Adding an accessor function `repo_config_values()` to safely access the new struct while detecting uninitialized repositories
- Renaming the struct member to `config_values_private_` to indicate encapsulation
- Adding initialization checks via a new `initialized` flag in `struct repository`

The series demonstrates the migration pattern through three concrete examples:
1. `core.attributesFile` (affecting attr.c and related files)
2. `core.sparseCheckout` (touching sparse-checkout, clone, and worktree code)
3. `branch.autoSetupMerge` (impacting branch, checkout, and push operations)

While the technical approach appears sound and the style refinements are being incorporated, broader architectural questions remain about repository initialization invariants and cross-repository configuration scoping. The series remains in RFC state pending resolution of these foundational issues.
