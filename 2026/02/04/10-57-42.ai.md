This is part 8/9 of the promisor remote enhancement series, focusing on storing server-side advertised filters in memory. The patch implements the infrastructure to capture and retain filter specifications advertised by promisor remotes during fetch operations, which will later be used by the `--filter=auto` functionality introduced in patch 6/9.

Christian Couder responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review feedback about naming the filter storage variable and whether to require filters. He explains that keeping the current "captured_filters" name (rather than switching to "advertised_filters") avoids confusion with the existing "advertised_filter" field, though suggests "server_filters" as a potential alternative. He also clarifies that servers may legitimately choose not to advertise filters, so a BUG() check would be inappropriate.

The technical implementation adds a `string_list` to store the captured filters during promisor remote processing. This builds on earlier work to handle promisor configuration and sets up the plumbing needed for the auto-filter combination logic that follows in the series. The discussion shows careful attention to naming clarity and backward compatibility considerations, with Patrick providing architectural guidance and Christian explaining the rationale behind certain design choices.
