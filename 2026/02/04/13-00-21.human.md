This is a discussion about AI-assisted translation workflows in Git's localization (l10n) process, specifically responding to Peter Krefting's strong opposition to using AI tools for Swedish translations. Michal Suchánek weighs in with a more nuanced perspective, acknowledging both the potential benefits and risks.

The email references a real-world case where Mozilla's Japanese community faced controversy after automatically applying AI translations to existing content. Michal suggests that while AI might help with abandoned or missing translations in general domains, Git's specialized terminology may make it a poor fit. He also raises concerns about maintainer workload if AI tools lead to an influx of low-quality submissions needing review.

The thread highlights a tension in the l10n community between those who see AI as a productivity aid and those who view it as a threat to translation quality. Michal's contribution adds practical considerations about domain-specific terminology and maintainer burden to the discussion, while still leaving room for AI use in limited contexts. The debate remains open, with no clear consensus yet from the broader l10n team.
