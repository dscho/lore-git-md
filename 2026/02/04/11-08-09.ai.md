This is part 4/8 of a series refactoring filter options handling in Git's promisor remote functionality. The patch converts the static `filter_options` variable in `builtin/fetch.c` to a local variable in `cmd_fetch()`, passing it explicitly through the call chain.

The change follows the same pattern as patch 3/8's refactoring of `builtin/clone.c` (which Patrick Steinhardt approved in v1). The key modifications are:
- Moving the `filter_options` definition from file scope into `cmd_fetch()`
- Adding it as a parameter to `prepare_transport()`, `do_fetch()`, `backfill_tags()`, `fetch_one_setup_partial()`, and `fetch_one()`
- Ensuring proper cleanup with `list_objects_filter_release()`

The refactoring maintains all existing behavior while making the ownership and lifetime of the filter options clearer. This prepares for subsequent patches in the series that will build on this infrastructure to implement `--filter=auto` functionality. The change is mechanical but important for the larger goal of improving promisor remote handling.
