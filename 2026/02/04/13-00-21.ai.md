This is a discussion about AI-assisted translation workflows in Git's localization (l10n) process, specifically responding to Peter Krefting's strong opposition to using AI tools for Swedish translations.

Michal Suchánek weighs in with a more nuanced perspective, acknowledging both the potential benefits and risks of AI-assisted translation. They reference a similar controversy in Mozilla's Japanese SUMO community where automatic AI application to existing translations caused problems. Michal suggests that while abandoned or missing translations might benefit from AI assistance (particularly for general topics), Git's specialized terminology may not be well-suited for current translation models. They also note the practical concern that making AI tools available could lead to an influx of low-quality submissions that maintainers would need to review.

The email situates this within the broader RFC discussion started by Jiang Xin about introducing AI agents to help with .po file updates and reviews. While Peter Krefting has taken a hard stance against any AI involvement for Swedish translations, Michal's response represents a more measured view that considers specific use cases where AI might be helpful while recognizing the limitations and potential downsides. The thread remains open with no clear consensus yet from the broader l10n team.
