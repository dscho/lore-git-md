The patch modifies two key parts of the verification logic: it now accepts both `GOODSIG` and `EXPKEYSIG` status lines from GnuPG, and recognizes the 'Y' (expired key) result code alongside 'G' (good signature). The author cites real-world motivation from Linux kernel commit signing practices and references prior discussion with Linus Torvalds about this discrepancy.

The technical change is straightforward - just two condition modifications - but touches security-sensitive signature verification code.
