This is part 8 of a 9-patch series enhancing Git's promisor remote functionality, focusing on storing server-advertised filters in memory. Christian Couder responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review feedback on naming and error handling.

The patch introduces infrastructure to track filters advertised by promisor remotes, which will be used by the new `--filter=auto` functionality in later patches. Christian addresses Patrick's suggestion to rename the `captured_filters` variable, proposing `server_filters` as an alternative that avoids confusion with the existing `advertised_filter` field. He also explains why a `BUG()` check isn't appropriate for missing advertised filters, as servers may legitimately choose not to advertise any filters.

The discussion shows careful attention to naming clarity while maintaining flexibility in the protocol design. This is a technical implementation patch that will enable the auto-filter behavior introduced in patch 6/9, with Patrick providing thorough review and Christian incorporating feedback while pushing back where the design requires flexibility.
