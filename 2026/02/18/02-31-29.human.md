This is a bugfix series for the contrib/subtree component, addressing issues with the subtree split operation's history handling. The series removes a problematic optimization that was incorrectly excluding commits from split history, which had led to regressions in previous fix attempts.

The 3-patch series comes from Colin Stagner and represents a complete solution after earlier attempts (in commits 98ba49ccc2 and 83f9dad7d6) introduced their own issues. The approach here is to remove the optimization entirely rather than trying to fix it, while significantly expanding test coverage to prevent future regressions. The series has been reviewed by subtree maintainer D. Ben Knoble, with only minor formatting feedback remaining.

Patch 1 adds test infrastructure, patch 2 expands test coverage in t7900-subtree.sh with explicit commit count verification, and patch 3 removes the problematic `should_ignore_subtree_split_commit` function entirely. The changes focus on ensuring correct history preservation during subtree splits, particularly for cases involving multiple or nested subtrees and rebased commits.

The series appears well-constructed with thorough test coverage (+268 lines of tests) and clear commit messages. It addresses issues originally reported by George and Christian Heusel, and represents part of ongoing subtree stabilization efforts.
