This is part 1 of a 5-patch series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate this global variable. The patch comes from Elijah Newren, the original designer of Git's "ort" merge strategy, so the changes can be considered authoritative for this subsystem.

The patch modifies `write_tree()` in merge-ort.c to take an explicit `struct repository*` parameter instead of implicitly using `the_repository`. It also simplifies the function signature by having it look up the hash algorithm internally rather than requiring callers to pass the hash size. The changes are mechanical but well-contained, touching only merge-ort.c and following established patterns seen in similar conversions elsewhere in the codebase.

This is a straightforward technical cleanup with no visible behavior change. The patch appears ready for review, though it will likely be considered alongside the rest of the series rather than in isolation. Given Elijah's deep expertise in this area and the non-controversial nature of the changes, the series seems likely to progress smoothly through review.
