This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing test failures related to `iconv(1)` availability on Windows/MSVC platforms. The patch focuses on `t6006-rev-list-format.sh`, making two tests conditionally use `iconv(1)` only when the ICONV prerequisite is met, falling back to UTF-8 encoding otherwise.

Junio Hamano's review confirms the approach is sound, noting the clever use of `test_encoding` being set to UTF-8 when ICONV is unavailable. The patch maintains test coverage while properly handling environments where the `iconv(1)` executable isn't present, particularly relevant for Windows builds where MinGW no longer ships it. 

The changes follow the pattern established in previous patches of the series: wrapping `iconv` usage in `test_have_prereq` checks and ensuring consistent behavior across platforms. Junio points out the slight oddity of still labeling files as ISO8859-1 when they may contain UTF-8, but acknowledges this doesn't affect test validity. With this final patch, the series appears ready for merging, having addressed all review feedback through three iterations.
