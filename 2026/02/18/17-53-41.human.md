The changes are mechanical but thorough, updating 12 locations where `the_repository` was being accessed unnecessarily. The commit message explains the safety of these changes, noting that `s->repo` is guaranteed to be initialized when these functions are called.

This continues the project's incremental approach to removing global state, with each patch making focused, verifiable changes.
