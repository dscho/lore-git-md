This is a meta-comment about project conventions rather than a technical review of the patch itself. The suggestion comes late in the thread - the patch series is already in its sixth iteration and has maintainer approval, with all technical issues resolved.

The core UTF-8 alias support work remains unaffected by this documentation-style suggestion. The thread shows the project's attention to both technical correctness and communication clarity, even for changes that are unlikely to affect users in practice.
