[PATCH 0/5] Remove `the_repository` from merge-ort and replay
Author: Elijah Newren
Thread type: Patch series (v1 -> completed)
Subsystems: merge-ort, replay
Related effort: `the_repository` removal (ongoing project)

This series systematically eliminated direct uses of the `the_repository` global variable and `the_hash_algo` from merge-ort.c and replay.c, replacing them with repository-specific parameters available via `opt->repo`. The changes are part of the broader project-wide cleanup to remove implicit repository dependencies.

Patches 1-3 converted `write_tree()` and other functions in merge-ort.c to take explicit `struct repository *` parameters, replacing most `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` uses with `opt->repo` equivalents. Patch 4 introduced compile-time enforcement against `the_repository` usage in merge-ort.c via macro redefinition, with a temporary exception for `prefetch_for_content_merges()` that was isolated between `#undef`/`#redefine` directives.

The final patch (5/5) completes the series by applying the same compile-time prevention mechanism to replay.c, ensuring any accidental reintroduction of `the_repository` will fail to compile. This defensive measure maintains the repository parameterization work despite `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` (required for `DEFAULT_ABBREV`) keeping the global technically accessible. The change follows patterns established in earlier patches and across the codebase by René Scharfe.

All changes are mechanical, behavior-preserving replacements that maintain correct merge-ort functionality while advancing the subsystem's alignment with the project's repository parameterization goals. Elijah's deep knowledge of merge-ort (which he originally designed) ensured technical soundness throughout. The series achieved its original scope without expansion or controversy, following established practices for global variable elimination. With all five now submitted, the series appears ready for merging.
