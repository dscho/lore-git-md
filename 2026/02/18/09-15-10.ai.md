This is a cover letter for a 5-part series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, targeting the merge-ort and replay subsystems. The series comes from Elijah Newren, the primary expert on Git's merge machinery.

The patches systematically eliminate direct uses of the `the_repository` global variable and `the_hash_algo` from merge-ort.c, replacing them with repository-specific parameters already available in the code (via `opt->repo`). The first three patches perform the mechanical conversions, while the last two add preventative measure - likely compiler warning flags or similar - to block future regressions.

The motivation references two recent mailing list discussions about removing these globals from merge-related code. As part of a well-established project-wide cleanup effort (with René Scharfe as a frequent contributor), this series follows a familiar pattern seen across many subsystems. Given Elijah's deep knowledge of merge-ort (he designed and implemented the "ort" strategy), these changes can be expected to be technically sound.

The series appears focused and well-structured, with the first three patches doing the actual conversion work and the last two adding guardrails. The changes are confined to merge-ort.c and replay.c, touching about 100 lines total with a roughly even split between additions and deletions. This looks like a straightforward, uncontroversial contribution to the broader `the_repository` elimination project.
