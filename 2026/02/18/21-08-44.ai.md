The patch continues the thread's theme of systematic documentation improvements while maintaining the zero-behavior-change approach of the earlier patches. With this change, the series has now addressed naming conventions (patches 1-4), test infrastructure (5-6), and documentation formatting (7-8) across the repo-info subsystem.
