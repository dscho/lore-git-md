This is the third and final patch in a bugfix series for the `contrib/subtree` component, addressing issues with the subtree split operation's history handling. The patch removes a problematic optimization function (`should_ignore_subtree_split_commit`) that was incorrectly excluding commits from split history, violating API contracts around commit hashes.

The change comes after thorough test coverage was added in the first two patches of the series, which verify commit counts and branch comparisons to ensure history preservation works correctly. Maintainer D. Ben Knoble has reviewed the series and provided minor feedback about trailer formatting (specifically that "git interpret-trailers" doesn't understand line-separated trailers), which the author plans to address in v2.

The fix builds on prior work that introduced and then attempted to correct this optimization (commits 98ba49ccc2 and 83f9dad7d6), and addresses issues originally reported by George and Christian Heusel. The series represents a complete solution to the subtree split history problems, with the maintainer indicating the technical approach is sound pending the formatting fix.
