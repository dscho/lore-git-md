Olamide Bello chimes in to confirm they've been working on a similar approach using the `repo_config_values` structure rather than lazy loading, and plans to continue migrating other repository-specific configuration variables once those patches are accepted. This reinforces Junio's point about preferring early validation while still achieving the architectural goal of removing globals.

The discussion highlights a recurring tension in the `the_repository` removal effort between architectural purity (lazy loading) and user experience (early validation). The participants are converging on a solution that maintains Git's traditional behavior of validating configuration values promptly while still moving away from global state.
