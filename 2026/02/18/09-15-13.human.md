This is part 3 of Elijah Newren's series to remove `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` from merge-ort and replay, continuing the project-wide effort to eliminate implicit repository dependencies. The patch mechanically converts all remaining uses of `the_hash_algo` in merge-ort.c to instead use `opt->repo->hash_algo`, affecting about a dozen locations where null object IDs are created or copied.

The changes are straightforward replacements that maintain identical behavior while making the repository dependency explicit. The patch touches various merge scenarios including submodule handling, content merges, directory renames, and conflict resolution - all areas where Elijah has deep expertise as the original designer of the "ort" merge strategy. 

This continues the pattern established in earlier patches of the series, with no new technical challenges introduced. The changes are non-controversial and align with similar conversions done by other contributors like René Scharfe in other parts of the codebase. The patch appears ready for review and merging as part of the ongoing cleanup effort.
