This is a follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing the migration of the `core.trustctime` configuration from a global variable to repository-specific storage.

The exchange highlights a recurring tension in this migration effort between clean architectural patterns (like lazy loading) and preserving immediate user feedback.
