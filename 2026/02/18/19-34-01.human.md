Performance optimization for shallow pushes in `git receive-pack`. This is the third and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series that systematically optimizes commit lookup performance during shallow pushes. The patch modifies `lookup_commit_reference_gently()` to use the commit-graph for parsing commits, building on the previous two patches that first optimized non-commit object lookups and then fixed commit-graph edge cases.

The change shows dramatic improvements: a 3.93x speedup (from 9.179s to 2.337s) and significant memory reduction (407MB to 15.5MB) when pushing to a repository with 600,000 commit references. Justin Tobler has reviewed the patch and confirms it looks good, completing the review cycle for this series. The optimization is well-motivated and carefully implemented, with each patch in the series building on the last to safely improve performance.
