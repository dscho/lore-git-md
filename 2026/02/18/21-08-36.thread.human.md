The author, Lucas Seiki Oshiro, has acknowledged this and plans to incorporate it in the next version. Patch 6 modernizes NUL-formatted output testing - initially the rationale wasn't clear, but Lucas has now explained the key benefit is more readable test failure output (avoiding binary diffs), a point previously raised by Junio Hamano. While Patrick Steinhardt questions whether the technical approach is clearly superior, the human-facing improvement seems likely to carry the day once better documented.

The other patches are straightforward mechanical changes: renaming types and functions for clarity (`repo_info_fields`→`repo_info_field`), standardizing documentation formatting (using `_NUL_`), and aligning test file names with their purpose (`t1900-repo.sh`→`t1900-repo-info.sh`). The series shows a clear pattern of establishing principles, applying them systematically, and incorporating review feedback - all without changing behavior. With the array naming guideline refined and the test modernization rationale clarified, the series appears ready to progress once these updates are made.
