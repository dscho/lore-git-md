The changes touching about 100 lines total, this is a focused contribution to the long-running cleanup effort that René Scharfe and others have been driving across the codebase. Given Elijah's deep expertise in merge machinery and the straightforward nature of these changes, the series appears uncontroversial and well-positioned for review.
