This is part 1 of a 5-patch series removing `the_repository` usage from merge-ort and replay, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate implicit repository dependencies. The patch focuses on the `write_tree()` function in merge-ort.c.

The change makes `write_tree()` take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. This allows removing the redundant passing of `hash_algo->rawsz` since the function can now access that directly from the repository parameter. The patch updates all three call sites to pass `opt->repo` through to `write_tree()`.

The modifications are mechanical and follow established patterns seen in other parts of the codebase undergoing similar conversions. The diff shows balanced additions and deletions (8 lines each) with straightforward parameter adjustments. Given Elijah Newren's role as the original author of merge-ort and his deep familiarity with this code, the technical approach appears sound.

This patch lays groundwork for the rest of the series by establishing the pattern of passing repository context explicitly through the merge-ort call stack. The changes are localized to internal implementation details with no user-visible effects.
