This is a performance optimization for `git rev-list` and related commands, adding a `--maximal-only` option to show only commits that are not reachable by other commits (i.e., the maximal elements in the commit graph). The patch comes from Derrick Stolee, who has deep expertise in Git's graph algorithms and performance optimizations.

The option is designed to help with cases where users want to identify "root" commits or the most recent commits in disconnected subgraphs, without having to filter through all reachable commits. This could be particularly useful for repository analysis tools or when working with complex histories containing multiple roots or disconnected development lines.

The patch has been stable in testing for several weeks according to Stolee, who is recommending it be moved to the 'next' integration branch. Given Stolee's track record with graph-related optimizations and the straightforward nature of this change (adding a new option with clear semantics), this appears to be a low-risk addition that could provide meaningful performance benefits for certain query patterns.
