Documentation patch series review. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" report regarding Jean-Noël Avila's documentation conversion series, indicating the patches converting `git-show`, `git-clone`, and `git-submodule` to synopsis style are ready for merging. The series is part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git's man page formatting using AsciiDoc markup, with Jean-Noël Avila as the primary driver of this multi-year documentation modernization project. Haugsbakk's brief reply suggests no objections to the changes and implicitly endorses their inclusion in the 'next' branch, though without providing detailed review commentary on the specific documentation changes made.
