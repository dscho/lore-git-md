The series has been split into three logical commits for clarity: first the refactoring, then an explicit compatibility-breaking change, and finally the main feature implementation.

The preparatory refactoring in this latest email makes the alias parsing more strict by using `parse_config_key()` instead of `skip_prefix()`, fixing what was arguably a bug in how dotted aliases were previously handled.
