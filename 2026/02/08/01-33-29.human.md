This is a bash completion patch for the `git stash` subcommands, specifically adding support for the relatively new `import` and `export` commands. The patch comes from D. Ben Knoble, who is known for his work on Git's build system and tooling.

Junio Hamano (the Git maintainer) has reviewed and accepted the patch, noting that while this manual completion update makes sense for now, there's a longer-term opportunity to automate completion generation from the parse-options infrastructure. The patch itself is straightforward - it adds the new subcommands to the completion list and implements appropriate completions for their options (`--print` and `--to-ref` for `export`) and arguments (refname completion for `import`).

The patch appears well-scoped and uncontroversial, building logically on existing completion patterns for other stash subcommands. Junio's quick acceptance suggests this is a clear improvement that fits cleanly into the current completion system while the larger automation effort remains future work.
