Performance optimization follow-up with benchmark data for the priority queue change in `limit_list()`. René Scharfe provides concrete measurements showing consistent 1.16-1.28x speedups across Git's own version ranges (v2.0.1 to v2.10.1 through v2.50.1) when using `git rev-list --bisect`. While these don't demonstrate the worst-case quadratic behavior the patch aims to address, they show meaningful improvements even in Git's relatively linear history.

The benchmarks validate Derrick Stolee's earlier suggestion to test against Git's own version ranges, though the results are more modest than the 4.3x speedups seen in synthetic merge-heavy cases from the original series. This suggests the optimization provides broad benefits beyond just pathological cases, with the largest gains likely appearing in repositories with complex merge histories. The data strengthens the case for merging the optimization while setting realistic expectations for typical repositories.
