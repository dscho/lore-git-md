This is a follow-up to the performance optimization series for `limit_list()` that replaces a linked list with a priority queue. The author, Kristofer Karlsson, reports additional success applying the same technique to `get_revision_1()`, achieving a 3.3x speedup for `git rev-list HEAD` in a monorepo. This would also simplify `process_parents` and make `commit_list_insert_by_date` obsolete.

The email raises a question about next steps - whether to start a new thread for this extension or revisit it later, noting they already have several optimization patches in progress. This comes after Derrick Stolee had previously acknowledged the benchmark results and waived the perf test requirement, effectively clearing the path for the original patch series.

The proposed change introduces some complexity - `rev_info` would need to maintain both a priority queue and a linked list of commits, with data being flushed between them during execution. The author is conscious of not overloading the current patch and seeks guidance on how to proceed with this natural extension of the optimization work.
