Git Merge 2026 has been announced for September 17-18 in Lisbon, Portugal, with JJ Day taking place on September 16. Early-bird ticket pricing is $99 for the main conference, with combination and JJ-only options available. Git and JJ contributors can receive free registration by contacting Taylor Blau off-list; Git contributors additionally gain access to the Contributor's Summit on the 18th. The Call for Proposals is open through June 30, and travel assistance requests should be sent to the Project Leadership Committee by May 15.
