This is a maintainer comment on the interface design for the `git add -p` word-diff feature. Junio C Hamano responds to D. Ben Knoble's suggestion of a more extensible `:show <opts>` command syntax (inspired by Vim) for viewing hunks with different diff options.

Junio acknowledges the merit of avoiding single-letter command proliferation, noting he had a similar thought about reusing 'p' with options. However, he raises a practical concern about usability for those who prefer single-key interactions (though admitting he personally doesn't fall into that category). The comment keeps the discussion grounded while gently questioning whether the more flexible interface might complicate the common case of just wanting word-diff.

This maintains the thread's focus on balancing extensibility against simplicity, with the maintainer weighing in on a key design tradeoff.
