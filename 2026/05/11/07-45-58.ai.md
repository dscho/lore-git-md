This is a follow-up discussion on the `git fetch --deepen` bugfix patch, focusing on whether to warn users when `--deepen` is used on non-shallow repositories. René Scharfe responds to Junio C Hamano's suggestion about adding a warning message.

René points out that `--deepen` has silently been a no-op on non-shallow repositories since its introduction in 2016 (commit cccf74e2da). He suggests that if a warning were to be added, it should be implemented in the `fetch` command itself rather than in the lower-level shallow handling code, and that such a change would warrant its own separate patch.

The technical fix for the regression (preventing `--deepen` from incorrectly converting full clones to shallow ones) remains unchanged and appears settled. This discussion now centers on the user experience question of whether to maintain the current silent behavior or add a warning, and where such messaging should live in the codebase. René's response indicates he views this as a separate concern from the core bugfix.
