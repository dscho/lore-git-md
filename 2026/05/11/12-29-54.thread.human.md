Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano have finalized the inclusion of two maintenance subsystem fixes in the upcoming Git 2.54.1 bugfix release. The patches address critical issues: one prevents stale lockfiles from blocking subsequent maintenance runs in detached mode, while the other properly restores the expected behavior of `gc.auto` as a fallback configuration when `maintenance.auto` is unset. 

The series had already received thorough technical review from Jeff King and Taylor Blau, with Junio now confirming the patches meet the criteria for merging to `next` (the integration branch for bugfix releases). This concludes the discussion, with no further technical questions remaining. The fixes will ensure reliable maintenance operation while maintaining backward compatibility for users who rely on `gc.auto=0` to disable automatic maintenance.
