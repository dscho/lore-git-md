Patrick Steinhardt posted a two-patch series fixing two bugs in the maintenance subsystem: lockfile cleanup when Git runs in `--detach` mode, and a regression where `gc.auto` stopped being honored after the transition to `git maintenance`. The series targets Git 2.54.0 and touches `builtin/gc.c`, `run-command.c`, and the lockfile API, with 95 lines of new tests.

Jeff King (Peff) has reviewed Patch 2 and identified a real correctness bug: the patch reads `gc.auto` with `repo_config_get_bool()`, but `gc.auto` is an integer option that sets a loose-object count threshold -- not a boolean. While `gc.auto=0` happens to work the same way in both parsers, a value like `gc.auto=100` would produce different results under the boolean path, making the approach fragile. The fix is straightforward (switch to `repo_config_get_int()` and check for zero), but this is a substantive correctness issue that will almost certainly require a v2. No other reviews have been posted.
