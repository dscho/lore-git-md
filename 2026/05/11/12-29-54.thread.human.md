This thread addresses two maintenance subsystem bugs that are now ready for merging. The first fixes a lockfile issue in `git maintenance run --detach` where stale locks could block subsequent runs - the solution moves lockfile ownership transfer into `daemonize()` itself, a design change Patrick Steinhardt adopted from Taylor Blau's suggestion. The second patch restores expected behavior for `gc.auto=0` configurations that was broken when maintenance stopped shelling out to `git gc`.

All technical discussion has concluded with consensus. Junio C Hamano and Taylor Blau have both reviewed the latest version and agree the approach is sound. While waiting for Jeff King's final sign-off on the unchanged second patch (a formality given his prior approval), Taylor notes these fixes are important enough to consider for a maintenance release due to the operational impact of the lockfile issue. The series will likely be merged into `next` soon.
