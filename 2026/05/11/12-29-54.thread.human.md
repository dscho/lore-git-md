Taylor Blau has weighed in on patch 1 of the v2 series with a substantive review that reopens the design of the lockfile ownership transfer in detached mode.

The v2 patch introduced a new dedicated function, `daemonize_without_exit()`, to handle the lockfile transfer at the single call site in `builtin/gc.c`. Taylor instead wants the transfer baked into the existing `daemonize()` function itself, arguing that every future daemonized caller will need this behavior and that the per-call-site approach sets up a fragile, accident-prone pattern. They feel "fairly strongly" about this and offer to add their Co-authored-by and Signed-off-by if Patrick adopts the approach (it would match the C changes Taylor already sketched off-list).

The rest of the review is positive: Taylor calls the FIFO-based test "much more robust" than what they had come up with, and says the rest of the test "looks good to me."

Patch 2 stands as-is (no new review comments on that part). The thread is no longer fully settled: a design discussion is now open on patch 1. No decision has been announced on which approach will prevail.
