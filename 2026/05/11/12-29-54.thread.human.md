Patrick Steinhardt's maintenance mode bugfix series will be included in the upcoming Git 2.54.1 release, as confirmed by maintainer Junio Hamano. The two patches address important issues: one fixes a lockfile handling problem in detached maintenance mode that could block subsequent runs, while the other restores the expected behavior of `gc.auto=0` to disable automatic maintenance (a regression from the transition to the newer `git maintenance` subsystem).

The fixes had already received thorough review and approval from Jeff King and Taylor Blau in earlier iterations. Junio's confirmation that they meet the criteria for inclusion in the bugfix release represents the final step in the process. The technical work is complete, with both patches merged into the `next` branch, and now they will flow into the maintenance release to address these issues for users.
