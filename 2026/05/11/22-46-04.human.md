This is a thread-closing reply from Thorsten Glaser, the original proponent of the `core.shell` feature request, in response to Brian m. carlson's detailed rebuttal.

Thorsten essentially concedes the point. After reviewing his own aliases, he found that the ones using mksh-specific features already invoked `mksh -c '…'` explicitly, and the alias that actually broke on a customer system was not a shell-compatibility problem at all -- it just used a recent POSIX construct that the customer's `/bin/sh` didn't implement yet. He says he can reword that alias instead of asking Git to support a different shell.

So the thread dies here. The `core.shell` feature request is effectively abandoned by its own author, who discovered that the problem was not a Git limitation but a fixable choice in how he wrote his aliases. Nothing left to discuss.
