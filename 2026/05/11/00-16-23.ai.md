Maintainer comment on `git add -p` word-diff interface design.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about adding word-diff functionality to `git add -p`, responding to D. Ben Knoble's proposal for a more extensible `:show <opts>` command syntax. The maintainer acknowledges the tension between single-letter mnemonics (like the proposed 'w' for word-diff) and more flexible command-based interfaces, noting he had considered reusing the existing 'p' command with options rather than introducing new single-letter commands. However, he raises the practical concern about usability for those who rely on single-key operations, showing awareness of different user workflows even while admitting his own preference for more verbose interfaces.

This brief but pointed comment from the maintainer suggests the interface design question remains open, with tradeoffs between extensibility and usability still to be resolved. Junio's mention of having "less sympathy than I should" for single-key users hints at his personal bias while acknowledging the need to consider diverse user preferences. The email doesn't take a firm position but rather keeps the discussion alive, inviting further consideration of how to balance these competing concerns in the final implementation.
