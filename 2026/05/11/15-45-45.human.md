This is the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's v5 series introducing the new `git format-rev` command. It's a straightforward style cleanup patch that brings two conditional blocks in `builtin/name-rev.c` into compliance with Git's coding guidelines.

The changes:
1. Adds braces around both branches of an `if/else` block in `get_rev_name()`
2. Similarly adds braces around an `if/else if` chain in `name_rev_line()`

This is purely mechanical work - no behavior changes - preparing the codebase for subsequent patches in the series. The patch follows Git's convention of including braces even for single-line blocks when they're part of a multi-arm conditional, as specified in `CodingGuidelines`.

The patch is clean and focused, exactly what you'd want from a preparatory change. It's already been through several iterations (note the v2 note about fixing stray formatting) and appears ready for inclusion.
