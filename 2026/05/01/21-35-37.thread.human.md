Harald will address these in a v23 iteration of this patch, but the rest of the series (patches 2-7) is unaffected and ready for merging. Junio has already confirmed the series is "pretty much done," and it will appear in an upcoming "What's cooking" report.

Key participants include Harald Nordgren (author), Phillip Wood (who provided high-weight feedback on safety and API design, including the stacked-branch protection refactor), and Junio C Hamano (maintainer, who endorsed the design and resolved his earlier correctness concerns).
