Harald Nordgren sent a follow-up to his `git branch --prune-merged` series (v8) that opens a separate discussion thread rather than replying in the existing one. The big news for the main series is that he agrees with Junio C Hamano's objection to `--force` bypassing the mergedness check and will remove that behavior in v9, removing the last roadblock to merging.

Separately, Harald floated a new concept: a `fetch.pruneLocalBranches` config option that would automatically delete local branches during `git fetch` when their upstream counterpart has been removed. This is a fundamentally different proposition from the explicit `git branch --prune-merged` command -- it would be automatic, triggered by fetch, and raises a whole new set of safety questions. There are no details yet on what guardrails it would have, how it would interact with `fetch.prune`, or whether per-branch opt-out would be supported. This is just a preliminary idea, not a patch, and has not been discussed by anyone else yet.

In short: the `--prune-merged` series is essentially ready to land after the `--force` fix in v9, while the `fetch.pruneLocalBranches` idea is a separate, nascent topic that may or may not go anywhere.
