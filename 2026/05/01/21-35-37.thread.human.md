This thread covers Harald Nordgren's series adding `git branch --prune-merged`, a safe mechanism for cleaning up local branches whose upstream counterparts have been deleted. The feature ensures branches are only removed if their tips are reachable from their upstream remote-tracking branches, with additional safeguards against deleting checked-out branches or default branches with matching names.

The series went through nine iterations, with Junio C Hamano approving the safety model in v8 but objecting to allowing `--force` to bypass the mergedness check. Harald addressed this in v9 by removing the `--force` override entirely, making the feature more conservative. The final version includes commands to list forked branches (`--forked`), prune merged branches (`--prune-merged`), a per-branch opt-out config (`branch.<name>.pruneMerged`), and a convenience flag for all remotes (`--all-remotes`).

With all technical concerns resolved and no outstanding objections, the series appears ready for integration. Harald's final email notes the removal of `--force` makes the feature "less of a foot-gun" while acknowledging some may find it less flexible. A separate proposal for `fetch.pruneLocalBranches` remains unattended and is not part of this work.
