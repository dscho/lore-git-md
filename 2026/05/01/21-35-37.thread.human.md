# Thread Summary (Human Mode)

Harald Nordgren has sent v9 of his five-patch series adding `git branch --prune-merged` for safe automated cleanup of local branches whose upstream tracking branches have been deleted. The series introduces `--forked <remote>` (list branches tracking a given remote), `--prune-merged` (delete local branches reachable from their configured upstream), a `branch.<name>.pruneMerged` config option for per-branch opt-out, and `--all-remotes` as a convenience flag. The core safety model -- a branch is deletable only if its tip is reachable from its upstream, it is not checked out, its upstream resolves, and it does not share a name with the remote's default branch -- was already approved by Junio C Hamano in v8.

Junio's sole remaining objection was that `--force` should not bypass the mergedness check. v9 patches 3-5 address this by dropping `--force` from the synopsis, making the reachability check always enforced, and updating tests accordingly. Patch 2, a pure refactoring (adding `warn_only` and `n_not_merged` parameters to internal deletion functions for future bulk use), is unchanged from v8 and mechanically correct. Patches 1 and 4-5 are similarly clean adjustments.

The series is substantively ready and awaiting Junio's queueing. The separate `fetch.pruneLocalBranches` idea that Harald once floated remains a preliminary proposal with no discussion.
