This is a refactoring patch that renames a worktree-related function as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

Phillip Wood renames `get_worktree_from_repository()` to `get_current_worktree()` to better reflect its actual behavior. The function always returns the current worktree since commit 0f779147602, and the new name makes this clearer. The change was suggested by Junio Hamano.

The patch is straightforward - it updates the function declaration and its two call sites in `worktree.c` and `wt-status.c`. This appears to be a mechanical cleanup with no behavior changes, typical of the kind of work being done to remove reliance on global state in the codebase. The small size and focused nature of the change suggest it should be uncontroversial.
