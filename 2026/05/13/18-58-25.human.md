Bugfix follow-up for the dumb HTTP walker's alternate-object-store URL parsing. This is a reply from Peff to Junio's earlier greenlight on the patch.

Peff agrees with Junio's sentiment that the dumb HTTP walker is a continuing source of bugs and vulnerabilities and would be better off gone. He mentions that a few years ago the idea of dropping it was discussed, but Eric Sunshine (the reviewer) pointed out that dumb clones are easier on the server for cases like gigantic public-inbox repos that keep most of their old history in one large pack. Peff notes that the verbatim pack-reuse feature tries to get smart clones closer to that performance, but says he hasn't measured recently.

The content is entirely conversational -- no new technical information about the patch, no code-level discussion, and no change to the earlier acceptance. It is a brief exchange between the author and maintainer agreeing that the subsystem is a problem, while acknowledging why it still exists.
