This is a follow-up review from Jeff King (Peff) responding to René Scharfe's patch that added `strbuf_add_oid_hex()`.

Peff confirms the patch's motivation and provides his own benchmark: `git cat-file --batch-all-objects --batch-check='%(objectname)'` showed approximately a 3% speedup, though he notes this is within run-to-run noise. The review is brief and entirely supportive.

The key line comes at the end: Peff says he thinks this is worth doing "solely for removing more instances of global buffers" -- meaning the thread-safety improvement is a sufficient reason by itself, even if the performance numbers are marginal. This is an endorsement from one of the project's most respected voices on both correctness and performance matters.

The review is substantive in that it provides an additional, more extreme benchmark, but it is brief and contains no requests for changes, no concerns, and no suggestions. This is effectively a "looks good" from Peff with a data point to support the author's claim. The patch remains on track to be uncontroversial and likely to land quickly.
