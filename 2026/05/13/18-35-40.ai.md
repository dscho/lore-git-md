This is a reply from Derrick Stolee to Taylor Blau's review question on patch 07/12 of the `--path-walk` and `--filter` interaction series (v3). Stolee is the author; the email addresses Blau's single substantive question from the previous round.

Stolee addresses whether calls to `odb_read_object_info()` during the path walk for the `blob:limit` filter should skip on-demand fetches in partial clones (using `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` or disabling `fetch_if_missing`). He argues against it: a user explicitly running `git pack-objects --filter=blob:<size>` from a blobless partial clone is knowingly requesting an inefficient operation that requires downloading blobs. Stolee considers the current behavior -- downloading missing blobs to check their size -- to be technically correct, even if it is not performant. For `git backfill`, he explains the size-limit filter is irrelevant anyway: backfill makes batch-download decisions based on local tree structure, not server-side size filtering, and direct blob requests to the server cannot be filtered by size.

This is a brief, decisive response that resolves Blau's question in the negative. Stolee is not changing anything; he is explaining why the current behavior is the right one. No disagreement from Blau is expected on this point, given Blau's admission of limited `git backfill` knowledge and his sign-off that "the rest looks good to me." This exchange is effectively closed.
