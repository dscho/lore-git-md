This thread documents a small but useful clarification to the `--word-diff` man page. The original patch explained that Git's word-diff operates in two stages: first computing a line-level diff, then applying word-level comparison within those hunks. This can produce counterintuitive results where word changes appear even when the actual words match, due to the line-level alignment.

The v2 iteration expands this explanation with two additional points: first, that this implementation may produce larger diffs than a dedicated word-diff tool would, and second, that the output could change if Git adopts a different implementation in the future. It also draws a parallel to `--diff-algorithm` which similarly documents potential output changes. The commit cites Vincent Lefevre and Johannes Sixt as contributors who encountered this confusion.

This remains a straightforward documentation improvement with no outstanding objections. The patch is small (8 lines added) and focused entirely on the Documentation/ directory. Given the clear motivation and lack of controversy, this should merge cleanly in the next cycle.
