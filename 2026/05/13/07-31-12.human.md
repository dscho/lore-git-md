A v3 cover letter from Patrick Steinhardt for a two-patch series fixing maintenance-mode issues in Git. This iteration (v3) comes after a v2 that already had the second patch settled; the change here is entirely about the first patch, which now adopts Taylor Blau's preferred approach for handling lockfile and tempfile ownership transfer when daemonizing.

Patch 1 (the reworked one) now makes `daemonize()` automatically reassign all tempfiles and lockfiles to the child process, rather than requiring the caller to manage this manually. Taylor had "fairly strong" feelings about this in v2 review, offered a Co-authored-by with an off-list design, and Patrick accepted it. The series grew from touching 5 files to touching 6 because `tempfile.c` and `tempfile.h` are now involved. The test (the FIFO-based one Taylor praised as "much more robust" in v2) and the rest of the infrastructure carry over.

Patch 2 is unchanged from v2 -- it restores the `gc.auto` integer threshold behavior that broke when `git maintenance` replaced the old `git gc` shell-out. This part was already fully settled: Jeff King and Junio both independently confirmed the correctness problem, and the fix (reading `maintenance.auto` first, falling back to `gc.auto` as an integer) follows the established `autoDetach` precedence pattern.

The thread summary indicates there are no remaining open questions or change requests on either patch. With Taylor's alternative implementation now incorporated, this series looks ready for `next`. Given that both bugs are real user-facing regressions and the fixes have been through three rounds with solid review, this is a high-confidence merge candidate for 2.54.0.
