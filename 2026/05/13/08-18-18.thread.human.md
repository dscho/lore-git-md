This email thread reports a bug where lowercase `head` in a linked worktree on a case-insensitive filesystem resolves to the main worktree's HEAD instead of the linked worktree's own HEAD, because Git's filesystem-backed ref store accidentally matches `.git/HEAD` via a case-insensitive file lookup.

Alexander Sandström filed the initial report and proposed two possible fixes: reject lowercase `head` with an error, or normalize it to route through the correct per-worktree codepath. He is not a C programmer and does not plan to submit a patch.

D. Ben Knoble replied, linking to two prior discussions from 2024 that already identified the same underlying issue. Knoble's view is that the filesystem-backed ref store is fundamentally incapable of fixing this at the ref-store layer, and that switching to the reftable backend would eliminate the problem naturally. His reply essentially marks the bug as "won't-fix for the files backend" and directs attention to the ongoing reftable transition as the eventual solution. No one has evaluated the reporter's proposed fix options. The thread is now dormant with no patch expected and no active further discussion.
