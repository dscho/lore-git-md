This is a follow-up from Peff to Junio's maintainer reply on the `http-walker.c` alternate-URL parsing bugfix.

Peff agrees that the dumb HTTP walker has been a persistent source of bugs and vulnerabilities. He recalls that the idea of dropping dumb protocol support was discussed a few years ago, but notes that Eric (almost certainly Eric Sunshine) countered that dumb clones are less server-intensive for certain use cases -- specifically large public-inbox repositories where most old history lives in one big, never-updated pack. Peff acknowledges that `git fetch`'s verbatim pack-reuse was intended to close that gap for smart clones, but he has not measured whether it has succeeded.

This email does not change the status of the patch -- the bugfix is still accepted and ready to merge. What it adds is a piece of project memory: there was a prior discussion about deprecating dumb HTTP support, it was tabled for good reasons, and those reasons may or may not still hold. That is relevant background if someone later revives the deprecation question, but it has no bearing on whether this specific fix should go in.
