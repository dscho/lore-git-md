This is a maintainer reply to Pushkar Singh’s administrative follow-up about a stash test patch that did not appear in the May 12, 2026 “What’s cooking” report.

Junio’s response is not an apology or an assurance of a missed patch — it is a polite but frank explanation of the project’s filtering process. He explains that topics in the “What’s cooking” report reflect patches that have drawn discussion or that he personally judges likely to be interesting. This patch, which adds tests for stash, attracted no discussion and left Junio unconvinced of its value: “I wasn’t sure what the point was myself. Surely we can pile on tests that demonstrates a command works in a certain way, but is this particular test demonstrate something we are likely to break, or something?” This is a clear signal that Junio did not intentionally overlook the patch — he simply did not see a reason to track it.

The unspoken implication is that the burden is on the submitter to justify why this specific test is worth adding, either by writing a stronger commit message or by eliciting reviewer interest. Without that, the patch will remain in limbo.

No other content is discussed — this is a single-issue reply, entirely about the triage status of one test patch, and it resolves the open question from the thread summary (was the patch overlooked?) with a definitive “not overlooked, just not yet justified.”
