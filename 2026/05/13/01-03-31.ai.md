Refactoring / cleanup. A follow-up from Junio C Hamano to Jeff King's single-patch thread on the RFC 2047 encoder.

Junio agrees that the patch is correct ("Very nice.") and then takes it one step further: he suggests that the project could systematically go through every `strbuf_grow()` call in the codebase and remove the ones that are ineffective pre-sizing -- those that are immediately followed by a `strbuf_addX()` call that will grow the buffer on its own anyway. He provides two examples from `apply.c` and `archive.c` to illustrate the pattern.

This is not a new submission or a request to change Peff's patch. It is a "yes, and" -- an acknowledgment that this particular fix is good, and a suggestion that the same logic could be applied more broadly as a future cleanup effort. Junio is not asking Peff to expand his patch, nor is he formally assigning work; he is planting the idea for anyone interested.

The thread is effectively concluded: Peff's patch is clearly correct, Junio has blessed it with a positive response, and follow-up discussion is unlikely unless someone wants to pick up the broader cleanup Junio hints at.
