This thread is a simple but important bugfix for Git's handling of ANSI color codes in progress messages. Jeff King posted a one-line patch to `sideband.c` that makes Git's SGR sequence parser accept colons (`:`) as sub-parameter separators, matching the ECMA-48 standard and fixing compatibility with terminals that emit 256-color (`\e[38:5:NNm`) or true-color (`\e[38:2:R:G:Bm`) escape codes. Without this change, these sequences appear as raw garbage in Git's output during operations like `git push`.

Johannes Schindelin has reviewed the patch and given it a clear thumbs-up, calling it "well-motivated and well-executed" and stating he has no objections. His review was brief and focused on the patch's overall merit rather than technical details, so while the endorsement is strong, it doesn't address potential edge cases or test coverage. The patch itself is additive—it only broadens what the parser accepts—and doesn't change how existing sequences are handled.

The patch is now effectively ready to merge, though Junio may still request tests for the new codepath before picking it up. There are no open technical questions or objections, and no other reviewers have weighed in yet. This is a straightforward fix that should resolve a long-standing compatibility issue with minimal risk.
