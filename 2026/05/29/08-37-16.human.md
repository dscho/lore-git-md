This is a review comment on a cleanup series removing deprecated commit list functions. Jeff King (Peff) acknowledges the correctness of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's v2 series but raises a broader question about Git's deprecation workflow.

Peff observes that while the current approach of marking functions deprecated and removing them later is working, it might be more efficient to simply remove obsolete functions immediately when callers are updated. He notes that either way requires updating in-flight topics, but the current two-step process creates more total work. His comment is framed as a general consideration for future API changes rather than criticism of this specific series.

The review doesn't require any changes to the patches - it's more of a philosophical observation about project process. Given Peff's long experience with Git's development, this perspective carries weight, though he presents it as a casual thought rather than a strong recommendation. The series itself remains on track with Patrick Steinhardt's Acked-by still valid.
