Performance analysis of path-walk integration with bitmaps and delta-islands.

Derrick Stolee follows up on his earlier review of Taylor Blau's series with concrete performance measurements from p5311 tests comparing path-walk behavior with and without bitmaps. The data shows mixed results: path-walk mode produces significantly smaller packfiles (up to 1730.9% larger sizes without path-walk) for small fetches, while bitmap mode maintains speed advantages for larger fetches (33.3% faster client operations in some cases). Notably, the path-walk implementation shows no regression when combined with bitmaps - the HEAD results match path-walk behavior without bitmaps.

Stolee's analysis reveals that path-walk's benefits manifest primarily in small pack scenarios, with diminishing returns as fetch size increases. He validates the technical correctness observed in his initial review but raises new questions about the feature's impact profile, noting that even problematic repositories like microsoft/fluentui (known for name-hash collisions) show similar patterns. The email concludes with an open question about why path-walk doesn't demonstrate more dramatic improvements in these test cases, signaling potential areas for further optimization research.
