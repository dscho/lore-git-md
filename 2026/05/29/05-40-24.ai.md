This is a bugfix follow-up discussion about HTTP pack index handling, specifically addressing tempfile cleanup patterns. Jeff King (Peff) continues his review of Lorenzo Pegorari's memory leak fixes by exploring an alternative approach to tempfile management.

Peff considers - but ultimately rejects - a more involved refactoring that would pass the tempfile struct up the call stack to enable earlier cleanup via `delete_tempfile()`. While this approach could theoretically allow more precise resource management, he concludes the complexity outweighs the benefits for this case. The key technical points are:

1. The current patch's approach of removing the redundant `unlink()` is simpler and still correct, since tempfiles are already registered for cleanup at process exit (since commit 63aca3f7f1).

2. A more thorough refactoring would require propagating the tempfile struct through multiple function calls, which would only provide marginal benefits (slightly earlier cleanup) at the cost of more invasive changes.

Peff includes a prototype diff showing what the alternative approach might look like, demonstrating his thorough consideration of the options, but explicitly states his preference for the simpler solution. This represents the final technical discussion on the patch, confirming the approach taken is sound while documenting the considered alternatives for future reference.
