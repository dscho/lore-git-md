Performance optimization for index-pack's delta resolution. Patch from Arijit Banerjee addresses an inefficiency where index-pack would prematurely free reconstructed delta base objects still needed for child delta resolution, forcing redundant reconstruction. The 1-line change removes a `free_base_data()` call to leverage the existing delta base cache, showing 15-16% faster wall times on linux.git with 1.9% RSS increase.
