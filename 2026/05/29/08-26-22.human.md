This is a final review response in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for pack-bitmap-write, specifically addressing patch 6/8 which sorts bitmaps before XOR compression.

Jeff King (Peff) confirms Taylor's findings about the impracticality of benchmarking pre-v2.31 bitmap generation due to extreme slowness, having attempted similar tests himself on linux.git and chromium.git. Peff agrees the sorting approach is correct regardless of when the issue originated.

The exchange also validates that the read-side performance impact is negligible, with Taylor's earlier measurements showing only minor timing variations within noise margins. This closes the technical discussion on patch 6/8, with Peff's approval signaling the change is ready for integration.
