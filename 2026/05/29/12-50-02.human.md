Bugfix follow-up for Git's date parsing of small Unix timestamps. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Luna Schwalbe's bug report about Git rejecting timestamps below 100000000 (pre-1973 dates) in environment variables and misinterpreting them via `--date=raw`.

The reply notes that small timestamps can be accepted by prefixing them with `@`, as in `@0 +0000`. This suggests the issue may stem from Git's parser requiring explicit marking of Unix timestamps below a certain threshold, though the underlying bug in the raw format handling remains unaddressed.

This is a helpful workaround but doesn't resolve the core inconsistency between environment variable parsing and `--date` option behavior that Luna identified. The thread is still in its early stages with no maintainer response yet. The workaround's existence implies this might be a known (if undocumented) parser behavior rather than a complete oversight.
