Git GUI environment variable handling improvement. Author Aina Boot proposes centralized management of GIT_DIR and GIT_WORK_TREE variables in git-gui, replacing scattered direct manipulations. Introduces five new procedures: set_worktree_env (sets GIT_WORK_TREE only with valid worktree), unset_worktree_env, clear_git_env (for submodule context), capture_git_env, and restore_git_env. Changes aim to prevent incorrect GIT_WORK_TREE settings and standardize environment handling. No open questions or objections raised yet; appears to be a straightforward internal cleanup targeting git-gui's environment management.
