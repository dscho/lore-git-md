This is a conversational follow-up to an already-closed review thread for René Scharfe's `strbuf_add_uint()` series. René is responding to a speculative aside from Peff about whether `__builtin_add_overflow()` could be faster than the current `unsigned_add_overflows()` pattern in `strbuf_grow()`.

René actually tested it. Using godbolt to check compiler output, he found that on x86-64 Clang and GCC emit identical instructions for both macros, but Clang on ARM64 fails to optimize away the redundant comparison when using `unsigned_add_overflows()`. He then patched `strbuf_grow()` to use the intrinsic and benchmarked on his Apple M1 (Apple's Clang), getting a 2% speedup on `git cat-file --batch-all-objects --batch-check='%(objectname)'`. The same patch had no effect with GCC 15.2.

This is a casual, empirical observation -- not a patch submission, not a request for inclusion, and not a review of the `strbuf_add_uint()` series. René provides the diff inline simply to show what he tested, and his tone is collegial ("Curious...") rather than persuasive.
