This is a discussion about Git project terminology, specifically the ongoing effort to replace "master" with "main" as the default branch name. The email comes from a contributor who strongly objects to this change, arguing that:

1. Git should remain neutral on social issues and focus solely on version control functionality
2. The term "master" has a long technical history that predates current social sensitivities
3. The change is being driven by people who may not be directly affected by the terminology
4. While individuals should be free to change their own repositories, the project shouldn't enforce or encourage this change universally

The author identifies as Black African and states they've never personally been troubled by the term. The tone is direct and somewhat confrontational, rejecting what they see as unnecessary social engineering through technical defaults.

This continues a long-running discussion about branch naming that has seen Git move toward "main" as the default in recent years while maintaining backwards compatibility. The email represents a dissenting view against that trend, arguing from both technical tradition and personal perspective.
