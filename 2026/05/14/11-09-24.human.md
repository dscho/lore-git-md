This is a technical aside in the finished `strbuf_add_uint()` thread, from the series author René Scharfe. He is responding to a speculative suggestion Jeff King made in a previous email about using `__builtin_add_overflow()` in `strbuf_grow()`.

René dug into it and reports that the intrinsic is a wash on x86-64 — both Clang and GCC emit identical code with or without it. But on ARM64 (Apple M1 + Apple's Clang), `__builtin_add_overflow()` avoids an unnecessary comparison instruction, shaving about 2ms off the `cat-file` benchmark (a ~2% gain). GCC 15.2 on the same platform shows no difference.

This is a clean, small finding — René prototyped the change in a short diff, benchmarked it properly on two compilers on one architecture, and shared the result. It is not a patch submission and not a request for anything; René is simply satisfying Peff's curiosity before closing the thread. The series itself remains complete and ready for Junio to pick up. This email has zero bearing on the review status of the `strbuf_add_uint()` patches.
