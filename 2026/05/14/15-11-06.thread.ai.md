Subject: strbuf_grow overflow check refactoring and performance optimization
Author: René Scharfe
Thread type: code cleanup and performance improvement

René Scharfe's series refactors strbuf_grow()'s overflow checking to use st_add3() (combining two validations into one call) and optimizes st_add() for Clang builds using __builtin_add_overflow(), yielding 5% speedup on M1 for strbuf-heavy operations. Junio Hamano raised concerns about interaction with ALLOC_GROW macro's multiple argument evaluation, prompting discussion of whether to document or modify the behavior. Jeff King (Peff) provided the original Clang optimization approach.

In response to Junio's review, a new proposal emerges to generalize the optimization by modifying unsigned_add_overflows() to match __builtin_add_overflow()'s pointer-based result parameter, potentially benefiting all 18 call sites rather than just st_add(). This maintainer-level suggestion shifts focus from a targeted performance fix to a broader architectural improvement, though it would require updating more call sites. The thread remains open with two paths forward: either adapt René's original Clang-specific optimization to address Junio's ALLOC_GROW concerns, or implement Junio's more comprehensive approach that would standardize overflow checking across the codebase. The core safety guarantees remain unchanged in either case.
