While acknowledging the code hygiene improvement, Junio raises a concern about potential multiple overflow checks due to ALLOC_GROW being a macro that evaluates its arguments multiple times. The feedback is technical and constructive, focusing on macro evaluation semantics rather than rejecting the change outright. This interaction exemplifies Git's careful approach to low-level changes, where even small cleanups receive scrutiny from the maintainer when they touch core infrastructure. The thread continues the project's ongoing effort to standardize overflow checking while maintaining performance.
