This is a follow-up reply from Pablo Sabater (the patch author) responding to Jeff King's
Coverity-spotlighted dead-code concern on the `git log --graph` placeholder
patch.

Pablo confirms the observation: the `if (seen_this) continue;` inside the
else block of `graph_output_post_merge_line()` is dead code and can go.
He explains that he copied the placeholder handling from
`graph_output_commit_line()` without realizing that
`graph_output_post_merge_line()` never encounters a case where a
placeholder column is also `seen_this` -- the two functions handle
different positions in the rendering pipeline.

This is a short, clean response from a contributor who recognized the
mistake and stated the fix plainly: drop the dead code. No deeper logic
error was hiding behind the dead guard. The patch itself remains
otherwise sound, and the only action item from Peff's review is
resolved with a trivial deletion.
