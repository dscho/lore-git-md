René Scharfe continues his work to improve Git's overflow checking infrastructure with a refactoring of strbuf_grow() that combines two validations into one st_add3() call while maintaining safety. The latest patch also optimizes the underlying st_add() helper for Clang builds, showing measurable 5% speedup on M1 hardware for strbuf-heavy operations. Jeff King (Peff) provided the suggested approach for the Clang optimization.

Junio Hamano has now weighed in with a substantive technical review, raising a concern about how the refactoring interacts with the ALLOC_GROW macro's multiple evaluation of arguments. While acknowledging the patch maintains all safety guarantees, Junio questions whether compilers will optimize away redundant overflow checks in this context. This maintainer-level feedback suggests René may need to either document this behavior or consider an alternative approach to address the multiple-evaluation concern. The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than the fundamental approach, with all participants agreeing on the value of the safety improvements.
