This thread began with a performance optimization series that replaces a linked list with a priority queue in `limit_list()`, yielding significant speedups (1.6-4.3x) for revision walking in merge-heavy repositories. The changes affect commands using symmetric difference like `--left-right`, `--cherry-mark`, and bisect. After thorough review from Derrick Stolee, Jeff King (Peff), and Junio C Hamano, the series was approved for merging.

Kristofer Karlsson later noted that extending the priority queue approach to `get_revision_1()` could unlock speedups (3.3x) for `git rev-list HEAD` in monorepos. In the latest email, Peff confirms this is viable, sharing that he has unpublished patches implementing the same idea. He offers his branch as a starting point, noting the approach would make `rev_info` use a priority queue as its primary structure while carefully handling non-date-sorted cases. Both agree this should be a follow-up series, building on the now-merged `limit_list()` optimizations. The discussion shows continued collaboration on improving revision walking performance, with multiple contributors independently converging on similar solutions.
