Kristofer Karlsson's performance optimization series for `limit_list()` has been approved by Junio Hamano and will be merged into Git after the standard integration period. The patch replaces a linked list with a priority queue, yielding 1.6-4.3x speedups in merge-heavy repositories, with particularly strong gains for ancestry-path queries (4.3x) and commit-graph cases (2.5x). The optimization affects several commands including `--left-right`, bisect, and `git status --ahead-behind`.

The series received thorough review from Derrick Stolee and Jeff King, with René Scharfe providing additional benchmark validation showing consistent 1.16-1.28x improvements even in Git's relatively linear history. Kristofer identified a related optimization opportunity in `get_revision_1()` that could bring 3.3x speedups, but Junio wisely deferred this to a future effort to keep the current change focused. The patch will now enter Git's standard integration pipeline - first to 'next' for testing, then 'master' if no issues emerge - and should appear in an upcoming release.
