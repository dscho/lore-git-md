This is a follow-up in the strbuf_grow optimization thread, where René Scharfe responds to Junio Hamano's concern about multiple evaluations in the ALLOC_GROW macro. René acknowledges the issue but argues that with compiler optimization enabled, the redundant overflow checks should be eliminated since addition has no side effects.

He then proposes a more substantial change - refactoring ALLOC_GROW itself to use a helper function (st_alloc_nr) that would evaluate the growth calculation only once. This would address Junio's concern at the macro level rather than just in the strbuf_grow case. The patch sketch shows how this could work while maintaining the same allocation growth logic.

Notably, René spots an orthogonal issue: the alloc_nr helper function currently lacks overflow checking, which he suggests should be addressed. This demonstrates his characteristic attention to safety and edge cases, even when reviewing his own proposed changes.

The email keeps the thread moving forward with two concrete paths: either proceed with the original strbuf_grow optimization trusting compiler behavior, or tackle the broader ALLOC_GROW refactoring that would standardize the evaluation pattern across all call sites. Both approaches maintain the safety guarantees while potentially improving performance.
