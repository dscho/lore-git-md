This is a maintainer's reply-in-closing to an already-finished review thread, and it is about as low-stakes as an email on this list can be.

Junio is responding to René Scharfe's final off-hand experiment in which René tested whether `__builtin_add_overflow()` could beat the existing `unsigned_add_overflows()` pattern in `strbuf_grow()`. René had demonstrated a 2% speedup on Apple's Clang on ARM64, with no effect on GCC 15.2 on x86-64. Junio's reply is brief and appreciative: he calls the result "satisfying" and notes that it is nice to see the compilers able to optimize the current code into the equivalent form.

That is the entire substance of this email. Junio offers no opinion on whether René should submit this as a patch, no signal about maintainer interest, and no new technical analysis. It is a collegial acknowledgment of a clever observation, nothing more.

This email changes nothing about the thread's status. The `strbuf_add_uint()` series is done. The `__builtin_add_overflow()` idea for `strbuf_grow()` remains what it was before Junio replied: a bench-racing observation that René showed his curiosity could sustain, but which he has not offered for inclusion. If someone wants to pursue it, they would need to start a new thread.
