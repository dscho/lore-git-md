Performance optimization patch series (v2) for revision walking's `limit_list()`, replacing a linked list with a priority queue to achieve O(log n) insertion cost in merge-heavy repositories. Shows 1.6-4.3x speedups in benchmarks, with largest gains for ancestry-path queries (4.3x) and commit-graph-enabled cases (2.5x). Affects commands using symmetric difference: `--left-right`, `--cherry-mark`, bisect, rebase fork-point, and `git status --ahead-behind`. The optimization has been reviewed and approved by Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, and Junio C Hamano, with all technical concerns addressed.
