Performance optimization patch series (v2) for revision walking's `limit_list()`, replacing a linked list with a priority queue to achieve O(log n) insertion cost in merge-heavy repositories. Shows 1.6-4.3x speedups in benchmarks, with largest gains for ancestry-path queries (4.3x) and commit-graph-enabled cases (2.5x). Affects commands using symmetric difference: `--left-right`, `--cherry-mark`, bisect, rebase fork-point, and `git status --ahead-behind`. The optimization has been reviewed and approved by Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, and Junio C Hamano, with all technical concerns addressed. Maintainer approval received for merging through standard integration process (next -> master), concluding active development.

Kristofer Karlsson identified potential extension to `get_revision_1()` (3.3x speedup for `git rev-list HEAD` in monorepos) but this will be pursued separately. René Scharfe validated the optimization with benchmarks across Git's version history (v2.0.1 to v2.50.1), showing consistent 1.16-1.28x speedups for `git rev-list --bisect` even in linear histories, though largest gains remain in merge-heavy cases. The series is now considered complete and ready for integration, with no outstanding technical objections.
