This thread has completed a focused bugfix series addressing several network address handling issues in Git's daemon mode (`daemon.c`). The original author, Sebastien Tardif, identified and fixed three distinct problems: IPv6 address corruption during hostname lookup, silent truncation of IPv6 addresses in logs, and potential crashes from NULL pointer dereferences when logging connection details.

The series went through three iterations, with v3 incorporating all feedback from reviewers. The final version splits the fixes into three clean commits, each addressing a specific issue while maintaining the existing logging format. Notable improvements include proper handling of different address families, correct buffer sizing for IPv6 addresses, and comprehensive NULL pointer safety checks.

Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt both reviewed the series, with Junio suggesting minor commit message improvements and confirming the technical approach. While some testing gaps remain (particularly for IPv6-only hosts), the fixes are considered ready for merging as they address clear correctness issues without introducing new risks. The changes are confined to `daemon.c` and don't affect the protocol or on-disk formats. This appears to be a solid, well-reviewed series that improves the robustness of Git's daemon mode.
