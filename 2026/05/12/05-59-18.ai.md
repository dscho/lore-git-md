A maintainer review response from Patrick Steinhardt on patch 1/2 of his two-patch maintenance-mode series. He acknowledges both of Junio C Hamano's earlier observations on the code and confirms his intent for the next revision.

On Junio's first point -- the comment in `maintenance_run_tasks()` about lockfile ownership in the parent process -- Steinhardt agrees the phrasing could be better and reports what he has changed it to. The new comment says "we drop ownership of the lockfile to prevent us from removing it upon exit," which captures Junio's suggestion that the point is relinquishment, not PID-based assignment to a specific child.

On Junio's second point -- the duplicated `#ifdef NO_POSIX_GOODIES` conditional in both `daemonize()` and `daemonize_without_exit()` -- Steinhardt confirms he considered avoiding it but deliberately chose minimality over cleanliness for this series. He explains two reasons: the existing `daemonize()` calls `die_errno()` on fork failure (which requires the conditional to remain), and his goal was to fix only the lockfile issue without risking behavioral changes elsewhere. He agrees that cleaning up the duplication is a good idea for a follow-up series, noting there are few callers of `daemonize()`, so the work should be straightforward.

No new technical issues are raised. This email is a polite, confirming response from the author accepting both of Junio's conversational observations. Steinhardt's v2 will include the comment wording change, while the `#ifdef` cleanup is deferred to a later series, consistent with the thread consensus that patch 1 is ready.
