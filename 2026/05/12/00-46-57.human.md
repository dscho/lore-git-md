This is patch 4/9 in Taylor Blau's pseudo-merge bitmap bugfix series, which the maintainer has indicated is nearly ready to merge.

The patch fixes an inverted binary search in the `pseudo_merge_at()` function. The "lo" and "hi" update logic was swapped -- when the midpoint offset was less than the target, the search would go left instead of right, and vice versa. This meant that lookups for pseudo-merges could fail when more than one pseudo-merge existed in a group, because the search would move in the wrong direction and miss the entry. With a single pseudo-merge group, the bug was masked since the lone entry sits at the midpoint of the table.

The fix is four lines of swapped conditionals in `pseudo-merge.c`. It promotes one test in `t5333-pseudo-merge-bitmaps.sh` from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success`: the test for applying pseudo-merges from multiple groups during fill-in traversal, which was one of the broken cases introduced in patch 2 of the series. This is a straightforward and obviously correct fix -- the logic was simply backwards.
