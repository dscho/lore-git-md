This is a call for review from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, on the v3 of the `worktree` includeIf condition series by Chen Linxuan. The series itself has already been summarized in the thread context: it introduces `worktree:` and `worktree/i:` patterns as new `includeIf` config conditions, complementing the existing `gitdir` and `onbranch` conditions, so that multi-worktree users can match config against the checkout path rather than the awkward `.git/worktrees/` directory.

What is new here is Junio’s assessment. He says the feature "sounds like a worthwhile thing to have" and the code "looks OK-ish" but he is not fully back up to speed and explicitly asks for one or two volunteers to review the patches. This is a relatively gentle signal: Junio often picks patches directly when he is confident, so the fact that he is deferring here means he either sees something non-obvious or simply lacks the bandwidth to give it the deep look it deserves. He is using his position to solicit broader engagement rather than holding the series up himself.

For a reader deciding where to spend attention: this series is small (three files changed, 130 insertions) and the concept is clearly motivated. It has gone through three iterations and Junio’s feedback has been incorporated. If you work with multi-worktree setups or care about config inclusion, this is likely worth reviewing. If your interest is more marginal, the thread is probably going to converge quickly once a review or two appears. The ball is now on the community to push this over the finish line.
