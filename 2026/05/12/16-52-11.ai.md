**Classification:** Bugfix, review of patch 2/3 of a v2 series targeting Git's approxidate date parser, focusing on the "specials" fix.

Junio C Hamano has posted a reply to Tuomas Ahola's patch 2/3. This is an incremental review -- Junio has already reviewed patch 1/3 (test infrastructure) in its own reply; this is his look at the core fix patch. The review is substantive but not deeply thorough: Junio reads the commit message and the diff carefully, but he does not go beyond the patch boundaries to trace callers or test coverage.

Junio raises three points:

First, the commit message could be clearer. The original message presents several examples of wrong output without explaining what the right answer should be. Junio suggests adding a brief "for example, here is what would work well" to contrast against the broken cases. He also notes specifically that saying "Friday of that week was May 8th" would help readers see why the output is wrong.

Second, the new comment in `date_time()` that explains the logic. Junio comments that he finds the version of the comment that Jeff King wrote in his earlier review of v1 easier to understand, implying he would prefer something closer to that wording.

Third, Junio flags a concern about adding `"today"` as an alias for `"now"`. He notes that `"today"` might not work well for commands like `git log --since=today` because the user might expect all patches from today to be included, whereas `--since` is exclusive (it means "strictly after"). Making `"today"` resolve to `"now"` (the current wall-clock time) would potentially exclude commits from earlier today, which is not what a naive user would expect. This is a real functional concern, not just a style nit.

Junio does not say the patch should be rejected, nor does he say he will merge it. The tone is matter-of-fact and the specific request for the `"today"` concern is left unresolved in this reply -- Junio is flagging it for discussion rather than demanding a change. Since the rest of patch 2/3 is straightforward, the likely outcome is that this point will need to be resolved before the patch can land, either by dropping the `"today"` alias or by justifying its safety.

Patches 2/3 and 3/3 have now both been posted and Junio has looked at 2/3; his review of 3/3 is still pending. The series is in a "discussing open points" state rather than "ready to merge."
