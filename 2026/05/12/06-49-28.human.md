This is the cover-letter wrap-up for v6 of Lorenzo Pegorari's GSoC
project series, which fixes the longstanding `NEEDSWORK` comment from
commit 5374a290 (2019) about preserving ref-to-packfile mappings in
`.promisor` files during repacks. The series proper is six patches
adding a `write_promisor_file_after_repack` helper, tests for both
regular and geometric repacks, documentation, and coding-style
cleanup.

What is notable here is that Junio is effectively nudging this series
toward the finish line. He explicitly notes that not many people are
reviewing the final round, then deliberately CCs Christian Couder --
who is deeply familiar with the promisor remote subsystem and whose
expertise Junio would like applied here. This is a gentle but clear
signal that Junio considers the series ready enough that he is
proactively seeking the remaining reviewer bandwidth to get it
merged.

The email itself is brief and procedural: it forwards Lorenzo's cover
letter, summarizes what is in the six patches, and invites Christian
to take a look if interested. It does not raise any technical
objections or suggest that anything is wrong with the series.

For a busy reader: this series looks well-cooked and uncontroversial.
It has been through six iterations, has tests, addresses a real gap
documented in the code for years, and Junio is shepherding it. The
main question is whether Christian or another promisor expert finds
time to give a final look; if they do, this is likely to land soon.
