Subject: [PATCH 2/4] Replace strbuf_addf() with strbuf_add_uint() for object sizes in cat-file

Author: René Scharfe.

Core change: In `builtin/cat-file.c`, replaces `strbuf_addf(&buf, "%"PRIuMAX, (uintmax_t)size)` with `strbuf_add_uint(&buf, size)` for the two `objectsize` and `objectsize:disk` atoms in `--batch-check` mode. Two lines changed, both mechanical and safe.

Performance: Attached benchmarks show ~4-7% speedup on full `--batch-all-objects` traversal (720 vs 752 ms for `objectsize`, 378 vs 405 ms for `objectsize:disk`). Gains from avoiding `strbuf_addf()` variadic argument parsing for simple integer printing. Methodology: 10 runs, `hyperfine`-style.

Status: Patch 2/4 in a batch of similar `strbuf_addf()` -> `strbuf_add_uint()` conversions. Uncontroversial, no review comments received. Expectation: ready for merge.
