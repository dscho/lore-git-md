The standout development is the graduation of `jc/neuter-sideband-fixup` to `master`. This is a multi-year effort to sanitize escape sequences received over sideband (e.g., from remote servers during push/fetch) that could enable terminal injection attacks. Junio deliberately graduated the *stricter* form -- which masks control characters by default -- rather than the looser final step of the series. This is an explicit experiment to give the stricter version real-world testing before potentially dialing it back at the Git 3.0 boundary, where the follow-on `jc/neuter-sideband-post-3.0` topic is waiting on hold.
