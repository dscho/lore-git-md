### Cover letter for bugfix series -- pseudo-merge bitmap fixes (v3)

This is the cover letter for a third iteration of Taylor Blau's nine-patch series fixing bugs in Git's pseudo-merge bitmap implementation. The series originated from Jeff King noticing that the pseudo-merge code path was never exercised by the test suite, which led to uncovering multiple compounding bugs in both the bitmap lookup and group selection logic.

The changes in v3 are minimal and purely test-hygiene: ordering `test_when_finished` above `git init` per convention, and avoiding a Git invocation on the left side of a pipe in a test in patch 9. The technical content is otherwise identical to v2. The series has been rebased onto the current tip of `master`. A range-diff showing the two minor adjustments is included.

Junio C Hamano has indicated in his draft "What's cooking" that he considers the series "so close to the finish line" and is awaiting this final update. With all substantive technical issues resolved in earlier rounds and only these cosmetic test fixups remaining, this series is effectively ready for merge.
