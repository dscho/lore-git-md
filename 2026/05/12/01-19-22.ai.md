This is a reply from Junio C Hamano (the maintainer) reviewing patch 1/2 of Patrick Steinhardt's maintenance-mode series.

Junio has two observations on the code, neither of which is a blocker. First, he finds the comment on the parent-process path in `maintenance_run_tasks()` slightly misleading: saying "we assign ownership of the lockfile to the child" implies the child's PID matters, when really any non-own-PID would do; he suggests something like "we relinquish ... to prevent us removing upon exiting" would better convey the intent. This is a documentation nit -- no code change required, just an observation.

Second, Junio notes that the new `daemonize()` wrapper around `daemonize_without_exit()` duplicates the `#ifdef NO_POSIX_GOODIES` conditional that already exists in the inner function. He would prefer to avoid that duplication, but immediately acknowledges two things that keep it acceptable: the `die_errno()` call for a fork failure means the conditional *must* remain in the caller, and changing what callers see when fork fails is out of scope for this topic anyway. So this is an "I wish it were cleaner, but I understand why it is this way" comment, not a demand for revision.

Overall this is a light, approving review from the maintainer. No substantive correctness issues were raised. Junio is satisfied with the approach and the code; the two points he raises are conversational rather than actionable. Combined with the earlier thread summary showing that Peff had substantive concerns only on patch 2, this means patch 1 is in good shape and ready to proceed when the series as a whole is revised.
