This is a small performance patch from René Scharfe, replacing two `strbuf_addf()` calls in `builtin/cat-file.c` with the more specialized `strbuf_add_uint()`. The two calls format the `%(objectsize)` and `%(objectsize:disk)` atoms when printing batch output.

The commit message is well-explained and includes benchmark results showing a 4% speedup for `%(objectsize)` and 7% for `%(objectsize:disk)` when running `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` -- a real, if modest, improvement. The change itself is mechanical and obviously correct: `strbuf_add_uint()` avoids the formatting overhead of the general-purpose `*printf` path.

This is the second patch in a 4-patch series, likely a set of similar micro-optimizations. Clean, low-risk, and the kind of tidy performance tightening René is known for.
