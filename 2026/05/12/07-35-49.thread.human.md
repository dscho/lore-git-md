This bug report was originally filed by Daniel Mach (SUSE) and has now been confirmed by **Jeff King (Peff)**, who independently discovered the same issue just over a week earlier. The problem is that `git clone --dissociate --reference <old-repo>` crashes with `fatal: unable to parse commit` when the reference repository has a commit-graph, because the dissociate operation invalidates the alternate but leaves the commit-graph referencing it in memory.

Peff has two candidate fixes in mind but hasn't settled on an approach: either a lazy fallback that gracefully degrades when the commit-graph goes stale, or aggressively pre-filling tree lookups from the commit-graph before closing it. No patch has been submitted yet, and no timeline has been offered. The thread remains open for discussion of the design tradeoff.
