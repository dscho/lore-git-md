This is a follow-up from Patrick Steinhardt to Junio C Hamano's review of patch 1/2 of the maintenance-mode locking series, sent alongside the v2 preparation.

Patrick acknowledges Junio's two observations and responds to each. On the lockfile-ownership comment, Patrick shows his revised wording -- "we drop ownership of the lockfile to prevent us from removing it upon exit" -- which Junio already indicated was just a documentation preference rather than a required change. On the duplicated `#ifdef NO_POSIX_GOODIES` in the new `daemonize()` wrapper, Patrick explains he chose to keep the duplication deliberately: he wanted the most minimal fix with the least chance of introducing regressions, preserving the existing `daemonize()` semantics unchanged. He agrees it would be nice to clean up in a follow-up series and notes there are few callers, making it a small piece of work.

This is a short, agreeable reply that confirms patch 1 is ready to go as-is for v2. Nothing new or contentious here -- just the author saying "yes, I see your points, here is the tweak I made, and I agree the ifdef cleanup can wait." Combined with the thread context showing patch 2 is the one with a real bug to fix, this email is a green light for the first half of the series.
