This is a v2 cover letter for a four-patch bugfix series, picking up after an earlier round of review and addressing Junio's feedback from the prior round. The patches are ready for merging.

The series fixes a latent bug where `-U`/`--unified` and `--inter-hunk-context` silently accept negative values, producing malformed diff output -- hunk headers with invalid line counts and patches that cannot be applied. The root cause is a historical artifact: both options were previously parsed with a custom `opt_arg()` that gated on `isdigit()`, so negatives were rejected without anyone realizing that property was the only thing holding the line. When they were mechanically converted to `OPT_INTEGER_F` and `OPT_CALLBACK` during the diff-parseopt migration (2019), the implicit validation was lost and has been missing ever since. The config-side equivalents, `diff.context` and `diff.interHunkContext`, have always rejected negatives, so this brings the CLI options into line.

The approach is straightforward: change the internal types from `int` to `unsigned int`, switch `--inter-hunk-context` to `OPT_UNSIGNED`, add a range check in the `-U` callback (which is a `OPT_CALLBACK_F` and can't use `OPT_UNSIGNED` directly), and add a defensive `BUG()` in xdiff's `xdl_get_hunk()` as a last line of defense. Patch 4 clarifies the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` comment in `parse-options.h` -- the v1 commit message incorrectly implied that `NONEG` had something to do with the bug, and has been corrected in this round.

The changes vs v1 are small and focused: rewritten commit messages for patches 1 and 4 to drop the misleading `NONEG` discussion, and patch 4 trimmed to just clarify that "negated" means `--no-<option>` without going into what it doesn't do. A range-diff is provided.

This is a well-scoped, well-explained fix for a real (if edge-case) problem that has been lurking for seven years. The technical approach is sound, Junio has already reviewed and accepted the solution, and the only outstanding item from the previous round was a cover-letter formatting remark, which Michael has acknowledged. This is ready to pick up.
