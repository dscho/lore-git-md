This is a follow-up email from Derrick Stolee, the author of the negotiation-control patch series, replying to Matthew Cheetham's earlier review of patch 6/7, which adds the `remote.<name>.negotiationInclude` config option.

The email is brief and procedural -- Stolee acknowledges two issues Cheetham spotted in his surface-level review. First, the documentation cross-reference in the config help text says "same as for `--negotiation-restrict`" but should say "same as for `--negotiation-include`". Stolee agrees. Second, the test for the empty-value reset is not quite robust: it checks that both `alpha_1` and `beta_1` appear in the trace, but `alpha_1` is actually being sent via the `--negotiation-restrict` CLI option, not via the include config. So a broken reset would still pass the test. Stolee suggests fixing this by pointing the restrict option at a different ref (`alpha_2`) to make the assertion meaningful.

Stolee also says "Will do" in response to Cheetham's two broader observations about the patch -- reusing `parse_transport_option()` versus inline resetting the list, and the fact that values could be commit SHAs as well as refs and globs -- which Cheetham carried over from his earlier review of the analogous patch 4/7.

Nothing new or controversial here. This is the author confirming simple, uncontested fixes for v4. The two issues are minor (a documentation typo and a test-robustness nit), and the email does not advance or alter the series' design. The real structural work in v4 -- the new negotiator callback and ref-resolution unification -- remains unposted.
