This is a brief, **substantive review** from Patrick Steinhardt of the second patch in a two-patch series introducing `worktree` and `worktree/i` as new `includeIf` conditions.

Patrick immediately signals the series looks good -- his opening is "Seems sensible" -- and he provides three small pieces of actionable feedback. First, he notes that the documentation block for the new conditions is essentially a literal copy of the `gitdir` documentation and suggests deduplicating into a common section. Second, he offers a mild forward-looking observation that the condition-matching code in `config.c` might eventually want to be table-driven, but explicitly says that does not need to happen in this series. Third, he points out two test gaps: whether the feature works with early config parsing (which already has a test for `gitdir:`) and whether it behaves correctly outside a repository entirely (the "nongit" case).

The review is approving in tone -- "Other than that this series looks good to me" -- and Patrick is a highly respected technical voice whose endorsement carries weight. Between his earlier endorsement of patch 1/2 and this review, the series now appears to have the qualified reviewer Junio was asking for.
