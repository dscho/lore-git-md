Jeff King's email is a brief reply that does three things. Second, it outlines the two candidate fixes he's considering. One is a lazy fallback: instead of requiring all commits to be parsed from the commit-graph, Git would fall back to manual object-reading when the commit-graph is closed or invalidated. The other is more aggressive: when dissociating, close the commit-graph and pre-fill all the tree lookups that the in-memory commits depend on. Peff notes the tradeoff depends on how many commits are actually examined after the close -- if most are, pre-filling may be cheaper; if only a few are, the lazy fallback wins.
