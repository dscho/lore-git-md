This is a small, clean refactoring series from René Scharfe, aimed at the "code hygiene" and "performance" boundary. René introduces a new function `strbuf_add_uint()` as a faster, specialized replacement for the common pattern of calling `strbuf_addf(sb, "%"PRIuMAX, value)` to append unsigned integers to a strbuf.

The series has four patches: the first adds the new function and its declaration; the next three convert callers in `git cat-file`, `git ls-files`, and `git ls-tree` to use it. The approach is classic René Scharfe -- a straightforward wrapper around the existing `strbuf_add_ull()` (which handles `unsigned long long`), with a check to ensure the value fits the narrower unsigned integer type on platforms where `uintmax_t` is wider.

The performance motivation is plausible: `strbuf_addf` goes through printf's format-string parsing, and switching to a direct numeric conversion should measurably speed up commands that output many object sizes (like `cat-file --batch` or `ls-tree --long`). The commit messages are characteristically terse but sufficient, and the patch is small enough (38 lines added, 14 removed) to review in seconds.

Nothing about this looks controversial -- it is a textbook example of a low-risk, well-motivated cleanup. The change is entirely mechanical and would be a straightfoward pickup for `next` after a quick look from a reviewer comfortable with strbuf internals.
