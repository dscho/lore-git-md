Documentation patch. This is the fourth and final patch of Michael Montalbo's four-patch series fixing the bug where negative numeric values were silently accepted by `-U` and `--inter-hunk-context`.

This patch clarifies the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag's in-code documentation in `parse-options.h` by adding the parenthetical "(i.e. rejects `--no-<option>`") to the existing comment. The change is motivated by the confusion that arose earlier in this thread -- as documented in the cover-letter summary, the original v1 incorrectly linked `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` to the negative-value bug, when in fact the flag only blocks the `--no-` boolean negated form of an option, not negative numeric values. This commit makes that distinction clear for future readers of the header.

The patch is a single-line comment addition. It is mechanically trivial, well-scoped, and directly addresses a concrete point of confusion that surfaced in the review process. No substantive technical risk, no tests needed, and no behaviour changes.
