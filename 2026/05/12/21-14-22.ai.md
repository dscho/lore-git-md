This is a **review** of patch 1 of a two-patch v2 bugfix series, and it is a **substantive review** -- Taylor Blau engages with the design choice in the patch, advocates for a different implementation strategy, and provides specific technical rationale.

Taylor Blau is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's v2 approach to the lockfile-leak-in-detached-mode fix. The v2 patch introduces a new function `daemonize_without_exit()` to explicitly handle ownership transfer of the lockfile at the singular call site in `builtin/gc.c`, rather than folding it into the general `daemonize()` function.

Taylor expresses a clear preference for the opposite approach -- performing the ownership transfer automatically inside `daemonize()` itself, so that every daemonized process inherits its locks and tempfiles correctly. The rationale is that future callers of `daemonize()` are overwhelmingly likely to want this behavior, and leaving the transfer to individual call-sites sets up an accident-prone pattern. The suggestion is to document the automatic-transfer behavior in `daemonize()`, and only introduce something like `daemonize_without_transfer()` if a caller ever arises that does not want it.

The review is framed as an invitation to discuss, but Taylor says they feel "fairly strongly" about this. They also offer to have their Co-authored-by and Signed-off-by lines used if Patrick adopts the alternative approach -- since it would make the C changes identical to what Taylor already posted in an earlier off-list sketch.

The rest of the review is positive: Taylor praises the FIFO-based test in the patch as "much more robust" than what they had come up with, and says the rest of the test "looks good to me."
