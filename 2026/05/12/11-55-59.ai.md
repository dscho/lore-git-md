The motivation is straightforward and the performance claim is plausible: formatting an unsigned integer to a decimal string is a simple loop of `x / 10` and `x % 10` operations, which will be measurably cheaper than routing through the full printf formatter. The commit message does not provide benchmark numbers, but for commands like `cat-file --batch` or `ls-tree -r` that can produce millions of lines of output, saving a few nanoseconds per line can add up.
