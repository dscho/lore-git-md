This is a small performance optimization from René Scharfe for `ls-files --format`. He replaces `strbuf_addf()` calls with `strbuf_add_uint()` plus manual padding in `builtin/ls-files.c`, affecting both the `%(objectsize)` and `%(objectsize:padded)` format specifiers. The change is straightforward and well-measured: roughly a 1.5% speedup on a Linux kernel tree for each variant, achieved by avoiding printf overhead when all that is needed is a decimal integer and optional right-padding. There is a nice side-benefit in simplified control flow for the non-blob case. The patch is uncontroversial, from a contributor known for precisely this kind of tidy, data-driven cleanup, and looks ready to apply.
