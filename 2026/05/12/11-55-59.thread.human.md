This is a short email from René in the `strbuf_add_uint()` thread. He

benchmarked three implementation variants — his own on-stack buffer, a

version using `pager.c::decimal_width()`, and Jeff King's

`__builtin_clz`-based sketch — and found all produced effectively identical

timings (within ~1 ms on a `cat-file --batch-all-objects

--batch-check='%(objectsize:disk)'` workload). He ends with the observation

that repeatedly dividing by ten is not a bottleneck for smallish object

sizes. The thread remains closed: both author and reviewer have now

independently confirmed the performance equivalence of all variants on

different systems. No outstanding issues remain, and the series is ready

for maintainer pickup.
