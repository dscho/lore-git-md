René Scharfe sent a four-patch series adding a `strbuf_add_uint()` convenience function to replace four uses of the more expensive `strbuf_addf(... PRIuMAX ...)` in `cat-file`, `ls-files`, and `ls-tree`.  The change is a clear performance win (a 4% speedup on one benchmark) and a readability improvement, and received a thorough, approving review from Jeff King ("Peff").  The one design trade-off — whether to format digits into a temporary buffer and then copy, or precompute the digit count and format in place — was explored three ways across the thread (René's version, a memmove variant, and Peff's `decimal_length_u64()` sketch), and all three were benchmarked as equivalent.  Peff explicitly endorsed the series and dismissed his own alternatives as over-engineering.  No changes were requested; no v2 is expected.  The series is ready for maintainer pickup.
