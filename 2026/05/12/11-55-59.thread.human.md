This thread is about a small, uncontroversial refactoring series from René
Scharfe that replaces four `strbuf_addf(sb, "%"PRIuMAX, (uintmax_t)value)`
calls with a new `strbuf_add_uint()` convenience function. The idea is to
avoid the printf machinery for formatting unsigned integers, substituting a
simple loop of division and modulo operations. No user-visible behavior
changes, no test changes. The affected commands are `cat-file`, `ls-files`,
and `ls-tree` -- all cases where millions of formatted lines may be produced.

The only potential review question is the naming convention, but this is
exactly the kind of tidy, low-risk plumbing optimization René is known for,
and the series is expected to land quickly. No open issues or disagreements.
