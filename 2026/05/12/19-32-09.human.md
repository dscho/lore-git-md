This is a follow-up from René Scharfe to Jeff King's review of patch 1/4 in the `strbuf_add_uint()` series.

René shares the implementation decision he wrestled with: should the digits be written directly into the strbuf buffer (possibly requiring a `memmove()`), or into a small on-stack temporary then `memcpy`'d in? He actually benchmarked all three approaches. On Apple silicon the no-copy (write-directly-into-strbuf) variant won; on an AMD Ryzen it lost slightly. The stack-buffer approach he settled on was the solid middle ground, and he's satisfied with it. He notes that a faster `decimal_width()` helper might shift the results, but doesn't think the juice is worth the squeeze.

This is a transparent, data-driven explanation from the author about an internal implementation detail. It confirms the review's conclusion -- the chosen approach is fine -- and shows that René already considered and benchmarked the alternatives Peff mused about. No open questions remain; the patch is ready.
