Bugfix (patch 4 of 9 in the pseudo-merge bitmap bugfix series).

This patch fixes a swapped binary-search comparator in `pseudo_merge_at()` that caused lookups for pseudo-merges to search in the wrong direction when more than one pseudo-merge group existed. With a single group the bug was masked, but with multiple groups the inverted `lo`/`hi` assignments caused searches to miss valid pseudo-merge entries and silently fall back to non-pseudo-merge behavior during fill-in traversal. The fix is a two-line swap in `pseudo-merge.c` that restores correct binary search logic: when the midpoint's offset is less than the target, search right (`lo = mi + 1`); when greater, search left (`hi = mi`).

The corresponding test -- `apply pseudo-merges from multiple groups during fill-in` -- is promoted from `test_expect_failure` to `test_expect_success`, meaning this is the patch that fixes one of the broken scenarios introduced in the series' test-expansion patch. A single test now passes that previously did not.

This is a small, mechanical fix to a genuine logic error. The commit message is clear about the bug's cause, its masking in the single-group case, and its practical consequence. No remaining technical issues. With the maintainer already signaling readiness to merge the series, this patch is uncontroversial and will land without further discussion.
