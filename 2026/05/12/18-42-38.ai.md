Classification: refactoring, minor performance optimization. This is a review reply from Jeff King engaging with René Scharfe's first patch in the series.

The review addresses the `strbuf_add_uint()` implementation's design tradeoff: René prepares the decimal digits in a small on-stack buffer and then appends the result to the strbuf with a `memcpy()`. Peff notes the obvious question this raises -- why not format directly into a pre-grown strbuf? -- and answers it himself: formatting right-to-left is simpler, and computing the digit count up front (to know where to start writing) would require a log-10 calculation with off-by-one pitfalls. He concludes the extra copy is probably fine.

This is a substantive review despite its brevity: Peff identifies the one genuinely interesting implementation decision in the patch, confirms that the chosen approach is reasonable, and does not suggest an alternative. There is no objection, no request for changes, and no criticism of the series as a whole. The only implied question -- "have you considered formatting in place?" -- is resolved by Peff's own reasoning in the same paragraph.
