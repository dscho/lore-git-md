remote group push support for `git push`

This is a review reply from Kristoffer Haugsbakk on patch 3/3 of Usman
Akinyemi's series adding remote group support to `git push`. The series
has already gone through five rounds of review and is functionally
complete -- this email is a final clean-up pass on the documentation
portion of the patch.

The review is at the surface level: it catches two instances of stray
double-backticks in the documentation and suggests surrounding a
remote name (`r1`) with backticks for consistency with how Git's
man pages typically format command-line arguments. Both are minor
formatting nits in the AsciiDoc source.

There is nothing here that changes the design or functionality of
the patch. The series appears ready to merge once these small
doc fixes are applied.
