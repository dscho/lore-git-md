Harald Nordgren's patch to improve `git status` advice for push-branch tracking has reached its final form after four iterations of review. The fix ensures that when a local branch tracks different remotes for upstream versus push operations (a common triangular workflow), the pull advice correctly references the push remote rather than defaulting to the upstream remote. 

The v4 patch addresses all edge cases identified during review, including a final refinement to suppress advice entirely when push refs cannot be cleanly parsed - such as with unconventional refspecs. Junio Hamano, who provided thorough review feedback throughout the process, has now approved the changes as correct and well-tested. The test suite covers five distinct scenarios, verifying both the qualified pull advice in triangular workflows and the graceful handling of malformed refs.

With all feedback addressed and no outstanding issues, the patch is ready for inclusion in the `next` integration branch. This concludes a focused effort to align `git status` advice with actual push tracking behavior, particularly benefiting users of complex remote configurations.
