The thread concerns a bugfix patch from Miklos Vajna addressing an inconsistency in `git log --follow` when tracking files across subtree merges. While `git blame --follow` correctly follows such files through their pre-merge history in the inner repository, `git log --follow` currently stops at the merge point. The fix mirrors blame's implementation by checking each merge parent for the file under different paths.

The patch (now at v2) includes a test case and touches only `log-tree.c`, making it a focused, low-risk change. Despite being technically sound and having clear precedent, the initial submission received no replies. Miklos has now politely pinged Junio Hamano for review while acknowledging the maintainer's backlog, indicating the patch is ready but awaiting attention. Given the lack of objections and the straightforward nature of the fix, this appears merge-ready once reviewed.
