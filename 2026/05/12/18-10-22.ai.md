This is a **bugfix** patch, part 3 of a 4-patch series. The thread is complete and all four patches have been technically approved.

The patch adds defensive `BUG()` guards in xdiff's `xdl_get_hunk()` to crash if a negative context length is ever passed. The commit message explains clearly why xdiff's fields cannot themselves be changed to unsigned -- the context-window arithmetic uses signed subtraction (e.g., `XDL_MAX(xch->i1 - xecfg->ctxlen, 0)`) that would break under unsigned arithmetic. So instead of changing types in xdiff, the series already fixed the root cause (patches 1 and 2) by rejecting negative values at the option-parsing layer. This third patch is defense-in-depth: a safety net in case a future caller bypasses the normal option-parsing path or a new caller is added that does not validate.

The implementation is clean and minimal. The two `BUG()` calls are placed at the top of `xdl_get_hunk()`, which is the single entry point called by both `xdl_emit_diff()` and `xdl_call_hunk_func()`, so one guard covers all xdiff consumers. The diff itself mechanically moves the existing `max_common` and `max_ignorable` computations after the new checks. No surprises.

This is the kind of straightforward, low-risk patch that the project handles efficiently. No open questions remain -- the series is ready for merging.
