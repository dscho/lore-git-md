This is a community announcement about Google Summer of Code (GSoC) participation. Kaartic Sivaraam shares the list of accepted contributors and their projects for Git's 2026 GSoC program.

Four contributors were selected with projects spanning different areas: K Jayatheerth will work on improving the new `git repo` command, Pablo Sabater on extending `git cat-file`'s remote-object-info functionality, Siddharth Shrimali on partial clone disk space recovery, and Tian Yuchen (an emerging reviewer who has contributed substantive technical feedback in the past) on reducing Git's global state. Each project has assigned mentors, including some former GSoC participants who are now mentoring.

The announcement expresses gratitude to all applicants and mentors, noting the difficulty in selecting from many qualified candidates. This continues Git's tradition of participating in GSoC under Christian Couder's coordination, with projects typically producing meaningful contributions while introducing new developers to the project's workflow. The selected projects align with ongoing efforts like global state reduction (related to the `the_repository` removal work) and partial clone improvements.
