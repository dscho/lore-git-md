This is a maintainer nitpick on the `git url-parse` series cover letter. Junio Hamano points out a minor wording issue in the justification for the new command. The cover letter claimed the command would let scripts avoid shelling out, but Junio notes they'd still need to shell out to use this new plumbing command - it just replaces one shell-out with another rather than eliminating them entirely.

The observation doesn't challenge the technical merits of the series (which appears complete with thorough testing and documentation after three iterations), but suggests the benefits might be framed more accurately. This is a surface-level comment about phrasing rather than a substantive technical concern. The series itself remains well-positioned for merging with all prior feedback addressed, including the Windows compatibility fixes in v3.
