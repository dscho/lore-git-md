Dominik Loidolt's series modernizing compiler compatibility macros in `compat/posix.h` has been approved by Junio C Hamano for inclusion in the `next` branch. The three-patch series improves Clang's handling of the UNUSED attribute while maintaining cross-platform compatibility, with all changes being compile-time only and thus low risk. 

The series went through multiple iterations, with Patrick Steinhardt providing substantive technical review that verified the behavioral equivalence of the changes. The final version separates style cleanups into a dedicated patch, fixes preprocessor indentation to match Git guidelines, and simplifies GCC version comparison logic. 

With all feedback addressed and no outstanding technical concerns, this represents a straightforward quality-of-life improvement for Clang users that exemplifies Git's careful approach to modernization. The patches will be part of the next integration cycle.
