This is an announcement about Google Summer of Code (GSoC) participation, welcoming the 2026 cohort of student contributors to the Git project. 

The email lists four accepted students and their projects: K Jayatheerth working on improving `git repo` with mentors Justin Tobler and Lucas Oshiro; Pablo Sabater extending `git cat-file`'s remote-object-info functionality with Karthik Nayak and Chandra Pratap; Siddharth Shrimali focusing on partial clone disk space recovery with Christian Couder and Siddharth Asthana; and Tian Yuchen (a known emerging reviewer) tackling global state reduction with Christian Couder and Ayush Chandekar.

The message also thanks all applicants and mentors, noting that some current mentors (Ayush, Lucas, and Chandra) are former GSoC participants themselves. This reflects Git's established tradition of growing contributors into leadership roles through programs like GSoC. The projects align with ongoing Git efforts - particularly Tian's work on reducing global state, which connects to the long-running `the_repository` removal initiative.
