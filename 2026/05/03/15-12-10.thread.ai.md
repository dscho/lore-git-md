Platform compatibility patch modifying the `UNUSED` macro in `compat/posix.h` to improve Clang's unused parameter warnings. Currently Clang identifies as GCC 4.2.1 for compatibility, resulting in less informative warnings. The change makes Clang use the same warning format as GCC 4.5+, showing the full "parameter declared as UNUSED" message (supported since Clang 2.9 in 2011). Effectively drops support for pre-2.9 Clang versions, which are 15+ years old. The change is minimal and focused, maintaining GCC behavior while improving Clang diagnostics.
