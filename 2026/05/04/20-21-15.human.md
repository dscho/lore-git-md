This is part 6 of Derrick Stolee's performance optimization series (v2) for `git pack-objects` and related commands, focusing on improving sparse-checkout handling. The patch introduces finer control over tree pruning behavior in the path-walk API.

The key change adds a `pl_sparse_trees` flag to control whether sparse patterns should prune both trees and blobs (current behavior) or just blobs (new option). This prepares for future `sparse:<oid>` filter support that needs to retain all trees while filtering blobs. The patch also fixes a subtle SEEN flag timing issue where identical objects at multiple paths could be incorrectly skipped.

The implementation includes:
- New flag in `path_walk_info` to toggle tree pruning
- Updated `add_tree_entries()` logic to defer SEEN marking until after path checks
- Test helper and `git backfill` integration
- New test case verifying the no-tree-pruning behavior
