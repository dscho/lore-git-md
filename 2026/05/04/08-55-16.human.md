Compiler warning fix follow-up for `name-rev`.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk acknowledges Junio C Hamano's suggested alternatives for fixing the uninitialized variable warning in `builtin/name-rev.c`. The warning, which only appears in DEVELOPER=1 builds, was originally identified by Ramsay Jones and fixed with an explicit NULL initialization. Junio proposed two more structural approaches that could eliminate the variable entirely by restructuring the control flow or casting directly in the function call.

Kristoffer indicates he will incorporate both of Junio's suggested fixes as squashed commits in the next version of the patch. This continues the cooperative thread where multiple valid solutions were proposed, with the author now opting for the more comprehensive structural improvements suggested by the maintainer. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, addressing a compiler warning without changing observable behavior.
