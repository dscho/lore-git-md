Test fix for filesystem-dependent ls -l output in MIDX tests.

Jörg Thalheim identifies a spurious test failure that occurs on copy-on-write or compressing filesystems like ZFS. The test in t7703-repack-geometric.sh compares full `ls -l` output to verify MIDX retention, but the "total N" line in `ls -l` can vary between invocations even when directory contents are identical. This causes the test to fail unnecessarily on certain filesystems.

The patch modifies two test cases to strip the first line of `ls -l` output before comparison, making the test more robust across different filesystems. The change is minimal and clearly motivated, addressing a real-world portability issue while maintaining the test's original intent to verify MIDX behavior.

This appears to be a straightforward and well-justified improvement to the test suite's reliability. The commit message references the specific commit (6ce9d558ce) that introduced the problematic test behavior, showing good attention to tracking down the root cause.
