This is a follow-up to the `git clone --dissociate` bugfix discussion, where Jeff King (Peff) identifies an alternative workaround by moving the dissociation step later in the clone process. The patch shows how delaying the `odb_close()` call until after checkout would prevent the commit-graph race condition, since no further object access would occur after dissociation.

Peff notes this is merely masking the underlying issue rather than fixing it properly - the race condition would still exist for other `odb_close()` callers. The proposed solution from the parent email (making `repo_get_commit_tree()` more resilient) remains the more robust approach, as it addresses the root cause rather than just avoiding the problematic timing.

This reinforces that the core issue stems from commit-graph cached objects becoming invalidated while still being needed during checkout. The thread demonstrates careful analysis of both the immediate symptoms and broader implications for object store lifecycle management.
