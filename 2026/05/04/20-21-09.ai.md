This is a performance optimization series from Derrick Stolee (with contributions from Taylor Blau) that integrates `--path-walk` with various `--filter` options in `git pack-objects`. The series builds on previous path-walk work and represents version 2 of the effort.

The key technical contribution enables `--path-walk` to work with three filter types:
1. `blob:none` - skips blobs during the path walk
2. `blob:limit=<size>` - filters out large blobs
3. `sparse:<oid>` - validates paths against sparse-checkout patterns

The most significant performance gains come from sparse filter integration, where checking patterns against entire path groups proves much faster than per-blob checks. The cover letter includes detailed benchmarks across several repositories showing both time and space improvements, with particularly strong results for the `sparse:<oid>` case (e.g., git/git goes from 72.53s to 29.00s).

The series also introduces infrastructure for future work, including a proposed `treesparse:<oid>` filter that would leverage cone-mode sparse-checkout patterns. Changes touch core path-walk logic and add new test coverage, including a new performance test script.

Version 2 incorporates feedback from the initial submission, rebasing onto en/backfill-fixes-and-edges and adding three patches to extend more filter options (`tree:0`, `object:type`, and `combine`). The implementation appears thorough with careful attention to both correctness and comprehensive test coverage, though the interaction between path-walk's tree pruning and sparse filters may warrant review from sparse-checkout experts.
