The thread continues to refine the fix for repository corruption caused by concurrent `git maintenance run --detach` operations. Jeff King (Peff), building on Jacob Keller's acknowledgment that a race between parent and child over tempfile ownership is tolerable, proposes a concrete improvement: have the parent assign ownership to "0" (no owner) before `fork()`, then reclaim ownership if the fork fails. This eliminates the risk of a double-delete — the worst remaining case is that nobody cleans up a lock if the child dies before taking control, which is unlikely and the least harmful outcome.

Peff's suggestion is a narrow, incremental refinement, not a reopening of the broader design debate. The architectural direction remains settled: the comprehensive tempfile-ownership fix should be baked into `daemonize()` itself, as Peff and Taylor Blau have converged on. The open question is still whether Derrick Stolee signs off on this fix-forward approach over a revert, and whether Junio Hamano weighs in on the release strategy.
