Taylor Blau's RFC series on making `--path-walk` work with reachability bitmaps and delta-islands continues to progress with strong technical validation from Derrick Stolee. The 7-part series, which addresses GitHub's infrastructure requirements while maintaining compatibility, has now received Stolee's approval on two key patches: the `tree:0` filter integration (patch 2) and the `object:type` filter support (patch 3). 

In his latest review, Stolee confirmed the technical correctness of how patch 3 handles tree-to-blob reachability through tags, specifically endorsing the changes to `setup_pending_objects()` and the revision walk adjustments. This builds on his earlier approval of the core approach in patch 2, where he noted the path-walk API's current limitations while accepting the implementation as sound. The collaboration between these two pack-objects experts has established a solid technical foundation for the series.

With Stolee's reviews addressing the most complex aspects of the integration, the remaining considerations focus on verifying mark propagation ordering, confirming depth recording consistency, and assessing large-repository performance. The series demonstrates that path-walk can indeed work with GitHub's required features while maintaining compatibility, with Stolee's forward-looking comments suggesting natural evolution points for the API.
