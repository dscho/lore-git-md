Taylor Blau has proposed an RFC series (7 patches) to make `--path-walk` work with reachability bitmaps and delta-islands, building on Derrick Stolee's earlier filter integration work. The goal is to support GitHub's infrastructure requirements by removing longstanding incompatibilities between these features. The series implements support for various filter types (`tree:0`, `object:type`, `combine`), integrates with bitmaps, and carefully handles delta-island mark propagation during path-walk traversal.

Derrick Stolee, whose work forms the foundation for this effort, has now reviewed the series. He supports reviewing both series together while the context is fresh, and specifically highlights the value of Taylor's new filter type implementations. Stolee notes he wasn't focusing on bitmap compatibility himself, making Taylor's contribution particularly welcome. The review focuses on the technical interactions between their work, with Stolee indicating he'll examine the delta-island integration carefully given its complexity. This exchange shows productive collaboration between two key contributors in the pack-objects subsystem, with technical alignment established and areas needing deeper review identified.
