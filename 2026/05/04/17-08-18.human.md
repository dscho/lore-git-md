This is the first patch in Johannes Schindelin's v2 series addressing Windows platform limitations with large objects (>4GB). The patch focuses on fixing truncation issues in `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` caused by 32-bit types on Windows.

The key change replaces `unsigned long` with `size_t` for object size tracking in both commands, ensuring proper handling of objects larger than 4GB. The patch also updates the shift arithmetic to operate in 64-bit space, preventing overflow during size decoding from packfiles.

The change is well-motivated by a clear platform limitation (Windows' 32-bit `unsigned long` even in 64-bit builds) and comes with a straightforward technical solution. The patch credits an anonymous contributor ("LordKiRon") for the original work while maintaining authorship under Schindelin's name as agreed.

This appears to be a solid foundation for the series, addressing a real issue that could cause corruption when handling large objects on Windows. The changes are focused and mechanical, with no apparent behavioral impact beyond fixing the size handling.
