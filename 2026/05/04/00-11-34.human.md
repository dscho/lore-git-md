This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's RFC series enabling `--path-walk` to work with delta-islands, completing the integration with GitHub's infrastructure requirements. The patch builds on earlier work that added support for various filters and bitmap integration with path-walk.

The key technical challenge addressed here is maintaining delta-island marks during path-walk traversal, which requires:
1. Propagating island marks from commits to their trees (matching the behavior of the regular traversal)
2. Recording tree depths during path-walk to ensure proper mark propagation order

The implementation carefully preserves the descendant-first commit ordering needed for correct island mark propagation, and uses the tree depth tracking helper from the previous patch to maintain consistency between traversal modes. The patch also removes the documented incompatibility between `--path-walk` and `--delta-islands` now that the integration is complete.

Test coverage in t5320 verifies the behavior works correctly with both regular repacks and bitmap-enabled repacks, ensuring cross-island deltas remain forbidden while intra-island deltas are allowed. The changes are relatively focused, touching mainly the path-walk callback logic in builtin/pack-objects.c while leaving the core delta-island machinery unchanged.

As the final piece of this RFC series, this patch appears technically sound and well-tested, though reviewers will likely want to scrutinize the performance implications of the island mark propagation during path-walk traversal, particularly in large repositories. The series as a whole represents a significant step forward in making path-walk a fully-featured alternative to the traditional object traversal.
