This is a refactoring patch that's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. It's the first in a 4-patch series to migrate the `trust_executable_bit` configuration from global to repository-scoped storage.

The patch removes redundant `extern` declarations for `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` from `st_mode_from_ce()` in read-cache.c, since these are already declared in environment.h which the file includes. This is a straightforward cleanup that doesn't change behavior, just eliminates duplicate declarations.

The patch comes with mentorship from Christian Couder and others, suggesting it's part of a guided effort to help newer contributors participate in the large-scale `the_repository` removal project. The change is small and mechanical, exactly the sort of incremental improvement that makes sense early in a refactoring series.
