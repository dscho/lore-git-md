This is a review of a refactoring patch in Tian Yuchen's series to migrate the `trust_executable_bit` configuration from global scope to repository-specific storage, as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

Christian Couder provides a surface-level review, catching a typo ("gloabl" -> "global") and suggesting a small documentation improvement to better connect this patch to the larger goal of eliminating `the_repository`. The review is light but constructive, focusing on clarity rather than technical concerns - appropriate given the mechanical nature of the changeset.

The patch itself (not shown in full here) modifies several helper functions in read-cache.c to take an explicit `index_state` parameter rather than relying on the implicit `the_repository` global. This is a straightforward but important step in the multi-year effort to remove Git's reliance on global state. The changes appear uncontroversial and well-scoped, with Christian's minor suggestions likely to be incorporated in a follow-up version.
