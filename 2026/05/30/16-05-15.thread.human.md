This thread is about the final step in migrating Git’s `trust_executable_bit` configuration from a global variable to repository-scoped storage, part of the broader effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global. Tian Yuchen’s three-patch series (now at v4) has been fully reviewed and is ready for merging. The key change moves the setting into `struct repo_config_values`, introduces a new getter function (`repo_trust_executable_bit()`), and updates all call sites while preserving existing behavior. Junio C Hamano has confirmed the series is complete, and reviewers—including Christian Couder, Patrick Steinhardt, and two mentees (Ayush Chandekar and Olamide Caleb Bello)—have signed off on the implementation.

The migration is purely mechanical, with no functional changes, but it establishes a pattern for future configuration migrations. The more complex `has_symlinks` migration was deferred due to implementation challenges, but this series demonstrates incremental progress. Earlier patches in the series laid groundwork by cleaning up redundant declarations and relocating helper functions, ensuring the final patch could focus on the core migration. The series touches `read-cache.c`, `environment.c/h`, and `apply.c`, and maintains performance by keeping eager parsing in hot paths. With all feedback addressed and Reviewed-by tags in place, this represents another step toward eliminating global state in Git’s codebase.
