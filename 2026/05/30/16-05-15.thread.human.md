This thread is about the final phase of migrating two core configuration flags—`trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks`—from global scope to repository-scoped storage as part of Git’s ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The work is split across a 4-patch series (v7) by Tian Yuchen, which builds on a prior iteration (v4) that was merged to `master` after a smooth review.

The series is now under review, with all substantive feedback addressed. The most recent hurdle was a build-breaking issue in patch 2/4, where an unused `repo` parameter in `ce_mode_from_stat()` triggered compiler warnings under `DEVELOPER=1`. SZEDER Gábor identified the problem and clarified that marking the parameter as `UNUSED` (and later removing the annotation when the parameter is used) is the correct fix, aligning with Git’s idioms for staged refactorings. This issue is now resolved.

Junio C Hamano’s latest review of patch 3/4 raised minor stylistic and defensive-programming questions—specifically, whether the new `repo_trust_executable_bit()` getter should defensively check for a NULL `repo` pointer or crash loudly to expose bugs. Tian agreed to Junio’s preference for the latter, removing the check and reformatting the ternary expression for clarity. The exchange was purely about consistency and did not challenge the patch’s correctness or design. The series remains uncontroversial and is likely to proceed to `next` once the final adjustments are applied.

Key participants include Tian (the author), Junio (who merged the earlier iteration and provided design feedback), and SZEDER Gábor (who caught the build-breaking issue). The migration pattern established here—unified getter functions and repository-scoped configuration—may serve as a template for future refactoring efforts. The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal project, which aims to eliminate implicit global state in Git’s codebase.
