This thread is about a four-patch series (v5) by Tian Yuchen that refactors Git’s `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags to be repository-scoped rather than global variables. The goal is to eliminate reliance on `the_repository` as part of the broader effort to remove this global state. The earlier v4 series, which migrated only `trust_executable_bit`, was merged to `master` after a smooth review, and this v5 iteration reintroduces the deferred `has_symlinks` migration in the final patch.

The series is now complete: the first two patches are preparatory cleanups (relocating a function and removing redundant declarations), the third migrates `trust_executable_bit`, and the fourth—just posted—migrates `has_symlinks` into the same repository-scoped storage. Both flags now use a unified getter pattern (`repo_has_symlinks()` and `repo_trust_executable_bit()`), with repository pointer validation and fallback to `the_repository` where needed. The mingw compatibility layer has been adjusted, though the author notes this as a pragmatic but potentially suboptimal solution for early platform-specific overrides.

Review on v5 is still pending, but the v4 iteration received substantive feedback from established contributors like Christian Couder and Patrick Steinhardt. The series is purely mechanical, preserving existing behavior while establishing a pattern for future configuration migrations. The author’s questions about mingw compatibility and potential follow-ups (like reducing `istate` passing) suggest areas for further iteration, but the core work appears uncontroversial. No objections have been raised so far, and the series is under active review.
