This thread is about a four-patch series (now at v5) that refactors Git’s `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags to eliminate their reliance on global state, part of the broader effort to remove the `the_repository` global variable. The series was started by Tian Yuchen and builds on a prior v4 iteration that migrated only `trust_executable_bit` and was merged to `master` without objections.

The v5 series reintroduces the previously deferred `has_symlinks` migration, establishing a unified getter pattern for both flags and making them repository-scoped. The third patch in this series (the one just summarized) moves the `trust_executable_bit` variable into the `repo_config_values` struct and introduces a getter function, `repo_trust_executable_bit()`, to access it. This is a mechanical change with no behavioral impact, updating call sites in `apply.c`, `environment.c`, and `read-cache.c` to use the new getter while preserving existing logic. The patch also includes repository pointer validation for safety in multi-repository contexts.

The series has seen substantive review in its earlier iterations, with feedback from Junio C Hamano, Christian Couder, Patrick Steinhardt, and others. The v5 patches are now under active review, with the first two patches noted as uncontroversial. Open questions include whether the mingw compatibility adjustments could be simplified and how this migration pattern might inform future configuration refactoring efforts. The author has also flagged potential follow-ups, such as reducing `istate` passing, but these are deferred for now. No regressions or objections have been raised so far, and the series is on track to complete the final phase of this configuration migration.
