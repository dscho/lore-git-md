This thread is a 4-patch series (v6) by Tian Yuchen that refactors Git’s `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags to be repository-scoped, completing the `the_repository` removal effort for these index-related settings. The series builds on v4, which migrated only `trust_executable_bit` and was merged to `master` after a smooth 10-day review. The current v6 iteration reintroduces the `has_symlinks` migration (deferred from v4) and adopts Junio C Hamano’s proposed design for mingw compatibility, replacing a global fallback variable with a compile-time macro and platform-specific function.

The series is now in its final review phase, with one remaining design question: Junio has objected to patch 2/4’s relocation of `ce_mode_from_stat()` from `read-cache.h` to `read-cache.c`, arguing the function could remain `static inline` in the header if modified to accept a `struct repository *` parameter. Tian’s current approach prioritizes encapsulation, but Junio’s concern about potential performance impacts in hot paths may require reworking the patch. The rest of the series—including the mingw compatibility layer (patch 4/4)—is uncontested, though Junio has suggested a minor commit message tweak to better highlight the platform-specific override mechanism.

Key participants include Tian (author), Junio (maintainer), Christian Couder, Patrick Steinhardt, and mentors Ayush Chandekar and Olamide Caleb Bello. The migration pattern established in patches 1–3 is seen as a template for future configuration refactoring, and the series remains under active review pending resolution of the `ce_mode_from_stat()` design question. No further behavioral changes are expected, and the mingw adjustments are now settled.
