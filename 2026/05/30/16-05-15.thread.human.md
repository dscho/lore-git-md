This thread is about a 4-patch series (v5) by Tian Yuchen that refactors Git’s `trust_executable_bit` and `has_symlinks` configuration flags to be repository-scoped, completing the removal of these settings from global scope as part of the broader `the_repository` elimination effort. The previous version (v4) of the series—covering only `trust_executable_bit`—was merged to `master` after a smooth review, but v5 reintroduces the deferred `has_symlinks` migration with new getter functions and adjustments for mingw compatibility.

The series is now under active review, with no major technical objections raised so far. Christian Couder reviewed the final patch (4/4) and suggested a minor commit message tweak, while Junio C Hamano recently acknowledged the discussion with thanks but did not comment on the series’ merge status or address the open question about mingw compatibility. The author, Tian Yuchen, has incorporated prior feedback and added preparatory cleanups (patches 1/4 and 2/4) to streamline the migration, but the mingw fallback mechanism remains a point of uncertainty. The established pattern for repository-scoped configuration could serve as a template for future refactoring, though some limitations (like `repo_config_values()`’s dependency on `the_repository`) persist. For now, the series is still in review, with no indication it has moved to `next` or `master`.
