Here's the updated human-readable thread summary:

The thread discusses adding signature preservation to the new `git history` command, which currently drops commit signatures during its history-rewriting operations. Alix Brunet initiated the discussion by pointing out this inconsistency with `git rebase`, which preserves signatures via its `-S` flag.

Patrick Steinhardt, who leads the `git history` development, has responded acknowledging the validity of the feature request. He agrees signature preservation would be a good addition but notes it's not currently a priority for his own work on the command. Patrick has explicitly invited other contributors to implement this functionality, making clear that without volunteer effort, the feature may take considerable time to materialize.

The discussion remains open for anyone interested in picking up this work. The technical precedent exists in `git rebase -S`, but implementing similar functionality in `git history` would require understanding both the signature infrastructure and the command's unique implementation approach. This presents a clear opportunity for a contributor looking to make a meaningful improvement to an important new feature.
