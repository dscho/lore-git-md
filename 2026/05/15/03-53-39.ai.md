This is a brief follow-up discussion in the already-closed thread about René Scharfe's `strbuf_add_uint()` optimization series. Jeff King (Peff) responds to René's earlier empirical testing of `__builtin_add_overflow()` versus the existing `unsigned_add_overflows()` pattern in `strbuf_grow()`.

René had found that while x86-64 compilers generate identical code for both approaches, Clang on ARM64 fails to optimize away a redundant comparison with the current implementation. His benchmarks showed a 2% speedup on Apple M1 (ARM64) when using the intrinsic, though no difference with GCC 15.2.

Peff acknowledges the findings, noting he had assumed there might be optimization potential but that compilers are generally smarter than expected at handling these cases. The exchange remains purely conversational - neither contributor suggests any action or follow-up patches from this observation. The thread remains closed with the original `strbuf_add_uint()` series ready for maintainer pickup.
