This is a discussion about case-sensitivity issues with `HEAD` resolution in linked worktrees on macOS filesystems. Torsten Bögershausen responds to Alexander Sandström's bug report, taking a firm stance that lowercase `head` should not be considered valid at all, rather than treating this as a bug to fix.

The key points:
- Torsten argues that `head` (lowercase) is not a valid reference name and shouldn't be expected to work, regardless of filesystem case sensitivity
- He notes this is a known limitation of case-insensitive filesystems, with many similar reports over time
- Suggests the solution is either to avoid case-insensitive filesystems or document the limitation, rather than changing Git's behavior
- Does not engage with Alexander's specific proposal about normalizing or rejecting case variants of HEAD

The response effectively closes the discussion path Alexander proposed (fixing the behavior) in favor of treating this as user education/documentation issue. This aligns with D. Ben Knoble's earlier implication that the files backend isn't the right place to solve this. The thread appears to be converging on "won't fix" with the expectation that reftable will eventually make this moot.
