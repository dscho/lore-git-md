Junio's stance suggests the team has flexibility to improve this, but any change should be well-justified. This moves the discussion forward by framing the decision as evaluating what makes semantic sense for users, rather than just preserving the current implementation.

The thread context shows this is part of a larger effort to fix edge cases in date parsing, with patch 2/4 now at a decision point about whether to keep, change, or remove the "today" alias behavior.
