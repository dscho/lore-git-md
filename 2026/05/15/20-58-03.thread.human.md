The thread proposes making "today" resolve to midnight in Git's date parsing, primarily to fix the surprising behavior where `git log --since=today` could include future commits due to falling back to the current time. The RFC patch series, now at v2, adds a `date_today()` function to handle this case while preserving time-qualified expressions like "noon today". Junio C Hamano approved the approach after the author expanded documentation about the tradeoffs between `--since` and `--until` behavior.

Tuomas Ahola, who has been working on related date parsing improvements for terms like "noon" and "tea", confirmed that his changes integrate cleanly with this midnight-handling patch. He'll soon post an updated version of his series that builds on top of this change. With no outstanding technical objections and maintainer approval, this quality-of-life improvement appears ready for merging once the final coordinated patches are submitted. The solution addresses a real usability issue while maintaining consistency with Git's existing date handling patterns.
