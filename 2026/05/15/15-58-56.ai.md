This is a substantive review of a git-gui bugfix patch in the ongoing series addressing repository and worktree detection issues. Johannes Sixt (git-gui maintainer) provides technical feedback on the environment variable handling changes.

The patch introduces centralized functions to manage GIT_DIR/GIT_WORK_TREE environment variables, preventing empty GIT_WORK_TREE from causing fatal errors. Johannes agrees with the core approach but notes two improvements needed: (1) the `global env` declaration may now be redundant and removable, and (2) he reiterates his position from parallel discussion that GIT_WORK_TREE should not be set at all in modes requiring a worktree.

The review confirms the technical correctness of the centralized variable management while pointing toward further simplification opportunities. This continues the thread's pattern of maintainer-guided refinement, where architectural decisions are being made about how git-gui should interact with Git's environment variables in different repository states. The feedback is specific and actionable, focused on making the solution both functionally correct and idiomatically clean.
