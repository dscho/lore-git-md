This is the final version (v9) of Harald Nordgren's series adding `git branch --prune-merged` for safe local branch cleanup. The series has already addressed all technical feedback from Junio C Hamano, with v9 specifically removing the ability for `--force` to bypass the mergedness check - the last remaining objection. 

The core functionality remains: `--prune-merged` will delete local branches whose tips are reachable from their upstream remote-tracking branches, with multiple safeguards in place (not checked out, resolvable upstream, name protection for default branches, and a new per-branch `pruneMerged` config opt-out). The series also includes companion features like `--forked` to list branches tracking a remote and `--all-remotes` for bulk operations.

Harald's cover note humorously acknowledges the reduced power (and danger) of the command after removing the `--force` bypass capability. With all feedback incorporated and no outstanding objections, this series appears ready for inclusion in `next`. The separate `fetch.pruneLocalBranches` concept mentioned in previous iterations remains an unattended proposal outside this series.
