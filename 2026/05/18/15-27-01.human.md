His suggestion to support glob patterns (`origin/*`) for more precise control is a constructive alternative.

The review is substantive, engaging with the actual behavior and use cases rather than surface-level issues. Phillip doesn't request changes to the current patch (which is already approved for integration) but plants a seed for potential future refinement of the feature's interface.
