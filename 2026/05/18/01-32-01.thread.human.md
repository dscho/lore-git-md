Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX repacking series, which refactors Git's repack and MIDX code to enable more efficient maintenance of large repositories, has cleared its final review hurdle. Jeff King (Peff) has signed off on the minimal updates Taylor made in response to prior feedback, confirming the changes look good without raising any new technical concerns. This marks the series as fully reviewed and ready for integration into Git's main development branch. The feature will allow Git to update multi-pack indexes without requiring full all-into-one repacks, significantly reducing maintenance overhead for large repositories. With no outstanding objections, the series is now positioned for Junio Hamano to pick up in the next "What's cooking" cycle. This represents another step forward in Git's ongoing MIDX and repack machinery improvements, an area where both Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt have been active contributors.
