Junio Hamano's May 2026 "What's cooking" report shows active development across Git's performance, infrastructure, and documentation fronts. Several topics have recently graduated to 'master', including improvements to `git backfill`, ORT merge strategy optimizations, and HTTP proxy validation fixes. Major ongoing efforts like `the_repository` removal and ODB abstraction continue to progress under their respective maintainers.

Taylor indicates the changes were minimal and either version would be merge-ready, signaling this performance optimization for large repositories is likely to land in the next integration window. This represents another step in the broader MIDX/repack machinery improvements that Patrick Steinhardt and Taylor have been driving.
