This is the v5 cover letter for Derrick Stolee's 8-patch series introducing new fetch/push negotiation controls. The series is now in its final form after thorough review, with all major feedback addressed and only minor cleanup remaining before submission to the maintainer.

The core feature adds two complementary negotiation controls: `--negotiation-include` (to guarantee certain refs are always advertised) and `--negotiation-restrict` (to limit which refs are considered). These solve a real problem in monorepos where long-lived branches like 'release' were being dropped from negotiation, causing massive unnecessary downloads. The series also adds corresponding config options (`remote.*.negotiationInclude` and `remote.*.negotiationRestrict`).

Key improvements in v5 are minor cleanups from Matthew Cheetham's review of v4, including:
- Adding Reviewed-by tags
- Fixing a parameter name in the `add_negotiation_tips()` helper
- Clarifying documentation about push negotiation behavior

The series is well-tested and addresses all technical concerns raised during review. The `have_sent()` negotiator interface (added in v4) provides a clean abstraction for tracking advertised commits. With this final polish, the series appears ready for integration into Git's development branches.
