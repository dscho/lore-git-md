This is a documentation discussion thread about clarifying the behavior of `git diff --word-diff` when handling whitespace changes. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) weighs in on Michael Montalbo's proposed documentation update.

Hannes questions whether explaining the internal algorithm (as Michael initially proposed) actually helps users understand the behavior they're seeing. He argues that the name "--word-diff" is already descriptive enough, and that documenting implementation details might unnecessarily constrain future changes to the algorithm. 

The key point Hannes makes is that we should be careful about what we document - technical implementation details may not be the most helpful for users, and could limit our ability to improve the algorithm later. He suggests the current approach of deferring to --word-diff-regex for technical details may be sufficient.

This continues the thread's theme of balancing between explaining observed behavior versus prescribing implementation. Hannes' perspective leans toward minimal documentation that doesn't over-define the algorithm, while still wanting users to understand what to expect from the command. The discussion remains open about how best to document this edge case without being either too vague or too prescriptive.
