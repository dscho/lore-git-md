This is patch 3/8 in Derrick Stolee's series introducing new negotiation options for fetch/push. The series is now in its final form after review, with all patches carrying Matthew Cheetham's Reviewed-by tags.

This particular patch performs a mechanical rename of internal variables and functions from `negotiation_tips` to `negotiation_restrict_tips` to better reflect the purpose of the `--negotiation-restrict` option (which replaces the older `--negotiation-tip`). The changes are straightforward and consistent across the transport layer, affecting:

1. The string_list storing CLI/config inputs in builtin/fetch.c
2. The oid_array in transport.h that tracks which refs to restrict negotiation to
3. Related function names and parameters throughout fetch-pack.c and transport.c

The rename maintains all existing functionality while making the code's intent clearer - these tips are specifically for restricting negotiation scope, not general negotiation. The patch follows the series' pattern of keeping the old `--negotiation-tip` name as an alias for backward compatibility while using the new terminology internally.

As with the rest of the series, this change has been thoroughly reviewed and is ready for maintainer consideration. It's a clean refactoring that improves code clarity without changing behavior.
