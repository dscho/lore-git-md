- Moves the `worktree_initialized` flag into `struct repository` from being a static global
- Updates all callers to pass `the_repository` explicitly
- Maintains the same safety check to prevent re-initializing with a different worktree path
- Touches 5 files with 17 insertions and 18 deletions

The changes are mechanical and follow the same pattern as previous patches in the series. The patch continues the thread-safe approach by moving state into the repository struct rather than keeping it in static variables. This is another incremental step in the larger effort to eliminate global state from Git's setup machinery. The patch appears straightforward and builds on the solid foundation established by earlier patches in the series.
