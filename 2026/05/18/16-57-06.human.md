This is a review of patch 5/8 in Derrick Stolee's series adding two-directional fetch controls for monorepo scenarios. The patch introduces a new `have_sent()` callback to the negotiator interface, addressing a key abstraction concern from the previous version.

Matthew Cheetham provides a **substantive review**, thoroughly analyzing the implementation across all three negotiator backends (default, noop, and skipping). He confirms the new callback properly marks commits as common and propagates this status to ancestors, validating the technical correctness of the approach. The review notes this is a cleaner solution than the v3 approach that overloaded the COMMON bit.

The patch itself is small (32 lines) and focused, cleanly separating the interface addition from the feature that will use it in patch 6/8. Cheetham gives the patch his LGTM (looks good to me), indicating it meets the quality bar for inclusion. With this approval, patches 1-5/8 of the series now have reviewer sign-off, leaving only the final three patches awaiting review.
