This is patch 3/8 in Derrick Stolee's series to improve fetch/push negotiation in monorepos. The patch performs a mechanical rename of internal variables and functions from `negotiation_tips` to `negotiation_restrict_tips` to better reflect the option's purpose (now called `--negotiation-restrict`).

The change is straightforward and touches several files to maintain consistency across the codebase. It follows the previous patch that introduced the new `--negotiation-restrict` CLI name while keeping `--negotiation-tip` as an alias. The rename helps distinguish this negotiation control from the new `--negotiation-include` option being introduced later in the series.

Matthew Cheetham has reviewed and signed off on the change, confirming it aligns with the series' technical approach. The patch is part of the infrastructure needed to support the bidirectional negotiation controls that will help optimize object transfers in large repositories.
