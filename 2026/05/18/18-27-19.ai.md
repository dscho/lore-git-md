This is a type safety fix in the ongoing remote group push feature series. The patch addresses sign-compare warnings in the push_cas_option struct by changing the `nr` and `alloc` fields from `int` to `size_t` to match their usage for tracking array sizes. 

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial - it was suggested by Junio Hamano in earlier iterations of the series to resolve compiler warnings. The patch doesn't change any behavior, just makes the type declarations match how the fields are actually used (for size-based comparisons and allocations). 

This was the first patch in the v6 series and remains unchanged from previous versions, with the later patches containing the actual remote group push functionality and documentation updates. The series as a whole appears ready for merging after six iterations of review and refinement.
