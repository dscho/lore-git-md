This is a follow-up to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" report regarding Mirko Faina's proposed `--max-count-oldest` option for `git rev-list`. The patch series introduces functionality to select the oldest N commits in a range rather than the default newest N.

Mirko responds that they've identified a bug when the new `--max-count-oldest` option is used in combination with `--boundary`. They indicate a v8 of the series will be forthcoming to address this issue. The bug wasn't caught in initial testing but surfaced during the review period.

This appears to be a normal iteration in the patch refinement process - the core functionality seems sound but needs edge case handling. The author is actively engaged in fixing the issue before the patch moves to the 'next' integration branch. The interaction between traversal options like `--boundary` and the new oldest-first ordering needs careful handling to maintain consistent behavior.
