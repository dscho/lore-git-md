The three `GIT_TEST_PACK_PATH_WALK=0` workarounds from patch 01/13 in `t5620-backfill.sh` are reverted, confirming the integration works. Performance data: 16% smaller packfiles for blobless packs in the Git repo, at the cost of about 70% more CPU time.
