This is a cover letter for a 7-patch series from Derrick Stolee that integrates `--path-walk` with various `--filter` options in `git pack-objects`. The series represents a performance optimization effort, particularly for sparse-checkout scenarios.

The key technical contribution is enabling `--path-walk` to work with three filter types:
1. `blob:none` - skips blobs during the path walk
2. `blob:limit=<size>` - filters out large blobs
3. `sparse:<oid>` - validates paths against sparse-checkout patterns

The most significant performance gains come from the sparse filter integration, where checking patterns against entire path groups proves much faster than per-blob checks. The cover letter includes detailed benchmarks across several repositories showing both time and space improvements, with particularly strong results for the sparse:<oid> case (e.g., git/git goes from 72.53s to 29.00s).

The series also introduces infrastructure for future work, including a proposed `treesparse:<oid>` filter that would leverage cone-mode sparse-checkout patterns, though this isn't implemented yet. The changes touch core path-walk logic and add new test coverage, including a new performance test script.

This appears to be a well-motivated optimization from a known for work in the pack and sparse-checkout subsystems. The performance data is compelling and the implementation looks thorough, though the interaction between path-walk's tree pruning and sparse filters may warrant close review from sparse-checkout experts like Elijah Newren. The series builds on previous path-walk work and maintains backward compatibility where needed.
