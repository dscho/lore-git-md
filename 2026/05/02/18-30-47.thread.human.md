The Git Rev News newsletter team has published edition 134, summarizing recent Git development activity for those who don't follow the mailing list closely. This volunteer-run digest, contributed to by Meet Soni, Toon Claes and Paulo Gomes under Christian Couder's coordination, provides highlights from technical discussions, notable patches, and release announcements. The team invites community participation for the next edition through issue #844, continuing their effort to make the high-traffic Git mailing list more approachable.
