This is a performance optimization series from Derrick Stolee that integrates `git pack-objects`'s `--path-walk` feature with various `--filter` options. The key improvements allow path-walking to work with blobless filters (`blob:none` and `blob:limit`), and most notably provides a significant speedup for sparse-checkout filters by checking patterns against entire paths at once rather than per-blob.

The cover letter provides detailed performance measurements across several repositories (git.git, nodejs/node, microsoft/fluentui, and microsoftdocs/azure-devops-docs) showing:
- Space savings from path-walking (220MB vs 250MB for git.git without filters)
- Dramatic speed improvements for sparse filters (29s vs 72s for git.git)
- Some cases where path-walking is slower with minimal space benefit (azure-devops-docs)

The series builds on Stolee's previous path-walk work, addressing the limitation where filters would disable path-walking entirely. The implementation requires careful handling of object visibility during the walk to properly support sparse filters. A future direction mentioned is adding `treesparse:<oid>` filtering to leverage cone-mode sparse patterns.

This appears to be a well-motivated optimization with clear performance wins, particularly for sparse-checkout workflows. The patch series includes comprehensive tests and benchmarks. Given Stolee's expertise in this area and the demonstrated benefits, this seems likely to be merged after review.
