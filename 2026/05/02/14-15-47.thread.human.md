Derrick Stolee's `--path-walk` and `--filter` integration series is in its fourth iteration, with all 13 patches now posted. The core goal is to ensure full interoperability between these features by moving filter logic into the path-walk API. A significant bug from v3 involving dropped tagged tree objects was fixed in patch 11, with new test coverage added in patch 10.

While Stolee maintains his design is memory-efficient and collision-safe, Blau worries about interface brittleness. Their exchange remains technical and constructive, with Blau having signed off on v3 despite this ongoing concern.
