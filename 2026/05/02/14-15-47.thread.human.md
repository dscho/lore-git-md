Derrick Stolee has completed his 7-patch series optimizing `git pack-objects` with `--path-walk` and sparse filters, specifically targeting performance improvements for sparse-checkout scenarios. The final patch integrates the `sparse:<oid>` filter with path-walk traversal, using cone-mode sparse-checkout definitions to efficiently skip blobs outside the sparse scope while retaining all reachable trees. This avoids quadratic behavior when checking objects against sparse patterns.

The implementation carefully handles edge cases where the same tree appears both inside and outside the sparse cone, ensuring blobs are properly discovered when re-walking trees at in-cone paths. Benchmarks show significant gains: 60% faster execution and 14% smaller packs compared to standard traversal. The series now provides three optimized filter types (`blob:none`, `blob:limit`, and `sparse:<oid>`) with documented performance characteristics.
