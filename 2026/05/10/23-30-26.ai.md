This is a follow-up to the terminal output corruption bugfix in sideband.c, where René Scharfe proposed moving the ANSI clear sequence to fix character truncation in full-width terminal lines. Junio C Hamano (the Git maintainer) is now weighing in with his review.

Junio concurs with René's approach, noting that the key requirement is ensuring previous line remnants are cleared, and that clearing before printing (rather than after) provides more predictable behavior across different terminal implementations. His comment suggests this is a conceptually simpler solution that avoids edge cases around terminal behavior at line boundaries.

The review is substantive, engaging with the technical rationale rather than just rubber-stamping. Junio's approval carries significant weight as maintainer, indicating this change is likely to be merged soon. While Mikael Magnusson's earlier concerns about broader terminal attribute handling remain valid, this specific fix appears uncontroversial and well-scoped.

The patch remains focused on the original reported issue (character truncation) while leaving other terminal behavior questions for potential future work. Junio's endorsement suggests this will likely graduate from "seen" to "next" in the integration branches soon.
