Documentation patch refining the `--follow`/`--no-follow` documentation for `git log`.

Junio Hamano provides a review of Tamir Duberstein's v2 patch documenting the `--no-follow` option. While generally supportive of documenting this long-standing feature, Junio questions whether describing `--follow` as working with "a single pathspec" (as the patch does) is technically accurate than the original "single file" wording. He explains that the rename-tracking mechanism specifically follows individual files across renames, not broader pathspec patterns.

The review suggests the commit subject line could be clearer by avoiding a double colon and proposes an alternative phrasing. Junio also notes the conventional documentation style of listing both `--foo` and `--no-foo` variants, which this patch correctly follows.

This is a substantive review that engages with the technical details of the documentation, particularly around the rename-tracking behavior. The feedback indicates the patch is on the right track but may need minor wording adjustments to precisely match Git's actual behavior. The discussion remains focused on documentation accuracy rather than introducing new functionality.
