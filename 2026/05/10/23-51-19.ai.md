This is the final iteration of a documentation patch series clarifying the behavior of `git log --follow` and `--no-follow`. The discussion has reached consensus on how to accurately describe these options' limitations, particularly around rename tracking.

The key points being documented are:
- `--follow` only works with a single filename (not general pathspecs)
- It fundamentally cannot track renames across merge commits due to implementation limitations
- Directory pathspecs affect traversal but don't enable rename tracking
- `--no-follow` overrides both command-line and config-based `log.follow`

The exchange demonstrates Git's philosophy of documenting actual behavior rather than aspirational features, even when that means being explicit about limitations.
