Maintainer feedback on a documentation typo-fix patch. Junio C Hamano (the Git maintainer) responds to Andrew Kreimer's patch that fixes various typos across the codebase using `codespell`. While acknowledging the value of the fixes, Junio notes two main issues:

1. The patch is too broad, touching too many files at once (29 files across different subsystems) to be reviewed effectively in one go. He suggests splitting it into smaller, more focused patches.

2. Some of the files (particularly in git-gui/ and po/) come from separately maintained trees and should be handled as separate patches. He specifically questions whether the translation files (po/el.po and po/ko.po) should even be updated, noting they haven't been touched since 2019 and may no longer be actively maintained.

Junio confirms he reviewed about half the changes before running out of time, finding the corrections valid, and hopes others can review the remainder. The feedback is constructive but firm - while the typo fixes themselves are good, the patch needs restructuring to follow Git's contribution norms before it can be accepted.
