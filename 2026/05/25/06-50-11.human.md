This is a follow-up in the commit-reach optimization thread, where Kristofer Karlsson responds to Derrick Stolee's review about flag bit usage in the commit-reach.c optimizations.

Kristofer reports three inaccuracies in the flag allocation documentation while auditing the code:
1. The renamed object-name.c (formerly sha1-name.c) still appears under its old name
2. Builtin/show-branch.c's flag usage is incorrectly documented as bits 0-26 when it actually uses 0 and 2-28

Additionally, he raises the broader question of whether there's interest in more systematic flag management (runtime registration, static analysis) to replace the current manual tracking approach, mentioning he has some experimental work in this area.

The email continues the thread's focus on cross-subsystem coordination while introducing a potential new direction for improving how flag bits are managed across Git's codebase. Kristofer's attention to documentation accuracy and suggestion for more robust flag management shows the optimization work is being approached with care for both immediate correctness and long-term maintainability.
