This is a substantive review from Derrick Stolee on Kristofer Karlsson's performance optimization series for commit-reach.c. Stolee suggests restructuring the approach to better encapsulate the queue management logic.

The review proposes creating a new wrapper struct that would contain both the prio_queue and the nonstale_count, along with methods to manage the ENQUEUED and STALE flags. This would:
1. Keep the counter and queue together logically
2. Centralize flag management
3. Potentially simplify callers by handling duplicate prevention internally

Stolee outlines a 3-step migration path to introduce this change while maintaining the optimization's benefits. The suggestion reflects his deep knowledge of Git's graph algorithms and data structures, though it would require significant rework of the current patch series.

The review doesn't block the current approach but offers an alternative design that might yield cleaner code organization. Stolee acknowledges the merit of the original idea while flagging potential improvements in data structure encapsulation. This feedback will likely lead to a v3 of the series incorporating some form of the suggested wrapper struct.
