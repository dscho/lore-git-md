This is a follow-up in the `worktree:` condition patch series, now at its fourth iteration. The series aims to extend Git's conditional config inclusion with `worktree:<pattern>` and `worktree/i:<pattern>` conditions that match against the working tree's checkout path.

The latest email from Chen Linxuan acknowledges a Windows CI failure that Junio C Hamano surfaced in the test run (job 77639885088). The failure appears to be related to the `includeIf.worktree:/.path` case not working as expected on Windows. This validates Patrick Steinhardt's earlier concern about case-sensitivity handling in the feature.

Chen indicates they will address this in v5 of the patch series. The issue appears to be a blocking one that needs resolution before the feature can proceed, though the exact nature of the Windows-specific failure isn't detailed beyond the path matching problem. Given Windows' case-insensitive filesystem by default, this was an anticipated area needing special handling.
