This is the second patch in a 3-part series refactoring how `git log -L` (line-log) integrates with Git's diff output pipeline. The patch focuses on routing line-log output through Git's standard `log_tree_diff()` pipeline rather than taking a separate shortcut.

The key changes in this patch:
- Renames `line_log_print()` to `line_log_queue_pairs()` and simplifies it to just queue pre-computed filepairs
- Makes line-log processing flow through `log_tree_diff()` and `log_tree_diff_flush()` like other diff paths
- Fixes several long-standing inconsistencies where `-L` bypassed standard diff features like pickaxe (`-S`, `-G`) and `--diff-filter`
- Aligns commit/diff separator behavior with regular log output
- Updates tests to verify the new behavior

The patch demonstrates careful attention to review feedback from v1, with clean separation of concerns and thorough test coverage. It's part of a well-structured effort to bring line-log behavior in line with Git's standard diff pipeline while maintaining all existing functionality. The changes are mechanical but important for consistency and future maintainability.
