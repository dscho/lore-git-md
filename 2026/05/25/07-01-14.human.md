This is a technical review from Jeff King (Peff) addressing flag cleanup in Kristofer Karlsson's commit-reach.c optimization series. The review focuses on a subtle issue where the ENQUEUED flag might leak when the queue is cleared early.

Peff notes that while the patch correctly clears ENQUEUED when processing commits, there's a potential leak when the loop terminates early and clears the queue via clear_prio_queue(). He contrasts this with the ahead_behind() variant which properly cleans up flags, and questions whether leaving STALE (and now ENQUEUED) flags set is intentional behavior that needs to be preserved.

The review is substantive, identifying a real edge case in the flag handling that could affect future operations. Peff doesn't propose a specific solution but raises the question clearly, leaving it to the author to decide whether and how to address it. This fits naturally into the ongoing thread discussion about flag management strategies in the optimization series.
