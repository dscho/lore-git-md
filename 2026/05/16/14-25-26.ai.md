This is a follow-up in the git-gui bugfix series addressing repository and worktree detection issues. Mark Levedahl responds to Johannes Sixt's review of patch 3/11, which introduced centralized functions to manage GIT_DIR/GIT_WORK_TREE environment variables.

The key development is Mark's agreement to implement Johannes' suggestion to stop setting GIT_WORK_TREE entirely in modes requiring a worktree, while still maintaining the ability to unset it. This represents a simplification of the earlier approach that conditionally set GIT_WORK_TREE when non-empty. The change aligns with the thread's ongoing refinement of git-gui's environment variable handling.

Mark also notes he will keep the `unset_gitdir_vars` function's ability to remove GIT_WORK_TREE from the environment, preserving cleanup behavior while eliminating the problematic setting of empty values. This response demonstrates the author's responsiveness to maintainer feedback and willingness to adjust the implementation to better match git-gui's needs.

The exchange continues the series' pattern of technical discussion focused on cross-platform correctness and maintainability, with particular attention to edge cases around bare repositories and worktree detection. The resolution of this point helps move the series closer to final acceptance.
