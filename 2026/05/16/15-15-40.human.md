This is the final patch in Tuomas Ahola's four-part series fixing edge cases in Git's date approximation logic ("approxidate"). The patch implements a deferred adjustment scheme for special date/time formats like "today" and "yesterday" when combined with time specifications like "noon" or "tea".

The key change introduces negative `tm_mday` values (-1 for yesterday, -2 for day before, etc.) to handle cases where the day adjustment should happen after considering any explicit time specifications. This ensures "tea yesterday" and "yesterday tea" produce the same result (tea time of yesterday) rather than different results as before.

The implementation:
- Modifies `update_tm()` to handle negative mday values as deferred adjustments
- Updates time-related functions (`date_time`, `date_noon`, etc.) to use the new scheme
- Adds test cases verifying consistent behavior for combinations like "noon yesterday" and "tea last saturday"

This completes the series' goal of making special time formats respect explicit day specifications consistently. The approach aligns with Junio's earlier feedback about making "today" mean midnight rather than "now". With this patch, the series appears ready for final review and potential merging.
