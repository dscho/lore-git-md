This is the second patch in Tuomas Ahola's four-part series fixing edge cases in Git's date approximation logic. The patch enhances the test infrastructure in `t/t0006-date.sh` to support time offset adjustments, which will be used to verify the date parsing fixes in subsequent patches.

The key addition is a new `add_time_offset()` helper function that can adjust timestamps by hours or days, plus modifications to `check_approxidate()` to optionally apply these offsets during test execution. This allows the test suite to verify date parsing behavior at different points in time without hardcoding multiple test cases.

The patch demonstrates the new test capabilities by adding two test cases for "January 5th yesterday" - one with the default timestamp and another with a +2 day offset to verify month/year rollover behavior. The implementation carefully preserves the original timestamp between test runs to avoid side effects.

This test infrastructure change was already accepted in previous iterations of the series (it was patch 1/4 in v3) and remains unchanged in v4. Its purpose is to support testing the more substantive fixes in patches 3/4 and 4/4 of the series, particularly around special date/time strings and day/month rollover cases.
