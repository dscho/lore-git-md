CI/build system patch from Patrick Steinhardt upgrading GitLab's macOS CI runners. This first patch in a 2-part series switches from M1-based runners to newer M2 Pro machines, which provide more CPU and RAM (6 cores/16GB vs 4 cores/8GB). The change cuts test job times significantly - osx-clang drops from 26 to 16 minutes, osx-meson from 19 to 13, and osx-reftable from 23 to 14 minutes. 

The patch is straightforward, modifying just one line in `.gitlab-ci.yml` to update the runner tag. Patrick has already tested these changes in GitLab's internal CI pipeline before submission. As a routine infrastructure update from GitLab's own CI maintainer, this low-risk change is likely to be merged quickly. The speed improvements are a clear win with no apparent downsides.
