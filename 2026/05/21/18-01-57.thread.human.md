This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s documentation standardization series for `git replay` configuration, which aims to synchronize the `replay.refAction` documentation between `git-config(1)` and `git-replay(1)` man pages. The series, now at its third version (4 patches), has been fully reviewed and is technically complete—Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt provided feedback on structure and formatting, while Toon Claes contributed minor stylistic suggestions, all of which have been addressed.

The work follows Git’s documentation conventions, converting bullet lists to description lists, adjusting default value indicators, and adding conditional linking and maintenance comments. The series was uncontroversial, with no open technical questions, and is ready for integration. However, the topic has stalled due to inactivity: no updates have been posted since Toon’s feedback over a month ago. Junio’s recent email is a procedural nudge to Kristoffer, warning that the series may be discarded if no new version is sent soon. The blocker is administrative, not substantive.
