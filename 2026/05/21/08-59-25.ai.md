This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's CI/build system series updating GitLab's macOS CI configuration. The first patch that upgraded the runner specs, this one updates the base image version from macOS 15 to macOS 26 for two of the three macOS test jobs (osx-reftable and osx-meson), while intentionally leaving the third job (osx) on the older version for broader test coverage.

The change is straightforward - just updating the image tags in `.gitlab-ci.yml` to use the newer macOS 26 images that are now generally available. Patrick notes this isn't strictly necessary yet (the macOS 15 images aren't deprecated), but proactively keeps the CI environment current. The patch maintains the existing pattern of testing across multiple macOS versions by keeping one job on the older image.

As with the first patch, this is a low-risk infrastructure maintenance update from a GitLab employee who regularly contributes CI-related patches. The changes have already been tested in GitLab's internal CI pipeline before submission. The series appears well-motivated and non-controversial, likely to be quickly merged as part of routine CI upkeep.
