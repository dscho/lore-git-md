This is patch 17/18 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The patch stubs out the remaining callback functions that weren't yet implemented for the loose backend.

The key additions are:
1. Transaction support stubs (marked as a known omission to be addressed later)
2. Alternates handling stubs (read_alternates and write_alternate callbacks)
3. Clear documentation that transaction support will come in a future series after related transactional interfaces stabilize

The patch follows the established pattern of the series by wiring up the remaining ODB source interface methods while maintaining all existing functionality. The changes are mechanical and focused, with clear TODO markers for future work. This continues the systematic conversion of loose object handling to the callback-based interface that's central to the ODB abstraction effort.

The patch appears straightforward and low-risk since it's just adding stub implementations that maintain current behavior (erroring out for unsupported operations). It keeps the series moving forward while properly deferring the more complex transaction work until the interfaces are ready.
