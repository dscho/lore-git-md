Patrick Steinhardt's 18-part series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort has received its first review - a light touch from maintainer Junio Hamano. The series aims to convert the loose object source into a proper `struct odb_source`, making it work through callback-based operations rather than direct function calls.

Junio's review focused narrowly on a grammatical error in the documentation for patch 3/18, which introduces the `free` callback and other foundational pieces of the loose source's odb_source interface. While the review was surface-level (addressing only wording rather than technical substance), it signals that the series is now on Junio's radar as it works through the mailing list. The remaining patches in the series - which complete the callback wiring and eliminate the last dependencies between loose and files backends - await deeper technical review from the community.
