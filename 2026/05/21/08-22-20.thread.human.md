Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the ODB abstraction effort has been successfully merged into the 'next' branch after addressing minor documentation feedback. The series completes the architectural separation of loose object handling from the files backend, establishing it as a standalone storage implementation. While transaction and alternates support remain stubbed for future work, the core isolation is now in place.

Junio Hamano reviewed and merged the series after Patrick addressed a couple of surface-level documentation comments in v2. The technical approach appears uncontroversial, with no substantive objections raised during review. Karthik Nayak recently provided a post-merge grammatical nitpick on the first patch's commit message, but this doesn't affect the already-merged code. The series represents another step forward in Patrick's ongoing effort to enable pluggable storage backends in Git.
