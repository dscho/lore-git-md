Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the ODB abstraction effort has been successfully merged into the 'next' branch after a smooth review process. The 18-patch series completes the architectural separation of loose object handling from the files backend, converting it to a standalone `struct odb_source` implementation. This foundational work enables future pluggable storage implementations while preserving existing behavior.

Junio Hamano reviewed and merged the series after Patrick addressed a couple of minor documentation comments. The only post-merge feedback came from Karthik Nayak, who initially suggested a grammatical tweak to the first patch's commit message but then reconsidered, confirming the original wording was correct. This minor exchange underscores how the technical substance of the change was uncontroversial - the series completed its review cycle without substantive objections despite its architectural significance.

With this merge, the loose object backend is now fully isolated, joining earlier conversions of other storage components. The work leaves transaction support and alternates handling stubbed for future implementation, but establishes the necessary foundation. The clean progression reflects broad agreement on the approach among the ODB working group, with Patrick Steinhardt as the driving force behind this multi-stage effort to modernize Git's object storage architecture.
