This is a technical discussion between Derrick Stolee and Taylor Blau about patch 04/13 in the `--path-walk` and `--filter` interaction series. The exchange focuses on the implementation marks directly-requested objects (those not discovered during tree traversal) by prefixing their paths with '/'.

Taylor Blau had raised concerns in his review that overloading the path string to serve multiple purposes (as a strmap key, user-visible identifier, and now as a direct-object marker) makes the interface brittle. He suggested explicitly tracking this state with a "direct" field in the data structure instead.

In this response, Derrick Stolee defends his design choice, arguing that:
1. The leading '/' is necessary to avoid path collisions anyway
2. The meaning is internal to the API and not exposed to callers in a way they could misuse
3. The current approach is more memory-efficient than adding a separate flag field

The discussion remains technical and constructive, with Stolee providing rationale for his design decisions while acknowledging Blau's concerns. This is part of an ongoing architectural discussion about how best to represent object origins in the path-walk machinery, with both contributors engaging substantively on the tradeoffs. The exchange shows healthy technical debate without blocking progress on the series.
