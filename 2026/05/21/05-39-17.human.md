Bugfix series for Git's maintenance subsystem, now merged into `next` and proposed for backport to 2.54.1. Patrick Steinhardt's two-patch series addresses two distinct issues in Git's maintenance mode that emerged during the transition from `git gc` to `git maintenance`.

The first patch fixes a lockfile handling problem in `git maintenance run --detach` mode where stale locks could block subsequent runs. The solution, co-authored with Taylor Blau, modifies `daemonize()` to properly reassign lockfile ownership between parent and child processes. The second patch restores the expected behavior of `gc.auto` thresholds that was broken when maintenance stopped shelling out to `git gc`, ensuring backward compatibility for users relying on `gc.auto=0` to disable maintenance.

All reviewers (including Jeff King and Junio Hamano) have approved the changes, with consensus that the lockfile fix in particular may warrant backporting due to its potential to block maintenance operations. The series includes thorough test coverage and builds on Patrick's ongoing work to improve Git's maintenance infrastructure. With no outstanding questions, this appears ready for inclusion in the upcoming bugfix release.
