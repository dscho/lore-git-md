This is patch 17/18 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The patch stubs out the remaining callback functions for the loose backend that weren't addressed in previous patches.

The key additions are:
- Transaction support stubs (`begin_transaction`), explicitly marked as a known omission to be addressed later when transactional interfaces are more settled
- Alternates handling stubs (`read_alternates` and `write_alternate`), with read implemented as a no-op and write returning an error since loose sources don't support alternates
- All new callbacks are wired into the loose source's vtable` structure

The patch follows the established pattern of the series by systematically converting loose object handling to the callback-based interface. The author notes that transaction support could theoretically be implemented now but is being deferred to avoid potential conflicts with ongoing work to polish transactional interfaces. This appears to be a pragmatic choice to keep the series focused and avoid premature implementation that might need rework.

The changes are limited to `odb/source-loose.c` and maintain the series' approach of mechanical conversion without behavioral changes. This continues the incremental progress toward making the loose object backend fully compliant with the odb_source interface.
