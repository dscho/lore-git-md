This is a documentation patch fixing a minor formatting issue in the `git-restore` man page.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk noticed that commit 69666e67 (doc: convert git-restore to new style format, 2025-01-10) accidentally introduced a double underscore in one place where a single underscore was intended. The patch corrects this by removing the extra underscore in the phrase "_<rev-A>__" to match the consistent single-underscore formatting used elsewhere in the document.

The change is straightforward and clearly correct - it's part of the ongoing effort to maintain consistent documentation formatting. The patch follows the project's conventions with a clear commit message referencing the original commit that introduced the issue.
