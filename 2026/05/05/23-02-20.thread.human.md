This is a four-patch series from Michael Montalbo fixing a bug where negative numeric values were silently accepted by `-U` and `--inter-hunk-context`. The core fix is done across the first two patches by adding validation at the option-parsing layer. The third patch adds a safety net with a `BUG()` guard in the xdiff caller. This final patch clarifies the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag's documentation in `parse-options.h` with a brief parenthetical -- it was the confusion around that flag (which only blocks `--no-` boolean forms, not negative values) that led to the wrong approach in the original v1. The series is technically approved, low-risk, and ready for merging.
