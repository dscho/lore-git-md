Documentation patch clarifying commit-graph behavior with replace objects and grafts.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk adds a note to both `git-commit-graph` and `git-replace` man pages explaining that the presence of replace objects or commit grafts will disable commit-graph functionality. This behavior was previously only documented in the technical documentation (`technical/commit-graph.adoc`), not in the user-facing manuals.

The patch is straightforward and well-motivated - it surfaces an important implementation detail that affects users who work with replace objects or grafts. The change follows Git's documentation conventions by adding a new "CAVEATS" section to both man pages with cross-references between them. The diff shows clean additions without any formatting issues.
