A build system patch addresses a compatibility issue between Clang and glibc 2.43's use of C11's `_Generic` feature, which was causing CI failures after upgrading to Ubuntu 26.04. The problem specifically affects developer builds (with `DEVELOPER=1`) that enforce C99 mode. Patrick Steinhardt's solution works around glibc's incomplete feature detection for Clang by relaxing the C99 requirement for Clang builds and adding appropriate warning suppression in Meson. While not ideal, this maintains GCC's ability to catch standards misuse while unblocking Clang users. The patch notes that moving to C11 could be a more complete solution, though platform support remains a consideration.
