This is a documentation patch fixing a minor formatting issue in the `git-restore` man page.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk noticed that a 2025 conversion of the man page to AsciiDoc format accidentally introduced a double underscore in one place where a single underscore was intended. The patch corrects `_<rev-A>__` to `_<rev-A>_` to match the consistent formatting used elsewhere in the document.

The change is straightforward and clearly correct - it's a simple typo fix in documentation that was introduced during the broader man page modernization effort. No functional impact, just cleaning up a small inconsistency in the markup.
