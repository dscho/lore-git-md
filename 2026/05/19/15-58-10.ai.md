This is patch 11/16 in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX repacking series, focusing on geometric repacking behavior when MIDX layers are involved. The patch teaches `pack_geometry_init()` to handle MIDX-aware repacking by:

1. Introducing logic to restrict geometric repacking candidates to packs in the tip MIDX layer only when that layer contains at least a threshold number of packs (configurable via `midx_layer_threshold`). This prevents repacking small tip layers that wouldn't benefit from geometric consolidation.

2. Adding tracking of whether any tip-layer packs were included in the repack (`midx_tip_rewritten`), which will inform MIDX chain updates in later patches. The tracking occurs during the geometry split phase by checking each pack's MIDX membership.

The changes are mechanical additions to the repack geometry subsystem, maintaining all existing behavior while adding MIDX-specific logic. The patch introduces three new fields in `struct pack_geometry` to support these features: `midx_layer_threshold`, `midx_layer_threshold_set`, and `midx_tip_rewritten`.

This continues the series' pattern of careful, incremental changes with clear separation of concerns - here isolating the MIDX-specific geometric repacking logic while preserving the core algorithm. The implementation appears straightforward and well-contained, with no obvious technical concerns. As with previous patches in the series, this builds toward the overall goal of efficient incremental MIDX maintenance for large repositories.
