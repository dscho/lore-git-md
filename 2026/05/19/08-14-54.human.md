Junio clarifies that `--follow` was designed for linear histories and has inherent limitations when dealing with merge commits. He illustrates with a diagram showing how path tracking can get confused when traversing multiple histories in a merge - the feature may switch to following a renamed path from one parent while missing parallel renames in other parents. This isn't a bug in Miklos's patch per se, but a design constraint of how `--follow` works.

The maintainer suggests that without addressing this deeper limitation, small tweaks to the behavior (like Miklos's patch) don't fundamentally improve the situation enough to warrant his personal attention. His stance implies that while the patch may be technically correct for its narrow case, it doesn't solve the core conceptual problem with `--follow` in merge-heavy histories.
