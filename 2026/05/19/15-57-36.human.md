This is the cover letter for v4 of Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX repacking series, now consisting of 16 patches. The series implements the final component of a multi-part effort to improve Git's repacking strategy for large repositories by maintaining incrementally growing and shrinking MIDX chains.

The changes since v3 are minor:
1. Renamed a parameter from "verb" to "subcommand" in `repack_prepare_midx_command()` (addressing SZEDER Gabor's terminology nit)
2. Simplified `clear_incremental_midx_files()` by removing a redundant conditional around `r->objects` (addressing Jeff King's dead-code observation)

The series has received only surface-level review feedback so far, with no substantive objections to the overall design. Jeff King has verified memory safety aspects but there hasn't been broader testing or review from other domain experts. The patches appear well-crafted with comprehensive test coverage, building on Taylor's established work in this area.

This represents a significant architectural improvement to Git's scalability for large repositories, completing a multi-year effort that Taylor has presented at conferences. The series is rebased on current master and appears ready for final review, though more thorough testing feedback would be valuable given the complexity of the changes.
