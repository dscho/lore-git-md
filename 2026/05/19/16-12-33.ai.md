This is a performance optimization series from Taylor Blau targeting the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, specifically focused on speeding up bitmap generation for large repositories. The series builds on his previous work with pseudo-merge bitmaps.

The key improvements in this 8-patch series include:

1. Optimizing the core bitmap generation path by reducing redundant lookups and adding caching for object positions (~50% speedup in the no-pseudo-merge case from 612s to 294s in test repo)
2. Sorting bitmaps before XOR compression to improve delta compression (reducing bitmap size from 635MB to 176MB)
3. Restructuring pseudo-merge handling to build them after regular bitmaps rather than mixing them in the selection process (pseudo-merge case drops from 575s to 328s)

The series demonstrates significant performance wins in both time and space for bitmap generation, particularly for the large repository used for testing (~4.8M commits, ~57M objects). The changes are focused on the pack-bitmap-write.c implementation with some interface additions to pack-bitmap.h.

This appears to be a well-motivated series with clear performance data showing substantial improvements. The changes are focused on the bitmap generation internals rather than user-facing behavior, making them relatively low-risk optimizations. The series builds on Taylor's deep expertise in the pack/bitmap subsystems.
