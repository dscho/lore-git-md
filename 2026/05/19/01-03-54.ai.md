Performance optimization review follow-up from Jeff King on the commit-reach.c improvements. This is a substantive review that examines the technical details of the object flag approach while acknowledging the author's successful implementation of his earlier feedback.

Jeff confirms the object flag solution is cleaner than the original decoration hash approach, but raises two technical considerations: potential flag bit conflicts (though he notes the RESULT flag appears safely scoped) and the remaining use of min_generation_index. He speculates this could potentially be replaced with a counter but recognizes the generation number tracking may serve a purpose in commit reachability that isn't immediately obvious.

The review stops short of a full ACK, deferring to Derrick Stolee's expertise on the commit-reach algorithms. This reflects both technical humility (Jeff notes this is his first deep look at this code) and the project's practice of seeking domain-expert validation for core algorithms. The tone is constructive - while raising questions, he explicitly states the current implementation appears correct.
