Performance optimization follow-up discussing two approaches to extending priority queue usage in revision walking. Kristofer Karlsson compares his approach (keeping a linked list for setup while adding a separate priority queue for walking) with Jeff King's more comprehensive replacement of the commit list with a priority queue throughout.

Both approaches show significant speedups (3-5x) in benchmarks against a 2.4M-commit monorepo, with Kristofer's showing slightly better performance (4%) in limited walks due to avoiding queue rebuilds. The key difference is architectural: Jeff's branch converts all commit list usage to priority queues (touching 12 files), while Kristofer's maintains the list during setup (touching only 4 files) and introduces a vtable-based walk strategy system.

Kristofer has posted a draft PR showing his implementation and invites feedback on which approach would be more maintainable long-term. The discussion represents a natural next step after the recently approved `limit_list()` optimizations, with both contributors converging on similar performance improvements through slightly different architectural paths.
