Bugfix follow-up addressing cleanup details in HTTP pack index error handling.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Lorenzo Pegorari's memory leak fix with additional observations about tempfile cleanup behavior. The feedback is substantive, analyzing both the immediate issue and related cleanup opportunities while acknowledging the original fix is correct as-is.
