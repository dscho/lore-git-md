This is patch 6/8 in Derrick Stolee's series implementing bidirectional fetch/push negotiation controls. The patch introduces the `--negotiation-include` option to complement the existing `--negotiation-restrict` (formerly `--negotiation-tip`).

The key addition is a mechanism to guarantee certain refs are always advertised as "have" lines during negotiation, regardless of what the negotiation algorithm would normally select. This solves real-world cases where critical refs (like release branches) being dropped from negotiation could trigger full-history transfers (60+ GB in the cited example).

The implementation:
- Adds CLI and config support for `--negotiation-include` (mirroring the restrict pattern)
- Handles exact refs and glob patterns, silently ignoring non-existent refs
- Integrates with the negotiator via the new `have_sent()` interface from patch 5/8
- Ensures no duplicate "have" lines when included refs would naturally be negotiated
- Adds comprehensive tests covering glob patterns, duplicates, and protocol versions
- Includes `--negotiation-include` in `git pull` passthrough options

The patch builds cleanly on the previous infrastructure changes and has been reviewed by Matthew Cheetham. It's part of a well-structured series that's now in its final form after multiple rounds of review.
