- The function signatures in notes.c and notes.h
- Call sites in log-tree.c and revision.c
- Documentation comments to clarify the parameter's purpose

This appears to be preparatory cleanup for the larger feature, ensuring consistent type usage before introducing new functionality. As part of a larger series, this change helps establish a cleaner foundation for the external notes command implementation to come in later patches.
