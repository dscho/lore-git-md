This is patch 3/8 in Derrick Stolee's series to improve fetch/push efficiency in monorepos by adding negotiation controls. The series is now in its final form after review.

This particular patch performs a mechanical rename of internal variables and functions from `negotiation_tips` to `negotiation_restrict_tips` to better reflect the renamed CLI option (`--negotiation-restrict`). The changes are straightforward and consistent across the transport layer (fetch-pack.c, transport.c) and builtin/fetch.c. 

The patch maintains the same functionality while improving clarity - the new names better indicate these are the tips used for restricting negotiation scope. The rename was suggested in earlier reviews and has been thoroughly vetted, with Matthew Cheetham providing a Reviewed-by tag. 

This is part of the larger effort to provide both restriction (`--negotiation-restrict`) and inclusion (`--negotiation-include`) controls for fetch/push operations, addressing inefficient object transfers in large repositories. The series is now ready for maintainer consideration.
