This is a follow-up discussion in the performance optimization thread about using priority queues in revision walking. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Kristofer Karlsson's observation that extending the priority queue approach to `get_revision_1()` could yield further speedups.

Peff confirms the approach is viable, noting he has local unpublished patches from years ago that implement this same idea. He shares his branch where these changes have been running successfully, offering Kristofer the opportunity to build upon them. The key technical insight is that `rev_info` could use a priority queue as its primary data structure, though care is needed to handle non-date-sorted cases (which the queue supports via NULL comparator).

The email suggests this optimization could be a natural follow-up to Kristofer's already-approved `limit_list()` changes, with Peff's branch providing a potential starting point. Peff agrees with Kristofer's assessment that this should be a separate patch series, noting it would benefit from being based on the now-approved `limit_list()` optimizations. The exchange indicates continued momentum for performance improvements in revision walking, with multiple contributors converging on similar architectural approaches.
