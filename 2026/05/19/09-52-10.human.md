This is patch 6/18 in Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove `the_repository` usage from Git's setup subsystem. The patch converts `verify_filename()` to take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global variable.

The change follows the established three-step pattern seen in previous patches: updating the function signature, modifying callers to pass `the_repository` explicitly, and adjusting the implementation. The patch affects 6 files with straightforward mechanical changes to `verify_filename()` call sites in `grep`, `reset`, `rev-parse`, and `revision` commands.
