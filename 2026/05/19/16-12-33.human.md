Performance optimization series for bitmap generation, focusing on large repositories and pseudo-merges.

Taylor Blau sends an 8-patch series that significantly speeds up reachability bitmap generation, particularly for very large repositories (tested on ~4.8M commits, ~57M objects). The changes focus on both regular bitmap generation and pseudo-merge handling.

Key improvements:
- Regular bitmap generation drops from ~612.5s to ~294.1s through optimizations like:
  - Avoiding redundant lookups in `fill_bitmap_tree()`
  - Skipping no-op subtree checks
  - Reusing stored bitmaps
  - Adding an object-ID-to-position cache
- Bitmap file size shrinks from ~635.5MiB to ~176.4MiB by better sorting before XOR operations
- Pseudo-merge generation drops from ~575s to ~328.4s by:
  - Building regular bitmaps first
  - Reusing stored bitmaps
  - Handling pseudo-merges separately from regular commit selection

The series builds on Taylor's previous pseudo-merge work and includes thorough benchmarking showing substantial wins. The changes are focused in pack-bitmap-write.c with clean separation between the different optimization approaches. The optimizations appear well-motivated and the results are impressive speedups, though the pseudo-merge handling changes might warrant close review given their impact on a relatively new feature.
