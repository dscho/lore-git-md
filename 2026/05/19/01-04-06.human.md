This is the final implementation of the `--max-count-oldest` feature, addressing the last remaining edge cases in the revision walk machinery. The patch fixes two subtle issues that emerged in v7:

1. Boundary commits were incorrectly being counted toward the `--max-count-oldest` limit, which has now been corrected by only counting non-boundary commits.

2. Graph output was producing "orphaned" boundaries and attempting to show parents of discarded commits, fixed by properly setting the `CHILD_SHOWN` flag on those parents.

The implementation maintains all the key properties established through seven iterations of review: the O(K) space/O(N) time sliding window algorithm, clean integration with other revision walk options, and comprehensive test coverage. With these final graph-handling fixes, the feature appears ready for merging after its extensive review cycle.
