This is the v6 cover letter for Derrick Stolee's 8-patch series introducing `--negotiation-include` and `--negotiation-restrict` options for fetch/push negotiation. The series is now in its final form after thorough review, with only minor documentation tweaks remaining from v5.

The core problem being solved is inefficient object transfer in monorepos with many branches, where critical refs (like 'release') might get dropped from negotiation, triggering full-history transfers. The solution provides two complementary controls: `--negotiation-restrict` (replacing the older `--negotiation-tip`) to limit which refs are considered, and `--negotiation-include` to guarantee specific refs are always advertised while still allowing opportunistic inclusion of others.
