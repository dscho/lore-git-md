1. Using `strbuf_realpath()` to handle relative gitdir paths (a new requirement since we can no longer rely on pre-cached values)

3. Cleaning up the temporary strbuf

The removal of the caching mechanism addresses thread-safety concerns raised in earlier reviews while maintaining identical behavior.
