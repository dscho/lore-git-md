Taylor Blau continues his performance optimization series for Git's pack bitmap generation, now focusing on reducing the size of generated bitmap files. The series has already demonstrated substantial speed improvements through caching and traversal optimizations, with earlier patches showing up to 50% faster generation times in large repositories.

The latest patch introduces a clever optimization: by sorting commits by date before computing XOR-compressed bitmaps, the writer can find better ancestor/descendant pairs for compression. This yields dramatic space savings - a 72% reduction in bitmap file size (from 635MB to 176MB) in the test repository, with no loss of query coverage. The change is minimally invasive, adding just a date-based sort before the existing XOR compression pass.

The series remains focused on internal optimizations with no user-facing changes, building on Taylor's deep expertise in Git's pack and bitmap subsystems. While there hasn't been review discussion yet, the data-driven approach and clear performance wins make a strong case for these changes. The remaining patches are expected to address pseudo-merge handling, which may show even larger gains.
