Taylor Blau has posted an 8-patch series optimizing bitmap generation for large repositories, building on his previous pseudo-merge bitmap work. The initial patch restructures how tree objects are handled in the bitmap generation process, yielding a modest but measurable 4.9% speed improvement by reordering operations to look up tree positions before loading their contents. This sets the stage for more significant optimizations to come in the series, which promises substantial speedups (50% in test cases) and space savings through reduced lookups, better bitmap sorting, and restructured pseudo-merge handling. The changes focus on pack-bitmap-write.c internals and are low-risk since they don't affect user-facing behavior. The series has not yet received reviews, but given Taylor's deep expertise in the pack/bitmap subsystems, it's likely to be carefully evaluated by other contributors working in this area.
