It's part of the final refinements for the promisor remote auto-configuration feature, which has already gone through extensive review and is now technically complete.

The patch follows Git's coding conventions and aligns with the project's ongoing efforts to use proper boolean types where appropriate. Since this is part of a larger series that has already received approval, this change is unlikely to generate discussion and can be considered ready for merging.
