The changes since v3 are minor:
1. Renamed a parameter from "verb" to "subcommand" in `repack_prepare_midx_command()` (addressing SZEDER Gabor's terminology nit)
2. Simplified `clear_incremental_midx_files()` by removing a redundant conditional around `r->objects` (addressing Jeff King's dead-code observation)
