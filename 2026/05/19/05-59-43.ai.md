This is the implementation follow-up in Pablo Sabater's `git log --graph` visualization series, responding to Junio Hamano's design decision favoring cascading indentation for root commits. Pablo clarifies the visual semantics of the indentation approach, confirming that vertical alignment will only indicate true parent-child relationships while indented commits are explicitly marked as parentless.

The key technical point is that the cascading indentation approach preserves unambiguous signaling of commit relationships - vertical alignment means ancestry, indentation means root commit. Pablo notes that multiple consecutive root commits are rare in practice, but the cascading approach handles them naturally by incrementing indentation depth. The implementation will use a depth counter to generate the progressive indentation while maintaining the existing line-drawing logic for non-vertical connections.

This email effectively confirms the technical feasibility of Junio's preferred approach and signals that the series will proceed with cascading indentation as the standard visualization for root commits in the graph output.
