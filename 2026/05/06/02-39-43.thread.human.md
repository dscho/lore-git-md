This thread addresses a subtle edge case in `git rebase --update-refs` where the rebase machinery could incorrectly process non-branch refs like HEAD when `rebase.instructionFormat` includes ref decorations. The original fix (v2) filtered decorations to only local branches, which received a Reviewed-by from rebase expert Phillip Wood after improving test coverage. 

However, maintainer Junio Hamano has now raised a concern that this solution might be overly broad - while it prevents HEAD from being incorrectly updated, it would also skip local tags that users might legitimately want moved during rebase. Junio proposed an alternative approach that more narrowly targets just the HEAD case while preserving the ability to update local tags. The thread is now waiting for the original author to respond, either by adopting Junio's suggested refinement or justifying why excluding all non-branch refs is the correct behavior. 

The discussion highlights the careful balancing act in fixing edge cases without inadvertently restricting valid use cases. With both Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano engaged - two of Git's most experienced contributors in this area - the eventual solution will likely represent a well-considered resolution to this corner of the --update-refs feature.
