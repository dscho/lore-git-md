Andrew Kreimer submitted a patch fixing typos across multiple files in the Git codebase using the `codespell` tool. The initial version was reviewed by Kristoffer Haugsbakk, who confirmed the correctness of the corrections but questioned whether translation files should be handled separately.

Maintainer Junio C Hamano has now weighed in with more detailed feedback. He notes the patch's scope is too broad, touching 29 files across different subsystems, and should be split into logical groupings (documentation, core code, git-gui, and translations). Junio also raises concerns about the translation files (po/el.po and po/ko.po), suggesting they may be unmaintained since 2019 and questioning whether they should remain in the tree at all. 

While Junio verified many of the corrections (like "ubitquitous"->"ubiquitous" and "accomodate"->"accommodate"), he stopped reviewing halfway due to time constraints. The patch will need to be restructured into smaller, focused submissions before proceeding further, with special attention needed for the translation file changes. This aligns with Kristoffer's earlier suggestion about handling translations separately.
