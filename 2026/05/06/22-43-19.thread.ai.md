- Performance optimization detects when only commit messages change (`reword` case), skipping expensive merge operations by reusing original trees when parent and merge base trees match exactly
- Merge bases computed upfront for reuse in both fast and slow paths
- Explicit rejection of octopus merges and merge reverts in current scope
