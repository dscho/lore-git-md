This is a short two-patch series from Kristofer Karlsson (via GitGitGutter) optimizing `paint_down_to_common()` for the case where only one merge-base is needed. When generation numbers are available and the caller hasn't requested all merge-bases (`--all`), the algorithm can exit early after finding the first valid one, producing a dramatic 100x speedup (from 5 seconds to 24 milliseconds in a 2.2-million-commit monorepo).

The series evolved through four versions. Patrick Steinhardt suggested switching from a boolean flag to an `enum merge_base_flags` (v3) and splitting the change into a pure refactoring patch followed by the optimization itself (v3). Junio Hamano provided early review on the initial submission. Both patches are now accepted: the first introduces the enum and updates callers, the second adds the generation-aware early exit. The thread closed with Junio's standard acceptance ("Will queue. Thanks."), accompanied by an observation about a minor naming inconsistency that he explicitly said did not need fixing. No substantive review or open issues remain.
