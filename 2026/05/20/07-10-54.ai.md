This is a continuation of the documentation patch thread that has now become a discussion about Git's workflow conventions around patch trailers and maintainer responsibilities. The original technical content (documenting commit-graph behavior with replace objects) was settled in v3 and is ready for merging.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Junio Hamano's earlier musings about whether contributors should be more proactive in adding Reviewed-by/Acked-by trailers themselves rather than relying on the maintainer to do so. Haugsbakk expresses personal willingness to follow this practice but raises practical concerns: many contributors don't update trailers when that's the only change needed, and the Git culture often accepts implicit approvals (like "LGTM") without formal trailer syntax.

The email explores how Junio's proposed stricter approach might play out - potentially stalling patches in the short term as contributors adjust, but possibly improving long-term scalability by distributing the trailer-maintenance burden. Haugsbakk also notes that another related SubmittingPatches guideline (resending final versions directly to the maintainer) is rarely followed in practice.

This discussion provides valuable insight into Git's evolving collaboration norms, showing how even small process questions can surface tensions between maintainer workload, contributor experience, and documentation accuracy. The thread remains productive despite its meta-nature, with participants thoughtfully considering how to balance these concerns.
