This is a meta-discussion about Git's patch workflow conventions, emerging from a now-complete documentation patch series about commit-graph behavior with replace objects and grafts. The thread has shifted from technical content to process questions about who should be responsible for adding Reviewed-by/Acked-by trailers to patches - contributors or the maintainer.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk weighs in on Junio Hamano's proposal to stop silently adding trailers himself, noting that this cultural shift might lead to fewer trailers overall since many contributors don't explicitly include them in their reviews (an "LGTM" is often treated as an implicit ack). He also points out that the current guidelines about resending patches to the maintainer with proper trailers are rarely followed in practice.

The discussion explores potential consequences of enforcing stricter trailer requirements, including stalled patch progression until contributors adjust their workflows. While the technical patch (v3) remains ready for merging with Derrick Stolee's dual approval, this exchange documents an ongoing tension between maintainer scalability and contributor experience in Git's email-based workflow.
