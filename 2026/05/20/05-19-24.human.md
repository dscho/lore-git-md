This is Junio Hamano's regular "What's cooking in git.git" status report, providing updates on the current state of patches in the Git project's integration branches. The report covers new topics, graduated patches, and ongoing work across various subsystems.

Several notable patches are moving forward:
- Jeff King (peff) has three patches improving commit-graph lazy loading, connection service handling, and quote dequoting internals
- Taylor Blau has a 9-patch series optimizing bitmap generation performance
- Patrick Steinhardt's team continues work on the ODB abstraction and reftable backend
- The `the_repository` removal effort progresses with setup.c conversions
- Performance optimizations are landing in multiple areas: revision traversal (limit_list), commit-reach calculations, and bitmap generation
- Documentation improvements continue, with synopsis-style conversions and clarifications
- Platform-specific fixes address Windows memory handling and Linux fsmonitor support

The report shows healthy activity across the project, with a mix of performance work, refactoring, and feature development. Junio notes several topics that need review or have outstanding questions, while others are queued for merging into 'next' or 'master'. The breadth of changes reflects Git's ongoing maintenance and evolution across its many subsystems.
