This is a brief technical clarification in the concluded `git mv --index-only` discussion thread, where D. Ben Knoble raises a subtle point about index consistency.

Knoble questions whether bypassing content verification (the key efficiency gain of the proposed `--index-only` flag) could create a footgun by allowing the index to drift from the working tree state. He acknowledges this is already a common situation in Git's normal operation, making it likely not a new concern. The observation serves as a thought experiment rather than a blocking objection, coming after Junio Hamano has already effectively closed the discussion by stating the feature isn't a priority.

The exchange reinforces that while the proposal is technically sound, its niche use case and the existence of workarounds mean it's unlikely to be implemented unless volunteer effort emerges. Knoble's comment reads more like an academic footnote than a reopening of debate in this now-settled thread.
