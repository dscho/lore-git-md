This is a follow-up to a two-part cleanup removing deprecated commit list functions from commit.h. Patrick Steinhardt, who originally introduced both the modern API and the deprecated wrappers, acknowledges the removal with a brief approval. The patch series is straightforward - first updating callers to use the modern equivalents (`commit_list_free` etc.), then removing the old functions (`free_commit_list` etc.) that were marked for deprecation back in February. 

The change is mechanical and non-controversial, following Git's standard deprecation process. Kristoffer Haugsbakk has verified no remaining uses exist in the integration branches. With Patrick's implicit validation of the removal, this cleanup appears ready to proceed without further discussion. The work fits into the project's ongoing codebase maintenance efforts.
