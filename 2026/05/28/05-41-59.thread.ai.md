Core problem: When local branches or other refs are symbolic references (e.g., `main` pointing to `master`, or a symref pointing to a tag), `git rebase --update-refs` fails to properly handle symref decorations, causing rebase failures even when the primary branch rebases successfully. The issue affects branch rename migration scenarios and cross-worktree workflows where symrefs coexist.
