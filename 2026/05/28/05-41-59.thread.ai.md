Thread identity: Bugfix series for `git rebase --update-refs` behavior with branch symrefs, addressing edge cases where symbolic references to branches or non-branch refs cause rebase failures during ref updates. Authored by Son Luong Ngoc. Fourth version (v4) anticipated following this review.

Core problem: When local branches or other refs are symbolic references (e.g., `main` pointing to `master`, or a symref pointing to a tag), `git rebase --update-refs` fails to properly handle symref decorations, causing rebase failures even when the primary branch rebases successfully. The issue affects branch rename migration scenarios and cross-worktree workflows where symrefs coexist. Additionally, a latent bug in `master` causes branches matching HEAD to incorrectly appear in the rebase todo list when multiple worktrees are involved, due to clobbering of the `head_ref` buffer during resolution.
