Thread identity: Bugfix series for `git rebase --update-refs` behavior with branch symrefs, addressing edge cases where symbolic references to branches or non-branch refs cause rebase failures during ref updates. Authored by Son Luong Ngoc. Fourth version (v4) of the two-patch series is now in preparation to address the final latent bug.

Core problem: When local branches or other refs are symbolic references (e.g., `main` pointing to `master`, or a symref pointing to a tag), `git rebase --update-refs` fails to properly handle symref decorations, causing rebase failures even when the primary branch rebases successfully. The issue affects branch rename migration scenarios and cross-worktree workflows where symrefs coexist. Additionally, a latent bug in `master` causes branches matching HEAD to incorrectly appear in the rebase todo list when multiple worktrees are involved, due to clobbering of the `head_ref` buffer during resolution.

Current status: Design debate is now fully resolved; the series will retain the current robustness approach (using `resolved_ref` for HEAD comparison). The third version (v3) is functionally complete and under final review. Junio C Hamano has confirmed that the remaining blocker is the latent bug in `master` (clobbered `head_ref` buffer during HEAD resolution), which will be addressed in v4. The current patch’s approach (using `resolved_ref` for HEAD comparison) aligns with this low-level permissiveness and is now supported by concrete use cases.
