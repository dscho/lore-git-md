Thread identity: Bugfix series for `git rebase --update-refs` behavior with branch symrefs, addressing edge cases where symbolic references to branches or non-branch refs cause rebase failures during ref updates. Authored by Son Luong Ngoc. Fourth version (v4) of the two-patch series is now in preparation to address the final latent bug and remaining design questions.

Core problem: When local branches or other refs are symbolic references (e.g., `main` pointing to `master`, or a symref pointing to a tag), `git rebase --update-refs` fails to properly handle symref decorations, causing rebase failures even when the primary branch rebases successfully. The issue affects branch rename migration scenarios and cross-worktree workflows where symrefs coexist. Additionally, a latent bug in `master` causes branches matching HEAD to incorrectly appear in the rebase todo list when multiple worktrees are involved, due to clobbering of the `head_ref` buffer during resolution.

Current status: The series remains under review with no merge status reported. The third version (v3) is functionally complete but faces two unresolved technical questions from Phillip Wood's substantive review of the second patch. These questions challenge the redundancy of symref resolution in `prepare_checked_out_branches()` and the necessity of the patch's deduplication logic for non-branch symrefs. The latent bug in `master` (clobbered `head_ref` buffer) remains confirmed as a blocker for v4.

Key technical details:
- Implementation spans `sequencer.c` and `branch.c`:
  - Patch 1: Uses `refs_resolve_refdup()` for memory-safe symref resolution in `add_decorations_to_list`, skips symrefs whose targets are under `refs/heads/` to prevent duplicate updates, maintains owned copy of resolved HEAD. Now requires: (1) explanatory comment at symref resolution site documenting purpose and `flags` check, (2) corrected HEAD comparison using `resolved_ref` instead of `decoration->name` to handle HEAD pointing outside `refs/heads/`, (3) test organization tweaks (moving a `test_grep` line and clarifying a checked-out branch comment). The v3 series fixed the v2 regression by removing the `!path` gate and reordering checks to ensure the current branch is always skipped.
  - Patch 2: Extends reservation logic to cover both literal symref name and its resolved target, tracks resolved symref targets in `todo_add_branch_context` to deduplicate updates, checks both symref and target against checked-out branches to prevent cross-worktree conflicts, and handles non-branch symrefs (tags, notes, etc.). Phillip Wood's review questions:
    1. Redundancy in `prepare_checked_out_branches()`: After Patch 1, the todo list should already skip symrefs and record only their resolved targets, making the symref resolution in this function potentially unnecessary. This could also introduce a race condition where two worktrees with `refs/heads/feature` and `refs/heads/symlink-to-feature` checked out might both be treated as independent reservations, violating the single-worktree-per-branch invariant.
    2. Overengineering: The patch's deduplication logic for non-branch symrefs (e.g., pointing to tags) may not be practically necessary, and the fix could be incremental, focusing first on the common case of branch-to-branch symrefs addressed in Patch 1.
