Performance optimization follow-up for `git fetch` connectivity checks.

Kristofer Karlsson responds to Junio C Hamano's review of his earlier patch that improved fetch performance by passing the transport parameter through to `check_connected()`. He confirms having audited other call sites and found:

4. `bundle.c` doesn't need the transport optimization

The response shows the patch author did due diligence in checking related code paths while acknowledging there may still be future opportunities to apply this optimization elsewhere.
