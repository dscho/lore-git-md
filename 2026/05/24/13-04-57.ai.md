Performance optimization follow-up for `git fetch` connectivity checks.

Kristofer Karlsson responds to Junio Hamano's review of his patch that improved fetch performance by passing the transport parameter to enable an optimization in `check_connected()`. He confirms he investigated other call sites and found:

1. `builtin/clone.c` already implements this optimization (the pattern he copied)
2. `builtin/receive-pack.c` has no transport available
3. `fetch-pack.c` skips the check entirely for --deepen operations
4. `bundle.c` doesn't need the transport

The response shows thorough investigation of related code paths while acknowledging there may be future opportunities to extend the optimization. The minimal 3-line change remains focused on the immediate `fetch` performance improvement, with benchmark results showing connectivity check time dropping from 22s to 5s in large repositories.

This completes the technical discussion around the patch, confirming it's ready for merging while leaving the door open for potential future optimizations in other areas.
