Maintainer response to a performance optimization for `git fetch` connectivity checks.

Junio C Hamano acknowledges Kristofer Karlsson's patch that improves fetch performance by passing the transport parameter through to enable an existing optimization in `check_connected()`. He notes the optimization is well-established (dating back to 2013) and recently refined, making this a logical extension.

While approving the change, Junio observes that other calls to `check_connected()` in `builtin/fetch.c` and elsewhere don't pass the transport parameter, and wonders if similar tweaks could benefit those cases. This suggests the optimization might have broader applicability, though he notes the pre-fetch connectivity check wouldn't benefit since it runs before any transport exists.

The response indicates the patch will likely be accepted while implicitly inviting follow-up work to audit other `check_connected()` call sites. Junio's "Impressive" comment on the benchmark results signals approval of the demonstrated performance gains (22s -> 5s connectivity check time).
