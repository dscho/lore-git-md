Performance optimization for ahead-behind calculations in commit-reach.c. This is the third and final patch in Kristofer Karlsson's series to improve performance in commit graph traversal algorithms.

The patch applies the same O(1) counter-based approach from the previous patch (which optimized merge-base calculations) to the ahead_behind() function. It replaces the O(n) queue scan with a nonstale_count that tracks the number of non-stale commits in the queue, incrementing and decrementing the counter as commits are enqueued and processed. The PARENT2 flag is used to deduplicate queue entries, similar to how ENQUEUED was used in the earlier optimization.

The implementation removes the now-unused queue_has_nonstale() function and updates the mark_stale() calls to maintain the counter. The change maintains the same behavior while improving performance, particularly for repositories with wide commit graphs where the queue grows large. The series as a whole has shown 2.5x speedups in benchmarks on large monorepos while maintaining performance for simpler cases.

This completes the series' goal of eliminating O(n) queue scans from both paint_down_to_common() (used by merge-base) and ahead_behind(). The changes are focused and well-contained within commit-reach.c, building logically on the preparatory work in the first two patches.
