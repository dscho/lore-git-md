Thread identity: Performance optimization for sparse index in `git restore`, a two-part patch series from Derrick Stolee targeting the sparse-index/sparse-checkout subsystem.

Core problem: `git restore --staged` with `..` pathspecs was unnecessarily expanding the sparse index, despite `SKIP_WORKTREE` paths being irrelevant for staged changes. The optimization avoids this expansion while maintaining correct behavior.

Current status: Complete v1 series consisting of:
1. Test coverage demonstrating the unnecessary expansion (reviewed by Junio C Hamano)

- Maintains all existing functionality while optimizing sparse case
