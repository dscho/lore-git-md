Performance optimization for commit-reach.c algorithms (part 2/3). This patch from Kristofer Karlsson continues the series to improve performance of merge-base and ahead-behind calculations by replacing O(n) queue scans with an O(1) counter.

The key change here replaces queue_has_nonstale()'s linear scan of the commit queue with a nonstale_count that tracks the number of non-stale commits in the queue. The counter is maintained by:
- Incrementing in maybe_enqueue() when adding a non-stale commit
- Decrementing when dequeuing a non-stale commit
- Decrementing via mark_stale() when a queued commit becomes stale

The patch builds on the ENQUEUED flag introduced in patch 1/3 to ensure each commit appears in the queue at most once, making the counter exact. The changes focus on paint_down_to_common() which is used by merge-base calculations, with ahead_behind() to follow in patch 3/3.

The optimization is particularly impactful for wide commit graphs where the queue grows large, as shown in the benchmark results from the cover letter (2.5x speedup on a 2.4M commit monorepo). The implementation appears clean and maintains the existing behavior while eliminating the O(n*m) overhead of repeated queue scans.
