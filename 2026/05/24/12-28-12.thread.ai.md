Git thread summary:

Performance optimization for `git fetch` connectivity checks. Kristofer Karlsson from Spotify identified and implemented a patch to pass the transport parameter through `store_updated_refs()` to enable an existing optimization in `check_connected()`, allowing skipping verification of objects already in newly received packs when the transport provides self-contained packs. Benchmark results show dramatic improvements: connectivity check time drops from 22s to 5s when fetching 200 new commits in a large repository (2.4M commits, 374K files). 

Junio C Hamano reviewed and approved the change, noting the optimization is well-established (dating back to 2013) and recently refined. Kristofer followed up with a thorough investigation of other `check_connected()` call sites, confirming that `builtin/clone.c` already implements this optimization (the pattern he copied), while `builtin/receive-pack.c` has no transport available, `fetch-pack.c` skips the check entirely for --deepen operations, and `bundle.c` doesn't need the transport. The minimal 3-line change remains focused on the immediate `fetch` performance improvement while leaving the door open for potential future optimizations in other areas. The technical discussion is now complete, with the patch ready for merging after demonstrating both the specific performance gains and consideration of broader implications.
