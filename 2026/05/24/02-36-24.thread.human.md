Junio C Hamano has now raised substantive concerns about the change. As the project's maintainer, Junio argues that since dotfiles like .gitignore are valid Git-tracked files, they should be equally visible in completions. This challenges the patch's core premise and suggests the author will need to either provide stronger justification for the behavior change or reconsider the approach. The review keeps the discussion open while signaling this may not be a straightforward change to land.
