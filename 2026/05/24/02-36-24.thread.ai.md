Core problem: Git’s path completion currently shows dotfiles (e.g., `.gitignore`) by default, deviating from standard shell completion behavior. The series aligns Git’s completion with shell conventions by hiding dotfiles unless explicitly requested with a leading dot (e.g., `git rm .`), while preserving explicit dotfile access.
