This is a community update announcement for the Git Rev News newsletter. Christian Couder is sharing a draft of the upcoming 135th edition of the newsletter, which summarizes recent Git development activity for the broader community.

The email invites contributions to the newsletter draft before its planned publication on June 2, 2026. Contributors can either directly edit the draft on GitHub via pull request or reply to the email with suggestions. The newsletter team (Christian, Jakub, Markus, and Kaartic) welcomes all forms of input - from proofreading to article suggestions to volunteering for interviews.

The newsletter serves as a digest of Git project activity, highlighting notable patches, discussions, and releases. This particular draft appears to include mentions of several contributors, as Christian notes he has attempted to notify everyone featured in this edition. The tone is collaborative and open, typical of Git's community-driven development process.
