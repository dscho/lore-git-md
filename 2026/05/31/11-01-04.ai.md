This is a community update announcement for the Git project's newsletter, Git Rev News. Christian Couder is sharing a draft of edition 135 and inviting contributions from anyone interested in proofreading, suggesting content, or helping with GitHub issues.

The draft is available on GitHub, and the team plans to publish the final version on June 2nd, 2026. Christian notes they've tried to CC all contributors mentioned in this edition but may have missed some. This is a routine call for community participation in the newsletter process, not a technical patch or proposal.

The email serves as both an announcement of the upcoming edition and an open invitation for the Git community to contribute improvements before publication. It follows the project's collaborative approach to maintaining its newsletter, with multiple editors (Jakub, Markus, Kaartic, and Christian) involved in the process.
