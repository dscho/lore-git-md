This is a routine call for participation in the upcoming Git Rev News edition 135, scheduled for publication on June 2nd, 2026. Christian Couder, one of the newsletter editors, has shared a draft on GitHub and is inviting community members to contribute by proofreading, suggesting additional content, or helping address open GitHub issues. The editing team (including Jakub, Markus, Kaartic, and Christian) aims to acknowledge all contributors mentioned in the edition but notes they may have missed some. This follows the project's collaborative approach to maintaining its community newsletter, where input from any interested party is welcomed before final publication.
