This thread began with Harald Nordgren’s patch series addressing macOS linker warnings about duplicate library references in Git’s Makefile build system. The series has now concluded with a consensus solution: suppressing the cosmetic warning at the linker level using `-Wl,-no_warn_duplicate_libraries`, version-gated for Xcode 15+ (ld64-907+, dyld-1009.5+) via `LD_MAJOR_VERSION` in `config.mak.uname`. D. Ben Knoble confirmed the patch works on both older (Xcode 14.2) and newer macOS systems, and Junio C Hamano’s proposed approach—inspired by a prior Meson fix—has been directly endorsed by Paolo Bonzini, the Meson maintainer who originally implemented the same solution. Bonzini called the warning "mostly a nuisance" and advised unconditional suppression in Meson builds, reinforcing the architectural soundness of the approach.

The v3 patch is ready for merging, but the thread has also revealed that the same warning appears in Meson builds, where it’s partially mitigated by `--fatal-warnings`. Patrick Steinhardt and Paolo Bonzini now agree the warning should be unconditionally disabled in Meson as well, with Bonzini indicating he may implement this himself. This follow-up doesn’t block the current patch but signals a likely extension of the suppression to Meson builds, ensuring cross-build-system consistency. The discussion has established a precedent for handling platform-specific linker behaviors in Git, aligning with upstream tools like Meson and prioritizing maintainability over cosmetic warnings.
