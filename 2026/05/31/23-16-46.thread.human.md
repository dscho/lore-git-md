Harald Nordgren’s patch series addressing macOS linker warnings about duplicate library references is now effectively complete. The series, which began as an exploration of Makefile restructuring, settled on Junio C Hamano’s proposed solution: suppressing the warning at the linker level with `-Wl,-no_warn_duplicate_libraries` in `config.mak.uname`, version-gated for Xcode 15 and later. This approach preserves the existing Makefile logic—necessary for handling circular dependencies between archives—while eliminating the cosmetic warning that cluttered developer terminals during test runs.

The patch has been tested on both older (Xcode 14.2) and newer macOS systems, with D. Ben Knoble confirming that the version gate works correctly: the flag is omitted on pre-Xcode 15 systems, and neither the Makefile nor Meson builds produce warnings or errors. This verification addresses the last open question in the thread—whether the version-gate regex (matching `ld`, `ld64`, and `dyld`) is robust across Xcode versions—and removes the final hurdle before merging. The patch is now ready for Junio to pick up, with no regressions expected and broad compatibility confirmed. The discussion also establishes a useful precedent for handling other platform-specific linker behaviors in Git’s build system, should similar issues arise in the future.
