Documentation patch clarifying commit-graph behavior with replace objects and grafts.

This v3 patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk is a minor update to the previously approved documentation change about how replace objects and commit grafts affect commit-graph functionality. The only change from v2 is the addition of an Acked-by from Derrick Stolee, confirming his approval of the final version.

The patch maintains the same key improvements from v2:
- Adds "CAVEATS" sections to both `git-commit-graph` and `git-replace` man pages
- Documents that commit-graph functionality is disabled when replace objects or grafts are present
- Includes cross-references between the two man pages
- Places the performance impact warning specifically in the `git-replace` documentation as suggested in review

With Stolee's explicit Acked-by now included, this documentation update appears fully approved and ready for merging. The change cleanly surfaces important technical information that was previously only in the internal technical documentation, making it more accessible to end users while maintaining appropriate separation between the man pages' focus areas.
