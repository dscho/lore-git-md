This thread documents the behavior of `git log --follow` and `--no-follow`, completing coverage of these long-existing but previously undocumented options. The discussion has clarified several key constraints: `--follow` only works with single filenames (not general pathspecs), cannot track renames across merge commits due to fundamental implementation limitations, and interacts with directory pathspecs in specific ways. The `--no-follow` option overrides both command-line and config-based settings.
