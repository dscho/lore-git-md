- The function declarations in notes.h
- The implementation in notes.c
- Call sites in log-tree.c and revision.c

This appears to be a clean, uncontroversial cleanup that improves type safety and readability. The change is minimal (7 lines changed total) and doesn't affect behavior - it just makes the code's intent clearer. The patch follows Git's convention of using proper boolean types where appropriate.

As part of a larger series, this patch serves as groundwork for the more substantial external notes functionality to come in patch 4/4. The author has clearly structured the series to separate mechanical improvements (like this one) from the new feature implementation.
