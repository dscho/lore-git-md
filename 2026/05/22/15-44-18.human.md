Bugfix for receive-pack's updateInstead behavior with core.worktree.

Alyssa Ross identifies a regression where push_to_checkout() was incorrectly modifying the shared environment by adding GIT_WORK_TREE, which then affected subsequent push_to_deploy() operations. The fix moves the environment modification to only affect the hook-specific options rather than the shared environment.

The patch includes a new test case that verifies the correct behavior when core.worktree is set, demonstrating both the successful push case and the expected failure when trying to delete the branch. This appears to be a straightforward correction of an oversight in the earlier hook race condition fix (a8cc594333).

The change is minimal (just two lines moved) but addresses a real interaction between two features. The test coverage gives confidence that the fix works as intended without introducing new issues.
