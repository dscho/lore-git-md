This is patch 4/6 in Harald Nordgren's series adding `git branch --prune-merged` for safe automated local branch cleanup. The series has evolved through 11 iterations with extensive review, and this patch implements the core deletion logic with robust safety checks.

The new `--prune-merged` option deletes local branches that are reachable from their upstream (indicating the work has landed upstream) while protecting against common pitfalls. It specifically:
- Skipping branches checked out in any worktree
- Preserving branches whose upstream no longer exists locally
- Protecting branches that push destination equals upstream (to avoid deleting trunk branches)
- Warning rather than failing on unmerged branches during bulk operations

The implementation builds on the refactored `delete_branches()` from patch 3/6, using its new `warn_only` and `no_head_fallback` flags. The safety model has been carefully refined through review, with push/upstream comparison being a key addition to distinguish topic branches from trunk branches.

The patch includes comprehensive tests covering:
- Basic deletion of merged branches
- Various upstream configurations (remote, local, multiple patterns)
- Edge cases (checked-out branches, missing upstreams, push/upstream equality)
- Warning behavior for unmerged branches

This appears technically solid and ready for final review, representing the culmination of significant design iteration to balance automation with safety. The remaining patches in the series will add push-tracking safety checks and wildmatch pattern support.
