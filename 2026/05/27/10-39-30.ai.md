Performance optimization discussion for fetch connectivity checks.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Kristofer Karlsson's withdrawal of a proposed `git fetch` optimization, exploring whether similar optimizations could work for fetches in principle. He analyzes the technical constraints around verifying connectivity of fetched objects when they reference existing repository history, noting that while some theoretical speedups might be possible by checking only the "edges" between new and existing objects, the implementation would be non-trivial and different from Kristofer's original approach.

Peff acknowledges the difficulty of benchmarking fetch operations accurately due to Git's internal optimizations around object transfer, validating Kristofer's experience of being unable to reproduce initial performance claims. The exchange demonstrates Git's culture of thorough technical review, where even withdrawn proposals spark deeper analysis of the underlying problem space. While no immediate solution emerges, the discussion surfaces valuable constraints about when packfile connectivity optimizations can apply and hints at potential future directions for fetch performance work.
