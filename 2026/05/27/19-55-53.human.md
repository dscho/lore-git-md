Performance optimization in Taylor Blau's bitmap generation series. This is patch 2/8 in v2, building on his earlier pseudo-merge bitmap work to speed up pack bitmap creation for large repositories.

The patch moves the bitmap position check earlier in the tree traversal process, avoiding unnecessary stack frame setup when a tree's bit is already set. This yields a 3.4% speedup (582.4s->562.8s) in test repositories by reducing instruction count, though the cycles-per-instruction ratio remains stable. The change is focused on `pack-bitmap-write.c` internals and maintains all existing behavior - it just does the work more efficiently.

Jeff King (Peff) has already reviewed the technical approach in v1, confirming the real-world benefits despite some theoretical puzzlement about why such gains are possible. The patch appears ready for integration as part of the larger optimization series.
