This is the seventh patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, focusing on pseudo-merge bitmap handling. The patch restructures how parent bitmaps for pseudo-merges are managed during bitmap generation.

The key change moves the temporary parent bitmaps from a separate array to being stored directly on the `bitmapped_commit` struct, matching the lifetime with the commit's other bitmap data. This is a preparatory change for the final patch in the series which will restructure pseudo-merge generation. The on-disk format remains unchanged - this is purely an internal reorganization to enable future optimizations.

The patch adds a new `pseudo_merge_parents` field to `bitmapped_commit` and updates the cleanup, initialization, and serialization paths to handle it. The changes are mechanical but carefully preserve all existing behavior while setting up for the more significant pseudo-merge restructuring in the next patch.

This continues the series' pattern of well-instrumented, incremental changes that each build on prior infrastructure while maintaining clean separation of concerns. The patch has already received thorough review from Jeff King in previous iterations, and no new technical issues are apparent. The series remains on track to deliver its promised performance improvements for large repositories.
