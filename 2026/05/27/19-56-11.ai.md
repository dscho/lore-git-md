This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, focusing on pseudo-merge bitmap generation. The series has systematically improved bitmap generation performance for large repositories, with this patch addressing a key bottleneck in how pseudo-merges were handled.

The patch restructures pseudo-merge bitmap generation to occur *after* regular bitmap generation, rather than interleaving them. This avoids the performance penalty of treating pseudo-merges (which are synthetic commit groupings) like real commits during the maximal commit selection phase. The change reduces the pseudo-merge generation overhead from +95.5% to just +11.7% compared to non-pseudo-merge bitmap generation, while maintaining the same on-disk format and traversal benefits.

Key technical changes:
- Excluding pseudo-merges from initial bitmap_builder_init()
- Adding separate build_pseudo_merge_bitmaps() pass after regular bitmap generation
- Teaching fill_bitmap_commit() to handle unparsed trees from pseudo-merge walks
- Preserving existing pseudo-merge bitmap reuse optimizations

The patch completes the series' promised optimizations, bringing cumulative speedups to 60.1% (612.5s->294.1s) with substantial storage reductions. Jeff King has provided thorough technical validation throughout the series, and this final patch appears ready for integration. The changes demonstrate Taylor's deep expertise in the pack/bitmap subsystems while maintaining backward compatibility.
