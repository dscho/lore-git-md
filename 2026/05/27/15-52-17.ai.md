René Scharfe (a frequent contributor known for cleanups and code hygiene patches) notes that:

2. The other two callers (`remove_redundant_no_gen()` and `repo_in_merge_bases_many()`) don't use the list order at all

The patch is small (just removing one line) and well-justified - it eliminates unnecessary work without changing any observable behavior.
