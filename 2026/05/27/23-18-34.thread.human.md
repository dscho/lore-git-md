Taylor Blau's series integrates delta-islands with path-walk in `git pack-objects`, resolving their historical incompatibility while maintaining bitmap support. The technical implementation has been validated through Derrick Stolee's thorough review, which confirmed correct behavior across edge cases and test scenarios.

Stolee has now provided performance data showing the tradeoffs between path-walk and bitmap modes. While path-walk produces significantly smaller packfiles for small fetches (sometimes reducing size by over 17x), bitmaps maintain their speed advantage for larger operations. Notably, the combination of both features works correctly without regressions. However, the expected benefits of path-walk appear limited to smaller fetches, with diminishing returns in larger repositories - even in known problematic cases like microsoft/fluentui. This raises open questions about whether further optimizations could extend path-walk's advantages to larger-scale operations, though the current implementation is technically sound and ready for merging.
