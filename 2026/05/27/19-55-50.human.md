This is the first patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem. The patch refactors `fill_bitmap_tree()` to take an object position parameter upfront rather than looking it up internally, preparing for future optimizations.

The change provides an unexpected 4.9% speedup (612.5s -> 582.4s) in bitmap generation for large repositories, likely due to improved CPU cache/pipeline behavior from reordering the tree lookup and position lookup operations. Performance metrics show a 5.1% reduction in cycles-per-instruction.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the patch, confirming the speedup while noting theoretical surprise since the change shouldn't affect the normal case where objects are found in pack/midx. The discussion suggests the improvement may come from architecture/compiler variations in how the reordering affects cache behavior.

This is a well-measured preparatory change that already shows real-world benefits while setting up for more substantial optimizations later in the series. The patch demonstrates Taylor's characteristic attention to performance measurement and careful refactoring of the bitmap generation internals.
