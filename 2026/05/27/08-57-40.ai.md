Performance optimization review in the pack-bitmap-write subsystem.

Jeff King (Peff) provides a substantive review of Taylor Blau's first optimization patch in the bitmap generation series. While acknowledging the patch's correctness and measured performance improvements (6% speedup on linux.git), Peff expresses surprise at the results since the change shouldn't affect normal cases where objects are found in the pack/midx. He notes the optimization comes from reordering operations rather than eliminating work, and speculates about potential architecture/compiler variations affecting the results.

The review confirms the technical approach is sound and prepares for subsequent optimizations, while highlighting an interesting performance anomaly that wasn't fully explained in the original patch. Peff's testing validates the real-world benefits despite the theoretical puzzle, suggesting the change is worth keeping regardless of the underlying mechanism. This interaction demonstrates the project's careful review culture where even seemingly straightforward changes receive thorough technical scrutiny from domain experts.
