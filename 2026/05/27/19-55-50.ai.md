This is the first patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, building on his previous pseudo-merge bitmap work. The patch refactors `fill_bitmap_tree()` to first find a tree's bit position before loading its contents, which surprisingly provides a measurable 4.9% speedup (612.5s->582.4s) in large repositories despite being theoretically neutral.

The change reorders operations in the tree traversal to do position lookups before tree loading, likely improving CPU cache behavior. Performance metrics show a 5.1% reduction in cycles-per-instruction. Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the patch, confirming the real-world speedup while noting theoretical puzzlement since the change shouldn't affect the normal case where objects are found in pack/midx. The discussion suggests compiler/cache effects may be responsible for the gains.

This preparatory change enables subsequent optimizations in the series while already providing standalone benefits. The patch demonstrates Taylor's characteristic attention to low-level performance details in the bitmap generation path.
