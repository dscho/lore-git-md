This is a performance optimization series from Taylor Blau targeting the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, specifically focused on speeding up bitmap generation for large repositories. The series builds on his previous work with pseudo-merge bitmaps.

The v2 series includes minor refactoring in patches 4 and 5 (consolidating `find_object_pos()` and introducing the object position cache) and removes a stale commit message paragraph in patch 8. The core optimizations remain unchanged from v1:

1. Core bitmap generation path optimizations via reduced lookups and object position caching (50% speedup from 612s->294s in test repo)
2. Bitmap sorting before XOR compression to improve delta compression (635MB->176MB size reduction)
3. Restructured pseudo-merge handling to build after regular bitmaps (575s->328s speedup)

The changes are focused on pack-bitmap-write.c internals with some interface additions to pack-bitmap.h. The series has received thorough review from Jeff King (Peff) who confirmed the technical correctness and performance improvements while discussing some architectural tradeoffs. No outstanding technical issues remain - this appears ready for integration.

The optimizations provide measurable benefits for large repositories while maintaining backward compatibility and not affecting user-visible behavior. The series demonstrates Taylor's deep expertise in the pack/bitmap subsystems and builds logically on his previous pseudo-merge bitmap work.
