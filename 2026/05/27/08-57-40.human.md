Performance optimization review in the pack-bitmap-write subsystem.

Jeff King (Peff) confirms the performance improvements from Taylor Blau's first patch in the bitmap optimization series, observing a ~6% speedup in bitmap generation for linux.git. He notes the speedup is surprising since the patch primarily reorders operations (passing the tree's bit position into `fill_bitmap_tree()` before loading its contents) rather than changing the fundamental work being done.

Peff identifies one possible explanation - the new ordering could skip hash lookups when objects aren't found, though he notes this should be rare in practice since missing objects would make the bitmap invalid anyway. His testing confirms the patch works as intended and provides measurable benefits, though the exact mechanism remains somewhat mysterious ("a gift horse in the mouth").

This is a substantive review from one of Git's most experienced contributors in the pack/bitmap subsystems, lending credibility to the optimization approach. The interaction demonstrates the typical Git review process where even straightforward changes receive careful scrutiny from domain experts. With Peff's confirmation of both the correctness and performance impact, this patch appears ready to proceed in the series.
