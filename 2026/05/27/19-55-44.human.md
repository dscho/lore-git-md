This is a performance optimization series from Taylor Blau targeting the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, specifically focused on speeding up bitmap generation for large repositories. The series builds on his previous work with pseudo-merge bitmaps and has already undergone significant review from Jeff King (Peff).

This v2 reroll makes minor refinements to the earlier series:
- Small refactoring in the object position caching patches
- Removal of a stale commit message paragraph about follow-up work that's no longer part of the series

The core optimizations remain unchanged from v1, delivering substantial performance wins:
- ~50% speedup in bitmap generation time (612s->294s in test repo)
- 72% reduction in bitmap file size (635MB->176MB)
- Reduced pseudo-merge overhead to just 11.7% while maintaining 20x traversal speedup

The series appears ready for integration, with Jeff King's thorough review having addressed all technical concerns. The changes focus on internal optimizations without affecting user-facing behavior, making this a low-risk performance win for large repositories.
