This thread discusses a small but meaningful optimization to Git's commit graph traversal code. René Scharfe identified that `paint_down_to_common()` in `commit-reach.c` was performing an unnecessary date-based sort of its results, even though none of its callers actually needed sorted output. His patch simply removes this redundant operation.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the change positively, noting it as a clear improvement while suggesting a potential follow-up optimization - having callers pass NULL when they don't need the result list at all. In the latest exchange, René agrees this could be a further refinement but notes it would require adding NULL checks to the function. Both acknowledge that while this additional change might offer minor performance benefits, the primary value of the current patch is in simplifying the code, since merge base lists are typically small anyway.

The discussion shows two of Git's most experienced contributors converging on a clean, minimal solution - René's original patch stands as a clear win, while Peff's suggestion remains a possible future optimization. The exchange is characteristically technical and focused, reflecting both contributors' deep knowledge of Git's internals and their shared preference for straightforward, maintainable code.
