This is a substantive review of Taylor Blau's bitmap optimization patch in his performance series. Jeff King (Peff) provides detailed technical analysis while confirming the patch's effectiveness.

Peff acknowledges the counterintuitive nature of adding a cache layer but validates the approach based on the significant performance improvements shown (26% speedup on linux.git). He explores alternative approaches like converting the packlist to a hash table but concludes the current solution is appropriate for this specific use case. The review identifies several key technical considerations:

1. The cache's O(1)-ish performance characteristics compared to the packlist's O(log n) lookups
2. The tradeoffs in storing full OIDs versus pointers
3. The clever handling of very large positions via BITMAP_POS_CACHE_VALID
4. Potential applicability to bitmap reading operations

While expressing some aesthetic reservations about adding caching complexity, Peff ultimately approves the patch as "cleanly done" and confirms its correctness. The review demonstrates deep understanding of both the immediate change and its broader architectural implications, typical of Peff's thorough review style. This continues the pattern of domain experts carefully examining Taylor's bitmap optimizations while validating their substantial performance benefits.
