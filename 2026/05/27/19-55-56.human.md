Performance optimization in Taylor Blau's bitmap generation work, part 3/8 of his series. This patch focuses on reusing stored bitmaps during the commit traversal phase to avoid redundant work when processing commits that already have bitmaps computed.

The key insight is recognizing that when `fill_bitmap_commit()` encounters an ancestor commit that was already processed (and thus has its bitmap stored in `writer->bitmaps`), it can simply OR that stored bitmap into the current one and skip walking the commit's tree and parents. This provides a substantial 42% speedup in the test repository, with corresponding reductions in CPU cycles and instructions.

The patch adds counters to track how often this optimization triggers (131 out of 1,382 calls in the test case, or ~9.5%) and includes the metrics in trace2 output. The change builds naturally on the position-first approach established in earlier patches while maintaining the same careful measurement and validation approach that has characterized the series so far.

This continues Taylor's pattern of deep, subsystem-specific optimizations that yield measurable real-world improvements without changing user-visible behavior. The patch appears technically sound and well-integrated with the surrounding bitmap generation logic.
