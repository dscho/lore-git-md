This is the second patch in a two-part series removing deprecated functions from commit.h. The patch follows through on a deprecation planned since Git 2.53.0, removing compatibility wrappers that were kept around to ease the transition for in-flight topics.

The changes are mechanical and non-controversial:
- Removes three deprecated inline functions from commit.h (`copy_commit_list`, `reverse_commit_list`, and `free_commit_list`)
- These were already marked for removal and have direct replacements (`commit_list_copy`, `commit_list_reverse`, and `commit_list_free`)
- The first patch in the series updated all callers to use the modern equivalents

The author has verified there are no remaining uses of these functions in the `seen` or `next` branches. This appears to be a straightforward cleanup that follows the project's deprecation policy - the functions were first marked obsolete, then removed after ensuring no active code still depended on them. The patch touches only commit.h and completes the work started in the first patch of the series. Given the mechanical nature and prior deprecation notice, this change is unlikely to generate discussion.
