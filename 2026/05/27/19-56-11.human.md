This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, focusing on pseudo-merge bitmap generation. The series has demonstrated consistent 60%+ speedups for large repositories through incremental optimizations.

The key change in this patch restructures how pseudo-merge bitmaps are built - separating them from the regular bitmap generation pass. Previously, pseudo-merges were treated like regular commits during bitmap construction, which caused significant slowdowns (95% overhead). The new approach:

1. First builds regular commit bitmaps without pseudo-merges
2. Then generates pseudo-merge bitmaps in a separate pass
3. Reuses existing pseudo-merge bitmaps when possible

The results are dramatic - reducing the pseudo-merge overhead from 95% to just 11.7% while maintaining the same on-disk format. The patch includes thorough performance measurements showing the improvement, with the cumulative series now making bitmap generation with pseudo-merges 46% faster than the original non-pseudo-merge generation time.

The implementation carefully preserves all existing behavior while restructuring the internal generation order.
