This thread addresses a macOS-specific linker warning related to the `packet_buffer` global variable in pkt-line.c. The warning occurs due to alignment constraints when linking the large (65,520 byte) uninitialized buffer on macOS. The proposed solution is straightforward - initialize the array to zeros to avoid the warning. 

The patch appears to be a legitimate platform-specific fix, as evidenced by the build output showing repeated warnings. The change is minimal and focused, just modifying the buffer declaration to include initialization. Since the buffer is always written to before being read, the zero-initialization shouldn't impact behavior. This seems like a clean solution to a real build issue on macOS.
