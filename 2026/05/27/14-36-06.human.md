This is a technical discussion in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem. The exchange focuses on patch 1/8, which restructures `fill_bitmap_tree()` to first find a tree's bit position before loading its contents, yielding an unexpected but measurable 6% speedup on linux.git.

Jeff King (Peff) had expressed surprise at the performance gain since the change shouldn't theoretically affect normal cases where objects are found in the pack/midx. Taylor confirms this analysis, noting he had added a BUG() check during development that never triggered, confirming the optimization path is rarely taken. Both agree the speedup likely comes from favorable cache behavior or code generation from reordering operations, even if the exact mechanism isn't fully understood.

The discussion demonstrates the project's careful approach to performance optimizations - even when results are counterintuitive, reproducible improvements are valued when they don't compromise correctness. The patch remains valuable both for its immediate speedup and as groundwork for subsequent optimizations in the series. With Peff's confirmation of the 6% improvement on his system, this technical discussion appears resolved.
