This is the second patch in a two-part cleanup series removing deprecated functions from commit.h. The patch follows through on a deprecation planned since Git 2.53.0, removing compatibility wrappers that were kept around to avoid breaking in-flight topics.

The changes are straightforward: the patch deletes three deprecated inline functions (`copy_commit_list`, `reverse_commit_list`, and `free_commit_list`) that were previously marked for removal. These functions were simple wrappers around their modern equivalents (`commit_list_copy`, `commit_list_reverse`, and `commit_list_free` respectively).

The author has verified there are no remaining uses of these functions in the `seen` or `next` integration branches. This appears to be a well-executed cleanup - the patch is focused, mechanical, and follows the project's deprecation policy. The first patch in the series (already summarized) updated all callers to use the modern function names.

Given the mechanical nature of the change and the prior deprecation notice, this patch is unlikely to generate controversy. It's a good example of routine codebase hygiene following established project practices.
