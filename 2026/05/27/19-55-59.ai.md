This is the fourth patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, part of his ongoing work to improve bitmap generation performance for large repositories. The series builds on his previous pseudo-merge bitmap work.

The current patch is a refactoring that consolidates the success path in `find_object_pos()` by storing the result in a local `pos` variable and sharing the common success return path between the packlist and MIDX cases. This prepares for future bookkeeping around object-position lookups while maintaining identical behavior. The change is mechanical and doesn't affect performance directly, but sets up for subsequent optimizations in the series.

Jeff King has reviewed previous patches in this series and confirmed their technical correctness and performance improvements. The patch follows the established pattern of incremental, instrumented changes that Taylor has demonstrated throughout this optimization effort. The series continues to show deep expertise in the pack/bitmap subsystems with clear technical alignment from reviewers.
