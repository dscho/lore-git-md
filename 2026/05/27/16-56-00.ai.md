This is a technical follow-up in Taylor Blau's bitmap optimization series, responding to Jeff King's (Peff) review questions about the commit sorting patch.

Taylor confirms the difficulty of benchmarking pre-v2.31 bitmap generation due to its extreme slowness, sharing a backport patch he attempted to use for testing. He provides performance measurements showing the sorting optimization has minimal impact on read-side operations - a slight slowdown with pseudo-merges (2.18s->2.61s) and slight speedup without them (52.15s->51.96s), both within noise margins. The response validates Peff's technical assessment while acknowledging the practical constraints of historical benchmarking.

The exchange demonstrates the thoroughness of Git's review culture, with both contributors engaging deeply on performance characteristics across different repository sizes and Git versions. Taylor's detailed response, including the shared test code, helps close the discussion while leaving open the historical context question due to testing constraints.
