This is the fifth patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for the pack-bitmap-write subsystem, building on his earlier pseudo-merge bitmap work. The series targets significant speed improvements for large repositories.

The patch introduces an object position cache to reduce repeated lookups during bitmap generation. In large repositories (~57M objects), the overhead of translating object IDs to bit positions was substantial. The new cache:

- Uses a direct-mapped design with fixed bounds (1K to 2M entries, max 80MB)
- Shows a 9.4% speedup in benchmarks (324.8s -> 294.1s)
- Achieves 1.024B cache hits vs 59.957M misses in testing
- Adds trace2 metrics for cache performance monitoring
- Maintains the series' pattern of careful, instrumented changes

The change is mechanical but enables further optimizations by reducing lookup overhead. Jeff King has reviewed earlier versions, and the approach aligns with the series' goal of halving bitmap generation time through incremental improvements. The patch appears well-constructed with clear performance measurements and maintains backward compatibility.
