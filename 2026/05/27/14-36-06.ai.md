Performance optimization discussion in the pack-bitmap-write series.

Taylor Blau responds to Jeff King's review of the first patch in his bitmap generation optimization series. The discussion centers around an unexpected performance improvement (6% speedup) from reordering operations in `fill_bitmap_tree()`, where the change shouldn't theoretically affect normal cases where objects are found in the pack/midx.

Taylor confirms he had initially added a BUG() check for the unexpected "not found" case but couldn't trigger it in testing. Both agree the speedup likely comes from improved cache behavior or code generation from the reordering, though the exact mechanism remains unclear. Jeff King had verified the 6% improvement on linux.git with gcc/x86, and Taylor notes the change also prepares for subsequent optimizations in the series.

This exchange demonstrates the project's careful approach to performance optimizations - even when results are empirically positive, reviewers and authors work to understand the underlying mechanisms. The discussion remains technical and productive, with both parties satisfied to proceed given the measurable benefits and the patch's role in enabling future improvements.
