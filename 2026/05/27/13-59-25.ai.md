This is a straightforward cleanup patch that replaces deprecated commit list functions with their modern equivalents. It's part of a two-patch series to remove long-deprecated functions from commit.h that were marked for removal after Git 2.53.0.

The patch mechanically updates three call sites to use `commit_list_free()` instead of the deprecated `free_commit_list()` function. The changes touch:
- `builtin/history.c` (two call sites)
- `replay.c` (one call site)
- `upload-pack.c` (two call sites)

The author, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, notes they've verified there are no remaining uses of these deprecated functions in the `seen` or `next` branches. This appears to be a well-scoped maintenance change that follows through on a planned deprecation, with no technical complexity or controversy expected. The patch is cleanly focused on its stated goal of preparing for the removal of deprecated functions in the follow-up commit.
