This thread began as a straightforward cleanup removing three deprecated commit list functions (`copy_commit_list`, `reverse_commit_list`, `free_commit_list`) that had been marked for removal since Git 2.53.0. Kristoffer Haugsbakk's two-patch series (now at v2) updates all callers to their modern equivalents and removes the deprecated functions, with Patrick Steinhardt (the original API author) having given his Acked-by. The technical work is complete and ready for merging.

The discussion has since evolved into a broader conversation about Git's deprecation workflow. Jeff King (Peff) questioned whether the standard deprecation period is always necessary for straightforward cases like this, while Junio Hamano defended the current process as a way to distribute maintenance work across the community rather than concentrating it on the maintainer. In a collegial exchange, Peff acknowledged Junio's perspective but raised two counterpoints about topic authors naturally handling updates during rebasing and the tradeoff between fixup effort and new topic management.

While no process changes are being proposed, this thread provides valuable insight into how senior contributors weigh workflow tradeoffs. The original technical cleanup remains uncontested and will proceed independently of this higher-level discussion.
