This thread covers a straightforward cleanup series removing deprecated commit list functions that were marked for removal after Git 2.53.0. Kristoffer Haugsbakk is driving the effort, which replaces three obsolete functions (`free_commit_list`, `copy_commit_list`, and `reverse_commit_list`) with their modern equivalents (`commit_list_free`, `commit_list_copy`, and `commit_list_reverse`). The changes are mechanical updates to callers in builtin/history.c, replay.c, and upload-pack.c, followed by removing the deprecated declarations from commit.h.

Patrick Steinhardt, who originally authored both the old and new APIs, has explicitly approved the removal with a humorous acknowledgment that his own code was the primary user of these deprecated functions. Kristoffer confirmed he specifically verified there are no remaining uses in the integration branches. The series is now at v2, with no substantive changes from v1 - just a rebase and refreshed cover letter. This cleanup exemplifies Git's standard deprecation workflow: marking functions obsolete, migrating callers, and finally removing the old APIs after verification. The work is uncontroversial and ready for merging.
