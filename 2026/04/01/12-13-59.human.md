This is a follow-up discussion about fsync behavior in the reftable portability series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio C Hamano's feedback on patch 2/6, which removes direct `fsync()` calls from the reftable stack implementation.

Patrick acknowledges Junio's concern that silently succeeding when no fsync callback is provided might be surprising behavior. He suggests two possible paths forward: either keep the current callback-based approach (where callers who don't want fsync can provide a no-op function) or move the fsync functionality into the new "system.{c,h}" interface being introduced in the series.

The exchange shows Patrick is open to revising the patch to make the fsync behavior more explicit, though he hasn't yet committed to a specific approach. This continues the thread's theme of carefully considering the contract between the reftable library and its callers, particularly around system-level operations. The discussion remains constructive, with both parties focused on finding the right balance between safety and explicitness in the API design.
