The Git Rev News team has published their 133rd edition, covering Git project developments from March 2026. Edited by Christian Couder with contributions from Olamide Caleb Bello, Bruno Brito, Štěpán Němec, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk, this community-driven newsletter provides a digest of key discussions, features, and fixes for those who don't follow every mailing list thread. The team has already opened issue #842 for contributions to the next edition, continuing their tradition of community involvement. The newsletter is available on git.github.io alongside other project resources.
