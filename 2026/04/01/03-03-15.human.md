This is the final patch in Justin Tobler's series completing the write operations interface for Git's object database (ODB) transactions, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch makes object writing pluggable by introducing a `write_object_stream()` callback in `struct odb_transaction`, allowing different ODB implementations to handle writes in their transactions.

The change renames `index_blob_packfile_transaction()` to the more generic `odb_transaction_files_write_object_stream()` and wires it up as the default implementation for the files backend. This completes the transition from direct packfile writes to using the transaction interface, with all callers now going through `odb_transaction_write_object_stream()`.

The patch appears well-structured and follows the pattern established in earlier parts of the series. It has already incorporated review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt, who has been actively involved in reviewing this effort. With this final piece in place, the series provides a complete foundation for future storage backends to implement their own write strategies while maintaining a consistent interface.
