April Fools' Day patch from Patrick Steinhardt proposing MD5 as a new hash algorithm option, satirizing the SHA-256 migration effort. The well-crafted patch implements full MD5 support including: hash algorithm infrastructure in hash.c/hash.h, chunk format handling, reftable backend, pack mtimes processing, and pkt-line parsing, using OpenSSL's EVP API. The commit message humorously argues MD5's shorter hashes (32 chars vs SHA-1's 40 or SHA-256's 64) provide sufficient collision resistance for Git's needs while quoting Linus Torvalds' original hash choice philosophy. Kristoffer Haugsbakk playfully endorsed the joke with "I've been waiting years for this!" and asked when it would be packaged on CD-ROM, highlighting the proposal's satirical nature through references to outdated technology. The exchange serves as April 1st humor, demonstrating Git's hash algorithm extensibility while poking fun at the SHA-256 transition process.
