Junio Hamano has acknowledged and apologized for an authorship discrepancy in commit 250e977a, which was originally part of René Scharfe's strvec_pushv() modernization series. The issue arose during merge conflict resolution when the contrib/coccinelle/ directory was reorganized, causing Junio to appear as the author while René's sign-off remained intact. Both maintainers have confirmed this was purely a metadata oversight with no impact on the technical content of the commit, which properly implements the strvec_pushv() improvements as René designed.

This clarification comes as part of Junio's ongoing "What's cooking" status report, which also tracks several other active efforts: Patrick Steinhardt's reftable portability work (awaiting a v2 submission) and ODB cleanup (ready for merging), Taylor Blau's MIDX repacking improvements, and Adrian Ratiu's parallel hooks implementation. The project maintains its characteristic transparency by documenting even minor metadata issues like this authorship clarification in keeping with its meticulous approach to version control history.
