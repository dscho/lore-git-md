3. Tests directory-prefix matching with trailing slashes
4. Verifies symlinked worktree paths are resolved before matching

The test cases are well-structured and cover edge cases that weren't previously tested, particularly around symlinks and multiple worktrees. The patch appears technically sound with clear assertions and follows Git's test style conventions.
