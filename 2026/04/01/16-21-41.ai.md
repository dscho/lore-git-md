Eric Sunshine responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that converted quoted heredocs to unquoted ones to work around a multibyte character handling bug in Dash v0.5.13. While agreeing with the technical solution, Eric suggests adding in-code comments to prevent future contributors from accidentally reverting the workaround by "modernizing" the heredoc syntax back to quoted form.

Eric proposes two types of comments: a header comment explaining the Dash bug (marked with *DONTQUOTE*) and per-usage notes referencing that explanation. This would make the intentional deviation from normal quoting conventions self-documenting. The suggestion reflects Eric's characteristic attention to long-term maintainability and his experience seeing how such workarounds can get lost over time.

The exchange remains focused on test infrastructure improvements, with Eric building on Patrick's technical analysis of the shell bug while adding documentation considerations. No new technical issues are raised about the patch itself - the discussion is purely about making the workaround more maintainable.
