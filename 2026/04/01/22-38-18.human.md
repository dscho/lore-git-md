This is an announcement for the 133rd edition of Git Rev News, the project's monthly newsletter summarizing recent development activity. Christian Couder shares the latest issue, which includes contributions from Olamide Caleb Bello, Bruno Brito, Štěpán Němec, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk. The newsletter provides a digest of mailing list discussions, new features, and notable changes for those who want to stay informed without following every thread. 

The announcement also invites contributions for the next edition, with an open issue on GitHub for anyone who wants to help summarize recent developments. This is part of the project's ongoing effort to make Git's development more accessible to casual observers and potential contributors. The newsletter team (Christian Couder, Jakub Narębski, Markus Jansen, and Kaartic Sivaraam) maintains a regular monthly cadence.
