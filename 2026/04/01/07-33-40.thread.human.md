Chen Linxuan has proposed adding new `includeIf` conditions for matching worktree paths in Git configs, addressing a limitation in multi-worktree setups where current `gitdir` conditions reference the main repository rather than the actual worktree path. The initial 3-patch series introduces `worktree:<pattern>` and `worktree/i:<pattern>` keywords for path glob matching, with thorough implementation in `config.c`, comprehensive tests, and clear documentation.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk provided the first review, noting a minor procedural oversight - while the cover letter had a Signed-off-by trailer, the individual patches did not. This is an easily fixed formality that doesn't affect the technical merits of the proposal. The quality of the initial submission and the nature of this first review suggest the feature may be well-received once this documentation detail is addressed in a v2 submission.
