Thread identity: Performance optimization series for partial clone behavior in `git cherry` and `git grep` commands, authored by Elijah Newren.

Core problem: Partial clones trade upfront blob downloads for on-demand fetching, but some commands suffer severe performance penalties when hundreds of single-blob fetches each take 3+ seconds. This series extends batch prefetching (from checkout/diff/merge) to `git cherry` and `git grep`.
