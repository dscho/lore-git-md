This thread covers performance optimizations for partial clone behavior in `git cherry` and `git grep`, where batch prefetching avoids the latency of hundreds of individual blob fetches. Elijah Newren's series is complete and ready to merge, with implementations for both commands that follow established prefetch patterns. The `git cherry` optimization handles root/merge commits and binary files, while `git grep` collects blob OIDs during tree walks and excludes submodules.

Phillip Wood's recent review raises an important architectural consideration: the cherry-pick detection prefetching could be generalized beyond just `git cherry`, as similar logic in `git rebase` (without `--reapply-cherry-picks`) could benefit from the same optimization. While the current implementation is technically sound, Phillip points out this might be too command-specific. The question now is whether to adjust the series to share this logic more broadly or handle it as follow-up work. Junio had previously approved the series after a documentation tweak, so this new insight may prompt either a small extension or a note about future optimization opportunities.
