Elijah Newren has completed a performance optimization series that improves `git cherry` and `git grep` operations in partial clone scenarios by batching blob fetches. The implementation follows established patterns from other commands and is ready for merging after addressing maintainer feedback to remove a design document.

Phillip Wood raised an insightful point about potentially generalizing the cherry-pick prefetch logic to benefit `git rebase` and other commands through `revision.c`. In response, Elijah acknowledged the technical merit but expressed reservations about prioritizing this optimization path. He noted that users with large repositories needing partial clones might be better served by avoiding cherry-pick detection entirely rather than optimizing it, and that his current work addressed a specific customer need rather than being part of a broader initiative. While open to reviewing such an extension if others pursue it, Elijah considers the current series complete as-is.

The thread shows consensus on merging the immediate optimizations while leaving the door open for future work on cherry-pick detection performance - a decision that balances solving today's concrete problems with maintaining architectural flexibility.
