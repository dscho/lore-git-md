This is part 8 of Patrick Steinhardt's 12-part series to enable `set -e` in Git's test suite (Bash 5+ only), focusing on making the test infrastructure more robust by catching silent failures. The series has been carefully reviewed by Jeff King (Peff) and is now in its third iteration.

This patch specifically addresses t0008-ignores.sh, where a `grep -v` command in a subshell is expected to sometimes fail (when it finds no matches) but currently swallows the error. With `set -e` coming, these failures would cause the test to abort. The fix is simple: append `|| :` to silence the expected non-zero exit code from `grep -v`.

The change is minimal and mechanical - just two lines modified to add the error suppression. This follows the pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, where expected failures are explicitly handled rather than relying on the test suite's current leniency. The patch maintains the test's behavior while making it compatible with stricter error checking.

As with previous patches in the series, this demonstrates careful attention to backward compatibility and incremental improvement. The change is clearly motivated and well-contained, continuing the series' pattern of small, focused patches that methodically prepare the test suite for `set -e`.
