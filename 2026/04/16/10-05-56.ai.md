This is a follow-up discussion about process improvements for patch submissions, specifically regarding the autostash feature series that was recently merged. Harald Nordgren responds to Junio Hamano's feedback about making version-to-version changes more readable in cover letters.

This exchange shows the project's iterative approach to both code and process - even after a successful merge, participants discuss how to make future contributions smoother. The technical content of the autostash series itself remains unchanged; this is purely about improving the patch submission workflow.
