Here's a human-readable summary of the gitk bugfix thread:

The thread addresses a long-standing race condition in gitk where switching views while diffs are still processing for a large commit could crash with a "no such variable" error. The issue stems from gitk's background task queue (runq) continuing to process operations after a view change clears the treediffs data they depend on.

The proposed fix introduces synchronization to prevent this race by adding a runq_active counter and wait_runq_empty procedure. This ensures view changes wait for pending diff operations to complete before proceeding. While effective, the solution comes with a performance tradeoff - it may wait for operations that become irrelevant after the view change. The author acknowledges this isn't ideal but found no clean way to selectively cancel pending diffs.

The patch demonstrates solid understanding of gitk's Tcl/Tk codebase and includes a reproducible test case. It touches core scheduling machinery, modifying how background operations are tracked. This appears to be the first proper fix for an issue that's been reported since at least 2013, though future optimizations could make the synchronization more selective. The changes are focused on gitk GUI specifically and don't affect Git's core functionality.
