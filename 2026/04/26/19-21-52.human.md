This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about fixing the regression in Git's alias handling. Jonatan Holmgren, the author of the original patch that introduced the regression, responds to Jeff King's proposal for handling dotted aliases.

Holmgren expresses concerns about King's "overlapping-namespace" approach where `alias.foo.bar` would be interpreted both as a single alias name and as a structured key. He acknowledges its compatibility benefits but argues it makes the configuration model harder to understand and document. Instead, he prefers his original approach that would restore dotted aliases except when they conflict with explicitly reserved keys like `command`.

The email highlights the tension between maintaining backward compatibility (important for existing workflows) and keeping the system clean for future extensions. Holmgren suggests that if namespace cleanliness becomes a priority, reserving specific prefixes would be better than moving to a completely separate namespace like `nalias`. He also raises an important question about whether dotted aliases should be considered a long-term supported feature or just a legacy behavior to be phased out.

The discussion remains technical and constructive, with both approaches appearing technically sound but differing in their tradeoffs between complexity and compatibility. The thread is now waiting for maintainer input to decide between these approaches before the regression fix can proceed.
