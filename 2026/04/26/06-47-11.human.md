This is a follow-up discussion about an Italian localization (l10n) patch that fixes alignment in help text output. The original patch changed the Italian translation of "or:" from "oppure:" to "o:" to maintain proper alignment with "usage:" lines in command help text.

Matteo Beniamino, the patch author, responds to Jiang Xin's review by noting that the Italian translation repository listed in TEAMS hasn't been updated in over five years. He mentions having opened a PR on the git-l10n repo, but it's failing checks due to obsolete entries. Matteo suggests Alessandro Menti (the Italian localization lead) might provide clarity on the current state of Italian translations.

The email also points out a minor workflow inconsistency - the default PR message on git-l10n still instructs contributors to use the mailing list, which Matteo notes might be confusing since it's inherited from the main Git repo but doesn't match the l10n workflow.

This exchange highlights the practical challenges of maintaining translations when team leadership may have lapsed, while also showing the collaborative nature of Git's localization efforts. The technical content of the patch itself appears settled, with the remaining questions being about process and repository maintenance rather than the alignment fix.
