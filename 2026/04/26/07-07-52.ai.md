The email doesn't advance the technical discussion about the `--fetch` feature itself, which remains in its previous state (v2 patch submitted but awaiting maintainer feedback). The exchange reveals differing workflow preferences among contributors, with Kristoffer representing the view that stable subjects aid asynchronous participation, especially for those who dip in and out of threads.
