This is a follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, where Olamide Caleb Bello is migrating eight configuration variables from global storage into `struct repo_config_values`. The patch series touches 20 files across various subsystems to eliminate direct global variable access in favor of repository-specific storage.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, converting configuration handling for compression settings, Unicode path handling, sparse-checkout behaviors, and now also ambiguous refname warnings. The series maintains the established pattern of keeping eager parsing semantics while preventing cross-repository state leakage - a key goal of this multi-year refactoring.

Junio notes a minor inconsistency in the commit message prefixes (some say "environment:" while others say "env:"), suggesting the author may want to standardize these. The diffstat shows the typical pattern of many small, focused changes rather than deep modifications to any single component. This continues the incremental but important work to eliminate Git's reliance on global variables.
