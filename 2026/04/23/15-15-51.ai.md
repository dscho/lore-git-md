Performance optimization discussion about cherry-pick prefetch generalization in rebase.

Phillip Wood follows up on Elijah Newren's response about sharing the cherry-pick prefetch logic with `revision.c` for use in `git rebase`. He raises two practical considerations:

1. Asks whether patch ID computation in rebase operates on all upstream commits or just those modifying the same paths as the rebased branch, suggesting potential optimization opportunities.

2. Notes the lack of a config setting for `--reapply-cherry-picks` (which avoids the expensive patch ID computation) and the complication that this option isn't supported by the apply backend.

Phillip expresses surprise that more users haven't complained about rebase performance in partial clones, given the known slowdowns. The exchange remains cordial, with Phillip accepting Elijah's position while surfacing real-world implications of the current design choices. The thread shows technical alignment on how the optimization could be implemented, but differing perspectives on its priority given workflow alternatives.
