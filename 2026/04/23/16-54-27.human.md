This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically migrating the `zlib_compression_level` configuration from a process-wide global variable to repository-specific storage in `struct repo_config_values`. The patch follows the established pattern for these configuration migrations.

The change affects six files that use zlib compression, converting direct global variable access to repository-specific lookups. The commit message explains the rationale for keeping eager parsing (needed for consistent compression behavior) while preventing cross-repository state leakage. The mechanical nature of the changes and limited scope (just 8 lines net change) make this a straightforward step in the larger refactoring effort.
