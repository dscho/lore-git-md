He maintains that his reserved-prefix approach provides clearer semantics while still supporting existing workflows. The discussion has touched on whether dotted aliases were ever an intentional feature, with Holmgren suggesting they may have been accidental but are now established in real-world use. He strongly opposes Junio Hamano's earlier suggestion of using a separate `nalias` namespace, seeing it as worse for users.

With two technically sound but philosophically different solutions on the table, the thread now awaits maintainer guidance on which direction to take. The decision will affect both immediate backward compatibility and the long-term design of Git's alias system.
