Feature: Subcommand autocorrection refinement

This patch is part of Jiamu Sun's now-complete series adding subcommand autocorrection to Git's parse-options API, which has received maintainer approval. The series provides consistent autocorrection behavior whether users mistype a main command or subcommand.

This particular patch is a straightforward renaming of the AUTOCORRECT_SHOW enum value to AUTOCORRECT_HINT to better reflect its purpose - showing similar commands without taking further action. The change is mechanical but improves clarity, touching three files where the enum is used. The patch follows the pattern of the series' careful attention to naming and semantics.

With all major technical questions resolved in previous iterations, this appears to be one of the final polish patches before the feature is ready for merging. The series as a whole demonstrates thorough implementation and testing, with this change being a small but meaningful improvement to the API's expressiveness.
