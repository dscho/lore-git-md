This thread documents the routine localization updates for Git 2.54.0, coordinated by Jiang Xin through the git-l10n/git-po repository. The second version of these updates has now been merged by Junio C Hamano, confirming it as a superset of the earlier v1 changes.

The most substantial changes are in the Traditional Chinese (zh_TW) translations, with over 1,800 insertions and 900 deletions representing either a major refresh or significant corrections, including a fix for the "Applying patch" string. Alexander Shopov continues his ongoing maintenance of Bulgarian translations, while Jean-Noël Avila contributes French updates as part of his broader documentation standardization work. The series also includes smaller updates for Catalan, Irish, Swedish and Turkish (from Emir SARI) translations, plus new contributor guidelines (AGENTS.md) and translation file management documentation.

This follows Git's standard localization workflow with no reported issues, marking another successful synchronization of non-English translations with the English source strings ahead of the 2.54.0 release.
