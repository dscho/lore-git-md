This is a review from Elijah Newren on Patrick Steinhardt's patch to remove `the_repository` usage in `setup_work_tree()`. While acknowledging the correctness of removing the `work_tree_config_is_bogus` global variable, Elijah points out that another global (`initialized`) remains in the function, which still limits the ability to set up multiple worktrees in a single process.

The review is substantive - it identifies a logical inconsistency in the refactoring approach where one global was removed while another remains, potentially undermining the multi-repository goals of the_repository removal effort. Elijah suggests moving `initialized` into `struct repository` as well, which would be consistent with the series' architectural direction.

This feedback comes late in the review cycle (after Karthik Nayak's approval) and from a respected contributor with deep merge machinery expertise, though not directly in the setup.c domain. The observation is technically sound but doesn't block the current patch - it's more of a "while you're here" note about future work.
