He catches a potential logic error in Peff's proposed implementation (suggesting `||` instead of `&&` for the condition) and generally supports making the stricter error checking more widely available through an opt-in mechanism.

The exchange shows continued interest in improving test suite robustness while maintaining flexibility across different shell environments. Knoble's response indicates this remains an active area of discussion even after the original series was merged, with developers exploring ways to expand the benefits of Bash 5+'s `set -e` behavior to other contexts.
