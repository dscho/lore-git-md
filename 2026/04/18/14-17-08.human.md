This is the third patch in Lorenzo Pegorari's v6 series improving promisor file handling during repacks, part of his GSoC project. The patch focuses on preserving the debug information in `.promisor` files when packfiles are repacked, rather than creating empty files as before.

For geometric repacking (a newer strategy that splits packs based on size), the patch adds logic to track excluded packs using a `strset`.
