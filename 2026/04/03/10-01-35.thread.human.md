This thread is about a bugfix patch that adds a configurable timeout (`core.configLockTimeout`, defaulting to 1000ms) to Git's config file locking to prevent failures in concurrent operations like `git worktree add -b`. The patch, now at v2, is conceptually accepted and ready for merging to `next` and eventually `master`. The key participants—Jörg Thalheim (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), Patrick Steinhardt, and Johannes Schindelin—have resolved the major technical concerns, though a minor debate about whether to simplify the timeout to be explicitly process-wide (Junio's preference) or retain the current caching behavior (Patrick's counterproposal) was deferred as a non-blocking optimization.

The latest email from Junio expresses mild procedural discomfort about the unresolved caching discussion, noting that after a month of debate, no decisive patch or argument has emerged to settle the question. However, he does not reopen the design or block the patch, which remains procedurally ready for integration. Johannes Schindelin has explicitly endorsed merging the current implementation, urging the project to proceed without further delay and handle any cache-related refinements in a follow-up patch. The patch introduces read-after-lock semantics, comprehensive test coverage, and follows the precedent of `core.packedRefsTimeout`. No open questions are actively blocking its progress.
