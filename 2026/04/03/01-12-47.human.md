This is a CI/build system patch that prepares for future Rust integration by adding Cargo (Rust's package manager) to the Alpine Linux CI environment. It's part of a 4-patch series (position 2/4) from brian m. carlson, who has been involved in Git's hash algorithm transition work.

The change is straightforward - it adds `cargo` to the list of packages installed in the Alpine CI container. The commit message indicates this is groundwork for making Rust the default in a future commit, suggesting this series is part of the ongoing Rustification effort. The patch itself is a simple one-line addition with no apparent issues, though the broader Rustification effort has been somewhat controversial in the project due to platform support concerns.
