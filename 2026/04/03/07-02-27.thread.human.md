This is a patch series (v3) that introduces two new `includeIf` config conditions: `worktree` and `worktree/i`, which match against worktree checkout paths rather than `.git` directory paths. The motivation is to handle multi-worktree setups more naturally -- the existing `gitdir` condition is awkward for linked worktrees because their gitdirs all live under the main repo's `.git/worktrees/` directory.

The series works in two patches: the first refactors the existing `include_by_gitdir()` path-matching into a generic helper, and the second adds the new conditions. Both support glob patterns, recursive `**/` matching, and home directory expansion, matching existing conventions. Use cases include per-worktree `user.email` or signing key configuration.

The series has gone through three iterations with some feedback from Junio C Hamano, the maintainer. In the latest round, Junio called the feature "a worthwhile thing to have" and said it looks "OK-ish" to him, but explicitly declined to do a full review because he is not fully caught up. He asked for one or two volunteer reviewers to give the patches a closer look before he merges them. No technical objections or open questions remain; the series is waiting on a thorough review from the community.
