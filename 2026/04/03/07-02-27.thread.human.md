The thread discusses a patch series from Chen Linxuan that adds `worktree` and `worktree/i`
conditions to Git's `includeIf` config directive, allowing configuration files to be conditionally
included based on which worktree's checkout path matches a glob pattern. This fills a real gap:
the existing `gitdir` condition is awkward in multi-worktree setups because linked worktrees all
store their gitdirs under the main repo's `.git/worktrees/`.

The series has received a full technical review from Patrick Steinhardt, a deeply respected
contributor. He called the preparatory refactoring in patch 1/2 a "good preparatory step" and the
new condition in patch 2/2 "good" and "sensible." His review identified three items for v4: move
the duplicated pattern-matching documentation into a shared section, add a test verifying the
condition works during early config parsing, and add a test for behavior outside any repository.
He also noted that a later, table-driven refactoring of the condition-matching logic would be
natural but deferred it as beyond this series' scope.

Junio had directionally approved the feature as "worthwhile" but explicitly said he would not
perform a full review himself and needed a volunteer before taking the series into `seen`. Patrick's
review of the full two-patch series directly addresses that requirement. The remaining work is
modest: Chen Linxuan has three straightforward changes to incorporate before sending a v4.
