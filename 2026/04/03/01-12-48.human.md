This is the third patch in a series enabling Rust support by default in Git builds, focusing on Linux platform compatibility. The change adds `-ldl` to the linker flags on Linux systems to ensure proper linking with libdl, which is needed for older Rust versions (like those in Debian 11's CI environment). The patch explains this is a no-op on modern systems where libdl is part of libc, but necessary for compatibility. The change is minimal (one line added to `config.mak.uname`) and well-justified, showing the author's characteristic attention to cross-platform details. As part of the broader Rustification effort, this is one of the less controversial technical pieces, though the series as a whole may still attract discussion given the project's mixed feelings about Rust adoption.
