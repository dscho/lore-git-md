Patrick and Justin have been working through these implementation choices, with Patrick committing to refactor the path field handling while carefully considering the implications of mixing different ODB source types.

The series is technically sound and moving forward, though some interface alignment with Justin's parallel work may be needed. Patrick's approach balances immediate needs with long-term flexibility in the ODB abstraction effort.
