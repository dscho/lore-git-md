This is a documentation patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's series clarifying git-interpret-trailers behavior. The patch standardizes on the term "trailer block" throughout the documentation, building on earlier patches that introduced this terminology.

The change refines the explanation of how trailers are added and located in messages, replacing more verbose descriptions with consistent use of "trailer block" as the conceptual unit. It clarifies that:
- New trailers are added at the end of the existing trailer block
- A trailer block will be created if none exists
- The blank line requirement is tied to the block concept
- The end-of-message detection rules apply to the block as a whole

The patch follows through on terminology established earlier in the series, making the documentation more precise while maintaining readability. It's part of a well-structured effort to document the intentionally strict parsing behavior of git-interpret-trailers, with this piece focusing on standardizing the vocabulary used to describe the trailer grouping mechanism. The changes are mechanical but meaningful, replacing circumlocutions with the now-defined term.
