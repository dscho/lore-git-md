This is a documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of a 9-patch series clarifying git-interpret-trailers' behavior around trailer key formats. The series emerged from a discussion about how periods in keys (like "My-6.11-Version") were silently rejected, despite no clear documentation of this constraint.

This first patch removes references to RFC 822 email headers from the git-interpret-trailers man page, arguing that comparing trailers to an obscure email standard is unnecessary and potentially confusing. Instead, it presents trailers simply as metadata key-value pairs with colon separators. The change makes the documentation more approachable while maintaining technical accuracy.

The patch is well-motivated and cleanly executed, removing outdated comparisons while preserving the essential description of trailer format. It's part of a larger effort to make the documentation more self-contained and example-driven, directly addressing the confusion that led to the original bug report. The series has already incorporated maintainer feedback and appears ready for merging.
