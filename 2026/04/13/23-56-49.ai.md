This is the fourth patch in Taylor Blau's bugfix series for pseudo-merge bitmaps, addressing a critical binary search implementation error. The patch fixes an inverted comparison in `pseudo_merge_at()` that was causing the search to look in the wrong direction when multiple pseudo-merge groups existed.

The key technical change swaps the `hi = mi` and `lo = mi + 1` assignments in the binary search logic, correcting the lookup direction. This enables proper pseudo-merge application during fill-in traversal when multiple groups are present, as demonstrated by the now-passing test case that previously failed. The fix is minimal (just two lines changed) but has significant impact on functionality.
