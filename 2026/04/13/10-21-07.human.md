This is a documentation patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk's series clarifying git-interpret-trailers behavior. It standardizes on the term "trailer block" throughout the man page, building on earlier patches that introduced this terminology.

The patch refines the explanation of how git-interpret-trailers locates and modifies trailer blocks in text. It replaces vague phrasing like "group of lines" with the now-standard "trailer block" term, making the documentation more precise. The changes show how new trailers are added (at the end of an existing block or in a new block if none exists) and how blocks are identified (preceded by blank lines, at message end or before `---` markers).

This is the 8th of 9 patches in v2 of the series, which emerged from a bug report about trailer parsing. The series as a whole has been through thorough review, with Junio Hamano and others providing feedback that Kristoffer has incorporated. The patches are merge-ready, with this one completing the terminology standardization effort.
