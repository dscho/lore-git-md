Patrick Steinhardt's 12-part series to make Git's test suite reliably work with `set -e` (errexit) is nearly complete, having undergone extensive review from Jeff King, brian m. carlson, D. Ben Knoble, and others. The work standardizes on `set -e` for Bash 5+ while maintaining compatibility with other shells via an opt-in `GIT_TEST_USE_SET_E` flag, with CI now enforcing this stricter error checking on Linux platforms. The series has addressed numerous subtle issues across the test suite, from shell arithmetic conversions to proper error handling in test helpers.

Junio C Hamano, in his final review, gives overall approval but raises two specific questions about implementation details: whether a `|| true` suppression in `check_fsck()` is truly necessary, and why this patch uses `|| true` where earlier changes preferred `|| :` for consistency. These are minor points in an otherwise polished series that has already passed all tests across supported shells and platforms. With these last questions addressed, the infrastructure improvement - which will catch previously silent test failures - appears ready for merging.
