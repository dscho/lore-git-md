Patrick Steinhardt continues his series to prepare Git's test suite for `set -e` (errexit) by fixing another edge case - this time in t0008-ignores.sh. The fix adds `|| true` to suppress this expected failure while maintaining the test's behavior.

This is one of many mechanical but important changes needed before enabling `set -e` across the test suite.
