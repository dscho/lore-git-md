This is a test infrastructure patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Git's test suite work with `set -e` (errexit) enabled. It's part 3 of 12 in the series, focusing on fixing the `stop_git_daemon()` helper function.

The patch addresses two issues that would cause test failures when `set -e` is enabled: an unconditional `wait` that expects to fail (but would trigger immediate exit with `set -e`), and a second `kill` call that often fails harmlessly. The solution wraps the `wait` in a conditional to properly capture its exit status and adds `|| :` to suppress the second `kill`'s failure.

Junio Hamano reviews the patch, noting that the same comment from patch 2/12 applies to the early part (likely about commit message style), and agrees that silencing the kill failure makes sense. The changes are mechanical but important for the series' goal of enabling stricter error checking across the test suite.

This is a well-contained change that maintains the function's error checking behavior while making it `set -e` safe.
