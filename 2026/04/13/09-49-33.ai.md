This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 12-part series to prepare Git's test suite for `set -e` (errexit) mode. The series has systematically updated test patterns that would fail under strict error checking, with each patch addressing a specific category of tests.

The culmination enables `set -e` in test-lib.sh, causing the test suite to abort immediately on any unchecked failures outside of test cases. This prevents silent failures like the recent case where a misspelled `test_expect_success` went undetected. The change is minimal (just 4 lines adding the `set -e` directive) but builds on all preceding infrastructure work to ensure tests remain reliable under strict error checking.

The series represents a significant step in Git's ongoing test modernization effort, following Junio Hamano's earlier discussion about adopting `set -e`. Review considerations include verifying no false positives from the error handling changes and maintaining cross-shell compatibility (Bash/Dash). The careful, incremental approach across 12 patches suggests this final switch should be well-tested, though some edge cases may still need follow-up fixes.
