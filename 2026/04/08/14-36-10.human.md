This is the final patch in Derrick Stolee's series adding `--must-have` negotiation support, completing the feature by extending it to push operations. The patch enables push negotiation (via `push.negotiate`) to respect the same `remote.*.mustHave` configuration and command-line options that were added for fetch in earlier patches.

The implementation passes the must-have refs from the transport layer through to the underlying fetch --negotiate-only subprocess, ensuring consistent behavior between fetch and push operations. The change is relatively straightforward, adding a new field to `send_pack_args` and plumbing it through the call stack. A new test case verifies the integration works with remote config, checking both the negotiation rounds and object transfer counts.

This completes the monorepo-focused improvement to Git's negotiation protocol, giving users reliable ways to ensure important branches are always considered during both fetch and push operations. The series appears well-tested and thoughtfully designed, with this final piece maintaining the same attention to detail seen in the earlier patches.
