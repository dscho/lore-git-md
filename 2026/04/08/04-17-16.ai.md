Bugfix investigation for NonStop platform issues with `--git-dir` and `writev()` in Git 2.54.0-rc1.

Jeff King responds to Randall S. Becker's report of test failures in bitmap-related operations (t5310, t5326, t5327) on the NonStop platform. The initial report showed two distinct problems: repository path resolution with `--git-dir` and network protocol errors during cloning from bitmapped repositories.

Peff analyzes the failures and suggests they may stem from different root causes. For the `--git-dir` issue in t5310, he notes the test hasn't changed recently and suspects an earlier test failure may have prevented proper setup of the `clone.git` directory. For the network errors in t5326/t5327, he identifies a more specific culprit - the recent introduction of `writev()` in `sideband_send()`, which appears to be failing on NonStop.

The response provides concrete debugging steps: running tests with `-i` to check for earlier failures and building with `NO_WRITEV=Nope` to test a fallback implementation. This suggests the issues may be platform-specific to NonStop's implementation of `writev()` rather than fundamental problems with the bitmap or `--git-dir` functionality.

The technical details point to subtle interactions between recent changes (the `writev()` optimization) and platform compatibility, with potential implications for the upcoming 2.54.0 release. The response maintains a troubleshooting tone while acknowledging the validity of the reported issues.
