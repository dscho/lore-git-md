Bugfix for SIGPIPE handling in parallel hook execution. This is a follow-up to Jeff King's analysis of a race condition that caused pre-receive hooks to incorrectly fail when they exited without reading input, particularly affecting NonStop platform.

Junio Hamano acknowledges Jeff's proposed fix, which reorders signal handler installation in `run_processes_parallel()` to ensure SIGPIPE is properly ignored even during child process cleanup. The fix addresses both the original NonStop-specific failure and a more general issue that could affect parallel hook execution on any platform.

The patch is timely as it needs to be included before the upcoming 2.54.0-rc2 release. While the technical details were thoroughly discussed in previous emails, Junio's response confirms the solution is sound and will be incorporated. This demonstrates effective collaboration between platform-specific debugging (Randall Becker), core process expertise (Jeff King), and the maintainer (Junio) to resolve an infrastructure-level bug.
