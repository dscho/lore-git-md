Derrick Stolee's series introducing new negotiation controls for fetch and push operations is now complete and ready for merging. The series addresses inefficient object transfers in monorepos by adding two key features: `--negotiation-include` to guarantee certain refs are always advertised, and `--negotiation-restrict` (formerly `--negotiation-tip`) to limit negotiation to specific refs. This solves real-world cases where dropped refs could trigger full-history transfers of 60+ GB.

The final patch completes push integration, ensuring the negotiation controls work consistently whether fetching or pushing. It extends the transport layer to pass the negotiation configurations through to the underlying fetch process that powers push negotiation.
