Derrick Stolee's patch series introducing new negotiation controls for fetch and push operations is now complete and ready for merging. The series addresses inefficient object transfers in monorepos by adding two complementary options: `--negotiation-include` to guarantee certain refs are always advertised, and `--negotiation-restrict` (formerly `--negotiation-tip`) to limit negotiation to specific refs. This solves real-world cases where dropped refs could trigger unnecessary full-history transfers of 60+ GB.
