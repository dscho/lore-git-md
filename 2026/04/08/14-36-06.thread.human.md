Derrick Stolee's series to improve fetch/push negotiation controls for monorepos is nearing completion, with the second version addressing nearly all prior feedback. The implementation introduces two key mechanisms: `--negotiation-require` to guarantee inclusion of specified refs and `--negotiation-restrict` to limit negotiation scope, both available as CLI options and config settings. The series has undergone thorough technical review, with only minor naming and interface polish remaining.

In the latest review, Patrick Steinhardt suggests reconsidering the `--negotiation-require` name to better reflect its additive behavior, proposing alternatives like `--negotiation-include`. He also raises a technical consideration about protocol flushing for large numbers of required refs, though acknowledges the current behavior may be intentional. Importantly, Patrick confirms the interaction between the two negotiation controls works as intended, describing the combined use case clearly. These final suggestions focus on interface clarity rather than functionality, indicating the technical approach is sound. With all substantive feedback addressed, the series appears ready for merging pending any naming refinements.
