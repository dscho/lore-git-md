Derrick Stolee's series improving fetch/push negotiation in monorepos is now complete and ready for merging. The series addresses inefficient object transfers by introducing two complementary controls: `--negotiation-include` to guarantee certain refs are always advertised, and `--negotiation-restrict` (renamed from `--negotiation-tip` for clarity) to limit negotiation to specific refs. This solves cases where dropped refs could trigger full-history transfers of 60+ GB in large repositories.

The series went through six iterations with thorough review from Matthew Cheetham, who approved the technical approach including the key `have_sent()` negotiator callback that enables the include functionality. All patches now carry Cheetham's Reviewed-by tags with the correct email address (mjcheetham@outlook.com). The implementation covers both fetch and push operations, with comprehensive test coverage and proper handling of edge cases across protocols. With all feedback addressed and no outstanding issues, the series awaits maintainer pickup.
