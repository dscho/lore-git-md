This is patch 7/8 of Derrick Stolee's v4 series adding negotiation controls for monorepo scenarios. It adds `remote.<name>.negotiationInclude` as the mirror of the existing `negotiationRestrict` config option from patch 4, together providing config-based control over which refs are always included or restricted in fetch negotiation.

The patch is mechanically sound and follows the exact pattern of its restrict counterpart — `handle_config()` in `remote.c`, `prepare_transport()` in `builtin/fetch.c`, and thorough documentation. Three new test scenarios cover empty-value reset, glob patterns, and CLI override behavior. No review or discussion has appeared on v4 yet.

Patches 1-7 have been posted. Only patch 8/8 (push negotiation wiring) remains to round out the feature. The `have_sent()` abstraction from patch 5 — introduced to address Matthew Cheetham's v3 concern about conflating the COMMON flag with unconditional inclusion — has landed without objection.
