Bugfix test case for SIGPIPE handling in parallel hooks, complementing the recently submitted fix. This patch adds a test to verify that parallel hooks continue working correctly when one hook exits without reading its input, which could previously trigger premature termination due to SIGPIPE handling.

The test creates two parallel hooks - one fast (exiting immediately) and one slow (sleeping for 1 second) - to demonstrate that the SIGPIPE from the fast hook doesn't kill the slow one. This builds on the recently fixed race condition in pre-receive hooks where successful hooks could incorrectly reject pushes if they didn't read input.

Jeff King notes this test should pass already due to the fix he just submitted, and suggests it could be included in Adrian Ratiu's parallel hooks series (either as-is or moved to another test file). The test setup carefully avoids quoting issues by adding scripts to PATH, and includes comments explaining the timing considerations for the slow hook.

This is effectively a follow-up verification step for the SIGPIPE fix, now extended to cover the parallel hooks case. The test demonstrates that the signal handling changes in run-command.c properly maintain isolation between parallel processes, preventing one hook's early exit from affecting others. The patch is ready for inclusion but may need minor adjustment once the fix commit ID is known after Junio applies it.
