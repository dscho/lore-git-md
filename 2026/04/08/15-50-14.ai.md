This is a bugfix addressing a race condition in Git's parallel process execution infrastructure, specifically affecting pre-receive hooks that exit without reading their input. The issue was originally reported by Randall Becker on NonStop systems but has broader implications across platforms.

Jeff King (Peff) identified the root cause in `run-command.c`'s signal handling sequence, where the SIGPIPE ignore handler was being installed before the parallel process initialization, allowing child cleanup handlers to override it. His proposed fix reorders these operations to ensure proper signal handling regardless of execution mode. The change makes SIG_IGN take precedence over child cleanup handlers, preventing incorrect hook failures.

The fix has been confirmed by Randall Becker to resolve the NonStop-specific manifestation, and Peff's analysis shows it also prevents potential race conditions in parallel hook scenarios on Linux. While this touches the same subsystem as Adrian Ratiu's parallel hooks work, it stands as an independent correctness fix for core process handling.

Junio C Hamano notes the fix should be included before the 2.54.0-rc2 release, indicating this is considered an important infrastructure fix. The patch is well-motivated with clear technical explanation and has already received validation from both the original reporter and the expert who proposed the solution.
