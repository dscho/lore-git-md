This is a bugfix patch that has now been merged by Junio Hamano, resolving a SIGPIPE handling issue in parallel process execution. The patch from Jeff King (Peff) corrects the signal handler ordering in `run_processes_parallel()` to prevent premature child process termination when hooks exit without reading all input.

The fix specifically addresses a race condition that was causing test failures on NonStop platforms (originally reported by Randall Becker) while also ensuring compatibility with future parallel hook execution. The key change moves the SIGPIPE ignore handler setup after process initialization to properly override child cleanup handlers, with corresponding reordering during teardown.

Junio has applied the patch after reviewing the technical approach and confirming it addresses both the immediate NonStop issue and potential future parallel execution scenarios.
