**Thread identity**: "negotiator: implement new negotiation and restrict options" series (v6), by Derrick Stolee. Type: patch series (8 patches). Final version after review.

**Core problem/goal**: Address inefficient object transfer in monorepos by providing bidirectional negotiation controls: `--negotiation-include` guarantees specified refs are always advertised during common-object search, while `--negotiation-restrict` (formerly `--negotiation-tip`) limits negotiation to a subset of remote refs. Solves cases where dropped refs (e.g. long-lived release branches) trigger full-history transfers (60+ GB in cited scenario). Now fully integrated for both fetch and push operations.
