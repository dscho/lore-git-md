**Thread identity**: "negotiator: implement new negotiation and restrict options" series (v3-v4), by Derrick Stolee. Type: patch series (7 patches). v3 fully reviewed by Matthew Cheetham; v4 scoped to include structural rework but not yet posted.

**Core problem/goal**: Provide fetch/push negotiation controls for monorepo scenarios: (1) `--negotiation-include` guarantees specified refs are always advertised during common-object search, and (2) `--negotiation-restrict` limits negotiation to a subset of remote refs. Replaces the existing single-valued `--negotiation-tip` with a two-direction mechanism.

Scope has expanded beyond originally planned fixes to include structural rework of the negotiator abstraction and layer unification.
