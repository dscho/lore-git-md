**Core problem/goal**: Provide fetch/push negotiation controls for monorepo scenarios: (1) `--negotiation-include` guarantees that specified refs are always advertised even when not part of the common-object search, and (2) `--negotiation-restrict` limits negotiation to a subset of remote refs. Replaces the existing single-valued `--negotiation-tip` with a more general two-direction mechanism.

**Current status**: Series is complete and ready for integration pending Stolee's v4, which is now scoped to include substantial structural changes beyond the previously-planned fixes. Stolee has acknowledged and accepted every issue Cheetham raised on patch 5/7. v4 will include: (a) a new callback function pointer on the negotiator struct for the consumer to announce "I sent this 'have'", letting the negotiator internally handle deduplication; (b) unification of where `--negotiation-restrict` and `--negotiation-include` resolve refs (both will happen at the builtin layer, not split between builtin and transport); (c) all documentation, test, and typo fixes previously mentioned. This is a larger v4 than Stolee had originally scoped.

**Key technical details**: Unchanged from v3 summary, except that the COMMON-flag abstraction breakage identified by Cheetham in patch 5/7 will be addressed via a new negotiator abstraction mechanism, not a simple bit correction. The ref-resolution layer for both options will be unified at the builtin layer.
