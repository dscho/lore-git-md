**Thread identity**: "negotiator: implement new negotiation and restrict options" series (v4), by Derrick Stolee. Type: patch series (8 patches). All 8 patches now received.

**Core problem/goal**: Provide fetch/push negotiation controls for monorepo scenarios: (1) `--negotiation-include` guarantees specified refs are always advertised during common-object search, and (2) `--negotiation-restrict` limits negotiation to a subset of remote refs. Replaces the existing single-valued `--negotiation-tip` with a two-direction mechanism. The motivating case is monorepos with long-lived release branches -- if the negotiator skips that branch, the server may assume no shared history and send the entire parallel commit tree (60+ GB in a real case Stolee cites).
