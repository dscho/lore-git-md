This is a bugfix addressing a race condition in Git's hook execution infrastructure, particularly affecting pre-receive hooks that exit without reading their input. The issue was originally reported by Randall Becker during testing of Git 2.54.0-rc0 on NonStop systems, but Jeff King's analysis shows it's a general problem that could also affect parallel hook execution (a feature still in development).

Adrian Ratiu, who has been working on configurable hooks, weighs in to support Jeff King's proposed fix. The solution involves adjusting signal handling in `run_processes_parallel()` to ensure SIGPIPE is properly ignored, preventing premature termination of hooks. Adrian suggests two paths forward depending on whether the fix resolves Randall's original NonStop case, but in either case the fix will be incorporated - either as a standalone patch or as part of the parallel hooks series.

The discussion shows good technical alignment between the contributors, with Adrian deferring to Jeff King's expertise while ensuring proper credit and integration planning. The fix appears well-motivated as it addresses both an immediate regression and prevents future issues with parallel hook execution.
