This is a bugfix addressing a race condition in hook execution, particularly affecting pre-receive hooks that exit without reading their input. The issue was originally reported by Randall Becker on NonStop systems but has now been shown by Jeff King to also affect parallel hook execution scenarios on Linux.

Adrian Ratiu (who has been working on the parallel hooks feature) responds to Peff's analysis, agreeing the fix should be applied regardless of whether it resolves Randall's specific NonStop case. The core technical issue involves SIGPIPE handling in `run_processes_parallel()` where child cleanup handlers can override the intended SIG_IGN signal disposition, causing premature termination of hooks. Peff's test case demonstrates this by creating fast and slow hooks running in parallel - the fast hook's SIGPIPE can incorrectly terminate the slow hook without the fix.
