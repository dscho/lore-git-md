This is a maintainer response to Derrick Stolee's proposed fetch negotiation feature. Junio C Hamano weighs in on the design of the `--must-have` option and `remote.*.mustHave` configuration, suggesting an alternative approach that would modify the existing `--negotiation-tip` behavior rather than introducing a new option.

Junio expresses dislike for the "must have" naming but focuses on the conceptual approach, proposing that the team could instead enhance `--negotiation-tip` with a boolean flag to enable opportunistic inclusion of other refs. This would effectively merge the functionality Stolee wants into the existing feature rather than creating a parallel mechanism. He playfully suggests names like `--negotiate-better` while acknowledging the naming challenge.

The response indicates the feature is conceptually sound but may need refinement in its implementation integrates with existing negotiation infrastructure. Junio's suggestion to modify rather than duplicate functionality reflects Git's design philosophy of building on existing primitives where possible. This moves the discussion toward whether to proceed with Stolee's approach or refactor the existing negotiation-tip feature to cover both use cases.
