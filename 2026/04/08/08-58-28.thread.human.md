This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series (v2) that makes Git more resilient when dealing with misconfigured servers advertising invalid bundle-URI data. The issue surfaces when servers send empty URIs, causing confusing "expected 'packfile'" errors on the client side.

The series combines client-side robustness (patch 1) with server-side validation (patch 2). The v2 update focuses on improving the client's error handling by ensuring the protocol stream is properly drained after encountering invalid data, while the server-side patch (unchanged from v1) silently filters out empty URIs to prevent them from being advertised to clients. Both patches include expanded test coverage to verify the fixes work as intended.

Key participants—Toon Claes (author of both patches) and Justin Tobler (server-side patch author)—agree on the combined approach, with Patrick Steinhardt supporting the design and suggesting additional documentation (now addressed in v2). The implementation is finalized, with no fundamental behavior changes, just better error handling and prevention of misconfigurations. The only open question is whether to document the URI behavior more explicitly, but this hasn't been a blocker. The series is ready for review and likely uncontroversial.
