Bugfix for SIGPIPE handling in hook execution, verified on NonStop platform.

Randall Becker confirms that Jeff King's fix for SIGPIPE handling in hook execution works correctly on the NonStop platform. This addresses the race condition where pre-receive hooks could incorrectly reject pushed references if they exited successfully without reading input. The fix ensures SIG_IGN takes precedence over child cleanup handlers in run-command.c, preventing premature hook termination.

With this verification from the NonStop maintainer, the path forward is now clear. Junio C Hamano had previously suggested applying the fix to master before the 2.54.0 final release regardless of NonStop verification status, allowing Adrian Ratiu to rebase his parallel hooks series afterward. Randall's confirmation removes any remaining uncertainty about platform compatibility.
