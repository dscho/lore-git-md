This is a bugfix that addresses a race condition in hook execution, particularly affecting pre-receive hooks that exit without reading their input. The issue was originally reported by Randall Becker on NonStop systems and was shown by Jeff King to also affect parallel hook execution scenarios on Linux.

Randall Becker confirms that Jeff King's proposed fix resolves the issue on NonStop systems, and the new test case for parallel hooks also passes. The fix involves proper SIGPIPE handling in `run_processes_parallel()` where child cleanup handlers were incorrectly overriding the intended SIG_IGN signal disposition.

With this confirmation, Adrian Ratiu will proceed with sending the fix as a standalone patch (crediting Jeff King) and integrate the test case into his parallel hooks series later. The discussion shows good technical alignment between the contributors, with the fix now verified across different platforms and execution scenarios.
