This is part 4 of a 6-patch refactoring series improving type safety and clarity in xdiff's record cleanup logic. The series has gone through several iterations to address a regression found in v4, with v5 version preserving the non-regressing improvements while fixing the issue.

This particular patch focuses on making the per-file limit calculations in `xdl_cleanup_records()` clearer by:
1. Introducing explicit per-file limit variables (`mlim1`, `mlim2`) rather than reusing a single `mlim` variable
2. Moving the limit initialization to a more logical location before the loops
3. Removing a redundant `!need_min` check that is now handled by the limit initialization
4. Simplifying the conditional expressions in the loops

The changes are purely internal - no behavior changes are intended beyond what was already established in earlier versions of the series. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by improving code clarity while maintaining type safety (using `ptrdiff_t` and `mlim` variables).
