This is a bugfix thread about SIGPIPE handling in Git's hook execution, now confirmed ready for integration after platform testing. The issue involved a race condition where pre-receive hooks could incorrectly reject pushed references if they exited without reading input, due to signal handler ordering in `run-command.c`. Jeff King (Peff) provided the technical solution, which has now been verified across platforms including NonStop (by Randall Becker) and with parallel hooks (by Adrian Ratiu).

Junio C Hamano had previously suggested landing the fix before the 2.54.0 final release regardless of NonStop verification status, and now that all validations are complete, Adrian Ratiu confirms this email that the path forward is clear. The fix will go into master first, allowing Adrian to rebase his parallel hooks work on top. This keeps the urgent bugfix separate from the feature development while maintaining proper integration.

The thread shows good collaboration between the participants, with clear technical understanding and consensus on the solution. The fix is well-motivated (preventing incorrect hook behavior) and thoroughly validated across different configurations.
