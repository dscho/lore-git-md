This is a review response to Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking series, which represents the final component of a multi-part effort to improve Git's repacking strategy for large repositories.

Jeff King (Peff) provides his final assessment of the series after reviewing the changes made in response to his earlier feedback. While noting there's always potential for subtle corner cases in complex functionality like this, Peff states he didn't find any obvious issues in the final patches beyond a minor possible resource leak. He indicates the series has reached a point where further review is unlikely to surface additional problems, effectively giving his approval for integration.

The review represents the last piece of feedback needed before maintainer integration, following Elijah Newren's earlier review that also found no issues with the updated version.
