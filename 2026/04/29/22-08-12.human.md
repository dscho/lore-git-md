This is the third patch in a six-part refactoring series for the xdiff subsystem, focusing on type safety improvements. The series has gone through multiple iterations with careful attention to feedback, particularly from Phillip Wood.

The current patch makes a straightforward but important change: converting the parameters of `xdl_clean_mmatch()` and local variables in `xdl_cleanup_records()` from `long` to `ptrdiff_t`. This ensures proper type safety when working with array indices and pointer differences, which is especially important given xdiff's memory-intensive operations. The change is mechanical but meaningful - it prevents potential integer overflow issues when dealing with large files.

The patch follows the series' overall goal of modernizing xdiff's internal types while maintaining identical behavior. It's a small but well-contained piece of the larger effort to improve xdiff's robustness, particularly as part of making the codebase more amenable to potential Rust integration in the future. The changes are purely internal and don't affect the diff output.
