This is a review response to Owen Stephens' bug report about `git fetch --deepen` unexpectedly resetting a repository's depth in Git 2.54.0. D. Ben Knoble examines the documentation and questions whether the behavior should necessarily be a no-op in non-shallow repositories.

Knoble points out that while the man page text could be read to imply no-op behavior, it doesn't explicitly guarantee this - the description of `--depth` (which relates to `--deepen`) is written unconditionally. He acknowledges the behavior change between 2.53 and 2.54 but hasn't yet verified the regression through reproduction or bisection.

The response is a neutral, documentation-focused analysis that neither confirms nor denies the bug's validity. It suggests the issue may involve interpretation of the command's specified behavior rather than being purely a regression. This moves the discussion toward clarifying the intended semantics of `--deepen` in non-shallow contexts.
