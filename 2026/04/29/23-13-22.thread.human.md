The discussion has now clearly separated two distinct failure modes - between repositories (fixed) and within repositories (still needing attention).

The thread remains focused on finding a complete solution that either modifies the prefetch task to continue with remaining remotes after failures or provides better configuration options to handle unreliable remotes.
