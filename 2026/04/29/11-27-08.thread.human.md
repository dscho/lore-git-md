The thread addresses a regression in `git fetch --deepen` that could incorrectly convert full clones to shallow ones, losing commit history. Owen Stephens originally reported the issue, which was traced to a change in Git 2.54.0. Samo Pogačnik developed the fix that prevents `--deepen` from modifying non-shallow repositories, with René Scharfe reviewing and approving the technical approach.

Junio Hamano has now weighed in with final feedback on the patch submission. While not questioning the core fix, he suggests two presentation improvements: clearer commit citations and consideration of whether to warn users when `--deepen` is used on non-shallow repositories (where it becomes a no-op). This appears to be the last round of review before the fix can be queued for inclusion, marking the resolution of a potentially serious issue that could lead to unintended data loss in certain fetch operations.
