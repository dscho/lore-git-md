Thread identity: Feature patch series implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`, now at **v8**. Originating author: Pablo Sabater. Thread type: patch series (RFC -> v1-v8).

Core problem: The series implements cascading indentation in `git log --graph` to clearly represent commits with excluded parents by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships. The fundamental challenge was safely accessing future commits in the revision walk to determine visual root status, given that commits may be rewritten or simplified after being enqueued. The architectural solution centers on a **lookahead buffer** inside `struct git_graph` populated by a unified helper function, ensuring commits are fully simplified before inspection and deterministic behavior regardless of command-line options.
