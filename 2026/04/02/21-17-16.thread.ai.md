Core problem: The series implements cascading indentation in `git log --graph` to clearly represent commits with excluded parents by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships. The fundamental challenge was safely accessing future commits in the revision walk to determine visual root status, given that commits may be rewritten or simplified after being enqueued.
