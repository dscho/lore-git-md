This is patch 9/12 in Jeff King's series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings, specifically focusing on the packet_reader.line field. The change is safe because the underlying buffer remains non-const, and the patch leverages the CONST_OUTPARAM macro introduced in patch 8/12 to maintain type safety with skip_prefix() calls. The modifications are straightforward - converting const char* to char* in the packet_reader struct and related parsing code. This matches the series' pattern of making constness changes where they reflect actual usage patterns rather than theoretical immutability.
