This is a follow-up discussion on patch 4/12 of Jeff King's const-correctness series, which addresses C23 warnings about const handling in the pager subsystem. The patch originally proposed casting away constness when using `strchr()` on strings from `split_cmdline()`, with an explanatory comment about the interface limitations.

Junio Hamano had suggested an alternative approach - casting the const away earlier when passing the pointer to `split_cmdline()` rather than at the `strchr()` call site. Jeff King (Peff) acknowledges this as a better solution that moves the cast closer to the root cause, and indicates he will incorporate this change in the next version of the patch series along with some other minor touch-ups.

This exchange shows the iterative refinement typical of Git's review process - the maintainer identifies a more architecturally sound solution, and the author promptly accepts the improvement. The discussion remains focused on technical merits, with both participants clearly understanding the const-correctness implications at different levels of the call chain. This patch appears to be on track for inclusion once these final adjustments are made.
