This is part 3 of Jeff King's 12-patch series addressing C23 compatibility warnings about const-correctness in string function usage. The patch focuses on the transport-helper subsystem, specifically fixing a warning that occurs when assigning a const char* from strchr() to a non-const char* variable.

The change makes the "key" variable non-const since it originates from a strbuf (which is writable) and needs to remain writable for the subsequent NUL assignment. This is a straightforward mechanical change that follows the series' pattern of adjusting const usage to satisfy C23's stricter requirements while maintaining correct behavior.

The patch builds on prior work from Collin Funk and Michael Forney, with Jeff incorporating feedback from the first round of reviews. The series as a whole has already seen substantial review, with Junio Hamano approving several patches. This particular change is a small but necessary piece of the larger const-correctness effort.
