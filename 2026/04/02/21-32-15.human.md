This is part 2 of a 7-patch series completing the write operations interface for Git's object database (ODB) transactions, a key piece of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch makes ODB transactions pluggable by using the `begin_transaction()` callback from `struct odb_source` that was introduced in an earlier commit (d6fc6fe6f8).

The change is straightforward - it replaces the direct call to `odb_transaction_files_begin()` with the new callback-based approach. This allows different ODB backends to provide their own transaction implementations while maintaining a consistent interface. The patch updates just two lines in `odb/transaction.c` and moves the include from "object-file.h" to "odb/source.h" to reflect the new dependency.

As part of the larger series that has already received positive review from Patrick Steinhardt, this patch appears well-considered and properly integrated into the broader ODB abstraction work. The change is mechanical but important for enabling future backend implementations.
