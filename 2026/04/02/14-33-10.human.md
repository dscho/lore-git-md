Test modernization patch in Johannes Schindelin's series preparing for Git 3.0's potential bare repository behavior change. This is patch 4/17, focusing on t0056-git-C.sh which tests `git -C` behavior with worktrees.

The patch makes a targeted exception to the series' general pattern of eliminating implicit bare repository discovery. Here, the tests specifically need to verify how `-C` interacts with `--work-tree` when pointing to what is technically a bare repo (a.git). Forcing `safe.bareRepository=all` preserves the test cases' validity while still being explicit about the configuration.

The change is minimal (just two test_config_global additions) and well-justified in the commit message. This maintains the series' careful approach of either eliminating implicit discovery or explicitly opting into it when truly needed. The patch follows the established pattern from earlier in the series while recognizing when a different approach is needed for specific test coverage.
