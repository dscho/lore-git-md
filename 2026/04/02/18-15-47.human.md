While acknowledging the technical soundness of the approaches used in the patches (summarized well in Johannes's cover letter), Junio's response signals that the underlying premise - changing the default bare repo behavior - may need more explicit discussion before this test modernization can be considered complete. His tone is constructive but cautious, typical of his maintainer role in ensuring changes have proper consensus.
