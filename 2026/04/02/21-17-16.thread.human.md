This thread implements visual root indentation for `git log --graph` to clarify commit ancestry when some parents are excluded. The feature, now in its eleventh iteration (v11), is authored by Pablo Sabater and introduces progressive indentation for visual roots—commits with excluded parents—while preserving parent-child relationships in the graph output.

The series is technically complete, with all major design decisions resolved. Usability refinements, including a four-level indentation wrap to avoid ambiguity and an opt-out mechanism (`--[no-]graph-indent` flag and `log.graphIndent` configuration), address prior feedback.
