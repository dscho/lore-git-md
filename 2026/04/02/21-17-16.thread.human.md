This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`, now at version 7. The series aims to improve the clarity of commit graphs by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships, addressing a long-standing challenge in safely accessing future commits during the revision walk.

The architectural core of the series—a lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`—has been finalized and approved by Junio Hamano, replacing the fragile peek-based approach from earlier versions. The v7 series is complete, with the test infrastructure refactoring (patch 1/3) already merged. The remaining patches—lookahead buffer implementation (2/3) and visualization logic (3/3)—are ready for final review after Pablo incorporates minor feedback. This latest email addresses surface-level improvements to patch 2/3, such as using `ARRAY_SIZE()` for maintainability and adding assertions to verify buffer invariants, while clarifying a non-critical guard in `revision.c`. No substantive changes to the core logic are needed.

Key participants include Pablo (author), Kristofer Karlsson (who proposed the lookahead redesign), Junio (maintainer), and Jeff King (who confirmed the fragility of the prior approach). Phillip Wood’s final review is still pending, but the series is on track for inclusion once the minor updates are submitted. Open questions include documentation of visualization rules and test brittleness, though these are not blockers. The series is compatible with related work, such as Jeff King’s removal of `revs->commits` and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic.
