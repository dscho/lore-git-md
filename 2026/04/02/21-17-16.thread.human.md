This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing **cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`**, now at its ninth iteration (v9). The goal is to make the graph output clearer by progressively indenting commits that have excluded parents—such as those filtered out by `--author` or `--grep`—while preserving parent-child relationships. The series has evolved through extensive review, addressing architectural challenges like safely accessing future commits in the revision walk, which was resolved with a **lookahead buffer** in `struct git_graph`.

The series is now **technically complete**, with all four patches ready for final review. The first patch (test infrastructure cleanup) has already been merged. The second and third patches introduce the lookahead buffer and a unified helper function for commit retrieval, laying the groundwork for the visualization logic in the fourth patch. This final patch adds the `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state to connect indented roots to their children, tracks indentation depth with new counters, and fixes an edge case where `--author` or `--grep` filters could break indentation. Test coverage is thorough, with 473 lines of new tests verifying behavior across various scenarios, including filtered parents, boundary commits, and octopus merges.
