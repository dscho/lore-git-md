This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`, now at version 7. The series aims to improve the clarity of commit graphs by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships. The key architectural challenge—safely accessing future commits in the revision walk—was resolved in v7 by replacing the fragile peek-based approach with a lookahead buffer inside `struct git_graph`, ensuring commits are fully simplified before inspection.

The series is now complete and ready for final review. The first patch (test infrastructure refactoring) is already merged, while the second (lookahead buffer implementation) and third (visualization logic) are pending minor adjustments. Pablo has agreed to incorporate Chandra Pratap’s feedback on defensive programming, including using `ARRAY_SIZE()` for maintainability and adding assertions to verify buffer invariants. He also plans to simplify the `revision.c` code by removing an unnecessary guard, as `--reverse` is incompatible with `--graph`. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have yet to sign off on the final patches, but the architectural direction is settled, and no substantive objections remain.

The series touches `graph.c`, `revision.c`, and related test files, introducing new symbols like `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` and `visual_root_depth` while removing deprecated peek functions. All failing test cases from v6 now pass, and the lookahead buffer is compatible with ongoing work like Jeff King’s removal of `revs->commits`. Open questions include documentation of visualization rules and test brittleness, but these are not blocking progress. The series is on track for inclusion once Pablo submits the updated patch 2/3.
