Pablo Sabater's v3 patch series to fix `git log --graph` rendering of
commits with excluded parents is nearing readiness for merge. The series
introduces an `is_placeholder` flag to prevent the graph code from
vertically aligning unrelated commits when parents are excluded, which
was causing misleading visual relationships.

The last remaining question came from Jeff King (Peff), who spotted a
dead-code guard flagged by Coverity. Pablo promptly responded,
acknowledging he'd cargo-culted a pattern from `graph_output_commit_line()`
into `graph_output_post_merge_line()` without adapting it to the different
control flow. He confirmed the guard is unreachable and will be dropped.
This is exactly the kind of clean resolution that closes a review item --
no deeper logic errors were hiding behind the redundant code.

Junio had already indicated the implementation approach seemed sound in
earlier rounds. With Pablo's acknowledgment and plan to remove the dead
code, the main substantive issue is resolved. Earlier open questions about
diagonal line artifacts and test file organization still stand but have
not come up again. The series has been in "What's cooking" since early
April and appears close to merging once the v4 respin lands.
