This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing visual root indentation for `git log --graph`, now at its eleventh iteration (v11). The series introduces indentation to clearly distinguish commits with excluded parents while preserving parent-child relationships, using a lookahead buffer to safely inspect future commits during the revision walk.

The v11 series is complete and consists of seven patches, with the first (test infrastructure refactoring) already merged. The remaining six patches—covering the lookahead buffer, visualization logic, four-level wrapping, configuration refactoring, and an opt-out mechanism—are ready for final review. All architectural and usability decisions, including the lookahead buffer design and the four-level wrap to avoid ambiguity, have been resolved. The series has no known technical issues, and GitHub CI reports no failures.

The only remaining feedback is minor: Chandra Pratap’s surface-level review of the final patch (7/7) caught two typos in the documentation ("deafault" and "graph.indent" instead of "log.graphIndent") and a test comment inconsistency. These are trivial to fix and won’t affect functionality. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have yet to sign off, but the series is otherwise ready for integration into `next`. Pablo Sabater has no further planned changes.
