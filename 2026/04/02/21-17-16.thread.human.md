This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing cascading indentation in `git log --graph` to better visualize commits with excluded parents. The series is now at version 9, with all technical issues resolved—including a lookahead buffer to safely inspect future commits during the revision walk. The first patch (test infrastructure cleanup) has already been merged, and the remaining three are ready for final review.

The core debate has shifted from technical feasibility to usability. Mirko Faina raised a late objection to the always-on indentation, arguing it clutters simple cases like single-patch series. Pablo initially proposed an opt-out default (`--no-graph-indent`) or a zig-zag pattern to reduce ambiguity, but Mirko has now refined the zig-zag idea into a **bounded zig-zag**: indentation resets to the left margin after two levels, preventing excessive clutter while still clarifying excluded parents. This keeps the feature opt-in (via `--oneline` or `--format=reference`) and avoids the ambiguity of Pablo’s original zig-zag sketch. The proposal is mechanical and low-risk, offering a middle ground that could resolve the stalemate.

Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have yet to weigh in on the usability question, and their decision will determine whether the series merges as-is or requires a follow-up redesign. The series is otherwise technically complete and compatible with related work like Jeff King’s `revs->commits` removal. The next steps hinge on whether the maintainers accept the current opt-out behavior, adopt Mirko’s bounded zig-zag, or explore another alternative.
