This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`, now at version 7. The series aims to improve the clarity of commit graphs by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships, addressing a long-standing challenge in safely accessing future commits during revision walks.

The architectural core of the series—a lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`—has been finalized and approved by Junio Hamano, replacing the fragile peek-based approach from earlier versions. The v7 series is complete, with the test infrastructure refactoring (patch 1/3) already merged. The remaining patches—lookahead buffer implementation (2/3) and visualization logic (3/3)—are ready for final review, pending minor cosmetic adjustments. Chandra Pratap’s recent feedback on patch 2/3 focused on defensive programming (assertions) and maintainability (`ARRAY_SIZE()` usage), but these are surface-level suggestions with no impact on functionality. Kristofer Karlsson, who prototyped the lookahead buffer, clarified the design intent, and Pablo is expected to incorporate the `ARRAY_SIZE()` change before Junio and Phillip Wood sign off.

The series is on track for inclusion, with all substantive technical concerns resolved. Open items include final reviews from Junio and Phillip, pending documentation of visualization rules, and a lingering concern about test brittleness due to exact output matching. The lookahead buffer is also compatible with Jeff King’s planned removal of `revs->commits` and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic, though reconciliation order remains TBD. No major objections or design disputes remain.
