This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series adding **visual root indentation to `git log --graph`**, now at its tenth iteration (v10). The feature indents commits with excluded parents to clarify parent-child relationships in the graph, using a lookahead buffer to safely inspect future commits during the revision walk. The series has evolved from an RFC to a polished implementation, with all major design and usability decisions now resolved.

The v10 series consists of seven patches, six of which are ready for final review. The first patch (test infrastructure cleanup) was merged earlier this month. The core work includes a unified lookahead buffer (approved by Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson), the indentation logic itself, and a four-level wrapping mechanism to avoid visual ambiguity. The latest addition is an **opt-out mechanism**—a `--[no-]graph-indent` flag and `log.graphIndent` configuration variable—allowing users to disable indentation if they prefer the traditional layout. This addresses earlier concerns about forced visual changes in formats like `--format=fuller`.

Key participants include Mirko Faina, who helped refine the wrapping design, and Kristofer Karlsson, who prototyped the lookahead buffer.
