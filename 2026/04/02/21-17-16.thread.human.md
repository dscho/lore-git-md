This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series (now at v6) implementing cascading indentation in `git log --graph` to better visualize commits with excluded parents. The series has evolved significantly since its initial submission, addressing a critical architectural fragility in how the graph rendering code (`graph.c`) directly accesses the revision walker’s internal commit queue (`revs->commits`).

The core of the discussion revolves around insulating `graph.c` from the walker’s internals. In v6, Pablo introduced an abstraction layer in `revision.c` that provides controlled access to the commit queue, resolving the long-standing fragility that blocked earlier versions. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have conditionally approved this abstraction, pending follow-up work to address safety and forward-compatibility concerns—specifically, adding a defensive `BUG()` check for unprocessed commits and avoiding direct access to `prio_queue` internals in favor of `prio_queue_for_each()`. Chandra Pratap also reviewed the abstraction layer, suggesting minor readability improvements (no functional changes).

The visualization logic itself (patch 3/3) is now complete, introducing cascading indentation for visual roots while preserving parent-child relationships. This includes new graph states (`GRAPH_PRE_ROOT`), counters for indentation depth, and special handling for merges and boundary commits. The series includes comprehensive test coverage, though Junio has raised concerns about the brittleness of exact output matching in tests.

Key participants include:
