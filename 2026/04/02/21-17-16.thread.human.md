This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series adding visual root indentation to `git log --graph`, now at its tenth iteration (v10). The feature makes it easier to follow commit history by indenting visual roots—commits with excluded parents—while preserving parent-child relationships. The series has evolved through extensive review, resolving architectural challenges (like safely accessing future commits via a lookahead buffer) and usability concerns (such as a four-level wrap to avoid ambiguity and an opt-out mechanism).

The v10 series is complete and ready for final review.
