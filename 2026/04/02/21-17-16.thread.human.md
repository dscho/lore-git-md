This thread implements visual root indentation for `git log --graph`, a feature that makes it easier to distinguish commits with excluded parents by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first). The series, now at **v12**, is authored by Pablo Sabater and is technically complete, with all design decisions resolved.

The core innovation is a **lookahead buffer** in `struct git_graph` that safely inspects future commits during the revision walk, ensuring parent-child relationships are preserved. The series includes seven patches: the first (test infrastructure refactoring) is already merged, while the remaining six—covering the lookahead buffer, visualization logic, four-level wrapping, configuration refactoring, and an opt-out mechanism—are ready for final review.
