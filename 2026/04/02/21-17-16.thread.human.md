The `git log --graph` series for cascading indentation of visual roots has reached **v7**, with the core architectural hurdle now resolved. Pablo Sabater rerolled the series after Junio Hamano adopted Kristofer Karlsson's lookahead buffer redesign, which replaces the fragile peek-based abstraction from earlier versions. This redesign ensures commits are fully simplified before the graph layer inspects them, fixing the test failures that stalled v6.

The series is now split into three patches: the first (test infrastructure cleanup) is already merged, while the second—the lookahead buffer implementation—has just been submitted. This patch introduces a two-slot buffer in `struct git_graph`, populated during revision walking, and updates `graph_is_interesting()` to treat buffered commits as "interesting" regardless of their `SHOWN` flag. The approach is deterministic, compatible with other in-flight topics (like Jeff King's `revs->commits` removal), and resolves the core fragility that blocked prior versions. The final patch, which implements the visualization logic itself, is pending review.

Junio and Phillip Wood have yet to sign off on the visualization changes, and two loose ends remain: documenting the indentation rules and addressing Junio's concern about test brittleness (the series relies on exact indentation counts). However, the architectural direction is settled, and the series is on track for inclusion once these final details are ironed out. The lookahead buffer has broad support, with Peff confirming its robustness and Kristofer's prototype serving as the foundation for the redesign.
