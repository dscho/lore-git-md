This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series (now at v8) that adds cascading indentation to `git log --graph` to better visualize commits with excluded parents. The series has been through several iterations, with the core architectural challenge—safely accessing future commits in the revision walk—resolved in v7/v8 by introducing a lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`. Junio Hamano approved this approach, and the first patch (test infrastructure cleanup) has already merged.

The series is now complete and ready for final review, with all known correctness issues addressed. However, Mirko Faina’s recent review of the final patch uncovered a real edge case: when using `--author` or similar filters that exclude commits without filtering their parents, the indentation logic fails. The root cause is a design-level oversight in how `graph_is_visual_root_candidate()` interacts with `simplify_commit()` and `get_commit_action()`. The filter is applied too late in the process, so the graph renderer can’t determine visual root status in time to indent correctly. This reveals a gap in test coverage (no tests for `--author` or similar filters) and a limitation in the visualization rules, which assume parents are either present or explicitly filtered to NULL.

The rest of the series remains technically sound. Junio and Phillip Wood’s final reviews are the last hurdle before merging to `next`, but this feedback may require a follow-up discussion or patch to address the filtering issue. Pablo will likely need to revisit how visual root status is detected during the revision walk, possibly by expanding the lookahead buffer’s role or adjusting the filtering logic.
