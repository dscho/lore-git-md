This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s eleven-iteration patch series that adds visual root indentation to `git log --graph`. The feature indents commits with excluded parents (except the first) to make the ancestry graph easier to read, using a lookahead buffer to safely inspect future commits during the revision walk.

The series is now at v11, with only the opt-out mechanism (patch 7/7) tweaked since v10. Junio Hamano just reviewed that patch and suggested a minor readability improvement to a conditional check; the change is purely mechanical and not a blocker. The first patch (test refactoring) is already merged; the remaining six are ready for final review and have no known issues. All design decisions—lookahead buffer architecture, four-level wrapping, and the opt-out flag plus configuration variable—are settled and implemented. Mirko Faina and Chandra Pratap have endorsed the usability choices, and Kristofer Karlsson and Junio have approved the technical foundation.

The only open items are Junio’s and Phillip Wood’s final sign-off on patches 2–7, a lingering concern from Junio about test brittleness, and a documented but unaddressed edge case around redundant indentation. The series is otherwise complete and passing CI.
