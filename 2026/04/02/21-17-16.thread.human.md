This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series adding **cascading indentation** to `git log --graph`, which visually distinguishes commits with excluded parents by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first). The series has gone through seven iterations, with the latest architectural shift resolving a long-standing fragility in how the graph code accesses future commits during revision traversal.

The core issue was that the original design relied on "peeking" at unsimplified commits, which could lead to incorrect indentation or crashes. After debate, Junio Hamano approved **Kristofer Karlsson’s lookahead buffer redesign**, which replaces the peek functions with a two-slot buffer populated during `get_revision_internal()`. This ensures commits are fully processed before inspection, fixing the three failing test cases from earlier versions. The v7 series, now in preparation, implements this approach and removes the problematic peek abstraction entirely.

Key participants include Pablo (the author), Kristofer (who proposed the lookahead solution), Junio (who approved the redesign), and Jeff King (who confirmed the fragility of the old approach). The series is now architecturally settled, though minor loose ends remain: documenting the indentation rules, addressing a redundant indentation edge case (documented but unresolved), and concerns about test brittleness due to exact output matching.
