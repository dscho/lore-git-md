This thread discusses Pablo Sabater's improvements to `git log --graph` rendering, now in its third iteration. The core issue addresses how commits with excluded parents are displayed, where the current implementation can create misleading visual alignments. Pablo's solution introduces an `is_placeholder` flag to properly indent subsequent commits, and he has already resolved a dead-code issue identified by Jeff King.

Phillip Wood has now weighed in with a late-stage design suggestion, proposing an alternative visual approach using blank lines rather than indentation to separate unrelated root commits. His method would keep child commits visually grouped with their actual parents, differing from Pablo's column-alignment approach. While this doesn't challenge the technical soundness of the implementation (which appears solid after three iterations), it raises a final aesthetic consideration about what makes for the most intuitive graph output.

The series seems nearly ready for merging, with only this remaining presentation question to resolve. Pablo will need to decide whether to incorporate Phillip's suggestion or maintain the current indentation approach in what will likely be the final version before integration.
