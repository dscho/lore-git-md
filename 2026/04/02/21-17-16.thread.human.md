This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing **cascading indentation in `git log --graph`**, now at version 7. The goal is to visually distinguish commits that appear root-like because their parents are excluded (e.g., due to filters or simplification) by progressively indenting them while preserving parent-child relationships. The series has undergone a major architectural shift—replacing a fragile peek-based approach with a **lookahead buffer** designed by Kristofer Karlsson—which Junio Hamano approved as the right long-term solution. This redesign ensures commits are fully simplified before inspection, resolving test failures and compatibility concerns.

The latest update (patch 3/3) delivers the actual visualization logic. It indents visual roots by two characters per level (except the first in a cascade) and introduces a `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state to connect indented roots to their children, similar to how octopus merges are handled. The implementation is backed by comprehensive tests in a new file, `t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh`, covering edge cases like merges, filtered parents, and cascading sequences. All previously failing tests now pass, though one minor edge case (redundant indentation with filtered parents) is documented as NEEDSWORK but deemed acceptable for now.

The series is **ready for final review**, with Junio and Phillip Wood yet to sign off on the visualization rules. Open questions include documenting the indentation logic more clearly and addressing Junio’s concern about test brittleness (reliance on exact output matching). The lookahead buffer approach is compatible with other in-flight topics, like Jeff King’s removal of `revs->commits` and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `prio-queue` refactoring. Once the remaining reviews are in, the series is on track for inclusion.
