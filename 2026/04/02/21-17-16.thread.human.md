This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing visual root indentation for `git log --graph`, now at its eleventh iteration (v11). The goal is to improve the clarity of commit graphs by indenting commits with excluded parents—except the first—while preserving parent-child relationships. The series has evolved through extensive review, resolving architectural and usability concerns.

The first patch, a test infrastructure refactoring, was merged on July 12. The remaining six patches are now ready for final review. These include the core lookahead buffer implementation (approved by Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson), the visualization logic, a four-level wrapping mechanism to avoid ambiguity, a configuration refactoring, and an opt-out mechanism via `--[no-]graph-indent` or `log.graphIndent`. All design decisions—including the lookahead buffer, four-level wrap, and opt-out defaults—are finalized, and the series is technically complete. GitHub CI reports no failures, and documentation and tests are updated.

Key participants include Pablo Sabater (author), who addressed Mirko Faina’s mechanical feedback in the opt-out patch; Kristofer Karlsson, who proposed the lookahead buffer; and Junio Hamano, who approved the architecture but has not yet reviewed the latest usability refinements.
