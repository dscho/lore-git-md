This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series adding visual root indentation to `git log --graph`, now in its ninth iteration. The series aims to make it clearer when commits have excluded parents by indenting visual roots—except the first—while keeping parent-child relationships intact. The architectural challenge of safely accessing future commits was resolved with a lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`, and the approach has been approved by Junio Hamano.

The series is technically complete, with all four patches ready for final review. The first patch, a test infrastructure cleanup, has already been merged. The remaining three—introducing the lookahead buffer and the visualization logic—are pending, but the main holdup is a late-stage usability decision. The original cascading indentation design has been refined into two bounded wrapping alternatives: Mirko Faina’s two-level wrap (simpler, less intrusive) and Pablo Sabater’s four-level wrap (more visually distinct, avoiding ambiguity in edge cases). Mirko has now endorsed the four-level approach, arguing it better reinforces the unrelatedness of commits by making the reset more abrupt. Both designs address prior objections, and neither requires changes to the approved infrastructure.

The only remaining questions are whether to adopt the two-level or four-level wrap and whether to retain the `--no-graph-indent` opt-out flag, particularly for non-oneline formats like `--format=fuller`. Junio and Phillip Wood have yet to weigh in on these points, and their decision will determine if the final patch needs adjustment. The series is otherwise ready for `next` once the maintainers rule on the indentation style and opt-out flag.
