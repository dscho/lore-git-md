This thread implements visual root indentation for `git log --graph`, a feature that makes it easier to distinguish commits with excluded parents by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while preserving parent-child relationships. Pablo Sabater’s v10 series, posted on July 16, is now complete with seven patches. The first patch, a test infrastructure cleanup, has already merged; the remaining six—covering a lookahead buffer, core visualization logic, a four-level wrapping mechanism, configuration refactoring, and an opt-out mechanism—are ready for final review.

The architectural foundation, a lookahead buffer in `struct git_graph`, was approved by Junio Hamano and Kristofer Karlsson and is now fully implemented. The visualization logic and four-level wrap resolve earlier usability concerns about symmetry-induced ambiguity, and the opt-out mechanism (via `--[no-]graph-indent` or `log.graphIndent`) defaults to indentation enabled. Mirko Faina reviewed the opt-out patch and gave it an LGTM, though his feedback was mechanical rather than substantive.
