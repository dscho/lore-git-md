This thread is about Pablo Sabater’s patch series implementing **cascading indentation for visual roots in `git log --graph`**, now at **v9**. The feature makes excluded parents clearer by progressively indenting visual roots (except the first) while keeping parent-child relationships intact. The core challenge was safely accessing future commits in the revision walk; the solution uses a **lookahead buffer** in `struct git_graph` to ensure commits are fully simplified before inspection.

The series is now complete and addresses all known issues. The latest revision (v9) fixes a design gap in v8 where filters like `--author` or `--grep`—which exclude commits *after* enqueuing—broke indentation by leaving parent pointers intact. The fix iterates over parents with `graph_is_interesting()` to detect exclusions, and new tests verify it works. The rest of the series is unchanged: patch 1/4 (test cleanup) is already merged, patch 2/4 unifies lookahead population, and patch 3/4 implements the buffer. Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood’s final reviews of patches 2/4 and 3/4 are pending, but the core logic (patch 4/4) is stable and well-tested. The series is likely ready for `next` once reviews are complete.

Key participants include Pablo (author), Mirko Faina (who identified the `--author`/`-grep` edge case), Kristofer Karlsson (lookahead buffer prototype), and Junio (maintainer). The design is compatible with Jeff King’s planned `revs->commits` removal and Kristofer Haugsbakk’s `kk/prio-queue-get-put-fusion` topic. A minor edge case (redundant indentation with filtered parents) is documented but deemed acceptable.
