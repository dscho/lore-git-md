This is patch 4/12 in Jeff King's series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings, specifically in the pager subsystem. The change deals with how `split_cmdline()` handles constness in its return value.

The patch makes the `argv` parameter non-const in `setup_pager_env()`, with an explicit cast when calling `split_cmdline()`. This is safe because we know the underlying memory (from `pager_env`) is writable, even though `split_cmdline()` declares its output as const. The change silences compiler warnings while maintaining the ability to modify the strings (needed to NUL-terminate them).

This is a pattern seen elsewhere in the series - making minimal changes to satisfy C23 requirements while preserving functionality. The commit message clearly explains why the cast is safe and why a broader change to `split_cmdline()` isn't practical. The patch has already been approved by Junio in the first round of review, with the v2 version incorporating Junio's suggestion to move the const-casting earlier in the process.
