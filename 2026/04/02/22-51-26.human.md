This is a follow-up discussion in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX repacking, specifically addressing the `--checksum-only` flag introduced in patch 5/16. Jeff King had raised concerns about the flag's name potentially being misleading, suggesting `--no-write-chain-file` as a clearer alternative.

Taylor agrees the naming could be improved and accepts Jeff's suggestion, noting it better describes the actual behavior (skipping chain file updates while still writing the MIDX) and leaves room for future additions. He also points out that checksum verification is already covered by `git multi-pack-index verify`.

The discussion then shifts to implementation details, with Taylor considering Jeff's proposal to use `is_lock_file_locked()` checks instead of direct flag tests. While acknowledging both approaches are roughly equivalent, Taylor finds the lockfile-based version more readable and outlines how he would implement it with appropriate assertions to maintain consistency. The exchange demonstrates the project's careful attention to both interface clarity and implementation robustness, even for what might seem like minor details.
