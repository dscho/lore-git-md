This is the fifth patch in Johannes Schindelin's 17-part series modernizing Git's test suite to prepare for a future change in bare repository handling. The patch focuses on t1020-subdirectory.sh, simplifying a test case that previously used a subshell to change directories into a bare repository.

The change replaces a subshell-based approach (`cd foo.git && git show`) with a direct `--git-dir` invocation (`git --git-dir=foo.git show`). This makes the test both simpler and more robust against the upcoming `safe.bareRepository=explicit` default, since it no longer relies on implicit bare repository discovery.

The modification is straightforward and follows the pattern established in earlier patches of the series. It maintains the test's original purpose while making it compatible with future Git versions. The patch is small (just 4 lines removed, 1 added) and focused, typical of Schindelin's careful test infrastructure work.
