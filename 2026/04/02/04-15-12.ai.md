This is patch 10/12 in Jeff King's series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings, specifically targeting the range-diff subsystem. The change removes a const qualifier from a pointer in range-diff.c that gets passed to strstr() and assigned to a non-const variable, which newer C23 implementations warn about.

The patch makes the pointer non-const since it originates from a writable strbuf buffer and will be written to later. This aligns with the series' pattern of matching constness to actual usage patterns. The change is safe because the earlier patch 8/12's CONST_OUTPARAM macro ensures skip_prefix() will handle the const/non-const matching automatically.
