This is a follow-up discussion in Jeff King's (Peff) series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings, specifically regarding the pseudo-merge bitmap handling in patch 7/12. Taylor Blau weighs in on the thread, agreeing with Peff's suggestion that a minimal fix (casting away constness with a warning comment) would be appropriate as a temporary measure to silence Coverity warnings.

Taylor notes that while investigating, he found several other issues in the experimental pseudo-merge subsystem that warrant more comprehensive fixes, but those can be addressed separately. The current approach allows the series to move forward with the immediate goal of compiler warning fixes while leaving room for deeper architectural improvements to the pseudo-merge code later.

This exchange shows the pragmatic approach the maintainers are taking - accepting a temporary solution for an experimental feature while acknowledging the need for more thorough work in that area. The discussion also highlights the collaborative nature of the review process, with Taylor validating Peff's suggestion after his own investigation of the code.
