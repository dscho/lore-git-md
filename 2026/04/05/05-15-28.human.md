This is a bugfix addressing an interaction between split-index and index.skipHash that was discovered during fsmonitor testing. Paul Tarjan confirms Johannes Schindelin's analysis that the root cause lies in write_shared_index() not generating a proper OID when index.skipHash is true, rather than being an fsmonitor-specific issue.

The patch proposes temporarily disabling index.skipHash during shared index writes to ensure proper OID generation. Testing shows this approach fixes the test failures without needing the original fsmonitor-specific bounds check. Paul indicates he'll drop the fsmonitor patch from his series and offers to contribute this fix either as a standalone patch or as part of his fsmonitor work, depending on maintainer preference.

The solution is clean and focused - it preserves the skipHash optimization for normal operations while ensuring shared indexes get proper identifiers. This appears to be the most direct fix among the three options previously discussed (the others being fixing an unconditional index reload or maintaining test workarounds). The change is well-scoped and comes with test verification, making it a solid candidate for inclusion.
