The discussion reveals deeper architectural question about error handling in repository-less operations. Peff maintains that silent no-ops are preferable to `BUG()` for transaction operations when no repository exists, as this matches the historical behavior and provides more flexibility to callers. He notes this is particularly important for maintaining the large-file handling path in `index_fd()`.
