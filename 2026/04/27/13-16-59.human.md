This is a substantive review from Derrick Stolee on Elijah Newren's performance optimization series for partial clone behavior, specifically addressing the second patch which implemented blob prefetching for `git cherry`. Stolee raises architectural concerns about the current implementation's placement in `builtin/log.c` rather than being more generally reusable in `diff.c`.

The review suggests that the prefetching logic could be better integrated with the existing diff infrastructure, particularly `diff_queued_diff_prefetch()`, to avoid duplicating diff queue handling. Stolee also notes that similar optimizations for `git grep` (patch 3) might benefit from sharing logic with operations like checkout or archive that also scan trees with pathspecs.

While praising the value of the optimization, Stolee gently pushes for a more general solution that could be reused by future commands rather than command-specific implementations. The review maintains a constructive tone, acknowledging the practical constraints while planting seeds for potential future refactoring. This is characteristic of Stolee's reviews - identifying architectural opportunities without blocking immediate improvements.
