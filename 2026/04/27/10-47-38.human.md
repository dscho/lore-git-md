Bugfix for the Reintegrate script's error reporting. This patch corrects a redirection bug in the `Reintegrate` script where warning messages about unexpected merge commit subjects were being sent to stdout instead of stderr. The issue caused these warnings to be silently embedded in the script's output when stdout was redirected (as is common in maintainer workflows), rather than appearing on the terminal where they could alert the user to problems.

The fix changes `2>&1` to `>&2`, properly sending the "Huh?" warning to stderr where it belongs. This matches the behavior of other diagnostics in the script. The change is straightforward and clearly correct - the original redirection was backwards from what was intended. No functional changes beyond fixing the error reporting behavior.
