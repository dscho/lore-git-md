This is a performance optimization patch for `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` that has now reached its final form after three iterations. The patch increases the I/O buffer size from 4KB to 128KB to reduce system call overhead, particularly benefiting FUSE filesystems where small writes are expensive.

Derrick Stolee, who had provided naming feedback on earlier versions, now confirms he's happy with the v3 implementation that renamed the constant to `DEFAULT_IO_BUFFER_SIZE` to better reflect its broader usage. He acknowledges his earlier suggestion about consolidating with `WRITE_BUFFER_SIZE` was based on outdated code context, and commends the author for discovering this.

The patch has received thorough review from both Stolee and Junio Hamano, with all substantive feedback addressed. Performance testing shows significant improvements - 10-11% faster clones on FUSE and 65% fewer pack file writes. This appears merge-ready and likely to provide measurable benefits, particularly for users working with remote filesystems.
