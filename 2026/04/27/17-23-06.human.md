Performance optimization follow-up for `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` buffer sizes.

Derrick Stolee provides additional review on Scott Bauersfeld's v2 patch that increases I/O buffer sizes from 4KB to 128KB. He expresses pleasant surprise at the significant performance improvements shown (10-11% faster clones on FUSE filesystems) and confirms the technical approach of centralizing the size definition in `git-compat-util.h`.

Stolee notes an interesting historical connection - the 128KB value matches an existing buffer size he previously introduced for index file writing. This prompts him to suggest the constant might be better named `DEFAULT_HASHFILE_BUFFER_SIZE` or `DEFAULT_FILESYSTEM_BUFFER_SIZE` since it's used beyond just packfiles. However, he doesn't request a change, just observes the naming consideration.

The review is substantive but not blocking - Stolee clearly approves of the technical direction and implementation, only raising the naming point as food for thought rather than a required revision. This suggests the patch is in good shape for merging after addressing any final minor points.
