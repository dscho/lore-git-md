The main technical point raised is whether the `trust_ctime` field should be declared as a `bool` type rather than `int`, since it's parsed as a boolean configuration value. The review also gently prods for more explanation about why this particular setting needs to be eagerly parsed into the repository config structure.

The tone is constructive and focused on small but meaningful improvements to the patch's implementation details.
