This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's series to enable `set -e` in Git's test suite, now focusing on a macOS-specific CI failure in the gitweb tests. The issue surfaced after merging the changes to the 'seen' branch, where Perl module detection for the DATE_PARSER prerequisite was silently failing on macOS but now properly triggers an error under `set -e`.

Junio Hamano confirms this is exactly the type of hidden test issue the series aims to uncover - the test was previously ignoring the missing Perl modules on macOS but now properly fails when they're unavailable. He acknowledges Patrick's proposed fix (converting the prerequisite check to use `test_lazy_prereq`) as the right approach, matching established patterns in the test suite.

The exchange shows the series is achieving its goal of making the suite more robust by exposing previously silent failures. With Junio's approval of the fix direction, this appears to be one of the final platform-specific edge cases being addressed before the changes can graduate to more stable branches. The interaction demonstrates how thorough review and real-world testing (via CI failures) are validating the series' approach to error handling.
