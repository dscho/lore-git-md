This is a technical discussion in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking (patch 6/16). The exchange clarifies design decisions around the `--base` option for MIDX writes.

Jeff King had raised a conceptual question about whether writing an incremental MIDX with `--base=none` is meaningfully different from writing a non-incremental MIDX. Taylor explains that while they are logically similar (both result in a single layer), the implementation must produce an incremental MIDX layer because the geometric repacking machinery cannot directly replace the main multi-pack-index file. This maintains consistency with the series' incremental approach.

The discussion also confirms that `--base` requires `--checksum-only` to preserve chain invariants when the caller is assembling a new chain structure. Taylor notes that the actual chain management and orphan cleanup will be handled by the repack layer in later patches. The technical details appear sound, with Jeff's review having already approved the implementation while surfacing these clarifying points. The series continues to progress through review with expert consensus.
