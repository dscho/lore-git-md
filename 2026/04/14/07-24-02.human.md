This is a test infrastructure patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to make Git's test suite compatible with `set -e` (errexit). The patch modifies the cleanup handlers `test_when_finished()` and `test_atexit()` to work correctly under strict error checking by replacing an `&& (exit $eval_ret)` pattern with simpler `|| eval_ret=$?` logic.

Junio Hamano raised a historical concern about whether this change interacts properly with a 2010 FreeBSD-specific workaround (commit b6b0afdc) that addressed shell behavior where `$?` didn't properly propagate into `eval`. Patrick acknowledges the concern and commits to testing the change on FreeBSD to verify compatibility.

The patch is part of a mature series that has already addressed numerous edge cases across the test suite, with only minor validation remaining before final acceptance. The discussion demonstrates Git's careful approach to test infrastructure changes, where even small modifications receive scrutiny regarding their interaction with historical quirks and platform-specific behaviors.
