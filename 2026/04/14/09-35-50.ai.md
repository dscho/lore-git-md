This is a follow-up discussion in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about migrating the `trust_ctime` configuration from a global variable to per-repository storage. Phillip Wood responds to Karthik Nayak's review of Olamide Caleb Bello's patch, providing additional rationale for why `trust_ctime` should be eagerly parsed in `repo_config_values` rather than lazily.

He advocates for maintaining the current eager parsing approach unless there's a compelling reason to change it, shifting the burden of proof to those proposing lazy parsing rather than those preserving eager parsing.

The exchange highlights the careful consideration being given to initialization patterns in this configuration migration effort.
