This is an update on Taylor Blau's ongoing feature series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The series is now in its third major iteration, building on previous work to refactor repack.c and implement MIDX layer compaction.

Taylor notes that review feedback has stabilized, with only minor changes planned for the next version (v2) after the 2.54 release. The key updates will be:
- Switching from string_list to strset for tracking retained MIDX files
- Reordering the keep_hashes array construction
- Renaming --checksum-only to --no-write-chain-file for clarity
- Using is_lock_file_locked() for chain file updates

The series continues to show strong technical quality and careful attention to edge cases, with Jeff King (Peff) providing thorough reviews. The incremental MIDX approach aims to significantly reduce repacking overhead in large repositories by maintaining an optimized chain of MIDX layers rather than periodic full repacks.

This represents the final component of Taylor's multi-year effort to improve Git's scalability for large repositories. The implementation appears well-integrated with existing infrastructure and includes comprehensive test coverage. With no outstanding technical concerns identified, the series is nearing readiness for merging.
