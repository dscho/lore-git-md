This is a follow-up discussion in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking, specifically about patch 7/16 which tracks ODB sources in the `existing_packs` struct. Jeff King (Peff) had raised a question about whether the `repo` field in that struct is still needed now that the ODB source is tracked directly. Taylor agrees that while it's possible to derive the repository from the ODB source (via `source->odb->repo`), there are practical cases like trace2 instrumentation that still require direct repository access. Both conclude that while there may be some over-reliance on the repository pointer, addressing it would complicate the current work without clear benefit, so they agree to leave the structure as is for now.

The exchange shows the typical pattern of this series: Taylor proposes architectural change, Peff provides thorough review catching subtle implications, and they converge on a pragmatic solution that keeps the series moving forward while flagging potential future cleanup areas. No changes to the patch are needed based on this discussion.
