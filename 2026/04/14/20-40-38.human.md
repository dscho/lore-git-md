Junio Hamano responds to a proposed fix for the fsmonitor test suite issues identified by SZEDER Gábor. The problem involves lingering daemon processes due to an invalid combination of `test_might_fail` with the new `maybe_timeout` helper, compounded by error output being redirected to /dev/null.

Junio suggests simplifying the fix by removing both the problematic `test_might_fail` wrapper and the error suppression, instead using just `maybe_timeout` followed by `|| :` to ensure the cleanup continues even if stopping the daemon fails. This addresses the immediate symptom (lingering processes) while maintaining the test's robustness.

The response shows the maintainer's typical pragmatic approach - focusing on the simplest effective solution rather than preserving potentially unnecessary layers of error handling. This appears to be the final polish needed before the already-approved fsmonitor series can be integrated.
