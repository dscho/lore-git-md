Siddharth Shrimali's series modernizing the tag-related tests in t7004-tag.sh has reached completion with positive reviews from both key stakeholders. The three-patch series systematically replaces brittle test patterns with more robust verification methods, implementing Junio Hamano's suggested "belt-and-suspenders" approach to state verification. The changes include capturing full tag lists before/after operations, replacing static assumptions with dynamic checks, and eliminating subshell patterns across eight test cases - all while reducing the overall line count.

Junio had already approved the approach in v1 with an "Excellent" comment on the second patch's methodology. Patrick Steinhardt, who had raised initial concerns about thorough verification in v1, has now given his explicit approval to v2, noting the feedback has been properly addressed. With both reviewers satisfied and no outstanding issues, the series appears ready for merging. The changes align with the project's broader test modernization efforts and Patrick's work on improving test reliability through the clar framework.
