This is the third version of a feature series from Derrick Stolee that introduces new options to control Git's fetch/push negotiation behavior. The series has reached technical consensus after addressing feedback from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt.

The key changes in v3 include:
- Renaming `--negotiation-require` to `--negotiation-include` for clearer semantics
- Making `--negotiation-tip` an alias of `--negotiation-restrict` (the new preferred name)
- Adding config options (`remote.*.negotiationInclude` and `remote.*.negotiationRestrict`)
- Improving error handling for missing refs/objects
- Marking included commits as COMMON to avoid duplicate negotiation haves
- Supporting these options in push negotiation when `push.negotiate` is enabled

The motivation comes from monorepo scenarios where certain long-lived branches (like 'main' and 'release') need guaranteed inclusion in negotiation, while still allowing opportunistic inclusion of other refs. The new `--negotiation-include` ensures specified refs are always advertised as "haves", while `--negotiation-restrict` focuses the negotiation set.

The implementation spans multiple files including `builtin/fetch.c`, `fetch-pack.c`, and `transport.c`, with thorough test coverage. The series appears ready for integration with only minor naming cleanup remaining.
