This is a security discussion follow-up about the `git-credential-libsecret` helper leaking credentials when invoked directly. Phillip Wood reinforces the point that credential helpers should only be accessed through the proper `git credential` interface, not invoked directly. He notes that the helpers are intentionally not in `$PATH` to discourage direct usage, and that `gitcredentials(7)` already documents the correct configuration approach without promoting direct helper invocation.

The exchange clarifies that while the helper's stdout output during store operations is technically allowed by the protocol (and discarded when used properly), the real issue is ecosystem-wide misuse of directly invoking helpers. Phillip's response suggests the solution lies in reinforcing proper usage patterns rather than treating this as a protocol flaw. This aligns with the emerging consensus that the vulnerability primarily affects those bypassing Git's intended interface.
