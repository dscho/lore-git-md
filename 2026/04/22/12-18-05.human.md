This is a small refactoring patch in Jiamu Sun's subcommand autocorrection series, now in its fifth iteration. The patch renames `AUTOCORRECT_SHOW` to `AUTOCORRECT_HINT` to better reflect the enum's purpose of showing similar commands without taking further corrective action. The change is mechanical but improves clarity, touching three files to update the enum name and its usage.

The series as a whole adds subcommand autocorrection to Git's parse-options API, matching the existing main command autocorrection behavior. This particular change was likely prompted by review feedback about the original name being ambiguous. With all major technical questions already resolved in previous iterations, this appears to be one of the final polish patches before the feature is ready for merging.
