This is the v3 cover letter for Derrick Stolee's feature series introducing negotiation control options for fetch/push operations. The series has evolved through substantial discussion and now appears technically complete, with only minor naming and documentation refinements remaining.

The core problem being solved involves monorepo scenarios where important long-lived branches (e.g. 'main' and 'release') were being truncated from fetch negotiation due to protocol limits, causing unnecessary large downloads. The series introduces two complementary mechanisms:

1. `--negotiation-restrict` (replacing `--negotiation-tip`) to focus negotiation on specific ref subset
2. `--negotiation-include` (renamed from `--negotiation-require`) to guarantee certain refs are always advertised while still allowing opportunistic inclusion of others

Key changes in v3:
- Finalized option names (`--negotiation-include`/`--negotiation-restrict`)
- Added config options (`remote.*.negotiationInclude`/`negotiationRestrict`)
- Improved error handling for missing refs/objects
- Mark included commits as COMMON to avoid duplicate negotiation
- Support for these options in push negotiation when `push.negotiate` is enabled
- Comprehensive test coverage

The implementation spans `builtin/fetch.c`, `fetch-pack.c`, `transport.c`, and related components, with careful attention to protocol behavior and edge cases. The series has addressed all substantive feedback from Junio and Patrick Steinhardt, indicating it's in final pre-integration state.
