The `git history fixup` series is moving toward integration. Patrick Steinhardt confirmed to Junio C Hamano that he has no plans for another iteration, effectively giving the green light to mark the v3 series for `next`. Junio had previously nudged Elijah Newren, D. Ben Knoble, and Tian Yuchen for final input, noting his own cursory review found nothing curious or fishy and that v2 and v3 received no other review comments. With Patrick's explicit go-ahead and no objections raised, the three-patch series — which lets users amend staged changes to an existing commit while automatically rebasing dependent branches — now appears on track to graduate from in-flight discussion to integration. No review has yet come from the other CCed contributors.
