This is a brief follow-up discussion about refname conventions for the autostash feature in `git checkout`. Jeff King confirms Harald Nordgren's proposal to rename `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH` to `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH_HEAD` would satisfy Git's documented rules for root-level refs (which must end with `_HEAD` or be `HEAD`). 

Jeff acknowledges the new name is somewhat verbose but notes there isn't a shorter compliant alternative. The exchange comes after the v9 patch series implementing autostash behavior has already received maintainer approval from Junio Hamano, indicating this would be a final naming polish rather than a substantive change. The core autostash functionality for conflict resolution remains unaffected by this refname adjustment.
