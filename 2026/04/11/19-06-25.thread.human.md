This thread documents a small but precise documentation fix from Jeff King (Peff) addressing AsciiDoc formatting issues in the Git glossary. The problem occurs when glossary entries contain bulleted sub-lists - the formatting can cause subsequent paragraphs to be incorrectly indented as if they were still part of the sub-list.

The patch fixes this in three glossary entries ("glob", "pathspec", and irregular refs in root hierarchy) by properly scoping the sub-lists with "--" markers. The changes are purely mechanical formatting adjustments that don't affect the actual content, just its rendering. As is typical for Peff's contributions, the commit message thoroughly explains the issue and shows before/after examples of the corrected formatting.

Given the straightforward nature of the fix and its limited scope (only documentation formatting), this is likely to be quickly picked up by Junio for merging without further discussion. The patch demonstrates Git's ongoing effort to maintain high-quality documentation, even for small formatting details.
