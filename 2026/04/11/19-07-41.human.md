This is a final naming refinement for the autostash feature in `git checkout -m`, addressing a convention observation from Jeff King. The patch series (already approved by Junio Hamano) introduces autostash behavior during branch switching with conflicts, but Peff points out that the proposed `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH` refname doesn't follow Git's documented rules for root-level refs (which should end with `_HEAD` or be `HEAD`). 

While there are existing exceptions like `MERGE_AUTOSTASH`, Peff notes the documentation explicitly states the list of exceptions should not be extended. He suggests considering a conformant name like `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH_HEAD` instead of adding another exception. This is a minor post-approval refinement - the core functionality remains sound, but the naming could be adjusted to better match Git's refname conventions before merging. The series has already gone through 9 iterations with all other technical feedback addressed.
