This is a minor naming refinement for the autostash feature in `git checkout`, addressing Jeff King's observation about refname conventions. Harald Nordgren proposes renaming `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH` to `CHECKOUT_AUTOSTASH_HEAD` to comply with Git's documented rules for root-level refs (which must end with `_HEAD` or be `HEAD`). 

The change is straightforward and aligns with existing patterns, though as Jeff noted earlier, there are precedents like `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` that don't follow this convention. The proposal comes after the v9 series has already approved by Junio Hamano, suggesting this would be a last-minute polish before merging rather than a substantive change. The core autostash functionality for conflict resolution remains unaffected.
