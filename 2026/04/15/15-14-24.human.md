This is part 5/7 of Derrick Stolee's series introducing negotiation controls for critical branches in Git's fetch/push operations, addressing monorepo scaling issues. The patch adds a new `--negotiation-require` option to `git fetch` that ensures specified refs are always included in negotiation, even when the algorithm would normally skip them.

The key addition is a mechanism to guarantee certain refs (like important release branches) are always advertised during negotiation, preventing expensive full downloads of parallel histories. The option complements the existing `--negotiation-restrict` by allowing critical refs to be prioritized while still letting the algorithm opportunistically include other refs. The implementation handles exact refs and glob patterns, silently ignoring non-existent refs and avoiding duplicates with the negotiator's selections.

The patch touches multiple files including `fetch-pack.c`, `transport.c`, and their headers, plus adds thorough test coverage in `t/t5510-fetch.sh`. The changes are well-motivated by real-world monorepo problems where missing critical branches in negotiation caused 60GB+ downloads. The implementation appears clean and maintains backward compatibility while solving the stated problem.
