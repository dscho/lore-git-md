This is a technical refinement discussion about the recently merged autostash feature for `git checkout -m`, focusing on error handling details. Phillip Wood follows up on Harald Nordgren's responses to two specific implementation concerns from the final polishing phase.

Harald indicates progress on this refinement, which Phillip confirms is the more critical fix needed before final release.
