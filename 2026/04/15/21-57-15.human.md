This is a review response from Junio Hamano on Derrick Stolee's patch to introduce `--negotiation-restrict` as a clearer alternative to `--negotiation-tip` for fetch/push negotiation controls. Junio approves of the translation-friendly warning message changes but suggests two small improvements:

1. He notes that the new option could potentially be implemented using `OPT_ALIAS()` rather than duplicating the option definition, which would be a cleaner way to maintain the backward compatibility.

2. He points out that the error message for `--negotiate-only` should follow the same pattern as the warning messages by using a `%s` format specifier for the option name, making it easier to translate consistently.

The feedback is minor and constructive, focusing on implementation details rather than the core functionality. Junio's tone suggests the patch is fundamentally sound and close to being ready, with just these small polish items to address. This continues the thread's pattern of converging on a well-designed interface for the negotiation controls feature.
