This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the `contrib/subtree` recursion limit patch series. Ben Knoble responds to Junio Hamano's repeated call for reviewers, confirming he has reached out to potential stakeholders but noting the continued absence of engagement.

The exchange highlights a structural challenge in the Git project: maintaining components like `contrib/subtree` that lack clear ownership. The technical merits of the patches themselves (which replace recursive processing in `git subtree split` to avoid shell recursion limits) remain secondary to the project governance question this thread has surfaced. With no active subtree maintainers stepping forward, this may become a case study in how the project handles contributions to under-maintained components.
