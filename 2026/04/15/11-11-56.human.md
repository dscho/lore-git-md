This is a bugfix patch in the final polishing phase of a merged series that enhances `git checkout -m` with autostash behavior. The patch addresses a resource leak in `merge_working_tree()` where early returns could leave the index lock held, though currently harmless since the process exits soon after.

The change adds `rollback_lock_file()` calls to all early return paths in `merge_working_tree()`, ensuring proper cleanup. While not currently causing issues (since the process terminates), this prevents potential problems if the function is called multiple times in the same process. The patch is straightforward and mechanical, adding the rollback calls while preserving all existing error handling behavior.

This is part of a larger effort that has already been merged to the `next` branch, with Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano providing review. The patch demonstrates Git's attention to proper resource management even in error paths, and while small, it contributes to the overall robustness of the autostash feature.
