This is part 5/7 of Derrick Stolee's series introducing negotiation controls for critical branches in fetch/push operations. The patch adds a new `--negotiation-require` option to `git fetch` that ensures specified refs are always included in negotiation, regardless of the algorithm's selection.

The implementation spans `fetch-pack.c`, `transport.c`, and related headers, with thorough test coverage in `t/t5510-fetch.sh`. Key technical aspects include:
- New `--negotiation-require` CLI option and corresponding plumbing through the transport layer
- Support for exact ref names and glob patterns (silently ignoring non-existent refs)
- Integration with both protocol v0 and v2 fetch negotiation
- Duplicate avoidance between required refs and those selected by the negotiator
- Passthrough support in `git pull`

The option solves a real monorepo scaling issue where important long-lived branches (like 'release') could be truncated from negotiation, causing expensive full downloads. Unlike `--negotiation-restrict` (added in part 4/7) which limits negotiation scope, `--negotiation-require` guarantees inclusion while still allowing opportunistic negotiation of other refs. The two options can be combined for precise control.

The implementation appears clean and well-tested, with comprehensive test cases verifying:
- Basic functionality with exact refs and glob patterns
- Additive behavior with normal negotiation
- Silent handling of non-existent refs
- Duplicate prevention between required and negotiated refs

This patch completes the technical solution for the monorepo negotiation problem described in the cover letter, providing both restrictive and inclusive controls. The series shows careful attention to interface design and thorough test coverage.
