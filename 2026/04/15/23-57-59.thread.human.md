This thread covers Elijah Newren's 3-patch series refining the new `git backfill` command, which optimizes partial clone operations by pre-fetching objects. The series was originally introduced by Derrick Stolee in the current development cycle. The patches add safety checks for invalid `rev-list` arguments, improve documentation, and refine blob downloading behavior.

Derrick Stolee, as the original author of the backfill command, has reviewed the entire series and confirmed it's ready to proceed. While he suggested some potential future improvements - like allowing certain `rev-list` options (`--first-parent` and `--author`) and considering warnings instead of hard failures for output-formatting options - these are noted as optional refinements rather than blockers. The current implementation, particularly the thorough test coverage for edge blob handling, has his approval.

The discussion demonstrates Git's collaborative development process, where new functionality undergoes careful review and refinement before merging. With Stolee's approval as the feature's originator, this series appears poised for inclusion in the next release cycle. The changes address core safety and usability concerns while maintaining the command's original purpose of optimizing partial clone workflows.
