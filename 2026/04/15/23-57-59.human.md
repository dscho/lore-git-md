This is a patch series addressing minor issues in the new `git backfill` command (introduced in the recent `ds/backfill-revs` series). The series comes from Elijah Newren, known for his expertise in Git's merge machinery.

The three patches make incremental improvements to the backfill feature:
1. Adds validation to reject nonsensical `rev-list` arguments that could be passed to `backfill`
2. Improves documentation to be more consistent with how revision ranges are documented elsewhere
3. Introduces a new `--[no-]include-edges` flag to better control blob downloading behavior

Elijah notes that the first two patches are minor fixes that might not be urgent for the upcoming release, while the third could be seen as a small enhancement rather than a pure fix. The changes are well-contained, touching documentation, command implementation, and tests. Given Elijah's track record with careful merge-related work, these changes are technically sound, though the late timing in the release cycle might mean they get deferred.
