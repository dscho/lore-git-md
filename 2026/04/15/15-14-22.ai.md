This is a refactoring patch in Derrick Stolee's series on fetch/push controls, specifically part 3/7 in v2. The patch renames internal variables and functions from `negotiation_tips` to `negotiation_restrict_tips` to better reflect their purpose after the introduction of the new `--negotiation-restrict` option in the previous patch.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching the transport layer (`transport.c`, `transport.h`), fetch implementation (`fetch-pack.c`, `fetch-pack.h`), and builtin fetch code (`builtin/fetch.c`). The rename maintains the underlying functionality while making the code's intent clearer - these tips are specifically for restricting negotiation scope, not just general negotiation hints.

Key technical points:
- The `oid_array` structure remains unchanged, just renamed in all declarations and usages
- Transport layer now uses `negotiation_restrict_tips` in `struct git_transport_options`
- Fetch-pack's API surface changes to use the new terminology in `struct fetch_pack_args` and `negotiate_using_fetch()`
- Backward compatibility is maintained since this is purely an internal rename

The patch demonstrates good attention to detail by updating all related functions and documentation strings consistently. As part of the larger series improving fetch/push negotiation controls for monorepos, this rename helps clarify the distinction between required refs (handled elsewhere in the series) and restricted negotiation scope.
