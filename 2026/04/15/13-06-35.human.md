This is a test infrastructure patch from Patrick Steinhardt, part of his ongoing series to enable `set -e` (errexit) in Git's test suite. The patch modifies the `test_must_fail()` helper function to work correctly when `set -e` is active.

The change ensures that when a command fails (as expected in `test_must_fail`), the shell won't immediately terminate due to `set -e`. Instead, it uses the pattern `exit_code=0; "$@" 2>&7 || exit_code=$?` to capture the exit status without triggering errexit. This is a mechanical but important preparation for the broader `set -e` effort.

The patch is straightforward and well-motivated, addressing a specific edge case in test infrastructure. It follows the established pattern seen elsewhere in the series for handling expected failures under `set -e`. As with the rest of the series, this change focuses on making the test suite more robust while maintaining compatibility across different shell environments.
