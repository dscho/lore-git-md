This is the thirteenth iteration of a feature patch series that enhances `git checkout -m` with autostash behavior during branch switching conflicts. The series has already been merged to the `next` branch and is now in its final polishing phase, addressing both technical refinements and process improvements.

The key changes in v13 focus on:
1. Finalizing error handling in `merge_working_tree()` to properly roll back locks on early returns
2. Improving test infrastructure in `t3903-stash.sh` by replacing repository recreation with tag-based resets
3. Refining the autostash triggering conditions to only attempt for specific `unpack_trees()` errors
4. Making `apply_autostash_ref` conditional on the `created_autostash` flag

The series maintains backward compatibility while making conflict resolution more consistent with Git's existing autostash behavior in rebase/merge. With these final refinements, the series represents a significant quality-of-life improvement for branch switching workflows with uncommitted changes.
