This is a feature proposal for Git's fetch/push negotiation system, part of a 7-patch series from Derrick Stolee addressing monorepo scaling issues. The series introduces new `--negotiation-require` and `--negotiation-restrict` options (with corresponding config settings) to better control which refs are used in negotiation.

The core problem involves fetch negotiation failing in large repos when important long-lived branches (like 'main' and 'release') get truncated from the haves list due to protocol limits. The current `--negotiation-tip` option isn't ideal as it completely excludes other branches rather than prioritizing certain ones.

Key changes in v2:
- Renamed `--must-have` to `--negotiation-require` for clearer semantics
- Added `--negotiation-restrict` as a synonym for `--negotiation-tip` to establish consistent terminology
- Added config support via `remote.*.negotiationRequire` and `remote.*.negotiationRestrict`
- Extended push negotiation to respect these settings when `push.negotiate` is enabled

The implementation looks thorough, with extensive test coverage. The proposal is particularly relevant for monorepo workflows where certain branches are more important than others but shouldn't completely dominate negotiation. The thread shows productive design iteration, with Junio Hamano suggesting unified terminology and Stolee exploring interface alternatives.
