This is a memory leak fix in the fsmonitor daemon implementation, part of a larger series adding cross-platform filesystem monitoring support. The patch addresses a cleanup oversight where the `state.cookies` hashmap and its allocated strings were not being freed when the daemon shuts down.

The fix adds proper cleanup in the `done:` label of `fsmonitor_run_daemon()`, iterating through the hashmap to free each cookie name before clearing the hashmap itself. This resolves a leak that would occur during daemon shutdown, particularly affecting the Linux inotify implementation.

The patch is straightforward and mechanical - it doesn't change any functionality, just adds the missing cleanup. As part of the 15th iteration of this series (which has already received maintainer approval), this represents one of the final polish items before integration. The change follows the same pattern seen elsewhere in the codebase for hashmap cleanup.
