This is a cover letter for a 3-patch series addressing minor issues in the new `git backfill` command (introduced in the current cycle via ds/backfill-revs). The series comes from Elijah Newren, known for his expertise in Git's merge machinery.

The series contains:
1. A safety check to reject invalid `rev-list` arguments that could cause unexpected behavior
2. Documentation improvements to align with standard revision range syntax
3. A behavior tweak to better prevent on-demand blob downloading by defaulting to include edge blobs, with a new `--[no-]include-edges` flag for control

Elijah notes the first two patches are minor and may not be urgent for the upcoming release, while the third could be seen as more of a feature addition than a pure fix. The changes touch documentation, command behavior, and add new test coverage in t/t5620-backfill.sh.

The backfill command appears to be a new addition focused on optimizing repository operations by pre-fetching objects, with this series refining its behavior around revision specification and blob handling. The third patch's edge blob inclusion suggests the command deals with partial clone scenarios where some objects may be missing.
