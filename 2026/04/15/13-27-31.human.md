This is a refactoring patch in the fsmonitor series, part of the cross-platform filesystem monitoring implementation. The patch renames `fsm-settings-darwin.c` to `fsm-settings-unix.c` to reflect that the settings logic is shared across all Unix platforms, not just macOS. It updates the build systems (meson and CMake) to use a new `FSMONITOR_OS_SETTINGS` variable for platform-specific settings, matching the approach already used for IPC.

The change is straightforward and mechanical - a pure rename with corresponding build system updates. The patch builds on prior work from Eric DeCosta and Marziyeh Esipreh, maintaining proper attribution. This is patch 7/13 in the v15 series that has already received maintainer approval, indicating the overall approach is sound. The rename helps prepare for the upcoming Linux fsmonitor implementation by making the Unix platform sharing explicit.
