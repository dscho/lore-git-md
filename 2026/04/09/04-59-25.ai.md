This is a memory leak fix in the fsmonitor daemon implementation, part of the ongoing fsmonitor feature series (now at v14). The patch addresses a resource management issue where the `state.cookies` hashmap and its allocated strings were not being freed during daemon shutdown.

The fix adds proper cleanup in the `done:` label of `fsmonitor_run_daemon()`, iterating through the hashmap to free each cookie name string before clearing and freeing the hashmap itself. This matches the pattern used elsewhere in the codebase for similar structures.

The change is straightforward and clearly correct - it follows the established practice for hashmap cleanup in Git's codebase. The patch demonstrates attention to detail in resource management, which is particularly important for a long-running process like the fsmonitor daemon. This was likely caught during final review of the series before potential inclusion.
