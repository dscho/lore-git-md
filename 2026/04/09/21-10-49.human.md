Platform compatibility issue with `writev()` fallback implementation on NonStop systems. Jeff King responds to Randall S. Becker's report about `NO_WRITEV=Nope` not being properly honored during builds, causing test failures in `t5310-pack-bitmaps.sh`.

The problem stems from Git's fallback `writev()` implementation being too strict in its validation, rejecting writes that would exceed `ssize_t` limits - a scenario that occurs on NonStop systems. Jeff identifies the specific check in the code that's triggering the `EINVAL` error, noting this attempts to emulate system behavior but fails on platforms where `ssize_t` can't handle Git's typical 64k writes.

Jeff outlines two potential solutions for the 2.54.0 release: properly implementing `MAX_IO_SIZE` support (which would be more involved) or temporarily reverting `writev()` usage in `send_sideband()`. The tone suggests this is a tricky tradeoff between correctness and compatibility, with the simpler revert being more feasible for the imminent release.
