This is a technical discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's 16-part series introducing an in-memory object database source (part of the ODB abstraction effort). The exchange focuses on patch 3's removal of a redundant object existence check in `odb_pretend_object()`.

He notes this would make it harder for callers to explicitly target the in-memory source and could unintentionally change behavior for enumeration functions.

The discussion shows both contributors thinking carefully about the long-term implications of the ODB abstraction while keeping the immediate change focused and minimal.
