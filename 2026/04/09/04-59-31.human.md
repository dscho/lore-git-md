This is a feature patch in the fsmonitor series, adding process isolation improvements to the run-command infrastructure. The patch introduces a `close_fd_above_stderr` flag to `struct child_process` that when enabled, makes child processes close all file descriptors above stderr (FD 2) before exec, while preserving the child-notifier pipe used for inter-process communication.

The change is part of the broader fsmonitor effort to ensure clean process environments, particularly important for the filesystem monitoring daemon that needs to run reliably across platforms. The implementation caps the FD scan at the smaller of `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX)` or 4096 for efficiency, and explicitly skips the child-notifier pipe to maintain required IPC channels.

This appears to be a well-contained, focused change that builds on the series' existing process isolation work. The patch follows Git's coding style and includes clear documentation of the new field's purpose. As part of the mature v14 series that has undergone extensive review, this addition seems ready for inclusion.
