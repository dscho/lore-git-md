This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series introducing an in-memory object database source, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The series has progressed through multiple rounds of review, with Karthik Nayak providing feedback that has now been addressed. 

Patrick acknowledges Karthik's positive assessment ("nice read") and indicates he'll send a final version after allowing time for any remaining feedback. The series implements a complete non-persistent ODB backend for transient objects, with applications including git-blame worktree commits and future use cases requiring temporary object storage.

- Full callback coverage (read/write/streaming/prefix resolution)
- Interface unification to `struct odb_source*`
- Oidtree-based storage with O(1) lookups
- Careful progression from infrastructure to integration
