This is a platform compatibility discussion about `writev()` optimizations in Git's network code, continuing the thread about test failures in Git 2.54.0-rc1 on NonStop systems. Jeff King responds to Patrick Steinhardt's proposal with several technical alternatives for handling platforms with small SSIZE_MAX values.

Jeff presents three potential approaches: 1) auto-detecting SSIZE_MAX at compile time with some Makefile adjustments, 2) temporarily reverting to USE_WRITEV instead of NO_WRITEV to effectively disable the optimization until it properly respects MAX_IO_SIZE, or 3) simply adding NO_WRITEV to NonStop's config.mak.uname as the quickest fix for the immediate release. He notes the auto-detection approach is more involved than ideal for an rc2 release, while the USE_WRITEV swap would be a temporary measure.

The email shows the careful balancing act between maintaining platform compatibility and preserving performance. Jeff's analysis suggests the simplest solution (adding NO_WRITEV to NonStop's config) may be the most practical immediate fix, though it leaves open the question of how to handle other platforms that might have similar SSIZE_MAX limitations. The discussion continues to weigh the tradeoffs between short-term fixes and longer-term architectural solutions.
