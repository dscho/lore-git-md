This is a follow-up in the `git checkout -m` autostash feature series, where Harald Nordgren provides an update on some previously reported edge cases. The issue disappears when executing directly with the correct GIT_EXEC_PATH. This confirmation removes the last open question about production readiness that had been raised in the previous email.
