This is a technical discussion thread about optimizing the empty tree handling in Patrick Steinhardt's in-memory ODB backend implementation (patch 4/17 of the series). Karthik Nayak had suggested checking for the empty tree case before iterating through all objects, as a potential optimization.

Patrick considers the suggestion but notes that in most cases `source->objects_nr` would be zero anyway, making the loop overhead negligible. He then proposes an alternative approach - storing the empty tree like other objects to avoid special casing entirely. This would allow future flexibility (like modifying tree mtimes) at the cost of an extra allocation and initialization.

The exchange shows careful consideration of tradeoffs in this low-level storage layer. Patrick's response indicates he's thinking ahead to potential future needs while acknowledging the current simple use case. The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than fundamental design, suggesting the overall approach is sound.
