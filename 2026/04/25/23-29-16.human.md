Peff suggests an alternative to Holmgren's compromise of reserving only `command` and `alias-*` prefixes - instead proposing that Git to treat dotted aliases as both potential commands and potential attribute lookups simultaneously. This would maintain maximum backward compatibility while still allowing for future attribute extensions like help text. He acknowledges this creates some edge cases (like `foo.command` being accidentally invocable), but considers them unlikely to cause real-world issues.

The discussion highlights two key tradeoffs: Peff notes that while reserving specific prefixes (Holmgren's approach) is workable, the three-level syntax is ultimately the forward-looking solution. He makes the pragmatic observation that cross-version compatibility concerns will fade over time as older versions become less common.
