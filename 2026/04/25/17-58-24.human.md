This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about adding a `--fetch` option to `git checkout`/`git switch`. The author, Harald Nordgren, responds to Junio Hamano's strong objections about automatically fetching remote refs during branch creation.

Key points in this response:
- Harald acknowledges Junio's clear stance against implicit fetching but seems to still advocate for the feature's usefulness
- He questions Junio's concern about working with the "latest commit", arguing developers will have to deal with upstream changes eventually anyway
- The discussion touches on the practical challenge of fetching only specific branches when multiple exist on the same remote
- Harald maintains a respectful tone while continuing to push back on some of the maintainer's technical objections

The exchange highlights the fundamental disagreement about whether automatic fetching aligns with Git's design philosophy. While Harald focuses on developer convenience, Junio's concerns center around stability and predictable behavior. The thread remains at an impasse, with the author not yet addressing the core architectural concerns raised by the maintainer.
