This is a bugfix addressing a regression in Git's alias handling system, specifically restoring support for hierarchical dotted aliases (like `pull.sub`) that broke in Git 2.54.0. The patch comes from Jonatan Holmgren, who originally introduced the regression in commit ac1f12a9de when adding three-level alias syntax support.

The fix has already received technical approval from Jeff King and René Scharfe, but maintainer Junio C Hamano raises a design concern about whether the solution might limit future extensibility of the alias system. In this follow-up, Holmgren acknowledges the challenge and suggests possible compromises - reserving `command` and `alias-*` prefixes rather than the current approach, while expressing reservations about moving to a completely separate namespace for aliases.

The discussion has shifted from the immediate technical fix (which appears sound) to broader questions about the alias system's design evolution. Holmgren's response indicates he's considering Junio's concerns but sees trade-offs in any alternative approaches. The patch remains likely to be accepted given its technical merits, but this exchange may influence future alias-related work.
