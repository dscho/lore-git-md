Performance optimization review from the maintainer.

Junio C Hamano responds to Scott Bauersfeld's patch increasing I/O buffer sizes in `index-pack` and `unpack-objects`. While generally approving the change, Junio makes two technical observations:

1. Corrects a minor documentation inaccuracy - `unpack-objects` doesn't write to a pack file (it writes loose objects), though the performance rationale remains valid.

2. Questions why the measured 66% reduction in writes falls short of the theoretical ~97% improvement expected from 4KB→128KB buffer sizing. Suggests investigating whether small writes (<4KB) that can't benefit from larger buffers might explain the difference, proposing additional metrics to capture this.

The review maintains the patch's technical focus while probing for deeper understanding of the performance characteristics. Junio also floats the idea of centralizing the 128KB buffer size constant (currently hardcoded in multiple places) into a shared header, though treats this as a potential follow-up rather than a blocking concern.

This is a substantive review that engages with both the implementation details and the performance analysis while remaining constructive. The questions raised aim to strengthen the patch's justification rather than challenge its core premise.
