This is a refactoring patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, part of his v3 series introducing the new `git format-rev` command. The patch makes a minor but correct style fix in `builtin/name-rev.c` to align with Git's coding guidelines by adding braces around both arms of conditional statements, even when one is a single line.

The change is purely stylistic - it doesn't affect functionality but improves consistency in the codebase. This appears to be preparatory work for the larger series that follows, which will refactor and share code between name-rev and the new format-rev command. The patch is small, focused, and correctly follows the project's style conventions.
