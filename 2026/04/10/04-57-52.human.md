The exchange shows consensus forming around Junio's maintainer decision while leaving the door open for future improvements. Patrick's involvement signals this is being treated as a temporary setback rather than abandonment of the `writev()` optimization effort. The tone remains collaborative, with all parties focused on stability for the imminent release.
