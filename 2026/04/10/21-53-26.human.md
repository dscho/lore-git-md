This is the final maintainer approval for a feature patch series that enhances `git checkout -m` with autostash behavior during branch switching conflicts. Junio C Hamano gives the green light to merge the ninth iteration of Harald Nordgren's series, which has undergone extensive review from Phillip Wood and others.

The series introduces several improvements: customizable conflict marker labels in `git stash apply` (now standardized as `--label-ours`, `--label-theirs`, and `--label-base`), silent autostash creation for non-interactive use, and unified conflict resolution behavior. Junio notes the option renaming makes the interface more consistent with other Git commands, though it slightly increases tab-completion length. He also approves keeping the autostash creation message on stdout rather than moving it to stderr, as that would be a separate behavioral change.

With all technical concerns addressed across nine iterations and comprehensive test coverage (including 19 test cases for checkout behavior), this series is now ready for merging. It represents a significant quality-of-life improvement for developers who frequently switch branches with uncommitted changes.
