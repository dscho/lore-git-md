This is the final clarification in Lorenzo Pegorari's v4 patch series about promisor files during repacks, part of his GSoC work mentored by Eric Sunshine. He acknowledges that even though some headers (like strbuf.h) might work transitively through other includes, the CodingGuidelines require explicit inclusion of all headers providing used types/functions. Lorenzo plans to add a 6th patch to include the missing headers (strmap.h, strbuf.h, hash.h, string-list.h, and strvec.h) to fully comply with the guidelines.
