This is patch 3/6 in Lorenzo Pegorari's v5 series on preserving promisor file contents during repacks, part of his GSoC work mentored by Eric Sunshine. The patch focuses on maintaining debugging information in `.promisor` files when repacking promisor packfiles.

The key change here is preserving the contents of existing `.promisor` files (which contain ref-to-packfile mappings for debugging) rather than creating empty ones after repacking. The patch introduces a `strset` to track excluded packs during geometric repacking and updates the documentation to clarify that `.promisor` files will now retain their contents.

The implementation uses the `copy_promisor_content()` helper from earlier in the series to transfer content from old to new `.promisor` files. For geometric repacking, it builds a set of excluded pack basenames to properly handle the split between repacked and retained packs. The changes are well-contained, touching just the repack-promisor.c implementation and the git-repack documentation.
