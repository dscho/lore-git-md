This is the third patch in Lorenzo Pegorari's v4 series on preserving promisor file contents during repacks, part of his GSoC work mentored by Eric Sunshine. The patch focuses on integrating the `copy_promisor_content()` helper function (introduced in patch 2/5) into the repack machinery.

The key change ensures that when repacking promisor packfiles, the new `.promisor` file preserves debug information from the original files rather than being created empty. For geometric repacking scenarios, it maintains a `strset` of excluded pack basenames to properly handle the split between repacked and retained packs. The implementation shows careful attention to both the normal repack case (where no packs are excluded) and the more complex geometric case.

Technical details include:
- Modified `finish_repacking_promisor_objects()` to accept the `not_repacked_basenames` strset
- Proper cleanup of the strset after geometric repacking
- Updated documentation to reflect that `.promisor` files now preserve content
- Maintains the debug format "<oid> <ref> <time>" when copying entries

The patch builds cleanly on the previous work in the series, with all major feedback from earlier iterations addressed. The geometric repack integration in particular shows thoughtful consideration of Taylor Blau's ongoing geometric repacking work. With only the minor header inclusion question from patch 2/5 remaining unresolved, this appears to be a well-constructed contribution nearing completion.
