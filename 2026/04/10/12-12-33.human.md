This is a performance optimization patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction series. It eliminates a duplicate object existence check in the in-memory ODB backend.

The patch removes a redundant call to `find_cached_object()` in `odb_pretend_object()`, since the function already checks for object existence through `odb_has_object()`. This avoids an unnecessary second lookup when storing objects in the in-memory cache.

As part of a well-reviewed 17-patch series that's nearly ready for integration, this change is a small optimization that follows naturally from the larger architectural work. The series as a whole introduces a complete in-memory ODB backend with thorough test coverage, and this minor inefficiency was caught during the review process. The change is straightforward and low-risk, touching only 3 lines in odb.c.
