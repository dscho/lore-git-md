This is patch 3/6 in Lorenzo Pegorari's v5 series on preserving promisor file contents during repacks, part of his GSoC work mentored by Eric Sunshine. The patch focuses specifically on maintaining debugging information in `.promisor` files when repacking promisor packfiles.

The key change here is modifying the repack machinery to preserve the contents of existing `.promisor` files rather than creating empty ones. The patch introduces a `strset` to track excluded packs during geometric repacking and uses the `copy_promisor_content()` helper (added in patch 2/6) to transfer relevant content to the new `.promisor` file. The documentation is updated to clarify that `.promisor` files will now contain "arbitrary contents" rather than being empty.

Technical details worth noting for future reference:
- Handles both regular and geometric repacking scenarios
- Uses `strset` to track excluded packs in geometric case
- Maintains compatibility with existing promisor file format
- Updates documentation to reflect behavior change
