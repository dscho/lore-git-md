This is a technical follow-up from SZEDER Gábor regarding Patrick Steinhardt's precompiled header (PCH) implementation for the Meson build system. The concern focuses on whether the PCH should be applied to reftable source files, most of which don't include git-compat-util.h.

SZEDER notes that while the current implementation works (accidentally, in their view), it's conceptually incorrect to force-include git-compat-util.h via PCH for reftable sources that deliberately avoid including it. This suggests the PCH configuration may need refinement to exclude the reftable directory from forced inclusion.

The observation is technically sound but doesn't appear to block merging - it's more about architectural correctness than a functional issue. Given that the series has already gone through multiple iterations and received positive feedback from other reviewers, this would likely be addressed as a follow-up if deemed important. The comment comes from someone who admits they "don't do meson", so the impact assessment may need verification from Meson experts on the list.
