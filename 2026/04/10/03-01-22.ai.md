This is a community outreach call for Google Summer of Code (GSoC) mentors in the Git project. Kaartic Sivaraam announces that Git has received 19 student proposals for GSoC 2026 but is limited to accepting only 3-4 students due to having just 6 mentors (3 primary, 3 co-mentors). The email highlights how mentoring helps grow the contributor base, noting that several current community members like Karthik, Ayush and Chandra started as GSoC participants.

The call is aimed at experienced Git contributors who can volunteer as mentors or co-mentors, emphasizing that even co-mentoring makes a significant difference. There's also a specific request for someone to mentor a student who submitted a self-proposed project. The email links to the official GSoC 2026 ideas page and the self-proposed project for reference.

This is a straightforward community-building effort rather than a technical contribution. The tone is positive and encouraging, framing mentoring as both impactful for the project and rewarding for the mentors themselves. The message serves as a reminder that Git's technical work depends on maintaining an active contributor pipeline, and that experienced developers have an opportunity to help shape the next generation of contributors.
