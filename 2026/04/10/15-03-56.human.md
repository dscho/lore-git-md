This is the third patch in Lorenzo Pegorari's v4 series on preserving promisor file contents during repacks, part of his GSoC work mentored by Eric Sunshine. The patch focuses on integrating the `copy_promisor_content()` helper function (introduced in patch 2/5) to maintain debug information in `.promisor` files when repacking.

The key change modifies the repack logic to preserve the contents of existing `.promisor` files rather than creating empty ones. For geometric repacking (which splits packs into repacked and retained sets), it introduces a `strset` to track excluded packs. The implementation handles both regular and geometric repack scenarios while maintaining the debug format of "<oid> <ref> <time>" entries.

The patch updates the `git-repack` documentation to clarify that `.promisor` files will now contain arbitrary contents rather than being empty. The changes touch `repack-promisor.c` and the documentation, with careful attention to error handling and cleanup.
