This is part 15 of Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series introducing an in-memory object database backend, a key piece of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The series is now in its third iteration and has received thorough technical review.

This particular patch stubs out the remaining ODB interface functions that aren't needed for the in-memory implementation. It adds no-op implementations for operations like closing the source and reprepare callbacks, while explicitly erroring out for unsupported features like transactions and alternates. The changes are straightforward - they complete the interface coverage without introducing new functionality.

The series as a whole is in good shape, with all technical concerns addressed and only minor terminology alignment remaining before integration. This patch represents the final plumbing needed to fully implement the ODB interface contract, showing careful attention to covering all edge cases even for operations that won't be used in practice.
