The Git project is seeking additional mentors for Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2026, as current mentor capacity only allows accepting 3-4 students despite receiving 19 promising proposals. Kaartic Sivaraam highlights how mentoring helps grow the contributor base, noting that several current active contributors (including Karthik Nayak, Ayush, and Chandra) began as GSoC participants.

The call targets experienced Git contributors who could serve as either primary mentors or co-mentors, emphasizing that even co-mentoring makes a meaningful difference. A specific need exists for a mentor to guide a student with a self-proposed project. This outreach underscores how Git's technical work depends on maintaining a pipeline of new contributors, with mentoring framed as both impactful for the project and rewarding for experienced developers.
