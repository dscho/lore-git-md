The thread discusses the need for additional mentors for Git's participation in Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2026. Kaartic Sivaraam initiated the discussion by reporting that while 19 student proposals were received, only 3-4 can be accepted due to a shortage of mentors (3 primary and 3 co-mentors currently available). He emphasized how mentoring has helped grow Git's contributor base, pointing to current active contributors like Karthik Nayak, Ayush, and Chandra who started as GSoC participants. The call specifically seeks volunteers for a student's self-proposed project.

Christian Couder, a Git PLC member and experienced GSoC mentor, has now joined the discussion to reinforce the importance of mentoring. He notes that the 19 proposals represent significant effort from students who have often already engaged with the project through micro-projects and patch reviews. Christian emphasizes that co-mentoring can require minimal time while still making a meaningful impact, and his endorsement as both a PLC member and GitLab employee (where several current contributors began as GSoC students) adds weight to the call for volunteers. The thread remains focused on community growth, highlighting how mentoring helps sustain Git's contributor pipeline.
