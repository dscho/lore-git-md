This is a review from Junio C Hamano on Karthik Nayak's patch about skipping lock files during ref consistency checks. Junio raises two substantive points about the patch's design:

1. He notes the commit message is misleading when it says "all other checks" when there's actually only one other check (files_fsck_refs_content) being skipped. This overstates the scope of the change.

2. More importantly, he questions the architectural choice of moving the lock file check up to files_fsck_refs_dir() rather than keeping it in files_fsck_ref(). He suggests the latter might be more future-proof if new callers of files_fsck_ref() emerge that would also need to skip lock files.

Junio's review is substantive - he's engaging with the design tradeoffs rather than just surface issues. He's not rejecting the patch outright but prompting the author to justify or reconsider the implementation approach. The core issue (that lock files should be skipped during validation) isn't in dispute, just the best way to achieve it.
