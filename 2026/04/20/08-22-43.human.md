This is part 13 of Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the setup subsystem. The patch maintains backward compatibility through the existing `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define in test-partial-clone.c.
