This is part 9 of Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on converting `setup_work_tree()` to use an explicit repository parameter instead of the global variable. The patch follows the established pattern of this mechanical conversion series, updating the function signature and all 21 call sites to pass `the_repository` explicitly.

The change required handling two pieces of state previously tracked via global variables:
1. Worktree initialization status - this was dropped entirely since the idempotent nature of the operations means we can safely repeat them when switching between worktrees
2. Bogus worktree configuration - this state was moved into the `repository` struct as `worktree_config_is_bogus`

The patch touches a wide range of files (21 total) but the changes are straightforward mechanical updates. The most notable impact is in `setup.c` where the initialization logic was simplified by removing the redundant check. This continues the series' pattern of methodically converting one logical unit at a time while maintaining backward compatibility.
