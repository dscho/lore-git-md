This is part 10 of Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the setup subsystem. The patch converts `set_git_work_tree()` to take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository` variable.

The change moves the worktree initialization tracking from a global variable into `struct repository`, making it repository-specific. This maintains the existing safety check that prevent reinitialization with different worktree paths, but now does so per-repository rather than globally. The patch updates all callers (primarily in clone.c and init-db.c) to pass `the_repository` explicitly.

This is a typical mechanical conversion in the ongoing effort to eliminate `the_repository` usage. The change is straightforward but touches core initialization paths, so careful review of the state management is warranted. The patch follows the established pattern of the series - methodically working through dependencies while maintaining backward compatibility.
