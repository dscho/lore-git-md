This is part 3 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing test infrastructure series to enable `set -e` (errexit) in Git's test suite. The patch focuses on adapting the `stop_git_daemon()` helper function to work correctly with stricter error checking.

The change modifies how the test suite handles git-daemon process termination in two ways:
1. Makes the `wait` call resilient to failure by capturing its exit code with `||` rather than letting it trigger immediate exit under `set -e`
2. Silences potential failures from a redundant kill attempt (via PID file) since the process is likely terminated by the first kill

These are mechanical changes needed to maintain existing behavior when `set -e` is enabled, following the pattern established in earlier patches of the series. The patch demonstrates careful attention to edge cases in process management while keeping the changes minimal and focused.

This continues the methodical approach seen throughout the series, with each patch addressing specific subsystems that need adjustment for `set -e` compatibility. The changes are well-contained and clearly motivated by the overall goal of making test failures more visible.
