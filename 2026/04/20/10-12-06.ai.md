This is the final patch in Karthik Nayak's series to consolidate object validation and peeling logic across Git's reference backends. The patch completes the work started in previous patches by modifying the packed and reftable backends to use the pre-peeled tag values now provided by the generic refs layer, rather than performing their own peeling operations.

The changes are straightforward mechanical updates to both backends:
- The packed backend now checks the `REF_HAVE_PEELED` flag and uses the `peeled` field from the `ref_update` struct when writing entries
- The reftable backend similarly uses the pre-peeled value when available, and removes its now-unnecessary `peel_object()` call
- Both backends drop their direct dependency on the object database for peeling operations

This patch builds on the infrastructure added in patch 7/8 (which introduced the `peeled` field to `ref_update`) and represents the payoff of the series' architectural changes. The end result is more consistent behavior across backends and elimination of duplicate peeling work. The changes align with the series' overall goal of moving backend-specific logic into the shared refs layer where possible.
