Patrick Steinhardt continues his methodical work to remove the `the_repository` global variable from Git's setup subsystem, now sending part 3 of his 18-patch series. This installment focuses on converting `is_inside_git_dir()` to take an explicit repository parameter, following the same pattern as the earlier `is_inside_worktree()` conversion. Both functions now compute their results dynamically rather than relying on cached values, which could produce incorrect results with relative paths.

The change is primarily mechanical, updating the function signature and its few callers in `rev-parse.c` and setup.c to pass the repository parameter. Like the previous parts, this builds on the established pattern for `the_repository` removal - carefully transforming one piece at a time while maintaining backward compatibility. The series remains focused on the setup subsystem, with more conversions of core functions like `setup_work_tree()` still to come in future patches.
