Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series to complete the `the_repository` removal in the setup subsystem has progressed to v2, with Elijah Newren having previously reviewed and approved most of the changes. The series systematically converts setup.c functions and their callers across 116 files to use explicit repository parameters instead of the global `the_repository` variable.

Junio Hamano has now weighed in with a substantive review of patch 9/18, which handles `setup_work_tree()`. While agreeing with the removal of the problematic `work_tree_config_is_bogus` variable, Junio raises an important performance consideration about the loss of idempotency when removing the `initialized` flag. His feedback suggests the series is nearly ready but may need minor adjustments to address this point before merging. The thread shows the project's careful approach to even mechanical changes - maintaining both correctness and performance as global state is eliminated.
