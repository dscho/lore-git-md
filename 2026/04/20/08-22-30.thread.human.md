The mechanical conversion touches 116 files, systematically replacing global variable usage with explicit repository parameters across core functions like `setup_git_directory()`, `setup_work_tree()`, and repository initialization logic.

He argues the overhead is negligible since the affected paths aren't performance-critical and the operations are inexpensive. While keeping the door open for future optimizations, Patrick considers this an acceptable tradeoff for the refactoring's benefits.
