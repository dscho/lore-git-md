This is a cover letter for a patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, targeting the setup subsystem. Patrick Steinhardt proposes an 18-patch series that systematically eliminates uses of the global `the_repository` variable from setup.c and its callers across the codebase.

The series represents the first of three planned steps to refactor Git's repository setup machinery. The current focus is purely mechanical - removing `the_repository` dependencies without yet tackling deeper architectural changes. The most notable patches modify core functions like `is_inside_worktree()`, `is_inside_git_dir()`, `setup_work_tree()`, and `set_git_work_tree()` to accept explicit repository parameters.

The changes are wide-ranging, touching 83 files with 393 insertions and 383 deletions. While most changes are straightforward mechanical conversions, the cover letter flags several patches that required more substantial logic changes to work without the global variable. The series builds on commit 5361983c07 from late March 2026 and has already gone through one revision addressing minor issues.

This appears to be a well-structured, incremental step in the larger `the_repository` removal project. The patch series follows the established pattern for such conversions - methodically working through dependencies while maintaining backward compatibility. The scope is appropriately limited to just the setup subsystem at this stage, with more substantive refactoring deferred to future series.
