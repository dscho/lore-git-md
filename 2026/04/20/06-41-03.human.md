This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's RFC series proposing a major reorganization of Git's source tree by moving libgit.a components into a new "lib/" directory. The thread has established broad consensus on the structural benefits, with Junio C Hamano supporting the change primarily for improved shell tab completion usability. 

The current email clarifies a minor point about Rust build requirements - while Patrick didn't originate the specific "rust/" subdirectory suggestion (contrary to brian m. carlson's recollection), he confirms he has no objection to that approach. This keeps the discussion moving forward on accommodating Rust's build system needs while achieving the directory cleanup goals.

The exchange shows the proposal is progressing smoothly with only minor implementation details being worked out. All major technical concerns raised in reviews (Rust build constraints, submodule handling, and discoverability impacts) have either been resolved or acknowledged as non-blocking. The patch appears well on its way to acceptance, representing one of the most significant source tree reorganizations in Git's history.
