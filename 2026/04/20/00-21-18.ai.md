This is a technical review from Jeff King (Peff) addressing edge cases in the `--reverse=before` revision walk patch series. The focus is on handling multiple `--reverse` flags and maintaining backward compatibility.

Peff proposes concrete changes to the option parsing logic in `revision.c` to properly handle combinations like `--reverse --reverse` (which should cancel out) and `--reverse=after --reverse=before` (which should use last-one-wins). His solution introduces a `--no-reverse` option for explicit negation while preserving the historical behavior of double `--reverse`.

The implementation uses an enum-based approach with three states (no reverse, reverse after, reverse before) and strict validation of the `--reverse=<value>` syntax to prevent ambiguity. Peff notes this would be simpler with `parse-options` infrastructure, but recognizes that conversion is out of scope for this series.
