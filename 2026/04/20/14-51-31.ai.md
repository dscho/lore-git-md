This is a follow-up in the UTF-8 truncation bugfix thread, where Elijah Newren responds to Lorenzo Pegorari's question about test coverage for control character handling. This specific pattern triggers the out-of-bounds read under AddressSanitizer (ASan) before the fixes are applied. Elijah indicates he'll send a corrected version of the patch with a properly constructed test case.
