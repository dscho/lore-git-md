This thread presents Karthik Nayak's 8-patch series refactoring Git's reference backends to consolidate object validation and peeling logic into the shared layer. The changes standardize behavior across the files, packed, and reftable backends while eliminating duplicate work. 

The series takes an incremental approach, with each patch making one focused change. Early patches prepared the infrastructure - standardizing backend initialization, updating transaction error handling, and moving object validation to the transaction layer. The key architectural shift came in patch 7, which introduced pre-peeled tag storage in the ref_update struct. The just-posted final patch completes the work by modifying the packed and reftable backends to use these pre-peeled values rather than performing their own peeling operations.

The changes are mechanical but significant, touching transaction handling, backend implementations, and protocol behavior while maintaining backward compatibility. The series aligns with the broader ref backend consolidation effort led by Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik. With all patches now posted and no substantive objections raised, the series appears ready for maintainer consideration. The incremental, well-contained nature of the changes and their alignment with established patterns suggest they may be uncontroversial despite their architectural impact.
