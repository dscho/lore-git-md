This thread covers Karthik Nayak's v3 patch series refactoring Git's reference backend code to consolidate object validation and peeling logic. The goal is to standardize these operations across all backends (files, packed, and reftable) by moving them into the shared refs layer, continuing a broader backend consolidation effort led by Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik.

The series has progressed through careful review, with each iteration incorporating feedback. This eliminates duplicate work and ensures consistent behavior while simplifying the backend implementations. The changes are mechanical, following through on the architectural direction established earlier in the series.

Patrick Steinhardt has approved the overall approach, and Toon Claes has verified the technical details. With all substantive feedback addressed and no new concerns raised, the series appears ready for final maintainer review.
