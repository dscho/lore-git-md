He also notes that even small cleanup efforts still require review bandwidth.

The comment doesn't affect the technical content of the promisor-remote series itself, but shows Junio's maintainer perspective on code quality - preferring immediate fixes over deferring known issues, even for stylistic concerns. This aligns with Git's general practice of fixing problems when they're noticed rather than accumulating technical debt. The exchange highlights how maintainers balance the cost of small improvements against the long-term health of the codebase.
