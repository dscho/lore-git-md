This is a review response in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking (currently at patch 12/16). Jeff King (Peff) examines the conversion of the `--write-midx` flag from a boolean to an enum-based callback option.

Peff notes that while the compiler wouldn't catch unconverted boolean checks against the new enum (since the variable name remains `write_midx`), he manually verified all usage sites were properly updated. He considers but ultimately dismisses the idea of explicitly setting `REPACK_WRITE_MIDX_NONE=0` to preserve boolean compatibility, reasoning that such compatibility would break anyway when future modes are added.

The review confirms the mechanical correctness of this preparatory refactoring patch, which maintains existing behavior while setting up the infrastructure for new MIDX-writing modes. Peff's thoroughness is characteristic of his reviews - he checks not just what changed, but what could have gone wrong during the change.
