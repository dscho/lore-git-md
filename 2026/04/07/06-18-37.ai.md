This is a review response in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking (currently at patch 12/16). Jeff King (Peff) examines the conversion of `--write-midx` from a boolean to an enum-based callback option.

He considered suggesting explicitly setting `REPACK_WRITE_MIDX_NONE` to 0 to preserve this behavior but after manually verifying all usage sites were properly updated, concludes this isn't necessary. He also observes that future enum additions would make such boolean checks ambiguous anyway.

The review confirms the mechanical correctness of the refactoring while highlighting a subtle consideration about transition safety. This continues the pattern of Peff's thorough reviews in this series, catching potential edge cases even when they don't materialize as actual issues in the current patch.
