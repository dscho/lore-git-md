This is a bugfix follow-up in Lorenzo Pegorari's GSoC series on promisor file handling during repacks. The email addresses two specific issues raised by Junio Hamano in the previous review:

1. The `localtime_r()` usage that was breaking macOS builds will be fixed. The author notes surprise that it compiled at all on their system, highlighting the value of CI in catching platform-specific issues.

2. More significantly, the author agrees to change the timestamp storage format from human-readable text ("%Y/%m/%d-%H:%M:%S") to Unix epoch time as suggested by Junio. This acknowledges the maintainer's point about robustness being more important than human readability for this debugging information.

The exchange shows the patch progressing through increasingly detailed review stages, with the author responsive to both implementation-level fixes (the macOS breakage) and design-level feedback (the timestamp format). This suggests the next version will be more portable and use a simpler time representation while maintaining the core functionality of tracking packfile relationships during repacks.
