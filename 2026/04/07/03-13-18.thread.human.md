This thread introduces a patch to preserve the 'change-id' header during commit rewrites like rebase, cherry-pick, and amend. The change addresses a practical need for tools like Gerrit, GitButler, and Jujutsu that rely on stable change IDs to track commits across modifications.

The implementation modifies the sequencer to detect and carry forward existing change IDs without altering their format, maintaining compatibility with various tools' conventions. The patch includes tests verifying the behavior in all three rewrite scenarios. The author explicitly avoids tackling the broader question of standardizing the change-id format, focusing instead on this targeted compatibility improvement. The change appears well-considered, with clear motivation and thorough test coverage.
