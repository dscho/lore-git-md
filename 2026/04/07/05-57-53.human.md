This is a review response in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. Jeff King (Peff) provides feedback on patch 6/16, which introduces customizable base layers for MIDX writes and compactions.

Jeff generally approves of the changes, noting they make sense for the geometric repacking approach. The discussion shows how these patches are building toward a sophisticated repacking strategy where layers can be replaced at arbitrary points in the chain.

The review confirms the technical soundness of the implementation while highlighting how some of the new options are becoming quite specialized - a sign this series is moving into advanced territory that may be immediately relevant only to developers working on the repacking machinery.
