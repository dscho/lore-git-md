The patch series (now in v3) aims to reduce command-line verbosity by allowing pathspecs to follow unambiguous push options without requiring an explicit "--" or "push" subcommand.

This approach would avoid needing to track which options are push-specific. Phillip also points out the current implementation doesn't handle negated options like `--no-stage`, and recommends documenting these limitations in the commit message.
