This is a review response in Taylor Blau's ongoing series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. Jeff King (Peff) provides feedback on patch 6/16, which introduces customizable base layers for MIDX writes and compactions.

Jeff generally approves of the approach but raises some conceptual questions about the `--base=none` case, wondering whether it's meaningfully different from writing a non-incremental MIDX. He acknowledges there may be good reasons for the current design (easier geometric repacking logic, potential conversion costs) but doesn't press for changes. The review confirms that the `--base` option's interaction with `--checksum-only` makes sense for maintaining chain invariants during atomic updates.

The implementation itself gets a clean bill of health - Jeff found no issues in the code. He does note that the MIDX options are becoming increasingly specialized, hinting that documentation may need to work harder to keep them accessible. This is a characteristic Peff review: technically thorough, conceptually probing where appropriate, but pragmatic about when to accept design choices. The exchange shows the series continuing to refine through expert peer feedback while maintaining its core architecture.
