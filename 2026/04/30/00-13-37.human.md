This is patch 11/16 in Taylor Blau's series implementing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking for large repositories. The series introduces a new repacking strategy that maintains an incrementally growing and shrinking MIDX chain to avoid periodic all-into-one repacks.

This particular patch teaches `pack_geometry_init()` to handle MIDX layer constraints during geometric repacking. The key changes are:

1. When a `midx_layer_threshold` is configured, the repacking candidates are restricted to only packs in the tip MIDX layer, provided that layer has enough packs (at least the threshold number).

2. The patch tracks whether any tip-layer packs were included in the repack (`midx_tip_rewritten`), which will be used by a subsequent commit to determine how to update the MIDX chain.

The implementation maintains safety properties while giving callers control over the repacking strategy. This is part of the larger effort to improve Git's scalability for large repositories, building on previous work refactoring repack.c and implementing MIDX layer compaction. The series has received thorough review from both object storage (Elijah Newren) and process management (Jeff King) experts.
