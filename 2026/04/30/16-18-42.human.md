This is a review response in the ongoing git-gui bugfix thread about handling bare repositories and missing worktrees. Mark Levedahl raises a substantive technical concern with the v3 patch's approach to repository detection.

Mark argues that the current solution could incorrectly associate a worktree with a different gitdir than the one git-gui was launched from, since bare repositories can be referenced by multiple worktrees. He suggests a simpler approach: directly checking `--is-inside-git-dir` and failing with a clear error if true, which would cover all cases where git-gui is launched from a gitdir rather than a worktree.

This review engages with the core technical challenge - the complex relationship between gitdirs and worktrees - and proposes an alternative solution. It doesn't just identify a problem but suggests a concrete technical direction, making it a substantive review. The feedback indicates the v3 solution may still have edge cases around repository/worktree mapping that need addressing.
