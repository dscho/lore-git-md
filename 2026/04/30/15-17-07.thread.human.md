Here's the human-readable summary:

Christian Couder has shared a draft of Git Rev News edition 134, the project's regular community update newsletter. The draft is open for community review and contributions until its planned publication on May 2, 2026. Contributors can provide feedback via GitHub (pull requests or issue comments) or by replying to the mailing list thread.

This follows the newsletter's standard process of circulating drafts for input before finalizing. The Git Rev News team - Christian Couder, Jakub Narębski, Markus Jansen, and Kaartic Sivaraam - will incorporate feedback and publish the final version. While not a technical discussion itself, the newsletter serves as an important digest of recent Git development activity for those who can't follow all mailing list traffic.
