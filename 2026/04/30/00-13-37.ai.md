This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's 16-part series implementing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The patch prepares the geometry calculation infrastructure to support incremental MIDX repacking by adding logic to selectively consider packs from the tip MIDX layer when certain conditions are met.

Key technical changes:
- Adds new fields to `struct pack_geometry` to track MIDX layer threshold and whether the tip layer was rewritten
- Modifies `pack_geometry_init()` to optionally restrict repacking candidates to packs in the tip MIDX layer when a threshold is configured
- Adds logic in `pack_geometry_split()` to track whether any MIDX'd packs were included in the repack rollup)
- Uses existing `midx_layer_contains_pack()` helper to identify packs in the tip layer

The patch enables two behaviors:
1. When a MIDX layer threshold is set, only packs in the tip layer (and only if that layer has enough packs) will be considered for repacking
2. Tracks whether any MIDX'd packs were rewritten, which will inform how the MIDX chain gets updated in later steps

This is a mechanical preparation patch that builds on the series' earlier infrastructure work while maintaining safety properties. It follows the incremental refactoring approach seen throughout the series, with focused changes that are reviewable in isolation while supporting the larger geometric compaction goal.
