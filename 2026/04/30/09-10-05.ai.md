This is a technical discussion about the `git fetch --deepen` regression, where Mikael Magnusson responds to D. Ben Knoble's analysis of the documentation ambiguity. The thread centers on whether `--deepen` should be a no-op in non-shallow repositories and the implications of the behavior change between Git 2.53.0 and 2.54.0.

Magnusson argues strongly that `--deepen` should be a no-op in non-shallow repositories, using a concrete example to illustrate the expected behavior of the shallow boundary moving incrementally through history until the repository becomes fully unshallow. He contends that Knoble's interpretation (where the boundary could reset to the tip) would lead to nonsensical behavior where a fully unshallow repository could revert to being shallow, which clearly contradicts user expectations and the practical use of `--deepen`.

The email also notes that the original bug report already confirmed the regression was introduced in 2.54.0, making further bisection unnecessary. Magnusson's tone is direct and somewhat dismissive of Knoble's documentation-based argument, emphasizing the practical consequences of the bug (data loss) over theoretical interpretations of the man page. The discussion remains unresolved but is moving toward consensus that the 2.53.0 behavior (no-op in non-shallow repos) is correct, regardless of documentation wording.
