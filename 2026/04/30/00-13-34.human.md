This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's 16-part series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The patch (10/16) extracts a helper function `repack_fill_midx_stdin_packs()` from `write_midx_included_packs()` to handle the common case of writing pack names to stdin when running `git multi-pack-index write`.

The change is purely structural - no behavior is modified. The new helper encapsulates the process of setting up `--stdin-packs` argument, starting the command, writing pack names to stdin, and finishing the command. This prepares for a future change where the same helper will be reused to capture MIDX checksums from stdout when writing MIDX layers with `--no-write-chain-file`.

The patch follows the series' pattern of incremental refactoring to improve maintainability while building toward geometric compaction capabilities. It has already received substantive technical review from both object storage (Elijah Newren) and process management (Jeff King) experts in earlier iterations.
