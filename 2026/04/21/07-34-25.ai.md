This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Git's test suite compatible with `set -e` (errexit), now ready for merging. The patch enables strict error checking by default for Bash 5+ (with opt-in via `GIT_TEST_USE_SET_E` for other shells), ensuring that failures outside test cases will abort immediately rather than being silently ignored.

The implementation:
- Adds CI configuration to enable `set -e` for Linux jobs
- Introduces careful boolean parsing of `GIT_TEST_USE_SET_E` to avoid environment variable issues
- Only enables by default for Bash 5+ due to inconsistent behavior in other shells
- Includes a real-world motivation - catching a recent undetected `test_expect_success` typo

This completes the comprehensive suite-wide adaptation that has gone through six iterations with thorough review from Jeff King, Junio Hamano, and others. All technical questions have been resolved, and the series represents a significant improvement in test reliability by ensuring failures are caught immediately rather than silently passing. The change is particularly valuable for catching setup/teardown errors and typos in test declarations that would otherwise go unnoticed.
