This is a bugfix series addressing issues with duplicate entries in trees and D/F conflict detection, with patches touching both the merge machinery and cache-tree verification. The series comes from Elijah Newren, the foremost expert on Git's merge subsystem.

The cover letter explains that real-world repositories with corrupt trees containing duplicate entries were triggering assertion failures in merge-ort. Additionally, the corruption could have been caught earlier if verify_cache() properly detected non-adjacent directory/file conflicts. The series includes 5 patches:

1. Three preparatory changes to merge-ort's error handling and resource management
2. A fix to make merge-ort abort when encountering duplicate tree entries
3. An enhancement to verify_cache() to catch directory/file conflicts even when they're not adjacent in the index

The series adds new test cases in t0093 and extends existing merge tests to verify the fixes. Given Elijah's deep expertise in this area and the clear real-world motivation, this appears to be a well-justified and carefully constructed series. The changes are focused on hardening edge case handling rather than introducing new functionality.
