Elijah Newren's five-patch series to strengthen merge-ort's error handling and directory/file conflict detection has completed its review cycle with Junio Hamano's thorough technical review of the final patch. The series addresses several key areas in the merge machinery: error propagation fixes, resource leak prevention, duplicate entry detection, and comprehensive D/F conflict handling - including the tricky case of non-adjacent conflicts (like "docs" conflicting with "docs-internal/README.md").

The implementation introduces a new `find_df_conflict()` helper that uses binary search via `index_name_pos_sparse()` to efficiently locate potential conflicts while maintaining O(n log n) performance. Junio's review confirmed the technical soundness of the approach, including edge case handling and proper verification of true conflicts. He suggested a minor polarity flip in the final comparison check for improved readability, though this wasn't a blocking concern.

With all five patches now maintainer-approved, the series is ready for merging into next/master. The changes come with expanded test coverage through new infrastructure (t0093-direct-index-write.pl) and test cases (t0093-verify-cache-df-gap.sh), ensuring the merge-ort backend can reliably detect all forms of directory/file conflicts while maintaining robust error handling. This represents a significant hardening of Git's merge machinery for complex real-world cases.
