Elijah Newren's series to improve merge-ort's handling of duplicate tree entries and directory/file conflicts has now received maintainer approval for all five patches. The changes strengthen Git's merge machinery against potential corruption scenarios by improving error handling, adding robust duplicate entry detection, and enhancing D/F conflict verification - particularly for non-adjacent cases where files might be separated by unrelated entries.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patches while on vacation, underscoring their importance. He approved the technical approach of each patch, including the final one's use of binary search to detect D/F conflicts across intervening index entries. While suggesting a minor readability improvement (extracting logic into a helper function), Junio confirmed the core functionality is sound. With all patches now reviewed and validated, the series appears ready for merging into Git's codebase. The thread also surfaced broader concerns about review bandwidth, with Junio noting the project needs more experienced contributors reviewing others' work.
