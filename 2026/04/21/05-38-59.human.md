The changes make the tests more robust while maintaining their original intent.

Patrick's review is brief but substantive - he indicates the series now meets his standards after the v2 revisions addressed the verification concerns raised in v1. With both Patrick's approval and Junio's previous positive feedback on the approach, this suggests the series is likely ready for merging into the main codebase.
