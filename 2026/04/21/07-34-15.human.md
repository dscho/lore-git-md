This is part 2 of Patrick Steinhardt's 12-patch series to make Git's test suite compatible with `set -e` (errexit), now in its final iteration (v6). The series is ready for merging after thorough review.

The patch modifies the `test_must_fail()` helper function to work correctly under `set -e` by ensuring it doesn't exit early when the command fails (which is the expected case for this helper). The change is mechanical but important - it replaces direct command execution with a pattern that captures the exit code safely (`|| exit_code=$?`).

This is one of many similar adaptations across the test suite to support strict error checking. The series as a whole improves test reliability by ensuring failures outside test cases are caught immediately. All technical questions have been resolved in previous iterations, and this patch represents a straightforward, uncontroversial part of the larger effort.
