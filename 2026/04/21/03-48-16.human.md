This is a technical follow-up in the `--reverse=before` revision walk patch series, where Jeff King (Peff) responds to Mirko Faina's question about handling multiple `--reverse` flags.

Peff clarifies the behavior of chained `--reverse` options, proposing two possible implementations for the state machine:
1. A simpler approach where each `--reverse` toggles the reverse state, losing the before/after distinction after an odd number of toggles
2. A more complex version that preserves the original before/after setting through toggles by separating the reverse flag from its timing value

He notes both approaches have trade-offs in terms of user expectations and implementation complexity, and emphasizes that whichever behavior is chosen should be clearly documented since `--reverse` and `--reverse=after` are not fully equivalent due to their different interaction patterns.

The exchange shows the discussion moving into fine-grained edge cases now that the core implementation approach has been settled.
