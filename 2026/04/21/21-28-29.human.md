Performance optimization discussion about extending blob prefetching to cherry-pick detection in `git rebase`.

Elijah Newren responds to Phillip Wood's review about sharing the `prefetch_cherry_blobs()` logic with `revision.c` to optimize `git rebase`'s cherry-pick detection. While acknowledging the technical feasibility of the change, Newren expresses reservations about expanding partial clone support in this area. He argues that in large repositories where partial clones are needed, computing patch-ids across extensive history is rarely worth the cost, and suggests users might be better served by using `--reapply-cherry-picks` or narrowing their history range.

The author explains he only implemented the `git cherry` optimization due to a specific customer need, and while the code could be reused for `git rebase`, he prefers to leave that extension to future contributors. This maintains the current series' focus while leaving the door open for others to generalize the prefetch logic later. The exchange highlights an architectural consideration that doesn't block the current patches but provides valuable context for future work.
