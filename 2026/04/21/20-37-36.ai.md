This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking series (8/16). The patch renames and exposes a previously internal function that checks whether a pack belongs to a specific MIDX layer, rather than recursively checking the entire MIDX chain.

The change renames `midx_contains_pack_1()` to the more descriptive `midx_layer_contains_pack()` and makes it publicly available via midx.h. The original recursive `midx_contains_pack()` function now uses this new helper. This prepares for future work where callers will need to check pack membership in specific layers rather than the entire chain.

The patch is straightforward with no behavior changes - it simply moves and renames existing functionality. This appears to be a preparatory step for upcoming patches in the series that will need to make layer-specific pack membership checks. The commit message clearly explains the change and its motivation, following the pattern of incremental, well-explained changes seen throughout this series.
