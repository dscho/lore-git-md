This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Elijah Newren's review of Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series converting the setup subsystem.

Patrick responds to Elijah's observation about passing repository parameters consistently in patch 16, explaining his general approach to these large-scale refactorings. He acknowledges the missed optimization Elijah identified in `repo_migrate_ref_storage_format()`, but explains his broader philosophy: when converting subsystems, he prefers to focus on making the minimal required changes to the subsystem itself while doing straightforward `the_repository` swaps in callers, rather than auditing every callsite's repository context. This conservative approach reduces risk by limiting the scope of changes that need verification.

The exchange highlights the practical challenges of these mechanical conversions - while the ideal is to eliminate all `the_repository` usage, the reality is that some builtins still mix repository parameters with global usage, and comprehensively fixing all call chains would require extensive analysis. Patrick indicates he makes exceptions when `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` is undefined, forcing proper parameter passing.

This is a thoughtful discussion about refactoring strategy rather than a technical issue with the series itself, showing the maintainers carefully considering how to balance correctness with pragmatism in this multi-year effort.
