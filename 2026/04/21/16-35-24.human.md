This is an announcement for the upcoming Git Merge 2026 conference, co-hosted by GitButler and GitHub. The event will take place September 17-18 in Lisbon, Portugal, with the main conference day on the 17th and Contributor's Summit/unconference sessions on the 18th. Google will also host a separate Jujutsu (jj) event on September 16th.

The venue is Suspenso in Lisbon, and GitLab will host a social event during the conference. Attendees should arrange their own hotel accommodations as no room block is available yet. Registration details will follow, and financial assistance for travel is available by contacting the Git PLC.

The announcement serves as a save-the-date and invites discussion about potential topics for the unconference sessions. Scott Chacon (GitButler) and Taylor Blau (GitHub) are the primary contacts for the event.
