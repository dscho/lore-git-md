This is the second patch in Elijah Newren's series fixing merge-ort issues with duplicate tree entries and D/F conflicts. The patch removes an unnecessary `show_all_errors` flag from `collect_merge_info()` that was originally copied from unpack-trees.c but serves no purpose in merge-ort's error handling.

The change is a legacy setting that was causing traverse_trees() to continue processing after errors rather than stopping immediately. Removing it enables proper error propagation that will be used in the next patch to handle corrupt trees. The modification is straightforward - just deleting one line that sets the flag - but it's an important prerequisite for the more substantial fixes that follow in the series.
