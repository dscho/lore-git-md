This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing MIDX/bitmap-based repacking series (patch 7/16). The change moves the ODB source tracking from directly accessing `repo->objects->sources` to storing it in the `existing_packs` struct that's already used to track pack information during repacking.

The immediate motivation is to prepare for the incremental MIDX repacking machinery that needs to know which source to use for reading existing MIDX/chain files. The patch also lays groundwork for potential future support of repacking across multiple object sources, though that's not implemented here.

The changes are mechanical and straightforward:
1. Adds a `source` field to `struct existing_packs`
2. Stores the ODB source during pack collection
3. Updates two call sites to use the stored source rather than direct access

This is a typical preparatory refactoring in a larger series - no behavior changes, just restructuring to support upcoming functionality. The patch follows the series' pattern of careful, incremental changes with clear commit messages explaining both immediate and future benefits.
