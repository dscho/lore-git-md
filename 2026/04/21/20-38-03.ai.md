This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's 16-part series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking, a major architectural improvement for large repositories. The patch relaxes a previous restriction that required `--write-midx=incremental` to be used with `--geometric`, now allowing it to operate in an append-only mode when used alone.

Without `--geometric`, the behavior becomes simpler: each repack appends a new MIDX layer containing just the newly written packs, preserving all prior layers unchanged. This provides a middle ground between full geometric compaction and no MIDX chain management at all. The implementation introduces a new `repack_make_midx_append_plan()` function that builds this simpler plan structure.

The patch updates documentation to clarify both modes of operation (with and without geometric repacking) and replaces the test that enforced the old restriction with one demonstrating the new append-only behavior. This completes the series' goal of providing flexible MIDX chain management options while maintaining the core geometric compaction strategy as the more sophisticated (and recommended) path.

As the concluding patch in a long-running effort, this represents the final piece needed to make incremental MIDX repacking fully operational. The implementation appears clean and well-tested, with clear attention to maintaining the safety and atomicity guarantees established in earlier patches. With this change, large repositories now have multiple graduated options for managing their MIDX chains during repacking operations.
