This git-gui bugfix series, originally addressing repository and worktree detection issues, has taken an unexpected turn with substantive pushback from gitk maintainer Johannes Sixt. The v8 series was nearly ready for merging, having unified repository validation and improved worktree handling, but now faces a fundamental rethink of its environment variable approach.

Johannes' latest review identifies a core architectural problem: the series' handling of GIT_WORK_TREE conflicts with gitk's needs and Git's documented behavior. He argues git-gui shouldn't export GIT_WORK_TREE in most cases, since Git already correctly infers the working tree from GIT_DIR alone. His alternative patch simplifies the logic while fixing the root cause of issues like broken `git branch --show-current` commands. This feedback effectively reopens what was thought to be settled design territory.

The discussion now centers on whether to adopt Johannes' approach, which would require reworking parts of the series but would better align with gitk's requirements and Git's core behavior. Shroom Moo, the original author, will need to respond to these concerns, either by incorporating Johannes' suggestions or making a case for the current approach. The gitk compatibility issues raised here are significant enough that the series likely can't proceed in its current form.
