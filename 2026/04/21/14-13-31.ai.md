This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about reorganizing Git's source tree by moving libgit.a components into a "lib/" directory. Derrick Stolee responds to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier message about potentially moving command implementations into a "bin/" directory as part of the reorganization.

Stolee acknowledges Patrick's preference for incremental changes and agrees that focusing on the "lib/" move first makes sense to keep the change manageable. He notes that while moving command-related files could also be beneficial, these files tend to have less churn in the main project compared to forks that might add new commands. 

The exchange shows consensus forming around the approach, with Stolee recognizing the value in reducing risk by tackling the reorganization in stages. This suggests the discussion is moving from design considerations toward implementation planning, with both contributors aligned on the general direction while being mindful of potential impacts on downstream forks.
