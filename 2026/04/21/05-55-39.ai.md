This is a continuation of the discussion about Patrick Steinhardt's proposal to reorganize Git's source tree by moving libgit.a components into a "lib/" directory. The thread has progressed from design considerations to practical implementation concerns.

Patrick responds to Derrick Stolee's concerns about the impact on in-flight patches and downstream forks. He expresses willingness to assist with the transition and notes that Johannes Schindelen (Git for Windows maintainer) has given preliminary approval. Regarding Stolee's suggestion to potentially move command implementations into a "cmd/" directory as well, Patrick reveals he had prototyped this (using "bin/") but chose to focus on the more impactful "lib/" move first to keep the change manageable.

The exchange shows consensus on the technical merits of the reorganization, with attention now turning to execution details. Patrick maintains his preference for incremental changes but remains open to a more comprehensive restructuring if the community prefers it. The discussion remains constructive, with both contributors focused on minimizing disruption while achieving the long-term benefits of a cleaner source tree layout.
