The Git community is preparing for Git Merge 2026, the annual conference co-hosted this year by GitButler and GitHub. The event will be held September 17-18 in Lisbon, Portugal, with the main conference on the first day followed by Contributor's Summit and unconference sessions on the second day. Google will also host a related Jujutsu (jj) event on September 16th at the same venue (Suspenso in Lisbon). 

Organizers Scott Chacon (GitButler) and Taylor Blau (GitHub) are currently gathering community input about potential topics for the unconference sessions while finalizing logistics. Registration details and potential travel assistance options through the Git PLC will be announced separately. This initial announcement serves to help attendees plan their travel to Lisbon for the September event.
