This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series (11/16) to implement incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking for large repositories. The series aims to avoid periodic full repacks by maintaining an optimized MIDX chain structure.

The current patch prepares the geometry-based repacking logic to work with MIDX layers by:

1. Adding logic to `pack_geometry_init()` that restricts repacking candidates to packs in the tip MIDX layer only when that layer meets a configured threshold size
2. Tracking whether any tip-layer packs were rewritten during repacking (via new `midx_tip_rewritten` flag)
3. Maintaining all existing functionality while adding the new MIDX-aware behavior

The changes are well-contained and follow the series' pattern of incremental preparation for upcoming functionality. The patch adds appropriate documentation and maintains the architectural clarity of the repacking logic. This continues the careful, step-by-step approach seen in previous patches of the series.
