Deveshi Dwivedi's patch series to improve `git stash`'s argument parsing has reached its third iteration, with the goal of making the command more ergonomic by automatically assuming a "push" subcommand when unambiguous push-specific flags are present. The v3 series has addressed all previous feedback, with comprehensive test coverage and documentation updates in place.

The discussion has turned to edge cases around option ambiguity. While most push-specific flags (`-p`, `--staged`, etc.) are clearly tied to "push", Mirko Faina noted that `--include-untracked` is also valid for the "show" subcommand. Phillip Wood has weighed in, observing that "-m" (for message) similarly appears in both "push" and "create", and suggesting the simpler approach of always assuming "push" when no subcommand is given, then erroring on any unsupported options. He also pointed out that negated options like `--no-stage` aren't currently handled.

The series appears technically sound and merge-ready, with the remaining discussion focusing on whether to adjust the implementation or simply document these edge cases. The core quality-of-life improvement - reducing command-line verbosity for common stash operations - remains uncontested. Deveshi has been responsive to feedback throughout the three iterations, and reviewers seem generally satisfied with the current state pending resolution of these final considerations.
