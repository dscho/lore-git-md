Jean-Noël Avila has completed a four-patch series standardizing several Git command manual pages (`git-difftool`, `git-range-diff`, `git-shortlog`, and `git-describe`) to the project's synopsis-style AsciiDoc format. This work continues his long-running effort to bring consistency to Git's man pages through mechanical formatting updates that don't alter content. Each patch follows the same established pattern: reorganizing SYNOPSIS sections with `[synopsis]` markup, wrapping commands and options in backticks, standardizing placeholders as `_<placeholder>_`, and normalizing cross-references. The changes are purely presentational, totaling about 200 lines modified across the four commands. This series represents another incremental step in the documentation standardization initiative that has been consistently uncontroversial, with reviewers primarily checking for correct style application rather than evaluating functional changes. The work is now complete and ready for integration.
