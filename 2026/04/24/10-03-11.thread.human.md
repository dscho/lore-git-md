Harald Nordgren's `--track=fetch` feature for `git checkout`/`git switch` appears ready for final consideration after ten iterations of refinement. The latest version (v10) cleanly rebases on master and incorporates all technical feedback from Junio C Hamano, including a control flow simplification that moves the ref existence check into `resolve_fetch_target()`. The implementation now has comprehensive test coverage for hierarchical remote names and various fetch scenarios.

While Junio initially raised philosophical objections about encouraging implicit fetches rather than explicit `fetch`+`rebase` workflows, he hasn't reiterated those concerns since v8. His silence on the technically polished v9 and v10 may indicate quiet acceptance, though the maintainer's final decision remains pending. The patch series stands as a well-executed solution to a common workflow pain point, with no outstanding technical issues.
