This thread is about a proposed `--track=fetch` feature for `git checkout` and `git switch`, which would automatically fetch from the remote when creating a tracking branch—a workflow improvement to eliminate the manual fetch step. The series is now in its 14th iteration, with all technical work complete and approved.

Harald Nordgren implemented the feature as a two-part series: the first patch refactored `branch.c` to expose tracking remote lookup functions, while the second (this email) adds the user-facing `--track=fetch` option. The implementation handles edge cases like ambiguous refspecs, offline failures, and hierarchical remote names, and includes comprehensive test coverage. Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood previously approved the technical design, but Junio has consistently expressed reservations about the feature’s workflow value—specifically, concerns about automatic network operations during checkout without user preview.

The series is now technically ready for merging, but its fate hinges on Junio’s final assessment of whether the feature’s benefits outweigh his concerns. Harald has acknowledged the maintainer’s authority to reject the feature and reflected on the review process, noting that technical feedback was given conditionally on feature acceptance. He has also reached out to Phillip Wood and D. Ben Knoble for their perspectives on the feature’s necessity, referencing their earlier observations. No new input has emerged yet, leaving the decision in Junio’s hands.
