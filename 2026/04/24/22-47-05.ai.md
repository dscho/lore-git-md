This is a follow-up discussion from the maintainer (Junio C Hamano) on the hierarchical aliases regression fix. While the technical solution in the patch has already been vetted by Jeff King and René Scharfe, Junio raises a broader design concern about whether the fix might limit future extensibility of the alias system.

Junio questions whether the patch's approach - falling back to treating dotted names as simple aliases when the final key isn't "command" - closes off the possibility of adding other alias attributes like help text in the future. He suggests that if this is the case, perhaps the original three-level alias syntax (introduced in commit ac1f12a9de) was a misstep, and a separate "nalias" configuration might have been a cleaner approach from the start.

This isn't a rejection of the patch, but rather probing whether the solution might have unintended consequences for future alias system evolution. The concern stems from wanting to maintain flexibility for potential features like alias help text, which would naturally fit the subsection syntax but might now be precluded by the compatibility fix.

The thread context shows this is the first maintainer-level feedback on the patch, coming after technical reviews from other contributors. Junio's comment suggests the patch may need to consider not just the immediate regression but also the long-term design implications for the alias subsystem.
