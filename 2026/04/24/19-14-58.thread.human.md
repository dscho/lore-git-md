The thread concerns Scott Bauersfeld's patch to improve I/O performance in `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` by increasing the input buffer from 4 KiB to 128 KiB. Reducing write system call overhead is particularly beneficial on FUSE filesystems. Junio C Hamano has now confirmed that the v4 patch has been merged into `next`, effectively closing the topic.

The series reached consensus through four iterations. Jeff King provided analysis of the network protocol layer, explaining why gains are bounded by `git-fetch`'s 64 KB sideband demuxer packets.
