This is a follow-up in the hierarchical aliases regression thread, where Git 2.54.0 broke documented functionality for dot-separated command aliases like `pull.sub`. Jonatan Holmgren (the author of the original change that introduced the regression) acknowledges the issue and confirms that the three-level alias syntax was previously accidentally supported and is now explicitly implemented with a new syntax.

Holmgren states they will send a patch to fix the regression, though notes it will take time to go through the release cycle. This confirms the technical analysis from Jeff King and René Scharfe that identified the root cause in Holmgren's commit ac1f12a9de. The response is brief but significant as it comes from the original author of the change and indicates forthcoming action to restore compatibility.

The email doesn't propose specific technical solutions, but its acknowledgment suggests the fix will likely follow the consensus approach discussed in the thread: preserving backward compatibility for existing hierarchical aliases while maintaining the new three-level syntax for future use. This keeps the thread moving toward resolution while users can employ the temporary workarounds (`alias.pull.sub.command` syntax) in the meantime.
