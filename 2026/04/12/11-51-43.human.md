This is part 2 of a 4-patch series implementing autostash behavior for `git checkout -m`, now in its 10th iteration and ready for merging. The series has been thoroughly reviewed by Phillip Wood and approved by Junio Hamano, with Jeff King weighing in on the final naming convention.

This particular patch adds silent autostash creation capability to the sequencer. It introduces a new `create_autostash_ref_silent()` function that suppresses the "Created autostash" message, while maintaining the existing behavior through the original functions. The change is straightforward - just adding a `silent` parameter to the internal implementation and exposing it through a new API. 

The silent variant will be used by `git checkout -m` to avoid cluttering output during branch switching operations. The patch is well-contained, touching only the sequencer implementation and its header file, with no user-visible changes beyond the new internal capability. It's a clean addition that follows the established pattern while enabling the more polished user experience the series aims for.
