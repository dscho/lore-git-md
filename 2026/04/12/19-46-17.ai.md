Feature patch discussing progress reporting interactions in `git backfill`.

Derrick Stolee raises important considerations about how the proposed `--[no-]progress` option for `git backfill` should interact with the underlying `git fetch` progress indicators. He notes that the current patch doesn't address whether backfill's progress meter should coordinate with or suppress fetch's own progress output, particularly when `--no-progress` is specified.

Stolee suggests two potential approaches: either unconditionally passing `--quiet` to fetch (which might be too restrictive) or introducing a separate `--[no-]verbose` flag to control fetch output independently. The latter would allow finer-grained control but raises questions about how to handle cases where both progress and verbose modes are active simultaneously. This feedback indicates the patch needs refinement to properly consider the command's overall output behavior rather than just adding isolated progress reporting.
