Justin Tobler responds to Jeff King's concerns by agreeing that the streaming path should be preserved rather than worked around. This approach would maintain the streaming behavior Jeff wants to preserve while avoiding the segfault from trying to use transactions without an ODB.

The proposed solution comes from Justin's ongoing work on ODB transactions, suggesting this could be extracted as a standalone fix if the approach is acceptable. This moves the discussion forward from identifying problems to evaluating a concrete implementation strategy, though the thread still awaits consensus on whether this is the right direction before proceeding.
