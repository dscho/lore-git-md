This is a follow-up discussion on a bugfix patch for `git stash`'s argument parsing. The patch (now in its third iteration) makes `git stash` automatically assume the "push" subcommand when unambiguous push-specific flags are present, rather than requiring explicit "push" or `--` before pathspecs.

Mirko Faina points out a subtle oversight in the current implementation: while most of the flags being handled are indeed push-specific, `--include-untracked` is also accepted by the `show` subcommand. They note that `--keep-index` is technically accepted by the deprecated `save` subcommand, but consider this less of an issue since `save` is rarely used.

This observation suggests the current implementation might be slightly overzealous in its assumptions. The thread had previously reached a consensus that the patch was merge-ready, but this new detail may require revisiting the logic to ensure we don't accidentally change behavior for valid `stash show --include-untracked` invocations. The patch author and other participants will need to weigh in on whether this edge case warrants adjustment to the proposed solution.
