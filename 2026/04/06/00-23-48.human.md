This is the third version of Lorenzo Pegorari's GSoC patch series addressing promisor file handling during repacks, now expanded to 5 patches. The series aims to solve the `NEEDSWORK` comment from commit 5374a290 by properly preserving ref-to-packfile mappings in `.promisor` files during repacks.

The v3 series introduces significant changes from v2, including:
1. A new "promisor ignorelist" to handle excluded packs (particularly important for geometric repacks)
2. Timestamp preservation in copied promisor content to maintain debugging information
3. More precise copying of lines to the correct new promisor files based on object presence
4. Expanded test coverage in both regular and geometric repack scenarios

The author notes the changes are substantial enough that they considered starting a new series. Performance impact appears minimal (~0.5% overhead) based on testing with large repositories. The implementation now better handles edge cases around partial repacks while maintaining backward compatibility.

This continues to be a well-motivated series addressing a real gap in promisor pack handling, with clear responsiveness to maintainer feedback. The addition of geometric repack testing in t7703 is particularly valuable given Junio's earlier concerns about that interaction. The series appears ready for review, though the header inclusion question about strmap.h and related headers may generate some discussion.
