This thread addresses a documentation correction for the new `git history` command, specifically its `--update-refs` option. René Scharfe noticed and fixed two inconsistencies in the help text across the command's `reword` and `split` subcommands. The patch removes an incorrect "print" option from the argument placeholder (only "branches" and "head" are valid) and standardizes the formatting between both subcommands. As with most of René's contributions, this is a small but meaningful improvement to code consistency and accuracy, though it doesn't change any runtime behavior. The change appears uncontroversial and well-justified.
