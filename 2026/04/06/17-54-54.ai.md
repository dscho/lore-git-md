This is a feature patch in the fsmonitor Linux implementation series, adding a new `close_fd_above_stderr` option to `struct child_process`. The patch ensures child processes don't inherit unwanted file descriptors from their parent by closing FDs 3 and above (while preserving the child-notifier pipe) between fork and exec.

The implementation caps the FD scan at either `sysconf(_SC_OPEN_MAX)` or 4096 (whichever is smaller) for safety. This is particularly useful for test harnesses where stray FDs could cause problems. The change is minimal - just 12 lines added to `run-command.c` and 9 lines documenting the new flag in `run-command.h`.
