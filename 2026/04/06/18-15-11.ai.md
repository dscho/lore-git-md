This is a follow-up discussion on a bugfix patch for `git stash`'s argument parsing. The patch (now in its third iteration) makes `git stash` automatically assume the "push" subcommand when unambiguous push-specific flags are present, rather than requiring explicit "push" or `--` before pathspecs.

Mirko Faina points out a potential oversight in the implementation: while most of the flags being handled are indeed push-specific, `--include-untracked` is also accepted by the "show" subcommand, meaning it's not uniquely a push indicator. They note that `--keep-index` is technically accepted by the deprecated "save" subcommand, but consider this less concerning since "save" is obsolete.

This observation suggests the current implementation might be slightly overzealous in its assumptions. The thread had previously reached consensus that the series was merge-ready, but this new detail about `--include-untracked` could warrant either a documentation clarification or a small adjustment to the logic. The comment invites other maintainers to weigh in on whether this edge case matters enough to require changes before merging.

The technical context remains focused on `builtin/stash.c`'s `force_assume` logic, with thorough test coverage in `t/t3903-stash.sh`. The core quality-of-life improvement - reducing command-line verbosity for common `git stash` workflows - remains valid regardless of how this edge case is resolved.
