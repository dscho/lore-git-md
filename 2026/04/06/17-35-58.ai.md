Maintainer feedback on `git backfill` progress reporting.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on Trieu Huynh's patch to add progress reporting to `git backfill`, agreeing with Derrick Stolee's earlier review points while adding his own stylistic observations. The discussion is converging on two key technical issues: whether to track batches or objects in the progress meter, and whether to use pre-increment operators in void contexts.

Junio confirms Stolee's concern about the current implementation tracking batches rather than objects, noting this may not provide the most useful feedback. He also reinforces the stylistic point about operator usage, observing that the patch's pre-increment (`++ctx->batches_requested`) violates Git's conventional use of post-increment and pre-decrement in void contexts.

The maintainer's brief response signals general agreement with the review direction while adding weight to the specific concerns about metrics and coding style. This suggests the patch will need revision to:
1. Switch from batch counting to object counting for progress reporting
2. Adjust the increment operator to follow project conventions
3. Likely address the output verbosity questions raised in earlier messages

No new technical concerns are introduced, but Junio's endorsement of Stolee's points makes clear these issues should be resolved in the next iteration. The thread remains focused on refining the implementation details rather than questioning the feature's overall value.
