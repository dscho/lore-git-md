This is a memory leak fix in the fsmonitor daemon implementation, part of the v13 series bringing Linux fsmonitor support to parity with Windows/macOS. The patch addresses a resource cleanup oversight where cookie names stored in a hashmap were not being freed during daemon shutdown.

The fix adds proper cleanup of the `state.cookies` hashmap in the daemon's shutdown path, iterating through entries to free individual cookie names (allocated via strbuf_detach) before clearing the hashmap itself. This follows established patterns for resource management in the codebase.

The change demonstrates careful attention to detail in the fsmonitor implementation, ensuring all allocated resources are properly released. As part of a mature patch series that has undergone 13 iterations, this refinement shows the final polishing touches being applied to an otherwise production-ready feature. The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial, addressing a clear oversight without introducing new complexity.
