This is a follow-up discussion in the rejected `git switch` detach behavior thread. Kristoffer Haugsbakk weighs in with a nuanced perspective on the `git checkout` vs. `git switch` debate, acknowledging both sides of the argument while the thread remains effectively closed after Junio's authoritative rejection.

Haugsbakk notes their personal preference for `git checkout` due to its detach behavior, expressing puzzlement at those who criticize its use in examples as "obsolete." However, they also recognize the official stance from Git's BreakingChanges document, which labels `git checkout` as "superseded" by `git switch` and `git restore` while acknowledging its continued widespread use.

The email serves more as commentary than a reopening of the discussion, as it doesn't propose any technical changes or directly challenge Junio's decision. It highlights the tension between Git's official guidance (phasing out `git checkout`) and real-world usage patterns, but does so in a way that accepts the maintainer's position while sharing a user perspective. The thread remains closed with no indication of further development on the configurable detach proposal.
