David Lin's patch fixes a subtle but important bug in Git's cache-tree validation logic, where an accidental inversion during a refactor caused missing tree objects to be incorrectly marked as valid. The regression was introduced in commit 062b914c84 when switching from `repo_has_object_file()` to `has_object()`, dropping a crucial negation operator. This particularly affected partial clone scenarios where proper validation of missing objects is essential.

The fix went through thorough review, with Derrick Stolee confirming the root cause analysis and Patrick Steinhardt later verifying the correctness of the solution. The v2 patch incorporated all feedback, including test improvements (using `test_grep !` instead of raw shell negation) and commit message clarifications requested by Junio Hamano. While the immediate issue is resolved, Junio noted an open question about the broader implications for index-to-tree consistency when cache-tree validation fails - a potential area for future investigation. With consensus from multiple experienced contributors and no outstanding objections, the patch appears ready for merging.
