This is a bugfix patch in the fsmonitor series, specifically addressing a potential hang condition in the daemon's cookie wait mechanism. The patch replaces an infinite `pthread_cond_wait()` with a one-second timeout using `pthread_cond_timedwait()` to prevent indefinite blocking when filesystem events fail to arrive.

The change handles edge cases where inotify watches succeed but events never get delivered (common in certain container/overlay filesystem configurations). When the timeout triggers, the daemon now reports an error and allows the client to proceed with a full scan rather than hanging forever. The implementation includes careful time conversion from `gettimeofday()` to `timespec` and proper ETIMEDOUT handling.

This patch builds on the extensive fsmonitor work that has gone through 13 iterations, with the series now addressing all major review feedback. The change demonstrates thoughtful consideration of real-world deployment scenarios where filesystem behavior may not match expectations. The timeout value (1 second) appears well-chosen - long enough to avoid false positives under normal operation but short enough to prevent noticeable user-facing delays when something goes wrong.
