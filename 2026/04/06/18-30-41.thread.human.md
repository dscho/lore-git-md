The discussion has narrowed to the test verification method, which uses grep patterns to check trace output for the `--quiet` flag. Huynh confirmed the grep syntax is correct, eliminating one potential cause of the discrepancy.

The conversation remains focused on making the test more robust across different environments rather than questioning the underlying fix.
