Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, strongly opposes this proposed change to make `git switch`'s behavior configurable to match `git checkout` when given a commit hash. He argues that the deliberate design choice to remove implicit detach behavior from `git switch` was intentional - the command was created specifically to provide a simpler, more focused alternative to `git checkout` that only switches branches. Making this behavior configurable would undermine that design goal.

Junio notes that users who want the old `git checkout` behavior (which automatically detaches when given a commit hash) can simply continue using `git checkout`, as it remains available and isn't going away. His position is that configurability here would make `git switch` more complex and less useful as a teaching tool, defeating its original purpose.

This appears to be a decisive rejection of the proposal from the maintainer's perspective. While the patch itself was responsive to earlier technical feedback (as seen in the thread summary), Junio's objection is philosophical rather than technical - he sees this as going against the fundamental design principles behind `git switch`. Given his role as maintainer, this likely means the proposal won't move forward in its current form unless there's new arguments that can change his perspective on the design tradeoffs.
