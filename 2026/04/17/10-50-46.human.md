This is the final version of Patrick Steinhardt's test infrastructure series to enable `set -e` (errexit) in Git's test suite for Bash 5+. The series has undergone multiple rounds of review from key contributors like Jeff King, SZEDER Gábor, and Junio Hamano, with all feedback addressed in this v4 iteration.

The most notable change in this version is a simplification in t9902-completion.sh where a `read -d ""` heredoc pattern was replaced with a clearer multi-line variable assignment, following Junio and SZEDER Gábor's suggestion. This change improves readability independent of the `set -e` changes.

The series is now in excellent shape, having passed all CI checks and received thorough technical review. It follows the careful pattern of first preparing all test infrastructure (patches 1-11) before actually enabling `set -e` in the final patch. The preparatory work includes fixes for signal handling, test helper functions, and individual test cases that would break with strict error checking.

This represents a significant step forward in test robustness by ensuring errors outside test assertions are caught rather than silently ignored. The changes are mechanical but important, and the careful multi-stage approach minimizes risk. With Junio's engagement on the final polishing, this appears ready for merging in the next cycle.
