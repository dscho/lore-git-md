This is a brief follow-up from Junio C Hamano (the Git maintainer) in the ongoing discussion about Patrick Steinhardt's proposal to reorganize Git's source tree by moving libgit.a components into a dedicated "lib/" directory.

Junio expresses support for the general direction of thinning out the top-level directory, noting a personal motivation: it would restore convenient tab completion for `builtin` files that currently conflicts with `build.rs`. His comment suggests he's experienced this as a minor but persistent annoyance in his workflow.

While not a deep technical review, Junio's response carries weight as the maintainer's indication that he's on board with the structural change. His practical concern about tab completion offers a concrete example of how the current flat structure creates friction, reinforcing the RFC's broader discoverability argument. The tone is characteristically pragmatic - focusing on a real workflow pain point rather than abstract organization principles.

This moves the thread closer to consensus, with both brian m. carlson and now the maintainer expressing support for the reorganization's goals.
