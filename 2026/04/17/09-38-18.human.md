This realization leads him to reconsider Junio Hamano's alternative approach of using a single bare submodule in `$GIT_DIR/modules/` shared across worktrees.

The discussion continues to weigh tradeoffs between three approaches: the original hardlink-based sharing, Junio's single-repository model, and Phillip's `commondir` proposal. Phillip's latest observation about GC behavior suggests Junio's approach may have an advantage in this area, though the full implications of each design still need evaluation.
