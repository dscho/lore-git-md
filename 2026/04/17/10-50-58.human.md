This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to enable `set -e` (errexit) in Git's test suite for Bash 5+. The patch activates strict error checking, ensuring that any test failures outside explicit assertions will cause immediate test suite termination rather than being silently ignored.

The change is carefully scoped to Bash 5+ due to inconsistent behavior in other shells and older Bash versions (particularly on macOS). The patch includes a CI configuration update to ensure at least one job runs with the new behavior. This follows 11 preparatory patches that systematically fixed test patterns and helper functions to work correctly under strict error checking.

The series has undergone extensive review from Jeff King, SZEDER Gábor, and Junio Hamano, with CI validation confirming Bash 5+ compatibility. This represents a significant improvement in test robustness by catching previously ignored errors, though the Bash version constraint means the protection won't apply universally. The careful, well-reviewed approach suggests this is ready for merging.
