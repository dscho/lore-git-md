This is a review of a bisect terminology patch, where Junio Hamano raises concerns about localization and grammar when using custom bisect terms (like "dog"/"cat" instead of "good"/"bad"). 

Junio notes that while the patch correctly replaces hardcoded "good"/"bad" with the configured terms, there are potential issues when these terms are used in translated messages. The current implementation assumes the custom terms will work grammatically as adjectives modifying "commit" or "revision", which may not hold true for all languages or whimsical term choices. He suggests adding quotes around the terms to clarify they're special markers rather than natural adjectives, though acknowledges some phrasing would become awkward.

The review focuses on several specific message updates in the patch where the terms appear unquoted in error and status messages. Junio doesn't request changes outright but highlights the tension between natural phrasing and unambiguous localization - a thoughtful consideration for edge cases rather than a blocking issue.
