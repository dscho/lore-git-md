This is a substantive review from Elijah Newren on Patrick Steinhardt's RFC proposal to reorganize Git's source tree by moving libgit.a components into a "lib/" directory. The review focuses on one specific aspect of the large-scale change.

Elijah expresses general support for the reorganization, agreeing that the change improves code discoverability by making the libgit.a boundary more visible and reducing root directory clutter. His only reservation is about moving the sha1collisiondetection submodule into the new lib/ directory, noting that submodule renames can sometimes be problematic. However, he quickly qualifies this concern by pointing out that the submodule is rarely updated, making this a minor practical issue rather than a structural objection.

The review reinforces the patch's core motivation while providing a small but technically informed caveat about submodule handling. Elijah's status as a long-time contributor with deep merge expertise lends weight to his assessment that the change is fundamentally sound. His feedback suggests the proposal is likely to proceed with only minor adjustments.
