This is a follow-up discussion about a proposed Coccinelle rule to enforce passing `struct strbuf` by pointer rather than by value in Git's codebase. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Deveshi Dwivedi's RFC patch, praising the improved approach over his earlier suggestion.

The key points:
- Jeff confirms the new rule is better than his initial proposal, as it catches the root issue (function signatures) rather than just call sites
- He notes the rule's use of "..." syntax is more robust, allowing it to catch cases like `save_untracked_files()` that his version missed
- The only minor concern is whether the automated patch output might confuse contributors by not including rationale, but he considers this an acceptable tradeoff

This appears to be converging toward acceptance - Jeff's feedback is positive and focuses only on minor presentation considerations rather than technical objections. The discussion shows good alignment between the contributors on both the importance of the convention and the implementation approach.
