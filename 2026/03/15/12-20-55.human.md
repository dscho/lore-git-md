This is a Google Summer of Code (GSoC) proposal draft focused on improving Git's advice messages. Arsh Srivastava, who recently completed a microproject related to branch switch failure messages, is proposing to refine advice messages across several common Git commands (checkout, push, merge, rebase) to enhance user experience while maintaining Git's preference for concise output.

The proposal comes from someone who has already demonstrated familiarity with Git's advice system through a previous contribution (unpack-trees.c modifications) and review process. The goal is not to introduce new mechanisms but to improve clarity of existing messages, particularly focusing on terminal usage scenarios.

The email includes an attached PDF with the full proposal draft and requests feedback on whether this direction would be useful to the project. This appears to be an early-stage proposal seeking community input before formal submission to GSoC. The author shows awareness of Git's design constraints around script-safety and output brevity while aiming to make the messages more helpful for interactive use.
