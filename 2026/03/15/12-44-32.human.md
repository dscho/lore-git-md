This is a Google Summer of Code (GSoC) proposal from Arsh Srivastava, who previously contributed a microproject improving branch switch failure messages. The proposal focuses on refining Git's advice messages across several commands (checkout, push, merge, rebase) to make them clearer while maintaining Git's characteristic conciseness and script-safety.

The proposal demonstrates thoughtful planning with a 12-week timeline that accounts for review cycles (including Hofstadter's Law buffer time) and focuses on incremental improvements rather than new mechanisms. It targets 12-15 specific scenarios where message clarity could be improved, with accompanying test and documentation updates. The scope appears well-considered for a GSoC project - focused enough to be achievable but impactful enough to matter.

Arsh shows familiarity with Git's advice system (advice.c) and the project's review process, having already contributed one patch. The proposal follows up on Pushkar Singh's suggestion to share the draft in plain text rather than PDF, demonstrating responsiveness to Git's mailing list norms. This looks like a promising candidate for GSoC - the work is clearly defined, appropriately scoped, and builds on existing infrastructure without introducing new complexity.
