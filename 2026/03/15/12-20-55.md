I am Arsh and I recently completed my microproject submission
related to improving advice messages when a branch switch fails
due to local changes.

Patch discussion:
https://github.com/git/git/pull/2233

Through this contribution, I became familiar with Git's advice
system, the relevant code paths in unpack-trees.c, and the
associated tests. The review discussion also helped me
understand the importance of keeping advice messages concise
while guiding users toward safe workflows.

Based on that experience, I am planning a GSoC proposal focused
on refining advice messages in several common scenarios across
Git commands (such as checkout, push, merge, and rebase). So that
I can preserve the user understanding while enhancing the UX.

The goal is not to introduce new mechanisms, but to improve
clarity of existing advice messages, while not only
preserving Git's preference for concise output and script-safe
behavior but also making the UI more user-friendly for the terminal
usage.

A rough draft of the proposal is attached below.

I would greatly appreciate any feedback on whether this direction
would be useful.

Thank you for investing time in reading my draft and proposal.
