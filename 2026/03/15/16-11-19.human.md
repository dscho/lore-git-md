This is a brief exchange between Junio Hamano and Johannes Sixt about the standardization of PO file filters in Git's localization workflow. The discussion stems from Jiang Xin's patch series introducing AI-assisted translation tools, which included two different filter types for stripping location information from PO files.

Junio agrees with Johannes' concern that the current split between `gettext-no-file-no-location` and `gettext-no-location` filters appears arbitrary rather than language-specific. He suggests the choice should be based on translator preference rather than tied to particular languages, and that the project would benefit from converging on a single standard approach. Junio notes the current comments implying language-specific requirements are misleading if the distinction is actually just about individual translator preferences.

This is a coordination question rather than a technical issue with the patches themselves. The exchange highlights how changes to core infrastructure can create ripple effects for related projects (like Gitk) that share similar translation needs. The maintainer's intervention suggests this may lead to further refinement of the filter standardization before the series is finalized.
