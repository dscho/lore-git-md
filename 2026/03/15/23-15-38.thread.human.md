This thread documents a successful bugfix contribution by GSoC participant Jialong Wang, addressing a long-standing issue in `git apply`'s error reporting. The problem - that error messages didn't indicate whether corrupt patches came from files or stdin - had gone unnoticed for 21 years because `git apply` is rarely used with multiple patches (unlike `git am`). 

Wang's solution cleanly added source context to error messages while maintaining backward compatibility, with changes focused in `apply.c`. The patch went through three iterations, with reviewer Karthik Nayak providing helpful feedback and Wang promptly addressing all concerns. The final version included proper line counter management between patches and expanded test coverage. Junio merged the series after confirming correctness, noting it revealed a historical oversight while serving as a model first contribution.

The thread concludes with Junio's confirmation that remaining potential improvements (in `find_header()` and `parse_git_diff_header()`) can be handled separately. His appreciative closing note highlights how Wang's responsive, thorough approach made this a textbook example of a successful GSoC contribution cycle - from problem identification through to maintainer approval.
