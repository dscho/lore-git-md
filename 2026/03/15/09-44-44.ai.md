Refactoring patch fixing the last remaining case of passing `struct strbuf` by value in the codebase, following up on Jeff King's earlier identification of this anti-pattern.

This second patch in the series addresses `save_untracked_files()` in `builtin/stash.c`, which was taking a `strbuf` parameter by value. The change converts it to take a pointer instead, matching the project's convention. The patch updates both the function signature and its single caller in `do_create_stash()`, plus fixes the internal usage to dereference the pointer when accessing the buffer and length.

The motivation remains the same as in the first patch - passing `strbuf` by value risks dangling pointers if the callee reallocates the buffer, since the caller and callee would be operating on different copies of the struct. The fix is straightforward and mechanical, exactly the kind of cleanup the Coccinelle rule from patch 1 was designed to catch. 

With this change, the codebase should now be free of this particular anti-pattern, and the new Coccinelle rule will help prevent regressions. The patch appears well-executed - it's a simple change that follows the project's conventions and doesn't introduce any new complexity. Since this was the only remaining case found by the Coccinelle rule, this likely concludes the series unless review uncovers additional instances.
