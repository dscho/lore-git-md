This is a GSoC (Google Summer of Code) proposal from Arsh Srivastava to improve Git's advice messages, following up on Pushkar Singh's suggestion to share the proposal in plain text rather than as a PDF attachment. The proposal builds on Arsh's prior microproject contribution to Git (PR #2233) and focuses on refining advice messages for common error scenarios in commands like checkout, push, merge, and rebase.

The proposal demonstrates a good understanding of Git's constraints - aiming to improve clarity while maintaining the project's preference for concise, script-safe output. It outlines a 12-week timeline with clear deliverables: auditing existing messages, identifying 12-15 scenarios for improvement, submitting patch series, and updating tests. The scope is appropriately narrow, focusing on wording refinements rather than architectural changes to the advice system itself.

Arsh's background shows relevant open source experience (including mentoring in SWoC and contributions to other projects) and familiarity with Git's development process. The proposal follows Git's incremental improvement approach, targeting specific pain points in user experience without overcommitting to broad changes. This looks like a well-scoped GSoC project that aligns with Git's development style and could produce mergeable improvements to the advice system.
