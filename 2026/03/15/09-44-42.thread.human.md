Deveshi Dwivedi has successfully concluded a small but important refactoring effort to eliminate the anti-pattern of passing `struct strbuf` by value rather than by pointer. This follows up on Jeff King's earlier identification of this issue, which can lead to inefficiency and subtle bugs when callers and callees operate on different copies of the buffer.

The two-patch series introduces a Coccinelle semantic rule to detect such cases and fixes the one remaining instance found in `builtin/stash.c`, converting `show_stash()` to take a pointer. Junio Hamano has reviewed and accepted the series, noting its technical merit and clean implementation. 

With only two cases found in the codebase (one already fixed by Peff, the other now addressed here), this demonstrates good project hygiene around `strbuf` usage. The new Coccinelle rule will serve as an ongoing safeguard against this pattern creeping back in. This marks a tidy conclusion to a focused code quality improvement.
