This is a final discussion thread about the cover letter formatting feature, specifically debating whether format names should require a "log:" prefix. The series is otherwise complete and merged to 'next'.

Phillip Wood weighs in on the "log:" prefix debate, arguing against Junio Hamano's insistence on keeping it. He points out that Git doesn't require such prefixes elsewhere and suggests an alternative approach: rejecting format strings that don't contain '%' placeholders and don't match known format names like "shortlog". This would catch typos while avoiding the awkward prefix requirement.

The exchange shows the project's tension between interface design principles - Junio prioritizes future-proofing and error detection, while Phillip advocates for consistency with existing conventions. Phillip's technical counterproposal offers a middle ground, though Junio's position as maintainer likely carries more weight in the final decision.

This appears to be the last open question on an otherwise polished feature that has gone through eight iterations. The discussion is now focused on this one remaining design point rather than any implementation concerns.
