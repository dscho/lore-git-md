Taylor Blau has submitted the third version of his patch series improving MIDX (multi-pack-index) repacking performance with bitmaps, now ready for merging into the 'next' branch. This 17-patch series builds on his previous work in this area, introducing optimizations that enable reachability bitmaps during MIDX compaction and implementing incremental repacking improvements. The changes include preparatory refactoring of the MIDX writing code to make it more maintainable, with new helper functions and cleaner pack ordering logic.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed an earlier version and identified several minor issues, all of which have been addressed in this iteration. The changes are focused on the MIDX subsystem and shouldn't affect user-visible behavior beyond the expected performance gains, particularly for repositories with many packfiles. This work continues Taylor's long-standing focus on improving Git's packfile handling, following his earlier contributions to MIDX and geometric repacking.

Patrick Steinhardt's ODB counting series has been confirmed ready for 'next', representing another step in the object database abstraction effort.
