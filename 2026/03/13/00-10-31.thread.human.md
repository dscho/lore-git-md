Taylor Blau's MIDX repacking optimization series is now cleared for merging into Git's development branch ('next') after receiving final approval from Jeff King (Peff). The 17-patch series improves performance of multi-pack-index repacking by enabling reachability bitmaps during compaction, building on Taylor's ongoing work to optimize Git's handling of repositories with many packfiles. 

Peff had reviewed version 2 of the series and identified minor issues with documentation and interfaces, all of which Taylor addressed in version 3. The changes are contained to the MIDX subsystem, introducing internal helpers like `midx_pack_perm()` while maintaining backward compatibility. With Peff's confirmation that no further changes are needed, the series is now ready for Junio to merge as part of the next development cycle.

This work continues Git's steady progress on large-repository performance optimizations, particularly for the packfile and MIDX subsystems that Taylor specializes in. The changes should provide measurable speed improvements for repositories with complex packfile histories, though end users won't see any behavior changes beyond faster operations.
