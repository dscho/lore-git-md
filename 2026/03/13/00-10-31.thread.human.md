Taylor Blau's MIDX repacking optimization series has completed its review cycle and is now approved for merging into Git's development branch. The 17-patch series, now in its third iteration, improves how Git handles repositories with many packfiles by enabling reachability bitmaps during multi-pack-index compaction. Jeff King (Peff) thoroughly reviewed the changes between versions, identifying and helping address minor interface and documentation issues that have now been resolved in v3.

The changes focus on the MIDX subsystem, introducing internal helpers like `midx_pack_perm()` while maintaining backward compatibility. With all feedback incorporated and no outstanding concerns, Junio C Hamano has confirmed the series is ready for integration. This represents another step in Taylor's ongoing work to optimize Git's performance for large repositories, building on his previous contributions to geometric repacking and MIDX handling. The series includes comprehensive test updates and documentation improvements alongside the core functionality changes.
