This is a coding style patch (1/2 in a series) from Kristoffer Haugsbakk that prepares for adding `--format` support to `git name-rev`. The change is purely mechanical - it wraps single-line conditional blocks in braces to comply with Git's CodingGuidelines, which recommend consistency when some arms of a conditional require braces.

The patch touches two functions in `builtin/name-rev.c`:
1. `get_rev_name` - adds braces around both arms of an if/else
2. `name_rev_line` - adds braces around the first condition in a more complex if/else chain

This is a straightforward cleanup that makes the codebase more consistent before introducing new functionality in the next patch. The changes are minimal and clearly motivated by the documented style guidelines. Since this is just preparatory work, the real substance of the feature addition will come in patch 2/2.
