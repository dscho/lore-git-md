This is a follow-up discussion in the git-gui maintenance modernization thread, focusing on the behavior of the `gui.gcwarning` configuration option. Johannes Sixt (the git-gui maintainer) responds to Wolfgang Faust's exploration of edge cases where the new auto-maintenance behavior might surprise users.

The thread has already reached consensus that `gui.gcwarning=false` will comprehensively disable maintenance in git-gui, creating a slight divergence from CLI behavior where maintenance would still occur. Johannes reinforces this position, arguing against introducing a new `gui.gcmaintenance` option as proposed by Wolfgang. He maintains that the simpler approach - running maintenance on every commit unless `gui.gcwarning=false` - is sufficient, and that more complex solutions should wait until there's evidence they're needed.

Johannes notes that while Wolfgang's hypothetical edge cases (users who configured maintenance but never run it) are technically possible, they're unlikely in practice. He advocates for keeping the change straightforward rather than adding complexity to handle rare scenarios. The email serves to close the discussion by affirming the maintainer-approved direction while acknowledging Wolfgang's thoughtful analysis of the tradeoffs.
