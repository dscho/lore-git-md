Bugfix for promisor-remote subsystem addressing recursive lazy-fetch issue, now with packet traces and reproduction scripts.

Paul Tarjan provides packet traces and deterministic reproduction scripts that confirm the recursive lazy-fetch behavior in the promisor-remote subsystem. The traces show that even with noop negotiation, servers can legitimately send thin packs containing REF_DELTAs against filtered-out blob bases when blob:none filtering is active. This creates a situation where index-pack attempts to backfill missing bases, spawning new fetch processes in a runaway cycle.

The reproduction demonstrates two key scenarios: a depth-1 case showing the recursion mechanism, and an unbounded case that would grow until killed (simulating the original 276GB incident). The proposed fix (propagating GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH) correctly prevents both scenarios while still allowing proper delta resolution in legitimate cases Jeff King identified earlier in the thread.
