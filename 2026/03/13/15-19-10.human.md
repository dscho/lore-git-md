Maintainer follow-up on test modernization patch.

Junio Hamano points out one final shell portability issue in Siddharth Shrimali's v2 patch that modernizes the `delete_object` helper in the partial clone test script. The patch had already incorporated several rounds of feedback to make the test hash-independent and properly scoped, but Junio notes that `test-lint` would flag the unquoted `local` variable assignments as problematic.

This is a minor but important fix - the `local` declarations for `repo` and `obj` need their right-hand sides quoted to handle cases where the parameters contain spaces or special characters. Junio provides the exact diff needed to address this, showing the project's attention to detail even in test infrastructure. 

The patch having already addressed all substantive concerns from multiple reviewers (Jeff King, Eric Sunshine, and others), this appears to be the final tweak needed before merging. It demonstrates how Git's review process catches both architectural issues and subtle portability concerns, even in test code.
