This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v4 performance optimization series for `git-upload-pack`, which has already been approved for merging. The patch focuses on improving keepalive behavior during high-concurrency operations.

The change modifies how `upload-pack` handles keepalive packets when buffering data. Previously, keepalives were sent based purely on a fixed poll timeout, which could lead to delayed communication if data was being buffered. The new approach tracks the last time data was actually sent (`last_sent_ms`) and adjusts the keepalive timeout dynamically based on this, ensuring more responsive communication even during buffering periods.

The implementation adds timestamp tracking throughout the data relay process and modifies the poll timeout calculation to account for actual transmission times rather than using a fixed interval. This becomes particularly important as subsequent patches in the series will introduce more aggressive buffering strategies.

The patch is well-contained and clearly motivated, with thorough explanations of both the current behavior and the improved approach. It maintains all existing functionality while making the keepalive mechanism more robust. As part of an already-reviewed series, this represents a polished final version of the maintainer has approved for inclusion.
