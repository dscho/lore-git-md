This is a brief follow-up in the `the_repository` removal thread for the `mktree` command refactoring. Junio Hamano confirms Tian Yuchen's understanding that `parse_options()` intercepts the `-h` help flag before any repository access occurs, making the refactoring safe. The exchange reveals an interesting design point in Git's codebase - the tight coupling of help text handling with option parsing in `parse_options()`.

Junio pushes back on Tian's characterization of `-h` as being less "business logic" than other options, asserting that all flags are equally relevant. This doesn't impact the technical soundness of the patch, which remains ready for inclusion, but provides insight into Git's internal design philosophy. The discussion highlights how even simple refactorings can surface non-obvious patterns in the codebase that contributors need to understand.
