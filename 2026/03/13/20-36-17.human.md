This is a bugfix for bash completion, addressing a missing feature in the `git range-diff` command's tab completion. The patch notes that while `--diff-merges`, `--no-diff-merges`, and `--remerge-diff` options are available in the completion list for `git log` and `git show`, they are inexplicably omitted from `git range-diff`'s completion options despite being valid flags for that command.

The issue is clearly demonstrated by comparing the completion definitions - the options are present in `__git_log_show_options` but not included in `_git_range_diff`'s case statement. The patch appears straightforward and well-motivated, fixing what looks like an oversight in the completion script. The bug affects users who rely on tab completion when using these diff-related flags with `range-diff`.
