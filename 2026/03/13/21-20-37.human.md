This is a follow-up from the maintainer on a feature patch that adds a "preparing" phase to Git's reference-transaction hook. Junio C Hamano provides some minor editorial feedback on the patch while indicating he plans to queue it.

Junio notes the author's email addresses don't match between the From and Signed-off-by lines, suggesting they may want to standardize on the GitLab address if this is company-sponsored work. He also suggests adjusting the commit subject to follow Git's standard "<area>: <description>" format.

The maintainer praises the patch's clear commit message and motivation, particularly appreciating the explanation of why the existing hook phases were insufficient for distributed systems needing to coordinate writes before locks are taken. He questions one detail about how exactly the "rearrangement" of transactions would work in practice, but doesn't see this as blocking the change.

Junio confirms the implementation looks correct and the backwards compatibility considerations are reasonable, agreeing with the author's decision not to introduce a v2 hook interface. He concludes by stating he will queue the patch, indicating this change is likely to be accepted into Git's codebase. This is a positive signal that the well-motivated and carefully documented extension to the reference-transaction hook has maintainer approval.
