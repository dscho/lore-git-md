This is the first patch in a 3-part series by Phillip Wood continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting worktree-related functions. The patch focuses on `is_current_worktree()`, eliminating its dependency on the global `the_repository` variable by instead using the repository pointer stored in the worktree struct itself.

The change makes the function's behavior more consistent - a worktree will now always be considered "current" when its path matches its repository's worktree path, rather than depending on whether its repository happens to be `the_repository`. The patch also simplifies `get_worktree_from_repository()` by removing its call to `is_current_worktree()` since the worktree it creates will now always be marked as current.

The modifications touch core worktree handling code but appear to preserve existing functionality while making it more robust. The diff shows straightforward mechanical replacements of `the_repository` with `wt->repo` in several locations. This is a clean, focused change that advances the broader `the_repository` removal effort in the worktree subsystem.
