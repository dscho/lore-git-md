Kristoffer Haugsbakk's `git format-rev` series has reached its fourth version, introducing a new command for flexible revision formatting from stdin input. The five-patch series progressed methodically from preparatory style cleanup to final implementation, with clear separation between mechanical changes and functional work. The command offers two input modes: processing one revision per line/record (`--stdin-mode=revs`) and finding/formatting commits in freeform text (`--stdin-mode=text`), with robust stream handling including NUL-termination support and deadlock prevention.

Reviewers Phillip Wood and René Scharfe provided substantive feedback on stream safety and NUL-handling design, along with surface-level style suggestions, all of which were addressed. The series recently saw a final documentation refinement in v4, where Kristoffer simplified the man page's explanation of NUL-handling in text mode to better reflect actual use cases, focusing on `-z` for interoperability with other NUL-terminating commands rather than theoretical passthrough cases. This thoughtful post-submission tweak demonstrates the author's continued attention to documentation clarity even after the core implementation was complete. The command is marked EXPERIMENTAL to allow for future refinement based on real-world usage.
