This is the final version (v4) of Patrick Steinhardt's performance optimization series addressing lock contention in `git-upload-pack` during high-concurrency scenarios. The series has been approved by Junio Hamano for merging after thorough review and platform-specific testing by Johannes Sixt.

The patch series implements `writev()`-based buffering across multiple subsystems to significantly reduce write syscalls (from ~400k to ~44k when cloning the Linux repository). Key improvements include:

1. New `writev()` infrastructure with cross-platform support (POSIX/Windows/NonStop)
2. Optimizations in `upload-pack`, `pack-objects`, `sideband`, and `csum-file` subsystems
3. Improved keepalive handling that prioritizes data flushing
4. Buffer size adjustments to maximize throughput while maintaining framing

The changes are particularly impactful for high-traffic Git servers, where they reduce lock contention when hundreds of `upload-pack` processes are simultaneously writing data. The series has been validated across all target platforms and subsystems, with all open questions resolved in previous iterations. The v4 changes are minor cleanup (removing a stale half-sentence) as the technical implementation was already finalized in v3.
