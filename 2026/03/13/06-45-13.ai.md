This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v4 performance optimization series for `git-upload-pack`, focusing on improving keepalive behavior during high-concurrency operations. The patch modifies how keepalive packets are triggered when buffering data, moving from a fixed poll timeout to a dynamic system that tracks time since last data transmission.

The key change introduces `last_sent_ms` tracking in `create_pack_file()` to better handle cases where data is buffered but not yet sent. The patch ensures keepalives remain timely even when aggressive buffering might otherwise delay transmissions, particularly during the initial "PACK" signature buffering phase. A new `did_send_data` flag is added to `relay_pack_data()` to accurately track when data is actually transmitted to the client.

This change is particularly important as it prepares for more aggressive buffering optimizations in subsequent patches while maintaining reliable client connections. The implementation has been reviewed by Johannes Sixt for platform-specific edge cases and is now in its final approved form as part of the larger performance series.
