This is a maintainer ping from Junio C Hamano about the subtree recursion limit patch series by Colin Stagner. The series (now at v2) aims to fix `git subtree split` failures on Debian/Ubuntu systems where the default POSIX shell enforces a 1000-call recursion limit when processing deep history graphs.

Junio notes the series has seen two iterations without any review feedback, prompting him to ask whether this indicates lack of interest in the feature or simply that the patches are well-crafted. He acknowledges he doesn't personally use `git subtree` and seeks input from contributors who might have more about the change's impact before considering merging it.

The technical changes in the series (which restructure the split operation's rejoin-deepening algorithm to reduce recursion depth) appear sound but remain unvalidated by community review. Junio's message serves both to highlight the orphaned status of these patches and to solicit the needed review from subtree users or experts before proceeding. The ball is now in the court of those who actively work with `contrib/subtree` to either approve the changes or suggest improvements.
