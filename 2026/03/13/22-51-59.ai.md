This is a maintainer ping from Junio C Hamano about the subtree recursion limit patch series from Colin Stagner. The series (now at v2) aims to fix `git subtree split` failures on Debian/Ubuntu systems where shell recursion depth is artificially limited to 1000 calls. 

Junio notes the lack of review feedback after two iterations, questioning whether this indicates disinterest in the subtree feature itself or simply that the patches are well-crafted. He acknowledges his own limited use of `git subtree` and hesitates to merge unreviewed changes that might affect active users. The message serves as a direct call for review from contributors who actually work with the subtree command.

The technical content remains unchanged from v1 - the patches restructure the split operation's rejoin-deepening algorithm to process commit ranges in bulk rather than recursively. The changes are confined to `contrib/subtree/git-subtree.sh` and appear complete, but still lack performance measurements or confirmation of fixing the Debian/Ubuntu issue despite being tested on a large repository.

Junio's intervention suggests this series may stall without active review from subtree users. The ball is now in the court of contributors who depend on or maintain the subtree functionality to either approve the changes or request modifications before integration.
