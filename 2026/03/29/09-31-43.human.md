This is a review comment on a feature patch adding TypeScript support to Git's userdiff patterns. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) responds to Dhruv Arora's GSoC contribution, suggesting the pattern should cover both TypeScript and JavaScript since they're closely related languages. He recommends naming the driver after the superset language and adding documentation to clarify the dual support.

Hannes points out this isn't the first attempt at adding JavaScript/TypeScript userdiff patterns, linking to five previous submissions that weren't completed. He encourages Dhruv to review those threads for additional context and potential improvements. The comment indicates the technical approach is acceptable but could benefit from broader language coverage and documentation.

The patch appears to be in good shape technically but needs refinement in presentation (as noted by Junio earlier) and now potentially expanded scope based on this feedback. As a GSoC project, it's getting thorough review attention from experienced contributors.
