This is the final patch in a series removing `the_repository` usage from Git's refs subsystem, specifically targeting the packed backend. It's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort led by René Scharfe.

The patch makes a straightforward change to the packed refs backend, replacing a use of the global `the_repository` with an explicit repository reference from the `ref_store` structure. This completes the elimination of `the_repository` from this file, allowing the removal of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro. The change affects how the packed refs lock reads its timeout configuration, now properly using repository-specific settings.

This is a mechanical but important change that follows the pattern established in earlier patches of the series. It maintains the same behavior while making the repository dependency explicit. The patch is small (just 3 lines changed) and focused, exactly what we want to see in this kind of refactoring work. With this change, all three ref backends (files, reftable, and packed) have now been converted to avoid `the_repository`.

The series as a whole has been well-received, with previous feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and others incorporated in earlier versions. This final patch appears uncontroversial and ready for merging, completing one more step in the long-running effort to eliminate global state from Git's codebase.
