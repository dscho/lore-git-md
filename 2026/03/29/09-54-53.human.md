This is a follow-up in the ongoing effort to remove `the_repository` global variable usage from Git's refs subsystem. Shreyansh Paliwal responds to Tian Yuchen's review of patch 5/5 in their series, which addresses the packed refs backend.

The exchange highlights a known limitation in the series - the static caching of timeout values that affects multi-repository scenarios. Shreyansh acknowledges this as valid technical debt that will need future work through the `repo-settings` integration, but explains they're keeping this patch focused on the immediate goal of removing `the_repository` dependencies while preserving behavior.

The tone is collaborative, with Shreyansh noting they'll improve the patch title in the next version to better reflect the scope. This continues the pattern of careful, incremental progress that has characterized this thread, with multiple reviewers (Patrick Steinhardt, Burak Kaan Karaçay, and Tian Yuchen) providing constructive feedback on different aspects of the changes.

The series represents solid progress in the multi-year `the_repository` removal effort, with all three ref backends (files, reftable, packed) now nearly free of direct usage except for one remaining dependency in reftable. The discussion remains focused on technical merits while acknowledging the broader context of this architectural improvement.
