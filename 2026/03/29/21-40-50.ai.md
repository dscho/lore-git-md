This is a feature patch series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking, the third and final major component of Taylor Blau's multi-part effort to improve Git's repacking strategy for large repositories. The series builds on previous work that refactored repack.c and implemented MIDX layer compaction.

The core innovation here is a new repacking strategy that avoids periodic all-into-one repacks by maintaining an incrementally growing and shrinking MIDX chain. The approach:
1. Repacks non-MIDX'd packs geometrically
2. Prepares new MIDX layers with fresh packs
3. Compacts adjacent MIDX layers to maintain geometric progression
4. Writes a new optimized MIDX chain

This creates a structure where older layers contain fewer, larger packs while newer layers contain many smaller packs - a pattern that significantly reduces repacking overhead in large monorepos that repack frequently. The series includes preparatory refactors, new MIDX write options needed for the strategy, and the core implementation.

The patches show careful attention to edge cases (no-op writes, memory leaks) and include new test coverage. The work appears well-integrated with existing repack infrastructure and builds on established concepts like geometric repacking. This represents a major architectural improvement to Git's scalability for large repositories, completing a multi-year effort that Taylor has been presenting at conferences. The series is rebased on current master and ready for final review.
