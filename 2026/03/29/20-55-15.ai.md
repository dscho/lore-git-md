Maintainer confirmation of mailmap caching behavior in `git cat-file --batch-command`.

Junio C Hamano confirms his understanding of the mailmap caching behavior in Siddharth Asthana's dynamic mailmap toggling implementation. He notes that while he initially wondered about potential caching of rewritten commit objects, the actual implementation (both before and after this patch) re-reads and re-maps objects on each request rather than caching the mailmap-applied versions. This means there's no risk of "tainted data" persisting across mailmap toggles, as the system always regenerates the output from scratch.

The maintainer points out an existing inefficiency where `replace_idents_using_mailmap()` may be called twice per object - once for size calculation and once for content generation - but clarifies this is orthogonal to the mailmap toggling functionality being added. This observation doesn't require changes to the current patch series, as it documents pre-existing behavior rather than introducing new concerns. The thread appears to be reaching consensus on the technical implementation, with Junio's comments serving as a final verification of the design's soundness before potential merging.
