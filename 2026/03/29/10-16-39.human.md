This is part 1 of a 5-part series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort in the refs subsystem. Shreyansh Paliwal's patch converts branch name handling functions to explicitly take a `struct repository*` parameter rather than relying on the global `the_repository`.

The change makes several branch-related helper functions repository-aware by:
- Adding `struct repository*` parameters to `copy_branchname()`, `check_branch_ref()`, `validate_branchname()`, and `validate_new_branchname()`
- Propagating these changes through callers in builtin commands (branch, checkout, worktree)
- Maintaining existing behavior while removing implicit global state dependencies

The patch follows established patterns from René Scharfe's long-running effort to eliminate global variables. It's a mechanical but focused change that touches 9 files, mostly updating function signatures and call sites. The changes are straightforward and maintain compatibility since most builtin callers already operate on `the_repository` and can pass it directly.

This appears to be a well-structured incremental step in the larger refactoring effort. The patch builds on prior work (jw/object-name-bitset-to-enum) and follows review feedback from previous versions of the series.
