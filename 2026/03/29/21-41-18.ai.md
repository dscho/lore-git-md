This is part 11 of Taylor Blau's 16-patch series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The patch focuses on preparing the pack geometry logic for the new incremental repacking strategy by teaching `pack_geometry_init()` to handle MIDX layer constraints.

The key changes are:
1. When a `midx_layer_threshold` is set, the function now restricts repacking candidates to only packs in the tip MIDX layer, provided that layer contains enough packs (more than the threshold). Packs in older layers or in undersized tip layers are excluded.
2. The patch adds tracking of whether any tip-layer packs were included in the repack (`midx_tip_rewritten`), which will be used later to determine how to update the MIDX chain.
3. New fields are added to `struct pack_geometry` to store the threshold value and rewritten status.

This is a preparatory change that builds on the earlier refactoring work in the series, specifically the new `midx_layer_contains_pack()` helper from patch 8. The modifications are focused on `repack-geometry.c` and introduce the infrastructure needed for the incremental strategy while maintaining the existing geometric repacking behavior when no threshold is set. The patch includes clear comments explaining the new logic and edge cases.
