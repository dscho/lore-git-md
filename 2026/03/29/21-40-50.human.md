This is a cover letter for a 16-patch series introducing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking, the final major component in Taylor Blau's multi-part effort to improve Git's repacking strategy for large repositories. The series builds on previous work that refactored repack code and implemented MIDX layer compaction.

The key innovation here is a new repacking strategy that avoids periodic all-into-one repacks by maintaining an incrementally growing and shrinking MIDX chain. The approach:
1. Repacks non-MIDX'd packs geometrically
2. Writes new MIDX layers for fresh packs
3. Compacts adjacent MIDX layers to maintain geometric progression
4. Prunes redundant layers

This creates a MIDX chain where older layers have fewer, larger packs while newer layers have many smaller packs - a structure that significantly reduces repacking overhead in large monorepos. The series includes preparatory refactors, new MIDX write options needed for the strategy, and the core implementation.

The patches appear well-structured and build on established work, with thorough test coverage (including three new test files). This looks like a mature contribution from a domain expert (Taylor is a packfile/MIDX specialist and PLC member) that addresses a known pain point in large repositories. The cover letter helpfully situates this within the broader effort and notes potential follow-up work.
