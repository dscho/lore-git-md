Taylor Blau's 16-patch series on incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking continues to progress through review with strong consensus. The series introduces geometric repacking with reusable `strbuf` tracking and has addressed numerous implementation details across earlier patches, with Jeff King (Peff) providing thorough reviews that have shaped the design.

In the latest development, patch 13/16 fixes a memory leak in packfile revindex handling that had gone unnoticed in practice. Peff confirms the technical correctness of the fix while explaining why it wasn't previously detected - the main repository's object database is rarely freed, and submodules (which are freed more often) typically don't need revindexes. The fix adds proper cleanup in `close_pack_revindex()` to fully reset the packed_git struct's state.

This continues the productive collaboration between Taylor's pack subsystem expertise and Peff's meticulous reviews, with the series steadily advancing toward efficient incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking. The new test cases are proving valuable by uncovering subtle pre-existing issues like this leak, while the core architecture remains stable with no outstanding technical concerns.
