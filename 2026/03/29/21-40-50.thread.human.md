Taylor Blau's ongoing 16-patch series introduces incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking, representing the culmination of his multi-year effort to improve Git's handling of large repositories. The work implements a strategy where packs are maintained in geometric progression across MIDX layers, avoiding periodic full repacks while keeping the object store efficiently organized.

The series includes extensive preparatory refactoring and several bugfixes uncovered during development. The latest patch addresses a memory leak in packfile revindex handling that became apparent with the new test cases. This straightforward but important fix ensures proper cleanup of in-memory revindexes when on-disk .rev files are unavailable, demonstrating the series' attention to edge cases and resource management.

Key components of the work include geometric repacking strategies, MIDX layer compaction, and careful refactoring of the repack infrastructure. The changes span multiple subsystems but maintain backward compatibility while introducing the new incremental approach. With this memory leak now addressed, the series appears to be in its final polishing stages before potential merging.
