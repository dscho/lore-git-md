Taylor Blau has completed his incremental MIDX repacking series (now at v4) with a final patch that enables standalone append-only MIDX layer creation. The series implements two complementary strategies for managing MIDX (multi-pack-index) layers: geometric compaction (which merges layers according to a split factor) and now append-only growth (which simply adds new layers without compaction). 

The implementation carefully handles atomic updates and verification throughout, with new configuration options (`repack.midxSplitFactor` and `repack.midxNewLayerThreshold`) controlling the compaction behavior. The series has addressed all prior technical feedback, including reviews from Jeff King on correctness issues and Elijah Newren on the geometric strategy. 

This work represents a significant scalability improvement for large repositories, completing functionality that was previously only presented in conference talks. The final patch relaxes an earlier restriction, allowing `--write-midx=incremental` to be used without `--geometric` when simple append-only behavior is desired. With all feedback incorporated and comprehensive test coverage added, the series appears ready for merging.
