Taylor Blau's series implementing incremental MIDX/bitmap-based repacking has reached maturity after iterative review. The series provides two main operational modes: an append-only mode that adds new layers to an existing multi-pack-index chain without compaction, and a geometric mode that merges layers when they exceed a configurable split factor. Key configuration knobs are `repack.midxSplitFactor` (default 2) for geometric merging and `repack.midxNewLayerThreshold` (default 8) for the minimum pack count before tip repacking kicks in.
