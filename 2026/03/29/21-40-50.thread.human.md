This thread is Taylor Blau's v4 incremental MIDX repacking series (16 patches), which adds three operational modes for MIDX-based repacking: geometric MIDX chain repacking, append-only MIDX chain growth, and planned atomic multi-layer assembly. The series remains the subject of the thread, and no technical objections to its overall design have emerged.

The only review of the new v4 round has come from Jeff King, who flagged a NULL-safety issue in patch 15's `clear_incremental_midx_files()` helper: the code calls `get_midx_chain_filename()` before the `if (r->objects)` guard, creating either a potential segfault (if `r->objects` can be NULL) or dead code (if it cannot). King traced both callers and found one already dereferences `r->objects->sources` unconditionally, suggesting there is no actual bug and the NULL check should simply be removed. This is a small, single-issue fix likely to be resolved in a trivial reroll.
