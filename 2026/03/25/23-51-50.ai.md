This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's series addressing pack-objects issues during geometric repacking. The patch converts the pack handling in `read_packs_list_from_stdin()` from using two separate `string_list`s (for included and excluded packs) to a single `strmap` keyed by pack basename, with values in a new `struct stdin_pack_info` that tracks both the pack pointer and its inclusion/exclusion status.

The change prepares for future introduction of new pack "kinds" (like the excluded-open packs coming in patch 4) by providing a more extensible data structure. The refactoring splits the logic into two clearer functions: `stdin_packs_read_input()` for parsing stdin and `stdin_packs_add_pack_entries()` for processing the packs. The strmap approach also avoids potential quadratic behavior when handling duplicate pack specifications.

The patch incorporates all feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on the v1 version, including proper strmap usage patterns. While purely mechanical, this refactoring enables the substantive changes in later patches while improving code organization and future maintainability. The series as a whole addresses a subtle edge case in geometric repacking where MIDX bitmap generation could fail due to reachability issues with open packs.
