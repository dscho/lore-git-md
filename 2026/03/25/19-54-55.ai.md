This is a refactoring patch from Adrian Ratiu, part of his larger series modernizing Git's hook configuration system. The patch performs a purely mechanical rename of callback function types in the hook subsystem from `cb_data_free_fn`/`cb_data_alloc_fn` to `hook_data_free_fn`/`hook_data_alloc_fn` to better reflect their purpose. 

The changes touch `hook.c` and `hook.h`, updating both the type definitions and all call sites. This was suggested by Patrick Steinhardt during review of the series. The patch maintains identical functionality while improving naming consistency - a typical example of the kind of incremental code quality improvement that gets incorporated during Git's thorough review process. 

As part of a now-merged series in 'next', this change represents the project's attention to detail in maintaining clean, well-named interfaces even for internal APIs. The patch follows Git's standard pattern of small, focused changes with clear commit messages explaining the "why" behind mechanical transformations.
