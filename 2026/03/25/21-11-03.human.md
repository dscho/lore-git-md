This is a refactoring patch in a series improving Git's xdiff subsystem, specifically focusing on making the record cleanup logic more readable. It's the third of five patches in v2 of Ezekiel Newren's series, which has significantly narrowed its scope from the original proposal to focus exclusively on clarity improvements.

The patch converts nested ternary operations in `xdl_cleanup_records()` into explicit if/else ladders when setting the `action1` and `action2` arrays. The change makes the decision logic for whether to DISCARD, KEEP, or INVESTIGATE each record more visually apparent while maintaining identical behavior. The diff shows the transformation from compact but potentially confusing ternaries to a more verbose but clearer conditional structure.

This continues the series' theme of targeted readability improvements in the xdiff codebase, following feedback on the initial broader proposal. The changes are mechanical and low-risk, touching only internal implementation details of the diff algorithm without affecting its output. The patch appears well-contained and focused on its single purpose of improving code comprehension.
