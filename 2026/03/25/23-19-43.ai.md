This is a technical follow-up in Taylor Blau's series fixing geometric repacking issues with MIDX bitmaps, specifically addressing Jeff King's (Peff) review feedback on patch 4. The discussion centers around handling NULL pack pointers in `add_object_entry_from_pack()`.

Taylor had previously proposed two approaches to address Peff's concern about a potential NULL pointer dereference: either adding a BUG() assertion or restructuring the logic to preserve the `want_object_in_pack()` check. In this email, Taylor reconsiders and rejects the second approach, explaining that calling `want_object_in_pack()` early could interfere with determining the object type, which must happen first. The implication is that the BUG() assertion approach is the better solution, as it documents the invariant that the caller must provide a non-NULL pack pointer while maintaining the correct order of operations.

This exchange demonstrates the careful attention to control flow and invariants required when modifying Git's core object handling code, particularly around pack reachability. The discussion between Taylor and Peff shows the project's rigorous approach to both current correctness and future maintainability in the pack-objects subsystem.
