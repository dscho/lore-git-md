Pablo Sabater responds to Johannes Sixt's (Hannes) feedback about the visual interpretation of lane truncation in the `--graph-lane-limit` feature. Pablo explains that horizontal truncation was chosen for implementation simplicity and backward compatibility with Git's 17-year-old graph rendering code, while acknowledging that rearranging lanes vertically (like gitk does) would require more extensive changes. He confirms plans to send a v5 incorporating all prior feedback, including:
- Documenting that zero/negative values mean "no limit"
- Addressing style suggestions from Hannes
- Potentially clarifying the truncation marker behavior in documentation

The exchange reveals a subtle but important design decision about how to visually represent truncated graph lanes, with the current approach prioritizing incremental improvement over a full rewrite.
