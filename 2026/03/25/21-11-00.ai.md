This is a refactoring patch series targeting Git's xdiff subsystem, now in its second iteration with significant changes from v1. The series comes from Ezekiel Newren, who is known for his work on Git's merge machinery and Rustification efforts.

The v2 series represents a major departure from the original approach, focusing narrowly on improving the readability of `xdl_cleanup_records()` rather than the broader structural changes proposed in v1. The 5 patches now exclusively target code clarity in `xprepare.c`, with changes like:
- Removing a local `recs` pointer in favor of direct array indexing
- Making limit calculations more explicit with separate `mlim1`/`mlim2` variables
- Simplifying the INVESTIGATE action logic
- Using more descriptive type names

The cover letter notes this is "part 3" of an xdiff cleanup effort, though the relationship to prior parts isn't fully explained. The changes are purely internal with no performance or behavioral impact claimed. The series appears well-scoped and focused on maintainability improvements to a complex diff algorithm.

This revision drops several v1 elements that had drawn feedback, particularly around the `ivec` type introduction and structural reorganization of xdiff data fields. The current approach seems designed to address specific readability concerns while avoiding the more controversial aspects of the original proposal. The maintainer has previously indicated awareness of Newren's ongoing work on this topic.
