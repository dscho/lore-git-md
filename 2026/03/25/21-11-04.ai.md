This is the fourth patch in a refactoring series focused on improving readability in Git's xdiff subsystem. The patch comes from Ezekiel Newren, who is known for his work on Git's merge machinery and Rustification efforts.

The patch simplifies the INVESTIGATE action handling in `xdl_cleanup_records()` by making the state transitions more explicit. Rather than combining the INVESTIGATE and KEEP checks in a single conditional, it now clearly shows how INVESTIGATE gets converted to either KEEP or DISCARD based on the result of `xdl_clean_mmatch()`. The changes include:

1. Breaking out the INVESTIGATE case into its own explicit if/else block that updates the action value
2. Separating the KEEP and DISCARD handling into distinct blocks
3. Adding a BUG() check for invalid states
4. Applying the same pattern to both xdf1 and xdf2 processing loops

The changes are purely internal refactoring with no behavioral impact - the logic remains the same, just expressed in a more straightforward way. This continues the series' theme of making the complex diff algorithm code more maintainable by improving clarity. The patch maintains the series' tight focus on non-controversial readability improvements after the broader structural changes from v1 were dropped.
