This is the final patch in Shreyansh Paliwal's series removing `the_repository` usage from the refs subsystem, specifically targeting the packed backend. The change is straightforward: it replaces a remaining use of `the_repository` in `packed-backend.c` with the repository pointer available through `ref_store->repo` when reading the packed refs timeout configuration.

The patch removes the now-unused `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from the file, marking completion of the `the_repository` elimination in this backend. This follows the same pattern seen in earlier patches of the series - identifying cases where repository context is available but unused, and making the dependency explicit. The change is mechanical and low-risk, touching only the configuration lookup for packed refs locking timeout.

With this patch, the packed backend joins the files and reftable backends in being free of direct `the_repository` usage (aside from the known `is_bare_repository()` dependency in reftable that will require future work). The series as a whole represents solid progress in the long-running effort to eliminate global state from Git's codebase.
