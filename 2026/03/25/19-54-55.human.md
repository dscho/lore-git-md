This is a refactoring patch from Adrian Ratiu, part of his larger series modernizing Git's hook configuration system. The patch renames two callback function types in the hook subsystem from `cb_data_free_fn`/`cb_data_alloc_fn` to `hook_data_free_fn`/`hook_data_alloc_fn` to better reflect their purpose and scope. 

The change was suggested by Patrick Steinhardt during review and is purely stylistic - it doesn't modify any functionality. The patch updates both the declarations in hook.h and their usage in hook.c, maintaining consistency across the codebase. This is a straightforward cleanup that improves naming clarity while the larger series adds more substantial hook configuration features. The change is already merged to 'next' as part of the completed series.
