The email reinforces Peff's position that stderr monitoring (his alternative proposal) might provide more reliable error detection without these shell-specific quirks. While acknowledging Junio's progress in making most test scripts `set -e` clean, Peff maintains his wariness based on past experience with `set -e` edge cases.
