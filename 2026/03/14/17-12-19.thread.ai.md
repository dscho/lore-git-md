[RFC PATCH] cocci: detect strbuf passed by value

Proposes a Coccinelle semantic patch to enforce passing struct strbuf by pointer rather than by value, following Git's convention. Originated from Jeff King's review feedback on a worktree fix by Deveshi Dwivedi. Current implementation finds two violations: write_worktree_linking_files() (being fixed) and save_untracked_files() in builtin/stash.c. Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the semantic patch, confirming it improves upon his earlier suggestion by targeting function signatures rather than call sites and using flexible parameter matching (`...`) to catch more cases. While noting the output could benefit from explanatory context, Peff approves the approach. The rule follows existing patterns for strbuf convention enforcement - purely mechanical change maintaining code consistency. Open question remains whether to add explanatory comments to the output.
