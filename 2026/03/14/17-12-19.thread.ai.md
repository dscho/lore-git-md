[RFC PATCH] cocci: detect strbuf passed by value

Proposes a Coccinelle semantic patch to enforce passing struct strbuf by pointer rather than by value, following Git's convention. Originated from Jeff King's review feedback on a worktree fix by Deveshi Dwivedi. Currently finds two violations: write_worktree_linking_files() (already being fixed) and save_untracked_files() in builtin/stash.c (new finding). The rule follows existing patterns for strbuf convention enforcement. Purely mechanical change with no runtime impact - maintains code consistency and prevents future misuse. Presented as RFC to gather feedback before formal submission.
