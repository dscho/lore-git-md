This is a cross-project coordination email in the ongoing kernel.org responsive design discussion. Junio C Hamano weighs in on the proposal to discuss kernel.org website improvements on the Git mailing list, where Rito Rhymes has been submitting responsive design patches.

He defers to Konstantin Ryabitsev (Linux Foundation infrastructure lead) for the final decision on appropriate discussion venues, while acknowledging the lack of obvious alternatives since kernel.org lacks its own public mailing list for such discussions.

The email highlights the tension between maintaining list focus and accommodating practical coordination needs for infrastructure that supports Git development (like the mailing list archives). Junio's response suggests he sees this as a temporary compromise rather than a precedent, with the final call resting with kernel.org's maintainers.
