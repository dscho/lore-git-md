This is a discussion about refining the interface for the experimental `git replay` command, specifically around the proposal to convert its mutually-exclusive options into subcommands. Siddharth Asthana weighs in on Toon Claes' RFC patch, supporting the subcommand direction while providing technical context about why `--advance` and `--revert` need to remain distinct operations.

Siddharth explains that while `--advance` (cherry-pick-like) and `--revert` might seem similar on the surface, they differ fundamentally in commit ordering and authorship handling. This justifies keeping them as separate subcommands rather than combining them with a modifier flag. He notes his upcoming v4 patch series will provide a clean base for this subcommand refactoring.

The thread continues to explore design tradeoffs for this plumbing command, with active collaboration between Siddharth, Toon, and Justin Tobler. Key points under discussion include: whether required options are acceptable in subcommands, how to handle backward compatibility, and whether positional arguments might improve usability. The experimental nature of `git replay` gives some flexibility to iterate on the interface before stabilization.

This email moves the discussion forward by clarifying technical constraints that affect the design, while showing alignment on the general direction of subcommands. The patch remains in RFC state as the interface details are worked out.
