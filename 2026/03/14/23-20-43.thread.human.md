This thread documents the final refinement phase of Mirko Faina's `git format-patch --commit-list-format` series, now merged to 'next'. The feature enhances commit list formatting with new presets and customization options, building on earlier work with `--cover-letter-format`. After thorough review and maintainer approval from Junio Hamano, the series entered post-merge validation where Phillip Wood confirmed its technical soundness.

Recent discussion focuses on two minor formatting quirks observed in real-world usage: missing indentation under headings and occasional spurious blank lines when using width specifiers. Mirko suggests the blank line issue may arise from the interaction between Git's wrapping logic and the fixed 72-character width guarantee in format strings. These observations are typical for a newly merged feature and will likely result in either documentation clarifications or small follow-up patches. Junio Hamano remains engaged in the discussion, having originally reported these edge cases, while Kristoffer Haugsbakk and D. Ben Knoble have contributed to finalizing the documentation. The series represents a complete evolution of Git's patch formatting capabilities, now progressing through the standard integration pipeline toward release.
