This is a follow-up discussion about the already-merged pluralization cleanup in remote.c. Phillip Wood points out a subtle internationalization consideration regarding the patch that replaced Q_() with _() for diverged branch status messages.

While the original change correctly noted that the singular case is impossible (since divergence requires at least 2 commits), Phillip observes that some languages (like Polish in his example) have more complex pluralization rules than just singular/plural. The ngettext() function (which Q_() wraps) handles these cases by considering the actual count, whereas a direct _() call would only use one form of the plural string.

The observation doesn't invalidate the merged change since the behavior remains correct for English and most languages, but it highlights a technical nuance about how pluralization works in gettext. This is more of an educational note than a request to revert the change, as the patch's core premise (removing dead code) still holds. The thread shows the kind of careful attention to edge cases that experienced contributors bring even to small, already-accepted changes.
