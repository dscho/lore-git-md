This is the final approval in the `the_repository` removal effort for the run-command subsystem. Junio C Hamano gives the green light to Burak Kaan Karaçay's two-patch series, noting only a minor stylistic nit (an extra blank line) in the second patch. The changes eliminate the last remaining uses of `the_repository` in `run-command.c` by converting the auto-maintenance functions to take explicit repository parameters and replacing a boolean flag with a direct object store pointer.

The series has gone through multiple iterations with input from Patrick Steinhardt and others, demonstrating how mechanical conversions can evolve into thoughtful interface redesigns. With all technical concerns addressed and positive reviews from key contributors (including Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt in earlier rounds), this marks a successful completion of this particular thread in the larger `the_repository` elimination project. The patches are now queued for merging.
