Junio C Hamano provides final guidance on version numbering and format naming for Mirko Faina's `git format-patch` cover letter formatting series. This is the maintainer's response to remaining open questions after the v8 series was merged to 'next'.

On versioning, Junio recommends treating follow-up patches as a new v1 series that builds on the existing 'next' branch, with a cover letter clearly explaining the relationship to the prior work. He points to Patrick Steinhardt's recent series as an example of this approach.

Regarding the format name, Junio rejects the proposed "chrono" abbreviation for the chronological format, preferring the full "chronological" to avoid arbitrary shortening that users would need to memorize. This aligns with his earlier insistence on the "log:" prefix as a non-negotiable design requirement for interface stability.

The exchange shows the project's preference for clear, unambiguous naming and incremental improvement workflows even for advanced series. With these final points addressed, the feature appears ready for eventual merging to 'master'.
