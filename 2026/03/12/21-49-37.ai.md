This is a bugfix patch addressing zombie process accumulation in containerized Git operations, specifically for network helper processes (git-remote-https, ssh) that get orphaned during abnormal exits. The patch is the final version (v4) of a series that has already received technical approval from Jeff King and Junio C Hamano.

The solution leverages Git's existing `run-command` infrastructure by setting two flags (`clean_on_exit` and `wait_after_clean`) on child processes in `connect.c` (proxy connections) and `transport-helper.c`. This ensures proper cleanup via SIGTERM-then-wait during abnormal exits, avoiding both zombie processes and potential deadlocks that could occur with direct waiting approaches. The implementation specifically targets transport-layer processes spawned during network operations, maintaining a focused scope as agreed in earlier discussion.

The patch represents a minimal 6-line change that modifies three locations where child processes are spawned for network operations. It builds on prior work (commit afe19ff7b5) and has been vetted for safety by Jeff King, who confirmed the kill-then-wait approach avoids deadlock risks. Junio has approved the technical solution while making minor documentation corrections in earlier versions.

Key technical details include the use of `clean_on_exit` to trigger cleanup on any exit path (not just atexit handlers) and `wait_after_clean` to ensure proper reaping. The solution addresses a real-world containerization pain point where thousands of zombie processes were observed, while deliberately not expanding to broader process management concerns that belong to init systems like tini. The patch is ready for merging with no outstanding technical questions.
