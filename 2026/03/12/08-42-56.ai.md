This is part 1 of Patrick Steinhardt's six-part series completing the ODB (object database) abstraction for object counting functionality. The series introduces generic counting interfaces to replace `repo_approximate_object_count()`.

The patch removes the circular include between "odb.h" and "odb/source.h", moving enum and typedef definitions that belong to the generic ODB interface back into "odb.h". This cleanup was suggested by Justin Tobler and makes the header relationships more logical - most callers shouldn't need to know about the internal `struct odb_source`. The change requires adding explicit includes of "odb/source.h" to several files that previously got it transitively through "odb.h".

This is a mechanical refactoring that doesn't change behavior, just header organization. It's the first step in a larger effort to provide proper ODB-level counting interfaces that will work with pluggable backends. The patch is straightforward and has already been reviewed in v1, with no outstanding issues.
