This is a follow-up in the ongoing series to migrate repository-specific globals into `struct repo_config_values`, part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Olamide Bello responds to Tian Yuchen's technical review of the compression level handling in `cmd_pack_objects`.

They confirm they'll update the option parsing to write directly to `cfg->pack_compression_level` in the next version of the patch series.

The exchange shows careful attention to subtle interactions between configuration sources (CLI vs repository storage) in this mechanical refactoring.
