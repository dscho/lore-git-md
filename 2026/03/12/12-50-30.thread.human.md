This thread began with a test modernization series for partial clone infrastructure in `t0410-partial-clone.sh`, which was merged after three rounds of review. The series, authored by Siddharth Shrimali with input from Pushkar Singh, Eric Sunshine, and Jeff King, achieved hash-algorithm independence and strict shell portability through careful use of `test_oid_to_path`, proper variable scoping, and removal of redundant operations. Junio Hamano provided final validation of the shell portability requirements before merging.

Building on lessons from that review, Junio has now sent a documentation patch to update the SubmittingPatches guidelines. The change adds a best practice reminder that contributors should verify test changes by running `make test` from the top level, rather than executing individual test scripts directly. This ensures full validation including shell portability checks that might otherwise be missed. The patch captures knowledge gained from the partial clone test review process, demonstrating how specific review experiences lead to broader improvements in Git's contribution workflow.
