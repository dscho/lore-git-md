This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the auto-maintenance functionality in `run-command.c`. Burak Kaan Karaçay's patch completes the conversion of `prepare_auto_maintenance()` and `run_auto_maintenance()` to explicitly take a `struct repository*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`.

The changes are mechanical but thorough - updating all callers across several builtin commands (am, commit, fetch, merge, rebase, receive-pack) to pass `the_repository` explicitly. The patch also removes the now-unneeded `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from run-command.c since there are no remaining global repository dependencies in that file.

This is the second patch in a v3 series that has already received positive feedback from key reviewers like Patrick Steinhardt (who suggested this extension) and Junio Hamano. The changes follow established patterns for `the_repository` elimination while maintaining backward compatibility. The patch appears well-tested and ready for inclusion, representing another incremental step in the project-wide refactoring effort.
