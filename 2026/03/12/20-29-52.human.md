This is a follow-up discussion on the final patch of a v5 series that adds `sign-if-invalid` mode to `git fast-import`'s signature handling. The series builds on earlier work by Christian Couder and Justin Tobler to enable re-signing of commits with invalid signatures.

Junio C Hamano suggested rewording the warning messages to clarify that the operation replaces existing invalid signatures rather than creating new invalid ones. Justin Tobler agrees with the suggestion and proposes using "replacing invalid signature for commit..." phrasing. He also notes that Patrick Steinhardt has proposed removing these warning messages entirely in a future follow-up series, but for now will update the wording in the next version.

The exchange shows the final polishing of an already-approved series, with only minor wording improvements remaining before merging. The discussion reflects Git's iterative development process where even small details like warning message phrasing receive careful attention from both contributors and the maintainer.
