This is the final approval in the `the_repository` removal effort for the `run-command.c` subsystem. Junio C Hamano confirms he will merge Burak Kaan Karaçay's two-patch series after a minor whitespace fix in the second patch. The series completes the conversion of `start_command()` and auto-maintenance functions to explicitly take repository parameters, allowing removal of `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from `run-command.c`.

The changes affect multiple builtin commands (gc, pull, am, commit, fetch, merge, rebase, receive-pack) by making their repository dependencies explicit through API parameters rather than implicit `the_repository` usage. The implementation introduces an `odb_to_close` pointer in `struct child_process` to replace the `close_object_store` boolean, maintaining the same behavior while improving clarity of repository ownership.

The thread shows strong consensus, with positive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt, Jeff King, and now Junio. This represents a successful model for future `the_repository` conversions - making dependencies explicit while minimizing impact on callers. With all technical concerns addressed, the series is ready to be part of the ongoing global variable elimination effort.
