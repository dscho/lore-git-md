Harald Nordgren's series to enhance `git checkout -m` with autostash behavior has completed its review cycle with all technical aspects finalized in v8. The implementation unifies conflict resolution behavior with `git rebase`, adds customizable conflict marker labels (including empty label support), and implements silent autostash creation. Junio Hamano has confirmed the series is ready for merge after verifying test coverage and environmental behavior, with Phillip Wood reviewing the autostash implementation details.

The series modifies several core components including `builtin/checkout.c`, `stash.c`, and the sequencer, adding new functions like `apply_autostash_ref_with_labels()` while extending existing ones with label support. Comprehensive testing covers 19 scenarios in t7201-co.sh.

In a final review comment after approval, Chris Torek suggested improving the user-facing conflict recovery instructions to recommend `git stash pop --index` when appropriate, particularly when dealing with previously staged changes. This represents a minor documentation refinement to an otherwise production-ready implementation. The series remains merge-ready, with this suggestion being a candidate for either a follow-up patch or immediate incorporation if the maintainer agrees.
