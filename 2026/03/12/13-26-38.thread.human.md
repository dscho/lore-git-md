This thread documents the successful 17-iteration development of autostash functionality for `git checkout -m`, now fully merged with robust conflict resolution customization. The work, led by Harald Nordgren with key input from Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano, spans core functionality, test infrastructure, and documentation - including custom conflict markers, silent operation modes, and comprehensive resource management (now with complete lockfile handling).

In a recent exchange, Harald and Junio reflect on process improvements for future contributions. Junio noted that the 350+ line range-diff in Harald's cover letter obscured the actual changes (which were minor function consolidations), making review unnecessarily difficult. Harald acknowledges this and proposes two technical solutions to streamline future patch submissions: a potential `git range-diff --rebase` option to focus on modified files, and GitGitGadget enhancements (PR 2212) for better diff presentation.

While the autostash implementation itself is complete, this follow-up discussion exemplifies Git's culture of continuous improvement - where even after successful merge, participants refine their processes. The suggestions could benefit the broader contributor community if implemented, showing how technical solutions emerge from constructive review experiences.
