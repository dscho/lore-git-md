Harald Nordgren's series to add autostash support to `git checkout -m` is now in its final polishing stage after eight iterations. The feature unifies conflict resolution workflows by bringing familiar autostash behavior to branch switching operations, with careful attention to backward compatibility and scriptability - including support for empty labels that Junio Hamano explicitly endorsed as analogous to rerere's label-agnostic approach.

The latest patch addresses the last two minor issues: fixing a naming inconsistency in parameters and adopting a clearer `label_*` prefix convention for the new label-related autostash options. With these changes, the series needs are fully met - all major technical concerns were resolved in earlier rounds, and the remaining items were purely about naming consistency. The implementation modifies several core components including the checkout and stash machinery, sequencer, and xdiff interfaces, with thorough test coverage (19 cases in t7201-co.sh).

Phillip Wood, who provided detailed technical review throughout, notes this infrastructure could later extend to improve `git rebase --autostash` labels as well. The series now awaits final merge with all maintainer approvals in place.
