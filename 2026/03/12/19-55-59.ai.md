This is a bugfix patch addressing zombie process cleanup in Git's network operations, now in its third iteration. The patch modifies `connect.c` and `transport-helper.c` to use Git's existing `clean_on_exit` mechanism for child process reaping during abnormal exits (like transport errors), replacing the earlier proposed atexit handler approach that risked deadlocks.

The key technical change sets `clean_on_exit` and `wait_after_clean` flags on child processes spawned for proxy connections and remote helpers (git-remote-https, ssh). This ensures proper cleanup via SIGTERM-then-wait even when Git exits abnormally, preventing zombie accumulation in containerized environments where Git processes may be repeatedly spawned. The solution now leverages Git's built-in `run-command` infrastructure rather than custom atexit handlers, addressing Jeff King's deadlock concerns from the previous round.

While the discussion has established that Git-as-PID-1 is atypical (with tini being the preferred solution), the patch maintains that Git should still clean up its own children as a matter of correctness. The changes are narrowly scoped to network operations where abnormal exits are most likely to bypass normal cleanup paths. The diff is minimal (6 lines added) and focused on the transport layer, avoiding broader architectural questions about process management responsibilities.
