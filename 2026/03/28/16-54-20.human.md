This is a review comment on a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Tian Yuchen raises two technical points about Shreyansh Paliwal's patch that makes branch name helpers repository-aware.

The tone is collegial ("just a thought") and the technical observations appear valid, though the suggested alternative might need validation against edge cases where `repo` is NULL.
