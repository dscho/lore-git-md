Shreyansh Paliwal has proposed a GSoC 2026 project to continue the ongoing effort to reduce Git's reliance on global state, particularly targeting `the_repository` and other global variables. This builds on prior work by Ayush Chandekar and Olamide Bello, as well as René Scharfe's long-term `the_repository` removal effort. Shreyansh has already contributed several merged changes to Git, demonstrating his familiarity with the codebase and development process.

Christian Couder, a Git Project Leadership Committee member and GSoC coordinator, has reviewed the proposal and provided constructive feedback. He suggests technical improvements to how Shreyansh describes his past work, particularly around commit statuses and terminology. Christian notes that some of Shreyansh's patches have already been merged into main branches, while others are still under review or being shepherded by more experienced contributors like Phillip Wood. The review is encouraging overall, indicating Shreyansh has made good progress in understanding Git's development workflow and that his proposal aligns well with the project's architectural goals. With these refinements, the proposal appears to be on track for consideration as a GSoC 2026 project.
