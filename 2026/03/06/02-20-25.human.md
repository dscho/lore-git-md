This is version 5 of a feature patch series introducing a `pre-add` hook mechanism, now facing fundamental design reconsideration despite technical completion. The hook would validate staged changes during `git add` by receiving both the original and proposed index states as tempfiles.

The implementation is technically sound with Windows compatibility, config support via `hook_exists()`, and comprehensive tests. However, maintainer Junio Hamano questions whether the hook's limited scope (only seeing changes from the current `git add` rather than all staged changes) provides enough value to justify inclusion. The rapid revision pace (v4 to v5 in minutes) has also raised concerns about insufficient consideration of these deeper design questions.

Reviewer Phillip Wood has identified several areas needing clarification, including documentation structure, dry-run behavior consistency with other hooks, and whether to extend the hook to cover `git commit` staging operations. The author acknowledges these points but suggests some may be better addressed in follow-up work to maintain scope.

The discussion has shifted from implementation details to whether the concept itself is valuable enough in its current form. The series may require significant redesign or reconsideration of whether to proceed, with Junio suggesting the current design may not justify its complexity. The author remains committed to the change but recognizes the need to address these fundamental concerns.
