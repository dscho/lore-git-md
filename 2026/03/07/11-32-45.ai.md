This is a follow-up in the thread about modernizing git-gui's maintenance behavior by integrating with Git's built-in auto maintenance feature. Johannes Sixt (the git-gui maintainer) responds to Wolfgang Faust's patch series that removes git-gui's legacy GC infrastructure.

Johannes generally supports the direction but raises a practical concern about users who might be affected by the change - specifically those who have disabled the loose objects warning (gui.gcwarning=false) but might not realize the broader implications of Git's maintenance running automatically through other commands. He suggests a conservative approach where gui.gcwarning=false could indicate no automatic cleanup is desired.

The response also includes maintainer guidance about patch organization - requesting that git-gui changes be separated from non-git-gui changes (like the Scalar modifications) since git-gui has its own repository. Johannes also asks to avoid modifying translation files in this cleanup series to reduce noise.

This moves the discussion forward by acknowledging the technical merit while surfacing a real-world usage consideration that wasn't addressed in the original proposal. The thread now needs to resolve whether to proceed with the current approach or modify it to respect gui.gcwarning=false more strictly.
