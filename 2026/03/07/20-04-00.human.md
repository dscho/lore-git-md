This is a GSoC 2026 project proposal from Shreyansh Paliwal to continue the ongoing effort to reduce Git's reliance on global state, particularly targeting `the_repository` and related global variables. The proposal builds on prior work by Ayush Chandekar and Olamide Bello in this area.

Shreyansh demonstrates solid preparation for this project, having already contributed several patches related to global state reduction, including work on `wt-status.c` and `tree-diff.c` to remove `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` dependencies. The proposal outlines a clear two-pronged approach: first tackling straightforward cases where repository instances are already available in the call stack, then addressing more complex cases involving global variables from `environment.c`.

The timeline shows good planning, with community bonding time allocated for design discussions, followed by incremental refactoring work during the coding period. Shreyansh has already established working relationships with key contributors like Phillip Wood and has shown ability to incorporate feedback, as seen in patches that evolved based on mailing list discussions.

This is a well-scoped continuation of Git's architectural modernization efforts. The proposal's strength lies in its concrete examples of prior work and clear classification of remaining tasks. It aligns well with the project's long-term goals around libification and multi-repository support while avoiding over-ambitious promises. The main area for improvement would be more precise documentation of patch statuses (merged vs in-review), as noted in Christian Couder's feedback.
