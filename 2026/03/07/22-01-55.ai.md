Maintainer discussion about git-gui maintenance behavior changes. This is part of an ongoing thread about modernizing git-gui's repository maintenance by integrating with Git's built-in auto-maintenance feature, replacing the legacy loose object warning dialog and manual compression option.

Junio C Hamano responds to Johannes Sixt's concerns about users who had disabled the old warning system (gui.gcwarning=false). While Johannes suggested these users might be a small minority who could be accommodated by respecting their setting, Junio takes a stronger stance - arguing that users who explicitly disabled warnings were likely doing so because they found them annoying, and thus would be even more likely to object to more aggressive automatic maintenance behavior.

The exchange highlights a key design question in the transition: whether the old gui.gcwarning setting should carry forward as an opt-out signal for the new maintenance behavior. Junio's position implies he sees the setting as an indicator of user preference against any automated cleanup, not just the warning dialog being removed. This suggests the patch series may need revision to either:
1) Strictly respect gui.gcwarning=false as disabling all automatic maintenance, or
2) Clearly document the behavior change and treat it as a breaking change for those users

The thread continues to work through the implications of matching git-gui's behavior to the CLI's maintenance.run --auto while considering real-world usage patterns of the GUI-specific configuration.
