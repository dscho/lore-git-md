This positive review follows his earlier praise for patch 1/12's approach to scoping fsck options, indicating the maintainer is aligned with the series' architectural direction.

The conversion touches nearly all aspects of fsck functionality - from core checking logic to built-in commands and test coverage - while maintaining identical behavior. With Junio engaged and providing constructive feedback, the series systematically addressing each component, this refactoring appears on track for eventual merging into Git's codebase.
