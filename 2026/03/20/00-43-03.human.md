This is a test improvement patch in Jeff King's series enhancing the `diff-highlight` contrib script. The change focuses on better test reliability by properly checking the exit status of the `diff-highlight` command during tests.

Currently, the test pipes `diff-highlight` output through a sanitizing function, which masks the command's exit status. This could hide crashes or unexpected failures. The patch modifies the test to use temporary files instead of pipes, ensuring any problems with `diff-highlight` itself will be caught.

The change is small but meaningful - typical of Jeff's thorough approach to even contrib scripts. It's part of a larger effort to improve test coverage and robustness for this diff highlighting tool, particularly around its integration with `diff-so-fancy`. The patch follows cleanly from the previous documentation updates in the series and maintains the script's Perl 5.8 compatibility.
