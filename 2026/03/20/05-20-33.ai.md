This is a platform compatibility discussion continuing the thread about const-correctness changes needed for glibc 2.43 and C23 standards compliance. Collin Funk responds to Jeff King's earlier email about handling string function const-correctness warnings, providing additional technical context about the glibc macro behavior.

Collin explains the two conditions under which `__GLIBC_USE(ISOC23)` evaluates to true: either using C23 standards flags or defining `_GNU_SOURCE`. He demonstrates how this affects compilation with a simple test case and shows a workaround involving undefining `__glibc_const_generic` after including `sys/cdefs.h`. The email reveals this issue has caused widespread build problems across GNU projects that vendor Gnulib, though most were able to release fixes without resorting to the workaround.

The discussion remains focused on practical solutions while acknowledging the systemic nature of the problem. Collin confirms he's comfortable with others contributing fixes to the remaining cases and mentions two simple patches already in Junio's "next" branch, plus one pending review.
