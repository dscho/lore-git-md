This is Junio C Hamano's review of patch 13/14 in Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove `the_repository` from the fsck subsystem. The patch converts the error reporting mechanism to explicitly pass a repository parameter rather than relying on the global variable.

Junio reiterates his earlier architectural suggestion that the repository pointer might better belong in the `fsck_options` struct rather than being passed through every function call. While he didn't comment on earlier patches in the series, he notes this pattern has persisted, reinforcing his view that storing it in the options struct would be cleaner. This is a significant design consideration that could affect the series' approach.

The review doesn't raise any specific issues with the current patch's implementation - the mechanical conversion appears correct - but suggests a potentially better way to structure the entire series. This is typical of Junio's maintainer-level feedback, focusing on the overall architecture rather than line-by-line details.
