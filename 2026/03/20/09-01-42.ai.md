This is a review comment on patch 3/14 of Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series. Karthik Nayak approves the change introducing `struct odb_for_each_object_options` to replace direct flag parameters in object iteration functions.

Karthik highlights two key benefits of the change: (1) it cleans up function arguments by grouping them in a structure, and (2) makes future additions easier by localizing diffs to the structure rather than requiring function signature changes. He specifically praises the improved readability when flags are initialized via the new structure rather than passed directly as bitwise OR operations.

The review notes that the rest of the patch looks good, indicating no technical concerns with the implementation. This positive feedback from Karthik, who is actively involved in the ODB abstraction work, suggests the change aligns well with the series' goals and maintains the expected quality level.
