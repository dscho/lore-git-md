While not blocking the immediate fix, this observation identified an opportunity for future error message improvements.

He provided a sample diff showing how error handling could be enhanced to distinguish between missing trees and blobs, but made clear this would be a follow-up change. The thread demonstrates Git's careful balance between addressing critical bugs promptly while noting opportunities for future refinement.
