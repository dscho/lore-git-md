This is a platform compatibility follow-up in the ongoing macOS regex investigation. Johannes Schindelin provides concrete evidence that the REG_ENHANCED flag disappearance is indeed tied to a recent CI environment change, not a Git code issue.

Schindelin shows meson build logs proving the REG_ENHANCED define vanished between two consecutive CI runs (March 13 vs March 20), correlating with the earlier observed runner update. This confirms his hypothesis that a Homebrew/clang update caused the regression, while also explaining why meson-built binaries weren't affected - the meson.build file's conditional handling detected and adapted to the missing define.

The findings validate the decision to avoid patching Git's code, as the problem stems from environmental changes outside the project's control. This appears to close the investigation with a clear root cause identified in CI infrastructure rather than Git's macOS compatibility layer. The thread may now shift to deciding whether to document this environmental quirk or adjust CI configurations.
