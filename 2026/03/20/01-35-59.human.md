This is a feature patch discussion about enhancing `git send-email`'s recipient selection capabilities. Junio C Hamano weighs in on the ongoing thread about whether to support executable scripts directly via `--to/--cc/--bcc` flags rather than the existing `--*-cmd` options.

Junio acknowledges the conceptual appeal of the new approach - automatically detecting and executing scripts passed as recipient arguments - but notes this comes too late given the existing `--cc-cmd` and `--to-cmd` infrastructure already in place. His comment suggests the ship has sailed on this particular design direction, implying the patch may need to adapt to work within the established pattern rather than introducing a parallel mechanism.

The discussion continues to revolve around whether this primarily fills a gap (the missing `--bcc-cmd`) or unnecessarily duplicates functionality. Junio's perspective as maintainer carries weight here - his "too late" remark indicates this approach likely won't be accepted as-is, though the underlying need (better bcc support) remains valid. The patch author may need to pivot toward extending the existing `--*-cmd` family rather than creating a new execution path.
