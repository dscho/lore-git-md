This is a platform compatibility follow-up regarding macOS regex behavior in Git's CI builds. Johannes Schindelin continues the discussion about CI failures on macOS 14 where the REG_ENHANCED flag appears to be missing, building on Junio C Hamano's initial patch attempt and his own earlier analysis.

Schindelin now realizes he initially misunderstood the role of USE_ENHANCED_BASIC_REGULAR_EXPRESSIONS in config.mak.uname - it indicates platform capability rather than forcing use of compat/regex.c. He pivots to propose a different solution: adding a NO_REGEX flag specifically for Homebrew's clang on macOS, which would trigger use of Git's fallback regex implementation when enhanced support is unavailable.

The email highlights the complexity of maintaining consistent behavior across different build systems (traditional make vs meson) and CI environments. Schindelin's proposed change would explicitly document the platform regression while maintaining correct behavior, rather than silently changing functionality as the initial workaround might have done. This continues the thread's theme of balancing quick fixes against proper solutions for platform-specific issues.
