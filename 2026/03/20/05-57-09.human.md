Performance-focused discussion about strbuf_getwholeline() buffer management.

Jeff King (Peff) pushes back on Junio Hamano's proposal to simplify strbuf_getwholeline() by always allocating a fresh buffer for getdelim(). While Junio's approach would eliminate complex buffer management code, Peff argues it would hurt performance in common cases where the same strbuf is reused across multiple calls - a pattern that currently avoids repeated allocations.

The core disagreement centers on whether the performance impact of Junio's proposal would be significant enough to justify keeping the current, more complex implementation. Peff notes this buffer reuse pattern is a documented optimization technique in Git's codebase, though he hasn't provided specific benchmarks yet. This exchange may lead to either performance measurements to quantify the tradeoff or a search for a solution that preserves both simplicity and correctness.
