This is a review response from Junio C Hamano on Patrick Steinhardt's patch series to remove `the_repository` dependencies from Git's fsck subsystem. The patch in question (13/14) converts the fsck error reporting mechanism to explicitly pass a repository parameter rather than relying on the global variable.

Junio notes that while he didn't comment on individual patches earlier, he's observing a pattern where adding the repository* to the fsck_options struct might be a cleaner approach than passing it through all function parameters. This echoes his earlier architectural suggestion about the series' design direction.

The maintainer's feedback suggests the series is on the right track but may benefit from reconsidering how repository context is propagated through the fsck subsystem. This isn't a blocking concern - the patch itself looks solid - but indicates an area where the overall design could potentially be refined in a future iteration. The review tone is constructive, focusing on long-term maintainability rather than immediate issues with the current patch.
