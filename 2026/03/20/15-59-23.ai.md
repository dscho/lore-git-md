René's findings suggest the root cause might involve how the CI scripts manipulate PATH rather than a fundamental incompatibility with Homebrew's clang. However, he expresses uncertainty about why these particular changes resolve the issue, indicating the need for further investigation into the CI environment's PATH handling.
