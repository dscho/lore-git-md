Bugfix addressing a memory leak in pack-objects' stdin-packs handling. This is the first patch in Taylor Blau's series dealing with geometric repacking and MIDX bitmap generation issues.

The patch fixes a straightforward oversight where `read_stdin_packs()` wasn't calling `release_revisions()` on its `rev_info` struct, leading to a memory leak. The issue dates back to the original introduction of the `--stdin-packs` option in 2021 and persisted through subsequent changes. The fix is simple - just adding the missing cleanup call before the function returns.

This appears to be a preparatory cleanup patch for the larger series, fixing a minor but real issue that was likely noticed while working on the more complex pack handling changes that follow. The change is small, focused, and clearly correct - exactly the sort of cleanup that's good to land early in a series to avoid distractions in later review.
