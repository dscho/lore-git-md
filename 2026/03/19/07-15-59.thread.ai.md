Thread summary:

Junio Hamano submits a cleanup to the rerere subsystem's conflict resolution code, removing obsolete NULL checks for strbuf.buf that have been unnecessary since strbuf behavior was changed in 2007 to guarantee .buf is never NULL. The patch removes 6 lines of defensive coding that persisted through multiple refactorings over 19 years, simplifying hash computation during conflict resolution by eliminating conditional checks before passing strbuf contents to git_hash_update().

Patrick Steinhardt reviews and confirms the change, agreeing with Junio's rationale that strbuf.buf has been guaranteed to never be NULL (pointing instead to a static NUL byte when empty) since 2007. He notes this makes the defensive checks unnecessary, as passing .buf directly to git_hash_update() is now always safe. Patrick's approval as a reviewer with deep knowledge of Git's data structures provides technical validation, particularly regarding correct handling of strbuf_slopbuf. The change affects rerere.c's hash resolution path but does not modify user-visible behavior or conflict resolution outcomes. Two senior contributors agree on a straightforward cleanup of historical technical debt in the rerere subsystem.
