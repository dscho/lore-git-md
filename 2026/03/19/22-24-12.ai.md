This is a cover letter for a 5-patch series addressing a pack-objects issue related to geometric repacking and MIDX bitmap generation. The series comes from Taylor Blau, who works on Git's pack subsystem at GitHub.

The core issue occurs when geometric repacking encounters packs that aren't closed under reachability (where some objects' parents are missing). In such cases, MIDX bitmap generation can fail when it finds objects reachable through excluded packs but not actually present in the MIDX. The series introduces a new "excluded-open" pack state (marked with '!' prefix) that tells pack-objects to include objects reachable from these packs while still excluding their direct contents.

The first two patches are preparatory: fixing a memory leak and refactoring pack list reading to use strmap. Patch 3 adds a test demonstrating the failure scenario. Patch 4 implements the core fix by adding excluded-open pack support to --stdin-packs. Patch 5 updates repack to mark non-MIDX packs above the geometric split as excluded-open.

This appears to be a well-motivated fix for a real-world issue GitHub encountered. The changes are focused and come with thorough explanation in both the cover letter and commit messages. The series touches sensitive pack machinery but seems carefully constructed to minimize risk - the new behavior is opt-in via the '!' notation rather than changing default behavior. Reviewers will likely want to scrutinize the reachability handling in patch 4 particularly closely.
