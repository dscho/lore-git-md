This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's series addressing geometric repacking and MIDX bitmap generation issues. It's the second of five patches, preparing the codebase for introducing a "excluded-open" pack state by restructuring how pack lists are handled in `pack-objects`.

The patch replaces two separate `string_list`s (for included and excluded packs) with a single `strmap` that tracks all pack information in a unified way. Each entry in the strmap points to a new `stdin_pack_info` struct that combines the pack pointer with a bitfield indicating whether the pack is included or excluded. The change enables cleaner extension to support additional pack states (like the upcoming "excluded-open" state) without duplicating logic.

Key technical details:
- Introduces `struct stdin_pack_info` to bundle pack pointer and kind flags
- Splits functionality into `stdin_packs_read_input()` (parsing stdin) and `stdin_packs_add_pack_entries()` (processing packs)
- Uses strmap instead of string_list to avoid quadratic costs when handling duplicate pack specifications
- Maintains all existing behavior while making the code more extensible

The change is purely internal - no user-visible behavior changes in this patch. It's a well-scoped preparatory refactoring that makes the subsequent patches in the actual fix (patches 4-5) cleaner to implement. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and approach, and the diff shows careful attention to maintaining existing functionality while improving the structure.
