Taylor Blau's v2 patch series (5 patches) resolves pack-objects issues during geometric repacking when MIDX bitmap generation fails due to packs not being closed under reachability. The solution introduces a new "excluded-open" pack state (marked with '!') to properly handle objects reachable through excluded packs while excluding their direct contents.

Patch 1 fixes a memory leak in `read_stdin_packs()`. Patch 2's strmap refactoring of pack handling evolved through technical discussion with Derrick Stolee, maintaining the core improvement from string_lists to strmap while correctly handling edge cases with duplicate pack specifications. Patch 3 demonstrates the failure case with once-cruft objects becoming reachable through new references. Patch 4 implements the core fix with `pack_keep_in_core_open` flag and thorough documentation. Patch 5 modifies `builtin/repack.c` to use excluded-open marking when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false.

The series has been:
- Fully approved by Derrick Stolee (packfile specialist)
- Incorporated feedback from Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt
- Demonstrated handling of edge cases in pack specification parsing
