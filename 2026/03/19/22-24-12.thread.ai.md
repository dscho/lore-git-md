Taylor Blau introduces a 5-patch series addressing an issue in pack-objects when geometric repacking encounters packs that are not closed under reachability (missing some objects' parents). The problem manifests when MIDX bitmap generation fails due to objects being reachable through excluded packs but not present in the MIDX. The series adds a new "excluded-open" pack state (marked with '!' prefix) that tells pack-objects to include reachable objects from these packs while excluding their direct contents.

Patches 1-2 prepare the ground: fixing a memory leak in builtin/pack-objects.c and refactoring pack list reading to use strmap. Patch 3 adds a test demonstrating the failure scenario. Patch 4 implements the core fix by adding excluded-open pack support to --stdin-packs. Patch 5 updates repack to mark non-MIDX packs above the geometric split as excluded-open.

The changes focus on sensitive pack machinery but are carefully scoped, introducing new behavior via opt-in '!' notation rather than altering defaults. The series comes from Taylor Blau, a GitHub engineer and PLC member who specializes in Git's pack subsystem (MIDX, geometric repacking). The motivation stems from real-world issues encountered at GitHub. Key areas for reviewer attention include the reachability handling in patch 4 and the interaction between geometric repacking and MIDX bitmap generation.
