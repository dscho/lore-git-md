This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's series addressing geometric repacking issues with MIDX bitmap generation. The patch focuses on the repack layer, completing the solution introduced in earlier patches.

The core change modifies how `builtin/repack.c` marks packs above the geometric split point when they weren't in the previous MIDX. Instead of marking them as excluded-closed ('^'), it now marks them as excluded-open ('!') when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false. This ensures pack-objects will traverse commits from these packs during the follow pass, rescuing any reachable objects that might otherwise be missing from the resulting pack.

The patch includes a test update that now passes, demonstrating the fix works for rescuing once-cruft objects above the geometric split. Taylor acknowledges a rare edge case where pre-existing MIDXs might still have issues, but explains why this is extremely unlikely in practice and how an all-into-one repack would resolve it.

The changes are focused and well-explained, building on the foundation laid in previous patches. As the final piece of the series, it cleanly integrates the new excluded-open pack state into the geometric repacking workflow. The patch maintains the careful, incremental approach seen throughout the series - introducing new behavior only where needed while preserving existing functionality.
