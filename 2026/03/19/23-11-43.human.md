Platform compatibility follow-up regarding macOS regex behavior. René Scharfe responds to Junio C Hamano's patch about conditional REG_ENHANCED usage by pointing out that macOS 14 (Sonoma) was released well after the original REG_ENHANCED patch, and that even older macOS versions (citing 10.12 specifically) document this flag in their man pages.

This raises questions about why the CI failure is occurring on macos-14 if the flag has been present for years. The response is brief but suggests the issue might need deeper investigation - either the CI environment is unusual, or there's more to the REG_ENHANCED availability story than initially apparent. As a simple compatibility fix, this remains straightforward, but René's historical context adds useful perspective about what systems should reasonably support this flag.
