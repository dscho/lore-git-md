Taylor Blau's patch series addresses a subtle issue in geometric repacking where MIDX bitmap generation could fail when packs weren't properly closed under reachability. The solution introduces a new "excluded-open" pack state to correctly handle objects reachable through excluded packs while excluding their direct contents. The five-part series has undergone thorough review from packfile specialists Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, and Patrick Steinhardt, with all technical questions resolved except for one remaining bug in the refactoring portion.

The latest discussion focuses on a specific issue in patch 2/5 where pack kind flags are incorrectly overwritten rather than accumulated when processing pack specifications. Derrick Stolee identified this edge case in the strmap conversion, noting the logic should use '|=' instead of '=' to properly handle multiply-specified packs. While the core functionality remains sound, this final refinement to the intermediate refactoring step will be addressed in the upcoming v3 submission. The series has otherwise received positive feedback and includes comprehensive test coverage for the geometric repacking edge cases it solves.
