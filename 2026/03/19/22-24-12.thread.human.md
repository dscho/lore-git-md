Taylor Blau's series on geometric repacking and MIDX bitmap generation continues to receive thorough review. The five-patch set addresses issues where objects in packs not closed under reachability were causing MIDX generation failures, introducing a new "excluded-open" pack state to properly handle these cases.

Patrick Steinhardt has now reviewed patch 2, which refactors pack-objects' stdin pack processing to use a strmap instead of string_lists. His characteristically detailed feedback notes several technical points: the preserved pack ordering via QSORT, redundant existence checks, and the need to document enum values. While identifying a subtle behavior change in pack validity checking, he acknowledges the existing safeguards and ultimately approves the refactoring as a solid foundation for the series' later patches.

Earlier in the thread, Jeff King caught a potential NULL pointer issue in patch 4's object-walking logic, to which Taylor proposed two solutions still under discussion. The series has drawn engagement from key contributors - Taylor driving the geometric repacking fixes, Peff providing critical safety review, and Patrick examining both the testing implications and refactoring details. The changes maintain Git's careful approach to pack handling while solving a real-world MIDX generation edge case. Remaining open items include finalizing the NULL pointer handling approach and considering broader test coverage for revision-walking memory leaks.
