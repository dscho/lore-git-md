Taylor Blau's patch series to fix pack-objects issues during geometric repacking has uncovered a bug in the refactoring portion of the changes. While the core functionality - properly handling "excluded-open" packs marked with '!' during MIDX bitmap generation - remains sound, the v2 series introduced an incorrect assumption in patch 2/5. The sorting comparator mistakenly treats the `util` field as pointing to a `packed_git` struct when it actually contains a `stdin_pack_info` struct, causing ASan CI failures.

Taylor has acknowledged the issue and plans to submit a v3 series with a fix, though he's waiting to incorporate any additional feedback from Derrick Stolee's review first. The series has otherwise received thorough review and approval from key contributors (Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, and Patrick Steinhardt) and addresses important edge cases in how geometric repacking interacts with MIDX bitmap generation, particularly around cruft packs and reachability. The bug doesn't affect the main purpose of the changes but needs correction before merging.
