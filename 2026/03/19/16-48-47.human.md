Maintainer feedback on the `git checkout -m` autostash series. Junio Hamano expresses strong approval for the current implementation, particularly praising the elimination of redundant dry-run checks and the safe fallback behavior of "stash && merge && unstash". He notes the technical execution is solid, with credit to both Harald Nordgren (the author) and Phillip Wood (who suggested key optimizations).

While Harald had expressed some hesitation about the expanded scope touching `-m` behavior, Junio - as a regular `checkout -m` user - indicates the changes look correct to him. He does acknowledge there may be corner cases around submodules or complex local modifications that haven't been tested, but his overall tone suggests these would be minor follow-up fixes rather than blockers.

This appears to be the maintainer green light Harald was waiting for. With Junio's approval of both the technical approach and behavioral changes, the series seems ready for final polish and merging. The remaining uncertainty about edge cases is normal for a change of this nature and wouldn't typically hold up integration.
