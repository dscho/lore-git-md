Patrick Steinhardt raises concerns about the approach taken in this patch from the ODB abstraction series. While the patch mechanically converts call sites to use the new `odb_source_files_try()` helper, Patrick questions whether simply skipping non-files backends is the right strategy. He argues that the proper solution would be to fully abstract these sites rather than silently ignoring alternate backends.

The critique suggests this change may be premature - since no alternate backends exist yet, the current behavior of aborting via BUG() may be preferable to silently skipping, as it forces proper abstraction when alternate backends are introduced. Patrick implies the patch trades short-term compatibility for potentially masking the need for deeper architectural changes.

This feedback indicates the series may require rethinking before proceeding. As a key driver of the ODB abstraction effort, Patrick's perspective carries significant weight here. The discussion will likely need to address whether these conversions represent proper abstraction or just a temporary workaround, and whether the changes align with the long-term goals of the ODB initiative.
