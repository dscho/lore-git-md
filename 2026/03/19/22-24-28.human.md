This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's series addressing geometric repacking issues with MIDX bitmaps. The patch completes the solution by updating `git repack` to properly mark non-MIDX packs above the geometric split as "excluded-open" (using '!' prefix) rather than "excluded-closed" ('^').

The key change makes pack-objects traverse commits in these packs during the follow pass, ensuring object reachability is maintained even when packs aren't fully closed. This fixes the test case from patch 3 that previously failed, now marked as successful. The implementation carefully handles edge cases where MIDX'd packs might not be closed under reachability, though this is noted as extremely rare in practice.
