This is a review comment on the second patch of Justin Tobler's ODB abstraction series. Patrick Steinhardt (a key driver of the ODB effort) questions the approach of skipping non-files backends during iteration, suggesting it may not be the right long-term solution.

Patrick notes that while the current state expects some breakage with alternate backends, the proper response would be to properly abstract these sites rather than silently skip them. He expresses skepticism that skipping is the correct behavior in most cases, calling the approach "a bit puzzling."

The exchange highlights a tension in incremental refactoring - whether to make minimal changes that unblock future work (Justin's approach) or hold out for more complete abstractions upfront (Patrick's preference). As the ODB abstraction lead, Patrick's concerns carry weight, though the thread shows no resolution yet.

This is a substantive technical discussion about architectural direction, not just code style. The patch author will likely need to either justify the approach or revise it based on Patrick's feedback. Other ODB contributors may weigh in as the discussion develops.
