Refactoring patch proposing Coccinelle rules to modernize strbuf handling.

Junio Hamano follows up on his earlier rerere.c cleanup by proposing a Coccinelle semantic patch to systematically eliminate unnecessary NULL checks of strbuf.buf throughout the codebase. The patch identifies two patterns: converting `SB.buf ? SB.buf : ""` to just `SB.buf` (which only affected the rerere.c case he already fixed), and more broadly flagging `!SB.buf` checks as invalid except for two documented exceptions.

The exceptions are carefully considered: one in `parse_list_objects_filter()` where the check serves as a BUG() guard against uninitialized structs, and another in `strbuf_getwholeline()` which temporarily violates the strbuf invariant to interface with getdelim(). Junio suggests a future refactoring of strbuf_getwholeline() could eliminate even this exception by using strbuf_attach() instead.

This is a natural progression from the earlier rerere.c cleanup, expanding it into a systematic effort to enforce strbuf invariants across the codebase. The Coccinelle rules are thoughtfully designed to avoid false positives while catching the majority of cases. As with the previous patch, this comes from the maintainer who understands the strbuf invariants deeply, and the change is purely about code hygiene with no behavior impact. The RFC nature suggests Junio wants to confirm there are no other legitimate exceptions before applying these rules widely.
