Maintainer response in core-vs-external tooling discussion. Junio C Hamano firmly rejects the proposal to add message-ID fetching directly to `git am`, arguing this functionality belongs squarely in the `b4` tool. His response makes two key points: (1) The interaction with lore.kernel.org's specific mbox format is too specialized for core Git, and (2) `git am`'s responsibility should remain narrowly focused on applying patches from a mailbox, regardless of how that mailbox was obtained.

The tone is definitive - Junio considers this a clear case where the external tool (`b4`) is not just an acceptable alternative but the *right* solution. This effectively closes the discussion unless new technical arguments emerge that challenge his boundary assessment. For contributors following the thread, this serves as a concrete example of where the maintainer draws the line between core functionality and external tool responsibilities.
