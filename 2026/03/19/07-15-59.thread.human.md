Junio Hamano is leading a systematic cleanup to remove obsolete NULL checks for strbuf.buf throughout Git's codebase, with Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff) providing technical review. The effort has two parts: specific fixes in rerere.c (already approved) and a proposed Coccinelle semantic patch to automate similar cleanups elsewhere. The rules would eliminate most NULL checks since strbuf.buf has been reliably non-NULL since 2007, pointing to strbuf_slopbuf when empty.

The discussion has focused on two exception cases. For parse_list_objects_filter(), Peff noted the check could potentially be removed but is harmless to keep. The more complex case involves strbuf_getwholeline()'s interaction with getdelim(), where Peff implemented Junio's suggested strbuf_attach() refactoring but found it didn't simplify the invariant handling. Junio now proposes a more radical approach - abandoning strbuf invariant restoration on error paths entirely. This represents experienced maintainers carefully evaluating edge cases in low-level string handling, with agreement on the overall direction but ongoing technical discussion about whether strbuf_getwholeline() should remain a special case.
