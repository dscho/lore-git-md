This is a refactoring series as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Patrick Steinhardt tackles the complex `setup.c` subsystem, which handles repository discovery and initialization. The series is the first of three planned steps to modernize this code.

The 18 patches systematically eliminate uses of the global `the_repository` variable from setup.c and related files, converting functions to take explicit `struct repository *` parameters instead. While most changes are mechanical, several patches (2/18, 3/18, 9/18, 10/18) require more careful attention as they involve logic changes beyond simple parameter passing.

The changes touch a wide surface area - 83 files modified with 393 insertions and 377 deletions. The series builds on Junio's integration branch from March 27 (5361983c07) and has a trivial merge conflict with the 'seen' branch. This appears to be a well-scoped, incremental step in the larger effort to remove global state from Git's codebase, with Patrick demonstrating his characteristic thoroughness in handling a core subsystem.
