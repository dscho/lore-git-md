This is a refactoring series targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically focusing on the setup subsystem. Patrick Steinhardt presents an 18-patch series that systematically eliminates uses of the global `the_repository` variable from setup.c and its callers.

The series represents the first of three planned steps to modernize the setup subsystem. While most changes are mechanical conversions to pass repository parameters explicitly, four patches (2/18, 3/18, 9/18, 10/18) require more careful attention as they involve logic changes beyond simple parameter passing. The changes touch a wide range of files (83 modified) but primarily focus on setup.c, with the bulk being straightforward call site updates.

The work builds on the broader project goal to eliminate implicit repository access through globals, making dependencies explicit. This series appears well-structured and follows the established pattern for `the_repository` removal patches, though the scope is ambitious given the setup subsystem's central role in repository initialization. The changes are based on recent master (5361983c07) and appear ready for review.
