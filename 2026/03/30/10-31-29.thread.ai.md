Git Rev News draft announcement for edition 133, organized by Christian Couder, is in collaborative review phase ahead of April 1st publication. Community feedback is solicited via GitHub PRs, email replies, or issue comments, with standard practice of CC'ing mentioned contributors. The newsletter summarizes February 2026 Git project developments including features, discussions, and community activity.

Key coverage includes a synthesis of the patch formatting issues discussion from February 2026, which examined problems affecting both email and non-email workflows when diffs in commit messages are processed by `patch(1)` or `git-am`, particularly with inconsistent indentation. Kristoffer Haugsbakk has provided positive feedback on this section, praising its handling of the complex topic and ability to coherently summarize multiple related subthreads - an endorsement of the draft's current treatment without requesting changes.

The draft remains open for additional contributions and feedback through standard channels before finalization. Christian Couder continues to coordinate this collaborative editing phase of the project's monthly development summary publication.
