The Git Rev News team, led by Christian Couder, is preparing edition 133 for April 1st publication and has shared a draft for community review. The newsletter covers recent Git project activity, with a particular focus on synthesizing complex discussions into accessible summaries.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk has responded positively to the draft's coverage of a February 2026 discussion about patch formatting issues that affect both email and non-email workflows. The newsletter successfully distilled the technical discussion about problems that can occur when diffs in commit messages are processed by `patch(1)` or `git-am`, particularly regarding indentation inconsistencies. Haugsbakk specifically praised how the draft handled this multi-threaded topic, indicating the current treatment is effective.

The review period remains open for additional feedback via GitHub PRs, email replies, or issue comments. The team continues to welcome contributions beyond this edition as well.
