The change is mechanical but touches several key areas: the function's declaration in `repository.h`, its implementation in `setup.c`, and two call sites in `list-objects-filter-options.c` (for partial clone support) and `worktree.c` (for relative worktree paths). The patch maintains all existing behavior while making the repository dependency explicit.

Each modified caller now passes `the_repository` explicitly, pushing the global variable usage one level higher in the call stack. The changes are small (6 lines added, 6 removed across 4 files) and follow the established pattern of this series. The patch continues the careful, incremental approach seen in previous patches, with no functional changes beyond the parameter passing.
