The thread concluded that the current strict parsing behavior (alphanumeric + hyphen only) should remain but be better documented.

It removes the comparison to email headers while keeping the line-folding example, since the email analogy was causing confusion about what rules apply. The change is minimal but important for setting up the next patch which will explicitly document the allowed character set.

The patch appears well-targeted, directly addressing the documentation gap that led to the original bug report. The author has done their homework, tracing the RFC 822 comparison back to its origins in the `--fixes` trailer topic.
