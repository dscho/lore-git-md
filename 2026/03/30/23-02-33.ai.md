This is a refactoring patch in the xdiff subsystem, part of Ezekiel Newren's series to improve code clarity in `xdl_cleanup_records()`. The patch converts nested ternary operations to explicit if/else statements when setting the `action1` and `action2` arrays, making the decision logic for record handling (DISCARD, KEEP, or INVESTIGATE) more readable while maintaining identical behavior.

Maintainer Junio C Hamano approves the change, noting he appreciates this type of readability improvement. The modification follows the series' pattern of enhancing maintainability without altering functionality, and comes after earlier patches in the series have already addressed test failures and build warnings. With all technical issues resolved in previous iterations, this appears to be a straightforward polish that maintains the code's behavior while making it easier to understand.
