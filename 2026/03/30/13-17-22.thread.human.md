Patrick Steinhardt has completed an 18-patch series refactoring `setup.c` to remove implicit use of the `the_repository` global variable, converting functions to take explicit `struct repository *` parameters instead. The changes systematically update repository initialization paths across 90+ files, including core commands like `git init`, `git clone`, and `git grep`, while maintaining all existing behavior. 

Karthik Nayak has reviewed patch 2/18, which deals with `is_inside_worktree()`, confirming the technical approach of removing the caching mechanism and noting the careful handling of relative paths via `realpath()`. His brief but substantive review demonstrates the project's thorough approach to these mechanical conversions, with attention to edge cases even in non-critical paths. 

The series represents a significant milestone in the long-running `the_repository` removal effort championed by René Scharfe, building on Junio Hamano's March 27 integration branch. While most of the series has gone unreviewed so far - likely due to its mechanical nature - Karthik's engagement suggests the changes are receiving appropriate scrutiny where needed. The work remains transparent to end users while making the codebase more maintainable.
