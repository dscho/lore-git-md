Patrick Steinhardt's 18-patch series refactoring `setup.c` to remove global state dependencies has reached a significant milestone, with Karthik Nayak's final approval and Junio C Hamano's endorsement of the architectural direction. The series converts core initialization paths to use explicit `struct repository *` parameters while maintaining existing behavior, affecting over 90 files. This completes the first phase of the long-running `the_repository` elimination effort.

Elijah Newren has now weighed in with substantive technical feedback on one of the patches. While confirming the correctness of removing the `work_tree_config_is_bogus` global, he points out that another global (`initialized`) remains in `setup_work_tree()`. This creates an architectural limitation preventing proper support for multiple worktree initialization within a single process. His expert review suggests the refactoring should eventually move this flag into `struct repository` as well, though this doesn't block the current changes.

The thread shows how mechanical refactoring is building toward larger structural improvements, with maintainer approval for splitting `setup.c` into separate discovery and configuration components in future work. The discussion has progressed from code-level reviews to considering broader implications of the architectural changes.
