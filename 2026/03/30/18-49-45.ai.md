This is a bugfix patch series addressing incorrect behavior in `receive.denyCurrentBranch=updateInstead` when pushing to bare repositories with worktrees. The email is a response to Junio Hamano's feedback on the cover letter, clarifying that the series was intentionally sent as a reply to Runxi Yu's original bug report (linked in the response) to maintain thread context.

Pablo Sabater acknowledges Junio's point that the cover letter could be clearer for readers who don't follow the full thread, and provides the direct link to Runxi Yu's initial report that identified the issue. The problem occurs when pushing to a bare repository with worktrees where the bare HEAD points to an unborn branch - Git incorrectly rejects pushes even when the worktree is clean.

The series consists of three patches: Runxi Yu's test case demonstrating the issue, Pablo's cleanup of test infrastructure, and the core solution that modifies `builtin/receive-pack.c` to check the worktree's HEAD rather than the bare repository's HEAD. The changes appear well-scoped and maintain backward compatibility while fixing this specific edge case. Junio has indicated he will review the technical content of the patches later.
