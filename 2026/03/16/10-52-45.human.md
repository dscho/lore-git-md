This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing build system improvements thread, where Phillip Wood and Patrick Steinhardt discuss precompiled header (PCH) compatibility with the reftable backend. Phillip had raised concerns about potential inconsistencies between builds with and without PCH in the reftable code, noting that the reftable library intentionally avoids git-compat-util.h dependencies.

In this response, Phillip accepts Patrick's pragmatic approach of monitoring for issues rather than preemptively splitting out compat/posix.h as a separate PCH. He acknowledges that while getting feedback via CI rather than local compilation isn't ideal, the problem is not expected to be common. This marks a resolution to the specific technical concern about PCH/reftable interaction, allowing the series to move forward with its current approach.
