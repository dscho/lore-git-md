This is a refactoring patch series that has now been accepted by the maintainer. Junio C Hamano has reviewed Deveshi Dwivedi's two-patch series addressing the anti-pattern of passing `struct strbuf` by value rather than by pointer, and indicates it will be queued for merging.

The series builds on prior work by Jeff King who identified this issue in `write_worktree_linking_files()`. The first patch introduces a Coccinelle semantic rule to detect such cases, while the second fixes the one remaining instance found in `builtin/stash.c` (changing `show_stash()` to take a pointer). 

Junio's brief response confirms the technical merit of the approach and the quality of implementation. This marks the successful conclusion of this small but important code quality improvement, which will help prevent subtle bugs from creeping in where `strbuf` parameters are incorrectly passed by value. The Coccinelle rule will serve as ongoing safeguard against this anti-pattern.
