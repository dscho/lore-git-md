This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's 8-patch series reorganizing Git's build infrastructure. The patch moves the Unicode data update script from `contrib/` to the new `tools/` directory, continuing the series' goal of consolidating developer-facing tooling.

The `update-unicode.sh` script is used to refresh Git's Unicode character data when new versions are released. Patrick argues this belongs with other core development tools rather than in `contrib/`, which is meant for optional add-ons. The change is purely organizational - the script's functionality remains identical, just in a new location that better reflects its role in the development workflow.

This is a straightforward mechanical change that follows the series' established pattern. The patch has no functional impact beyond file paths, and like the earlier parts of the series, it appears uncontroversial and ready for merging. The series as a whole is nearing completion, with all technical concerns addressed in v2.
