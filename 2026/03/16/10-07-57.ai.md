Build system reorganization patch moving Coccinelle infrastructure from contrib/ to tools/. This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's 8-part series to consolidate build tooling and add precompiled header support.

The patch relocates all Coccinelle-related files (semantic patches, test cases, and helper scripts) from contrib/coccinelle/ to the new tools/coccinelle/ directory. The justification is that Coccinelle has become an integral part of Git's build infrastructure, used both in CI and by developers to detect antipatterns, making its placement in contrib/ misleading since that directory is meant for optional add-ons.

Key changes include:
- Moving 20+ .cocci files and their test cases
- Updating Makefile references to point to the new location
- Adjusting the spatchcache helper script's documentation
- Adding the coccinelle subdirectory to tools/meson.build
- Removing coccinelle from contrib/meson.build

The patch is purely mechanical - no functional changes to the Coccinelle rules or their behavior. This continues the effort started in an earlier cleanup of the contrib/ directory, with Coccinelle being the first significant component to move to the new home. The relocation may impact Junio Hamano's Coccinelle workflow, but this is being addressed as part of the overall series discussion rather than in this specific patch.
