Jiang Xin notes that Gitk's simpler codebase (single source file) makes the full location removal clearly preferable, while Git's more complex codebase may benefit from keeping filenames in some cases.

Key technical points include:
- Proposal to rename filters to better match gettext's `msgcat` terminology (`gettext-no-location` and `gettext-no-line-number`)
