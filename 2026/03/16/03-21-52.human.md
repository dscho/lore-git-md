The email proposes renaming the filters to better match gettext's terminology, suggesting `gettext-no-location` (removes both filenames and line numbers) and `gettext-no-line-number` (keeps filenames). This would replace the current `gettext-no-file-no-location` naming scheme. Jiang Xin seeks feedback on this change before incorporating it in v4 of his patch series.
