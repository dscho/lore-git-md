This is part 2/8 of Patrick Steinhardt's build system improvement series, focusing on infrastructure reorganization. The patch moves the Coccinelle semantic patch infrastructure from `contrib/` to the new `tools/` directory, reflecting its status as a core build tool rather than optional contributor material.

The change updates all references to the Coccinelle files across the build system - in the Makefile, meson.build, and CI scripts. The patch preserves all existing Coccinelle rules and test files exactly as they were, only changing their location. This includes the spatchcache helper script and various semantic patches like `the_repository.cocci` and `flex_alloc.cocci`.

The justification is straightforward: Coccinelle has become an integral part of Git's build and CI infrastructure, used to detect antipatterns and enforce coding standards. Its previous location in `contrib/` no longer matches its importance. The change is mechanical but thorough, touching all build systems (Makefile, CMake, Meson) to maintain consistency.

This patch follows the series' first step (creating the empty `tools/` directory) and precedes further moves of build-related scripts. It's a well-executed piece of infrastructure cleanup that should have minimal impact beyond the path changes, though Junio has expressed some concern about potential workflow impacts that may need monitoring.
