This thread discusses a 2-part series by Justin Tobler introducing a new `odb_source_files_try()` helper function as part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The series aims to gracefully handle non-files object sources (like future alternative backends) by returning NULL rather than aborting when encountering them, converting 22 call sites across the codebase to use this safer approach.

The initial patches appeared uncontroversial, providing infrastructure for the larger ODB abstraction work led by Patrick Steinhardt. However, Patrick has now raised substantive concerns about the approach. While acknowledging the mechanical correctness of the changes, he questions whether silently skipping non-files backends is the right architectural direction. He suggests that the current BUG()-on-unknown behavior might actually be preferable until proper abstraction can be implemented, as it forces future developers to explicitly handle alternate backends rather than papering over the issue.

As the primary driver of the ODB abstraction effort, Patrick's feedback carries significant weight and suggests the series may need reconsideration. The core tension appears to be between making incremental progress versus ensuring the changes properly support the long-term architectural goals. This will likely require further discussion about whether these conversions represent genuine abstraction or just a compatibility shim, and how they align with the project's direction for object storage.
