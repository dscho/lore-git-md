This is an update on Taylor Blau's MIDX repacking optimization series, which is now ready to progress to the 'next' integration branch. The 17-patch series improves Git's multi-pack-index handling with incremental repacking capabilities, particularly around reachability bitmaps during compaction.

The series has gone through several iterations, with Jeff King (Peff) providing review feedback that led to a v3 addressing minor issues like outdated comments and interface naming. Taylor confirms these have been resolved and the changes are now ready for merging. The work builds on previous MIDX improvements to enable more efficient repacking of object storage while maintaining bitmap compatibility.

This represents a significant but incremental improvement to Git's packfile handling, continuing Taylor's ongoing work in this area. The changes are well-vetted through multiple rounds of review and appear technically sound, though as with any storage-layer modification, real-world testing in 'next' may surface additional edge cases. The series is part of Git's broader effort to optimize large repository performance.
