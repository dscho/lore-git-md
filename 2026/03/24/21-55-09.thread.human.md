This thread centers on Justin Tobler's 4-patch series to make `git fast-import`'s signature handling more robust and consistent between commits and tags. The series introduces three modes (`strip-if-invalid`, `sign-if-invalid`, and `abort-if-invalid`) for both `--signed-commits` and `--signed-tags`, building on recent work to improve signature validation.

Junio C Hamano raised an important point during review: while `fast-import` currently checks invalid signature modes early during option parsing, `fast-export` waits until encountering signed commits before failing. Justin has acknowledged this inconsistency and proposes to align the behavior by moving validation earlier in `fast-export`'s option parsing. He favors modifying `parse_opt_sign_mode()` directly since both tools will soon support the same modes, with only `fast-export` needing to restrict some of them.

The technical work is largely complete, with comprehensive test coverage in t9305 and t9306 verifying all three behaviors across different signature types. The remaining task is to resolve this cross-tool consistency issue before the series can be merged. Justin's constructive response indicates this will be addressed in a revision.
