This thread continues Olamide Caleb Bello's work on removing global variables as part of the broader `the_repository` elimination effort. The series, now up to seven patches in its second version, systematically migrates repository-specific configurations from global variables to per-repository storage in `struct repo_config_values`. 

The latest addition targets the `sparse_expect_files_outside_of_patterns` variable used in sparse-checkout operations, following the same proven pattern as previous conversions. The variable is moved into `struct repo_config_values`, initialized with a default value, and all references are updated to use the repository-specific version. This change affects three files (environment.c, environment.h, and sparse-index.c) and maintains existing behavior while improving repository isolation.

Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi have reviewed the new patch, as they did with the earlier additions covering `core.sparsecheckoutcone` and `core.precomposeunicode`. The series as a whole now addresses seven configuration areas across nearly 50 files, touching subsystems from compression settings to platform-specific filename handling. While the changes are mechanical and follow established patterns, they warrant careful review to ensure no subtle behavioral changes, particularly in sparse-checkout operations and platform-specific cases. The work aligns with multiple ongoing efforts including René Scharfe's global variable removal and Derrick Stolee's sparse-checkout optimizations.
