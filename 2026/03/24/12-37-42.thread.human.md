This thread documents the ongoing effort to eliminate Git's `the_repository` global variable by migrating configuration settings into repository-specific storage. The current patch series (now at v4) systematically moves performance-critical configurations while maintaining their eager parsing behavior to avoid slowdowns in hot paths.

Bello Olamide has submitted part 7 of 8, which migrates the `sparse.expectfilesoutsideofpatterns` setting - a sparse-checkout related configuration that controls whether Git should warn about files existing outside the specified patterns. Like previous parts in the series, this change touches a small number of files (environment.c, environment.h, sparse-index.c) while preserving the eager parsing optimization needed for this fundamental setting. The patch follows the established pattern from earlier migrations of core.trustctime, compression levels, and Unicode handling.

The series has received positive reviews from Christian Couder, Usman Akinyemi, and others, with maintainer Junio Hamano indicating readiness to merge once the final confirmations are in. Only one patch remains after this one to complete the series. While the changes are mechanical, they represent important progress in the broader `the_repository` removal effort led by René Scharfe, which aims to eliminate cross-repository state leakage. The work connects to several other ongoing initiatives including sparse-checkout improvements and platform compatibility work.
