This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the `core.precomposeunicode` configuration. The patch moves the `precomposed_unicode` global variable into `struct repo_config_values` to make it repository-specific rather than process-wide.

The change affects Unicode filename normalization behavior on macOS and other platforms where decomposed Unicode filenames are common. The patch updates all access points to use the repository-specific storage, including the probe function that detects whether the filesystem prefers precomposed Unicode, the string conversion helper, and directory reading operations. The upload-pack config callback is also updated to set the repository-specific value.

This appears to be a straightforward mechanical conversion following the established pattern for migrating repository-dependent globals. The changes are limited to four files and maintain all existing behavior while eliminating another piece of cross-repository state. The patch continues Olamide Caleb Bello's series under Christian Couder's mentorship, with this being the fifth of eight patches in v2 of the series.
