This is part 8 of Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series refactoring Git's object database (ODB) subsystem to enable pluggable storage backends. The patch makes the `close()` operation pluggable by introducing a callback in `struct odb_source`.

The discussion focuses on whether to add a NULL check in the new `odb_source_close()` wrapper function. Justin Tobler suggests adding a `BUG()` or `ASSERT()` for NULL sources, while Patrick Steinhardt argues against it, noting this would be inconsistent with other wrapper functions in the series and that NULL checks are more appropriate for free functions than close operations.

This continues the pattern seen throughout the series of carefully considering API design choices, with Patrick maintaining consistency with the established approach while engaging with reviewer feedback. The exchange demonstrates the attention to detail in this architectural refactoring, where even small API decisions are scrutinized for their long-term implications.
