This is a technical discussion about performance optimizations in `git-upload-pack`, specifically around reducing write syscalls during high-concurrency operations. Jeff King (Peff) is responding to Junio C Hamano's concerns about integer overflow handling in the `writev()` compatibility wrapper that was introduced in Patrick Steinhardt's patch series.

This represents a potential alternative direction for the performance optimization work, though it would require reworking parts of the packet-writing infrastructure.
