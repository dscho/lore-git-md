This is a performance optimization discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's series reducing write syscalls in `git-upload-pack`. Jeff King (Peff) provides detailed feedback on the buffering strategy for packfile data writes.

Peff questions the 2/3 buffer fill threshold as arbitrary, noting it may not optimally handle varying input patterns from `pack-objects`. The review suggests two potential improvements: 1) sending buffered data alongside keepalives to reduce header overhead, though Peff notes this may have limited impact since keepalives mostly occur before pack data starts, and 2) reconsidering the buffer fill strategy to better handle edge cases where input patterns don't align with the 2/3 threshold.

This continues the thread's technical debate about optimal buffering strategies, with Peff maintaining a preference for simplicity while recognizing the performance benefits of the approach.
