This is a bugfix patch in Jeff King's series addressing memory leaks in pack index mmap handling. The patch refactors `check_connected()` to delay opening a pack index until just before it's needed, avoiding the need to clean it up in early-exit paths. Jacob Keller has reviewed and approved the change.

The change moves the pack index initialization later in the function, after the rev-list process has started, since we know we'll need to clean up that process anyway. This eliminates some cleanup complexity while maintaining the same functionality. Jeff notes there are potential further optimizations (like skipping rev-list entirely for self-contained packs) but keeps them out of scope for this leak-focused series.

The patch demonstrates careful attention to resource lifecycle management, a hallmark of Jeff's work. With Jacob's review, this appears ready for inclusion in the series which is already well-tested and reviewed. The change is a focused improvement that doesn't introduce new complexity while solving a real resource management issue.
