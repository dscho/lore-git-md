This is a documentation patch that adds guidance to Git's "SubmittingPatches" document. Justin Tobler proposes two new best practices for contributors:

1. Checking for prior discussions or related work before submitting patches, to avoid duplication and enable coordination with others working in the same area.

2. Allowing time for reviewers to respond before sending new patch versions, to gather broader feedback and reduce churn from rapid iterations.

The changes are straightforward additions to the existing document, inserted in logical places within the patch submission workflow section. The new text is clear and well-written, matching the tone of the surrounding documentation. This appears to be a non-controversial improvement to the contributor guidelines, though it may generate some discussion about whether these points need to be called out explicitly (versus being considered general good practice).
