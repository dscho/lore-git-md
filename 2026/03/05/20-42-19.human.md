Junio C Hamano raises an important design question about Patrick Steinhardt's completed ODB abstraction series, specifically regarding callback requirements in the new pluggable interface. While the series has already been reviewed and acked, Junio notes that the documentation doesn't clearly specify which callbacks in `struct odb_source` are mandatory versus optional for alternative backends.

The concern centers on potential segfaults if future backends (like read-only implementations) omit certain callbacks that the current wrapper functions dereference directly. Junio suggests two possible solutions: either document mandatory callbacks in the header, or modify the wrapper functions to handle NULL pointers (with `BUG()` for required functions or error returns for optional ones).

This is a maintainer-level question about interface design rather than a blocking issue - the series remains technically sound and ready for integration. However, it highlights an important consideration for future backend implementers that wasn't fully addressed in the initial design. The thread shows the project's attention to long-term maintainability even after technical approval.
