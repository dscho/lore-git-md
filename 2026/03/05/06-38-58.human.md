This is a patch in the fsmonitor series that introduces a pre-exec callback mechanism for child processes, addressing process isolation concerns. The patch responds to feedback from Junio Hamano about balancing specific needs with generalizability.

The change restores a more targeted approach to file descriptor cleanup between fork and exec, stepping back from an earlier attempt at a fully generic callback mechanism. This maintains the immediate goal of clean process isolation for fsmonitor while leaving room for future generalization if needed.

The patch is part of a mature series (v9 of 12) that has already addressed numerous technical challenges around cross-platform fsmonitor support. This particular iteration focuses narrowly on the process management aspect, showing responsiveness to maintainer feedback while keeping the scope contained. The series as a whole appears production-ready, with this being one of the final refinements before integration.
