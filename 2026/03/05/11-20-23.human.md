CI/build system patch completing a series to update GitLab CI infrastructure for macOS 15. This is the third and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing the transition from version 14 to 15 of the macOS runners used in Git's CI pipeline.

The patch simply updates the image references in `.gitlab-ci.yml` from `macos-14-xcode-15` to `macos-15-xcode-16`, keeping the same test matrix structure (clang, reftable, and meson builds). This change follows the first two patches which handled the technical work needed to make Git's builds compatible with macOS 15's problematic iconv implementation.

The series appears well-structured and complete - the first patch simplified Meson's iconv handling, the second added comprehensive testing workaround for macOS 15's iconv issues, and this final piece updates the CI configuration to actually use the new platform. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, being a necessary maintenance update to keep CI working as the underlying platform evolves.
