This is a final approval for a two-patch series that introduces a type-safe cleanup API for oidmap structures. The series adds `oidmap_clear_with_free()` with explicit callbacks, replacing the old boolean-based `oidmap_clear()` that implicitly assumed `free()`. The patches convert rev-list's missing_objects and odb's replace_map to use the new API, demonstrating its value by handling cleanup of both path strings and entry structs through callbacks.

Patrick Steinhardt (a key contributor to Git's object storage layer) gives his approval, noting the v3 changes meet all requirements. The series has been narrowed from its original scope - list-objects-filter and sequencer conversions were dropped after maintainer feedback. Comprehensive test coverage was added in this iteration, and the rev-list patch in particular shows the API's type-safety benefits by replacing manual path cleanup. With both conversions now meeting maintainer requirements and all outstanding questions resolved, the series appears ready for merging.
