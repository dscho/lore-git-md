This is a review ack for the final version of Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series abstracting Git's object database (ODB) to support pluggable storage backends. The series represents the completion of a major architectural change to Git's core object storage layer, enabling future development of alternative backends while maintaining the existing files-based implementation.
