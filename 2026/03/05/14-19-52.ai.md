This is part 12/17 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Git's object database (ODB) subsystem pluggable. The patch converts the `freshen_object()` operation to use the callback mechanism being established across the series.

The change moves the freshening logic from odb.c into the files backend implementation, adding a new `freshen_object` callback to the `struct odb_source` vtable. The files backend maintains the existing behavior of checking both packed and loose objects. The core ODB function now simply iterates through sources and dispatches to their callbacks.

This follows the established pattern of the series - identifying a specific ODB operation, extracting its backend-specific logic, and moving it behind a callback interface. The implementation is straightforward and maintains all existing functionality while progressing the architectural goal of making backends interchangeable.

Justin Tobler has been consistently reviewing this series, and there are no new concerns raised about this particular patch. The change builds cleanly on the previous work and keeps the series moving toward its goal of full ODB abstraction.
