This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's object database (ODB) subsystem to support pluggable storage backends. Patch 12/17 makes the `freshen_object()` function pluggable by introducing a new callback in the `struct odb_source` vtable.

The change moves the freshening logic from the core ODB layer into backend-specific implementations, following the pattern established in earlier patches of this series. The files backend maintains its existing behavior of checking both packed and loose objects. The patch includes thorough documentation of the new interface in `odb/source.h`.

This continues the systematic conversion of ODB operations to callback-based dispatch that the series has been building. The implementation appears clean and well-contained, with Justin Tobler continuing to provide technical review. The change fits naturally into the larger architectural effort while maintaining backward compatibility.
