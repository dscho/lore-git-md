Performance optimization discussion in the `git-upload-pack` series.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that reduces lock contention in `git-upload-pack` by buffering packfile data. The patch aims to minimize write syscalls by waiting until the buffer is 2/3 full before flushing, though Peff questions whether this threshold is optimal. He notes the buffering behavior depends on upstream patterns and could lead to suboptimal read/write alternation in some cases.

Peff remains somewhat skeptical about buffering in `upload-pack` due to subtle interactions between buffering layers, though he made similar points in v1 without changing Patrick's approach. He suggests an additional optimization: bundling buffered data with keepalive packets when possible, though acknowledges this may provide limited benefit since keepalives typically occur before pack data starts flowing.

The exchange represents ongoing refinement of the performance optimization series, with Peff providing detailed technical feedback while recognizing the core approach appears sound. His comments focus on edge cases and potential improvements rather than fundamental objections to the patch.
