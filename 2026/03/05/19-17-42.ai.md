This is the final approval for a two-patch series introducing type-safe cleanup for oidmap structures. The series has been narrowed down from its original five-patch scope based on maintainer feedback, now focusing only on converting rev-list's missing_objects and odb's replace_map to use the new `oidmap_clear_with_free()` API.

The key technical change is the introduction of a callback-based cleanup mechanism that properly handles both the map entries and their associated resources (like path strings in the rev-list case). The rev-list conversion demonstrates this by replacing manual cleanup with a dedicated `missing_objects_map_entry_free` callback. The odb conversion similarly updates the replace_map handling.

Maintainer Junio Hamano confirms the series is ready for merging after all requested changes were incorporated in v3, including renaming the callback function and adding comprehensive test coverage. Patrick Steinhardt had previously driven the scope reduction to focus on these two clear-value conversions. The series now stands approved for inclusion in the 'next' integration branch, marking the successful conclusion of this API improvement effort.
