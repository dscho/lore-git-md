This is a feature proposal (v3) from Pablo Sabater, part of their GSoC project, introducing a `--graph-lane-limit` option to constrain the width of `git log --graph` output. The patch series addresses the problem of overly wide commit histories by truncating excess graph lanes while preserving key commit markers.

The v3 iteration responds to Junio Hamano's feedback by renaming the option from `--max-columns` to `--graph-lane-limit` and making it implicitly enable `--graph`. However, Johannes Sixt has raised new concerns about this implicit behavior potentially complicating future configuration options. The implementation adds a `graph_max_columns` field to `struct rev_info` and modifies graph.c to replace excess lanes with '.' characters while showing '*' for commits on the first hidden lane.

The series includes three patches: the core implementation, visual truncation logic, and documentation/tests. The author identifies two open design questions about whether to suppress redundant merge/collapse lanes in limited graphs and how to handle information loss from hidden lanes. The patch appears technically sound with thorough test coverage, though the naming and implicit behavior questions may require another revision to reconcile the competing design inputs from reviewers.
