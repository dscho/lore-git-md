Review comment on backfill argument validation patch.

He also clarifies the correct syntax for negated matches (`test_grep !` rather than `! grep`). This is a minor but thoughtful suggestion that follows Git's test suite best practices.

The comment indicates the patch is otherwise sound and doesn't raise any new concerns about the core functionality. Wood's participation here is characteristic of his contributions - focused on subtle behavioral correctness and test quality, particularly around the test infrastructure itself.
