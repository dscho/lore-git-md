Junio now proposes an alternative to keeping the workaround patch: adding a 5-line comment in `compat/regcomp_enhanced.c` documenting the issue and linking to the email thread. This preserves the knowledge for when the issue resurfaces in future CI updates, while avoiding baking in an unneeded workaround. The approach demonstrates Git's preference for minimal, well-documented solutions over proactive workarounds when issues are currently mitigated.
