Derrick Stolee raises an important consideration about Siddharth Shrimali's v3 to make `git backfill` reject unexpected arguments. This is a thoughtful architectural consideration from someone familiar with the codebase's direction. The patch itself remains merge-ready from a technical standpoint, but may need to be held briefly to ensure alignment with the parallel development effort.
