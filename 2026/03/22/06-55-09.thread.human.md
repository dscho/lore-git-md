Mateo Patino, a new contributor and GSoC applicant, has proposed reviving the idea of a `STRBUF_INIT_CONST` macro to initialize `strbuf` structures with constant strings without heap allocation. This optimization has been discussed three times before (2019, 2020, 2021) but never merged, most recently noted by Patrick Steinhardt as a #leftoverbit in 2025. 

While the technical motivation is clear - avoiding unnecessary allocations for constant strings - the proposal currently lacks concrete performance data or compelling use cases where this would make a measurable difference. The thread's future direction will depend on whether maintainers see value in pursuing this optimization after its previous stalled attempts, or if they consider the approach not worth the complexity given Git's actual performance characteristics.
