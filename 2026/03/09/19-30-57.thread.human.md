This thread discusses a proposal to refactor the experimental `git replay` command's interface. Toon Claes initiated the discussion with an RFC patch that would replace the current mutually-exclusive options (`--onto`, `--advance`, `--revert`) with three subcommands (`rebase`, `pick`, `revert`), introducing a consistent `--ref` option across all subcommands while maintaining backward compatibility with `--onto` for the rebase case.

Justin Tobler has raised several concerns about the proposal. He questions whether the rebase subcommand's continued reliance on `--onto` actually improves clarity, finds the subcommand-plus-required-option pattern awkward, and notes the backward compatibility implications (though acknowledges this may be acceptable for an experimental command). He suggests exploring positional arguments as a potentially more intuitive alternative.

The discussion focuses on design tradeoffs for this low-level alternative to rebase that works with bare repositories. Key files affected include `builtin/replay.c`, documentation, and tests. The thread remains in the RFC stage, with Toon's original patch still marked as needing splitting once the design direction is settled.
