This thread documents the completion of a three-patch series that moves Git's `trust_executable_bit` setting from global to repository-scoped storage, part of the ongoing effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The setting, which controls how Git handles executable bits when applying patches and computing diffs, is now properly stored in `struct repo_settings` and initialized via `prepare_repo_settings()`.

The final patch in the series removes the last vestiges of global handling by transitioning the lazy-loading of `core.filemode` configuration into the repository settings machinery. All call sites have been updated to reference the setting through `the_repository->settings` (a transitional step until callers can be further refactored to take explicit repository parameter). The changes follow the established pattern for such conversions and appear uncontroversial, with previous rounds of review feedback about initialization and formatting having been addressed in earlier iterations.

As a mechanical change that aligns with Git's architectural direction, this series seems ready for merging into the larger `the_repository` removal effort. The work exemplifies the project's multi-year campaign to eliminate global state by making configuration properly repository-scoped.
