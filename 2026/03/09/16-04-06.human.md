CI/build system update for macOS 15 compatibility in GitLab CI.

Justin Tobler gives a straightforward ack to Patrick Steinhardt's patch updating GitLab CI to use macOS 15 images (macos-15-xcode-16) instead of the deprecated macOS 14 ones. This is the final patch in a series that first addressed technical issues with iconv behavior on macOS 15 across both Makefile and Meson builds. The change affects three test jobs (osx-clang, osx-reftable, and osx-meson) and follows the team's careful approach of fixing platform-specific issues before updating CI configurations. With Justin's review, this appears ready for merging as part of the ongoing maintenance of Git's CI infrastructure.
