Junio C Hamano has updated the SubmittingPatches documentation to formalize expectations around patch series hygiene during review. The new guidance explicitly discourages sending incremental "fixup" patches in follow-up iterations, instead encouraging contributors to rewrite their series history to incorporate fixes cleanly - presenting each new version as if it were correct from the start. This codifies a long-standing maintainer preference that Junio has articulated in reviews over the years, as referenced in the commit message's citations of prior discussions.

The change is a straightforward addition to the contribution guidelines, adding one paragraph that makes clear this is an expected practice rather than optional advice. As a documentation patch from the maintainer about core workflow expectations, it will likely be merged without debate. The update serves both to educate new contributors and reinforce standards for established ones, emphasizing tools like interactive rebase for maintaining clean patch histories throughout the review process.
