This is a follow-up discussion about a bugfix targeting the diff machinery's `-U` option handling. Tian Yuchen investigates Junio Hamano's earlier patch that `git show -U` (without a number) incorrectly accepts the option when it should require a numerical value, matching the behavior of `-Unan` which properly errors out.

Yuchen confirms the patch would break test 157 in t4013, which explicitly relies on the current lenient behavior where `-U` defaults to 3 lines of context like `-u`. He notes this contradicts the documentation in diff-context-options.adoc, which states `-U` requires a numerical value. The email highlights the tension between fixing an inconsistency and maintaining backward compatibility with tests (and potentially scripts) that depend on the current behavior.

The discussion illustrates a common dilemma in mature projects - whether to correct long-standing but technically incorrect behavior that may have become relied upon. Yuchen's tone is observational rather than prescriptive, noting how such quirks can become entrenched in workflows. The thread remains unresolved regarding whether to prioritize correctness or compatibility in this case.
