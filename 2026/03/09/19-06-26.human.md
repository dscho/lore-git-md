This is an update on Ezekiel Newren's paused xdiff refactoring series, which aims to restructure xdiff internals for future optimizations and Rust compatibility. The series was previously blocked by merge conflicts with Phillip Wood's smaller xdiff cleanups that were merged in February 2026.

Junio Hamano has now inquired about the status of the series, noting that the conflicting changes have been integrated and suggesting a rebase. Ezekiel Newren responds that he plans to rebase with significant changes, indicating the next version will differ substantially from the original.

The original series faced several technical concerns that remain unresolved - particularly around the IVec implementation's safety, efficiency claims of certain changes, and unclear connections to Rust interoperability goals. These issues will need to be addressed in the revised version. The thread shows the project's careful approach to refactoring performance-critical subsystems while balancing competing technical priorities.
