This is a refactoring patch in Adrian Ratiu's hook configuration series, specifically addressing the internal storage of hook configuration data. The patch replaces the direct `strmap` pointer in `struct repository` with a named `struct hook_config_cache` wrapper, and changes the string storage to use a dedicated `struct hook_config_cache_entry` instead of raw `char*` pointers.

Junio Hamano's review raises a thoughtful objection to the wrapper struct, noting that while the entry-level struct makes clear sense for future extensibility, the top-level cache wrapper may be adding sufficient value to justify its indirection. He suggests keeping the cache as a bare `strmap` since all the anticipated growth is happening at the entry level.

The technical changes touch `hook.c`, `hook.h`, and `repository.h`, introducing the new structs and updating all related access patterns. The patch maintains identical behavior while preparing for future metadata storage needs - a classic preparatory refactoring. Junio's feedback indicates this might be slightly over-engineered at the cache level, though the core review is positive about the direction.

This patch sits in the middle of Adrian's 10-part series that's already been rebased and queued for integration, with Junio's comments representing final polish rather than blocking concerns. The discussion reflects Git's careful attention to API design even for internal structures, with maintainers weighing future flexibility against current complexity.
