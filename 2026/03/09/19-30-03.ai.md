Test modernization follow-up addressing incorrect application of test_path_exists helper. Eric Sunshine points out that the four changes in t0004-unwritable.sh are inappropriate because they modify test_lazy_prereq conditions rather than test assertions. The original patch from PRASHANT S BISHT mechanically replaced all `test -e` calls with `test_path_exists`, but these particular instances are control-flow checks for prerequisite testing, not assertions about expected state. 

Sunshine references the SoC microproject guidelines which explicitly caution against this conversion in control-flow contexts. He suggests the author look for other, more suitable instances of `test -e` to modernize in different test scripts. This is a typical review interaction in the test modernization effort, where experienced contributors help newer participants understand the distinction between assertion helpers and control-flow constructs in the test suite.
