This is a follow-up discussion in an ongoing thread about converting the `trust_executable_bit` global variable to a repository-scoped setting as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. 

Tian Yuchen points out two process issues with Dorna Raj Gyawali's v2 patch - the maintainer wasn't CC'd on the update, and reviews from the previous iteration weren't addressed before sending the new version. More substantively, Yuchen identifies a technical concern about the implementation: calling `prepare_repo_settings()` directly in the config parser (`git_default_core_config()`) may undermine the lazy-loading design of repository settings. They suggest moving the config reading into `repo-settings.c` instead.

The exchange highlights the careful balance required when refactoring global variables - not just the mechanical conversion, but also maintaining the intended initialization patterns. While the thread shows progress on eliminating the global, it surfaces deeper questions about how repository settings should properly be initialized from configuration. The discussion remains constructive, with Yuchen providing clear technical guidance while also helping the contributor follow project norms.
