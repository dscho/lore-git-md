This is a style cleanup patch in Adrian Ratiu's ongoing hook configuration series, addressing minor issues in the `git hook list` command. Eric Sunshine points out that the error message being fixed should also drop its trailing period to match Git's style guidelines, which avoid punctuation in error messages. The patch is part of a larger 10-part series that's already been through several rounds of review, with Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt having provided earlier feedback. This particular email shows the kind of polish that happens late in a patch series, where reviewers catch small style nits before final integration.
