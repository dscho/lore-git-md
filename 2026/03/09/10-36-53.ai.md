This is the final patch in Phillip Wood and Li Chen's series adding `--trailer` support to `git rebase`, now in its 8th iteration. The patch refactors trailer handling to separate the core logic from I/O operations, making it reusable across commands.

This separation enables the subsequent move of the core logic to trailer.c where it can be shared with the rebase implementation.
