This is a follow-up in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing the conversion of the `trust_executable_bit` global to a repository-scoped setting.

Drona Raj Gyawali acknowledges Tian Yuchen's review of their v2 patch, which identified an architectural issue where the patch was calling `prepare_repo_settings()` during global config parsing - violating Git's repository settings lazy-loading pattern. The author agrees with the critique and plans to move the config handling to `repo-settings.c` using `repo_config_get_bool()` in the next iteration.

The exchange demonstrates the careful balance required when converting globals to repository-scoped variables - even straightforward mechanical conversions need to respect deeper architectural patterns. As a new contributor, Gyawali shows good responsiveness to feedback on this non-trivial aspect of Git's internals. The thread suggests this will need at least one more iteration to properly handle the config loading semantics.
