This is a discussion about refactoring editor configuration as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Karthik Nayak weighs in on the ongoing debate about whether `core.editor` should remain process-global (as in the current patch series) or become repository-specific (as advocated by Burak Kaan Karaçay and Tian Yuchen).

He cautions against prematurely bloating `struct repository` without careful consideration of the tradeoffs.

The email highlights the tension between immediate practical concerns (users rarely need per-repo editors) and long-term architectural goals (clean libification). Karthik's position suggests a middle path where the project makes technical improvements that move toward the desired end state without forcing premature decisions about repository-specific behavior. This continues the thread's pattern of weighing process-wide behavior against repository-specific configuration while considering both user expectations and libification requirements.
