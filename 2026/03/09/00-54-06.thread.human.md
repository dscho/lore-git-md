Adrian Ratiu's 10-patch series modernizing Git's hook configuration has been merged to the 'next' branch after addressing all substantive review feedback. The series introduces several user-facing improvements to `git hook list`, including scope visibility matching `git config --show-scope` behavior and clear marking of disabled hooks, while laying infrastructure for future hook metadata storage. 

Post-merge discussion continues with Patrick Steinhardt proposing an enhancement to validate standard hook names by default (with a `--allow-unknown-hook-name` escape hatch for custom hooks) and noting stylistic opportunities to modernize boolean flag usage from `unsigned int:1` bitfields to the `bool` type. Junio Hamano has engaged playfully on the style point, reflecting the project's attention to detail even after patches are queued. While functionally correct as merged, these observations may inform a potential v3 revision if Adrian chooses to respin for the validation changes. The series represents the culmination of Adrian's multi-part effort to integrate hook configuration more deeply into Git's config system.
