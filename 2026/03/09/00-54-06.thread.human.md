Adrian Ratiu's multi-patch series modernizing Git's hook configuration system has now been fully merged into the 'next' integration branch, marking the culmination of this substantial effort. The series provides user-facing improvements to hook management while establishing infrastructure for future metadata storage, with features including config scope visibility, disabled hook display, and validation for unknown hook names (with an escape hatch for custom hooks via `--allow-unknown-hook-name`).

Junio Hamano has confirmed successful integration of the series into 'next' after rebuilding it on top of the current master branch. The maintainer noted one minor naming quirk - both `struct hook` and `struct repository` contain similarly named `event_disabled` fields, which could cause grep ambiguity. He suggested considering a rename of the repository's string_list version to something like `disabled_events` in future cleanup work, though this is not blocking. Junio also requested Adrian to verify the parallel-hooks integration when it's pushed.

The series has addressed all substantive technical feedback through multiple iterations, with Adrian implementing the features and Junio reviewing and merging the work. The changes touch hook.c, hook.h, builtin/hook.c, documentation, and tests, while maintaining machine-parseable output and comprehensive test coverage. With this merge, the thread reaches its natural conclusion, having established a modernized hook configuration system that may serve as a foundation for future extensions.
