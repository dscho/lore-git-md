Adrian Ratiu has been working on improving Git's hook configuration support, building on his previous work that's already in the 'next' branch. This latest series adds several small but useful features to make hook management more transparent. The most notable addition is a new `--show-scope` flag for `git hook list` that displays where each hook is configured (system, global, or local), addressing a suggestion from Junio Hamano. 

The series also includes various cleanups and preparations for future work, such as standardizing documentation terminology, improving test coverage of hook execution order, and refactoring internal data structures. The final patch, suggested by Patrick Steinhardt, modifies `git hook list` to show disabled hooks with a "(disabled)" annotation rather than hiding them entirely. When combined with `--show-scope`, this gives users a complete view of all configured hooks and their status. The changes maintain backward compatibility while making the hook subsystem more robust and easier to manage.
