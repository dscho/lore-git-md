This is a bugfix patch in Adrian Ratiu's ongoing hook configuration series, currently the 4th of 10 patches building on work already in the 'next' branch. The patch adds defensive programming checks to catch two invalid hook states that should never occur: encountering an unknown hook type, and finding a configured hook that is enabled but lacks a command to run. Both cases now trigger BUG() assertions.

The changes are minimal (just 4 lines added to hook.c) but serve as important safeguards. The patch follows Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion to add these checks, continuing the pattern of incorporating review feedback into the series. While not user-visible functionality, these assertions help maintain internal consistency as the hook subsystem grows more complex with config-based hooks.

The patch fits cleanly into the series' broader goal of making hooks more configurable and robust. It shows the author being proactive about catching edge cases early rather than waiting for them to surface as mysterious bugs later. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial given they're purely defensive.
