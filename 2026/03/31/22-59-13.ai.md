This is a technical follow-up in the ODB abstraction effort, specifically discussing interface placement for a new stream helper function. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Justin Tobler's consideration of where to expose the `odb_write_stream_from_fd()` helper function introduced in patch 5/6 of the series.

Patrick agrees with Justin's suggestion that "odb/streaming.h" might be more appropriate than "odb/transaction.h" for this helper, since the functionality relates more generally to streaming writes rather than being transaction-specific. He notes that while currently all uses are for writing objects, the helper isn't inherently tied to transactions, as evidenced by the existing `odb_write_object_stream()` function. Patrick also confirms that putting it in the main "odb.h" would feel inappropriate.

This brief exchange represents the final polish on the interface design before the series is ready for merging, showing the careful attention to proper abstraction boundaries in the ODB work. The discussion continues the pattern of constructive technical feedback that has characterized this entire patch series.
