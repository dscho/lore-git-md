This is a refactoring patch in the fsmonitor series that brings Linux support to parity with existing Windows and macOS implementations. The patch renames `fsm-settings-darwin.c` to `fsm-settings-unix.c` to reflect that the settings logic is shared across Unix platforms, not just macOS. It updates the build systems (CMake and meson) to use a new `FSMONITOR_OS_SETTINGS` variable for platform-specific settings, matching the approach already used for IPC code.

The change is straightforward and well-justified - the settings handling is indeed Unix-common rather than macOS-specific. The patch builds on previous work from Eric DeCosta and Marziyeh Esipreh, showing good attribution. This is part of the mature v12 series that has been production-tested for months, with all substantive technical feedback addressed. The remaining discussion around this series focuses on process and review norms rather than technical concerns.
