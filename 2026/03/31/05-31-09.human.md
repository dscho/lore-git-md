This is a review comment from Patrick Steinhardt on Jayesh Daga's `the_repository` removal patch for `unpack-trees.c`. While Patrick agrees with the general direction, he points out that the current approach doesn't fully eliminate `the_repository` usage - the function still initializes its `repo` variable from the global. He suggests a more thorough solution: first modifying `unpack_trees()` to take an explicit repository parameter, which would allow completely removing the global dependency. 

Patrick's tone is constructive, framing this as an opportunity to go further rather than a criticism of the current patch. He hints at the larger goal of potentially being able to remove `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` entirely once all call sites are updated. This aligns with the project's long-term direction but would require more extensive changes than Jayesh's current focused patch. The comment invites the author to consider expanding the scope of their work.
