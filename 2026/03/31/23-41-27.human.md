This is the second patch in Jeff King's series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings. It focuses on the http-push subsystem, specifically marking a `path` field in a custom `struct repo` as `const` since it points to a string that is never be modified.

The change is straightforward - the `path` field is being pointed to a const argv string and never written to, so adding `const` properly reflects its usage and silences the new C23 warnings from glibc about const-correctness in `strchr()` usage. This follows the pattern established in the first patch of the series where safe const additions are made to pointer fields that don't modify their data.
