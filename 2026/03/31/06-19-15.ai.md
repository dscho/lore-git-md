This is a bugfix patch in the fsmonitor series, specifically addressing a potential hang condition in the daemon shutdown process. The patch adds a 30-second timeout to the `fsmonitor--daemon stop` command, which previously unbounded polling for daemon termination.

The change ensures that if the fsmonitor daemon fails to shut down properly (due to being stuck or wedged), the stop command will fail with an error rather than blocking indefinitely. The implementation tracks elapsed time during polling and returns an error if the IPC state remains in LISTENING state after 30 seconds (30000ms) of waiting, with 50ms sleep intervals between checks.

This is part of the mature v12 series that brings Linux fsmonitor support to parity with Windows and macOS backends. The patch demonstrates careful consideration of edge cases - in this instance, ensuring robust process management even in failure scenarios. The series has been production-validated for months and this change further improves reliability by preventing hangs during shutdown.
