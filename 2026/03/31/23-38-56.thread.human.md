The work systematically fixes warnings about string function usage (strchr() and related) across Git's codebase, building on prior contributions from Collin Funk and Michael Forney.

The series has progressed methodically through various subsystems - from convert.c and http-push in early patches to now covering the refs backend in patch 12/12. It follows the established CONST_OUTPARAM pattern introduced earlier in the series, similar to how skip_prefix() was handled.
