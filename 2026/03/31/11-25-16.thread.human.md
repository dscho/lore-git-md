He points out that the code doesn't actively detect sparse status but rather defaults to sparse mode, which becomes a no-op in non-sparse repositories. Junio suggests this might be more accurately described as defaulting to `--sparse` behavior rather than true auto-detection. He's brought in Derrick Stolee (the original author of the sparse-related backfill code) for deeper technical input, indicating the conceptual approach may need refinement. The test case itself appears sound and hasn't been challenged, but the framing and implementation details of the fix are now under discussion.
