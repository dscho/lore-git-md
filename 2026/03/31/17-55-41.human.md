This is a bugfix patch in the long-running fsmonitor series, specifically handling split-index interactions. Junio C Hamano raises important questions about the approach taken in the patch, which removes assertions that could catch real bugs in non-split-index cases. He suggests the fix might be too broad and wonders if we should maintain stronger validation.

The technical changes are focused on two areas: adding a split-index guard in `tweak_fsmonitor()` to match existing protections, and replacing an assertion in `fsmonitor_ewah_callback()` with a bounds check. Junio's main concern is that silently skipping positions beyond cache_nr might mask real issues in non-split-index cases, and he questions whether this should be coordinated with the author of the earlier scalar commit that worked around the issue.

This comes late in a well-tested series (v12) that has been production-validated, with split-index interaction being one of the last edge cases to address. The discussion highlights Git's careful approach to maintaining both bisectability and proper validation, even when fixing edge cases. Junio seems open to the changes but wants to ensure we're not losing important safety checks in the process.
