Maintainer feedback on reftable system abstractions. Junio C Hamano responds to the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series that introduces a "reftable-system.h" header to consolidate platform-specific dependencies. While acknowledging the technical correctness of the change, Junio raises architectural questions about the separation between platform-neutral interfaces (in system.h) and platform-specific implementations (in system.c and now reftable-system.h).

The key concern is conceptual clarity - Junio notes that system.h currently mixes abstraction definitions (like reftable_mmap()) with Git-specific implementations (like REFTABLE_INLINE), which may confuse the boundary between portable interfaces and their concrete realizations. He suggests the inline macro and zlib compatibility header might better belong in the new reftable-system.h if they're Git-specific.

This isn't a blocking objection - the patch remains technically sound - but Junio is flagging an opportunity to refine the abstraction layers before finalizing the series. The feedback continues the thread's pattern of careful design scrutiny, now focusing on making the architecture's intentions as clear as its implementation. Patrick will likely either adjust the header organization or explain why the current structure best serves both Git and libgit2's needs.
