This is a bugfix patch in the fsmonitor series, addressing an edge case between fsmonitor's EWAH bitmap tracking and Git's split-index feature. The patch fixes two locations where the fsmonitor_dirty bitmap could reference positions beyond the current index state during split-index operations.

Junio raises two key concerns in his review. First, he questions whether this fix should be coordinated with the author of a related scalar commit that previously worked around this issue by disabling split-index testing. Second, he wonders if the fix is too broad - it removes assertions that would catch real bugs in non-split-index cases, whereas the test-only workaround left the production code's assertions intact.

The patch itself makes two changes: (1) adds a split-index guard in `tweak_fsmonitor()` to skip the bitmap assertion (matching existing protections elsewhere), and (2) replaces an assertion in `fsmonitor_ewah_callback()` with a bounds check. This resolves a BUG() that had previously required disabling split-index testing.

While the technical solution appears sound, Junio's review highlights important process considerations about how fixes should be coordinated across related subsystems and whether assertions should be preserved for non-split-index cases. The patch is part of the mature v12 fsmonitor series that has been production-tested for months, but these architectural questions may need resolution before final acceptance.
