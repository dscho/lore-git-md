This thread tracks the status of several related patch series that improve `git format-patch`'s cover letter formatting capabilities. The changes introduce new formatting options and configuration while modernizing the feature. 

The first two series (already merged to 'next') add a `--commit-list-format` option (originally called `--cover-letter-format`) with placeholders like `%(count)` and `%(total)`, plus a "modern" preset format. A third pending series completes the work by adding documentation and fixing an issue with 72-character line wrapping. 

Junio Hamano has now confirmed he will hold merging the first two series until the documentation fixes in the third series are ready, avoiding any disruption from the temporary line-wrapping behavior. This careful coordination between Mirko Faina (the author) and the maintainer ensures these improvements will land cleanly together in an upcoming release. The changes are part of Git's broader effort to modernize its output formatting while maintaining backward compatibility.
