This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's six-part refactoring series improving flag handling in Git's object database (ODB) subsystem. The series has been systematically converting flag parameters to typed enums and standardizing their naming conventions across the ODB interface.

This concluding patch is a straightforward cleanup that removes unnecessary header includes and forward declarations from odb.h. It eliminates unused includes like "hashmap.h" and drops redundant forward declarations for types that are already included via other headers (like struct packed_git from "object.h"). The changes are purely mechanical and don't affect functionality - they just tidy up the interface after the previous flag-handling improvements.

The patch follows Patrick's characteristic style of careful, well-structured changes. While small, it completes the series' goal of making the ODB interface more consistent and maintainable as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort. With this patch, the series appears ready for merging as uncontroversial internal improvements.
