This is patch 5/12 in Jeff King's series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings, specifically focusing on `run-command.c`. The patch deals with a case where `strchr()` is used on a const string, with the result assigned to a non-const `void *` field in a string_list.

The change moves the const-cast to the point where the string is assigned to the `util` field, rather than at the `strchr()` call. This better reflects the actual source of the type mismatch - the `util` field's `void *` type can't preserve constness. The commit message acknowledges this is a compromise, as the `void *` type means we lose type safety against accidental writes, but argues this is the least bad place for the cast.

This is a typical Peff patch - carefully considered, with a clear explanation of the tradeoffs. It's a small but meaningful improvement in type safety while maintaining existing behavior. The change is isolated to `run-command.c` and doesn't affect other parts of the series.
