This is a bugfix patch in the fsmonitor series, specifically addressing split-index interaction issues that were previously worked around in tests. The patch modifies two locations where the fsmonitor_dirty bitmap could reference positions beyond the current index state during split-index operations.

Junio raises two key concerns about the approach. First, he questions whether this fix should be coordinated with the author of the scalar commit that originally worked around the issue by disabling split-index testing. Second, he wonders if the fix is too broad - by removing assertions that would catch real bugs in non-split-index cases, we might be masking other potential issues.

The technical changes are straightforward: adding a split-index guard in `tweak_fsmonitor()` to match existing protections elsewhere, and replacing an assertion in `fsmonitor_ewah_callback()` with a bounds check. However, Junio's comments suggest the patch may need reconsideration of its scope and whether it should maintain stronger validation in non-split-index cases.

This comes late in a long-running series (v12) that has otherwise been production-validated, with the split-index interaction being one of the last edge cases to address. The discussion highlights Git's careful approach to maintaining bisectability and proper validation even when fixing edge cases.
