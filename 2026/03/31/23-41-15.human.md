This is the first patch in Jeff King's 12-part series addressing C23 const-correctness warnings around `strchr()` usage. The patch makes a straightforward change to `convert.c`, converting two pointer variables (`dollar` and `spc`) from non-const to const since they don't need to be modified. This silences compiler warnings that would appear under C23's stricter type checking while maintaining the same behavior.

The change is small and clearly safe - these pointers are only used to scan through strings without modification. It's a typical example of the series' approach where possible: adding const qualifiers where the code's semantics already support it. The patch demonstrates the careful, precise style Jeff King is known for - addressing a real but subtle compatibility issue before it becomes a problem.
