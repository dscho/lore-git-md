While this doesn't block the series, it highlights the project's attention to technical precision in infrastructure work.

Key open questions remain about fsync behavior (whether to fail explicitly when no callback is provided) and header organization (how to separate platform-neutral from Git-specific declarations). The series has seen productive collaboration between Patrick, Junio, and René, with brian m. carlson raising broader questions about the need for certain abstractions given modern compiler support.
