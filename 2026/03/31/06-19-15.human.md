This is a bugfix patch in the fsmonitor series, specifically addressing a potential hang in the daemon shutdown process. The patch adds a 30-second timeout to the `fsmonitor--daemon stop` command, which previously could spin indefinitely if the daemon failed to exit properly.

The change is straightforward - it introduces a timeout counter that checks if the daemon hasn't shut down after 30 seconds of polling, returning an error in that case rather than waiting forever. This is particularly important for containerized environments where a stuck daemon could cause operational issues.

This patch is part of the mature v12 series that has been production-tested for months, bringing Linux fsmonitor support to parity with Windows and macOS. The series has addressed all substantive technical feedback, with this patch demonstrating continued refinement of edge cases. The implementation appears solid, with clear trace2 instrumentation and proper error handling.
