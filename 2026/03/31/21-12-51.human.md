This is a brief follow-up in the reftable portability series, specifically addressing the first patch that introduced the `REFTABLE_INLINE` macro. René Scharfe points out a potential inaccuracy in Patrick Steinhardt's commit message - while Git does have some handling of the `inline` keyword in `compat/regex/regex_internal.h`, that definition is isolated and not generally available to the rest of the codebase.

The comment is a minor correction rather than a substantive critique of the patch itself. René's observation suggests the commit message could be more precise about where Git's inline handling exists, though this doesn't affect the actual change's correctness or usefulness. The exchange shows the careful attention to detail that characterizes Git's mailing list discussions, even for small technical points in already-approved patches.
