Junio Hamano's response to Justin Tobler's fast-import signature handling series.

This is the maintainer's acknowledgment that Justin's 5-patch series extending signature validation modes in `git fast-import` is ready to be merged. Junio notes that while the patches are well-done and the inter-iteration changelog is clear, the thread structure made it hard to navigate between versions (a common mailing list issue). He mentions being the only reviewer, which he finds unfortunate given the quality of the work, and confirms he'll mark the topic for inclusion in the 'next' integration branch in his next "What's cooking" report.

The series itself completes the implementation of three signature handling modes (strip/sign/abort on invalid signatures) for both commits and tags in fast-import, building on prior work. Junio's brief but positive assessment suggests the changes are uncontroversial and technically sound, with no remaining issues blocking integration. His comment about being the only reviewer hints that this may be a niche but well-executed improvement to the import/export plumbing.
