Christian Couder's series introducing URL-based allowlisting for promisor remotes continues to progress methodically. Christian and Patrick Steinhardt are discussing whether to add tests for the warning message text itself, showing their attention to both functionality and user experience details. While this change is minor, it reflects the series' broader emphasis on making promisor remote behavior transparent.

Junio Hamano has marked the topic as expecting a reroll but not yet final. The series maintains a clear separation between these foundational improvements and the forthcoming 7-patch feature series that will introduce the actual URL whitelist functionality.
