Meanwhile, Kristoffer Haugsbakk raised a style question about whether tests should use `--option=value` (as the patch proposed) or the "stuck form" `--option value` recommended in `gitcli(7)`. 

Junio Hamano has now weighed in with a third perspective, suggesting that enforcing consistency in tests may be unnecessary since Git accepts both forms in practice. He implies that test suite diversity could be beneficial for exercising more code paths. This leaves the discussion with three viewpoints: Toon's original consistency approach, Kristoffer's alignment with documentation, and Junio's pragmatic acceptance of variation.

The series remains pending while these discussions play out.
