This is a follow-up discussion on patch 2/4 of Lorenzo Pegorari's series about promisor file handling during repacks, part of his GSoC project. Junio Hamano raises concerns about whether simply concatenating and deduplicating `.promisor` file contents (as proposed in the patch) sufficiently preserves the debugging information these files are meant to provide.

Junio points out that the current approach loses temporal information - when multiple packfiles` recorded the same ref/object pair at different times, deduplication erases that history. He suggests considering adding timestamps (from the original promisor files) to each line to preserve this debugging context, noting that the implementation would need to handle both timestamped and non-timestamped formats.

The core issue is that the patch implements the minimal solution hinted at in the original NEEDSWORK comment without sufficiently justifying why this approach meets the debugging needs. Junio emphasizes that NEEDSWORK comments are starting points, not specifications, and expects the design to document considered alternatives (like timestamps) and rationale for the chosen approach.

This feedback will likely require a design iteration from Lorenzo, either to:
1) Enhance the approach to preserve temporal information, or
2) More thoroughly justify why simple deduplication suffices for debugging needs

The discussion touches on deeper questions about what exactly needs preserving from promisor files for effective debugging of partial clone issues. This may affect not just this patch but the series' overall direction.
