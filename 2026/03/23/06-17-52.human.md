This is a coordination update in the `git backfill` argument validation thread, now acknowledging that the proposed validation patch would conflict with upcoming revision argument support.

Siddharth Shrimali confirms they will drop their patch series for validating `git backfill` arguments, as Derrick Stolee's upcoming revision argument work will handle this differently. Stolee's approach will pass unknown arguments to Git's standard revision parsing machinery, producing errors like "unknown revision or path" rather than the simpler "unknown argument" message the patch implemented.

The author agrees this is the right direction, though notes the revision walker's error messages may be less clear to users than direct argument validation. They suggest Stolee might want to add a test case for this behavior in his series, but defer to his judgment. The thread shows good technical coordination - the author recognizes when their work would conflict with a more comprehensive solution and steps back appropriately.

This marks the likely conclusion of the validation patch series, as the functionality will be superseded by Stolee's revision argument support. The discussion demonstrates how Git contributors navigate overlapping work and prioritize more complete solutions over incremental fixes.
