This is a follow-up discussion on a patch series about preserving promisor file information during repacks, part of Lorenzo Pegorari's GSoC project. Junio Hamano raises concerns about whether simply concatenating and deduplicating `.promisor` file contents is sufficient for debugging purposes.

Junio questions whether the current approach (which deduplicates lines across all `.promisor` files) adequately preserves the historical debugging information these files are meant to provide. He suggests that timestamps might be valuable to retain, as they could show when different packfiles were created with the same ref/object mapping. The current implementation would lose this temporal information by collapsing duplicate entries.

The maintainer emphasizes that NEEDSWORK comments are starting points for deeper consideration, not final specifications. He expects the patch series to include more thoughtful design rationale about what debugging information needs preserving and why the chosen approach is sufficient. While not rejecting the current work outright, Junio signals that the justification needs strengthening before this would be ready for inclusion.

This feedback suggests the series may need another revision to either:
1) Better justify why simple deduplication meets debugging needs, or
2) Enhance the approach to preserve more historical context (like timestamps)

The discussion shows the maintainer engaging thoughtfully with a student contributor's work, pushing for more rigorous design thinking while remaining constructive.
