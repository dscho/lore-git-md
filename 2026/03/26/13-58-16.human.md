This is a follow-up discussion about ongoing work to extend Git's object database (ODB) abstraction, specifically around packfile handling and object disambiguation. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Aaron Paterson's patch proposing new vtable methods for the ODB interface.

Patrick notes two potential conflicts with existing work: his own `ps/odb-generic-object-name-handling` series already introduces similar callbacks for object abbreviation handling, and Justin Tobler is working on packfile writing through the ODB transaction interface. He expresses some puzzlement about the approach, suggesting more coordination would be needed given how much these areas are currently in flux.

The exchange highlights the challenge of parallel development on major infrastructure changes - while Aaron's patch appears technically sound (as noted in the parent summary), Patrick indicates the proposed changes may overlap with or duplicate work already underway. This seems like a case where the technical merits of the patch may be secondary to timing and coordination with other ongoing efforts in the same subsystem.
