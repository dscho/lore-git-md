This is a small refactoring patch that improves const-correctness in the ident-to-worktree conversion code. The change modifies two pointer declarations in `convert.c` to be `const` where they point to data that isn't modified, specifically the `dollar` and `spc` variables used when processing ident strings.

The author notes this involves an implicit cast to const for non-C23 compilers where `strchr()` isn't const-correct. While a minor change, it follows Git's general preference for const-correctness where possible. The patch is part of a 6-part series (this being patch 4), though the overall series goal isn't stated in this individual patch. The change is straightforward and unlikely to be controversial.
