This is a review comment on the first patch of Christian Couder's 16-part series introducing URL-based whitelisting for promisor remotes. Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on the refactoring that changes the promisor remote fetching order to prioritize accepted remotes.

Patrick agrees with the change's intent - making promisor_remote_get_direct() try accepted remotes first better reflects the client-server agreement when a server advertises promisor remotes. He asks two clarifying questions: whether the ordering importance is documented anywhere, and whether future plans might avoid storing announced promisors in config entirely.

The review also includes two concrete suggestions:
1. Simplifying the boolean logic in try_promisor_remotes() to `if (!accepted_only != !r->accepted)`
2. Adding a test to verify the new ordering behavior

The feedback is constructive and focused on implementation details rather than challenging the patch's core premise. Patrick's questions about future direction suggest he's thinking ahead to how this change fits with the series' broader goals of auto-configuring promisor remotes.
