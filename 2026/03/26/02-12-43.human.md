This is a follow-up in Lorenzo Pegorari's patch series about preserving promisor packfile debug information during repacks, part of his GSoC project under Eric Sunshine's mentorship. The thread has progressed to version 2, with Junio Hamano having raised concerns about how the current approach handles geometric repacking scenarios where only some promisor packs might be repacked.

In this brief response, Lorenzo acknowledges Junio's point about the geometric repacking edge case and indicates he will be reworking the series to address it. The core issue is that the current implementation assumes all promisor packs will be repacked at once, which may not hold true with geometric repacking strategies that selectively repack only smaller packs. 

This email doesn't contain new technical content but signals the author's intent to significantly revise the approach. The next version will need to either modify the behavior to properly handle partial repacks or document the limitations of debug information preservation in such cases. The series remains important for maintaining debugging capabilities in partial clone scenarios, but needs more work to be merge-ready.
