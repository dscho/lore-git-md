This is a performance-focused patch series addressing edge cases in geometric repacking with MIDX bitmaps. The final patch (5/5) completes the solution by properly marking non-MIDX packs above the geometric split point as excluded-open ('!') rather than excluded-closed ('^') when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false. This ensures pack-objects will traverse through these packs during the follow pass, rescuing any reachable objects that might otherwise be missed.

Derrick Stolee, who has been reviewing the series, approves of this final patch, noting that the concise implementation builds well on the foundational changes in earlier patches. The series has evolved through multiple rounds of review from Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King, with Stolee providing maintainability-focused suggestions that Taylor Blau has incorporated. The solution handles the subtle case where once-cruft objects could become unreachable if their containing pack was incorrectly treated as closed, while maintaining backward compatibility with existing MIDX behavior. The test case that previously expected failure now succeeds, demonstrating the fix works as intended.
