This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, where Patrick Steinhardt responds to Aaron Paterson's patch adding three new vtable methods for packfile handling, object abbreviation iteration, and hash conversion. 

Patrick notes two key conflicts with parallel work: his own ps/odb-generic-object-name-handling series already introduces similar abbreviation handling, and Justin Tobler is working on packfile writing through the ODB transaction interface. He expresses puzzlement at the lack of coordination, given these overlapping efforts in the same subsystem.

The tone suggests this patch may need to be reworked to align with the existing direction rather than introducing parallel abstractions. Patrick's response indicates the changes touch areas where design decisions are still in flux, making independent contributions difficult to integrate cleanly. The technical merits of the specific vtable methods are not disputed, but the timing and coordination appear problematic given other active work in the same code.
