This is a follow-up in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, where Aaron Paterson responds to Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about coordination with parallel work. Aaron clarifies that his patches are adapted from a fork supporting sqlite-git integration, with the goal of making sqlite-git redistributable by allowing users to bring their own local backends similar to how remote helpers work today.

Aaron acknowledges that parts of his first series will be covered by Patrick's existing work (ps/odb-generic-object-name-handling) and proposes focusing next on git-local-* helpers instead. These would allow alternative storage formats beyond packs and reftables, positioning this as a more maintainable approach than direct sqlite support in core. He provides links to his implementation but indicates willingness to wait for Patrick's work to land first if needed. The exchange highlights the coordination challenges in this active subsystem where multiple contributors are working on overlapping abstractions.
