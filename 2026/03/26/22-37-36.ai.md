Bugfix in Taylor Blau's pack-objects refactoring series. This follow-up email identifies a critical issue in patch 2/5 where the sorting comparator incorrectly assumes `->util` points to a `packed_git` struct when it actually contains a `stdin_pack_info` struct. The mismatch causes ASan CI failures.

The problem occurs in the newly introduced `stdin_packs_add_pack_entries()` function where `pack_mtime_cmp()` is called on string list items whose `util` field was set to `stdin_pack_info` pointers rather than the expected `packed_git` pointers. This is a direct consequence of the v2 refactoring that changed the data structure from string lists to a strmap.

Taylor acknowledges the oversight and plans to resubmit the series with a fix, but will wait for discussion on Derrick Stolee's review feedback to conclude first. The issue doesn't affect the core functionality being introduced in the series (handling excluded-open packs during geometric repacking), but is a clear bug in the intermediate refactoring step.

This email serves as both a bug report and a notice that a v3 series will be forthcoming. The series has otherwise received positive reviews from key contributors (Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, Patrick Steinhardt) and addresses important edge cases in MIDX bitmap generation during geometric repacking.
