This is a bugfix follow-up in Taylor Blau's series addressing pack-objects issues during geometric repacking. The patch author caught a critical error in the v2 implementation where the pack sorting logic was incorrectly assuming the wrong data structure type in the comparison function. The mistake causes ASan (Address Sanitizer) CI builds to fail, though it wasn't caught before submission due to timing.

The issue stems from the refactoring in patch 2/5 that converted pack handling from string_lists to a strmap. While the overall approach was sound, the sorting comparison function still expected direct packed_git pointers rather than the new stdin_pack_info wrapper structure. Taylor acknowledges the oversight and plans to resubmit the series with a fix, but will wait for ongoing discussion about Derrick Stolee's review feedback to conclude first.

This is a straightforward correction to align the series' data structures - the core concept of using strmap remains valid, but the implementation needs adjustment. The series has substantial review from packfile experts and addresses a real edge case in geometric repacking behavior, so this appears to be a minor implementation hiccup rather than a conceptual flaw.
