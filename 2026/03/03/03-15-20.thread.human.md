Derrick Stolee's `for-each-repo` worktree fix series has reached its final form after four iterations. The patches address an issue where the command failed to work correctly from secondary worktrees by introducing a new `clear_local_repo_env()` helper function and making related fixes in `builtin/for-each-repo.c` and `run-command.c`. 

Junio Hamano has now given his final approval, specifically noting the removal of an unnecessary `int i` declaration as good cleanup work. With this acknowledgment, the series is officially queued for integration into Git's development branches. This marks the successful conclusion of Stolee's latest worktree-related improvements, which build on his ongoing efforts in this area of the codebase. The changes will be included in an upcoming Git release.
