Feature implementation in the `git-history` command family. This is a reply from D. Ben Knoble to Patrick Steinhardt's patch series introducing a new `git history split` subcommand.

The email provides feedback on the 8th patch in the series, which implements the actual split functionality. Knoble notes the convenience of the feature for separating changes to independent areas that have become mixed together. He raises a question about whether rejecting empty splits (where all or no hunks are selected) could be made a no-op instead of an error, though acknowledges this might be better addressed later.

The reply also includes a lighthearted comment about not fully understanding the "ext" suffix in the function name `commit_tree_with_edited_message_ext()`, suggesting this naming choice could use clarification. Knoble indicates he didn't closely review the rest of the implementation details.

This feedback appears constructive and generally positive, focusing on one specific aspect of the interface behavior (empty splits) and a minor naming question, while acknowledging the overall usefulness of the feature. The tone suggests the patch is on the right track but could benefit from some refinement in these areas.
