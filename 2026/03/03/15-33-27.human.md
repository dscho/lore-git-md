This is a cover letter for a five-patch series introducing AI-assisted workflows for Git's localization (l10n) process, specifically targeting .po file handling. The series comes from Jiang Xin, the Chinese translation maintainer, and represents version 2 incorporating feedback from the initial RFC.

The changes focus on three main areas: adding a .gitattributes file to simplify location filtering in PO files, creating a new po/AGENTS.md document with optimized AI workflow instructions, and updating po/README.md. The series aims to make AI-assisted translation more efficient while maintaining human oversight, showing claimed improvements of up to 82% reduction in workflow steps for some tasks.

The cover letter notes these AI workflows are optional and emphasizes human translators remain the final authority. Performance metrics show significant reductions in steps/time for update-pot and update-po tasks, plus a new review scoring system. Testing was done using the qwen model via git-po-helper's agent-test and agent-run commands.

This appears to be a well-structured and carefully measured proposal from an established l10n maintainer. The performance claims are specific and the human oversight principle is clearly stated. The series seems likely to progress given it addresses previous maintainer feedback and focuses narrowly on workflow improvements rather than the broader philosophical questions about AI in translations that were raised in earlier discussion.
