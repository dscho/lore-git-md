This is a follow-up in the thread about deprecating `git whatchanged`. Antoine Cousson confirms they've tested the suggested `git log --stat` alternative and found it preferable to the original `whatchanged` output format, appreciating its clearer presentation of file changes. The exchange shows successful user adoption of the replacement options, with Antoine specifically endorsing `--stat` over the exact-but-verbose `--raw --no-merges` alternative. This wraps up the thread with no remaining technical questions - the deprecation plan appears to be working as intended, with users finding the modern alternatives actually improve their workflow.
