Documentation patch improving pronoun agreement in the pack protocol documentation. This is the first of three patches in Lorenzo Pegorari's final v3 series, which systematically improves the `gitprotocol-pack.adoc` file's readability.

The patch makes two straightforward changes to match plural pronouns with their antecedents: "its" becomes "their" when referring to clients sending information, and "it" becomes "they" when describing what clients must send. These are purely grammatical fixes that don't affect the technical content. The changes are small (4 lines modified) but part of a larger effort to polish the documentation's presentation.

As noted in the thread summary, this series has already been reviewed and approved by Junio Hamano, with all feedback addressed. The three patches work together to improve the document's grammar, structure, and formatting consistency. This first patch shows the contributor's attention to detail in catching subtle language issues that could confuse non-native English speakers reading the protocol specification.
