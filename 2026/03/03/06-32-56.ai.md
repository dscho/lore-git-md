This is a CI/build system patch addressing a Windows-specific issue in GitLab CI pipelines. The thread has reached consensus that the only viable solution is to unset the `GITLAB_FEATURES` environment variable in `ci/lib.sh` when running on Windows CI, as its 6.3KB size was causing the total environment (17KB) to exceed MSYS2's hardcoded 12KB argument length limit for xargs(1).

Justin Tobler initially suggested exploring whether the limit could be increased, but Patrick Steinhardt confirmed this isn't possible in MSYS2. The variable's removal reduces the environment to 10.7KB, safely under the limit. Both participants are GitLab team members familiar with their CI system, and no concerns were raised about side effects from removing this feature list variable.

The fix is minimal (4 lines in `ci/lib.sh`) and narrowly targeted at Windows CI infrastructure. The discussion remained technical and focused, with no remaining open questions or connections to other in-flight topics. This appears ready to be picked up as a straightforward CI maintenance fix.
