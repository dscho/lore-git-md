Performance optimization in the sideband subsystem, part of Patrick Steinhardt's series reducing write syscalls in `git-upload-pack`. This patch refactors `send_sideband()` to use `writev()` for sending pktlines, combining what were previously two separate syscalls (header and data) into one. The change cuts write syscalls roughly in half for this operation - from ~133,000 to ~67,000 in testing.

The implementation builds on the new `writev_or_die()` wrapper introduced earlier in the series, maintaining consistent error handling while improving efficiency. The patch follows Jeff King's suggestion and fits into the broader effort to optimize high-concurrency scenarios where kernel contention becomes significant. With Junio's initial approval and Peff's technical sign-off on earlier versions, this appears to be a well-vetted improvement to Git's network protocol efficiency.
