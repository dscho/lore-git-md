This is a bugfix follow-up to the submodule remote handling patch series that was previously reviewed and queued. Ramsay Jones reports a test flakiness in `t5526-fetch-submodules.sh` where the order of submodule fetch output is inconsistent under stress testing. The issue appears when fetching from custom-named remotes (the main feature of the series), where the FETCH_HEAD messages for different submodules sometimes appear in different orders.

The core functionality remains sound - Jacob Keller's review and Junio Hamano's final ack confirmed the technical approach is correct. This appears to be a minor test stability issue rather than a problem with the underlying implementation. The test expects a specific output order that isn't guaranteed by the code, which was already queued in the 'seen' branch. Ramsay notes that other tests in the series passed without issue.

This is the kind of minor test instability that often surfaces after merging - the patch author or maintainers will likely need to adjust the test to either sort the output or make the order checking more flexible. Given that the core functionality has already been thoroughly vetted, this shouldn't block the series from progressing.
