This is part of Jiang Xin's ongoing effort to introduce AI-assisted workflows for Git's localization (l10n) process, specifically focusing on .po file handling. The patch adds detailed instructions in po/AGENTS.md for AI agents updating language-specific PO files, showing significant efficiency improvements in testing.

The key change is a new section in AGENTS.md that provides optimized commands for AI agents to update PO files, avoiding redundant verification steps that were previously required. Benchmark data shows this reduces interaction turns by 82% and execution time by 76% compared to the previous approach where agents had to reference po/README.md.

The patch maintains the principle that human translators remain in control, with AI acting as an efficiency tool rather than replacing human role in translations. It's part of a larger series that has addressed technical feedback from the maintainer and other contributors, though some legal concerns about AI-generated content remain unresolved. The implementation appears clean and well-measured, with clear performance benefits demonstrated.
