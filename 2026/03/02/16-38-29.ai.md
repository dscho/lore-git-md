This is a brief exchange in the ongoing thread about adding path-related fields to `git repo-info`. The discussion centers on the behavior of the `--path-format` flag compared to `git rev-parse`.

Junio C Hamano notes that while `git rev-parse` allows changing the `--path-format` between options (enabling mixed-format output), this flexibility comes at the cost of incremental option handling that often confuses users. The implication is that `git repo-info`'s simpler, single-format approach may be preferable despite being less flexible.

The email highlights a key tradeoff in the design: `rev-parse`'s per-option format control provides more power but creates a more complex interface, while `repo-info` opts for consistency at the expense of mixed-format capability. This aligns with the thread's earlier consensus to accept the limitation of no mixed-format `--all` output (requiring separate commands for different formats).

The exchange doesn't introduce new technical elements but reinforces the project's preference for simplicity in the `repo-info` interface, even when it means diverging from `rev-parse`'s more flexible but complex behavior.
