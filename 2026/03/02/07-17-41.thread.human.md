Here's a human-readable summary of the thread:

This thread investigates two performance regressions in Git's receive-pack and fetch operations, identified by Patrick Stehtardt through Bencher testing. The first and more serious issue shows a 2.66x slowdown in receive-pack when handling 10,000 refs with the reftable backend, traced to commit fc148b146a which converted update hooks to a new API. The regression manifests as a significant system time increase from 89ms to 376ms. Adrian Ratiu, who worked on the hooks conversion, has been notified.

A second, less critical regression affects both receive-pack and fetch operations with many refs, caused by commit 452b12c2e0 changing the default maintenance strategy to geometric. This triggers unwanted ref packing during benchmarking scenarios. Patrick considers this a benchmarking infrastructure issue rather than a true regression, as the behavior change is expected (though problematic for benchmarks). He plans to address it by disabling auto-maintenance in the test setup. The thread remains open while the first regression awaits further investigation.
