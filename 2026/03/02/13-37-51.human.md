This is a follow-up in an ongoing performance regression thread about `receive-pack` and `fetch` operations, specifically focusing on a slowdown in the reftable backend when handling update hooks. Adrian Ratiu, who authored the hooks conversion that introduced the regression, responds to Patrick Steinhardt's initial report.

Adrian suggests the regression might be related to Junio Hamano prematurely merging version 2 of Adrian's configurable hooks series into the `next` branch, despite version 3 (with addressed feedback) having been sent the previous day. He asks if reverting the merge commit (6a04cca28e) resolves the performance issue, noting he doesn't have the benchmark setup to verify himself.

The thread context shows this is one of two identified regressions - the other being a geometric maintenance change affecting traditional ref backends. This particular issue appears more serious as it shows a 2.66x slowdown in system time for reftable operations with 10k refs. Adrian's response indicates the problematic changes may have landed earlier than intended, suggesting a possible need to revert or fast-track the corrected version.
