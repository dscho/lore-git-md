This is an update on Adrian Ratiu's hook API conversion series, which introduces configurable hooks and multi-hook support. The series had been queued in Junio's 'next' branch but Adrian requested a temporary hold to incorporate feedback from Junio and Patrick Steinhardt.

Adrian now confirms that v3 of the patch series addresses all outstanding feedback and will be sent as an incremental update rather than requiring a revert. The key context is that this work is part of a larger effort to modernize Git's hook infrastructure, with future plans for execution parallelization. The series had previously been blocked by a performance regression in `receive-pack` that has now been resolved.

The exchange demonstrates Git's careful review process - even after initial acceptance into 'next', substantive feedback can still lead to further iterations before final merging to 'master'. Adrian's responsiveness to the concerns raised by both the maintainer (Junio) and a domain expert (Patrick) shows the collaborative nature of the project's development.
