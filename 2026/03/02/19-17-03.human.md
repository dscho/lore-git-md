This is a bugfix patch addressing a performance regression in the hook subsystem, specifically affecting the `update` hook when output is sent over a sideband. The regression was introduced during the conversion to the new hook API, where an "exit early" optimization was accidentally removed for hooks with sideband output.

The patch restores the early exit behavior when no hook is found, avoiding the overhead of spinning up async threads that would just no-op. The issue was independently identified by both Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff), with Peff proposing a similar fix. The author has verified the fix passes CI tests.

This appears to be a straightforward correction of an unintended behavioral change, with clear motivation and confirmation from two experienced contributors. The change is small (12 lines) and focused on the `builtin/receive-pack.c` file. Given the regression affects performance-critical paths and has consensus on the solution, this seems likely to be merged quickly.
