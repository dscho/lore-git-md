This is a performance fix targeting the hook subsystem in `git-pack-receive`. The patch addresses a regression introduced during the conversion to the new hook API where sideband hooks were no longer exiting early when no hook was found, causing unnecessary overhead from spinning up async threads that would do nothing.

The issue was independently identified by Patrick Steinhardt and root-caused by Jeff King (Peff), with Adrian Ratiu now submitting the fix. The change adds back the early-exit check for hooks that output over a sideband, specifically in `builtin/receive-pack.c`. This avoids the overhead of creating and tearing down async threads when there's no actual hook to run.

The patch is small (12 lines) and focused, touching only the receive-pack builtin. It has already passed CI testing on GitHub. Given that this fixes a clear performance regression introduced by recent changes, and has been vetted by two experienced contributors (Patrick and Peff), it appears to be a straightforward and well-justified fix.
