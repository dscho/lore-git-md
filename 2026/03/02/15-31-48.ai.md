This is a follow-up discussion in Derrick Stolee's patch series improving environment handling in `git for-each-repo`, specifically addressing feedback from Jeff King (Peff) on the second patch. The exchange focuses on refining how environment variables are managed when spawning child processes in different contexts.

Stolee agrees with Peff's suggestion to consolidate environment variable policy into a single location rather than having subtly different behaviors across commands. He notes that while `prepare_other_repo_env()` would be ideal, it requires a computed gitdir which isn't available in this context. Instead, he proposes extracting and reusing just the environment clearing logic from that function.

The discussion reveals several technical insights:
- Current handling of `GIT_CONFIG_*` variables was incomplete (only dropping `GIT_CONFIG_COUNT`)
- `NO_REPLACE_OBJECTS_ENVIRONMENT` may not belong in repository-specific environment lists
- Historical context shows upload-pack already handles replacement refs internally
- Defensive checks for `EXEC_PATH_ENVIRONMENT` are deemed unnecessary maintenance burden

Peff emphasizes the importance of consistent environment variable propagation policies, suggesting either reusing existing infrastructure like `prepare_other_repo_env()` or factoring out its logic into a shared helper. The exchange demonstrates how architectural decisions in Git's environment handling affect multiple commands, with both contributors favoring simplicity and consistency over exhaustive validation.

Stolee indicates he'll incorporate this feedback in a v3, focusing on proper `GIT_CONFIG_*` handling while avoiding over-engineering. The thread shows productive collaboration between a patch author and a domain expert, with Peff providing both technical corrections and broader architectural perspective while Stolee demonstrates responsiveness to review feedback.
