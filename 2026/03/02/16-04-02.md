Just like piping "grep" output to "sed" is an anti-pattern, piping
"sed" output to an invocation of "sed" is often an anti-pattern.

Perhaps something like this would replace the original "grep | sed"
pipeline?

```
	sed -E -e "
		/^[^.]+\.[^.]+$/b out
		/^[^.]+\.[^.]+\.[^.]+$/b out
		d
		: out
		s/\..*//
	" human |
	sort -u

```
