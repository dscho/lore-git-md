CI/build system patch addressing Windows CI pipeline issue with large environment variables.

Justin Tobler replies to Patrick Steinhardt's patch about a GitLab CI pipeline failure on Windows. The issue occurs when the `GITLAB_FEATURES` environment variable (6.3KB) causes xargs(1) from Git for Windows to hit a POSIX argument length limit, triggering an assertion failure. 

Justin confirms the analysis, noting that unsetting `GITLAB_FEATURES` would reduce the environment size from 17KB to 11KB, bringing it under the 12.5KB limit. He asks whether the limit could be increased as an alternative solution, though this seems more like a thought exercise than a serious proposal since the patch's approach is straightforward.

The patch itself is minimal - just 4 lines added to `ci/lib.sh` to unset the problematic variable when running under GitLab CI. Justin gives the change a quick review and indicates it looks good, though this isn't a formal Reviewed-by tag. The discussion is still early, but the fix appears uncontroversial and well-targeted at the specific CI issue.
