This is a patch in Phillip Wood and Li Chen's ongoing series to add `--trailer` support to `git rebase`. The series is now in its 7th iteration, with this being patch 4/5 in v7.

The patch simplifies trailer handling in `git commit` and `git tag` by changing from `OPT_PASSTHRU_ARGV` to `OPT_STRVEC` for parsing `--trailer` arguments. This means the trailer_args now contain just the trailer values rather than the full `--trailer=<value>` strings, allowing removal of the prefix-stripping code in `amend_file_with_trailers()`.
