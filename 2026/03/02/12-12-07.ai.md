Performance optimization discussion between Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King (Peff) regarding lock contention in `git-upload-pack`. This is a follow-up to Patrick's initial patch that reduced write syscalls by buffering packfile data before transmission.

Patrick acknowledges Peff's suggestion to increase the write size from `pack-objects` (via `csum-file.c`) as an alternative approach, noting it would help but pointing out the need to consider cases where the downstream process isn't `pack-objects` (due to `uploadpack.packObjectsHook`). He also expresses interest in Peff's `writev()` proposal for combining pkt-line headers with data writes, though notes he hasn't yet investigated platform compatibility concerns.

The discussion reveals measurement methodology differences - Patrick used strace while Peff found it distorted timing-sensitive measurements, preferring `perf stat`. Patrick indicates he'll send a v2 incorporating these considerations, though the exact approach remains open between three options: Patrick's original buffering strategy, Peff's buffer size increase, or Peff's `writev()` proposal. The exchange demonstrates the project's characteristic attention to low-level performance details and preference for solutions that avoid introducing new edge cases.
