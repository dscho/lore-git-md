Performance regression investigation in receive-pack and fetch operations. Patrick Steinhardt reports two distinct performance issues detected by Bencher:

1. A 2.66x slowdown in `git-receive-pack` when handling 10,000 refs with the reftable backend, bisected to commit fc148b146a ("receive-pack: convert update hooks to new API"). The regression appears in the update hooks conversion and shows significant system time increase (89ms -> 376ms).

2. A separate regression in both `git-receive-pack` and `git-fetch` operations when handling many refs, bisected to commit 452b12c2e0 ("builtin/maintenance: use 'geometric' strategy by default"). This appears to be caused by automatic maintenance kicking in after fetches, triggering ref packing that wasn't happening before. The regression doesn't affect the reftable backend since it doesn't require this optimization.

Patrick identifies the second issue as a benchmarking infrastructure problem rather than a true regression, since the behavior change is expected (though undesirable for benchmarking). He plans to fix it by disabling auto-maintenance in the benchmarks. The first regression appears more serious and requires investigation, with Adrian Ratiu (who worked on the hooks conversion) copied for awareness.
