The proposal builds on Eric Ju's previous work that stalled after v11 due to unresolved issues, with mentorship from Christian Couder and feedback from Karthik Nayak and Chandra Pratap.

Pablo demonstrates strong preparation through his study of Eric's v11 series, merged test patches, and identification of new issues. The proposal also outlines potential future extensions like supporting additional placeholders (`%(objectsize:disk)`, `%(deltabase)`).
