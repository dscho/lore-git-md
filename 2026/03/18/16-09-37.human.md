Performance regression follow-up to bitmap cleanup patch.

René Scharfe follows up on his earlier patch that simplified bitmap cleanup in `ahead_behind()`. His initial change replaced a priority queue cleanup with a bulk `deep_clear_bit_arrays()` call, but benchmarking reveals this is actually 10% slower. He withdraws the patch, noting that while the code was cleaner, performance regressed because the new approach visits all commits that ever had bitmaps rather than just the few remaining in the queue.

He suggests an alternative approach - iterating through the queue array directly - but finds it performs the same as the original. The thread shows good practice: proposing an optimization, measuring its impact, and withdrawing when the results don't justify the change. This is characteristic of René's careful, data-driven approach to contributions.
