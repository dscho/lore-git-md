Performance improvement for MIDX repacking with bitmaps.

Junio C Hamano confirms that Taylor Blau's MIDX optimization series is now ready to merge into 'next' after Jeff King's final review. This 17-patch series improves multi-pack-index repacking by enabling reachability bitmaps during compaction, building on Taylor's ongoing work to optimize Git's handling of repositories with many packfiles. The changes include refactoring MIDX internals (like introducing the `midx_pack_perm()` helper) while maintaining backward compatibility.

Jeff King had previously identified minor issues in v2 version of the series (including outdated comments and interface concerns), all of which were addressed in v3. With no further feedback needed from reviewers, Junio is now preparing to merge the topic into the integration branches. This represents the culmination of several rounds of review and refinement for this performance optimization work.

The changes are contained to the MIDX subsystem and come with accompanying test updates and documentation fixes. This approval marks the final checkpoint before the series enters the main development pipeline, where it will be cooked in 'next' before eventually graduating to 'master' for inclusion in a future Git release.
