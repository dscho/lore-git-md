René Scharfe has proposed a performance optimization for the `ahead_behind()` function in commit-reach.c. This change is small but meaningful - it removes unnecessary complexity while maintaining the same memory safety guarantees, as the cleanup order doesn't matter at this stage. The patch is characteristic of René's work: focused, clean, and removing unnecessary overhead. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and safety considerations.
