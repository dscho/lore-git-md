Maintainer feedback on branch interpretation flags refactoring.

Junio C Hamano responds to Jialong Wang's patch that converted branch interpretation flags from macros to an enum type. While acknowledging the general direction of the change, Junio points out several issues with the current implementation.

He notes the patch has technical problems (overlong lines without sign-off, inconsistent line wrapping style) and suggests following the style shown in the first hunk as a template. More substantively, he questions whether "allowed" is the right name for the enum type, proposing "interpret_branch_kind" instead to better reflect that the values represent types of branches rather than permissions.

Junio also shares thoughts about potential future directions - suggesting the enum could eventually be unified with similar branch classification used elsewhere in Git, possibly becoming a more general "branch_kind" or "ref_kind" type. However, he explicitly states these are just ideas for future work and should not be part of the current patch.

The feedback maintains the project's characteristic balance between immediate technical corrections and broader architectural thinking, while making clear the distinction between required changes and optional future directions.
