This is a follow-up discussion in Adrian Ratiu's parallel hook execution series (v4 8/9), focusing on output stream handling for hooks. The thread has progressed from the initial proposal of a repository-wide `extensions.hookStdoutToStderr` configuration to a simpler approach that avoids breaking changes.

Adrian responds to Junio Hamano's surprise that the solution wasn't per-hook configurable, acknowledging that this alternative hadn't occurred to him initially. He proposes an even simpler solution: since parallel execution is now opt-in (defaulting to serial), they can document that enabling parallelism via `hook.jobs` or `-jN` will merge stdout to stderr, making the extension unnecessary. This maintains backward compatibility while allowing parallel execution when requested.

The exchange shows Adrian's thinking evolving based on Junio's feedback - he plans to drop both the extension patch and its follow-up in the next revision. Junio had raised the possibility of a more fundamental fix at the Git 3.0 boundary, but Adrian's simpler approach avoids needing a breaking change while still solving the parallel execution requirement. The discussion demonstrates how maintainer feedback can lead to significant simplification of a feature's implementation.
