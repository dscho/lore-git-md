This is the final patch in Adrian Ratiu's config-based hooks series, implementing visibility of disabled hooks in `git hook list` output. The patch builds on the previous infrastructure for config-based hooks while maintaining compatibility with traditional filesystem hooks.

The key change is that disabled hooks (marked with `hook.<name>.enabled=false`) are now retained in the hook cache with a "disabled" flag rather than being filtered out entirely. This allows `git hook list` to show them with a "(disabled)" annotation, including their scope when using `--show-scope`. The patch also handles edge cases like disabled hooks without commands by issuing warnings rather than fatal errors.

The implementation touches several components:
- Updates the hook cache to track disabled state in `struct hook_config_cache_entry`
- Modifies `list_hooks_add_configured()` to propagate the disabled flag to `struct hook`
- Adjusts `hook_exists()` to skip disabled hooks when checking for existence
- Ensures disabled hooks are skipped during actual execution in `pick_next_hook()`
- Adds test coverage for the new behaviors in t1800-hook.sh

The patch follows the series' established patterns for config parsing and cache management while extending them to handle disabled state. It completes the core functionality for config-based hooks that has been under development across the 12-patch series, with all major technical challenges addressed in prior patches. The changes are well-contained and maintain the separation between config-based and traditional hooks that was established earlier in the series.
