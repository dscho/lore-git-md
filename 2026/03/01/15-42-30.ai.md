This is a discussion thread about refactoring editor.c to remove global state as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Shreyansh Paliwal responds to feedback from Burak Kaan Karaçay regarding their earlier patch that moved the `editor_program` global variable from environment.c to editor.c.

The exchange highlights a key tension in the refactoring effort - whether to prioritize complete elimination of state (Burak's position) or maintain existing validation behavior (Shreyansh's position). This mirrors broader architectural questions in the project about state management tradeoffs. The discussion remains technical and collegial, with Shreyansh open to further input while standing by their current design choices.
