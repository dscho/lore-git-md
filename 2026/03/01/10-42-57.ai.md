This is a refactoring patch series that's part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The two-patch series from Shreyansh Paliwal targets the global state in `editor.c`, specifically addressing two issues: the `editor_program` variable (currently defined in `environment.c` but only used in `editor.c`) and the remaining usage of `the_repository` in the sequence editor functionality.

The first patch relocates `editor_program` to be local to `editor.c` while maintaining the existing initialization path through `git_default_core_config()`. The second patch removes the dependency on `the_repository` by threading a `struct repository` parameter through `git_sequence_editor()` and its callers, allowing the file to drop its `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` requirement.

The changes are mechanical but well-contained, touching only the editor subsystem and its immediate callers. The series appears to be a straightforward contribution to the project's larger goal of eliminating global state, with cleanly separated patches and appropriate updates to both implementation and header files. The modifications follow the established pattern for `the_repository` removal work, suggesting they should integrate smoothly with the ongoing effort.
