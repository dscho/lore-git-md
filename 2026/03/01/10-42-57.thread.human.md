This higher-level design question is being deferred for now, allowing the mechanical improvements to proceed while the community continues discussing the optimal long-term solution.

The work is part of Shreyansh's Google Summer of Code project and represents solid progress on the larger `the_repository` removal effort, following the project's preferred approach of making incremental improvements even when broader design questions remain unresolved.
