This is the second patch in a series removing `the_repository` global state from editor.c, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate this variable. The patch removes the now-unneeded `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define from editor.c.

This follows the established pattern for such refactorings - the changes are localized to editor.[ch] and its callers, with no behavior changes beyond removing the global state dependency.
