This thread introduces a user interface improvement for git-gui that visually distinguishes comment lines in commit messages (those that will be stripped by `wash_commit_message`) by displaying them in gray. The patch adds Tcl code to apply a "commit_comment" tag with gray foreground color to matching lines, making it immediately obvious which parts of a draft commit message won't be included in the final commit.

The author acknowledges their limited Tcl experience and specifically invites feedback on the implementation approach, particularly regarding the duplication of the comment-matching regex from `wash_commit_message`. The patch is relatively small (19 lines added to git-gui.sh) and appears to be a straightforward quality-of-life improvement for git-gui users.
