This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about adding path-related fields to `git repo-info`. The email clarifies the relationship between Lucas Seiki Oshiro's current patch series and a similar effort by another contributor (Eslam), while addressing Phillip Wood's questions about the design approach.

Lucas explains that his work predates Eslam's patch series (which was based on earlier GSoC project ideas) and has been in development since July. A key difference is that Lucas's implementation handles the full range of path formatting options from `git rev-parse`, including four default formats (relative, relative-if-shared-root, canonical/absolute, and unmodified) plus forced relative/absolute formats. He notes that while `repo-info` isn't bound by `rev-parse`'s legacy constraints, the complex formatting options might not all be necessary for the new command.

The discussion reveals consensus forming around Phillip Wood's preference for a simpler `--path-format` flag approach rather than having different defaults per path or verbose key names for absolute/relative versions. Lucas acknowledges the tradeoff - this prevents returning both absolute and relative paths in a single call, but considers the overhead acceptable since users can simply call the command twice if needed.

The email also serves to connect relevant parties by CC'ing Eslam (author of the alternative implementation), potential GSoC 2026 mentors (Karthik, Justin, Ayush, Siddhart), and Patrick Steinhardt (Lucas's original GSoC mentor who helped design the `--path-format` approach). This helps align ongoing efforts while leaving room for future contributors to implement additional `path.*` fields.
