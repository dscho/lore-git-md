This is part 2 of Tian Yuchen's 3-patch series to migrate encoding-related functionality from global variables to repository-specific storage, as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch focuses on plumbing `struct repository` through the pretty-printing APIs.

The key technical change is updating the pretty-printing functions `pretty_print_commit()` and `pp_commit_easy()` to take a `struct repository*` parameter, allowing them to access repository-specific encoding settings rather than relying on the global `log_output_encoding`. The patch updates callers across 14 files, including builtins like `log`, `merge`, `stash`, and `show-branch`, as well as lower-level components like the sequencer and range-diff machinery.

Where no repository context is readily available (particularly in builtin commands), the patch maintains backward compatibility by falling back to `the_repository`. This follows the established pattern for incremental migration of global state. The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching commit formatting paths that affect many commands.

It builds on Phillip Wood's earlier suggestion to store encodings directly in `struct repository` rather than `repo_settings`.
