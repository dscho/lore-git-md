This is a cleanup patch in Adrian Ratiu's ongoing hook configuration series, addressing a build failure caught by SZEDER Gábor. The patch moves `unsorted_string_list_remove()` from hook.c to string-list.[ch] where it more logically belongs, but introduced a build break because three call sites weren't updated to match the function's new signature (which added a `free_util` parameter). Adrian acknowledges the oversight and will fix it in v2 of the series.

The change is straightforward - the function is being relocated to a more appropriate home in the codebase as part of standardizing the infrastructure around hooks. While this particular patch is mechanical, it's part of a larger effort to improve Git's configuration system for hooks, with most of the interesting discussion happening around other patches in the series regarding output formats and metadata storage.
