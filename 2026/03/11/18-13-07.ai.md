Performance optimization for `git help` configuration listing, now being withdrawn. This follows up on Siddharth Shrimali's earlier patch that moved a `strbuf` declaration out of nested loops in `list_config_help()` to reduce allocations. While the optimization was technically sound (confirmed by Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano), Junio noted it would conflict with a larger refactoring of the same function by Amisha that restructures the configuration listing into separate helper functions (`set_config_vars()` and `set_config_sections()`).

Siddharth now acknowledges the broader refactoring makes his optimization unnecessary, as the new structure already addresses the allocation pattern he was trying to improve. He withdraws the patch and indicates he'll focus on other contributions instead. This illustrates how even small, valid optimizations can become redundant when larger architectural changes are underway, and the importance of coordinating with parallel work in the same code area. The thread shows good technical judgment in recognizing when to step back from a local improvement that no longer fits the evolving design.
