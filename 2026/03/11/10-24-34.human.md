This echoes Junio's earlier suggestion about improving the machine-readable output.

Second, Patrick notes the somewhat ugly but functional approach of packing the config scope enum into the string list's util pointer, acknowledging it works but pointing out the alternative of heap allocation. The thread context shows this is part of Adrian's work to standardize hook infrastructure, with all feedback being constructively incorporated into the upcoming v2.
