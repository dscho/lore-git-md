The exchange highlights an ongoing tension in Git's CLI design between human readability and machine parseability. Both contributors seem aligned on moving toward a more structured output format, though they acknowledge the project's general reluctance to adopt standard formats like JSON. This discussion directly impacts patch 10/10 in Adrian's series, which adds disabled hook display functionality.
