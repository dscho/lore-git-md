The change is mechanical and behavior-preserving, affecting 46 files across various subsystems including built-ins, test helpers, and core commands. Each conversion follows the established pattern of making the global repository dependency explicit at call sites. The patch includes the expected updates to header includes and macro definitions needed to maintain compilation.

This completes the systematic elimination of `the_repository` from `start_command()` calls, building on the previous patches that introduced `repo_start_command()` and converted callers in stages. The series has progressed smoothly with no objections raised, demonstrating the effectiveness of the incremental approach to this project-wide refactoring effort.
