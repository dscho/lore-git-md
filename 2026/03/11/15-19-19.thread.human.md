The final patch, just submitted, uses a Coccinelle semantic patch to mechanically convert the remaining 46 call sites across built-ins, test helpers, and core commands. The changes are behavior-preserving and maintain backward compatibility during the transition.

The series has progressed smoothly with no objections, demonstrating the effectiveness of the incremental approach to this project-wide refactoring.
