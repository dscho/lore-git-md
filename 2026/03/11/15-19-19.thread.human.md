This thread continues the project-wide effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable, focusing on converting callers of `start_command()`. The initial patch introduced `repo_start_command()` as a repository-aware replacement while maintaining backward compatibility. The new patch converts the first batch of callers that already have repository context available, touching five files across different subsystems. The changes are mechanical replacements that pass the local repository pointer instead of relying on the global.

The series follows the established pattern for these conversions - starting with straightforward cases where callers already have the necessary context, before moving to more complex situations. The approach maintains backward compatibility throughout the transition. No objections or open questions have been raised; this appears to be a routine continuation of the long-running `the_repository` removal effort that René Scharfe has been leading. The next patches in the series will presumably handle the remaining callers, including those that may require more involved changes.
