Pablo has proposed extending their GSoC project on `git cat-file --remote-object-info` to include functionality that would analyze tree objects in partial clones and report missing blobs, potentially sorted by size. This would help users understand what objects they lack before fetching. The idea is still in early discussion, with questions remaining about whether it belongs in cat-file and how it would integrate with existing partial clone workflows.

Chandra Pratap, in a mentoring response, advised Pablo to treat this as future work rather than including it in their main GSoC scope. They suggested Pablo explore the concept further in the pre-GSoC period and submit an RFC patch to make the proposal more concrete, pointing to Junio Hamano's example RFCs as a model. The discussion now awaits Pablo's response on whether they'll pursue an RFC implementation and how this feedback will shape their GSoC application. Other community members may yet weigh in on the technical merits of the proposal itself.
