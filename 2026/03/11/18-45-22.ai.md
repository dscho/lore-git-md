This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about the recent `start_command()` conversion series. Jeff King (Peff) concurs with René Scharfe's earlier suggestion that the mechanical conversion approach taken in the original patch series could be reconsidered for a more targeted solution.

The key observation is that only 3 callers (`gc`, `pull`, and `auto-maintenance`) actually use the `close_object_store` functionality that creates the repository dependency. Rather than converting all 46 callers to pass a repository parameter (as the original series did), they propose storing an explicit object store pointer in `struct child_process` when needed, leaving most callers unchanged.

This exchange highlights an important design tension in the global variable removal project - whether to mechanically convert all call sites or to redesign interfaces to minimize churn. While the current series is complete, this discussion may influence future similar conversions. The thread shows consensus among experienced contributors (Scharfe and King) that interface redesign can sometimes be preferable to mechanical conversion when dependencies are narrowly scoped.
