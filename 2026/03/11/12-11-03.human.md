This is a platform compatibility patch series updating `imap-send`'s OpenSSL usage to modern APIs. The changes are motivated by upcoming OpenSSL 4.0 compatibility needs, though they also work with OpenSSL 1.1.0+.

The first two patches fix compilation issues by replacing deprecated OpenSSL access patterns with their modern equivalents for reading SSL certificate fields (subject alternative names and common names). The last two patches are cleanups that remove now-unnecessary string length checks and refactor hostname matching logic.

The series appears well-scoped and focused - each commit has a clear purpose, and the changes are limited to `imap-send.c`. The author notes they're dropping support for pre-1.1.0 OpenSSL versions, which seems reasonable given those versions are over 7 years old. The patches look like straightforward maintenance work rather than introducing new functionality.
