This is a platform compatibility note regarding Git's maintenance crontab entries. D. Ben Knoble reports that some cron implementations (specifically fcron) fail to properly parse the crontab entries generated by Git's maintenance setup. He has filed a bug report with the fcron project and suggests this may not require immediate changes in Git since workarounds exist and the issue may be fixed upstream.

The message serves as a heads-up to the community about this interoperability issue, particularly for users of alternative cron implementations. The author doesn't propose any Git-side changes at this time, but the information could be relevant for troubleshooting maintenance scheduling problems on affected systems.
