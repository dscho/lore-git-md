The patch modifies `builtin/mktree.c` to pass the repository pointer explicitly through the call chain from `cmd_mktree()` down to helper functions, replacing direct accesses to the global with the passed parameter. The changes are straightforward - updating hash algorithm access and OID parsing to use repository-specific versions.

The author notes they initially looked at addressing a more complex FIXME in the test suite but pivoted to this simpler cleanup opportunity. They raise two questions in the commit message: one confirming that `oid_to_hex()` still relies on global state (which is correct to do at this stage), and another about how to properly CC domain experts for review of such mechanical changes. The patch appears correct and aligns with the project's direction, requiring no test modifications since it's purely an internal refactoring with no behavior change.
