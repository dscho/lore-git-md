Junio acknowledges the logical progression of the approach - first introducing a wrapper function, then migrating callers, and finally converting `start_command()` to a macro. The maintainer's feedback indicates the series is conceptually sound but may need adjustments based on deeper technical considerations around repository transitions. This reflects the careful balancing act required in large-scale refactorings between mechanical conversion and thoughtful interface design.
