This is a feature patch discussion in Adrian Ratiu's parallel hook execution series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to v5 patch 9/12, which added warnings for misconfigured hook job counts when users confuse friendly-names with event names.

Patrick acknowledges the technical correctness of the warning system but raises a broader design question about whether the configuration namespaces should be more strictly separated to prevent this confusion entirely. He explicitly states he won't push for such a change at this stage, indicating this is more of a philosophical observation than a blocking concern.

The exchange shows the series is nearing completion with only minor design considerations remaining. Patrick's comment suggests the current approach - warning rather than enforcing strict separation - is acceptable for now, though the namespace overlap may warrant future attention. This maintains the series' momentum toward integration while leaving the door open for potential refinements later.
