This is a refactoring patch series targeting Git's xdiff subsystem, now in its third iteration. The series comes from Ezekiel Newren, who is known for his work on Git's merge machinery and Rustification efforts.

The v3 series builds on the v2 changes by addressing all compiler warnings that appear when building with DEVELOPER=1. The core focus remains improving the readability of `xdl_cleanup_records()` in xdiff's xprepare.c, with changes like:
- Using more precise types (ptrdiff_t/size_t) to avoid signed/unsigned warnings
- Making limit calculations more explicit
- Simplifying the INVESTIGATE action logic flow
- Removing a local `recs` pointer in favor of direct array indexing

The series is purely internal refactoring with no behavior or performance changes claimed. The changes appear well-scoped and focused on maintainability improvements to this complex diff algorithm component. The maintainer has already approved the type improvements in previous iterations, and this version addresses the final build warnings that were identified.

This continues to be "part 3" of a larger xdiff cleanup effort, though the relationship to other parts remains unspecified.
