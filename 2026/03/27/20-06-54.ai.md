This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's v3 series addressing pack-objects issues during geometric repacking. The patch specifically handles cases where MIDX bitmap generation fails due to packs not being closed under reachability.

The key change is marking non-MIDX packs above the geometric split as "excluded-open" (with '!' prefix) rather than "excluded-closed" (with '^'). This ensures pack-objects will properly rescue objects that become reachable through these packs, fixing a subtle edge case where once-cruft objects might otherwise be lost. The patch includes:

1. Logic in `builtin/repack.c` to determine when to use the '!' marker based on whether packs were in the previous MIDX
2. A test case that now passes (previously marked as `test_expect_failure`) demonstrating the fix for once-cruft objects becoming reachable
3. Documentation explaining the rare edge cases where pre-existing MIDXs might still have issues

The solution maintains backward compatibility while fixing the reachability problem, and the series as a whole has received positive reviews from multiple core contributors. This appears to be the final polished version of the fix, ready for merging into the geometric repacking infrastructure.
