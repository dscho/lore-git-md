This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's v3 series addressing pack-objects issues during geometric repacking. The patch specifically fixes a subtle edge case where packs above the geometric split that weren't in the previous MIDX could incorrectly be treated as closed under reachability, potentially leaving objects stranded.

The solution introduces a new '!' marker (excluded-open) for these packs, ensuring pack-objects will traverse through them during the follow pass to rescue any reachable objects. The change is well-motivated by the scenario where once-cruft objects become reachable but would otherwise be missed. The patch includes both the core fix in `builtin/repack.c` and updates a test case that now passes, demonstrating the solution works.

This completes the series' goal of properly handling reachability during geometric repacking with MIDX bitmaps, particularly when dealing with cruft packs. The approach has been thoroughly reviewed by key contributors (Derrick Stolee, Jeff King, Patrick Steinhardt) and appears ready for merging. The changes are focused and maintain backward compatibility while fixing a real edge case in the pack-objects machinery.
