This is a follow-up in the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort, where Patrick Steinhardt responds to Aaron Paterson's proposal for git-local-* helpers as a mechanism for alternative storage backends. Patrick outlines GitLab's broader roadmap for pluggable object databases over the next several months, suggesting this foundational work needs to land before helper-style extensions would be practical.

Patrick shares that GitLab has about 10 more patch series pending in this area, building toward making the in-memory, loose, and packed backends into proper ODB sources with backend-specific logic for consistency checks, optimizations, and packfile generation. He notes Justin Tobler is separately revamping write transactions, which will eventually handle packfile writing. The timeline suggests these changes will span at least two more Git releases before the foundation stabilizes.

While supportive of the general direction, Patrick hints that GitLab's approach may favor shared object plugins over helper executables. He offers to coordinate off-list to avoid stepping on each other's work, acknowledging the complexity of concurrent changes in this area. The email provides useful context about the scale of planned changes while maintaining a collaborative tone about eventual alignment.
