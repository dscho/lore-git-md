This makes sense as a follow-up to the earlier introduction of flexible formatting - if users want wrapping, they can now choose it explicitly rather than having it forced upon them.

The implementation is straightforward, removing the unconditional `strbuf_add_wrapped_text` call and its associated buffer. The documentation update accurately reflects that "modern" now includes wrapping while other formats do not. This appears to be a logical refinement of the feature, giving users more precise control over formatting while maintaining reasonable defaults.
