He notes the current documentation's wording about "non-option arguments" is confusing since pathspecs (the only non-option arguments) are actually allowed with proper syntax.

The patch aims to resolve the discrepancy Junio identified, though Bernet acknowledges the trade-off between grammatical precision and clarity.
