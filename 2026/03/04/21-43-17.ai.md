This is a maintainer discussion thread about finalizing the scope of a refactoring series that introduces type-safe cleanup for oidmap structures. The series originally had 5 patches but is being narrowed down to just the first two after maintainer feedback.

Junio confirms Patrick Steinhardt's position that only the first two patches (converting rev-list and odb to use the new `oidmap_clear_with_free()` API) should be kept, dropping the remaining three. He notes that while a v3 iteration is needed to rename the callback as Patrick requested, there's no need to resend the patches that are being dropped (3/5 through 5/5).

The key points:
- The list-objects-filter conversion (patch 3/5) is definitively dropped per maintainer consensus that its benefits weren't clear enough
- The sequencer conversion (patches 4/5 and 5/5) is also being dropped from the series
- Only the rev-list and odb conversions will move forward in the next version
- A v3 is still needed to address Patrick's callback naming request

This email represents the final decision on the series scope, with Junio and Patrick in agreement about which conversions provide clear value. The thread shows the maintainers working to refine the series to focus on the most justified use cases of the new API.
