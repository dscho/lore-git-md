This thread discusses modernizing Git's build system by deprecating autoconf in favor of Meson, with a target removal timeline of Git 2.55 (~1.5 years out). Patrick Steinhardt initially proposed replacing autoconf with Meson, but Junio Hamano clarified that autoconf is merely a tool that generates Makefiles—not the build system itself. This shifted the discussion to whether the project should (1) keep autoconf-generated Makefiles alongside Meson, (2) move to direct Makefile usage without autoconf, or (3) fully transition to Meson as the primary build system.

Patrick has since responded, emphasizing autoconf as the "least-loved" part of the build system—difficult to maintain and not the recommended approach (direct Makefile usage is typically suggested instead). While he leans toward removing autoconf while keeping Meson support (option 1), Junio's earlier comments suggest he may prefer modernizing the existing Make-based approach (option 2). The discussion now hinges on resolving this fundamental direction before proceeding with any deprecation timeline. The key open questions include long-term maintenance costs of dual build systems and how packager needs factor into the decision. Patrick is expected to refine his proposal based on Junio's feedback.
