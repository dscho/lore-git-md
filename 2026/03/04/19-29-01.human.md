This is a patch series update in the ongoing refactoring of Git's oidmap cleanup mechanism. The author Seyi Kuforiji is responding to feedback from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt about patch 3/5 in the series, which would have applied the new type-safe cleanup to the list-objects-filter subsystem.

The maintainers have concluded this particular conversion doesn't provide enough benefit to justify the change, with Patrick suggesting it might actually confuse readers about the purpose of the refactoring. Seyi acknowledges this feedback and confirms they'll drop patch 3/5 from the next version of the series.

This leaves the series with four approved patches that focus on more clearly beneficial conversions in the rev-list, odb, and sequencer subsystems. The thread shows the project's typical refinement process where maintainers help authors focus changes where they'll have the most impact.
