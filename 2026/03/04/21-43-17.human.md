This is a patch series discussion about refactoring Git's oidmap cleanup mechanism. The maintainers have reached consensus to drop the third patch (which targeted list-objects-filter) from the series, as its benefits were unclear. The first two patches (converting rev-list and odb) have been approved and will proceed, while the sequencer conversion (patch 4/5) remains pending due to an open technical question about cmpfn checks.

Junio Hamano notes that if only the first two patches are kept, the author may not need to resend the series, though a v3 would still be needed to address Patrick Steinhardt's requested callback renaming. The discussion shows the maintainers carefully evaluating each conversion case to focus only where the type-safe cleanup provides clear value.
