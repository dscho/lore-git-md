This is a build system proposal marking the beginning of autoconf deprecation in favor of Meson. Patrick Steinhardt, who has been involved in the Meson transition, proposes to formally start the deprecation process with a target removal in Git 2.55 (roughly 1.5 years from now). The series consists of two patches: updating INSTALL to recommend Meson over autoconf, and adding deprecation warnings to configure.ac itself.

The change comes after previous discussions that reached rough consensus on moving away from autoconf, with Meson having been introduced as the replacement and now considered stable. The proposal acknowledges that feedback from packagers may require timeline adjustments, showing awareness of downstream impact. The technical changes are minimal at this stage - primarily documentation and warning messages rather than functional removal.

This represents a significant milestone in Git's build system modernization, though the actual removal would still be some time away. The RFC nature suggests Patrick is seeking confirmation that the community remains aligned with the earlier consensus before proceeding with concrete deprecation steps. The patch is likely to generate discussion about timing and packager readiness, but appears well-positioned technically given Meson's established presence in the codebase.
