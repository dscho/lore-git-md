This is a platform compatibility patch addressing Windows support for the fsmonitor daemon's pthread_cond_timedwait() implementation. The patch is part of the larger fsmonitor thread that brings Linux support to parity with existing Windows and macOS backends.

Paul Tarjan responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review feedback on the v7 patch, confirming that directly returning ETIMEDOUT for WAIT_TIMEOUT matches POSIX specifications. He explains why he chose not to modify the existing err_win_to_posix() mapping (which currently translates ERROR_TIMEOUT to EBUSY), as that could have unintended consequences for other callers. The change is already incorporated in v8 of the patch series.

This is a focused technical response that resolves one of the remaining open questions in the fsmonitor thread. The discussion shows careful consideration of both POSIX compatibility and the potential impact on existing code.
