This is a bugfix patch addressing a memory leak in Git's filesystem monitoring (fsmonitor) daemon. The patch is part of a larger series introducing Linux support for fsmonitor using inotify.

The specific issue fixed here involves the `state.cookies` hashmap in the fsmonitor daemon, where cookie names allocated via `strbuf_detach()` were not being properly freed during daemon shutdown. Paul Tarjan acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt's review comment pointing out this oversight and confirms the fix in v8 of the patch series now properly iterates through the hashmap to free each cookie name before clearing the hashmap.

This appears to be a straightforward and necessary cleanup patch in an otherwise complex series. The fix is well-contained and clearly motivated by the kind of careful memory management that's particularly important for long-running daemon processes like fsmonitor. The patch demonstrates good responsiveness to review feedback, with the author quickly identifying and addressing the issue.
