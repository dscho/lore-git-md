This is a bugfix patch addressing an interaction between `git log -L` (line-log) and pickaxe options (-G/-S/--find-object). The patch is part of a two-patch series where the first patch attempts to fix a crash by bypassing `diffcore_std()` to preserve rename pairs needed for line-log's rename detection.

The thread has evolved beyond just fixing the crash, with Junio Hamano raising architectural questions about the approach. The maintainer suggests that line-log should perhaps use its own `diff_options` struct rather than reusing the one from command-line options, similar to how `blame.c` handles rename detection. The author confirms this would be a better approach and plans to update the patch series accordingly.

Key technical details:
- The current patch works around the issue by skipping `diffcore_std()` to preserve rename pairs
- Junio questions whether `--pickaxe-all` could help, but the author explains it wouldn't solve the fundamental issue
- The solution may involve creating a dedicated `diff_options` for line-log's rename tracking needs
- Files modified: `line-log.c`, `builtin/log.c`, `t/t4211-line-log.sh`
- The fix prevents crashes but doesn't yet make pickaxe options work with -L
