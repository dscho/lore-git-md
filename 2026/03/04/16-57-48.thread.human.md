This thread addresses a critical bug in Git's promisor-remote subsystem where recursive lazy fetches could cause runaway resource consumption (demonstrated by a production incident writing 276GB of promisor packs in 90 minutes). Paul Tarjan's v3 patch provides a client-side fix by propagating `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH=1` to prevent recursion while maintaining correct delta resolution, with packet traces and reproduction scripts confirming the solution works.

While the core fix is validated, Patrick Steinhardt raised questions about `fetch_objects()` persistently rewriting blob filter configuration - an adjacent issue not blocking the immediate fix. After a month without resolution, maintainer Junio Hamano has now dropped the topic from his integration branches as standard housekeeping, signaling the patch needs either clarification on the remaining points or confirmation that those concerns can be addressed later. The technical consensus remains strong for merging the recursion fix, but the thread is effectively on hold until Paul either responds to the open questions or confirms they can be decoupled.
