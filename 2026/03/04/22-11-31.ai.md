This is a technical discussion about the hashfile interface consolidation in Patrick Steinhardt's performance optimization series. Junio C Hamano questions the design choice of splitting parameters between direct arguments and the new `hashfd_options` struct in the `hashfd_ext()` function.

Junio notes that while grouping some parameters into a struct provides future extensibility, he questions why only `progress` and `buffer_len` were selected for the struct while leaving `algop`, `fd`, and `name` as direct parameters. He specifically suggests that `algop` and `fd` might be better candidates for inclusion in the struct since they would typically remain constant across multiple operations on the same stream.

The exchange represents a final design review point in the otherwise mature optimization series, where Junio is ensuring the API design follows consistent principles before accepting the change. This follows earlier positive reviews of other components in the series and addresses one of the last remaining architectural questions about the unified hashfile implementation.
