This is a review comment in the ongoing fsmonitor Linux inotify support thread. Patrick Steinhardt points out two issues with patch 5/10 of the series, which renames the macOS fsmonitor IPC implementation to "unix" to reflect broader applicability.

Patrick notes the commit message inaccurately claims "deduplication" when the patch is actually just renaming the file in preparation for future sharing between macOS and Linux. He also suggests removing two unrelated changes from the patch: adding a BUG() check for NULL worktree and some whitespace cleanup. These should either be separate commits or part of patches where they are more logically connected.

The comment continues the thread where Patrick has been providing detailed technical review of the fsmonitor implementation, particularly around proper resource management and accurate commit messages. His latest feedback focuses on keeping patches focused and accurately describing their changes, while the series as a whole works toward unifying Unix platform support for fsmonitor IPC.
