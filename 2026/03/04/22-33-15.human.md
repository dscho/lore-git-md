This is a bugfix patch addressing an interaction between `git log -L` (line-log) and pickaxe options (-G/-S/--find-object). The patch series aims to fix a crash that occurs when these options are combined, caused by diffcore_pickaxe() discarding diff pairs needed for line-log's rename detection.

In this follow-up email, author Michael Montalbo responds to Junio C Hamano's review feedback. The key points:

1. Montalbo explains that using `--pickaxe-all` wouldn't fully solve the issue, as it still discards rename-only commits (where no content changes match the pattern).

2. Junio suggested line-log might be misusing the diff machinery by reusing the user's diff_options rather than creating its own dedicated one for rename tracking. Montalbo agrees this is a better architectural approach and proposes to follow the pattern from `blame.c`'s rename handling in the next version.

The discussion has evolved from just fixing the crash to considering deeper architectural improvements to how line-log handles diffs. While the immediate crash fix remains valid, Junio's feedback indicates the solution may need refinement to better separate line-log's internal diff needs from user-facing options.
