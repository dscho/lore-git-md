Performance optimization in the sideband subsystem, part of Patrick Steinhardt's larger series reducing write syscalls in `git-upload-pack`. This patch refactors `send_sideband()` to use `writev()` for sending pktlines, combining what were previously two separate writes (header and data) into a single syscall. Junio C Hamano reviews the change positively, calling it "straight-forward" and "looking good."

The optimization reduces write syscalls from ~133,000 to ~67,000 when sending sideband data, continuing the series' theme of reducing kernel contention under high concurrency. The implementation uses the new `writev_or_die()` infrastructure introduced earlier in the series, with careful handling of both banded and non-banded packet headers. The change is relatively small (14 lines added/changed) but impactful, demonstrating the kind of targeted optimization that can yield significant performance improvements in Git's networking code.
