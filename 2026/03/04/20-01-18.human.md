This is a bugfix discussion thread about `git log -L` (line-log) interactions with pickaxe options (-G/-S/--find-object). Junio C Hamano is responding to Michael Montalbo's patch that fixed a crash when these options, but questions whether the solution is architecturally sound.

He expresses concern that line-log may be misusing the diff machinery by piggybacking on user-supplied diff options when it really needs its own configuration for rename tracking.

The thread shows a maintainer carefully considering not just whether a patch works, but whether it's the right long-term direction. Junio's comments indicate this fix might be treating symptoms rather than addressing the underlying architectural question of how line-log should interact with diffcore. This suggests the discussion may continue with Michael either justifying the current approach or revising the patches to address Junio's concerns.
