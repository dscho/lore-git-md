This is a build system proposal to deprecate Git's autoconf infrastructure in favor of Meson. Patrick Steinhardt, who has been involved in modernizing Git's build system, suggests removing autoconf support in Git 2.55 (about a year from now) based on previous discussions and Meson's stability.

The two-patch series updates INSTALL to recommend Meson over autoconf and adds deprecation warnings to configure.ac. The proposal acknowledges that feedback from packagers may require timeline adjustments. This follows a 1.5-year-old discussion where the community broadly agreed to move away from autoconf.

The change is significant as it continues Git's build system modernization, but the RFC nature suggests Patrick is open to discussion about timing and implementation details. The patches appear straightforward, focusing on documentation and warning messages rather than immediate removal of functionality.
