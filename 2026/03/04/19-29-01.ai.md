This is a patch series update in the ongoing refactoring of Git's oidmap cleanup mechanism. The email confirms the decision to drop patch 3/5 from the series, which would have converted the list-objects-filter subsystem to use the new type-safe `oidmap_clear_with_free()` API.

The thread context shows maintainers Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt agreed this particular conversion offered minimal benefit, as the existing cleanup pattern was already straightforward. The new version will focus only on cases where the type-safety improvements are clearly justified, specifically the rev-list and odb conversions that were already approved. The sequencer conversion remains under discussion pending resolution of a technical question about `map->map.cmpfn` checks.

The author Seyi Kuforiji acknowledges the feedback and indicates they will send an updated series without the list-objects-filter change. This reflects the project's typical workflow where maintainers help shape patch scope through iterative review, and contributors adapt their submissions accordingly.
