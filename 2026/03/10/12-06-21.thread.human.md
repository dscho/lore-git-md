This thread covers an 8-patch series that refactors repository-specific global variables into a new `struct repo_config_values`, part of Git's ongoing effort to reduce global state and improve repository isolation. The series systematically moves various environment-related settings - including filesystem metadata handling, compression levels, Unicode normalization, sparse checkout behaviors, and object/refname collision warnings - from global variables to per-repository storage.

Each patch follows the same careful pattern: removing a global from environment.h/c, adding it to the repository-specific struct, and updating all call sites to access through `the_repository`. The work builds on prior infrastructure and aligns with René Scharfe's long-running `the_repository` elimination effort. The series was mentored by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi, demonstrating Git's onboarding process for new contributors through focused refactoring tasks.

Christian recently provided a minor review comment on the cover letter, noting that since the base series has now been merged to master, the reference to it being unmerged should be removed. This small documentation cleanup marks the final touch on the series. With no technical objections raised throughout the thread and all planned variables now migrated, this work appears complete and ready for integration.
