This thread discusses an 8-patch series that continues the project's ongoing effort to eliminate global state by moving repository-specific variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series systematically converts several globals (including compression levels, sparse checkout settings, and Unicode handling flags) to per-repository storage, following the established pattern of accessing them through `the_repository`.

Christian Couder provided initial review feedback on the cover letter documentation, noting that a reference to the base series could now be removed since it has been merged. Olamide Bello, the series author, promptly acknowledged this and confirmed the change would be made. Earlier in the thread, Tian Yuchen had identified a more substantive technical issue regarding `pack_compression_level` handling - while most functions now read from repository storage, the command-line option parsing still modifies a local variable. Olamide has acknowledged this oversight and plans to fix it in future versions.

The patches are mechanical but wide-ranging, touching 47 files across core subsystems. With the documentation tweak noted and the compression level issue flagged for resolution, the series appears to be in good shape for its next iteration. This work builds on prior infrastructure changes and aligns with René Scharfe's broader `the_repository` elimination effort.
