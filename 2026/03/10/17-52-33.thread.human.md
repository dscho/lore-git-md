Patrick Steinhardt continues his series reorganizing Git's build infrastructure by moving Coccinelle semantic patches from `contrib/coccinelle/` to the new `tools/coccinelle/` directory. This change reflects Coccinelle's role as a core development tool rather than optional contrib material. The patch updates all references across the codebase - including Makefile rules, CI scripts, Meson configuration, documentation, and the spatchcache helper - while keeping the semantic patch files themselves unchanged.

This is the second of eight patches in the series, following the initial creation of the `tools/` directory. The change is mechanical but thorough, maintaining all functionality while better organizing essential build tools. The series has two parallel goals: consolidating scattered development tooling and adding Meson precompiled header support for faster compilation. So far, the author is the only participant in the thread, but reviewers like D. Ben Knoble (build system expert) and Johannes Schindelen (Windows/CMake) will likely weigh in as the series progresses, particularly on the upcoming Meson PCH changes that claim ~30% compilation speed improvements.
