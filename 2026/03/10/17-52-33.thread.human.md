Patrick Steinhardt has proposed reorganizing Git's build infrastructure by creating a new `tools/` directory to consolidate scattered development tooling (Coccinelle scripts, coverage-diff.sh, build script generators) while also adding precompiled header support to the Meson build system. The 8-part series shows promising results, with PCH support cutting build times by 40% in benchmarks.

Junio Hamano, while not opposing the changes, has raised a practical concern about the Coccinelle relocation potentially disrupting developer workflows if it changes where generated files appear. This is a cautionary note rather than a blocking objection - the series appears technically sound and likely to proceed, but may need minor adjustments if the Coccinelle move proves problematic for regular users. Other build system experts (particularly D. Ben Knoble and Johannes Schindelen) may want to weigh in on the workflow implications before finalizing the changes.

The PCH implementation itself - which precompiles the massive git-compat-util.h header - seems uncontested, having demonstrated significant performance gains without introducing platform issues. The series builds on prior build system cleanup work and represents another step in Git's ongoing modernization of its development toolchain.
