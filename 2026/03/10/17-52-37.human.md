This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's 8-part series reorganizing Git's build infrastructure. The patch moves the `update-unicode.sh` script and its supporting files from `contrib/` to the new `tools/` directory, continuing the series' goal of consolidating developer-facing tooling.

The change is straightforward - it simply relocates the Unicode update script (used to refresh Git's Unicode character data when new standards are released) without modifying its functionality. The patch follows the same pattern as earlier parts of the series, treating this as essential build infrastructure rather than optional contrib material.

As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a well-justified reorganization with no behavioral changes. The series is progressing methodically, with each patch handling a discrete piece of the build system cleanup before moving on to the Meson precompiled header work in later patches. Reviewers will likely focus on whether the new `tools/` directory organization makes sense as a whole rather than debating this individual move.
