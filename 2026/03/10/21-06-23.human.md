This is a follow-up discussion about naming in the recently merged `git fast-import` signature handling series. The thread focuses on whether the newly added `re-sign-if-invalid` mode should be renamed to `sign-if-invalid` for greater accuracy.

Junio Hamano suggests that "sign" would be more precise than "re-sign" since the operation creates a fresh signature rather than modifying an existing one. Justin Tobler acknowledges the point, noting that "if-invalid" already implies the object was previously signed, making "re-" redundant. He's open to sending a follow-up patch to rename the option to `sign-if-invalid` and update the corresponding enum value.

The discussion is technical but low-stakes - the functionality is already merged and working, this is just about potentially improving the naming.
