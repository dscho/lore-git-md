This is a follow-up discussion in the completed `git fast-import` signature handling series, where Junio C Hamano is suggesting future directions for the feature. The original series added a `sign-if-invalid` mode to re-sign commits with invalid signatures, and Junio is proposing an extension to also sign previously unsigned commits.

Junio notes that the infrastructure built for re-signing invalid signatures could naturally extend to signing unsigned commits as well, suggesting a potential `--sign-commits=all` option. He's responding to Justin Tobler's observation that the current `--signed-commits` options don't provide enough granularity for handling both unsigned and invalidly-signed commits separately.

This exchange shows the maintainer thinking ahead about how the new signing capability might evolve, while acknowledging the current implementation's focus on just the invalid signature case. The discussion remains conceptual at this point, with no patches proposed for the extended functionality.
