This is a refactoring patch in Adrian Ratiu's hook configuration series, specifically addressing feedback from Junio Hamano on patch 8/10. The patch originally introduced a `struct hook_config_cache` wrapper around a `strmap` to store hook configurations, with the goal of making the structure extensible for future metadata storage.

Junio questioned the necessity of the wrapper struct, noting that since the expansion happens in the entry-level `struct hook_config_cache_entry`, the top-level wrapper adds unnecessary indirection without clear benefit. Adrian agrees with this assessment and confirms he will simplify the approach in v2 by keeping the cache as a bare `strmap` while maintaining the entry struct for individual hook configurations.

The change touches hook.c, hook.h, and repository.h, but is purely a structural refactoring with no behavior changes. This continues Adrian's ongoing work to improve Git's hook configuration subsystem, building on his previously accepted config-based hook work. The series is currently queued for integration with only minor design questions remaining.
