This is a follow-up in a thread about improving `git format-patch` cover letter generation, originally proposed by Junio C Hamano and implemented by Pablo Sabater as part of Google Summer of Code (GSoC). 

The patch aimed to simplify cover letters by replacing the author-grouped shortlog format with a simple numbered list of patches (`[1/2]: subject`), which would be more useful for single-author series. However, Junio has now pointed out that Martin Røkke (Mirko) had already proposed a similar cover letter formatting improvement in 2025.

Pablo's response acknowledges the oversight and indicates they will look for another area to contribute, effectively withdrawing this patch in light of the prior work. The exchange shows good contributor etiquette - promptly recognizing and deferring to existing efforts when made aware of them.

The thread highlights how even well-intentioned improvements can overlap with prior work, and how maintainers like Junio help steer contributors toward non-duplicative efforts. For now, this particular cover letter format change appears to be on hold pending review of Martin Røkke's earlier solution.
