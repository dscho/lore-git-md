This is the final patch in an 8-part series refactoring repository-specific global variables into `struct repo_config_values`, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch moves `warn_on_object_refname_ambiguity` - a setting controlling whether Git warns when object names could be ambiguous with refnames - from a global variable to per-repository storage.

The change follows the established pattern seen in previous patches: removing the global declaration from environment.h, adding it to the repository-specific struct, updating initialization in environment.c, and modifying all access sites to go through `the_repository`. The patch touches 7 files, with most changes being mechanical replacements of direct variable access with repository-specific lookups. Key files modified include object-name.c (where the warning is actually triggered) and several commands that temporarily disable the warning during bulk operations (cat-file, pack-objects, revision walking, and submodule handling).

The series as a whole has now moved 8 different configuration-related globals into per-repository storage, touching 47 files cumulatively. This final patch completes the mechanical portion of the series, though as with previous patches, the commit message could better explain why this particular setting benefits from repository-specific storage. The changes maintain existing behavior while eliminating cross-repository state leakage, an important step toward proper repository isolation. The work was mentored by Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi, continuing the project's pattern of onboarding new contributors through focused refactoring efforts.
