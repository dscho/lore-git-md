Performance optimization in the sideband subsystem. This patch is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to reduce lock contention in `git-upload-pack` during high-concurrency scenarios (now at v3 with 10 patches). The series focuses on cutting write syscalls through `writev()`-based buffering.

This specific change targets the sideband pktline transmission, which previously required two syscalls per packet (one for the header, one for data). By using `writev()`, it combines these into a single syscall, cutting the number of writes roughly in half (from ~133k to ~67k in testing). The implementation builds on the new `writev_or_die()` wrapper introduced earlier in the series.

The change is straightforward - replacing two `write_or_die()` calls with a single `writev_or_die()` that handles both header and data in one operation. The patch maintains all existing functionality while improving performance, and follows the pattern established in earlier parts of the series. Jeff King (Peff) suggested this approach, and the implementation appears clean with no obvious drawbacks.
