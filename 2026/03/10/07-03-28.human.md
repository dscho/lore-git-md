Performance optimization for `git help` config listing. Siddharth Shrimali improves the `list_config_help()` function in builtin/help.c by moving a `strbuf` declaration outside of loops where it was previously being repeatedly allocated and freed. The change reduces heap pressure by reusing the same buffer via `strbuf_reset()` and also fixes a minor memory leak in the SHOW_CONFIG_HUMAN case. The patch is small, focused, and clearly motivated by performance considerations.
