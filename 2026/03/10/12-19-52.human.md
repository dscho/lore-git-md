This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to make Git's object database (ODB) pluggable. The thread has already seen extensive review and was merged to the 'next' integration branch, but Junio Hamano raises a late-stage design question about callback contracts in the ODB interface.

Junio notes that the current implementation assumes all callbacks in the `struct odb_source` vtable are mandatory, with wrapper functions directly dereferencing function pointers. He suggests either documenting which callbacks are truly required or adding NULL checks to handle optional operations (like a read-only backend skipping write callbacks). This architectural consideration wasn't addressed during earlier review rounds.

Patrick acknowledges the point, agreeing it will become more relevant as alternative backends are developed. He notes he has pending series to convert existing sources to the new interface and will address the callback contract question in future work. The exchange is collegial and forward-looking, with both parties recognizing this as a refinement point rather than a blocker for the current series.

The discussion highlights a thoughtful late-stage review catch by Junio about interface design for future extensibility, while Patrick demonstrates the pragmatic approach of landing incremental improvements with plans to address the feedback in follow-up work. This exemplifies the project's balance between careful design and iterative development.
