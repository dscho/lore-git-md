Maintainer feedback on build system reorganization and PCH support.

Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's 8-patch series that reorganizes build tooling into a new `tools/` directory and adds Meson precompiled header support. While not objecting to the changes outright, Junio raises a practical concern about the Coccinelle relocation potentially disrupting developer workflows if the generated patch locations change. His tone is cautious but not blocking - he's flagging a potential issue for others to consider while acknowledging the series may proceed if the impact proves minimal.

The maintainer's comment suggests this series is likely to proceed but may need minor adjustments if the Coccinelle move causes unexpected workflow issues. His early warning gives other developers (particularly those using Coccinelle regularly) a chance to weigh in before the changes are finalized. The technical merits of the PCH implementation and other relocations appear uncontested at this stage.
