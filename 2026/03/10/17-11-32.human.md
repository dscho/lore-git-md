Performance optimization series addressing lock contention in `git-upload-pack` during high-concurrency scenarios. This is the final version (v3) of Patrick Steinhardt's series that reduces write syscalls through `writev()`-based buffering across multiple subsystems. The series has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano, who confirms the changes look sensible and will queue them.

The optimizations demonstrate significant improvements - reducing write syscalls from ~400k to ~44k when cloning the Linux repository. Key technical components include new `writev()` wrappers with proper overflow handling, generalized `csum-file.c` interface, and buffer management refinements that maintain progress visibility while reducing kernel lock contention.

The series also includes a bugfix removing an accidental debug message in `upload-pack.c` that had been sent to clients since 2006. While one discussion point from Jeff King about `upload-pack` buffering remains unresolved, it doesn't block these changes. The comprehensive cross-platform `writev()` compatibility infrastructure supports the optimizations across all supported Git platforms.
