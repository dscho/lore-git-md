This is a performance optimization patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing lock contention in `git-upload-pack`. The patch focuses on improving how keepalive packets interact with buffered data during high-concurrency operations.

The change makes `git-upload-pack` prioritize flushing buffered data over sending empty keepalive packets when the keepalive timer expires. While the author notes this won't be a major performance win (since most keepalives occur before pack data starts), it's a logical optimization that fits naturally with the series' buffering improvements.

The patch also includes a minor cleanup - removing a leftover comment about stack allocation that was no longer relevant after review feedback. Junio C Hamano had questioned this during review, prompting Patrick to clarify and clean up the commit message.

This is patch 3/10 in the v3 series that has already been approved for merging. The change is a small but thoughtful refinement to the larger buffering strategy, maintaining the series' focus on reducing syscalls while preserving protocol correctness.
