This is part 3 of an 8-patch series refactoring repository-specific global variables into `struct repo_config_values`, continuing the project's effort to reduce global state and improve repository isolation. This patch moves the `zlib_compression_level` setting from a global variable to per-repository storage.

The change follows the established pattern seen in previous patches: removing the global variable from environment.h/c, adding it to the repository-specific struct, and updating all call sites to access it through `the_repository`. The patch touches 6 files where zlib compression is initialized, including object-file.c (for loose objects), http-push.c (for pushing), and diff.c (for generating patches).

The commit message explains the motivation clearly - to avoid cross-repository state leakage while preserving existing functionality. The change is mechanical but important for library-style usage with multiple active repositories. The patch appears well-scoped and follows the same approach as the earlier patches in the series for `trust_ctime` and `check_stat`.

This continues René Scharfe's ongoing `the_repository` elimination effort, with mentoring from Christian Couder and Usman Akinyemi. The series is making steady progress on converting various global settings to per-repository storage.
