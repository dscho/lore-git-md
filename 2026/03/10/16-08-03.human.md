This is a follow-up in the ongoing thread about adding `ADVICE_STASH_BEFORE_CHECKOUT` advice for handling uncommitted changes during branch switches. The patch author, Arsh Srivastava, acknowledges feedback from Junio C Hamano, Karthik Nayak, and Patrick Steinhardt regarding the need to better communicate the irreversible nature of using `git checkout -m` to users.

The discussion has progressed through multiple iterations (now at v4) with significant mentoring from experienced contributors. The key remaining issue is ensuring the advice message clearly warns users about the risks of the `-m` option, which performs an irreversible merge of uncommitted changes. This follows Junio's emphasis that the messaging must prioritize user understanding of consequences, not just technical correctness.

The thread demonstrates effective mentoring of a GSOC contributor through multiple review cycles addressing integration points, test coverage, and now final UX polish around risk communication. With all technical concerns resolved in previous rounds, this final refinement focuses purely on ensuring users understand the implications of their choices when encountering this advice.
