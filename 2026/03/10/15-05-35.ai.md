This is a follow-up in a thread about improving `git format-patch` cover letter generation, now considering prior work. The original patch proposed simplifying cover letters by replacing author-grouped shortlog format with a simple numbered list of patches. 

Junio C Hamano pointed out that Martin Røkke had proposed similar cover letter improvements in 2025, raising the question of whether this duplicates existing work. Pablo Sabater (the GSoC participant who submitted the initial patch) acknowledges the oversight and indicates they'll look for another area to contribute, recognizing their solution is less comprehensive than Martin's prior work.

The exchange shows the patch likely won't proceed given the existence of a more mature solution to the same problem. The thread demonstrates Git's culture of checking for prior art before accepting changes, even when the proposed improvement comes from a maintainer's suggestion. No technical discussion of the approaches occurs - the mere existence of prior work is enough to redirect the contributor's efforts elsewhere.
