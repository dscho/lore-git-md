This is a review comment in the final iteration (v7) of a patch series adding configurable cover letter formatting to `git format-patch`. Phillip Wood suggests two improvements to the error message when the new `%(count)` and `%(total)` placeholders are used without a `rev_info` context.

The patch in question adds these placeholders to support numbered lists in cover letters, with `%(count)` showing the zero-padded current item number and `%(total)` showing the total count. Phillip notes that the current error message ("this format specifier can't be used with this command") is not as helpful as it could be - it should name the problematic specifier. He also observes that since this error case can't currently be triggered (the placeholders are only usable in contexts that provide `rev_info`), the change should be squashed into the patch that introduces their usage.

This is a minor polish suggestion on an otherwise complete series that has already been through seven iterations and received maintainer approval. The feedback is constructive but not blocking - the core functionality and design are settled, and this is about improving an edge-case error message. The author may choose to incorporate this suggestion in a final squash before merging.
