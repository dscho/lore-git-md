This is part 5 of an 8-patch series refactoring repository-specific global variables into `struct repo_config_values`, continuing the project's effort to reduce global state and improve repository isolation. The patch moves the `precomposed_unicode` setting (which controls Unicode filename normalization behavior) from a global variable to per-repository storage.

The change follows the established pattern seen in previous patches: removing the global variable from environment.h/c, adding it to the repository-specific struct, and updating all call sites to access it through `the_repository`. The patch touches 4 files (compat/precompose_utf8.c, environment.c, environment.h, upload-pack.c) with 19 insertions and 11 deletions.

This is a mechanical but important change that helps prevent cross-repository state leakage, particularly relevant for library-style usage where multiple repositories may be active in the same process. The patch maintains the existing behavior while making it properly repository-scoped. Like the rest of the series, it builds on René Scharfe's ongoing `the_repository` elimination effort.
