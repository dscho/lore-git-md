This is a follow-up clarification in Patrick Steinhardt's performance optimization series for `git-upload-pack` lock contention. The thread has already seen the full v3 series approved by Junio, with this being a minor correction to patch 3/10's commit message.

Patrick responds to Junio's query about an incomplete sentence in the original commit message ("drop the useless"). He explains it was a remnant from an earlier draft where he considered converting `output_state` to a stack variable, but kept it heap-allocated to avoid potential stack overflow. The half-sentence was accidentally left in during final editing.

The technical content of the patch itself remains unchanged - it optimizes `upload-pack` to prefer flushing buffered data over sending keepalive packets when both are possible. This was one of several optimizations in the series that collectively reduced write syscalls during clones by an order of magnitude. The exchange shows the maintainer's attention to detail in reviewing commit messages, even for approved patches.
