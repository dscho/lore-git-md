Patrick Steinhardt's patch series to introduce generic object counting interfaces as part of the ODB abstraction effort has reached a stable state. The six-part series replaces `repo_approximate_object_count()` with new `odb_source_count_objects()` and `odb_count_objects()` APIs, maintaining existing behavior while enabling future pluggable backends. 

After addressing all review feedback in v2 - including fixes to pointer initialization, documentation, and commit messages - the series has now received positive confirmation from both Toon Claes and Junio C Hamano. Toon verified the MIDX handling details and confirmed their earlier technical concerns were resolved, while Junio reviewed the overall approach. The changes touch object-file.c, packfile.c, and related headers, with careful attention to preserving caching behavior and error handling.

With no outstanding technical concerns and all identified issues addressed, this completes the object counting portion of the ODB abstraction work. The series appears ready for integration into the next maintenance cycle.
