This is a refactoring patch from Patrick Steinhardt as part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The change reorganizes header file dependencies between "odb.h" and "odb/source.h" to better reflect their logical relationship.

The patch moves enum and typedef definitions that belong to the generic ODB interface from "odb/source.h" into "odb.h", while making "odb/source.h" include "odb.h" instead of the reverse. This makes more sense architecturally since most callers should interact with the higher-level ODB interface rather than dealing with source implementations directly. The change requires adding explicit includes of "odb/source.h" in several files that previously relied on the transitive inclusion.

This appears to be a straightforward mechanical change with no behavior impact, following the pattern of incremental improvements in the ODB abstraction work. The commit message clearly explains the rationale, and the diff shows the necessary adjustments to maintain compilation across the codebase. Justin Tobler is credited with suggesting the change.
