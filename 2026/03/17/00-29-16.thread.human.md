Derrick Stolee has completed his 5-patch series enhancing `git backfill` to support targeted blob downloads in partial clones. The series now fully supports both revision arguments and pathspec filtering - including wildcard patterns - which will enable more efficient operations like `git blame` and `git log -L` in large repositories.

The final patch adds wildcard pathspec support, complementing the prefix matching from earlier patches. It introduces careful handling of complex patterns while maintaining the optimizations for simple cases. Test results show the expected reduction in blob downloads - from 48 to 40 blobs when using a pattern like "d/file.*.txt". The implementation distinguishes between exact pathspecs (using a direct optimization path) and wildcards (requiring full matching), while ensuring all relevant trees are visited for accurate results.

With this series complete, users can now specify exactly which parts of history they need when working with partial clones, significantly reducing unnecessary data transfers. The changes maintain backward compatibility while adding this powerful new filtering capability. While the series hasn't yet seen extensive review from others, the methodical implementation and thorough test coverage suggest it's ready for broader evaluation.
