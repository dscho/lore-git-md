All five patches have been reviewed and approved by Junio Hamano, with the author addressing feedback on commit messages, fallback behavior, and wildcard pathspec implementation. This is purely a formatting suggestion that doesn't affect the functionality, which Junio has already acked. Given the late stage of review, this appears to be a trivial cleanup that could be addressed in a follow-up patch if the author agrees, but it won't impact the series' readiness for merging.
