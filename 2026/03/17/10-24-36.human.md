This is a technical follow-up in Phillip Wood's series to remove `the_repository` from the worktree subsystem, specifically addressing the definition of "current worktree" semantics. The discussion has revealed that the current implementation ties "current" status to `the_repository` (the process's starting repository), while Wood's patch would base it on the worktree's own repository (`wt->repo`).

Wood provides additional justification for the change, noting that the current behavior leads to inconsistencies - when visiting submodules via separate processes, each considers itself current, but threaded visits don't. He also points out a practical optimization benefit: using `the_repository` as the determinant forces unnecessary reopening of ref stores that are already available in `wt->repo`.

The email acknowledges this is a behavior change but argues it makes the system more consistent and efficient. Wood plans to re-roll the patch with more detailed commit message explanations, though notes he'll be delayed due to other commitments. This continues the thread's careful examination of previously undocumented assumptions in the worktree API, with both Wood and Patrick Steinhardt engaging in thoughtful technical discussion about the implications of changing these semantics.
