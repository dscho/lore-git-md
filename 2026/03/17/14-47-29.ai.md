This is a response to review feedback on a GSoC proposal for improving the `git repo info` command, focusing on repository introspection and libification. Jayatheerth Kulkarni addresses Karthik Nayak's suggestions while defending some of their original design choices.

Jayatheerth pushes back on implementing glob pattern matching for now, arguing the current key set is small enough that globbing isn't yet necessary, though they're open to discussing it later. The timeline concerns are acknowledged, with Jayatheerth noting they've already doubled the allocated time for path resolution and libification work in their updated proposal. They emphasize they've built in buffer time (3 weeks for path/libification, 2 for querying) to accommodate reviews. The response shows thoughtful engagement with the feedback while standing by some technical judgments, particularly around when to introduce globbing support.
