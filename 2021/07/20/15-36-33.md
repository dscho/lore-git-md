I do not think so.  Plain-vanilla rebase is to carry forward our

```
            x--x--x (side)
           /
   ---o---o---o---o---o---o (upstream)
          ^
       (old upstream)

```

inherently ancestry relationship between the old upstream and the
current upstream when rebasing 'side' to 'upstream'.
