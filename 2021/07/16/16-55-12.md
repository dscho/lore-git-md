	NO_EXPAT=''
	NO_PERL=''
	NO_PTHREADS=''
	NO_PYTHON=''
	NO_UNIX_SOCKETS=''
	X=''

```

Why does the FSMONITOR_DAEMON_BACKEND option require a nonexistent line
as opposed to an empty one?
