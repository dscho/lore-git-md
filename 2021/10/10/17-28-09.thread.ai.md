Core problem or goal
Fix a fragile test in `t/t3420-rebase-autostash.sh` that incorrectly used `! grep dirty file3` to verify the absence of dirty content after `git rebase --quit`. The test must instead assert that `file3` does not exist at all, using `test_path_is_missing file3`.
