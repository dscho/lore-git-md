If all else fails I think it's perfectly OK to just have that failure be
triggered by a:

```
    git_env_bool("GIT_TEST_FAIL_UPDATE_REF", 1) or
    git_env_bool("GIT_TEST_FAIL_UPDATE_REF_FOR_POST_CHECKOUT", 1)

```
