This simulates what I came up with in my actual code:

$ curl --silent 
'https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/spice/win32/spice-nsis/-/commits/master' 
| raku -ne 'my Str $x=slurp(); $x~~s/.*? "rebase on " //; $x~~s/ 
("</a>") .* //; print "$x\n";'

0.164
