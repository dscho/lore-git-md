Add `cat_file_mode` member in struct `ref_format`, when
`cat-file --batch` use ref-filter logic later, it can help
us reject atoms in verify_ref_format() which cat-file cannot
use, e.g. `%(refname)`, `%(push)`, `%(upstream)`...
