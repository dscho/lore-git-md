I wonder why that is the case. A..F is so simple it doesn't have to be
explained, Ruby even expands that obvious range.

```

    from                 to

```

And B..E:

```
  ---A---B---C---D---E---F
         ^           ^
        from         to

```

In Ruby the range can be defined simply as: 'A'..'F'

  ["A", "B", "C", "D", "E", "F"]
