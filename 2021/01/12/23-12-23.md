that can be mixed and matched, but CLEANUP and VERBATIM do not make

```
	if ((flags & (CLEANUP_MSG|VERBATIM_MSG)) == (CLEANUP_MSG|VERBATIM_MSG))
		BUG("cleanup and verbatim asked at the same time");
