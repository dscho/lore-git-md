An approach along the lines of ...

```
	NUM="[0-9][0-9]*"
	sed -n -e "s/^author //p" \
	-e "s/^$OID_REGEX \($NUM $NUM $NUM\)$/\1/p"

```

... would allow you to drop "cut" and also not assume that names do
not have more than 3 tokens.
