Sidenote: 

  open BINOUT, '>', \$fullhashbinpath

should open in-memory file, and not be equivalent to

  open BINOUT, '>', "\$fullhashbinpath"

Hmmm... in the case of !$caching_enabled, $fullhasbinpath is *STDOUT.


BTW replacing 

  open BINOUT, '>', \$fullhashbinpath

with

  open BINOUT, '>&STDOUT'

or

  open BINOUT, '>&', \$fullhashbinpath

could be simpler, alternate solution.
