Bash 4.1.5:
> time (for (( i = 0; i < 200000; i++ )); do [ -z $foo ]; done)

real    0m3.430s
user    0m3.240s
sys     0m0.180s
> time (for (( i = 0; i < 200000; i++ )); do [[ -z $foo ]]; done)

real    0m2.219s
user    0m2.090s
sys     0m0.100s
