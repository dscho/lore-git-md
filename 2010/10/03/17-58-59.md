I think you should protect against defining both NO_FNMATCH and 
NO_FNMATCH_CASEFOLD (your version would link compat/fnmatch/fnmatch.o twice 
in this case):

ifdef NO_FNMATCH
...
else
ifdef NO_FNMATCH_CASEFOLD
...
endif
endif

Otherwise, the patch looks fine.
