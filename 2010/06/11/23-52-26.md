> ```diff
> +find_blame() {
> +	sed -e 's/^.*(/(/g'
> +}
> ```
>


Two issues:

 - No need for "g" as your pattern is anchored at the left;

 - As ".*" is greedy, you will eat a lot more than what you expect when
   the line in the blamed contents happen to have '(' on it.

I'd rewrite it as:

    sed -e 's/^[^(]*//'
