```
“Finished cherry-pick of commit f67ab8.” sounds more better to my ear.

	Finished cherry-pick of foo.
	Finished cherry-pick of bar.
```

```
	Finished cherry-pick of baz.
	etc

```

```
	Finished one cherry-pick.
	Finished one cherry-pick.
	Finisehd one cherry-pick.
