> I can't reproduce it with 1.5.1 here, does URI-escaping the "@{0}"

Yes.

```
 ...
 $ svn cp -m reflog file:///tmp/foo/source 'file:///tmp/foo/not-a%40%7B0%7Dreflog'

 $ (cd wc && svn update)
 A    not-a@{0}reflog
 Updated to revision 2.

```
