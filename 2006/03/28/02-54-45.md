rev-list: --parents-with-boundary

The new flag acts like --parents, but uninteresting parents are
marked by prefied '-' sign.

```
        $ git-rev-list --parents-with-boundary HEAD^^..HEAD
        acb7257... 9c48666...
        9c48666... -dff86e2..

```
