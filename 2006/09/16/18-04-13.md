> * refs/heads/pu: does not fast forward to branch 'pu' of

Did you put '+' before 'pu' line in 'origin' file in remotes?
It should read

        Pull:+refs/heads/pu:refs/heads/pu

Alternatively you can give --force option to git-fetch.
