list displays: log, shortlog, etc.  We now display:
```
	blob | commitdiff
	blob | commitdiff | diff_to_current
```

and
	tree | commitdiff
Instead of the old history format where "blob" and "tree"
are between "commitdiff" and "diff_to_current" if present/
applicable.
