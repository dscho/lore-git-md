Fast-forward doesn't result in merge.

If you have

```
  1---2---3        <branch 1, or branch locally>
           \
            4---5  <branch 2, or branch at remote>

```

then this is fast-forward case. After pull (or push) you have

  1---2---3---4---5 <branch 1>

without merge.
