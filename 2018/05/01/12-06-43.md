72518e9c26 ("more lightweight revalidation while reusing deflated
stream in packing", 2006-09-03) which added it, but Derrick Stolee

5f8b1ec1-258d-1acc-133e-a7c248b4083e@gmail.com. Makes sense to me, add
that as a comment.
