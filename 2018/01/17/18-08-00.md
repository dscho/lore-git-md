**Thread**: [thread](./18-08-00.thread.md#:~:text=2018/01/17/18-08-00)

The SMTP protocol has both, the 'Reply-To' and the 'In-Reply-To' header
fields. We only use the latter. To avoid confusion, rename the variable
for it.
