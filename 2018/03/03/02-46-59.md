**Thread**: [thread](./../../02/28/09-27-22.thread.md#:~:text=2018/03/03/02-46-59)

This saves 8 bytes in sizeof(struct object_entry). On a large
repository like linux-2.6.git (6.5M objects), this saves us 52MB
memory.
