This maintains the series' focus on the current minimal but secure validation approach while acknowledging potential for future evolution.

The discussion highlights a key design decision in the series - the choice to implement a straightforward matching mechanism rather than a more flexible validation framework. This aligns with the overall approach seen in the thread of incrementally extending the promisor-remote protocol while maintaining backward compatibility and security constraints.
