This is a policy discussion about maintaining niche contrib scripts, specifically the thunderbird-patch-inline script. Phillip Wood responds to Patrick Steinhardt's question about who would use this updated script, which provides Thunderbird integration for editing patches.

Phillip acknowledges he doesn't personally use the script much, finding the "toggle line wrap" feature more convenient (though notes compatibility issues with newer Thunderbird versions). He agrees with Patrick's earlier point that having the patch on the mailing list could be useful for future reference, which is why he sent the v2 version addressing Junio's technical feedback.

The email also notes an unrelated documentation issue - the format-patch manpage references outdated Thunderbird extensions ("external editor" and "toggle word wrap") that have been superseded by newer versions. This suggests a potential follow-up documentation update.

The core debate remains unresolved: whether to keep this niche script in contrib given its low demonstrated usage. Phillip's response doesn't strongly advocate for keeping it, which may strengthen the case for removal that Patrick and Collin Funk have been making. The discussion now seems to hinge more on general contrib maintenance policy than technical merits.
