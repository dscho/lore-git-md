This is a policy discussion about maintaining niche contrib scripts, specifically the thunderbird-patch-inline tool. Collin Funk weighs in supporting Patrick Steinhardt's position that the script should be removed rather than updated, arguing that the functionality is simple enough to do manually and questioning the long-term maintenance burden.

Collin's main points are that (1) the script's functionality can be easily replicated with basic command-line tools, and (2) there's no guarantee the replacement extension (ExternalEditor Revived) won't itself become unmaintained in the future. He demonstrates this by showing a simple workflow using `git format-patch` and `xclip` as an alternative to the script.

This email reinforces Patrick's earlier argument that contrib scripts should have clear, demonstrated users rather than being maintained for hypothetical use cases. The exchange is moving the discussion toward establishing clearer criteria for what belongs in contrib - with both Patrick and Collin favoring a more aggressive pruning approach unless there's active maintainer interest. The ball remains in Phillip Wood's court to either demonstrate personal use of the script or concede the removal.
