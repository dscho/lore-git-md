This is a test infrastructure patch in the ongoing RFC series for the `--skip-connectivity-check` option in `git receive-pack`. Justin Tobler responds to Patrick Steinhardt's feedback on the test implementation from the first patch in the series.

He plans to update the tests to capture and verify this output pattern, addressing Patrick's concern about making the connectivity failure more explicit in the test assertions.
