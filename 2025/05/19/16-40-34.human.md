This is a status update from Derrick Stolee on three patch series currently in Junio's "What's cooking" report, confirming their progress and providing brief context.

The first series (`ds/sparse-apply-add-p`) integrates sparse index support with `git apply` and `git add -i/-p`, avoiding unnecessary index expansion. Stolee notes the v2 incorporates good feedback from v1 and is now solid.

The third (`ds/path-walk-2`) introduces path-based delta compression in `git pack-objects` with a new `--path-walk` option and related configuration. Stolee acknowledges receiving feedback from Taylor Blau but indicates he won't rush a response to this v3 version.

All three series represent mature work - the first two appear ready for merging while the path-walk feature awaits final review. The email serves primarily to confirm receipt and status rather than propose new changes.
