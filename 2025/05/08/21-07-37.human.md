Build system discussion on sysinfo detection and autoconf future. Eli Schwartz confirms that Ramsay Jones's patch #5 correctly fixes sysinfo detection on Solaris in the autoconf build, providing a technical ACK. However, they raise a broader question about whether maintaining autoconf support is worthwhile given the ongoing Meson migration. 

The email notes that recent autoconf fixes (including this sysinfo check and a prior Cygwin fix) were made by developers who don't actually use autoconf themselves, rather were discovered while working on Meson or config.mak.uname. This prompts Schwartz to question who still relies on autoconf, pointing to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier comment that Meson could soon replace autoconf once git-gui/gitk support is complete.

The discussion appears to be shifting from a technical fix to a project policy question about build system maintenance. While the specific sysinfo fix is validated, the thread may now turn toward evaluating autoconf's future role in the project.
