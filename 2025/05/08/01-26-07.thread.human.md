Junio Hamano's latest "What's cooking" report provides a snapshot of Git's development, with several significant efforts underway. The email also notes upcoming deprecation of the legacy `git whatchanged` command.

Many topics are marked as expecting rerolls, indicating active discussion and refinement. Junio's branch assignments show the typical progression from `seen` (early integration testing) to `next` (more stable) to `master` (ready for release), with the object store work currently in `seen` while smaller fixes are further along.
