Performance optimization for `git repack` when handling promisor packs with kept packs. This is a follow-up to Han Young's patch that improves upon the initial fix by Tomáš Trnka for ignoring `--keep-pack` and `.keep` files during promisor repacking.

The key improvement here is that the patch avoids enumerating objects from kept packs entirely during promisor repacking, rather than just skipping them later in the packing process. This provides significant performance boost - in test cases, repack time dropped from 67.6s to 18.8s, and the resulting pack was smaller (1.6MB vs 4.5MB). The patch achieves this by marking `--keep-pack` packs as kept in-core and then skipping both in-core and on-disk kept packs during object enumeration.
