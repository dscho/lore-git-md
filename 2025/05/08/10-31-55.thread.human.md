For OAuth support, the series adds comprehensive documentation covering both Gmail and Outlook authentication methods. The final patch completes this by listing Aditya's own credential helpers (`git-credential-gmail` and `git-credential-outlook`) alongside existing options in the official documentation. These helpers provide cross-platform OAuth support specifically for `git-send-email`.

The changes are well-contained, touching only the email-sending infrastructure. While the FQDN validation required careful regex refinement across multiple versions, the OAuth documentation additions are straightforward but important for users needing modern authentication methods.
