This is the final patch in a series improving Git's email functionality and documentation, specifically adding references to two OAuth credential helpers for email authentication. 

The patch adds documentation for Aditya Garg's own `git-credential-gmail` and `git-credential-outlook` helpers to the list of OAuth credential helpers in `gitcredentials.adoc`. These helpers are positioned as cross-platform solutions specifically designed to authenticate Gmail and Outlook accounts when using `git send-email`. 

This completes the series' documentation improvements around OAuth authentication, complementing the earlier expanded OAuth coverage in `git-send-email.adoc`. The helpers are community-maintained projects hosted on GitHub, with clear links provided for users who need this functionality. 

The change is straightforward and well-contained, just adding two entries to an existing list of OAuth helpers. It follows naturally from the series' focus on modernizing Git's email authentication options, particularly as providers move away from basic authentication. The patch appears ready for inclusion, though reviewers may want to verify the helpers' compatibility with current Git versions.
