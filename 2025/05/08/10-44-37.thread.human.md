This thread discusses a performance optimization for `git repack` when handling promisor packs that are marked as kept (either via `--keep-pack` command-line option or `.keep` files). The issue was originally identified by Tomáš Trnka, with implementation work by Han Young.

The optimization avoids unnecessary enumeration of objects from kept packs during promisor repacking by using two new flags (`SKIP_IN_CORE_KEPT_PACKS` and `SKIP_ON_DISK_KEPT_PACKS`) in `builtin/repack.c`. The performance impact is significant - in one test case with a 2.7GB promisor pack, the change reduced processing time from 67.6 seconds to 18.8 seconds (3.6x faster), while also reducing output pack size from 4.5MB to 1.6MB.

The patch is relatively small (14 lines added, 4 modified) and builds on previous work in this area.
