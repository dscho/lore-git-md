This thread discusses a performance optimization for `git repack` when handling promisor packs that contain kept packs (either via `--keep-pack` or `.keep` files). The issue was originally identified by Tomáš Trnka, with an initial implementation by Han Young that improved performance by passing `--keep-pack` to `pack-objects`. The current patch, now awaiting review, takes this further by completely skipping the enumeration of objects from kept packs during promisor repacking using new `SKIP_IN_CORE_KEPT_PACKS` and `SKIP_ON_DISK_KEPT_PACKS` flags.

The optimization shows dramatic results in testing: processing time for a 2.7GB promisor pack drops from 67.6 seconds to 18.8 seconds, while also producing smaller output packs (1.6MB vs 4.5MB). The change is contained to `builtin/repack.c` and works by marking kept packs during collection and then skipping them entirely during enumeration. Maintainer Junio C Hamano has flagged this for review by Christian Couder, who has expertise in the promisor pack area. The patch appears ready for final review, building on the original work while delivering significant performance gains for this specific case.
