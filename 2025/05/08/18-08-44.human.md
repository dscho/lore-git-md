This is the final approval for a series improving `git send-email`'s domain validation and OAuth2.0 documentation. Junio Hamano gives the green light to merge the three-patch series after seven iterations of refinement. 

The series tightens RFC1035 domain name validation in `valid_fqdn()` to properly reject malformed domains that could cause SMTP failures, while maintaining special handling for macOS `.local` domains. It also restructures OAuth2.0 documentation with provider-specific examples for Gmail and Outlook, and references community credential helpers for these services.

Junio notes the documentation now clearly distinguishes between OAUTHBEARER and XOAUTH2 methods and maintains proper formatting standards. His approval confirms all technical and documentation concerns raised during review have been addressed, with the series demonstrating effective collaboration between the author and maintainers.

The patches will be queued for inclusion in the 'next' branch unless objections surface in the coming days. This marks the successful conclusion of a well-executed improvement to Git's email functionality.
