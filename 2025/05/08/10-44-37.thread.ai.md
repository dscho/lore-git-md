Performance optimization for `git repack` when handling promisor packs with kept packs (either via `--keep-pack` or `.keep` files). Originally identified by Tomáš Trnka, with implementation by Han Young. The patch avoids unnecessary enumeration of objects from kept packs during promisor repacking by using `SKIP_IN_CORE_KEPT_PACKS` and `SKIP_ON_DISK_KEPT_PACKS` flags in `builtin/repack.c`: `collect_pack_filenames()` marks `--keep-pack` packs as kept in-core, and `repack_promisor_objects()` skips both types of kept packs. Performance impact is significant: test case with 2.7GB promisor pack shows time reduction from 67.6s to 18.8s, output pack size reduction from 4.5MB to 1.6MB, and object enumeration count dropping from 19,091,575 to 20,952. Junio C Hamano has now requested review from Christian Couder as an active contributor in the promisor area. The change is contained (14 lines added, 4 modified) and builds on previous work while further optimizing by skipping enumeration entirely rather than just during packing.
