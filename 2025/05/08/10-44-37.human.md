This is a performance optimization patch for `git repack` when handling promisor packs (used in partial clone scenarios). The patch ensures that `--keep-pack` and `.keep` files are properly honored when repacking promisor objects, fixing a case where these settings were previously ignored.

The key improvement comes from skipping keep packs earlier in the process - during object enumeration rather than during packing. The author provides concrete benchmarks showing significant speedup: from 67.6 seconds to 18.8 seconds in their test case, along with producing a smaller packfile (1.6MB vs 4.5MB). The performance gain comes from avoiding unnecessary enumeration of objects that would ultimately be skipped anyway.

The change modifies `builtin/repack.c` to properly mark keep packs and adds flags to skip them during promisor object enumeration. The patch appears well-motivated with clear performance data, and builds on an earlier approach by Tomáš Trnka while improving its efficiency. This would be particularly relevant for users working with large promisor packs in partial clone setups.
