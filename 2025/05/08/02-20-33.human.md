This is an announcement about the Git Merge 2025 conference. Taylor Blau is extending the Call for Proposals (CFP) deadline from May 13 to May 20, 2025, at 11:59 PM UTC-7. The extension aims to accommodate two groups: remote attendees who now have more time to prepare submissions, and those who may have missed the original deadline or needed extra time.

The announcement references two previous emails - one that originally set the conference dates and another that added remote attendance options. Taylor notes the original deadline was visible on the Sessionize website but wasn't mentioned in the initial announcement.

The email encourages potential speakers to take advantage of the extended deadline and offers to answer any questions about the submission process or conference details. This appears to be a routine administrative update for the annual Git contributor conference, providing more flexibility for potential speakers.
