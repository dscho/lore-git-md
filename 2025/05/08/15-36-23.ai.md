Performance optimization for `git repack` handling of promisor packs with kept packs. This is a follow-up to Han Young's patch that improved performance by skipping kept packs during promisor repacking. The key change here is that Junio C Hamano is seeking Christian Couder's review as an active contributor in the promisor area.

The technical details remain the same as previously summarized: the patch avoids unnecessary enumeration of objects from kept packs (either via `--keep-pack` or `.keep` files) during promisor repacking by using `SKIP_IN_CORE_KEPT_PACKS` and `SKIP_ON_DISK_KEPT_PACKS` flags. The implementation marks `--keep-pack` packs as kept in-core in `collect_pack_filenames()` and skips both types of kept packs in `repack_promisor_objects()`.

The performance impact is demonstrated with the same test case showing dramatic improvement: from 67.6s to 18.8s for a 2.7GB promisor pack, with output pack size dropping from 4.5MB to 1.6MB. The object enumeration count drops from 19 million to just 20,952 objects. The patch also produces smaller output packs by avoiding repacking objects that exist in multiple packs but are marked as kept.

This appears to be a straightforward performance optimization with clear benefits, now awaiting review from domain experts. The change is contained to `builtin/repack.c` with 14 lines added and 4 lines modified. The implementation builds on Tomáš Trnka's original identification of the issue and Han Young's initial solution, now refined to be more efficient by skipping enumeration entirely rather than just skipping during packing.
