This is a technical discussion about potential optimizations to Git's index handling, continuing the "two-file index" proposal thread.

Jon Forrest responds to K Jayatheerth's detailed explanation of the current index implementation, acknowledging some points while maintaining interest in his alternative approach. He draws a parallel to Postgres's approach to handling concurrent access, though the discussion remains theoretical without performance data.

The exchange highlights the tension between optimizing for edge cases (very large repos) versus maintaining simplicity for the common case. While Jon seems to be stepping back from his original proposal's scope, he's still exploring whether a lighter-weight variant might have merit.
