Documentation cleanup removing obsolete mentoring list reference. This is the first patch in a v3 series (1/3) that finalizes the modernization of the MyFirstContribution tutorial, following earlier review and merge of the v2 series.

The deletion is clean - 8 lines removed from MyFirstContribution.adoc with no other impact.
