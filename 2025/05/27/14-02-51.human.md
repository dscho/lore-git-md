This is a test infrastructure patch that's part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to improve TAP output compliance in Git's test suite. The series has already been technically reviewed and is awaiting final integration.

This particular patch fixes a TAP formatting issue where a warning about unsupported BASH_XTRACEFD was being printed to stderr without the required "#" prefix that marks it as a TAP comment. The change is minimal - just adding the "#" prefix to maintain proper TAP output format when tests are run with tracing enabled on older Bash versions.

The patch is straightforward and mechanical, aligning with the series' goal of strict TAP validation. Since the series has already been reviewed by Karthik Nayak and Phillip Wood, and Junio Hamano appears ready to integrate it, this patch likely represents one of the final cleanups before the series is applied. The change doesn't affect test behavior, just ensures the output conforms to TAP standards.
