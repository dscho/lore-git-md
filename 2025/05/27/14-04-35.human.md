This is a bugfix patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing race conditions in Git's maintenance operations. It's part 9 of 11 in the series, focusing specifically on fixing lockfile races when packing refs and reflogs during auto-maintenance.

The patch moves the "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" maintenance tasks to run before detaching the process, matching the safer behavior already used by `git gc`. This prevents potential races with other processes that might try to access these files while they're being maintained. The change is minimal but important - just two lines modifying function pointer assignments in the task configuration array.

The commit notes that the "gc" task has a similar issue but will be handled separately in a subsequent patch due to additional complexity. This continues the series' careful progression from preparatory refactoring to targeted fixes, with each change building on the structural improvements made in earlier patches. The fix is straightforward and clearly motivated by the race condition problem identified earlier in the thread.
