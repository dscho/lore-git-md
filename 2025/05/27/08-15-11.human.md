This is a status update on two ongoing patch series related to the Git object store and midx optimizations, in response to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" summary.

Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on two of his patch series mentioned in the status report. For the midx optimization series (which avoids repeatedly checking for non-existent packfiles), he notes he considered sending another iteration to clean up some pointer casting ugliness with a macro, but decided against it since Taylor Blau has already built on top of these patches.

The bulk of the email focuses on his larger object store cleanup series (18 patches) that's part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The only remaining discussion point appears to be naming - whether to use `odb_alternate` or `odb_source` for some structures. Patrick indicates the series is otherwise ready unless there's strong preference for the alternative naming. The changes involve significant refactoring of object store internals, including renaming many functions and structures to be more consistent and remove reliance on the global `the_repository`.

Both series appear to be in good shape and nearing completion, with the midx optimization already queued for merging to 'next' and the object store cleanup awaiting final naming consensus before progressing further.
