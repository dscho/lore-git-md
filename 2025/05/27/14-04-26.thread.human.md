Patrick Steinhardt's series to fix race conditions in Git's maintenance operations has progressed to version 11, with the core work now complete. The series restructures maintenance task execution around ref locking model to prevent races during auto-maintenance, particularly during repacking references. The architectural changes introduce a split-phase execution model ("before detach" and "after detach") that all maintenance tasks now follow.

This patch was pivotal in the series, establishing the framework that subsequent patches then applied to specific tasks like "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire."
