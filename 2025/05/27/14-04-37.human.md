This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing race conditions in Git's maintenance operations around ref locking. It addresses a similar locking race in the "gc" task that was previously fixed for "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" tasks.

The patch splits the "gc" task execution into two phases to match git-gc's behavior: before detaching, it runs `gc_before_detach()` (renamed from `gc_before_repack`) to handle ref packing and reflog expiration, and after detaching it spawns git-gc with a new `--skip-maintenance-before-detach` flag to avoid duplicate work. This ensures ref operations happen before detach, preventing races with other processes.

The changes are straightforward - mostly moving code between functions and adding the new flag. Test updates verify the new flag is properly passed through. This completes the series' goal of making maintenance operations race-safe by properly sequencing operations before and after detach. The preparatory refactoring in earlier patches pays off here with a clean solution that maintains consistency with git-gc's behavior.
