Junio Hamano (the Git maintainer) gives the green light for merging the 9-part documentation series about `--stdin` behavior in git-notes and `core.commentChar` references. All open questions from previous rounds have been resolved, with Junio's implementation concerns addressed by documenting current behavior as known limitations. The series builds upon prior work while aligning with Git's documentation standards.
