This is a discussion about naming in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically around terminology for object store components. Justin Tobler weighs in on the debate between `odb_alternate` and `odb_source` that Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano were discussing earlier in the thread.

Justin clarifies his understanding of the object store model - there's still a primary storage with optional additional backends, but with the future possibility of pluggable ODBs where these could be different storage providers rather than just files. He notes that calling all of these "alternates" feels odd when some are primary, but agrees with Junio that it's not terribly confusing. He also draws a parallel to reference backends, though notes the key difference that object stores can have multiple active backends simultaneously.

The discussion remains inconclusive, with no clear preference emerging between the two naming options. Justin's contribution adds some technical context about the future pluggable ODB direction but doesn't strongly advocate for either naming approach. The thread shows the careful consideration being given to naming in this foundational refactoring work.
