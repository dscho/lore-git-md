This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series fixing race conditions in Git's maintenance operations, specifically addressing the "gc" task's locking behavior. The patch completes the series' work by splitting the "gc" task execution into pre-detach and post-detach phases, mirroring the approach taken for "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" in previous patches. The key change moves `gc_before_repack()` (renamed to `gc_before_detach()`) into the pre-detach phase while adding a new `--skip-maintenance-before-detach` flag to prevent duplicate execution when spawning `git gc`.

Technical details show careful handling of the transition: the `maintenance_task_gc` function is split into `_before_detach` and `_after_detach` variants, with the latter passing the new flag to avoid re-running maintenance steps. The patch updates tests to verify the new flag appears in subcommand invocations. This maintains consistency with the series' theme of making control flow explicit while eliminating race conditions - here ensuring reference operations complete before detaching occurs.

The changes complete the architectural shift introduced in earlier patches, applying the split-phase model to the final maintenance task. With this patch, the series achieves its goal of bringing `git-maintenance`'s locking behavior in line with `git-gc`'s safer approach.
