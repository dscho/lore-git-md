Hamano agrees in principle but notes the removal should follow Git's established deprecation process, specifically referencing the Documentation/BreakingChanges file pattern used for other recent removals.

This represents a significant escalation from the initial warning fixes, moving toward what appears to be a likely deprecation of git-cvsserver. The discussion shows the project's methodical approach to legacy code - first fixing immediate issues, then removing unused components, and finally considering full removal with proper documentation. The reference to BreakingChanges suggests this would be treated as a backward-incompatible change requiring clear communication to any remaining users.
