This is a status update on two ongoing patch series related to the `the_repository` removal effort and object store improvements.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" update regarding two of his patch series. For the midx-negative-packfile-cache series (2 patches), he notes he considered sending another iteration to address the `(void *)(intptr_t)-1` ugliness with a macro, but decided against it since Taylor has already built on these patches. This series optimizes MIDX handling by memoizing non-existent packfile lookups.

The bulk of the email focuses on the larger ps/object-store series (18 patches), which continues the `the_repository` removal work in the object store/ODB code. The series includes numerous renames (`object_directory` to `odb_alternate`, `raw_object_store` to `object_database`, etc.) and refactorings to eliminate `the_repository` usage. The only remaining discussion point is whether to use `odb_alternate` or `odb_source` naming - Patrick is open to changing if there's consensus but otherwise considers the series ready. The changes touch core object access APIs and alternate handling infrastructure across multiple files.

The email shows steady progress on the long-running `the_repository` removal effort, with these changes representing significant plumbing work to modernize Git's object storage internals. The midx optimization appears uncontroversial while the larger ODB refactoring has one minor naming question remaining before potential integration.
