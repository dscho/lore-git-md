This is a response in the ongoing discussion about cleaning up Git's `contrib/` directory. Jeff King (Peff) weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt's proposal to reorganize or remove unmaintained components, focusing on three specific tools he has worked on.

Peff agrees that `diff-highlight` and `git-jump` could be moved out of `contrib/`, though he notes `diff-highlight` might eventually be better implemented directly in `git-diff`. For credential helpers (like `osxkeychain`), he acknowledges the maintenance challenge - while they could theoretically be spun off, some like the macOS helper are widely used but lack clear maintainers. He reveals he originally wrote the macOS helper but doesn't use it himself or have access to macOS for maintenance.

The email continues the thread's progression from theoretical framework to practical considerations, highlighting the real-world maintenance challenges that complicate even well-intentioned cleanup efforts. Peff's perspective as an original author adds weight to the discussion, particularly around the credential helpers that many users may depend on.
