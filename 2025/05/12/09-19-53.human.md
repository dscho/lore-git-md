This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory (patch 3/11 in v3 series). The patch removes obsolete stubs for Mercurial and Bazaar remote helpers that were converted to stubs back in 2014 when the actual implementations moved to external projects. 

The stubs simply printed warnings directing users to the new locations, and their removal is justified by the fact that (1) the external projects have been the canonical source for a decade, and (2) the stubs serve no useful function beyond pointing elsewhere. The patch deletes three files: two stub scripts and their README.

This is a straightforward removal that fits the series' documented criteria - these are clearly obsolete components with maintained alternatives elsewhere. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the broader policy framework established in the cover letter for handling such cases.
