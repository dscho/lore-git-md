This is a discussion about whether to retain `git-contacts` in Git's `contrib/` directory as part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing cleanup series. Phillip Wood argues against removing `git-contacts`, despite it being flagged for potential removal due to having an alternative (`git-related`).

The key points are:
- Phillip notes `git-contacts` serves an important onboarding function by helping new contributors identify who to CC on patches without requiring external tool installation
- He highlights the script is currently functional and documented as the recommended way to find patch recipients
- Expresses skepticism about switching recommendations to `git-related` based on past maintenance experience with that project

This represents a new consideration in the `contrib/` cleanup discussion - while most components are being evaluated on technical merits and maintenance status, Phillip raises the pedagogical value of keeping certain tools that serve the contributor workflow, even when alternatives exist. The comment suggests the removal criteria may need to account for tools that play a role in Git's own development processes versus general utilities.

The position contrasts with earlier discussion where `git-contacts` was seen as a candidate for removal due to `git-related` being a more functional alternative. Phillip's intervention adds a new dimension to the policy discussion about what constitutes sufficient justification for keeping a `contrib/` component.
