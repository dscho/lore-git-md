This is a contributor housekeeping discussion about the future of specific `contrib/` components, continuing the broader thread about legacy code management in Git.

Jeff King (peff) responds to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier classification of actively maintained `contrib/` items, focusing on three components he originally contributed: `diff-highlight`, `git-jump`, and credential helpers. For `diff-highlight` and `git-jump`, he expresses willingness to move them to separate projects if needed, noting that `diff-highlight` might eventually be better implemented within `git-diff` itself with a different architecture.

The credential helpers present more complexity - while technically separable (as demonstrated by the independently maintained Git Credential Manager), King raises practical concerns about maintenance continuity, particularly for the macOS `osxkeychain` helper. He notes his own inability to maintain it properly (lacking macOS access) despite its likely widespread use, suggesting this component would need new maintainers before any transition.

This email advances the operational phase of the `contrib/` cleanup discussion by:
1. Confirming willingness to relocate two tools (`diff-highlight`, `git-jump`)
2. Identifying a maintenance gap for credential helpers
3. Reinforcing the pattern seen elsewhere in the thread where technical separability must be balanced against real-world usage and maintenance capacity

The response maintains the thread's trajectory toward concrete action while surfacing new considerations (maintainer recruitment) that may inform Junio Hamano's proposed triage framework. It leaves the credential helper question open while moving two other components closer to resolution.
