Bugfix and cleanup patch in Patrick Steinhardt's systematic `contrib/` directory cleanup series (v3 03/11). This removes obsolete stubs for Mercurial and Bazaar remote helpers that were converted to external projects a decade ago (commit b2c851a8e67). The stubs only contained warnings pointing to their new upstream locations (git-remote-bzr and git-remote-hg on GitHub), making them effectively non-functional documentation.

The patch deletes three files: the stubs themselves (`git-remote-bzr` and `git-remote-hg`) plus their `README`, totaling 37 lines removed. This aligns with the series' established removal criteria - these qualify as "broken functionality" (criterion 1) since they don't actually implement the helpers, and "clear alternatives exist" (criterion 2) via the external projects. The commit message avoids time-based justification despite the decade-old context, following Junio Hamano's guidance from earlier in the thread.

This is a straightforward removal that fits the series' policy framework, with no special cases or complications like the `packinfo.pl` retention discussed elsewhere in the thread. The external projects remain available, so users needing these helpers can still obtain them from their current maintainers.
