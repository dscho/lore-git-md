This is a removal patch in Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory (v3 series). It targets the obsolete "thunderbird-patch-inline" script and its documentation.

The removal follows the series' established criteria for obsolete/broken functionality.

This removal is straightforward as the functionality is clearly non-functional today and fits the series' policy framework for cleaning up unmaintained components.
