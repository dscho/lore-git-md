This is a counterproposal in the ongoing discussion about removing the unmaintained "thunderbird-patch-inline" contrib script. While the thread had been moving toward removing the script due to its dependency on an unmaintained Thunderbird extension, Phillip Wood suggests updating it instead to work with a maintained fork called External Editor Revived.

The patch updates both the script and its documentation to reference the new extension, and makes technical adjustments to handle differences in how the new extension formats messages (specifically changing how it detects the separator between email headers and body content). The changes appear straightforward and well-contained.

This introduces a new consideration to the removal discussion: while the original proposal argued the script was effectively unmaintained and unused, Wood's patch demonstrates there is at least one user who finds value in keeping it working with modern tooling. The quality of the patch suggests this isn't just theoretical - the author has clearly tested the integration with the revived extension.

The thread now has two clear paths forward: proceed with removal as originally proposed, or accept this update to keep the functionality working with maintained dependencies. The decision may hinge on whether other contributors see value in maintaining Thunderbird integration versus reducing contrib/ maintenance burden.
