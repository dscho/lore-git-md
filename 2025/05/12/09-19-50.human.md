This is a cover letter for a major cleanup series targeting Git's `contrib/` directory, now in its third iteration. Patrick Steinhardt systematically removes unmaintained or broken components while establishing clearer policies for future maintenance. The series removes 11 categories of scripts and tools from `contrib/`, including obsolete remote helpers (`persistent-https`, Bazaar/Hg stubs), unused utilities (`git-resurrect.sh`, `thunderbird-patch-inline`), and example directories. Each removal is justified by either technical brokenness (like the Perl scripts that no longer compile) or the existence of better alternatives. 

Notable changes in v3 include keeping `packinfo.pl` (which provides useful pack statistics that may eventually migrate to `git-verify-pack`) and clarifying that removal decisions are based on technical factors rather than just age. The cover letter outlines a vision for `contrib/` as a staging area - tools should either graduate to core Git, spin out as independent projects, or be removed. Future work may involve creating a separate `tools/` directory for development utilities.

The series has evolved through discussion, with v3 incorporating feedback about maintaining useful functionality while cleaning up cruft. The policy framework being established here will help prevent similar accumulation of unmaintained code in the future.
