This is a platform compatibility discussion regarding the `git send-email` FQDN detection follow-up patches. The thread has been focused on improving cross-platform hostname resolution, particularly addressing Julian Swagemakers' concern about `--fqdn` flag availability across different `hostname` implementations (Alpine Linux/BusyBox and macOS).

Junio Hamano expresses reservations about switching from `--fqdn` to `-f` for FQDN detection, despite the broader compatibility. His concern centers on potential ambiguity - while `--fqdn` is explicit about its purpose, `-f` might be repurposed by some implementations to mean something other than "fully qualified domain name". He notes that any implementation supporting `--fqdn` would at least attempt to provide the desired output, whereas `-f` lacks this semantic guarantee.

This discussion occurs in the context of already-approved changes where the series has:
- Added FQDN detection via `hostname` command
- Implemented proper error handling and validation
- Maintained the fallback chain structure
- Ensured results pass through the RFC1035-compliant `valid_fqdn()` check

The thread shows maintainer attention to subtle portability concerns even after technical approval, highlighting Git's careful approach to cross-platform behavior. The validation through `valid_fqdn()` provides some protection against malformed output, but Junio's comment suggests deeper consideration of interface semantics beyond just syntactic validation.
