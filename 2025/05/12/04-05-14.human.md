This is a contributor housekeeping patch in an ongoing thread about removing the obsolete "thunderbird-patch-inline" script from Git's contrib directory.

The original proposal noted the script's dependency on an unmaintained Thunderbird extension and its own lack of maintenance. Collin Funk chimes in to mention a more actively maintained fork of the extension ("External Editor Revived") that works with modern Thunderbird, though he agrees this doesn't change the script's irrelevance.

The exchange suggests the script is effectively abandoned and unlikely to be missed if removed. The discussion remains technical and focused on the practical realities of Thunderbird extensions rather than debating the removal itself. This appears to be a straightforward cleanup of obsolete tooling with no apparent opposition.
