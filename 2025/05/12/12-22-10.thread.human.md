## Thread Summary

This thread addresses memory leaks in Git's pack-bitmap loading paths, originally reported by Lidong Yan. After six iterations of review and refinement, the series has been consolidated into three focused patches that fix the leaks while simplifying the code.

The first patch (authored by Taylor Blau with Lidong Yan's sign-off) eliminates redundant cleanup code in `load_bitmap()` by removing error-handling gotos and relying on callers to perform cleanup via `free_bitmap_index()`. This removes about 17 lines of code while maintaining safety - a rare case where fixing a bug actually reduces complexity. Jeff King reviewed the approach and Junio C Hamano noted this as a positive outcome.

The second patch clarifies misleading comments in `test_bitmap_commits()` to accurately describe bitmap lookup table behavior, while the third introduces comprehensive test coverage through a new test helper and targeted corruption tests. The series has received thorough review from Jeff King and final approval from Taylor Blau, with all technical concerns addressed across multiple iterations.

The changes affect pack-bitmap.c, pack-bitmap.h, and test infrastructure, providing both immediate fixes and reusable testing tools for future bitmap validation work. The patches are now in their final form and ready for merging.
