This is a refactoring series cleaning up the oidmap implementation and related code. Jeff King presents three straightforward patches that were sitting in his local tree.

The first patch renames `oidmap_free()` to `oidmap_clear()` to better reflect its actual behavior (it clears the map but doesn't free the underlying structure). The second adds a new `oidmap_size()` function to query the number of entries. The third removes a redundant pointer in the raw object store's replace_map implementation.

The changes are mechanical and touch several files that use oidmap functionality, including commit-graph, list-objects-filter, and sequencer code. The patch series appears well-scoped and uncontroversial - it's the kind of straightforward cleanup that typically gets merged quickly after any minor review feedback.
