This is a platform compatibility fix addressing the macOS linker issue with `NOT_CONSTANT` symbols. Koji Nakamaru confirms the problem occurs with Xcode 14.2 on macOS Monterey and provides two concrete solutions: either modify the Makefile to avoid duplicate `$(LIB_FILE)` inclusion (which appears to trigger the linker bug), or initialize the `false_but_the_compiler_does_not_know_it_` variable to change its symbol type. Both approaches successfully resolve the undefined symbol error while maintaining the original functionality. The email advances the thread by moving from diagnosis to specific, tested fixes, though it doesn't yet address which solution might be preferable from a maintainability perspective. The issue remains narrowly scoped to macOS systems with this specific linker behavior, and the proposed changes are minimal and non-disruptive to other platforms.
