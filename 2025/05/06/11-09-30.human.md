This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series refactoring Git's object database (ODB) subsystem as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch renames `read_object_with_reference()` to the more descriptive `odb_read_object_peeled()`, better reflecting its actual behavior of recursively "peeling" commit and tag objects until reaching the requested type.

The change follows the pattern established in earlier patches of standardizing ODB-related function names and interfaces. The new name aligns with the series' goal of making object database operations more explicit and self-documenting. The patch touches several callers across the codebase (cat-file, fast-import, grep, etc.), but the changes are straightforward mechanical updates since the function's behavior remains unchanged.

As with previous patches in the series, no compatibility wrapper is needed here due to limited usage of this function. The patch completes the ODB interface standardization work that began earlier in the series, marking a significant step toward removing `the_repository` dependencies from Git's object storage layer.
