Maintenance discussion about Git's contrib/ cleanup and legacy code management. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's proposed framework (promote to core, spin off, or remove) for handling contrib/ scripts, drawing a parallel to Linux kernel staging drivers. He notes the lack of movement on many untouched contrib/ pieces and plans to propose removing some while suggesting others (CMake, Coccinelle rules, shell completion, etc.) might deserve promotion to core. The email reinforces the distinction between truly unmaintained scripts and actively used tools that just lack a proper home, while acknowledging the earlier correction that git-svn is a core command rather than part of contrib/ directory. This continues the thread's evolution from specific git-svn bugs to broader policy about legacy code management.
