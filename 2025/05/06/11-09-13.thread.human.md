Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing 17-patch series to refactor Git's object database (ODB) subsystem continues to progress, with the foundational rename of `raw_object_store` to `object_database` in patch 1/17 now receiving Junio Hamano's confirmation that the mechanical change is sound. This establishes the container-level naming in the three-layer hierarchy (container/instance/backend) that the series introduces.

While this first rename is uncontroversial - verified by compilation and with no conflicts from parallel development - the thread remains active with substantive discussion about naming conventions. Derrick Stolee has raised important questions about the "backend" terminology in patch 2/17, suggesting alternatives like `odb_shard` or `odb_slice` to better distinguish physical storage locations from future pluggable backends. The mechanical conversion patches (4-12/17) removing `the_repository` dependencies continue to receive approval with only minor feedback.

Junio's confirmation moves patch 1/17 closer to acceptance while the series awaits resolution on the more complex naming questions in subsequent patches. The thread shows careful attention to both the technical correctness of the changes and the long-term clarity of the naming hierarchy being established.
