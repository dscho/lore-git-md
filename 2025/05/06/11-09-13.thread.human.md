Patrick Steinhardt's major refactoring of Git's object database (ODB) subsystem has reached completion, with Junio Hamano confirming the series will be merged as-is. The 17-patch series systematically eliminated implicit `the_repository` usage across all object database operations, establishing consistent `odb_*` naming and explicit `struct object_database*` parameters throughout the codebase. 

The final version addressed minor organizational issues to maintain bisectability and kept the controversial rename of object-store.h to odb.h, which Junio accepted despite the noisy diff. The changes in v2 were minimal, primarily renaming `struct odb_backend` to `struct odb_alternate` - a change Junio had no strong opinion about. 

This marks a significant architectural milestone, with ~333 files modified to transition all major object operations to the new explicit ODB interface while maintaining identical behavior. The work paves the way for future pluggable ODB backends, completing one of the largest remaining pieces in Git's ongoing effort to eliminate global state dependencies. With Junio's final approval, the changes will soon land in Git's main branch.
