This thread continues Patrick Steinhardt's major refactoring of Git's object database (ODB) layer as part of the broader effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The 17-patch series is being carefully reviewed by Derrick Stolee, who has provided detailed technical feedback on naming and architectural decisions.

The discussion has established a three-layer naming scheme for the ODB components: container (`object_database`), instances (currently called `object_directory` but likely to be renamed to `odb_shard` or `odb_slice`), and future backends (vtable structures). The latest patch (7/17) removes `the_repository` from `odb_mkstemp()` by making the ODB parameter explicit, which Stolee confirms is technically sound despite a minor documentation error in the commit message (referencing the wrong function).

Several open questions remain about the naming hierarchy, particularly around separating instance versus backend concepts, and the timing of variable renames. The thread shows the project's characteristic attention to detail in foundational changes, with Stolee's reviews ensuring the abstractions will support future extensions while maintaining clarity. The series appears to be progressing well, with each patch receiving thorough review before moving forward.
