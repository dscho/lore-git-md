Patrick Steinhardt's 17-part series refactoring Git's object database (ODB) subsystem is nearing completion, with the v3 iteration receiving final approval from maintainer Junio Hamano and Reviewed-by from Derrick Stolee. The series establishes a new architectural foundation for pluggable ODB backends by systematically removing reliance on the `the_repository` global variable and introducing clearer naming conventions.

The first patch renamed the core `raw_object_store` structure to `object_database` (ODB), affecting 8 files with mechanical renames like `raw_object_store_new()` becoming `odb_new()`. The second patch continues this pattern by renaming `object_directory` to `odb_alternate` across 28 files, better reflecting its role in the future architecture where object storage may not be directory-based. Both changes are purely naming improvements that maintain existing behavior while preparing for future extensibility.

With all technical aspects settled and the changes in final form, the series provides the groundwork for subsequent work on pluggable ODB backends. The patches have undergone thorough review, with no substantive changes needed from the v2 state. Performance validation and non-code follow-ups remain as separate future work items.
