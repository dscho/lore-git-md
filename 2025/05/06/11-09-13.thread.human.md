Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's object database subsystem has reached patch 7/17 in its fifth iteration. The work systematically removes reliance on the `the_repository` global variable while preparing for pluggable ODB backends. The latest patch converts `odb_mkstemp()` to take an explicit `object_database` parameter, updating its 7 call sites in fast-import, index-pack, and related commands. This follows the established pattern from earlier patches that renamed core structures (now `object_database` and `odb_source`) and converted functions like `assert_oid_type()`.

The series has evolved through multiple review rounds with input from Derrick Stolee and others, resolving naming questions and establishing consistent `odb_`-prefixed APIs. While touching over 140 files so far, the changes remain mechanical conversions that maintain backward compatibility. With core infrastructure in place and no outstanding concerns on the recent patches, the work appears ready for Junio Hamano's consideration. The remaining patches will continue converting functions to use explicit ODB parameters, culminating in renaming `read_object_with_reference()` to complete the architectural shift.
