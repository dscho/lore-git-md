Platform compatibility fix addressing a macOS Monterey/Xcode 14.2 linker issue with Git's `NOT_CONSTANT` wrapper. This is one of two proposed solutions (the other being Makefile changes) to resolve undefined symbol errors for `false_but_the_compiler_does_not_know_it_` when linking `run-command.o`. The patch initializes this compiler-trick variable to 0, changing its symbol type from 'C' to 'S' which the linker then properly resolves. Torsten Bögershausen confirms this approach works where the Makefile-only solution did not, with Koji Nakamaru's assistance in diagnosing the root cause. The change is minimal (one line in `not-constant.c`) and maintains the original safety checks while fixing the platform-specific compilation failure. This continues the thread's progression from diagnosis to concrete fixes, though the choice between this initialization approach versus Makefile modifications remains open for discussion. The fix is narrowly targeted at the macOS linker behavior without impacting other platforms.
