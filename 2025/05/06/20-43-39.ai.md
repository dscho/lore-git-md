This is the final approval from Junio Hamano for Patrick Steinhardt's completed series cleaning up Git's `contrib/` directory. The email confirms the removal strategy for all ten components, distinguishing between those that can be removed immediately (like the MediaWiki tools and persistent-https helper) versus those needing Git 3.0 coordination (like `remotes2config.sh`). Junio agrees with the Linux kernel staging drivers model as a future policy for `contrib/` and supports the idea of splitting development tools into a new `tools/` directory and moving documentation examples out of `contrib/`. The email marks the conclusion of this systematic cleanup effort, with all technical and policy questions now resolved except for timing considerations on two specific removals.
