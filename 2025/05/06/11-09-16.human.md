This is part 3/17 of Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring the object database (ODB) subsystem as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Following the previous patches that renamed key structures (`struct raw_object_store` to `struct object_database` and `struct object_directory` to `struct odb_backend`), this patch updates the filenames to match the new terminology.

The patch renames "object-store.{c,h}" to "odb.{c,h}" to better reflect their contents after the structural renames. This is a mechanical change that updates 126 files to use the new header name, with no functional changes. The commit message clearly explains the motivation - the old filenames became confusing after the structure renames in prior patches.

This continues the series' pattern of making the ODB subsystem more self-contained and properly named while maintaining existing functionality. The change is straightforward but wide-ranging, touching many parts of the codebase that include the header. Reviewers should verify the mechanical updates are complete and correct, though the risk of introducing bugs is low since this is purely a filename change.
