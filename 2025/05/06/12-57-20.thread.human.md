While not rejecting the feature, Junio's comments push the discussion toward architectural considerations about how best to integrate this functionality with Git's existing trailer infrastructure.

The key open questions now are whether to refactor the implementation to use interpret-trailers, and whether the specialized `--reviewby` flag remains justified given the general `--trailer` option. These decisions will affect both the current series' design and future trailer-related extensions.
