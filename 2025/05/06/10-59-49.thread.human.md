The series has grown to 10 patches addressing various aspects of test output handling, with particular attention to ensuring consistent behavior between the traditional Make-based and newer Meson-based builds.

Key reviewers including Eric Sunshine, Phillip Wood, and Junio Hamano have provided detailed feedback, leading to several iterations. Patrick acknowledges the technical merit but has concerns about readability and consistency across the many test files. They've reached a compromise where the changes will be split into more focused commits to make the refactorings more reviewable.

- Refined shell trace output handling to properly separate stdout/stderr
- Improved handling of unexpected test passes
- Aligned behavior between Make and Meson builds
- Added platform-specific handling for macOS and Cygwin

Junio appears ready to merge the series once the remaining questions about stream handling methodology are resolved.
