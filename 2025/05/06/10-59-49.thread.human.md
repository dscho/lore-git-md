This thread covers a series of patches improving Git's Meson build system integration with the test suite, specifically around TAP (Test Anything Protocol) output handling. The initial patches fixed malformed TAP output by redirecting repository creation output, converting prerequisite checks, and consolidating test parameters in meson.build. The final patch enabled proper TAP protocol parsing in Meson 1.8+, building on an upstream Meson bugfix contributed by Patrick Steinhardt.

While the series showed clean TAP output parsing in demonstrated cases, Patrick has now discovered MinGW-specific compatibility issues and has asked for a temporary hold on merging to the 'seen' branch while he investigates. The nature of the MinGW problems isn't yet clear—they could be new issues introduced by this series or pre-existing quirks in Windows test execution. The pause reflects responsible cross-platform maintenance practices, as the core TAP parsing approach appears sound based on the working Unix examples shown earlier in the thread. 

The series remains mechanically sound and focused on test infrastructure (t/*.sh, test-lib.sh, meson.build) without touching core Git functionality. Once the MinGW investigation concludes, this should be ready to proceed, possibly with Windows-specific adjustments to the TAP output handling.
