Patrick Steinhardt's series to integrate Meson test infrastructure has completed technical review, with all platform-specific issues resolved. The patches enhance TAP protocol support and test output handling, particularly addressing macOS and Cygwin edge cases in the grep test suite. Karthik Nayak and Phillip Wood have signed off on the technical implementation, which includes fixes for TAP output redirection and test argument consolidation.

The discussion took an interesting turn when Ramsay Jones pointed out that Meson's stricter TAP parsing revealed long-standing inconsistencies in test handling - specifically that traditional make/prove runners silently accept unexpected passes while Meson surfaces them. This led to a philosophical discussion about whether test runners should fail on unexpected passes. Junio Hamano has now weighed in, expressing openness to unifying this behavior across build systems, though this consideration goes beyond the current series.

With all technical issues addressed and reviewed, the series is ready for integration. The broader questions about test runner behavior may influence future infrastructure work, but won't hold up these immediate improvements to Meson test support.
