Patrick Steinhardt's Meson test integration series has reached its final form (v4) after thorough technical review, with all outstanding issues addressed. The series improves TAP output parsing for Meson builds while ensuring cross-platform consistency, requiring Meson 1.8+ for proper support. Key changes include refined test output handling (with new fd3 redirection for verbose mode), platform-specific test corrections, and cleanup of prerequisite announcements that broke TAP format.

The most recent patch addresses an edge case in t7815's grep tests, where macOS was found to exhibit the same regex behavior as Cygwin (both inheriting from FreeBSD's implementation) when matching NUL bytes. This adds macOS-specific handling while noting that a more complete solution involving configure-time regex checks may be needed later.

Reviewers including Karthik Nayak, Eric Sunshine, and Phillip Wood have signed off on the technical approach, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk helping polish the commit messages. The series now appears ready for maintainer integration, having resolved all feedback while maintaining focus on its core goal of reliable test infrastructure for Meson builds.
