Platform compatibility fix for macOS linker issue with `NOT_CONSTANT` symbol. Koji Nakamaru reports reproducing the compilation failure on Xcode 14.2/macOS Monterey, confirming it stems from a linker bug when handling the `false_but_the_compiler_does_not_know_it` symbol introduced in commit 82e79c63642c. The email provides two working solutions: a Makefile tweak removing the redundant `$(LIB_FILE)` reference that appears to trigger the linker issue, or modifying the symbol definition in `not-constant.c` to initialize it (changing its type). This moves the thread forward from diagnosis to concrete fixes, with both approaches appearing viable. The issue remains specific to certain macOS toolchain versions but now has clear reproduction steps and potential resolutions.
