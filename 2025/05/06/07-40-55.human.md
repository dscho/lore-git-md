Bugfix follow-up in the maintenance task decomposition series. Christian Couder points out that a memory leak identified by Eric Sunshine in v3 of the worktree-prune task implementation still appears to be present in v4. The leak occurs in `worktree_prune_condition()` when the function exits early via `goto out` after finding a worktree to prune, failing to free the `wtpath` allocated by `should_prune_worktree()`. This is a straightforward oversight in an otherwise well-structured patch that implements worktree pruning as a standalone maintenance task. The series as a whole has received positive reviews and appears ready for merging once this final leak is addressed.
