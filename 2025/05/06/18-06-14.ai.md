This is a discussion about file renaming in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing `the_repository` removal series (specifically patch 3/17). Derrick Stolee raises concerns about renaming "object-store.{c,h}" to "odb.{c,h}" as part of the structural changes to the object database subsystem.

Stolee questions the necessity of the file rename, noting two main issues: (1) the tree-wide impact of header changes across 162+ files, and (2) potential complications for historical code investigation due to the rename. He suggests the file rename might create semantic merge conflicts with parallel development work, and proposes considering it as a separate change after the method renames are completed.

The discussion occurs in the context of Patrick's larger series that renames `raw_object_store` to `object_database` and related structures. While Stolee acknowledges the series' goals, he specifically pushes back on this particular mechanical change, suggesting it may not provide enough benefit to justify the churn. This represents a point of potential contention in an otherwise largely mechanical refactoring series.
