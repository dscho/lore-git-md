This is another patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to clean up Git's `contrib/` directory, this time removing the `git-resurrect.sh` script. The script was designed to help recover lost branch tips by searching reflogs and merge history, but it hasn't been touched since its introduction in 2009.

The removal follows the same pattern as previous patches in the series - targeting unmaintained components that have seen no activity for many years. The script was moderately complex (181 lines) with several search strategies (-a/-r/-m/-t flags) to find branch remnants, but appears to have fallen out of use. 

This is patch 8/10 in the series, continuing the systematic cleanup of `contrib/`. Like the other removals, this doesn't affect core Git functionality - it simply removes a script that was already effectively abandoned. The commit message is brief but sufficient, noting the script's age and inactivity as justification.

The series continues to methodically remove components that meet its criteria of being broken, unmaintained, or obsolete. This particular removal seems straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, following the same uncontroversial pattern as previous patches in the series.
