This thread documents Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, a multi-patch effort to remove obsolete and unmaintained components while establishing clear maintenance policies. The series has progressed through eleven patches, each demonstrating different removal rationales through concrete examples.

The work has removed twenty-seven items from `contrib/`, organized into categories like externally-migrated projects, ecosystem obsolescence, and functionality replaced by core Git features. The final patch removes three statistical analysis scripts (`git-common-hash`, `mailmap.pl`, and `packinfo.pl`) that have been unused for over a decade, with modern alternatives now available in core Git. Each removal follows the established pattern of thorough documentation and technical justification.

Throughout the series, Patrick has consulted with original developers where relevant (like Matthieu Moy for the MediaWiki helper) and built consensus around removal criteria. The work has been consistently reviewed by Junio Hamano, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk providing empirical data to inform decisions. The series now shifts to finalizing the `contrib/README` policy document, which will codify the maintenance principles demonstrated through these removals - including how to handle cases of ecosystem changes, platform incompatibilities, and core feature replacements.

This cleanup represents a significant reduction of technical debt in Git's contrib directory, while establishing patterns for future maintenance. The systematic approach - removing actual cases first, then documenting policy based on those examples - has proven effective in building consensus around what belongs in contrib/ and how it should be maintained.
