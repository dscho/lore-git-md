Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory is nearing completion, with the third version of his 12-part series now in its final documentation refinement stage. The work removes 20 unmaintained components while establishing clear criteria for what belongs in `contrib/` going forward. The latest round has focused on precisely documenting the removal rationale for each component, with primary technical reasons (like broken functionality or better alternatives) clearly distinguished from secondary considerations like age.

The series has received substantive approval from Elijah Newren on its documentation approach and technical decisions, including the removal of the "thunderbird-patch-inline" script due to its dependency on an unmaintained Thunderbird extension. Toon Claes has added his positive review, confirming the technical soundness of the changes. The only remaining question is whether to retain `git-contacts` based on Phillip Wood's argument about its pedagogical value for teaching patch workflow concepts - a discussion Junio Hamano is holding the series to consider. With all other feedback addressed and multiple reviewers validating the approach, the series appears ready for merging once this final administrative question is resolved.
