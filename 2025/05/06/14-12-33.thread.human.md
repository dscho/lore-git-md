This thread continues Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, which has expanded into a broader discussion about the directory's purpose and maintenance expectations. The initial focus was on removing `git-new-workdir` (now superseded by `git worktree`), where consensus was reached to remove it while acknowledging Junio Hamano's personal usage of existing workdirs. This led to discussions about formalizing `contrib/` as an explicitly experimental area without stability guarantees.

The latest discussion centers on `git-contacts`, revealing more nuanced case. While the script appears in Git's contribution documentation, Eric Sunshine and Todd Zullinger note it has limited real-world usage. Historical context emerged about alternative implementations and a prior cleanup attempt by Felipe Contreras 11 years ago. Patrick is now considering moving `git-contacts` to `tools/` rather than removing it entirely, as it serves Git development purposes rather than being a general user tool. This contrasts with the clearer case for removing `git-new-workdir`, showing how different components require different approaches based on their usage and role in the ecosystem. The thread continues to shape a more formal policy for managing `contrib/` going forward.
