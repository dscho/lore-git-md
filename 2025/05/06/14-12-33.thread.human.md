This thread documents Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, which has now reached its final stages with the removal of obsolete components. The series has established clear patterns for technical removals while formulating maintenance policy, including discussions about potential relocations of still-useful tools.

The core effort has completed the technical work of removing 27 unmaintained items across 11 patches, with the recent removal of the obsolete `stats/` directory in patch 11/11. Junio Hamano has endorsed Elijah Newren's policy concerns about avoiding time-based rationales for removals, emphasizing that justifications should focus on technical brokenness or core alternatives. The discussion has expanded to include formal criteria for future removals and relocation policies for functional but specialized tools.

A new dimension was added with D. Ben Knoble's email about `git-contacts`, highlighting practical considerations when removing tools. While `git-related` is technically superior, its Ruby dependency and lack of packaging make it less convenient than the built-in Perl script. This underscores the need to consider real-world usage patterns and installation friction, especially for tools like `git-contacts` that some contributors use as their `ccCmd`. The thread continues to balance cleanup goals with usability concerns, with final documentation updates to `contrib/README` still pending to codify these policies.
