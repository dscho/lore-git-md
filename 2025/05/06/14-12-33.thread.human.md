Patrick Steinhardt's comprehensive cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory has reached its final approval from maintainer Junio Hamano. The series removes ten obsolete or unmaintained components, including the MediaWiki integration tools, a broken Go-based remote helper, and several ancient scripts like `git-resurrect.sh`. Junio has given the green light for most removals to proceed immediately, though two items - `remotes2config.sh` and `git-new-workdir` - will wait for Git 3.0 due to test dependencies and breaking changes.

The cleanup follows a clear rationale: components were removed if they were broken (like the persistent-https helper that no longer compiles), unmaintained (some scripts hadn't been updated in over a decade), or better maintained elsewhere (the MediaWiki tools now have a dedicated external project). Junio endorsed using the Linux kernel's staging drivers model as inspiration for future `contrib/` maintenance policy, suggesting a more systematic approach to managing experimental or third-party code. With this approval, the multi-part cleanup is complete, marking a significant reduction in technical debt and clearer boundaries for what belongs in Git's core distribution.
