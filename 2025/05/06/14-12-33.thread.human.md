Here's the updated human-readable summary:

Patrick Steinhardt continues his systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory with an 11-part series now in its third version. The effort establishes clear policies for the directory as a staging area while removing 17 unmaintained components that either have core Git alternatives or are no longer functional. 

The latest patch removes the obsolete `git-new-workdir` script (last updated in 2014), which has been fully superseded by core Git's `git-worktree` functionality. This follows the series' established pattern of removing items that meet one of three criteria: broken functionality, clear alternatives in core Git, or being obsolete stubs. The removal includes the script itself, its supporting files, and updates affected tests.

Junio Hamano has affirmed the approach, particularly the rejection of time-based justifications for removal in favor of technical criteria. Notable remaining work includes determining the integration path for `packinfo.pl` (retained as a useful stats tool without replacement) and potential future reorganization of tools. The series appears complete and ready for merging, having incorporated feedback from earlier discussions with D. Ben Knoble and Eric Sunshine.
