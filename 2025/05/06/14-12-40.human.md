This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to clean up Git's `contrib/` directory by removing broken or unmaintained components. The patch removes the "persistent-https" remote helper, which was designed to speed up SSL operations by maintaining persistent connections to remote servers.

The helper is clearly obsolete - it hasn't been updated since 2016 and can no longer compile with modern Go toolchains (it fails with Go 1.23+ due to missing `go.mod` files). The patch removes all related files: the Go source code, Makefile, README, and LICENSE (Apache 2.0). 

This follows the same pattern as previous patches in the series, targeting clearly abandoned functionality. The removal is straightforward with no impact on core Git behavior. The helper appears to have been effectively unused for years, as evidenced by the fact nobody noticed it was no longer compilable with modern Go versions.

The patch continues the systematic cleanup of `contrib/` by removing another piece of technical debt, bringing the series to 6 of its planned 10 removals. Like the previous removals, this one seems uncontroversial given the clear evidence of abandonment.
