Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about CI redundancy by acknowledging the inefficiency but arguing for maintaining full test coverage across platforms. He notes that while some jobs like the "pedantic" Fedora build could potentially be combined with others, GitLab needs its own pipeline to support their merge request workflow. 

The exchange highlights the tension between wanting faster CI feedback and needing comprehensive platform coverage. Patrick makes the practical point that distributed development naturally leads to some redundancy, and that he personally waits for all platform tests (especially non-Linux ones) before submitting patches. 

The discussion remains at a conceptual level - while both agree there's room for optimization, neither proposes concrete changes beyond the original MSVC Meson job tweak. The thread shows maintainers grappling with how to balance resource usage against thorough testing across Git's CI systems.
