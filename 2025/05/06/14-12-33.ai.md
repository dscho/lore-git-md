This is a cover letter for a contributor housekeeping series targeting the `contrib/` directory cleanup. Patrick Steinhardt proposes removing several clearly broken or unmaintained tools from `contrib/` as part of an ongoing effort to rationalize this directory. The series removes 10 components that meet one of three criteria: being broken (failing to compile), unmaintained (no updates in 5+ years), or obsolete (having better alternatives). 

The cover letter frames this as the first step toward establishing a clearer policy for `contrib/`, analogous to Linux's staging drivers - components should either graduate to core Git, spin out as independent projects, or be removed. It also hints at future reorganization, suggesting a separate `tools/` directory for development utilities like CMake and Coccinelle scripts, and moving examples into documentation.

The 10 patches remove: remotes2config.sh, examples directory, remote-helper stubs, thunderbird-patch-inline, hooks directory, mw-to-git (MediaWiki integration), persistent-https remote helper, git-resurrect.sh, emacs directory, and git-new-workdir. The deletions total ~6800 lines across 50 files, with some being substantial (like the 1390-line mediawiki remote helper). The commit messages will need to document each removal's justification, but the cover letter establishes consistent criteria. This appears to be a well-motivated cleanup of technical debt in a neglected part of the codebase.
