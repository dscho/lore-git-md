Performance optimization for xdiff `--minimal` mode. This is the final version of Niels Glodny's patch that modifies the `cleanup_records` heuristic to skip marking lines as changed when surrounded by many changes, but only when `--minimal` is explicitly requested. The change produces shorter diffs in about 1.3% of cases while showing no measurable performance impact in benchmarks.
