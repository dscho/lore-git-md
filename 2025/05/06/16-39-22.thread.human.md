This thread documents the successful completion of a series optimizing memory usage in Git's packed-refs backend. The three-patch series, authored by shejialuo, originated from suggestions by Jeff King (Peff) and Patrick Steinhardt, and went through five iterations (v1-v5) of review and refinement.

The changes standardize memory handling for packed-refs operations while maintaining compatibility with historical behavior, particularly around empty packed-refs files. The series includes: (1) filesystem safety checks for empty files with a new fsck warning, (2) refactored buffer allocation into a reusable helper function, and (3) application of the new memory strategy to fsck verification. All changes preserve Windows-specific considerations and backward compatibility.

After thorough review from Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt, with final refinements to commit messages and implementation details in v5, Junio Hamano has now given the go-ahead to merge the series. This concludes the optimization effort with full technical consensus among all key reviewers, marking the successful standardization of packed-refs memory management across different operations.
