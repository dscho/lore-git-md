This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to clean up Git's `contrib/` directory. It removes obsolete stubs for Mercurial and Bazaar remote helpers that were converted to external projects over a decade ago.

The patch deletes three files: two shell script stubs (`git-remote-bzr` and `git-remote-hg`) that just print warnings about the helpers being maintained externally, and a README pointing to their new locations. These were rendered obsolete in 2014 when the actual helper implementations moved to separate repositories (linked in the commit message).

The removal is straightforward and well-justified - the stubs serve no purpose other than to redirect users, and the commit message notes it's very unlikely anyone still tries to use them. This continues the series' pattern of removing clearly obsolete components from `contrib/`, with the author providing clear historical context and references to the current locations of the functionality.

Like the previous patches in the series, this appears to be a non-controversial cleanup of technical debt. The patch is small (37 lines removed) and focused, with no behavior changes beyond removing files that were effectively just documentation.
