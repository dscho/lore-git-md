This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing `contrib/` cleanup series, focusing on the potential removal of `git-contacts`. Eric Sunshine responds to Todd Zullinger's note about documentation references to `git-contacts` in `MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches`.

Eric confirms that `git-contacts` meets the removal criteria (having a better-maintained alternative in `git-related`) and notes that while the tool is referenced in documentation, a recent update already clarifies it's not part of core Git. His web search found no evidence of widespread use beyond Git's own docs. 

He shares historical context: he originally ported `git-related` from Ruby to Perl for inclusion in `contrib/`, and mentions that Felipe Contreras (author of `git-related`) had proposed removing `git-contacts` in a similar cleanup effort 11 years ago. The discussion reinforces that `git-contacts` is a strong candidate for removal, with the documentation updates being the main consideration.
