This is a design discussion about the interactive context control feature in patch 3/3 of the series. Phillip Wood responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion about command-line style context adjustment during interactive sessions by proposing an alternative approach focused on per-hunk context adjustment within `git add -p` rather than modifying the general `git add -i` interface.

Phillip argues that allowing context adjustment during hunk review (rather than upfront via command-line) would be more practical, since users often don't know how much context they'll need until they're examining specific hunks. He notes the current workaround - quitting and restarting with different context - creates problems particularly for `git stash -p` where interrupted stashes can't be cleanly reapplied.

The email suggests the series' command-line options (added in patch 2/3) already cover the use case of known context needs, making the interactive adjustment proposed in patch 3/3 less critical. This moves the discussion toward potentially simplifying the series by focusing on the core command-line overrides and deferring interactive UI questions. Phillip's perspective as a frequent reviewer carries weight here, indicating the feature may be narrowing to its most essential elements.
