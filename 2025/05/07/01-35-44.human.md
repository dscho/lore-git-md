This is a status update on the path-walk delta compression series from Taylor Blau, the primary reviewer. The series introduces a new `--path-walk` option for improved delta compression by considering file paths when packing objects.

Taylor confirms completing their review of the entire 13-patch series after previously reviewing the first 8 patches. While they note there are enough comments to warrant a reroll (v3) of the series, the tone suggests the feedback is primarily polish rather than fundamental issues. The series had previously been approved by Junio Hamano for inclusion in 'next', and Taylor's thorough review appears to be the final step before integration.

The update indicates the path-walk feature is technically complete and ready to proceed, pending incorporation of Taylor's feedback in a new version. This maintains the positive momentum for a feature that has shown significant performance improvements (16-57% speedups in various scenarios) while reducing pack sizes.
