This is a performance-focused follow-up in the path-walk delta compression series. Taylor Blau is reviewing patch 11/13, which implements threading for the path-based compression algorithm. The patch shows impressive performance gains - up to 60% faster repack times in some cases - while maintaining similar pack sizes.

Taylor notes the performance improvements seem to come from better delta selection due to the path-based approach, though he questions the exact mechanism behind the speedup. He suggests the two compression passes in path-walk may explain the tradeoff between computation time and compression gains. 

The review identifies a few minor issues: a typo ("extrac"), a potentially confusing double-while loop in the threaded implementation that may relate to work-stealing, and a suggestion to use Q_() instead of _() for better single-threaded output. These appear to be polish items rather than fundamental problems.

The patch continues the series' trend of significant performance improvements for path-similar repositories while maintaining compatibility with existing name-hash approach. Taylor's review focuses on implementation clarity rather than functionality, suggesting the core approach is sound.
