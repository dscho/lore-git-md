Refactoring patch in a series to decompose `git gc` functionality. This is the first of six patches in v5 of Patrick Steinhardt's series that completes the multi-year effort to break up `git gc` into standalone maintenance tasks. The patch fixes indentation of function parameters in `cmd_gc()` to align with Git's coding style. While purely cosmetic, it's part of the preparatory cleanup needed before extracting worktree pruning and rerere garbage collection functionality into separate maintenance tasks. The series has undergone extensive review and appears technically complete, with this initial patch setting up the codebase for the more substantive changes that follow.
