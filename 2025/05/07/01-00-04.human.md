This is a technical discussion about naming in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing patch 2/17 in Patrick Steinhardt's series to refactor the object database subsystem.

Derrick Stolee follows up on his earlier feedback about the proposed rename of `object_directory` to `odb_backend`, clarifying his vision for the object database abstraction layers. He suggests three distinct concepts: the top-level `object_database` (or possibly just `odb`), individual database instances (`odb_shard` or `odb_slice`), and implementation backends containing vtables. 

The discussion reveals some naming tension - Stolee acknowledges that his initial suggestion might conflict with the `object_database` rename from patch 1/17. He's searching for a term that distinguishes individual object storage units (currently alternate odbs) from both the container and the implementation, proposing "shard" or "slice" as possibilities.

This email shows the careful consideration going into foundational naming decisions that will affect future extensibility, particularly around pluggable backends. The thread has already approved patch 1/17's rename, but patch 2/17 remains pending while this naming hierarchy gets resolved. Stolee's feedback demonstrates the project's attention to getting these abstractions right at this early stage of the refactoring effort.
