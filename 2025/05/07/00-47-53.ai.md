This is a positive review of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's object database refactoring series, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Derrick Stolee explicitly approves the renaming from `raw_object_store` to `object_database` (and the ODB acronym) that was proposed in patch 1/17. He notes this is a straightforward mechanical change where correctness can be verified by successful compilation.

The approval focuses specifically on the naming choice, validating Patrick's rationale that "object_database" is already widely used in the codebase, avoids confusion with "object directories", and works well with the established ODB acronym in function names. This positive feedback contrasts with Stolee's more cautious stance on the file renaming proposed in patch 3/17 (as noted in the thread context), showing he's selectively supportive of different aspects of the refactoring approach.

The brief but clear endorsement from a core contributor suggests this foundational structural change is uncontroversial and likely to proceed as-is, while the thread context indicates more discussion will be needed around the subsequent rename of object-store.{c,h} files. The approval helps move forward one piece of the larger ODB` subsystem refactoring while leaving other aspects open for further debate.
