This is a follow-up to a bug report about lock conflicts in Git 2.47.2's maintenance operations. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the issue (which causes `HEAD.lock` conflicts between `git fetch` and subsequent commands) and confirms it matches another known report. He indicates a patch series is forthcoming to address the locking behavior in detached maintenance mode.

The response is brief but significant - it confirms this is a recognized regression that will be fixed, connecting it to prior discussion. The promised patch series suggests the solution may require more than a trivial fix, likely involving coordination between maintenance operations and regular repository activities. Readers affected by this issue can expect a proper fix soon rather than needing to investigate workarounds.
