`$` is the anchor metacharacter in this context (end of string)
according to Posix.

https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9699919799/basedefs/V1_chap09.html#tag_09_03

```
    $
        The <dollar-sign> shall be special when used as an anchor.
```
