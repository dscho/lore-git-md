This thread presents the sixth iteration of a two-patch series by Aditya Garg addressing subtle issues in `git send-email`. The first patch fixes a threading bug that occurs when editing the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to`, ensuring the internal `$message_num` counter increments correctly during edits. The second patch, which just received its final refinement, improves logging accuracy when email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery - it now shows the actual delivered ID in logs while making the reassignment notice debug-only.

Junio C Hamano reviewed version 5 of the series, approving the technical approach while requesting improved documentation. Aditya has fully incorporated these suggestions in v6, including clearer commit messages and more precise subject lines. The technical solution is now stable, having addressed all substantive feedback. While a future unification of `$message_num` and `$message_id_serial` variables remains possible, that work is explicitly deferred from this series. With no remaining open questions, these mature fixes for email threading and logging edge cases appear ready for final review and potential inclusion.
