This is a bugfix series for `git send-email` addressing two email threading and logging issues. The series is on its fourth iteration, with each version refining the explanation and implementation.

The first patch fixes a subtle threading bug that occurs when editing the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` and shallow threading enabled. The core issue is that the internal `$message_num` counter increments unnecessarily during message edits, breaking the thread structure. The fix carefully preserves the counter state during edits by decrementing it before re-processing, with detailed analysis of the threading logic in the commit message. This affects the `process_file` subroutine in `git-send-email.perl` and its handling of the `$message_num` and `$message_id_serial` variables.

The second patch improves logging when Outlook modifies Message-IDs, ensuring the final header display shows the actual delivered Message-ID rather than the original one. This modifies the `$header` variable handling and adjusts debug output, with clear before/after examples provided in the commit message. The change makes the logs more accurate for troubleshooting email delivery issues.

Both patches demonstrate careful analysis of edge cases in email threading behavior, with the first being particularly subtle in how it only manifests under specific combinations of `--in-reply-to`, shallow threading, and editing the first message. The series appears well-tested with clear commit messages explaining both the problems and solutions.
