This is a discussion about maintaining credential helpers in Git's contrib directory, specifically responding to Jeff King's concerns about finding maintainers for these components. Brian m. carlson weighs in on the practical usage and distribution of several credential helpers.

The email focuses on three credential helpers: osxkeychain (macOS), libsecret (Linux), and wincred (Windows). carlson notes these are widely distributed (by Apple, Homebrew, and Linux distros) and heavily used, arguing they should be preserved rather than removed. They observe that while the Windows wincred helper isn't as popular as Git Credential Manager (GCM), it serves as a lighter alternative and appears similarly maintained. carlson suggests promoting these helpers to the main repository with platform-specific build options rather than spinning them off.

While not volunteering as primary maintainer for libsecret, carlson commits to contributing fixes as a user, drawing a comparison to their involvement with Kerberos support. The response provides practical deployment context missing from earlier discussion, emphasizing these helpers' importance for secure credential storage across platforms. The tone suggests these components are too critical to deprecate without clear replacements, though the maintenance question remains unresolved.
