This thread proposed adding a `commit.signoff` configuration option to automatically add Signed-off-by lines to commits, similar to the existing `format.signoff` for patches. After initial technical review and consensus among contributors D. Ben Knoble and Chris Down, maintainer Junio C Hamano rejected the feature on principle, arguing that built-in signoff automation differs from user-created aliases and could dilute the legal meaning of signoffs.

The discussion has now concluded with Collin Funk reinforcing Junio's position by drawing parallels to FSF copyright assignment processes. Collin highlights potential legal risks, particularly for exempt employees who may not own copyright to their contributions. The thread establishes a clear project policy: while individual aliases for signoffs are acceptable, Git will not provide first-class configuration options that lower the barrier to automatic signoffs, preserving their legal significance. The existing `format.signoff` option remains as a historical exception but won't be extended to commit-time behavior.
