This thread centers on a proposal to add a `commit.signoff` configuration option that would persistently enable signoffs for commits, mirroring the existing `format.signoff` option for patches. The initial implementation by the author was technically sound, with clean additions to `builtin/commit.c`, documentation, and tests. D. Ben Knoble provided constructive review feedback on test improvements.

The discussion took a turn when maintainer Junio C Hamano raised philosophical objections, questioning whether historical acceptance of `format.signoff` (added in 2009) should automatically justify this new option. Chris Down has now pushed back, challenging Junio to provide concrete examples of problems caused by the long-standing patch signoff configuration rather than relying on general principles. This represents a direct challenge to the maintainer's stance, shifting the debate from implementation details to whether automatic signoffs—for either commits or patches—represent good practice.

The technical work remains ready to merge, but the thread is now at an impasse between precedent-based justification and maintainer skepticism. Junio's position carries significant weight, but Chris's request for evidence introduces a new dimension to the discussion. Unless Junio provides specific examples of harm from `format.signoff` or softens his stance, this proposal may stall despite its technical readiness.
