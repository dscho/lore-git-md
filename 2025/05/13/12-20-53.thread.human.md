This thread discusses adding a `commit.signoff` configuration option to enable persistent signoff behavior for commits, mirroring the existing `format.signoff` option for patches. The technical implementation is straightforward, adding the config option in `builtin/commit.c` with corresponding documentation and tests.

Chris Down proposed the feature, citing the long-standing precedent of `format.signoff` (merged in 2009) as justification. D. Ben Knoble reviewed the implementation positively but initially suggested addressing Junio Hamano's philosophical objections about automatic signoffs. After Chris responded by challenging opponents to demonstrate actual problems caused by `format.signoff` over its 15-year existence, Ben has now clarified that he considers the precedent argument sufficient and no longer objects.

The discussion now hinges entirely on Junio's position as maintainer. While the technical implementation is sound and precedent exists, Junio has expressed reservations about the principle of automatic signoffs regardless of practical impact. Unless Junio either provides evidence of harm from the existing `format.signoff` feature or softens his stance, this feature may stall despite having addressed all technical and precedent-based concerns. The key participants are now Chris (proponent), Ben (satisfied reviewer), and Junio (maintainer with outstanding objections).
