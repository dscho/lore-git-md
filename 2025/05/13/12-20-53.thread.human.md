This thread centers on adding a `commit.signoff` configuration option to enable persistent signoff behavior for commits, mirroring the existing `--signoff` flag and `format.signoff` for patches. The technical implementation in `builtin/commit.c` and accompanying tests have been reviewed and approved by D. Ben Knoble and Chris Down, with no outstanding implementation issues.

The debate has shifted to philosophical concerns raised by Junio C Hamano, who objects to automating legal attestations on principle. Chris Down has proposed framing the feature as a repository-specific policy decision rather than casual automation, suggesting documentation improvements and now a restriction to repository contexts (excluding global configuration) to address these concerns. He argues this makes signoffs more deliberate than current workarounds like global shell aliases.

While the technical reviewers have aligned, Junio's maintainer perspective remains the deciding factor. The discussion with Chris now focuses on whether repository-specific enforcement sufficiently addresses concerns about signoff meaning dilution. The outcome hinges on whether Junio accepts this compromise or maintains his principled opposition despite the technical consensus.
