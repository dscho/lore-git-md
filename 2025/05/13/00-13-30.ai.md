This is a discussion about credential helper maintenance in the ongoing `contrib/` cleanup thread. Brian m. carlson responds to Jeff King's concerns about credential helpers in `contrib/` directory, particularly focusing on platform-specific helpers (`osxkeychain`, `wincred`, `libsecret`).

The email provides important context about real-world usage patterns: `osxkeychain` is shipped by Apple and Homebrew, `libsecret` is the recommended choice for Linux desktops, and `wincred` serves as a lightweight Windows alternative to GCM (Git Credential Manager). Carlson argues these helpers are too widely used to simply deprecate, noting that GCM itself appears under-maintained. They propose promoting the helpers to core with platform-specific build options rather than spinning them off, drawing a parallel to their own role maintaining Kerberos support without being the primary maintainer.

Key technical details mentioned include the potential use of Makefile knobs for platform-specific builds and CI integration. The response highlights the thread's progression from theoretical classification to practical implementation concerns, with credential helpers emerging as a special case where removal would break many users' workflows. Carlson stops short of volunteering as primary maintainer but commits to contributing fixes for `libsecret`, suggesting a distributed maintenance model might work for these components.
