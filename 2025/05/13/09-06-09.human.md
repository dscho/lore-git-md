This is a discussion about the future of Git's credential helpers and other contrib/ components. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Brian m. carlson's proposal about handling credential helpers, agreeing that security-sensitive helpers like `libsecret` and `osxkeychain` should be moved into Git's main repository rather than spun off as separate projects. He expresses reservations about including the `netrc` helper due to its inherent security weaknesses.

The email continues the broader thread about cleaning up Git's contrib/ directory, with this segment focusing specifically on credential storage solutions. Steinhardt aligns with carlson's view that platform-specific credential helpers are too widely used and important to remove, though he doesn't volunteer to maintain them either. The discussion is moving toward concrete decisions about which components to promote to core versus deprecate, with security being a key consideration.
