Documentation refinement in the `contrib/` cleanup series. Elijah Newren approves Patrick Steinhardt's v3 patch series that systematically cleans up Git's `contrib/` directory, noting the improved documentation around removal criteria. The series removes 20 unmaintained components while establishing clear policy guidelines for future maintenance decisions.

The approval comes after Patrick incorporated feedback to clarify that lack of updates should only be secondary evidence for removal, not a primary reason. The v3 series also retains `packinfo.pl` (a useful tool without replacement) and refines the justification language to emphasize technical factors like broken functionality and available alternatives over simple age-based reasoning.

This marks the final approval for the series' technical work, which has already addressed earlier feedback from multiple contributors. The remaining discussion focuses on whether to make an exception for `git-contacts` based on its pedagogical value for new contributors, but that appears to be a separate consideration from the core cleanup work being approved here. The series now provides a documented framework for evaluating future `contrib/` components.
