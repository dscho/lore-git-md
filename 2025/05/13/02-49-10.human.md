Bugfix and refactoring in mailinfo's header decoding. This is v4 of a series addressing memory leaks in email header processing, now focusing on cleaning up the `decode_header()` function's error paths. The patch fixes a case where a failed character set conversion could leak a strbuf, while also completing the architectural shift to caller-allocated strbufs that was proposed in earlier rounds.

The changes are now minimal - just removing the last few stray `strbuf_init()` calls that violated the new contract, and cleaning up some braces in error handling. The core fix ensures `strbuf_release()` is called on the `dec` buffer when `convert_to_utf8()` fails. This appears to be the final polish on a well-reviewed series that both fixes memory leaks and improves the code's structure. The changes are confined to `mailinfo.c` and maintain the established pattern of the v3 changes.
