This is a v4 bugfix patch in the mailinfo header decoding series, addressing the final polish items noted in Junio Hamano's review of v3. The patch completes the architectural refactoring to use caller-allocated strbufs throughout the header decoding subsystem in `mailinfo.c`.

The changes:
1. Remove the last remaining `strbuf_init()` calls in `decode_q_segment()` and `decode_b_segment()`, replacing them with `strbuf_grow()` to maintain the caller-initialized contract
2. Eliminate unnecessary braces in `convert_to_utf8()` error handling that Hamano flagged
3. Consistently use stack-allocated `strbuf dec` in `decode_header()` rather than heap allocation
4. Apply the same pattern to `decode_transfer_encoding()`

The patch maintains all the memory safety improvements from previous versions while finalizing the cleaner ownership model where callers manage strbuf lifecycle. The changes are focused and mechanical, directly responding to the specific review feedback on v3. With these changes, the series appears ready for acceptance, having addressed both the original leak and the architectural concerns through careful iteration.
