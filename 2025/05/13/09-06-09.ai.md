**Contributor housekeeping and credential helper maintenance discussion**

Patrick Steinhardt responds to the ongoing discussion about cleaning up Git's `contrib/` directory and handling credential helpers. The email focuses on credential storage components, building on Jeff King's and Brian m. carlson's earlier points about maintenance challenges.

Steinhardt agrees with Carlson's proposal to promote certain credential helpers (libsecret and osxkeychain) into Git's main repository rather than spinning them off as separate projects, citing their widespread use and security role. He expresses reservations about the netrc helper due to its inherent security weaknesses, questioning whether it should be included in such a promotion. This continues the thread's progression from identifying maintenance gaps to discussing concrete implementation strategies for platform-specific components.

The email reinforces the emerging consensus that some `contrib/` components - particularly security-sensitive ones like credential helpers - warrant special treatment rather than removal or spin-off. Steinhardt's position aligns with Carlson's earlier technical proposal for platform-specific build options while introducing a security-based distinction between different types of credential helpers. The discussion remains focused on operational details rather than high-level policy, with the netrc helper emerging as a potential exception to the promotion approach.
