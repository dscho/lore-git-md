This is a follow-up discussion in the FQDN detection thread, specifically addressing platform-specific behavior of the `hostname` command. Aditya Garg acknowledges Junio Hamano's observation that the current implementation's platform restrictions (limited to Linux and macOS) coincidentally avoid issues with BSD systems where `hostname --fqdn` would fail but `hostname -f` would work. Garg admits the earlier mistake in v2 was due to incomplete testing of both flags across platforms, and confirms the subsequent v3 and v4 patches properly handle this distinction. The exchange highlights how the implementation's current scope (Linux/macOS only) provides natural protection against BSD flag incompatibilities, while also serving as a reminder about the importance of thorough cross-platform testing. This discussion reinforces the consensus reached in v4 about the platform-specific approach to FQDN detection.
