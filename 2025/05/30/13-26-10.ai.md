Performance optimization discussion regarding promisor_remote_get_direct() and is_promisor_object() checks. Han Young responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about their earlier patch that removed promisor object verification from failed fetch handling.

The email provides important technical details about why is_promisor_object() is slow - it must parse every object in promisor packfiles (millions in large repos) and their references. Han acknowledges Junio's point that callers may pass missing non-promisor objects, making the original assumption unsafe. They also confirm the behavior change in error handling - the function will now die on any fetch failure rather than just for promised objects.

Key new information includes test results showing git-cat-file and git-diff fail in both cases but with different error messages. Han references Calvin Wan's prior analysis that caching promisor objects is difficult due to their dynamic nature (local objects can become promisor objects). The discussion remains focused on whether the performance optimization can be achieved while maintaining safety of the promisor object verification, with no clear resolution yet.
