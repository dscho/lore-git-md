Documentation patch series refining Git's email-related documentation, now in its 7th iteration. This version focuses on consistency improvements and credential helper references.

The series continues Aditya Garg's work to improve Git's email documentation, with four main changes:

1. Adds references to Yahoo's credential helper and Google's `sendgmail` tool as alternative email sending methods
2. Standardizes formatting throughout `git-send-email` documentation, particularly around email headers (now consistently backtick-wrapped)
3. Removes redundant credential helper links from `gitcredentials` documentation (keeping them only in send-email docs)
4. Clarifies Gmail authentication requirements, encouraging OAuth2.0 over app passwords

The most notable change in v7 is the complete standardization of header formatting - all email headers like `To:`, `Cc:`, and `Subject:` are now consistently wrapped in backticks. This resolves the last outstanding formatting debate from previous versions, bringing the documentation in line with Junio Hamano and Ben Knoble's preferred style.

The patch series appears well-polished at this point, with thorough attention to detail in both content and formatting. The changes are clearly motivated by real documentation needs (including references to an external git-scm.com PR) and maintain backward compatibility while improving clarity.
