Standardization patch finalizing BUG() message conventions across the codebase.

This final patch in Lidong Yan's v3 series completes the project-wide standardization of BUG() assertion messages by removing translation markers (`_()`) from all remaining instances. The changes affect three files: builtin/mktag.c, builtin/worktree.c, and pack-bitmap-write.c. Each modification follows the established principle that BUG() messages are programmer-facing rather than user-facing, and thus should not be translated.

The patch mechanically converts the last remaining `BUG(_("..."))` patterns to `BUG("...")` without altering the actual message content. The changes are straightforward and consistent with the series' earlier work on the sequencer's BUG() messages. The modifications touch error handling in tag validation (mktag), worktree operations, and bitmap writing logic, all cases where the assertions represent internal consistency checks rather than user-visible errors.

This concludes the combined effort that began with fixing a specific memory leak in the sequencer and expanded to establish clear conventions for BUG() usage. The series has received maintainer approval from both Phillip Wood (for sequencer changes) and Junio C Hamano (for style standardization), with no outstanding issues remaining. The standardization establishes a clear precedent for future contributions regarding assertion message formatting.
