This is a style nitpick in the ongoing `imap-send` feature series adding OAuth2.0 authentication support. Eric Sunshine points out that the multi-line comment formatting in the OAuth2.0 implementation doesn't match Git's standard style - it should use `/*` on its own line with subsequent lines prefixed by ` * `, rather than having the opening `/*` on the same line as the comment text.

The comment itself documents the OAUTHBEARER string format, which combines RFC5801's gs2 header with RFC7628's key-value pairs. While this is purely a style issue, it's the kind of detail maintainers look for in patch review. The series overall is quite mature (now at v9) and this appears to be one of the last remaining polish items before finalization.
