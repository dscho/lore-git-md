Refactoring patch that modernizes maintenance task initialization in `builtin/gc.c`. This is the first of 12 patches in Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series addressing race conditions in Git's maintenance operations.

The patch converts the array of maintenance tasks to use designated field initializers (e.g., `.name = "prefetch"` instead of positional initialization). This makes the code more readable and maintainable, as adding new fields to the `maintenance_task` struct won't require updating every task definition. The change is purely structural - no behavior is altered.

This appears to be a straightforward preparatory change that sets up the codebase for the more substantial modifications coming later in the series. The commit message is clear about the motivation (easier future maintenance) and the diff shows a mechanical but careful conversion of all task definitions.
