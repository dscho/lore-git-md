This is a documentation patch series focused on improving Git's email-related documentation, particularly around credential helpers and formatting. It's the sixth iteration of changes that have been progressively refined through review feedback. The series:

1. Adds links to Yahoo's credential helper and Google's `sendgmail` tool, removing outdated Yahoo SMTP instructions that were cluttering the docs.
2. Standardizes formatting in `git-send-email` documentation, fixing inconsistent quoting styles and adding missing punctuation. The author notably defends their choice of single quotes for mail headers ('To', 'Cc') over backticks for better readability.
3. Consolidates credential helper links by removing them from `gitcredentials.adoc` (they remain in `git-send-email` docs where they're more relevant).
4. Clarifies Gmail authentication requirements, emphasizing that app passwords are mandatory and OAuth2.0 is a more secure alternative. The patch carefully rewords this section to present options neutrally after earlier review feedback.

The changes show thoughtful attention to both technical accuracy and documentation readability, with the author pushing back slightly on strict formatting rules when they believe it harms clarity. The series appears ready for final review, with the main outstanding question being whether the maintainers will accept the author's stylistic preferences for header formatting.
