This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing race conditions in Git's maintenance operations, specifically focusing on the "gc" task. The patch follows the same pattern as earlier fixes for "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" by splitting the GC task into pre- and post-daemonization phases to avoid lockfile races.

The key change moves the `gc_before_repack()` logic (renamed to `gc_before_detach()` for clarity) to run before daemonization, while adding a new `--skip-maintenance-before-detach` flag to prevent duplicate execution when spawning the actual git-gc process. This maintains the same behavior while eliminating the race condition where lockfiles could be held across the daemonization boundary.
