This is a follow-up discussion on a v2 bugfix series addressing memory leaks in pack-bitmap.c's boundary object traversal. The series consists of two patches: one to unconditionally free a bitmap that was previously only freed conditionally, and another adding test coverage for the leak scenario.

Junio C Hamano is asking for clarification on the relationship between the two patches. He notes that if the test in the second patch demonstrates the bug fixed by the first patch, they would typically be combined into a single commit. However, if they're independent changes, keeping them separate would be appropriate.

The question suggests the series might benefit from either:
1. Combining the fixes if they're directly related (test proves the bug exists and the fix works)
2. More clearly explaining why they're separate if they address different aspects

This comes after the v2 already incorporated review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and appears technically sound. The core fix (always freeing roots_bitmap) and test coverage are both present, but the patch organization is being questioned on workflow grounds rather than technical merit.
