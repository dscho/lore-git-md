Documentation patch finalizing the cleanup of email-related credential helper references. This is part of a now-finalized documentation series (v7) that reorganizes Git's email-sending documentation.

The patch removes Gmail and Outlook credential helper links from gitcredentials.adoc, as these were deemed out of place in a document focused on repository operations rather than email sending. The rationale is clear: gitcredentials.adoc doesn't discuss email sending, while git-send-email.adoc (where these helpers remain documented) is the more appropriate location. The change originated from work on git-scm.com PR #2005 that identified this documentation organization issue.

This appears to be a straightforward cleanup that follows through on earlier decisions in the series. The patch maintains the strict backtick consistency and other formatting standards established in previous rounds of review. With all substantive feedback addressed in earlier versions, this final mechanical change seems ready for inclusion.
