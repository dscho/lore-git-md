This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to fix race conditions in Git's maintenance operations. It's the second of twelve patches in version 2 of the series.

The patch simplifies the quiet mode handling in `builtin/gc.c` by eliminating a redundant local variable. Previously, there were two variables tracking quiet mode - a local `quiet` variable and the `quiet` field in `struct maintenance_run_opts`. The patch removes the local variable and consistently uses the struct field throughout the code, making the behavior clearer and reducing potential confusion.

The change is mechanical but improves code clarity by removing duplicate state. All references to the local `quiet` variable are replaced with `opts.quiet`, including in progress reporting for packing, pruning, and commit graph writing operations. The patch maintains identical behavior while making the code more maintainable.

This is a straightforward cleanup that fits into the series' broader goal of improving maintenance operation reliability through code quality improvements. The change is small and focused, with no apparent downsides or controversy.
