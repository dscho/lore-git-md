This is a documentation patch that finalizes the Gmail authentication instructions in the `git-send-email` documentation. It's part of a completed v7 series that has already addressed all previous review feedback.

The patch clarifies two authentication paths for Gmail users of `git send-email`: app-specific passwords (required for accounts with multi-factor authentication) and the more secure OAuth2.0 method. The new wording makes it clearer that OAuth2.0 is the preferred approach when available, working for all accounts regardless of MFA status.

Junio Hamano approves the changes, calling them "much nicer" than the previous version. This marks the final polish on documentation that has undergone seven rounds of review, with all formatting and content issues now resolved. The changes align with the parallel effort to implement OAuth2.0 support in `git-imap-send`, though that technical work remains under separate review.
