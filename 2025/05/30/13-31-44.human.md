This is a test infrastructure patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to improve TAP output compliance between Make and Meson builds. The patch focuses on fixing a specific TAP formatting issue in test-lib.sh.

The change is small but important for TAP compliance - it adds a "#" prefix to a warning message about unsupported BASH_XTRACEFD functionality in older Bash versions. Without this prefix, the warning would break TAP parsers expecting properly formatted output. The warning appears when tests are run with tracing enabled (-x) on systems with Bash versions that don't support the BASH_XTRACEFD feature.

This is part of a larger effort (now up to 10 patches in v3) to standardize test output formatting across build systems. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, following the pattern of previous TAP compliance fixes in the series. It maintains the warning's functionality while making it properly machine-readable.
