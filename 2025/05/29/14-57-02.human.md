This is a bugfix patch addressing threading issues in `git send-email`. It's the first of two patches in v6 of a series that has already gone through several rounds of review and refinement.

The patch fixes a subtle but important edge case where editing and resending the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` and shallow threading enabled could break the entire thread structure. The root cause is an incorrect increment of the internal `$message_num` counter during message edits, which affects how subsequent messages determine their threading relationships.

The solution is straightforward - decrementing `$message_num` when a message is edited or skipped, ensuring the counter remains accurate for proper threading. The patch includes clear explanations of both the problem and solution in the commit message, and the changes are minimal (10 lines added) and focused.

This iteration incorporates all of Junio C Hamano's feedback from v5, including improved commit message wording and documentation. The technical approach appears sound and the patch seems ready for inclusion. The second patch in the series (not shown here) deals with related Message-ID logging improvements.
