This is the second patch in a v6 series fixing threading and logging issues in `git send-email`. The patch specifically addresses how Message-ID changes by email clients are logged.

The change modifies the logging output to show the actual Message-ID assigned by email servers (like Outlook) rather than the original ID generated by Git. The patch also makes the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled, since the final log already shows the delivered Message-ID.
