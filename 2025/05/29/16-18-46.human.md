This is the second patch in a finalized bugfix series for `git send-email`, addressing logging issues with email clients. The patch ensures that when Outlook modifies Message-IDs during delivery, the logs accurately reflect the final delivered ID rather than the original one. It also makes the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled, since the main log will already show the updated ID.

Junio Hamano has reviewed the technical approach and approved the changes, though he questions whether an inline comment adds value given the self-explanatory regex. He indicates he will queue the series after fixing a typo in the first patch. The author, Aditya Garg, asks if another revision is needed but seems to accept Junio's minor suggestions. This represents the final iteration of a well-reviewed fix for a subtle logging issue.
