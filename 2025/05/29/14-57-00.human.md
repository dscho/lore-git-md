This is the sixth iteration of a two-patch bugfix series addressing threading and logging issues in `git send-email`. The series remains focused on two specific edge cases in email processing.

The first patch fixes a subtle threading bug where editing the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` and shallow threading enabled could break the thread structure. The issue stemmed from incorrect incrementing of the internal `$message_num` counter during message operations. The v6 version incorporates Junio's feedback by refining the commit message wording (preferring "increment" over "increase") and removing an unnecessary adjustment to the `$message_id_serial` variable that was present in earlier versions.

The second patch improves logging when email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery, making the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled. This appears unchanged from v5.

Aditya Garg has been responsive to review feedback, with v6 showing careful attention to documentation and variable handling. The changes are small (14 lines modified total) but address real edge cases in email threading behavior. The series looks ready for inclusion, with the main discussion points around documentation and commit message clarity having been addressed in this iteration.
