This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. The patch clarifies the commit message for patch 8/9, which introduces configurable cruft pack exclusion from MIDX.

The email corrects a range-diff formatting issue from the previous submission and provides an updated explanation of the cruft pack inclusion behavior. The key clarification is that the `--stdin-packs=follow` mode (introduced in patch 7/9) is not yet the default behavior, which affects how objects from cruft packs are handled during geometric repacking. The commit message now better explains the scenario where a once-unreachable object in a cruft pack becomes reachable but might be missing from the MIDX bitmap generation if the cruft pack isn't included.

This patch series has been through multiple iterations (now at v4) with thorough review from Elijah Newren and Junio Hamano, and has been validated in production at GitHub. The changes are well-motivated by real-world performance needs while maintaining Git's reachability guarantees. The series appears ready for merging pending final minor style cleanup.
