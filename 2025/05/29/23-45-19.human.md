Maintainer feedback on subtree GPG signing implementation. Junio Hamano provides detailed technical review of the first patch in Patrik Weiskircher's series adding GPG signing to `git subtree`. The review focuses on implementation details of the option parsing and signing flag handling, suggesting several potential simplifications:

1. Notes the `gpg_sign_opt()` helper function may be unnecessary and proposes alternative approaches to handle the `-S` flag directly in the option parsing loop.

2. Points out potential edge cases in the current parsing logic, particularly around how the `-S` option interacts with other flags and whether it properly handles both stuck (`-Skeyid`) and separate (`-S keyid`) forms.

3. Observes the script's general lack of whitespace handling in arguments (a pre-existing issue) and suggests either documenting this limitation or considering more robust parsing.

The feedback is constructive and technical, clearly coming from someone deeply familiar with Git's codebase and shell scripting patterns. While not rejecting the approach outright, it identifies several areas where the implementation could be made more robust or idiomatic. The author will need to address these points in a revised version before the feature can proceed.
