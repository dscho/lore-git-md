This is the second patch in a two-part series fixing logging and threading issues in `git send-email`. The patch addresses a specific case where email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery. Currently, while `git send-email` logs the new Message-ID when Outlook changes it, the final header log still shows the original ID. This patch ensures the final log reflects the actual delivered Message-ID by updating the `$header` variable.

The change is straightforward - it modifies the Message-ID in the header log to match what Outlook assigned, and makes the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled (since the final log will now show the correct ID anyway). The patch includes clear before/after examples showing the improved logging behavior.

This appears to be a well-considered solution to a real-world usability issue, particularly for users sending through Outlook servers.
