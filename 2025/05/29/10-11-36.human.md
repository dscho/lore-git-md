This is a platform compatibility fix for the reftable subsystem, addressing a build failure on NonStop systems. The patch modifies the `REFTABLE_UNUSED` macro definition to be conditional on GNU C compilers, falling back to an empty definition otherwise.

The issue was reported by Randall S. Becker, the NonStop port maintainer, who found that the current unconditional use of GNU's `__attribute__((__unused__))` breaks compilation on NonStop's C99 compiler. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's solution follows a common pattern seen in Git's codebase for handling compiler-specific attributes.

The change is minimal (just 4 lines added to `reftable/basics.h`) but important for maintaining compatibility across platforms. It preserves the unused parameter warning suppression for GNU compilers while allowing other compilers to build successfully. The patch appears well-targeted and follows Git's established practices for handling such platform differences.
