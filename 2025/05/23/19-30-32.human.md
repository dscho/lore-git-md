Bugfix follow-up addressing potential divide-by-zero in parallel fetch operations.

Junio Hamano responds to Alex Guo's initial patch that fixed a divide-by-zero risk in `builtin/fetch.c` by validating `max_children <= 0`. While agreeing the problem exists, Hamano questions whether the solution is complete, pointing out several scenarios where zero could still propagate through the system:

1. Buggy `online_cpus()` implementations returning zero
2. User configurations explicitly setting parallel jobs to zero
3. Similar issues in submodule fetch job parsing

He suggests two alternative approaches: either fix the issue at the source by hardening `online_cpus()` (providing a patch that adds validation there), or add a stronger safeguard that forces `max_children` to at least 1 before entering the parallel fetch code paths.

The response indicates this is more complex than the initial patch addressed, with multiple potential failure modes to consider. Hamano's tone is constructive but cautious, inviting further discussion about the best way to comprehensively fix the issue. This moves the thread from a straightforward bugfix to a deeper discussion about system-wide validation of parallel job counts.
