Documentation patch completing a 9-part series on git-notes documentation improvements. This final patch standardizes option formatting in git-notes.adoc to use the "stuck form" (`--option=value`) consistently, bringing the last remaining option (`--ref`) into alignment with Git's recommended style from gitcli(7).

The change is straightforward - converting `--ref <ref>` to `--ref=<ref>` - and fits naturally with the series' broader goal of documentation standardization. The author notes this was an oversight in earlier rounds and considers it a minor enough change to include in the final patch rather than spinning up a new thread.

This concludes a thorough documentation cleanup that has addressed numerous technical inaccuracies and inconsistencies in git-notes' documentation, particularly around `--stdin` behavior and comment character handling. The series builds on previous work and has incorporated feedback from multiple reviewers in its v1 iteration.
