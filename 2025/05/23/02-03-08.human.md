This is a technical discussion about MIDX (multi-pack-index) handling during repository reprepare operations, following up on the performance optimization thread.

Jeff King raises a question about how `prepare_multi_pack_index_one()` handles existing MIDX chains when `reprepare_packed_git()` is called. He notes that the current code appears to create a loop in the MIDX chain by appending the new MIDX while keeping the old one, rather than properly replacing it. This could lead to stale MIDX entries being consulted even after a reprepare.

The discussion builds on the recently approved performance patches that optimized packfile lookups by caching missing pack information. Taylor Blau had previously confirmed that `reprepare_packed_git()` does properly reload MIDX files, but Jeff's analysis suggests there may still be an edge case where old MIDX data remains accessible through the chain of `next` pointers.

This appears to be a subtle implementation detail that wasn't caught during the main review of the optimization series. While the performance improvements are sound, this exchange highlights another area where MIDX chain handling could be made more robust. The discussion is likely to lead to either a clarification of the intended behavior or a follow-up patch to fix the potential loop creation.
