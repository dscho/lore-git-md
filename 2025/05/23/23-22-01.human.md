brian m. carlson responds to Ramsay Jones's question about why `git-compat-util.h` is being included in `reflog-walk.h`. While acknowledging the general rule that `git-compat-util.h` should be included first in compilation units, the author defends the change as necessary for tooling support but offers to remove it if strongly opposed.
