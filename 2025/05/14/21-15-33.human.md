Feature discussion about integrating tree-level blame functionality into existing commands.

Marc Branchaud continues advocating for integrating the tree-level blame functionality into `git blame` rather than creating a new `git-blame-tree` command. He argues that context-dependent behavior (different output for files vs directories) is common in Unix tools like `ls` and `diff`, and that extending an existing command improves discoverability for current Git users. He counters Toon Claes' concerns about machine parsing by noting that `git blame` isn't officially plumbing, and scripts would know what output to expect based on their arguments.

The discussion remains focused on the naming and integration approach rather than technical implementation details. Marc maintains that the functionality should live under the `git blame` command with possible heuristics or flags to trigger tree-mode behavior, though he doesn't propose specific flag names here. The email reinforces the ongoing debate between three approaches: full integration, separate command, or plumbing/public split, with Marc strongly favoring integration for usability reasons.
