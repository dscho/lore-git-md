This is part 14/17 of Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's object database (ODB) subsystem to remove `the_repository` global state. The patch renames `repo_read_object_file()` to `odb_read_object()` to align with the naming conventions established earlier in the series for ODB-related functions.

The change is mechanical but widespread, updating 47 files across the codebase to use the new function name. A compatibility wrapper is provided to avoid breaking in-flight topics. The rename follows the pattern seen in previous patches of prefixing ODB-related functions with `odb_` and passing the object database explicitly rather than implicitly via `the_repository`.

This continues the systematic approach of the series - maintaining functionality while making the object database interactions more explicit and properly scoped. The patch appears straightforward with no behavioral changes, just the function rename and corresponding updates to callers. It builds on the architectural foundation laid in earlier patches that introduced the `object_database` structure and related APIs.
