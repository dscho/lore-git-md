This is part 13/17 of Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's object database (ODB) subsystem to remove `the_repository` global state. The patch focuses on renaming the `oid_object_info()` function to `odb_read_object_info()` and its extended variant to `odb_read_object_info_extended()`, bringing them in line with modern naming conventions for ODB-related functions.

The change is mechanical but widespread, touching 48 files across the codebase to update callers. The patch maintains compatibility by introducing wrapper functions so that in-flight topics continue to compile. This follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, where functions are systematically renamed to use the `odb_` prefix and take explicit ODB parameters rather than relying on the global `the_repository`.

The changes are straightforward and well-contained, with the bulk being simple renaming of function calls. The patch continues the series' careful approach of maintaining functionality while modernizing the codebase. Given this is part of an established series with previous positive feedback (including Reviewed-by tags from Derrick Stolee on earlier patches), it appears to be a low-risk change that advances the overall goal of removing global state from the ODB subsystem.
