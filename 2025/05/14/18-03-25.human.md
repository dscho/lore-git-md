Jeff King's response in the memory leak fix thread for pack-bitmap.c. This is a review follow-up in an ongoing bugfix discussion about bitmap loading paths.

King agrees with Taylor Blau's analysis of a second memory leak scenario beyond the initial fix from Lidong Yan. While affirming that the original patch is correct and can be merged independently, he notes Blau identified an additional cleanup opportunity where partially loaded bitmaps could leak when `load_bitmap()` fails mid-operation. 

The discussion has shifted from just fixing the immediate validation-order leak to recognizing a broader pattern of redundant cleanup code. King supports Blau's proposed approach of removing the `failed` label cleanup in favor of relying on callers' `free_bitmap_index()`, which would both fix the leak and simplify the code. 

King notes the practical challenge of testing these edge cases with intentionally corrupted bitmaps, suggesting it may not be worth the test suite complexity despite the theoretical benefit. The tone is positive and collaborative, with clear technical agreement on the direction while leaving room for Blau to submit the follow-up fix.
