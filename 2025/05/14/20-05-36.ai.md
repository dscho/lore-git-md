This is a continuation of the ongoing discussion about maintenance and security of credential helpers in Git, specifically addressing the trade-offs between keeping them in-tree versus spinning them out as separate projects.

Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King's concerns about maintainer burden, noting that moving credential helpers from `contrib/` to `tools/` wouldn't significantly increase his workload as release manager. He acknowledges the security implications King raised about the Windows credential helper (`wincred`), particularly its past vulnerabilities and questionable matching strategy, but defers to Windows experts like Johannes Schindelin for current status assessment.

The email reinforces the thread's emerging consensus that security-sensitive helpers like `libsecret` and `osxkeychain` should likely promotion to core, while maintaining flexibility for other helpers based on their security profiles and maintenance situations. Hamano's response suggests the project infrastructure can handle the additional maintenance load of promoted components, removing one potential objection to keeping important helpers in-tree. The discussion continues to balance security considerations with practical maintenance realities, with Windows-specific concerns now explicitly flagged for Windows maintainers' input.
