This is a continuation of the ongoing discussion about integrating the proposed `git-blame-tree` functionality, now focusing on UI design and naming. Marc Branchaud responds to Junio Hamano's hybrid UI proposal and naming concerns.

Marc expresses support for Junio's suggested hybrid approach where `git blame` would automatically switch to tree mode when given directories or multiple files, while maintaining traditional line-by-line blame for single files. He also agrees with Junio's position that the operation isn't conceptually "blame" and suggests alternative names like "ascribe-tree" or possibly integrating it into `git ls-tree` with an `--ascribe` flag.

The discussion highlights the tension points in the design: maintaining backward compatibility for single-file blame while providing intuitive tree-level functionality, and finding a name that accurately reflects the operation's nature (which is more akin to parallelized `git log -1` than traditional blame). Marc's openness to both the hybrid UI and a completely separate command suggests the technical implementation is flexible enough to accommodate either path, with the remaining questions being primarily about user experience and terminology.
