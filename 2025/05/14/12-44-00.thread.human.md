Lidong Yan's patch series has addressed both a specific memory leak in the sequencer's `update_squash_messages()` function during interactive rebase operations and established a project-wide convention for BUG() assertion messages. The series has gone through three iterations, with the final version now merge-ready.

The original issue involved improper command type validation in the sequencer that could lead to memory leaks. The fix introduces early validation using `is_fixup()` and adds BUG() assertions with proper cleanup. This then expanded into a broader standardization effort to ensure all BUG() messages (which are programmer-facing assertions) remain untranslated across the codebase. The final patch completes this standardization by removing translation markers from BUG() messages in builtin/mktag.c, builtin/worktree.c, and pack-bitmap-write.c.

The series has received approval from both Phillip Wood (sequencer maintainer) and Junio C Hamano, with all discussion points resolved. The changes establish clear conventions for future contributions while fixing the original memory management issue. The standardization represents a notable style decision that may influence how assertions are written in future patches, though no further action is required on this specific series.
