This is a follow-up discussion about test infrastructure modernization in the reftable subsystem, specifically addressing the organization of helper functions. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's concern about the initial approach of adding new Clar-based test helpers without immediately removing the old ones.

Patrick agrees with the staged approach but suggests the new helper functions should live in `t/unit-tests/lib-reftable.{c,h}` rather than the more general `unit-test.{c,h}`, since they are reftable-specific.

This is part of the ongoing v3 series converting all reftable unit tests to the Clar framework. The discussion highlights maintainer preferences about code organization while confirming the overall migration strategy is sound.
