This is a follow-up discussion in the feature patch series implementing standalone maintenance tasks to replace `git gc`, specifically addressing the rerere garbage collection component. The email responds to Junio Hamano's performance concerns about the three-phase approach (collect, prune, cleanup) for rerere GC that was introduced in v2. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the validity of the concern that counting stale rerere entries before pruning may add unnecessary overhead, especially since the enumeration and staleness checking likely dominates the actual pruning cost. He proposes simplifying the approach to either just count rr-cache directories without checking staleness, or revert to the original simpler existence check (verifying rr-cache has at least one subdirectory). The discussion highlights platform-specific performance considerations (Windows vs Linux/macOS) and leaves open the possibility of iterating on the heuristic if the simpler approach proves insufficient. This represents the last open question in the otherwise-approved series, with Patrick indicating a preference to start with the simpler model for now.
