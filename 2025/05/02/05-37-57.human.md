This is an administrative follow-up in the Git 20th anniversary community interview project. Kaartic Sivaraam from the Git Rev News team shares the compiled draft containing responses from Luca Milanesio, Lucas Seiki Oshiro, and Elijah Newren, now available for review on GitHub before finalizing the special anniversary edition. The email serves as a notification to the project has moved from the response collection phase to editorial review, with contributors invited to verify their answers in the draft.

The message also addresses Lucas Seiki Oshiro's earlier question about sharing interview questions with local communities, confirming this is acceptable and suggesting interesting new submissions could be included in future editions. While primarily procedural, it maintains the project's collaborative spirit by keeping contributors informed and engaged in the final stages of preparing this milestone celebration of Git's history and community perspectives.
