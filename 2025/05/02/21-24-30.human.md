This is the final approval for the path-walk delta compression series. Junio Hamano notes that since there were no further comments on v2 of the patch series, he is marking it for inclusion in the 'next' branch. 

The series introduces path-based delta compression grouping through a new `--path-walk` option and `pack.usePathWalk` config, with implementations in pack-objects, repack, and Scalar. The key innovation is replacing name-hash grouping with path-based grouping during delta selection, which shows significant benefits for repositories with many hash collisions (like JavaScript projects) and large Scalar-managed repositories. Performance data shows size reductions up to 11.8% in some cases, though with a runtime cost.

The series is now complete with all 13 patches, including threading optimizations, shallow clone compatibility through the new `edge_aggressive` mode, and full documentation. While some open questions remain about bitmap interaction and delta island compatibility, the core functionality is ready for wider testing in the 'next' branch. This represents a significant optimization for certain repository types, particularly Microsoft's monorepo use case where it reduced push sizes by 100x in their CI environment.
