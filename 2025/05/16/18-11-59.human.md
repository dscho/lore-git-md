**Feature enabling path-walk compression for Scalar repositories**

This patch enables the new path-walk delta compression by default for Scalar repositories by setting `pack.usePathWalk=true` in their configuration. As part of the larger path-walk compression series (now in its third iteration and approved for merging), this change specifically targets Scalar's use case where repositories are typically large client-side clones that stand to benefit most from the path-based compression approach.

The change is minimal - just a single line addition to scalar.c - but builds on the substantial infrastructure developed in earlier patches. The commit message explains the rationale clearly: Scalar repositories are expected to be large client-side repos where path-based delta compression shows significant benefits (as demonstrated by the 100x size reductions in some Microsoft internal cases mentioned in the thread summary).

This appears to be a straightforward application of the new capability to its most obvious beneficiary, with no controversy expected given the thorough review the series has already undergone. The patch maintains consistency with the series' goal of making path-walk available where it helps most, while keeping it opt-in for general use via the config option.
