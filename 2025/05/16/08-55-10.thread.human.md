This thread discusses a performance optimization for packfile handling that reduces unnecessary system calls when dealing with missing packs. The original patch from Patrick Steinhardt reorders checks in `add_packed_git()` to verify pack existence before checking auxiliary files (.keep, .promisor, .cruft), cutting syscalls by 75% in edge cases where stale multi-pack-index references exist.

Junio Hamano has responded with thoughtful feedback, acknowledging the optimization's benefits while raising important historical context. He notes the original check ordering was designed to ensure packfile integrity by verifying .idx files before .pack files, and questions whether this safety mechanism still serves its purpose given changes to the pack naming scheme. While recognizing the 75% reduction is valuable, Junio suggests the remaining syscalls might also be avoidable, and hints that stale multi-pack-indexes (the root cause) should perhaps be addressed directly rather than optimizing around them.

The discussion has opened up deeper questions about packfile integrity checking that may need revisiting, though the current optimization appears safe to proceed with as a low-risk improvement. The thread remains open to further technical discussion about whether the original safety constraints still apply to modern Git's pack handling.
