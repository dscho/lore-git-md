This is a documentation patch removing outdated references to the now-unused git-mentoring mailing list from the MyFirstContribution tutorial. It's part of a v2 series (1/3) that appears separate from the earlier GSOC tutorial modernization work.

The patch removes an 8-line section describing the git-mentoring@googlegroups.com list, which was previously positioned as a more beginner-friendly alternative to the main mailing list. The rationale given is that new contributors now successfully engage directly with git@vger.kernel.org and receive responses, making the separate mentoring list obsolete. The removal helps avoid confusion by not pointing newcomers to an inactive resource.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial - it simply deletes text rather than modifying any active documentation. The patch follows the style of the ongoing documentation modernization effort, though it addresses a different aspect than the repository-aware config work from the previous series. As a minor cleanup, it likely requires little review attention beyond confirming the mentoring list is indeed no longer in use.
