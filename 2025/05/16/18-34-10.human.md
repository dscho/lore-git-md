This is a performance optimization discussion about packfile handling, specifically addressing syscall overhead when checking for missing packs. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that reorders checks in `add_packed_git()` to avoid unnecessary `access()` calls for auxiliary files when the main packfile doesn't exist.
