The motivation is both semantic (the server is uploading, not fetching) and practical (it helps CodeQL static analysis by avoiding confusion with similarly-named enums elsewhere).

All state transitions and logic remain identical, just with the new enum names. This continues the series' pattern of addressing CodeQL findings through non-disruptive improvements, in this case resolving potential static analysis confusion about enum definitions while making the code's intent clearer to human readers.
