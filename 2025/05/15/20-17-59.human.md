This is a discussion about build system maintenance in the final stages of a 5-part series improving Git's build infrastructure. The email from Eli Schwartz responds to Ramsay Jones' earlier comments about the usefulness of maintaining Autoconf support, particularly regarding a patch that adds sysinfo() detection for Cygwin.

Eli acknowledges that Gentoo uses Meson (finding it useful), while noting Cygwin still relies on Autoconf. The key insight is that build system usefulness should be judged by actual usage rather than personal preference. While Eli isn't volunteering to maintain Autoconf, they recognize its continued need for some users and suggest any deprecation should be a deliberate decision rather than passive neglect.

The exchange reveals the pragmatic considerations behind build system choices, with both contributors carefully avoiding strong advocacy while acknowledging different projects' needs. This comes as the broader series nears completion, having addressed all technical and documentation requirements across Make, Meson and Autoconf systems.
