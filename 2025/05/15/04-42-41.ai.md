Documentation patch discussion follow-up. Reto responds to Elijah Newren's proposal to prefix rebase todo list commit descriptions with '#' to clarify they are comments rather than directives.

Reto acknowledges the motivation behind the change (preventing user confusion when editing commit messages in the todo list) but argues against the implementation. His primary objection is that syntax highlighting in editors would dim the commit messages when marked as comments, making them harder to read. He considers these messages his main navigation aid during rebases since he doesn't memorize commit SHAs.

The response suggests alternative approaches might be preferable, though doesn't propose specific solutions beyond potentially adding explanatory text in the footer. This introduces a design consideration not addressed in the original patch - the tradeoff between preventing mistaken edits and maintaining readability/visual prominence of the commit messages.

Key technical points raised:
- Syntax highlighting behavior in editors would be affected by comment markers
- Commit messages serve as primary navigation aids during rebases
- Alternative solutions like footer explanations are hinted at but not detailed

This response marks the first substantive critique of the proposed change, focusing on usability impact rather than technical implementation. The thread now has two clear positions to reconcile: the original proposal's goal of preventing user errors versus the respondent's concern about degraded readability.
