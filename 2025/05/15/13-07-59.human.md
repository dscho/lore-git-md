This is the final approval for Elijah Newren's `--dry-run` option for `git merge-tree`, marking the completion of the patch series. Junio Hamano gives his OK to merge after catching one last minor issue - the need for test coverage of incompatible option combinations (like `--dry-run` with `--messages` or `--stdin`). 

The series introduces an efficient mergeability checking mode that avoids writing objects and exits early on conflicts, primarily targeting Git hosting platforms. The implementation has gone through multiple rounds of review with naming refinements (from `--mergeability-only` to `--dry-run`) and thorough testing. 

With all feedback addressed and final approval from both Junio and documentation reviewer Kristoffer Haugsbakk, this performance optimization is now ready for inclusion. The patch provides a clean solution for use cases that need to check mergeability without performing full merges.
