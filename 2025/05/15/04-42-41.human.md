Documentation discussion regarding commit message visibility in rebase todo lists. This is a reply to Elijah Newren's proposal to prefix commit descriptions with '#' in interactive rebase todo lists to clarify they are comments rather than directives.

Reto expresses reservations about the proposed change, arguing that while the motivation (preventing user mistakes) is valid, the solution would make commit messages less visible in editors that syntax-highlight comments. They note these messages serve as important navigation aids during rebases, more so than the commit hashes.

The response suggests alternative approaches like adding explanatory text in the footer rather than altering the visual presentation of commit messages. This indicates the initial proposal may need refinement to balance clarity with usability, particularly for experienced users who rely on the current formatting. The discussion appears to be in early stages with no clear resolution yet.
