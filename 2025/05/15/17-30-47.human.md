This is a discussion about naming and integration of the proposed `git-blame-tree` functionality, part of the ongoing RFC thread about tracking modifications across directory trees. Marc Branchaud responds to Patrick Steinhardt's defense of the "blame" terminology by acknowledging that if the operation is conceptually similar to blaming, it should be integrated into `git blame` rather than being a separate command. He also retracts his earlier suggestion of "ascribe-tree" as a name, recognizing it would be problematic for non-native English speakers and ironically similar to "blame" anyway.

The exchange highlights the tension between technical implementation similarities (both commands track object modifications) and user mental model differences (line-by-line vs tree-level attribution). While the technical implementation appears solid, the naming and integration approach remain open questions, with Marc leaning toward integration into `git blame` if the conceptual similarity holds, but recognizing the need for an accessible name that doesn't confuse users. The discussion continues to weigh technical parallels against user experience considerations.
