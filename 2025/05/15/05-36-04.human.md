This is a follow-up discussion in the git-gui commit message formatting thread, confirming technical details about the solution. Johannes Sixt responds to Junio Hamano's observation about git-gui's direct use of `commit-tree` (rather than the porcelain `git commit`) requiring careful blank line handling. 

Sixt confirms this is correct and provides historical context: git-gui previously used `git stripspace` (introduced in b9a4386) but reverted it in c0696df due to compatibility issues with older Tcl versions and macOS. He notes this approach might be reconsidered if version requirements are raised in the future.

The exchange serves as acknoledgement that the current solution (using `string trim` in `lib/commit.tcl`) is the right approach given the constraints, while leaving the door open for potential future improvements. The thread appears settled with all parties in agreement on the technical approach.
