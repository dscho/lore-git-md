This is the second patch in a documentation reorganization series that moves email-related credential helper references from gitcredentials.adoc to git-send-email.adoc. The change follows earlier discussion where Junio Hamano approved consolidating email provider documentation in one place.

The patch removes references to git-credential-gmail and git-credential-outlook from gitcredentials.adoc, as these helpers are specifically for email authentication rather than repository operations. The author notes these links remain available in git-send-email documentation where they are more contextually appropriate.

This completes the structural simplification started in the series, addressing the original observation that gitcredentials.adoc wasn't the right place for email-specific helpers. The change is clean and focused, removing exactly the targeted section while preserving all other credential helper documentation. With both patches in this series, the email provider documentation is now better organized without losing any technical information.
