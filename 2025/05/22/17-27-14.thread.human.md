This small but important change maintains the series' focus on robust resource handling.

The architectural shift to curl's native OAuth2.0 support has produced a more maintainable solution that complies with RFC standards while accommodating major providers like Gmail and Outlook. With this last memory leak addressed, the series appears ready for merging, having addressed both technical debt and modern authentication requirements in the IMAP subsystem.
