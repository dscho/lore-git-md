Follow-up discussion on the IMAP recipient helper proposal. Aditya Garg responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about the proposed `git imap-get-recipients` tool, acknowledging the valid points raised. The exchange clarifies that the tool would query a user-specified IMAP mailbox to find recipients by message ID, but Hamano's earlier critique remains unaddressed regarding the fundamental approach of simply copying previous recipients rather than intelligently incorporating thread participants.

Garg appears to shelve the idea after this feedback, stating "It's best to keep it to myself." The discussion highlights Git's general principle that theoretical proposals carry less weight than working implementations, and that contributors should focus on creating demonstrably valuable tools rather than seeking pre-approval for concepts. No code has been submitted, and the thread seems to be concluding with the proposal effectively withdrawn in light of the maintainer's concerns about both technical approach and philosophical fit with Git's contribution model.
