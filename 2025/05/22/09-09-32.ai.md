The key changes in v2 include:
- More precise documentation clarifying that the flag bypasses validation of object existence in the transitive closure of reachable objects (not just general connectivity)

Karthik's approval suggests the series is technically sound and ready for final consideration, though the thread summary notes that security review from connectivity experts (likely Jeff King) may still be pending.
