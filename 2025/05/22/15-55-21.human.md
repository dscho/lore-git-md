This is the second patch in a 4-part bugfix series addressing integer overflow issues in Git's multi-pack-index repack functionality. The patch specifically targets a potential 64-bit overflow scenario that could occur when calculating expected pack sizes during repacking of very large repositories.

Phillip Wood explains that the current calculation of `pack_size * referenced_objects` could overflow on 64-bit systems when dealing with extremely large packs (theoretical case: 256 million objects averaging 1KB each). The solution cleverly reorders operations and uses bit-shifting arithmetic to maintain precision while avoiding overflow, with a shifted version of the object count being divided by total objects before multiplication. The approach maintains about 4 significant digits of accuracy while being safe for pack sizes up to 1 petabyte.

The patch builds on the first patch's introduction of safe arithmetic utilities (u64_mult and u64_add) and has already received positive feedback from Taylor Blau in the v1 discussion. The implementation appears well-considered, with clear comments explaining the mathematical approach and its safety boundaries. This is part of a thorough series that also includes 32-bit fixes and improved documentation, showing careful attention to edge cases across different architectures.
