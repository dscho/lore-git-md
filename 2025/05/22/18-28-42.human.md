Bugfix follow-up in the `imap-send` series, addressing memory leaks in the CRAM-MD5 authentication path. Aditya Garg and Eric Sunshine discuss two approaches to fixing a leak where the 'response' buffer from `cram()` wasn't being freed on socket write failure. Sunshine suggests the simpler approach of unconditionally freeing the buffer immediately after use rather than duplicating the `free()` call in the error path, which Garg agrees to incorporate into the series.

This continues the thread's focus on memory safety in the legacy IMAP code, with the discussion showing consensus on the fix approach. The patch appears straightforward and uncontroversial, though it's worth noting this is part of a larger effort to clean up `imap-send`'s memory management, with other potential leaks (like `cfg->host` and `server_fill_credential()`) still needing attention in future patches.
