This is a naming discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's object database subsystem. The specific patch being discussed renames `struct object_directory` to `struct odb_alternate` as part of preparing for pluggable ODB backends. Justin Tobler raises concerns about the "alternate" terminology, noting it might confuse readers since the structure applies to both primary and alternate object sources, not just alternates.
