Key contributors include Leon Michalak as the primary implementer, with reviews from Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano. There are two remaining open questions from Junio about design choices: whether specialized option macros are justified, and how to best handle validation logic across commands. Phillip has responded with technical justifications for the current approach while suggesting callback-based validation as a potential improvement. The series appears functionally complete pending resolution of these final design considerations.

The changes affect how these interactive commands display diffs, allowing users to control context lines through either configuration or command-line options (-U/--unified and --inter-hunk-context), with proper validation to reject invalid values. Test coverage has been modernized and expanded throughout the series.
