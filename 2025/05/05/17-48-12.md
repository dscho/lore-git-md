We’re excited to announce **IEEE Summer of Code 2025**, a flagship open-source initiative organized by the IEEE Student Branch at **BMS Institute of Technology & Management (BMSIT)**, Bengaluru. This program brings together students and mentors to contribute to impactful open-source projects.

📌 **Event Details:**
```
• Duration: May – June 2025  
• Format: Hybrid (Online + In-Person)  
• Venue: BMSIT, Bengaluru  
• Objective: Encourage real-world open-source contributions under mentorship
```


We’d love to include your repository in our contributor project pool. Selected students will submit pull requests under the tag **#IEEESOC25**, and we kindly request that each PR be reviewed and acknowledged (via merge, feedback, or rejection) within **36 hours**, to ensure a smooth learning experience.

Additionally, if you're open to it, we welcome optional support through:
```
• Sponsorship  
• Social media promotion  
• Internship offers  
• Brand goodies for contributors
```


Learn more at: **www.ieeesoc.xyz**

We’d be delighted to collaborate and are happy to share more details if needed.
