This is a follow-up discussion on the final patch in the Scalar maintenance configuration series. Junio Hamano raises an important design question about the behavior of the new `--no-maintenance` flag when used with `scalar reconfigure`.

The patch adds maintenance control to `scalar reconfigure` to match the existing functionality in `register` and `clone`, but Hamano points out an asymmetry in the semantics. While `--no-maintenance` means "skip enabling maintenance" during initial setup (register/clone), it would be more intuitive for `reconfigure` if it meant "explicitly disable maintenance" on existing repositories. He suggests a three-state approach (enable/disable/leave-unchanged) might be more appropriate for the reconfigure case.

The discussion highlights a subtle but important distinction in configuration workflows - initial setup versus ongoing management. The current implementation is technically complete and consistent with the earlier patches, but Hamano's comment suggests there may be room for refinement in how maintenance states are managed during reconfiguration operations. This doesn't block the current series but may inform future design decisions.
