Platform compatibility follow-up with style nitpicks for BSD errno handling in `open_nofollow()`.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Collin Funk's patch addressing BSD-specific errno values (EFTYPE on NetBSD, EMLINK on FreeBSD) when opening files with O_NOFOLLOW.
