This is the final patch in a test modernization series converting reftable unit tests to the Clar framework. The email is a follow-up to Patrick Steinhardt's review of the v2 series, specifically addressing his feedback about helper function organization.

The patch moves the remaining test helper functions from unit-test.{c,h} to lib-reftable.{c,h} and renames them with a `cl_reftable_*` prefix, completing the three-phase migration. Patrick had noted this would be cleaner than the original approach of temporarily placing helpers in unit-test.{c,h}.

In this reply, Seyi Chamber seeks clarification on Patrick's suggestion about introducing both original and Clar-based variants of the helper functions from the start. The exchange highlights ongoing discussion about the optimal migration strategy, though the technical work of the conversion itself appears complete.

The patch represents the culmination of a well-executed test modernization effort, with only minor build system cleanup opportunities remaining (as noted in the thread summary). The maintainer will need to decide whether those warrant another revision of the series.
