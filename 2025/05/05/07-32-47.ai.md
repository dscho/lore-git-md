This is a follow-up in the feature patch series implementing the final standalone maintenance tasks to replace `git gc`, specifically addressing the rerere garbage collection auto-condition design.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's critique of the entry-count heuristic for rerere GC by proposing a simplified approach: the task will run whenever the rr-cache directory exists and contains at least one entry, while maintaining the integer `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` configuration for consistency with other maintenance tasks. This preserves the series' pattern of configurable auto-execution thresholds while addressing the concern that counting entries provides little practical benefit over a simple boolean check.

The key technical points are:
- Keeps integer-based `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` config for uniformity
- Simplifies condition to check only for existence of rr-cache directory and at least one entry
- Maintains process spawning compatibility
- Preserves error handling through existing `die(FAILED_RUN, "rerere")` mechanism

This appears to be the final design iteration before merging, resolving the last substantive discussion point in the series. The change represents a pragmatic compromise between Junio's preference for boolean simplicity and Patrick's desire to maintain config consistency across maintenance tasks, while eliminating the more complex entry-counting heuristic that was questioned. The series remains technically complete and approved for merging aside from this final adjustment.
