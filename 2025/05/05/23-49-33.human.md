Junio Hamano provides additional technical review of the first patch in the `git send-email` series, which tightens domain name validation. He questions whether the patch's approach of falling back to "localhost.localdomain" is truly solving the root problem (misconfigured hostnames) rather than just working around it. 

The review raises important concerns about potential regressions - the stricter validation might force legitimate users onto the "localhost.localdomain" fallback, which some SMTP servers may reject. While acknowledging the current implementation likely works for most cases, Junio suggests the commit message should more accurately frame this as a workaround rather than a complete solution. He also provides specific suggestions for improving the debugging information in the commit message to better illustrate the SMTP exchange before and after the fix.

This feedback indicates the patch may need minor revisions to its framing and documentation before final acceptance, though the technical implementation appears sound. The discussion remains focused on edge cases and messaging rather than fundamental flaws in the approach.
