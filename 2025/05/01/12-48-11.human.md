Bugfix follow-up regarding `git apply --intent-to-add` behavior. This email from Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Jason Cho's inquiry about the status of a 2021 bugfix patch for `git apply --intent-to-add`. The original issue caused the command to mark tracked files as deleted in the index, and Johannes Altmanninger had proposed a v3 patch series to fix it by making `--intent-to-add` imply `--index`.

Haugsbakk reports that after searching, they cannot find any commits by Altmanninger addressing this issue, nor any commits with the expected subject line. This suggests the patch may not have been merged, though the email doesn't rule out the possibility that the fix was implemented under another commit message.

The response leaves the thread's core question unanswered - whether the fix exists in any form in released Git versions. This appears to be a case where a seemingly straightforward bugfix with a clear technical solution may have fallen through the cracks in the review process. The thread now awaits either confirmation that the patch was overlooked, or perhaps discovery that it was merged under a different commit message.
