Bugfix addressing a heap-use-after-free in `git rebase --rebase-merges` reflog handling. Phillip Wood confirms Jeff King's diagnosis that the issue stems from `ctx->reflog_message` pointing to freed memory when a strbuf is reallocated during the sequencer's reset path. 

The problem occurs specifically in complex rebase operations involving merges, where `do_reset()` may reallocate the strbuf containing the reflog message while `do_merge()` still holds a pointer to the old memory location. This explains the intermittent nature of the corruption Kristoffer Haugsbakk observed, where reflog entries would contain garbage data instead of proper action messages.

Phillip notes that Jeff's proposed solution - changing the `reflog_message` field to track ownership properly - correctly addresses the root cause.
