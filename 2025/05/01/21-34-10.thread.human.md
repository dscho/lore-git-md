This thread documents Junio C Hamano's ongoing effort to standardize Git's man page documentation, building on Jean-Noël Avila's earlier synopsis-style conversion work. The series focuses on three key improvements: consistent backquoting of option names, splitting negatable options (like --[no-]opt) into separate entries, and cleaning up non-option text in OPTIONS sections.

Through discussion between Junio and Eric Sunshine, the thread has established clear formatting principles. These include backquoting commands, options, and literal paths while allowing some stylistic flexibility in examples. The series has already updated documentation for verify-commit/tag/pack, var/write-tree, and git-daemon, with particular attention to systematic backquoting and OPTIONS section cleanup.

Avila recently submitted additional refinements to catch remaining inconsistencies in four key files, converting single quotes to backquotes and standardizing variable formatting. In his latest email, he clarifies that these changes were intended as review follow-ups rather than a formal patch submission, leaving the integration approach (folding into existing patches vs. new submission) to Junio's discretion. This exchange represents the final coordination phase before merging, demonstrating Git's documentation polish process where maintainers and contributors collaborate on last-minute refinements. The entire series remains purely presentational, with no behavior changes to Git itself.
