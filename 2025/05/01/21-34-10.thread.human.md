Junio C Hamano has completed his documentation standardization series with a final patch updating the `git-daemon` man page. This wraps up a focused effort to bring consistency to Git's documentation by applying the synopsis-style formatting established by Jean-Noël Avila's earlier work. The series established clear principles through discussions with Eric Sunshine, particularly around when to use backquoting (for commands, options, and literal paths users must type) versus allowing flexibility in examples.

The `git-daemon` patch follows the same patterns seen throughout the series: converting the SYNOPSIS section to [synopsis] format, systematically backquoting commands and options, and splitting negatable options like `--[no-]informative-errors` into separate entries. These changes are purely presentational, maintaining all existing behavior while making the documentation more consistent and grep-friendly. With this final patch, the series appears ready for merging, having standardized documentation across several key commands including verify-commit, verify-tag, verify-pack, var, write-tree, and now git-daemon. The thread serves as a valuable reference for future documentation contributors on the project's formatting standards.
