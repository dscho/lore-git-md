The key technical discussion centers on strengthening commit parsing and validation when importing stashes from refs. The implementation uses `get_oidf()` for tree validation and will now parse commit messages to enforce the "git stash: " prefix convention.

The patch leverages existing stash validation infrastructure through `assert_stash_like()` rather than duplicating checks. Brian confirms this approach is sufficient for verifying commit structure. The changes maintain the non-destructive import behavior established earlier in the series while adding stronger safeguards against importing malformed or non-stash commits.
