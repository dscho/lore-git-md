This is a community announcement regarding Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2025 participation. Kaartic Sivaraam shares the official results of Git's GSoC contributor selection for 2025, welcoming three new contributors and their projects.

The selected contributors and their projects are: Ayush Chandekar working on refactoring to reduce global state (mentored by Christian Couder and Ghanshyam Thakkar), Lucas Seiki Oshiro developing a machine-readable repository query tool (mentored by Karthik Nayak and Patrick Steinhardt), and Meet Soni focusing on consolidating ref-related functionality into git-refs (mentored by Patrick Steinhardt and Jialuo She).

The email expresses gratitude to all applicants while acknowledging the difficult selection process, and notes that mentors will soon be reaching out to the selected contributors. The announcement includes links to the official GSoC results and Git's organization page.
