This is a discussion about the potential removal of `git-contacts` from Git's `contrib/` directory, as part of the ongoing cleanup effort. The email from D. Ben Knoble responds to earlier points made by Eric Sunshine and Todd Zullinger regarding the script's usage and alternatives.

The key new points raised are:
1. While `git-related` is a more functional alternative to `git-contacts`, Knoble notes it requires maintaining a Ruby environment and isn't packaged, making it less convenient than the Perl-based `git-contacts` that comes with Git.
2. Knoble highlights that `git-contacts` is used as a `ccCmd` by some contributors, suggesting removal should consider the replacement's ease of installation.
3. The email references historical context about `git-contacts` being mentioned in contribution documentation (`MyFirstContribution.adoc` and `SubmittingPatches`), though recent changes have noted it's not part of core Git.

This discussion adds a practical user perspective to the policy debate, emphasizing that replacement tools need to consider installation friction, even when they are technically superior. The thread continues to balance between cleaning up unmaintained code and maintaining usability for contributors who rely on these tools.
