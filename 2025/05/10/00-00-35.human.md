This is the final policy discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's 11-part series cleaning up Git's `contrib/` directory. Junio Hamano weighs in on Elijah Newren's concerns about using "no updates in 5 years" as a removal criterion, agreeing it should not be a standalone justification. 

Hamano suggests omitting the time-based criterion entirely from the official rationale, even if it was useful as an initial filter, to prevent future complaints about removing working-but-unmaintained tools. This aligns with Newren's position that removals should be based on technical merits (brokenness, alternatives) rather than just inactivity.

The exchange represents the final policy refinement before the series can be merged, with consensus that the `contrib/README` needs clear documentation of the staging-area principle and removal criteria. Hamano's input as the maintainer signals the discussion is nearing resolution, pending Steinhardt's updates to the policy documentation. The technical work itself (removing 27 unmaintained items) appears uncontroversial, with the debate focused on establishing proper precedents for future maintenance.
