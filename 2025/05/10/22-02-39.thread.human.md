Elijah Newren's patch series adding a `--quiet` option to `git merge-tree` has been approved for merging after several iterations. The feature provides hosting platforms with an efficient way to check mergeability without generating unnecessary objects or output, built on merge-ort plumbing with a new `mergeability_only` flag. The interface evolved through naming feedback from Junio Hamano, settling on `--quiet` after considering `--mergeability-only` and `--dry-run`. Phillip Wood gave the final technical review, confirming the implementation meets all requirements while maintaining clear restrictions (like incompatibility with `--stdin` mode).

With Junio's merge confirmation, this work is now complete and will appear in an upcoming release. A separate discussion initiated by Kristoffer Haugsbakk explores potential batch processing enhancements, but that remains future work. The merged solution delivers the core optimization - conflict detection without side effects - that platforms need for efficient merge pre-checks.
