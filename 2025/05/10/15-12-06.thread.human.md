Gabriel Scherer has proposed addressing an issue where symlinks in sparse-checkout configurations become dangling when their targets lie outside the checked-out area. This problem particularly affects large repositories like typst/packages that use symlinks for file deduplication, causing CI workflows to fail.

The proposal outlines two potential solutions: (1) copying the target file's content when needed, which could lead to duplication, or (2) automatically expanding the sparse-checkout to include symlink targets, which would require special handling during reapplication. The specific case involves a scenario where b/file symlinks to a/file while only b/ is in the sparse-checkout.

This thoughtful proposal presents a clear real-world problem and offers alternative technical approaches for discussion. The community will need to weigh the trade-offs between maintaining repository invariants and solving the immediate issue, particularly regarding sparse-checkout semantics. The discussion will likely focus on which solution better balances correctness with practical usability.
