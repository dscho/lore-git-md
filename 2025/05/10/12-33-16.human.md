This is part of Jean-Noël Avila and Junio C Hamano's ongoing documentation standardization series, specifically focusing on the `git-verify-*` family of commands. The patch updates the documentation for `git-verify-commit`, `git-verify-tag`, and `git-verify-pack` to align with newer formatting conventions established in earlier work.

The changes are purely presentational, applying consistent markup rules: converting SYNOPSIS sections from [verse] to [synopsis] format, properly backquoting options like `--verbose` and `--raw`, and removing non-option argument descriptions from OPTIONS sections. The patch also updates terminology, replacing "SHA-1" with "object-name" per the Git glossary.

This is the third in a four-part series that has systematically standardized documentation across Git's command set, from simpler manuals like `git-var` to more complex ones like `git-daemon`. The changes are well-motivated and follow established patterns from commit 22293895. The patch maintains the high quality seen throughout this standardization effort, with clean diffs and no behavioral changes.
