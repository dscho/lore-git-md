This is a discussion about the potential removal of `git-contacts` from Git's `contrib/` directory, as part of the ongoing cleanup effort. D. Ben Knoble responds to earlier points about the script's usage and alternatives.

The email notes that while `git-related` is a more functional alternative to `git-contacts`, it requires maintaining a Ruby environment and isn't as easily installed as the Perl-based `git-contacts`. Knoble points out that some users rely on `git-contacts` as their `ccCmd` configuration, suggesting that removal should consider the replacement's ease of installation.

The discussion reveals that `git-contacts` is mentioned in Git's contribution documentation but appears to have little real-world usage beyond that. Eric Sunshine had previously ported `git-related` from Ruby to Perl for inclusion in Git, though the Ruby version now has more features. The thread also references an 11-year-old patch series that proposed removing `git-contacts` but was never merged.

This adds a practical consideration to the policy discussion - that replacements for removed `contrib/` scripts should be at least as easy to install and use as what they replace. The email doesn't take a strong position on removal but highlights an important user experience factor to weigh.
