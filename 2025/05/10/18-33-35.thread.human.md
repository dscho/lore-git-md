This thread proposes changing `git stash apply` to default to restoring the index (like `--index`) when breaking changes are enabled, fixing what the author sees as a historical "footgun" where staged changes are lost. The 9-patch series includes test updates showing unexpected benefits for submodule handling.

However, Junio Hamano has raised significant concerns based on his own workflow experience. He tried using `--index` by default via aliases but found it often caused problems when index restoration wasn't needed. He also notes the asymmetry in typing effort would penalize users who don't need the index restored. While not rejecting the proposal outright, his feedback suggests the current behavior may be more ergonomic for many common cases.

The discussion now balances the technical merits (including submodule improvements) against these real-world workflow considerations. Key open questions include whether this change warrants being a breaking change, its impact on existing workflows, and whether the benefits outweigh the potential ergonomic costs Hamano highlights.
