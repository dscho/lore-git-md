This is the final policy discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's `contrib/` cleanup series, with Junio Hamano weighing in on removal criteria. The thread has progressed from technical execution to establishing formal maintenance policies for the `contrib/` directory.

Junio agrees with Elijah Newren's earlier concern about using "no updates in 5 years" as a standalone removal rationale, suggesting it should not even be mentioned in the commit messages. The discussion now centers on finalizing the policy documentation in `contrib/README`, which needs to codify:
1. The staging-area purpose of `contrib/`
2. Clear graduation/removal criteria prioritizing technical factors over temporal ones
3. Guidelines for commit messages when removing components

Junio's position aligns with Newren's earlier analysis that patches 4 (`git-remote-bzr`) and 11 (`stats/`) may need revision to better justify their removals without relying on the time-based rationale.
