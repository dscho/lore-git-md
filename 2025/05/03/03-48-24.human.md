Platform compatibility follow-up regarding FreeBSD's errno behavior with O_NOFOLLOW.

Collin Funk confirms brian m. carlson's suspicion that FreeBSD exhibits similar non-POSIX errno behavior as NetBSD, returning EMLINK instead of the standard ELOOP when O_NOFOLLOW encounters a symlink. This matches the test failures seen in t0602-reffiles-fsck.sh, though the FreeBSD case wasn't covered by the initial NetBSD fix.

The email indicates Collin will prepare a separate patch to handle FreeBSD's EMLINK case, similar to the EFTYPE translation already implemented for NetBSD. Both developers express frustration with BSD variants not following POSIX errno specifications, even when _POSIX_SOURCE is defined, though they acknowledge this as a systemic platform issue.

This is a straightforward technical follow-up to the approved NetBSD fix, confirming the need for parallel handling of FreeBSD's errno quirk. The exchange suggests this will be a similar low-risk change, though Collin plans to research FreeBSD's rationale before submitting the patch.
