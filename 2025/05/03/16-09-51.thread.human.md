Junio Hamano has now weighed in on a core design question - how to handle group memberships when remotes are renamed. He argues against automatic updates to group definitions during renames, since Git can't distinguish between intentional functional changes and simple typo fixes. Instead, he suggests implementing explicit group management commands (`--list`, `--get`, `--set`, `--rename`) that would give users precise control over group memberships.
