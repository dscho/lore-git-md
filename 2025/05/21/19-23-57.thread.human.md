Junio Hamano has raised significant concerns about Aditya Garg's proposal for a `git imap-get-recipients` helper script to automate recipient selection in patch revisions. The core issue is philosophical: simply copying To/Cc lists from previous messages may lead to suboptimal recipient selection, as v2 emails should consider both original recipients and commenters from the discussion. 

Hamano points out several technical gaps in the proposal, including unclear details about IMAP data sources (personal mailbox vs public archive), authentication methods, and whether the tool would analyze just the original message or also its replies. These missing details make it difficult to evaluate the tool's usefulness. 

More broadly, Hamano reminds contributors that Git's development philosophy favors demonstrating value through implementation rather than seeking pre-commitment through discussion. The proposal now awaits a more concrete implementation from Aditya that addresses these concerns, particularly around smarter recipient selection and the technical specifics of IMAP integration. Until then, the idea remains in discussion rather than moving toward implementation.
