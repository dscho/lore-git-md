He conceded Hamano's point about v2 patches needing both original recipients and commenters with a simple "Fair", and appeared to back away from the proposal after absorbing feedback about architectural fit and Git's contribution philosophy. The email suggests potential abandonment of the effort rather than pursuing revisions.

Key technical points now include: IMAP-based message lookup approach clarified, but authentication and mailbox selection remain unspecified. Core objections stand regarding Perl dependency avoidance, contrib/ directory policy, and whether the tool encourages suboptimal practices by replicating recipients without context.
