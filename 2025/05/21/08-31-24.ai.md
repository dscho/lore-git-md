Performance optimization for `git repack` handling of promisor packs with kept packs. This is a follow-up to Tomáš Trnka's original report and Han Young's initial implementation, now being reviewed by Christian Couder at Junio's request.

The patch builds on the previous work that introduced `SKIP_IN_CORE_KEPT_PACKS` and `SKIP_ON_DISK_KEPT_PACKS` flags to avoid unnecessary object enumeration from kept packs during promisor repacking. Han notes this email isn't properly threaded as a reply to the original discussion, pointing to the correct message ID for context.

Key technical details remain unchanged from the thread summary: the optimization skips enumeration of objects from both `--keep-pack` specified packs and `.keep` file protected packs during promisor repacking. The performance impact is substantial, with test cases showing ~3.6x speed improvement and ~2.8x reduction in output pack size. The implementation remains focused in `builtin/repack.c` with minimal changes (14 lines added, 4 modified).

This appears ready for final review, with the main outstanding question being Christian Couder's evaluation of the approach. The patch maintains the original optimization strategy while fixing the email threading issue, suggesting it's likely be the final version before potential inclusion.
