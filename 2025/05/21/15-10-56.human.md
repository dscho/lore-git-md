This is a brief thread reply about patch submission conventions, specifically the ordering of commit trailers (Signed-off-by and Helped-by lines). 

Junio C Hamano confirms Patrick Steinhardt's observation that the trailers in D. Ben Knoble's patch were in the wrong order. He explains the standard convention: helper credits (Helped-by) should appear before the final sign-off (Signed-off-by) from the patch author, as this reflects the chronological sequence of contributions.
