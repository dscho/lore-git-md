This is the final approval for a performance optimization series addressing redundant syscall overhead in multi-pack-index handling. Junio Hamano has queued Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series after reviewing the changes from v1.

The series now consists of two well-focused patches: one documenting the critical ordering requirements for packfile auxiliary files (explaining why an earlier optimization attempt was unsafe), and another implementing a negative lookup cache in midx to avoid repeated syscalls for missing packfiles. Performance testing showed dramatic improvements - a 4x speedup for some operations and a 99.6% reduction in syscalls.

The thread has reached clear consensus, with all participants agreeing the current solution provides meaningful performance benefits while maintaining Git's packfile integrity guarantees. The patches are now headed for inclusion in the main Git tree, marking a successful conclusion to this optimization effort.
