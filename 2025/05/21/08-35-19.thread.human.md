This doesn't affect functionality but could be clearer in the user experience.

The series remains at version 1 for both patches, with the core improvements being:
1) Better error reporting for lock failures through the `LOCK_REPORT_ON_ERROR` flag
2) Standardized error messages ("could not write index") across `git am`, `git commit`, `git merge` and merge-ort internals

The changes are focused on improving diagnostic clarity without changing core functionality. Han Young's latest observation points to one remaining area where the error reporting flow could be made even more intuitive for users.
