This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing thread about Git's exec-path security and compatibility issues, specifically addressing a minor point about commit message trailers.

Patrick Steinhardt clarifies the proper ordering of Signed-off-by (SOB) and Helped-by trailers in commit messages, noting that SOB should always come last. He explains that this convention is why `git rebase --signoff` may re-add the SOB trailer even when it's already present - the tool assumes the SOB belongs at the end.
