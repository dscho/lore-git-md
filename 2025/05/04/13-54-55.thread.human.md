This thread discusses a three-patch series by Aditya Garg improving `git send-email` functionality, with a focus on FQDN validation and OAuth documentation. The first patch enforces RFC1035-compliant domain name validation in `git-send-email.perl`, fixing edge cases that previously caused SMTP failures while maintaining special handling for macOS `.local` domains. Kristoffer Haugsbakk initially suggested a minor formatting tweak to separate footnotes from trailers, but has since indicated this may not warrant a full v2 resubmission unless other reviewers request changes. The second patch adds detailed OAuth2.0 configuration examples and workflow guidance to the documentation, while the third patch references new credential helpers for Gmail and Outlook authentication. The technical changes appear uncontested, with only the question of whether to submit a v2 for the formatting tweak remaining open. The series is otherwise ready for review, with no outstanding objections to its substantive improvements to email sending configuration and validation.
