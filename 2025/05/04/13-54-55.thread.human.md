This thread has finalized a series of improvements to `git send-email`, focusing on two key areas: RFC1035-compliant domain validation and OAuth2.0 authentication documentation. The series reached its sixth and final version (v6) after thorough review and refinement.

The technical implementation was approved in v5, with v6 making final documentation adjustments - most notably standardizing the use of backticks for all user-visible elements as definitively clarified by maintainer Junio Hamano. The series consists of three well-tested patches that address real-world SMTP issues while maintaining backward compatibility.

Key improvements include:
- Strict domain validation that properly handles edge cases and includes macOS-specific behavior
- Restructured OAuth2.0 documentation with clear provider-specific subsections
- References to community-maintained credential helpers for Gmail and Outlook
- Concrete examples demonstrating the documented workflows

The author, Aditya Garg, has demonstrated the solution's effectiveness by using the documented `git-credential-outlook` helper to send the final patches. With Junio's approval and all technical and documentation concerns addressed, this series is now ready for merging, representing a solid improvement to Git's email submission capabilities.
