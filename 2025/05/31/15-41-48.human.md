This is a community announcement about the upcoming Git Rev News edition. Christian Couder shares that draft #123 is now available for review and contributions from the Git community. The draft is hosted on GitHub where anyone can submit edits via PR or comment on the associated issue (#775). The team plans to publish the final version on June 2nd, 2025.

The email serves as both an invitation for community input (proofreading, content suggestions, volunteer interviews) and a heads-up to those featured in this edition who may have been accidentally omitted from the CC list. This follows the project's typical collaborative approach to newsletter production, with multiple maintainers (Jakub, Markus, Kaartic) involved in the final publication process.
