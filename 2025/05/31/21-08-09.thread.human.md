This thread documents a straightforward fix to the `git-column` manpage's EXAMPLES section, correcting formatting issues where list continuation markers (`+`) were incorrectly used at top level when blank lines were added around block delimiters. The error originated in commit 227c4f33a03 and caused literal `+` characters to appear in rendered output.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk submitted the initial patch, which removed these markers from three example blocks while preserving the intended blank line spacing. In v2, he made minor tweaks: adjusting the subject line from "before" to "around" to better match the commit message phrasing, and adding Jean-Noël Avila's ack. Jean-Noël, who leads the broader documentation synopsis-style conversion effort, reviewed and approved the change.

The patch is purely presentational and uncontroversial, with no functional impact. As part of the ongoing manpage standardization work, it appears ready for merging without further discussion. The documentation maintainer's ack signals this will likely proceed smoothly.
