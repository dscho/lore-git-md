This is a test infrastructure patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to improve TAP compliance in Git's test suite. The patch addresses how shell trace output (from flags like `-x` or `--verbose`) is handled to avoid breaking TAP parser compatibility.

The core issue is that test output was being split between stdout and stderr in a way that corrupted TAP output format when parsed. The solution redirects all output to stderr, sacrificing the ability to distinguish stdout from stderr but ensuring clean TAP output. Karthik Nayak reviews the change, noting it looks technically sound while musing about potential future improvements to preserve the stdout/stderr distinction.

The change appears straightforward and well-motivated, continuing the series' focus on making test output reliably parseable. Karthik's review suggests the tradeoff is reasonable given current needs, though he hints at possible enhancements if the distinction becomes important later. This is a small but meaningful piece of the larger TAP compliance effort that has been under active discussion and review.
