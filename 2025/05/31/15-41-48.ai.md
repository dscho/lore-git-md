This is a community announcement about the Git Rev News publication process. Christian Couder is sharing a draft of edition 123 of the Git Rev News newsletter and inviting community participation before its scheduled June 2nd publication. The draft is available on GitHub where contributors can submit edits via PRs or comment on issue #775. The message emphasizes that all forms of contribution are welcome - from proofreading to content suggestions to volunteering for interviews. The email serves both as a notification that the draft is ready for review and as a call for last-minute community input before publication. The newsletter appears to be a collaborative effort involving Christian Couder, Jakub, Markus, and Kaartic.
