Platform compatibility follow-up for BSD CPU detection. Brad Smith pings the thread about his earlier patch to fix CPU count reporting on OpenBSD/NetBSD systems when SMT is disabled. The patch modifies `online_cpus()` to properly use `HW_NCPUONLINE` where available, falling back to existing methods, ensuring accurate reporting of active CPUs rather than physical cores.

The change has already been reviewed and approved by Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed it aligns with OpenBSD documentation. This is a straightforward, low-risk improvement that's ready for inclusion. The ping suggests the patch may have been overlooked in the mailing list traffic and is a gentle nudge for maintainers to pick it up.
