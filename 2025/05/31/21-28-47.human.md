This is a bugfix patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing test infrastructure series, specifically addressing an unexpectedly passing test in the TAP output parsing work. The patch fixes test t7815 which was incorrectly passing on macOS due to platform-specific regex behavior matching NUL bytes, similar to the known Cygwin case.

The test was meant to fail everywhere except Cygwin, where '.' in regex matches NUL bytes. The patch reveals this same behavior has been occurring on macOS, likely due to shared BSD lineage in their regex implementations. While the ideal solution would be a configure-time check for consistent regex behavior across platforms, this patch takes the pragmatic approach of simply excluding Darwin (macOS) from the test alongside Cygwin, keeping the series focused on TAP output standardization.

This continues the series' pattern of addressing platform-specific test quirks uncovered during the Meson/TAP integration work. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, though it highlights a deeper platform compatibility issue that may warrant future attention.
