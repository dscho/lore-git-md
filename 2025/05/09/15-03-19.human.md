This is a follow-up discussion in the completed series optimizing memory usage in the packed-refs backend. The email addresses a specific issue in patch 3/4 where the author (shejialuo) acknowledges a mistake in changing the mmap strategy condition check during refactoring.

The patch had originally modified the condition from checking `mmap_strategy != MMAP_OK` to checking specifically for `MMAP_TEMPORARY`, which Jeff King and Junio Hamano noted could be problematic. The author now agrees this was incorrect and plans to revert the change in the next version, maintaining the original more defensive check.

This is a minor correction to an already-acked series that's otherwise ready for integration. The discussion serves as a good example of careful code review catching subtle logic changes during refactoring, even in patches that are otherwise straightforward extractions of functionality. The author's quick acknowledgment and planned fix shows the series remains on track.
