Maintainer response to the sequencer reflog message fix. Junio Hamano reviews and approves Phillip Wood's patch that addresses a use-after-free vulnerability in rebase operations by restructuring reflog message handling. The key technical change moves reflog message generation closer to commit creation and passes it as a parameter rather than storing it in `struct replay_ctx`, eliminating the risk of stale buffer references.

Hamano notes the logical grouping of `reflog_action` with `replay_opts` parameters in the modified functions (`run_git_commit`, `try_to_commit`, `do_commit`), despite no longer being part of the opts struct. The review confirms the solution's soundness and indicates the patch will be queued for inclusion, marking the conclusion of this bugfix thread. This follows the earlier preparatory refactoring patch and maintains the series' clean separation of concerns while fixing the memory safety issue.
