This is a cover letter for Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, now in its second version. The series removes 11 unmaintained or obsolete components from `contrib/`, building on earlier discussions about establishing clearer policies for the directory's contents.

The patch series represents the technical execution of a now-consensus-backed plan to transition `contrib/` from a "batteries included" approach to a more focused maintenance model. Each removal falls into one of three categories: clearly broken tools, unmaintained components (no updates in 5+ years), or those with clear alternatives in core Git. Notable removals include the MediaWiki helper (now a separate project), obsolete remote helpers, and scripts like `git-resurrect.sh` that have been superseded by core functionality (`git reflog`).

The v2 iteration adds removal of `contrib/stats` and provides more detailed justifications for several components, including StackOverflow usage data showing `git-resurrect.sh` was rarely used compared to native alternatives. The cover letter also previews next steps: formalizing a `contrib/` policy document (building on Junio Hamano's suggested model) and potentially reorganizing development tools into a new `tools/` directory.

This represents a well-considered, incremental step in Git's ongoing maintenance work, with each removal carefully justified and the series building on established patterns from earlier component removals. The thorough explanations and clear categorization make it easy to evaluate each proposed deletion.
