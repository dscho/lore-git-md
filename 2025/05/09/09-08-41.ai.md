This is a follow-up in the incremental Git backup discussion, specifically addressing filesystem-level deduplication approaches. The tone suggests this is more of a wry observation than a serious technical counter-proposal, given the thread's prior consensus that pack-based solutions (either bundle or state-tracking) are superior for space efficiency and timestamp robustness.

The email doesn't introduce new technical data or arguments, but maintains the position that filesystem deduplication could serve as a fallback option despite its storage inefficiencies. This keeps the filesystem approach nominally on the table, though the thread's technical progression has clearly favored pack-based solutions.
