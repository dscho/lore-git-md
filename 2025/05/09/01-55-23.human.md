Maintainer feedback on stash export/export internals. This is a response to a patch in the v5 series that modifies OID lookup behavior in `object-name.c` to support the new stash export/import functionality. The patch adds a `GET_OID_GENTLY` flag to handle reflog history lookups more gracefully during stash operations. Junio Hamano notes the change makes error handling more consistent within the function, though he speculates that future work might simplify this further by removing the special case entirely. The feedback indicates this technical detail is acceptable as-is for the current series, though it hints at potential future refactoring opportunities. No changes are needed to proceed with the stash export/import feature.
