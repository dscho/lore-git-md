This is a contributor housekeeping discussion about the final policy wording for the `contrib/` directory cleanup series. Elijah Newren raises a thoughtful concern about using "not updated in 5 years" as a standalone removal criterion in the cover letter and some patches. He suggests this rationale should either be paired with the policy that `contrib/` is meant as a staging area (to be documented in a follow-up), or deferred until that policy is established. 

Newren provides a detailed breakdown showing most removals actually rely on stronger justifications like broken functionality or better alternatives, with only patches 4 and part of 11 primarily using the inactivity metric. He approves the series overall but wants to prevent the "5 years" metric from being misinterpreted as a general Git maintenance rule. The discussion reflects careful attention to how policy precedents are set through technical work.
