This is a feature request/bug report regarding `git log` filtering and display issues when trying to exclude unwanted commits (particularly from bot authors) while maintaining a useful history view.

The author describes several pain points when trying to filter out bot-generated commits from repository history. First, they note the lack of a user-friendly `--exclude-author` option, forcing them to use a complex Perl regex workaround. More significantly, they identify display problems when using `--graph` with filtered output - the graph's history simplification doesn't properly handle excluded commits, breaking the visual history flow.

The author also reports that filtering by subdirectory (as an alternative approach) causes missing branch/tag decorations since the filtered output omits the commits those refs point to. They suggest parent rewriting could be applied here to show "fake" decorations (with appropriate visual indication) to maintain context. These issues particularly impact `--all` views and also affect gitk.

The report highlights real usability problems in Git's filtering capabilities, particularly around maintaining visual history context when excluding unwanted commits. The issues appear well-motivated by a concrete use case (filtering bot spam from a low-traffic repo) and touch on multiple related display aspects that could benefit from coordinated improvements.
