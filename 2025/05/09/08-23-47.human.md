This is a final approval notice for a refactoring patch in the reftable subsystem. The patch removes an unused macro (REFTABLE_REALLOC_ARRAY) that was identified as potentially unsafe due to a theoretical memory leak. After three iterations of review and refinement, the reftable maintainer Patrick Steinhardt gives his final approval to the version submitted by Lidong Yan.

The thread has reached its natural conclusion with all feedback addressed, including stylistic concerns about preserving the formatting of the remaining REFTABLE_CALLOC_ARRAY macro. The change is purely a code cleanup with no behavioral impact since the macro wasn't being used. This represents a straightforward improvement to the reftable codebase's hygiene.
