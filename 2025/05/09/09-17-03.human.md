This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, specifically targeting obsolete remote helper stubs for Mercurial and Bazaar. The patch removes three files that have served as placeholder notices since 2014, when the actual helper implementations were moved to external repositories.

The stubs being removed simply printed warnings directing users to download the real implementations from GitHub, where they've been maintained independently for over a decade. The patch follows the established pattern for removing clearly obsolete components that have external alternatives and haven't been touched in years.

This is patch 3/11 in the v2 series, continuing the systematic cleanup that has already seen maintainer approval for several other removals. The justification here is straightforward - these stubs serve no practical purpose after 10+ years and their removal reduces maintenance burden. The actual functionality remains available externally for those who need it.
