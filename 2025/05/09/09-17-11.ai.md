This is the final patch (11/11) in Patrick Steinhardt's series cleaning up Git's `contrib/` directory, focusing on removing the obsolete `stats" directory and its contents. The patch completes the systematic removal of unmaintained components from contrib/ by addressing three statistical analysis scripts that have fallen into disuse.

The removed scripts include:
1. `git-common-hash` (last updated 2012) for analyzing hash prefix uniqueness - made obsolete by Git's built-in prefix length heuristics
2. `mailmap.pl` (last updated 2012) for mailmap analysis - now broken due to Perl syntax changes
3. `packinfo.pl` (last updated 2008) for packfile statistics - still functional but likely unused, with potential functionality better suited for core `git-verify-pack`

The removal follows the established pattern from previous patches in the series, demonstrating the "obsolete functionality with modern alternatives" case. Each script's obsolescence is thoroughly documented, with specific technical evidence provided (like the Perl compilation errors for mailmap.pl). The patch cleanly removes all three scripts and their supporting files (308 lines total) without requiring any test adjustments, as these were standalone utilities.

This concludes the technical removal portion of the series, having addressed twenty-seven contrib/ items across eleven patches. The work now shifts to finalizing the contrib/README policy documentation that will guide future maintenance decisions, building on the concrete examples established by these removals.
