This is a cover letter for Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's `contrib/` directory, marking the transition from individual component removals to policy formulation. The series removes 11 more components from `contrib/` while building toward formal documentation of the directory's purpose and maintenance standards.

The patch series follows three established criteria for removal: broken state, 5+ years without updates, or clear alternatives. Notable removals include `git-resurrect.sh` (obsoleted by native `git reflog` with StackOverflow data showing 3000:16 preference ratio), `mw-to-git` (author-confirmed abandonment), and `remotes2config.sh` (non-functional migration path). The v2 additions strengthen justifications with technical details about why each tool is safe to remove, like `remotes2config.sh`'s inability to handle `.git/branches/` migrations.

The cover letter outlines next steps: formalizing Junio Hamano's stated policy that `contrib/` components should "move up, move out, or disappear," potentially splitting development tools into a new `tools/` directory, and moving examples into documentation. The series serves as both technical cleanup and empirical basis for the forthcoming `contrib/README` policy document, with concrete removal patterns now covering abandoned, obsolete, and unmaintained components.
