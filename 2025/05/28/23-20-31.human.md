Performance optimization patch introducing a new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for `git pack-objects`. This is part 8/9 of Taylor Blau's series to optimize MIDX and cruft pack interactions during repacking.

The patch adds a new mode that changes how `--stdin-packs` handles objects from packs not explicitly listed in its input. In "follow" mode, objects from unlisted packs will be included if they are (1) reachable from the included packs and (2) not in any excluded packs. This is particularly useful for handling cruft packs, where objects may become reachable later, while maintaining reachability guarantees needed for bitmap generation.

The implementation adds a new `stdin_packs_mode` enum and modifies the pack hint callbacks to include objects when in follow mode. The change comes with comprehensive tests verifying the new behavior, including cases with unknown packs and loose objects. This prepares for future repacking strategies that need to maintain reachability closure while still excluding certain packs.

The patch builds on earlier refactoring in the series and has already received positive feedback from Junio Hamano. It demonstrates careful consideration of edge cases while keeping the implementation relatively small and focused.
