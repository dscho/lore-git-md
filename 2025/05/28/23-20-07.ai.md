This is a cover letter for a performance optimization patch series (v4) about MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. The series is now in its final polishing stage, having incorporated all previous feedback and showing successful real-world deployment.

The 9-part series introduces a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option (default true) to control whether cruft packs must be included in MIDX files during repacking. The key optimization allows excluding cruft packs from MIDX when reachability can be guaranteed through other means, improving performance while maintaining correctness guarantees.

- New `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for pack-objects to handle reachability properly
- Delta compression optimization for loose objects when using `--unpacked` with `--stdin-packs`
