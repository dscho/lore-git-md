This is version 5 of a two-patch bugfix series addressing threading and logging issues in `git send-email`. The series continues to refine fixes for edge cases in email message processing, with improved commit messages and documentation in this iteration.

The first patch fixes a subtle threading bug where editing and resending the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` and shallow threading enabled would break the thread structure. The issue stems from incorrect incrementing of the `$message_num` and `$message_id_serial` counters during message edits. The fix involves decrementing these counters before re-processing to maintain proper threading relationships. The commit message has been significantly improved in v5 with better explanations and fixed grammar/spelling.

The second patch improves logging when email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery. It makes the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled and ensures the logs show the actual delivered Message-IDs. This change helps users better understand what's happening when their email client modifies headers.
