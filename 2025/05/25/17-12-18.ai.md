This is the first patch in a v3 series fixing a threading bug in `git send-email`. The patch modifies the `send_message` subroutine in `git-send-email.perl` to decrement both `$message_num` and `$message_id_serial` when either skipping or editing a message. This ensures proper message sequencing is maintained through edits and skips. The implementation takes a straightforward approach of counter adjustment rather than more complex state management alternatives discussed earlier in the thread.

Key technical details: the fix handles both editing (`e` response) and skipping (`n` response) cases by decrementing counters before returning.
