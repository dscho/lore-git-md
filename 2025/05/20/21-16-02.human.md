Test cleanup patch removing obsolete leak-check variables from test scripts.

Justin Tobler reviews the patch and confirms it appears to catch all remaining instances of these variables in the test suite.

The patch looks correct and uncontroversial, with Justin giving it an implicit "looks good to me" review.
