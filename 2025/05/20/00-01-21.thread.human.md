This reflects an ongoing design discussion about how strictly to couple the no-repository flag with repository-dependent features.

The series has evolved through careful consideration of edge cases, with Jacob addressing Junio's earlier feedback about slash handling in prefix_path_gently() and expanding test coverage to 110 lines.
