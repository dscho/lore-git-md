Junio argues these are logically separate changes that should be split for clarity, while the author maintains they're intrinsically linked in the implementation. Junio suggests adding such tests to lock in the optimization behavior.

The technical approach appears sound (using `submodule_from_name()` for path safety and `wildmatch()` for activation patterns), but the patch organization remains under discussion.
