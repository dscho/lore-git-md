Bugfix addressing integer overflow in multi-pack-index repacking on 32-bit systems. This is the first patch in a 4-part series tackling overflow issues in midx repack calculations.

Phillip Wood fixes a concrete case where `git multi-pack-index --repack --batch-size=120M` fails on 32-bit systems due to a size_t overflow when estimating object sizes. The patch replaces 32-bit arithmetic with 64-bit calculations and adds new overflow-safe helper functions in git-compat-util.h. It also handles potential overflow in the total size accumulation by using saturating arithmetic (capping at SIZE_MAX rather than wrapping around).

The changes are focused on midx-write.c's pack selection logic, where size calculations could previously overflow when dealing with large packfiles. The patch appears well-constructed with clear commit message explanations and adds appropriate overflow checks. This addresses a real-world issue affecting users on resource-constrained systems like Raspberry Pi, though the impact is limited to edge cases with very large repositories.
