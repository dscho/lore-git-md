This is a refactoring patch that's part of a series adding pathspec support to `git diff --no-index`. The patch exposes previously internal pathspec matching functionality by making `match_pathspec_with_flags()` public and exporting two of its flags (`DO_MATCH_LEADING_PATHSPEC` and `DO_MATCH_DIRECTORY`) while keeping `DO_MATCH_EXCLUDE` private.

The patch itself is straightforward - it moves the function declaration to pathspec.h and removes the `static` modifier to make it public, while keeping the same implementation.

This change enables the subsequent patches in the series to use these pathspec matching capabilities for the `--no-index` directory comparison functionality.
