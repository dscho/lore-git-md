Performance optimization for packfile handling, addressing syscall overhead when MIDX references missing packs. This is version 2 of a patch series that originally proposed reordering auxiliary file checks but now takes a different approach based on thread discussion.

Patrick Steinhardt's new version drops the original optimization (which turned out to be unsafe due to potential race conditions with auxiliary file creation) and instead adds two improvements: First, it documents the critical ordering requirements for packfile integrity checks in `packfile.c`. Second, it implements a negative lookup cache in `midx.c` to avoid repeated failed lookups of missing packs, building on Jeff King's suggestion in the thread.

The changes are relatively small (19 lines added, 2 removed) and focused on pathological cases where a stale multi-pack-index references packs that no longer exist. While Patrick notes this won't affect most well-maintained repositories, the solution is clean and maintains all safety constraints that emerged during discussion. The thread shows consensus around this approach, with both Junio Hamano and Jeff King having previously agreed to the caching strategy.
