The motivation comes from the author's personal experience discovering this update mechanism through external searching rather than being surfaced by Git itself.

The proposed implementation would involve modifying help.c to conditionally include this tip when compiled for Windows. The change appears straightforward from a technical perspective, though the patch itself isn't provided in this email. However, the proposal doesn't address whether this might clutter the help output for users who prefer manual updates or use other update mechanisms.
