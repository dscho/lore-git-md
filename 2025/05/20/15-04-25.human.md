This is the second patch in a bugfix series addressing integer overflow issues in multi-pack-index repack calculations, with a focus on 64-bit systems. Phillip Wood identifies a potential overflow scenario where multiplying a large pack size by referenced objects could exceed 64-bit limits (demonstrated with a theoretical 1PB pack example).

The patch changes the arithmetic approach in `midx-write.c` to use shifted integer operations, dividing the pack size by total objects before multiplication to prevent overflow. The new calculation maintains ~4 significant digits of accuracy while handling pack sizes up to 1PB safely. The implementation uses the new `u64_mult()` and `u64_add()` helpers introduced in the first patch of the series.

This appears to be a well-considered change - the commit message clearly explains both the problem scenario and the mathematical approach taken to solve it. The patch maintains the same logical flow while making the calculation overflow-safe, suggesting it's a focused improvement rather than a risky rewrite. As part of a series already addressing 32-bit issues, this extends protection to theoretical 64-bit edge cases that could affect repositories with extremely large packfiles.
