This is a reply in the ongoing bugfix series addressing integer overflow issues in multi-pack-index repack functionality. The email discusses patch 2/4 which handles potential 64-bit overflow cases in pack size calculations.

Taylor Blau responds positively to Phillip Wood's approach in patch 2/4, which uses shifted integer arithmetic to prevent overflow when calculating total referenced object sizes. The original patch had proposed dividing the pack size by total objects before multiplying by referenced objects, with a 14-bit shift to maintain accuracy while avoiding overflow for pack sizes under 1PB.

Key technical details from the discussion:
- The specific calculation at risk is `p->pack_size * pack_info[i].referenced_objects`
- The solution involves reordering operations and using bit-shifting
- Taylor confirms the practical relevance, having seen packs with ~2^28 objects
- The 14-bit shift preserves reasonable accuracy while preventing overflow

The reply indicates consensus forming around this technical approach, with Taylor explicitly endorsing the solution after initially having some reservations about related aspects in patch 1/4. This moves the thread closer to resolution on the arithmetic handling strategy for both 32-bit and 64-bit overflow cases.
