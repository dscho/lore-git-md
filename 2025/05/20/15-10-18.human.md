Final refinements for GitLab-specific receive-pack optimization

This is the second patch in a finalized series adding a `--skip-connectivity-check` option to `git receive-pack`, specifically for GitLab's Gitaly service. The patch implements the actual feature after the test infrastructure was established in patch 1.
