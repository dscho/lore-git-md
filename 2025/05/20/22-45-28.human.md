This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing series adding pathspec support to `git diff --no-index`. Jacob Keller responds to Junio Hamano's review of the final patch in the series.

2. Jacob agrees to clarify the documentation wording around pathspecs applying to both sides of the diff, changing "as they" to "and they" or similar.
3. Junio suggests simplifying some test cases by using `--name-only` or `--name-status`, which Jacob seems receptive to implementing in the next version.

The exchange shows the series is in its final polishing stages, with only minor documentation and test improvements remaining.
