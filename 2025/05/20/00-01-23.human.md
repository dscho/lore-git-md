This is a refactoring patch that's part of a series adding pathspec support to `git diff --no-index`. The patch exposes previously internal pathspec matching functionality needed for the feature.

The change makes `match_pathspec_with_flags()` public and exports two of its flags (`DO_MATCH_DIRECTORY` and `DO_MATCH_LEADING_PATHSPEC`), while keeping the `DO_MATCH_EXCLUDE` flag private. This allows the upcoming `git diff --no-index` pathspec support to properly handle directory matching and leading pathspec cases without exposing all internal implementation details.

The patch is focused and mechanical - it moves existing code with minimal changes, just making previously internal functions and flags available to other parts of Git. The commit message clearly explains the change and its motivation in the context of the larger series. This appears to be a well-constructed plumbing change that enables the more user-visible functionality coming in later patches.
