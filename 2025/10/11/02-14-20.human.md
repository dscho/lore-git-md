This is a test infrastructure discussion about flaky GPG2-related tests, specifically t1016-compatObjectFormat.sh.

Todd Zullinger confirms that the GPG2 prereq tests in t1016 consistently fail in CI environments (including Fedora's build infrastructure) while rarely failing locally, even under stress testing. This makes the issue particularly difficult to debug. While Todd initially suspected timing problems, they couldn't reproduce the behavior reliably.

The discussion follows Junio C Hamano's suggestion to temporarily leave the broken state in 'seen' to attract debugging attention. Todd expresses appreciation for this approach, hoping it might prompt someone familiar with the test suite to identify the root cause, though acknowledges that availability of such experts may be limited.
