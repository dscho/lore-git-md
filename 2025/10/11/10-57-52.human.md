This is a follow-up in the CI/build system thread about Ubuntu 25.10's sudo-rs compatibility issues. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's suggestions about making the workaround more robust. He explains that the current check for `/etc/alternatives/sudo` is sufficient since no other distros currently have the sudo-rs/sudo.ws split, though he's open to adding an additional check for `/usr/bin/sudo.ws` if preferred. 

Patrick notes that all solutions are somewhat brittle given potential upstream changes, and shares that the 32-bit Ubuntu case is moot since Ubuntu has dropped 32-bit support (with a pending patch to switch that CI job to i386/debian instead). The exchange shows the maintainers converging on a practical solution while acknowledging the temporary nature of this workaround until sudo-rs adds `--preserve-env` support.
