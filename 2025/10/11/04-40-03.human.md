This is a documentation patch discussion regarding `git worktree` advice. Eric Sunshine provides a detailed review of Michal Suchánek's patch that adds a recommendation against nested worktree creation.

Sunshine strongly objects to the proposed documentation change, arguing it lacks justification. He notes the patch inverts the importance of its changes - the new recommendation should be the focus rather than the examples link. He points out there's no technical reason to avoid nested worktrees (Git handles them fine) and many users successfully use this pattern. The only minor downside is aesthetic (needing to update gitignore files), which doesn't warrant a warning.

The review suggests either:
1. Dropping the patch entirely due to insufficient rationale, or
2. Substantially improving it by listing concrete pros/cons if any convincing ones exist

This continues the thread's theme of carefully considering worktree UX documentation, but shows pushback against adding warnings without strong justification. The discussion remains focused on documentation approach rather than behavior changes.
