This is a refactoring patch that's part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing effort to remove uses of `the_repository` global variable. It's the third patch in a 12-part series introducing a new `git-history` command with `reword` and `split` subcommands.

The change is straightforward - it replaces one instance of `the_repository` with the repository pointer that was already being passed to the `create_commit()` function. The commit message notes this doesn't completely eliminate the need for `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` due to some remaining global configuration dependencies.

This appears to be a clean, mechanical change that aligns with Git's ongoing architectural improvements. The patch is small and focused, just two lines changing one function call. It's part of the larger series' effort to share infrastructure with `git-replay` while modernizing the codebase. The change doesn't affect functionality but helps move toward better repository handling patterns.
