This is a continuation of the ongoing worktree UX discussion, with Junio Hamano responding to Eric Sunshine's points about nested worktree usage patterns. The email focuses on two key aspects:

First, Junio offers an alternative interpretation of why complaints about worktree "noise" in status output are rare - suggesting most users simply don't create worktrees inside their main worktree directory, rather than the issue being unimportant. This maintains his earlier position about the design intent while acknowledging the UX concern.

Second, regarding Eric's suggestion to document the bare repository + worktrees pattern, Junio agrees it's worth documenting but makes the pointed observation that users of bare repositories would be unlikely to make the same nested-worktree mistake since there's no primary worktree to nest within. This reinforces the core design distinction between worktrees (peer instantiations) and the main repository.

The exchange shows the discussion moving from behavioral questions to documentation improvements, with both participants agreeing that better documentation of established patterns (like bare+worktrees) would help, while maintaining different perspectives on how common or problematic nested worktree usage actually is. Junio's response continues to emphasize the original design philosophy while being open to practical documentation improvements.
