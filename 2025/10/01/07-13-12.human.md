**Release planning discussion with ecosystem compatibility concerns**

Luca Milanesio responds to the Git 3.0 release planning thread, expressing concerns about rushing the release without full SHA-256 interoperability across the Git ecosystem. Having recently attended the Git Mini-Summit in Amsterdam (but missing the main Contributor Summit discussion), he seeks clarification on the earlier deliberations and asks for any available notes or recordings.

The email highlights potential risks of breaking changes affecting non-C-Git implementations like JGit, suggesting the community should carefully consider ecosystem-wide impacts. Milanesio notes that JGit representatives were present at the Summit and offers his own participation in the interoperability effort, while pointedly asking about resource commitments from other stakeholders' organizations.

This continues the thread's central tension between schedule pressure and feature completeness, with Milanesio leaning toward ensuring broader compatibility before a major version bump. The tone is constructive but cautious, reflecting the real-world challenges of coordinating major changes across interdependent projects.
