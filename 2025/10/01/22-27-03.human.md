This is a continuation of the ongoing discussion about git-worktree behavior and UX, specifically addressing documentation gaps and design philosophy.

Junio Hamano responds to Eric Sunshine's observation that complaints about worktree "noise" in status output are rare, suggesting this may indicate most users don't create worktrees inside their main worktree. He agrees with Eric's proposal to document the bare repository + worktrees pattern, noting it's officially supported but currently undocumented.

The exchange highlights two key points:
1. The rarity of complaints may reflect that most users naturally avoid nesting worktrees (Junio's interpretation), rather than the issue being unimportant (Eric's interpretation).
2. While bare+worktrees is a supported workflow, Junio doubts anyone would nest worktrees in this case since there's no primary worktree to nest under.

This reinforces the thread's central theme about worktree design philosophy while moving toward practical documentation improvements. The tone remains technical but collegial, with both maintainers aligning on next steps for documentation while maintaining their perspectives on user behavior patterns.
