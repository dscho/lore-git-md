Sergey Organov responds to Junio Hamano's example of a recommended worktree layout (with a clear "primary" worktree and siblings) by suggesting that users often expect a more symmetric model where worktrees are peers under a common `.git` directory, with no single primary worktree. Organov suggests the command could be more opinionated - either automatically placing new worktrees as siblings or at least warning when creating nested worktrees.
