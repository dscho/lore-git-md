Bugfix proposal for `git diff --quiet` regression with a design discussion on output suppression.

Jeff King follows up on the ongoing discussion about fixing the `git diff --quiet` regression where it incorrectly produces content for newly staged files. His email presents two key technical approaches:

Jeff acknowledges this would require more invasive changes but suggests it as a cleaner architectural direction.
