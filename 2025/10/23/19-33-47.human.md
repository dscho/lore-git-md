This is a feature patch that adds configurable geometric repack splitting to Git's maintenance tasks, part of the ongoing geometric repacking strategy work.

Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that introduces a new `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor` configuration option. This allows users to customize the geometric factor used during maintenance repacking, which defaults to 2 (meaning each pack must be at least twice as large as the previous one). The patch includes documentation updates, implementation changes in gc.c, and new tests.

Taylor notes this wasn't exactly what they had envisioned - they were thinking of a more general `repack.geometricFactor` config that could be used outside maintenance contexts. However, they agree this change is worthwhile on its own and doesn't preclude that future work. The implementation looks correct, with proper documentation and test coverage added. The discussion shows alignment between the developers on this incremental improvement to the geometric repacking feature.
