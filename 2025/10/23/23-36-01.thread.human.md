Junio Hamano's latest "What's cooking" update has sparked discussion about fixing a regression in Git's diff output suppression behavior that appeared in v2.51.1. The thread focuses on different diff modes (-w --name-status and -w --raw) where output suppression wasn't working as expected. Jeff King (Peff) has analyzed the issue and proposed three potential solutions for the remaining -w --raw problem, while Junio has already merged an initial fix for -w --name-status to the 'next' branch.

In the latest development, Junio has clarified the backport strategy: only the critical fix for -w --name-status will be backported to the stable 'maint' branch, as it directly addresses a regression. The more complex changes needed for -w --raw will remain in the development branches ('next' and 'master') for further testing. This conservative approach ensures stability in maintenance releases while allowing the more involved fixes to mature. The thread also notes progress on other fronts, including SHA1-SHA256 interoperability work and test modernization efforts, though the diff regression remains the primary focus of discussion.
