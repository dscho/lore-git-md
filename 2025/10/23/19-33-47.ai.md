This is a feature patch that makes the geometric repack factor configurable in Git's maintenance tasks, building on the ongoing geometric repacking strategy work.

Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch adding a `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor` configuration option. While Taylor had originally envisioned a more general `repack.geometricFactor` configuration that would apply to all geometric repacks (not just maintenance), they acknowledge this patch is still valuable and doesn't preclude that future change. The patch adds documentation, implementation, and tests for the new config option which controls the geometric sequence factor during maintenance repacks, defaulting to 2 to match `git repack --geometric=2` behavior.

The implementation modifies `builtin/gc.c` to read the new config value and pass it to the repack command, while the tests in `t7900-maintenance.sh` verify the configurable behavior works as expected. Taylor approves the current approach while noting there may be room for a more general geometric factor configuration in the future. This is a straightforward addition to the geometric repacking infrastructure that gives users more control over pack consolidation behavior in large repositories.
