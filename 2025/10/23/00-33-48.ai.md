Bugfix follow-up in the `git diff --quiet` regression thread. This email from Lidong Yan responds to Junio Hamano's patch that extended the quiet-mode protection to `--name-status` output paths by suggesting an alternative approach to handling the diff queue flushing.

The core technical discussion revolves around whether the current implementation - which temporarily redirects output to `/dev/null` and disables color-moved during the flush - is the optimal solution. Lidong proposes creating a separate queue for items that need flushing after the quiet processing loop completes, preserving their output rather than discarding it. This would avoid the need for the current state toggling of `options->file` and `options->color_moved`.

The email continues the thread's examination of architectural tradeoffs in the diff machinery, specifically around:
- State management of diff output destinations (`options->file`)
- Color-moved handling during quiet operations
- The efficiency of N-path redirections versus queue separation
- Whether output should be discarded (current approach) or preserved for later flushing (proposed alternative)

This represents an important design discussion point in the regression fix, as it questions the fundamental approach taken in the current solution while maintaining focus on the original goal of ensuring silent operation for `--quiet` and proper handling of `--name-status`. The suggestion implies potential simplification of the state restoration logic at the cost of additional queue management complexity.
