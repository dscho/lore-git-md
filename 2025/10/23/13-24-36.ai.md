Patrick acknowledges Jeff's concerns about two broader test fragility issues: shell evaluation problems with spaces in paths, and potential key exposure when falling back to the user's real SSH_AUTH_SOCK. The discussion explores but rejects alternative approaches like relative paths ("./sshsock") due to working directory risks, and abbreviated socket names that wouldn't provide enough path length reduction. Patrick advocates for merging the minimal fix now, with potential follow-ups to improve test robustness.
