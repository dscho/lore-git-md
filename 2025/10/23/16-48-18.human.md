This is the final ack for the geometric repacking strategy patch series, marking its completion. Junio C Hamano gives his approval to Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series, noting that while he didn't scrutinize every detail, the overall implementation appears sound. The series introduces configurable geometric repacking for Git maintenance, particularly beneficial for monorepos, with v2 adding the ability to configure the geometric split factor via `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor`.

The patch series has undergone thorough review from multiple contributors (Justin Tobler, Taylor Blau, and Junio himself) and represents a significant enhancement to Git's maintenance capabilities. It implements a hybrid approach that intelligently combines incremental repacks with periodic full repacks using cruft packs, with complete strategy control for both manual and scheduled maintenance operations. The implementation is now ready for inclusion in the main Git codebase.
