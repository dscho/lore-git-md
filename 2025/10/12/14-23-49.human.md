Test fix for GPG configuration persistence in t1016-compatObjectFormat.sh.

Todd Zullinger confirms that Junio Hamano's one-line fix resolves test failures that were occurring in CI and Fedora builds. The issue stemmed from a 2024 commit that accidentally changed a persistent `git config` call to `test_config` when setting up the GPG program configuration, causing the setting to be reverted after the first test case rather than persisting as intended.

Zullinger notes they had previously investigated similar test failures but didn't connect them to this configuration issue, having tried other approaches like forcing `--faked-system-time`. While expressing some frustration at missing the simple solution, they acknowledge the fix works and appears to resolve the test failures they were seeing.

The change is minimal - just reverting one line from `test_config` back to `git config` for the gpg.program setting. This restores the intended behavior where the GPG configuration persists across all test cases in t1016-compatObjectFormat.sh. The fix appears straightforward and effective, with no indication of controversy or need for further changes.
