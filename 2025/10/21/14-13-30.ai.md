This patch completes the maintenance strategy unification by making the "gc" strategy explicitly configurable, addressing the final gap in the series' design. It modifies the strategy handling to allow explicit selection of the default GC-based maintenance approach, which was only implicitly used before.

The key changes include:
- Adding "gc" as a documented strategy option in maintenance.adoc
- Renaming `default_strategy` to `gc_strategy` and extending it to work for both manual and scheduled maintenance
- Updating `parse_maintenance_strategy()` to recognize the "gc" strategy
- Modifying `initialize_task_config()` to use `gc_strategy` as the default
- Adding test cases verifying the gc strategy works for both manual and scheduled maintenance

The patch maintains backward compatibility while resolving two specific limitations: inability to override a global strategy with gc for a specific repo, and inability to use gc for scheduled maintenance. Test coverage is expanded to verify both the new explicit gc strategy and its behavior in scheduled contexts.

This change aligns with the series' goal of providing complete, flexible maintenance strategy control, particularly benefiting monorepos. The implementation follows the established pattern from earlier patches, with clean separation of strategy definitions and consistent configuration handling. The patch appears well-integrated with the existing codebase and maintains the series' high standard of test coverage.
