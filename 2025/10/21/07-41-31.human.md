This is a technical review comment on the final patch of a hook subsystem refactoring series. Patrick Steinhardt provides two specific suggestions for Adrian Ratiu's receive-pack hook conversion patch (v2 10/10):

1. The `lines_batch_size` parameter in `feed_receive_hook()` should be changed from `int` to `unsigned` since it represents a count of lines.
2. The loop index variable `i` should also be made unsigned to match.

The patch being reviewed is part of a larger effort to convert Git's hooks to a callback-based API, with this particular piece handling batched input processing for receive-pack hooks to avoid per-line overhead. Patrick's feedback continues his pattern of detailed technical scrutiny throughout the series, focusing on type consistency and API cleanliness.

The suggestions are minor but characteristic of the thorough review this series has received, with Patrick and others paying close attention to implementation details even as they approve the overall design direction. No new major issues are raised here - just small refinements to complete the conversion work.
