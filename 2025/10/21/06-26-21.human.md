Performance optimization for macOS mmap/munmap operations. Torsten Bögershausen provides review feedback on Koji Nakamaru's patch that queues munmap operations on macOS to reduce system overhead. The patch itself (already summarized in the thread context) shows dramatic performance improvements, particularly for repositories with many mmap operations.

Bögershausen suggests several improvements: rewording the commit message for clarity, renaming COUNT_MAX to something more specific like MAX_UNMAP_COUNT, adding a Makefile knob (DEFER_MUNMAPS) to make the feature more configurable, and clarifying the restriction to read-only mappings. The feedback is constructive and focused on making the implementation more maintainable and flexible while preserving its significant performance benefits.

The review doesn't raise any fundamental objections to the approach, just thoughtful suggestions for refinement. The patch appears to be moving toward acceptance with these minor adjustments. The performance gains (reducing sys time from 27 minutes to 24 seconds in one case) make this a compelling change for macOS users.
