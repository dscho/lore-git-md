This is a discussion about improving the user experience of `rerere` (reuse recorded resolution) as part of the proposal to enable it by default. D. Ben Knoble responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's concerns about the visibility of rerere messages during conflict resolution.

The email highlights ongoing usability issues with rerere's output - specifically that "Reused recorded resolution" messages can be easily missed when mixed with other conflict output, potentially confusing users who aren't familiar with the feature. Knoble seconds Haugsbakk's observation and adds that even after rerere automatically resolves conflicts, the status output might still suggest the resolution needs approval, which could be unclear to new users.

The discussion doesn't introduce new technical changes but reinforces the need for better UI feedback if rerere is to be enabled by default. This continues the thread's theme of balancing rerere's technical maturity against its user-facing rough edges, with multiple contributors now noting specific pain points in the current implementation. The quality of these concrete usability observations suggests the default-enablement proposal may need accompanying UI improvements.
