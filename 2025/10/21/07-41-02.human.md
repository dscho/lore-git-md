This is a review comment on a hook subsystem refactoring patch. Patrick Steinhardt provides a small nitpick on patch 2/10 of Adrian Ratiu's series converting Git's hook system to a callback-based API.

The patch in question introduces two mutually exclusive ways to provide stdin to hooks - either via a file path or a callback function. Patrick notes that while the patch correctly detects conflicting configurations with BUG() assertions, the error messages in two locations differ slightly. He suggests making them consistent by using the same phrasing ("options path_to_stdin and feed_pipe are mutually exclusive") in both places.

This is a minor consistency improvement to an otherwise solid patch that's part of a larger, well-received series modernizing Git's hook infrastructure. The review interaction shows careful attention to detail from both the author and reviewer, with Patrick catching a small opportunity for improved consistency in error messaging.
