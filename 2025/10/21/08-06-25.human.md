This is a performance optimization discussion for macOS mmap/munmap operations. Jeff King (peff) is reviewing Koji Nakamaru's patch that batches munmap calls to reduce system overhead on macOS. While acknowledging the potential benefits shown in the benchmarks, peff raises several important questions about the underlying conditions and implications.

The key concern is why the test cases require so many mmap operations in the first place - peff notes that well-packed repositories should need far fewer mappings (only 27 in his test). He suggests investigating whether running `git gc` or adjusting `core.packedGitWindowSize` might address the root cause more effectively.

Peff also probes the mechanics of the batching implementation, questioning whether the performance gains come from actual batched unmapping or simply from deferring unmaps until process exit. He raises concerns about potential memory pressure from holding onto unused mappings and wonders how the solution would perform with commands that actually fill the 65k-entry queue.

The discussion points suggest that while the technical implementation appears sound (as noted in previous reviews), there may be opportunities to address the performance issues through repository maintenance or configuration changes rather than (or in addition to) the proposed munmap optimization.
