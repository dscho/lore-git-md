Maintainer integration note for `git-history` series.

Junio Hamano indicates he will integrate Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series introducing the `git-history` command, which now includes both `reword` and `split` subcommands for targeted history editing. The series has been significantly refined since earlier iterations based on Git Contributor Summit discussions, focusing on non-conflicting operations.

The maintainer notes the series depends on another nearly-ready topic (likely the atomic ref updates work in `git-replay`), but expects minimal changes to the parts this series builds upon. While some minor squashing may still be needed, the overall readiness suggests this feature will land in the near future.

This marks the culmination of a well-reviewed effort that has addressed all technical feedback while making incremental progress on `the_repository` removal and adding substantial test coverage (447 lines across 18 new tests). The series positions `git-history` as a complementary tool to `git-rebase` for specific editing operations.
