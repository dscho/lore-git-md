This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` series, splitting the add-patch declarations into their own header file.

The patch moves all add-patch related declarations from `add-interactive.h` to a new `add-patch.h` header, leaving only the add-interactive specific parts in the original header. This creates clearer boundaries between the two subsystems, making the codebase more maintainable. The changes are straightforward - declarations are copied verbatim to the new location with appropriate includes added.

This is part of the larger infrastructure work supporting the new `git-history` command, specifically its interactive editing features. The clean separation will likely make future development easier, though the patch itself doesn't introduce any functional changes. The implementation looks clean and follows Git's established patterns for header organization.
