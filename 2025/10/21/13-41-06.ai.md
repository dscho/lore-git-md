This is a discussion about type safety and casting in the ongoing xdiff refactoring series for Rust FFI compatibility. Junio C Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's concerns about the proliferation of `(long)` casts in the codebase despite converting struct members to `size_t`.

Key technical points:
- The series converted all `long` struct members to `size_t` but introduced 38 `(long)` casts (vs 4 in master)
- Phillip argues this undermines type safety goals and creates maintenance burden while Rust remains optional
- Junio acknowledges the impedance mismatch but notes the complexity of moving from signed to unsigned types
- The discussion highlights the tension between type purity for Rust FFI and practical maintainability of C code during transition

The exchange reveals an important implementation-level debate about where to draw boundaries in the type conversion process, with Junio suggesting some casting may be necessary as a pragmatic solution during the transition period. This follows previous discussions about type choices (uint8_t* vs char*, ssize_t vs ptrdiff_t) and shows the refactoring's ongoing challenges in balancing C and Rust interoperability needs.
