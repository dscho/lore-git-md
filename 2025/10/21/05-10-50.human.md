This is a brief follow-up in the completed xdiff refactoring series that modernizes data structures for Rust FFI compatibility. Junio Hamano responds to Ezekiel Newren's observation that patch 5/9 (which split xrecord_t.ha into two distinct fields) received no review feedback despite being a significant structural change.

Hamano notes that lack of comments doesn't necessarily indicate approval - reviewers may simply have been too busy. The exchange highlights how even well-executed technical changes can sometimes slip through without discussion due to reviewer bandwidth constraints.

The underlying patch in question had cleanly separated hash functionality into `line_hash` (for content hashing) and `minimal_perfect_hash` (for unique identifiers), maintaining all existing behavior while improving type safety. While the technical merits appear sound based on the thread summary, this email serves as a reminder that silent review cycles don't necessarily imply consensus.
