This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about enabling `rerere.enabled` by default. Junio Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's detailed workflow for selectively removing resolutions from the rerere cache. 

The exchange confirms that the current `git rerere forget` workflow requires being in a conflict resolution state to work - you must first hit the conflict, then forget the resolution, then either abort and restart or continue resolving. Junio notes this is the correct approach, though points out steps 5-6 (aborting and restarting) may sometimes be unnecessary if you can proceed directly to recording a new resolution.

This reinforces earlier concerns in the thread about rerere's user interface being non-intuitive, particularly around managing the resolution cache. The technical behavior appears well-understood by experienced users like Junio, but the discussion continues to highlight usability pain points that might affect less experienced users if rerere becomes enabled by default. The thread remains focused on whether these UI issues should be addressed before or after flipping the default.
