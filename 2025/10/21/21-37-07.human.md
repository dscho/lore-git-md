This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about enabling `rerere.enabled` by default. D. Ben Knoble reinforces earlier concerns about the visibility of rerere's "Reused recorded resolution" messages during conflict resolution, noting they can be easily missed in the output stream. They also highlight a potential disconnect where `git-status` may still report conflicts even after rerere has automatically resolved them, which could confuse users unfamiliar with the feature.

The email acknowledges these are UI polish issues rather than blockers for enabling rerere by default. Knoble explicitly supports the graduation of rerere feature to default-on status while recognizing there will be additional user experience improvements needed. This continues the thread's trend of balancing the feature's maturity against its current usability rough edges.

The discussion remains focused on whether to enable rerere now (with known UI issues) in Git 2.52 as Taylor Blau suggested, or wait until Git 3.0 as Junio Hamano proposed while potentially addressing the visibility and status reporting concerns first. Knoble's contribution adds weight to the argument that some UI improvements may be desirable regardless of the timing decision.
