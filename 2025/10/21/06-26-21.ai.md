Performance optimization for macOS mmap/munmap operations. Torsten Bögershausen provides review feedback on Koji Nakamaru's patch that queues munmap operations on macOS to reduce system overhead. The thread has already established the technical approach (thread-safe batching of munmap calls) and demonstrated dramatic performance improvements (27m15s -> 24s sys time in extreme cases).

Bögershausen suggests several improvements: clarifying the commit message explanation of queue behavior, renaming COUNT_MAX to MAX_UNMAP_COUNT, adding documentation that the optimization only applies to read-only mappings (Git's current usage pattern), and introducing a Makefile knob (DEFER_MUNMAPS) to make the feature more maintainable and potentially portable. The feedback is constructive and focused on making the robust implementation even clearer and more flexible.

Key technical details remain unchanged from the original patch: thread-safe queue of 65530 munmap operations (matching Linux's vm.max_map_count default), restricted to private read-only mappings, with automatic cleanup on process exit. The review doesn't raise any fundamental objections to the approach, just suggestions for improved documentation and configurability.
