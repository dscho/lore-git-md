This is a follow-up discussion in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, now in its finalization stages. Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on Junio Hamano's earlier suggestion to make the `submodule.<name>.gitdirpath` config the authoritative source of truth for submodule paths when the encoding extension is enabled.

Patrick strongly supports this approach, outlining several benefits: it provides clear consistency rules for encoded repositories, enables straightforward migration when enabling the extension, allows future encoding scheme changes without breaking compatibility, and gives clear error handling when paths are missing. His key point is that this design future-proofs the system by decoupling the encoding mechanism from the stored paths.

The discussion represents final polish on an already-settled architectural decision in this series. Patrick's endorsement reinforces the consensus that emerged earlier in the thread, with no new technical concerns raised - just additional rationale for why the chosen approach is robust. This email helps cement the design by highlighting its flexibility advantages, particularly the ability to evolve the encoding scheme without breaking existing repositories.
