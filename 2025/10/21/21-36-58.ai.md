Maintainer response to documentation patch for `git add -p`'s 'P' option.

Junio C Hamano responds to René Scharfe's patch that improves visibility of the 'P' (pager) option in `git add -p`. The original patch updated documentation in `git-add.adoc`, help text in add-patch.c, and test expectations in t/t3701-add-interactive.sh to properly distinguish between 'p' (print without pager) and 'P' (print with pager) options.

The maintainer expresses approval of the change, noting that while the historical reason for originally hiding 'P' behind 'p' isn't clear, making the distinction explicit is sensible. This appears to be a straightforward ack of an uncontroversial documentation improvement, with no requested changes or additional commentary needed. The response suggests this patch will likely proceed to the next stage of review or be merged without further discussion.
