Refactoring patch in the `git-history` series, focusing on the add-patch subsystem. This is patch 9/12 in v5 of Patrick Steinhardt's series implementing flexible history editing commands.

The patch removes `add-patch.c`'s dependency on the `add-interactive` subsystem by directly managing its interactive configuration. Instead of initializing through `init_add_i_state()`, it now stores and initializes the configuration in `struct add_p_state` using the new `interactive_config` infrastructure introduced in earlier patches. The changes are mechanical but thorough - updating all references from `s->s.cfg` to `s->cfg` and adjusting initialization/cleanup.

This continues the architectural cleanup from patches 7-8, further separating the add-patch implementation from add-interactive. The changes appear straightforward with no behavior modifications, just internal plumbing updates to use the new configuration structure. As with the rest of the series, the changes are well-contained and maintain backward compatibility while progressing the larger goal of cleaner subsystem boundaries.
