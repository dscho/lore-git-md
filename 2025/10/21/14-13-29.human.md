This patch extends Git's maintenance strategy configuration to cover manual maintenance runs, not just scheduled ones. It's part of a larger series introducing geometric repacking strategies for repository maintenance, particularly targeting monorepo use cases.

The change makes `maintenance.strategy` apply globally rather than just to scheduled tasks, addressing a potential conflict where scheduled and manual maintenance could use different strategies. The patch carefully maintains backward compatibility for the "incremental" strategy by keeping its existing behavior while adding gc tasks for manual runs.

Key technical changes include:
- Updated documentation explaining the expanded scope of maintenance strategies
- Modified strategy initialization logic in gc.c to handle both manual and scheduled maintenance
- New test cases verifying strategy behavior across different maintenance types
- Special handling for the "incremental" strategy to preserve historical behavior
