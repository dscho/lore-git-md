This is part 5/6 of a refactoring series focused on the packfile store API, introducing a new macro to simplify pack iteration patterns. The patch adds a `repo_for_each_pack()` macro that replaces repetitive boilerplate code for iterating through all packs in a repository, including those referenced by multi-pack indices.

The change touches 20 files across various subsystems - from core operations like pack-bitmap and pack-objects to maintenance commands (gc, repack) and even HTTP backend code. Each conversion follows the same pattern: replacing manual `packfile_store_get_all_packs()` loops with the new macro. The implementation is clean and mechanical, with no behavior changes - just improved readability and consistency.

This continues the series' strategy of first introducing safer alternatives before removing old APIs. The macro encapsulates the now-standard way to iterate packs while handling MIDX cases correctly. Given the thorough review and approval from Taylor Blau in earlier iterations, and the careful incremental approach taken throughout the series, this appears to be a well-executed internal cleanup with no user-facing impact.
