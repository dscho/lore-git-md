The thread discusses the introduction of a `commitGraph.changedPaths` config option for commit-graph Bloom filters, a feature primarily driven by Microsoft's Office monorepo needs. Taylor Blau and Emily Yang developed the implementation, which Junio Hamano has already queued for 'next' after Derrick Stolee's Reviewed-by on version 2. The design carefully distinguishes between command-line flags (which can remove filters) and config settings (which preserve existing filters when set to `false`).

SZEDER Gábor has raised two important late-stage concerns. First, they argue the current behavior where `commitGraph.changedPaths=false` preserves existing filters is counterintuitive and wasteful, as it continues computing filters the user tried to disable. Second, they note the test coverage doesn't include split commit-graph scenarios - a notable gap given this feature's target environment of large monorepos where split graphs are common. While these don't challenge the feature's core design, they highlight edge cases that could affect real-world usage.

The series appears technically sound otherwise, with documented precedence rules and Scalar integration. However, SZEDER's points suggest the team may want to reconsider the preservation behavior or at least expand test coverage before final merge, particularly given the feature's importance for large-scale deployments. The Microsoft Office monorepo remains the primary validation environment for ongoing performance evaluation.
