This thread has converged on a solution for configuring changed-path Bloom filters in commit-graphs, with Microsoft's Office monorepo needs driving the design. Taylor Blau initiated the effort to establish persistent configuration through a `commitGraph.changedPaths` option, with Emily Yang now leading the implementation. The key insight is distinguishing command-line behavior (where `--no-changed-paths` removes filters) from config behavior (where setting to `false` preserves existing filters) - a design Derrick Stolee originally proposed and now explicitly approves in v2.

Junio Hamano's documentation concerns have been addressed by clearly explaining this asymmetric behavior. The implementation uses a three-state config (-1/0/1 values), integrates with Scalar for large monorepos, and includes expanded test coverage. With Stolee's ack of the updated series and no outstanding objections, this appears ready for merging. The solution not only serve Microsoft's immediate needs but may establish a pattern for similar config/flag interactions elsewhere in Git. Real-world performance validation in the Office monorepo remains, but the technical consensus is now clear.
