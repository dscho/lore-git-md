This is a documentation and potential deprecation discussion regarding the `--committer-date-is-author-date` option in `git-rebase`. The email continues the thread about warning users when this option creates problematic timestamp ordering in commit histories.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Phillip Wood's earlier suggestion about deprecating the option for `git-rebase` (as opposed to `git-am` where it has valid uses), agreeing with the rationale and offering to mark it as deprecated in the next patch version. He seeks additional opinions on this direction from other contributors.

The email introduces a new consideration: whether to implement a stderr warning when the option is used with `git-rebase`, making the issue more visible than just documentation changes. Kristoffer questions why this approach hasn't been used before in `git-rebase`, speculating it might relate to concerns about breaking scripts that parse stderr output.

This moves the discussion beyond documentation into potential implementation changes, though still focused on user awareness rather than immediate behavior modification. The thread continues to distinguish between `git-am` (where the option remains useful) and `git-rebase` (where it increasingly appears problematic).
