This is a follow-up discussion about the documentation patch warning against `--committer-date-is-author-date` in `git-am` and `git-rebase`. The conversation focuses on refining the warning text and considering deprecation of the option specifically for `git-rebase`.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Phillip Wood's review comments, agreeing to several documentation improvements: removing the unclear reference to `git-rev-list` about clock skew, changing "lie" to "override" in the warning text, and adjusting wording to be more specific to rebase contexts. Phillip had also suggested adding a warning about using this option with interactive rebase (`-i`), though the exact phrasing remains to be determined.

The key new development is the emerging consensus that this option may not make sense for `git-rebase` at all, since rebasing typically moves commits forward in time (whereas the original `git-am` use case involved applying patches to older bases). Kristoffer indicates willingness to mark the option as deprecated for rebase in the next version of the patch, pending further input from other contributors.

The discussion remains focused on documentation improvements rather than behavioral changes, but now includes the prospect of eventual deprecation for one of the two commands where this option appears. Phillip Wood will be unavailable for 10 days but plans to review any revisions upon return.
