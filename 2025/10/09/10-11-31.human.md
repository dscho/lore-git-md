This is a naming discussion in the post-merge review of Patrick Steinhardt's refs subsystem refactoring series. Toon Claes weighs in on the debate about whether `struct reference` is appropriately named for its role in the ref iteration interface.

The email supports Patrick's position that `struct reference` accurately represents what a reference is (containing refname, target, oid, and flags), but acknowledges Jeff King's concern about the name being potentially too generic. Toon suggests tweaking the struct's documentation comment to better clarify its purpose while keeping the name, and notes that future naming conflicts could be addressed if they arise.

The discussion also touches on the related issue of `struct ref` being poorly named (as it handles both local and remote refs via `peer_ref` links), though Toon doesn't propose a specific solution for that case. The tone is constructive, with Toon positioning himself as a mediator between the two viewpoints while leaning toward Patrick's more expansive vision for `struct reference` as a foundational type.
