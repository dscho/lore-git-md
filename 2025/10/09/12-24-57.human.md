This is the final patch in a series adding consistent signature handling (PGP, X.509, SSH) to `git fast-import` and `git fast-export`. It introduces a `--signed-tags=<mode>` option for `git fast-import` to match the existing `--signed-commits` option, completing the feature parity between the two commands.

The patch adds five handling modes (verbatim, warn-verbatim, warn-strip, strip, abort) that mirror the behavior of `fast-export`, though with a default of 'verbatim' rather than 'abort'. The implementation uses the same `parse_signed_buffer()` infrastructure from earlier in the series to handle all signature formats uniformly. New tests verify the behavior with all three signature types (PGP, X.509, SSH) across the different modes.
