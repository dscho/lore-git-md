This is the final patch in a refactoring series for Git's packfile store API, specifically addressing Taylor Blau's concern about potential merge conflicts from renaming `packfile_store_get_all_packs()` to `packfile_store_get_packs()`. 

Patrick Steinhardt argues that the rename is safe because most callers already need to handle MIDX'd packs correctly, and the previous behavior where `get_packs()` and `get_all_packs()` influenced each other was problematic anyway. He notes that the series' changes have shown existing callers handle this correctly, suggesting new callers would need similar robustness.

The discussion reflects careful consideration of backward compatibility in a complex subsystem, with Patrick concluding the risk is low. This wraps up a well-reviewed series that standardizes pack iteration patterns while maintaining behavior, though Taylor's caution about timing remains as a minor open consideration. The patch itself is a straightforward rename touching four files (fast-import, pack-objects, and packfile internals), completing the API simplification started earlier in the series.
