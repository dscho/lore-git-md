This is a follow-up in the ongoing thread about GPG test reliability, specifically addressing Todd Zullinger's earlier patch series that went unnoticed.

Junio Hamano is reviving discussion of Zullinger's July 2024 patches that fixed GPGSM prerequisite test issues but also exposed flaky GPG2-related test failures. The original series didn't receive attention at the time, and Zullinger notes these tests remain unreliable in CI environments like Fedora's builds. Hamano is now picking up the patches but requests additional review while the community is actively discussing related test infrastructure improvements.

The context shows this is part of broader work on signature handling tests, where Christian Couder recently incorporated some of Zullinger's GPGSM fixes while marking the remaining GPG2 issues with NEEDSWORK comments. The email suggests these lingering reliability problems may warrant test disabling rather than keeping flaky tests, though the decision awaits further input from other maintainers.
