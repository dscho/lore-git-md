Documentation refinement and potential deprecation discussion for `--committer-date-is-author-date` option in `git-rebase`.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Phillip Wood's review of the documentation patch warning about timestamp ordering violations when using `--committer-date-is-author-date`. The discussion has shifted from just documenting the issue to considering whether the option should be deprecated for `git-rebase` specifically, while keeping it for `git-am` where it has a clear use case (preserving commit IDs when reapplying patches).

Phillip Wood suggests several documentation improvements: removing an unclear reference to `git-rev-list` about clock skew, changing "lie" to "override" in the warning text, and adjusting wording to be rebase-specific. He also raises the particular concern about interactive rebase (`-i`) where commit rearrangement could exacerbate timestamp ordering problems.

Kristoffer agrees with most suggestions and asks for clarification about the interactive rebase warning - whether it needs special mention in the documentation. The email ends with an open question to the list about whether to proceed with deprecating the option for `git-rebase`, indicating this may be the direction for the next version of the patch. The exchange shows the documentation effort evolving into a broader reconsideration of the option's appropriateness in different commands.
