This is a final design discussion email in the atomic ref updates patch series for `git replay`. Phillip Wood responds to Siddharth Asthana's question about the interface design, weighing the simplicity of boolean flags (`--[no-]update-refs`) against the extensibility of an enum-based approach (`--update-refs=<mode>`).

Phillip notes that while the enum approach offers better future extensibility, a boolean flag would suffice for current needs and could always be extended later if required. This confirms the thread's earlier resolution to use the enum-based design while acknowledging the boolean approach would also be workable. The email serves as a final check on the interface decision before the series moves toward merging.
