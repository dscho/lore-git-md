Final patch in the packfile store API refactoring series, cleaning up naming after previous simplifications. This is a straightforward refactoring that renames `packfile_store_get_all_packs()` to the simpler `packfile_store_get_packs()`, now that the confusing distinction between "all packs" and regular packs has been eliminated in earlier patches.

The change updates callers in fast-import and pack-objects, plus the macro definition in packfile.h. As with the rest of the series, this is an internal API change with no user-facing impact, completing the standardization of pack iteration patterns across the codebase. The patch follows the series' careful methodology of first introducing better patterns before removing old ones, and has already received technical approval from Taylor Blau.
