This is a follow-up discussion in the refactoring thread about the naming of fields in the new `struct reference` introduced by Patrick Steinhardt's refs modernization series. The series has already been merged, but Jialuo raised a post-merge question about why the field storing symbolic ref destinations was named `target` rather than `referent`.

Patrick responds that he doesn't have a strong preference between the two options, considering both names acceptable. This appears to be a minor stylistic discussion rather than a substantive technical issue, as both terms would be functionally equivalent in the code. The exchange shows the community's attention to detail in naming conventions even after a major refactoring has been completed.
