Hamano confirms that "integrate" was intended as a generic term for combining changes between branches, not limited to just merging. The email highlights the challenge of finding clear terminology that doesn't overwhelm users with implementation details (like forcing them to understand both merge and rebase) while still being technically precise. Hamano acknowledges Evans' attempts to find alternatives but doesn't propose a specific solution, instead reinforcing the conceptual framework behind the current wording.
