This is a post-merge discussion about naming conventions in the refs subsystem, specifically around `struct reference` and `struct ref`. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's concerns about the generic naming of `struct reference` by arguing that having a central, well-defined structure for references could be beneficial for future interfaces. He suggests that `struct ref` might be the misnamed entity, proposing `struct remote_ref` as an alternative while acknowledging the complexity due to its widespread use and the presence of `peer_ref` links that complicate the naming.

Key technical points:
- Patrick sees `struct reference` as a foundational structure for the refs subsystem, analogous to `struct object` in the object database.
- He envisions future interfaces like `refs_read_ref()` adopting this structure to provide a unified way to handle references with metadata.
- The discussion reveals that `peer_ref` links in `struct ref` complicate any potential rename to `remote_ref`, as they connect both local and remote refs.

The exchange highlights an ongoing tension between naming clarity and the practical challenges of refactoring widely-used structures. Patrick remains open to alternative naming suggestions but maintains that establishing a central reference structure could benefit the codebase's future evolution. The thread continues to explore the balance between specificity and generality in core data structures.
