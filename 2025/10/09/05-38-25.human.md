This is a post-merge discussion about naming in the recently completed refs modernization series. Jeff King (peff@peff.net) responds to Patrick Steinhardt's series summary, praising the removal of the `peel_iterated_object()` hack but raising a concern about the new `struct reference` name being too generic. He suggests more specific alternatives like `iterated_ref` or `each_ref_data` that better indicate the struct's role in the iteration interface.

While the series has already been merged, Jeff flags this as a potential future source of confusion given how frequently "reference" and "ref" terminology appears in the codebase. The suggestion is framed as a minor stylistic improvement rather than a functional issue, acknowledging it would require annoying follow-up changes but might prevent confusion down the line. This continues the thread's post-merge discussion of naming conventions, following earlier debates about field names and verification philosophy.
