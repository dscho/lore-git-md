Packfile iteration refactoring follow-up. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's feedback on the pack iteration macro introduced in patch 5/6 of their series. The key change is agreeing to rename `packfile_store_for_each_pack()` to `repo_for_each_pack()` as suggested, since iteration typically spans all pack stores rather than targeting specific ones. 

Patrick notes they won't BUG() on multiple pack stores (as that's often the desired behavior) but may need to BUG() for non-pack object stores in future pluggable ODB scenarios. They decline adding a `packfile_store_num_packs()` helper for now due to insufficient call sites, though the suggestion remains open for future consideration.

This continues the series' pattern of thoughtful API design discussions between the two maintainers, with Patrick implementing Taylor's naming suggestion while maintaining the technical approach. The exchange shows careful consideration of both current needs and future extensibility in the pack subsystem.
