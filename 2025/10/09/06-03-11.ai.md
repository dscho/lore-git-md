Security-focused follow-up discussion on signed/unsigned integer handling in string-list API.

Jeff King (peff@peff.net) responds to shejialuo's v3 patch converting string_list_find_insert_index() to use size_t, pointing out a subtle edge case in the lookup_prefix() function where Coverity flags a potential unsigned integer wrap when i-- is called on i=0. While acknowledging this is technically correct behavior (the loop exits before the wrapped value is used), he notes it illustrates the kind of subtlety that makes signed/unsigned conversions tricky to reason about.

This reinforces the security rationale behind the series' choice of size_t while demonstrating the practical challenges of working with unsigned types. The observation comes after the patch series was already approved, suggesting it as a teaching point rather than requesting changes. The discussion continues the thread's theme of balancing security considerations (favoring size_t's overflow protection) against developer ergonomics (where signed types may be more intuitive).
