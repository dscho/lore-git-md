This is a follow-up discussion in the refs modernization thread, focusing on naming conventions for ref-related data types. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's suggestion about renaming `struct reference` (introduced in the recently merged series) to something more specific like `iterated_ref`.

The key points:
- Jeff had raised concerns that `struct reference` is too generic and could cause confusion with the existing `struct ref`
- Patrick counters that `struct reference` actually represents the canonical reference concept, while `struct ref` is the misnamed one (being specific to remote contexts)
- He proposes renaming `struct ref` to `struct remote_ref` as an alternative solution
- Acknowledges this would be a widespread change affecting many callers

The discussion continues the post-merge debate about interface clarity, with Patrick offering a different perspective on where the naming problem actually lies. This suggests the naming question may require more discussion before reaching consensus.
