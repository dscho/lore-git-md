This is a post-merge discussion about naming in the recently completed refs modernization series, specifically focusing on the `struct reference` introduced in the ref iteration interface.

Jeff King (peff@peff.net) responds positively to Patrick Steinhardt's series that eliminated the `peel_iterated_object()` hack through refactoring, but raises a concern about the naming of the new `struct reference` that encapsulates ref iteration data. While praising the technical approach, Jeff suggests the name is too generic given how frequently "reference" and "ref" appear in the codebase, potentially causing confusion about when to use this struct versus other ref-related types. He proposes more specific alternatives like `iterated_ref` or `each_ref_data` that would better tie the struct to its iteration context.

This continues the post-merge naming discussion thread (previously about the `target` field name) but shifts focus to the container struct itself. The feedback is stylistic rather than functional, and comes from a maintainer perspective concerned with long-term codebase clarity. The suggestion would require changes across the 56 callers updated in the series, making it non-trivial to implement post-merge. The thread shows maintainers carefully considering interface design choices even after technical approval.
