This thread introduces configurable diff algorithm selection for `git blame`, allowing users to choose between Myers (current default), minimal, patience, and histogram algorithms. The changes maintain backward compatibility while enabling new behavior through both command-line (`--diff-algorithm`) and config (`diff.algorithm`) options. Comprehensive tests demonstrate how different algorithms produce meaningfully different blame results.
