This is part 5 of a 9-part refactoring series modernizing xdiff's data structures for Rust FFI compatibility. The patch splits the dual-purpose `ha` field in `xrecord_t` into two distinct fields: `line_hash` (a simple hash of the line content) and `minimal_perfect_hash` (a unique identifier assigned by the classifier). 

The change makes the code's intent clearer by separating concerns that were previously conflated - the straightforward hashing needed for line comparisons versus the unique identifiers used for diff processing. While the functionality remains the same, the split helps document that hash collisions between files are properly handled via `xdl_recmatch()`.
