Koji Nakamaru initially observed dramatic performance issues (like `git ls-tree` taking 27 minutes) and developed thread-safe wrappers that showed significant improvements in artificial test cases. Reviewers including Torsten Bögershausen and Jeff King (Peff) engaged with the proposal, with Peff particularly questioning whether the optimization addressed real-world usage patterns. Nakamaru's follow-up testing confirmed normal Git operations show minimal `mmap` activity (just 9 calls versus millions in the constrained configuration), making the optimization unnecessary for standard usage.

In the final exchange, Peff acknowledges the findings validate that the original issue was configuration-specific, though he finds the macOS kernel behavior observations interesting as background knowledge. The thread concludes with consensus that proper repository configuration (avoiding artificially small memory limits) and regular maintenance (`git gc`) are the right solutions, rather than implementing `munmap` queueing. Nakamaru has withdrawn the patch after incorporating some documentation improvements suggested during review.
