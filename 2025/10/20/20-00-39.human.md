Documentation refinement for `git reset` man page's introductory section. This is part of the final polishing stage of Julia Evans' 4-patch series restructuring the `git reset` documentation.

The discussion focuses on finding the clearest way to introduce `git reset`'s dual nature - its ability to either change HEAD's commit reference or update the index without moving HEAD. Julia proposes avoiding circular definitions ("reset means reset") by using "Set HEAD or the index to a previous state," though Junio notes the state isn't always previous. They debate terminology for describing these two behaviors, with Junio preferring "modes" (despite its overloaded meaning in this context) for its implication of mutual exclusivity, while Julia tentatively suggests "things."
