Refactoring patch in a series consolidating refs optimization code. This is part 2 of 3 in Karthik Nayak's series to clean up redundant code paths in the refs subsystem.

The patch renames `pack_refs_opts` to `refs_optimize_opts` and updates all related flag names (e.g., `PACK_REFS_PRUNE` becomes `REFS_OPTIMIZE_PRUNE`). This follows the first patch's removal of the `pack_refs()` function, continuing the transition to using only the more generic `optimize` interface. The changes are mechanical and touch multiple files across the refs backend implementations (files, packed, reftable) to maintain naming consistency.

The patch appears straightforward and well-executed, with no behavior changes - just renaming to reflect the current architecture where all packing operations go through the `optimize` path. It's a logical next step in the series after removing the old `pack_refs` interface.
