The change simplifies `get_ssh_key_fingerprint()` by eliminating intermediate strbuf allocations when processing `ssh-keygen` output. Instead of splitting the output into temporary strbufs just to extract one token, it now directly finds and copies the needed fingerprint portion. The security checks remain intact while making the code more straightforward.
