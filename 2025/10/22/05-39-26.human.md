Performance optimization in the ongoing trailer refactoring series. This patch simplifies output handling in `interpret-trailers` by using `strbuf_write()` directly to stdout instead of a manual `fwrite()`, eliminating a redundant buffer copy. The change is part of a larger effort to move trailer processing into Git's core and improve efficiency. The patch is small and focused, with a clear explanation of its benefit. As part of the well-reviewed v5 series, it appears ready for inclusion.
