Performance optimization patch completing a refactoring series, now queued for integration.

This final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 14-part series introduces lazy object parsing in `ref-filter.c` to avoid unnecessary work when formatting refs. The key addition is a new `get_or_parse_object()` helper that conditionally parses objects only when needed, caching the result in a new `maybe_object` field of `struct expand_data`. Junio Hamano notes the implementation could be slightly more ergonomic by simplifying the calling pattern seen in three places, but considers this a minor polish rather than a blocker.

The change aligns with `ref-filter.c`'s original goal of minimizing work, and benchmarks show a 13% speedup for `git for-each-ref --format='%(raw)'` operations. With positive review from Karthik Nayak and now Junio's administrative handling, this performance optimization caps off the series' progression from architectural refactoring through to measurable improvements.
