This is a performance optimization withdrawal notice for macOS. Koji Nakamaru is formally withdrawing his earlier patch that proposed queueing munmap operations on macOS to reduce system overhead from frequent mmap/munmap alternation. The thread summary shows this was originally motivated by dramatic performance improvements in artificial test cases (reducing `git ls-tree` sys time from 27 minutes to 24 seconds in one extreme case).

However, Nakamaru discovered the extreme mmap counts were caused by an unusually small `packedGitLimit = 128m` setting in his local configuration, not by general Git behavior. Normal operations show only minimal mmap activity (9 calls vs 3 million with the restrictive config), making the optimization unnecessary for standard usage. The withdrawal validates Jeff King's earlier concerns that the performance issue was configuration-induced rather than inherent to Git's macOS implementation.

The email includes Nakamaru's responses to Torsten Bögershausen's documentation-focused review suggestions, agreeing with proposed improvements to naming and Makefile structure, though these changes are now moot. The thread concludes with consensus that proper repository maintenance (`git gc`) and configuration are the appropriate solutions rather than munmap queueing.
