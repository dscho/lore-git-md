This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing trailer API series, specifically part 4 of 29 in the 5th iteration. The change moves the initialization of two linked list heads (`config_head` and `arg_head`) inside a conditional block where they're actually needed, rather than declaring them at function scope. This is a tiny but sensible optimization that avoids allocating these structures when they won't be used (in `only_input` mode).

The patch is straightforward and mechanical - it doesn't change any behavior, just moves declarations to where they're needed. This continues the series' trend of cleaning up the internal trailer API. Given this is the 5th iteration of the series and the change is simple and well-contained, it appears ready for inclusion. The commit message clearly states the scope and non-functional nature of the change.
