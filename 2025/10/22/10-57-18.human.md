This is a review response to the final version of Patrick Steinhardt's refactoring and performance optimization series for tag peeling and ref handling. The series has evolved through three iterations, with v3 now incorporating feedback and resolving merge conflicts with other in-progress topics.

Karthik Nayak confirms the changes from v2 to v3 look good, having previously reviewed version 1. The series began as a simple performance optimization for `git-for-each-ref` but grew into a comprehensive refactoring that:
1. Eliminates the confusing `peel_iterated_object()` hack
2. Fixes issues with tag verification where object types might not match their stored OIDs
3. Adds backend validation to prevent storing mismatched peeled OIDs
4. Concludes with the original performance goal - lazy object parsing that provides a 13% speedup

While Patrick expresses some reservations about the tag verification approach in patches 11-13 (calling it "whack-a-mole"), the series appears ready for merging with this positive review. The changes maintain robustness while delivering measurable performance improvements, completing a significant modernization of the refs subsystem.
