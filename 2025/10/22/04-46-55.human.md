This is a follow-up in the `git diff --quiet` regression thread, focusing on a technical refinement to the proposed fix. Lidong Yan points out that the current solution in `diff_flush_patch_quietly()` permanently redirects output to `/dev/null` after its execution, which could affect subsequent operations. The patch currently sets `o->file = xfopen("/dev/null", "w")` but doesn't restore the original file handle, potentially causing issues for any later diff operations that might need to produce output.

The observation is technically sound but appears to be a minor oversight in an otherwise well-reasoned fix that has already received positive feedback from Junio Hamano. The thread has established that redirecting to `/dev/null` is the right approach for quiet mode, but this note correctly identifies that the redirection should be scoped more carefully. This is the kind of refinement one expects during final polish of a patch series.
