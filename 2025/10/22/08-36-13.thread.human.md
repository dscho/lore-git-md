This thread addresses a bugfix for `git bisect` that corrects its handling of subcommands, particularly around invalid inputs and help text display. The issue stemmed from regressions introduced during the 2022 builtin conversion, where unrecognized commands were incorrectly processed through the state machine rather than being rejected or showing help text. Ruoyu Zhong originally reported the issue and submitted the patch.

The fix modifies `cmd_bisect__helper()` to explicitly handle the `help` subcommand and properly reject invalid commands while maintaining backward compatibility with configured term aliases (like accepting both "bad" and "new"). Junio Hamano has approved the patch after thorough review, confirming that the validation logic—combining `check_and_set_terms()` with `one_of()` checks—correctly handles all cases. The discussion has now concluded with agreement between the author and maintainer on the solution's correctness, and the patch is ready for merging as-is.

While there was some consideration of broader subcommand infrastructure improvements (like using `OPT_SUBCOMMAND`), these were explicitly deferred to potential future work to keep the current fix focused and maintain backward compatibility. The thread shows clear consensus that the immediate bugs are properly addressed while leaving room for possible follow-up enhancements to the subcommand handling system.
