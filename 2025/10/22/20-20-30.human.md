This is a follow-up discussion confirming the approval of a bugfix for `git bisect`'s subcommand handling. The patch, originally submitted by Ruoyu Zhong, addresses two issues: restoring proper help text display for `git bisect help` and correctly rejecting invalid subcommands instead of processing them through the state machine.

Maintainer Junio Hamano has carefully reviewed the validation logic and confirmed its correctness, particularly how it handles custom terms (like `new`/`old` instead of `good`/`bad`) while properly rejecting truly invalid commands. The discussion reveals that while there are potential cleanups possible (like treating `help` as a proper subcommand), both author and maintainer agree the current approach is the right balance for this bugfix.

The email serves as final confirmation that the patch is ready for merging as-is, with any broader subcommand infrastructure improvements deferred to potential future work. This concludes the thread that began with a reported regression from Git's 2022 builtin conversion effort.
