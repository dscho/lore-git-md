Feature patch introducing a new "geometric" maintenance strategy, completing the geometric repacking series. This final patch in the v4 series adds a third maintenance option alongside "gc" and "incremental" strategies, specifically designed for large repositories.

The geometric strategy combines the full cleanup capabilities of "gc" (reflog expiration, worktree pruning) with geometric repacking's scalability benefits. Key technical aspects include:
- New `geometric_strategy` struct defining task schedules (hourly commit-graph, daily repack/pack-refs, weekly reflog/worktree tasks)
- Modified `parse_maintenance_strategy()` to recognize the new option
- Documentation updates explaining the tradeoffs (delayed unreachable object pruning vs. scalability)
- Test coverage verifying both manual and scheduled execution paths
- Hybrid approach that falls back to full GC when geometric repack would consolidate all packs anyway

The strategy addresses limitations of both existing approaches - "gc"'s inflexibility and "incremental"'s inability to prune stale data. It represents the culmination of the series' goal to provide configurable, scalable maintenance for large repos while maintaining backward compatibility. The patch follows the established pattern from prior strategy implementations while adding the geometric-specific behavior described in the series' earlier technical groundwork.
