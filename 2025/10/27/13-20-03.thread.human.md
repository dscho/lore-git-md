Alice Carlotti has identified and fixed a bug in Git's whitespace highlighting where trailing whitespace errors (WS_BLANK_AT_EOF) weren't being detected in certain edge cases - specifically when the final diff hunk had either zero old lines (as with new files) or zero new lines (as with deleted files). The issue stems from how diff hunk headers represent empty ranges differently between empty files (0,0) and non-empty files (1,N).

The proposed patch modifies `diff.c:find_lno()` to properly handle these zero-length hunks. Alice notes there's similar range parsing logic in `apply.c` that might be reusable, though the current solution focuses specifically on the whitespace highlighting case. The analysis shows good understanding of Git's diff machinery and the patch appears well-targeted to fix this specific whitespace detection issue without broader impacts.
