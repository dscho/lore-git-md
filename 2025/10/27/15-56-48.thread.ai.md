Randall Becker reports test failures on NonStop systems when building with OpenSSL 3.5, specifically in t7900 maintenance tests involving `test_subcommand` assertions with negations (`test_subcommand !`). The root cause is a shell selection divergence: Make uses `/bin/bash` for recipes while test scripts' `#!/bin/sh` shebang invokes ksh on NonStop. Jeff King's proposed `TEST_SHELL_PATH=/bin/bash` workaround has been confirmed effective by Randall Becker, resolving the immediate test failures by ensuring consistent bash usage across Make recipes and test execution. Randall notes this addresses the `/bin/sh`->ksh mapping divergence on NonStop systems but indicates other ksh-related issues with Make's `SHELL` setting may surface in other tests.

This proposal comes in the context of multiple issues being found in recent maintenance releases (v2.51.1 and v2.51.2), though the specific NonStop test failures have already been addressed via the `TEST_SHELL_PATH` workaround.
