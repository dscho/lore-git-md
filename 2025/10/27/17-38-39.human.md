This suggests the root cause lies deeper in how the GPG wrapper's timestamp control interacts with the test environment.

The email includes technical details about the test environment (GPG 2.4.8, libgcrypt 1.11.2) and demonstrates the specific test failure output where a commit's SHA-1 hash verification produces different results than expected. The discussion remains focused on diagnosing why the timestamp-controlled GPG wrapper isn't producing consistent signatures across environments, with no clear solution yet identified.
