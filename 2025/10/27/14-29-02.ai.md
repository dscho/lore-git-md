This is a follow-up discussion in the bugfix thread about Git's `**` pattern matching behavior. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about potential correctness issues with the proposed "minusone" solution that preserves one character of context during prefix stripping in `match_pathname()`.

Key technical points:
- Jeff argues that stepping back one character (to preserve context for `fnmatch()`) should not introduce new matching problems since it provides more matching context, not less
- He notes the solution maintains the invariant that the stepped-back character already matches in both pattern and name
- The discussion reveals Junio's earlier suggestion to limit the fix to `**/` patterns was correctness-driven rather than performance-motivated
- Jeff acknowledges but does not investigate a separate backslash-handling bug mentioned in the original report, considering it unrelated to this optimization

The email shows the technical refinement of the solution, with Jeff providing rationale for why the general "minusone" approach shouldn't introduce new matching edge cases. This builds on the earlier benchmarking that showed the approach preserved optimization benefits while fixing the `foo**/bar` matching bug. The backslash issue is noted as a potential future direction but deliberately scoped out of this fix.
