Test fix for the geometric repacking maintenance series.

Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano collaborate on a final tweak to the test suite for the recently merged geometric repacking feature. The test was flaky because `git repack` always regenerates the MIDX file (multi-pack index) even when no packs have changed, causing timestamp differences that made the test fail. The fix modifies the test to only compare *.pack files rather than the entire pack directory, sidestepping the MIDX issue.

This is a minor but important stabilization of the test suite. The underlying MIDX regeneration behavior is noted as a known issue that could be optimized in the future, but since it's not specific to this series, the test workaround is deemed sufficient for now. The patch carries both authors' sign-offs and represents the final polish on this feature before it graduates from the 'next' branch.
