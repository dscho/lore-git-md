This is the final patch in a series introducing a new "geometric" maintenance strategy, completing the work to provide a modern alternative to Git's traditional garbage collection approach. The patch adds the geometric strategy as a configurable option that combines the benefits of geometric repacking (which only does full repacks when truly needed) with the full cleanup capabilities of `git gc`.

The geometric strategy performs all the standard maintenance tasks - reflogs, worktree pruning, rerere cleanup - but uses geometric repacking instead of always doing full repacks. It intelligently falls back to full repacks (with cruft pack generation) when geometric repacking would effectively do the same thing anyway. This provides better performance for large repositories while still maintaining repository health.

The implementation includes documentation updates explaining the new option and comprehensive test coverage verifying the strategy's behavior for both manual and scheduled maintenance. The strategy is positioned as particularly suitable for large repositories where the overhead of frequent full repacks is significant.

This patch appears well-polished, with clear commit message explaining the tradeoffs and thorough test coverage. It builds on the earlier patches in the series that established the geometric repacking infrastructure and strategy framework. With maintainer approval already given for the series, this final piece looks ready to provide users with a valuable new maintenance option.
