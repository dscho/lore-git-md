Bugfix for whitespace highlighting in diffs. Alice Carlotti identifies an edge case where Git fails to highlight whitespace errors (WS_BLANK_AT_EOF) when the final diff hunk has either zero old lines or zero new lines, occurring with new files or `-U0` output. The root cause is in `diff.c:find_lno()` handling of zero-length ranges (0,0 vs 1,N). The patch proposes adjusting line number calculations for zero-length hunks, noting similar logic in `apply.c:parse_range()` could potentially be reused. The analysis shows awareness of historical hunk header numbering decisions. The focused fix targets this specific whitespace detection edge case while raising questions about potential interactions with apply machinery's parsing logic.
