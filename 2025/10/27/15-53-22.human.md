This is a test fix for the geometric repacking maintenance series. The patch addresses a flaky test in t7900-maintenance.sh where `git repack` was unnecessarily regenerating MIDX files even when no packs had changed, causing timestamp differences that made the test fail. The fix modifies the test to only compare *.pack files rather than the entire pack directory, working around the MIDX regeneration issue.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial - it's a mechanical test adjustment that doesn't affect the actual geometric repacking functionality. Junio Hamano is applying it directly since it was identified as needed in the v4 review but wasn't included in time. The patch maintains the test's purpose of verifying no-op geometric repacks while being more robust against MIDX-related timestamp changes.

This is a minor follow-up to the larger geometric repacking series that has already received maintainer approval, addressing a test stability issue that was noted during review. The core functionality remains unchanged.
