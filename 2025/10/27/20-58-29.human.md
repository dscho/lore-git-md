Test fix for the geometric repacking maintenance series.

This is a follow-up patch addressing a flaky test in the recently merged geometric repacking maintenance feature. The issue occurs because `git repack` always regenerates the MIDX file (multi-pack index) even when no packs have changed, causing timestamp differences that make the test fail intermittently.

The fix modifies the test to only compare *.pack files rather than the entire pack directory contents, avoiding the MIDX timestamp issue. While this papers over the symptom rather than fixing the root cause (MIDX regeneration in no-op cases), the patch notes this is a pre-existing issue not introduced by the geometric repacking work.

The change follows the pattern used in similar maintenance task tests and has been reviewed by both the original author and the maintainer. It represents the final stabilization needed for the geometric repacking series, which had already received multiple rounds of review and was merged to 'next'. The MIDX behavior remains a known optimization opportunity for future work.
