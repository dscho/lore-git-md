This patch extends the geometric repacking maintenance series by making the "gc" strategy explicitly configurable. It addresses two limitations in the current implementation: the inability to override a global strategy with the default "gc" strategy for specific repositories, and the lack of "gc" task support in scheduled maintenance.

The key changes include:
- Adding "gc" strategy documentation alongside "none" and "incremental" in maintenance.adoc
- Modifying the default strategy to run `git gc` for both manual and scheduled maintenance (via `MAINTENANCE_TYPE_SCHEDULED` bit)
- Updating `parse_maintenance_strategy()` to recognize the "gc" strategy
- Extending test coverage in t7900-maintenance.sh to verify the "gc" strategy works in both manual and scheduled contexts

The patch maintains backward compatibility while completing the strategy system's coverage of maintenance operations. It follows the series' pattern of using trace2 logs to verify exact command execution in tests. This appears to be a straightforward extension of the existing strategy framework, with no apparent technical concerns or open questions.
