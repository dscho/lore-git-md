This is a follow-up discussion in the `git-history` feature series, specifically about the "reword" subcommand implementation. Patrick Steinhardt responds to feedback from D. Ben Knoble and Junio Hamano regarding whether merge commits should be supported in the initial version.

The key points:
- Junio had questioned why the reword implementation uses the "pick" machinery when it could be simpler, and raised concerns about merge commit support
- D. Ben Knoble noted that Jujutsu (the inspiration for this feature) allows rewording merge commits
- Patrick agrees merge support would be nice but proposes deferring it to keep the initial implementation focused on simpler cases

This continues the thread's pattern of making pragmatic choices to scope the initial feature while leaving room for future expansion. The discussion shows the team balancing between completeness and incremental delivery, with Patrick opting for the latter approach here. No code changes are proposed in this email - it's purely a design discussion response.
