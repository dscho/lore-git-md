This is a follow-up in the thread about fixing intermittent test failures in GPG signature verification. Todd Zullinger confirms Eric W. Biederman's discovery that adding an exclamation point to GPG's `--faked-system-time` parameter was the key to making SHA-1 signatures reproducible. 

Zullinger reports successful verification of the fix in the Fedora build system, where the test failures had been consistently reproducible before. He notes this explains earlier difficulties where GPG wrapper behavior seemed inconsistent, even when the wrapper was being used correctly. 

The email serves as positive confirmation of the solution from another independent tester, reinforcing that the obscure GPG timestamp syntax was indeed the root cause of the flaky tests. No new technical details are added, but the real-world validation from a different build environment provides additional confidence in the fix.
