This is part 9/10 of Ezekiel Newren's series modernizing xdiff data types for Rust FFI compatibility, specifically converting the `rindex` field in `xdfile_t` from `long` to `size_t`. 

The change is straightforward - `rindex` is used as an array index, so `size_t` is the more appropriate type. The patch notes this won't require any cascading changes since the field is used directly for array access. This follows the pattern established in earlier patches of the series where similar size-related fields were converted to `rindex` had already been renamed to `reference_index` in a previous patch, so this completes its type modernization.

The series as a whole has already been reviewed and approved, with this patch being one of the final cleanups in the effort to make xdiff's data structures more type-safe and Rust-compatible. The change is small and uncontroversial, maintaining the same behavior while improving type correctness.
