This thread is a proposal from Bello Olamide for an Outreachy internship focused on continuing Git's `the_repository` removal effort. The goal is to refactor remaining global variables in `environment.c` (like `git_work_tree_cfg` and `is_bare_repository_cfg`) into `struct repository` and `struct repo-settings`, following the pattern established by Patrick Steinhardt and others who moved `core.hooksPath` and `core.sharedRepository` into repository-local storage.

Bello has prior experience with a merged microproject cleaning up `strbuf_split*()` usage and demonstrates solid understanding of the codebase, though this would be their first major open source contribution. The proposed timeline spans from code study (Now-Dec 2025) through implementation (Jan-Feb 2026), with plans to create new accessor functions, update callers, and maintain test coverage. While the proposal is thorough, it could be strengthened with more specific examples of untouched variables that would be good candidates for refactoring. Success would meaningfully advance Git's maintainability and multi-repository support capabilities.
