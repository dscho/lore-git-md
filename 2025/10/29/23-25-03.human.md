This is a discussion about patch 7/8 in the ongoing packfile store abstraction series, focusing on MRU (most-recently-used) packfile list management. Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that makes packfile MRU list additions consistent by always adding packs to the MRU when they're first added to the store.

He acknowledges this would be a larger change since the current system only tracks packs that are already in the MRU.

The exchange shows careful consideration of the MRU management strategy as part of the broader packfile store refactoring. Taylor gives his approval for the immediate change while planting a seed for possible future optimization.
