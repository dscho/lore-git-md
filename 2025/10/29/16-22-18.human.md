The discussion builds on prior thread consensus that all worktrees must share the same ref storage format, but differs on how strictly to enforce the "run from main worktree" requirement. Ben sides with Kristoffer's UX-focused perspective against Patrick's concern about potential user surprise, suggesting the automatic switch could be implemented with trace output for transparency.
