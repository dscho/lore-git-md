Feature patch introducing a new "geometric" maintenance strategy for Git repositories, particularly targeting large monorepos. This is the final patch in an 8-part series that has been progressively building up the infrastructure for geometric repacking strategies.

The patch adds a new `geometric` strategy that combines the full cleanup capabilities of `git gc` (pruning reflogs, rerere caches, stale worktrees) with geometric repacking via `git repack --geometric=2`. The key innovation is its hybrid approach: when a geometric repack would merge all packfiles anyway, it falls back to an all-into-one repack with cruft packs to handle unreachable objects. This addresses the main limitation of geometric repacking (accumulation of unreachable objects) while maintaining its scaling benefits.

The implementation adds the strategy definition in builtin/gc.c with appropriate task scheduling (hourly commit-graph updates, daily geometric repacks, weekly maintenance tasks), documents it in maintenance.adoc as a recommended choice for large repositories, and extends the test suite in t7900-maintenance.sh to verify its behavior. The strategy is available for both manual and scheduled maintenance runs.

This completes the series' goal of providing a scalable alternative to traditional `git gc` that better handles large repositories while maintaining data integrity. The patch maintains the series' pattern of thorough testing and documentation while introducing the final user-facing component.
