This is a refactoring patch in a series standardizing reference optimization terminology in Git's refs subsystem. It renames `pack_refs_opts` to `refs_optimize_opts` and updates all related flag names (`PACK_REFS_PRUNE` to `REFS_OPTIMIZE_PRUNE` etc.) across multiple files to maintain consistency after the transition from `pack_refs` to `optimize` terminology.

The patch touches core refs infrastructure including header files, backend implementations (files, packed, reftable, debug), and the pack-refs command. Justin Tobler suggested additional minor cleanups in the variable naming (changing `pack_refs_opts` to `refs_optimize_opts` in the local variable declaration, and `pack_opts` to just `opts` in the packed backend), which Karthik Nayak agrees to incorporate in a re-roll.

This continues the mechanical renaming work started in earlier patches of the series, with no behavior changes. The changes are straightforward and uncontroversial, having already received positive review from Patrick Steinhardt for the overall series. The patch demonstrates good attention to detail by updating all related instances of the terminology, including documentation strings and unused parameters.
