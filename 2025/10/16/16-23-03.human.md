Junio C Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about deprecating the `--committer-date-is-author-date` option in `git-am` and `git-rebase`. The thread has reached consensus that the original use case (using `git-am` to pseudo-import commits while preserving object IDs) was fundamentally flawed, as a mailbox of patches isn't a suitable commit storage mechanism.

Hamano suggests two possible approaches: either officially deprecate and schedule the option for removal (if the community is confident it should be used), or simply document why the original use case doesn't make sense while leaving the option available. He personally favors the latter, proposing to explain the option's problematic history in WARNING/NOTES sections without an outright "do not use" directive, letting users decide whether to use it despite the risks.

This email represents the final stages of the discussion, focusing on communication strategy rather than technical debate. The core technical position is settled - the option enables workflows that violate Git's object model assumptions. The remaining question is how strongly to phrase the documentation warnings.
