This could preserve archive provenance while still meeting RFC uniqueness requirements. The core tension remains between supporting kernel workflows (which need header-based storage) versus maintaining robust archive links and history operations (which favor trailers or alternative approaches).

No consensus has emerged yet, but the thread is converging on recognizing archive traceability as a critical requirement. The path forward likely needs to either modify the header storage approach to preserve archive links or find a way to make trailers work in kernel workflows.
