Feature: New "geometric" maintenance strategy for large repositories.

This final patch in the series introduces the new "geometric" maintenance strategy, which aims to provide a better alternative to both the traditional "gc" and "incremental" strategies, particularly for large repositories. The strategy combines geometric repacking (using `git repack --geometric=2`) with the full suite of maintenance tasks like reflog expiration and worktree pruning.

Key characteristics of the geometric strategy:
- Performs geometric repacking by default, only falling back to all-into-one repacks with cruft packs when packs would be completely merged
- Maintains all auxiliary data structures (commit-graph, reflogs, etc.)
- Runs all standard maintenance tasks on appropriate schedules (hourly, daily, weekly)
- Designed as a full replacement for the "gc" strategy, particularly recommended for large repositories

The patch adds documentation explaining the strategy and its tradeoffs (notably that unreachable objects may persist longer than with "gc"), implements the strategy in builtin/gc.c, and extends the test suite to verify its behavior. This completes the series' goal of providing more flexible and scalable maintenance options, with the geometric strategy positioned as the new recommended approach for large repositories.
