This is a discussion in the ongoing thread about adding Message-ID tracking to Git commits, specifically for email-based workflows like the Linux kernel.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk clarifies their earlier reference to Change-ID systems, noting that while the two concepts serve different purposes (Change-ID tracks changes across commits in Git-native workflows, while Message-ID links commits to mailing list discussions), they share the technical approach of using commit headers for implementation.

James Bottomley had previously explained that Message-ID functionality is specifically needed for email-based CI tooling, where commits originate from mailing list discussions and need to maintain that linkage. Haugsbakk's follow-up emphasizes that their Change-ID reference was meant to highlight the header-based approach similarity, not to conflate the use cases.

The exchange helps refine the scope of the proposed Message-ID feature by distinguishing it from prior Change-ID work, reinforcing that these are complementary solutions for different workflow needs. The technical implementation remains focused on email-centric automation while maintaining compatibility with existing trailer-based approaches.
