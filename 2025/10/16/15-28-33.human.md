This is a documentation discussion in the ongoing thread about deprecating the `--committer-date-is-author-date` option in `git-am` and `git-rebase`. Johannes Sixt responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's questions about how to structure the warning text in the documentation.

The key point is Johannes' strong stance that the original use case for this option (using `git-am` to repeatedly import patches while preserving commit IDs) is fundamentally flawed - he argues that a mailbox of patches shouldn't be treated as a commit storage mechanism. This reinforces the thread's emerging consensus that the option's very existence may be problematic rather than just needing better warnings.

On the documentation front, Johannes provides feedback on wording suggestions, preferring either a direct "Do not use this option" lead-in or keeping the warning standalone, but rejects parenthetical phrasing. The discussion remains focused on how to most effectively communicate the dangers of this option while the deeper technical questions about its validity continue to be debated.

This email moves the thread forward by solidifying the position that the option's core use case may be invalid, which will likely influence how strongly the documentation warns against it or whether it should be deprecated entirely.
