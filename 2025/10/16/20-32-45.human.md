Maintainer response to Message-ID tracking proposal. Junio Hamano weighs in on James Bottomley's series to store email Message-IDs in commit headers, expressing concerns about the approach.

While acknowledging the usefulness of the mailinfo changes and accepting Message-IDs in commit trailers, Hamano strongly objects to storing them in commit object headers. He cites maintenance headaches around rebases and cherry-picks as key concerns. The response also questions whether the implementation might break existing "format-patch | am" workflows by requiring Message-ID headers that wouldn't exist when rebasing old commits.

This represents the first substantive pushback on the technical implementation of the series, though Hamano hasn't yet reviewed the full patchset. The concerns focus on long-term maintenance burden and backward compatibility rather than the concept's merit. The thread now faces a key decision point between the proposed header-based approach and potentially limiting the feature to trailers only.
