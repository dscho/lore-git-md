This is a clarification email in the Message-ID tracking feature proposal thread. James Bottomley responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's references to prior Change-ID discussions, distinguishing the current Message-ID proposal from those approaches.

The key distinction made is that Change-ID systems (like Gerrit/Git Butler/Jujutsu) track changes across commits in Git-native workflows, while Message-ID tracking specifically serves email-based workflows by linking commits back to their originating discussion threads. James emphasizes that Message-IDs enable CI tooling in email-centric projects (like the Linux kernel) to maintain commit-to-email relationships, which Change-ID doesn't address since email-less workflows wouldn't have Message-IDs at all.

The email reinforces that these are orthogonal solutions serving different workflow models, with Message-ID tracking being particularly valuable for projects with extensive email-based automation (like tip bots that need to reference the original discussion thread). This clarification helps frame the current proposal's scope against historical alternatives mentioned in the thread.
