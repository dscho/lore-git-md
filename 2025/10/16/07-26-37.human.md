This is a feature patch in the ongoing geometric maintenance strategy series, extending the `maintenance.strategy` configuration to cover manual maintenance runs in addition to scheduled ones.

The patch addresses a current limitation where maintenance strategies only applied to scheduled runs, while manual maintenance would default to `git gc`. This could lead to conflicting strategies between scheduled and manual runs. The change makes strategies apply globally while maintaining backward compatibility - the "incremental" strategy will still skip garbage collection during manual runs to match historical behavior.

Key changes include updating documentation to clarify the expanded scope of strategies, modifying the strategy initialization logic in builtin/gc.c to check the strategy for both manual and scheduled runs, and adding tests to verify the new behavior. The implementation carefully preserves existing behavior for users who had configured the incremental strategy, demonstrating thoughtful attention to backward compatibility.

This is a logical next step in the series, building on the previous infrastructure for maintenance types while making the strategy system more consistent and useful. The patch includes thorough test coverage and clear documentation updates, showing it's well-prepared for review.
