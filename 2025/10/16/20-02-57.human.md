This is a documentation and build system patch series addressing formatting inconsistencies in Git's technical documentation. It's the third iteration of Ramsay Jones' effort to clean up asciidoc warnings and align the Makefile build with the meson build.

The series focuses on fixing formatting issues in several technical documents (`remembering-renames.adoc`, `sparse-checkout.adoc`, `commit-graph.adoc`) and adds `large-object-promisors.adoc` to the Makefile build. The changes address warnings about list numbering, section heading levels, and monospaced text formatting, though some tricky formatting problems remain unresolved (particularly with monospaced text containing curly braces and URL encoding issues).

Ramsay acknowledges needing help from asciidoc experts to fully resolve all formatting quirks, particularly around special characters in monospaced text and URL encoding. The patches represent incremental improvements even with these outstanding issues. The series builds on previous work that was partially merged into the 'next' branch, with Junio Hamano clarifying that these remaining patches can proceed independently.

The changes are mechanical formatting fixes that don't alter content, though they do reveal some inconsistencies between asciidoc and asciidoctor behavior. The patch series shows careful attention to detail and thorough testing across different documentation processors, though the author humorously notes their "intense dislike" of both asciidoc and asciidoctor after wrestling with these formatting challenges.
