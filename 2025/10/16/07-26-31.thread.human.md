This thread has progressed through four iterations of a series introducing configurable maintenance strategies with geometric repacking, culminating in the addition of a new "geometric" strategy option. The work, primarily by Taylor Blau with reviews from Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt, has now reached a stable state with maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano.

The series implements a flexible system where maintenance tasks (like repacking) can be controlled through different strategies - originally "gc" and "incremental", now extended with the new "geometric" approach. The geometric strategy is designed for large repositories, combining the thorough cleanup of "gc" (reflog expiration, worktree pruning) with the scalability benefits of geometric repacking. It schedules different tasks at appropriate intervals - hourly commit-graph updates, daily repacking and pack-refs, and weekly reflog cleanup.

Technical refinements throughout the series include new configuration options like `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor`, type-based task execution control, and careful handling of backward compatibility. The implementation uses stack-allocated strvecs instead of globals and provides comprehensive test coverage. While one edge case regarding unnecessary MIDX updates was identified (and will be addressed separately), the consensus is that the series is production-ready. The final patch completes the vision by making the geometric strategy available alongside the existing options, with clear explanations of the tradeoffs in the documentation.
