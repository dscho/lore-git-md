This thread covers the geometric repacking maintenance series, now in its fourth iteration, which introduces configurable maintenance strategies including a new "geometric" option. The series has received maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano and ack from Taylor Blau, with all substantive feedback addressed. The implementation provides three maintenance strategies ("gc", "incremental", and "geometric") with type-based execution control and configurable split factors.

A recent discussion emerged about a test race condition where `git-repack` unnecessarily regenerates MIDX files even when no packs have changed. Both Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt agree this is a `git-repack` issue rather than a problem with the maintenance series itself. While Patrick proposed a potential fix, they consider this an optimization to be handled separately. The series remains production-ready, with this edge case representing a known but non-blocking issue that may be addressed in follow-up work. The geometric repack strategy combines full cleanup capabilities with scalable repacking, completing the series' goal of providing flexible maintenance options while maintaining backward compatibility.
