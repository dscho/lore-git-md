The geometric repacking maintenance task series, now in its fourth iteration, has reached a mature state with maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano and final Ack from Taylor Blau. The series implements configurable maintenance strategies with hybrid geometric repacking, including type-based task execution control and configurable split factors. Key technical elements involve the `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor` configuration and core logic in `maintenance_task_geometric_repack()`.

Patrick Steinhardt has contributed a refactoring patch in v4 series that eliminates the global `repack` variable in `builtin/gc.c`, replacing it with stack-allocated `strvec` instances passed through function parameters. This mechanical change improves code structure without modifying behavior, enabling cleaner reuse of repack argument-building logic in subsequent patches. The solution carefully handles edge cases around the dual use of `need_to_gc()` by both automatic and maintenance tasks.

While the series is ready for integration, some open items remain for follow-up work, including resolution of midx regeneration in no-op cases (identified by Jeff King and confirmed by Patrick as a git-repack issue) and potential optimizations of reachability marking and shared pack geometry calculations. The thread shows strong consensus on the series' technical soundness, with all substantive feedback incorporated in this final iteration.
