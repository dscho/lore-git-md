This thread has introduced and refined a new geometric repacking strategy for Git repository maintenance, with particular benefits for large repositories and monorepos. The series, which has seen multiple iterations, implements configurable maintenance strategies featuring a hybrid approach that combines incremental geometric repacks with periodic full repacks using cruft packs.

The latest patch completes the strategy configuration by adding "geometric" as a full replacement for both traditional "gc" and "incremental" approaches. The geometric strategy performs all standard cleanup tasks while intelligently switching between geometric repacking and full repacks with cruft packs when it would be more efficient. This balances performance with periodic deep cleaning of unreachable objects.

Key contributors Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt have reached consensus on the design, which has been reviewed and approved by Justin Tobler and Junio C Hamano. The implementation includes comprehensive test coverage (now in t7900-maintenance.sh) and maintains backward compatibility while introducing the new strategy option. Documentation has been updated to explain the tradeoffs and recommend the geometric strategy for large repositories.

The series represents a significant improvement in Git's maintenance capabilities, particularly for monorepos, and appears ready for merging.
