This is a CI infrastructure patch in the context of a larger series introducing Rust-C interoperability support via cbindgen. The patch adds two missing Linux CI jobs (AlmaLinux 8 and Debian 11) to GitLab CI to match GitHub's test matrix coverage. 

Ezekiel Newren reviews Patrick Steinhardt's patch and gives it a straightforward "Looks good" approval. The changes are minimal - just two additions to the job matrix in `.gitlab-ci.yml` and a corresponding update to the OS detection logic in `ci/lib.sh`. The new jobs will test Git on AlmaLinux 8 (a RHEL 8 compatible distro supported until 2029) and Debian 11 (supported until 2026).

This is part of the broader effort to ensure consistent testing across platforms as the project adds Rust integration. The patch appears uncontroversial and well-contained, addressing a clear gap in test coverage between the two CI systems. With Newren's approval, this seems ready to proceed as part of the series.
