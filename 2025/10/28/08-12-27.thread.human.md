Christian Couder's patch series to clean up and internationalize error messages in fast-export, fast-import, and gpg-interface has reached its final form. The five-part series started with two technical cleanups to GPG/SSH interface code, then systematically marked all remaining untranslated messages across these components. 

Junio C Hamano has now given his approval to the final three patches (v2 3-5) that complete the translation work. These include downcasing message initials, removing redundant prefixes, and making corresponding test adjustments - all implemented per earlier feedback. The changes maintain functionality while improving message consistency, with fast-import.c receiving particularly thorough treatment (270 strings wrapped).

With the maintainer's "looking good" on the final patches and no outstanding technical concerns, the series appears ready for merging. This completes a focused effort to make these components fully translation-ready while following Git's style guidelines. The work connects to broader internationalization efforts led by Jean-Noël Avila and Jiang Xin.
