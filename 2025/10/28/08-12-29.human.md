This is a small refactoring patch in Christian Couder's series combining code cleanups and translation work. It's the second of five patches, focusing on the gpg-interface component.

The patch standardizes how boolean constants are defined in gpg-interface.h, changing from direct numeric values (1, 2, 4) to left-shift operations (1<<0, 1<<1, 1<<2) for the GPG_VERIFY_* flags. This makes it clearer that these are bitwise flags while maintaining identical runtime behavior. The author also fixes a minor indentation issue with one of the existing definitions.

The change is straightforward and mechanical - it doesn't modify any functionality, just makes the code more consistent with Git's typical style for bit flags. As part of a larger series that includes more substantive changes (particularly around marking strings for translation), this patch stands as a simple cleanup that was apparently dropped from a previous series.

The patch appears well-formed and focused, though as a pure style change, it's unlikely to generate much discussion unless reviewers spot some subtle implication of the change that isn't immediately obvious.
