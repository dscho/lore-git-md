This thread documents a small but precise fix to the git-checkout manpage's AsciiDoc markup. The patch corrects two instances where branch name placeholders were incorrectly marked with backticks instead of underscores, which was causing unintended HTML formatting. The change follows up on commit 042d6f34 that originally introduced these examples.

The patch has already gone through two iterations, with version 2 receiving an Acked-by from Julia Evans, indicating the change has been reviewed and approved. While the author considered tweaking the commit message wording, they ultimately kept it as-is since the intent is clear. This straightforward documentation fix is now being submitted as a standalone patch after previously being part of a larger series.
