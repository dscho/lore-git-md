This is a refactoring series targeting Git's packfile subsystem, part of the ongoing effort to improve packfile management. Patrick Steinhardt proposes moving packfile list tracking into the packfile store, which is a preparatory step for deeper architectural changes to how Git handles object storage.

The series builds on recent work introducing the packfile store concept, now taking the next logical step by having it manage pack lists rather than using global linked lists. This paves the way for future per-object-source pack tracking. The changes touch several areas that interact with packfiles, including HTTP transports and pack-objects.

The most notable and potentially controversial change comes in the final patch, which consolidates two existing packfile lists (MRU and mtime-sorted) into just the MRU list. The author acknowledges this might be fragile due to potential infinite loops during iteration and has added a workaround, but invites extra scrutiny on this design decision.

The series appears well-structured and motivated, with cleanly separated commits that each tackle a specific aspect of the refactoring. Given the core nature of the packfile subsystem and the careful approach shown here, this would be worth attention from anyone working on Git object storage internals. The author's request for focused review on the MRU consolidation suggests that particular change may benefit from discussion before merging.
