This thread presents an 8-part refactoring of Git's packfile management system, part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing packfile store abstraction effort. The series systematically modernizes how Git tracks and accesses packfiles, with each patch building carefully on the last.

The first six patches laid the groundwork by replacing custom hashmap usage with `strmap`, introducing a new `packfile_list` API, fixing MIDX object counting, and moving core packfile management into `packfile_store`. The seventh patch standardized MRU (most-recently-used) list handling by making pack additions automatically update the MRU list.

The final patch, just submitted, completes the series by consolidating two separate packfile lists (one tracking age, one tracking usage) into a single MRU-ordered list. This simplification required careful handling of a potential infinite loop edge case during pack iteration, solved by introducing a `skip_mru_updates` flag. The changes touch sensitive areas of packfile.c/h and pack-objects.c but maintain all existing behavior while removing now-redundant MRU-specific functions.

The series as a whole demonstrates the project's careful approach to infrastructure changes - each patch is mechanical and focused, with clear explanations of both the changes and their rationale. The work positions the codebase well for future packfile store integration while already delivering cleaner, more maintainable code. With all eight patches now submitted, the series appears ready for maintainer consideration.
