This is the final patch in an 8-part refactoring series that consolidates Git's packfile management. The series has been systematically restructuring how packfiles are tracked as part of the packfile store abstraction effort.

The patch eliminates one of two redundant packfile tracking lists, keeping only the MRU (most-recently-used) list while removing the separate `packs` list that sorted packs by age. The commit message argues that MRU ordering better serves varied access patterns than the previous dual-list approach. A key technical challenge addressed is preventing infinite loops during pack traversal by introducing a `skip_mru_updates` flag.

The changes touch core packfile handling in `packfile.c` and the diff shows careful mechanical updates to maintain behavior while simplifying the infrastructure. This completes the series' goal of modernizing packfile management with cleaner abstractions. The patch appears well-considered, with clear attention to edge cases and behavioral consistency.
