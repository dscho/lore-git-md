This is a CI infrastructure patch in the ongoing cbindgen support series. Ezekiel Newren is reviewing Patrick Steinhardt's patch that adds missing Linux CI jobs to GitLab CI to match GitHub's test coverage. The changes add AlmaLinux 8 (RHEL-compatible) and Debian 11 jobs to GitLab CI, with corresponding updates to `ci/lib.sh` to recognize these platforms. The patch maintains the existing structure while expanding platform coverage, with Newren giving his approval ("Looks good"). This aligns with the series' broader goal of ensuring consistent testing across CI platforms as Rust integration infrastructure is added. The changes are straightforward and uncontroversial, focusing on test coverage parity between GitLab and GitHub CI systems.
