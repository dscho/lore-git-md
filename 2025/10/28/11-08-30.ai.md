This is a refactoring series targeting the packfile subsystem, part of the ongoing effort to restructure packfile storage. The series moves packfile list management into the packfile store, preparing for future changes to push the store into the object source layer.

The key technical changes involve replacing global linked lists with packfile store-owned structures. Patch 1 introduces a `strmap` for pack name lookups. Patches 2-6 systematically move list management (MRU and mtime-sorted) into the store while updating callers. The most notable change comes in patch 8, where the author consolidates the two packfile lists into just the MRU list, arguing the mtime-sorted list causes confusion and isn't needed.

The author flags patch 8 as needing extra review due to a potential infinite loop edge case when reading objects while iterating packs. They address this with a new field to skip MRU updates during iteration, but express some concern about fragility. The series builds on prior work renaming packfile store APIs and targets Git v2.51.2 as its base.

Key files affected include packfile.c/h (major restructuring), several HTTP-related files (caller updates), and pack-objects.c (simplified object finding logic). The changes touch core packfile infrastructure but appear to maintain existing behavior where intended, with the exception of the MRU/mtime list consolidation in the final patch.
