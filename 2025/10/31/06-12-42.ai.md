Refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series, moving the loose object cache into the new `struct odb_loose_source`. This continues the architectural separation work by relocating cache-related state (`subdir_seen` bitmap and `oidtree` cache) from `struct odb_source` to the loose-specific structure. The change includes renaming functions to `odb_loose_source_cache()` and updating all callers across object-file.c, loose.c, object-name.c, and odb.c. The patch maintains the existing behavior while better encapsulating loose object state, with the functions still taking `struct odb_source` as input to support future pluggable backends (where they'll internally cast to the loose-specific type). This appears to be a straightforward mechanical change in the ongoing effort to separate loose object handling from the core ODB interface.
