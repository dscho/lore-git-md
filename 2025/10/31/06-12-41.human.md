This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to improve Git's loose object handling as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The patch introduces a new `struct odb_loose_source` to contain all state related to loose objects, moving it out of the more general `struct odb_source`.

The change is a preparatory step toward making the loose object backend pluggable. It adds allocation and free functions for the new structure (`odb_loose_source_new()` and `odb_loose_source_free()`) and hooks them into the existing ODB infrastructure. The patch touches several key files including object-file.c, odb.c, and their headers.

Reviewer Karthik Nayak had previously suggested an alternative name (`obd_source_loose`) which Patrick considered but ultimately kept the current naming scheme in v2. The change appears well-contained and mechanically sound, continuing the series' pattern of careful, incremental refactoring while maintaining behavior. This is one of several patches in the series that have received positive technical feedback, with naming conventions being the primary point of discussion.
