This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to modernize Git's object database (ODB) abstraction. The patch focuses on updating function names to match the current naming scheme after a previous structural rename.

The changes rename two key functions in odb.c to better reflect their purpose and align with modern Git naming conventions:
1. `free_object_directory()` becomes `odb_source_free()`
2. `free_object_directories()` becomes `odb_free_sources()`

The motivation stems from the fact that these functions operate on `struct odb_source` (previously called `struct object_directory`), but their names hadn't been updated when the structure was renamed in commit a1e2581a1e. The new names follow Git's current naming pattern where the subject (`odb_source`) comes before the verb (`free`).

The patch makes straightforward mechanical changes across odb.c, updating all call sites to use the new function names. This is part of the larger effort to clean up and standardize the ODB interface, though this particular change is purely about naming consistency rather than functional modification.

The changes are minimal (5 lines modified) and focused, continuing the pattern seen in previous patches of the series where Patrick incrementally improves the codebase while maintaining existing behavior. This follows the positive reception of earlier patches in the series, with reviewers like Karthik Nayak having approved similar cleanups in previous rounds.
