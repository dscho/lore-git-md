**Technical discussion about SHA-256 support in Git bindings**

Michal Suchánek responds to Patrick Steinhardt's assumption that language bindings like pygit2 automatically inherit SHA-256 support from libgit2. He points out that bindings often require significant redesign to support new features, noting that pygit2 currently has no SHA-256 support despite libgit2's experimental implementation.

The email highlights potential compatibility challenges, suggesting that some bindings might need major version bumps (libgit3/pygit3) to properly support SHA-256, or may never gain support at all. This adds complexity to the ongoing SHA-256 transition planning, as it reveals another layer of ecosystem impact beyond core implementations.
