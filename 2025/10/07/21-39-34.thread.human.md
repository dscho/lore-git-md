This thread discusses proposed updates to Git's SubmittingPatches documentation, specifically around topic branch naming conventions and multi-series patch submission practices. Taylor Blau authored the two-part series with input from maintainer Junio Hamano, who had previously experimented with topic naming conventions in March 2024.

The first part, focusing on topic branch naming conventions (using XX/your-topic-name format), is now under reconsideration after Junio noted its limited adoption since the initial rollout. Kristoffer Haugsbakk has joined the discussion with specific suggestions to improve the proposal, recommending clearer formatting markers like "Topic summary:" to make these elements stand out in cover letters and potentially allow more flexible placement. He also raises questions about how the documentation handles co-authorship cases.

The second part, covering multi-series submission guidance, appears uncontested and may be ready for adoption. The discussion now centers on whether to refine, remove, or mark as experimental the topic naming portion, with Junio leaning toward viewing it as a failed experiment while Kristoffer proposes ways to potentially revive it through better visual distinction in the documentation.
