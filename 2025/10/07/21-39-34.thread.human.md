Taylor Blau proposed a two-part documentation update to Git's SubmittingPatches guidelines, formalizing experimental practices around topic branch naming conventions (using "XX/your-topic-name" format) and multi-series patch submission workflows. The initial patches had Junio Hamano's input via Suggested-by tags and appeared ready for inclusion.

However, Junio has now weighed in with an important observation - the topic naming convention introduced in March 2024 (commit d255105c99) hasn't seen much adoption in practice. He suggests this may indicate a failed experiment that should be retracted rather than promoted to documented status. This feedback specifically addresses the first part of the series while leaving the multi-series submission guidance untouched. The discussion now needs to determine whether to: (1) remove the topic naming documentation entirely, (2) keep it but mark it clearly as a discontinued experiment, or (3) find ways to encourage adoption before making a final call. The second part of the series about multi-series workflows remains uncontested.
