This is a discussion about the path encoding scheme for submodule gitdirs, part of an ongoing series to prevent filesystem conflicts. Adrian Ratiu responds to Junio Hamano's feedback about the encoding format, agreeing to switch from a custom encoding (where 'A' becomes '_a') to standard URL percent-encoding ('A' as %41). 

The key points are that URL encoding will work on case-insensitive filesystems (contrary to initial concerns) and avoids potential future versioning headaches by being a permanent solution. Adrian acknowledges Junio's point about avoiding temporary solutions that might require migration later, dropping the idea of supporting multiple encoding schemes in the future.

This email marks alignment on one of the last major open questions in the series - the encoding format - leaving primarily implementation details and documentation to resolve before the feature can be finalized. The discussion shows the series is nearing completion with all architectural decisions now settled.
