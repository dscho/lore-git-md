This thread documents a six-part series by Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt refactoring Git's internal packfile store API. The work systematically modernized how Git iterates over packfiles, first removing the deprecated `packfile_store_get_packs()` function, then converting callers to use `get_all_packs()`, and finally introducing a new `packfile_store_for_each_pack()` macro to standardize iteration patterns. The final part simplifies the API further by renaming `get_all_packs()` to just `get_packs()` now that the naming conflict is resolved.

The changes are internal quality-of-life improvements with no user-facing impact, touching pack operations, object lookup paths, and maintenance commands across 20+ files. The series demonstrates the authors' methodical approach to pack subsystem cleanup - each step builds on the last while maintaining identical behavior. With the final naming cleanup, the series concludes its goal of modernizing pack iteration patterns in the codebase, leaving no open questions or follow-ups needed. The changes align with the project's ongoing efforts to improve internal APIs while keeping the pack subsystem stable and maintainable.
