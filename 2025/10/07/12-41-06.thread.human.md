This thread covers a technical refactoring series from Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt that standardizes how Git iterates through packfiles internally. The series introduces cleaner iteration patterns while carefully preserving behavior, culminating in the removal of a deprecated API function. 

The work touches many parts of Git's pack subsystem but doesn't change user-facing behavior. Key changes include introducing a new `repo_for_each_pack()` macro that simplifies 20+ instances of pack iteration, and removing the confusing `packfile_store_get_packs()` function after migrating all callers. The series follows a methodical approach: first introducing better alternatives, converting callers, then removing old interfaces.
