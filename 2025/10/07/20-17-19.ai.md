Refactoring patch in the `tb/incremental-midx-part-3` series, responding to review feedback about avoiding global hash algorithm access.

This email is Taylor Blau's response to Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion on PATCH 07/49, which proposed using C99 designated initializers for the `write_oid_context` struct. The patch originally introduced this struct to avoid using the global `the_hash_algo` variable by passing the repository's hash algorithm through the context struct.

Taylor explains his design choice to initialize the struct fields explicitly near their usage rather than using designated initializers. He cites two reasons: (1) keeping initialization close to usage avoids ambiguity, and (2) while compound literals could achieve both locality and designated initialization, they remain experimental in Git's codebase and would add unnecessary verbosity.

The exchange shows careful consideration of code style tradeoffs within Git's established practices. Patrick's suggestion represented a potential improvement, but Taylor's response demonstrates a reasoned decision to maintain the current approach.
