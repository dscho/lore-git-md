This would simplify the mental model by making the encoding an implementation detail rather than something users might try to reverse-engineer.

The email includes a detailed example of how this could be documented to users - explaining the purpose (filesystem safety) and nature (opaque transformation) of the encoding without exposing implementation specifics.
