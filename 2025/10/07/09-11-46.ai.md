This is a licensing policy discussion in the ongoing Rust infrastructure thread, specifically addressing the GitOxide relicensing concerns raised earlier in the thread. - Agrees SFC consultation would be valuable
- Notes no substantive opposition to the relicensing concept itself, only practical implementation concerns
- Proposes the Project Leadership Committee (PLC) should be the ones to initiate contact with SFC rather than individual contributors

The email advances the licensing track by formalizing next steps (PLC involvement) while acknowledging the core tension between technical desire for Rust adoption and legal complexities of relicensing legacy code. It maintains focus on the administrative process rather than technical implementation, keeping the thread's policy-first approach intact. The PLC's involvement suggests this may move from open discussion to formal project governance channels.
