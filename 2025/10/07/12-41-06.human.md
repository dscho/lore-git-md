This is a refactoring series targeting the packfile subsystem, specifically cleaning up the `packfile_store_get_packs()` interface. Patrick Steinhardt follows up on previous discussion by removing the confusing distinction between `packfile_store_get_packs()` and `packfile_store_get_all_packs()`, where the behavior depended on whether `get_all_packs()` had been called first.

The series converts all callers to use `get_all_packs()` consistently, then removes the now-redundant `get_packs()` function. It also introduces a new macro for iterating through packs and renames the remaining function to drop the "all" qualifier since it's now the only option. The changes touch many parts of the codebase that interact with packfiles, including object lookup, GC, grep, and various pack-related commands.

The patch appears well-motivated by the confusing behavior of the current interface and maintains consistency across the codebase. The changes are mechanical but widespread, affecting 22 files with mostly straightforward replacements. The series builds on recent work in the packfile area and resolves some minor conflicts with the ongoing incremental-midx work.
