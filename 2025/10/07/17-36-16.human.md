**Platform compatibility and release planning discussion in SHA-256 transition**

Randall Becker from HPE NonStop chimes in on the ongoing Git 3.0 release planning thread regarding SHA-256 interoperability. His contribution highlights a specific platform constraint: HPE NonStop systems currently lack Rust support, which is a dependency for the SHA-256 transition work. He notes this is under discussion with their OS vendor but estimates resolution will take at least a year.

The email provides useful context about his organization's readiness - their front-end implementation has supported SHA-256 for two years, with the only blocker being Rust availability in their Git builds. This demonstrates how platform-specific constraints can create uneven readiness across the ecosystem, even when individual implementations are prepared.

Randall's response comes amid broader discussions about gathering stakeholder input and setting realistic timelines for the SHA-256 transition. While not proposing new solutions, his message serves as a concrete example of the kind of platform-specific considerations that need to be factored into release planning. The tone suggests cautious optimism about eventual Rust support while acknowledging the timeline is outside his direct control.
