Refactoring patch in a series modernizing Git's ref iteration and tag peeling infrastructure.

Karthik Nayak reviews patch 4/13 in Patrick Steinhardt's series, which introduces a new way to access peeled object IDs during ref iteration. The patch replaces a fragile global-state mechanism with a direct `peeled_oid` field in `struct reference`, accessible via a new `reference_get_peeled_oid()` function. This eliminates the need for callbacks to use the global `current_ref_iter` variable and `peel_iterated_oid()` function.

Nayak confirms the technical approach makes sense, noting it avoids unnecessary round-trips to the object database (which would only be needed for loose refs in the files backend). He observes the implementation properly handles the peeled_oid field across all three ref backends (files, packed, reftable), though he reiterates his earlier concern about ensuring fields are properly reset between iterations.

The review is positive overall, with Nayak stating "All of this looks good and as expected." This continues the productive technical discussion around the series' goal of cleaning up ref iteration infrastructure while maintaining correctness. The patch appears ready to proceed in the series.
