This is a licensing discussion email in the ongoing Rust infrastructure and xdiff compatibility thread.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Phillip Wood's suggestion about consulting the Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC) regarding Rust code licensing issues. The email notes that while there wasn't time to discuss this at the recent Contributor's Summit, Steinhardt agrees that SFC advice would be valuable. He observes that nobody has objected to the core idea itself - the concerns are purely around the relicensing challenges.

The key new development is Steinhardt's proposal to have the Project Leadership Committee (PLC) formally approach the SFC rather than individual contributors doing so ad-hoc. This suggests the discussion is moving from technical implementation concerns toward establishing proper governance channels for resolving the complex licensing questions.

The email maintains the thread's focus on the Rust code's license compatibility with xdiff's LGPL requirements, but shifts the conversation toward process - specifically who should initiate the legal consultation that multiple participants have now agreed is necessary. This represents a small but meaningful step forward in addressing the licensing track's open questions.
