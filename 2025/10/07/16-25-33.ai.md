This is a final maintainer approval for the reftable fsck validation series. Junio C Hamano confirms Patrick Steinhardt's positive review of Karthik Nayak's v6 patch series, which has now been merged to 'next'. The series implements stack integrity checks for the reftable backend through a callback-based architecture in `reftable/fsck.[ch]` with fsck integration.

This change addressed feedback from Jeff King while maintaining the core functionality of table name format validation, strict `parse_names()` checking, and cross-backend fsck message consistency.

The series represents the culmination of six iterations that progressively addressed race conditions, completed documentation, and finalized test correctness. With both maintainer approval and Patrick Steinhardt's final ack, this work brings reftable fsck to parity with files-backend capabilities while maintaining extensibility for future enhancements through its callback-based design.
