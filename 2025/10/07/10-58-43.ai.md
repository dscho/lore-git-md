Refactoring patch in a series modernizing Git's refs subsystem, specifically targeting the ref-filter interface. This patch (6/13) modifies the ref-filter subsystem to propagate peeled object IDs through the ref array structure rather than discarding them, eliminating the need for subsequent `peel_iterated_oid()` calls.

Key technical changes:
- Adds `peeled_oid` field to `struct ref_array_item` to store pre-peeled tag targets
- Modifies `new_ref_array_item()` and `ref_array_push()` to accept and store peeled OIDs
- Updates `populate_value()` to use the stored peeled OID when available, falling back to manual peeling
- Converts `apply_ref_filter()` to take a `struct reference` parameter instead of individual fields
- Updates callers in `builtin/ls-remote.c`, `builtin/tag.c`, and `builtin/verify-tag.c` to pass NULL for peeled_oid (maintaining current behavior)
- Modifies `pretty_print_ref()` signature to accept peeled_oid parameter

The change enables future optimizations by preserving peeled OIDs through the ref-filter pipeline, though current callers don't yet take advantage of this. The patch maintains backward compatibility while preparing for later optimizations in the series (particularly patch 13's `git-for-each-ref` speedup). The refactoring affects core ref-filter logic but leaves behavior unchanged for now.
