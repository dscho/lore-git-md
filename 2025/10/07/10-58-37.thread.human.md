This thread covers a substantial 13-patch series by Patrick Steinhardt that refactors Git's ref iteration and tag peeling infrastructure while fixing related bugs. The work originated from performance optimization goals for `git-for-each-ref`, which uncovered deeper technical debt in how Git handles tag objects and reference iteration. The series introduces a new `struct reference` to encapsulate ref information (including a pre-computed `peeled_oid`), modernizes the reference flags system, and updates over 65 files to use the new interfaces - including a final conversion of `upload-pack.c` to demonstrate the API's benefits. Performance benchmarks show a 13% speedup in the Chromium repository.

After thorough review from Taylor Blau and Junio Hamano, with technical validation from Karthik Nayak and Justin Tobler, the series is now merge-ready. The most recent email simply confirms incorporation of final typo fixes from Kristoffer Haugsbakk in the optimization patch that started this whole effort. All major technical questions have been resolved, with only minor naming convention cleanups and documentation improvements noted as potential follow-up work. This represents a significant step in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing effort to modernize Git's refs infrastructure.
