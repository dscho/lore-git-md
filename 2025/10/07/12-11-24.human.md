This is a bugfix and refactoring series for the reftable backend, now in its sixth iteration. Karthik Nayak continues refining the reftable consistency checks that were previously approved for merging to 'next', addressing a final piece of feedback from Jeff King about test case hygiene.

The key change in v6 is a small but important test fix: replacing root refs (`branch-$i`) with properly namespaced refs (`refs/heads/branch-$i`) in the test script. This aligns with Git's ref naming conventions and prevents future confusion when reference-level checks are implemented. The test was passing only because reftables don't yet validate ref names at the reference level - a known gap that may be addressed in future work.

The series maintains all the core improvements from previous versions: table name validation, trailing newline checks in 'tables.list', and the callback-based architecture that keeps fsck logic separate from the reftable library internals. Documentation and test coverage remain thorough, with the fsck message IDs properly documented and duplicate entries cleaned up.

This appears to be the final polish before the series graduates from 'next' to 'master'. The changes are minimal and focused, showing good attention to detail even after maintainer approval. The only outstanding question is whether this test fix warrants a re-roll given that the substantive changes are already in 'next', but the fix is clearly correct and prevents future confusion.
