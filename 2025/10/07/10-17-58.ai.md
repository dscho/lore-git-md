This is a discussion email in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, focusing on handling long path names in the encoded submodule directory scheme. Adrian Ratiu responds to Junio C Hamano's concerns about the current approach of erroring out when encoded submodule names exceed filesystem limits.

The email reveals a shift in direction - Adrian now agrees with Junio that the current error-out behavior (implemented in patch 5/5) may not be necessary and could be dropped in the next version. He acknowledges Jeff King's earlier question about whether this path length handling is worth pursuing at all, and suggests falling back to letting the OS handle failures naturally.

Key technical points discussed:
- The current v3 design maintains a simple filesystem mapping where `.git/modules/` paths are encoded except when dealing with pre-existing unencoded directories
- Junio proposes alternative approaches like automatic truncation+uniquification of long names rather than erroring out
- Adrian floats a new suggestion of using hashed prefixes (`<hash>_<name>`) for encoded submodule paths
- The discussion highlights the tradeoffs between complexity (maintaining explicit mappings) versus filesystem-based approaches

This email effectively marks the abandonment of the strict length-checking approach from patch 5/5, with Adrian signaling willingness to either drop that commit entirely or replace it with a more lenient strategy. The conversation continues to explore alternative designs for path handling while maintaining the core encoding functionality established earlier in the series.
