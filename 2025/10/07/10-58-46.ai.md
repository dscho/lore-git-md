Refactoring patch in a series modernizing Git's refs subsystem and tag peeling behavior.

This patch (9/13) removes the now-obsolete infrastructure for peeling references via iterator interfaces, following the introduction of `peeled_oid` in `struct reference` in earlier patches. The change deletes the `ref_iterator_peel()` function and all its implementations across various backends (files, packed, reftable), along with related vtable entries and documentation. The most notable behavioral change is in `write_with_updates()` in the packed backend, which now directly uses the pre-computed `peeled_oid` rather than peeling via the iterator.

The removal affects all major ref backends uniformly and represents a significant simplification of the codebase, eliminating what was previously a performance optimization hack (`peel_iterated_oid()`) in favor of a more direct approach. This continues the series' theme of reducing global state and consolidating reference metadata handling. The patch appears mechanically sound, with straightforward deletions and one logical update to the packed backend's write logic.
