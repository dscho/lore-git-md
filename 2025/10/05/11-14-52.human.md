Performance investigation into untracked files cache behavior. Devste Devste confirms the caching inconsistency reported earlier, where `--untracked-files=all` only uses the cache when `status.showUntrackedFiles=all` is also set. Their testing reveals an additional wrinkle: certain status flags (like `--porcelain` or `--no-optional-locks`) appear to prevent cache updates, though the exact culprit isn't isolated yet. 

The email shows timing data demonstrating the performance impact - initial status operations take ~3.4 seconds without caching, dropping to ~0.8s when cached. Interestingly, once the cache is populated (with the right config), it persists even when the config is later unset, suggesting the cache activation logic has stateful side effects. 

This builds on Matthew Hughes's earlier findings about the config/flag symmetry requirement, while surfacing new questions about how other status flags interact with the cache. The behavior particularly affects tools/IDEs that hardcode these flags, making it a real-world performance concern beyond just command-line usage. No solution is proposed yet, but the reproducible test cases help clarify the problem space.
