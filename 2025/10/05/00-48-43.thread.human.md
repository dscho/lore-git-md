The original reporter identified and fixed the issue by adjusting when colors are applied in the `wt-status.c` code.

Jeff King provided important context that while `-z` typically indicates machine-readable output, the combination with `--short` explicitly maintains human-readable (colored) output with NUL separators. The fix aligns the behavior for untracked files with other status markers by applying colors before the null-termination check. A new test case was added to verify this behavior.
