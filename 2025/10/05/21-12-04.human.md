This is a documentation patch focused on improving translation-friendliness in Git's man pages. Jean-Noël Avila points out that breaking sentences across multiple lines (like "lego pieces") makes them harder to translate properly. The patch rewrites a section of `pretty-options.adoc` to keep sentences whole while maintaining the same content.

The change affects documentation for the `--notes` option, restructuring how the default behavior is described for `git log`, `git show`, and `git whatchanged`. The actual meaning remains identical - it's purely a formatting improvement for better translation support. The patch is small (6 lines added, 5 removed) and focused, with a clear explanation of its purpose in the commit message.

This appears to be a straightforward quality-of-life improvement for translators, following Git's established documentation practices. The change is uncontroversial and unlikely to require much discussion.
