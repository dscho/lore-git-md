This is patch 17/49 in Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery and eliminate global variables. The patch continues the systematic restructuring of `builtin/repack.c` by making the "packdir" parameter explicit when removing redundant packs.

Instead, the directory path is now passed explicitly through the call chain from `cmd_repack()` down to the actual pack removal functions. 1. Adds a "packdir" parameter to `existing_packs_remove_redundant()`
2. Passes it through to the helper function `remove_redundant_packs_1()`
3. Updates the single call site in `cmd_repack()`
