This is part of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series (tb/incremental-midx-part-3) to eliminate `the_repository` usage in the repack machinery, specifically patch 5/49 in v2 of the series.

The patch modifies `repack_promisor_objects()` to accept a `struct repository*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. The change is mechanical but important for the series' goal of removing global state. The function handles repacking of promisor objects (part of Git's partial clone functionality) by iterating over packed objects with `for_each_packed_object()` and writing new packfiles.

Key technical details:
- Adds `repo` parameter to `repack_promisor_objects()` function signature
- Updates the `for_each_packed_object()` call to use the passed repository
- Modifies the single call site in `cmd_repack()` to pass the repository pointer
- Maintains identical behavior while removing global state dependency
- Follows the same pattern established in earlier patches of the series

The change is straightforward and follows the established pattern from previous patches in the series. It's part of the systematic elimination of `the_repository` usage that forms the backbone of this refactoring effort. The patch appears well-contained and low-risk, focusing specifically on promisor object handling without broader behavioral changes.
