This is a design discussion about interface clarity in Taylor Blau's repack refactoring series (patch 44/49). Jeff King (Peff) examines how `write_pack_opts` structs are being passed to `finish_pack_objects_cmd()`, noting the current approach creates partially-filled structs that may confuse API users about required fields.

Peff identifies several pain points: the struct serves multiple purposes (regular/cruft/filtered packs), fields get shadowed in different code blocks, and destination fields are sometimes overridden. He suggests two alternative approaches - either using block-scoped struct initialization or compound literals - but acknowledges both have tradeoffs in readability versus encapsulation.

The discussion continues the series' pattern of carefully evaluating interface design choices, though Peff doesn't insist on changes, recognizing this may be the least bad option given the constraints. This comes as Taylor prepares to respin the series incorporating prior feedback on patches 39-49.
