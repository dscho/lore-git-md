This is a documentation refinement discussion in the ongoing `gitdatamodel.adoc` series. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review of version 3, focusing on three specific wording improvements.

The discussion centers on making the documentation more precise while avoiding implementation details that could confuse readers.
