This thread documents a completed 9-part refactoring series that modernizes the xdiff subsystem's core data structures (xrecord_t and xdfile_t) to prepare for Rust FFI compatibility while maintaining existing behavior. The work builds on two previously merged cleanup efforts (en/xdiff-cleanup and am/xdiff-hash-tweak) and follows an incremental approach - each patch focused on one specific modernization.

The series systematically updated types for clarity and safety: converting pointers to uint8_t*, sizes to size_t, hash values to uint64_t, and splitting the ha field into line_hash and minimal_perfect_hash. The final patch renamed rindex to reference_index in xdiffi.c and xprepare.c to better document its purpose as a mapping between compacted and original line references. All changes were carefully implemented to preserve diff algorithm behavior, with attention to API boundaries, platform-specific considerations, and FFI compatibility (aligning type choices with usize/u8 in Rust).

The refactoring touches all major xdiff files but maintains the subsystem's core functionality, demonstrating a methodical approach to preparing infrastructure for the broader Rustification effort. With the final naming improvement now in place, the series is complete - having achieved its goals of type safety modernization while keeping xdiff's behavior stable.
