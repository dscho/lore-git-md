The work systematically updated xdiff's core structures with unambiguous types and clearer naming while maintaining behavior, including type safety updates (converting fields to ssize_t, uint8_t*, size_t, and uint64_t), field separation (splitting the ha field into line_hash and minimal_perfect_hash), and naming improvements (renaming rindex to reference_index).
