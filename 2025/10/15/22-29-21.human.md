This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series to modularize Git's repack machinery (part 42/49). The change moves the `find_pack_prefix()` function out of `builtin/repack.c` and into the new `repack.c` module, making it available via a new `write_pack_opts_pack_prefix()` function.

The key improvement is eliminating redundant pack prefix calculations by having the callees compute it from the `write_pack_opts` struct rather than requiring callers to pre-compute it. This both reduces boilerplate and prevents potential parameter-swapping bugs where `packdir` and `packtmp` could be accidentally reversed.

The patch follows the series' pattern of moving functionality out of the builtin while maintaining the same behavior. It's a clean, focused change that continues the architectural improvements seen throughout the series. The implementation looks solid, with all call sites updated appropriately and the new function following the established naming conventions.
