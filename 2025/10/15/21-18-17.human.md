This is part 5/9 of a refactoring series preparing xdiff for Rust FFI compatibility, focusing on splitting a dual-purpose field in the xdiff data structures.

The change separates the `ha` field in `xrecord_t` into two distinct fields: `line_hash` (a simple hash of the line content) and `minimal_perfect_hash` (a unique identifier assigned during diff processing). This clarifies the code by making explicit what was previously an implicit dual-purpose use of a single field. The patch updates all xdiff algorithms (myers, histogram, patience) to use the new field names.
