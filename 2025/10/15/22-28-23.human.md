This is part of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery (patch 22/49 in v2 series). The patch continues the systematic elimination of static variables by making the `packtmp` path explicitly passed to `generated_pack_populate()` rather than accessed as a global.

The change is mechanical but important - it removes another implicit dependency as the code is being prepared for movement out of `builtin/repack.c`. The patch follows the established pattern seen in earlier parts of the series, where global state is converted to explicit parameters to improve modularity. 

Three call sites are updated to pass the new parameter, all maintaining the same behavior. This is a straightforward step in the larger effort to create clean interfaces for the repack functionality, with no visible controversy or complications in the change itself. The series as a whole has already received positive review feedback, with this patch representing another incremental improvement in the codebase modernization effort.
