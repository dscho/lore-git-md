This is the first patch in Taylor Blau's 49-part series refactoring Git's repack machinery, specifically targeting the removal of `the_repository` global variable usage. The patch modifies `builtin/repack.c` to use the `repo` parameter passed to `cmd_repack()` instead of the global `the_repository` variable.

The changes are mechanical but thorough - 15 instances of `the_repository` are replaced with the local `repo` parameter. The patch serves as groundwork for the larger refactoring effort, showing the systematic approach Taylor is taking throughout the series. The commit message notes this is just the beginning, with more `the_repository` removals to come in subsequent patches.

As the first patch in a well-planned series that has already gone through one round of review (now at v2), this appears to be a solid foundation. The changes are straightforward and focused, with no behavior modifications - just the kind of clean, incremental change that makes large refactors manageable. The patch follows the series' goal of modularizing repack functionality while eliminating globals.
