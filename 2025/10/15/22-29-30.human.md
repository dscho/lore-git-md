This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series to modularize Git's repack machinery, specifically moving functionality out of `builtin/repack.c` into dedicated modules.

The patch relocates `finish_pack_objects_cmd()` from the repack builtin to `repack.c`, marking another step in the series' goal of creating cleaner module boundaries. The function handles post-processing after `pack-objects` completes, verifying object IDs and populating pack metadata. Taylor notes this prepares for future moves of similar pack-writing functions (`write_cruft_pack()` and `write_filtered_pack()`).

The change is straightforward - the function is copied verbatim to its new location with no behavioral modifications. It now operates through the `write_pack_opts` struct interface introduced earlier in the series, continuing the pattern of reducing direct global variable access. The patch appears well-contained and follows the series' established patterns, making it a low-risk change that advances the larger refactoring effort.
