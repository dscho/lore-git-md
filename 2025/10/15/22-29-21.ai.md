Refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's repack modularization series (patch 42/49). This continues the effort to clean up the repack builtin by moving the `find_pack_prefix()` function out of `builtin/repack.c` and into `repack.c` as `write_pack_opts_pack_prefix()`, making it operate on the `write_pack_opts` struct rather than taking individual parameters.

The change eliminates redundant pack prefix calculations in callers by having the pack-writing functions compute the prefix themselves from the `write_pack_opts` struct's fields. This prevents potential parameter-swapping bugs where `packdir` and `packtmp` could be accidentally reversed, since the function now accesses them directly from the struct. The patch updates two call sites in `cmd_repack()` that previously computed the prefix manually.

This is part of the broader pattern in the series of moving functionality out of the builtin and into dedicated modules while improving type safety and reducing boilerplate. The change maintains identical behavior while making the code more maintainable. The patch-specific changes align with the series' overall goals of modularization and interface cleanup, though this particular change wasn't specifically requested in prior review feedback.
