This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series to modularize `builtin/repack.c` and eliminate global variables. Patch 36/49 moves bitmap cleanup logic directly into `write_midx_included_packs()` rather than having the caller handle it separately.

The change inlines the `remove_redundant_bitmaps()` call that was previously made by the caller (`cmd_repack()`), moving it into `write_midx_included_packs()` itself. This simplifies the interface by making the MIDX-writing function responsible for its own cleanup. The patch maintains all existing functionality - bitmaps are still removed for packs included in the MIDX, with the same ENOENT handling - but reduces the burden on callers to track which packs were included.

The diff shows the mechanical changes: the bitmap removal call moves from `cmd_repack()` to the end of `write_midx_included_packs()`, with proper error handling preserved through the new `done` label. This prepares for future changes where the caller won't need to track the included packs at all, as noted in the commit message. The change is straightforward and maintains the series' focus on cleaner interfaces while preserving behavior.
