Refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX repack series (patch 40/49). This change introduces a new `struct write_pack_opts` to consolidate common parameters used by pack-writing functions in `builtin/repack.c`.

The patch bundles several frequently-used parameters (`pack_objects_args`, `destination`, `pack_prefix`, `packdir`, `packtmp`) into a single options struct. This reduces parameter passing redundancy between `write_filtered_pack()` and `write_cruft_pack()`, and makes the interface clearer by avoiding multiple similar-looking string parameters. The change is purely structural - no behavior is modified.

This continues the series' pattern of methodically modularizing the repack codebase while eliminating global variables. The commit message notes a potential future cleanup (deriving `pack_prefix` from other fields) that's deliberately deferred to keep this change focused. The patch fits cleanly into the larger refactoring effort, with clear mechanical changes that follow the established pattern of the series.
