This is the final naming decision discussion for the atomic reference updates in `git replay` series. Maintainer Junio Hamano weighs in on the naming choice between `replay.refAction`/`--ref-action` (Option 1) and `replay.updateRefs`/`--update-refs` (Option 2), expressing a clear preference for Option 1. 

Hamano finds `refAction` more semantically precise - it directly describes the action being taken on references rather than posing a yes/no question about whether to update them. While acknowledging there may have been previous support for `updateRefs` in earlier reviews, he invites any remaining counterpoints to be raised before finalizing the decision. 

This email represents the last open question in the otherwise technically complete patch series, which has already addressed all other design concerns and achieved consensus on implementation details. The naming choice here will determine the final patch's configuration interface before merging.
