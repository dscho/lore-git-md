Refactoring discussion in the repack series, focusing on struct initialization patterns.

Jeff King (Peff) examines patch 44/49 of Taylor Blau's repack refactoring series, which passes a `write_pack_opts` struct to `finish_pack_objects_cmd()`. 1. Current approach: Create a partially-filled `write_pack_opts` at function scope
2. Block-scoped version: Introduce a new block to limit the struct's scope
3. Compound literal: Use C99 compound literal syntax for one-time initialization

Peff acknowledges the tradeoffs, noting the current approach may be the least bad option despite its imperfections.
