This is part of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery and eliminate global variables, specifically targeting `the_hash_algo` in this patch. The change is a straightforward mechanical conversion in `repack_promisor_objects()` where a direct reference to the global `the_hash_algo` is replaced with the hash algorithm from the repository structure that's passed into the function. This maintains the same behavior (checking that object ID lines from pack-objects are the correct length) while continuing the series' systematic removal of global state dependencies.

The patch is small (just a one-line change) and follows the established pattern seen in earlier parts of the series. It's part of a larger, well-reviewed effort to modernize the repack codebase, with this particular change falling under both the refactoring and `the_repository` removal categories. The change is low-risk since it's a simple substitution that doesn't alter functionality, just the way the hash algorithm is accessed.
