This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series to modularize `builtin/repack.c` as part of the MIDX-based repacking effort. Jeff King (Peff) is reviewing patch 38/49, which moves the pack list computation into `write_midx_included_packs()` rather than having the caller handle it first.

The change simplifies the control flow by making the MIDX-writing function self-contained in determining which packs to include. Taylor's rationale is that this will better accommodate future incremental MIDX functionality, avoiding awkward conditional logic in the caller. Peff confirms the change is behaviorally neutral and accepts the design direction, though he notes the benefits won't be fully visible until the incremental MIDX code is added later.

This continues the series' theme of pragmatic refactoring - making targeted improvements now that set up for future functionality while keeping each step reviewable. The patch appears uncontroversial and well-justified as part of the larger effort.
