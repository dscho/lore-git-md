This is the final naming decision discussion for the atomic reference updates in `git replay` series. Junio Hamano weighs in on the naming choice between `replay.refAction`/`--ref-action` (Option 1) and `replay.updateRefs`/`--update-refs` (Option 2), expressing a clear preference for Option 1. His reasoning is that "refAction" better conveys the action being taken on references, while "updateRefs" could be misinterpreted as a simple yes/no toggle. 

The email confirms the naming direction that Christian Couder had previously supported, effectively resolving the last open question in the series. Junio acknowledges there may have been prior support for Option 2 in earlier reviews but doesn't recall specific arguments for it, inviting any remaining counterpoints to be raised before finalizing. 

This represents the maintainer's approval of the naming scheme that was already gaining consensus, with the implementation having addressed all technical concerns from previous reviews (batched transactions, enum-based interface, comprehensive testing). The series is now proceed toward merging with the `--ref-action`/`replay.refAction` naming.
