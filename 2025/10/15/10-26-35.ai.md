This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing series to modularize `builtin/repack.c` (tb/incremental-midx-part-3). Jeff King (Peff) is reviewing patch 38/49, which moves the pack list computation for MIDX writing into the function that actually uses it, rather than having the caller compute it first.

The change inlines the `midx_included_packs()` call into `write_midx_included_packs()`, eliminating an intermediate `string_list` that was passed between them. Taylor's rationale is that future incremental MIDX functionality will need different pack selection logic, and this restructuring makes it clearer to add those variants without duplicating the calling pattern. Peff confirms the change is behavior-preserving and accepts the design direction, though notes the full benefit won't be visible until the incremental MIDX code is added later in the series.

This continues the series' pattern of incremental improvements to prepare for future functionality while keeping each step reviewable. The patch touches the MIDX-writing path in repack but doesn't yet introduce the promised incremental MIDX support - that appears to be coming in the remaining unreviewed patches (39,42,44-49). The review exchange shows consensus building around these preparatory refactorings before the more substantial MIDX changes land.
