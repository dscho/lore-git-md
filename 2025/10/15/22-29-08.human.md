This is part 37/49 of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery, specifically focuses on improving the MIDX (multi-pack-index) handling code.

The patch simplifies the interface of `midx_included_packs()` by having it take a single `repack_write_midx_opts` struct pointer rather than multiple individual parameters. This change both reduces parameter passing and prepares for future work where the caller (`cmd_repack()`) won't need to track included packs directly. The key technical change is adding an `existing` field to the `repack_write_midx_opts` struct to maintain the set of existing packs.

This continues the series' pattern of methodically improving code structure while maintaining behavior. The change appears well-contained and builds logically on prior work in the series. As with other patches in this well-reviewed series, it demonstrates careful attention to interface design and incremental improvement.
