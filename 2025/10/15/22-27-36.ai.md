This is part of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to remove global variables from the repack machinery, specifically targeting `the_hash_algo` in this patch. The patch modifies `write_oid()` in `builtin/repack.c` to avoid using the global `the_hash_algo` by introducing a new `write_oid_context` struct that carries both the child process handle and the hash algorithm pointer. This continues the series' pattern of threading repository-specific data through callbacks rather than relying on globals.

The change is mechanical but necessary - since `write_oid()` is a callback for `for_each_packed_object()`, it can't directly take additional parameters. The solution introduces a context struct to pass both the original `cmd` pointer and the hash algorithm from the repository. The actual behavior remains identical, just with the hash algorithm now coming from `repo->hash_algo` instead of the global.

This patch follows the same careful approach seen in earlier parts of the series, maintaining existing functionality while removing global state. It touches promisor object handling during repacking, though that's incidental to the main goal of eliminating `the_hash_algo`. The change is straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, being a clear incremental improvement in the broader effort to modernize the repack codebase.
