It renames `pack_refs_opts` to `refs_optimize_opts` and related flag names (`PACK_REFS_PRUNE` to `REFS_OPTIMIZE_PRUNE`, etc.) to maintain consistency after the recent transition from `pack_refs` to `optimize` callbacks. The changes are mechanical and touch multiple files across the refs backend implementations.
