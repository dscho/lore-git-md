This is a follow-up in the thread about simplifying the `git whatchanged` deprecation warning. Kristoffer Haugsbakk notes that the current deprecation behavior (which Greg Couch had criticized as unnecessarily cumbersome) is scheduled to appear in the upcoming Git 2.51.1 release. 

The email serves as a heads-up that the controversial warning will soon reach more users unless the maintainers act on Greg's suggestion to modify it. While Kristoffer doesn't take a position on the proposed change, the timing observation adds urgency to the discussion by indicating the warning is about to ship in a maintenance release.

The key technical detail remains the deprecation warning in `builtin/whatchanged.c`, which currently suggests using `--i-still-use-this` and emailing the list rather than simply recommending `git log` as the alternative. The open question is whether the maintainers will accept Greg's proposed simplification before 2.51.1 ships.

This is a simple status update rather than a technical proposal, but it moves the thread forward by establishing a timeline for when the current behavior will become more widely visible to users.
