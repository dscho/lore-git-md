This maintains identical behavior while continuing the series' systematic elimination of global state. The change is small and focused, following the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series.

This patch builds on the foundation laid in patch 1-5/49 (as noted in the thread summary) where similar conversions were made, and it specifically relates to the promisor object handling functionality that was combined with hash algorithm parameterization in patch 5.
