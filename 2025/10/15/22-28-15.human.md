This is patch 19/49 in Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery, specifically targeting the `existing_packs` API. The patch moves the entire `existing_packs` implementation out of `builtin/repack.c` and into the new `repack.[ch]` files, completing the isolation of this subsystem.

The `existing_packs` API tracks packfiles during repack operations, classifying them as kept, non-kept, or cruft packs, and handles their deletion. This change follows the series' pattern of moving repack-related functionality into dedicated compilation units while maintaining behavior. Some functions become static (implementation details) while others remain public (interface).

The patch is a straightforward mechanical move with no behavior changes, continuing the series' systematic approach to repack modularization. It builds on previous patches that established naming conventions and type safety, and follows Jeff King's review of earlier versions. The change helps reduce `builtin/repack.c`'s size by 173 lines while making the pack tracking logic more reusable.
