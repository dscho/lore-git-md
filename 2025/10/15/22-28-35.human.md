The change continues the series' pattern of extracting repack-related functionality into separate compilation units while maintaining the same behavior. The promisor-specific code is now cleanly separated from generic repack utilities, with the function declared in `repack.h` but implemented in its own file. This follows the established approach seen in earlier patches for other repack components.
