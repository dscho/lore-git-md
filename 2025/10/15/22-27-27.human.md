This is part 4/49 of Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to eliminate `the_repository` global variable usage in the repack machinery. The patch focuses on pack removal functionality, specifically modifying `remove_redundant_pack()` and its callers to accept and use a `struct repository*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`.

The changes are mechanical but important - they pass the repository pointer through the call chain from `remove_redundant_existing_packs()` down to `remove_redundant_pack()`, where it's used for MIDX operations. The patch maintains identical behavior while removing global state dependencies, following the established pattern seen in earlier patches of this series.

This continues the systematic, careful approach seen throughout the series - each patch tackles a small, well-defined portion of the codebase. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, as they're part of the broader and well-reviewed effort to modernize Git's repack infrastructure. The patch demonstrates good attention to detail by updating all related functions in the call chain consistently.
