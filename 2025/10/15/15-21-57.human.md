Final approval for Rust CI infrastructure series. Junio Hamano gives the green light to Patrick Steinhardt's v3 patch series (6 patches) that establishes comprehensive CI tooling for Git's Rust components. The series has been reviewed through multiple iterations and now includes all planned features: rustfmt checks, Clippy linting, MSRV validation, and Windows compatibility. 

Hamano notes the clear structural improvements in this version, though he defers to others on the Windows-specific implementation details. The approval comes with an explicit "Will replace" indication that this will supersede any previous versions in the Git maintainer's queue. 

This marks the completion of a significant infrastructure effort that has involved multiple contributors addressing formatting policies, safety documentation, and cross-platform concerns. The series appears ready for merging into Git's main development branch, providing a solid foundation for future Rust work in the project.
