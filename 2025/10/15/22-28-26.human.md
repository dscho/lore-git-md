Refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX repack series (patch 23/49). This continues the modularization of `builtin/repack.c` by making `generated_pack_install()` take explicit `packdir` and `packtmp` parameters instead of relying on static variables. The change follows the same pattern as previous patches in the series, systematically removing implicit state dependencies.

The patch is straightforward - it modifies the function signature and updates the single call site to pass the new parameters. Taylor notes in the commit message that having three consecutive `const char*` parameters could lead to ordering mistakes, but considers introducing a new struct for them to be overkill at this stage.

This is part of the broader effort to clean up repack's architecture while maintaining behavior. The change is mechanical and low-risk, continuing the series' pattern of careful, incremental improvements. With Jeff King's review of earlier patches complete and only minor feedback incorporated, this appears to be another well-considered step in the refactoring.
