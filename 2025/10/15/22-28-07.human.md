This is patch 16/49 in Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring series to modularize Git's repack machinery, specifically targeting the removal of redundant pack files. The patch moves the `remove_redundant_pack()` function from `builtin/repack.c` to the new `repack.[ch]` compilation unit, renaming it to `repack_remove_redundant_pack()` to follow the subsystem's naming conventions.

The function handles cleanup of redundant pack files during repacking, including MIDX (multi-pack-index) maintenance when packs are removed. This is part of the broader effort to break up the monolithic `builtin/repack.c` into logical components while systematically removing global variables like `the_repository`. The change is mechanical - the implementation remains identical, just relocated and renamed.

This patch continues the series' pattern of careful, incremental refactoring. It's a straightforward move of functionality that had two callers (one of which will eventually move to its own compilation unit). The change helps pave the way for further modularization of the repack codebase while maintaining existing behavior. The series as a whole has been well-reviewed, with Jeff King having already reviewed the full set of patches.
