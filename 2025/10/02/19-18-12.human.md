This is a follow-up in the build system refactoring thread, confirming the approach for consolidating xdiff and reftable functionality directly into libgit.a. 

Ezekiel Newren responds to Junio Hamano's approval of the plan to remove the separate XDIFF_LIB and fold xdiff objects directly into LIB_OBJS. Junio notes that since patch 1/3 already reorganized the Makefile lines, there's no longer a merge-friendliness advantage to keeping the XDIFF_OBJS variable separate. 

The exchange confirms consensus on the technical approach, with Newren indicating he'll implement this change in the next version of the patch series. This continues the thread's progression toward simplifying Git's build system by eliminating intermediate static libraries, matching meson's behavior, and paving the way for Rust integration. The discussion shows all parties now aligned on the implementation details after earlier consideration of alternative approaches.
