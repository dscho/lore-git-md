This is the final patch in a 5-part series focused on CI/build system improvements, specifically addressing a test failure in t8020-last-modified.sh. 1. Create a standalone repository to avoid interference from other tests
2. Explicitly test the directory-modification scenario rather than criss-cross merges
3. Remove the problematic test_when_finished call that wasn't subshell-safe
4. Use clearer commit/tag names to prevent the ambiguous output that caused failures
