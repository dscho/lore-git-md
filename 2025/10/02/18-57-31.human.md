This is the third and final patch in Ezekiel Newren's series to restructure Git's static library build process. The patch completes the work by removing the REFTABLE_LIB static library and folding its objects directly into LIB_OBJS, mirroring the earlier removal of XDIFF_LIB in patch 2.

The change follows the same pattern as the xdiff changes - eliminating a separate static library that was redundant with libgit.a's structure. As Patrick Steinhardt notes in the thread, these are sensible build system simplifications that stand on their own merits, though they were originally motivated by Rust integration needs. The meson build system already handles these libraries the same way, so no meson changes are required.

With this patch, the series achieves its goal of unifying the static library structure between Makefile and meson builds while removing potential naming conflicts for future integration work. The discussion indicates general approval from reviewers, with the only remaining question being whether to maintain intermediate *_OBJS variables for organization (as currently implemented) or follow meson's more direct approach. That appears to be an organizational preference rather than a blocking issue.

The changes are purely build-system focused with no behavioral impact on reftable or xdiff functionality. The series represents a clean, well-motivated simplification of Git's build infrastructure that happens to also pave the way for future Rust integration work.
