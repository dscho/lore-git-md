Refactoring patch in the libgit.a build system restructuring series.

This email continues the discussion about how to organize object files in Git's Makefile as part of the effort to consolidate xdiff and reftable functionality directly into libgit.a. Ezekiel Newren responds to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier question about whether to maintain separate XDIFF_OBJS variables or merge them directly into LIB_OBJS, noting that while he personally prefers Patrick's simpler approach (which matches meson's structure), he'll defer to Junio Hamano's preference for keeping the logical organization via intermediate variables.

The email provides useful context by showing how meson.build already includes all xdiff and reftable source files directly in its libgit_sources variable. This illustrates the inconsistency between the two build systems that the series aims to resolve. The discussion remains constructive, with all parties acknowledging the tradeoffs between organizational clarity and build system simplicity.

This appears to be the final point of discussion before the series can proceed, with Ezekiel explicitly asking Junio to make the final call on which approach to take. The technical merits of both approaches have been clearly laid out, and the decision now rests on which organizational philosophy the project prefers for the Makefile structure.
