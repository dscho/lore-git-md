This thread discusses documentation improvements for the `--merge-base` option in `git-merge-tree`. Elijah Newren has submitted a patch that makes three key clarifications: it improves readability by splitting a long sentence, explicitly warns that results may differ from regular merges when multiple merge bases exist (potentially losing some changes), and notes that trees rather than commits are sufficient with this option. The changes are purely documentation-focused and don't modify functionality, but they provide important context about behavioral differences that users should be aware of. The patch comes from an experienced contributor and follows the project's documentation standards.
