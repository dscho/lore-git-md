This is the final patch in the Rust infrastructure series, focusing on CI modernization to validate Rust compilation in the breaking-changes test jobs. The patch adds Rust toolchain installation (`cargo`) to both Fedora/AlmaLinux and Ubuntu/Debian CI environments in `ci/install-dependencies.sh`. It then enables Rust compilation in the breaking-changes jobs by setting `WITH_RUST=YesPlease` and `-Drust=enabled` in `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`, ensuring the Rust-enabled builds are tested alongside the breaking changes infrastructure.

This completes the CI validation for the Rust integration, with the breaking-changes jobs now testing both the new policy infrastructure (via `-Dbreaking_changes=true`) and Rust compilation (via the new flags). The changes are minimal but critical for ensuring the Rust infrastructure works in CI, particularly for the Fedora-based `fedora-breaking-changes-meson` job that was repurposed in earlier patches. The patch maintains consistency with the series' phased approach by only enabling Rust in the breaking-changes jobs rather than across all CI, reflecting the optional-first transition strategy documented in `BreakingChanges.adoc`.
