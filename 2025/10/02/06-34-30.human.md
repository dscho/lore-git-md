This is a technical review of patch 3 in Adrian Ratiu's hook subsystem refactoring series, focusing on the post-rewrite hook conversion in sequencer.c. Patrick Steinhardt raises several implementation concerns about potential deadlocks and unnecessary operations in the stdin feeding mechanism.

The review questions whether the strbuf_reset() call is needed after writing to the child process, suggesting the run-command subsystem should handle pipe closure automatically. Patrick also notes a seemingly unrelated change to the notes command execution that appears out of scope for this hook conversion patch.

The feedback continues the pattern of detailed technical scrutiny seen in earlier reviews of this series, with attention to both correctness (potential deadlock scenarios) and code hygiene (single-line statement formatting). The exchange shows active collaboration on refining the implementation, though this particular patch may need a respin to address the concerns raised.
