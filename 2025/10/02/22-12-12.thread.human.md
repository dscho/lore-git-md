This thread documents an effort to improve the formatting of Git's technical documentation when built with different toolchains. The patch series (4 parts) focuses on fixing asciidoc/asciidoctor warnings that appear when building several technical documents (`commit-graph.adoc`, `sparse-checkout.adoc`, etc.), particularly around list numbering and section hierarchy issues. The changes aim to bring parity between the Makefile and meson build systems.

The author has diagnosed specific formatting problems including list item numbering mismatches, section title inconsistencies, and rendering issues with monospaced text containing special characters. While the patches make incremental improvements, the author notes several unresolved issues - particularly around URL encoding and monospaced text formatting - and is explicitly seeking help from asciidoc experts to finalize the solutions. The work appears connected to a broader "misc build updates" effort, though the exact relationship isn't fully detailed in this thread.
