He maintains that a Git-specific solution would better serve users who want consistent behavior without impacting their general terminal environment. The discussion now centers on whether Git should provide its own width control mechanism independent of terminal constraints, with Junio leaning toward Unix conventions while Jeff advocates for a more Git-centric approach.
