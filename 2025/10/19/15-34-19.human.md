This is a discussion about a proposed change to make `git add` respect the `ignore=all` submodule configuration consistently with other commands like `status` and `diff`. Phillip Wood raises several thoughtful concerns about the patch series.

Phillip points out that the current behavior of `git add` ignoring submodule configurations was an intentional design choice from 2017, meant to ensure submodules could always be added or reset. He questions whether simply removing the override flag would be a simpler solution than the proposed `--force` approach.

The key concerns raised are:
1. The historical rationale for the current behavior needs addressing
2. Using `--force` conflates two different concepts (ignoring tracked vs untracked paths)
3. A new option specific to submodule ignore might be cleaner
4. The impact on `git reset` behavior needs consideration

Phillip notes he lacks submodule experience to judge the overall merit of changing the behavior, but his technical critique suggests the current approach may need refinement. The discussion appears to be at a point where the original author might need to address these concerns before the patch series can move forward.
