This is Junio Hamano's review response to the second part of Olamide Caleb Bello's SSH key handling refactoring series, which converts `strbuf_split*()` to `string_list_split*()` in `gpg-interface.c`.

Junio reiterates his earlier feedback from part 1, noting the commit message still has an apparent cut-off in the first paragraph. He suggests the explanation about string splitting counts could be trimmed since it was covered in part 1. More significantly, he questions whether using string_list at all is the right approach - proposing that simply finding the newline with `strchr()` and copying the substring might be cleaner than either splitting approach.

The review maintains the thread's technical focus on modernizing SSH key parsing while raising architectural questions about the optimal solution. Junio doesn't block the current approach but floats the idea of a more fundamental rewrite that would abandon string_list splitting entirely in favor of simpler string operations. This suggests the series may see another revision or spin-off work to explore alternative implementations.

Key technical points referenced:
- The ongoing conversion from `strbuf_split_max()` to `string_list_split_in_place()`
- The count semantics difference (1 split vs 2 tokens)
- Potential alternative using `strchr()`/`xmemdupz()`
- Preservation of existing behavior through the changes

The email shows the maintainer engaging with both the immediate patch quality and the architectural direction of the change, while acknowledging the current approach is technically sound if not necessarily optimal.
