Olamide Caleb Bello's two-part series refactoring SSH key handling in `gpg-interface.c` has reached its third iteration, converting from `strbuf_split*()` to `string_list_split*()` for better handling of read-only strings. The changes modernize both `get_ssh_key_fingerprint()` and `get_default_ssh_signing_key()`, consistently using `STRING_LIST_INIT_NODUP` and adjusting split counting logic while preserving existing behavior.

Junio Hamano has given final approval for merging the series in its current form, though his latest review raises deeper architectural questions. While acknowledging the technical soundness of the string_list approach, he suggests that a simpler solution using `strchr()` and `xmemdupz()` might be cleaner - an idea he first mentioned in reviewing part 1. He also notes some minor documentation tweaks needed in the commit message, including removing redundant explanations about split counts that were covered in the first patch.

The thread shows productive collaboration between Olamide, Christian Couder (who helped shape v3), and Junio. While the immediate series is ready to go in, Junio's comments hint this area might see follow-up work exploring alternative implementations. The changes are part of a broader effort to replace `strbuf_split*()` with more appropriate string handling throughout the codebase.
