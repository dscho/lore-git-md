Bugfix response to regression in `git diff --quiet` behavior. Jeff King confirms Jake Zimmerman's report that `git diff --quiet HEAD` incorrectly produces output for newly staged files in v2.51.1, violating the documented silent behavior promise of `--quiet`. The regression was introduced by commit b55e6d36eb.

King notes the surprising lack of test coverage for this case and observes that while a fix was proposed earlier today in a separate thread, it doesn't explicitly address this issue. He suggests a conservative approach for the maintenance release: reverting the problematic hunk from the original commit to prevent output leaks in all cases, even if it means some unnecessary processing.

The response indicates this is a high-priority regression that needs addressing in the maintenance release, with King favoring a broad fix that prevents all potential output leaks over a more targeted solution. The discussion highlights the tension between fixing the immediate regression and maintaining the original intent of b55e6d36eb's changes to diff behavior with ignore options.
