This is a final round of documentation polishing for Julia Evans' `git-reset` man page improvements, specifically addressing the pathspec behavior clarification in patch 4/4. Junio Hamano provides targeted feedback on two aspects of the wording:

For other tree-ish arguments, it's more accurately described as changes being applied to match the specified commit. He also questions avoiding the term "patch" when describing the interactive mode, suggesting it could make the explanation clearer by embracing the option's terminology.
