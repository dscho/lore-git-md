This is part 8 of Adrian Ratiu's hook subsystem refactoring series, focusing on converting receive-pack's 'update' hook to use the new `hook.h` infrastructure. The patch introduces sideband output handling via a new `hook_output_to_sideband` callback, replacing direct child process management.

The key discussion point here is around keepalive behavior - Emily Shaffer raised concerns that introducing keepalive functionality (which wasn't present in the original hook implementation) might affect sideband consumers not expecting it. Adrian acknowledges this as a valid concern and plans to remove the keepalive functionality in v2 since it's unnecessary for this short-lived hook.

This patch represents one of the more complex conversions in the series, dealing with receive-pack's update hook while maintaining the same hook arguments (refname, old_oid, new_oid) through `run_hooks_opt.args`. The technical implementation appears sound, with Adrian being responsive to feedback about potential behavior changes. The v2 revision should address the keepalive concern while maintaining the core functionality improvements of the hook conversion.
