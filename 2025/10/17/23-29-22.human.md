This is a follow-up in the rejected proposal to make `git grep` ignore binary files by default. Thomas Braun weighs in with a more modest suggestion: keeping the proposed `grep.ignoreBinary` config option but without any plans to change the default behavior in future Git versions. He shares his personal workflow pain point of frequently needing to ignore binaries and forgetting to use his alias for `git grep -I`.

The email doesn't fundamentally change the maintainer's objections (script compatibility, lack of GNU grep precedent, 20 years of status quo), but offers a narrower compromise that might address individual user preferences without breaking existing behavior. Thomas also raises an interesting question about real-world use cases for grepping binary files, though this doesn't directly counter Junio's concerns about backward compatibility.

Given Junio's firm rejection in the parent email, this seems unlikely to revive the proposal unless more users voice similar needs or present compelling binary-grepping scenarios that the current `-I` flag doesn't adequately address. The exchange highlights a tension between individual workflow preferences and Git's conservative approach to behavior changes.
