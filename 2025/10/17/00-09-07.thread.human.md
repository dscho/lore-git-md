This thread reports a regression in Git v2.51.1 where `git diff --quiet HEAD` incorrectly produces diff output for newly staged files, violating the documented promise of the `--quiet` flag to suppress all output. Jake Zimmerman identified the issue through careful version comparison, showing v2.51.0 behaves correctly while v2.51.1 shows unwanted output. A bisect points to commit b55e6d36eb ("diff: ensure consistent diff behavior with ignore options") as the likely cause. The report provides a clear reproduction recipe and establishes this as an unintentional behavioral change that contradicts the documented contract of `--quiet`. The issue appears isolated to `builtin/diff.c` when comparing HEAD to the index with new staged content.
