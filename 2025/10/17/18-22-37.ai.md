Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's proposed fix for the `git diff --quiet` regression, strongly endorsing the approach while noting potential future simplifications. Junio agrees this is the right minimal fix for the regression, though he speculates about possibly removing the now-redundant `diff_flush_patch_quietly()` logic in future work.
