Documentation formatting fix for recently merged `git-checkout` improvements.

This is a second version of a post-merge documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, addressing markup consistency in the `git-checkout` man page. The change corrects two instances where branch placeholders were incorrectly marked up with backticks (`_<branch>_`) instead of underscores (_<branch>_), bringing them in line with AsciiDoc conventions. The patch has been reviewed and acked by Julia Evans, the original author of the documentation improvements being fixed.

The change affects the description of the `-B` flag in two locations, where the markup was causing unintended HTML rendering effects (applying `<code>` to underscores and `<em>` to placeholders). Kristoffer notes this was likely an oversight in the original commit 042d6f34 ("doc: git-checkout: clarify `-b` and `-B`"). The v2 adds Julia's ack and considers but retains the "probably" qualifier about the mix-up being unintentional.

This continues the pattern of post-merge polish seen throughout this documentation thread, with Kristoffer having previously submitted two other formatting fixes that were applied. The patch is minimal (4 lines changed) and uncontroversial, focused purely on presentation rather than technical content. With Julia's approval, it appears ready for inclusion.
