Junio Hamano (Git maintainer) responds to Christian Couder's review of Okhuomon Ajayi's patch, agreeing with the feedback about using present tense in commit messages. He expands on Git's conventional commit message structure: (1) describe current behavior in present tense, (2) optionally propose solution, (3) use imperative mood for changes. Hamano suggests this guidance might be documented for new contributor programs like Outreachy/GSoC.
