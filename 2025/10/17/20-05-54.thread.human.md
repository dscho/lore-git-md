Julia Evans' documentation series to clarify the `git reset` man page has reached its final polishing stage, with Junio Hamano providing targeted feedback on the last patch focusing on pathspec behavior. The four-patch series has already successfully reorganized the DESCRIPTION section, standardized terminology, and improved explanations of the different reset modes, making the command's behavior more discoverable while maintaining technical accuracy.

Junio's latest feedback hones in on two remaining areas needing precision: the pathspec behavior description and `--patch` mode explanation. For pathspecs, he suggests wording that more clearly distinguishes between operations that move HEAD versus those that update only the index, noting that pathspec resets affect just the index without touching HEAD or working tree files. He also recommends clarifying when users might prefer `git restore` instead. Regarding `--patch` mode, Junio points out two subtle inaccuracies in the proposed text - the avoidance of "patch" terminology despite the option name, and an overgeneralized use of "unstaged" that doesn't fully capture all cases.

This represents the final fine-tuning of a series that has already achieved its core goals of making `git reset` more approachable while preserving its technical precision. The changes standardize terminology, reorder mode explanations to prioritize the default `--mixed` behavior, and improve safety warnings. With Junio's feedback incorporated, the series will complete its mission of clarifying this fundamental but often confusing command, building on Julia's previous successful documentation improvements to other core commands.
