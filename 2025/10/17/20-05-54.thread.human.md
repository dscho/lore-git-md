Julia Evans has concluded her work on restructuring the `git reset` man page documentation, announcing she is stepping away from the effort due to time constraints and lack of funding for Git documentation work. This marks the completion of her 4-patch series that had been under collaborative review with Junio Hamano and D. Ben Knoble.

The series successfully addressed the major technical questions, including resolving the "working tree" vs "working directory" terminology debate (with Junio confirming "working tree" as the correct Git term) and refining descriptions of `--soft` behavior beyond just the HEAD~1 case. The documentation now properly frames reset's dual purposes of changing HEAD versus updating staged files, avoiding circular definitions in the explanations.

While most substantive issues were resolved through this collaborative process, a few minor wording refinements remain - specifically around edge cases for `--hard` file operations, the relationship of `--merge` to other abort commands, and optimal introductory phrasing. Julia's departure means these final touches will need to be handled by others in the community. The email includes valuable context from the review process, particularly Junio's technical clarifications about reset's dual operations, which may help guide whoever picks up the remaining documentation polish work.
