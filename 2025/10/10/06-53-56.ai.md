This is a naming discussion for the new repository statistics command in the ongoing `git repo stats` series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Justin Tobler's consideration of renaming the command from `git repo stats` to `git repo structure`, suggesting several verb-based names that might better convey the command's purpose. The alternatives proposed (`inspect`, `analyze`, `scan`, `survey`, `measure`) all emphasize active examination of repository characteristics rather than passive reporting. This continues the thread's exploration of how to best describe the command's scope and functionality, with Patrick expressing a mild preference for verb-based names but leaving the final decision to Justin. The discussion remains focused on naming rather than implementation details, as the technical aspects of the statistics collection and reporting have already been addressed in previous patches.
