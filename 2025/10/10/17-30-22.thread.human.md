This thread focuses on standardizing the build system for Git's credential helpers in the contrib directory (libsecret for Linux and osxkeychain for macOS). Thomas Uhle's initial patch aligned these Makefiles with other contrib components, introducing technical improvements like proper config.mak inclusion and install targets. 

Junio Hamano raised two main points in review: defending keeping the primary target name early in osxkeychain/Makefile for documentation purposes, and questioning the $(MAIN) variable indirection in both Makefiles as reducing readability. Thomas explained the historical rationale for $(MAIN) - it simplified a prior rename from git-credential-gnome-keyring - and proposed $(GIT_CREDENTIAL_HELPER) as a clearer alternative. 

In his latest email, Thomas seeks guidance on two open questions: whether to simply resend the problematic "format=flowed" patch (which can corrupt Makefile whitespace) or submit a formal v2, and whether to proceed with renaming $(MAIN) to $(GIT_CREDENTIAL_HELPER). The discussion remains focused on build system conventions rather than functional changes to the helpers themselves, with Thomas appearing ready to adopt Junio's preferences on both the submission format and variable naming.
