This thread discusses standardizing the Makefiles for Git's credential helpers in the `contrib` directory (libsecret for Linux and osxkeychain for macOS). Thomas Uhle's initial patch aligned these with other contrib components by improving config.mak* inclusion, flag handling, and adding proper install targets. 

Junio Hamano reviewed the changes, defending keeping the primary target name early in osxkeychain/Makefile for documentation purposes and questioning the $(MAIN) variable indirection as potentially reducing readability. Thomas responded by explaining the historical reason for $(MAIN) - it simplified the rename from gnome-keyring to libsecret - and suggested $(GIT_CREDENTIAL_HELPER) as a clearer alternative. 

The discussion remains focused on build system conventions, with agreement on the value of standardization but ongoing consideration of whether to keep the variable indirection for maintainability or switch to direct naming for clarity. Both parties are maintaining a constructive technical discussion about these implementation details.
