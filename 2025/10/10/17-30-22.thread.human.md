Thomas Uhle started a thread proposing improvements to the Makefiles for Git's credential helpers (libsecret for Linux and osxkeychain for macOS) in the contrib directory. The changes aimed to standardize these Makefiles with other contrib components by adding proper build flags, installation targets, and pattern rules while following established conventions.

Junio Hamano responded with feedback on two specific aspects. He agreed with the overall approach but questioned the use of a $(MAIN) variable indirection, arguing it reduced readability more than it helped. He also noted that keeping the primary target name early in the osxkeychain Makefile served as useful documentation. The discussion remains focused on build system improvements with no functional changes to the credential helpers themselves, and the tone is constructive and technical.
