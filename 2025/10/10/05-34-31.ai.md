Platform compatibility follow-up discussing alternative clang warning suppression approach.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Johannes Schindelin's patch addressing clang's `-Wunreachable` warning in the files refs backend. While agreeing with the general approach of using `NOT_CONSTANT()` to suppress the false positive warning, Steinhardt suggests an alternative implementation that would apply the macro at the `create_ref_symlink()` definition site rather than its call site. His untested patch modifies the `NO_SYMLINK_HEAD` case to wrap the hardcoded `(-1)` return in `NOT_CONSTANT()`, which would automatically propagate the suppression to all callers.

The key technical difference is whether to handle the warning suppression locally at the single callsite (as in Johannes's original patch) or globally at the function definition. Steinhardt notes this is currently the only callsite anyway, making his suggestion more of a conceptual alternative than a practical improvement. He explicitly states no strong preference and defers to Johannes's judgment.

This maintains the thread's focus on platform-specific compiler warning suppression while exploring implementation variations. The discussion remains technical and collaborative, with both approaches achieving the same runtime behavior and warning suppression goal. No new concerns or objections are raised about the core solution.
