This is a follow-up in the platform compatibility thread about suppressing a clang compiler warning in the refs backend. The discussion has narrowed to two equivalent technical solutions for using `NOT_CONSTANT()` to silence the warning about unreachable code on `NO_SYMLINK_HEAD` platforms.

Johannes Schindelin responds to Patrick Steinhardt's alternative implementation suggestion, preferring to keep the original approach that applies `NOT_CONSTANT()` at the callsite in `create_symref_locked()` rather than modifying the `create_ref_symlink()` macro definition. His rationale is that compiler workarounds should be placed closer to where the symptom appears.

The thread has already reached consensus that this warning suppression is the right approach, with agreement from Junio Hamano and Patrick that this is a reasonable interim solution while planning to eventually deprecate the `core.preferSymlinkRefs` configuration. The discussion now appears to be wrapping up with Johannes making the final implementation choice between two functionally identical options.
