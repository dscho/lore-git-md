This is a documentation discussion email in the ongoing thread about Julia Evans' `gitdatamodel.adoc` man page series. Ben Knoble responds to Julia's beta reader feedback with additional insights about reference naming constraints.

Ben points out that beyond the directory/file naming conflict issue Julia mentioned (where `julia/ticket` and `julia/ticket/task` can't coexist), case-insensitive filesystems introduce another limitation - branches differing only in case (like `julia` vs `JULIA`) cannot exist simultaneously. This has caused real-world problems when users try to fetch case-variant branches.

The email continues the thread's exploration of how much implementation detail to include in the documentation. Ben supports Julia's approach of teaching through Git commands rather than `.git/` directory structure, noting that plumbing commands like `git cat-file` already provide raw data access without filesystem knowledge. However, he acknowledges the format strings for viewing references and reflogs are complex and potentially error-prone.

This exchange highlights the challenge of documenting Git's data model while balancing pedagogical clarity, technical accuracy, and the realities of different storage backends (files vs reftable). The discussion remains focused on finding the right level of implementation detail to explain constraints without overwhelming readers.
