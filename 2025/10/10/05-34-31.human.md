This is a follow-up discussion in the platform compatibility thread about suppressing a clang compiler warning in the refs backend. Patrick Steinhardt suggests an alternative approach to Johannes Schindelin's original solution for the `-Wunreachable` warning issue.

Patrick proposes moving the `NOT_CONSTANT()` wrapper directly into the `create_ref_symlink()` macro definition for platforms with `NO_SYMLINK_HEAD`, rather than applying it at the callsite as Johannes did. He notes this would address the warning at its source, though acknowledges Johannes's solution is also reasonable since there's only one callsite.

The discussion remains technical and collegial, with Patrick explicitly stating his suggestion can be disregarded if preferred. This appears to be a minor optimization consideration rather than a substantive disagreement with the already-approved approach. The core solution (using `NOT_CONSTANT()` to suppress the false positive warning) remains unchanged between both versions.
