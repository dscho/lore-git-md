This is a brief technical discussion in the ongoing refactoring of `builtin/repack.c` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The exchange between Taylor Blau and Jeff King focuses on patch 04/49, which removes `the_repository` usage from the `remove_redundant_pack()` function by passing a `struct repository *` parameter instead.

Jeff King notes that while the change is good, there might be an opportunity to tighten the interface further by passing an `object_source` pointer rather than the full repository pointer. However, he acknowledges this would require modifying `clear_midx_file()` and that such refinements could reasonably be left for future work. Taylor agrees, suggesting this could be marked as #leftoverbits (Git's term for follow-up cleanups).

The discussion shows both developers finding a practical balance between ideal interface design and incremental progress in the larger refactoring effort. This patch appears to be a straightforward improvement that moves the codebase toward eliminating global state while avoiding unnecessary churn of related interfaces.
