Refactoring patch in the `git refs optimize` series, continuing the transition from pack-related terminology to optimize terminology. This patch renames `pack_refs_opts` to `optimize_refs_opts` and updates all related flag names (`PACK_REFS_*` to `OPTIMIZE_REFS_*`) across the codebase. The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching multiple backend implementations (files, packed, reftable) and their interfaces. This maintains consistency after the previous removal of the `pack_refs()` function, further cleaning up the refs subsystem's internal API. The patch includes thorough updates to documentation strings and maintains the same flag semantics under new names. As part of a larger series introducing a `--required` flag for optimization checks, this change helps establish a clearer conceptual model where "optimize" is the primary operation rather than "pack".
