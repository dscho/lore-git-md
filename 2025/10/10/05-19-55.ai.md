This is a review comment on an early patch in Taylor Blau's major refactoring series for `builtin/repack.c`, part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Jeff King (Peff) examines the approach of passing a `struct repository*` through the `existing_packs` struct rather than more narrowly scoped objects like `object_database` or `packfile_store`.

Peff acknowledges the current approach is pragmatically sound, noting that while passing the repository pointer might seem conceptually broad (since `existing_packs` doesn't inherently need the full repository context), it provides future flexibility and matches Git's typical access patterns where repository objects are readily available. He speculates this pattern will recur throughout the series.

The comment highlights an architectural tension in the refactoring - balancing precise scoping against practical convenience. Peff suggests theoretically "more correct" alternatives but ultimately endorses the chosen approach as the most maintainable path forward. This exchange exemplifies the careful consideration going into the repack refactoring's design decisions.
