This thread addressed a subtle bug in Git's pattern matching where `foo**/bar` incorrectly matched `foobar` in `.gitignore` and `.gitattributes` files, contrary to documentation requiring slashes around `**` for special meaning. Jeff King (Peff) diagnosed the issue in `match_pathname()`'s optimization that stripped matching prefixes before `fnmatch()`, losing directory boundary context.

After benchmarking alternatives, Jeff proposed a "minusone" solution that preserves one character of context during prefix stripping. His benchmarks showed this maintained a 1.46x speedup for exact matches while fixing the bug. Junio Hamano reviewed the approach, suggesting structural improvements that led to a two-patch series: first reorganizing the code to check complete matches earlier, then implementing the context-preserving fix.

The second patch, now posted, implements the solution by decrementing the prefix length by 1 before `fnmatch()`, with test cases verifying correct `**` behavior. The change is minimal (7 lines) but effectively balances correctness and performance. With no outstanding objections and clear test coverage, this appears to be the final resolution - a good example of Git's careful approach to optimization-preserving bugfixes.
