This is a documentation patch clarifying the `git-blame` manpage. Philip Patsch noticed that the terms "sourceline" and "resultline" in `git blame --incremental` output weren't clearly explained in the documentation, leading to confusion (as evidenced by a Stack Overflow question).

The patch adds explicit definitions for these terms to `git-blame.adoc`. The changes are straightforward - just 7 lines added to clarify these technical terms. While the patch is simple, it addresses a real documentation gap that has tripped up users before.

The author notes they couldn't locally verify the manpage build due to docbook toolchain issues, but the changes are minimal and focused enough that this shouldn't be a major concern. This seems like a useful quality-of-life improvement for anyone working with `git blame`'s incremental output format.
