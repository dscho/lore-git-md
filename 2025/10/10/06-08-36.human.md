This is a naming convention discussion in the ongoing repack.c refactoring series (tb/incremental-midx-part-3). Jeff King (Peff) notes an inconsistency in how functions and structs from the repack module are being named as they're moved out of builtin/repack.c.

The key observation is that while `remove_redundant_pack()` was given a `repack_` prefix to avoid namespace collisions, the new `existing_packs` struct and related API were not similarly namespaced. Peff isn't insisting on a change - he acknowledges the current names are probably fine in practice - but raises the question of whether they should follow the same pattern for consistency and clarity.

This is part of the broader technical review of Taylor Blau's 49-part series to modularize Git's repack functionality. The discussion reflects the careful attention being paid to API design and naming conventions as this complex subsystem is restructured. Peff's feedback continues his pattern of thoughtful, constructive review that identifies potential issues without demanding changes when the current approach is reasonable.
