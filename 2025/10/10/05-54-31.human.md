Refactoring discussion about repack's delta_base_offset handling.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Taylor Blau's patch that moves the `delta_base_offset` static variable into `struct pack_objects_args`. While acknowledging the technical correctness of the change, Peff questions whether this is the right level of abstraction, since both regular and cruft pack-objects invocations will always use the same value. He suggests the flag might better belong in the higher-level `repack_ctx` struct instead, but recognizes this could complicate the interface for `prepare_pack_objects()`.

The discussion highlights an ongoing tension in the repack refactoring between precise parameter passing and practical convenience. Peff's main concern is about maintainability - the risk of forgetting to copy the flag between structs - but he concedes the current approach is workable since there's only one place where the value needs to be transferred. This exchange continues the pattern of careful technical review that has characterized the entire repack refactoring series.
