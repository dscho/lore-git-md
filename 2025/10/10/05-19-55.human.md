This is a review comment on a refactoring patch from Taylor Blau's ongoing effort to modularize `builtin/repack.c` and eliminate `the_repository` usage. The patch in question (02/49 in the series) adds a `struct repository*` field to `existing_packs` to avoid using the global `the_repository`.

Jeff King (Peff) provides thoughtful feedback on the design choice. While noting it feels slightly odd to pass a full repository struct when only the packfile store is needed, he acknowledges the practical benefits - having the repo available may help future refactoring, and the alternative (passing narrower objects) wouldn't provide much advantage. He concludes the current approach is reasonable and expects similar patterns will appear elsewhere in the series.

The comment shows careful consideration of the tradeoffs in this architectural change, with Peff ultimately agreeing with Taylor's direction while articulating his thought process for future reference. This kind of design discussion is typical for such a large-scale refactoring effort.
