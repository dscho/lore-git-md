This is a follow-up in the thread about standardizing Makefiles for Git credential helpers (libsecret and osxkeychain). Thomas Uhle responds to Junio Hamano's review of the initial patch, addressing two specific points of feedback.

First, regarding the placement of the primary target in osxkeychain/Makefile, Thomas acknowledges Hamano's preference for keeping it early as documentation, explaining this wasn't done in other Makefiles due to the $(MAIN) variable indirection pattern. Second, on the $(MAIN) variable itself, Thomas reveals its historical rationale - it made renaming from git-credential-gnome-keyring to git-credential-libsecret easier by requiring only one line change. He proposes $(GIT_CREDENTIAL_HELPER) as a potentially clearer alternative variable name.

The response maintains the technical focus of the thread while providing useful context about the variable naming convention's origins. Thomas reiterates his original motivation was adding missing install targets while aligning with other contrib/Makefile patterns. The discussion remains constructive, with both parties agreeing on the value of standardization while debating specific implementation choices. The key open question remains whether to keep the $(MAIN) indirection or adopt direct target naming for better readability.
