This is a refactoring patch in a series standardizing reference backend optimization operations. Patrick Steinhardt provides a review of patch 3/9, which renames `pack_refs_opts` to `optimize_refs_opts` to match the new unified optimization interface. These are minor documentation-level changes that don't affect functionality but would improve naming consistency. The review is positive overall, indicating the series is progressing well with its goal of consolidating reference optimization interfaces.
