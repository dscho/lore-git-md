This is a follow-up discussion on the build system patch standardizing Makefiles for Git's credential helpers. Junio C Hamano responds to Thomas Uhle's proposed changes to the libsecret and osxkeychain credential helper Makefiles.

The response focuses on two main points: First, Hamano notes that having the primary target name early in the Makefile serves as useful documentation, suggesting this aspect of the original osxkeychain Makefile had value. Second, he questions the introduction of the $(MAIN) variable in both Makefiles, arguing that while it might offer minor convenience during writing, it actually hurts readability for the more common case of reading the Makefiles.

Hamano generally approves of the effort to standardize these Makefiles, noting it's good to make them similar for easier comparison, but offers these specific readability concerns about the current implementation. The discussion remains technical and constructive, focused on optimizing the Makefiles for maintainability.
