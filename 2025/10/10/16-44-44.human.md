Maintainer feedback on Ubuntu 25.10 CI fix. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch addressing CI breakage caused by Ubuntu's switch to sudo-rs. While acknowledging the issue, Hamano suggests two potential improvements: (1) making the sudo implementation switch conditional on `/usr/bin/sudo.ws` existing rather than just checking `/etc/alternatives/sudo`, and (2) possibly moving the fix into a helper function shared between the `ubuntu-*` and `i386/ubuntu-*` cases. The feedback indicates the core approach is sound but could be made more precise in its scope. This appears to be a straightforward refinement of the original solution rather than a fundamental objection.
