This is a summary of discussions from the 2025 Git Contributor's Summit, serving as a starting point for community discussion. Taylor Blau shares notes from the event covering 11 major topics that were discussed, including SHA-256 interoperability, first-class conflict handling, Rust integration, pluggable object databases, and plans for Git 3.0. The notes are available in both email and Google Docs format, with more detailed breakdowns of each topic discussion promised in follow-up emails. The email serves as both a record of the event and an invitation for broader community feedback on the discussed topics and the summit format itself.
