Documentation patch refining the proposed `gitdatamodel.adoc` man page, addressing review feedback on Git's core concepts. Julia Evans responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's detailed review, focusing on several key documentation decisions.

The author agrees to incorporate full ref names (like `refs/heads/main`) while reducing direct `.git` directory references, including packed refs. She defends the inclusion of reflogs as a fundamental concept due to their undo capabilities, while acknowledging they're auxiliary data. The discussion of `git gc` and packfiles remains contentious - Evans wants to include implementation details to address common user misconceptions about storage efficiency, but recognizes this may be too technical.

Several style and structural issues are being addressed: fixing term formatting (symbolic references), considering cross-references to other man pages, and evaluating the document's discoverability. The author plans to gather beta reader feedback on confusing sections before finalizing. This continues the effort to create a conceptual foundation for Git's data model, now balancing technical accuracy with approachability for new users.
