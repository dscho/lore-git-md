Documentation patch improving the introductory text of `git-push` for clarity. This is the first of five patches in Julia Evans' v5 series that refines push-related explanations in the documentation.

The patch addresses user confusion about technical terms like "refs" and "objects" in the `git-push` man page introduction. Based on feedback from 5 users who found these terms unclear and 3 who didn't understand "complete the refs," the patch uses more accessible language ("branches, tags, or other references") and moves the discussion of receive hooks to later in the document where it won't distract from the core explanation.

The changes make the introduction more approachable for users who may not be familiar with Git's internal terminology while maintaining technical accuracy. The patch is part of a larger effort to improve push documentation that has already gone through several rounds of review and refinement (now at version 5), with this particular change focusing on the initial impression users get when reading about `git push`.
