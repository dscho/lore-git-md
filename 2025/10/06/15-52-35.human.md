This is a discussion about the ongoing bugfix for HEAD ref name validation in the files backend. Junio C Hamano and Jeff King are debating the validation approach in the `split_symref_update()` function.

The key point of contention is whether validating symref names at this layer is necessary, since `split_symref_update()` operates on existing symrefs that should have already been validated when created. Jeff King argues the validation might be redundant as `resolve_ref_unsafe()` already performs some validation, and suggests the real issue might be with repositories that became corrupted through other means.

Junio acknowledges this perspective, recalling that Karthik had previously noted the test case essentially demonstrates behavior with a corrupt repository. Both maintainers seem to agree the current patch's validation approach might not be the most robust solution, with Jeff hinting at a more comprehensive symref validation series he plans to send soon.

The discussion reveals deeper questions about where validation should occur in the refs subsystem and how to handle potentially corrupt repository states. The conversation is moving toward waiting for Jeff's upcoming series that addresses broader symref validation gaps, which may provide a better solution than the current patch's focused approach.
