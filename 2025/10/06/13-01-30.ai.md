- Type consistency: fixing `size_t` vs `int` iterator mismatch noted by Patrick
- Comment style: correcting multi-line comment syntax from `/**` to `/*` per Junio Hamano's observation
- The patch maintains existing functionality while implementing these fixes

The changes respond to specific review feedback from both Patrick and Junio, showing good engagement with reviewer comments.
