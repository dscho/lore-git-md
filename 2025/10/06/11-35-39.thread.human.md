For the UI, they now propose reordering the output to show the drop confirmation first, followed by an updated stash count that reflects the post-pop state. This would make the output both accurate and non-redundant.

The thread appears to be converging on this approach, which would modify `builtin/stash.c` to use the `--no-show-stash` flag and reorder the output. Once the exact phrasing and ordering are agreed upon, the next step would be a revised patch with test coverage for the new behavior.
