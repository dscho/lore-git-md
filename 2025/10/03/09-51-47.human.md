**Licensing discussion follow-up**

Phillip Wood responds to Jeff King's clarification about Rust code licensing in Git, specifically regarding the GitOxide project. The exchange sharpens the focus on two key legal challenges: crafting appropriate relicensing grants for new Rust code, and handling the complex authorship history of existing code that might be ported.

Wood agrees with King that the core issue remains unchanged for existing code, regardless of whether the license exception targets GitOxide specifically or uses a more general permissive approach. He raises concerns that project-specific exceptions (like those modeled after libgit2's git.git-authors file) may introduce additional complications beyond the already difficult relicensing process.

The email suggests consulting the Software Freedom Conservancy for guidance and inquires whether this topic was discussed at the recent Contributor's Summit, indicating these licensing questions may require formal legal review. This continues the thread's pattern of deepening policy discussions while technical implementation remains on hold pending resolution of these governance questions.
