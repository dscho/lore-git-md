Julia Evans has proposed a new `gitdatamodel.adoc` man page to provide a structured explanation of Git's core concepts (objects, references, index, and reflogs), addressing a gap in the existing documentation. The patch carefully balances conceptual clarity with technical accuracy, omitting some implementation details like pseudorefs and submodules to maintain focus.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk has provided detailed feedback on the proposal, praising the overall approach while suggesting refinements. Key discussion points include whether to mention packed refs (which Julia had noted but Kristoffer questions including) and whether to show full ref names like `refs/heads/main` (which Kristoffer argues is important for users). Other suggestions touch on wording improvements, cross-references, and the appropriate level of implementation detail, particularly regarding the upcoming reftable backend. The conversation remains constructive, with Kristoffer's feedback identifying several areas where the documentation could be strengthened while maintaining its conceptual focus.
