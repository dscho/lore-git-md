After five iterations and extensive review from key contributors including Junio Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and others, the technical content has been finalized with particular attention to accurately distinguishing between Git objects and their hexadecimal names while acknowledging the special role these names play in Git's reference system.

The discussion has now shifted to pedagogical presentation, with D. Evans references her teaching approach that often uses command output (like `git cat-file -p`) to explain relationships, suggesting this may be more effective than complex diagrams for the document's purpose. This final exchange represents the polishing of presentation choices after all substantive technical issues were resolved, and may influence how future Git documentation incorporates visual aids.
