The current discussion focuses on refining terminology around commit parents, reference naming, and reflogs.

The thread shows how the documentation is carefully balancing accuracy with accessibility, with both technical experts and educators contributing to shape the final result.

With most major questions now resolved, the remaining open items are relatively small - edge cases around symbolic references, some terminology refinements, and presentation details.
