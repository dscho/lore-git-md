Julia Evans is working on a new `gitdatamodel.adoc` man page to explain Git's core concepts (objects, references, index, and reflogs), filling a gap in the documentation. The patch has received detailed review feedback from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, who suggested including full ref names in examples and reconsidering the exclusion of packed refs, given the ongoing reftable backend work. Junio Hamano flagged a CI failure due to the missing SYNOPSIS section, a structural requirement for all man pages.

Julia has acknowledged the CI issue and will add the required SYNOPSIS section. The discussion continues on several open questions, including how much detail to include about packed refs, where to place the garbage collection discussion, and the optimal density of cross-references. This patch is part of a broader effort to improve Git's conceptual documentation while aligning with Jean-Noël Avila's standardization of man page formatting.
