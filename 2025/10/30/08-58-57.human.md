This is a follow-up discussion on patch 5/8 of the packfile store abstraction series, focusing on code simplification in `builtin/pack-objects.c`. 

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's review of his earlier patch that simplified object lookup logic by using `repo_for_each_pack()`. Taylor had two observations: (1) the `(void *)1` sentinel check appears unnecessary since `repo_for_each_pack()` won't return such values, and (2) there might be further optimization potential using `kept_cache` for local-but-kept packs.

Patrick agrees with both points - he'll remove the redundant sentinel check entirely (noting NULL would work just as well) and acknowledges the optimization opportunity, though he proposes deferring that improvement to a future series to keep the current changes focused on readability improvements. The exchange shows good technical alignment between the developers, with minor cleanups being incorporated while more substantial optimizations are intentionally postponed.
