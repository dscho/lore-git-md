The patch had already received positive reviews from Taylor Blau and was considered ready for inclusion, with previous agreement to remove the `(void *)1` sentinel check.

Toon Claes points out that the `last_found` cache check could be moved earlier in the loop for better efficiency, building on the earlier optimization discussion. Patrick Steinhardt immediately acknowledges this as a good catch and agrees with the suggested change. This represents a minor but straightforward improvement to the already-reviewed patch.

The change affects `builtin/pack-objects.c` and specifically the logic for finding kept or nonlocal objects. The optimization moves the `last_found` cache check to the beginning of the loop, which should provide slightly better performance by avoiding unnecessary iterations when the cached pack is found early. This refinement maintains the series' readiness for inclusion while incorporating one last optimization suggestion.
