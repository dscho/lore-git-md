This is the cover letter for an 8-part refactoring series in the packfile subsystem, part of the ongoing packfile store abstraction effort. The series moves packfile list management into the packfile store, eliminating global linked lists in favor of per-store tracking. This prepares for future integration with object sources.

Key changes in v2 include:
- Typo fixes in commit messages
- Optimization to avoid opening pack indexes unnecessarily in `repo_approximate_object_count()`
- Further simplifications to `has_sha1_pack_kept_or_nonlocal()`
- Reworded motivation for MRU list handling changes

The most notable change is in patch 7/8, which now unconditionally adds packfiles to the MRU list when they're added to the store, fixing a fragile edge case where packfiles could be inaccessible until the store was reprepared. The final patch (8/8) consolidates the dual packfile lists into just the MRU list, though Patrick notes some concerns about potential infinite loops and invites extra review of this change.

The series has already incorporated feedback from reviewers (Taylor Blau and Toon Claes) and appears ready for inclusion, with all major design questions resolved. The changes are focused on packfile.c/h but touch several other files that interact with packfiles. This represents the second-to-last step in Patrick's planned refactoring of the packfile subsystem.
