Maintainer response on git clean -X behavior ambiguity.

Junio Hamano responds to Johannes Schindelin's analysis of the `git clean -X` pathspec handling bug, acknowledging the two possible interpretations of how exclusions should interact with the `-X` flag. As the Git maintainer, Hamano states he has no personal preference between the two behavioral interpretations since he rarely uses `-X` himself and lacks strong motivation to change its current behavior.

Key points:
- Hamano confirms the decision ultimately rests with the maintainer (himself)
- He acknowledges Schindelin's clear explanation of the two possible interpretations
- Expresses no personal stake in the outcome ("not really my thing")
- Cautions against changing behavior without strong justification due to risk of disrupting existing users
- Implicitly accepts the current behavior as valid if it can be logically explained

The response effectively puts the ball back in Schindelin's court - while not rejecting either interpretation, Hamano indicates he won't drive a change himself. This suggests the next step would be for Schindelin or other interested parties to propose a specific resolution, either documenting the current behavior as intended or submitting a patch to alter it with appropriate justification. The regression test provided earlier remains valuable for either path forward.
