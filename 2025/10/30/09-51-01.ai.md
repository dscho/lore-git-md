Patrick Steinhardt responds to Toon Claes' questions about two key aspects of the Rust-C interop build system. While not ideal, he agrees this is a reasonable tradeoff given the current single-subsystem Rust implementation and simplified build setup. He suggests Ezekiel Newren's proposed Rust workspace structure might help address this in the future, but considers it out of scope for the current series.
