Junio Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's suggestion about maintenance release candidates in the context of the NonStop platform issues. He questions the value of maintenance release candidates, pointing out that 'master' already serves as a continuous test bed where changes must first pass through the 'next' branch. 

Hamano notes that if NonStop-specific issues exist, they should ideally be caught during the normal development cycle on 'master' or 'next' rather than waiting for maintenance releases. He expresses skepticism that adding another pre-release step for maintenance branches would help, since those who aren't testing 'master' likely wouldn't test maintenance candidates either.

The response maintains the thread's focus on release engineering practices while challenging the proposed solution's effectiveness. It implicitly supports Randall Becker's earlier position favoring stability in maintenance releases, though from a different angle - questioning whether the suggested process change would actually catch the types of issues seen on NonStop.
