This is the final patch in a refactoring series that consolidates Git's packfile tracking into a single MRU (most-recently-used) list, eliminating the previous dual-list approach. The change simplifies the packfile subsystem as part of the ongoing packfile store abstraction effort.

The patch removes the separate MRU list entirely, repurposing the main `packs` list to track packfiles in MRU order. This addresses concerns that the previous dual-list approach caused confusion and didn't clearly benefit performance. The author argues that MRU ordering better handles both recent and historical object access patterns compared to the age-based ordering previously used for the main list.

A key technical challenge is preventing infinite loops during packfile iteration - when `for_each_packed_object()` would modify the list it's iterating over. The solution introduces a `skip_mru_updates` flag to temporarily disable MRU reordering during such operations. The changes affect core packfile operations in `packfile.c` and require updates to pack-objects and iteration logic.

This patch follows extensive discussion and review, with all major design questions resolved in earlier iterations. While the series is otherwise ready for inclusion, this final consolidation step was flagged for extra scrutiny due to potential fragility in the MRU handling. The implementation appears thorough, with clear documentation of the edge case handling.
