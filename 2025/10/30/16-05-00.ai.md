This is a follow-up discussion about documentation of pattern matching behavior differences between gitignore and pathspecs, building on the recently resolved bug fix for `**` pattern handling.

Ben Knoble and Jeff King continue their examination of the subtle differences between gitignore patterns and pathspec matching. The key points raised:

1. Pathspec anchoring behavior differs fundamentally from gitignore - pathspecs are implicitly anchored to path start while gitignore patterns are not unless explicitly anchored with `/`. This means `foo` in gitignore matches `some/dir/foo` but the same as a pathspec would not.

2. Additional differences include pathspecs allowing `*` to match directory separators (unlike gitignore behavior) and the literal matching behavior of pathspecs that isn't fully clear from current documentation.

3. Jeff acknowledges the pathspec documentation could be improved but expresses caution about changing behavior due to backward compatibility concerns. He notes some of these differences may be "pointless inconsistencies" but the impact of changing them is unknown.

4. Both agree this documentation work could be handled separately from the current gitignore pattern fixes, with Jeff noting he doesn't plan to tackle it himself.

The discussion highlights ongoing documentation challenges around clearly explaining these subtle but important differences between related pattern-matching subsystems in Git. While the technical implementation questions have been resolved, the conversation surfaces potential areas for future documentation improvements regarding pathspec behavior and its relationship to gitignore patterns.
