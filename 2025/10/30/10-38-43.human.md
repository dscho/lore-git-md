This is part 6 of an 8-part refactoring series moving packfile management into a new `packfile_store` abstraction. The patch moves the main packfile list from being a simple linked list in `struct packed_git` to being managed by the new `packfile_list` API within `struct packfile_store`.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, updating all code that previously traversed the `next` pointer directly to instead use the new list API. This includes packfile window scanning, pack closing, MRU list building, and object lookup operations. The patch also removes the now-unused `find_oid_pack()` helper since its functionality is covered by the new list API.

This continues the series' pattern of centralizing packfile management in the store abstraction. The changes appear straightforward and well-contained, with no behavior changes intended. The patch builds on previous work in the series and follows the same approach used for the MRU list migration in earlier patches.

Reviewers have generally approved the series' direction, though some implementation details around list management were discussed earlier in the thread. This patch appears to incorporate that feedback while maintaining the series' goal of cleaner packfile management.
