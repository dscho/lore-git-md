This is the final patch in a refactoring series for Git's packfile subsystem, consolidating the dual packfile lists into a single MRU (most-recently-used) list. The patch eliminates the separate `mru` list in favor of using the main `packs` list with MRU ordering, addressing concerns about the previous dual-list approach's complexity and potential confusion.

Key technical changes:
- Removes `struct packfile_store::mru` entirely, repurposing the `packs` list for MRU ordering
- Introduces a `skip_mru_updates` flag to prevent infinite loops during `for_each_packed_object()` iteration
- Updates all packfile access patterns to use the unified MRU-ordered list
- Removes now-unnecessary MRU preparation functions and accessors
- Maintains the same MRU behavior for object lookups while simplifying the underlying structure

The patch handles a critical edge case where iterating over all packed objects could cause infinite loops due to MRU reordering during iteration. This is addressed by temporarily disabling MRU updates during such operations via the new `skip_mru_updates` flag.

The changes affect core packfile operations in `packfile.c`, `packfile.h`, and `builtin/pack-objects.c`, with the most significant impact being the removal of the separate MRU list maintenance. The patch represents the culmination of the series' effort to simplify packfile tracking while maintaining performance characteristics.

This follows previous discussion where Patrick Steinhardt had expressed concerns about the fragility of the dual-list approach, and now provides the consolidation solution while addressing the iteration edge case. The series as a whole has received positive feedback from reviewers Taylor Blau and Toon Claes, with all prior feedback incorporated.
