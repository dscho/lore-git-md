This is a refactoring patch that simplifies packfile tracking in Git's packfile subsystem. It's the first in an 8-part series aimed at restructuring packfile management as part of the packfile store abstraction effort.

The patch replaces a custom hashmap implementation with Git's existing `strmap` utility for tracking packfiles by name. The change reduces code by 18 lines while maintaining the same functionality - packfiles can still be quickly looked up by their path names. The modification touches core packfile handling in `packfile.c` and its header file, removing the custom comparison function and hashmap entry from the `packed_git` struct.

Taylor Blau has already reviewed this change positively in previous iterations. The patch appears straightforward and well-contained, focusing solely on this internal data structure change without affecting external behavior. It serves as groundwork for subsequent patches in the series that will further restructure packfile management.
