This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing packfile store abstraction effort, specifically addressing the MRU (most-recently-used) list management for packfiles. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's review questions about patch 2/8 in the series.

The patch moves the MRU list from being embedded in `struct packed_git` to a new `struct packfile_list` within `struct packfile_store`. Taylor had questioned why a new list type was needed and how alternates would be handled. Patrick clarifies that this series is preparatory work - it doesn't yet implement per-source packfile lists but sets up the infrastructure for that future change. The alternates handling will come in a subsequent series.

Patrick explains two key motivations for the new list type: (1) to avoid the foot-gun of global list assumptions when the lists should be per-store, and (2) to better separate concerns between packfiles (which should be standalone) and the store (which manages collections of them. The change makes the code's structure more explicit and easier to reason about.

The discussion shows careful consideration of architectural boundaries, with Patrick emphasizing the importance of clean separation between packfiles and their management. Taylor's questions have been satisfactorily addressed, and the patch appears to be on solid technical footing as part of the larger refactoring effort.
