This is a technical clarification in the ongoing packfile store refactoring series, specifically addressing questions about patch 2/8 which moves the MRU packfile list into `struct packfile_store`. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's review comments about alternates handling and the rationale for introducing a new `struct packfile_list`.

Key points clarified:
1. The current series doesn't yet implement per-alternate packfile stores - that's planned for a future series. Currently packfiles remain in a global list across alternates.
2. The new `struct packfile_list` serves two purposes: (a) avoiding the foot-gun of exposing list pointers in `struct packed_git` which could imply global scope, and (b) maintaining cleaner separation where `packed_git` handles single packfiles while the store manages collections.
3. The change makes the code's structure match the intended mental model more clearly and prepares for future separation where packfiles exist independently of object stores (important for transport layers).

Technical context:
- Maintains the distinction between MRU-ordered and mtime-ordered packfile lists
- Preserves current global behavior while laying groundwork for per-source management
- Affects packfile.c/h with new list management structure
- Part of broader effort to support pluggable object databases

The discussion resolves Taylor's questions about alternates handling and validates the design approach, with Patrick explaining the architectural benefits of the new list type. This maintains forward progress on the series with all major concerns addressed.
