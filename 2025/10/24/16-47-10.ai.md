Performance optimization in the commit-reach code. René Scharfe replaces quadratic-time `commit_list_insert_by_date()` calls with a more efficient pattern of appending commits and sorting once at the end. The change affects three functions in commit-reach.c that find merge bases: `paint_down_to_common()`, `merge_bases_many()`, and `get_merge_bases_many_0()`.

The patch introduces a new performance test (t/perf/p6010-merge-base.sh) that creates a complex merge history to demonstrate the improvement. In the test case with 16384 merge bases, the change yields a 94.5% speedup (0.55s -> 0.03s). The commit message notes this won't affect normal repositories where merge base counts are small, but protects against pathological cases.

Key technical details: The change replaces individual `commit_list_insert_by_date()` calls with `commit_list_append()` to a list tail, followed by a single `commit_list_sort_by_date()`. The performance test builds two parallel commit histories with 2^15 merge bases between their tips to stress the algorithm. The test infrastructure uses git fast-import with marks to construct the test repository.
