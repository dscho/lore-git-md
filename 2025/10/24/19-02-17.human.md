This is a discussion about configuring the geometric repack factor in Git's maintenance tasks, part of the ongoing geometric repacking strategy series that has already received maintainer approval.

The conversation clarifies the relationship between two proposed configuration options: `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor` (specific to maintenance tasks) and a potential future `repack.geometricFactor` (for `git repack` generally). Taylor Blau and Patrick Steinhardt agree that while both could coexist, the maintenance-specific config makes sense in its context since all other maintenance tasks follow the `maintenance.*` naming pattern.

The key points settled here are:
1. The maintenance task will use its own config value when calling `git repack --geometric=<n>`
2. A future `repack.geometricFactor` could provide defaults for bare `--geometric` usage
3. The maintenance config would override the general repack config when both exist
4. The current approach is acceptable despite not providing a single global default for all geometric repacking

This appears to be finalizing details on an already-approved feature, with both contributors in agreement about the design tradeoffs. The discussion has a "wrapping up" tone, with Taylor calling these remaining items "#leftoverbits".
