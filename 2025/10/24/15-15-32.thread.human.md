This thread introduces a helpful documentation improvement to the MyFirstContribution guide, aimed at making the patch submission process less daunting for newcomers. The patch adds a TIP section explaining how to use lore.kernel.org to verify that patches sent via `git send-email` have successfully reached the mailing list - a common point of uncertainty for first-time contributors.

The change is straightforward, adding just 7 lines to MyFirstContribution.adoc immediately after the existing email submission instructions. While the patch appears technically sound, one might suggest expanding it to note that there can be a delay (often minutes to hours) before emails appear in the archive. This would help set appropriate expectations for new contributors checking their submissions.

The patch addresses a real pain point in the contributor experience without introducing any technical complexity, making it a clear quality-of-life improvement for those making their first submissions to the project.
