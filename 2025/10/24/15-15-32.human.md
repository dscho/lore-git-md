This is a documentation patch for the Git contribution guide. Queen Ediri Jessa proposes adding a helpful tip to `MyFirstContribution.adoc` that explains how new contributors can verify their patches successfully reached the mailing list.

The patch adds a new "TIP" section right after the instructions for sending patches with `git send-email`. It suggests checking https://lore.kernel.org/git/ and searching for one's name or patch subject to confirm delivery. This addresses a common point of uncertainty for first-time contributors who may not be familiar with how mailing lists work.

The change is straightforward and well-contained, adding just 7 lines to the documentation. The tip seems genuinely useful for newcomers, providing a concrete way to verify their submission went through. The patch follows Git's documentation style with proper asciidoc formatting.
