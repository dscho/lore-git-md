Patrick Steinhardt's 13-patch series refactoring Git's object handling as part of the ODB abstraction effort has reached completion with Karthik Nayak's final review approval. The series systematically encapsulated loose object operations into a dedicated source interface, standardizing naming conventions and reorganizing header files in the process. 

Karthik, the primary reviewer throughout the series, has now signed off on the last outstanding patch (5/13), which introduces `struct odb_source_loose` to hold loose object state. His approval confirms the naming change from `odb_loose_source` aligns with Junio Hamano's earlier feedback and maintains consistency established across the series. 

The work represents a significant step in the broader ODB abstraction effort, cleanly separating loose object operations while preserving all existing behavior. With all patches mechanically refactored and no outstanding concerns from reviewers, the series appears ready for Junio to consider merging into the mainline. This completes the loose object portion of the ODB work, though follow-ups may still be needed for remaining repository-level functions.
