Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's loose object handling as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort has reached its final stages, with only minor naming conventions left to settle. The 13-patch series has undergone thorough review from both Junio Hamano and Karthik Nayak, with most technical feedback already addressed across three phases of cleanups, interface creation, and caller refactoring.

The current discussion centers on whether the new `struct odb_loose_source` should follow Karthik's suggested `obd_source_loose` pattern for better extensibility to future backends, or retain Patrick's original naming that aligns with existing reference backend conventions. While Patrick plans to keep his version in v2, he remains open to reconsidering if others voice strong support for the alternative. This represents one of the last open questions in a series that has otherwise received consistently positive feedback.

The changes span multiple files and introduce important architectural separation while maintaining behavior, with only `open_istream_loose()` remaining as a repository-level function after these changes. The careful attention to naming patterns reflects the long-term considerations at play as Git moves toward a more extensible object storage architecture. With most technical concerns resolved and only this stylistic preference to settle, the series appears ready for inclusion in the near future.
