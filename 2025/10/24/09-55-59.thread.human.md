Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series has reached completion with the thirteenth and final patch in version 3. This concluding patch adds streaming write support through a new `odb_write_object_stream()` interface, while renaming the low-level implementation to `odb_source_loose_write_stream()` to maintain consistency with the series' established pattern. The change follows the mechanical refactoring approach seen throughout the series, updating callers in builtin/unpack-objects.c and elsewhere to use the new abstraction layer.

The series, which has been progressing through multiple versions with incremental improvements, has focused on consolidating helper functions, standardizing naming (settling on the `odb_source_loose_*` pattern after discussion between Karthik Nayak and Junio Hamano), and reorganizing headers to properly separate concerns. Reviewers Karthik Nayak and Junio Hamano have provided consistently positive feedback, with no outstanding technical concerns remaining. The work represents a significant step in the broader ODB abstraction effort to enable pluggable backends, having now encapsulated all major loose object operations including reading, writing, caching, and state management.
