This is a feature patch in the geometric repacking series, adding configuration support for the geometric split factor in maintenance tasks. The patch introduces a new `maintenance.geometric-repack.splitFactor` config option, allowing users to tune how aggressively packs are merged during geometric repacking (default remains 2). This gives administrators of large repositories finer control over maintenance behavior.

The discussion clarifies that this is distinct from (but compatible with) Taylor Blau's earlier suggestion for a more general `repack.geometricFactor` config. Patrick Steinhardt explains the design choice to keep this maintenance-specific rather than modifying `git repack` directly, maintaining consistency with other maintenance task configurations. The two approaches could coexist with the maintenance-specific setting taking precedence.

This appears to be a well-justified addition to the series, with clear agreement between the developers on both the implementation and its relationship to potential future work. The patch maintains the series' high standard of configuration consistency while addressing real-world needs for large repository maintenance.
