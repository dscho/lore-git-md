This patch extends the maintenance strategy configuration to cover manual maintenance runs, unifying the behavior between scheduled and manual maintenance. It's part of the ongoing geometric repacking strategy series that has already received extensive review and maintainer approval.

The key change makes `maintenance.strategy` apply to both manual (`git maintenance run`) and scheduled maintenance, where previously it only affected scheduled runs. The patch carefully maintains backward compatibility for the "incremental" strategy by keeping its tasks scheduled-only while adding manual GC, matching the historical behavior where manual maintenance would default to GC.

Technical details include:
- Updates to Documentation/config/maintenance.adoc explaining the expanded scope
- Modification of builtin/gc.c to apply strategy regardless of maintenance type
- New test cases in t7900-maintenance.sh verifying strategy behavior
- Special handling for the "incremental" strategy to preserve existing behavior
- Error handling for invalid strategy names

The implementation builds on previous patches in the series that introduced maintenance type flags (MAINTENANCE_TYPE_SCHEDULED/MANUAL) and strategy parsing. This change completes the unification of maintenance strategy handling across all execution contexts while maintaining careful backward compatibility.
