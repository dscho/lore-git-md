CI housekeeping patch in the Rust cbindgen series.

This is the first patch in the v2 series introducing cbindgen support to Git's Rust infrastructure. It reorders the Linux job matrix in GitLab CI to match GitHub's ordering, making it easier to compare configurations between the two CI systems. The change is purely organizational with no functional impact - it simply moves the `linux-TEST-vars` job up in the list and swaps the positions of `linux-meson` and `linux-musl-meson`.

The patch demonstrates attention to developer experience by addressing a minor but legitimate pain point in comparing CI configurations. As part of the broader cbindgen effort, this shows the series is being developed with CI considerations in mind from the start. The change is straightforward and low-risk, touching only the job ordering in `.gitlab-ci.yml`.

This patch follows through on Patrick Steinhardt's earlier commitment to improve GitLab CI test coverage as mentioned in the thread context. It doesn't resolve any of the open questions about header location or build system integration, but serves as preparatory work for the more substantive Rust-C interop changes that follow in the series.
