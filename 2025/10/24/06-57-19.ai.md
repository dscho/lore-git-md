Refactoring patch in the geometric repacking series, focusing on code readability improvements.

This patch restructures the maintenance strategy definitions in builtin/gc.c to use nested designated initializers, making the code more readable by clearly showing which fields belong to which maintenance task. The change was suggested by Taylor Blau and implements a stylistic improvement to the strategy configuration arrays that were introduced earlier in the series.

The patch modifies the initialization of three strategy structs (none_strategy, default_strategy, and incremental_strategy) by nesting the task-specific fields under their respective TASK_* identifiers. This makes the relationship between tasks and their enabled/schedule fields visually clearer in the code. The change is purely cosmetic - no behavior is altered, and all test coverage from previous patches continues to pass.

This appears to be a straightforward quality-of-life improvement for maintainers working with the maintenance strategy code. The change follows the pattern of other recent cleanups in keeping with Git's code style guidelines. As part of the larger geometric repacking series that has already received maintainer approval, this patch represents final polish before integration.
