This is a documentation patch for the MyFirstContribution guide. The patch adds a helpful tip for new contributors about how to confirm their patches successfully reached the mailing list after using `git send-email`. 

The change inserts a new TIP section that explains how to use lore.kernel.org to search for one's name or patch subject to verify delivery. This addresses a common point of uncertainty for first-time contributors who may not be familiar with how to track their submissions. The tip is placed immediately after the existing instructions about sending patches via email.

The patch is straightforward and well-contained, modifying only the MyFirstContribution.adoc file with a 7-line addition. The change is clearly motivated by improving the contributor experience, particularly for newcomers who may not be familiar with mailing list workflows. The commit message is clear and the change appears technically sound, though it might benefit from mentioning that it can take some time (often minutes to hours) for emails to appear in the archive.
