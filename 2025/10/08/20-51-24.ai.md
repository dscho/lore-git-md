Kristoffer had proposed making the topic summary more explicit with markers like "Topic summary:" to improve machine-parsability and human visibility.

The discussion reveals two key points: (1) the original implementation may have suffered from discoverability issues, and (2) there's openness to alternative formatting approaches that could make the experimental practice more visible.

The email focuses specifically on the first part of Taylor Blau's patch series about topic naming conventions, while the second part regarding multi-series submission guidance remains uncontested.
