This is part 5 of a 14-part refactoring and bugfix series modernizing Git's tag peeling behavior in the refs subsystem. The patch focuses on cleaning up the interface for accessing peeled object IDs (the targets of tag objects).

The change introduces a new `peeled_oid` field in `struct reference` that backends can populate, along with a new `reference_get_peeled_oid()` function to access it. This replaces the previous awkward mechanism that relied on global state tracking the current iterator. The patch updates several callers to use the new interface, with more conversions coming in later patches.

The change is straightforward and mechanical - it doesn't change behavior, just provides a cleaner way to access data that was already available. As part of the larger series that has already received extensive review, this patch appears ready to go. It's one of the foundational pieces that enables the performance improvements and safety fixes in later patches.
