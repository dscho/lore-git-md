This is the third patch in Julia Evans' documentation series for `git-pull`, focusing on removing a misleading example from the man page. The example showed a merge scenario, which could confuse users since `git pull` now defaults to `--ff-only` behavior (fast-forward-only merges). The patch simply deletes the outdated example rather than replacing it, as fast-forward scenarios are straightforward enough to describe without visual examples.

The change is a clean 26-line removal from `git-pull.adoc`, continuing the series' goal of making the documentation more accurate and less confusing for users. This follows feedback that the example implied merge was the default behavior. The patch maintains the series' consistent approach of prioritizing clarity and correctness over preserving historical documentation elements.
