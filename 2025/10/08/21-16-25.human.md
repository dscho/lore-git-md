This is a design discussion email in the ongoing `git replay` atomic ref updates series, where Siddharth Asthana confirms the final interface design based on feedback from Elijah Newren and Phillip Wood.

The email resolves the remaining open question about the command-line interface for atomic reference updates in `git replay`. After considering both simple boolean flags (`--[no-]update-refs`) and a more extensible enum-based approach (`--update-refs=<mode>`), Siddharth agrees with Phillip Wood's recommendation to use the enum style for future extensibility. The design will support three modes: `yes` (default atomic updates), `print` (output commands), with room for future additions like `allow-partial`.

The discussion shows consensus forming around this approach, with Elijah Newren explicitly endorsing Phillip Wood's suggestion over his own simpler proposal. Siddharth also notes the config option (`replay.updateRefs`) will mirror the command-line interface for consistency. This appears to be the final design decision needed before the series can be merged, as all major technical questions have now been addressed.
