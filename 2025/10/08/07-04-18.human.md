This is part 3 of Adrian Ratiu's hook subsystem refactoring series, focusing on converting the 'post-rewrite' hook in sequencer.c to use the new hook.h infrastructure. The patch introduces a `pipe_from_strbuf` callback for feeding stdin to hooks.

The email responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review feedback, confirming that potential deadlock scenarios existed in the old implementation too, and that the new approach maintains the same behavior while being more efficient with batched input for high-volume hooks like post-receive. Adrian acknowledges several style and correctness issues that will be addressed in v2, including removing unnecessary `strbuf_reset()` calls and dropping an unrelated variable rename that crept into the patch.

The discussion highlights important considerations around hook performance and deadlock avoidance, though these are not new problems introduced by this refactoring. The patch appears technically sound but will see some cleanup in the next revision based on reviewer feedback. This continues the series' pattern of careful modernization while maintaining backward compatibility.
