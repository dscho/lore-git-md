Based on feedback from 15 Git users, the series aims to better explain `git pull`'s relationship with fetch/merge/rebase, clarify the current `--ff-only` default behavior, and help users choose between integration strategies.

The four patches restructure parameter documentation, clarify integration options, remove a misleading historical example, and modernize conflict resolution guidance.
