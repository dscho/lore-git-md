This is part 3/4 of a documentation patch series fixing asciidoc formatting issues in Git's technical docs. The current patch focuses on `sparse-checkout.adoc`, addressing several formatting problems that cause warnings during builds.

The changes include fixing list numbering (changing from 0-based to 1-based indexing), correcting section heading levels (from `===` to `==`), and improving nested list syntax. The patch also handles various other formatting quirks like literal blocks, labeled lists, and trailing spaces in examples.

He confirms the changes are purely formatting-related and don't affect the actual documentation content, similar to the previous patch in the series.

The patch appears thorough and well-justified, continuing the series' goal of making the documentation render correctly across different toolchains while maintaining source readability.
