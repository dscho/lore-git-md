This is the final patch in a series refactoring the packfile store API, specifically focusing on the transition from `packfile_store_get_packs()` to `packfile_store_get_all_packs()`. Taylor Blau is reviewing Patrick Steinhardt's changes to object-name.c, which handles object disambiguation and abbreviation length calculations.

The patch converts two functions in object-name.c to use `get_all_packs()` instead of `get_packs()`. Taylor confirms the changes are safe since both functions already properly account for multi-pack index (MIDX) cases - `unique_in_pack()` discards MIDX'd packs, and `find_abbrev_len_for_pack()` similarly handles them correctly. 

This appears to be a straightforward mechanical change that completes the series' goal of simplifying the pack iteration API by eliminating the confusing stateful behavior between the two functions. The review indicates no concerns with the implementation, suggesting this final piece of the refactoring is ready to proceed.
