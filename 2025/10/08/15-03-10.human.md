This is a follow-up discussion in a refactoring series about Git's refs iterator interface. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Karthik Nayak's concern about potential stale data in the iterator's `.ref` field during `_advance()` calls. 

The original patch (02/13) introduced a `.ref` field in the base iterator to avoid repeatedly creating new `struct reference` instances. Karthik noted this could lead to stale data if backends don't properly reset the field between iterations. Patrick initially hesitated due to performance concerns in hot loops but has now benchmarked the impact and found no measurable difference from adding a reset. He confirms he'll add another patch to implement the reset.

This exchange shows the careful consideration being given to both correctness and performance in this refactoring series. The resolution demonstrates the series is still evolving based on review feedback, though the performance validation suggests the change won't impact the 13% speedup previously measured.
