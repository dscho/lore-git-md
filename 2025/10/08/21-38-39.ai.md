This is a documentation formatting patch in a series addressing asciidoc warnings in technical documentation. It specifically fixes issues in `remembering-renames.adoc` that were revealed by the new meson build verification.

The patch makes several mechanical adjustments to comply with asciidoc/asciidoctor requirements:
- Renumbers a list from 0-8 to 1-9 to satisfy list numbering rules
- Changes section headers from `===` to `==` level to correct nesting
- Consolidates multi-line section headers into single lines
- Wraps ASCII diagrams in literal blocks
- Fixes sub-list formatting and special character rendering
- The changes are purely presentational, preserving all technical content while making the document properly processable by documentation tooling.

Ramsay Jones confirms these fixes were made under the assumption that current policy requires asciidoc compliance, despite the unfamiliarity with asciidoc's "standard markup".

This patch follows earlier work in the series that addressed similar issues in `sparse-checkout.adoc` and `commit-graph.adoc`.
