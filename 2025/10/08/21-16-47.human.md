This is a discussion about hook integration in the `git replay` command, following up on the recently finalized atomic ref updates patch series.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk clarifies their earlier suggestion about using `git replay` with post-rewrite hooks. They emphasize they weren't proposing to add direct hook support to `git replay`, but rather suggesting that custom output formatting could enable users to drive existing hooks themselves. This comes in response to Elijah Newren's caution about adding hooks to the new command.

The thread shows the maintainers are being careful about expanding `git replay`'s scope too quickly, even as they acknowledge future needs for client-side workflows. Siddharth Asthana had previously seen Kristoffer's use case as motivation for potential `--format` support, but the current consensus seems to be keeping the focus on the core atomic ref update functionality for now.

The exchange highlights the ongoing tension between keeping the new command focused on its initial server-side use cases while leaving room for future client-side features. The enum-based design recently adopted for ref updates appears flexible enough to accommodate such extensions later if needed.
