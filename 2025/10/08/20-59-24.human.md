This is a feature patch finalizing the atomic reference updates for `git replay`, now making them the default behavior. The email captures a key design discussion between maintainers about how to structure the configuration option for this feature.

Junio Hamano responds to Siddharth Asthana's question about whether to use a boolean (`replay.updateRefs`) or string-based enum (`replay.defaultAction`) configuration. While acknowledging the boolean is simpler now, Junio argues for the enum approach to maintain future extensibility, particularly anticipating Kristoffer Haugsbakk's proposed `--format` option that might come later. He warns that starting with a boolean could lead to awkward configuration if additional modes are added in the future.

The discussion represents one of the final design decisions in this otherwise mature patch series, which has already addressed performance concerns, simplified the interface, and gained maintainer approval on most aspects.
