This is a feature patch series for the experimental `git-repo-info` command, implementing the `--all` flag design that was recently settled in the mailing list discussion. The series comes from Lucas Seiki Oshiro as part of GSoC work, with mentorship from Karthik Nayak and Patrick Steinhardt.

The two-patch series factors out field printing logic into a helper function (patch) before implementing the `--all` flag (second patch). The key change in v3 is making `--all` mutually exclusive with specifying individual keys, addressing scripting safety concerns raised in the thread. The implementation now cleanly errors out if both are provided, rather than allowing potentially confusing duplicate output.

The changes look well-considered, with clean separation of concerns in the refactoring and thorough test coverage of the new behavior. The series appears ready for inclusion, representing the consensus reached in the design discussion while maintaining the command's experimental status. This moves the feature forward while preserving Git's scripting-first philosophy.
