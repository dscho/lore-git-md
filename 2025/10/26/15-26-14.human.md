This is a follow-up in the bugfix thread addressing Git's `**` pattern matching behavior in `.gitignore` and `.gitattributes` files. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Junio Hamano's review of his proposed fix, which preserves one character of prefix context when passing paths to `fnmatch()` to correctly handle directory boundary detection.
