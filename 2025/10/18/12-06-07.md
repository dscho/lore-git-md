Dear Professor  

We would like to invite you to submit a paper in the "2nd International Conference on Applied Mathematics and Computer
Simulation", that will be held in Rome, Italy, December 29-31, 2025. See www.icamcs.eu

Please, upload your paper only via the web site www.icamcs.eu
Do not send papers by email. 

The conference Proceedings will be published by the IEEE CPS https://www.computer.org/conferences/cps.
The indices ISI, Scopus, Compendex etc will receive the material from the IEEEXplore.
You can also publish your paper as Chapter in our Books in Springer Verlag or in our ISI - Scopus indexed collaborating journals
(after the approval of the Editor-in-Chief)
Papers will be evaluated based on quality, relevance, and originality. The conference will be conducted in hybrid mode, allowing
for both in-person and online presentations.

The conference will be held in a Hotel in the Center of Rome, in a walking distance from the Central State Station and in a
walking distance from all the ancient monuments of Rome (Colloseum,etc), Archaeological Museum.  

The ICAMCS is a well-known  prestigious international forum that aims to bring
together academics, researchers, engineers, and industry professionals
to exchange and share their research results, innovative ideas, and
practical experiences in both theoretical and applied aspects of these
fields.

Extended versions of all the papers will be published in the following
MDPI Special Issue: Symmetry Journal  Special Issue on Simulation and
Modelling in Natural Sciences, Biomedicine, and Engineering
Impact Factor 2.2 and CiteScore 5.4
https://www.mdpi.com/journal/symmetry/special_issues/Simulation_Modelling_Natural_Sciences_Biomedicine_Engineering_2022

For further information, please visit the official conference website:
 www.icamcs.eu
