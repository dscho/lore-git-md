This is a test modernization patch where Junio C Hamano provides detailed feedback on Solly's proposed conversion of path checks in t2401-worktree-prune.sh. The original submission mechanically replaced shell `test -f`/`test -d` checks with their `test_path_*` helper equivalents, but Junio points out this was done without fully understanding the helpers' purpose.

Junio explains that the test helpers aren't primarily for readability (as Solly claimed) but rather for better failure diagnostics - they explicitly report what went wrong when a test fails. More importantly, he notes the conversion was incorrect in some cases: using `! test_path_is_file` gives the opposite diagnostic behavior from what's wanted when verifying a path shouldn't exist. For those cases, he recommends `test_path_is_missing` instead.

The feedback is thorough and constructive, walking through exactly why the original substitutions were problematic and how to fix them. This appears to be a teaching moment about Git's test helper philosophy rather than a rejection of the modernization effort itself. The thread shows good technical discussion about test infrastructure best practices.
