This is a small cleanup patch in the ongoing `--trailer` support for `git rebase` series. It removes a redundant `strbuf_release()` call in `read_basic_state()` that was being called both inside and outside a conditional block, which could lead to double-freeing the buffer if `strbuf_read_file()` failed. The change ensures the buffer is released exactly once regardless of the execution path.

The patch is straightforward and uncontroversial - it's a minor memory management improvement that doesn't affect functionality. It's part of a much larger series (29 patches) that's already gone through several review iterations, with this particular change coming in response to earlier feedback about memory handling. The change aligns with the series' broader goal of optimizing and cleaning up the rebase trailer implementation.
