This is a small optimization patch in the ongoing `--trailer` support for `git rebase` series. The change modifies `read_basic_state()` in builtin/rebase.c to reuse an existing string buffer rather than releasing and reallocating it when reading trailer arguments. The patch replaces `strbuf_release()` with `strbuf_reset()` before processing the trailer state file, which maintains the buffer's allocated capacity while clearing its contents.

The change is straightforward and appears to be a sensible micro-optimization, though the performance impact would likely be negligible given this occurs during rebase state reading rather than in a hot path. It fits with the series' broader theme of memory management improvements and follows the established pattern of buffer reuse seen elsewhere in Git's codebase. The patch maintains all existing functionality while slightly reducing allocation overhead.
