This is a test patch in the ongoing series adding `--trailer` support to `git rebase`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk provides feedback email pointing out a whitespace issue in Li Chen's test case for trailers without values.

The test in question verifies that `git rebase --trailer` correctly handles trailers that have a separator but no value (like "Acked-by:"), preserving the trailing space after the separator. However, the reviewer notes the test adds a literal trailing space in the source code, which would trigger Git's whitespace check in CI. They suggest either using a placeholder character (like underscore) that gets converted to space during the test, or finding a way to represent the space through a variable.

This is a minor but important technical correction to maintain the project's coding standards. The feedback is constructive, offering specific suggestions from similar cases in the test suite while acknowledging the test's valid purpose. The exchange shows the careful attention to detail in this long-running series about trailer handling.
