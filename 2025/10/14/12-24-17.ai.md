This is a refactoring patch in the larger `--trailer` support series for `git rebase`. The change moves the declaration of two linked list heads (`config_head` and `arg_head`) inside the conditional block where they are actually used, rather than declaring them at function scope. This is a straightforward memory efficiency improvement - the lists are only needed when `opts->only_input` is false, so there's no need to allocate them otherwise.

The patch makes no functional changes to the trailer processing logic itself, just optimizes the scope of these variables. It's a common pattern in C to defer variable declarations until they're needed, both for clarity and to avoid unnecessary stack allocation. The change is small (5 lines touched) and well-contained, with a clear commit message explaining the motivation.
