Refactoring patch in the packfile store API series, responding to Patrick Steinhardt's changes.

Justin Tobler confirms understanding of Patrick Steinhardt's patch that switches object-name.c from `packfile_store_get_packs()` to `packfile_store_get_all_packs()`. The change is part of a larger effort to eliminate confusing stateful behavior between these two functions, where `get_packs()` could unexpectedly include MIDX-referenced packs depending on whether `get_all_packs()` had been called first.

The patch modifies two functions in object-name.c that handle object disambiguation and abbreviation length calculation. Justin notes these call sites are already prepared to handle MIDX-referenced packs, making the transition straightforward. The changes are mechanical replacements with no behavior change, just clearer semantics as part of the ongoing pack iteration cleanup.

This appears to be a straightforward and well-justified change in the context of the larger refactoring series. The patch is small (4 lines changed) and focused, with Justin providing a positive review indicating the change looks good.
