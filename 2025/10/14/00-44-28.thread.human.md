This thread explores a potential refactoring in the GPG interface code, where an Outreachy applicant has identified two functions (`get_ssh_key_fingerprint` and `get_default_ssh_signing_key`) that currently use `strbuf_split_max()` for string splitting. The proposal suggests replacing this with `string_list_split()`, following a pattern seen in prior discussions about modernizing string handling in the codebase.

At this stage, the proposal is more of an exploratory inquiry than a concrete change - the author is seeking feedback on whether this would make a suitable microproject before proceeding with implementation. No patch has been submitted yet, and the technical merits of this specific change haven't been analyzed in terms of behavior changes, edge cases, or performance. The discussion appears to be in its early phases, with the main contribution so far being the identification of potential refactoring targets in the GPG/SSH key handling code.
