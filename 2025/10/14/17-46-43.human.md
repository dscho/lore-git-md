This is the final discussion in a resolved thread about naming the flag for checking reference optimization needs. Junio Hamano and Karthik Nayak are settling on `--check-for-auto` as the final choice for the flag name in the `git refs optimize` command.

Junio Hamano reinforces that the chosen name should reflect the advisory nature of the check - determining whether optimization would be beneficial rather than mandatory.
