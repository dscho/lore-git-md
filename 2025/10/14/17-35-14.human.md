This is a follow-up in Adrian Ratiu's hook subsystem refactoring series, specifically about patch 1's file descriptor handling changes. After initially agreeing to change the file descriptor marker from `0` to `-1` (as suggested by Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano), Adrian now reports that implementing this change is more complex than expected. 

The issue stems from the run-command API's widespread use of `0` to indicate "no file descriptor" throughout Git's codebase. Changing this to `-1` would require extensive modifications across the project, introducing significant risk of errors while being outside the scope of the hook refactoring work. Adrian proposes keeping the current behavior rather than undertaking this disruptive change.

This represents a reversal from the v2 plan, as the complexity wasn't apparent during initial review. The decision now rests with reviewers to either accept the status quo or insist on the more correct but riskier `-1` approach. This doesn't block the rest of the series but does affect one of the early foundational changes.
