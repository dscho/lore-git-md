The exchange reveals two competing priorities: brian's concern about future SHA-1 deprecation requirements (particularly for government users) versus Junio's focus on maintaining stable patch ID matching across time. The discussion suggests this may need either a configurable approach or careful documentation update rather than a simple code change.

This is a nuanced technical debate that will likely require input from other contributors to reach consensus. The outcome could affect both the implementation and documentation of patch IDs, with implications for long-term maintenance as SHA-1 deprecation deadlines approach.
