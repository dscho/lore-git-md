Follow-up in bugfix thread about `**` pattern matching in `.gitignore` files. Jeff King clarifies his earlier patch submission was meant to be marked as RFC (Request For Comments) but he forgot to label it as such in the subject line. The patch in question addresses an inconsistency where Git incorrectly matches patterns like `foo**/bar` against paths like `foobar`, contrary to documented behavior requiring `**` to be surrounded by slashes for special meaning.

This email serves as a correction to the thread's status, indicating the patch should be considered provisional while technical concerns are worked out. The core issue remains the same: the `match_pathname()` optimization in `dir.c` strips too much prefix when passing to `fnmatch()`, losing directory boundary context needed for correct `**` interpretation. Jeff King had previously described his solution as a "disgusting hack" that works but may need refinement for edge cases and implementation approach.
