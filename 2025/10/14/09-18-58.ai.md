This is a documentation patch discussion in the ongoing thread about Julia Evans' `gitdatamodel.adoc` man page series, specifically addressing how to explain reference hierarchy concepts.

Julia Evans responds to Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about the phrasing around reference hierarchy explanations in the proposed documentation. She questions why his suggested replacement text is preferable to her original version, which stated that Git handles references differently based on their position in the hierarchy. Her intent was to communicate that branches, tags etc. receive different treatment based on their hierarchy location.

The discussion centers on technical precision in describing how Git treats various reference types, particularly:
- The distinction between root refs (like HEAD) and pseudo-refs (like FETCH_HEAD)
- Whether storage location correlates with special handling
- The pedagogical balance between accuracy and simplicity

Key technical points at issue:
- Original phrasing implied all references are treated differently based on hierarchy position
- Patrick's correction notes that while pseudo-refs (outside hierarchy) are handled differently, root refs (also outside hierarchy) are treated like regular refs despite their special meaning
- Julia seeks clarification on what exactly makes her version misleading
- Current proposal avoids mentioning pseudo-refs entirely to prevent confusion

This exchange represents the final refinement of the reference hierarchy explanation before the documentation moves forward, addressing one of the last open questions in the thread.
