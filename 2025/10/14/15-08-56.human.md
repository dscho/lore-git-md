This is the final discussion in a series introducing reference optimization checks, focusing on naming the new flag for the `git refs optimize` command.

The thread has reached consensus that the initial `--required` flag name was misleading (as it implied mandatory action) and `--dry-run` was too strong (suggesting full execution). Junio Hamano proposed `--check-for-auto` as an alternative that better reflects the advisory nature of the check. Karthik Nayak now suggests two simpler options: the minimal `--check` or the more descriptive `--is-worthwhile`, noting that verbosity is acceptable for this specialized flag.

All technical aspects of the 9-patch series have been settled through extensive review, with this being the last remaining decision point. The implementation provides a lightweight way for services like GitLab's Gitaly to check if reference optimization would be worthwhile before performing the potentially expensive operation.
