Optimization patch in the ongoing rebase trailer series. This small change from Li Chen reuses an existing string buffer rather than releasing and reallocating it when reading trailer arguments during rebase state restoration. The modification to `read_basic_state()` in builtin/rebase.c replaces a `strbuf_release()` with `strbuf_reset()`, allowing the same buffer to be reused for reading the trailer state file. This is a minor memory optimization that fits into the larger series' focus on efficiency and cleanup around trailer handling. The change is straightforward and maintains the existing behavior while reducing allocations.
