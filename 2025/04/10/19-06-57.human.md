This is a build system patch continuing the effort to port header checking from Makefile to meson. The email captures Junio Hamano's response to Phillip Wood's review of Karthik Nayak's implementation.

Junio strongly agrees with Phillip's two suggestions: (1) renaming `headers_generated` to `generated_headers` for consistency with other variable conventions, and (2) eliminating the manual header file list maintenance in favor of a more automated approach like the Makefile uses. He provides thoughtful rationale about why explicit file listing creates maintenance burden without actually catching the most common errors (like forgetting to `git add` new headers).

The discussion shows consensus forming around these refinements to Karthik's otherwise complete implementation of header checking in meson. This represents one of the final open items in the thread before the series can be merged, following Junio's earlier endorsement of the "check-headers" naming convention. The exchange demonstrates careful attention to build system maintainability and consistency between the Makefile and meson implementations.
