Junio Hamano's response to the finalized POSIX-compliant printf solution for submodule tests. While acknowledging the technical correctness of the implementation combining `printf` with `test_seq | sed p`, he questions whether this approach offers practical advantages over the simpler loop-based solution previously endorsed by Eric Sunshine. 

Key technical observations:
- Notes that modern shells implement `printf` as a builtin, negating any potential fork/exec savings
- Points out the command-line length concern when scaling beyond 2000 submodules (approximately 18KB argument size)
- Suggests a `while` loop would be more maintainable for future test expansion
- Implicitly reaffirms preference for the simpler loop approach despite the POSIX compliance achieved

The email serves as a maintainer's stylistic guidance rather than a blocking objection, emphasizing long-term maintainability over clever one-liners. While the thread had reached technical consensus on POSIX compliance, this response highlights practical engineering tradeoffs that weren't fully addressed in earlier discussion. The core test functionality remains unaffected by this stylistic debate.
