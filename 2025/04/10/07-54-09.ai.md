This is a feature proposal for adding automatic signoff functionality to `git commit`. The author identifies a real pain point for developers who need to consistently add Signed-off-by trailers across multiple repositories, particularly when commits may originate from various tools.

The proposal outlines four potential solutions, with the most straightforward being a new `commit.signoff` config option that would make `git commit` behave as if `--signoff` was always passed. The alternatives include extending `commit.gpgsign`'s behavior (either by adding a third value or changing its semantics) or relying on developer discipline - both of which have drawbacks.

Key technical details include:
- Current limitations with aliases (can't override `git commit` directly)
- Problems with hook-based solutions (scalability and potential conflicts)
- The distinction between GPG signing (already configurable via `commit.gpgsign`) and signoff trailers
- Backward compatibility concerns with changing existing config options

The proposal is well-motivated by real workflow needs and thoughtfully considers multiple implementation approaches. The most likely point of discussion will be whether to introduce a new config option or modify the behavior of the existing `commit.gpgsign` setting. The author appears open to feedback on the preferred approach.
