Performance optimization follow-up for bundle ref handling. This is a reply to Karthik Nayak's patch that replaces the O(N^2) duplicate ref checking in bundle creation with an O(1) strset-based solution, showing 6x speedup at 100k refs (14.6s → 2.4s). Toon Claes confirms the patch performs as expected in real-world repositories with fewer refs, noting no regression at smaller scales. The implementation removes `object_array_remove_duplicates()` entirely in favor of tracking seen refnames with a strset during bundle header writing, while maintaining correct handling of both same-refname cases and exclusion patterns. 

The thread context shows this was the final patch in a v2 series that had already established consensus on correctness, with comprehensive test coverage in t6020-bundle-misc.sh. Toon references his prior work on bundle race conditions (b2a6d1c686) in validating edge cases. The only remaining issue noted in the thread is a simple merge conflict with ps/object-wo-the-repository, suggesting this optimization is otherwise ready for integration. The changes touch bundle.c, object.c and object.h, with the strset approach demonstrating linear scaling where the original showed quadratic behavior.
