This is a discussion about change IDs in the context of Git workflow tools, specifically comparing different approaches to tracking evolving commits.

Martin acknowledges these workflows may be less directly applicable to core Git due to differences in how Git handles detached HEAD states and commit rewrites.

The discussion remains technical and constructive, with Martin suggesting experimentation with Jujutsu to better understand the change ID implementation.
