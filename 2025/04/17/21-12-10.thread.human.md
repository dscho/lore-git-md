Taylor Blau's series to enable bitmap lookup tables by default has drawn its first substantive review from maintainer Junio Hamano. The core change - making these tables the standard for new bitmaps - remains uncontested, with performance data showing clear benefits after nearly three years of production use at GitHub. However, Junio has raised an important architectural concern about how the change is implemented across two separate subsystems: the flag enabling lookup tables in pack-objects (BITMAP_OPT_LOOKUP_TABLE) is distinct from the equivalent in multi-pack-index handling (MIDX_WRITE_BITMAP_LOOKUP_TABLE), with no automatic synchronization between them. 

This doesn't block the immediate change, but highlights a maintainability risk - future contributors might need to update both flags independently when making bitmap-related changes, with no obvious way to verify consistency. The discussion may evolve toward documenting this relationship, creating synchronization mechanisms, or potentially restructuring the flag systems. For now, Taylor will likely need to address this observation before the series can progress, though the fundamental direction of enabling lookup tables by default appears sound.
