The patch recognizes that the initial repository repacking step doesn't need to be timed (as it's just setup) and shouldn't be repeated multiple times by the performance test harness. This makes the test suite more efficient without affecting test validity. The change is straightforward - just swapping one test helper for another in the bitmap test library.
