This is part 4/7 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to harden Git's integer parsing in the parse-options infrastructure. The patch focuses on renaming the `OPT_MAGNITUDE()` macro to `OPT_UNSIGNED()` to better reflect its purpose.

The change comes after patch 3/7 extended `OPT_INTEGER()` to support unit factors (k/m/g suffixes), which means the key difference between the two macros is now their signedness rather than unit support. The new name `OPT_UNSIGNED()` makes this distinction clearer at the API level.

The patch updates all callers across builtin commands (gc, multi-pack-index, pack-objects, repack), test infrastructure, and documentation. The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching 9 files with 47 line changes. The test suite is updated to reflect the new naming while maintaining all existing functionality.

This is a straightforward rename that follows logically from the previous patch's changes. The series has already reached design consensus, and this patch appears uncontroversial - it's essentially finishing the naming cleanup started earlier in the series.
