The email also touches on related UI considerations, with Knoble suggesting GitHub could theoretically track commit evolution through force-push detection without Change-IDs, though he recognizes this might be more challenging than it appears. He expresses appreciation for previous thread summaries while noting the complexity of finding a "universal core" solution to the tracking problem.
