Bugfix for Outlook thread handling in `git send-email`. This is the second patch in a two-part series that also adds OAuth2 support for Microsoft email services.

The patch addresses a specific issue where Outlook servers break email threads by replacing the original Message-ID with their own random one, storing the original in a proprietary header. This causes replies to fail to thread properly. The fix extracts the server-assigned Message-ID from the SMTP response when using Outlook's servers (smtp.office365.com or smtp-mail.outlook.com) and uses that instead of the locally-generated one.

The implementation is straightforward - it adds a check for Outlook servers after sending the email, then parses the Message-ID from the server's confirmation response. The patch includes both success and failure cases, with appropriate warning if the ID can't be extracted. The change is minimal (11 lines added) and focused on the Outlook-specific behavior.

This appears to be a well-motivated fix for a real-world interoperability issue, though it's worth noting the solution is Outlook-specific rather than a general SMTP standard approach. The patch builds on the first in the series (OAuth2 support) to provide full Microsoft email service compatibility.
