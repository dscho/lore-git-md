This is part 2/3 in a series adding Outlook compatibility to `git send-email`, specifically addressing Message-ID handling. Outlook has non-standard behavior where it replaces the Message-ID header with its own X-Microsoft-Original-Message-ID and generates a random Message-ID, breaking thread replies.

The patch modifies `git-send-email.perl` to extract the server-assigned Message-ID from Outlook's SMTP response (which appears in a `<...>` wrapper) and uses that instead of the locally generated one. The change only activates for known Outlook SMTP endpoints (office365.com and outlook.com) and includes both success logging and failure warnings.

This appears to be a well-scoped fix for a specific interoperability issue, with clear motivation and targeted implementation. The change is relatively small (11 lines) and maintains backward compatibility while adding the Outlook-specific behavior. As part of a larger OAuth2/Outlook compatibility effort, this patch helps complete the end-to-end email sending workflow for Outlook-hosted accounts.
