- Size discrepancy appears in `git count-objects -vH` output (5.62GB vs 4.93GB size-pack)
- The repository contains no LFS objects, ruling that out as a cause

The email includes the complete batch script used for pushing, which shows careful handling of branch pushing with size-based chunking and forced pushes.
