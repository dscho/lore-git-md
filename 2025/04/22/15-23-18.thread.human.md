This thread focuses on improving `git send-email` compatibility with modern email services by adding OAuth2 authentication support. Aditya Garg and Julian Swagemakers have submitted a series (now at v3) that implements SMTP bearer authentication (OAUTHBEARER and XOAUTH2 mechanisms) in `git-send-email.perl`, since Perl Authen::SASL doesn't support these protocols. The changes include new helper functions for generating proper authentication strings according to RFCs 5801 and 7628, with TLS encryption required as per standards.

The series also addresses Outlook-specific Message-ID handling and adds a `--generate-password` option for scripting contexts. The authors have been using these changes successfully with Outlook in production, and the patch includes a Tested-by from the git-credential-oauth maintainer. Documentation updates explain the new OAuth2 requirements and provide example configurations for Gmail and Office 365 setups. While the implementation appears thorough with proper attention to security, some questions may remain about maintaining Outlook-specific behaviors versus broader standards compliance.
