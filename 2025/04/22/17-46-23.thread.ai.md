This RFC thread continues discussion of the proposed `git-blame-tree` command (or alternative integration), a tool for tracking modifications across directory trees. The 7-patch series implements core functionality in new files `blame-tree.[ch]` and `builtin/blame-tree.c`, with test coverage in `t/t8020-blame-tree.sh` and performance benchmarks in `t/perf/p8020-blame-tree.sh`.
