This thread focuses on improving `git send-email` compatibility with modern email services, particularly Microsoft's ecosystem. The two-patch series combines OAuth2 authentication support (needed for services like Office 365 and Gmail) with a fix for Outlook's non-standard Message-ID handling that breaks email threading.

Aditya Garg and Julian Swagemakers collaborated on the series, with v2 rebased from earlier work in January 2025. The OAuth2 implementation supports both OAUTHBEARER and XOAUTH2 protocols with detailed RFC compliance, while the Outlook fix specifically addresses how Microsoft's servers replace Message-IDs with proprietary headers. The second patch extracts the server-assigned Message-ID from SMTP responses when using Office 365 or Outlook SMTP servers, ensuring proper threading when replying to patches.

The changes have been tested in practice by sending the patches themselves through the modified `send-email`, with validation from the git-credential-oauth maintainer. While currently focused on Microsoft/Google ecosystems, the implementation maintains standards compliance and includes fallback behavior when parsing fails. The main open question is how to handle dependencies on modified third-party credential helpers, but the series appears ready for broader review as a solution to real-world email compatibility issues.
