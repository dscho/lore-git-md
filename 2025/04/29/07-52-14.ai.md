This is a v3 patch series (7/7) continuing the object store cleanup effort, now focused on removing `repo_has_object_file()` in favor of the newer `has_object()` API. The series builds on previous work reorganizing object store headers and functions.

The key change in this version is a shift in approach - rather than trying to fix up all callsites where promisor object fetching occurs, Patrick now opts for a simpler 1:1 conversion strategy. The series:

1. Adds a new HAS_OBJECT_FETCH_PROMISOR flag to `has_object()` to maintain existing promisor fetch behavior where needed
2. Systematically converts all `repo_has_object_file()` calls to equivalent `has_object()` calls with matching flags
3. Finally removes the deprecated `repo_has_object_file()` functions

Notable technical details:
- The flag mapping preserves existing behavior:
  - `repo_has_object_file()` -> `has_object()` with both RECHECK_PACKED and FETCH_PROMISOR flags
  - `repo_has_object_file_with_flags(OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT)` -> just RECHECK_PACKED
  - `repo_has_object_file_with_flags(OBJECT_INFO_QUICK)` -> just FETCH_PROMISOR
- Changes span 17 files across multiple subsystems including fetch-pack, refs, http-walker, and bulk-checkin
- The conversion is intentionally conservative - questions about whether certain calls should fetch promisor objects are deferred

The series appears well-structured with clean commit messages explaining the rationale. The v3 changes address previous feedback by:
- Moving `hash_object_file()` to the correct header
- Clarifying the deprecation rationale for `repo_has_object_file()`
- Simplifying the approach to focus on direct conversions first

This appears ready for merging as the final step in removing `repo_has_object_file()`, though some follow-up may be needed to optimize promisor fetching behavior at specific callsites. The changes maintain compatibility while paving the way for future improvements.
