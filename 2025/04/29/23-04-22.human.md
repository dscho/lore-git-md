This is a test modernization patch in the reftable test conversion series. Junio Hamano is reviewing the first patch of Seyi Kuforiji's v2 series that introduces Clar-compatible helper functions for reftable tests.

The patch adds new test helper implementations in unit-test.{c,h} that match the Clar framework's style, rather than modifying the existing lib-reftable helpers. Junio notes this creates some duplication rather than showing a direct migration path, which makes the transition harder to follow. He suggests this might be worth discussing with Patrick Steinhardt, who has been involved in the reftable work.

The helper functions being added will be used by subsequent patches in the series to convert the actual reftable tests. While the technical content looks correct, the review highlights a stylistic concern about how the transition is being presented in the patch series. This appears to be a minor polish issue rather than a functional problem with the test conversion itself.
