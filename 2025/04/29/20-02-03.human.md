This is a review response in the ongoing series to replace `git gc` functionality with granular maintenance tasks, specifically discussing the new "worktree-prune" task implementation.

Derrick Stolee responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch introducing worktree pruning as a standalone maintenance task. He initially considered whether this could have been merged with an earlier refactoring patch but concludes the new condition-checking implementation justifies its own patch. The discussion focuses on the design choice to implement the pruning condition check directly in the maintenance builtin rather than relying solely on the `git worktree prune` command, noting this avoids unnecessary process creation (particularly important on Windows).

Stolee approves the approach and suggests verifying that the `gc.worktreePruneExpire` config value is properly respected in the implementation. The response indicates this patch and the series are progressing well toward the goal of fully decomposing `git gc` functionality, with both worktree pruning and rerere garbage collection now implemented as standalone tasks.
