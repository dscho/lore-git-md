This is a debugging update in the ongoing investigation of reflog corruption during `git rebase --rebase-merges`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk reports back with additional findings from attempting to reproduce the issue where merge commit reflog actions contain uninitialized memory instead of proper messages.

The key new information is that the corruption appears to be and not reliably reproducible under GDB - while the issue occurs in normal operation, attempts to inspect the problematic `opts->ctx->reflog_message` value with GDB did not show corruption. However, Kristoffer did find old debugging notes showing the expected corruption pattern where `ctx->reflog_message` contains garbage memory (shown as `"\250y\267UUU"`) at the critical point in `sequencer.c:1158` where the reflog action string is used.

This suggests the issue may be timing-sensitive or affected by debugger overhead, making it harder to diagnose. The report confirms the corruption does originate from `ctx->reflog_message` being improperly initialized or corrupted before being used in `run_git_commit()`, but the intermittent nature complicates root cause analysis. The debugging continues to focus on the interaction between `do_merge()` and `run_git_commit()` in sequencer.c, particularly when handling merge commits during rebase operations.
