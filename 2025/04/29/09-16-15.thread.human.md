Leon Michalak proposed improving Git's interactive patch commands (`git add -p` and related) to respect diff context configuration, as they require a workaround today. He notes this would align with how other porcelain-level settings work, though acknowledges the current limitation stems from the plumbing foundations these commands are built on. While other diff-related configs could potentially be included later, Jeff suggests starting with just the context setting to gauge user needs.

The path forward may depend on whether backward compatibility concerns outweigh the benefits of more intuitive behavior for interactive use.
