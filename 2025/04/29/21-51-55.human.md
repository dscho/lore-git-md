This is a debugging suggestion in an ongoing thread about reflog corruption during `git rebase --rebase-merges`. Jeff King (peff) responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's observation that the corruption (where reflog entries contain garbage data) appears intermittently and isn't reproducible under GDB.

Peff suggests building with `SANITIZE=address,undefined` to detect memory issues, speculating that the problem might be a use-after-free bug. The theory is that `ctx->reflog_message` could be holding an old pointer to a static strbuf that gets reallocated elsewhere. This is a plausible direction given the intermittent nature of the corruption and the difficulty reproducing it under a debugger.

The thread has already established as investigating a serious but elusive bug in the sequencer's merge handling during rebase, where reflog messages sometimes contain uninitialized memory. This email moves the investigation toward potential memory safety issues rather than logical errors in the rebase flow.
