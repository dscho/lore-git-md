This is a review response in the ongoing series to decompose `git gc` into granular maintenance tasks. Derrick Stolee is providing feedback on Patrick Steinhardt's 7-patch series that implements the final two missing components: worktree pruning and rerere garbage collection.

Stolee approves of the overall approach and the cleanups in the first few patches. He specifically highlights the worktree-prune implementation as the most substantial new behavior, though he notes he'll provide more detailed comments on that patch separately. The rerere-gc implementation is described as a clean "one-two punch" addition.

The review suggests these new tasks could potentially be added to the default maintenance schedule in a follow-up. This positions the series as completing the technical prerequisites for eventually deprecating `git gc`, though that deprecation itself remains future work. The collaboration between Stolee (original series author) and Steinhardt (implementing key optimizations) appears productive, with Stolee validating the design choices around direct condition checking in the maintenance builtin rather than using child processes.
