This email from JAYATHEERTH K confirms understanding and acceptance of Jeff King's proposed fix for the pathspec wildcard matching issue. The problem occurs when `git add f*` fails to properly match both literal `f*` files and pattern-matched files (like `foo`) on first run due to an overzealous optimization in the pathspec matching code.

Jeff King's solution modifies `do_match_pathspec()` in dir.c to only skip re-checking pathspec matches when `nowildcard_len == len` (indicating no wildcards are present). This preserves the optimization for literal matches while ensuring wildcard pathspecs are always fully evaluated. The fix aligns with similar checks elsewhere in the codebase and maintains backward compatibility with Git's opt-in globbing behavior.
