- Conversion of all option references to backtick format (`-f`, `--cached`)
- Standardized placeholder notation (_<file>_, _NUL_)
- Updated submodule documentation with proper config key formatting (`submodule.<name>`)
- Pathspec explanations now reference the glossary entry with proper markup
