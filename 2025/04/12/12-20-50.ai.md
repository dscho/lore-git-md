Documentation patch finalizing the documentation modernization series by explicitly adding character markup rules to CodingGuidelines.

This seventh and final patch in the v2 series codifies the practice already used in the converted man pages for `git-reset`, `git-rm`, and `git-mv` - marking up special characters like `_LF_`, `_CR_`, and `_NUL_` with underscores. The change adds a clear guideline to Documentation/CodingGuidelines alongside the existing rules for placeholder notation.

The patch is straightforward, adding just 3 lines to document the character formatting convention that was implicitly followed in the earlier conversions. It completes the standardization effort by making the practice an official part of Git's documentation guidelines rather than just an ad-hoc convention.

This follows the pattern seen throughout the series of both modernizing the documentation and ensuring consistent practices are documented for future contributors. The change is minimal but important for maintaining consistency as more documentation gets converted to the new AsciiDoc format. With this patch, the series now fully addresses all aspects of the conversion process - from toolchain modifications to built-in help text updates to contributor guidelines.
