This is the final patch in a documentation standardization series, explicitly adding markup guidelines for special characters in the CodingGuidelines document. The patch codifies existing practice from the series' earlier conversions of `git-reset`, `git-rm`, and `git-mv` man pages, where special characters like _LF_, _NUL_, and _EOF_ were already being marked up with underscores. 

The change is straightforward - it adds a new section to the documentation standards specifying that special control characters should be wrapped in underscores, similar to how placeholders are formatted. This matches the pattern established in the man page conversions and helps maintain consistency across Git's documentation.

As the concluding patch in a well-reviewed series that has already addressed most technical concerns, this change appears non-controversial. The only remaining open issue from earlier reviews (TROFF rendering quirks) isn't affected by this particular patch. The addition serves to document the project's actual practice rather than introduce new formatting rules.
