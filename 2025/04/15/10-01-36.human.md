This is a follow-up discussion in the completed Meson benchmark integration series, focusing on Perl path handling.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's analysis of Perl script execution approaches, continuing the discussion about whether to use `#!/usr/bin/env perl` shebangs versus explicit `perl` invocation. The thread had previously standardized on using `PERL_PATH` for portability, particularly for systems like NixOS.

Patrick makes the case for `env`-based shebangs, arguing they provide better usability by working automatically when Perl is in PATH (even in non-standard locations), while acknowledging Junio's point about clearer error messages when Perl is completely missing. He leaves the decision open, offering to adapt if there's strong preference for the explicit approach.

This represents the final discussion point in the otherwise complete series, with Patrick providing a technical counterpoint but deferring to maintainer preference. The exchange shows thoughtful consideration of both user experience and portability tradeoffs, typical of the thorough review this series has received.
