This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing object storage modularization series (v3 04/10). It moves the `xmmap()` family of functions from object-file.c to wrapper.c, where they better belong since they're general-purpose memory mapping utilities rather than object-file-specific code.

The change is straightforward - the functions are copied verbatim to wrapper.c (which already declares them in its header) and removed from object-file.c. The functions include `xmmap()`, `xmmap_gently()`, `mmap_os_err()`, and their helper `mmap_limit_check()`. This continues the series' theme of better organizing Git's codebase by moving functions to more logical homes.

This patch appears well-executed - it's a clean move with no behavior changes, and it follows the pattern established earlier in the series. The change makes sense architecturally since wrapper.c already contains similar low-level utility functions. As part of a larger refactoring effort that's already addressed all major discussion points in previous iterations, this patch seems unlikely to generate controversy.
