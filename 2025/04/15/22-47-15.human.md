Performance optimization for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's 9-part series improving pack maintenance, introducing a configurable way to avoid including cruft packs in the MIDX when they're not needed for reachability.

The patch adds a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option (defaulting to true for backward compatibility) that, when set to false, allows Git to exclude cruft packs from the MIDX during geometric repacking. This is made possible by the new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode introduced in patch 8/9, which ensures reachability closure by copying any needed objects from cruft packs into new packs.

The change addresses a performance issue where MIDX lookups were slowed down by including many unreachable objects from cruft packs. The implementation carefully handles edge cases where cruft packs must still be included (like when they're needed for bitmap reachability) and includes comprehensive tests verifying the behavior under different configurations.

This completes the series' goal of making pack maintenance more configurable and efficient, particularly for repositories with many unreachable objects. The change builds on extensive refactoring in earlier patches and has been through multiple rounds of review, with only minor refinements remaining before potential inclusion.
