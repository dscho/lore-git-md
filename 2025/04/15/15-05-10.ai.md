This is a discussion about Cygwin version support in the ongoing build system alignment series.

Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt and Ramsay Jones regarding the proposal to drop pre-v2.x Cygwin support. He agrees in principle with reducing maintenance burden by removing support for older versions, but defers to Adam Dinwoodie (Cygwin maintainer) for the final decision. Junio acknowledges the practical approaches of either: (1) maintaining support for untestable older systems with clear caveats about untested status, or (2) explicitly dropping support for those versions entirely.

The email highlights the project's broader philosophy around platform support - balancing maintenance costs against backwards compatibility, particularly for systems the maintainers cannot test. This discussion directly impacts the NO_REGEX conditional in config.mak.uname (patch 07/13) and potentially other Cygwin-specific workarounds in the series.

Key technical details mentioned include:
- The NO_REGEX conditional currently targets Cygwin v1.7 specifically
- Ramsay Jones has a prepared patch to remove all pre-v2.x Cygwin support
- The project has recently been more aggressive about dropping support for ancient OS versions
- Testing limitations for older systems create maintenance challenges

This continues the thread's central tension between modernization and backwards compatibility, with maintainers generally leaning toward simplification but awaiting definitive input from the Cygwin ecosystem.
