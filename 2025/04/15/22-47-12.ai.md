Performance optimization for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. This is patch 8/9 in Taylor Blau's series, introducing a new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for `git pack-objects` that enables more flexible handling of cruft packs during repacking operations.

The patch adds a new mode that changes how `pack-objects` treats objects from packs not explicitly listed in stdin. In "follow" mode, objects from unlisted packs will be included if they are (1) reachable from the included packs and (2) not found in any excluded packs. This is particularly useful for maintaining reachability guarantees when working with cruft packs, allowing once-unreachable objects to be "resurrected" when they become reachable through new commits.

Key technical details:
- Introduces `enum stdin_packs_mode` with three states: `NONE`, `STANDARD`, and `FOLLOW`
- Modifies `show_object_pack_hint()` and `show_commit_pack_hint()` to handle the new follow mode by adding reachable objects from unlisted packs
- Adds a new callback `parse_stdin_packs_mode()` to handle the option parsing
- Includes comprehensive tests in t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh that verify the behavior with various pack combinations
- Updates documentation to explain the new mode and its use cases

The implementation builds on previous patches in the series that refactored the pack-objects internals, particularly the stdin-packs handling. This change enables future geometric repacking strategies to maintain reachability closure while still allowing selective pack inclusion. The tests demonstrate the behavior with both packed and loose objects, showing how the follow mode preserves reachability through unlisted packs.
