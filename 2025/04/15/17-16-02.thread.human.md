This thread addressed a regression in Git's meson build system that caused builds to fail when the `target_shell` variable became undefined. The issue was first identified and fixed by Ramsay Jones, who traced it to commit 916e0fb7c0 from Patrick Steinhardt's ps/meson-build-perf-bench branch, where a `find_program()` call initializing `target_shell` was accidentally dropped during merging.

Junio Hamano has now confirmed Ramsay's diagnosis, noting this was exactly the issue he had suspected when investigating the build breakage. The fix is straightforward - restoring the dropped meson configuration line that properly initializes `target_shell` using standard meson patterns. With the root cause confirmed and the maintainer taking ownership of correcting the problematic merge, this thread appears headed for resolution once the adjusted merge is published. The regression was specific to meson builds and didn't affect other build systems.
