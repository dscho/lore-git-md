This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series hardening integer handling in Git's parse-options infrastructure, specifically addressing signedness mismatches in option parsing.

The patch introduces compile-time safety checks for integer signedness in option parsing, fixing a systemic issue where `OPT_INTEGER()` was being misused with unsigned variables (like `size_t`). This caused subtle bugs on big-endian platforms (s390x, sparc64) where the `--min-batch-size` option in `git-backfill` failed silently. The solution adds two new build assertions (`BARF_UNLESS_SIGNED` and `BARF_UNLESS_UNSIGNED`) that trigger compiler errors when the wrong type is used.

Key technical changes include:
- New signedness validation macros in `git-compat-util.h`
- Updates to `OPT_INTEGER()`, `OPT_UNSIGNED()`, and `OPT_MAGNITUDE()` to enforce type safety
- Fixes for four affected commands: `apply`, `backfill`, `column`, and `grep`
- Conversion of incorrectly signed options to their proper types (e.g., changing `OPT_INTEGER` to `OPT_UNSIGNED` for size-related options)

The patch completes the series' goal of making numeric option parsing robust across platforms while maintaining the architectural consistency established in earlier patches. It addresses specific bug reports while providing systemic protection against similar issues. The changes are narrowly focused on the parse-options interface with minimal disruption to callers, demonstrating careful attention to backward compatibility.
