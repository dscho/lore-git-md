This thread addresses the growing problem of AI-generated spam in Git's mentoring programs (GSoC and Outreachy). Christian Couder reports that 71 out of 79 GSoC 2025 proposals were spammy or AI-generated, representing a significant increase that has made manual review increasingly burdensome. In response, mentors and org admins have developed new anti-AI guidelines, documented in GitHub PR #771, which are now live on Git's General Application Information page.

The guidelines appear effective—other organizations that implemented similar measures report reduced spam volume. While currently limited to mentoring programs, the email raises the question of whether these guidelines should expand to cover the entire Git project, suggesting this may be the start of broader discussions about AI use in contributions. The linked PR provides the specific language of the new rules, which could serve as a template if adopted more widely.
