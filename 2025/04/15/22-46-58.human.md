Performance optimization patch in the MIDX/cruft pack series. This is the 4th patch in Taylor Blau's 9-part v3 series improving pack maintenance performance, specifically focusing on how cruft packs interact with MIDX during repacking.

The patch refactors how `pack-objects` handles reachability traversals when processing packs from stdin. It moves the `rev_info` initialization and traversal code out of `read_packs_list_from_stdin()` and into its caller `read_stdin_packs()`. This prepares for future changes where the same reachability traversal will be reused for the `--unpacked` case.

The change is primarily structural - the behavior remains identical, but the code organization better supports upcoming functionality. This continues the series' careful approach of separating refactoring from feature additions, making each change easier to review. The patch follows cleanly from the previous refactoring work in patches 1-3 and sets up for the new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode coming in patch 8/9.
