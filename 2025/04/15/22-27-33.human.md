This is a minor follow-up discussion in the ongoing performance optimization series about MIDX and cruft pack interaction. The exchange focuses on a small but noticeable detail in patch 1/8's refactoring of option incompatibility checks in `pack-objects.c`.

Taylor Blau and Junio Hamano are discussing whether to preserve the exact ordering of error message components when incompatible options (`--stdin-packs` and `--filter`) are used together. While Taylor initially wanted to maintain the original ordering for consistency, Junio points out that the new standardized `die_for_incompatible_opt2()` function naturally produces output in a different order, and the test expectations would need adjusting either way.

The discussion reveals an interesting quirk - preserving the code's check order would actually reverse the error message output order compared to the original. This is purely a cosmetic/consistency concern with no functional impact, and Junio suggests it's not worth a reroll just for this. The exchange shows the attention to detail in the review process, even for minor aspects of the changes.
