This is a review feedback email in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to harden integer handling in Git's parse-options infrastructure. Junio C Hamano questions the naming and documentation of a new `precision` field in the `OPTION_INTEGER` struct, which was introduced in patch 2/5 of the series.

Junio raises two main points about the terminology and practical usage of the new field. First, he suggests that "precision" may not be the most intuitive term for what the field represents (the size/range of the integer storage), proposing "range" as an alternative. Second, he requests clarification in the documentation that the value represents "number of bytes" since the current description only references `sizeof()`. He also asks whether there are existing cases where this value wouldn't match the natural `sizeof()` of the target variable.

The feedback is narrowly focused on documentation clarity for this one field, not challenging the overall approach of the patch series. This appears to be a refinement suggestion rather than a blocking concern, continuing the pattern of detailed technical review this series has been receiving (as noted in the thread summary's mention of Phillip Wood's earlier feedback on overflow detection).
