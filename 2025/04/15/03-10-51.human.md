Performance optimization in pack-objects for better delta compression with loose objects.

This is part 6/8 of Taylor Blau's series improving MIDX and cruft pack interactions during repacking. The patch fixes an oversight where loose objects weren't being considered as potential delta bases when using `--unpacked` with `--stdin-packs`. While not affecting correctness, this was missing an opportunity for better delta compression.

The change passes the `rev_info` struct through to the loose object enumeration code, allowing commits to be added as reachability tips for the name-hash traversal. This should produce slightly more efficient packs by finding better delta relationships. Elijah Newren notes the patch could benefit from tests showing the improved delta selection, though the core approach looks sound.

The modification touches several functions in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, primarily plumbing the `rev_info` through the loose object handling chain. The series as a whole has been receiving detailed review, with this patch representing one of the more technical optimizations in the set.
