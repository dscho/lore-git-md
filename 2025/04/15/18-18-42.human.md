Post-merge CI failure investigation for Meson benchmark integration.

Junio Hamano reports that after merging D. Ben Knoble's series to wire up benchmark support in Meson, multiple CI jobs are failing - both Meson-related tasks and even documentation verification. The failures weren't present in pre-merge testing, suggesting either a merge conflict resolution issue or some environment-specific behavior that wasn't caught earlier.

Two specific failure modes are highlighted: a Linux Meson job failing during header file detection with a Git command error (status 128), and a Windows Meson test failing in Python's asyncio. Junio suspects a "stupid merge mistake" may be to blame and has asked Patrick Steinhardt (who provided key review feedback on the original patches) to investigate.

The core functionality of the benchmark integration remains technically sound - this appears to be an integration/CI-specific issue rather than a design flaw. The failures are broad enough to affect even unrelated documentation verification, suggesting the problem may lie in some fundamental build system interaction rather than the benchmark-specific changes themselves. Next steps depend on Patrick's diagnosis of the root cause.
