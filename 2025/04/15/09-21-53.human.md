This is a contributor housekeeping email regarding AI-generated spam in mentoring programs like GSoC and Outreachy. Christian Couder shares that the project has added new AI guidelines to their General Application Information page after seeing a dramatic increase in AI-generated spam proposals (71 out of 79 this year, up from just a handful in previous years). The guidelines were developed through discussions among GSoC mentors and org admins, with input from other organizations experiencing similar issues.

The email notes that other open source projects have found such guidelines effective at reducing spam, and there's been significant discussion about this on the GSoC mentors list. Couder invites feedback on whether these guidelines should be expanded to cover the entire Git project beyond just mentoring programs. The context suggests this is becoming a pressing issue for open source communities managing volunteer contributor programs.
