Bugfix follow-up to a Perforce test improvement series. Junio C Hamano identifies and fixes a test regression introduced in the final version of Anthony Wang's patch series that modernized the `t9811-git-p4-label-import.sh` test. The series had converted the test from fragile text matching to direct ref verification, but the v7 patch incorrectly added a negative check for TAG_F1_ONLY that caused CI failures on macOS.

The issue stems from the test expecting TAG_F1_ONLY to not exist, when in fact the CI environment shows it should exist. Hamano notes this was an over-eager conversion - the original suggestion for a negative test was sound, but the specific tag chosen for verification was incorrect. The fix simply removes the problematic check while keeping all other verification improvements from the series intact.

This demonstrates the careful attention given to test reliability in Git, where even after seven iterations and final approval, unexpected platform-specific issues can surface. The fix maintains the series' core improvements while correcting the macOS-specific regression. The thread shows how experienced maintainers help guide contributors through these final polishing stages.
