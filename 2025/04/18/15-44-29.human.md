This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about enabling bitmap lookup tables by default. Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King's technical analysis about whether the pack-bitmap and midx bitmap flags need to be synchronized.

The key takeaway is that Junio accepts Jeff's assessment that while the flags could technically be unified, there's no strong practical need since the systems can operate with different settings without causing problems. Junio concludes the architectural refactoring isn't worth the effort since divergent flags won't lead to misbehavior.

This email marks resolution of one of the thread's open questions - the maintainer is now satisfied with keeping the separate flag systems rather than pursuing unification. The discussion continues on other aspects like test coverage philosophy and measurement accuracy, but this particular technical concern appears settled.
