This is a performance optimization series targeting bundle creation, specifically addressing non-linear scaling when handling repositories with many references. The cover letter presents a compelling case with clear benchmarks showing the problem: bundle creation time grows quadratically (O(N^2)) with reference count, becoming 2200x slower at 100k refs compared to 100 refs.

The root cause is identified as an inefficient duplicate refname check during bundle creation. The series consists of two patches: first adding test coverage (including catching a previously missed edge case), then replacing the O(N^2) algorithm with an O(1) strset-based approach that provides a 6x speedup. The changes touch bundle.c, object.c, object.h, and add new tests in t6020-bundle-misc.sh.

The author notes a potential merge conflict with the ongoing 'ps/object-wo-the-repository' series but considers it easily resolvable. The technical approach appears sound, with thorough benchmarking and test coverage. The performance impact is significant enough that this would likely be prioritized for repositories with large numbers of references.
