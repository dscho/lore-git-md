This is a review response in Patrick Steinhardt's series eliminating Perl dependencies from Git's test suite. Johannes Schindelin provides feedback on patch 12/20, which refactors tests that depend on Perl for printing data.

Schindelin agrees with the overall approach of replacing Perl printing functionality with POSIX tools like awk, sed, and test-tool genzeros. He suggests two minor refinements: that genzeros could be enhanced to generate non-NUL byte streams (which would make some conversions even cleaner), and notes that one case of printing data might be better handled with `sort -r` rather than awk.

The feedback is positive and constructive, coming after the series has already received broad approval from other reviewers. This appears to be final polish on an otherwise technically sound patch that's part of a larger, well-received effort to reduce Perl dependencies in Git's test infrastructure. The exchange suggests the series is nearing final acceptance, with only minor organizational feedback remaining.
