Bundle performance optimization series by Karthik Nayak addressing O(N^2) scaling in bundle creation due to inefficient duplicate refname checks, now complete with all three patches. The series begins with comprehensive test coverage (t6020-bundle-misc.sh) documenting current behavior and known bugs, followed by the core optimization replacing quadratic `object_array_remove_duplicates()` with an O(1) `strset`-based solution in `write_bundle_refs()`, yielding 6x speedup (14.6s → 2.4s at 100k refs) and fixing test-identified bugs where duplicates slipped through with `--all` and short/long refname variants. The solution handles both same-refname cases (regardless of object identity) and exclusion patterns, focusing only on INTERESTING objects for bundle creation.

Version 2 incorporates the stylistic change from explicit `strset_init()` to `STRMAP_INIT` macro while maintaining identical functionality. Implementation removes `object_array_remove_duplicates()` entirely, touching bundle.c, object.c, and object.h. Toon Claes verified edge cases beyond the test suite and approved the approach, referencing his prior work on bundle race conditions (commit b2a6d1c686). The test patch (1/3) adds 57 lines of coverage across four scenarios, explicitly marking the bug cases that the optimization addresses. The final patch (3/3) introduces the `strset` to track seen refnames during bundle header writing, eliminating full duplicate checking while handling both same-refname cases (identical/different objects) and exclusion patterns.

The series demonstrates consensus on correctness with benchmarking confirming linear scaling where the original was quadratic.
