Bundle performance optimization series addressing O(N^2) scaling in bundle creation due to inefficient duplicate refname checks. Authored by Karthik Nayak, the two-patch series first established comprehensive test coverage (t6020-bundle-misc.sh) documenting current limitations, then implemented the optimization. The second patch replaces the quadratic `object_array_remove_duplicates()` with an O(1) `strset`-based solution in `write_bundle_refs()`, yielding 6x speedup (14.6s → 2.4s at 100k refs) and linear scaling where the original showed 2200x slowdown. The optimization handles two key cases: identical refnames pointing to same/different objects (including short/long variants and exclusion patterns), focusing only on INTERESTING objects for bundle creation. Implementation removes the now-unneeded `object_array_remove_duplicates()` entirely. Toon Claes reviewed the implementation, confirming it correctly handles both same-refname cases (regardless of object identity) and exclusion patterns, while raising a minor style question about using `STRMAP_INIT` macro rather than explicit `strset_init()`. Test changes confirm fixes for previously marked edge cases, with Claes providing additional verification. Technical approach appears sound with comprehensive benchmarking showing dramatic performance improvements, particularly relevant for repositories with large numbers of references.
