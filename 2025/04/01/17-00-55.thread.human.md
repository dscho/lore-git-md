Karthik Nayak has proposed a performance optimization series for Git's bundle creation, addressing a significant O(N^2) scaling issue when checking for duplicate refnames. The series introduces a more efficient `strset`-based approach that reduces bundle creation time from 14.6 seconds to 2.4 seconds when handling 100,000 refs—a 6x improvement. 

The optimization fixes two known bugs where duplicate refs could slip through, particularly with `--all` and when dealing with short/long refname variants. The implementation replaces the quadratic `object_array_remove_duplicates()` with an O(1) solution in `write_bundle_refs()`, tracking seen refnames to avoid redundant checks. The changes span `bundle.c`, `object.c`, and `object.h`, with comprehensive test coverage added in `t6020-bundle-misc.sh`.
