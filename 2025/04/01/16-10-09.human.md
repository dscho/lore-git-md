This is a follow-up in the Cygwin regression fix thread, where Johannes Schindelin responds to Mark Levedahl's patch series that addresses Windows/Cygwin compatibility issues in gitk. 

Schindelin acknowledges the breakage was caused by copying code from git-gui to gitk without proper Cygwin consideration, and approves the current approach of restricting exec/open overrides to Windows only. He notes the patches look good after reviewing them, though wishes for better review tooling (like `-w` diff flag) to make the changes more obvious. 

The response indicates no technical objections to the series, which has already incorporated feedback from other maintainers. Schindelin's approval ("these patches look good to me" and apology for the original breakage) suggests this is likely the final form before merging. The thread appears to be wrapping up with consensus on both the technical solution and the need for more careful cross-platform consideration in future changes.
