This is a follow-up discussion in the rebase status reporting improvements series, specifically about patch 3/3 which deals with merge instruction messaging during interactive rebase.

Johannes Schindelin raises an important technical question about the limitations of the current approach. While the patch correctly suggests `git rebase --continue` to preserve authorship information during merge operations in a rebase, Johannes points out an inconsistency: during interrupted `pick` operations, `git commit` does retain authorship information. He questions why this same mechanism can't be applied to merge operations, suggesting there may be deeper architectural considerations at play.

The discussion touches on the interaction between:
- The rebase sequencer's handling of authorship information
- Differences in behavior between `pick` and `merge` operations during rebase
- Potential inconsistencies in how interrupted operations preserve metadata

This query doesn't challenge the patch's correctness but probes the underlying design decisions that lead to the current behavior. The question implies there might be room for future improvements in how authorship information is handled consistently across different rebase operations. The thread context shows this is part of an otherwise well-reviewed series that's nearing submission, with Phillip Wood having approved the technical approach.
