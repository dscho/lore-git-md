This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series hardening integer handling in Git's parse-options infrastructure, specifically addressing signedness mismatches. The patch fixes a real-world issue where `--min-batch-size` in `git-backfill` failed on big-endian systems (s390x and sparc64) due to passing a `size_t` to `OPT_INTEGER()`.

The solution introduces compile-time assertions (`BARF_UNLESS_SIGNED` and `BARF_UNLESS_UNSIGNED`) to catch type mismatches early. The patch updates several problematic call sites across `apply.c`, `backfill.c`, `column.c`, and `grep.c` to use the correct signed/unsigned variants. The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions from `OPT_INTEGER` to `OPT_UNSIGNED` where appropriate.

This completes the series' goal of making integer option parsing more robust by adding compile-time safety checks. The patch has already identified and fixed multiple latent bugs, showing the value of the approach. The changes are well-contained and follow the patterns established in earlier patches of the series.
