Meet Soni's GSoC proposal to consolidate Git's reference-related commands into a unified `git refs` command has evolved through discussion on the mailing list. The original vision included folding in reflog functionality, but after feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak, there's now consensus to keep `git reflog` as a standalone command to avoid awkward subcommand hierarchies.

The current proposal focuses on merging `update-ref`, `show-ref`, `for-each-ref` and `pack-refs` into structured subcommands under `git refs`, with `symbolic-ref` and `check-ref-format` as stretch goals. Meet has already implemented a proof-of-concept `list` subcommand and demonstrated responsiveness to technical feedback through multiple iterations. The design includes careful consideration of backward compatibility and a structured approach to option sharing through a common options struct.

Implementation would proceed in four phases from June through August, building on Meet's prior experience with refspec consolidation and reftable work. While the scope remains ambitious for a single GSoC period, Meet's track record of delivering complex refactoring work suggests the project is well-grounded. Some open questions remain around configuration hierarchy details and symref implementation approaches, but the core architecture decisions appear settled.
