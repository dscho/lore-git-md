Meet Soni has submitted a GSoC proposal to consolidate Git's reference-related commands into a unified `git-refs` interface. Building on their prior contributions (including refspec work and performance optimizations), they propose creating a new `git refs` command with subcommands like `list`, `exist`, and `pack` that would absorb functionality from `update-ref`, `show-ref`, `for-each-ref`, and `pack-refs`. 

The proposal demonstrates careful consideration of backward compatibility, with detailed mappings of existing flags to new subcommand options. A proof-of-concept implementation for the `list` subcommand is already available. The three-phase timeline aims to tackle one major command consolidation per GSoC coding period segment, with stretch goals including `symbolic-ref` integration. Soni's demonstrated familiarity with Git's development process and thorough technical approach make this a well-grounded proposal for modernizing the refs interface.
