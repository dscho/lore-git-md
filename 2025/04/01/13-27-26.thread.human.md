Kristoffer initially defended the plural form for consistency with other documentation but acknowledged the singular version reads more naturally. Since the series is already merged, this is unlikely to trigger a new patch unless a maintainer opts to incorporate it later. The discussion remains low-stakes but reflects the project’s ongoing attention to clarity.
