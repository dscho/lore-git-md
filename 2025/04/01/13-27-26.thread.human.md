This thread documents a thorough improvement to `git-interpret-trailers`'s documentation, prompted by a bug report about its strict parsing of trailer keys (allowing only ASCII alphanumerics and hyphens). Kristoffer Haugsbakk led an 11-patch series that systematically clarified the command's behavior, terminology, and edge cases—particularly around invalid keys like those containing periods. 

The series standardized terms like "metadata" and "trailer block," removed outdated references, and added clear examples of both valid and rejected inputs. D. Ben Knoble provided consistent feedback on terminology and organization, which Kristoffer incorporated. The final patch, just submitted, adds a dedicated section explaining comment line handling via `core.commentString`, completing the documentation's coverage. 

Junio Hamano has merged the series after multiple rounds of review, resolving the original confusion while maintaining the intentional strict parsing behavior. The result is a comprehensive, pedagogically structured man page that clearly defines what trailer keys are allowed and how the command processes them.
