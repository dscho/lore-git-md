This thread began with a bug report about git-interpret-trailers' inability to handle trailer keys containing periods (like "My-6.11-Version"). Maintainer Junio Hamano confirmed this strict parsing (alphanumeric + hyphen only) is intentional for reliability. Rather than making the parser more lenient, Kristoffer Haugsbakk took this as an opportunity to comprehensively improve the documentation through a 9-patch series.

The series systematically refactored the man page to clarify key concepts: it standardized terminology (using "metadata" and "trailer blocks"), removed outdated RFC 822 references, explicitly stated the ASCII alphanumeric + hyphen requirement for keys, and documented the parser's complete behavior - including how it handles blank lines and now, in this final patch, comment lines. The documentation now properly covers all aspects of trailer processing, from key-value structure to block formation rules.

Reviewers Junio Hamano and Ben Knoble provided feedback that helped refine the series, particularly around terminology consistency and pedagogical structure. The final patch adds the last missing piece - noting that comment lines (marked by core.commentString) are ignored, primarily for hook compatibility. With all patches reviewed and approved, this documentation overhaul is complete, making git-interpret-trailers' behavior and limitations clear to users.
