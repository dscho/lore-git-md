This thread began as a bug report about `git-interpret-trailers`' strict parsing of trailer keys, which silently ignored keys containing periods (e.g., "My-6.11-Version"). Junio Hamano confirmed this was intentional for reliability, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk subsequently landed an 11-patch documentation series that clarified the command's behavior with explicit definitions, standardized terminology, and clear examples. The series was merged with feedback from D. Ben Knoble and final polish from Junio.

Since the merge, the thread has focused on minor stylistic details. The only remaining discussion is whether the `DESCRIPTION` section of the man page should use **"trailer metadata"** (singular) or **"trailers metadata"** (plural). Matt Hunter recently suggested reverting to the singular form for better readability, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk agreed, though no follow-up patch is planned unless a maintainer chooses to adopt the change. The thread now serves as an example of the project's attention to phrasing clarity, even in settled work.
