The approach centralizes the implementation details in hash.h while maintaining compile-time safety, eliminating the need for duplicate detection code. This aligns with the thread's earlier technical decisions to use symbol-based reporting from hash.h while keeping the maintenance burden low.

- The implementation remains contained to hash.h (for symbol definitions) and help.c (for output formatting)
