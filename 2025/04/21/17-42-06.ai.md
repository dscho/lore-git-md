This is a follow-up in the macOS launchctl maintenance scheduler bugfix thread. Junio C Hamano reviews and approves Josh Heinrichs' patch that corrects the calendar interval specifications for Git maintenance jobs on macOS.

The patch replaces incorrect `Day` keys with `Weekday` keys in launchctl plist configurations, properly aligning with launchd.plist(5) documentation. Junio notes that while the original implementation mistakenly scheduled daily jobs for days 1-6 of each month (rather than weekdays 1-6), the new version correctly targets Monday through Saturday. For weekly jobs, the change from `Day:0` to `Weekday:0` properly schedules them for Sundays.

Junio observes some inconsistencies in how different schedulers handle Sunday (day 0/7) across platforms but considers this out of scope for the current macOS-specific fix. He confirms the technical correctness of the solution and queues the patch, though he notes his limited personal experience with macOS/launchctl. The review maintains focus on the specific launchctl scheduling issue without expanding to broader scheduler consistency questions.
