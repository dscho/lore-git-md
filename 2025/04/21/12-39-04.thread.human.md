The work builds on Git for Windows' existing ARM64 compatibility efforts.

Junio Hamano has acknowledged the series with a positive note, indicating he will queue the patches for integration without requesting changes. His "cleanly structured" comment suggests the submission follows Git's contribution norms well. While this isn't a deep technical review, the maintainer's acceptance signals the changes are likely to proceed unless other reviewers raise objections. 

The next steps will involve verifying the patches work as intended on actual ARM64 Windows runners and watching for any platform-specific issues that emerge in testing. The co-authors - particularly Dscho with his Git for Windows expertise - would likely handle any follow-up fixes needed. With Junio's approval, this support should land in an upcoming release, expanding Git's platform coverage.
