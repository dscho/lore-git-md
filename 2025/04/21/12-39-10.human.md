Platform compatibility patch addressing stack overflow issues on Windows/ARM64 with clang builds. This is the final patch in a 6-part series enabling Git to work properly on Windows/ARM64 systems, particularly targeting GitHub's new ARM64 runners.

The patch lowers the maximum tree depth from 2048 to 1280 for clang-based Windows/ARM64 builds, mirroring a similar change made previously for MSVC builds. The issue manifests as a stack overflow during `git archive` operations on deep trees, which gets reported as exit code 127 (normally "command not found") due to Cygwin's handling of the STATUS_STACK_OVERFLOW error. The author verified the overflow occurs around depth 1403, making 1280 a safe conservative limit.

This change follows the established pattern from the series - identifying and working around platform-specific limitations while maintaining functionality. The patch is narrowly scoped to affect only clang/ARM64 Windows builds, leaving other platforms unchanged. As with the rest of the series, this appears to be a well-justified change based on actual testing, though reviewers may want to verify the chosen depth threshold.
