The thread reveals how Git's 20-year history has led to some organic inconsistencies in the build system that are challenging to reconcile during modernization.

Current consensus maintains the generic qsort() detection approach rather than encoding known slow platforms, though this differs from Makefile's platform-specific handling for AIX and MinGW. The macro naming inconsistency is acknowledged as problematic but difficult to change due to backwards compatibility requirements. With git-gui already Meson-compatible and gitk changes in review, the autoconf deprecation timeline remains on track, though some inherited behaviors from the Makefile system continue to be questioned.
