CI infrastructure update response from a key contributor. Brian Carlson replies to Junio Hamano's questions about migrating Git's CI from Ubuntu 20.04 to 22.04 ahead of GitHub's April 15 deprecation. They confirm that Docker-based jobs can remain on 20.04 for now (since Docker Hub still hosts the image), but runner-based jobs must update. Key points include: GCC-8 testing should be updated since it likely won't be available on 22.04; the 32-bit Linux job could move to Ubuntu Jammy or Debian; and Johannes Schindelen (Dscho) should handle the sparse package updates. Carlson notes they're time-constrained today but offers to follow up with patches if needed. The response provides practical guidance while deferring some decisions to other maintainers, keeping the transition moving forward without blocking on open questions.
