Response to RFC about preserving commit headers during rebase. Brian m. carlson raises significant concerns about propagating unvalidated commit headers during rebase operations, drawing from extensive experience with malformed commit metadata in production systems. They argue for configurable opt-in behavior rather than automatic header preservation, citing numerous real-world examples of corrupted headers they've encountered (invalid timezones, malformed emails, non-UTF-8 names, etc.). While not fundamentally opposed to the feature, they emphasize the risk of permanently propagating invalid data that current Git versions can't validate, suggesting this could create long-term data quality issues. The response invites further discussion, particularly from Patrick Steinhardt who is CC'd, while acknowledging the practical needs of forges that motivated the original proposal. This appears to be a thoughtful counterpoint that will likely shape the feature's design constraints if it moves forward.
