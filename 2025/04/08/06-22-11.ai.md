This is a cover letter for a 6-patch series introducing a "reflog-expire" maintenance task, part of the ongoing effort to modernize Git's maintenance system. The series builds on previous discussion about replacing `git gc` with more granular maintenance tasks.

The key technical work refactors reflog expiration to remove global state, moving expiry dates into a `reflog_expire_options` struct with new `default_expire_total` and `default_expire_unreachable` fields. This enables the new maintenance task while improving encapsulation, though some design limitations remain (acknowledged by the author as a pragmatic tradeoff).

The cover letter positions this as part of a broader strategy to make maintenance more flexible, outlining a potential rollout plan: first enabling reflog-expire with the experimental strategy, then eventually making "incremental" the default strategy. The v2 changes are minor typo fixes from the initial submission.

The series touches several areas: reflog expiration internals (moving state management), maintenance task plumbing (adding the new task), and documentation updates. It represents an incremental but important step in the maintenance system evolution, with the author noting work on other maintenance gaps (like worktree pruning) may follow.
