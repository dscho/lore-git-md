This is a platform compatibility patch addressing a build warning from sparse with newer glibc headers. The original patch by Đoàn Trần Công Danh adds `-D__STDC_NO_VLA__` to sparse flags to suppress warnings about variable-length arrays in glibc's regex.h implementation, which are an implementation detail not relevant to Git's usage.

Johannes Schindelin replies with appreciation, noting the patch is timely as it's needed for his follow-up CI change that moves the sparse job from Ubuntu 20.04 to 22.04 runners. His patch removes the custom sparse package installation since Ubuntu 22.04 provides a suitable version natively.
