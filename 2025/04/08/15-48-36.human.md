This is patch 2/8 in Elijah Newren's series to remove the legacy merge-recursive implementation in favor of merge-ort. The patch switches `builtin/merge-recursive.c` from using `merge_recursive_generic()` to `merge_ort_generic()`, continuing the systematic conversion of all callers to the new merge backend.

The change is straightforward - a simple function swap with identical parameters and error handling. However, the patch documents several test adjustments needed due to behavioral differences between the two merge backends:

1. Improved directory/file conflict handling in merge-ort affects t6430, with some tests simplified and one removed entirely since it depended on merge-recursive-specific output.

2. Rename/delete conflict handling is more correct in merge-ort (creating two index entries per conflict), requiring updates to t6434's expectations.

3. The special "Already up to date" message for toplevel tree matches is removed in merge-ort, with t6424 updated to verify identical trees rather than checking output.

The changes appear well-justified and maintain the series' careful approach of mechanical conversions first. The test updates align with known merge-ort improvements documented in previous commits. This continues the low-risk transition strategy while cleaning up test expectations to match merge-ort's more robust behavior.
