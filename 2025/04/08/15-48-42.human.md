This is the final patch in Elijah Newren's series to fully remove the legacy merge-recursive implementation in favor of merge-ort. The patch removes test scaffolding that was previously used to switch between the two merge backends during testing.

With merge-recursive now completely gone and 'recursive' remapped to use merge-ort internally, the GIT_TEST_MERGE_ALGORITHM environment variable is no longer needed. The patch removes all references to this variable from three commands that previously used it (merge, rebase, and revert), simplifying their code.

The changes are straightforward mechanical removals, deleting 20 lines while adding just 1 (a default NULL assignment). This cleanup follows naturally from the earlier patches in the series that completed the merge-ort transition. The patch represents the last step in removing test infrastructure that was specific to the now-obsolete merge-recursive backend.

As with the rest of the series, this appears to be a well-considered change that completes the architectural cleanup of Git's merge machinery. The patch is small, focused, and clearly motivated by the broader context of the series.
