Performance optimization patch for `git bundle create` that fixes O(N^2) scaling with many refs. This is the second and final patch in Karthik Nayak's series, following the test coverage added in v2 1/2. The patch replaces the quadratic `object_array_remove_duplicates()` function with an O(1) `strset`-based solution in `write_bundle_refs()`, achieving a 6x speedup (14.6s → 2.4s at 100k refs) while also fixing previously identified bugs where duplicates could appear in bundle headers.

The optimization focuses on eliminating duplicate refnames during bundle creation without comparing referenced objects, which is sufficient for the bundle format's needs. The patch handles two key scenarios: same-refname duplicates (regardless of object identity) and exclusion patterns where refnames may point to both INTERESTING and UNINTERESTING objects. The implementation removes the now-unused `object_array_remove_duplicates()` function entirely, touching bundle.c, object.c, and object.h.

The thread shows consensus on the approach, with Toon Claes having verified edge cases beyond the test suite. The series appears ready for merging with comprehensive test coverage and no outstanding technical objections, though it needs to resolve a simple merge conflict with the ps/object-wo-the-repository work.
