This is the first patch in a major refactoring series to remove Git's legacy merge-recursive implementation in favor of merge-ort. The patch switches `builtin/checkout.c` from using `merge_trees()` (from merge-recursive) to its merge-ort equivalent `merge_ort_nonrecursive()`.

The change is straightforward - just updating the include and function call while keeping the same parameters and behavior. This is part of the systematic transition of all callers to merge-ort before the final removal of merge-recursive code. The patch shows the typical pattern for these conversions: minimal changes to call sites while maintaining identical functionality.

Since this is part of a larger v3 series that has already received maintainer approval for the technical approach, and given the mechanical nature of this particular change, it appears uncontroversial and well-executed. The series as a whole represents the culmination of a multi-year effort to modernize Git's merge machinery.
