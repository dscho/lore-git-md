This is part of the major refactoring series to fully replace merge-recursive with merge-ort, marking the final stages of this multi-year effort. Patch 5/8 focuses on converting the remaining callers in `builtin/merge.c` and `sequencer.c` to use `merge_ort_recursive()` instead of the legacy `merge_recursive()`.

The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions, removing conditional checks for the "ort" strategy since it's now the only option. The sequencer changes include updating comments to reflect the new reality, though a TODO remains about potentially optimizing the merge result handling. A test script is also updated to remove an obsolete reference to the recursive algorithm.

This patch follows the established pattern of the series - systematically switching callers while maintaining behavior. The changes are low-risk since merge-ort has been the default since Git 2.33.0 and has proven stable. With this conversion, all major callers have now been transitioned away from merge-recursive, paving the way for its complete removal in later patches.
