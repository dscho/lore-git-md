Patrick Steinhardt's major refactoring of Git's object-file subsystem has been merged, completing a 9-patch series that reorganizes how Git handles object storage to enable future pluggable backends. The changes touched over 124 files, carefully separating generic utilities, object storage, and index operations while maintaining all existing functionality. Junio Hamano merged the series despite some initial integration challenges, indicating the work is now considered complete for the next release.

A follow-up question has emerged from Elijah Newren about the first patch in the series, which moved directory creation utilities from object-file.c to dir.c. While agreeing with the overall move, Newren questions the characterization linking `mkdir_in_gitdir()` with `safe_create_leading_directories()`, as they don't share a direct caller-callee relationship. The clarification seeks to better understand the organizational rationale behind grouping these utilities together during the refactoring. This is a minor point on an already-merged series, but reflects the careful attention the community gives to architectural decisions, even after integration.
