This is the final approval and merge notice for Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove Perl as a mandatory test dependency. Junio C Hamano confirms he will merge the v4 patches into the 'next' branch after receiving Johannes Schindelin's sign-off. The series has gone through multiple rounds of review with no substantive changes needed in this final version.

The 20-patch series successfully eliminates Perl as a core test requirement while maintaining backward compatibility, reducing the Windows installer size by 10MB. Only HTTPD/CGI tests retain an explicit Perl dependency. The work preserves all test behavior through careful conversions to shell/awk/sed alternatives.

With Schindelin's approval (as the Windows maintainer) and Phillip Wood's earlier review, this marks the conclusion of a major infrastructure modernization effort that began with establishing the PERL_TEST_HELPERS framework. The series has seen consistent positive reception through its development and is now ready for integration into Git's main development branch.
