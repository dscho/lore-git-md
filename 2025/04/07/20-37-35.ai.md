Maintainer response in trailer parsing discussion. Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing debate about git-interpret-trailers' handling of period characters in trailer keys, siding with Christian Couder's position that maintaining strict parsing rules is preferable to introducing configurability. The response reinforces the view that relaxing key format requirements (currently alphanumeric + hyphen) would be harmful to the ecosystem, potentially leading to false positives where regular text gets misparsed as trailers. Hamano goes further to question the wisdom of even the existing #BUGID special case, suggesting it may have been a mistake to deviate from strict email-header-like syntax. While acknowledging documentation could be improved, the message clearly signals maintainer preference against implementing configurable leniency, effectively closing the door on that solution path unless compelling new arguments emerge. This appears to settle the thread's central question in favor of maintaining current behavior with documentation improvements, though the test case demonstrating the version-tracking use case remains available should someone propose an alternative approach that addresses the maintainers' concerns about parser reliability.
