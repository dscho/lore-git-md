This is a positive review of a cleanup patch in the `the_repository` removal effort. Patrick Steinhardt is responding to Arnav Bhate's patch that deletes two unused global variables (`packed_git_window_size` and `packed_git_limit`) from environment.h. 

The patch had already been verified in the thread as removing truly obsolete variables - their functionality was moved to struct repo_settings in commit d284713bae back in December 2024. Previous reviews from Eric Sunshine and René Scharfe confirmed the variables were unused and the change was merge-ready. 

Steinhardt's brief reply serves as another positive review, calling it a "good catch" and stating the patch "looks obviously good." This appears to be the final approval needed before this straightforward cleanup can be merged. The patch represents incremental progress in the larger effort to eliminate global variables from Git's codebase.
