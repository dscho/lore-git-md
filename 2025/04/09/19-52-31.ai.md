The error appears consistently and persists after complete reinstallation of Git for Windows.

The error message contains corrupted characters (likely due to encoding issues when displaying Portuguese text in the phrase about certificate revocation function failure. This suggests the problem might be locale-specific or related to how schannel handles certificate checks in non-English environments.
