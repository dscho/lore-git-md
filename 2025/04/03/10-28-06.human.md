This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing GSoC proposal thread about consolidating Git's reference-related commands. Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on two aspects of Meet Soni's proposal.

First, regarding the plan to update call sites of legacy commands, Patrick suggests this doesn't need to be part of the GSoC project itself since the old commands will remain for backward compatibility, but could be mentioned as future work. This reflects the practical reality that many external scripts rely on these commands.

Second, Patrick addresses Meet's enthusiasm about potentially starting work early during the community bonding period. While supportive of the initiative, Patrick cautions against completing too much work before the official GSoC period begins, suggesting instead to frame additional command consolidations as stretch goals. This maintains flexibility while setting reasonable expectations.

The exchange shows the proposal is in its final refinement stage, with Patrick providing practical guidance about scope and timeline management. The tone remains constructive, focusing on setting up the project for success while acknowledging the contributor's eagerness.
