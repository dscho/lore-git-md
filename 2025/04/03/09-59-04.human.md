This is feedback on a GSoC proposal about refactoring Git's global state, specifically addressing the removal of `the_repository` and other globals. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Arnav Bhate's initial plan, offering practical advice about scope and approach.

Patrick suggests the proposal's goals may be too ambitious given the timeframe, noting that removing ~3500 `the_repository` occurrences is a multi-year effort already in progress. He recommends focusing on a more limited, well-defined subset of variables rather than attempting to tackle all global state at once. The feedback also emphasizes working in smaller, iterative batches rather than distinct phases of problem definition, solution development, and deployment.

The tone is constructive, with Patrick drawing from experience with large-scale refactoring projects. He doesn't reject the proposal outright but rather suggests refining the scope and methodology to make the work more manageable within the GSoC timeline. This appears to be early-stage guidance to help shape a more focused and achievable project plan.
