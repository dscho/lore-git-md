This is a follow-up discussion in the rebase status reporting improvements series, specifically addressing authorship preservation during conflicted rebases.

Johannes Schindelin clarifies an important distinction in behavior: while `git cherry-pick` preserves authorship when conflicts are resolved via `git commit`, the same doesn't hold true for `git rebase` operations (both `pick` and `merge` commands). This confirms Phillip Wood's earlier observation that authorship loss is consistent across all rebase operations when using `git commit` to resolve conflicts, not just merges as initially thought in the patch discussion.

The email raises an architectural question about why the authorship-preserving mechanism from `cherry-pick` can't be applied to rebase operations. This doesn't directly impact the current patch series (which focuses on status reporting improvements), but points to a broader usability issue worth investigating separately. The discussion remains technical and constructive, with Johannes building on Phillip's earlier clarification to probe deeper into the underlying behavior differences.
