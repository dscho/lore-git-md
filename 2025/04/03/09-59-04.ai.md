This is feedback on a GSoC proposal about refactoring Git's global state, specifically addressing the `the_repository` removal effort. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Arnav Bhate's initial proposal, offering practical advice about scoping the ambitious project.

The response suggests the original proposal is too broad - noting the ~3500 occurrences of `the_repository` make this a multi-year project rather than something achievable in a GSoC timeframe. Patrick recommends focusing on specific subsets of global variables rather than attempting comprehensive coverage. He also advises working in distinct phases (problem definition, solution development, deployment) and instead suggests smaller, iterative batches of work that combine these elements.

Key technical points mentioned include the need to properly scope which variables to target, and the suggestion to work in logical groupings of related variables that can be moved together in coherent patch series. The feedback maintains the connection to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort while tempering expectations about what can realistically be accomplished in the proposed timeline.
