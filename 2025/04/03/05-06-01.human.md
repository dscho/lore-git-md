This is part 10/20 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Perl optional in Git's test suite, focusing on refactoring tests that use Perl's transliteration operator. The patch replaces Perl's "y/" and "tr/" operators with the standard Unix `tr` command in several test scripts, allowing the removal of PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisites from those tests.

The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions, touching seven test files where Perl was previously used for simple character substitution. The modifications maintain identical functionality while reducing the test suite's Perl dependency. Notably, one test in t4103-apply-binary.sh still requires Perl for more complex validation of truncated binary diffs, keeping its PERL_TEST_HELPERS marker.

This continues the systematic approach seen throughout the series - each patch tackles a specific category of Perl dependency. The changes are clean and focused, with each conversion clearly serving the overall goal of Perl removal while maintaining test coverage. The patch follows the established pattern of the series and appears ready for inclusion.
