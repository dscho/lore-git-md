This is a follow-up in Meet Soni's GSoC proposal thread about consolidating Git's reference-related commands into a unified `git-refs` command. The email primarily addresses feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on naming conventions, implementation phasing, and configuration design.

Key updates in this response:
- Meet agrees to rename the "pack" subcommand to "optimize" (more backend-neutral) and "check-format" to "check-name" (more precise)
- Configuration structure will be revised to embed common options in subcommand-specific structs rather than a top-level container
- Implementation timeline will be adjusted to group related functionality (show-ref/for-each-ref overlap) and potentially move show-ref work earlier
- Clarifies that early work during community bonding would only proceed if officially selected for GSoC

The discussion shows steady progress on the proposal's technical design, with Meet demonstrating responsiveness to feedback while maintaining the project's core goal of creating a more unified interface for ref operations. The exchange suggests the proposal is maturing well, with concrete improvements to naming and structure emerging from the review process.
