Karthik Nayak provides a review of Patch 4/16 in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable API refactoring series. This is a refactoring change that simplifies how restart points are tracked in reftable blocks.

The patch eliminates redundant tracking of block length and restart point locations by consolidating them into a single `restart_off` field. Karthik confirms the technical approach is sound, noting the change makes sense given the block structure where restart points are located after record data. He offers two minor nits: (1) clarifying the commit message to show both `block_len` and `restart_bytes` are being removed (not just `block_len`), and (2) suggesting `header_offset` might be clearer than `header_off` for consistency. The review concludes with "The patch looks good," indicating approval pending these minor documentation tweaks.

This continues the series' pattern of positive technical reviews with minor suggestions for improved clarity, maintaining the high quality seen in earlier patches.
