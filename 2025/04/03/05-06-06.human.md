This is patch 15/20 in Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove Perl as a mandatory test dependency. The patch refactors the `name_from_description()` test helper function, converting it from Perl to a combination of sed and tr commands. This change allows two test scripts (t6002-rev-list-bisect.sh and t6003-rev-list-topo-order.sh) to drop their PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisite since they no longer need Perl for this functionality.

The conversion maintains the same behavior - it still converts non-alphanumeric characters to dashes, compresses multiple dashes, trims leading/trailing dashes, and lowercases the result. The patch follows the series' established pattern of mechanical substitutions where possible while maintaining backward compatibility.

This is a straightforward, well-contained change that continues the series' steady progress toward eliminating Perl dependencies. The patch has already received positive feedback in previous iterations and maintains the high quality seen throughout the series. With this change, the series is now 15/20 patches complete in its goal to make Perl optional for Git's test suite.
