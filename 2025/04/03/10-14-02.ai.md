This is feedback on a GSoC project proposal for a new `git metadata` command, focusing on repository information querying and JSON output. Patrick Steinhardt provides constructive criticism and suggestions to refine the proposal.

Second, Patrick recommends restructuring the development timeline to deliver smaller, reviewable components rather than one large deliverable at the end. He suggests identifying self-contained work units that can be submitted upstream earlier to get feedback and iterate on the design. This would reduce risk compared to the current plan of implementing all data structures first before any community review.
