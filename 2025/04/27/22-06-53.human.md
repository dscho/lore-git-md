Performance optimization for xdiff's `--minimal` mode, building on previous discussion. Niels Glodny's patch modifies the `cleanup_records` heuristic to avoid marking unchanged lines as changed when they're surrounded by many changes, but only when `--minimal` is explicitly requested. This addresses a case where the heuristic could produce longer-than-necessary diffs in about 1.3% of cases.

The change is narrowly scoped to only affect `--minimal` output, where shortest-possible diffs are the expected behavior. The implementation adds a new test case verifying that unchanged lines surrounded by changes don't appear in minimal diffs, and modifies the marking logic in `xdl_cleanup_records()` to respect the `XDF_NEED_MINIMAL` flag.

This follows up on Phillip Wood's earlier feedback about implementation details, though the current version still passes the `need_min` parameter through functions rather than deriving it from `cf->flags` as suggested. The patch appears technically sound and focused on its stated goal of making `--minimal` truly minimal when requested.
