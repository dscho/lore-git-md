This is a follow-up in the ongoing CI/build system discussion about handling external dependencies more efficiently in Git's CI pipeline.

The email continues the conversation about caching third-party software downloads (JGit, Perforce, Git-LFS) in CI jobs rather than redownloading them for every run. Junio Hamano had previously agreed that the current approach of running install-dependencies.sh repeatedly seems wasteful, suggesting a "once-per-platform" caching model would be better.

Shejialuo confirms they will investigate implementing this caching approach, though the details of how to do this effectively remain to be worked out. This builds on the series' earlier work that made dependency downloads optional with proper failure handling, while maintaining atomic operations and safety mechanisms.

The caching question appears to be the last major open item in this CI improvement effort, separate from the already-implemented warning presentation refinements and JGit source location update. The discussion remains focused on technical implementation rather than any disagreement about the value of caching.
