This is a community announcement about the upcoming Git Rev News edition. Christian Couder shares a draft of edition 122, which notably features content around Git's 20th anniversary. The email invites community contributions - including proofreading, content suggestions, and volunteer interviews - either via GitHub pull requests/issue comments or by replying to the email. The draft is currently available for review, with publication planned for May 2nd, 2025. The message serves both to preview the upcoming edition and to actively solicit last-minute community input before finalization.
