Bugfix investigation with memory corruption confirmation in `git rebase --rebase-merges`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk follows up on Jeff King's suggestion to use address and undefined behavior sanitizers, which successfully catches a heap-use-after-free error during a complex rebase operation with multiple merge conflicts.

The sanitizer trace shows that `ctx->reflog_message` in `sequencer.c` becomes invalid when the underlying strbuf is reallocated while an old pointer is still being used. This confirms Jeff King's hypothesis about a use-after-free scenario, explaining both the intermittent nature of the bug and why it was difficult to reproduce under GDB. The error occurs during `run_git_commit()` when trying to format the reflog message, with the problematic pointer originating from earlier `reflog_message()` calls.

This provides concrete evidence that the reflog corruption issue stems from improper management of the static strbuf used for reflog messages during merge operations in rebase. The sanitizer output gives developers clear pointers to the problematic memory management sequence, moving the investigation from hypothesis to confirmed root cause. The next step would be to design a fix that properly maintains the reflog message pointer through strbuf reallocations.
