This is a follow-up to the Git Merge 2025 conference announcement, addressing accessibility concerns for attendees who cannot travel to the US. Taylor Blau provides updates on remote participation options in response to feedback about travel challenges, particularly for international attendees.

The email announces that Git Merge will now accommodate remote speakers and attendees, with specific provisions for virtual participation in both the main conference and Contributor's Summit. Two-way A/V equipment will be set up to facilitate remote engagement in the Summit. Free remote attendance is offered, with options for refunds for those switching from in-person to virtual participation.

Key logistical details include a May 15, 2025 deadline for financial assistance requests (to allow time for visa processing) and flexibility for speakers to switch to virtual presentations. The changes aim to make the event more inclusive while acknowledging the disappointment of not meeting in person.

The email maintains the conference's inclusive tone while providing concrete solutions to the accessibility issues raised, particularly following brian m. carlson's earlier expression of safety concerns about traveling to the US. It serves as both an update to the original announcement and an invitation for further private discussion of individual concerns.
