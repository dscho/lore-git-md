Junio C Hamano is reviewing the second patch, which converts an `assert()` in `builtin/mv.c` to a `BUG()` call. Junio agrees with the approach, noting that using `BUG()` is preferred over `assert()` in the Git codebase. He references a prior series by Elijah Newren that established this pattern.
