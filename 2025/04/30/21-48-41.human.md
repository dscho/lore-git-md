This is an update to the Git Merge 2025 conference announcement addressing accessibility concerns. Taylor Blau responds to feedback about travel challenges to the US by announcing remote participation options. The conference will now allow virtual speakers and attendees, with two-way A/V equipment for the Contributor's Summit. Remote tickets are free, and existing in-person attendees can switch to virtual. The financial assistance deadline for travel support is set for May 15 to allow time for visa processing. While expressing sadness about not seeing everyone in person, the organizers aim to make the event more inclusive through these remote options. The email maintains the conference's welcoming tone while pragmatically addressing the real barriers some community members face in attending US-based events.
