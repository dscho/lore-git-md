This thread announces the upcoming Git Rev News edition celebrating Git's 20th anniversary, with Christian Couder sharing a draft of edition 122 for community review before its May 2, 2025 publication. Contributors are invited to participate by editing the GitHub draft, commenting on the coordination issue, or replying to the email—all forms of input, from proofreading to content suggestions, are welcome. The draft and issue are hosted at specified GitHub URLs, with Christian noting that while they tried to CC all relevant contributors, some may have been missed. The effort involves Christian Couder, Jakub, Markus, and Kaartic. This edition is expected to include retrospective content alongside regular project updates given the anniversary theme.
