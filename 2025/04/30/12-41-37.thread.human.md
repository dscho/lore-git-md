This thread is coordinating the upcoming Git Rev News edition 122, which will celebrate Git's 20th anniversary. Christian Couder is leading the effort, working with Jakub, Markus, and Kaartic to prepare the newsletter for its May 2, 2025 publication. The community is invited to contribute through GitHub (via pull requests or comments on issue 767) or by email, with contributions ranging from proofreading to content suggestions. 

So far, Lucas Seiki Oshiro has confirmed the draft's coverage of their Haskell-based Git reimplementation as a notable community project. Markus Jansen has also contributed, with Christian approving minor corrections and merging Bruno's suggested changes. The thread remains focused on administrative coordination, with no technical changes to Git itself under discussion. The team continues to refine the draft ahead of the anniversary edition's release.
