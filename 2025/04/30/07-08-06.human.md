This is a discussion email in the ongoing series to replace `git gc` with granular maintenance tasks. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Derrick Stolee's question about whether the new worktree-prune and rerere-gc tasks should be added to the default maintenance schedule.

Steinhardt agrees they should be included, proposing to modify `git maintenance run --auto` to use the fine-grained tasks instead of `git gc` by default. He suggests implementing this through high-level "strategies" that would apply to both scheduled and manual maintenance runs. The current "incremental" strategy could be complemented with others, including one matching `git gc`'s behavior and potentially a "geometric" strategy.

The email looks forward to future possibilities like auto-selecting strategies based on repository size, though acknowledges this would be a later enhancement. The immediate focus remains on completing the technical groundwork to fully replace `git gc` with the new task-based approach while maintaining equivalent functionality.
