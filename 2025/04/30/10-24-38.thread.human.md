This thread continues the discussion about adding a `--no-maintenance` flag to Scalar's repository setup commands (`scalar register` and `scalar clone`). The core idea - allowing users to opt out of maintenance during setup to avoid lock conflicts - remains uncontested, but the implementation details are being carefully reviewed.

Junio Hamano has raised architectural concerns about where maintenance control should live, arguing it belongs at the command level rather than being bundled into the directory registration function. Meanwhile, Patrick Steinhardt has identified a more immediate issue: the current implementation actually deregisters existing maintenance when `--no-maintenance` is used, contrary to the documented behavior of merely skipping new maintenance setup. He also notes the tests don't verify this preservation of existing configurations.

The discussion now has two parallel tracks: Steinhardt's specific feedback about the maintenance deregistration behavior (which appears to be a clear bug needing fixing) and Junio's broader design critique about proper separation of concerns in the code. Derrick Stolee is expected to send a v2 addressing both the implementation issues and the architectural questions, though the latter may require more substantive reworking of how Scalar handles maintenance configuration.
