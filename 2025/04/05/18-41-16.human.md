This is a revised GSoC proposal for refactoring Git's global state, focusing on the systematic removal of `the_repository` and other globals. Arnav Bhate presents an updated plan that reflects feedback from mentor Patrick Steinhardt, showing a more iterative approach compared to the initial ambitious three-phase proposal.

The proposal now focuses first on environment.c's globals (~3500 `the_repository` occurrences remain long-term) before potentially expanding to repository.h components. Bhate demonstrates growing familiarity with the codebase through several merged and pending patches, including sign comparison fixes and unused variable removal. The timeline shows a methodical approach: 7 weeks for config-related variables (core and others), 3 weeks for non-config globals, with explicit time for mentor review and bug fixing.

Notable improvements from v1 include more concrete time estimates, acknowledgment of the need for incremental delivery matching Git's development norms, and clearer separation between config vs non-config variables. The student commits to 60hr/wk during summer with 20hr/wk post-GSoC follow-through, showing awareness of the project's long-term nature. The proposal still leaves some design questions open, particularly around how granular the planning artifacts should be, but demonstrates responsive iteration on mentor feedback.
