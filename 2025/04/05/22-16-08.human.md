This is part 2/8 of Elijah Newren's series to fully remove the merge-recursive backend in favor of merge-ort. The patch converts builtin/merge-recursive.c to use merge_ort_generic() instead of merge_recursive_generic(), marking another step in the transition away from the old implementation.

The changes are straightforward - swapping the include and function call - but the bulk of the patch updates test expectations that differ between the two backends. The commit message explains three categories of test changes: improved directory/file conflict handling in t6430 (which now shows both versions of conflicted files), better rename/delete conflict resolution in t6434 (now showing two index entries per conflict), and removal of merge-recursive's special "Already up to date" optimization in t6424 (which merge-ort handles differently by checking tree matches more thoroughly).

The patch appears well-constructed, with test updates that match the behavioral differences documented in prior merge-ort commits. As part of the larger removal effort, this is a mechanical but important step that eliminates one of the last direct users of merge-recursive infrastructure. The changes are consistent with the series' goal of making merge-ort the sole merge backend.
