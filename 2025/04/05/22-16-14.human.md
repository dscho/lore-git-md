This is the final patch in Elijah Newren's series to fully remove the legacy merge-recursive implementation, completing the transition to merge-ort as Git's sole merge backend. 

The patch removes the GIT_TEST_MERGE_ALGORITHM environment variable and related test scaffolding from three commands (merge, rebase, revert) that previously used it. This test infrastructure was originally created to allow easy switching between the old 'recursive' and new 'ort' backends during testing, but is now obsolete since 'recursive' is simply an alias for 'ort' and the old implementation is gone.

The changes are straightforward mechanical removals of environment variable checks and fallback logic, with no functional impact on normal operation. This cleanup affects the merge, rebase, and revert commands, removing about 20 lines of now-unnecessary code.

As the last patch in the series, this represents the final step in a multi-year effort to modernize Git's merge architecture. The changes are well-contained and appear to be a logical conclusion to the earlier patches that removed all other merge-recursive infrastructure. With this removal, the codebase is fully transitioned to merge-ort with no remaining vestiges of the old implementation.
