This is the v2 cover letter for a major refactoring series that completes the removal of the legacy merge-recursive implementation in favor of merge-ort. The series represents the culmination of a multi-year effort to modernize Git's merge machinery.

The changes since v1 are minor - just a couple of typo fixes in commit messages. The core work remains the same: after preparing the codebase by switching all remaining callers to merge-ort (patches 1-5), the series deletes merge-recursive.[ch] (patch 6) and cleans up related test infrastructure (patches 7-8). The diffstat shows significant deletions (over 5000 lines removed) but most of this is straightforward removal of now-unused code.

The series appears well-prepared and thorough, with all merge functionality now handled by merge-ort (including recently added diff-algorithm selection support). This represents a major architectural cleanup that has been in progress since merge-ort became the default in Git 2.33.0. The changes are mechanical in nature with no functional impact, just removing obsolete code paths. Given the extensive prior testing and gradual migration, this final removal seems low-risk and ready for merging.
