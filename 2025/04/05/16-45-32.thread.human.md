Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed it positively, calling it "obviously good" - confirming this is now a well-established, non-controversial type of change in the project. Both patches are straightforward deletions of unused declarations with no behavioral impact, representing clean mechanical progress in the larger effort to eliminate globals.
