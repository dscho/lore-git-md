Community announcement for Git's 20th anniversary celebration organized by Johannes Schindelin (Dscho). Event will take place on April 7, 2025 (date of Git's first commit) via Discord server in dedicated #20th-anniversary channel. Format is asynchronous text-based sharing of Git-related experiences (funny, interesting, or scary stories) to accommodate global time zones. Participation details: Discord invite link (https://discord.gg/UcjvsNQR) with instructions for new members to self-identify in #roles channel. Purely social/community event with no technical changes or patches involved. Anti-spam measures implemented.
