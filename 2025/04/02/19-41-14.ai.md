This is a community announcement about Git's upcoming 20th anniversary celebration. Johannes Schindelin is organizing an informal gathering on Git's Discord server to mark the occasion on April 7th, 2025 - the date of Git's first commit. The event will take place in a dedicated `#20th-anniversary` channel where participants can share memorable Git stories.

The announcement provides all necessary details for participation: the Discord invite link (https://discord.gg/UcjvsNQR) and instructions about the required self-identification in the `#roles` channel for new Discord members. The tone is celebratory and inclusive, encouraging both users and contributors to share their experiences - whether funny, interesting, or even "scary" Git-related stories.
