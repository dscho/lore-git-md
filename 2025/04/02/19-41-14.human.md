Community announcement for Git's 20th anniversary celebration.

Johannes Schindelin invites the Git community to celebrate Git's 20th anniversary on April 7th, 2025, marking two decades since the first collaborative development commit. He's created a dedicated `#20th-anniversary` channel on Git's Discord server where users and contributors can share their most memorable Git stories - whether funny, interesting, or cautionary tales.

The invitation is open to all, though new Discord participants will need to self-identify in the `#roles` channel first as an anti-spam measure. This appears to be a lighthearted community-building effort rather than a technical discussion, with the goal of collecting and sharing personal experiences from Git's long evolution. The timing aligns precisely with the historical record of Git's first commit date.
