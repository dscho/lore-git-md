Git will celebrate its 20th anniversary on April 7th, 2025, marking two decades since Linus Torvalds' first commit. Johannes Schindelin is organizing an informal online celebration on Git's Discord server to commemorate this milestone. 

The event will take place in a dedicated #20th-anniversary channel where community members can share their memorable Git stories - whether funny, interesting, or even cautionary tales. New participants need to join via the provided invite link (https://discord.gg/UcjvsNQR) and self-identify in the #roles channel first. The text-based, asynchronous format makes the celebration accessible across time zones while maintaining appropriate anti-spam measures.

This is purely a community engagement initiative with no technical changes involved. The timing precisely matches the historical anniversary date, and the event aims to bring together both long-time contributors and everyday users to reflect on Git's journey.
