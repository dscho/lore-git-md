This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to eliminate Perl dependencies from Git's test suite (now at 20 patches). The exchange focuses on patch 06/20 which introduced the PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisite.

Johannes Schindelin points out two additional Perl-using components that weren't mentioned in the original patch: `git request-pull` (which uses Perl to parse `ls-remote` output) and `git filter-branch` (when using `--state-branch`). Patrick acknowledges these omissions and considers two approaches: either properly marking these tools as Perl-dependent in the build system, or converting them away from Perl entirely since they appear to be "low-hanging fruit" for removal.

The discussion also confirms the technical correctness of patch 06/20, with Schindelin verifying through manual inspection that it properly handles the PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisite additions across test files. The patch follows the expected pattern of either adding the stand-alone prerequisite or the test preamble for Perl-dependent tests.

This exchange highlights the incremental nature of the Perl removal effort, where even while working on test suite conversions, new opportunities for Perl removal in other areas are being identified. The tone remains constructive, with Patrick receptive to the additional information while maintaining focus on the broader goal of complete Perl removal where feasible.
