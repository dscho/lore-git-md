This is a technical discussion in the ongoing series enhancing Git's `cat-file` batch mode filtering. The specific exchange focuses on type safety in callback parameters.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Toon Claes' question about why the `_payload` parameter in `batch_each_object()` is typeless (void pointer). Patrick explains this design choice is necessary because the payload gets forwarded to different callbacks that may accept different types - in this case, it's used with both `struct object_cb_data` and `struct oid_array` pointers.

This is one of the two remaining technical points in the series (along with pointer comparison robustness) that reviewers have flagged. The discussion shows Patrick defending the current implementation's approach to handling polymorphic callback data, which maintains flexibility at the cost of some type safety. The thread context indicates this architectural concern from Toon Claes remains unresolved, though all style-related feedback has been addressed in prior rounds.
