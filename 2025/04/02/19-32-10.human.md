This is a review response in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to eliminate Perl dependencies from Git's test suite (now at 20 patches). Johannes Schindelin completes his review of the series, giving it a positive assessment and noting its good timing. He highlights that the work enables practical benefits for Git for Windows, where Perl is a significant dependency primarily used for `git svn` and could potentially be removed from minimal installations, saving at least 10MB. Schindelin also suggests the possibility of replacing `prove` with a `test-tool` command in Windows CI jobs to further reduce Perl dependencies. The review indicates the series is technically sound and ready for maintainer consideration, with all major concerns addressed.
