This is a brief clarification about the `change-id` standardization discussion from the maintainer of the `b4` patch management tool. Konstantin Ryabitsev notes that while `b4` also uses change IDs, its implementation differs significantly from the tools under discussion (Gerrit, GitButler, Jujutsu) in both purpose and format. 

The key distinction is that `b4`'s change IDs are series-level identifiers that remain in patch footers rather than being embedded in commits, and they use a free-form string format rather than the structured encoding proposed for commit-level IDs. This serves as a useful boundary marker for the standardization effort, indicating that some tools in the ecosystem may have legitimate reasons to maintain different change ID semantics despite the broader unification push.
