This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable API series (patch 10/16). The change simplifies the block iterator implementation by storing a pointer to the entire `reftable_block` structure rather than copying individual fields (`block`, `block_len`, `hash_size`) into the iterator.

The key technical changes are:
- Removes redundant field copies in `struct block_iter`
- Now stores single `const struct reftable_block *block` pointer
- Affects `reftable/block.[ch]` with 17 lines removed vs 9 added
- Prepares for future lifetime decoupling of iterators from seeks

Justin Tobler's review confirms the simplification is an improvement, noting "This is much simpler. Nice!" while also catching a minor typo in" vs "into" in the commit message. The change appears technically sound and aligns with the series' goals of API cleanup and consistency. This continues the positive review trend for this mature series, with feedback focused on minor refinements rather than fundamental issues.
