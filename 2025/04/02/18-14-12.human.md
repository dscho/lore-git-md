This is a GSoC (Google Summer of Code) proposal targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, with broader goals of reducing Git's global state. The student, Arnav Bhate, proposes to systematically move global variables (starting with `the_repository`) into repository-specific structs to enable better multi-repository handling and improve code maintainability.

The proposal comes from a second-year IIT Bombay student with some initial Git contributions (one merged patch fixing sign comparison warnings, two others under review). The plan shows a clear understanding of the problem space, breaking the work into phases: identifying global variables, determining their ideal locations in repository structs, and refactoring callers. The timeline allocates the summer break for intensive coding, tapering to part-time when classes resume.

While the scope is ambitious (290 files reference `the_repository` alone), the incremental approach — grouping related variables into logical structs and tackling them in patch series — seems practical. The proposal would benefit from more concrete examples of non-`the_repository` globals to be migrated, but demonstrates enough technical grasp to suggest the student could make meaningful progress with mentor guidance. This aligns well with Git's ongoing architectural efforts.
