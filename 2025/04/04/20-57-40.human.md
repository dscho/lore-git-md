This is a follow-up discussion on a bugfix for the packed-refs backend's handling of UTF-8 characters in refnames. Jeff King (peff) reviews Patrick Steinhardt's patch that fixes a BUG() crash when comparing Unicode refnames (like emoji-containing branch names) against ASCII refnames.

The key points from peff's review:
1. Confirms the signed/unsigned character comparison issue is a real problem, noting this matches strcmp()'s unsigned behavior by design
2. Approves the technical solution of casting to unsigned char in `packed_ref_iterator_advance()`
3. Raises two minor considerations:
   - The test case's Unicode character () might cause filesystem issues on some platforms
   - The test's use of command substitution without checking rev-parse's exit code, while not critical, might draw scrutiny from strict exit-code reviewers

The review is positive, with peff thanking both the author and Elijah Newren (who originally reported the issue) for their quick work. The discussion appears to be wrapping up with no major objections to the fix.
