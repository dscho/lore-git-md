Bugfix for remote HEAD tracking in git fetch. This is a follow-up to Jeff King's earlier series that fixed cases where `git fetch` was too aggressive about updating refs/remotes/<remote>/HEAD. The original changes were based on discussion with Junio Hamano and Taylor Blau, and now Jeff is proposing one final readability improvement before merging.

The patch simplifies the control flow around the `set_head()` call in builtin/fetch.c, making the error-handling behavior more obvious. While functionally equivalent to the current code, the new version uses a clearer if-block structure rather than a short-circuit AND expression. Jeff notes this addresses Taylor's earlier readability concern.

The change appears straightforward and low-risk, just reorganizing existing logic for better maintainability. Since the core behavior was already reviewed and approved, this final polish seems ready to go in. Jeff suggests it could either be squashed into the first patch or applied on top, and is waiting for confirmation from Bence Szakács (the original author of the followRemoteHEAD feature) before proceeding.
