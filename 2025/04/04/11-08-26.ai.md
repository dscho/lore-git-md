Feature refinement discussion for git-refs consolidation project. Karthik Nayak responds to Zheng Yuting's analysis of implementation approaches for the `list` subcommand in the proposed `git-refs` command. The discussion focuses on whether to combine filtering/formatting functionality (Approach 1) or split them into separate `filter`/`show` subcommands (Approach 2).

Karthik provides several technical insights: he suggests `--sort` belongs in a separate category from filtering, questions whether certain legacy formatting options (like `--tcl`) are still needed, and shares benchmark data showing format choice significantly impacts performance between `git-for-each-ref` and `git-show-ref`. He expresses preference for Approach 1 (unified command) but acknowledges its complexity, suggesting starting with a simpler `git refs show` implementation first.

The email maintains alignment with Patrick Steinhardt's established constraints (backward compatibility, `refs.c`-only changes) while probing deeper into UX and performance considerations. Key open questions remain about command structure and whether to prioritize incremental implementation versus comprehensive design. The discussion continues to shape the technical direction of this GSoC project's ref-related functionality consolidation.
