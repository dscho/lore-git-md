This is the sixth patch in Taylor Blau's series optimizing cruft pack and MIDX interactions during repacking. It introduces a new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for `git pack-objects` that ensures reachability closure by including objects from unspecified packs when they're referenced by objects in included packs.

The key addition is a "follow" mode that walks object graphs beyond just the explicitly listed packs, which solves a problem with cruft packs and MIDX files. Currently, if an object in a cruft pack becomes reachable through a new pack, we must include the cruft pack in the MIDX to maintain bitmap correctness. The new mode allows generating packs that are reachability-closed without forcing cruft packs into the MIDX.

The implementation adds a new `stdin_packs_mode` enum and modifies the pack-hint callbacks to either just hint (original behavior) or fully include objects (new "follow" mode). The patch includes thorough tests verifying that:
1. Regular `--stdin-packs` excludes objects from unknown packs
2. `--stdin-packs=follow` includes reachable objects from unknown packs
3. The behavior works correctly with `--unpacked` to include loose objects
