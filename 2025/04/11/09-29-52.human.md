Refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's object-file reorganization series. This is part 3 of 9 in v2 of the series to better organize Git's object-file subsystem.

The patch moves the `xmmap()` family of functions from object-file.c to wrapper.c, where they more logically belong since they're general utility functions rather than object-file specific. The functions include:
- `xmmap()` and `xmmap_gently()` for memory mapping with error handling
- `mmap_os_err()` for platform-specific error messages
- `mmap_limit_check()` for size validation

The change is a straightforward code movement with no functional changes - all 48 lines are deleted from object-file.c and identically recreated in wrapper.c. This continues the series' goal of better subsystem separation by grouping related functionality together.
