Taylor Blau has completed his RFC series on optimizing how cruft packs interact with multi-pack indexes (MIDX) during repacking. The eight-part series introduces a way to exclude cruft packs from MIDX files when possible, reducing their size and improving lookup performance, while maintaining correctness through careful reachability tracking.

The final patch implements the core optimization teased in the preparatory work. It builds on the `--stdin-packs=follow` mode introduced earlier, which ensures geometrically-repacked packs contain copies of any objects that become referenced. This allows safely omitting cruft packs from MIDX when no existing MIDX already includes them. The implementation adds checks for MIDX presence and modifies repack command construction to handle both cases where cruft packs can be excluded and where they must be included for correctness.

The series progresses methodically from refactoring through to the final optimization. Early patches standardized option handling and improved delta compression, while later ones added the tracking infrastructure needed for the final change. The solution addresses problems noted in commit ddee3703b3 regarding MIDX bloat from rarely-accessed cruft objects. Comprehensive tests verify both the exclusion and required-inclusion scenarios.
