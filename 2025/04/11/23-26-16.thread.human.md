This thread focuses on Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking, now at version 6 with 9 patches. The series introduces configurable optimizations for pack maintenance, particularly around cruft pack inclusion in MIDX files, with significant performance improvements (5-20%) observed in production.

The core technical work involves refactoring pack-objects behavior, standardizing option handling, and optimizing loose object delta compression. A key addition is the new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option. The series has undergone thorough review, with Junio Hamano approving several patches (1, 3-5) and providing minor style feedback, while Elijah Newren contributed detailed technical review catching small issues like comment typos.

A recent discussion emerged around incremental MIDX handling after Jeff King discovered a test failure under specific conditions. Blau fixed the issue by implementing proper nested traversal of MIDX chains, but the conversation revealed deeper questions about whether the incremental MIDX concept should be abstracted at the API level. While Blau has addressed the immediate bug, he's chosen not to add specific incremental MIDX tests to maintain test focus, instead relying on existing build detection.

The series appears ready for merging with all known issues resolved and approvals from key reviewers. The remaining open question about MIDX API design may warrant future work, but doesn't block the current optimization effort. This represents a significant step in Git's ongoing pack maintenance improvements, particularly for large repositories.
