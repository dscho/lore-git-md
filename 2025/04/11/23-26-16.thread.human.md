Taylor Blau's patch series continues to refine the performance optimization for MIDX and cruft pack handling during repacking, now at version 6 with all 9 patches. The series introduces configurable pack maintenance improvements, particularly around how cruft packs interact with MIDX files, with measurable 5-20% speed improvements seen in GitHub's production environment.

The latest patch (4/9) moves reachability traversal code in pack-objects to enable better logic sharing between different operations, a key step for the upcoming `--stdin-packs=follow` mode. Junio Hamano reviewed and approved this refactoring, though he noted some initial confusion about a function declaration that had moved from an earlier patch. This change builds on previous patches that standardized option handling and improved scope management, with Elijah Newren having caught minor issues like a comment typo in patch 6/9.

The series has received thorough review from core contributors - Junio approved the key refactoring in patch 3/9 and praised the optimization in patch 5/9, while Jeff King identified a test failure under specific MIDX conditions that was subsequently fixed. With all known issues addressed and approvals from both Junio and Elijah Newren, the series appears ready for merging, culminating in patch 9/9 which adds the `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option for fine-grained control over cruft pack handling.
