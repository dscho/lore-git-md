Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction has reached its final form with Elijah Newren's approval, introducing configurable control over how cruft packs are included in MIDX during repacking. The 9-part series includes several technical refinements, from standardizing option handling to improving delta compression, with Junio Hamano praising key elements like the "clever" optimization in patch 5/9. 

Just as the series appeared ready for merging, Carlo Arenas flagged a portability issue in the test script for the final patch - the use of non-POSIX `grep -o` that would break on platforms like AIX. He suggested a `sed` alternative that maintains the same test coverage while working across all supported systems. While this doesn't affect the core functionality (the new repack.midxMustContainCruft config remains sound), it's the sort of last-minute platform consideration that often surfaces in Git's cross-platform environment. The series maintainers will need to decide whether to address this with a follow-up fix or during integration.
