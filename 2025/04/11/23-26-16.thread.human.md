Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking has reached its final stages. The 9-part series introduces configurable optimizations for pack maintenance, particularly around how cruft packs interact with MIDX during repacking. The series had received positive reviews from both Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren, with Junio praising the clever loose object delta compression optimization and approving key refactoring work.

A last-minute issue was discovered by Jeff King where cruft packs could incorrectly appear in the MIDX under specific test conditions involving incremental MIDX writing. Taylor has now provided a fix that properly accounts for multi-layered MIDX structures when determining which packs should be excluded. The solution carefully traverses all MIDX layers (including base_midx chains) when collecting pack names, ensuring correct behavior while maintaining the series' optimization goals.

With this fix addressing the final known issue, along with plans to incorporate Junio's earlier style suggestion, the series appears ready for merging. Production validation has shown 5-20% speed improvements from these optimizations, which represent an important step in Taylor's broader work on MIDX and geometric repacking improvements.
