This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's now-merged series refactoring Git's object-file subsystem. The patch moves `safe_create_leading_directories()` and related directory-creation utilities from `object-file.c` to `dir.c`, where they better belong since they provide general filesystem functionality rather than object-specific operations.

The change is straightforward - the functions are copied verbatim to their new location while updating all callers to include the correct header. The commit message clearly explains the motivation: these utilities are generic and don't logically belong in the object-file subsystem. This is part of a larger effort to better organize Git's codebase by moving functionality to more appropriate locations.

The patch affects 13 files with 150 lines added and 148 removed, mostly header adjustments. Since this is part of a completed series that has already been merged, we know this change was ultimately successful and uncontroversial. It establishes better code organization by grouping directory-related utilities together in `dir.c`.
