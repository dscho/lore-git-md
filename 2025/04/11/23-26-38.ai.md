This is the seventh patch in Taylor Blau's series optimizing cruft pack and MIDX interaction during repacking. The patch adds infrastructure to track which packs are currently referenced by the MIDX, which will be used in the next patch to conditionally generate packs during geometric repacking.

The technical changes introduce a new `PACK_IN_MIDX` flag bit in `builtin/repack.c` and modify the pack collection logic to mark packs that appear in the MIDX. Key modifications include:
- Adding the `PACK_IN_MIDX` flag constant alongside existing `DELETE_PACK` and `RETAIN_PACK` flags
- Introducing `pack_mark_in_midx()` helper function to set the flag
- Updating `collect_pack_filenames()` to track MIDX status when categorizing packs into kept/cruft/non-kept lists

This is a preparatory change that builds on the earlier refactoring and `--stdin-packs=follow` introduction, now adding the tracking needed for the final patch's MIDX-aware repacking logic. The patch maintains the series' pattern of small, focused changes with clear progression toward the overall goal of optimizing pack maintenance.
