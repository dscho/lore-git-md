This is a follow-up in the resolved thread about `safe.directory` wildcard behavior discrepancies. Nikolaus Rath confirms Jeff King's explanation about why `safe.directory = *` works for single-process operations like `git status` but fails during multi-process operations like `git clone`. The email serves as a confirmation that the security model explanation makes sense in practice, with Nikolaus verifying that their real-world scenario matches the technical details Jeff provided earlier in the thread.
