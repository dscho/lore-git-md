The email provides concrete examples of how the current workarounds (unsetting `core.hooksPath` or using command-specific `--no-verify`) are cumbersome and error-prone. Knoble suggests this feature could also help advocate for moving important checks to server-side hooks rather than mandatory client-side ones. While acknowledging the patch is technically sound (following the pattern of `--no-advice`), the discussion remains open about whether this is the right solution versus fixing slow hooks directly.
