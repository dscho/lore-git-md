This is a platform compatibility patch in the ongoing build system alignment series. It adds `clock_gettime()` support to Cygwin builds in the Makefile configuration, matching what's already available in autoconf and meson builds. The change is straightforward - it simply adds two `HAVE_` flags for `clock_gettime` and `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` to Cygwin's section of `config.mak.uname`.

The patch is part of a larger effort (now at v2 with 13 patches) to align build system behavior across different platforms and build methods. This particular change follows a similar pattern seen earlier in the series (like patch 8/13 which added `HAVE_GETDELIM` for Cygwin), where platform capabilities that were already detected by autoconf/meson are now explicitly enabled in the Makefile configuration.

The change appears uncontroversial and well-justified, as Cygwin has supported these timing functions for some time. It's a small but meaningful step toward build system consistency across platforms. The patch is clearly focused and follows the established pattern of the series.
