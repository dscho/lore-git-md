Build system alignment patch removing unnecessary compiler flag in meson build. This is the first of 13 patches in v2 of a series focused on Makefile/meson consistency and Cygwin-specific fixes.

The patch removes `-DCURL_DISABLE_TYPECHECK` from meson's `libgit_c_args` since this flag was only needed for sparse builds to suppress "using sizeof on a function" warnings in curl-related files (http-push.c, http.c, http-walker.c, remote-curl.c). The meson build doesn't support sparse targets, making this flag unnecessary. The change re-enables curl's type checking in the meson build while maintaining identical behavior to the Makefile build where the flag remains for sparse builds.

Technical context shows this was originally added in commit 9371322a60 to handle sparse-specific warnings. The removal aligns with the series' goal of reducing discrepancies between build systems while maintaining functionality. The change is straightforward with no expected impact on non-sparse builds, and follows the pattern established in the Makefile build where the flag is target-specific.
