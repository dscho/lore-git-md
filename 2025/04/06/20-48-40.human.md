The discussion highlights how the new approach maintains correctness while improving performance: it prevents duplicate refs in bundle headers without needing to compare objects, handling both identical refnames and exclusion patterns (like `master^!`). Test cases that previously exposed bugs now pass, and additional edge case testing by Claes found no issues.
