This thread reports a bug where `git rebase --rebase-merges` can write corrupted binary data to reflog action fields instead of the expected text (like "rebase (continue)"). The issue appears intermittently in complex merge scenarios, though the reporter couldn't reproduce it with a simple test case. A bisect points to commit d188a60d722 ("sequencer: stop exporting GIT_REFLOG_ACTION") as the likely culprit. The corruption occurs in the sequencer's merge handling code, specifically during operations that would normally write human-readable actions to the reflog. While the exact conditions triggering the bug remain unclear, the detailed backtrace and real-world reproduction scenario provide a solid starting point for investigation.
