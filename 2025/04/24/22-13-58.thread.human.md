This thread began as an effort to make Git's CI more resilient to outages of external services (JGit, Perforce, Git-LFS) by improving error handling during dependency downloads. The core approach of treating downloads as atomic operations with proper cleanup has been agreed upon, with two backport-targeted patches already in progress. Maintainers Junio Hamano and Johannes Schindelen have been discussing finer points of warning presentation - whether to show platform-specific markers during downloads or keep them at the final check stage.

A new dimension has been introduced by Jialuo, who suggests using GitHub Actions' caching mechanism for these dependencies as an alternative to repeated downloads. This could provide more resilience against service outages by reducing dependency on external availability. While this doesn't directly address the immediate outage response that prompted the original patches, it opens up an interesting avenue for future CI infrastructure improvements. The suggestion is exploratory, as Jialuo acknowledges unfamiliarity with the CI implementation details, but it adds a longer-term perspective to the discussion beyond the current error-handling focus.

The thread currently has two parallel tracks: the near-term work on robust download handling (where consensus exists on the approach but details are being refined), and this new caching idea that could inform future work. Junio's position remains that the current warning timing should stay at the final check, while Johannes continues to advocate for more prominent CI warnings during the download phase.
