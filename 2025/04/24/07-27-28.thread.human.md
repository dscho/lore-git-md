This means the original workaround wouldn't help their core use case.

The situation now has two layers: Git's internal HTTP handling (potentially fixable via configuration) and the standalone curl utility (currently without HTTP/2 support in recent Windows builds). The impact remains significant for corporate environments where alternative curl installations aren't permitted.
