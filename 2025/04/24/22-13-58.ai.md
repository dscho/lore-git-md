This is a CI/build system patch addressing an immediate outage affecting Git's Linux CI jobs. The patch modifies the dependency installation script to handle failures when downloading JGit from repo.eclipse.org, which is currently experiencing an extended outage.

The key change makes the JGit download and permission-setting operations continue on failure, with a warning message, rather than causing the entire CI job to fail. This matches the existing pattern where the script later checks for JGit's presence and continues with a warning if it's missing. Junio Hamano notes the irony that the current "set -e" behavior causes early failure before reaching that graceful handling code.

The patch is proposed for immediate backporting from 'maint' through 'master' to the development branches to unblock CI. Junio solicits input from those more familiar with JGit's status, while also floating the idea of removing "set -e" from ci/lib.sh as a future maintainability improvement. The change is minimal (3 lines added, 2 changed) and focused specifically on the JGit download failure case.
