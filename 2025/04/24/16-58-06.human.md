This is a documentation follow-up in the ongoing `git send-email` OAuth2 support thread. Junio Hamano responds to Aditya Garg's realization that the `send-email` documentation could better cross-reference Git's credential helper system as an alternative to direct OAuth2 token generation.

This wraps up one of the thread's loose ends, leaving just the OAuth2 implementation (patch 1) and Outlook Message-ID handling (patch 2) as the remaining technical components needing review before the series can proceed.
