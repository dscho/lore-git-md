**CI / build system**: This is a proposed hotfix for Git's CI system to handle an ongoing outage of repo.eclipse.org, which hosts JGit downloads needed for Linux CI jobs. 

Junio Hamano reports that the eclipse.org outage is causing all Linux CI jobs to fail when they attempt to download JGit early in the process. The proposed patch modifies `ci/install-dependencies.sh` to continue execution even if JGit download fails, rather than failing the entire CI run. The change adds error handling that echoes a warning but allows the script to proceed.

The email notes an interesting observation - while the script already had code to handle missing JGit later on (printing a warning), the early `set -e` behavior was causing the job to fail before reaching that point. Junio suggests removing `set -e` might be a good long-term maintainability improvement, but treats that as a separate discussion.

This appears to be a straightforward, targeted fix for an immediate CI problem. The patch is minimal (5 lines changed) and focused specifically on making CI resilient to the eclipse.org outage. Junio is seeking input from those more familiar with JGit before applying the change across Git's maintenance branches.
