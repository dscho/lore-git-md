Git 20th anniversary community interview initiative. The Git Rev News team is collecting responses from the community on 9 reflective questions about Git's evolution, usage, and future for a special anniversary edition. Responses include technical insights, historical reflections, and future predictions from contributors like Luca Milanesio, Lucas Seiki Oshiro, and Elijah Newren.

Key responses cover: (1) Luca's experience with a 2013 force-push disaster and wish for SHA-256 from inception, (2) Lucas's focus on code archaeology tools and CLI consistency suggestions, (3) Elijah's technical critique of Git's index design and concerns about scaling challenges. The project has transitioned from response collection to editorial review, with a draft now available on GitHub for contributor review before final publication. Kaartic Sivaraam confirmed sharing interview questions with local communities is acceptable, noting interesting new responses could be included in future editions. The administrative update ensures all contributors are properly included in the thread while maintaining focus on the celebratory nature of the anniversary initiative.
