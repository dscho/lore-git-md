Git 20th anniversary community interview initiative. The Git Rev News team is organizing a collaborative interview project to mark Git's 20th anniversary, collecting responses from the community on 9 reflective questions about Git's evolution, usage, and future. Questions range from practical tips ("favorite Git trick") to historical reflections ("worst Git disaster") and future predictions ("version control in 20 years"). Responses will be collected until approximately April 25th for inclusion in a special anniversary edition of Git Rev News. The effort is designed to be inclusive and generate diverse perspectives from both long-time contributors and users.
