This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction. It's the third of eight patches in v2 of the series.

The patch makes two small but clear improvements to `add_object_entry_from_pack()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`: it moves the `revs` variable closer to where it's actually used, and switches to using a designated initializer for the `object_info` struct. Both changes follow good coding practices by reducing variable scope and making initialization more explicit.

While minor, these changes are part of the preparatory work for the larger series that will introduce configurable optimization of pack maintenance. The patch is clean and focused, with a clear explanation of each change in the commit message. It builds on the documentation improvements made in patch 2/8 while setting up for the more substantial changes to come later in the series.
