This thread documents the final refinements to a 5-patch series that enhances Git's promisor-remote protocol with configurable validation of remote attributes during clone and fetch operations. The series, now at version 4, has been through extensive review and addresses both server-side advertisement and client-side verification of fields like partialCloneFilter and token while maintaining backward compatibility.

The latest patch implements a suggestion from Junio Hamano to replace a custom linked list with Git's standard `string_list` structure for managing promisor remote information. Christian Couder made this change, renaming `remote_nick_find()` to the clearer `promisor_info_find()` while maintaining the list's sorting behavior where beneficial. This follows through on earlier design feedback to use more familiar collection types rather than custom implementations, improving code maintainability without altering functionality.

All major design points are now settled, with Justin Tobler, Patrick Steinhardt, and Christian Couder having reviewed and approved the implementation approach. The series includes comprehensive test coverage and documentation, with all reviewer feedback addressed. With no remaining open questions, this appears ready for merging into Git's promisor-remote infrastructure.
