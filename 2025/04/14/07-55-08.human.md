This is a platform compatibility fix in the ongoing build system alignment series, specifically addressing RAM calculation for Cygwin's `sysinfo()` implementation.

Patrick Steinhardt confirms Ramsay Jones's findings about Cygwin's `sysinfo()` behavior where memory sizes are reported in multiples of `mem_unit` (4096 bytes) rather than bytes directly. The patch modifies `total_ram()` in `builtin/gc.c` to properly handle this case by multiplying `totalram` by `mem_unit` when needed, while maintaining compatibility with Linux systems where `mem_unit` is 1.

The change is straightforward and well-justified by the platform differences observed. Patrick notes that while the guard clause checking `mem_unit > 1` might be technically unnecessary (since all systems should have a proper value), it's harmless to keep. This patch represents a small but important piece of the larger effort to ensure consistent behavior across platforms in the build system series.
