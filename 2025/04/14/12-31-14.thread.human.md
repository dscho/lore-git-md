This thread documents the Git Rev News team's community interview project celebrating Git's 20th anniversary. The initiative collected reflections from contributors on Git's evolution, usage patterns, and future direction through nine thought-provoking questions. 

Several notable responses have been compiled: Luca Milanesio shared a force-push recovery story and advocated for SHA-256 adoption from Git's inception. Lucas Seiki Oshiro highlighted Git's archaeology tools while critiquing CLI inconsistencies. Elijah Newren provided particularly insightful technical analysis, praising `git range-diff` while critiquing the index design and raising concerns about Git's long-term scalability.

The project has now transitioned from response collection to editorial review. Kaartic Sivaraam shared the compiled draft on GitHub for final review, ensuring contributors can verify their responses before publication. The team also clarified that sharing interview questions with local communities is encouraged, potentially yielding additional perspectives for future editions. This administrative update maintains the project's collaborative spirit while handling the practical aspects of preparing this special anniversary feature.
