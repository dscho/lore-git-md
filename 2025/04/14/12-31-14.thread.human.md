The Git Rev News team is organizing a special community interview to celebrate Git's 20th anniversary. They've prepared nine thoughtful questions that span Git's history, practical usage, and future possibilities - from favorite Git tricks to worst disasters and predictions about version control in 2044. 

This inclusive initiative invites responses from across the Git community until April 25th, with plans to compile the submissions into a commemorative edition of Git Rev News. The questions are designed to elicit both nostalgic reflections and forward-looking insights, making this a meaningful way for the diverse Git user and contributor base to participate in the anniversary celebration. The team's approach suggests they aim to capture the full spectrum of perspectives that have shaped Git's evolution over two decades.
