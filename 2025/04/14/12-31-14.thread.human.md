The Git Rev News team is gathering community reflections for Git's 20th anniversary through a set of nine interview questions, with responses due by April 25th. The initiative aims to capture diverse perspectives on Git's history, usage patterns, and future direction, ranging from personal anecdotes to technical insights.

Luca Milanesio has contributed responses to two questions, sharing specific experiences. He references a 2013 force-push incident where Gerrit's replication plugin proved crucial for disaster recovery, and expresses a wish that Git had adopted SHA-256 hashing from the beginning rather than undertaking the current migration path. His comments add concrete examples to the historical record, particularly around infrastructure resilience and technical debt. The SHA-256 observation may resonate with ongoing work in the project to transition away from SHA-1. Other community members are encouraged to share their perspectives as the team compiles this anniversary edition.
