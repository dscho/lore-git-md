This is a refactoring patch for Git's Meson build system, specifically targeting the Coccinelle integration. 

The patch renames two variables (`sources` and `headers`) in the Coccinelle meson.build file to be more specific (`coccinelle_sources` and `coccinelle_headers`). The change prevents these variables from polluting the top-level Meson build namespace, where they could potentially cause naming collisions or be mistakenly used. 

The author also takes the opportunity to standardize the empty list notation to match other parts of the codebase. The changes are straightforward and mechanical, with no functional impact - just making the build system more maintainable. 

This appears to be part of a larger series (v3 1/4) about wiring up sparse checking via Meson, though the specific connection to sparse isn't visible in this individual patch. The changes look clean and well-justified, following the principle of keeping variable scopes as narrow as possible.
