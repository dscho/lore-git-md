Performance optimization for pack-objects with a new '--stdin-packs=follow' mode. This is part 7/8 of Taylor Blau's series improving MIDX and cruft pack interactions during repacking.

The patch introduces a new mode for `--stdin-packs` that makes pack-objects include objects reachable from the specified packs, even if those objects reside in other (unspecified) packs. This is particularly useful for handling cruft packs, where objects may become reachable again through new commits. The change ensures reachability bitmaps can be properly generated in such cases.

The implementation adds a new `STDIN_PACKS_MODE_FOLLOW` option that modifies the pack-objects traversal to include these reachable objects. The commit includes thorough test coverage demonstrating the behavior with various pack combinations, including cases with loose objects and excluded packs.

This builds on earlier preparatory work in the series (particularly the rev_info restructuring in patch 5/9) and addresses the MIDX/cruft pack interaction issues identified in commit ddee3703b3. The patch appears well-constructed with clear documentation and comprehensive tests.
