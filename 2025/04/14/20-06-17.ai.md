Refactoring patch in the pack-objects subsystem, part of a larger performance optimization series.

This is the third patch in Taylor Blau's v2 series (8 parts) about optimizing MIDX and cruft pack interactions during repacking. The change focuses on `add_object_entry_from_pack()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, which handles objects from identified packs during `--stdin-packs` operations.

The patch makes two localized improvements: moving the `revs` declaration later in the function where it's actually needed, and using a designated initializer for the `struct object_info` to clarify that only the `typep` field is being used. This follows the documentation work in patch 2/9 about `OBJECT_INFO_INIT` by showing a practical example of initializing the struct.

The changes are purely code organization - no behavior is modified. The patch maintains the existing functionality where commit objects from included packs become starting points for revision walks. This appears to be a preparatory cleanup as part of the larger series, with the actual performance optimizations coming in later patches. The change is small (4 lines removed, 2 added) and focused, with no apparent impact on functionality.
