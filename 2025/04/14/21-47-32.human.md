This is the final review and queueing of a documentation modernization series converting `git-reset`, `git-rm`, and `git-mv` man pages to the new AsciiDoc format. Junio C Hamano has reviewed version 2 of the 7-patch series and is queuing it for inclusion.

The series standardizes documentation formatting across these commands with consistent synopsis blocks, proper placeholder notation, and updated toolchain handling for special characters. Version 2 incorporates all reviewer feedback from Martin Ågren and specifically addresses the tricky handling of triple-dot notation in command syntax versus ellipses.

Junio notes the improvements are clearly good, though he jokes about needing extra care when reviewing the regex changes for triple-dot processing. The final patch adding character markup rules to CodingGuidelines is particularly important as it ensures future contributors follow the same conventions. 

With this review, the series is now complete and ready for merging, marking another step in Git's ongoing documentation standardization effort. The only remaining minor issue (TROFF rendering quirks for special characters in bold spans) appears to be a backend limitation rather than a problem with the patches themselves.
