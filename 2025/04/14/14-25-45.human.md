This is a technical refinement in the ongoing Meson build system conversion effort, specifically addressing header checking functionality. Karthik Nayak responds to Toon Claes' suggestions about improving the way generated headers are tracked in the Meson build configuration.

The discussion focuses on two proposed approaches for maintaining the list of generated headers (currently 'command-list.h', 'config-list.h', and 'hook-list.h'). Toon suggested either dynamically collecting headers from source lists (builtin_sources, libgit_sources, third_party_sources) or explicitly listing them near their creation points. Karthik agrees the explicit approach near generation points would be cleaner and help prevent maintenance oversights.

This exchange represents the final polishing of a feature that has already seen extensive review, with consensus reached on major architectural points. The patch series is nearing completion, having addressed feedback from multiple reviewers including Phillip Wood and Patrick Steinhardt.
