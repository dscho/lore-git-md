Performance test infrastructure patch in the ongoing Meson integration series, specifically addressing benchmark wiring.

This email from Patrick Steinhardt responds to Toon Claes' feedback about the minimal console output when running Meson benchmarks (patch 4/5 in the series). The key technical points are:

Patrick acknowledges Toon's suggestion about modifying `t/perf/run` to accept Meson build directories as input (rather than full source directories) as a potential future improvement, but considers this orthogonal to the current series.

The discussion continues to highlight the careful balance being struck between:
- Maintaining existing `t/perf/run` functionality for cross-version comparisons
- Providing usable Meson-native benchmarking
- Keeping the scope of the current series focused

This response helps resolve one of the open issues from the thread (minimal console output) while deferring the cross-version benchmarking integration question to future work.
