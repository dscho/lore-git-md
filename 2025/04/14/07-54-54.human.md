Patrick Steinhardt follows up on Ramsay Jones' discovery that meson's handling of DEFAULT_EDITOR and DEFAULT_PAGER values fails when they contain quotes or backslashes. The patch proposes only setting these build variables when they differ from the defaults ('vi' for editor, 'less' for pager), which is a sensible optimization but doesn't solve the underlying quoting issue.

The email reveals an important limitation in meson's path handling that affects real-world use cases where editors or pagers might have spaces or special characters in their paths. While the immediate patch is a straightforward improvement, the thread highlights a deeper technical challenge that remains unresolved in the build system alignment effort.
