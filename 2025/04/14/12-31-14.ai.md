Community engagement initiative for Git's 20th anniversary. The Git Rev News team is organizing a collaborative community interview to celebrate Git's 20th year. They've prepared a set of 9 reflective questions about Git's evolution, usage patterns, and future directions, inviting responses from the broader community.

The questions cover a range of topics from practical Git tips ("What's your favorite Git trick?") to historical reflections ("What was your worst Git disaster?") and forward-looking speculation ("boldest prediction about version control in 20 years"). The team plans to collect responses until around April 25th before curating them into a special anniversary edition of Git Rev News.

This appears to be a well-organized community engagement effort with thoughtfully crafted questions that should elicit interesting perspectives from long-time Git contributors and users. The questions are broad enough to allow for diverse responses while being specific enough to yield substantive answers. The Git Rev News team is clearly positioning this as an inclusive opportunity for community participation in the anniversary celebration.
