Community engagement initiative for Git's 20th anniversary.

The Git Rev News team is organizing a special community interview to celebrate Git's 20th anniversary. They're inviting responses from anyone in the community to a set of reflective questions about Git's evolution, personal experiences, and future predictions. The questions cover a wide range of topics from favorite Git tricks to historical design regrets and future visions for version control.

The team plans to collect responses until around April 25th before curating them into a special edition of Git Rev News. The questions appear well-crafted to elicit both practical insights and philosophical perspectives from long-time Git contributors and enthusiasts. This looks like a thoughtful way to mark the project's milestone while capturing institutional knowledge and community wisdom.

Participation is open to all, with responses to be shared directly in this thread. The initiative seems particularly valuable for newer contributors who might learn from veterans' experiences, as well as for documenting the project's history through personal narratives. The questions strike a good balance between technical depth and accessibility.
