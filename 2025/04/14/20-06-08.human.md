This is a feature patch series (v2) from Taylor Blau that optimizes how cruft packs interact with MIDX during repacking. The core idea is to avoid including cruft packs in the MIDX when possible, while maintaining correctness through reachability closure.

The series builds on the previous RFC version with several improvements:
- Added a new commit explaining designated initializers in object-store-ll.h
- Generalized the reasoning beyond just cruft packs (based on discussion with Jeff King)
- Introduced a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config knob to control the behavior
- Improved test readability and coverage

The key technical change is in the final patch, which implements logic to exclude cruft packs from the MIDX when they're not needed for reachability closure. This helps reduce MIDX bloat while maintaining bitmap correctness. The series includes comprehensive tests that verify both the exclusion and fallback cases where cruft packs must be included.

Taylor notes this version is ready for review and plans to test it in GitHub's infrastructure. The changes touch pack-objects.c, repack.c, and related test files, with careful progression from preparatory refactors to the final optimization. The series shows thoughtful design and thorough testing, making it a solid contribution to Git's pack maintenance capabilities.
