This is a build system fixup in the Windows/ARM64 platform compatibility series. Junio Hamano points out that two patches in the v2 series (patch 1 and 2) incorrectly indented Makefile conditionals with tabs rather than spaces, violating a style rule established in commit c18400c6. The fixup adjusts the indentation in `config.mak.uname` for both the nedmalloc exclusion on CLANGARM64 systems and the MSVC toolchain prefix handling.

The changes are straightforward mechanical adjustments to align with GNU make's requirements for conditional statements. The substantive content of the patches remains unchanged - they still implement the same Windows/ARM64 support by:
1. Disabling nedmalloc specifically for CLANGARM64 builds (patch 1)
2. Adding /clangarm64 prefix support for MSVC toolchain (patch 2)

Junio provides range-diffs showing the exact indentation fixes being applied to both patches while preserving their original functionality. This appears to be a final polish before queuing the series, addressing a minor but important style issue that was missed in earlier reviews. The changes don't affect the technical approach to Windows/ARM64 support but ensure the build system follows established conventions.
