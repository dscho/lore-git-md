This is a follow-up in the ongoing `git send-email` OAuth2 support thread, specifically addressing the first patch in the series that implements SMTP bearer authentication. The email comes from Aditya Garg responding to Junio Hamano's review of Julian Swagemakers' original patch.

The discussion centers around two main points: the need for domain expertise review of the Authen::SASL interaction (which Junio flagged as outside his expertise), and line length/style issues in the credential callback implementation. Aditya acknowledges the technical review gap but emphasizes the importance of OAuth2 support for modern email providers, while also CC'ing Greg (the original script author and Perl module maintainer) to solicit expert input.

Key technical details mentioned include the deep indentation causing line length violations in the credential callback sub, with Junio suggesting potential refactoring by moving the callback definition elsewhere. The patch itself (not shown in this follow-up) implements direct SMTP bearer auth to work around Authen::SASL limitations, supporting both OAUTHBEARER and XOAUTH2 formats.

The thread remains in a holding pattern awaiting review from domain experts, though Junio has positively noted the clear documentation and commit message. The security implications of the OAuth2 implementation and its interaction with Perl's authentication modules remain the primary open technical question.
