Aditya Garg and Julian Swagemakers' patch series to add OAuth2 support and Outlook compatibility fixes to `git send-email` is now in its fourth iteration. The series addresses two key pain points: implementing OAuth2 authentication (required by many modern email providers) and fixing Outlook's nonstandard Message-ID handling. Junio Hamano has reviewed all three patches, approving the Outlook Message-ID approach (patch 2) with minor documentation tweaks needed, while patches 1 (OAuth2 implementation) and 3 (dynamic token generation) require style fixes and security review respectively.

M Hickford confirms the patched version works successfully with Outlook, though notes the v4 submission broke thread continuity in the mailing list archive - a minor procedural issue that doesn't affect the technical content. The series remains in good shape pending resolution of Junio's security concerns about command execution in the token generation workflow and some remaining style issues. With these addressed, the changes appear ready for merging.
