Review feedback on object-store cleanup series. Karthik Nayak provides positive feedback on Patrick Steinhardt's 13-part refactoring series that modernizes Git's object storage code. The review notes a few minor nits but overall approves the approach, particularly praising the logical commit split. This comes as part of the ongoing effort to remove the `the_repository` global variable and clean up object storage internals.

The series focuses on moving definitions to proper headers, removing deprecated wrapper functions like `repo_has_object_file()`, and making promisor object handling more explicit through the new `has_object()` API. Karthik had previously reviewed several individual parts of the series (patches 03, 05, 07, and 09) and this message appears to be their final approval of the overall direction. The positive reception suggests this substantial refactoring is likely to proceed toward inclusion.
