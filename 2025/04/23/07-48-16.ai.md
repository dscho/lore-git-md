Refactoring patch in the ongoing object-file-cleanup series, converting deprecated `repo_has_object_file()` calls to `has_object()` with explicit flags. This sixth patch in the 13-part series systematically replaces all trivial callsites of the deprecated functions across 17 affected files, maintaining existing behavior through flag combinations:

- `repo_has_object_file()` becomes `has_object()` with `HAS_OBJECT_RECHECK_PACKED | HAS_OBJECT_FETCH_PROMISOR`
- `repo_has_object_file_with_flags(OBJECT_INFO_QUICK|OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT)` maps to `has_object()` with no flags
- `repo_has_object_file_with_flags(OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT)` maps to `HAS_OBJECT_RECHECK_PACKED`

The patch leaves some callsites unconverted where promisor object fetching behavior needs further analysis, to be addressed in subsequent patches. Key subsystems affected include fetch/push operations, shallow clones, cache-tree handling, and object existence checks throughout the codebase. The changes are mechanical but thorough, with 52 insertions and 41 deletions across core commands and infrastructure.
