He explores potential edge cases where older clients without Patrick's fix might mishandle certain REF_DELTA chains, particularly in cross-pack delta scenarios or when servers make assumptions about client object availability. While not challenging the already-approved series, this discussion surfaces important version compatibility considerations for future work in delta optimization space, highlighting how server-side pack optimizations might need to account for mixed client versions. The conversation remains technical and constructive, with no outstanding action items beyond general awareness of these implications.
