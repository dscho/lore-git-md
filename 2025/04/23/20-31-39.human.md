This is a final approval for a macOS-specific bugfix in Git's maintenance scheduler. Junio C Hamano confirms he's incorporating Derrick Stolee's positive review ("good find!") and will merge Josh Heinrichs' patch into the 'next' branch. 

The patch fixes incorrect scheduling behavior where daily maintenance jobs were only running the first six days of each month and weekly jobs were running daily on macOS systems using launchctl. The solution replaces the malformed `Day` key with `Weekday` in the launchd configuration, properly aligning with macOS documentation.

The thread shows clear consensus on the fix, with no outstanding concerns. The change is narrowly scoped to builtin/gc.c and only affects macOS users who use launchctl for maintenance scheduling. This appears ready for integration with no further discussion needed.
