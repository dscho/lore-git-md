This is a feature patch series for `git send-email`, adding OAuth2 support and fixing Outlook compatibility issues. The series comes from two authors (Aditya Garg and Julian Swagemakers) and is now in its fourth iteration.

The main additions are OAuth2 authentication support (now required by Microsoft services), fixes for Outlook's proprietary Message-ID handling that was breaking email threads, and a new `smtp-passeval` option to generate OAuth2 tokens dynamically. The author notes they're actually using these patches to send the email series from Outlook, providing real-world validation.

The changes touch both the `git-send-email.perl` implementation and its documentation. The v4 updates focus on clarifying log messages and documentation. This appears to be a well-motivated series addressing real-world email sending needs, particularly for users who must interact with Microsoft's authentication requirements and Outlook's idiosyncrasies. The patches have gone through several rounds of review with CI feedback incorporated.
