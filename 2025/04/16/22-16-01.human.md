This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. The patch introduces a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option (defaulting to true) that controls whether cruft packs must be included in the MIDX during repacking to maintain reachability guarantees for bitmap generation.

The email exchange shows Taylor responding to Elijah Newren's review feedback on the commit message wording. Elijah had pointed out some potential confusion around when the `--stdin-packs=follow` option (introduced earlier in the series) would be used, and suggested some phrasing improvements. Taylor acknowledges the feedback but decides to keep most of the original wording, explaining that the distinction between the option existing versus being used by geometric repacking is intentionally subtle.

The patch itself has already been through several rounds of review, with positive feedback from Junio Hamano on the technical approach. All previous documentation and scope issues have been addressed, and this final version appears ready for merging. The series as a whole provides more flexible control over pack maintenance strategies while maintaining the critical reachability guarantees needed for proper MIDX bitmap generation.
