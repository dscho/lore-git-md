This is a follow-up discussion on the Perl-to-shell conversion of `git-request-pull`, part of the completed series to make Perl optional in Git.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's review about the ref pattern matching in the converted shell script. The original Perl version had looser matching that could handle suffixes beyond just `^{}` (the standard dereferencing suffix). While the shell conversion could be adjusted to match this behavior exactly, both agree that since this is meant to be a faithful rewrite rather than a behavior change, they'll keep the current implementation that only handles `^{}`.

The exchange shows careful attention to detail in maintaining exact behavior during the Perl removal effort, while also being pragmatic about when strict fidelity isn't necessary. This was the final polish on the series before it was merged.
