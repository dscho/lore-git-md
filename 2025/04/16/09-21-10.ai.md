Performance optimization in the pack-objects subsystem, part of Taylor Blau's series on MIDX/cruft pack interaction. This patch improves delta compression for loose objects when using `--unpacked` with `--stdin-packs`.

The change makes loose objects participate in the name-hash traversal when they're added to the pack, which helps find better deltas. Previously, `add_unreachable_loose_objects()` couldn't access the `rev_info` struct needed for this optimization. Now that the struct is available, the patch passes it through and modifies `add_loose_object()` to add commit objects as traversal tips when a `rev_info` is provided.

The implementation is minimal (12 lines added, 8 removed) but effective - Junio Hamano notes it's "clever" and "surprisingly small". The change maintains all existing behavior when no `rev_info` is passed (like in cruft pack enumeration), only activating the optimization during regular pack operations with `--unpacked`.

This is patch 5/9 in the v3 series introducing the `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config. While the series' main focus is MIDX/cruft interaction, this patch stands out as a discrete optimization that improves pack quality regardless of the broader feature. The change is well-contained and unlikely to be controversial, having already received positive feedback from Junio.
