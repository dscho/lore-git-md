This thread documents the completion of a multi-year effort to decompose `git gc` into granular maintenance tasks, with Patrick Steinhardt's v3 series implementing the final two missing operations: worktree pruning and rerere garbage collection. The series has evolved through careful review, with v3 specifically simplifying the rerere GC approach in response to Junio Hamano's performance concerns - replacing a three-phase check with a simpler directory entry count heuristic.

Derrick Stolee, who has been involved in the maintenance command work from its inception, has reviewed and approved the series for merging. The implementation maintains child process spawning for compatibility while optimizing condition checks, and introduces new config options (`maintenance.worktree-prune.auto` and `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto`) to control automatic execution. 

The technical consensus is now solid, with the series addressing all major review feedback. While some higher-level questions remain about when to add these tasks to the default maintenance schedule and the eventual deprecation timeline for `git gc` itself, those are intentionally deferred to future work. This series successfully completes all the technical prerequisites for full `git gc` replacement, marking a significant milestone in Git's maintenance infrastructure evolution.
