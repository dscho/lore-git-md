CI/build system discussion about caching external tools in CI. Jialuo She raises the question of whether Git's CI should use caching mechanisms for third-party dependencies like JGit, Perforce, and Git-LFS, rather than downloading them fresh for every CI run. This comes in the context of ongoing CI stability issues caused by outages at repo.eclipse.org (hosting JGit).

The suggestion is that caching could provide resilience when third-party services are down, though the author notes they're not deeply familiar with the CI system's current implementation. This is a new angle in the thread that had previously focused on error handling and warning presentation for downloads. The core maintainers (Junio Hamano and Johannes Schindelin) have been discussing technical implementation details, while this email introduces a more architectural consideration about CI design.

The proposal seems reasonable on its face - caching is a common CI pattern - but it's unclear how it would interact with the current atomic download-cleanup pattern the maintainers have settled on. The email doesn't provide specific implementation details, instead opening the floor for discussion about whether caching would be a viable approach to improve CI reliability.
