Performance optimization for bundle-uri reference handling. This is part 1/2 of Scott Chacon's v7 series that expands bundle reference negotiation from just `refs/heads/` to all `refs/` namespaces while preserving hierarchy, demonstrating significant performance improvements (reducing object downloads from 32% to 1%).

The patch modifies `bundle-uri.c` to handle all references through a simple but impactful one-line change to the `skip_prefix` check, while updating tests to look for references in the new `refs/bundles/heads/` hierarchy rather than directly under `refs/bundles/`. Documentation is also updated to reflect that the refspec behavior is now fixed at `+refs/*:refs/bundles/*` rather than being configurable.

This change helps subsequent fetches by including nearly all references in negotiation rather than just branch heads. The series has already been technically approved by Junio Hamano (queued as v4 in 'seen') and incorporates all reviewer feedback, with this v7 iteration containing only final whitespace fixes to documentation. The comprehensive test suite (263 lines) verifies path/HTTP bundle handling, branch/tag verification, failure modes, and negotiation cases.
