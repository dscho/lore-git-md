This is a patch series aimed at completing the functionality of `git maintenance` to match `git gc`. The series implements the remaining tasks needed to fully replace `git gc` with finer-grained maintenance tasks, though the actual replacement isn't done yet.

Patrick Steinhardt's 7-part series refactors parts of `git gc` to make its components available as standalone maintenance tasks. The key additions are two new maintenance operations: "worktree-prune" (to clean up stale worktree references) and "rerere-gc" (to clean up old rerere records). The series also includes preparatory refactoring to extract these operations from `git gc` and make them reusable.

The changes touch several areas including worktree management and rerere functionality, with corresponding updates to documentation and tests. The work appears well-structured, with each logical change in its own commit and test coverage added for the new maintenance tasks. This continues the ongoing effort to modernize Git's maintenance infrastructure by moving away from the monolithic `git gc` approach.
