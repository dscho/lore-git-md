This is patch 07/13 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring the object store subsystem, part of the ongoing effort to remove `the_repository` global. The patch focuses on index-pack's collision check behavior with promisor objects.

The patch changes how index-pack handles promised objects during its collision check phase. Currently, the check may unnecessarily download promised objects just to verify their contents match what's in the packfile being indexed - a redundant operation since these would be the same objects just fetched. Patrick explains this creates a circular verification where we fetch an object to confirm it matches the object we just fetched.

Karthik Nayak had reviewed an earlier version, suggesting some wording clarifications in the commit message. Patrick clarifies that the collision check does indeed verify content (not just OIDs), but maintains that fetching promised objects for this check is unnecessary. The patch appears well-reasoned and builds on Karthik's positive review of the surrounding patches in the series.

This continues the series' theme of cleaning up object storage interactions, particularly around promisor objects. The change seems straightforward and unlikely to be controversial given the clear rationale and prior review approval of related changes.
