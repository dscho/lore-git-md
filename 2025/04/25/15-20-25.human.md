This is a discussion about potential caching improvements for Git's CI dependency downloads, building on the ongoing thread about handling outages of third-party services.

Junio Hamano responds to Jialuo's suggestion about using CI caching for third-party software downloads (JGit, Perforce, Git-LFS) that were recently made optional. He agrees that repeatedly downloading these dependencies in every CI job seems wasteful, and suggests the idea of running install-dependencies.sh just once per platform type, then caching the results for reuse.

The exchange represents an expansion of the original CI stability discussion from just handling outages to now considering performance optimizations. While the core solution for handling download failures is already in place (and backported), this suggests looking at longer-term improvements to make the CI more efficient. The thread shows the maintainers are open to exploring caching approaches while keeping the immediate outage-handling solution intact.
