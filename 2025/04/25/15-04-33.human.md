This is the final version of Aditya Garg's patch to fix thread tracking in `git send-email` when using Outlook SMTP servers. The patch addresses Outlook's nonstandard behavior of rewriting Message-IDs, which previously broke email threading by causing replies to reference the wrong message identifiers.

The solution is now streamlined to just 21 lines in `git-send-email.perl` that detect Outlook servers and parse their SMTP responses to get the correct Message-IDs. This maintains backward compatibility while fixing the threading issue. The previously separate OAuth2 authentication work has been successfully upstreamed to perl-authen-sasl (with Erik Hulsmann's review), making this a clean, focused solution.

The patch appears well-considered, with multiple user validations and clear technical justification. It handles the core Outlook SMTP servers (`smtp.office365.com` and `smtp-mail.outlook.com`) while leaving the door open for future enterprise configuration needs. This seems ready for inclusion, representing the culmination of several iterations to solve a real-world pain point for Outlook users of Git's email workflow.
