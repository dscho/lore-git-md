Bug report: `git stash --include-untracked` modifies timestamps of .pack files in object store without content changes, causing unnecessary backup transfers. Issue occurs even when no untracked files are present to stash, suggesting it may be related to preparatory steps of the operation rather than core functionality. Affects specific .pack files consistently within a repository, though selection criteria is unclear. Problem persists regardless of .keep file presence. Impacts backup systems like rsync by making unchanged files appear modified. No proposed fix yet included in report.
