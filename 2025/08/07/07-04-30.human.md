This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing "commit-graph: remove reliance on global state" series, specifically addressing whether Bloom filter counters should use signed or unsigned integers. The email is a response to Junio Hamano's concerns about using unsigned integers in certain contexts.

Patrick Steinhardt agrees with Junio's general point that signed integers are preferable when dealing with sentinel values or error-returning functions, but argues that in this specific case (Bloom filter counters), unsigned integers are more appropriate since they never need negative values or sentinels. The discussion is part of a broader philosophical debate about integer type selection that emerged during the review of this series.

The exchange shows the technical considerations behind what might seem like a simple type change, with both maintainers agreeing on the general principles while differing on this specific implementation choice. This patch was already accepted in v2 of the series, so this is more of a post-acceptance clarification than a substantive change request.
