This is a minor follow-up to a refactoring patch in the recently completed push error handling series. The series has already been reviewed and approved by both Junio Hamano and Patrick, with this being a small cleanup suggestion on top. Given the series is already been marked for inclusion, this will likely be a trivial amendment before merging. The interaction shows the typical back-and-forth where maintainers suggest small improvements even on already-polished patches.
