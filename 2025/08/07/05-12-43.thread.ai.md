Performance optimization for Bloom filters in revision traversal with wildcard pathspecs, originating from Lidong Yan's patch series (now at v5 with final refinements). For wildcards, it uses the non-wildcard portion up to the last directory separator for Bloom matching, with clear fallback to non-filtered traversal for first-component wildcards.
