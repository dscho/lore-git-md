This is patch 6/16 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the "kept pack cache" from `struct object_database` to the new `packfile_store` structure, continuing the mechanical relocation of packfile-specific state.

The change affects three files:
- `odb.h` removes the `kept_pack_cache` struct and its documentation
- `packfile.c` updates references to use the new location
- `packfile.h` adds the cache to `struct packfile_store`

Maintainer Junio Hamano questions whether this move should have been done in the initial patch (01/16) that created `packfile_store`, noting that this might be the last piece of "private data" being relocated. This suggests the series' organization could be more logical, though the technical change itself is straightforward.

The patch maintains identical behavior while continuing the architectural goal of separating packfile management from the generic object database. It touches the same areas as previous patches in the series (packfile operations, object database internals) and follows the established pattern of moving packfile-related state to the dedicated store structure.
