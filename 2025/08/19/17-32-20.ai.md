This is a maintainer review comment on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile refactoring series. Junio Hamano raises two specific concerns about the naming and structure of the new `packfile_store` abstraction.

First, he questions the field name `packfiles` in `struct object_database`, suggesting `packfile_store` would be more accurate since the structure represents more than just a collection of packfiles. Second, he notes the circular reference where `object_database` points to `packfile_store` which in turn references back to the ODB, and expresses uncertainty about what architectural benefit this first step provides since the patch doesn't yet demonstrate any functional use of the new structure.

The feedback comes after Junio had previously given general approval to the series' overall approach. This email focuses narrowly on implementation details of the initial scaffolding patch, suggesting the naming could be more precise and that the patch's value might not be immediately obvious to readers without seeing later steps in the series. The tone is constructive but probing, characteristic of maintainer review that seeks to ensure clarity and intentionality in foundational changes.
