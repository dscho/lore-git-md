This is the first patch in a 16-part series refactoring Git's packfile management as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. Patrick Steinhardt introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile-related data that's currently scattered between `struct packed_git` and `struct object_database`. 

The patch lays the groundwork by defining the new structure and its basic allocation/free functions, though it's currently just a shell that will be populated in later patches. The change is purely internal - no behavior changes yet. The commit message clearly explains this is an intermediate step toward eventually making packfile management pluggable at the ODB source level.

The implementation touches odb.c/h and packfile.c/h, adding about 30 lines of new code. This appears to be a well-structured first step in a larger architectural change, with the commit message providing context about both immediate and future goals. The patch follows the series' stated goal of centralizing packfile management before making it pluggable.
