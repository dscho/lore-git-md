This is a preparatory refactoring patch for the `git-history` command series, modifying the cache-tree infrastructure to support working with arbitrary in-memory indexes.

The patch generalizes `write_in_core_index_as_tree()` to accept any `index_state` rather than always using the repository's index. This small but important change allows future patches in the series to work with temporary in-memory indexes when performing history operations, rather than being limited to the repository's main index. The modification is straightforward - just adding a parameter and updating the few callers.

The change is well-contained, touching only cache-tree.c and its header plus one caller in checkout.c. The commit message clearly explains the change's purpose in relation to the larger series. This appears to be solid groundwork for the more user-visible history editing commands to come later in the series.
