This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing packfile store refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt, which is part of the broader object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The email contains Junio Hamano's response to the series cover letter.

Junio expresses general approval of the approach, calling it "Nice" and saying the plan "Makes sense". He provides some architectural context about how packfiles relate to object stores and alternates, noting that the current implementation links packfiles (including those from alternates) into a single list for convenience. He suggests this might need to be addressed in future work.

The response indicates the series is on the right track with its goal of centralizing packfile management in `struct packfile_store` to support pluggable ODB backends. Junio's comments about alternates hint at potential future considerations but don't raise any objections to the current approach. This aligns with the thread's previous technical discussions which have focused on minor documentation and style points rather than substantive challenges.

Key technical context from the series includes:
- Introduction of `struct packfile_store` as central packfile manager
- Migration of packfile state from `struct object_database`
- 16 mechanical patches moving various packfile-related components
- Preparation for future pluggable ODB backends
- No behavior changes - purely structural refactoring

The discussion remains positive and forward-looking, with maintainer approval for the architectural direction. Future work mentioned includes further decoupling of packfile management from the ODB level.
