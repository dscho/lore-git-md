The function now prepares the packfile store and returns its packfiles, including those referenced by multi-pack indices. The change is mechanical but wide-ranging, touching 19 files where the function is called to iterate over all packfiles.

This continues the series' pattern of isolating packfile-specific operations within the new store abstraction while maintaining identical behavior.
