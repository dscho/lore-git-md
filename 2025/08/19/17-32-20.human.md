This is a maintainer review comment on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile refactoring series. Junio Hamano raises two specific questions about the naming and structure of the new `packfile_store` abstraction:

1. He questions whether the field name `packfiles` is appropriate for the new `struct packfile_store*` member in `object_database`, suggesting `packfile_store` might be more accurate since it represents more than just a collection of packfiles.

2. He notes the circular reference where `object_database` points to `packfile_store` while `packfile_store` also contains an `object_database` pointer, and expresses some uncertainty about what this initial step accomplishes since the new structure isn't yet being used for anything concrete.

The feedback is constructive but not blocking - Junio explicitly says he'll continue reviewing the series to understand the broader context. This comes after his earlier approval of the overall architectural approach in the cover letter. The questions focus on implementation details rather than the fundamental design, suggesting the series is on the right track but could benefit from clearer naming and documentation of the intermediate steps.
