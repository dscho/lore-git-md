This is part 10 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure as part of the object database abstraction effort. The patch focuses on `install_packed_git()`, which adds packfiles to the object store.

The change renames `install_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_add_pack()` and modifies it to operate directly on a `packfile_store` rather than a repository. This better reflects its actual scope and continues the pattern seen in previous patches of moving packfile-specific operations out of the generic repository/ODB context. The implementation remains functionally identical - it still adds the pack to the store's list and hashmap - but now works with the packfile store directly.

The patch updates all callers across several files (fast-import, index-pack, http, midx) to use the new interface. The changes are mechanical but wide-ranging, touching core packfile operations. This continues the series' careful separation of packfile management from broader repository concerns, maintaining existing behavior while preparing for future pluggable ODB backends.

As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a well-executed step in the larger refactoring effort, with thorough updates to all affected call sites. The changes are focused and maintain the same functionality while improving the architectural separation.
