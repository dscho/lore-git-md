This is a refactoring patch in the final part of a series improving error handling in `git describe`. The series has already seen significant review, with patches 1-3 approved and patch 4 under active discussion.

Patrick Steinhardt is responding to Jeff King's patch 5/5, which removes a redundant commit lookup in `describe_commit()` by having callers pass the commit struct directly rather than an OID. While Patrick agrees the refactoring makes sense, he raises a subtle concern about commit-graph edge cases where commits might be found in the graph but not the ODB when `GIT_COMMIT_GRAPH_PARANOIA=false`.

The comment is more of a thought experiment than a blocking concern - Patrick explicitly states the refactoring still makes sense regardless. This continues the series' pattern of methodically tightening up error handling while maintaining backward compatibility. The discussion shows the maintainers are being thorough in considering even unlikely edge cases as they finalize this safety-focused series.
