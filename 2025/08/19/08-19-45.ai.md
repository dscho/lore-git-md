This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 16-part series refactoring Git's packfile subsystem as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The patch completes the transition of packfile-related functionality from operating on `the_repository` to working with the new `struct packfile_store`.

The change refactors `get_packed_git_mru()` to become `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()`, making it operate directly on a packfile store rather than a repository. This follows the pattern established in previous patches of moving packfile-specific operations out of the generic repository/ODB context. The function now takes a `packfile_store` parameter and returns the MRU list from that store after ensuring it's prepared.

The patch updates one caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to use the new function, passing the repository's packfile store. The changes are mechanical but complete the series' goal of centralizing all packfile management in the dedicated store structure. With this patch, all major packfile operations have been migrated from `struct object_database` to `struct packfile_store`, setting the stage for future pluggable ODB backends.

The series represents significant infrastructure work that maintains identical behavior while restructuring internal APIs. Each patch has followed a consistent pattern of moving state and operations to the packfile store, with this final patch tying up the remaining loose ends around MRU list handling.
