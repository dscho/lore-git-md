This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store` as part of the object database abstraction effort.

The patch relocates the packfile map from `struct object_database` to the new `packfile_store` structure. This map tracks which packfiles have already been loaded by their path names. The change follows the established pattern of the series by moving packfile-specific state out of the generic ODB structure.

Key technical changes:
- The `pack_map` hashmap and its initialization/cleanup are moved from odb.c to packfile.c
- The comparison function `pack_map_entry_cmp()` becomes static since it's now only used within packfile.c
- All references to the map are updated to use the new location in `packfiles->map`

The changes are mechanical and maintain the same behavior, continuing the careful separation of packfile management from the broader ODB work. This patch builds on the foundation laid by the first three patches in the series and keeps the codebase moving toward the goal of pluggable ODB backends.
