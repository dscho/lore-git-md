Karthik Nayak's reftable fsck validation series has been merged to 'next' after six iterations, with final approval from both Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. The series implements stack integrity checks for Git's reftable backend through a callback-based architecture in `reftable/fsck.[ch]`, bringing it to parity with the files backend's fsck capabilities. 

The final version (v6) incorporated Jeff King's feedback about test case design, replacing root refs with proper branch refs (`refs/heads/branch-$i`) in the test suite to avoid confusion when reference-level validation is added later. The core functionality remains unchanged - it validates table name formats, enforces strict parsing of 'tables.list', and maintains consistent fsck messages across backends. The series went through careful refinement over its six versions, addressing race conditions, completing documentation, and polishing test cases before receiving maintainer approval. The callback-based design leaves room for future enhancements while providing robust validation for the reftable format today.
