Thread identity: RFC proposal for `git-history` command with seventeen-patch v4 series (including `reword` and `split` subcommands), authored by Patrick Steinhardt. Series builds on Git's sequencer while drawing inspiration from Jujutsu's history editing capabilities, now focused on non-conflicting operations after Git Contributor's Summit discussions.

Core problem/goal: Implement flexible history editing operations (`reword` and `split` subcommands) with supporting infrastructure refactoring (interactive add/patch, cache-tree modifications) to enable in-memory index operations. Current implementation avoids `drop` and management commands based on community feedback.
