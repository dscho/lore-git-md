Thread identity: RFC proposal for `git-history` command with twelve-patch v5 series (including `reword` and `split` subcommands), authored by Patrick Steinhardt. Core problem/goal: Implement flexible history editing operations (`reword` and `split` subcommands) with supporting infrastructure refactoring (interactive add/patch, cache-tree modifications, and wt-status tree-to-tree diff capabilities) to enable in-memory index operations. Current implementation avoids `drop` and management commands based on community feedback.
