Thread identity: RFC proposal for new `git-history` command with subcommands `drop`, `reorder`, and `split`, inspired by Jujutsu (jj) functionality. Authored by Patrick Steinhardt. Series now at v1 with thirteen patches - three preparatory, command scaffolding, three subcommand implementations, and supporting refactoring work.

Core problem/goal: Provide flexible history editing operations building on Git's sequencer infrastructure while taking inspiration from Jujutsu. Current subcommands: `drop` (commit removal), `reorder` (commit rearrangement), and `split` (interactive commit division). Ongoing discussion examines command boundaries with rebase/cherry-pick, architectural implications, and user experience details.
