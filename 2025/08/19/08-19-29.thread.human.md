Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing work to abstract Git's object database continues with part 15/16 of his series, which refactors `get_all_packs()` to work directly with the new `packfile_store` structure rather than going through the repository. This mechanical but wide-ranging change renames the function to `packfile_store_get_packs()` and updates its signature while maintaining the same underlying behavior, including MIDX pack preparation. The patch updates 19 call sites across the codebase to first get the packfile store from the repository.

The series as a whole systematically moves packfile management out of the generic ODB structure into a dedicated store, with each patch handling a specific component like the packed_git list, MRU cache, or hashmap. Recent discussion has focused on standardizing MRU list handling (with input from Jeff King about historical design) and whether to incorporate style cleanups now or defer them (Patrick prefers deferring to maintain focus on structural changes). The work remains technically sound, with careful attention to maintaining behavior while enabling future pluggable ODB backends.
