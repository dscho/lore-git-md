Patrick had already explained in the original commit that this was preparatory work for future steps, and Justin's comment confirms this understanding. The exchange serves as a post-merge clarification rather than raising new issues, as the technical approach remains uncontested. The series stands as an important architectural milestone enabling future work on pluggable object storage backends.
