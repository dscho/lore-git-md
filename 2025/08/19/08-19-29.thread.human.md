This thread continues Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile management as part of the larger object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The goal is to centralize packfile handling in a new `struct packfile_store` rather than having it scattered through `struct object_database`. 

The series is now at version 4 with 15 patches. Patch 2/15 moved the core packfile list, while patch 3/15 (the latest) relocates the `packed_git_initialized` flag from `object_database` to `packfile_store`, converting it from a bitfield to a proper boolean type in the process. These are mechanical, internal changes that maintain existing behavior while improving the architecture. 

Review feedback has been positive overall, with all major architectural questions resolved in earlier rounds. Only minor documentation nits remain open, and the series appears merge-ready pending final maintainer approval. Key participants include Patrick (author), Karthik Nayak (implementation review), Junio Hamano (maintainer oversight), and Jeff King (design input). The changes represent an important step toward enabling pluggable ODB backends in Git.
