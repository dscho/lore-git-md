This thread continues Patrick Steinhardt's 16-part series abstracting Git's packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`, moving functionality piece by piece from the object database (ODB). The latest patch (10/16) refactors `install_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_add_pack()`, completing another step in isolating packfile operations.

Taylor Blau reviewed the patch positively, noting the changes are correct and straightforward. He mildly preferred keeping "install" in the function name to better indicate the packfile would become active for lookups, but explicitly stated this wasn't a blocking concern. The naming discussion remains the only open point on this patch, as no technical issues were identified.

The series continues making steady progress, with patch 9 having surfaced a locking consideration between the packfile store and ODB layers that remains under discussion. Earlier patches have addressed style suggestions, memory management concerns, and documentation gaps while systematically migrating packfile state and operations. The work touches core areas like pack handling, MRU lists, and MIDX integration across multiple commands and test helpers.

With most architectural questions resolved in prior reviews, the current focus is on completing the mechanical moves of functionality to the new store interface while maintaining thread safety and clear layer separation. The series remains on track toward its goal of enabling future pluggable ODB backends.
