Patrick Steinhardt's 16-patch series refactoring Git's packfile subsystem has progressed through review, with maintainer Junio Hamano providing both high-level approval and detailed feedback. The work introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management as part of the broader object database (ODB) abstraction effort.

Junio initially approved the architectural approach, calling it "Nice" and saying the plan "Makes sense," while noting that the current implementation links packfiles from alternates for convenience - a detail that may need future attention. The series has undergone thorough technical review from both Junio and Karthik Nayak, with feedback on documentation clarity and coding style in early patches.

In his latest review, Junio has raised more pointed questions about the initial scaffolding patch. He suggests renaming the `packfiles` field in `struct object_database` to `packfile_store` for accuracy, and questions the immediate value of the circular reference between ODB and the new store structure. This reflects Junio's characteristic attention to both naming precision and architectural intentionality, even when generally supportive of a change.

The series has already addressed earlier review feedback, including Karthik's requests for documentation improvements and style clarifications. With the core architectural changes now approved and only these final implementation details under discussion, the series appears close to being ready for integration. The changes affect numerous packfile-related operations across the codebase while maintaining identical behavior, laying important foundations for future ODB abstraction work.
