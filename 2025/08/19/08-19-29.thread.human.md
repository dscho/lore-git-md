Patrick Steinhardt continues his series refactoring Git's packfile subsystem as part of the broader object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The 16-patch series systematically moves packfile-related state from the generic `struct object_database` to a new dedicated `struct packfile_store`.

The latest patch (6/16) relocates the "kept pack cache" - used by `git-pack-objects` for handling cruft objects - from the ODB to the packfile store. This follows the established pattern of patches 1-5, which already moved the packed_git list, MRU list, packfile map, and related flags. Each change maintains identical behavior while cleaning up the architecture.

The changes are mechanical but foundational, touching core files like packfile.c and odb.h as the series prepares for future pluggable ODB backends. So far the series has progressed without review feedback, suggesting reviewers are comfortable with this infrastructure work. The remaining patches are expected to continue moving packfile management components into the dedicated store structure.
