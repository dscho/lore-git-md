This thread centers on Patrick Steinhardt's major refactoring of Git's packfile subsystem as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The 16-patch series introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management, moving state and operations out of the generic ODB structure. Each patch systematically relocates components like the packed_git list, MRU cache, and packfile map into the new store while maintaining identical behavior.

Karthik Nayak has been providing thorough review feedback, with recent comments focusing on style nits and architectural questions about MIDX edge cases and data structure choices (strmap vs hashmap). The latest contribution comes from Jeff King (Peff), who weighs in on why the implementation maintains both a regular packfile list and an MRU-ordered list - a historical artifact from when the MRU optimization was first introduced. Peff suggests this might now be a good time to consolidate to a single MRU-ordered list, though he cautions this should be handled carefully to avoid iteration issues.

The discussion remains technically focused, with no major objections to the series itself. Key open questions awaiting Patrick's response include the MIDX edge cases, data structure choices, and now Peff's suggestion about MRU list consolidation. The thread demonstrates the project's careful approach to significant refactoring - maintaining backward compatibility while methodically improving the architecture.
