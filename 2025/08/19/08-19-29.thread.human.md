Patrick Steinhardt's series to refactor Git's packfile management continues to progress, now at part 11 of 16. The current patch standardizes how packfiles are added to the most-recently-used (MRU) list when first loaded, addressing an inconsistency where some subsystems had to manually add them later. Karthik Nayak approved the change, seeing it as a step toward better encapsulation of the new `packfile_store` structure's internals.

The discussion builds on earlier exchanges about the relationship between the main packfile list and MRU list in the `packfile_store`. While Karthik had previously questioned whether both lists are needed long-term, his latest comment suggests he's comfortable with the current approach as an intermediate step toward cleaner APIs. The series remains focused on mechanical refactoring to move packfile operations out of the generic object database structure, with each patch carefully reviewed by Karthik and others. No new technical concerns have emerged, and the work continues to align with the project's broader object database abstraction goals.
