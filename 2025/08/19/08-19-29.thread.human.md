Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series has reached its final stage with broad consensus among key contributors. The 15-patch series, now at version 6, successfully centralizes packfile management in a new `struct packfile_store`, migrating all packfile state from `struct object_database` while maintaining behavior across approximately 50 call sites. This marks a significant architectural milestone in Git's ongoing ODB abstraction effort.

The series has now received all necessary approvals for merging into 'next'. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, had previously confirmed the topic was ready after minor documentation refinements in v6. Justin Tobler provided additional technical review, particularly around the forward declaration scope and MIDX interactions. The final acknowledgment comes from Taylor Blau, who reviewed the changes via range-diffs from v4 to v6 and added his Acked-by tag, noting no new issues in the final version despite some delay in reviewing v5.

With all major participants in agreement and no outstanding concerns, this foundational refactoring is now poised to enter the development branch, paving the way for future ODB abstraction work. The clean separation of packfile management from object database responsibilities establishes clearer architectural boundaries that will benefit subsequent development.
