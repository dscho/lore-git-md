Patrick Steinhardt continues his 16-part series refactoring Git's packfile subsystem as part of the broader object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The series introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management, moving state and operations out of the generic ODB structure.

Junio Hamano has provided both high-level approval of the architectural direction and detailed implementation suggestions. He initially questioned some aspects of the first patch (naming and circular references) and now suggests that patch 6's "kept pack cache" move might have logically belonged in the initial patch. The series has also received minor documentation and style feedback from Karthik Nayak.

The latest patch (07/16) is a straightforward code reorganization in packfile.c that eliminates a forward declaration of `prepare_packed_git()`. Junio notes the change makes sense while gently correcting the terminology in the commit message - what was called an "extra declaration" is more properly called a "forward declaration". This is a mechanical cleanup that doesn't change behavior, following the series' pattern of incremental refactoring to support the larger architectural goal.

The work affects multiple areas of Git's codebase but maintains identical behavior while preparing for future pluggable ODB backends. With Junio's architectural approval secured, the discussion now focuses on implementation details and patch organization as the series progresses toward completion.
