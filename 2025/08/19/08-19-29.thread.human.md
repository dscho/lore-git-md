Here's a human-readable summary of the thread's current state:

Patrick Steinhardt is leading a major refactoring of Git's packfile subsystem as part of the ongoing object database (ODB) abstraction effort. His 16-patch series introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management, moving related state out of the more generic `struct object_database`. This foundational work will enable future pluggable ODB backends.

The series has received detailed review from both Karthik Nayak and maintainer Junio Hamano. Junio initially approved the architectural approach but has since raised specific concerns about implementation details. In particular, he questioned whether the first patch's structure and naming be improved, and in the latest review of patch 6, he suggested the "kept pack cache" relocation might have fit better in the initial scaffolding patch.

So far, the series has methodically moved various packfile components - including the packed_git list, MRU cache, and now the kept pack cache - to the new store structure. Each change maintains existing behavior while cleaning up the architecture. The changes span multiple commands and subsystems but focus primarily on packfile internals.

The technical work appears sound, but the discussion has shifted to whether the patch organization could better reflect the logical progression of the changes. Junio's feedback suggests he wants to see the series demonstrate clearer intentionality in how it builds up the new structure. The core concept remains approved, but the implementation is being refined through review.
