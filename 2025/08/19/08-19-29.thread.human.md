This thread documents Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing effort to refactor Git's object database (ODB) by introducing a dedicated `struct packfile_store` to manage packfile-related state and operations. The 16-patch series systematically moves packfile functionality out of the generic `struct object_database` into this new abstraction.

The latest patch (16/16) completes the transition by converting `get_packed_git_mru()` to work directly with the packfile store, renaming it to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()`. Like previous patches, this is a mechanical change that maintains existing behavior while improving encapsulation. The only caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` has been updated accordingly.

Throughout the series, Patrick has addressed feedback from reviewers like Jeff King (Peff) and Karthik Nayak. Key discussion points have included the standardization of MRU list handling, edge cases with MIDX-indexed packs, and whether to incorporate style cleanups now or defer them. The changes are technically sound and follow a clear pattern of moving packfile operations to the new store structure.

With this final patch, all packfile management has been successfully moved to the dedicated store, achieving the series' goal of better separation between generic ODB functionality and packfile-specific operations. This foundational work enables future development of pluggable ODB backends while maintaining Git's existing behavior.
