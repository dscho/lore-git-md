Patrick Steinhardt has completed his major refactoring of Git's packfile management, centralizing all related functions under a new `struct packfile_store` as part of the ongoing object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The 15-patch series, now fully merged, cleanly separates packfile operations from the broader repository infrastructure.

The final changes included renaming the last remaining packfile-related functions to reflect their new scope: `get_packed_git()` became `packfile_store_get_packs()`, `get_all_packs()` transitioned to `packfile_store_get_all_packs()`, and in this concluding patch, `get_packed_git_mru()` was updated to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()`. These changes affect about 50 call sites across the codebase while maintaining identical behavior.

The refactoring establishes clear architectural boundaries, with packfile operations now properly contained within the packfile store subsystem. The only remaining transitional detail is direct MIDX (multi-pack-index) access, which will be addressed in future work. This series represents a significant milestone in Git's ongoing effort to make its object storage layer more modular and maintainable.
