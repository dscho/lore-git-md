This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. Patch 13/16 relocates the `get_multi_pack_index()` function from packfile.c to midx.c, where it more logically belongs as part of the multi-pack index subsystem.

The change involves exposing `packfile_store_prepare()` (previously static) since it's needed by the moved function. The patch maintains identical behavior while better organizing the code - MIDX-related functionality now lives entirely within midx.c/h. This prepares for future changes where `get_multi_pack_index()` will be called from outside the packfile system.

Key technical details:
- Moves `get_multi_pack_index()` implementation to midx.c
- Adds its declaration to midx.h
- Removes declaration from packfile.h
- Makes `packfile_store_prepare()` non-static and documents it in packfile.h
- Updates includes as needed
- All call sites continue working unchanged

This continues the series' pattern of carefully separating concerns while maintaining behavior, here specifically around MIDX access. The change is mechanical but helps untangle packfile and MIDX subsystems in preparation for pluggable ODB backends.
