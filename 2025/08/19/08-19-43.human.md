This is part 14 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring of Git's packfile subsystem to better support pluggable object database backends. The patch removes the redundant `get_packed_git()` function, which had overlapping functionality with `get_all_packs()` - both ultimately returned the same list of packfiles, but with slightly different initialization behavior.

The change affects several callers in builtin/gc.c, builtin/grep.c, and object-name.c, all of which are updated to use either `get_all_packs()` or the more direct `packfile_store_prepare()` instead. The author explains that each caller is either unaffected by or benefits from the more complete initialization that `get_all_packs()` provides, making the separate `get_packed_git()` function unnecessary.

This is a clean-up patch that follows logically from the earlier work in the series to centralize packfile management in the `packfile_store` structure. The changes are straightforward and well-justified, with clear analysis of each affected caller. The patch continues the series' pattern of simplifying the packfile interface while maintaining identical behavior.
