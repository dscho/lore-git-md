This is patch 6/16 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile subsystem as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The patch continues the mechanical relocation of packfile-related state from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`.

The change moves the "kept pack cache" - used by `git-pack-objects` to handle cruft objects - from the ODB structure to the packfile store. The patch:
1. Removes the `kept_pack_cache` field from `struct object_database`
2. Adds an equivalent `kept_cache` field to `struct packfile_store`
3. Updates all related functions in packfile.c to reference the new location
4. Maintains identical behavior while changing only the storage location

The changes follow the established pattern of the series, with straightforward updates to the cache management functions (`maybe_invalidate_kept_pack_cache` and `kept_pack_cache`) to use the store's field instead of the ODB's. The patch touches odb.h, packfile.c, and packfile.h, with a net reduction of 2 lines due to removed comments.

This continues the systematic separation of packfile management from the generic ODB structure, preparing for future pluggable ODB backends. The change is mechanical and well-contained like previous patches in the series, with no expected behavioral impact.
