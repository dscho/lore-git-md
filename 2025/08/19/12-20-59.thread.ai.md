Reftable fsck validation series (v3 in progress) - completing stack integrity checks. Authored by Karthik Nayak, this evolving 5-patch series adds comprehensive fsck validation for reftable stack integrity while maintaining separation between Git's fsck and reftable internals. Key tensions remain around runtime strictness vs fsck-only validation, platform-specific behaviors (Windows file deletion), and the window to tighten rules before reftable becomes default. The series represents incremental progress in the larger reftable backend effort, with validation scope potentially expanding based on this discussion's outcome around concurrency handling and edge cases.
