This is part 2 of Patrick Steinhardt's 16-patch series refactoring Git's packfile management into a new `struct packfile_store`. The series is part of the larger object database (ODB) abstraction effort.

The patch moves the list of packfiles from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`, introducing it as a `packs` field. It also relocates related cleanup logic - extracting packfile closing and freeing operations from `odb_clear()` into new `packfile_store_close()` and `packfile_store_free()` functions. The changes are mechanical updates to all code paths that previously accessed `packed_git` directly, now routing through the store's `packs` field instead.

The changes are wide-ranging but straightforward, touching core packfile operations like installation, preparation, and MRU list management. As with the rest of the series, there are no user-visible behavior changes - this is purely internal restructuring to support future goal of making packfile management pluggable per ODB backend. The patch appears well-executed, with thorough updates to all affected code paths and clear separation of concerns in the new store interface.
