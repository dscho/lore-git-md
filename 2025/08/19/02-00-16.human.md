This is a clarification email in the ongoing RFC series about Rust integration and xdiff optimizations. Elijah Newren responds to Ramsay Jones' observation that the patch series doesn't actually build on *all* platforms (specifically failing on Cygwin), despite the claim in the cover letter. 

Elijah explains that Ezekiel Newren (the series author) meant the patch builds on all platforms covered by GitGitGadget's CI system, not literally every possible platform. He points to the GitHub Actions run linked in the original email as evidence of the tested platforms, which includes various Windows versions, macOS builds, musl, and other CI configurations. 

The exchange highlights the importance of precise wording in platform compatibility claims, especially for a change that introduces Rust as a hard dependency. This is a minor clarification in the broader thread, but it underscores the careful attention being paid to platform support details throughout this significant architectural change.
