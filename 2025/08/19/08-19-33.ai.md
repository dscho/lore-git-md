This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store` as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort.

The patch relocates the packfile map (a lookup table tracking loaded packfiles by their paths) from `struct object_database` to the new `packfile_store` structure. The changes include:
- Moving the `pack_map` hashmap field from ODB to `packfile_store`
- Updating all references to use `store->map` instead of the old `odb->pack_map`
- Converting the `pack_map_entry_cmp()` helper function to static since it's now only used within packfile.c
- Initializing and clearing the map in the packfile_store lifecycle functions

The changes are mechanical but touch several key areas of packfile handling, including pack installation and preparation. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by moving packfile-specific state out of the generic ODB structure while maintaining identical behavior. This continues the groundwork for making packfile management implementation-specific in the ODB abstraction.

The diff shows careful attention to all the necessary updates across multiple files (midx.c, odb.c/h, packfile.c/h) with no apparent omissions. The commit message clearly explains the what and why of the change, though like previous patches in the series, it doesn't discuss potential performance or memory implications of the reorganization.
