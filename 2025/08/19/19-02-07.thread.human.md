This thread documents a straightforward improvement to Git's contribution guide, adding Discord as an unofficial community support channel alongside existing options like IRC. The patch has gone through two iterations, with the second version incorporating maintainer feedback from Junio Hamano to clarify wording - changing "the unofficial" to "an unofficial" Discord server to better reflect that it's one of several community options rather than the sole unofficial channel.

The 9-line addition to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` describes Discord's real-time chat features while maintaining clear distinction of its unofficial status. The permanent invite link matches what's already listed on git-scm.com, addressing maintainer concerns about link stability. With Junio's approval of the wording changes and no outstanding objections from other contributors, this change appears ready for merging. It represents a small but meaningful step in making Git's community resources more discoverable to newcomers while maintaining clear expectations about official vs. unofficial support channels.
