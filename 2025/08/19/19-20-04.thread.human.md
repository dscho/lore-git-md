This thread started with Jeff King's series fixing incorrect behavior around dangling symrefs in `git fetch`, particularly when `followRemoteHEAD=create` is configured. The patches were settled in 'next' with Junio C Hamano making the final call to keep the `git -C` test isolation approach despite some readability concerns. The core fix prevents fetch from overwriting explicitly created remote HEAD symrefs while allowing creation when missing.

Now Toon Claes has identified an edge case where the fix unintentionally blocked a legitimate workflow: intentionally deleting dangling symrefs by updating them to the null OID. The proposed follow-up tweaks the check in both refs backends to allow null-to-null updates while maintaining all other protections. The change is minimal - just adding a condition to the existing checks - and comes with a new test case. This appears to be a well-scoped adjustment to the oversight in the original implementation, though it's unclear whether blocking such deletions was an intentional design choice. The patch follows the same style as the original series and has been submitted for consideration.
