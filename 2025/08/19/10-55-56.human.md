This is a feature proposal introducing a new `git history` command for easier history editing, inspired by Jujutsu's functionality. Patrick Steinhardt proposes three initial subcommands: `drop` (remove a commit), `reorder` (move a commit), and `split` (divide a commit). The RFC acknowledges these are just a starting point, with potential future additions like `reword` and `squash`.

The 11-part series builds on existing cherry-pick infrastructure (with known limitations around merge conflicts and merge commits) and includes significant refactoring of the add-patch subsystem to support in-memory operations. The implementation comes with thorough test coverage across three new test files.

The proposal clearly positions this as learning from Jujutsu's strengths while adapting them to Git's workflow. Current limitations (like merge commit handling) are openly acknowledged as areas for future improvement. The patchset appears well-structured with clean separation of preparatory refactors from the new functionality.
