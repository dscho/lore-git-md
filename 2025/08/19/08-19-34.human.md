This is part 5 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store` as part of the object database abstraction effort. 

The patch relocates the Most-Recently-Used (MRU) list of packfiles from `struct object_database` to the new `packfile_store` structure. The MRU list helps optimize object access by prioritizing packfiles that have been recently used. The changes are mechanical but wide-ranging, updating all references to the MRU list across midx.c, odb.c, packfile.c and related headers.

The patch follows the established pattern of the series: moving packfile-specific state out of the generic ODB structure while maintaining identical behavior. It builds on the previous patches that moved the packed_git list, initialization flag, and packfile map. The changes are straightforward and appear to be well-contained, with no behavioral impact expected.

This continues the groundwork for making packfile management implementation-specific in the ODB abstraction, a prerequisite for future pluggable ODB backends. The series is progressing methodically through the packfile-related state in the ODB, with more patches expected to follow moving remaining components like the packfile chain and kept pack cache.
