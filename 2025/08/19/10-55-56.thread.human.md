Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` command series continues to progress toward integration, with the latest patch focusing on shared infrastructure. This change enables both `git-replay` and the new `git-history` to share core functionality while maintaining clear subsystem boundaries. The 110-line move was straightforward to verify thanks to Git's `--color-moved` diff capability.
