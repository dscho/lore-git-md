Patrick Steinhardt's proposed `git-history` command continues to evolve through thoughtful discussion, with the v7 series now complete and awaiting experimental inclusion in Git's 'next' branch. The command aims to provide Jujutsu-inspired history editing capabilities (`reword` and `split` subcommands) while working within Git's existing replay infrastructure. 

Junio Hamano has signaled his preference for the command to automatically handle commits across all branches by default, with Phillip Wood now supporting this approach while suggesting an opt-out `--single-branch` flag. This aligns with Matthias Beyer's concerns about user expectations, though the discussion has revealed deeper philosophical divides. Martin von Zweigbergk's proposal for Jujutsu-style immutable ref patterns has clarified that such protections would intentionally block operations on protected branches - not as a middle ground, but as an orthogonal safety layer.
