But SZEDER argues the default behavior—rewriting all local branches—may violate Git's porcelain conventions by being too aggressive, and that the documentation doesn't sufficiently explain tradeoffs compared to alternatives like `git rebase --reword`.

While Junio's approval suggests these are polish items for follow-up rather than blockers, the critique highlights a tension between the command's innovative design and Git's conservative approach to history modification. The series remains on track for merging, but the discussion suggests future iterations may need to refine defaults and documentation to better align with community expectations around safety.
