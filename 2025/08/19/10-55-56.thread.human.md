This RFC thread introduces Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for a new `git-history` command with three subcommands (`drop`, `reorder`, and `split`), inspired by Jujutsu (jj) functionality. The thirteen-patch series includes preparatory work, command scaffolding, and implementations of each subcommand, with the `split` functionality being particularly feature-rich (interactive hunk selection, pathspec support, and extensive test coverage).

D. Ben Knoble began reviewing the series but encountered email delivery issues missing patches 8-11, a common problem with GMail's aggressive filtering of mailing list traffic. Patrick confirmed this is a known issue and suggested retrieving the missing patches from the lore archive. While this exchange doesn't advance the technical discussion, it highlights the practical challenges of mailing list collaboration. The series remains in early review phase with several open questions around merge commit support, conflict handling, and performance characteristics.
