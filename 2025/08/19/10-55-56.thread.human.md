This RFC thread continues to refine the design of the proposed `git-history` command, which aims to provide flexible history editing operations inspired by Jujutsu (jj). Patrick Steinhardt leads the effort with an eighteen-patch v3 series that includes subcommands like `drop`, `reorder`, and `split`, while deferring `reword` for now. Key technical refinements include:
- Simplified implementation of the `skip_commit_summary` flag following Phillip Wood's suggestion
- Enhanced `split` subcommand that now allows editing messages for both resulting commits
- Ongoing discussion about HEAD management strategies, with Phillip advocating for rebase-style detached HEAD for cleaner reflogs

Patrick has been responsive to such simplification suggestions throughout the thread, having already agreed to drop the current `reword` approach in favor of future sequencer integration.
