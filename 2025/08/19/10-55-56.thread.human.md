This RFC thread explores Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for a new `git-history` command, currently focused on `reword` and `split` subcommands for flexible history editing. The v6 series has addressed technical concerns through shared validation logic and improved test coverage, with Junio Hamano suggesting experimental merging to 'next' for wider testing. While the core functionality is now in good shape, open questions remain about multi-branch commit handling and documentation of the command's experimental status.
