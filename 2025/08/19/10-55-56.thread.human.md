Patrick Steinhardt has proposed a new `git-history` command with subcommands for more flexible history operations (`drop`, `reorder`, and the newly added `split`), inspired by Jujutsu's workflow. The RFC series is now complete at v1 with thirteen patches, including preparatory refactoring of sequencer internals and the `add-patch` subsystem. While the technical implementation appears solid with extensive test coverage, some limitations remain around merge commit support and conflict handling.

D. Ben Knoble has acknowledged the series with interest, though encountered email delivery issues missing patches 8-11. This indicates the proposal is reaching reviewers, who will need to retrieve the complete series from archives to provide substantive feedback. The thread remains in early discussion stages awaiting technical reviews of the implementation details.
