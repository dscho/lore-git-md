This thread proposes a new `git-history` command with `reword` and `split` subcommands, building on Git's replay machinery while drawing inspiration from Jujutsu's history editing capabilities. Patrick Steinhardt has led the effort through ten iterations of the patch series, with significant input from merge machinery expert Elijah Newren.

The v10 series has reached consensus on all major technical points, including the scope (local branches only), performance considerations, and conflict reporting design. Newren has given his final technical sign-off after Patrick addressed his remaining concerns - notably standardizing conflict return codes to use `1` (matching Git's merge conventions) and removing an unused `merge_conflict` field from the `replay_result` struct. The series now includes comprehensive test coverage (47 new tests) and supports detached HEAD operations.

Junio Hamano has been involved throughout, suggesting simplifications to the core replay logic. The implementation moves 369 lines of replay functionality into libgit.a as shared infrastructure, with the command itself focusing on non-conflicting operations first. While some option validation cleanup remains for future work, the current series appears technically complete and ready for maintainer evaluation.
