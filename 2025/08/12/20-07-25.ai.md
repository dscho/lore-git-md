This is the first patch in a documentation series aimed at improving the `git-add` man page, specifically focusing on simplifying the SYNOPSIS section. The author, Julia Evans, proposes removing the exhaustive list of options from the SYNOPSIS, replacing it with a more concise `[<options>]` placeholder.

The patch makes a focused change to Documentation/git-add.adoc, reducing the SYNOPSIS from 5 lines to just 1. The rationale is threefold: (1) long option lists overwhelm new users and obscure the basic command syntax, (2) most options are independently usable and well-documented in the OPTIONS section anyway, and (3) this approach aligns with other Git commands like `git rebase` and `git status`.

The author acknowledges that while some options are mutually exclusive (like the --all/-A/--update group), the current presentation is already hard to parse. This change appears well-motivated and follows existing patterns in Git's documentation, though as noted in the thread context, the SYNOPSIS restructuring is flagged as an area needing review. The patch is cleanly focused on its stated goal without introducing new terminology or collateral changes.
