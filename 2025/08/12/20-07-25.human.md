This is the first patch in a documentation series aimed at improving the `git-add` man page. It focuses on simplifying the SYNOPSIS section by removing the exhaustive list of options, replacing them with a generic `[<options>]` placeholder. The author, Julia Evans, provides three motivations: reducing visual clutter for newcomers, noting that mutually exclusive options are already hard to parse in their current form, and pointing to precedent in other Git commands like `git rebase`.

The change is straightforward - a 5-line reduction in the SYNOPSIS that removes all specific options while maintaining the basic command structure. The author acknowledges this might be controversial and is explicitly seeking feedback on the approach. This appears to be a well-reasoned change that aligns with the series' goal of making the documentation more accessible, though its success will depend on community consensus about how much detail belongs in the SYNOPSIS versus the OPTIONS section.
