This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans' series improving the git-rebase man page. It consolidates two separate explanations of merge conflict resolution into a single, clearer section near the top of the document.

The patch removes 29 lines of redundant text while preserving all key information about handling conflicts during rebase. The new unified explanation appears right after the initial rebase example, structured as a numbered list of three options: continue (after resolving), abort, or skip. The removed content included a duplicate explanation later in the file and a more verbose version in the "DESCRIPTION" section.

This change follows the series' goal of reducing duplication and improving information flow in the documentation. The conflict resolution instructions are now more discoverable by appearing earlier, while maintaining technical accuracy about the rebase process and commands (`git add`, `git rebase --continue/--abort/--skip`). The patch is part of the final v7 series that was already merged after addressing Junio Hamano's technical review.
