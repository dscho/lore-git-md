Maintainer feedback on git-add documentation simplification. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans' first patch in her series that removes detailed options from the SYNOPSIS section of git-add's man page.

He agrees the simplification makes sense for `git add` since it has a single mode of operation, but cautions this approach wouldn't work for commands with multiple modes like `git branch`. He suggests looking at `git branch -h` output as a balanced model and mentions the ongoing t0450 test effort to align command help and man page synopses.

The maintainer acknowledges the challenge of presenting mutually exclusive options clearly, agreeing the current format is hard to parse. He proposes an alternative approach - keeping the options but formatting them more cleanly with line breaks and simplified option presentation (showing just short forms where possible), citing `git-commit.adoc` as a better model.

This feedback indicates the maintainer is generally supportive of the documentation improvements but wants to ensure the changes consider both new and experienced users, and maintain consistency with other commands. The response suggests the patch may need revision to find a middle ground between simplicity and completeness.
