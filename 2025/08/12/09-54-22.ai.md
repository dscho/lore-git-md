This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v3 series (8/8) improving reftable/libgit2 compatibility, focusing on error handling and concurrent write safety in the reftable backend.

The patch addresses a race condition in stack modification by making `REFTABLE_STACK_NEW_ADDITION_RELOAD` mandatory for all operations that write to the reftable stack. Previously, some operations could fail unnecessarily when concurrent writes occurred, even though Git's locking mechanism already protects against actual reference conflicts. The change ensures consistent behavior across all stack-modifying operations while maintaining safety checks for reference state conflicts.

Key technical changes include:
- Removal of a redundant manual `reftable_stack_reload()` call in `prepare_transaction_update()`
- Addition of `REFTABLE_STACK_NEW_ADDITION_RELOAD` flag to all remaining operations: `rename_ref`, `copy_ref`, `create_reflog`, `delete_reflog`, and `reflog_expire`
- Reordering of operations in `reflog_expire` to create the addition before initializing the iterator, fixing a potential race
- The changes affect `refs/reftable-backend.c` with 12 insertions and 11 deletions

The patch completes the series' work on making reftable operations robust against concurrent modifications while maintaining Git's safety guarantees. It follows the pattern established in earlier patches of explicit state management and careful error handling. The implementation appears thorough, with all stack-modifying operations now consistently handling concurrent writes.
