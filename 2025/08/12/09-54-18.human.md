This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable/libgit2 compatibility series (v3 4/8). It addresses a compiler warning about brace initialization in older toolchains by replacing a `{0}` macro with explicit `memset()` initialization in the reftable stack code.

The change removes the `REFTABLE_ADDITION_INIT` macro and moves initialization into `reftable_stack_init_addition()`, which callers were already required to use. This both silences the warning and simplifies the calling convention. The patch references prior discussion about similar compiler warnings in Git's codebase, where the consensus was to avoid workarounds for old compilers when cleaner solutions exist.

The technical change is straightforward - replacing macro-based initialization with `memset()` in three locations while maintaining identical behavior. This continues the series' trend of improving code quality and portability while removing Git-specific patterns. The patch is small (14 lines changed) and focused, with no apparent downsides to the approach.
