Maintainer feedback on terminology documentation patch in git-add man page series.

Junio Hamano responds to Chris Torek's observation about the `--index` vs `--cached` flag behavior in `git apply`, agreeing this is an important edge case that shouldn't be glossed over in the proposed terminology note. While supporting the general idea of explaining the index/cache/staging area equivalence, Junio argues the current wording in patch 5/5 is misleading by implying all these flags always mean the same thing.

The key technical point is that `--cached` typically means "index only" while `--index` can mean "both index and working tree" in commands like `git apply`. Junio suggests either removing the "Many commands have flags..." sentence entirely (since it's not strictly necessary for understanding `git add`) or being more precise about the flag behavior differences. He notes this isn't just about `git apply` being a current exception - the flag distinction could reasonably appear in other commands too.

This continues the thread's theme of balancing clarity with technical accuracy in documentation. Junio's feedback suggests the terminology note needs refinement to properly handle edge cases while still serving its educational purpose. The patch series appears otherwise complete, with this being one of the final points to resolve before potential merging.
