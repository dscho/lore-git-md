This is the final patch in Julia Evans' documentation series improving the git-add man page, specifically addressing the terminology note about "index"/"cache"/"staging area" usage. The patch responds to feedback from Junio Hamano and Chris Torek about accurately representing the relationship between these terms and their corresponding flags.

The key technical point revolves around clarifying that while the terms are generally interchangeable, the flags `--cached` and `--index` can have distinct meanings in some commands (notably `git apply`). Junio provides detailed rationale for why the original wording suggesting flag equivalence was problematic, noting that `--index` may indicate operations affecting both index and working tree, while `--cached` typically refers to index-only operations.

Julia agrees to remove the oversimplified second sentence about flag equivalence while keeping the core terminology explanation. The discussion surfaces important documentation philosophy questions about balancing historical accuracy with pedagogical clarity, particularly around edge cases that might confuse beginners. This final patch wraps up the series by incorporating maintainer feedback to ensure technical precision in the terminology section while maintaining the overall goal of making the documentation more accessible.
