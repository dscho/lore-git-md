This is the final patch in Julia Evans' documentation series improving the `git-add` man page, specifically addressing the inconsistent terminology around the index/staging area. The discussion reveals an important nuance: while most commands treat flags like `--staged`, `--index`, and `--cached` as referring to the index, `git apply` is a notable exception where `--index` and `--cached` have distinct meanings. Maintainer Junio Hamano suggests removing an oversimplified statement about flag equivalence, arguing it could mislead users. Julia agrees to remove the problematic sentence while keeping the core terminology explanation.
