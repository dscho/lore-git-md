This is a documentation patch that improves the git-rebase man page by adding an introductory example. It's part of a larger series (1/5) that was ultimately merged after several iterations of review.

The patch moves the rebase example to the very beginning of the DESCRIPTION section, mirroring the approach used in git-merge's documentation. This makes the core concept immediately clear to readers by showing a concrete scenario where rebasing would be useful (moving commits A-B-C onto an updated master branch). The example is followed by a clear visualization of the before/after state.

The patch also adds a brief mention of rebase's ability to reorder or combine commits, pointing readers to the INTERACTIVE MODE section for details. The original example text is removed from later in the document to avoid duplication.

This change represents a thoughtful improvement to the documentation's pedagogical approach - leading with a practical example before diving into syntax details. The patch is cleanly implemented and fits well with the series' goal of making rebase documentation more approachable.
