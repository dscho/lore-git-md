Documentation patch reorganizing git-rebase's `--onto` explanation. This is part of Julia Evans' series to improve the git-rebase man page's structure and flow, now in its final v7 iteration.

The patch moves the detailed explanation and examples of `git rebase --onto` from the middle of the DESCRIPTION section to a new dedicated "TRANSPLANTING A TOPIC BRANCH WITH --ONTO" section near the end of the document. This makes the documentation more navigable by giving the `--onto` discussion a clear home that can be referenced from the options section. The content itself remains unchanged - it still covers the same three use cases with their ASCII-art commit graphs and command examples: transplanting a topic branch to a new base, rebasing part of a branch, and removing a range of commits.

This follows the series' overall goal of reducing duplication and improving information flow in the rebase documentation. The change was reviewed and merged after addressing Junio Hamano's technical feedback in earlier rounds. The patch is cleanly focused - it just relocates 87 lines of content while adding a reference from the options section to the new location.
