This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable/libgit2 compatibility series. It adds a flags parameter to `reftable_stack_add()` to support future functionality, particularly for libgit2 integration where automatic stack reloading may be needed when modifications occur before locking.

The change is straightforward - it enables future patches in the series to pass flags through this interface. The commit message notes this won't be used immediately in Git's codebase but will be important for libgit2's needs. Justin Tobler points out in the reply that the series does actually use this flag field in later patches.

This appears to be a well-contained change that follows the series' pattern of making the reftable stack operations more robust and flexible. The patch is small (just adding the flags parameter and passing it through) and clearly motivated by the series' broader goals of improving reftable's standalone usability.
