It also fixes a subtle ordering issue in reflog expiration where the log iterator was being created before acquiring the lock.

The changes are relatively straightforward - mostly adding the reload flag to existing calls - but they complete the series' goal of robust concurrent write handling. The patch removes one redundant stack reload that is now handled automatically by the flag.
