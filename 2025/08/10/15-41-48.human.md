This is a follow-up discussion on the already-merged documentation patch series that reorganized the `git-rebase` man page. Phillip Wood is responding to the first patch in the series, which moved the basic rebase example to the start of the documentation.

Phillip acknowledges the improvement in making rebase's core functionality clearer but suggests adding a mention of other important rebase uses (like rearranging commits and squashing fixups) early in the documentation, with a link to the INTERACTIVE MODE section. This would help readers understand rebase's full capabilities beyond just changing a branch's base.

The original patch being discussed was part of Julia Evans' successful series that restructured the rebase documentation for better accessibility while preserving all technical content. All five patches were merged after incorporating feedback, with this being a retrospective suggestion for potential future improvements. The series is notable for its model collaboration between a first-time contributor and maintainers, resulting in comprehensive documentation improvements.
