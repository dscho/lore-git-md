This is a cover letter for a 2-patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting `fmt-merge-msg`. Ayush Chandekar presents version 2 of their work to eliminate global variables from the merge message formatting code, addressing feedback from Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood.

The series aims to remove two remaining globals: `merge_log_config` (patch 1) and `the_repository` usage (patch 2). The changes span several files including the fmt-merge-msg implementation, merge command, and environment handling. A new test ensures `git fmt-merge-msg -h` works outside a repository.

Key improvements in v2 include switching from `git_config()` to `repo_config()` as requested, though the diff shows some unresolved conflicts in the implementation (notably around `adjust_shortlog_len()` handling). The cover letter acknowledges the strict requirements around maintaining existing behavior for deprecated features like `merge.summary` precedence.

This appears to be a technically sound effort that's progressing through careful iteration. The remaining challenges center on perfectly preserving all edge cases while removing the globals - a delicate balance that's typical for `the_repository` removal work. The next version will need to fully resolve the config handling to satisfy the maintainers' non-negotiable compatibility requirements.
