Bugfix discussion for `git-jump`'s filename handling. Phillip Wood follows up on the thread about fixing filenames with spaces in the `git-jump` contrib script, noting an important detail about Git's filename quoting behavior. He points out that filenames containing literal tabs would actually be quoted in the diff output (e.g., `"foo\t"`), meaning the current proposed solution of unconditionally stripping trailing tabs wouldn't create ambiguity after all - the quoted tabs would remain distinct from the unquoted delimiter tab.

This observation strengthens the case for the simple regex solution currently under consideration, as it would now handle all three cases correctly: (1) filenames with spaces, (2) filenames with literal tabs, and (3) the combination case. Phillip also notes that while `git-jump` currently doesn't handle quoted filenames, adding support would be straightforward using Git.pm's unquoting functionality if needed.

The email reinforces the thread's earlier conclusion that parsing the "diff --git" line (while more comprehensive) may be unnecessary complexity for this particular fix, given that the simple approach now appears to cover all practical cases without ambiguity. The discussion remains focused on balancing correctness with maintainability for this contrib script.
