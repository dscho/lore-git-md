This is the final patch in a performance optimization and refactoring series for Git's line-log functionality (`git log -L`). The patch focuses on a small cleanup in `process_ranges_arbitrary_commit()` that reorders a merge commit check to make the logic more straightforward.

The change flips a condition that previously checked "is this not a merge commit?" (via a compound condition testing for root or single-parent commits) to instead directly ask "is this a merge commit?" (checking for multiple parents). While Junio Hamano noted the original wasn't overly complicated, Derrick Stolee agrees with the author that the new formulation reads more naturally, though acknowledges this is somewhat subjective.

This patch is the smallest in the series (just 3 lines changed) and comes after more substantial performance improvements and refactorings in earlier patches. The series has already received positive reviews from both Stolee (who confirmed the performance gains hold across different repository configurations) and Junio, suggesting it's likely to be accepted. While this final change is minor, it aligns with the series' goal of improving code clarity in the line-log implementation.
