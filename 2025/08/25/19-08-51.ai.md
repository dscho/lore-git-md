This is the third patch in a documentation series improving the `git-checkout` man page. It focuses on removing potentially confusing "reset" terminology from the branch creation/update explanation.

The patch makes two key changes to the `-B` option description:
1. Replaces "otherwise, it is reset" with a more explicit "otherwise `<new-branch>` is forced to point at the commit"
2. Simplifies the transactional behavior explanation by removing reset terminology entirely, instead saying "the branch will not be created or modified unless `git checkout` is successful"

The changes maintain technical accuracy while making the text more accessible to users unfamiliar with Git's "reset" concept. The patch continues the series' pattern of net-zero line changes (5 added, 6 removed) and maintains consistency with the overall goal of clarifying checkout behavior without altering functionality.

This change addresses a specific pain point identified in user feedback that motivated the series - the term "reset" was causing confusion for some experienced users. The new wording better aligns with the pedagogical approach seen in earlier patches, favoring plain language explanations over Git-specific jargon when possible.
