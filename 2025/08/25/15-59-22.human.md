Maintainer follow-up on `git whatchanged` replacement discussion.

Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's technical analysis of the differences between `git whatchanged` and its proposed `git log` replacement. He acknowledges that while `git log` shows empty commits (due to the `revs.always_show_header` flag) and `git whatchanged` does not, this difference is contextually appropriate for each command's purpose. 

Hamano emphasizes that byte-for-byte identical output isn't necessary for the migration, though he admits his perspective may be biased as maintainer. The discussion remains focused on whether the additional timestamps from empty commits would meaningfully impact Dan Allen's filesystem mtime synchronization use case, but no new technical solutions or changes to Git are proposed here. This appears to be winding down the thread with an implicit endorsement of the `git log --raw --pretty=%at` replacement despite the minor behavioral differences.
