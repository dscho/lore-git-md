Feature patch adding a short option for `git pull --set-upstream`.

Julien Jerphanion proposes adding `-u` as a short form for `--set-upstream` in `git pull`, matching the existing short option in `git push`. This provides consistency between the two commands and offers users a more concise way to set upstream branches during pulls.

The change is minimal - just adding the `-u` flag to the option parsing in `builtin/pull.c` - and comes with a test verifying the new short form works identically to the long form. The patch appears straightforward and well-tested, addressing a small but legitimate usability improvement.

This seems like a reasonable quality-of-life change that maintains consistency with `git push`'s behavior. The implementation is cleanly done with proper test coverage, making it likely to be uncontroversial. Users who frequently set upstream branches during pulls will appreciate the shorter syntax.
