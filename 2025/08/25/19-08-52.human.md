This is the fourth patch in a documentation series improving the `git-checkout` man page. It focuses on deduplicating the explanation of the `--detach` option.

The patch simplifies the `--detach` section by removing redundant text that was already covered in the branch switching description. Instead of repeating details about working tree behavior, it now concisely states that `--detach` works like a normal checkout but points HEAD directly at the commit rather than the branch. The change maintains all the same information while being more direct and avoiding repetition.

This continues the series-wide approach of making the documentation more concise and focused, with careful attention to eliminating redundancy while preserving technical accuracy. The change is small (3 lines added, 10 removed) but improves readability by reducing cognitive load when reading about detached HEAD states.
