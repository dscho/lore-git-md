This is a review comment on patch 2/16 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to migrate packfile management to `struct packfile_store`. Taylor Blau appreciates the incremental approach but raises a potential footgun with the new packfile_store implementation.

Taylor notes that in the original code, `odb_clear()` would both close and free packfiles while nulling out the list. The new implementation splits this between `packfile_store_close()` and `packfile_store_free()`, but leaves dangling pointers in the store object after freeing. He suggests two solutions: either modify `packfile_store_free()` to also null out the parent pointer, or document that callers must null the pointer themselves.

The review also points out some minor readability concerns with the new chained accessors (`current->repo->objects->packfiles->packs`) and suggests renaming `packs` to something more descriptive like `head`. Taylor confirms the correctness of the packfile closing/freeing logic but notes the double-close in `packfile_store_free()` is unnecessary since `close_object_store()` is already called first.

Overall this is constructive feedback on an otherwise mechanical change, focusing on potential pitfalls in the new abstraction's design. The series continues to move forward with careful review from experienced contributors.
