This is a positive review of a performance optimization and refactoring series for Git's line-log functionality. Derrick Stolee is responding to SZEDER Gábor's 4-patch series that improves merge commit handling in `git log -L`.

The series combines one performance optimization (avoiding unnecessary tree diffs when processing merge commits) with three code cleanups in the same area. Stolee had previously verified the performance claims in the first patch, which showed significant speedups (2.25x in git.git, 1.32x in linux.git) by restructuring merge commit processing to combine two passes into one.

The remaining patches are subjective but welcome improvements: eliminating a dynamically allocated array, fixing initialization of a diff queue, and simplifying control flow. Stolee endorses the entire series as "excellent" without suggesting any changes, indicating these are clean, well-motivated improvements to the line-log internals. The changes are purely internal with no user-facing behavior modifications.
