Julia Evans' documentation series for `git-checkout.adoc` has reached its final form after four iterations, with the v4 patches now receiving only minor polish feedback. The series comprehensively documents `git checkout`'s dual behaviors (branch switching and file restoration) while making the content more accessible to users without sacrificing technical precision.

The latest feedback comes from D. Ben Knoble, who praises the successful reduction of jargon (like "pathspec" and "tree-ish") but notes a potential tension between accessibility and completeness. He suggests that after merging, he may propose adding some references to advanced features like pathspecs in the glossary, though he considers this a minor post-merge consideration rather than a blocking issue.

The series has benefited from thorough review throughout its development, with Junio Hamano ensuring key terminology like "index" was preserved, and multiple contributors helping refine the balance between technical accuracy and readability. The final version clarifies complex concepts like merge conflict resolution, detached HEAD behavior, and file restoration mechanics while maintaining all the necessary precision about Git's internals. With all substantive technical questions resolved in earlier versions, this now stands as a model documentation improvement that serves both new and experienced users well.
