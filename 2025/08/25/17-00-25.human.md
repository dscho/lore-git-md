Performance optimization discussion in progress meter signal handling.

This email from Junio Hamano continues the thread about modernizing Git's progress meter signaling, specifically responding to Johannes Sixt's patch that fixed the GIT_PROGRESS_DELAY functionality. The discussion has moved from the initial bugfix to refining implementation details.

Hamano agrees with Sixt's approach of documenting the established 1-second delay behavior, noting it makes sense given the community's comfort with the current behavior. Despite this minor stylistic point, he approves of the core approach of clearing the global `progress_update` flag while preserving its state for the rest of the function's logic.
