**Thread Summary:**

This thread addresses a corruption issue in `git archive` when creating ZIP files containing large files (exceeding `core.bigFileThreshold`), which could result in deflate errors or bad CRC checksums. Justin Tobler initially diagnosed the problem in `archive-zip.c`, identifying improper zlib compression loop handling. Toon Claes has led the effort to fix this with a 4-patch series that restructures zlib usage to follow proper patterns with correct `git_deflate()` looping.

The series has progressed through careful review, primarily from René Scharfe, who has provided consistent and thorough feedback. The first three patches have already been approved: 
- Patch 1 simplified buffer management in `write_zip_entry()`
- Patch 2 removed an unnecessary conditional around `write_or_die()`
- Patch 3 refactored the zlib compression with a proper `do-while` loop

The final patch (Patch 4) moves the Z_FINISH flush into the main deflation loop, completing the solution. Scharfe's latest review notes a few remaining considerations: maintaining consistency in zero-length checks, avoiding redundant Z_NO_FLUSH assignments, and ensuring proper termination on Z_STREAM_END to comply with zlib's API. These are the last remaining issues before the series can be considered complete.

The technical focus remains on `archive-zip.c`'s compression path, with all changes improving both correctness and maintainability. Testing against the chromium.git reproduction case has been successful. With Scharfe's approval of the first three patches and only minor adjustments needed for the fourth, the series appears ready for finalization and merging.
