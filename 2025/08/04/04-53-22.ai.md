This is a maintainer response in the ongoing feature series about expanding `-` option availability. Junio C Hamano acknowledges the core improvement of making `--help-all` work outside repositories, which aligns the behavior with `-h` for parse-options commands. However, he expresses surprise and concern about the revived discussion of allowing `-h` with other arguments, which had previously been rejected due to technical and usability concerns.

The email clarifies that the current series does not actually reintroduce argument-tolerant `-h` behavior (despite ambiguous phrasing in the cover letter), but maintains the existing special cases for `ls-remote` and `grep`. Hamano provides concrete examples (`git add -h foo`) to illustrate why interpreting `-` mid-command would create confusing output that ignores the additional arguments.

Key technical points reinforced:
- The distinction between `-h` and `--help-all` for parse-options commands should be consistent regarding repository context
- Special handling for `ls-remote` and `grep` remains unchanged
- Argument-tolerant `-h` behavior would require fundamentally different help system design

This email serves as final confirmation that the controversial `-h` mid-command feature remains shelved, while approving the uncontroversial parts of the series (repository-less `--help-all` and test fixes). The response indicates the series is ready for merging once this clarification about scope is acknowledged.
