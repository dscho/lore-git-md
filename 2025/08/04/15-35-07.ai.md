Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's analysis of wildcard matching behavior in the `git refs list` implementation, which inherits its pattern matching from `git for-each-ref`. This is part of the ongoing discussion about the `git refs list` series that aims to provide a thin wrapper around `for-each-ref` functionality.

Key points in this email:
- Confirms the current behavior where `**` not between slashes (like `refs/heads/m**`) is silently converted to `*`, matching only top-level branches starting with 'm'
- Notes Phillip's discovery that comprehensive matching requires using both `refs/heads/m*` and `refs/heads/m*/**` patterns
- Expresses acceptance of the current rules once understood, despite their non-intuitive aspects
- Agrees with Phillip's conclusion that while the behavior could be improved, maintaining compatibility with `for-each-ref` is preferable
- Effectively closes the wildcard behavior discussion by affirming the status quo

This email represents the maintainer's final position on the wildcard matching question, settling the last open design question in the series. The discussion documents the limitations of the current approach while confirming the decision to maintain compatibility with `for-each-ref`'s existing behavior.
