Compiler warning fixes for remote.c and clar.c addressing -Wmaybe-uninitialized under GCC -Og, split into two patches in v2. Denton Liu's clar.c patch initializes `matchlen=0` when no filter is provided, suppressing the warning with a logical default (zero matched bytes) that Patrick Steinhardt may consider for libgit2.

Technical details: The remote.c changes enforce argument validation before option handling in set_head(), removing the conditional block around `remote_get()`. The clar.c initialization (from libgit2) handles a test framework edge case. Both changes are behavior-neutral - remote.c restructures validation logic while preserving functionality, and clar.c adds a harmless default. The v2 split assigns ownership clearly: Peff for remote.c refactoring, Liu for clar.c initialization.
