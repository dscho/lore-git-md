Maintainer feedback on wildcard behavior in ref listing commands.

Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's analysis of wildcard matching behavior in `git for-each-ref` and the new `git refs list` command. The discussion clarifies that `**` in refspecs is silently converted to `*` unless it appears between slashes (as `/**/`), which maintains compatibility with existing `for-each-ref` behavior but creates some inconvenience for users wanting recursive matching.

Hamano expresses satisfaction with the current rules once understood, noting they are serviceable despite not ideal. He agrees with Wood's assessment that while the behavior could be more intuitive, the existing implementation works and doesn't require changes at this time. This confirms the design decision to maintain compatibility in the new `refs list` command rather than introducing new wildcard semantics.

The exchange serves as useful documentation of the edge case behavior for future reference, while reinforcing that the series can proceed with its current approach to wildcard handling. No changes to the patch series are suggested based on this discussion.
