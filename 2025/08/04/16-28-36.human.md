Maintainer input on git mv --after proposal

Junio Hamano, Git's maintainer, weighs in on the discussion about adding a `--after` flag to `git mv`. His brief reply serves as an implicit endorsement of the technical objections raised earlier in the thread.

Hamano thanks Kristoffer Haugsbakk for providing a link to Linus Torvalds' 2005 email explaining Git's design philosophy around rename detection, noting this saves him from having to do so himself. This suggests the maintainer views Torvalds' original explanation as the definitive answer to why Git handles renames heuristically rather than explicitly tracking them.

The response carries significant weight as it comes from the project's BDFL and effectively closes the door on the proposed feature. While Hamano doesn't explicitly reject the idea, his endorsement of the historical documentation and lack of any counter-suggestion implies agreement with the established design. The thread appears to have reached its natural conclusion with this maintainer input, confirming Git's rename detection will remain heuristic-based rather than adopting Mercurial's explicit tracking model.
