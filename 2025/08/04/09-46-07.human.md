This is a bugfix patch in Patrick Steinhardt's reflog migration series, addressing a race condition in HEAD reflog generation. The patch focuses on the "files" backend's handling of reflog entries when HEAD points to a reference being updated.

The core issue is that `split_head_update()` currently reads HEAD at transaction start but doesn't verify its state after locking, leading to two race scenarios: missing reflog entries when HEAD moves to a processed reference, or creating incorrect entries when HEAD moves away. The solution introduces a `REF_LOG_VIA_SPLIT` flag that triggers post-lock verification of HEAD's target, though it only fully fixes the second race scenario.

The implementation adds careful handling of the parent-child relationship between updates, including proper old-oid propagation and transaction cleanup when races are detected. While not eliminating all possible races (some would require impractical locking strategies), this represents a significant improvement in reflog consistency during concurrent operations.

This is the 7th patch in a 9-part series that's been through multiple rounds of review, with this particular race condition being identified during earlier discussion. The patch shows careful consideration of edge cases and maintains the series' high standard of technical precision.
