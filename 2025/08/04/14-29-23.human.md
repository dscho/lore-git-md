This is a status update on two ref-related patch series being prepared for the Git 2.51-rc0 release. Maintainer Junio Hamano notes some confusion in Patrick Steinhardt's response about which of his two series have final updates ready for merging.

The `ps/remote-rename-fix` series (7 commits) improves `git remote rename` behavior for unborn HEAD refs and optimizes ref iteration during renames. The `ps/reflog-migrate-fixes` series (9 commits) fixes several bugs in reflog migration between ref backends. Both have undergone multiple iterations and appear technically sound.

Patrick indicates both series are ready for merging, with `ps/remote-rename-fix` awaiting final approval from Jeff King. However, Junio observes that Patrick's comments about version updates may have been accidentally swapped between the two series in his response. The exchange suggests these are the final steps before these ref-handling improvements can land in the next release candidate.
