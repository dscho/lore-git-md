This is a follow-up on the 3-patch series introducing a `--max-depth` option for Git's diff machinery, now in its second version. The series adds depth-limiting functionality similar to `git grep --max-depth`, stopping recursive tree traversal at a specified depth from pathspec matches.

Junio Hamano is checking in on the status of the series after it received no comments on v2, suggesting it might be ready for inclusion in the next release cycle. The v2 updates addressed previous feedback with several improvements: added unit tests for the `within_depth()` function, made error messages translatable, improved documentation, and added const-correctness. The series also now supports `--max-depth=-1` for unlimited depth.

The patches appear well-tested with comprehensive unit and integration tests, and have already gone through review cycles with Patrick Steinhardt validating the core logic. The changes look polished and ready for consideration, with the main author (Jeff King) still credited via "Based-on-patch-by" trailers where appropriate.
