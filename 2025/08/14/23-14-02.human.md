This is a follow-up discussion in the `git-jump` bugfix thread about handling filenames with spaces in diff output. The patch itself has already been agreed upon (v2 with all reviewer approvals), but this email explores some edge cases around filename quoting.

Jeff King confirms that `git-jump` currently doesn't handle quoted filenames at all, which would be needed for paths containing control characters like tabs. He explains his original choice to use the "+++" line from diffs was specifically to avoid quoting complexities, though this creates a limitation for rare cases.

The discussion weighs potential solutions (like using Git.pm for unquoting) against their tradeoffs, ultimately concluding that supporting space-containing filenames (the common case) while leaving quoted paths unsupported is a reasonable compromise. Jeff also tests Vim's quickfix parsing capabilities and finds they don't automatically handle backslash-escaped quotes either.

This is mostly closing out loose ends after the main fix - the practical solution for space-containing filenames is already settled, and this just documents why certain edge cases remain intentionally unsupported. The thread shows good technical diligence in exploring the boundaries of the solution.
