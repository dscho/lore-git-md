Documentation refinement discussion for `git push --mirror` behavior.

Junio Hamano responds to Gustavo Velasco-Hernández's patch that clarified how `--mirror` handles remote-only refs. While agreeing with the technical correctness, Junio suggests the current approach of enumerating cases may not be the most robust way to document the behavior. He proposes instead a more comprehensive description that captures the mirroring operation's essence in three clear actions: removing remote-only refs, force-updating diverged refs, and adding local-only refs.

The discussion shows the maintainer's preference for documentation that explains concepts holistically rather than through case enumeration. This is a higher standard than just fixing an omission, aiming for clarity that prevents future misunderstandings. The patch author now has clear guidance to either adjust their approach or justify the case-based documentation style.

This exchange demonstrates Git's documentation review process in action - catching not just correctness but also quality of explanation, with maintainers providing concrete suggestions for improvement while acknowledging the value of the original contribution.
