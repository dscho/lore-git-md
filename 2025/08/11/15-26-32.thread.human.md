The core fix, now agreed upon by all participants, dynamically determines abbreviation length based on the repository's hash algorithm (SHA-1 or SHA-256) rather than using fixed constants.

René Scharfe then proposed a refinement to simplify the logic in the helper function, which Junio further improved by completely removing redundant length checks.
