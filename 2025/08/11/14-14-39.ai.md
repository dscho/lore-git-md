This is a performance optimization discussion in the ongoing xdiff string hashing thread. Alexander Monakov responds to Phillip Wood's benchmarking results and questions about the proposed hash optimization.

- Explains why atomic_signal_fence is insufficient for their needs compared to the assembly-based REASSOC_FENCE
- Requests Phillip's GCC version and prefers perf stat over hyperfine for more comparable cycle measurements

The discussion remains focused on validating the real-world performance impact of the hash optimization, with Monakov maintaining that the assembly optimization should help even on modern CPUs without specific architecture tuning.
