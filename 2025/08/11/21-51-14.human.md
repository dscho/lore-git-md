This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans' series to improve the git-rebase man page. It's the fourth of five patches in the final version of the series that has already been merged.

The patch moves the detailed explanation and examples of `--onto` usage from the middle of the long DESCRIPTION section to its own clearly labeled section near the end of the document. This makes the information easier to find and reference, particularly since the OPTIONS section can now point to it by name. The content itself remains unchanged - it still shows the same three clear examples of using `--onto` to transplant topic branches, rebase part of a branch, and remove problematic commits.

This is part of the series' overall goal to make the rebase documentation more approachable by improving its structure. The change follows through on the earlier work to reduce duplication and improve information organization, while preserving all the technical content. The patch is well-executed, maintaining all examples and just reorganizing them for better discoverability.
