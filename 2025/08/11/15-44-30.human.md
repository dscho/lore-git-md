This is a follow-up discussion in the `git repo info` command series, specifically addressing the output sorting behavior of the `references.format` field. Junio Hamano is responding to Phillip Wood's critique of the lexicographical sorting design choice.

Phillip Wood had raised concerns in a previous email about the current implementation's decision to sort output keys lexicographically, arguing that this makes script parsing unnecessarily complex. He points out that maintaining input order would be more user-friendly for scripting purposes, since scripts could then rely on positional output matching their input order. He also notes that the original justification (JSON output deduplication) no longer relevant, and deduplication could be handled more simply without sorting.

Junio Hamano concurs with Phillip's assessment, calling it "very good" feedback. This suggests the maintainers may reconsider the sorting behavior before finalizing the series, though no specific decision is stated in this email. The discussion represents one of the final design questions remaining in this otherwise merge-ready series.
