This thread documents an improvement to the `git push --mirror` behavior description in the official documentation. Gustavo Velasco-Hernández initially identified that the docs omitted a key detail - that remote-only refs (not present locally) get deleted during mirror pushes. 

After Junio C Hamano suggested a more comprehensive rewrite, Gustavo submitted a v2 patch that systematically documents all three mirror operations: deletion of remote-only refs, force-updating diverged refs, and adding local-only refs. The technical content has now been approved by Junio, who called it "very clear."

The patch is nearly ready for merging, with just two administrative items to resolve: aligning Gustavo's email addresses between the From and Signed-off-by lines, and potentially replacing an informal "(Based on Junio feedback)" note with proper Helped-by attribution. Once these minor items are addressed, this documentation improvement will be complete, giving users a clearer understanding of mirror push behavior.
