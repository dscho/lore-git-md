This is a post-merge review comment on the final patch in Julia Evans' documentation restructuring series for `git-rebase`. Patrick Steinhardt provides two specific suggestions to improve the technical accuracy and clarity of the internals section.

First, he proposes converting the step-by-step description of rebase's operation into a bulleted list format to better highlight the multi-stage process. The current text already clearly separates the steps (identifying commits, checking out new base, replaying commits, updating branch), but visual formatting could make this even clearer for readers.

Second, he points out a potential contradiction in the description of `ORIG_HEAD` behavior and suggests rewording to clarify that while `ORIG_HEAD` is set at rebase start, it may be modified by other operations during the rebase. He also correctly notes that the term "pseudo-ref" is outdated terminology that was recently clarified in the documentation.

These are minor but thoughtful refinements to an already merged patch. Patrick's tone is constructive and his suggestions align with the series' goal of making the rebase documentation both more accessible and technically precise. The changes would not affect the behavior described, only its presentation and terminology.
