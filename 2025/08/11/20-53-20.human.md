This is the final patch in a 6-part documentation series introducing automated linting for Git's man pages. The patch adds enforcement for consistent backtick-wrapping of option names in synopsis-style documentation, completing the linter suite.

The change adds a 6-line addition to `lint-documentation-style.perl` to check that option names in definition lists are properly wrapped in backticks when using the synopsis style (e.g., converting `--option::` to `` `--option`:: ``). It fixes two files (`git-checkout.adoc` and `git-refs.adoc`) with 17 insertions and 11 deletions, standardizing the formatting of command names, options, and parameters.

This builds on Jean-Noël Avila's parallel effort to convert man pages to synopsis style, ensuring mechanical consistency in how options are presented. The linter will now catch cases where option names appear without backticks in synopsis sections. The patch has been through three iterations and appears ready for merging, completing the series' goal of establishing consistent documentation formatting standards.
