This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract Git's packfile management. It introduces `struct packfile_store` as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort.

The patch establishes the basic structure and initialization of the new packfile store, but as Junio Hamano notes in his review, the immediate benefits aren't yet visible - this is foundational work for later changes. Junio raises several points: he suggests renaming the field from `packfiles` to `packfile_store` for clarity, questions the circular reference between ODB and packfile_store, and notes the patch doesn't yet demonstrate concrete improvements.

Patrick responds that the naming was chosen for brevity but he's open to changing it, and clarifies that the circular reference is intentional (the store needs to know its parent ODB). He acknowledges this is primarily preparatory work to enable future refactoring of packfile management.

This exchange shows the careful balance required in such foundational changes - while the architectural direction has maintainer approval, the implementation details are being scrutinized for clarity and logical progression. The discussion remains constructive, with Junio probing for clearer naming and more obvious incremental value.
