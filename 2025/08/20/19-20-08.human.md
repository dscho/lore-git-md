This is a technical discussion about the packfile MRU list refactoring in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB abstraction series.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to Karthik Nayak's question about why the packfile store maintains both a regular list and an MRU (most-recently-used) list of packfiles. Peff explains this is historical - when he originally added the MRU optimization, he kept the original list order to avoid breaking any code that might depend on iteration order. He suggests it might now be safe to consolidate to just the MRU list, but cautions this should be done carefully to avoid issues with concurrent iteration and lookup operations.

The exchange shows careful consideration of backward compatibility during refactoring. Peff recommends handling any list consolidation as a follow-up to Patrick's current series, since the focus now is on the mechanical relocation of packfile state. This maintains the thread's pattern of incremental, reviewable changes while leaving optimization opportunities for later.
