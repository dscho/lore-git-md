Junio Hamano responds to Daniele Sassoli's concerns about documenting Git contribution workflows, maintaining his position that new contributors should be guided toward using `gitgitgadget/git` as their remote from the start. This is part of an ongoing discussion about how to best structure the `MyFirstContribution.adoc` documentation.

He points out that those who clone from `git/git` will need to learn extra steps to use GitGitGadget (GGG) and repeat those steps for every contribution. His primary concern is efficiency - he wants contributors spending time polishing their work rather than navigating workflow differences.

The response suggests Junio sees this as more than just documentation style; he's advocating for optimizing the contributor experience by reducing repetitive configuration work. While he states he has "no strong opinions," his preference for guiding users to `gitgitgadget/git` remains clear. The discussion now hinges on whether to maintain the current incremental approach (mentioning both remotes) or restructure the entire document to consistently use `gitgitgadget/git` from the beginning.
