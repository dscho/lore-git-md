This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. Patch 14/16 removes the redundant `get_packed_git()` function, consolidating packfile access through `get_all_packs()` instead.

Karthik Nayak asks for clarification about when a packfile might be indexed by a multi-pack-index but not returned by `get_packed_git()`. The patch explains that the distinction between these functions isn't actually necessary - all current callers can safely use `get_all_packs()`, which handles both regular packfiles and those referenced by MIDX.

The change affects three callers: `builtin/gc.c` (which checks packfiles not in MIDX), `builtin/grep.c` (which just needs packfiles loaded), and `object-name.c` (which already handles MIDX cases). The removal appears straightforward, though Karthik notes a minor wording nit in the commit message.

This continues the series' steady progress in simplifying the packfile interface while maintaining behavior. The question about MIDX edge cases suggests reviewers are carefully considering the changes, but there don't appear to be any serious objections to the approach.
