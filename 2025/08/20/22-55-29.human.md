Refactoring patch as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. This is the first of three patches to modernize the bulk-checkin subsystem by consolidating its global state.

The patch introduces a new `struct odb_transaction` to group together three previously separate global variables that tracked bulk-checkin state: the nesting level, temporary object directory, and packfile details. This is a preparatory step before later wiring the structure through the APIs rather than relying on globals.

The changes are mechanical - moving variable declarations into the new struct and updating all references to use the struct members instead. The patch touches only bulk-checkin.c, with 24 lines added and 22 removed. No behavior changes are introduced yet; this is purely structural preparation for the subsequent patches that will eliminate the remaining global state.

The work follows established patterns seen in other subsystem refactorings for `the_repository` removal. The changes appear straightforward and well-contained, with all affected call sites updated consistently. This looks like a clean first step in the series.
