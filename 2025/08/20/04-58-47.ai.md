This is patch 6 in Patrick Steinhardt's 16-part series refactoring Git's packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the "kept pack cache" from `struct object_database` to the new store structure, completing the encapsulation of all packfile-related state.

Junio Hamano questions whether this move should have been included in the initial patch (01/16) that created `packfile_store`, or if it makes sense here since it removes the last piece of packfile-specific private data from ODB. Patrick confirms this is the latter case - the comment about private data" becomes redundant only after this move, and he'll update the commit message to clarify.

The change is mechanical, moving `kept_pack_cache` from ODB to `packfile_store` while maintaining identical behavior. This continues the series' pattern of incrementally relocating packfile management components to their new home, with each patch focusing on one logical piece. The discussion highlights the careful consideration being given to patch organization within this large refactoring effort.
