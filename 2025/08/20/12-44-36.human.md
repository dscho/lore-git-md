This is a technical clarification request in the ongoing packfile refactoring series. Karthik Nayak is seeking to understand the relationship between two packfile lists being moved to the new `struct packfile_store` in Patrick Steinhardt's series.

The patch in question (05/16) moves the MRU (most-recently-used) list of packfiles from the object database to the new `packfile_store` structure. Karthik notes that there are now two representations of the packfiles in the store - the original `packs` list (moved in patch 02/16) and this new MRU list. He asks:

1. Whether the MRU list contains the same packfiles as the main `packs` list, just in a different order
2. Why both representations are needed rather than just using the MRU list
3. Whether the MRU ordering is purely an optimization for searches

This comes in the context of Patrick's large series to centralize packfile management in the new `packfile_store` structure. The question doesn't challenge the patch's correctness but seeks to understand the design rationale behind maintaining both list forms, which could help reviewers evaluate whether this is the right approach for the MRU functionality.
