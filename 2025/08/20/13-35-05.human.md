This is part 11 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The patch ensures packfiles are consistently added to the most-recently-used (MRU) list when they are first added to the store, rather than waiting until their objects are accessed. This eliminates an inconsistency where some subsystems were manually adding to the MRU while others weren't.

Karthik Nayak responds positively to the change, noting it makes sense to handle the MRU addition consistently. He suggests that ideally the MRU details would be completely hidden inside `packfile_store` rather than exposed to callers, seeing this as part of the broader direction toward better encapsulation of packfile internals.

The change appears straightforward and uncontroversial, continuing the series' mechanical refactoring while improving internal consistency. Karthik's comment about API encapsulation hints at potential future work to further hide implementation details, but doesn't raise any objections to the current patch.
