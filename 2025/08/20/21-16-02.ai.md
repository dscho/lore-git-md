The discussion hinges on whether the documentation should maintain its current structure (starting with `git/git` and explaining alternatives later) or be restructured to promote `gitgitgadget/git` as the primary path. The thread continues to explore the tradeoffs between documentation consistency and workflow efficiency for new contributors.
