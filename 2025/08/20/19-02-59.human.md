Bugfix for `git repack --path-walk` initialization issue. This is the second patch in a 3-part series addressing cases where singleton objects could be incorrectly omitted during repacking with sparse pack usage.

Junio Hamano confirms the fix's correctness while raising a thoughtful question about whether other index extensions (REUC and TREE) might need similar attention. The patch itself is straightforward - it properly initializes the `maybe_interesting` flag for tree and blob objects during path walking, which was missed in the original implementation.

The change is minimal (just two lines adding the flag initialization) and comes with a test that now passes, demonstrating the fix works. Hamano particularly praises the planned follow-up refactoring (coming in patch 3) that will prevent similar oversights by centralizing the initialization logic.

This appears to be a well-justified fix for a real issue, with good test coverage and maintainability improvements coming in the series. The discussion suggests the change is ready to proceed, though Hamano's question about other index extensions might warrant a brief response from the author.
