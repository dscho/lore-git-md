This is the second patch in Julia Evans' documentation series reorganizing the `git-rebase` man page, focusing on deduplicating the merge conflict resolution instructions.

The patch consolidates two separate explanations of how to handle merge conflicts during rebase into a single, clearer section near the top of the document. The new unified explanation keeps all three resolution options (`--continue`, `--abort`, and `--skip`) together with their context, removing about 30 lines of redundant text while preserving all the information. The placement early in the document follows the series' goal of front-loading practical information for newcomers.

This version incorporates feedback from Junio Hamano on the initial submission, particularly around clarifying the relationship between `git add` and `git rebase --continue`. The changes maintain technical accuracy while improving readability, continuing the series' pattern of mirroring the `git-merge` documentation structure. As with the rest of the series, this is purely a documentation improvement with no behavior changes.
