Julia Evans' documentation series improving the git-rebase man page has been successfully merged after several iterations. The goal was to make rebase's documentation more approachable through structural improvements—adding clear examples early, consolidating duplicate content, and improving overall information flow. The series went through multiple rounds of review, with Junio Hamano providing key technical feedback, particularly on the internals section, which was reworked into a clearer 5-step breakdown.

A notable post-merge discussion involved Karthik Nayak, Phillip Wood, and Ben Knoble regarding whether to use `git checkout` or `git switch` in examples. The consensus, reinforced by Junio, was to retain `git checkout` for detached HEAD operations due to its more straightforward behavior. The final version also includes refinements such as clarifying that rebase` uses `git patch-id` (not SHA) for duplicate commit detection, fixing line wrapping, and correcting a `git checkout` command from `-C` to `-B`.

All open questions have been resolved, and the series now provides a more navigable and technically accurate explanation of `git rebase`, aligning with the project's broader documentation style improvements.
