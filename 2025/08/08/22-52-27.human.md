This is the cover letter for the final version (v3) of Julia Evans' documentation patch series restructuring the `git-rebase` man page. The series has already been merged, with this version incorporating maintainer feedback on the last patch.

The series represents a comprehensive reorganization of the rebase documentation to make it more accessible to newcomers while preserving all technical content. The author has simplified technical language (avoiding terms like "detached HEAD") and incorporated Junio Hamano's suggestions about commit selection wording and step descriptions.

Notable changes in this version include removing duplicate explanations, consolidating the `ORIG_HEAD` discussion into a single note, and clarifying that rebase is conceptually similar to repeated cherry-picks while acknowledging there may be differences. The cover letter shows the author's thoughtful approach - they explicitly note where they've made judgment calls about technical accuracy versus accessibility.
