This is the fourth patch in a completed documentation series reorganizing the `git-rebase` man page. The patch moves the detailed explanation of `--onto` usage from the middle of the DESCRIPTION section to its own dedicated section near the end of the document.

The change is purely organizational - no content is added or removed, just relocated (81 lines moved down, with 87 lines inserted in the new location to maintain formatting). The new "TRANSPLANTING A TOPIC BRANCH WITH --ONTO" section now cleanly groups all `--onto` examples together, making them easier to reference from the OPTIONS section (which now includes a cross-reference).

This follows the series' overall goal of improving documentation accessibility by grouping related content together. The patch maintains all existing examples and ASCII diagrams showing branch topologies before and after rebasing with `--onto`. As with the rest of the series, this change was reviewed and merged with positive feedback from the maintainer.
