This thread addressed a long-standing issue in the `git-jump` contrib script where filenames containing spaces in diff output were incorrectly processed due to Git's behavior of appending tabs after such filenames. The solution, now agreed upon in v2 of the patch, uses a simple Perl regex to strip trailing tabs while explicitly not supporting the edge case of filenames ending with literal tabs (which would be quoted in Git's output anyway).

All reviewers - Phillip Wood, D. Ben Knoble, and Junio Hamano - have approved the approach, agreeing that supporting the common case of space-containing filenames is more important than handling rare edge cases. The follow-up discussion confirmed that Git's automatic quoting of control characters (including tabs) provides natural protection against ambiguity, and that more complex solutions like adding Git.pm dependencies or relying on Vim's parser would be overkill. The patch is now ready for inclusion, having clearly documented its scope and limitations.
