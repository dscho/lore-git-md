Here's a human-readable summary of the thread:

The thread addresses a long-standing issue in the `git-jump` contrib script where filenames containing spaces in diff output were incorrectly processed due to Git's diff.c behavior of appending tabs after such filenames. The proposed solution modifies a Perl regex to optionally strip trailing tabs, fixing the common case of spaces in filenames while intentionally breaking the rare edge-case of filenames ending with literal tabs.

D. Ben Knoble validated the fix works for space-containing filenames, and Junio Hamano (while noting he doesn't personally use git-jump) agreed with this practical tradeoff. The change is minimal - just a one-line regex modification from `(.*)` to `(.*?)\t?$` - and well-documented in the commit message, which clearly explains the three filename cases (with spaces, with tabs, and with both). 

While alternative approaches were noted (like conditional tab removal or using `--patch-with-raw`), the consensus is that the current solution strikes the right balance between simplicity and effectiveness for this contrib script. The extremely rare case of tab-terminated filenames remains an edge case that could be revisited if users report problems, but this isn't expected to be a practical concern.
