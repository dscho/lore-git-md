This thread addressed a long-standing issue in the `git-jump` contrib script where filenames containing spaces in diff output were incorrectly processed due to Git's behavior of appending tabs after such filenames. The solution, now in its second iteration, implements a simple Perl regex to strip trailing tabs while intentionally not supporting the rare edge case of filenames ending with literal tabs.

Key participants D. Ben Knoble and Phillip Wood validated the approach, with Wood noting that Git's quoting behavior for control characters (including tabs) provides natural protection against misinterpreting truly tab-terminated filenames. The v2 patch improves the commit message with clear examples of this quoting behavior and explicit documentation of supported/unsupported cases.

Reviewers agree the tradeoff favoring practical usability over rare edge cases is appropriate, with Phillip Wood explicitly endorsing the solution and Junio Hamano concurring with prioritizing simplicity. The discussion has reached clear consensus, with all technical concerns addressed, making the patch ready for inclusion. The fix maintains `git-jump`'s lightweight nature while solving the common case of space-containing filenames that users actually encounter.
