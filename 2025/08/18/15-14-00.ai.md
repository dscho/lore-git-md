Bugfix proposal for autosquash rebase behavior with commit.cleanup=strip and prepare-commit-msg hooks.

Phillip Wood responds to Simon Cheng's bug report about commit message cleanup not working during autosquash rebases when using prepare-commit-msg hooks with commit.cleanup=strip configuration. He identifies the root cause in commit f7d42ceec52, which changed the behavior to preserve fixup commit messages verbatim during rebase, overriding the cleanup setting. Phillip questions this design choice, noting that regular "pick" commands do respect commit.cleanup, and suggests the behavior should be made consistent.

The key technical points are:
- Current behavior stems from a 2021 change to preserve fixup messages exactly
- Phillip argues this is inconsistent with how "pick" commands handle cleanup
- The prepare-commit-msg hook's ability to modify messages means the "verbatim" preservation goal isn't fully achievable anyway
- Suggests aligning fixup behavior with pick commands regarding commit.cleanup

Phillip indicates he may work on a fix after returning from a week-long absence, unless someone else addresses it first. The proposed direction would affect how the rebase machinery processes commit messages when squashing fixup commits, particularly in relation to the interaction between hook-injected content and the cleanup=strip setting.
