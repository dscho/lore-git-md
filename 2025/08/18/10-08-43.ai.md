Feature proposal for `git format-patch` to add line numbers in patch emails. Seyi Kuforiji suggests adding a `--with-line-numbers` flag to include line numbers in the context lines of generated patches, aiming to improve review efficiency on the mailing list. The motivation comes from personal experience where reviewers' comments were harder to follow without line number references, requiring manual counting through diff hunks.

The proposed feature would be purely visual, not affecting patch application mechanics (`git am`/`git apply`). Key benefits highlighted include clearer reviews, easier reference to specific lines in comments, and reduced friction for new contributors. The author acknowledges potential concerns about visual clutter but suggests this could be addressed through careful formatting.

This appears to be a well-motivated proposal addressing a specific pain point in the review workflow. The author is seeking feedback before implementation, indicating a thoughtful approach. The change would primarily affect `builtin/log.c` and related documentation, with no expected impact on patch application logic. The proposal seems likely to spark discussion about formatting aesthetics and whether the benefits outweigh any visual noise in patch emails.
