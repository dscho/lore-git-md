The two-patch series comprehensively addresses all known problems, fixing an assertion failure, an infinite loop, and multiple output correctness issues. The core changes involve 15 lines in `line-log.c` to properly handle boundary conditions when processing multiple line ranges. The fixes reveal that some test cases from 2013 had incorrect expected output due to these longstanding bugs.
