This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing Meson build system integration thread, specifically addressing Ramsay Jones' concerns about declaring Meson as officially supported.

1. Acknowledges the need to port commit 586919c3b2's libexecdir handling to git-gui's Meson build, though notes this will be handled upstream.

On the broader question of removing Meson status, Steinhardt:
- Agrees Jones' pending build updates and the unresolved shell quoting issue (from previous discussions) mean more work remains
- Clarifies his "feature compatibility" claim referred specifically to user-facing functionality matching Makefile capabilities
- Maintains optimism about Meson's current usability while acknowledging need for follow-up fixes

The exchange highlights the tension between technical completion (Steinhardt's position that core functionality is now covered) and production readiness (Jones' caution about outstanding issues). Steinhardt's response is pragmatic - recognizing specific gaps while defending the overall assessment that Meson is now viable for most use cases. The thread remains open on when to officially endorse Meson, with both technical (quoting issues) and process (pending patches) factors still in play.
