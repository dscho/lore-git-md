This thread addresses a regression in `git subtree` (a contrib command) where squashed merges of nested subtrees under prefixes were being incorrectly ignored during split operations. The issue was introduced in v2.44.0-rc0 and stems from faulty commit examination logic in `should_ignore_subtree_split_commit`.

Colin Stagner proposed a fix that has now been thoroughly reviewed by Phillip Wood. They've confirmed the solution correctly places the checks in `check_parents()` to properly prune parent recursion while maintaining the necessary subtree processing order. The discussion revealed that the ancestor-first iteration order makes additional ancestor examination redundant, and while the `git-subtree-dir:` trailer check appears untested, it will be retained conservatively to avoid regression risks.

All technical points are now resolved, with consensus on keeping the current implementation approach. Colin's patch remains minimal for the 2.44 maintenance track, though there's recognition that future performance improvements through bulk commit examination could be explored separately. The original regression came from commit 98ba49ccc2, and while some test coverage gaps around `--ignore-joins` functionality remain, they're not addressed in this focused fix.
