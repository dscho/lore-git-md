Bugfix for progress meter delay handling in the ongoing signal modernization effort.

Johannes Sixt identifies and fixes a long-standing bug in Git's progress meter implementation where the configured delay time (default 2 seconds) was being ignored, effectively always showing progress after just 1 second. The issue stems from not properly resetting the `progress_update` flag after each second elapses, causing rapid successive decrements of the delay counter.

The fix involves properly tracking the flag state and resetting it immediately, while also changing the default delay to 1 second to maintain current behavior since no complaints have been raised. This is presented as a minimal patch that could be extended with further cleanups.

This comes as part of the broader discussion about modernizing progress meter signaling, where Sixt has been advocating for a signal-free approach. The patch serves both as an independent bugfix and as groundwork for potential future changes to the signaling mechanism. While the thread has largely moved toward Sixt's signal-free proposal, this particular fix addresses a concrete issue that exists in current code regardless of which signaling approach is ultimately adopted.
