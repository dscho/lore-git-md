This allows callers to write tree objects from any in-memory index, not just the one associated with the repository. The change is mechanical - the function's behavior remains the same when passed the repository's index, but it now has the flexibility to handle other cases.

The patch updates the single existing caller in `builtin/checkout.c` to pass the repository's index explicitly. This is part of the larger effort to support the `git-history` command's needs for working with in-memory indexes during history editing operations.
