The issue stems from incorrect assumptions in `archive-zip.c` about zlib's behavior - the code expected `git_deflate()` with Z_FINISH to complete in one pass, but zlib may require multiple calls when the output buffer is too small.

While the logic could theoretically be merged with the first loop, they've agreed to keep the fix minimal and clear with a separate loop.
