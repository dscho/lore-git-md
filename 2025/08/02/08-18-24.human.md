This is a documentation patch in the ongoing `git last-modified` series discussion. Christian Couder is responding to Junio Hamano's proposed clarification to the CodingGuidelines about the difference between `S_release()` and `S_clear()` functions.

The patch being discussed adds explicit wording to clarify that `S_release()` only frees resources without reinitializing the structure for reuse, while `S_clear()` does both. This distinction came up during review of the `last-modified` command's implementation.

Christian Couder agrees with the clarification, indicating this will help prevent confusion among contributors about these naming conventions. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, stemming from maintainer feedback during the post-merge refinement of the `last-modified` feature. This is part of Git's typical attention to detail in documenting development patterns, even for established conventions.
