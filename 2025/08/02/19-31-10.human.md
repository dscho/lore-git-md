Bugfix investigation for fsck failure in blobless clones. Jeff King confirms the reproduction case and analyzes the root cause of the fetch failure when `fetch.fsckObjects` is enabled in blobless clones. The issue occurs when fsck verifies objects during fetch and encounters missing blobs that are intentionally omitted due to the `blob:none` filter.

Key findings: The failure happens in `index-pack` when it encounters a missing blob (0020d54b979) that is referenced by a tree but not present in the pack. This is expected behavior for blobless clones, but the fsck verification incorrectly treats it as an error. Jeff notes the existing `--promisor` flag should theoretically handle this case but appears ineffective here.

Proposed solution: Jeff suggests a patch to `builtin/index-pack.c` that would skip verification for promisor objects, but expresses uncertainty about potential performance implications and whether this is the complete fix. He CCs Jonathan Tan (original author of related code) for further input.

This moves the discussion from reproduction to potential fixes, though the exact correct approach remains unclear. The patch appears to resolve the immediate issue but may need refinement based on deeper protocol expectations around promisor objects.
