This is a review comment on the strbuf-to-string_list refactoring series, focusing on potential edge cases in the conversion.

Jeff King (Peff) agrees with the series' goal of replacing the problematic `strbuf_split*()` API with `string_list` alternatives, noting particular appreciation for the `skip_prefix()` simplification in sub-process.c. While finding the patches generally unobjectionable, he raises three subtle compatibility considerations that warrant verification:

1. The differing "max" parameter semantics between the APIs, where string_list_split() counts pieces rather than splits. This could be improved later but would require careful interface changes.

2. Potential divergence in handling repeated delimiters (e.g. whether "foo//bar" produces empty elements between slashes). The thread summary doesn't indicate this was previously discussed.

3. The broader whitespace trimming behavior - while STRING_LIST_SPLIT_TRIM does bilateral trimming versus the original's right-only approach, Peff judges this acceptable since space-based splitting makes left-side spaces unlikely in practice.

The comment reinforces the series' technical soundness while identifying subtle behavioral differences that might need documentation or verification. Peff's approval of the core approach and lack of objections to the specific conversions suggests these are minor considerations rather than blockers.
