Performance optimization for `git describe` using a priority queue.

This is the first of two patches from René Scharfe that replaces the list-based priority queue in `git describe` with Git's `prio_queue` data structure. The change avoids quadratic worst-case complexity and shows measurable performance improvements - about 29% faster when describing a range of 4751 commits (v2.41.0 to v2.47.0) in benchmarks.

The patch modifies `builtin/describe.c` to use `prio_queue` operations instead of `commit_list` functions, introducing a new helper `all_have_flag()` to check queue contents. The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions of the queue operations while maintaining the same logic. The commit message includes clear benchmark results showing the improvement.

This appears to be a well-motivated optimization from an experienced contributor, with the performance claims backed by concrete measurements. The change is focused and doesn't appear to introduce any new complexity.
