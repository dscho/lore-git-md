This is the second patch in a two-part series introducing a new `--trailer` option to `git rebase`. The feature allows appending custom trailers (like Signed-off-by) to commit messages during rebase operations.

The patch implements the user-facing `--trailer` option for the rebase command, building on the trailer processing infrastructure refactored in the first patch. Key aspects include:
- The option forces use of the merge backend (rejecting apply/am backends early)
- Automatically enables `--force-rebase` when trailers are specified
- Includes thorough validation of trailer arguments (rejecting empty or malformed inputs)
- Adds comprehensive test coverage for normal operations, conflicts, root rebases, and invalid inputs

The implementation appears well-considered, with careful handling of edge cases and good test coverage. The patch maintains backward compatibility while adding this new functionality that could be useful for automating trailer management during rebases. The series as a whole represents a clean separation of concerns between the trailer processing internals and the rebase integration.
