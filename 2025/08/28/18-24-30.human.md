This is part of Julia Evans' ongoing documentation patch series improving the `git-checkout` man page. In this response, she clarifies her documentation philosophy in response to Junio Hamano's question about audience targeting.

Julia explains she's primarily writing for existing Git users (1+ years experience) rather than complete newcomers, based on two observations: experienced users are the main man page audience, and Git's documentation prioritizes technical accuracy which often requires some baseline knowledge. However, she remains open to making the text more accessible where possible without compromising accuracy.

The discussion highlights the tension between precision and approachability in technical documentation. Julia acknowledges the challenge of explaining concepts like switching branches to newcomers when even experienced users struggle with terms like "HEAD" and "index." She plans to add some introductory material about branch switching while maintaining the technical rigor expected in Git's man pages.

This exchange provides insight into the careful balancing act required when documenting complex version control concepts, where the project values both correctness and usability. The patch series continues to evolve through this thoughtful review process.
