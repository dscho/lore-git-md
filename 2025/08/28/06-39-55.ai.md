This is a follow-up in the thread about structured deprecation messaging for `git whatchanged`. The email confirms the final wording for the deprecation warning system, specifically addressing how to guide users when they can't find suitable replacements for deprecated commands.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk agrees with both Eric Sunshine's proposed wording and Junio Hamano's endorsement of it. The key phrasing will explicitly link the "contact maintainers" prompt to unsuccessful attempts at finding replacements from the provided hints. Haugsbakk notes this differs from the `pack-redundant` deprecation which lacks this linking, and confirms he'll implement Sunshine's suggestion exactly as proposed.

The technical implementation remains unchanged from previous discussions, maintaining the three-step guidance structure in `usage.c` with URL generation via `strbuf_add_percentencode`. This email marks the resolution of the wording debate for Patch 1 of the series, with Haugsbakk now preparing to submit v2 of patches 2-4 which will complete the implementation.
