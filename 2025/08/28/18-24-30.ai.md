This is a documentation patch in an ongoing series improving the `git-checkout` man page. Julia Evans responds to Junio Hamano's concern about making the documentation accessible to Git newcomers, clarifying her primary audience is existing Git users (1+ years experience) who currently find the man pages difficult to understand.

She acknowledges the challenge of explaining concepts like "switch to branch" without relying on terms like "HEAD" or "index" that even long-time users may not fully grasp.

Key points in this email:
- Defends the target audience choice (experienced users) while remaining open to newcomer accessibility
- Notes the tension between technical precision and approachability in Git's documentation tradition
- Commits to attempting a newcomer-friendly explanation of branch switching in future revisions
- Maintains the series' focus on making the man pages more useful for daily reference by practitioners

The exchange highlights an ongoing documentation challenge - whether to optimize for onboarding new users or serving as a precise reference for experienced ones. Julia's position suggests a hierarchical approach where the primary content targets experienced users, with supplementary explanations for newcomers where feasible. This aligns with the thread's broader theme of improving clarity while preserving Git's technical documentation standards.
