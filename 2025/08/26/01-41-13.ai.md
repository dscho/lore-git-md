This is patch 4/16 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the packfile hashmap from the object database (ODB) to the new `store->map` location, continuing the systematic migration of packfile-related state.

The change is straightforward - relocating the existing hashmap that tracks loaded packfiles by their paths into the new dedicated structure. As part of this, the comparison function `pack_map_entry_cmp()` becomes static since it's now only used within packfile.c. Taylor Blau's brief review indicates no technical concerns with this particular patch, just an acknowledgment that it matches expectations.

This continues the series' pattern of incrementally moving components while maintaining identical behavior. The hashmap relocation follows the same approach as previous patches that moved the packed_git list and initialized flag. The lack of substantive feedback suggests this is a non-controversial step in the larger refactoring effort to enable future pluggable ODB backends.
