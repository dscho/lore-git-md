Julia Evans continues refining her documentation series to make `git-push` concepts more accessible, with the fourth patch focusing on refspecs - one of Git's more confusing aspects for newcomers. The series builds on Jean-Noël Avila's synopsis-style conversion work and incorporates research insights from Julia's user studies.

Junio Hamano has been closely reviewing the changes, particularly endorsing the new "try X, then Y" positive language style introduced in patch 2 as a documentation best practice. The current discussion centers on the refspec rewrite (patch 4), where Julia is addressing Junio's technical and structural feedback while maintaining her pedagogical approach. Key points under discussion include clarifying optional refspec components, force-push semantics, and deletion rules.

Notable progress includes identifying an inaccuracy in Git's existing `--force` documentation regarding tag pushes, which may warrant a separate fix. While most of Junio's feedback is being incorporated, Julia maintains some early explanations (like equating `+` prefix with `--force`) that help bridge users' existing knowledge based on her research findings. The series is shaping up to significantly improve one of Git's more opaque documentation areas while establishing new clarity standards for future man pages.
