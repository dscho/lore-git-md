As maintainer, Junio's position carries significant weight in this architectural decision.

The author has agreed to make remaining documentation improvements, including making the command description backend-agnostic and clarifying test descriptions. With Junio's guidance now providing clarity on header placement, the series appears technically sound and likely to proceed once these final refinements are incorporated.
