This is a review response in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to migrate packfile management to `struct packfile_store`. Taylor Blau is reviewing patch 8/16, which refactors `prepare_packed_git()` and related functions to operate on the packfile store rather than the repository object.

Taylor generally approves the changes, noting the clean refactoring of MRU list preparation and the inlining of `rearrange_packed_git()`. However, he raises a concern about potential layering violations - the packfile store now needs awareness of its parent object store to handle alternates preparation. While he doesn't have an immediate solution, this flags an architectural consideration for the series.

The review continues the pattern of positive but thoughtful feedback on this substantial refactoring effort. Taylor's comments suggest the changes are technically sound but may warrant discussion about the ideal abstraction boundaries as the packfile store takes on more responsibility from the object store. The patch appears to be progressing well overall, with this being the only notable question raised in the review.
