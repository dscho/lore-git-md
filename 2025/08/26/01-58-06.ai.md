This is a review response in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to migrate packfile management to `struct packfile_store`. Taylor Blau provides feedback on patch 8/16 which refactors `prepare_packed_git()` and related functions to operate on the packfile store rather than a repository.

The key technical changes in the patch involve:
- Renaming `prepare_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_prepare()`
- Inlining `rearrange_packed_git()` into its sole caller
- Updating `prepare_packed_git_mru()` to `packfile_store_prepare_mru()`
- Changing parameter types from `struct repository*` to `struct packfile_store*`

Taylor's review notes the changes are generally good but raises one architectural concern about layering - he questions whether the packfile store should need to know about its containing object store when calling `odb_prepare_alternates()`. While he doesn't propose an alternative solution, this flags a potential design issue that may need addressing in future iterations.

The review continues the pattern of positive but thoughtful feedback on this series, with Taylor verifying the mechanical changes while keeping an eye on the broader architectural implications. The patch appears to be progressing the series' goal of separating packfile concerns from the object database, though the layering question may need resolution in future versions.
