This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans' series improving the `git-push` man page, specifically focusing on rewriting the refspec specification section (patch 4/4). Junio C Hamano provides detailed feedback on the proposed changes.

The patch aims to make refspec syntax clearer by: reorganizing content into numbered lists, adding examples, explaining optional components earlier, and covering special cases like negative refspecs (`^`) and patterns (`*`). The changes are substantial (82 lines changed) but maintain technical accuracy while improving readability.

Junio's review acknowledges the improvements in conciseness and organization but raises several technical concerns: the "optional" markers for `:<dst>` and `+` may be premature without context, the "fully expanded form" example is incorrect, and some explanations about deletion behavior could be clearer. He suggests moving certain explanations to more logical positions in the flow.

The feedback is constructive and technical, focusing on ensuring the documentation remains precise while becoming more accessible. This continues the thread's theme of balancing clarity with accuracy, particularly challenging for the refspec syntax which was noted earlier as the most invasive change in the series. Junio's comments suggest the patch is moving in the right direction but needs some refinement in how and when certain concepts are introduced.
