This is a documentation patch series (4 parts) aimed at improving clarity in the `git-push` man page, specifically targeting the DESCRIPTION section and refspec definition. The author, Julia Evans, conducted user research with 16 Git users (including long-time users) and found confusion around the term "ref" and overall readability issues.

The series proposes:
1. An updated introduction to `git-push`
2. Clarifications about "where to push"
3. Clarifications about "what to push"
4. A rewritten refspec specification (noted as more invasive than usual)

Key context from the cover letter:
- The refspec rewrite is particularly challenging to balance between clarity and accuracy
- Many experienced users don't understand the term "ref" despite years of Git usage
- The author is open to feedback and may drop the refspec patch if it proves problematic

The changes touch only Documentation/git-push.adoc, showing a net reduction of 2 lines despite significant rewrites (204 lines modified). The series builds on commit c44beea485f0f2feaf460e2ac87fdd5608d63cf0 and is submitted via GitGitGadget PR #1964.

This appears to be a well-motivated documentation improvement based on actual user research, though the refspec changes may warrant particular scrutiny given their invasive nature and the author's own caution about them. The patch series follows Git's standard documentation format and maintains the overall structure while aiming for better clarity.
