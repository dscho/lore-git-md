This is a documentation patch series aimed at improving the clarity of `git-push`'s man page, particularly focusing on the "DESCRIPTION" section and refspec definitions. Julia Evans conducted a survey of 16 Git users (including long-time users) and found confusion around the term "ref" and other aspects of the push documentation.

The series consists of 4 patches that rewrite sections of the documentation while trying to preserve the original information. The most significant change is in the refspec specification, which the author notes is more invasive than usual and may need reconsideration if problematic. The goal is to make the documentation more accessible without changing its technical accuracy.

The patches focus on: (1) updating the introduction, (2) clarifying "where to push" explanations, (3) improving "what to push" descriptions, and (4) rewriting the refspec specification. The author shows awareness of documentation best practices by noting the survey feedback and being open to feedback on the more substantial refspec changes.

This appears to be a well-motivated documentation improvement based on actual user research, targeting areas that clearly confuse even experienced Git users. The careful approach to rewriting while preserving technical accuracy suggests this will be a valuable contribution if the changes are found to be accurate by reviewers.
