This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about deprecating `core.commentChar=auto`. Phillip Wood has submitted version 3 of his patch series, which originally proposed removing the dynamic comment character selection feature due to its maintenance burden outweighing its benefits.

The new version includes minor cleanups but leaves unresolved the question of how to best warn users when `core.commentChar` is set in submodules. Phillip notes this aspect needs more user feedback before potential improvements can be made. The series is currently being considered for merging into the 'next' branch.

The changes represent part of Git's broader effort to simplify and maintain configuration options, with this particular feature being deemed not worth the complexity it introduces. The patch series appears well-considered, with the author taking a measured approach to gathering feedback before finalizing the warning behavior in submodules.
