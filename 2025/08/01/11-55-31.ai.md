Formatting nitpick in reflog race condition documentation.

This is a minor follow-up to Patrick Steinhardt's patch addressing race conditions in HEAD reflog updates. Toon Claes points out an awkward phrasing in the commit message where "point to not point to a reference" is redundant. He suggests the clearer alternative "HEAD gets concurrently updated to no longer point to a reference".

The observation doesn't impact the technical content of the patch, which has already been merged. The patch itself introduced the `REF_LOG_VIA_SPLIT` flag to mitigate race conditions when generating reflog entries for HEAD, with post-lock verification to handle concurrent updates. This follow-up simply improves the clarity of the commit message's explanation of one of the race scenarios.

The thread context shows this is part of a completed 9-patch series on reflog migration infrastructure between files and reftable backends. The series included fixes for committer identity handling, race conditions, and all-zero OIDs during migration. While the core technical issues are resolved, this shows ongoing attention to documentation quality in the codebase.
