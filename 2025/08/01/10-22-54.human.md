Patrick Steinhardt responds to Christian Couder's suggestion about function naming conventions in the `last-modified` implementation. Christian had proposed renaming `last_modified_release()` to `last_modified_clear()` to better align with Git's cleanup function naming patterns.

He argues the current `last_modified_release()` name is correct since it falls into the latter category.
