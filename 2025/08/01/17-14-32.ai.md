This is a discussion about pattern matching behavior in the `git refs list` feature, which is part of the ongoing refactoring effort to consolidate ref listing functionality.

Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's concern about the counterintuitive behavior of single-asterisk pattern matching in ref listings. The current implementation treats `refs/heads/m*` as matching only at a single hierarchy level (e.g., `morning` but not `mid/night`), while `refs/heads` does a prefix match. Junio clarifies this is intentional "anchored at hierarchy boundary" behavior rather than simple prefix matching, explaining why `refs/hea` wouldn't match branches either.

The exchange confirms the decision to maintain the existing `for-each-ref` matching semantics in the new `refs list` command, with potential future consideration of `**` syntax for cross-hierarchy matching.
