Patrick Steinhardt's reftable/libgit2 compatibility series has reached its final version (v3) with this 8-patch set. The series focused on improving error handling, locking, and atomicity in the reftable backend, particularly for concurrent write operations. 

The work standardized error codes (REFTABLE_IO_ERROR, REFTABLE_LOCK_ERROR) and introduced the `REFTABLE_STACK_NEW_ADDITION_RELOAD` flag to ensure atomic stack modifications. The final patch completes this effort by applying the reload flag consistently across all stack-modifying operations (rename_ref, copy_ref, create_reflog, delete_reflog) and fixes a race condition in reflog expiration by reordering operations to acquire locks earlier. 

Justin Tobler provided positive review feedback, particularly noting the improved operation ordering in reflog handling. The series has addressed all previously noted issues, including compiler warnings and documentation typos. With these changes, the reftable backend now has more robust error handling and better concurrency support, marking an important step toward production-ready reftable integration in Git.
