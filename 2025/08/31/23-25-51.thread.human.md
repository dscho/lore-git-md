Phillip Wood has been providing technical analysis and initially created a test script that didn't reproduce the issue, leading to some confusion. The key difference appears to be that Brooke's real-world workflow involves multiple exports and pushes of the same ref, while Phillip's script didn't fully replicate this scenario. They've determined that the reflog creation workaround Phillip suggested earlier doesn't actually solve the remote update problem, though it makes the error disappear without properly updating the remote stash.

Currently, the main open questions are why `--force-with-lease` fails in these multi-export scenarios and whether there's a safer alternative to full force pushes. There's also uncertainty about how `git stash import` would handle forced updates from remotes. Both Brooke and Phillip continue to investigate the exact conditions under which the lease checks fail, with the goal of finding a robust solution for this distributed stash workflow.
