Core problem: How to safely synchronize modified stashes across machines without resorting to force pushes, particularly when `git stash export --to-ref` creates stashes in `refs/stashes/$USER` that get rejected as non-fast-forward updates during push. - `--force-with-lease` protection mechanism appears ineffective for this use case
- Involves manual management of stash refs in `refs/stashes/` namespace
