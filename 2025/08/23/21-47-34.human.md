Maintainer feedback on progress meter signal handling modernization

Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about replacing Git's progress meter signal infrastructure, expressing measured support for Johannes Sixt's proposed signal-free approach despite earlier reservations. While Hamano had previously questioned the wisdom of replacing the current "set and forget" signal handlers with frequent wall-clock checks, he now acknowledges the practicality of the approach.

The key shift in Hamano's position comes from recognizing that modern `gettimeofday()` implementations are optimized for frequent calls, and that the progress meter checks would occur during normal operation rather than in a dedicated spin loop. This addresses his earlier concerns about system impact while maintaining the benefits of the signal-free approach - particularly its Windows compatibility advantages.

This email represents a significant development in the thread, as the maintainer's initial skepticism appears to be softening. While not a full endorsement, Hamano's technical assessment that the approach is "probably fine" suggests the signal-free direction may now have clearer sailing, provided the implementation details hold up under scrutiny. The discussion appears to be moving toward consensus around Sixt's prototype, though final implementation and performance characteristics will likely need verification.
