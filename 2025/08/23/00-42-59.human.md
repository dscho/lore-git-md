This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans' v9 series improving the git-rebase man page, now fully approved and ready for merge. This particular patch consolidates duplicate explanations about handling conflicts during rebase into a single, clearer section.

The patch removes two separate conflict-handling explanations that previously appeared in different parts of the man page and combines them into one comprehensive section near the start of the DESCRIPTION. The new unified explanation maintains all the key information while eliminating redundancy: it describes how to resolve conflicts with `git add`, continue with `--continue`, abort with `--abort`, or skip the problematic commit with `--skip`.

This change is part of the final refinements to the rebase documentation that have already been reviewed and approved by Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood. The patch follows the series' goal of making the documentation more logically organized and easier to follow, while actually reducing the overall line count (a net 9-line reduction in this case). The conflict resolution instructions are now presented just once, in a more prominent position where users are likely to encounter them when first learning about rebase.
