Junio Hamano's response in the progress meter signal handling thread expresses reservations about Johannes Sixt's proposed signal-free approach. While acknowledging the safety of the current minimal signal handler approach, Hamano questions the wisdom of replacing it with frequent wall-clock checks (gettimeofday() calls) in busy loops. He contrasts the "set and forget" nature of signals with what he perceives as potentially more intrusive periodic time checks, though he stops short of outright rejection, framing his concerns as unease rather than technical objections.

Key technical points raised:
- Current signal approach is "very safe" with minimal work in handlers
- Expresses discomfort with frequent time checks replacing signals
- Notes OS optimizations for gettimeofday() but remains skeptical
- Characterizes the tradeoff as signal dirtiness vs timer check dirtiness

This represents the first significant pushback in the thread against Sixt's otherwise well-received proposal, introducing performance concerns that may need addressing before final implementation. The comment maintains the thread's technical focus while adding a maintainer's perspective on system impact tradeoffs.
