What began as Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's proposal to switch to `alarm()` has evolved into a more fundamental redesign led by Johannes Sixt (Hannes), who advocates removing signal handling completely in favor of periodic wall-clock time checks.

The implementation maintains the same user experience while simplifying the code and improving Windows compatibility. With Belón's explicit endorsement of this direction and Sixt's Windows expertise, ownership has naturally shifted to him for the final implementation.

The work is now in its final testing phase, with Sixt waiting for CI validation before submitting formal patches. The thread has effectively settled all major design questions, having rejected both the original `setitimer()` and intermediate `alarm()` approaches in favor of this cleaner signal-free solution.
