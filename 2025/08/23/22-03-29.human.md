This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about modernizing Git's progress meter signal handling, where Johannes Sixt is advocating for his signal-free timer-based approach. The email responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about performance impacts from frequent gettimeofday() calls in the proposed solution.

Sixt points out that Git already uses wall-clock timing in other contexts like display_throughput(), suggesting the performance impact may be as bad as feared. However, he acknowledges some unease about the new timing calls in display_progress() and commits to providing performance measurements. He also notes an observed behavior change with delayed progress indicators that needs investigation before submitting final patches.

The exchange shows the technical discussion is moving toward resolution, with Sixt addressing the maintainer's concerns while identifying remaining implementation issues to work through. The signal-free approach appears to be gaining traction, though some performance validation and behavior adjustments are still needed before it can be finalized.
