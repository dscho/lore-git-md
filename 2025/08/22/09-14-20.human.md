Follow-up discussion on Git contribution documentation. Daniele Sassoli responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion about guiding new contributors to use `gitgitgadget/git` as their remote rather than `git/git`. 

Sassoli maintains that for first-time contributors, the minor disadvantages of using `git/git` don't outweigh the potential confusion of introducing remote-switching instructions mid-document. He argues the current flow - where users naturally clone from `git/git` initially - provides the simplest path for beginners, and that those who contribute regularly will organically learn to optimize their workflow.

The exchange reveals a philosophical difference in documentation approach: Junio favors optimizing for long-term contributor efficiency by standardizing on the `gitgitgadget/git` workflow, while Sassoli prioritizes immediate simplicity for first-timers. Both agree on the end goal of making contributions easier, but differ on how prescriptive the documentation should be. The discussion remains open-ended, with no resolution yet on whether to restructure the guide or maintain the current neutral presentation of both options.
