This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about case-sensitivity issues in bare repository fetches. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's rejection of simply accepting the current behavior, clarifying his earlier proposal to make ref transactions failure-tolerant. 

The key point is that Patrick's suggested approach would allow fetches to complete by accepting the first conflicting ref (rather than the last, as in pre-2.51.0 behavior), while still maintaining that the result is technically "wrong" due to case collisions. He emphasizes the importance of warning users about this situation and guiding them toward proper solutions.

The exchange highlights the tension between maintaining backward compatibility and pushing users toward the reftable solution. While Patrick's proposal offers a middle ground, Junio's earlier position suggests the maintainer favors a harder push to reftable adoption rather than adding more complexity to the files backend. The discussion appears to be converging on reftable as the definitive solution, with this email serving as a final clarification of the alternative approach before likely concluding the thread.
