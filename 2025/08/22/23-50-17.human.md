This is a style discussion in the context of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store abstraction series. Junio C Hamano weighs in on a minor formatting question about bitfield declarations that came up during review.

The thread had previously seen Karthik Nayak suggest standardizing how bitfields are formatted in the `struct packfile_store` code. Junio now proposes a specific style - putting the colon directly after the field name without spaces (`unsigned my_field:1;`) - as a way to avoid both alignment churn and clang-format modifications. Karthik had indicated openness to either approach as long as it was consistent.

This is a small but practical resolution to a formatting nit that came up during the larger technical discussion about the packfile store refactoring. The exchange shows the project's attention to both code quality and maintainability, even for minor stylistic choices. The decision avoids introducing new formatting rules while preventing visually distracting alignment attempts.
