This is the final patch in a refactoring series that completes the transition of Git's bulk-checkin subsystem away from `the_repository` global. The patch makes several notable changes:

1. It removes redundant parameters from `finish_tmp_packfile()` by accessing needed information through the `odb_transaction` structure instead. This simplifies the interface and reduces parameter passing.

2. It replaces direct calls to `repo_get_object_directory(the_repository)` with access to the path through `transaction->odb->sources->path`. While functionally equivalent, Junio notes this creates a potential maintenance hazard if the internal representation of object directories changes in the future.

3. The patch merges the previously internal `deflate_blob_to_pack()` function into its single caller `index_blob_bulk_checkin()`, eliminating an unnecessary layer of indirection. This change logically belongs with the previous patch's transaction management changes but was deferred until now.

The implementation still requires `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` for two remaining globals (`pack_compression_level` and `pack_size_limit_cfg`), but otherwise completes the bulk-checkin subsystem's transition to repository-parameterized interfaces. Junio approves the technical approach while noting the surprise factor of the deferred function merge and potential future maintenance concerns about the open-coded path construction.

This represents the completion of the bulk-checkin refactoring work within the broader `the_repository` removal effort, with all patches in the series now reviewed and ready for integration.
