This is a feature patch implementing the "split" subcommand for the proposed `git-history` command, part of Patrick Steinhardt's RFC series. The discussion focuses on the user experience of splitting commits, specifically how to handle commit messages during the operation.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on the design of the split operation's message handling, suggesting that when splitting a commit, both resulting commits should receive the original commit message as a template. His rationale is practical - it's easier to remove unwanted parts of a message than to recall and retype information that might be lost. He shares his personal workflow where split operations often occur while writing commit messages, making preservation of the original content valuable.

The exchange highlights the ongoing UX considerations in this new command, with Junio providing maintainer perspective on real-world usage patterns. This follows earlier discussion where D.
