This is the fourth and final patch in Julia Evans' documentation series improving the `git-add` man page. It addresses a long-standing terminology inconsistency that often confuses beginners - Git's use of three different terms ("staging area", "index", and "cache") to refer to the same concept.

The patch adds a simple but important NOTE box explaining that these terms are used interchangeably for historical reasons. This clarification comes after the section describing the index/staging area's role in storing changes for the next commit, making it contextually relevant for readers who might be confused by the varying terminology.

The change is small (4 lines added) but meaningful, completing the series' goal of making the documentation more approachable while maintaining accuracy. It follows the established pattern of focused, concrete improvements seen in the previous patches. With this addition, the series appears ready for integration, having addressed reviewer feedback while maintaining a clear focus on beginner accessibility.
