Bugfix in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable/libgit2 compatibility series (v4, patch 8/8) addressing a race condition in reftable stack operations. This final patch in the series standardizes the use of `REFTABLE_STACK_NEW_ADDITION_RELOAD` across all stack modification operations to ensure consistent handling of concurrent writes.

The change modifies several functions in refs/reftable-backend.c to always reload the stack after acquiring a lock, eliminating a race window where concurrent modifications could cause operations to bail out unnecessarily. Key modifications include:
- Adding the reload flag to `reftable_stack_add()` calls in rename_ref, copy_ref, create_reflog, and delete_reflog operations
- Reordering operations in reflog_expire to create the addition before initializing the iterator, fixing a remaining race condition
- Removing a redundant manual stack reload in prepare_transaction_update

The patch maintains the safety checks for reference conflicts while improving the user experience by reducing false-positive failures from concurrent writes. This completes the series' transition to atomic stack operations and standardized error handling, with all changes focused on the reftable backend implementation.
