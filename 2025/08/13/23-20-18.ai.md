This is the fourth and final patch in Julia Evans' v2 documentation series improving the `git-add` man page. The patch adds a clarifying note about Git's inconsistent terminology for the staging area, explicitly stating that "staging area", "index", and "cache" are used interchangeably for historical reasons.

The change is a small but important clarification that addresses a well-known source of confusion for Git beginners. The note is inserted just before the OPTIONS section, making it visible early in the document while still being contextually relevant to the preceding discussion of the index/staging area's role.

Key technical details:
- Adds a [NOTE] block explaining the terminology inconsistency
- Specifically calls out "staging area", "index", and "cache" as equivalent terms
- Attributes the inconsistency to historical reasons
- Positioned after the index/staging area explanation but before the options
- 4 lines added (the note plus surrounding whitespace)
