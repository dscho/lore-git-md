This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about handling race conditions in reflog migration, part of the `ps/reflog-migrate-fixes` series that's blocking other ref-related patches.

Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on the v3 (explicit rejection) vs v4 (silent skip) debate for handling HEAD updates during reflog migration. While acknowledging Jeff King's technical arguments for v4's silent approach, Patrick leans toward v3's explicit transaction abort as the safer choice. His key points: the race condition is implicit (not user-requested), the scenario is extremely unlikely to occur in practice (demonstrated by `git commit` not triggering it), and v3's approach requires less complex logic and testing.

Patrick offers to prepare a final reroll reverting to v3's behavior if the maintainers agree. This would unblock the dependent `ps/remote-rename-fix` series. The email moves the discussion toward resolution while maintaining technical rigor - noting both the safety benefits of v3 and the practical rarity of the edge case.
