Documentation patch completing a 6-part series introducing automated linting for Git's man pages. This final patch adds enforcement for proper backtick-wrapping of option names in synopsis-style documentation, ensuring consistent formatting.

The patch modifies `lint-documentation-style.perl` to detect un-backticked option names in definition lists when the `[synopsis]` marker is present. It fixes two files as examples: `git-checkout.adoc` (one instance) and `git-refs.adoc` (10 instances), converting bare option names like `--option>` to backtick-wrapped `` `--option` `` format. The linter change is minimal (6 lines) but completes the suite of automated style checks for documentation. This follows the series' pattern of incremental linter additions with corresponding documentation fixes, now covering linkgit macros, section formatting, definition lists, `--[no-]` options, and finally synopsis backtick requirements.

The changes are mechanical and align with Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing synopsis conversion work, maintaining compatibility with parallel efforts like the refs list subcommand and reflog migration. With this patch, the series establishes comprehensive automated enforcement of documentation formatting standards across Git's man pages.
