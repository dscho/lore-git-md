Compiler warning fix for the unit test framework. This is the second patch in a two-part series addressing `-Wmaybe-uninitialized` warnings under GCC's `-Og` optimization level. Denton Liu's change initializes `matchlen=0` in clar.c's test runner when no filter is provided, suppressing a compiler warning about potential uninitialized use. The patch notes this is theoretically impossible in practice due to the `exact` flag acting as a guard, but adds the initialization anyway for compiler satisfaction.

The change is minimal (one line modified) and behavior-neutral - it simply makes explicit what was already implicitly true in the control flow. This matches the project's preference for maintainable solutions over suppression pragmas. The patch has been split from the original series to clearly separate the clar.c fix from Jeff King's remote.c changes, with both authors now owning their respective areas. No open questions remain - this appears ready for integration as a straightforward warning cleanup.
