This documentation series has reached its final form after three iterations, establishing automated linting tools to enforce consistent formatting across Git's man pages. The six-patch set introduces focused Perl linters (ranging from 3 to 48 lines each) that systematically address different aspects of documentation style, with each linter accompanied by corresponding fixes to existing files.

The series has evolved through feedback from reviewers including Collin Funk (who provided a Reviewed-by for patch 3) and Ramsay Jones (who identified portability issues that will be addressed in a future reroll). The linters now cover five key areas: proper use of `linkgit:` macros, section formatting rules, definition list structure, handling of `--[no-]` options, and backtick-wrapping of option names in synopsis-style documentation. The final patch completes this work by enforcing backtick formatting in two remaining files (`git-checkout.adoc` and `git-refs.adoc`).

With 117 files modified (452 insertions and 234 deletions), this effort complements Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing synopsis conversion work while maintaining a clear separation from content-level changes.
