This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about reflog migration race condition handling, specifically regarding whether to abort transactions when detecting concurrent HEAD updates.

Jeff King (Peff) agrees with Patrick Steinhardt's assessment that the race condition scenario is extremely unlikely to occur in practice, particularly on client machines where most ref updates happen through HEAD or remote tracking branches. While server-side scenarios where HEAD gets updated during pushes are slightly more plausible, they're still rare.

The key takeaway is that both developers now favor reverting to the v3 approach of explicitly aborting transactions when these races are detected, prioritizing safety and simplicity over the v4 approach's silent-skip behavior. Jeff explicitly gives his approval for Patrick to send a final reroll of the v3 logic.

This consensus moves the discussion forward, clearing the way for Patrick to update the patch series and unblock the dependent remote rename fixes. The decision aligns with maintainer Junio Hamano's initial leaning toward the v3 approach while incorporating Jeff's technical perspective on the practical likelihood of the race condition occurring.
