This is a brief follow-up in the now-closed discussion about adding a `--after` flag to `git mv`. Johannes Sixt expresses personal sympathy for the feature request (having wanted it himself), but notably doesn't challenge the maintainer's decision that it's incompatible with Git's design. He suggests that if such a flag were to exist in Git, it would follow Git's existing conventions by being named `--cached` rather than `--after`.

The email doesn't reopen the discussion - it's more of a "I feel your pain" note that acknowledges the usefulness of the proposed functionality while implicitly accepting the technical reasons it can't be implemented. The thread remains effectively closed, with this serving as a minor postscript from an experienced contributor.
