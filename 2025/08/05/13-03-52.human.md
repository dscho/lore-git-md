This is the fifth patch in a 6-part documentation series focused on improving Git's man page consistency through automated linting tools. While the previous patches added new linters, this one focuses on manual cleanup of `git-for-each-ref.adoc` to prepare for the final linter in the series that will enforce synopsis formatting.

Jean-Noël Avila makes extensive stylistic improvements to the man page, including:
- Fixing synopsis syntax (adding parentheses around pattern alternatives)
- Standardizing formatting of option names and placeholders (using `_<var>_` and backticks consistently)
- Clarifying descriptions (e.g. changing "If `--stdin` is supplied" to "The list of patterns is read from standard input")
- Fixing typos and inconsistent terminology (e.g. "committish" -> "commit-ish")

The changes are mechanical but thorough - 264 lines modified with equal parts additions and deletions. This aligns with the series' goal of documentation consistency, though unlike the previous automated linter patches, these changes required manual review. The patch serves as groundwork for the upcoming synopsis-style linter while improving readability of this complex man page.
