This is the fourth patch in a 6-part documentation series introducing automated linting tools for Git's man pages. The patch adds a new linter that enforces a specific documentation style rule: it prohibits the use of `--[no-]parameter` syntax in option documentation, requiring instead that such options be split into separate `--parameter` and `--no-parameter` entries.

The change affects 36 documentation files, with 159 lines added and 78 removed. The modifications systematically split combined `--[no-]option` entries into their positive and negative forms across commands like `git-push`, `git-clone`, `git-send-email`, and many others. A small addition to `lint-documentation-style.perl` implements the new check.

The rationale given is improved searchability - having separate entries makes it easier to find documentation for both forms of the option. This continues the series' pattern of mechanical, uncontroversial formatting improvements to establish consistent documentation standards. The change is purely about presentation and doesn't affect functionality.

As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a well-executed change with clear motivation and thorough implementation, touching all relevant documentation files. It follows the established pattern of adding focused linters (this one is just 3 lines) alongside the corresponding documentation fixes.
