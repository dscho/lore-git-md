This is a follow-up discussion in the merged documentation improvement series for `git-rebase`.

Phillip Wood responds to Julia Evans' thoughts about documenting performance characteristics of rebase operations. The discussion focuses on whether to include details about the "fast" (commit selection via `git log`) and "slow" (cherry-pick detection via `git patch-id`) steps in the rebase internals documentation.

Phillip questions whether most users would understand the underlying mechanics well enough to benefit from this level of detail, noting that cherry-pick detection requires understanding patch-IDs and diff generation. He suggests that if performance warnings are included, they should also mention that the slow step can be skipped (presumably via `--reapply-cherry-picks`).

The email also references an ongoing technical discussion with Junio Hamano about how to properly delineate the conceptual steps in the rebase process. This is part of the post-merge refinement of the documentation changes that restructured the `git-rebase` man page.

The exchange shows the careful balance being struck between technical accuracy and approachability in the rebase documentation, with contributors working to determine what level of implementation detail will actually help users understand and troubleshoot rebase operations.
