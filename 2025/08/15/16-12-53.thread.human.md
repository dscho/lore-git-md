This thread discusses a performance optimization for `git ls-files` when working with sparse indexes. The patch, proposed by Derrick Stolee, modifies how sparse index expansion works in `ls-files` to only trigger when encountering a sparse directory that matches the pathspec, rather than doing a full index expansion upfront. This avoids unnecessary operations when pathspecs align with sparse-checkout patterns.

Elijah Newren, an expert on Git's merge and sparse-checkout machinery, reviewed the patch and provided helpful feedback. He pointed out that the performance benefit comes not just from the delayed expansion, but also from how `prune_index()` filters index entries by pathspec prefix before iteration begins. Newren suggested clarifying this interaction in the commit message. The technical discussion confirmed the approach is safe, as expanding the index mid-iteration doesn't affect earlier entries. The included test case demonstrates the optimization working as intended.
