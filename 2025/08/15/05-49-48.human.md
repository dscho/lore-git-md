This is part 2/6 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove global state from the commit-graph subsystem, continuing the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch refactors how the commit-graph stores hash algorithm information - instead of just storing the hash length, it now stores a pointer to the full hash algorithm structure. 

This is a mechanical change that updates all the places where the hash length was previously used to instead access the length via the algorithm pointer. The change is straightforward but touches many parts of the commit-graph code, as the hash length is used throughout for various calculations and lookups. 

The patch appears well-executed, with consistent updates to all relevant call sites. It's a necessary step for the larger goal of making the commit-graph code repository-agnostic, as future patches will need access to the full hash algorithm rather than just its length. The change maintains all existing functionality while paving the way for the subsequent patches in the series.

This continues the trend seen in the series of methodically replacing global state with repository-specific alternatives. Reviewers had already approved the architectural approach in previous iterations, and this patch doesn't introduce any new complexity - it's a focused, mechanical update that keeps the series moving toward its goal.
