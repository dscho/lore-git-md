Performance optimization patch in the Rust xdiff series, specifically handling the `--ignore-cr-at-eol` whitespace flag case. This is patch 16/17 in the ongoing RFC series introducing Rust as a hard dependency with xdiff optimizations.

The patch adds special-case handling for when `--ignore-cr-at-eol` is the only whitespace flag set, making this case as fast as the no-whitespace-flags path. The implementation handles both line hashing (in `WhitespaceIter`) and line comparison (in `line_equal`) by checking for CRLF line endings and comparing the content while ignoring the final CR if present. The author notes that other whitespace flags remain slower in the Rust implementation for now due to technical constraints around xxhash's chunk-based processing and the dual use of iterators for both hashing and equality testing.
