Junio Hamano provides feedback on the bugreport template formatting patch, suggesting additional improvements to make bug reports more readable. While acknowledging the value of the `>` prefixes for distinguishing questions from answers, he identifies the lack of blank lines between questions and answers as a more significant readability issue.

He proposes several alternative approaches to encourage better formatting by reporters:
1. Adding more blank lines between questions
2. Using numbered questions (Q1/A1 style)
3. Adding placeholder text for answers (though notes this might annoy users)

Junio specifically questions whether the introductory text needs `>` prefixes since users won't be writing responses there. His comments suggest the current patch is a step in the right direction but could be enhanced further to address spacing issues that make real-world bug reports hard to parse.

This feedback indicates the patch may need another revision to incorporate these spacing suggestions while maintaining the core prefixing improvement. The tone is constructive, focusing on practical improvements rather than rejecting the current approach.
