This is the fourth version of Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove global state dependencies from the commit-graph subsystem, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series has been refined through several rounds of review, with v4 dropping some earlier patches that addressed `-Wsign-compare` warnings to focus on the core architectural changes.

The 6-patch series systematically eliminates remaining uses of `the_hash_algo` and `the_repository` in the commit-graph code, moving to repository-specific hash algorithms and removing redundant repository parameters. Key changes include converting hash-algorithm-dependent macros to functions, storing hash algorithms directly rather than just their lengths, and refactoring `parse_commit_graph()` to take a repository parameter.

The series has seen significant discussion around integer type choices in previous versions, with maintainer Junio Hamano providing detailed feedback that Patrick has incorporated while maintaining backward compatibility. The current version appears ready for merging, having addressed all major technical concerns while advancing the project's goals of removing global state and enabling pluggable object databases. Reviewers including Derrick Stolee and Taylor Blau have already approved key parts of the series.
