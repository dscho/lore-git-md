This is a status update on the 3-patch series introducing the `--max-depth` option for Git's diff machinery. Patrick Steinhardt confirms all his previous review comments have been addressed in v2 of the series, which was originally authored by Jeff King and is now being shepherded by Toon Claes.

The series adds depth-limiting functionality to diff operations, similar to `git grep --max-depth`, with careful handling of edge cases and comprehensive test coverage. Key changes in v2 included unit tests for the `within_depth()` function, support for `--max-depth=-1` as unlimited depth, and various code quality enhancements like const-correctness and BUG() macro usage.

Junio Hamano had asked whether the series was ready for inclusion post-2.51, and Patrick's response indicates approval from a technical standpoint. The thread appears to be in its final stages, with no outstanding technical issues remaining before potential merge. The original motivation (optimizing operations like git-blame-tree) and implementation details remain unchanged from the v2 submission.
