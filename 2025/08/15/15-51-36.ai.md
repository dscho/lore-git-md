This is a final discussion email in the `git-jump` bugfix thread, confirming consensus on the solution's scope. Phillip Wood and Jeff King agree that the patch should not expand to handle quoted filenames (which weren't previously supported anyway) to avoid unnecessary complexity. The discussion notes that Git automatically quotes filenames containing control characters (including tabs), providing natural protection against ambiguity in the regex solution. Both maintainers prefer keeping the simple regex fix focused on space-containing filenames rather than adding Git.pm dependency for full quoting support, considering quoted filenames an edge case. This confirms the boundaries established in the v2 patch: supporting common cases while explicitly not handling quoted names, relying on Git's existing quoting behavior for control character protection.
