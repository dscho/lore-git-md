This is part 3/6 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove global state dependencies from the commit-graph subsystem, specifically targeting `the_repository` removal. The patch refactors `parse_commit_graph()` to take a `repository` parameter instead of `repo_settings`, allowing it to use the repository's hash algorithm rather than the global `the_hash_algo`.

The change is straightforward but has important architectural implications - it moves the commit-graph parsing logic one step closer to being repository-agnostic. The patch includes a special case for the fuzzer tests, which don't have a fully initialized repository, by manually setting `settings.initialized`. It also removes a redundant function declaration in the fuzzer code.

This continues the mechanical but important work of the series to eliminate global state. The changes are well-contained and maintain existing functionality while advancing the larger goal of making the commit-graph subsystem work with pluggable object databases. The patch appears ready for merging as part of the complete series.
