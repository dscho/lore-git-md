This is a follow-up discussion on a bugfix patch in the multi-pack-index (midx) series. The patch restores a missing error code propagation in `write_midx_internal()` that was accidentally dropped in a previous commit. While the patch was initially framed as cleanup, Junio Hamano points out it actually fixes behavior by properly propagating errors to callers in `builtin/multi-pack-index.c`.

Taylor Blau clarifies that while this is indeed a bugfix, it's separate from the critical segfault issue addressed in the first patch of the series. He agrees with the change but suggests adding a test case that would verify the behavior by intentionally corrupting a MIDX and observing the proper error handling. The discussion shows careful attention to error propagation paths in the MIDX code, with both reviewers recognizing this as more than just cleanup despite the original framing.
