The change eliminates the need for signed/unsigned comparison warnings while maintaining the same functionality - using the maximum uint32_t value to indicate no preferred pack rather than -1.

The patch carefully updates all comparisons to check for `NO_PREFERRED_PACK` rather than negative values, with Taylor Blau reviewing and approving Derrick Stolee's implementation.
