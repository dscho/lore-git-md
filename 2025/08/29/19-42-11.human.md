This is a refactoring patch in a series introducing Rust support to Git's xdiff subsystem. It's patch 7/15 in the series, which focuses on preparing xdiff for Rust integration through incremental cleanup and modernization.

The patch removes unnecessary forward declarations in xdiff/xprepare.c by reordering functions so that `xdl_prepare_env()` appears after all the functions it depends on, rather than needing declarations at the top. This is a straightforward code organization improvement that eliminates 66 lines of declarations while maintaining identical behavior. The change is mechanical but helps reduce clutter before the more substantial Rust integration work that follows in later patches.

The patch fits into the series' strategy of cleaning up the C codebase first before introducing Rust components. While minor on its own, these preparatory steps are important for the larger goal of making xdiff more maintainable and interoperable with Rust. The change is well-contained (only touching xprepare.c) and follows the series' pattern of careful, incremental modifications.
