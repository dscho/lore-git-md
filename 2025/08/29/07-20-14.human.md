This suggestion comes alongside ongoing work by Julia Evans on both a `linkgitglossary:` macro implementation and interactive HTML tooltips for git-scm.com. The discussion appears to be converging on technical solutions, with Haugsbakk's footnote idea representing an alternative presentation approach that could complement the existing linking mechanisms.
