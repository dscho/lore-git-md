This thread covers a routine localization update for Git 2.51.0, coordinated by Jiang Xin. The update includes translations in 10 languages, with significant contributions from 12 translators. The most substantial changes are in Catalan (3,000+ lines updated by Mikel Forcada), Vietnamese (1,500+ changes), and Swedish (1,100+ changes), while Chinese (both Simplified and Traditional) each received around 700 lines of updates. 

The timing suggests these updates are intended for the final 2.51.0 release, following the 2 candidate. As with most l10n updates, this appears to be a straightforward maintenance task without any controversial changes or special notes in the typical translation workflow. The changes represent ongoing maintenance rather than new functionality, with the net addition being about 2,200 lines after accounting for updates to existing translations.
