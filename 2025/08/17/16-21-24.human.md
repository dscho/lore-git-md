This is a final approval for the `git repo` command series from maintainer Junio Hamano. The email confirms the documentation formatting for the `--format` option follows Asciidoctor best practices (using `:::` rather than `;;` for description lists) and marks the series ready for merging into the `next` branch. 

The patch series (now at version 11) introduces a new `git repo` command hierarchy to handle repository metadata, migrating functionality from `git rev-parse`. The maintainer's approval comes after multiple rounds of review from mentors and other contributors, with all feedback addressed. The series includes thorough test coverage and documentation, and implements both human-readable and machine-parseable output formats.
