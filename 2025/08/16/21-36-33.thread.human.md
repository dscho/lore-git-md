This thread documents the final stages of the submodule gitdir path encoding series, now in its 8th iteration. The work enables configurable submodule gitdir paths through a new `extensions.submodulePathConfig` extension, implementing a four-tier fallback system (plain/encoded/numbered/hashed paths) with case-folding protection via RFC3986 encoding. The changes span multiple files including `submodule.c`, `builtin/submodule--helper.c`, and `setup.c`, with comprehensive test coverage in `t7425-submodule-gitdir-path-extension.sh`.

Adrian Ratiu has led the implementation, which has undergone extensive review from key contributors. The series has addressed all major technical concerns, including relative path validation, error message improvements, config naming standardization, and test isolation fixes. Patrick Steinhardt, a domain expert on this topic, has given his final approval with only two minor outstanding comments. Junio Hamano has already acked the series for merging, and other reviewers like Phillip Wood (design) and Ben Knoble (documentation) have signed off.
