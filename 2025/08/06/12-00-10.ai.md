Continuing the `the_repository` removal effort in the commit-graph subsystem, this patch converts two hash-algorithm-dependent macros into functions to eliminate their reliance on the global `the_hash_algo`. The macros `GRAPH_DATA_WIDTH` and `GRAPH_MIN_SIZE` are replaced with `graph_data_width()` and `graph_min_size()` functions that take the hash algorithm as a parameter. 

The change is mechanical but important for the commit-graph subsystem's transition away from global state. All call sites are updated to pass `the_hash_algo` explicitly, maintaining current behavior while removing the implicit dependency. This follows the pattern established earlier in the series and aligns with reviewer feedback about proper parameterization.

The patch touches core commit-graph validation logic (file size checking) and data structure access patterns, but makes no functional changes. It's a straightforward refactoring that continues the series' progression toward making the commit-graph code fully repository-agnostic. With key reviewers already approving the approach in earlier patches, this change should integrate cleanly into the v2 series that's considered merge-ready.
