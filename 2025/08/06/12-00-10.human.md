This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort in the commit-graph subsystem. Patch 5/10 in Patrick Steinhardt's series converts two hash-algorithm-dependent macros (`GRAPH_DATA_WIDTH` and `GRAPH_MIN_SIZE`) into functions that take the hash algorithm as a parameter, removing their reliance on the global `the_hash_algo` variable.

The change is mechanical but important for the series' goal of eliminating global state. The patch replaces the macro definitions with static functions (`graph_data_width()` and `graph_min_size()`) and updates all call sites to use these functions with `the_hash_algo` as an argument. This maintains the same behavior while making the hash algorithm dependency explicit.

The patch follows the established pattern in this well-reviewed series, which has already addressed all substantive feedback from key reviewers (Derrick Stolee and Taylor Blau) and incorporates maintainer input from Junio Hamano. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial at this point in the review process.
