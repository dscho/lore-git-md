Bugfix for interactive rebase's handling of merge commits with 'drop' command. This follows up on Johannes Sixt's patch that fixed error messages for merge commits with incompatible sequencer commands but missed the 'drop' case. The change is minimal - just adding TODO_DROP to the allowed cases in check_merge_commit_insn() alongside TODO_MERGE, preventing a BUG() from being triggered when dropping merge commits.

Junio C Hamano's response acknowledges the fix while musing about whether exhaustive switch statements could help catch such omissions, though he notes this isn't always practical. The patch includes a test case verifying that dropping merge commits now works without errors. While the change is straightforward, the discussion highlights how subtle gaps in switch statement coverage can lead to bugs even in well-tested code.
