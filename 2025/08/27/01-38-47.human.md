This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to migrate packfile management to `struct packfile_store`. The patch proposes removing the redundant `get_packed_git()` function in favor of `get_all_packs()`, which provides more complete packfile information including MIDX (multi-pack index) data.

Taylor Blau's review raises several thoughtful points about the change. While generally supportive of the consolidation, he identifies two potential issues in the conversion: `cmd_grep()`'s subtle handling of packfiles and `want_object_in_pack_mtime()` potentially double-processing MIDX packs. He suggests the patch message could better explain why the conversion is safe for all callers.

The discussion reveals an interesting tension - while having two similar functions is confusing, there may be legitimate cases where callers specifically want non-MIDX packs. Taylor floats the idea of more explicit naming (like `get_non_midx_packs()`) if keeping both variants. The patch appears technically sound but would benefit from addressing these edge cases and improving the justification in the commit message.

This continues the series' pattern of careful API refinement, with reviewers paying close attention to subtle behavioral differences and performance implications. The discussion highlights how even straightforward-seeming refactors in core Git infrastructure require deep consideration of edge cases.
