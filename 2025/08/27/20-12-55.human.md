This is a policy discussion in the ongoing Rust infrastructure thread, specifically addressing security backporting concerns. Taylor Blau responds to Junio Hamano's pushback about the underestimated costs of maintaining dual C/Rust support for security fixes.

Blau clarifies that while he acknowledges the additional maintenance burden of supporting both codebases, he believes the Git security team has demonstrated capability in backporting fixes to older versions. He reiterates his earlier point that Rust's potential security advantages might reduce the frequency of needed backports compared to C.

The exchange highlights the core tension in the Rust adoption debate: Blau emphasizes the project's track record and potential security benefits, while Junio remains concerned about the practical sustainability of maintaining parallel implementations. The discussion remains at an impasse, with Blau offering reassurance but no concrete solution to the maintenance cost concerns.
