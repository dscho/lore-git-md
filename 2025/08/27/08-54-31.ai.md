This is a follow-up in the SMTP autoconfiguration feature thread, addressing two critical security and sustainability concerns raised by Brian Carlson. Aditya Garg responds to Brian's questions about Mozilla ISPDB integration and SSL verification.

The email focuses on seeking Mozilla's explicit permission for Git to use their ISPDB service long-term, given Git's widespread deployment and potential for high-volume automated usage. Garg directly contacts Brendan, the maintainer of Mozilla's autoconfig code, to get official approval. This addresses Brian's concern about embedding external services without considering their maintenance implications and potential for accidental DDoS.

Garg also acknowledges Brian's second point about SSL verification in HTTP::Tiny, agreeing to implement `verify_SSL => 1` if Mozilla approves the ISPDB usage. This would fix a security vulnerability in older Perl versions where certificate verification was disabled by default.

The response shows the patch author taking appropriate steps to resolve the two remaining blockers before this feature could be considered for inclusion: Mozilla's service permission and proper SSL verification. The thread remains pending until Mozilla-related concerns are clarified, with all other technical aspects of the SMTP autoconfiguration feature appearing ready.
