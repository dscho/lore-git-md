Performance optimization for `git ls-files` sparse index handling. This is a follow-up to Derrick Stolee's earlier patch that optimized sparse directory expansion during pathspec matching. The patch had already received positive technical reviews from Elijah Newren and Junio Hamano, confirming its safety regarding index ordering invariants.

Stolee notes this optimization may be more broadly useful than initially thought, citing a recent user report where `git mergetool` triggered unnecessary sparse index expansion due to its use of `ls-files` with pathspecs. The original change moves `ensure_full_index()` inside the iteration loop, expanding only when encountering sparse directories that match the pathspec rather than doing full expansion upfront.

The discussion has validated both the technical approach (with reviewers confirming index stability during mid-iteration expansion) and the practical value (with real-world cases now emerging where the optimization helps). While small in scope, this appears to be a well-vetted improvement to sparse index behavior that's ready for inclusion.
