This thread discusses the final steps in changing Git's default initial branch name from "master" to "main" for users who enable the WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES compile-time option. The 6-patch series, building on a 2020 deprecation warning, has seen extensive review and is nearing completion.

Phillip Wood has addressed most feedback and is preparing a revised version. Key technical decisions include maintaining backward compatibility while updating documentation and tests to reflect the new default. The test infrastructure strategy has settled on a compromise: keeping the existing GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_INITIAL_BRANCH_NAME variable while leaving open the possibility of a more branch-agnostic TEST_DEFAULT_BRANCH approach in the future. Patrick Steinhardt has now confirmed agreement with this pragmatic middle ground, noting that most tests have already been converted from "master" to "main" references.

With this test strategy resolved and documentation clarifications being incorporated, the series appears ready for final revision. The discussion has addressed implementation details, migration considerations, and impacts on various hosting platforms, with no outstanding objections remaining on the technical approach.
