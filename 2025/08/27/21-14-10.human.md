This is a refinement discussion about the deprecation warning messaging for `git whatchanged` and other soon-to-be-removed commands. The thread has been working on improving the user guidance steps shown in the error message, with previous patches establishing a three-step approach (read docs, check mailing list, contact maintainers). 

Junio Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's suggestion about rewording the contact prompt, agreeing that the current "let us know" phrasing might generate too much noise from individual users. He proposes instead focusing the contact suggestion on tool developers who might be affected at scale, suggesting wording that specifically asks lead developers of widely-used tools to notify the list if they see breakage reports from their users.

The discussion shows careful consideration being given to balancing user education against maintainer workload, with the goal of catching systemic issues (like Jenkins integration problems) while avoiding individual user reports. This continues the thread's pattern of refining the messaging based on practical maintainer experience.
