This is the final UX discussion in the `core.commentChar=auto` deprecation series. Oswald Buddenhagen weighs in on Junio Hamano's question about how to handle the transition from warnings to hard errors for the deprecated 'auto' setting.

He suggests including guidance to consult the manual for alternatives, making this a teachable moment rather than just an error.

The exchange represents the last open question in this otherwise technically complete series, which has already addressed all implementation details across multiple revisions. The maintainer will now decide between two approaches: Buddenhagen's proposed explicit rejection with explanation, or a simpler hard rejection without special handling of the 'auto" string.
