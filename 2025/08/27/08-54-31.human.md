This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about adding SMTP autoconfiguration to `git send-email` via the `--get-smtp-server` option. The thread has progressed through several versions of the patch, with most technical concerns addressed in v4. 

Aditya Garg responds to Brian Carlson's two remaining concerns about the patch: Mozilla ISPDB service permissions and SSL verification. Brian raised important questions about whether Mozilla is comfortable with Git potentially generating significant load on their ISPDB service for many years to come, given Git's long lifespan in distributions. He also noted a security issue where older Perl versions of HTTP::Tiny don't verify SSL certificates by default.

Aditya acknowledges these concerns and directly reaches out to Brendan, the maintainer of Mozilla's autoconfig code, to get official permission for Git to use the ISPDB service. He indicates the SSL verification issue will be addressed if Mozilla approves the ISPDB usage.

This email moves the final decision point to Mozilla's maintainers while showing the patch authors are taking the sustainability and security concerns seriously. The patch series appears well-developed otherwise, with comprehensive fallback methods and good documentation, but these two issues must be resolved before inclusion.
