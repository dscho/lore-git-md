This thread has been working through the final details of a series to improve Git's command deprecation system, particularly around handling aliases that shadow deprecated commands. The v5 iteration, which has maintainer approval for its core approach, adds alias shadowing support with recursion detection and better user messaging.

The latest discussion focuses on a minor but important edge case in alias recursion detection, where Jeff King identified that the `-h` flag could produce redundant messages when encountering alias loops. Kristoffer Haugsbakk has confirmed Jeff's proposed fix works as intended, though it initially caused some test failures. This affects all aliases, not just deprecated ones, and Kristoffer plans to incorporate the change in the next version.

The series is otherwise complete, with all major technical aspects settled, comprehensive test coverage, and full documentation updates. Junio Hamano approved the core approach in v4, and the current discussion represents the final polish before integration. Key contributors like Jeff King, Eric Sunshine, and Patrick Steinhardt have provided review feedback throughout the series' evolution.
