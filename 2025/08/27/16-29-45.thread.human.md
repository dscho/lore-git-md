This thread documents Kristoffer Haugsbakk's series improving guidance around deprecated commands, particularly `git whatchanged`. The four-patch series has been well received, with Junio Hamano providing positive feedback on the documentation improvements and technical approach. 

The series adds structured error messages with actionable steps when users encounter deprecated functionality, including a specific `git log --raw --no-merges` equivalent for `git whatchanged`. It also cleans up documentation by removing outdated claims while preserving factual deprecation information. The only outstanding issue was a whitespace inconsistency in `builtin/log.c`, which Junio has now confirmed is being fixed rather than introduced - a positive sign that the series is in good shape.

With the maintainer's approval of the whitespace fix and no other objections raised, this series appears ready for potential inclusion. The changes provide clearer guidance for users while maintaining backward compatibility, addressing a clear pain point in the transition away from deprecated commands.
