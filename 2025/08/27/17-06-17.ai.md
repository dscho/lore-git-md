Junio Hamano responds to the ongoing Rust infrastructure discussion, specifically addressing Randall Becker's concerns about platform support for HPE NonStop. This is part of the broader thread about introducing Rust into Git's xdiff implementation.

Hamano pushes back against Taylor Blau's earlier assessment that maintaining dual support (C and Rust) for security fixes would be manageable. He argues that Blau significantly underestimates the maintenance burden of supporting both code paths, particularly for commercial platforms like NonStop. While acknowledging Becker's position as a maintainer for an actively supported commercial x86_64 POSIX platform, Hamano suggests that such vendors should bear the primary responsibility for supporting their platforms when Rust transition necessitates it.

The email reinforces the fundamental policy divide in the thread: whether Rust's benefits justify excluding certain platforms versus the project's traditional portability commitments. Hamano's position leans toward making platform support the vendor's responsibility when language transitions create compatibility challenges, though he frames this as a practical consideration rather than an outright rejection of Becker's concerns. This continues the thread's evolution from technical implementation details toward higher-level project policy questions about language adoption criteria.
