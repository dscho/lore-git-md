This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to migrate packfile management to `struct packfile_store`. The patch aims to standardize how packfiles are added to the Most Recently Used (MRU) list when they're added to the packfile store.

Taylor Blau raises several technical concerns about the change. He questions why MIDX (multi-pack index) packs are being added to the MRU cache at all, since they appear to be handled separately in the code and don't actually participate in the MRU behavior. He notes that the current patch would make all pack additions (not just MIDX packs) add to the MRU cache, which might not be desirable for packs that haven't yet been accessed.

The discussion reveals deeper questions about the MRU cache's design and whether MIDX packs should participate in it at all. Taylor suggests that rather than standardizing the MRU addition behavior, it might be better to remove MIDX packs from the MRU cache since they don't seem to benefit from it. This patch appears to the ongoing architectural discussion about how packfiles should be managed in the new store abstraction, with particular attention to cache behavior and consistency.
