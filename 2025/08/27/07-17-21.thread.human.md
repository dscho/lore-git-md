This thread documents a small but long-overdue improvement to Git's man pages. It turns out that both `git cherry-pick` and `git revert` have accepted standard revision-list options like `--no-walk` and `--left-right` since 2010, but these options were never properly documented - they only appeared in examples without being formally listed. Andrew Jeffery (not Jeffrey, as initially misspelled) originally spotted this for `git cherry-pick`.

Emily Shaffer stepped in to fix the oversight, first sending a patch for just `git cherry-pick` (v1) and then expanding it to cover `git revert` as well in v2. The change follows Git's standard pattern for documenting these shared options by including the common `rev-list-options.adoc` file. While the options documentation is somewhat verbose, everyone agrees it's better than having undocumented features.
