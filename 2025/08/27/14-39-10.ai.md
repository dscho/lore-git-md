This is a policy discussion in the ongoing Rust infrastructure thread, specifically addressing platform support concerns. Randall Becker from HPE NonStop responds to Taylor Blau's arguments about Rust adoption's portability impact.

Becker acknowledges Blau's points about security backporting practices and the reality of platform limitations, but emphasizes that HPE NonStop is a commercially supported x86_64 POSIX platform that would be left behind by Rust adoption. His email serves as a reminder that the affected platforms aren't obscure or abandoned systems, but actively maintained commercial environments.

The exchange highlights the tension between Rust's security benefits and the project's historical portability commitments. Becker's position represents the perspective of platforms that would be excluded by Rust adoption despite having modern architectures and vendor support. This continues the thread's central policy debate about whether the benefits justify breaking compatibility with certain platforms, now with specific focus on NonStop's commercial status.
