This is a discussion about patch 11 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. The patch aims to standardize MRU (most-recently-used) list handling by always adding packs to the MRU when they're added to the store, rather than waiting until first access.

Taylor Blau raises several technical concerns about the change. He questions why MIDX (multi-pack-index) packs are being added to the MRU at all, since they appear to be handled separately in lookup paths and never moved to the front of the MRU list. He traces this behavior back to commit af96fe3392 but finds its rationale unclear. Taylor also notes that some existing callers of `packfile_store_add_pack()` intentionally don't add packs to the MRU immediately, suggesting the proposed change might be too eager.

The key technical points are:
1. The patch moves MRU list addition into `packfile_store_add_pack()`
2. MIDX packs may not need MRU handling at all
3. Some subsystems deliberately defer MRU entries until first access
4. The change affects midx.c and packfile.c

This discussion touches on thread focus areas #10 (MRU list standardization) and #4 (API design clarity). It suggests the patch might need reconsideration or that MIDX pack handling could be simplified further. The locking issue from patch 9 remains the series' primary open concern, but this MRU discussion now presents another design question to resolve.
