This is part 8 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch continues the mechanical conversion of packfile-related functions to operate on the packfile store rather than the repository object.

The key changes in this patch:
- Renames `prepare_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_prepare()` and updates its signature to take a `packfile_store` instead of `repository`
- Similarly renames `prepare_packed_git_mru()` to `packfile_store_prepare_mru()`
- Inlines the small `rearrange_packed_git()` helper into `packfile_store_prepare()`
- Updates all callers (about a dozen locations) to use the new function name and pass the packfile store
- Maintains identical behavior while clarifying the function's scope through naming

The changes are straightforward mechanical updates following the pattern established in earlier patches. The patch affects core packfile operations but doesn't modify any behavior - it just moves the plumbing to work through the new abstraction layer. All existing callers are updated consistently, including functions dealing with:
- Packfile MRU (most-recently-used) list management
- Multi-pack-index handling
- Object count approximation
- Packfile entry lookup

This continues the series' systematic migration of packfile-specific functionality out of the generic object_database structure. The changes are well-contained to packfile.c with no ripple effects to other subsystems. The commit message clearly explains the scope of the change and the patch follows the established pattern of the series.
