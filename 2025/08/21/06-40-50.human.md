This is part 14 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The patch removes the redundant `get_packed_git()` function, consolidating all packfile access through `get_all_packs()`.

The key insight is that these two functions were nearly identical - both load packfiles via `packfile_store_prepare()`, with `get_all_packs()` doing slightly more work to properly mark MIDX-indexed packs. Since the performance difference is negligible and all callers can handle the extra MIDX preparation, the simpler single-function approach makes sense.

Patrick acknowledges Karthik Nayak's feedback about unclear commit message wording and MIDX edge cases, promising to clarify both in the revised message. The change affects packfile access in `builtin/gc.c` and `builtin/grep.c`, but maintains identical behavior while simplifying the interface.

This continues the series' steady progress in cleaning up packfile management, with careful attention to edge cases and reviewer feedback. The change is mechanical but meaningful, removing a subtle distinction that could confuse future maintainers.
