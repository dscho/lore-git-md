This is part 10 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The patch refactors `install_packed_git()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than taking a full repository parameter, renaming it to `packfile_store_add_pack()` to better reflect its purpose.

The change is mechanical but wide-ranging, updating callers in fast-import.c, index-pack.c, http.c, midx.c, and packfile.c to use the new interface. The function's core behavior remains the same - it adds a packfile to the store's linked list and hashmap - but the API now properly reflects that this is a packfile-specific operation rather than a general repository one.

This continues the series' pattern of moving packfile operations out of the generic object database infrastructure. The patch appears well-executed with thorough updates to all affected call sites. Since this is part of a larger, carefully reviewed series that has already established the architectural direction, the changes here are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial.
