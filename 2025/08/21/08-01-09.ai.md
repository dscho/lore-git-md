Bugfix discussion follow-up regarding `git repack --path-walk` initialization issue.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Derrick Stolee's patch fixing the `maybe_interesting` flag initialization in path-walk's pending objects setup. While acknowledging the fix addresses the immediate issue where tracked index paths could be incorrectly omitted during repacking, Steinhardt raises concerns about the fragility of the `maybe_interesting` mechanism itself.

The key technical point under discussion is whether the performance optimization provided by the `maybe_interesting` flag (introduced in commit 6333e7ae0b) justifies its maintenance complexity. Steinhardt suggests two potential paths forward: either removing the optimization entirely if its performance impact is minimal, or (as the patch series proposes) formalizing it through dedicated APIs to prevent similar initialization bugs.

This follows the established thread context where:
- Patch 2's technical approach was already validated by Junio Hamano
- The fix specifically targets `setup_pending_objects()` in `path-walk.c`
- The series includes a planned refactoring (patch 3) to consolidate initialization patterns

Steinhardt's comment doesn't challenge the correctness of the current fix but questions the underlying design's value versus complexity tradeoff. The discussion now includes consideration of whether to keep the optimization at all, alongside the ongoing work to improve test coverage for REUC/TREE extension cases noted by other reviewers.
