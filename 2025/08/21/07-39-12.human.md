Packfile refactoring: This patch removes the redundant `get_packed_git()` function in favor of `get_all_packs()` as part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure.

The change simplifies the API by eliminating one of two similar functions that both retrieve packfiles but with subtle differences in MIDX (multi-pack index) handling. While `get_packed_git()` only loaded basic packfile info, `get_all_packs()` also prepares MIDX metadata - a lightweight operation since the packfiles and MIDX are already loaded. The patch updates callers in gc.c, grep.c, and object-name.c to use the more comprehensive function.

This is a straightforward cleanup that follows the series' pattern of consolidating packfile-related functionality. The change maintains identical behavior while reducing API surface area and potential confusion between the two similar functions.
