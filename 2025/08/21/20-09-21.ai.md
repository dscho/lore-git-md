This is the final patch in a documentation series improving the `git-add` man page, specifically focusing on clarifying how ignored files are handled. The patch simplifies the explanation of ignored file behavior while maintaining all key technical details. 

The change replaces a more verbose and somewhat confusing explanation with a clearer, more direct description.
