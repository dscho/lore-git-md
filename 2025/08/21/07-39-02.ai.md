This is patch 4/16 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the packfile hashmap from `struct object_database` to the new store structure, continuing the mechanical relocation of packfile-specific state.

Key technical changes:
- Removes `pack_map` field from `struct object_database` and adds it to `struct packfile_store` as `map`
- Moves initialization of the hashmap from `odb_new()` to `packfile_store_new()`
- Updates all packfile lookup calls to use `store->map` instead of the old ODB field
- Makes `pack_map_entry_cmp()` static since it's now only used within packfile.c
- Maintains identical behavior while changing the underlying storage location

The patch affects packfile lookup paths in midx.c and packfile.c, requiring updates to several functions that check for already-loaded packfiles. The changes are straightforward mechanical moves following the pattern established in earlier patches, with no functional impact on packfile loading or lookup behavior.

This continues the series' systematic approach to separating packfile concerns from the generic object database infrastructure, preparing for future pluggable ODB backends. The patch builds on previous work moving the packfile list and MRU tracking, with subsequent patches in the series continuing to relocate other packfile-specific state.
