This is part 15 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch continues the mechanical conversion of packfile-related functions to operate on the new store structure rather than directly on repositories.

The specific change here modifies `get_all_packs()` to take a `packfile_store` parameter instead of a repository, maintaining the same functionality while better reflecting its actual scope. Karthik Nayak had suggested some style cleanups (removing unnecessary curly braces in single-statement loops), but Patrick prefers to defer those as "while-at-it" changes to keep the focus on the structural refactoring.

The patch affects packfile.c and builtin/gc.c, where `get_all_packs()` is called. This continues the series' pattern of methodically moving packfile operations to the new abstraction layer while preserving existing behavior. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, following the architectural direction already established in earlier patches.

Key technical details:
- Function signature change from `get_all_packs(struct repository *)` to `get_all_packs(struct packfile_store *)`
- Maintains same packfile list access pattern via `store->packs`
- Still includes MIDX pack preparation via `prepare_midx_pack()`
- Call sites in gc.c updated to first get store from repository

This patch fits cleanly into the series' progression and doesn't introduce new design questions. The only open discussion point is whether to incorporate Karthik's suggested style tweaks now or leave them for future cleanup.
