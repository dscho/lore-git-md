This is a discussion about testing methodology in the context of the ongoing `git repack --path-walk` bugfix series. Junio Hamano responds to Derrick Stolee's defense of the "failing test first" approach used in the patch series.

The core disagreement centers on whether to split test demonstrations from their fixes. Stolee advocates for the current approach where patch 1 shows a failing test and patch 2 fixes it, arguing this allows verification that the test properly catches the bug. Hamano counters that this separation makes it harder to understand the test's purpose during review, and suggests combining test and fix in one patch since reviewers can always test the failure case by checking out previous versions.

The exchange highlights a philosophical difference in patch presentation styles, with no immediate resolution. This doesn't affect the technical correctness of the bugfix series, but may influence how future test-and-fix pairs are structured in Git development. The discussion remains collegial, with Hamano acknowledging Stolee's methodology while firmly stating his preference for combined patches.
