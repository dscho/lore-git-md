This is part 8 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The patch focuses on `prepare_packed_git()` and related functions, converting them to operate directly on the packfile store rather than going through the repository object.

The key change is renaming and restructuring `prepare_packed_git()` as `packfile_store_prepare()`, along with its helper functions, to make their scope clearer. The new version takes a `packfile_store` parameter instead of a `repository`, though all callers still pass the store via `r->objects->packfiles`. The behavior remains identical - it still handles packfile and multi-pack-index preparation, MRU list setup, and pack sorting.

This is a mechanical change that follows the series' pattern of gradually moving packfile-specific operations out of the generic object database code. The patch touches several key packfile operations including MRU list management, packfile loading, and object count approximation.
