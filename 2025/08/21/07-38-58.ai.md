This is a refactoring series introducing a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management in Git's object database (ODB) subsystem. The series is part of the ongoing effort to enable pluggable ODB backends by moving packfile-specific state out of the generic `struct object_database`.

The 16-patch series systematically relocates all packfile-related state and operations from `object_database` to the new `packfile_store` structure. Key changes include moving the packfile list, MRU list, packfile map, and kept pack cache into the new store structure. The series also refactors related functions to operate on the packfile store rather than directly on the repository or ODB.

Version 2 includes minor improvements like converting an initialization flag to a boolean, polishing commit messages, and adjusting formatting. The changes are mechanical refactorings that maintain existing behavior while improving encapsulation. The series affects numerous files across the codebase but focuses on packfile.c/h and odb.c/h.

This foundational work enables future steps to:
1. Move packfile list pointers into the store (currently still in `packed_git`)
2. Push the packfile store down to the ODB source level
3. Support pluggable ODB backends

The thread shows careful review of design decisions, with discussion around MRU list handling, MIDX pack preparation, and code style. Patrick Steinhardt has addressed previous feedback and deferred some style suggestions to keep focus on the structural changes. The series represents significant progress in the ODB abstraction effort while maintaining careful attention to architectural soundness.
