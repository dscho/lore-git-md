Bugfix series discussion about test presentation methodology in the `git repack --path-walk` fixes.

Junio Hamano weighs in on the debate about whether to split the failing test case into a separate patch (as currently done in patch 1) versus including it with the fix (patch 2). He acknowledges Derrick Stolee's preference for the failing-test-first approach but argues that for reviewers, seeing the test and fix together provides better context. Hamano notes that reviewers can always check the pre-fix behavior by building from HEAD^ if needed, making the separate test less valuable. He requests future patches combine test and fix unless there's a strong reason not to.

This continues the thread's theme of balancing development practices against review ergonomics. While not challenging the technical correctness of the fix, it represents a style preference from the maintainer that may influence how future bugfix patches are structured.
