This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. Patch 2/16 moves the core `packed_git` list from `struct object_database` to the new store structure, along with related cleanup operations.

The patch relocates the main packfile list pointer (`packed_git`) and its associated cleanup logic from the ODB to the packfile store. It introduces new `packfile_store_close()` and `packfile_store_free()` functions to handle packfile cleanup, replacing direct manipulation in `odb_clear()`. The changes are mechanical but wide-ranging, updating all packfile iteration sites to use `store->packs` instead of the old `odb->packed_git` path.

Key technical details:
- Adds `packs` field to `struct packfile_store` to host the packfile list
- Moves packfile cleanup from `odb_clear()` to new `packfile_store_free()`
- Extracts packfile closing logic into new `packfile_store_close()`
- Updates ~15 packfile.c functions to reference the new location
- Maintains identical behavior while changing access paths

The changes follow the established pattern from earlier patches in the series, continuing the mechanical relocation of packfile-specific state from the generic ODB to the dedicated store structure. This prepares for future pluggable ODB backends by further isolating packfile management. The patch appears straightforward with no behavioral changes, just structural reorganization.
