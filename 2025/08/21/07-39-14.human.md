This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The patch converts `get_packed_git_mru()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than going through the repository, following the pattern established in previous patches.

The change is straightforward - the function is renamed to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` and updated to take a `packfile_store` parameter instead of a `repository`. The implementation remains essentially the same, just operating on the store's MRU list directly. The patch updates the single caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to use the new function name and access the store through the repository->objects->packfiles path.

This completes the mechanical transition of all packfile-related functions to work with the new store structure, a key step in the series' goal of separating packfile management from the generic object database. The change maintains identical behavior while improving architectural clarity. As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a well-executed, focused change that follows the established pattern.
