This patch continues Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into the new `struct packfile_store`. It specifically addresses an inconsistency in how packfiles are added to the most-recently-used (MRU) list.

The patch standardizes MRU list handling by moving the MRU addition into `packfile_store_add_pack()` itself, rather than having some callers do it separately. This eliminates the inconsistency where some subsystems (like midx.c) were manually adding to the MRU after calling the add function, while others weren't. The change affects both midx.c (which removes its manual MRU addition) and packfile.c (which now handles it centrally).

This is a logical next step in the series that follows up on earlier discussion about MRU list design (noted in the thread summary where Jeff King suggested potential consolidation of packfile lists). The change maintains identical behavior while making it more consistent - packfiles will now always be MRU-tracked from the moment they're added to the store. The implementation is straightforward and mechanical, affecting only 2 files with minimal changes.

Key technical details:
- Moves MRU list addition from midx.c into `packfile_store_add_pack()`
- Updates both the packfile hashmap and MRU list in one atomic operation
- Maintains same MRU ordering (using `list_add_tail`)
- Follows the series' pattern of centralizing packfile operations in the store abstraction

The patch appears well-contained and addresses a clear inconsistency that was noted in earlier thread discussion about MRU handling. It doesn't introduce new behavior, just makes existing behavior more consistent.
