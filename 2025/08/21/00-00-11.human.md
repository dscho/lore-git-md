This is the final patch in a three-part series refactoring Git's bulk-checkin subsystem as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series systematically eliminates global state from the code that handles writing multiple objects to the object database in a single transaction.

Junio Hamano, Git's maintainer, responds positively to Justin Tobler's patch series, acknowledging that the global variables being removed were originally his own oversight. His brief reply serves as an informal maintainer approval, with the joking tone ("Thanks for cleaning after me") suggesting the changes are welcome and uncontroversial.

The technical work was completed in the first three patches, which consolidated transaction state into `struct object_database` and wired through repository parameters to all bulk-checkin functions. This reply indicates the series is likely to proceed without further discussion, representing another step forward in Git's architectural cleanup efforts.
