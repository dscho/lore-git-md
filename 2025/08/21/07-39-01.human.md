This is part 3 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch relocates the `packed_git_initialized` flag from `struct object_database` to the new store structure, where it more logically belongs as part of packfile-specific state.

The change is straightforward - the initialization bit that tracks whether packfiles have been loaded is moved from the generic ODB structure to the packfile store, and converted to a boolean type in the process. The patch updates three call sites to reference the flag through its new location. This continues the series' pattern of systematically moving packfile-related state out of the object database into the dedicated store structure.

As with previous patches in the series, this is a mechanical change that maintains identical behavior while improving code organization. The patch is well-contained and follows the established pattern of the refactoring effort. It touches the same key files as other patches in the series (odb.h, packfile.c, packfile.h) and helps advance the overall goal of separating packfile management from the generic object database functionality.
