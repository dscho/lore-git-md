This is part 3 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch relocates the `packed_git_initialized` flag from `struct object_database` to the new store structure, converting it to a boolean type in the process.

The change is mechanical - the flag's functionality remains identical, just moved to its more logical home in the packfile store. The patch updates three key locations in packfile.c where the flag is checked or modified: during initial packfile preparation, reprepare operations, and when clearing the loose object cache. The commit message clearly explains the motivation for moving this bit to the new structure.

This continues the series' pattern of systematically moving packfile-related state out of the generic ODB structure. The changes are straightforward and maintain existing behavior while improving code organization. The boolean conversion is a minor modernization that follows Git's coding guidelines.

For future reference, this patch touches the initialization state tracking for packfiles, moving it from `object_database->packed_git_initialized` to `packfile_store->initialized` while converting from a bitfield to a boolean. The changes affect packfile preparation and reprepare logic in packfile.c, with no functional impact on packfile operations themselves.
