This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch specifically relocates the MRU (most-recently-used) list of packfiles from the object database structure to the new store.

The discussion reveals some historical context from Jeff King about why Git maintains both a regular packfile list and an MRU-ordered list - originally kept separate to avoid breaking order-dependent code. Patrick notes there are currently a few places where packfiles aren't added to the MRU list, but his series will standardize this behavior in later patches.

Both Jeff and Patrick agree that consolidating the two lists into a single MRU-ordered list would be a good future optimization, but should be done in a separate follow-up series after this refactoring is complete. The patch appears to be a straightforward mechanical move of the MRU list field as part of the larger packfile store abstraction effort.
