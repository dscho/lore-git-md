This thread examines an inconsistency in how `transfer.hideRefs` filters references differently for packed versus loose refs in `upload-pack`. The discussion began with a bug report noting that after commit 59c35fa, clients saw different branch visibility depending on whether refs were packed. Taylor Blau corrected the analysis, identifying commit 18b6b1b5c5 as the actual source of the behavior change.

The original reporter has now provided a concrete reproduction case demonstrating the issue: when `transfer.hideRefs=refs/heads/sura` is set, loose refs (like `sura-001`) remain visible, but after `git gc` packs the refs, they become hidden. This confirms the packed refs implementation uses more aggressive front-matching compared to the loose refs handling.

With both the technical context (from Taylor) and a clear reproduction, the thread now has what it needs to evaluate solutions. The key question remains whether to align the behaviors (either by making loose refs skip hidden refs at the filesystem level like packed refs, or adjusting packed refs to match loose behavior) or document this as an expected difference. The reproduction shows this can affect real workflows when server-side GC operations change ref visibility unexpectedly. Maintainers and refs subsystem experts like Patrick Steinhardt or Jeff King may now weigh in on the preferred resolution.
