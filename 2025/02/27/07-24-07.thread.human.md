Jeff King identified commit 59c35fac54 as the root cause after initial confusion with 18b6b1b5c5.

This expands the scope from aligning just packed/loose behavior to ensuring consistency across all three storage backends. While the core technical approach (modifying packed-backend.c's jump list logic) remains valid, the solution must now account for reftable's behavior too.
