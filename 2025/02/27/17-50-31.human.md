This is the final approval for a documentation patch series that clarifies Git's submodule conflict resolution behavior in merge strategies. 

The patch series, now in its fourth iteration, documents previously implicit behavior where Git attempts fast-forward merges for submodules when one commit is a descendant of another, and suggests common ancestors when available. These behaviors were implemented years ago but never properly documented until now.

Maintainer Junio Hamano and merge strategy expert Elijah Newren have both reviewed and approved the changes, with no further modifications needed. The documentation covers both the modern 'ort' strategy and legacy 'recursive' strategy, maintaining consistency between them despite their different implementations.

With this final "looks good" from Newren and Hamano's acknowledgment, the series appears ready to be merged into Git's main documentation. The changes are purely explanatory - they don't modify any runtime behavior - but will make this important submodule merge functionality more discoverable for users.
