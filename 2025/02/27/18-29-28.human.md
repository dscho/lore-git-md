This is a refactoring patch that simplifies cruft pack aggregation logic in `builtin/repack.c`. It's the first in a two-part series addressing object freshening behavior when working with multiple cruft packs.

The patch replaces a more complex algorithm for combining small cruft packs under `--max-cruft-size` with a simpler approach. Previously, Git would accumulate packs until their combined size reached the threshold, maintaining a running total. The new version simply combines all cruft packs smaller than the threshold individually, letting `pack-objects` handle any necessary splitting of the result.

The change results in slightly different behavior - packs are now combined opportunistically rather than in size-accumulated batches - but the author notes this difference is minor and removes a now-irrelevant test case that specifically checked the old accumulation behavior. The simplification removes about 40 lines of code including a custom sorting function and some bookkeeping logic.

This appears to be a clean, well-justified refactoring that was discovered while working on the more substantial object retention fixes in patch 2 of the series. The change is focused and comes with corresponding test updates, though reviewers may want to confirm the behavioral difference doesn't introduce any edge cases.
