The key technical changes introduce a new `ignore_packed_keep_in_core_has_cruft` flag and modify `want_found_object()` to properly compare mtimes when deciding whether to retain objects from existing cruft packs. The patch ensures we:
1. Preserve objects from non-cruft packs unconditionally
2. For cruft pack objects, only keep them if their mtime is older than the version being packed
3. Add comprehensive test coverage in `t/t7704-repack-cruft.sh` to verify the new mtime comparison logic

The changes primarily affect `builtin/pack-objects.c`, with supporting modifications to `packfile.c` and `packfile.h` to expose the `kept_pack_cache()` function. The implementation handles edge cases where mtimes can't be loaded by falling back to the pack's mtime.
