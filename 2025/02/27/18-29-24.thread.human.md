This thread discusses a finalized two-patch series by Taylor Blau that improves cruft pack handling during repacks. The first patch simplifies cruft pack aggregation by removing complex size-based logic in favor of directly combining all small packs, while the second patch fixes object freshening behavior in multi-cruft pack scenarios. Elijah Newren reviewed and approved both patches, with all technical questions resolved and commit messages finalized.

Just as the series appeared ready for merging, Patrick Steinhardt raised a new concern about the long-term implications of the simplification in Patch 1. He questions whether combining all small packs without size tracking might lead to perpetual repacking, as the resulting combined packs could remain under the size threshold and thus be candidates for combination in subsequent repacks. This introduces a new design-level consideration about pack stability that wasn't previously discussed, though it doesn't challenge the technical correctness of the implementation.

The core functionality remains well-tested and the patches are in final form, but this late-stage question about operational characteristics may need addressing before the series can proceed. Taylor will likely need to respond regarding whether the perpetual repacking scenario is a practical concern or if the simplification's benefits outweigh this potential downside.
