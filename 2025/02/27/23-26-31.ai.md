Bitmap iteration architecture discussion in cat-file filtering series.

Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that adds bitmap iteration support to the cat-file filtering series, focusing on architectural concerns. The key issue revolves around whether to use a callback-based approach (as proposed in Patrick's patch) versus direct EWAH iterator access. Taylor references his earlier work in commit 2189649b that introduced layered bitmap handling, suggesting the callback wrapper may be unnecessary given existing EWAH iterator capabilities.

The email reveals two important technical points: First, Taylor had previously considered but rejected a similar next-pointer chasing approach due to its error-prone nature. Second, he explicitly states his preference for direct iterator interaction over callbacks, which represents the primary outstanding technical decision blocking progress on this performance optimization series.

Taylor also notes that Patrick's review of his own pending bitmap work could help unblock the dependency situation, while maintaining his position that the current callback architecture in patch 6/9 may not be the right approach. This continues the thread's pattern of careful bitmap subsystem scrutiny, with both authors demonstrating deep familiarity with the performance-sensitive bitmap iteration requirements.
