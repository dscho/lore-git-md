This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to decouple the reftable library from Git-specific dependencies, now focusing on removing the `sleep_millisec()` helper.

Patch 12/18 in the v6 series replaces Git's `sleep_millisec()` with a direct call to `poll()` in the reftable stack code. The change is minimal - just swapping one sleep implementation for another - but continues the architectural goal of removing Git-specific utilities. The patch notes that while poll() isn't the most direct way to sleep, it's the POSIX-compliant alternative that provides millisecond precision.

The change affects retry logic in `reftable_stack_reload_maybe_reuse()`, where the code implements exponential backoff when retrying operations. This is a straightforward substitution that maintains the same behavior while removing another Git dependency. The patch follows the series' established pattern of methodically replacing Git utilities with standard or reftable-native equivalents.

As with previous patches in the series, this change is internal plumbing with no user-facing impact, and the maintainers have already approved the overall approach. The patch is well-contained (just one line changed) and follows the series' high standard of quality.
