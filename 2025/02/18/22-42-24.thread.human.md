This thread presents a two-patch series addressing Japanese filename encoding issues in gitk, Git's graphical interface. The originating author, Kazuhiro Kato, identified a problem where merge conflicts involving Japanese filenames were displayed incorrectly due to improper UTF-8 handling in the Tcl/Tk-based interface.

The first patch established proper UTF-8 encoding system initialization in gitk, creating the foundation for correct character handling. The just-arrived second patch builds on this by specifically fixing filename and content decoding during merge conflict display. It modifies the `parseblobdiffline` procedure to properly convert UTF-8 filenames while maintaining the raw versions for processing, ensuring Japanese characters appear correctly in the interface.

The changes appear focused and well-structured, though reviewers may want to verify the behavior across different platforms given the encoding-sensitive nature of the fixes. Notably, the series doesn't yet include test coverage for these platform-dependent scenarios. The patches complete the reported fix for garbled Japanese filenames during merges, with no outstanding technical questions raised so far. As this is a relatively straightforward bugfix for a specific interface issue, it may receive quick review attention.
