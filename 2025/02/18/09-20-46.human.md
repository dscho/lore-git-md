This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to decouple the reftable library from Git core dependencies. Patch 10/18 continues the systematic removal of Git-specific macros, this time targeting `ARRAY_SIZE()` in the reftable reader code.

The change is straightforward - it replaces a single use of Git's `ARRAY_SIZE()` macro with the standard C idiom `sizeof(array)/sizeof(*array)` when iterating through the `sections` array in the debug printing function. This is a clear continuation of the series' goal to make the reftable library self-contained, though it's one of the more minor changes in terms of impact.

The patch maintains the same functionality while removing another small Git dependency. Like previous patches in the series, it's a clean, focused change that doesn't affect user-facing behavior. The series as a whole appears to be in its final stages, with this being one of the remaining small cleanups before completion.
