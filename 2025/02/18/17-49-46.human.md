This is a platform compatibility discussion in the context of the completed series to eliminate `-Wsign-comparison` warnings in `apply.c`. Junio C Hamano raises a philosophical question about the type conversion strategy used in patch 2/6 of the series, where line number variables in `find_pos()` were changed from `int` to `size_t`.

Hamano points out that while the conversion appears safe in this case (since the values are known to be positive), the underlying parameter `line` remains `int` because it can temporarily hold negative values. He questions whether it's better to keep these variables as `int` and instead fix the places that want to compare them with `size_t` values, rather than changing their type to match.

This is not a blocking concern - the series has already been reviewed and the technical correctness validated - but rather a higher-level discussion about Git's type strategy. The exchange highlights the tension between strict type safety and maintaining the natural types that fit the semantic meaning of variables (line numbers being signed quantities in many contexts).

The thread shows the careful consideration given to even seemingly mechanical type changes in Git's codebase, with maintainers weighing both technical correctness and long-term architectural philosophy. While this particular series is complete, the discussion may influence future similar efforts.
