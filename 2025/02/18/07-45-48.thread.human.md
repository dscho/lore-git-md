This thread follows Patrick Steinhardt's 12-part series adding Meson build system support to Git's contrib components, building on prior Meson integration work. The series focuses on standardizing build infrastructure and wiring up remaining contrib items like credential helpers, gitk, and Coccinelle support.

The series shows steady progress, with wincred (incorporating Mirth's MSVC fix), osxkeychain, and now netrc credential helpers addressed. Remaining work includes git-gui, sparse, and perf tests in contrib/. The changes follow established Meson patterns while handling platform specifics like MSVC requirements and macOS keychain integration.
