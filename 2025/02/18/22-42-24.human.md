Bugfix for gitk's filename encoding issues, particularly affecting Japanese characters during merge conflict resolution. This two-patch series from Kazuhiro Kato addresses garbled display of Japanese filenames in gitk's interface, specifically when resolving merge conflicts. The changes modify gitk's core file to properly handle character encoding.

The patches are narrowly focused, touching just 7 lines in gitk-git/gitk with 5 additions and 2 deletions. While the commit messages are brief (the second even redundantly repeats the cover letter text), the problem being solved is clear - a real pain point for Japanese-speaking users working with non-ASCII filenames.

Given gitk's role as Git's built-in GUI, these encoding issues would directly impact usability for affected users. The changes appear straightforward, though reviewers might want to verify the solution handles other character sets beyond Japanese and consider whether similar fixes are needed elsewhere in gitk's filename handling.
