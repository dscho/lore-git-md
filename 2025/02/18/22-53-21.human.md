HTTP authentication regression follow-up from maintainer. Brian M. Carlson confirms the reported netrc-file authentication issue in git-http-push is indeed a problem, though notes the netrc support has always been undocumented and untested. He explains the proactiveAuth feature was specifically designed to work with credential helpers (which can provide tokens) rather than netrc files, since libcurl API doesn't expose whether netrc credentials would be available. 

The maintainer plans to investigate but warns it may take a few days to prepare a fix, suggesting users affected by the regression can temporarily use the git-credential-netrc helper from contrib as a workaround. This response indicates the issue is recognized as legitimate but may require careful consideration to fix without breaking the intended proactiveAuth behavior for credential helpers. The interaction highlights tension between supporting legacy netrc workflows and newer credential helper systems.
