This is a build system fix for the macOS credential helper, part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to add Meson support for Git's contrib components. 

The patch addresses a compilation warning in the osxkeychain helper by properly validating command-line arguments. The change replaces a simple NULL check with a more thorough validation that ensures at least two arguments exist (the command name plus an action) before proceeding. This matches the pattern seen in earlier patches for other credential helpers (libsecret and wincred) in the series.

The fix is minimal and focused - just a one-line change to the argument validation logic while maintaining the same behavior. Like the rest of the series, this keeps the credential helpers building cleanly across different build systems (Makefile, CMake, and now Meson) and platforms.

This continues the steady progress on the 12-part series, with the osxkeychain helper now joining libsecret and wincred in having its build warnings resolved. The change appears uncontroversial and follows established patterns from previous patches in the series.
