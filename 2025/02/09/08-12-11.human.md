Platform compatibility patch addressing `-Wsign-comparison` warnings in the apply subsystem. This is part 1 of a 6-patch series from Zejun Zhao's GSOC project to eliminate signed/unsigned comparison warnings.

The patch changes two fields in `struct apply_state` from `int` to unsigned types: `.max_change` becomes `unsigned` (as it's just a counter) and `.max_len` becomes `size_t` (as it represents a length). The changes ripple through to the `show_stats()` function where related variables are updated to match. The patch includes a necessary cast when passing `size_t` to `printf()`, with a note explaining this is acceptable since the value is bounded.

This appears to be a straightforward and well-justified change, continuing the thread's theme of type safety improvements while maintaining existing behavior. The patch is focused and comes with a clear explanation of the rationale, including the safety considerations around the printf cast. As part of an already-reviewed series, it seems likely to be uncontroversial.
