This is a review response to the promisor-remote/LOP patch series (v5), focusing on future extensions to the capability.

Patrick Steinhardt gives a positive review of Christian Couder's v5 series implementing the promisor-remote protocol capability, noting it's ready to land in its current form. While approving the core functionality, he identifies three natural extensions that could be built on top of this foundation: persisting advertised promisors in client config, handling dynamic updates when promisor sets change, and authentication integration for secured remotes. 

The response positions these as follow-up work rather than blockers, suggesting the current design leaves room for these enhancements without requiring architectural changes. This comes after significant thread discussion about security models, configuration design, and protocol mechanics that were resolved in earlier versions. The review confirms the series has reached a stable point where these future considerations can be addressed incrementally.
