Performance optimization discussion for git bisect.

Uwe Kleine-König responds to Junio Hamano's experimental midpoint traversal optimization for `git bisect` with an alternative approach suggestion. While acknowledging the core optimization's effectiveness (previously shown to reduce bisect initialization from 20 minutes to 30 seconds), Uwe proposes a different algorithm that would perform a scaled bisection on the topological commit list. The approach would select the middle commit from `git rev-list --topo-order` output, then adjust the search position based on how far the commit's weight deviates from the midpoint, effectively performing a weighted binary search through the commit graph.

This suggestion appears to be a conceptual alternative rather than a critique of the existing patch, noting it might not find the optimal bisection point but could offer different performance characteristics. The email maintains the thread's focus on bisection algorithm improvements while introducing a new direction for consideration, separate from the midpoint merge approach that has already demonstrated significant speedups and received positive feedback. The discussion continues to explore the tradeoffs between different bisection strategies, with this proposal adding another option to evaluate alongside the previously discussed first-parents-first and loosened threshold approaches.
