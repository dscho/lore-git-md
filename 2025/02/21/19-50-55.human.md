This is a follow-up in the ongoing Meson build system integration thread, specifically about coverage reporting functionality. Ramsay Jones confirms Patrick Steinhardt's earlier claim that Meson has native coverage support by testing it out with `gcovr`. The results show decent coverage metrics (85.2% lines, 93.3% functions, 76.0% branches) though Ramsay notes some projects would consider these numbers low. This exchange helps validate that one of the remaining static analysis/documentation targets (coverage reporting) is already properly handled by Meson's built-in functionality, leaving only a few other targets (hdr-check, check-pot, check-builtins, check-docs) to be addressed in future work. The tone is positive, with Ramsay providing practical confirmation of the coverage implementation.
