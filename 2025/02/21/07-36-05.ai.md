This is a technical discussion about progress reporting in the bundle-URI HTTPS download feature, specifically focusing on error handling and inter-process communication design.

The email continues the thread about how to properly separate progress output from error messages when downloading bundle-URIs via HTTPS. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Toon Claes' exploration of different approaches, agreeing that overriding die() functions or filtering stderr would be problematic. The discussion centers on a proposed solution where the child process (git-remote-https) would send progress updates back to the parent through a structured protocol rather than mixing them with stderr output.

Key technical points discussed:
- Two potential formats for progress reporting are proposed: one with raw numbers (`progress <size> <total> <throughput>`) and another with pre-rendered text messages (`log <message>`)
- The tradeoffs between simplicity (text messages) versus flexibility (machine-readable numbers) are considered
- The possibility of parallel bundle downloads is raised as a potential future consideration that might favor machine-readable progress data
- The discussion builds on previous work to integrate curl's progress callbacks with Git's progress meter system while avoiding stderr contamination

This email represents ongoing refinement of the v2 patch series' approach to clean progress reporting, with particular focus now on the protocol design between parent and child processes. The discussion shows careful consideration of both immediate needs and potential future extensions.
