Review comment on hunk splitting behavior in `git add -p`.

Justin Tobler provides feedback on Phillip Wood's patch that changes how `git add -p` handles hunk selection state during splitting. The patch makes split hunks default to "undecided" rather than inheriting their parent's selection state, which Phillip argued better matches user expectations when they choose to split a hunk.

He raises a minor consideration about potential tedium when splitting very large hunks into many small ones, but agrees this isn't a major drawback compared to the improved intuitiveness.
