This is the final acceptance of the promisor-remote/LOP patch series (v5) by Junio Hamano, following Patrick Steinhardt's review. The series introduces protocol v2 capability for promisor remote advertisement and has now been deemed ready for inclusion after addressing all major concerns.

Junio agrees with Steinhardt's assessment that several important follow-up features remain - including configuration persistence, dynamic updates, and authentication - but explicitly approves deferring these for future work. He notes these deferred items have significant security implications that would require careful design, making it wise to land the core functionality first.

The exchange confirms the series has achieved its immediate goals of implementing server-side advertisement (via `promisor.advertise` config) and client-side acceptance controls (via `promisor.acceptFromServer` with thorough test coverage in t5710-promisor-remote-capability.sh. The thread concludes with mutual appreciation for the collaborative effort.
