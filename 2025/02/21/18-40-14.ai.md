Junio Hamano's response to Patrick Steinhardt's final review of the promisor-remote/LOP series (v5) marks the completion of this effort, with the patches now accepted for queuing. The email focuses on three future enhancement areas that Steinhardt identified but explicitly excluded from this series due to their complexity and security implications.

The first proposed enhancement would persist advertised promisors client-side to avoid re-negotiation, while the second ("promisor remote agility") would handle dynamic updates to promisor sets. The third and most sensitive area involves authentication mechanisms for promisor remotes. Junio strongly agrees with keeping these out of the current series, noting their "grave security implications" and the value of establishing the fundamentals first.

This exchange confirms the series has reached its immediate goals for protocol v2 promisor remote advertisement capability, with all major technical and security concerns addressed in the implementation. The thread now concludes with clear separation between the production-ready foundation and potential future work that would build upon it, particularly around configuration persistence, dynamic updates, and authentication - all recognized as requiring careful security consideration in subsequent development.
