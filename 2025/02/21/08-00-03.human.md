This is a performance optimization discussion regarding ambiguity checks in object ID lookups, part of a larger refs subsystem optimization series.

Jeff King (peff) responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that introduces a new flag to skip ambiguity checks in the `get_oid()` family of functions. While acknowledging the potential benefits of the reply questions whether the new flag-based approach is necessary given the existing global `warn_on_object_refname_ambiguity` flag. Peff suggests that simply toggling this global flag in `update-ref` (patch 3 of the series) would achieve the same performance gain with much simpler changes, making patches 1 and 2 unnecessary.

The email comes late in the review cycle (after the series has been merged) and peff notes they're catching up after being away. The suggestion represents a potential simplification of the already-merged changes, though it's unclear if this will be acted upon given the series' advanced state. The discussion touches on a key tradeoff in the series - whether to use a new per-call flag mechanism or modify the existing global flag behavior.
