This is a performance optimization discussion regarding ambiguity checks in object ID lookups, part of the larger refs subsystem optimization series.

Jeff King responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch introducing a new `GET_OID_SKIP_AMBIGUITY_CHECK` flag for the `get_oid()` family of functions. While acknowledging the potential benefits of the approach, Jeff suggests an alternative solution that would simplify the patch series. He observes that since the global `warn_on_object_refname_ambiguity` flag will remain in place anyway, it might be more straightforward to have `update-ref` simply unset this global flag rather than introducing new per-call flag plumbing. This would eliminate the need for the first two patches in the series and reduce patch 3 to a one-line change.

The email notes this is a focused response to patches 1-3 of the 16-part series, with Jeff acknowledging he hasn't reviewed the full series due to personal commitments. The discussion centers on implementation strategy rather than the underlying performance goals, suggesting the ambiguity check optimization might be achievable with simpler changes to the existing mechanism.
