This is a technical discussion about progress reporting in HTTP bundle downloads, part of an ongoing series to improve bundle-URI functionality. Jeff King responds to Toon Claes's patch that silences stderr when progress is enabled by redirecting it to /dev/null, calling it a workable but "hacky" solution.

The key points are:
1. The current approach suppresses all stderr output when progress is shown, which may not be desirable since errors and progress are logically separate concerns
2. Jeff suggests progress reporting should instead use structured communication over stdout, which would better support future parallel downloads
3. This continues the thread's exploration of how to cleanly separate progress updates from error messages while maintaining good UX

The discussion shows the series is nearing completion but still working through design tradeoffs, with parallel downloads emerging as an important future consideration that may influence the current implementation choices. Jeff's comment indicates the stderr silencing approach may be a temporary solution while the team converges on a more robust protocol design.
