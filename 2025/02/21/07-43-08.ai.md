Progress meter infrastructure patch in the bundle-URI HTTPS download series.

This is a response to the first patch in Toon Claes' v2 series implementing progress reporting for bundle-URI. Jeff King (Peff) is reviewing the proposed `progress_set_total()` function that allows dynamically updating the total size of a transfer after it begins, which is needed for HTTP downloads where the Content-Length may not be known immediately.

The core observation is that the simple implementation (just updating the total field) is probably sufficient, though Peff considers whether triggering an immediate display update would be beneficial. He notes this would require choosing between `display_progress()` and `display_throughput()`, and concludes the brief staleness before the next progress update is likely acceptable in practice, especially for byte-by-byte transfers.

This continues the thread's technical discussion about proper progress meter integration with curl's callback system, maintaining the separation between progress updates and error handling that was established in earlier messages. The exchange shows careful consideration of edge cases in the progress reporting UX while keeping the implementation simple.
