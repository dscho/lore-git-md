This is a bugfix series addressing edge cases in `git add -p`'s hunk splitting behavior, specifically focusing on how already-selected or edited hunks are handled during splitting.

The series comes from Phillip Wood and consists of two patches that modify the interactive patch selection interface. The first patch ensures that when a hunk is split, the resulting sub-hunks are marked as "undecided" rather than inheriting the parent hunk's selection state. The second patch fixes an issue where the splitability status of hunks wasn't properly updated after manual editing, which could lead to confusing UI behavior.

The changes are focused in `add-patch.c` with corresponding test additions in `t/t3701-add-interactive.sh`. The test coverage appears thorough, adding 31 lines of new tests to verify the fixes.
