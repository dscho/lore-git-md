This is a follow-up discussion in the `the_repository` removal effort thread regarding David Aguilar's difftool refactoring series. Elijah Newren responds to Junio Hamano's question about whether the global variable encapsulation in patch 1 is truly necessary preparation for `the_repository` removal.

Elijah clarifies that while two of the variables (`trust_exit_code` and `symlinks`) could theoretically be handled separately, the `has_symlinks` variable is actually tied to `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` through its declaration in environment.h. This means removing `the_repository` usage would require addressing `has_symlinks` anyway, making it logical to handle all three globals together in the same patch.

The response confirms that Junio's observation about orthogonality was partially correct for two variables, but identifies the specific technical dependency that justifies the combined approach for `has_symlinks`. This moves the discussion forward by providing concrete implementation details that weren't evident from just reading the commit message. The exchange shows careful attention to the technical nuances of the refactoring while maintaining the series' overall goal of eliminating global state.
