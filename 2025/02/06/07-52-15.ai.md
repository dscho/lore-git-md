This is part 13/18 of Patrick Steinhardt's v4 series to fully decouple the reftable library from Git core infrastructure. The patch continues the pattern of replacing Git-specific utilities with reftable-native implementations, this time targeting the `SWAP()` macro.

The change introduces a new `REFTABLE_SWAP` macro that replicates the functionality of Git's `SWAP()` while being completely independent. The implementation:
- Uses direct memory operations with `memcpy` rather than relying on Git's utility
- Includes a size-checking mechanism similar to Git's build assert
- Is applied consistently across merged.c, pq.c, and record.c where swapping was previously done

The technical approach follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series - open-coding Git utilities while maintaining identical behavior. The change affects several core reftable operations including priority queue management and record handling, but the impact is contained to internal implementation details rather than changing external behavior.

This continues the series' systematic elimination of git-compat-util.h dependencies, with `SWAP()` being one of the remaining utility macros to replace. The patch maintains the series' high quality standards with thorough mechanical conversions and no behavioral changes.
