This is part 11 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove `the_repository` global by refactoring path-handling functions. The patch focuses on the rerere subsystem, specifically changing how paths for conflict resolution files are handled.

The patch modifies `rerere_path()` to accept a caller-provided `strbuf` buffer rather than returning static paths from `git_path()`. This follows the pattern established in earlier patches of moving from implicit static buffers to explicit memory management. The change affects all callers in `rerere.c` and `builtin/rerere.c`, requiring them to initialize and clean up their own buffers.

The conversion is mechanical but thorough - every call to `rerere_path()` is updated to pass a local `strbuf`, and corresponding cleanup is added. The patch maintains all existing functionality while preparing for future removal of `git_path()`. This continues the series' systematic approach to eliminating global state dependencies in path handling.
