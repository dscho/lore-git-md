Maintainer feedback on OS version capability implementation.

Junio Hamano responds to Usman Akinyemi's question about removing the `transfer.advertiseOSInfo` configuration option, confirming his preference to rely solely on the existing `GIT_USER_AGENT` mechanism for privacy control rather than adding a new config knob. While noting this isn't yet a formal conclusion (as only two participants have weighed in), Hamano clearly states his position that OS-specific configuration would be at the wrong granularity level compared to the more comprehensive `GIT_USER_AGENT` approach.

This email represents the final refinement in the OS capability series, where the maintainer is validating the architectural decision to avoid protocol fragmentation by extending the agent string rather than introducing separate capabilities or configuration. The exchange shows Hamano's preference for simpler, more unified mechanisms that build on existing controls rather than adding point solutions.
