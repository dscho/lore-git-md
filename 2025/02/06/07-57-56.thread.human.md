The feedback comes after merge, suggesting it as potential follow-up work rather than a blocking issue.

The series represents a major step in the multi-year effort to remove `the_repository` global variable, with only three subsystems (copy.c, pack-write.c, tempfile.c) remaining temporarily exempted.
