Follow-up review in the `the_repository` removal series for `difftool`. Elijah Newren approves David Aguilar's second patch, which removes all direct `the_repository` accesses from `builtin/difftool.c` by propagating a `struct repository*` parameter through the call stack. The changes are mechanical but thorough, touching all major functions (`parse_index_info`, `use_wt_file`, `changed_files`, `get_symlink`, `run_dir_diff`) while maintaining behavior. Newren notes the patch is easier to review thanks to the prior separation of options into a dedicated struct (patch 1/3). The review confirms the changes look correct and maintains the expected functionality, bringing us one step closer to eliminating this global state from the difftool subsystem. No further changes appear necessary for this patch.
