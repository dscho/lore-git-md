This is the 17th patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 18-part series to fully decouple the reftable library from Git's core infrastructure. The series has been through rigorous review and is now in its final stages.

The patch completes the architectural separation by removing the last direct dependency on `git-compat-util.h` in the reftable library, replacing it with the newly created `compat/posix.h` header. This change means the reftable library now only depends on system-level POSIX functionality (provided via `compat/posix.h`) and its own `system.h`/`system.c` files, which projects using the library will need to implement.

The change is straightforward - it swaps out one header include for another in `system.h` and adds a transitional include of `git-compat-util.h` in `system.c` (likely for build compatibility during the transition). This follows through on the previous patch's work to isolate POSIX functionality, and represents the final step in making reftable truly standalone.

With this change, other projects like libgit2 could theoretically use the reftable library without pulling in Git's entire codebase. The series has addressed all major technical concerns through multiple iterations, and this patch appears to be a clean, well-justified conclusion to the decoupling effort.
