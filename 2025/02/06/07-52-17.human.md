This is part 15/18 of Patrick Steinhardt's v4 series to fully separate the reftable library from Git core infrastructure, focusing on Windows platform compatibility. The patch reorganizes MinGW and MSVC compatibility headers by splitting POSIX-related functionality into separate files, creating a clearer architectural boundary.

The change moves Windows-specific POSIX implementations from `mingw.h` to `mingw/posix.h` while keeping Git-specific compatibility utilities in a new `mingw/compat-util.h`. The same split is applied to MSVC headers. This prepares for a future split of `git-compat-util.h` into POSIX-compatibility and Git-specific utility sections.

The patch affects build configurations across Makefile, CMake and meson, updating paths to the reorganized headers. It's a mechanical change that follows the series' pattern of cleanly separating concerns, with no functional impact beyond the header reorganization. The change appears well-contained and low-risk, continuing the series' careful progression toward complete reftable independence.
