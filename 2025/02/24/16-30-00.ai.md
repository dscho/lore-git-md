Performance discussion about rename detection optimization opportunities in path-filtered diffs. Elijah Newren responds to Devste Devste's report of slow `--find-renames` performance when filtering paths in merge commits. He requests clarification on several key points about the test case: what exactly "modified files" means (relative to which parent/base), whether changes include additions/deletions beyond modifications, and the exact setup that causes the slowdown. Newren notes that rename detection already has optimizations to skip processing when there are no additions/deletions (checked in diffcore-rename.c), suggesting the issue may relate to interaction between path filtering and rename detection rather than the core algorithm. He hints at potential optimizations similar to those needed for `--follow-renames` but needs more precise reproduction details before proposing solutions. The thread continues investigating why path-filtered diffs trigger expensive rename detection even when no renames could possibly be found in the filtered set.
