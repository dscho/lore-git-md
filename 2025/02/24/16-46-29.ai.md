The response indicates the maintainer is leaning toward resolution rather than maintaining the current undocumented state, though the specific path forward (documentation vs removal) remains open.

This email moves the thread toward decision-making by clarifying the scope of the proposed changes while showing maintainer alignment with addressing the technical debt around netrc support.
