Maintainer response on netrc authentication discussion.

Junio Hamano (the Git maintainer) responds to Brian Carlson's proposal about the future of netrc authentication in Git. Carlson had suggested either properly documenting and testing netrc support as a first-class feature or removing it in favor of the `git-credential-netrc` helper from contrib.

Hamano seeks clarification on whether "this" refers to netrc usage generally or specifically to the HTTP walker (an older component). He expresses surprise that anyone would still be using the HTTP walker regardless of credential storage. 

The maintainer indicates he's fine with Carlson's proposed approach of potentially removing built-in netrc support in favor of the credential helper alternative. This suggests the discussion may be moving toward deprecation rather than investing in maintaining netrc as a core feature. The response keeps the door open for either path while showing preference for simplifying the codebase by removing undocumented functionality.
