This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Git's clean/smudge filters and history rewriting, focusing on the philosophical and practical challenges of attribute handling across historical commits.

Second, a technical clarification request about environment variable propagation, specifically whether the `GIT_ATTR_SOURCE_ENVIRONMENT` variable is passed to commands invoked via rebase's `-x` switch.

This continues the thread's exploration of the tension between Git's historical commit preservation principles and practical needs for consistent filtering behavior.
