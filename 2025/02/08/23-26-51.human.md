The email also questions the environment variable propagation behavior for attribute handling during rebase operations, particularly whether the `--attr-source` setting gets passed to commands invoked via the `-x` switch. This continues the thread's exploration of edge cases in Git's filter application model, with Josef maintaining his position that filters should behave consistently across all commits rather than being commit-specific.

The exchange highlights a philosophical divide between Git's current architecture (where filter behavior can vary by commit) and some users' expectations of uniform filtering.
