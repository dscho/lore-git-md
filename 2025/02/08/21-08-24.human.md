This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Git's renormalization behavior with clean/smudge filters. Josef Wolf questions Phillip Wood's suggested use of `--attr-source=$(git rev-parse HEAD)` during rebase operations, expressing surprise that this isn't the default behavior.

The key point of contention is around when filter settings should apply during history rewriting operations. Josef argues that filters should consistently apply to all commits across all branches by default, rather than potentially changing during a rebase. He challenges the group to provide a use case where varying filter settings during an operation would be desirable.

This email highlights a philosophical difference in expectations about filter behavior - whether filters should be strictly consistent (Josef's view) or contextually variable (implied by Phillip's solution). The thread has already established technical solutions for handling renormalization, but this exchange surfaces deeper questions about the intended semantics of Git's filtering system.
