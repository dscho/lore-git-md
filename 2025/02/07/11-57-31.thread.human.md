This thread discusses adding a `--no-reflog` option to `git refs migrate` to optimize reference backend migrations by allowing administrators to skip reflog migration. Karthik Nayak originally proposed this as a performance optimization, particularly useful for server-side repositories where reflogs are often unnecessary. Karthik has accepted this sequencing and committed to working on the prerequisite reflog deletion functionality after completing his work on partial transactions. While the patch is technically ready and has received positive feedback, its merge will be deferred until after the foundational reflog management capabilities are in place.
