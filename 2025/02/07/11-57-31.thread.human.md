This thread began with a performance optimization for `git refs migrate`, introducing a `--skip-reflog` flag to avoid the cost of migrating unwanted reflogs during reference backend changes. The implementation was technically sound, with Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler providing thorough reviews, and Karthik Nayak addressing all feedback. Junio C Hamano had previously approved the technical details after several rounds of refinement.

However, Junio has now raised a fundamental design concern, questioning whether this optimization belongs in the migration command at all. He suggests that reflog management should be handled by a dedicated tool like `git reflog drop`, which could serve the broader need for reflog cleanup at any time, not just during migrations. This shifts the discussion from implementation details to architectural appropriateness, potentially requiring a redesign. The patch series, while technically complete, may need to be reworked or supplemented with a more general reflog management solution.
