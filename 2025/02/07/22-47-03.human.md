The author suggests workarounds like setting `TZ=UTC` in shell initialization files or editor environments, and recommends enforcing timezone policies at the server/CI level rather than in client Git.

The reply provides practical advice for users wanting UTC timestamps while explaining why baking timezone handling into Git would be problematic.
