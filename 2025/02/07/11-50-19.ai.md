This is a follow-up discussion in the reftable decoupling series, specifically addressing the UNUSED macro replacement in patch 14/18. The thread context shows this is part of the final fine-tuning of implementation details after the major architectural work is complete.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Toon Claes' observation about the newly introduced REFTABLE_UNUSED macro. Steinhardt acknowledges this is correct - he had focused on replacing UNUSED (which has compiler-specific implementations) but agrees MAYBE_UNUSED would work equally well while being more standardized.

The exchange represents the final polishing of the reftable separation work, with Steinhardt indicating he'll revise the patch to use MAYBE_UNUSED instead of introducing a new macro. This resolves one of the two remaining open questions from the thread summary (the attribute macro design choice), leaving only the deferred header inclusion style discussion as the last outstanding item before the series can be finalized.
