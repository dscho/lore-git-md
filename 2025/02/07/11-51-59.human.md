This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series (18 patches) to finalize the architectural separation of the reftable library from Git core infrastructure. The series has already addressed all major technical feedback from previous iterations.

This continues the series' goal of eliminating Git-specific dependencies from the reftable code. The change also adds a safety check to avoid potential NULL pointer issues when copying zero-sized arrays, returning `REFTABLE_OUT_OF_MEMORY_ERROR` in such cases.
