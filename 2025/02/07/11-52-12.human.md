This is the penultimate patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 18-part series to fully decouple the reftable library from Git's core infrastructure. The series has systematically replaced Git-specific dependencies with self-contained implementations, and this patch marks a key milestone by removing the last direct inclusion of "git-compat-util.h" from the reftable library.

The change switches to including "compat/posix.h" instead, which provides the POSIX-like environment the library needs without pulling in Git-specific helpers. The only remaining Git-specific pieces are now "system.h" and "system.c", which external projects will need to provide their own implementations of. This finalizes the architectural separation that makes reftable usable as a standalone library by projects like libgit2.

The patch is mechanically straightforward - it updates two includes in system.c and system.h while maintaining all functionality. This comes after extensive preparatory work in earlier patches to replace all Git-specific utility functions with reftable-specific versions. The change appears well-motivated and cleanly implemented as part of the larger series.

With this patch, the reftable library achieves its goal of being properly decoupled while still working within Git itself. The only remaining patch in the series appears to be cleanup or final tweaks, making this effectively the completion of the major technical work.
