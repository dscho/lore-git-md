This is the final approval for the `the_repository` removal effort in `difftool`. Maintainer Junio Hamano gives the green light to merge the three-part series after receiving positive reviews from Elijah Newren and Patrick Steinhardt. The patches have gone through multiple iterations, with Steinhardt noting this version is "much easier to read" than previous attempts. 

The series successfully eliminated all global state from `builtin/difftool.c` through a methodical approach: first encapsulating configuration in a struct, then removing direct `the_repository` accesses, and finally cleaning up transitional scaffolding. The work maintains all existing behavior while properly parameterizing repository access.

With all technical questions resolved and no outstanding issues, Hamano marks the topic for inclusion in the 'next' branch. This concludes a well-executed collaborative effort between contributor David Aguilar and the Git maintainers to modernize this component of the codebase.
