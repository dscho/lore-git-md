This is a follow-up discussion in the completed `the_repository` removal series for path handling functions. Patrick Steinhardt responds to feedback from shejialuo about the series' organization.

Shejialuo had expressed difficulty reviewing patches 13-16, finding them less connected to the earlier patches, and suggested combining the first two phases of the work (interface unification and removal of implicit dependencies). Patrick politely disagrees, explaining that keeping these as separate phases was intentional - the first establishes new interfaces while the second removes old ones, and combining them would create confusion by mixing distinct tasks.

The exchange highlights how even well-executed large refactorings can present review challenges due to their scale. Patrick maintains his original approach was the right one for clarity, while acknowledging the reviewer's perspective. This is typical post-merge discussion about the structure of major refactoring work, where different developers may have preferences about how to organize such changes.
