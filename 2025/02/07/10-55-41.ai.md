This is a confirmation and prioritization message in the ongoing GSoC 2025 organization thread, specifically addressing project selection and mentor assignments.

Patrick Steinhardt confirms the proposed mentor assignments for all four projects are acceptable, but raises an important prioritization concern regarding his mentoring capacity. He strongly prefers mentoring the "Consolidate ref-related functionality into git-refs" project over the "Implement support for reftables in 'dumb' HTTP transport" project, as he can only mentor one. He suggests either dropping the HTTP transport project or clearly communicating this preference to avoid unfairness to students who might apply for the less-favored project.

The email also notes that Kaartic Sivaraam has already submitted Git's GSoC application, with results expected by February 26. This represents the final piece of the mentor assignment puzzle that was previously marked as pending in the thread summary. The only remaining open items are the documentation infrastructure decision and the actual selection of which 3 projects (out of the 4 proposed) will proceed, given the constraint that Patrick can only mentor one.
