Follow-up discussion on the `difftool` refactoring series.

Patrick Steinhardt chimes in on the thread discussing David Aguilar's patch series that refactors `difftool` to remove global variables as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. He's responding to Elijah Newren's explanation about why the `has_symlinks` global variable needed to be moved into the new `difftool_options` struct (because its declaration in `environment.h` is guarded by `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE`).

Steinhardt notes that while this change could theoretically break something if the variable was being modified elsewhere, he confirms that `has_symlinks` is only read and never modified in the codebase, so the refactoring should be safe. This provides additional validation of the design decision to encapsulate these variables, complementing the earlier approvals from Elijah Newren and Junio Hamano. The discussion appears to be wrapping up with no new concerns raised about the approach.
