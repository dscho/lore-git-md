The email raises two possibilities: either Patrick intentionally chose a more visible annotation style (separate line) for future maintenance, or there may be an opportunity to use a more standard attribute. This is a minor implementation detail in the broader reftable separation work, which as noted in the thread context is otherwise technically complete with only header inclusion style remaining as an open question.

The query doesn't challenge the overall approach but seeks clarification on a specific design choice in the macro implementation. Given the thoroughness of the series so far, this seems like a thoughtful suggestion rather than a criticism, and the answer will likely help finalize this small but precise aspect of the reftable independence effort.
