This is a discussion about potential future directions for signed commit handling in Git's fast-export/import functionality, building on the recently approved v5 patch series that added signed commit support.

Patrick Steinhardt chimes in to confirm that the use case Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren are discussing - having importers add their own attestation signatures - aligns with real-world needs. He shares that they encountered this exact scenario where a customer couldn't use git-filter-repo due to commit signature issues, which motivated their involvement in reviving and completing Luke Shumaker's original work on signed commit support.

The email serves as validation that the proposed future direction (importer attestation signatures) addresses actual pain points, specifically mentioning git-filter-repo as a key beneficiary. This comes after the core functionality has been thoroughly reviewed and approved, with all major technical and documentation concerns addressed in the v5 series.

Key technical context from the thread:
- The approved v5 series provides comprehensive signed commit handling in fast-export/import
- Current implementation focuses on preserving/removing existing signatures
- Future work could add new attestation signatures during import
- git-filter-repo has seen multiple requests for this functionality
- The discussion confirms the practical need for importer attestation signatures
- The approved v5 series provides the foundation for such future extensions
- git-filter-repo would be a primary beneficiary of this enhancement
- No technical details yet proposed for the attestation feature
