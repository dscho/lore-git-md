Performance optimization for bundle-uri reference negotiation during fetches, originally proposed by Scott Chacon. Current implementation copies only `refs/heads/` references from bundles to `refs/bundles/`, causing suboptimal fetch negotiation. Chacon's one-line change widens refspec to `refs/`, demonstrated to reduce downloaded objects from 32% to 1% in GitLab OSS repository with improved clone times. Benchmark data shows significant performance improvement.
