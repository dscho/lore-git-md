This is a refactoring patch in Jeff King's zlib inflation series, specifically addressing error handling in loose object unpacking. The patch simplifies the control flow in `unpack_loose_rest()` by converting a success-then-error-check pattern into a single if/else cascade that checks errors first before falling through to the success case.

The key change replaces a structure that first checked for success (Z_STREAM_END with no remaining bytes) and then redundantly re-checked those same conditions for error reporting, with a more straightforward sequence that checks error conditions first. This eliminates the possibility of the two checks getting out of sync (which was the source of a previous bug) and makes the code's intent clearer.

The patch touches only `object-file.c`, modifying the error handling logic while maintaining all existing functionality. It's a small but meaningful improvement to the code's robustness, continuing the series' theme of hardening zlib-related operations against edge cases. The change is purely internal - no user-visible behavior or interfaces are affected.
