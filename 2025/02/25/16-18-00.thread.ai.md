Documentation patch clarifying submodule merge behavior in Git's merge strategies documentation, now in its third revision (v3) as part of a GSoC project. The patch adds two paragraphs to merge-strategies.adoc explaining conflict resolution for submodule merges: when one submodule commit is a descendant of another (Git attempts fast-forward), and when merge commits exist containing both conflicting submodule commits (Git suggests resolution but leaves unmerged). Maintains consistency with existing behavior implemented in merge-ort.c and merge-recursive.c (commit 68d03e4a6e44), noting both 'ort' and 'recursive' strategies handle submodules similarly despite implementation differences.
