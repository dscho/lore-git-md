This is a final review response for a bugfix series enhancing `git fsck`'s packed-refs validation.

Patrick Steinhardt provides a mostly positive review of shejialuo's v5 patch series, noting only "a couple of small nits" while indicating the series appears nearly ready for merging. Patrick's review suggests the technical implementation is sound, building on earlier feedback from multiple reviewers including himself, Junio Hamano, and Karthik Nayak.
