This is a discussion about edge cases in zlib inflation handling, specifically around the `OBJECT_INFO_ALLOW_UNKNOWN_TYPE` flag in Jeff King's ongoing patch series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff's patch that adds a BUG() when trying to inflate content with unknown object types.

- Both authors question the utility of `OBJECT_INFO_ALLOW_UNKNOWN_TYPE`, noting it was added in 2015 but never fully utilized
- Patrick confirms the `hdrbuf` is still needed for reading type/length, just not content
- The discussion reveals potential confusion around 32-byte vs 32-bit terminology in the code
