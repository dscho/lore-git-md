This is a follow-up discussion in the signed commits for fast-export/import thread, focusing on a potential future enhancement rather than the current patch series. The email exchange between Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren touches on an orthogonal feature idea that has come up during the final review of the signed commits implementation.

Junio notes that while the current series (now approved) handles importing existing signatures, there might be value in a separate feature allowing importers to add their own attestation signatures to commits and tags during import, regardless of whether they were originally signed. This would serve as a way to certify the import process itself.

Elijah Newren confirms this is a real need, having seen similar requests for git-filter-repo, though both agree this is outside the scope of the current work. The discussion serves as acknoledgement of a potential future direction while making clear the current series remains focused and complete as-is.

Key points for future reference:
- The suggestion involves new importer-side signing capability, distinct from the export/import signature handling in the current series
- git-filter-repo has seen demand for similar functionality
- Both correspondents explicitly state this is orthogonal to the approved v5 series
- No technical details or implementation approach are proposed in this exchange
- The thread context shows the main series is considered production-ready with all major concerns addressed
