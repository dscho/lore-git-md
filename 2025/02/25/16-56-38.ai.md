This is a clarification email in the signed commits fast-export/import thread, where Junio Hamano responds to Elijah Newren's confusion about the scope of the patch series. 

Junio explains that while the current v5 series doesn't implement the attestation signatures feature that was discussed earlier (which would allow re-signing commits during import), it serves as foundational work that enables future implementation of that feature. He interprets Christian Couder's statement about git-filter-repo use cases as indicating this series is just the first step - it handles existing signatures (via abort/strip/verbatim modes) while leaving the attestation capability for follow-up work.

The email reinforces the distinction between the current production-ready functionality (signature handling) and the future attestation feature, noting both are needed for complete git-filter-repo support. This aligns with the thread's established understanding that the v5 series is complete as-is while laying groundwork for future development.
