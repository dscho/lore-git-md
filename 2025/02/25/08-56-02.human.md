Performance optimization completing a refs subsystem series. This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 16-part series improving refname availability checks across Git's ref backends. The patch enables iterator reuse during refname checks, which previously required reallocating iterators for each reference being verified.

The change shows significant gains for the reftable backend (1.25x faster when creating many refs) but minimal impact on the files backend, which remains bottlenecked by filesystem operations. The implementation builds on the iterator seek capability introduced earlier in the series, modifying `refs_verify_refnames_available()` to reseek existing iterators rather than recreating them.

This completes the series' cross-backend iterator optimizations, with all three backends (files, reftable, packed-refs) now supporting efficient iterator reuse. The patch is small (12 lines changed) but represents the culmination of substantial preparatory work across the series. Performance measurements confirm the expected benefits, particularly for reftable where the optimization avoids repeated iterator setup costs.
