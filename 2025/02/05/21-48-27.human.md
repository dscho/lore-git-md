Junio Hamano provides final review feedback on the OS version capability series, now in its fourth iteration. While agreeing with the feature's benefits (simplicity, backward compatibility, debugging value), he questions the granularity of the privacy controls. He suggests that privacy-conscious users would likely want to suppress the entire agent string, not just the OS portion, and notes they can already do so via `GIT_USER_AGENT`. 

The maintainer proposes simplifying the interface by removing the `transfer.advertiseOSInfo` config knob entirely, since those needing privacy would suppress the whole string anyway. He also suggests renaming `os_info_sanitized()` to just `os_info()` since the function always returns sanitized output. This appears to be the last round of polish before merging, addressing high-level design rather than implementation details. The feedback maintains the series' trajectory while trimming what the maintainer sees as unnecessary configurability.
