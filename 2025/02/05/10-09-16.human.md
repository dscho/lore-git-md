Bugfix addressing NUL characters in packed-refs refnames. This is part 5/8 of a series improving packed-refs validation, with Patrick Steinhardt providing review feedback.

The patch fixes a subtle edge case where embedded NUL characters in refnames could bypass validation. Currently, `next_record()` calculates refname length by pointer subtraction but doesn't detect NULs within the name. The solution adds a check that scans for interior NULs before validating the refname format.

Patrick suggested simplifying the NUL check to a single `memchr()` call rather than using a helper function, but Shejialuo argues the helper will be reused in future validation work. The discussion also notes this can't be handled in `check_refname_format()` due to its C-string interface.

This is a well-motivated fix for a real (if rare) corruption case, with clear technical explanation in the commit message. The implementation looks solid, though there's minor debate about code organization, both approaches seem reasonable. The patch fits cleanly into the larger series' goal of thorough packed-refs validation.
