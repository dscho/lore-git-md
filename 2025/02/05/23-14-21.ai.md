Performance benchmarking infrastructure discussion continues with new participant perspective. Emily Shaffer (Google) joins the thread about Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for continuous Git performance monitoring, offering Google's perspective and use cases. She identifies two concrete contributions Google could make: (1) exploring dedicated GCE-hosted benchmarking to avoid CI noise, and (2) sharing performance data from Android's massive repositories (either closed-source with anonymized metrics or via Android Open Source Project). The email raises important practical questions about test repository characteristics that would be most useful to track (repo size metrics vs. specific content) and suggests long-term development of standardized test repositories representing different scaling challenges. Shaffer strongly endorses the mailing list alert concept, particularly for regression notifications rather than routine success reports. The message provides valuable real-world context about recent Google/Android performance pain points and organizational willingness to invest in solution development, while thoughtfully considering the project's need for transparency in benchmarking methodology.
