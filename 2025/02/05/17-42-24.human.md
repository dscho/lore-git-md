Junio C Hamano responds to D. He worries that if rebase settings can bypass this, it defeats the purpose for users who prefer rebasing workflows. The discussion highlights a tension between two valid perspectives: Knoble's view that branch-specific settings represent explicit user intent that should take precedence, versus Hamano's view that safety measures should be hard to override.

The technical implementation of the patch isn't in question here - the debate centers on whether the proposed behavior aligns with Git's design philosophy. Hamano's response suggests he's not yet convinced the current approach is correct, though he acknowledges the ambiguity in the documentation that Knoble pointed out earlier.
