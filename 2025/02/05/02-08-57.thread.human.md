This thread tracks the coordination between Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt on Git's development status following Junio's February 2025 "What's cooking" report. While there were earlier concerns about Windows-specific implementation details and architectural consistency regarding compat-util reduction, the series has received positive feedback from Johannes Schindelin for the Windows aspects and now from Toon Claes for the general approach. Other active work includes Meson refinements, repository initialization improvements, and Rust bindings development, but the reftable decoupling appears to be the most mature of the in-flight topics, nearing completion pending resolution of Toon's final feedback. The thread demonstrates the project's careful approach to architectural changes, with Windows compatibility and codebase consistency being key considerations in the review process.
