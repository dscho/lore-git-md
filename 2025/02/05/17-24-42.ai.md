This is the final patch in the 7-part series introducing the `--revision` clone option, now being reviewed by Junio Hamano. The patch implements the core functionality that allows cloning a repository at a specific revision without creating remote-tracking branches, with HEAD detached at the requested commit.

- `update_head()` now uses `lookup_commit_or_die()` for safer error handling when resolving the revision
- Junio notes one minor documentation nit about bare vs non-bare repository behavior that could be clarified
