This is the final patch in Meet Soni's refactoring series to centralize refspec-related functions, completing the relocation of all remaining refspec logic from remote.c to refspec.c. The patch moves `apply_refspecs()` and `apply_negative_refspecs()` - the last two refspec-handling functions still in remote.c - to their new home in refspec.c, where all other refspec operations now live.

The changes are purely mechanical: the functions are copied verbatim to refspec.c (with corresponding additions to refspec.h) and removed from remote.c/remote.h. As with previous patches in the series, this maintains exact behavior while improving code organization by grouping related functionality together. The commit message clearly explains the motivation for the move.

This patch represents the completion of a well-executed GSoC project that has gone through multiple rounds of technical review, with all feedback incorporated in previous iterations.
