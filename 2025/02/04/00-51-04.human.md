This is a UX-focused follow-up in the ongoing discussion about improving Git's branch status messages to better reflect their probabilistic nature in distributed workflows.

Bram van Oosterhout responds to Chris Torek's suggestion for rewording the "up to date" message, offering an alternative phrasing that more clearly communicates the temporal limitation of the information. Where Chris proposed "up to date as of the most recent information from fetch", Bram suggests "Your local branch is unchanged since your last fetch from..." as potentially clearer wording.

The exchange continues the thread's exploration of how to balance accuracy and usability in Git's status messages, with both proposals attempting to acknowledge that the status reflects only local knowledge while remaining concise. Bram's version emphasizes the local perspective more explicitly and hints at the possibility of remote changes without stating them as certain.

This represents progress in the UX track of the discussion, showing concrete alternatives being proposed for the problematic messaging. The suggestions remain focused on wording changes rather than technical solutions, suggesting the participants see this as a more immediately tractable approach than implementing new metadata tracking.
