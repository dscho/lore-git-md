The patch makes mechanical changes across three files (remote.c, remote.h, and builtin/remote.c) to update all call sites and declarations. This follows the series' established pattern of improving naming consistency while preserving behavior, with the v4 iteration implementing the final agreed naming conventions after review feedback.
