This is a follow-up in the thread about extensible missing object reporting in `git rev-list`, specifically addressing quoting behavior. 

Justin Tobler responds to Phillip Wood's feedback on patch 2/4 of v3, which had proposed adding a `QUOTE_PATH_IGNORE_CONFIG` flag. Phillip had questioned why `rev-list` should behave differently from other plumbing commands regarding `core.quotePath` configuration. 

Justin acknowledges the point and agrees to drop the first two patches of the series in the next version, as the maintainers have clarified that the output should respect `core.quotePath` like other plumbing commands. This aligns with Junio Hamano's earlier guidance that only minimal quoting (for control characters and spaces) is required, without special config handling.

The exchange shows the patch series converging toward the maintainer-approved design direction, with Justin now focusing on revising the implementation accordingly. The core functionality of tracking missing objects with path/type information remains, but the quoting approach will be simplified to match Git's standard plumbing behavior.
