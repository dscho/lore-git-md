This is the final patch in a 7-part series introducing a new `--revision` option to `git clone`. The feature allows cloning a specific ref or commit hash without creating remote-tracking branches, detaching HEAD at the requested revision. This is particularly useful for CI systems that need minimal clones of specific revisions.

The patch implements the core functionality after extensive preparatory refactoring in previous patches. Key aspects include:
- Accepts fully qualified refs (like `refs/pull/123`) or commit hashes
- Creates no tracking branches or fetch refspecs
- Works with `--depth` for minimal clones
- Includes comprehensive tests covering various ref types and edge cases
- Properly handles incompatibilities with `--branch` and `--mirror`
