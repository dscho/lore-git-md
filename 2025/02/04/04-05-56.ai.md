Refactoring patch in Meet Soni's GSoC series to centralize refspec functions. This is part 3/5 of the v4 series that has already gone through extensive review and is now implementing the final agreed-upon naming conventions.

The patch renames three refspec-related functions to better reflect their purposes and maintain consistency with the new subsystem-prefixed naming scheme:
1. `query_refspecs()` becomes `refspec_find_match()` (now clearly indicates it finds a single match)
2. `query_refspecs_multiple()` becomes `refspec_find_all_matches()` (better conveys it collects all matches)
3. `query_matches_negative_refspec()` becomes `refspec_find_negative_match()` (for consistency, though it was static)

The changes are mechanical renames affecting remote.c, remote.h, and builtin/push.c, with corresponding updates to error messages and BUG() calls. This follows the series' pattern of moving refspec logic to refspec.c while preserving behavior - part 2/5 already moved the core matching functions, and this completes the naming standardization.

The patch implements the final naming decisions agreed upon in previous review rounds with Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, maintaining the high quality seen throughout the series. No behavior changes are introduced - this is purely a naming/consistency improvement as part of the larger refactoring effort.
