This is the final patch (5/5) in Meet Soni's refactoring series to centralize refspec-related functions from remote.c to refspec.c, part of a GSoC project. The patch completes the relocation effort by moving the remaining two functions - `apply_refspecs()` and `apply_negative_refspecs()` - to refspec.c where they logically belong with other refspec operations.

The changes are purely mechanical moves with no behavior modifications, following the pattern established in previous patches of the series. The functions are moved verbatim from remote.c to refspec.c, with their declarations added to refspec.h and removed from remote.h. This completes the architectural goal of having all refspec logic reside in refspec.c while maintaining exact functional behavior.

Key technical details:
- `apply_refspecs()`: Finds matching refspec for a given name and returns destination
- `apply_negative_refspecs()`: Filters ref_map by removing entries matching negative refspecs
- Both functions now properly subsystem-prefixed in refspec.h
- Header includes adjusted to maintain all dependencies
- All call sites already updated in previous patches to use the refspec.h declarations
