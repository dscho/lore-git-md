This is a follow-up discussion in the thread about progress reporting during multi-pack-index verification's QSORT operation. Ayush Chandekar responds to Junio C Hamano's technical concerns about the fundamental approach in their RFC patch series.

The key development is that Ayush acknowledges Junio's point about the flawed assumption that qsort() comparisons would show increasing positions. They originally thought tracking the highest position seen could estimate progress, but now recognize this isn't reliable since qsort() implementations make no guarantees about comparison order. 

This leaves the progress reporting feature at an impasse - the current position-tracking mechanism can't work as intended, and no alternative approach is proposed yet. The discussion has shifted from implementation details to whether any meaningful progress reporting is possible during this QSORT operation given the constraints.

The exchange shows good technical engagement - Ayush demonstrates understanding of the core issue raised and doesn't try to defend the problematic approach. However, the original goal of providing progress feedback during MIDX verification now appears more challenging than initially thought, requiring either a fundamentally different technical solution or possibly abandoning the effort if no reliable progress indicator can be found.
