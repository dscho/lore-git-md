Performance optimization for merge-recursive's rename processing, with review feedback from Elijah Newren. This continues the discussion of Meet Soni's patch that replaces O(n²) string_list_insert() calls with O(n log n) string_list_append() plus explicit string_list_sort() in merge-recursive.c's process_renames() function.

Newren raises several technical points about the patch: (1) the removed FIXME comment about duplicate handling was actually important documentation, (2) confirms the sorting is necessary since the source lists are sorted by different keys, and (3) suggests clarifying the commit message to specify this only process_renames() is being optimized, not all string_list construction. He also notes merge-recursive is slated for eventual removal but acknowledges the optimization is still worthwhile in the interim.

The review confirms the technical correctness of the approach while suggesting minor documentation and commit message improvements. Newren indicates the patch is otherwise sound, marking it as "looks good to me" with those caveats. The discussion remains focused on this specific optimization, without expanding scope to other potential string_list optimizations in the file.
