Junio Hamano initiated a thread to standardize how boolean values are documented across Git's man pages, establishing git-config(1) as the canonical reference. The changes consolidate previously scattered definitions into one authoritative source, listing all accepted variants (like "true"/"false", "yes"/"no", etc.) and updating related documentation to point there instead of maintaining separate partial lists. 

Eric Sunshine reviewed the patch and suggested a minor grammatical improvement to the commit message's subject line, changing "spell" to "to spell" for better readability. Junio, as both the maintainer and patch author, has accepted this correction. The thread shows clear consensus on the standardization effort itself, with this being the only suggested change so far. The patch remains at v1 and appears ready to be merged once the typo fix is incorporated.
