This thread examined two performance optimizations for merge-recursive's string list processing. The first patch, optimizing `process_renames()` from O(n²) to O(n log n) via sorting, was approved by Elijah Newren and confirmed ready for integration after verification that diffcore_rename guarantees no duplicate paths. The second optimization attempt for `get_unmerged()` was conclusively rejected after Newren demonstrated test failures (t1004, t6430, t6409, t3424) proving it broke path consolidation requirements. 

Meet Soni, the patch author, acknowledged the technical limitations and testing methodology gaps, accepting that merge-ort's strategic direction makes optimizing legacy merge-recursive code non-essential. The thread serves as an educational case study - highlighting the importance of both theoretical analysis and empirical testing, the necessity of proper build-test cycles, and the standard test suite's effectiveness at catching regressions. With all technical questions resolved and both patches' fates decided (one approved, one abandoned), the discussion stands concluded with valuable lessons for future contributors about performance optimization validation.
