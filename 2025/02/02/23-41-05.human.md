This is a security-related discussion about the proposed warning for git-credential-store usage. 

Junio Hamano weighs in on Jeff King's critique of the RFC patch that proposed warning users about the insecure nature of the credential-store helper. The thread has evolved from the initial implementation details to broader questions about whether warnings are the right approach at all.

Junio agrees with Jeff's argument that since credential-store isn't enabled by default and already carries warnings in its documentation, users who choose it are making an informed decision. He notes this aligns with earlier concerns raised by Brian Carlson about unnecessary friction for users in constrained environments where credential-store may be the only viable option.

The exchange suggests growing skepticism about the original warning approach, with maintainers questioning whether the proposed solution would actually help users who have deliberately chosen this helper despite its known security tradeoffs. The discussion appears to be shifting focus toward either accepting credential-store as a legitimate (if discouraged) option or considering more radical solutions like deprecation.
