Maintainer input on mentoring infrastructure transition. Junio Hamano weighs in on the proposal to replace the problematic git-mentoring@googlegroups.com list, offering practical guidance for the transition.

He supports shutting down the current Google Groups list given its maintenance issues, and is open to using Discord if it's already proving effective for new contributors. However, he raises several key organizational questions that need addressing before finalizing any changes: 

First, he suggests compiling a comprehensive list of all available help channels (starting with Discord if that's the primary option) that community members actively monitor. Second, he questions where this resource should be documented - suggesting either the Contributing guide or the Maintainer's notes. Finally, he highlights the need for a maintenance plan to keep this information current.

The email doesn't propose specific alternatives to Discord but focuses on the logistical aspects of whatever solution is chosen. It moves the discussion forward by identifying concrete next steps for documentation and maintenance planning, while leaving the platform choice open based on what's already working for newcomers.
