This is a community announcement about the upcoming Git Rev News edition, a periodic digest of Git project activity. Christian Couder shares a draft of edition 120 and invites contributions from the community before its planned March 2nd publication. The draft is available on GitHub for direct editing via pull requests, and there's also an open issue for discussion. The email serves both to notify contributors mentioned in the edition (with apologies for any omissions) and to solicit broader participation — whether through proofreading, content suggestions, or volunteering for future interviews. The collaborative nature of the message reflects Git Rev News' role as a community-driven project newsletter.
