Taylor Blau responds to Jeff King's zlib inflation bugfix series, now at 10 patches. This is a brief review/acknowledgment rather than a technical discussion.

He notes that patches 4 and 5 were co-developed together, so he didn't have much to add on those. For the rest of the series, he confirms everything looks good after a re-read, though he's not particularly fond of the final patch (10/10) which restructures return logic in `unpack_loose_rest()`. He considers this change subjective - he'd be fine either including it or dropping it, though he slightly prefers the state after patch 9.

The email serves as a positive review of the series, with only minor reservations about one optional cleanup patch. Taylor indicates the series is in good shape to proceed, whether or not that final patch is included.
