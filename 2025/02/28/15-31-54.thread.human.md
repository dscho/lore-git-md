The test involves Git's partial clone functionality (`--filter=blob:none`) and the experimental `git backfill` command with specific focus on batch fetching behavior. While the test also checks that no objects remain missing after backfill, the immediate failure occurs in the trace2 event verification. Zullinger notes this appears to be s390x-specific, possibly related to endianness or platform-specific behavior in the batch fetching logic.
