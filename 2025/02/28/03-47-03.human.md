This is a documentation patch series addressing consistency in file extensions after the transition from .txt to .adoc format. Todd Zullinger noticed broken links in the howto-index.html output due to the recent format change and proposes three straightforward changes:

1. Updates howto-index.sh to use .adoc extensions consistently throughout the script rather than just where globbing occurs
2. Renames git-contacts.txt to .adoc in the contrib/contacts directory
3. Does the same for git-subtree.txt in contrib/subtree directory

The changes are mechanical renames with corresponding Makefile updates. Zullinger notes there's one remaining file in contrib/mw-to-git that could also be renamed (git-remote-mediawiki.txt) but holds off due to uncertainty about its maintenance status. The patch series appears clean and focused on maintaining consistency after the documentation format transition.
