Follow-up discussion on third-party project documentation proposal.

Junio Hamano responds to his own earlier proposal about documenting where to report issues for major Git ecosystem components (git-scm.com, Git for Windows, ProGit book) in MaintNotes. He notes the lack of feedback ("no news is a bad news") and plans to rescind the proposed update from the next edition of the maintainer's notes unless discussion emerges. The original patch had added a 40-line "Other places" section with GitHub issue tracker links and contribution guidelines for these three projects.

Key points in this follow-up:
- Reiterates the three open questions from the original proposal (maintenance cost, inclusion criteria, maintenance approach)
- Includes the full proposed diff showing the exact documentation changes that would be reverted
- Maintains the same technical details (GitHub URLs, MaintNotes location) as the original patch
- Sets a clear timeline for feedback (before next maintainer's notes edition in mid-March)

The thread remains focused on the core tension between improving contributor experience by directing them to appropriate venues versus the maintenance burden of keeping such a list current. The maintainer appears willing to drop the idea unless community interest is demonstrated, suggesting this may be a low-priority quality-of-life improvement rather than a critical need.
