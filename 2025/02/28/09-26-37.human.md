Performance optimization series for refs subsystem (v4). This 16-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt continues the work to optimize refname availability checks across Git's reference backends, building on previous versions that were reviewed by shejialuo and benchmarked by brian m. carlson.

The series addresses three key performance bottlenecks in ref operations:
1. Removes unnecessary ambiguity checks in `git-update-ref` that were triggering excessive reads (patches 1-4)
2. Introduces batched prefix checking using a `strset` to avoid redundant verifications of common ref prefixes (patches 5-9)
3. Implements iterator reuse by adding reseek capability to all ref iterators, allowing verification checks to reuse existing iterators rather than recreating them (patches 10-16)

Benchmarks show significant improvements, particularly for the reftable backend which sees up to a 7.5x speedup in the original test case (deleting and recreating refs) and nearly 5x faster performance in more realistic scenarios with preexisting refs. The files backend also benefits with more modest 1.2-1.3x improvements.

This v4 iteration includes minor refinements to commit messages and documentation, with no major technical changes from v3. The series appears well-tested and carefully implemented, maintaining cross-backend consistency while delivering measurable performance gains. The changes are focused on internal optimizations that shouldn't affect user-visible behavior beyond the speed improvements.
