Performance optimization in MIDX bitmap handling.

Patrick Steinhardt follows up on Taylor Blau's patch that introduced a new `ewah_or_iterator` for type bitmap iteration in pack-bitmap.c. The change replaces direct EWAH iterator usage with a more sophisticated OR-based iterator that can handle layered bitmaps from MIDX files.

Steinhardt raises a thoughtful question about whether to maintain the simpler non-layered iterator path for non-MIDX cases, but acknowledges the tradeoff: keeping both paths would add conditional complexity while the OR-based approach likely has negligible performance impact even for single-pack cases. He notes the implementation assumes proper initialization of `base_nr` and `*_all` fields in all cases.

This exchange shows the careful consideration being given to both correctness and maintainability in the MIDX bitmap optimizations. The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than fundamental design, suggesting the core approach is sound. The patch appears to be part of a well-considered series with attention to edge cases and performance implications.
