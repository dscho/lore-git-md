This is a proposal to refactor Git's garbage collection heuristics by moving reflog expiration and other maintenance tasks into `git-maintenance` rather than `git gc --auto`. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion about improving GC triggering conditions by proposing a more architectural solution.

The key idea is that `git-maintenance` already has a better framework for handling discrete maintenance tasks with conditional execution, making it a more natural home for reflog expiration and other cleanup operations. Steinhardt suggests adding a dedicated reflog expiration task to maintenance and eventually having maintenance replace `git gc --auto` entirely by composing specific subtasks.

The email acknowledges the technical challenges Junio raised about querying stale data in reflogs and other subsystems, noting that current APIs would require expensive full scans. Steinhardt proposes a practical heuristic alternative - just checking the HEAD reflog as a proxy for overall reflog state, which would be much cheaper to implement.

This represents a significant shift in approach from the current thread discussion, moving from tweaking `gc --auto` heuristics to considering a larger architectural change that would distribute GC responsibilities across the more modular maintenance subsystem. The proposal seems well-considered but would require substantial implementation work to realize its benefits.
