Bugfix follow-up regarding HTTP authentication and netrc usage. Peter Georg responds to Brian m. carlson's suggestion to use `git-credential-netrc` as a replacement for direct netrc file support in Git's HTTP authentication. 

Georg acknowledges the reasonableness of this approach, noting that he was previously unaware of the credential helper's existence but confirms it works for his use case. He suggests that if the undocumented netrc support is eventually removed, the credential helper alternative should be properly documented to help users transition.

This email represents a user perspective on the ongoing discussion about HTTP authentication technical debt, specifically supporting the direction of moving netrc functionality to a dedicated credential helper rather than maintaining built-in support. The exchange highlights the importance of discoverability when deprecating features, even undocumented ones that users may rely on.
