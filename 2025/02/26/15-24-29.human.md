Refactoring patch in a series to enable reflog expiration as a maintenance task.

This is the fourth patch in a 6-part series that will eventually add a "reflog-expire" maintenance task. The series is systematically refactoring reflog expiration code to remove global state and make it reusable by the maintenance subsystem. 

This patch moved configuration-related functions out of builtin/reflog.c and into reflog.c, making them available for other parts of Git to use. The key changes are:
- Moving `find_cfg_ent()`, `reflog_expire_config()`, and `set_reflog_expiry_param()` (renamed to `reflog_expire_options_set_refname()`) to reflog.c
- Adding corresponding declarations to reflog.h
- Replacing magic numbers with named constants (`REFLOG_EXPIRE_TOTAL` and `REFLOG_EXPIRE_UNREACH`)

The changes are mechanical and focused on code organization - no behavior is altered. This continues the pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, where functionality is being extracted from builtin/reflog.c to make it more modular. The patch appears well-structured and follows the logical progression of the series.
