Refactoring patch in a series adding reflog expiration to Git maintenance. This is the first of six patches that will eventually introduce a new "reflog-expire" maintenance task.

The patch renames `struct cmd_reflog_expire_cb` to `struct reflog_expire_options` to better reflect its purpose as a container for expiration parameters rather than just a callback structure. This change is preparatory work for exposing the structure more widely in the codebase, particularly for use in `builtin/gc.c` in later patches.

The rename is mechanical but thorough, updating all related variable names from `cmd` to `opts` across three files (builtin/reflog.c, reflog.c, and reflog.h). The change appears clean and well-contained, with no functional changes beyond the naming update. This sets up the codebase for the subsequent patches that will refactor reflog expiration to work with the maintenance infrastructure.
