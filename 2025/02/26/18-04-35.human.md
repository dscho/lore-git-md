This is a refactoring patch that's part of the `git-diff-pairs` plumbing command development. It's the first in a 3-part series that has already gone through multiple rounds of review and is now in its final polishing stage.

The patch splits the existing `diff_addremove()` and `diff_change()` functions into two layers - low-level functions that return the created `diff_filepair` structure, and thin wrapper functions that maintain the original behavior of discarding the filepair after adding it to the queue. Junio Hamano notes this is the right approach and gives the patch his approval, though he suggests the commit message could be more explicit about how the original functions are preserved as wrappers.

This foundational refactoring enables the subsequent patches in the series to build the new `git-diff-pairs` command, which provides efficient batch processing of blob diffs. The series as a whole has incorporated feedback from multiple reviewers and appears ready for inclusion, with this particular patch being a straightforward internal restructuring that maintains existing behavior while providing the needed infrastructure for the new feature.
