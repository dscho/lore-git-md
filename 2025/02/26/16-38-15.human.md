The email highlights a version-aware configuration approach that could address the backspace visibility issue without compromising tab display in diffs (the key drawback of earlier proposals). While the suggestion appears technically sound, the author hasn't yet verified how this approach composes with other `less` version-specific settings, leaving that as an open question. The proposal maintains the thread's focus on finding solutions that work within Git's constraints of preserving diff readability while addressing the backspace display inconsistency.
