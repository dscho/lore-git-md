Refactoring patch for xdiff library's signed comparison warnings.

This is the first of a 6-part series that moves the `DISABLE_SIGN_COMPARE_WARNINGS` macro from the shared xinclude.h header into individual xdiff source files that need it. The change makes the warning suppression more targeted, only applying to files that actually perform signed/unsigned comparisons (xemit.c, xhistogram.c, xpatience.c, and xutils.c), while leaving xmerge.c and xprepare.c unaffected since they don't have these warnings.

The patch appears straightforward and well-scoped, with a clear commit message explaining the rationale. This looks like a clean refactoring that improves the codebase by localizing compiler warning suppression rather than applying it globally. The change is mechanical but sensible, and the series title suggests there are more xdiff improvements coming.
