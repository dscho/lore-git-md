Maintainer feedback on reflog migration optimization. Junio C Hamano questions the motivation for adding a `--skip-reflog` flag to `git refs migrate`, suggesting it may be too narrowly focused on the migration use case. He proposes instead creating a more general `git reflog drop` subcommand that could handle reflog removal in any context, not just during backend migration.

This appears to be a fundamental design challenge rather than an implementation issue - the maintainer is questioning whether the optimization belongs in the migration path at all. The feedback suggests the current approach may be solving a migration-specific case of a more general problem (unwanted reflogs) that could be better addressed with a dedicated reflog management command.

The patch had previously received positive technical reviews and was considered ready for merging, but this maintainer feedback may require reconsidering the architectural approach. The author will likely need to either justify why migration is the right place for this functionality or explore implementing a more general solution.
