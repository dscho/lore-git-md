Documentation patch clarifying submodule merge behavior in both ort and recursive strategies.

Lucas Seiki Oshiro submits version 2 of a documentation patch that explains Git's special handling of submodule conflicts during merges. The patch adds identical explanatory paragraphs to both the 'ort' and 'recursive' strategy sections in merge-strategies.adoc, describing two special conflict resolution cases introduced in commit 68d03e4a6e44.

The first case handles when one submodule commit is a descendant of the other (attempting a fast-forward), while the second case suggests a resolution when there exists a common descendant of the two conflicting commits. The v2 revision moves the documentation from git-merge.adoc to merge-strategies.adoc and improves the clarity of the explanation based on feedback from Elijah Newren.

The patch is straightforward and well-motivated, documenting behavior that was previously only visible in the merge-ort and merge-recursive source code. The identical text in both strategy sections is intentional since both implementations share this behavior. The change helps users understand what to expect when encountering submodule conflicts during merges.
