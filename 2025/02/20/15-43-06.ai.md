The critique focuses on architectural consistency rather than the specific verify-tag changes. Junio notes this pattern appears elsewhere in the series (referencing patch 1/12) and suggests a systematic fix would be better than case-by-case workarounds. His proposal would maintain the same configuration behavior while eliminating special cases in individual commands.

This feedback challenges a core assumption of the series' methodology - that each builtin must handle NULL repository cases locally.
