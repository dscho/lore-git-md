Performance investigation follow-up on `git bisect` slowdowns.

D. Ben Knoble points to a related thread about `git bisect` performance issues, suggesting this may be an ongoing problem rather than an isolated case. The original report from Uwe Kleine-König described extremely slow bisect initialization (18+ minutes on SSD, nearly an hour on HDD) in a large kernel repository with 572k revisions. While the new email doesn't provide additional analysis, the reference to another thread indicates this might be part of a broader performance pattern worth investigating.

The lack of technical details in this follow-up leaves open whether these are the same underlying issue or different manifestations of bisect performance problems. Maintainers will need to examine both threads to determine if this represents a regression, expected behavior for large histories, or multiple distinct performance bottlenecks in the bisect machinery.
