This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Git's pack file regeneration behavior during auto-gc. Pierre Ossman confirms that while partial cloning (as suggested by Han Young) does contribute to the issue, they're also seeing the same problematic behavior in non-partial-clone repositories like their TigerVNC repo, where small pack files are still being unnecessarily regenerated despite being only a week old.

The email serves to both acknowledge Han's technical insight about promisor packs while expanding the scope of the issue beyond just partial clones. Pierre is looking for ways to help investigate further, suggesting this may be a more general problem with Git's pack reuse logic rather than just a partial clone edge case. The mention of a 21MB pack being regenerated shows the behavior occurs even below typical size thresholds that would trigger repacking.

This keeps the thread moving forward by validating one potential cause while opening up the investigation to other scenarios, making it would be helpful for packfile experts to weigh in on the broader behavior.
