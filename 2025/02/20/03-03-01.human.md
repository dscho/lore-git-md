Bugfix follow-up regarding packfile regeneration in auto-gc. Han Young responds to Pierre Ossman's report about Git unnecessarily repacking objects during auto-gc by suggesting the issue might relate to partial (promisor) clones. They note that `git-repack` currently always repacks promisor packs, even when they're marked as keep packs, and point to a proposed fix from another thread. This adds a new technical dimension to the discussion, identifying a specific edge case (partial clones) that could explain the observed pack duplication behavior. The response is brief but provides a concrete lead for investigation, though it doesn't confirm whether this actually matches Pierre's scenario. This would primarily interest developers working on Git's object storage and partial clone functionality.
