Junio Hamano provides review feedback on the first patch in Usman Akinyemi's `the_repository` removal series. This is a minor technical discussion about the initialization refactoring of `verify-tag`.

The reviewer makes three key points about the patch that moves the `git_config()` call in `cmd_verify_tag()`:
1. Questions whether this small change qualifies as a "refactor"
2. Notes the new config call placement could prevent future configuration of options that appear earlier in the function
3. Disagrees with the stated rationale for switching from `git_config()` to `repo_config()`, arguing that top-level commands can legitimately use the global `the_repository`

The feedback suggests the change may be premature optimization and could unintentionally limit future flexibility. The discussion focuses on proper initialization patterns rather than the series' broader goal of removing `the_repository` usage. This appears to be a minor course correction early in what would become a successful 12-patch series.
