**Integration workflow discussion**

Jeff King responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion to use the 'jch' branch for Coverity scanning, reporting that he's already made this switch in his workflow. The change has surfaced some new static analysis findings - mostly noise but a few potentially significant issues that he's following up on in their respective threads.

The core concern Jeff raises is about workflow efficiency. While he's willing to help shoulder some of the integration burden, he worries that scanning 'jch' might create duplicate work rather than distribute it - if he and Junio both independently investigate the same CI failures from other contributors' topics. He notes this could further delay his own backlog of work.

Jeff acknowledges Junio currently handles much of this integration testing and is open to trying the 'jch'-based approach for now to evaluate its practical impact. The tone is pragmatic concern rather than outright objection, with a clear intent to assess the real-world effects before drawing conclusions. This continues the thread's thoughtful examination of how to optimize static analysis coverage without overburdening maintainers.
