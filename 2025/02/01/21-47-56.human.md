This is an announcement for the monthly Git Rev News digest. Christian Couder shares that edition 119 of the Git community newsletter is now available, highlighting contributions from several community members (Justin Tobler, D. Ben Knoble, Brandon Pugh, Štěpán Němec, and Adam Johnson). The newsletter provides updates on Git development, including patches, discussions, and other community activities from the past month.

The announcement also notes that issue #747 is open for contributions to the next edition, inviting community members to participate in curating future newsletters. This is a routine monthly update rather than a technical contribution - it's primarily of interest to those who follow Git development news and community activities. The newsletter itself would contain the substantive content about recent Git development happenings.
