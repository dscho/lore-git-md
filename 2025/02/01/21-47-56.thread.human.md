The Git Rev News team has published the 119th edition of their monthly digest summarizing Git development activity. This edition includes contributions from Justin Tobler, D. Ben Knoble, Brandon Pugh, Štěpán Němec, and Adam Johnson. Christian Couder announced the publication on behalf of the regular editorial team (which also includes Jakub, Markus, and Kaartic), providing a direct link to the newsletter.

The announcement notes that issue #747 is already open for contributions to the next edition, maintaining the newsletter's open contribution model where community members can suggest items for inclusion. The Git Rev News serves as a valuable resource for tracking the project's activity by compiling highlights from mailing list discussions, new features, and other notable developments. While this particular email is simply a publication notice rather than containing technical content itself, it points to where such content can be found in the published digest.
