Refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's reftable decoupling series (v3 10/18). This continues the effort to remove Git-specific dependencies from the reftable library by eliminating the last use of the `ARRAY_SIZE()` macro in the reader implementation. The change replaces it with a direct calculation of array length using `sizeof(sections) / sizeof(*sections)` in the block printing function. This is a straightforward mechanical change that maintains identical functionality while further reducing ties to Git's utility macros. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by removing Git-specific constructs without altering behavior. 

The modification affects only debug/printing code (`reftable_reader_print_blocks()`) rather than core functionality, making it a low-risk change. The series has already addressed more substantial architectural dependencies in prior patches, with this representing one of the final cleanups of incidental macro usage. Reviewers have previously approved similar macro replacements in this series, and this change aligns with the project's goal of making reftable usable as a standalone library.
