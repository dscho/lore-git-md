This is a resolution to the GSoC 2025 microproject expiration debate between Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano. 

Patrick clarifies that he and Junio were actually in agreement about the core expiration mechanism - microprojects should automatically expire on their stated date without requiring explicit removal patches. The key point is that while expiration happens mechanically, interested contributors can proactively advocate to extend a project's lifespan before its expiration date. 

This email resolves the philosophical disagreement that had been blocking progress on the GSoC 2025 planning. With this settled, the focus can now shift to the practical aspects of mentor assignments and project selection that Patrick outlined in earlier messages. The resolution maintains a lightweight process (automatic expiration) while preserving the community's ability to intervene for projects they want to keep active.

The tone is collegial, with Patrick recognizing they were "talking past one another" rather than fundamentally disagreeing. This clears the way to move forward with both the expiration policy and the concrete GSoC planning that had been progressing in parallel.
