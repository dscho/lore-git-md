This is a design discussion about quoting behavior in `git rev-list`'s, specifically around the proposed `CQUOTE_IGNORE_CONFIG` flag for consistent output formatting. Junio Hamano raises concerns about the approach, questioning whether forcing ASCII-only quoting (via ignoring `core.quotePath`) is the right solution for machine-readable output.

Junio notes that while `core.quotePath` was originally designed for email patch safety (where 8-bit cleanliness was a concern in 2005), rev-list output has different requirements. He suggests that for machine parsing, we might want to avoid excessive quoting of non-ASCII bytes, similar to how `--objects` shows paths verbatim (with only LF and quote characters escaped). 

The key tension is between:
1. The patch's goal of configuration-independent output
2. Junio's view that rev-list output should minimize unnecessary quoting for better readability and future compatibility (he mentions a potential future where `core.quotePath` defaults to 'no')

This email represents a significant design-level pushback from the Git maintainer, suggesting the current approach may need reconsideration. The thread remains blocked on resolving this fundamental question about the appropriate quoting strategy for rev-list's machine-readable output.
