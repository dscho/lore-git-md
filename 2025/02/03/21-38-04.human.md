This is a status update on two performance-oriented patch series from Junio C Hamano in response to Derrick Stolee's progress report.

The email confirms that Stolee's `ds/backfill` series (introducing bulk blob prefetching for sparse checkouts) has been rebased onto master after its prerequisite path-walk changes were merged, with a v3 iteration coming soon. This addresses the lazy-loading inefficiency in blobless clones by adding `--sparse` and `--min-batch-size` options to the new `git backfill` command.

For the `ds/name-hash-tweaks` series (adding configurable name-hash algorithms to improve delta compression), Hamano notes it has received Taylor Blau's review approval and appears stable. The series introduces `--name-hash-version` options to `pack-objects` and `repack`, along with corresponding test infrastructure. Hamano indicates readiness to merge this to the 'next' branch.

The exchange highlights smooth progress on two significant storage optimization efforts, with both series now past their technical hurdles and moving toward integration. The tone suggests these are uncontroversial improvements that have cleared review requirements, though the backfill series still awaits its final revision before full acceptance.
