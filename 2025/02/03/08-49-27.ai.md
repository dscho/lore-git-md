This is a clarification in the ongoing GSoC 2025 organization thread regarding the microproject expiration mechanism debate between Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano.

Patrick confirms alignment with Junio's position that microprojects should expire automatically at their stated date without requiring explicit removal patches or community discussion. He clarifies that his earlier comment about retirement patches was only meant to allow for the possibility of community members advocating to keep a project by extending its expiration date, not to require discussion for removal. The key point is that expiration happens mechanically by default, with the burden on those who want to keep projects to justify their continued inclusion.

This resolves the apparent disagreement between the two maintainers, establishing that:
1. Projects will be automatically removed when their expiration date passes
2. The only community involvement needed is if someone wants to prevent expiration by proposing an extension
3. No patches or discussion are required for the default expiration case

The resolution of this design question clears a major blocker for finalizing the GSoC 2025 microproject list, though mentor availability constraints (as detailed in Patrick's previous email) remain as practical considerations for project assignments. The thread now has consensus on the philosophical approach to project expiration while still needing to work through the logistical challenges of mentor coverage.
