This is a brief acknowledgement in the ongoing test infrastructure discussion about failure handling in unit tests.

Junio C Hamano concurs with the alignment reached between Patrick Steinhardt and Phillip Wood regarding the test framework's design. The email notes this could be potential work for Seyi's Outreachy project, though no specific action is proposed. The thread has reached consensus on the design philosophy while leaving implementation timing open.
