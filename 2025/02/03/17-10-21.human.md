Stolee confirms this version appears stable with Taylor Blau's review and could potentially graduate to 'next'. He also mentions this needs to merge before his planned `--path-walk` repack option work, which has both textual conflicts and deeper design considerations.

Both features represent significant performance-oriented work - backfill for network efficiency in sparse checkouts, and name-hash for better pack compression. The updates suggest steady progress through the review pipelines, with backfill nearing another iteration and name-hash tweaks possibly ready for broader testing in 'next'.
