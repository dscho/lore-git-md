Maintainer workflow discussion about Coverity scan triage burden.

This email continues the ongoing discussion about optimizing Coverity static analysis scanning workflows, specifically focusing on the workload implications of scanning the 'jch' integration branch. Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's concerns about duplicate investigation efforts when both maintainers independently analyze the same Coverity findings.

Key points:
- Junio acknowledges the current approach creates redundant work as both maintainers investigate the same CI failures
- He reflects on initial hopes that triage could be delegated to original authors, recognizing this doesn't scale
- Proposes potentially throttling topic flow from 'seen' to 'jch' to make findings more manageable
- Raises but immediately questions whether pre-'jch' vetting would help, noting this would just shift rather than reduce workload
- The core tension remains unresolved: early scanning (in 'jch') catches issues sooner but multiplies investigation work, while later scanning (in 'next') reduces duplicates but delays issue detection

The email captures the maintainers' pragmatic acceptance of an imperfect solution while explicitly noting the fundamental workflow challenge remains open. This positions the discussion as an ongoing optimization effort rather than one expecting immediate resolution.
