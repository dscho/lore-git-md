This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing test infrastructure discussion about failure handling in unit tests.

Junio Hamano chimes in to express full agreement with both Patrick Steinhardt and Phillip Wood's positions in the thread. The discussion has reached consensus that the current test framework's behavior of continuing after failures is intentional design, and that migration to the `clar` framework (which aborts on failures) would involve different tradeoffs.

The email serves as confirmation that the core maintainers are aligned on this design philosophy, though it doesn't introduce any new technical content. The thread appears to be winding down now that the key points have been aired and agreement reached, with potential future work possibly being handled through Seyi's Outreachy project.

For readers following the test framework discussion, this email signals that the design questions have been resolved to the satisfaction of the maintainers, though the actual implementation path forward remains open.
