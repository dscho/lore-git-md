This is a naming refinement suggestion in Meet Soni's refspec refactoring series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to patch 3/5, which renamed several refspec-related functions for clarity before their move to refspec.c.

Patrick offers two main suggestions. First, he proposes formatting the commit message as a bulleted list of renames for better readability. The email also questions why static functions are being renamed when they wouldn't need to be exposed, suggesting this might be unnecessary churn.
