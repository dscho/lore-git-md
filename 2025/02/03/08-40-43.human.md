Bugfix follow-up in the packed-refs validation series. Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on the final patch integrating ref verification into `git fsck`. He questions the usefulness of the progress meter showing only 0/1 states, suggesting it doesn't provide meaningful feedback. More significantly, he proposes adding a `--[no-]references` option to control whether ref verification runs, noting users who want ref-only checking can use `git refs verify` directly. This continues the thread's pattern of refining the validation behavior while maintaining flexibility, with Patrick focusing on the user interface aspects of the final integration. The patch appears technically sound but could benefit from these usability tweaks before finalization.
