Performance optimization follow-up in merge-recursive string list processing. This is Elijah Newren's review response to Meet Soni's second patch in the series, which applies the same O(n²) to O(n log n) optimization pattern to `get_unmerged()` after the first patch for `process_renames()` was approved.

Newren raises several substantive questions about the patch's claims and implementation. He questions whether the optimization is needed (noting that conflict counts are typically small and other O(n²) operations may dominate), whether the performance improvement is measurable (requesting concrete numbers), and whether the implementation actually maintains correctness (implying the test coverage may be insufficient). He also suggests that with the index's properties, an O(n) solution might be possible, though likely not worth pursuing given merge-recursive's eventual deprecation.

The review continues the thread's technical rigor, with Newren applying his deep knowledge of merge-recursive's internals. His skepticism suggests this patch may need more work before being accepted, particularly around performance measurements and correctness verification. The exchange demonstrates the project's careful approach to optimizations even in code slated for removal.
