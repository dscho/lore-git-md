This is a response to a reported performance regression in Git's reftable backend when handling mass ref creation. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges brian m. carlson's findings showing a 30x slowdown (57.44s vs 5.5s) when creating 50,000 refs compared to the traditional files backend. While Steinhardt was aware of some minor performance differences (~10% slower), he notes this extreme slowdown is in a different category altogether. 

The issue appears significant for initial push operations in repositories with many refs, though the exact workflow impact remains unclear. Steinhardt confirms he'll investigate but indicates it may take until next week to look into the root cause. The response suggests this is a serious performance issue that warrants attention, though not an immediate emergency. The tone is appreciative of the clear report while acknowledging the concerning nature of the findings.
