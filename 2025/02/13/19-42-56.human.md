Performance optimization discussion about ambiguous ref warnings in bulk operations. Jeff King responds to Patrick Steinhardt's analysis of the `refs_verify_refname_available()` bottleneck in the reftable backend, focusing on the overhead from ambiguous refname warnings during mass ref updates.

King proposes two approaches to eliminate the warning overhead: a quick fix that temporarily disables the global warning flag in `update-ref`, and a more thorough solution that adds a `GET_OID_SKIP_OBJREF` flag to suppress warnings per-call. The accompanying patch demonstrates the flag-based approach but reveals complications in revision.c where the same codepath handles both command-line and stdin inputs. 

The discussion suggests the warning may not provide enough value to justify its cost, particularly for bulk operations. King leans toward either keeping the simple global flag approach or removing the warning entirely, noting it would be more useful at ref creation time rather than during lookups. This continues the thread's focus on optimizing the reftable backend's worst-case performance while maintaining correctness.
