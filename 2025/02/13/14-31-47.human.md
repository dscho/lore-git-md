Performance optimization follow-up for reftable's ref verification bottleneck. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's suggestion about batching ref verification checks, explaining why the proposed approach would be problematic at scale. 

The core issue remains the `refs_verify_refname_available()` function, which accounts for 80% of runtime in the 50,000-ref test case. While batching ref updates seemed promising, Patrick notes that fully iterating through millions of refs would be impractical. Instead, he proposes a more surgical approach: making ref iterators reseekable to allow targeted checks without full scans.

This builds on Patrick's previous work optimizing reftable iterators, but reveals deeper architectural challenges. The current iterator implementation destroys state after reaching the end, preventing reseeking. Patrick plans to overhaul iterator lifetimes to enable this optimization path, though he invites feedback if this seems like over-engineering.

The email confirms the investigation is moving from analysis to implementation, with Patrick taking ownership of the iterator redesign needed to properly address the verification bottleneck. This represents a more fundamental change than the initial batching proposal, but one that could yield greater long-term benefits for the reftable backend's performance characteristics.
