Git-for-Windows maintainer Johannes Schindelin raises concerns about Patrick Steinhardt's header reorganization patch in the reftable decoupling series. While the technical approach has been approved by other reviewers, Schindelin reports significant build failures in Git-for-Windows CI and objects to the introduction of duplicate filenames across directories (like `compat-util.h` appearing in both `mingw/` and `msvc/` subdirectories). He notes this complicates debugging and code navigation, suggesting Git should reduce filename duplication rather than add more instances. The feedback indicates Windows-specific integration challenges remain despite the series being otherwise merge-ready, with Schindelin's team needing more time to adapt their downstream patches to these structural changes. This appears to be the final blocking issue before the reftable separation can be completed.
