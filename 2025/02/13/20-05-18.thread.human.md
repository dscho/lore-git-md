This thread documents the completion of a terminology standardization effort across Git's documentation, specifically in `git-commit.txt` and `git-rebase.txt`. The change transitions from using "subject" to "title" when referring to commit message first lines, while preserving "subject" for email contexts. M Hickford's patch, now in its second version, extends their earlier accepted work on `git-commit.txt` to cover `git-rebase.txt`'s interactive rebase documentation.

The changes are purely mechanical wording improvements with no functional impact, covering autosquash behavior (`--fixup`, `--amend`, `--squash`), commit message construction, and rebase interaction patterns. Junio C Hamano has indicated he will apply the patch with a minor adjustment (changing `.adoc` to `.txt` in the diffstat) for maintenance branch compatibility, though no immediate 2.48.2 release is planned. The thread shows clear consensus, with this being the final piece of the standardization effort that began with `git-commit.txt`. The patch follows the same clean, uncontroversial pattern as the earlier approved work, maintaining proper sign-off and formatting.
