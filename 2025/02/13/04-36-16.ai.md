Maintainer feedback on documentation terminology for pickaxe options.

This email from Junio Hamano responds to a documentation patch that proposed standardizing placeholder terms in `gitdiffcore.txt` for the `-G` and `-S` pickaxe arguments, changing from "regular-expression"/"block-of-text" to the shorter "regex"/"string" used elsewhere in the codebase. While acknowledging the consistency argument, Hamano expresses concern that "block-of-text" was chosen originally to emphasize that `-S` typically operates on multi-line blocks (like function bodies) rather than short strings. 

The maintainer suggests that if the change proceeds, the documentation should compensate by explicitly noting this multi-line expectation, since "string" might imply single-line content. He references Linus Torvalds' original design intent where `-S` was meant to detect unique blocks within the codebase. This feedback continues the documentation discussion from earlier in the thread about accurately representing pickaxe behavior, though here focusing on terminology rather than implementation details.

The email maintains the thread's pattern of careful attention to both technical accuracy and documentation clarity, while showing the maintainer's historical perspective on why certain phrasing choices were made. It doesn't block the change but requests additional explanatory text if the shorter terms are adopted. This fits with the series' ongoing refinement of pickaxe-related documentation to balance conciseness with precision.
