Performance optimization discussion for reftable conflict checking during mass ref updates. This email exchange between Patrick Steinhardt and Jeff King digs deeper into the reftable performance issues identified earlier, focusing on the expensive `refs_verify_refname_available()` checks during mass ref creation after deletions.

Key points:
- Steinhardt confirms `refs_verify_refname_available()` is the primary bottleneck after the earlier `repo_get_oid()` optimization, consuming ~80% of runtime
- King explains the function's inefficiency comes from needing to check both parent directories ("foo") and child refs ("foo/bar/baz") for each new ref, requiring full table scans
- King proposes batching ref updates to enable a single merged iteration over all refs being added, rather than per-ref scans
- They identify another optimization opportunity in `repo_get_oid()` where warning checks for ambiguous refnames (added in 798c35f) add overhead - suggesting either a global config (`core.warnAmbiguousRefs`) or new API flag to disable warnings during bulk operations

This discussion moves from problem diagnosis to concrete optimization strategies, with two clear paths forward: batching ref updates to reduce conflict check overhead, and reducing warning-related overhead in object ID parsing. The exchange suggests these could provide substantial additional performance gains beyond the initial `repo_get_oid()` optimization.
