This is a documentation patch that standardizes terminology in the `git-commit` man page. The author, M Hickford, notes that the first line of a commit message is currently referred to inconsistently as either "title" or "subject" in the documentation. The patch updates several instances in the `--fixup` and `--squash` option descriptions to consistently use "title" instead of "subject", except when specifically discussing email contexts.

The changes are straightforward and mechanical, affecting only documentation wording without any functional impact. The patch appears well-scoped and correctly formatted, though the commit message could benefit from mentioning why "title" was chosen as the preferred term over "subject". This seems like a minor but reasonable consistency improvement for Git's documentation.
