Performance optimization follow-up in the reftable backend investigation, focusing on the `refs_verify_refname_available()` bottleneck. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Jeff King's suggestion about batching ref updates by explaining implementation challenges with the current iterator design.

The key technical hurdle is that while the reftable backend has reseekable iterators, the generic ref iterator infrastructure doesn't support this capability yet. Steinhardt notes that generic iterators are immediately freed when exhausted, making reseeking impossible. He reveals this is part of ongoing work he's been doing for months to optimize iterator lifetimes, similar to previous improvements made to reftable-specific iterators.

The email highlights two specific technical constraints:
1. The naive approach of parallel iteration through refnames and ref iterator would be inefficient with millions of refs
2. Current iterator lifecycle management prevents the desired optimization path of reusing and reseeking iterators

Steinhardt indicates he'll proceed with revamping the generic iterator implementation to support reseeking, while humorously inviting feedback if this approach seems misguided. This represents a deeper architectural change than the originally proposed batching solution, suggesting the performance improvements will require more foundational work than initially hoped. The email maintains focus on the core issue (iterator infrastructure limitations) while acknowledging the complexity of implementing an optimal solution.
