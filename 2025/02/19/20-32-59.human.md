This is part 11/12 of Usman Akinyemi's systematic removal of `the_repository` global variable usage, specifically targeting the `for-each-ref` builtin. Following the established two-phase pattern seen throughout the series, this patch completes the conversion after the initialization restructuring done in patch 10/12.

The changes are straightforward and consistent with previous conversions: removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define, activating the `repo` parameter (previously marked UNUSED), and switching from `git_config()` to `repo_config()`. The patch maintains the special handling of the `-h` help flag that was restructured in the previous patch, ensuring help works properly even outside a Git repository.

As with the rest of the series, this is a clean, focused change that follows the project's established patterns for removing global state dependencies.
