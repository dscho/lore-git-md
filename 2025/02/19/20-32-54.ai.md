This is part 6/12 of Usman Akinyemi's series to remove `the_repository` usage from Git builtins, specifically targeting `send-pack`. Following the established two-phase pattern seen in previous patches (verify-tag and verify-commit), this even-numbered patch completes the conversion by actually removing `the_repository` dependencies after the preparatory work done in patch 5/12.

The changes are straightforward mechanical replacements: removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define, un-marking the `repo` parameter as `UNUSED`, and switching from `git_config()` to `repo_config()`. The most significant change is updating the final `send_pack()` call to use the passed `repo` parameter instead of `the_repository`. The patch maintains the same careful handling of the `-h` help flag case where `repo` may be NULL.

This continues the series' consistent approach of preserving behavior while enabling the architectural change. The changes are minimal (7 lines modified) and follow the same patterns successfully applied to other builtins earlier in the series. The patch includes the same mentoring attribution to Christian Couder seen throughout the series.
