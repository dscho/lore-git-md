This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series adding Meson build system support for Git's contrib components, focusing on CI testing of credential helpers. The patch enables credential helper testing across different CI platforms by:

1. Adding libsecret development packages as a dependency for Linux builds
2. Configuring the appropriate credential helper for each platform in CI:
   - wincred for Windows/MSVC builds
   - libsecret and netrc for Linux Meson builds
   - osxkeychain for macOS Meson builds
3. Maintaining the existing musl locale configuration fix from earlier in the series

The changes are straightforward CI plumbing to ensure the newly Meson-built credential helpers are properly exercised in automated testing. This completes the series' goal of bringing contrib components under the Meson build system while ensuring they remain tested across platforms. The patch appears well-contained and follows the established patterns from earlier in the series.
