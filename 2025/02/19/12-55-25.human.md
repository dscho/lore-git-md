Bugfix discussion for `git repack`'s promisor pack handling. This is a follow-up in the ongoing thread about ensuring promisor objects properly honor `--keep-pack` and `.keep` file rules during repacking.

Han Young confirms Tomáš Trnka's patch works as intended in their testing with a repository containing one large promisor pack and many small ones - the patch correctly prevents repacking of the kept pack while still consolidating smaller packs. They retract their earlier concern that keep packs might still be repacked.

However, Han points out a subtle inefficiency: while the patch achieves the correct end result, it still has `repack_promisor_objects()` iterate over all objects in kept packs before `pack-objects` filters them out. They suggest optimizing this by skipping kept pack objects earlier in the process to avoid unnecessary work.

The discussion also touches on test placement, with Han agreeing that `t0410-partial-clone.sh` is more appropriate than `t7700-repair.sh` for these partial-clone specific tests. The thread shows good progress toward consensus on both the practical effectiveness of the solution and its proper integration, with only performance optimizations remaining as a clear next step.
