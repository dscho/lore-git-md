This is a progress meter improvement patch in the bundle-URI download progress reporting series, primarily authored by Jeff King (Peff) though submitted by Toon Claes. It addresses a specific display issue when showing download progress for single large files where traditional percentage-based progress is meaningless.

The patch modifies Git's progress meter to show clean throughput-only output (e.g. "22.00 MiB | 10.97 MiB/s") when downloading a single item where we don't have meaningful count information. Previously, the progress meter would show misleading percentage counts (0/1 or 1/1) or awkward formatting when counts were unknown.

The change is implemented by modifying the progress display logic to omit the count when we're on the zero-th item of an unknown total. The patch includes thorough test coverage in t0500-progress-display.sh to verify the new display behavior. This is a focused improvement that builds on the earlier progress meter infrastructure work in the series, making the output more accurate and readable for single-file downloads.
