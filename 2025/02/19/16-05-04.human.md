Maintainer sign-off for Perl version standardization series.

This is the final administrative approval for Peter Oliver's two-patch series that aligns Meson's Perl version requirements with Git's existing Makefile standard (5.26.0). The patches have gone through multiple review iterations with Patrick Steinhardt, who now explicitly gives his Acked-by/Reviewed-by approval. Junio Hamano, while noting he wasn't involved in earlier reviews, confirms the series is ready for merging into 'next' after observing the completed review process.

The technical work was already well-documented in previous rounds, handling edge cases in Perl version string parsing across different Meson versions while maintaining backward compatibility. With no outstanding concerns and clear maintainer approval from both technical reviewers (Steinhardt) and the project lead (Hamano), this concludes the standardization effort that began with the Makefile changes in commit 702d8c1f3b. The patches are now cleared for integration in the next development cycle.
