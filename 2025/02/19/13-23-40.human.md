This is patch 13/16 in a performance optimization series for the refs subsystem, implementing seek capability for ref-cache iterators. The change builds on the iterator reseeking infrastructure introduced earlier in the series, now applying it to the ref-cache backend.

The patch splits the seeking logic out from `cache_ref_iterator_begin()` into a new `cache_ref_iterator_seek()` function, allowing the iterator to be reused with different prefixes. This required removing an optimization that returned an empty iterator when no references existed, since that would prevent reseeking to a different prefix. The author notes this shouldn't significantly impact performance since the iterator will still bail out early when no more directories need searching.

The implementation adds new state tracking (cache and prime_dir fields) to the iterator struct and updates the vtable to include the seek operation. This completes another piece of the series' goal to optimize refname availability checks by enabling iterator reuse across operations. The change is well-contained (74 lines modified) and follows the pattern established by previous iterator reseeking patches in the series.
