Performance optimization in the refs subsystem (patch 3/16 of series). Justin Tobler follows up on Patrick Steinhardt's patch that disables object ID ambiguity checks in `git update-ref` for performance reasons, noting one remaining unconverted call to `repo_get_oid()` in `parse_cmd_symref_update()`.

The original patch showed significant speedups (1.19x for files backend, 2.32x for reftable) when creating many refs by skipping unnecessary ambiguity warnings that wouldn't be seen in this plumbing command anyway. The change follows precedent from `git cat-file`'s similar optimization.

Justin's question highlights a potential inconsistency - while most calls were converted to use the new `repo_get_oid_with_flags()` with `GET_OID_SKIP_AMBIGUITY_CHECK`, one call in the symref update path remains. This appears to be an oversight rather than an intentional exception, suggesting the optimization could be made more complete. The question doesn't challenge the patch's approach but seeks to understand whether there's a technical reason for the remaining call's different treatment.
