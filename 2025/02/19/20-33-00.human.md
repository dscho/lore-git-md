This is the final patch (12/12) in Usman Akinyemi's series removing `the_repository` global variable usage, completing the conversion of the `checkout-index` command. The patch follows the established two-phase pattern seen throughout the series, where initialization was handled in a previous patch and this one removes the remaining global dependencies.

The changes systematically replace `the_repository` with the passed `repo` parameter throughout `checkout-index.c`, including in helper functions `checkout_all()` and `checkout_file()`. Key technical changes include removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define, activating the previously unused `repo` parameter, and switching from `git_config()` to `repo_config()`. The patch maintains the existing behavior where `-h` flag handling works outside a repository context.
