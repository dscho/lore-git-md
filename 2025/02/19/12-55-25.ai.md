This is a technical follow-up in the ongoing discussion about `git repack`'s handling of promisor packs with keep options. Han Young confirms that Tomáš Trnka's patch works as intended in practice, retracting their earlier objection about keep packs being repacked. However, they note a performance optimization opportunity: while the current implementation correctly prevents repacking of keep packs at the `pack-objects` stage, it still unnecessarily iterates over all objects in those packs via `for_each_packed_object()`. 

The email suggests moving the keep-pack filtering earlier in the process to avoid the overhead of sending objects to `pack-objects` only to be discarded. Han also weighs in on test placement, recommending `t0410-partial-clone.sh` over `t7700-repair.sh` since the behavior is specific to partial clones.

- Confirmed working behavior with promisor packs (both big and test repos)
- Current implementation correctly honors keep flags via `want_found_object()`/`add_object_entry()`
- Performance concern: `for_each_packed_object()` still processes kept objects unnecessarily
- Test location preference established (partial-clone test suite)
