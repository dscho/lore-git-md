This is part of a series implementing progress reporting for HTTP downloads in Git's bundle-URI feature. The patch focuses on wiring up the progress display in git-remote-curl.

The change enables progress reporting for regular HTTP(S) downloads through git-remote-curl when the 'option progress true' capability is set. It builds on earlier work in the series that implemented the core progress reporting infrastructure. The patch is straightforward - it adds a new http_get_options struct to pass the progress flag through to the underlying HTTP layer, and includes a test verifying the progress output appears as expected.

This appears to be a well-constructed piece of a larger effort, with clean integration into existing systems and proper test coverage. The change is small and focused, doing just what's needed to expose the progress reporting capability through the remote-curl interface. It follows naturally from the foundational work done in the series' earlier patches.
