Performance optimization for `git update-ref` as part of the refs subsystem optimization series (patch 3/16). This patch modifies `builtin/update-ref.c` to skip expensive refname ambiguity checks during OID parsing by using the new `GET_OID_SKIP_AMBIGUITY_CHECK` flag with `repo_get_oid_with_flags()` (introduced in patch 1/16). The change is motivated by two factors: (1) `update-ref` is plumbing that primarily deals with explicit OIDs where ambiguity warnings are less relevant, and (2) the checks create significant overhead in batch operations.

The patch shows measurable speedups: 1.19x faster for files backend and 2.32x for reftable when creating 10k refs against 100k existing refs. The relative improvement is greater for reftable due to its faster baseline performance. The implementation follows precedent from `git cat-file` which made a similar optimization in 2013. All four call sites in `update-ref.c` are updated to use the new flag, maintaining consistent behavior while eliminating the performance penalty of ambiguity checks. The change is backward compatible and doesn't affect the command's core functionality.
