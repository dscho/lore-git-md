Performance optimization follow-up for refs verification in the files backend.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Karthik Nayak's question about why the earlier optimization for reftable backend (which avoids re-verifying common refname prefixes) wasn't also applied to the files backend. He explains the files backend is more complex due to intertwined lockfile creation and verification steps, with some potentially racy checks against packed refs.

However, he identifies two cases where the optimization can still be applied to the files backend with measurable speedups:
1. During normal transactions (5% faster in benchmarks)
2. During initial transaction/migration (33% faster when converting from reftable)

The response shows careful consideration of the tradeoffs - while the full optimization isn't straightforward to apply everywhere in the files backend, there are still worthwhile gains to be had in specific cases. This continues the thread's theme of systematically optimizing ref operations across different backends.
