This is a technical discussion about a bugfix for `git repack`'s handling of promisor packs with keep options. Han Young responds to Tomáš Trnka's proposed RFC patch, pointing out a flaw in the current approach.

The core issue remains that promisor packs bypass `--keep-pack` and `.keep` file checks during repacking, which violates expectations around `gc.bigPackThreshold`. While Trnka's initial solution proposed copying keep-pack logic to `repack_promisor_objects()`, Han Young explains why this wouldn't actually prevent keep packs from being repacked.

Han suggests an alternative approach: filtering keep packs during the `for_each_packed_object()` iteration in `repack_promisor_objects()`. This would more directly address the problem by preventing keep packs from being included in the repack operation at all.

The discussion highlights the technical complexity of properly handling promisor packs during repacking operations.
