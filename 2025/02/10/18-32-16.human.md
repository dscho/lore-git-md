Maintainer note on completed `the_repository` removal series. 

Junio Hamano acknowledges Jialuo She's final approval of Patrick Steinhardt's 16-patch series eliminating `the_repository` dependencies in Git's path subsystem. The series has now received all necessary technical approvals (from Karthik Nayak) and final sign-off (from She), making it ready for merging. 

Hamano notes one potential textual conflict with She's parallel work on `get_worktrees_without_reading_head()`, but considers it trivial to resolve. This marks the completion of a major phase in the multi-year effort to remove implicit repository dependencies, with only three subsystems (copy.c, pack-write.c, tempfile.c) remaining for future work. The changes maintain existing behavior while making path handling fully repository-aware through explicit parameter passing.
