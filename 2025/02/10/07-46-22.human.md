This is a performance/UX improvement patch adding progress reporting during multi-pack-index verification. It's the first of a two-part series that makes the QSORT operation during MIDX verification show incremental updates rather than jumping from 0% to 100% completion.

The patch modifies `midx.c` to track progress of the sorting operation by monitoring position changes during the comparison function. It introduces a sparse progress display helper to avoid performance overhead from too-frequent updates, checking progress only every 4096 items. The change is contained to the MIDX verification path and appears well-isolated, with clear variable scoping (static variables for progress tracking).

The implementation looks straightforward and follows Git's existing progress reporting patterns. The commit message could be more detailed about why this matters (e.g., how large MIDX files might benefit from this), but the technical approach seems sound. Reviewers will likely check whether the progress tracking adds noticeable overhead and whether the sparse update interval is appropriately chosen.
