Review feedback on MIDX progress meter implementation.

Junio C Hamano raises technical concerns about Ayush Chandekar's patch adding progress reporting during MIDX verification's sorting phase. The core issue is whether the progress tracking logic makes unsafe assumptions about how qsort() processes array elements.

The implementation tracks progress by recording the maximum position seen during comparisons, assuming this will increase monotonically. Junio points out this may not hold true - different qsort implementations (even different quicksort variants) could compare elements in non-sequential orders, potentially causing the progress meter to jump around or complete prematurely.

This is a substantive technical review questioning the fundamental approach. While the patch's goal (adding progress feedback) is reasonable, the implementation may need rethinking to work reliably across different platforms and qsort implementations. The discussion is likely to focus on whether:
1) The current approach can be salvaged with different assumptions
2) A completely different progress tracking method is needed
3) The feature should be abandoned if reliable progress tracking proves too difficult
