This is a bugfix for `git repack`'s handling of promisor packs, continuing an RFC discussion about how to properly honor keep-pack rules. Tomáš Trnka responds to Han Young's technical concerns by providing real-world motivation: their 40GB promisor packs were being unnecessarily repacked despite being above `gc.bigPackThreshold`, causing significant performance and storage overhead on their btrfs system.

The email serves as practical validation that the proposed approach works in at least one concrete case, with Trnka observing that their patch successfully kept the large packs while still repacking smaller ones. They point to `git-pack-objects` documentation and implementation details to support their interpretation of how keep-pack behavior should work.

The thread remains in RFC status with open technical questions about the implementation approach, but this email provides important real-world impact data that strengthens the case for fixing the issue. The discussion now has both theoretical analysis (from Han Young) and empirical evidence (from Trnka) about the problem's effects.
