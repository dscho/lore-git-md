The change is straightforward - a one-character fix to properly spell "sorgente" (source) in the error message that appears when `git mv` encounters a bad source path. The patch is cleanly formatted with a proper sign-off and affects only the intended line in the translation file. No functional changes are made to Git's behavior - this improves the accuracy of the Italian localization.
