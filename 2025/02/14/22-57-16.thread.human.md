Usman Akinyemi continues their systematic removal of `the_repository` global variable from Git's codebase with part 6/12 of their series, completing the conversion of `send-pack`. Following the established two-phase approach seen in earlier patches (first restructuring initialization, then removing dependencies), this patch makes the actual switch from `the_repository` to using the passed repository parameter.

The changes are mechanical but important - replacing `git_config()` with `repo_config()`, updating the final `send_pack()` call to use the repository parameter, and cleaning up now-unneeded markers. The patch carefully maintains the special handling for the `-h` flag case where the repository parameter may be NULL. At just 7 lines changed, it follows the same minimal, behavior-preserving pattern that has made previous conversions in this series successful.

Christian Couder continues to guide the work as mentor. With this patch, the series has now completed conversions for verify-tag, verify-commit, and send-pack, with 6 more builtins remaining to follow the same proven approach. The work remains aligned with René Scharfe's broader effort to eliminate `the_repository` across Git's codebase.
