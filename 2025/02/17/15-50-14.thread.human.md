This thread is about a performance optimization series (v2, 16 patches) for Git's refs subsystem, primarily focused on improving refname availability checks across different backends (files, packed, and reftable). The series originated from identified inefficiencies in handling tombstone references in the reftable backend. Karthik Nayak is the primary author, with Patrick Steinhardt providing infrastructure oversight, and it has been reviewed by shejialuo and benchmarked by brian m. carlson.

The first patch introduced `repo_get_oid_with_flags()` as a backward-compatible API extension, while this new patch (2/16) adds a `GET_OID_SKIP_AMBIGUITY_CHECK` flag to bypass unnecessary reference ambiguity checks in OID lookups. This is a small but important building block that will enable later optimizations in the ambiguity check is known to be redundant. The implementation modifies `hash.h` and `object-name.c` to support this new flag while maintaining backward compatibility.

The series has already addressed several technical concerns, including resolving a filesystem leak issue in `files_fsck_refs_dir()` and establishing clearer iterator lifecycle semantics. Benchmarks show promising speedups (1.58x-7.56x for reftable, 1.19x-2.32x for files backend). Some open questions remain about CI's ability to detect resource management issues and opportunities for further generalization, but the series is progressing well with active review and validation from key contributors.
