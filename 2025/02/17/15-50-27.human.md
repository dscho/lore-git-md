Performance optimization patch completing the ref iterator reseeking implementation for the files backend. This is part 13/14 of a larger series addressing refname availability checks across Git's ref storage backends.

The patch adds seek capability to the files backend's ref iterator by implementing the new `seek` vtable function. Like other backends in the series, it delegates to the underlying ref-cache iterator. The change follows the same pattern seen earlier in the reftable and packed-refs backends, maintaining consistency across implementations.

Notably, the patch also explicitly prevents seeking on reflog iterators (which remains unsupported across all backends) by adding a BUG() call, matching the behavior established in previous patches. This completes the files backend's adaptation to the new iterator model while maintaining the same limitations as other backends.

The change is straightforward and follows the established approach from earlier in the series, suggesting it should integrate cleanly with the other optimizations. With this patch, all major ref iterators now support reseeking, fulfilling one of the series' key infrastructure goals.
