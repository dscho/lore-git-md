This is a refactoring patch in the performance optimization series for the refs subsystem, specifically implementing seek functionality for merged ref iterators.

Patrick Steinhardt extends the merged ref iterator implementation to support seeking, a capability introduced earlier in the series. The change is relatively small but important for the overall optimization effort. The key modification is splitting the iterator ownership tracking from the active iterators, allowing the underlying iterators to persist even when exhausted, which is necessary for proper seek behavior.

The patch shows careful attention to iterator lifecycle management, maintaining the earlier refactoring work that replaced `ref_iterator_abort()` with `ref_iterator_free()`. The implementation follows the pattern established by other iterator types in the series, adding a `.seek` vtable function while preserving the existing advance and peel operations.

This change is part of the broader effort to optimize refname availability checks, particularly benefiting the reftable backend. While not a performance improvement by itself, it enables more efficient nested operations by allowing iterators to be reused rather than recreated. The patch appears well-contained and builds logically on the infrastructure changes made earlier in the series.
