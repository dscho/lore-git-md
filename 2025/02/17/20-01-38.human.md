Bugfix for `git merge-tree --stdin` deadlock issue. This is a follow-up to Phillip Wood's patch that adds a missing stdout flush between merge operations in stdin mode, preventing deadlocks when scripts try to read output incrementally. Elijah Newren reviews and approves the change, noting that Phillip's performance measurements confirm the flush has negligible impact (less than 1% difference in runtime). The fix is straightforward - just adding a `maybe_flush_or_die()` call in the stdin processing loop. This completes the deadlock resolution that was started in the thread, with all contributors now aligned on the solution.
