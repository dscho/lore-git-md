Bugfix patch addressing threading issues in `git send-email` when editing messages. This is the first of two patches in a v7 series that has already been approved for merging after minor documentation cleanup.

The patch fixes a subtle bug where editing and resending the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` and shallow threading enabled could break the thread structure. The root cause was an incorrect increment of the internal `$message_num` counter during message edits, which affected how subsequent messages were threaded. The fix decrements `$message_num` when editing or skipping messages to maintain proper threading.

Aditya Garg provides a thorough explanation of the issue, showing how the threading logic depends on `$message_num` being 1 for the first message. The solution is straightforward - adjusting the counter when messages are edited or skipped.
