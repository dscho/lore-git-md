This is the final polish iteration of a test modernization series converting reftable unit tests to use the Clar framework. The series has already undergone thorough technical review across three previous versions, with v4 being prepared to address minor stylistic cleanups before integration.

The work represents the completion of a multi-phase effort to standardize Git's test infrastructure by migrating from custom test frameworks to Clar (originally from libgit2). All substantive technical work was completed in v3, including full conversion of reftable test assertions to cl_assert*() macros and creation of shared helper functions. The v4 iteration will focus on final adjustments like standardized assertion styles and fixing a misleading test function name.

The series has received contributions from multiple developers, with Seyi Kuforiji now preparing the final version after feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano. With all technical aspects resolved and no outstanding objections, this appears ready for inclusion in the upcoming 2.50 release window. The changes follow established patterns from similar conversions in other Git subsystems.
