This is a naming clarification request in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing `the_repository` removal and ODB refactoring series. 

Toon Claes questions the naming choice of `odb_source` versus `odb_files_backend` in patch 2/17 of the series, which renames `object_directory` to `odb_source`. The original patch explains the rationale for choosing `odb_source` over alternatives like `odb_backend` and `odb_alternate`, emphasizing that it better represents the high-level concept of object storage without implying implementation details.

Claes suggests `odb_files_backend` might be more appropriate, though this appears to run counter to the earlier naming discussion that deliberately avoided "backend" terminology for this structure. This is a typical naming bikeshed discussion that often arises during large refactoring efforts, particularly when establishing new subsystem terminology. The exchange shows careful attention to the series' naming consistency, which is especially important given these changes will form the foundation for future pluggable ODB work.
