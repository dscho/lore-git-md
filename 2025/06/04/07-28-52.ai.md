This moves the discussion forward by acknowledging that both approaches have merit depending on whether the preparation call is handled internally. The thread appears to be converging toward either keeping the getter/setter pattern if it handles initialization, or standardizing on direct access with explicit preparation calls throughout the codebase. The decision will affect how the 14 affected commands access this setting going forward.
