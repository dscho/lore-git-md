This is the final version of a two-patch bugfix series for `git send-email`, addressing threading and logging issues. The series has gone through six previous iterations with thorough review from Junio C Hamano, who has approved it pending minor documentation tweaks.

The first patch fixes a subtle threading bug where editing and resending an email would incorrectly increment the message counter, potentially breaking thread continuity. The second patch improves debug logging when email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery, now showing the final assigned ID and making the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on smtp-debug mode.

Aditya Garg has incorporated all feedback from previous rounds, with v7 making final cleanups like removing a redundant comment and fixing a typo. The changes are focused and well-tested, modifying just 13 lines in git-send-email.perl. This represents mature solutions to these specific edge cases after careful iteration, and will likely be merged once the last documentation phrasing is polished.
