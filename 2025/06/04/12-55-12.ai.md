This is the seventh and final version of a two-patch bugfix series for `git send-email`, addressing threading and logging issues. The series has been through extensive review and iteration, with Junio C Hamano indicating readiness to merge after minor documentation cleanup.

The first patch fixes a subtle threading bug where editing and resending the first message in a thread with `--in-reply-to` would incorrectly increment the internal `$message_num` counter, breaking thread continuity. The fix involves proper counter management during message reprocessing. The patch has been refined through six versions, with the latest changes focusing on commit message clarity and dropping an earlier `$message_id_serial` adjustment that proved unnecessary.

The second patch improves debug logging when email clients like Outlook modify Message-IDs during delivery. It now accurately shows the final Message-ID in logs via regex substitution of the `$header` variable and makes the "Message-ID reassigned" notice conditional on `smtp-debug` being enabled. The v7 iteration removes a redundant comment and further clarifies the commit message.

Both patches modify `git-send-email.perl` (13 lines net change) and represent mature solutions after careful review. The series is now in its final form, with only minor documentation phrasing cleanup remaining before merge. The changes address specific edge cases in email threading and debugging output that could affect users who edit and resend messages through certain email providers.
