This is a follow-up discussion in the compiler warnings series, specifically about patch 4/4 which addresses an unreachable-code warning in the files-backend code when `NO_SYMLINK_HEAD` is defined on Windows.

Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion about modifying the `create_ref_symlink` macro directly rather than working around it at the call site. Steinhardt had proposed wrapping the macro itself in `NOT_CONSTANT` to suppress the warning more cleanly. Hamano acknowledges this as a clever approach, though notes it wouldn't work for the original case that motivated the `NOT_CONSTANT` macro's creation.

The exchange shows the maintainer engaging with the technical merits of different approaches to warning suppression. While Hamano doesn't explicitly approve the change, his positive reaction ("That's clever ;-)") suggests openness to this alternative solution that would address the root cause more directly. The discussion remains focused on technical tradeoffs rather than style or process concerns.

This continues the thread's pattern of carefully distinguishing between real bugs and compiler limitations while seeking the most maintainable solutions. The interaction demonstrates the project's preference for fixing issues at their source when possible, even for what might seem like minor warning cleanups.
