This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing series about Git's code style enforcement tools, specifically addressing the line length configuration in .editorconfig.

Karthik Nayak responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about setting the max_line_length to 120 characters in .editorconfig. Junio had suggested that 80 characters might be more appropriate to avoid awkward wrapping scenarios where a 125-character line could become a 100-character line plus a short remainder. Karthik explains that neither .editorconfig nor .clang-format currently support Junio's ideal behavior of wrapping long lines to 80 columns, and that the 120-character limit was chosen to give developers flexibility while preventing extremely long lines.

Karthik acknowledges he could change the limit to 80 characters, which would cause supported editors to wrap at 80 columns by default, though users could override this setting. The discussion references GitHub issue #89 about "rulers" (visual line length indicators) in editorconfig, which both agree would be a useful feature for their needs. This remains an open discussion about style preferences rather than technical implementation, with no resolution yet on the final line length value to use.
