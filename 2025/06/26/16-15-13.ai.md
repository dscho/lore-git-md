This is a bugfix for Git daemon's socket handling during interrupted system calls (EINTR). The patch corrects a long-standing issue where the daemon would incorrectly advance to the next socket on EINTR during accept(), potentially leaving clients waiting. The problem dates back to a 2005 commit that introduced IPv6 support.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk provides minor editorial feedback on Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's original patch, pointing out two typos in the commit message: "childs" should be "children" and "unlilely" appears to be a misspelling of "unlikely". The feedback is purely grammatical and doesn't question the technical approach of the patch itself.

The original patch proposed modifying service_loop() in daemon.c to add EINTR-specific retry logic (with a maximum of 2 attempts) before socket advancement, while preserving existing EAGAIN/ECONNABORTED fallthrough behavior. It also included calls to check_dead_children() between retries. The change was focused and relatively small (14 lines).
