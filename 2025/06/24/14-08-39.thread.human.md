This thread addresses signal handling issues in git-daemon, particularly around zombie process cleanup on OpenBSD and crashes on AIX. The core problem involves platform-specific behavior of interrupted system calls during signal handling, with a race condition between check_dead_children() and poll() operations.

The discussion has evolved through several versions of the patch series, with Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belon initially proposing an SA_RESTART toggling approach. Phillip Wood has strongly advocated for a simpler solution that would drop SA_RESTART manipulation entirely, instead documenting platform behaviors and relying on git-daemon's existing EINTR handling. Junio Hamano has now signaled agreement with Phillip's approach, noting that git-daemon's code paths are already designed to handle EINTR properly. This would allow removing two patches from the series while accepting the remaining race condition as a known limitation.

The thread has reached technical closure on several points, including Windows-specific errno handling, but the self-pipe trick - which could address the race condition - remains a potential future direction that isn't being pursued at this time. With Junio's endorsement, the series appears likely to move forward with Phillip's simplified approach, representing a significant reduction in complexity while maintaining functionality for the most critical use cases.
