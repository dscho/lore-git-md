The Git daemon signal handling improvements have reached their conclusion after four iterations. The series, which transitions from signal() to sigaction() while maintaining Windows compatibility, has been approved by maintainer Junio Hamano for integration. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón implemented the Windows-specific fixes, including correcting typos in the Cygwin implementation and preserving Windows' SIG_ERR behavior without errno. Phillip Wood advocated for and achieved POSIX-consistent behavior across all platforms.

All technical issues have been resolved, including the removal of SA_RESTART dependencies and preservation of OpenBSD's zombie cleanup behavior. The final version maintains a clear separation between these signal handling changes and potential future work on pipe-based tracking. With this last acknowledgment email from Carlo confirming the typo fixes, the series is now complete and ready for merging into Git's main development branch. Documentation updates will follow separately.
