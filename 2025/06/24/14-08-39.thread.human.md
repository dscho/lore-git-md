This thread addresses a subtle signal handling issue in git-daemon that causes zombie processes on OpenBSD and crashes on AIX. The core problem involves how interrupted system calls behave during signal handling, particularly with the SA_RESTART flag's platform-specific effects on poll() operations. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón originally proposed a solution (now at v4) that toggles SA_RESTART around poll() calls using a new helper function, aiming for comprehensive platform support.

Phillip Wood has emerged as a key voice advocating for a simpler approach. In his latest email, he argues that git-daemon was already designed to handle EINTR (interrupted call) cases, making the SA_RESTART manipulation unnecessary complexity. He suggests dropping patches 1 and 4 of the series entirely, instead documenting platform behaviors while acknowledging an underlying race condition that a self-pipe would properly solve.

Junio Hamano has reviewed both approaches, noting flaws in SA_RESTART detection. The discussion now centers on whether to:
1) Keep Carlo's more comprehensive but complex SA_RESTART toggling solution, or
2) Adopt Phillip's minimalist approach that relies on existing EINTR handling while documenting platform quirks.

Both solutions leave the race condition between check_dead_children() and poll() unresolved, though Phillip notes a self-pipe implementation could address this in future work. The thread has reached a decision point between these competing visions for the fix's architecture.
