Performance optimization patch in the MIDX/cruft pack series. This is patch 5/9 in Taylor Blau's v6 series, which Junio Hamano has already approved with minor style notes.

The patch improves delta compression quality when repacking with `--unpacked` by treating loose commit objects as traversal tips during the name-hash traversal. Previously, only packed commits were considered as tips, which could lead to suboptimal delta chains. Now loose commits are included in this traversal, potentially producing slightly better compression.

Taylor acknowledges this is likely a minor optimization whose impact varies by workflow, but it's a logical extension of the series' goal to optimize pack maintenance. The change builds on earlier refactoring that moved the `rev_info` struct declaration to make this possible.

This patch has already been reviewed and approved by Junio Hamano, who confirmed the technical approach of treating loose commits as branch tips to improve object reachability analysis. It's one of several performance-oriented changes in this series that has been validated in production at GitHub with reported speed improvements of 5-20%.
