Git daemon signal handling bugfix series (v4) addressing zombie process cleanup on OpenBSD and crashes on AIX, with architectural discussion between Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belon, Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood, and Chris Torek. Core issue involves interrupted system calls during signal handling with platform-specific behavior around SA_RESTART in poll() operations, plus race condition between check_dead_children() and poll().

Current implementation uses SA_RESTART toggling approach with set_sa_restart() helper wrapping poll() calls in daemon.c service loop. Phillip Wood strongly advocates for alternative minimal approach that would drop SA_RESTART manipulation entirely, remove patches 1 and 4, document platform behaviors, acknowledge remaining race condition, and align with git-daemon's original EINTR assumptions.
