The git-credential-netrc helper now properly treats symbolic ports as ports rather than protocols, fixing the Emacs authinfo.gpg compatibility issue where "smtps" was incorrectly interpreted as a protocol.

The implementation includes comprehensive test updates, with git-send-email now validating SMTP ports and failing on invalid specifications. The test suite gains a test_expect_failure case for invalid symbolic port names.
