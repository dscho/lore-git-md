This is part 2 of a bugfix series addressing signal handling in the Git daemon, specifically targeting OpenBSD zombie processes and AIX crashes. The patch modernizes signal handling by replacing the legacy `signal()` call with `sigaction()` in `daemon.c`, which allows proper flag specification (SA_NOCLDSTOP and SA_RESTART) and removes the need for manual handler rearming on SysV systems. 

Junio C Hamano notes a typo in the commit message ("hs" -> "has") and questions whether the empty child handler is still needed now that rearming is unnecessary. The implementation uses `sigemptyset()` for signal mask initialization and maintains the existing empty handler function, though its continued necessity will presumably be addressed in subsequent patches. 

The change is part of a broader effort to consolidate platform-specific signal behavior handling, with this patch focusing specifically on the daemon's child process management. The use of `sigaction()` here prepares for future changes that will need to adjust signal flags, while immediately providing more reliable BSD semantics across platforms. The patch touches only `daemon.c`, modifying the signal setup in `service_loop()` and simplifying the `child_handler()` implementation. 

This intermediate step in the series doesn't yet fully resolve the OpenBSD/AIX issues - that comes with the later NO_SIGINTERRUPT handling - but establishes the proper infrastructure for signal control. The empty handler remains temporarily, though Junio's comment suggests this may be revisited. The change is straightforward and builds on the platform-agnostic SA_RESTART handling established in patch 1 of the series.
