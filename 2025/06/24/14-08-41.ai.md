This is part 2 of a bugfix series addressing signal handling issues in the daemon, specifically targeting an AIX crash. The patch modernizes signal handling in `daemon.c` by replacing the legacy `signal()` call with `sigaction()` for the child handler.

The key technical change is switching from `signal(SIGCHLD, child_handler)` to a proper `sigaction()` setup with `SA_NOCLDSTOP` and `SA_RESTART` flags. This eliminates the need for manual signal handler rearming (previously required for SysV systems) and provides more reliable BSD semantics. The change prepares for future signal flag modifications while fixing a known crash on AIX.

The patch touches only `daemon.c`, modifying the signal handler installation in `service_loop()` and removing the now-unnecessary rearming call from `child_handler()`. The implementation follows standard POSIX signal handling patterns, using `sigemptyset()` to initialize the signal mask and explicitly setting handler flags.

This appears to be a straightforward modernization that aligns with the series' goal of fixing platform-specific signal handling issues. The change is well-contained and maintains backward compatibility while removing legacy behavior.
