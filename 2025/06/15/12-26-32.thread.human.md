Alexander Shopov has resubmitted an update to the Bulgarian (bg) translation for git-gui, hoping to include it in the upcoming Git 2.50 release. The patch makes comprehensive changes to po/bg.po with over 1,700 line updates, refreshing translations for interface strings, error messages, and menu items while also updating copyright years and timestamps in the file header.

This version follows up on a previous submission from May 2025 that also raised the question of whether git-gui and gitk should standardize their po-file format to match core Git's approach of omitting source location comments. That broader discussion remains open, though this particular patch focuses solely on the Bulgarian translation updates. The changes appear technically sound and limited to the localization file, but await review from the l10n maintainers - particularly Jean-Noël Avila and Jiang Xin who coordinate translation efforts - before being considered for merging.
