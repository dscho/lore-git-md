Documentation follow-up to the maintenance task refactoring series.

Derrick Stolee responds to Patrick Steinhardt's v4 series that refactored Git's maintenance tasks to prevent race conditions. The series had already received positive reviews from Kristoffer Haugsbakk and Ben Knoble, with only minor message tweaks since v3. Stolee notes he has reviewed the final version and found it technically sound, with just one small documentation opportunity - he suggests clarifying the mutual exclusion between `--task` and `--schedule` options in the man pages, which was a behavior change introduced in the series. He mentions he's already sent a proposed documentation diff that could be incorporated.

This appears to be the final piece of feedback before the series can be merged, addressing the last remaining loose end (documenting the new option restrictions) in what has been a thoroughly reviewed and tested refactoring effort.
