Follow-up in curl typecheck warning thread. Johannes Schindelin reports additional compiler warnings in the `osx-gcc` CI job that persist even after applying Jeff King's patch series. He provides a parallel patch converting more `int` parameters to `long` in `curl_easy_setopt()` calls across `http-push.c`, `http.c`, and `remote-curl.c`, focusing on boolean flags like `CURLOPT_UPLOAD`, `CURLOPT_NOBODY`, and `CURLOPT_HTTPGET`. The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions of `0`/`1` to `0L`/`1L`. Schindelin notes these warnings only appeared after Homebrew updated to curl 8.14.0 and asks why King's original series didn't catch these cases, suggesting they be incorporated into the fix series. This appears to be additional fallout from the same curl 8.14 typecheck improvements, affecting different parts of Git's HTTP code that weren't caught in the initial review. The patch follows the same established pattern of adding `L` suffixes to integer constants being passed to curl APIs expecting `long` parameters.
