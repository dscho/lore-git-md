This is a follow-up discussion in the curl typecheck warnings thread, focusing on the technical details behind why certain type mismatches were problematic. Jeff King and Johannes Schindelin analyze why some systems (like macOS) caught additional warnings while others (like Linux) didn't, though they agree the underlying issue should be fixed regardless.

The conversation reveals that the type mismatches could theoretically cause problems on big-endian LP64 systems, though no actual failures have been reported there. Schindelin explains how the varargs implementation means the issue would manifest differently across platforms - working "by mistake" on little-endian systems but potentially failing on big-endian ones. Both agree this is mostly academic curiosity since the solution (adding `L` suffixes to integer constants) is clearly correct.

The thread shows consensus that the fix should proceed as-is, with Schindelin's additional patches addressing cases that only appeared on macOS CI. The discussion provides useful historical context about why these warnings appeared now (curl 8.14's stricter checks) and why they weren't caught earlier (platform-dependent behavior).
