This is a patch in the ongoing `imap-send` improvement series, specifically addressing CRAM-MD5 authentication handling. The patch fixes a case where CRAM-MD5 authentication would crash when OpenSSL wasn't available, replacing the crash with a graceful error message. 

Junio Hamano suggested this change would fit better earlier in the series (before the OAuth2.0 and PLAIN authentication additions), as it helps establish a cleaner foundation for subsequent authentication methods. The author has already sent a v15 of the series with the suggested reorganization.

The change itself is straightforward - it adds an OpenSSL availability check before attempting CRAM-MD5 authentication and provides better error handling. This is part of the broader effort to make `imap-send` more robust and feature-complete, particularly around authentication methods. The patch appears well-contained and addresses a clear edge case in the authentication flow.
