Jean-Noël Avila has completed the conversion of Git's second-largest man page (`git-log`) and its related documentation to the standardized AsciiDoc synopsis format in a 9-patch series. This mechanical conversion maintains all technical content while improving consistency, with special handling for log format descriptions containing escaped parentheses and %-prefixed placeholders.

Junio Hamano has reviewed and approved the series, praising the improved formatting of the `--decorate` option (now showing values in a clearer enumerated list) and noting only two minor points: a grammatical ambiguity in the `--decorate-refs` phrasing (which may inform future style guide refinements) and a question about patch organization regarding the "% to literal" change in patch 5/9. His overall reaction was positive, with a smiley face acknowledging the substantial size of the changes.

This marks the successful completion of converting `git-log`'s documentation as part of the ongoing project-wide effort to standardize Git's man pages. The series preserves all technical details while implementing the new format, including backtick-wrapped commands/options and standardized description lists for config options. With Junio's approval, the changes are expected to be merged, continuing the documentation modernization that Jean-Noël has been leading.
