This thread documents the completion of Jean-Noël Avila's multi-patch series standardizing Git's documentation format, now in its third iteration. The effort converts man pages to a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style, with v3 addressing final formatting polish after Junio Hamano approved the substantive changes in v2. The latest patch (6/9 in v3) focuses on `git-log`'s pretty-format options, mechanically wrapping option names like `--pretty` and `--abbrev-commit` in backticks while converting placeholders to italicized form.

The series has now converted all major documentation components including `git-log` (the project's second-largest man page), revision listing options, and line range syntax. Only minor presentational tweaks remain in v3, with no outstanding technical issues. While the core work is complete, parallel discussions continue about potential future enhancements like special space notation (`␣`), though these are being treated as separate follow-up work. With all patches reviewed and approved, the series is effectively ready for merging, marking a significant milestone in Git's documentation standardization effort.
