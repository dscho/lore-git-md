This thread documents the conversion of Git's `git-log` man page and related documentation to the new synopsis format style. Jean-Noël Avila leads this ongoing project to standardize Git's documentation, and this series tackles one of the largest remaining targets - the second biggest man page after `git-config`. 

The 9-patch series systematically reformats various sections including revision listing options, line ranges, pretty formats, diff options, and log configuration. While purely mechanical (no functionality changes), the conversion required special handling for the log format description due to parentheses escaping requirements. The changes touch 11 files with roughly 490 lines added and 470 removed, representing a significant but straightforward documentation update.

This follows the established pattern seen in previous documentation standardization efforts, though the size and complexity of `git-log` makes it more involved than typical conversions. The series appears ready for merging as a documentation improvement that aligns with current standards.
