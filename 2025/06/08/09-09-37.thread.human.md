Jean-Noël Avila's comprehensive conversion of the `git-log` documentation to the new synopsis format has been completed and merged after thorough review. The 10-patch v2 series standardized the man page and related documentation files, with Junio Hamano approving the entire set after Jean-Noël incorporated feedback about translation-friendly phrasing and clearer list formats. 

A minor post-approval observation from Kristoffer Haugsbakk noted that arrow symbols in the `--decorate` option documentation might render unexpectedly in HTML output (showing `→` instead of `->`), though this doesn't affect the correctness of the content itself. Kristoffer also mused about alternative ways to denote spaces in verbatim blocks, but this was more of an exploratory thought than a requested change. 

With the series now complete, this represents another step forward in Git's ongoing documentation standardization effort, particularly for complex commands with extensive option sets. The work establishes patterns for handling both command-line options and their configuration counterparts in the new format, which will inform future documentation conversions.
