Documentation patch continuing the standardization of Git's man pages to the new synopsis format. This is part 6 of 9 in Jean-Noël Avila's series converting `git-log` documentation.

It also standardizes the formatting of format specifiers like `%<placeholder>` and deprecated options like `--show-notes`.

The changes are mechanical and consistent with prior patches in the series, maintaining all technical content while improving presentation.
