This thread documents the final stages of Ayush Chandekar's 2-patch series removing `the_repository` usage from `builtin/prune.c`, part of the broader effort to eliminate this global variable from Git's codebase. The series takes a two-phase approach: first migrating the `repository_format_precious_objects` configuration into `struct repository`, then converting the entire prune command to use repository instances.

After multiple iterations since its June 8 submission, the series has now received final technical approval from Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed the proper sequencing of repository initialization after option parsing and comprehensive test coverage. The implementation demonstrates the established pattern for command lifecycle management during these conversions, with tests verifying that `git prune -h` works correctly both inside and outside repository contexts.

All technical feedback has been addressed, leaving only a final review from René Scharfe, who oversees the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The series serves as a reference implementation for handling repository-specific flags during such conversions, having successfully migrated both configuration checks and object traversal operations. While Patrick suggested a more comprehensive structural improvement (embedding `struct repository_format` in `struct repository`), this has been deferred to future work. The patches are now ready for final consideration by the maintainers.
