Thread summary: Performance investigation of `git log --follow` slowness with small files. Kai Koponen reports significant slowdown (2.3s vs 0.07s for basic `git rev-list`) when tracing history of a small test file (12-line test/index0.go in golang/go repo), even with commit-graph and changed-path Bloom filters enabled. The test case shows a 1-line modification being processed inefficiently. Potential causes identified: 1) unnecessary rename/copy detection for small, non-creation diffs, 2) possible lack of commit-graph optimization for `--follow`, 3) potential failure to respect `--max-count` or format options. The reproducible case uses golang/go at specific commit, suggesting optimization opportunities in rename detection heuristics and commit-graph integration for file history traversal.
