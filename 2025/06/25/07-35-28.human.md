Bugfix for Git daemon signal handling, part of a series addressing platform-specific issues with zombie process cleanup. This third patch in the v2 series focuses on ensuring reliable EINTR handling during poll() operations when SIGCHLD signals are received.

The patch introduces a new `set_sa_restart()` helper function that toggles the SA_RESTART flag around poll() calls in the daemon's service loop. This ensures the poll() call will properly interrupt when child processes terminate, allowing timely zombie reaping. The change is particularly important for platforms where SA_RESTART would otherwise prevent EINTR from being reported.

The implementation carefully wraps the poll() call with SA_RESTART disabled, then re-enables it afterward, maintaining the expected signal behavior while fixing the zombie cleanup issue. This builds on previous patches in the series that modernized signal handling to use sigaction() instead of the deprecated siginterrupt().
