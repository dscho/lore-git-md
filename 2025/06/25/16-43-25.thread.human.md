This thread has been discussing refinements to Git's clang-format rules and their integration into the build system. The 3-patch series, now approved by Junio Hamano, makes the RemoveBracesLLVM rule permanent, removes automatic line-wrapping by setting ColumnLimit to 0, and adds meson build support for git-clang-format. The series evolved from initial debates about line length preferences to focus on practical formatting improvements, with key contributions from Karthik Nayak, Justin Tobler, and Toon Claes addressing technical details like cross-compilation support and proper tool detection.

In the latest development, Toon Claes has identified an edge case in the formatting rules where spaces are automatically added around the `<<` operator in bit-shift operations (changing `(1<<7)` to `(1 << 7)` in pathspec.h). While the major technical points about line wrapping and brace removal have been settled, this observation highlights that some automatic formatting behaviors may need further attention or documentation. The thread has otherwise reached consensus, with the series ready for merging pending only minor discussion about the meson target naming. This represents a focused improvement to Git's formatting infrastructure that maintains developer flexibility while reducing unnecessary formatting noise.
