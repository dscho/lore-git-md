This is the second posting of a 2-patch series by Ayush Chandekar as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the `prune` builtin. The series first moves the `repository_format_precious_objects` global into `struct repository`, then updates `builtin/prune.c` to use repository instances rather than the global variable.

The author is resurfacing the series after it initially went unnoticed, as it hasn't received any review feedback yet. The changes follow established patterns for `the_repository` removal, touching several files including `gc.c`, `repack.c`, and `setup.c` to accommodate the moved configuration flag. The second patch completes the conversion by updating object lookups, path operations, and repository initialization in the prune command to use the passed repository parameter.

The work appears technically sound based on prior similar conversions, though reviewers will want to verify the changes don't affect reachability traversal behavior. This is a straightforward but important step in the larger effort to eliminate `the_repository` usage throughout the codebase. The lack of initial response may simply be due to timing - this repost gives the community another chance to review these incremental improvements.
