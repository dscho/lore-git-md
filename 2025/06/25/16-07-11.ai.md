This is a platform compatibility discussion in the ongoing Git daemon signal handling series, focusing on build system configuration naming. Junio C Hamano provides feedback on the `USE_NON_POSIX_SIGNAL` flag introduced in v2 of the patch series to handle platforms where `sigaction()` lacks proper `SA_RESTART` support.

Junio critiques the flag name as potentially misleading, suggesting it could imply a general preference for `signal()` over `sigaction()` when the actual situation is more specific: certain platforms' `sigaction()` implementations fail to properly support the restart behavior needed around `poll()` calls in the daemon. He proposes a more precise naming scheme like `SIGACTION_LACKS_XYZ` that directly describes the missing functionality rather than the workaround approach.

The email confirms the technical direction of the series is sound (transitioning to `sigaction()` where possible) while pushing for clearer communication of platform limitations through naming. Junio notes this is particularly important for future maintainers who weren't involved in the current discussion. The feedback focuses on documentation and naming rather than core functionality, indicating the series is nearing readiness pending these final polish items.
