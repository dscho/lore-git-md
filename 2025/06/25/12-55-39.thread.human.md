This thread focuses on optimizing Git's pathspec bloom filters to improve performance when handling multiple pathspec queries. The original report from Kai Koponen identified that combined pathspec operations were slower than separate invocations, prompting this optimization work.

The patch series introduces a new `struct bloom_keyvec` to manage multiple bloom keys (patch 1) and modifies the bloom filter checking logic in revision.c (patch 2). After several iterations incorporating feedback from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, the technical implementation is largely settled. Recent discussions have focused on API naming consistency and moving test-specific hash calculations out of the public API.

In the latest exchange, Lidong Yan addresses Junio's concerns about case sensitivity handling in bloom filters, particularly regarding PATHSPEC_ICASE on case-insensitive systems. While recognizing potential optimization opportunities here, both agree this should be deferred to a future series after polishing the current work. The thread shows the patches are in their final stages, with only minor naming and documentation issues remaining before they're ready for merging. The case sensitivity considerations have been noted as a potential area for optimization in future work.
