Lidong Yan's optimization series for Git's pathspec bloom filters has reached completion after six iterations, with final approval from both Derrick Stolee and Junio Hamano. The work introduces a new `struct bloom_keyvec` to handle multiple pathspec queries efficiently, replacing direct bloom key usage in `rev_info`. Key improvements include strict "for every" matching semantics, proper handling of literal pathspecs while rejecting wildcards, and a 7.5x speedup in large repositories like LLVM.

The series underwent thorough review from multiple domain experts, with Stolee providing semantic clarifications and Hamano ensuring API consistency. All technical concerns were addressed, including test coverage for various edge cases and performance validation. Junio has now confirmed the series meets all requirements, including DEVELOPER=1 linting status, and will merge it. This concludes a significant optimization for revision traversal with multiple pathspecs, though future work remains possible for case sensitivity handling and nowildcard_len optimizations.
