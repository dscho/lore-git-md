This thread focuses on optimizing Git's pathspec bloom filters, initially prompted by Kai Koponen's report that combined pathspec queries were slower than separate invocations. The current patch series (v3) introduces `struct bloom_keyvec` to handle multiple bloom keys while preserving the single-pathspec fast path, with all of Junio Hamano's review feedback addressed. The patches modify bloom filter checking in revision.c to properly handle multiple pathspecs and add comprehensive test coverage.

In a technical follow-up discussion, Junio Hamano and Lidong Yan are exploring whether the `.nowildcard_len` optimization - previously used for PATHSPEC_EXCLUDE - could be extended to other pathspec magic flags like FROMTOP, MAXDEPTH, and LITERAL. Lidong's analysis suggests most flags could benefit from this approach by restricting checks to directory portions, though PATHSPEC_ICASE may present special challenges due to case-insensitive matching requirements. This builds on SZEDER Gábor's 2020 work while acknowledging the current bloom filter implementation has diverged significantly.

The main patch series appears ready for final review, while this side discussion lays groundwork for potential future optimizations. The thread demonstrates careful consideration of both immediate improvements and longer-term optimization opportunities for pathspec handling in Git's bloom filters.
