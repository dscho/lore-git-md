This thread documents the completion of a performance optimization series for Git's pathspec bloom filters, addressing Kai Koponen's report that combined pathspec queries were slower than separate invocations. Lidong Yan led the implementation, which introduces a new `struct bloom_keyvec` to manage multiple bloom keys, replacing direct pointer usage in `rev_info` and enabling optimization for multiple literal pathspecs.

The technical work is now fully polished, with all feedback incorporated from key reviewers including Junio Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and Derrick Stolee. The series shows significant performance gains (1.33x-7.5x speedups) while maintaining backward compatibility and adding comprehensive test coverage. The implementation carefully handles various pathspec scenarios while preserving the single-pathspec fast path.

After six iterations, the series has reached its final form with all technical and administrative items resolved, including improved commit message wording and proper sign-off ordering. Both Stolee and Hamano have given their Reviewed-by tags, confirming the work is ready for merging. While some future optimization opportunities remain (like case sensitivity handling), this series provides a complete solution for the immediate performance issues with multiple pathspec queries. The thread demonstrates the project's attention to both technical correctness and documentation quality throughout the review process.
