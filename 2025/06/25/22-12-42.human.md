This is a status update in the ongoing "What's cooking" thread regarding the SHA-256 default hash transition effort.

brian m. carlson confirms they will be sending a v2 of their patch series to enable SHA-256 by default in Git's breaking changes mode (3.0+). The original 10-commit series, which Junio Hamano flagged as "needs review", handles various test and initialization cases for the hash algorithm transition. 

The author notes they're currently working on SHA-256 interop work for `index-pack` but plan to send the revised series this weekend. They remain open to incorporating additional feedback before finalizing v2. This appears to be a well-planned transition effort with thorough test coverage across multiple scenarios (repo initialization, built-in commands, and various test scripts).

The work represents a significant step in Git's multi-year SHA-256 transition plan, now approaching readiness for the breaking changes track. The author's mention of concurrent `index-pack` work suggests related SHA-256 compatibility efforts are progressing in parallel.
