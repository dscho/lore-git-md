This is a follow-up discussion on a patch in the clang-format integration series. The patch in question adds a 120-character line length limit to `.editorconfig` as a soft guideline for editors, separate from the stricter clang-format rules.

Junio Hamano questions whether setting the limit to 120 characters is the right approach, suggesting that lines over 80 characters should ideally be broken down to fit within 80 columns rather than just warning at 120-character threshold. He notes this is meant as a suggestion rather than enforcement, but wonders if the lower 80-character limit would be more appropriate.

The original patch author, Karthik Nayak, had justified the 120-character limit by noting that `.editorconfig` rules are editor hints (not CI-enforced) and that the Git project sometimes extends beyond 80 characters for readability. The patch is minimal, just adding one line to `.editorconfig`.

This exchange highlights the tension between strict style enforcement and practical readability considerations. The discussion remains open-ended, with Junio expressing uncertainty ("I dunno") about the best approach while acknowledging the informative background links provided in the patch.
