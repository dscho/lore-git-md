This thread discusses standardizing a clang-format rule that removes optional braces from simple single-statement control flow bodies (if/else/for/while) while keeping them for multi-statement cases. The rule, called 'RemoveBracesLLVM', was previously tested in CI since July 2024 without issues and is now being moved from experimental CI-only status to a standard project-wide configuration.

The patch makes this change by adding the rule directly to `.clang-format` and simplifying the CI script to use this main config file, removing 17 lines that previously generated a temporary configuration. The change maintains the same formatting behavior while applying it consistently across all development rather than just in CI checks. The approach is inspired by LLVM's coding style. The straightforward change appears well-justified after its successful trial period.
