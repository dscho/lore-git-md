Junio Hamano's review of patch 1/3 in the sparse-checkout refactoring series. This is a technical review focusing on correctness concerns around when `prepare_repo_settings()` is called relative to accesses of the new `repo->settings.sparse_checkout` field.

3. The same concern applies to all code paths accessing the new field.

Junio notes that while some cases (like in `builtin/backfill.c`) are safe because `prepare_repo_settings()` is called explicitly, others need similar guarantees.
