This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically part 2 of a 3-patch series moving sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to `repo_settings`. The patch migrates the `core_sparse_checkout_cone` setting into the repository settings structure.

Junio Hamano's review focuses on initialization safety - while the patch itself correctly moves the variable, he notes that several call sites (particularly in `builtin/grep.c` and `builtin/mv.c`) lack the required `prepare_repo_settings()` calls that ensure the configuration is properly loaded before access. This follows up on similar concerns raised about patch 1 in the series.

The patch appears technically sound in its implementation, but the review highlights an important architectural requirement for this migration pattern: every access point must ensure repository settings are initialized first. The sparse-checkout.c changes are correctly handled with an early `prepare_repo_settings()` call, but other files need similar verification. This series is nearing completion but requires careful auditing of initialization sequences before merging.
