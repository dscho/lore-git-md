While merge scenarios may remain difficult to support, Michal notes his primary use case involves examining changes and tracking history - operations where improved detection might be more feasible without disrupting merge behavior.

Participants are carefully weighing whether and how to address this edge case, considering the tradeoffs between correctness, backwards compatibility, and performance.
