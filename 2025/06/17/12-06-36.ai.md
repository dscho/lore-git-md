It also removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` workaround from sparse-index.c, showing progress on the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The implementation follows the same direct-access pattern established in earlier patches of the series, reading config values through the repository settings when needed rather than caching them globally.
