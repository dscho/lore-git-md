This is a follow-up discussion in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about two merged patches that were part of a v3 series. Lidong Yan responds to Junio Hamano's post-merge feedback about behavioral implications.

The first patch completed the transition of `run_builtin()` away from global state by replacing remaining `the_repository` uses. Junio suggests reverting an associated comment change, which Lidong agrees to do in a separate commit, explaining the original change was just for clarity while reading the code.

The second patch implemented stricter validation for invalid `.git` entries, changing behavior from silently skipping to erroring on non-file/non-directory paths (like FIFOs or devices). Junio questions the practical value of this change given how rare such cases are, suggesting it might break obscure but deliberate setups. Lidong now plans to discard this change entirely based on Junio's feedback.

The exchange shows good maintainer-contributor dynamics - Junio provides thoughtful post-merge review about real-world impact beyond technical correctness, and Lidong demonstrates responsiveness by immediately agreeing to revert one change and drop another. The thread highlights how even technically sound changes need evaluation of their practical effects, especially when modifying long-standing behaviors.
