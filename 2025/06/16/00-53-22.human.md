This is a follow-up discussion in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically about historical context around repository initialization behavior. Junio Hamano corrects some historical inaccuracies in Lidong Yan's understanding of when and why `repo_get_git_dir()` stopped performing lazy initialization.

The key clarification is that the behavior change actually came from commit 73f192c9 in 2017 (which introduced a hard `BUG()` for uninitialized cases), not 246deeac as originally stated. Junio also points out that the comment being discussed was originally about explaining the `startup_info->have_repository` check, not about avoiding `get_git_dir()` calls.

Junio suggests that if the comment needs improving (which he implies may not be necessary), it should be done as a separate patch since the current patch's focus is removing `the_repository` dependencies rather than comment archaeology. The discussion shows careful attention to Git's historical evolution while keeping the immediate patch focused on its core goal.
