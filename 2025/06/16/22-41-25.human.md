This is a follow-up to Jacob Keller's 6-part series improving submodule remote lookup logic, which initially received no feedback. Junio Hamano notes the lack of the original submission (during Git's pre-release freeze and summer slowdown period) may explain the silence, suggesting the timing rather than the content might be the issue.

Given the lack of objections and the series' clear separation of mechanical vs functional changes, it seems likely to be in good shape for eventual merging once the timing improves.
