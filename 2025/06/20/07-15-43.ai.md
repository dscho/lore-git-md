This is a follow-up bugfix series addressing test failures in batched reference updates, building on the recently merged v4 series by Patrick Steinhardt. Karthik Nayak submits two final patches to resolve lingering issues in the files backend and receive-pack implementation.

The first patch fixes a segfault in `refs/files-backend.c` where failed updates weren't properly skipped during batched processing. It adds checks for `update->rejection_err` in both loops of `files_transaction_finish()` and includes new test cases in `t/t1400-update-ref.sh` to verify the fix.

The second patch modifies `execute_commands_non_atomic()` in `builtin/receive-pack.c` to handle directory/file (D/F) conflicts by splitting reference updates into two phases - deletions first, then other operations. This matches the approach used in `git fetch` (commit 9d2962a7c4) and maintains consistency with pre-batched behavior where deletes could succeed while creates fail. The patch adds a NEEDSWORK comment noting the limitation and potential for future transactional conflict resolution.

Key technical details include:
- Standardized "D/F conflict" terminology (replacing "F/D")
- Lazy transaction creation to optimize the two-phase approach
- Test coverage in `t/t5516-fetch-push.sh` and `t/t1416-ref-transaction-hooks.sh`
- Documentation of the known transactional limitation via NEEDSWORK

The series has undergone five iterations with v5 adding the NEEDSWORK comment and cross-referencing the similar `git fetch` fix as requested by Junio Hamano. The changes appear well-tested and maintain the performance benefits of batched updates while resolving the immediate production issues encountered at GitLab. The thread is effectively concluded with these final fixes, though the transactional behavior for D/F conflicts remains marked for future work.
