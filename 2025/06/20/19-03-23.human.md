This is a follow-up discussion in the git-credential-netrc symbolic port bugfix thread. Brian Carlson raises important questions about the broader implications of supporting symbolic ports across Git's credential system.

The email questions whether symbolic port handling should be implemented in credential helpers at all, suggesting git-send-email should convert symbolic ports to numbers before credential lookup instead. Carlson notes this appears to be a git-send-email-specific feature rather than a general credential system capability, pointing out there's no documentation suggesting symbolic ports work for HTTP or other protocols.

This introduces a new architectural consideration to the previously technical discussion about regex validation. While the original patch's technical solution (RFC6335-compliant port names) had reached consensus, Carlson's email shifts focus to whether the credential helper layer is the right place for this functionality at all. The response may require rethinking the approach rather than just finalizing the current patch.
