This is a response in the ongoing `imap-send` feature series adding OAuth2.0 authentication support. The email addresses review feedback from Phillip Wood about the OAuth2 implementation.

He acknowledges the fragility of the current `snprintf()`-based approach (agreeing it should use `strbuf` or `xstrfmt()`), and clarifies that host/port fields are optional per RFC 7628 section 3.1, despite being mentioned in the comment.

The exchange shows the patch is in its final refinement stage, with only minor implementation details left to address. Garg also requests to be added to CC since he doesn't regularly follow the mailing list, suggesting future discussion may happen directly with reviewers rather than on-list. This patch remains part of the larger effort to modernize `imap-send` with OAuth2 support and other improvements that has been through extensive review over 18 iterations.
