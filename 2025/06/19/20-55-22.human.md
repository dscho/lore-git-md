This is a documentation patch in an already-approved series converting Git's man pages to a new format. The email is a follow-up to Junio Hamano's review of patch 2/9, which standardized the notation for commit ranges in the `rev-list-description.adoc` file.

The key point here is a visual observation from Jean-Noël Avila about how the notation changes affect manpage rendering in his special setup. While Junio noted the semantic correctness of the change (switching from single quotes to backticks for commit range notation) and observed minimal visual impact in standard rendering, Avila points out that in his color-coded setup, the dots in commit range notation now appear in bold (red) due to the backtick formatting.

This is a minor aesthetic note that doesn't affect the patch's acceptance (the series was already approved) but provides useful feedback about how formatting choices can have different visual consequences in non-standard rendering environments. The thread shows the attention to detail both in the original conversion work and in the review process.
