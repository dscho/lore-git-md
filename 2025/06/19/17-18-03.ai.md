This is a performance optimization thread discussing the `git fetch --prune` implementation. The email exchange between Junio C Hamano and Jeff King focuses on a minor behavioral side effect of the optimization - the changed ordering of dangling ref warnings in the output. Both maintainers acknowledge that while the new output loses some information about which ref deletion caused a HEAD to become dangling, this is not particularly important functionality. They note this warning has existed for 15 years without being widely noticed or used. The consensus is that users who really need this information can use `git symbolic-ref` to investigate, and the significant performance gains (from 410s to 4s in test cases) justify the minor output change. This discussion appears to be closing the last open question about the patch series, suggesting it's likely to proceed to acceptance.
