Performance optimization in the pack-objects subsystem, part of Taylor Blau's series improving MIDX and cruft pack interactions. This patch (5/9) enhances delta compression for loose objects when using `--unpacked` by including them in the name-hash traversal.

The change modifies how loose objects are handled during packing by passing the `rev_info` struct to `add_unreachable_loose_objects()`, allowing these objects to participate in the reachability analysis. While this doesn't affect pack correctness (the objects were already being included), it enables better delta compression opportunities by considering their relationships to other objects.

Key technical changes:
- Modified `add_loose_object()` to accept and use the `rev_info` parameter
- Updated `add_unreachable_loose_objects()` to pass through the `rev_info` struct
- Adjusted all call sites to maintain existing behavior where needed (passing NULL when not doing reachability analysis)
- Special handling for OBJ_COMMIT types to add them as pending objects in the revision walk

This builds on the refactoring from patches 3/9 and 4/9 that moved the `rev_info` setup, and has been praised by Junio Hamano for its clever optimization approach. The series has been production-validated at GitHub showing 5-20% speed improvements.
