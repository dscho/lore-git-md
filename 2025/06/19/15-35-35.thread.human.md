This thread discusses a proposed change to make `make coccicheck` fail when Coccinelle suggests code modifications, rather than silently succeeding while generating patch files. The original author noted that the current behavior makes it too easy for developers to miss potential improvements. Jeff King (Peff) has replied in support, sharing that he's personally encountered this issue and validating the usefulness of the change. The proposal involves modifying the Makefile to explicitly check for non-empty patch files, and so far the straightforward approach appears uncontested. With support from an experienced contributor like Peff, this small but meaningful build system improvement seems likely to move forward unless other concerns are raised.
