This is a follow-up in the bug report thread about `git checkout -b` succeeding during rebase when it should fail. Grant Birchmeier responds to the workarounds suggested by Jacob Keller and Phil Hord, noting the practical inconvenience of losing partially completed rebase work (6 of 8 files already manually resolved). He considers a manual workaround involving copying rebased files to a temp directory before aborting, but remains concerned about the unexpected behavior difference between `git checkout` and `git checkout -b` during rebase. The email reinforces that this behavior seems unintentional and counterintuitive, even for experienced users. While acknowledging the technical explanations, Grant maintains this is likely a bug rather than a design choice. The thread remains in the investigation phase, with Grant planning to test the suggested behavior where both branches end up at the rebase tip.
