This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. The series introduces configurable optimization of pack maintenance through enhanced pack-objects behavior, particularly for cruft pack inclusion in MIDX during repacking while maintaining reachability guarantees.

The patch adds a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config option (defaulting to true) that allows excluding cruft packs from the MIDX when possible. This addresses a performance issue where MIDX would unnecessarily track many unreachable objects from cruft packs, slowing down object lookups. The patch ensures geometric repacks using `--stdin-packs=follow` can safely exclude cruft packs while maintaining reachability guarantees for bitmap generation.

The implementation includes thorough checks to determine when cruft packs can be excluded, with comprehensive tests covering various scenarios. The change builds on previous patches in the series that introduced the `--stdin-packs=follow` mode and related infrastructure. This is a well-polished final patch in a production-validated series that has received thorough review rounds, with all edge cases addressed.
