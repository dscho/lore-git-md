Performance optimization introducing a new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for pack-objects, part of a v5 series addressing MIDX/cruft pack interactions during repacking. This patch builds on previous refactoring (patches 3-7) to add a mode that includes objects from unlisted packs when they are reachable from included packs and not excluded. The change enables geometric repacking to maintain reachability guarantees while avoiding the need to always include cruft packs in the MIDX.

Key technical details:
- Adds `STDIN_PACKS_MODE_FOLLOW` enum and modifies `show_object_pack_hint()`/`show_commit_pack_hint()` to handle this mode
- In follow mode, includes reachable objects from unlisted packs (like cruft packs) while respecting exclusions
- Special handling for potentially missing blobs (trees/commits already handled by revision machinery)
- Extends documentation with new mode explanation and adds comprehensive tests
- Tests verify behavior with unknown packs, loose objects, and edge cases around missing objects

The patch connects to the series' overall goal of optimizing pack maintenance by allowing more flexible control over which objects get included during repacking while maintaining correctness. It specifically addresses the scenario where objects in cruft packs become reachable through new commits in included packs.
