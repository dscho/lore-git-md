This is a v5 cover letter for Taylor Blau's performance optimization series that improves how cruft packs interact with MIDX during repacking. The series is now fully reviewed and ready for merging, with two small but important refinements based on production deployment at GitHub.

The key changes in this reroll are:
1. Better handling of missing objects during `--stdin-packs=follow` traversals, ensuring we don't add non-existent objects to the packing list
2. More robust MIDX behavior after noop repacks, where cruft packs may need to be included depending on existing pack structure

Production results show significant improvements:
- ~5% speedup for operations on repos with many unreachable objects
- ~20% faster average repack times across GitHub's infrastructure

The series also includes a platform compatibility fix from Carlo Arenas Belón to replace non-portable `grep -o` usage. While the core technical work was already approved in v4, these final refinements and production validation make this a strong candidate for merging in the upcoming release cycle.
