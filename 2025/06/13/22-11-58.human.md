This is an announcement about the upcoming Git Merge 2025 conference and Contributor's Summit. Taylor Blau shares that the schedule of accepted talks is now available on the conference website, noting they received many strong submissions but had to make difficult selections due to time constraints.

The conference will take place September 29-30 at GitHub's San Francisco headquarters, with the main conference on the first day and breakouts/Contributor's Summit on the second. Registration for the Contributor's Summit is now open.

The announcement serves as both a thank-you to submitters and an invitation for questions about the event. While primarily informational, it also functions as a reminder for interested contributors to register for the Summit portion. The tone is positive and welcoming, with an offer to answer questions either on-list or privately.
