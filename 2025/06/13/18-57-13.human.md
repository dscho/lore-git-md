This is the final approval for Patrick Steinhardt's bugfix series addressing edge cases in batched reference updates. The series has gone through four rounds of review and is now being queued by Junio Hamano, though he notes a known limitation around transactional behavior for directory/file conflicts that will need future work.

The two-patch series fixes critical production issues GitLab encountered with batched reference updates: a segfault in the files backend when handling already-failed updates, and inconsistent handling of directory/file conflicts between batched and sequential modes in `git receive-pack`. The fixes include improved error handling, clearer phase-based processing, and comprehensive test coverage.

Christian Couder confirms the v4 changes - which standardized terminology to "D/F conflicts", fixed typos, and cleaned up comments - address all his previous stylistic concerns. The series builds on earlier work to make batched reference updates production-ready while maintaining performance benefits.

While the immediate issues are resolved, Junio notes the solution for D/F conflicts isn't fully transactional, marking this as a known limitation via a NEEDSWORK comment and test_expect_failure case. This completes the current round of fixes while leaving the transactional behavior as future work, particularly regarding differences between files and reftable backends.
