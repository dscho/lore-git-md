This is a platform compatibility discussion about preventing future Solaris build issues through CI testing. Collin Funk responds to Jean-Noël Avila's suggestion about setting up Solaris CI to catch portability regressions like the recent sed compatibility problems. 

Collin notes that Solaris packagers are accustomed to dealing with such issues and suggests not feeling too guilty about them, acknowledging the difficulty of remembering all portability quirks. He provides a practical suggestion to use GitHub Actions with vmactions to test on Oracle Solaris and OmniOS (an illumos-based system), though he's unsure if this would have caught the specific sed issues since the builds still worked despite the broken commands.

The response reflects the ongoing challenge of maintaining compatibility with non-GNU systems while balancing development overhead. Collin's tone is pragmatic, recognizing both the value of better testing infrastructure and the reality that some issues may still slip through. The discussion continues the thread's theme of favoring portable solutions while exploring ways to catch problems earlier.
