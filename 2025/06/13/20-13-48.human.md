Platform compatibility follow-up: Jean-Noël Avila suggests adding CI testing for Solaris and similar non-Linux systems to catch compatibility regressions earlier. This comes after a thread where two Solaris build issues were fixed - one involving sed's extended regex support and another with newline handling in generated files. While the immediate problems were resolved by Junio Hamano's patches, Avila (who authored one of the original problematic commits) notes this is the second time such regressions have slipped through.

The suggestion builds on the thread's broader discussion about Solaris toolchain differences, where contributors agreed portable solutions using standard tools are preferable to requiring GNU utilities. Avila's proposal would help maintain this standard by automatically verifying builds on diverse platforms. The idea comes after successful verification by Brad Smith and Collin Funk that the fixes work on both Solaris and Linux.

This is a reasonable suggestion given the project's cross-platform goals, though implementing Solaris CI would require infrastructure work. The thread shows clear consensus that such regressions are worth preventing, given how easily they can be introduced when developers primarily test on Linux systems.
