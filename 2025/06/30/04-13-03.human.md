Bugfix for git-daemon's child process reaping logic. This patch addresses a long-standing issue where a badly timed signal could prevent proper cleanup of zombie processes. The problem dates back to 2008 when the reaping logic was moved out of the SIGCHLD handler, but error handling for `waitpid()` failures was overlooked.

Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's patch adds proper error checking for `waitpid()` calls, specifically handling EINTR (which can occur when the call is interrupted by a signal) and treating other errors as bugs. The change is small but important - just 4 lines modified in daemon.c to add the missing error handling.

This appears to be a well-justified fix for a subtle edge case that could lead to resource leaks in the git-daemon. The commit message provides clear historical background tracing the issue through two previous refactorings (2008 and 2010). The fix itself is straightforward and comes with a BUG() assertion for unexpected errors, suggesting careful consideration of error cases.
