This thread addresses a long-standing bug in Git's daemon process where interrupted `waitpid()` calls (EINTR) could leave zombie child processes. The original patch by Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón fixes this by properly retrying on EINTR while treating other errors as bugs, preserving correct behavior for successful cases (pid > 0) and no-child cases (pid == 0).

In the discussion, Phillip Wood raised several implementation details: style consistency (missing braces in an else-if clause), the use of unsigned formatting for pid_t (which POSIX specifies as signed), and a note about Linux's manpage claiming EINTR can't occur with WNOHANG—though he agrees the BUG() handling is correct for non-EINTR cases.

Carlo responded to these points, acknowledging the Linux manpage discrepancy but noting that other platforms may behave differently, suggesting the issue might need broader consideration in `run_command.c`. On pid_t formatting, Carlo defended the use of `uintmax_t` casting, citing Git's historical precedent (since 2008) and the fact that PIDs are always positive in practice. The discussion also touched on platform-specific considerations, such as AIX's 4-byte pid_t and Solaris's 8-byte PIDs, though both stay within INT_MAX.

The core fix remains sound, but the thread continues to explore edge cases in signal handling and platform-specific behavior to ensure robustness.
