This is a documentation patch proposing to explicitly forbid the use of generative AI tools when contributing to Git, following QEMU's lead. The change would add a new section to SubmittingPatches explaining that AI-generated content cannot be properly certified under the Developer's Certificate of Origin due to unclear copyright and licensing status of such output.

The patch cites QEMU's reasoning that contributors cannot credibly assert DCO compliance when using AI tools, as the legal status of AI-generated code remains unsettled. The new documentation section would make it clear that contributions known or suspected to use AI may be rejected.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial in its implementation - just adding clear documentation about the project policy. However, the underlying issue touches on important open questions about AI-generated content in open source that many projects are currently grappling with. The patch positions Git alongside other conservative projects taking a cautious approach while the legal landscape remains unclear.
