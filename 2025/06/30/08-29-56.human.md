This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Git's line length configuration and editor behavior. Karthik Nayak responds to Justin Tobler's discovery that vim's handling of `.editorconfig`'s `max_line_length` affects all text operations, not just limiting excessively long lines as intended. 

The key revelation is that setting a 120-character limit in `.editorconfig` would cause vim to actively reformat text to 120 columns during normal editing, which contradicts the project's goal of maintaining 80-column readability with only rare exceptions. Karthik acknowledges this unintended consequence and decides to drop the proposed change to `.editorconfig`.

This exchange highlights the technical challenge in implementing the desired line length policy (80-col guideline with 120-col hard limit) across different editors. The discussion has moved from debating numbers to understanding how editor implementations interpret these settings, with vim's behavior proving particularly problematic for the proposed approach. The patch is now being withdrawn as the team seeks alternative solutions that won't interfere with developers' normal editing workflows.
