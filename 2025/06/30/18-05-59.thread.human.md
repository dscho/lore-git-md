This thread documents a 5-patch series improving `git send-email` configuration documentation, with a focus on practical scenarios for various email services. The series was authored by Aditya Garg, who maintains the `git-protonmail` tool mentioned in the final patch.

The patches systematically address documentation gaps for:
1. SSL certificate configuration (with OpenSSL details)
2. Outlook-specific `outlookidfix` option (previously only documented as a flag)
3. AOL OAuth authentication (documenting the `git-credential-aol` helper)
4. Alternative SMTP clients via `sendmailCmd` (covering msmtp and proprietary tools)
5. Proton Mail integration (completed in this latest email, documenting both the official Bridge and community `git-protonmail` options)

The series maintains a consistent style throughout, with each patch focusing on real-world usage patterns. The Proton Mail documentation follows the same approach as earlier patches, providing clear guidance for users of both free and paid accounts. With this final patch, the series now comprehensively covers the major email service integration scenarios that were previously under-documented.
