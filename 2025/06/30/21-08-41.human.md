This is a maintainer comment on the final version of a refactoring series that removes sparse-checkout-related global variables as part of the `the_repository` elimination effort. Junio Hamano raises a performance consideration about the placement of `prepare_repo_settings()` calls in the patch series.

The series, now in its fifth iteration, has already addressed all prior technical feedback and initialization safety concerns. It moves sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to the `repo_settings` struct while maintaining identical functionality. The maintainer's note focuses on one remaining architectural consideration: the performance impact of calling `prepare_repo_settings()` in deep call chains rather than just at command entry points.

While not blocking the series (which appears otherwise ready to merge), this comment suggests future contributors should be mindful of where repository settings initialization occurs, especially in performance-sensitive paths. The observation doesn't require immediate changes but provides guidance point for similar future refactorings in the `the_repository` removal effort.
