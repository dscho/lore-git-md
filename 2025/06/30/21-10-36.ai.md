Maintainer comment on case-insensitive refs and reftable adoption.

Junio C Hamano responds to brian m. carlson's suggestion that case-insensitive filesystem users adopt the reftable backend to avoid pruning issues with case-conflicting refs. The response is brief and lighthearted, expressing general support for increased reftable adoption ("the more adopters we have, the merrier") while acknowledging through humor that early adopters may encounter some rough edges (referring to them as "guinea pigs" before correcting to "adopters").

This maintains the thread's technical context about reftable being a more robust solution for case-insensitive systems compared to the file-based backend, while not committing to any specific technical direction. The tone suggests the maintainer sees reftable as a viable path forward, though the explicit endorsement is left implicit in the joking encouragement of adoption. The comment doesn't address the alternative approaches previously discussed (filtering solutions, transaction improvements), keeping focus on reftable as brian m. carlson had proposed.
