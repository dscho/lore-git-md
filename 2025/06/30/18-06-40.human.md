Documentation patch adding OAuth2.0 helper info for AOL accounts to `git send-email` docs. This is part 3 of a 5-part series improving sendemail documentation.

The patch notes that while Yahoo and AOL claim to support app passwords for third-party apps, this functionality has been unreliable. Since Yahoo already had an OAuth2.0 credential helper documented, the author adds similar documentation for AOL accounts, pointing to their cross-platform `git-credential-aol` helper on GitHub. The change maintains consistency with the existing Yahoo helper documentation format.

This continues the series' practical focus on documenting real-world configuration needs for email providers. The addition appears straightforward and well-scoped, matching the quality of previous patches in the series. Readers using AOL` for patch submission may find this particularly useful given the reported unreliability of app passwords.
