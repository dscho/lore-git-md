This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing bugfix thread for Git daemon's EINTR handling during socket operations. Hridoy Ahmed weighs in on a code organization question regarding variable declarations in the patch.

The discussion centers on whether to group all variable declarations together before the `accept()` call, as Junio Hamano suggested, or keep them closer to their usage. Hridoy argues that Junio's proposed arrangement is unnecessarily restrictive and goes against the spirit of `-Wdeclaration-after-statement`, even if it doesn't violate the letter. They find the original patch's organization cleaner but agree to include the change in v3 along with other suggestions.

This is a minor stylistic debate in a thread that has otherwise largely converged on technical solutions. The core EINTR handling logic (with 3 retry attempts and preserved error fallthrough) remains unchanged from previous versions. The only outstanding technical question about SA_RESTART interaction remains unaddressed in this exchange.
