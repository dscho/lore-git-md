Meet Soni's RFC series introducing `git refs list` as a wrapper around `git for-each-ref` has progressed through four versions, with all major architectural concerns addressed. The series establishes shared core logic between the commands via a new `for_each_ref_core()` helper and consolidated documentation, while maintaining behavioral consistency. Patrick Steinhardt, as mentor, has indicated the technical implementation is nearly merge-ready, with only minor documentation and commit organization nits remaining.

In the latest feedback, Phillip Wood raises a user experience concern about inheriting what he considers confusing pattern-matching behavior from `for-each-ref`. Specifically, he points out inconsistencies where patterns like `refs/h*` fail while `refs/heads/m*` works, and suggests the new command should establish more consistent matching rules - either always requiring wildcards or doing prefix matching by default. This comes as one of the final pieces of review before potential merging, with previous rounds having settled the architectural approach of sharing implementation between the commands through the `for_each_ref_core()` helper. The core participants - Soni, Steinhardt, and Junio Hamano - now need to decide whether to address this behavioral concern in the current series or defer it as a follow-up.
