This thread documents the evolution of a new `git refs list` command, proposed as a thin wrapper around `git for-each-ref` functionality. The series, authored by GSoC participant Meet Soni under Patrick Steinhardt's mentorship, has gone through five iterations to reach its current state. The implementation establishes shared infrastructure between the two commands, including core ref listing logic, documentation, and test coverage.

The discussion recently focused on the behavior of wildcard matching, which the new command inherits from `for-each-ref`. Phillip Wood identified a subtle aspect of the matching rules where patterns like `refs/heads/m**` get converted to `refs/heads/m*`, making it impossible to predict matching behavior from the pattern alone. While this could be seen as a limitation, Junio Hamano has now confirmed that maintaining compatibility with `for-each-ref`'s existing behavior is the right approach, effectively settling this final design question.

With comprehensive test coverage in place and all architectural questions resolved, the series appears ready for final review. Key contributors include Meet Soni as the primary author, Patrick Steinhardt providing guidance, and reviewers like Eric Sunshine and Jean-Noël Avila helping refine the documentation aspects. The wildcard matching discussion, while highlighting an edge case, has served to properly document the behavior rather than change it, leaving the series in good shape for potential inclusion.
