This is a documentation patch series (cover letter) converting the `git-log` man page and related documentation to the new standardized synopsis format. The series represents a major milestone in Git's documentation standardization effort, as `git-log` is the second largest man page after `git-config`.

The v2 series makes two key improvements over v1: it reworks the grammar around plural placeholders to be more descriptive and translation-friendly, and converts inline option descriptions to more readable lists (as seen in the `--decorate` option changes). The changes are mechanical conversions to AsciiDoc format while preserving all content, with special handling needed for log format descriptions containing parentheses.

The 9 patches comprehensively cover all aspects of `git-log` documentation including rev-list options, line ranges, pretty formats, diff options, and log configuration. Junio Hamano has already reviewed and approved the series with only minor observations, particularly noting how the backtick-wrapped commit range notation now renders differently in colorized viewers.

This work continues Jean-Noël Avila's leadership in standardizing Git's documentation format, following the same proven approach used for other commands. The changes are largely non-controversial and focused on presentation rather than content, making this a low-risk but high-value improvement to Git's documentation.
