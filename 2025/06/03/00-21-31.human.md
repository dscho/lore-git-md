This is a documentation and portability follow-up to the recently resolved OpenBSD sed issue. The thread had concluded with a maintainer-approved solution that restructured config.adoc files to avoid problematic sed line continuations. 

In this email, Junio Hamano responds to Jacob Keller's suggestion about documenting the new formatting convention where related config variables must be listed on separate lines (rather than comma-separated) in the documentation. Hamano agrees this is the simplest approach but raises the question of where to document this new convention, suggesting the CodingGuidelines document as a natural place since it already contains markup guidelines.

The exchange shows the maintainer's attention to both technical correctness and documentation hygiene - wanting to properly codify the new formatting rules that emerged from the portability fix. This appears to be a minor but thoughtful wrap-up to the earlier technical solution, ensuring future contributors will understand the documentation requirements.
