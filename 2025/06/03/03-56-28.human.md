Platform compatibility fix for BSD systems regarding header handling. This is a follow-up to Brad Smith's original patch that standardized header definitions across BSD variants by removing `_XOPEN_SOURCE` to resolve `getdelim()` function warnings. 

Collin Funk had previously tested and reviewed the patch, but questioned the removal of `_NETBSD_SOURCE`, wondering if it might still be needed for older systems. In this response, Brad explains that `_NETBSD_SOURCE` was only needed to counteract `_XOPEN_SOURCE`, and since they're removing that, `_NETBSD_SOURCE` becomes unnecessary - though he notes it's harmless to keep if desired.

The patch has already received a Reviewed-by from Collin after testing on OpenBSD 7.6 and NetBSD 10.0. The only remaining question is whether to keep the `_NETBSD_SOURCE` definition for potential backward compatibility, which Brad leaves open to maintainer preference. The changes are minimal (5 insertions, 6 deletions in `compat/posix.h`) and focused specifically on BSD platform compatibility.
