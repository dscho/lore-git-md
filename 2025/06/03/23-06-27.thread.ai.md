Thread identity: Bug report and feature proposal regarding submodule behavior when `clone.defaultRemoteName` differs from "origin", reported by Jacob Keller, with Junio Hamano now participating.

Core problem: Submodule operations fail when `clone.defaultRemoteName` is configured to something other than "origin" due to `repo_get_default_remote` in `builtin/submodule--helper.c` hardcoding "origin". This affects fetching submodule commits and other remote-dependent operations, particularly in detached HEAD states.

Current status: Discussion has advanced beyond initial proposals. Junio Hamano has identified additional complexities around detached HEAD states and configuration persistence. Potential solutions now include: (1) checking for single remote first, (2) respecting `clone.defaultRemoteName` (with caveats), (3) reflog-based origin tracing for detached HEADs, (4) recording remote names during initialization, or (5) enforcing "origin" consistency by ignoring user config in submodule contexts.

Key technical details: The issue centers on `repo_get_default_remote`'s hardcoded behavior. New considerations include: reflog inspection for detached HEAD origins, persistence of remote names during `submodule init`, and whether submodules should inherit superproject remote configuration. The change would affect submodule operations including update, fetch, and initialization workflows.

Open questions: Should the solution persist remote names during initialization? Is reflog inspection reliable enough for detached HEAD resolution? Should submodules enforce "origin" consistency or respect user configuration? How to handle cases where `clone.defaultRemoteName` differs between superproject and submodule contexts?

Participant positions: Jacob Keller initially proposed solution directions. Junio Hamano has expanded the design space with deeper technical considerations, questioning whether flexibility or consistency should dominate and suggesting additional approaches including reflog inspection and initialization-time recording.

Related work: Connects to broader submodule design questions about independence versus superproject control. The hardcoded "origin" assumption appears to be a long-standing behavior that intersects with configuration flexibility efforts. The discussion may influence future submodule remote handling design.
