This is a follow-up in the ongoing investigation of Windows+Meson test hangs related to the `--skip-connectivity-check` feature for `git receive-pack`. Patrick Steinhardt reports progress on the mysterious test hang that only occurs in CI environments (both GitHub and GitLab) but not locally, even when trying to match CI conditions.

Key findings:
- The issue appears when piping `send-pack` output through `tee` to `receive-pack`
- A workaround exists by using a wrapper script instead of direct piping
- The problem is reproducible in CI but remains elusive in local testing

The proposed fix replaces the direct pipe with a wrapper script, aligning with how other tests handle similar cases. While this solves the immediate issue, the root cause of why piping fails specifically in Windows+Meson CI environments remains unclear. The patch maintains all test functionality while working around the CI-specific hang.

This continues the thread's pattern where the core functionality (the connectivity check bypass) is sound, but test infrastructure quirks require attention. The investigation has now shifted from Johannes Schindelin to Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler, with the wrapper script approach emerging as a likely solution despite the lingering mystery of why it's needed.
