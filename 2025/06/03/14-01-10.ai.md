This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v4 series addressing maintenance task execution. The patch eliminates a redundant `quiet` variable in `builtin/gc.c` that duplicated functionality already provided by the `quiet` field in `struct maintenance_run_opts`.

The modification is straightforward, replacing all `quiet` references with `opts.quiet` while maintaining identical functionality.

This patch builds on the series' broader goal of improving code quality and reducing confusion in the maintenance infrastructure. It follows the initial patch that introduced designated field initializers, continuing the systematic cleanup of the maintenance codebase.
