This thread documents Ayush Chandekar's successful effort to eliminate global variables related to sparse-checkout configuration as part of Git's ongoing `the_repository` removal project. The three-patch v4 series moves `core_apply_sparse_checkout` and `core_sparse_checkout_cone` settings into the `repo_settings` struct and localizes the `sparse_expect_files_outside_of_patterns` variable to its single usage site.

The changes follow patterns established by earlier configuration migrations, centralizing config parsing in `repo-settings.c` while maintaining the same sparse-checkout behavior. Junio Hamano provided key architectural guidance during review, helping shape the final approach that reads config values directly through repository settings rather than caching them globally. The series removes several `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` workarounds and demonstrates a clean model for future similar migrations.

With all three patches now finalized and incorporating feedback from multiple reviewers including Patrick Steinhardt and Christian Couder, the series appears ready for merging. It represents focused, incremental progress on Git's long-term modularity goals while keeping the changes mechanical and behavior-preserving. The work completes an important piece of the broader effort to eliminate repository-related globals, particularly for the sparse-checkout subsystem.
