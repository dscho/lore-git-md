Platform compatibility patch addressing header handling for OpenBSD and NetBSD systems. 

This patch modifies the `_XOPEN_SOURCE` macro handling in `compat/posix.h` to treat OpenBSD and NetBSD similarly to FreeBSD and DragonFly systems. The change removes the need to set `_XOPEN_SOURCE` to 700 for OpenBSD (which was causing build warnings about implicit function declarations for `getdelim`), instead following the simpler approach used for other BSD variants. 

The patch also cleans up the conditional logic by reorganizing the BSD-related checks to be grouped together, and removes an outdated comment about OpenBSD needing `_XOPEN_SOURCE` 600 for `S_ISLNK()`. The change appears straightforward and maintains consistency with how other BSD systems are already handled in the codebase.
