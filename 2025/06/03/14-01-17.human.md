Race condition fix for maintenance tasks. This patch from Patrick Steinhardt's v4 series addresses a locking race between maintenance tasks that modify references and reflogs. It moves the "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" tasks from background to foreground execution to prevent lockfile contention, matching the same safety approach used in `git gc`. The change is minimal (just two line changes) but important for correctness, as explained in the commit message. The "gc" task will be handled separately in a later patch. This is part of the larger effort to systematically eliminate race conditions in maintenance operations while improving code quality.
