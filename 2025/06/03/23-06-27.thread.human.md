This thread explores a submodule behavior issue where operations fail when users configure `clone.defaultRemoteName` to something other than "origin". Jacob Keller initially reported that `repo_get_default_remote()` in the submodule helper hardcodes "origin", breaking workflows with custom remote names. He proposed several solutions including respecting the configured default or adding submodule-specific configuration.

Junio Hamano has now joined the discussion, raising deeper design considerations. While acknowledging the problem, he points out complications: the current configuration isn't stored in-repo, detached HEAD states create edge cases, and there's tension between flexibility (honoring user config) and predictability (enforcing "origin" consistency). He suggests alternative approaches like tracking the remote via reflog or recording it during initialization, but also floats the idea of mandating "origin" for submodules to maintain superproject control.

The discussion has revealed that what initially appeared as a straightforward bug fix may require more substantial design work. Key unresolved questions include how to handle detached HEAD states, whether to persist remote names during initialization, and whether submodules should have configuration autonomy or follow superproject dictates. The thread remains at the design stage, with no patches yet proposed to implement any particular solution.
