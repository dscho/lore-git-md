This is a follow-up discussion about a subtle Makefile issue discovered during final review of the reftable test conversion series. Junio Hamano points out that accidentally removing the final newline in the top-level Makefile (a cosmetic change in patch 6/10) causes `make check-docs` to fail in an unexpected way. The failure occurs because the Documentation/lint-manpages.sh script relies on a Makefile parsing trick that breaks when the file lacks a trailing newline.

The email includes both a demonstration of the failure (showing how it incorrectly reports all manpages as "removed") and a proposed fix to make the lint-manpages.sh script more robust by changing the order of Makefile parsing. This isn't a critique of the reftable series itself, but rather an interesting side observation about Makefile robustness that surfaced during final review.

The thread context shows this is the final polishing stage of a well-reviewed series, with all substantive work already completed. Junio's note serves both to document this edge case and to share a useful lesson about Makefile parsing quirks. The actual reftable test conversion remains technically sound and ready for integration.
