Bugfix follow-up discussion about test script portability.

Eric Sunshine weighs in on the ongoing discussion about `sed` command syntax in the mailmap bugfix tests. While Junio Hamano had suggested using multiple `-e` flags for better readability and portability, Sunshine notes that semicolon-separated commands are already widely used in Git's test suite without reported issues, even on picky platforms like Solaris/SunOS. 

He references a specific past issue where a missing semicolon before a closing brace caused problems, but clarifies this was due to incorrect syntax rather than a fundamental portability limitation. The response maintains that both syntax styles (semicolons vs multiple `-e` flags) are technically portable, and that Git's project guidelines don't officially prefer one over the other - the choice remains subjective.

This appears to be a minor stylistic discussion wrapping up the thread, with no technical changes needed to the already-approved patch. The exchange serves to document historical context about `sed` usage in Git's codebase rather than alter the bugfix implementation.
