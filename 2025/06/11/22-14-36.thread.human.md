This thread concerns Sebastian Andrzej Siewior's effort to modernize Git's byte-order handling in bswap.h, particularly performance optimizations for big-endian systems. The first patch, fixing a critical regression in ntohl() behavior on big-endian platforms, was quickly merged into Git 2.50 after Junio Hamano corrected a minor typo in the commit message. 

The remaining five patches - introducing __BYTE_ORDER__ support, MSVC compatibility improvements, macro standardization, and __builtin_bswap optimizations - were marked "Stalled" by Junio due to lack of response to review feedback. These architectural changes would benefit musl libc users and maintain future compatibility with C23 and POSIX standards, but several open questions remain about compiler optimization impacts and implementation details. Documentation typos were also identified but not yet fixed.

Sebastian has now re-engaged with a courtesy response acknowledging the stalled status, though without yet addressing the substantive technical feedback. The series remains in limbo until he provides updates resolving the outstanding issues around optimization behavior and future standards compatibility. The core functionality is secure thanks to the merged regression fix, so there's no urgency to rush the remaining improvements.
