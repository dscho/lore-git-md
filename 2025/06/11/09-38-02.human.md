This is a follow-up discussion on a bugfix for mailmap handling in `git cat-file`. The patch, originally submitted by Siddharth Asthana with a report from Vasilii Iakliushin, addresses incorrect newline and buffer handling when processing commit/tag objects with different author and committer identities.

Christian Couder confirms his review of the patch, though notes some uncertainty about whether his GitLab review counts for the mailing list submission. He suggests "Helped-by" might be more appropriate than "Reviewed-by" in this context, but ultimately gives the patch his approval after re-reviewing it here.

The technical discussion focuses on the specific fix in `ident.c` where buffer offsets needed adjustment after mailmap-induced reallocations. Christian explains the failure case clearly: without this fix, the parser would incorrectly terminate when encountering the newline between author and committer headers. The patch appears solid with thorough test coverage, and this follow-up email serves as additional confirmation from a reviewer that the solution is correct.
