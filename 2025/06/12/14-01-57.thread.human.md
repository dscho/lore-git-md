This thread announces localization updates for Git 2.50.0, including translations for eight languages and introducing a brand new Irish (ga.po) translation. The update comes from a coordinated effort by several translators: Aindriú Mac Giolla Eoin (Irish), Alexander Shopov (Bulgarian), Arkadii Yakovets (Ukrainian), Bagas Sanjaya (Indonesian), Emir SARI (Turkish), Jean-Noël Avila (French), Ralf Thielow (German), and Teng Long (Chinese). Jiang Xin submitted and merged the changes, which total over 32,000 lines of updates against Git 2.50-rc2.

The Irish translation is the most significant addition, appearing complete at nearly 30,000 lines. Among the updated translations, Bulgarian shows the most changes with over 1,300 lines modified. These localization updates are routine maintenance ahead of the 2.50.0 release and don't require further discussion - they'll likely be merged directly into the release branch.
