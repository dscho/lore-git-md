The Git 2.50.0 localization update thread continues with Jiang Xin coordinating translation updates for the upcoming release. The initial pull request included updates to eight existing translations (Bulgarian, German, French, Indonesian, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Chinese) and introduced a completely new Irish translation, totaling 32,401 lines added across the PO files.

A follow-up version 2 of the pull request has now arrived, adding Traditional Chinese (zh_TW) translations from Yi-Jyun Pan. This completes the planned localization updates for 2.50.0, bringing the total to 10 updated PO files with 33,060 lines added. The Irish translation remains the largest single addition at 29,758 lines, while the Traditional Chinese contribution adds 1,191 lines (659 additions, 532 deletions). All changes are based on Git 2.50-rc2 and will be included in the final 2.50.0 release. The internationalization effort involves multiple translators including Aindriú Mac Giolla Eoin (Irish), Alexander Shopov (Bulgarian), and Jean-Noël Avila (French), with Jiang Xin overseeing the coordination.
