This is a status update from Junio Hamano on the bswap.h refactoring series. He indicates that only the first patch (which fixes a regression on big-endian systems) will be included in the upcoming Git 2.50 release, while the remaining patches will be deferred to the next development cycle.

The first patch reverts a change that broke ntohl() macros on big-endian machines, restoring the previous working state. While the rest of the series contains promising improvements to byte-order handling (including optimized versions and platform-specific support), Junio notes these aren't urgent since systems will continue working with the fallback implementations they've always used.

The decision reflects a conservative approach for stable releases - fixing the immediate regression while leaving architectural improvements for the next development window. The series appears well-received overall, with Junio describing the materials as "very good," suggesting the remaining patches have a good chance of being accepted after 2.50 ships.
