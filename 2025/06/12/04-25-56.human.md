Solaris build compatibility follow-up. Collin Funk responds to Eli Schwartz's concerns about requiring GNU sed (`gsed`) on Solaris systems, clarifying that while GNU sed is commonly available on Solaris machines he accesses, it's not part of the default installation. He acknowledges Junio Hamano's solution (using basic regex instead of extended regex with `-E` flag) as the preferable path, avoiding any requirement for GNU tools. The exchange reinforces the project's commitment to portable solutions that work with native Solaris utilities, with Funk noting this approach is particularly important given Solaris' non-standard `date` command behavior in the past. The thread shows consensus around maintaining compatibility with minimal external dependencies.
