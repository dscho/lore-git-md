The two patches formalize earlier experimental work that was discussed in separate threads. The implementation touches merge and pull command internals, documentation updates across three files, and adds substantial test coverage in t7600-merge.sh. The changes appear well-considered, with Junio clearly building on previous community feedback to produce a polished submission.

For users who frequently perform merges, this could provide more actionable at-a-glance information about file operations while taking up less screen space than a full diffstat.
