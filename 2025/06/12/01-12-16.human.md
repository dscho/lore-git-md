**Feature implementation for stash import/export as refs (v8)**  
This is the eighth and likely final version of a series introducing `git stash export` and `import` subcommands to move stashes between repositories via refs rather than reflogs. The implementation appears mature after extensive review, with all major technical concerns addressed across seven previous iterations.

The core functionality remains unchanged from v7: exports create commit chains under `refs/stash-export/` while imports validate and append to the local stash stack. This version makes two final adjustments based on review feedback: clarifies documentation phrasing and fixes ownership semantics in `write_commit_with_parents()` by no longer freeing caller-allocated memory. It also adds explicit handling of invalid argument combinations to `export` with corresponding tests.

The series now includes 268 lines of test coverage in `t/t3903.sh` spanning round-trip operations, edge cases, and the new argument validation. Documentation in `git-stash.adoc` has been refined for consistency with Git's standard option descriptions. Platform compatibility was verified by Ramsay Jones, and Phillip Wood has indicated only two outstanding items remain before final approval.

With all substantive feedback incorporated and positive validation from multiple reviewers (including Junio Hamano's approval of the core approach), this appears ready for merging pending resolution of the two minor remaining items. The implementation introduces reusable components like the `GET_OID_GENTLY` flag and commit helpers that may benefit other reflog operations.
