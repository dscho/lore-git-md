This is a platform compatibility patch discussing FreeBSD build configuration. Carlo Arenas responds to Brad Smith's suggestion about enabling `memmem()` on FreeBSD 6.0+, pointing out that the system's implementation was buggy and incompatible until FreeBSD 12. He suggests testing whether the system version is actually faster than Git's bundled implementation before enabling it.

Arenas agrees with dropping support for FreeBSD 4.x, noting it's too old to build current Git anyway due to missing C99 and POSIX features. The exchange shows careful consideration of backward compatibility tradeoffs versus potential performance gains, with a preference for correctness over blindly enabling system features. The discussion remains technical and focused on build system details.
