Platform compatibility follow-up in the FreeBSD build cleanup thread. This is a review response regarding the removal of legacy FreeBSD 4.x build conditionals in config.mak.uname. Collin Funk confirms the earlier removal of NO_MEMMEM (now known to be available since FreeBSD 5.2.1 per Gnulib docs) with a Reviewed-by tag, but questions the removal of the FreeBSD 4.x-specific PTHREAD_LIBS and integer type definitions. The email notes FreeBSD 4.2 dates back to 2000, implicitly questioning whether such old system support is still needed. The thread continues discussing the maintenance tradeoffs of keeping vs removing legacy platform support code, with this email serving as a) confirmation of one technical detail and b) raising a new question about the scope of legacy system support removal.
