This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Solaris build compatibility issues with sed. Brad Smith responds to Collin Funk's analysis of two sed-related build problems on Solaris. While both agree the first issue (missing newline warning) is minor, Brad focuses on the more serious second problem - Solaris sed's lack of `-E` flag support for extended regexes. He expresses uncertainty about Collin's suggestion to check for `gsed` (GNU sed), noting that GNU tools haven't always been standard on Solaris systems. The discussion remains in the diagnostic phase, with no proposed solutions yet. The core issue revolves around platform divergence in sed implementations and how to handle the recent introduction of `-E` flag usage in Git's build system (from commit e1b81f54da). This appears to be a genuine platform compatibility issue that will need maintainer attention, though the impact seems limited to Solaris builds.
