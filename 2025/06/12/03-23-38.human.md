This is a platform compatibility report about issues with Git's build system on Solaris.

Brad Smith reports two problems encountered when building Git on Solaris. The first is a warning from `sed` about a missing newline when generating version-def.h, which appears to be a cosmetic issue. The second is more serious - a build failure when generating config-list.h due to Solaris `sed` not supporting the `-E` flag that was recently introduced in commit e1b81f54da.

The email doesn't propose specific fixes but rather brings these Solaris-specific build issues to the community's attention in case anyone has suggestions. The recent nature of the second issue (introduced by a June 2025 commit) suggests this may be a regression that needs addressing for Solaris users. The tone is observational rather than demanding fixes, leaving the door open for discussion about appropriate solutions that maintain Solaris support while keeping the modern `sed` functionality.
