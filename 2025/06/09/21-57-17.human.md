This is a follow-up in the ongoing refactoring of byte-order handling macros in `compat/bswap.h`. The thread has revealed that Sebastian Siewior's earlier changes, while fixing big-endian system builds, unexpectedly broke Windows compilation. Junio Hamano has reverted the patch from the integration tree due to these failures.

Sebastian acknowledges the Windows breakage and indicates he's preparing both a revert of the problematic commit and a cleaned-up version that accounts for this new platform-specific issue. He plans to post the revised patches after further testing.

The situation highlights the delicate cross-platform nature of byte-order handling, where fixes for one architecture (s390x) can inadvertently affect another (Windows). The thread remains active as the community works to find a solution that works reliably across all supported platforms. Sebastian appears to be taking a careful approach, suggesting the next iteration will incorporate learnings from this failure.
