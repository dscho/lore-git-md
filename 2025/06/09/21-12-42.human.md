This is a follow-up discussion in the concluded thread about removing trailing whitespace from Git notes editor templates. The thread had previously settled on a focused fix in `notes.c` while documenting a more architectural alternative in `pretty.c` for potential future use.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk confirms their motivation is purely about editor aesthetics - they (and D. Ben Knoble) find highlighted trailing whitespace visually distracting in their editors. This reinforces the practical justification for the already-agreed solution without reopening technical discussion.

The email also notes that while the `pretty.c` approach could theoretically help with `git commit -v` templates too, Haugsbakk's testing shows those templates don't actually introduce trailing whitespace in the first place. This observation helps maintain the boundary between the immediate notes-specific fix and any potential future work on other editor integrations.

The exchange serves as a light confirmation that the thread's resolution remains appropriate, with no new technical points raised. It's essentially a friendly acknowledgement of shared editor preferences among some contributors, coming after all substantive discussion had concluded.
