This is a thread-closing email in the rejected `commit.signoff` configuration proposal discussion. Chris Down acknowledges Junio C Hamano's final rejection of the feature, which was based on philosophical objections to built-in signoff automation and legal implications raised by Collin Funk. The email serves as a polite conclusion to the thread, with no new technical content or arguments introduced. The discussion had previously reached consensus that the implementation (in `builtin/commit.c` with tests in `t7500-commit-template-squash-signoff.sh` and documentation in `signoff-option.adoc`) was technically sound but unacceptable on policy grounds.
