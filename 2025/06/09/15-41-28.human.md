User experience improvement patch for `imap-send`, part of a larger series (v17 9/10) that has been through extensive review. This is a straightforward quality-of-life change that makes the command's output more informative without altering any functionality. The implementation is cleanly done with parallel changes to both backends, maintaining consistency.
