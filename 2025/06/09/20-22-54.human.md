This is a user experience improvement patch in the ongoing `imap-send` feature series. It enhances the progress output during email sending to show the destination folder name, making the operation more transparent to users.

The change modifies two functions (`append_msgs_to_imap` and `curl_append_msgs_to_imap`) to include the folder name in their progress messages. Where previously users would just see "sending 1 message", they'll now see "Sending 1 message to Drafts folder..." (or whatever folder is configured). This matches similar improvements made elsewhere in the series to make IMAP operations more informative.

The patch is straightforward - just two fprintf() calls updated with the additional folder information. It's part of a larger, well-reviewed series that's now at v18, indicating this particular change has likely been through multiple rounds of review. The improvement is small but meaningful for users who want confirmation about where their messages are being stored.
