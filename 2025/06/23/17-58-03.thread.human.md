Kai Koponen reported a performance issue with `git rev-list` when filtering commits using multiple paths with the commit-graph feature enabled. Testing against the Go repository, they found that querying two paths together takes 650ms - significantly longer than the sum of individual path queries (90ms + 100ms). This nonlinear scaling persists across different result limits (-1, -10, unlimited), suggesting the commit-graph's path filtering doesn't properly optimize for early termination when multiple paths are involved.

The behavior contradicts expectations that combined path queries should take no more time than the sum of individual queries, and potentially less due to shared traversal opportunities. The report includes clear reproduction steps and timing measurements but doesn't propose a specific fix. This appears to be a genuine optimization opportunity in how commit-graph handles multi-path filtering.
