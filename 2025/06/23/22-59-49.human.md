This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack handling. It's patch 4/9 in the v6 series, which focuses on improving pack-objects behavior during repacking.

The patch moves the initialization and reachability traversal code for `--stdin-packs` from `read_packs_list_from_stdin()` to its caller, preparing the codebase to share this logic with the `--unpacked` case in future changes. The change is part of a larger effort to optimize how Git handles cruft packs and MIDX files during repacking operations.

Junio Hamano reviewed the patch and found it makes sense, though he notes some minor confusion about the forward declaration placement (which turns out to be just a moved declaration rather than anything problematic). The change appears clean and well-justified as part of the series' progression.

This is a technical plumbing change that enables the more user-visible improvements coming later in the series, particularly the new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` configuration option in patch 9/9. The series has received thorough review from multiple maintainers and appears ready for merging.
