Performance optimization for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking (v6 cover letter)

This is the sixth version of Taylor Blau's series to optimize how cruft packs interact with multi-pack indexes (MIDX) during repacking. The series is now complete with all known issues addressed, including a recently discovered edge case with incremental MIDX writing.

The core improvement is a new `repack.midxMustContainCruft` config (default true) that allows excluding cruft packs from the MIDX when safe, reducing bloat while maintaining reachability guarantees. The series includes preparatory refactoring of pack-objects internals and introduces a new `--stdin-packs=follow` mode for handling reachable objects from unlisted packs.

Version 6 makes two minor fixes from v5: adjusting a line split in `show_object_pack_hint()` per Junio's style suggestion, and properly handling multi-layered MIDXs when gathering pack names (fixing a test failure Jeff King found). The series has received positive reviews from Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren, with production testing showing 5-20% speed improvements.

The 9-patch series progresses logically from refactoring through to the final config option, with comprehensive tests in t7704-repack-cruft.sh. This appears ready for merging after the final style tweaks, representing a meaningful optimization for repositories using both MIDX and cruft packs.
