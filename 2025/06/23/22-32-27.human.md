This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction. It's part 7 of 9 in the v6 series.

The patch simply reorders two functions in `builtin/pack-objects.c` - moving `show_commit_pack_hint()` to appear after `show_object_pack_hint()`. This is a preparatory change requested by Elijah Newren to minimize code movement in a future patch where `show_commit_pack_hint()` will need to call `show_object_pack_hint()`.

The change is straightforward and mechanical - it doesn't modify any behavior, just the function ordering. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and notes this was suggested during review. This appears to be a minor but sensible cleanup as part of the larger series that has already received significant review and approval.
