Performance optimization for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking (v5, part 9/9). This email addresses a newly discovered bug in the final patch of Taylor Blau's series that allows Git to exclude cruft packs from MIDX files when possible.

Jeff King found that under specific test conditions (with both GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX and GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL set), the cruft pack was incorrectly included in the MIDX when it should have been excluded. Taylor confirms this is a real bug caused by the current implementation not properly handling multi-layered/incremental MIDX files. The fix involves modifying the pack gathering logic to traverse through all MIDX layers (including base MIDXs) when determining which packs are already included.

Taylor provides a proposed fix that walks through the MIDX chain to collect all pack names, which resolves the issue. He notes this could be slightly simplified with another pending patch series ('tb/prepare-midx-pack-cleanup') but kept them separate for now. The email indicates Taylor will send a new version of the patch series incorporating this fix along with addressing a previously noted style issue from Junio Hamano.

This appears to be the final issue blocking what has otherwise been a well-reviewed series that received approvals from both Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren. The bug fix maintains the series' goal of optimizing repack performance by safely excluding cruft packs from MIDX files when they're not needed for reachability.
