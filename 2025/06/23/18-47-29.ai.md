This is a bugfix for the final patch in Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for MIDX and cruft pack interaction during repacking. The patch addresses a newly discovered issue where cruft packs could incorrectly appear in the MIDX when using incremental MIDX writing under specific test conditions (GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX=1 and GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL=1).

The root-causes the problem: the current implementation fails to account for multi-layered/incremental MIDXs when gathering the set of packs already in the MIDX. This causes it to miss cruft packs in non-tip layers, leading to incorrect exclusion decisions. Taylor provides a fix that properly traverses all MIDX layers (including base_midx chains) when collecting pack names.

The proposed solution involves:
1. Allocating space for packs from both current and base MIDX layers
2. Iterating through all MIDX layers (following base_midx pointers)
3. Collecting pack names from each layer

The fix maintains the series' core optimization goal of excluding cruft packs from MIDX where possible while ensuring correctness. Taylor notes this is independent of another pending cleanup (tb/prepare-midx-pack-cleanup) and plans to incorporate this fix along with addressing Junio's earlier style nit in the next version.

This appears to be the final issue blocking the series, which has otherwise received positive reviews and production validation showing 5-20% speed improvements. The fix demonstrates careful consideration of MIDX's layered structure and maintains the series' careful handling of edge cases around pack reachability.
