Performance issue with `git rev-list --changed-paths` path filtering in commit-graph. Kai Koponen reports that querying multiple paths scales worse than linearly - a two-path query takes 650ms vs 90ms+100ms for individual queries in the Go repository, despite early termination opportunities with `-n` limit. The problem persists across different count limits (-1, -10, unlimited), suggesting inefficient handling of multiple-path filtering in commit-graph's early termination logic. Reproduction steps and timings provided but no solution proposed.
