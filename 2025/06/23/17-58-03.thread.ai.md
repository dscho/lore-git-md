Performance issue with `git rev-list --changed-paths` path filtering in commit-graph. Kai Koponen reports and analyzes a performance anomaly where querying multiple paths (`src/clean.bash` and `src/Make.dist`) takes 650ms in the Go repository, while individual queries sum to only 190ms (90ms + 100ms), despite count limits (`-10`) that should enable early termination. The issue persists across Git 2.50.0 (built with GCC 14.2, glibc 2.41, OpenSSL 3.4.1 on x86_64) and different count limits (-1, -10, unlimited), suggesting fundamental inefficiency in the commit-graph's multi-path filtering implementation. The expected behavior would be for combined queries to take no longer than the sum of individual queries, with potential for early termination savings. Reproduction steps confirm the algorithmic nature of the problem, likely in how path filters intersect during traversal. No solution proposed yet, but the detailed timings and environment specifics provide a clear benchmark for optimization work.
