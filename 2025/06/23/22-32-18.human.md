Performance optimization patch in a series improving MIDX and cruft pack handling. This is patch 4/9 in Taylor Blau's v6 series, which has already received significant review and approval from key maintainers.

The patch moves the initialization and execution of a reachability traversal (used for name hash generation during delta selection) out of `read_packs_list_from_stdin()` and into its caller `read_stdin_packs()`. This refactoring prepares for future changes where the same traversal logic will be reused for handling loose objects with `--unpacked`.

The change is part of a larger effort to optimize pack maintenance operations, particularly around cruft pack inclusion in MIDX files during repacking. The series has been validated in production at GitHub with reported speed improvements of 5-20%. With approvals from Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren, and all known edge cases addressed, this appears to be a well-vetted technical improvement to Git's object packing internals.
