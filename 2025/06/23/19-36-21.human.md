This is a performance discussion about `git rev-list`'s path filtering behavior with commit-graph bloom filters. Junio Hamano confirms Kai Koponen's findings that multiple-path queries are significantly slower than single-path ones, explaining this is expected behavior due to bloom filter limitations.

The key insight is that commit-graph bloom filters are intentionally disabled for multiple pathspecs (or any complex pathspecs) as revealed by Git's internal documentation. This explains why combining two paths (`src/clean.bash` and `src/Make.dist`) performs worse than their individual queries - the optimization simply isn't applied to multi-path cases.

While the performance characteristic is working as designed, the discussion highlights an area where Git's path filtering could potentially be improved, especially given that users might reasonably expect combined path queries to be at least as efficient as separate ones. The thread doesn't propose solutions but establishes clear technical context for anyone considering optimizations in this area.
