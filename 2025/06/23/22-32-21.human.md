Performance optimization in pack-objects for better delta compression of loose objects. This is part of Taylor Blau's 9-part series improving MIDX and cruft pack interactions during repacking.

The patch fixes an oversight where loose objects added via `--unpacked` weren't being considered as reachability tips for name-hash traversal. While this didn't affect correctness, it meant Git wasn't finding optimal deltas for these objects. The change passes the `rev_info` struct to the loose object handling code, allowing these objects to participate in delta selection.

This builds on earlier refactoring in the series (particularly patch 4/9) that moved the `rev_info` declaration to make this sharing possible. The implementation is clean and focused - just 12 lines added and 8 removed, with the main change being passing the `rev_info` through to the loose object callback.

Junio Hamano had previously praised this optimization in earlier rounds, and the patch appears ready for inclusion. It's a clear win for repositories with many loose objects, though the impact will vary based on object size distributions. The series as a whole has been thoroughly reviewed and is in final form.
