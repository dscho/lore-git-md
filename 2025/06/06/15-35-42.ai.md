This is the final patch in a 4-part series addressing platform-specific compiler warnings from cURL 8.14.0's stricter type checking in `curl_easy_setopt()` calls. The patch focuses on converting remaining `int` constants to `long` in `http-push.c`, `http.c`, and `remote-curl.c` that were missed in earlier fixes, particularly affecting macOS builds with different compiler settings.

The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions of numeric literals (like `->` 1L) and boolean flags (0 `->` 0L) passed to cURL options. Junio Hamano notes that while the patch matches what was previously identified as remaining issues, the grep command used to verify completeness might have missed some cases involving symbolic constants that still need `(long)` casts. However, since this matches the known remaining cases and all 23 `curl_easy_setopt()` calls were manually verified, the series is considered complete.

The patch series as a whole demonstrates how cross-platform development surfaces type safety issues differently across build environments, with this final part addressing macOS-specific cases that weren't caught by the Debian-focused initial fixes. Junio indicates he will incorporate this into the `next` branch after verifying no other urgent changes are pending.
