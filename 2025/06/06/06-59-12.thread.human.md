While not yet committing to the change, his tone suggests he's likely to accept the refactoring.

The discussion has evolved from a narrow technical debate about compiler warnings to a broader conversation about code organization principles and the learning process for newer contributors. With the maintainer's strong stance and the author's growing alignment, the thread appears headed toward adopting Junio's proposed helper function approach in the next version of the patch series.
