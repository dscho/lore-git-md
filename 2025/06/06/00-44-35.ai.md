This is a brief update on the ongoing `git stash` import/export feature development, part of the "bc/stash-export-import" topic mentioned in Junio Hamano's status update. Brian Carlson confirms there will be a v8 revision of their patch series, which introduces new `git stash` subcommands for importing and exporting stash entries via refs. The series includes preparatory refactoring (moving revision parsing into a dedicated function) and changes to `get_oid` behavior. The work appears to be in its final stages, with the expectation that v8 will be the last iteration before potential inclusion. This continues the stash-related work that was noted as being in the 'seen' branch in Junio's development pipeline update.
