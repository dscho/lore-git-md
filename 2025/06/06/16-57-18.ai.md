This is a refactoring patch addressing byte-order handling in `compat/bswap.h`. The change moves the redefinition of `ntohl`/`htonl` macros to occur only after determining the system's endianness, fixing an issue where these macros were being incorrectly redefined on big-endian systems after commit 6547d1c9cb.

The patch reorganizes the macro definitions to ensure the `ntohl`/`htonl` family of macros only get replaced with byte-swapping operations on little-endian systems where `bswap` functions are available. On big-endian systems, they remain as no-ops. The change maintains the same behavior but with more precise conditional logic.

The implementation moves 21 lines of macro definitions and adds 16 lines in a different location, with the net effect being a more correct handling of byte order conversions across architectures. The author reports successful builds and test suite runs on both s390x (big-endian) and x86-64 (little-endian) platforms, suggesting the change works as intended across different CPU architectures.
