This is a platform compatibility patch focusing on byte-swapping macros in `compat/bswap.h`. Sebastian Andrzej Siewior noticed that after commit 6547d1c9cb ("bswap.h: add support for built-in bswap functions"), the macros for network-to-host and host-to-network byte order conversion (`ntohl*`/`htonl*`) were being incorrectly replaced with byte-swapping operations even on big-endian machines where they should remain no-ops.

The patch reorganizes the macro definitions to ensure these conversions only happen on little-endian architectures where byte-swapping is actually needed. The change is mechanical but important for correctness - it moves the `ntohl*`/`htonl*` macro redefinitions to occur only when we've determined we're on a little-endian system. The author has tested this on both s390x (big-endian) and x86-64 (little-endian) systems with the test suite passing.

This appears to be a straightforward fix for a subtle regression introduced by the earlier bswap optimization work. The patch is small (16 lines added, 21 removed) and focused, with a clear explanation of both the problem and solution.
