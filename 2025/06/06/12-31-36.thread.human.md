This thread documents a minor but noticeable formatting fix for the git-diff manpage. The issue involves spurious backticks around ".." in a "mode" example line that were causing incorrect rendering in Asciidoctor-generated documentation. The problem originated in commit 0b080a70ab, which inconsistently applied backticks to this line while leaving a similar "index" example unmarked. 

The patch cleanly removes the problematic backticks to match the surrounding style, resolving the rendering artifact without changing the semantic content. While not critical enough to warrant backporting to the stable 2.50 release, the fix improves documentation consistency and corrects a toolchain-specific formatting issue that was caught during routine doc-diff comparisons between versions.
