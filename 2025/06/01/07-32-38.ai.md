This is a withdrawn documentation cleanup patch in the `imap-send` feature/bugfix series. Aditya Garg proposed fixing spelling and grammar in error messages, but Eric Sunshine pointed out the changes conflicted with Git's coding guidelines for error messages (no periods in single-sentence errors, no capitalization of first words unless they would be capitalized mid-sentence). The patch attempted to add periods and full capitalization to messages like "cannot get certificate subject" and added more specific context to the CRAM-MD5 error message.

While the CRAM-MD5 message improvement was valid, the bulk of the changes went against project conventions, leading to the patch being withdrawn. This was part of the final polishing phase of the larger `imap-send` series that added OAuth2.0 support and folder management. The thread shows careful attention to maintaining consistency with Git's established style, even in error message formatting. The withdrawn patch doesn't affect the core functionality of the series, which remains technically complete in v10.
