This is a user experience improvement in the ongoing `imap-send` feature and bugfix series (v10). The patch enhances the progress output during message sending to include the destination mailbox name, making it clearer where messages are being delivered. Previously, the output only showed the count of messages being sent (e.g., "Sending 1 message"), but now it includes the folder name (e.g., "Sending 1 message to Drafts folder...").

The change is straightforward, modifying two similar progress messages in both the OpenSSL and libcurl backend implementations. This appears to be a well-contained, non-controversial quality-of-life improvement that builds on the series' broader goal of making `imap-send` more user-friendly and informative. The patch follows the series' pattern of standardizing and improving output messages, and since it's part of the v10 iteration, it has presumably already undergone thorough review.
