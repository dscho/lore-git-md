This is a withdrawn documentation patch in the `imap-send` feature/bugfix series. Aditya Garg proposed fixing spelling and grammar in `imap-send` error messages, but Eric Sunshine pointed out the changes conflicted with Git's coding guidelines for error messages (no periods in single-sentence errors, no capitalization of first words unless they're proper nouns). While the patch added useful context in some cases (like clarifying a CRAM-MD5 error), the bulk of its changes went against project conventions. The author acknowledged the feedback, and this patch won't be part of the final series. The thread shows the project's careful attention to consistency in error message formatting, even as it expands `imap-send`'s functionality.
