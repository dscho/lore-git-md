This thread introduces a 2-patch series from a GSoC participant that continues the ongoing effort to remove global variables from Git's codebase, specifically targeting the preload-index subsystem. Together these changes touch config.c, environment.c/h, and preload-index.c while reducing the codebase by 11 lines.

This appears to be a well-scoped contribution that makes incremental progress on the larger `the_repository` removal initiative.
