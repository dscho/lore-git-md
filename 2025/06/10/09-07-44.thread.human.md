The thread continues to debate where batch branch operations should live - in the user-friendly `git branch` porcelain or the lower-level `update-ref` plumbing. Junio Hamano, while maintaining his preference for the plumbing approach, has laid out specific conditions under which he'd consider a porcelain solution: the interface must completely separate batch operations from force behavior, and there needs to be clear community support for this direction.

Andrea Stacchiotti, who originally proposed the porcelain approach, has acknowledged Junio's requirements but hasn't yet presented a redesigned interface. The discussion is now at a standstill, waiting for either: (1) proponents to rally community support and propose a porcelain design that meets Junio's criteria, or (2) the conversation to shift toward implementing this functionality through `update-ref` instead. The outcome will set an important precedent for how similar batch operations are handled in Git's architecture going forward.
