The latest review from Karthik shifts focus from implementation details to higher-level design questions. This comes after several rounds of technical review that refined the implementation's enum-based field categorization, memory management, and output formats (JSON/plaintext).

Key open questions center on architectural boundaries - what belongs in `repo-info` versus other commands - and finalizing the plaintext format's safety with pathnames. The thread shows steady progress, with core technical decisions solidified and now moving toward clarifying the command's conceptual foundation before final polish.
