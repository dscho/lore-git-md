The change maintains the series' direct-printing approach rather than buffering, and includes thorough tests verifying both formats work correctly with all implemented fields.

Mentors Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak continue to guide the implementation, which has maintained consistent architectural patterns while expanding functionality. Some open questions include whether to add `-z` as a shortcut for the null format, and the final command namespace (currently `git repo` but alternatives remain under discussion).
