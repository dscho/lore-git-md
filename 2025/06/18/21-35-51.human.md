This is a final review response for a documentation patch series converting the `git-log` man page to the new synopsis format style. Junio Hamano, Git's maintainer, gives his approval to the 9-part series from Jean-Noël Avila that standardizes Git's second-largest man page and its related documentation files.

The review notes that all changes appear reasonable, with only one minor question about whether a particular change in patch 5/9 (adding % to literals) properly belongs in its stated patch context. This doesn't appear to be a blocking concern, just an observation about patch organization. The series has already incorporated Junio's earlier feedback about improving the `--decorate` option formatting.

The review comes with an implicit approval - the ";-)" and general positivity suggest this series is ready to be merged as part of Git's ongoing documentation standardization effort. The work continues Jean-Noël's track record of high-quality documentation conversions, now covering one of Git's most complex and frequently referenced man pages.
