This is a performance optimization patch in an ongoing thread about improving `git fetch --prune` efficiency. Jacob Keller provides a review of Phil Hord's patch that reduces the operation from O(N^2) to O(N*logN) time complexity.

Keller confirms the patch is reasonable, noting the dramatic speed improvement (410s to 4s in the test case) justifies the minor change in output ordering of dangling ref messages. He adds his Reviewed-by tag, indicating formal approval. The email clarifies one detail about the timing claims (noting a discrepancy between "under 4 seconds" here versus "under a second" in the cover letter) but doesn't see it as problematic.

The review suggests the current optimization level (logarithmic) is sufficient for practical purposes, though leaves open the possibility of future O(N) improvements if someone wants to pursue them.
