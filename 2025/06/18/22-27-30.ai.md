Reftable configuration clarification. Brian M. Carlson confirms that `reftable.blockSize` is indeed configurable despite not being prominently documented, addressing Haylin Moore's observation about 4KB writes during network clones. The response notes that JGit uses 64KB blocks by default, suggesting larger sizes may be beneficial for write-heavy operations. However, it cautions about potential read performance tradeoffs since larger blocks mean reading unnecessary ref data when accessing single references. The recommendation to use power-of-two sizes provides practical guidance for tuning. This moves the thread from investigation to actionable configuration, though the performance implications suggest this may warrant further discussion about optimal defaults for different workloads.
