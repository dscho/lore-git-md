This is a documentation patch series completion notice from Junio Hamano, serving as the final review/acknowledgment for Jean-Noël Avila's 9-part conversion of the `git-log` man page and related documentation to the new synopsis format style.

Junio gives the series his approval with only one minor observation - he questions whether the "% to literal" change in patch 5/9 properly belongs in that particular patch (though he doesn't dispute its correctness). His comment "everything looked pretty reasonable" and the smiley face reaction to the size of the changes suggest overall satisfaction with the work.

The series represents the completion of converting Git's second-largest man page (`git-log`) to the standardized AsciiDoc format, following Junio's earlier feedback about improving the `--decorate` option formatting. This wraps up a significant documentation modernization effort that maintains all technical content while improving consistency across Git's documentation.

Key technical aspects preserved through the conversion include proper handling of log format descriptions with escaped parentheses and %-prefixed placeholders, backtick-wrapped commands/options, and standardized description lists for config options. The only remaining loose end is the minor grammatical ambiguity around `--decorate-refs` phrasing that Junio noted earlier, which may inform future style guide refinements but doesn't affect the current series.
