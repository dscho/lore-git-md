Phil Hord's RFC series optimizing `git fetch --prune` performance has gained significant traction, with the O(N*logN) approach receiving positive feedback from key reviewers. The series restructures the pruning logic to avoid O(N^2) behavior, showing dramatic improvements (410s → 4s in test cases with 28k refs). Jacob Keller has given a Reviewed-by for the first patch, Jeff King provided detailed technical validation, and Junio C Hamano reacted positively to the cleanup patch.

A new suggestion from Lidong Yan proposes moving the sorting operation deeper into `refs_warn_dangling_symref()` and revisiting the `strset` alternative. However, this appears unlikely to change the core approach since Jeff King had previously considered and dismissed `strset` due to interface constraints, and the current implementation already has maintainer approval. The discussion now centers on minor implementation refinements rather than fundamental changes, suggesting the series is likely ready for merging in its current form.
