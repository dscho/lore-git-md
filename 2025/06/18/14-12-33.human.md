This is a platform compatibility fix for the `diff --no-index` command. The patch addresses a build regression on AIX and NonStop systems caused by commit 09fb155f11, which introduced direct use of `e->d_type` without considering platforms where `d_type` isn't part of `struct dirent`.

Marc Branchaud provides a minor review of Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's patch, suggesting two typo fixes in the commit message (changing "will fail" to "git fails" and "nake" to "naked"). The core fix itself is straightforward - replacing the direct `e->d_type` access with the portable `DTYPE(e)` macro that properly abstracts platform differences in dirent handling.

The patch has already been tested and reviewed by Collin Funk on AIX 7.3, confirming it resolves the build failure.
