This is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort (patch 2/17 in v4 of the series). Patrick Steinhardt proposes renaming the `object_directory` struct to `odb_source` to better reflect its future role in Git's pluggable object database architecture.

The change is a straightforward rename with no functional changes, touching 28 files across the codebase. The commit message makes a strong case that "object directory" is becoming a misnomer as Git moves toward supporting alternate storage backends beyond filesystem directories. The new name `odb_source` was chosen over alternatives like `odb_backend` and `odb_alternate` after discussion on the mailing list.

The patch appears mechanically sound, with a thorough commit message explaining the rationale. It's part of a larger architectural shift, so reviewers will want to consider how this naming fits with the series' overall direction.
