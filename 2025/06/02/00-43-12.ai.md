Feature polish for `imap-send` progress reporting, part of the larger IMAP series (v11 8/9). This patch enhances the progress output during message sending to include the destination folder name, making it clearer where messages are being delivered. The change is straightforward - modifying two fprintf() calls in both the OpenSSL and libcurl backend implementations to append the folder name from server->folder to the existing "Sending X message(s)" output.

The improvement is small but meaningful for users who may be sending to different folders and want verification that their configuration is being honored. The patch maintains consistency with the series' established style for progress reporting and error messages. As this is part of the final v11 iteration of the IMAP series, it represents a last-round polish rather than introducing new functionality - the folder name was already available in the context, just not displayed. Junio Hamano's simple "Nice ;-)" response suggests this uncontroversial change is likely to be accepted as part of the larger series.
