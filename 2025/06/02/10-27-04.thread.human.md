This thread is part of the ongoing effort to remove the `the_repository` global variable from Git's codebase. The current patch (v4 3/17) performs a mechanical but wide-ranging rename of "object-store.{c,h}" to "odb.{c,h}" to better match the renamed structures they contain (`struct object_database` and `struct odb_backend`). The change updates 126 files to use the new header name, including build system configurations.

The patch follows logically from earlier structural renames in the series and appears uncontroversial, though such large-scale renames always carry some risk of missing edge cases. The commit message clearly explains the motivation as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort. This is one of many incremental steps in René Scharfe's long-running project to eliminate implicit global state from Git's codebase.
