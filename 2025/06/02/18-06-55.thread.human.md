This thread began with Brad Smith's patch to fix `getdelim()` declaration warnings on BSD systems by removing `_XOPEN_SOURCE` from `compat/posix.h`. Collin Funk tested and confirmed the fix works on OpenBSD 7.6 and NetBSD 10.0, providing a Reviewed-by tag. The discussion then turned to whether to keep the redundant `_NETBSD_SOURCE` macro - while technically unnecessary now, Collin argued it provides backward compatibility and future-proofing for when NetBSD might implement POSIX features as extensions first, similar to glibc's `_GNU_SOURCE` handling.

Junio Hamano has now queued the patch, accepting both the removal of `_XOPEN_SOURCE` (which resolves the immediate warnings) and retaining `_NETBSD_SOURCE` as Collin recommended. The changes involve 5 insertions and 5 deletions in `compat/posix.h`, specifically excluding OpenBSD and NetBSD from the `_XOPEN_SOURCE 600` definition while keeping them grouped with other BSD variants. The patch has all necessary approvals (Collin's Reviewed-by and Junio's sign-off) and is ready for integration. What started as a straightforward warning fix evolved into a considered discussion about BSD compatibility strategies, with the final solution balancing immediate needs with long-term maintainability.
