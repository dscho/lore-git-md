This thread discusses a platform compatibility patch addressing header handling for BSD systems, originally proposed by Brad Smith. The patch standardizes header definitions across BSD variants (OpenBSD, NetBSD, FreeBSD, DragonFly) by removing `_XOPEN_SOURCE` to fix implicit function declaration warnings for `getdelim()` in `compat/posix.h`.

Collin Funk has tested the patch on OpenBSD 7.6 and NetBSD 10.0, confirming it works as intended and providing a Reviewed-by tag. The main outstanding question is whether the removal of `_NETBSD_SOURCE` definition - which was part of the original change - should be reconsidered. Collin notes this definition might be useful for older systems and appears harmless, suggesting it could potentially be kept for backward compatibility.

The changes are relatively small (5 insertions, 6 deletions in `compat/posix.h`) but address an important cross-platform consistency issue. With Collin's positive review and testing confirmation, the patch appears ready for merging pending resolution of the `_NETBSD_SOURCE` question - either keeping it as Collin suggests or documenting why it can safely be removed.
