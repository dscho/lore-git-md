This thread centers on platform compatibility fixes for BSD systems, specifically addressing header handling in `compat/posix.h`. Brad Smith's original patch standardized header definitions across BSD variants (OpenBSD, NetBSD, FreeBSD, DragonFly) by removing `_XOPEN_SOURCE` to resolve implicit function declaration warnings for `getdelim()`. Collin Funk tested and confirmed the patch works on OpenBSD 7.6 and NetBSD 10.0, providing a Reviewed-by tag.

The main remaining question is whether to retain the `_NETBSD_SOURCE` definition that was removed in the original patch. Collin suggested it might be useful for older systems and appears harmless, while Junio Hamano has acknowledged the question as worth considering but hasn't yet taken a position. The changes are relatively small (5 insertions, 6 deletions) and focused on BSD platform compatibility macros in `compat/posix.h`. With Collin's Reviewed-by and successful testing, the patch appears ready for integration pending resolution of this final question about `_NETBSD_SOURCE` handling.
