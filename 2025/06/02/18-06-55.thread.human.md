This thread addresses BSD platform compatibility issues in `compat/posix.h`, specifically around header definitions for `getdelim()`. Brad Smith's patch removed `_XOPEN_SOURCE` to resolve implicit function declaration warnings across BSD variants (OpenBSD, NetBSD, FreeBSD, DragonFly), which Collin Funk tested and confirmed working on OpenBSD 7.6 and NetBSD 10.0, providing a Reviewed-by.

The discussion has evolved to consider whether to retain the now-redundant `_NETBSD_SOURCE` macro. Brad initially suggested it could be removed, but Collin makes a case for keeping it—citing backward compatibility for older systems and future-proofing for potential POSIX features that might first appear as NetBSD extensions. He draws parallels to how glibc handles `_GNU_SOURCE` with newer POSIX standards.

With the immediate build warnings resolved and Collin's Reviewed-by in place, the patch is technically ready. The remaining open question is whether Junio will accept Brad's original minimal approach or retain `_NETBSD_SOURCE` based on Collin's broader compatibility arguments. The changes are confined to `compat/posix.h` (5 insertions, 6 deletions) and have been validated on multiple BSD platforms.
