This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove `the_repository` global from Git's object database (ODB) subsystem, now at version 4. The patch converts `assert_oid_type()` to take an explicit ODB parameter and renames it to `odb_assert_oid_type()` as part of the standardized `odb_` prefix convention established earlier in the series.

The change is mechanical, updating three call sites in `commit-tree.c` and `commit.c` to pass `the_repository->objects` explicitly. The implementation now uses the repository pointer stored in the ODB struct rather than accessing `the_repository` directly. This continues the systematic conversion pattern seen in previous patches, with no functional changes to the type assertion behavior itself.

The patch fits cleanly into the series' established approach and naming conventions, receiving positive reviews on earlier versions. It's one of the final steps in removing `the_repository` dependencies from the ODB subsystem, paving the way for future pluggable backend work.
