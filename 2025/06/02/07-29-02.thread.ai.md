- Updates comment about OpenBSD's S_ISLNK() requirement which is now handled differently

Current status: Initial version of the change proposed, awaiting review. The approach suggests not setting `_XOPEN_SOURCE` for BSD variants is preferable to setting it to 700 (OpenBSD's requirement).
