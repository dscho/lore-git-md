The change is mechanical but wide-ranging, updating 126 files to use the new header name. This is a clear improvement in naming consistency following the structural changes made earlier in the series.

The patch follows the established pattern in the series of separating mechanical renames from substantive changes, making it easier to review. No behavior changes are introduced - this is purely a codebase-wide rename to align filenames with their current contents.
