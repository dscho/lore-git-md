Part of Patrick Steinhardt's v3 series addressing race conditions in Git's maintenance tasks, specifically focusing on lockfile contention between reference and reflog operations.

This patch moves the "pack-refs" and "reflog-expire" maintenance tasks from background to foreground execution to prevent race conditions around their lockfiles (`packed-refs.lock` and reflog lockfiles). The change mirrors git-gc(1)'s existing behavior of completing these operations before detaching. The commit explains that while the "gc" task has a similar issue, its fix requires more work and will be addressed separately.

The implementation is straightforward - it modifies the `maintenance_task` struct in `builtin/gc.c` to assign the task functions to the `foreground` rather than `background` function pointers for these two tasks. This builds on the foreground/background phase split infrastructure introduced earlier in the series (patch 8/12).

Key technical points:
- Changes affect `TASK_PACK_REFS` and `TASK_REFLOG_EXPIRE` entries in the `tasks[]` array
- Maintains same task functions (`maintenance_task_pack_refs` and `maintenance_task_reflog_expire`) but moves them to foreground phase
- Keeps existing auto-condition checks (`pack_refs_condition` and `reflog_expire_condition`)
- Follows the pattern established in git-gc to avoid lockfile races
- Part of the series' second phase applying the new infrastructure to actual race conditions
- Prepares for future patch addressing similar issues in the "gc" task
- Maintains backward compatibility while fixing the execution timing

The change is minimal (4 lines changed) but strategically important for the series' goal of eliminating maintenance task race conditions. It demonstrates the value of the foreground/background split architecture by cleanly solving a real concurrency issue.
