This thread has followed the development of a 3-patch series enhancing `git cat-file`'s batch mode with new inspection capabilities, now approved for merging to 'next'. The series introduces `%(objectmode)` and `%(rest)` format specifiers, improves test coverage, and revises submodule handling - receiving final approval from maintainer Junio Hamano and reviewers Jeff King and Victoria Dye.

In the latest discussion, Jeff King explores some finer points of the submodule output format and potential optimizations. More interestingly, he identifies an optimization where `--batch-check` could skip existence checks for submodules when only basic info is needed, since these objects typically aren't present locally. He provides a proof-of-concept patch demonstrating this with a new `skip_submodule_info` flag, though this isn't blocking the current series.

The core functionality remains approved as-is, with these suggestions representing possible future improvements. The thread shows the project's attention to detail even after consensus is reached, with experienced contributors like Jeff continuing to think through edge cases and optimization opportunities.
