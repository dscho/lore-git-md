Bugfix follow-up for Windows test hang in `receive-pack` connectivity check series.

Justin Tobler responds to a report from Johannes Schindelin about a test hang in the Windows+Meson CI environment. The issue affects only the newly added test in `t5410-receive-pack.sh` that verifies the `--skip-connectivity-check` functionality, which was recently approved as part of a 2-patch series for GitLab's Gitaly service.

The test passes in other Windows environments (like "win+VS test" jobs) but hangs specifically in GitHub's "win+Meson" configuration. Justin acknowledges the report and indicates he'll investigate, though he doesn't yet have an immediate explanation for the platform-specific behavior.
