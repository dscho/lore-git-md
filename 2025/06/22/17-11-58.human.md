This is a follow-up in the git-gui localization thread, focusing on the ongoing discussion about po-file format improvements rather than the already-accepted Bulgarian translation update.

Alexander Shopov responds to Johannes Sixt's concerns about maintaining readable diffs in translation files. He explains that gettext's sorting options (-s/--sort-output) are deprecated and that the default file-based sorting (-F) still doesn't prevent string movement when lines are added. He notes that line numbers in location comments exacerbate the diff noise.

Shopov interprets Sixt's position as being open to submissions without location comments (which aren't used at runtime and can be regenerated) and signals his intent to voluntarily remove them in future git-gui/gitk translation submissions. This would make diffs cleaner and reduce repository growth, though the broader infrastructure changes to enforce this workflow remain an open discussion point.

The email shows alignment between the two contributors on moving toward location-free submissions, though the technical implementation path for the build system changes needed to be discussed separately. The tone is cooperative, with Shopov proactively offering to adopt the cleaner format in his future work.
