**Build system improvement for unreachable code detection**

Jeff King follows up on the discussion about enabling `-Wunreachable-code` warnings in developer builds. The thread had previously settled on testing the warning in a limited CI job (linux-sha256) to evaluate its usefulness. Jeff now proposes a different approach: enabling the warning by default in `config.mak.dev` but disabling it specifically on macOS, where it produces false positives due to platform-specific behavior of `sigfillset()`.

The patch adds a platform check to skip the warning on Darwin systems, while keeping it active elsewhere. Jeff notes this is more proactive than the limited CI approach, allowing broader testing while avoiding known macOS issue. He acknowledges this might surface more false positives but considers it worthwhile for exploration. The change builds on Junio Hamano's earlier approval of warning evaluation, now taking a more expansive (but still platform-aware) approach to gathering data about the warning's utility.
