Performance enhancement for incremental MIDX bitmaps, part of Taylor Blau's ongoing series (v4 06/13). This patch improves pack-reuse behavior when working with layered bitmaps by:

1. Extending pack enumeration to consider all packs across MIDX layers (not just the current one) when doing multi-pack reuse, ensuring we don't miss reuse opportunities in older layers.

2. Modifying single-pack reuse to prefer the oldest layer's preferred pack, which likely contains more reusable sections due to Git's object accumulation patterns. The change walks back through the MIDX chain to find the base layer before selecting its preferred pack.

The changes are focused on the `reuse_partial_packfile_from_bitmap()` function, with careful handling of the MIDX chain structure established in earlier patches. This continues the series' theme of making bitmap operations work efficiently across MIDX layers while maintaining correctness. The implementation appears straightforward and builds logically on the infrastructure from previous patches, though some may want to verify the performance impact of always walking to the oldest layer for single-pack reuse.
