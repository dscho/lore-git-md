Maintainer discussion on assertion practices in Git's codebase.

This email continues the ongoing debate between Elijah Newren and Junio Hamano about assertion usage patterns in Git. While the thread began with a specific merge-ort bugfix, it has evolved into a broader discussion about error handling conventions.

Elijah clarifies his position on `if (condition) BUG(something)` patterns, agreeing they can be useful in some cases but arguing they shouldn't be mandatory for all situations. He maintains that forcing descriptive messages in every case can be awkward or redundant. The discussion then shifts to technical solutions for preventing assertions with side effects, with Elijah proposing a static analysis approach (referencing a separate patch series he submitted) as a more thorough solution than manual code review or documentation.

Junio had previously expressed concerns about assertions being compiled out via NDEBUG, which Elijah addresses by either excluding NDEBUG from BUG_ON() or through his static analysis proposal. The email shows both maintainers finding some common ground on technical implementation while maintaining their philosophical differences about when and how to document error conditions.

This exchange represents an important standards discussion that could influence future Git development practices, though it remains separate from the original merge-ort fix which appears settled. The tone remains professional and focused on finding practical solutions despite differing viewpoints.
