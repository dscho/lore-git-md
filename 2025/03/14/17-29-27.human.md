This is a discussion about potential licensing concerns with the assertion detection mechanism in the ongoing "safer assertions" patch series.

The thread has established that the technical approach (using a clever assert() redefinition to detect side effects) works well, but now focuses on whether its implementation might have licensing issues since the core idea was inspired by StackOverflow content. Elijah Newren confirms the technique was used before but notes it may be too trivial to be copyrightable. Junio Hamano acknowledges the concern and suggests someone else (who hasn't seen the original implementation) would need to propose an alternative if the project wants to avoid any licensing questions.

The discussion doesn't block the overall series - the safer BUG_IF_NOT() infrastructure and conversions in other patches remain valid - but may require reimplementation of the detection mechanism in patch 2/3. The exchange shows the project's careful attention to licensing matters even for small but clever implementation details.
