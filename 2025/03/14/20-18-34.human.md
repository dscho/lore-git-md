Performance patch in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX bitmap series (v4 05/13). This small but important change adapts the `show_objects_for_type()` function to properly handle pack lookups across MIDX layers by using `nth_midxed_pack()` instead of direct array access. 

The modification ensures correct behavior when querying objects that may reside in earlier MIDX layers than the current bitmap's layer. This is part of the broader effort to enable efficient bitmap operations across multi-pack index layers, where different layers may contain different subsets of the repository's objects.

The change is straightforward and well-contained, replacing a direct array access with the proper abstraction that understands the layered MIDX structure. It follows naturally from the base infrastructure established in earlier patches of this series. While small, it's a necessary piece for the overall functionality to work correctly across MIDX layers.
