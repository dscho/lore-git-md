Junio Hamano has decided to proceed with documenting third-party Git-related project issue trackers in the maintainer's notes (MaintNotes), reversing his earlier position to withdraw the proposal due to lack of feedback. The documentation now includes GitHub issue tracker links and contribution guidelines for git-scm.com, Git for Windows, and the ProGit book.

This marks a resolution to the month-long discussion about whether and how to document these external resources. The maintainer appears to have concluded that the benefits of directing contributors to appropriate venues outweigh the maintenance concerns, though he remains open to corrections and updates to the listed information.

The decision addresses three key questions that were previously unresolved: (1) the documentation will live in the maintainer-facing MaintNotes (rather than user docs), (2) the initial list includes only closely Git-affiliated projects, and (3) maintenance will presumably be handled through the same process as other MaintNotes updates.

This represents a low-risk experiment in improving contributor experience - if the links become stale or prove unhelpful, they can be removed in future editions. The move suggests Junio believes even minimal guidance is better than none when redirecting misdirected issue reports.
