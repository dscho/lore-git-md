Lucas Seiki Oshiro proposed an RFC patch series introducing a `--subject-extra-prefix` flag for `git format-patch`, aimed at helping participants in mentoring programs (like GSoC and Outreachy) clearly identify their contributions in email subjects. The technically complete series of 6 patches added the flag, implemented subject line construction, and included comprehensive tests. Eric Sunshine reviewed one patch, noting only a minor commit message typo.

Junio Hamano has now responded with a design-level suggestion to instead leverage the existing `--rfc` flag with a parameter (e.g., `--rfc=GSoC`) rather than adding a new flag. This approach would achieve similar subject marking while avoiding interface proliferation. The maintainer's feedback shifts the discussion from implementation details to design philosophy, presenting a crossroads for the series: adapt to use `--rfc`, justify keeping the new flag, or explore another approach. The technical work done may need significant reworking if the `--rfc` path is chosen.
