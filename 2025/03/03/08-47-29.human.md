This is a cover letter for a 12-part patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on object-related subsystems. Patrick Steinhardt continues the work to eliminate dependencies on the global `the_repository` variable, with an eye toward eventual libification and pluggable object databases.

The series tackles dependencies across multiple object-handling components including csum-file, pack operations, delta islands, and hash algorithms. While comprehensive in scope, it deliberately stops short of addressing `object-file.c`, which the author notes will require its own dedicated series. The changes are presented as standalone improvements that would be valuable even if the larger pluggable ODB effort doesn't materialize.
