This is part 2/12 of the `the_repository` removal series, focusing on object-related APIs. The patch refactors several functions in `object.c` and their callers to explicitly pass a repository parameter instead of implicitly using the global `the_repository`. Key changes include:

- Converting `get_max_object_index()` and `get_indexed_object()` to take a repository parameter
- Updating `parse_object_or_die()` and `clear_object_flags()` similarly
- Mechanical updates to 15 call sites across various commands (fsck, grep, pack-objects, etc.)

The changes are straightforward and maintain the same behavior while making repository dependencies explicit. The patch follows the established pattern seen in part 1 of the series, showing careful attention to call site updates. This continues the important architectural work to eliminate global state, though it's worth noting the series will still leave some object-file.c cases unaddressed.
