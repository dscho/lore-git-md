[PATCH v2 0/12] object: reduce use of `the_repository` in object APIs

Series by Patrick Steinhardt completes the systematic elimination of global `the_repository` usage in object-related APIs across 12 patches covering csum-file.c, object.c, pack-write.c, pack-check.c, core.bigFileThreshold conversion, pack-revindex.c, pack-bitmap-write.c, object-file-convert.c, delta-islands.c, hash algorithm refactoring, hash.c warning fixes, and final `null_oid()` conversion. The work builds on previous efforts to remove implicit repository state while maintaining behavior.
