This is a minor bugfix patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal series, addressing compiler warnings in the newly created hash subsystem.

Patrick Steinhardt submits patch 11/12 in René Scharfe's series to eliminate `the_repository` usage. While most of the series focuses on architectural changes, this patch is a straightforward cleanup: it fixes several "-Wsign-compare" compiler warnings in `hash.c` by changing loop counters and parameters from `int` to `size_t` where appropriate. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial, touching only type signatures and loop variables.

The patch removes a now-unneeded `DISABLE_SIGN_COMPARE_WARNINGS` define and updates three functions that iterate over hash algorithms to use consistent unsigned types. The header file is also updated to match the new signature of `hash_algo_by_length()`. 

As part of a larger series that has been carefully reviewed, this patch appears to be risk and maintains the series' high quality standards. It's a small but correct improvement that helps keep the codebase warning-clean while the hash subsystem work progresses.
