This is a patch in the `git-diff-pairs` series that introduces a new diff option to control status resolution behavior. The patch adds a `skip_resolving_statuses` flag to `struct diff_options` that prevents `diffcore_std()` from automatically resolving file pair statuses when set, preserving any manually-set status information.

The change is small but important for the series' goal of enabling batch diff processing with preserved status information. It replaces the previous `found_follow` workaround in `tree-diff.c` with a more general mechanism. The implementation modifies just two files - adding the new field to `diff.h` with clear documentation, and updating the condition in `diff.c` that controls when status resolution occurs.

Junio has reviewed and approved the change with a simple "Makes sense" comment, indicating this architectural piece is now finalized. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by maintaining backward compatibility while adding the new capability needed for the `git-diff-pairs` plumbing command's status preservation requirements.
