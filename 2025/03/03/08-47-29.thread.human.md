René Scharfe continues his long-running effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable from Git's codebase, this time focusing on object-related APIs. The series consists of 12 patches that mechanically convert implicit global state into explicit `struct repository *` parameters, touching 87 files across various object handling subsystems. 

The first patch specifically targets the csum-file subsystem, refactoring functions like `hashfd()`, `hashfd_check()`, and `hashfd_throughput()` to take explicit hash algorithm parameters instead of relying on `the_repository`. This change affects 14 files with mostly straightforward mechanical updates, requiring callers to pass `the_repository->hash_algo` where needed. While the patch doesn't introduce new functionality, it makes repository dependencies explicit - an important step toward the larger goal of repository independence. The changes are valuable improvements on their own, regardless of whether the broader pluggable ODB effort moves forward. Future patches in the series will address remaining cases in object-file.c and other subsystems.
