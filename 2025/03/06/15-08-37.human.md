Performance optimization for the files ref backend, part of a series addressing refname availability checks. This patch (6/16) extends the batched refname checking approach to the files backend, following similar changes made to reftable in the previous patch. The author identifies two key cases where batching makes sense: during normal reference updates and when initializing a repository. The implementation queues up refnames for checking rather than verifying them individually, though notes a race condition remains where packed-refs could change after the check. The patch maintains existing locking semantics to avoid surprising users, despite this being suboptimal. This is a mechanical refactoring that enables future optimizations in the series, though doesn't yet show performance gains itself. The changes are focused on `lock_raw_ref()` and related functions in `refs/files-backend.c`, with careful attention to maintain existing behavior while introducing the new batching infrastructure.
