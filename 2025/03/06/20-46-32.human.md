This is a follow-up discussion in the thread about bash completion fixes for remote names containing slashes. Junio Hamano weighs in with historical context about remote naming conventions, questioning whether slashes in remote names were ever intended to be supported.

The patch series being discussed has already implemented technical fixes for completion behavior with slashed remote names (like "github/dseomn"), but Junio raises a deeper design question: whether such naming patterns should be allowed at all. He notes that the original 2005 remote namespace design assumed simple remote names to enable powerful globbing patterns like "refs/remote/*/master", which breaks down with slashed remote names. He suggests this might warrant a breaking change at a future major version (Git 3.0).

The email doesn't propose immediate action but serves as a thoughtful reflection on the implications of the completion fixes, suggesting the team might want to reconsider remote naming rules more fundamentally. This adds architectural context to what had been primarily a completion-script-focused discussion, though the immediate technical solutions in the patch remain valid regardless of this larger design question.
