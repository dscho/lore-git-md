- All pack-writing operations (index creation, reverse index, mtimes files) now take `struct repository*` instead of `the_repository`
- Functions converted include `write_idx_file()`, `write_rev_file()`, `create_tmp_packfile()`, and `stage_tmp_packfiles()`
- 7 files updated with 41 insertions and 41 deletions
- `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` removed from pack-write.c
- Callers in fast-import, index-pack, pack-objects, bulk-checkin, and midx-write updated to pass repository context

The changes follow the pattern established in earlier patches of the series, maintaining behavior while removing implicit global state. This continues the systematic conversion of object-related code to explicit repository passing, supporting the larger goal of making repository state fully plumbed through APIs. The patch appears mechanically sound with thorough updates to all call sites.
