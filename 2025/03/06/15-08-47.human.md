Performance optimization completing the refs iterator reuse series. This is the final patch (16/16) in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series improving refname availability checks across Git's ref backends. The patch enables iterator reuse when checking for nested refname conflicts, avoiding the overhead of repeatedly allocating new iterators.

The change shows significant gains for the reftable backend (1.25x faster in benchmarks creating 10,000 refs), though the files backend sees only marginal improvement (1.01x) due to its inherent filesystem overhead. The implementation builds on the iterator reseek capability introduced earlier in the series, modifying `refs_verify_refnames_available()` to conditionally reuse iterators when possible.

With this patch, the series now provides complete iterator reseek support across all ref backends (reftable, ref-cache, packed-refs, and files). Reviewers have confirmed the approach is technically sound, with only minor documentation nits remaining. The cumulative effect demonstrates reftable now outperforming the files backend in realistic workloads, validating the optimization strategy.
