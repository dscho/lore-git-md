Performance optimization series for refs subsystem (v4, 16 patches). This is a review response from Karthik Nayak on the completed series that optimizes refname availability checks across Git's ref backends (files, packed, reftable). The series originated from performance issues with reftable tombstones but grew to address broader inefficiencies in ref operations.

The reviewer notes the v4 version looks generally good with only minor nits, not requiring another revision. The series implements three key optimizations: (1) skipping redundant ambiguity checks in `git-update-ref`, (2) batching prefix checks using a `strset`, and (3) reusing ref iterators rather than recreating them. Benchmarks show significant speedups - up to 7.56x faster for reftable in some cases, with more modest but still meaningful 1.19-1.27x improvements for the files backend.

The review indicates the technical approach is sound and the implementation appears thorough. While some documentation clarifications remain as follow-up work, the core optimizations are now complete and ready for potential merging. This marks the culmination of a multi-version effort to address performance bottlenecks in ref operations across all Git's storage backends.
