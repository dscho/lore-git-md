Meet Soni's reftable error handling series, mentored by Patrick Steinhardt as part of GSoC, has reached its final form in v4 with three focused patches. The series standardizes error propagation in the reftable writer implementation, touching block.c, record.c, and writer.c. 

The first two patches established consistent error codes (replacing generic -1 returns with specific REFTABLE_*_ERROR codes) and refined writer_add_record()'s error discrimination. The newly added third patch completes the work by fixing write_object_record() to properly distinguish between block-full conditions (which should trigger retry) and fatal errors like memory allocation failures. Patrick Steinhardt has guided the series through multiple iterations, with v4 incorporating all prior feedback through improved commit organization and clearer comments.

The implementation now handles error propagation robustly while maintaining the existing block-full retry behavior. This represents a significant improvement in the precision of reftable's error handling, particularly for edge cases in object record writing. With all three patches addressing distinct aspects of the error handling pipeline, the series appears ready for final review and potential merging.
