Meet Soni's reftable error handling bugfix series, developed as a GSoC project under Patrick Steinhardt's mentorship, has reached completion after five iterations of review and refinement. The series standardizes error handling across the reftable writer implementation (block.c, record.c, writer.c) by introducing specific REFTABLE_* error codes to replace generic -1 returns, with particular focus on distinguishing temporary block-full conditions from permanent errors.

The final v5 iteration addressed all outstanding concerns, including Patrick's request for an additional REFTABLE_ENTRY_TOO_BIG_ERROR check in write_object_record(). Key improvements include robust error propagation throughout the writer stack, clear documentation of retry logic, and proper handling of block-full conditions during object record writing. Patrick has now explicitly acknowledged his satisfaction with the series, signaling it's ready for potential inclusion. This marks the successful conclusion of a multi-iteration review process that has significantly improved error reporting and recovery behavior in the reftable implementation while maintaining all existing functionality for valid inputs.
