Bugfix completing a two-patch series to align `--exclude` behavior across Git's ref backends. This second patch addresses a subtle inconsistency where the packed and reftable backends would incorrectly match partial refnames (like treating "refs/heads/ba" as matching "refs/heads/bar"), while the files backend did not. The fix normalizes exclude patterns by adding trailing slashes in the common `refs_ref_iterator_begin()` function, ensuring all backends treat patterns consistently.

The change makes exclude patterns more precise - now "refs/heads/ba" will only exclude refs under "refs/heads/ba/" rather than anything starting with that prefix. This matches the files backend's behavior and fixes a potential issue where `upload-pack` might advertise different refs depending on whether they were packed or loose. The patch includes a test update to reflect the corrected behavior, noting that the original test was actually verifying buggy behavior.

This builds on the first patch's work to handle empty exclude patterns, completing the series' goal of making ref iteration behavior consistent across backends. The solution was chosen for its simplicity (a single change point) and effectiveness (fixing both packed and reftable backends simultaneously). The patch carries sign-offs from both the original reporter and Jeff King, suggesting it's been vetted for correctness.
