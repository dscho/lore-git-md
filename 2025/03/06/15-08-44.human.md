Performance optimization for ref-cache iterators (patch 13/16 in refs optimization series)

This patch implements seeking capability for ref-cache iterators, completing the iterator reseek functionality across all Git ref backends. The change splits the seeking logic out of `cache_ref_iterator_begin()` into a new `cache_ref_iterator_seek()` function for reuse, while making the prefix field heap-allocated to support reseeking.

The implementation drops an optimization that previously returned an empty iterator when no references existed, since that would prevent reseeking to different prefixes. The author notes this shouldn't significantly impact performance due to new early bailout checks in `advance()`. The patch maintains consistency with the series' architectural approach while handling edge cases like proper cleanup of the heap-allocated prefix.

The changes demonstrate careful attention to iterator lifecycle management and show the performance-focused mindset of the overall series.
