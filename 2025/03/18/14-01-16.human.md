This is a review comment on the reflog management enhancement series, specifically addressing the second patch that implements the 'drop' subcommand for `git reflog`.

Christian Couder raises two points about the implementation details. First, they suggest that `--current-worktree` might be a clearer name than `--single-worktree` for the flag that limits processing to the current worktree, but acknowledge the need for consistency with the existing `expire` subcommand's naming. 

More substantively, they point out that the `--single-worktree` flag only has effect when used with `--all`, and silently does nothing when used alone. While this matches the behavior of `git reflog expire`, they argue this is suboptimal user experience. The observation is made constructively, noting the series is otherwise well-executed.

The comment doesn't propose specific changes, but highlights a potential UX issue that might warrant consideration. This comes after the series has already addressed previous feedback and appears ready for inclusion, suggesting it's a minor refinement point rather than a blocking concern.
