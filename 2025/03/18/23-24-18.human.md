This is a patch series addressing build issues with the libgit-sys Rust crate, specifically around `cargo package` functionality. The series comes from Josh Steadmon and consists of 4 patches targeting various aspects of the build process.

The main focus is fixing packaging problems for libgit-sys, though the author notes that libgit-rs will remain broken until libgit-sys gets published first. The most notable change is #2, which adds a symlink to the Git repo root but has the unfortunate side effect of requiring a clean worktree during builds. The author explicitly asks for alternative suggestions to this approach.

Other changes include moving some components to a separate contrib directory, parallelizing builds using Cargo's jobserver, and excluding unnecessary directories from the packaged source. The series appears to be a well-structured attempt to solve concrete packaging problems, though the worktree-cleaning requirement in patch #2 may need further discussion before merging.
