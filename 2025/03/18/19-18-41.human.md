This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing protocol v2 fetch behavior improvements thread, specifically addressing edge cases around remote HEAD updates.

Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's question about the behavior of `git fetch origin HEAD` with respect to updating refs/remotes/origin/HEAD. Jeff had noted that currently this does nothing (especially in protocol v2), and while his recent patches don't change this behavior, he wondered if it might be worth addressing separately.

Junio makes two key points: (1) With protocol v2, Git has direct knowledge of the remote's position without needing to examine refs/heads/, and (2) There's no strict requirement to ensure remote-tracking HEAD references never dangle. He agrees with Jeff's approach of leaving this edge case unchanged for now, as it falls outside the scope of the current optimizations and behavior refinements.

This exchange represents a final clarification in what has been a comprehensive thread about protocol v2 fetch behavior, with all major technical questions now resolved. The discussion maintains the conservative approach taken throughout the thread - making targeted improvements while avoiding unnecessary behavior changes.
