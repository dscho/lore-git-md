This is a performance discussion regarding the MIDX bitmap implementation, specifically about the tradeoffs of using `ewah_or_iterator` for type bitmap operations.

Jeff King responds to Taylor Blau's patch (v4 12/13) that switched from `ewah_iterator` to `ewah_or_iterator` for type bitmap lookups in the incremental MIDX bitmap work. He notes this change makes sense architecturally but raises a potential performance consideration - the new approach adds an extra layer of indirection that might be slightly slower for non-layered (single pack) cases.

While acknowledging this is likely a micro-optimization, Jeff suggests it might be worth benchmarking the change against a single packfile to verify there's no unexpected performance impact in common cases. He characterizes this as a precautionary measure rather than a required change, maintaining the pattern of his previous reviews that identify potential optimizations while respecting the current implementation.

The discussion continues the thread's technical depth around bitmap operations, now focusing on the practical implications of the iterator unification approach introduced in this patch series. Jeff's comment suggests the architectural change is sound but leaves open the question of whether the simpler code is worth any potential minor performance tradeoff.
