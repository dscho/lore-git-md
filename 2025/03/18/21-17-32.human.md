This is a response in an ongoing discussion about commit note formatting in `git format-patch` output. Junio C Hamano pushes back on Tuomas Ahola's proposed change to use raw formatting for notes in email/mbox outputs, demonstrating concrete problems with the approach.

Hamano shows that the raw format (1) loses the "Notes (amlog):" header that identifies the note's origin, and (2) strips leading spaces that protect against misparsing note content as patch boundaries. His test case reveals how lines in the note could be misinterpreted as patch headers without proper indentation. He concludes the change would break existing workflows and needs reconsideration.

The response effectively vetoes the patch in its current form, though leaves room for alternative solutions that might preserve the note metadata and formatting. The thread now awaits either a revised approach from Ahola that addresses these concerns or confirmation that the idea should be abandoned. This illustrates Git's careful consideration of backward compatibility and parsing robustness in email-based workflows.
