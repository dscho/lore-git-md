This is a follow-up discussion in the reflog management enhancement series, specifically about the `--single-worktree` flag behavior in the new `git reflog drop` subcommand.

Junio Hamano acknowledges Christian Couder's and Toon Claes' concerns about two aspects of the implementation:
1. The potentially confusing behavior where `--single-worktree` silently does nothing when used without `--all`
2. The naming preference for `--current-worktree` over `--single-worktree`

Junio notes these quirks exist for consistency with the existing `expire` subcommand's behavior, and suggests two possible paths forward:
- Accept the current behavior as a known quirk for consistency
- First improve the `expire` subcommand's behavior/terminology, then mirror those improvements in the new `drop` subcommand

The discussion indicates these are minor polish items that don't block the current implementation, with Junio expressing willingness to move forward as-is. This email serves as acknoledgement of the feedback while signaling the series is likely ready for inclusion after the feature freeze period.
