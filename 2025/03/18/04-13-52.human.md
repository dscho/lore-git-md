Performance enhancement for incremental MIDX bitmaps. This is patch 6/13 in Taylor Blau's series implementing incremental multi-pack index bitmaps, which enables more efficient bitmap operations across layered packfiles.

Elijah Newren provides a minor review comment on wording ("most amount" vs "larger number/size") but otherwise doesn't raise any technical concerns about the patch. The change modifies pack-reuse behavior to consider all packs in the MIDX chain (not just the current layer) when reusing packfile sections, and specifically uses the oldest layer's preferred pack when reusing from a single pack, as this is likely to contain the most reusable content.

The implementation follows the established pattern of the series by traversing the MIDX chain via the `base_midx` pointer. The changes are focused on `pack-bitmap.c` with straightforward modifications to the pack enumeration and preferred pack selection logic. This appears to be a logical extension of the incremental MIDX bitmap functionality that maintains consistency with the series' architectural approach.
