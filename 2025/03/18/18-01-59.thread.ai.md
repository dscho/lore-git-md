Core problem or goal: Current approxidate handling has multiple issues when processing special date/time strings with explicit day components:
1. Ignores day-of-month with special date strings (e.g., "last week 15th" dropped "15th")
2. Inconsistent tm_mday field setting for "now"/"yesterday" calculations
3. Incorrect day rollover for time-based specials ("noon", "tea") when combined with explicit days -- the "most recent" rollback fires even when a specific day was explicitly provided, producing a contradictory date (e.g., "10 May at tea" on 2026-05-12 11:00 yields 2026-05-09 instead of 2026-05-10)
4. Month/year rollover edge cases when processing dates near boundaries with time-based specials
5. For "now" and "yesterday" specifically: the order-of-operations interaction with time-of-day specials means "tea yesterday" is computed as "most recent tea-time" then "yesterday" applied on top, often producing a date two days ago rather than yesterday's tea-time.
