This is a review comment on the reflog management enhancement series, specifically the 'drop' subcommand implementation.

Christian Couder raises two points about the recently submitted patch series that adds `git reflog drop` functionality. First, he suggests that `--current-worktree` might be a clearer name than `--single-worktree` for the flag that limits operations to the current worktree, though acknowledges the value of consistency with the existing `expire` subcommand's terminology. 

More substantively, he points out that the `--single-worktree` flag only has effect when used with `--all`, and silently does nothing when used alone - the command would exit successfully without performing any action. While this matches the behavior of `git reflog expire`, Christian considers this potentially confusing for users. 

The comment comes after the series has already addressed all substantive review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and received positive indications from Junio Hamano about eventual inclusion post-feature-freeze. The observation about flag behavior doesn't appear to block the series but suggests a possible usability improvement that could be considered for future iterations. The implementation itself remains technically sound, with comprehensive test coverage and clear documentation of the flag interactions.
