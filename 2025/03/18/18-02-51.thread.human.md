The discussion began with Tuomas Ahola proposing to change how `git format-patch` handles commit notes in email outputs, suggesting raw formatting instead of the current pretty-printed style. While the idea had some merit for preserving note formatting, it lacked strong justification for changing the existing behavior.

Junio Hamano identified serious problems with the proposal, showing how it would break important functionality - particularly for the `amlog` notes ref use case. The current pretty-printing serves two crucial purposes: it clearly marks notes with headers like "Notes (amlog):" and adds protective indentation that prevents notes content from being misinterpreted as patch boundaries (critical when notes contain email metadata).

In his latest response, Ahola acknowledges these flaws, particularly the danger of losing the protective indentation that prevents patch corruption. He also recognizes this isn't a new idea - a similar proposal was discussed and rejected in 2017, likely for the same reasons. With no revised approach offered, it appears the proposal will be abandoned given the fundamental incompatibility with existing workflows and the risk of breaking patch email processing. The thread is effectively concluding with this recognition of the technical constraints.
