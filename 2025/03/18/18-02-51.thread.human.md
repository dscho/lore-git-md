This thread discusses a small but potentially useful change to how `git format-patch` handles commit notes in email/mbox output formats. The patch proposes switching from pretty-printed to raw formatting for notes in these contexts, arguing the current behavior isn't ideal for these output types.

The change is minimal - just three lines in log-tree.c - and appears low-risk, though the commit message could better explain the practical benefits. The technical impact means notes will preserve their original whitespace and formatting rather than being reflowed, which could matter for tools parsing these patch emails. The patch currently lacks test coverage, which reviewers reasonably might request to verify the behavior change.

Given the contained nature of the change and its focus on a specific output format case, this seems like a straightforward improvement that could be merged after addressing any review feedback about motivation clarity and testing.
