This is a response in the ongoing discussion about `format-patch`'s handling of commit notes in email outputs. Brian Carlson provides feedback on Tuomas Ahola's proposed patch to use raw formatting for notes in CMIT_FMT_EMAIL and CMIT_FMT_MBOXRD formats.

The response critiques the original patch's justification as being too vague, requesting more concrete details about why the current pretty-printed format is problematic and what specific benefits the raw format would provide. Carlson suggests the commit message should clearly explain both the current output's shortcomings and the proposed solution's advantages, making the case understandable even to those not deeply familiar with the notes subsystem.

While admitting limited familiarity with notes functionality, Carlson speculates the motivation might relate to unwanted prefixing in format-patch output that makes notes less useful as supplementary explanations in patch emails. The response maintains a constructive tone while pushing for more thorough justification of the change, particularly regarding its impact on email-based workflows. No technical objections are raised to the implementation itself, focusing instead on the need for better documentation of the rationale.
