Junio Hamano responds to Eric Sunshine's review of a GSoC contributor's test modernization patch. This is a mentoring discussion about contributor onboarding and test infrastructure guidance.

Hamano agrees with Sunshine's assessment that the patch shows a misunderstanding of where test modernization efforts should be focused (core test scripts rather than chainlint files). He emphasizes that contributors need to first understand *why* test_path_* helpers are preferred over direct shell tests before making changes. 

The most concerning aspect for Hamano is the patch's reference to "Git Microprojects" as justification, suggesting this might indicate misleading documentation or insufficient mentor guidance for new contributors. He raises the important question of whether the project needs to provide more explicit direction at this early stage, or if the responsibility lies with students to research proper contribution practices.

This email continues the thread's theme of balancing mentorship with expectations for contributor initiative, while also surfacing potential issues with how GSoC microprojects are being presented to newcomers. The discussion remains constructive but highlights gaps in the project's onboarding materials or processes.
