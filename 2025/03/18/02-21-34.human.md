This is a review response in the ongoing MIDX bitmap implementation thread. Jeff King (Peff) provides his overall assessment of Taylor Blau's v4 patch series implementing incremental multi-pack index bitmaps.

Peff acknowledges the series looks reasonable and doesn't raise any major objections, though he notes the complexity makes it hard to catch all potential corner cases through code review alone. He suggests the best validation will come from real-world testing, particularly on GitHub's infrastructure where the feature will get heavy usage. 

His main focus was ensuring the changes don't negatively impact non-incremental cases, and he found no significant issues there. While he left some minor optimization questions, he considers them negligible in terms of measurable impact. 

The review indicates the series is ready to move forward to the next phase of real-world testing, with the safety net that the new feature is opt-in and won't affect users who don't enable it. This continues Peff's pattern of thorough but non-blocking reviews throughout the series.
