Tuomas Ahola's v2 series fixing approxidate edge cases is under active review. The three patches address long-standing bugs from a 2006 Linus Torvalds report where special date strings like "last week 15th" or "10 May at tea" produce wrong dates.

Jeff King has now weighed in on patch 1/3, a test infrastructure change adding an optional hour offset to the `check_approxidate` helper. He flags two design concerns: the fourth positional argument forces callers to always spell out the optional "success" parameter, and using "+48" as an opaque relative offset from a test epoch is less self-documenting than accepting full timestamp strings. Both are observations, not demands -- King acknowledges the fix may be "too big a rabbit hole" before the series lands.

Junio C Hamano's earlier review of patch 1/3 requested three coding-style changes to the shell test helper. Patches 2/3 and 3/3 have King's Reviewed-by. Patch 2/3 has a substantive open question from Junio: adding "today" as an alias for "now" would make `git log --since=today` exclude earlier-today commits, which users might not expect. The core fix in that patch is unaffected if the alias is dropped. Junio has not yet reviewed patch 3/3.

The thread's remaining work is a v3 responding to Junio's style feedback on patch 1/3 and resolving the "today" discussion on patch 2/3. King's interface comments on patch 1/3 add an aesthetic dimension but were explicitly not framed as blockers. No structural objections to any patch have been raised, and the series appears close to landing once the open items are addressed.
