This is a follow-up in the thread about `git format-patch`'s notes formatting behavior. Tuomas Ahola acknowledges Junio C Hamano's critique of their proposed change to use raw formatting for notes in email/mbox contexts. 

The key concession is regarding Hamano's point about how raw formatting would break critical functionality - specifically the loss of leading spaces that prevent notes content from being misinterpreted as patch boundaries (when notes contain lines starting with "diff", "---", etc.). Ahola also acknowledges this would break existing users, particularly those relying on the current behavior with notes refs like `amlog`.

Ahola notes they should have found the 2017 discussion of a similar proposal, implying they now recognize this isn't a new idea and the previous rejection likely had similar reasoning. The email doesn't propose any revised approach, suggesting the original proposal may be abandoned in light of these technical constraints. The thread appears to be concluding with this recognition of the change's fundamental flaws regarding email patch safety and backward compatibility.
