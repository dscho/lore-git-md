This is a technical refinement patch within Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX bitmap series (v4 03/13). Elijah Newren provides minor editorial feedback on the patch, pointing out two grammatical issues in the commit message ("chain" vs "chain of" and subject-verb agreement with "ensures"). 

The patch itself adds infrastructure for handling incremental bitmap layers by introducing a `base` pointer and `base_nr` counter in `struct bitmap_index` to track previous MIDX layers. Key changes include:
- New filename handling for split MIDX bitmaps
- Loading of pack reverse indexes across all MIDX layers
- Recursive bitmap loading through the base chain
- Proper cleanup of base bitmaps on free

The changes are mechanical plumbing work to support the incremental bitmap functionality, with no substantive objections raised - just the minor language nits from Newren. This continues the series' pattern of careful implementation that has earned positive reviews from Jeff King and others.
