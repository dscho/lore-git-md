This thread addressed an inconsistency in `git blame`'s porcelain output where ignored/unblamable line markers ('?' and '*' respectively) weren't being shown with `--ignore-rev`/`--ignore-revs-file` options. The discussion evolved through several iterations, with key contributions from Karthik Nayak (implementation), Patrick Steinhardt (design approach), Toon Claes (use case validation), and Eric Sunshine (testing improvements).

The solution settled on adding new metadata lines in `--line-porcelain` mode while preserving backward compatibility, implemented through a new `emit_porcelain_per_line_details()` function. Junio Hamano provided critical guidance on maintaining the SHA-1 line format and approved the final design. The series went through three versions, with v3 incorporating final optimizations like function renaming and output improvements.

The most recent email shows Junio acknowledging a minor test output readability issue where variable interpolation wasn't working in test titles due to single-quote usage - a legacy limitation now fixed by switching to double quotes. With this final polish, the series appears ready for merging, having addressed all technical concerns while maintaining compatibility and extensibility for future porcelain output additions.
