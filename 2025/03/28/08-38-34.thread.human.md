This thread consolidates a series of build system fixes (v2) from Patrick Steinhardt and several contributors, addressing various issues in Git's build infrastructure. The series includes six patches improving Meson build detection, fixing gitweb JavaScript generation, ensuring proper test flag handling, enhancing cross-compilation support, and updating CI workflows.

Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) has now weighed in with important feedback. While he approves most of the technical changes, he signals a significant shift in Windows CI strategy - advocating to completely drop CMake/Visual Studio builds due to their excessive resource consumption (9.5 CPU hours per build). This exceeds GitHub's concurrency limits and aligns with his existing plans to simplify CI infrastructure. His position suggests the current CI-related patches may serve as temporary measures before more substantial simplification.

The technical aspects of the series appear sound, with only minor editorial issues remaining (a commit message typo) and one optional suggestion for future-proofing file extension handling in the gitweb JavaScript generation. Multiple contributors have reviewed the patches positively, including Toon Claes and Eli Schwartz. The cross-compilation changes remain the only area without explicit approval from Dscho due to his stated unfamiliarity with Meson's cross-compilation features. The thread demonstrates effective collaboration, with Patrick incorporating feedback in the v2 iteration.
