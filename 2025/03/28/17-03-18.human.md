This is a bugfix and user experience improvement series for `git rebase -r` (rebase with merges) and related status messages.

Philippe Blain submits three patches that address issues discovered while working on rebase status messages. The series started with patch 3/3, which fixes a confusing status message that incorrectly suggested `git commit` instead of `git rebase --continue` after resolving merge conflicts during a rebase. The other two patches emerged during this work: patch 2/3 improves status message formatting during rebases, and patch 1/3 fixes a bug where empty resolutions during merge rebases weren't properly creating merge commits.

The changes touch the sequencer and wt-status code, with corresponding test additions to verify the fixes. The most notable improvement is ensuring users don't accidentally lose authorship information by following incorrect status suggestions. The series appears well-tested with 99 new lines of test coverage across two test files.

While the patches could theoretically be split (with 1/3 being a standalone bugfix), they work well together as they all relate to improving the rebase-with-merges experience. The changes seem focused and well-motivated by actual user experience pain points.
