This is a review/feedback email in the ongoing GSoC proposal thread about refactoring Git's global state, specifically regarding the `the_repository` removal effort.

The reviewer draws on their own GSoC experience to warn about the time investment needed to justify structural changes to the community.

The tone is supportive while being technically cautious, emphasizing risk reduction through incremental progress. The email confirms the importance of the overall project (eliminating globals for multi-repo safety) but questions whether the proposed implementation timeline adequately accounts for review/iteration cycles on structural changes. No new technical objections are raised to the fundamental approach of moving variables out of global scope.
