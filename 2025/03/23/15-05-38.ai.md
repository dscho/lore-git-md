Ayman acknowledges this as a valid catch and raises a follow-up question about whether both `F_OK` and `X_OK` should be performed.

The exchange continues the thread's progression from architectural discussion to implementation-level security hardening. This particular sub-thread is now focused specifically on the permission validation mechanism, which there appears to be emerging consensus that `X_OK` is necessary (at minimum) for proper security. The question about combining both checks suggests potential consideration of defense-in-depth approaches, though the thread hasn't yet settled on a final implementation.
