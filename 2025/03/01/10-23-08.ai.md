Performance optimization follow-up for bundle-uri reference handling. Scott Chacon responds to Derrick Stolee's concerns about tag handling in the bundle-uri performance improvement patch.

Key technical points in this response:
- Questions the practicality of requiring special bundle creation procedures (peeling tags to commits) for the optimization to work
- Proposes alternative approaches:
  1. Adding a `--bundle-for-uri` option to `git bundle` to handle reference sanitization
  2. Simply allowing tags in `refs/bundle/tags/` during fetch negotiation then deleting them afterward
- Notes that tests have been updated to check for `refs/bundle/heads/` rather than `refs/bundle/` directly
- Asks for clarification on what "usable" means regarding tags in `refs/bundle/tags/`

The email reveals ongoing tension between:
1. The clear performance benefits of the broader refspec approach
2. The complexity of proper tag handling
3. The desire to avoid making bundle creation more complicated for users

Chacon leans toward simpler solutions that handle the complexity in the fetch negotiation rather than pushing requirements onto bundle creation. The response indicates a new patch series is forthcoming with updated tests, but core questions about tag handling philosophy remain unresolved.
