This is the final patch in Elijah Newren's series to completely remove the legacy merge-recursive implementation, marking the completion of the merge-ort migration project. The patch removes the last vestiges of testing infrastructure that supported comparing the two merge backends.

The change deletes all references to the GIT_TEST_MERGE_ALGORITHM environment variable from three commands (`merge`, `rebase`, and `revert`) that previously used it to toggle between merge strategies during testing. Since merge-ort is now the only backend (with 'recursive' remapped to it), this testing scaffolding is no longer needed. The diff shows straightforward removal of environment variable checks and associated fallback logic in the affected builtins.

This is a mechanical cleanup following the substantive work in earlier patches that migrated all callers to merge-ort and removed the merge-recursive implementation. The changes are low-risk deletions with no functional impact on normal operation, only affecting test infrastructure that's now obsolete. With this patch, the series completes its goal of fully removing merge-recursive from the codebase after merge-ort's successful multi-year adoption.
