This is a major refactoring and cleanup series targeting the merge machinery, specifically the removal of the legacy merge-recursive implementation in favor of merge-ort. The series comes from Elijah Newren, who has been leading the merge-ort development.

The series consists of 8 patches that:
1. First switch all remaining callers from merge-recursive to merge-ort (patches 1-5)
2. Then completely remove merge-recursive.[ch] (patch 6)
3. Finally clean up test infrastructure that was supporting both algorithms (patches 7-8)

Key technical details:
- The diffstat shows massive deletions (over 5000 lines) from removing merge-recursive.[ch] and related test infrastructure
- merge-ort gains diff-algorithm selection capability to match merge-recursive's functionality
- All test changes are either removing GIT_TEST_MERGE_ALGORITHM infrastructure or updating tests to work with ort backend
- The series builds on years of work to make merge-ort the default merge strategy

This represents the culmination of the merge-ort project, removing the legacy recursive backend entirely. The changes are primarily mechanical switches to the new backend rather than functional changes. The test updates are extensive but expected given the merge backend change. The series appears well-structured with clear preparatory steps before the final removal.
