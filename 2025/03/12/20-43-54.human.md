This is a follow-up discussion in the cruft pack size limit thread, where Junio Hamano responds to Elijah Newren's proposal about decoupling the size thresholds for cruft packs.

Junio points out a practical limitation in Elijah's suggestion to have separate `--combine-cruft-below-size` and `--max-pack-size` parameters. He illustrates with concrete numbers (180/200MB thresholds) how there could be scenarios where it's impossible to satisfy both constraints simultaneously - you might have a partially filled pack at 170MB but the next smallest cruft pack is 40MB, which would exceed the maximum when combined.

While acknowledging that the naming could be clearer (since one parameter acts as a minimum while the other is a true maximum), Junio suggests the fundamental issue isn't solved by just renaming parameters. This maintains the thread's conclusion that the current v3 approach with strict size enforcement is the most practical solution, though the discussion surfaces some of the inherent challenges in cruft pack size management.

The exchange shows the maintainer considering but ultimately finding limitations in an alternative proposal, reinforcing the decision to stick with the simpler, stricter size enforcement approach that was previously settled upon.
