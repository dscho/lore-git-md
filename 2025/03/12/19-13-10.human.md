Final resolution on cruft pack size limits. This email from Elijah Newren proposes an alternative approach to the recently settled cruft pack size limit policy, suggesting a separation of concerns between the maximum pack size (`--max-pack-size`) and a new parameter for controlling cruft pack combination (`--combine-cruft-below-size`).

The thread had previously concluded with Taylor Blau accepting Junio Hamano's position that strict enforcement of `--max-pack-size` is non-negotiable, withdrawing his earlier proposal for a +1 object allowance. Newren now suggests decoupling these concerns entirely by introducing a separate threshold for when cruft packs should be combined, set lower than the hard pack size limit.

This appears to be a late-stage suggestion that comes after the technical discussion was considered closed. While the proposal aims to address the niche case of repeatedly repacking cruft packs that hit the size boundary, it's unclear whether this will reopen the settled discussion. The idea would require modifying the recently agreed-upon implementation in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and related repack logic.

Given the thread's resolution status and the maintainer's strong stance on size limits, this suggestion may represent more of a conceptual alternative than a likely path forward. The email doesn't indicate whether this was discussed offline or if there's appetite to revisit the settled policy.
