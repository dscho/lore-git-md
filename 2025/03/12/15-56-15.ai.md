Refactoring of the refs iterator lifecycle in Patrick Steinhardt's performance optimization series (patch 9/16). This change detaches iterator lifecycle management from iteration completion, replacing the automatic cleanup at exhaustion with explicit `ref_iterator_free()` calls. The patch standardizes iterator behavior across Git's codebase by:

1. Introducing `ref_iterator_free()` to explicitly deallocate iterators rather than having them auto-free on completion
2. Renaming `abort` methods to `release` in iterator vtable implementations
3. Removing return values from cleanup functions since errors during cleanup were unused
4. Updating all iterator implementations (files, packed, reftable backends) to match the new pattern
5. Adding corresponding `dir_iterator_free()` for the special-case dir_iterator

The change enables potential future optimizations like iterator reuse while simplifying call sites that previously had to carefully manage `ref_iterator_abort()` timing. The patch touches 13 files with 105 insertions and 186 deletions, primarily mechanical conversions of cleanup patterns. This builds on the series' earlier infrastructure work (patches 1-8) and maintains compatibility with the batched verification optimizations introduced in prior patches.
