Performance optimization completing the refs subsystem iterator seek series. This final patch (16/16) implements iterator reuse during refname availability checks, building on the seek capabilities added in previous patches (11-15/16) across all ref backends.

The change modifies `refs_verify_refnames_available()` to reuse rather than recreate iterators when checking for nested references, yielding a 1.25x speedup for reftable (39.8ms → 31.9ms in benchmarks) though minimal impact on the files backend (392.3ms → 387.7ms). The implementation:
- Adds conditional iterator creation (only when null)
- Uses `ref_iterator_seek()` when reusing existing iterators
- Maintains iterator state between checks
- Preserves all existing error handling

The patch completes the series' goal of optimizing ref operations by eliminating redundant iterator allocations, particularly benefiting reftable where seek operations are more efficient. It follows the pattern established in prior patches while addressing the specific case of refname verification. The files backend shows limited gains due to its inherent filesystem overhead, but the change maintains consistency across all backends.
