Performance optimization in refs verification, part of a larger series addressing refname availability checks. This patch (8/16) introduces a `strset` to track already-checked directory prefixes, avoiding redundant verification of common paths like "refs/heads/". The change builds on earlier patches in the series that established batch verification infrastructure.

shejialuo notes in their review that the reftable backend shouldn't need to sort refnames, though this appears to be a continuation of their earlier feedback about sorting consistency between backends rather than a new issue with this specific patch. The optimization itself looks straightforward and well-contained, adding the directory tracking without disrupting the existing verification flow.

This is one of several optimizations in the series that collectively achieve significant speedups (1.19-1.27x for files backend, 2.32-7.56x for reftable). The thread indicates the series has received final approval from Karthik Nayak with all technical concerns addressed, suggesting this patch is likely ready for inclusion.
