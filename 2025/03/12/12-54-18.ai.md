This sets up for future optimizations where batch processing can be made more efficient, though the immediate functional behavior remains unchanged.

- Removes per-ref `refs_verify_refname_available()` calls in favor of single `refs_verify_refnames_available()` call
- Includes cleanup of the new string list
- Sorting operation questioned as potentially premature optimization
