Performance optimization in the refs subsystem (patch 8/16 of a completed series). This patch eliminates redundant verification of common refname prefixes (like "refs/heads/") by using a strset to track already-processed directories. The change is part of a larger effort to optimize refname availability checks across Git's ref backends.

The patch received positive feedback from shejialuo, who noted the optimization makes sense while observing that sorting refnames isn't necessary for the reftable backend. Patrick Steinhardt concurs with this assessment in his brief reply.

This is one of several optimizations in a performance-focused series that has now been fully reviewed and approved, showing significant speedups (1.19-1.27x for files backend, 2.32-7.56x for reftable). The series addresses inefficiencies in refname checking, particularly around tombstone references. All documentation feedback, including comments about early termination behavior in later patches, has been resolved.
