This is a review comment on patch 2/16 in a performance optimization series for the refs subsystem. Reviewer shejialuo is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that introduces a `GET_OID_SKIP_AMBIGUITY_CHECK` flag to bypass expensive object ID ambiguity checks when safe.

The reviewer notes that while they understand the technical approach, the standalone nature of this patch makes its motivation unclear until reading the subsequent patch that actually uses the new flag. They suggest the two patches could potentially be combined, but ultimately accept the current separation as reasonable.

This is a minor stylistic point in an otherwise technically sound series that has already received final approval from Karthik Nayak. The comment doesn't raise any blocking concerns about the implementation itself, just a readability consideration about patch organization. The series as a whole aims to optimize refname availability checks across Git's storage backends, with this particular patch laying groundwork by adding a performance optimization knob to the object lookup API.
