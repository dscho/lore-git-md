Feature patch introducing path-based delta compression in pack-objects, part 2/13 of a larger series.

Taylor Blau responds to Derrick Stolee's patch adding the `--path-walk` option to `git pack-objects`. The option reorganizes object walking to group objects by path rather than name-hash, aiming to improve delta compression (especially for repositories with hash collisions) while maintaining UNINTERESTING objects as delta bases. The current implementation performs delta calculations during the walk, which slows enumeration and isn't threaded yet, though Stolee notes future plans to optimize this.

Blau's review is generally positive, noting the implementation seems reasonable. He suggests documenting the implicit bitmap index incompatibility and expresses interest in the future "regions" approach for threaded delta computation. The patch is part of a larger effort to optimize packing for large repositories, particularly Microsoft's Scalar-managed monorepos, though performance tradeoffs with simpler alternatives remain an open discussion point in the thread.
