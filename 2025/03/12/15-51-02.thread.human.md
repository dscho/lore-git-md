The issue stems from an integer overflow in `xdiff/xemit.c` during context length calculation (`2 * ctxlen + interhunkctxlen`).

This appears to be a legitimate bug in the diff machinery, though the practical impact is minimal since such extreme context values are rarely used. The report is thorough, including reproduction steps, version details, and a comparison with GNU diff's correct behavior.
