Maintainer follow-up on cruft pack size limits

Junio Hamano, the Git maintainer, weighs in on the ongoing discussion about cruft pack size thresholds in response to Taylor Blau's series. While the core implementation is settled, this email explores the broader implications of size-limited pack behavior.

Junio observes that the "nearly full pack" problem Taylor identified isn't unique to cruft packs - it could affect any repacking operation with size limits. He questions whether allowing slight size violations (Taylor's withdrawn proposal) is the best solution, suggesting alternative approaches like using .keep files or extending pack metadata to record why a pack was closed.

The maintainer's perspective focuses on the general case: how Git should handle packs that hit size limits during creation, whether they contain cruft or regular objects. He proposes that future repacks could be smarter about avoiding repeated attempts to repack these "frozen" packs if they're marked. While not blocking the current series, this suggests potential future work to improve pack handling logic.

This appears to be closing discussion on the cruft pack series while planting seeds for possible follow-up work on pack management. The core implementation remains unchanged, but Junio's comments may influence future pack-related developments.
