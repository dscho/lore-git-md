Bugfix for progress reporting in maintenance tasks. This is the first patch in a 2-part series that addresses issues with progress indicators during non-interactive maintenance operations.

The patch fixes a specific issue where the `--no-quiet` flag wasn't being properly propagated to child processes during maintenance tasks, causing progress indicators to be suppressed even when explicitly requested. The changes affect several maintenance subcommands in builtin/gc.c, including loose-object packing, commit-graph writing, and multi-pack-index operations.

Key technical details:
- Modifies 5 maintenance subcommands in builtin/gc.c to properly pass through progress/quiet flags
- Adds `--progress` flag when `--no-quiet` is set (inverse of existing `--no-progress` behavior)
- Specifically adds `--no-quiet` flag to pack-objects during loose-object packing
- Prepares for a test case in the following patch that will verify this behavior

The changes are straightforward and follow established patterns for command-line flag propagation. The patch appears well-contained and focused on the specific issue, with no obvious edge cases or complications. The commit message clearly explains the problem and solution, though it doesn't provide specific reproduction steps for the bug.
