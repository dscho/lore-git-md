GSoC proposal discussion on ref command consolidation. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Yuting Zheng's initial proposal about unifying ref-related commands under `git-refs` subcommands. He confirms there hasn't been significant public discussion about this consolidation approach, though he and Peff have privately discussed the idea. Steinhardt points to a GitLab issue (#330) as informal documentation of his intent to consolidate these commands. He identifies consensus-building and interface design as key challenges for the project, offering to provide feedback once Yuting develops a more concrete proposal. The exchange highlights both the technical opportunity (unifying scattered ref commands) and the social challenge (driving community agreement) inherent in this potential GSoC project.
