Git Merge 2025 location discussion for the 20th anniversary conference. Patrick Steinhardt proposes two options, breaking from the usual US/EU alternation pattern: GitHub HQ in San Francisco (following normal rotation after Berlin 2024) or colocating with Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam. Amsterdam consideration stems from concerns about US political climate potentially making travel discomfort for some community members. Initial Linux Foundation discussions about Amsterdam colocation are positive, with confirmation expected early April. Thread seeks community feedback on both the validity of US travel concerns and general location preferences before decision. Discussion appears to be early-stage sentiment gathering rather than finalized proposal.
