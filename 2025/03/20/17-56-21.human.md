This is a cover letter for the fifth version of Taylor Blau's series implementing incremental MIDX bitmaps, now expanded to 14 patches. The series has reached final refinement after thorough review from Elijah Newren and Jeff King, with all technical concerns addressed through empirical measurement and test coverage gaps closed.

The series enables efficient bitmap operations across multi-pack index (MIDX) layers through structured bitmaps, representing the second of three planned efforts in the MIDX bitmap implementation. Key improvements in this version include:

1. Documentation clarifications about object sorting semantics in incremental layers
2. Renaming of `ewah_or_iterator_free()` to `ewah_or_iterator_release()` (originally in patch 10/13 but moved during rebase)
3. Added explicit verification of MIDX layer structure in tests
4. Numerous commit message and comment wording improvements throughout

The implementation now has:
- Full read/write support for incremental bitmaps
- Layer-aware verification
- Pack-reuse optimizations for multi-layer scenarios
- Confirmed negligible performance impact from the `ewah_or_iterator` approach
- Jeff King has approved the implementation, confirming no regressions in non-incremental paths and the architectural soundness of the design.

The series is now in its final form before merging, with Taylor Blau preparing to incorporate any remaining minor feedback. This represents a significant milestone in the MIDX bitmap implementation effort, enabling cheaper bitmap operations in large repositories by avoiding full bitmap rebuilds when adding new commits.
