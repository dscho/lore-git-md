Patrick Steinhardt's comprehensive 20-part series to eliminate Perl dependencies from Git's test suite has reached its conclusion with positive feedback from Phillip Wood. The series systematically converted all test components - including text processing, cryptographic artifacts, HTTP protocol testing, and pack delta analysis - while maintaining behavior fidelity. The final patch focused on HTTP protocol tests in t5703, replacing Perl-based capability filtering with `test_grep` and reducing the remaining Perl dependencies to just Gitweb tests and HTTPD CGI setup (where removal would require HTTPD changes beyond Git's control).

Phillip Wood, who had provided feedback on earlier versions, reviewed the final v3 series and found the changes "look good," specifically approving the removal of unnecessary sed indirections. With Eric Sunshine's prior feedback also addressed and no new issues raised, the series appears ready for inclusion. This marks a significant milestone in making Git's test suite more maintainable by reducing its surface area of Perl dependencies to the absolute minimum required.
