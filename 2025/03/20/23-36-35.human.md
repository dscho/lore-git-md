This is a follow-up in the bugfix thread about advice suppression during clone operations. Justin Tobler responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review of his patch that fixes unexpected "initial branch" advice messages appearing during bundle clones despite suppression flags.

Justin agrees with Patrick's observation that the advice makes no sense in this context since users can't control the branch name during clone. He plans to enhance the solution in two ways for the next version: converting the direct `advise()` call to `advise_if_enabled()` to make suppression available to other call sites, and adding a test to verify the advice is properly suppressed.

The exchange shows the patch is moving toward a more robust solution while maintaining its core purpose - fixing the regression where clone's bundle operations bypassed advice suppression. The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than fundamental disagreements, with both reviewers and author aligned on the technical approach.
