Bugfix investigation for advice suppression regression during bundle clones. Justin Tobler provides a technical deep dive into why the "Using 'master' as name for initial branch" hint appears despite suppression flags (GIT_ADVICE=0 and --quiet) when cloning from bundles, a regression introduced in commit 199f44cb2ead.

The analysis reveals how the problematic commit changed the initialization sequence, moving the first call to `git_default_branch_name()` (now `repo_default_branch_name()`) from `create_reference_database()` (which suppresses advice) to `guess_remote_head()` (which doesn't). This explains why the hint appears specifically for bundle clones where the transport must guess the remote HEAD.

Tobler proposes fixing this by modifying `guess_remote_head()` to support quiet operation, allowing the clone command to suppress the advice message as intended. The explanation is thorough and clearly identifies both the root cause and a viable solution path. This appears to be a well-reasoned analysis of a subtle interaction between Git's initialization sequence and advice suppression system.
