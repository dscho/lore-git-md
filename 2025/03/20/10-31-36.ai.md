Community event planning discussion for Git Merge 2025.

Patrick Steinhardt is proposing two potential locations for the 20th anniversary Git Merge conference, breaking from the usual US/EU alternation pattern. The options are: hosting at GitHub HQ in San Francisco (which would follow the normal rotation after last year's Berlin event) or colocating with Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam. The latter is being considered due to concerns about the current US political climate potentially making some community members uncomfortable traveling there.

Initial discussions with the Linux Foundation about Amsterdam colocation have been positive, though details won't be confirmed until early April. The email seeks community feedback on both the validity of the US travel concerns and general location preferences before making a decision. This appears to be an early-stage discussion to gauge community sentiment rather than a finalized proposal.
