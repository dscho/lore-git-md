This is a bugfix patch in Martin Ågren's ongoing series refactoring Git's pretty-format parsing code. The patch addresses lingering state issues in padding directive handling.

The patch ensures that after processing padding directives ("%<" or "%>"), all related state in the `struct padding_args` is fully reset rather than leaving partial state. While the previous commit fixed a more serious bug related to this, this change provides additional safety by completely clearing the padding state after use. Patrick Steinhardt agrees this is correct behavior and suggests a minor follow-up improvement to avoid duplicating initialization code.

The change is small but meaningful - it adds two lines to clear the `truncate` and `padding` fields after processing, matching the initialization pattern from earlier in the series. A test was also added to verify this behavior. While unlikely to affect real-world usage, the change makes the code's behavior more predictable and maintainable.
