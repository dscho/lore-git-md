This is a follow-up review in the cruft pack handling series, confirming the v2 changes. Elijah Newren is giving his approval to Taylor Blau's patch series introducing `--combine-cruft-below-size` as a replacement for the problematic `--max-cruft-size` behavior in `git repack`.

- More precise wording about how large cruft packs are handled ("leaves alone" instead of "prohibits")
- Explicit documentation that size parameter accepts k/m/g suffixes
- Clarified relationship between `--max-cruft-size` and the new option

Elijah confirms the series maintains its careful staged approach - first reorganizing tests, then modifying the old behavior, and finally introducing the new option. The implementation appears solid, and with the documentation improvements from v2, he gives his approval without requesting further changes. This suggests the series is likely ready for merging into Git's main branch.
