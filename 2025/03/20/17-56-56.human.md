This is part of Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX bitmap series (v5 10/14), focusing on pseudo-merge support. The patch modifies how pseudo-merges (a bitmap optimization technique) are applied when working with layered multi-pack indexes.

The change makes `apply_pseudo_merges_for_commit_1()` traverse through all MIDX layers (via the `base` pointer chain) to check for applicable pseudo-merges, rather than just checking the current layer. This ensures pseudo-merge optimizations work correctly across the full MIDX hierarchy. The modification is straightforward - it replaces a single call with a while loop that walks the chain of bitmaps.

The patch fits cleanly into the series' established pattern of handling MIDX layers recursively. While small, it's an important piece for maintaining bitmap performance characteristics in the incremental MIDX context. The change maintains the existing behavior when there's only one layer (no base MIDX), so it shouldn't affect non-incremental cases.
