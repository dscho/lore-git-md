This is a review comment on patch 12/20 in the series to make Git's test suite Perl-optional. Eric Sunshine points out two potential issues with the Perl-to-awk conversions in the reftable and git-serve tests.

For the reftable test, he notes the awk version changes the test's output from one extremely long line to 50,000 separate lines, which may or may not be what the test intended to verify. He suggests a pure shell alternative that would maintain the original behavior if single-line output was important.

For the git-serve test, he confirms the awk version matches the Perl behavior (producing 65,536 lines) but again notes this could be implemented in pure shell. The review is constructive, focusing on whether the semantic changes are intentional and offering alternative implementations, while staying aligned with the series' goal of removing Perl dependencies.
