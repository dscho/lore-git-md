Performance optimization in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX bitmap series (v5 11/14). This patch introduces a new `ewah_or_iterator` structure to efficiently OR together multiple EWAH bitmaps from different MIDX layers during reachability traversals.

The key innovation is avoiding decompression of entire bitmaps when only partial results are needed. The new iterator allows traversing the logical OR of multiple bitmaps without materializing them fully, which is particularly useful for type-level bitmaps (commits, trees, blobs) that span MIDX layers. Taylor considered two alternative approaches - full decompression/OR-ing (too memory intensive) and recursive traversal (inefficient for sparse regions) - before settling on this more elegant solution.

The implementation adds three main functions to the EWAH bitmap library: initialization (`ewah_or_iterator_init`), iteration (`ewah_or_iterator_next`), and cleanup (`ewah_or_iterator_release`). This fits naturally into the series' goal of efficient cross-layer bitmap operations while maintaining the established patterns for MIDX chain traversal.

As with previous patches in this well-structured series, the change is focused and comes with clear rationale in the commit message. It addresses a specific performance consideration in the larger incremental MIDX bitmap effort while keeping the changes localized to the EWAH bitmap handling code.
