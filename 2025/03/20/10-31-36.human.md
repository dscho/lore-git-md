Event planning discussion for Git Merge 2025.

Patrick Steinhardt is proposing two potential locations for the 20th anniversary Git Merge conference, noting the usual alternation between US and EU venues would suggest San Francisco this year (following last year's Berlin event). However, he raises concerns about the current US political climate potentially making attendees uncomfortable with travel there, and suggests considering Amsterdam colocated with Open Source Summit Europe as an alternative.

Initial discussions with the Linux Foundation about colocation possibilities have been positive, though details won't be confirmed until early April. The email seeks community feedback on both the validity of the US travel concerns and preferences between the two locations before making arrangements. This appears to be an early-stage discussion to gauge sentiment rather than a finalized plan.
