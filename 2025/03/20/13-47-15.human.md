This is an update on the Git Merge 2025 conference planning, specifically about the location decision. Taylor Blau clarifies that GitHub has confirmed they will host the event at their San Francisco headquarters in September, with exact dates still being finalized. This resolves the earlier discussion about potentially breaking the usual US/EU alternation pattern to colocate with Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam.

The email serves as a correction to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier message presenting both options, making it clear that the San Francisco venue is now secured with GitHub's support. While the thread initially raised concerns about US travel difficulties for some community members, this follow-up indicates the core organizers have moved forward with the traditional US location for this anniversary year.

Taylor promises a formal announcement with specific dates soon, suggesting the planning is in its final stages rather than still gathering community feedback. The tone is enthusiastic about celebrating Git's 20th anniversary, though some readers may still wonder whether the earlier-discussed travel concerns were adequately addressed in this decision.
