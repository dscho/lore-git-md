This is a bugfix patch that's part of a resolved thread about restoring zlib version reporting functionality. The patch adds a direct include of "git-zlib.h" to help.c to fix a regression where `git version --build-options` stopped showing the zlib version after a header reorganization in commit 41f1a8435a.

The change is minimal (just one line added) and specifically targets the version reporting functionality without introducing new dependencies or side effects. The patch maintains the existing behavior where the zlib version is only printed when ZLIB_VERSION is defined. Junio Hamano has reviewed and approved the change, noting it won't cause unintended breakage, and has queued it for inclusion.

This was the first of two patches in the series - the second added zlib-ng support. With both patches reviewed and ACKed by Patrick Steinhardt, the thread is effectively complete with no outstanding issues. The fix precisely addresses the regression while keeping the code's original behavior intact.
