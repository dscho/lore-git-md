This is the third and final patch in a series preparing for the merge-recursive to merge-ort transition, focusing on verbosity control. 

The patch adds support for verbosity level 0 in merge-ort, matching behavior that exists in merge-recursive. Some Git commands like `git am` and `git checkout` use this zero-verbosity setting to suppress conflict messages during automated operations. While callers could achieve this by bypassing certain wrappers, the patch makes it simpler by allowing verbosity=0 to work directly through the standard merge-ort wrapper interfaces.

The change is minimal (just a 2-line addition in merge-ort.c) but important for maintaining behavioral parity between the old and new merge strategies during the migration. It follows the pattern of the first two patches in the series by adding compatibility features that make merge-ort a drop-in replacement for merge-recursive. The patch appears straightforward and well-justified, completing this preparatory series from an experienced contributor working on the long-running merge strategy modernization effort.
