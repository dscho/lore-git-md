This thread introduces a new `git reflog drop` subcommand that allows users to delete entire reflogs (not just individual entries), addressing a long-standing gap in Git's reflog management capabilities. The feature was proposed by Karthik Nayak, with the implementation refined through review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano.

The series went through two iterations, with the first patch (a refactoring of builtin/reflog.c as part of the `the_repository` removal effort) being withdrawn after Junio raised architectural concerns. The final version focuses solely on the new functionality, which includes support for both single-reference deletion and repository-wide cleanup via an `--all` flag. The implementation properly accounts for worktrees and includes comprehensive test coverage.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk provided real-world validation of the feature's utility, particularly for workflows that need toggling between different core.logAllRefUpdates modes. All technical concerns were addressed, including documentation clearly distinguishing between "drop" (reflog structure), "expire" (entries), and "delete" (refs). With all review feedback incorporated and no outstanding issues, the series appears ready for merging. The withdrawn refactoring work remains an open question for future discussion as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort.
