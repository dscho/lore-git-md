This thread introduces a new `git reflog drop` subcommand that allows users to delete entire reflogs, addressing a limitation where previously only individual entries could be expired. The two-patch series comes from Karthik Nayak, with substantial review input from Patrick Steinhardt.

The first patch completed necessary refactoring in `builtin/reflog.c` as part of Git's ongoing effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The second patch implements the new functionality, supporting both single-ref deletion (`git reflog drop <ref>`) and repository-wide cleanup (`--all` flag), with careful attention to worktree awareness and error handling.

After several rounds of review addressing technical implementation, documentation clarity, and test coverage, the discussion has now concluded with final stylistic refinements to variable initialization and scoping in the `cmd_reflog_drop()` function. With all feedback incorporated and no outstanding issues, the series appears ready for inclusion, providing a useful enhancement to Git's reflog management capabilities while maintaining alignment with the project's architectural direction.
