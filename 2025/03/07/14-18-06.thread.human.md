The fix came as a two-patch series: the first (already merged) restored standard zlib version reporting by adding an include directive, while the second added explicit support for zlib-ng builds. Both patches were minimal in scope - totaling just 8 lines added - and received positive reviews from Patrick Steinhardt, who called them "obviously good." Junio Hamano merged the first patch and has now confirmed the complete series is appropriate, effectively closing the thread. The changes precisely restore the lost functionality without introducing new complexity, with test coverage verifying the fixes work for both zlib variants.
