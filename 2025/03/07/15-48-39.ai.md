This is a cover letter for a patch series preparing for the removal of merge-recursive in favor of merge-ort, part of the ongoing merge strategy modernization effort. The series introduces three small but strategic features needed to complete the transition.

The 3-patch subset being submitted here provides key building blocks for converting callers from recursive to ort: (1) a new merge_ort_generic() function that will serve as a drop-in replacement for merge_trees(), (2) the ability to disable rename detection in merge-ort (matching recursive's capability), and (3) support for zero verbosity level in merge-ort output. The author notes these are the first of five planned series totaling 19 patches for the full merge-recursive removal.

The changes touch merge strategy documentation and several merge-ort implementation files, adding about 100 lines net. The author offers to expand this series to include the first actual caller conversion (git-am.sh) if reviewers prefer to see the new merge_ort_generic() in context. This appears to be well-planned technical groundwork from an experienced contributor, with each patch serving a clear transitional purpose. The series builds on the long-running effort to make merge-ort the default strategy.
