This thread introduces a 3-patch series preparing for the removal of merge-recursive in favor of merge-ort, part of the ongoing merge strategy modernization effort led by Elijah Newren. The series provides key building blocks for converting callers from recursive to ort: (1) merge_ort_generic() as a drop-in replacement for merge_trees(), (2) disableable rename detection in merge-ort matching recursive's capability, and (3) support for zero verbosity level in merge-ort output. These changes touch merge strategy documentation and merge-ort implementation files (merge-ort.c, merge-ort.h) with ~100 net new lines. The author notes this is the first of five planned series totaling 19 patches for full merge-recursive removal, and offers to expand this subset to include the first actual caller conversion (git-am.sh) if reviewers prefer context. The work builds on the long-running effort to make merge-ort the default strategy, with these patches serving as strategic technical groundwork from an experienced contributor.
