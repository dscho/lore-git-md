This is a refactoring patch that's part of the merge strategy modernization effort, specifically preparing for the removal of merge-recursive in favor of merge-ort. 

The patch introduces a new `merge_ort_generic()` function in merge-ort-wrappers.c, providing the third and final entry point needed to match merge-recursive's API surface. This completes the trio of equivalent functions (alongside existing `merge_ort_trees()` and `merge_ort_recursive()`), allowing callers to switch from merge-recursive to merge-ort without interface changes. The implementation handles OID-to-commit conversion, merge base processing, and index locking, delegating the actual merge logic to `merge_ort_recursive()`.

Key technical details include: a new `get_ref()` helper for object references, exposure of `make_virtual_commit()` in merge-ort.h for shared use, and relaxed assertions in `merge_incore_recursive()` to accommodate builtin/am's fake ancestor use case. The changes span four files with 93 net new lines, mostly concentrated in the new wrapper implementation.

This foundational work comes from a trusted contributor (Elijah Newren) and represents a strategic step in the long-planned merge-ort transition. The patch appears carefully crafted with thorough consideration of existing callers' needs, though reviewers will likely scrutinize the error handling paths and the decision to expose `make_virtual_commit()`. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and fits cleanly into the larger series context.
