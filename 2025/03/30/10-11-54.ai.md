The suggested changes maintain the same verification logic (checking that the intersection of left and right commits is empty) while making the test work cross-platform and providing better debugging output. This is a minor but useful refinement to the test case that doesn't affect the core bugfix in revision.c, which was already established as correct in previous discussion.
