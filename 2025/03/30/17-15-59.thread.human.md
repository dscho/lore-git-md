Jean-Noël Avila's documentation modernization series converting the `git-reset`, `git-rm`, and `git-mv` man pages to modern AsciiDoc format has reached a significant milestone. The seven-patch series, now in its second version, standardizes the synopsis blocks, placeholder notation, and formatting across these commands while updating the documentation toolchain to handle special cases like triple-dot notation and character markup. 

The work has received thorough review from Martin Ågren, who identified several formatting nuances, and has now gained maintainer approval from Junio Hamano. Junio specifically highlighted the improvements to triple-dot notation handling and the beneficial update to CodingGuidelines that formally documents the character markup conventions. All substantive feedback from the first version has been addressed, with only a minor TROFF rendering quirk for special characters in bold spans remaining as a known limitation.

With the series now queued for integration, this completes a significant step in the broader documentation standardization effort. The work demonstrates careful attention to detail in both content and toolchain requirements, ensuring consistent rendering across output formats while preserving semantic meaning. The CodingGuidelines update in patch 7/7 will help future contributors follow the same conventions, making this a sustainable improvement to Git's documentation practices.
