Community infrastructure discussion: Christian Couder adds support for shutting down the problematic git-mentoring Google Group, suggesting existing alternatives alongside Discord.

This brief reply from Christian Couder reinforces the growing consensus to retire the Google Groups-based mentoring list due to its moderation issues. He concurs with Johannes Schindelin's Discord proposal while also mentioning two other established channels - the #git-devel IRC channel and the main git@vger.kernel.org mailing list - as additional options for new contributors.

The response is notable for what it doesn't say: no objections to the shutdown, no concerns about Discord's limitations, and no new alternatives beyond what's already been discussed. This suggests broad agreement with the migration plan among active participants. The mention of IRC alongside Discord indicates some contributors see multiple existing channels as sufficient replacements, though the thread has previously noted Discord's better moderation capabilities compared to IRC.

This moves the discussion closer to implementation, with remaining questions about how to document and publicize these options rather than whether to make the change. The lack of new concerns raised in this response suggests the community is converging on a solution.
