This is a platform compatibility discussion about regex behavior on Cygwin, specifically regarding the NO_REGEX setting for Cygwin 1.7 and its interaction with test t7815-grep-binary.sh. Junio Hamano responds to Ramsay Jones's earlier explanation about why the NO_REGEX patch wasn't updated sooner, questioning whether maintaining support for the decade-old Cygwin 1.7 is still worthwhile. He notes the diminishing returns of keeping compatibility for such old systems.

The discussion centers on divergent regex behavior across platforms, particularly whether the '.' pattern should match NUL bytes. Junio observes that his glibc 2.40 system doesn't match NUL with '.', while Cygwin/FreeBSD do, though he finds it curious that GNU grep and sed on his system do exhibit this NUL matching behavior. The key question is whether to standardize this behavior in Git's test suite or accept the platform divergence.

Three options are on the table for handling test t7815: keeping it as-is, disabling it for Cygwin, or removing it entirely (Ramsay's preference). Junio seems ambivalent about making this a success test, noting that while platform-dependent behavior is generally disliked, the specific NUL matching case doesn't appear harmful. The thread highlights the careful consideration needed when adjusting long-standing platform workarounds, particularly when they intersect with test behaviors. The technical change to remove NO_REGEX for Cygwin remains sound, but the test suite interaction requires resolution.
