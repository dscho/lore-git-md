This thread documents the evolution of a series improving cruft pack handling in `git repack`, led by Taylor Blau. The work transitions from the confusing `--max-cruft-size` behavior to clearer semantics with a new `--combine-cruft-below-size` option.

The series began with careful test infrastructure preparation - Taylor first reorganized 250 lines of repack tests into a more logical home (t7704-repack-cruft.sh) and refactored test descriptions to separate concerns. Elijah Newren confirmed these as straightforward improvements. Subsequent patches then introduced the behavior changes: first modifying `--max-cruft-size` to act as a strict override for pack sizes, then implementing the new `--combine-cruft-below-size` option that aggregates small cruft packs while leaving larger ones untouched.

The implementation has received thorough review. Newren provided key documentation feedback that Taylor incorporated in v2 - clarifying wording about large pack handling and making size units explicit. With these changes, Newren has now approved the series, following Junio Hamano's earlier review that prompted the size unit clarification. The careful staged approach - from test reorganization through behavior changes - and the positive reviews suggest this work is ready for merging. The new options provide clearer semantics while maintaining flexibility in cruft pack management.
