This is a follow-up discussion in the thread about introducing safer assertion macros in Git's codebase. Junio Hamano responds to the first patch's introduction of the BUG_IF_NOT() macro, suggesting alternative naming that better reflects its purpose as a safer version of assert() rather than being associated with Git's BUG() mechanism.

Key points in this email:
- Junio objects to the BUG_IF_NOT() name since it lacks the key characteristic of BUG() (being unconditional)
- Proposes several alternative names that better convey the "safer assert" purpose: ASSERT(), safer_assert(), safe_assert(), or sane_assert()
- Draws parallel to existing safe_istest() and sane_ctype[] patterns in the codebase
- The naming discussion doesn't challenge the technical approach but focuses on making the intent clearer through naming
