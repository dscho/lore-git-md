Cover letter for a series introducing a new `--combine-cruft-below-size` option for `git repack`. This is part of the ongoing work to improve cruft pack handling.

The series replaces the confusing `--max-cruft-size` behavior with a clearer alternative. The new `--combine-cruft-below-size` flag tells repack to only combine cruft packs smaller than a given threshold, rather than trying to limit output pack size (which was the problematic behavior of `--max-cruft-size`). The author acknowledges this breaks backward compatibility but argues the old behavior was too broken to preserve.

The first three patches are preparatory test cleanups, moving cruft pack tests to more logical locations and improving their clarity. The fourth patch modifies `--max-cruft-size` while keeping some old infrastructure, making the final transition in patch 5 smoother. The author notes this staged approach makes review easier despite some temporary complexity.

This appears to be a well-considered improvement to cruft pack management, building on previous discussions about the limitations of `--max-cruft-size`. The series shows careful attention to both technical implementation and reviewability, with clear commit messages and thoughtful test reorganization. The changes affect core repack behavior but seem justified given the acknowledged flaws in the current implementation.
