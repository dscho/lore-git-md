This is a platform compatibility discussion in the ongoing Cygwin regex handling series (currently at 6/12). Junio Hamano responds to Ramsay Jones's explanation about the test anomaly in t7815-grep-binary.sh where Cygwin's regex library matches NUL bytes with '.' patterns. 

Junio questions whether maintaining NO_REGEX for Cygwin 1.7 (now 10 years old) is still worthwhile, suggesting it may be past the point of diminishing returns for compatibility maintenance. He notes that while platform-dependent behavior is generally undesirable, the specific case of '.' matching NUL doesn't seem particularly harmful.

The email reveals platform divergence in regex behavior - Junio's glibc 2.40 system rejects NUL matching while Cygwin (and presumably FreeBSD) accepts it. He observes that GNU grep and sed on his system also match NUL with '.', suggesting this may be a broader pattern matching convention rather than a Cygwin-specific anomaly.

Junio frames the test case decision around whether we want to eventually make it a success test, though he seems ambivalent about the importance of standardizing this behavior. The discussion remains open-ended, with Ramsay's preference being to simply remove the test as it doesn't appear to test anything meaningful. The thread shows the careful consideration needed when adjusting long-standing platform workarounds, particularly when they intersect with test suite behaviors.
