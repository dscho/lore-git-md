This is a brief side discussion in the larger protocol v2 fetch improvements thread, touching on test infrastructure rather than the main patch series.

The exchange starts with Junio Hamano and Jeff King discussing the use of `test_commit` versus raw `git commit` in test scripts. Both agree that `test_commit` has drawbacks - it creates unnecessary working tree cruft and tags by default, and its main benefit (automatic `test_tick` advancement) is often not needed. Jeff King notes he typically uses raw `git commit` out of habit, while acknowledging this might not be the preferred style.

Taylor Blau chimes in to agree with their assessment, but notes that converting the 2,570 existing instances of `test_commit` in the codebase would be disruptive. This is a lighthearted aside in an otherwise technical thread, showing the kind of minor stylistic debates that sometimes emerge during review. The discussion doesn't affect the main patch series' technical merits, which remain fully approved.
