This is a platform compatibility discussion about Cygwin regex behavior and test suite interactions, part of a larger series (currently at 6/12 patches) revisiting Cygwin-specific workarounds.

Junio Hamano responds to Ramsay Jones's explanation about why the NO_REGEX workaround for Cygwin 1.7 wasn't updated sooner, focusing on whether it's time to drop support for this decade-old version entirely. He questions whether maintaining compatibility with Cygwin 1.7 is still worthwhile given the maintenance burden, though he leaves the decision open.

The discussion then shifts to a specific test case (t7815-grep-binary.sh) where Cygwin's FreeBSD-derived regex library behaves differently by matching NUL bytes with '.' patterns, unlike glibc systems. Junio notes that GNU grep and sed on his system show similar NUL-matching behavior, suggesting this might be a broader BSD-vs-glibc difference rather than Cygwin-specific. He remains uncertain whether the test should be kept (despite platform differences), disabled for Cygwin, or removed entirely as Ramsay prefers.

The exchange reveals the careful consideration needed when adjusting platform-specific behaviors, weighing technical correctness against practical maintenance concerns. The core build system change (limiting NO_REGEX to Cygwin 1.7) appears sound, but the test suite interaction requires resolution before finalizing the series.
