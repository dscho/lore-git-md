Taylor Blau weighs in on Phillip Wood's suggestion regarding the diff-delta.c comma operator replacement, advocating for the most conventional approach that aligns with Git's CodingGuidelines. He strongly prefers using explicit curly braces for the conditional blocks rather than either the `(void)` cast approach or single-line statements with semicolons. His key argument is that the braced form is both more readable and less prone to accidental misuse (like dropping braces for what appears to be a single statement). He dismisses compressed single-line variants as either misleading (resembling single-statement ifs) or aesthetically unappealing.

The discussion centers around maintaining the visual correlation between shift operations and bit setting while avoiding comma operators. Taylor's position reinforces earlier preferences from Jeff King and others for semicolon-separated statements within explicit blocks, though he doesn't directly reference Johannes Schindelin's vertical alignment argument. This email represents the final stylistic debate in a series that has otherwise achieved technical consensus across all platforms and subsystems.
