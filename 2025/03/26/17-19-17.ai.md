This is a status update on the incremental MIDX bitmaps feature (tb/incremental-midx-part-2), part of the ongoing performance optimization work for multi-pack indexes. Taylor Blau indicates the patch series appears ready for merging after receiving positive feedback from key reviewers (Elijah Newren and Jeff King). The series implements incremental updates for MIDX bitmaps across 14 commits, touching core bitmap handling in pack-bitmap.c, adding new EWAH iterator functionality, and updating documentation. Key technical aspects include new bitmap layer tracking, disk-usage computation for incremental MIDXs, and support for pack-reuse operations. The author notes all suggested changes from v4 reviews were incorporated in v5, though remains open to further feedback before final merge. This represents a significant step in making MIDX bitmap maintenance more efficient by avoiding full recomputation.
