Junio Hamano weighs in on the final stylistic debate in the comma operator refactoring series, proposing an alternative approach to the remaining contentious cases in `wildmatch.c` and `diff-delta.c`. While the series had reached consensus on most technical aspects, this email addresses the last open question about how to handle performance-sensitive cases where comma operators were preserved.

Hamano expresses skepticism about Johannes Schindelin's approach of using `(void)` casts to mark intentional comma operator usage, arguing it doesn't improve readability. Instead, he suggests more explicit rewrites that avoid comma operators entirely. For `wildmatch.c`, he splits the loop condition into separate statements, and for `diff-delta.c`, he converts a series of bitmask operations from comma-separated expressions to full `if` blocks.

This represents the final stylistic decision point in an otherwise complete series that has already addressed comma operator usage across multiple subsystems (networking, diff algorithms, pattern matching, and test frameworks). The changes are purely about code presentation - there's no functional difference between the approaches under discussion. The thread has seen extensive review from multiple maintainers, with only this last readability question remaining before potential merge.
