This is a historical clarification in the merge-ort conversion thread. Jeff King provides background on why rename detection was disabled in GitHub's merge implementation, explaining it was originally a temporary measure during their migration from shell scripts to libgit2 and later to merge-tree. The decision was made to minimize changes during transitions ("only change one thing at a time") rather than being a deliberate design choice. While the context is specific to GitHub's internal use, Peff notes that having a rename-disable option in merge-ort could still be generally useful. The email doesn't propose any technical changes to the ongoing series but provides important historical context about the real-world deployment considerations that motivated some of the design decisions being discussed.
