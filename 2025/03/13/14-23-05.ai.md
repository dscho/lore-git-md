Feature patch adding Verilog language support to Git's diff highlighting.

This patch introduces built-in Verilog syntax patterns to Git's userdiff mechanism, allowing for better highlighting of Verilog source files in diffs. The changes consist of two parts: adding Verilog file pattern recognition in `.gitattributes` (matching `.v` extensions) and defining Verilog-specific regex patterns in `userdiff.c` to identify module/function boundaries and language tokens.

The patterns focus on core Verilog constructs - they match module/task/function declarations for hunk headers and typical Verilog identifiers, numeric literals, and operators for word boundaries. The implementation follows the established pattern of other language drivers in the file, though the author notes they are still learning the development process and may need guidance on test cases.
