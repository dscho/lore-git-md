This is a discussion about cruft pack size thresholds, continuing the ongoing thread about Taylor Blau's implementation. Junio Hamano and Elijah Newren are debating the implications of having two separate size thresholds for cruft packs: a "combine below" size and a "max pack" size.

Junio points out a practical scenario where the two-threshold approach might not actually solve the problem it aims to address. With a 170MB cruft pack and thresholds at 180MB/200MB), trying to add a 40MB object would exceed the max size, leaving the pack stuck below the "combine below" threshold. This mirrors the original issue Taylor's series tried to solve, suggesting the two-threshold approach may not be as effective as hoped.

The exchange highlights the conceptual tension between:
1. The hard constraint of `--max-pack-size` (which must not be violated)
2. The advisory nature of `--combine-cruft-below-size` (which guides when to attempt combining)

This is part of the broader thread where Jeff King recently suggested reframing the discussion around combination efficiency rather than size limits. The technical implementation remains settled, but this email shows maintainers still working through the conceptual implications of the design choices.
