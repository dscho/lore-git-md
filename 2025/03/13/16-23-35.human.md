This is a discussion about cruft pack size thresholds in the ongoing cruft pack implementation series. Elijah Newren is responding to Junio Hamano's concerns about the proposed two-threshold system for combining cruft packs.

The key points:
- Newren clarifies that `--combine-cruft-below-size` is a guideline rather than a hard constraint like `--max-pack-size`
- He argues that even if combining isn't possible in one repack (due to large individual cruft objects), future repacks may be able to make progress when smaller cruft becomes available
- He suggests setting the combine threshold to half of max size, which would guarantee any two existing cruft packs could be combined
- The discussion reveals that the core issue is about finding a practical balance between avoiding fruitless repacks and respecting size constraints

This continues the thread's evolution from focusing on strict size enforcement to finding more flexible approaches that still solve the original problem of efficient cruft pack maintenance. The exchange shows the maintainers working through the practical implications of the threshold design.
