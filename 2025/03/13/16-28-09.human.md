Junio Hamano raises important architectural questions about Christian Couder's promisor-remote bugfix patch (v4). While acknowledging the patch fixes a NULL dereference crash, Junio questions several design aspects that remain unresolved from previous rounds.

The core concerns focus on the warning system's logic and the fundamental approach of using remote names as fallback URLs. Junio finds the explanation of warning conditions unclear, particularly challenges why empty URLs should trigger special warnings when other malformed URLs don't. He also reiterates his earlier question about why unconfigured remotes should remain as candidate loopback remotes at all.

This email represents a continuation of the architectural debate rather than a rejection of the patch - Junio had previously approved the core fix for inclusion in Git 2.49.0. The discussion now centers on whether the current design aligns with promisor-remote's specific needs versus general remote conventions, with Junio suggesting the warnings and name-fallback approach may be overcomplicating the solution. Christian will likely need to either clarify the rationale or reconsider these aspects in a future iteration.
