This is part 6/9 of the protocol v2 fetch optimization series, focusing on HEAD advertisement for config-less fetches. The patch addresses a case where fetches without explicit refspecs would fail to properly advertise the remote HEAD, which is needed for updating the local remote-tracking HEAD reference.

Jeff King and Taylor Blau discuss two technical points in this follow-up: First, they confirm that while there's a theoretical possibility of duplicate HEAD prefixes being sent to the server, in practice this can't happen due to the conditions under which each case triggers. Second, they have a brief exchange about test style - Jeff explains his preference for raw `git commit` over the `test_commit` helper in tests, noting that while `test_commit` handles test_tick automatically, it comes with some overhead and potential gotchas.

The patch itself is well-reviewed (with Taylor's Reviewed-by tag) and fits into the larger series' architectural improvements. This particular change helps ensure proper HEAD tracking behavior even in minimal fetch scenarios without configuration, while maintaining the protocol v2 optimizations that are the series' main focus. The discussion shows the careful attention to edge cases that has characterized this entire effort.
