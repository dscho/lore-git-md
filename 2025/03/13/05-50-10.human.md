This is a discussion follow-up in the protocol v2 fetch optimization series, specifically addressing patch 8/9 about HEAD advertisement behavior. Jeff King (the series author) responds to Taylor Blau's review approval, raising a design question about the `set_head()` feature's behavior with exact-oid fetches.

The core question is whether `set_head()` should be more aggressive about updating HEAD even when fetching objects that aren't refs (like exact OIDs), which would require keeping the `ls-refs` server round-trip that the series currently optimizes away in these cases. Jeff notes he doesn't have a strong opinion but wants to surface the trade-off: preserving the optimization versus more consistent HEAD behavior.

This comes after Taylor Blau completed a thorough review of the 9-patch series and gave his Reviewed-by tag, with only minor comments remaining. The discussion shows the series is nearing completion, with this being one of the last design points to resolve before finalizing the optimizations.
