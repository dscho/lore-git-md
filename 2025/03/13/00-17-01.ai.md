- Modifies `show_object()` to handle both named and unnamed cases directly
- Maintains identical output format (space-separated OID and path)
- Preserves existing newline and path cleaning behavior (stopping at first newline)
- Touches builtin/rev-list.c, revision.c, and revision.h
