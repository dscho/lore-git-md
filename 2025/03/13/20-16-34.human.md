This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about reproducible Git bundle generation, focusing on verification techniques. Simon Josefsson confirms that Jeff King's suggested approach of using `git -c pack.threads=1 repack -adf` before bundle creation successfully produces identical bundle hashes across different clones, solving the immediate reproducibility problem he encountered. 

The email shows concrete verification of this solution, with identical SHA-256 hashes produced from two separate clones of the same repository after applying the repack+bundle workflow. This appears to address the core technical challenge Simon originally reported, though he notes the solution isn't particularly ergonomic.

Simon also engages with Jeff's alternative proposal of checksumming Git's logical model rather than the bundle bytes, questioning how to interpret the output and whether it could be made bundle-compatible. However, since the repack solution works, this line of inquiry appears secondary. The exchange demonstrates practical progress toward deterministic bundle generation while acknowledging tradeoffs between bit-for-bit reproducibility and workflow complexity.
