- Updating tests to verify the new behavior, including checking that packs smaller than the threshold are combined while larger ones remain separate

- Explicitly combining all packs below the size threshold rather than building up packs until reaching the threshold
- Allowing the resulting combined pack to potentially exceed the threshold size (when aggregating multiple small packs)
- Maintaining clearer separation between size-limiting (`--max-cruft-size`) and pack-combining (`--combine-cruft-below-size`) behaviors
