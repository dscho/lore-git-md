This is a technical refinement in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX bitmap series (patch 11/13), addressing Jeff King's feedback about memory management for type bitmap arrays. The patch maintains tracking of object type bitmaps (commits, trees, blobs, tags) across MIDX layers through new arrays `*_all` in `struct bitmap_index`.

Jeff King suggested a micro-optimization to avoid allocating separate arrays for non-incremental cases by directly pointing to the single-layer bitmaps. Taylor acknowledges this could work but expresses concern about coupling the allocation/free logic to the `base` pointer's NULL state, calling it a "potential landmine." He provides a sample diff showing how the optimization could be implemented but seems to prefer the current simpler approach.

The exchange confirms that the arrays are only meaningful at the top level of MIDX chains, as lower layers don't perform bitmap computations. This is one of the final polish discussions in the series, focusing on implementation details rather than core functionality. Taylor appears open to the optimization in principle but prioritizes maintainability over micro-optimizations at this stage.
