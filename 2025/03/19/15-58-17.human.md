This is a design discussion follow-up in the NUL-delimited output feature series for `git rev-list`. Justin Tobler responds to Junio Hamano's preference for separate `--nul-delimited-input` and `--nul-delimited-output` options rather than a combined `--nul-delimited={all,input,output}` syntax.

Justin agrees with Junio's reasoning about the benefits of explicit, orthogonal options at the plumbing level, despite the increased verbosity. He plans to modify the next version of the patch series to make `-z` only affect output (matching `git log` behavior) and suggests a potential follow-up series to add the separate input/output options.

The email also notes an inconsistency in delimiter-related option naming across Git commands (`--null` vs `-z` in `git commit`), suggesting that the new `--nul-delimited-*` options could provide a more consistent naming scheme going forward. This appears to be a thoughtful response that accepts the maintainer's design guidance while identifying a broader opportunity for standardization.
