Performance optimization discussion in MIDX bitmap series.

This email from Taylor Blau responds to Jeff King's concern about potential performance impact from unconditionally using `ewah_or_iterator` for type bitmap operations in the MIDX bitmap implementation. The change adds one extra allocation and function call per iteration, but Taylor's benchmarks show no statistically significant difference in `pack-objects` performance between the current implementation and the modified version.

The email confirms that the optimization is safe to proceed with, as the microbenchmark shows the change to be performance-neutral (with a 1ms difference well within noise levels). This is part of the final refinements in the 13-patch series implementing incremental MIDX bitmaps, where most technical concerns have already been addressed and the series is effectively complete.
