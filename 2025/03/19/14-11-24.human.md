This is a feature proposal for `git bisect` to support multiple worktrees during bisection.

Jim Cromie suggests enhancing `git bisect` to allow switching between two or more worktrees while searching for a regression. The key motivation is maintaining both "good" and "bad" states in separate worktrees for easier comparison and forensic analysis. The author speculates this could enable more sophisticated debugging tools like `rr` to perform side-by-side record/replay comparisons.

The proposal also floats a secondary idea for a `git bisect try` command that would let users test specific hunches (like `HEAD~20`) rather than following the standard bisection algorithm. This would be the inverse of `skip` - testing a commit at the user's direction rather than the tool's calculation.

The email is light on implementation details but presents an interesting conceptual direction for making bisection more flexible and powerful, particularly for complex debugging scenarios where comparing states is valuable. The ideas seem speculative at this stage rather than fully formed, but could spark useful discussion about bisect workflow improvements.
