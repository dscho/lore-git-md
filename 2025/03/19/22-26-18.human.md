This is a brief follow-up in the safer assertions thread, where Taylor Blau and Elijah Newren discuss documentation placement for the new assertion checking system. 

Taylor had suggested adding guidance about side-effect-free assertions to Documentation/CodingGuidelines, noting that developers might be surprised when CI fails on assertions that pass local testing. Elijah responds by pointing out that many other CI checks (like coccinelle patches, fuzz tests, and platform-specific builds) also aren't covered in CodingGuidelines, which he views as primarily for non-automatable rules. He suggests SubmittingPatches might be a better place for this kind of test coverage documentation.

The exchange is cordial and resolves the minor documentation question, with Taylor accepting Elijah's reasoning. This doesn't introduce any new technical content or changes to the patch - it's just a wrap-up of a documentation side discussion in the now-complete series about safer assertions.
