This is the final discussion in a series implementing a new `git reflog drop` subcommand. The thread has reached consensus on merging the current implementation despite some acknowledged UX quirks in the `--single-worktree` flag behavior.

The key points settled in this email:
1. The maintainers (Christian Couder and Junio Hamano) agree the current behavior - where `--single-worktree` silently does nothing without `--all` - is suboptimal but acceptable for now, as it matches existing `git reflog expire` behavior.
2. Two paths forward are identified for future improvement: either fix `expire` first then align `drop`, or leave both as-is for consistency. Christian is open to either approach.
3. The naming of `--single-worktree` (versus `--current-worktree`) remains an open but non-blocking discussion point.

The series is now considered merge-ready, with these UX polish items potentially addressed later as follow-up work. This represents the final approval before inclusion in Git's codebase, with all technical concerns from Patrick Steinhardt, Christian Couder, and Toon Claes having been addressed in previous rounds.
