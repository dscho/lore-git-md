- Validation that the solution needs to address both the clone regression and the architectural flaw where direct `advise()` calls bypass suppression
- Plans to audit all advice calls throughout the codebase

The discussion has moved beyond just fixing the bundle clone case to designing a more systematic approach to advice suppression.
