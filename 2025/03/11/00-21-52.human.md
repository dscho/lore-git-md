This is the final patch in Taylor Blau's v4 series optimizing cruft pack behavior, specifically addressing object freshening when dealing with cruft packs that exceed size thresholds. The patch fixes a subtle edge case where objects in large cruft packs couldn't be properly freshened (updated with newer mtimes) during repacking.

The core issue arises when Git splits cruft packs by size: objects in packs that exceed the threshold remain frozen, making it impossible to update their mtimes through normal repacking. The solution introduces a new `ignore_packed_keep_in_core_has_cruft` flag and `want_cruft_object_mtime()` helper to carefully compare mtimes when deciding whether to repack objects, allowing freshening even for objects in retained cruft packs when needed.

The patch includes thorough test coverage demonstrating the fix works for both packed and loose objects, with careful mtime comparisons. This completes the series' goal of robust cruft pack handling while maintaining the ability to freshen objects regardless of pack size thresholds. The changes are well-contained to pack-objects.c with supporting infrastructure in packfile.[ch], and the test additions follow the series' established patterns.
