This is a proposal to change Git's internal timestamp representation from unsigned to signed integers. The email notes that `timestamp_t` is currently defined as `uintmax_t` (unsigned), while most platforms' native `time_t` is signed. This mismatch causes compiler warnings during comparisons and prevents Git from handling dates before 1970 (though the author acknowledges this is unlikely to be needed in practice).

The change would require updating numerous references to `timestamp_t` throughout the codebase. The author is seeking feedback on whether this change is worth pursuing before undertaking the work. While the technical rationale seems sound (aligning with platform conventions and eliminating warnings), the proposal acknowledges there might be hidden assumptions in the code about timestamp values never being negative. 

This appears to be a well-reasoned but potentially invasive change that would need careful review to avoid introducing subtle bugs. The author is taking the right approach by soliciting feedback before implementation.
