Junio Hamano's response to the promisor-remote NULL URL fix discussion. This is a follow-up in the ongoing thread about handling NULL pointers in the promisor-remote subsystem.

Hamano agrees with Christian Couder's assessment that broader questions about strvec/xstrdup NULL handling semantics should be deferred until after the 2.49.0 release, while acknowledging the immediate need for the promisor-remote hotfix. He expresses some surprise that the original bug made it into the 'seen' branch.

The email focuses on API design philosophy, noting that allowing NULLs in strvec_push() may not align with its purpose of emulating argc/argv[] behavior. Hamano rejects the idea of modifying xstrdup() itself to handle NULLs, calling it "way too low a level" for such checks. The discussion remains technical but clearly separates the immediate bugfix from longer-term API design considerations.

This response indicates the promisor-remote fix is likely to proceed as-is for 2.49.0, while leaving the door open for future discussion about strvec behavior. The tone suggests approval of Couder's pragmatic approach to handling the urgent issue first.
