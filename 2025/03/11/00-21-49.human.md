This is a performance optimization patch in the cruft pack series, simplifying how small cruft packs are aggregated during repacking. 

Taylor Blau proposes a simpler approach to combining cruft packs when they're below a size threshold. The current implementation carefully sums pack sizes to stay under the limit, but the patch notes we can just combine all packs smaller than the threshold and let `pack-objects` handle any necessary splitting afterward. This removes some complex sorting and accumulation logic from `builtin/repack.c` while maintaining the same overall behavior.

The change does slightly alter the pack selection order - instead of combining the smallest packs first, it now combines all eligible packs opportunistically. This makes the removed test case (which verified the smallest-first behavior) no longer relevant, so it's deleted along with the now-unused comparison function.

This appears to be a straightforward cleanup that builds on the established cruft pack infrastructure. The change is well with the thread's trajectory of simplifying and optimizing cruft pack handling, and follows the pattern of thorough test coverage seen in previous patches. Since the core object freshening behavior was already settled in earlier versions, this focuses purely on the size aggregation logic.
