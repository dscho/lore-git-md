This is a brief follow-up in the cruft pack object freshening thread, confirming the withdrawal of a problematic simplification patch from the series.

Taylor Blau acknowledges that after re-reading the earlier discussion with Patrick Steinhardt, the first patch in the v2 series (which would have removed size-based cruft pack aggregation) should not be included. The thread had already established that this simplification could lead to pathological repacking cycles when `--max-cruft-size` approaches `--max-pack-size`.

The author states they will send a cleaned-up v3 series containing only the object freshening fix (Patch 2), which has already received thorough review and approval. This email serves as confirmation that the series is being reduced to just the uncontroversial portion, making it ready for maintainer consideration in its simplified form.

The technical discussion appears fully resolved at this point, with all participants in agreement about maintaining the existing size-tracking logic for cruft packs while proceeding with the object freshening improvements.
