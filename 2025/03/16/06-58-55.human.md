**Refactoring patch removing obsolete include as part of merge-recursive cleanup**

This is the first of five preparatory patches from Elijah Newren in a series aimed at removing the legacy merge-recursive implementation. The change is straightforward: it removes an unnecessary `#include "merge-recursive.h"` from builtin/stash.c.

The header became obsolete after stash was switched to use `merge_ort_nonrecursive()` back in 2022 (commit 874cf2a60444). As part of the ongoing effort to eliminate merge-recursive entirely, this patch cleans up the last vestiges of its usage in the stash code.

The change is purely mechanical and behavior-preserving - it simply removes a now-unused header. The patch is well-motivated and clearly explained, with a direct reference to the commit that made this include unnecessary. As the first step in a larger cleanup effort, it sets the stage for more substantial changes to come in the series.
