This is a review ack for a refactoring series that eliminates comma operator usage in Git's codebase. Phillip Wood gives his approval to Johannes Schindelin's v3 patch series, noting that the range-diff looks good and thanking Johannes for making the codebase clearer.

The series has gone through several iterations with input from multiple maintainers (Eric Sunshine, Patrick Steinhardt, and Phillip Wood himself). Key changes in this final version include:
- Portable `sed` syntax for compiler detection
- Conversion of comma operators to explicit statements in `diff-delta.c` following consensus preference
- Replacement of comma operators with `goto` in `wildmatch.c` for better readability
- Full integration of `-Wcomma` warnings in both Makefile and Meson builds

The review indicates all technical concerns have been addressed and the series is ready for integration. This represents the culmination of a thorough cleanup effort spanning multiple Git subsystems (HTTP transport, rebase, pattern matching, diff algorithms, regex, and test framework) while maintaining exact behavior.
