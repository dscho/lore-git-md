This is a style suggestion in the ongoing refactoring series to eliminate comma operators from Git's codebase. Chris Torek responds to Taylor Blau's work on `diff-delta.c` with an alternative approach to the pattern of conditionally writing bytes and setting flags.

Rather than using comma operators or the previously agreed-upon explicit curly braces approach, Torek proposes a macro-based solution that would:
1. Encapsulate the repeated pattern in a `do-while` macro
2. Eliminate both comma operators and `(void)` casts
3. Make the bit-shift logic more explicit through parameterization

The suggestion comes late in a well-settled thread where the curly-brace style had already reached consensus, making this more of a thought experiment than a likely change. Torek acknowledges it might be "overkill" while offering it as a potentially cleaner alternative to the current pattern. The macro approach would trade some verbosity for complete elimination of comma operators and improved pattern visibility, though it introduces macro complexity that may not be justified for just four similar statements.
