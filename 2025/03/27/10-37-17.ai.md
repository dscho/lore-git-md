This is part 19/20 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to eliminate Perl dependencies in Git's test suite, specifically targeting the pack delta depth tests in t5316. The patch refactors the `max_chain()` helper function to use awk instead of Perl for extracting delta chain length from `git index-pack` output.

The change replaces a Perl one-liner that parsed "chain length = N" lines with an awk implementation that captures the fourth field and strips colons. This allows removing the PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisite from four test cases that verify delta chain behavior under different `--depth` settings. The tests confirm that packing produces expected chain lengths (including zero-length chains when deltas are disabled) and that the refactored helper maintains the same behavior as the original Perl version.

This continues the series' pattern of direct command-to-command replacements (Perl → awk here) while preserving test behavior. The change is straightforward with no apparent edge cases, building on the established approach used in prior parts of the series. The patch maintains the test's existing validation of packing heuristics reliability and delta chain length verification.
