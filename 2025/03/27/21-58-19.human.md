**Implementation sharing in the git-blame-tree debate**

Taylor Blau has shared GitHub's production implementation of the Bloom filter-based `git-blame-tree` approach, marking a key moment in the ongoing discussion between competing implementations. The code is available in the 'tb/blame-tree' branch of Taylor's repository, though it comes with several caveats: it's a raw dump of over a decade's work from GitHub's fork, lacks test passing (specifically t9932), and needs significant cleanup before proper review.

The email confirms the Bloom filter approach's core advantage - using changed-path filters to skip unnecessary tree-diff computations when no unblamed paths were modified. Taylor also notes that Derrick Stolee's earlier caching work for blame-tree still requires the max-depth diff functionality that Jeff King questioned.

This sharing addresses the thread's critical blocker, though the implementation's rough state means substantial work remains before it can be fairly compared against Toon Claes' nearly complete pathspec trie series. The email hints at the implementation's long evolution at GitHub while being careful to credit Victoria Dye's work in splitting the topics and disclaim potential mangling of others' contributions.
