This is a discussion about documentation formatting in Git's manual pages, continuing the thread about adding blank lines around AsciiDoc block delimiters to prevent translation issues.

Brian m. carlson responds positively to Jean-Noël Avila's initial patch, agreeing that the blank line addition is a reasonable solution to the problem where Chinese translations could accidentally create section headers from block delimiters. They note that Chinese text's compact nature (fewer Unicode code points per word) makes this issue particularly likely to occur.

The discussion briefly touches on a potential future direction of converting all AsciiDoc files to ATX-style headers (using leading = characters) instead of the current setext-style (underlined) format. While carlson is personally open to this change and confirms it works with both Python Asciidoc and Asciidoctor, they speculate that Junio Hamano might be hesitant due to the churn it would create. They suggest that difficulty remembering the underline hierarchy for deeper heading levels could be a compelling argument for switching styles.

The exchange remains constructive and focused on documentation quality, with no objections raised to the immediate patch while leaving room for future style discussions.
