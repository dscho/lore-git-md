Platform compatibility patch for Cygwin builds. This is part 8 of a 12-patch series (no cover letter seen for context).

Ramsay Jones adds `HAVE_CLOCK_GETTIME` and `HAVE_CLOCK_MONOTONIC` to the Cygwin section of `config.mak.uname`, bringing the make-based build in line with what autoconf and meson already do for this platform. The change is straightforward - Cygwin does support these timing functions, so this just ensures consistent feature availability across all build systems.

The patch is small and focused, with a platform-specific scope. It follows the pattern of other Cygwin-specific settings in the same file. While not earth-shattering, it's the kind of incremental platform improvement that helps maintain build consistency.
