Platform compatibility patch for Cygwin builds. This is part 7 of a 12-patch series (though no cover letter was provided for context).

Ramsay Jones notes that Cygwin has supported the `getdelim()` function since at least 2011, and both autoconf and meson builds already detect and use it. This patch aligns the make-based build system by adding `HAVE_GETDELIM = YesPlease` to the Cygwin section of `config.mak.uname`.

The change is straightforward and appears well-justified - it brings consistency across build systems for a function that's clearly been available on Cygwin for over a decade. The patch is minimal (just a single line addition) and follows the existing pattern for Cygwin-specific configuration.
