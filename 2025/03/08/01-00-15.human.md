Bugfix for a rare edge case in rename detection when using both break detection and `--follow` together.

This patch fixes a BUG() that could occur when using `-B` (break detection) and `--follow` simultaneously in Git commands like `git log`. The issue stems from an assumption made in commit 9db2ac56168e (2020) about how broken rename pairs would be processed - specifically that the destination half of a broken pair would always be processed immediately after the source half. The `--follow` option violates this assumption by sometimes skipping the destination file.

The bug lay dormant for nearly 4 years before being reported. The fix adds additional safety checks around the recorded indices for broken pairs. The patch includes a test case that demonstrates the issue (though interestingly, it only fails under valgrind due to uninitialized memory access in normal runs).

This is a relatively obscure corner case, but important to fix since it could lead to incorrect results or crashes when these two options are combined. The patch comes from experienced contributor Elijah Newren and includes a clear explanation of both the problem and solution.
