This is part 15/20 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Perl optional in Git's test suite, specifically targeting the test helper infrastructure. The patch refactors the `name_from_description()` function in t/lib-t6000.sh from Perl to a combination of sed and tr commands, allowing removal of PERL_TEST_HELPERS prerequisites from two test scripts (t6002 and t6003) that use this library.

The conversion follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, replacing Perl's pattern matching and case conversion with equivalent sed operations. The key changes are:
- Replaces Perl's `s///` substitutions with sed's `-e` expressions
- Maintains identical functionality for converting descriptions to names by:
  * Replacing non-alphanumeric chars with dashes
  * Collapsing multiple dashes
  * Trimming leading/trailing dashes
  * Lowercasing letters
- Removes PERL_TEST_HELPERS checks from t6002-rev-list-bisect.sh and t6003-rev-list-topo-order.sh
- Keeps the same test coverage while eliminating Perl dependency

This continues the series' systematic approach to Perl removal, though reviewers may scrutinize the sed implementation style as they did with patch 11/20. The change is mechanical and maintains behavior, following the series' successful pattern of verified conversions.
