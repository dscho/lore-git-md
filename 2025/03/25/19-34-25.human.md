Maintainer approval for comma operator cleanup patches.

Junio Hamano (Git's maintainer) acknowledges Johannes Schindelin's two patches that remove unnecessary comma operator usage in `remote-curl.c` and `builtin/rebase.c`. He notes these particular cases looked particularly questionable - so much so that they might have raised suspicions of hidden malicious behavior if not for the clear explanation. The changes simply replace commas with semicolons where the expression-chaining behavior of the comma operator served no purpose.

Hamano confirms he will include these in the queue for merging, marking the first formal maintainer approval in this thread about comma operator cleanup. This comes after technical discussion from other contributors (including Jeff King) about where such changes are appropriate versus cases where the comma operator serves legitimate purposes in performance-sensitive code. The two patches being approved represent straightforward, uncontroversial fixes where the comma usage was clearly unintentional.
