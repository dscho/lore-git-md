Johannes Schindelin's series refactoring comma operator usage in Git's codebase is nearing completion, with all major technical questions resolved and only minor stylistic polish remaining. The series has unified the codebase around Taylor Blau's recommended style of explicit curly braces, while also addressing build system integration and platform compatibility.

The most recent discussion focused on a subtle `sed` syntax issue affecting BSD/macOS systems in the compiler detection logic. Eric Sunshine and Jeff King confirmed that BSD-lineage `sed` implementations require explicit terminators after the `q` command, unlike GNU sed. They settled on `sed -n '/ version /{p;q;}'` as the robust cross-platform solution, ensuring proper behavior when compiler version strings appear after copyright notices or other preamble text.

All key contributors have signed off on their respective areas: Phillip Wood reviewed the rebase.c changes, Jeff King approved the build system updates, and Junio Hamano has already queued the remote-curl.c modifications. The only remaining open item is Chris Torek's macro-based alternative for diff-delta.c, which Taylor Blau is considering alongside his preferred braced-block approach. With platform-specific edge cases now fully addressed and consensus established on the core changes, this series appears ready for final integration once these last stylistic decisions are made.
