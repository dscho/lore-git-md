This is part 15/20 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to make Perl optional in Git's test suite, focusing on refactoring test helper functions. The patch converts the `name_from_description()` helper from Perl to a combination of `sed` and `tr`, allowing two test scripts (`t6002-rev-list-bisect.sh` and `t6003-rev-list-topo-order.sh`) to drop their Perl dependencies.

The conversion follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, replacing Perl's pattern matching and case conversion with equivalent `sed` operations. The helper function transforms test descriptions into sanitized names by converting non-alphanumeric characters to dashes, compressing multiple dashes, trimming leading/trailing dashes, and lowercasing letters.

This change is part of the broader effort to reduce Perl dependencies in the test suite, which has already achieved 97% test capability without Perl. The patch appears mechanically sound, following the same conversion methodology that has been validated in previous patches of the series. Reviewers have been actively engaged in verifying these conversions throughout the series.
