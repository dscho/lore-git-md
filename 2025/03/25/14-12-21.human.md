Follow-up in comma operator cleanup thread. Johannes Schindelin responds to Philip Oakley's question about how the unnecessary comma operators were discovered in the original patches. Schindelin reveals he used GitHub's CodeQL static analysis tool to identify these cases, mentioning he previously collaborated with the CodeQL team to add this detection capability during his time at GitHub. This provides useful context about the tooling behind the stylistic changes, though doesn't change the nature of the patches themselves (which remain simple, uncontroversial refactorings). The thread continues to show consensus around these changes, now with additional insight into the detection methodology.
