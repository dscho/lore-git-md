Bugfix for vimdiff mergetool layout configuration. This patch fixes a specific limitation where the "@" marker (used to designate which window's contents should become the final conflict resolution) worked for LOCAL, BASE, and MERGED windows but not for REMOTE. The change is straightforward - just adding REMOTE to the conditional check that determines the final target window.

The commit message does an excellent job explaining the problem with clear examples of both working and non-working configurations. The fix itself is minimal (3 lines added) and focused, with the impact being limited to users who specifically configure their vimdiff layout to target the REMOTE window. This appears to be a clean, well-justified fix for a known limitation, completing the symmetry of the layout targeting feature. The patch is part of a 2-patch series, with the second patch presumably adding tests (as indicated in the thread summary).
