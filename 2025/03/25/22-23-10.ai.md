Bugfix for vimdiff mergetool layout configuration, specifically the "@" marker targeting REMOTE window. This is the first patch in a two-part series addressing a known limitation where the "@" symbol could target LOCAL, BASE, or MERGED windows but failed to work for REMOTE. The patch adds the missing case to detect "@REMOTE" in the layout string, making it consistent with other window targets. The change is minimal (3 lines added) and well-contained in the vimdiff mergetool script. The commit message provides clear examples of both working and previously-broken configurations, showing the practical impact. The fix is straightforward with low risk, and comes with proper attribution to both the reporter and suggester. The accompanying test patch (2/2) will verify this behavior, making this a complete solution. This affects users who customize their vimdiff merge layouts to edit the REMOTE buffer directly.
