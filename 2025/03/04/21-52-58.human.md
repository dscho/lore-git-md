This is a discussion about cruft pack aggregation behavior in `git repack`. Elijah Newren is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about the simplified approach to combining small cruft packs.

The conversation focuses on potential long-term effects of the new strategy that combines all small cruft packs (below `--max-cruft-size`) rather than carefully aggregating them up to the size limit. Patrick worries this might lead to perpetual repacking cycles where small packs are constantly being recombined.

Elijah clarifies that this would only be an issue if `--max-cruft-size` approaches or exceeds `--max-pack-size`, which would be an atypical configuration. In normal usage where `--max-pack-size` is much larger, the simplified approach should work well, with any oversized combinations being split by `pack-objects` automatically.
