Hisham Hasan, a computer science student looking to participate in Google Summer of Code 2025 with Git, has reached out to the mailing list seeking guidance on how to begin contributing. He proposed the idea of "nanoprojects" - even smaller than the existing microprojects - as a way to familiarize himself with the codebase, while also being open to starting directly with microprojects if preferred.

In response, Usman Akinyemi provided practical onboarding advice, explaining that while there are no formally defined nanoprojects, many existing microprojects are already well-suited for newcomers. He pointed Hisham to two key resources for getting started: the "MyFirstContribution" documentation on git-scm.com and the INSTALL file in the Git repository. The discussion remains at the orientation stage, with Hisham still exploring potential technical areas to focus on, but the community is engaging helpfully to guide this new contributor through the initial steps of becoming involved with Git development.
