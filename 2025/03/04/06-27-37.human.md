This is a review response in the ref consistency checks patch series discussion. Patrick Steinhardt gives his approval to shejialuo's 9-part series that enhances `git fsck` with various packed-refs validation checks, but raises a timing concern about one particular change.

The series (which has already incorporated feedback from multiple reviewers) adds several validation steps for packed-refs files, including format checks, refname sanity checks, and header verification. The comment reflects the careful balance between improving validation and maintaining stability. Patrick leaves the decision open, noting he might be "overly cautious" about the timing.
