This is a documentation patch discussion about naming conventions for build-time flags.

Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on the ongoing discussion about how to name the conditional build flag for deprecated repository layout features. The thread had previously established that the current `without-breaking-changes` naming leads to awkward double negatives in both documentation (`ifdef::!without-breaking-changes[]`) and test conditions (`!WITHOUT_BREAKING_CHANGES`).

Steinhardt confirms Junio Hamano's earlier observation that the naming is suboptimal, noting that he also wasn't happy with the double negation when he saw it. He goes a step further by offering to rename the test prerequisite as well for consistency, showing willingness to align both the documentation and test infrastructure naming.

The discussion remains focused on improving readability while maintaining consistency between different parts of the codebase. There appears to be emerging consensus that a positive formulation (likely `with-breaking-changes`) would be clearer, though no specific alternative has been formally proposed yet. The technical approach of conditionally including deprecated feature documentation remains uncontested.
