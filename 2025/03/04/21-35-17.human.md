This is a final version of a two-patch series addressing object freshening behavior in multi-cruft pack scenarios. The series has gone through review and incorporates all feedback, with only minor textual updates to commit messages since the previous version.

The first patch simplifies cruft pack aggregation logic in `builtin/repack.c`, replacing complex size-based sorting with direct combination of all small packs. While this changes behavior slightly by making pack combination more aggressive, the author and reviewers agree this is a worthwhile simplification.

The second patch fixes a subtle edge case where objects in retained cruft packs couldn't be freshened if their mtime was newer than the version being packed. The solution introduces new logic in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to properly handle mtime comparisons when deciding whether to retain objects from existing cruft packs. The patch includes extensive test coverage in `t/t7704-repack-cruft.sh`.

While the technical implementation appears sound and well-tested, Patrick Steinhardt has raised a new question about whether the simplified aggregation approach might lead to perpetual repacking cycles in the long term. This operational consideration hasn't been fully addressed yet, though the core correctness of the patches isn't in doubt.

The series is otherwise ready for maintainer consideration, representing a thoughtful solution to a tricky edge case in cruft pack handling during repacks. The author has clearly put significant effort into testing and documenting the behavior changes.
