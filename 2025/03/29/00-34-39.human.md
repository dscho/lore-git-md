This is a response to Junio's "What's Cooking" update about the `ib/diff-S-G-with-longhand` series, which introduces long-form `--patch-grep` and `--patch-modifies` alternatives to the short `-G` and `-S` diff options. They're asking for guidance on how to properly handle such cases where a contributor may have gone inactive without completing the process. The message is considerate of Junio's vacation time, hoping others might chime in with procedural advice.

The underlying technical work appears uncontroversial - the "core part looked mostly good" in Junio's assessment - but the patch series seems stuck on contributor-process questions rather than technical ones. This could be a useful case study for how the project handles contributions that need shepherding when original authors become unavailable.
