The thread shows the project's pragmatic approach: even well-developed technical solutions will be abandoned when they hit insurmountable platform constraints. The exchange serves as the formal endpoint for this particular Rust integration effort, with all five patches being withdrawn after reaching this dead end.

No new technical content is introduced here - it's the project maintainer accepting the technical reality that Windows symlink limitations prevent this approach from moving forward, despite the significant work already invested in the solution.
