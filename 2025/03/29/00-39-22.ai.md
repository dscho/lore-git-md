This is the final approval and queueing of the refactoring series to eliminate comma operators in Git's codebase. Junio Hamano confirms the series is ready for integration after receiving Phillip Wood's positive review of the final changes. The series has undergone multiple rounds of review addressing all technical concerns across subsystems including networking (remote-curl.c), porcelain (rebase.c), diff algorithms (kwset.c/xdiff/diff-delta.c), pattern matching (wildmatch.c/regex), and test infrastructure. The series now covers all platforms and compiler variants including a fix for Clang/CUDA edge cases, with explicit exceptions documented for imported regex code. With no outstanding technical or stylistic questions remaining, Junio indicates the work will be queued for the next release.
