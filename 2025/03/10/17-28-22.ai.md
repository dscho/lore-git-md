This is a review comment on the "PATH WALK II" patch series introducing path-based delta compression. Junio Hamano responds to Derrick Stolee's performance data showing significant improvements from the new --path-walk feature, particularly for repositories with many hash collisions.

Key points from the response:
- Expresses surprise that name-hash-v2 shows little improvement over v1 in the kernel repo tests, contrary to expectations
- Appreciates the real-world validation from Microsoft's internal monorepo where path-walk reduced push sizes by 100x
- Notes the tradeoff with bitmap/delta-island features but sees value in the approach
- Hopes the various delta compression strategies can eventually be consolidated into simpler user-facing choices rather than requiring manual knob-twiddling

The response indicates general approval of the direction while flagging the kernel repo results as unexpected. It positions this as part of an ongoing evolution of delta compression strategies, with path-walk showing particular promise for collision-heavy repositories. The mention of Microsoft's production use case lends credibility to the approach's real-world benefits.
