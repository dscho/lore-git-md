This is a design discussion follow-up in the completed NUL-delimited output format series for `git rev-list`. Junio Hamano responds to Justin Tobler's implementation details with suggestions for simplifying and unifying the output format.

Junio questions the need for double NUL delimiters in the proposed format, suggesting they may be unnecessary. He proposes a more unified structure where all output variants (regular objects, --objects, and --missing) share a common pattern: `<oid> NUL [<attr>=<value> NUL]...`. Key aspects of his proposal include:

1. Records would be implicitly delimited by the start of the next record (identified by a new hex-only oid)
2. Paths would be encoded as `path=<path>` attributes
3. Missing object markers would become `missing=yes` attributes
4. The format remains extensible for future attributes

This represents a significant simplification from the current patch series' approach, eliminating the special cases and double-NUL markers while maintaining machine-parseability. The suggestion comes after the series was already completed, indicating potential for a follow-up iteration if the authors find the unified approach preferable. The discussion focuses purely on output format design, with no concerns raised about the implementation quality or test coverage from the original series.
