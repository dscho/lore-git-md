This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to eliminate `the_repository` global variable usage, specifically targeting the object-file conversion subsystem. The patch updates all callers (in builtin/tag.c, commit.c, and object-file.c) to pass `the_repository` where needed, maintaining backward compatibility while removing the implicit dependency.

This follows the established pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, systematically converting another subsystem to work with explicit repository contexts.
