This thread introduces a new `--path-walk` option for `pack-objects` and `repack` that improves delta compression by considering file paths when grouping objects, rather than just their names. The approach is particularly strong for large repositories and shallow clones, with Microsoft's internal tests showing dramatic size reductions in path-similar cases (up to 100x) and measurable improvements in real-world scenarios like the FluentUI repository.
