This thread introduces a new path-based delta compression feature for Git's pack-objects and repack commands, developed by Derrick Stolee. The series adds a `--path-walk` option and `pack.usePathWalk` configuration to improve compression by considering file paths when selecting delta candidates, rather than just object names. The implementation shows significant performance gains - up to 16.4% speedup for large packs and 57.7% improvement in repack operations.

After thorough review from Taylor Blau, who focused on interface details and documentation polish, the series has been approved by Junio Hamano for inclusion in the 'next' branch. Taylor has now completed reviewing all 13 patches, confirming the technical implementation is sound while providing suggestions for final refinements. The most notable optimizations include a two-phase compression approach (first within paths, then across paths) and special handling for shallow clones. Scalar will enable this feature by default given its benefits for large repositories.

With all review feedback addressed and performance validated across multiple scenarios, the series appears ready for integration. The remaining steps would be either a v3 reroll incorporating Taylor's final suggestions or direct application to 'next' if the changes are minor enough. This represents a significant optimization for Git's object packing, particularly benefits large monorepos and Scalar users.
