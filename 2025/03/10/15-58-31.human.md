This is a documentation refinement in Christian Couder's signed commits series, addressing feedback from Elijah Newren. The patch clarifies warnings about using 'verbatim' mode with signed tags in `git-fast-export`, explaining that signatures may become invalid not just from user transformations but also from internal canonicalization during export/import.

The key improvement expands the warning to note that signatures can be invalidated by any transformation affecting tags *or their commit history*, including those performed automatically by fast-export/import (like permission normalization or tree sorting). This makes the documentation more accurate about when verbatim mode is truly safe to use.

The change is a direct response to Newren's review, with Couder agreeing the stronger wording better captures the risks.
