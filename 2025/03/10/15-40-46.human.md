Maintainer follow-up on unreachable code warning discussion. Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's discovery that enabling `-Wunreachable-code` causes OSX CI failures due to `sigfillset()` in `run-command.c`. The warning triggers because the compiler can statically determine `sigfillset()` never fails on OSX, making the error handling code unreachable.

Junio acknowledges the awkward tradeoff between clean warnings and practical code maintenance, suggesting the warning might be more trouble than it's worth. He reacts negatively to Jeff's proposed workaround (checking `errno` rather than the return value) as being too subtle, and seems to lean toward not enabling the warning after all, though he doesn't explicitly reject it. The tone suggests frustration with the limitations of compiler warnings rather than disagreement with Jeff's technical analysis.

This email effectively puts the `-Wunreachable-code` proposal on hold, though it leaves open whether to abandon it entirely or find a different solution. The discussion remains connected to the earlier ref transaction series through the warning's role in catching a real bug in that code.
