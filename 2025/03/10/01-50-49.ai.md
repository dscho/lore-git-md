This is part 07/13 of the path-walk delta compression series, adding the `--path-walk` option to `git repack`. The patch exposes the pack-objects path-walk functionality through repack, allowing direct comparison of repacking strategies and including the option in performance tests.

Key technical details:
- Adds `--path-walk` flag to `git repack` which passes through to pack-objects
- Updates repack documentation to explain the option and its incompatibility with delta-islands and filter options
- Modifies p5313 performance tests to include path-walk measurements alongside name-hash versions
- Includes performance data showing path-walk's benefits in various repositories (FluentUI, Git, Node.js, Linux kernel)
- Notes current limitations: sequential operation (no threading yet) and some cases where path-walk performs worse than name-hash-v2

The performance results demonstrate path-walk's advantages in collision-heavy repos (FluentUI shows 439MB → 142MB reduction) while maintaining benefits even in well-structured repos (Linux kernel shows 2.5GB → 2.2GB). The patch maintains the series' focus on measurable improvements while acknowledging areas for future optimization (threading) and edge cases where combining with name-hash-v2 may be beneficial.
