Series introducing path-based delta compression via --path-walk option and pack.usePathWalk config has completed technical review with all feedback addressed across three iterations, now including threaded path-walk compression optimization. Core implementation replaces name-hash grouping with path-based delta selection featuring optimizations for shallow clones through edge_aggressive mode and now parallel processing.
