This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about enabling `-Wunreachable-code` warnings in developer builds. The thread has revealed platform-specific complications, particularly with OSX builds failing due to `sigfillset()` in `run-command.c` being statically determined as never failing.

Jeff King responds to Junio Hamano's frustration about the warning-induced maintenance burden. While they could work around the specific `sigfillset()` case with a subtle `errno`-based solution, both agree it's an ugly hack. King suggests an alternative approach: enabling the warning selectively in just one Linux-based CI job (like linux-sha256) to gather more data on how often such false positives occur, rather than either abandoning the warning entirely or dealing with platform-specific workarounds.

The discussion highlights the tension between code quality tools and practical maintenance concerns, with the maintainer leaning toward accepting that some warnings may not be worth the cost. The thread remains open on whether to pursue selective CI enablement or drop the warning altogether.
