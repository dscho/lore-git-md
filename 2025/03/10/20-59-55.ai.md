This is a design discussion in the ongoing NUL-delimited output format thread, with Junio Hamano providing feedback on the implementation approach.

Junio questions the current double-NUL delimiter design in the `rev-list -z` output format, suggesting a simpler single-NUL approach would be preferable. He specifically critiques the `print_object_term()` helper function's implementation, noting its current design with a `nul_delim` parameter seems unnecessarily complex when a more general `line_delim` approach would be cleaner.

The feedback focuses on two key technical points:
1. The output format uses two NUL bytes as delimiters, which Junio argues could be simplified to single NULs
2. The implementation introduces a specialized `print_object_term()` function that handles NUL case specially, when a more general delimiter-handling approach would be cleaner

Junio suggests alternative approaches:
- Using a single `line_delim` variable/parameter instead of special-casing NUL
- Potentially making the delimiter behavior consistent regardless of whether it's NUL or newline
- Avoiding separate variables that could confuse future maintainers

This continues the thread's theme of refining the output format design, following earlier discussions about unifying the format structure. The feedback doesn't reject the overall feature but pushes for simpler, more maintainable implementation choices. The discussion remains focused on internal design quality rather than functionality, with Junio maintaining his role as design reviewer while acknowledging the technical merits of the overall approach.
