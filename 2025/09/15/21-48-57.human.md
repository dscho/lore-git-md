This is a post-merge discussion about documentation terminology in Julia Evans' recently merged patch series improving the `git-push` man page.

Junio Hamano responds to Julia's discomfort with the term "triangular workflow" in the context of describing upstream branch relationships. He notes this is an established term in Git's documentation history, though acknowledges Julia's point that it may not cover all multi-repo workflow variations. Hamano suggests alternative phrasing could describe the concept more generically as pushing to a different location than where you fetch from, without needing to label it as "triangular".

The exchange shows the careful balancing act in documentation between using established terminology and making concepts accessible. While the patches are already in good shape (having been merged after thorough review), this follow-up discussion continues refining the language to best serve users. The tone is collaborative, with Hamano validating Julia's concerns while explaining the historical context.
