Junio had suggested an alternative phrasing that explicitly mentions objects (commits, directories, files) and the purpose of making history available. Julia's counter-proposal keeps the focus on branches/tags while still conveying that data transfer occurs. She also notes a formatting issue with section spacing that she'll correct.
