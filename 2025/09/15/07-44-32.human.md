This is a follow-up discussion in the resolved packfile store refactoring series, specifically addressing the behavior of `packfile_store_free()`. 

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's concern about an unexpected change in behavior where `packfile_store_free()` was calling `packfile_store_close()`. Taylor had pointed out this seemed to deviate from the original behavior where packfiles were closed separately from being freed. 

Patrick initially defends the change, explaining that it doesn't actually free the packfiles (just closes them) and maintains the existing call to `packfile_store_close()` in `close_object_store()`. However, after Taylor presses for justification of this behavioral change, Patrick agrees to drop it, preserving the original fragile-but-working behavior for now. 

This represents a minor adjustment to the already-merged series, showing the careful attention being paid to maintaining exact behavior during this refactoring. The discussion demonstrates the project's commitment to only making necessary changes when dealing with sensitive packfile handling code, even when that means deferring potential improvements.
