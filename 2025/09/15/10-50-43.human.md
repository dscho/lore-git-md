Housekeeping follow-up in the Rust infrastructure series.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges a minor oversight in the build system integration patches for Rust support. While the series had properly added `Cargo.lock` to `.gitignore`, it missed updating the `clean` target to remove this file during cleanup. This was caught by both SZEDER Gábor and Junio C Hamano in their reviews of the v4 series.

The fix appears straightforward and doesn't impact the core functionality of the Rust integration work. It's a pattern seen before in the series - careful reviewers catching small polish items as the major technical components fall into place. This kind of attention to detail suggests the series is receiving thorough scrutiny as it approaches finalization.
