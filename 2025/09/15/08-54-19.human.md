Refactoring patch introducing a central packfile management structure as part of the ODB abstraction effort.

This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 15-part series to centralize packfile management in a new `struct packfile_store`. Currently, packfile information is scattered between `struct packed_git` and `struct object_database`, making responsibilities unclear and complicating future pluggable ODB implementations.

The patch introduces the empty structure and basic allocation/free functions, with the actual field migrations coming in later patches. The author notes this is an intermediate step - the structure will eventually move to the ODB source level rather than the ODB level, but introducing it now makes that future transition easier.

The changes are minimal in this initial patch - just adding the new structure pointer to `struct object_database` and implementing basic store management functions. The thread context shows this series has already gone through extensive review and reached consensus, with v5 incorporating all feedback. This foundational change enables cleaner separation of concerns for future ODB work while maintaining existing behavior.
