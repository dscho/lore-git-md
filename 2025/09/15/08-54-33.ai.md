This is the final patch (15/15) in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, completing the migration of packfile management to the new `struct packfile_store` by converting the MRU (most-recently-used) list functionality.

The patch refactors `get_packed_git_mru()` into `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()`, making it operate directly on the packfile store rather than taking a repository parameter. This follows the pattern established in previous patches of moving packfile-related functions to work with the packfile store directly. The change updates one caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and moves the function declaration in `packfile.h` to be grouped with other packfile store accessors.

Key technical details for future reference:
- The new `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` maintains the same behavior of calling `packfile_store_prepare()` before returning the MRU list
- The MRU list head is now accessed directly from the packfile store (`store->mru`)
- The single caller in pack-objects.c is updated to use the new function via `the_repository->objects->packfiles`
- The old `get_packed_git_mru()` declaration is removed from packfile.h
- No functional changes are made to the underlying MRU list behavior

This completes the mechanical conversion of all packfile management functions to operate on the packfile store, finalizing the architectural separation between packfile management and the object database that was the goal of this series. The change is straightforward and follows the established patterns from previous patches in the series.
