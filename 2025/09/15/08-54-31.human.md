This is part 13/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, which centralizes packfile management in a new `struct packfile_store` as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch refactors `get_packed_git()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_get_packs()` for clarity.

The change updates callers in `gc.c`, `grep.c`, and `object-name.c` to use the new interface. The function's behavior remains the same - it prepares the packfile store and returns its packfiles - but the new name better reflects its scope and relationship to the packfile store. The patch also documents that this function doesn't load MIDX or referenced packs, clarifying its boundaries.

This is a mechanical refactor that maintains existing behavior while advancing the series' goal of separating packfile management from object storage. With all technical discussion already resolved in previous iterations, this patch represents a straightforward step in the established transition path.
