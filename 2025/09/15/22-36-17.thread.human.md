The discussion now centers on three possible paths forward: keeping the explicit `--all` flag, adopting Patrick's default-all proposal, or finding a compromise solution. Junio's feedback suggests the project may favor maintaining Git's scripting-oriented conventions, though the experimental nature of the feature leaves room for further discussion. The outcome will affect both the implementation (in builtin/repo-info.c) and documentation of this new command.
