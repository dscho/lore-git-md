This is part 2/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, moving the list of packfiles from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`. The patch focuses on relocating the core packfile tracking infrastructure while maintaining identical behavior.

The key changes move the `packed_git` list from `struct object_database` to `struct packfile_store` (now named `packs`), along with related cleanup logic. The patch introduces two new functions in packfile.c: `packfile_store_close()` for closing packfiles without freeing them (matching the old `close_object_store()` behavior) and updates `packfile_store_free()` to handle packfile cleanup (moving logic from `odb_clear()`).

The changes are mechanical but widespread, updating ~12 direct references to `objects->packed_git` to instead use `objects->packfiles->packs`. The patch maintains all existing invariants, including MRU list management and packfile sorting behavior. The separation of close/free operations preserves the ability to temporarily close packfiles without losing their metadata.

This establishes the foundational structure for subsequent patches in the series to build upon, with all packfile list management now routed through the new store abstraction. The changes are consistent with the series' goal of separating packfile management from core object database functionality.
