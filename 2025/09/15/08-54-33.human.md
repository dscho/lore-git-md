This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, completing the migration of packfile management to the new `struct packfile_store`. The patch refactors `get_packed_git_mru()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` for consistency with the rest of the series.

The changes are mechanical and follow the established pattern seen in previous patches: the function now takes a `packfile_store` parameter instead of a `repository`, and callers are updated to access the store via `the_repository->objects->packfiles`. The MRU (most-recently-used) list functionality remains unchanged, just moved to operate on the new abstraction.

This concludes the 15-part series that centralizes all packfile management in the new `packfile_store` structure, a significant architectural change that enables future ODB (object database) abstraction work. The series has already been thoroughly reviewed and all technical concerns addressed in previous iterations. With this final patch, the transition is complete and all packfile-related functionality now operates through the new store interface.
