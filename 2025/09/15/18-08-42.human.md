Jeff King (peff) responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about when to use `.git_cmd` in the context of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's earlier refactoring patch.

While acknowledging the change is mostly stylistic (since `.git_cmd` essentially just prepends "git"), Jeff expresses no objection to standardizing on `.git_cmd` as a preferred style.
