This is a discussion response in the ongoing thread about extending `--ignore-other-worktrees` to rebase operations. Phillip Wood provides feedback on Gabriel Scherer's patch series, agreeing with the general direction but expressing reservations about the rebase-specific changes.

Phillip supports the improved hint messages for checkout failures (as discussed in the thread) but questions whether `--ignore-other-worktrees` should be added to `git rebase`. His concern is that rebase actively updates branches, which could cause confusion when those branches are checked out in other worktrees - a more dangerous scenario than the checkout case where the discussion is moving toward finalizing the warning messages while still debating whether rebase should support branch independence at all. Phillip's input represents a maintainer perspective cautioning against potential user confusion from rebase operations across worktrees, which may influence the final design decisions.
