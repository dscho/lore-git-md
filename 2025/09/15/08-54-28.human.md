This is part 10/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, continuing the effort to centralize packfile management in `struct packfile_store`. The patch refactors `install_packed_git()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_add_pack()` to better reflect its scope.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, updating 7 call sites across various subsystems (fast-import, index-pack, http, midx) to use the new interface. The core functionality remains the same - adding a packfile to the store's tracking structures - but now operates directly on the packfile store rather than going through the repository layer.

This is a straightforward continuation of the series' architectural goal to separate packfile management from the broader object database abstraction. The patch appears well-executed with thorough updates to all affected callers and clear documentation of the new function in packfile.h.
