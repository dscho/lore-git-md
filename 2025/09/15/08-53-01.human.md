This is a refinement of the advice messaging in Gabriel Scherer's worktree safety check series, specifically addressing how Git warns users about branch conflicts across worktrees. Junio Hamano provides detailed feedback on the proposed hint messages, arguing they don't sufficiently explain the risks and tradeoffs to users who encounter the conflict.

The core critique is that the current hints are too vague about consequences: "proceed anyway" doesn't warn about index/working tree desynchronization, "impact" is ambiguous about what cross-worktree changes actually mean, and the detach suggestion lacks motivation. Hamano outlines what a truly helpful message would cover - the synchronization risks, when to create a new branch versus detach, and how detached HEAD prevents accidental commits.

This continues the thread's focus on making worktree safety checks more user-friendly while maintaining correctness. The feedback suggests the series needs deeper consideration of how novice users interpret these warnings, not just technical correctness. Hamano's comments will likely lead to another revision of the advice text before this patch is finalized.
