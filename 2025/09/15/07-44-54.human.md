3. Patrick emphasizes this is part of a larger transition and subsequent changes depend on this refactoring, so he'd prefer to keep it as-is.

The discussion shows Patrick maintaining his position that this change is architecturally important despite the short-term inconvenience, while Taylor had previously argued it was premature optimization.
