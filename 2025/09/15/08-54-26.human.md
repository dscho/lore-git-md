Refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store series (8/15). This continues the effort to centralize packfile management in `struct packfile_store` by updating the core packfile preparation functions.

The patch reworks `prepare_packed_git()` and related helpers to operate directly on a packfile store rather than a repository. Key changes include:
- Renaming functions to `packfile_store_prepare*` to reflect their new scope
- Updating all callers to pass the packfile store instead of repository
- Maintaining the same initialization sequence but routing through the store
- Preserving all existing behavior including MRU list management and pack sorting

This is a mechanical change that follows the architectural direction established earlier in the series, with no functional differences. The patch updates about a dozen call sites across packfile.c while keeping the same control flow. It's part of the larger effort to separate packfile management from the object database abstraction.

The changes look straightforward and align with the series' goals. Since this is patch 8/15 in a v5 series where earlier patches have already been reviewed and agreed upon, this appears to be a non-controversial step in the established refactoring path.
