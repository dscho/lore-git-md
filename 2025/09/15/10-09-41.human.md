This is a discussion response in the ongoing thread about extending `--ignore-other-worktrees` to more Git commands. Phillip Wood weighs in on Gabriel Scherer's patch series that aims to make worktree branch conflicts more manageable.

Phillip expresses support for the checkout improvements but raises concerns about applying the same approach to `git rebase`. He agrees with Junio's earlier feedback that the advice messages need clearer wording about when to use `--detach` or `--ignore-other-worktrees`. However, he questions whether rebase should support ignoring other worktrees at all, since rebase actively updates branches and could cause confusion if those branches are checked out elsewhere.

The response doesn't propose specific changes but signals an important design consideration - that some commands may fundamentally conflict with the "ignore other worktrees" philosophy due to their branch-modifying nature. This adds a new dimension to the thread's discussion beyond just polishing the advice messages.
