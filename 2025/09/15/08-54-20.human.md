This is part 2/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's refactoring series to centralize packfile management in `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the list of packfiles from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`, establishing it as the proper home for packfile-related state.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, updating ~30 references to `packed_git` across packfile operations to instead access the list through the new `packs` field in the store. The patch also extracts packfile closing and freeing logic from `odb_clear()` into new `packfile_store_close()` and `packfile_store_free()` functions, making the object database cleanup more modular.

This is a foundational step in the larger ODB abstraction effort, cleanly separating packfile management from the object database while maintaining all existing behavior. The changes are straightforward and well-contained, with no apparent loose ends or questionable design choices. The patch follows the series' established pattern of incremental, behavior-preserving refactoring.
