This is a discussion contribution in the ongoing thread about Git patch workflow optimization. Patrick Steinhardt raises two key concerns about proposals to speed up the patch integration process.

First, regarding Taylor Blau's suggestion to require at least one positive "ack" before merging, Steinhardt questions whether this would actually accelerate the process or just add another bureaucratic step. He notes this might slow things down further rather than help.

Second, on the topic of adding more maintainers, Steinhardt identifies a potential imbalance this could create - subsystems with more active contributors (often those maintained by hosting providers) might see faster patch processing, while less active areas (where drive-by contributions tend to cluster) could be disadvantaged by lack of subsystem maintainers.

The email doesn't propose new solutions but thoughtfully critiques existing suggestions, highlighting potential unintended consequences. It maintains the thread's constructive tone while surfacing important practical issues about fairness and efficiency in the project's review processes. The concerns seem well-reasoned and worth considering as the discussion continues.
