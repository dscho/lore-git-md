This is a follow-up in the thread about retaining the deprecated `git whatchanged` command. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Henrik Bennin's request to keep the command by providing a direct translation of `whatchanged` functionality into equivalent `git log` invocations.

The email offers specific technical guidance for users wanting to migrate from `whatchanged` to `log`, noting that `git log --no-merges --raw` provides identical functionality. It further suggests alternative output formats (`--stat` or `--name-only`) that may be more readable than `--raw` for some use cases.

This reply reinforces the existing deprecation stance by demonstrating how trivial the transition is, while still being helpful migration advice. The response implicitly counters the original request's premise by showing the command's functionality is already fully available through non-deprecated alternatives. No new arguments for or against deprecation are introduced - the email simply provides practical transition information that maintains the status quo.
