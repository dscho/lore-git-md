This is a meta-discussion about the organization and coordination of the Rust integration patches between Ezekiel Newren's expanded series and Patrick Steinhardt's parallel work. Junio Hamano raises concerns about potential duplication and unclear integration strategy between the two efforts.

The email responds to Newren's v2 series (18 patches) which builds on his previous 15-patch RFC and incorporates elements from Steinhardt's policy foundation work. Hamano notes the series includes Makefile fixes for libgit.a, Rust introduction, GitHub workflows, build-helper/cbindgen infrastructure, and varint implementation as a test case.

Key coordination issues highlighted:
1. Overlap with Steinhardt's in-flight patches - unclear whether Newren intends to supersede or coexist with them
2. Lack of clarity about how this series relates to other active topics in the 'next' branch
3. Potential for duplicate commits doing identical work in parallel branches
4. Missing explanation of the intended merge strategy for combining these efforts

Hamano suggests the immediate priority is resolving the series organization before evaluating technical changes, emphasizing the need for clear developer coordination in this complex, multi-contributor effort. The response reflects ongoing challenges in managing the large-scale Rust integration where infrastructure, policy, and implementation work are progressing simultaneously through different patch series.
