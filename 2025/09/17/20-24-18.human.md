This is a documentation patch in the ongoing command deprecation series, specifically addressing the `git whatchanged` documentation. 

Kristoffer Haugsbakk submits patch 8/9 in v6 of the series, making two small but meaningful changes to the deprecation warning in git-whatchanged.adoc. The patch removes the subjective claim that `whatchanged` "is not even shorter to type than `log --raw`" and changes "default" to the plural "defaults" to better match the command's actual behavior.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, focusing on improving the clarity and professionalism of the deprecation notice. The patch maintains the core warning message while removing what could be seen as unnecessary editorializing about command length. This follows through on the series' goal of providing clear migration guidance for deprecated commands, with `git log --raw --no-merges` remaining the recommended replacement.

As part of the larger deprecation framework effort, this documentation tweak helps ensure users receive consistent, helpful information when transitioning away from `git whatchanged`. The change is minimal but contributes to the overall polish of the deprecation system.
