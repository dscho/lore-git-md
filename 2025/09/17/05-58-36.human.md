This is a discussion about coordinating Rust integration efforts in Git. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Ezekiel Newren's 18-patch series introducing Rust infrastructure (build system, CI, cbindgen, varint implementation) by suggesting a more incremental approach.

Steinhardt notes the series is large and not yet ready, while his own smaller 3-patch series focusing just on Rust adoption policy is already in flight. He proposes merging the policy patches first, then having Newren rebase the larger infrastructure work on top, allowing for smaller, more manageable steps toward Rust integration.

While agreeing with the general direction (cbindgen, Windows support, multiple workspaces), Steinhardt argues these don't need to land simultaneously. The exchange shows two maintainers working to coordinate parallel efforts on Rust integration - Steinhardt advocating for smaller, sequential changes while Newren's series attempts broader infrastructure in one go.
