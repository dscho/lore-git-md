The patch follows through on the series' documentation refinements seen in patch 8/9, continuing the trend of removing subjective language. It references the earlier `jc/you-still-use-whatchanged` work that introduced the `--i-still-use-this` requirement. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, simply removing an outdated status report rather than making any substantive policy change about the deprecation process itself.

This completes the BreakingChanges.adoc updates for the series, which has already established comprehensive deprecation infrastructure including alias shadowing support, warning systems, and migration guidance.
