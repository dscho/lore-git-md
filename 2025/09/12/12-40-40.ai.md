This is a cover letter for a feature patch series introducing signature handling control to `git fast-import`. The series adds a `--signed-commits=<mode>` option to match the existing functionality in `git fast-export`, with plans to extend it further in future work.

The first patch refactors the `enum sign_mode` parsing code from fast-export.c into gpg-interface.[ch] to make it reusable. The second patch implements the new fast-import option with initial modes matching fast-export's behavior: "verbatim" (keep signatures), "strip" (remove them), and "warn" variants of each. The implementation handles both SHA-1 and SHA-256 signatures.

Key technical details include:
- New `sign_mode` enum in gpg-interface.h with values like SIGN_VERBATIM, SIGN_STRIP
- Signature parsing moved to parse_one_signature() helper
- Tests in t9305-fast-import-signatures.sh verify behavior with both SHA-1 and SHA-256 signatures
- Future plans mentioned for modes like "re-sign" and "strip-if-invalid"

The author seeks feedback on two design questions:
1. Whether to keep synonym modes ("ignore" for "verbatim") which may be confusing
2. Whether warning variants should remain as separate modes or be controlled by a general --verbose flag

The changes since v1 include improved error handling, better warning messages, test cleanup, and removal of an intermediate import_signature() function. The series appears well-tested with passing CI runs and demonstrates careful consideration of the design space for this new feature.
