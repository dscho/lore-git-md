This is a documentation patch, part of a series improving the `git-push` man page based on user research. It's the first of four patches in v2 of the series, and focuses on revising the introductory section to be more accessible.

The patch addresses feedback from users who found terms like "ref" and "objects" confusing in the original text. It replaces technical jargon with more familiar language about "branches, tags, or other references" and moves the discussion of hooks (which users felt was prematurely placed) to the end of the section. The changes are straightforward and well-motivated by the user research cited in the commit message.

The patch has already been merged with some post-merge feedback incorporated, and the overall approach has been approved by Junio Hamano. It establishes a documentation style that will be used throughout the rest of improvements to the push documentation. The changes are purely about presentation and clarity - no behavior or functionality is affected.
