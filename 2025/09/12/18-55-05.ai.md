Key changes in v2 include dropping the most invasive refspec rewrite (now deferred for future work) and adding a new "UPSTREAM BRANCHES" section that explains upstream tracking concepts across push/pull/fetch. The series now focuses on: 1) clarifying the introduction (already merged), 2) new upstream explanation, 3) "where to push" behavior, and 4) "what to push" behavior. Technical changes include renaming `urls-remotes.adoc` to `urls-remotes-upstreams.adoc` to accommodate the new content, plus numerous wording improvements throughout the push documentation. The approach emphasizes numbered precedence lists and cross-references between sections rather than relying on implicit knowledge of Git internals. The work builds on Jean-Noël Avila's documentation style while addressing specific pain points identified in user testing.
