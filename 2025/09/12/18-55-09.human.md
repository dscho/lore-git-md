This is the final patch in Julia Evans' documentation improvement series for `git-push`, focusing on clarifying the "what to push" behavior. The patch addresses multiple points of confusion reported by users, including unclear syntax (like the meaning of "..." in refspecs), confusing references to configuration", and the late placement of the default behavior explanation.

The patch reorganizes the explanation into a clear numbered list showing the precedence order Git uses to determine what to push: 1) explicit refspecs/options, 2) remote.*.push config, 3) push.default config. It moves the safety check about upstream branches into its own paragraph and cross-references both the CONFIGURATION and UPSTREAM BRANCHES sections added earlier in the series.

This follows the same successful pattern as previous patches in the series - taking a confusing section and restructuring it as a clear decision flow while maintaining technical accuracy. The changes are well-motivated by specific user feedback and the patch maintains consistency with the terminology improvements made earlier in the series. With this patch, the entire push workflow (where to push and what to push) now has clearer documentation following the same organizational principles.
