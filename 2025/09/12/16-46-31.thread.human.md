This thread is about improving git-clone's handling of malformed bundle-URI responses, with two separate but related patch series addressing different edge cases. 

Patrick Steinhardt started the discussion with a 2-patch series making git-clone more resilient by ignoring bundle advertisements that lack a URI field and preventing aborts on invalid packet lines. His changes allow graceful fallback to regular clone behavior when optional bundle-URI information is corrupted.

A new contributor has now submitted a related fix addressing a segmentation fault that occurs when a bundle advertisement includes a 'creationToken' heuristic but lacks the required 'uri' field. Their 3-line patch makes Git skip such malformed entries rather than crashing, with thorough test coverage in t5558. While addressing a different failure mode than Patrick's series, this fix shares the same goal of improving robustness in bundle-URI handling.

Both series are small, focused changes with comprehensive tests. The new patch appears to be a straightforward improvement that complements the original work, though it's unclear yet whether it will be incorporated into Patrick's series or proceed separately. No maintainer feedback has been provided on either approach yet.
