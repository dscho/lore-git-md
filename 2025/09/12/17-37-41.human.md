Bugfix discussion for bundle-URI error handling. Justin Tobler responds to Toon Claes's patch that would downgrade bundle-URI parsing errors to warnings, allowing clones to continue when servers send malformed bundle advertisements. While agreeing with the technical approach of preventing aborts on invalid data, Justin questions whether this is the right design direction - suggesting that misconfigured servers should be fixed rather than having clients silently tolerate their errors.

The exchange reveals a philosophical difference: Toon's patch prioritizes uninterrupted cloning (graceful degradation), while Justin (echoing earlier feedback from Junio Hamano) argues clients should maintain strict validation to surface configuration problems. The technical details remain focused on packet line parsing in `connect.c`, but the discussion has shifted to error handling strategy rather than implementation correctness. This positions the patch as potentially controversial design change rather than a straightforward bugfix.
