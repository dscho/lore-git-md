This thread discusses the ongoing effort to make SHA-256 the default hash algorithm for new Git repositories, targeting Git 3.0 in 2026. Brian Carlson has been the technical lead on this multi-year project. Emily Shaffer has reached out to offer assistance from Google and the broader community, asking how others can help with tasks like testing at scale, program management of the roadmap, and identifying work that could be delegated. She notes this is being discussed publicly (rather than privately) to be inclusive of all potential contributors. The email highlights that while corporate resources aren't immediately committed, understanding the project's needs will help advocate for support as the critical 3.0 timeline approaches.
