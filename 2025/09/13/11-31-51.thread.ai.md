Core problem: gitk fails to open its "Tags and Heads" view when a remote tracking branch has been deleted (e.g. via `git push --delete`). This occurs due to inconsistent state between two internal data structures: `headids` (tracking branch heads) and `upstreamofref` (tracking remote relationships). When a remote branch is deleted, its entry is removed from `headids` but may remain referenced in `upstreamofref`, causing an error when gitk attempts to access the missing headid.
