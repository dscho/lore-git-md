This thread continues discussion of Gabriel Scherer's series improving worktree safety checks and the `--ignore-other-worktrees` feature. The patches have evolved through three versions, with consensus reached for checkout/switch cases but significant maintainer concerns remaining about rebase integration.

Junio Hamano has raised fundamental safety objections to allowing rebase operations across worktrees, arguing that unlike checkout (which can be read-only), rebase actively modifies branches in ways that could make original worktree positions unreachable. He provides concrete examples of dangerous scenarios and advocates for detached HEAD operations as a safer alternative for temporary checkouts. Gabriel has defended some design choices while remaining open to improving warning messages and documentation.

The discussion now centers on philosophical differences: Gabriel views branch conflicts as recoverable inconveniences, while Junio sees them as potential data hazards. Key open questions are whether to support the feature for rebase at all, and how strongly to warn about risks. Documentation improvements are needed regardless of the final design decisions. The thread remains active with these safety considerations being the primary focus before the series can progress further.
