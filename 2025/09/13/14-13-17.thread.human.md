This thread focuses on Gabriel Scherer's patch series improving worktree safety checks, particularly around the `--ignore-other-worktrees` option for both checkout and rebase operations. The series appears functionally complete, with open items being documentation refinements and potential future extensions to worktree commands. The technical discussion has shifted to verification and polish, with no fundamental objections raised. Gabriel will likely address these final review comments before the series is ready for merging.
