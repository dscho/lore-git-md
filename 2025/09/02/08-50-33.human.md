This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor packfile management into a dedicated `packfile_store` structure. The email specifically addresses feedback on patch 10/16, which renames `install_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_add_pack()`.

Patrick responds to Taylor Blau's mild preference for keeping "install" in the function name, explaining that the previous distinction between "add" (just instantiating) and "install" (making active) no longer exists in the new architecture. He argues that since all added packfiles immediately become active, "add" is both more accurate and slightly shorter. While both maintainers acknowledge the naming is somewhat arbitrary, Patrick plans to keep "add" unless strong objections emerge.

The exchange shows consensus on the technical implementation while revealing the subtle considerations behind API naming decisions during major refactoring. This patch is part of the larger effort to cleanly separate packfile operations from the object database layer, with most architectural questions already resolved in earlier reviews.
