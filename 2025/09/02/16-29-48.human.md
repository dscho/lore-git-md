Git maintainer Junio C Hamano responds to a user's concern about the deprecation of `git whatchanged` and its replacement with `git log --raw`. This is part of the ongoing discussion about improving the transition path for users accustomed to `git whatchanged`.

Hamano clarifies that `--raw` is the documented compatible option for `git whatchanged` functionality, but acknowledges it may not be the most intuitive name. He notes there are other potentially better alternatives like `--name-only` or `-p` depending on the level of detail needed, and shares his personal preference for `--compact-summary`.

The response maintains the deprecation path but shows openness to user experience concerns, while stopping short of endorsing any specific alternative naming scheme. The tone is informative and non-confrontational, suggesting users have flexibility in choosing their preferred output format while the core functionality remains available through existing options.
