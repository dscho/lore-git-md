This is a follow-up discussion about locking in the packfile store refactoring series, specifically addressing patch 9's split of `reprepare_packed_git()` into ODB and packfile components.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges Taylor Blau's discovery of a missing `odb_read_lock()` in the new `odb_reprepare()` function. The issue stems from the fact that while `packfile_store_reprepare()` requires the lock to be held, its caller `odb_reprepare()` wasn't acquiring it. Patrick notes this oversight is particularly subtle because most Git commands don't use the ODB read lock - only `git grep` explicitly enables it via `enable_obj_read_lock()`, explaining why the missing lock didn't cause immediate failures.

The key technical points:
- The lock management inconsistency between `odb_reprepare()` (which doesn't acquire) and `packfile_store_reprepare()` (which requires) is confirmed as a bug
- Patrick agrees the lock should be handled within `odb_reprepare()` rather than by callers
- The oversight was masked by Git's limited use of ODB read locking in practice
- A fix will be provided to properly acquire the lock in `odb_reprepare()`

This discussion resolves one of the open questions from the thread summary regarding patch 9's locking issue. The solution maintains the architectural principle that locking should be handled internally by the ODB layer rather than requiring caller awareness. The exchange demonstrates careful review catching subtle synchronization issues in this complex refactoring.
