This is patch 10/15 in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing packfile store refactoring series, continuing the work to separate packfile management from the object database. The patch refactors `install_packed_git()` to operate on a packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_add_pack()` to better reflect its purpose.

The change is straightforward - the function now takes a `packfile_store` parameter instead of a repository, and updates the store's internal pack list and hashmap directly. The patch updates all 6 call sites across fast-import, index-pack, http, midx code to use the new function. Documentation is also updated to reflect the new name and purpose.

This is another incremental step in the series' goal of cleanly separating packfile management from the broader object database. The changes are mechanical and behavior-preserving, with no test modifications needed. The patch follows the established pattern from previous refactors in the series, maintaining consistency in how packfile operations are migrated to the new store-based approach.
