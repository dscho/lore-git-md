This continues the series' careful approach of moving state while maintaining identical behavior. With this change, all packfile-specific state has now been moved out of the object database, achieving a cleaner separation of concerns that will eventually enable pluggable ODB backends. The changes touch odb.h, packfile.c, and packfile.h, with no test modifications needed since the behavior remains unchanged.
