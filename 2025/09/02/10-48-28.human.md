This is part 3/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile-related state out of the generic object_database structure and into a dedicated packfile_store. The series is systematically preparing for future pluggable ODB backends by cleaning up the internal architecture.

This particular patch moves the `packed_git_initialized` flag from `struct object_database` to `struct packfile_store`, renaming it to simply `initialized`. The flag tracks whether the packfile list has already been populated to avoid redundant initialization. The patch also takes the opportunity to convert the field from a bitfield to a proper boolean now that the codebase allows it.

The changes are straightforward mechanical updates to references of the flag throughout packfile.c, maintaining all existing behavior. This continues the series' pattern of moving packfile-specific state while keeping the same functionality. The patch is well-contained, touching only the expected files (odb.h, packfile.c, packfile.h) with clean diffstat (9 lines added, 9 removed).

As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a carefully executed step in the larger architectural effort, with no obvious issues or controversial changes. Reviewers have been generally positive about the direction, though some discussion remains about convenience wrappers in later patches.
