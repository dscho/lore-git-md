This is part 15 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch refactors `get_all_packs()` to work directly with the packfile store rather than a repository.

The key point of discussion is whether removing the convenience wrapper `get_all_packs()` is premature. Taylor Blau argues that callers shouldn't need packfile store awareness yet, and the change makes common operations more verbose. Patrick acknowledges this but explains the refactoring is a stepping stone toward the series' ultimate goal of enabling pluggable ODB backends. He agrees to add temporary local variables to improve readability and mentions plans for a future `packfile_store_for_each_pack()` helper to further simplify the syntax.

The patch affects several callers that need direct packfile access, particularly those dealing with low-level repository layout. While the change makes some code temporarily more verbose, it's part of a deliberate architectural shift. Patrick is responsive to readability concerns while maintaining the series' technical direction.
