This is a technical discussion follow-up in the `git subtree` bugfix thread, focusing on the implementation details of commit examination logic for squashed subtree merges.

Phillip Wood responds to Colin Stagner's analysis of the `should_ignore_subtree_split_commit` function in `contrib/subtree/git-subtree.sh`. The discussion confirms several key points: the `git-subtree-mainline:` trailer check is necessary for excluding incompatible squashed merge commits, while the `git-subtree-dir:` check appears to lack test coverage and may be removed. Phillip agrees with Colin's assessment that ancestor traversal isn't needed due to the forward iteration order of the parent `rev-list` command.

The email explores the behavior of different subtree operations, noting that squashed merges and splits produce distinct commit structures with different trailer patterns. Phillip suggests the current checks might be better placed within the `check_parent()` recursion rather than the main commit examination loop. Both developers acknowledge the need for Zach's input on the original intent behind the trailer checks, though this isn't blocking the fix.

Implementation-wise, Phillip and Colin agree on switching to `git show --format` for cleaner trailer parsing in v2, preferring `--no-patch` over `-s` for readability. The discussion highlights gaps in test coverage, particularly around the `git-subtree-dir:` trailer behavior, which may influence whether that check is kept or removed in the final version.
