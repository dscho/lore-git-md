This is patch 5/15 in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile-specific state from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`. The patch relocates the MRU (most-recently-used) list of packfiles, which helps optimize object access patterns by favoring recently used packs.

The changes are mechanical but thorough: the MRU list moves from `objects->packed_git_mru` to `objects->packfiles->mru`, with corresponding updates to initialization, access, and manipulation code. The patch touches several key files including packfile.c, odb.c, and midx.c, but maintains identical behavior - this is purely an internal code organization improvement.

As with previous patches in the series, this continues the architectural goal of concerns separation to eventually enable pluggable ODB backends. The changes are well-contained and follow the established pattern from earlier patches. Reviewers have already vetted the overall approach in previous rounds, and this patch introduces no new technical concerns. The series remains on track with 10 more patches planned after this one.
