This email continues the discussion about MRU (most-recently-used) list handling for packfiles in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing packfile store abstraction series. The focus is on patch 11/16, which proposes always adding packfiles to the MRU list when they're added to the store.

Patrick responds to Taylor Blau's concerns about why MIDX (multi-pack-index) packs are being added to the MRU list. He identifies two key motivations: (1) the current behavior is inconsistent since some subsystems add packs to MRU while others don't, and (2) this change is a prerequisite for a future refactoring (discussed with Jeff King) that would eliminate the distinction between the MRU list and the main packfile list entirely.

The email reveals that while the immediate benefit of the change may not be clear, it's part of a larger architectural plan to simplify the packfile tracking system. Patrick acknowledges the change could be deferred until that subsequent refactoring, but argues the current inconsistency is confusing since the MRU list doesn't reliably contain all packfiles.

This discussion touches on core packfile management internals, particularly around how MIDX packs interact with the MRU cache, and represents an ongoing architectural discussion about how to structure the packfile store abstraction. The thread shows careful consideration of both immediate impacts and long-term design goals for the packfile subsystem.
