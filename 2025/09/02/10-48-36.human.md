This is part 11 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to separate packfile management from the object database. The patch introduces a new helper function `packfile_store_load_pack()` that consolidates the common pattern of checking if a packfile exists in the store and adding it if not.

The new function handles the non-trivial logic of reconstructing packfile keys, checking the packfile map, and adding new packfiles to the store. It replaces several manual implementations of this pattern across fast-import.c, index-pack.c, midx.c, and packfile.c, simplifying the call sites. The changes are behavior-preserving - the same packfile operations occur, just through a cleaner interface.

This continues the series' methodical approach to moving packfile operations into the store-based paradigm. The patch appears well-constructed with thorough updates to all relevant call sites and maintains the architectural direction established in earlier patches. No test changes were needed since this is purely an internal refactoring.
