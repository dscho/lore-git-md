This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, converting `get_packed_git_mru()` to work directly on the packfile store interface. The patch renames the function to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` and updates its signature to take a packfile store rather than a repository, completing the architectural transition started earlier in the series.

The changes are mechanical and behavior-preserving, affecting only the pack-objects command and the packfile internals. The MRU (most-recently-used) list functionality remains identical, just accessed through the new store-based interface. The patch follows the series' pattern of cleanly separating packfile operations from the broader object database context.

With this change, all major packfile access functions have been migrated to the new store interface. The series has been carefully reviewed throughout its development, with all earlier technical concerns addressed. This final patch sets the stage for any remaining interface consolidation and documentation updates to complete the effort.
