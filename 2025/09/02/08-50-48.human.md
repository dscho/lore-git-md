This is a discussion about MRU (most-recently-used) list handling in the ongoing packfile store abstraction series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Taylor Blau's concerns about eagerly adding MIDX packs to the MRU list in patch 11/16.

The key points are:
1. Patrick argues the current behavior is inconsistent - some subsystems add packs to MRU immediately while others wait until first access. His patch standardizes this by always adding to MRU when adding a pack.
2. Taylor questions why MIDX packs need MRU handling at all, since they're processed separately in lookup functions and never moved to the front of the MRU.
3. Patrick reveals a longer-term motivation: this change enables a future refactoring (discussed with Jeff King) to eliminate the distinction between the MRU list and the main packfile list entirely.
4. He's open to deferring this change until that follow-up series, acknowledging it stands better on its own merits when combined with the list consolidation.

The discussion highlights an architectural tension between immediate consistency and waiting for broader structural changes. Patrick's position is technically sound but acknowledges the change may be premature without its ultimate payoff. The thread shows careful consideration of Git's packfile lookup performance characteristics.
