This is part 2/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile management into a dedicated `struct packfile_store`. The patch moves the core `packed_git` list from the object database (ODB) structure to the new packfile store, along with related cleanup logic.

The changes are mechanical but thorough - the list head moves from `odb->packed_git` to `store->packs`, and all iteration points are updated. The patch also relocates the packfile closing/freeing logic from `odb_clear()` to new `packfile_store_close()` and `packfile_store_free()` functions. A memory safety fix from v2 (adding NULL assignment after free) is included.

This continues the architectural effort to separate packfile-specific state from the generic ODB structure, paving the way for pluggable ODB backends. The changes are well-contained and maintain identical behavior while moving implementation details to their new home. The patch appears cleanly implemented with comprehensive updates to all packfile access points.
