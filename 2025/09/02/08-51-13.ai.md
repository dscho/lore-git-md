This is part 15 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to abstract packfile management into `struct packfile_store`. The patch refactors `get_all_packs()` to work directly on the packfile store rather than a repository, continuing the architectural shift from the previous patches.

The key discussion point here is whether removing the convenience wrapper `get_all_packs()` is premature. Taylor Blau argues that forcing callers to directly access `the_repository->objects->packfiles` exposes implementation details too early, while Patrick maintains this is a necessary step toward the long-term goal of decoupling the ODB from packfile storage. Patrick acknowledges the verbosity but plans to introduce a `packfile_store_for_each_pack()` helper in a future series to mitigate this.

The patch affects several callers that need direct packfile access (like `builtin/cat-file.c`), which Patrick notes are inherently tied to on-disk layout details. While this creates some temporary verbosity, it's positioned as an intermediate step toward a cleaner architecture where most access would go through higher-level ODB interfaces. The thread shows the typical tension between immediate convenience and long-term design goals in refactoring work.
