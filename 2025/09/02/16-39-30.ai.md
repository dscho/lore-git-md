This follows up on earlier concerns carlson raised about platform compatibility risks from manual structure management.

The email indicates carlson will submit this as a separate patch (available in their `rust-policy` branch) rather than modifying the current series, respecting Newren's request to keep the initial Rust infrastructure changes focused.
