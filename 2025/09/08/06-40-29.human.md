This is a policy discussion email in the ongoing Rust infrastructure thread, specifically addressing transition timelines and maintenance commitments. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about the proposed 3-year support window for non-Rust Git versions.

Steinhardt proposes a compromise timeline between Hamano's suggested 6 months and Phillip Wood's original 3-year proposal. His plan would:
- Set Git 3.0 for end of next year as the hard cutoff for mandatory adoption
- Provide 1 year of security backports for the last pre-Rust-mandatory version (2.99)
- Allow community maintenance beyond that point without official project support

The email reflects the ongoing tension between enterprise needs (longer support) and maintainer burden (shorter windows). Steinhardt's middle ground attempts to balance these while keeping the transition moving forward. The proposal notably ties the Rust requirement to the major version bump to Git 3.0, which may help signal the importance of the change to users.
