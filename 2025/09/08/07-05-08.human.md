This is a technical follow-up in the ongoing thread about managing inode exhaustion on NFS servers storing many small Git repositories. Kousik Sanagavarapu reports back on implementing some of Brian M. Carlson's earlier suggestions for reducing inode usage through Git configuration.

Key updates:
- The `transfer.unpackLimit` setting was found while exploring `fetch.unpackLimit` and appears to be exactly what was needed for their use case
- They've implemented aggressive GC settings (`gc.auto=1`, `gc.autopacklimit=1`) but are curious about potential performance impacts given their high-volume push operations
- Filesystem changes remain off the table due to infrastructure constraints (stuck with ext4 on NFS)

The email shows thoughtful engagement with the technical suggestions from earlier in the thread, implementing several of them while noting real-world constraints. The performance impact question about aggressive GC settings is particularly relevant one for their high-volume environment. This continues the thread's practical focus on finding workable solutions within the stated constraints (must keep all repos, can't change filesystem).
