This is a documentation patch in the command deprecation series, specifically addressing the `git whatchanged` messaging. Kristoffer Haugsbakk simplifies the deprecation warning by removing the redundant comparison about command length and fixes the grammar of "default" to plural "defaults". The patch maintains the core information that `git log --raw --no-merges` is the recommended replacement while making the warning more concise. This change follows through on the series' goal of providing clear, actionable guidance for deprecated commands. The modification is minimal (a single line change in the man page) but contributes to the overall polish of the deprecation messaging system being established in this series.
