Christian Couder has announced Git's participation in the December 2025 Outreachy internship cohort, continuing his long-standing role as coordinator. The project's involvement depends on securing mentors, interns, and funding, with potential sponsorship from GitLab, GitHub, and the Git project itself. Outreachy faces financial challenges and is seeking additional sponsors.

In response to the call for mentors, Usman Akinyemi has volunteered to serve as a co-mentor for the upcoming cohort. While this helps address the mentoring aspect of the program, the sponsorship challenges mentioned in the original announcement remain open. The thread demonstrates Git's ongoing commitment to Outreachy participation under Couder's coordination, with community members stepping up to support the initiative in their areas of capability.
