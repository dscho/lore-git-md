Christian Couder has announced Git's participation in the December 2025 Outreachy internship program, continuing his role as coordinator. The project is working to secure mentors, interns, and funding, with potential sponsorship from GitLab, GitHub, and the project itself. So far, Usman Akinyemi has volunteered as a co-mentor, and Kaartic Sivaraam has now offered to serve as a backup mentor and assist with program support if needed.

While these mentor commitments help strengthen the program's foundation, the thread highlights ongoing challenges in securing sufficient financial sponsorship for the internships. Outreachy itself faces funding difficulties, and the Git project is still seeking additional sponsors to make the December 2025 cohort possible. Community members remain invited to contribute by mentoring, proposing microprojects, improving documentation, or helping address the sponsorship needs.
