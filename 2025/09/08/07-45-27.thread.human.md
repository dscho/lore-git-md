The Git project is preparing for its December 2025 Outreachy internship participation, coordinated by Christian Couder. The primary focus will be a project to remove global state from the codebase, building on work started during Ayush's 2025 Google Summer of Code contribution. Mentor coordination is progressing well, with Usman Akinyemi now officially committed as co-mentor and Kaartic Sivaraam serving as backup mentor and program organizer. 

While the technical side of the internship is taking shape, the project scope and mentor team now established, the critical outstanding issue remains securing financial sponsorship from corporate backers like GitLab or GitHub. Without sponsorship funds, the internship cannot proceed despite the prepared project and willing mentors. The community continues to welcome additional co-mentors and sponsorship assistance as the September 26 project submission deadline approaches.
