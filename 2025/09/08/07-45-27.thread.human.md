Christian Couder has registered the Git project for the December 2025 Outreachy internship cohort, continuing his long-standing role as coordinator. While the signup deadline (September 10, 2025) necessitated early registration, final participation depends on securing mentors, suitable projects, and funding—with GitLab, GitHub, and the Git project itself as potential sponsors. Outreachy is currently facing financial challenges and welcomes additional support.

The email invites community members to contribute by co-mentoring, proposing microprojects, or improving Outreachy documentation. This follows Git's established participation pattern in the program, which provides internships to underrepresented groups in tech. Couder's announcement maintains an inclusive tone while transparently addressing the program's funding constraints.
