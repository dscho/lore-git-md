This appears to be a clean, mechanical change that properly scopes the `submod_gitdir_path` variable and uses `strbuf` for path handling. As the first patch in the series, it sets up the foundation for the more substantial changes to come by ensuring all submodule gitdir path construction flows through the common helper function.
