Refactoring with performance improvement in xdiff subsystem. This is the first of four patches in a series (no cover letter provided yet).

Alexander Monakov presents a refactoring of `xdl_hash_record()` that splits it into two functions (`_verbatim` and `_with_whitespace` variants) and makes the whitespace flag check inline. The change allows the compiler to optimize the common case (no whitespace flags) by hoisting the check out of the hot loop in `xdl_prepare_ctx()`. 

Phillip Wood's benchmark shows an 8% speedup when running `git log --oneline --shortstat` over a substantial commit history (v2.0.0 to v2.5.0). The patch is cleanly split between moving the existing implementation to the new `_with_whitespace` function and creating a simplified `_verbatim` version, with an inline wrapper maintaining the original API.

The change appears well-motivated and implemented, with clear performance measurements showing the benefit. As the first in a series, it sets up infrastructure that subsequent patches will presumably build upon, though the exact direction isn't clear without seeing the rest of the series.
