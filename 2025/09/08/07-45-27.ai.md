This is a community participation announcement regarding the Outreachy internship program. Christian Couder informs the Git mailing list that he has signed up the Git project for the December 2025 Outreachy cohort, where he will act as coordinator and mentor as in previous years. The deadline for community signup was approaching (September 10, 2025), prompting this early registration, though final participation depends on securing mentors, suitable interns, and sponsorship funding. 

Couder mentions that GitLab, GitHub, and the Git project itself are potential sponsors, but notes that Outreachy is currently facing financial difficulties and welcomes additional sponsorship support. He provides links to Outreachy's recent blog posts about the December 2025 cohort and their funding challenges. 

The email invites community members to participate in various ways: co-mentoring, proposing microprojects or project ideas for interns, and contributing updates to the Git project's Outreachy-related documentation pages. This follows Git's established pattern of participating in Outreachy internships, with Couder continuing his long-standing role in coordinating this effort. The tone is informational and inclusive, encouraging broader community involvement while being transparent about the program's current financial constraints.
