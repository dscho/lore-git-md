This is a community participation announcement regarding the Outreachy internship program. Christian Couder has signed up the Git project for the December 2025 Outreachy cohort as coordinator, with the signup deadline approaching on September 10. The participation is conditional on finding mentors, suitable interns, and sponsors - with GitLab, GitHub and the Git project itself suggested as potential sponsors given Outreachy's current financial challenges.

The email serves as both an announcement and call for participation from the community. Contributors are invited to get involved as co-mentors, suggest microprojects, or help update the project's outreach documentation. Links are provided for those wanting more information about Outreachy's current situation and the upcoming cohort.

This follows Git's established pattern of participating in Outreachy programs, with Christian Couder continuing his role from previous years. The tone is positive and inclusive, emphasizing that while initial signup has been completed, broader community engagement will determine whether the project ultimately participates in this cohort.
