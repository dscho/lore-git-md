This is a user workflow concern raised in response to Patrick Steinhardt's `contrib/` cleanup series, specifically about the removal of `git-new-workdir`. The email comes from Gabriel Scherer, who reports being an active user of the script despite its removal in Git 2.51.

Scherer explains that while `git worktree` is the modern replacement, it imposes a restriction that prevents checking out the same branch in multiple worktrees - a workflow they frequently use for testing branches. They suggest the restriction might be configurable, but acknowledge that keeping `git-new-workdir` would be simpler than modifying `git worktree`'s.

The email makes two key points: first, that the script remains useful for some workflows despite its age, and second, that its lack of recent updates indicates low maintenance burden. Scherer requests reconsideration of the removal and proposes discussing with other affected users.

This appears to be a thoughtful challenge to one removal in the otherwise comprehensive cleanup series. While most technical aspects of the series are settled, this highlights how even long-unmaintained tools can have niche users. The discussion may need to weigh such workflow needs against the broader goal of reducing unmaintained code.
