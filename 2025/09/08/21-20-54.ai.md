This is a documentation patch fixing formatting in the `git-fast-import` manpage. It's a v2 submission of a previously reviewed patch, now with an Ack from Jeff King.

The patch corrects what appears to be an asciidoc formatting mistake in the documentation for the `feature export-marks` option. The original commit 68061e34702 had incorrectly formatted a security warning paragraph as a literal block (indented text) when it should have been regular paragraph text. The content of the warning remains unchanged - it still advises caution about trusting the fast-import stream generator - but the presentation now matches Git's standard documentation style for such notes.

The change affects four lines in `Documentation/git-fast-import.adoc`, removing the leading indentation. Jeff King confirmed in review that the original indentation was not intended as a special callout block, making this a straightforward documentation formatting fix. The patch is small, focused, and already reviewed, making it likely to be merged quickly. This aligns with ongoing efforts to maintain consistency in Git's documentation, though it's not part of the larger synopsis-style conversion project.
