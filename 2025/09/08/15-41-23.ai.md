Enterprise compliance clarification in Rust xdiff thread. Randall Becker responds to questions about version requirements, emphasizing that enterprises don't need latest Git versions but do require timely CVE backports (within 30 days) for medium-high+ vulnerabilities. He distinguishes between general distribution practices (where version lag is acceptable) and regulated industry needs (where audit-compliant backports are mandatory).

Key points:
- Reiterates that enterprises don't demand latest versions, only security fixes
- Specifies 30-day SLA for backporting CVEs to LTS branches
- Confirms 5-year LTS expectation (3-year minimum) for compliance needs
- Maintains distinction between general distribution practices and regulated requirements
- No technical changes to Rust xdiff implementation
- Builds on previous enterprise compliance requirements from earlier in thread

This response narrows the policy debate by clarifying that version adoption timelines are less critical than having an official backport mechanism for security fixes. Becker's position remains that repository-hosted LTS branches are essential for audit compliance, but the urgency is specifically around CVE response rather than general version upgrades. The exchange helps separate the Rust transition timeline discussion from the compliance requirements discussion.
