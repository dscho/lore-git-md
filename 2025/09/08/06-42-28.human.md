This is a policy clarification in the ongoing Rust infrastructure series, specifically about the LTS support timeline for the C-only version of Git during the Rust transition.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Eli Schwartz's feedback on the proposed 2-year LTS window for maintaining a C-only version. The original proposal suggested handing off maintenance to dependents after two years, and Schwartz emphasizes the importance of keeping this LTS branch officially hosted by the Git project rather than letting it fork elsewhere. Steinhardt agrees, clarifying that while maintenance responsibilities may shift to dependents after the initial period, the branch would still follow the project's normal review and merge processes through the mailing list and Junio Hamano.

The exchange shows alignment on maintaining official project oversight while allowing for community maintenance after the initial support window. This addresses concerns about long-term support for users who cannot immediately adopt the Rust version, particularly those waiting for gccrs compatibility. Steinhardt notes he'll reword the documentation to make this clearer, continuing the series' pattern of refining policy details based on community feedback.
