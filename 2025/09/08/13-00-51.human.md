This is a minor cleanup patch in the reftable fsck validation series. Karthik Nayak responds to Junio Hamano's feedback on the first patch of the series, which alphabetizes the `fsck_msg_type` enum and fixes some formatting issues. 

The original patch attempted to both alphabetize the enum and adjust clang-format spacing in one go, making it hard to review the actual ordering changes. Junio suggested separating these changes, and Karthik agrees to split out the clang-format changes into a separate commit at the end of the series to keep the review focused on the substantive changes.

This is purely a cosmetic change that doesn't affect functionality, just making the code more maintainable. The discussion shows the careful attention to detail in the review process, even for small preparatory patches in a larger series. The patch itself is straightforward and uncontroversial, though the exchange highlights the importance of keeping formatting changes separate from logical changes during review.
