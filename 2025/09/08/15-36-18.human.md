This is a documentation patch in the ongoing command deprecation series, specifically addressing the `git whatchanged` deprecation messaging. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposes a small but meaningful tweak to the warning text in the `git-whatchanged.adoc` man page.

The change removes the "not even shorter to type" justification from the deprecation warning, keeping only the core explanation that `whatchanged` is just `git log` with different defaults. The patch also fixes a grammatical issue by changing "default" to "defaults" to match the plural subject.

This appears to be a refinement based on prior discussion in the thread about making deprecation messages more focused and actionable. The patch maintains the clear recommendation to use `git log --raw --no-merges` as the replacement while removing what was perhaps seen as an unnecessary stylistic critique of the command name length.

As part of the larger deprecation framework (now in its third iteration), this change helps streamline the user-facing messaging while the technical underpinnings for alias shadowing and command discovery were handled in earlier patches of the series. The modification is straightforward and uncontroversial, aligning with the thread's established direction.
