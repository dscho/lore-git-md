Refactoring patch introducing a central packfile management structure as part of the ODB abstraction effort.

This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 15-part series to centralize packfile management in Git's object database. It introduces a new `struct packfile_store` that will eventually become the single source of truth for packfile information, which is scattered across `struct packed_git` and `struct object_database` today.

The patch creates the basic scaffolding - defining the new structure and its allocation/free functions - while leaving it empty for now. Subsequent patches will migrate the actual packfile-related fields into this structure. The change is careful to maintain backward compatibility throughout.

This foundational work enables the larger goal of making object database backends pluggable by providing a clear boundary for packfile management. The series has undergone multiple rounds of review and appears technically sound, with only minor documentation nits remaining in later patches.
