This is a discussion about Git's `git add` behavior with mixed valid/invalid pathspecs. Jon Forrest responds to Jeff King's historical analysis of why Git currently fails completely when given invalid paths (rather than partially succeeding with valid files). While acknowledging the issue isn't critical, Jon suggests a potential solution: adding a command-line flag to let users choose between the current strict behavior or a more permissive mode that ignores invalid files. The proposal is tentative and recognizes the backward compatibility challenges Jeff noted earlier in the thread. The exchange remains exploratory rather than proposing concrete changeset, with both participants treating this as a philosophical design discussion about failure modes in Git's CLI.
