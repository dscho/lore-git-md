Refactoring patch in the xdiff/Rust compatibility series, focusing on removing an indirection layer in xdiff's emit functionality.

This is patch 4/17 in Ezekiel Newren's series preparing xdiff internals for potential Rust integration. The patch removes the `xdl_get_rec()` helper function which was providing indirect access to `xrecord_t` fields, instead having callers access the fields directly. The change makes control flow easier to follow by eliminating the indirection and making field accesses explicit.

The changes touch `xdiff/xemit.c`, modifying several functions that previously used `xdl_get_rec()` to instead directly use `xdf->recs[ri]->ptr` and `xdf->recs[ri]->size`. Elijah Newren provides review feedback suggesting the patch could be split to separate the functional change from style cleanups (like brace placement), and notes that while the style changes align with guidelines, mixing them with the functional change makes review harder.

Key technical details:
- Removed function: `xdl_get_rec()`
- Modified functions: `xdl_emit_record()`, `match_func_rec()`, `is_empty_rec()`
- Field accesses now direct rather than through helper
- Behavior remains identical, just implementation detail change
- Elijah suggests separating style changes (brace placement, whitespace) into a follow-up patch
- The change continues the series' pattern of simplifying xdiff internals while maintaining external behavior

The patch appears uncontroversial in its technical approach, though the review suggests the series could benefit from separating functional changes from style cleanups for easier review. This continues the incremental refactoring approach seen in earlier patches of the series.
