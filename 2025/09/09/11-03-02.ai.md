2. Adds a new `initialized` boolean field to `struct packfile_store` in packfile.h
3. Updates all references in packfile.c to use the new location and boolean syntax

This maintains identical behavior while continuing the architectural cleanup. The patch touches 3 files with 9 lines changed (6 additions, 9 deletions). As with previous patches in the series, this is an internal refactoring with no user-visible changes, just moving state to its more logical home as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort.
