This is a brief follow-up discussion in the ongoing color handling bugfix series, specifically about patch 2/4 which makes `add-interactive` respect `color.diff` settings. Patrick Steinhardt had previously suggested using `GIT_COLOR_UNKNOWN` instead of `-1` in the new `check_color_config()` function. Jeff King explains why this would require a much broader refactoring of Git's color handling system, noting that `git_config_colorbool()` also uses `-1` and that the codebase mixes boolean and tri-state color handling inconsistently. He argues for keeping the current `-1` usage to match existing patterns and avoid scope creep in this regression fix. Patrick accepts this rationale, indicating the discussion on this point is resolved. The exchange shows careful consideration of codebase consistency while pragmatically prioritizing the immediate bugfix over a larger architectural cleanup.
