Performance tuning discussion for NFS-hosted Git repositories with inode constraints.

Kousik Sanagavarapu follows up on earlier configuration advice from Brian M. Carlson regarding GC settings for repositories on NFS storage. The thread has been exploring ways to manage inode exhaustion when hosting thousands of small repositories, with previous messages covering pack optimizations and filesystem tuning.

Brian had warned that Kousik's current settings (`gc.auto=1` and `autopacklimit=1`) would trigger expensive repacks after every push, sharing that their workplace uses a more moderate approach of repacking every ~40 pushes. Kousik acknowledges this feedback and plans to experiment with higher values to find a sustainable balance between inode conservation and performance.

The exchange confirms that `transfer.unpackLimit=1` (previously recommended) is safe from a performance perspective, as major forges use this setting successfully. The discussion remains focused on practical operational tradeoffs rather than introducing new technical solutions, with Kousik now testing adjustments to the repack frequency based on Brian's real-world benchmarks.
