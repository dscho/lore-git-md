The changes are mechanical and straightforward - moving the MRU list initialization and operations from `struct object_database` to `struct packfile_store`, where it logically belongs as part of the packfile management system. The patch updates all callers to use the new location while maintaining identical runtime behavior.

This continues the series' pattern of clean, incremental refactoring steps that improve code organization without changing functionality.
