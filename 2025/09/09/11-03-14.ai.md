This is the final patch (15/15) in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, continuing the effort to move packfile operations to a dedicated subsystem. The patch refactors `get_packed_git_mru()` to operate on a packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` in the process.

The change follows the series' established pattern of encapsulating packfile operations within the packfile store subsystem. The function now takes a `struct packfile_store*` parameter instead of `struct repository*`, and its implementation is simplified to directly access the store's MRU list after ensuring the store is prepared. The patch updates the single caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to use the new interface.

This completes the mechanical transition of all major packfile-related functions to operate on the packfile store rather than the repository object. The series has systematically moved all packfile state and operations out of `struct object_database` and into the dedicated `struct packfile_store`, achieving better separation of concerns while maintaining identical behavior. With this final patch, the packfile store refactoring appears complete and ready for merging.
