The Git Project Leadership Committee (PLC) has issued its annual status report to the community, covering financial health, trademark matters, and governance under the Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC). The project's finances remain strong with $99k in reserves, up $6k from last year, primarily supported by donations (97%) and Amazon royalties (3%). Notable expenses included increased conference travel costs and trademark filings, while savings were achieved by moving git-scm.com to GitHub Pages.

The PLC reports ongoing trademark enforcement, including a renewal and action against infringement, while maintaining the existing policy. They raise questions about future spending priorities, such as potentially self-funding Outreachy internships currently sponsored by GitHub and GitLab. The four PLC members (Junio Hamano, Christian Couder, Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason, and Taylor Blau) emphasize that the SFC handles only non-technical aspects, with no influence on technical direction. Community discussion is invited both on the mailing list and at the upcoming Contributor Summit.
