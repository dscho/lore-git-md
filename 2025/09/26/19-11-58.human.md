This is an annual status report from the Git Project Leadership Committee (PLC) about the project's finances and activities under the Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC). Taylor Blau provides the 2025 update ahead of Git Merge, following the same format as previous years.

The report covers three main areas: financials, trademark matters, and potential future expenses. Financially, the project remains healthy with $99k USD in accounts (a $6k increase from last year), mostly from donations and Amazon affiliate royalties. Notable expenses include $1,846 for conference travel and $1,250 for trademark filings. The project has saved money by moving git-scm.com to GitHub Pages.

On trademarks, the PLC handled two filings this year and continues to field requests about the "GIT" mark usage, though the policy remains unchanged. The report raises questions about potential future spending, particularly around funding Outreachy internships independently if corporate sponsorships decrease.

The tone is transparent and invites community input, with opportunities for further discussion at the upcoming Git Merge event. The report maintains continuity with previous years while highlighting new developments like the trademark filings and hosting changes.
