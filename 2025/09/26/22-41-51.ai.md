This is a refactoring patch in the xdiff modernization series, specifically removing the now-unused `xdl_get_rec()` helper function. The patch is part of Ezekiel Newren's ongoing effort to clean up xdiff internals for better maintainability and potential Rust compatibility.

The change eliminates `xdl_get_rec()` which was previously used to walk a linked list of records, but has become unnecessary since the records are now accessed directly as a contiguous array. The function's simple operation (just returning a record pointer and size) is inlined at its three call sites in `xdl_emit_record()`, `match_func_rec()`, and `is_empty_rec()`. 

This is a straightforward mechanical cleanup that:
1. Removes 16 lines of code while adding 7 (net -9)
2. Touches only xdiff/xemit.c
3. Maintains identical behavior
4. Follows the series' pattern of simplifying xdiff's internal structure
5. Was likely identified during the broader structural simplifications in earlier patches

The change is low-risk and uncontroversial, being a mechanical removal of code that is provably unused after previous refactorings. It continues the series' trend of eliminating unnecessary indirection in the xdiff codebase. The commit message clearly explains the rationale for removal, noting the historical context while justifying the current change.
