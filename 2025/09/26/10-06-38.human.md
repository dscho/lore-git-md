This is a licensing discussion in the ongoing Rust infrastructure thread, focusing on the practical implications of a proposed GPLv2+exception approach for Git's Rust code.

Phillip Wood responds to Jeff King's clarification about the intent behind the licensing proposal, pointing out that Johannes Schindelin's earlier suggestion specifically mentioned an exception for GitOxide (similar to libgit2's approach). Wood expresses skepticism about the practicality of such a project-specific exception, noting it could create confusion about code provenance or enable license circumvention.

The exchange highlights the tension between wanting permissive reuse (King's position) and concerns about license tracking complexity and potential loopholes (Wood's position). Wood references libgit2's existing git.git-authors file as a real-world example of the challenges in managing project-specific exceptions. The discussion remains unresolved but is moving toward seeking formal legal guidance through the Software Freedom Conservancy.
