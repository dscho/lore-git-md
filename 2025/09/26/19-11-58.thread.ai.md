PLC considering self-funding Outreachy internships (currently sponsored by GitHub/GitLab). Couder suggests first approaching GitLab/GitHub for sponsorship as in past years before committing project funds.

PLC maintains oversight of non-technical aspects while SFC has no technical influence. Community input invited via mailing list discussion and upcoming Contributor Summit.
