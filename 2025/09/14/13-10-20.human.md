This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the deprecated `git whatchanged` command. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Bjoern Bastian's preference for the command's brevity by suggesting a simple alias as an alternative. The proposed `wh` alias would provide the same convenience of quick typing while using the non-deprecated `git log --raw --no-merged` equivalent. 

The exchange highlights the tension between official deprecation and user habits - while the project wants to simplify its interface, some users continue relying on deprecated commands for their workflow efficiency. The suggestion of creating aliases appears to be a practical middle ground that could satisfy both maintainers' goals and users' needs. 

No new arguments about the deprecation policy itself are introduced here - this is purely a technical suggestion for users who want to maintain their current workflow efficiency while moving away from the deprecated command. The thread continues to demonstrate that command-line convenience remains a significant factor in user behavior, even when functionality is available through other means.
