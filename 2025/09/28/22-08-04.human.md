This is patch 14 in Taylor Blau's 49-part series refactoring `builtin/repack.c` to remove global variables and prepare for incremental MIDX-based repacking. The patch moves the `prepare_pack_objects` function out of `builtin/repack.c` and into the new `repack.c` module, making it available as a general utility function.

The function, which constructs the command-line arguments for `git pack-objects`, had previously been converted to use the new `pack_objects_args` struct in earlier patches. Now that it no longer depends on any static variables, it can be safely moved to the shared location. The patch maintains all existing functionality while continuing the series' goal of modularizing the repack codebase.

This is a straightforward step in the larger refactoring effort, demonstrating the incremental progress being made to break up the monolithic repack implementation. The change is mechanical but meaningful - each such move brings the codebase closer to the series' dual goals of eliminating globals and enabling future MIDX improvements.
