This is a meta-commentary on patch submission practices from Junio Hamano, responding to Taylor Blau's cover letter for the repack refactoring series.

Junio highlights and praises two specific aspects of Taylor's submission that he considers exemplary: (1) clearly stating dependencies on other in-flight topics (the ps/packfile-store series), and (2) situating the work within a larger effort (tb/incremental-midx-part-3) while suggesting a logical topic branch name. 

The email suggests these practices should be documented for contributors, emphasizing three key points: declaring non-master dependencies, explaining how a series fits into a larger vision, and proposing topic branch names. This isn't about the technical content of the repack series itself, but rather about maintaining good workflow hygiene in the project's development process.

The context shows this comes at the conclusion of a major 49-patch refactoring effort that modularized Git's repack machinery while removing global variables - a series that itself followed these very practices being commended.
