This is patch 19/49 in Taylor Blau's series refactoring `builtin/repack.c` for modularity and `the_repository` removal. The patch moves the `existing_packs` API out of the builtin and into the new `repack.c` module, completing its isolation from the main repack logic.

The `existing_packs` API tracks packfiles during repack operations, classifying them as kept (via .keep files), non-kept, or cruft packs, and handles their deletion marking/retention. The patch relocates 173 lines from `builtin/repack.c` to `repack.c` while adding 35 lines of declarations to `repack.h`. Key functions moved include:
- `existing_packs_collect()` - classifies packs into the three categories
- `existing_packs_mark_for_deletion()` - flags packs for removal
- `existing_packs_remove_redundant()` - executes pack deletions
- Various helper functions for pack marking/unmarking

The move follows the series' pattern of extracting functionality into modules while maintaining behavior. Some functions become static within `repack.c` since they're no longer needed externally. The patch builds on prior work standardizing pack handling (patch 15) and parameterizing pack directory operations (patch 17), and prepares for further modularization where pack tracking will interact with MIDX functionality.
