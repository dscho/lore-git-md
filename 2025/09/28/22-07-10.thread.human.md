Taylor Blau's extensive repack refactoring series continues to progress, now at version 2 with 49 patches. The series systematically modularizes Git's repack machinery while eliminating global variables like `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo`. Jeff King (Peff) has reviewed the entire series and provided mostly minor feedback, which Taylor has been incorporating. The latest patch (21/49) factors out pack installation logic into a new `generated_pack_install()` function, continuing the pattern of moving functionality from `builtin/repack.c` into dedicated modules while maintaining identical behavior. This builds on patch 20/49's standardization of the `generated_pack` naming convention.

The series is nearing completion, with only patches 4, 39, and 45-49 still needing review before final submission. Taylor has addressed feedback on patches 43-44, including API design concerns from Peff and documentation requests from Patrick Steinhardt. The work lays important groundwork for future repack improvements while carefully balancing clean interfaces with legacy behavior. Peff endorses the overall approach, and the series demonstrates Taylor's methodical strategy of combining multiple modernization efforts (global variable elimination, interface design, and MIDX infrastructure) into a coherent whole.
