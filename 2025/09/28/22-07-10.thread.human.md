Taylor Blau continues his substantial refactoring of Git's repack machinery with part 12/49 of his series, building on Jeff King's thorough review of earlier patches. The series systematically modularizes `builtin/repack.c` while eliminating global variables like `the_repository` and preparing for future MIDX-based repacking improvements.

This latest patch introduces a `struct repack_config_ctx` to cleanly pass both cruft` and non-cruft `pack_objects_args` to configuration callbacks, laying groundwork to eventually remove the `delta_base_offset` static variable in a future patch. While the change is mechanical - maintaining existing behavior while restructuring the plumbing - it demonstrates the series' careful, incremental approach to untangling the repack codebase.

Jeff King has completed his review of the full 49-part series with mostly minor feedback, endorsing the overall approach. Taylor has incorporated suggestions like using `starts_with()` instead of `skip_prefix` and adding clearer documentation. With patches 1-12 now refined and patches 4,39,45-49 still awaiting review, the series appears to be nearing completion. The work forms part of Git's broader repack machinery overhaul and contributes to multiple ongoing modernization efforts including global variable elimination and hash algorithm independence.
