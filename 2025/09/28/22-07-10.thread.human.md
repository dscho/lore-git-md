Junio Hamano praised the series' organization, while Patrick Steinhardt has provided thorough code review across multiple patches. Patrick's feedback has been constructive but not blocking, ranging from style suggestions (like using C99 designated initializers) to questions about specific design choices (such as retaining the `packdir` variable). His latest comment is minor - just a grammatical tweak to a commit message in patch 22/49 to change "accesses" to "accessed" for tense consistency. 

The series appears technically sound, with Patrick's reviews focusing on polish rather than fundamental issues. A few loose ends remain noted as `NEEDSWORK` items, particularly around delta optimization and promisor file handling), but these are deferred rather than blocking.
