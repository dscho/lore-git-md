Taylor Blau's ongoing refactoring of `builtin/repack.c` continues to receive detailed review from Jeff King (Peff) and Patrick Steinhardt, with the first 37 of 49 patches now thoroughly examined. Peff has given general approval to the architectural approach of splitting the 1700+ line file into modular components while eliminating global variables, though he raised minor naming and design questions about some interfaces. Taylor acknowledges these points while explaining the pragmatic trade-offs made - some interfaces like `existing_packs` could theoretically be cleaner, but the current approach minimizes risk when moving fragile repack code. 

The discussion reveals the careful balance being struck between code movement and interface changes, with both Taylor and reviewers recognizing that this series establishes a foundation for future improvements rather than perfecting every interface immediately. Patrick Steinhardt has focused on interface quality in his reviews, catching missing const qualifiers and suggesting documentation improvements, all of which Taylor has accepted and will incorporate in a respun series planned for early next week. 

Meanwhile, a parallel discussion initiated by Junio Hamano about improving documentation of patch series submission practices continues, with Taylor citing existing documentation commits and planning to submit a formal series on the topic. The repack refactoring work remains on track, with only 7 substantial patches left to review and general alignment between all participants on both the technical direction and the need for some pragmatic compromises in this large-scale refactoring effort.
