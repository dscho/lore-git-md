Taylor Blau continues refining his 49-part series refactoring Git's repack machinery, with the latest patch focusing on eliminating the global `the_hash_algo` variable. The series, now at v2, systematically modularizes `builtin/repack.c` while removing reliance on global state, with Jeff King (Peff) having reviewed the entire series and Taylor incorporating feedback.

The newest change modifies `write_oid()` to use a new `write_oid_context` struct that carries both the child process handle and repository-specific hash algorithm, rather than using the global variable. This maintains identical behavior while following the series' pattern of threading repository data through callbacks. The change is mechanical but important for the broader goal of removing global state.

Peff has completed his review of all 49 patches with mostly minor suggestions, several of which Taylor has already implemented. The series is nearing completion, with just patches 4, 39, and 45-49 still needing review. Taylor has demonstrated careful attention to interface design throughout, balancing ideal abstractions with practical constraints of the existing codebase. The work lays important groundwork for future repack functionality while contributing to Git's ongoing modernization efforts around global variable elimination and interface design.
