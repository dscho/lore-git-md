This is a major refactoring series preparing for incremental MIDX-based repacking, split into 49 patches. Taylor Blau is restructuring the `builtin/repack.c` codebase to make it more maintainable before adding new functionality.

The series moves most of the repacking logic out of the monolithic builtin and into several new compilation units under a new `repack.h` API, organized by functionality: cruft packs, geometry repacking, MIDX handling, promisor objects, etc. The author notes this is purely structural - no behavior changes are introduced, just code movement with careful attention to maintainability.

Key aspects:
- First 10 patches reduce use of `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` globals
- Subsequent patches systematically move chunks of functionality to new files
- Each commit does minimal changes to make review easier (suggesting `--color-moved`)
- The end result is a much cleaner `builtin/repack.c` (reduced from 1700+ to more manageable size)

This is preparatory work for future incremental MIDX/bitmap repacking features, though not strictly required for them. The series depends on an earlier packfile store refactoring (commit dd52a29b78). The author suggests queuing this as `tb/incremental-midx-part-3.1`.

The scale of the refactoring (49 patches touching many files) suggests this will need careful review, but the incremental approach and clear organization should help. The changes appear methodical and well-motivated, with each patch focused on a specific subsystem move. This looks like solid groundwork for future repacking improvements.
