This is patch 45 in a 49-part series refactoring Git's repack machinery, combining modularization with `the_repository` removal. The patch moves `finish_pack_objects_cmd()` from `builtin/repack.c` to the new `repack.c` module, continuing the series' pattern of extracting standalone functionality.

The function processes output from `pack-objects`, validating object IDs and tracking generated packfiles. The move is straightforward - the implementation is copied verbatim to the new location while adding its declaration to `repack.h`. This follows the series' established methodology of first parameterizing dependencies (done in previous patches) before relocating functions.

The change prepares for future extraction of two remaining pack-writing functions (`write_cruft_pack()` and `write_filtered_pack()`). The patch maintains identical behavior while reducing `builtin/repack.c`'s size and improving modularity. As with earlier patches in the series, this is a clean, focused change that advances the overall goal of modernizing the repack implementation.
