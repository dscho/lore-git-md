This is part 42 of Taylor Blau's ongoing series to refactor `builtin/repack.c` into modular components while removing global variables. The patch moves the `find_pack_prefix()` function out of the builtin and into the new `repack.c` module, where it becomes `write_pack_opts_pack_prefix()`.

The change eliminates redundant pattern where callers had to manually compute the pack prefix before constructing a `write_pack_opts` struct. Now the prefix is handled internally by the pack-writing functions, reducing boilerplate and preventing potential parameter-swapping mistakes. The patch updates two call sites (`write_filtered_pack()` and `write_cruft_pack()`) to use the new helper function.

This continues the series' consistent approach of encapsulating functionality and reducing direct parameter passing. The change is relatively small but fits into the larger goal of making the repack code more maintainable and less error-prone. As with previous patches in the series, the modification maintains identical behavior while improving the code structure.
