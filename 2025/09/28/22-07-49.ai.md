This is the 10/49 patch in Taylor Blau's series refactoring `builtin/repack.c` as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch continues the systematic elimination of global variable dependencies, now targeting `hash_to_hex()` usage in pack geometry handling.

The author notes this represents the practical limit of straightforward `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` removal in this area, with remaining cases like `is_bare_repository()` requiring more involved refactoring. The change is small (4 lines) but continues the series' consistent approach to global variable elimination while preserving existing behavior.
