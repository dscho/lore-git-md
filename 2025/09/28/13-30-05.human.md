This is a technical follow-up in the gitk window geometry persistence series. Mike Rappazzo responds to Johannes Sixt's question about why the `after idle` callback is needed for geometry restoration on some platforms but not others.

Mike explains his testing results across MacOS, Windows 11, and Gnome (Ubuntu), confirming platform-specific behavior where MacOS works without the deferred callback while Windows and Gnome require it. He theorizes this relates to differences in how window managers handle initial window mapping and geometry setting sequences, with `after idle` ensuring the geometry is applied after the window manager completes its setup.

The email serves as both an explanation of the current behavior and a preview of the upcoming v3 patch series, where Mike plans to split the `manage_showrefs_geometry` function as previously discussed. The response demonstrates careful cross-platform testing and engagement with the technical details of the Tcl/Tk window management behavior.
