Refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal and repack modularization series. This is patch 16/49 in Taylor Blau's incremental MIDX preparation work.

The patch moves the `remove_redundant_pack()` function from `builtin/repack.c` to the new `repack.c` module, renaming it to `repack_remove_redundant_pack()` to follow the subsystem's naming conventions. The function handles cleanup of redundant packfiles, including MIDX cache invalidation when needed. This is part of the broader effort to extract repack functionality from the monolithic builtin into modular components.

Key technical details:
- Functionality remains identical, just relocated and renamed
- Now accessible via `repack.h` header for other repack-related code
- Still takes explicit `repository` parameter (no `the_repository` usage)
- Handles both packfile deletion and MIDX cache maintenance
- Two call sites updated in `builtin/repack.c` (pack geometry and existing packs code)

The change is mechanical but important for the series' architectural goals. It follows the pattern established in prior patches of moving repack-specific functionality out of the builtin while maintaining clean separation of concerns. The patch continues the consistent, incremental approach seen throughout the series.
