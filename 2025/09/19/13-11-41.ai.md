Bugfix follow-up identifying regression source in stash double-free crash. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Lauri Niskanen's original report of a double-free crash in `git stash show -p` with invalid options, pinpointing the regression to commit 748bd094 ("builtin/stash: fix leak in `show_stash()`", 2024-06-11). The crash occurs when error handling in `show_stash()` attempts to free already-freed memory via `strvec_clear()` after option parsing fails.

The technical context remains unchanged from the original report: invalid arguments (like `--invalid`) trigger a crash with "free(): double free detected in tcache 2" due to incorrect memory management during cleanup. The bisect result suggests this was introduced by a recent leak-fix commit that may have disrupted the ownership semantics of the strvec being cleared.

This moves the thread forward by identifying the specific change that introduced the regression, though the exact fix strategy (whether to adjust the error handling in show_stash() or revise the strvec management from 748bd094) remains open. The narrow scope (only affecting invalid-option cases in `show_stash()`) and clear regression trail make this a strong candidate for backport consideration once fixed.
