2. Makes the `check-docs` target depend on `Documentation/GIT-EXCLUDED-PROGRAMS` to ensure documentation linting respects the exclusion list.

The author notes the dependencies around `GIT-EXCLUDED-PROGRAMS` are still problematic but suggests this as a first step toward cleanup.
