This is a technical discussion about fixing memory management issues in Git's argument parsing, specifically around `strvec_clear()` and `setup_revisions()`.

Jeff King (peff) responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about their proposed minimal NULL-termination fix for the double-free bug in `git stash show`. While Jeff initially suggested simply NULL-terminating at the boundary of consumed arguments, Junio pointed out this might not be sufficient when multiple elements are consumed, since `strvec_clear()` operates on the full `.nr` count rather than stopping at NULL.

Jeff acknowledges this reveals deeper problems - many parts of the codebase rely on the current behavior where consumed arguments remain untouched. He's working on a more comprehensive solution that addresses these dependencies but hasn't sent patches yet due to the complexity. The discussion highlights the tension between a minimal fix for the immediate crash and properly addressing the underlying architectural issues with strvec` management in `setup_revisions()`.
