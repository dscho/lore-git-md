Performance optimization in xdiff refactoring series. This is patch 8/10 in the preparatory cleanups for Rust compatibility, focusing on replacing xdiff's arena allocator (chastore_t) with a simpler array-based approach for storing xrecord_t entries.

The change removes the chastore_t arena from xdfile_t and switches to using a contiguous xrecord_t array, which provides a 5% performance improvement according to the benchmarks shown. The patch touches multiple xdiff/ files to update all the record access patterns from pointer-based to direct array access.

This continues the series' pattern of simplifying xdiff internals while maintaining identical behavior. The commit message explains the historical context (chastore_t being a leftover from when records were linked lists) and notes the FFI benefits for future Rust integration. The performance measurements show this change offsets the 5% regression from patch 6's hash array removal.

The patch is well-documented with clear benchmarks and follows the series' pattern of including "--color-words" viewing instructions for reviewers. It has already received technical approval from Phillip Wood in earlier versions.
