This thread documents a regression in `git stash show -p` where passing invalid options (like `--invalid`) triggers a double-free crash with the error "free(): double free detected in tcache 2". The original report from Lauri Niskanen identified the crash occurring during error cleanup in `show_stash()` when `strvec_clear()` attempts to free already-freed memory.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk followed up by bisecting the regression to commit 748bd094 ("builtin/stash: fix leak in `show_stash()`", 2024-06-11), which inadvertently disrupted the memory ownership semantics during error handling. The issue is narrowly scoped to invalid-option cases in `show_stash()` and doesn't affect other stash subcommands. 

While the technical details are now well-understood (the crash occurs at `builtin/stash.c:1047` during cleanup after failed option parsing), the thread is still waiting for a proposed fix. The solution will likely involve adjusting either the error handling in `show_stash()` or revising the strvec management from the problematic commit. Given the clear regression trail and severity (crash on invalid input), this will likely be a candidate for backporting once fixed.
