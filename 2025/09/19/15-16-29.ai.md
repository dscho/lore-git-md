Refactoring patch in the xdiff preparation series for Rust compatibility.

This patch (v3 07/10) continues the xdiff internals cleanup by simplifying the `xdlclass_t` struct in xdiff/xprepare.c. It removes three redundant fields (`ha`, `line`, and `size`) that were aliases of fields in the contained `xrecord_t` struct, replacing them with direct use of the `rec` field. The changes affect record classification logic but maintain identical behavior.

Key technical details:
- Removes `xdlclass_t.ha` (now accessed via `rec.ha`)
- Removes `xdlclass_t.line` (now `rec.ptr`)
- Removes `xdlclass_t.size` (now `rec.size`)
- Updates all access sites in `xdl_classify_record()` to use the indirection
- Includes `--color-words` trailer for clear review of field access changes
- The change reduces potential confusion from field aliasing
- No performance impact expected (purely structural change)

- Builds on patch 6's removal of `xdfile_t.ha` array
