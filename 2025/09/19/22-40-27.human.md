The immediate problem is that when `setup_revisions()` drops "--" from the argument list, it leaks the memory for those removed elements. Jeff King's solution follows the pattern used elsewhere by setting `free_removed_argv_elements` to properly free consumed arguments.

The patch includes a test case that would fail under leak detection before the fix. While the test case itself (`git stash show --`) isn't particularly meaningful (since the "--" does nothing here), it serves to verify the leak is fixed without locking in any particular behavior. The commit message notes there may be room for improving `git stash show`'s argument parsing in general, but that's left as potential future work.
