This is a refactoring patch in the xdiff preparation series for Rust compatibility. It simplifies the `xdlclass_t` struct by removing redundant fields that were aliasing fields from `xrecord_t` (`ha`, `line`, and `size`), replacing them with a direct `xrecord_t` member. The change eliminates potential confusion from having duplicate fields while maintaining identical behavior.

The patch updates all references to the removed fields to instead access them through the new `rec` member. This is a straightforward cleanup that reduces the cognitive overhead of maintaining parallel field sets and helps prevent future refactoring mistakes where the aliased fields might get out of sync.

As part of the larger preparatory series (patches 1-10) that's being merged to 'next', this change continues the theme of simplifying xdiff's internal data structures without altering functionality. The patch includes a "Best-viewed-with" trailer to help reviewers see the changes clearly with word-level diff highlighting.
