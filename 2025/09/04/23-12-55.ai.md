Junio Hamano raises conceptual objections to Patrick Steinhardt's commit graph refactoring series in the_repository removal/pluggable ODB effort. While acknowledging the technical execution of moving commit graph tracking from object_database to odb_source, Hamano argues the change misunderstands commit graphs' fundamental role - they logically span the entire object store rather than individual sources. He presents a scenario where commit graphs might cover history ranges that cross source boundaries (like semi-online vs readily available storage), noting graphs don't intrinsically depend on packfile internals like MIDX does. This challenges the series' core premise that commit graphs "belong" to specific sources, suggesting the architectural direction may need reconsideration despite the clean implementation. The critique focuses on design philosophy rather than code quality, marking a potential pivot point for the pluggable ODB effort's approach to commit graphs.
