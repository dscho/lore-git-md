Maintainer response regarding Rust build system concerns. Junio Hamano responds to brian m. carlson's critique of the Rust infrastructure patch series, specifically the meson version requirements and Makefile support.

The exchange highlights two key practical issues with the current proposal: First, the meson 1.9.0 requirement conflicts with Git's platform support policy (which targets meson 0.56.2) and would prevent most users from testing the Rust integration, as even recent Debian/Ubuntu releases ship older versions. Second, the lack of Makefile support excludes many build environments including Debian's official packaging.

Junio acknowledges these concerns while maintaining a neutral stance, noting the limitations were already signaled in the cover letter. The response suggests these are serious practical barriers that need addressing before the Rust infrastructure can be widely usable, though stops short of demanding specific fixes. This appears to be part of the ongoing technical vetting of the broader Rust proposal rather than a rejection.
