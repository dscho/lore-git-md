This is a performance investigation update regarding self-compiled Git binaries on macOS. D. Ben Knoble follows up on their earlier report of a 5x slowdown in their self-built Git compared to Homebrew's version, now noting the issue may be specific to their managed corporate machine. 

The key new observation is that on a personal (non-corporate) machine, the performance difference disappears, suggesting the slowdown may be related to corporate security tooling rather than build configuration. The developer had tried building with `-Oz` optimization flags but saw inconsistent application during the build process.

This doesn't yet explain the binary size difference (4.4MB vs 3.5MB) noted in the original report, but shifts focus from build flags to potential environmental factors. The thread remains open for further investigation into whether corporate security scanners might be interfering with the self-built binary's performance.
