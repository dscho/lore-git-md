This is a follow-up discussion about the deprecation of `git whatchanged` and improving its user messaging. Kristoffer Haugsbakk is considering revisions to their patch series that updates the warning messages for this deprecated command.

The original series (kh/you-still-use-whatchanged-fix) improved the deprecation warnings to suggest `git log` as an alternative and cleaned up related documentation. Now Haugsbakk is contemplating two potential next steps based on feedback: either incorporating Jeff King's suggested patch into a v3 of their series, or making it a follow-up. The key addition would be mentioning that users can create an alias to shadow the deprecated command.

The discussion appears to be refining the user experience around this long-deprecated command, with attention to making the transition as smooth as possible for users who might still be using it. The patch series is currently marked for merging to 'next' in Junio's "What's cooking" report.
