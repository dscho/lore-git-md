This is a refactoring patch as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting the sequencer code. It's patch 4/18 in Patrick Steinhardt's v3 RFC series introducing a new `git-history` command.

The patch modifies `sequencer_remove_state()` to accept a `struct repository` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. While this is a straightforward change, the commit message notes that much of the sequencer still uses `the_repository` and that broader refactoring is left for future work.

The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching call sites in `builtin/rebase.c`, `builtin/revert.c`, and multiple locations in `sequencer.c`. The patch appears to be a clean, focused step in the larger goal of removing global state from Git's codebase, though it's just one piece of a much larger series introducing new history editing functionality.
