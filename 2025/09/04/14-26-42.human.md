This is a major infrastructure proposal announcing Git's planned transition to requiring Rust as part of its build system, starting with Git 3.0. Patrick Steinhardt introduces this as a "test balloon" series to experiment with Rust integration, focusing first on the simple varint subsystem conversion to validate C-Rust interoperability.

The RFC outlines three key goals: allowing experimentation time, giving distributors time to adapt to new toolchain requirements, and formally announcing Rust will become mandatory. The current implementation is minimal - just Meson support and a varint.rs conversion - with explicit acknowledgement that CI jobs, formatting tools, and Makefile support will need to be added later.

The patch series includes three parts: Meson build infrastructure for Rust, the varint subsystem conversion, and a BreakingChanges document update announcing the future requirement. The approach is deliberately incremental, separating the infrastructure discussion from potential future Rust-based features.

This appears to be a serious, well-motivated proposal from an established contributor, though the mandatory Rust requirement will likely generate significant discussion given its impact on build requirements and platform support. The technical implementation looks clean for this initial step, focusing on a non-critical subsystem to validate the approach.
