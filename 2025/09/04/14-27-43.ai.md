This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` RFC series (v3, patch 13/18). It continues the work of separating the interactive add/patch subsystem from the add-interactive code.

The patch removes the remaining dependency on `add-interactive.h` by:
1. Replacing the embedded `add_i_state` struct with direct `repository` pointer and `interactive_config` fields in `add_p_state`
2. Updating all references to use the new `cfg` field instead of the old `s` path
3. Switching from `init_add_i_state()` to `interactive_config_init()`
4. Replacing `clear_add_i_state()` with `interactive_config_clear()`

The changes are mechanical - no behavior modifications - and complete the separation started in the previous patch. This continues the series' pattern of careful subsystem boundary definition while building new functionality. The patch is cleanly scoped and well-explained, with all 68 lines of changes being straightforward field access updates.

This is part of the larger preparatory work for the `git-history` command, specifically the infrastructure cleanup needed before implementing the interactive history editing features. The separation of concerns here will make the subsequent history editing implementation cleaner.
