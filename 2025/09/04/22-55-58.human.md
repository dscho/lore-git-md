This is a status update in the ongoing Rust integration series, specifically regarding the FFI tooling discussion. Ezekiel Newren confirms he's now actively incorporating cbindgen (a tool for generating C bindings from Rust code) throughout the patch series, reversing his earlier position. He explains this change came from hands-on experience with Rust FFI implementation, where cbindgen proved more practical than manual bindings.

The update indicates the series is progressing toward a v2 submission with cbindgen integrated from the start, though not yet ready for review. This aligns with brian m. carlson's earlier recommendation and moves the technical debate about FFI tooling into concrete implementation work. The change suggests the Rust adoption effort is maturing through practical experience rather than theoretical debate.
