This is a documentation refinement patch in the final stages of the xdiff internals refactoring series. Ezekiel Newren responds to Phillip Wood's request for clearer rationale in the commit message about removing temporary variable aliases (rchg1/rchg2) in xdl_build_script().

The email provides three concise reasons for the change: better tooling support (like ctags/IDE navigation), reduced refactoring churn since the struct fields will be modified later, and the fact that the aliases didn't add any semantic meaning beyond the original struct fields. This addresses Phillip Wood's feedback from earlier in the thread about documenting the purpose behind these final cleanups.

The change is purely internal - it doesn't affect behavior or performance - and represents one of the last steps in the multi-phase xdiff modernization effort that has already seen most of its preparatory patches merged. The discussion here is focused on polishing the documentation before this final batch of patches is ready for merging.
