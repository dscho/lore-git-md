This is a refactoring patch discussion in the ongoing ref optimization thread. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Meet Soni's initial architectural patch that introduced a new `refs_optimize()` API alongside the existing `pack_refs` infrastructure. While agreeing with the conceptual direction, Patrick questions the need to maintain both `pack_refs_fn` and `optimize_fn` when they currently perform the same function. He suggests a more direct approach: renaming the existing infrastructure (functions, callbacks, and structs) to use "optimize" terminology throughout rather than adding parallel APIs. The key technical points are the proposed renames of `pack_refs_fn` to `optimize_refs_fn`, `refs_pack_refs()` to `refs_optimize()`, and `struct pack_refs_opts` to `struct refs_optimize_opts`. Patrick acknowledges this would create a larger patch but argues the change is justified given the limited number of affected call sites. This feedback represents a potential simplification of the architecture before the series progresses further, though it would require updating subsequent patches in the series that build on the current dual-API approach.
