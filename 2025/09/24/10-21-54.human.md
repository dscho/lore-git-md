This is a review response in the final stages of the xdiff refactoring series aimed at modernizing the codebase for Rust compatibility. Phillip Wood provides feedback on patch 12/13, which introduces enum-like macros (NONE/SOME/TOO_MANY) to replace magic numbers in the matching logic and renames dis1/dis2 variables to matches1/matches2.

Phillip generally approves of the changes but offers several minor suggestions:
1. Questions whether related variables rdis/rpdis should also be renamed for consistency
2. Notes some oddly formatted comments that weren't reflowed
3. Suggests initializing variables at declaration rather than later
4. Recommends using "ret' or "res" instead of "status" for error codes
5. Points out the !! may not be needed when converting to bool
6. Observes some lines are getting long with the new names

The review is positive overall, praising the BUG() check for invalid states and the bool conversion. These are the final tweaks to a series that has already seen extensive review, with all major technical changes settled. The feedback focuses on minor style and naming polish before the series is ready for merging.
