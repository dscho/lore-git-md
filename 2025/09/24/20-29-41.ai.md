The discussion highlights the tension between ideal documentation (clear, complete) and real-world usage patterns (users often skip reading). Knoble's feedback continues the thread's theme of balancing technical precision with practical usability, while maintaining the series' goal of reducing confusion around `git pull` behaviors.
