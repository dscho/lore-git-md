This is a review comment on patch 8/9 in the SHA-1/SHA-256 interoperability series, focusing on test infrastructure changes. Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on two test-related changes from brian m. carlson's patch.

Patrick suggests a minor wording improvement ("if" -> "whether") in the comment.
