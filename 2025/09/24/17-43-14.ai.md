This is a governance discussion email in the Rust infrastructure series, specifically addressing the long-term support (LTS) policy wording in `BreakingChanges.adoc`. The key development is alignment between Steinhardt and Hamano on keeping the LTS governance flexible by removing specific backporting and tagging procedures from the breaking changes document. The new wording leaves the exact handover process to be determined when needed, rather than specifying it upfront. This resolves one of the remaining open governance questions in the series while maintaining the phased Rust adoption timeline (optional in 2.52, default in 2.53, mandatory by 3.0).

The email confirms the project can proceed with other consensus items while this deferred decision remains compatible with both Hamano's preference for flexibility and Steinhardt's phased approach. This follows earlier discussions where Steinhardt expressed willingness to serve as LTS maintainer but considered the decision premature, and Hamano had questioned concrete versioning mechanics. The change removes potential friction points around repository tagging specifics and backport responsibilities that were previously outlined.
