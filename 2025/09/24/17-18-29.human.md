This is a follow-up to the settled discussion about date formatting in `git log`. Despite the thread reaching clear consensus that Git's current 1-based month and day numbering is correct and conventional, Velocifyer proposes adding a configuration mechanism for 0-based date display. The suggestion involves either an environment variable (`$USE-0-BASED-INDEX-FOR-DATE`) or a dotfile (`$HOME/.config/use-0-based-index-for-date`).

The email offers no new technical justification beyond personal preference, coming after multiple contributors (including Jeff King and Ben Knoble) had thoroughly explained why the current behavior matches standard locale conventions. The thread had previously concluded that users wanting alternative formats should use Git's existing `--date=format:` options rather than expect core behavior changes.

This appears to be a restatement of the original position that was already discussed and rejected, now wrapped in a configuration proposal. Given the thread's definitive resolution and the availability of existing customization options, this suggestion seems unlikely to gain traction. The email does contain a typo fix from a previous version, but doesn't otherwise advance the discussion.
