This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to centralize packfile management in `struct packfile_store`. Patch 04/15 moves the packfile path-to-object map from `struct object_database` to the new `struct packfile_store`, completing the logical grouping of packfile-related state.

The change is mechanical but important for the architectural goal - the `pack_map` hashmap that tracks loaded packfiles now lives alongside other packfile management data. The patch updates all access sites (including one in midx.c) to use the new location. As a minor cleanup, the comparison function `pack_map_entry_cmp()` is made static since it's now only used within packfile.c.

This continues the series' pattern of moving packfile-related functionality while maintaining existing semantics. The changes are straightforward and well-contained, with no apparent controversy in the thread so far. The midx.c access is noted as an expected implementation detail that will be addressed by future work.
