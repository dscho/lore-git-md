Final structural simplification in xdiff refactoring series. This patch (9/13 in v5) removes the redundant `chastore_t` arena allocator from `xdfile_t`, completing the elimination of duplicate field storage in xdiff's data structures. The change converts `xdfile_t.recs` from an array of pointers to an array of direct `xrecord_t` embeddings, requiring updates to all field access sites across xdiff codebase.

Key technical changes:
- Removes `xdfile_t.rcha` (chastore allocator) and changes `recs` to store `xrecord_t` directly
- Updates all `xdf->recs[i]->field` accesses to `xdf->recs[i].field` across xdiff/
- Includes performance measurements showing ~5% speedup from removing chastore (though removing ha fields would be ~5% slower)
- Completes the series' goal of eliminating redundant structures while preserving algorithm semantics

This is the last major structural change in the xdiff modernization effort, following earlier elimination of `diffdata_t`, `ha` array, and other redundant fields. The series is now fully complete with all technical and documentation refinements addressed through v5.
