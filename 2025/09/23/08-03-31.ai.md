This is a follow-up discussion in the thread about build system and documentation changes for deprecated commands when `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` is enabled. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion about properly excluding deprecated commands from documentation.

The key new points in this email are:
1. Patrick agrees with Junio's proposed change to add `git-whatchanged` to `EXCLUDED_PROGRAMS` when `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` is set, matching its existing conditional exclusion from `BUILT_INS`.
2. He notes this will have the side effect of stopping documentation linting for excluded commands, which may or may not be desirable.
3. Patrick raises an alternative idea: instead of completely removing man pages for deprecated commands, they could be kept with replacement notices that inform users about the removal and suggest alternatives.

The discussion continues to focus on the build system's handling of deprecated commands, with Patrick introducing a new consideration about how to best handle their documentation.
