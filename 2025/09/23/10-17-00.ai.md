This is the foundational patch in Patrick Steinhardt's merged packfile store refactoring series (v6 01/15). It introduces a new `struct packfile_store` to centralize packfile management, which was previously split between `struct packed_git` and `struct object_database`. The patch creates the basic structure and initialization functions while noting this is just the first step - future work will migrate more fields and eventually move the structure to the object database source level.

Key technical details:
- Adds `struct packfile_store` with just an ODB pointer initially
- Introduces `packfile_store_new()` and `packfile_store_free()`
- Updates `struct object_database` to contain a `packfiles` pointer
- Touches odb.c, odb.h, packfile.c, and packfile.h
- Explicitly notes this doesn't yet move the `packed_git::next` pointer

The patch establishes the architectural foundation for the rest of the series, which will migrate packfile-related fields into this new structure. The commit message clearly explains the transitional nature of this change and its role in enabling pluggable ODB implementations. This was the first of 15 patches that were ultimately merged after thorough review.
