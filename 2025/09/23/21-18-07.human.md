This is a naming discussion in the ongoing refactoring series to fix `git format-patch`'s inconsistent notes display between the main commit message and range-diff output.

Junio C Hamano weighs in on the naming debate for the new `strvec` member in the `rev_info` struct, which was called `rdiff_other_arg` in the initial patch. Kristoffer Haugsbakk had suggested simpler alternatives like `log_arg` or `log_args`. 

Junio explains his general philosophy on singular vs. plural naming for arrays/collections: he prefers singular names when individual elements are frequently accessed (like `dog[0]`), but is fine with plural when the collection is mostly handled as a unit (as with `strvec` arguments). He concludes that `log_args` is acceptable since argument vectors are typically treated as complete sets when passed around.

The discussion shows the patch series is progressing well, with the technical approach settled and now focusing on naming polish before final acceptance.
