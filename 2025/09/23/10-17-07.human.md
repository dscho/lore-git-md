This is part 8/15 of Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring packfile management into a new `struct packfile_store`. The patch focuses on the `prepare_packed_git()` family of functions, which handle loading packfiles and multi-pack indexes.

The key change here is converting these functions to operate directly on a `packfile_store` rather than taking a full `repository` parameter. This better reflects their actual scope and dependencies. The patch:
- Renames `prepare_packed_git()` to `packfile_store_prepare()`
- Splits out MRU list preparation into `packfile_store_prepare_mru()`
- Updates all callers to use the new interface

The changes are mechanical - no behavior is altered, just the function signatures and internal variable access patterns. This continues the series' goal of centralizing packfile management while maintaining existing functionality. The patch appears cleanly implemented with thorough updates to all call sites.

This is part of the larger architectural shift to prepare for pluggable object database backends, where packfile operations need to be clearly separated from repository-level concerns. The series has already been through several rounds of review and is now in its final polishing phase.
