- Phillip notes the current `fixup`/`squash` command design assumes fixing ancestors, not descendants
- Describes personal workflow where `fixup -C` is used similarly to regular `fixup` but with message rewording

This introduces a new consideration for the patch - whether the changed behavior aligns with other common workflows beyond the split-commit case Mathias described. The discussion now has two distinct use patterns to reconcile regarding author metadata handling in `fixup -C` operations.
