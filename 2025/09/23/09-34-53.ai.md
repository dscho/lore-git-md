This is a naming discussion follow-up in the completed packfile store refactoring series.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Justin Tobler's suggestion to rename two functions from `*_reprepare()` to `*_reload()`. While acknowledging the suggestion has merit in isolation, Patrick argues for keeping the current names to maintain consistency with existing terminology in the codebase ("preparing" and "repreparing" being long-established terms in this context).

The discussion concerns two functions introduced in the refactoring:
1. `odb_reprepare()` - Clears caches and reloads alternates/object sources
2. `packfile_store_reprepare()` - Clears packfile caches and detects new packfiles

This is a minor stylistic point that doesn't affect the technical implementation of the already-merged series. The exchange shows consensus on the functionality while differing on naming preferences, with Patrick opting for consistency over potentially clearer naming. No further action appears required unless maintainers choose to weigh in.
