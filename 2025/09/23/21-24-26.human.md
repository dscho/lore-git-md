Refactoring patch in the xdiff modernization series. This is part 9/13 of v5, continuing Ezekiel Newren's work to simplify xdiff internals for Rust compatibility by removing redundant chastore arena allocator from xdfile_t.

The patch eliminates the chastore_t arena allocator in favor of direct xrecord_t storage in xdfile_t, removing duplicate field storage and simplifying memory management. Performance benchmarks show mixed results - removing chastore alone gives a 5% speedup, while removing both chastore and ha fields is roughly neutral. The change makes the code more straightforward by eliminating an outdated allocation scheme that was kept around after xrecord_t storage was modernized.

This completes the removal of redundant data structures in xdiff, following previous patches that eliminated diffdata_t and ha array duplication. The series is now fully complete with all structural simplifications finished, ready for merging to 'next'.
