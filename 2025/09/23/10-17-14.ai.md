This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, completing the transition of packfile management functions to operate on the new `struct packfile_store` rather than directly on repositories. The patch renames `get_packed_git_mru()` to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` and updates it to take a packfile store parameter instead of a repository. This change aligns with the series' goal of centralizing packfile operations within the packfile store abstraction.

The implementation updates three call sites: the function declaration in packfile.h, its definition in packfile.c, and its usage in builtin/pack-objects.c. The changes are mechanical - the function now operates on the packfile store's MRU list directly rather than accessing it through the repository's object store. The new function maintains the same behavior of preparing the packfile store (via `packfile_store_prepare()`) before returning the MRU list.

This completes the architectural separation of packfile management from the object database, with all packfile-related functions now properly scoped to operate on the packfile store. The series has successfully migrated ~50 call sites while preserving behavior, with this final patch handling the MRU list access pattern used during object packing.
