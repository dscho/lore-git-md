This is a refactoring patch in the final stages of the xdiff internals series, specifically renaming the `rchg` field to `changed` in the `xdfile_t` structure. The field's purpose remains the same - it's a boolean array indicating which lines in the diff are changed. The rename doesn't affect behavior but makes the code's intent clearer.

It's a straightforward rename with no functional impact, building on previous structural simplifications that eliminated redundant field storage and allocators.
