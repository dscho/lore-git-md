This is a discussion thread about localization of technical email headers in Git's patch workflow.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk follows up on Eric Sunshine's historical context about why `Range-diff:` and `Interdiff:` headers were made translatable in 2018. While Sunshine had noted there were arguments on both sides at the time, Haugsbakk clarifies his concern isn't about machine readability but rather about users potentially unintentionally sending localized patch headers to English-language mailing lists when they might prefer English technical output regardless of their personal UI language.

Haugsbakk acknowledges he might be missing valid use cases, such as non-English patch workflows, and makes clear he's not proposing any changes given how rarely this has been questioned. The thread appears to be winding down with no immediate action planned, though Sunshine's earlier openness to reverting the behavior leaves the possibility for future discussion if others share Haugsbakk's concerns. The exchange surfaces an interesting tension between localization consistency and technical workflow needs that doesn't have an obvious right answer.
