This is a discussion about localization (l10n) of technical email headers in `git-format-patch` output. Eric Sunshine responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's question about why "Range-diff:" and "Interdiff:" headers are translated, noting this was a deliberate 2018 design choice that received no pushback at the time.

Sunshine acknowledges the current behavior may be debatable, recalling there were arguments both for and against translation that he no longer remembers. He suggests human readers (not machines) are the target audience, and mentions some projects might prefer non-English headers. However, he explicitly states he wouldn't oppose changing this behavior now.

The email provides useful historical context - this is only the second time the translation choice has been questioned, and Sunshine recalls Junio previously rejected making diffstat` output translatable. This suggests the project may have an undocumented principle about what technical outputs should remain in English. The door appears open for Kristoffer to propose a change if desired.
