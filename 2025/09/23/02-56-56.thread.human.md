Justin Tobler has responded to Junio Hamano's feedback on the `git repo stats` series, clarifying the design philosophy behind the command's metrics reporting. While Junio questioned whether historical metrics are actionable enough to include, Justin argues they provide valuable insight into repository characteristics that could inform future optimizations, even if they don't directly suggest fixes. He plans to add "level of concern" indicators in a follow-up series to bridge this gap.

The discussion reveals a productive tension between Junio's focus on immediately useful diagnostics and Justin's vision for comprehensive repository visibility. Justin agrees to some technical adjustments (switching column widths from `size_t` to `int`) while maintaining the core functionality. Both acknowledge room for evolution in output formats, with machine-readable options coming later in this series and potential HTML/CSV support in the future. The exchange helps align expectations around what this first version will deliver versus longer-term possibilities.
