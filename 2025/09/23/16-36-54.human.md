This is a follow-up in the thread about deprecation warnings in Git 2.51.0. The original reporter, S0AndS0 of Digital Mercenaries LLC, had expressed concerns about potential removal of both `git whatchanged` and date-filtered `git log` functionality.

In this email, S0AndS0 acknowledges that they were likely mistaken about `--since` being deprecated, as they can no longer reproduce the warnings and D. Ben Knoble confirmed the release notes don't mention such a deprecation. This resolves one of the two open questions from the thread - the date filtering functionality appears safe to continue using.

The thread now has clear resolution on both original concerns: `git whatchanged` has known alternatives (`git log --raw --no-merges` and related formats), and date filtering with `--since` is not deprecated. S0AndS0 expresses gratitude for the help received and for Git in general, suggesting this thread may be winding down unless new information about the source of the original date-filtering warnings emerges.
