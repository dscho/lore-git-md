This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's completed packfile store refactoring series, which centralizes packfile management in `struct packfile_store` as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The patch refactors `get_packed_git_mru()` to work directly on the packfile store rather than a repository, renaming it to `packfile_store_get_packs_mru()` for consistency with the new naming scheme.

The change is mechanical but important for completing the separation of concerns - the MRU (most-recently-used) list of packfiles is now clearly scoped to the packfile store operations. The patch updates the single caller in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to use the new interface and moves the function declaration to packfile.h with other packfile store operations.

This concludes the architectural work to separate packfile management from object database responsibilities, with all components now properly located in their logical subsystems. The series has been through multiple rounds of review and is now fully merged, representing a clean foundation for future work on the object database abstraction.
