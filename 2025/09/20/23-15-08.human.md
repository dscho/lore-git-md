This is a follow-up discussion about Git's trailer formatting behavior, spun off from the now-merged xdiff refactoring series. Jeff King responds to Junio Hamano's observation about empty trailer lines appearing in `git log` output.

The core issue is that `%(trailers)` always appends a newline, which when combined with `--format`'s own newline creates blank lines in the output. Jeff demonstrates this with a clearer example showing commit hashes and subjects alongside trailers. He explains why simply removing the newline from `%(trailers)` would break existing use cases where trailers are followed by other content (like `---` separators).

Jeff suggests a potential solution would require either a new formatting option or placeholder to control newline behavior, but notes this would need careful consideration to avoid breaking existing scripts that may rely on the current behavior. The discussion remains theoretical at this point, with no immediate changes proposed to Git's trailer handling.
