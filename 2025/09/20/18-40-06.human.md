The change uses Tcl/Tk's `after idle` mechanism to ensure proper timing for geometry restoration across different operating systems. This is the first of two patches in a series addressing window persistence issues in gitk, with this one specifically targeting the main window's position. The change is minimal (just 2 lines added) and appears well-scoped to solve the stated problem. No test changes are included, which is typical for gitk's Tcl/Tk UI code. The patch follows up on the series' goal of making gitk window geometry properly persistent across sessions.
