Johannes Sixt (Hannes) has been working on making gitk's window geometry persistent across sessions, addressing a long-standing usability issue. The current v2 patch series has two parts: one fixes the main window's position restoration using a deferred Tcl/Tk callback to handle OS-specific timing issues, while the second adds proper geometry management for the Tags/Heads windows, storing their positions in config. Historical context from Mark Levedahl confirms the original 2008 removal of this feature was due to Cygwin-specific bugs that are no longer relevant.

In the latest exchange, Mike Rappazzo responds to Johannes's technical questions about the Tags/Heads implementation. Mike reports cross-platform testing showing the deferred callback is needed on Windows and Gnome but not MacOS, though the exact mechanism remains unclear. He also acknowledges Johannes's critique of the single-function-with-actions design pattern in the helper code, promising to split it into cleaner separate functions in the next version. The discussion remains focused on implementation refinements, suggesting the core design is sound but needs minor adjustments before finalizing.
