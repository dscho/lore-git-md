This is a response in an ongoing discussion about making Rust mandatory in Git's build system, from a contributor maintaining PowerPC/Darwin ports. Sergey Fedorov shares their experience porting various languages (including Rust via mrustc) to niche platforms, noting that while they don't oppose optional Rust support, mandating it would break compatibility with systems where Rust toolchains are still problematic. They highlight active maintenance of PowerPC toolchains through GCC and express concern about open-source accessibility, suggesting the policy might be reconsidered once gccrs matures. The email provides specific references to Rust platform support issues and emphasizes the importance of bootstrapability. This comes from a technically informed but non-ideological perspective focused on practical portability constraints.
