This is the second patch in a bugfix series addressing curl type safety issues, specifically focusing on the IMAP subsystem. Junio C Hamano provides a maintainer review of Johannes Schindelin's patch that improves type safety when casting message sizes in `imap-send.c`.

The patch replaces a direct cast to `curl_off_t` with a new helper function `cast_size_t_to_curl_off_t()` that properly handles the conversion from `size_t`. Junio notes this is particularly important since `curl_off_t` must be a signed 64-bit type per libcurl's requirements. He confirms the math is safe (the subtraction won't underflow) and that the change follows the pattern established in the series.

Junio gives the patch his approval ("Looking good. Will queue.") while suggesting a minor documentation clarification about the signedness of `curl_off_t`. This matches his earlier ack of the first patch in the series, indicating the technical approach is sound. The review confirms this patch completes the type-safety improvements for the IMAP side, complementing the HTTP fixes in the other patches.
