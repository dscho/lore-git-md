This is a technical response in the ongoing discussion about Rust adoption challenges on NonStop platforms, specifically addressing cross-compiler possibilities.

Randall Becker provides concrete details about compiler availability for NonStop systems, directly responding to Ezekiel Newren's inquiry about cross-compilation options. The key points are that NonStop has C99/C11/C17 compilers, but the only cross-compiler option (a C99 compiler for Windows) requires proprietary licensing from HPE that Becker cannot provide. He also notes that standard configure processes don't work for Windows-to-NonStop compilation.
