This is a follow-up in the xdiff refactoring series, addressing remaining concerns about the NO/YES/MAYBE macros introduced in patch 10/10.

Ezekiel Newren responds to Phillip Wood's suggestion about the macro namespace issue, agreeing to move the NO/YES/MAYBE macros into xprepare.c and refactor the `char rchg` to use `bool changed` instead. This addresses the namespace pollution concern raised earlier in the thread. 

The email also clarifies a misunderstanding about stdbool.h inclusion - while Newren initially thought its movement to compat/posix.h might cause issues, Phillip points out (and Newren confirms) that git-compat-util.h still includes it indirectly through compat/posix.h, making the bool conversion safe.

This resolves one of the two remaining open questions from the merged preparatory series (patches 1-10), leaving only the performance question about patch 9/10's removal of `rchg` variable aliases still outstanding. The change maintains the series' goal of behavior-preserving refactoring while improving code quality.
