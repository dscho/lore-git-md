This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing xdiff cleanup series, specifically addressing the removal of the `diffdata_t` struct. Phillip Wood raises a post-merge question about the rationale behind some of the implementation choices in patch 5/10 of the now-merged preparatory series.

The patch removes the `diffdata_t` struct which was previously used as an alias for fields in `xdfile_t`, directly accessing the `xdfile_t` fields instead. While the commit message explains that each `diffdata_t` field had a direct `xdfile_t` equivalent, Phillip notes it doesn't explain why removing the local variables `rchg[12]` and `rindex[12]` in favor of direct `xdf[12]` dereferencing was beneficial.

This appears to be a clarification request rather than a technical issue, as the changes are already merged. The question touches on the series' broader goal of simplifying xdiff's internals for eventual Rust compatibility, but focuses specifically on the design rationale for this particular change in access patterns. The exchange represents the kind of post-merge discussion that helps document the reasoning behind such refactorings for future maintainers.
