This is a follow-up in the xdiff refactoring series addressing the remaining open question about the NO/YES/MAYBE macros from patch 10/10. Ezekiel Newren responds to Phillip Wood's suggestion about moving these macros into xprepare.c and using bool` instead of `char` for the `rchg` field.

Newren confirms he will implement Wood's suggested approach of moving the macros to xprepare.c and changing the type to `bool`. This resolves the namespace pollution concern raised earlier by Junio Hamano, as the macros will now be file-local. The email also clarifies that including `stdbool.h` is not an issue since it's transitively included via `git-compat-util.h`'s inclusion of `compat/posix.h`.

This response leaves only one remaining open question from the merged series - the performance impact of patch 9/10's removal of local `rchg` variable aliases. The change discussed here (moving macros and using bool) is a straightforward improvement that maintains the series' goals of both immediate code quality improvements and Rust compatibility preparation.
