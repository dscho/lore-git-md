This is a technical proof-of-concept patch in the ongoing color handling cleanup series, demonstrating a struct-based approach to type safety for color configuration. Authored by Jeff King (Peff) and explicitly marked "DO NOT APPLY", it serves as a discussion piece rather than a proposed change.

The patch converts the `git_colorbool` enum into a struct wrapper (`git_colorbool_f` inner enum) to enforce explicit field access and prevent implicit bool/enum conversions. It mechanically updates 31 files to use the new type, requiring all operations to explicitly reference the `.f` field. While the approach preserves behavior, it introduces syntactic overhead - struct initializers must use `{GIT_COLOR_AUTO}` syntax and comparisons require explicit field access (`foo.f == GIT_COLOR_AUTO`).

Junio Hamano acknowledges the technical demonstration with amusement, noting the surprisingly small diff size given the complexity of the change, and confirms he won't apply it as requested. The patch appears well-executed as a proof-of-concept, touching all necessary call sites consistently, though its practical adoption would require weighing the type safety benefits against the syntactic burden it imposes.
