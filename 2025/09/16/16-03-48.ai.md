The new wording replaces the previous description that only mentioned local operations (creation/update/deletion) without addressing remote-side refs.

Key technical details captured in the new documentation include the complete mirroring semantics, the scope of refs affected (all under `refs/`), and the relationship to the `remote.<remote>.mirror` configuration.
