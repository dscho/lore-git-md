Refactoring patch in a series improving Git's color handling. This is patch 5/13 in a series standardizing color variable usage across Git's codebase.

The patch fixes a subtle performance issue in the diff machinery where color-moved line detection was being performed unnecessarily when output wasn't actually being colored. The root cause was using a direct boolean check on `use_color` rather than the proper `want_color()` helper that accounts for auto-color detection timing.

While the output was technically correct (empty color strings were ultimately used), the patch shows a measurable 4% speedup in `git log --color-moved` benchmarks by avoiding the line detection work when colors won't be shown. The change is small (5 lines modified) but demonstrates the benefits of the series' broader enum-based approach to color handling.

The patch maintains careful timing around pager setup, preserving the behavior from daa0c3d971 that delays auto-color decisions. This is part of a systematic cleanup where similar fixes appear throughout the series, though this particular optimization wasn't part of the original plan - it emerged during the refactoring work.
