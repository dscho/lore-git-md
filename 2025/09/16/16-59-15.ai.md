Maintainer guidance on bundle-URI error handling philosophy. This email from Junio Hamano clarifies his position in the ongoing discussion about how git-clone should handle malformed bundle-URI responses. The thread had previously established a distinction between server transmission errors (where graceful degradation may be appropriate) and client configuration errors (where stricter validation helps users catch mistakes).

Junio responds to Toon Claes' question about implementation specifics, reinforcing that his earlier comment was about the principle of explicit error reporting rather than prescribing a particular technical solution. He emphasizes the preference for "honestly returning failure with explanation" over "pretending success without actually doing anything," but leaves the exact implementation approach open.

This maintains the thread's trajectory toward stricter validation for configuration errors while keeping the door open for different technical approaches to achieve that goal. The exchange shows the maintainer focusing on the design philosophy rather than micro-level implementation choices, suggesting the discussion may be ready to move to implementation phase with this principle as guidance.
