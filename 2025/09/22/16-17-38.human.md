Gentoo maintainer Sam James reinforces Michael Orlitzky's concerns about Rust's impact on Linux package management in this ongoing debate about making Rust mandatory in Git. The email strongly supports Orlitzky's position that Rust's dependency model forces distributions to violate their own packaging policies by bundling outdated crates, effectively breaking the package manager's ability to efficiently handle updates across hundreds of packages. 

James highlights the practical burden this creates for maintainers, noting that while Gentoo can technically support Rust (even on niche platforms like riscv/musl), the experience is painful enough that "a normal person would be completely helpless." The core objection remains Rust's ecosystem instability and its incompatibility with Linux distribution principles, with Git's importance not justifying the damage to fundamental package management workflows.

This continues the thread's expansion from technical compatibility concerns to broader systems administration impacts, with distribution maintainers now clearly aligned against mandatory Rust adoption due to its disruptive effects on their core responsibilities. The exchange underscores how the Rust debate has moved beyond language preferences to confront fundamental differences in software distribution philosophies.
