This is a follow-up in the thread about push optimization and object reuse, specifically addressing Jeff King's suggestion to use `git repack -adb` to work around the issue.

Sainan confirms that repacking with bitmaps does resolve the object reuse problem when amending commits, though notes it requires further testing. However, they identify a new wrinkle: after a `git pull`, the repository state loses its bitmap, which would reintroduce the original push inefficiency. While repacking before pushing would work as a manual workaround, Sainan questions whether this is sufficiently efficient as a general solution.

The email highlights an important practical consideration for any bitmap-based solution - the need to maintain fresh bitmaps after common operations like pulling. This suggests that while bitmaps can help, they may require additional infrastructure (like automatic repacking) to be a complete solution. The discussion continues to explore the tradeoffs between different optimization approaches, with Sainan providing real-world validation of one proposed direction while surfacing its limitations.
