Bugfix follow-up addressing an edge case in dangling symref handling.

Toon Claes reports that Jeff King's recent patch to prevent dangling symref clobbering (450fc2bace) breaks their workflow at $DAYJOB where they intentionally delete dangling symrefs by updating them to the null OID. The proposed follow-up patch modifies the check in both refs backends to allow such deletions when the new OID is also null, while maintaining the original protection against accidental clobbering.

The change is minimal - just adding a `!is_null_oid(&update->new_oid)` condition to the existing checks in files-backend.c and reftable-backend.c. A new test case demonstrates the allowed deletion scenario. This appears to be a legitimate use case that was unintentionally blocked by the original fix, though it's unclear if this was an oversight or a deliberate design choice in the initial implementation.

Key technical details:
- Maintains all existing protections against non-null updates to dangling symrefs
- Only relaxes the check when both old and new OIDs are null (explicit deletion case)
- Affects both files and reftable backends identically
- Adds test case showing successful deletion of dangling symref via null-to-null update

This appears to be a well-scoped, technically sound adjustment to the original fix, though it may warrant discussion about whether the original behavior was intentional. The patch follows the same style and approach as the series it modifies, with appropriate test coverage.
