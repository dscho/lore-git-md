Gentoo maintainer Sam James responds to brian m. carlson's Rust integration proposal, focusing on distribution packaging concerns. This exchange continues the broader discussion about making Rust mandatory in Git, with particular attention to how distributions will handle Rust's dependency management.

James expresses relief at carlson's commitment to using only stable, well-packaged crates (targeting Debian stable versions) and minimal dependency churn. This addresses some of Gentoo's concerns about ecosystem instability, though James notes Gentoo may need to adapt its crate packaging approach to match Debian's model.

The email highlights the tension between Rust's safety benefits (which carlson strongly advocates) and the practical maintenance burdens it creates for distributions. While James seems more optimistic after hearing carlson's conservative dependency policy, the underlying platform support challenges (particularly for niche architectures) remain unresolved in this exchange. The discussion remains constructive, with distribution maintainers and Git contributors working to find common ground on implementation details.
