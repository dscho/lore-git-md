This is a historical clarification in the gitk window geometry persistence thread. Mark Levedahl provides context about why position restoration was originally disabled in gitk back in 2008 - it was due to bugs in Cygwin's outdated Tcl/Tk 8.4.1 port. He notes this became irrelevant after 2011 when Cygwin switched to a modern X11-based Tcl/Tk implementation.

The email confirms that the current patch's approach of restoring window geometry (including position) using `after idle` is now reasonable, as the original Cygwin compatibility concerns no longer apply. This removes a potential historical objection to the patch series' approach.

The response helps move the thread forward by addressing Johannes Sixt's question about the original rationale for removing position restoration, suggesting the current work can proceed without being constrained by those old platform limitations.
