This thread addresses an inconsistency in how `git format-patch` displays Git notes when generating range-diffs for single-commit series. The issue was initially described as notes appearing differently between the main commit message and range-diff output, but Junio C Hamano's review revealed a more serious implication - the `--no-notes` option could be silently ignored in single-commit cases.

The two-part patch series introduces a new `rdiff_other_arg` member to the `rev_info` struct to properly propagate notes arguments to the range-diff subprocess. While the technical approach has been deemed sensible, Junio identified several areas needing improvement: the commit message should better reflect the severity of the issue, the new struct member requires proper initialization, and its name may be too generic. 

The series remains at v1 while these concerns are addressed. Junio has reviewed both patches but hasn't yet provided formal Reviewed-by tags, suggesting a v2 will be needed to incorporate the feedback. The changes are focused on this specific edge case and don't appear to conflict with other ongoing efforts in the codebase.
