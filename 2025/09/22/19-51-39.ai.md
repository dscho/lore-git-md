Refactoring patch in the xdiff internals cleanup series, specifically addressing redundant variable aliasing in xdiffi.c.

This patch (10/12 in v4) continues Ezekiel Newren's systematic simplification of xdiff's data structures by removing the redundant `rchg1` and `rchg2` local variable aliases in `xdl_build_script()`. The change directly accesses through the `xe->xdf1.rchg` and `xe->xdf2.rchg` struct members instead. This follows the pattern established in earlier patches of eliminating intermediate variables that merely shadow struct fields.

The modification is straightforward - it removes the local variable declarations and updates the three places they were used to reference the struct members directly. The behavior remains identical, as confirmed by the test suite. This change aligns with the series' broader goal of making the code more straightforward for eventual Rust integration by reducing indirection and aliasing.

This patch builds on previous structural simplifications including the removal of the `ha` array (patch 7/12) and `chastore_t` allocator (patch 9/12). It represents one of the final cleanups before the planned Rust-specific type changes. The change is uncontroversial and follows the same proven pattern as earlier refactorings in the series.
