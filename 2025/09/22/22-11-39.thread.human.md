This thread began with a user report about deprecation warnings in Git 2.51.0 for `git whatchanged` and date-filtered `git log` usage. The original reporter, S0AndS0 of Digital Mercenaries LLC, sought clarification on whether these features were being removed and what alternatives existed. 

Kristoffer Haugsbakk quickly provided suitable replacements for `git whatchanged` (`git log --raw --no-merges` and related output formats), which the reporter has accepted and plans to alias locally. D. Ben Knoble then clarified that `--since` date filtering is not actually deprecated according to the release notes, narrowing the scope of concern. 

In the latest update, S0AndS0 acknowledges this clarification and reports being unable to reproduce the initial warnings about date filtering, effectively retracting that part of the concern. While the source of those original warnings remains unexplained, the reporter appears satisfied that the date filtering functionality itself is not at risk. 

With the `whatchanged` alternatives established and the date filtering question resolved, the thread has largely reached its conclusion. The only remaining loose end is the origin of the initial date filtering warnings, but this doesn't appear to require further action from the community.
