This thread began with a report from S0AndS0 about new deprecation warnings in Git 2.51.0 for `git whatchanged` and date-filtered `git log` usage, seeking clarification on the deprecation plans and alternative commands. Kristoffer Haugsbakk has now responded with concrete alternatives for `git whatchanged`, suggesting `git log --raw --no-merges` as a direct replacement that produces similar output showing changed files with modes and hashes. He also mentions `--stat` and `--name-only` as alternative output formats with different levels of detail.

While this provides clear migration paths for users of `git whatchanged`, the response does not address the other part of the original query regarding date-based filtering warnings in `git log`. The thread remains open for maintainers or other contributors to weigh in on whether these features will actually be deprecated and to provide guidance on the date-filtering syntax. The initial deprecation warnings appear to be part of Git's ongoing CLI cleanup efforts to phase out older commands in favor of more maintainable alternatives.
