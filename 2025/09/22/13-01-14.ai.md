This is a documentation patch in the Rust infrastructure series, specifically addressing the BreakingChanges announcement about Rust becoming mandatory in Git 3.0. The email is a response to Phillip Wood's feedback on the timeline and LTS maintenance model.

- LTS maintenance handoff process to distributors
- Windows support blockers being addressed in follow-up patches
