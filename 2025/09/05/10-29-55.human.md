This is a follow-up email in the ongoing discussion about Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` command proposal. Kristoffer Haugsbakk points out that the cover letter from v3 is slightly outdated - the `reword` subcommand was actually already included starting from v2 of the patch series, contrary to what the cover letter suggests.

The email serves as a minor correction to the project's documentation, noting that the feature list in the cover letter doesn't reflect the current state of the implementation. The `git-history` proposal remains an active RFC with 31 patches in its third revision, introducing various history editing subcommands inspired by Jujutsu (jj). The series has seen significant development since its initial proposal, with `reword` being one of the more recently added subcommands that handles commit message editing while preserving other commit metadata.

No new technical content or changes are proposed in this email - it's purely a documentation clarification about what's already implemented in the series. The thread continues to be one of the more substantial and active discussions on the mailing list regarding new Git functionality.
