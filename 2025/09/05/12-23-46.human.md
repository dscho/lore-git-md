Patrick Steinhardt responds to Derrick Stolee's explanation about `git backfill` not being optimized for treeless clones. Steinhardt ultimately recommends against using treeless clones entirely, reinforcing Stolee's position that they're not suitable for workflows requiring later refilling of missing objects.
