**Technical follow-up on Rust varint implementation**

This is a brief technical clarification from Junio Hamano regarding the performance characteristics of the Rust varint implementation in Patrick Steinhardt's RFC series. Junio corrects his initial observation that the Rust `decode_varint()` showed more optimized disassembly than the C equivalent when compiled with clang-19 -O2. After further investigation with the correct compilation options, he confirms both implementations produce identical assembly output.

This email serves as a minor but important correction in the ongoing technical evaluation of the Rust infrastructure proposal. While it doesn't change the overall positive assessment of the Rust implementation's quality (which Junio had previously validated), it removes what could have been a misleading performance advantage claim. The thread's core discussion about transition timelines and platform support continues unaffected by this clarification.

The email demonstrates the careful technical scrutiny the Rust proposal is receiving, particularly around performance-sensitive components like the varint subsystem. Junio's attention to such details reinforces the maintainers' thorough review process for this major architectural change.
