Build system patch modifying version string formatting for snapshot builds.

This changes `GIT-VERSION-GEN` to stop converting hyphens to dots in snapshot version strings (like `2.51.0.178.g2462961280`), instead keeping the standard `git describe` format (`2.51.0-178-g2462961280`). The motivation is SemVer compliance and better tooling compatibility, specifically mentioning b4's current failure with the dotted format. The patch is minimal - just removing a sed substitution that was added in 2007's 5c7d3c9507f7 ("Allow building of RPM from interim snapshot."). The change appears straightforward and well-motivated, though it might affect any tools that have come to depend on the dotted format over the past 17 years. The commit message clearly explains both the problem and solution, though it doesn't discuss potential backwards compatibility concerns.
