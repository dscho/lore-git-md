This is a response in the ongoing discussion about making blobless clones the default. Konstantin Ryabitsev pushes back on the proposal, arguing that shallow clones (a related optimization) create significant server load due to the lack of precomputed packs. He suggests that making this behavior default could backfire by slowing down clones overall and potentially overwhelming servers.

The email is brief but raises an important operational concern about the scalability impact of changing clone defaults. While the thread had previously focused on client-side benefits and implementation details, Ryabitsev shifts attention to server-side consequences that would need to be addressed before any default changes. This introduces a new dimension to the discussion that wasn't previously highlighted in the infrastructure impact of such optimizations.
