Build system discussion about version string formatting, continuing the debate over whether to maintain Git's historical dotted format or switch to standard hyphenated `git describe` output.

Jonas Rebmann responds to Brian M. Carlson's objections about SemVer compatibility, clarifying they're only advocating for the lexical format (hyphen-separated components) rather than semantic versioning rules. They note the current dotted format doesn't match any known versioning scheme, including modern RPM guidelines, and question whether this is truly Git's canonical version format or just an incidental output format.

The email provides two key technical references: (1) current Fedora packaging guidelines showing the dotted format doesn't match RPM standards, and (2) a link to their submitted b4 patch that would handle Git's current version format. This suggests the immediate b4 compatibility issue could be solved either way, shifting focus to the broader question of what format makes most sense for Git's build system to produce.

The discussion remains unresolved between two perspectives: maintaining 17 years of historical behavior versus adopting a more widely recognized format that matches `git describe` and common version parsing expectations. No new technical implementation concerns are raised in this email - it primarily serves to refine the debate's framing and provide supporting evidence about current packaging standards.
