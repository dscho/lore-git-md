Follow-up discussion about default branch name advice messages in the now-merged "master" to "main" transition series. Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's concerns about the current advice being annoying, arguing that post-3.0 we'll need different advice focused on usability rather than preference.

Junio distinguishes two scenarios: the current advice helps users who dislike "master" name (a preference issue), while his proposed new advice would help users following old tutorials that assume "master" (a usability issue). He emphasizes that even users who prefer "main" may need temporary "master" branches when following older materials, suggesting a one-time rename hint rather than configuration changes.

Phillip had questioned whether the advice is still needed post-transition, noting it serves to warn about the change but may become annoying. Junio counters that new users arriving after 3.0 will still encounter old tutorials and need guidance, making the usability-focused advice at least as important as the current preference-oriented messages.

This continues the thread's theme of refining the user experience around the technical change, now focusing on documentation and messaging rather than implementation. The exchange shows maintainers considering both veteran users adapting to change and newcomers encountering version mismatches.
