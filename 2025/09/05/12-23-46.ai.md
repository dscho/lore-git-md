This is a technical discussion about the challenges of backfilling trees in treeless clones, building on the ongoing thread about partial clone optimizations.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Derrick Stolee's point about `git backfill` not being optimized for treeless clones by proposing a depth-based batching approach for tree backfilling. The suggested algorithm would fetch trees level by level (root trees first, then progressively deeper levels), though Patrick acknowledges this would still perform poorly compared to blob backfilling. He explores the idea of server-side assistance for tree fetching but concludes the required information would be equivalent to having the trees themselves.

The email reinforces Stolee's position that treeless clones are problematic for backfilling workflows, with Patrick explicitly recommending against using treeless clones altogether. This contributes to the thread's exploration of partial clone tradeoffs by highlighting a key technical limitation in one of the proposed approaches (treeless clones) while the community considers broader changes to clone defaults and workflows.
