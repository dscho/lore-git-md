This is a response in the ongoing Rust infrastructure discussion, specifically addressing build system compatibility concerns raised by Junio Hamano and brian m. carlson regarding the initial meson-only implementation.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the current RFC status of his meson-based Rust support patches and confirms his intention to add Makefile support in future versions if the general approach is approved. He responds to two key technical concerns:

1. The meson version requirement (>=1.9.0) that conflicts with current stable distributions (Debian 13 at 1.7.0, Ubuntu lacking 1.9.0 entirely) - Patrick notes Eli Schwartz's earlier comment that this version check may be unnecessary and indicates he'll investigate removing it.

2. The lack of Makefile support in the initial RFC - Patrick clarifies this was intentional for the RFC phase to focus discussion on the overall direction, with Makefile integration planned for subsequent versions.

The response maintains the thread's focus on build system compatibility while suggesting potential resolution paths for the meson version requirement. It doesn't introduce new technical details but reinforces the experimental/RFC nature of the current implementation. The exchange highlights the ongoing tension between introducing modern build requirements and maintaining broad platform support, with Patrick positioning his approach as flexible to feedback during the RFC phase.
