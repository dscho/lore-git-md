This is a policy discussion in the ongoing Rust infrastructure series, where Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's proposed transition timeline for making Rust mandatory in Git builds.

Junio generally agrees with Wood's three-phase transition plan (optional Rust -> mandatory Rust -> security updates for last Rust-optional version) but raises concerns about the specific timeframes. While he supports having a clear deadline, he questions whether 6 months is sufficient for the optional period and 3 years for security updates is too long. He suggests alternative durations that would give platform maintainers more time to adapt while still maintaining a reasonable burden on the project.

The email highlights a key remaining point of negotiation in the otherwise solidifying consensus around the Rust transition. Junio's perspective as Git's maintainer carries significant weight, suggesting this timeline discussion may require further refinement before finalizing the policy. The technical implementation of the Rust xdiff work continues unaffected by this policy discussion.
