This is a refactoring patch in the midx-write bugfix series, converting the `preferred_pack_idx` field from `int` to `uint32_t` with a proper sentinel value. The change eliminates one of the reasons for signed/unsigned comparison warnings in the midx-write code.

Derrick Stolee responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review feedback, agreeing to improve the commit message wording regarding the pack count range improvement (from 2^31 to 2^32-1). The more substantive discussion centers on whether the struct field initialization is necessary - Stolee explains that while the previous implicit initialization to -1 worked, explicitly setting the NO_PREFERRED_PACK sentinel makes the code's intent clearer and prevents potential confusion with the 0th packfile.

This patch is part of a larger series that has already received positive reviews from multiple maintainers, with only minor documentation and style points being discussed. The technical changes here are straightforward and uncontroversial, representing incremental improvements to type safety in the midx-write subsystem.
