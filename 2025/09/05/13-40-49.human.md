This is a clarification about treeless clone use cases in the ongoing discussion about partial clone defaults.

Derrick Stolee responds to Patrick Steinhardt's recommendation against treeless clones by acknowledging their limited utility while carving out one specific valid use case. He suggests treeless clones may be appropriate for ephemeral CI builds where speed is critical and only commit history (not file history) needs analysis. This builds on the thread's earlier technical analysis of treeless clone limitations while providing a concrete scenario where they might still be useful.

The email reinforces the emerging consensus that treeless clones are unsuitable for general development workflows, while offering a narrow exception that maintains technical accuracy. This helps focus the broader discussion on blobless clones as the more practical partial clone strategy for most users. The exchange continues to shape the project's understanding of when (if ever) to recommend treeless clones as part of Git's partial clone feature set.
