Ezekiel Newren's xdiff internals refactoring series has reached its final form with v6, completing a comprehensive modernization of Git's diff algorithm implementation. The work has progressed through three phases: preparatory cleanups (already merged), structural simplifications, and final naming/documentation refinements. 

The latest patch (v6 09/12) removes the chastore_t arena allocator, converting xdiff to store records in a contiguous array instead. This change maintains identical diff behavior while simplifying memory management, completing the elimination of redundant data structures that included earlier removals of diffdata_t, the ha array, and xdlclass_t field duplication. Performance impact is minimal (1% slower when combined with previous ha array removal).

Throughout the series, Newren has worked closely with reviewers Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano. Wood approved the final bool conversion of the xdfile_t.changed field and suggested documentation improvements, while Hamano provided guidance on balancing tooling benefits with readability in the justification for removing variable aliases. A philosophical discussion emerged about whether Rust porting considerations alone justify changes to working C code, with Newren maintaining the changes serve broader purposes while accepting Hamano's request to focus justification on readability.

The series now stands complete with all technical and documentation questions resolved, including final refinements to similarity matching macro names (now DISCARD/KEEP/INVESTIGATE) and identification of one deferred code organization issue. With all structural simplifications finished and performance impacts measured, the work is ready for merging to 'next'.
