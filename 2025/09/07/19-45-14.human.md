This ensures the Rust-style type definitions (u8, i64, etc.) are available throughout the xdiff codebase. The patch maintains the series' pattern of mechanical, non-controversial changes that lay groundwork for future Rust interop while keeping current functionality intact.

As with previous patches in the series, this appears to be a well-contained change with no test modifications needed. The author continues the incremental approach of introducing Rust-compatible infrastructure without yet adding any actual Rust code.
