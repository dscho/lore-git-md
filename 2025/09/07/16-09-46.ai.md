1. Quantifying organizational impact (hundreds of thousands of users affected by CVE policies)
2. Revealing active enterprise discussions about Git alternatives due to Rust uncertainty
3. Providing historical evidence that codebase divergence previously hindered platform adoption
4. Emphasizing the auditing/certification requirements that make third-party patches problematic

This shifts the policy discussion from theoretical transition timelines to concrete enterprise risk assessment, with Becker serving as a voice for downstream maintainers who need predictable security support regardless of upstream implementation changes.
