As with previous patches in the series, this is a preparatory change that maintains backward compatibility while establishing infrastructure for future Rust interop work. The patch doesn't modify any functionality or introduce new types - it just makes the Rust-compatible types from patch 10 available where they'll be needed.

- Impact: Makes Rust-style types available to xdiff internals
