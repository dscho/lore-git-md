This thread discusses a refactoring of the string-list API's index return convention, specifically changing `string_list_find_insert_index()` to use a boolean `exact_match` parameter instead of negative-index encoding for exact matches. The change is technically straightforward and updates callers in `add-interactive.c`, `mailmap.c`, and `refs.c` accordingly.

Junio C Hamano initially raised concerns about the justification for breaking the established negative-index convention, arguing the patch needed to first demonstrate why the full `size_t` range is needed before proposing changes. The author (shejialuo) has now acknowledged this feedback, agreeing that calling the current convention "bad" was too strong and accepting Junio's suggested structure for presenting the argument. They plan to restructure the justification in the next version while keeping the technical approach unchanged.

The discussion remains focused on design rationale rather than implementation, with the author working to better motivate why the change is necessary despite the established convention. Junio's concerns about proper justification appear to be guiding the next iteration of the patch series.
