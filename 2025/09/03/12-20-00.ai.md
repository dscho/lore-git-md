This is a follow-up discussion in the `git-history` RFC series, specifically about the "split" subcommand's interactive-only design. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's feedback about potentially making the split operation stateful for external tool integration.

The exchange clarifies that the current implementation intentionally keeps `git-history` as a porcelain command focused on user-friendly interactive workflows rather than exposing plumbing for external tools. Patrick acknowledges the idea of stateful split sessions could be considered in the future but emphasizes the current priority is establishing a solid baseline for common history editing tasks.

Key technical details:
- Confirms `split` remains interactive-only in current design
- Positions `git-history` as porcelain rather than plumbing
- Leaves door open for future stateful session support if use cases emerge
- Maintains focus on improving common workflows over advanced integration

The discussion shows the series maintainer balancing immediate usability goals with potential future extensibility while setting clear boundaries around the current scope. This helps frame expectations for reviewers considering the command's design philosophy.
