This is a discussion about header file placement in the ongoing `git refs optimize` subcommand series, which aims to consolidate ref-related commands under a new namespace.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Meet Soni's justification for placing the new `pack-refs.h` header in the root directory (following the precedent of `for-each-ref.h`). Patrick disagrees with this placement, arguing that since the logic is specific to builtins and not part of libgit.a, it belongs in the `builtin/` directory. He goes further to suggest that `for-each-ref.h` should also be moved to `builtin/` for consistency.

This exchange represents an ongoing architectural discussion in the series, where the team is working through the proper organization of shared functionality between the existing `git pack-refs` and new `git refs optimize` commands. The technical implementation itself (the factoring out of core logic) appears uncontroversial, but the header placement remains an open question that may need resolution before the series can proceed.
