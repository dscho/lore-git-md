This is a cache-tree infrastructure patch in the `git-history` RFC series, addressing feedback from Junio Hamano about potential applications beyond the immediate needs of the history editing commands.

The patch introduces a `struct index_state` parameter to `write_in_core_index_as_tree()`, allowing callers to write arbitrary in-memory indexes as trees rather than being limited to the repository's main index. This change was originally motivated by the needs of the history editing commands, but Junio notes it could potentially simplify the code paths for `git commit -o <pathspec>`, which currently uses a temporary index.

Patrick responds that while the sequencer also writes temporary indexes to disk (a behavior he had to debug), he's unsure whether this infrastructure would actually improve the `commit -o` case. The discussion remains open-ended, with Patrick acknowledging the possibility but not committing to explore it further in this series. The exchange shows the patch serving its immediate purpose while leaving room for future generalization, with no immediate action required on the `commit -o` angle. The technical details about index handling and disk writing constraints are noted for future reference in the thread.
