Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for gradual Rust adoption in Git. This is part of the ongoing discussion about introducing Rust infrastructure for xdiff, where technical implementation is settled but policy questions around platform support and transition timing remain contentious.

Hamano acknowledges Steinhardt's suggestion to make Rust optional until Git 3.0, allowing time for build system iteration and platform preparation. He agrees this approach would be ideal if applied to truly modular new components like alternative merge/rebasing backends or transport helpers, but questions how many such cleanly separable areas exist in practice. The response highlights concerns about maintaining both C and Rust implementations long-term while recognizing the risk of forcing Rust adoption too abruptly.

The email also raises a practical question about the status of two existing Rust-related directories in `contrib/` that weren't addressed in the current xdiff series. This suggests some housekeeping may be needed to consolidate Rust-related code paths if the project moves forward with adoption.

Hamano's tone remains cautious, reflecting his previously stated concerns about maintenance burden and platform support, while still engaging constructively with Steinhardt's compromise proposal. The exchange continues to explore middle-ground solutions between immediate adoption and indefinite postponement of Rust integration.
