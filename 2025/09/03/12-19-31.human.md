Refactoring patch in the `git-history` RFC series. This is part of the ongoing effort to support new history editing operations in Git, building on the sequencer infrastructure.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to D. Ben Knoble's observation about repeated switch statements in the sequencer code by proposing a cleaner refactor. The patch extracts the common logic that maps replay actions (`REPLAY_PICK`, `REPLAY_HISTORY_EDIT`, `REPLAY_REVERT`) to their corresponding todo commands (`TODO_PICK`, `TODO_REVERT`) into a new helper function `action_to_command()`. This eliminates duplicate switch statements from three locations in `sequencer.c`.

The change is straightforward and improves code maintainability without altering behavior. It's a small but meaningful cleanup that fits naturally into the larger `git-history` effort, where the sequencer's internals are being adapted to support new history editing operations. The patch demonstrates good attention to code quality during the RFC process.
