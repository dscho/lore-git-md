This is a cover letter for a 10-patch series continuing the effort to convert Git's remaining hooks to use the new `hook.h` interface. The work builds on earlier efforts by Emily Shaffer and Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason to modernize hook handling.

The series introduces two key capabilities needed for full hook conversion: feeding stdin to hooks via a callback (enabling parallelization) and capturing collated output from server-side hooks. The implementation aims for performance parity with existing code while removing string_list assumptions and extra copies. The changes touch several hook types including post-rewrite, pre-push, update, and receive hooks.

Adrian Ratiu notes they intentionally avoided more aggressive optimizations like mmap to maintain backwards compatibility, though these could be explored later. The work also unblocks future config-based hooks and parallelization efforts. The series appears well-grounded in prior discussion (referencing specific feedback from Ævar) and has passed CI testing.

The changes are substantial, touching core hook execution paths in run-command.c and multiple hook implementations, but the careful approach to maintaining existing behavior while enabling future improvements suggests this is a thoughtful evolution of the hook system. The series seems ready for review from those working on hook infrastructure.
