- Current documentation frames this as a "safety measure" which Junio finds conceptually misplaced
- Julia seeks to understand whether the safety-check interpretation is correct before adjusting the text

The exchange reveals deeper pedagogical questions about how to best teach push behavior - whether to emphasize Git's need for explicit targets (Junio's approach) or the safety mechanisms built into default behaviors (Julia's interpretation). This touches on the recently merged documentation's three-level precedence structure (command-line args, remote.*.push config, push.default).
