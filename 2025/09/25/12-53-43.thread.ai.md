Thread summary: Hook subsystem refactoring series (v6, 13 parts) by Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer standardizes all major Git hooks on the new `hook.h` API, now complete with the receive-pack receive hook conversion in the final patch. The series provides structured stdin handling, output capture, and parallel execution capabilities while maintaining backward compatibility.

Key technical changes:
- New `feed_pipe_fn` and `consume_output_fn` callbacks in `struct run_hooks_opt` for streaming stdin/capturing output, with strict BUG() assertions for mode combinations
- Part 1 introduces `struct parallel_child` and `child_is_working()` helper in `run-command.c` for parallel execution state management
- Parts 2-3 add stdin handling to `run_processes_parallel()` with `feed_pipe_fn` callback and pipe management infrastructure
- Parts 4-6 convert post-rewrite, pre-push, and reference-transaction hooks with specialized callbacks (`pipe_from_strbuf`, `feed_pre_push_hook_data`, `transaction_hook_feed_stdin`)
- Parts 7-9 implement output control with `ungroup` field and `consume_output_fn` callback, enabling receive-pack sideband support
- Parts 10-11 convert receive-pack's update and post-update hooks with `hook_output_to_sideband()` helper
- Final patch (13) completes receive-pack receive hook conversion with:
  - Batched stdin processing (500 lines per `feed_receive_hook_cb()` callback)
  - Preserved sideband output via `consume_output` callback
  - Push certificate/option environment handling moved to `run_hooks_opt`
  - Removal of direct `find_hook()` calls and manual SIGPIPE management
