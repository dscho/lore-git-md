Thread summary: Hook subsystem refactoring series (v6, 12 parts) by Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer standardizes Git hooks on the new `hook.h` API, now including all major hook types (receive-pack's update, post-update, and receive hooks) in parts 10-11. The series provides structured stdin handling, output capture, and parallel execution capabilities while maintaining backward compatibility.

Key technical changes:
- New `feed_pipe_fn` and `consume_output_fn` callbacks in `struct run_hooks_opt` for streaming stdin/capturing output, with strict BUG() assertions for mode combinations
- Part 2 adds stdin handling to `run_processes_parallel()` with `feed_pipe_fn` callback and pipe management
- Part 3 introduces `feed_pipe` callback mechanism for incremental stdin feeding
- Part 4 converts post-rewrite hook in sequencer.c with `pipe_from_strbuf` callback
- Part 5 converts pre-push hook in transport.c with `pre_push_hook_feed_stdin` callback - now updated to clarify status filtering behavior (switch statement intentionally covers all rejected ref cases as optimization)
- Part 6 converts reference-transaction hook with streaming callback per ref update
- Part 7 adds `ungroup` field for parallel process output control
- Part 8 introduces `consume_output_fn` callback for capturing parallel process output
- Part 9 exposes output capture functionality for server-side hooks
- Parts 10-11 convert receive-pack's hooks with batched stdin processing and sideband output preservation, including `hook_output_to_sideband()` callback that handles sideband protocol and keepalive packets
- State management helpers: `child_is_working()`, `child_is_ready_for_cleanup()`, `child_is_receiving_input()`
- Complete conversion of all major hook types with backward compatibility
