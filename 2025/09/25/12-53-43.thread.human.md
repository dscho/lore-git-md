Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's 10-part series refactoring Git's hook subsystem has reached completion with the conversion of receive-pack's update and post-update hooks to the new structured API. The series establishes a callback-based system in `run-command.c` and `hook.c` that enables safe parallel execution of hooks with proper stdin handling and output capture capabilities.

The final patch focuses on receive-pack, the last remaining component using direct hook execution. It introduces batched stdin feeding (processing 500 lines per callback) to maintain performance with large inputs while preserving sideband output handling through a new `hook_output_to_sideband` callback. The implementation carefully maintains push certificate handling and environment variable management while replacing manual process management with the standardized `run_hooks_opt()` approach.

This work represents the culmination of a multi-version effort to modernize Git's hook infrastructure. The series has addressed all major technical challenges - from memory safety and signal handling to performance optimization and backward compatibility. Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have approved the design, with Kristoffer Haugsbakk contributing documentation reviews. The v3 iteration successfully converts all remaining hooks (receive-pack, pre-push, post-rewrite, and reference-transaction) while laying groundwork for future parallel execution capabilities.
