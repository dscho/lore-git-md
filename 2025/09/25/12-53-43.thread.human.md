Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's hook subsystem refactoring series continues to progress, now in its second and third iterations (v2/v3 mixed, 10 parts). The work systematically converts Git's hooks to a callback-based API, with the latest focus on receive-pack hook conversion and performance optimizations. The series has established core infrastructure in `run-command.c` and `hook.c`, including the `struct run_hooks_opt` with its `ungroup` bitfield and mechanisms for parallel process output capture.

Patrick Steinhardt recently provided technical feedback on patch 03/10, suggesting a refinement to how callback data is accessed in the post-rewrite hook conversion. While not raising functional concerns, he proposed using `opt.feed_pipe_cb_data` rather than directly accessing `hook_cb->options->feed_pipe_ctx` for better consistency with the series' emerging patterns. This follows earlier reviews where Patrick and Junio Hamano approved the design direction while tracking several open items, including test failures in patch 6/10, performance validation needs, and edge case testing.

The series has made significant progress in converting all hooks to the new API, with the final patch (v2 10/10) implementing batched input processing for receive-pack hooks via `feed_receive_hook_cb` to optimize performance. While test coverage in t0061 has expanded, receive-pack-specific validation remains outstanding. The work continues to evolve with these incremental refinements as it moves toward completion.
