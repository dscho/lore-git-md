Adrian Ratiu continues his methodical refactoring of Git's hook subsystem, now converting the post-update hook to use the modern `hook.h` interface in patch 9/10 of his series. This follows the successful conversion of the receive-pack 'update' hook in patch 8, demonstrating the flexibility of the new interface across different hook types.

The post-update hook conversion follows the established pattern from previous patches, replacing manual process management with the cleaner `run_hooks_opt` infrastructure. The changes simplify the code significantly (removing 19 lines while adding just 6) while maintaining all existing functionality, including sideband protocol support through the `hook_output_to_sideband` callback. The patch preserves the ref filtering logic that skips failed or nonexistent refs.

This series has now modernized several critical hooks including post-rewrite, pre-push, reference-transaction, and both receive-pack hooks. Each conversion builds on tested foundations while expanding the interface's capabilities. The post-update hook work shows the system's ability to handle another hook type with minimal changes, further validating the design. With only one patch remaining in the series, the focus will likely shift to performance validation and edge case testing before potential integration.
