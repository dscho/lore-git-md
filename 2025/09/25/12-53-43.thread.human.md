This thread documents the final stages of Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's major refactoring of Git's hook subsystem, now in its sixth iteration. The 12-part series standardizes all major hook types (including receive-pack's update, post-update, and receive hooks) onto a new `hook.h` API that provides structured stdin handling, output capture, and parallel execution while maintaining backward compatibility.

The most recent discussion focused on part 5's conversion of the pre-push hook, where Junio Hamano weighed in to support Patrick Steinhardt's earlier position regarding status filtering behavior. Both maintainers agreed that Adrian's optimization to skip stdin feeding for certain rejected ref cases could unintentionally break hooks that expect to see attempted-but-rejected updates. Junio provided a concrete example where this distinction matters, demonstrating the project's careful attention to backward compatibility. The consensus is to either drop these cases unless they fix a specific documented bug, or to properly explain the bugfix in a separate commit.

With this resolution, the series has addressed all substantive feedback from key reviewers including Junio, Patrick Steinhardt, and Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason (who signed off on the pre-push conversion). The implementation now handles all major hook types while preserving critical behaviors like receive-pack's sideband support. Only minor documentation updates remain in part 12 before the series can be considered complete and ready for integration.
