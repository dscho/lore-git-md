Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's comprehensive hook subsystem refactoring series has now concluded with version 9, having successfully modernized Git's hook infrastructure while maintaining backward compatibility. The 12-part series converted all major hooks - including receive-pack's update and post-update hooks in the final patches - to use the new API, with performance optimizations for parallel execution and improved I/O handling. 

The work received thorough review from key contributors including Junio Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and Jeff King, with multiple sign-offs indicating strong consensus. Chris Darroch, who originally identified the pre-push hook behavior that required special stream separation handling, has now confirmed the series passes the Git LFS test suite - an important real-world validation from a major hook consumer. This successful conclusion marks a significant modernization of Git's hook subsystem, with all technical goals achieved and production readiness confirmed through both test suite validation and maintainer approval.
