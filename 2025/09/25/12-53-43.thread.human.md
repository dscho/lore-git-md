This thread documents the final stages of Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's multi-year effort to modernize Git's hook subsystem, now in its seventh iteration. The series standardizes hook execution on a new API with parallel execution support while maintaining backward compatibility. The most recent patches resolved critical performance issues in parallel execution, particularly addressing Kristoffer Haugsbakk's latency reports and Jeff King's deadlock concerns through optimized bidirectional I/O handling.

In this latest exchange, Junio Hamano and Adrian Ratiu discuss a lingering quirk in `run-command.c` where file descriptors are initialized to 0 - a known limitation that would require substantial refactoring beyond the current scope. While the core technical work is complete and has maintainer approval from both Junio and Patrick Steinhardt, this final discussion highlights the attention to detail characteristic of Git's review process, with Junio suggesting at least documenting the quirk to prevent future confusion. The series now awaits only minor documentation polish before completion, marking the culmination of a significant infrastructure improvement that paves the way for config-based hooks and parallel execution capabilities.
