Adrian Ratiu's hook subsystem refactoring series continues to progress with productive collaboration between Adrian and Phillip Wood. The series modernizes Git's hook infrastructure with significant code reduction while maintaining compatibility.

This follows their earlier resolution of technical concerns around parallel execution safety and stdin feeding patterns in patches 3-4.

The series has seen steady refinement through review feedback cycles, with key technical elements now including `feed_pipe_fn` for stdin streaming, `consume_sideband` output capture, and batched line feeding in receive-pack. Several open items remain for v2, including documentation improvements, test failure resolution, and performance validation, but the core technical approach appears well-vetted through Adrian's responsive engagement with reviewers.
