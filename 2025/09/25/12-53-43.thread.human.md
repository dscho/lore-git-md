This thread documents the completion of Adrian Ratiu and Emily Shaffer's multi-version effort to modernize Git's hook subsystem through a 12-part patch series, now at version 9. The work converts Git's hook handling to a new `run_hooks_opt` API that eliminates manual process management while maintaining backward compatibility. The final patch focuses on receive-pack's update and post-update hooks, completing the series' real-world demonstrations of the API's flexibility.

The conversion brings several technical improvements: automatic signal handling, removal of custom `child_process` management, and preparation for future config-based hook specification. The implementation handles sideband output and preserves push certificate environment variables while maintaining the existing skip-broken-refs behavior. Earlier patches in the series established parallel execution capabilities, stdin streaming, and converted other core hooks (post-rewrite, pre-push, and reference-transaction).

The series has achieved maintainer consensus with sign-offs from Junio Hamano and thorough reviews from key contributors including Jeff King (who suggested the parallel approach), Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason (API review), and Patrick Steinhardt (documentation). With all technical hurdles addressed and CI validation passed, this final patch completes a significant modernization of Git's hook infrastructure that has been in development across multiple iterations.
