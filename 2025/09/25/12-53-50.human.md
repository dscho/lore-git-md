This is part 7 of a 10-patch series modernizing Git's hook subsystem, focusing on output capture capabilities. The patch adds a `consume_sideband` callback to the hook interface, allowing server-side hooks to relay messages to clients rather than printing directly to stderr. The change is minimal (just 9 lines) but enables important functionality for `receive-pack` and future config-based workflows.

The patch builds on earlier work in the series that introduced stdin streaming, now completing the bidirectional communication capabilities. The implementation maintains backward compatibility by falling back to stderr when no callback is provided. Test coverage was added in patch 6 of the series.

This appears to be a well-considered addition to the hook modernization effort, with clear commit messages and proper attribution to previous contributors. The change is focused and follows the series' pattern of incremental improvements while preserving existing behavior. Server-side hook authors will particularly want to review this capability for potential use in their workflows.
