This is a follow-up in the xdiff internals refactoring series, specifically addressing the removal of variable aliases in the diff algorithm implementation. Ezekiel Newren is responding to Junio C Hamano's feedback about justifying such changes primarily for readability rather than Rust porting benefits.

Newren has reconsidered his position and now plans to drop the patch that removed the `rchg` aliases, as he finds himself using similar patterns elsewhere. This represents a reversal from his earlier stance where he advocated for removing aliases both for Rust compatibility and tooling support. The change comes after Junio suggested focusing the justification on readability improvements rather than technical motivations.

The discussion highlights the subjective nature of code style decisions, even in a technically sound refactoring series that has otherwise modernized xdiff's internals. This patch was part of the final cleanup phase (v5) of a larger effort to prepare xdiff for potential Rust integration, but the series remains complete without it. The core modernization work eliminating redundant structures and improving type safety is unaffected.
