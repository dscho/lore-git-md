This is a style configuration patch for clang-format, part of the `git repo stats` series (3/7). It adjusts the formatter's behavior around control macros like `if` and `while` to match Git's coding conventions.

The change modifies `.clang-format` to prevent clang-format from adding spaces between control macros and their parentheses, which wasn't the project's preferred style. The patch switches from `ControlStatements` to `ControlStatementsExceptControlMacros` to maintain consistency with Git's existing codebase.
