Bugfix for `git add -p` hunk splitting behavior. This is the final version of Phillip Wood's patch series addressing two issues in interactive patch selection, now simplified to make the behavior changes unconditional per Junio Hamano's guidance.

The changes improve the workflow when splitting already-selected hunks in `git add -p`:
1. Split hunks are now always marked as "undecided" rather than inheriting their parent's selection state, making it easier to modify individual split hunks without navigating back
2. The code now properly updates hunk->splittable_into counts after edits to maintain accurate splitting capability

The series drops the previously proposed `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` conditional approach after Junio rejected version-gating for this change. Phillip has implemented Junio's preferred solution of making the behavior change unconditionally, with the option to revert if users complain. The implementation appears clean with thorough test coverage in t/t3701-add-interactive.sh.

This represents the resolution of a thread that saw significant discussion about the right approach, with the maintainer ultimately favoring simplicity over configurability for this edge case. The patches are now in their final form and appear ready for merging.
