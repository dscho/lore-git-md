This is a review response in the ongoing Rust infrastructure series, now at v4. Brian m. carlson provides feedback on Patrick Steinhardt's RFC introducing Rust as a future mandatory component of Git.

The review is generally positive, with carlson noting the series looks reasonable and confirming compatibility with their own SHA-256 interoperability work. They express willingness to contribute additional patches for missing tooling like CI jobs and formatting checks if time permits. The main outstanding concern raised is about the LTS maintenance process, where carlson wants to ensure the security team is on board with the proposed model, though they don't take a strong position on the specifics.

This represents significant progress for the Rust initiative, as carlson's technical review of the build system approach in earlier patches was thorough and their approval carries weight. The remaining discussion points appear focused on process rather than technical implementation, suggesting the core infrastructure work is nearing readiness for merging once the LTS questions are resolved.
