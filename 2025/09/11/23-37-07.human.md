This is a follow-up discussion about a patch in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile store refactoring series, specifically regarding the removal of `get_packed_git()`. Taylor Blau points out that contrary to the initial claim in some cases `get_packed_git()` and `get_all_packs()` do not return identical packfile lists due to MIDX (multi-pack index) handling differences.

Taylor suggests either properly preparing all callers of `get_packed_git()` to handle MIDX packs (which would be a significant undertaking) or deferring this patch to a future series. He leans toward the latter approach, noting the current series already contains substantial changes and this particular issue deserves focused discussion.

The email represents a final technical clarification in what has otherwise been a resolved thread, with Junio Hamano having already approved the series' merge. Taylor's input here doesn't block progress but documents an area for future work, maintaining the consensus for gradual migration of callers rather than comprehensive upfront changes.
