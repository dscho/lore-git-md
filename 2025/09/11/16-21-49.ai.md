This is a follow-up in the `sparse-checkout clean` and `the_repository` refactoring thread, where Derrick Stolee is reconsidering one of the patches in the series due to edge case behavior with SKIP_WORKTREE bits.

Stolee reports discovering unexpected behavior where files cleaned by `sparse-checkout clean` show up as locally deleted in `git status`, even when their parent directories are collapsed in the sparse index. The issue appears related to differences between in-memory and on-disk sparse index representations, with unpack_trees_options potentially playing a role.

After investigation, Stolee concludes the current approach may not be cleanly fixable and proposes dropping this particular patch (v2 8/8) from the series, deferring the more aggressive cleaning behavior to a future update. He notes having a v3 revision nearly ready but will wait briefly for any last feedback before proceeding.

Key technical details:
- The problematic behavior involves SKIP_WORKTREE bit management after directory removal
- Discrepancy between in-memory vs on-disk sparse index representations
- Potential involvement of unpack_trees_options configuration
- Current test cases show files incorrectly marked as deleted post-clean

This represents a tactical retreat on one aspect of the feature while preserving the core functionality, with Stolee prioritizing correctness over expanded scope. The thread remains active with v3 expected soon, minus this problematic patch.
