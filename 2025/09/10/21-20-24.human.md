This is a maintainer response to the Rust adoption announcement in the RFC series introducing mandatory Rust support. Junio C Hamano provides feedback on the wording and practical implications of the transition plan.

The email focuses on two main concerns with the proposed BreakingChanges documentation. First, Junio questions the framing of Rust features as "test balloons" when the intent is actually to force preparation for mandatory adoption. Second, he raises practical concerns about the proposed long-term support handoff process, particularly around security fixes for the pre-Rust LTS release. He suggests the "hand over but still review" model may not be workable for embargoed security fixes.

Junio supports the overall transition timeline (optional in 2.52, default-on in 2.53, mandatory in 3.0) but notes the intentionally vague gap between steps 2 and 3. The response maintains the constructive but pragmatic tone typical of Junio's maintainer feedback, focusing on making the plan operationally sound rather than challenging the technical direction.

This email represents important maintainer input that will likely shape the final wording of the BreakingChanges documentation and LTS handoff process as the series progresses toward inclusion.
