This is a documentation patch announcing Rust's planned transition from optional to mandatory in Git's build process, part of the ongoing Rust infrastructure series (v4 7/9). Patrick Steinhardt proposes adding a formal notice to BreakingChanges.adoc that Rust will become required starting with Git 3.0, likely in late 2026.

The announcement outlines a phased rollout: optional in 2.52 (current), default-enabled in 2.53, and mandatory in 3.0. It acknowledges platform challenges (missing toolchains, bootstrapping issues) while emphasizing Git's critical infrastructure role. The document provides concrete build flags (`-Drust=enabled`, `WITH_RUST=YesPlease`) and details a long-term support plan for the final pre-3.0 release to ease distributor transitions.

This patch serves as the formal policy counterpart to the technical infrastructure work in earlier series patches. It's well-structured with clear milestones and addresses ecosystem concerns head-on, though some may question the accelerated timeline given outstanding Windows support questions noted in the thread context. The LTS commitment shows thoughtful consideration of downstream impacts.
