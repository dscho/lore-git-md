This is a build breakage report related to the `the_repository` removal effort. Jeff King reports that Patrick Steinhardt's patch to remove deprecated ODB wrapper functions (which was previously reviewed and approved) causes compilation failures when merged with the 'jch' branch. The issue stems from an implicit dependency where `pack-objects.c` was relying on `odb.h` to indirectly include `repository.h` for access to `struct repository` definitions.

The specific break occurs in `prepare_in_pack_by_idx()` where code from the ps/packfile-store series tries to access `pdata->repo->objects->packfiles` without a direct include of `repository.h`. Jeff suggests a straightforward fix - adding an explicit `#include "repository.h"` to `pack-objects.c`, either as part of the merge resolution or as a preparatory step in the packfile-store topic.

This highlights a subtle cross-dependency between the ODB cleanup and other ongoing work. While the wrapper removal itself is correct, it exposed an implicit header inclusion that wasn't caught during review. The proposed solution is minimal and maintains the intended direction of both efforts - the ODB cleanup proceeding while ensuring dependent code has proper explicit includes.
