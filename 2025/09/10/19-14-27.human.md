Documentation patch improving clarity of `--detach` explanation in `git-checkout`.

This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans' v4 series refining `git-checkout` documentation, focusing on making the `--detach` option's behavior clearer. The change replaces a technical description that duplicated branch-checkout text with a concise comparison to the more familiar `git checkout <branch>` behavior, explicitly noting the key difference in how HEAD is positioned (pointing directly to a commit ID rather than a branch).

The patch responds to user feedback about confusion around detached HEAD use cases, though it acknowledges that fully explaining those scenarios may be practical to do here. Instead, it provides just enough context by contrasting with the branch-switching case that users are more likely to know. This maintains the series' focus on precise technical accuracy while improving accessibility.

The change is small (3 lines added, 10 removed) and well-contained, continuing the pattern of v4's editorial refinements after the substantive technical consensus was reached in v3. It demonstrates careful attention to actual user confusion points while preserving all necessary technical information about the command's behavior.
