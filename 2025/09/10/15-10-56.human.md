This is a final review discussion for a bugfix in the `git subtree` contrib command, addressing a regression in squashed subtree merges. The technical aspects of the fix were already settled in previous rounds, and this email focuses on a minor shell scripting syntax clarification.

The discussion between Colin Stagner and Junio Hamano centers on the difference between `${var-}` and `${var:-}` syntax in shell scripts when used with `test -n`. Junio notes that while there's no practical difference in this case (since both evaluate to an empty string when the variable is unset or empty), the version without the colon (`${var-}`) is technically more correct when considering scripts that might use `set -u` (which causes unset variable references to error). Colin acknowledges this while pointing out that in this specific context, neither form is strictly necessary.
