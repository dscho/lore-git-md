Junio Hamano responds to Chirayu Desai's admission of still using the deprecated `git whatchanged` command. While acknowledging the muscle memory aspect, he reinforces the deprecation path by suggesting modern alternatives.

This maintains the project's removal stance while providing practical migration advice. The tone is helpful rather than confrontational, focusing on improved alternatives rather than debating the deprecation itself. No new technical concerns are raised about the removal plan.
