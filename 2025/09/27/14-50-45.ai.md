This is a clang-format configuration patch that's already been applied (merged) as part of the `git repo stats` series. It changes Git's code formatting rules to avoid adding unwanted spaces between control macros and their parentheses.

The patch switches from `SpaceBeforeParens: ControlStatements` to `SpaceBeforeParens: ControlStatementsExceptControlMacros` in `.clang-format`. This prevents clang-format from inserting spaces after control macros like `if`, `while`, and `for` when they appear as macro names rather than language keywords. The change aligns with Git's existing coding style where such spaces are not typically used with control macros.

Karthik Nayak provided assistance on this change. As noted in the thread context, this was one of the early preparatory patches in the series (patch 3/7) and was merged uncontroversially. The change is straightforward and focused purely on formatting preferences, with no impact on functionality or behavior.
