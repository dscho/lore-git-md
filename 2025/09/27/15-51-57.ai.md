This is a follow-up discussion about the already-merged clang-format patch that was originally part of the `git repo stats` series. The patch (previously 3/7 in the series) changed `.clang-format` to exclude control macros from space-before-parentheses rules, aligning with Git's coding style.

Junio Hamano notes that while the change is good, it didn't belong in the repo stats series due to being unrelated to that functionality. Justin Tobler agrees with this assessment and plans to resubmit the clang-format change as a separate topic while preparing a new version of the repo stats series without this patch.

The technical content remains unchanged from the original patch - it still modifies `.clang-format` to use `ControlStatementsExceptControlMacros` instead of `ControlStatements` for the `SpaceBeforeParens` setting. This prevents unwanted spaces between control macros and their parentheses, which Karthik Nayak had helped identify.

This exchange serves as a post-merge acknowledgement of the organizational feedback, with the author agreeing to follow Git's practice of keeping unrelated changes in separate topics going forward. The actual clang-format change remains valid and merged, just now recognized as belonging to its own distinct effort rather than the repo stats feature development.
