This is a follow-up in the `git whatchanged` deprecation discussion thread. Junio Hamano clarifies the purpose of the `--i-still-use-this` flag requirement, explaining it's not about proving usage but rather identifying unanticipated use cases that might need special consideration. 

He confirms Stephen Harding's shell alias (`gwch` mapping to `git whatchanged -p --abbrev-commit --pretty=medium`) can be directly replaced with an equivalent `git log` command, suggesting `--no-merges` should be added to match typical `whatchanged` behavior. Junio notes that `--pretty=medium` is already the default for `git log`, making the transition even simpler.

The exchange reinforces that the deprecation is proceeding as planned, with the focus now on ensuring users have clear migration paths for their specific workflows. The tone is matter-of-fact, acknowledging the command's redundancy while providing practical guidance for affected users.
