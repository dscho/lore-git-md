The exchange confirms the series is technically sound and compatible with brian m. carlson's own Rust-related work. While the cover letter inaccuracy about build system support remains, it's deemed unimportant since the actual code handles both build systems correctly. This moves the experimental Rust integration forward in Git's development pipeline, though it remains in the 'seen' branch for further testing rather than being merged to main branches yet.

The interaction shows maintainer and contributor alignment on this incremental step in Git's potential Rust adoption, with the technical concerns from earlier reviews now resolved.
