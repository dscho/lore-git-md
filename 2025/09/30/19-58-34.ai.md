Documentation patch (v4 5/5) adding a basic usage example to `git-push.adoc`. This final patch in Julia Evans' documentation series introduces a straightforward example of the most common push syntax (`git push origin main`) at the start of the man page, before diving into more complex default behavior cases. The addition serves as an accessible entry point for users who may not need to understand the nuanced default behaviors covered in previous patches. The example demonstrates pushing a local branch to its same-named counterpart on a remote, intentionally placed early in the documentation to provide immediate practical value while the more complex upstream branch behavior explanations (documented in patches 1-4) remain available for those who need them. This aligns with the series' goal of improving pedagogical clarity while maintaining technical precision about Git's push behavior.
