- Removal of the misc::varint Rust implementation
- Dropped build-helper crate and Cargo.lock integration

This appears to be a significant reversal from the previously positive trajectory of Rust integration work, though no rationale is provided for dropping the series. The xdiff cleanup work remains uncontroversial and ready for merging.
