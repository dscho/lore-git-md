This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about deprecating `git whatchanged`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Stephen Harding's report of using the command via an oh-my-zsh alias, confirming that `git log` can serve as a direct replacement with equivalent functionality.

Haugsbakk suggests Harding could migrate his shell alias to use `git log` instead, possibly adding `--no-merges` to match typical `whatchanged` behavior.

This reply reinforces the deprecation rationale while providing practical migration advice. The tone is matter-of-fact, positioning the change as a long-overdue cleanup rather than a disruptive break.
