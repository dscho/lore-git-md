Bugfix for autosquash rebase behavior with commit.cleanup and hooks.

Phillip Wood has followed up on the earlier discussion about commit message cleanup during autosquash rebases by submitting a patch. The issue occurs when prepare-commit-msg hooks inject comments into commit messages while commit.cleanup=strip is configured - these comments persist in squashed commits when they should be stripped, unlike regular commits where the cleanup works as expected.

The patch aims to make fixup/squash commands respect commit.cleanup consistently with pick commands, addressing the inconsistency introduced by commit f7d42ceec52 in 2021. Simon Cheng had previously agreed with this approach and suggested updating the git-config(1) man page to document which commands are affected by commit.cleanup, though Phillip notes this will require some auditing work.

The patch appears to be a direct response to the well-documented issue, with Phillip having clearly identified the root cause in previous discussion. The change would align behavior across different rebase operations while maintaining the expected cleanup of hook-injected content. This seems like a straightforward improvement to make the behavior more consistent and predictable.
