This is an update about the Git Contributor's Summit 2024 organization. Taylor Blau provides additional details about the event's agenda planning process, moving away from the previous pre-event topic voting system which proved ineffective. 

Participants who want to propose discussion topics or presentations are now email Taylor directly for access to a shared spreadsheet where topics will be collected. The actual voting on these topics will happen at the start of the summit itself, with the day's schedule then being built around the selected topics. 

This change aims to make the agenda-setting process more dynamic and responsive compared to previous years' approaches. Taylor invites feedback on this new method or other suggestions about running the event, accepting input both on the mailing list and privately. 

The email builds on previous summit announcements but focuses specifically on improving the topic selection workflow rather than registration logistics. It shows the organizers are actively refining the event format based on past experience while maintaining an open channel for community input.
