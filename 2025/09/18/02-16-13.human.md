This is a platform compatibility report about a build issue on NonStop systems. Randall Becker reports that recent changes introducing `uintptr_t` in the Git test suite (`clar` tests) have broken CI builds on NonStop x86 platforms. 

The immediate workaround appears to be adding `-D__NSK_OPTIONAL_TYPES__` to the build flags, though Randall notes he's unsure what other changes might be needed. He plans to work on a fix locally, which may require a patch series unless a simpler solution emerges.

The message serves both as a heads-up to other NonStop users who might encounter the same issue, and as notice that a fix is forthcoming. Randall includes a lighthearted signature noting his long experience with NonStop and UNIX systems (dating back to 1983 for UNIX and 1974 for NonStop, per the timestamp references). The tone suggests this is a known type of platform-specific issue that will be addressed, not a critical blocker.
