Refactoring patch in the xdiff preparatory cleanup series, eliminating redundant field aliasing in xdlclass_t.

This patch (7/10 in v2 series) removes three fields from xdlclass_t that were direct aliases of fields in xrecord_t: `ha`, `line`, and `size` are replaced with a single `rec` field containing the full xrecord_t. The change simplifies the code by removing duplicate storage and reducing the surface area for potential inconsistencies between aliased fields. All access to these fields is updated to go through the `rec` member instead.

The changes are mechanical but touch several parts of xdl_classify_record() where the fields were previously accessed directly. The patch maintains identical behavior while reducing the cognitive overhead of tracking aliased fields. This is part of the broader effort to clean up xdiff internals in preparation for potential Rust integration, though this particular change is language-agnostic.

The rest of the v2 series (patches 1-10) has already incorporated feedback and is ready for merging to 'next', with this patch being one of the final preparatory cleanups before the Rust-specific changes in the follow-up series. Performance impact appears neutral as this doesn't change the fundamental data structures, just how they're accessed.
