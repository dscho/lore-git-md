This thread continues the discussion about documenting the `git replay` command's `--contained` option, with a focus on finding the clearest terminology. The original documentation patch from Kristoffer Haugsbakk described the option as updating "branches contained in" the revision range, which Junio had approved. Phillip Wood later suggested this phrasing might be obscure to users and proposed alternatives using "tip" terminology, which is more established in Git's documentation.

Junio has now weighed in on Phillip's suggestion, agreeing that terms like "branch head" and "tip of the branch" are commonly used interchangeably in Git's docs. He proposes yet another phrasing that explicitly mentions "commits in the replayed range" to make the behavior clearer. While he's not entirely certain this is the best wording, it represents another option in the ongoing discussion about how to precisely describe this functionality.

The core behavior being documented remains unchanged - the option updates any branches pointing to commits that get replayed. The debate is purely about finding the most intuitive wording that aligns with Git's existing documentation style. Kristoffer, as the original author, will likely incorporate one of these suggestions in a v2 of his patch series. The thread shows the care being taken to get the documentation right for this relatively new command, with multiple experienced contributors offering perspectives on the clearest way to explain the feature.
