This thread documents improvements to the `git replay` man page, focusing on clarifying the behavior of the `--contained` option. Kristoffer Haugsbakk's v2 series (3 patches) incorporates feedback from the v1 discussion, with the key change being a more precise definition of `--contained` as "update all branches that point at commits in <revision-range>" instead of the vaguer "advance all branches contained in revision-range". The patch also documents that `--onto` is required when using `--contained`, which was an undocumented requirement.

The series has addressed most concerns raised in v1, with Junio Hamano, Phillip Wood, and Elijah Newren's earlier approvals implicitly carrying forward. Toon Claes noted a potential future consideration about silent conflict behavior, but this doesn't block the current documentation improvements. The changes maintain consistency between the documentation and implementation while making the actual behavior clearer to users. The series appears ready for merging, with the only loose end being whether to add an example illustrating `--contained` behavior.
