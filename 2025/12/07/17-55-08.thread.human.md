This thread documents improvements to the `git replay` man page, focusing on clarifying the command's behavior and standardizing its documentation format. The original series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk addressed three gaps: documenting the command's silent conflict resolution (unlike most Git commands), adding missing documentation for the `--contained` option, and standardizing Asciidoc markup.

Junio Hamano reviewed the series positively while noting a potential ambiguity in how `--contained` handles branches that span revision range boundaries. Toon Claes supported the changes but suggested the silent conflict behavior might warrant future reconsideration beyond documentation.

In the latest development, Phillip Wood has proposed a clearer wording for the `--contained` option: "Update all branches whose head commits are replayed. Requires --onto." This concise phrasing improves upon the original implementation's language by making the behavior and dependency more explicit. The discussion remains focused on refining documentation rather than changing functionality, with participants collaboratively working toward the clearest possible explanation of this relatively new command's behavior. The series appears ready for merging with this final wording improvement.
