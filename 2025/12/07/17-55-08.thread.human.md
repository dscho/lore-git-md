This thread documents improvements to the `git replay` man page, clarifying its behavior and completing its documentation coverage. Kristoffer Haugsbakk submitted a three-patch series that: 1) documents the command's silent conflict resolution (unlike most Git commands), 2) adds missing documentation for the `--contained` option, and 3) standardizes the man page's Asciidoc markup. 

Junio C Hamano reviewed the series and found it sensible, with only a minor observation that the phrasing of `--contained` (inherited from the original implementation) seemed "a bit sketchy" but not incorrect. The series appears headed for acceptance as-is. 

Kristoffer has followed up seeking clarification about how to best document the `--contained` option's behavior, suggesting this might be refined in future work. The discussion remains focused on documentation wording rather than functionality, with no changes expected to block the current series. This represents another step in Git's ongoing effort to improve and standardize its documentation.
