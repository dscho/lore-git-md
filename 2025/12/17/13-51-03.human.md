This is a documentation patch finalizing Git's policy on sign-off automation, following a settled discussion thread. Johannes Sixt provides a minor suggested wording tweak ("pile" vs "pile on") to the newly added FAQ section explaining why Git won't add configuration variables for automatic sign-offs. He notes the change is optional and approves the patch either way.

The email confirms the thread's conclusion - Git will maintain its position that sign-offs must remain manually triggered (except for the grandfathered `format.signoff`) to preserve the legal intentionality of the Developer Certificate of Origin. The documentation now clearly states this policy in both the FAQ and signoff-option documentation, with cross-references between them.

This represents the final polish on a carefully vetted policy decision, with all substantive discussion already resolved. The patch appears ready for inclusion with or without the suggested minor wording adjustment.
