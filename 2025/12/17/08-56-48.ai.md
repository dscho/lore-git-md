The suggestion centers around git-branchless's ability to apply formatters (like `cargo fmt`) across a commit stack using a worktree strategy, which could avoid merge conflicts by transforming the trees per snapshot rather than through three-way merges. The approach is described as running formatters on entire commit stacks efficiently (with parallel jobs) while maintaining clean history.
