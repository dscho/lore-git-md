Documentation refinement discussion for `git replay` man page.

Elijah Newren chimes in on the ongoing discussion about how to phrase the `--contained` option documentation in the `git replay` man page. The thread has seen multiple phrasing proposals, with Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood previously debating whether to use terms like "branch head", "tip commit", or more explicit wording. Kristoffer Haugsbakk had suggested removing "the replayed" from Junio's latest proposal for conciseness.

Newren's brief response indicates he's happy with either of the two most recent proposals: Junio's "Update all branches that point at commits in the replayed <revision-range>" or Kristoffer's slightly shorter version without "the replayed". This aligns with Phillip Wood's earlier acceptance of both variants.

The exchange shows the documentation effort is nearing consensus, with multiple contributors now agreeing the phrasing is clear whether or not it includes "the replayed". This appears to be the final loose end in a series that has already documented silent conflict resolution behavior and standardized man page links. The discussion remains focused on wording precision rather than any functional changes to `git replay` itself.
