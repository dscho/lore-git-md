**Build system discussion on macOS iconv workarounds**

Carlo Arenas Belón raises an important consideration in the ongoing macOS iconv discussion, focusing on the build-system approach that prefers Homebrew's libiconv. While acknowledging Homebrew libraries are generally better, he points out they may not be ideal for all scenarios - particularly when building fat binaries that need to work across CPU architectures on macOS.

The key concern is that forcing Homebrew as a dependency could complicate distribution, since system libraries (even with their known bugs) might be preferred in multi-architecture builds. This adds a new dimension to the tradeoffs between the three proposed solutions (code workaround, platform detection, and Homebrew preference).

Carlo also floats the idea of adding a `NO_HOMEBREW` Makefile flag, analogous to existing flags for other package managers, to give users more control over library selection. This suggestion indicates the discussion may be expanding from technical implementation details to broader build system flexibility considerations.

The email doesn't directly challenge the technical merits of any solution, but introduces practical deployment concerns that could influence which approach (or combination) ultimately gets adopted. It keeps all three solution paths viable while adding new criteria for evaluation.
