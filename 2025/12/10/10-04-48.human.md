This is a status update on two of Karthik Nayak's patch series related to ref handling. The first series (`kn/ref-location`) introduces GIT_REF_URI for specifying reference backends and directories, which would be useful for ref migration. Karthik notes he's currently on leave but plans to address Patrick Steinhardt's review comments in a future version, though it will be delayed.

The second series (`kn/fix-fetch-backfill-tag-with-batched-ref-updates`) fixes issues with tag fetching when batched ref updates are involved. Karthik indicates the only outstanding issue is a minor typo in test comments, which he considers trivial enough that a reroll may not be necessary, though he's willing to provide one if preferred. 

This appears to be routine maintenance update on two technical ref-related improvements, with one series awaiting more substantial revisions and the other nearly ready pending a decision about whether to fix a minor documentation issue. Both seem like well-considered changes to Git's ref handling internals.
