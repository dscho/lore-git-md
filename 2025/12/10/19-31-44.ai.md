This is a follow-up discussion on Patrick Steinhardt's bugfix series addressing object database issues, specifically focusing on the first patch about geometric repacking with promisor remotes.

Justin Tobler raises some thoughtful questions about the patch's approach to handling promisor objects during geometric repacks. The key points are:

1. **Clarification request**: Justin questions whether the original explanation about `--stdin-packs` and `--exclude-promisor-objects` incompatibility might be backwards in its description of which option does what. He provides his understanding that `--stdin-packs` uses input packs as object sources while `--exclude-promisor-objects` does a normal walk excluding promisor objects.

2. **Behavior verification**: Justin seeks confirmation that geometric repacks already implicitly exclude promisor packfiles when merging, which would make the explicit `--exclude-promisor-objects` flag unnecessary. The test case shows promisor packs remain unchanged after geometric repack while regular packs are repacked.

3. **Potential concern**: Justin raises a new consideration about whether this approach could lead to accumulation of many promisor packfiles that never get repacked, since they're excluded from geometric repacking operations.

The discussion highlights that while the technical fix is sound (moving the promisor check outside geometric repack logic), there may be broader implications for partial clone maintenance that warrant consideration. The test case demonstrates the current behavior but doesn't address the long-term pack accumulation scenario Justin mentions. This could prompt either clarification of the existing behavior or consideration of future improvements to promisor pack maintenance.
