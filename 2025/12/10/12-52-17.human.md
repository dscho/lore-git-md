Performance optimization for MIDX (multi-pack index) handling during repacking. This is the second version of a 3-patch series that avoids unnecessary MIDX rewrites when the index is already up-to-date, particularly beneficial for geometric repacking scenarios.

The series builds on previous MIDX work and addresses feedback from the initial version. Key changes in v2 include moving the skip logic into `write_midx_internal()` to better handle `--stdin-packs` cases and dropping the preferred pack handling (which may be addressed separately if deemed important). The author notes this can provide a 31x speedup for no-op geometric repacks.

The first commit fixes a `BUG()` when getting preferred pack without a reverse index, while the subsequent commits introduce and apply the optimization logic. The changes affect midx-write.c, midx.c, and pack-revindex.h, with new tests added to verify the behavior.

The thread shows technical consensus on the approach, with Taylor Blau having reviewed and Patrick Steinhardt incorporating feedback. The implementation appears solid, focused on performance without introducing new user-facing behavior, and maintains compatibility with existing MIDX infrastructure.
