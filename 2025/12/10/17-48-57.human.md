This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about HTTP authentication behavior when .netrc credentials are insufficient. Randall Becker raises an important concern about the potential impact on credential helpers, particularly custom ones, if the proposed changes to 403 handling were to force credential wipes.

The email emphasizes that any solution must not disrupt existing credential helper workflows, especially on non-Linux platforms where .netrc may not be used. Randall specifically warns that credential wipes without proper protocol updates could break custom helpers, calling this a potential blocker for some users.

This intervention comes as the thread was already moving away from credential-erasing approaches, with Ashlesh Gawande having withdrawn his initial proposal in favor of a more conservative solution that only prompts on 403 when no credentials exist. Randall's comments reinforce the need for careful consideration of edge cases and platform differences before implementing any changes to the credential flow.

The discussion remains focused on finding a solution that maintains backward compatibility while addressing the original issue of poor UX with read-only .netrc credentials. Randall's input highlights that the test infrastructure being developed should likely include credential helper scenarios to ensure no regressions in that area.
