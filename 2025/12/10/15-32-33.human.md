Refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt (v2) continuing the ODB alternates abstraction work. This 8-patch series restructures how Git handles alternate object directories to work through a "source" abstraction rather than direct filesystem operations, preparing for pluggable object database backends that may not use traditional filesystem paths.

The key changes in v2 are:
- Renamed `odb_add_source()` to `odb_add_alternate_recursively()` to better reflect its recursive nature (addressing feedback from Justin Tobler)
- Maintains the same systematic approach as v1, just with clearer naming

The series builds on positive reviews of the first five patches in v1, with patch 7/8 also receiving approval. The changes are part of Git's ongoing object storage modernization effort, specifically enabling future support for non-filesystem backends by abstracting alternates handling. The implementation appears well-considered, with clean separation of concerns and thorough handling of edge cases.

This continues productive collaboration between Patrick Steinhardt (driving the ODB abstraction work) and reviewer Justin Tobler, who has provided constructive feedback throughout. The only remaining architectural question noted in the thread - about alternates representation in `struct object_database` - appears deferred for future work rather than blocking this series.
