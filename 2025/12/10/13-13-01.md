**Thread**: [thread](./13-13-00.thread.md#:~:text=2025/12/10/13-13-01)

Introduce a new macro MEMZERO_ARRAY() that zeroes the memory allocated
by ALLOC_ARRAY() and friends. And add coccinelle rule to enforce the use
of this macro.
