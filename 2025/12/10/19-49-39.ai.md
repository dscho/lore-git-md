This is a follow-up discussion on Patrick Steinhardt's bugfix patch for commit graph maintenance in `builtin/gc.c`. Justin Tobler provides additional review commentary on the technical details of the fix.

This would provide additional coverage for the SEEN flag logic. The existing test in t/t7900-maintenance.sh demonstrates the basic functionality with merge commits but could be strengthened in this regard.
