This is a documentation discussion thread about terminology for the `git replay` man page.

Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's suggestion about clarifying the `--contained` option description in the `git replay` documentation. Phillip had proposed using "tip" terminology (like "tip commit") to make it clearer that the option updates branches pointing to replayed commits, finding the original "contained branches" phrasing obscure.

Junio agrees that "branch head", "tip of the branch", and similar terms are interchangeable in Git's documentation, and that including "commit" would add clarity. He offers an alternative phrasing: "Update all branches that point at commits in the replayed <revision-range>", though expresses some uncertainty about whether this is the best wording.

The discussion continues the ongoing debate about how to precisely describe the `--contained` option's behavior while maintaining consistency with Git's existing documentation conventions. This follows earlier approval from Junio of Kristoffer Haugsbakk's original phrasing, showing the careful consideration being given to getting the terminology right for this relatively new command.
