Refactoring series (v2) from Patrick Steinhardt continuing the ODB alternates abstraction work. This is a complete 8-patch revision of the previously reviewed series, now incorporating naming feedback from Justin Tobler.

Key changes in v2:
- Renames `odb_add_source()` to `odb_add_alternate_recursively()` to better reflect its recursive nature (patch 2)
- Maintains all other technical changes from v1:
  - Moves relative path resolution into parsing phase
  - Consolidates normalized path computation
  - Restructures recursive alternates processing
  - Implements source-based reading/write of alternates

The series systematically replaces direct filesystem access with source-based operations, preparing for future backend support where alternates may not use traditional filesystem paths. Patch 8 introduces `odb_source_write_alternate()` as the write-side counterpart to the read operations refactored earlier.

Technical context for future discussion:
- All alternates handling now flows through source abstraction
- URI-based source differentiation planned for future backends (e.g. "files://path", "postgres://host")
- Open architectural questions remain about alternates representation in `struct object_database` (single-list fragility during transactions)
