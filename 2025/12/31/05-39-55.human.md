This is a performance discussion thread about `git pack-refs` behavior on NFS with high ref counts. Martin Fick pushes back on the NFS explanation for Git's slowness compared to JGit, noting that with only 37 pack files, the NFS overhead shouldn't account for the dramatic 3+ minute runtime difference when JGit handles the same environment in 20 seconds. He questions whether the object lookups are truly the bottleneck, as the observed speed would imply implausibly slow (~1s per lookup) NFS operations. The discussion continues to probe why Git's verification checks are so much more expensive than JGit's approach in this scenario, with the reftable backend emerging as a potential long-term solution but no immediate fixes identified yet.
