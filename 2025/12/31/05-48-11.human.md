This is a performance discussion follow-up in the ongoing investigation of `git pack-refs --all` slowness on large repositories. Martin Fick responds to Jeff King's explanation about tag peeling overhead, questioning why Git's implementation would be significantly slower than JGit's when both need to perform similar verification.

Martin acknowledges the technical explanation about object verification and peeling, but pushes back on this being the sole bottleneck. He notes that since JGit handles the same operations much faster, there may be inefficiencies in Git's write path - possibly due to unbuffered writes or lack of optimization in this less-frequently-used code path. 

The email keeps focus on the core mystery: why Git's implementation shows orders-of-magnitude worse performance than JGit despite similar requirements, with Martin suggesting the write operations rather than reads might be the real culprit.
