This thread tracks two major patch series in Junio Hamano's December 2025 "What's cooking" report. Adrian Ratiu's 11-patch series modernizes Git's hook API with structured receive/update hooks and parallelization support, replacing the current ad-hoc `run_command()` approach. While Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed v4 positively with one outstanding implementation question, the series awaits Junio's architectural review and Josh Steadmon's subsystem expert review (delayed due to personal matters). 

The second series by Aaron Plattner (2 patches) optimizes promisor object enumeration by skipping unnecessary blob parsing. Jeff King reviewed v2 positively, and Junio has verified that v3 properly incorporates the feedback, promoting it to 'next'. This administrative update simply confirms Junio received Emily Shaffer's clarification about Josh's availability, with no change to the hook series' status. Both changes remain on track - the promisor optimization advancing toward mainline, while the hook API modernization awaits final reviews.
