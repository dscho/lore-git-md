This is a maintenance patch in the Scalar configuration cleanup series, now in its third iteration. It reorganizes and simplifies Scalar's configuration settings after the audit performed in previous patches.

The main changes alphabetize the configuration options (with platform-specific ones grouped at the end) and removes the 'overwrite_on_reconfigure' setting from most options, making them optional rather than required. The one exception is `index.skipHash`, which remains forced to 'true' to correct a previous incorrect setting of 'false' - this will help users upgrade to the proper configuration.

The patch shows careful consideration of backwards compatibility and user impact, particularly around the Windows-specific `core.untrackedCache` setting which remains disabled due to performance issues identified in testing. The changes follow through on the series' goal of making Scalar's configuration more maintainable and better documented while preserving important optimizations.
