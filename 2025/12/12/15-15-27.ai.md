Maintenance patch in the Scalar config cleanup series, focusing on reorganization and simplification of configuration settings.

This patch alphabetizes Scalar's configuration settings while maintaining platform-specific groupings, and removes the "overwrite_on_reconfigure" flag from most settings to make them optional rather than required. The key exception is `index.skipHash`, which remains forced to `true` to correct a previous incorrect setting of `false` (This addresses a regression since commit 4933152cbb.) The reorganization groups Windows-specific settings (`http.sslBackend` and `core.untrackedCache`) at the end, with detailed comments preserved about the Windows performance tradeoffs.

The changes maintain all existing functionality while making the configuration more maintainable and user-friendly. The patch follows through on the series' goal of thorough config documentation and cleanup, with special attention to backward compatibility concerns for the `index.skipHash` case. This appears ready for integration, building on previous review feedback from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. The careful handling of the Windows-specific `core.untrackedCache` setting (with its performance caveats) and the transitional handling of `index.skipHash` demonstrate the series' attention to real-world deployment concerns.
