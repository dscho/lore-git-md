This is a platform compatibility update to the macOS iconv workaround patch series.

René Scharfe responds to Junio's "What's cooking" status report, asking to hold off on merging the macOS iconv workaround patch (rs/macos-iconv-workaround) to the 'next' branch. He indicates he's just sent a v2 of the patch that adds a NO_HOMEBREW option.

The original patch (as noted in Junio's status report) addresses broken ISO-2022 string handling in macOS's built-in iconv by using Homebrew's iconv instead. The new version appears to make this optional, though the email doesn't specify whether NO_HOMEBREW would disable the workaround entirely or fall back to another solution.

This seems like a straightforward platform-specific fix that's still being refined. The addition of a build-time option suggests the author is being thoughtful about making the solution configurable for different macOS environments. Since it's actively being revised, holding for v2 makes sense.
