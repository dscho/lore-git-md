This is the final documentation patch in a series about Scalar's configuration settings, building on previous refactoring and maintenance work. The patch adds comprehensive documentation explaining the rationale behind each config setting that Scalar modifies during clone`, `register`, and `reconfigure` operations.

The documentation covers over 20 config settings, organized into platform-agnostic and platform-specific sections. Each entry provides clear justification for the chosen value, often citing performance benefits for large repositories or cross-platform compatibility needs. Notable technical details include the temporary use of commitGraph.generationVersion=1 (pending upgrade path verification), performance-oriented defaults like index.skipHash=true and status.aheadBehind=false, and platform-specific handling of core.untrackedCache and http.sslBackend.

The patch reflects feedback from Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, with particularly detailed explanations for settings that interact with background maintenance (gc.auto=0, fetch.writeCommitGraph=false) and those with platform-specific considerations. The documentation also notes where Scalar's choices differ from Git's experimental feature groups (feature.experimental=false, feature.manyFiles=false) and explains the tradeoffs for commitGraph.changedPaths=true.

This completes the series' goal of documenting Scalar's configuration approach after a thorough audit and reorganization. The documentation will help users understand why these settings are recommended and provide context for future maintenance. The series has received positive reviews and appears ready for integration.
