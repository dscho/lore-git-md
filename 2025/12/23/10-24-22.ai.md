This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about adding default branch comparison to `git status` output. The author Harald Nordgren responds to Junio C Hamano's concerns about triangular workflows and branch naming, providing concrete examples from their own workflow.

The email explains the author's motivation for wanting this feature - they currently manually track the default branch (via `origin/HEAD`) on their working branches to maintain visibility of drift from mainline, but find this workflow difficult to explain to less experienced developers. They demonstrate their current manual process involving `git symbolic-ref` to track the default branch and acknowledge that "default" may be GitHub-centric terminology.

Key technical details mentioned:
- The proposed comparison would use `refs/remotes/upstream/HEAD` as the reference point
- Current workaround involves complex commands that could be simplified by built-in status reporting
- The author suggests "upstream/HEAD" might be a better term than "default branch" for this functionality

The response indicates the author is receptive to design feedback but maintains that the core need (visibility of mainline drift) is valid, especially for less experienced users who may be hesitant to modify tracking branches manually. The discussion continues to focus on finding the right abstraction and terminology that works across different workflows.
