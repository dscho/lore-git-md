Harald Nordgren's long-running effort to enhance `git status` with push tracking information has reached completion after 22 iterations of review. The 2-patch series refactors the tracking info display logic and then extends it to show divergence from both upstream and push branches when they differ. Junio Hamano has given final approval, marking the series ready for inclusion in the `next` integration branch.

The implementation introduces a new `format_branch_comparison()` helper that centralizes the display logic while maintaining all existing behavior, including quick mode and advice messages. The second patch adds push tracking support through new BRANCH_MODE flags and handles complex cases like remapped refspecs. Extensive test coverage in t6040-tracking-info.sh verifies the behavior.

Key contributors included Ben Knoble for workflow advice, Phillip Wood for code review, and Patrick Steinhardt for flag convention guidance. Junio particularly noted the successful resolution of variable naming (`cmp_fetch`/`push_cmp_fetch`) as a good compromise between clarity and consistency. Any remaining minor issues, like a known pluralization quirk, will be addressed in follow-up patches rather than delaying this now-polished series.
