Harald Nordgren's enhancement to `git status`, which shows divergence from both upstream and push tracking branches, has completed its 22-iteration review cycle and received final approval from Junio C Hamano for inclusion in the `next` branch. The two-patch series refactors the tracking display logic into a new helper function and cleanly extends the output format while maintaining backward compatibility. Key contributors included Ben Knoble, Phillip Wood, and Patrick Steinhardt, who provided feedback on workflow, code structure, and flag conventions respectively. Kristoffer Haugsbakk, while not participating in the technical review, contributed documentation guidance about versioning conventions in the thread's later stages. With all substantive discussion concluded and no outstanding concerns, this marks the successful conclusion of a long-running effort to improve branch tracking visibility in Git's status output.
