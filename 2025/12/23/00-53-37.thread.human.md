This thread documents the completion of Harald Nordgren's long-running effort to add configurable branch comparison to `git status`. After 28 iterations and extensive review from Jeff King and Junio Hamano, the feature is now ready for merging in its initial limited form.

The implementation introduces a `status.compareBranches` configuration that will show comparisons against multiple branches in `git status` output. Following Junio's guidance, the feature is being rolled out in phases - the first version will support only `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` syntax, with full DWIM resolution to follow in a future release. The technical foundation comes from Jeff King's refinements to `repo_dwim_ref()` and includes comprehensive test coverage (337 lines in t6040-tracking-info.sh). All edge cases around dangling refs and tag resolution have been addressed.

With all technical concerns resolved and maintainer approval secured, Harald has confirmed the feature is ready for merging.
