The auto-configuration series has gone through multiple rounds of review from Patrick Steinhardt, Junio, Toon Claes, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk, addressing all technical and documentation feedback. The just-arrived final patch improves the documentation for the `promisor.acceptFromServer` config option, clarifying its behavior relative to the new `acceptFromServerUrl` option and presenting the information more clearly while maintaining all security considerations. 

Both series now have complete implementations, thorough test coverage, and polished documentation, with no outstanding technical issues. They represent production-ready enhancement of promisor remote capabilities, with the auto-configuration feature particularly notable for its secure handling of remote naming and URL patterns. The work is fully baked and ready for integration into Git's main branch.
