Documentation patch finalizing Git's stance on automatic sign-offs. This patch adds clear language to both the FAQ and command documentation explaining why Git will not support configuration options like `commit.signoff` to automatically add Signed-off-by trailers. The rationale centers on preserving the legal intentionality of the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) - automated sign-offs could undermine their credibility in legal contexts.

The text originates from earlier discussions involving Elijah Newren and Johannes Sixt, with Junio Hamano serving as the final editor. The FAQ section provides a detailed explanation of the legal reasoning, while the signoff-option documentation includes a brief note cross-referencing the FAQ. Both sections explicitly call out `format.signoff` as a historical exception that won't be expanded.

This represents the culmination of a long-standing policy discussion, with all key contributors (including brian m. carlson) now aligned on both the substance and presentation of this documentation. The patch cleanly implements the consensus that emerged from the thread about maintaining sign-off intentionality while providing clear guidance to new contributors.
