Johannes Sixt responds to Junio Hamano's proposal to keep the legal rationale about sign-offs primarily in the Git FAQ rather than duplicating it in the command documentation. While agreeing with the overall approach, Sixt argues that users looking to automate sign-offs are more likely to search command manuals than the FAQ, so at least a cross-reference should be added to guide readers to the full explanation.

The exchange represents the last open question in this now-concluded discussion about Git's stance on automatic sign-offs. The remaining question is purely about documentation structure - whether to include a pointer to the FAQ in the command documentation.
