The key point is that the merge machinery uses `--no-rename-empty` by default, which affects how empty files are handled during rename detection. Newren shows the different outputs with and without this option, and traces the behavior back to a 2012 commit that intentionally disabled rename detection for empty files during merges for safety reasons.

He notes there are several other subtle differences in how the merge machinery is configured compared to regular `git diff`, though these rarely surface in normal usage. The email serves as a definitive explanation for the observed behavior, confirming it's intentional rather than a bug.
