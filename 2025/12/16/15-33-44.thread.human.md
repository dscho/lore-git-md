Johannes Schindelin and Karsten Blees have completed their work on a 5-patch series that lays the technical groundwork for proper symlink support on Windows. The changes touch several areas: fixing `git rev-parse` behavior in symlink-containing paths, ensuring early config parsing during `git init`, optimizing symlink buffer handling, and making path separator handling platform-agnostic. 

The series went through thorough review, with Windows-specific aspects vetted by Schindelin (Git for Windows maintainer) and Blees, while Junio Hamano ensured the solutions remained cleanly cross-platform. The final iteration shows a net reduction of 20 lines across 6 files while solving subtle edge cases, particularly around Windows' path length limits and separator handling. 

Junio has now given his final approval, indicating the series will be queued for the 'next' integration branch after a brief waiting period. This marks the completion of the preparatory work needed before actual Windows symlink support can be implemented in a follow-up topic. The changes maintain backward compatibility while establishing consistent behavior across platforms.
