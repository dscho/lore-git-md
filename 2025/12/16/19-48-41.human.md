This is a documentation patch that finalizes Git's policy on sign-off automation, closing out a long-standing discussion thread. The patch adds clear documentation explaining why Git will not add configuration options like `commit.signoff` to automatically append Signed-off-by trailers, citing legal concerns about maintaining the intentionality of DCO certification.

The documentation appears in two places with identical rationale: a new FAQ entry and an addition to the signoff-option documentation. The text explains that `format.signoff` exists as a historical anomaly but should not be replicated, as automated sign-offs could undermine their legal standing. The patch has already received positive feedback from multiple contributors during the review process.

This represents the settled consensus of the Git project on this matter, with all technical and legal considerations now clearly documented for future reference.
