This is a follow-up discussion in the hook subsystem refactoring series, specifically addressing part 5 (pre-push hook conversion). Patrick Steinhardt questions why the new switch statement in transport.c now skips more ref status cases than the original code did. Adrian Ratiu explains this was an intentional optimization to avoid feeding stdin to the pre-push hook for refs that will be rejected anyway, though acknowledges the rationale wasn't clearly documented.

The key technical point is whether to maintain the expanded status filtering (covering all REJECT_* cases) or revert to the original more limited filtering. Adrian offers two options: (1) split the change into a separate commit with better explanation, or (2) drop the new cases since they're a minor optimization. Patrick leans toward option (2) unless there's a demonstrable bug being fixed, suggesting that if it is fixing something, it should be properly explained in a separate commit.

This discussion represents the final review points being resolved in the series, with Patrick's response indicating the pre-push hook conversion is otherwise acceptable. The exchange shows maintainers converging on a solution while maintaining high standards for clear justification of behavior changes, even for minor optimizations.
