Feature/bugfix for the experimental `git replay` command, now at v3 with punctuation improvements from Junio Hamano's feedback. This patch modifies `git replay` to drop commits that become empty during replay (when their changes already exist in the target branch), matching the behavior of `git rebase --reapply-cherry-picks --empty=drop` and `git cherry-pick --empty=drop`. Originally-empty commits are preserved. The implementation compares tree OIDs to detect empty commits and updates branch pointers when tip commits are dropped.
