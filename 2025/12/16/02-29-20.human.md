This is a documentation patch finalizing Git's stance on automatic sign-off configuration, following a thread that explored expanding sign-off support beyond `git format-patch`.

Junio C Hamano responds to Elijah Newren's feedback about the clarity of legal rationale text in the signoff-option documentation. The proposed text explains why Git won't support automatic sign-offs (beyond the historical `format.signoff` exception) due to concerns about weakening the legal standing of the Developer Certificate of Origin.

Newren had noted the documentation felt abrupt and lacked context for readers. Junio agrees with this assessment and suggests a simpler solution: keeping the legal explanation only in the FAQ section while leaving the signoff-option documentation unchanged. This maintains the policy consensus while addressing readability concerns.

The exchange represents the final polishing of documentation that codifies Git's position against automatic sign-offs, following earlier agreement among maintainers about both the policy and its legal justification. The thread has now moved from substantive discussion to purely editorial improvements.
