This is a localization (i18n) issue in the ongoing `repo structure` command series. Jiang Xin points out an inconsistency in how the string "byte" is marked for translation - it's being used both with and without plural forms in different parts of the codebase, which causes `make pot` to fail.

The conflict arises between:
1. The new plural-aware usage in Justin Tobler's patch (using `Q_("byte", "bytes", bytes)`)
2. An existing singular-only usage in test-simple-ipc.c (using `N_("byte")`)

Jiang Xin suggests resolving this by making the test-simple-ipc.c usage reuse the plural-form translation rather than having its own singular definition. This is a straightforward fix that maintains consistency with the new i18n approach while preserving the existing functionality.

The issue appears minor but is important for proper localization support, and the proposed solution seems reasonable. This is the kind of small but important detail that often comes up in final rounds of review for i18n-related changes.
