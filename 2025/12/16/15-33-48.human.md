Performance improvement for Windows symlink support. This is the fourth patch in a series preparing for better symlink handling on Windows, co-authored by Karsten Blees with sign-off from Johannes Schindelin (Git for Windows maintainer).

The patch removes an artificial PATH_MAX limitation in `strbuf_readlink()`, allowing it to handle arbitrarily long symlink targets. While the change is small (just 4 lines modified), it's particularly relevant for Windows where path length limits are higher than traditional Unix PATH_MAX. The implementation now uses an unbounded loop similar to `strbuf_getcwd()`, growing the buffer as needed until the full link target can be read.

This continues the series' theme of removing Windows-specific limitations in core Git utilities. The change is straightforward and well-motivated, with clear sign-off from the platform expert. While the immediate impact is on Windows, the improvement is general and could benefit other platforms with long path support.
