This is a status update in the ongoing review of Adrian Ratiu's hook API modernization series (ar/run-command-hook). Junio Hamano responds to Adrian's suggestion that the 11-patch series is ready to merge to 'next' since tests pass and there's been no recent feedback.

Junio cautions that passing tests only confirms no regressions under current test coverage, not necessarily code quality or readiness for future development. He notes the lack of feedback could indicate either perfection or disinterest rather than consensus. Junio plans to personally review the series with fresh eyes before deciding on promotion to 'next', signaling he wants to ensure the foundational hook API changes are solid before they become part of Git's development branch. This maintains Junio's careful approach to core infrastructure changes even when they appear technically sound.
