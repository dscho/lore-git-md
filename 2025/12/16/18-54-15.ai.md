Documentation patch finalizing the Git project's stance against automatic sign-offs beyond the existing `format.signoff` exception. This patch adds identical legal rationale text to both the FAQ and signoff-option documentation, implementing the consensus reached in the thread that automatic sign-offs should remain limited to maintain the legal integrity of the Developer Certificate of Origin.

The FAQ section receives a substantial new entry explaining why configuration variables like `commit.signoff` will never be added, emphasizing the need for explicit sign-off actions to preserve legal intentionality. The signoff-option documentation gets a shorter cross-reference to this explanation. Both sections describe `format.signoff` as a historical anomaly that shouldn't be replicated.

The patch represents the final implementation of the thread's conclusions, incorporating wording improvements from Elijah Newren and Johannes Sixt while maintaining the identical-text approach favored by maintainer Junio Hamano. It serves as the authoritative documentation of Git's position that sign-offs must remain manually triggered actions except for the grandfathered `format-patch` case, closing the door on future requests for broader automation.
