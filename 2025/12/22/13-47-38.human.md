This is a discussion email in the ongoing RFC thread about the proposed `git-history` command, focusing on branch rewrite behavior. D. Ben Knoble weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for implicit commit range definition (HEAD-to-target walk) and its implications for stacked branches.

The email acknowledges that previous discussion has addressed some of Knoble's initial questions about `--update-refs` behavior. Knoble presents this as a tradeoff - the current behavior provides an implicit backup via untouched stacked branches, but may require more user intervention for complete history updates.
