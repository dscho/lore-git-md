This is a critical response to the proposed `checkout.remoteBranchTemplate` feature. Junio C Hamano raises fundamental concerns about the design rationale and potential complications, suggesting the feature may solve a self-inflicted problem.

The response challenges the patch's core premise, arguing that naming local branches differently from their remote counterparts creates more problems than it solves. Key objections include:
1. The justification is presented backwards - the unusual naming scheme should be proven useful before adding support
2. Push behavior becomes complicated, especially with matching push configurations
3. The proposed solution may be unnecessary since avoiding the unusual naming scheme would prevent these problems entirely

The critique focuses on architectural concerns rather than implementation details, questioning whether the feature belongs in Git at all. The maintainer explicitly states they are not yet convinced of the feature's merit, signaling this will need significant discussion before proceeding. The response doesn't reject outright but sets a high bar for justification, particularly around edge cases in push behavior.
