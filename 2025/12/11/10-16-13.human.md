This is a refactoring patch that updates Git's build requirements to mandate C99-style flexible array members (FAMs) rather than supporting older compiler-specific workarounds. 

Junio Hamano proposes removing support for the historical GNU extension (`array[0]`) and the fallback "waste one element" approach (`array[1]`), requiring compilers to support the standard C99 syntax (`array[]`). The change is documented in `BreakingChanges.adoc` and implemented by modifying the `FLEX_ARRAY` macro logic in `git-compat-util.h`.

The patch is part of Git's ongoing effort to modernize its codebase by requiring more recent C language features. The implementation uses a `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` guard, suggesting this may be staged for a future major version (3.0). The change is straightforward and well-motivated, though it may require some compiler updates for users on very old toolchains.
