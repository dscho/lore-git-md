Bugfix for a submodule-related memory leak in `git-grep`. This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series (the first fixed a commit-graph memory issue).

The patch addresses a leak that occurs when reading data from submodules during `git-grep` operations. The root cause is an incorrect cleanup sequence in `odb_free()` where sources were being freed before being closed, causing resources to leak. The fix simply reorders the cleanup to call `odb_close()` before `odb_free_sources()`.

The commit includes a detailed stack trace showing the leak's origin and a clear explanation of the incorrect cleanup sequence. The change itself is minimal (a two-line swap in `odb.c`) but effectively plugs the leak. This appears to be a straightforward, well-explained fix with no obvious downsides.

This patch was previously reviewed in v1 and remains unchanged in v2, suggesting it's considered ready. The series as a whole (now down to two patches after withdrawing a problematic geometric repack change) looks solid and focused on clear memory management issues.
