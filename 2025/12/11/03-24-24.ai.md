Git maintainer Junio C Hamano responds to the RFC proposing to modify `git reset --hard` behavior for uncommitted staged changes. He expresses skepticism about the proposal's core premise, pointing out that `--hard` has always been explicitly documented as a destructive operation that removes changes without warning. His brief "I dunno" suggests he doesn't see a compelling need to change this long-standing behavior, though he leaves the door open for further discussion rather than outright rejecting the idea. This maintains the thread's RFC status while signaling that the proposal may face resistance based on Git's philosophy around explicit destructive operations. The response doesn't address the implementation details or documentation aspects raised in the original proposal, focusing instead on the conceptual justification.
