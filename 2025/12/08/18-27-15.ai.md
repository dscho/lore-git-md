Performance optimization for MIDX handling in geometric repacking.

This is the second patch in a two-part series addressing MIDX rewrite efficiency, specifically during geometric repacking operations. The patch introduces logic to skip MIDX regeneration when the existing MIDX already reflects the current pack state, which is particularly valuable for geometric repacking where no new packs may be created despite the repack invocation.

The core change adds a `midx_needs_update()` function that checks four conditions requiring MIDX rewrite: missing MIDX, requested bitmap missing, preferred pack change, or differing packfile set. The patch includes thorough test coverage in both `t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` and `t7703-repack-geometric.sh`, verifying retention of up-to-date MIDX files and proper regeneration when needed. Benchmark results show a dramatic 31x speedup for no-op geometric repacks with MIDX writing.

Key technical details: The new function compares pack basenames (stripping .idx/.pack suffixes) between current and proposed MIDX contents. It handles edge cases like closed object databases and interacts with both bitmap and non-bitmap MIDX scenarios. The changes are confined to `repack-midx.c` with test additions, maintaining the series' focus on MIDX optimization without introducing new user-facing behavior.

This completes the series started with the MIDX reverse-index BUG() fix, together addressing performance pain points in geometric repacking workflows. The implementation appears robust with comprehensive test coverage of the new conditional MIDX writing logic.
