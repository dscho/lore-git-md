This is a performance optimization patch series targeting the multi-pack index (MIDX) handling during repacking.

Patrick Steinhardt proposes two related changes to avoid unnecessary MIDX rewrites. The main focus is on geometric repacking scenarios where no new packfiles are created but the MIDX is still regenerated. The first patch fixes a BUG() that could occur when checking preferred packs without a reverse index, while the second implements the core optimization to skip MIDX regeneration when it's already up-to-date.

The changes touch midx.c and pack-revindex.h for the bugfix, and introduce new logic in repack-midx.c. The series includes substantial test coverage with 135 new lines of tests across two test files, suggesting careful validation of both the bugfix and optimization.

The motivation references prior discussion (linked in the cover letter) about avoiding redundant MIDX operations. The patches appear focused and well-tested, addressing a clear performance optimization opportunity in the repacking workflow.
