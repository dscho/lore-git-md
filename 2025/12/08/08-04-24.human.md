This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt's refactoring series to modernize Git's alternates handling as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The patch continues the transition from direct filesystem operations to a source-based interface.

The change renames `read_info_alternates()` to `odb_source_read_alternates()` and modifies it to operate on an `odb_source` object rather than taking a raw path string. This better aligns with the series' goal of abstracting alternates handling to support future pluggable ODB backends. The patch updates all callers to use the new interface while maintaining identical behavior.

The diff shows straightforward mechanical changes - the function signature update and corresponding caller adjustments. This continues the pattern seen in earlier patches of the series, where each step makes incremental progress toward the larger goal while keeping behavioral changes minimal. The patch appears well-contained and follows the established direction of the refactoring effort.

As with previous patches in the series, this change is preparatory work for enabling alternate ODB backends that may not use traditional filesystem-based alternates files. The series as a whole represents careful, methodical refactoring of a core Git subsystem.
