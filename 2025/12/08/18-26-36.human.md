This is a review comment on patch 08/17 in Taylor Blau's MIDX compaction series, focusing on the introduction of `struct write_midx_opts`. Patrick Steinhardt provides two main pieces of feedback on the refactoring.

First, he suggests that mandatory parameters like `struct odb_source` might be better kept as direct function arguments rather than struct members, since they aren't truly optional. This would make the interface more self-documenting.

Second, he points out that the patch uses C99 compound literal syntax (`(struct foo){.member=value}`) in several places, which is currently only allowed in Git's codebase as an experimental "test balloon" until mid-2026. The syntax appears in the patch when calling `write_midx_internal()` from `expire_midx_packs()` and `midx_repack()`.

The review is generally positive, calling the change "a nice improvement" aside from these two points. The feedback is technical and constructive, focusing on codebase conventions and forward compatibility rather than fundamental design issues. This patch is part of the larger MIDX compaction effort that's been carefully reviewed throughout the series.
