This is a follow-up in the critical bug report thread about a submodule race condition introduced by the `ar/submodule-gitdir-tweak` series. Adrian Ratiu responds to Jeff King's discovery of a reproducible failure in parallel checkout of nested submodules during stress testing of `t7450-bad-git-dotfiles.sh`.

The issue stems from commit 099fe37397 ("submodule: always validate gitdirs inside submodule_name_to_gitdir"), which moved gitdir validation checks. Adrian acknowledges the problem after discussing with Patrick Steinhardt and identifies that one validation check needs to remain outside `submodule_name_to_gitdir()` to prevent the race condition. He promises a fix in v6 of the series.

Key technical details:
- The race occurs during parallel checkout of nested submodules
- Test `t7450-bad-git-dotfiles.sh` fails under stress testing
- The validation check relocation in 099fe37397 appears to be the culprit
- The fix will involve keeping some validation outside the centralized function

This is a security-sensitive issue given the potential for malicious gitdir clashing, and represents an urgent regression that needs to be addressed before the submodule changes can proceed. The discussion involves core submodule and parallel checkout maintainers.
