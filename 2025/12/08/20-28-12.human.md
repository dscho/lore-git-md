Performance optimization discussion for promisor pack handling. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Aaron Plattner's concerns about skipping hash verification for non-blob objects in the promisor pack optimization. Peff argues that skipping hashes is acceptable in this context since the primary goal is speed rather than thorough consistency checking, comparing it to hypothetical future optimizations like a secondary index of promised objects.

The email clarifies that there are two distinct use cases for object parsing: thorough verification (like fsck) and fast traversal (like promisor packs). Peff suggests the patch series should proceed with: (1) a separate fix for OBJ_NONE handling (acknowledging it may be hard to demonstrate impact), followed by (2) the SKIP_HASH optimization. The response provides clear technical rationale while leaving the final decision to the patch author, maintaining the constructive tone of the thread.
