This is a revised version of the `git repo info --keys` feature patch, addressing review feedback from Junio Hamano. The patch adds a `--keys` flag to list available keys without their values, building on the `lo/repo-struct-z` work.

The author acknowledges Junio's points about making `--keys` explicitly incompatible with `--all` and key arguments, and considers how to handle the `-z` format flag. Two potential approaches are discussed: either adding a default format that changes behavior based on flags (keyvalue for normal output, table for structured, puts for `--keys`), or simply erroring out when incompatible flags like `--format`, `-z`, or `--all` are used with `--keys`.

The patch appears to be in good shape technically, with clear direction from reviewers and the author thoughtfully considering the feedback. The remaining decisions seem to be about choosing the most maintainable approach for handling flag interactions, with both proposed solutions being reasonable. This refinement should make the feature more robust before final acceptance.
