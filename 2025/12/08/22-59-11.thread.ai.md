Yee Cheng Chin confirmed this is a regression in macOS 15 (not present in 14.8.2), despite Apple's broader iconv implementation switch from GNU to custom in macOS 14. Collin Funk provided historical context about Apple's custom iconv being based on a buggy FreeBSD version.
