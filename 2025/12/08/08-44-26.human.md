Bugfix follow-up addressing a race condition in submodule validation. This is a response to Jeff King's report of a reproducible failure in parallel checkout of nested submodules, which was introduced/exacerbated by commit 099fe37397 in Adrian Ratiu's `ar/submodule-gitdir-tweak` series.

Adrian acknowledges the issue and explains that after discussing with Patrick Steinhardt, they've identified the root cause: one of the validation checks needs to remain outside `submodule_name_to_gitdir()` to prevent the race condition. He promises a fix in v6 of the series.

The issue appears serious as it affects parallel checkout operations and has potential security implications regarding gitdir validation. The fix seems well-understood and targeted, suggesting the next version should resolve the problem cleanly. This is part of ongoing work to improve submodule handling security.
