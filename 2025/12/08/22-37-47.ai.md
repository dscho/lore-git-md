This is a review response in the ongoing refactoring series for Git's object database alternates handling. Justin Tobler comments on Patrick Steinhardt's first patch which introduced `parse_alternates()` to replace the previous entry-by-entry parsing approach.

Justin notes that the new implementation maintains the same parsing behavior while reading all entries into a `strvec` upfront, which he finds cleaner than the previous loop-based approach. He observes that while this doesn't yet resolve the mutual recursion issue between parsing and linking alternates (which will be addressed in later patches), it's a necessary preparatory step. The review confirms the technical soundness of the change while noting that recursive alternate handling during linking remains unchanged at this stage.

Key technical points from the review:
- Validates that quoted paths, comments, and unquoted paths are still processed identically
- Notes the structural shift from incremental parsing to batch processing via `strvec`
- Confirms linking still happens in a separate phase that may trigger additional parsing
- Indicates general approval of the direction while recognizing this is part of a larger series

The review doesn't raise any objections or suggest changes, instead providing positive feedback on the refactoring approach. This suggests the patch is likely to proceed with minimal controversy as part of the broader alternates handling modernization effort.
