This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 8-part series refactoring Git's alternates handling as part of the object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The patch moves the resolution of relative paths in alternates from the linking phase into the parsing phase, making the alternates handling more self-contained.

The key change is relocating the path resolution logic from `link_alt_odb_entry()` (renamed to `odb_add_source()`) into `parse_alternates()`. This better separates concerns between generating the alternates list and linking them into the ODB. The patch maintains the same error handling behavior where unresolvable paths are ignored, as the later `alt_odb_usable()` check will still catch invalid paths.

The changes are focused on odb.c, with 32 lines added and 32 removed. This continues the series' goal of restructuring alternates handling to work through a "source" abstraction rather than direct filesystem operations, which will eventually enable support for non-traditional ODB backends. The patch appears to be a clean, focused step in the overall refactoring effort.
