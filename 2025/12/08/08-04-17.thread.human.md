Patrick Steinhardt has posted an 8-part series refactoring Git's object database alternates handling as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The series systematically replaces direct filesystem operations with a new "source" abstraction layer, preparing for future pluggable backends that may not use traditional "objects/info/alternates" files.

The first seven patches restructured the reading side, introducing `parse_alternates()`, moving path resolution earlier in the pipeline, eliminating recursion, and renaming functions to better reflect their new roles. The final patch, just posted, completes the transition by restructuring alternates writing to use the same source-based approach. It introduces `odb_source_write_alternate()` to handle file operations that were previously done directly in `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`.

While the changes are extensive (touching over 600 lines in odb.c), they remain behavior-preserving - the public interface stays the same, and all existing alternates functionality continues to work as before. The series establishes the foundation for future work where different object database backends could implement their own alternates handling. So far there haven't been any reviews or responses to the series on-list.
