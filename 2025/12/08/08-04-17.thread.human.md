This thread continues Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's alternates handling as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The initial 8-patch series systematically replaced direct filesystem operations with source-based abstractions, with Justin Tobler providing thorough review feedback. 

In the latest exchange, Justin agrees with Patrick's technical approach but suggests refining the naming of `odb_add_source()` to be more specific as `odb_add_alternate_source()`, which Patrick had already indicated he would change. More substantively, Justin raises an architectural concern about how alternates are represented in `struct object_database` - noting that while the current single-list approach works for reads, write operations only use the first source, which could lead to fragility during ODB transactions that reorder the list. He suggests either making alternates more explicitly separate from the primary source in the structure, or handling write-target selection differently.

The discussion shows the two collaborators working through interface design questions that emerge as the ODB abstraction takes shape, with Justin's review catching both naming nuances and deeper architectural implications of the current implementation. This level of careful review and design discussion is characteristic of the project's approach to major refactoring efforts like the ODB work.
