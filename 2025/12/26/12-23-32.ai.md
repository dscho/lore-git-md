This is part 9/11 of the hook subsystem refactoring series, focusing on output capture capabilities for server-side hooks. The patch adds a `consume_output` callback to `struct run_hooks_opt`, allowing callers to intercept and process hook output rather than having it automatically printed to stderr. This is particularly important for server-side operations where output needs to be sent over sideband channels.

The change is minimal but strategic - it exposes the parallel execution infrastructure's existing `consume_output_fn` capability through the hooks API. The implementation simply passes the callback through to `run_parallel_processes()`, maintaining consistency with the established pattern for `feed_pipe` handling. The documentation in `hook.h` explicitly references `run-command.c` and the test helper for implementation examples.
