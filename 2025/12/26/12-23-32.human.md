Hook subsystem refactoring (part 9/11): Output capture capability. This patch from Emily Shaffer (with Adrian Ratiu as the submitter) adds the ability to capture hook output rather than having it print directly to stderr, which is needed for server-side hooks that need to send output over sideband.

The change is minimal but important - it exposes the `consume_output` callback in the hook API by adding it to `struct run_hooks_opt` and plumbing it through to the parallel processes infrastructure. The commit message notes this is specifically for server-side hooks, and the diff shows the straightforward addition of the field and its documentation.

This is part of the larger hook refactoring series that has already addressed all major technical concerns and received sign-offs from key reviewers. The patch appears well-considered and focused, with clear documentation of the new capability. As the thread summary indicates, this series is now in its final form with only minor style tweaks remaining from earlier versions.
