This is part 6 of a major hook subsystem refactoring series, converting the reference-transaction hook to use the new structured hook API. The patch replaces direct `run-command` usage with the standardized `run_hooks_opt` infrastructure, including a new stdin feed callback that processes one ref update at a time.

The conversion follows the pattern established in earlier parts of the series, moving from manual child process management to the new API's callback-based approach. The key change here is the introduction of `transaction_hook_feed_stdin`, which handles the ref update data streaming that was previously done inline. The implementation maintains all existing functionality while being more maintainable and consistent with the rest of the hook system.

Notably, the author mentions the possibility of batching multiple ref updates per callback invocation for better pipe throughput, though the current implementation processes them one at a time with no observed degradation. The patch carries sign-offs from both Emily Shaffer (the co-author of the series) and Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason, indicating it has passed core reviewer scrutiny.
