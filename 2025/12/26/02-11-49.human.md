This is a discussion about potential behavior changes for `git branch --show-current` when combined with the `-v` flag. Junio Hamano responds to Sergey Organov's inquiry about why `-v` is currently ignored, clarifying that `--show-current` was originally designed as a simple alternative to `git symbolic-ref HEAD` rather than as a variant of the list output.

Hamano notes that while there's no fundamental objection to adding `-v` support, the output format might need to differ from `--list -v` since the two-column indentation wouldn't make sense for single-branch output. More importantly, he points out that silently ignoring `-v` is likely a bug - commands should complain about unrecognized options rather than silently accepting them.

The discussion suggests this could be a straightforward enhancement if someone proposes a patch, but raises important questions about output formatting and backward compatibility. The current behavior appears to be an oversight rather than an intentional design choice, making this a potential bugfix target rather than just a feature request.
