Documentation patch fixing AsciiDoc formatting issues in multiple man pages. This is part of a merged series that addresses both markup and wording problems across Git's documentation.

The patch makes several technical corrections: fixing incorrect backtick usage in `git-checkout.adoc` (replacing them with underscores for parameter notation), standardizing list formatting in `git-send-email.adoc` (converting tabs to spaces and improving readability of validation steps), and properly escaping option syntax (`-f/--force`) in `git-worktree.adoc`. It also adds missing `<path>` parameter notation for `--smtp-ssl-cert-path` in the send-email documentation.

These changes follow Git's documentation maintenance practices, with attention to both human readability and machine-processability (important for translation work). The patch has already been reviewed and merged as part of a larger documentation cleanup effort, demonstrating the project's collaborative approach to maintaining high-quality docs. The changes are non-controversial and focused on technical correctness rather than content changes.
