This is a review response in the ongoing hook subsystem refactoring series. Patrick Steinhardt provides feedback on the final patch converting receive-pack's receive hooks to the new hook API.

The review consists of several minor style and implementation nits:
1. Suggests moving a variable declaration inside a loop
2. Notes missing curly braces around a single-line else clause
3. Questions the heap allocation of a state structure that could be stack-allocated

These are all presented as optional suggestions, with Patrick explicitly stating they're nits and leaving it to the author to decide whether to incorporate them in a potential v6. The tone is constructive and acknowledges the overall quality of the series, calling the remaining changes "all good" and hoping this might be the final reroll.

The feedback comes after extensive prior review of this 13-part series, which has already addressed major technical concerns and received sign-offs from key reviewers. This appears to be final polish on an otherwise complete conversion of Git's hook system to a unified API.
