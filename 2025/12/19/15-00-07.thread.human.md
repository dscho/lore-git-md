This thread documents an ongoing effort to clarify the terminology and behavior of `git worktree` commands, particularly around the `prune` subcommand. The discussion began with Sam Bostock's patch series (now at v2) addressing inconsistencies in how the documentation describes worktrees with absent directories, standardizing on "missing" rather than "unused" terminology.

The conversation has evolved into a broader documentation improvement effort, with Junio Hamano and Eric Sunshine collaborating on precise wording. They're now distinguishing three scenarios for worktree removal: `remove` for intentional deletion, `prune` for missing worktrees, and `repair` for moved worktrees. Junio initially included some phrasing that Eric pointed out was redundant or misleading, leading to refined text that clearly specifies `prune` only applies to worktrees with missing working trees.

The current state reflects consensus on several points: standardized terminology, removal of circular explanations, and clear distinctions between the three removal scenarios. The remaining open questions focus on final documentation phrasing and whether to reorganize the patch series per Junio's feedback. With Eric indicating Junio's latest proposal "sounds fine," the thread appears to be nearing resolution, though the patches may undergo minor refinements before being picked up for merging.
