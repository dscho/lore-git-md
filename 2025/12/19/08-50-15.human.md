Windows symlink support follow-up: finalizing PATH_MAX removal

This email from Johannes Schindelin concludes the technical discussion around removing PATH_MAX limitations in `strbuf_readlink()`, the last piece of the Windows symlink preparation series. Responding to Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about unbounded growth, Schindelin proposes a practical upper bound of 32,768 characters for symlink targets on Windows, citing Microsoft's documentation while acknowledging some edge cases could technically exceed this.

The proposal strikes a balance between removing artificial PATH_MAX constraints (the original goal) and preventing potential memory issues from maliciously long symlinks. This follows earlier sign-offs from Karsten Blees and Junio Hamano confirming the safety of the unbounded approach, now refined with Windows-specific considerations. The discussion appears settled, with all technical concerns addressed and the change ready for inclusion.
