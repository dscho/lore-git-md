Feature discussion regarding diff indicator suppression. Phillip Wood responds to Harald Nordgren's proposed `--no-indicators` option for `git diff`, which would replace the '+', '-', and ' ' margin indicators with spaces while preserving colors. Phillip raises two key points: (1) the patch still leaves leading spaces that may be undesirable for copy-pasting, and (2) suggests the existing `--output-indicator-*` options could achieve similar results through aliasing, though they can't remove indicators entirely since they error on empty strings. He also questions whether the "no newline" marker should be stripped as well. The response keeps the discussion going about whether this functionality merits a new option versus being handled through existing tools, building on Collin Funk's earlier skepticism about the feature's necessity. No new code is proposed - the exchange remains focused on design tradeoffs.
