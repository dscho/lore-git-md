Feature proposal follow-up in a skeptical thread. Harald Nordgren responds to criticism of their proposed `--no-indicators` option for `git diff` by providing a concrete workflow example where they want to extract old code snippets from diffs without the +/- markers. They demonstrate copying React component code from a diff where the markers interfere with pasting into their editor.

The response doubles down on the personal workflow justification, arguing that `git restore -p` (suggested by multiple thread participants as an alternative) is too cumbersome when they just want to grab a few lines. They also express interest in extending the feature to `git show`.

This comes after several experienced contributors questioned the feature's necessity, suggesting existing tools (terminal column selection, `sed`, `git restore`) already cover the use case. The maintainer expressed particular skepticism about whether this solves a widespread problem. Harald's response doesn't address the technical concerns about space stripping or newline markers raised earlier, focusing instead on reinforcing their personal motivation.

The exchange highlights a tension between a niche workflow optimization and Git's design philosophy of providing orthogonal tools that compose well. With multiple alternatives demonstrated and no new technical arguments presented, the proposal appears unlikely to progress without stronger justification of why existing methods fall short.
