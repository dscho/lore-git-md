This is a discussion about a proposed `--no-indicators` option for `git diff` that would replace the +/- indicators with spaces while preserving colors, aimed at making copy-pasting cleaner.

Ben Knoble responds to Harald Nordgren's justification for the feature, questioning why one would copy from diffs at all when `git restore` exists for retrieving old code. The thread has seen skepticism from multiple contributors, including the maintainer, about whether this feature provides enough unique benefit over existing alternatives like terminal emulator selection tools, `sed` post-processing, or Git's own `--output-indicator-*` options.

The discussion remains at the design level, with core questions about the feature's necessity and edge case handling still unresolved. Ben's comment introduces a new perspective by suggesting the underlying workflow (copying from diffs) might not be the most Git-idiomatic approach to the problem. The maintainer's earlier skepticism about terminal emulator capabilities and cross-tool comparisons remains an important factor in the discussion.
