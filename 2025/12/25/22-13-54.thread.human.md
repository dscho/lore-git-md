This thread investigates why `git pack-refs --all` runs slowly (5+ minutes) on large Gerrit servers with ~3M refs, while JGit completes in 20s on the same NFS-mounted repositories. Martin Fick's systematic testing has confirmed the bottleneck lies in mmap'd packfile access rather than ref handling itself - pre-reading the pack file reduces runtime to match JGit's performance.

Jeff King initially suspected the mmap/NFS interaction, and Patrick Steinhardt has now acknowledged this as the definitive issue while emphasizing NFS remains non-negotiable for Gerrit's mirroring requirements. The discussion has shifted from general optimization to specifically addressing mmap performance on NFS, with potential solutions including runtime configuration of NO_MMAP behavior. Patrick continues advocating for profiling with `perf(1)` to gather more data, though recognizes production constraints may limit this approach.

While the files backend could see some lock contention improvements, Patrick clearly favors focusing on the reftable backend as the long-term architectural solution rather than optimizing the current problematic mmap/NFS interaction. The thread remains technically focused, with participants aligned on the root cause but differing on whether to pursue immediate workarounds or invest in the reftable transition.
