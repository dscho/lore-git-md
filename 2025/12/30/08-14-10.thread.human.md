The fsmonitor daemon series has reached completion, bringing Linux support for filesystem monitoring to parity with existing Windows and macOS implementations. The final patches focus on process management reliability, with the latest addition introducing a `close_fd_above_stderr` flag to prevent file descriptor leaks in long-running child processes. This mechanism closes all descriptors above stderr (fd 2) after forking but before exec, capping the scan at 4096 file descriptors for efficiency.

Junio Hamano has merged the series into his 'seen' branch after extensive review and testing. While he suggested a more generic callback mechanism might be preferable in the future, he found the current solution sound. The implementation has been validated in production environments by Paul Tarjan, who confirmed its stability even with challenging filesystem configurations like overlayfs. Patrick Steinhardt's reliability concerns were addressed through careful handling of edge cases, completing the series' goal of robust cross-platform fsmonitor support. With all technical issues resolved and no remaining open questions, this marks the successful conclusion of the Linux fsmonitor integration effort.
