The fsmonitor series is back on track. After Junio C Hamano temporarily reverted the Linux fsmonitor patches from `next` to `seen` because a Gentoo build failed, he asked the thread for a verdict: was the failure a problem with the series, or just a downstream packaging issue? D. Ben Knoble, who originally flagged the failure, has now confirmed it was nothing more than a clash with Gentoo's local patches. He explicitly said he would not hold the series back for Gentoo's sake, and that Junio should feel free to promote it back to `next`. With that green light, the only remaining obstacle is gone. The Linux fsmonitor implementation -- using inotify, process isolation with `setsid()` and `close_fd_above_stderr`, daemon shutdown timeouts, and the associated IPC refactoring -- is expected to return to `next` and continue toward `master`. No other issues have been raised.
