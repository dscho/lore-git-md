This is the final patch in a documentation series improving the `git reset` man page, specifically focusing on clarifying the pathspec behavior. Junio Hamano provides a technical review of the wording around `git reset -p` (interactive mode), pointing out that neither "unstaged" nor "added to the index" accurately describes how the command modifies the index.

The discussion reveals subtle behavior differences depending on whether the operation targets HEAD or another commit: with HEAD, hunks are reverted from the index (as if unstaging), while with other commits, hunks are applied to move toward that commit's state. Hamano suggests more neutral wording ("modifies the index using the chosen hunks") to avoid implying directionality, since the actual prompt text already indicates the operation's effect to users.
