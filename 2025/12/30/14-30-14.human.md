This is a follow-up in the `git replay` error handling thread, where Kristoffer Haugsbakk acknowledges Phillip Wood's observation about error message consistency between `--advance` and `--onto` options. 

The discussion has revealed that Kristoffer's initial patch would have made `--advance` error messages worse by removing its specific "must be a reference" check. Phillip noted there are only two callers to the `peel_committish()` function, making it feasible to handle errors at the call sites rather than in the helper function. 

Kristoffer confirms they've incorporated Junio Hamano's earlier suggestion to move error handling to the callers in version 2 of the patch, which should maintain the existing quality of `--advance` error messages while improving `--onto` messages. The exchange shows good responsiveness to code review feedback, with the author quickly recognizing and addressing the potential regression in error message quality.
