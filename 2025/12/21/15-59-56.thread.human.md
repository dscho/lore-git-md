While the response provides more concrete use cases, Junio's fundamental concerns about architectural implications - particularly around push behavior - remain unaddressed. The discussion now centers on whether these real-world constraints justify adding the proposed complexity to Git's core behavior. The feature remains at version 1 of the patch series while this design debate continues.
