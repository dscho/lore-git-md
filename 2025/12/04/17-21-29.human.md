Performance optimization for promisor object handling. Aaron Plattner identifies that `add_promisor_object()` unnecessarily decompresses and hashes blob objects when initializing the promisor objects set, even though blobs don't reference other objects. This causes significant slowdowns in repositories with large pack files - the example shows a 176GB promisor pack where the operation drops from 76 minutes to under 2 minutes after the patch.

The change uses `odb_read_object_info()` to check object types first, skipping full parsing for blobs. The patch includes clear before/after benchmarks showing the dramatic improvement. This appears to be a well-motivated optimization targeting a specific performance bottleneck in promisor pack handling, with straightforward implementation and measurable benefits.
