This is part 6 of an 11-part series refactoring Git's hook subsystem to use a new structured API. The patch converts the reference-transaction hook to use the new `hook.h` infrastructure instead of direct `run-command` calls.

The change eliminates manual child process setup, hook path finding, and signal handling by leveraging the new API's standardized patterns. Each reference update is processed individually through a callback that formats the ref update data into stdin for the hook. The implementation maintains all existing functionality including handling of null OIDs, symbolic refs, and log-only updates.

Performance appears unaffected by the per-update callback approach, though the commit notes batching could be added later if needed. The patch follows the series' established patterns for memory safety with proper strbuf management and includes sign-offs from key reviewers who worked on earlier parts.

This continues the series' systematic conversion of major hook types while maintaining backward compatibility. With multiple sign-offs and no outstanding technical concerns, the change appears ready for inclusion as part of the broader hook modernization effort.
