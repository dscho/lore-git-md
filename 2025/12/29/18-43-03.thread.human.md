The core problem stemmed from the function's original assumption that both sides would always be directories. The fix required careful handling of dirmask/filemask bits during type mismatches and proper marking of merge cleanliness. The test case demonstrates the failure through a carefully constructed scenario with multiple merge bases and enough files to trigger the optimization path where the bug occurs.
