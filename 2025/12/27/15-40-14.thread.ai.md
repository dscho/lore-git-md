Bugfix for receive-pack crash when handling symbolic refs pointing beyond their namespace. Patch modifies `check_aliased_update_internal()` in `builtin/receive-pack.c` to avoid NULL dereference by working with fully-qualified ref names (including namespaces) when checking symref updates, rather than stripping namespaces prematurely. Adds test case in `t/t5509-fetch-push-namespaces.sh` verifying push to symref with out-of-namespace target succeeds without crashing. Fix builds on prior work from commits ded8393610 (namespace handling) and da3efdb17b (symref update checks). Changes are focused on the crash scenario while preserving existing consistency checks for in-namespace symref updates.
