This thread addresses a straightforward bugfix for a duplicate free operation in the repository path cache cleanup code. The patch (now at v2) removes a redundant `FREE_AND_NULL(cache->squash_msg)` call in `repo_clear_path_cache()`, which was introduced in 2022 and likely resulted from a copy/paste error. While not causing a true double-free vulnerability (since `FREE_AND_NULL` nulls the pointer), it's still a correctness issue worth fixing.

Eric Sunshine provided helpful historical context, confirming the duplicate line has existed since the function's initial implementation and that all other freeable members of `repo_path_cache` are properly handled. The patch remains small and focused - just a one-line deletion with no behavior change - and has garnered consensus as a clear improvement. With no outstanding technical concerns raised, this appears ready for inclusion in the next release cycle.
