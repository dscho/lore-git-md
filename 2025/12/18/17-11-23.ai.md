This is part 9 of an 11-part series refactoring Git's hook subsystem to use a new standardized API. The patch adds output capture capability to hooks, allowing server-side hooks to collect output for sideband transmission rather than printing directly to stderr.

The change exposes a `consume_output_fn` callback in the `run_hooks_opt` struct that gets passed through to the underlying `run_parallel_processes()` machinery. This matches the series' pattern of providing structured callbacks for hook customization while maintaining backward compatibility. The implementation is minimal - just plumbing the existing `consume_output` field from `run_hooks_opt` through to the parallel processes infrastructure.

Key technical points:
- Adds `consume_output` field to `struct run_hooks_opt` with documentation
- Passes the callback through to `run_parallel_processes()` in hook.c
- Maintains the series' pattern of optional callbacks for customization
- Enables output capture needed for receive-pack sideband support in later patches

The patch has sign-offs from both Emily Shaffer and Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason, indicating alignment with the series direction. It builds on the parallel execution infrastructure introduced earlier while following the established callback-based design pattern. The change is focused and well-contained, with clear documentation of the new capability.
