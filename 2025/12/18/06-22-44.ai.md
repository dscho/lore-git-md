This is a follow-up in the performance regression thread regarding object info handling in promisor packs. Patrick Steinhardt confirms Junio Hamano's diagnosis of the root cause - the problematic use of a static `blank_oi` struct for NULL object_info cases that leads to incorrect comparisons in `packfile_store_read_object_info()`. 

Steinhardt reveals he had independently identified the same issue and was preparing a fix, but delayed sending it due to conflicts with another in-progress patch series. He now plans to submit his solution, which aligns with Junio's approach of eliminating the `blank_oi` pattern entirely in favor of explicit NULL handling. 

The email indicates this is part of Steinhardt's broader local patch series addressing the regression, suggesting the fix may include additional related improvements beyond just the immediate NULL handling issue. The interaction demonstrates the project's collaborative debugging process, with both the original author (Steinhardt) and maintainer (Hamano) converging on the same technical solution through independent analysis.
