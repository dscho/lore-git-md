This thread addresses test suite maintenance for compatibility with curl 8.18.0, focusing on HTTP header handling changes. The original series fixed three test failures: cookie path comparisons, CRLF enforcement in headers, and whitespace assumptions in WWW-Authenticate header unfolding. The first two patches (cookie paths and CRLFs) are uncontroversial and remain necessary, while the third patch's approach to whitespace handling has sparked deeper discussion.

Curl maintainer Daniel Stenberg initially proposed a fix to restore curl's previous whitespace behavior, but Jeff King noted this only partially resolved the issue—it preserved tabs during unfolding, which may not comply with HTTP standards. King suggested a more comprehensive solution that would always use spaces during unfolding, regardless of the original whitespace character. The discussion reveals a tension between backward compatibility, standards compliance, and the brittleness of Git's test assumptions. While the test suite updates are still valid for the transition period, the collaboration between Git and curl maintainers may lead to longer-term improvements in both projects' handling of HTTP standards. The thread remains open pending verification of curl's final behavior.
