This thread addresses a severe performance regression in Git's object handling code, where `git commit` times ballooned from 0.38s to 1508s due to incorrect assumptions about static "blank" object_info structs. The regression was introduced by the recently merged ps/object-read-stream topic and was reported by Aaron Plattner.

Junio Hamano quickly proposed and implemented a fix that removes the problematic static blank_oi variables and adds explicit NULL checks, restoring performance to normal levels. Aaron verified the fix and provided positive Reviewed-by and Tested-by tags, confirming it resolves the issue.

Patrick Steinhardt has now weighed in with an alternative approach. While acknowledging Junio's solution works, he mentions having a more comprehensive patch series in progress that not only fixes the immediate regression but also addresses related inconsistencies in object info handling and includes additional performance optimizations. Patrick offers to either rebase his series on top of Junio's fix or replace it entirely, deferring to Junio's preference on how to proceed.

The discussion remains technical and collaborative, with both solutions appearing sound. The choice now is between accepting Junio's minimal fix that solves the immediate problem, or incorporating Patrick's broader series which would provide more thorough improvements but require additional review.
