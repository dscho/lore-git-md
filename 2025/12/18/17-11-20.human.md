This is part 6 of a 11-part series refactoring Git's hook subsystem to use the new `hook.h` API. The patch converts the reference-transaction hook to the new API, eliminating manual `child_process` setup and signal handling.

The change replaces the direct `run-command` implementation with a callback-based approach using the series' new `feed_pipe` mechanism. Each reference update is processed individually in the `transaction_hook_feed_stdin` callback, streaming the ref update data through a pipe. The implementation maintains all existing behavior including handling of null OIDs and symbolic refs, while being more maintainable through the standardized API.

The patch has received positive review from Emily Shaffer and Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason, and follows the established patterns from earlier parts of the series. Performance impact appears minimal, though the author notes the design could be extended to batch updates if needed. This continues the series' steady progress in modernizing Git's internals while preserving backward compatibility.
