This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB. The patch moves the multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`, completing the architectural shift where all packfile-related data is managed by the packfile store.

The changes are straightforward mechanical updates to move MIDX handling into the packfile store, with corresponding updates to all MIDX-related functions. The patch touches midx.c, odb.c, packfile.c and their headers, updating about 30 lines across 5 files. Key changes include removing MIDX from `struct odb_source`, adding it to `struct packfile_store`, and updating all MIDX accessors to use the new location.

This concludes the series' goal of making packfile operations explicitly source-aware. With this patch, the packfile store now fully owns all pack-related data structures (regular packs, kept packs, and MIDX), completing the transition to source-level packfile management. The changes maintain identical behavior while providing better isolation between ODBs, which will enable future work on pluggable ODB backends.
