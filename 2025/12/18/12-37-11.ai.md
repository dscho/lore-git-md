This is a response from curl maintainer Daniel Stenberg regarding the test suite maintenance series for curl 8.18.0 compatibility. The response indicates the test fixes will be compatible with both old and new curl versions, with Daniel taking responsibility for adjusting curl's implementation to maintain the expected whitespace handling that Git's tests rely on. This resolves the only outstanding concern from the series while validating the overall approach of modifying test expectations rather than core functionality.
