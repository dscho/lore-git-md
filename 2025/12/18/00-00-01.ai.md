This is the final acknowledgment in the major Windows symlink support series from Junio Hamano, the Git maintainer. The email confirms the series (now at v5 with 18 patches) is ready for integration after thorough review. Junio notes he has examined the non-Windows-specific portions of the changes and found them sound, praising the implementation quality. The diffstat shows the extensive work spans 19 files, with the bulk in `compat/mingw.c` (667 lines changed) and includes test updates across multiple test scripts. This marks the culmination of a decade-long effort to bring comprehensive symlink support to Windows, addressing everything from basic symlink operations to edge cases like index corruption. With the maintainer's approval, this series appears poised to land in a future Git release, representing a significant step forward in Windows-POSIX compatibility.
