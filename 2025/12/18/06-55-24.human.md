This is part 5/10 of Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB. The patch moves the packfile store from `struct object_database` to `struct odb_source`, completing the logical separation where each source maintains its own packfiles.

The change is mechanical but wide-ranging, touching 11 files with 243 insertions and 145 deletions. Key modifications include:
- Moving packfile store initialization from ODB to source creation
- Updating packfile operations to iterate through all sources' pack stores
- Adding source-level packfile store access in odb.h
- Adjusting callers in fast-import, grep, index-pack, and other commands

The patch notes that most callers don't need changes since they use high-level iteration helpers like `repo_for_each_pack()`. The remaining direct packfile interactions are updated to handle multiple sources, with transitional cases marked for future follow-up. This continues the series' incremental approach to moving packfile operations to the source level while maintaining compatibility.

The changes are well-contained within the packfile/ODB abstraction layer and follow the established pattern from earlier patches in the series. The diff shows careful attention to maintaining existing behavior while moving functionality, with clear comments marking transitional states. This foundational work positions the codebase for the eventual abstraction of packfile storage behind pluggable ODB interfaces.
