This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to move packfile handling from object database (ODB) to ODB source level. The series is part of the larger effort to enable pluggable ODB backends.

Patch 6/10 simplifies `packfile_store_get_packs()` to only prepare the MIDX (multi-pack index) for the specific packfile store being operated on, rather than preparing all stores in the ODB. This change is possible now that packfile stores are tracked per source rather than per ODB. Patrick notes this shouldn't affect behavior since callers either operate on a single store or already iterate through sources.

Justin Tobler, who has been reviewing the series, gives this patch a straightforward approval with no requested changes. The change appears to be a mechanical cleanup following the architectural shift in earlier patches, removing now-unnecessary preparation of unrelated stores. This continues the series' pattern of updating packfile handling to work with the new source-level storage model while maintaining existing functionality.
