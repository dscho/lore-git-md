Platform compatibility follow-up regarding Windows symlink series authorship.

Johannes Sixt (Hannes) provides a final review note on the now-complete Windows symlink support series (v5, 18 patches). While confirming the technical soundness of the implementation, he raises an administrative concern: primary author Karsten Blees' emails are bouncing, and Hannes suggests potentially redirecting authorship attribution to an alternative email address listed in `.mailmap`. 

This comes after Hannes' earlier detailed review of patch 13's `readlink()` implementation, where he identified a minor documentation discrepancy (now resolved). His current message indicates general approval of the series while acknowledging his limited expertise with Windows symlink APIs. The note about email bounces appears to be the last loose end before final integration, as Junio Hamano has already approved the series for merging after its decade-long development history.

The technical work itself remains unchanged from the comprehensive symlink support described in previous summaries, covering creation, reading, management, and index tracking of both file and directory symlinks across Windows versions. The series touches `compat/mingw*` files and `read-cache.c`, with particular attention to POSIX compatibility, UTF-16/UTF-8 conversion, and edge case handling around MAX_PATH limitations and non-elevated symlink creation.
