This is a documentation refinement suggestion in the ongoing discussion about commit signing configuration. Collin Funk responds to Junio Hamano's proposal to resurrect a 2020 documentation patch that explains why Git won't support automatic Signed-off-by (SOB) trailers via configuration. The original patch text warns that config-based SOB additions could weaken the legal standing of the Developer Certificate of Origin in court by enabling "inertia" sign-offs.

Collin's contribution focuses on improving clarity in the proposed documentation text, suggesting spelling out "Signed-off-by" instead of using the abbreviation "SoB" for consistency with Git's existing documentation style. This is a minor but thoughtful edit to make the legal warning more accessible to readers unfamiliar with the abbreviation.

The email reinforces the thread's established position against config-based SOB while demonstrating careful attention to documentation quality. It shows the discussion moving from debating the feature's merits to refining how best to communicate the rationale to future contributors. The legal concerns about automatic sign-offs remain central, with Collin's suggestion aimed at making that warning more effective through clearer wording.
