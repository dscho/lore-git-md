Thread summary:

Feature proposal to extend commit signing configuration by generalizing the `commit.signOff` option beyond `git format-patch` to cover `commit`, `merge`, `cherry-pick`, and `revert` commands, motivated by lazygit's needs. The discussion has uncovered significant historical and legal context about Signed-off-by (SOB) trailer semantics, with Junio C Hamano and Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón providing key insights about the Developer Certificate of Origin's legal standing.

The core tension remains between user convenience (particularly for tools like lazygit) and preserving the intentionality behind SOB certification. Git's maintainers view explicit `--signoff` flags as necessary to maintain the trailer's legal significance in open source contribution workflows. Collin Funk has now contributed a documentation refinement suggestion, proposing to spell out "Signed-off-by" instead of using "SoB" in the warning text for consistency with Git's documentation style. This improves clarity while maintaining the strong position against config-based SOB additions.

The historical context establishes that automatic sign-offs via configuration were rejected in 2020 due to concerns about weakening the legal standing of contributions. The discussion continues to explore whether alternative approaches could satisfy tooling needs without violating these principles, with the documentation refinement reflecting careful attention to communicating the rationale effectively. The legal concerns about "inertia" sign-offs remain central, with the position against config-based SOB now being more clearly articulated in the proposed documentation wording.
