This is a discussion reply in the ongoing thread about generalizing commit signing configuration. Carlo Arenas Belón references a previous discussion where Junio Hamano noted that Signed-off-by (SOB) trailers are primarily meaningful in open source contexts and should be explicitly added rather than automated. 

The reply doesn't introduce new technical proposals but reinforces an important cultural consideration about SOB trailers - they carry legal significance in open source that may not translate to closed-source environments. This perspective adds weight to the earlier concerns about automatically applying SOBs across all Git operations.

The exchange highlights the tension between UX consistency (having uniform config support across commands) and the semantic meaning of SOB trailers. While the lazygit use case shows real demand for this functionality, the thread is still in early stages of weighing the implications before any implementation would be considered.
