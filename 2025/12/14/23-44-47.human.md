Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about adding a `signOff` config option, referencing a 2020 patch that explicitly rejected automatic SOB (Signed-off-by) trailers on legal grounds. The key point is that SOB trailers carry legal significance in open source contexts, and automatically adding them could weaken their evidentiary value in disputes. The email suggests reviving this old patch's documentation to prevent contributors from wasting time on implementations that would ultimately be rejected. This reinforces the position that SOB trailers should remain explicitly given rather than config-driven, though the thread remains open to further discussion about how this applies across different Git commands. The exchange highlights the tension between user convenience and the legal/cultural weight of contributor certifications.
