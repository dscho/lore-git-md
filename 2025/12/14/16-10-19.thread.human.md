This thread documents Git's policy on automatic sign-offs (Signed-off-by trailers) and the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO), originating from a feature request to expand sign-off automation beyond `git format-patch`. The discussion established clear maintainer consensus against such expansion due to legal intentionality requirements, with Junio C Hamano and Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón providing key historical and legal context.

The final implementation leaves `format.signoff` as the sole exception for automatic sign-offs, with no expansion to other commands like `commit`, `merge`, or `revert`. Documentation now includes identical legal rationale in both the FAQ and signoff-option.adoc, clearly explaining why configuration variables like `commit.signoff` will never be added. The policy is that only manual `-s/--signoff` or explicit `format.signoff` configuration can add SOB trailers to maintain DCO legal standing.

After extensive review from multiple contributors including Elijah Newren and brian m. carlson, the thread concludes with a minor editorial refinement suggested by Johannes Sixt - changing "pile on mistakes" to "add more mistakes" in the FAQ text for better clarity. Junio accepted this final wording tweak, marking the completion of this policy documentation effort. All technical and legal aspects are now settled with unanimous approval, and the authoritative documentation clearly states Git's position that sign-offs must remain manually triggered except for the grandfathered `format-patch` case.
