This thread established Git's firm policy on automatic sign-offs, concluding that they will not be expanded beyond the existing `format.signoff` exception for `git format-patch`. The discussion centered on legal implications, with Junio C Hamano and others emphasizing that the Developer Certificate of Origin requires intentional sign-offs to maintain their legal standing.

The final documentation patch, approved by all key participants including Elijah Newren, adds two complementary explanations: a detailed FAQ entry outlining the legal rationale, and a cross-reference in the signoff-option documentation. These make it clear that automatic sign-offs in other commands (like commit/merge/revert) would undermine the DCO's purpose, and that `format.signoff` remains a historical exception not to be replicated.

With this documentation now in place, the thread is effectively closed, providing clear guidance to future contributors about Git's sign-off policy. The consensus reflects the project's careful approach to legal matters, ensuring sign-offs retain their intended meaning as deliberate attestations rather than automated additions.
