This thread established Git's firm policy against expanding automatic sign-off (Signed-off-by) functionality beyond the existing `git format-patch` exception. The discussion, involving key maintainers and contributors, centered on legal implications of the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) and the necessity of intentional sign-offs.

Junio C Hamano and Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón provided crucial historical and legal context, explaining why sign-offs must remain manually triggered in most cases to maintain the DCO's legal standing. The final implementation documents this policy in both the FAQ and configuration documentation, with clear cross-references between them. Johannes Sixt and Elijah Newren reviewed the documentation wording, with Elijah providing final verification that all requested edits were properly incorporated.

The thread demonstrates Git's meticulous approach to policy documentation - even minor phrasing ambiguities were clarified before final acceptance. With unanimous approval from participants including Junio, Elijah, brian m. carlson, and Johannes Sixt, the matter is now clearly documented and closed to further discussion. The only remaining automatic sign-off mechanism is the grandfathered `format.signoff` option for `git format-patch`, with all other cases requiring explicit user action.
