This thread began with a feature request to expand automatic sign-off support beyond `git format-patch` to commands like `commit` and `merge`. However, it quickly revealed deeper considerations about the legal standing of the Signed-off-by (SOB) trailer in the Developer Certificate of Origin. Maintainers Junio C Hamano and Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón provided crucial context, explaining how automatic sign-offs could weaken the legal validity of contributions by enabling "inertia" sign-offs without explicit intent.

The discussion transitioned from a technical proposal to a documentation effort, with Junio drafting authoritative text (co-authored with brian m. carlson) that codifies Git's position. The documentation makes clear that while `format.signoff` exists as a historical exception, no further automatic sign-off configurations will be added, as explicit `--signoff` flags are required to maintain legal intentionality. Brian m. carlson confirmed approval of this stance, representing maintainer consensus.

Elijah Newren then proposed editorial refinements to improve the documentation's clarity and flow while preserving the legal rationale. With Junio's latest email confirming final agreement on the text, the policy discussion is now closed. The thread demonstrates Git's careful balance between user convenience and legal requirements, with the outcome being clear documentation that maintains the integrity of contribution certification while explaining the project's reasoning to users.
