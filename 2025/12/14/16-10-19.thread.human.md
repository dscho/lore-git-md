This thread established Git's policy on automatic sign-offs, concluding that only `git format-patch` can automatically add Signed-off-by trailers (via the `format.signoff` config) due to legal requirements around the Developer Certificate of Origin's intentionality clause. All other commands must require explicit user action to add sign-offs. The discussion involved key input from Junio Hamano and Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón on the legal rationale, with Johannes Sixt, Elijah Newren, and brian m. carlson contributing to the final documentation wording.

The policy is now fully documented in both the FAQ and signoff-option manpage, with cross-references between them. The only remaining activity is minor editorial polish - Kristoffer Haugsbakk recently submitted a patch to properly format a reference to the gitfaq manpage, maintaining consistency with Git's documentation standards. This change doesn't affect the settled policy or its carefully reviewed legal/technical content, just improves the presentation.
