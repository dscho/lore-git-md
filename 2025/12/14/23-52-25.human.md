This is a documentation refinement suggestion in the ongoing discussion about commit signing configuration. Collin Funk responds to Junio Hamano's proposal to resurrect a 2020 patch that explicitly documents why Git won't support automatic Signed-off-by (SOB) trailers via configuration. The original patch text warns that config-based SOB could weaken the legal standing of the Developer Certificate of Origin by enabling "inertia" sign-offs.

Collin's contribution is narrowly focused on terminology consistency, suggesting the documentation should spell out "Signed-off-by" rather than using the abbreviation "SoB" since that form doesn't appear elsewhere in Git's docs. This comes amidst broader agreement that the feature itself (commit.signOff config) shouldn't be implemented due to the legal implications previously established in the thread.

The email doesn't challenge the core position against config-based signing, instead offering a small but thoughtful improvement to how the rationale is presented. It shows careful attention to documentation norms while staying aligned with the thread's consensus about maintaining the intentionality behind Signed-off-by trailers.
