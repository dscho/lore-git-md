Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB is now complete. The series enables pluggable ODBs by ensuring all object access routes through owning sources (main repo/alternates), with packfiles and their metadata (including MIDX handling) now properly scoped to individual sources via packfile stores.

The series saw positive review from Justin Tobler on the early patches and Toon Claes on the kept-pack cache refactoring, with particular appreciation for the improved documentation and clearer function naming. The changes are mechanical but important for the architectural goal of making packfile operations properly track their owning source. All 10 patches are now complete, establishing a model where each ODB source manages its own packfiles independently through packfile stores. This work paves the way for future pluggable ODB backends.
