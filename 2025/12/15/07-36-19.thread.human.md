Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series refactoring Git's packfile storage for pluggable ODB backends has reached its third iteration (v3), with the architectural shift now complete. The 10-patch series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, with the final patch updating MIDX handling to reference MIDX via packfile stores. Key changes include packfile stores being initialized per-source rather than per-ODB, simplification of `prepare_pack()`, and updates to various packfile operations to explicitly prepare stores.

The series has received thorough review from multiple contributors. Justin Tobler reviewed the early patches, while Toon Claes provided detailed feedback on several middle patches, initially misunderstanding one simplification before recognizing its correctness. Kristoffer Haugsbakk caught a typo that Patrick promptly fixed. Junio Hamano has indicated the series looks ready for inclusion in 'next' pending any last-minute feedback.

In the latest development, Karthik Nayak reviewed patch 4/10, which cleans up the `unuse_one_window()` function. Karthik confirmed Patrick's analysis that the function's previous behavior was misleading due to an unused NULL check, and agreed the new version taking the object database directly is a clear improvement. This feedback further validates the series' careful refactoring approach that preserves behavior while cleaning up internal structure. With all open questions resolved and positive reviews from multiple domain experts, the series appears poised for merging.
