Patrick Steinhardt has completed a major refactoring of Git's packfile storage system with his 10-patch series, now in its third version. The work reorganizes how Git tracks packfiles to be per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB, a key architectural change that enables future pluggable backends. The series affects over 30 files, updating core functions like `find_pack_entry()` to be source-aware while maintaining identical behavior.

The changes have been thoroughly reviewed by Justin Tobler and Toon Claes, with all feedback addressed in this version - including a typo fix in patch 8's commit message spotted by Kristoffer Haugsbakk. The series builds on two recently merged topics and represents the last major step before introducing pluggable ODB backends, though Patrick notes the abstractions will need further refinement. The wide-ranging changes touch packfile handling across several commands but are largely mechanical, with careful attention to maintaining existing behavior. With all open questions resolved and reviews completed, the series is now ready for inclusion in the next integration cycle.
