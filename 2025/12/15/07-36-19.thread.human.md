Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage for pluggable ODB backends has reached its final form. The series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, with the last patch completing the architectural shift by updating MIDX (multi-pack index) handling to reference packfile stores directly. The changes touch core packfile operations while carefully maintaining identical behavior, with 40+ files modified across the series.

The technical work involved restructuring how packfiles are initialized and accessed, simplifying key functions like `prepare_pack()`, and making MIDX tracking source-aware. Reviewers Justin Tobler and Toon Claes provided detailed feedback on earlier versions, with Patrick addressing all concerns - including fixing a typo spotted by Kristoffer Haugsbakk. Junio Hamano has indicated the series appears ready for inclusion in 'next', noting only minor fixes were needed between v2 and v3. This marks an important step toward pluggable ODB backends, though as Patrick notes, some abstractions remain "leaky" and will need further refinement. The series builds on two other merged topics and represents significant progress in Git's ongoing object storage modernization efforts.
