Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database backends has reached consensus and appears ready for merging. The series moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, with the final patch updating MIDX handling to reference MIDX via packfile stores. 

The review process has been thorough, with Justin Tobler (who reviewed patches 1-3), Karthik Nayak, and Toon Claes providing feedback that Patrick incorporated in v3. Karthik confirmed the correctness of the `unuse_one_window()` simplification, while Toon initially questioned but ultimately approved patch 7's changes. Kristoffer Haugsbakk caught a minor typo that was fixed. 

Junio Hamano has indicated the series looks ready for inclusion in 'next', and Justin's latest review confirms no further changes are needed. This marks an important milestone in the ongoing ODB abstraction work, cleaning up internal structure while maintaining behavior as the project moves toward pluggable backends. The series builds on two recently merged topics and appears poised to graduate to the main branch after the usual 'next' baking period.
