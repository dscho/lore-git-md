Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to support pluggable object database backends has reached completion with the final patch. The series moves packfile ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, a key architectural change needed for future pluggable backends.

The final patch consolidates multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking into the packfile store, removing the last remaining packfile-related data from the ODB source structure. This completes the transition where all packfile operations now work through the packfile store abstraction. The changes touch MIDX handling across several core files but maintain identical behavior while cleaning up the internal structure.

Throughout the series, Patrick has worked closely with reviewers including Justin Tobler and Toon Claes, addressing all feedback across multiple iterations. The v3 version incorporated typo fixes from Kristoffer Haugsbakk and resolved initial misunderstandings about some of the simplifications. With all open questions addressed and the architectural goals achieved, this series is now ready for inclusion in the main branch. The changes pave the way for future work on pluggable ODB backends while keeping current functionality intact.
