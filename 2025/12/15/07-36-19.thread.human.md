Patrick Steinhardt continues his series refactoring Git's packfile storage as part of the larger ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The goal is to move packfile handling from being managed at the object database level to being owned by individual sources (main repository or alternates), which is a key step toward enabling pluggable ODB backends.

The series now includes five patches, with the latest one relocating the packfile store from `struct object_database` to `struct odb_source`. This better matches the architectural vision where each source manages its own packfiles independently. The change updates various operations in fast-import, grep, index-pack and other areas to work with per-source stores, while maintaining the existing behavior through helper functions like `repo_for_each_pack_data`.

Earlier patches in the series laid groundwork by restructuring packfile store initialization, simplifying interfaces, and refactoring the kept-pack cache. The changes are mechanical but extensive, touching 19 files so far. The work builds on Patrick's previous series (ps/object-read-stream and ps/odb-misc-fixes) and continues the pattern of well-contained, behavior-preserving refactors toward the pluggable ODB architecture. No objections have been raised in the thread, suggesting consensus on the direction.
