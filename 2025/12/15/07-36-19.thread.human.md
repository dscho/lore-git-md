Here's a human-readable summary of the thread's current state:

Patrick Steinhardt is leading a significant refactoring of Git's packfile storage system, working toward making the object database (ODB) backend pluggable. The 10-patch series (now at v3) moves packfile store ownership from the ODB layer to individual ODB sources, requiring changes across 30+ files while maintaining identical behavior. 

The latest patch (part 5) shifts packfile store initialization from being per-database to per-source, recognizing that each source connected to the database manages its own packfiles. This affects core operations like packfile window management and object lookup, though most callers continue working through high-level iteration functions. The changes are mechanical but foundational for the larger architectural goal.

Reviewers Justin Tobler and Toon Claes have provided detailed feedback throughout the series, with Patrick addressing all comments. Earlier patches have already simplified pack preparation, refactored the kept-pack cache, and cleaned up legacy code in packfile window management. The series builds on two recently merged topics and appears ready for inclusion, though Patrick notes the abstractions remain somewhat "leaky" at this transitional stage. This work represents the final major step before introducing fully pluggable ODB backends.
