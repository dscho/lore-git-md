Patrick Steinhardt has completed his 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage as part of the ongoing object database (ODB) abstraction effort. The series systematically moves packfile handling from operating at the ODB level to working with individual ODB sources (main repository and alternates), laying groundwork for future pluggable ODB backends.

The final patch moves multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`, completing the architectural shift of all packfile-related data to their proper location. This follows earlier patches that similarly relocated regular packfiles and kept packs. The changes are mechanical but foundational, touching core object storage code across 30 files while maintaining existing behavior.

The series has progressed without objections, with each patch following the established pattern of moving functionality to the source level. With this work now complete, the project has cleared a significant technical hurdle in the path toward pluggable ODB implementations. The changes will likely be picked up by Junio for integration into the next development cycle.
