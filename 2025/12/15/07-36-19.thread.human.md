Patrick Steinhardt is proposing a significant refactor of Git's packfile storage as part of the ongoing effort to enable pluggable object databases. The 10-patch series moves packfile tracking from being managed per-object-database to being handled by individual ODB sources (like the main repository or alternates). This architectural change completes key groundwork for the pluggable ODB effort by ensuring all object access - whether from packs or loose objects - consistently goes through its owning source.

The changes are substantial, touching 13 files and restructuring core packfile handling including pack entry lookup, MIDX handling, and packfile store management. The implementation appears careful to maintain existing behavior while enabling the new architecture. The series builds on two recent topics (object read streaming improvements and ODB fixes) that Patrick also worked on.

This appears to be a logical next step in the established refactoring direction toward pluggable ODBs, so it's likely to be accepted after technical review. The changes are deep in Git's object storage layer but shouldn't affect most users directly - they're primarily enabling future work rather than changing current behavior.
