Documentation patch formalizing Git's stance against automatic sign-off configuration.

Junio C Hamano proposes adding explicit documentation to prevent recurring discussions about adding `commit.signoff` and similar configuration options. The patch adds two sections explaining Git's longstanding position that Signed-off-by trailers must remain an explicit action to maintain their legal significance under the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO).

The documentation makes two key points: First, it states that automatic sign-offs weaken the legal standing of the DCO certification by making it plausible that the trailer was added "by inertia" rather than intentional certification. Second, it acknowledges `format.signoff` as a historical exception that shouldn't be replicated elsewhere in Git's configuration.

The patch places this explanation in both the Git FAQ (for general visibility) and the signoff option reference documentation (for technical users). The text maintains Git's strong stance against configuration-based sign-offs while providing the legal rationale that has been discussed in the thread. This appears to be the culmination of the recent discussion, providing a clear reference point for future inquiries about automatic sign-off configuration.
