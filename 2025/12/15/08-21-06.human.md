This is a refactoring patch in Justin Tobler's series to improve Git's object size reporting, specifically touching the string formatting of human-readable byte values. Junio Hamano points out a potential localization regression in the new implementation.

The patch splits the humanization logic into a separate `strbuf_humanise_bytes_value()` function, but in doing so introduces assumptions about number formatting that may not hold across all locales. Junio notes three specific issues: the hardcoded decimal point (which some locales render as a comma), the fixed number-before-unit ordering, and the mandatory single-space separator - all of which were previously handled more flexibly by the translation system when the entire string was translated as one unit.

While the decimal point issue could be addressed relatively easily, fixing the ordering and spacing requirements would be more involved. This feedback suggests the refactoring, while conceptually sound, needs to preserve the localization flexibility of the original implementation. The observation comes from Junio in his maintainer role, catching a subtle but important regression that wasn't apparent in the technical implementation itself.
