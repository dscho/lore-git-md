Patrick Steinhardt's 10-patch series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB continues with patch 5/10, completing the logical transition where each source maintains its own packfile store. The series enables pluggable ODBs by ensuring all object access routes through owning sources (main repo/alternates). 

Key architectural changes in patch 5/10:
- Moves packfile store from `struct object_database` to `struct odb_source`
- Adds `packfiles` member to `struct odb_source` while removing it from `struct object_database`
- Initializes packfile stores during ODB_source creation in `odb_source_new()`
- Updates packfile operations across 11 files (fast-import, grep, index-pack, pack-objects, http, midx, odb)
- Modifies `repo_for_each_pack()` to traverse all sources' pack stores
- Maintains backward compatibility while making packfile ownership consistent
