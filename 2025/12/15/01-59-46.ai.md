Documentation patch formalizing Git's stance against automatic sign-off configuration.

Junio C Hamano proposes adding explicit documentation to prevent recurring discussions about automatic sign-off configuration. The patch adds two sections explaining Git's longstanding position: one in the FAQ and another in the signoff option documentation. Both sections contain identical legal rationale text explaining that automatic sign-offs weaken the Developer Certificate of Origin's legal standing by enabling "inertia" sign-offs where contributors might not consciously certify their work.

The documentation acknowledges `format.signoff` as a historical exception but firmly states this won't be expanded to other commands. The text warns that making sign-off too easy could undermine its legal validity in court, where opponents might argue the trailer was added without proper consideration. The patch includes Brian m. carlson as a co-author for their input on the discussion.

This documentation codifies the consensus reached in previous discussions (notably in 2020) and provides authoritative reference material for future queries. The legal argument is presented with technical precision, using the exact same wording in both locations to ensure consistency. The patch doesn't introduce new functionality but serves as a definitive statement of Git project policy regarding contribution certification workflows.
