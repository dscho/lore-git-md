This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB. The patch moves the multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`, completing the architectural shift to consolidate all packfile-related data within the packfile store.

The change is mechanical but significant - it ensures the MIDX (which is inherently tied to packfiles) lives alongside other packfile data rather than being tracked separately at the ODB source level. The patch updates MIDX access in midx.c, odb.c, and packfile.c to use the new location, while maintaining identical behavior. The diff shows careful handling of MIDX cleanup during store closure and proper NULL checks throughout.

This concludes the series' core work of moving packfile ownership to the source level, a key step in the larger ODB abstraction effort. The changes are consistent with the series' established pattern of methodical refactoring while preserving behavior. With this patch, all packfile-related data now properly resides in the packfile store, leaving the ODB-owned/store-source-initialized inconsistency as the remaining architectural issue to be addressed in future work.
