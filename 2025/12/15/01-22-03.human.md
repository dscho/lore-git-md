Documentation refinement in the ongoing Signed-off-by (SOB) discussion. Brian m. carlson responds to Junio C Hamano's suggestion to resurrect a 2020 patch that would document the rationale against config-based SOB additions. The thread has established that automatic sign-offs via configuration were rejected due to concerns about weakening the legal standing of contributions under the Developer Certificate of Origin.

Carlson agrees with the approach of making this rationale more visible, suggesting it could go in the Git FAQ or documentation (or both) to prevent contributors from wasting time on rejected approaches. They also provide a minor grammatical correction to Junio's proposed documentation text, noting that a period should be a comma since the quotation is part of a larger sentence.

This continues the thread's careful attention to communicating the legal rationale clearly while maintaining Git's strong position against config-based SOB additions. The exchange shows the project's thoughtful approach to documentation - ensuring technical accuracy while making policy decisions accessible to contributors. The proposed documentation would help users understand why Git maintains explicit `--signoff` flags despite the convenience of configuration options.
