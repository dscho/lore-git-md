This is part 6 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing refactoring series to move packfile storage handling from the object database (ODB) level to the ODB source level, a key step toward enabling pluggable ODB backends.

The patch simplifies `packfile_store_get_packs()` to only prepare the MIDX (multi-pack index) for the specific packfile store being accessed, rather than preparing MIDXes for all sources in the ODB. This change follows naturally from the previous patches that moved packfile store ownership to individual sources. The commit message notes this shouldn't affect behavior since callers either operate on a single store or already iterate over sources themselves.

The change is small (4 lines removed, 2 added) and mechanical, continuing the series' pattern of focused, behavior-preserving refactoring. It touches the same core packfile handling code as previous patches but with a narrow scope, just adjusting the MIDX preparation logic to match the new source-level ownership model. The series continues to show careful attention to detail with clear commit messages explaining both the change and why it's safe.
