This thread discusses a platform-specific fix for iconv behavior on newer macOS versions (14/15). The author has sent a 3-patch RFC series building on René Scharfe's earlier macOS iconv workaround branch. The changes focus on three areas: adding an ICONV_RESTART_RESET flag in utf8.c to handle macOS-specific iconv quirks, reorganizing Darwin build configurations, and conditionally enabling the workaround for affected macOS versions. The author has tested the changes on macOS 15 and presents the series as technically sound but is seeking feedback before finalizing. The changes are relatively contained, touching only three files, and specifically target character encoding conversion behavior that could impact internationalization features.
