This is the 10th and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring Git's packfile storage to track packs per object database (ODB) source rather than per ODB. The patch moves the multi-pack index (MIDX) tracking from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`, completing the architectural shift of packfile-related data to their proper location.

The change updates all MIDX-related operations to work through the packfile store instead of directly accessing the ODB source. Key modifications include:
- Moving the `midx` field from `struct odb_source` to `struct packfile_store`
- Updating MIDX access in `midx.c` to go through `source->packfiles->midx`
- Simplifying `odb_close()` by removing direct MIDX handling (now handled by `packfile_store_close()`)
- Adding MIDX cleanup to `packfile_store_close()`
- Updating MIDX checks in packfile operations to use the store's MIDX

The patch continues the series' pattern of mechanical changes that maintain behavior while moving functionality to the correct architectural level. With this change, all packfile-related data (regular packs, kept packs, and now MIDX) are properly owned by the packfile store rather than the ODB source. This completes a key piece of groundwork for the larger ODB abstraction effort.
