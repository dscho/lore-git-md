Refactoring patch in the `git-history` series focusing on add-patch/add-interactive separation.

This patch (v6 07/11) continues the infrastructure work for the `git-history` command by refactoring the add-patch and add-interactive subsystems. Patrick Steinhardt splits out a new `struct interactive_options` to hold common configuration, addressing Phillip Wood's earlier feedback about unclear boundaries between the subsystems. The key changes move shared options into this new struct while maintaining the dependency direction where add-interactive depends on add-patch (not vice versa). 

The discussion reveals ongoing tension in the refactoring approach - while the patch improves separation of concerns, both Patrick and Phillip acknowledge the resulting interface remains somewhat confusing. Patrick argues the new structure is still clearer than the original tangled implementation, particularly for future maintainers. The patch leaves one remaining entanglement (initializing `add_i_state` in the add-patch subsystem) for a follow-up commit.

Technical details include:
- New `struct interactive_options` in add-patch.c (not moved to add-interactive.c due to dependency direction)
- Continued work toward eliminating cross-subsystem initialization
- Maintains existing test coverage since behavior doesn't change
- Part of broader effort to enable in-memory index operations for `git-history`

This represents incremental progress on the series' infrastructure goals, with acknowledged tradeoffs in interface design. The exchange shows the careful balancing act between clean architectural boundaries and practical implementation constraints in this complex refactoring.
