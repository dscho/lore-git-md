This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `git-history` series, specifically addressing the separation of interactive add/patch subsystems. 

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Phillip Wood's feedback about the `struct interactive_options` refactoring in patch 7/11 of the v6 series. The change splits out common options between add-patch and add-interactive into a new struct, though Phillip expressed concerns about the naming and location of these structures. Patrick explains his rationale for keeping the new struct in add-patch.c due to dependency direction, while acknowledging the result still isn't perfectly clean.

The exchange highlights the architectural challenges in disentangling these subsystems - a necessary step for the in-memory index operations needed by the `git-history` command's reword and split functionality. While the refactoring improves clarity compared to the previous intertwined state, both developers recognize there's room for further improvement in the structure's organization. This is part of the broader infrastructure work supporting the history editing features, where clean separation of components is important for maintainability.
