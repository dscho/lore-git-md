This is a two-patch series addressing cross-compilation issues in Git's Meson build system. The patches come from Toon Claes, who encountered these problems while cross-compiling for s390x architecture.

The first patch adds `subprojects/.wraplock` to `.gitignore`, preventing this Meson-generated file from being tracked. The second patch makes the ICONV_OMITS_BOM detection more robust by only attempting it when possible during cross-compilation scenarios.

The changes are small and focused - just two lines modified across two files. The motivation is clear from the cover letter: these are quality-of-life improvements for developers working with Meson builds, particularly in cross-compilation contexts. The patches appear straightforward with no obvious technical concerns, though they're narrow in scope.
