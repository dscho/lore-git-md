The thread now has two viable paths forward with distinct tradeoffs: Taylor's refined per-file tracking solution that avoids ref conflicts but retains filesystem atomicity challenges, versus Junio Hamano's centralized log proposal that solves namespace issues but introduces distributed system coordination complexities. The discussion appears to be converging on implementation quality and configuration details rather than fundamental design disagreements.
