Taylor Blau's multi-pack-index compaction series, now approved by Junio Hamano, introduces a new `git multi-pack-index compact` command that combines adjacent MIDX layers while maintaining bitmap compatibility. The 18-patch series addresses scalability needs for large repositories through careful handling of MIDX version compatibility (v1 vs v2) and object ordering during compaction.

The discussion has evolved from core functionality to platform considerations, with Randall S. Becker raising questions about endian-safety in arithmetic operations. Junio Hamano confirmed that the overflow checking helpers use standard arithmetic that isn't endian-dependent, pointing to similar patterns in Git's existing utility functions. While no actual platform issues were found, the exchange demonstrates the project's attention to cross-platform correctness, particularly for the NonStop port.

With all technical concerns addressed and comprehensive test coverage in place, the series is now complete and ready for merging. The final patches include optimizations suggested by Junio (removing specialized helpers) and documentation clarifications about MIDX version behavior. This work builds on Taylor's earlier MIDX and bitmap infrastructure contributions, further improving Git's handling of large repositories.
