This is a follow-up discussion in the security-focused series that eliminated insecure `mktemp(3)` usage. Jeff King (Peff) and René Scharfe are working through the final implementation details of the cleanup.

The conversation focuses on whether to keep the `NO_MKDTEMP` conditional wrapper pointing to the new `git_mkdtemp()` function. Scharfe had raised practical concerns about build system complications across different platforms, suggesting this cleanup should be handled in a separate patch. Peff agrees with this approach after understanding the complexity involved.

They also discuss the longer-term question of whether to completely ban `mkdtemp(3)` (like they did with `mktemp(3)`) or keep the redirection. Peff notes this should likely match whatever approach they take with `mkstemp()`, and raises a consideration about third-party code in the test suite that currently calls `mkdtemp()` directly.

The exchange shows the maintainers carefully weighing implementation tradeoffs while maintaining the security improvements from the series. The discussion appears to be concluding with consensus to handle the remaining cleanups in future work.
