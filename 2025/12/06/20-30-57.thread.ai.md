**Core functionality**: The `compact` subcommand takes MIDX layer checksums as range endpoints and combines them into a single layer while preserving the MIDX chain structure. The final patch adds `--[no-]bitmap` option control and ensures bitmap compatibility through careful object ordering during compaction.
