Performance optimization for promisor pack handling, specifically addressing unnecessary blob processing in `add_promisor_object()`. This is a response to v2 of Aaron Plattner's patch, which Jeff King (Peff) is reviewing.

The patch improves performance when initializing promisor object sets by avoiding full decompression and hash verification of blobs, which don't reference other objects. Jeff notes the solution also fixes a related bug in `parse_object_with_flags()` for `OBJ_NONE` objects, though he suggests this might belong in a separate patch. He raises a minor consideration about skipping hash checks for all objects (not just blobs), referencing prior discussion about when such checks are necessary.

Jeff gives the patch his approval ("looks great to me") while offering these minor suggestions for potential improvement. The changes are minimal (4 lines across 2 files) but have dramatic real-world impact - the original report showed a reduction from 76 minutes to under 2 minutes for a 176GB promisor pack. This appears to be a well-justified optimization that maintains correctness while significantly improving performance for repositories with large promisor packs.
