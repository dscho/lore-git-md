This is a refactoring patch in Taylor Blau's ongoing MIDX compaction series (14/17). The change extracts fanout layer handling from `compute_sorted_entries()` into a new `midx_fanout_add()` helper function, making the code more modular and easier to follow. 

The patch maintains identical behavior while preparing for future MIDX compaction work where fanout handling will need to differ slightly. The existing logic for processing objects from MIDX and pack sources one fanout layer at a time is preserved, just moved to a dedicated function. 

This continues the series' pattern of careful, incremental improvements to the MIDX infrastructure. The change is straightforward and well-contained, touching only midx-write.c with a net addition of 8 lines (25 new, 17 removed). The commit message clearly explains the motivation without needing deep MIDX expertise to understand.
