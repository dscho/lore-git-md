The new function handles both the MIDX fanout addition (when in non-incremental mode) and pack fanout addition, including special handling for the preferred pack.

The change maintains all existing functionality while making `compute_sorted_entries()` more focused on its core responsibility of sorting and deduplicating objects. This is part of the series' broader pattern of incremental improvements to the MIDX infrastructure in preparation for compaction functionality. The patch follows the series' established style of careful refactoring with no behavior changes, keeping the same error handling and edge cases while improving code organization.
