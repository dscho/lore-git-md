This is a follow-up discussion in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, specifically addressing build-time configuration options.

Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt are concluding the discussion about whether to support build-time configuration for the submodule encoding feature. Both agree that runtime configuration via `/etc/gitconfig` is sufficient, and that adding build-time options (like tying it to `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES`) would be unnecessary complexity. This aligns with the series' overall direction of keeping the implementation purely runtime-configurable.

The exchange confirms the architectural decision to avoid build-time influence on the feature's behavior, which was one of the last open questions in this long-running thread. With this point settled, the series appears ready to move forward with its final refinements focused on atomic enablement and transition documentation.
