This is a follow-up discussion in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, focusing on the operational aspects of enabling the extension. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion for an atomic enablement command that would both persist gitdir paths and activate the extension in one step.

Junio expresses appreciation for the simplicity of Patrick's proposed approach but raises a practical concern about migration complexity for existing users with custom pathname schemes. This reflects the series' ongoing challenge of balancing robustness with backward compatibility.

The email doesn't introduce new technical elements but rather evaluates the operational implications of the proposed atomic enablement command. Junio's comment suggests he's generally aligned with the direction but wants to ensure the solution accounts for real-world migration scenarios.

This discussion occurs as the series nears finalization, with most architectural questions resolved in favor of runtime-only configuration via `/etc/gitconfig`. The remaining work (planned for v6) includes implementing this atomic enablement command and finalizing documentation.
