This is a cover letter for a feature series implementing MIDX layer compaction, part of an ongoing effort to improve incremental repacking strategies in Git's multi-pack index (MIDX) system. The series builds on previous work to refactor repack.c and introduces the second major component: the ability to compact contiguous MIDX layers into a single layer while preserving bitmap validity. This enables more scalable repacking in large repositories by avoiding full MIDX rewrites.

Taylor Blau proposes a three-part strategy for incremental MIDX/bitmap repacking, with this series focusing on the compaction mechanism. The key insight is that compacting adjacent layers preserves pseudo-pack ordering, allowing bitmap reuse without expensive permutations. The implementation maintains geometric progression of object counts across layers while keeping the chain length bounded (typically under 10 layers).

The 17-patch series begins with preparatory cleanups and documentation fixes, then introduces core compaction functionality in later patches. Notable technical aspects include relaxing lexicographic pack ordering requirements, adding helper functions for pack permutation handling, and enabling bitmap support during compaction. Test coverage is added in t5335, though the author notes it could be expanded further.

This appears to be a well-motivated change with clear performance benefits for large repositories. The implementation follows established patterns and builds on prior MIDX work. The cover letter provides thorough context about both the immediate technical changes and their role in the larger repacking strategy. Reviewers will likely want to examine the bitmap handling details and consider additional edge cases in testing, but the overall approach seems sound.
