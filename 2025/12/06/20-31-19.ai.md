Performance optimization patch in Taylor Blau's MIDX compaction series. This is patch 7/17 in the series implementing MIDX layer compaction for more efficient repacking.

The change addresses a subtle issue in `midx_pack_order()` where bitmap positions were being assigned by iterating through packs in `ctx->pack_perm` order rather than their natural order in `ctx->info`. While this worked in current code, it would cause problems with the upcoming compaction changes that will modify how `pack_perm` works. The patch simplifies the logic by directly iterating through `ctx->info` instead.

The technical details involve how MIDX tracks bitmap positions for packs - initially setting them to `BITMAP_POS_UNKNOWN` and later updating them. The current `pack_perm`-based iteration isn't strictly necessary and could cause issues when compaction introduces new wrappers around `pack_perm`. The change is minimal (just one line) but prevents potential crashes when dealing with compaction ranges where the base layer has packs.

This appears to be a well-justified preparatory change for the larger compaction work, removing a potential landmine before it becomes a problem. The commit message clearly explains both the current safe-but-suboptimal behavior and why the change is needed for future functionality.
