Bugfix for geometric repacking with promisor remotes. This is the first patch in a 3-part series addressing object database issues discovered during other work.

Patrick Steinhardt fixes an interaction between geometric repacking and promisor remotes where `git repack` would incorrectly pass both `--stdin-packs` and `--exclude-promisor-objects` to `git-pack-objects`. These options are fundamentally incompatible - the former uses stdin to identify packs to merge, while the latter filters objects based on revisions. The patch moves the promisor objects exclusion to only apply in non-geometric repack cases, since geometric repacks don't need this protection (they only merge packs or pack loose objects, neither of which can contain promisor objects).

The change is small (5 lines in builtin/repack.c) but comes with a thorough new test case in t7703-repack-geometric.sh that verifies promisor packs remain untouched while geometric repacking still works. The test creates a partial clone, checks for missing objects, then confirms the repack succeeds while preserving promisor packs. This appears to be a clean, focused fix with good test coverage for a specific edge case in repacking behavior.
