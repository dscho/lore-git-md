Patrick Steinhardt's memory management bugfix series is now complete and ready for merging. The two-patch series addresses distinct but related issues in Git's object handling:

The first patch fixes a memory leak and object counting issue in commit-graph writing (in `builtin/gc.c`), where `commit_list_append()` was incorrectly used instead of `commit_list_insert()`. This could lead to double-counting of tip objects. The fix includes enhanced test coverage and has been thoroughly reviewed by Karthik Nayak and Justin Tobler, who both gave their final approval.

The second patch (the subject of this latest email) plugs a 192-byte memory leak that occurred during `git grep` operations on submodules. The root cause was an incorrect cleanup sequence in `odb.c` where sources were being freed before being closed. This simple but important one-line fix has also received final review acks from Justin Tobler and Karthik Nayak.

Both fixes are now considered technically sound with all feedback addressed. The series represents two contained, well-tested improvements to Git's memory management, with the submodule ODB leak being the final piece to complete the puzzle. The patches are ready for Junio to pick up in the next integration cycle.
