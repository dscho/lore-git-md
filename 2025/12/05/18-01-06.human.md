This is a performance optimization follow-up in the promisor pack handling thread. Jeff King builds on Aaron Plattner's initial patch to skip blob parsing in promisor object initialization, proposing an alternative approach using `parse_object_with_flags()` with `PARSE_OBJECT_SKIP_HASH_CHECK`. This leverages existing commit-graph optimizations while maintaining the same functionality.

The key improvement is that Jeff's approach reduces the benchmark time further - from Aaron's 2 minutes down to 49 seconds for the linux.git case when commit-graph is available. The optimization works by both skipping blob loading and using commit-graph data for commits, while also avoiding hash computations on loaded objects. Jeff notes this may be particularly beneficial for tree objects that still need processing.

The email serves as a technical refinement to the ongoing discussion, showing how to integrate with Git's existing optimization infrastructure rather than adding new special cases. It maintains the thread's focus on reducing CPU overhead in promisor pack handling without introducing new complexity. The change appears well-motivated by concrete performance measurements and fits naturally with Git's object parsing architecture.
