This is a performance optimization discussion for promisor object handling in packfiles, continuing a thread about reducing overhead in partial clone scenarios.

He notes this builds on existing parse_object optimizations rather than adding new type-checking logic. The change remains focused on the promisor pack initialization path but may have broader applicability to other code paths that could benefit from the skip-hash flag.
