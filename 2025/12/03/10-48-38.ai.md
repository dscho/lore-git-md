This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` series, modifying the cache-tree infrastructure to support in-memory index operations. The change allows `write_in_core_index_as_tree()` to work with arbitrary index states rather than being limited to the repository's main index.

The patch modifies the function signature to accept an explicit `struct index_state` parameter, removing its implicit dependency on `repo->index`. This enables the upcoming `git history split` subcommand to operate on temporary in-memory indexes during commit splitting operations. The change is minimal (6 lines modified across 3 files) and maintains backward compatibility by updating the single existing caller in `builtin/checkout.c` to pass `the_repository->index` explicitly.

This builds on earlier infrastructure work in the series (particularly patch 9/12's in-memory index patching support) and aligns with the broader goal of enabling history editing operations that don't require working tree modifications. The change is straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, though reviewers may want to verify it doesn't impact performance-sensitive index operations.
