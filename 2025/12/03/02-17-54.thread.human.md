This is an announcement of Git Rev News edition 129, a regular newsletter summarizing Git development activity. Christian Couder shares that this edition is now available online, providing highlights of recent Git development including notable commits, discussions, and community contributions. The current issue acknowledges the work of several contributors in its preparation: Ayush Chandekar, Štěpán Němec, Bruno Brito, and D. Ben Knoble.

The newsletter serves as a valuable resource for those who want to stay informed about Git development without closely following the mailing list. The announcement includes a link to the published content and notes that contributions for the next edition (issue #813 on GitHub) are welcome, maintaining the newsletter's tradition of being a community-driven publication.
