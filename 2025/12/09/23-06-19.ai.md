This is a discussion about standardizing terminology for index-related flags in Git commands, specifically the thread about `--staged` vs `--cached`. Junio C Hamano responds to Lucas Seiki Oshiro's proposal to add `--staged` to more commands by questioning whether it might be simpler to just remove the "obsolete" label from `--cached` and drop `--staged` entirely.

The key technical point raised is that whatever terminology is used must maintain the distinction between `--cached` (index-only operations) and `--index` (operations involving both index and working tree). Hamano suggests that rather than expanding `--staged` usage, it might be cleaner to stop treating `--cached` as deprecated terminology, since the flag's meaning is well-established.

This shifts the discussion from "how to expand the newer terminology" to "whether we keep the older terminology at all" - a more fundamental question about Git's interface stability versus terminology consistency. The email doesn't take a firm position but invites consideration of this alternative approach to resolving the inconsistency.
