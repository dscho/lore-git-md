Performance optimization extending existing skip-hash behavior to handle OBJ_NONE objects. This is the first of two patches in v3 of a series improving promisor object handling in partial clones.

The patch tweaks `parse_object_with_flags()` to apply its `PARSE_OBJECT_SKIP_HASH_CHECK` optimization to objects with type `OBJ_NONE` (untyped objects) in addition to `OBJ_BLOB`. This matters for promisor objects marked uninteresting during revision walks, which currently get fully parsed even when their contents aren't needed. The change is made symmetrically for tree objects when `discard_tree` is set.

The 4-line diff modifies two conditions in `object.c` to include `OBJ_NONE` alongside the type-specific checks. The commit message clearly explains the current behavior, the optimization gap being addressed, and references the prior commit that introduced the blob-specific optimization. This appears to be a well-motivated, surgical change building logically on existing optimizations.
