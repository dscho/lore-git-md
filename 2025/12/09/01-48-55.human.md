Performance optimization for promisor object handling. This two-patch series improves the performance of `--exclude-promisor-objects` by extending hash-check skipping optimizations to unknown blob objects and applying them in `add_promisor_object()`.

The first patch modifies `parse_object_with_flags()` to apply the `PARSE_OBJECT_SKIP_HASH_CHECK` optimization to objects marked as `OBJ_NONE` (unknown blobs), not just known object types. The second patch then leverages this in `add_promisor_object()` to skip hash verification when adding promisor objects to the object store.

The changes are small (4 lines modified total) but targeted at a specific performance bottleneck in promisor object handling. The commit messages are clear about the motivation and expected impact, though they don't provide benchmark numbers. The changes appear focused and well-contained, touching only the necessary parts of `object.c` and `packfile.c`.
