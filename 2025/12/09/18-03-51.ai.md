This is a documentation discussion follow-up in the `git replay` man page improvement series. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Phillip Wood's suggested alternative wording for the `--contained` option documentation, which proposed "Update all branches whose head commits are replayed. Requires --onto."

Kristoffer expresses personal reservations about introducing "head" terminology into the documentation, preferring to maintain the current phrasing that references `refs/heads/` directly. He cites two reasons: (1) a personal preference for viewing branches simply as refs pointing to commits rather than as "lines of development," and (2) a desire to avoid introducing the potentially confusing "head" noun into the documentation where it currently doesn't appear. He notes this contrasts with the more complex branch definition in gitglossary(7).

The email hints that Kristoffer will elaborate further on the current phrasing ("Advance all branches contained in <revision-range>") in a subsequent message. This continues the thread's focus on precisely documenting `--contained`'s behavior while maintaining consistency with Git's existing terminology patterns. The discussion remains focused on wording choices rather than any functional changes to the command.
