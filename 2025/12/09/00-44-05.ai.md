This is a clarification email in the ongoing Meson cross-compilation thread, providing technical details about the target platform. Carlo Arenas confirms the specific architecture being discussed is 64-bit Big Endian Linux for s390x (IBM Z/LinuxOne), not Z/OS, based on the ELF binary analysis shared by Toon Claes. The binary shows it's built for GNU/Linux 3.2.0 with S/390 architecture using System V ABI.

Arenas notes his surprise at the initial mention of Z/OS compatibility, as CMake has known issues building natively for that platform. He offers to test the cross-compiled binaries on his RHEL9/s390x system, suggesting the validation could be as simple as installing and running `make test`.

This email provides the definitive platform confirmation that was missing from earlier discussion, while also demonstrating community engagement to verify the cross-compilation results.
