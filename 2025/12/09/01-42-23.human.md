This is a brief follow-up in the MIDX compaction series, specifically addressing naming conventions in the checksum handling code.

Taylor Blau responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review of patch 02/17, which originally split checksum handling into `get_midx_checksum()` and `get_midx_hash()`. Patrick suggested more descriptive names (`midx_get_checksum()` and `midx_get_checksum_hex()`) that better reflect the functions' purposes and align with modern Git naming conventions.

Taylor agrees with both suggestions - the clearer distinction between binary and hex representations, and the `midx_` prefix convention. This is a straightforward resolution to a minor naming issue in the larger MIDX compaction work, showing the careful attention to detail in both review and implementation of this series.
