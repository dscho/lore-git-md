Technical follow-up in ongoing ODB refactoring thread between Justin Tobler and Patrick Steinhardt, focusing on source-based alternates handling.

Justin responds to Patrick's clarification about moving alternates handling into the ODB source abstraction, agreeing with the approach but suggesting the function should be named `odb_add_alternate_source()` rather than the more general `odb_add_source()`, since it specifically handles alternate sources. Patrick had already agreed to this naming change in the quoted exchange.

The email then raises a broader architectural question about how alternates are represented in `struct object_database`. Justin notes that while the current single-list approach works for reading objects (where all sources are equal), writing operations only use the first source, leading to potentially fragile behavior during ODB transactions that reorder the list. He suggests either making alternates more explicitly separate from the primary source in the structure, or handling write-target selection through a different mechanism than list reordering.

This continues the thread's technical discussion about interface design for the ODB abstraction, specifically around how to cleanly represent the distinction between primary and alternate sources while maintaining the recursive nature of alternates handling.
