This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to modernize Git's object database alternates handling. The patch moves relative path resolution from the linking phase into the parsing phase, making the alternates handling more self-contained.

Justin Tobler provides feedback on the patch, asking for clarification about the long-term goal - whether alternates will be moved under the primary ODB source or just have their info recorded in the source structure. He also suggests the function name `odb_add_alternate_source()` might be more precise than the proposed `odb_add_source()`, since it specifically handles alternate sources.

The technical review notes that moving the relative path resolution into `parse_alternates()` appears reasonable. The patch continues the series' pattern of restructuring alternates handling to work through the new "source" abstraction rather than direct filesystem operations, while maintaining existing behavior. This is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort to support pluggable backends.
