This is a discussion follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt's refactoring series for Git's object database alternates handling. The email clarifies design intent around the recursive nature of alternate ODB sources and agrees with Justin Tobler's naming suggestion.

Patrick explains that the goal is to move alternate handling into the ODB source abstraction generally, not just for primary sources, since alternates are inherently recursive. This supports future backends that may not use filesystem paths at all. He agrees with Justin's proposal to rename the function to `odb_add_alternate_source()` to better reflect its specific role in handling recursive alternates.
