This is a documentation discussion thread about clarifying the `--contained` option for `git replay`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk is responding to Phillip Wood's suggested wording for the option's documentation, which proposed phrasing it as "Update all branches whose head commits are replayed. Requires --onto."

Kristoffer expresses some reservations about introducing "head" terminology into the documentation, preferring to stick with the current phrasing that references "branches contained in <revision-range>." He cites personal preferences about branch terminology and concerns about potential confusion, contrasting the simple view of branches are refs pointing to commits with Git's glossary definition that describes branches as lines of development.

The discussion reveals an underlying tension between technical precision and pedagogical clarity in documentation. Kristoffer plans to elaborate further on why he prefers the existing phrasing in a follow-up email. This exchange shows the careful consideration going into the exact wording of the `--contained` option documentation, with multiple contributors weighing in on how best to describe the behavior while maintaining consistency with Git's terminology.
