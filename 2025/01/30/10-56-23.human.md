This is a discussion about project categorization for Git's GSoC 2025 participation, specifically regarding the inclusion criteria for microprojects.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Christian Couder's suggestion about creating a separate section for slightly larger follow-up projects. While Christian argues these could help participants who complete microprojects early, Patrick expresses concerns about two particular projects ("Add more builtin patterns for userdiff" and "Replace a run_command*() call") being too vaguely defined. He suggests focusing first on establishing a solid documentation framework with clearly defined, uncontroversial microprojects before considering more ambiguous additions.

The exchange shows ongoing refinement of the GSoC project structure, with Patrick advocating for a more conservative, iterative approach to project selection while Christian looks to provide more opportunities for engaged contributors. This touches on the broader challenge of balancing project accessibility with clear definition and maintainer consensus.
