This is a bugfix for `git repack`'s handling of promisor packs, following up on Tomáš Trnka's patch. Junio Hamano is not reviewing the technical aspects but points the author to two relevant prior discussions about repack and promisor objects interactions, suggesting those authors might be better reviewers.

The patch itself fixes an inconsistency where keep-pack settings (like `--keep-pack`, `.keep` files, and `gc.bigPackThreshold`) were not being respected for promisor packs during ALL_INTO_ONE repacks. The solution passes the keep-pack logic through to the pack-objects subprocess while maintaining existing behavior when `pack_kept_objects` is true.

Junio notes the patch appears technically sound but defers to others more familiar with promisor pack handling for review. The original patch included an RFC question about test placement (whether to add tests to t7700-repack.sh or t0410-partial-clone.sh), which remains an open question for the thread.
