This is part 6/13 of Patrick Steinhardt's Meson build system refactoring series, focusing on curl dependency management. The patch addresses an architectural issue where libcurl was being unnecessarily linked into all executables through `libgit_dependencies`, even when not needed.

The key change moves curl linkage from being a universal dependency to being properly scoped. The patch:
1. Modifies the curl dependency to only propagate include directories (needed for version resolution in help.c) rather than full linkage
2. Maintains proper curl linkage for the specialized `libgit_curl` component (which handles HTTP-related functionality)
3. Ensures curl-dependent executables like git-remote-http still get the full curl linkage they require

This continues the series' theme of cleaning up dependency relationships in the Meson build system, following the introduction of `libgit_curl` in the previous patch. The change is particularly relevant for platforms where curl linkage might be problematic or unnecessary, though it maintains all existing functionality for HTTP-related commands.

The patch appears well-contained and builds on the architectural improvements from earlier in the series. It demonstrates careful consideration of what actually needs curl linkage versus what only needs curl headers, suggesting thoughtful build system optimization rather than just mechanical restructuring.
