This is a discussion about Git's participation in Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2025, specifically focusing on project documentation and scope. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about how to maintain the list of proposed GSoC projects in Documentation/Projects.txt.

The email raises two key questions about the proposed "best-before" maintenance system for GSoC projects: whether project topics should have specific owners (and what happens if those owners leave), and whether "relevant" might be a better term than "accurate" to describe project status. Junio also weighs in on three specific proposed projects, expressing skepticism about the "-Wsign-compare" warnings project and agreeing with Patrick that the "builtin patterns for userdiff" and "run_command*() replacement" projects seem too open-ended.

This continues the thread's ongoing discussion about how to balance well-defined microprojects against more open-ended work in the GSoC program. Junio's input as Git's maintainer carries significant weight in these decisions, particularly regarding code quality concerns with the sign comparison warnings project. The exchange shows the project leadership working through the practical details of maintaining a useful and actionable list of GSoC projects.
