Thread summary:

Bugfix addressing integer overflow vulnerability in `git apply`'s hunk header parsing. Phillip Wood identified that `strtoul()` could silently ignore numeric overflows when processing line numbers in patch files, potentially leading to incorrect patch application. The fix properly initializes and checks `errno` after `strtoul()` calls in `apply.c`'s `parse_num()` function, though error messages remain generic ("corrupt patch") for now. Includes test case in `t4100-apply-stat.sh` demonstrating overflow detection with extreme line numbers (98765432109876543210).

Junio Hamano has acknowledged and accepted the patch, praising its narrow scope (single-line change in `parse_num()`) and defensive nature against potential malicious input. While noting the same issue exists in `git add -p`'s similar parsing logic, that remains unaddressed pending resolution of another related patch series. The change is now queued for inclusion, with maintainer agreement that the generic error message is sufficient given the unlikelihood of users encountering this in normal operation.
