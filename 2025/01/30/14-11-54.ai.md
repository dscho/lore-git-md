This is a follow-up discussion in the `pre-checkout` hook proposal thread. Konstantin Khomoutov adds a practical example reinforcing Brian m. carlson's earlier point about the bypassability of local branch naming policies. The contributor describes their personal workflow of working on detached HEAD and pushing directly to remote branches without creating local branch names, demonstrating yet another way the proposed hook could be circumvented. This serves as a concrete illustration of Git's inherent flexibility in local operations, though Khomoutov notes this isn't a common workflow pattern. The email doesn't introduce new arguments but strengthens the existing case against the hook by showing additional real-world scenarios where local branch name validation would be ineffective. The exchange continues to highlight the tension between workflow policy desires and Git's design philosophy of local repository autonomy.
