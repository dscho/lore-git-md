Response to feature proposal for a `pre-checkout` hook, arguing against its adoption. Brian m. carlson explains that Git intentionally avoids restricting local actions, as users are assumed to have full control over their own repositories. They point out multiple ways users could bypass such a hook (like `git branch -m` or `git update-ref`), making it ineffective for enforcement. The response cites the Git FAQ's position that policy enforcement belongs on the server side (via `pre-receive` hooks or CI), not in local hooks that users can easily bypass. While acknowledging others might disagree, the email suggests this hook doesn't offer enough general utility outside restricted policy environments to justify inclusion. The tone is polite but firm in its skepticism about the proposal's viability.
