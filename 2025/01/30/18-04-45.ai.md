Bugfix refinement in the packed-refs validation series, specifically around worktree handling during verification. This is a review response from Junio Hamano addressing Sheji Luo's patch that modified how worktrees are accessed during ref verification.

The core issue is that the original implementation used `get_worktrees()` which would die on encountering a broken HEAD reference, while the verification process aims to collect all possible errors rather than failing fast. The patch switched to `get_worktrees_without_reading_head()` to avoid this premature termination.

Junio suggests several improvements to the commit message wording to better explain the motivation. He notes that while avoiding HEAD reads prevents early termination, the verification should still check HEAD contents elsewhere in the process. The suggested rewording clarifies that the goal is to continue checking other refs after finding HEAD issues, not to skip HEAD validation entirely.

This appears to be a straightforward refinement of the test infrastructure that aligns with the series' goal of comprehensive validation. The review focuses on improving clarity in the commit message rather than suggesting functional changes, indicating the technical approach itself is sound. The discussion remains focused on this specific worktree-handling aspect of the larger packed-refs validation effort.
