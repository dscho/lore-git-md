Maintainer follow-up on help output consistency audit.

Junio Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's script that identified builtins outputting help text to stderr rather than stdout. The script (which filters `git --list-cmds=builtins` output through a pipeline to isolate commands printing usage to stderr) provides concrete data showing the stderr behavior is indeed the minority case among Git builtins. This confirms the technical basis for standardizing on stdout as the preferred output channel for help text, aligning with Junio's earlier expressed preference in the thread.

The email serves as both acknowledgement of the audit work and implicit validation of the stdout direction, though it stops short of explicitly stating next steps. The "minority" observation suggests the stdout camp has gained technical backing beyond just philosophical preference, strengthening the case for systematic changes like those proposed by Jeff King earlier in the thread. This moves the discussion from debate over direction to implementation strategy, with the audit data now available to guide prioritization of fixes.
