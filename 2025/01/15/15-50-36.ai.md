This is a brief follow-up in the zlib-ng integration thread, addressing a tangential tooling issue. Konstantin Ryabitsev responds to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier comment about `b4` using `--full-index` for patch generation, which had come up during the review of the zlib-ng patch series.

The email points out that the `--full-index` behavior in `b4` was introduced in a specific commit (23a9ddba10a057bfa9c438c0b50ac36d278ae022) and questions whether it's actually necessary for `--binary` patches. Ryabitsev invites Steinhardt to submit a fix for this in the `b4` tool if desired, while also acknowledging that his own backlog of `b4` patches has been delayed due to infrastructure priorities.

This exchange doesn't affect the technical content of the zlib-ng series itself, but rather clarifies a peripheral discussion about patch submission tooling that arose during review. The main series remains in its final polishing stage as described in the thread summary.
