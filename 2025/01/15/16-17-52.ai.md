Performance optimization series (zlib-ng support) - constness fix.

This is a minor follow-up in the zlib-ng optimization series, addressing a constness mismatch between Git's `git_zstream::next_in` and zlib-ng's `z_stream::next_in` field. While zlib-ng marks its input pointer as const, Git's wrapper structure does not, leading to compiler errors during assignment. The patch fixes this by adding a const cast when assigning between the two structures.

The change is straightforward and maintains the series' goal of compatibility between standard zlib and zlib-ng backends. It follows naturally from the earlier refactoring work that introduced the `git_zstream` abstraction layer. The patch appears to be a clean solution to the immediate compilation issue without introducing new technical debt.

This is part of the final polishing phase of the series, which has already addressed all major technical concerns and is nearing potential integration. The change is small but necessary for correct compilation with zlib-ng, and doesn't appear to introduce any new compatibility concerns with either zlib backend.
