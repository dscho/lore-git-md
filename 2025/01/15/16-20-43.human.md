This is a brief follow-up in the zlib-ng performance optimization series, specifically addressing a minor tooling question about patch generation. 

Junio Hamano confirms Konstantin Ryabitsev's observation that the `--full-index` flag isn't necessary when generating binary patches with `git format-patch`, since `--binary` already includes full object names by default. This exchange clarifies that the flag was likely added by the `b4` tool (used for patch submission) rather than being essential to the patch series itself.

The discussion is tangential to the main technical work of the zlib-ng series, which as noted in the thread summary is otherwise complete and in final review. This email simply resolves a minor tooling question that came up during the review process, with no impact on the series' technical content or readiness for merging.
