This confirms the effort requires different technical approaches for different command architectures.

The test infrastructure, developed by Jeff King, already supports both transitional checking (via GIT_TEST_HELP_MUST_BE_STDOUT flag) and strict validation modes. While concrete progress is being made, deeper architectural questions remain about handling vreportf()'s stderr assumption and standardizing exit codes, which will be addressed after the output routing is fixed.
