This is a performance optimization patch series introducing zlib-ng support, currently in its final polish phase. The email exchange clarifies a build system detail regarding header inclusion paths on Windows/MinGW platforms.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Karthik Nayak's question about why they can't simply name the new zlib compatibility header "compat/zlib.h". He explains that while most platforms don't include "compat/" in the compiler's header search path, Windows/MinGW builds do (via config.mak.uname), which could cause the wrong header to be included. This confirms the original patch's approach of using "compat/zlib-compat.h" was correct.

The thread shows the series is in its final stages, with all major technical discussions resolved and only minor clarifications like this one being addressed.
