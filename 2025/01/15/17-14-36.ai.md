Bug report follow-up with additional implementation details. Jonas Konrad confirms Kristoffer Haugsbakk's audit findings about Git commands outputting help to stderr, noting they found 40 out of 142 builtins exhibit this behavior. The email extends the investigation beyond builtins to several Git-related tools: `git-scalar` (stderr), `git-lfs` (no -h support), `git-p4` (stdout without -h), and mentions untested cases for `git-svn` and `git-cvsserver`. Two special cases are called out for testing: `git-http-backend` and `git-filter-branch`.

This provides additional data points for the ongoing standardization effort around help output behavior. The findings align with and extend Kristoffer's earlier audit, reinforcing the scope of the inconsistency problem. The mention of non-builtin tools suggests the issue may be broader than initially scoped, though the core focus remains on builtins. The email doesn't propose solutions but contributes to the evidence base, showing the stderr behavior is widespread across both core and auxiliary Git components.
