Junio Hamano weighs in on the ongoing discussion about Git help output streams, siding with Jonas Konrad's position that help text should be considered primary output and thus belong on stdout. He reframes Matěj Cepl's "substantial material" argument by calling the help text "the primary output in response to the end user request," implying it should go to stdout rather than stderr. Hamano suggests a practical test to identify inconsistent commands by redirecting their help output to /dev/null, which would reveal those writing to stderr. This brief but pointed intervention from Git's maintainer appears to favor standardizing help output to stdout across all Git commands, though he stops short of explicitly stating a policy change. The email moves the discussion forward by providing maintainer perspective on what constitutes primary output, potentially breaking the philosophical impasse in the thread.
