This is a clarification in the ongoing security release thread regarding the v2.47.2 version metadata issue. Junio Hamano responds to Randall Becker's concern about being unable to package v2.47.2 due to strict requirements about unmodified commits in their regulated environment. Hamano points out that Becker's concern may be misplaced - the DEF_VER inconsistency in GIT-VERSION-GEN that causes tarball builds to incorrectly show v2.47.1 doesn't actually affect the version reported in practice, since both cloned repositories and official tarballs will correctly show v2.47.2 through other mechanisms (the git tag in clones and the version file in tarballs). 

The email serves to correct what appears to be a misunderstanding about the scope of the version reporting issue, emphasizing that the actual security fixes are properly versioned as 2.47.2 in all practical usage scenarios. This suggests Becker's packaging constraints may not be as limiting as initially thought, since the version metadata issue they're concerned about doesn't manifest in the ways they'd actually be distributing Git to their customers. The response maintains the thread's focus on clarifying the real-world impact of the build system quirk while trying to help downstream packagers work within their constraints.
