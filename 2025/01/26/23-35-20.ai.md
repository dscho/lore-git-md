This is a bugfix investigation for `git fetch` tag behavior in shallow clones, now focusing on transport protocol differences. Bence Ferdinandy follows up on Danila Manturov's discovery that the issue (tags on shallow boundary commits not being fetched) works correctly with JSch but fails with native SSH/HTTPS transports.

Bence revisits the earlier fix (6c915c3f85) that addressed a similar issue by avoiding unnecessary HEAD requests, noting the original thread suggested simply reordering tag/HEAD prefix additions might solve the problem. He proposes a patch that moves the HEAD prefix addition before tags, observing this appears to work but admits it feels like a band-aid without understanding why the order matters. The key technical elements are the modified `do_fetch()` function in builtin/fetch.c, the `uses_remote_tracking()` check, and the `transport_ls_refs_options.ref_prefixes` manipulation. The discussion highlights an ongoing mystery about why prefix order affects behavior differently across transport protocols, suggesting deeper protocol-specific handling may be needed rather than just reordering operations. This continues the thread's progression from initial bug report to transport-layer investigation.
