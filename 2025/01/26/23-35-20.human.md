Bugfix investigation into `git fetch` tag behavior in shallow clones, now focusing on transport protocol differences. Bence Ferdinandy follows up on Danila Manturov's discovery that the tag-fetching regression (introduced in Git 2.47.1→2.48.rc0) behaves differently across transports - working correctly via JSch but failing with native SSH/HTTPS. 

Bence traces the issue back to commit 6c915c3f85's approach of avoiding unnecessary HEAD requests, noting the original thread never fully explained why tag/HEAD ordering matters. He proposes a patch that swaps the order of tag and HEAD prefix additions in `builtin/fetch.c`, which empirically fixes the issue but leaves the underlying mechanism unclear. 

The email highlights an ongoing mystery in Git's fetch mechanics - why transport protocol choice affects tag resolution in shallow clones, and why simple ordering changes produce different outcomes. Bence's pragmatic approach suggests this may need another temporary fix while the deeper transport-layer interactions remain to be understood. The patch appears straightforward but carries an air of "we're papering over something we don't fully grasp" - a sentiment Bence explicitly acknowledges.
