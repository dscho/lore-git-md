This is the third and final patch in a refactoring series moving refspec-related functions from remote.c to refspec.c. The patch relocates `apply_refspecs()` and `apply_negative_refspecs()`, which handle the application of refspec patterns to references. The changes are purely organizational - moving 31 lines from remote.c to refspec.c while adding corresponding declarations to the headers, maintaining all existing functionality.

The series follows a clear pattern of centralizing refspec logic in refspec.c while leaving remote-specific operations in remote.c. This patch completes the relocation of core refspec operations identified by the author, though they note there may be about 200 more lines of related code that could potentially be refactored in future work. The changes are mechanical and well-contained, with no behavioral impact - just improved code organization. As an RFC, it's still awaiting feedback on the overall architectural approach.
