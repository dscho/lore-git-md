The thread discusses a bugfix for reflog migration of stash refs when switching between Git's reference backends. The issue was introduced in a recent change (commit 246cebe320) that added reflog migration support but didn't account for the default behavior of `core.logAllRefUpdates` excluding stash refs. Patrick Steinhardt provided the fix - a one-line change adding the `REF_FORCE_CREATE_REFLOG` flag to ensure reflog entries are created regardless of configuration, along with a test case verifying stash reflog retention during migration.

He confirms the solution makes sense and states he will queue the patch for merging. The fix is based on the maint branch, indicating it's considered important enough fix to be backported to stable releases.
