The third and final patch in this refspec refactoring series moves the remaining refspec application logic from remote.c` to `refspec.c`. This completes the mechanical relocation of pure refspec operations to their new canonical home, following the pattern established in the first two patches. The patch moves `apply_refspecs()` and `apply_negative_refspecs()` - which handle refspec transformations and negative refspec filtering respectively - along with their declarations, totaling +40/-39 lines. These functions operate on refspec patterns and ref mappings without remote-specific dependencies, making them natural candidates for `refspec.c`. The implementation remains unchanged, preserving all existing behavior while improving code organization. With this patch, the series has now relocated all clearly separable refspec logic identified by the author, though they note approximately 200 additional lines of mixed concerns remain for potential future work. The patch maintains the RFC status of the series, which has yet to receive reviewer feedback on the overall approach.
