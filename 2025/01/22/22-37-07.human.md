This is a performance-focused patch in Derrick Stolee's series introducing alternative name hash algorithms for pack-objects delta compression. Taylor Blau provides a detailed review of the proposed third hash version (v3), which aims to minimize collisions at the cost of locality.

The review notes that while v3 shows impressive collision reduction in some repositories (like Microsoft's fluentui monorepo), its benefits are less clear-cut than v2's improvements over v1. Performance metrics show v3 sometimes improves on v2 but often regresses, particularly in repositories like Git and Linux that already benefit from v1's characteristics. Taylor suggests the modest gains may not justify maintaining v3 long-term, especially since v2 already provides dramatic improvements over v1.

The review thoughtfully considers whether to include detection mechanisms to help users choose between v2 and v3, but ultimately leans toward dropping v3 entirely to avoid long-term maintenance burden. This reflects the series' maturity - with v2 established as the clear winner, the discussion now focuses on scope refinement rather than technical implementation. Taylor's analysis suggests this final patch may be more complexity than it's worth given the project's maintenance philosophy.
