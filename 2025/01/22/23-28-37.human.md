This is a review response in the ongoing discussion about pack-objects name hash algorithms. Taylor Blau provides their final thoughts on Derrick Stolee's v3 patch series introducing alternative name hash versions for delta compression.

Taylor acknowledges the long review delay and offers mostly cosmetic comments on the patches. The key recommendation is to drop the proposed version 3 of the name hash algorithm, despite its collision reduction benefits, in favor of focusing solely on version 2. Their reasoning is that version 2 provides clear improvements over the current version 1 in most cases, while version 3's tradeoffs (some performance regressions, unclear heuristics for when to use it) don't justify the added maintenance burden.

The review expresses strong support for the overall approach of versioned name hashes, noting it will simplify future bitmap-related changes. Taylor explicitly approves the technical direction but leaves the final decision on version 3's inclusion to Stolee, while clearly stating their preference to exclude it. The tone is positive and collaborative, indicating the series is nearly ready to proceed pending this one design decision.
