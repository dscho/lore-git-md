This is a follow-up in the reftable migration bug thread, where Karthik Nayak confirms and investigates Brian Carlson's report about stash reflog entries not being properly migrated to the reftable format.

Karthik verifies they can reproduce the issue where `git stash show` and related commands fail after converting a repository to reftable storage. While the stash ref itself appears in `git for-each-ref` output, the reflog entries seem to be inaccessible. Karthik notes they haven't yet determined the root cause but plans to investigate further this week.

The exchange highlights the value of thorough testing during the reftable migration effort, with both contributors maintaining a constructive tone - Brian framing their earlier bug reports as intended to help improve the feature, and Karthik expressing appreciation for the testing feedback. This appears to be a genuine migration edge case that will need addressing, though not indicative of fundamental problems with reftable itself.
