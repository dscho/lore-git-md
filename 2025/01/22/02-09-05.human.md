Bugfix for reftable migration of stash entries. Brian M. Carlson reports that `git refs migrate` fails to properly convert stash reflogs when migrating to the reftable backend, potentially causing users to lose their stashes. They provide a reproducible test case showing that while stashes are visible before migration, they disappear afterward.

The issue appears to be a gap in the reflog migration logic, though Carlson notes they haven't had time for deeper investigation due to personal commitments. They clarify this is meant as constructive feedback rather than criticism of the reftable work, which they actively support and are testing as promised.

This seems like a legitimate bug that would affect users migrating repositories with stashes. The report is well-documented with a clear reproduction script, though it lacks a proposed fix. The tone is constructive and acknowledges the broader context of reftable development.
