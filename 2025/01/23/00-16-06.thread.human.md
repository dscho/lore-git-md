This thread documents the addition of `http.sslCertType` and `http.sslKeyType` configuration options for HTTPS client certificate support. The options control certificate and private key formats, with "PEM", "DER", and "P12" supported for certificates, and "PEM", "DER", and the OpenSSL-specific "ENG" (for PKCS#11 tokens) supported for keys. The implementation maps to libcurl's underlying options while clearly documenting platform-specific limitations.
