The thread continues organizing Git's participation in Google Summer of Code 2025, with progress on both mentor recruitment and project planning. Kaartic Sivaraam remains the primary Org Admin, now joined by Christian Couder who has volunteered to both co-administer and mentor. 

Patrick Steinhardt has committed as a mentor and proposed three substantial projects focused on architectural improvements: unifying ref-related commands, refactoring environment.c to reduce global state (building on the ongoing `the_repository` elimination effort), and creating a machine-readable repository metadata command. Christian Couder has supplemented these with suggestions for smaller "microprojects" that could help onboard contributors, including work on the `the_repository` cleanup and addressing signed/unsigned comparison warnings in the codebase.

The project ideas page still needs updating from last year's content, and additional mentors may still be needed. Two upcoming meetups provide opportunities for coordination - one in Brussels on February 1st (previously mentioned) and a potential additional event in Madrid on January 29th. The contributor application period runs from March 24 to April 8.
