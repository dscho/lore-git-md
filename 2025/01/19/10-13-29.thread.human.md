The GSoC 2025 organization thread has been working through the details of how to document and maintain microprojects for the upcoming contributor program. After initial proposals from Kaartic Sivaraam and subsequent discussions between Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, the group has largely converged on a documentation system that balances visibility with maintainability.

Key agreements now in place include using "relevant/not relevant" status markers rather than "accurate" labels, clarifying that "owner" means primary contact rather than exclusive authority, and keeping the sign comparison warnings microproject with added notes about its complexity. The most recent development comes from Patrick Steinhardt, who has responded to Junio's proposal for mechanically expiring stale microproject entries. While agreeing with the automatic expiration concept, Patrick suggests that actual removals should go through the normal patch submission process, ensuring community members have visibility and can provide feedback before any microproject is retired. This refinement addresses concerns about abrupt removals while still enabling maintenance of the documentation.

With this addition, the thread appears to be reaching consensus on the documentation approach. The remaining open items include finalizing implementation details for the expiration system, resolving the status of two other debated microprojects (builtin patterns for userdiff and run_command*() replacements), and awaiting input from Christian Couder on the `the_repository` elimination microproject scope. Kaartic Sivaraam's response to these developments is also still pending, but the core documentation framework now seems largely settled.
