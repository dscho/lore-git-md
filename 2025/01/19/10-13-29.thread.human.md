This thread announces Git's participation in Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2025, with Kaartic Sivaraam continuing as Org Admin but stepping back from mentoring due to other commitments. The timeline is set, with organization applications due February 11 and contributor applications open March 24 through April 8. The community is asked to volunteer as mentors and help update the project ideas page, which still reflects 2024's suggestions. Sivaraam recommends offering projects of varying scope to accommodate different experience levels. 

A meetup is planned for February 1 at FOSDEM in Brussels to discuss Git's GSoC involvement; details are available upon request. The email notes the program remains largely unchanged from previous years and invites questions, serving as the annual call to organize Git's GSoC efforts.
