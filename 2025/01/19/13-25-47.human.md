This is part of a bugfix series addressing SIGBUS crashes on sparc64 when reading pack files. The patch modifies both `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` to use `get_be32()` for reading pack headers instead of direct struct access, ensuring proper alignment handling.

The change follows the same pattern as earlier patches in the series that fixed writing to pack headers. While the author considered factoring out the common logic, they found the interface would be too awkward given the different buffer management approaches in each command. The patch also introduces a `pack_version_ok_native()` helper to simplify version checking.

This appears to be a well-considered solution to a real alignment issue, completing the protection for both read and write operations. The changes are straightforward replacements of unsafe direct struct access with alignment-safe byte operations, maintaining the same functionality while fixing the sparc64 crash. The patch series as a whole shows careful attention to platform compatibility issues in Git's pack handling code.
