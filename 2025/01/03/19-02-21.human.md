Maintainer feedback on remote-prune maintenance task

Junio Hamano provides review comments on Shubham Kanodia's v3 patch adding a `prune-remote-refs` maintenance task. While the series has Junio's sign-off and is in his integration branch, he raises several technical concerns about the current form.

The key points center around two issues: First, the patch modifies prefetch task behavior (continuing after remote failures) without proper justification or separation from the new prune functionality. Junio suggests this should be split into two patches - one fixing prefetch behavior if warranted, and another introducing the prune task. Second, he critiques the error reporting helper function, noting internationalization problems, grammatical issues with single remotes, and questioning whether the consolidated error reporting adds value over the underlying commands' native output.

This appears to be final polish pass before merging - the core concept is approved, but needs cleaner separation of concerns in the implementation. The error reporting discussion may lead to simplifications in the final version. The thread shows the patch is in late-stage review with the maintainer actively shaping final details.
