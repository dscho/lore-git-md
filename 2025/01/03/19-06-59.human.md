Performance follow-up on CI containerization. Jeff King provides timing measurements comparing Patrick Steinhardt's containerized GitHub Actions jobs against the current non-containerized runs. The data shows minimal impact - most jobs fall within a ~30 second variance of their previous run times, with some slightly faster and others marginally slower. The largest difference is a 37-second slowdown in the linux-sha256 job, but overall the numbers suggest container overhead is negligible in practice. This addresses Jeff's earlier question about potential performance impact, effectively clearing that concern for the containerization approach in patch 4/10 of Patrick's series. The results support proceeding with the change as proposed.
