Patrick Steinhardt's CI/build system modernization series has hit a snag with its rootless container implementation, though a solution may be at hand. The series, now at v4, aims to modernize Git's CI infrastructure with several approved components including SIGPIPE test verification (reviewed by Jeff King) and various CI platform updates. However, Johannes Schindelin identified a critical issue where the `builder` user in GitHub Actions' rootless containers lacks permissions to write debug logs and artifacts to the temporary directory, making test failures impossible to diagnose.

In a recent update, Schindelin revealed he has already submitted a fix for this permission problem through a separate pull request, though he didn't initially mention it in this thread. While the exact details of his solution aren't provided here, it appears to maintain the security benefits of non-root operation while restoring debuggability. This development suggests the blocker may soon be resolved, though the series will likely need a v5 to incorporate the fix. The other components of the series - including Windows fixes and broader CI modernization - remain ready and approved, unaffected by this container permission issue. With key contributors like Jeff King and Christian Couder already having signed off on other parts, the path forward now depends on verifying and integrating Schindelin's fix.
