Patrick Steinhardt's CI/build system modernization series (v4) has hit a significant snag in its final review. While the 10-patch series had previously addressed all feedback and was awaiting integration, Johannes Schindelin has identified a critical issue with the rootless container implementation. The problem occurs when tests fail - the non-root `builder` user can't write to GitHub Actions' temporary directories, preventing test logs and artifacts from being captured. This makes debugging CI failures impossible, a showstopper for practical use.

The series had previously incorporated all review feedback, including Jeff King's approval of the SIGPIPE test verification and Christian Couder's documentation suggestions. Schindelin's intervention introduces a new technical challenge that wasn't surfaced in earlier review rounds. He suggests the solution may involve adjusting group permissions for the temporary directories, but notes this requires further investigation. This issue puts the rootless execution approach in question and may necessitate a v5 revision, despite the series having otherwise completed its review cycle. The ball is now back in Patrick's court to address this last-minute blocker before the series can proceed to integration.
