This is a follow-up discussion in Patrick Steinhardt's CI modernization series, specifically about containerizing GitHub Actions jobs. Jeff King raises some practical considerations about the transition from direct machine pool execution to containerized jobs.

Jeff acknowledges the benefits of containerization (flexibility in choosing images, non-root execution) but shares two concerns from experience: potential dependency issues (citing a past problem with dynamically-linked node.js in containers) and possible runtime impacts from pulling Docker images. While he thinks these are likely manageable - noting GitHub's optimized container infrastructure - he suggests monitoring run times to verify there's no significant slowdown.

The exchange shows the project's careful approach to CI changes, balancing modernization with operational reliability. Jeff's comments reflect real-world operational experience that complements Patrick's systematic improvements, though he ultimately agrees the change seems reasonable to proceed with. This is the kind of practical review that helps catch edge cases in infrastructure changes.
