Bugfix follow-up regarding combined diff path handling. Wink Saville confirms Jeff King's proposed fix works for the `git diff-tree --cc` segfault when tracing is added, but raises deeper concerns about strbuf invariants in the combined diff machinery.

The immediate issue was that parent path strbufs in `combine-diff.c` were only initialized during certain conditions, causing crashes when trace output tried to access them. Jeff's solution properly guards these accesses. Wink notes they had to correct a typo (`combined_all_paths` vs `combine_all_paths`) when applying the fix.

More significantly, Wink points out this violates strbuf's documented invariant that `buf` should never be NULL, suggesting the underlying `combine_diff_path` management may have broader issues. They question whether other strbuf invariants might also be violated and whether this warrants a new bug report.

The email also touches on Git's bug tracking process, asking where active bugs are documented and whether mailing list discussions suffice for initial reporting. This suggests the thread may expand into both technical discussion of strbuf handling and process questions about Git's issue management.
