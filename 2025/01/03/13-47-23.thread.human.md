Jean-Noël Avila has completed his three-part series standardizing the git-commit documentation to match Git's evolving AsciiDoc conventions. The series began by reformatting the main git-commit.txt man page, followed by the related config/commit.txt file, and now concludes with updates to i18n.txt and signoff-option.txt. 

Each patch applied mechanical formatting improvements: converting command and option references to backtick-wrapped format, standardizing placeholder notation, and introducing conditional AsciiDoc directives where needed. The changes were purely presentational, with no functional modifications to the documentation content. The final patch made minor adjustments to just 6 lines across the remaining files, maintaining the same careful attention to detail shown throughout the series.

This work continues Jean-Noël's broader effort to modernize Git's documentation presentation, following established patterns from prior accepted documentation updates. The series received no technical objections and can be considered a straightforward completion of this documentation standardization effort.
