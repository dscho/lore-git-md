CI modernization series continuation (part 6/10). This patch removes the redundant "linux-old" job from GitLab CI configuration, which had lost its original purpose of testing against the oldest supported Ubuntu LTS after being updated to Ubuntu 20.04 (same as other jobs). The change eliminates 3 lines from .gitlab-ci.yml by dropping the matrix entry for this job.

The removal is justified by:
1. The job no longer tests unique platform combinations (Ubuntu 20.04 is already covered elsewhere)
2. Maintains the series' focus on eliminating redundant/obsolete CI configurations
3. Follows the pattern of previous patches in streamlining the test matrix

This continues Patrick Steinhardt's systematic cleanup of Git's CI infrastructure, now totaling 10 patches in the series. The change is straightforward with no expected compatibility impact since the tested configuration is already covered by other jobs. No functional changes to test behavior - purely a configuration simplification.
