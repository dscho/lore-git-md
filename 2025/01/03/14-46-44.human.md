CI modernization: Adding 32-bit Linux testing to GitLab CI

This is the 7th patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to modernize Git's CI infrastructure. It adds a 32-bit Linux (i386) test job to GitLab CI, matching what's already present in GitHub Actions workflows.

The change is straightforward: it adds a new job definition using the i386/ubuntu:20.04 container image to the GitLab CI matrix, and updates the architecture detection in ci/lib.sh to recognize this image. The commit message notes this brings parity between the two CI systems.

This continues the series' pattern of aligning and standardizing CI configurations across platforms. The patch is small (3 lines net change) and focused, following the established approach of the previous patches. As with the rest of the series, it demonstrates careful attention to maintaining consistency while expanding test coverage.

The change appears well-justified given the series' goals, though the commit message could perhaps explain why 32-bit testing is valuable to maintain (beyond just matching GitHub's setup). No technical concerns are apparent in the implementation.
