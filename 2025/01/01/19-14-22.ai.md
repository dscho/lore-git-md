This is a performance/CI patch addressing a race condition in LSan (LeakSanitizer) thread handling. Jeff King investigates an unexpected interaction between Git's test suite and LSan's stack unwinding that causes false positive leak reports in threaded programs.

The key discovery is that the `fast_unwind_on_malloc=0` LSan option, added in 71f26798f2 to improve backtrace quality, triggers a race condition that manifests as false leak reports. Jeff demonstrates this with a minimal reproduction and shows the issue disappears when removing this option. He explores several solutions:

1. Remove `fast_unwind_on_malloc=0` entirely (simplest but degrades backtraces)
2. Keep it by default but disable in CI (preserves local debugging)
3. Per-test granular control (complex to maintain)
4. Filter out known false positives (current proposal)

The proposed solution (option 4) adds LSan's `exitcode=0` to suppress failures while maintaining the better stack traces, combined with log filtering to ignore the specific false positive pattern from `GetThreadStackTopAndBottom`. This approach avoids both the original race and the barrier workarounds from the merged series while preserving diagnostic quality.

The patch touches test-lib.sh's LSan configuration and leak detection logic. It relates to the recently merged thread-utils barrier work (jk/lsan-race-with-barrier) but offers a potentially better solution. The change is low-risk but involves tradeoffs between diagnostic quality and reliability that may warrant discussion.
