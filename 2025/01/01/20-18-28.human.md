Performance optimization for LSan leak detection in tests. This is patch 5/6 in Jeff King's series addressing false positives in threaded test execution under LeakSanitizer.

The patch simplifies leak detection by changing the test infrastructure to look specifically for `DEDUP_TOKEN` lines in LSan output rather than trying to filter out uninteresting lines. Since LSan always includes these tokens when reporting leaks (due to the `dedup_token_length` option), this provides a more reliable way to detect real leaks while being less prone to false positives from noisy output.

The change is minimal - just a one-line modification to `test-lib.sh` that replaces a complex `grep -qv` filter with a simple `grep -q` for the token. This builds on earlier work that separated test output directories and removed ineffective thread synchronization barriers, continuing the series' trend toward more robust leak detection.

The approach appears well-considered, leveraging LSan's built-in deduplication feature rather than maintaining custom filters. This should make the test infrastructure both more reliable and easier to extend with additional false-positive detection in the future.
