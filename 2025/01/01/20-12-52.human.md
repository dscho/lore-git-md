Performance optimization reverting a previous attempt to fix LSan race conditions in threaded index-pack operations. This is the second patch in a 6-part series addressing LeakSanitizer false positives during parallel test execution.

Jeff King reverts commit 993d38a ("index-pack: spawn threads atomically"), which attempted to prevent LSan race conditions by ensuring all pthread_create() calls completed before any thread could trigger exit(). The patch explains this approach was insufficient because LSan's thread setup happens within each thread's execution, not during thread creation. The race can still be easily reproduced with `make SANITIZE=leak` followed by stress testing t5309-pack-delta-cycles.sh.

The change removes thread synchronization barriers (work_lock/work_unlock) from index-pack's delta resolution code, returning to the simpler pre-atomic spawning behavior. This revert clears the way for a more comprehensive solution to the LSan race conditions that will be introduced later in the series. The patch is straightforward - just a 2-line deletion removing the locking calls - but represents an important step in properly diagnosing and fixing the underlying threading issues affecting LSan's leak detection.
