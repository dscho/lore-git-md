This is a performance-related patch that reverts a previous attempt to fix LSan (LeakSanitizer) race conditions in threaded index-pack operations. It's part 2 of a 6-patch series addressing LSan false positives during test execution.

The patch removes thread synchronization barriers (`work_lock()`/`work_unlock()`) that were added in commit 993d38a066 to try preventing race conditions between LSan's stack unwinding and thread exit. The author explains this approach was insufficient because the problematic setup occurs in thread execution rather than thread creation. The revert demonstrates the issue can still be triggered via stress testing with `t5309-pack-delta-cycles.sh`.

The change affects `builtin/index-pack.c`, specifically the delta resolution path where worker threads are spawned. The diff shows removal of the locking calls around `pthread_create()`, returning to the original thread spawning pattern. This is an intermediate step in the series - the commit message hints at a forthcoming alternative solution after this revert.
