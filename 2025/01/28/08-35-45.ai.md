This is a follow-up refinement to the zlib-ng optimization series, specifically addressing a const-cast issue in the zlib compatibility layer. The patch adds an explanatory comment about why the cast is needed when assigning between zlib and zlib-ng's differently-typed `next_in` pointers.

The key technical detail is that zlib-ng marks its `next_in` pointer as const while standard zlib does not, requiring an explicit cast in the compatibility layer's `zlib_post_call()` function. Justin Tobler had suggested adding a comment to clarify this non-obvious cast, which Patrick Steinhardt agrees in this response.
