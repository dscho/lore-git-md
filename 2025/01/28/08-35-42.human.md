Performance optimization (zlib-ng series) - This is part of the ongoing zlib-ng optimization series, specifically addressing compatibility stubs for older zlib versions.

The patch adds no-op stubs for `deflateSetHeader()` and `gz_header_s` when building with zlib versions older than 1.2.2.1. Justin Tobler suggested adding documentation to clarify the no-op behavior, which Patrick Steinhardt agrees would be helpful given the non-obvious nature of safely skipping this logic.

This is a small but important piece of the larger zlib-ng integration effort, ensuring backward compatibility while allowing the performance benefits of the new backend. The series has undergone thorough review rounds, and this particular patch represents one of the final refinements based on reviewer feedback. The implementation maintains the series' high quality standards with careful attention to compatibility details.
