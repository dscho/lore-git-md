This is part of the ongoing reftable library refactoring effort to make it fully portable and independent from Git's core infrastructure. Patch 9/20 in the series focuses on standardizing big-endian conversion functions.

Patrick Steinhardt introduces reftable-specific wrappers for big-endian integer conversion (16, 24, 32, and 64-bit variants) to replace the mixed usage of Git-provided and reftable-provided functions. The patch removes the Git-specific implementations from reftable/basics.c and adds comprehensive inline functions in reftable/basics.h with consistent naming (`reftable_get_*`/`reftable_put_*`).

The changes propagate through the codebase, updating all call sites to use the new wrappers. The patch includes corresponding test updates in the unit test suite to verify the new implementations. This is a straightforward but important step in the larger decoupling effort, ensuring the reftable library can operate without Git's endian conversion utilities.
