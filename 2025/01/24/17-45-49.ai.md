This is a follow-up discussion in the pack-objects name hash algorithm series, now in its final stages before submission. Derrick Stolee responds to Taylor Blau's review of v3, confirming the decision to drop the proposed Version 3 hash algorithm from the series while keeping Version 2 as the clear improvement over the current behavior.

The key points from this email:
- Stolee acknowledges Blau's review comments (mostly cosmetic) and plans to send a v4 with those fixes on Monday unless further feedback emerges
- They agree with Blau's assessment that Version 2 provides clear improvements in most cases while Version 3's benefits were too situational to justify the maintenance cost
- The discussion highlights the importance of clear user guidance - keeping only Version 2 avoids the problem of users defaulting to "newer is better" when Version 3 might actually perform worse
- While removing Version 3 from the main series, Stolee notes keeping it in a personal fork for potential future reconsideration if better heuristics emerge

This email confirms the final technical direction of the series after thorough review, with only minor cleanup remaining before submission. The core architecture of versioned name hashing remains intact, now simplified to focus on the clearly beneficial Version 2 implementation.
