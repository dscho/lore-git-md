This is a follow-up discussion in the final stages of reviewing a performance optimization series for pack-objects delta compression. The thread has reached consensus on implementing version 2 of the name hash algorithm while dropping version 3.

Derrick Stolee responds to Taylor Blau's thorough review, agreeing with the assessment that version 3 of the name hash algorithm isn't worth maintaining at this time. The key points are that version 2 provides clear improvements over version 1 in most cases, while version 3's benefits are less certain and would burden users with unclear choices between versions. Stolee plans to send a v4 of the series with cosmetic updates and the removal of version 3, keeping it only in his personal fork for potential future consideration.

The exchange confirms the technical direction of the series is sound, with only minor cleanup remaining before final submission. Both maintainers appear satisfied with version 2 as the right balance between improved delta compression and maintainable code. The discussion shows this long-running effort to optimize pack-objects is nearing completion after careful consideration of the tradeoffs between different hashing approaches.
