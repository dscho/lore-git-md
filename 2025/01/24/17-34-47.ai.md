This is a follow-up discussion in the pack-objects name hash algorithm series, specifically addressing the proposed removal of version 3 (v3) from the patch series. The thread has reached consensus that v3 should be dropped due to its mixed performance impact and lack of clear heuristics for when it would be beneficial over version 2 (v2).

Derrick Stolee agrees with Taylor Blau's assessment that v2 provides clear improvements over v1 in most cases, while v3 shows inconsistent results - sometimes better but often worse than v2. The key points are:
1. There's no straightforward way to detect when v3 would outperform v2
2. The maintenance burden of supporting v3 long-term outweighs its benefits
3. The framework established in the series allows for potential future iterations if better heuristics emerge

The discussion confirms the series will proceed with only versions 1 (existing behavior) and 2 (improved path-component hashing), dropping the experimental v3 that focused purely on collision reduction. This represents the final decision point for the series' scope before potential integration.
