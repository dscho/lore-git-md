Jeff King (Peff) has proposed a third approach that represents a middle ground between earlier solutions. His latest patch eliminates the format->bases string_list entirely by storing commit references directly in the individual atom structs (used_atom.u.base.commit). While this doesn't fully resolve the architectural questions about where this state ultimately belongs (ref_format vs ref_filter vs atom structs), it removes one problematic data structure while maintaining the same calculation logic. King acknowledges this may be a transitional solution, noting it keeps the door open for future refactoring.
