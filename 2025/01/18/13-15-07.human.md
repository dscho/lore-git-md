This is a follow-up to a bugfix series addressing unaligned memory access in packfile handling. Jeff King (peff) notes that he plans to revise the patch series to incorporate Junio's sparse fix suggestion and add another patch for proper 32-bit big-endian reading. He also solicits feedback on whether to simply align the buffers instead of the current approach.

The original series (now in 'next') fixed potential unaligned memory accesses in `index-pack` and `unpack-objects` by refactoring pack header parsing to avoid magic numbers and unaligned writes. This appears to be a solid technical improvement to prevent potential platform-specific issues, though the author is still considering alternative implementations.

The email suggests this is still a work in progress rather than ready for final merging, with the author actively seeking design input on the best approach before proceeding. The changes touch low-level packfile handling code but don't appear to affect user-visible behavior.
