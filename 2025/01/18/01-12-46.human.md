This is a follow-up discussion about the final design decision for the `help.autocorrect` boolean handling, coming from Junio Hamano as the maintainer. 

Junio acknowledges that Jeff King's suggested approach (where "0" would behave like other boolean falses by showing but not running the corrected command, while reserving "never" for the absolute no-op case) is more logical than the current v4 implementation. He takes responsibility for the current state, calling it his fault, and praises Jeff's careful review.

The email indicates the v4 patch series is in a "stuck state" with this inconsistency, and Junio suggests the behavior should be updated to match Jeff's more natural proposal. However, he notes this would require another revision (v5) and makes no firm commitment to produce it himself, saying only "if I find time".

This appears to be the final word on the technical design from the maintainer, effectively blessing Jeff's approach as the correct resolution, though leaving the actual implementation update as a TODO item. The thread had previously reached consensus on all other aspects, making this the last outstanding issue before merging.
