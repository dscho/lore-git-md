Bugfix for credential-cache's authtype capability handling, now at v5. This patch fixes a case where the credential-cache helper was incorrectly sending authtype and credential data even when the requesting client didn't specify the capability to receive them. The change aligns the behavior with Git's documented expectations in git-credential.txt.

The technical change is straightforward - replacing two CREDENTIAL_OP_HELPER checks with CREDENTIAL_OP_RESPONSE in the credential-cache daemon code. A new test case demonstrates the fix by showing how "credential fill" would previously print incomplete credentials when receiving unexpected authtype data.

The patch has gone through several rounds of review, with Junio Hamano suggesting improvements to the commit message to better explain the rationale and impact. Version 5 incorporates this feedback with a more detailed message that clearly connects the change to the documented behavior. The patch appears technically sound and well-tested, addressing all previous review comments.

This seems ready for merging, as it properly implements capability negotiation in the credential-cache helper while maintaining backward compatibility. The change is part of PR #1842 and has been through thorough review iterations.
