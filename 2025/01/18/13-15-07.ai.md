This is a follow-up in the thread about alignment fixes for pack header parsing, which was previously classified as a bugfix targeting potential unaligned memory access in `index-pack` and `unpack-objects`.

Jeff King responds to Junio Hamano's "What's Cooking" report where the original patch series was marked as ready to merge to 'next'. He indicates he plans to reroll the series with two additions: incorporating Junio's sparse fix and adding another patch to handle `get_be32()` on the reading side. He also solicits feedback on whether simply aligning the buffers might be a better approach, noting he's currently undecided on that strategy.

The original series (3 commits) had already addressed the unaligned access issue by:
1. Using `skip_prefix` to avoid magic numbers
2. Fixing unaligned writes in `parse_pack_header_option()`
3. Factoring out pack header argument parsing

This follow-up suggests the fix may expand in scope, potentially changing the approach from careful access patterns to buffer alignment. The discussion remains technical and focused on low-level memory handling in packfile processing code.
