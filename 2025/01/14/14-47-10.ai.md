The change is minimal - just a single line added to Documentation/technical/meson.build to declare the dependency. This follows up on v2 where an incorrect dependency was removed after feedback from Patrick. The issue manifests specifically in parallel builds where the documentation targets might try to build before their dependencies are ready.
