Performance optimization introducing zlib-ng support as an alternative zlib backend. This patch (8/10 in the series) adds build system support for linking against zlib-ng's prefixed API (`zng_*` symbols) while maintaining compatibility with standard zlib.

The patch enables a ~25% speedup in operations like `git cat-file --batch-all-objects` by leveraging zlib-ng's optimized implementations (SSE2/AVX2). It handles the common distribution case where zlib-ng is installed without replacing system zlib by implementing symbol remapping in `compat/zlib-compat.h`. Both Makefile and meson build systems are updated to support the new `ZLIB_NG` option.

The implementation is thorough, maintaining backward compatibility while adding the new optimization path. The series has been carefully reviewed and appears ready for integration, with all major concerns addressed in previous iterations. This represents the final piece of infrastructure needed to make the zlib-ng optimization available to users.
