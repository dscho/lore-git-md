This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing performance optimization series introducing zlib-ng support. The patch focuses on cleaning up zlib header includes as part of the broader effort to enable runtime switching between standard zlib and the optimized zlib-ng backend.

The change moves the zlib compatibility header include from the broad `git-compat-util.h` to the more specific `git-zlib.h`, which better reflects actual usage patterns. This is a logical refactoring since only a handful of files actually need zlib functionality. The patch carefully handles the reftable subsystem as a special case, maintaining its independence from Git's zlib wrapper by moving its include to `reftable/system.h`.

The changes are mechanical but important for the overall architecture - they help isolate zlib dependencies while preserving the ability for reftable to be used independently. This continues the series' pattern of thoughtful, incremental improvements to the zlib integration layer. The patch appears well-considered with no obvious issues, and follows naturally from the earlier infrastructure work in the series.
