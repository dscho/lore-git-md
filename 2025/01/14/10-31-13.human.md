This is a follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing Meson build system conversion series, specifically addressing fuzzer integration. The patch itself (which was previously reviewed) adds Meson support for building Git's fuzzers, which was one of the known gaps in the initial Meson implementation.

Junio Hamano notes this is important for achieving feature parity with the Makefile build system, suggesting a goal of full parity within two releases. Patrick confirms he's working on other missing features like static analysis and asks for input on whether to include "contrib/" and "gitk/" in the Meson conversion - he's inclined to skip "contrib/" but defers the gitk" decision to Johannes Schindelin.

The exchange shows steady progress on the Meson transition, with fuzzer support now addressed and attention turning to remaining components. The discussion remains collaborative, with clear maintainer guidance on priorities while leaving room for contributor judgment on less critical components.
