Review feedback on documentation standardization series (final patch). Patrick Steinhardt provides a stylistic critique of Jean-Noël Avila's v2 patch series converting git-commit documentation to AsciiDoc format, focusing on the fifth and final patch in the series. While approving the technical content ("the patch series looks fine"), Patrick suggests the commit messages could better explain the context and rationale behind the changes rather than just listing them bullet-point style. This echoes a theme from his v1 feedback where he previously commented on parameter notation.

The feedback comes after maintainer Junio Hamano has already approved patches 1 and 4 of this 5-part series, which systematically converts git-commit.txt and related files to use standardized AsciiDoc patterns like `[synopsis]` blocks and conditional includes. The series appears technically sound and ready for integration, with Patrick's comments being more about documentation style than substance. This marks the final piece of review feedback before likely merging of the documentation standardization effort.
