This is part 2 of a 5-patch series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, focusing on pack-writing functionality. The patch converts `index_pack_lockfile()` to take an explicit `struct repository*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository` variable.

The change affects several callers in `receive-pack.c` and `fetch-pack.c`, which now pass `the_repository` explicitly. The function also switches from using the global `the_hash_algo` to accessing the hash algorithm through the repository parameter. While this still uses globals at the caller level, it follows the incremental approach of first making dependencies explicit before eventually removing them.

The patch is a straightforward mechanical conversion that maintains existing behavior while moving toward the long-term goal of eliminating global state. It follows the established pattern seen in similar refactorings across the codebase. The changes are limited to pack-related functionality and don't introduce any new concepts or complications.
