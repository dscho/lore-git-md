This is a review comment on the final patch in Jean-Noël Avila's 5-part series standardizing the git-commit documentation format. Patrick Steinhardt offers feedback on the series as a whole, noting that while the technical content looks fine, the commit messages could provide more context about the changes' purpose and benefits rather than just listing mechanical changes. 

The series has already received maintainer approval for its core approach (patches 1 and 4 were approved by Junio Hamano), and this final patch completes the conversion of supporting files to the new AsciiDoc format. Patrick's comment suggests the work is technically sound but could better document its rationale for future readers. Given the prior approvals and lack of substantive objections, this appears to be minor polish feedback on an otherwise ready-to-merge documentation standardization effort.
