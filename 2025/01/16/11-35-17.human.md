This is the fifth and final patch in a series continuing the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting pack-writing functionality. The patch converts the `write_rev_*()` and related mtimes functions in `pack-write.c` to take an explicit `const struct git_hash_algo*` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_hash_algo`.

The changes are mechanical and consistent with the series' approach - each function up the call chain is updated to pass through the hash algorithm parameter. The patch affects pack index writing in several commands including `index-pack` and `pack-objects`, though most callers simply pass through `the_hash_algo` for now. The midx-write case is handled specially since that subsystem has already been converted.

As with previous patches in the series, this is a straightforward refactoring with no behavior changes. The patch removes the last `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from pack-write.c, marking completion of this portion of the global variable elimination effort. The changes are well-contained and follow the established pattern seen in earlier patches, making this a low-risk conclusion to the series.
