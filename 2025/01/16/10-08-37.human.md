This is part 5 of a 10-patch refactoring series addressing compiler warnings and type correctness in the reftable codebase. This change propagates through the codebase, updating all related functions and callers to use the new unsigned type.

The change is straightforward and well-justified - hash sizes are inherently unsigned quantities, and the patch removes now-unnecessary assertions that previously guarded against negative values. The modifications span multiple reftable files including basics.c, block.c, reader.c, and record.c, with corresponding updates to test code.
