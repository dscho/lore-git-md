Test modernization converting priority queue tests to Clar framework, part of a larger series converting Git's unit tests. This patch (3/4) focuses on `t-prio-queue.c` → `u-prio-queue.c` conversion, following the same pattern as previous conversions in the series.

The conversion preserves all test coverage while removing an unnecessary cast that became redundant after the type change.
