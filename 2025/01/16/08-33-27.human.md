This is a follow-up discussion in the zlib-ng performance optimization series, specifically about header inclusion strategies for the zlib compatibility layer. The exchange clarifies a technical detail about why the new header was named "zlib-compat.h" rather than "zlib.h" - it's to avoid potential conflicts with system zlib headers on MinGW/Windows platforms where "compat/" is in the compiler's include path (as configured in config.mak.uname).

Karthik Nayak had initially missed this platform-specific detail, and Patrick Steinhardt points out where this configuration lives. The discussion confirms the header naming choice was intentional and correct, addressing a subtle but important compatibility consideration.
