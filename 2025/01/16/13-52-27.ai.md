Performance optimization discussion for `git bisect`'s quadratic behavior in large repositories. This email continues Jeff King's analysis of the `do_find_bisection()` performance issues, specifically addressing algorithmic approaches to solve the O(n²) problem.

Jeff King retracts his earlier suggestion of using a simple reverse-topological order traversal, noting it would double-count commits reachable from multiple parents. He draws a parallel to Git's reachability bitmap computation, which solves a similar problem (counting unique reachable commits) through a single complex traversal. While acknowledging this approach is heavyweight, he suggests it demonstrates the possibility of a more efficient solution than the current implementation.

The email indicates that while the previously proposed patch (limiting flag clearing) provides measurable improvement, a complete solution would require significant algorithmic changes to the bisection counting logic. The comparison to bitmap computation suggests potential directions but also the complexity involved in implementing them correctly. This appears to be setting up future discussion about whether to pursue deeper architectural changes versus accepting the incremental optimization.
