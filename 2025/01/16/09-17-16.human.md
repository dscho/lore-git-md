This is part 3/10 of the performance optimization series introducing zlib-ng support. The patch creates a new compatibility layer for zlib by introducing "compat/zlib-compat.h" as a stepping stone for the zlib-ng integration.

The change is straightforward - it replaces direct `#include <zlib.h>` statements with the new compatibility header, which currently just includes the system zlib header. This indirection will allow the next patches to transparently switch between zlib and zlib-ng implementations. The header is carefully named "zlib-compat.h" rather than "zlib.h" to avoid potential include path conflicts.

The patch touches three files: creating the new header and updating two existing files to use it (git-compat-util.h and reftable/block.c). This appears to be a well-contained change that follows the series' goal of abstracting zlib access while maintaining compatibility. The commit message clearly explains the naming choice for the header, showing thoughtful consideration of potential build system interactions.
