This is a continuation of the ongoing standardization of Git's help output behavior, specifically addressing the parse-options vs direct usage() caller dichotomy. Junio Hamano responds to Jeff King's earlier analysis by noting that while some commands can be converted to use parse-options' built-in -h handling (as demonstrated with `git branch`), approximately 40 commands require special handling because they don't use parse-options at all. These commands implement their own -h checks and call usage() directly, making them a distinct category that needs separate attention in the standardization effort.

Junio reveals he's preparing a 6-patch series to address these cases, suggesting concrete progress is being made on this parallel track of the parse-options conversions he's already working on. The email highlights an important distinction in the codebase between commands that can be mechanically converted versus those requiring more fundamental changes due to their architecture. This reinforces the thread's emerging understanding that the standardization effort involves multiple technical approaches for different command types.
