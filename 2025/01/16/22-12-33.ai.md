This is the final patch in a 10-part series cleaning up sign-compare warnings in the reftable codebase. It addresses the last few trivial cases where signed/unsigned comparisons were flagged by the compiler, and removes the now-unneeded `DISABLE_SIGN_COMPARE_WARNINGS` macro from system.h.

The changes are straightforward type adjustments to match existing unsigned variables (like changing loop counters to `size_t` where they index into `size_t`-length arrays). Junio notes one potentially questionable change where `common_prefix_arg.max` is converted to `size_t` to match other changes in the series, though he observes this might be following questionable type choices elsewhere rather than being inherently correct. The patch appears to complete the series' goal of eliminating sign-compare warnings while maintaining consistency with prior type changes in the codebase.

Key technical details:
- Final removal of warning suppression infrastructure (`DISABLE_SIGN_COMPARE_WARNINGS`)
- Loop counters in stack.c updated to `size_t` to match `readers_len`
- `common_prefix_arg.max` changed to `size_t` (though this may be debatable)
- String length handling in record.c uses proper `size_t` for `strlen()` results
- The changes span stack.c, writer.c and record.c
- Builds on prior patches that changed various size-related functions in the reftable codebase

The patch appears to successfully conclude the series' type-safety work, though one change may warrant closer review regarding whether `size_t` is truly appropriate for the common prefix length tracking.
