This is a bugfix patch in a series addressing packed-refs validation issues. Patrick Steinhardt is reviewing patch 5/10, which adds detection of embedded NUL characters in packed refnames. The original patch from shejialuo identified that while `check_refname_format()` validates refnames, it could miss embedded NULs if the truncated refname remains valid. The solution adds a `refname_contains_null()` helper that checks for length mismatches between the strbuf and its string representation.

Patrick suggests two improvements: (1) correcting terminology from "NULL binaries" to "NUL characters" for technical accuracy, and (2) replacing the strlen-based check with a more efficient `memchr()` call. He also expresses initial surprise that the fix modifies packed-refs reading rather than fsck code, but ultimately agrees the approach is sensible. The change affects packed-backend.c's refname parsing logic, specifically in the `next_record()` function where refnames are extracted from the packed-refs file. This continues the thread's theme of hardening packed-refs validation against malformed input.
