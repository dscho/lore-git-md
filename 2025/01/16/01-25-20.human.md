This is part 3 of a 6-part series standardizing Git's help text output behavior. The patch converts 13 builtin commands to use the new `show_usage_help_and_exit_if_asked()` helper function, ensuring their `-h` help output goes to stdout rather than stderr. 

The changes are mechanical but widespread, touching commands like `git commit`, `git merge`, `git rebase`, and `git gc`. Each conversion follows the same pattern: replacing direct `usage_with_options()` calls with the new helper. The patch also notes that a test in t7600 for `git merge -h` could potentially be retired since the behavior is now covered by the more general t0012 test.

This continues the series' careful approach to maintaining backward compatibility while moving toward consistent behavior. The changes are straightforward and well-contained, building on the infrastructure established in earlier patches. The series appears to be progressing smoothly with no objections raised so far.
