This is part 4/10 of a performance optimization series introducing zlib-ng support. The patch refactors zlib header includes to be more targeted, moving them from the broad `git-compat-util.h` to the more specific `git-zlib.h` where they're actually needed.

The change makes the codebase cleaner by reducing unnecessary includes in `git-compat-util.h`, which is included nearly everywhere. The reftable library gets special treatment - it includes zlib directly via `reftable/system.h` to maintain independence from Git's wrapper. This is a sensible architectural improvement that helps keep the reftable codebase more self-contained.

The patch touches several files that use zlib functionality (archive.c, config.c, etc.), adding explicit includes where needed. The changes are mechanical and straightforward, just moving includes to more appropriate locations. This is part of the larger zlib-ng integration effort that's been through multiple review rounds and appears ready for merging.
