Refactoring patch in the reftable warning cleanup series. This is the second of ten patches addressing compiler warnings as part of making reftable a standalone library.

The patch removes an unused `print` function pointer from the reftable record vtable structure. The functionality was previously inlined into its only caller in commit 42c424d69d (August 2024) but the vtable entry wasn't cleaned up at that time. The change is straightforward - just deleting three lines from record.h that defined the now-unused debugging interface.

This appears to be a simple cleanup that logically follows the earlier refactoring, though it's not directly related to the series' main focus on signed/unsigned comparison warnings. The change is minimal and low-risk, touching only header definitions rather than any runtime behavior. As with the rest of the series, this helps streamline the reftable code in preparation for its eventual extraction as a standalone library.
