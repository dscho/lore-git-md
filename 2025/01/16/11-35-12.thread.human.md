This thread documents a completed 5-patch series that eliminates global variable usage in Git's pack-writing functionality as part of the long-running `the_repository` removal effort. The series, authored by Karthik Nayak, systematically converts functions in `pack-write.c` and related areas to explicitly take repository contexts and hash algorithm parameters rather than relying on the `the_repository` and `hash_algo` globals.

The technical work was largely complete by v2, with the v3 iteration focusing on documentation refinements based on feedback from Toon Claes and Patrick Steinhardt. Key improvements include clarifying terminology around passing the full `git_hash_algo` struct (rather than just "the hash function") and fixing ambiguous wording in patch 5/5 about parameter flow. The series maintains behavioral compatibility while making the codebase more maintainable, with upper layers temporarily still passing `the_hash_algo` where needed.

Reviewers have been particularly attentive to technical precision in the commit messages, with Toon Claes providing consistent documentation review across multiple patches and Junio Hamano confirming the approach as a pragmatic foundation for future work. The series builds on Git 2.48 and completes the conversion of `pack-write.c`, following prior cleanup of `write-midx.c` in this multi-year effort to eliminate global state from Git's codebase.
