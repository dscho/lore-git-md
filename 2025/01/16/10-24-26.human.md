This is a technical discussion in the ongoing thread about standardizing Git's help output behavior. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Junio Hamano's thoughts about implementing the transition to stdout for help messages.

The exchange focuses on practical implementation details of the standardization effort. Peff acknowledges Junio's point about potentially overzealous manual `-h` checks in the codebase, agreeing that many of the 40 commands with manual checks may not actually need them, but notes that auditing each one would require significant effort for limited benefit.

They also discuss the strictness of test enforcement, with Peff's initial test patch requiring empty stderr for help output. Junio suggests this might be overly strict (since other output like deprecation notices could appear), but both agree to handle any such cases as they arise rather than preemptively complicating the test infrastructure.

The conversation shows the maintainers working through edge cases in this large-scale refactoring, balancing thoroughness with practicality as they convert Git's help output behavior to the new stdout standard.
