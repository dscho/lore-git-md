Follow-up review of the `git backfill` implementation. Patrick Steinhardt provides additional feedback on patch 2/5 of Derrick Stolee's series introducing the new command. The review focuses on implementation details of the blob-fetching functionality, suggesting two potential improvements: using `repo_has_object_file()` for object existence checks rather than the current manual approach, and moving context cleanup to the caller for better separation of concerns. Patrick also notes that some field initialization appears redundant since `PATH_WALK_INFO_INIT` already handles it. The tone remains constructive, building on Patrick's earlier positive assessment of the series while pointing out minor technical nits. This continues the productive review of what appears to be a well-structured new command implementation.
