Junio Hamano follows up on the ongoing effort to standardize Git's help output behavior, noting that some of the 40 commands with manual `-h` checks genuinely need them because they don't use parse-options at all. This is a technical clarification in the thread about moving help output from stderr to stdout, where most discussion has focused on parse-options users. Hamano observes that while many commands (like `git branch`) were unnecessarily doing manual checks, others have no alternative since they bypass parse-options entirely. He mentions preparing a 6-patch series to address this, suggesting we'll soon see concrete progress on this subset of commands. This moves the standardization effort forward by properly scoping the remaining work between parse-options conversions (already underway) and these special cases that require different handling.
