Performance optimization for `git bisect` on large repositories. Jeff King follows up on his earlier analysis of the quadratic-time behavior in `do_find_bisection()` with a concrete patch that reduces the Linux kernel bisection time from ~17 minutes to ~9.5 minutes. The change modifies `clear_distance()` to only clear flags from reachable commits rather than the entire list, matching the traversal pattern of `count_distance()`.

The patch demonstrates the suspected optimization works as hoped, though the fundamental O(n²) behavior remains. King notes the algorithm might need a complete rewrite using reverse-topo ordering for further gains, but considers that a more involved change. He also shares a handy command (`git rev-list --bisect`) for benchmarking the core bisection logic independently.

This represents progress from analysis to implementation, showing measurable improvement while acknowledging deeper architectural work may still be needed. The change is narrowly focused on the flag-clearing behavior identified as a key bottleneck in the previous discussion.
