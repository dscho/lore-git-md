This is a discussion about deprecation warning mechanisms in Git, specifically responding to the proposed environment variable approach for silencing warnings about legacy reference names.

Robert Coup weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt's proposal to use `GIT_ALLOW_DEPRECATED_REMOTES` for suppressing warnings about the "branches/" and "remotes/" naming conventions. While supportive of the general idea, Coup suggests a more scalable approach that could handle multiple future deprecations - either a single environment variable with a colon-separated list of warnings to ignore (like `GIT_IGNORE_DEPRECATION_WARNINGS=remotes:splines:llamas`) or a config-based solution.

The suggestion comes as part of the ongoing discussion about how to best handle the transition period before Git 3.0 removes these legacy features. Coup's proposal aims to avoid proliferation of individual environment variables as more deprecations are announced. The tone is constructive, offering an alternative implementation approach while maintaining the thread's focus on user experience during the deprecation process.
