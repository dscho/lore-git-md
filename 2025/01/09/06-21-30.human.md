Bugfix for `git blame` addressing an out-of-bounds read when using very large `--abbrev` values. Patrick Steinhardt fixes a regression introduced in December 2024 where switching from `printf` to `fwrite` for displaying abbreviated hashes caused Git to read past the end of object IDs when `--abbrev` exceeded the hash length. The fix reverts to using `printf` while capping the length at `GIT_MAX_HEXSZ` to prevent integer overflow.

The patch includes a test case that verifies the behavior with an absurdly large abbreviation length (9000 characters). This appears to be a straightforward correction of a clear bug, with proper attribution to Johannes Schindelin who reported the issue. The change is minimal (4 lines added, 1 changed) and well-contained to the blame output formatting logic.
