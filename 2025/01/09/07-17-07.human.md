This is a test fix addressing flakiness in the submodule test suite. The discussion centers around t7422, which tests SIGPIPE handling in submodule status output. The test was previously unreliable due to timing issues with buffered stdout.

Patrick Steinhardt proposes a solution that generates a large number of submodules (2.5k) to produce enough output to reliably test the SIGPIPE behavior, while keeping the setup efficient through direct use of plumbing commands like `git hash-object` and `git mktree`. Jeff King agrees this approach is better than his earlier attempts at workarounds, noting it's O(1) in process overhead despite the large data volume.

While both acknowledge the setup is non-trivial, they agree it's worth maintaining test coverage for the recent SIGPIPE fix (commit 082caf527e). The exchange shows careful consideration of test reliability versus complexity, with both maintainers converging on a robust solution. This appears in a v3 patch series that King plans to review.
