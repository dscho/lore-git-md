This thread documents improvements to Git's credential helper documentation, specifically addressing how to properly store personal access tokens (PATs). The original series from M Hickford identified that users often misuse the credential cache for long-lived PATs due to unclear documentation, leading to insecure workarounds. The v2 series restructures the documentation to clearly warn against this practice and point users toward secure alternatives.

The changes come in two parts: first, a reorganization of the "Available helpers" section in gitcredentials(7) to better distinguish built-in versus third-party credential helpers, and second, explicit warnings in git-credential-cache.txt about its unsuitability for PAT storage. The latest email completes the second patch, adding a dedicated "PERSONAL ACCESS TOKENS" section that explains why the cache helper's ephemeral nature (including vulnerability to daemon restarts) makes it inappropriate for PATs, while cross-referencing the persistent storage solutions documented in the first patch.
