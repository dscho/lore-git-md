Project organization for Git's GSoC 2025 participation. This email from Patrick Steinhardt responds to Christian Couder's suggestion about refreshing the list of microprojects (like `the_repository` removal and compiler warning cleanup) for this year's Google Summer of Code.

Patrick agrees these are good microproject candidates and proposes moving the project ideas list into the Git repository itself as "Documentation/Projects.txt". This would make the list more discoverable and easier to keep updated compared to the external website currently used. The file would serve as a living document of ongoing project priorities, though Patrick acknowledges it might not always be perfectly accurate.

This suggestion comes as the GSoC mentor team (now including Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak) is finalizing project scopes before the application period begins in March. The proposal to document projects in-tree reflects Git's broader effort to make contributor onboarding smoother while maintaining consensus about technical direction. The email doesn't introduce new technical work but rather suggests an improvement to how the project organizes and presents its ongoing initiatives to potential contributors.
