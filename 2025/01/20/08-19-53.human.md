GSoC 2025 organization update with new mentor volunteer. Christian Couder confirms he will serve as both mentor and Org Admin for Git's Google Summer of Code 2025 participation, joining Kaartic Sivaraam (Org Admin) and Patrick Steinhardt (mentor). He suggests expanding the microproject ideas list to include tasks related to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and compiler warning cleanup (-Wsign-compare). 

Christian also notes there may be an additional GSoC meetup in Madrid on January 29th alongside the previously announced Brussels event during FOSDEM. The email reinforces the key deadlines (Org applications due February 11, contributor applications March 24-April 8) and the need to refresh project ideas from last year's page.

This continues the thread's momentum with another experienced participant (Christian has mentored in previous years) stepping up, while highlighting opportunities to connect with potential contributors through both European meetups. The microproject suggestions align well with Git's current technical priorities.
