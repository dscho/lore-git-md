Refactoring patch in the reftable type-safety series. This is the second patch in a 10-part series cleaning up compiler warnings and improving type safety in the reftable codebase.

The patch removes an unused `print` function pointer from the `reftable_record_vtable` struct in record.h. This function was made obsolete by commit 42c424d69d which inlined the printing functionality directly into its single caller. The removal is straightforward - just deleting the unused vtable member and its associated comment.

This change is part of the larger effort to make reftable a standalone library, where cleaning up unused code and improving type safety are important preparatory steps. The patch follows naturally from the earlier inlining change and doesn't introduce any new behavior or complexity. Since it's part of a reviewed series where the overall approach has already been vetted, this particular change appears uncontroversial and well-justified.
