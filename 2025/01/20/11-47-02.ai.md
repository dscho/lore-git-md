This is the third patch in a series addressing reftable migration issues, specifically focusing on preventing update index modifications after header writing begins. The discussion centers on tightening the validation around `reftable_writer_set_limits()` to prevent subtle corruption.

Karthik Nayak responds to Patrick Steinhardt's suggestions for improving the patch's protection mechanism. The current version adds a BUG() check when limits are modified after writing begins, but Patrick proposed two stronger approaches: (1) validating that no records have been queued yet, or (2) eliminating `set_limits()` entirely by making indices required parameters in `writer_new()`. Karthik agrees with the direction and will explore implementing the second, more fundamental approach in the next version, though notes it may require some refactoring of the callback flow between `reftable_addition_add()` and `reftable_writer_new()`.

They also agree to change the error handling from BUG() to returning REFTABLE_API_ERROR for better library behavior and to avoid git-compat-util.h dependencies. The discussion shows technical alignment on hardening the API, with Karthik planning to implement the more comprehensive solution that would make the limits immutable by construction rather than adding runtime checks.
