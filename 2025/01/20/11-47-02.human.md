This is the third patch in a series addressing reftable corruption issues, specifically focusing on preventing unsafe modifications to update indices after header writing begins. The patch currently adds a BUG() check when `reftable_writer_set_limits()` is called too late, but Patrick Steinhardt suggests two improvements: either making the validation stricter by checking for any queued records, or eliminating `set_limits()` entirely by requiring indices at writer creation.

Karthik Nayak agrees with the direction and will explore both approaches, favoring the more fundamental solution of passing indices to `reftable_writer_new()`. He also accepts the recommendation to use REFTABLE_API_ERROR instead of BUG() for better library behavior. The discussion shows technical alignment on the core issue while leaving implementation details open for the next version. This patch appears well-motivated by real corruption scenarios and is progressing toward a robust solution.
