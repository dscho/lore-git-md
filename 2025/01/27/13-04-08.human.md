This is a major refactoring series aimed at decoupling the reftable library from Git's core codebase. Patrick Steinhardt proposes 19 patches to eliminate all dependencies on "git-compat-util.h" in the reftable code, replacing Git-specific helpers with open-coded alternatives or new POSIX-focused compatibility wrappers.

The primary motivation is to make the reftable library truly standalone, allowing other projects like libgit2 to use it by simply copying the source files. The series systematically removes Git-specific utilities (like `read_in_full`, `BUG()`, `COPY_ARRAY`) and introduces a new "compat/posix.h" header for POSIX-specific functionality that remains needed.

The changes are wide-ranging but focused - touching reftable internals, compatibility layers, and build system integration. The series appears well-structured, with each commit tackling a specific dependency removal. The final result should make the reftable codebase more portable while maintaining its current functionality.

This represents the culmination of ongoing efforts to make reftable a reusable library, and the changes look thorough and carefully considered. Developers working on reftable or interested in its potential use outside Git will want to review this series closely.
