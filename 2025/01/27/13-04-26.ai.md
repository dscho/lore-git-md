This is the 18th patch in a 19-part series decoupling the reftable library from Git's core infrastructure. The patch completes the architectural separation by removing the final direct dependency on `git-compat-util.h` and replacing it with the newly created `compat/posix.h` header introduced in patch 17.

The change is straightforward but significant - it switches the reftable library's base include from Git's compatibility utilities to the minimal POSIX compatibility layer that was extracted in the previous patch. The only remaining Git-specific components are `system.h` and `system.c`, which projects using reftable as a standalone library will need to provide their own implementations for. The patch maintains zlib support by keeping its include directive.

This represents the culmination of the series' systematic removal of Git dependencies, leaving reftable with just two small Git-specific components that can be easily replaced by downstream consumers. The changes are mechanical but complete the architectural goal of making reftable truly standalone while preserving all functionality through the new compatibility layers established in the series.
