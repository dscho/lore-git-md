This is the 18th patch in a 19-part series decoupling the reftable library from Git's core infrastructure to make it usable as a standalone library. The patch completes the separation by removing the final direct dependency on `git-compat-util.h` and replacing it with the new `compat/posix.h` header introduced in the previous patch.

The change is straightforward but significant - it switches the reftable library's base include from Git's compatibility utilities to a minimal POSIX compatibility layer. The only remaining Git-specific components are `system.h` and `system.c`, which projects using reftable will need to provide their own implementations for. The patch maintains all functionality while achieving the series' goal of making reftable truly independent.

This represents a major milestone in the effort to make reftable usable by other projects like libgit2. The mechanical but careful work throughout the series has systematically replaced Git dependencies with either direct POSIX calls or reftable-specific implementations, and this patch finalizes that architectural separation. The changes are well-contained and follow the established pattern of the series.
