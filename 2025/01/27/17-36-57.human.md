This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing reftable independence series, specifically addressing error handling in record initialization. 

Justin Tobler responds to Patrick Steinhardt's patch that replaces a `BUG()` call in `reftable_record_init()` with an error return, as part of removing Git-specific dependencies from the reftable library. The change makes the function return `REFTABLE_API_ERROR` when given an unknown record type rather than aborting the program.

Tobler initially questioned whether `REFTABLE_API_ERROR` was the most appropriate choice but concludes it fits the documented use case of "misuse of the API." He considers but dismisses the idea of adding a more specific error type for unsupported record types as unnecessary. 

This is a small but meaningful step in the larger effort to make reftable fully standalone, showing careful consideration of error handling semantics while maintaining functionality. The discussion appears technical and constructive, with no apparent points of contention remaining.
