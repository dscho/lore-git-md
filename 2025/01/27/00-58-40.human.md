This is a follow-up discussion in the zlib-ng optimization series, specifically about a const-cast fix in the zlib compatibility layer. Justin Tobler is reviewing Patrick Steinhardt's patch that addresses a constness mismatch between zlib and zlib-ng's `next_in` pointer handling.

The patch itself is a simple one-line change casting away constness when assigning between the zlib stream structures, which was necessary to make the code work with both zlib variants. While technically correct, Justin notes the change looks suspicious without context and suggests adding a comment to clarify why the cast is needed.

This is a minor polish suggestion on an otherwise appears to be a well-justified technical fix in the zlib-ng integration work. The series as a whole has already gone through extensive review and is in its final shape, with this being one of the last remaining small cleanups.
