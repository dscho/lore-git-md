This is part 10 of a 19-patch series refactoring the reftable library to remove dependencies on Git's core infrastructure. The series aims to make reftable fully portable for standalone use by other projects like libgit2.

This particular change is a straightforward mechanical conversion, replacing the Git-specific `ARRAY_SIZE()` macro with a direct calculation of array length (`sizeof(sections) / sizeof(*sections)`) in the reftable reader's block printing function. The patch is small (just one line changed) and follows the series' consistent pattern of removing Git-specific utilities while maintaining identical behavior.

The change affects debugging output functionality (`reftable_reader_print_blocks()`), not core reftable operations, so it's low-risk. Like previous patches in the series, this is a well-contained change with a clear motivation (reducing Git dependencies) and no apparent impact on functionality. The series as a whole appears to be methodically working through all Git-specific dependencies in the reftable codebase.
