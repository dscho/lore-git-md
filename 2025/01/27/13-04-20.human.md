This is part 12 of a 19-patch series refactoring the reftable library to remove Git-specific dependencies. The series systematically replaces Git utility functions with direct POSIX calls or reftable-specific implementations.

This patch replaces Git's `sleep_millisec()` helper with a direct call to `poll()` in reftable/stack.c. The change occurs in the code that handles retry delays when reloading the reftable stack, where a random delay is introduced between attempts. The author notes that while poll() isn't the most direct way to implement millisecond sleeps, it's the closest POSIX equivalent available.

The change is straightforward - just a one-line replacement that maintains identical behavior while continuing the series' goal of eliminating Git-specific dependencies. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by making focused, behavior-preserving changes to remove each dependency one at a time.
