The exchange reveals some tension between the document's server-side focus and Junio's request for more concrete client-side examples. Security considerations around protocol negotiation timing (clone vs fetch) are clarified - Christian notes fetches also perform negotiation, with lazy fetches potentially using tokens. He also adds nuance about when clients should be allowed to offload created (vs fetched) objects directly to LOPs.
