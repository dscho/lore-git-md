Meet Soni's GSoC project to centralize refspec-related functions in Git has reached its final stages with the v4 series. The work systematically moves functions from remote.c to refspec.c while maintaining exact behavior, following extensive review from both Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt.

The series has progressed through multiple iterations addressing technical restructuring, documentation, and naming conventions. Part 3/5 of v4, the latest addition, completes the naming standardization by renaming three key functions with clearer, subsystem-prefixed names: `query_refspecs` becomes `refspec_find_match`, `query_refspecs_multiple` becomes `refspec_find_all_matches`, and `query_matches_negative_refspec` becomes `refspec_find_negative_match`. These changes affect remote.c, remote.h, and builtin/push.c but introduce no behavioral changes.

Earlier parts of v4 already moved core matching functions to refspec.c (part 2/5) and implemented other agreed-upon naming conventions (part 1/5). The series has received consistent positive feedback, with Junio praising the documentation quality and Patrick providing detailed suggestions on naming and commit message style that have all been incorporated. With all technical and naming issues resolved, this well-executed refactoring appears ready for merging.
