Final patch in a series standardizing help text output behavior. This completes the conversion of remaining builtin commands to send `-h` help text to stdout rather than stderr, using the new `show_usage_if_asked()` helper introduced earlier in the series. The changes are mechanical replacements of direct `usage()` calls with the new helper function.

The patch updates 19 commands that had manual `-h` checks, including `diff-files`, `fast-import`, `index-pack`, and `rev-list`. It also tightens the test in t0012-help.sh to now strictly require empty stderr when checking help output, removing the previous lenient mode that accepted output on either stream.

Junio Hamano (the maintainer) is submitting this final patch after Jeff King's review and ack. The series has established consensus on both the architectural approach and the mechanical conversions, with all technical concerns addressed. This appears ready for merging as the last step in standardizing Git's help text behavior.
