This is the third and final patch in a series addressing reftable migration improvements, specifically focusing on preventing corruption when handling large reflogs. The patch fixes a subtle bug in the reftable writer where update index values could be modified after the header was written, potentially causing header/footer mismatches.

The change adds a simple but important safeguard in `reftable/writer.c` that throws an error if `reftable_writer_set_limits()` is called after the first block is written. This ensures the header and footer values remain consistent, addressing a potential source of corruption that could occur during reftable conversion.

The patch is small (just 7 lines added) but meaningful, completing the series' goal of hardening the reftable backend. It follows logically from the previous two patches (which dealt with encapsulation and integer sizing) by adding runtime protection against invalid state changes. The change appears well-considered and low-risk, with a clear downsides to the added protection.
