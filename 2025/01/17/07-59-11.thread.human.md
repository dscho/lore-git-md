This shows the careful attention being paid to both code structure and maintainer knowledge preservation.

The core technical improvements from the series remain uncontested, with Patrick now suggesting refinements to two aspects: the final patch's error handling approach (where he prefers making indices required parameters upfront rather than using BUG()), and this documentation preservation in the first patch. These are thoughtful enhancements being proposed by one of the reftable subsystem's primary maintainers, indicating the series is nearing its final form.
