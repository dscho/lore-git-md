Eric Sunshine spots and fixes a typo in Junio Hamano's recent documentation patch about command-line option precedence. This is a straightforward typo fix that doesn't alter the meaning of the documentation. It comes as a quick response to Junio's patch that formalized Git's CLI > config precedence rules, which was itself prompted by earlier discussion about `git grep` negatable options.
