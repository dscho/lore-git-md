This is a CI/build system patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing Meson conversion series (v2 10/11). The patch makes Meson warnings fatal in CI builds by adding the `--fatal-meson-warnings` flag to the setup command, ensuring build warnings don't go unnoticed.

The change is straightforward - a single line addition to `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh` that forces CI to fail when Meson emits warnings about unsupported features or other issues. Toon Claes confirms this would have caught his earlier mistake of using `fs.copyfile` (a Meson 0.64.0 feature) while targeting version 0.61.0, which currently only produces a warning.

This patch demonstrates good attention to detail in the Meson transition, proactively catching potential compatibility issues early. The change aligns with Git's quality standards by making warnings hard to miss in automated testing. As part of the larger series that's already received maintainer approval on technical merits, this appears to be a well-justified hardening of the CI process.
