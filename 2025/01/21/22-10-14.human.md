Test suite fix for printf format consistency in blame tests.

Junio Hamano is reviewing and accepting Jan Palus's patch that improves the printf format specifications in Git's blame test suite. The patch replaces ambiguous "%0.s" patterns with clearer "%11s" and "%${hexsz}s" formats for padding spaces in test expectations. 

The change was previously reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt (as noted in the thread summary) and now gets final approval from the maintainer. Junio notes the new version is "obviously much clearer" than the original approach of concatenating spaces, and confirms the unquoted $hexsz variable reference is safe in this context.

This is a straightforward technical improvement to make the test suite more robust across different shell implementations. The patch is minimal (just 2 lines changed) and maintains consistency with patterns used elsewhere in the test suite. With Junio's approval, this will likely be merged soon.
