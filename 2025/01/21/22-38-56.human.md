Junio Hamano weighs in on the discussion about batch removal of remotes, supporting Christian Fredrik Johnsen's proposal to extend `git remote remove` to accept multiple arguments. He counters the workaround suggestions from Emily Shaffer and D. Ben Knoble by highlighting practical limitations: shell loops become inefficient with many remotes (like 1000), and manual config editing risks mistakes while leaving remote-tracking branches behind. 

Hamano notes that while this wouldn't solve the extreme case of removing all remotes at once, it would make xargs-based solutions more efficient. He explicitly endorses the multiple-argument approach, drawing a parallel to `git branch --delete`'s existing behavior. The response shifts the discussion from "is this needed" toward implementation considerations, with the maintainer signaling openness to the change.
