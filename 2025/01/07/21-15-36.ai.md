Maintainer approval for `the_repository` refactoring series.

Junio Hamano, the Git maintainer, weighs in to approve Patrick Steinhardt's patch series that refactors progress subsystem usage of `the_repository`. This comes after reviewers Toon Claes and Karthik Nayak reached consensus on Patrick's incremental approach of bubbling up `the_repository` usage to subsystem boundaries rather than attempting more comprehensive dependency removal.

The key technical point is that this strategy maintains "bug-to-bug compatibility" - a conservative approach that exactly preserves existing behavior while moving the global variable usage to higher layers. Junio explicitly endorses this as safer than attempting concurrent behavioral improvements that might introduce regressions.

This maintainer approval marks the series as ready for integration into the `next` branch, concluding the design discussion that had earlier seen some divergence between incremental versus comprehensive approaches to `the_repository` removal. The resolution favors safety and verifiability over more aggressive dependency elimination, with all participants now aligned on this path forward for this phase of the broader refactoring effort.
