What began as a multi-patch series has been consolidated into a single, well-reviewed patch in its second iteration. The technical substance - converting test cases to use direct function definitions in the clar style while preserving all original test coverage - received approval from both Patrick Steinhardt (the clar framework champion) and maintainer Junio Hamano.

The final email from Seyi Kuforiji addresses the last remaining administrative details: fixing macro whitespace (adding back spaces between `while` and `(0)`) and ensuring proper alphabetical ordering in meson.build. These minor style nits were the only outstanding issues after Junio's review confirmed the technical correctness of the conversion. With all participants in agreement and these final tweaks promised, this work is now fully resolved and ready to be merged as part of Git's ongoing test infrastructure improvements.
