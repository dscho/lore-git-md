This is a brief follow-up in the thread about removing deprecated remote configuration mechanisms. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's historical note about the advantages of the old `.git/branches/` approach for users managing frequently-changing remotes. 

Patrick argues that the widespread unfamiliarity with these old mechanisms today suggests `git remote` has effectively replaced them in practice, though he's willing to reword the phrasing in documentation. The exchange highlights how historical workflow considerations factor into decisions about removing long-deprecated features, even when those features have fallen out of common use. This comes as the final patch in the series prepares to remove the `branches/` and `remotes/` directory-based configuration methods after 14-20 years of deprecation.
