Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's implementation of warning messages for the deprecated `remotes/` and `branches/` directory access. While agreeing with the warning approach, Junio suggests making the warnings less noisy by only triggering them when the legacy configurations are actually used, rather than just when the files are read. This refinement would prevent unnecessary warnings during operations that may check these files but don't ultimately rely on their contents.

The exchange shows the careful consideration being given to user experience during the deprecation process, balancing the need to notify users who still depend on these legacy features while avoiding spamming those who don't. This follows the earlier consensus in the thread about implementing robust warning systems before removing long-deprecated features.
