Proposal feedback on git-request-get script. Junio Hamano responds to Matěj Cepl's RFC for a new `git-request-get` companion to `git-request-pull`, offering several key pieces of feedback before the proposal can move forward. 

First, he critiques the presentation - linking to an 8-year-old GitLab issue as context makes it hard for reviewers to understand the problem being solved. The proposal needs to stand alone with clear motivation. Second, he notes the implementation needs to follow Git's existing patterns for Perl scripts (naming conventions, Makefile integration) rather than creating a new Makefile. Third, he suggests practical testing advice - the author should test the patch by emailing it to themselves and applying with `git am` to verify it works with Git's existing build system.

The response highlights several technical concerns: the debug logging approach may not align with Git's test harness, the remote management implementation needs review, and the branch naming strategy (author+date) may need refinement. Hamano's feedback focuses on making the proposal reviewable and integratable into Git's codebase rather than commenting on the core concept's validity. The ball is now in the submitter's court to revise based on this maintainer feedback.
