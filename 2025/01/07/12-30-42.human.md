This is the third version of Patrick Steinhardt's CI modernization series, primarily focused on test stability and infrastructure cleanup. The series has already seen significant discussion around its most complex change - fixing a flaky SIGPIPE test in t7422 - which has now reached a stable solution after multiple iterations with input from Jeff King.

The key changes in this version are:
1. A final fix for the t7422 submodule test that properly handles buffered stdout issues that were causing flakiness
2. Continued work on standardizing containerized CI jobs across platforms
3. Removal of outdated CI infrastructure (Azure Pipelines, Ubuntu 16.04 support)
4. Addition of i386 testing via a new linux32 job

The series appears well-tested and mature at this point, with most of the discussion having focused on the SIGPIPE test fix in previous rounds. The changes are largely mechanical improvements to CI infrastructure rather than controversial design decisions. The removal of legacy systems and addition of 32-bit testing are particularly welcome maintenance improvements.

This version shows the series is nearing completion, with only minor tweaks since v2. The thorough test coverage and clear commit messages suggest it's ready for final review and merging. The SIGPIPE test solution, while complex, appears to be a reasonable compromise that preserves the specific submodule behavior being tested while eliminating flakiness.
