CI infrastructure cleanup patch in Patrick Steinhardt's modernization series. This is the sixth patch in a 10-part series that's overhauling Git's continuous integration system.

The patch removes the redundant "linux-old" job from GitLab CI. This job tested against Ubuntu 20.04, but that same configuration is already covered by other CI jobs in the system. The removal follows an earlier change that updated the job from testing Ubuntu 16.04 (now deprecated) to 20.04, making it duplicative.

This is a straightforward cleanup that eliminates unnecessary CI overhead while maintaining the same test coverage. The change is small (just 3 lines removed from the GitLab CI configuration) and uncontroversial, building on the series' broader goal of streamlining the CI infrastructure. The patch follows logically from the earlier Ubuntu 16.04 deprecation mentioned in the thread context.
