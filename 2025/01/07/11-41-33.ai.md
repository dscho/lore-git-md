Continuation of the `the_repository` removal discussion with a new perspective.

Karthik Nayak weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt's patch series that refactor subsystems to pass repository pointers through `struct progress` rather than using `the_repository` global. While the thread previously saw debate between Patrick's incremental approach and Toon Claes' suggestion for more comprehensive removal, Karthik offers a third-party assessment after reviewing the patches.

Karthik acknowledges the two possible approaches - either bubbling up `the_repository` usage to upper layers (Patrick's current method) or attempting to resolve dependencies at each layer to eliminate `the_repository` entirely. He explicitly endorses Patrick's safer, more incremental strategy, noting it appears less risky while still making progress on the overall goal. His review of the first half of the series found the patches technically sound.

This email provides external validation of Patrick's verification-focused approach from another contributor, though it doesn't introduce new technical arguments. The endorsement may help move the discussion toward consensus on the incremental strategy, though the core design question about the pace and scope of `the_repository` removal remains open for further input from other maintainers.
