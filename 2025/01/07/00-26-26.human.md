This is a follow-up discussion in the completed patch series addressing MSVC compiler warnings through type safety improvements. Junio Hamano responds to Brian Carlson's review of the first patch (add-patch.c changes), raising two key points:

1. He reiterates his earlier suggestion that the root issue might be better solved by consistently using `unsigned long` rather than introducing `size_t`, since the code doesn't interact with system functions requiring `size_t` and the patch size limits make 32-bit `unsigned long` sufficient. He notes this approach would avoid platform-specific discussions.

2. He points out a contradiction between Carlson's recommendation to use `%zu` for `size_t` formatting and Git's CodingGuidelines, suggesting the project's standards may differ from strict C99 conventions.

The exchange represents final polish on an otherwise technically complete series, with Hamano providing maintainer perspective on implementation choices. The discussion remains focused on achieving the cleanest solution for Git's codebase rather than introducing new technical concerns.
