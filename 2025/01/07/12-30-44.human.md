This is a test fix addressing a flaky SIGPIPE test in the submodule subsystem. The patch (part 2 of a 10-part series) fixes a race condition in t7422 where `git submodule status --recursive` wasn't reliably triggering SIGPIPE during testing. The original test used grep to terminate the pipe after the first match, but due to buffered stdout, the submodule process could exit before the signal was delivered.

The solution creates 2000 nested submodules to force the submodule process to fill its stdout buffer completely, ensuring the subsequent write will block and properly trigger SIGPIPE when grep terminates. The commit includes a helpful demonstration of how to reproduce the original race condition by adding sleep calls, showing the fix's effectiveness.

The change is well-motivated with clear technical explanation and test evidence. It's part of a larger series (as noted by the v3 02/10 marker) but stands well on its own as a focused test improvement. The approach appears robust and has been reviewed by Jeff King (acknowledged in the sign-off).
