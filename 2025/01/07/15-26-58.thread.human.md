Patrick acknowledged the issue spans beyond his original scope, noting OpenSSL's CSPRNG appears problematic - it's primarily used only on NonStop platforms, is prone to misconfiguration, and is outperformed by modern alternatives like arc4random and getrandom. 

The thread is moving toward considering deprecating OpenSSL's CSPRNG support entirely, though this would require verifying NonStop platform capabilities. Randall's report of sudden issues between rc1 and rc2 without Git-side changes suggests possible environmental factors may also be at play. The conversation now encompasses both the original reftable-specific questions and these broader architectural considerations about Git's random number generation strategy.
