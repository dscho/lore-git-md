CI infrastructure patch removing redundant test job in GitLab CI configuration.

This patch from Patrick Steinhardt's CI modernization series (v3 06/10) eliminates the "linux-old" job from GitLab CI. The job originally tested against Ubuntu 16.04 but was updated to use Ubuntu 20.04 in a previous commit (c85bcb5de1). Since Ubuntu 20.04 is already tested by other CI jobs, this specific job no longer provides unique test coverage.

The change is straightforward, removing three lines from `.gitlab-ci.yml` that defined the now-redundant job configuration. This aligns with the series' broader goal of streamlining the CI infrastructure while maintaining comprehensive test coverage. The removal follows the earlier deprecation of Ubuntu 16.04 support and helps reduce CI resource usage without impacting test quality.

This patch builds on the series' major accomplishments of standardizing containerized CI across platforms and removing legacy components. It's a minor but logical cleanup in the context of the larger infrastructure overhaul, which has already addressed more complex issues like rootless container execution and SIGPIPE test races.
