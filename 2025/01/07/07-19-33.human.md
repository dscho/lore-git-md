This is a follow-up in the `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing Patrick Steinhardt's patch to remove `the_repository` usage from the progress subsystem. The email responds to Toon Claes' review questioning whether the patch should use locally available repository pointers rather than defaulting to `the_repository`.

Patrick defends his approach of consistently using `the_repository` in this initial pass, explaining that substituting with local repository pointers could inadvertently change behavior if those pointers reference a different repository. He argues this mechanical approach makes verification easier by limiting the scope of changes to one subsystem at a time, even if it means temporarily preserving some `the_repository` usage that could theoretically be eliminated.

The exchange highlights a strategic tension in large-scale refactoring: Patrick prioritizes incremental safety and verifiability, while Toon advocates for more aggressive removal of `the_repository` dependencies where possible. Patrick's response suggests this patch series will continue with the conservative approach, though the discussion may influence future iterations of the broader effort. The patch appears well-considered but represents just one step in a multi-phase refactoring process.
