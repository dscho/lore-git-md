This is a follow-up discussion on Patrick Steinhardt's reftable entropy handling bugfix series. Randall Becker confirms they've observed similar test failures through the same path where OpenSSL's entropy pool gets exhausted during concurrent operations. The patch being discussed modifies reftable's stack operations to use less secure random bytes (via the new `CSPRNG_BYTES_INSECURE` flag) for temporary filenames and retry delays - cases where cryptographic-grade randomness isn't needed.

Becker notes they'll retest the changes on both NonStop (where the original failures occurred) and x86 platforms, though they expect the hardware randomizer on x86 will prevent entropy exhaustion there. This serves as real-world validation of the patch's effectiveness while also highlighting the platform-specific nature of the issue. The response indicates the solution is on the right track for addressing the concrete problem while maintaining appropriate security boundaries.
