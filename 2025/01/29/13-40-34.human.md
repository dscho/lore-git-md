This is a status update on two patch series from Derrick Stolee, following up on Junio Hamano's "What's cooking" summary.

The first series (ds/name-hash-tweaks) introduces a new name hash version for pack-objects to improve delta compression. After Taylor Blau's review, Stolee believes it's now ready to graduate to the 'next' branch. This 8-part series adds both a new hash function and related testing infrastructure, with the goal of producing better delta chains in packfiles.

The second series (ds/backfill) is a new `git backfill` command for efficiently downloading missing blobs in blobless clones. Stolee notes this still needs a reroll, which he plans to submit in about a week after focusing on other work. The series currently has 6 commits implementing core functionality with sparse-checkout integration and batch size controls, building on top of path-walk infrastructure from another branch.

Both series represent performance-oriented work - the first optimizing pack creation and the second addressing lazy-loading costs in partial clones. The update suggests the name-hash changes are now review-complete while backfill remains in active development. Stolee also hints at possibly submitting an unrelated 'git-survey' series soon.
