This thread is discussing an RFC bugfix for `git repack` that addresses how promisor packs interact with keep options (`--keep-pack` and `.keep` files). The core issue is that promisor objects were bypassing these keep checks during repacking, which could violate `gc.bigPackThreshold` expectations. The proposed fix modifies `repack_promisor_objects()` to properly honor keep-pack logic.

The technical discussion is currently on hold due to a formatting issue in the initial patch submission - Tomáš Trnka has acknowledged that their email client (KMail) unexpectedly re-wrapped the lines and has promised to resend the patch with correct formatting. brian m. carlson had noted this as a blocking issue, though the technical merits of the change remain to be evaluated.

Once the properly formatted patch is resent, the thread will need to resolve two key questions: where to place the tests (between t7700-repack.sh and t0410-partial-clone.sh) and whether the approach has buy-in from repack/partial-clone experts like Patrick Steinhardt or Jeff King. The thread remains in RFC status while these procedural and technical questions are addressed.
