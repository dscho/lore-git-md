Follow-up clarification on reftable I/O safety. 

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's concern about potential width mismatch between `off_t` and `size_t` when converting file sizes for reftable operations. While the original patch included a negative value check, Steinhardt acknowledges that 32-bit systems with large-file support could indeed have a 64-bit `off_t` alongside a 32-bit `size_t`, making the current cast unsafe in edge cases. 

This is a technical refinement to the already-accepted reftable decoupling series, specifically addressing the low-level I/O changes in patch 01/19. Steinhardt indicates he'll fix this in v3, though the maintainer had previously accepted the pragmatic approach after discussion. The exchange demonstrates the project's attention to portability details even after architectural consensus is reached.
