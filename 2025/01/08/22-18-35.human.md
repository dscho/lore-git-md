He also confirms plans to implement Hamano's suggested output syntax using C-style quoting for special characters.

The discussion shows the patch evolving from its initial type-reporting focus toward a more general metadata framework, with clear technical considerations about edge cases (multiple references, special characters in paths) and backward compatibility.
