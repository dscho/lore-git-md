This is part of the ongoing series implementing client-side support for remote object-info functionality (patch 6/8 in v9). The patch focuses on server-side advertisement of supported object-info features, specifically adding the ability for servers to advertise that they support the "size" feature of object-info.

The change modifies `serve.c` to advertise available object-info components (currently just "size") when the capability is enabled. This allows clients to check server capabilities before attempting object-info requests, falling back to regular fetch if needed. The advertisement is controlled by a new `transfer.advertiseobjectinfo` config, which defaults to off for compatibility.

The patch builds on previous work in the series that established the object-info protocol framework. It's a straightforward change that follows the established pattern for capability advertisement in Git's protocol, with clean integration into the existing serve.c infrastructure. The commit message clearly explains the motivation and behavior, and the change has been reviewed by multiple contributors (Jonathan Tan and Christian Couder are credited as helpers).
