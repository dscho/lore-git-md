This reinforces his broader philosophy of treating the string as a unit rather than something to be parsed.

The implementation remains flexible to support either approach while moving forward with Junio's preferred solution. Usman has committed to documenting the custom string support. The fundamental disagreement about structured versus opaque data persists, but the practical work continues with Junio's direction prevailing for now.
