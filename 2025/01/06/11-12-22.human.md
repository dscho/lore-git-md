This is a test fix addressing a flaky SIGPIPE test in the submodule test suite. The test verifies that `git submodule status --recursive` properly propagates SIGPIPE signals, but was unreliable due to pipe buffering issues. Jeff King identified the root cause - the test could complete before the pipe buffer filled, avoiding SIGPIPE entirely - and proposed a robust solution by pre-filling the pipe buffer with dummy data. Patrick Steinhardt confirms this reliably reproduces the issue and suggests retaining the original grep pattern to ensure we test SIGPIPE propagation from the recursive subprocess rather than the parent. The discussion shows careful attention to edge cases (like subshell behavior with SIGPIPE) while maintaining the test's original intent.
