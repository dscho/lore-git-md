Bugfix discussion regarding credential-cache capability handling protocol compliance.

This email from Brian M. Carlson provides a technical review of the v2 patch attempting to fix the credential-cache helper's capability handling. The reviewer identifies a key protocol misunderstanding in the original patch's approach - while the patch correctly modified the credential helper to only provide authtype fields when requested (using CREDENTIAL_OP_RESPONSE), it incorrectly assumed response capabilities should be a subset of request capabilities. 

The review makes two important technical distinctions:
1. The credential helper must always advertise its capabilities (like authtype) in responses, regardless of whether they were requested, so Git can properly understand what the helper supports
2. The conditional output of authtype and credential fields based on CREDENTIAL_OP_RESPONSE (rather than CREDENTIAL_OP_HELPER) appears correct for those specific fields

The email doesn't propose specific code changes but clearly marks which parts of the diff are problematic versus likely correct. This suggests the patch needs revision to maintain the capability advertisement behavior while keeping the improved field output logic. The discussion remains focused on technical protocol compliance rather than broader design questions.
