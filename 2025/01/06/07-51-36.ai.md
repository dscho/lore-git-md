The implementation follows the established infrastructure pattern from earlier patches in the series, using both compile-time and test-time guards.

This continues the series' approach of removing features that have been deprecated for many years (in this case, `pack-redundant` was deprecated in 2011) while maintaining the ability to build with legacy features during transition.
