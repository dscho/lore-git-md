This is a technical follow-up in the MSVC type safety patch series, specifically addressing a potential edge case in the date.c conversion.

Andreas Schwab responds to Eric Sunshine's concern about a possible integer wrap-around in the `approxidate_alpha()` function when converting from `int` to `size_t`. The original change modified a string length comparison that subtracted 1 from the length, which could theoretically wrap if the length was zero (though it's unclear if zero-length strings are possible in this context).

Schwab proposes a simple but effective solution: rearranging the comparison to avoid the subtraction by moving the constant to the other side of the inequality. This maintains the same logical comparison while eliminating the potential wrap-around risk. The change appears to preserve the original behavior while making it more robust against edge cases.

This email moves the discussion forward on the one remaining concern in the otherwise complete type safety series, showing how a subtle technical issue can be resolved with careful attention to integer arithmetic.
