Type safety improvement in date.c with edge case fix.

This email from Andreas Schwab responds to Eric Sunshine's concern about a potential edge case in the date.c type safety changes from Sören Krecker's patch series. The discussion focuses on the `approxidate_alpha()` function where converting `strlen()` results from `int` to `size_t` could theoretically cause problems if `len` were zero, due to unsigned integer wrap-around when subtracting 1.

Schwab proposes a simple but effective solution: rearranging the comparison to avoid the subtraction operation entirely by moving the constant to the other side of the inequality (`match_string(date, tl->type)+1 >= len`). This maintains the same logical comparison while eliminating the potential wrap-around risk.

The change demonstrates careful attention to edge cases in the ongoing type safety work, particularly important given this is part of a series addressing MSVC compiler warnings. The solution preserves the original behavior while being more robust against edge conditions, addressing the last remaining concern in the patch series' date.c component. This follows the series' pattern of maintaining strict behavioral equivalence while improving type safety.
