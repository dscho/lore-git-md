This is a status update in the ongoing security discussion about ANSI escape sequence handling in Git's sideband channel.

Junio Hamano responds to Ondrej Pohorelsky's assessment that the proposed security fixes may not be adopted soon, if at all. While acknowledging the current state of the discussion - where some have raised concerns about the approach and potential regressions - Hamano notes it's still early in the process. He suggests there could still be room for more convincing arguments to be made about accepting any potential regressions.

The email serves as a reality check on the patch series' prospects while keeping the door open for further discussion. Hamano's tone indicates the architectural debate about whether Git should filter control characters (versus leaving it to terminal emulators) remains unresolved, but he's not ready to declare the matter settled yet. This leaves distributors like Red Hat in a difficult position as they await clearer guidance on whether and how to address the CVEs in their products.
