Junio Hamano responds to Ondřej Pohořelský's assessment about the sideband security patches, providing a nuanced view of the current state. While acknowledging the patchset has received pushback from Brian Carlson (who argues terminal emulators should handle security) and some agreement from others, Hamano notes the discussion remains unresolved. He observes the lack of counter-arguments makes the situation appear settled against the patches, but personally believes it's premature to conclude given the potential for more convincing arguments about accepting the tradeoffs. The response highlights the tension between the implemented solution (which Hamano had previously indicated was technically sound) and the ongoing architectural debate about whether Git should filter control sequences at all. This maintains the uncertainty for downstream distributors like Red Hat who need clarity on whether to backport the changes.
