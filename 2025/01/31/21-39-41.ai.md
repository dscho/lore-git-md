This is a final review ack for Derrick Stolee's performance optimization series introducing configurable name hashing for pack-objects delta compression. Taylor Blau gives the series his approval, noting that all major technical concerns have been addressed through the four iterations. The v4 changes included style fixes, consolidation of version validation logic in `validate_name_hash_version()`, and removal of the less useful Version 3 hash algorithm to avoid user confusion. 

The series has evolved from its initial `--full-name-hash` proposal to the current `--name-hash-version` approach with two implementations (preserving the original algorithm as Version 1 and introducing the improved Version 2). The final version includes comprehensive testing infrastructure, performance benchmarks showing significant improvements (like 37GB → 7GB reductions in some cases), and safety checks to prevent mid-operation version changes. 

With Taylor's approval and Junio Hamano's earlier documentation typo fix, the series appears ready for integration, having demonstrated both technical merit and thorough review process. The new testing components (`test-tool name-hash`, `GIT_TEST_NAME_HASH_VERSION`, etc.) provide future maintainability while the versioned approach preserves backward compatibility.
