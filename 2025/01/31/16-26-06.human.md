Junio Hamano responds to Eric Sunshine's suggestion about using `\EOF` instead of `EOF` for heredoc markers in test scripts. Eric had noted this as a minor style improvement that wasn't worth a reroll of the patch series on its own. Junio confirms the reasoning behind this modern style - quoting the heredoc marker with a backslash serves as a visual indicator that the heredoc content is literal text without shell variable interpolation.
