This is a discussion about finalizing the expiration mechanism for GSoC 2025 microproject documentation. Junio Hamano pushes back on Patrick Steinhardt's suggestion that expiring microprojects should require a formal patch submission process. 

Junio strongly reiterates his position that expiration should be completely mechanical - entries should automatically drop when their timestamp passes without requiring any patch or community review. He argues the burden should be on those wanting to keep entries, not on those removing stale ones. This contrasts with Patrick's earlier position that even removals should go through the normal patch review process for transparency.

The exchange shows the last remaining point of disagreement in what has otherwise been a highly productive thread that's reached consensus on most aspects of the GSoC microproject system. While they agree on the core concept of timestamp-based expiration, they differ on whether the removal process should be silent/automatic (Junio) or require explicit patches (Patrick). This appears to be the final sticking point before the documentation can be finalized.
