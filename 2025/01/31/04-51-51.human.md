This is a follow-up in the ongoing GSoC 2025 organization thread, specifically addressing the documentation system for microprojects. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's proposal for mechanically expiring stale microproject entries based on a timestamp comparison.

Patrick agrees with Junio's mechanical expiration approach but adds that retirements should still go through the normal patch submission process to allow community feedback. This maintains accountability while adopting Junio's simpler rule-based expiration system. The exchange shows the two converging on a solution that balances automation with community oversight.

The discussion represents progress on one of the thread's key open questions - the documentation maintenance system. With Patrick's agreement, the mechanical expiration proposal now has support from both major participants in this sub-thread. The next step would likely be for Kaartic Sivaraam (who proposed the original "best-before" system) to weigh in on this development.
