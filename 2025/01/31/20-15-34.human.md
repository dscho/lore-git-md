This is a protocol design discussion in the ongoing thread about adding OS version capability to Git's protocol.

Junio Hamano responds to Usman Akinyemi's question about the ordering of OS information in the agent string, reinforcing his position that the exact format doesn't matter as long as it's consistent. He makes three key points: (1) Git's own implementations will maintain consistent ordering, (2) third-party implementations will likely follow Git's lead if the purpose (statistics collection) is clearly documented, and (3) most users won't customize the string, making any privacy-conscious customizations statistically insignificant noise.

The response continues Junio's stance that the agent string should be treated as an opaque blob for statistics rather than a structured, parseable field. This maintains the thread's central tension between Junio's preference for simplicity/privacy and Christian Couder's arguments for structured parsing. The implementation remains flexible to accommodate either approach while the design debate continues.
