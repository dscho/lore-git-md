This is a protocol design discussion in the ongoing `os-version` capability thread, specifically addressing the formatting of agent strings. Junio Hamano responds to Usman Akinyemi's question about the ordering of version and OS information in the agent string.

Junio takes a pragmatic stance, stating that either format ("git/2.47.1 Windows" or "Windows git/2.47.1") would be acceptable, but emphasizes three key principles: (1) Git's own implementations should be consistent in their formatting, (2) third-party implementations will likely follow Git's lead if the purpose (statistical collection) is clearly documented, and (3) most users won't customize the string, making privacy-conscious customizations statistically insignificant noise.

The response reinforces Junio's established position from the thread that the agent string should be treated as an opaque identifier for statistical purposes rather than a structured, parseable field. He shows no concern about the specific ordering of components, focusing instead on the broader philosophy of treating the string as a unit rather than something to be parsed. This continues the architectural debate with Christian Couder's position favoring structured parsing, though here Junio is addressing an implementation detail that arises from his preferred approach.
