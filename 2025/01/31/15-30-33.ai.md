Refactoring patch in the `--revision` clone option series, introducing a `struct clone_opts` to reduce global variables in `builtin/clone.c`.

This patch (5/6 in v4) begins the transition from global variables to a structured approach by introducing `struct clone_opts` to hold clone configuration state. The initial version focuses on `wants_head` tracking, which determines whether to fetch the remote HEAD when both `--single-branch` and `--branch` are specified. The change modifies `wanted_peer_refs()` to take the opts struct and reorganizes its HEAD handling logic accordingly.

Key technical changes:
- Adds `struct clone_opts` with `wants_head` field (default true)
- Makes `tail_link_ref()` non-static by moving it to remote.h for cross-file use
- Restructures `wanted_peer_refs()` to use the opts struct, separating HEAD handling from branch/tag logic
- Maintains existing behavior while preparing for future `--revision` feature

The patch continues the series' pattern of careful refactoring with clear intermediate steps, though it notably doesn't yet migrate most globals to the new struct. The `wants_head` focus appears to be a tactical choice to enable later changes rather than a complete conversion. The externalization of `tail_link_ref()` suggests this helper will be needed by other parts of the codebase as the refactoring progresses.
