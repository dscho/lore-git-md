This is a CI/build system patch removing a redundant test job from GitLab CI configuration. It's part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series modernizing Git's CI infrastructure (v4, patch 6/10).

The patch eliminates the "linux-old" job which previously tested against older Ubuntu LTS releases but was recently updated to use Ubuntu 20.04 - the same version already tested by other CI jobs. The change follows an earlier transition from Ubuntu 16.04 to 20.04 (c85bcb5de1) and removes now-redundant coverage.

The modification is straightforward, deleting three lines from `.gitlab-ci.yml` that defined the parallel matrix entry for "linux-old". This continues the series' theme of CI simplification while maintaining test coverage, following the containerization and modernization work described in earlier patches. The change appears uncontroversial and well-justified, with no apparent loss of meaningful test coverage.
