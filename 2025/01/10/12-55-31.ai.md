Performance optimization patch series (part 4/8) continuing the zlib backend refactoring work. This patch moves the zlib header inclusion from the broad `git-compat-util.h` to the more targeted `git-zlib.h`, reducing unnecessary zlib exposure across the codebase. The change affects several core files (`archive.c`, `config.c`, `csum-file.c`, `environment.c`) which now explicitly include `git-zlib.h` where needed.

A special case is made for the reftable library, which maintains its own zlib inclusion via `reftable/system.h` to preserve independence from Git-specific wrappers. This aligns with the series' goal of creating a clean abstraction layer for zlib backend switching while maintaining compatibility. The changes are mechanical header adjustments with no functional impact, continuing the preparatory work for the upcoming zlib-ng integration.
