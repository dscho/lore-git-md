Follow-up to documentation patch about credential cache limitations for personal access tokens (PATs).

Junio Hamano (the Git maintainer) responds to M Hickford's patch that documented why `git-credential-cache` isn't suitable for storing PATs. He agrees with the core premise but suggests two small improvements to make the documentation more actionable:

1. The patch could explicitly name persistent credential helpers like `libsecret` and `osxkeychain` rather than just saying "persistent storage"
2. It might help to directly reference the section of the manual that lists available credential helpers, rather than just pointing to `gitcredentials(7)` generally

The tone is positive and constructive - Hamano clearly agrees this is useful documentation to add, and his suggestions are about making it even more helpful to readers. This appears to be early in the review cycle with no major objections raised yet. The patch seems likely to be accepted with these minor refinements.
