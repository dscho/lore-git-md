This is a follow-up discussion in the zlib-ng backend optimization thread. Taylor Blau raises an important point about build system parity between Meson and Makefile for the new zlib-ng support.

The core concern is about ensuring the zlib-ng backend option is available in both build systems, not just Meson. Taylor notes that while the patch series introduces Meson build options for selecting zlib and zlib-ng backends, there's no mention of equivalent functionality in the traditional Makefile. He suggests either documenting the Makefile approach if it already works, or aligning the two build systems to offer the same capabilities.

This touches on Git's ongoing transition to Meson - Taylor emphasizes that new features should be available in both build systems unless/until the Makefile is officially deprecated. The comment doesn't reject the zlib-ng work but rather asks for consistency in how it's exposed to users building Git. The tone is constructive, focusing on practical distribution concerns rather than technical objections to the zlib abstraction itself.
