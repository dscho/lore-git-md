Security bugfix for `git blame` boundary commit handling. This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing three security vulnerabilities in `git blame`'s object ID formatting. The specific fix here corrects an out-of-bounds write when handling blank boundary commits (those marked with `-b` or `^` prefixes) by replacing a dangerous `memset(hex, ' ', length)` call with a safer `strlen(hex)` approach. Johannes Schindelin approves the solution as effective, though notes the stylistic change of adding curly braces wasn't strictly necessary. Junio Hamano is preparing to include these fixes in an imminent release, expressing cautious optimism about their stability. The patch builds on the series' earlier work to safely handle extreme abbreviation values while maintaining output formatting expectations. With this final approval, all three security issues in the series appear resolved and uncontroversial.
