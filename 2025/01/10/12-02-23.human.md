Bugfix for git grep's EOF matching inconsistency. Jeff King follows up on his earlier investigation with a proposed fix for the `git grep '^$'` false positive at end-of-file. The patch adds a check in `grep.c` to explicitly skip matches that occur at the buffer end, which solves the immediate issue while acknowledging the underlying weirdness in regex implementations.

The solution is straightforward but reveals deeper complexity - the behavior varies between regex engines (notably working correctly with PCRE) and depends on whether look-ahead is used. King notes the inconsistency stems from how different regexec implementations handle the special case of matching empty strings at buffer boundaries, particularly when processing whole buffers versus line-by-line.

This appears to be a robust fix for the reported issue, though the discussion suggests there may be more to explore about regex behavior in edge cases. The patch is small and focused, directly addressing the identified problem while leaving room for future consideration of regex engine differences.
