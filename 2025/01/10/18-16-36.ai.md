This is a documentation patch discussion regarding credential cache limitations for personal access tokens (PATs). Junio C Hamano is responding to M Hickford's initial patch that proposed adding warnings about PAT storage in `git-credential-cache`.

The maintainer agrees with the core premise that the credential cache is unsuitable for PATs due to its ephemeral nature, but suggests making the documentation more actionable. He proposes explicitly mentioning specific persistent credential helpers like libsecret and osxkeychain rather than just generically recommending "persistent storage". He also notes there may be an appropriate existing section in the documentation that lists credential backends which could be referenced.

The response maintains the technical focus on credential storage solutions while pushing for more concrete guidance in the documentation. The thread shows alignment on the fundamental issue (cache isn't right for PATs) while discussing how to best present the alternatives to users. No objections to the core proposal are raised - this is refinement of the documentation approach.
