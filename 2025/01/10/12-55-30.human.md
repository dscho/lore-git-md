Performance optimization patch introducing a new zlib header abstraction as part of the zlib-ng integration effort. This is patch 3/8 in a series preparing Git to optionally use zlib-ng as an optimized zlib backend.

The patch creates a new `compat/zlib-compat.h` header that serves as a wrapper around the system's `<zlib.h>`. This indirection will allow future patches to transparently switch between standard zlib and zlib-ng implementations. The author notes they couldn't name it simply `zlib.h` to avoid potential include conflicts.

The changes are minimal but strategic - updating includes in `git-compat-util.h` and `reftable/block.c` to use the new wrapper. This appears to be a well-considered preparatory step in the larger performance optimization series, maintaining compatibility while enabling the upcoming backend switch. The patch follows cleanly from the previous removal of obsolete zlib compatibility code in the series.
