Tan also notes an asymmetry in the current implementation where lines are only rejected when the left-side count is greater than the right-side count, for unclear reasons. The discussion stems from differences between Git and jj's VCS implementations, with Tan concluding that some divergence in diff output between tools is inevitable given the many configurable factors involved.
