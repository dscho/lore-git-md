This is a CI/build system cleanup patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing CI modernization series. It removes now-obsolete special casing for Ubuntu 16.04 from the CI scripts, following the complete transition to Ubuntu 20.04 that happened in an earlier patch (c85bcb5de1). 

The change is straightforward - it eliminates a conditional block in `ci/lib.sh` that previously disabled HTTP/2 testing on Ubuntu 16.04 due to its outdated Apache version. With all CI jobs now running on Ubuntu 20.04 or newer, this special case is no longer needed and HTTP/2 tests can be enabled unconditionally.

This is part of a larger effort to modernize and simplify Git's CI infrastructure. The patch appears well-scoped and mechanical, with no visible downsides - it's cleaning up technical debt after a completed migration. The change aligns with the series' theme of removing legacy CI support while maintaining test coverage.
