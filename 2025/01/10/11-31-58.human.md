This is a test fix addressing a flaky SIGPIPE test in the submodule test suite. The patch is part of Patrick Steinhardt's v4 CI/build system improvements series.

The test in t7422 checks whether `git submodule status --recursive` properly handles SIGPIPE when its output is piped to a command (like grep) that exits early. The test was unreliable because of a race condition between git-submodule writing output and grep exiting - sometimes the pipe would close before git-submodule filled its buffer, sometimes after.

The fix creates 2000 nested submodules to ensure git-submodule's output buffer fills completely, forcing it to block on writes and reliably trigger SIGPIPE when grep exits. This approach was validated by Jeff King and follows the same pattern of robust test design seen elsewhere in the series. The patch includes a detailed explanation of the race condition and demonstrates the fix's effectiveness against a deliberately broken version.

This change exemplifies the series' focus on making tests reliably verify edge cases across platforms, in this case ensuring signal handling works as expected even with buffered output. The solution is somewhat heavyweight (creating thousands of submodules) but appears necessary to reliably trigger the condition being tested.
