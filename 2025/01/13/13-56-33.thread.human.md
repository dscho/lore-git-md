Brian Carlson has confirmed they will test Karthik Nayak's fix for the reftable migration corruption issue in their real-world repositories. While the core solution—which addresses header/trailer max_update_index mismatches during multi-batch operations—remains technically sound, Carlson raises a forward-looking consideration: the patch currently uses `unsigned int` for tracking indices, but the reftable format itself supports 64-bit integers. For repositories with extremely large commit histories, switching to `uint64_t` might prevent future overflow issues. 

This is a minor refinement to an otherwise complete fix that has already been analyzed by Patrick Steinhardt and reviewed by Junio Hamano. The thread is now in its final stages, awaiting Carlson's verification and potential adoption of this integer-sizing suggestion before the patch is queued for integration.
