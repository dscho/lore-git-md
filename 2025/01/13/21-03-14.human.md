Documentation patch proposing to add Vim modelines to Git's AsciiDoc files. The author notes that Git's documentation format is often mistaken for Markdown, and suggests adding `vim: syntax=asciidoc` modelines to help editors provide proper syntax highlighting and editing support. This is positioned as a contributor-friendly change, particularly helpful for newcomers who often start with documentation edits.

The patch demonstrates the modeline addition on `git-add.txt` as an example. The author also mentions two alternative approaches: moving the modeline location (which doesn't matter functionally) or renaming files with `.adoc` extensions for broader tool compatibility. The change is marked as RFC (Request For Comments), suggesting the author is open to discussion about the best approach.

This appears to be a straightforward, low-risk change focused on improving the contributor experience. The patch is cleanly implemented and well-motivated, though the RFC tag indicates the exact solution may still be under discussion.
