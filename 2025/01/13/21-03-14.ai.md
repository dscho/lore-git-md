Documentation patch proposing to add Vim modelines to Git's AsciiDoc documentation files. The patch adds a `vim: syntax=asciidoc` line to `git-add.txt` as an example, with the goal of helping editors provide proper syntax highlighting and making documentation editing easier for contributors. The author notes that AsciiDoc is often mistaken for Markdown, and suggests two alternative approaches: moving the modeline to a different position in the file, or renaming files with an `.adoc` extension for broader tool compatibility. The patch is marked as RFC (Request For Comments), indicating the author is seeking feedback on the approach. The change is minimal and focused on contributor experience rather than end-user functionality.
