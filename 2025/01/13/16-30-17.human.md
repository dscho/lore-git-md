This is a technical clarification in the ongoing discussion about bare repository mirror behavior.

Junio Hamano responds to Bence Ferdinandy's suggestion about handling HEAD in mirror/non-mirror remotes by explaining why non-bare mirror repositories are impractical in most cases. He outlines three theoretical scenarios where a non-bare mirror might work, but argues that all are either not useful (static checkouts might as well be tarballs) or would be broken by HEAD overwrites during fetches.

The response serves to reinforce the current approach of focusing the mirror HEAD behavior fixes on bare repositories, which is where the actual reported problems occur. This maintains the thread's technical focus while gently steering the discussion away from what Hamano sees as a less practical edge case. The tone is characteristically thoughtful and explanatory rather than dismissive, typical of Hamano's style when guiding technical discussions.
