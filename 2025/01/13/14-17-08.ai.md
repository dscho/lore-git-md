Build system alignment notice about duplicate patches. Kristoffer Haugsbakk points out that Alex Xu's proposed change to move `config-list.h` from `libgit_sources` to `builtin_sources` in `meson.build` appears identical to part of a larger patch series from Patrick Steinhardt submitted the same day. The change itself is a straightforward build system adjustment that correctly scopes the generated header's dependency to builtins rather than the core library, matching Makefile behavior. This appears to be a case of independent parallel development of the same technical fix, though the thread summary indicates no prior discussion had occurred on Alex Xu's version before this duplication notice. The observation doesn't challenge the technical merit of either patch but highlights the need for coordination to avoid duplicate work.
