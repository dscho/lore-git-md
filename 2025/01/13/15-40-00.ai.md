This is a review comment on Jeff King's combine-diff refactoring series, specifically addressing changes in patch 8/14. Patrick Steinhardt notes a subtle type change in the `path_appendnew()` function's `len` parameter from `int` to `size_t` that wasn't mentioned in the commit message, suggesting it should be documented. He also observes that the patch's approach of moving path concatenation into `emit_path()` makes sense given there's only one caller of `path_appendnew()`.

The change continues the series' pattern of simplifying the combine-diff machinery by eliminating intermediate steps - here by having the caller handle path concatenation before calling `path_appendnew()` rather than doing it inside the function. This follows Junio Hamano's earlier praise for similar simplifications in the series. The review confirms the change maintains the single-caller invariant while cleaning up the implementation.
