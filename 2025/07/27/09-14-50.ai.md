This is a follow-up in the git-daemon regression thread, providing additional context about the system configuration that may have contributed to the issue. Russell King shares that he had a forgotten systemd override that specified paths directly in the git-daemon command line, which may have interacted unexpectedly with the `--export-all` and `--user-path` options. He notes that after removing the explicit paths and adding `--base-path`, the service now works correctly.

The email suggests there may have been a behavioral change where `--export-all` stopped working as expected, causing the path list to function as an exclusion list rather than an inclusion list. This adds another potential angle to investigate regarding how path validation interacts with export controls in git-daemon, though the immediate SELinux solution remains valid for the Fedora-specific case.
