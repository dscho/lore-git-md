Junio Hamano responds to Justin Tobler's observation that Git already filters out "C" and "POSIX" locales when NO_GETTEXT is defined. Junio emphasizes that while the specific filtering criteria ("C"/"POSIX") could be debated, the more important architectural concern is having consistent behavior throughout the code. He agrees with Justin's assessment that stricter validation may not provide much practical benefit, prioritizing maintainability over theoretical purity.
