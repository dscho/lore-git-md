This is the final patch in a refactoring series that moves sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to the `repo_settings` struct, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch specifically removes the global variable `sparse_expect_files_outside_of_patterns` and moves its handling into `repo_settings`.

The discussion with Phillip Wood revealed an important behavioral consideration: the original implementation would error out immediately on invalid config values, while the new approach would only fail when the setting is actually used. After some back-and-forth, the author agrees to move the setting into `repo_settings` to maintain consistent behavior with other settings, leveraging the existing `prepare_repo_settings()` call that happens early in the process.

This appears to be a technically sound conclusion to the series, addressing all previous review concerns. The patch is now in its final form, though the series may be temporarily dropped from the 'seen' branch to avoid conflicts with other changes if no immediate action is taken. The author has demonstrated good responsiveness to feedback throughout the series.
