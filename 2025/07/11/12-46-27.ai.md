This is a cleanup patch in the performance optimization series for pathspec bloom filters, addressing Junio Hamano's observation about unused variables in `prepare_to_use_bloom_filter()`. The patch removes two now-unused variables (`bloom_keyvec` and `p`) that were left behind after refactoring the bloom keyvec creation logic into a helper function.

The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, simply cleaning up artifacts from the earlier refactoring that introduced the `struct bloom_keyvec` abstraction. This was part of the series' effort to optimize multiple literal pathspec queries by managing bloom keys more efficiently. The unused variables were noted during review but don't affect functionality, as their roles were properly moved to the new `convert_pathspec_to_filter()` helper.

The patch maintains all the performance improvements from the series while removing dead code, keeping the implementation clean as it approaches finalization. Lidong Yan confirms the fix will be included in the upcoming v6 series, indicating this was the last minor issue to address before merging.
