This is a platform compatibility patch that adds support for __BYTE_ORDER__ in Git's byte-swapping utilities. The change makes Git's endianness detection more portable by using compiler-provided macros (available in gcc, clang, and icc) as a fallback when the traditional libc macros aren't available, such as on MinGW.

The patch cleanly adds a new condition to `compat/bswap.h` that checks for the compiler-defined macros before falling back to Git's hardcoded endianness values. This is part of a 5-patch series (position 1/5), though the cover letter wasn't provided in this digest request.
