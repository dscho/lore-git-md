Thread identity: Patch series optimizing byte-swapping operations in Git's codebase, focusing on ntohl() and related functions across platforms. Authored by Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón.

Core problem: Standardizing and optimizing byte-swapping operations while maintaining performance across different platforms (Linux/musl, Windows) and compilers (gcc/clang/MSVC). Modern compilers can optimize manual shifting into efficient opcodes, making some platform-specific optimizations unnecessary.

Current status: v3 of series, building on previous discussion. Modifies only compat/bswap.h (114 lines changed, net -18 lines). Leaves put/get_be*() macros unchanged as they're already well-optimized except on MSVC.

Key technical details:
- Adds __BYTE_ORDER__ support for better compatibility
- Explicitly defines endianness for MSVC
- Standardizes ntohl/ntohll macro definitions across platforms
- Removes x86-specific optimized bswap implementations
- Provides compiler-based bswap implementations where possible

Open questions/loose ends:
- MSVC still struggles with byte operations in put/get_be*() macros
- Potential follow-up work for MSVC optimization
