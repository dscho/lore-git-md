This is a platform compatibility patch in a series optimizing byte-swapping operations, specifically targeting MSVC compiler support. The patch comes from Sebastian Andrzej Siewior, who is contributing to Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's ongoing byte-swapping optimization series (currently at v3).

The change explicitly defines endianness constants for MSVC, which lacks the standard `__BYTE_ORDER__` definition found in other compilers. Since MSVC only supports little-endian architectures (x86 and arm64), the patch hardcodes `GIT_BYTE_ORDER` as little-endian. This simplifies future endianness checks while maintaining correctness for MSVC builds.

The implementation is straightforward - it adds the standard endianness constant definitions (1234/4321 pattern) and ensures they're only defined when not already set by other compilers. The change is small (5 lines added, 1 modified) and focused solely on the MSVC compatibility header.

This appears to be a well-justified change that aligns with the series' goal of standardizing byte-swapping operations across platforms. The patch is technically sound and follows the pattern established in previous iterations of the series.
