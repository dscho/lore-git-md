Platform compatibility patch in the byte-swapping optimization series, specifically targeting MSVC compiler support.

This patch addresses a gap in the ongoing byte-swapping standardization work by explicitly defining endianness constants for MSVC. Since MSVC doesn't provide the standard __BYTE_ORDER__ macro or equivalent, the patch hardcodes the assumption that MSVC targets (x86 and arm64) are little-endian. It introduces GIT_LITTLE_ENDIAN, GIT_BIG_ENDIAN, and GIT_BYTE_ORDER macros specifically for MSVC builds, placed alongside the existing MSVC-specific bswap implementations.

The change is straightforward - 5 lines added to define the endianness macros when compiling with MSVC, plus a one-line modification to make the existing fallback definitions conditional on GIT_BYTE_ORDER not being already defined. This maintains the existing behavior for other platforms while properly handling MSVC's limitations.

This appears to be a well-justified platform-specific workaround, as the author notes MSVC only supports little-endian architectures. The patch fits cleanly into the series' goal of standardizing byte-swapping operations across compilers. No test changes or behavioral impacts are expected - this is purely a build-time definition to support correct compilation on Windows.

Key technical details for future reference:
- Adds MSVC-specific GIT_*_ENDIAN definitions in compat/bswap.h
- Makes existing fallback definitions conditional on !defined(GIT_BYTE_ORDER)
- Maintains all current behavior for non-MSVC platforms
- Follows the series' pattern of compiler-specific optimizations
