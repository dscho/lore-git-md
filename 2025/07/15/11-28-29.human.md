This is a refactoring patch in the final stages of the `git for-each-ref` pagination series. Karthik Nayak removes an unnecessary `else` clause in `ref-filter.c` that follows a `die()` call, which makes the code flow clearer by reducing indentation levels. The change is purely stylistic - the behavior remains identical since the `die()` would have already terminated the program before reaching the `else` block.

This patch was suggested by Christian Couder during review of the pagination feature, and represents one of the final cleanups in the v5 series. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, aligning with Git's code style guidelines about avoiding unnecessary control flow structures. The patch is small (30 lines changed) and focused, with no functional impact beyond improved readability.
