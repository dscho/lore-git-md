This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing series to make the loose object backend pluggable by removing `the_repository` dependencies. The thread has previously established that moving compression level settings into repo settings caused a regression in `git fast-import` where invalid values were no longer caught early.

Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood have raised concerns about scattering `prepare_repo_settings()` calls throughout the code, advocating instead for early config parsing like builtin commands do. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges this feedback and suggests calling `prepare_repo_settings()` when repositories are opened as a potential solution, though he notes this needs more thought.

The exchange shows the series is now addressing important architectural questions about config parsing timing that emerged from earlier patches. Steinhardt's response indicates he's taking the maintainer's guidance seriously but hasn't yet settled on a specific implementation approach. This discussion will likely influence how other settings are migrated from global to repository-specific storage in future work.
