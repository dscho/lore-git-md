This is a technical discussion about configuration parsing timing in the ongoing `the_repository` removal series. Phillip Wood responds to Patrick Steinhardt's proposal for handling repository settings initialization, focusing on maintaining consistent config parsing behavior during the transition away from global variables.

This would address the regression in `git fast-import` where invalid compression levels are now caught (a change) but at a different time than before (the regression).

The discussion highlights the tension between architectural ideals (centralized, typed configuration validated at startup) and practical constraints of incremental refactoring. Both contributors acknowledge the complexity of the problem while trying to find a path forward that doesn't introduce new inconsistencies during the transition period.
