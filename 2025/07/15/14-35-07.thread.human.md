René Scharfe's series optimizing commit traversal with priority queues has completed its journey through review and is now merged. The technical work, which improves worst-case performance in merge-heavy histories from O(N²) to O(N log N) while maintaining O(N) best-case efficiency, received thorough review from Jeff King and others before approval. The changes touch several key files (commit.c, walker.c, etc.) and introduce a new prio_queue_replace() operation.

After technical consensus was reached, the thread saw some administrative follow-ups regarding patch formatting - first a build system note from SZEDER Gábor, then Johannes Schindelin spotting an email header fragment that had slipped into a commit subject line. René promptly addressed these, and Junio has now merged the corrected version to master. The series demonstrates Git's attention to both technical correctness and presentation quality, though in this case the final tweaks were purely cosmetic. The optimization is now part of the codebase, ready to benefit users with complex merge histories.
