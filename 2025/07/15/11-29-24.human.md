This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 7-part refactoring series to move MIDX tracking from global state to per-source storage in `struct odb_source`. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort for pluggable backends.

The patch removes the now-unused global MIDX linked list infrastructure after all callers have been converted to use the per-source MIDX storage. It deletes the `next` pointer from `struct multi_pack_index`, removes the `multi_pack_index` field from `struct object_database`, and cleans up the last remaining global MIDX references in `midx.c` and `packfile.c`. The changes are straightforward deletions of now-dead code, with no behavior changes.

This completes the series' goal of fully decoupling MIDX handling from global object database state. The work has been thoroughly reviewed by Taylor Blau and Justin Tobler, with all feedback incorporated in this v2 submission. The series builds cleanly and passes tests, representing a significant step in the ODB abstraction effort.
