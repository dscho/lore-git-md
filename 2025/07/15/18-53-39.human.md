This is the final ack for a refactoring series that standardizes the use of C99 `bool` for predicate functions in Git's codebase. Junio C Hamano concurs with Elijah Newren's review of Phillip Wood's v2 patches, which convert string utility functions and `strbuf` methods from `int` to `bool` returns. The changes are purely mechanical type signature updates with no behavioral impact, following Git's established pattern for gradual C99 adoption.

The series has been through thorough review, addressing feedback from multiple contributors including typo fixes from Eric Sunshine and style guidance from Brian m. carlson. While Randall S. Becker raised a technical concern about `<stdbool.h>` inclusion on some platforms, this doesn't block the series' inclusion and may be handled as follow-up work.

With all reviewer feedback addressed and the changes deemed safe after an 18-month experimental period, this series appears ready for merging into Git's main branch. The work represents a clean, well-executed modernization of Git's codebase that improves type safety and readability for predicate functions.
