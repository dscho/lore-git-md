This is a final review ack for a refactoring series standardizing the use of C99 `bool` in Git's codebase. Elijah Newren gives his approval to Phillip Wood's v2 patch series, which converts string utility predicates from `int` to `bool` return types after an 18-month experimental period. The series consists of three mechanical changes: updating CodingGuidelines to permit `bool`, converting case-sensitive string utilities in git-compat-util.h, and updating strbuf predicate methods. 

The only changes from v1 were typo fixes in the commit message for patch 2 (correcting "sting" to "string" and improving grammar), which were suggested by Eric Sunshine. The series has already received technical review from multiple contributors, with Elijah Newren previously raising hyphenation questions that were resolved with style guidance from Brian m. carlson. 

With this ack from a key reviewer and no outstanding issues, the series appears ready for inclusion. The changes are straightforward refactoring with no behavior impact, continuing Git's conservative approach to adopting C99 features after sufficient real-world usage. This completes the standardization effort that began in December 2023.
