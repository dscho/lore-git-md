This is a final ack for a refactoring series standardizing `bool` usage in Git's predicate functions. Brian m. carlson provides a positive review of Phillip Wood's v2 series, noting the change makes return types more intuitive (true/false rather than 0/-1 conventions). The series has already received thorough review from multiple contributors over its 18-month experimental period, with all feedback addressed in v2. The change affects string utility functions like `starts_with()` and their case-insensitive variants, converting their return types from `int` to `bool` without altering behavior.

The review highlights one of the practical benefits - clearer semantics when reading code that uses these predicates. This marks the successful conclusion of a carefully managed transition, demonstrating Git's conservative approach to adopting C99 features even after extensive real-world testing. With this final ack from carlson joining earlier approvals from key maintainers, the series appears ready for merging with no outstanding concerns.
