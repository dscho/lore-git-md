This is a follow-up comment in the ongoing C99 `bool` standardization thread. Randall S. Becker raises a technical concern about the series, noting that `bool` requires explicit inclusion of `<stdbool.h>` in some C99 implementations, unlike others where it may be implicitly available. This is particularly relevant for platforms with non-standard C99 compliance.

The comment doesn't oppose the overall direction of the series (which has already received final review acks from Elijah Newren and prior acceptance from Junio C Hamano), but flags a potential portability issue that may need addressing. The series currently modifies string utility functions in `git-compat-util.h` and `strbuf` methods to use `bool` returns, following an 18-month experimental period that began in December 2023.

Key technical details for future reference:
- The change affects predicate functions like `skip_prefix()`, `starts_with()`, and their case-insensitive variants
- `strbuf` methods modified include `starts_with`, `istarts_with`, and `ends_with`
- All changes are mechanical type signature updates (29 insertions, 26 deletions across 4 files)
- The thread has already resolved style questions about hyphenation ("case-dependent" vs "case independent")

This appears to be the first mention of `<stdbool.h>` inclusion in the thread, suggesting it may be an oversight in the otherwise thorough review process. The comment implies the need to verify that all platforms in Git's support matrix properly handle `bool` with the current header inclusion strategy, though it doesn't provide specific platform examples where this might fail.
