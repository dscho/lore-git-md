This thread discusses a refactoring effort to standardize and optimize byte-swapping operations in Git's codebase, particularly focusing on cross-platform compatibility. The series aims to simplify how Git handles ntohl() and related functions while maintaining performance across different compilers and platforms.

The author addresses several specific platform concerns, including musl libc's function-call implementation of ntohl() and Windows' lack of ntohll() support. The changes focus on compat/bswap.h, introducing __BYTE_ORDER__ support for better MSVC compatibility, and standardizing macro definitions while removing x86-specific optimizations that modern compilers can now replace with efficient opcodes. The series represents a net reduction in code while improving maintainability, building on feedback from previous versions.
