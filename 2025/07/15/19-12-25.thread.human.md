This thread focuses on optimizing byte-swapping operations in Git's codebase, particularly around `ntohl()` and related functions. The series removes x86-specific assembly in favor of compiler optimizations, adds built-in detection for `__builtin_bswap32/64`, and maintains fallback implementations while reducing the code size by 40 lines net.

The changes are confined to `compat/bswap.h` and preserve all existing behavior. The series has evolved through three versions, with v3 addressing only minor typo fixes from v2.
