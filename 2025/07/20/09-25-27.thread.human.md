Aditya Garg's patch series to add IMAP sent-folder archiving to `git send-email` has progressed through four versions, now reaching its final polishing phase. The feature supports both traditional SMTP-with-archiving and a new pure-IMAP mode that archives messages without SMTP transmission. Junio Hamano has been actively involved in shaping the implementation, particularly around naming ("imap-sent-folder") and ensuring the design properly reflects the dual workflow capabilities.

The latest email shows Junio providing a minor documentation formatting fix, requesting that the `--use-imap-only` and `--no-use-imap-only` options be listed on separate lines to match Git's standard documentation style. This is the sort of final tweak that typically precedes merging - all substantive technical issues were resolved in earlier rounds, with Eric Sunshine having previously contributed documentation polish that has now been fully integrated. The series appears ready for inclusion, with the pure-IMAP mode addition providing a distinct use case that strengthens the feature's justification in core Git.
