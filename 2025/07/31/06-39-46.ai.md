This is the second patch in Junio Hamano's 5-part series refactoring Git's string-list API, focusing on aligning the behavior of `string_list_split()` with its `_in_place` counterpart. The key change in this patch is modifying `string_list_split()` to accept multiple delimiter characters (passed as a string) rather than just a single character, matching the existing capability of `string_list_split_in_place()`. 

The patch makes mechanical changes across 19 files to update callers that were passing single-character delimiters to now pass them as strings (e.g. changing `','` to `","`). The core implementation in string-list.c is updated to use `strpbrk()` instead of `strchr()` to handle multiple delimiters. Documentation in string-list.h is correspondingly updated to reflect the new behavior.

This change is part of the series' broader goal of unifying and enhancing the string-list split functionality. The patch appears straightforward and well-executed, with comprehensive updates to both implementation and tests. No behavioral changes are introduced beyond the explicitly stated delimiter handling expansion. The changes are mechanical in nature and unlikely to be controversial, serving as groundwork for subsequent patches in the series that will build on this unified API.
