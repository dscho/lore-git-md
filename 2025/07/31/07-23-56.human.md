This is the 4th patch in a 5-part series extending the promisor-remote protocol to support configurable validation of remote attributes during clone/fetch operations. The series is now at v7 and appears technically complete, with this patch focusing on client-side validation of additional fields.

The patch introduces a new `promisor.checkFields` configuration that lets clients verify server-advertised fields (currently just "partialCloneFilter" and "token") against local configuration before accepting a promisor remote. The validation ensures three conditions are met for each checked field: local config must exist, the server must advertise the field, and values must match exactly (case-sensitive comparison).

The implementation builds on the series' earlier work with `struct promisor_info` and includes thorough documentation explaining the validation rules. Test coverage verifies the field checking works as expected, including a case where a remote is rejected due to filter mismatch. The patch maintains consistency with the series' approach to case sensitivity (strict for protocol, flexible for config) and integrates cleanly with existing promisor-remote infrastructure.

This appears to be a well-constructed piece of the larger effort, with clear motivation (preventing inconsistencies in filtering behavior and authentication) and solid technical execution. The only remaining discussion points in the thread are terminology ("field" vs "configuration variable") and future error handling considerations, neither of which block this patch's functionality.
