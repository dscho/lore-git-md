This is a build system patch in the ongoing git-gui modernization series, specifically addressing the askpass helper implementation. It's part of the v2 series that consolidates platform support changes for git-gui.

The patch makes git-gui--askpass respect TCLTK_PATH consistently with git-gui itself, fixing a long-standing inconsistency since 2008 where the askpass helper would call whatever wish interpreter was in the path. The solution involves converting git-gui--askpass into a build-time generated script, similar to how git-gui is handled.

Key changes include:
- Adding a new generate-script.sh helper to process the askpass script
- Updating Makefile and meson.build to properly generate the askpass executable
- Renaming git-gui--askpass to git-gui--askpass.sh to clarify its role as a template
- Ensuring clean handling of the generated files

This appears to be a well-considered change that aligns with the series' architectural decisions around Tcl/Tk dependency management. The patch maintains consistency between build systems (Make and meson) and properly integrates with the existing build infrastructure. It's part of the broader effort to standardize git-gui's platform support and remove legacy behaviors.
