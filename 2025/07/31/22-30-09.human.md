This is a community announcement about the Git Rev News publication. Christian Couder shares a draft of the upcoming edition 125 of Git Rev News, inviting contributions from anyone interested. The draft is available for review on GitHub, where people can either directly edit via pull requests or comment on the associated issue. The team plans to publish the final version on August 2nd, 2025.

The email serves as a call for participation, welcoming various forms of contributions such as proofreading, content ideas, or volunteering for interviews. Christian notes that while they've tried to CC all relevant contributors, there might be some omissions. This is a routine update for the community newsletter, not a technical discussion or patch submission.
