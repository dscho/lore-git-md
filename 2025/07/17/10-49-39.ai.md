This is part 19/21 of Patrick Steinhardt's series removing `the_repository` global from config-related functionality, specifically targeting unused wrapper functions. The patch removes four remaining wrapper functions from `config.h` that were implicitly using `the_repository` but appear to be unused in the codebase:

1. `git_config_get_bool_or_int()`
2. `git_config_get_maybe_bool()`
3. `git_config_get_pathname()`
4. `git_config_set_multivar_in_file()`

The removal is straightforward - these functions were already conditionally compiled behind `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` and simply delegated to their `repo_config_*` counterparts. The patch follows the established pattern of the series by mechanically removing wrappers without functional changes. The commit message notes these were unused, suggesting this is effectively dead code removal rather than an active refactoring of call sites. This leaves only 2 more patches remaining in the series to complete the config-related `the_repository` removal work.
