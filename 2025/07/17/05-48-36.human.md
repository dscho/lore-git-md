This is a continuation of the discussion about configuration options for auto-stashing behavior during `git pull` operations. Junio Hamano responds to Lidong Yan's concern that changing which config key is checked during merge operations (from `rebase.autoStash` to `merge.autoStash`) might confuse users who currently rely on the existing behavior.

Junio acknowledges this would be a behavior change but suggests it would be a one-time adjustment. He proposes that users who notice the change could simply update their configuration, either by setting the new `pull.autoStash` (which he notes he wouldn't necessarily recommend) or `merge.autoStash`. 

The maintainer expresses particular enthusiasm for Yan's suggestion to have `pull.autoStash=false` override both `rebase.autoStash` and `merge.autoStash`. This would allow users to maintain different stashing behaviors for local operations (where they understand both sides) versus pulls (where they might want more caution). The discussion continues to weigh the tradeoffs between backward compatibility and cleaner configuration design.
