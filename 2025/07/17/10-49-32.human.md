This is patch 12/21 in Patrick Steinhardt's series to remove `the_repository` global from config-related functions. The patch removes the `git_config_set_in_file()` wrapper function, converting all callers to use `repo_config_set_in_file(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The changes affect submodule configuration handling (submodule.alternateLocation and submodule.alternateErrorStrategy) and worktree setup (core.worktree), with modifications in builtin/submodule--helper.c and dir.c. The patch follows the established pattern of making repository dependencies explicit while maintaining identical behavior.

This is a mechanical conversion like previous patches in the series, removing 6 lines from config.h wrapper declarations while adding `the_repository` parameters at call sites. The author notes that some call sites could potentially use a different repository handle in the future, but that optimization is left for later work. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to be controversial, continuing the incremental progress toward eliminating implicit `the_repository` usage.
