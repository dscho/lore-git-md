Part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, this patch continues Patrick Steinhardt's series converting config wrapper functions to explicit repository-aware versions. 

This sixth patch removes the `git_config_get_string_multi()` wrapper function, replacing it with direct calls to `repo_config_get_string_multi(the_repository, ...)` at all call sites. The changes are mechanical conversions in builtin/gc.c, builtin/log.c, reachable.c, and versioncmp.c, along with removing the wrapper declaration from config.h. 

The commit message explains this follows through on the intent established in commit 036876a1067 to eventually remove all config wrappers that implicitly depend on `the_repository`. While some call sites may already have repository instances available (which could be used directly in future patches), this intermediate step maintains current behavior while making the global repository dependency explicit.
