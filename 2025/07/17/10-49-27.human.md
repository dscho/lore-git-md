This is part 7 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove implicit dependencies on the `the_repository` global variable, specifically targeting config-related functions. The patch removes the `git_config_get_string()` wrapper function, converting all its callers to explicitly use `repo_config_get_string(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The change follows the established pattern from previous patches in the series, making the repository dependency explicit while maintaining identical behavior. The patch touches 12 files across various subsystems including apply, branch, gc, log, submodules, and transport. The diff shows a net reduction of 5 lines by removing the wrapper while adding 6 explicit repository parameters at call sites.

This is a mechanical conversion that doesn't change functionality - it's part of the larger effort to eventually enable non-`the_repository` usage. The commit message notes that some call sites could be further improved by using available repository pointers rather than `the_repository`, but leaves that for future work. The changes appear straightforward and well-contained, continuing the systematic approach seen in earlier patches of this series.
