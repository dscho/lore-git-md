This is a major refactoring series targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically focusing on config handling. Patrick Steinhardt proposes removing all wrapper functions in config.c that implicitly rely on the global `the_repository` variable, replacing them with explicit repository-aware`repo_config_*` variants. The series also moves Git-specific config parsing (like core.sharedRepository) into environment.c.

The changes are extensive (21 patches touching 161 files) but mechanically straightforward - most patches simply convert `git_config_foo()` calls to `repo_config_foo(the_repository, ...)`. The motivation comes from difficulties encountered when trying to parse repository settings early in Git's process lifecycle. While the diffstat shows significant churn (+1009/-1096 lines), the actual conceptual changes are minimal.

This appears to be a well-structured series that continues Git's architectural shift away from global state. The author notes it's based on recent master and has trivial merge conflicts with other in-flight topics. The changes are focused and consistent with the project's direction, though reviewers will want to verify no subtle behavior changes were introduced in the mechanical conversions.
