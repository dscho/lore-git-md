This is a major refactoring series targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically focusing on config-related functionality. Patrick Steinhardt proposes removing all `the_repository`-dependent config wrapper functions from config.c and moving Git-specific config parsing into environment.c.

The series consists of 21 patches, nearly all of which are mechanical replacements of `git_config_*()` wrapper calls with their `repo_config_*()` equivalents that explicitly take a repository parameter. The key structural changes are:
1. Eliminating wrapper functions that implicitly relied on `the_repository`
2. Relocating core Git settings parsing (like `core.sharedRepository`) to environment.c

The motivation stems from difficulties encountered when trying to parse repository settings early in Git's process lifecycle. The changes aim to make such early parsing more feasible by removing implicit global state dependencies. The series touches a massive 161 files across the codebase, with net changes removing ~550 lines while adding ~500.

This appears to be a carefully executed mechanical conversion - the cover letter notes most patches are trivial replacements, and the diffstat shows the changes are primarily moving/rewiring code rather than altering behavior. The series builds on recent work (SHA-256 by default changes) and acknowledges some trivial conflicts with the 'seen' branch.
