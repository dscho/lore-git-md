This is patch 8/21 in Patrick Steinhardt's series removing implicit `the_repository` usage from config functions. The patch removes the `git_config_get_string()` wrapper function, converting all its callers to explicitly use `repo_config_get_string(the_repository, ...)` instead. 

The change affects 14 files across various parts of the codebase, including fetch, gc, submodules, and protocol handling. Key config options touched include `fetch.bundleuri`, `maintenance.strategy`, `remote.partialclonefilter`, and `protocol.version`. The patch follows the established approach of making repository dependencies explicit while maintaining current behavior.

This is a mechanical change that continues the systematic conversion pattern seen in previous patches of the series. The author notes that while some call sites could be further optimized by using available repository handles, that cleanup is left for future work. The patch removes 5 lines from config.h while making net reductions across the codebase.

The change is part of the long-running effort to eliminate global state in Git's codebase, building on earlier work by René Scharfe. No objections have been raised to the approach in previous patches. The series appears well-planned and carefully executed, with each patch focused on a specific set of conversions.
