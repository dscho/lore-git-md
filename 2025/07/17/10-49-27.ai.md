This is part 7/21 in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove implicit `the_repository` dependencies from config functions, continuing the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch removes the `git_config_get_string()` wrapper function, converting all callers to explicitly use `repo_config_get_string(the_repository, ...)` instead. 

The conversion follows the established pattern seen in previous patches, mechanically replacing wrapper calls with their repository-aware equivalents while maintaining identical behavior. The changes span 12 files including core functionality (apply.c, branch.c), builtins (gc.c, log.c, submodule--helper), and helper utilities (transport.c, sequencer.c). The patch removes 5 lines from config.h while adding 25 and deleting 30 across the codebase.

Key config options affected by these changes include apply.whitespace, gc.repackfilter, log.initialdecorationset, submodule.alternateLocation, and protocol.allow. The commit notes this is an intermediate step - some call sites already have repository handles available and could be further optimized in future work, but this mechanical conversion ensures no behavioral regressions.
