Patrick Steinhardt's latest response focuses on the administrative path forward for resolving the relicensing questions. Importantly, he proposes that the Project Leadership Committee (PLC) should take the lead in engaging with SFC, signaling a shift from open discussion to formal governance channels. This comes after the Contributor Summit failed to address the issue due to time constraints.

The technical implementation remains on hold pending these policy resolutions, with the newly identified xdiff license header issue adding urgency.
