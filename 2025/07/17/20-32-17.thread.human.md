Elijah Newren has proposed a new approach to break the impasse in the Rust infrastructure thread, suggesting that Junio Hamano formally designate maintainers for a Rust-optional LTS branch - similar to how Linux handles stable releases. This would provide the official repository-hosted branch that regulated industries require for compliance, while allowing the main development line to proceed with Rust integration.

However, the discussion reveals a persistent communication gap. Newren feels Randall Becker keeps restating the compliance requirements (30-day CVE SLA, audit validation needs) without engaging with this specific proposal. Becker maintains that enterprises need official backports from the main repository, not just any LTS branch, for their compliance frameworks. 

The technical situation remains unchanged - the Rust interop layer (`ivec`) and module structure are stable, with parallel C/Rust maintenance continuing. But the policy debate has now crystallized around whether a Junio-blessed LTS branch would satisfy enterprise needs, or if the compliance requirements fundamentally require keeping Rust optional in the main repository. 

With both sides agreeing some form of official LTS support is needed but disagreeing on the implementation, the thread remains at an impasse. The ball appears to be in Junio's court regarding whether he would be willing to establish and delegate maintenance of such a branch.
