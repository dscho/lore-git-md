This RFC thread on introducing Rust infrastructure for xdiff has reached a policy impasse after technical discussions largely converged. The series, now at v3, proposes Rust modules for xdiff with a custom `ivec` type designed for C interoperability, which has received technical endorsement from Brian Carlson and Ben Knoble after Ezekiel Newren explained why Rust's native types wouldn't work for FFI. 

The debate has shifted to platform support and maintenance implications. Taylor Blau argues Rust's security benefits justify dropping some platform support, pointing to the security team's proven backporting capabilities. However, Randall Becker revealed that NonStop - a commercial POSIX platform - is actually maintained by individual volunteers rather than vendor resources, complicating Junio Hamano's assumption that vendors would handle Rust transitions. 

In the latest exchange, Hamano questions the long-term viability of maintaining dual C/Rust implementations, suggesting the ecosystem may evolve to resolve platform support issues naturally. While not rejecting Rust outright, he expresses skepticism about intermediate solutions and raises strategic concerns about whether backporting capability will remain relevant post-Rust adoption. The thread remains at this policy level, with technical implementation details largely agreed upon but broader adoption questions unresolved.
