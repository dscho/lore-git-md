This thread follows Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing work to remove dependencies on the `the_repository` global variable from Git's config-related functionality. The series, now at 20 of 21 planned patches, systematically replaces implicit `git_config_*()` wrappers with explicit `repo_config_*()` variants that take a repository parameter.

The latest patch moves Git-specific config parsing functions from `config.c` to `environment.c`, which better aligns with that file's role in handling global state. This mechanical change required updating include statements in 55 files but introduces no functional changes beyond fixing a compiler warning. The patch also removes the now-unneeded `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` declaration.

Previous patches in the series have converted various config wrapper functions and removed unused ones, all following the established pattern of mechanical conversions first. The work builds on earlier SHA-256 integration and remains focused on the uncontroversial aspects of the larger `the_repository` removal effort. With just one patch remaining, the series appears to be nearing completion without having drawn any review objections.
