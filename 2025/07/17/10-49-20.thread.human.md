So far the changes have been mechanical conversions that maintain identical behavior while making repository dependencies explicit. The series has progressed smoothly, with no objections raised in review. Each conversion follows the same uncontroversial pattern, removing wrapper functions from config.h while updating call sites to use the new repository-aware variants.
