This is a status update in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically regarding repository configuration handling. Phillip Wood responds to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier message about attempting to move configuration parsing into `repo_config()` as part of the transition away from global state.

Patrick had reported significant difficulties with this approach, particularly around `core.shared_repository` which is widely used throughout the codebase. After attempting 15 patches worth of fixes without reaching a working state, he proposes dropping the last three patches of his series to allow forward progress on other parts of the refactoring.

Phillip agrees this is a sensible approach, acknowledging the challenges Patrick encountered. The exchange shows the practical difficulties in migrating configuration handling while maintaining compatibility, with `core.shared_repository` emerging as a particularly problematic case due to its pervasive use. This suggests the configuration aspects of `the_repository` removal may need more incremental or alternative approaches.
