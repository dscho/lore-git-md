This is part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt's 21-patch series refactoring config-related functionality to remove dependencies on the `the_repository` global variable. The patch removes the `git_config_get_value()` wrapper function, converting all its callers to use `repo_config_get_value(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The change is mechanical, affecting several builtin commands (gc, pull), rebase functionality, and test helpers. Each call site now explicitly passes `the_repository` rather than relying on the implicit global state. The commit message notes this is an intermediate step - some call sites may already have repository instances available that could be used instead, but those will be addressed in later patches.

This continues the systematic conversion pattern seen in earlier patches, removing another config wrapper while maintaining identical behavior. The diff shows a net reduction of 7 lines (12 removed, 5 added), primarily from removing the wrapper declaration in config.h. The changes are straightforward and unlikely to introduce regressions, as they simply make existing implicit dependencies explicit.
