This is part 10 in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove implicit `the_repository` usage from config functions, continuing the established pattern of converting wrapper functions to their explicit repository-taking counterparts. 

The patch removes the `git_config_get_ulong()` wrapper in favor of direct `repo_config_get_ulong(the_repository, ...)` calls. It updates 4 call sites across fast-import, gc, and http-backend code, affecting config options like pack.depth, gc.maxcruftsize, and http.maxrequestbuffer. The changes are mechanical and maintain current behavior while making the implicit repository dependency explicit.

This continues the series' trend of net code reduction (5 lines from config.h) while preparing for future work where callers might use non-`the_repository` instances. The patch follows the same uncontroversial approach as previous conversions in the series, with no behavioral changes or new edge cases introduced.
