This is a follow-up in the thread about the SSH signing tempfile leak fix, now touching on both technical testing considerations and the DCO/pseudonym policy discussion.

The author redoste responds to brian m. carlson's suggestion about testing the tempfile cleanup by proposing a test that would verify no temporary signing key files remain after operations. The test would use the current directory via TMPDIR rather than /tmp to avoid filesystem pollution concerns. The proposed test looks reasonable but awaits feedback on whether to include it in a v2 submission.

On the DCO/pseudonym front, redoste shares their personal situation as a trans person in transition who hasn't settled on a permanent name, explaining why they used a pseudonym. They reference the Linux kernel's move away from "real name" requirements and express willingness to use a different name if needed, though they note the patch may not even be copyrightable given its simplicity.

The technical fix itself (converting strbuf_detach to xstrdup) remains uncontroversial, with the discussion now focused on these separable process questions. The email shows the author engaging thoughtfully with both the technical and policy aspects of contributing to Git.
