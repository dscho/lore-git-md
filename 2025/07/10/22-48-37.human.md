Refactoring patch completing the MIDX tracking migration from global to per-source storage. This is the final patch (8/8) in Patrick Steinhardt's series to move MIDX handling from the object database level to individual `struct odb_source` instances.

The patch removes the now-unused global linked list of MIDX files and related infrastructure, including:
- The `next` pointer from `struct multi_pack_index`
- The `multi_pack_index` field from `struct object_database`
- Associated cleanup code in `midx.c` and `packfile.c`

Justin Tobler's review notes the simplicity of these final removals, confirming the earlier refactoring has successfully migrated all MIDX tracking to the per-source model. The changes follow naturally from the previous patches in the series, which had already shifted all MIDX operations to work with individual sources rather than the global list.

This completes the technical goal of the series - MIDX files are now properly associated with their corresponding object sources rather than being tracked globally, better matching their on-disk organization and supporting future pluggable backends. The clean removal of the old infrastructure indicates the transition was thorough and well-executed.
