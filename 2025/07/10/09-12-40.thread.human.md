Patrick Steinhardt traced this to commit 75a044f748's header restructuring and proposed a fix that explicitly includes <ctype.h> early to activate its header guards.

Patrick and Toon Claes confirmed the solution works in the problematic environment while maintaining compatibility elsewhere.
