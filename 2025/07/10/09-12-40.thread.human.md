This thread addresses a build failure specific to Amazon Linux 2 with GCC 7.3.1, where Git's ctype macro definitions were corrupting system header declarations. Patrick Steinhardt diagnosed the issue through bisection, tracing it to header inclusion order problems stemming from commit 75a044f748. The collision occurs when OpenSSL headers (included via git-compat-util.h) pull in system <ctype.h> after Git's "sane-ctype.h" macros are defined.

While the patch itself remains truncated in the emails, Toon Claes has now verified that the proposed solution works in the original problematic environment. The fix appears to target the specific header inclusion chain without requiring broader changes to Git's ctype handling. The verification is encouraging, though the community will need to see the full patch contents to properly assess its approach and ensure it doesn't introduce regressions on other platforms. The thread remains open pending that review and any additional platform testing.
