This is a platform compatibility fix for a build failure on Amazon Linux 2 with GCC 7.3.1. The issue occurs when system headers get included after Git's ctype macros, causing function declaration conflicts. Patrick Steinhardt had previously diagnosed the problem and provided a patch, though its details were truncated in the email thread.

Toon Claes confirms the patch successfully resolves the build failure in their testing environment. They note the problematic header inclusion chain comes through OpenSSL headers, but emphasize this is just implementation detail - the core issue is the macro collision, and the patch appears to handle it.

The fix seems straightforward and well-targeted, addressing a specific platform compatibility issue without requiring broader changes to Git's ctype handling. With positive testing results from at least one environment, this appears ready for consideration as a focused platform-specific workaround.
