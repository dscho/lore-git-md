This is a status update on Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for pack-objects and MIDX handling around cruft packs, which is now ready to merge to 'next'. The series introduces several improvements including a new `--stdin-packs=follow` option and name-hash traversal for unpacked objects, with the main goal of avoiding references to objects in cruft packs from MIDX files.

The patch series has undergone review from Elijah Newren and Jeff King (Peff), with only minor bugfix-related changes since their last review. It has been running successfully in production at GitHub for 1-2 months, providing confidence in its stability. The changes affect pack-objects behavior and MIDX handling, particularly around cruft pack management.

Junio Hamano indicates he will merge the series to 'next' soon unless unexpected objections arise from the previous reviewers. This represents the final step before potential inclusion in Git 3.0, following successful real-world testing and positive technical reviews.
