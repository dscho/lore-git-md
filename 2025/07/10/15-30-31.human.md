This is a follow-up in the ongoing SIGCHLD handling discussion for `git daemon` on Windows platforms.

Junio Hamano responds to Johannes Sixt's comments about Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's proposal to change MinGW's SIGCHLD signal number from 17 to 20 to match Cygwin/macOS behavior. While Johannes doesn't object to the change, he expresses curiosity about why the different signal number produces different error behavior (EINVAL with 20 vs silent failure with 17). 

Junio emphasizes that any commit changing this value must clearly explain in its log message both why the change matters and the source of the behavioral differences between signal numbers. This continues the thread's technical discussion about Windows' non-POSIX signal handling quirks, though the core issue remains unresolved pending further investigation into the platform-specific behaviors.

The exchange shows maintainers being cautious about platform-specific changes, wanting clear documentation of the rationale even for seemingly minor adjustments to signal handling constants. This reflects Git's careful approach to cross-platform compatibility issues.
