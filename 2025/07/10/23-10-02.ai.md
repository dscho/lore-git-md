Refactoring patch in the per-source MIDX tracking series. This is Taylor Blau's review of the initial patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 8-part series restructuring MIDX handling to attach to individual `struct odb_source` instances rather than tracking globally.

Taylor seeks clarification on the distinction between "object database" and "source" concepts, particularly regarding how alternates fit into this model. He suggests that if alternates are considered distinct sources, the approach makes sense, but wants confirmation this aligns with the patch's intent. The review notes the conversion of `prepare_multi_pack_index_one()` to work with `struct odb_source` rather than repository+path pairs, though questions whether the `local` parameter should also be a source property.

Several style observations are made: the mixed brace usage in conditionals (which Taylor would prefer as a separate cleanup), the verbose `multi_pack_index` field name (suggesting `midx` instead), and some unrelated declaration reordering in header files that obscures the actual changes. The technical substance of moving MIDX tracking to per-source storage is understood and appears sound, with Taylor noting the one-to-one relationship between source and object_dir simplifies the lookup logic.

The review is constructive but highlights some areas where the patch could be clearer or split for better reviewability. No major technical objections are raised to the core approach of attaching MIDX state to sources rather than the global object database.
