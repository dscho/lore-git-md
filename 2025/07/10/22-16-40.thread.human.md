The conversation has now shifted to validation strategies. Justin Tobler suggested length-based validation (requiring 2-3 character ISO 639 codes) as an alternative to explicit filtering. This represents a middle ground between minimal filtering and complex locale mapping.

Key open questions remain about whether this qualifies as a bugfix (affecting merge timing) and whether length-based validation provides enough practical benefit over the current approach to warrant changing direction.
