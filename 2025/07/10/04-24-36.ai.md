This is a status update in the ongoing discussion about Taylor Blau's performance optimization series for pack-objects and MIDX handling around cruft packs. The 9-patch series introduces `--stdin-packs=follow` and name-hash traversal to avoid pointing into cruft packs from MIDX files, improving performance for pack operations. 

The key development in this email is Elijah Newren confirming the series is ready to merge to 'next', following Taylor Blau's earlier confirmation that it has been running successfully in GitHub's production environment for months. Junio C Hamano acknowledges this and indicates he will proceed with merging unless unexpected objections arise. 

The series has already undergone review from Elijah Newren and Jeff King, with only minor bugfix-related changes since their last review. This marks the final approval needed before the performance optimizations can progress towards inclusion in Git 3.0, joining other major infrastructure changes like SHA-256 default and reftable adoption that are also moving forward.
