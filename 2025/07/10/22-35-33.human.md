This is patch 5/8 in Patrick Steinhardt's series refactoring MIDX handling to move from global tracking to per-source storage, part of the broader effort to prepare for pluggable object database backends. The patch converts `get_multi_pack_index()` to operate on individual `struct odb_source` instances rather than returning a global linked list, requiring callers to explicitly prepare alternates and iterate through sources.

The changes touch several key areas where MIDX lookups occur, including pack-objects, repack, and bitmap handling. Justin Tobler's review notes the necessity of the new `odb_prepare_alternates()` calls and praises the simplification in `lookup_multi_pack_index()`, which now directly queries the source rather than walking a global list. The patch maintains careful backward compatibility while advancing the series' goal of eliminating global MIDX state.

This continues the series' pattern of methodical, well-explained refactoring with thorough updates to all affected call sites. The changes are mechanical but important for the larger architectural shift, and reviewers have so far found the approach sound.
