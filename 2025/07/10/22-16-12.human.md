This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series to move MIDX tracking from global state to per-source storage. The patch introduces a `multi_pack_index_loaded` flag to avoid repeatedly attempting to load non-existent MIDX files for a source.

Justin Tobler's review raises a good question about whether this optimization is actually needed, since `prepare_packed_git()` (which initializes MIDX state) only runs once per repository anyway. The patch appears technically sound but may be solving a problem that doesn't exist in practice.

The discussion highlights an interesting tension in refactoring work - while the change makes logical sense for the new per-source model, it's worth questioning whether each piece of complexity is truly necessary. This kind of careful review helps keep the codebase clean during major internal reorganizations.
