This is a discussion about wording choices in the ongoing documentation patch series regarding Git's contributor name policy.

Jeff King (peff) responds to brian m. carlson's concerns about using CNCF-style legal phrasing in the updated SubmittingPatches documentation. The thread has established that pseudonyms are acceptable as long as they provide persistent, verifiable identities for legal compliance (DCO, OFAC sanctions, license enforcement), but there's now debate about how to phrase this requirement clearly.

Peff acknowledges carlson's points about the CNCF wording being potentially confusing, especially for non-native English speakers, while still appreciating how it emphasizes the policy's purpose - ensuring contributors can be contacted about their work. The key tension is between legal precision (CNCF approach) and approachable language (carlson's Wikipedia-inspired alternative).

The exchange shows consensus on the policy's substance but refinement needed in presentation. Carlsen plans to incorporate feedback in a v2 patch that will maintain all legal requirements while using more natural language. This represents the final polishing stage of a policy change that has already received broad agreement on its core principles after thorough legal and technical review.
