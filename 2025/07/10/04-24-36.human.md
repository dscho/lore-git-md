This is a status update on the `tb/midx-avoid-cruft-packs` performance optimization patch series. The 9-commit series from Taylor Blau teaches `pack-objects` to avoid pointing into objects in cruft packs from MIDX files, introducing new functionality like `--stdin-packs=follow` and name-hash traversal optimizations.

The series has been running successfully in GitHub's production environment for 1-2 months without issues. After positive reviews from Elijah Newren and Jeff King earlier, and with only minor bugfix-related changes since then, both Taylor and Elijah confirm the series is ready to merge to 'next'. Junio Hamano acknowledges this and will proceed with merging unless unexpected objections arise.

This is part of Git 3.0's infrastructure improvements, alongside other major changes like SHA-256 default hashes and reftable adoption. The patch appears well-tested and production-proven, with no outstanding technical concerns from reviewers.
