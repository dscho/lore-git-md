This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's series moving MIDX tracking from global to per-source storage. The patch introduces a `multi_pack_index_loaded` flag to `struct odb_source` to avoid repeatedly attempting to load non-existent MIDX files when iterating sources individually.

Justin Tobler's review questions whether this optimization is necessary given that `prepare_packed_git()` (which initializes MIDX state) currently only executes once per repository due to the `packed_git_initialized` check. He confirms the technical correctness of the patch but suggests the commit message may overstate the problem being solved.

The patch touches core MIDX handling in `midx.c` and `odb.h`, adding state tracking while maintaining the series' goal of moving MIDX management to individual object sources. This continues the incremental transition from global to per-source MIDX tracking, with Justin noting the temporary dual-storage approach appears necessary for the refactoring. The interaction with existing initialization checks remains an open question that may need clarification in the commit message.
