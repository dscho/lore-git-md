Refactoring series to standardize use of C99 `bool` type for predicate functions in Git's codebase, concluding an 18-month experimental period that began with commit 8277dbe987 in December 2023. Series consists of 3 patches with cover letter, authored by Phillip Wood and now fully reviewed with final acks from Elijah Newren, Junio C Hamano, and Brian m. carlson.

First patch updates CodingGuidelines to explicitly permit using `bool` from `<stdbool.h>` for predicate functions. Second patch converts case-insensitive string prefix matching functions (`skip_iprefix()` and `skip_iprefix_mem()`) in `git-compat-util.h` from `int` to `bool` returns. Final patch standardizes return types in strbuf functions (`starts_with()`, `istarts_with()`, `starts_with_mem()`, `skip_to_optional_arg_default()`) from `int` to `bool`, completing the series with 20 lines changed across 4 files (28 insertions, 20 deletions).
