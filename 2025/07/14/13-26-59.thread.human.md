This thread concludes a long-running effort to standardize the use of C99 `bool` type for predicate functions in Git's codebase. The series, authored by Phillip Wood, refactors string utilities in `git-compat-util.h` and `strbuf` methods to return `bool` instead of `int`, with no behavioral changes. The change follows an 18-month experimental period that began with commit 8277dbe987 in December 2023.

The series received thorough review from key contributors: Elijah Newren provided technical review and final approval, Eric Sunshine corrected documentation typos, and Brian m. carlson contributed style guidance. The last remaining concern - platform compatibility regarding `<stdbool.h>` inclusion raised by Randall S. Becker - was resolved when Junio C Hamano confirmed the header has been unconditionally included via `git-compat-util.h` for the past 18 months without issues. Becker's follow-up message acknowledged this explanation, providing the final sign-off.

With all reviews addressed and no remaining concerns, the series is now cleared for inclusion. This marks the successful conclusion of the effort to modernize predicate function signatures while maintaining Git's conservative approach to C99 adoption, demonstrating the project's commitment to thorough compatibility validation even for long-settled changes.
