This thread documents the completion of a long-running effort to standardize the use of C99 `bool` for predicate functions in Git's codebase. Phillip Wood's 3-patch series converts string utility functions in `git-compat-util.h` from `int` to `bool` returns, formalizing 18 months of production use since the initial experiment began in December 2023. The changes are purely mechanical type updates with no behavioral impact, accompanied by documentation updates to the CodingGuidelines.

The series received thorough review from key contributors: Elijah Newren provided technical review, Eric Sunshine caught typos, Brian m. carlson offered style guidance, and Junio Hamano contributed semantic rationale. Randall S. Becker confirmed platform compatibility, addressing the last potential concern. The only remaining issue was a minor subject line typo ("Guildlines") spotted by Jeff King, which was promptly corrected.

In a lighthearted coda to the technical discussion, Elijah Newren shared a humorous note about "Muphry's law" - the observation that any criticism of writing errors tends to contain its own errors. This exchange serves as a playful epilogue to a series that has now received final approval, with all substantive issues addressed and the bool standardization effort concluded successfully.
