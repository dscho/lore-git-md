This thread documents the completion of an 18-month effort to standardize Git's use of C99 `bool` for predicate functions. Phillip Wood led the series, which began with an experimental commit in December 2023 and has now concluded with full approval from core maintainers including Junio Hamano, Elijah Newren, and Brian m. carlson.

The changes are purely mechanical, converting function return types from `int` to `bool` while maintaining identical behavior. The series started by updating CodingGuidelines to officially permit `bool` for predicates, then converted case-insensitive string matching functions, and now concludes with standardizing `strbuf` helper functions like `starts_with()` and `skip_to_optional_arg_default()`. Reviewers verified all aspects - from technical correctness (Elijah Newren) to style (Brian m. carlson) and platform compatibility (Randall S. Becker). Even minor documentation typos were caught and fixed during review.

With this final strbuf conversions (20 lines across 4 files), the project has fully adopted `bool` for predicate functions where appropriate, marking a small but meaningful step in Git's gradual modernization while maintaining its conservative approach to C99 features. The changes will appear in an upcoming release, closing this long-running standardization effort.
