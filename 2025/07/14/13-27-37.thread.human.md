This thread explores the potential adoption of C99 compound literals in Git's codebase, sparked by Phillip Wood's initial test balloon patch that modified `strbuf_init()` to demonstrate the technique. The proposal highlights readability benefits for struct initialization and init functions, particularly for simple cases without resource management needs.

Junio C Hamano has weighed in with measured concerns, acknowledging the pattern's elegance for one-time-use structs but cautioning about maintainability risks when structs later require cleanup logic. He specifically worries that widespread adoption could complicate future additions of members needing `_release()` functions, and suggests marking the `strbuf.c` change as experimental to prevent premature copying of the pattern. The discussion now centers on balancing code clarity against Git's conservative approach to compiler requirements and long-term code health, with future exchanges likely to focus on specific compiler support and guidelines for appropriate use cases.
