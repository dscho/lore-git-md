License clarification for git-phoenix recovery tool. Daniil Iaitskov responds to Christian Couder's concern about the project license by pointing to the BSD-3-Clause license specified in both the project's cabal file and its Hackage package listing. The response indicates the license choice was made for popularity rather than specific compatibility considerations, and invites input on alternative license preferences. This addresses the key legal question raised in the thread while leaving open the possibility of license changes if community members express strong preferences. The tool remains in early stages with its technical approach still undefined, though now with clearer licensing status that may affect adoption decisions. The pending Git Rev News mention could still drive further community engagement with the project.
