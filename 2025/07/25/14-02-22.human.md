Feature enhancement for `git rebase` conflict resolution transparency. Phillip Wood responds to Junio Hamano's proposed implementation for warning users when commits are skipped during rebase due to empty changes after conflict resolution.

Wood identifies two potential edge cases where the warning might incorrectly trigger: after a "break" command or failed "exec" command. He suggests checking for rebase_path_message() to avoid false positives and proposes reading REBASE_HEAD to make the warning more informative by including the skipped commit details. The response also notes the warning would consistently appear for both `--continue` and `--skip` operations, which Wood considers reasonable behavior.

This moves the discussion toward implementation specifics, addressing how to make the warning both accurate and useful while maintaining the original goal of making rebase behavior more transparent. The technical focus remains on sequencer.c, with no mention yet of extending this to other commands like cherry-pick.
