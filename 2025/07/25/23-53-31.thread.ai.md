Thread summary:

This thread discusses the potential Rustification of Git's xdiff library, examining implications for downstream projects and platform compatibility. The core debate centers on whether to introduce a hard Rust dependency for xdiff, with Patrick Steinhardt noting libgit2 is unlikely to adopt Rust-based changes. Ben Knoble points to an existing C-only xdiff fork maintained by libgit2 (github.com/libgit2/xdiff) that could serve as an alternative for projects like Vim, though he expresses preference for eventual Rust adoption to gain performance and safety benefits.

Technical considerations include build system modifications to support alternate xdiff implementations, Rust compiler availability on niche platforms (Gentoo, HPPA, Alpha, m68k), and potential circular dependencies between the git2 crate and libgit2. Ben offers to assist with Vim integration and proposes an intermediate solution allowing custom xdiff library linking.

The discussion remains unresolved, highlighting tensions between modernization (Rust's safety/performance advantages) and broad platform compatibility concerns. Key participants are Patrick Steinhardt (GitLab) and Ben Knoble, with the libgit2-maintained C fork emerging as a potential compromise solution for downstream consumers not ready for Rust dependencies.
