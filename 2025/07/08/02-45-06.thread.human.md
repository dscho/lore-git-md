This thread documents a straightforward correction to Git's glossary regarding pathspec matching behavior. Russell Hanneken identified and fixed an inaccuracy where the documentation incorrectly claimed that "**/foo" and "foo" were equivalent in glob pathspec contexts. In reality, these patterns behave differently - "**/foo" matches at any directory level while "foo" only matches at the top level.

The change is minimal (just a few lines in glossary-content.adoc) but important for accurate documentation.
