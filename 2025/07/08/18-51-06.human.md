Maintainer feedback on comment character deprecation. Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's series removing the problematic `core.commentChar=auto` feature, suggesting simplifications to the migration path. He questions the current approach of showing verbose `git config` commands to users, proposing instead to treat "auto" as equivalent to "#" after Git 3.0. This would make the transition smoother by avoiding the need for users to manually update their configs. He also notes the current advice nudges users toward global config when project-specific settings might be preferable. The feedback indicates general agreement with the deprecation but suggests refining the user experience aspects of the change.
