Process discussion continuation in the fast-export signature format thread. This email from Junio Hamano responds to Christian Couder's defense of his review response methodology, where Christian argued that delivering concrete solutions in new patch versions can be more thoughtful than immediate acknowledgments.

Junio acknowledges that well-reasoned responses take time but suggests a middle ground - providing preliminary feedback within a few days even if the full solution takes longer. He notes that completely silent periods can discourage continued discussion, as reviewers may assume their points were ignored. Junio also gently challenges Christian's distinction between newcomer and experienced contributor norms, suggesting that consistent communication patterns help the whole community.

The exchange highlights an ongoing tension in open source collaboration between:
1) The value of deep, focused work before responding
2) The need for timely signals to keep review discussions alive
3) Balancing individual working styles with shared community expectations

This continues the thread's parallel tracks of technical progress on signature handling and meta-discussion about review process norms. The technical implementation appears stable in v5, while the process discussion remains active with Junio advocating for more intermediate communication even from experienced contributors.
