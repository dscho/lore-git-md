This is a follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt's Meson build system improvement series, specifically addressing the feature summary display. Justin Tobler confirms the changes in patch 3/8, which improves the auto-detected features output by:

1. Showing version numbers for dependencies (like curl 8.14.1 and expat 2.7.1) where available
2. Consistently formatting all entries as YES/NO (previously some showed "true/false")
3. Adding colorization (green for YES, red for NO) as a side effect of the bool_yn change

The change is straightforward - it passes dependency objects directly to the summary() function rather than just their .found() status, and enables the bool_yn formatting option. Justin verifies the output matches expectations and gives the patch his approval, noting the colorization was an implicit but welcome improvement.

This is a small but user-visible polish to the build system's output, making it more informative and consistent. The change is well-contained (12-line diff) and builds on the series' broader modernization of Git's Meson infrastructure. With Justin's confirmation, this piece appears ready to proceed as part of the overall series.
