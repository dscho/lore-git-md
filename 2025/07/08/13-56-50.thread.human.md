Junio C Hamano has expressed reservations about full deprecation, suggesting alternatives like making "auto" silently default to "#" or documenting the feature's limitations instead. Phillip maintains that neither approach solves the core architectural problems and that modern Git's support for multi-byte comment strings reduces the need for automatic selection.

In the latest exchange, Phillip incorporated minor wording improvements from Ayush Chandekar's review of the first patch's commit message, fixing grammatical issues without changing the technical substance. The discussion remains at an impasse between Phillip's preference for explicit deprecation with migration guidance and Junio's inclination toward a simpler behavior change. The patches are now technically refined but still await a final decision on whether to proceed with deprecation.
