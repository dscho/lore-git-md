Performance optimization series for bloom filters in revision traversal. This is the final version (v6) of a series that enables bloom filter optimizations for multiple literal pathspecs, addressing a performance issue reported by Kai Koponen where combined pathspecs were slower than separate invocations.

The key change is introducing `struct bloom_keyvec` to manage multiple bloom keys, replacing direct `struct bloom_key *` usage in `rev_info`. The implementation now processes all `revs->pruning.pathspec.items` through a new `convert_pathspec_to_filter()` helper function. The optimization applies when all pathspec items are literal (no wildcards or special magic bits).

Performance improvements are substantial: 1.33x faster in the Git repository (55.1ms vs 73.1ms) and 7.5x faster in the LLVM repository (263ms vs 1.974s) for multi-pathspec queries. The series includes comprehensive test coverage for various pathspec scenarios and maintains backward compatibility with single-pathspec cases.

This version incorporates all feedback from reviewers (Junio Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, Derrick Stolee, and others), with final cleanup of unused variables in `prepare_to_use_bloom_filter()` and better isolation of pathspec conversion logic. The changes touch bloom.c/h, revision.c/h, blame.c, line-log.c, and test updates in t4216-log-bloom.sh.

The series appears well-tested and reviewed, with all substantive technical concerns addressed. It's a clear performance win for a common case (multiple literal pathspecs) while maintaining correct behavior for wildcard cases. The benchmark results show the optimization is particularly effective in larger repositories.
