Platform compatibility response to Windows line ending issue. Torsten Bögershausen responds to Jason Cho's report about unexpected CRLF endings despite `core.eol=lf` and `core.autocrlf=false` settings. The response suggests two key points: first, that the file may have been committed with CRLFs originally (which Git preserves by default), and second, that using `.gitattributes` with `text=auto` would provide more reliable line ending control. The reply requests additional diagnostic information via `git ls-files --eol` to determine the file's stored line endings, and recommends the standard practice of adding a `.gitattributes` file with `* text=auto` followed by a renormalization commit. This maintains the technical focus of the thread while providing concrete next steps for investigation and resolution. The response implies that the observed behavior may be working as designed rather than being a bug, though this depends on the file's actual stored line endings.
