This is a final discussion point in the deprecation of `core.commentChar=auto`. Oswald Buddenhagen weighs in with two key suggestions to simplify the implementation. First, rather than providing specific migration instructions in the deprecation warning, they propose simply directing users to `git config list --show-scope` to identify where the problematic setting originates. This avoids getting into policy questions about whether users should unset or override the value. Second, they strongly endorse the already-decided approach of making Git refuse to operate when encountering 'auto' post-3.0, calling this the simplest and most fail-safe solution.

The email represents the last bits of feedback before Phillip Wood's planned final submission of the patch series next week. With Junio C Hamano having already approved the implementation approach and all technical questions resolved, Oswald's suggestions appear to be minor refinements rather than objections to the core plan. The thread shows consensus forming around making the deprecation as straightforward as possible while ensuring users clearly understand how to migrate away from the problematic 'auto' behavior.
