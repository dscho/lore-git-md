This is a documentation patch proposing more inclusive contributor guidelines for Git, specifically regarding name requirements in commit signatures (Signed-off-by trailers). It's a reply in an ongoing thread where brian m. carlson initially proposed relaxing the "real name" requirement to accommodate various legitimate cases where contributors might prefer pseudonyms or preferred names.

Jacob Keller provides a straightforward review, expressing full agreement with the proposed changes. The original proposal (which this email is responding to) argued for allowing non-real names in several situations: for transgender/non-binary contributors avoiding deadnames, well-known pseudonymous developers, and those with privacy/safety concerns. The key balance being struck is between traceability (still requiring some consistent identity) and inclusivity.

The patch appears well-motivated and thoughtfully considered, addressing real concerns about making the project more welcoming while maintaining accountability. With this positive review from another contributor, the proposal seems likely to move forward unless other concerns are raised in the thread.
