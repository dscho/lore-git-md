The test uses the now-standard pattern of `test_expect_code 129` combined with case-insensitive `test_grep` for the usage string.

This follows the established template from earlier patches in the series, applying it to the transport command `send-pack`. The test logic remains identical to its original form - only its location changes. The move demonstrates that help behavior testing is independent of the command's primary context (in this case, transport operations).

The patch is mechanically similar to the previous moves in the series (checkout-index, for-each-ref, etc.), completing the coverage of plumbing, porcelain, and now transport commands.
