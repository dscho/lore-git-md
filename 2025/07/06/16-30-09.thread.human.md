The thread discusses a proposal to relax Git's requirement for real names in Signed-off-by trailers, allowing pseudonyms or preferred names while still prohibiting anonymous contributions. The initial patch from brian m. carlson modifies Documentation/SubmittingPatches to replace strict "real name" language with more flexible wording, citing the Linux kernel's "known identity" approach as precedent. The change aims to better accommodate contributors with privacy concerns, transgender/non-binary developers avoiding deadnames, and well-known pseudonymous contributors.

Jacob Keller has responded with a brief but positive review, providing a Reviewed-by tag without suggesting any modifications. This indicates at least some community support for the change, though broader discussion may still emerge about how to balance inclusivity with accountability. The proposal remains in its initial version (v1), and further feedback may address wording refinements or explore the policy's implications for project governance.
