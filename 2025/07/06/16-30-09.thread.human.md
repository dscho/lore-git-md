This thread discusses a proposed change to Git's contribution guidelines that would relax the real-name requirement in Signed-off-by trailers, allowing pseudonyms and preferred names while maintaining traceability. The initial proposal from brian m. carlson has gained support from Jacob Keller (Reviewed-by) and Junio Hamano, though Junio emphasizing the need to prohibit complete anonymity for legal compliance - a point reinforced by Theodore Ts'o's analysis of OFAC sanctions requirements.

The discussion has expanded to address multiple dimensions: technical identity persistence (raised by Ayush Chandekar), project-specific policy framing, and now two distinct legal considerations - Theodore Ts'o's sanctions compliance analysis and Phillip Wood's new suggestion to consult the Software Freedom Conservancy about license enforcement implications. While there's clear consensus forming around allowing pseudonyms with verifiable persistence as the path forward, the thread now awaits: (1) incorporation of Junio's stronger anonymity prohibition language, (2) resolution of Chandekar's identity persistence concerns, and (3) legal review from both sanctions and license enforcement perspectives. The next version of the patch will need to address all these aspects while maintaining the balance between inclusivity and legal compliance that has emerged as the thread's central theme.
