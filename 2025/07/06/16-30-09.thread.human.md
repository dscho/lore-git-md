This thread discusses proposed changes to Git's contribution guidelines regarding name requirements in Signed-off-by trailers, with the goal of allowing pseudonyms while maintaining legal compliance. The discussion has expanded from initial policy principles to address multiple legal dimensions: DCO enforceability, OFAC sanctions compliance, and GPL license enforcement.

The current proposal, now at v1, has received three reviews and is evolving to incorporate feedback. Junio Hamano supports the direction but insists on stronger language prohibiting complete anonymity, while Theodore Ts'o and Phillip Wood have raised important legal considerations around sanctions and license enforcement. Jeff King (peff) has now weighed in, supporting pseudonyms but emphasizing the need to properly document the precedent set by the Linux kernel's similar policy change (commit d4563201f33a) and CNCF's DCO guidelines.

Key points of consensus are emerging: pseudonyms will be allowed but must be verifiably persistent identities, and the policy change needs clear legal justification addressing all compliance dimensions. Next steps include drafting language that satisfies these requirements, with consultation from the Software Freedom Conservancy recommended by multiple participants. The thread remains active as these legal and implementation details are worked through.
