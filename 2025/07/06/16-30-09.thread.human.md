The discussion has evolved from initial policy principles to detailed implementation concerns, covering DCO enforceability, OFAC sanctions, GPL license enforcement, and alignment with legal precedents from the Linux kernel and CNCF.

Martin Agren confirmed the Linux kernel's similar policy had legal vetting.
