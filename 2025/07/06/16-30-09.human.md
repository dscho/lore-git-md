This is a documentation patch proposing changes to Git's contribution guidelines regarding author names. Brian M. Carlson suggests relaxing the requirement for contributors to use their real names in the `Signed-off-by` trailer, while still maintaining accountability by requiring a "known identity" (pseudonymous or otherwise).

The patch makes a thoughtful case for this change, citing several valid scenarios where real names may be problematic: transgender/non-binary contributors avoiding deadnames, well-known pseudonymous developers, privacy/security concerns, and harassment risks. The proposal maintains the prohibition on fully anonymous contributions while making the policy more inclusive.

The change is minimal - just 5 lines modified in SubmittingPatches - but addresses an important community issue. The author acknowledges the wording may need refinement and invites discussion. This appears to be a well-considered change that aligns with Git's code of conduct values around inclusivity, while preserving accountability through the "known identity" requirement.
