This is a reply in the ongoing thread about adding X-Change-ID headers to Git email formats. The email from Aditya Garg raises concerns about the proposed approach, arguing against using custom email headers due to compatibility issues with various email providers and clients.

The key objections are:
1. Many email providers (specifically mentioning ProtonMail Bridge and MS Graph) strip or don't preserve custom headers
2. Common email clients like Thunderbird and Mutt may not handle custom headers well
3. Suggests putting the change ID in the email body (like Signed-off-by trailers) would be more reliable

This introduces a new technical consideration to the thread's open questions - whether header-based approach is viable given real-world email infrastructure limitations. The reply doesn't propose an alternative implementation but clearly states a preference for body-based storage of the change ID.

The thread context shows this is part of a v2 series that added parsing support (patch 2/2) while keeping the header generation unchanged from v1 (patch 1/2). Previous discussion had focused on testing challenges (with Jeff King providing a workaround) but hadn't yet addressed this email infrastructure compatibility angle.
