This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's object database (ODB) subsystem to eliminate `the_repository` global state. Patch 15/17 continues the systematic renaming of ODB-related functions, changing `has_object()` to `odb_has_object()` to match the established `odb_` prefix convention.

The change follows the same pattern as previous patches in the series, introducing a compatibility wrapper while updating 30 call sites across the codebase. The patch is mechanical and straightforward, with no behavior changes - it's purely about naming consistency as part of the larger architectural effort to make ODB operations more explicit and enable future pluggable backends.

Review feedback from earlier patches in the series has been incorporated, and this change aligns with the already-reviewed patterns. The series as a whole is nearing completion with high confidence in the architectural changes. This particular patch appears well-executed and uncontroversial, continuing the methodical conversion of the earlier patches.
