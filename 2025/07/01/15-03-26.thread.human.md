This thread documents the development of pagination support in `git for-each-ref` and the associated MemorySanitizer (MSan) instrumentation challenges that surfaced during testing. The pagination feature, implemented by Karthik Nayak with `--start-after` support, has been merged to 'next' after addressing all critical issues around memory safety, integer handling, and seek behavior. The implementation now provides O(log N) performance across all ref backends with comprehensive tests.

The MSan discussion, led by Jeff King, revealed that the warnings were false positives originating from cryptographic library interactions - specifically OpenSSL's `csprng_bytes()` propagating through `git_mkstemps_mode()`. Jeff identified three potential solutions: manual memory unpoisoning (demonstrated in a patch), Clang's ignorelist feature (suggested by Karthik), or full instrumentation of system libraries. The thread reached consensus that proper instrumentation of dependencies is the most robust long-term solution, though the immediate issues can be worked around. Importantly, these MSan warnings do not indicate actual bugs in Git's pagination implementation.

Junio Hamano has verified the design decisions and documentation, with only final polish remaining before full stabilization. The thread shows complete technical resolution of both the pagination feature and associated instrumentation challenges, with contributions from Karthik Nayak (implementation), Jeff King (MSan investigation), and Kyle Lippincott (initial bug reports). The cryptographic library interactions have been identified as a known challenge point for MSan that extends beyond this specific feature.
