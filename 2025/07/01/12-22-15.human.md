This is part 3/17 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to refactor Git's object database (ODB) subsystem, continuing the work to eliminate the `the_repository` global state. 

The patch renames the core object storage files from "object-store.{c,h}" to "odb.{c,h}" to better reflect their purpose after the previous structural renames in the series. This is a mechanical change that updates the include paths in 126 files across the codebase, with no functional changes to the actual code. The rename aligns with the new naming conventions established earlier in the series where `struct raw_object_store` was renamed to `struct object_database` (ODB).

The change is straightforward and well-contained, touching only include directives and build system references. It follows logically from the previous patches in the series and helps maintain consistency in the codebase's terminology. The patch has already received review from Derrick Stolee and Justin Tobler earlier in the series.
