Policy proposal refinement on AI-generated contributions in Git.

Junio Hamano responds to Christian Couder's concerns about the scope of the proposed AI contribution ban, agreeing that the current wording may be too broad. The discussion centers on two key aspects: what types of AI-generated content should be prohibited, and whether AI-assisted review should be allowed.

Hamano supports extending the prohibition beyond just code to include all contributions like logos and documentation, seeing this as equally important for maintaining clear provenance. He views commit messages as integral parts of contributions that should fall under the ban. However, he acknowledges Couder's point that the current wording might unintentionally prohibit beneficial uses of AI like code review assistance, which would be analogous to human peer review.

The email shows the maintainer engaging with the feedback constructively, inviting specific wording suggestions to better distinguish between prohibited generation and permitted assistance. This moves the discussion forward from the initial broad-strokes proposal to more nuanced implementation details, while maintaining the core principle of protecting the project from copyright uncertainty. The exchange suggests the final policy will likely be more precisely scoped than the initial draft.
