This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series (v6, 2/17) to refactor Git's object database (ODB) subsystem, specifically targeting the removal of `the_repository` global state. The patch renames `struct object_directory` to `struct odb_source` to better reflect its role as a storage interface that may not always be directory-based in the future (e.g., for pluggable backends like databases).

The change is mechanical but widespread, updating 28 files with straightforward renames (`object_directory` -> `odb_source`) and related function/variable name changes. The commit message explains the rationale well: the current name is too filesystem-specific, and alternatives like `odb_backend` or `odb_alternate` were rejected in favor of the more neutral `odb_source`. 

This foundational rename sets up clearer terminology for future work on pluggable ODBs. The patch has already been reviewed by Justin Tobler and Derrick Stolee, with all feedback addressed in this v6 iteration. As part of a larger architectural effort, this change is low-risk but important for the series' goal of making object storage more flexible.
