Performance optimization follow-up on pathspec bloom filters. This is a technical discussion between Junio Hamano and Lidong Yan about potential future optimizations for Git's bloom filter handling of multiple pathspecs.

The exchange builds on the recently proposed bloom filter changes (patches 1-2 in this series) that introduced support for multiple pathspec queries. Junio suggests exploring whether `.nowildcard_len` could be used to optimize wildcard pathspec handling, rather than completely disabling bloom filters for them. Lidong responds that this approach could potentially work for most pathspec magic flags except PATHSPEC_EXCLUDE, though notes uncertainty about PATHSPEC_ICASE's behavior.

Lidong provides a comprehensive list of all pathspec magic flags (PATHSPEC_FROMTOP, PATHSPEC_MAXDEPTH, etc.) to help evaluate which might benefit from this optimization. The discussion remains theoretical at this point, identifying this as a promising direction for future work rather than proposing immediate code changes. The technical depth suggests both participants are thinking carefully about how to maximize bloom filter's efficiency while maintaining correctness across all pathspec types.
