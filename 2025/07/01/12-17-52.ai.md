This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal series, specifically addressing a naming question from Justin Tobler about patch 2/17. The original patch renamed `struct object_directory` to `struct odb_source` and updated related functions, including changing `find_odb()` to take the new type. Justin had suggested the function should be renamed to `find_odb_source()` to better match the new type name.

Patrick Steinhardt clarifies that this naming will be further refined in a subsequent patch (5/17) where the function becomes `odb_find_source()`, following the established `odb_` prefix convention for the new API. This confirms the naming concern is already addressed in the series' planned progression, with the current patch being an intermediate step in the larger refactoring effort. The exchange shows the careful attention to naming consistency throughout this architectural change.
