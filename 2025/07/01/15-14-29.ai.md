Documentation patch standardizing terminology in git-config subcommands. This is part of a v2 series addressing inconsistencies in the ps/config-subcommands merge (fe3ccc7aab).

The patch updates all occurrences of `--value=<value>` to `--value=<pattern>` in both the git-config.adoc documentation and builtin/config.c implementation. This aligns with the terminology used in the "Deprecated Modes" section and better reflects that the parameter accepts pattern matching. The change affects the synopsis and usage strings for `get`, `set`, and `unset` subcommands.

The patch carries an Acked-by from Patrick Steinhardt, indicating maintainer approval. It resolves one of the inconsistencies identified in the initial submission and fixes a test failure in t0450.142 by making the documentation match the implementation. The series notes show this was part of v2 changes to address testing gaps from the first submission.
