Christian Couder responds to Junio Hamano's initial patch that would forbid AI-generated contributions to Git, noting that the current proposal may be overly broad in its restrictions. Second, the phrasing might be interpreted to forbid even using AI tools for code review or validation, which wasn't the intended scope.

The email suggests the policy needs more precise language to target only the problematic cases (AI-generated code submissions) while allowing legitimate uses of AI assistance in the development process.
