This is the second patch in a 3-part refactoring series that moves sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to the `repo_settings` struct, as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

The patch specifically migrates the `core.sparsecheckoutcone` setting, eliminating the global `core_sparse_checkout_cone` variable. The change follows the same pattern as the first patch (which handled `core.sparsecheckout`), with careful attention to avoiding redundant `prepare_repo_settings()` calls. The modifications span 10 files, primarily updating access patterns in `builtin/sparse-checkout.c` and related plumbing.

The series appears technically sound, with all prior feedback addressed in this v6 iteration. The changes are focused and mechanical, touching only the necessary spots while maintaining existing behavior. This continues the project's gradual elimination of globals while improving code organization and libification readiness.
