This is a new contributor guidance email from Brian Carlson, offering additional suggestions to Eric Frederickson about how to get started contributing to Git. The email doesn't introduce any technical changes, but rather provides additional onboarding advice that complements the microprojects-first approach already discussed in the thread. It's particularly notable for its practical suggestions about how to identify good first tasks in the codebase (grepping for TODOs, looking at test failures) and its realistic perspective about the learning curve involved in contributing to Git.
