This is the first patch in a v6 series refactoring sparse-checkout configuration as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. It moves the `core.sparsecheckout` setting from a global variable to the `repo_settings` struct, eliminating the `core_apply_sparse_checkout` global.

The patch carefully manages initialization by adding `prepare_repo_settings()` calls where needed (in backfill, clone, mv, and wt-status operations) while removing redundant calls (in worktree and unpack-trees operations). It also removes the now-unneeded `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from backfill.c.

The changes are well-considered, with each modification clearly explained in the commit message. The patch appears technically sound, having gone through five previous iterations addressing feedback about initialization timing and performance. It's part of a larger effort to improve Git's modularity by removing global state, and this particular change looks ready for inclusion.
