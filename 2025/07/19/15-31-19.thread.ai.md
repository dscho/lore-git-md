Core problem: `git bisect` output may include known-good commits in the "could be bad" list when only skipped commits remain, despite having correctly identified the actual bad commit. The reproduction case uses a linear 3-commit history (good, skipped, bad) where marking HEAD as bad and HEAD~2 as good, then skipping HEAD~1, shows both the good and skipped commits as potential bad candidates.
