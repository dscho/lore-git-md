This is an announcement for a community event rather than a code contribution. Patrick Steinhardt is organizing a Git Mini Summit alongside Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam on August 28th. The half-day event will feature lightning talks and networking time, serving as a more accessible alternative to Git Merge 2025 for those who can't attend the main conference or are already going to OSS Europe.

The summit will cost $10 (either as an add-on to OSS Europe tickets or standalone), with registration opening soon. Talk proposals are being accepted until July 28th, to be vetted by Patrick and other organizers. This appears to be a well-organized community initiative with clear logistics and reasonable pricing, though the short lead time between announcement and event date might limit participation from those needing to arrange travel.
