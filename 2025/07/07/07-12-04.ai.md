This is a discussion email in the ongoing thread about change ID tracking in Git's email workflow, specifically addressing signature handling and metadata placement.

Drew DeVault responds to Martin von Zweigbergk's questions about commit signatures in the email workflow, clarifying that there's currently no support for preserving commit signatures through `git format-patch`/`git-am`. He notes the common confusion between commit signatures and PGP signatures, emphasizing they are separate systems that can't meaningfully interact.

The email strongly advocates for using email headers rather than commit trailers or body placement for metadata like change IDs. Drew argues against putting additional metadata between the `---` separator and diff or after the final `--` line, maintaining that email headers are the proper structured format for this purpose. This reinforces his earlier position supporting Junio Hamano's generic header preservation approach over the original change-ID-specific proposal.

Key technical points include:
- Current lack of signature preservation in format-patch/am workflow
- Distinction between commit signatures and PGP signatures
- Two proposed alternative placements for metadata (between `---` and diff, or after final `--`)
- Continued preference for header-based solution over body-based approaches

The email doesn't introduce new code but provides important design input on the ongoing discussion about how to handle metadata preservation in Git's email workflow, particularly regarding signature handling and the debate between header-based versus body-based approaches.
