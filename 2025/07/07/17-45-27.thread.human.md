The Git community is organizing a Mini Summit co-located with Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam on August 28th, 2025. Patrick Steinhardt announced the half-day event featuring lightning talks and networking, with tickets priced at $10 and talk proposals due by July 28th. The event serves as an accessible complement to Git Merge 2025, particularly for those already attending OSS Europe.

A follow-up question from Seyi Kuforiji has surfaced regarding whether talk proposals are limited to in-person attendees, suggesting interest from remote contributors. While the event's co-location with OSS Europe indicates a physical focus, this inquiry may prompt organizers to clarify their stance on virtual participation. The organizers plan to send another email when registration opens in the coming week.
