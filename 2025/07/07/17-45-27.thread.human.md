Patrick Steinhardt has announced a Git Mini Summit to be held alongside Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam on August 28th, 2025. This half-day event will feature lightning talks and networking opportunities, designed as a more accessible alternative for those unable to attend Git Merge 2025 or already planning to be at OSS Europe.

The event will have a nominal 10 USD ticket price (available as either an add-on to OSS Europe or standalone registration), with signups opening the following week. Talk proposals are being accepted until July 28th, with Patrick and other organizers reviewing submissions. The announcement provides all key logistical details while noting a follow-up will come when registration officially opens.

With its short 7-week lead time and strategic co-location, this appears to be a well-considered effort to create additional community engagement opportunities around existing conference travel plans. The low cost and complementary positioning relative to Git Merge suggest thoughtful planning to maximize participation.
