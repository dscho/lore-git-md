The Git Mini Summit 2025, co-located with Open Source Summit Europe in Amsterdam on August 28th, is taking shape as an accessible community event organized by Patrick Steinhardt. The half-day gathering will feature lightning talks and networking, with tickets priced at 10 USD (available as an add-on for OSS Europe attendees or standalone). Talk proposals are due by July 28th.

Seyi Kuforiji raised an important question about whether talk proposals would be restricted to in-person attendees. Patrick Steinhardt clarified that remote proposals are welcome, though the technical logistics for remote presentations still need to be worked out with the Linux Foundation. This keeps the event inclusive while acknowledging the practical challenges of supporting virtual participation in what is primarily a physical gathering.

With the talk proposal deadline just weeks away, organizers will need to quickly finalize the remote participation details. The thread shows the community's interest in making the event accessible while maintaining its focus as a satellite to Git Merge 2025. The quick response to Seyi's question demonstrates the organizers' commitment to addressing attendee concerns transparently.
