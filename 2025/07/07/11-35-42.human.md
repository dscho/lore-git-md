Derrick Stolee provides additional technical context for Lidong Yan's patch that replaces direct `struct bloom_key *` usage with a new `struct bloom_keyvec` abstraction.

The key insight is that the logic needs to flip from "NOT TREESAME if any bloom key reports NO" to "NOT TREESAME if every pathspec has at least one bloom key that reports NO" when handling multiple pathspecs. Stolee explains why this necessitates the new data structure rather than just using arrays of bloom keys.
