Junio Hamano withdraws his lightweight tag idempotency proposal in this email, marking a shift in the ongoing duplicate tag handling discussion. After receiving no feedback on his earlier patch that would have allowed repeated lightweight tag creation without `--force` when pointing to the same commit, he concludes the idea isn't worth pursuing and suggests abandoning it ("Let's chuck it").

The withdrawn patch had proposed a behavioral change where `git tag T O` would succeed without `--force` if tag T already pointed to commit O, while maintaining the existing requirement for annotated tags and cases where the target commit differs. The implementation modified `builtin/tag.c` to compare object IDs and added corresponding test cases in `t/t7004-tag.sh`.

This leaves the thread with two remaining approaches: Mirth Hickford's message-only improvements (distinguishing between harmless and dangerous duplicate tags) and Hilco Wijbenga's more comprehensive tag idempotency proposal requiring exact attribute matching. Junio's withdrawal suggests he may be returning to his original preference for minimal changes, though the thread remains open questions about whether error message improvements alone will satisfy the original safety concerns that sparked the discussion.
