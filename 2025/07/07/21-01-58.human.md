Documentation patch series completion notice. This is the final administrative email confirming that Jean-Noël Avila's 9-patch series converting `git-log` documentation to the new synopsis format is ready for merging into Git's 'next' branch. The series represents the completion of a major documentation standardization effort that began earlier.

Junio Hamano has reviewed all three versions of the series and confirms the v3 iteration addresses the last remaining formatting issue (an unclosed backtick in the `--pretty` option documentation). The changes affect multiple documentation files including `git-log.adoc`, `line-range-options.adoc`, and `diff-options.adoc`, standardizing their formatting while preserving all content.

The author notes that future work on formatter logic improvements will be handled in a separate patch series. With no outstanding issues remaining, this marks the successful conclusion of converting Git's second-largest man page to the new standardized format.
