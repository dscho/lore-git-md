This is a bugfix for the `git log --diff-filter` regression that was recently identified and fixed in the thread. Junio C Hamano is responding to Jeff King's patch that corrects the behavior where lowercase exclusion filters (like `d` to exclude deletions) weren't working properly.

The issue stemmed from a 2022 change that split diff filter bits into separate `filter` and `filter_not` fields, but didn't update the logic in `setup_revisions()` to check both fields when determining whether diffs were needed. Jeff's one-line fix in `revision.c` properly considers both fields, restoring the expected behavior where exclusion filters work regardless of output format.

Junio acknowledges the fix with his characteristic dry humor ("Ah, ouch"), noting the irony that the original "be more careful" change actually made things less careful about negative bits. The thread appears fully resolved at this point, with the fix having been tested and reviewed by multiple parties.
