This is a legal compliance follow-up in the ongoing discussion about relaxing Git's real-name requirements for Signed-off-by trailers. Junio Hamano responds to Theodore Ts'o's point about OFAC sanctions risks with anonymous contributions, strongly agreeing that the project needs protection against sanctioned entities attempting to bypass restrictions.

The exchange reinforces the emerging consensus that while pseudonyms may be allowed, true anonymity must be prohibited - not just for copyright reasons (as previously discussed) but now also for sanctions compliance. This adds another legal dimension to the policy framework being developed, beyond the original focus on contributor inclusivity and DCO enforceability.

The tone suggests this input will likely shape the next version of the patch series, which will need to incorporate both Junio's earlier request for stronger anonymity language and these new legal constraints. The discussion remains at the policy level, with no technical implementation details yet proposed for verifying contributor identities against sanctions lists.
