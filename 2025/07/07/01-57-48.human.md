This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing thread about relaxing Git's real-name requirements for Signed-off-by trailers. Ayush Chandekar raises a practical concern about the proposed policy change: what happens when someone creates a pseudonymous persona (claiming it's either their real name or alias), contributes under that identity, and then abandons it? They argue this would effectively be an anonymous contribution despite appearing to meet the "known identity" requirement.

The email doesn't propose specific solutions but highlights a potential loophole in the current proposal. This comes after Junio Hamano's earlier feedback emphasizing the need for stronger language rejecting anonymity and project-specific policy rather than referencing Linux kernel practices. The thread shows the community working through the balance between inclusivity (allowing pseudonyms) and maintaining meaningful contributor traceability.
