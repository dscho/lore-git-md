This is part 9 of Patrick Steinhardt's 21-patch series removing `the_repository` global variable usage in config-related functions. The patch removes the `git_config_get_int()` wrapper function and converts all callers to use `repo_config_get_int(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The change affects configuration handling for various features including credential storage locking, pack file settings, fetch operations, garbage collection parameters, merge behavior, parallel checkout, ref locking, and rebase labels. The conversion is mechanical and maintains existing behavior while making repository dependencies explicit at call sites.

This follows the established pattern from earlier patches in the series, continuing the systematic replacement of `git_config_*()` wrappers with their `repo_config_*()` counterparts. The patch updates 13 files with straightforward substitutions, similar to previous conversions in the series. As with prior patches, this leaves potential optimizations (like passing actual repository instances where available) for future work.

The change appears well-executed and uncontroversial, following the same disciplined approach seen in earlier parts of the series. It makes progress on the larger `the_repository` removal effort while maintaining backward compatibility.
