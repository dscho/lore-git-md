This is a response in the ongoing Rust integration discussion, focusing on platform support and dependency management concerns raised by Gentoo maintainers. Patrick Steinhardt engages with Sam James' specific points about Rust's ecosystem challenges.

Steinhardt expresses willingness to delay Rust adoption if alternative implementations like gccrs could provide wider platform support in the near future, but notes these projects still lack critical features. He shares concerns about Rust's lack of LTS releases and the pressure to constantly update compilers, calling this a sign of immaturity for a systems language. The email also acknowledges supply chain risks from excessive dependencies, advocating for Git's traditional conservatism in dependency selection.

The response shows Steinhardt taking distribution concerns seriously while maintaining his overall support for Rust adoption. He doesn't propose solutions but frames these as issues the project would need to carefully navigate, particularly around version targeting and dependency vetting. The tone is pragmatic, recognizing both the benefits of Rust and the real-world constraints faced by downstream packagers.
