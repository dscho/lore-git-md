This is part 12 of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove `the_repository` global by converting config-related wrapper functions to explicit repository-aware variants. The patch removes the `git_config_set_in_file()` wrapper in favor of `repo_config_set_in_file()`, updating three call sites in submodule--helper.c and dir.c that handle submodule configuration and worktree settings.

The change follows the established pattern of the series - mechanically converting wrapper functions while maintaining identical behavior. The commit notes this is an intermediate step, as some call sites could eventually use a proper repository instance rather than `the_repository`. The patch is small (13 lines net change) and focused, continuing the series' disciplined approach to this refactoring effort.

Like previous patches in the series, this appears uncontroversial and well-structured, making repository dependencies explicit without changing functionality. The changes affect configuration handling for submodule alternates and worktree settings, but maintain all existing behavior. This continues the systematic removal of config wrappers as part of the broader `the_repository` elimination effort.
