This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting config-related functionality. Patch 7/21 in the series removes the `git_config_get_string()` wrapper function, converting all callers to use `repo_config_get_string(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The change follows the established pattern from commit 036876a1067, making repository dependencies explicit while maintaining identical behavior. The patch updates 12 files across various subsystems including apply, branch, gc, log, submodules, and transport. Key configuration settings affected include apply whitespace rules, branch descriptions, GC repack filters, log decorations, submodule URLs, and transport protocols.

The mechanical conversion leaves potential optimizations (like using available repository pointers at call sites) for future work, focusing first on making the `the_repository` dependency visible. The patch maintains the series' disciplined approach, with straightforward replacements and no behavior changes. Like previous patches in the series, this appears uncontroversial and well-structured, continuing the systematic removal of config-related wrappers.
