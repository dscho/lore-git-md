This is a bugfix discussion in the reflog migration infrastructure thread, focusing on the `REF_HAVE_OLD` flag behavior for log-only updates. Justin Tobler is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's explanation about how unsetting this flag during reflog writes was inadvertently causing migrated reflog entries to record all-zero OIDs instead of the intended old values.

The core issue stems from the flag serving two purposes: it both enables old-OID verification (which isn't needed for reflogs) and signals that an old OID was provided (which is needed for correct reflog migration). Justin seeks clarification on whether all reflog migration operations use the `REF_LOG_ONLY` flag, as this would help confirm the safety of the proposed change. The discussion appears technically sound, with both participants carefully considering the implications of the flag's dual role in the ref transaction system.
