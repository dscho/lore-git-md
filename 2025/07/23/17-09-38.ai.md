While some content-sensitive options like whitespace ignoring have been special-cased to affect exit codes, Hamano argues it wouldn't make sense for them to affect metadata-focused outputs like `--name-only` or `--raw`.

Key technical details include:
- The `diff_from_contents` flag that controls when content examination is required
- How `XDF_WHITESPACE_FLAGS` and `ignore_regex_nr` interact with this flag
- The distinction between content-examining options and metadata-focused output formats

Hamano's position is that the current behavior is correct - metadata-focused outputs should not be affected by content-ignoring options, as this would make their answers pathologically dependent on ignore patterns.
