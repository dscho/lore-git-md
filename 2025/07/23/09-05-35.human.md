Feature patch with a bugfix for IMAP sent-folder archiving in `git send-email`.

Aditya Garg reports a small but important bug in their previously submitted patch series that adds IMAP-based sent message archiving to `git send-email`. The current implementation incorrectly processes IMAP archiving even when `--dry-run` is specified, which violates the expected dry-run behavior where no actual operations should occur. 

The author notes this discovery has also revealed a simpler implementation path for a potential `--use-imap-only` mode that Junio Hamano had previously suggested. This suggests the bug finding may lead to improvements beyond just fixing the dry-run issue.

This email comes after the v3 patch series had appeared technically complete, with naming and implementation details settled through multiple rounds of review. The author's earlier message in this thread had raised philosophical questions about whether the feature belongs in Git at all, but here they're proceeding with practical improvements, indicating they may be moving forward with the implementation despite those doubts.

The bug itself seems straightforward to fix, and the side benefit of discovering a cleaner implementation approach for future extensions is a positive development. This keeps the feature moving forward while maintaining its technical foundations.
