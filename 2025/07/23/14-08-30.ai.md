This is part 9 of Patrick Steinhardt's 21-patch series removing `the_repository` global variable usage in config-related functions. The patch removes the `git_config_get_int()` wrapper function and converts all callers to use `repo_config_get_int(the_repository, ...)` instead.

The change affects 13 files across various subsystems including credential storage, fast-import, fetch, garbage collection, fsmonitor, merge, parallel checkout, refs, and sequencer. The conversions are mechanical and maintain identical behavior while making repository dependencies explicit at call sites. Key configuration settings being accessed include credential store lock timeouts, pack index versions, unpack limits, maintenance auto-configurations, checkout workers, ref lock timeouts, and rebase label lengths.

The patch follows the established approach from previous conversions in the series (referencing commit 036876a1067) and leaves potential optimizations of passing actual repository instances (rather than `the_repository`) for future work. The changes are straightforward replacements with no behavioral modifications, continuing the series' pattern of making repository dependencies visible while maintaining functionality.
