Test helper cleanup discussion (reply)

This is a reply in the ongoing thread about test helper resource management, specifically addressing Jeff King's proposal to simplify `test-delta.c` by using `die()` for error handling rather than explicit cleanup. Eric Sunshine concurs with King's approach, agreeing that test helpers can reasonably use `die()` since they exit immediately on errors anyway, making cleanup paths unnecessary.

Sunshine acknowledges two additional benefits King identified in his simplified approach: it incidentally fixes a potential integer truncation bug (using `st.st_size` directly with `xmalloc()`) and an unchecked short read case in `read_in_full()`. While noting these could be security issues in other contexts, Sunshine recognizes they're less critical in test helpers, though still worth fixing.

The exchange reinforces the emerging consensus that test helpers should prioritize simplicity over meticulous resource cleanup, given their transient nature. This reply solidifies agreement on King's direction after earlier discussion exposed flaws in more complex cleanup approaches. The conversation has now clearly shifted from debating cleanup methods to accepting that immediate termination via `die()` is the most maintainable approach for test helper error handling.
