This thread has been refining the behavior of `git imap-send` regarding how it marks messages as read/unread in IMAP folders. The initial implementation introduced a CLI flag (`--[no-]mark-as-read`) to control this behavior, with different default behaviors between the OpenSSL and curl backends due to technical constraints.

After extensive discussion, the scope was narrowed to keep the change minimal and avoid complicating matters with curl version requirements. Junio C Hamano rejected integrating this with `send-email` due to these constraints, and the team settled on a CLI-only approach without adding new config options.

The latest email from Aditya Garg raises a final implementation question: whether to standardize the default behavior now that broader integration plans are off the table. Currently, OpenSSL doesn't mark messages as read by default (since it doesn't require new APIs to implement this), while curl does. This inconsistency was previously tolerated due to plans for future integration, but with those plans deferred, the question remains whether to align the behaviors.

The thread has effectively in its final stages, with only this last implementation detail to resolve before the feature can be considered complete. All major design decisions have been made, and the remaining work is limited to finalizing the behavior in `builtin/imap-send.c`. The discussion has primarily involved Aditya Garg and Junio C Hamano, with clear consensus emerging around keeping the change minimal and focused.
