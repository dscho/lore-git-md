This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to remove `the_repository` global variable usage, specifically focusing on config-related functions. Patch 2/21 mechanically converts the `git_config_clear()` wrapper to direct calls to `repo_config_clear(the_repository)`.

The commit message explains this is part of a planned transition to make repository dependencies explicit, with the pattern established in an earlier commit (036876a1067).

This appears to be a clean, mechanical change that maintains identical behavior while progressing the larger refactoring goal. The patch is small (8 lines removed, 3 added) and focused, touching only the config clearing functionality. It follows the established pattern from earlier in the series and doesn't introduce any new complexity. The changes are exactly what we'd expect for this stage of the `the_repository` removal effort.
