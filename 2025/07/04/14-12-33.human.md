This is the final version of a 2-patch series removing `the_repository` usage from `builtin/prune.c`, part of the broader effort to eliminate this global variable. The series comes from Ayush Chandekar, a GSoC participant who has iterated on the patches since their initial submission on June 8, incorporating all review feedback.

The first patch moves the `repository_format_precious_objects` global into `struct repository`, a prerequisite for the second patch which fully converts `builtin/prune.c` to use repository instances. The changes are straightforward but important for the long-term goal of libification. The second patch also adds a test verifying that `git prune -h` works outside a repository context, following the pattern established by similar tests.

All technical feedback has been addressed, including proper commit message formatting and moving the `repo_init_revisions()` call after `parse_options()` to maintain correct command lifecycle handling. The test addition was suggested by Usman Akinyemi and approved by Junio Hamano. The patches demonstrate clean, incremental progress on the `the_repository` removal effort with no outstanding technical issues.
