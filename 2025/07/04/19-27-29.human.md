This is a technical response in the ongoing SHA-256 transition discussion, clarifying the correct approach for hash conversion. brian m. carlson explains why simply re-hashing SHA-1 strings (as proposed by Aditya Garg) would not workable, as it would preserve SHA-1's collision vulnerability. The proper method involves independently computing SHA-256 hashes of object contents while maintaining interoperability between hash formats.
