This is a discussion about refining the output and behavior of the new `git sparse-checkout clean` command, specifically around verbosity levels and safety controls. Junio Hamano and Derrick Stolee are debating two key aspects:

The discussion reveals ongoing tension between simplicity (Stolee's preference for directory-level operations) and granular control (Hamano's suggestions leaning toward file-level visibility). Neither commits to a specific resolution, maintaining an exploratory tone while recognizing the command's core purpose is directory cleaning in sparse-checkout contexts.
