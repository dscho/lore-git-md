This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's 19-part series to refactor object-file.c for pluggable object backends as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. The patch removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` preprocessor macro from object-file.c, marking the completion of the series' goal to eliminate all global state dependencies in this critical subsystem.

The change is straightforward - just two lines removed - but represents a significant achievement after the extensive refactoring in the previous 18 patches. The removal confirms that object-file.c no longer relies on any global repository state, having systematically migrated all functionality to use explicit repository parameters and the new `odb_source` interfaces introduced earlier in the series.

This patch serves as a clean-up and validation step, showing that the complex preparatory work has successfully eliminated the need for this macro. It's a satisfying conclusion to a substantial refactoring effort that makes Git's object handling more modular and better prepared for future pluggable backends.
