Refactoring patch in a series moving MIDX tracking from global to per-source storage. This is the third of eight patches in v1 of the series.

The patch removes the remaining uses of the global MIDX linked list in cleanup paths, completing the transition to per-source MIDX tracking. The changes are straightforward mechanical conversions from list-based to source-based iteration, with no behavior changes beyond the internal structure.

- Updates both functions to loop through `odb_source` chain
- Maintains same NULL-setting behavior but now at source level
- Touches core MIDX and packfile cleanup paths
- Builds on previous patches' per-source MIDX pointer addition
