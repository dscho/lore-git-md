2. **Safety concerns**: Examines edge cases around merge/rebase conflicts and unstaged changes. While conflicted files are protected by sparse index mechanics, Newren points out resolved conflicts may leave modified files that could be unexpectedly deleted. Suggests this might need force flags or clearer warnings.

3. **Implementation details**: Questions whether the command actually handles tracked files at all due to present-despite-skipped checks in the sparse index conversion. Proposes the command may need pairing with `reapply` for full effectiveness.

4. **Output granularity**: Supports the idea of status-like output showing why files/directories are being kept (untracked, ignored, modified etc), possibly via --verbose.

The email deepens the technical discussion around edge case handling while maintaining focus on the core goal of safely removing unwanted sparse directories.
