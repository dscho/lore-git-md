This is a refactoring series improving type safety in Git's parse-options infrastructure. René Scharfe extends precision handling to all integer-based option types (PARSE_OPT_CMDMODE, OPTION_SET_INT, OPTION_BIT, OPTION_NEGBIT, OPTION_BITOP, and OPTION_COUNTUP), replacing direct pointer manipulation with type-safe operations while maintaining backward compatibility.

The v2 series incorporates feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on the initial version, with several notable improvements: splitting out helper functions for cleaner code, better error reporting for invalid precision values, and more consistent BUG() handling. The changes affect core parse-options machinery and several builtin commands (am, rebase, update-index, write-tree), though there are no user-facing behavior changes.

The series follows a methodical approach with each patch tackling one option type. While sign handling (particularly for OPTION_COUNTUP) is noted as a potential future direction, the author explicitly keeps it out of scope for this focused refactoring. The implementation adds bounds checking and precision validation while preserving existing behavior, making it a solid internal improvement that should ease future maintenance.
