This is a follow-up in the ongoing documentation patch series about allowing non-real names in Signed-off-by trailers. The author, brian m. carlson, responds to Jeff King's suggestion to incorporate CNCF-style wording into the commit message for v2 revision.

The email focuses on wording concerns, with carlson expressing reservations about the CNCF's ambiguous definition of "real name" and preferring Wikipedia's more flexible "set of names by which an individual is known" phrasing. Key points include: carlson's critique of legalistic language redefinition as potentially misleading (especially for non-native English speakers), examples of naming collisions in real-world projects like Debian, and plans to craft more approachable language while still maintaining the legal requirements established in the thread.

The response indicates the v2 patch will incorporate some CNCF inspiration but with clearer phrasing that avoids redefining common terms. This continues the thread's focus on balancing legal requirements (DCO, OFAC, license enforcement) with contributor accessibility, now moving into precise wording choices. The exchange shows consensus on the core policy direction but with ongoing refinement of how to document it most effectively.
