Refactoring patch in a series moving MIDX tracking to per-source storage. This is the third of eight patches in Patrick Steinhardt's series to eliminate global MIDX tracking in favor of attaching MIDX files to individual object database sources (`struct odb_source`).

The patch changes how MIDX files are closed, moving from recursively traversing a global linked list to iterating through the per-source MIDX pointers. The changes affect both `midx.c` and `packfile.c`, with the main logic shift being that `close_midx()` now only closes a single MIDX file (plus its base) rather than recursively closing the entire chain. The cleanup operations in `clear_midx_file()` and `close_object_store()` are updated to match, now explicitly iterating through sources to close their MIDX files.

The changes are straightforward mechanical updates to match the new architecture, removing 10 lines while adding 11 (mostly due to the more explicit iteration pattern). This continues the series' careful, incremental approach to restructuring MIDX handling while maintaining temporary backward compatibility with the global linked list during the transition.
