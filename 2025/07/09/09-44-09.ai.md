This is a refactoring patch in the parse-options integer type precision series. It enforces that OPTION_BITOP options must be declared with PARSE_OPT_NOARG since they don't take arguments, similar to other bit-related options. The change adds OPTION_BITOP to the existing validation check in parse_options_check() that already covered OPTION_BIT, OPTION_NEGBIT, and others.

The patch is minimal - just a one-line addition to include OPTION_BITOP in the case statement that verifies no-argument options are properly flagged. This follows the pattern established for similar option types and addresses a consistency gap identified in the review of the initial version. The change helps maintain type safety in the parse-options infrastructure by ensuring all bit operation options follow the same rules.
