This is a follow-up discussion about a stalled patch series related to SIGCHLD handling in `git daemon`. The series (originally from June 2025) aimed to improve signal handling reliability by replacing `signal()` with `sigaction()` and addressing platform-specific behaviors.

Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón clarifies that the series isn't truly stalled but rather awaiting review, particularly for patch 2, and notes some controversy around the changes. The core motivation is to eliminate reliance on two unspecified behaviors: whether `signal()` enables SA_RESTART by default, and whether poll() can be interrupted when SA_RESTART is active. Using sigaction() gives explicit control over these behaviors.

The email mentions a related (but withheld) patchset that would enhance notifications using a self-pipe, suggesting this is part of broader signal-handling improvements. A platform-specific note indicates Windows (mingw) handles SIGCHLD differently (signal 17 vs 22 on Cygwin), potentially causing errno issues that need consideration. The discussion remains open on whether to keep patch 4 (SA_RESTART fallback tracking) depending on the SA_RESTART decision.

Key technical points for future reference:
- Changes target `git-daemon.c` and compatibility layers (`compat/mingw`, `compat/posix.h`)
- Core issue: replacing `signal(SIGCHLD)` with `sigaction()` for defined behavior
- Open question: whether to enable SA_RESTART (affects patch 4's necessity)
- Windows-specific: SIGCHLD is signal 17 (vs 22 on Cygwin) with potential errno handling quirks
- Related future work: self-pipe notification enhancement patchset

The exchange shows active but cautious development around signal handling reliability, with platform-specific considerations and some unresolved design decisions.
