This is the final patch in a refactoring series moving MIDX tracking from global state to per-source storage, part of the larger effort to prepare for pluggable object database backends. The series has systematically migrated all MIDX-related functionality to operate on individual `struct odb_source` instances rather than maintaining a linked list in the global object database.

Patch 8/8 removes the now-unused global MIDX linked list and related infrastructure, cleaning up after the previous conversions. It deletes the `next` pointer from `struct multi_pack_index`, removes the `multi_pack_index` field from `struct object_database`, and eliminates the last remaining global MIDX updates in `prepare_multi_pack_index_one()`, `clear_midx_file()`, and `close_object_store()`.

The changes are straightforward deletions of now-dead code, with no behavior changes - all functionality was already migrated in prior patches. This completes the series' goal of making MIDX handling properly source-local, which better matches their on-disk organization and supports future pluggable backend work. The series appears well-structured and carefully executed, with each patch building logically on the last.
