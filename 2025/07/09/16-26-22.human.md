This is a status update on the stalled SIGCHLD handling series for `git daemon`. 

Junio C Hamano follows up on Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's series to replace `signal()` with `sigaction()` for more reliable child process handling. The series has been marked as stalled" not for lack of author activity, but because it needs more review attention, particularly for patch 2.

Carlo clarifies the technical motivation: currently the daemon relies on two unspecified behaviors regarding signal handling and poll() interruptions, which would be properly defined by using sigaction(). He notes the series could be simplified if they decide against using SA_RESTART, making patch 4 potentially unnecessary.

The email also references previous Windows-specific feedback from Johannes Sixt about signal number discrepancies between platforms. While the technical merits seem clear, the series remains somewhat controversial and needs focused review before progressing further. This appears to be a case where platform differences and subtle signal behavior details are slowing down what would otherwise be a straightforward improvement.
