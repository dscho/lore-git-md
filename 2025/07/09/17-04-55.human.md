This is a technical discussion in the ongoing Git daemon signal handling redesign thread. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón responds to Phillip Wood's concerns about the SA_RESTART approach in their proposed solution for handling zombie processes in `git-daemon`.

The email defends the SA_RESTART toggling approach (patch 4 in their series) as addressing the root cause of the zombie process issue, where OpenBSD systems only clear zombies after new connections. Carlo acknowledges the approach may seem odd but argues it directly solves the documented problem where systems typically have SA_RESTART enabled for SIGCHLD.

Carlo notes that platforms without SA_RESTART (mainly Windows) are less concerning because Windows doesn't fail system calls with EINTR anyway, and other non-SA_RESTART platforms have few users. This continues the thread's technical debate about whether to maintain platform-specific behaviors versus enforcing POSIX consistency.

The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than introducing new concepts, with Carlo maintaining their position that the SA_RESTART approach is valid despite Phillip's preference for simpler solutions.
