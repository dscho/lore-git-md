Refactoring follow-up in parse-options integer handling series. Patrick Steinhardt suggests a naming improvement for the integer value helper functions introduced in René Scharfe's patch series. The current implementation has `do_get_int_value()` and `get_int_value()`, where the latter dies on parse failure. Patrick proposes renaming them to `get_int_value()` and `get_int_value_or_die()` respectively for clearer intent signaling.

This is a minor but thoughtful suggestion in the context of the larger type-safety refactoring of Git's parse-options infrastructure. The series has been methodically working through all integer option types (PARSE_OPT_CMDMODE, OPTION_SET_INT, etc.) to add precision handling and bounds checking. The naming tweak would make the failure behavior more immediately obvious to future readers of the code.

The thread shows careful collaboration between René (implementer) and Patrick (reviewer), with this being one of several refinements made during the review process. The suggestion maintains the series' high quality bar while being non-controversial - exactly the kind of incremental improvement that makes code more maintainable.
