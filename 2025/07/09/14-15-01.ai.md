This is a follow-up in the ongoing SIGCHLD handling thread for `git daemon`, part of platform compatibility work.

Phillip Wood responds to Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón's status update on the SIGCHLD handling series, requesting a reroll of the patches. The thread concerns replacing `signal()` with `sigaction()` for more reliable child process handling in the Git daemon across different operating systems. 

Key technical points mentioned in this exchange:
- The series addresses undefined behavior around `SA_RESTART` and `poll()` interruptions when using `signal()`
- Windows-specific issues with SIGCHLD being signal 17 (vs 22 on Cygwin) and errno handling quirks
- An open question remains about whether to enable `SA_RESTART`, which could make one of the patches obsolete
- A related future patchset using self-pipe notification is being held back while this series matures

Phillip's brief response indicates the series needs more review (particularly for patch 2) and suggests it's somewhat controversial, while Carlo maintains the changes are independently useful for standardizing behavior across platforms. The exchange shows active but cautious development around this platform-specific reliability improvement.
