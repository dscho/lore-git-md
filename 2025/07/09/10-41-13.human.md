This is a follow-up discussion about a stalled patch series related to `git daemon`'s SIGCHLD signal handling. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón clarifies that the series isn't actually stalled - it's just awaiting reviews, particularly for the second patch which modifies how child processes are handled using `sigaction()` instead of `signal()`.

The changes address two implementation-defined behaviors in the daemon's current approach: whether SA_RESTART is enabled for signals (which affects system call interruption) and whether poll() can be interrupted when SA_RESTART is set. The series makes this behavior explicit and controllable. Carlo notes there's some controversy around these changes, and mentions a related patchset (currently held back) that would enhance notifications using a self-pipe.

The email also calls out a Windows-specific consideration regarding SIGCHLD signal numbering differences between mingw (17) and cygwin (22), tagging Johannes Sixt for input on this platform-specific behavior. The discussion suggests this is a technical improvement to make signal handling more predictable, though the exact approach (particularly around SA_RESTART) may still under discussion.
