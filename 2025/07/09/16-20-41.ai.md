Philosophically, Junio appears to be moving toward accepting Phillip's position that the feature is fundamentally unworkable due to third-party interference in commit messages, though he still expresses some reservations. The discussion has narrowed to whether to (1) make Git die with "auto" in 3.0 requiring user action, or (2) document the limitations while keeping the feature - with Phillip strongly favoring the former approach.
