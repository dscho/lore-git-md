Performance optimization and bugfix for remote ref renaming. This is part 3/4 of Patrick Steinhardt's series addressing severe performance issues when renaming remotes with many refs. The patch fixes two key problems: dangling symrefs weren't being properly renamed (leaving orphaned HEAD references), and the operation used an extremely inefficient O(n^2) approach with the files backend (taking hours for 100k refs).

The solution restructures the ref renaming into two transactions (delete old refs then create new ones) rather than per-ref operations, yielding dramatic speedups: 113x faster for files backend (238s -> 2s) and 7x for reftable (8.6s -> 1.2s). The patch also properly handles symrefs by using `refs_for_each_rawref()` and adds comprehensive tests covering edge cases like nested remotes and reflog preservation. A minor tradeoff is the loss of precise progress reporting during the operation.

This builds on the preparatory refactoring from earlier patches in the series and demonstrates careful attention to both correctness (fixing the symref bug) and performance (the benchmark results are compelling). The changes are focused in `builtin/remote.c` with extensive test coverage added.
