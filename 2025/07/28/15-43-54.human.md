This is a maintainer response to the performance optimization series for remote ref renaming. Junio Hamano acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt's work fixing quadratic runtime behavior when renaming large numbers of remote refs, which now uses two transactions instead of one per ref. He notes the significant speed improvements (from hours to seconds for large ref counts) and the continued performance advantage of the reftable backend.

Hamano raises one concern about a known D/F false-positive bug in the transaction mechanism that could theoretically leave the repository in an inconsistent state if the second transaction fails, though he seems generally satisfied with the solution. He indicates he'll queue the series while noting it may need rebasing if the underlying reflog migration fixes require further work.

The response shows approval of the technical approach while flagging one potential area for future improvement, maintaining the thread's focus on performance optimization of remote operations. The tone is positive and collaborative, typical of maintainer feedback on well-prepared patch series.
