This is a performance optimization patch in the remote ref renaming series, specifically addressing inefficiencies in how remote refs are renamed while preserving reflogs. Junio C Hamano provides a detailed review of Patrick Steinhardt's implementation, focusing on code quality and edge cases rather than introducing new functionality.

The review highlights several stylistic concerns: questioning the word "inperformant," suggesting better function naming (`renamed_refname` could be clearer), and noting the unfortunate reuse of system call names for variables. Junio also points out some awkward but unavoidable aspects of the implementation, like the need to reconstruct full ident strings and the messy handling of symref resolution.

Key technical points under review include the careful management of reflog entries during renames (to maintain proper ordering) and the use of temporary strbufs passed through the `rename_info` struct. While the approach works, Junio suggests it could be made clearer by better separating the strbuf uses. The review also notes the yucky but necessary handling of symref resolution due to API limitations.

This is the third patch in a 4-part series that has already received maintainer approval, with Junio planning to queue it while noting a few minor cleanups. The changes build on earlier preparatory work to standardize reflog callbacks and consolidate migration data, now showing concrete performance wins (hours to seconds for large ref counts).
