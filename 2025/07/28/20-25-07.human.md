This is a follow-up in the performance optimization series for xdiff string hashing. Alexander Monakov clarifies the licensing status of his proposed optimized djb2 hash implementation, which was a key concern raised earlier in the thread.

He explains that while inspired by Noah Goldstein's work in Glibc, his implementation is newly written to meet xdiff's specific requirements (particularly length-bounded iteration) and thus can be contributed under GPLv2. This addresses Junio Hamano's licensing concerns from earlier in the thread.

The email serves both to confirm the legal clearance for the technical approach and to invite review of the actual implementation (patch 2 in the series) against the Glibc version for verification. With this clarification, the path seems clear for the performance improvements to proceed, assuming the technical issues in the implementation are sound. The author's direct involvement in reviewing the original Glibc work lends credibility to the adaptation.
