This is a licensing clarification in the ongoing xdiff string hashing optimization thread. Eli Schwartz responds to Junio Hamano's concern about potential GPLv3 incompatibility with Glibc's optimized djb2 hash implementation, pointing out that Glibc is actually licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later, which is compatible with Git's GPLv2 license.

The email cites two GNU licensing resources and specifically references Glibc's documented license status, noting it is more permissive than Git's license. This directly addresses the key blocking issue raised in the thread about whether the proposed optimization could be legally incorporated. The response suggests the technical approach can proceed without licensing obstacles, though it doesn't provide direct confirmation of the specific code's license status within Glibc.
