This is the final patch in a performance optimization series addressing remote ref renaming inefficiencies, now approved by the maintainer. The patch introduces `refs_for_each_rawref_in()` to enable prefix-limited iteration when renaming remote references, avoiding the wasteful full-ref iteration of the previous approach.

The change builds on earlier preparatory work that standardized reflog callbacks and consolidated migration state. While the previous patches focused on dangling symref handling and transaction restructuring (yielding a 113x speedup for files backend), this final optimization provides an additional 1.35x improvement for files and 2.07x for reftable backends. The maintainer confirms the technical approach makes sense, noting that even with lexicographic ordering, the old method still required processing irrelevant refs before reaching the target namespace.

The series as a whole demonstrates methodical optimization work, with comprehensive testing and clear performance data showing dramatic improvements (from 238s to 2s for files backend in the worst case). The maintainer plans to queue the series while noting a known D/F false-positive edge case that may need future attention, and observes that the reftable backend maintains its inherent performance advantage.
