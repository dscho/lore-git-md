This is a follow-up in the ongoing git-gui platform modernization series, specifically addressing concerns about the macOS Tcl/Tk 8.5 deprecation workarounds. Johannes Sixt raises two key technical issues with the proposed changes:

Second and more critically, he identifies that patch 5 (ensuring git-gui--askpass version usage) breaks RUNTIME_PREFIX builds. While he hasn't tested it, his analysis indicates the current approach needs revision. He also requests verification on Meson compatibility and askpass functionality, noting his own lack of expertise with these build systems.

The email doesn't propose specific fixes but flags these as blocking issues that need resolution before the series can proceed. The technical concerns focus on build system interactions (RUNTIME_PREFIX handling) and cross-platform consistency, continuing the thread's theme of modernizing while maintaining compatibility.
