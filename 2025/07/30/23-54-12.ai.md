This is a technical response in the ongoing git-gui platform support thread, specifically addressing concerns about macOS Tcl/Tk integration and askpass helper resolution. Carlo Arenas clarifies several key points about the macOS situation and responds to Johannes Sixt's feedback.

The email provides important context about why the macOS "Git Gui.app" bundle has been problematic - multiple breakages over time from macOS 11 onwards, with various workarounds (like `use_ttk=0`) proving unreliable. He explains that Homebrew already works around these issues by forcing use of third-party Tcl/Tk rather than the system version.

Regarding the askpass helper (patch 5), Carlo acknowledges Johannes' concern about RUNTIME_PREFIX breakage but suggests Mark Levedahl's alternative approach (using Tcl's `argv0` resolution) might be superior to the current exec-path based solution. He also notes the meson build works in isolation but can't be fully tested until git-gui is properly integrated into Git's main meson.build.

The response highlights several practical considerations:
- The macOS App bundle's multiple points of failure
- Current workarounds in the wild (Homebrew's approach)
- Testing requirements for the askpass helper (specific SSH_ASKPASS_REQUIRE setup)
- Limitations in meson testing due to incomplete integration
