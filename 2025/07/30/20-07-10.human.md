Junio Hamano acknowledges this as "an obvious improvement" in his reply, suggesting this uncontroversial cleanup is ready to proceed. The change is part of a larger series modernizing git-gui's platform support, though this particular patch stands on its own as a simple housekeeping item.

The removal aligns with the series' broader goal of cleaning up build system cruft, though it doesn't directly address the more fundamental architectural questions about Tcl/Tk handling that have emerged in later discussion. This appears to be one of the less contentious changes in the series.
