This is a platform compatibility patch series addressing macOS-specific issues with `git-gui` and its Tcl/Tk dependencies. The series removes outdated macOS-specific components that relied on the system's deprecated Tcl/Tk 8.5 framework, while adding support for third-party Tcl/Tk 8.6 installations. The main change retires the long-broken "Git Gui.app" bundle and cleans up related build infrastructure. 

The patches also address a quirk where Xcode-provided Git includes `git-gui--askpass` even without `git-gui`, ensuring the correct version is used. The changes appear well-motivated given macOS's outdated Tcl/Tk situation, with clean removal of obsolete code and thoughtful handling of edge cases. The series could be split - the first two patches are general fixes that could go to maint, while the rest are macOS-specific modernization.
