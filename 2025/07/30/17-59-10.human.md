This is a status update on the `git last-modified` command series, specifically addressing the Bloom filter optimization work. 

Toon Claes reports that after extensive investigation, the expected performance improvements from the Bloom filter integration weren't materializing as hoped. While the optimization was included in v2 of the series, the results didn't meet expectations. After spending significant time trying to resolve this, Claes has decided to submit v6 of the series without these patches for now, though remains interested in solving the performance puzzle in future iterations.

The message indicates this is a temporary deferral rather than an abandonment of the optimization work. The current v6 series still includes the core functionality with comprehensive tests and other performance improvements (50.6% speedup for top-level checks, 36.4% for subdirectory checks), just without this particular Bloom filter enhancement. The thread context shows this was one of the last open questions in an otherwise mature series that has addressed most technical concerns through multiple iterations.
