This is an announcement about expanded travel sponsorship opportunities for the upcoming Git Merge 2025 conference.

Taylor Blau follows up on the initial conference announcement to share that additional travel sponsorships are now being offered. The sponsorships are being extended beyond previous Google Summer of Code (GSoC) and Outreachy participants to include current GSoC contributors as well.

Interested applicants from these programs should email git@sfconservancy.org before July 15, 2025 for details. The conference itself will take place in September 2025, with both in-person and virtual attendance options.

This appears to be a straightforward administrative update aimed at broadening participation opportunities for contributors from mentorship programs. The announcement is clear and provides all necessary details for interested parties to take action.
