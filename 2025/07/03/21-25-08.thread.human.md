This thread announces expanded travel sponsorship opportunities for Git Merge 2025, the annual Git community conference. Taylor Blau (GitHub, PLC member) shares that eligibility has been broadened to include current Google Summer of Code (GSoC) contributors, in addition to previous GSoC and Outreachy participants. 

Interested applicants should contact git@sfconservancy.org before July 15, 2025 for details. The announcement builds on a prior Git Merge 2025 notice (linked but not quoted) and focuses specifically on making attendance more accessible through these sponsorships. Taylor encourages participation both in-person and virtually, offering to answer questions publicly or privately. 

The update appears uncontroversial, serving primarily as an administrative notice about conference logistics rather than a technical discussion. With the July deadline and clear application instructions, this provides actionable information for those who may need financial assistance to attend the September event.
