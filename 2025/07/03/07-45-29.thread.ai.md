Thread summary:

This thread introduces a new feature adding X-Change-ID email header support for change tracking systems. The initial patch from the author modifies pretty.c to scan commit messages for "change-id" lines and include them as X-Change-ID headers in emails generated by format-patch and send-email. The implementation is intentionally limited to the email output side (7 lines changed) to provide standalone value while avoiding potential conflicts with downstream tools like Jujutsu that may handle change IDs differently. The author seeks maintainer feedback on impacts to other change-tracking systems before considering git-am integration. The patch is small and focused on the pretty-printing code path, specifically targeting interoperability with Patchwork and Sourcehut workflows. No technical objections or alternative approaches have been raised yet in the thread.
