This thread concerns a patch series to improve how Git handles `-h` and `--help-all` options across commands, particularly making them work without a repository and allowing `-h` mid-command. The series started with four patches, but after review, the final patch (enabling `-h` anywhere in the command line) is being dropped due to both security concerns from Junio and technical inconsistencies identified by Jeff King and D. Ben Knoble.

The first three patches—which refactor parse-options internals, enable `--help-all` outside repositories, and preserve special cases for `ls-remote` and `grep`—remain uncontroversial and will proceed. Jeff King's latest email confirms that the fourth patch's approach would break future commands requiring repository setup (`RUN_SETUP`) because help detection happens before option parsing. Junio also raised security concerns about making `-h` too permissive, as it could mask unintended invocations.

The discussion has clarified that earlier patch application issues were due to missing test infrastructure from the `ua/t1517-short-help-tests` branch. With the technical and security objections now well-understood, the consensus is to move forward with just patches 1-3, leaving the more complex behavioral change for potential future work. The remaining patches are expected to proceed without further major revisions.
