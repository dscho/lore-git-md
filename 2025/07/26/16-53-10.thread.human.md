These findings complicate the already-contentious discussion around patch 4, which Junio Hamano had previously opposed on security grounds due to risks with alias handling.

Meanwhile, test infrastructure improvements from D. Ben Knoble have been upstreamed and can be dropped from future iterations. The parse-options.c refactoring approach remains under discussion, with Junio suggesting simpler alternatives to the current enum-based design. While much of the series appears ready, the fate of mid-command `-h` support remains uncertain, with fundamental objections now compounded by newly uncovered technical hurdles. The thread shows no clear path forward for this controversial aspect of the proposal.
