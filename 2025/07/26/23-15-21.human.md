This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about Git's conservative approach to adopting C99 compound literals. Junio C Hamano acknowledges Collin Funk's report that Gnulib and Coreutils have successfully used compound literals since 2017, including support in the pcc compiler. However, Junio notes that Git's primary concern remains compatibility with proprietary platforms outside the open source ecosystem, which wouldn't benefit from these open-source project experiences.

The exchange reinforces Git's established position from earlier in the thread: while the reftable subsystem will serve as a test case for compound literals until 2026, the project maintains its conservative stance due to concerns about unknown platform compatibility issues. This appears to be a polite but firm closing of the discussion, indicating the project's position won't be swayed by this particular data point from other open source projects. The thread seems effectively concluded unless new evidence emerges from monitoring the reftable usage.
