This is a follow-up in the ongoing refactoring series to move sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to repository-specific storage, part of the `the_repository` removal effort.

Ayush Chandekar responds to Junio Hamano's feedback on the v6 series, addressing two main concerns. First, regarding the scattered calls to `prepare_repo_settings()`, Ayush acknowledges the performance and maintainability issues but is unsure of the ideal solution - suggesting either central call in `git.c:run_builtin()` or within `repo_config()`. Second, for the user-facing behavior regression in patch 3/3, Ayush confirms the issue isn't fully resolved but explains the current approach relies on existing `prepare_repo_settings()` calls in `repo_read_index()`.

The email also includes a notable architectural discussion point: Junio questions why these settings live in `repo_settings` rather than directly in `struct repository`. Ayush explains the current grouping logic (keeping `core.*` settings together in `repo_settings`), though this appears to conflict with emerging maintainer consensus that `struct repository` may be more appropriate. The exchange highlights the series is in flux regarding fundamental design decisions, with performance considerations and architectural clarity still being weighed. Ayush also acknowledges and apologizes for a formatting issue with email signature usage.
