This is the final version of a documentation patch establishing Git's policy for allowing pseudonymous contributions while maintaining legal compliance. The patch has undergone extensive review and addresses all outstanding concerns from previous discussions.

The change modifies Documentation/SubmittingPatches to explicitly allow contributors to use pseudonyms or preferred names in Signed-off-by trailers, provided the identity is persistent, verifiable, and not misleading. The update maintains the prohibition on anonymous contributions while accommodating valid cases where using a legal name may cause discomfort or safety concerns - particularly for transgender contributors, privacy-conscious developers, and those facing potential harassment.

Key aspects of the policy include requiring distinctive and identifying names (ruling out unreadable characters like zero-width joiners), aligning with Linux kernel precedent (which has legal approval), and maintaining the ability to contact contributors about their work. The patch also preserves the section header ID for linking purposes.

This represents the culmination of thorough legal and technical review, with consensus reached among all involved parties. The v2 incorporates final wording improvements while maintaining all substantive policy decisions from previous iterations. The change helps make Git's contribution process more inclusive while preserving necessary legal protections.
