This is the final implementation of the documentation patch establishing Git's policy for allowing non-real name contributions in Signed-off-by trailers, following extensive legal and community review. The v2 patch incorporates all resolved feedback from the thread, including strengthened language around anonymous contributions (per Junio Hamano's request) and Wikipedia-inspired wording (per consensus between brian m. carlson and Jeff King).

The patch modifies Documentation/SubmittingPatches to explicitly allow pseudonyms while maintaining key requirements: identities must be (1) distinctive, (2) identifying, and (3) not misleading. It preserves the prohibition on anonymous contributions to ensure traceability, while acknowledging valid reasons contributors might use pseudonyms (gender identity concerns, privacy/safety issues, established community reputation). The text draws direct parallels to Linux kernel policy (d4563201f33a) and CNCF guidelines, both having undergone legal review.

Notable technical changes from v1 include: clearer distinction between allowed pseudonyms and prohibited anonymity, added rationale about community goals, and explicit mention of shortened names/initials as acceptable variants. The patch retains the existing section header ID for stable linking. With all substantive debates resolved in the thread, this represents the final policy formulation ready for inclusion.
