This is a final review ack for a refactoring series standardizing C99 `bool` usage in Git's predicate functions. Karthik Nayak confirms the patches look good after their earlier clang-format style feedback was addressed. The series, which began as an experimental change 18 months ago, has now received thorough review from multiple core contributors and appears ready for merging.

The changes are purely mechanical type signature updates (converting `int` returns to `bool` for string matching functions) with no behavioral impact. Karthik notes they weren't previously aware of the experimental period but approves the changes, jokingly offering Phillip Wood the option to ignore their remaining minor clang-format complaints (which appear to be about parameter alignment in one function).

This marks the conclusion of a well-reviewed effort to modernize Git's codebase by formally adopting C99's `bool` type for predicate functions, following an extended real-world testing period. With all substantive feedback addressed and final acks from key maintainers, the series seems poised for inclusion in the next release cycle.
