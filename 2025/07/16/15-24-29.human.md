This is the final sign-off for the bugfix series addressing comment character selection issues when core.commentChar="auto". Phillip Wood provides his official sign-off on patch 2/2, which implements his architectural solution to standardize comment character handling. This was the last remaining step before integration, as all technical aspects had already been approved.

The series fixes a bug where Git would incorrectly consider conflict markers as candidate comment characters during operations like rebase and cherry-pick. Patch 1 (by Ayush Chandekar) prevents conflict markers from being scanned, while patch 2 (based on Phillip Wood's design) ensures a clean slate by resetting to '#' when auto-selection is active.

With this sign-off, the series is now complete and ready for integration. The maintainer will decide whether to take it directly or incorporate it into Wood's parallel work preparing for the eventual deprecation of core.commentChar="auto" in Git 3.0. The solution provides a robust interim fix that will remain relevant until that deprecation is complete.
