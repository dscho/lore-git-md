- Review of test infrastructure in t/perf/p1501-rev-parse-oneline.sh, particularly the build_history() function that creates binary merge trees to stress test the commit ordering
- Suggestions for improving test documentation and presentation, including clarifying the merge tree construction and considering alternative test_perf formatting
