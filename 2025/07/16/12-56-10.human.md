This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about moving configuration settings into `struct repo_settings` as part of the object-file refactoring series. Patrick Steinhardt reports back on Phillip Wood's suggestion to call `prepare_repo_settings()` within `repo_config()` to maintain consistent config parsing timing.

The key finding is that implementing this change causes widespread breakage, particularly around `core.shared_repository` which is used in many places. After attempting 15 patches worth of fixes without reaching a stable state, Steinhardt proposes dropping the last three patches of the series (including this compression level migration) to unblock progress on the rest of the work.

This email highlights the practical challenges in maintaining config parsing behavior while modernizing Git's internals. The discussion shows the careful balance between architectural improvements and maintaining compatibility, with the author opting to defer this particular change rather than risk introducing widespread regressions. The thread continues to demonstrate the project's thoughtful approach to complex refactoring - making measured progress where possible while acknowledging when certain changes require more foundational work.
