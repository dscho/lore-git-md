Performance optimization follow-up discussing heap rebalancing in the priority queue patch series.

Jeff King (Peff) responds to René Scharfe's patch adding `prio_queue_replace()` to optimize commit traversal. The new function combines the get+put operations common in history walking, avoiding redundant heap rebalancing work. Peff confirms the approach still maintains proper ordering by calling `sift_down_root()`, but questions whether it truly reduces work since some rebalancing is still needed.

He observes that while the big-O complexity remains the same (log n), the patch likely cuts the constant factor by skipping the "bubble up" operation from a full put. This aligns with the series' goal of optimizing commit traversal performance, particularly benefiting pathological cases with deep histories while maintaining normal-case speeds. The exchange shows careful technical review of the implementation details, with Peff validating the approach while seeking clarification on the exact performance characteristics.
