Platform compatibility follow-up regarding RHEL 6 build issues. Carlo Marcelo Arenas Belón responds to Ramsay Jones' workaround suggestion for the `getrandom()` linking failure on RHEL 6, noting that simply passing `CSPRNG_METHOD=` on the make command line may not work due to precedence rules with other configuration files.

The email clarifies that the workaround needs to be written to `config.make` (not `config.mak` as previously suggested) to properly override the default settings from `config.mak.uname` and `config.mak.autogen`. The suggested sequence is to first clean the build (`make clean`), then create the configuration override file, then proceed with the modified settings. This builds on the earlier discussion where Ramsay Jones had identified RHEL 6 as likely too old for official support but provided the `CSPRNG_METHOD` workaround as a way to bypass the missing `getrandom()` symbol issue.

Key technical detail: The build system's configuration precedence means workarounds for legacy systems need to account for multiple layers of defaults. The thread continues to explore practical solutions for users needing to build on unsupported platforms while maintaining the position that RHEL 6 is beyond Git's officially supported platform matrix.
