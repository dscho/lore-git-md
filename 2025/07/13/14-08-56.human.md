This is a platform compatibility discussion about Git's line ending behavior on Windows. Jason Cho follows up on Lidong Yan's explanation that `core.eol=lf` only affects line ending conversion during staging (`git add`), not during checkout. Lidong had clarified that files committed with CRLF line endings will always check out with CRLFs unless overridden by attributes, regardless of the `core.eol` setting.

Jason acknowledges this subtle distinction that had previously escaped their notice. The exchange serves as a helpful clarification of Git's line ending handling philosophy, where checkout preserves the original line endings by default. This thread provides important context for Windows users who need to understand why their `core.eol` settings might not produce the expected results, and reinforces that external tooling may be needed for cases requiring strict line ending normalization.
