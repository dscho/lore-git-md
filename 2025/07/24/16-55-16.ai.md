This is a follow-up email in the resolved thread about C99 compound literals policy in Git. Collin Funk provides additional context from other projects' experiences with compound literals, noting that Gnulib/Coreutils have used them since 2017 without issues, including support in the pcc compiler. This serves as supporting evidence for the project's test balloon approach, though it doesn't change the already-established policy (which remains conservative with the 2026 evaluation timeline). The email reinforces that the reftable usage is likely to be unproblematic based on broader ecosystem experience, while maintaining the thread's conclusion that Git will proceed cautiously with this language feature.
