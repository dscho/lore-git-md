This is a feature patch in the `git refs list` series, addressing implementation details of the command's integration with `git for-each-ref`. Junio Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt's review of v3 with several technical observations about the current approach.

The discussion focuses on code organization concerns around the shared usage strings between `git refs list` and `git for-each-ref`. Both agree the current duplication of `REFS_LIST_USAGE` macros is suboptimal, with Hamano noting the technical challenges in sharing them due to differing command name lengths affecting indentation. He suggests a potential refactoring path - extracting common logic from `cmd_for_each_ref()` into a helper function that both commands could use, which would avoid the current approach of having `for-each-ref` be aware of `refs list`.

Hamano also points out the ugliness of checking `argv[0]` to determine command context, comparing it to known problematic patterns from other parts of the codebase. While acknowledging this may be the pragmatic solution here, he strongly recommends adding warning comments to prevent this approach from being copied elsewhere.

The exchange shows the series is nearing final form, with these being polish-level concerns rather than fundamental issues. Hamano's tone suggests these are refinements that could be addressed in future iterations rather than blockers, consistent with his earlier informal sign-off on the overall approach.
