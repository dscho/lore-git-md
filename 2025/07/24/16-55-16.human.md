The email notes that Gnulib and Coreutils have been successfully using compound literals since 2017 without reported issues, even on compilers like pcc. This serves as additional real-world validation beyond Git's own reftable test case, suggesting the feature is widely supported.

The comment doesn't propose any changes to Git's documented plan (which establishes a moratorium until mid-2026), but provides useful supporting evidence that aligns with the project's conservative approach. The information is presented as a data point rather than a challenge to the established policy.

This appears to be a helpful addition to the thread's historical record, though it doesn't require any action since the policy was already finalized in earlier messages. The information may be useful for future reference when the 2026 evaluation period arrives.
