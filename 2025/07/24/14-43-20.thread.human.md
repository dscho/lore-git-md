This would maintain the same level of detail while solving the core visibility problem. The implementation remains focused on sequencer.c modifications, with the main open question being how to precisely distinguish legitimate empty-step skips from other rebase operations.

The discussion has converged on a solution that balances awareness without being intrusive, though some details about warning triggers and potential extensions to similar commands like cherry-pick remain open for consideration.
