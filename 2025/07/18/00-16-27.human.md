This is a performance optimization patch in the `git last-modified` series, adding Bloom filter support to avoid unnecessary diff computations. Taylor Blau (original author of the related GitHub `blame-tree` implementation) provides implementation-level feedback.

The patch uses Bloom filters to skip diff computations when the filters indicate no path modifications occurred, showing significant speedups (50.6% for top-level checks, 36.4% for subdirectories). Taylor notes the subdirectory test may be skewed by testing an unusually small directory (".github") and suggests alternatives like testing the largest subdirectory.

Taylor's review focuses on technical correctness and potential improvements:
1. Confirms proper Bloom key initialization/cleanup in `last_modified_release()`
2. Notes a hacky use of `generation_numbers_enabled()` to prepare commit-graph and suggests exposing `prepare_commit_graph()` directly
3. Verifies no double-free risk in the Bloom key cleanup

This continues Taylor's pattern of detailed implementation review while supporting the overall approach. The feedback suggests minor refinements rather than fundamental changes, indicating the series is maturing well. The performance gains appear legitimate, though the test methodology could be strengthened as noted.
