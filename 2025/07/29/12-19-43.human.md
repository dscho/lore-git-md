This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing refactoring series to move sparse-checkout configuration from global variables to repository-specific storage. Derrick Stolee responds to Ayush Chandekar's question about whether to also move the `index.sparse` setting to `struct repository`.

Stolee advises keeping the scope focused for now, suggesting they only address the sparse-checkout globals (`core.sparsecheckout`, `core.sparsecheckoutcone`, and `sparse.expectfilesoutsideofpatterns`) in this series. He notes that `index.sparse` has different characteristics - it's more isolated in usage and interacts with `feature.experimental` through `prepare_repo_settings()`. The sparse-checkout settings, in contrast, are used more widely across the codebase.

The exchange confirms the architectural direction established earlier in the thread - these settings belong in `struct repository` rather than `repo_settings` - while pragmatically limiting the scope of changes for this particular series. Stolee hints that broader restructuring of `repo_settings` and `prepare_repo_settings()` could be tackled separately later work.
