This is a follow-up in a refactoring thread about removing an unused parameter from Git's blame subsystem. The original patch eliminated the `detailed` parameter from `get_commit_info()` since it was always passed as `1` and showed no measurable performance benefit. 

Junio Hamano had asked for clarification on whether any performance impact was at least measurable, and Han Young confirms that even with extensive testing (100 runs on a 5000-line file), the speedup was under 1 second out of 180 seconds total - effectively indistinguishable from system noise. This final confirmation supports the original assessment that the parameter serves no purpose and can be removed cleanly.

The thread has already reached clear consensus, with Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano both acking the change. This email provides the last piece of requested data, showing there's no hidden performance reason to keep the dead code. The patch appears ready to merge as a straightforward cleanup.
