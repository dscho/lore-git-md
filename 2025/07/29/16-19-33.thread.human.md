René Scharfe and Ayush Chandekar's effort to remove `the_repository` usage from `fmt-merge-msg` has progressed to a second revision, with Christian Couder now mentoring the work. The updated 2-patch series restructures the config handling to use `repo_config()` while maintaining the critical precedence rules that Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano insisted on: `merge.summary` must still override `merge.log` despite being deprecated, and command-line options must take precedence over config settings. 

The new version localizes the `merge_log_config` variable and properly handles the NULL repository case for `-h` flag usage, adding a test to verify this edge case. However, some open questions remain around preserving the exact behavior of `adjust_shortlog_len()` and ensuring proper repository timing. While the architectural direction looks sound, the implementation will need careful review to confirm no regressions in the deprecated-but-still-supported features. The series appears to be converging on a solution that satisfies the thread's non-negotiable constraints, but hasn't yet fully demonstrated compliance with all requirements.
