René Scharfe and Ayush Chandekar's series to remove `the_repository` usage from `fmt-merge-msg` is nearing completion with v2. With all edge cases addressed and approvals in place, the series appears ready for merging as part of the ongoing effort to eliminate `the_repository` usage.
