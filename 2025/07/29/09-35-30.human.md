This is a discussion about quoting strategy in the ongoing `git repo info` command series. Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on Eric Sunshine's recommendation to implement `quote_c_style` output formatting from the start rather than deferring it.

The exchange highlights two perspectives: Eric argues for immediate implementation of proper quoting to establish good habits and prevent future compatibility issues, while Lucas (the series author) had proposed deferring it. Patrick sides with Eric, noting that while the NUL-terminated format is preferred for tooling, implementing proper quoting now is simple and future-proof.

This discussion represents one of the few remaining open questions in the otherwise mature v6 series, which has already addressed test infrastructure concerns and other technical feedback. The quoting decision doesn't block the series but would affect how consumers parse the human-readable output format.
