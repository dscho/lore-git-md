This is a final review acknowledgment for a feature series adding diff context configuration support to interactive patch commands. Phillip Wood gives a positive review of Leon Michalak's v5 patch series, noting the range-diff looks good. 

The series (now complete after several iterations) adds consistent support for configuring diff context lines in interactive commands like `git add -p` through both config (diff.context/diff.interHunkContext) and new command-line options (-U/--unified, --inter-hunk-context). The implementation includes comprehensive test modernization (converting grep/config calls to test_grep/test_config), validation against negative values, and centralized documentation.

Key reviewers like Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood have signed off on the technical approach, including the use of OPT_DIFF_* macros across 6 commands. The series appears ready for merging after addressing all major design concerns raised during review. Phillip's brief approval suggests the implementation meets Git's quality standards and the feature will soon be available to users.
