This is a follow-up in the sparse-checkout refactoring thread, where the architectural direction has now been clearly established to move sparse-checkout configuration into `struct repository` rather than `repo_settings`. Derrick Stolee responds to Ayush Chandekar's question about whether to also move `index.sparse` as part of this effort.

Stolee advises keeping the current series focused on just the sparse-checkout globals (`core.sparsecheckout`, `core.sparsecheckoutcone`, and `sparse.expectfilesoutsideofpatterns`), deferring `index.sparse` to a potential future series. He notes two key differences: `index.sparse` has more isolated usage and interacts with `feature.experimental` through `prepare_repo_settings()`, while the sparse-checkout settings are widely used across the codebase.

The email reinforces the current boundaries of the refactoring effort while acknowledging that broader changes to `repo_settings` and `prepare_repo_settings()` might be addressed separately. This maintains focus on the immediate goal of eliminating the sparse-checkout globals while leaving the door open for future structural improvements. The response suggests the v7 series will proceed with the agreed-upon `struct repository` placement for just the originally targeted variables.
