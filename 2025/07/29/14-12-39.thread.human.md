Patrick Steinhardt continues his work to integrate Git's multi-pack index (MIDX) handling with the object database (ODB) source system, now sending version 2 of his 8-part refactoring series (expanded to 9 patches in this iteration). The core goal remains eliminating redundant information between MIDX structures and their ODB sources, a key part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.

The new version incorporates all feedback from v1, including Junio Hamano's correction about "deduplicating" versus "duplicating" in the series title and Toon Claes' terminology suggestions. It also adds a new commit (4/9) that simplifies the ODB interface by removing a redundant path parameter from `link_alt_odb_entry()`, an improvement identified during the v1 discussion. The changes remain mechanical refactorings - no user-visible behavior is modified - but they significantly clean up the internal interfaces.

The series touches 15 files across MIDX loading/writing, bitmap handling, and revindex operations, with about 240 lines each of additions and deletions. It builds on prior work in the ODB abstraction effort and follows patterns from the parallel `the_repository` removal project. With all review feedback addressed and the interfaces further refined, this appears ready for maintainer consideration.
