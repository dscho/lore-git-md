This is a review comment on the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically regarding Patrick Steinhardt's `ps/object-file-wo-the-repository` branch. 

Toon Claes provides feedback on the second version of this 17-commit series that removes `the_repository` dependencies from the object-file subsystem. He notes having previously done a thorough review of v1 and a lighter review of v2, observing that the range-diff between versions is small. The key change in v2 is the removal of some controversial commits related to global config handling, though this means the series doesn't yet eliminate USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE from object-file.c. 

Despite this limitation, Toon considers the series worthwhile. His comment appears in response to Junio's "What's cooking" status update, which had listed this branch among the active topics. The series is part of the broader effort to reduce implicit assumptions on `the_repository` throughout Git's codebase, with this portion focusing specifically on object-file operations like loose object handling and writing.
