This is part 2 of a 2-patch series in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically targeting `fmt-merge-msg`. The patch mechanically converts repository access from the global `the_repository` to a passed `struct repository` parameter, but introduces a subtle behavior change by reordering config parsing to happen after command-line option processing.

Junio Hamano points out this violates Git's standard pattern where command-line options should override config values, not the other way around. The patch author, Ayush Chandekar, acknowledges the issue but raises a question about handling NULL repository cases, suggesting the reordering may have been an attempt to address that edge case.

The discussion highlights the tension between mechanical refactoring goals and preserving established behavior patterns. While the `the_repository` removal itself appears straightforward, the incidental config parsing order change has become the focus of review attention. Chandekar commits to updating the commit message to explain the rationale, but the core design question remains unresolved - how to maintain proper config/option precedence while completing the repository parameterization.

This patch sits at the intersection of technical debt cleanup (removing globals) and maintaining stable interfaces, with reviewers emphasizing the importance of not introducing subtle behavior changes even in refactoring work. The resolution will likely require either finding a way to keep the original parsing order or clearly documenting the new behavior if intentionally changed.
