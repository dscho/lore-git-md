This is a feature patch continuing the IMAP integration discussion for `git send-email`. Aditya Garg responds to Junio Hamano's architectural proposals for organizing IMAP functionality, focusing on naming and practical usage concerns.

Garg questions the utility of Junio's proposed "B2" mode (using IMAP as primary transport), arguing that SMTP/MSA remains superior and noting `imap-send`'s historical instability. He agrees to rename the option to `--imap-sent-folder` but expresses concern that this naming might overly constrain usage - he personally prefers archiving to custom folders (like a dedicated 'git' folder) rather than just a Sent folder.

The exchange highlights the tension between semantic clarity (Junio's preference for precise naming) and flexibility (Garg's desire to support varied IMAP use cases). While the technical implementation appears sound, the interface design debate continues, with Garg seeking a balance between descriptive naming and practical configurability. The patch remains in good shape technically, awaiting final consensus on the user-facing options.
