This is a cover letter for a 2-patch series proposing to remove the experimental status markers from `git-switch` and `git-restore`. The series comes from a previous stalled attempt to do this, addressing lingering concerns about command interface stability.

The cover letter makes the case that after 6 years, these commands have proven stable enough to remove their experimental labels. It acknowledges past discussions about potentially changing short flags (`-c` for create and `-m` for merge) but argues that changing them now would be disruptive given established usage patterns. The author suggests dropping the less common `-m` short flag entirely while keeping `-c`, though the actual patch series only removes the experimental markers (the flag removal appears to be a separate consideration).

Key technical points: the changes touch command documentation and the checkout.c implementation. The motivation cites improved user adoption by removing hesitation caused by the experimental label, and positions these commands as clearer alternatives to the overloaded `git-checkout`. The series builds on commit 4e43b7ff from 2019 that originally marked them experimental.

This appears well-reasoned and non-controversial, though reviewers may want to confirm the interface is indeed stable enough (particularly regarding the short flags discussion). The patches modify Documentation/*.adoc files and builtin/checkout.c, with the second patch being the main substantive change while the first appears to be a preparatory cleanup.
