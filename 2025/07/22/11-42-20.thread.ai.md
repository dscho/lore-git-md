The patch modifies `builtin/blame.c` to pass `0` (basic info) instead of `1` (full details) to `get_commit_info()` at two call sites (`emit_other()` and `find_alignment()`), building on a 2006 optimization (cee7f245d) that wasn't fully utilized. Patrick Steinhardt has requested two additions to strengthen the patch: 1) concrete performance measurements showing speedup impact, particularly for large repositories, and 2) explicit documentation of why these call sites don't need full commit details. The core optimization approach appears sound but requires stronger justification before merging.
