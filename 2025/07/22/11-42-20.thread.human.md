This thread started as an attempt to speed up `git blame` by reducing unnecessary commit parsing, but performance testing has revealed the optimization provides minimal benefit (less than 1 second improvement on a 180-second run). Patrick Steinhardt initially requested concrete measurements and documentation, while Junio Hamano raised the broader question of whether the partial-parse capability was worth maintaining at all if unused.

Han Young's test results have now confirmed the optimization's negligible impact, validating both Patrick's request for data and Junio's architectural concern. The thread has taken a turn from optimization to simplification, with Han indicating they'll submit a patch to remove the partial-parse capability entirely from `get_commit_info()`. This would effectively revert a 2006 optimization that turned out to be more complex than useful. The discussion demonstrates Git's performance-sensitive culture, where even long-standing optimizations get re-evaluated when their value comes into question.
