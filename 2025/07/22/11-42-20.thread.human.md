Han Young proposed an optimization to `git blame` that reduces unnecessary commit parsing by having two callers request only basic author information instead of full commit details. The change builds on a 2006 optimization that wasn't fully utilized and targets `emit_other()` and `find_alignment()`, which only need author metadata. 

Patrick Steinhardt responded, agreeing with the technical approach but requesting additional justification before merging. He asked for performance measurements showing the speedup, especially for large repositories where this would matter most, and explicit documentation of why these call sites don't need full commit details. The patch appears sound but will likely need revision to include benchmarking data and clearer rationale. This exchange reflects Git's performance-sensitive culture where even small optimizations require solid evidence of their impact.
