This is a policy discussion thread continuation regarding the Rust xdiff integration effort. Patrick Steinhardt responds to concerns about downstream impacts on libgit2 and other projects that use xdiff. He argues that divergence between Git's Rust-based xdiff and libgit2's C version is acceptable given libgit2's maintenance status, noting there's already an existing xdiff fork that could serve as a canonical C-only version if needed. The response downplays circular dependency concerns by suggesting the Rust changes are unlikely to significantly affect libgit2's ecosystem.

Key technical context includes: the existence of libgit2's xdiff fork (github.com/libgit2/xdiff), the historical divergence between bundled xdiff versions, and libgit2's maintenance mode status. Steinhardt's position aligns with his earlier advocacy for full Rust adoption rather than maintaining dual implementations, though here he focuses specifically on downstream compatibility questions raised by Haelwenn Monnier and Eli Schwartz. The response doesn't address Schwartz's version support concerns or Monnier's build system suggestions, instead providing a pragmatic perspective on ecosystem fragmentation.
