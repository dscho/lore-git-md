Performance optimization follow-up for `git blame` commit parsing. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Han Young's patch that reduces unnecessary commit parsing in `builtin/blame.c` by having two callers request only basic author info instead of full commit data. 

Steinhardt requests two key additions to the patch's justification: 1) concrete performance measurements showing the speedup impact, particularly for large repositories where this optimization would matter most, and 2) explicit documentation of why the modified call sites (`emit_other()` and `find_alignment()`) don't need the full commit details they were previously requesting. 

The response maintains the technical focus of the original optimization (building on the 2006 `cee7f245d` commit's early-termination capability) but pushes for stronger evidence of the change's value. This suggests the patch may need revision before merging, though the core approach appears sound. The exchange highlights Git's performance-sensitive culture where even small optimizations require clear benchmarking and justification.
