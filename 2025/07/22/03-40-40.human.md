This is the final naming discussion for Aditya Garg's IMAP sent-message archiving feature in `git send-email`. The technical implementation has been validated through two revisions, with the only remaining question being whether the configuration option names (`sendemail.imapfolder` and `--imap-folder`) should be more specific to the sent-message use case.

Garg responds to concerns from Junio Hamano and brian m. carlson about the generic naming potentially limiting future IMAP-related features. He maintains that while saving to Sent folders is the primary use case, the generic naming intentionally preserves flexibility for other IMAP folder uses (like Outbox or Drafts). He's open to alternative names but wants to keep the design adaptable.

Both reviewers acknowledge the feature's utility - brian m. carlson specifically notes it solves a real problem for self-hosted mail servers and some providers. The discussion is now purely about naming semantics, with no technical objections remaining. Garg's response suggests this may be the final exchange before maintainers decide whether to accept the current naming or request more specific to sent messages.
