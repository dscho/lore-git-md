This is a discussion about handling version control metadata directories (like `.jj` for Jujutsu and `.sl` for Sapling) as precious files in Git. The email builds on previous discussions about precious files protection but focuses on the specific case of VCS metadata directories that can cause problems when colocated with Git repositories.

The key points are:
- Currently `git clean -ixd` will offer to delete `.jj` directories even when gitignored, which could lead to accidental data loss
- These VCS directories are particularly problematic because deleting them can cause significant work loss (similar to deleting `.git`)
- The author questions whether these should be special-cased (like `.git`) or handled by a more general precious-files mechanism
- References are made to prior discussions about precious files and a blog post about Jujutsu usage
- Two specific VCS directories are mentioned: `.jj` (Jujutsu) and `.sl` (Sapling), with notes about their typical usage patterns
