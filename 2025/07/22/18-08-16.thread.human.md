This thread concerns removing the experimental status markers from `git-switch` and `git-restore`, commands that have been stable for six years despite originally being introduced as experimental. The discussion has split into two tracks: straightforward documentation updates (now nearing approval) and a stalled debate about interface design choices.

Junio Hamano has approved the documentation changes in principle but clarified that removing the experimental label reflects a "failed experiment" in UI improvement rather than something to celebrate. Justin Tobler, who proposed the changes, has agreed to adjust the wording to reflect this more neutral stance. The documentation updates (patch 2/2) can now proceed independently while leaving the more contentious interface questions unresolved.

The underlying debate about command flags - particularly the handling of the `-m` short option - remains at an impasse, with Junio maintaining his position and Justin acknowledging the stalemate. After six years of real-world usage, the thread participants agree that breaking changes are no longer feasible, making the experimental markers misleading even if the commands didn't evolve as originally hoped. The documentation updates will likely ship soon, while the interface design discussion may remain unresolved as a historical footnote.
