This thread has completed Patrick Steinhardt's 9-patch series on reflog infrastructure, which introduced a new `git reflog write` subcommand with strict refname validation (no DWIM resolution). The series addressed several technical challenges including HEAD race conditions, committer identity handling, and OID integrity during reflog migration between files and reftable backends. Junio Hamano merged the series after verifying the technical soundness of the approaches.

A follow-up discussion emerged about the inconsistency between the new strict `write` subcommand and existing reflog operations (`drop`, `delete`, `expire`) that do use DWIM resolution. Karthik Nayak noted this discrepancy, while Junio acknowledged that while plumbing-like behavior (explicit refnames) would be ideal for all reflog operations, the historical DWIM behavior in some commands is too entrenched to change. The thread concluded that the new `write` command's strict validation aligns with its plumbing-like purpose, while the existing DWIM behavior in other commands will remain but should be clearly documented. This maintains consistency with Git's broader philosophy where plumbing commands have stricter requirements than porcelain ones.
