This is a follow-up discussion in the IMAP sent-folder archiving feature thread, where Junio C Hamano responds to Aditya Garg's point about alternative workflows. 

Junio acknowledges Garg's suggestion that users could manually pipe `git format-patch` output to `git imap-send` as an alternative to the proposed IMAP archiving feature. However, he points out that this simplistic approach misses the value of `git send-email`'s richer functionality - like dynamic CC list management and cover letter composition - which the new IMAP archiving feature would preserve when saving sent messages.

The exchange reinforces why the current patch series (now in its third iteration) is valuable despite the existence of basic alternatives. Junio's response indicates approval of the feature's design direction, noting that the convenience and integration with `send-email`'s existing capabilities justify the implementation. This appears to be the final conceptual clarification needed before the settled patch series can proceed.
