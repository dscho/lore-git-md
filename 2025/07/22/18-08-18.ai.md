This is the second patch in a 2-part series removing the experimental status markers from `git-switch` and `git-restore`. The patch focuses on updating the documentation to remove the "THIS COMMAND IS EXPERIMENTAL" warnings from both man pages.

The commit message provides historical context, noting these commands were marked experimental in 2019 (commit 4e43b7ff) to allow for breaking changes, but after 6 years of stable use with minimal interface changes, the experimental label is no longer justified. It references a previous attempt to remove these markers that stalled due to concerns about the `-c` and `-m` short flags in `git-switch`. The first patch in this series addressed the `-m` flag removal, while this patch handles the documentation updates.

Key technical details include:
- Removes experimental warnings from Documentation/git-restore.adoc and Documentation/git-switch.adoc
- Builds on the first patch's removal of the `-m` short flag while keeping `-c` due to its common usage
- Argues the commands are stable enough for general use after years without major changes

The patch is straightforward with no behavioral changes - it simply removes the experimental markers from documentation. This appears well-motivated given the commands' long stable history, though reviewers may want to confirm there are no outstanding interface concerns before finalizing the status change. The series as a whole represents a formalization of these commands' stable status in Git's interface.
