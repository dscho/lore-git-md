Feature refinement for IMAP sent-folder archiving in `git send-email`

Aditya Garg responds to Junio's suggestion about alternative IMAP integration modes in the ongoing discussion about archiving sent emails. The thread has settled on naming the feature "imap-sent-folder" (mode B1 in Junio's taxonomy), where emails are both sent via SMTP and archived to a user-specified IMAP folder. 

Garg pushes back against implementing mode B2 (pure IMAP sending without SMTP), arguing that users who want that workflow can already pipe `format-patch` output to `git imap-send` directly. He notes that `imap-send` has historically been underused and sometimes broken, making SMTP the preferred transport method. 

The email raises one remaining naming concern: while "imap-sent-folder" accurately describes the common case of archiving to a Sent folder, it might unintentionally constrain users who want to archive to other folders (like a dedicated "git" folder). This appears to be the last open question before the feature can be finalized.

The exchange shows thoughtful consideration of both technical implementation and user experience, with Garg demonstrating willingness to adapt to project preferences while advocating for practical flexibility in the feature's use. The discussion remains focused and productive, suggesting this feature is nearing readiness for inclusion.
