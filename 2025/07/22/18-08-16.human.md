This is a feature/cleanup patch series proposing to remove the "experimental" status from `git-switch` and `git-restore` commands. The author argues these commands have been stable for six years since their introduction, with no major interface changes needed. The series also removes the `-m` short flag from both commands (keeping `--merge` as a long option) since it was identified as a potential point of contention, while keeping the more commonly used `-c/--create` flag for branch creation.

The patch series addresses lingering concerns from past discussions about these commands' interfaces, particularly around short flag choices. The author makes a pragmatic case that changing established flags now would cause more confusion than benefit, and that removing the experimental label will encourage wider use of these commands that were specifically designed to replace the overloaded `git-checkout`.

The changes are straightforward - removing documentation markings and adjusting flag handling in the implementation. This appears to be a well-considered proposal to finalize these commands' status after their long probationary period. The discussion history shows this has been contemplated before but stalled on minor interface concerns that the current patches directly address.
