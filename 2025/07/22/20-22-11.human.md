This is a reply in the thread about removing experimental markers from `git-switch` and `git-restore`. Junio Hamano responds to Justin Tobler's patch series with a somewhat resigned acceptance of the situation.

Hamano acknowledges that while the original intent was to keep these commands experimental to allow for future UI improvements, the reality after six years is that people have come to rely on the current interface (particularly the `-c/--create` flag). He agrees with the practical conclusion that we can no longer break backward compatibility, but expresses disappointment that the hoped-for improvements never materialized - calling it a "clearly failed experiment" rather than something to celebrate.

The response doesn't raise any new technical concerns about the patches themselves, but provides important historical perspective about why these commands were marked experimental in the first place. This suggests the patches will likely be accepted, though perhaps not with the enthusiastic endorsement the author might have hoped for. The thread appears headed toward resolution with the experimental markers being removed as proposed.
