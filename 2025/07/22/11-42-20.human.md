Performance optimization for `git blame`. The patch modifies two calls to `get_commit_info()` in `builtin/blame.c` to avoid parsing detailed commit information when only basic author metadata is needed. This builds on a 2006 optimization capability in the function that wasn't being fully utilized. The change is small (just two lines) but could provide measurable speed improvements for blame operations, especially on large repositories where commit parsing overhead adds up. The commit message clearly explains the change and its historical context, though it doesn't provide benchmark numbers to quantify the expected improvement. This appears to be a straightforward, low-risk optimization that aligns with Git's performance-conscious design.
