Bugfix series addressing file descriptor leaks in test helpers (`test-truncate`, `test-delta`) and the `archive` builtin. The first patch fixes a leak in `t/helper/test-truncate.c` where a file descriptor opened with `open()` for truncation via `ftruncate()` was not being closed before returning. The change adds a missing `close(fd)` call in three lines that maintain existing style and error handling. While limited to test environments, this prevents potential resource exhaustion in repeated test-truncate calls and follows the codebase's pattern of explicit descriptor cleanup. The series will also address leaks in test-delta and archive builtin, with all changes being minimal style-preserving additions focused on error path cleanup in builtin/archive.c and t/helper/test-*.c. No behavior changes beyond resource management in edge cases.
