This is a policy discussion about Rust adoption in Git's xdiff library, specifically addressing downstream impacts on projects like libgit2. Patrick Steinhardt responds to concerns about Rust becoming a hard requirement, particularly regarding distribution packaging and circular dependencies with libgit2.

Steinhardt downplays the impact on libgit2, noting that xdiff implementations have already diverged and that libgit2 is in maintenance mode. He suggests that any downstream projects needing a C-only version could use libgit2's existing xdiff fork as an alternative upstream. The response acknowledges but doesn't directly address the distribution-specific concerns raised earlier about architectures without Rust support.

The email continues the thread's theme of weighing Rust adoption tradeoffs, focusing here on ecosystem impacts rather than the technical performance questions discussed earlier. Steinhardt presents a pragmatic view that divergence is acceptable given xdiff's slow evolution and libgit2's status, offering a potential migration path for affected projects.
