This is a refactoring patch that finalizes the adoption of the `RemoveBracesLLVM` clang-format rule into Git's main configuration. The rule, which removes optional braces from simple single-statement control blocks (like `if`/`else`), had been previously tested in CI jobs since July 2024 without issues. 

The patch moves the rule from being CI-exclusive to the main `.clang-format` file, simplifying the style-check script by removing its temporary rule injection. This change follows the thread's consensus that the LLVM-style brace removal improves code readability without causing problems. 

As part of a v3 series that's ready for merging, this patch represents a settled technical decision - the rule has Junio Hamano's approval and the CI testing period demonstrated its safety. The change is straightforward, deleting 17 lines of CI-specific handling while adding 6 lines to the permanent configuration.
