This is the first patch in a 3-part series improving Git's clang-format integration, now approved for merging. The patch removes the 80-character ColumnLimit from .clang-format, shifting line-wrapping responsibility to developers while maintaining the project's 80-character recommendation as a guideline rather than enforced rule. The change addresses long-standing issues where the formatter produced false positives by aggressively wrapping lines below 80 characters, sometimes concatenating shorter lines in ways that hurt readability.

The commit message provides historical context, noting two previous attempts (2017 and 2024) to adjust line break penalties that proved insufficient. The removal of ColumnLimit is framed as a pragmatic solution to make the formatter more usable while acknowledging this as a temporary measure - the NEEDSWORK comment suggests future work to find optimal settings that could reinstate automated line wrapping. The patch also references the companion .editorconfig change (from a now-dropped earlier version) that suggested 120 characters as a softer guideline.

Technically, the change removes both the ColumnLimit:80 setting and all related penalty configurations (BreakAssignment, BreakString, etc.), since these only affect line-wrapping behavior. The diff shows these deletions alongside the new ColumnLimit:0 setting and its explanatory comment. This aligns with the thread's resolution to sidestep line-length debates while improving the formatter's practical utility.
