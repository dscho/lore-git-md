K Jayatheerth has followed up on their "kj/renamed-submodule" patch series from early June, which addresses edge cases in submodule handling when a new submodule is added at a path that previously contained a different submodule. While the technical approach was previously explained, the series has been awaiting review feedback for nearly a month without progress. This work is distinct from the ongoing jk/submodule-remote-lookup-cleanup effort mentioned in Junio's status report.
