The Git Rev News team has published edition 124 of their regular community update. Christian Couder announced the release, thanking contributors Luca Milanesio, Bruno Brito, Lee Reilly and Štěpán Němec for their work on this issue. The publication provides a summary of recent Git development activity for community members who want to stay informed. 

This appears to be a routine announcement for an established project with multiple maintainers (Christian Couder, Jakub Narębski, Markus Jansen and Kaartic Sivaraam). The team has already opened issue #786 on git/git.github.io to track contributions for the next edition. The email serves primarily to notify the mailing list of the publication's availability rather than to discuss technical changes.
