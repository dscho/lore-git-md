Junio Hamano has now also given his maintainer ack, though it references v2 version of the series - this indicates the approach has had maintainer approval since an earlier iteration. The core team appears aligned on introducing reftable as the default via Git's established breaking change process, with proper guardrails in place. While some ecosystem coordination (with JGit/libgit2) remains ongoing, there are no outstanding technical objections blocking this change. The series looks ready for inclusion in Git 3.0's development track.
