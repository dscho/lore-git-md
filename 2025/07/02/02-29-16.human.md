This is a brief follow-up discussion in the parse-options refactoring series, specifically about the final patch handling OPTION_COUNTUP precision. Patrick Steinhardt had raised a question about whether COUNTUP should enforce signed integers, noting it was surprising the current implementation accepts unsigned values. René Scharfe responds by acknowledging the technical merit but declines to make the change in this series, citing the scope impact on existing callers as too disruptive. He references his earlier cover letter where this limitation was noted, and leaves the door open for future work on the signedness issue. Patrick accepts this resolution with a "Fine with me, thanks!" closing the discussion on this point. The exchange is brief and technical, confirming the series will ship as-is while documenting a potential future improvement.
