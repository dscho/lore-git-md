Eric Salem reported a bug where `git log --diff-filter`'s lowercase exclusion filters (like `d` to exclude deletions) weren't working with default output formats. Jeff King (peff) diagnosed this as a regression from a 2022 change (commit 75408ca949) that had unintentionally made exclusion filters only work when Git computed diffs, rather than affecting commit selection as intended.

The fix was straightforward - a one-line change in `revision.c` to properly consider both filter fields during revision setup. Karthik Nayak reviewed and acked the solution, while Eric confirmed it resolved the issue. Now Junio Hamano has given his final approval, noting the patch correctly restores the pre-regression behavior where exclusion filters work regardless of output format. With the maintainer's ack, this small but important fix will be merged soon, closing the thread with all parties satisfied.
