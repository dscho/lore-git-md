This is a follow-up discussion on the final fix for the `git diff` exit status bug with `--quiet` and rename/copy detection flags. The email provides performance benchmarking data and some technical discussion about the solution.

D. Ben Knoble confirms that René Scharfe's v2 patch (which disables all rename detection in quiet mode) both fixes the original issue and provides significant performance improvements - showing a 3.6x speedup in their benchmark of the problematic flag combination (`--cached --quiet --no-ext-diff --find-copies-harder`).

The discussion touches on some subtle aspects of the diff machinery's behavior. Knoble questions why having changes doesn't allow early termination in quiet mode, indicating some remaining uncertainty about the deeper interaction between the diff queues and early termination logic. However, they acknowledge that the current solution works correctly and covers both cases (with and without `--no-ext-diff`).

The email serves as both a verification of the fix's effectiveness and a record of the substantial performance benefit gained by skipping unnecessary rename detection work in quiet mode. No new issues are raised - this appears to be an acceptance of the final solution after the earlier architectural discussion about quiet mode's relationship to rename detection.
