The implementation comes with comprehensive test coverage in t3650-replay-basics.sh that verifies basic reverts, revert-of-revert cases, conflicts, and multi-commit ranges. The documentation clearly explains the new option and its relationship to standard revert operations, noting the intentional incompatibility with `--contained` to prevent cross-branch inconsistencies. The changes touch builtin/replay.c, sequencer.c, and the git-replay.adoc documentation while maintaining the command's server-side focus.
