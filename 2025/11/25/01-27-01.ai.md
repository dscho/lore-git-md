Junio C Hamano's follow-up in the `git clone` config parsing thread:

This email continues the discussion about whitespace handling in `git clone -c` arguments, with Junio responding to Jeff King's analysis of the historical behavior. Junio acknowledges Jeff's explanation that the old behavior (allowing whitespace in `-c` arguments) was effectively treating them as "stuck" short options with leading whitespace in the value.

Junio initially thought this was a bug but now sees it as a quirk of option parsing, though he questions the utility of preserving leading whitespace in config values. He suggests the ideal solution would have been to reject such whitespace from the start, but recognizes it's too late for that now. 

The email floats the idea of treating this as a regression to fix while deprecating the whitespace feature, but Junio expresses skepticism about whether that engineering effort would be worthwhile. The discussion appears to be moving toward accepting the new behavior as correct, despite breaking some historical usage patterns.
