Maintainer follow-up on integer parsing safety in cache-tree.

Junio Hamano responds to Phillip Wood's review of Jeff King's patch that replaces strtol() with custom parsing in cache-tree code. The discussion focuses on edge cases in integer parsing - specifically handling of negative signs and validation of numeric input.

Wood had noted two potential issues with the current implementation: it allows multiple '-' signs (unlike strtol) and accepts a standalone '-' as valid input. Hamano acknowledges these points but argues against adding complexity to make the parsing stricter, noting that his own attempt to do so resulted in messy code.

The maintainer shares an incomplete alternative implementation that shows a more thorough approach - tracking whether digits were seen, properly handling overflow, and validating the full numeric range. However, he seems to suggest this level of rigor may be overkill for the cache-tree use case.

This exchange represents the final discussion point in an otherwise ready-to-merge series of ASan-related fixes. The parsing refinements appear to be left as potential future work rather than blocking the current changes. The thread shows the project's pragmatic approach to balancing correctness with maintainability in low-level parsing code.
