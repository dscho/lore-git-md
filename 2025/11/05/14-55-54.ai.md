Bug report follow-up identifying Delta pager as the source of `git grep -l` filename formatting issue.

Lucas Seiki Oshiro responds to the ongoing thread about `git grep -l` displaying dashes as colons, pointing to a known Delta pager bug (#1259 in Delta's issue tracker) as the likely cause. They confirm the issue doesn't occur with `--no-pager` and suggest testing with alternative pagers (`less` and `cat`) to verify the behavior. The email shifts focus from Git itself to Delta's handling of the output, though it doesn't completely rule out some Git interaction being involved.

Key technical details:
- Delta pager issue #1259 specifically mentions the dash-to-colon transformation
- Suggests testing with `core.pager=less` and `core.pager=cat` as diagnostic steps
- Implies the bug may be entirely in Delta rather than Git, though Git's pager handling could still be a factor

This changes the investigation direction from a Git regression to a potential pager interaction issue. The email doesn't provide new reproduction cases but offers a concrete lead to follow up on. The suggestion to test with different pagers could help isolate whether the issue is purely in Delta or involves Git's pager handling.
