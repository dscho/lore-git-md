This is a bugfix patch in Junio Hamano's series introducing "incomplete-line" whitespace detection. It corrects how line numbers are counted when handling "\ No newline at end of file" markers in diffs.

The patch fixes an issue where line numbers were only being incremented for the preimage (old file) when encountering these markers, even though they can appear after added, deleted, or unchanged lines. The solution adds a `last_line_kind` field to track what type of line preceded the marker, allowing proper incrementing of both preimage and postimage line counters as needed.

While the incorrect counting didn't affect functionality (since these numbers are only used for whitespace warnings), the change makes the behavior more logically consistent. The new tracking of line types will also be useful for the broader goal of the series - detecting and handling incomplete lines as configurable whitespace errors.

This is the fourth patch in a 12-part series, following preparatory cleanups and preceding the core implementation of incomplete-line detection. The change is straightforward but important for accurate diagnostics, and includes a BUG() check to catch any unexpected line types.
