CI / build system update to migrate macOS CI jobs from macos-13 to macos-14 images. This is a straightforward mechanical change updating the pool specification in GitHub Actions workflow for four macOS jobs (osx-clang, osx-reftable, osx-gcc, and osx-meson). The patch comes from Junio C Hamano who notes it's a direct replacement but cautions there may be follow-up adjustments needed for tool version differences on the new images.

The change is being fast-tracked to ensure CI passes for upcoming releases, with the macos-13 images being fully deprecated on December 4th after being marked deprecated since September 22nd. The patch has already been test-merged to 'master' and 'next' branches and appears sufficient for CI to pass, though the author acknowledges potential future adjustments may be needed for toolchain version differences. The diff shows simple string replacements in the workflow file's job definitions.
