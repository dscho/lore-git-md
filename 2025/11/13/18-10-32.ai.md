The changes are straightforward mechanical updates to add the new flag where needed. The bulk of the work is in `t/lib-submodule-update.sh` which contains shared test infrastructure, with one additional change in `t7508-status.sh`. Each instance where tests were explicitly adding submodules now includes the new flag to maintain the expected behavior under the new regime.

This patch follows the established pattern from earlier in the series, ensuring test coverage remains accurate while the underlying behavior changes. The modifications are minimal and focused - just adding the necessary option to existing test commands without restructuring the test logic. This maintains test validity while adapting to the new submodule ignore behavior being introduced in the series.
