This is a bugfix patch in the final stages of review, addressing a regression in `git fetch`'s tag handling during batched reference transactions. Junio Hamano provides final review feedback on Karthik Nayak's v5 patch series, suggesting potential improvements to the cleanup logic.

3. Provides an untested diff showing this approach, where transaction committing becomes the final cleanup step rather than being split between main flow and cleanup.
