This is a follow-up discussion about brian m. carlson's patch to prevent adding submodules with mismatched hash algorithms. Jeff King provides a detailed code review focusing on two edge cases in the implementation.

He also notes the warning/error behavior around directly adding repositories may evolve in the future.
