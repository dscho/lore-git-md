This is a follow-up discussion in the OSX keychain credential helper bugfix thread. Koji Nakamaru responds to Junio Hamano's concerns about the proposed revert of commit e1ab45b2, which introduced problematic state tracking in the credential helper.

Hamano had questioned whether a straight revert was appropriate given that the behavior has been present since Git v2.46 and might disrupt existing workflows. He suggested a proper fix would be preferable to a simple revert unless addressing a critical regression.

Nakamaru acknowledges these concerns and indicates they will pursue an alternative solution - enhancing the state tracking mechanism to encode the full credential information from the last "get" operation, rather than just a simple "seen" flag. This approach would maintain the optimization's intent while properly handling cases where invalid credentials are followed by valid ones from other helpers.

The discussion shows the patch author is pivoting from the revert strategy to developing a more robust fix that addresses the original optimization's shortcomings while avoiding potential regressions for existing users. This suggests the next submission will likely be a new implementation rather than a revert.
