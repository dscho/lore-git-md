This is a follow-up discussion in the `mkstemp()` refactoring thread. René Scharfe responds to Junio Hamano and Jeff King's suggestion about how to handle the remaining direct `mkstemp()` calls in Git's codebase.

The discussion centers on whether to define `mkstemp()` as a macro (as proposed in the patch) versus adding it to Git's banned functions list. René acknowledges that banning `mkstemp()` outright would be simpler than the macro approach, since there are very few callers (only one remaining in Git's code). He agrees with Jeff King's assessment that the inconvenience of losing the standard function would be minimal given how rarely it's used.

This email represents the final confirmation that the refactoring approach is sound, with all parties now aligned on the solution. The thread has effectively concluded with consensus that Git's internal `git_mkstemp_mode()` provides better performance and cross-platform consistency than the system `mkstemp()`, and that the remaining direct calls can be safely migrated.
