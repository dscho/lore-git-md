This thread began with a bug report from Anders Kaseorg about incorrect bitmap initialization in `git-last-modified`, particularly visible on s390x systems. Toon Claes quickly provided a one-line fix (adding `sizeof(eword_t)` to a memset calculation) which Jeff King reviewed and Junio merged to 'next' for backporting. The fix addresses behavior that had been incorrect since v2.52.0.

The discussion then expanded to consider broader architectural improvements around array zeroing patterns, with Jeff King suggesting potential helper macros and Toon Claes submitting a v2 patch proposing a `MEMZERO_ARRAY` macro. While technically sound, the proposal was ultimately deemed unnecessary for the current codebase, with Junio Hamano preferring to keep just the minimal fix. 

In the final email, Junio confirms the thread's conclusion - only the original bugfix will be merged, while noting Patrick Steinhardt's separate suggestion about standardizing array zeroing patterns could be considered as a future cleanup effort. The key participants (Kaseorg, Claes, King, and Hamano) reached consensus on the technical solution while evaluating but ultimately rejecting broader maintainability changes. The outcome is a focused correction to `builtin/last-modified.c` that properly sizes the memset operation for bitmap initialization.
