Bugfix for `git-last-modified` bitmap initialization issue in `builtin/last-modified.c`, with architectural follow-up discussion. The immediate bug (incorrect `memset` size calculation causing incorrect behavior, more visible on s390x) was reported by Anders Kaseorg, fixed by Toon Claes with a one-line solution multiplying count by `sizeof(eword_t)`, reviewed by Jeff King (Helped-by), and merged to 'next' for backport to maint. The fix addresses behavior dating back to v2.52.0.

Discussion evolved to architectural improvements with Jeff King suggesting (1) a `CLEAR_ARRAY` macro and (2) `bitmap_clear()` function encapsulation. Toon Claes submitted v2 proposing `MEMZERO_ARRAY` macro in `git-compat-util.h` using `st_mult()` for array zeroing. Jeff King identified 5 potential use sites across last-modified, simple-ipc, diff-delta, hashmap, and pack-revindex subsystems where the macro could replace existing `memset` patterns. While technically sound, King questioned its broader value given limited use cases. Junio Hamano remained neutral between approaches, noting the immediate fix was already merged.

Toon Claes confirmed the architectural discussion is concluded, accepting the maintainer's implicit preference for the minimal fix without standardizing array zeroing patterns. Junio Hamano acknowledges this agreement while noting potential merit in Patrick Steinhardt's unthreaded suggestion to standardize on `CLEAR_ARRAY()` as a future Coccinelle refactoring exercise. The thread shows consensus on technical correctness while evaluating but ultimately rejecting maintainability tradeoffs of new helper macros, with the final outcome being only the original bugfix merged to 'next' and maint. The technical detail remains the `sizeof(eword_t)` correction in `builtin/last-modified.c` bitmap initialization, leaving broader array-zeroing patterns as a hypothetical future cleanup opportunity. Key participants (Kaseorg, Claes, King, Hamano) refined both solution and architectural context, with Steinhardt's late suggestion noted but not pursued.
