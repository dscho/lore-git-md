Bugfix follow-up discussing memory initialization in `git-last-modified`. Jeff King agrees with Toon Claes's fix for the bitmap initialization issue, where the memset() call was incorrectly passing element count instead of byte count. The one-line patch multiplying by sizeof(eword_t) is the straightforward solution.

King explores two broader considerations that could prevent similar bugs: (1) introducing a CLEAR_ARRAY macro for zeroing memory (though notes limitations), and (2) encapsulating the bitmap internals with a bitmap_clear() function. However, he concludes these may be overengineering for this specific case, as the current implementation is unlikely to change. The discussion remains focused on this particular bug rather than proposing additional changes.
