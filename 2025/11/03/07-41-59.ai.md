Refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ODB abstraction series (4/13 in v3). This mechanical change moves the `fetch_if_missing` global variable declaration from object-file.h to odb.h, better reflecting its scope as an object-database-wide setting rather than being loose-object-specific. The variable controls whether missing objects should be fetched when extensions.partialClone is set, defaulting to 1 (fetch enabled).

The patch follows the series' pattern of reorganizing object database functionality into logical groupings, with odb.h becoming the canonical header for ODB-wide declarations. This continues the incremental refactoring approach seen in previous patches, maintaining behavior while improving code organization. The change is straightforward - just moving the declaration and its documentation between headers - with all callers continuing to work unchanged since they include both headers transitively.

This builds on earlier work standardizing ODB-related names and consolidating helper functions. The patch appears well-scoped and low-risk, similar to the mechanical renames in patch 3/13. Reviewers have consistently approved this style of change in previous iterations, suggesting this will likely be uncontroversial. The series as a whole is progressing toward enabling pluggable ODB backends by properly abstracting object storage operations.
