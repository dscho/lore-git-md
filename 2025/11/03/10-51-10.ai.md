This is a bug report about inconsistent behavior in `git diff` when combining the `--submodule=diff` and `--name-only` options. The issue appears to affect all versions since `--submodule=diff` was introduced.

The reporter demonstrates that `--name-only` fails to properly recurse into submodules when used with `--submodule=diff`, only showing the submodule path rather than the individual changed files within the submodule. This creates a discrepancy between the output suggests (just the submodule path) and what actually changed (files within that submodule). The bug is demonstrated through a simple reproduction case comparing line counts between two commands.

The report is clear and provides a straightforward way to verify the issue, though it doesn't propose a solution. This appears to be a genuine inconsistency in behavior that could affect users trying to programmatically process submodule diffs. The bug has likely gone unnoticed because the combination of these two options may not be commonly used together.
