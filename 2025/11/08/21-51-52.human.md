This is a feature patch series introducing a new 'is-needed' subcommand for `git maintenance` to check whether maintenance tasks are required without executing them. The series builds on previous work to expose backend optimization checks and integrates them into the maintenance framework.

The 5-patch series has evolved through several rounds of review, with the current version addressing naming clarifications (changing 'use_heuristics' to 'use_geometric') and resolving a discussion about boolean return styles. The implementation now includes:

1. Ref backend infrastructure (patches 1-3) that exposes optimization checks through a new `optimize_required` field in the ref storage interface, with specific implementations for different backends including reftable.

2. Maintenance integration (patches 4-5) that adds the checking logic to `pack_refs_condition()` and introduces the user-facing 'is-needed' subcommand. The subcommand can check specific maintenance tasks or use auto-heuristics, with plans to eventually support schedule-based verification.

The series is technically complete with Reviewed-by tags from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler, and has addressed all feedback including Junio Hamano's guidance on boolean handling. It depends on two other in-flight topics (`kn/refs-optim-cleanup` and `ps/ref-peeled-tags`) but should merge cleanly with the codebase. The implementation looks solid with thorough testing and documentation, providing a foundation for future maintenance-related features.
