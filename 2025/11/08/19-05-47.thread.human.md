The core issue stemmed from performing expensive rename detection even when the results wouldn't be shown due to `--quiet`.

René Scharfe's solution, now in its second iteration, fundamentally changes quiet mode behavior to skip rename detection entirely when output is suppressed. Performance testing by D.
