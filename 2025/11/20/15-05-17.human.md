This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's `git-history` series, specifically addressing the interaction between `add-patch` and `add-interactive` subsystems. Phillip Wood provides a review with some concerns about the approach.

The patch moves configuration handling from `add-interactive.c` to `add-patch.c`, introducing new `interactive_options` and `interactive_config` structs to better separate concerns. It aims to disentangle the circular dependency where `add-interactive` had to know about `add-patch`'s options.

Phillip Wood raises several thoughtful points in his review:
1. Questions whether renaming `struct add_p_opt` is appropriate since its members are only relevant to `run_add_p()`
2. Suggests that moving the new structs to `add-patch.c` might be confusing given their "interactive" naming
3. Expresses skepticism that passing `interactive_options` is any clearer than the previous `add_p_opt` approach

The changes are substantial, touching 10 files with 400+ lines changed, mostly moving and reorganizing code rather than adding new functionality. Phillip's review indicates this refactoring may need further discussion about the best way to structure these interrelated components.

This appears to be a well-intentioned cleanup, but the review suggests the approach might benefit from more discussion about naming and structure before being finalized. The patch is part of a larger series that's already had significant review, so these concerns are being raised at an appropriate point in the process.
