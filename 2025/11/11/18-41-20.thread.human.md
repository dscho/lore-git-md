This thread addressed a long-standing but harmless misspelling in Git's `.gitattributes` file, where `!indent` had been incorrectly specified (it should have been `-indent` to disable indent checking) since 2008. Junio Hamano proposed simply removing the ineffective attribute rather than correcting it, as this would make any future whitespace policy changes immediately visible to developers. The change is minimal—a one-line removal—but Junio submitted it for review anyway to maintain consistency in how all changes are treated in the project, even trivial ones.

Jeff King (peff) reviewed and fully endorsed the approach, calling the reasoning "perfect" and the result sensible. The thread concluded with clear consensus, and no further discussion is expected. The patch is now ready to be picked up for merging. Junio also noted a similar issue in `git-gui/.gitattributes`, which may warrant a follow-up patch, but that is outside the scope of this thread.
