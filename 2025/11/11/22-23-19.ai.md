This is a refactoring patch in Ezekiel Newren's xdiff modernization series, specifically addressing type safety for pointer-relative offsets. The change converts `dstart` and `dend` fields in `xdfile_t` from `long` to `ptrdiff_t` to properly represent their role as pointer differences. 

Junio Hamano acknowledges the technical merit of the type change but questions the rationale for moving these fields to the end of the struct, noting that their eventual removal (planned in a future patch) makes the intermediate repositioning seem unnecessary. The patch itself is straightforward - a one-line type change and field reordering in `xdiff/xtypes.h`.

This continues the series' systematic approach to type safety, though the maintainer's comment suggests the field movement may be an over-optimization given the fields' planned deprecation. The discussion highlights the careful balance between incremental refactoring and final architecture in this cross-language compatibility work.
