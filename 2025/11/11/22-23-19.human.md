Maintainer feedback on xdiff refactoring patch. This is part of Ezekiel Newren's series modernizing xdiff types for Rust FFI compatibility, specifically addressing the `dstart` and `dend` fields in the `xdfile_t` struct.

Junio Hamano acknowledges the technical correctness of changing these fields from `long` to `ptrdiff_t` since they represent pointer offsets. However, he questions the patch's claim that moving these fields now will "disturb the struct layout less" when they'll eventually be removed entirely. The maintainer suggests the field ordering change doesn't provide clear benefits and could be deferred until the fields are actually removed in a later series.

The exchange shows careful attention to structural changes even in preparatory work, with the maintainer ensuring each step has clear justification. This patch appears uncontroversial technically but receives scrutiny regarding its placement in the refactoring sequence.
