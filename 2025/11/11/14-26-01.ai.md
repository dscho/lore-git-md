Bugfix discussion in the whitespace handling series. Phillip Wood points out that Junio's patch 04/12 still incorrectly increments line numbers when processing "\ No newline at end of file" annotations in diffs. While Junio's version made the incrementing more coherent by tracking the last line type (+, -, or space), Phillip argues the counters shouldn't be incremented at all since these annotations refer to the previous line rather than being new lines themselves. The discussion centers around the `lno_in_preimage` and `lno_in_postimage` counters in `diff.c`'s emit callback machinery, which track line numbers being processed for the before/after sides of a diff. This is a minor but technically important correction to the whitespace series' diff display logic.
