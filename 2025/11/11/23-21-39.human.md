This is a maintainer review response in the xdiff refactoring series for Rust FFI compatibility. Junio Hamano is reviewing Ezekiel Newren's patch that splits the `ha` field in `xrecord_t` into two distinct fields: `line_hash` (a `uint64_t`) and `minimal_perfect_hash` (a `size_t`).

The change improves type safety by separating concerns - the line's content hash from its position in the diff's hash table. Junio notes the 33% memory increase per record (from one `ulong` to two 8-byte fields) but considers this acceptable. He highlights the removal of "random-looking" casts in `xdl_classify_record()` as a particularly clean improvement.

This continues the series' careful balancing act between type safety and memory efficiency, with Junio's review validating both the technical approach and the tradeoffs. The change appears well-justified for its Rust compatibility benefits, though readers should be aware it's part of a larger 10-patch series modernizing xdiff's internals.
