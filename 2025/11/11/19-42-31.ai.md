The change is straightforward since `rindex` is only used for array indexing and doesn't require cascading changes elsewhere in the code. This follows the pattern established in earlier patches of the series where array-related fields are consistently updated to use `size_t` for proper size representation. The modification maintains behavior while improving type safety and platform independence.
