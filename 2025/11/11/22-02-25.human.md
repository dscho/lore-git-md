This is a documentation/cleanup patch that concludes a settled discussion about removing an ineffective whitespace attribute from Git's `.gitattributes` file. The patch removes the long-standing but incorrectly specified `!indent` attribute (which should have been `-indent` to properly disable indent checking) since it has been a no-op since 2008. 

Junio Hamano explains two motivations for sending this simple change through the normal patch review flow: first, to maintain consistency in how all contributors' changes are treated, and second, to ensure the change is properly archived in the mailing list history for `git log --notes=amlog` tracking. Jeff King had already reviewed and approved the technical approach, calling the reasoning "perfect" and the result sensible.

The thread appears fully resolved with this acknowledgment, marking the end of this minor but principled cleanup. The change is straightforward and uncontroversial, serving mainly to keep Git's own codebase consistent with its contribution practices.
