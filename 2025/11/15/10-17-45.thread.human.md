This thread discusses adding improved Lisp dialect support to Git's userdiff functionality, which helps `git diff` highlight function definitions and other structural elements. The initial proposal from Scott L. Burson suggested a new "lisp" driver specifically for Common Lisp, complementing the existing "scheme" driver. However, Junio Hamano suggested that a single driver might handle all Lisp dialects, including Scheme and Emacs Lisp.

Scott has refined his proposal in response to feedback, fixing a regex issue with tab handling and adding case-insensitive matching to better support Lisp's case-insensitive nature. While he now agrees a unified driver could work technically, he suggests naming it "lisp" rather than "scheme" for better discoverability, and proposes a driver aliasing mechanism to support both names without breaking existing configurations.

The discussion has moved from debating whether to have separate drivers to working out the details of a unified solution. Key remaining questions are whether Atharva Raykar (maintainer of the scheme driver) finds the patterns safe for Scheme code, and whether the final implementation will use Scott's aliasing suggestion or another approach. Junio appears open to unification but hasn't yet commented on the naming or aliasing proposals. The technical solution is converging, with the exact naming and configuration details being the primary open items.
