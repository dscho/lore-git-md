Kiril Ivanov's patch series introduces a quiet mode for Git's bash prompt upstream indicators, providing a cleaner display when branches are in sync while maintaining divergence indicators. The implementation is thorough, with modernized tests and comprehensive coverage of all mode combinations. The series is currently at version 1 and appears technically complete.

Junio C Hamano noted in passing that while the patches themselves are appropriate for the bash-specific context, the test script still uses a `#!/bin/sh` shebang despite being fundamentally a bash script. He suggested updating this to `#!/bin/bash` for accuracy, though this is a minor maintainability point separate from the feature work. The core functionality of the quiet mode remains uncontested, with no substantive objections raised so far. The series seems ready for further review or potential merging once any final polish (like the shebang suggestion) is addressed.
