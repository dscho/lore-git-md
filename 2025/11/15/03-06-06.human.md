This is a documentation-focused discussion in Ezekiel Newren's xdiff refactoring series for Rust FFI compatibility. Ramsay Jones provides a technical review of the newly added `unambiguous-types.adoc` documentation, specifically focusing on the characterization of C's `char` type.

Jones points out that the documentation incorrectly describes C's `char` as "signless" when in fact its signedness is implementation-defined behavior that must be documented by each compiler. He cites the C standard (sections 6.2.5 and 6.3.1.1) and notes that some compilers like GCC even allow controlling this behavior via flags like `-fsigned-char`.

The feedback is narrowly focused on the technical accuracy of one section of the documentation, suggesting the wording should be adjusted to properly reflect the C standard's requirements. This appears to be a precision-oriented review rather than a fundamental challenge to the overall approach of the type mapping documentation.
