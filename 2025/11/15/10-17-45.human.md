This is a feature patch adding a new userdiff driver for Lisp dialects, particularly targeting Common Lisp. The patch introduces a "lisp" driver to complement the existing "scheme" driver, as the author notes the scheme driver doesn't quite work well for Common Lisp syntax.

The new driver handles two key aspects of Lisp syntax: (1) function definitions (matching lines starting with parentheses or slightly indented "(def" patterns) and (2) symbol recognition (supporting Common Lisp's vertical-bar-delimited symbols and general word boundaries). The implementation is a straightforward addition to userdiff.c with regex patterns similar to other language drivers in the file.

The patch appears well-contained and focused, though the commit message could benefit from more detail about why the scheme driver is insufficient and what specific Common Lisp constructs the new driver handles better. The change looks technically sound for its stated purpose of improving diff highlighting for Lisp code.
