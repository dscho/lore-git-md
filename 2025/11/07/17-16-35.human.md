This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about reporting binary file attributes in Git's diff output format. Justin Tobler responds to Torsten Bögershausen's suggestion about using TAB as a separator by proposing two alternative output formats for the `--raw-extended` option.

The first format keeps the traditional diff structure but moves the binary/crlf attributes before the TAB delimiter, while the second more radical proposal puts the attributes at the very start of the line. Justin acknowledges that parsers would need to handle space-delimited key=value pairs until hitting the TAB, but notes the second format might be visually cleaner. He expresses mild preference for the latter but remains open to feedback.

This email continues the technical refinement of the output format, though it's happening against the backdrop of Junio Hamano's earlier architectural challenge about whether diff output is the right place for these attributes at all. The discussion remains focused on parsing details while the larger design question looms unresolved.
