This thread discusses the potential removal of the `git whatchanged` command, which shows file modifications per commit. Franz Brauße initiated the discussion by advocating to keep the command, citing its usefulness in their regular workflow. The command is considered redundant with `git log --stat` and other options, but has established users who rely on its specific output format.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk responded by clarifying that the deprecation is already decided, as `git log` with flags like `--no-merges --raw` provides equivalent functionality. He offered migration guidance, suggesting alternative flags for different use cases. While Franz's feedback highlights real-world usage patterns, Kristoffer's response indicates this is more about documenting the transition than reopening the deprecation decision. The discussion may continue around whether the migration path adequately serves existing workflows, but the technical direction appears settled.
