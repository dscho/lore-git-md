This is a refinement discussion in the ongoing RFC for `git diff --raw-extended` attribute reporting. Torsten Bögershausen responds to Justin Tobler's latest single-line format proposal, suggesting the use of TAB as a separator before the filename rather than spaces, drawing from his experience with `git ls-files --eol` output format.

The email notes that TAB separation worked well for machine parsing in the EOL reporting context while maintaining human readability, and proposes applying the same approach to the new binary attribute reporting. This would keep all attribute information before the TAB delimiter, making it easier for parsers to reliably split fields. The suggestion appears well-considered, referencing prior art in Git's own tooling where this approach has proven successful.

The thread continues to focus on output format details that maintain backward compatibility while improving parseability. Torsten's input carries weight given his experience with similar output formatting challenges in Git, though the proposal doesn't fundamentally alter Justin's key=value attribute approach - it just refines the field separation strategy. This seems like a constructive suggestion that could simplify parsing without disrupting the core design consensus that's emerging in the thread.
