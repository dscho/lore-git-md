This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the reftable optimization series. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio Hamano's question about parameter naming in patch 2 of the series.

The key point is whether to rename the `use_heuristics` parameter to something more specific like `geometric`, since the current implementation only uses geometric progression heuristics. Patrick confirms that no other heuristics are planned in the near future, and agrees that renaming would be appropriate.

This appears to be the last open question before the series can be integrated. The rest of the technical discussion seems settled, with the series having received positive reviews and being otherwise ready for inclusion. The exchange is brief and constructive, with Patrick providing clear confirmation that helps move the discussion toward resolution.
