Rust integration update. Patrick Steinhardt reports that after discussion with Ezekiel Newren, they've agreed to let Ezekiel take over the `ps/rust-cbindgen` topic (which introduces cbindgen for Rust bindings generation). The topic is being removed from the integration branches for now while Ezekiel leads its further development.

This was one of the experimental Rust-related efforts in the 'seen' branch, separate from gitoxide, that aimed to improve Rust interoperability. The patch series included build system changes to generate bindings via cbindgen, CI updates, and some infrastructure cleanup. 

The decision to hand off leadership suggests the topic may see significant restructuring, possibly incorporating Ezekiel Newren's earlier suggestion about workspace organization. This leaves the NonStop platform compatibility question (raised by Randall Becker) still to be addressed in future iterations.

The change doesn't affect other Rust-related work in the pipeline, nor the other topics Patrick had previously approved for promotion to 'next'. The handoff appears amicable and reflects the project's collaborative approach to complex technical initiatives.
