This is a proposal for an Outreachy internship project focused on refactoring Git's global state, specifically targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The proposal comes from Bello Caleb Olamide, a first-time open source contributor who has already had one microproject merged into Git's codebase.

The project aims to identify and relocate repository-scoped global variables from `environment.c` into `struct repository` or `struct repo-settings`, following patterns established in prior work. The proposal demonstrates familiarity with previous refactoring efforts, citing specific examples like `core.hooksPath` and `core.sharedRepository` migrations, and discusses both successful strategies (adding accessor functions) and alternative approaches (passing parameters directly).

Notable challenges highlighted include handling variables used during early startup (like `have_git_dir()`) and complex cases like `git_attributes_file` where the relationship with the attributes subsystem requires careful consideration of ownership. The proposal references ongoing discussions about these edge cases and shows awareness of the need to understand why some previous attempts (like moving `is_bare_repository_cfg`) were not completed.

The execution plan spans from code study through implementation, with scheduled periods for community feedback and a structured approach to evaluating which variables are suitable candidates for relocation criteria. The timeline accounts for iterative review cycles and documentation updates, concluding with a final report period. The applicant emphasizes full-time availability and commitment to continued involvement with the Git community post-internship.
