Build system policy discussion in ASan series.

This is a reply in the ongoing ASan fixes thread, specifically addressing build system synchronization between Makefile and Meson configurations. Jeff King responds to Taylor Blau's question about maintaining consistency between the two build systems.

Jeff expresses a preference for Meson-specific changes to be handled separately by those who care about Meson, either as independent patches or as squashed additions that maintainers can apply. He argues against requiring all contributors to maintain Meson compatibility, citing the extra burden on those not using Meson and potential for distraction from the main topic.

The response notably includes Jeff's personal perspective on Meson's impact on the project, characterizing it as "mostly a hassle" from his viewpoint as someone who doesn't use it. This provides context for his position on the synchronization question while acknowledging the pragmatic approach taken by Patrick Steinhardt in this series.

The exchange doesn't propose any concrete policy changes but clarifies Jeff's stance on how Meson maintenance should be handled going forward, with implications for future contributions that touch both build systems. The thread's technical focus on ASan fixes remains unaffected by this tangential discussion.
