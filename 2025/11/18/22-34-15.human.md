The change is mechanical but requires numerous casts at call sites where the pointer is passed to functions expecting `char*`. The commit notes several places where casting was unnecessary but left unchanged to avoid a larger refactoring. This follows the series' established pattern of methodical updates while maintaining backward compatibility.

As with previous patches in the series, this change builds on the type mapping standards established earlier, applying the principle that byte buffers should use unsigned types. The thread context shows this work has already gone through extensive review, with maintainer approval of the overall approach. This particular change appears uncontroversial - it's a straightforward application of the type guidelines documented in earlier patches.
