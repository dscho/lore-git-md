This is the final approval for the `git-repo-info` feature series, marking the completion of Lucas Seiki Oshiro's GSoC project under mentorship from Karthik Nayak and Patrick Steinhardt. Junio C Hamano gives the green light to merge the v5 series after all review feedback has been addressed in the two patches.

The series introduces a new experimental `git-repo-info` command with two key components: first, a refactoring to optimize field printing by writing directly to stdout, and second, the implementation of an `--all` flag to show all repository information fields. The v5 iteration made final adjustments to function signatures and loop variable types as requested in previous reviews.

With Junio's approval ("Looking good. Will replace.") and suggestion to mark the topic for 'next', this experimental feature is now ready to be included in Git's development branch. The series has gone through multiple rounds of review and refinement, with all technical concerns resolved and comprehensive test coverage in place. While future enhancements like a `--keys` option have been suggested, these are noted as non-blocking improvements that could be added later.
