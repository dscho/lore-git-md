This is a follow-up discussion on Patch 2/9 in Jeff King's ASan fixes series, focusing on name-hash lookups in incremental bitmaps. The exchange with Taylor Blau confirms the correctness of the lexical order handling for name-hash lookups, which was initially unclear in the implementation. Jeff acknowledges Taylor's validation of the approach and adds an assertion for additional safety in v2, though both agree this is more defensive than strictly necessary.

The discussion reveals some subtle points about the bitmap implementation: the name-hash array follows lexical OID order rather than pack order, and there's existing validation of hash-cache sizes during bitmap opening that makes additional bounds checking redundant. While Taylor suggests some potential improvements (like bounds checking the hash offset), Jeff considers these non-essential for the immediate ASan fix.

This patch remains focused on memory safety while clarifying some bitmap internals. The v2 will include the added assertion but otherwise maintains the current approach, having received positive technical review from Taylor and Patrick Steinhardt earlier in the thread. The discussion also surfaces some ongoing questions about error handling philosophy (BUG() vs die()) that may warrant future standardization.
