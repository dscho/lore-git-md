This is a follow-up discussion on the refactoring patch that merges `xmkstemp()` into `xmkstemp_mode()`. Jeff King (peff) responds to Junio Hamano's approval of the patch with some additional observations.

The email confirms that the original `strlen()` comparison in `xmkstemp()` was a workaround for certain `mkstemp()` implementations that could truncate paths in problematic ways, but notes this isn't a concern with Git's own `git_mkstemp_mode()`. Peff also points out that after this change, there's only one remaining direct `mkstemp()` call in the codebase (in `entry.c`), suggesting it might be worth converting that to use Git's wrapper as well, though he explicitly marks this as out of scope for the current patch.

The discussion doesn't raise any objections to the patch itself, which has already been accepted, but provides useful historical context about the original implementation and flags a potential future cleanup opportunity. The tone is collegial, with Peff clearly identifying his additional thoughts as tangential to the already-approved change.
