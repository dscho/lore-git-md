This is the maintainer's approval of Ezekiel Newren's xdiff refactoring series, marking the completion of a major modernization effort for Rust FFI compatibility. Junio Hamano gives the green light to merge the final version into the 'next' branch after five iterations of review and refinement.

The series systematically updates core xdiff data structures and functions to use unambiguous, platform-independent types while maintaining backward compatibility. Key changes include type standardization (converting `long` to `size_t` and `char*` to `uint8_t*`), splitting a dual-purpose hash field into semantically distinct components, and renaming the obscure `rindex` to the clearer `reference_index`. 

The work establishes foundational documentation standard (`unambiguous-types.adoc`) for future FFI efforts and has benefited from extensive review by standards and compiler experts. While the changes increase memory usage by 33% per record, this was deemed acceptable given the benefits of type safety and cross-language compatibility. The series now stands ready to serve as a base for Rust integration work.
