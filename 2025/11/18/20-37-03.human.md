This is the first of two patches in the final v5 series implementing the experimental `git-repo-info` command, part of a GSoC project. The patch refactors the field printing logic into a dedicated `print_field()` function that can be reused by other parts of the code.

The key changes are:
1. Extracting the field printing logic from `print_fields()` into a new standalone `print_field()` function
2. Optimizing the output by having `quote_c_style()` write directly to stdout rather than using an intermediate strbuf
3. Removing the now-unnecessary `quotbuf` strbuf

The refactoring appears clean and more efficient, eliminating buffer copying while maintaining the same output behavior. This is a well-contained change that follows through on earlier review feedback to optimize the implementation. The patch is part of a mature series that has gone through multiple rounds of review and is now in its final form.
