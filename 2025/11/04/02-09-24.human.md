This is a refactoring patch in Junio Hamano's series to improve handling of incomplete lines (missing newlines at end of file) in Git's diff and apply machinery. 

The patch revamps how `git apply` parses patches containing incomplete lines (marked by "\ No newline at end of file"). Currently, the parser processes these markers independently without tracking which line they apply to, which would prevent proper whitespace checking. The change makes the parser properly associate incomplete-line markers with their corresponding content lines by dropping the newline during parsing, matching how the application side already handles these cases.

The implementation introduces a new `adjust_incomplete()` helper that detects and measures incomplete-line markers, then modifies the parsing loop to properly account for them. This maintains existing behavior while preparing for future whitespace checking of incomplete lines. The changes are surgical and well-explained, continuing the series' pattern of careful preparatory refactoring before introducing new functionality.
