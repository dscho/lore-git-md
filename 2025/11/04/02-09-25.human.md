This is a preparatory refactoring patch in Junio Hamano's series introducing "incomplete-line" whitespace detection. It reallocates bit positions in the diff flags word to make room for future whitespace rules, shifting existing flags to higher bit positions while maintaining their relative ordering.

The patch moves several flag groups in the diff subsystem: whitespace rules expand from bits 0-11 to 0-15, the WSEH_* highlighting flags move from 12-14 to 16-18, the blank-at-EOF marker shifts from bit 16 to 19, and color-moved flags move from 17-19 to 20-22. The WS_RULE_MASK constant in ws.h is similarly updated to cover the expanded range.

As a pure refactoring change, this doesn't modify any behavior - it just creates space for the upcoming WS_INCOMPLETE_LINE feature (bit 0x8000) that will be introduced later in the series. The changes are mechanical and well-contained, touching only three header files with straightforward bit position updates. This follows the series' approach of carefully preparing the infrastructure before adding new functionality.
