This is a follow-up in the thread about `git grep -l` output formatting issues with filenames containing dashes. Kristoffer Haugsbakk reports seeing similar behavior when using the Delta pager, where hyphens in filenames get replaced with colons, though they note the coloring issue might be separate. 

The email suggests the pager (Delta) could be involved in at least part of the problem, though the original reporter saw the issue even without a pager. This adds a new data point for debugging - the behavior might manifest differently depending on whether a pager is used and which one. The core issue remains that something in Git's output pipeline is incorrectly transforming dashes to colons in certain filename display scenarios.

The thread continues to investigate a regression where `git grep -l` misformats filenames with dashes, now with potential pager involvement as another factor to consider. No solution has been proposed yet, but the discussion is narrowing down the possible causes.
