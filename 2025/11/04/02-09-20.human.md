This is a bugfix in Junio Hamano's series adding "incomplete-line" whitespace detection. The patch corrects how line numbers are counted when processing the "\ No newline at end of file" marker in diffs.

Currently, the code always increments the preimage line counter when seeing this marker, regardless of whether it follows a deleted, added, or unchanged line. The patch introduces a `last_line_kind` field to track what type of line preceded the marker, allowing proper incrementing of both preimage and postimage counters as needed.

While the author notes this doesn't affect functionality (since these counters are only for blank-at-EOF warnings), the change makes the behavior more logically correct. It also lays groundwork for the series' broader goal of better tracking incomplete line states before and after changes.

The implementation adds a BUG() check for invalid states, continuing the series' pattern of defensive programming. The change is focused and well-explained, with the commit message clearly detailing both the immediate fix and its relevance to the larger feature.
