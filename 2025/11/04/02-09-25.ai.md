This is a preparatory refactoring patch in Junio Hamano's series introducing "incomplete-line" whitespace error detection. It reallocates bit positions in the diff flags word to accommodate future whitespace rules, specifically making room for the upcoming WS_INCOMPLETE_LINE bit (0x8000) that will be introduced later in the series.

The patch shifts several bitmask definitions in diff.c to higher positions: whitespace rules now occupy bits 0-15 (up from 0-11), WSEH flags move to 12-14 to 16-18, blank-at-EOF moves from 16 to 19, and color-moved flags shift from 17-19 to 20-22. Corresponding updates are made in diff.h and ws.h to maintain consistency, with WS_RULE_MASK expanded to cover 16 bits.

This is purely an infrastructure change with no behavioral impact, maintaining all existing functionality while creating space for the new incomplete-line detection feature. The changes are mechanical but carefully preserve the relationships between the various bitmask constants. The patch follows the series' pattern of cleaning up and preparing the codebase before introducing new functionality.
