Refactoring patch in the "incomplete-line" whitespace series, standardizing variable naming in the diff subsystem.

This is the sixth patch in Junio Hamano's 12-part series introducing new whitespace handling for incomplete lines (missing newlines at end of file). The change renames the `ecb` parameter to `ecbdata` in `emit_rewrite_lines()` to match the naming convention used throughout the rest of the diff code. This is a straightforward consistency improvement with no behavior changes.

The patch touches the diff.c file's rewrite diff handling, specifically the line emission logic that tracks line numbers across preimage and postimage. The renamed parameter is used for line counting (`lno_in_preimage`/`lno_in_postimage`) and passing to line emission functions (`emit_del_line`, `emit_add_line`). The change also updates the parameter name in the call to `emit_diff_symbol` for the NO_LF_EOF case.

This continues the series' pattern of preparatory cleanups before introducing the new WS_INCOMPLETE_LINE functionality. Like earlier patches in the series, it demonstrates careful attention to code consistency and maintainability. The change is small (6 lines changed) and focused, with no apparent risk of introducing regressions since it only affects variable naming.
