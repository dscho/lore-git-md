This thread began as an investigation into why `git grep -l` was incorrectly displaying filenames containing dashes as colons with unusual coloring. After thorough analysis by multiple participants, the consensus has shifted to identify the Delta pager as the likely root cause, with its known issue #1259 exhibiting this exact dash-to-colon transformation behavior.

Key findings show the problem only occurs when using Delta - it disappears with `--no-pager` and doesn't manifest in environments without Delta installed. Multiple contributors, including the original reporter Michael Roach and Kristoffer Haugsbakk, have independently reproduced this behavior patterns. While the primary issue lies with Delta, there remains some curiosity about whether changes in Git's pager handling between versions 2.49.1 and 2.51.1 might have exposed this Delta bug, though this appears secondary.

The discussion has taken on a collaborative tone, with participants working together to isolate the issue and the original reporter acknowledging the pager connection. Suggested next steps include testing with alternative pagers like `less` or `cat` to further confirm the behavior, though these results haven't been reported yet. The thread demonstrates how community investigation can efficiently move from initial bug report to likely root cause identification.
