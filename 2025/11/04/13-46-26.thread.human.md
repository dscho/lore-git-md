Queen Ediri Jessa has proposed an Outreachy internship project to continue Git's ongoing effort to reduce global state usage in the codebase, particularly building on René Scharfe's work to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. The proposal outlines a 12-week plan to systematically identify and refactor global variables while maintaining test coverage through small, reviewable patches. While the proposal shows good understanding of Git's development process, it remains at the discussion stage without yet having a confirmed mentor or specific target subsystems identified.

The idea has received initial support from Usman Akinyemi, who responded with a brief "Acked" indicating approval, though without further technical discussion. The proposal still needs mentor sponsorship and project approval before moving forward, and would benefit from input from established contributors working on related `the_repository` removal and ODB abstraction efforts. The structured approach aligns well with Git's mailing-list based workflow and resembles successful prior internship projects under Christian Couder's mentorship.
