This is part 3 of Ezekiel Newren's ongoing xdiff refactoring series to improve type safety for Rust FFI compatibility. The patch changes the `xrecord_t.ptr` field from `char*` to `uint8_t*` to better reflect its role as a byte pointer rather than a character string.

The change is mechanical but requires explicit casting at interface boundaries where the pointer is passed to functions expecting `char*`. The author notes several places where casting was unnecessary and could be removed, though most call sites retain their casts for now to avoid a larger refactoring avalanche. One exception is `get_indent()` where the local variable was updated to match the new type.

This continues the series' pattern of incremental type safety improvements while maintaining backward compatibility. The change is straightforward and well-contained, touching only the xdiff subsystem with no expected behavior changes. It builds on previous patches in the series that established the type safety patterns and documentation.
