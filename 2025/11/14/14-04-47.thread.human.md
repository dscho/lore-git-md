Kristoffer Haugsbakk's documentation patch standardizing cross-references between `git commit --dry-run` format options and their `git-status` counterparts has been queued in 'next' by Junio. The patch addresses historical inconsistencies in how these related commands were documented, particularly adding proper git-status(1) links for the `--branch`, `--long`, and `--null` options.

Jeff King, who authored one of the original commits that introduced the inconsistency in 2012 (f3f47a1e), responded positively to the changes. He jokingly attributed his earlier omission of proper cross-references to youthful enthusiasm compared to the more thorough 2009 commit (7c9f7038) that did include them. The discussion confirms this is purely a documentation improvement that better reflects the shared internals between `git commit --dry-run` and `git status` without changing any behavior. With Junio having already picked up the patch and Jeff's approval, this appears ready to proceed toward the next release.
