2. **Security Considerations**: The patch includes credentials in plaintext within the state buffer. Nakamaru confirms this doesn't introduce new security risks beyond what's already present in the credential helper protocol, which inherently handles credentials in plaintext during store operations.

The exchange shows the patch is technically sound but undergoing Git's characteristic thorough review process for implementation details.
