This is a follow-up discussion in the ongoing xdiff refactoring series aimed at making the codebase more Rust FFI friendly. Ezekiel Newren responds to Junio Hamano's feedback about the current patch series (part 2 of the cleanup) and outlines future plans.

Junio had noted that while the series successfully updates core data structures (xrecord_t and xdfile_t) with proper types, it leaves function parameters and local variables unchanged, requiring temporary casts. He suggests this could be addressed in a future "part 3" series.

Ezekiel acknowledges this is intentional - the current 10-patch series was already substantial, and further type unification will come in subsequent cleanup efforts. He emphasizes this is just one of many planned series to modernize xdiff's tangled codebase, with the ultimate goal of making future maintenance easier. The exchange confirms the current work is mergable as-is while establishing a clear roadmap for completing the type consistency work.
