Jean-Noël Avila has completed a documentation standardization effort for the `git fetch`, `git pull`, and `git push` commands, converting their man pages to the project's consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style. The three-patch series makes purely presentational improvements to enable better automated rendering while preserving all functional content. 

The final patch in the series, now at version 2, standardizes the `git push` documentation and its related config files, following the same pattern as the earlier `fetch` and `pull` conversions. Changes include replacing angle brackets with underscored placeholders, wrapping options in backticks, and reformatting bullet lists - totaling nearly 400 lines changed across two files. A minor typo (an extraneous underscore) was fixed in v2 after careful review.

This work completes the standardization of these three interrelated transport commands' documentation, bringing them in line with the project's multi-year effort to unify all man page formatting. The lack of discussion during review suggests the changes are uncontroversial and align with established documentation practices. With all three commands now converted and polished, the series appears ready for merging as part of the ongoing documentation modernization.
