The email from brian m. carlson responds to Junio C Hamano's observation that Windows/Meson CI failures occur when the interoperability series is present in the 'seen' branch, even with an empty test commit (672cb7c62e). The failures don't appear when testing the base commit (950efaac03) without the series.

Key points in this update:
- The author confirms the Windows/Meson failures predate their changes, suggesting an underlying issue rather than a problem introduced by the Rust components
- They suspect the build.rs file might be involved but lack Windows systems to investigate
- The thread is waiting for Windows expertise (potentially from Patrick Steinhardt) to diagnose the root cause

This represents the final integration hurdle for the otherwise complete 15-patch series.
