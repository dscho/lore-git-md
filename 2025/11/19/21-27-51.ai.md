This is a bugfix patch in Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing ODB refactoring series, specifically fixing `index-pack --fsck-objects` behavior outside repositories. The patch addresses a segfault that could occur when deferred fsck checks were attempted without a repository context.

The key changes are:
1. Adds a new `fsck_has_queued_checks()` helper that detects pending gitmodules/gitattributes validations by comparing `_found` and `_done` oidsets
2. Makes `index-pack` fail gracefully with a descriptive error when queued checks can't be completed outside a repo
3. Maintains the ability to validate self-contained pack streams (where blobs follow their referring trees) without a repo

Junio Hamano confirms the approach is reasonable, noting it handles the common case while properly failing for incomplete pack streams. The solution is technically sound within the series' ODB abstraction goals, though Junio observes it's a "best-effort" validation that might not catch all malformed packs from non-Git implementations. The patch builds on the new `oidset_equal()` function from earlier in the series (6/13) and fits cleanly with the overall architectural direction already approved by the maintainer.
