This is a final review discussion for patch 3/3 in Karthik Nayak's series fixing batched reference update regressions in `git fetch`. The patch ensures critical post-fetch operations (FETCH_HEAD updates, upstream tracking, remote HEAD setting) persist through batched update failures unless `--atomic` is specified.

Junio Hamano responds to Eric Sunshine's review of the test cases, focusing on two specific test improvements:
