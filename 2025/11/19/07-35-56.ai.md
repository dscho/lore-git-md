Final review approval for git-repo-info feature. Eric Sunshine confirms all his review comments have been addressed in the v5 patch series implementing the experimental `git-repo-info --all` functionality. While Eric had previously noted control flow symmetry observations, he now confirms these are non-blocking stylistic preferences rather than functional issues.

With both Junio's technical approval and Eric's confirmation that all review feedback has been addressed, the path is clear for merging this experimental feature into the 'next' branch.
