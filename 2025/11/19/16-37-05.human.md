Bug report follow-up regarding test failures in t8020.16 and t8020.19. Randall Becker confirms the issue is consistently reproducible on NonStop with Git 2.52.0, though they suspect it may not be platform-specific. The tests involve `git last-modified` incorrectly outputting commit hashes instead of expected tag names (m1.t, m2.t) during merge operations.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk had pointed to a related report that may provide additional context. Becker acknowledges this with a brief note, suggesting the connection is valid but not expanding further. The exchange indicates this is likely a genuine bug rather than a platform-specific quirk, though the exact cause remains unclear. The fact that it appeared between rc0 and final release suggests it might be related to changes in that window, though no specific culprit is identified yet.
