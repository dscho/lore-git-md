Bugfix report for test failures in `t8020.16` and `t8020.19` on NonStop platform.

Randall Becker reports two test failures that appeared in Git 2.52.0's test suite on NonStop systems. The failures are in the `t8020` test script, specifically tests 16 ("cross merge boundaries in blaming") and 19 ("last-modified merge undoes changes"). Both tests involve Git's `last-modified` functionality during merge operations.

The first failure shows that after merging two branches (m1 and m2), the `last-modified` output incorrectly shows the merge commit hash for m1.t rather than the expected tag name. The second failure similarly shows merge commit hashes appearing where tag names (b5 and b2) were expected in the `last-modified` output after a complex merge scenario.

The reporter notes these failures are 100% reproducible on NonStop, appeared in the final release but not in rc0, and occur with OpenSSL 3.4 (though they believe this is unrelated). The detailed test output shows the exact point of failure in each case through the `test_cmp` diff output.

This appears to be a genuine bug in Git's `last-modified` functionality during merges, though it's unclear yet whether it's specific to NonStop or affects other platforms. The reporter has provided clear reproduction steps and evidence of the issue.
