Feature addition to `git replay` command, refining the proposed revert capability based on feedback. Siddharth Asthana responds to Elijah Newren and Phillip Wood's critiques about the initial design, agreeing that `--revert` should be a distinct operation mode rather than a flag that combines with `--onto` or `--advance`.

The key design change is to restructure the command to require exactly one of three mutually exclusive modes: `--onto` for rebasing, `--advance` for cherry-picking, or `--revert` for reversing commits. This addresses the semantic confusion of combining directional terms like "advance" with "revert". The `--contained` option will now be restricted to only work with `--onto`.

Asthana acknowledges the utility of both commit-by-commit reverts (for traceability) and single-bulk reverts (via `merge-tree`), suggesting the documentation will cover both approaches. The response shows good engagement with the feedback, with clear plans to restructure the implementation while maintaining the core functionality needed by GitLab's Gitaly service. The discussion appears to be converging on a cleaner design after working through the initial awkwardness of the flag combinations.
