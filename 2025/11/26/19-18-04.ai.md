This is a bug report update regarding platform-specific test failures in `git last-modified` during merge operations, specifically on NonStop systems.

Randall Becker provides additional technical analysis of the failing tests (t8020.16 and t8020.19) where `git last-modified` incorrectly shows commit hashes instead of expected tag names (m1.t, m2.t). Having investigated Jeff King's earlier suggestion about problematic casts without finding issues, Randall now focuses on potential platform differences in bitwise operations between enums and integers, particularly around the `BOUNDARY` flag handling on big-endian systems.

The email notes this appears to be a regression in Git 2.52.0 (not present in rc0) and raises packaging concerns as Randall's team considers whether to ship this version on NonStop platforms (both ia64 and x86). While the build system's `SANITIZE=address,undefined` flag issues remain separate, the core problem appears related to how NonStop's compiler handles enum vs. int operations, possibly with sign extension or bitfield behavior differences.

Randall requests specific debugging guidance for `builtin/last-modified.c` to help verify the behavior, as the team struggles to fully understand the code's expected vs. actual behavior. The issue's platform-specific nature makes it particularly challenging to diagnose without deeper knowledge of both Git's merge boundary handling and NonStop's compiler characteristics. The email maintains focus on the original test failures while expanding the investigation to include potential compiler/architecture interactions with Git's flag handling.
