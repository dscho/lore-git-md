Bugfix for `git replay` signature handling. Elijah Newren acknowledges Phillip Wood's patch that fixes an oversight where `git replay` wasn't properly excluding SHA-256 GPG signature headers when copying commit metadata. The patch adds "gpgsig-sha256" to the exclusion list in builtin/replay.c to match the behavior of `git commit --amend`. 

This follows Junio Hamano's earlier decision that the bugfix should take precedence despite conflicts with ongoing development work in Patrick Steinhardt's ps/history branch. The change is minimal (a one-line addition) but important for maintaining signature validity during replay operations.

While the immediate issue is resolved, Phillip Wood had noted this duplicates similar logic found in two other files (builtin/commit.c and sequencer.c), suggesting a potential future refactoring opportunity to centralize the signature exclusion handling. That architectural question remains open but separate from this straightforward bugfix.
