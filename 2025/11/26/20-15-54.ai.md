Performance optimization follow-up for patience diff LCS calculation. Yee Cheng Chin acknowledges Junio Hamano's review approval of their earlier patch that optimized the `find_longest_common_sequence()` function in `xdiff/xpatience.c`. The author notes their surprise at how effective the simple boundary condition check was at avoiding the binary search path in most cases.

Chin plans to send a v2 of the patch solely to fix a typo in the commit message where "PyTorch" was misspelled. No functional changes to the code are planned, as the optimization has already been validated through benchmarks showing 8-14% speedups in `git log --patience` operations across multiple codebases. The core optimization remains the same - bypassing binary searches when lines appear after the current longest subsequence, which profiling showed handled 50-60% of cases.
