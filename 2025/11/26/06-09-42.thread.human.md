This thread has followed the development of the experimental `git-last-modified` command through multiple iterations, culminating in its approval for wider testing. The command, which shows when files were last modified in a repository, began with basic functionality and evolved through community feedback to refine its depth control behavior and documentation.

Toon Claes led the development through three versions, with significant input from Junio C Hamano and other reviewers. The most notable change in the final version was making the default behavior non-recursive (only showing modifications for explicitly specified paths) while maintaining backward compatibility through a new `-r/--recursive` option. The implementation was simplified by unifying control through a single `max_depth` parameter, and documentation was standardized throughout.

Junio has now approved the series for merging into the `next` integration branch, indicating it has addressed all substantive feedback.
