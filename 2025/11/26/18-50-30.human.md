Performance optimization for the patience diff algorithm. This is a follow-up to Yee Cheng Chin's patch that reduces unnecessary binary searches in the patience diff's LCS calculation. Junio C Hamano reviews the change, calling it a "stupid and obvious optimization that is quite clever" - high praise from the Git maintainer.

The optimization works by checking if a unique line appears after the current longest subsequence before performing a binary search. In this common case (which profiling showed occurs 98.5-99.99% of the time), it can simply append the line rather than searching for its position. The change preserves the fallback to binary search when needed, maintaining correctness while improving performance.

Benchmarks from the original patch showed 8-14% speedups in `git log --patience` operations across several large codebases. The change is minimal - just 4 lines of boundary checking logic added to `xdiff/xpatience.c`. Given Junio's positive reaction and the demonstrated performance benefits, this seems likely to be accepted into the codebase.
