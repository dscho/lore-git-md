Bugfix for the `git replay` command regarding signature headers. Phillip Wood points out that when replaying signed commits, the command currently copies the "gpgsig-sha256" header from the original commit even though this signature won't be valid for the replayed commit. The patch adds this header to the exclusion list, matching the behavior of `git commit --amend` and fixing an oversight where only the "gpgsig" header was being excluded.

The change is small (just one line modifying an array of excluded headers) but addresses a clear inconsistency in how signature headers are handled during commit replay. Phillip notes there are now three copies of this exclusion list in the codebase (in commit.c, replay.c, and sequencer.c) and suggests this might benefit from centralization in the future.

The patch is based on maint for potential backporting, though this creates a conflict with an active topic branch that moves this code. Phillip offers to rebase if the backport isn't deemed worthwhile. The change appears straightforward and well-justified, fixing what looks like an unintentional omission in the original implementation.
