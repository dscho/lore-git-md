Optimization for patience diff's LCS calculation in xdiff/xpatience.c. The patch reduces unnecessary binary searches in `find_longest_common_sequence()` by directly appending unique lines that appear after the current longest subsequence, avoiding searches in common cases. Profiling showed binary searches accounted for 50-60% of runtime. Benchmarks across multiple repositories (Vim, PyTorch, CPython, Git) demonstrate 8-14% speedups in `git log --patience` operations, with the binary search path being rarely taken (0.01-1.46% of cases). The change adds a 4-line boundary check while maintaining fallback to binary search when needed, preserving output correctness. Performance measurements from M1 Max system show consistent real-world improvements.
