This is a bug report investigating platform-specific test failures in Git 2.52.0 on NonStop systems. Randall Becker continues investigating why `git last-modified` fails tests t8020.16 and t8020.19 by showing commit hashes instead of expected tag names (m1.t, m2.t) during merge operations.

The discussion has narrowed to potential platform differences in how NonStop handles bitwise operations between enums and integers, particularly around the `BOUNDARY` flag checks. Randall notes this could relate to big-endian behavior or sign extension issues, though he hasn't found problematic casts yet. He's requesting help identifying specific lines in `builtin/last-modified.c` to examine with gdb, as his team considers whether to hold the NonStop release due to these test failures.

The thread previously established that the merge operation itself produces correct topology (per Junio), with the issue lying in name resolution. Randall's new angle examines whether platform-specific compiler behavior in flag handling could explain why NonStop shows different behavior than other platforms. The investigation remains active, with packaging decisions for Git 2.52.0 on NonStop hanging in the balance.
