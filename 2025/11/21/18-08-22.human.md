This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt's object database streaming interface series (specifically patch 2/19). Junio Hamano is reviewing the change that removes the deferred `open()` callback from the streaming interface.

The patch simplifies the stream initialization flow by making stream opening immediate rather than setting up a callback to be invoked later. Junio notes some confusion with the commit message's explanation but agrees with the technical direction, seeing it as a reasonable simplification. He questions whether some parts of the diff are just noise (like moving the `ret` variable declaration).

This change is part of a larger effort to clean up and rationalize Git's object streaming interface, with the immediate goal being to prepare for allocating stream structures in source-specific logic. The review suggests the change is conceptually sound but could benefit from clearer explanation of what exactly is being simplified and why the previous approach was problematic.
