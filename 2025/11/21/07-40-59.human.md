This is part of Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing series to refactor Git's object database streaming interface, specifically targeting the removal of `the_repository` global usage. Patch 14/19 makes the `odb_read_stream` structure definition public by moving it from streaming.c to streaming.h.

The change prepares for upcoming work where backend subsystems will take responsibility for allocating object read streams. By exposing the stream structure, backends will be able to properly initialize streams themselves. The patch maintains all existing functionality while making the internal structure available to other parts of the codebase.

The diff shows the straightforward movement of the structure definition along with its associated function pointer types. The commit follows the series pattern of incremental, focused changes that move toward the goal of better backend isolation. This appears to be a well-justified preparatory step in the larger refactoring effort.
