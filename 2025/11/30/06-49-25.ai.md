This is the final patch in Johannes Schindelin's 10-part series improving Windows test suite compatibility with symlinks, specifically addressing MSYS2's planned symlink support enablement.

Junio Hamano reviews and approves the 10th patch which fixes path handling in t7800-difftool.sh tests on Windows. The issue stems from MSYS2's path conversion where absolute paths begin with drive letters (e.g., "C:") rather than forward slashes. The patch cleverly modifies the test to match paths by looking for trailing colons in `ls` output rather than relying on Unix-style path prefixes.

Junio acknowledges the challenge of maintaining cross-platform test compatibility, noting that Linux/macOS developers naturally write tests expecting Unix behavior. He suggests this could be addressed more proactively rather than reactively fixing breakages. Despite this broader concern, he praises the specific solution as "clever and clean" and indicates he will queue the entire series, which he finds "quite sensibly reasoned."

The patch completes the series' goal of preparing Git's test suite for Windows environments with proper symlink support, particularly for MSYS2's upcoming changes. With Junio's approval of this final patch, the series appears ready for integration, having addressed Windows-specific test failures across multiple subsystems while maintaining behavior on other platforms.
