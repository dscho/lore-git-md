This is a follow-up discussion in the `git clone` config parsing regression thread, where Jeff King (peff) and Junio C Hamano are finalizing their position on whether to fix the whitespace-trimming behavior.

- Git LFS is confirmed to be affected by this specific case
- No appetite for a deprecation period or complex solution
- The alternative would be Junio's simple "trim" patch (referenced from earlier in thread)

The discussion appears to be winding down with both maintainers leaning toward accepting the regression as an intentional correction, despite some known impact on third-party tools.
