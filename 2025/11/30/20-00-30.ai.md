Bugfix discussion follow-up regarding `git last-modified` sparse checkout behavior.

Derrick Stolee responds to Johannes Schindelin's patch fixing `git last-modified`'s handling of paths outside sparse checkouts when using the `--` separator. While acknowledging the fix (adding `PARSE_OPT_KEEP_DASHDASH` to parse options), Stolee raises two important questions about the expected behavior boundaries.

First, he expresses surprise that such a minimal change (just one flag) was sufficient to fix the issue, implying the solution might be simpler than expected. Second, he probes the intended behavior by asking whether the command should work without the `--` separator for paths that exist at HEAD but are outside the sparse checkout - this suggests the current implementation might be too permissive or not properly validate paths against sparse checkout rules.

The questions indicate potential areas for further clarification or testing, though the original fix itself appears correct for the documented `--` separator case. The discussion is now shifting from implementation to behavior specification, particularly around sparse checkout edge cases.
