This is a feature patch continuing the discussion about Lisp dialect support in Git's userdiff driver. Scott L. Burson responds to D. Ben Knoble's concerns about the unified "lisp" driver approach, clarifying that the proposal combines rather than replaces existing Scheme patterns. The key technical clarification is that patterns are being added disjunctively (OR'd together) to avoid false negatives while maintaining all current Scheme matching behavior.

The email addresses two specific concerns raised: pattern matching for deeply nested forms (explaining the disjunctive approach handles this) and tab handling in indentation (clarifying the intent was only to match 1-2 spaces before "(def" patterns). Scott reports gathering community input from r/scheme (with 4300 views) that surfaced similar concerns already addressed by the design, though remains open to additional feedback channels if needed.

The exchange reveals an implementation detail important for future thread context: the driver will use combined patterns (existing Scheme + new Lisp) rather than replacement, with specific whitespace matching constraints. Scott indicates an updated patch is forthcoming, suggesting the technical discussion may be converging toward a solution that accommodates both dialects' syntax patterns while preserving backward compatibility.
