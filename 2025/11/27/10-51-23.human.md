Procedural note about patch series transmission issue. Johannes Sixt responds to Scott L. Burson's concern that only the first patch of their v2 series was sent to the mailing list. Sixt explains that GitGitGadget behaved as expected by sending both patches with a cover letter, since the first patch hasn't been integrated upstream yet. He notes that if Burson intended to send just the second patch as a fixup, that workflow isn't supported by GitGitGadget, and suggests (without elaborating here) that fixup commits may not be the right approach for this situation.

This is purely about the mechanics of patch submission - the technical content of the Lisp userdiff driver series itself is complete and was previously accepted in the thread. The exchange serves to clarify the patch submission process rather than discuss the feature implementation.
