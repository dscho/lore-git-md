This is a feature patch in the ongoing discussion about improving Lisp dialect support in Git's diff highlighting. Scott L. Burson responds to D. Ben Knoble's concerns about the proposed unified "lisp" userdiff driver, clarifying that the approach would combine rather than replace existing Scheme patterns.

The key clarification is that the new Lisp patterns would be added alongside current Scheme patterns in a logical OR configuration, ensuring no existing matches are lost. Scott addresses two specific concerns: the handling of indentation (explaining the intent was only to match 1-2 spaces before "(def" forms, not arbitrary whitespace) and community input (noting his Reddit post received substantial engagement with similar feedback that the combined approach would address).

Scott indicates he has an updated patch ready incorporating these clarifications, suggesting the discussion may be nearing resolution. The exchange shows the careful balancing act between supporting multiple Lisp dialects while maintaining backward compatibility with existing Scheme highlighting.
