More significantly, he probes whether the command should also work *without* the `--` separator for paths that exist at HEAD but aren't checked out - indicating the current implementation might not properly validate paths against sparse checkout rules. 

While the original fix appears correct for the documented `--` separator case, Stolee's questions shift the discussion toward clarifying the expected behavior in edge cases, particularly around sparse checkout interactions.
