Bugfix for `git-last-modified` bitmap initialization, now marked for backport to maintenance releases. This is a follow-up to the earlier discussion about incorrect memory clearing in the command's scratch bitmap.

Junio C Hamano acknowledges Toon Claes' one-line fix that properly sizes the `memset()` call by multiplying the element count by `sizeof(eword_t)`. The issue, while more visible on s390x big-endian systems, was fundamentally about incorrect byte counting rather than endianness. Hamano notes this bug has existed since v2.52.0 and qualifies as maintenance-worthy material.

The patch itself is straightforward and already reviewed by Jeff King, with the thread showing consensus on both the immediate fix and its importance. No new technical discussion is introduced in this email - it primarily serves as maintainer approval and backport designation for what appears to be a clear-cut correction of a long-standing (though likely low-impact) bug.
