This is a documentation refinement in response to Kristoffer Haugsbakk's earlier patch about Git's advice messages. Junio Hamano suggests strengthening the justification for preferring `git help` over `man` in error messages, noting two key reasons: some platforms may lack `man` entirely, and some users may prefer HTML output (available via `git help -w`).

The proposed wording change is minimal - just a few additional words in the advice text to better capture these motivations. This builds on the original patch's goal of making Git's documentation references more robust and user-friendly while maintaining the same technical approach of simple message adjustments.

The exchange shows the maintainer's attention to clear, complete rationales even for small changes, and suggests this refinement will likely be incorporated into the accepted version of the patch.
