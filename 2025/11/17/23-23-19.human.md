This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about adding Lisp syntax support to Git's userdiff drivers. Scott L. Burson responds to Junio Hamano's suggestion of unifying the syntax patterns across Lisp dialects rather than creating a separate "lisp" driver.

Key points in this response:
1. Scott acknowledges a bug in his original regex (incorrect handling of tabs) and plans to fix it by using literal spaces and case-insensitive matching.
2. He agrees with Junio's point about the safety of "(def" patterns but emphasizes that the more important consideration is handling top-level forms starting with "(" in column 0.
3. While open to unification, Scott suggests that if a single driver is used, it might be better named "lisp" rather than "scheme" for discoverability by non-Scheme users.
4. He proposes adding driver aliasing as a potential solution that could satisfy both discoverability and unification goals.

The discussion remains technical and constructive, with Scott demonstrating thoughtful consideration of both the implementation details and community impact. The path forward now depends on feedback from the Scheme community (via Atharva Raykar) and whether Junio would accept a driver aliasing mechanism.
