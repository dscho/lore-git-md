This is a follow-up in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, addressing deployment concerns raised by Josh Steadmon from Google. Adrian Ratiu responds to two practical migration challenges for organizations with existing encoded submodule repositories: the inability to automatically enable the new `extensions.submoduleEncoding` config across many repositories, and the desire to set this as a default for new repositories.

Adrian proposes solving both issues with a build-time configuration option that would force-enable the extension, avoiding the need for downstream patches while still allowing organizations like Google to standardize the setting across their infrastructure. He also commits to adding more test coverage in v5 for backward compatibility scenarios where the extension is disabled after being enabled.

The exchange highlights how this technically sound solution now faces real-world adoption hurdles, with Adrian offering pragmatic solutions that maintain the series' architectural integrity while addressing large-scale deployment needs. The thread has evolved from technical implementation to operational considerations, with Google's experience providing valuable validation of both the approach and its migration challenges.
