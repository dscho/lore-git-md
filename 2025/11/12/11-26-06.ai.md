This is a review response in the ongoing ASan fixes series, focusing on Patch 4** which replaces `strtol()` with a custom `parse_long()` helper in cache-tree extension parsing.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges Jeff King's fix for avoiding `strtol()` on non-NUL-terminated buffers in cache-tree entries, agreeing it's good to fix the potential read overflow even if the security implications are limited. However, he raises concerns about the implementation's lack of error handling - specifically noting it doesn't detect numeric overflows and could silently accept malformed input if the resulting index entry appears valid. 

Steinhardt suggests the helper would be better modeled after `git_parse_int()` for consistency and future reuse, though he won't block the current implementation. This continues the thread's theme of debating when to create specialized helpers versus more general parsing utilities, previously seen in the discussion around Patch 8's timestamp parsing.

The review doesn't challenge the core safety improvement but flags maintainability concerns, suggesting this could be another candidate for the potential generalized parsing utility that's been discussed in the thread. The response leaves the decision to Jeff King while documenting the tradeoffs for future consideration.
