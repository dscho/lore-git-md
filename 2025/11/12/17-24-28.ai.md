This is a maintainer response in the ongoing `--committer` flag discussion thread. Junio Hamano weighs in on the technical merits of the proposed `--committer` option, building on Phillip Wood's earlier critique about its practical utility.

The key technical point is that `--committer` may be solving a non-problem: when amending commits to fix misconfigured user information, users must already use `--author` to correct the author field, and the committer information gets updated automatically during the amend operation. Hamano reinforces Phillip Wood's position that the asymmetry between author and committer fields makes `--author` more justified than `--committer`.

The discussion appears to be moving toward rejecting the feature based on insufficient motivation, with Hamano's response suggesting the maintainer perspective sees this as an unnecessary addition to the interface. This represents a potential roadblock for the patch series, as maintainer buy-in is crucial for inclusion. The thread shows the proposal facing skepticism about its core value proposition despite the technical implementation being sound.
