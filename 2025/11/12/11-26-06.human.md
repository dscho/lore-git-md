This is a review response in Jeff King's ASan fixes series, focusing on patch 4 which replaces `strtol()` with a custom parser in cache-tree extension handling.

Patrick Steinhardt agrees with the security analysis (that while the issue is low-risk, it's good to fix) but raises concerns about the implementation approach. He notes the new `parse_long()` helper lacks overflow detection and proper error handling, suggesting it could lead to undetected bad inputs if they happen to produce plausible index entries. He advocates for a more robust interface similar to `git_parse_int()` that would be easier to reuse across the codebase.

While not objecting to the current patch, Patrick flags this as a potential opportunity for a more general parsing utility that could prevent future ad-hoc implementations. The review maintains the technical discussion's constructive tone while clearly identifying what he sees as architectural tradeoffs. This positions the patch as a reasonable incremental improvement while leaving the door open for future refactoring.
