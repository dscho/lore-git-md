In a recent follow-up, brian m. carlson clarified that while their ongoing hash interoperability work does allow SHA256 repositories to fetch from SHA1 remotes, this currently comes with important limitations: it only works with full clones and doesn't support repositories containing submodules. These constraints help explain the boundaries of Marc Branchaud's proposed mirror workaround, while making clear that more complete interoperability remains a work in progress.
