This is a discussion about a proposed revert of a bugfix in the OSX keychain credential helper. The original commit (e1ab45b2dab) introduced an optimization to skip redundant credential storage operations, but it caused problems when invalid credentials from one helper were followed by valid ones from another (like git-credential-oauth), as the state tracking incorrectly blocked storage of valid credentials.

Junio C Hamano raises concerns about the timing and approach of this revert. While acknowledging the problem, he questions whether reverting now is the right move since: 1) the original commit isn't a recent regression (it appeared in v2.46), 2) there doesn't appear to be an active effort to develop a proper solution, and 3) reverting might introduce new problems for users who have adapted to the current behavior. He suggests that if this were part of a series that included a proper fix, the revert might make sense as a first step, but as a standalone change, it may just trade one set of problems for another.

The technical discussion centers around whether the state-tracking mechanism should be completely reverted (as proposed) or if a more sophisticated solution (like encoding full credential state) should be developed instead. The patch itself is simple - removing 11 lines of state-tracking code from the credential helper.
