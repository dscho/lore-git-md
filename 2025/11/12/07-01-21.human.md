This is a bugfix reverting a problematic change to the macOS keychain credential helper. The patch removes a recently added optimization that tried to skip unnecessary credential storage operations by tracking state with a "seen" flag.

The reverted commit (e1ab45b2) introduced a mechanism to mark when credentials were retrieved from the keychain, allowing subsequent "store" operations to be skipped. However, this caused issues when multiple credential helpers are chained - if the keychain returned invalid credentials that were later corrected by another helper (like git-credential-oauth), the keychain helper would incorrectly skip storing the valid credentials due to the lingering "seen" state.

The author acknowledges the optimization could potentially be salvaged with a more sophisticated state tracking mechanism, but opts for a straightforward revert as the immediate solution. The issue was reported by Petter Sælen, suggesting real-world impact. The change is cleanly implemented as a simple deletion of the state-tracking code.

This appears to be a well-justified revert addressing a concrete regression, though it does sacrifice a performance optimization. The commit message clearly explains the problem and rationale, making it easy to understand the change's purpose.
