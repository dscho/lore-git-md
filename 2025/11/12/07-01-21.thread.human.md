Here's a human-readable thread summary:

The thread addresses a bug in the OSX keychain credential helper where an optimization intended to skip redundant credential storage operations was causing valid credentials to be incorrectly ignored. The problematic commit (e1ab45b2dab) introduced a state-tracking mechanism that used a "seen" flag, but this broke cases where invalid credentials from one helper were followed by valid ones from another (like git-credential-oauth) - the flag would prevent storing the valid credentials.

Petter Sælen reported the issue, prompting a revert that completely removes the state-tracking code (11 lines from the OSX keychain helper). While a more sophisticated solution might be developed later (perhaps tracking full credential state rather than just a seen flag), the immediate fix is to revert the optimization entirely as the safest approach. The change is limited to the OSX keychain helper in contrib/ and resolves a real-world issue affecting credential helper chains.
