Bugfix reverting a problematic optimization in the OSX keychain credential helper.

This patch reverts commit e1ab45b2dab which attempted to optimize credential storage by skipping redundant `store` operations in the OSX keychain helper. The optimization used a `state[]=osxkeychain:seen=1` flag to track when credentials had already been seen, but this caused issues when invalid credentials were followed by valid ones from another helper (like git-credential-oauth). The state flag would incorrectly prevent storing the valid credentials.

The revert removes the state tracking mechanism entirely from the credential helper's C code, eliminating the `state_seen` variable and related logic. The commit message notes that a more sophisticated solution could be developed (like encoding full credential state rather than just a seen flag), but for now a full revert is the safest approach. The issue was reported by Petter Sælen and affects credential helper chains where multiple helpers may be involved.

The change affects only the OSX keychain credential helper (`contrib/credential/osxkeychain/`), removing 11 lines of state-tracking code. This appears to be a straightforward and justified revert of a problematic optimization that could cause credential storage failures in real-world usage scenarios.
