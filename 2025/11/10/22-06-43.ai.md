Both maintainers express skepticism about whether the `--committer` option is truly the best solution, despite acknowledging the symmetry argument with `--author`. Junio notes the fundamental asymmetry between author and committer roles in Git's model. The discussion remains open-ended, with both participants agreeing they're not strongly opposed but struggling to identify clear scenarios where the new flag would be the optimal choice over existing alternatives.
