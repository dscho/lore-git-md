This is the final edge case discussion in the submodule gitdir path encoding series. Aaron Schrab responds to Adrian Ratiu's proposed solutions for handling case-folding collisions when adding a new all-lowercase submodule that conflicts with an existing one.

Aaron points out that Adrian's current v4 approach (requiring manual `submodule.foo.gitdir` configuration) would impact not just users adding submodules, but also anyone pulling changes on case-folding filesystems - including automated environments where manual intervention would be problematic. He notes this could affect new clones too, depending on collation rules.

While Adrian suggested automatically deriving new paths (like "foo2" or "foo_") as an alternative, Aaron doesn't strongly prefer either option but leans slightly toward "foo2" for clarity, while acknowledging the scenario will likely be rare. The discussion represents the last open question in this otherwise architecturally-settled series that implements path encoding to prevent submodule gitdir filesystem conflicts.
