This is a follow-up discussion on patch 04/12 from Junio Hamano's merged series introducing incomplete-line whitespace handling. The exchange focuses on line counting semantics in the diff subsystem when processing "\ No newline at end of file" markers.

Phillip Wood questions why line numbers are being incremented at all for these markers, since they are annotations on the previous line rather than content lines themselves. Junio acknowledges there's no functional need for the counting (as the numbers aren't used after seeing the markers), but explains his change at least makes the unused counting more logically consistent than the previous version which unconditionally incremented the preimage counter.

The technical detail centers around the new `last_line_kind` field in `struct emit_callback` that tracks whether the preceding line was an addition ('+'), deletion ('-'), or context (' '), allowing proper incrementation of either preimage or postimage counters (or both for context lines). Junio notes this tracking will be independently useful for determining whether files end with incomplete lines before/after changes.

This is a post-merge refinement discussion about implementation details that doesn't affect the series' functionality. The exchange provides insight into the diff machinery's handling of line annotations while confirming the counting behavior is effectively dead code. Phillip's question highlights an area where the implementation could potentially be simplified, though Junio's response suggests the current approach may have future utility.
