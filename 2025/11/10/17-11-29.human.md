This is a follow-up in the submodule gitdir path encoding series, addressing the final edge case in case-folding collision detection. Adrian Ratiu responds to Aaron Schrab's concern about a scenario where a second submodule (with no uppercase characters to encode) would conflict with an existing case-variant submodule.

The current v4 implementation would require manual configuration (`submodule.foo.gitdir`) to resolve such conflicts. Adrian proposes two potential solutions: either document this as a known limitation requiring manual intervention, or implement automatic path derivation (suggesting variants like `foo2` or `foo_`) to avoid conflicts without user action. He seeks feedback on which approach would be preferable.

This represents the last open question in an otherwise complete and well-architected series that has achieved consensus among key contributors. The discussion shows careful attention to edge cases in the filesystem collision handling, with Adrian demonstrating responsiveness to community feedback. The solution here will likely be the final piece needed before the series can be merged.
