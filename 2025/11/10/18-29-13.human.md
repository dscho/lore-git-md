This is a follow-up discussion on patch 04/12 of Junio C Hamano's merged series introducing whitespace handling for incomplete lines (missing newlines at end of files). The patch originally fixed line number counting in the diff output when handling "\ No newline at end of file" markers.

Phillip Wood questions why line numbers need to be incremented at all for these markers, since they are annotations on the previous line rather than content lines themselves. Junio acknowledges there's no functional need - the counters aren't used after these markers - but defends the change as making the unused counters behave more coherently than the previous version which unconditionally incremented the preimage counter.

The exchange reveals this was a minor implementation detail in an already-merged patch, with no impact on the series' functionality. Junio's response suggests the change was more about code cleanliness than fixing a real bug, though he notes tracking the last line type could be useful for future enhancements. The discussion is technical but low-stakes, primarily of interest to those working on the diff subsystem's internals.
