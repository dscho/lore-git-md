**Thread**: [thread](./../02/23-49-27.thread.md#:~:text=2019/10/07/22-35-16)

Add trace2 regions in transport.c and builtin/push.c to better track
time spent in various phases of pushing:

```
* Listing refs
* Checking submodules
* Pushing submodules
* Pushing refs
```
