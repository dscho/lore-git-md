Graphical explanation:

Deleting to the left:
      v----- pointer
X_WORD_X
  ^--^------ selection

Deleting to the right:
  v--------- pointer
X_WORD_X
  ^--^------ selection


Current implementation doesn't behave correctly when there's multiple
spaces:

Deleting to the left:
        v--- pointer
X_WORD____X
       ^---- selection

Deleting to the right:
  v--------- pointer
X____WORD_X
  ^--------- selection
