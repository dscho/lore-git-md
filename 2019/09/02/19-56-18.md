**Thread**: [thread](./../../08/31/10-51-37.thread.md#:~:text=2019/09/02/19-56-18)

Selecting whether to do a "New Commit" or "Amend Last Commit" does not have
a hotkey.

With this patch, the user may toggle between the two options with
CTRL/CMD+e.
