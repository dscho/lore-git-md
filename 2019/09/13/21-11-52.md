**Thread**: [thread](./../05/20-09-52.thread.md#:~:text=2019/09/13/21-11-52)

Selecting whether to "Amend Last Commit" or not does not have a hotkey.

With this patch, the user may toggle between the two options with
CTRL/CMD+e.
