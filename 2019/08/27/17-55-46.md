Hmm, could it be perhaps that CFLAGS is being overwritten? To debug
Git with GDB I always add this to my config.mak file:

CFLAGS += -g3 -O0
LDFLAGS += -g3 -O0

And then just compile with `make`. Maybe give it a try?
