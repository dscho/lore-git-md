Please don't do

```
	producer \
	| consumer

```

instead, write

```
	producer |
	consumer

```

With two fewer bytes, and is far more common, judging from the
output of

    $ git grep -e '^[   ]*| [A-Za-z]' t

i.e. indent with whitespace or tab, pipe, space and alpha (i.e. the
beginning of the command, possibly a single-shot environment
assignment).
