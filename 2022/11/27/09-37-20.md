**Thread**: [thread](./09-37-16.thread.md#:~:text=2022/11/27/09-37-20)

Support comma-separated list of options in --diff-merges=. This allows
for shorter:

  --diff-merges=on,hide

instead of:

  --diff-merges=on --diff-merges=hide
