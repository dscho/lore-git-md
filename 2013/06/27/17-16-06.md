In case somebody wants to polish it to a real patch, this part
should at least be:

```
		case "$command" in
		'#'* | exec)
			# Be careful for oddball commands like 'exec'
			# that do not have a short-SHA-1 at the beginning
			# of $rest.
			;;
		*)
			sha1=$(git rev-parse --verify --quiet ${rest%% *}) &&
			rest="$sha1 ${rest#* }"
			;;
```
