Just a hint.  I think it is far safer and robust over time to do
something like this:

```
	sed -e 's/ Z$/ /' <<-\EOF
        Fixes: Z
        Acked-by: Z
        EOF

```

instead of a comment, which can warn human developers but does not
do anything to prevent their editors' auto-fix features from kicking
in.
