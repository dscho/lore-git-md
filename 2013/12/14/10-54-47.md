**Thread**: [thread](./../11/14-15-26.thread.md#:~:text=2013/12/14/10-54-47)

We've been avoiding PATH_MAX whenever possible. This patch avoids
PATH_MAX in get_pathname() and gives it enough room not to worry about
really long paths.
