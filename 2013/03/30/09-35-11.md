**Thread**: [thread](./../16/02-24-31.thread.md#:~:text=2013/03/30/09-35-11)

%>(N,trunc) truncates the right part after N columns and replace the
last two letters with "..". ltrunc does the same on the left. mtrunc
cuts the middle out.
