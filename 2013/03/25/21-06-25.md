Ugh, that is horrible. At first I thought it was even wrong, as we pass
&tail and not &dummy.next to read_loose_refs. But two wrongs _do_ make a
right, because read_loose_refs, rather than do:

```
  *tail = new;
  tail = &new->next;

```

does:

```
  (*tail)->next = new;
  *tail = new;

```
