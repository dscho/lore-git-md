**Thread**: [thread](./08-15-43.thread.md#:~:text=2013/07/31/08-15-43)

Empty ranges -LX,+0 and -LX,-0 are nonsensical in the context of blame
yet they are accepted (in fact, both are interpreted as -LX,+2).  Report
them as invalid.
