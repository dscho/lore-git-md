**Thread**: [thread](./12-52-45.thread.md#:~:text=2013/07/07/12-52-45)

When GIT_PS1_SHOWCOLORHINTS is turned on, there is no need to put a
detached HEAD within parenthesis: the color can be used to discriminate
the detached HEAD.
