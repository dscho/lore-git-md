I'm not 100% convinced about this, because the git-rebase.sh uses:

  "$GIT_DIR/hooks/pre-rebase" ${1+"$@"}

I haven't been able to find documentation for the ${1+"$@"} syntax.
Is it in POSIX?  It's not in the Bash manual:
