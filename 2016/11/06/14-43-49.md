> See the section on parameter expansion in "man bash". Basically:
>
> ${foo:-bar}
>
> expands to $foo, or "bar" if it is unset or empty. Without the colon:
>
>  ${foo-bar}
>
> expands to $foo, "bar" if it unset (but not if it is empty).

or I overlooked it.
