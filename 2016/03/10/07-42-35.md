I always wondered why we do LC_ALL=C.
Isn't that begging for trouble, when we feed UTF-8, ISO-8895-1
or other stuff into a program and say LC_ALL=C at the same time ?

On my Debian Linux system I have
LANG=en_US.UTF-8

and

$ locale -a
C
C.UTF-8
en_US.utf8
POSIX
--------------

Mac OS has LANG unset, and reports
locale -a
en_US
en_US.ISO8859-1
en_US.ISO8859-15
en_US.US-ASCII
en_US.UTF-8
#(and a lot more )
C
POSIX

-----
My Centos has 
LANG=en_US.UTF-8

and reports e.g.
en_US
en_US.iso88591
en_US.iso885915
en_US.utf8
(And many more)
