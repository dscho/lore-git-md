**Thread**: [thread](./12-58-37.thread.md#:~:text=2016/07/21/12-58-37)

We already suggest 'git rebase --abort' during a conflicted rebase.
Similarly, suggest 'git merge --abort' during conflict resolution on
'git merge'.
